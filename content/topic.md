# Topic and category pages

Read by `scripts/topic-page.py`, across 48 topic pages and 8 Level-1 category indexes.

The *How to read these* block is the one that stops a reader mistaking a derived view for an original one, and the absent-heading paragraph is the one that stops them reading a gap in the evidence as a gap in the world. Both are load-bearing.

The second paragraph of *How to read these* carries a `{base}` link and has not moved here yet.

## how-to-read

Both documents are **derived views**. Every paragraph and every table row in them was written for, and checked in, the report of the place it appears under — nothing is written at the topic level, and no fact reaches this page that is not already sourced on the sentence that carries it. Sections are places, in alphabetical order.

## category-caveat

This is an index, not a report. A Level-1 report would be a composition of the same material one level up, and the taxonomy is a strict single-parent tree, so it can be built later from exactly these documents and costs nothing to defer.
