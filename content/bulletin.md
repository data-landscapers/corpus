# Bulletins

The closing notes on the country and topic bulletins, read by `scripts/bulletin.py`. Markdown, not HTML: the bulletins are markdown documents that `render.py` converts later.

Both explain the same discipline — an item is summarised once and cross-referenced everywhere else it belongs — which is what stops a reader counting the same development twice when it appears under three countries. They differ only in what "once" is keyed to, so they are two blocks rather than one with a placeholder: the sentences are not the same sentence with a word swapped, and pretending otherwise would make both worse.

## tail-country

*Each item is summarised once — under a region where it carries one, otherwise under the first place its record lists — and cross-referenced from every other country and region it touches.*

## tail-topic

*Each item is summarised once, under the first topic its record lists, and cross-referenced from every other topic it carries. The topic bulletin does not subdivide by country; the country bulletin covers the same window place by place.*
