## 17 September 2026

- Every country and region now has its reports checked against the latest evidence about once a month. Out-of-date or unsupported statements in a report you read may have been corrected since your last visit.
- The alerts page now puts the RSS feed address for your chosen countries and topics directly under the sign-up button. You can follow the catalogue in a feed reader without giving an email address.
- The main site's newsletter sign-up now opens the alerts page. One form covers news from data-landscapers.io and the catalogue, and old newsletter links still work.

## 16 September 2026

- Weekly email alerts launched for new catalogue documents on up to five countries and five topics. You get one email on Monday and can change or stop your alerts from a link in any email.
- Existing Data Landscapers newsletter subscribers now receive the weekly Monday alert email. The content is the same, but it arrives once a week.
- Every citation in the 54 country status reports was checked against its source, and 3,774 claims were corrected or removed. A figure or statement you took from a status report before this date may have changed.
