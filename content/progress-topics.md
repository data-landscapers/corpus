Every country progress report answers the same fixed frame of 121 indicators over the same window. This page counts the answers: for each indicator, how many of the 54 countries came out at each value.

Nothing here is computed beyond the counting. Each number is a tally of what the country progress reports say, and every row sums to 54 because every report answers every indicator. Follow a topic to its progress report, or an indicator to its section within that report, to read what the countries behind a number actually did.

*No evidence* means the base holds nothing on that indicator for that country. It is a statement about this base, not about the country.
