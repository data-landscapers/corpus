## Summary of the period

<!-- narrative: summary -->
On 2026-07-27 the SADC-RTGS [took a second settlement currency](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system), having settled in rand alone since 2013; on 2026-07-30 the region's central bank governors [approved regional frameworks](https://independentnews.co.sz/46874/business/central-banks-rally-to-protect-regional-financial-system-against-new-age-cyber/) on cybersecurity, climate risk and cross-border crisis management. Both are the work of one institution, the Committee of Central Bank Governors.

The community's own digital instruments did not move: the Digital Transformation Strategy remains the [draft tabled to ministers on 12 October 2022](https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2025-08/EN%20-%205.2.3B%20-%20CM--SADC-ICT-INFO-MINISTERS-2023-4.8D%20-%20Draft%20SADC%20DTS_1.pdf), and the [2012 infrastructure master plan](https://www.sadc.int/document/regional-infrastructure-development-master-plan-executive-summary-2012) reaches its horizon in 2027 with no revision on file.

Cross-border capacity was added by operators: a fibre corridor [carrying traffic from 2026-07-06](https://itweb.africa/article/powertel-paratus-zimbabwe-switch-on-new-digital-highway/Gb3Bw7WadXZq2k6V), a subsea system [announced on 2025-09-24](https://seacom.com/news/unveiling-seacom-20). Where regional coordination is documented over the period, it is COMESA's rather than SADC's, across an overlapping membership.

<!-- narrative: instruments -->
The instruments that moved were made by central banks. Meeting at Ezulwini, the SADC Committee of Central Bank Governors' subcommittee on banking supervision and financial stability [finalised and approved](https://independentnews.co.sz/46874/business/central-banks-rally-to-protect-regional-financial-system-against-new-age-cyber/) regional frameworks on cybersecurity, climate-related financial risk and cross-border crisis management. Central Bank of Eswatini governor Phil Mnisi [said single-country data no longer suffices](https://independentnews.co.sz/46874/business/central-banks-rally-to-protect-regional-financial-system-against-new-age-cyber/) for regional stability, and urged supervisors to extend oversight to cloud hosting providers, payment processors, and artificial intelligence used in credit scoring and fraud detection.

The Botswana Communications Regulatory Authority [announced on 2026-03-13](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/13/botswana-and-five-sadc-countries-slash-roaming-tariffs-under-one-network-area-framework/) that Botswana and five other member states — Malawi, Lesotho, Mozambique, Zambia and Zimbabwe — had reduced and harmonised roaming tariffs under the One Network Area framework, with reported cuts of between 10% and 98.6%.

The community's own instruments did not move. The Digital Transformation Strategy and Action Plan is the [draft of 12 October 2022](https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2025-08/EN%20-%205.2.3B%20-%20CM--SADC-ICT-INFO-MINISTERS-2023-4.8D%20-%20Draft%20SADC%20DTS_1.pdf), with no adoption decision on record. <!-- narrative: systems -->
The SADC-RTGS [admitted the Angolan kwanza on 2026-07-27](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system), the first currency added since it began operating in 2013 and the second it settles in. Fifteen of the sixteen member states participate through more than 80 banks, and the system settles R250.7bn a month. The SARB's [payments head](https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system) named the Botswana pula and the Mozambican metical as next, and a SARB spokesperson [told Central Banking](https://www.centralbanking.com/central-banks/currency/7976492/southern-african-countries-consider-joining-sadc-rtgs) that Mauritius, Tanzania, Zambia and Zimbabwe are weighing onboarding, potentially by the end of 2027 — intent attributed to the system's operator, not decisions by those central banks.

Retail settlement moved on a smaller scale. TCIB [activated its South Africa-Botswana corridor](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/02/payincs-tcib-unlocks-real-time-cross-border-payments-between-south-africa-and-botswana/) in March 2026, through a single bank. COMESA's Digital Retail Payments Platform, [launched on 2025-10-10](https://techcabal.com/2025/10/10/comesa-payment-system-trade-local-currencies/), is piloting between Malawi and Zambia against a target of under 3% of transaction value, from a stated average of about 8%.

The systems SADC would own are earlier. The cross-border digital-ID and eKYC framework [announced in August 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries) with Co-Develop and FinMark Trust covers all 16 member states on paper and follows a South Africa-Lesotho pilot; a year on, the base has no build, budget or launch date for it. The regional internet exchange point stood at a [two-bidder shortlist](https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk) — BoFiNet of Botswana and one Zimbabwean company — on 2025-11-27, with no award in the eight months since.

Capacity that arrived was commercial: the Powertel-Paratus corridor's first phase [went live on 2026-07-06](https://itweb.africa/article/powertel-paratus-zimbabwe-switch-on-new-digital-highway/Gb3Bw7WadXZq2k6V), and Seacom [announced a 48-fibre-pair Indian Ocean system](https://seacom.com/news/unveiling-seacom-20) on 2025-09-24 with no route, cost or in-service date on record.

<!-- narrative: coordination -->
The IDEA programme, US$2.48bn approved in June 2024 with COMESA leading regional coordination, held its [first annual forum in Lusaka from 27 to 29 July 2026](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/forum-recomenda-harmonizacao-das-politicas/) and closed it with a Strategic Action Plan for 2026-2027 — harmonised regional digital policies, standards and interoperability frameworks, and investment in digital public infrastructure — and a first Annual Digital Action Matrix.

The programme's composition has changed since the base last recorded it. The World Bank's [2024 factsheet](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/factsheet/2024/06/27/inclusive-digitalization-in-eastern-and-southern-africa-program-afe-angola) named phase 1 as Angola, DR Congo and Malawi; [COMESA's release of 2026-07-27](https://www.comesa.int/comesa-launches-inaugural-idea-annual-digital-forum-to-drive-inclusive-cross-border-digital-transformation-in-africa/) names its first phase as Zambia, DR Congo, Malawi and Angola, with Eswatini, Tanzania and Madagascar preparing to join under Phase II.

COMESA also opened work towards a regional AI strategy, with a [first stakeholder activity in Lusaka on 2026-07-14](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/zambia-and-comesa-launch-ai-initiative-to-advance-regional-digital-transformation/). No draft is published.

SADC's own coordination in the period runs through spectrum: its third preparatory meeting for the ITU World Radiocommunication Conference 2027 opened in Luanda on 2026-08-03 on spectrum management, connectivity and [infrastructure resilience](https://www.novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/terceira-reuniao-da-sadc-sobre-radiocomunicacoes-acontece-na-ressaca-do-ataque-cibernetico-73684.html), ahead of a common position that is not yet agreed. Jornal de Angola reports it opened five days into a cyberattack on Angola's largest operator.
