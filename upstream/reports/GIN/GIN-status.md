# Guinea: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/GIN/ledger.csv` (86 systems and instruments, 13 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Guinea's digital build is concentrated in payments and identity. The central bank [opened NimbaPay, a national instant payment rail, on 22 July 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/le-pm-amadou-oury-bah-lance-les-operations-de-la-plateforme-nimbapay-initiee-par-la-bcrg-nous-devons-rattraper-notre-retard/). The identity stack is being rebuilt domestically after the World Bank's WURI project [closed two years early in June 2024](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061); [biometric birth certificates and national IDs are enrolled in one commune](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push).

The instruments that would govern either are absent: no data-protection law, no cybersecurity statute, no payments statute and no national AI strategy. The payments law and the payments governance council both [sit at terms-of-reference stage under a World Bank project](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

Two things ran the other way. [Facebook, YouTube and TikTok have been blocked since 28 July 2026](https://www.guinee360.com/28/07/2026/perturbation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-guinee-lablogui-denonce-un-blocage-cible-de-facebook-youtube-et-tiktok/), unacknowledged by any authority. The state's own biometric-enrolment contractor [stated that fees not provided for in the regulations were being taken](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/) at centres issuing documents the government had declared free.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
International connectivity rests on a single submarine cable. GUILAB, the landing company, [described the ACE cable as saturated after some 13 to 15 years as the country's only landing](https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/atterrissement-dun-second-cable-sous-marin-en-guinee-la-guilab-et-ses-partenaires-se-concertent-a-conakry/). Two further landings were contracted within the period — a construction and maintenance agreement for [MEDUSA Africa in May 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/2e-cable-sous-marin-la-guinee-propulse-sa-souverainete-numerique-avec-la-signature-de-laccord-medusa-africa/) and a [co-investment in Via Africa agreed in June 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/06/saturation-du-cable-sous-marin-ahmed-karifa-diawara-dg-de-la-guilab-devoile-la-riposte-de-la-guinee/) — neither carrying a published service date.

The ministry's [2025 review reported the backbone quadrupled to 200 Gbps and 585 schools connected under GIGA](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026), against a [2,200-school target for end-2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0711-50249-guinea-kicks-off-plan-to-bring-internet-to-2-200-schools); no independent measurement is on file.

Hosting and naming came under national control in September 2025, with a [Tier III data centre](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-48427-guinea-opens-tier-3-national-data-center-to-strengthen-digital-transition) and [sovereign .gn management](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/05/guinea-launches-national-domain-gn-and-unveils-first-tier-iii-data-center). The [state has held 87.5% of MTN Guinee since 30 December 2024](https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/mtn-sells-guinea-conakry-business-to-guinean-government).

FESATEL [threatened to strike on 21 May 2026 over FTTH and Wifi-Zone restrictions, and stated that Starlink is not recognised in Guinea](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/05/interdiction-des-activites-des-wifi-zones-et-restriction-du-ftth-la-fesatel-hausse-le-ton-et-menace-dune-greve-ni-les-appels-ne-marchent-ni-la-connexion-internet-ne-marche/); no Starlink licensing decision is on file. Facebook, YouTube and TikTok have been blocked since 28 July 2026 on [independent tests by the bloggers' association ABLOGUI](https://www.guinee360.com/28/07/2026/perturbation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-guinee-lablogui-denonce-un-blocage-cible-de-facebook-youtube-et-tiktok/), [still running 7 August 2026](https://www.jeuneafrique.com/1830582/politique/guinee-des-reseaux-sociaux-coupes-alors-que-mamadi-doumbouya-est-a-letranger/); a consumer-protection union [additionally reported WhatsApp restricted and bandwidth reduced for months, with operators saying the decision was not theirs](https://www.guinee114.com/2026/08/04/guinee-bany-sidibe-denonce-les-restrictions-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux-et-la-degradation-de-linternet/). ANSSI is [named as an established agency](https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/), but no founding instrument for it is held. Guinea's cybersecurity statute, [Loi L/2016/037/AN of 2016](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l2016037an_relative_a_la_cybersecurite_et_protection_des_do.pdf), is now held — an image-only scan whose cybercrime and evidence-collection provisions are known only from ANSSI's own summary.

A decree of 30 July 2026 [converted the distance-learning institute ISFAD into the Universite Numerique de Guinee (UNG)](https://www.africaguinee.com/enseignement-superieur-lisfad-devient-officiellement-luniversite-numerique-de-guinee-ung/), a public scientific establishment mandated for online higher education and digital-pedagogy support; no programme or budget is published.

[NimbaPay opened on 22 July 2026](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/le-pm-amadou-oury-bah-lance-les-operations-de-la-plateforme-nimbapay-initiee-par-la-bcrg-nous-devons-rattraper-notre-retard/), making banks, electronic-money institutions and microfinance institutions interoperable nationwide, [built in 127 days on Mojaloop by Guineenne de Monetique](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2807-57779-guinea-launches-nimbapay-its-national-instant-payment-system). [Tresor Pay, for state administrative fees, launched on 22 December 2025](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/guinea-rolls-out-tresor-pay-as-digital-gateway-for-public-payments). The BCRG has also [gone to tender for a resilient SWIFT infrastructure, bids due 31 August 2026](https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Avis-Swift.pdf). The [BCRG's own project register](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/) — undated, its latest dated entry 28 January 2025 — records the rest: RTGS migration to ISO 20022 undelivered, held by unpaid invoices and customs delays; the unique bank identifier project at consultant recruitment; PAPSS participation with no transaction recorded; and ECOWAS EPSS withdrawn from the bank's priorities after one 2024 evaluation meeting.

[WURI-Guinea closed on 28 June 2024 with about 8,000 unique IDs issued](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050225110559061); ONECI [began enrolling biometric birth certificates and national IDs in Sanoyah commune in March 2026, phase one covering 2,000 certificates](https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push). The RNPP became the electoral file's single source [under a January 2026 DGE-ONECI convention](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/), which [carried 6.95m electors across 23,681 stations for the 31 May 2026 elections](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/04/fichier-electoral-la-dge-annonce-pres-de-7-millions-delecteurs-pour-les-elections-couplees-du-31-mai/). The biometric payroll FUGAS [confirmed about 130,000 of some 277,000 registered civil servants](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3011-50979-guinea-claims-26-9m-saved-since-2024-with-biometric-audit-of-public-workforce); the unique personal identification number called for then is still not on record. Free issuance is policy and contested in practice: the state's technical partner Multimedia Glory Guinee [stated on 28 July 2026 that unprovided-for fees were being taken at enrolment centres, with no ministry response](https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/).

The platform layer is populated, from [TELEMO for procurement](https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/26/guinea-digitizes-public-procurement-with-launch-of-telemo-platform/) to [LANDAYA for industrial licences](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/industrie-dre-diaka-sidibe-lance-landaya-pour-fluidifier-la-relation-entre-letat-et-les-promoteurs-industriels/); the layer beneath it is not: [GovStack is at workshop stage](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4820/guinea-launches-govstack-workshop-to-advance-digital-public-services), the [NG Tech Innovation platform signed 24 July 2026 discloses no value, duration or scope](https://horoya.net/2026/07/25/guinee-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-sallient-pour-moderniser-lidentification-et-letat-civil/), and [RETSSM has software but no legal provisions, security posture or user base](https://letravail224.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AMI-IT-WARDIP-3_Copier.pdf).

A data-protection law is now held, [Loi L/2016/037/AN](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l2016037an_relative_a_la_cybersecurite_et_protection_des_do.pdf) — see Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, above — previously only [flagged at Transform Africa Summit 2025](https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee) with no text, date or authority recorded. Guinea [ratified the African Union convention on cyber security and personal data protection by decree on 26 July 2017](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ratification.jpg), a date the Union's own list puts at 31 July 2018; [both the ratifying decree and the promulgation decree for the authorising law are now held, in the same August 2017 gazette](https://journal-officiel.sgg.gov.gn/JO/2017/guinee-jo-2017-08.pdf), though the law itself remains held only by title. [Loi L/2016/035/AN of 2016 frames electronic signature, digital archiving and online commerce](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l-2016-035-an_relative_aux_transactions_electroniques.pdf), and a [2021 decree makes the telecoms regulator the supervisor of electronic transactions including mobile money](https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/DECRET-D-2021-092-PRG-SGG-Copie-2.pdf). Absent are a payments statute and a Conseil National de Paiement, [at terms-of-reference stage awaiting the World Bank's no-objection](https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/).

A [decree of 21 May 2026 created a Digital Compliance Fee on foreign digital services, tiered from an initial flat rate](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2605-55916-guinea-introduces-3-tax-on-foreign-digital-services), a Digital Sovereignty Fund financed from it, and a Digital Analysis and Regulation Platform to monitor foreign services; neither fund nor platform is on record as operating, and a six-month transition runs before enforcement. [Drafting of a National Data Governance Strategy opened on 19 June 2026](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2606-56858-guinea-begins-work-on-national-data-governance-strategy).

The day after NimbaPay opened, the consumer body AGUIDUTEN [published twelve conditions](https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/) — a tariff grid, complaint deadlines, security audits and fraud-statistics publication — with no regulator or operator response on file.

The digital portfolio held through the [reshuffle of 27 July 2026](https://actuguinee.org/guinee-le-president-mamadi-doumbouya-remanie-son-gouvernement-plusieurs-nouveaux-visages-font-leur-entree/), MCENI keeping the minister gained at its February 2026 merger; a World Bank tender that week still names the predecessor, MPTEN.

No official explanation for the block is on record; a consumer-protection union [said operators and ISPs told it they were compelled to comply, without naming the authority](https://www.guinee114.com/2026/08/04/guinee-bany-sidibe-denonce-les-restrictions-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux-et-la-degradation-de-linternet/).
