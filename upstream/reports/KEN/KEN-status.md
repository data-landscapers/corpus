# Kenya: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-06 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/KEN/ledger.csv` (134 systems and instruments, 4 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Kenya has a data-protection statute in force since 2019, a mobile-money rail that moved KES 41.68tn in the year to March 2026, an identity register the Interior Ministry reports as holding more than 42 million digitised records, and a government services platform carrying over 22,000 services. It has no adopted artificial-intelligence instrument, no national count of registered data controllers, and no cloud region in commercial service. The state's own digital spending fell for a second consecutive year, to [KSh 8.6bn for 2026/27](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ict-allocation-drops-to-sh8-6-billion-in-budget-5493262) from KSh 16.3bn in 2024/25. The National AI Strategy 2025-2030 is costed at KSh 152bn over five years and [carries no dedicated allocation](https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/) in that budget. Service migration onto the eCitizen platform ran at [1,695 of a target 11,000](https://nation.africa/kenya/business/agencies-defy-ruto-s-e-citizen-order-1-695-services-yet-to-be-migrated-5506558) in the year to 31 March 2026, against 3,458 the year before.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Satellite broadband reached 24,999 subscribers and 0.9% of the fixed market by March 2026, and the operator then [suspended new sign-ups across seven counties](https://techweez.com/2026/07/08/starlink-kenya-waitlist-capacity-counties/), diverting orders to a deposit-backed waitlist, with median download speeds down 26% year on year to 34.55 Mbps. Traffic across the London Internet Exchange's Nairobi and Mombasa points [rose twentyfold in a year to 330 Gbps](https://itweb.africa/article/linx-expands-kenya-peering-network/5yONP7ErXpRMXWrb), with 55 or more networks connected. Against the 100,000km national fibre target, the only delivered figures published are the regulator's for the universal-service-funded slice: [2,193km under phase one and 1,173 last-mile sites under phase two](https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-07/Annual%20Report%20FY%202024-2025.pdf) at 30 June 2025.

The US$1bn Microsoft and G42 facility at Olkaria missed a May 2026 launch and the project's [full 1GW draw is about a third of Kenya's roughly 3GW supply](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-and-g42-data-center-in-kenya-stalled-due-to-lack-of-power-capacity/). No data-centre tariff, gazetted large-load connection rule or grid-priority instrument is held, so every power arrangement on record is bilateral to one project. The National Treasury told transaction advisers in April 2026 that the Konza National Data Centre runs [706 kW of certified capacity at an actual load of about 300 kW, with 100 of its 144 racks in use](https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf) and hosts 145 of 451 government institutions; its expansion to 288 racks drew no appropriation at the 2026/27 budget and is being structured as a public-private partnership.

The National Cyber Security Agency Order was gazetted as Legal Notice 89 on 2026-05-15 and [announced as approved on 2026-06-22](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-approves-national-cyber-security-agency/wbrpO7g2QdevDLZn), at a tabled establishment cost of about KSh 4bn. The presidency's own website was [defaced on 2026-07-18 with a 5 BTC ransom demand and restored on 2026-07-20](https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-restores-presidential-website-after-cyber-attack/Gb3Bw7WadxLq2k6V), the second compromise inside twelve months; cause, access vector and attribution are undisclosed.

The Interior Ministry reports [more than 42 million identity records digitised](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/kenya-moves-to-institutionalize-trust-as-digital-government-services-expands) in 2025/26, with biometric health enrolment extended to dependants aged 7 to 17. Registered mobile-money accounts reached [94.09 million with 564,330 active agents in May 2026](https://www.centralbank.go.ke/national-payments-system/mobile-payments/), from 87.46 million and 451,157 in August 2025. A High Court ruling of March 2026 [recognised a registered phone number as a personal digital identifier](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/kenyan-high-court-ruling-recognizes-phone-numbers-as-digital-id), barred recycling dormant numbers without consent, and gave the Attorney-General six months to legislate; that window closes about September 2026 and nothing on file shows a framework tabled.

eCitizen carries [more than 22,000 services from over 100 agencies](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/ecitizen-grows-to-22000-services-as-kenya-briefs-parliament-on-digital-platform-audit/) and collects KSh 600m a day, while the Auditor-General alleges KSh 9.4bn was irregularly routed to private entities. A teachers' union reported members [turned away and billed in cash](https://peopledaily.digital/news/kuppet-nyamira-raises-alarm-over-sha-system-failures-ahead-of-rutos-public-address) when health-claims authorisation failed, with the Authority answering that the Digital Health Agency runs the system on its behalf. Kenya's clearest written interoperability requirement is a procurement document: the health ministry's enterprise-system tender [makes financial, tax, insurance, pensions, payroll, laboratory and identity interfaces day-one mandatory](https://health.go.ke/node/2517) and requires primary hosting inside an approved Kenyan government or health-sector environment.

The Vision 2030 Delivery Secretariat scored all 47 counties on service automation, revenue systems and data use in February 2026, [from 98.3 for Murang'a to 28.8 for Wajir](https://vision2030.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/ASSESSMENT-REPORT-AUTOMATION-EMPLOYMENT-IN-COUNTIES-23-02-2026.pdf), with six counties graded as reliant on manual and cash-based systems — a desk review, not a survey of users. The national DPI roadmap is still unpublished; GIZ [tendered technical assistance on 2026-07-31 to develop a bankable Kenyan digital public infrastructure project](https://www.service.bund.de/IMPORTE/Ausschreibungen/vmp-giz/2026/08/51947.html), with offers closing 2026-09-01.

Kenya's artificial-intelligence position rests on a draft, a bill and a strategy without money. The [draft AI and Other Emerging Technologies Policy](https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/AI%20Policy%20Doc/draft-kenya-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy-2026.pdf) runs to 226 pages, was open for comment from 2026-07-21 to 2026-08-04, and asserts [effects-based jurisdiction over any entity outside Kenya](https://nairobibusinessmonthly.com/kenyas-draft-ai-policy-seeks-power-over-foreign-tech-giants/) whose AI outputs have direct and foreseeable effects in the country while leaving "high risk" undefined and stating no penalties. Deployment is running ahead of the rules that would govern it. Venture funding fell sharply: Kenyan startups raised [US$126m in the first half of 2026, third in Africa and the weakest half since early 2021](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/27/african-venture-capital-is-backing-fewer-founders-than-ever/), against US$1.04bn across 2025.
