# Ghana: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-11 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/GHA/ledger.csv` (121 systems and instruments, 9 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Ghana has a data-protection statute in force since 2012, an identity credential registering 19.2 million adults and integrated with 262 institutions, a mobile-money market carrying [GH¢492.9bn on 954 million transactions in June 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/mobile-money-transactions-hit-gh%c2%a2492-9bn-in-june/), and a national artificial-intelligence strategy launched in April 2026 at a US$270m headline. The 2026 appropriation carries [GHS 729,947,850 for ICT infrastructure, regulation and capacity building](https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf). It has no register of licensed digital lenders, no open-data instrument, and no telecom statistics from the regulator later than the third quarter of 2024, and no published text for any of the bills the government has named.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
A request for applications now offers eleven spectrum lots on a US$230m aggregate reserve, after the exclusive wholesale 5G licence was deleted with effect from 15 July 2026. The regulator's [consolidated response of 31 July 2026](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NCA-Consolidated-Response-5G-RFA-2026.pdf) moves the submission deadline to 27 August, refuses any extension to the 70% population coverage obligation of 6 March 2027, and rewrites the dominant operator's rural schedule into fixed dates of 2029, 2032 and 2035.

Operators recorded [more than 8,000 fibre cuts a year](https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/ghanas-digital-backbone-comes-under-strain.html) as at May 2026. The dominant operator's own half-year accounts show [capital expenditure of GHS 2,100.4m for the six months to 30 June 2026](https://mtn.com.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Scancom-PLC-MTN-Ghana-H1-2026-Results.pdf), down 36.3%, with intensity falling from 29.1% to 14.0%. The [submarine carrier services authorisation](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Electronic-Communication-Submarine-Carrier-Services-Authorisation_November-2024-V5.pdf) issued in November 2024 runs five years, charges an annual fee of 1% of revenue, and requires six years' retention of charging and financial data. The copy held is the template.

The largest facility, at [30 MW designed for expansion to 150 MW](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/investors-pour-into-africas-digital-backbone-as-data-centre-projects-near-900-mw/), is known only from a regional trade round-up, while an analyst brief for the EU digital investment facility put the whole commercial market at [about 4.8 MW of live critical IT load in 2024](https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ghana.pdf). No power arrangement is published for any named Ghanaian site.

The [Cybersecurity Act 2020](https://csa.gov.gh/resources/cybersecurity_Act_2020%28Act_1038%29.pdf) establishes the Cyber Security Authority and provides for designation and compliance audit of critical information infrastructure, though nothing has been published under those powers. The authority's community media project, launched on 3 August 2026, is to train [about 60 media houses and more than 100 journalists](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cyber-security-authority-czech-republic-partner-to-train-journalists-in-cybercrime-reporting.html) in Accra through August 2026 and in Kumasi in September, certifying those who complete every session into a proposed press corps. The regulator's latest statistical bulletin covers [July to September 2024 and appeared eight months later](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Q3-2024-Statistical-Bulletin-Third-Quarter-Volume-9-Isuue-3.pdf), putting one operator on 75.09% of the market, and its register of authorised providers has [42 of 78 operational](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Q2-2025-Final-LIST-OF-AUTHORISED-ISPS-IN-GHANA-1.pdf).

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Amendment regulations came into force on 9 June 2026 making biometric matching against the register the only lawful means of verification and barring photocopying, scanning and retention. Banks, telecoms operators and hospitals have [until 2 November 2026](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/banks-telcos-and-hospitals-have-until-november-2-to-stop-taking-ghana-card-photocopies.html) to install readers and integrate. The biometric SIM re-registration announced for the first quarter of 2026 has still not begun.

Registered wallets rose from 76.4 million to 84.6 million in the year to June 2026 with 26.4 million active at ninety days; interoperable transfers ran to [GH¢6.2bn, about 1.3% of total mobile-money value](https://www.myjoyonline.com/mobile-money-transactions-hit-gh%c2%a2492-9bn-in-june/). The digital-credit licensing deadline of 30 June 2026 elapsed and the central bank [named twenty unlicensed lending applications on 3 August 2026](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Notice-No.25-BOG-SEC-GOV-2026-Notice-PUBLICATION-OF-UNLICENSED-DIGITAL-CREDIT-SERVICE-PROVIDERS-ON-THE-BANKS-WEBSITE-SOCIAL-MEDIA-PLATFORMS-AND-OTHER-COMM-CHANNELS-1.pdf), framing the harm first as a violation of customer data privacy. The promised register of licensed providers is not published.

The central bank and the securities regulator launched the Ashanti Regional phase of the [National Virtual Asset Literacy Initiative](https://myjoyonline.com/bog-and-sec-roll-out-ashanti-navali-initiative-to-drive-responsible-virtual-asset-adoption/) on 10 August 2026, a template for nationwide rollout with no funding or date stated.

The customs valuation system had [analysed about 366,000 declarations to 17 July 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/publican-ai-system-generates-gh%c2%a2300m-additional-revenue-since-rollout-ato-forson/) with collections up 17.5%, on the finance ministry's own unaudited figures, which importers contest. The land use planning system is [being redeveloped into a web-based platform for planning, development control and street addressing](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MLGCRA.pdf) inside a sub-programme appropriated GHS 36,454,968 for 2026, from which no digital share is separable. The statistical service published the third wave of its [Governance Series](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/politics/ghana-news-governance-reforms-yielding-results-but-remain-fragile-government-statistician.html) on 6 August 2026, a six-monthly repeat panel across all 16 regions, and the Government Statistician put its findings to digital remedies — digitising public services and publishing official fees.

The 2012 Act is unamended, a draft replacement published during 2025 has not been enacted, and the regulator declared 2026 a year of enforcement without publishing a fine, prosecution or registered-controller count. The central bank issued a [cyber and information security directive on 26 March 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-strengthens-financial-sector-cyber-defences-with-new-security-directive-launch/). The enforcement action framed as a data-privacy violation in August 2026 was taken by the central bank, not by the data-protection regulator. The health ministry's [draft AI health policy, presented on 5 August 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-develops-ai-policy-to-guide-safe-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-ghanas-healthcare-system/), would bar patient data from leaving the country without safeguards and cover the full lifecycle across public and private facilities.

Ghana refused a United States health data-sharing agreement in April 2026. The data-protection regulator's executive director told a state news agency that the offer [covered not only health data sets but metadata, dashboards, reporting tools, data models and data dictionaries](https://english.news.cn/20260730/b5516c3a8f034f38aae45823311057c6/c.html), gave ten United States entities access requiring no prior Ghanaian approval, sought 25 years of citizens' health records and settled disputes under United States law, against US$109m subject to congressional appropriation. No text of the offer is held.

The virtual-asset statute was [passed by Parliament on 19 December 2025](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/VASP-Act-FAQs.pdf). The central bank's [policy position of November 2025](https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Ghanas-Policy-Position-on-Virtual-Assets-and-Service-Providers.pdf) records more than 3 million users and over 100 service providers captured by a registration exercise in July 2025, before any law. On the Bank's own account the Act splits supervision with the securities regulator by activity and places virtual assets outside deposit protection and outside any statutory compensation fund. No gazetted text is on file.

The communications minister named a [National Data Exchange Hub the government plans to establish](https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/govt-committed-to-building-ai-ecosystem-to-create-jobs-promote-economic-growth/) on 29 July 2026, without saying whether it revives the exchange launched in 2009 or replaces it. The EU mid-term review supplied the [EUR 136m reserved for 2025 to 2027](https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/10d4ba3f-ee66-426e-8c60-bcd89b09fd11_en?filename=ad-mip-2024-c2024-7502-sub-saharan-africa-annex_en.pdf), split between green growth, cities, governance and support measures, again with no digital priority area.

Of the [GHS 591,947,850 domestically funded](https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf) under ICT infrastructure, regulation and capacity building in 2026, GHS 536,745,448 goes to the information technology agency, GHS 25,346,198 to the cyber-security authority, GHS 17,308,560 to the ICT capacity centre and GHS 2,827,244 to the domain registry. The data-protection regulator's GHS 9,720,400 is entirely its own internally generated funds, with no Consolidated Fund allocation at all.

The universal-access fund carries the rural telephony project as its top priority, with the last disbursement figure 60.2% as at May 2022; the draft [development fund bill would convert it](https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/30/amcham-ghana-joins-key-stakeholder-talks-on-new-digital-economy-fund/) into a wider digital-economy and innovation fund, with the fate of its rural-access earmark unestablished. No dedicated community-network regime exists, and the regulator told a policy dialogue on 29 July 2026 that [an existing hotspot authorisation could found one](https://www.myjoyonline.com/isoc-ghana-urges-policy-reforms-to-expand-community-networks-and-bridge-rural-digital-divide/) — four such authorisations exist, against a [single authorised rural provider that is not operating](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Q2-2025-Final-LIST-OF-AUTHORISED-ISPS-IN-GHANA-1.pdf).

An unlimited 100 Mbit/s fibre package [fell from GH¢987 to GH¢299 a month in June 2026](https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-major-reduction-in-mtn-fibre-broadband-prices/), a cut extracted from one operator rather than a licensing change letting others compete. The demand side has not been measured nationally since 2019, when the regulator recorded [16.8% of households with internet access](https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Household-Survey-on-ICT-in-Ghana-Abridged-NRF.pdf) and smartphone ownership at 58.4% urban against 28.1% rural.
