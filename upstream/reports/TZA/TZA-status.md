# Tanzania: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-06 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/TZA/ledger.csv` (84 systems and instruments, 14 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The national identity register is the deepest measured system in the base. At December 2025 it held [26.4m people registered, 22.3m numbers issued and 20.6m cards distributed, about 81% of eligible adults](https://www.bot.go.tz/Publications/Regular/Annual%20Report/en/2026072316300213.pdf), with [more than 138 institutions integrated](https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/tanzanias-financial-inclusion-expands-but-usage-gap-is-becoming-the-next-challenge-2026-07-28-120905) for electronic verification. The 1.7m gap between numbers issued and cards in citizens' hands did not close over the year.

Payments carry the largest volumes and the newest rules. The instant payment system cleared [651m transactions worth TZS 54.95tn in 2025](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/tanzania-steps-up-cash-lite-drive-as-digital-payments-hit-new-highs-5541316), against 453m worth TZS 29.82tn in 2024. Since [1 July 2026](https://fbattorneys.co.tz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/1784910470319-1.pdf) an order under the electronic transactions Act has made nine scheduled classes of transaction electronic-payment-only, and the central bank has [capped several retail charges while rejecting a general price cap](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/biashara/makato-kikwazo-kuelekea-malipo-ya-kidijitali-5542380).

Government digital services rate highly from outside and thinly from inside. An external maturity index places Tanzania in its [top group, second in Africa](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-joins-global-leaders-in-digital-government-5325056); a ministerial inspection of the national backbone recorded [942.5 km built in one region against 65 citizens connected region-wide](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/waziri-kairuki-watanzania-wengi-hawajanufaika-na-mkongo-wa-taifa-5536718).

The base holds no Tanzanian cybersecurity strategy, no incident-response team, no cybercrime instrument, no open-data policy and no domestic state appropriation. Fourteen of the rows below are ***Not held***.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The state network is built ahead of its take-up. About [14,000 km of the national backbone is laid, carrying four public data centres and a third landing cable](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/waziri-kairuki-watanzania-wengi-hawajanufaika-na-mkongo-wa-taifa-5536718), and its regional manager states that phase one connected government offices and public institutions with individual citizens only next. No national connected-user total is held.

Commercial capacity is measured better than public capacity. The regulator's quarterly report to 31 March 2026 records [1,804 5G facilities on 10,084 towers, 32.83% population coverage and 11.04% geographic](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/telecom-infrastructure-investment-drives-5g-expansion-despite-low-smartphone-uptake-5491080), and names low smartphone penetration and a 23.7% geographic broadband gap as sector constraints. On the same report smartphone users fell to 28.5m while penetration rose to 42.5%; the base does not reconcile the two.

The state's data-centre estate cannot be sized. [Four public data centres have been commissioned](https://dailynews.co.tz/communications-sector-surges-400pc/) since March 2021 and no rack, floor-space or power capacity is published for any. The commercial estate is specified where the public one is not: the second phase at [Mbezi carries 110 racks](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-shift-gathers-pace-with-50-million-data-centre-4970620), unchanged since March 2025.

Security is the thinnest position here: no national cybersecurity strategy, cybercrime instrument or incident-response team is held, while the base holds such records for several neighbours. Energy appears only as other parties' caveat.

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Identity has both the coverage and the integration figures, and an enabling law that trails the operation. Registration from birth is [running as a pilot while the amendment to the registration Act is still to be passed](https://habarileo.co.tz/nida-kuanza-kusajili-watoto/), with child registration announced from FY2026/27; the [Infant Registration Bill was set for the session opening 27 January 2026 and no enactment is on file](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/tanzania-working-on-legislative-framework-for-infant-id-registration). The common identifier is designated but [still not issued as a distinct number, its end-2025 target passed unmet](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-rolls-out-infant-registration-for-stronger-citizen-id-5316036). A [second phase is tendered for iris and facial biometrics and offices in 31 districts, on an external loan of about US$70m](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/tanzania-seeks-biometrics-contractors-for-phase-ii-of-national-digital-id-project).

Payments are regulated at the price as well as the rail. Charges are capped per instrument rather than by general tariff: [bank-to-wallet interoperability at TZS 5,000 above TZS 500,000, instant and retail bank-to-bank transfers at TZS 2,000, card payments at zero](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/biashara/makato-kikwazo-kuelekea-malipo-ya-kidijitali-5542380). [113 providers are licensed](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407) and [all 47 participating financial service providers are connected](https://www.bot.go.tz/Publications/Regular/Annual%20Report/en/2026072316300213.pdf). A central bank digital currency remains under study.

The exchange layer is the weakest documented part of an otherwise well-rated stack. The service bus carries external validation but [no held source states how many institutions joined by the deadline of 30 July 2025](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/majaliwa-aagiza-taasisi-za-umma-kujiunga-na-e-ga-atoa-wiki-tano-5092568). Jamii X-Change, a separate build often conflated with it, is [still under construction with no go-live date](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-jamii-x-change-kuunganisha-mifumo-ya-tehama-ya-kisekta). Nothing is held on council-level systems across the 184 local authorities or on the statistical system.

Data protection is in force and its compliance base is unmeasured. The [Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 commenced on 1 May 2023](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/notification-obligations-arising-from-personal-dat) with a supervisory commission, a registration duty, a mandatory officer requirement and a breach-notification duty; practitioners record that *security breach* and *without undue delay* remain undefined and that there is no duty to notify data subjects. A [registration deadline of 8 April 2026 was set by ministerial directive, with nationwide inspections from the following day](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-to-begin-data-protection-inspections-in-april-5356792), followed by a [seven-day ultimatum to part-registered bodies](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/wananchi-waonywa-kutoa-taarifa-binafsi-kwa-taasisi-zisizosajiliwa-5494426), health and education named as still processing unregistered. No count of registered controllers and no inspection outcome, fine or decision is held after that date.

The statutory officer duty now has a [certification programme, the open university training and assessing and the commission certifying](https://mediawireexpress.co.tz/out-pdpc-launch-national-data-protection-programme/), with no intake number on file.

Restriction of access is recorded only from outside government. A monitoring organisation counts [eight shutdowns across 2025, the highest of any African state in its data](https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/), including a five-day nationwide blackout at the October election. No gazetted instrument, directive or regulator order is held for any of them, and the monitor states its counts are subject to revision.

The [National Data Management Framework was in stakeholder drafting for a January 2026 target](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/tanzania-yajipanga-ukinara-ulinzi-wa-data-uchumi-wa-kidijitali-afrika-5173946) and no record of completion or slippage is held.

<!-- narrative: ai-tech -->
Tanzania has no adopted artificial-intelligence instrument. Guidelines are [still being drafted, and an external outlook of 30 July 2026 places Tanzania among states still developing a strategy](https://cipit.strathmore.edu/east-african-artificial-intelligence-policy-outlook-report/) — the commission having said at the first national AI forum that one was [expected in 2025](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-charts-path-toward-responsible-ai-integration-for-a-tech-driven-economy-5135592). The sources name guidelines and a strategy separately and the base does not establish whether they are the same object. A [regional strategy is in drafting at East African Community level](https://itweb.africa/article/eac-moves-to-create-unified-ai-sovereignty-bloc/), with no adoption date or Tanzanian ratification step on file.

What exists ahead of the rules is hardware and ambition. The ICT ministry states that a [national AI data centre is under construction, justified on ending raw-data export and training models on domestic hydro and geothermal power](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/tanzania-yaonesha-mafanikio-ya-utekelezaji-wa-akili-unde-ai-katika-mkutano-wa-kimataifa-wa-waico-2026), with no site, cost, capacity or completion date, and that a Kiswahili large language model is written into the 2050 vision with no developer, budget, corpus or timetable. Sectoral permissioning has arrived before the national instrument: a statement of 31 July 2026 sets out [three requirements — health data stored inside Tanzania, healthcare AI systems approved by the relevant authorities, and local capacity to build them](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-steps-up-ai-drive-in-healthcare-amid-data-privacy-push-5542208), naming no instrument and no approving authority.

Deployments are few and small. A cardiac institute reports [digital patient tracking in service alongside AI-enabled echocardiography](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/jkci-s-digital-patient-tracking-system-sets-new-standard-for-follow-up-healthcare-in-tanzania-5528476), without patient volumes or procurement route. The only measured outcome in the base is a ports enquiry chatbot, [95.1% weighted task success with fifteen domain experts](https://digitalcommons.kennesaw.edu/ajis/vol18/iss2/3) in a peer-reviewed study, with no production deployment.

The sector's scale is stated as intent: the [framework targets the ICT share of GDP doubled to 3% by 2029](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-economy-to-contribute-1bn-to-tanzania-s-gdp-by-2034--5334400) from about 1.5% for 2022/23, with no intervening annual figure held.
