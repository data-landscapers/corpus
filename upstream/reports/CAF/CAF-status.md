# Central African Republic: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-10 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/CAF/ledger.csv` (70 systems and instruments, 14 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The Central African Republic's digital state exists mostly as law and appropriation rather than as systems. A [personal data protection law has been in force since January 2024 with no implementing decree](https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic) and the authority it creates is [not established, eighteen months past its statutory deadline](https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic); a [cybersecurity law of January 2024 created a national agency that appears nowhere in the 2024, 2025 or 2026 estimates](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf).

The appropriations show the scale: the 2026 programme budget carries [FCFA 4,000,000 for network and information-system security and FCFA 2,000,000 for digital trust](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf), FCFA 6,000,000 for the year for the whole country, against FCFA 1,600,000,000 transferred from the same ministry to the regulator.

Connectivity limits everything else. Internet use is [about 670,000 users, 12.0%, at October 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-central-african-republic), and satellite service live since March 2026 costs [about 136% of monthly income per head](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1703-136713-starlink-en-centrafrique-entre-couverture-nationale-et-defis-d-accessibilite). Fourteen of the rows below are ***Not held***.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
The backbone has not moved in three years. [935 km of terrestrial and sub-fluvial fibre has been in service on the Bangui-Douala corridor since February 2023](https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo), with no new kilometrage or route since, while the 2026 estimates give [FCFA 2,000,000 to connect prefecture capitals to Bangui over it](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf). Against that the minister has stated a [twelve-thousand-kilometre fibre commitment](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/02/02/presentation-de-voeux-le-ministere-de-leconomie-numerique-devoile-ses-grandes-orientations-pour-lannee-2026/), roughly ten times what is built, with no contract, financing or timetable.

Mobile service is licensed at all three operators and thin on the ground. [The third launched fourth-generation service in Bangui in May 2026](https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/5420/telecel-centrafrique-launches-4g-services-in-bangui-to-expand-national-connectivity); a licence is not coverage, and 4G population coverage was measured at 0.3% in 2023. Where one operator is the only network, its failure removes everything at once: at one northern town subscribers reported [no calls, no internet and no mobile money for close to a month, some officials unpaid because salary transfers run over mobile services](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/).

Satellite access arrived and was at once conditioned: service has been [live since 16 March 2026 with 450 government kits](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1703-136713-starlink-en-centrafrique-entre-couverture-nationale-et-defis-d-accessibilite), and in April the ministry [deactivated roaming on kits acquired abroad, on traceability and national-security grounds](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/central-african-republic-suspends-starlink-kits-citing-security-and-equipment-traceability-requirements/).

Nothing is built for hosting: the [national Tier III data centre remains unbuilt](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2705-138800-la-centrafrique-modernise-l-infrastructure-numerique-de-son-administration-avec-huawei), five threads and three sponsors circling it, a 2026 pitch repeating a 2022 target. The only cybersecurity capability delivered rather than announced is a [certification platform in a funder's 2023 delivered scope](https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo), with no operator or issuance record. Electricity access, the constraint every item here meets, was [17% in 2023](https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republic-explores-world/).

<!-- narrative: dpi -->
Identity reaches citizens, and it is priced informally. The card's [legal tariff of FCFA 4,500 was restored by interministerial order in June 2025 after five years of charging above it](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-prix-des-cartes-didentite-enfin-reduit-apres-cinq-ans-de-violations-criminelle); at one prefecture police are reported to [serve one religious group first and to demand FCFA 100,000 to 200,000 from another](https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-business-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-reprend-de-plus-belle-a-ndele/).

The one identity system with a hard number is electoral: a definitive list of [2,398,158 registered voters — 1,641,083 from 2020 plus 757,075 registered in 2025 — across 3,910 voting centres](https://french.xinhuanet.com/20250825/216678a5ca894a1d915b2f5528ff0973/c.html). A [national digital identity system was announced in November 2025 at US$13m, unifying existing biometric and civil-registration databases, on a 2027 to 2032 window](https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration), procurement still ahead.

Government platforms exist and publish no use: a [unified public-services portal launched in December 2025](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1212-51337-central-african-republic-launches-unified-digital-portal-for-all-public-services) with no usage figure; a [ministry-wide planning platform digitised one whole ministry](https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/03/central-african-republic-fully-digitizes-ministry-with-launch-of-dunia-platform/) at a cost stated nowhere; and the [online tax platform has published no filing figure since its March 2025 launch](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne). The only state system with a delivered result is a [payroll clean-up removing more than 2,000 ghost workers](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052925135520222/pdf/BOSIB-50d33b34-4918-409e-92f7-070a1773ac56.pdf). The state electricity utility's bills became [payable over one operator's mobile-money service on 2026-08-04, with no tariff, transaction fee or volume disclosed](https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/07/centrafrique-telecel-et-enerca-signent-un-partenariat-pour-digitaliser-le-paiement-des-factures-delectricite/).

Below that the layer is missing: no interoperability or data-exchange framework is held, the minister's own January 2026 statement being that state information systems remain heterogeneous; no health, education or agriculture system is held as a system, only appropriations of [FCFA 6,000,000 for education and FCFA 2,000,000 for agriculture](http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf); and nothing on local-government systems, in a year when municipal and regional elections were held for the first time in about 36 years.

<!-- narrative: governance -->
Two statutes are in force and neither has produced an institution. The [data protection law of January 2024 has no implementing decree](https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf) and its authority, which would wield a sanction ceiling of 5% of pre-tax turnover, is [not established](https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic). The [cybersecurity and cybercrime law of January 2024, carrying six to ten year sentences and fines of FCFA 5m to 10m](https://www.africatopsuccess.com/un-projet-de-loi-pour-lutter-contre-la-cybercriminalite-en-centrafrique/), is unchanged in text and was named by a commentator in July 2026 as the instrument now used against online content; its own text is not held. No national cybersecurity strategy, response team or operations centre exists: the only evidence is a 2022 appraisal recording all three absent and a score of 3.6 on a cybersecurity index against 29.5 for the region.

A [new public procurement code was adopted in December 2025, replacing a 2008 law, with a FCFA 10,000,000 publicity threshold and a 5% preference margin for national and regional firms](https://acap-rca.com/2025/12/11/centrafrique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-un-nouveau-cadre-legislatif-pour-la-commande-publique/); promulgation is not recorded and the enacted text is not held.

The universal service fund is a rule without an outcome. Provided for by a 2018 law and financed by a [2% levy on operators' previous-year turnover, it is still not operational, with effective implementation targeted for 2026](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026), and has never published an amount collected or a balance in the seven years since the levy took effect.
