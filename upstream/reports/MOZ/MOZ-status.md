# Mozambique: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-10 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/MOZ/ledger.csv` (129 systems and instruments, 18 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Enacted* — an instrument passed into law; pair with a qualifying clause for its in-force date. *Under review* — a law or policy currently being reconsidered. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
Mozambique's cyber-security statutes are enacted but not yet in force, and its data-protection statute is neither. Law 13/2026 and Law 14/2026 were published on 1 July 2026 and take effect on 29 September 2026, with implementing regulations due within 180 days ([gazette](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244)). The personal data protection law has been in preparation since at least April 2025, and the Cabinet submission the institute planned for June 2026 is not on record ([ministerial account](https://news.isocmedias.co.mz/2026/07/23/governo-prepara-lei-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais/)).

The institutional layer was rebuilt in a single quarter. A digital transformation agency, a multisectoral technical commission and an artificial-intelligence commission were all created by decree in March 2026 ([agency account](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mozambique-builds-institutional-framework-for-digital-transformation)), and none of the three creating decrees is held.

Where the state has built, it has built payments and identity. The instant payment system entered production in March 2026 and carried more than 11,000 transfers a day by 30 July ([central bank communique](https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/)), and the identification directorate produced 856,101 identity cards in the first half of 2026 ([directorate account](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-dnic-produces-over-800-thousand-national-identity-cards-in-the-first-semester/)). The state's own money behind that is small: the communications ministry's central apparatus holds MZN 111,260,610 of operating budget against a capital budget of MZN 2,637,800,000 that is entirely external ([budget maps](https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/publicacoes/politicas/plano-economico-e-social-e-orcamento-do-estado-pesoe/pesoe-2026/mapas-pesoe-2026/mapas-finais)).

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Spectrum for fifth-generation mobile service was assigned on 23 June 2026 to the three operators, by administrative consignment rather than auction ([regulator account](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-clears-path-for-5g-services/j5alr7QAwA97pYQk)); one reported paying US$56m for 210 MHz. The licence instruments are not published, but the regulator states the obligations: 710,000 subsidised smartphones and broadband for at least 3,000 schools and 1,000 community institutions ([award account](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-incm-awards-5g-licences-to-mobile-operators/)), with no deadline or penalty attaching to a named operator.

Rural coverage runs through one instrument. The universal access fund awarded 60 mobile stations to a single operator in October 2025, doubled from the 30 tendered ([award account](https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/mozambique-to-install-60-new-mobile-stations-to-boost-rural-connectivity/)), and no commissioning report has followed. The regulator's 2024 report leaves 6 of 453 administrative posts and 209 of 1,222 localities without mobile coverage ([regulation report](https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Relatorio-de-Regulacao-das-Comunicacoes_2024.pdf)), and no later figure is published.

The hosting estate is mostly commercial. Two carrier-neutral facilities and a university data centre inaugurated on 3 August 2026 are what the base can name; the state's own facility at Maluana is described on the regulator's site as capable but underused ([regulator-hosted account](https://intic.gov.mz/podemos-falar-da-inteligencia-artificial-sem-data-center/)). The communications minister put the research and education network at 60 Gbps, funded for fifteen years, on 3 August 2026, and the same speaker gave 3.75 Gbps a week earlier; reconcile established on 2026-08-09 that the two measure different things. MoRENet's own account, presented to UbuntuNet Connect 2025 at Harare on 30 October 2025, is that its **international** bandwidth was raised from 5 Gbps to 60 Gbps over the AmLight-SACS South Atlantic link, with full utilisation awaiting the national backbone expansion that would carry it to the universities — so the 60 Gbps is transit capacity, not capacity delivered to institutions. What the 3.75 Gbps rising to 10 Gbps measures is not established by anything on file, and the fifteen-year funding term rests on that single announcement and is corroborated nowhere. Of the 29 critical systems the regulator assessed in May 2025, 19 came back in the highest risk band ([risk assessment](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio_NCRA_Maio-2025_assinado.pdf)).

Wallet-operator participation in the instant payment system is mandatory ([launch account](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/central-bank-launches-mozambique-instant-payment-system-metix-watch/)).

Free first issuance of the identity card, in place since November 2023, was [widened to the birth certificate](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/), and the free birth registration campaign targets about 7 million people ([launch account](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-launchs-free-birth-registration/)). The statistics institute is rehearsing the 2027 census, the first to be run entirely in digital form, in a US$430,000 pilot across 177 enumeration areas ([pilot account](https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/mozambique-invests-430000-in-digital-census-pilot-ahead-of-2027-count/)); the operation is costed at EUR 96.2m and was reported US$68m short of the US$110m required in March 2026.

Decree 40/2026, reported on 3 August 2026, mandates automatic information-sharing between the Tax Authority, the Registry of Legal Entities, Social Security, the State Financial Administration and immigration services, and the account of it names no safeguard or oversight ([decree account](https://360mozambique.com/development/government-approves-regulation-allowing-certificates-for-low-risk-activities-to-be-issued-within-two-hours/)). About 140 services are inventoried for the citizen portal ([ministerial account](https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-digitaliza-mais-de-cem-servicos-publicos-com-prioridade-para-passaportes-e-cartas-de-conducao/)). Municipal digitalisation runs under a 2025 memorandum across all 65 municipalities, with no delivery report ([signing account](https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/digitalizacao-moderniza-gestao-municipal-no-pais/)).

Under the bilateral health memorandum the government owns and operates [SIS-RME as the core electronic medical record](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf), its minimum viable version targeted for September 2026, with a health data exchange expected functional by the end of 2026 and a national health data warehouse by the end of 2027.

The [Cyber Security Law](https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244) creates a Cyber Security Fund at articles 67 to 71, financed by a flat 1% of licensed cybersecurity providers' prior-year gross revenue, and the Cybercrime Law repeals six Penal Code articles.

The ground for [voiding provisions of the Telecommunications Traffic Control Regulations on 29 July 2026](https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf) was organic unconstitutionality, so the powers remain available to the Assembly of the Republic by statute, and the government stated on 4 August that it will seek the same end by another route, naming no bill or timetable ([government response](https://mznews.co.mz/chumbo-do-regulamento-de-telecomunicacoes-pelo-cc-deixa-governo-de-boca-aberta-mas-recusa-engolir-sapos/)).

Since 2003 a central bank notice has required data centres to be located in the country, with offshore replicas only by prior approval ([inception report](https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf)). The draft data governance policy proposes an independent council of 11 to 17 members standing above the ministries, alongside a protection authority the law has not created ([zero draft](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf)). The breach reporting platform pilot has no statute behind it ([platform account](https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-intros-data-breach-platform/)).

A Data Sharing Agreement and a Specimen Sharing Agreement were signed the same day as the bilateral health memorandum and annexed to neither instrument, so [their terms cannot be read from the memorandum that penalises failure to fulfil them](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf); the State Department has published neither, and [a suit for their release](https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026.04.02-Complaint.pdf) was unresolved at 2 April 2026.
