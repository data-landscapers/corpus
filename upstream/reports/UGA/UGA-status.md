# Uganda: status report

*Compiled 2026-08-06 from the Data Landscapers source base, from `outputs/reports/UGA/ledger.csv` (108 systems and instruments, 17 of them ***Not held***). Each section opens with its ledger; specific events are covered in the monthly updates. Figures are dated because most are time-varying.*

**Status values.** *Implemented* — in operation or in force. *Piloting* — running with a limited user group or in a controlled environment. *In development* — build or drafting under way, not yet operating. *Planned* — announced or provided for, no build or draft on record. *Discontinued* — closed or superseded. ***Not held*** — the base carries no reliable statement of status; these are the gaps to fill and are listed again at the end.

## Summary of position

<!-- narrative: summary -->
The national identity system is the deepest layer of Ugandan digital public infrastructure and the one with the clearest measurement. Migration of [28,571,893 legacy records](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/uganda-migrates-more-than-28m-records-to-new-national-id-system) completed in February 2026, and the registrar's own review sets renewals at [14.3m of a 15.8m target, 90.5%, against first-time enrolment of 6.4m of 17.2m, 37.3%](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/uganda-migrates-more-than-28m-records-to-new-national-id-system). Of [10,152,559 cards printed, 2,581,457 have been collected](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/35-million-ugandans-registered-in-national-id-NV_227736_062026). The system is efficient for people already on the register.

Data protection moved from a paper position to a demonstrated one. The protection office's five corrective orders against a foreign controller were [confirmed complied with on 17 July 2026](https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/), inside the statutory timelines and without an appeal. Orders against a second foreign controller issued a year earlier have [no compliance record at all](https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf), and the office has issued nothing on the personal-data processing that ran through the January 2026 election.

Payments carry the largest measured volumes in the base: [UGX 326tn of e-money in FY2024/25, agent banking up 76.1%, and debit-card use down 5.1% with internet-banking users down 6.6%](https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6). Growth is in agents and handsets while both screen-based channels contract.

The compiled finance layer holds no domestic state budget for Uganda at all, so every state financing figure in this report is an announcement, a levy remittance or a donor commitment rather than an appropriation. Seventeen of the rows below are ***Not held***.

<!-- narrative: infrastructure -->
Uganda's international capacity position improved through the window without any published change in what the state's own backbone delivers. Two new terrestrial routes entered service — [a Goma-to-Mombasa route through Kampala in February 2026](https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fiber-route) and [a Nairobi–Kampala route in June 2026 with 1 Tbps activated and headroom to 30 Tbps](https://seacom.co.ke/news/seacom-deploys-a-new-high-capacity-nairobikampala-route-to-strengthen-east-africas-digital-backbone) — while the National Backbone Infrastructure's most recent published utilisation figure remains the [under 30% of full capacity Parliament recorded in December 2023](https://www.parliament.go.ug/documents/3743/hansards-2023-december) when approving its fifth phase. Parliament attributed part of that to the absence of grid power at sites the fibre reaches, and nothing later in the base measures either.

Satellite access is licensed on sovereignty terms rather than coverage terms. The [five-year operating licence signed in May 2026](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/starlink-enters-ugandas-telecom-sector-after-NV_233702_052026) requires a national gateway, a physical point of presence, a staffed local office and registration of every in-country device, and follows a period in which [terminals were disabled on regulatory direction](https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/05/starlink-shuts-down-satellite-internet-services-in-uganda-following-regulatory-directive/). A January 2026 ruling that access would run wholesale through licensed local operators and the May direct licence are not reconciled on the compiled page.

Restriction is the other standing infrastructure fact. [Social media and over-the-top messaging remain blocked](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-restores-partial-internet-connectivity-e-NV_226464_032026) with no lifting on record, having been retained after general internet was restored in January 2026.

The security position is stated rather than measured. An updated national information security framework was [issued in July 2026 citing a 60% year-on-year rise in attacks](https://itweb.africa/article/uganda-launches-updated-cybersecurity-framework/LPwQ57lboKAqNgkj), but the framework document is not held, and the most recent police series remains [446 recorded cybercrime cases for 2024 with UGX 72.1bn lost](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/316617/experts-urge-tougher-cybersecurity-as-financial-sector-faces-rising-cyber-threats). Uganda has no national computer emergency response team on the record.

Beyond identity, the exchange layer is where the record thins. The government service bus is [baselined at 37% of agencies integrated, against a 73% target for FY2029/30](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/), and the only other figure the base holds for it — more than 100 agencies and private entities — [rests on a 2024 case study](https://dial.global/research/integrated-national-data-exchange-uganda-case-study) using a different denominator. The two are not reconciled. The national authentication platform has no user or transaction figure at any date.

The registry layer moved on three fronts in 2026: a [centralised digital register for companies and non-governmental organisations](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/centralised-digital-register-to-streamline-bu-NV_233277_052026), [registration of resident foreign nationals](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-kicks-off-first-registration-of-foreigne-NV_234932_062026) of whom about 1.95m of the roughly 2m in scope are refugees or asylum-seekers, and a [birth-registration drive with a published fee schedule](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-intensifies-birth-registration-drive-lau-NV_234727_062026). No birth-registration coverage figure has been published since 2016.

Payments are where the state is legislating hardest. From 1 January 2027 over-the-counter withdrawals are [capped at UGX 50m a day for individuals and UGX 500m a day for businesses](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/bou-sets-cash-withdrawal-limits-for-individua-NV_234854_062026), with interbank cheque limits halved, in a measure whose stated purpose is a cash-lite economy. Around it, private rails are being extended faster than public ones: a [stablecoin settlement corridor across four East African markets](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/16/scrypt-expands-stablecoin-settlement-network-across-four-east-african-markets/), a [tokenised-asset exchange under acquisition](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/08/global-settlement-holdings-to-acquire-majority-stake-in-akiba/), and [insurance sold over a mobile-money channel](https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/airtel-money-uganda-and-britam-partner-to-expand-digital-insurance-and-financial-services/). The national payment switch and the real-time settlement replacement are both recorded as in progress with no confirmed go-live.

The 2024 census final report is not held as a source, so the denominator under every identity-coverage claim in this section reaches the wiki second-hand.

The instrument that most changed position did so by being removed. On 17 March 2026 the Constitutional Court [nullified sections 11, 23 and 26 to 29 of the Computer Misuse Act, together with the Penal Code's criminal-libel provisions](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026), on quorum grounds and for vagueness. Those sections were the state's principal tools for prosecuting online speech.

Enforcement capacity sits unevenly against that. The data-protection office ran a full cycle to confirmed compliance against a foreign platform while the January 2026 electoral processing has produced no decision, complaint entry or public statement on file. The [directives behind the four-day pre-election shutdown were ordered tabled to Parliament on 10 March 2026](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-ordered-to-table-directives-behind-pre-e-NV_229569_062026) and the base holds no record that they were.

Planning instruments now carry numbers a reader can hold the state to: the five-year agency strategy targets [active e-government use rising from 9.2% to 40%, service-bus coverage from 37% to 73% and IT-law compliance from 67% to 81% by FY2029/30](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/). Against that, the government's own review [found gaps and inconsistencies in the data agencies submit through the national monitoring system](https://www.publicservice.go.ug/public-sector-transformation-programme-pstp-secretariat-intensifies-efforts-to-improve-the-quality-and-consistency-of-performance-reporting-across-ministries-departments-and-agencies/), stating no error rate and no remediation date. The measurement pipeline for those targets is the thing the state says is unreliable.

Open contracting is measured and low: the procurement portal publishes [26 of 148 required data elements, 17.6%, and 4.2% of required project-level disclosure paths](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/why-govt-is-moving-to-upgrade-procurement-por-NV_222664_022026). No Ugandan open-government-data policy or portal is held at all.

A bill capping foreign funding at [20,000 currency points, about UGX 400m a year, above which ministerial approval is required](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/bou-governor-atingi-ego-say-sovereignty-bill-NV_232814_052026) was before joint parliamentary committees in April 2026 and was described as recently enacted on 2 August 2026; no assent date, gazette notice or text is held, so the base cannot say whether it is law.

Uganda has no adopted artificial-intelligence instrument. The strategy was described as being in its final stages in April 2025 and was [still in drafting on 30 July 2026](https://cipit.strathmore.edu/east-african-artificial-intelligence-policy-outlook-report/), sixteen months later, with no text, adoption date or gazette record. A [secretariat operates inside the ICT ministry](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/govt-crafts-national-strategy-to-harness-ai-o-NV_229746_062026) with no budget line or mandate document on file. On 2 August 2026 the minister announced a [separate policy to regulate artificial intelligence and social media](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/360765/editors-raise-alarm-over-ai-misuse-journalist-identity-data-protection-gaps); the announcement is the whole of the evidence, and the wiki holds no other record of it.

What exists is compute and curriculum rather than rules. A [national AI research cloud opened at Makerere in March 2026](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/minister-musenero-launches-national-ai-resear-NV_229938_032026), framed as replacing foreign cloud on data-sovereignty grounds and published with no capacity, cost or user figure.

The sector itself is small on the only comparable series the base holds: [four startups raised a disclosed US$5.5m in 2025 against two raising US$2.4m in 2024](https://disruptafrica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/The-African-Tech-Startups-Funding-Report-2025.pdf), on a tracker counting disclosed rounds only. No sector output, employment or contribution-to-GDP figure is held.

The largest external commitment in the section is not commercial. An agreement of 10 December 2025 gives the United States [direct, real-time access to nine national health data systems for seven years against up to US$1.7bn over five years](https://www.propublica.org/article/trump-state-department-africa-uganda-aid-medical-data-privacy). Its text is unpublished, the State Department says it will release documents in September 2027, and it was unreported in Uganda until July 2026.
