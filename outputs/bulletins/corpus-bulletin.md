### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[Stellenbosch legal expert warns against Meta pay-or-consent privacy model](https://www.citizen.co.za/network-news/lnn/article/stellenbosch-legal-expert-warns-against-meta-pay-or-consent-privacy-model/)** — The Citizen (South Africa), 11 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZAF/" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

A Stellenbosch law lecturer argues that South Africa's data-protection, consumer and competition laws are too fragmented to police platforms that make users choose between paying a subscription and surrendering personal data. The study, in a law review, calls for reform. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Public debate and participation in policymaking](#public-debate-and-participation-in-policymaking).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="AGO" markdown="1">

**[Deliberação sobre processos contravencionais](https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1)** — Agência de Protecção de Dados, 11 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/AGO/" title="AGO">Angola</a>

Angola's data protection agency publishes its register of fines: 15 sanctions between 2022 and 2026, from US$75,000 to US$525,000, with banks, telecoms operators, the national airline and the Luanda water utility among those penalised. The largest 2026 fine was for storing personal data in a cloud abroad without authorisation. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Cybersecurity](#cybersecurity).*</span>

</div>

### Regional collaboration

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="XAF" markdown="1">

**[AFRINIC’s proposed policy petition is a Board-absence mechanism](https://btw.media/en/governance/rir-watchdog/afrinic/story/afrinic-policy-petition-board-absence)** — BTW Media, 11 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/XAF/" title="XAF">Africa</a>

An analysis of the second draft of AFRINIC's policy-process guidelines finds that its petition route, which lets the working group seek ratification of a policy, opens only when no operative Board exists. It needs 15 supporters from three subregions and still depends on a change to the Bylaws. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Public debate and participation in policymaking](#public-debate-and-participation-in-policymaking).*</span>

It is reached through the e-Houwiya digital identity and covers civil-status records, driving licence and vehicle data, traffic fines, inspection bookings, health-insurance and social-security services and e-payments. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov).*</span>
