## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

**[DATA DRIVE! UBOS unveils week-long Africa Statistics Day programme, puts water crisis in spotlight](https://redpepper.co.ug/data-drive-ubos-unveils-week-long-africa-statistics-day-programme-puts-water-crisis-in-spotlight/152738/)** — Red Pepper, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/UGA/UGA-monthly.html" title="UGA">Uganda</a>

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics announced a programme for 12 to 19 November 2026 to mark Africa Statistics Day on 18 November. The theme is water and sanitation statistics. The 2024 census found 81.1% of Ugandans had safely managed drinking water and 43.1% had safely managed sanitation. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [National statistics](#national-statistics).*</span>

</div>

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Internet Service Provider Data](https://www.ncc.gov.ng/market-data-reports/internet-service-provider-data)** — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's communications regulator lists subscribers for each internet service provider. For the second quarter of 2026, Spectranet shows 111,384 and Starlink 98,642. The page also carries third quarter 2026 fibre figures, led by MTN with 242,916. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [National statistics](#national-statistics).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Ground Segment](https://ncc.gov.ng/ground-segment)** — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's communications regulator lists the licensed ground segment operators for satellite services. Starlink Internet Services Nigeria holds 19 gateway earth station licences, each effective from July 2022. Capacity from OneWeb, Yahsat, Avanti and Rascom is also listed. The latest entry is effective 2 September 2026.

</div>
