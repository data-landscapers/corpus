<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MUS" markdown="1">

**[Campagne nationale : Pas de censure des réseaux sociaux, jure l’État, qui revoit la loi](https://lexpress.mu/node/564465)** — L'Express (Maurice), 7 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/MUS/MUS-monthly.html" title="MUS">Mauritius</a>

Mauritius's ICT ministry and regulator ICTA launched a three-month campaign on social-media use on 6 November 2026, L'Express reported. Amendments to the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 have gone to the State Law Office, the ministry said. New regulations are being prepared under the ICT Act. CERT-MU recorded 5,754 incident reports between 1 January and 5 November 2026. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Cybersecurity](#cybersecurity).*</span>

### Registries

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="BEN" markdown="1">

**[TERRA BÉNIN et PPMEC II pour accélérer la sécurisation foncière](https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/)** — Fraternite, 7 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/BEN/BEN-monthly.html" title="BEN">Benin</a>

Benin's land agency ANDF launched the TERRA BÉNIN and PPMEC II programmes in Cotonou on 6 November 2026, Fraternité reported. They extend the national cadastre, which holds 643,993 registered parcels in 12 communes. TERRA BÉNIN's first phase (2025-2030) has USD 100 million from the World Bank and targets 1.5 million parcels in 14 communes. PPMEC II (2026-2031) has EUR 20 million from the Netherlands. It covers six more communes and the e-Foncier Bénin platform. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Geospatial data](#geospatial-data).*</span>

</div>

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="GHA" markdown="1">

**[Travelling to Ghana? New mandatory health declaration rule takes effect next week](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/travelling-to-ghana-new-mandatory-health-declaration-rule-takes-effect-next-week.html)** — Graphic Online, 7 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/GHA/GHA-monthly.html" title="GHA">Ghana</a>

Ghana will require every arriving traveller to file an online Mandatory Health Declaration Form from 12 November 2026, Graphic Online reported on 7 November. Travellers must complete it on the Ghana Health Service portal before embarking, for public-health surveillance. The announcement says nothing on how the data are kept, shared or protected.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[MK party wants eThekwini votes quarantined over KZN IEC 'system failures'](https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/elections/mk-party-wants-ethekwini-votes-quarantined-over-kzn-iec-system-failures/)** — The Citizen, 7 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZAF/ZAF-monthly.html" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

South Africa's MK Party lodged a formal objection with the Electoral Commission on 6 November 2026, The Citizen reported. It alleges a failure in live results capture at the eThekwini Results Operations Centre. Leaderboard figures, it says, differ from party-signed results slips. The party wants affected voting districts quarantined and an independent forensic audit of the results backend.

</div>
