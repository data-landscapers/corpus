## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

**[DATA DRIVE! UBOS unveils week-long Africa Statistics Day programme, puts water crisis in spotlight](https://redpepper.co.ug/data-drive-ubos-unveils-week-long-africa-statistics-day-programme-puts-water-crisis-in-spotlight/152738/)** — Red Pepper, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/UGA/UGA-monthly.html" title="UGA">Uganda</a>

The Uganda Bureau of Statistics announced a programme for 12 to 19 November 2026 to mark Africa Statistics Day on 18 November. The theme is water and sanitation statistics. The 2024 census found 81.1% of Ugandans had safely managed drinking water and 43.1% had safely managed sanitation. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [National statistics](#national-statistics).*</span>

</div>

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Internet Service Provider Data](https://www.ncc.gov.ng/market-data-reports/internet-service-provider-data)** — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's communications regulator lists subscribers for each internet service provider. For the second quarter of 2026, Spectranet shows 111,384 and Starlink 98,642. The page also carries third quarter 2026 fibre figures, led by MTN with 242,916. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [National statistics](#national-statistics).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Ground Segment](https://ncc.gov.ng/ground-segment)** — Nigerian Communications Commission, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's communications regulator lists the licensed ground segment operators for satellite services. Starlink Internet Services Nigeria holds 19 gateway earth station licences, each effective from July 2022. Capacity from OneWeb, Yahsat, Avanti and Rascom is also listed. The latest entry is effective 2 September 2026.

</div>

## DPI

### Registries

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="BFA" markdown="1">

**[CEFORE : Préparer votre création d'entreprise](https://creerentreprise.me.bf/)** — Maison de l'Entreprise du Burkina Faso (CEFORE), 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/BFA/BFA-monthly.html" title="BFA">Burkina Faso</a>

Burkina Faso's business registration centre CEFORE runs an online guide to starting a business. It asks founders to check that a trade name is available and then prepare their documents. It sets out the choice between a sole proprietorship and a company such as a SARL, SA or SAS.

</div>

### Sectoral management information systems

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="SOM" markdown="1">

**[Feisal Mohamed Hashi: The Vision Behind Somalia's Revenue Reforms](https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Nov/206761/feisal_mohamed_hashi_the_vision_behind_somalias_revenue_reforms.aspx)** — Hiiraan Online, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/SOM/SOM-monthly.html" title="SOM">Somalia</a>

Somalia's Council of Ministers appointed Feisal Mohamed Hashi on 12 November 2026 as Director General of the new Somali Revenue Authority, Hiiraan Online reported. He had been Director General of Revenue at the Ministry of Finance since May 2023. The article is a profile of his career.

</div>

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="COD" markdown="1">

**[i-impôts DGI RDC : déclarer et payer ses impôts en ligne (guide 2026)](https://lepoint.cd/i-impots-dgi-rdc-declarer-payer-impots-en-ligne/)** — Le Point (lepoint.cd), 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/COD/COD-monthly.html" title="COD">DRC</a>

Le Point (lepoint.cd) published a guide on 13 November 2026 to i-impôts, the DR Congo's online tax filing platform. Companies use it to file and pay VAT, payroll tax and corporate tax. On 11 November its sign-up page for individuals was still under development.

</div>

## Technology

### Innovation ecosystem

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

**[Govt to open ICT innovation hub at Mountains of the Moon University](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-to-open-ict-innovation-hub-at-mountains-NV_243990_112026)** — New Vision, 13 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/UGA/UGA-monthly.html" title="UGA">Uganda</a>

Uganda's Ministry of ICT announced on 10 November 2026 a new ICT innovation hub at Mountains of the Moon University in Fort Portal. It joins regional hubs at Soroti, Muni, Kabale and Busitema universities. At the same event, four startups from the UJ-Connect programme shared grants totalling US$6,500.

</div>
