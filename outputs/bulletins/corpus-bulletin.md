## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MRT" markdown="1">

**[Stratégie Nationale de Paiement Numérique (SNPN)](https://fintech.bcm.mr/fr/ressources/SNPN/)** — Banque Centrale de Mauritanie, 6 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/MRT/" title="MRT">Mauritania</a>

Mauritania's central bank publishes its National Digital Payment Strategy on its fintech portal, presented as a natural extension of the national financial inclusion strategy and aimed at widening access to financial services for rural and under-banked citizens. It sets five objectives: easing the regulation of payment and electronic-money institutions to attract fintech investment, rebuilding the digital payments infrastructure, modernising financial services around innovation, building adoption and use, and putting a governance system in place across the whole financial-digital sector. No implementation report or measured outturn against any of them appears on the page. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Payments and Fintech](#digital-payments-and-fintech).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="CIV" markdown="1">

**[Transformation numérique : Les travaux retiennent 59 services prioritaires et dix « quick wins » attendus pour la fin 2026](https://afriksoir.net/transformation-numerique-les-travaux-retiennent-59-services-prioritaires-et-dix-quick-wins-attendus-pour-la-fin-2026/)** — AFRIK SOIR, 5 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/CIV/" title="CIV">Côte d'Ivoire</a>

A national prioritisation workshop closed having examined 140 of 620 inventoried public services through nine working groups, retaining 59 for immediate priority and expecting ten quick wins by the end of 2026 - among them diploma authentication, digital proof of life for pensioners and the computerised patient record. The list itself is not published. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov), [Data Exchange](#data-exchange) and [Digital Identity and CRVS](#digital-identity-and-crvs).*</span>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[NITDA Advocates Risk-Based Approach To Nigeria's Digital Sovereignty](https://www.tvcnews.tv/nitda-advocates-risk-based-approach-to-nigerias-digital-sovereignty/)** — TVC News, 5 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/NGA/" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's technology development agency called for a risk-based approach to digital sovereignty, arguing the concept should rest on the classification and risk profile of data rather than on where data is stored or who controls it. The position was set out on a panel at a hyperscaler conference in Lagos. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Data Storage](#data-storage) and [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[NIMASA Embraces Digitisation to End Operational Delays](https://von.gov.ng/nimasa-embraces-digitisation-to-end-operational-delays/)** — Voice of Nigeria, 5 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/NGA/" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Nigeria's maritime administration said it is moving towards full automation of the national ship registry so operators can register and transact remotely, including payment, without attending its offices, framing it as part of reforms to remove human contact and block revenue leakages. No date, cost or vendor is stated. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov).*</span>
