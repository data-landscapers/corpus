## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF XAF" markdown="1">

**[Remarks by Deputy Minister Thandi Moraka on the occasion of the panel discussion “Governing the Digital Commons: Africa’s Agency in Shaping AI for Peace, Security and Development”, during the Sixth Aswan Forum, Aswan, Egypt, 22 November 2026](https://dirco.gov.za/remarks-by-deputy-minister-thandi-moraka-on-the-occasion-of-the-panel-discussion-governing-the-digital-commons-africas-agency-in-shaping-ai-for-peace-security-and-development/)** — Department of International Relations and Cooperation, South Africa, 22 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZAF/ZAF-monthly.html" title="ZAF">South Africa</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XAF/XAF-monthly.html" title="XAF">Africa</a>

South Africa's Deputy Minister of International Relations, Thandi Moraka, spoke on AI governance at the Sixth Aswan Forum in Egypt on 22 November 2026. She set out five principles for Africa's digital commons. They are sovereignty with openness, equity and inclusion, ethics and accountability, security and resilience, and African relevance. She cited South Africa's 2024 AI policy framework and the African Union's AI strategy. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai) and [Digital sovereignty](#digital-sovereignty).*</span>

</div>

### Legislation and regulation

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MAR" markdown="1">

**[Morocco's personal data protection law revision reaches government secretariat](https://en.hespress.com/149668-moroccos-personal-data-protection-law-revision-reaches-government-secretariat.html)** — Hespress, 22 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/MAR/MAR-monthly.html" title="MAR">Morocco</a>

Hespress reported on 22 November 2026 that a draft bill amending Morocco's personal data protection law, Law 09-08, has reached the General Secretariat of the Government. The national data protection commission prepared it with the Ministry of Justice. It covers artificial intelligence, blockchain and precision medicine. The report rests on an unnamed source. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Data protection](#data-protection).*</span>

</div>

### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[SERAP sues NDPC, INEC over alleged AI manipulation of voters' register](https://serap-nigeria.org/2026/11/22/serap-sues-ndpc-inec-over-alleged-ai-manipulation-of-voters-register/)** — Socio-Economic Rights and Accountability Project (SERAP), 22 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

SERAP has sued Nigeria's Data Protection Commission and Independent National Electoral Commission at the Federal High Court in Abuja. It wants the data commission ordered to investigate suspected AI-generated photographs in the preliminary voters' register. It also asks for a decision on whether the Data Protection Act 2023 was breached. The electoral commission had said its biometric system flagged 268 records. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Registries](#registries).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Nigeria advocates risk-based approach to child online safety and AI governance at Global Privacy Assembly](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8434/nigeria-risk-based-approach-child-online-safety-ai-governance-global-privacy-assembly)** — Tech Review Africa, 22 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

Vincent Olatunji, National Commissioner of Nigeria's Data Protection Commission, spoke at two sessions of the Global Privacy Assembly in Brussels. He argued for a risk-based approach to age-assurance tools and close scrutiny of third-party providers. He said Nigeria is considering options on child online safety that include restriction, prohibition and other regulation. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>

</div>

### Regional collaboration

The Governor of the Bank of Ghana, Johnson Asiama, said the bank is developing a regulatory framework for fintech companies. He spoke at the Ghana Investment Promotion Authority's Investment Summit in Accra. He listed fintech innovation hubs, an open banking framework and guidelines for digital credit providers. He said the bank is working on a digital services recognition agreement with seven African countries and on fintech passporting with Rwanda. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Payments and Fintech](#digital-payments-and-fintech) and [Data Exchange](#data-exchange).*</span>
