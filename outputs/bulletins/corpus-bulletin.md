### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[SA Home Loans hit by second data breach](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-home-loans-hit-by-second-data-breach/j5alrvQAXBzvpYQk)** — ITWeb South Africa, 26 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZAF/ZAF-monthly.html" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

SA Home Loans detected unauthorised access to some of its systems, including a collections system, on 13 November 2026. Customers' names, bank account numbers, branch codes and some payment details may have been exposed. <div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

**[Eight government institutions fail data protection audit](https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/eight-government-institutions-fail-data-prote-NV_244898_112026)** — New Vision, 26 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/UGA/UGA-monthly.html" title="UGA">Uganda</a>

Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office audited 15 public bodies between November 2025 and April 2026. Two were compliant, five partly compliant and eight non-compliant. The average rating was 63.71%. The results came at the launch of an audit manual built with World Bank support.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

**[Eight Government Agencies Fail Data Protection Audit](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/390990/eight-government-agencies-fail-data-protection-audit)** — Nile Post, 26 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/UGA/UGA-monthly.html" title="UGA">Uganda</a>

An audit of 15 Ugandan ministries, departments and agencies, run from November 2025 to April 2026, found eight non-compliant, five partially compliant and two compliant. The average score was 63.71%, which the regulator classed as unsatisfactory. Weaknesses included breach and complaint handling, data subject rights, cross-border transfers and impact assessments. The Personal Data Protection Office released the findings on 26 November 2026 as it launched an audit and inspection manual.
