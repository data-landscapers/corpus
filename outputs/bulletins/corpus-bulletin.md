## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ETH" markdown="1">

**[High Digital Transaction Costs, Fraud Gaps Threaten Ethiopia’s Financial Inclusion Targets: Report](https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/53174/)** — The Reporter, 3 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ETH/" title="ETH">Ethiopia</a>

An assessment of Ethiopia's second financial inclusion strategy found adult account ownership at 66 per cent in 2025 against a 70 per cent target, with some banks charging up to 11 per cent for transfers to mobile wallets. Only 29.56 per cent of 157.6 million mobile money accounts were active in June 2026; a third strategy, for 2026 to 2030, was launched alongside it. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Payments and Fintech](#digital-payments-and-fintech), [Access to services](#access-to-services) and [Cybersecurity](#cybersecurity).*</span>

</div>

### Legislation and regulation

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="COD" markdown="1">

**[RDC : FRPC sensibilise autour de la promotion du droit d'accès à l'information publique](https://actualite.cd/2026/10/03/rdc-frpc-sensibilise-autour-de-la-promotion-du-droit-dacces-linformation-publique)** — ACTUALITE.CD, 3 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/COD/" title="COD">DRC</a>

A federation of community radio stations held a press meeting in the Democratic Republic of Congo on the access-to-information bill, declared admissible by the National Assembly in March. The government spokesman said finishing the law is among the government's priorities; no date for a vote was given. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Open data](#open-data).*</span>

### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="RWA XAF" markdown="1">

**[Africa CDC official: Continent lacks infrastructure to run its own health AI](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39420/news/health/africa-cdc-official-continent-lacks-infrastructure-to-run-its-own-health-ai)** — The New Times, 3 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/RWA/" title="RWA">Rwanda</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/XAF/" title="XAF">Africa</a>

The head of digital health at the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention said most health AI models presented to her are hosted on a foreign cloud, because the continent lacks the infrastructure to run them. The agency is drafting a continental health data governance framework with more than 40 member states, setting who may access health data, for what purpose and for how long. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Regional collaboration](#regional-collaboration), [Standards](#standards), [Sectoral management information systems](#sectoral-management-information-systems), [AI](#ai), [Digital sovereignty](#digital-sovereignty) and [Data Storage](#data-storage).*</span>

## DPI

### Digital Identity and CRVS

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MUS" markdown="1">

**[Permis de conduire numérique : Le compte à rebours est lancé](https://lexpress.mu/node/563226)** — L'Express (Maurice), 3 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/MUS/" title="MUS">Mauritius</a>

Mauritius's digital driving licence takes legal effect on 1 January 2027, after a transition period that began on 29 September. A driver may show either the digital licence on the KOREK app or the original physical licence, and the ICT minister said 500,000 people have registered on MauPass, the account needed to reach it. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov) and [Access to services](#access-to-services).*</span>

### Digital Payments and Fintech

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="COD RWA" markdown="1">

**[RDC : l’AP sollicite l’examen de l’OFAC sur l’accord de Bank of Kigali avec le système chinois CIPS](https://actualite.cd/2026/10/03/rdc-lap-sollicite-lexamen-de-lofac-sur-laccord-de-bank-kigali-avec-le-systeme-chinois)** — ACTUALITE.CD, 3 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/COD/" title="COD">DRC</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/RWA/" title="RWA">Rwanda</a>

A Congolese civic platform has asked the United States Treasury's sanctions office to examine Bank of Kigali's direct participation in China's cross-border renminbi payment system, signed on 8 September. It argues the channel could be used to get round American sanctions on the Rwandan army and on networks trading Congolese minerals. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [China activities](#china-activities) and [US / hyperscaler activities](#us-hyperscaler-activities).*</span>

Madagascar's Mvola has made merchant payments free for customers, with the merchant bearing the fee. The mobile money operator reports close to 4 million users, more than 60,000 cashpoints and more than 50,000 merchants accepting payment. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Access to services](#access-to-services).*</span>
