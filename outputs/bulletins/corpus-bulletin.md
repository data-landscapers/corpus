### Legislation and regulation

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="" markdown="1">

**[US pushes looser approach to AI regulation, while EU pushes new law](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/2/us-pushes-looser-approach-to-ai-regulation-while-eu-pushes-new-law)** — Al Jazeera, 2 September 2026

At a G20 innovation ministerial in Chapel Hill on 1 September, the United States urged other governments to adopt the Carolina Principles, a framework arguing that regulation should not single out particular technologies. The European Commission moved the other way in the same week, sending information requests to more than 30 AI companies worldwide under the AI Act. The two positions are the choice most African governments drafting AI rules will be asked to pick between. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [US / hyperscaler activities](#us-hyperscaler-activities), [EU activities](#eu-activities) and [AI](#ai).*</span>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[ICASA turns to ConCourt to clarify its powers](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/icasa-turns-to-concourt-to-clarify-its-powers/JBwErvn3lyb76Db2)** — ITWeb South Africa, 2 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZAF/" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

ICASA has asked the Constitutional Court to settle whether it may condone a late licence-renewal notice from a class licensee, having lost the question in both the Pretoria High Court and the Supreme Court of Appeal to Open Heaven, a Hammanskraal community radio station that filed two weeks late. The regulator reads section 11(9) of the Electronic Communications Act as granting that power only for individual licences, and section 19(2) as deliberately withholding it for class licences; Open Heaven argues the Act leaves latitude where good cause is shown. The court heard the matter on 27 August and has not ruled. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Connectivity](#connectivity).*</span>
