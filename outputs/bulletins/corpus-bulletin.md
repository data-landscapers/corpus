Uganda's ICT and national guidance minister set digital infrastructure, data and artificial intelligence as instruments of a tenfold growth strategy toward a US$500 billion economy, opening the third annual ICT National Summit in Kampala. She called for investment in digital skills, stronger local innovation and scaled homegrown solutions, and named digital fraud, misinformation and cyber threats as the counterweight. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[South African online shoppers prefer local to Temu, Shein](https://www.connectingafrica.com/ecommerce/south-african-online-shoppers-prefer-local-to-temu-shein)** — Connecting Africa, 4 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZAF/" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

South African cross-border e-commerce growth fell to 7% in 2025 from an annual range of 30 to 50% before 2024, after the November 2024 removal of the small-parcel import concession put parcels under R500 on the standard 45% duty plus 15% VAT. Shein's growth slowed to 11% and Temu recorded an average monthly decline of 42%, while roughly 19 million cross-border platform orders in 2025 still made up 18.6% of South African e-commerce transactions. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [ICT Industry](#ict-industry).*</span>
