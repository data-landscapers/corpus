## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZMB" markdown="1">

**[Zambia unlocks digital earnings for creators](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-unlocks-digital-earnings-for-creators/wbrpOqg2eyLMDLZn)** — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZMB/" title="ZMB">Zambia</a>

Zambia's technology and youth ministries are developing a framework for local content creators to earn from digital platforms, and are talking to Meta, TikTok and Google about the legal and technical arrangements. The President named it a priority when opening the 14th National Assembly, and a national database of creators is being compiled. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [ICT Industry](#ict-industry).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[We regulate cybercriminals, now it’s time to regulate the platforms they use](https://www.businessday.co.za/lifestyle/2026-10-12-we-regulate-cybercriminals-now-its-time-to-regulate-the-platforms-they-use/)** — Business Day, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZAF/" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

Two authors summarise their report for a free-expression campaign, arguing that South Africa should regulate the platforms cybercriminals use by borrowing from the European Digital Services Act: notice-and-action, trusted flaggers, systemic-risk duties and transparency reports. It is opinion; the report itself is not held. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Legislation and regulation](#legislation-and-regulation) and [Cybersecurity](#cybersecurity).*</span>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="UGA" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="SDN" markdown="1">

The prime minister of Sudan's TASIS-backed administration announced plans for a national smart identity card for banking, judicial and administrative transactions in the territory it runs, alongside new vehicle registration, licensing and customs procedures. <div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MOZ" markdown="1">
