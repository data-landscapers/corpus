### Regional collaboration

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA BEN CMR" markdown="1">

**[Customs, Afreximbank plan Nigeria-Benin, Cameroon border upgrade](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/customs-afreximbank-plan-nigeria-benin-cameroon-border-upgrade/amp/)** — Vanguard News, 18 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/BEN/BEN-monthly.html" title="BEN">Benin</a><a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/CMR/CMR-monthly.html" title="CMR">Cameroon</a>

Nigeria Customs Service opened talks with Afreximbank on modernising the Sèmè–Kraké and Mfum–Ekok border corridors with Benin and Cameroon. The plans include a system for exchanging customs declarations between the three countries on a common data model; no cost or loan has been agreed. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Data Exchange](#data-exchange).*</span>
