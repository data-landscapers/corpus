## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="CIV" markdown="1">

**[Côte d’Ivoire : 32 entreprises du numérique sélectionnées pour accélérer leur croissance](https://www.afriksoir.net/cote-divoire-32-entreprises-du-numerique-selectionnees-pour-accelerer-leur-croissance/)** — Afrik Soir, 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/CIV/CIV-monthly.html" title="CIV">Côte d'Ivoire</a>

Côte d'Ivoire has picked 32 digital companies for 24 months of support, Minister of Digital Transition Djibril Ouattara announced on 4 November 2026. They come under the Ivoire Tech Next 15 and Ivoire Tech Scale Up programmes. More than 150 firms applied. The government aims to raise 5 to 10 billion FCFA from banks, funds and donors. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Innovation ecosystem](#innovation-ecosystem).*</span>

Ahmed Darwish, a former Egyptian Minister of State for Administrative Development, said on 8 November 2026 that Egypt needs a law on data disclosure and circulation. He said it should set which information can be obtained from government bodies and how it may be reused. He saw no need for a criminal law specific to artificial intelligence. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Open data](#open-data), [Data protection](#data-protection) and [Public debate and participation in policymaking](#public-debate-and-participation-in-policymaking).*</span>

### Data protection

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="GHA" markdown="1">

**[TraffiTech-GH: Protect motorists' data from criminals – Kumadoe](https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/11/08/traffitech-gh-protect-motorists-data-from-criminals-kumadoe/)** — ChannelOne News, 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/GHA/GHA-monthly.html" title="GHA">Ghana</a>

Security analyst Richard Kumadoe warned on Channel One TV on 7 November 2026 that data from Ghana's TraffiTech-GH traffic cameras could expose motorists to privacy breaches if criminals obtained it. He said the data includes motorists' movements and facial identities, and that it must be protected. The warning is his opinion, not a report of a breach. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Public debate and participation in policymaking](#public-debate-and-participation-in-policymaking).*</span>

## ICT Infrastructure

### Connectivity

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Nigeria’s internet capacity expands as data centre links hit 1Tbps - Businessday NG](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigerias-internet-capacity-expands-as-data-centre-links-hit-1tbps/)** — BusinessDay Nigeria, 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

The link between Digital Realty's Medallion data centre and Equinix in Lagos has reached 1 terabit per second. Medallion's link to Rack Centre runs at 500 gigabits per second. Muhammed Rudman, head of the Internet Exchange Point of Nigeria, gave the figures at its 20th anniversary event. BusinessDay reported them on 8 November 2026.

</div>

### Data Storage

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ETH" markdown="1">

**[Digital Sovereignty Priority for Africa’s Future: Innovation & Technology State Minister](https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9895536)** — Ethiopian News Agency, 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ETH/ETH-monthly.html" title="ETH">Ethiopia</a>

Ethiopia aims to host its national data on its own infrastructure, State Minister Solomon Soka said on 8 November 2026. He put that data at about 600 petabytes by 2030, under the Digital Ethiopia 2030 strategy. He also called for African-owned data centres and cloud. He spoke at the Second African Political Parties Summit in Addis Ababa. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital sovereignty](#digital-sovereignty) and [AI](#ai).*</span>

### Other GovTech and e-Gov

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[Technology streamlines 2026 voting process](https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/technology-streamlines-2026-voting-process)** — SAnews (South African Government), 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/ZAF/ZAF-monthly.html" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

South Africa's Electoral Commission used 56,000 voter management devices in the 2026 Local Government Elections, Chief Electoral Officer Sy Mamabolo said on 8 November 2026. That is 16,000 more than its 2021 fleet. Of the new devices, 8,000 were bought and 8,000 donated by Gauteng. The results API took 14.3 million requests. 1,149 users joined the new Virtual Results Operating Centre.

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="LBR" markdown="1">

**[LRA Commissioner General Highlights Revenue Growth, Accountability and Digital Reforms at ATAF Meetings](https://knewsonline.com/lra-commissionergeneral-highlights-revenue-growth-accountability-and-digital-reforms-at-ataf-meetings)** — Knews Liberia, 8 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/LBR/LBR-monthly.html" title="LBR">Liberia</a>

The Liberia Revenue Authority showed a public revenue dashboard and an AI virtual taxpayer assistant, Fatu, at the African Tax Administration Forum meetings in Cairo, reported on 8 November 2026. The dashboard gives monthly collections by revenue category and tax type. Commissioner General James Dorbor Jallah said collections rose from US$612 million in 2023 to US$848 million in 2025. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>
