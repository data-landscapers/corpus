## Governance

### Legislation and regulation

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[Nigeria’s telecom operators sue federal lawmakers over service-quality probe](https://techcabal.com/2026/11/09/nigerian-telecom-operators-sue-reps/)** — TechCabal, 9 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/NGA/NGA-monthly.html" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

ALTON and four Nigerian operators filed a summons at the Federal High Court in Abuja on 4 November 2026, TechCabal reported. They ask whether the Constitution lets the House of Representatives investigate their network quality and summon their chiefs. The House says each operator holds its own licence and must account for its performance. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Connectivity](#connectivity).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="CIV" markdown="1">

**[Côte d'Ivoire : ce que la nouvelle loi bancaire change pour les fintechs](https://bankfinancemag.com/a-la-une/cote-divoire-ce-que-la-nouvelle-loi-bancaire-change-pour-les-fintechs/)** — Banque Finance Magazine, 9 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/CIV/CIV-monthly.html" title="CIV">Côte d'Ivoire</a>

The economic and finance committee of Côte d'Ivoire's National Assembly unanimously adopted the banking regulation bill on 4 November 2026. The bill transposes the UMOA uniform banking law of 16 June 2023 and places fintechs, payment institutions and electronic-money issuers in one framework with banks. It also admits Islamic finance and adds an early-intervention mechanism. The bill still has to complete its passage before promulgation. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Payments and Fintech](#digital-payments-and-fintech).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="LBR" markdown="1">

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="XWA" markdown="1">

**[ECOWAS Heads of Immigration sign Freetown Declaration to mandate ENBIC acceptance by January 1, 2027](https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-heads-of-immigration-sign-freetown-declaration-to-mandate-enbic-acceptance-by-january-1-2027/)** — ECOWAS Commission, 9 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/reports/XWA/XWA-monthly.html" title="XWA">West Africa</a>

ECOWAS Heads of Immigration adopted the Freetown Declaration on 4 November 2026. Member states commit to accept any valid ECOWAS National Biometric Identity Card at official border crossings from 1 January 2027, whether or not the issuing state has started its own rollout. National rollout plans are due to the Commission by December 2030. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Identity and CRVS](#digital-identity-and-crvs).*</span>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="XGL" markdown="1">

**[AI, satellites, spectrum take centre stage as ITU resets global telecom priorities](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ai-satellites-spectrum-take-centre-stage-as-itu-resets-global-telecom-priorities/)** — BusinessDay NG, 9 November 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/catalogue/#places=XGL" title="XGL">Global</a>
