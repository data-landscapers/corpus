## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZMB" markdown="1">

**[Zambia unlocks digital earnings for creators](https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-unlocks-digital-earnings-for-creators/wbrpOqg2eyLMDLZn)** — ITWeb Africa, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZMB/" title="ZMB">Zambia</a>

Zambia's technology and youth ministries are developing a framework for local content creators to earn from digital platforms, and are talking to Meta, TikTok and Google about the legal and technical arrangements. The President named it a priority when opening the 14th National Assembly, and a national database of creators is being compiled. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [ICT Industry](#ict-industry).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="SDN" markdown="1">

**[TASIS outlines governance plans for 24 million Sudanese](https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/tasis-outlines-governance-plans-for-24-million-sudanese/)** — The Sudan Times, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/SDN/" title="SDN">Sudan</a>

The prime minister of Sudan's TASIS-backed administration announced plans for a national smart identity card for banking, judicial and administrative transactions in the territory it runs, alongside new vehicle registration, licensing and customs procedures. He put that territory at about 90 localities and 24 million people, figures that are not independently verified. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Digital Identity and CRVS](#digital-identity-and-crvs).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MOZ" markdown="1">

**[Mozambique: Government updating national cybersecurity strategy](https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-updating-national-cybersecurity-strategy)** — Club of Mozambique, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/MOZ/" title="MOZ">Mozambique</a>

Mozambique's communications minister said the national cybersecurity strategy for 2026 to 2030 is being updated and will be the main instrument for implementing the Cybersecurity Act. He put cyber incidents recorded in 2025 at about 39,600 and urged operators of critical infrastructure to set up their incident response teams. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Cybersecurity](#cybersecurity).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="MOZ" markdown="1">

**[Mozambique Regulator Pushes Postal Sector Reform to Tackle Informal Deliveries](https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/12/mozambique-regulator-postal-sector-reform-informal-deliveries/)** — Tech Africa News, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/MOZ/" title="MOZ">Mozambique</a>

Mozambique's communications regulator held its first postal operators forum on 9 October and called for the sector to be formalised and modernised as e-commerce grows. The country has 38 licensed postal operators, and the regulator named unlicensed delivery through digital platforms as a problem for oversight and consumer protection. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [ICT Industry](#ict-industry).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZWE" markdown="1">

**[Government gives postal sector 90 days for transformation plan](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-gives-postal-sector-90-days-for-transformation-plan/)** — The Herald, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZWE/" title="ZWE">Zimbabwe</a>

Zimbabwe's ICT minister gave her ministry, the regulator and the postal operator 90 days to produce a costed plan for the postal and courier sector to 2030, with quarterly reporting. She also directed operators to comply with the Cyber and Data Protection Act and required pilots of artificial intelligence and drones to carry a named lead, a timetable and measurable benefits. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Other GovTech and e-Gov](#other-govtech-and-e-gov) and [Access to services](#access-to-services).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="TCD" markdown="1">

**[Chad moves towards adopting its national artificial intelligence strategy | East African Policy Observer](https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/news/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy/)** — East African Policy Observer, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/TCD/" title="TCD">Chad</a>

Chad held a pre-validation workshop on its draft national artificial-intelligence strategy, drafted with the UN Economic Commission for Africa. The draft covers 2027 to 2035 and names agriculture, public administration, security and defence, education and health as priority sectors. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="ZAF" markdown="1">

**[Big Tech or public good? What Cape Town’s data centre battle means for residents](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-10-12-data-centres/)** — Daily Maverick, 12 October 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/ZAF/" title="ZAF">South Africa</a>

One of the organisations appealing against land-use rights for a planned hyperscale data centre in Cape Town argues that the mayor should uphold the appeal because the application did not state the site's water use, emissions or jobs. It writes that the appeal went before the city's advisory panel on 12 October and that the mayor then has 90 days to decide. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [Data Storage](#data-storage) and [Energy](#energy).*</span>
