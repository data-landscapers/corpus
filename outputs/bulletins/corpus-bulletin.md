## Governance

### Strategies, plans and policies

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="XAF" markdown="1">

**[WEBINAR 23-24 SEP 12:00 UTC – African Union Open Stakeholder Consultation on AI Governance and Regulation](https://news.internetsociety.org/webinar-23-24-sep-1200-utc-african-union-open-stakeholder-consultation-on-ai-governance-and-regulation/)** — Internet Society News, 23 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/XAF/" title="XAF">Africa</a>

The African Union Commission is holding an open online consultation on 23 and 24 September on its draft Guidelines on AI Governance and Regulation, which implement the 2024 Continental AI Strategy. Day two covers regulation, security and standards, including the AU Model AI Law; written submissions are accepted until 30 September. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">

**[UNGA 81: NITDA DG advocates inclusive global approach to AI](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/unga-81-nitda-dg-advocates-inclusive-global-approach-to-ai/)** — Vanguard, 23 September 2026 <a class="wip-item-card__status wip-item-card__status--active country-box" href="https://corpus.data-landscapers.io/countries/NGA/" title="NGA">Nigeria</a>

At a UNGA 81 high-level dialogue, NITDA director-general Kashifu Inuwa called for AI that reflects local languages and contexts and gives citizens agency rather than only access. He set out four pillars of Nigeria's AI strategy (Connect, Skill, Compute and Create), including sovereign cloud and AI capability. <span class="bulletin-item__also">*Also under [AI](#ai).*</span>

</div>

<div class="bulletin-item" data-places="SEN" markdown="1">

Senegal's digital ministry validated a draft national Blueprint on Data Valorisation at a workshop on 21 and 22 September, run with Smart Africa's Data Governance in Africa programme and GIZ. <div class="bulletin-item" data-places="NGA" markdown="1">
