# Sudan

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Sudan's standards body is the Sudanese Standards and Metrology Organization, which [publishes an online catalogue of adopted Sudanese Standards](<https://www.ssmo.gov.sd/Content/SectorDetails/783020af-a1ce-46f5-aaa1-525dd82ca005>), each with its number, its year and its international equivalent. The catalogue lists [a 2019 standard on the digital repository and a 2016 standard on an application protocol for open systems interconnection](<https://www.ssmo.gov.sd/Content/SectorDetails/783020af-a1ce-46f5-aaa1-525dd82ca005>), [a 2015 standard on managing metadata for records and a 2020 standard on the messages financial cards exchange](<https://www.ssmo.gov.sd/Content/SectorDetails/783020af-a1ce-46f5-aaa1-525dd82ca005>), and [a 2018 information technology standard on the yield of printer toner cartridges](<https://www.ssmo.gov.sd/Content/SectorDetails/783020af-a1ce-46f5-aaa1-525dd82ca005>).

**Certification.** In July 2026 the organisation was [piloting electronic payment of its standards certification fees](<https://sudanhorizon.com/ssmo-integrates-eisali-payment-service-with-baladna-platform/>) in Khartoum. The report names no certification against a standard on information technology or data, and does not say what law set the organisation up.
