# Guinea

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Guinea's standards body is the Institut Guinéen de Normalisation et de Métrologie. [A presidential decree read on national television on 7 January 2023](<https://guineenews.org/2023/01/07/decret-les-attributions-lorganisation-de-institut-guineen-de-normalisation-et-de-metrologie-fixes/>) fixes its attributions, organisation and operation as a public administrative body under the industry and finance ministries. The decree charges it with drafting national standards, or adopting foreign or international ones, and with publishing them. The report of the decree names no standard on information technology or data that the institute has issued.

**Certification.** The [decree of January 2023](<https://guineenews.org/2023/01/07/decret-les-attributions-lorganisation-de-institut-guineen-de-normalisation-et-de-metrologie-fixes/>) is the only text held on the institute.
