# Algeria

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Algeria's standards body is the Algerian Institute of Standardisation, known as IANOR. It was [created by executive decree 98-69 of 21 February 1998](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-D_exe_98-69_instituant-IANOR-FR.pdf>) as a public establishment under the industry ministry, charged with drawing up and publishing Algerian standards. [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1499.html>), read in November 2026, describes it as Algeria's national standards body. Its [draft national standardisation programme for 2026](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>), published in February 2026, lists the revision of an existing national standard, NA 13037. The revision brings it up to the 2025 edition of the international standard on comparing and ordering character strings in information technology.

**Certification.** The [1998 decree](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-D_exe_98-69_instituant-IANOR-FR.pdf>) also charges the institute with adopting conformity marks and authorising their use. Nothing in it, or in the draft programme for 2026, names certification against a standard on information technology or data, or an organisation certified against one.
