# Algeria

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Algeria's standards body is the Algerian Institute of Standardisation, [created by executive decree 98-69 of 21 February 1998](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-D_exe_98-69_instituant-IANOR-FR.pdf>) as a public industrial and commercial establishment under the industry ministry, charged with drawing up, publishing and disseminating Algerian standards. Its [draft national standardisation programme for 2026](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PROJET-DE-PROGRAMME-NATIONAL-DE-NORMALISATION-2026.pdf>), published in February 2026, lists the revision of an existing national standard, NA 13037, against the 2025 edition of the international standard on comparing and ordering character strings in information technology.

**Certification.** The [1998 decree](<https://www.ianor.dz/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/2-D_exe_98-69_instituant-IANOR-FR.pdf>) also charges the institute with adopting conformity marks and authorising their use. Nothing in it, or in the draft programme for 2026, names certification against a standard on information technology or data, or an organisation certified against one.
