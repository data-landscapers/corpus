# Mali

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Mali's standards body is the Agence Malienne de Normalisation et de Promotion de la Qualité, [created by an ordinance of 19 March 2012](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mli152386.pdf>) as a public administrative body with legal personality and financial autonomy, charged with implementing national policy on standardisation and quality promotion. The international standards organisation [records it as Mali's national standards body and a full member](<https://www.iso.org/fr/member/1915.html>). Its board, meeting on 29 January 2026, [adopted a 2026 budget of 243.3 million CFA francs](<https://leronier.ml/2026/02/02/14eme-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lamanorm-le-projet-de-budget-2026-est-equilibre-en-recettes-et-en-depenses-a-la-somme-de-243-306-303-francs-cfa-contre-318-369-036-francs-c/>), against 318.4 million in 2025. None of the three names a national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [2012 ordinance](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mli152386.pdf>) charges the agency with creating and managing the national conformity mark. It names no certification against a standard on information technology or data.
