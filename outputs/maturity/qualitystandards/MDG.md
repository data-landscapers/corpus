# Madagascar

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Madagascar's standards body is the Bureau des Normes de Madagascar, which [was created by decree 98-944 of 4 November 1998](<https://www.bnm.mg/description-et-activites/>) and is now governed by decree 2014-1570 of 30 September 2014. A United Nations Development Programme assessment published in December 2025 [describes it as the national standards body and a correspondent member of the international standards organisation](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-12/dra_final_report_madagascar_vf_0.pdf>), and says public information shows no specific programme on standards for artificial intelligence or data governance. Neither source names a national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The bureau's [own description of its activities](<https://www.bnm.mg/description-et-activites/>) is the nearest text held. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified.
