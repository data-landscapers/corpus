# Gabon

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Gabon's national standardization body is the Gabonese Standardization Agency (Agence Gabonaise de Normalisation), AGANOR, [created by decree 0227/PR/MIMT of 23 June 2014](<https://aganor.ga/aganor/a-propos-de-laganor/>), with power to draw up, approve and publish Gabonese standards. The international standards organisation's [member directory](<https://www.iso.org/member/548909.html>) lists the agency. The decree makes it the country's national body for standardization, and its standards are published as Gabonese standards. Neither page names a standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** AGANOR's [own page](<https://aganor.ga/aganor/a-propos-de-laganor/>) lists certification among the agency's services, and names no standard on information technology or data that an organisation could be certified against. The agency's page gives no certified organisation by name, and the one information security certification held is [ST DIGITAL's ISO 27001 certification, announced on 3 March 2026](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>).

***Also on file***. [ISO 27001 certification of ST DIGITAL's information security management system](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>).

***Not found***. No source found names a national standard on information technology or data.
