# Djibouti

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Djibouti's standards body is the Agence Djiboutienne des Normes et de la Qualité, [created by a law of 7 January 2021](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n100-an-20-8eme-l-portantes-creation-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite/>) as a public industrial and commercial body attached to the commerce ministry and named the national reference body for drawing up standards. [A decree of 4 May 2022](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2022-092-pr-mct-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite/>) organises it. The international standards organisation's [member record](<https://www.iso.org/member/10466380.html>) lists it as the national standards body, put into operation in 2022. The Council of Ministers of 13 January 2026 [examined its 2026 budget of 134.9 million Djibouti francs](<https://www.presidence.dj/conseil-des-ministres/2026-01-13>), up from 112.1 million in 2025, and recorded that in 2025 it made two technical committees operational, for agri-food products and for electrical and household appliances. No standard on information technology or data is named.

**Certification.** The [2022 decree](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2022-092-pr-mct-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lagence-djiboutienne-des-normes-et-de-la-qualite/>) charges the agency with certifying conformity to standards and accrediting the bodies that assess it. It names no scheme for information technology or data.
