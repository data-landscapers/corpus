# South Sudan

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** South Sudan's standards body is the National Bureau of Standards, [established by the National Bureau of Standards Act, 2012](<https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Bureau-of-Standards-Act-2012.pdf>) as a body corporate with the function of formulating, adopting and endorsing standard specifications as South Sudan standards. The text of the Act held names no standard on information technology, software or data. The bureau was operating in May 2026, when its technical manager [spoke for it at a World Metrology Day event on 20 May](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/05/21/ssnbs-plans-to-tackle-internet-data-bundle-billing-complaints/>) about complaints over the billing of internet data bundles.

**Certification.** The [2012 Act](<https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Bureau-of-Standards-Act-2012.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified.
