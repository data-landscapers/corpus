# South Sudan

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** South Sudan's standards body is the National Bureau of Standards. The [National Bureau of Standards Act, 2012](<https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Bureau-of-Standards-Act-2012.pdf>) set it up as a body corporate. Its function is to formulate, adopt and endorse standard specifications as South Sudan standards. The text of the Act names no standard on information technology, software or data. The bureau was operating in May 2026, when its technical manager [spoke for it at a World Metrology Day event on 20 May](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/05/21/ssnbs-plans-to-tackle-internet-data-bundle-billing-complaints/>). He spoke about complaints over the billing of internet data bundles. In November 2026 the [government's eServices portal described the bureau](<https://accounts.eservices.gov.ss/mdas>) as the national body for the quality, safety and standardisation of goods and services.

**Certification.** The [2012 Act](<https://mojca.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/National-Bureau-of-Standards-Act-2012.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified.
