# Lesotho

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Lesotho's standards body is the Lesotho Standards Institution. The trade and industry minister [said on 16 September 2020](<http://www.trade.gov.ls/news/official-address-by-the-minister-of-trade-and-industry-honourable-dr-thabiso-molapo-at-the-operationalisation-of-the-lesotho-standards-institution-lsi/>) that the institution, created by an Act published in 2014, was now operational as the country's national standards body, with a mandate to develop and publish national standards. The minister's address is the only source found on the institution, and it names no national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [minister's address of 2020](<http://www.trade.gov.ls/news/official-address-by-the-minister-of-trade-and-industry-honourable-dr-thabiso-molapo-at-the-operationalisation-of-the-lesotho-standards-institution-lsi/>) sets out the institution's mandate to develop and publish standards. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the institution has certified.
