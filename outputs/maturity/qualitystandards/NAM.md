# Namibia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Namibian Standards Institution [is established by the Standards Act, 2005](<https://www.nsi.com.na/download/standards-act-18-of-2005/>), signed on 23 December 2005, as the national institution for the promotion, issue and maintenance of standards, under a Namibian Standards Council. Its chief executive [said in October 2025](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/10/namibia-lags-behind-region-with-235-standards-plans-to-develop-1200/>) that it had published 235 standards since it was set up in 2008, that the national stock is 275, and that a national standardisation strategy targets 1,267 within three years. A notice of 15 June 2026 [announced four new Namibian Standards](<https://nsi.com.na/officially-developed-namibian-standards-nams-food-products-and-food-safety-management/>), on chilled seafood, dates, laundry processes and the energy performance of refrigerating appliances. ISO's member page [lists the institution as Namibia's national standards body](<https://www.iso.org/member/1569.html>). No standard on information technology or data is named.

**Certification.** The [Standards Act](<https://www.nsi.com.na/download/standards-act-18-of-2005/>) is the nearest text held, and it names no certification against a standard on information technology or data.
