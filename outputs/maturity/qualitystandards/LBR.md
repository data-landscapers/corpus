# Liberia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Liberia's standards body is the Liberia Standards Authority. [An Act to amend the Executive Law and establish the authority](<https://www.moci.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/An%20Act%20To%20Amend%20The%20Executive%20Law%20Of%20Liberia%20And%20To%20Establish%20The%20Liberia%20Standards%20Authority.pdf>) was published by the commerce ministry on 5 August 2022. Its preamble cites West African quality infrastructure and the World Trade Organization's agreement on technical barriers to trade. [ISO's member page says the authority began work in 2024](<https://www.iso.org/member/11464839.html>) as the national body that develops and maintains Liberian standards. Neither source names a national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/11464839.html>) and [the Act](<https://www.moci.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/An%20Act%20To%20Amend%20The%20Executive%20Law%20Of%20Liberia%20And%20To%20Establish%20The%20Liberia%20Standards%20Authority.pdf>) name no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data.
