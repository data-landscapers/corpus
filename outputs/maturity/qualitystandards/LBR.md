# Liberia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Liberia's standards body is the Liberia Standards Authority. [An Act to amend the Executive Law and establish the authority](<https://www.moci.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/An%20Act%20To%20Amend%20The%20Executive%20Law%20Of%20Liberia%20And%20To%20Establish%20The%20Liberia%20Standards%20Authority.pdf>) was published by the commerce ministry on 5 August 2022. Its preamble cites the quality infrastructure of the Economic Community of West African States and the country's obligations under the World Trade Organization's agreement on technical barriers to trade. Only the preamble of the Act is held, and it names no national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [Act's preamble](<https://www.moci.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/An%20Act%20To%20Amend%20The%20Executive%20Law%20Of%20Liberia%20And%20To%20Establish%20The%20Liberia%20Standards%20Authority.pdf>) is the only text held on the authority. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the authority has certified.
