# Benin

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Benin's standards body is the Agence nationale de Normalisation, de Métrologie et du Contrôle Qualité. The international standards organisation's member record states that it [was established by Order 2017-031 of 23 January 2017](<https://www.iso.org/member/308845.html>) as a public institution under the Ministry of Industry, with the mandate to manage the national standardisation system. The record names no national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [same record](<https://www.iso.org/member/308845.html>) gives the agency the mandate to implement the national certification system as well as the standardisation system. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation certified by the agency. The agency's national conformity mark, reported for electrical equipment, is listed below.

***Also on file***. [Marque nationale de conformité NB](<https://anm.bj/fr/actualite/7503>); [ST DIGITAL ISO 27001 certificate](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>).
