# Eswatini

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Eswatini Standards Authority [is established by the Standards and Quality Act](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/swa142025.pdf>), assented to on 8 November 2001, as a body corporate with the power to draft, issue, amend and withdraw national standards. [General Notice No. 79 of 2025](<https://times.co.sz/25189/business/eswatini-issues-19-new-national-standards-across-key-sectors/>), under the Standards and Quality Act, 2023, designated 19 new Eswatini National Standards in August 2025. They include the National Addressing Standard in two parts, SZNS ISO 19160-1:2015 and SZNS ISO 19160-2:2022, which set out the classes of address and how addresses are assigned and maintained.

**Certification.** The [report of the 2025 notice](<https://times.co.sz/25189/business/eswatini-issues-19-new-national-standards-across-key-sectors/>) names no certifier for the addressing standard and no organisation certified against it.
