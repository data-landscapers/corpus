# Nigeria

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Nigeria's standards body is the Standards Organisation of Nigeria, [established by an Enabling Act of 1971 to prepare standards](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>), as the international standards organisation's member page for it states. That page is the one source found on the body's standing, and it dates the body's founding rather than any later work. It names no national standard on information technology or data, and neither does any other source found.

**Certification.** The nearest held fact is the same [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>), which records the Organisation as the body set up by the 1971 Act to prepare standards. It records no certification against a standard on information technology or data, and no source found says that an organisation in Nigeria can be certified against one.
