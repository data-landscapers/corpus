# Nigeria

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Nigeria's standards body is the Standards Organisation of Nigeria, [established by an Enabling Act of 1971 to prepare standards](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>). The international standards organisation's member page for it says so. That page is the one source found on the body's standing. It dates the body's founding and not any later work. It names no national standard on information technology or data, and neither does any other source found.

**Certification.** The nearest fact found is the same [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1982.html>), which records the Organisation as the body set up by the 1971 Act to prepare standards. It records no certification against a standard on information technology or data. No source found says that an organisation in Nigeria can be certified against one.

***Also on file***. These include the [MTN Nigeria ISO 27001:2013 information security management system certificate (ISMS/R234/0006)](<https://www.mtn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ISO-27001-Information-Security-Management-System-Nigeria.pdf>) and the [Device Management System (DMS)](<https://von.gov.ng/ncc-deploys-8526-telecom-sites-vows-to-end-call-masking/>). They also include the [Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR)](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/type-approval-enforcement-why-your-phone-may-stop-working-soon/>) and [NDIAF Assurance Certification](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/NATIONAL-DIGITAL-INFRASTRUCTURE-ASSURANCE-FRAMEWORK-2026_Final.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found names a national standards body, or says there is none.
