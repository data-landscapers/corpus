# South Africa

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** South Africa's standards body is the South African Bureau of Standards, whose board [acts under the Standards Act, 2008](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202604/54520gen3890.pdf>), the law that sets the norm for developing South African National Standards. Notice 3890 of 2026, gazetted on 17 April 2026, [lists SANS 19784-1:2026](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202604/54520gen3890.pdf>), the second edition of the national standard on the programming interface for biometric applications, among the information technology standards revised under section 24 of the Act.

**Certification.** The [gazette notice of April 2026](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202604/54520gen3890.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It lists standards issued, revised and withdrawn. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against the biometric standard or any other standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified.
