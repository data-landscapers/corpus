# Botswana

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Botswana Bureau of Standards was established by [the Standards Act of 1995, which commenced on 1 August 1996](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bot91763.pdf>), as a body corporate with a Standards Council, charged with preparing and publishing Botswana Standards. It publishes [an annual work programme of standards projects](<https://bobstandards.bw/standards-annual-work-programme/>); issue 341, updated on 16 April 2026, covers April 2026 to March 2027. In November 2025 the trade minister [told Parliament](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89185>) that the bureau had developed voluntary national standards on school uniforms. None of the three sources names a standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [Standards Act](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bot91763.pdf>) is the nearest text held, and it names no certification against a standard on information technology or data. Neither does the work programme of April 2026.
