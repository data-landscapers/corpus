# Sierra Leone

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Sierra Leone Standards Bureau states that its standards department [develops and publishes national standards under the Standards Act No. 2 of 1996](<https://slsb.gov.sl/standards-overview/>), and that it has published 204 national standards in the public gazette. The bureau's overview, published in 2023, names a technical committee on information security management systems. It names no national standard on information technology or data that the committee or the bureau has issued.

**Certification.** The [bureau's overview](<https://slsb.gov.sl/standards-overview/>) is the only text held on it. It describes the development and publication of standards, and names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data and no organisation the bureau has certified. It gives no date for its count of 204 standards.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a national standard on information technology or data.
