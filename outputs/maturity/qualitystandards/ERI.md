# Eritrea

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Eritrea's standards body is the Eritrean Standards Institution, [established by Proclamation No. 75 of 1995](<https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/>) as an autonomous body with legal personality. The proclamation makes it the sole national organ for standardisation, quality assurance and metrology, and gives it the power to prepare compulsory Eritrean Standards. The record of the proclamation, dated 20 September 1995, is an abstract and the only source held on the institution, and it names no standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [1995 proclamation](<https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/>) covers quality assurance as well as standardisation and measurement. The record held names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the institution has certified against any standard.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a national standard on information technology or data.
