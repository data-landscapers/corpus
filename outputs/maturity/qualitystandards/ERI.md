# Eritrea

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Eritrea's standards body is the Eritrean Standards Institution, [established by Proclamation No. 75 of 1995](<https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/>) as an autonomous body with legal personality. The proclamation makes it the sole national organ for standardisation, quality assurance and metrology, and gives it the power to prepare compulsory Eritrean Standards. The international standards organisation's member record [gives 446 national standards published](<https://www.iso.org/member/318778.html>), and calls the institution the sole standardisation body in the country. The proclamation names no standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [1995 proclamation](<https://www.ecolex.org/details/legislation/proclamation-to-provide-for-the-establishment-of-the-eritrean-standards-institution-no-75-of-1995-lex-faoc203313/>) covers quality assurance as well as standardisation and measurement. The record held names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the institution has certified against any standard.
