# Seychelles

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Seychelles Bureau of Standards [is established by the Seychelles Bureau of Standards Act, 2014](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/syc_e/wtaccsyc52_leg_2.pdf>), assented to on 28 March 2014, as a body corporate with the function of preparing draft standards and declaring them Seychelles Standards by notice in the Gazette. The Act [commenced on 16 June 2014 and was amended in 2016 and 2021](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/2014/2/eng@2014-03-31>). The President [appointed an eight-member board for three years from 1 August 2025](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-09/Gazette%20No%2064%20-%2029th%20September%202025.pdf>). The ISO membership page for the bureau says it [was established as a government agency under the 1987 Act and develops, declares and promotes Seychelles Standards](<https://www.iso.org/member/2084.html>). None of the four sources names a standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [2014 Act](<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/acc_e/syc_e/wtaccsyc52_leg_2.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified against any standard.
