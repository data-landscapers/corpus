# DR Congo

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Office congolais de contrôle (OCC) is the country's national body for metrology, standardisation and certification. [ISO's member page for the office](<https://www.iso.org/member/308894.html>), dated 2026, says it was created by Ordinance No. 74-013 of 1974, so it rests on an act of the state and not on a private initiative. The same page names no national standard on information technology or data that the office has issued or adopted, and says nothing of the content of its catalogue.

**Certification.** [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/308894.html>) gives certification as one of the office's three functions, beside metrology and standardisation. It does not say what the office certifies, names no certification against a standard on information technology or data, and names no public body, ministry or agency that the office has certified. It gives no figure for the organisations that hold its certificates.
