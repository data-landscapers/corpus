# Ethiopia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The national standards body is the Institute of Ethiopian Standards, which the international standards organisation's member page says was [renamed by Proclamation 1263/2021](<https://www.iso.org/member/1725.html>). On 24 April 2026 the Ethiopian Standards Board [officially approved 5 mandatory cybersecurity standards and 31 guidelines and frameworks](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m>), according to the Information Network Security Administration, which drafted them with the Ethiopian Standards Institute. The approval is the most recent dated fact held on the country's standards, and it names the board as the approving body.

**Certification.** The nearest held fact is the approval in April 2026 of [mandatory cybersecurity standards and guidelines](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m>) by the Ethiopian Standards Board. The announcement describes the standards and who drafted them. It names no organisation that has been certified against them and no certifier that offers certification.
