# Ethiopia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The national standards body is the Institute of Ethiopian Standards. The international standards organisation's member page says it was [renamed by Proclamation 1263/2021](<https://www.iso.org/member/1725.html>). On 24 April 2026 the Ethiopian Standards Board [officially approved 5 mandatory cybersecurity standards and 31 guidelines and frameworks](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m>). The Information Network Security Administration, which drafted them with the Ethiopian Standards Institute, reports the approval. [In October 2026 the institute held a database of more than 11,000 standards](<https://addisfortune.news/authorities-bid-for-digital-quality-sparks-fury-among-cash-strapped-businesses>). It is one of four bodies formed from the former quality and standards authority.

**Certification.** The nearest held fact is the approval in April 2026 of [mandatory cybersecurity standards and guidelines](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7453356614822277120-hb2m>) by the Ethiopian Standards Board. The announcement describes the standards and who drafted them. It names no organisation that has been certified against them and no certifier that offers certification.
