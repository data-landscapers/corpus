# Ghana

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Ghana's standards body is the Ghana Standards Authority. [The Ghana Standards Authority Act, 2022](<https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2022/1078>), Act 1078, was assented to and published in the Gazette on 30 June 2022, and was amended by the Ghana Accreditation Service Act, 2023. An industry brief of 2024 [names the authority as the national standards body](<https://gogla.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ghana-Country-Brief.pdf>). On 19 March 2026 it [hosted a steering committee meeting](<https://gsa.gov.gh/2026/03/gsa-hosts-steering-committee-meeting-on-national-metrology-institute-ii-project/>) on the second phase of a metrology institute project with Germany's metrology institute, and on 4 September 2026 the communications minister [said it works with two regulators](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-telecom-masts-pose-no-health-risks-here-to-serve-you-better-sam-george.html>) to see that cell sites meet approved radiation and safety levels. None of these names a standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [2022 Act](<https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2022/1078>) is the nearest text held; only its citation and history are held, and they name no certification scheme.
