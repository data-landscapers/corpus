## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The president had [recently signed a decree setting up the Office comorien de la normalisation et de la métrologie](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/office-national-de-la-normalisation-i-un-projet-de-loi-sur-la-qualit%C3%A9-en-voie-de-validation.html>). The economy minister said so in October 2026. It is the national standards and metrology body. No standard on information technology or data is named.

**Certification.** The [same report](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/office-national-de-la-normalisation-i-un-projet-de-loi-sur-la-qualit%C3%A9-en-voie-de-validation.html>) names no certification scheme for information technology or data.

***Also on file***. [ANRTIC mobile quality-of-service control campaign](<https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/consommation/comores-la-qualite-des-services-des-operateurs-mobiles-epinglee/>).
