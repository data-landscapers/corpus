# Uganda

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Uganda National Bureau of Standards is a statutory body under the trade ministry, [established by an Act of 1983 and operational since 1989](<https://www.unbs.go.ug/attachments/menus/34/UNBS%20Annual%20Report%20FY%202024-2025.pdf>), governed by a National Standards Council that declares national standards. It held 4,862 national standards at the end of June 2025. The ministry [said in August 2026](<https://www.mtic.go.ug/trade-minister-sanjay-tanna-tasks-unbs-to-improve-efficiency-in-service-delivery/>) that the council had reported 551 new standards developed in the year to June 2026. A notice approved on 16 December 2025 [proposes 15 standards for compulsory declaration](<https://www.unbs.go.ug/attachments/alerts/1/DECLARATION%20NOTICE%20DECEMBER%202025%20-%20Copy.pdf>), on cosmetics, paints, toys, chemicals, paper, appliances and cables. None named in the three sources is on information technology or data.
