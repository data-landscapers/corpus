# Mauritania

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Mauritania's standards body is the Office National de Normalisation et de Métrologie, which [Decree 2023-113 of 7 September 2023 says was instituted by a decree of 9 May 2023](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229551.pdf>). The same decree [creates a national council for standardisation and quality promotion](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229551.pdf>), an advisory body under the industry minister, under a 2010 law on standardisation and quality promotion, and gives the office its secretariat. The decree was published in the official journal of 15 October 2023. It names no national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** [Decree 2023-113](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229551.pdf>) also gives the office the secretariat of the accreditation cell it creates, the Mauritanian accreditation committee listed below. It names no body accredited to certify, and no certification against a standard on information technology or data.

***Also on file***. [Comité Mauritanien d'Accréditation](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229551.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names a national standard on information technology or data.
