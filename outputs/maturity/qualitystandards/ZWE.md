# Zimbabwe

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Zimbabwe's standards body is the Standards Association of Zimbabwe. The National Quality Policy 2021–2025 says it [develops national standards but rests on no enabling legislation](<http://www.mic.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NATIONAL-QUALITY-POLICY-FOR-WEB.pdf>), and that the government will legislate for it and rename it. The association [lists ZWS ISO/IEC 27001:2022](<https://www.saz.org.zw/accreditations-2/>), on information security management systems, as a Zimbabwe standard.

**Certification.** The association's certification arm states that its scheme for certifying organisations against the information security standard [was accredited in February 2026](<https://www.saz.org.zw/accreditations-2/>) by the Southern African Development Community's accreditation service, under the international standard for bodies that certify management systems. Its schemes for quality, environmental and occupational health management were accredited earlier. The page names no organisation, public or private, certified against the information security standard.
