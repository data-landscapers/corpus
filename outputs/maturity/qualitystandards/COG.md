# Congo

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Congo's standards body is the Agence Congolaise de Normalisation et de la Qualité, known as ACONOQ. The international standards organisation's [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/11493926.html>) says that Law No. 19-2015 of 29 October 2015 created it as the national standardization body, and that it manages the national standards register. The page names no standard on information technology or data that the agency has issued, and gives no catalogue of national standards or count of them.

**Certification.** The same [member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/11493926.html>) describes an agency that manages the national standards register. It records no certification offered or granted by the agency against a standard on information technology or data, and names no organisation the agency has certified, whether in government or outside it.

***Also on file***. [ISO 27001 certification of ST DIGITAL](<https://st.digital/en/blog/st-digital-s-blog-1/st-digital-526>).

***Not found***. No source found names a national standard on information technology or data.
