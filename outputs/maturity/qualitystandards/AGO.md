# Angola

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Angola's standards body is the National Institute of Quality Infrastructure, [formed by Presidential Decree 95/21 of 20 April 2021](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-95-21-de-20-de-abril/>), which merged the standardisation institute and the accreditation institute. The decree gives it the coordination of national standardisation and the job of developing, publishing and approving Angolan standards. The institute [made its 2026 catalogue available on 9 March 2026](<https://iniq.gov.ao/2026/03/13/%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%90-%f0%9d%90%83%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%92%f0%9d%90%8f%f0%9d%90%8e%f0%9d%90%8d%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%81%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%8b%f0%9d%90%88%f0%9d%90%99/>), with 140 new national standards in the annual update, approved after technical committee work and public consultation. The announcement names no standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The international standards organisation's [member register](<https://www.iso.org/member/316519.html>) records the institute as a public body, under a statute approved in April 2021, that draws up and sells technical standards and holds correspondent membership. It records no certification against a standard on information technology or data.
