# Rwanda

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Rwanda Standards Board [is established by Law 50/2013 of 28 June 2013](<https://www.rsb.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Quick_links/Rwanda_Standards_Board_-_RSB.pdf>), with legal personality and the mission to establish and publish national standards, which the law defines as voluntary. The international standards organisation said in October 2025 that the board [has developed and published more than 4,130 standards](<https://www.iso.org/news/2025/10/iso-arso-kigali-agreement-trade>), covering food, engineering, services, water, chemistry and consumer products. The Official Gazette of 20 November 2025 [carries the board's notice publishing a list of national standards](<https://minijust.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minijust/Official_gazettes_2/_____2025_Igazeti_ya_Leta/_UGUSHYINGO-November-Novembre/OG_n___Special_of_20.11.2025__RSB__Amabwiriza_y_Igihugu_y_ubuziranenge___ASSIMPER.pdf>); the passages held do not show the list. A Korean grant of 8 million dollars for 2024 to 2028 [supports the use of digital tools in the board's work](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/18071/news/featured/rsb-koica-launch-8-million-project-to-boost-technology-in-advancing-rwandas-industrial-standardisation>). No standard on information technology or data is named.

**Certification.** On 6 March 2026 the board [signed mutual recognition arrangements](<https://www.rsb.gov.rw/updates/news-detail/rsb-signed-mutual-recognition-arrangements-with-aconoq-and-saz-to-promote-trade-as-arso-joined-to-the-certification-of-local-industries-under-the-acap-programme>) with the standards bodies of Congo and Zimbabwe. No certification against a standard on information technology or data is named.
