# Mozambique

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Mozambique's standards body is the Instituto Nacional de Normalização e Qualidade, which the [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1978.html>) says was established on 24 March 1993 as the central body coordinating standardisation and quality nationally. [Decree 8/2022 of 14 March 2022](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz211531.pdf>) approves the regulation on standardisation and conformity assessment and gives the institute the job of coordinating standardisation and implementing Mozambican technical standards. On 7 May 2026 the economy ministry and the institute [announced that a certificate of conformity becomes mandatory from 1 June 2026](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/a-importacao-de-arroz-alimentos-veiculos-usados-e-materiais-de-construcao-tera-novas-regras-no-pais/>) for listed imports, among them rice, fortified foods, used vehicles, tyres and construction steel. Neither names a standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [2022 decree](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz211531.pdf>) also charges the institute with certifying management systems, products, services and people. On 16 March 2026 it [awarded a quality management certificate to the finance area of the National Institute of Health](<https://ins.gov.mz/area-financeira-do-ins-certificada-com-a-norma-iso-90012015/>); no certificate against a standard on information technology or data is named.
