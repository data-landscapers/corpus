# Kenya

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Kenya's standards body is the Kenya Bureau of Standards, which [was established by the Standards Act, Chapter 496, and started operations in July 1974](<https://www.iso.org/member/1854.html>). A 2026 release says it provides [standards development, metrology, conformity assessment, training and certification](<https://scienceforafrica.foundation/media-center/africa-raises-bar-global-grant-accountability-launch-revised-good-financial-grant>). In August 2026 it was [led by a director general and overseen by a National Standards Council](<https://www.thecoast.co.ke/2026/08/28/kenya-to-permanently-host-arso-headquarters-backs-common-certificate/17/54/business-news/thecoast/18260/04/>). The ICT Authority's Government Data Centre Standard of January 2020 [names the bureau as the source on valid national and international standards](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-05/Data%20Centre%20Standard.pdf>). The bureau helped develop the authority's standards. A November 2026 analysis by a law firm says [the bureau has issued KS 3007, a Code of Practice for AI Applications](<https://cioafrica.co/ai-governance-fragmentation-cause-for-concern/>). It is voluntary technical and governance guidance.

**Certification.** In July 2025 the Kenya Accreditation Service [extended the bureau's accreditation so it can certify organisations to ISO/IEC 27001:2022](<https://blog.kenas.go.ke/empowering-quality-trade-and-digital-security-kenas-accredits-kebs-to-four-strategic-international-standards/>), the international information security standard. The [2026 release](<https://scienceforafrica.foundation/media-center/africa-raises-bar-global-grant-accountability-launch-revised-good-financial-grant>) lists certification among the bureau's services.

***Also on file***. These include the [ARSO common certificate (One Standard, One Test, One Certificate)](<https://www.thecoast.co.ke/2026/08/28/kenya-to-permanently-host-arso-headquarters-backs-common-certificate/17/54/business-news/thecoast/18260/04/>) and the [Good Financial Grant Practice Standard (ARS 1651:2026)](<https://scienceforafrica.foundation/media-center/africa-raises-bar-global-grant-accountability-launch-revised-good-financial-grant>). Another is the [Government ICT Standards, Data Centre Standard ICTA.2.002:2019](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-05/Data%20Centre%20Standard.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says ISO/IEC 27001 is adopted as a Kenyan standard. None says anyone certifies against KS 3007 or another national standard on information technology or data.
