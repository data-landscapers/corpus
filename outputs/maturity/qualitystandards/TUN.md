# Tunisia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Tunisia's standards body is the Institut National de la Normalisation et de la Propriété Industrielle, which [ISO's member registry](<https://www.iso.org/member/2158.html>) describes in October 2026 as a public institution under the Ministry of Industry, in charge of standardisation and of product and quality systems certification. On 25 May 2026 it [issued standardisation bulletin no. 441](<http://www.innorpi.tn/sites/default/files/2026-05/Officiel%20EP%20441_0.pdf>), opening a two-month public enquiry on draft Tunisian standards under a 2009 law and a 2011 decree. The drafts cover measurement, quality management, and electrical and fibre-optic cables, and include no standard on information technology, information security, software or data.

**Certification.** In December 2025 the institute [headed Tunisia's delegation](<https://lncsm.org.ly/2025/12/18/%d8%a7%d8%ae%d8%aa%d9%8f%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%aa-%d8%a3%d8%b9%d9%85%d8%a7%d9%84-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%ac%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%b4%d9%80%d8%b1%d9%8a%d9%86-%d9%84%d9%81%d8%b1%d9%8a/>) to the twentieth meeting of a working group with Libya on mutual recognition of conformity certificates. The report names no certification against a standard on information technology or data.
