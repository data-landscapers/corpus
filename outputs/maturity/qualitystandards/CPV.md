# Cape Verde

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Cabo Verde's standards body is the Instituto de Gestão da Qualidade e da Propriedade Intelectual, which publishes [the list of Cabo Verdean standards](<https://igqpi.cv/download/158/acervo-normativo/8119/lista-de-ncv-2.pdf>), updated in March 2024. The list runs from NCV 001:2015 to NCV 025:2023. Of its 25 national standards, [21 are on agricultural, fisheries and food products and four on tourism](<https://igqpi.cv/download/158/acervo-normativo/8119/lista-de-ncv-2.pdf>); it holds none on information technology, information security, software or data. The list does not state the law that set the institute up.

**Certification.** The [catalogue of March 2024](<https://igqpi.cv/download/158/acervo-normativo/8119/lista-de-ncv-2.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It lists standards and no certification scheme. Certificates held by a port company, a data centre and the state's digital agency against international standards are listed below, and none names the institute or a certifier accredited in the country.

***Also on file***. [ISO 9001 certification of ENAPOR](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/mf/-/vice-primeiro-ministro-e-ministro-das-finan%C3%A7as-destaca-engajamento-do-governo-em-continuar-a-investir-nas-infraestruturas-portu%C3%A1rias>); [ISO 27001 certification of ENAPOR (targeted for 2026)](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/mf/-/vice-primeiro-ministro-e-ministro-das-finan%C3%A7as-destaca-engajamento-do-governo-em-continuar-a-investir-nas-infraestruturas-portu%C3%A1rias>); [Praia data centre Uptime Institute Tier III certification](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3100977/Relat%C3%B3rio%20e%20Contas%20NOSi%20-%202024.pdf/2fe4a66a-1826-4951-4da3-9cce82aef26e>); [NOSi public-key infrastructure ISO-27001 certification](<https://www.nosi.cv/documents/20121/2842828/PAO_2024.pdf>).
