# Libya

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Libya's standards body is the National Centre for Standardization and Metrology, [created by Law 5 of 1990](<https://aladel.gov.ly/home/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-5-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1990%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85/>), issued on 15 May 1990. The law charges the centre with quality control and with preparing, adopting and supervising standards, and makes the standards it adopts binding nationwide. On 3 February 2026 the centre's supreme committee, meeting at the planning ministry, [adopted its plan for 2026 and a number of Libyan standards](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=350300&lang=ar>). The report of the meeting names none of them as a standard on information technology or data. [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1890.html>) lists the centre as Libya's national standards body, a public organization established in 1990, which issues and approves standards.

**Certification.** The [1990 law](<https://aladel.gov.ly/home/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-5-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1990%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85/>) gives the centre quality control as well as standard-setting. Neither it nor the report of the 2026 meeting names certification against a standard on information technology or data.
