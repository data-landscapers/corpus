# Gambia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Gambia Standards Bureau [was established by an Act of Parliament in 2010](<https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf>) to develop and publish national standards in all sectors and to provide conformity assessment and metrology services. Its director general [said in 2026](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/iso-secretary-general-visits-gambia-to-deepen-partnership>) that it has more than 70 staff, having absorbed the former weights and measures department. The bureau's [2022 catalogue](<https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf>) lists GAMS ISO/IEC 27001:2005, on information security management systems, as a Gambian Standard adopted from the international one. The international standards organisation [lists the bureau as a correspondent member](<https://www.iso.org/member/576505.html>).

**Certification.** The [2022 catalogue](<https://tgsb.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/TGSB-STANDARDS-CATALOGUE-2022.pdf>) says the bureau provides conformity assessment services. It names no certification scheme for the information security standard, which adopts the 2005 edition of the international one, and no organisation certified against it.
