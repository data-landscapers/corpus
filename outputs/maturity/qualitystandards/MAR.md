# Morocco

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Morocco's national standards body is IMANOR, the Moroccan Institute for Standardisation, which [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1931.html>) lists under its own heading as Institut Marocain de Normalisation. The same page says IMANOR was created in 2010 to replace SNIMA, the earlier body, and that it produces Moroccan standards. Nothing found names a Moroccan standard on information technology or data, or says which of its standards cover that field.

**Certification.** The nearest held fact is the [ISO member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1931.html>), which describes IMANOR as the national standards body producing Moroccan standards and says nothing about certification. Nothing found says IMANOR or a certifier accredited in Morocco offers certification against a standard on information technology or data, or that any organisation, public or private, has been certified against one.
