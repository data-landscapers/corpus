# Chad

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Chad's standards body is the Agence Tchadienne de Normalisation. Its page on the standardisation law says the law [creates the national standardisation structures](<https://www.atnor.td/la-loi-relative-a-la-normalisation/>), among them the agency, charged with preparing, publishing and issuing Chadian standards and with adopting international standards as national ones. The agency's site describes it as a public administrative establishment under the commerce and industry ministry, and [records its technical committees adopting draft Chadian standards](<https://atnor.td/>) on 31 July, 5 August and 7 August 2026, among them ten international standards on quality management. None of the drafts is on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The agency's [site](<https://atnor.td/>) is the nearest text held, and it names no certification against a standard on information technology or data.

***Also on file***. [Conseil National de la Qualité (CONAQ)](<https://www.atnor.td/la-loi-relative-a-la-normalisation/>); [Comité Interministériel de Règlementation (CNIR)](<https://www.atnor.td/la-loi-relative-a-la-normalisation/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a national standard on information technology or data.
