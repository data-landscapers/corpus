# Burundi

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Burundi's standards body is the Bureau burundais de normalisation et de contrôle de la qualité. On 29 August 2025 it [signed a memorandum in Bujumbura with the Office congolais de contrôle](<https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires>), its counterpart in the Democratic Republic of Congo, on cooperation in standardisation, quality control and certification. The report of the signing, which presents the memorandum as a step to reduce non-tariff barriers to trade between the two countries, names no standard on information technology or data that the bureau has issued.

**Certification.** The [memorandum of August 2025](<https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires>) covers certification as one of three fields of cooperation between the two bodies, with standardisation and quality control. The report says nothing of certification against a standard on information technology or data, and names no organisation that the bureau has certified.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Politique Nationale Qualité du Burundi](<https://www.presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Conseil-du-02-Fevrier-2022.pdf>).
