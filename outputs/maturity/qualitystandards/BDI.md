# Burundi

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Burundi's standards body is the Bureau burundais de normalisation et de contrôle de la qualité. The international standards organisation's member page describes it as an [incorporated administrative public institution whose aims include preparing draft national standards](<https://www.iso.org/member/1591.html>). On 29 August 2025 it [signed a memorandum in Bujumbura with the Office congolais de contrôle](<https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires>), its counterpart in the Democratic Republic of Congo, on cooperation in standardisation, quality control and certification. Neither source names a standard on information technology or data that the bureau has issued.

**Certification.** The [memorandum of August 2025](<https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/0109-1626-commerce-rdc-burundi-vers-une-reduction-des-barrieres-non-tarifaires>) covers certification as one of three fields of cooperation between the two bodies, with standardisation and quality control. The report says nothing of certification against a standard on information technology or data, and names no organisation that the bureau has certified.
