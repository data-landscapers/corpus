# Tanzania

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Tanzania's standards body is the Tanzania Bureau of Standards. It [published its list of new and revised compulsory Tanzania Standards as of October 2025](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/LIST%20OF%20COMPULSORY%20TANZANIA%20STANDARD_1785231917.pdf>), enforceable under the Standards Act, which the list cites as chapter 130 of the laws in its revised edition of 2023. The entries held are on batteries, conductors, fasteners, wire and similar goods, and the list continues beyond what is held. None of the entries held is a standard on information technology, information security, software or data.

**Certification.** The [list of compulsory standards](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/LIST%20OF%20COMPULSORY%20TANZANIA%20STANDARD_1785231917.pdf>) is the nearest text held. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified against any standard.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a national standard on information technology or data.
