# Tanzania

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Tanzania's standards body is the Tanzania Bureau of Standards. The international standards organisation's member page says it was [established by the Standards Act of 1975 and operational from 6 April 1976](<https://www.iso.org/member/2120.html>). Its [list of compulsory Tanzania Standards as of October 2025](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/LIST%20OF%20COMPULSORY%20TANZANIA%20STANDARD_1785231917.pdf>) cites the Standards Act. That is chapter 130 of the laws, revised in 2023. The entries held are on batteries, conductors, fasteners, wire and similar goods. In February 2025 the bureau [circulated drafts for comment, including adoptions of ISO/IEC 23894, 27032, 27035, 27036 and 27043](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1739785468-Stakeholders'%20Comments%20Letter%20Feb%202025.pdf>). In October 2026 the head of the communications regulator [said the bureau has newly introduced a Tanzania Standard on accessibility of communications and ICT products and services for persons with disabilities](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>). The same month the bureau [recognised the regulator's work to implement the national ICT accessibility standard](<https://ipris.digital/the-ipris-project-marks-three-years-and-launches-its-tenth-cohort/>).

**Certification.** The bureau [lists the e-Government Authority as certified to ISO 27001:2022 from 30 January 2026](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1787571805-CERTIFIED%20CLIENTS%20UNDER%20ISO%2027001%20ISMS.pdf>), under certificate TZ-ISMS-01, for its offices in Dar es Salaam, Iringa and Dodoma. The certificate runs to 29 January 2029.
