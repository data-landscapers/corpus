# Tanzania

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Tanzania's standards body is the Tanzania Bureau of Standards, which the international standards organisation's member page says was [established by the Standards Act of 1975 and operational from 6 April 1976](<https://www.iso.org/member/2120.html>). It [published its list of new and revised compulsory Tanzania Standards as of October 2025](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/LIST%20OF%20COMPULSORY%20TANZANIA%20STANDARD_1785231917.pdf>), enforceable under the Standards Act, which the list cites as chapter 130 of the laws in its revised edition of 2023. The entries held on that list are on batteries, conductors, fasteners, wire and similar goods. In October 2026 the Director General of the Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority [said the bureau has newly introduced a Tanzania Standard on accessibility of communications and ICT products and services for persons with disabilities](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>). In February 2025 the bureau [circulated drafts for comment, including adoptions of ISO/IEC 23894, 27032, 27035, 27036 and 27043](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1739785468-Stakeholders'%20Comments%20Letter%20Feb%202025.pdf>).

**Certification.** The [list of compulsory standards](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/LIST%20OF%20COMPULSORY%20TANZANIA%20STANDARD_1785231917.pdf>) is the nearest text held on certification. It names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified against any standard.
