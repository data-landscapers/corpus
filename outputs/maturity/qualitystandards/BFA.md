# Burkina Faso

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Burkina Faso's standards body is the Agence Burkinabè de Normalisation, de la Métrologie et de la Qualité. [Decree 2016-357 of 13 May 2016](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bkf165557.pdf>) approves its statutes as a public administrative establishment, created by a 2012 decree under the 2007 law on standardisation, certification, accreditation and quality, and charges it with drawing up and issuing national standards. On 4 February 2026 the agency [signed a framework convention with the telecoms regulator](<https://abnorm.bf/2026/02/05/signature-de-la-convention-cadre-pour-la-gestion-des-identifiants-dobjets-oid/>) on managing object identifiers, which names the regulator's executive secretary as the national registration authority. Neither text names a national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [2016 statutes](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/bkf165557.pdf>) rest on a law that covers certification and accreditation as well as standardisation. They name no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data.
