# Somalia

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Somalia's standards body is the Somali Bureau of Standards. The international standards organisation's member record states that it is [a national standards body established by an Act of parliament](<https://www.iso.org/member/8867946.html>), the Somali Standards and Quality Control law, numbered Lr27 in the record, and that it has been operational since early 2020. The record, published in August 2026, is the only source found on the bureau, and it names no national standard on information technology or data.

**Certification.** The [member record](<https://www.iso.org/member/8867946.html>) names no scheme for certifying an organisation against a standard on information technology or data, and no organisation the bureau has certified against any standard.
