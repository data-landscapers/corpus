# Mauritius

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Mauritius Standards Bureau [became a corporate body under the Mauritius Standards Bureau Act 1993](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Lists/AnnualReports/Attachments/20/MSB%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20%202021-2022.pdf>), in effect from 16 July 1993, with the object of preparing and revising Mauritian Standards. The national standard on information security management is [MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>), the designation under which the bureau's certification register lists it.

**Certification.** The bureau certifies organisations itself. Its certification unit [ran the national information security certification scheme in 2021/2022](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Lists/AnnualReports/Attachments/20/MSB%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20%202021-2022.pdf>), under regulations of 2008, and kept eight organisations certified; the scheme is accredited by the national accreditation service. A telecoms company says the bureau [first certified its data centre in 2013](<https://www.emtel.com/sites/default/files/2019-11/iso-certification.PDF>). The bureau's register at 31 December 2025 [lists ten certificates, all marked as accredited](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>). Six are held by public bodies: [the Passport and Immigration Office, the national computer emergency response team, the Mauritius Revenue Authority, the central information systems division of the technology ministry, the police information technology unit and the National Sanctions Secretariat](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>).
