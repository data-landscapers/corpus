# Mauritius

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Mauritius Standards Bureau [became a corporate body under the Mauritius Standards Bureau Act 1993](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Lists/AnnualReports/Attachments/20/MSB%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20%202021-2022.pdf>), in effect from 16 July 1993, with the object of preparing and revising Mauritian Standards. [ISO's member page](<https://www.iso.org/member/1944.html>), read in November 2026, says it is mandated to develop standards and to certify management systems. Under Government Notice 119 of 25 January 2025 the bureau [published five ISO/IEC standards on information technology and cybersecurity as Mauritian Standards](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards/Published%20Mauritian%20Standards%20in%202025.pdf>). The national standard on information security management is [MS ISO/IEC 27001:2022](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf>), the designation under which the bureau's certification register lists it.

**Certification.** The bureau certifies organisations itself. Its certification unit [ran the national information security certification scheme in 2021/2022](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Lists/AnnualReports/Attachments/20/MSB%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20%202021-2022.pdf>), under regulations of 2008, and kept eight organisations certified. A telecoms company says the bureau [first certified its data centre in 2013](<https://www.emtel.com/sites/default/files/2019-11/iso-certification.PDF>). The bureau's register at 31 December 2025 [lists ten certificates, all marked as accredited](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>). Six are held by public bodies, among them [the Passport and Immigration Office, the Mauritius Revenue Authority and the police information technology unit](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2031-12-2025.pdf>). By 30 June 2026 the register [listed 11 certified organisations](<https://msb.intnet.mu/Documents/Certification-Schemes/General/Registered%20firms/Attachment%202%20-%20List%20of%20registered%20firms%20ISMS%2030-06-2026.pdf>), the Passport and Immigration Office's certificate dated 30 October 2025.
