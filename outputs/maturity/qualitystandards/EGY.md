# Egypt

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** The Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality states that it is [the sole official body for preparing and issuing Egyptian standards](<https://www.eos.org.eg/ar/page/11>), set up by Presidential Decree 2 of 1957 and given its present name by Presidential Decree 83 of 2005. ISO's member page says a [1979 Presidential Decree reorganised it](<https://www.iso.org/member/1700.html>) as the only official authority for standardization, quality control and metrology. Its register lists [Egyptian Standard 7982-1/2025](<https://www.eos.org.eg/en/standard/14180>), endorsed on 1 October 2025, which adopts the international standard on the exchange of electricity metering data and is classed under information technology. The standard is not obligatory. A [ministerial decision of 2022](<https://www.eos.org.eg/ar/page/27>) lets an international or foreign standard stand where no Egyptian one exists.

**Certification.** The [register entry](<https://www.eos.org.eg/en/standard/14180>) for the 2025 standard is the nearest text held, and it names no certifier and no organisation certified against the standard.
