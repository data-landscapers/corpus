# Togo

## National standards for information technology and data

**Standards body and its standards.** Togo's standards body is the Agence togolaise de normalisation, which the agriculture ministry describes as a technical structure of the Haute Autorité de la Qualité et de l'Environnement. The ministry says the agency [drew up 46 national standards](<https://agriculture.gouv.tg/fsrp-togo-46-normes-nationales-et-31-programmes-de-certification-pour-renforcer-la-qualite-et-la-competitivite-des-produits-agroalimentaires/>), all on agri-food value chains such as rice, soya, poultry and meat, which were presented in Lomé on 17 and 18 August 2026. The ministry's account names no standard on information technology or data, and does not say what law or decree set the agency up.

**Certification.** The [ministry's account](<https://agriculture.gouv.tg/fsrp-togo-46-normes-nationales-et-31-programmes-de-certification-pour-renforcer-la-qualite-et-la-competitivite-des-produits-agroalimentaires/>) reports 31 certification programmes for agri-food products beside the standards. It names no certification against a standard on information technology or data. A decree on a national mechanism for assessing the conformity of products, adopted on 30 July 2026, is listed below.
