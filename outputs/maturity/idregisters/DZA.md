# Algeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Algeria's national identification number, the NIN, is used across government. [The Interior Ministry says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government bodies and partner sectors](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). They include justice, banking, social security, taxation and education. [La Sentinelle reported in April 2024 that the NIN is carried on civil-status registers and extracts](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/04/04/une-revolution-dans-le-service-public/>) kept by communes and consulates.

**Main registers.** Two main registers are named: social security and taxation. [The ministry's account](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>) does not name the electoral roll, the public payroll or the business register. [The national electronic medical record is to be built around each person's identifier](<https://medica-magazine.com/lalgerie-fixe-la-date-de-la-generalisation-du-dossier-medical-numerique/>) from summer 2026.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [In 2023, 23 ministries and public bodies consulted civil status data nearly 111 million times](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/>), the Interior Ministry said.
