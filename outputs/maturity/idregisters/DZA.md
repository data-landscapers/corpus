# Algeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [Algeria's national identification number (NIN) is carried on the civil-status registers and extracts kept by communes and consulates, per La Sentinelle](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/04/04/une-revolution-dans-le-service-public/>). [Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government authorities and partner sectors including justice, banking, social security, taxation and education](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). [The Interior Ministry communique says generalisation of the national identification number and access to Registre national de l'Etat civil data gave nearly 111 million consultations by 23 ministries and public bodies in 2023](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/>).
