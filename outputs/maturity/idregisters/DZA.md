# Algeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government authorities and partner sectors including justice, banking, social security, taxation and education](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
