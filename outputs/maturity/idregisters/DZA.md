# Algeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [Algeria's national identification number (NIN) is carried on the civil-status registers and extracts kept by communes and consulates, per La Sentinelle](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/04/04/une-revolution-dans-le-service-public/>). [Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government authorities and partner sectors including justice, banking, social security, taxation and education](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
