# Gabon

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Gabon plans to key two public registers on its new biometric population register, the RBPP. The amended Digital Gabon financing agreement, [in force from 2 September 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>), provides for linking the social protection beneficiary registry to the RBPP. It provides the same for the civil servants' human resources database. No source found shows a register in service with the ID number.

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows a main public register using the ID number. The nearest held fact is the [same financing agreement](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>). It names two registers to be linked. They are the social registry and the civil service database.

**Matching reported.** In September 2026 the civil service minister said a census of public agents [had been cross-checked against the payroll and immigration directorate records](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-fonction-publique-loperation-dassainissement-du-fichier-solde-livre-ses-conclusions/>). The posts it freed are expected to save about FCFA 11 billion. The source does not say the payroll is keyed on the ID number.
