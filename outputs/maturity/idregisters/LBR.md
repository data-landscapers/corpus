# Liberia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Liberia's government payroll is checked against the National Identification Registry, NIR. [In February 2024 the NIR said it and the finance ministry had built an interface to audit the payroll](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>) as new employees join. [The Civil Service Agency puts the NIN on government employees' ID cards](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>). FrontPage Africa reported that this has helped remove ghost names from the payroll. [A 2025 study found the electoral commission and the NIR keep two separate databases](<https://doi.org/10.22271/allresearch.2025.v11.i6d.12680>).

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>).

**Matching reported.** No figure since October 2023 from checking a register against the ID has been found. [The NIR says a 2018 payroll check found only 60,000 of about 80,000 names](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>) in its records.
