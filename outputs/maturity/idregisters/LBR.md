# Liberia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Liberia's government payroll is checked against the National Identification Registry. In February 2024 the registry said [it and the finance ministry had built an interface to audit the payroll](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>) as new employees join. A May 2024 memorandum [lets the Civil Service Agency check the payroll in real time on the registry's verification portal](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/liberian-banks-no-longer-require-national-id-card-says-cbl-and-nir/>). [The Civil Service Agency puts the NIN on government employees' ID cards](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>).

**Main registers.** [A 2025 study found the electoral commission and the registry keep two separate databases](<https://doi.org/10.22271/allresearch.2025.v11.i6d.12680>). The voter roll is not drawn from the NIN. The registry's director said in June 2025 that [a memorandum with the electoral commission was being finalised](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/government-extends-national-id-deadline-to-august-31/>), for checking voters' identity. The payroll is the only main register shown using it.

**Matching reported.** The Civil Service Agency's 2024 report says [5,536 employees were removed from the payroll from March to October 2024](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-frontpage-africa-investigation-uncovers-conflicting-accounts-in-govt-payroll-cleanup/>), saving $1.84 million. The IMF counts 3,775. Both figures cover ghost workers, double dippers, duplicate NINs and shared bank accounts together. A June 2025 report puts [the saving from removing ghost names through the portal at about US$2.5 million](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/liberian-banks-no-longer-require-national-id-card-says-cbl-and-nir/>).
