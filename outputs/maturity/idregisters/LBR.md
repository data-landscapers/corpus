# Liberia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [The Civil Service Agency integrates National Identification Numbers into government employees' ID cards through the NIR portal, and Sargbe says this has helped remove ghost names from the payroll](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>). [The paper's findings state the NEC and NIR databases operate as two separate databases, so the voter register is not drawn from the NIR](<https://doi.org/10.22271/allresearch.2025.v11.i6d.12680>). [An MoU between the Civil Service Agency and NIR provides that civil servants' working ID cards will carry National Identification Numbers and that NBIS is used in the government employee verification headcount](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/csa-and-nir-sign-mou-for-service-delivery/>).

**Main registers.** No source held since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>).
