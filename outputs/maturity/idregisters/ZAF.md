# South Africa

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** South Africa plans to check the government payroll against the population register. [In May 2026 Home Affairs said it had built a real-time employee verification portal for National Treasury](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-builds-digital-verification-platform-national-treasury-combat>). The portal links to the population register to check payroll records. The department said it would go live on 15 June 2026. No source found since then shows it in service or reports what its checks found.

**Main registers.** In September 2025 National Treasury said [it was cross-checking the PERSAL civil service payroll with tax and population data](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/national-treasury-deploys-tech-to-terminate-ghost-workers/GxwQDM1DWXR7lPVo>). It had no final results. In November 2025 Home Affairs said [its biometric check of public servants against the population and payroll databases was still under way](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34202/>).
