# Benin

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Benin builds its electoral list from the national register of persons. [In September 2025 ANIP said the provisional list was extracted directly from the civil status file](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). The same file issues the identity card and the tax identifier. [ANIP's director general said the same in February 2025](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/2994/inquietudes-autour-fiabilite-liste-electorale-informatisee-benin-anip-reaffirme-credibilite-grace-systeme-mise-jour-continue/>). [Since October 2026 the public-sector pension fund asks retirees for their NPI](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>), with the pension book number, to prove they are alive.

**Main registers.** Two main registers are shown: the electoral list and the pension fund. [In March 2024 La Nation reported that the tax directorate had been testing links to the register for several years](<https://lanationbenin.bj/actualites/interoperabilite-avec-le-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-des-structures-publiques-et-privees-entretenues-sur-les-avantages>). ANIP was extending the NPI to about 100 other bodies.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [In October 2026 La Nouvelle Tribune reported that retirees prove they are alive in the app by entering their NPI](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/10/benin-15-189-retraites-ont-deja-effectue-leur-preuve-de-vie-la-campagne-se-poursuit/>).
