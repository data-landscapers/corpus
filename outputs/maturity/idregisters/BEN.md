# Benin

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [ANIP says the provisional electoral list is extracted directly from the Fichier National de l'État Civil, the same database used to issue the national identity card and the IFU tax identifier](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [The tax directorate and the emigration and immigration directorate have been trying interoperability with the RNPP for several years, and ANIP is extending it to about 100 other bodies to integrate the NPI](<https://lanationbenin.bj/actualites/interoperabilite-avec-le-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-des-structures-publiques-et-privees-entretenues-sur-les-avantages>). [From 15 October 2026 the public-sector pension fund asks each retiree for their NPI, with the pension booklet number, to confirm they are alive](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>).

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>).
