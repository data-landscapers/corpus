# Benin

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Benin builds its electoral list from the national register of persons. In September 2025 ANIP said [the provisional list was extracted directly from the civil status file](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). That file also issues the identity card and the tax identifier. [Since October 2026 the public-sector pension fund asks retirees for their NPI](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>) to prove they are alive.

**Main registers.** [Since December 2021 the tax directorate has issued tax identifiers online from the NPI](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>). By 25 October 2023 [439,333 of 1,077,679 tax identifiers were attached to an NPI, 40.76%](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>), the finance ministry said. [A World Bank programme agreed in 2026 ties $8 million to linking the two files](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>).

**Matching reported.** The tax directorate [matched its file against the register to find tax identifiers with no NPI](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>), before connecting the two systems. The October 2023 count is the only figure given.
