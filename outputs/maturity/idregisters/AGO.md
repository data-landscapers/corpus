# Angola

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [Intelwatch says Angola does not run an independent biometric voter registration and its electoral roll is derived from the civil identity database; the 2025 Lei do Recenseamento Eleitoral Oficioso replaces in-person registration with an automatic register extracted from the identity base](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [Minister Marcy Lopes says the Bilhete de Identidade is the main document for the update of the electoral register (Registo Eleitoral Oficioso) running from 15 June 2026 to 31 March 2027](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-120-mil-bilhetes-de-identidade-aguardam-levantamento-em-todo-o-pais/>).

**Main registers.** No source held since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>).
