# Angola

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Angola draws its electoral roll from the identity base. [Intelwatch reported in August 2026 that the roll is extracted from the civil identity database](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>) under a 2025 law on automatic registration. [The Justice Minister said in July 2026 that the identity card is the main document for updating the roll](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-120-mil-bilhetes-de-identidade-aguardam-levantamento-em-todo-o-pais/>). The update runs from June 2026 to March 2027. [A Secretary of State said in September 2025 that the identity card number is also the tax number](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Main registers.** Two main registers use the number: the electoral roll and the tax register. [A 2026 plan for the identity database](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>) lists the criminal, vehicle, commercial and land registries for integration. It does not say they will use the number.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [In July 2026 the Justice Minister said the identity database exchanges data with the citizens' database](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>) to update the roll.
