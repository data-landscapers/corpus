# Angola

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [Intelwatch says Angola does not run an independent biometric voter registration and its electoral roll is derived from the civil identity database; the 2025 Lei do Recenseamento Eleitoral Oficioso replaces in-person registration with an automatic register extracted from the identity base](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [Minister Marcy Lopes says the Bilhete de Identidade is the main document for the update of the electoral register (Registo Eleitoral Oficioso) running from 15 June 2026 to 31 March 2027](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-120-mil-bilhetes-de-identidade-aguardam-levantamento-em-todo-o-pais/>). [In September 2025 the Secretary of State for Justice said the civil identification and tax systems are synchronised so the Bilhete de Identidade number is also the tax identification number](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>).
