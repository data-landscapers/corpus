# Ethiopia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Ethiopia's tax register is keyed on Fayda, the national ID. [National ID Ethiopia's May 2026 slides say the Ministry of Revenue has harmonised over 5 million existing tax numbers with Fayda](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). Fayda is now mandatory for every new tax number. [Since December 2023 each new taxpayer number has been tied to a Fayda number](<https://ictd.ac/general/ethiopia-national-digital-id-breakthrough-tax-system-transformation/>), ICTD says. [A 2026 MOSIP case study by the National ID Program says the same](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Main registers.** One main register is shown: the tax register. [The May 2026 slides](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>) name no electoral roll, payroll, social registry or business register using Fayda. [A 2024 Fayda deck lists a civil service HR system built on Fayda among planned uses](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>). [In October 2024 the Addis Ababa city administration and the revenue ministry signed on to the programme](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/42222/>), agreeing to end their own biometric registration. [In November 2026 the Agricultural Transformation Institute said its farmer register, with nearly one million farmers enrolled, is linked to Fayda](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/11/08/ethiopia-targets-four-million-farmers-for-digital-registration-by-2028/>). A farmer register is not one of the five main registers. [Finland's foreign ministry says the digital land registry is being linked to Fayda](<https://um.fi/results-of-development-cooperation/-/asset_publisher/o2A1dCQquJyC/content/miljoonille-etiopialaisille-oikeus-omaan-maahan/35732>), among other databases, in 2026.

**Matching reported.** [The slides count over 5 million existing tax numbers linked to Fayda](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). They do not say what the linking found, such as duplicate or false records. No other figure from checking a register against the ID has been found.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>).

***Not found***. No second main register has been found using Fayda. No source found says what linking tax numbers to it turned up.
