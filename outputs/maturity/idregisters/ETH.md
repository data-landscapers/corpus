# Ethiopia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Ethiopia's tax register is keyed on Fayda, the national ID. [National ID Ethiopia's May 2026 slides say the Ministry of Revenue has harmonised over 5 million existing tax numbers with Fayda](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). Fayda is now mandatory for every new tax number. [A 2026 MOSIP case study by the National ID Program says the same](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Main registers.** One main register is shown: the tax register. [The May 2026 slides](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>) name no electoral roll, payroll, social registry or business register using Fayda. No other source found since October 2023 shows a second main register using it.

**Matching reported.** [The slides count over 5 million existing tax numbers linked to Fayda](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). They do not say what the linking found, such as duplicate or false records. No other figure from checking a register against the ID has been found.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>).

***Not found***. No second main register has been found using Fayda. No source found says what linking tax numbers to it turned up.
