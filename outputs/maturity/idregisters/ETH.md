# Ethiopia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [National ID Ethiopia's May 2026 slides say the Ministry of Revenue has harmonised over 5 million existing tax identification numbers with Fayda and made Fayda mandatory for new TINs nationally](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). [A 2026 MOSIP case study by the National ID Program says the same: more than 5 million existing tax numbers are harmonised with Fayda, and every new taxpayer number now requires it](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the tax register's use of Fayda, the one main register the slides name as keyed on the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Matching reported.** [The same slides count over 5 million existing tax identification numbers matched to Fayda records, the only figure held from checking a public register against the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).
