# Nigeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** The National Social Register used for cash transfers [requires a national identification number, NIN](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>). Since 2023 people without one are disqualified. In July 2026 the electoral commission's chairman said [online voter registration is anchored on the NIN](<https://thenationonlineng.net/six-months-to-presidential-poll-inec-raises-preparation-tempo/>). The commission and the identity agency [piloted joint voter and NIN enrolment in Anambra in July 2025](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/inec-nimc-partner-to-scale-up-nin-registration-nationwide/>).

**Main registers.** The tax administration act [makes the NIN the tax number for individuals](<https://guardian.ng/news/nin-cac-number-to-serve-as-tax-id-firs/>) from January 2026. Under the NIMC Act 2026, [registering to vote and filing tax returns legally require a NIN](<https://oal.law/the-nimc-act-2026-explained-what-nigerias-new-identity-law-means-for-you/>), a law firm notes. In September 2025 the President said the identity agency [had linked data with 125 partner agencies](<https://guardian.ng/featured/126m-nigerians-secure-nin-database-capacity-hits-250m-fg/>), the Federal Inland Revenue Service among them for tax records. The agriculture ministry [is linking farmers to their NIN](<https://fmino.gov.ng/at-agrow-dialogue-in-abuja-kyari-calls-for-national-digital-farmers-register-to-end-duplication-target-support-to-real-farmers/>).

**Matching reported.** The social programmes agency says [1,628,587 NINs in the social register have been validated by the identity agency](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). It does not say how many records the check removed or corrected.
