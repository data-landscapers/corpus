# Nigeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [In 2025 the Niger State government launched an initiative integrating the NIN into the Social Safety Register, to improve identity verification for poor and vulnerable households](<https://www.techcityng.com/the-gender-gap-in-rural-niger-states-digital-identity/>). [NSIPA says NIN is integrated into the NSR database, with 1,628,587 NINs validated by NIMC](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [Nigeria's agriculture ministry has a partnership with NIMC to link farmers to their National Identity Number, building toward a national digital farmers register, per the minister](<https://fmino.gov.ng/at-agrow-dialogue-in-abuja-kyari-calls-for-national-digital-farmers-register-to-end-duplication-target-support-to-real-farmers/>). [Education minister Tunji Alausa said the ministry is seeking to link NIN information to education records covering 75-80 million students, to track pupils, students and teachers](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>). [The Federal Inland Revenue Service said in December 2025 that under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act, due to take effect in January 2026, the NIN would serve as the tax ID for individuals](<https://guardian.ng/news/nin-cac-number-to-serve-as-tax-id-firs/>).
