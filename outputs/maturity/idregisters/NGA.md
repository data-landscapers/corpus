# Nigeria

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** The National Social Register used for cash transfers [requires a national identification number, NIN](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>), and since 2023 people without one are disqualified. NSIPA says [1,628,587 NINs in the register have been validated by NIMC](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>), the identity agency. In July 2026 the INEC chairman said [online voter registration is anchored on the NIN](<https://thenationonlineng.net/six-months-to-presidential-poll-inec-raises-preparation-tempo/>) as its check on identity.

**Main registers.** Two main registers use the NIN: the social register and the electoral roll. The tax administration act [makes the NIN the tax number for individuals](<https://guardian.ng/news/nin-cac-number-to-serve-as-tax-id-firs/>) from January 2026. The agriculture ministry [is linking farmers to their NIN](<https://fmino.gov.ng/at-agrow-dialogue-in-abuja-kyari-calls-for-national-digital-farmers-register-to-end-duplication-target-support-to-real-farmers/>) for a national register of farmers. The education ministry [is seeking to link NINs to the records of 75 to 80 million students](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>).

**Matching reported.** The nearest fact is [the validation count for the social register](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>): 1,628,587 NINs checked by NIMC. It does not say how many records the check removed or corrected.
