# Sao Tome and Principe

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [The Bilhete de Identidade is to become the base document for automatic and permanent voter registration, updating the electoral rolls from civil registry data, says the Camoes institute on the PReSE project](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe>). [The EU-funded PReSE project says an interoperability platform, being developed by the Universidade de Aveiro, will link the civil registry databases (SIGA and Bilhete de Identidade) to the electoral roll so voters are registered automatically](<https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NOTA-DE-IMPRENSA-1.pdf>). [The Director of Registries and Notaries says a first-phase single identity card is planned to bring together civil identification, tax, social security, health and electoral commission information](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2024/09/28/45916/sao-tome-e-principe-vai-avancar-com-o-cartao-de-identidade-unica/>).
