# Sao Tome and Principe

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** São Tomé and Príncipe plans to register voters automatically from the identity card. [The Camões institute says the Bilhete de Identidade is to become the base for permanent voter registration](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe>). [In September 2025 the EU-funded PReSE project said a platform being built by the University of Aveiro will link the civil registry to the roll](<https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NOTA-DE-IMPRENSA-1.pdf>). [In September 2024 the registries director said a single identity card is planned](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2024/09/28/45916/sao-tome-e-principe-vai-avancar-com-o-cartao-de-identidade-unica/>). It would bring together civil, tax, social security, health and electoral data.

**Main registers.** In March 2024 the justice minister said [an identity card carrying social security, tax and voter data was designed and awaited financing](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2024/03/12/43558/bilhete-de-identidade-emitido-na-ilha-do-principe/>). No main register is shown using the number.

**Matching reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure from checking a register against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe>).
