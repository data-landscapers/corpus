# Sudan

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [An Omdurman land registration employee told Ayin Network in October 2026 that the electronic land records can be searched by owner name and national number](<https://3ayin.com/real-estate-ownership-dispute-/>). Applications for replacement deeds carry the owner's national number. Owners are recovering deeds lost in the war. Backup copies of the database are held, and deeds can be checked against it before they are reissued.
