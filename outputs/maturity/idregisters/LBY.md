# Libya

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Public payroll in Libya is keyed on the national number. In December 2025 the Attorney General said checks had [deleted nearly 40,000 salary numbers](<https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/>) linked to national ID numbers that do not exist. In August 2022 his office [found 8,690 citizens paid under forged national numbers](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/515274>). The electoral roll uses the number too. In April 2025 the election commission, HNEC, [registered 560,896 voters for municipal elections](<https://libyareview.com/54785/560896-voters-registered-for-libyan-municipal-elections/>) by SMS with their national ID number.

**Main registers.** Two main registers use the national number: payroll and the electoral roll. A December 2025 regulation [kept everyone who had registered with the national number in earlier elections](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/>) on the preliminary voter lists. In December 2025 the labour ministry [asked the civil registry for a direct electronic link to the national number system](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/499953>). In June 2025 a UN-backed advisory committee [proposed limiting voter registration to holders of the national number](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/479250>).

**Matching reported.** In December 2025 Attorney General Al-Siddiq Al-Sour said checking had [deleted more than 17,000 national ID numbers that do not exist](<https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/>). Nearly 40,000 salary numbers linked to them went too. In 2022 the same office [reported 88,819 incorrect national numbers](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/515274>). Of these, 63,986 were absent from the civil registry.

***Also on file***. [National Number system](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/515274>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number.
