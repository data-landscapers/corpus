# Namibia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Namibia checks social grant recipients against its population register. [The ministries responsible signed an agreement linking the National Population Registration System to the social protection system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). It checks whether a person exists and is alive. All applicants for the Covid-19 emergency income grant were checked against the register. [In July 2026 the Office of the Prime Minister said electronic ID checks stop grant payments to deceased people](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>).

**Main registers.** One main register is shown: the social grants register. No source found since October 2023 shows the electoral roll, the tax register, the payroll or the business register using the ID number. [The Office of the Prime Minister's July 2026 statement](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>) speaks only of social grants.

**Matching reported.** [In July 2026 the Office of the Prime Minister said ID checks had saved about N$16 million on social grants](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). It did not say how many records were removed.

***Also on file***. [e-ID](<https://www.nampa.org/text/23017294>); [e-ID card](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>).

***Not found***. Only the social grants register has been found using the ID. No other main register has been found.
