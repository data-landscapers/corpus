# Uganda

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** The SAGE social grant [reaches only people on the National Identification Register](<http://digitalrightsalliance.africa/ugandas-digital-id-barrier-worsens-as-court-delivers-blow-to-access>) with a national ID, a digital rights group wrote in September 2025. The education portal, EMIS, asks learners and staff for their national identification number, NIN. By 30 September 2026 the Uganda Revenue Authority [had linked 2.13 million of its 5.41 million individual taxpayer records to NINs](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). The driving-permit system [was still being joined to the new identification platform](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/>) in September 2026.

**Main registers.** Three main registers use the NIN. Social protection and tax are given above. In October 2025 the Daily Monitor reported that [the national voter register was compiled from biometric data taken from NIRA](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/one-man-one-vote-inside-new-ec-biometric-system-5249126>), the national identification authority. A 2025 amendment [makes the NIN the tax number for individuals](<https://softpower.ug/nin-tin-transition-will-not-automatically-create-tax-liability-lugoloobi/>), and Cabinet [approved the move in September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/uganda-moves-to-make-national-digital-id-number-its-tax-identifier>).

**Matching reported.** The nearest fact is [the tax authority's count of linked records](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>): 39 per cent of individual taxpayer records carried a NIN by September 2026. It does not say what checking the records against the ID found.
