# Uganda

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [The author says the SAGE social grant only benefits people on the National Identification Register with a national ID, and the education portal EMIS requires a NIN of learners and staff](<http://digitalrightsalliance.africa/ugandas-digital-id-barrier-worsens-as-court-delivers-blow-to-access>). [Uganda Business News, citing the finance state minister to Parliament, reports the Uganda Revenue Authority had linked 2.13 million of its 5.41 million individual taxpayer records to National Identification Numbers by 30 September 2026](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). [NIRA says the driving-permit system is still being integrated with the new identification platform, with an interim workaround in place](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/>). [The Ministry of Finance says a 2025 amendment to the Tax Procedures Code Act makes the NIN the tax identification number for individuals, with a three-phase move to NIN-based taxpayer login under way in October 2026](<https://softpower.ug/nin-tin-transition-will-not-automatically-create-tax-liability-lugoloobi/>). [Cabinet approved the move to the NIN as individuals' tax identifier in September 2026, Biometric Update reports](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/uganda-moves-to-make-national-digital-id-number-its-tax-identifier>).
