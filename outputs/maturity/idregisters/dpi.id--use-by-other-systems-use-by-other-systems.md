# Use by other systems: use by other systems

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Union's interoperability framework and Data Policy Framework ask that the national ID be the single source of truth for identity across public registers. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Not used | A source from the past three years says public registers do not use the national ID number. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 One register | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Main registers | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Matching reported | Yes to 4 |

## Countries

### Namibia: 5 Leading

Matching of public registers against the national ID was reported with figures in 2026.

- The Office of the Prime Minister says government has saved approximately N$16 million on social grants through improved identity verification ([source, 2026-07-25](https://informante.web.na/?p=398234)).

### Malawi: 4 Operating

Three or more main public registers used the national ID number in 2024; no matching figures are known.

- The EU Delegation says Malawi's National ID underpins voter registration, social protection targeting and payroll management, three main registers ([source, 2024](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/malawi/10-years-fruitful-partnership-government-malawi-civil-registration_en)).

### Algeria: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2023; use by three main registers is not known.

- Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government authorities and partner sectors including justice, banking, social security, taxation and education ([source, 2023](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf)).

### Angola: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2026; use by three main registers is not known.

- Intelwatch says Angola does not run an independent biometric voter registration and its electoral roll is derived from the civil identity database; the 2025 Lei do Recenseamento Eleitoral Oficioso replaces in-person registration with an automatic register extracted from the identity base ([source, 2026-08-15](https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/)).
- Minister Marcy Lopes says the Bilhete de Identidade is the main document for the update of the electoral register (Registo Eleitoral Oficioso) running from 15 June 2026 to 31 March 2027 ([source, 2026-07-15](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-120-mil-bilhetes-de-identidade-aguardam-levantamento-em-todo-o-pais/)).

### Botswana: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2026; use by three main registers is not known.

- The minister's proposed reforms keep Omang as the primary ID for voter registration and give political parties access to the verified electronic voters' roll ([source, 2026-08-17](https://allafrica.com/stories/202608190305.html)).

### Egypt: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2026; use by three main registers is not known.

- The Public Prosecution says criminal judgments are recorded against citizens' national number in its digital systems ([source, 2026-07-27](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx)).
- CITC supports integration of the National ID database with external parties including the Labor Permits Department, General Organization for Social Insurance, Ministry of Social Solidarity and Central Bank, and the databases include elections ([source, 2024](https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html)).

### Gambia: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2024; use by three main registers is not known.

- The World Bank says the eCRVS has become the foundational interoperability system across ministries, using the unique national identification number as the common key ([source, 2024-03](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia)).

### Ghana: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2025; use by three main registers is not known.

- Republic Online reports that GRA revenue collection and NHIS member verification depend on NIA biometric verification, with GRA owing $377 million and NHIS $58 million for it ([source, 2025-09-21](https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/)).

### Liberia: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2024; use by three main registers is not known.

- The Civil Service Agency integrates National Identification Numbers into government employees' ID cards through the NIR portal, and Sargbe says this has helped remove ghost names from the payroll ([source, 2024-05](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/)).

### Mauritius: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2026; use by three main registers is not known.

- The Electoral Commissioner's office says voters can check the electoral register on its website by entering their national identity card number ([source, 2026-05-14](https://electoral.govmu.org/oec/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Communique-Enregistrement-des-electeurs.pdf)).
- The Government Information Service says National Savings Fund employer returns must give each employee's National Identity Card number with contributions paid ([source, 2024](https://govmu.org/EN/infoservices/socialsecurity/Pages/services.aspx)).

### Nigeria: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2025; use by three main registers is not known.

- In 2025 the Niger State government launched an initiative integrating the NIN into the Social Safety Register, to improve identity verification for poor and vulnerable households ([source, 2025](https://www.techcityng.com/the-gender-gap-in-rural-niger-states-digital-identity/)).

### Uganda: 3 Established

A public register used the national ID number in 2025; use by three main registers is not known.

- The author says the SAGE social grant only benefits people on the National Identification Register with a national ID, and the education portal EMIS requires a NIN of learners and staff ([source, 2025-09](http://digitalrightsalliance.africa/ugandas-digital-id-barrier-worsens-as-court-delivers-blow-to-access)).

### Djibouti: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2024.

- The Ministry of Digital Affairs says use of the NNI is obligatory for all institutions to exchange data between public bodies, and an interoperability platform with ANSIE is being set up ([source, 2024](https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15)).

### Guinea: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2026.

- The DGE and ONECI signed a convention making data of the registre national des personnes physiques the sole official source of voter identification data for the biometric electoral file, as the DGE director-general states ([source, 2026-01-22](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/)).

### Libya: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2025.

- The UN-backed advisory committee recommends limiting candidacy and voter registration in elections to holders of the national number, which administrative-number holders criticise ([source, 2025-06-07](https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/479250)).

### Sao Tome and Principe: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2026.

- The Bilhete de Identidade is to become the base document for automatic and permanent voter registration, updating the electoral rolls from civil registry data, says the Camoes institute on the PReSE project ([source, 2026-03-19](https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe)).
- The EU-funded PReSE project says an interoperability platform, being developed by the Universidade de Aveiro, will link the civil registry databases (SIGA and Bilhete de Identidade) to the electoral roll so voters are registered automatically ([source, 2025-09-01](https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NOTA-DE-IMPRENSA-1.pdf)).

### Sierra Leone: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2026.

- The Electoral Commission said on 10 August 2026 that linking its voter registration with the NCRA identity system would help verify eligible voters and reduce duplication ahead of the 2028 elections ([source, 2026-08-10](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/)).
- NCRA and the Ministry of Agriculture agreed to integrate the National Digital Farmer Registry with the NIN database to verify and de-duplicate farmers ([source, 2025-05-19](http://thesatellitenews.com/index.php/de/announcements/23-news-articles/1730-with-the-national-id-system-for-nin-ncra-ministry-of-agriculture-partner-on-national-digital-farmer-registry-integration)).

### South Africa: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2026.

- Home Affairs built a real-time employee verification portal for National Treasury, linked to the population register, to check government payroll records, going live 15 June 2026 per the department ([source, 2026-06-15](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-builds-digital-verification-platform-national-treasury-combat)).

### Tanzania: 2 Nascent

A public register's use of the national ID number was under way in 2026.

- The NIDA Director General listed the taxpayer identification number (TIN) among the services NIDA plans to connect to the national ID ([source, 2026-09-16](https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047)).
- The Prime Minister directed the Home Affairs minister and NIDA to connect social and economic institutions to the ID system, and NIDA said health insurance and a Jamii Namba holding all social services are to be built on it ([source, 2026-09-16](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344)).
