# Ghana

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Ghana's civil service payroll requires the Ghana Card PIN. [From 10 January 2024 the Mechanized Payroll System uses the PIN to validate public sector employees and pensioners](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>), the National Identification Authority said. Existing staff were to submit their PIN at NIA offices. [In September 2025 Republic Online reported that the revenue authority and the health insurance scheme depend on NIA verification](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>). It said they owed the NIA $377 million and $58 million for it.

**Main registers.** Three of the main registers are on record: the [civil service payroll](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>) the [revenue authority's collection](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>) and the SSNIT social security fund. In September 2025 the NIA said [the Ghana Card is the taxpayer number and the SSNIT number](<https://atlfmnews.com/nia-to-integrate-student-database-with-ghana-card-for-easy-access/>). It is also the health insurance number. In October 2026 the NIA said [National Service Scheme recruits are checked against it](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>). They are checked again before each payment.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [The payroll link was made to validate employees and pensioners](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>). No count of names removed is given.

***Also on file***. [Ghana Card](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>); [Ghana Card / National Identification Register](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).
