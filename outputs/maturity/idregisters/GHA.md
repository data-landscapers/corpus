# Ghana

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** From 10 January 2024 the [civil service payroll, the Mechanized Payroll System, requires the Ghana Card PIN to validate recruited public sector employees and pensioners](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>), with existing staff to submit their PIN at NIA offices, the National Identification Authority says. [Republic Online reports that GRA revenue collection and NHIS member verification depend on NIA biometric verification](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>), with GRA owing $377 million and NHIS $58 million for it.

**Main registers.** Two of the main registers are on record: the [civil service payroll](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>) and the [revenue authority's collection](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>). In October 2026 the NIA's Bedzrah said [people enrolling in the National Service Scheme must undergo NIA verification and are verified again before payments are made](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).

**Matching reported.** The payroll link was made [to validate employees and pensioners against the NIA database](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>); no count of names removed is given.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ghana Card](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>); [Ghana Card / National Identification Register](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).

***Not held***. No electoral, social protection or business register is held using the Ghana Card number, and no figure from checking a register against the ID.
