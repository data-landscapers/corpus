# Ghana

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Ghana's civil service payroll requires the Ghana Card PIN. [From January 2024 the Mechanized Payroll System uses the PIN to validate public sector employees and pensioners](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>), the identity authority said. In February 2026 the Controller and Accountant-General [told staff to verify their Ghana Card details or have salaries suspended](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/02/cagd-verify-ghana-card-details-by-march-15-or-face-salary-suspension/>). Payroll entries are keyed on the card number.

**Main registers.** In September 2025 the identity authority said [the Ghana Card is the taxpayer number, the SSNIT social security number and the health insurance number](<https://atlfmnews.com/nia-to-integrate-student-database-with-ghana-card-for-easy-access/>). The household registry for social protection [collects each registrant's Ghana Card number](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>), its July 2026 privacy policy says. [National Service Scheme recruits are checked against the card](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>) before each payment.

**Matching reported.** In September 2024 the then Vice-President, Mahamudu Bawumia, said [checks against the Ghana Card removed 29,000 ghost names at SSNIT and 44,000 at the National Service Scheme](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghost-names-removed-from-government-payroll-thanks-to-ghana-card-bawumia/>). He put the savings at GH₵400 million and over GH₵300 million.

***Also on file***. [Ghana Card](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>); [Ghana Card / National Identification Register](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).
