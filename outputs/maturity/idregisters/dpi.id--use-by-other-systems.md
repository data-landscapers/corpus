# Use by other systems: use by other systems

*As at 2026-11-08.*

## The norm

The African Union's interoperability framework and Data Policy Framework ask that the national ID be the single source of truth for identity across public registers. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Not used | A source from the past three years says public registers do not use the national ID number. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 One register | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Main registers | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Matching reported | Yes to 4 |

## Countries

### Ghana: 5 Leading

Payroll, tax and social security used the ID number, and 2024 checks removed 73,000 ghost names; no audited payroll figure is published.

- The National Identification Authority says the civil service payroll (the Mechanized Payroll System) now requires the Ghana Card PIN to validate recruited public sector employees and pensioners, with existing staff to submit their PIN at NIA offices, launched 10 January 2024 ([source, 2024-01-10](https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/)).
- The NIA Executive Secretary says the Ghana Card already serves as the Taxpayer Identification Number, SSNIT and NHIS numbers ([source, 2025-09-18](https://atlfmnews.com/nia-to-integrate-student-database-with-ghana-card-for-easy-access/)).
- Mahamudu Bawumia says Ghana Card checks removed 29,000 ghost names at SSNIT (saving GH¢400 million) and 44,000 at the National Service Scheme (over GH¢300 million); these are his own figures ([source, 2024-09-24](https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghost-names-removed-from-government-payroll-thanks-to-ghana-card-bawumia/)).

### Egypt: 4 Operating

The voter roll, payroll tax and the social registry used the national ID number by 2026; no figure from matching is reported.

- The National Elections Authority says voter entries were linked to the national number, and voters are looked up by their 14-digit national number ([source, 2025-09-19](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356)).
- Egyptian Tax Authority head Rasha Abdel Aal said its payroll system calculates employment income tax for 12 million state employees by making the salary statement available by national number ([source, 2026-07-02](https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657)).
- Raafat Shafik of the Ministry of Social Solidarity says the national social registry of 13 million households is linked to 27 government databases through the national ID number ([source, 2026-11-06](https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/)).
- The head of the Egyptian Tax Authority said that from 15 December 2024 every employer must file monthly payroll data on the wage and salary tax system, listing each employee's national number, insurance number and gross salary, under Finance Minister decision 386 of 2023 ([source, 2024-12-15](https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3329890)).

### Malawi: 4 Operating

Voter registration, social protection and payroll rested on the national ID in 2024; results of matching them are not known.

- The EU Delegation says Malawi's National ID underpins voter registration, social protection targeting and payroll management, three main registers ([source, 2024](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/malawi/10-years-fruitful-partnership-government-malawi-civil-registration_en)).

### Nigeria: 4 Operating

The social register, voter registration and tax filing used the national ID number by 2026; no figure from matching is reported.

- The National Social Register used for cash transfers requires a NIN, and beneficiaries without one are disqualified, says the minister's media aide ([source, 2023-10](https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/)).
- NSIPA says NIN is integrated into the NSR database, with 1,628,587 NINs validated by NIMC ([source, 2025](https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html)).
- INEC chairman Amupitan says INEC's online voter registration platform is anchored on the NIN as a verification protocol ([source, 2026-07-16](https://thenationonlineng.net/six-months-to-presidential-poll-inec-raises-preparation-tempo/)).
- Olaniwun Ajayi LP says the NIMC Act 2026 gives statutory basis to requirements, many already in practice, that registering to vote and filing tax returns need a NIN ([source, 2026-06-26](https://oal.law/the-nimc-act-2026-explained-what-nigerias-new-identity-law-means-for-you/)).
- The Federal Inland Revenue Service said that under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act, due to take effect in January 2026, the National Identification Number automatically serves as the Tax ID for individuals ([source, 2026-01](https://guardian.ng/news/nin-cac-number-to-serve-as-tax-id-firs/)).
- President Tinubu said NIMC had linked data with 125 partner agencies, including the Federal Inland Revenue Service for tax records ([source, 2025-09-16](https://guardian.ng/featured/126m-nigerians-secure-nin-database-capacity-hits-250m-fg/)).

### Tanzania: 4 Operating

Tax, social protection and business registration used the national ID number in 2026; no figure from matching registers is known.

- Government spokesman Gerson Msigwa named taxpayer registration, TASAF social services and business registration among services now using the national ID number ([source, 2026-09-02](https://www.jamhurimedia.co.tz/nida-yazindua-kampeni-ya-kipo-chukua-kamilisha-safari-yako/)).
- Government spokesman Gerson Msigwa said use of the national ID number has expanded to taxpayer registration, TASAF social services and business registration, among other services ([source, 2026-09-02](https://www.jamhurimedia.co.tz/nida-yazindua-kampeni-ya-kipo-chukua-kamilisha-safari-yako/)).

### Uganda: 4 Operating

The social grant, the tax records and the voter register used the national ID number by 2026; results of matching them are not known.

- The author says the SAGE social grant only benefits people on the National Identification Register with a national ID, and the education portal EMIS requires a NIN of learners and staff ([source, 2025-09](http://digitalrightsalliance.africa/ugandas-digital-id-barrier-worsens-as-court-delivers-blow-to-access)).
- Uganda Business News, citing the finance state minister to Parliament, reports the Uganda Revenue Authority had linked 2.13 million of its 5.41 million individual taxpayer records to National Identification Numbers by 30 September 2026 ([source, 2026-09-30](https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch)).
- The Daily Monitor says Uganda's national voter register was compiled using biometric data extracted from Nira, the national identification authority ([source, 2025-10-29](https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/one-man-one-vote-inside-new-ec-biometric-system-5249126)).

### Algeria: 3 Established

Social security and tax used the national ID number from 2023; use by the electoral roll or payroll is not known.

- Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that in 2023 the NIN was adopted by all government authorities and partner sectors including justice, banking, social security, taxation and education ([source, 2023](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf)).

### Angola: 3 Established

The electoral roll and the tax register used the identity card number in 2025 and 2026; a third main register is not known.

- Intelwatch says Angola does not run an independent biometric voter registration and its electoral roll is derived from the civil identity database; the 2025 Lei do Recenseamento Eleitoral Oficioso replaces in-person registration with an automatic register extracted from the identity base ([source, 2026-08-15](https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/)).
- Minister Marcy Lopes says the Bilhete de Identidade is the main document for the update of the electoral register (Registo Eleitoral Oficioso) running from 15 June 2026 to 31 March 2027 ([source, 2026-07-15](https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-120-mil-bilhetes-de-identidade-aguardam-levantamento-em-todo-o-pais/)).
- Secretary of State Osvaldo Amaro said the civil identification systems and the tax administration (AGT) are synchronised so that the Bilhete de Identidade number is also the tax identification number (NIF) ([source, 2025-09-18](https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/)).

### Benin: 3 Established

The electoral list and the public pension fund used the national ID number in 2025 and 2026; its use for tax is not known.

- ANIP says the provisional electoral list is extracted directly from the Fichier National de l'État Civil, the same database used to issue the national identity card and the IFU tax identifier ([source, 2025-09-11](https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/)).
- ANIP's director general said Benin's computerised electoral list is extracted directly from the national register of natural persons ([source, 2025-02-21](https://www.gouv.bj/article/2994/inquietudes-autour-fiabilite-liste-electorale-informatisee-benin-anip-reaffirme-credibilite-grace-systeme-mise-jour-continue/)).
- Ecofin Agency reports the public-sector pension fund FNRB's proof-of-life service requires each retiree's NPI alongside the pension booklet number ([source, 2026-10-15](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely)).

### Botswana: 3 Established

### Ethiopia: 3 Established

The tax register was keyed on the national ID in 2026; use by other main registers, and what checking found, are not known.

### Gambia: 3 Established

The civil registration system used the national ID number as its key in 2024; use by the main registers is not known.

### Lesotho: 3 Established

The voters' roll and the payroll used the national ID by 2026; use by a third main register is not known.

- The IEC said its voter re-registration uses National Identification Cards as the sole form of identification, with registration open to all citizens aged 18 and above under the National Identity Card Act ([source, 2024-10](https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/)).

### Liberia: 3 Established

The government payroll was checked against the national ID registry in 2024; the voter register is kept apart.

- The Civil Service Agency integrates National Identification Numbers into government employees' ID cards through the NIR portal, and Sargbe says this has helped remove ghost names from the payroll ([source, 2024-05](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/)).
- The NIR and the finance ministry built an interface to audit the government payroll against the registry as new employees join, shared with the audit and civil service agencies ([source, 2024-02-05](https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams)).
- The Civil Service Agency's 2024 annual report says payroll interventions removed duplicate National Identification Numbers, among other ineligible entries, from the government payroll ([source, 2024-10](https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-frontpage-africa-investigation-uncovers-conflicting-accounts-in-govt-payroll-cleanup/)).

### Libya: 3 Established

Payroll and the electoral roll used the national number in 2025, an audit removing 40,000 salary numbers; a third main register is not known.

- Attorney General Al-Siddiq Al-Sour says checking national ID numbers led to deletion of more than 17,000 non-existent ID numbers along with nearly 40,000 salary numbers that had been linked to them ([source, 2025-12-09](https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/)).
- Libya's High National Elections Commission (HNEC) says voters register for the municipal elections by SMS to 17017 with their national ID number and polling-centre number, and published a voter list of 560,896 registered as of 13 April 2025 ([source, 2025-04-13](https://libyareview.com/54785/560896-voters-registered-for-libyan-municipal-elections/)).

### Mali: 3 Established

### Namibia: 3 Established

### Rwanda: 3 Established

The voters' roll and public-service payroll used the national ID by 2026; a third main register is not known.

- The High Commission of Rwanda in Nigeria says everyone aged 18 or over with a Rwandan national ID card is automatically registered on the voters' list ([source, 2024-05-18](https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150)).

### Sierra Leone: 3 Established

Government payroll was linked to the national ID number by 2024; no source found reports use by other main registers.

### Togo: 3 Established
