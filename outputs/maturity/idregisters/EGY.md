# Egypt

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Egypt's electoral roll is keyed on the 14-digit national number. [In September 2025 the National Elections Authority said voter entries were linked to the national number](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). Voters are looked up by it. [For the 2025 Senate election each voter's entry carried a QR code](<https://www.elections.eg/events-menu/news/202-2025>) to check the national number. [The Public Prosecution records criminal judgments against the national number](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>), it said in July 2026.

**Main registers.** Two main registers use the number. [In July 2026 the head of the Tax Authority said payroll tax for 12 million state employees is worked out from salary statements held by national number](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657>). [A vendor page](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>) says it supports links between the ID database and the social insurance and social solidarity bodies. It does not say those registers use the number.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [The QR codes on the 2025 voter lists](<https://www.elections.eg/events-menu/news/202-2025>) check each voter's national number. No count of corrections is given.

***Also on file***. [national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/egypt-expands-biometric-sim-registration-after-identity-misuse-case>); [National ID System (national number project)](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>).

***Not found***. No third main register has been found using the national number. No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found.
