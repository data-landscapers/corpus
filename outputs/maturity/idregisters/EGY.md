# Egypt

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Egypt's electoral roll is keyed on the 14-digit national number. In September 2025 the National Elections Authority said [voter entries were linked to the national number](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>), and voters are looked up by it. [The Public Prosecution records criminal judgments against the national number](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>), it said in July 2026.

**Main registers.** Since 15 December 2024 [every employer files monthly payroll data on the wage tax system](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3329890>), listing each employee's national number. In July 2026 the Tax Authority said [payroll tax for 12 million state employees is worked out from salary statements held by national number](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657>). In November 2026 the social solidarity ministry spoke of the national social registry of 13 million households. It said [the registry is linked to 27 government databases through the national number](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>).

**Matching reported.** The ministry said [the links are used to verify registrants' data and target support](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). It gave no count of records corrected. [The QR codes on the 2025 voter lists](<https://www.elections.eg/events-menu/news/202-2025>) check each voter's national number.

***Also on file***. [national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/egypt-expands-biometric-sim-registration-after-identity-misuse-case>); [National ID System (national number project)](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>).
