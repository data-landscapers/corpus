# Tanzania

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [NIDA says 8.4 million taxpayer numbers had been registered using national ID numbers](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>) by September 2026. [Its May 2026 slides list the revenue authority's taxpayer registration among 147 or more institutions joined to the ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). More are planned. [The Prime Minister told NIDA to connect social and economic institutions to the ID system](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). Health insurance and a single social services number, Jamii Namba, are to be built on it.

**Main registers.** [In September 2026 the government spokesman said the ID number was used for taxpayer registration](<https://www.jamhurimedia.co.tz/nida-yazindua-kampeni-ya-kipo-chukua-kamilisha-safari-yako/>), TASAF social services and business registration. [NIDA names the same three](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>): tax numbers, TASAF beneficiary checks and business registration by BRELA.

**Matching reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure from checking a register against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>).
