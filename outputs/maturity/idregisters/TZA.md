# Tanzania

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [The NIDA Director General listed the taxpayer identification number (TIN) among the services NIDA plans to connect to the national ID](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>). [The Prime Minister directed the Home Affairs minister and NIDA to connect social and economic institutions to the ID system, and NIDA said health insurance and a Jamii Namba holding all social services are to be built on it](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [NIDA's May 2026 slides list the Tanzania Revenue Authority's taxpayer registration among 147 or more institutions integrated with the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Main registers.** [In September 2026 government spokesman Gerson Msigwa said use of the national ID number had spread to taxpayer registration, TASAF social services and business registration](<https://www.jamhurimedia.co.tz/nida-yazindua-kampeni-ya-kipo-chukua-kamilisha-safari-yako/>).
