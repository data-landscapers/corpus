# Somalia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Somalia plans to link its government payroll to the national ID. [In August 2026 the Office of the Accountant General directed ministries and agencies to join the payroll to NIRA's identification system](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-to-national-id-by-end-of-2027>). A pilot in five institutions was to start on 1 October 2026. Full use is set for 31 December 2027. [In January 2025 NIRA's director general said the electoral commission is to use the ID card to check voters](<https://www.bbc.com/somali/articles/c1kmzpg73xeo>).

**Main registers.** A World Bank team told the EU asylum agency that by March 2026 [the national ID card was required for business services](<https://www.euaa.europa.eu/somalia-displacement-mogadishu/43-access-services>), among other uses. No main register is shown keyed on the number.
