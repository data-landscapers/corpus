# Tunisia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Tunisia links its sectoral identifiers to the citizen's unique identifier, the IUC. [The 2026 country playbook says the IUC register holds health, social and pupil identifiers in a correspondence table](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). Ministries add or update their identifiers through web services. [A 2024 deck describes the same table](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>) as the route from sectoral identifiers to the IUC.

**Main registers.** No main register is named as using the IUC. [The playbook](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>) names the social identifier. It does not name the social registry, the tax register, the payroll, the electoral roll or the business register.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [The 2026 playbook](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>) gives no count of identifiers linked or of records corrected through the table.
