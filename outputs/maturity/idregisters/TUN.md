[The deck says the move from sectoral identifiers (social, education, health) to the new IUC is done through a central correspondence table](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
