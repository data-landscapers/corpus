# Gambia

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** The Gambia's civil registration system uses the national identification number, the NIN. [In March 2024 the World Bank said the eCRVS had become the interoperability system across ministries](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>), with the NIN as the common key. [Under a March 2024 agreement the Interior Ministry is to share issued-ID data with the Health Ministry](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>). The aim is to clean duplicate NINs from the eCRVS and reach one NIN across the two systems.

**Main registers.** No main register is shown using the NIN. [The World Bank's account](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>) names no electoral roll, tax register, payroll, social registry or business register.
