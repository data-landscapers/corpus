[NCRA and the Ministry of Agriculture agreed to integrate the National Digital Farmer Registry with the NIN database to verify and de-duplicate farmers](<http://thesatellitenews.com/index.php/de/announcements/23-news-articles/1730-with-the-national-id-system-for-nin-ncra-ministry-of-agriculture-partner-on-national-digital-farmer-registry-integration>). [In April 2024 an NCRA official said the NIN is linked to government payroll, among other government services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202404/sierra-leone-putting-digital-id-at-the-center-of-its-digital-transformation-agenda>).
