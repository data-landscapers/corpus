# Sierra Leone

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** [In April 2024 an NCRA official said the NIN is linked to government payroll](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202404/sierra-leone-putting-digital-id-at-the-center-of-its-digital-transformation-agenda>), among other government services. Other registers are joining. [NCRA and the agriculture ministry agreed to link the farmer registry to the NIN database](<http://thesatellitenews.com/index.php/de/announcements/23-news-articles/1730-with-the-national-id-system-for-nin-ncra-ministry-of-agriculture-partner-on-national-digital-farmer-registry-integration>), to verify and de-duplicate farmers. [On 10 August 2026 the Electoral Commission said it would link voter registration with the NCRA identity system](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>) before the 2028 elections. [In November 2026 the National Investment Board was working with NCRA to put the NIN into business registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-nib-online-business-registration/>).

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the Electoral Commission planned to link voter registration with the NCRA identity system](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>).

**Matching reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure from checking a register against the ID. The nearest fact: [the Electoral Commission expects the link to cut duplicate voters before the 2028 elections](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>).
