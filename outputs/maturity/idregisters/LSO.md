# Lesotho

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Lesotho registers voters on the national identity card. [In October 2024 the electoral commission said re-registration accepts the national ID card as the only identification](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [In July 2026 it said its voter registration system is linked to the national identity register](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>) through unique identification numbers. [In September 2025 the minister said legal identity had strengthened the payroll and removed ghost workers](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/>).

**Main registers.** Two main registers are shown at most: the voters' roll and the payroll. [The minister's 2025 statement](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/>) gives no detail of how the payroll uses the number. No source found since October 2023 shows the tax register, a social registry or the business register using it.

**Matching reported.** No figure from checking a register against the ID has been found. [The minister said ghost workers had been removed](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/>). He gave no count.

***Also on file***. [Digital/service identification system](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-first-introduced-new-digital-service-identification-system/>); [MOSIP-based national digital identity ecosystem](<https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows a third main register using the ID number. No figure from checking a register against it has been found.
