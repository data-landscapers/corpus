# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** ONECI's director general said in June 2025 that [a decree requires administrative procedures to go through the national identification number, NNI](<https://digitalmag.ci/ago-christian-kodia-le-numero-national-didentification-nni-est-lelement-principal-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite/>). The NNI is the key of the national register of persons. Close to 10 million people had been given one.

**Main registers.** In the same interview he said [taxpayer accounts, social security numbers and health insurance numbers are to be linked to the NNI](<https://digitalmag.ci/ago-christian-kodia-le-numero-national-didentification-nni-est-lelement-principal-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite/>) at very short notice. So are employees' staff numbers in companies. He asked the information system heads of public bodies to build the register and the NNI into their systems.

**Matching reported.** The interview [gives no figure from checking a register against the NNI](<https://digitalmag.ci/ago-christian-kodia-le-numero-national-didentification-nni-est-lelement-principal-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite/>). It names no register already checked against the national register of persons.
