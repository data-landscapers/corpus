# Togo

## Use by other systems

**Register keyed on ID.** Togo is building its social registry on the national e-ID. [A 2025 government presentation says the Social Registry of Individuals and Households, RSPM, was in development and initial integration](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>). It uses the e-ID as its unique key. No source found shows the registry in service. [A 2026 deck lists the number's integration across health, education, civil registration, social protection and pensions](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>) as a step in its adoption.

**Main registers.** No source found since October 2023 shows three or more main public registers using the national ID number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).

**Matching reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure from checking a register against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).
