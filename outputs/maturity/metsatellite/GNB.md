# Guinea-Bissau

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [INM-GB states it completed installation of a CLIMSA/PUMA2025 station on 21-25 August 2026, tested and accepted, receiving EUMETSAT Meteosat Third Generation data in near real time at up to 10-minute intervals](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/>).
