# Guinea-Bissau

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Guinea-Bissau among eight member states [still waiting for a PUMA station](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The national meteorology institute INM-GB then [completed installing a ClimSA and PUMA2025 station](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/>) on 21 to 25 August 2026. It says the station was tested and accepted. It receives Meteosat Third Generation data in near real time, as often as every ten minutes. Nothing found says forecasts use the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 INM-GB began receiving Meteosat Third Generation data in near real time](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest fact: [INM-GB tested and accepted its new CLIMSA/PUMA2025 station in August 2026](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/nova-esta%C3%A7%C3%A3o-climsa-e-puma2025/>).

***Also on file***. [Common Alerting Protocol (CAP)](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/guiné-bissau-dá-passo-histórico-na-modernização-dos-sistemas-de-alerta-com-a-operacionalização-do-cap/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
