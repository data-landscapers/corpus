# Meteorological use of satellite data: satellite data in weather services

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The Integrated African Strategy on Meteorology asks for sustained access to geostationary and polar-orbiting satellites, WMO's observing standards and ISO 9001 certification for every national weather service. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |

| 2 Receiving | Yes to 1 |

## Countries

### Burkina Faso: 4 Operating

The national weather service issued warnings drawing on satellite data in 2025; ISO 9001 certification is not on record.

- ANAM states that the 10-minute satellite data reception is decisive for tracking fast weather events and for issuing more responsive early alerts, and that the images help early detection of violent storms ([source, 2025-08](https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/)).

### Ethiopia: 4 Operating

The national weather service issued warnings drawing on satellite data in 2026; ISO 9001 certification is not on record.

- The Ethiopian Meteorological Institute's page carries a Rapidly Developing Thunderstorms convection warning product for nowcasting, produced with NWC-SAF software ([source, 2026-08-28](https://www.ethiomet.gov.et/satellite-imagery/)).

### Libya: 4 Operating

The national weather service issued warnings drawing on satellite data in 2024; ISO 9001 certification is not on record.

- The National Meteorological Centre says it issued and disseminated early warnings to public sectors and the media 48 hours and 72 hours ahead of the two August 2024 episodes, whose assessments drew on satellite images, and updated them more than once ([source, 2024-08-20](https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc)).

### Senegal: 4 Operating

The national weather service issued warnings drawing on satellite data in 2026; ISO 9001 certification is not on record.

- ANACIM's head of forecasting says MTG Lightning Imager data are critical during the rainy season for alerting communities to extreme weather ([source, 2026-05-18](http://www.eumetsat.int/features/forecasting-storm-speed-across-africa)).

### Tunisia: 4 Operating

The national weather service issued warnings drawing on satellite data in 2024; ISO 9001 certification is not on record.

- The Institut National de la Meteorologie presented the role of EUMETSAT products in supporting Tunisia's flood and drought early warning systems ([source, 2024-09-16](https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf)).

### Lesotho: 3 Established

The national weather service used satellite data in its forecasts in 2023; warnings drawing on it are not on record.

- A 2023 project by the provider deployed a Lesotho nowcasting system integrating meteorological observations, satellite data and model outputs, with an internal portal for meteorological service staff ([source, 2023](https://wisconsultancy.com/projects/)).

### South Sudan: 3 Established

The national weather service used satellite data in its forecasts in 2025; warnings drawing on it are not on record.

- WMO reports South Sudan Meteorological Services forecasters were trained in Juba to analyze satellite data and began a pilot of daily forecasts verified each day against EUMETSAT satellite information ([source, 2025-06](https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership)).

### Burundi: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2026; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- IGEBU's Images satellitaires viewer shows Meteosat Second Generation layers over the Indian Ocean (precipitation rate, SEVIRI IR3.9, convection RGB) on automatic update ([source, 2026-08-28](https://www.igebu.bi/data-tools/)).

### Cameroon: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2026; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- The national meteorological service's own page describes access to EUMETSAT NWCSAF satellite-based nowcasting products (0-6 hours) with real-time satellite data visualisation via the ADAguc platform ([source, 2026-01-20](https://meteocameroon.gov.cm/en/nwcsaf/)).

### Central African Republic: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- ASECNA's AIP lists METEOSAT (MSG) satellite images among the supplementary equipment of the Bangui/M'Poko Centre Meteorologique Principal, which also offers satellite imagery on request in flight documentation ([source, 2025-08-07](https://aim.asecna.aero/html/eAIP/FR-_04AD-2.FEFF-fr-FR.html)).

### Gambia: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2024; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- The meteorology unit of the Department of Water Resources received and had installed Meteosat Third Generation PUMA and ClimSA stations at a handover on 30 August 2024, enabling continued reception of MTG data and satellite imagery, funded by the EU through the African Union Commission ([source, 2024-08-30](https://meteogambia.gm/news/department-of-water-resources-of-the-gambia-receives-advanced-meteosat-third-generation-puma-and-climsa-stations/)).

### Kenya: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- EUMETSAT says its SEWA project will sustain and upgrade the Regional ATOVS Retransmission Services network by modernising ground stations in Gabon, Kenya, Niger and South Africa for polar-orbiting satellite data ([source, 2025-10-24](http://www.eumetsat.int/strengthening-early-warning-africa-sewa)).

### Liberia: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- The Liberia Meteorological Service, with Tecnavia SA, completed installation of the EU-funded ClimSA/PUMA2025 Meteosat Third Generation system at Roberts International Airport on 24 October 2025, to receive MTG model data and high-resolution satellite imagery ([source, 2025-10-24](https://meteoliberia.com/news/liberia-meteorological-service-lms-installs-climsapuma2025-meteosat-third-generation-system-at-the-robert-international-airport/)).

### Malawi: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- DCCMS says it completed installation of the PUMA 2025 satellite reception system at its Blantyre headquarters, replacing PUMA 2015 and giving access to Meteosat Third Generation products ([source, 2025-08-01](https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/)).

### Mauritania: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- The African Union Commission says its ad hoc committee inspected ClimSA and PUMA infrastructure deployed and installed in Nouakchott, Mauritania, the reception and processing chain for Meteosat Second and Third Generation data from EUMETSAT ([source, 2025-09-23](https://au.int/sites/default/files/pressreleases/45397-pr-Press_Release_Deployment_Final.pdf)).

### Niger: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2026; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- Niger's national meteorological directorate (DMN) runs a live satellite imagery page serving EUMETView/Meteosat products (Natural Colour Enhanced RGB, GEO/LEO-blended precipitation rate, Dust RGB for dust-storm monitoring) ([source, 2026-03](https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/)).

### Nigeria: 2 Nascent

The national weather service was receiving or installing a satellite data stream in 2025; its use in forecasts is not on record.

- NiMet says it completed installation of the PUMA 2025 / ClimSA Meteosat Third Generation satellite receiving station at its headquarters on 9 May 2025, replacing the earlier MSG 2015 station ([source, 2025-05-09](https://nimet.gov.ng/news?id=123)).
