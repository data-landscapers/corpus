# Lesotho

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Lesotho among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. In 2023 a consultancy [deployed a nowcasting system](<https://wisconsultancy.com/projects/>) for the Lesotho Meteorological Service, combining observations, satellite data and model output. No later source says it still works.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [2023 nowcasting project](<https://wisconsultancy.com/projects/>), which built an internal portal for the service's staff.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>) of PUMA stations.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts.
