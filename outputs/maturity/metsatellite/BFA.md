# Burkina Faso

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In August 2025 the national meteorology agency ANAM [said its new PUMA 2025 station](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>) receives Meteosat Third Generation data every ten minutes, not fifteen. ANAM says the [sharper images raise the precision of its forecasts](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>). In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Burkina Faso among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** ANAM says the ten-minute data [help it issue more responsive early alerts](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>). The images also help it spot violent storms early, when weather moves fast. It describes the station as part of a wider modernisation of its observing network.

**ISO 9001 certified.** A 2025 ANAM presentation to a cooperation meeting says the agency [obtained ISO 9001:2015 certification](<https://climateservices.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2_SLAPIS_Coop-et-Meteo-Sawadogo.pdf>) that year. The same deck says it has run a weather model at three-kilometre resolution on a high-performance computer since 2020.
