# Burkina Faso

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [ANAM states that the PUMA 2025 and CLIMSA stations strengthen its forecasting capacity and that the higher-resolution satellite images raise the precision of its short- and medium-term forecasts](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>). [ANAM states that its newly installed PUMA 2025 station lets it receive real-time data and images from the third-generation MTG meteorological satellite, with reception every 10 minutes instead of 15](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>).

**Warnings issued.** [ANAM states that the 10-minute satellite data reception is decisive for tracking fast weather events and for issuing more responsive early alerts, and that the images help early detection of violent storms](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://meteoburkina.bf/news/lanam-poursuit-la-modernisation-de-ses-infrastructures-des-stations-de-derni%C3%A8re-g%C3%A9n%C3%A9ration-pour-mieux-servir-le-burkina-faso/>).
