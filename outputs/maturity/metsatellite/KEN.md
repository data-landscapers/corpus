# Kenya

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In August 2024 the Kenya Meteorological Department says [the African Union Commission installed PUMA 2025 and ClimSA stations](<https://meteo.go.ke/news/puma-and-climsa-stations-tot-workshop/>) at its offices. They capture Meteosat Third Generation data. EUMETSAT says the [Nairobi station was the first PUMA-2025 receiver](<https://www.meteorologicaltechnologyinternational.com/news/satellites/eumetsat-installs-receiving-stations-for-kenyas-meteosat-third-generation-geostationary-satellites.html>) installed in Africa, under a 2022 agreement with the African Union. A November 2024 workshop trained staff to use the stations. In 2025 EUMETSAT also planned to [upgrade a regional polar-satellite ground station in Kenya](<http://www.eumetsat.int/strengthening-early-warning-africa-sewa>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The department's [account of the workshop](<https://meteo.go.ke/news/puma-and-climsa-stations-tot-workshop/>) calls the stations vital for early warning, but does not say warnings draw on them.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification.
