# Ghana

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In November 2025 the Ghana Meteorological Agency [received PUMA-2025 and ClimSA stations](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3202/ghana-meteorological-agency-enhances-forecasting-capabilities-with-new-eu-funded-climsa-systems>) under the EU-funded ClimSA project, TechReviewAfrica reported. They give access to Meteosat Third Generation images every ten minutes, for faster forecasts. Staff were trained in forecasting and running the system. A 2022 paper had already described [satellite nowcasting in use at the agency](<https://doi.org/10.22541/au.165712587.76658353/v1>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is from 2022, when the agency [sent nowcasts and impact-based forecasts to stakeholders](<https://doi.org/10.22541/au.165712587.76658353/v1>) for early warning.

**ISO 9001 certified.** In March 2025 the agency [received ISO 9001 re-certification](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/03/gmet-receives-international-organization-for-standardization-re-certification/>) from the International Certification Group of the UK, the Ghana News Agency reported. The audit closed gaps found at the first stage.
