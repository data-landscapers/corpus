# Liberia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** On 24 October 2025 the Liberia Meteorological Service and Tecnavia [completed installing a ClimSA and PUMA2025 system](<https://meteoliberia.com/news/liberia-meteorological-service-lms-installs-climsapuma2025-meteosat-third-generation-system-at-the-robert-international-airport/>) at Roberts International Airport. Funded by the EU, it receives Meteosat Third Generation model data and high-resolution imagery. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Liberia among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). Nothing found says the service's forecasts use the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.
