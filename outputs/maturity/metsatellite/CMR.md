# Cameroon

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** Cameroon's national meteorological service [describes access to EUMETSAT nowcasting products](<https://meteocameroon.gov.cm/en/nwcsaf/>) on its own site. They cover the next six hours, with satellite data shown in real time. On 22 July 2025 its National Meteorological Directorate [began installing a PUMA-2025 reception station](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/2530/cameroon-takes-major-leap-in-weather-forecasting-with-puma-2025-station-installation>), TechReviewAfrica reported. The station receives Meteosat Third Generation data every ten minutes. No other source on the subject was found.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [TechReviewAfrica report of the July 2025 installation](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/2530/cameroon-takes-major-leap-in-weather-forecasting-with-puma-2025-station-installation>), which does not mention certification.
