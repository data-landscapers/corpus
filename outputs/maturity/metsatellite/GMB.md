# Gambia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** At a handover on 30 August 2024 the meteorology unit of the Department of Water Resources [received Meteosat Third Generation PUMA and ClimSA stations](<https://meteogambia.gm/news/department-of-water-resources-of-the-gambia-receives-advanced-meteosat-third-generation-puma-and-climsa-stations/>). They were installed to receive MTG data and imagery, with EU funds through the African Union Commission. In May 2026 the Commission listed Gambia among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). Nothing found says the unit's forecasts use the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
