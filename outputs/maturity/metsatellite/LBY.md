# Libya

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [The Libya National Meteorological Service said its assessment of the extreme rain of August 2024 rested on satellite images and numerical models](<https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc>). [The rain fell in the southwest and southeast](<https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc>).

**Warnings issued.** [The National Meteorological Centre said it issued early warnings 48 and 72 hours ahead of the two August 2024 episodes](<https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc>). [It sent them to public sectors and the media and updated them more than once](<https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc>). [Its assessments of the episodes drew on satellite images](<https://wmo.int/media/news-from-members/parts-of-libya-affected-extreme-weather-lnmc>).
