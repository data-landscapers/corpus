# Mali

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** On 10 March 2026 Mali-Meteo [commissioned PUMA 2025 and ClimSA equipment](<https://fr.apanews.net/disasters/mali-meteo-dotee-dun-systeme-satellitaire-de-nouvelle-generation/>), APAnews reported. It can now receive Meteosat Third Generation data in real time for its analysis and forecasts. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Mali among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). No other news story read for this study says more.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [APAnews report of the March 2026 commissioning](<https://fr.apanews.net/disasters/mali-meteo-dotee-dun-systeme-satellitaire-de-nouvelle-generation/>), which does not mention certification.
