# Botswana

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In December 2023 China [donated a P31 million transportable satellite ground system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76992>) to the Department of Meteorological Services, the government's Daily News reported. A memorandum between the two governments was still to be signed. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Botswana among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations receive satellite imagery. Nothing found says the department's forecasts use it.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Daily News report of the 2023 donation](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76992>), which does not mention certification.
