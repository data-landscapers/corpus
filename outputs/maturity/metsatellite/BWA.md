# Botswana

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [In December 2023 China, through the China Academy of Space, donated a P31 million multi-satellite meteorological transportable ground system to the Department of Meteorological Services](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76992>), the government's Daily News reported from COP28. The minister signed the cooperation agreement at a ceremony with several developing countries; a memorandum between the two governments was still to be signed. A donated station sets stage 2. Nothing found says it has been installed, receives data, or feeds the forecasts the department issues.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the donation given above](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76992>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the donation given above](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/76992>).
