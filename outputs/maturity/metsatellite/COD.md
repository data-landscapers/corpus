# DR Congo

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [In February 2024 a METTELSAT document hosted by WMO said the agency has PUMA 2015 and MESSIR NEO equipment](<https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2024-02/4.%20Syst%C3%A8me%20d%E2%80%99Observation%20de%20la%20METTELSAT.pdf>). [It is connected to the EUMETCast Africa data stream](<https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2024-02/4.%20Syst%C3%A8me%20d%E2%80%99Observation%20de%20la%20METTELSAT.pdf>). [It receives satellite images and regional and global model data](<https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2024-02/4.%20Syst%C3%A8me%20d%E2%80%99Observation%20de%20la%20METTELSAT.pdf>). [In July 2024 a MeteoSwiss peer review said METTELSAT's PUMA reception system at its headquarters is functional](<https://meteordcongo.cd/documents/14/DRC-CHD-Report-NGA-Annex_2.pdf>). [It gives access to EUMETCast](<https://meteordcongo.cd/documents/14/DRC-CHD-Report-NGA-Annex_2.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [in February 2024 METTELSAT said it receives satellite images over EUMETCast Africa](<https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2024-02/4.%20Syst%C3%A8me%20d%E2%80%99Observation%20de%20la%20METTELSAT.pdf>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [in July 2024 a MeteoSwiss peer review examined METTELSAT's satellite reception](<https://meteordcongo.cd/documents/14/DRC-CHD-Report-NGA-Annex_2.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts.
