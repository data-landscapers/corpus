# DR Congo

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [The MeteoSwiss peer review says METTELSAT has a functional PUMA satellite reception system at its headquarters giving access to EUMETCast](<https://meteordcongo.cd/documents/14/DRC-CHD-Report-NGA-Annex_2.pdf>). [The WMO-hosted METTELSAT document says the agency has PUMA 2015 and MESSIR NEO equipment connected to the EUMETCast Africa data stream to receive satellite images and regional and global model data](<https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2024-02/4.%20Syst%C3%A8me%20d%E2%80%99Observation%20de%20la%20METTELSAT.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://meteordcongo.cd/documents/14/DRC-CHD-Report-NGA-Annex_2.pdf>).
