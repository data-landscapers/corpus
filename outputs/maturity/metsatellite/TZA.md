# Tanzania

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** A 2024 DMI peer review says the Tanzania Meteorological Authority [relies on EUMETSAT satellite imagery and radars](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>) for its forecasts. They help most with nowcasting. In 2025 the National Audit Office found the authority [struggled to process satellite data](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Management_of_Meteorological_Services.pdf>), because the sources lacked local detail. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Tanzania among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the authority issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is its [ISO 9001:2015 certification](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>), which covers aeronautical services only.

**ISO 9001 certified.** The same peer review says the authority [holds ISO 9001:2015 certification](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>) for its aeronautical meteorological services only. It passed a re-audit in January 2024.

***Also on file***. [Data Processing and Forecasting System (DPFS)](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Management_of_Meteorological_Services.pdf>); [Weather radars](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
