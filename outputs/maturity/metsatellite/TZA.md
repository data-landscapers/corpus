# Tanzania

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [Tanzania's National Audit Office reports that the Tanzania Meteorological Authority faced challenges in processing data from satellites and global observation centres because these sources lacked adequate localization](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Management_of_Meteorological_Services.pdf>). [The DMI peer review for SOFF says the Tanzania Meteorological Authority relies on EUMETSAT satellite imagery and radars, which are particularly beneficial for nowcasting, to deliver its forecasts](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [The DMI peer review says the Tanzania Meteorological Authority holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for its aeronautical meteorological services only, successfully re-audited in January 2024](<http://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CHD-Tanzania-official-version.pdf>).
