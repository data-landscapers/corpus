# Egypt

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In September 2026 the head of forecasting and early warning at the Egyptian Meteorological Authority described [satellites as a key part of its observation system](<https://www.egyptke.com/293734>). He said third-generation satellites send images about every two and a half minutes. They help track clouds, fog and dust storms for forecasts. A 2014 training book already listed [Meteosat and polar-orbiter reception](<http://ema.gov.eg/pdf/Training%20book-may%202014_final%201-8.pdf>) at the authority.

**Warnings issued.** The same official said the authority [has run an early warning system since 2020](<https://www.egyptke.com/293734>). It combines observations, satellites among them, with eight global numerical models. Warnings go to citizens and public agencies.

**ISO 9001 certified.** The Ministry of Civil Aviation's review of 2025 says the authority [renewed its ISO 9001:2015 certification](<https://www.civilaviation.gov.eg/Media/NewsDetails?NewsID=1620&title=%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF+%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A6%D8%A9+%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9+%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF+%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9+%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9+%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85+2025>) that year. It also renewed environmental and safety certificates.
