# Sao Tome and Principe

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed São Tomé and Príncipe among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. The Commission lists satellite imagery among the data the stations carry. No national source read for this study describes the station or its use.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records the station and not its use.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the same [list of PUMA stations](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts.
