# Tunisia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [In September 2024 the Institut National de la Meteorologie said it has used Meteosat data since the 1980s](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>). [It has progressively upgraded its receiving and exploitation systems](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>). [The institute presented satellite imagery and cloud-top alert products as improving its nowcasting](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>). [The same products improve its real-time risk management of convective storms](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** [In September 2024 the institute presented the role of EUMETSAT products in supporting Tunisia's early warning systems](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>). [The systems warn of floods and of droughts](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2025-01/Report%20of%20the%2016th%20EUMETSAT%20User%20Forum%20in%20Africa%20-%20EN%20-%20FINAL_1.pdf>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification.
