# Malawi

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In April 2025 the Department of Climate Change and Meteorological Services said it uses the [Flash Flood Guidance System](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/flash-flood-warnings-what-you-need-to-know-before-its-too-late/>). It combines satellite rainfall estimates with ground observations and hydrological models into live flood-risk maps. In August 2025 the department [installed a PUMA 2025 reception system](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/>) at its Blantyre headquarters. The African Union Commission listed Malawi in May 2026 among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** The department says [its flash flood warnings are produced with tools](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/flash-flood-warnings-what-you-need-to-know-before-its-too-late/>) that include that guidance system. In 2025 it also [piloted a drought tool, PRISM, in five districts](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>), which uses satellite imagery.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [department's August 2025 account](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/>) of the new station.
