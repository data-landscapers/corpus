# Malawi

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [DCCMS says it completed installation of the PUMA 2025 satellite reception system at its Blantyre headquarters, replacing PUMA 2015 and giving access to Meteosat Third Generation products](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/>). In August 2025 the department [piloted WFP's PRISM drought-monitoring tool in five districts](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>), which its officials say combines satellite imagery with rainfall, soil moisture and vegetation data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [PRISM pilot](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>), which officials say gives real-time insight into drought in the five districts where it runs.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.metmalawi.gov.mw/news/dccms-enhances-weather-monitoring-capabilities-with-climsa-satellite-system-upgrade/>).
