# Eritrea

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Eritrea among eight member states [still waiting for a PUMA station](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. The contract covers all 49 member states, and 41 had a station by then. The list does not say when Eritrea's will arrive. No national source read for this study describes satellite data at the weather service.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a station still to come.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the same [list of PUMA stations](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).
