# Somalia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [The SCAA says it launched a satellite weather system with an MTG receiver in Mogadishu giving near real-time data for monitoring and forecasting, replacing a decade-old platform](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/07/02/somalia-launches-advanced-satellite-system-to-boost-aviation-weather-monitoring/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Warnings issued.** No source held since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/07/02/somalia-launches-advanced-satellite-system-to-boost-aviation-weather-monitoring/>).
