# Somalia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In July 2025 Somalia's civil aviation authority [launched a satellite weather system with an MTG receiver](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/07/02/somalia-launches-advanced-satellite-system-to-boost-aviation-weather-monitoring/>) in Mogadishu. It gives near real-time data for monitoring and forecasting, and replaces a platform ten years old. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Somalia among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). No other source on the subject was found.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [authority's July 2025 launch notice](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/07/02/somalia-launches-advanced-satellite-system-to-boost-aviation-weather-monitoring/>), which does not mention certification.
