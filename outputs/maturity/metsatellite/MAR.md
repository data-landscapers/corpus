# Morocco

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In December 2025 Morocco's meteorology directorate DGM said [its observation draws on satellite images refreshed every ten minutes](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/de-lobservation-aux-alertes-comment-la-dgm-prepare-ses-bulletins-meteo_74T4ZDGOOZEBTD4RQVUTJNIYPE/>). In January 2025 it [announced Meteosat Third Generation data](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/anticipation-des-risques-meteorologiques-la-dgm-se-dote-du-systeme-meteosat-3eme-generation>), replacing the second generation. In March 2025 a DGM spokesman told SNRT News it had [bought third-generation satellite receivers](<https://snrtnews.com/article/117313>). They came with 400 automatic stations and 12 radars.

**Warnings issued.** The spokesman said the new equipment, satellite receivers included, [lets the DGM issue accurate warning bulletins](<https://snrtnews.com/article/117313>). He linked the investment to flash floods in the south-east in the summer of 2024.

**ISO 9001 certified.** On 29 October 2025 the DGM [ordered a second follow-up audit](<https://tenderly.ma/marches-attribues/audit-de-suivi-n2-pour-le-maintien-de-la-certification-du-systeme-290704>) to keep its ISO 9001:2015 certification. The order, worth MAD 48,000, went to Certi-Trust Africa and Middle East.
