# Algeria

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [On 23 March 2026 Boualem Sayah of the Office National de la Météorologie said](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-l-onm-muscle-ses-moyens-pour-des-previsions-meteo-plus-precises/>) Algeria exchanges millions of data a day with the network of the World Meteorological Organization. They come from land stations, ships, aircraft and new-generation satellites. He said these data track air movement, cloud formation and storm zones, and make the office's numerical models finer.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since November 2023 says the office's warnings draw on satellite data. [The same official said better observation improves early warnings](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-l-onm-muscle-ses-moyens-pour-des-previsions-meteo-plus-precises/>), and that the office's vigilance map was extended beyond 24 hours in 2024.

**ISO 9001 certified.** [In April 2026 the office announced the renewal of its ISO 9001:2015 certification](<https://www.almasdar-dz.com/178184-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%8A%D8%B2%D9%88-9001-2015-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A>). The certificate covers its aeronautical weather services. It has been renewed ten years running.
