# Congo

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Congo among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. Brazzaville had an earlier one: in September 2004 [a PUMA station for Meteosat Second Generation data was inaugurated](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2020-04/pdf_conf_6auf_report_en.pdf>) at Maya-Maya airport. No source read for this study says how the weather service uses the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records the station and not its use.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [EUMETSAT report of 2004](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2020-04/pdf_conf_6auf_report_en.pdf>) on the first station.
