# Chad

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** WMO reports that work with EUMETSAT and Spain's AEMET [let Chad deploy satellite-based nowcasting](<https://wmo.int/media/news/strengthening-early-warning-services-chad>). It tracks storms a few hours before they hit. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Chad among eight member states [still waiting for a PUMA station](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). No other source on the subject was found.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [WMO's 2026 account of nowcasting in Chad](<https://wmo.int/media/news/strengthening-early-warning-services-chad>), which does not mention certification.
