# Comoros

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Comoros among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. In 2019 the Comoros meteorological service said it [received the EUMETCast stream through Synergie and PUMA stations](<https://severeweather.wmo.int/TCFW/RAI_Training2019/Comoros.pdf>). No source since then says how it uses the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. In 2019 the agency ANACM said it [issued cyclone, heavy rain and flood warnings using that stream](<https://severeweather.wmo.int/TCFW/RAI_Training2019/Comoros.pdf>), but nothing since repeats it.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>) of PUMA stations.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts or warnings.
