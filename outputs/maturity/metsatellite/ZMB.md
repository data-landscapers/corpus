# Zambia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In October 2025 Zambia's Minister of Green Economy and Environment [told the National Assembly](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12604>) of new investment in the weather service. It includes high-performance computers and advanced satellite receiving systems. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Zambia among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). Nothing found says the forecasts use the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [minister's October 2025 statement to the National Assembly](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12604>), which does not mention certification.
