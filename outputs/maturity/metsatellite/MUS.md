# Mauritius

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In September 2026 the acting director of Mauritius Meteorological Services [listed MSG and MTG satellite imagery](<https://archive.uneca.org/archive2/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/CFND/RESIsland/Mauritius/mms_resisland.pdf>) among the service's working tools. The same deck dates the first satellite image reception to 1968. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Mauritius among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). Older reports describe [Meteosat and polar-orbiter reception](<https://metservice.intnet.mu/pdfs/technical-note-on-cyclone-season.pdf>) since the 1990s.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is from 2007, when the service [prepared cyclone warnings from satellite imagery](<https://metservice.intnet.mu/pdfs/technical-note-on-cyclone-season.pdf>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The [September 2026 deck](<https://archive.uneca.org/archive2/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/CFND/RESIsland/Mauritius/mms_resisland.pdf>) calls the service ISO certified since 2008, without naming the standard.

***Also on file***. [GCF FP161 regional early warning project](<https://www.greenclimate.fund/portfolio/projects/fp161>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
