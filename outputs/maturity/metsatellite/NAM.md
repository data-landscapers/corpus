# Namibia

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Namibia among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. In July 2018 China [handed over FY-3 satellite data receiving and processing systems](<https://na.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/dsxx/hdjh/201807/t20180711_7709077.htm>) to the Namibia Meteorological Service. No source since then says how the service uses satellite data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records the station and not its use.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Chinese embassy's 2018 account](<https://na.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/dsxx/hdjh/201807/t20180711_7709077.htm>) of the FY-3 handover.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts.
