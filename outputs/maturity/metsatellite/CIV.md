# Cote d'Ivoire

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** Daouda Konaté, director of the national meteorology service, [says Meteosat data let its forecasters issue nowcasts](<https://www.preventionweb.net/news/towards-early-warnings-all-africa>). Those very short-range forecasts underpin the country's early warning platforms. He spoke to EUMETSAT in 2024. The platform links meteorologists, local authorities and relief agencies.

**Warnings issued.** Konaté says that when record rainfall was forecast for Abidjan in June 2024, [an orange alert was issued at once](<https://www.preventionweb.net/news/towards-early-warnings-all-africa>). People in high-risk areas were told through several channels to find safe shelter. More than 20 people died in the floods.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is [Konaté's 2024 account](<https://www.preventionweb.net/news/towards-early-warnings-all-africa>) of satellite nowcasting, which calls for better antennas, broadband and forecaster training.
