# Rwanda

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 Meteo Rwanda [said it runs PUMA-2025 reception stations](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/9%20-RWANDA_MTG_and_Radar_Synergy_PDF.pdf>) that process and display Meteosat Third Generation data in real time. A 2026 UNDP study says [forecasters use the imagery mostly as static cloud maps](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-09/sh-tracked-d4_final_report_ai_and_ml_integration.pdf>). A 2023 FMI peer review found the service's [forecasting draws on EUMETSAT imagery](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rwanda_CHD-Report_20231021_signed.pdf>) beside a radar.

**Warnings issued.** The same May 2026 deck says [lead time for severe thunderstorm warnings rose](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/9%20-RWANDA_MTG_and_Radar_Synergy_PDF.pdf>) from 15 minutes in 2021 to a projected 60 in 2026. It notes a jump since MTG data began. It also describes impact-based warnings for flash floods and storms.

**ISO 9001 certified.** The deck says the service's [operations are ISO 9001:2015 certified](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/9%20-RWANDA_MTG_and_Radar_Synergy_PDF.pdf>). The 2023 peer review had dated [the certification to 2018](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rwanda_CHD-Report_20231021_signed.pdf>), covering all branches of the service.
