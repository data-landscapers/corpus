# Rwanda

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [The UNDP/Neuralio feasibility study says METEO RWANDA receives satellite data from EUMETSAT's Meteosat Second and Third Generation via EUMETCast, with imagery as often as every 15 minutes, used mainly as static images (JPEG cloud maps) by forecasters](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-09/sh-tracked-d4_final_report_ai_and_ml_integration.pdf>). [The FMI peer review says Rwanda accesses EUMETSAT satellite products through a ground-based satellite receiver, and that Meteo Rwanda's forecasting draws on EUMETSAT satellite imagery alongside a C-band radar](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rwanda_CHD-Report_20231021_signed.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-09/sh-tracked-d4_final_report_ai_and_ml_integration.pdf>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** [The FMI peer review says Meteo Rwanda has an ISO9001:2015 certified QMS since 2018, last audited in 2022 and valid until 2025, including all branches of service (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rwanda_CHD-Report_20231021_signed.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [PUMA system (ECMWF products)](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Rwanda_CHD-Report_20231021_signed.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
