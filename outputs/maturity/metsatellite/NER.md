# Niger

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** Niger's meteorological directorate [runs a live satellite imagery page](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>). It serves Meteosat products, among them precipitation rates and dust-storm imagery. A service displaying satellite products is taken to use them in its work. No other source on the subject was found. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings from satellite data. [A second CREWS phase adds early-warning reports in selected areas](<https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-2-0/>). It does not mention satellites.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>).

***Also on file***. [CREWS Niger](<https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-2-0/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data.
