# Niger

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [Niger's national meteorological directorate (DMN) runs a live satellite imagery page serving EUMETView/Meteosat products (Natural Colour Enhanced RGB, GEO/LEO-blended precipitation rate, Dust RGB for dust-storm monitoring)](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings from satellite data. [A second CREWS phase adds early-warning reports in selected areas](<https://crews-initiative.org/project/niger-2-0/>). It does not mention satellites.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>).
