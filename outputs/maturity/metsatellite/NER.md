# Niger

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** Niger's meteorological directorate [runs a live satellite imagery page](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>). It serves Meteosat products, among them precipitation rates and dust-storm imagery. A service displaying satellite products is taken to use them in its work. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Niger among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [directorate's satellite imagery page](<https://www.niger-meteo.ne/image-satelitaire/>), which does not mention certification.
