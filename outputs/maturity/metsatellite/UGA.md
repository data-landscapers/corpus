# Uganda

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** The Ministry of Water and Environment says the National Meteorological Centre has [a Synergie system receiving satellite imagery](<https://mwe.go.ug/about/departments/meteorological-services/forecasting-services>). An MDD system receives satellite data and model products. The page is undated and was read in 2026. In April 2026 the ministry [began piloting a flood detection app in Kampala](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-rolls-out-flood-detection-app-5417276>), the Daily Monitor reported. It uses Meteosat Third Generation rainfall estimates and satellite nowcasting, and is to reach other cities later.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mwe.go.ug/about/departments/meteorological-services/forecasting-services>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mwe.go.ug/about/departments/meteorological-services/forecasting-services>).
