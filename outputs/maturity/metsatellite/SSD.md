# South Sudan

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In 2025 WMO reported that [South Sudan's forecasters were trained in Juba to analyse satellite data](<https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership>). They began a pilot of daily forecasts checked against EUMETSAT data. A pilot and a training course are not yet established use. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed South Sudan among eight member states [still waiting for a PUMA station](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [WMO's 2025 account of the forecasting pilot](<https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership>), which does not mention certification.
