# South Sudan

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In 2025 WMO reported that [South Sudan's forecasters were trained in Juba to analyse satellite data](<https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership>). They began a pilot of daily forecasts checked against EUMETSAT data. A pilot and a training course are not yet established use, so the service is counted as under way. No other source on the subject was found, and the systems themselves were not examined. A later source showing daily use would move the stage.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wmo.int/media/project-update/crews-east-africa-supports-south-sudan-through-regional-twinning-partnership>).
