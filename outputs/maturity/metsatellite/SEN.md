# Senegal

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [In May 2026 ANACIM's head of forecasting said forecasters use near-real-time Meteosat Third Generation imagery](<http://www.eumetsat.int/features/forecasting-storm-speed-across-africa>). [They use it to see fine-scale convective cloud features](<http://www.eumetsat.int/features/forecasting-storm-speed-across-africa>). [In October 2025 AICCRA said ANACIM, the national meteorological agency, now generates crop-specific agro-climatic bulletins through the AgData Hub](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>). [The bulletins combine near-real-time forecasts, satellite observations and seasonal outlooks](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>).

**Warnings issued.** [In May 2026 ANACIM's head of forecasting said data from the MTG Lightning Imager are critical during the rainy season](<http://www.eumetsat.int/features/forecasting-storm-speed-across-africa>). [ANACIM uses them to alert communities to extreme weather](<http://www.eumetsat.int/features/forecasting-storm-speed-across-africa>).
