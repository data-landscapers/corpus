# Guinea

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** Guinea's meteorological agency [publishes Meteosat products on its site](<https://anmeteo.gov.gn/imagerie-satellitaire/>). They include dust RGB, precipitation maps and a thunderstorm nowcasting product. The page is undated and was read in 2026. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Guinea among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). No news story read for this study says more.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [agency's satellite imagery page](<https://anmeteo.gov.gn/imagerie-satellitaire/>), which does not mention certification.
