# Madagascar

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Madagascar among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). The stations come under the EU-funded ClimSA programme, through a contract with Tecnavia signed in November 2023. By then 50 of 58 planned sites across the continent had one. In 2019 the forecasting office [presented a recently upgraded PUMA 2015 station](<https://severeweather.wmo.int/TCFW/RAI_Training2019/Madagascar.pdf>) and said it used Meteosat imagery to follow tropical cyclones. No source since then says how it uses the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [forecasting office's 2019 account](<https://severeweather.wmo.int/TCFW/RAI_Training2019/Madagascar.pdf>) of cyclone monitoring with satellite imagery.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>) of PUMA stations.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the weather service uses satellite data in its forecasts.
