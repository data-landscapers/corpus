# Djibouti

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [The GeoSphere Austria peer review says Djibouti's ANM has no nowcasting observations other than satellite imagery available on the web, and that its PUMA satellite workstation is not operational so information is received through the web](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Djibouti-CHD.pdf>). [The World Bank roadmap says Djibouti's national meteorological agency (ANM) has a Meteosat Second Generation receiving station with PUMA workstations that is currently out of service, and proposes modernising the station and antenna (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061323094013108/pdf/P1705480165e750370a40903df6490528c1.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Djibouti-CHD.pdf>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Djibouti-CHD.pdf>).
