# Mozambique

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [In March 2025 WMO said Mozambique's warnings rest on forecasts using satellite imagery and surface observations](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>). [In September 2023 the World Bank said the national meteorology institute, INAM, used satellite images for warnings](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>). [It also used radar and observation-network data](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>).

**Warnings issued.** [WMO says satellite-aided warnings went out through community radio and volunteers](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>). [They did so ahead of Cyclone Freddy in 2023](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>). [In September 2023 the World Bank said INAM's warnings were relayed through 70 community radios](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>). [Community brigades relayed them as well, the World Bank said](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>).
