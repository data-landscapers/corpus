# Mozambique

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** [WMO says Mozambique's meteorological warnings are based on forecasts using satellite imagery and surface observation data](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>). [The World Bank reports that Mozambique's national meteorology institute INAM produces its weather warnings from satellite images, radar and observation-network data (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>).

**Warnings issued.** [WMO says meteorological warnings based on satellite-aided forecasts were disseminated through community radio and volunteers, and ahead of Cyclone Freddy in 2023](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>). [The World Bank reports that INAM issues weather warnings based on satellite images, radar and network data, which the Instituto de Comunicação Social then relays through 70 community radios and community brigades (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.worldbank.org/pt/news/feature/2023/09/11/early-warning-system-saves-lives-in-afe-mozambique>).

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wmo.int/site/science-action/weather-forecasts-and-early-warnings/mozambiques-life-saving-early-warning-systems>).
