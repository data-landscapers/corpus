# Mauritania

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** In September 2025 an African Union Commission committee [inspected the ClimSA and PUMA equipment installed in Nouakchott](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/pressreleases/45397-pr-Press_Release_Deployment_Final.pdf>). It receives and processes Meteosat Second and Third Generation data from EUMETSAT. In May 2026 the Commission listed Mauritania among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). Nothing found says the weather service's forecasts use the data.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification. The nearest held fact is the [Commission's September 2025 inspection report](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/pressreleases/45397-pr-Press_Release_Deployment_Final.pdf>), which does not mention certification.
