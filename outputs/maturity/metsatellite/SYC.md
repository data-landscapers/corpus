# Seychelles

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** A South African Weather Service peer review in June 2025 says the Seychelles Meteorological Authority [reaches EUMETSAT satellite products over the internet](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CHD-Report-Seychelles-final-draft_12June2025.pdf>). Its forecasters use the data for nowcasting. In March 2026 the environment minister [named satellites among the observations behind forecasts and warnings](<https://www.meteo.sc/news/seychelles-celebrates-world-meteorological-day-2026-marks-55-years-of-aviation-meteorological-services-and-strengthens-partnership-with-geosphere-austria/>). In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed Seychelles among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>).

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the authority's own warnings draw on satellite data. The nearest held fact is [the minister's March 2026 speech](<https://www.meteo.sc/news/seychelles-celebrates-world-meteorological-day-2026-marks-55-years-of-aviation-meteorological-services-and-strengthens-partnership-with-geosphere-austria/>), which spoke of weather services in general.

**ISO 9001 certified.** The same peer review says the authority [holds ISO 9001:2015 certification](<https://alliancehydromet.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/CHD-Report-Seychelles-final-draft_12June2025.pdf>) for international air navigation services. The certification also covers its climate services.

***Not found***. No source found says the Seychelles Meteorological Authority issues early warnings or impact-based forecasts that draw on satellite data.
