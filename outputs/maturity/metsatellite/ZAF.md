# South Africa

## Satellite data in weather services

**Satellite data stream.** The South African Weather Service's 2024 overview of its observations [lists EUMETSAT's Meteosat Second Generation satellite](<https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/2024-11/SAWS_Early%20Warning%20Systems_Nov%202024-FV%20%28wecompress.com%29.pdf>). It has 12 channels, a 15-minute cycle and a view of the whole continent. In May 2026 the African Union Commission listed South Africa among 41 member states with [a PUMA station installed](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>). No news story read for this study says more.

**Warnings issued.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service issues warnings drawing on satellite data. The nearest held fact is the [African Union Commission's May 2026 list](<https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-05/7-%20Infrastructure%20and%20Capacity%20Building_AUC_PDF.pdf>), which records a PUMA station and not how it is used.

**ISO 9001 certified.** No source found since October 2023 says the weather service holds ISO 9001 certification.
