# Madagascar

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [AfricaNenda's 2025 consumer deck for Madagascar cites Global Findex 2025](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>): 24% of adults own a formal account. Findex is a World Bank survey of adults. The deck was published in November 2025. No other survey of Malagasy adults since 2023 has been found.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 24% with a formal account](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>) is well short of four in five adults. It is also under the quarter of adults that the first rung above stage 1 asks for.

**Paying digitally.** [The same deck puts adults who used digital payments in the past year at 22%](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>). That is about one adult in five. The frame's top rung asks for four in five. Both shares come from the same Findex survey of adults.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey found since 2023 puts adults with an account at a quarter or more. No survey found other than Findex gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
