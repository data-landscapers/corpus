# Madagascar

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The AfricaNenda SIIPS 2025 Madagascar consumer deck cites Global Findex 2025 as putting the share of adults owning a formal account at 24% (22% used digital payments in the past year)](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/Consumer-Deck_Madagascar_SIIPS-2025_compressed.pdf>).
