# Guinea

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In July 2026 the central bank [put the account access rate at nearly 36% in 2024, up from 4% in 2011](<https://www.africaguinee.com/inclusion-financiere-la-bcrg-realigne-sa-strategie-nationale-sur-les-ambitions-de-simandou-2040/>). Its first deputy governor gave the figure at a meeting of central banks in Luanda.

**Account target met.** [The 2024 rate of nearly 36%](<https://www.africaguinee.com/inclusion-financiere-la-bcrg-realigne-sa-strategie-nationale-sur-les-ambitions-de-simandou-2040/>) is well short of four in five. The central bank said the rate alone does not show the real level of inclusion. It does not say the figure comes from a survey. The central bank is [revising its national financial inclusion strategy](<https://www.africaguinee.com/inclusion-financiere-la-bcrg-realigne-sa-strategie-nationale-sur-les-ambitions-de-simandou-2040/>), Africa Guinée reported.
