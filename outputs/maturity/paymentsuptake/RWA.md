# Rwanda

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [KT Press reported in December 2025 that FinScope puts adults in Rwanda using services such as mobile money at 73%](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/12/new-survey-reveals-consumer-digital-fraud-risks-in-rwanda/>). The report does not give the survey's year. FinScope is a survey of adults, run by FinMark Trust.

**Account target met.** [The FinScope share of 73%](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/12/new-survey-reveals-consumer-digital-fraud-risks-in-rwanda/>) is short of four in five. No survey found since 2023 gives the share of Rwandan adults holding any account. Four in five would need about six points more than [FinScope's 73%](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/12/new-survey-reveals-consumer-digital-fraud-risks-in-rwanda/>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [FinScope's 73% using services such as mobile money](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/12/new-survey-reveals-consumer-digital-fraud-risks-in-rwanda/>). FinScope's figure counts adults using services such as mobile money.

***Also on file***. [eKash](<https://african.business/2026/07/innov-africa-deals/rwanda-unifies-digital-payments-with-national-launch-of-ekash>).

***Not found***. No survey found gives the share of adults holding any account, or the survey year behind the 73%. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
