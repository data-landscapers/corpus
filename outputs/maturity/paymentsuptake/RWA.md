# Rwanda

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In June 2024 the Ministry of Finance launched FinScope 2024. It found [77% of adults holding a registered mobile money account, up from 60% in 2020](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/96-of-rwandans-are-financially-included-finscope-survey-2024>), and 22% banked. [KT Press reported FinScope at 73% of adults using services such as mobile money](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2025/12/new-survey-reveals-consumer-digital-fraud-risks-in-rwanda/>) in December 2025.

**Account target met.** [FinScope 2024 put formal financial inclusion at 92% of adults](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/96-of-rwandans-are-financially-included-finscope-survey-2024>), 7.5 million people, up from 77% in 2020. That is above four in five. The formal figure also counts insurance and pension products.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [that about 86% of Rwandans used mobile money in 2024](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/96-of-rwandans-are-financially-included-finscope-survey-2024>). Overall inclusion, formal and informal, [reached 96% of adults in 2024](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/news-detail/96-of-rwandans-are-financially-included-finscope-survey-2024>).
