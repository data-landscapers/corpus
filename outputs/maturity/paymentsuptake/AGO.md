# Angola

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The central bank reported that 31.54% of the population held at least one bank account in 2024](<https://diariodosnegocios.com/inclusao-financeira-angola-desafios-bancarizacao-2024/>), up 0.76 points on 2023. In December 2025 the Minister of State for Economic Coordination [put the share of the population with a bank account at 31%](<https://afi-global.org/news/angolas-national-financial-inclusion-strategy-aims-to-boost-economic-and-social-development/>). He spoke at the launch of the national financial inclusion strategy.

**Account target met.** [The 2024 banking rate of 31.54%](<https://diariodosnegocios.com/inclusao-financeira-angola-desafios-bancarizacao-2024/>) is well short of four in five. It counts bank accounts only. [FinScope Angola 2022 found 36% of adults with a transactional account](<https://lbc-global.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/FinScope-Angola-2022_Livrete-dos-Resultados_JUN2023-PT.pdf>), a bank account or mobile payments. No survey since 2023 has been found.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the minister’s statement that digital payments are still emerging](<https://afi-global.org/news/angolas-national-financial-inclusion-strategy-aims-to-boost-economic-and-social-development/>). He gave no share of adults using them.
