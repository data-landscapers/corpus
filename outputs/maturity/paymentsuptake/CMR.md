# Cameroon

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The World Bank's Global Findex puts account ownership among Cameroonian adults at 60.87% in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=CM>). The figure counts accounts at a financial institution or a mobile money provider. Findex is a survey of adults. No later survey of Cameroonian adults has been found.

**Account target met.** [The 2024 Findex share of 60.87%](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=CM>) is short of four in five adults. No survey found since 2023 gives a higher share. Four in five would need about 18 points more than [the 2024 Findex share](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=CM>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the 2024 Findex account share of 60.87%](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS?locations=CM>). The Findex share counts accounts, not the payments made from them.

***Not found***. No survey found since 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
