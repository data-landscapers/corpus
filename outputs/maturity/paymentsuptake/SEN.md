# Senegal

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The BCEAO's national director said in July 2024 that Senegal's financial inclusion rate reached 83.3% in 2023, up from 61.9% in 2016](<https://www.orbit.sn/le-taux-dinclusion-financiere-du-senegal-est-superieur-a-la-moyenne-de-la-zone-uemoa-selon-le-directeur-national-de-la-bceao/>). The rate is the share of adults with a bank, postal, treasury, microfinance or e-money account. The BCEAO is the central bank of the West African monetary union.

**Account target met.** [The 2023 rate of 83.3%](<https://www.orbit.sn/le-taux-dinclusion-financiere-du-senegal-est-superieur-a-la-moyenne-de-la-zone-uemoa-selon-le-directeur-national-de-la-bceao/>) is above four in five adults. No survey of Senegalese adults since 2023 has been found. [The rate stood at 61.9% in 2016](<https://www.orbit.sn/le-taux-dinclusion-financiere-du-senegal-est-superieur-a-la-moyenne-de-la-zone-uemoa-selon-le-directeur-national-de-la-bceao/>), so it rose by more than 21 points in seven years.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the BCEAO's rate of 83.3%](<https://www.orbit.sn/le-taux-dinclusion-financiere-du-senegal-est-superieur-a-la-moyenne-de-la-zone-uemoa-selon-le-directeur-national-de-la-bceao/>). The BCEAO's rate counts adults holding an account, not the payments they make with it.
