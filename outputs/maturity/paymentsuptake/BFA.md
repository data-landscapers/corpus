# Burkina Faso

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In November 2026 the director of the national financial inclusion agency [put the overall rate of use of financial services at 81.8% at the end of 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150583=>). The rate was 79.04% in 2023 and 70.5% in 2019. [C’Finance gave 79.7% at the end of 2024](<https://cfinance.news/article/inclusion-financiere-avec-797-a-fin-2024-le-burkina-en-avance-de-61-points-sur-le-taux-global-de-luemoa/>), with half of those included through e-money.

**Account target met.** [The rate of 81.8% at the end of 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150583=>) is above four in five. The first national strategy set a target of 75% for 2023, which the country passed. A second strategy [aims for 90% by 2031](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150583=>). The strict bank account rate [was 23.4% at the end of 2024](<https://cfinance.news/article/inclusion-financiere-avec-797-a-fin-2024-le-burkina-en-avance-de-61-points-sur-le-taux-global-de-luemoa/>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [that half of those included were included through e-money in 2024](<https://cfinance.news/article/inclusion-financiere-avec-797-a-fin-2024-le-burkina-en-avance-de-61-points-sur-le-taux-global-de-luemoa/>).
