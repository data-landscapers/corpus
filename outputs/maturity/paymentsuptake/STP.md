# Sao Tome and Principe

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The central bank's second demand-side survey on financial inclusion and literacy, run in early 2025, found 38% of adults with a bank account](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Relatorio_lado_da_procura-Final-PT.pdf>). The Alliance for Financial Inclusion published the report in February 2026. No other survey of adults in São Tomé and Príncipe since 2023 has been found.

**Account target met.** [The survey's 38% with a bank account](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Relatorio_lado_da_procura-Final-PT.pdf>) is well short of four in five adults. It counts bank accounts alone, without mobile money. Four in five would need 41 points more than [the 2025 bank account share](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Relatorio_lado_da_procura-Final-PT.pdf>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the 2025 survey's 38% with a bank account](<https://afi-global.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Relatorio_lado_da_procura-Final-PT.pdf>). The survey's figure counts bank accounts, not payments.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey share of adults with a mobile money account, or with any account, has been found. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
