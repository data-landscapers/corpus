# Somalia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** The Central Bank of Somalia launched the Somalia Financial Access Survey 2026 in July 2026. It is the country’s first nationally representative survey of financial access. It [found 90% of Somali adults financially included, largely through mobile money](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>), the Somali Microfinance Association reported.

**Account target met.** [The survey’s 90%](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>) is above four in five. An opinion piece on the same survey [put bank penetration at about 19% of adults](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/opinionsomalias-financial-inclusion-story-beyond-access-to-economic-resilience>). Mobile money accounts make up most of the rest. The same survey [found 74% of small businesses excluded from credit](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the survey’s 90% included, largely through mobile money](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>).

***Also on file***. [SIPS](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>); [SOMQR](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>); [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. The survey's share holding a bank or mobile money account has not been found apart from its inclusion rate. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
