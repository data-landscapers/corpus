# Somalia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** The Central Bank of Somalia launched the Somalia Financial Access Survey 2026 in July 2026. It is the country's first nationally representative survey of financial access. An opinion piece on the survey [puts mobile money reach at 87% of adults](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/opinionsomalias-financial-inclusion-story-beyond-access-to-economic-resilience>). [It puts bank penetration at about 19% of adults](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/opinionsomalias-financial-inclusion-story-beyond-access-to-economic-resilience>). The Somali Microfinance Association reported that [the survey found 90% of adults financially included, largely through mobile money](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>).

**Account target met.** [The survey's 87% for mobile money](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/opinionsomalias-financial-inclusion-story-beyond-access-to-economic-resilience>) is above four in five. [The same survey found 74% of small businesses excluded from credit](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/somali-microfinance-association-somma_somma-somalia-financialinclusion-activity-7488596234996371457-auqx>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the survey's 87% mobile money reach](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/opinionsomalias-financial-inclusion-story-beyond-access-to-economic-resilience>).
