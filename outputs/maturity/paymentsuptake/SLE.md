# Sierra Leone

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The WTO's 2025 trade policy review summary says only about 29% of adults in Sierra Leone have an account with a formal financial institution or a mobile money provider](<https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s470_sum_e.pdf>). The figure counts bank and mobile money accounts together, so it is a share of adults rather than a count of accounts, and it puts Sierra Leone above a quarter of adults but well below half.

**Account target met.** No survey held puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact is [the WTO summary's figure of about 29% of adults with an account](<https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s470_sum_e.pdf>), short of four in five by some fifty points.

**Paying digitally.** No survey held gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment in the past year. The nearest held fact is [the WTO summary's account figure](<https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s470_sum_e.pdf>), which says who holds an account and nothing of payments made or received.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. The 29% figure names no survey and no year. Nothing held puts adults with an account at over half, or gives the share paying digitally.
