# Morocco

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [Bank Al-Maghrib's 2024 financial-inclusion report cites the Global Findex 2024 survey: 44% of Moroccan adults hold a bank or payment account](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>). The survey gave 29% for 2017. [The same report puts its own bank account holding rate at 58% at the end of 2024, up from 53% in 2021](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>). That rate counts residents with an active bank account against the adult population.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 44%](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>) is well short of four in five adults. [The bank's own rate of 58%](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>) is short of it too.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of Moroccan adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the Findex account share of 44%](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>). [The central bank's own rate of 58%](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>) counts bank accounts, not payments.

***Also on file***. [M-Wallet](<https://www.bkam.ma/fr/content/download/844036/9139518/Rapport%20SNIF%202023.pdf>); [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. No survey found since 2023 puts adults with an account at 53% or more. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
