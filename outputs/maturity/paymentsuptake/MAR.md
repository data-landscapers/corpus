# Morocco

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [Bank Al-Maghrib's annual financial-inclusion report puts the adult bank account holding rate at 58% at end-2024, up from 53% in 2021](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>). [Bank Al-Maghrib's SNIF report cites Global Findex 2021 at 44% of Moroccan adults with an account (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.bkam.ma/fr/content/download/844036/9139518/Rapport%20SNIF%202023.pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>).
