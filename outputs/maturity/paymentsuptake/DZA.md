# Algeria

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [A presentation of Global Findex data put Algerian adults with an account at 35.3% in 2024](<https://maghrebemergent.news/fr/les-chiffres-lattestent-lepargne-dort-un-potentiel-inexploite/>). Maghreb Emergent reported it in April 2026. [La Voix de l’Algérie gave the Findex rate as 35%](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/selon-global-findex-2025-le-numerique-ouvre-de-nouvelles-perspectives-pour-la-finance-en-algerie/2025/12/00/>). The world average is 79%.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 35%](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/selon-global-findex-2025-le-numerique-ouvre-de-nouvelles-perspectives-pour-la-finance-en-algerie/2025/12/00/>) is well short of four in five. [A study of the Findex data](<https://journals.univ-msila.dz/index.php/jorfa/article/view/10489>) reports bank account ownership at 35% in 2024, a decline. No survey has been found that puts the share above half.

**Paying digitally.** [Global Findex puts Algerian adults who made or received a digital payment at 13%](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/selon-global-findex-2025-le-numerique-ouvre-de-nouvelles-perspectives-pour-la-finance-en-algerie/2025/12/00/>). Phone ownership is 98% and internet use 83%, the same report says. Formal saving stands at 17% of adults and formal borrowing at 7%, [the same report says](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/selon-global-findex-2025-le-numerique-ouvre-de-nouvelles-perspectives-pour-la-finance-en-algerie/2025/12/00/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey has been found that puts adults with an account at half or more.
