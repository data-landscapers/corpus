# Egypt

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Central Bank of Egypt says 74.8 percent of Egyptians aged 15 and above were actively using financial accounts at the close of 2024, up from 70.7 percent in 2023](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>).
