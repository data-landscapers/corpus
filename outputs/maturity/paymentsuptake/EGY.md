# Egypt

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Central Bank of Egypt said in February 2025 that 74.8% of Egyptians aged 15 and over were actively using financial accounts at the end of 2024](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>). The rate was 70.7% in 2023. The bank called the figure a new record for financial inclusion.

**Account target met.** [The 74.8% rate](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>) is short of four in five. No survey of Egyptian adults since 2023 has been found. Four in five would need about four points more than [the 2024 rate of 74.8%](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>). The rate rose by about four points from 2023 to 2024.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the central bank's 74.8% using accounts](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>). The central bank's figure counts Egyptians aged 15 and over using an account, not the payments they make with it.

***Not found***. No survey share of adults with an account has been found; the central bank does not say its figure comes from one. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
