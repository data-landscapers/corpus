# Egypt

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [Global Findex 2025 puts Egyptian adults with a formal account at 43%](<https://www.alborsaanews.com/2025/07/21/1902282>), Al Borsa News reported in July 2025. The survey year is 2024. The same report gives 36% who made or received a digital payment.

**Account target met.** The Central Bank of Egypt [put its financial inclusion rate at 79% at the end of June 2026](<https://www.businesstodayegypt.com/Article/1/8078/Egypt%E2%80%99s-financial-inclusion-rate-rises-to-79-in-June-2026>), up from 77.6% at the end of 2025. About 56.4 million citizens held active accounts. The bank’s rate counts people with accounts and is not a survey. [It stood at 74.8% at the end of 2024](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/3/138692/CBE-shares-new-financial-inclusion-record-74-of-eligible-Egyptians>).

**Paying digitally.** [Global Findex 2025 puts Egyptian adults who made or received a digital payment at 36%](<https://www.alborsaanews.com/2025/07/21/1902282>). That is under half of the four in five the frame takes as its target.

***Also on file***. [Meeza](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>); [InstaPay](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>); [Meeza Digital](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>).

***Not found***. No survey has been found that puts adults with an account at half or more.
