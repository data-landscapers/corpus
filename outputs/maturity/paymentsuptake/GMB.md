# Gambia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In June 2026 the Governor of the Central Bank of The Gambia, Buah Saidy, launched the FinScope Consumer Survey 2025. He said it found [82% of adults had access to formal financial services](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>), over 1.22 million people. The share was 19% in 2019.

**Account target met.** [The survey’s 82%](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>) is above four in five. The central bank [aims for full coverage by 2030](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>) through digital services. The figure counts adults formally served, which includes accounts.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the survey’s 82% with access to formal financial services](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>). The central bank is [rebuilding Gamswitch, the national switch, on Mojaloop](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>) to widen digital services.

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>); [Gamswitch](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-financial-inclusion-reaches-82-central-bank-targets-full-coverage-by-2030-through-digitalisation/>).

***Not found***. The survey's share holding an account alone has not been found. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
