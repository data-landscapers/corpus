# Ghana

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In November 2026 the Governor of the Bank of Ghana, Johnson Pandit Asiama, opened the 11th meeting of the Payment Systems Advisory Committee. He told it that [recent global surveys put about 81% of Ghanaian adults as owning a financial account](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Opening-Remarks-by-Governor-Dr-Johnson-Pandit-Asiama-at-the-11th-Meeting-of-the-Payment-Systems-Advisory-Committee-PSAC-021126.pdf>). He gave the figure as a sign that financial inclusion had improved significantly.

**Account target met.** The governor's [figure of about 81% of adults with a financial account](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Opening-Remarks-by-Governor-Dr-Johnson-Pandit-Asiama-at-the-11th-Meeting-of-the-Payment-Systems-Advisory-Committee-PSAC-021126.pdf>) is above four in five. It counts adults, not households or firms.

**Paying digitally.** No survey held gives the share of Ghanaian adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest held fact is [the governor's figure of about 81% of adults with an account](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Opening-Remarks-by-Governor-Dr-Johnson-Pandit-Asiama-at-the-11th-Meeting-of-the-Payment-Systems-Advisory-Committee-PSAC-021126.pdf>). That figure gives no share of adults paying bills, sending money or receiving salaries digitally.

***Not found***. No survey held gives the share of adults paying digitally. The survey behind the 81% figure is not named.
