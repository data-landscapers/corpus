# Ghana

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In November 2026 the Governor of the Bank of Ghana, Johnson Pandit Asiama, opened the 11th meeting of the Payment Systems Advisory Committee. He told it that [recent global surveys put about 81% of Ghanaian adults as owning a financial account](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Opening-Remarks-by-Governor-Dr-Johnson-Pandit-Asiama-at-the-11th-Meeting-of-the-Payment-Systems-Advisory-Committee-PSAC-021126.pdf>). He gave the figure as a sign that financial inclusion had improved significantly.

**Account target met.** [Citi Newsroom reported in November 2026 that the Deputy Minister of Finance cited Global Findex 2025 as putting adult account ownership above 80%](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/11/financial-inclusion-expands-but-economic-well-being-remains-low-at-22/>). That and [the governor's figure of about 81%](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Opening-Remarks-by-Governor-Dr-Johnson-Pandit-Asiama-at-the-11th-Meeting-of-the-Payment-Systems-Advisory-Committee-PSAC-021126.pdf>) are above four in five.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of Ghanaian adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the Findex share of above 80% with an account](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/11/financial-inclusion-expands-but-economic-well-being-remains-low-at-22/>).
