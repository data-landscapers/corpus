# Nigeria

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [EFInA's A2F 2026 survey, released 16 September 2026, puts formal financial inclusion of Nigerian adults at 73%, about 87.2 million people](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>). [The same EFInA A2F 2026 survey, fieldwork April-June 2026, puts formal financial inclusion at 73% of adults, above half](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/05/nigeria-hits-73-financial-inclusion-target-but-60-4-million-adults-remain-vulnerable/>). [EFInA's 2026 Access to Financial Services in Nigeria survey (weighted by NBS) puts formal financial inclusion at 73% of adults, about 87.2 million](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/05/nigeria-hits-73-financial-inclusion-target-but-60-4-million-adults-remain-vulnerable/>). [EFInA's A2F 2026 survey puts formal financial inclusion of Nigerian adults at 73% in 2026, up from 64% in 2023](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>).

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>); [Remita](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>).

***Not found***. No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more.
