# Nigeria

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [EFInA's Access to Financial Services in Nigeria survey, released on 16 September 2026, puts formal financial inclusion at 73% of adults](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>). That is about 87.2 million people. [EFInA gives 64% for 2023](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>). [Fieldwork ran from April to June 2026](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/05/nigeria-hits-73-financial-inclusion-target-but-60-4-million-adults-remain-vulnerable/>), and [the results are weighted by the National Bureau of Statistics](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/05/nigeria-hits-73-financial-inclusion-target-but-60-4-million-adults-remain-vulnerable/>).

**Account target met.** [The 2026 share of 73%](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>) is short of four in five adults. No other survey of Nigerian adults since 2023 has been found. [Formal inclusion rose from 64% in 2023 to 73% in 2026](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>), nine points in three years.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [EFInA's 73% formally included](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>). EFInA's measure counts adults formally included, not the payments they make.
