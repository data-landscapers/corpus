# Nigeria

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [NITDA's Nigeria Tech Outlook Report 2026, launched on 11 November 2026, says 86.5% of adults hold financial accounts](<https://von.gov.ng/nitda-unveils-nigeria-tech-outlook-report/>). It drew on 2,147 household interviews. [EFInA's Access to Financial Services survey, released on 16 September 2026, puts formal financial inclusion at 73% of adults](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>), about 87.2 million people. [Its fieldwork ran from April to June 2026, weighted by the National Bureau of Statistics](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/10/05/nigeria-hits-73-financial-inclusion-target-but-60-4-million-adults-remain-vulnerable/>). [EFInA gives 64% for 2023](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>).

**Account target met.** [NITDA's figure of 86.5% is above four in five adults](<https://von.gov.ng/nitda-unveils-nigeria-tech-outlook-report/>). The report speaks of financial accounts, without naming bank or mobile money accounts. [EFInA's 73% for 2026 is short of four in five](<https://independent.ng/nigerias-financial-inclusion-reaches-79-as-efinas-a2f-2026-survey-points-to-next-challenge/>). [EFInA, reading the World Bank's Global Findex for 2025, puts adults with an account at 63%](<https://efina.org.ng/2025/08/28/beyond-access-turning-financial-inclusion-into-financial-resilience-in-nigeria/>).

**Paying digitally.** [EFInA, reading the 2025 Global Findex, says 54% of Nigerian adults make digital payments](<https://efina.org.ng/2025/08/28/beyond-access-turning-financial-inclusion-into-financial-resilience-in-nigeria/>). [NITDA's 2026 survey, as reported, gives no share for digital payments](<https://von.gov.ng/nitda-unveils-nigeria-tech-outlook-report/>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>); [Remita](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>).

***Not found***. No survey found puts adults who made or received a digital payment at 79% or more. The two 2026 surveys disagree on account holding, at 86.5% and 73%.
