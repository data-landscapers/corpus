# Ethiopia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [A 2025 paper citing Global Findex puts account ownership among Ethiopian adults at 49% in 2024](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>). The same paper gives 22% for 2014 and 35% for 2017. [A World Bank blog of March 2024 gave 46% from the Findex for 2022](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>).

**Account target met.** [The 2024 Findex share of 49%](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>) is well short of four in five adults. [The National Bank of Ethiopia put adults with a bank account at about 45% in 2020](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). No survey found since 2024 gives a higher share.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the 2024 Findex account share of 49%](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>). Account ownership rose by 27 points [between 2014 and 2024](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>).
