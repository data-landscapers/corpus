# Ethiopia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [A 2025 paper citing Global Findex says 49% of Ethiopian adults held an account in 2024](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>). [That was up from 22% in 2014 and 35% in 2017](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>). [In March 2024 the World Bank put Ethiopia's financial inclusion at 46% as of 2022](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). [The National Bank of Ethiopia says adults with a bank account rose from 22% in 2014 to about 45% in 2020](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 53% or more.

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.51244/ijrsi.2025.1210000333>).
