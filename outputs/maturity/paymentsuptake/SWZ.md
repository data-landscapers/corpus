# Eswatini

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Times of Eswatini reported in August 2025 that Global Findex 2025 puts 63% of adults with an account at a financial institution or a mobile money provider](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Mobile+money+drives+financial+inclusion+growth&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=435>). Findex is a World Bank survey of adults. The paper's headline credits mobile money with the growth.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 63%](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Mobile+money+drives+financial+inclusion+growth&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=435>) is short of four in five adults. No later survey of adults in Eswatini has been found. Four in five would need 16 points more than [the 2025 Findex share](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Mobile+money+drives+financial+inclusion+growth&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=435>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the Findex account share of 63%](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Mobile+money+drives+financial+inclusion+growth&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=435>). Findex counts accounts at a financial institution or a mobile money provider, not the payments made from them.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey found since 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
