# Uganda

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [FinScope Uganda 2023 found 64% of adults registered for mobile money with a SIM card, up from 46% in 2018](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>). A mobile money account counts towards the frame's measure. No newer survey of Ugandan adults has been found. Registration was with a SIM card, [the survey says](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>).

**Account target met.** [The 2023 share of 64%](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>) is short of four in five adults. No survey found gives the share holding a bank or mobile money account. [Registration rose from 46% in 2018](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>), 18 points in five years.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [FinScope's 64% registered for mobile money](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>). FinScope's figure counts adults registered for mobile money, not the share paying digitally.
