# Uganda

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [FSD Uganda, quoting Global Findex 2025, says 73% of Ugandan adults have a financial account](<https://fsduganda.or.ug/connected-but-not-included/>). The survey year is 2024. [FinScope Uganda 2023 found 64% registered for mobile money](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>), up from 46% in 2018.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 73%](<https://fsduganda.or.ug/connected-but-not-included/>) is short of four in five. FSD Uganda notes that 79% of adults own a mobile phone. No survey has been found that puts account holding at 79% or more.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [FSD Uganda’s finding that wallets are used mainly to receive money and cash out](<https://fsduganda.or.ug/connected-but-not-included/>). Andrew Tugume of FSD Uganda [wrote in May 2026](<https://fsduganda.or.ug/connected-but-not-included/>) that owning a phone does not by itself bring people into finance.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey has been found that puts adults with an account at four in five. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
