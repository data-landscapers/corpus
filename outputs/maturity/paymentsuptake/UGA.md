# Uganda

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [FinScope Uganda 2023 reports that 64% of adults are registered for mobile money with a SIM card, up from 46% in 2018](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ufla.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Final-Finscope-Uganda-2023-Survey.pdf>).
