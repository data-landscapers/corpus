# Cote d'Ivoire

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [World Bank Global Findex puts Côte d'Ivoire's financial inclusion rate at 58% in 2024, up from 41% in 2017](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>). [The regulator ARTCI's 2024 survey found 50.3% of people aged five and over with a personal mobile money account](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>). That base includes children, so it is not a share of adults.

**Account target met.** [The 2024 Findex rate of 58%](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>) is short of four in five adults. [ARTCI's survey puts mobile money account holding at 55.4% for men and 45.1% for women](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>). The Findex rate rose by 17 points [between 2017 and 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the 2024 Findex rate of 58%](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>). The nearest share on mobile money is [ARTCI's 50.3% of people aged five and over with an account](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>).
