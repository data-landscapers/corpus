# Cote d'Ivoire

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [World Bank Global Findex puts Côte d'Ivoire's financial inclusion rate at 58% in 2024, up from 41% in 2017](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>). [The regulator ARTCI's 2024 national survey found 50.3% of people aged five and over held a mobile money account](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>). [That is over 13 million people, with 55.4% of men and 45.1% of women holding one](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>). [The survey's base is everyone aged five and over, not adults alone](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rsum_Excutif_MSI_2024_200226.pdf>).

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0910-59598-cote-d-ivoire-seeks-to-diversify-fintech-sector-beyond-digital-payments>).
