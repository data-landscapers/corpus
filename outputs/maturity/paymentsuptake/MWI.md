# Malawi

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Bankers Association of Malawi's chief executive said in July 2025 that Global Findex 2025 puts adults with a financial account at 50% in 2024](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>). [FinScope's 2023 survey of 3,000 adults found 69% currently using mobile money and 13% banked](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>). FinScope's figure is for use of mobile money in 2023.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 50%](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>) is well short of four in five adults. [FinScope's 69% using mobile money in 2023](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>) is short of it too. [FinScope found 13% of adults banked in 2023](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>), far below either figure.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the 2024 Findex account share of 50%](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>).
