# Malawi

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The FinScope Consumer Survey Malawi 2023 findings report (FinMark Trust, 3,000 adults) says about two in every three adults (69%) currently use mobile money, up from 3% in 2014, and that 13% of adults are banked](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>). [The World Bank's Global Findex 2025 report indicates that 50 percent of Malawian adults had a financial account in 2024, as reported by the Bankers Association of Malawi's chief executive](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>).

**Account target met.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey held since October 2023 puts adults paying digitally at 79% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://finmark.org.za/Publications/2023_FinScope_Consumer_Survey_Malawi_2023_Report.pdf>).
