# Cape Verde

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Banco de Cabo Verde’s 2025 payment system report put the banking rate at 85.6%](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/25/mais-cabo-verdianos-com-contas-bancarias-e-maior-uso-de-pagamentos-digitais/104797>). The rate is the share of the resident population holding a current or payment account. It was 81.5% in 2024, Expresso das Ilhas reported in September 2026.

**Account target met.** [The 2025 rate of 85.6%](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/25/mais-cabo-verdianos-com-contas-bancarias-e-maior-uso-de-pagamentos-digitais/104797>) is above four in five. It rose by 4.1 points in a year. The report counts residents with an account, and does not say the figure comes from a survey.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the same report’s account rate of 85.6%](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/25/mais-cabo-verdianos-com-contas-bancarias-e-maior-uso-de-pagamentos-digitais/104797>), which counts accounts held, not payments made.
