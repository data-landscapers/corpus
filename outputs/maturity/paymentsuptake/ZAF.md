# South Africa

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The payments group ASAPP, citing FinMark Trust's FinScope surveys of 2024 and 2025, says 89% of South African adults have a bank account](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). Marketing Spread published the statement in September 2026. FinScope is a survey of adults. [The figure comes from FinScope national surveys run in 2024 and 2025](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>).

**Account target met.** [The FinScope share of 89%](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>) is above four in five adults. It counts bank accounts alone, without mobile money. Four in five is ten points below [the FinScope share](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of South African adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the FinScope share of 89% with a bank account](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). FinScope's figure counts adults with a bank account, not the payments they make with it.
