# Zambia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [ITWeb reported in July 2026 that Bank of Zambia figures from the 2025 FinScope survey put mobile money usage at 76.2%](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [The same figures put financial inclusion at 80.1%, up from 69.4% in 2020](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).

**Account target met.** [The mobile money share of 76.2% in 2025](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>) is just short of four in five adults. No survey found gives the share holding a bank or mobile money account. [Financial inclusion rose from 69.4% in 2020 to 80.1% in 2025](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>), the Bank of Zambia's figures show.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [FinScope's 76.2% using mobile money](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). FinScope's figure counts use of mobile money, not the share of adults paying digitally.
