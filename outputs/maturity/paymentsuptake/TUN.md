# Tunisia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [Global Findex puts Tunisian adults with an account at a financial institution or mobile money service at 37.8% in 2024](<https://www.challenges.tn/economie/bancarisation-en-tunisie-moins-de-comptes-plus-dagences-quel-cap/>), Challenges reported in November 2026. [Tunisie Numérique gave 37% for 2024](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/inclusion-financiere-mondiale-un-record-historique-de-titulaires-de-comptes-bancaires/>), with 29% among women. The rate for low-income adults was 32%.

**Account target met.** [The Findex share of 37.8%](<https://www.challenges.tn/economie/bancarisation-en-tunisie-moins-de-comptes-plus-dagences-quel-cap/>) is well short of four in five. [Findex 2021 put it at 37%](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040002202354875/pdf/IDU010adfdbe08bd3044f808e620311d82b11461.pdf>), unchanged from 2017. The number of bank accounts [fell 1.8% to 10.5 million in 2025](<https://www.challenges.tn/economie/bancarisation-en-tunisie-moins-de-comptes-plus-dagences-quel-cap/>).

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the Findex share of 37.8% with an account](<https://www.challenges.tn/economie/bancarisation-en-tunisie-moins-de-comptes-plus-dagences-quel-cap/>). Bank branches [rose to 2,081 in 2025](<https://www.challenges.tn/economie/bancarisation-en-tunisie-moins-de-comptes-plus-dagences-quel-cap/>) while account numbers fell.

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://en.africanmanager.com/tunisian-banks-urged-to-join-pan-african-papss-system/>); [D17 mobile wallet](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040002202354875/pdf/IDU010adfdbe08bd3044f808e620311d82b11461.pdf>).

***Not found***. No survey has been found that puts adults with an account at half or more. No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment.
