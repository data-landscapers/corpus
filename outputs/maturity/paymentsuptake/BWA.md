# Botswana

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [FinScope's 2024 survey found that 75% of Batswana use mobile money, TechCabal reported in September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/16/botswana-future-of-banking-not-start-with-bank/>). A mobile money account counts towards the frame's measure. FinScope is a survey of people, run by FinMark Trust.

**Account target met.** [The 75% using mobile money](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/16/botswana-future-of-banking-not-start-with-bank/>) falls short of four in five. No survey found gives the share of adults holding either a bank or a mobile money account. Three in four Batswana use mobile money on [FinScope's 2024 figure](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/16/botswana-future-of-banking-not-start-with-bank/>). Four in five would need about four points more.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults in Botswana who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [FinScope's 75% using mobile money](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/16/botswana-future-of-banking-not-start-with-bank/>). FinScope's figure counts use of mobile money, not the share of adults who made or received a digital payment in the past year.
