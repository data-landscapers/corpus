# Namibia

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** [The Namibia Statistics Agency's 2025 Financial Inclusion Survey found 75.6% of adults aged 15 and over with a bank account](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Namibia-Financial-Inclusion-Survey-Report.web_.pdf>). The same survey puts adults with a smart card account at 18.3%. Fieldwork ran in October and November 2025.

**Account target met.** [World Bank economist Leora Klapper said in October 2025 that Global Findex 2025 puts Namibian adults with a financial account at 73%](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/10/most-namibians-struggle-to-access-emergency-funds-world-bank/>). Neither that nor the agency's 75.6% reaches four in five. The gap from the agency's figure to four in five is about three points.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [the agency's 75.6% with a bank account](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Namibia-Financial-Inclusion-Survey-Report.web_.pdf>). The agency's survey also puts [adults with a smart card account at 18.3%](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2025-Namibia-Financial-Inclusion-Survey-Report.web_.pdf>).
