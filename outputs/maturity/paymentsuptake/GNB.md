# Guinea-Bissau

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** In September 2026 the national director of the regional central bank, the BCEAO, [put the overall access rate to financial services at 79.5% at the end of 2025](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=59036>). The rate was 20.5% in 2018. She said the rise came mainly from electronic money.

**Account target met.** [The rate of 79.5% at the end of 2025](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=59036>) is just above four in five. She gave the figure at the launch of a national financial education plan for 2026 to 2030. The bank does not say it comes from a survey.

**Paying digitally.** No survey found gives the share of adults who made or received a digital payment. The nearest fact found is [that electronic money drove the rise in access](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=59036>).
