# Gabon

## Population uptake

**Adults with an account.** The GSMA's [October 2026 report on Gabon](<https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/all-documents/gsma-calls-for-more-affordable-smartphones-as-over-half-of-gabons-population-remains-off-mobile-internet-despite-network-coverage/>) says 62% of adults hold a mobile money account. It does not name the survey behind the figure. An earlier report put [about 54% of adults unbanked](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/gabon-pens-deal-with-uncdf-to-accelerate-financial-inclusion>) in the World Bank's Global Findex 2025. That leaves about 46% with a bank account.

**Account target met.** No survey puts adults with an account at 79% or more. The nearest held fact is the [GSMA's figure of 62%](<https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/all-documents/gsma-calls-for-more-affordable-smartphones-as-over-half-of-gabons-population-remains-off-mobile-internet-despite-network-coverage/>) for mobile money accounts alone.

**Paying digitally.** No survey puts the share of adults who made or received a digital payment in the past year. The [GSMA report](<https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/all-documents/gsma-calls-for-more-affordable-smartphones-as-over-half-of-gabons-population-remains-off-mobile-internet-despite-network-coverage/>) calls mobile money accounts a base for wider everyday digital payments.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No survey puts account holding at four in five adults, or gives the share paying digitally.
