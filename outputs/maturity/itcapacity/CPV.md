# Cape Verde

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [NOSi ran the site that published the May 2026 legislative election results](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/18/legislativas-2026-presidente-do-nosi-pede-desculpas-por-alegado-ciber-ataque-durante-a-divulgacao-dos-resultados-das-eleicoes/>), sending data over the State Private Technology Network. [On election night it ran the results portal and acted during the 17 May incident](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/25/legislativas-2026-nosi-ainda-nao-divulgou-conclusoes-sobre-anomalias-na-noite-eleitoral/>). [In October 2024 the finance ministry's technology team worked to restore the SIGOF finance system](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2024/10/11/instabilidade-no-sigof-pode-causar-atrasos-nos-pagamentos-do-estado/93685>).

**A technology body for government.** [A former NOSi manager wrote in June 2024 that the state company builds and upgrades government systems](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2024/06/22/nosi-e-os-seus-25-anos/>). [In 2015 it ran the State's private network for over 14,000 users and 500 institutions](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2024/06/22/nosi-e-os-seus-25-anos/>). [In May 2026 it sent election data to TCV and to public servers over that network](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/18/legislativas-2026-presidente-do-nosi-pede-desculpas-por-alegado-ciber-ataque-durante-a-divulgacao-dos-resultados-das-eleicoes/>). [It developed and put in place the Justice Information System for the justice ministry](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>).

**Built in-house.** [The NOSi team developed the SIPP criminal-case part of the Justice Information System](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>). [It was formally handed over in March 2026 and is in use in every comarca](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>). [The justice ministry says NOSi built it with the ministry's IMIJ](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [NOSi, the state information-society company, runs the election-results publication system and portal eleicoes.cv with its own staff](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/27/legislativas-2026-nosi-diz-que-relatorio-preliminar-aponta-para-causas-de-ordem-tecnica/>). [The justice information system, developed and implemented by the state company NOSi, passes for maintenance and development to the Justice Ministry's IMIJ, in coordination with NOSi](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>). [NOSi, the government's technology arm, develops and maintains IGRPWeb among its e-government solutions](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>). [The finance ministry's own technology team restored the SIGOF public-finance system](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2024/10/11/instabilidade-no-sigof-pode-causar-atrasos-nos-pagamentos-do-estado/93685>). [NOSi, the government's technology arm, develops and maintains SNIAC among its e-government solutions](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>).
