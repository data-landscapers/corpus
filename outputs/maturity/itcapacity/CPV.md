# Cape Verde

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [NOSi operated the site publishing the legislative election results and secured data delivery through the State Private Technology Network](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/18/legislativas-2026-presidente-do-nosi-pede-desculpas-por-alegado-ciber-ataque-durante-a-divulgacao-dos-resultados-das-eleicoes/>). [NOSi, led by its board chair, ran the election-night results dissemination infrastructure and portal and took operational measures during the 17 May incident, separating TCV channels from the portal](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/25/legislativas-2026-nosi-ainda-nao-divulgou-conclusoes-sobre-anomalias-na-noite-eleitoral/>). [The government communique says the finance ministry's technology team is intervening to restore SIGOF](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2024/10/11/instabilidade-no-sigof-pode-causar-atrasos-nos-pagamentos-do-estado/93685>).

**A technology body for government.** [NOSi supplied election data to TCV under a protocol and to state public servers through the State Private Technology Network it runs](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/18/legislativas-2026-presidente-do-nosi-pede-desculpas-por-alegado-ciber-ataque-durante-a-divulgacao-dos-resultados-das-eleicoes/>). [NOSi, described as the Ministry of Justice's technology partner, is stated to be responsible for developing and implementing the Justice Information System for the ministry](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>).

**Built in-house.** [The SIPP criminal-case component of the SIJ, developed by the NOSi team, was formally delivered and is stated to be operating in every comarca](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>). [The SIJ criminal-case subsystem, developed by NOSi with the IMIJ, was handed over at a ceremony and is in use](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [NOSi, the state information-society company, runs the election-results publication system and portal eleicoes.cv with its own staff](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/27/legislativas-2026-nosi-diz-que-relatorio-preliminar-aponta-para-causas-de-ordem-tecnica/>). [The justice information system, developed and implemented by the state company NOSi, passes for maintenance and development to the Justice Ministry's IMIJ, in coordination with NOSi](<https://www.governo.cv/nosi-faz-entrega-formal-do-sistema-de-informacao-da-justica-ao-ministerio-da-justica/>). [NOSi, the government's technology arm, develops and maintains IGRPWeb among its e-government solutions](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>). [The finance ministry's own technology team restored the SIGOF public-finance system](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2024/10/11/instabilidade-no-sigof-pode-causar-atrasos-nos-pagamentos-do-estado/93685>). [NOSi, the government's technology arm, develops and maintains SNIAC among its e-government solutions](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>).
