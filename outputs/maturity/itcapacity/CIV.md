# Cote d'Ivoire

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** GUCE CI is a state company. In 2024 it [managed and maintained the GUCE single window for foreign trade](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>), with support from the trade ministry. Under a 2018 decree [the civil service ministry's own IT directorate keeps SIGFAE running](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.ci/index.php/front-page/navigator/statics/menu/ministere/dsi>).

**A technology body for government.** SNDI is a state company. In July 2026 Ivoirexpress said it [develops and deploys business applications for the government](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>). In September 2026 an SNDI representative said [it has run its own satellite network since 2006](<https://afriksoir.net/aspex-2026-le-satellite-au-coeur-des-enjeux-de-connectivite-et-de-resilience-numerique-en-cote-divoire/>), linking administrations, universities and health centres. [ANSUT manages five public data centres](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>). SNDI's is the central hosting point for the administration.

**Built in-house.** SNDI [built version 1 of SIGOMAP](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>), which in 2026 replaced SIGMAP for ministries' public procurement. On 25 July 2024 GUCE CI launched [GUCE Mobile, a five-module app built and run entirely by its own teams](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** In July 2026 Ivoirexpress said [SNDI built SIGFIP for public finance, SIGOMAP, and the CEPICI one-stop shop for business formalities](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>). It also built the building-permit one-stop system. No contractor is named for any of them.
