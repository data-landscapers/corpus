# Cote d'Ivoire

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [GUCE CI, a state company, manages and maintains the GUCE single window for foreign trade, with technical support from the trade ministry (2024)](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>). Older sources say [the civil service ministry's own information systems directorate keeps SIGFAE running under a 2018 decree](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.ci/index.php/front-page/navigator/statics/menu/ministere/dsi>) and that [the state company SNDI developed and deployed SIGFIP for public finance and SIGMAP for procurement (2014)](<https://marcopolis.net/overview-of-sndi-it-development-company-in-cote-d-ivoire.htm>).

**A technology body for government.** [An SNDI representative said at the 25 September 2026 plenary that SNDI has operated its own satellite network since 2006 connecting administrations, universities and health centres, and had provided internet to the University of Korhogo](<https://afriksoir.net/aspex-2026-le-satellite-au-coeur-des-enjeux-de-connectivite-et-de-resilience-numerique-en-cote-divoire/>). [RTI Infos describes SNDI as the state's IT operator since 1999, expected to play a larger role in the digital transformation of public administration](<https://rti.info/administration-numerique-la-cote-divoire-vise-700-services-publics-digitalises-dici-2029/>). [GUCE CI runs the GUCE transaction platform used by ministries' technical services and traders (2024)](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>).

**Built in-house.** [GUCE Mobile, a five-module app for following trade procedures, was launched on 25 July 2024, developed and put into operation entirely by GUCE CI's own teams](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** Two systems held since October 2023 are run or built by a state body with no contractor named: [the GUCE single window and its GUCE Mobile app, both GUCE CI's](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>).
