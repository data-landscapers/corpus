# Cote d'Ivoire

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The civil service ministry's own Direction des Systemes d'Information is charged under decree 2018-38 with keeping SIGFAE running, its data intact, technical support and the civil servants database (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.ci/index.php/front-page/navigator/statics/menu/ministere/dsi>). [SNDI, a state-owned company, says it developed and deployed SIGFIP (public finance) and SIGMAP (public procurement), used nationwide by the Government (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://marcopolis.net/overview-of-sndi-it-development-company-in-cote-d-ivoire.htm>).

**A technology body for government.** [An SNDI representative said at the 25 September 2026 plenary that SNDI has operated its own satellite network since 2006 connecting administrations, universities and health centres, and had provided internet to the University of Korhogo](<https://afriksoir.net/aspex-2026-le-satellite-au-coeur-des-enjeux-de-connectivite-et-de-resilience-numerique-en-cote-divoire/>). [RTI Infos describes SNDI as the state's IT operator since 1999, expected to play a larger role in the digital transformation of public administration](<https://rti.info/administration-numerique-la-cote-divoire-vise-700-services-publics-digitalises-dici-2029/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://afriksoir.net/aspex-2026-le-satellite-au-coeur-des-enjeux-de-connectivite-et-de-resilience-numerique-en-cote-divoire/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://afriksoir.net/aspex-2026-le-satellite-au-coeur-des-enjeux-de-connectivite-et-de-resilience-numerique-en-cote-divoire/>).
