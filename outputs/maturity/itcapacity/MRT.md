# Mauritania

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/237149>).

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/237149>).

**Built in-house.** [Mauritania's population register agency ANRPTS launched the Hewiyeti app on 26 December 2023, letting citizens apply and pay for ID cards, passports and civil-status extracts online; the interior minister said it was developed by national engineers](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/237149>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/237149>).
