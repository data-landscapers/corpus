# Equatorial Guinea

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The GEX case-file platform [was developed by CNIAPGE's own technical team](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/cniapge-forma-a-funcionarios-de-la-primatura-en-el-uso-de-la-plataforma-de-gestion-de-expedientes/>). Staff of its Deploy department train the Prime Minister's office to use it. In 2026 the Vice-President told the state company GETESA to [plan for its own staff to take over maintenance](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/getesa_presenta_su_plan_de_accion_estrategico_2026-2030>). That work on the telecom network is now contracted out.

**A technology body for government.** CNIAPGE is the national centre for computerising public administration. It [is rolling GEX out to the Prime Minister's office](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/cniapge-forma-a-funcionarios-de-la-primatura-en-el-uso-de-la-plataforma-de-gestion-de-expedientes/>), with ministries to follow.

**Built in-house.** On 19 November 2025 the Supreme Court [launched its institutional website](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2025/11/19/la-corte-suprema-de-justicia-presenta-su-pagina-web-institucional/>). The court's own Directorate General of Archives designed it.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. Two are found built by government teams: [GEX](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/cniapge-forma-a-funcionarios-de-la-primatura-en-el-uso-de-la-plataforma-de-gestion-de-expedientes/>) and [the Supreme Court website](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2025/11/19/la-corte-suprema-de-justicia-presenta-su-pagina-web-institucional/>).

***Also on file***. [National video-surveillance system](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_y_huawei_concluyen_exitosamente_las_negociaciones_sobre_los_anexos_del_contrato>).
