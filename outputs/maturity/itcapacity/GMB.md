# Gambia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source held since October 2023 names a system that the government's own technical staff run or maintain. The nearest held fact: [ICT professionals across ministries and agencies form one government ICT cadre](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gicta-hosts-forum-to-strengthen-public-sector-digital-transformation>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries. The nearest held fact: [the Gambia ICT Agency, GICTA, has administrative oversight of that cadre](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gicta-hosts-forum-to-strengthen-public-sector-digital-transformation>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [the cadre given above](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gicta-hosts-forum-to-strengthen-public-sector-digital-transformation>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** [Under the GICTA Act of 2019, the government's ICT professionals across ministries and agencies form an ICT cadre under GICTA's administrative oversight](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gicta-hosts-forum-to-strengthen-public-sector-digital-transformation>), as a June 2025 forum of the cadre reported.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says which systems government staff run, that GICTA builds or runs systems for ministries, or that a system built in-house went live since October 2023.
