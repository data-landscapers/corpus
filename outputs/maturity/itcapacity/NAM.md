# Namibia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Ministry of Finance, through the Central Procurement Board, sought consultant firms to support, maintain and develop the Integrated Financial Management System for five years (EOI closing 30 July 2025)](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>). [The Office of the Prime Minister's own IT Sub-Division states its core functions include developing systems, implementing and maintaining systems, systems administration, networking and maintaining ICT equipment for the Office (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://mwt.gov.na/documents/1150081/1182489/Charter+OPM_IT_+Brochure+%281%29.pdf/977e85e9-658a-7dea-2969-3ef3797ded13?t=1663746256094>).

**A technology body for government.** [The OPM IT Sub-Division lists other offices, ministries and agencies (OMAs), state-owned enterprises, the Auditor General and regional councils and local authorities as external clients of its ICT services (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://mwt.gov.na/documents/1150081/1182489/Charter+OPM_IT_+Brochure+%281%29.pdf/977e85e9-658a-7dea-2969-3ef3797ded13?t=1663746256094>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [this source](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [this source](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>).
