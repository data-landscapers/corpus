# Namibia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Electoral Commission said in June 2024 that its voter registration system is managed under its own IT policies](<https://omutumwa.com/2024/06/ecn-explains-slow-start-of-mobile-voter-registration-system/>). A fully-fledged IT Division is entrusted with its IT systems. [The Ministry of Finance, through the Central Procurement Board, sought consultant firms to support, maintain and develop the Integrated Financial Management System](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>). The work was to run for five years. Expressions of interest closed on 30 July 2025.

**A technology body for government.** [The OPM IT Sub-Division lists other offices, ministries and agencies (OMAs), state-owned enterprises, the Auditor General and regional councils and local authorities as external clients of its ICT services (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://mwt.gov.na/documents/1150081/1182489/Charter+OPM_IT_+Brochure+%281%29.pdf/977e85e9-658a-7dea-2969-3ef3797ded13?t=1663746256094>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. [The Electoral Commission's voter registration system was upgraded under the Public Procurement Act ahead of 2024 registration](<https://omutumwa.com/2024/06/ecn-explains-slow-start-of-mobile-voter-registration-system/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names a system that government staff run with no contractor doing the work. [The financial management system is maintained with outside consultants](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>).

[X-Road / UXP interoperability solution](<https://ega.ee/project/governmental-interoperability-in-namibia/>); [Namibia Integrated Employment Information System (NIEIS)](<https://mol.gov.na/documents/53329/5009470/Develpoment+of+NIEIS.pdf/58f2b2c3-6e98-8208-9719-be6f46d550ee>); [Integrated Financial Management System (IFMS)](<https://cpbn.com.na/index/bid/78>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government.
