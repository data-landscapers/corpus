# Libya

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The technical education ministry's information systems section [is overseeing the update of its electronic archiving system](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=366096&lang=ar>). Users across the ministry's offices shaped the update. On 31 July 2025 the Central Bank of Libya said [it developed and runs the Ratibak Lahzi public payroll platform itself](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/485074>).

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company runs systems for the rest of government. [The ministry's own section runs its archive](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=366096&lang=ar>).

**Built in-house.** In August 2026 the Agency for Combating Illegal Migration [unveiled a unified digital system linking its headquarters and branches](<https://libyareview.com/67705/libya-launches-unified-digital-system-to-monitor-migration-operations/>). The agency says its own engineers built it entirely.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The central bank's payroll platform is one it runs itself](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/485074>).
