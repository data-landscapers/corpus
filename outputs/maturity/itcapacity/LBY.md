# Libya

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Libya's Ministry of Technical and Vocational Education states its Informatics and Documentation Department, through its Information Systems Section, is overseeing the update project of the ministry's electronic archiving system, developed from feedback by users across the ministry's offices](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=366096&lang=ar>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/2024/03/11/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%86-%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1/>).

**Built in-house.** [The General Information Authority thanked its own engineer and technical team who designed and developed the updated tenders platform, which went live](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/2024/03/11/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%86-%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/2024/03/11/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%86-%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1/>).
