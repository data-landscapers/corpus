# Togo

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Cadre_de_digitalisation_des_services_publics/html/gouvernance.html>).

**A technology body for government.** [The digitalisation framework's governance model gives Agence Togo Digital the role of deploying digital solutions for public services, setting standards, and providing the low-code/no-code integration platforms that public institutions' integrators use](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Cadre_de_digitalisation_des_services_publics/html/gouvernance.html>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Cadre_de_digitalisation_des_services_publics/html/gouvernance.html>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Cadre_de_digitalisation_des_services_publics/html/gouvernance.html>).
