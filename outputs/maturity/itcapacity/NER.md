# Niger

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [On 23 April 2025 ANSI, the state ICT agency, handed the National Guard SIGEPAP-GNN](<https://anp.ne/lansi-lance-le-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-de-la-gnn-2/>), its personnel, postings and pensions system. ANSI's technical teams designed and built it.

**A technology body for government.** [ANSI built SIGEPAP-GNN for the National Guard](<https://anp.ne/lansi-lance-le-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-de-la-gnn-2/>) under a memorandum the two signed in August 2024. ANSI is the national information society agency.

**Built in-house.** [ANSI's technical teams designed and developed SIGEPAP-GNN](<https://anp.ne/lansi-lance-le-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-de-la-gnn-2/>). It was presented to the National Guard on 23 April 2025 and described as deployed.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found names three systems run wholly by government staff. The one system named is [SIGEPAP-GNN, built by ANSI for the National Guard in 2025](<https://anp.ne/lansi-lance-le-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-de-la-gnn-2/>).

***Also on file***. [SIGRHEP](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/niger-biometrie-paie-carrieres-la-fonction-publique-se-numerise>); [Solde2Budget](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/niger-biometrie-paie-carrieres-la-fonction-publique-se-numerise>).

***Not found***. No source says who runs SIGEPAP-GNN since its handover. No source names three or more systems run wholly by government staff.
