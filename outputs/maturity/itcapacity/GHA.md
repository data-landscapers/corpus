# Ghana

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The NIA's Acting Executive Secretary said on 21 September 2025 that the National Identification System relies on its PPP partner Margins ID Group for technical expertise, system maintenance and card production, and that NIA alone could not repair equipment quickly under government procurement rules](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [this source](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [this source](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [this source](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ghana Electronic Document Wallet (GEDW)](<https://nita.gov.gh/gedw-stakeholder-engagement/>).

***Not held***. Nothing further is asked of this indicator.
