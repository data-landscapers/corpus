# Ghana

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Ghana Revenue Authority and NITA built the CitizenApp, an upgrade of Ghana.gov, with other state agencies](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/10/bawumia-unveils-new-citizenapp-to-promote-efficiency-in-public-service-delivery/>). Not every system is in-house. [India's Lightwave built and runs the health ministry's LHIMS under a 2019 contract](<https://techfocus24.com/data-protection-commission-probes-indian-owned-data-management-system-in-ghanas-hospitals/>). In April 2025 the communications minister said [the contractor KelniGVG runs the platform monitoring inbound international traffic](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-communication-ministry-saddled-with-debt-strangulating-contracts-minister.html>). He told the regulator to take it over.

**A technology body for government.** [NITA runs the Government Cloud and the NITA Data Centre](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>). Ministries and agencies can host on them, though some bypass them. In August 2024 its director general said [NITA serves all public institutions from its Tier 3 data centre](<https://techfocus24.com/nita-gets-three-iso-certifications-at-a-go/>), managed with the private partner Smart Infraco.

**Built in-house.** [The Vice President launched the CitizenApp on 7 October 2024](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/10/bawumia-unveils-new-citizenapp-to-promote-efficiency-in-public-service-delivery/>). No contractor is named. It gathers public services, from water bills to passports, in one app.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The CitizenApp is the one named](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/10/bawumia-unveils-new-citizenapp-to-promote-efficiency-in-public-service-delivery/>), and a private partner runs the national identity system.

***Also on file***. [Ghana Electronic Document Wallet (GEDW)](<https://nita.gov.gh/gedw-stakeholder-engagement/>); [G-Cloud](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).
