# Ghana

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The NIA's Acting Executive Secretary said on 21 September 2025 that the National Identification System relies on its PPP partner Margins ID Group for technical expertise, system maintenance and card production, and that NIA alone could not repair equipment quickly under government procurement rules](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

**A technology body for government.** [The analyst says NITA, mandated under Act 771 to provide core government IT infrastructure, runs the Government Cloud (G-Cloud) and the NITA Data Centre that ministries, departments and agencies can host on or bypass](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ghana Electronic Document Wallet (GEDW)](<https://nita.gov.gh/gedw-stakeholder-engagement/>); [G-Cloud](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
