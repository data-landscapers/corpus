# Botswana

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Botswana Examinations Council built its DataSmart application in-house](<https://www.bec.co.bw/images/2025/annual-report/bec-annual-report-2025-final-09-12-25_.pdf>). It captures marks for the PSLE, JCE and BGCSE.

**A technology body for government.** [The communications minister said government services are housed at the Department of Shared Digital Services](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>). Government plans to move them to the new Bofinet data centre. The statement was published in November 2025.

**Built in-house.** [The Council's 2024/25 annual report says DataSmart was developed and deployed that year](<https://www.bec.co.bw/images/2025/annual-report/bec-annual-report-2025-final-09-12-25_.pdf>). It serves all three examination levels. The report says it made mark capture more accurate.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [DataSmart is the one named](<https://www.bec.co.bw/images/2025/annual-report/bec-annual-report-2025-final-09-12-25_.pdf>). The Council's other systems, Malepa, e-marking and e-authoring, are not said to be built in-house.
