# Malawi

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Malawi Revenue Authority said in October 2026 that it is developing its second Integrated Tax Administration System, ITAS II, in-house](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>). [In April 2025 the Electoral Commission had its ICT Directorate upload 2,563 outstanding voter records onto the Election Management System database](<https://mec.org.mw/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/20250408-Speech-for-press-briefing-on-Voter-Transfers-and-Inspection-of-Voters-Register.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** No source found since October 2023 names a body that builds or runs systems for the rest of government. [The Revenue Authority builds its own tax system](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>), not systems for other bodies.

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. [ITAS II was still being developed in October 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found names a system that government staff run with no contractor doing the work. [The Electoral Commission's own ICT Directorate works on its Election Management System](<https://mec.org.mw/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/20250408-Speech-for-press-briefing-on-Voter-Transfers-and-Inspection-of-Voters-Register.pdf>).
