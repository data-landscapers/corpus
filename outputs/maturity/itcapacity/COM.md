## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** In May 2026 the head of information systems at the health insurer AMG said [its openIMIS system still depends heavily on external developers](<https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques>). Only two local developers had been trained. The local firm ETECH was not operating.

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government. The one held fact is [AMG's account of its reliance on outside developers](<https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. As of May 2026 [AMG's openIMIS depended on outside developers](<https://openimis.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OP/pages/5154603013/Webinaire+Souverainet+Appropriation+des+syst+mes+de+sant+num+riques>).
