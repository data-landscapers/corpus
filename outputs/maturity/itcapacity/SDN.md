# Sudan

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** In January 2025 Sudan Customs said [its own software officers programmed the electronic customs valuation database](<https://sagiapress.net/2025/01/07/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%b4%d9%86-%d9%86%d8%b8%d8%a7%d9%85/>) and the e-violations system. In September 2025 customs launched [an electronic container tracking system at Southern Port](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/09/01/customs-launches-electronic-container-tracking-system-at-southern-port/>). Its own ICT directorate developed it with Zain Telecom.

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government. [Customs' IT directorate builds for customs alone](<https://sagiapress.net/2025/01/07/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%b4%d9%86-%d9%86%d8%b8%d8%a7%d9%85/>).

**Built in-house.** The acting director general [launched the customs valuation database at North Port in January 2025](<https://sagiapress.net/2025/01/07/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%b4%d9%86-%d9%86%d8%b8%d8%a7%d9%85/>). Customs IT officers built it, not a contractor.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The valuation database is the one found](<https://sagiapress.net/2025/01/07/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1-%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85-%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b1%d9%83-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%83%d9%84%d9%81-%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%b4%d9%86-%d9%86%d8%b8%d8%a7%d9%85/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No government body is found building or running systems for the rest of government since 2023.
