# Congo

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-laden-examine-ses-documents-cadres-161959>).

**A technology body for government.** [Congo's Agence de développement de l'économie numérique (Aden) is stated to ensure the technical management of the government's official web portal gouvernement.cg, per Les Dépêches de Brazzaville](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-laden-examine-ses-documents-cadres-161959>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-laden-examine-ses-documents-cadres-161959>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-laden-examine-ses-documents-cadres-161959>).
