# Burundi

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>).

**A technology body for government.** [SETIC, the government ICT secretariat, lists as its services developing software and applications for public institutions, hosting national platforms, running a network operations centre and operating and maintaining the network and systems infrastructure of public institutions](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Système Intégré de Gestion des Finances Publiques (SIGFP)](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/04/08/le-burundi-prepare-la-mise-en-place-du-systeme-integre-de-gestion-des-finances-publiques/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
