# Burundi

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems. [SETIC is the government ICT secretariat](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>). It lists software development and hosting for public institutions among its services.

**A technology body for government.** [SETIC, the government ICT secretariat, lists its services for public institutions](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>). [They include developing software and applications, hosting national platforms and running a network operations centre](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>). [They also include operating and maintaining the network and systems infrastructure of public institutions](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff, not by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023. [SETIC is the government ICT secretariat](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>). It lists software development and hosting for public institutions among its services.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work. [SETIC is the government ICT secretariat](<https://setic.gov.bi/?pages%2F10253095=>). It lists software development and hosting for public institutions among its services.

***Not found***. No source found from 2023 or later says government staff run or maintain any of its systems.
