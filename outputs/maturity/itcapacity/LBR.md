# Liberia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Liberia Revenue Authority programmers developed the online police clearance platform](<https://gnnliberia.com/lnp-lra-digitize-police-clearance-in-major-reform/>). The authority's information services division said it showed local professionals' skills. The finance ministry's budget system is run differently. In 2024 FrontPageAfrica reported that [the vendor RoviaGate runs and reconfigures it under annual contracts](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-frontpageafricas-latest-investigation-unveils-procurement-breach-in-ministry-of-finance-roviagate-contract/>), at US$200,000 that year.

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company runs systems for the rest of government. [The revenue authority built the police platform](<https://gnnliberia.com/lnp-lra-digitize-police-clearance-in-major-reform/>) for one other body.

**Built in-house.** [The police clearance platform was launched at police headquarters on 1 May 2026](<https://gnnliberia.com/lnp-lra-digitize-police-clearance-in-major-reform/>). It replaced a manual process. The revenue authority says [its own tech team built and launched its online duty-free application platform](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-digitizes-rural-customs-offices-rolls-out-online-duty-free-platform/>) in July 2025.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The police clearance platform is one built by government staff](<https://gnnliberia.com/lnp-lra-digitize-police-clearance-in-major-reform/>).
