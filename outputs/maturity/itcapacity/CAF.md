# Central African Republic

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** In September 2026 Expertise France advertised for [local IT experts to run the tax directorate's servers and administer SIGICA](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/i5pour100/16150/1/safe-rca-expert-e-local-e-en-informatique-h-f>), the tax system. The posts belong to SAFE, an EU-funded project for the finance ministry. SIGICA's publisher and an outside contractor deliver its modules and the SIM_ba budget system.

**A technology body for government.** No source found from 2023 or later says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government. The one held fact is [the EU project's posting for outside IT experts](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/i5pour100/16150/1/safe-rca-expert-e-local-e-en-informatique-h-f>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. In 2026 [the tax system's modules came from its publisher and a contractor](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/i5pour100/16150/1/safe-rca-expert-e-local-e-en-informatique-h-f>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found names a system run wholly by government staff. [The tax directorate's servers are to be run by project-hired experts](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/i5pour100/16150/1/safe-rca-expert-e-local-e-en-informatique-h-f>).

***Also on file***. [NADA RCA (Portail National d'Archivage des Données)](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/355-nada-rca-nouvelle-interface-de-la-page-daccueil>).

***Not found***. No source found from 2023 or later says government staff run or maintain any of its systems.
