# Tanzania

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The e-Government Authority says it developed the M-Kilimo system with the Ministry of Agriculture, the MUVU Cooperative Management System with the Cooperative Development Commission, and the Fertiliser Information System with the fertiliser regulator](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>).

**A technology body for government.** [The e-Government Authority, mandated to coordinate ICT across public institutions, runs MUVU (over 6,000 cooperative societies registered), M-Kilimo, the Government Mailing System and e-Office for the agriculture ministry and its institutions](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [National ICT Broadband Backbone (NICTBB)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/23/ttcl-equips-dit-students-with-practical-broadband-infrastructure-skills/>).
