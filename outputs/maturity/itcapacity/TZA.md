# Tanzania

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Business Wiz reports the National e-Procurement System (NeST) is developed and maintained by the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority's own technical team](<https://thebusinesswiz.co.tz/2026/09/25/tanzanias-digital-procurement-system-cuts-processing-time-and-costs/>). [The e-Government Authority says it developed the M-Kilimo system with the Ministry of Agriculture, the MUVU Cooperative Management System with the Cooperative Development Commission, and the Fertiliser Information System with the fertiliser regulator](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>).

**A technology body for government.** [The e-Government Authority, mandated to coordinate ICT across public institutions, runs MUVU (over 6,000 cooperative societies registered), M-Kilimo, the Government Mailing System and e-Office for the agriculture ministry and its institutions](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>). [The e-Government Authority says its Msaada AI system is used by the Tanzania Tourist Board and the President's Office Public Service Management](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Built in-house.** [The Integrated National Digital Library System was developed by the Tanzania Library Services Board with the e-Government Authority and launched by the Education Minister at a conference running 20-22 October 2026](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>). [The e-Government Authority says Msaada AI, designed at its own research centre, is in use by two institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The e-Government Authority designed Msaada AI at its own research centre and keeps improving it from users' feedback](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>). [NeST is developed and maintained by the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority's own technical team](<https://thebusinesswiz.co.tz/2026/09/25/tanzanias-digital-procurement-system-cuts-processing-time-and-costs/>). [The e-Government Authority developed the M-Kilimo system with the Ministry of Agriculture](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>). [The Integrated National Digital Library System was developed by the Tanzania Library Services Board with the e-Government Authority](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>).
