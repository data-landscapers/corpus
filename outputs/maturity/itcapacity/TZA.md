# Tanzania

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [NeST, the national e-procurement system, is developed and maintained by the procurement regulator's own technical team](<https://thebusinesswiz.co.tz/2026/09/25/tanzanias-digital-procurement-system-cuts-processing-time-and-costs/>), The Business Wiz reports. [e-GA says it built the government service bus itself and runs it](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). The e-Government Authority [developed M-Kilimo, the MUVU cooperative system and the fertiliser information system](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>) with the bodies that use them.

**A technology body for government.** [e-GA was set up by Act No. 10 of 2019 to coordinate ICT in public institutions](<http://www.michuzi.co.tz/2024/07/ega-yajipanga-katika-katika-kubuni.html>). [By July 2024 it had connected 148 institutions to the government service bus](<http://www.michuzi.co.tz/2024/07/ega-yajipanga-katika-katika-kubuni.html>). [It runs MUVU, with over 6,000 cooperative societies, M-Kilimo, the government mail system and e-Office](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>). [Its Msaada AI system is used by the Tourist Board and the public service office](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Built in-house.** [The Library Services Board and e-GA developed the national digital library system](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>), launched in October 2026. [Msaada AI, designed at e-GA's own research centre, is in use by two institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The e-Government Authority designed Msaada AI at its own research centre and keeps improving it from users' feedback](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>). [NeST is developed and maintained by the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority's own technical team](<https://thebusinesswiz.co.tz/2026/09/25/tanzanias-digital-procurement-system-cuts-processing-time-and-costs/>). [The e-Government Authority developed the M-Kilimo system with the Ministry of Agriculture](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ega-drives-digital-farming/>). [The Integrated National Digital Library System was developed by the Tanzania Library Services Board with the e-Government Authority](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>).
