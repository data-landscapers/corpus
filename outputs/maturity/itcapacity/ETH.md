# Ethiopia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INSA says its own developed tools, including Ergamail and the electronic tax system, are already in use across key government agencies](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Ethiopian Business Review reports INSA says it has developed government systems, including the Ergamail email client deployed across federal institutions and the electronic tax system used by the Ethiopian Revenues and Customs Authority](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Fayda national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners>); [livestock information systems](<https://developmentgateway.org/blog/a-year-after-usaid-reclaiming-control-of-data-systems/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
