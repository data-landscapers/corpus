# Ethiopia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INSA, the state's network security agency, says tools it developed are in use across key government agencies](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>). [They include the Ergamail email client and the electronic tax system](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>). [Since 4 August 2026 the state-owned FaydaVerse, not a supplier, has managed the national identity system](<https://kanatelevision.com/news/what-is-faydaverse-the-ethiopian-national-id-program-that-became-a-company-and-how-it-plans-to/>). [The ID programme said in 2024 that it had in-house technical and cybersecurity teams](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [INSA leads the Federal Supreme Court's court management and electronic records systems](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>), Addis Fortune reports. [It says it built the Ergamail email client used across federal institutions](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>), and the revenue authority's electronic tax system. [FaydaVerse's e-services arm manages government platforms for commerce, social protection, agriculture, tax and trade](<https://kanatelevision.com/news/what-is-faydaverse-the-ethiopian-national-id-program-that-became-a-company-and-how-it-plans-to/>).

**Built in-house.** [The National Election Board said in June 2025 that its voter and candidate registration applications were operational](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6739876>). Its own technical team designed them entirely. All candidate registration for the coming election was to run through them. [In October 2026 a cyber drill platform built entirely by INSA's experts was used by 17 institutions](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8150/ethiopia-s-insa-organises-national-cyber-drill-competition-to-strengthen-institutional-cybersecurity-readiness>) during the national cyber security month.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The election board's own team designed its voter and candidate registration applications entirely](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6739876>). [INSA's experts built the cyber drill platform entirely](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8150/ethiopia-s-insa-organises-national-cyber-drill-competition-to-strengthen-institutional-cybersecurity-readiness>). [FaydaVerse, a state company and not a supplier, manages the national identity system](<https://kanatelevision.com/news/what-is-faydaverse-the-ethiopian-national-id-program-that-became-a-company-and-how-it-plans-to/>).
