# Ethiopia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INSA, the state's network security agency, says tools it developed are in use across key government agencies](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>). They include the Ergamail email client and the electronic tax system. [In October 2026 its own experts built the cyber drill platform used by 17 institutions](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8150/ethiopia-s-insa-organises-national-cyber-drill-competition-to-strengthen-institutional-cybersecurity-readiness>).

**A technology body for government.** [INSA leads the Federal Supreme Court's court management and electronic records systems](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>), Addis Fortune reports. [It says it built the Ergamail email client used across federal institutions](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>), and the revenue authority's electronic tax system.

**Built in-house.** [The National Election Board said in June 2025 that its voter and candidate registration applications were operational](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6739876>). Its own technical team designed them entirely. All candidate registration for the coming election was to run through them. [In October 2026 a cyber drill platform built entirely by INSA's experts was used by 17 institutions](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8150/ethiopia-s-insa-organises-national-cyber-drill-competition-to-strengthen-institutional-cybersecurity-readiness>) during the national cyber security month.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run, maintained and changed by government staff alone. The nearest held fact: [INSA's account of the tools it developed for government](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).
