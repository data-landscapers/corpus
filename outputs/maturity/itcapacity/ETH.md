# Ethiopia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INSA says its own developed tools, including Ergamail and the electronic tax system, are already in use across key government agencies](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Addis Fortune reports INSA, the Information Network Security Administration, is spearheading the Federal Supreme Court's Integrated Court Management Information System and Electronic Records Management System](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>). [Ethiopian Business Review reports INSA says it has developed government systems, including the Ergamail email client deployed across federal institutions and the electronic tax system used by the Ethiopian Revenues and Customs Authority](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**Built in-house.** [The National Election Board said in June 2025 that its voter and candidate registration applications, designed entirely by its in-house technical team, were operational](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6739876>). All candidate registration for the coming election was to run through them.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>).

***Also on file***. [Fayda national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/zambia-plans-domestic-digital-id-development-with-help-from-international-partners>); [livestock information systems](<https://developmentgateway.org/blog/a-year-after-usaid-reclaiming-control-of-data-systems/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff.
