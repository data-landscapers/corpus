# Gabon

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [PROXIGAB, an SME platform, was designed and developed by ANINF's own experts, per the Gabonese press agency](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). [The GED document management system is developed by ANINF, with World Bank and other partner support, per GabonReview](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-la-ged-amorce-le-basculement-de-ladministration-vers-le-numerique/>). [The GED electronic document management platform was developed with ANINF's expertise, per Tech Africa News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/gabon-launches-electronic-document-management-platform/>). [ANINF said in January 2026 that its own authorised teams administer and operate FUR, the state payroll file in service for over 20 years](<https://www.gabonreview.com/communique-mise-au-point-sur-le-traitement-de-la-solde-de-letat/>).

**A technology body for government.** [ANINF signed a minute on 2 October 2026 handing the PROXIGAB solution to the SME directorate-general, a platform it built for another government body](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). [ANINF is building a shared digital base for the 29 ministries to connect to progressively, starting with two pilot administrations](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-la-ged-amorce-le-basculement-de-ladministration-vers-le-numerique/>).

**Built in-house.** [ANINF experts' PROXIGAB platform was officially presented and handed over on 2 October 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [ANINF, the state digital agency, designed the FUR civil-service HR and payroll application and is fixing its coding failures](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/fonction-publique-redynamiser-le-fichier-unique-de-reference>). [The KEWA platform, which published the 2026 primary-school certificate results, is run by the state agency ANINF, its engineers credited for it](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/06/27/cep-2026-kewa-revolutionne-la-publication-des-resultats-laninf-confirme-son-leadership-dans-la-transformation-numerique-du-gabon/>). [ANINF's own directorates manage its SecBOM digital-asset mapping system](<https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/>). [The PROXIGAB SME platform was designed and developed by ANINF's own experts](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>).
