# Gabon

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [ANINF's own experts designed and developed PROXIGAB, an SME platform](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). [ANINF develops the GED document management system](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-la-ged-amorce-le-basculement-de-ladministration-vers-le-numerique/>), with World Bank support. [ANINF said in January 2026 that its own authorised teams administer and operate FUR, the state payroll file in service for over 20 years](<https://www.gabonreview.com/communique-mise-au-point-sur-le-traitement-de-la-solde-de-letat/>). The budget system Vectis is run differently. From 2021 to 2025 the budget directorate [paid the vendor Involys FCFA 4.92 billion to support, maintain and extend it](<https://depeches241.com/?p=29580>).

**A technology body for government.** On 2 October 2026 ANINF [handed the PROXIGAB platform it built to the SME directorate-general](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). ANINF [is building a shared digital base for the 29 ministries](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-la-ged-amorce-le-basculement-de-ladministration-vers-le-numerique/>), starting with two pilot administrations. It [develops and maintains ministries' business applications](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-des-dcsi-en-poste-pour-batir-la-souverainete-numerique-du-pays/>), Gabonreview said in October 2024.

**Built in-house.** [ANINF experts' PROXIGAB platform was officially presented and handed over on 2 October 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). In January 2026 the delegate budget minister [launched SIGFIP, which he said was developed in-house](<https://gabon-info.com/2026/01/15/le-gabon-reprend-la-main-sur-sa-souverainete-financiere-et-numerique/>). In April 2026 the National Assembly [launched GTLAN, a legislative platform ANINF built](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0405-55229-gabon-s-national-assembly-launches-digital-platform-to-modernise-legislative-process>). In August 2026 the budget directorate's own IT unit [launched OLIA, a staff database](<https://www.dgb-gabon.net/actualite-detail/84/details>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [ANINF, the state digital agency, designed the FUR civil-service HR and payroll application and is fixing its coding failures](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/fonction-publique-redynamiser-le-fichier-unique-de-reference>). [The KEWA platform, which published the 2026 primary-school certificate results, is run by the state agency ANINF, its engineers credited for it](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/06/27/cep-2026-kewa-revolutionne-la-publication-des-resultats-laninf-confirme-son-leadership-dans-la-transformation-numerique-du-gabon/>). [ANINF's own directorates manage its SecBOM digital-asset mapping system](<https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/>). [The PROXIGAB SME platform was designed and developed by ANINF's own experts](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>).
