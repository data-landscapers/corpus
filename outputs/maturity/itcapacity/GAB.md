# Gabon

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The GED electronic document management platform was developed with ANINF's expertise, per Tech Africa News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/gabon-launches-electronic-document-management-platform/>). [PROXIGAB, an SME platform, was designed and developed by ANINF's own experts, per the Gabonese press agency](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>). [The GED document management system is developed by ANINF, with World Bank and other partner support, per GabonReview](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-la-ged-amorce-le-basculement-de-ladministration-vers-le-numerique/>).

**A technology body for government.** [ANINF supports administrations including the government secretariat and exams directorate with the GED, document security and user support](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/gabon-launches-electronic-document-management-platform/>). [ANINF signed a minute on 2 October 2026 handing the PROXIGAB solution to the SME directorate-general, a platform it built for another government body](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>).

**Built in-house.** [ANINF experts' PROXIGAB platform was officially presented and handed over on 2 October 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-entrepreneuriat-la-plateforme-numerique-proxigab-officiellement-lancee/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/gabon-launches-electronic-document-management-platform/>).
