# Mali

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The ITU framework states AGETIC, a government agency, administers a government data centre with its own specialised cybersecurity team, running its security and a real-time-replicated disaster-recovery site (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [L'Essor reports AGETIC, the state ICT agency, is progressively interconnecting all public and para-public services into the government Intranet, with 13 services integrated in a single-window portal, and managing 13,441 .ml domain names](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>). [The ITU framework states AGETIC, an agency under the communications and digital ministry, provides hosting services (IaaS, SaaS, backup) to the public administration from its data centre (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Single-window portal of public services](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>); [.ml country-code domain registry](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>); [Guichet unique](<https://agetic.gouv.ml/sous-la-presidence-de-monsieur-le-ministre-de-la-communication-de-leconomie-numerique-et-de-la-modernisation-de-ladministration-sest-tenue-la-session-du-conseil-d/>); [Mali national fibre-optic backbone (SMTD-SA)](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0810-142240-le-mali-ouvre-une-nouvelle-route-vers-l-internet-mondial-par-le-senegal>); [SMTD data centre](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
