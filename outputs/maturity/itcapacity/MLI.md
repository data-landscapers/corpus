# Mali

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [AGETIC, the state ICT agency, managed 13,441 .ml domain names and 30 registrars in December 2025](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>). It runs the country's domain registry. [In 2022 it also ran a government data centre with its own cybersecurity team](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [AGETIC is linking all public services into the government Intranet](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>). [Its single-window portal held 13 services in December 2025](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>). [In 2022 it hosted systems for the administration from its data centre](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** On 17 April 2026 the mines minister presented [a new mining cadastre built entirely by public administration staff](<https://maliactuel.com/secteur-minier-le-president-de-la-transition-preside-la-presentation-du-nouveau-cadastre-minier/>). It held 631 mining titles. [Online portals for criminal records and nationality certificates were launched on 10 February 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mali-launches-online-criminal-record-and-nationality-certificate-services>). The digital ministry and AGETIC developed them, and no contractor is named.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The mining cadastre replaced an old system that depended on foreign companies](<https://maliactuel.com/secteur-minier-le-president-de-la-transition-preside-la-presentation-du-nouveau-cadastre-minier/>). [The SIGRH civil-service HR system was designed by the administration's own IT staff](<https://www.maliweb.net/fonction-publique/operationnalisation-du-sigrh-tres-bientot-un-outil-efficace-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-de-letat-3106550.html>) and paid for from the national budget. In February 2025 the justice ministry launched [court case-management software developed at AGETIC](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/troisieme-edition-de-la-semaine-de-la-justice-le-logiciel-de-gestion-des-dossiers-judiciaires-lance-3093413.html>). No contractor is named for any of the three.
