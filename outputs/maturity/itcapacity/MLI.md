# Mali

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The ITU framework states that AGETIC, a government agency, runs a government data centre with its own cybersecurity team and a replicated disaster-recovery site (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [L'Essor reports AGETIC, the state ICT agency, is progressively interconnecting all public and para-public services into the government Intranet, with 13 services integrated in a single-window portal, and managing 13,441 .ml domain names](<https://lessor.ml/posts/cyber-espace-le-mali-renforce-sa-viabilite-avec-le-point-ml-69538f175e535>). [The ITU framework states AGETIC, an agency under the communications and digital ministry, provides hosting services (IaaS, SaaS, backup) to the public administration from its data centre (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** [Online portals for criminal records and nationality certificates, developed by the digital ministry and AGETIC with the Ministry of Justice, were launched on 10 February 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mali-launches-online-criminal-record-and-nationality-certificate-services>). No contractor is named.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The SIGRH human resources software was designed and developed by the administration's own computer specialists and paid for from the national budget](<https://www.maliweb.net/fonction-publique/operation-didentification-des-agents-de-la-fonction-publique-de-letat-et-des-collectivites-la-deuxieme-phase-du-projet-sigrh-lancee-a-segou-3041661.html>). Its second phase began in October 2023, with its database to be hosted in Mali.
