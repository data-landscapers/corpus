# Sierra Leone

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The finance ministry's DFMST, set up in 2022, supports mission-critical systems such as IFMIS](<https://mof.gov.sl/dfmst/>). In November 2026 [ICT officers in ministries and agencies began training to run their own websites on gov.sl](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/09/sierra-leone-begins-training-government-ict-officers-on-website-management-and-security/>). The e-procurement system is run differently. A December 2023 tender [made the contracted firm responsible for running and supporting it](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Design-DevelopmentCustomization-Supply-Installation-and-Maintenance-of-an-Electronic-Government-Procurement-e-GP-System-in-Sierra-Leone.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [The communications ministry's Directorate of Technology is mandated to help ministries plan, deploy and maintain software](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>). [It also builds government-wide data infrastructure and directs e-Cabinet, e-Parliament and citizen portals](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>). Tech Africa News reported in November 2026 that [the ministry builds and hosts official websites for ministries and agencies on shared gov.sl infrastructure](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/09/sierra-leone-begins-training-government-ict-officers-on-website-management-and-security/>). In July 2025 the energy ministry [commissioned DSTI to build its energy dashboard, asset database and market portal](<https://owlpress-sl.com/dsti-and-ministry-of-energy-launch-development-of-core-energy-sector-systems/>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023. [The communications ministry builds government websites](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/09/sierra-leone-begins-training-government-ict-officers-on-website-management-and-security/>), but no launch date is given.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run three or more systems with no contractor doing the work. The nearest found fact: [the finance ministry's DFMST supports IFMIS and CSDRMS](<https://mof.gov.sl/dfmst/>).
