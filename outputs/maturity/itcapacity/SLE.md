# Sierra Leone

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Ministry of Finance's DFMST, established in 2022, lists providing technical support to mission critical systems such as IFMIS and CSDRMS among its core functions](<https://mof.gov.sl/dfmst/>).

**A technology body for government.** [The MoCTI Directorate of Technology, with a Software Development and Application Services Unit and a Data Science Unit, is stated to be mandated to support MDAs in planning, deploying and maintaining software, to build and maintain government-wide data infrastructure, and to direct delivery of e-Cabinet, e-Parliament and citizen portals](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [e-Cabinet](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>); [e-Parliament](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-technology/>); [IFMIS](<https://mof.gov.sl/dfmst/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
