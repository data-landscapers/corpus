# Seychelles

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [A National Assembly committee visit found the Department of Information Communication Technology's own Software Development Section develops and tests applications for the Electronic Government Network and its Network Operations Section administers the network; DICT has 74 staff, 75% technical and 90% Seychellois, per its Principal Secretary](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2025-06/media-youth-sports-and-culture-committee-conduct-visit-department-information>).

**A technology body for government.** [The Department of Information Communication Technology is stated to run the Electronic Government Network and develop applications for use across it by ministries, departments and agencies, and to support their IT operations day to day](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2025-06/media-youth-sports-and-culture-committee-conduct-visit-department-information>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2025-06/media-youth-sports-and-culture-committee-conduct-visit-department-information>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2025-06/media-youth-sports-and-culture-committee-conduct-visit-department-information>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ease of Doing Business Portal](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/tender-Award_Sep-Dec_2025_-_board_only.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
