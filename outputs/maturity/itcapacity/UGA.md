# Uganda

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [A NITA-U IT professional said UGHub is a national data integration platform managed by NITA-U, using infrastructure developed by NITA-U](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [NITA-U's own UGPass technical lead and director of e-government services describe running UGHub and UGPass and developing a digital signature service on UGPass](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nita-u-moves-to-replace-paper-based-land-veri-NV_240761_092026>). [NITA-U's terms of reference state that NITA-U operates the National Public Key Infrastructure at the National Data Centre](<https://nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-08/PKI%20Audit%20TORs-%20Final%20-%2019-08-26.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [NITA-U manages UGHub, which connects the NIRA registry to more than 150 ministries, departments and agencies, per a NITA-U IT professional](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [An opinion writer says the National Data Centre hosts government cloud, backup and disaster recovery services and that NITA-U records more than 300 applications for 100 entities](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/blogs/plan-now-for-infrastructure-to-power-ai-NV_242203_102026>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nita-u-moves-to-replace-paper-based-land-veri-NV_240761_092026>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [NITA-U's own staff run UGPass and are developing a digital signature service on it](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nita-u-moves-to-replace-paper-based-land-veri-NV_240761_092026>). [NITA-U operates the National Public Key Infrastructure at the National Data Centre](<https://nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-08/PKI%20Audit%20TORs-%20Final%20-%2019-08-26.pdf>). [ECCMIS, the courts' case management system, was developed locally by Judiciary staff, who support it](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>). [NITA-U sets up, integrates, supports and maintains UGHub, the data integration platform in the government data centre](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>).
