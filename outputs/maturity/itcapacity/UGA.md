# Uganda

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [A NITA-U IT professional said UGHub is a national data integration platform managed by NITA-U, using infrastructure developed by NITA-U](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [NITA-U's own UGPass technical lead and director of e-government services describe running UGHub and UGPass and developing a digital signature service on UGPass](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nita-u-moves-to-replace-paper-based-land-veri-NV_240761_092026>). [NITA-U's executive director says NITA-U's UGHub data integration platform currently connects 37 percent of ministries, departments and agencies, with a target of 73 percent by FY2029/30](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/nita-u-launches-strategic-plan-fy202526-fy202930-boost-reach-digital-government>).

**A technology body for government.** [NITA-U manages UGHub, which connects the NIRA registry to more than 150 ministries, departments and agencies, per a NITA-U IT professional](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [An opinion writer says the National Data Centre hosts government cloud, backup and disaster recovery services and that NITA-U records more than 300 applications for 100 entities](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/blogs/plan-now-for-infrastructure-to-power-ai-NV_242203_102026>). [NITA-U says its National Data Centre and Disaster Recovery Centre host more than 308 government applications for over 100 ministries, departments and agencies](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>).
