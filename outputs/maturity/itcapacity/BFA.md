# Burkina Faso

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The state ICT agency ANPTIC lists [the government systems it has developed](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>). They include Bondoo, Tawale, Toumnana (SIGED), SIGEPE, a helpdesk and e-conseil des ministres. The page is undated. [From 2007 a government engineer led the upkeep of RESINA, the national administration network](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338>).

**A technology body for government.** ANPTIC [helped design the ANAGRASC claims-registration platform](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/03/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-pour-le-recensement-du-passif-de-trois-societes-immobilieres/>), launched on 3 September 2026. [As ANPTIC's director of operations, one official oversaw RESINA and the administration's messaging platforms](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148508=>).

**Built in-house.** The Treasury's director general said [Lanaya and e-BDT were developed by the Treasury's own administration](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>). They were launched on 3 April 2026 in Ouagadougou. On 2 April 2024 the digital ministry [launched the ATI platform, developed in-house by its own directorates](<https://gouvernement.gov.bf/actualites/dematerialisation-des-procedures-de-ladministration-la-plateforme-ati-lancee-officiellement/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The Treasury's own administration, not a contractor, developed Lanaya](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>). [It also developed e-BDT](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>). [ANPTIC lists Toumnana (SIGED) among the government systems it developed](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>). [It lists SIGEPE too](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>). [The ATI platform names no contractor](<https://gouvernement.gov.bf/actualites/dematerialisation-des-procedures-de-ladministration-la-plateforme-ati-lancee-officiellement/>).
