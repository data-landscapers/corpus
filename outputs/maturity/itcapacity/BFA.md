# Burkina Faso

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [ANPTIC lists under its own 'services and applications developed' the government systems Bondoo, Tawale, Toumnana (SIGED), SIGEPE, Helpdesk and e-conseil des ministres (undated page, published date used)](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>). [The profile states that from 2007 an engineer in the government's Direction generale chargee de la coordination des programmes TIC led the maintenance, extension and reinforcement of RESINA, the national administration network, including fibre backbone, WiMAX stations, servers, IP telephony, DNS and DHCP (2007, before the three-year window)](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148338>).

**A technology body for government.** [The ANAGRASC claims-registration platform was designed with the help of ANPTIC, the state ICT agency, and launched on 3 September 2026](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/03/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-pour-le-recensement-du-passif-de-trois-societes-immobilieres/>). [Profile states that as ANPTIC director of operations and technical support he oversaw operation of RESINA, the government network, and the administration's institutional messaging platforms](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148508=>).

**Built in-house.** [The Treasury's director general said Lanaya and e-BDT were developed by the Treasury's own administration (not a contractor), and they were officially launched on 3 April 2026 in Ouagadougou](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The Treasury's own administration, not a contractor, developed Lanaya](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>). [The Treasury's own administration, not a contractor, developed e-BDT](<https://www.sidwaya.info/tresor-public-lanaya-et-e-bdt-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>). [ANPTIC, the state ICT agency, lists Toumnana (SIGED) among the government systems it developed](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>). [ANPTIC, the state ICT agency, lists SIGEPE among the government systems it developed](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>).
