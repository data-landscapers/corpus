# Benin

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The national agency ASIN developed the electronic signature, encryption and secure messaging solutions that 300 police officers were equipped with in a training campaign from 26 June to 8 July 2026 (date taken from the article's published date for the campaign end)](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

**A technology body for government.** [ASIN, the national information systems agency, made its own developed signature, encryption and secure messaging solutions available to the Republican Police as national tools for securing digital exchanges](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>). [ASIN, formed by merging four bodies under the finance and digital ministries, carries out the national AI strategy and built GPT BJ, a government chatbot on Benin's legal codes presented in May 2023](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Programme de Renforcement des Structures Centrales de Gouvernance (PRSCG) national network](<https://numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/lassi-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-de-la-gestion-des-services-informatiques-de-letat>); [National data centre (PRSCG)](<https://numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/lassi-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-de-la-gestion-des-services-informatiques-de-letat>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
