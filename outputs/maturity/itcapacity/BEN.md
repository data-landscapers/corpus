# Benin

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The national agency ASIN developed the electronic signature, encryption and secure messaging solutions that 300 police officers were equipped with in a training campaign from 26 June to 8 July 2026 (date taken from the article's published date for the campaign end)](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

**A technology body for government.** [ASIN, the national information systems agency, made its own developed signature, encryption and secure messaging solutions available to the Republican Police as national tools for securing digital exchanges](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>). [Levellers says operational delivery of the AI strategy runs through ASIN, formed by merging four predecessor bodies under the finance and digital ministries, which built the GPT BJ chatbot (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Programme de Renforcement des Structures Centrales de Gouvernance (PRSCG) national network](<https://numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/lassi-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-de-la-gestion-des-services-informatiques-de-letat>); [National data centre (PRSCG)](<https://numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/lassi-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-de-la-gestion-des-services-informatiques-de-letat>); [GPT BJ](<https://asin.bj/opportunite/28/dossier-appel-offres-mise-oeuvre-solutions-developpement-socle-assistants-conversationnel-bj/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
