# Benin

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The national agency ASIN [developed the electronic signature, encryption and secure messaging tools issued to 300 police officers in mid-2026](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>). Other systems are run differently. [Cybernetica says it still helps Benin maintain the citizen portal and the data exchange platform](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/benin/>), having built them with eGA as prime contractor. In August 2026 ASIN [gave the private integrator MAPCOM a two-year contract to maintain the state data centre](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/08/24/souverainete-tech-comment-la-sbin-sest-fait-evincer-dun-contrat-cle-de-lasin/>) and renew its software licences.

**A technology body for government.** [ASIN, the national information systems agency, gave its own signature, encryption and messaging tools to the Republican Police](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>) to secure digital exchanges. [Levellers says ASIN delivers the national AI strategy](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>). It was formed by merging four bodies under the finance and digital ministries.

**Built in-house.** [The Justice Ministry's own information systems directorate, with the national criminal-record centre, led the instant criminal-record service that went live on service-public.bj on 1 November 2026](<https://www.linfodrome.com/international/127167-benin-wadagni-concretise-sa-promesse-le-casier-judiciaire-instantane-a-1-900-fcfa>). No contractor is named. [ASIN built the GPT BJ chatbot in 2023](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>). On 31 July 2025 the national response team bjCSIRT [released oju, a monitoring platform it designed and developed itself](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/zonas_github-cybers%C3%A9curit%C3%A9-bjcsirt-activity-7356834874999070720-X0Ge>), as open source.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names a system that government staff run with no contractor doing the work. [The citizen portal and the data exchange platform are maintained with Cybernetica's support](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/benin/>).
