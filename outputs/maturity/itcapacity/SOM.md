# Somalia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government body's own technical staff run, maintain or support one of its named systems; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).

**Built in-house.** [NIRA says it launched three systems (HUBIYE, e-Aqoonsi, CDS) entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank Scaled-UP Project support](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [NIRA's HUBIYE system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). [NIRA's e-Aqoonsi system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). [NIRA's CDS system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).
