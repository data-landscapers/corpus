# Somalia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [NIRA's Director General said in May 2025 that the authority put its in-house expertise into software development](<https://nira.gov.so/news/somalia-showcases-progress-on-national-id-at-id-4-africa-2025-annual-conference>). [It rolled out its own platforms, Hubiye, e-Aqoonsi and the Certificate Delivery System](<https://nira.gov.so/news/somalia-showcases-progress-on-national-id-at-id-4-africa-2025-annual-conference>).

**A technology body for government.** No source found since October 2023 names a government agency or state company that builds or runs systems for ministries. [NIRA built its own identity systems](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).

**Built in-house.** [NIRA launched three systems in May 2025: HUBIYE, e-Aqoonsi and CDS](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). [Somali engineers at NIRA developed all three](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>), with World Bank support.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [NIRA's HUBIYE system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). [NIRA's e-Aqoonsi system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). [NIRA's CDS system was entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, with World Bank project funding](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).
