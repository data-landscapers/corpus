# Somalia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The National Identification and Registration Authority says Somali engineers at NIRA developed its HUBIYE verifier platform, Certificate Delivery System and e-Aqoonsi digital ID app entirely](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>), with the World Bank's Scaled-UP Project supporting the infrastructure and technical capacity behind them.

**A technology body for government.** The nearest held fact is NIRA's own: [the Minister of Interior urged institutions across sectors to integrate with HUBIYE](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>), the identity verification platform NIRA launched.

**Built in-house.** [NIRA launched the three systems in May 2025 at an event in Mogadishu](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>), and its Director General said they reflect national ownership and growing technical capacity. The Second Deputy Prime Minister called them pillars of Somalia's digital public infrastructure.

**A career structure for IT staff.** The nearest held fact is the [World Bank's support for the technical capacity and institutional development NIRA needed to implement the platforms](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No government technology body serving the rest of government is held, and no ICT cadre or scheme of service for government IT staff.
