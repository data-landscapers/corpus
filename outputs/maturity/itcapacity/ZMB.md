# Zambia

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The Smart Zambia Institute [runs ZamPass, the national authentication service, with its own ZamPass team](<https://pass.gsb.gov.zm/about>). For other systems it buys help. Under the World Bank-financed DZAP it [sought a consulting firm to build and support a compliance system for the Data Protection Commission](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461498>).

**A technology body for government.** On 4 September 2026 the institute [handed the Smart Office Management System to Zambia Airports Corporation](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/04/sc-encourages-smart-office-platform-utilisation/>). The Secretary to the Cabinet asked it to report by October on bringing ministries, provinces and state companies onto it. [ZamPass is a single sign-on for public services across government](<https://pass.gsb.gov.zm/about>). In September 2025 its e-Government Division said [it had connected all 116 local authorities to the government network](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/08/smart-zambia-unveils-six-milestones-in-drive-toward-a-digitally-empowered-nation/>). It also rolled out two management systems for the local government ministry.

**Built in-house.** The government's chief technology officer said [SmartCare 2.0 is locally developed and owned](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/zambia-targets-nationwide-health-connectivity-as-smartcare-2-0-pilot-goes-live-in-ndola/>). Its pilot went live at Ndola Teaching Hospital on 27 April 2026. SMART Zambia launched it with the Ministry of Health, with IHM Southern Africa as collaborator.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [Smart Zambia Institute operates ZamPass, the national authentication service, with its own ZamPass team](<https://pass.gsb.gov.zm/about>). [SmartCare Pro was developed by the Ministry of Health and the Smart Zambia Institute](<https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-govlink-private-4g-network/G98YdMLGBZw7X2PD>). [SMART Zambia's e-Government Division developed the SMART Office Management System in-house](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/23/zambia-advances-public-sector-digitisation-with-smart-office-system-rollout/>). [SMART Zambia's e-Government Division developed the Citizens Support Portal](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=2814>).
