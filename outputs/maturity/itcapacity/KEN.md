# Kenya

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** Murang'a County's governor says [its in-house software developers enhanced AfyaKE](<https://nation.africa/kenya/counties/muranga/afyake-system-shutdown-65-000-patient-records-at-centre-of-kang-ata-duale-showdown-5634726>), a system adopted from national government. The Ministry of Health calls it a private arrangement between the county and its developers. In Makueni County, [the ICT directorate supports the projects tracking system](<https://umanyi.makueni.go.ke/storage/success_stories/68b949613618a_Success_Story.pdf>) that its own team built.

**A technology body for government.** ICT Authority Kenya is a state corporation. It [runs a network operations centre and a cloud data centre, and designs e-services systems for government](<https://icta.go.ke/faqs>). The Konza data centre [serves government departments and state agencies](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/nakuru-county-explores-partnership-with-konza-technopolis-data-center/>), the Kenya News Agency said in February 2024.

**Built in-house.** Makueni County's in-house ICT team [built its Projects Monitoring and Tracking System without outside vendors](<https://umanyi.makueni.go.ke/storage/success_stories/68b949613618a_Success_Story.pdf>). It was launched in March 2024.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [Makueni's tracking system is one](<https://umanyi.makueni.go.ke/storage/success_stories/68b949613618a_Success_Story.pdf>). The county buys only cloud hosting, SSL, bulk SMS and email hosting as services.
