# Kenya

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Murang'a Governor Irungu Kang'ata says AfyaKE, adopted from a national government system, was enhanced by the county's in-house software developers and its staff were trained to use it; the Ministry of Health says it is a private arrangement between the county and its developers](<https://nation.africa/kenya/counties/muranga/afyake-system-shutdown-65-000-patient-records-at-centre-of-kang-ata-duale-showdown-5634726>).

**A technology body for government.** [ICT Authority Kenya, a state corporation, says it runs a Network Operation Centre and Cloud Data Centre and designs integrated e-services systems for government](<https://icta.go.ke/faqs>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://icta.go.ke/faqs>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://icta.go.ke/faqs>).
