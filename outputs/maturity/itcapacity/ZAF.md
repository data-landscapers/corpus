# South Africa

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Electoral Commission's ICT department runs its own ICT environment on VMware](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/top-ict-tenders-iec-targets-tech-upgrades-renewals/Pero3MZ31YYqQb6m>), and is seeking licence renewal. [It is again seeking bids to redevelop its public mobile app](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/top-ict-tenders-iec-targets-tech-upgrades-renewals/Pero3MZ31YYqQb6m>), its framework called outdated and insecure. [The Road Accident Fund wants a provider to monitor its in-house Microsoft Sentinel threat detection](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/top-ict-tenders-sita-sees-activity-surge/lwrKx73YyZRqmg1o>). [The health department developed the national primary care medical record in-house](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>), a written reply said in June 2026. [Home Affairs said in December 2025 that its own officials and interns run its civic-records digitisation](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34864/>), with no foreign company contracted.

**A technology body for government.** [The communications minister told Parliament that the State IT Agency (SITA) delivered 117 e-services in 2025/26](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/41100/>). [They came through 35 digital products for more than 30 departments and public entities](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/41100/>).

**Built in-house.** [The Electoral Commission built its public mobile app in-house for the 2019 elections](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/top-ict-tenders-iec-targets-tech-upgrades-renewals/Pero3MZ31YYqQb6m>), updating it in 2021 and 2024.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The health department developed its primary care medical record system in-house](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/38770/>), without a procurement adjudication.
