# Nigeria

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Anambra State ICT Agency (ICTA) presented its upgraded SolutionLens platform and moved it into operational use by the state Ministry of Information, with a formal handover planned](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/ict-agency-information-ministry-deepen-digital-collaboration-as-solutionlens-moves-into-operational-use/>). [Galaxy Backbone, the Federal Government's state company, is stated to be responsible for deploying and maintaining the federal public service personnel and payroll system and the government gateway and national portal (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://galaxybackbone.com.ng/mandate/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Galaxy Backbone, described as the Federal Government's digital infrastructure and shared services provider, delivers cloud, connectivity and shared platforms to ministries, departments and agencies](<https://spacewatchafrica.com/galaxy-backbone-unveils-government-service-portal-to-simplify-access-to-public-services/>). [Galaxy Backbone's managing director said more than 30 federal ministries, departments and agencies have moved onto one government platform run by GBB for approving documents and memos paperlessly](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/11/gbb-promises-to-increase-numbers-of-digitalised-mdas-from-current-30-says-adeyanju/>).

**Built in-house.** [The Government Service Portal, stated as developed by Galaxy Backbone in collaboration with the communications ministry and KOICA, was soft-launched on 28 July 2026](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>).
