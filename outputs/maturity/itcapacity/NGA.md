# Nigeria

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Anambra State ICT Agency (ICTA) presented its upgraded SolutionLens platform and moved it into operational use by the state Ministry of Information, with a formal handover planned](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/ict-agency-information-ministry-deepen-digital-collaboration-as-solutionlens-moves-into-operational-use/>). [Galaxy Backbone, the Federal Government's state company, is stated to be responsible for deploying and maintaining the federal public service personnel and payroll system and the government gateway and national portal (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://galaxybackbone.com.ng/mandate/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Galaxy Backbone's managing director said more than 30 federal ministries, departments and agencies have moved onto one government platform run by GBB for approving documents and memos paperlessly](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/11/gbb-promises-to-increase-numbers-of-digitalised-mdas-from-current-30-says-adeyanju/>). [Anambra State ICTA is moving SolutionLens into operational use by the Ministry of Information and Value Reformation, training its officers and issuing them government email accounts](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/ict-agency-information-ministry-deepen-digital-collaboration-as-solutionlens-moves-into-operational-use/>).

**Built in-house.** [The Government Service Portal, stated as developed by Galaxy Backbone in collaboration with the communications ministry and KOICA, was soft-launched on 28 July 2026](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>).
