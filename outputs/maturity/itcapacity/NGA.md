# Nigeria

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Anambra State's ICT agency moved its upgraded SolutionLens platform into use at the state Ministry of Information in October 2026](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/ict-agency-information-ministry-deepen-digital-collaboration-as-solutionlens-moves-into-operational-use/>). [In 2019 Galaxy Backbone, a federal state company, maintained the federal payroll system](<https://galaxybackbone.com.ng/mandate/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Galaxy Backbone runs one platform on which more than 30 federal bodies approve documents without paper](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/11/gbb-promises-to-increase-numbers-of-digitalised-mdas-from-current-30-says-adeyanju/>). Its managing director said so in October 2026. [Anambra's ICT agency is training the state information ministry's officers on SolutionLens](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/ict-agency-information-ministry-deepen-digital-collaboration-as-solutionlens-moves-into-operational-use/>).

**Built in-house.** [Galaxy Backbone developed the Government Service Portal with the communications ministry and KOICA](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>). It was soft-launched on 28 July 2026.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [The Government Service Portal](<https://von.gov.ng/nigeria-unveils-unified-government-service-platform/>) was built with an outside partner.
