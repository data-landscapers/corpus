# Chad

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** In 2023 the World Bank said [the identity agency ANATS developed a mobile app for checking the population register against the national ID number](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>). The same diagnostic said IDEMIA provides the SIGPTS system, and that maintenance skills must be passed on. On 20 April 2026 the state mining company SONEMIC [presented an operations management system its own engineers designed](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/20/tchad-la-sonemic-devoile-son-nouveau-systeme-numerique/>). It is in use there. [VisasNews reports the police e-Visa department runs evisa.td itself, with its own IT unit](<https://visasnews.com/en/chad-e-visa-platform-continues-to-gain-momentum/>).

**A technology body for government.** [Engineers of SONEMIC and of the national ICT agency ADETIC design and build the Td-connect, E-Santé, E-éducation and Td-scolar platforms](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/11/17/education-et-sante-la-sonemic-offre-du-materiel-informatique/>), Tchadinfos reported in November 2026. On 17 November [SONEMIC handed servers, network and security equipment to the education ministry and the digital health programme](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/11/17/education-et-sante-la-sonemic-offre-du-materiel-informatique/>). They are to carry those platforms.

**Built in-house.** [SONEMIC's teams also built mapping, document management, workflow and dashboard tools](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/20/tchad-la-sonemic-devoile-son-nouveau-systeme-numerique/>), unveiled in April 2026 with the main system.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 names three systems run wholly by government staff. [SONEMIC's system is the one found](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/20/tchad-la-sonemic-devoile-son-nouveau-systeme-numerique/>).
