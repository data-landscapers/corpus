# Eswatini

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Independent News reports the Digital Landscape Assessment Report found 15 ministries have no in-house ICT officers and depend entirely or substantially on RSTP for technical functions, with only 18 dedicated ICT professionals supporting the whole government digital estate assessed](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [this source](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [this source](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [this source](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Digital Eswatini Project](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-06bn-digital-eswatini-puts-people-before-technology/>).
