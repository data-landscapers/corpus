# Senegal

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Production of Senegal's biometric identity cards is done under contract by a Malaysian firm, and the Interior Minister said production would move progressively to national companies as the contract ends](<https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html>). [Sénégal Numérique SA said its own Cyberdefense, Systems and Networks teams were mobilised to protect its information systems after attempted cyberattacks, per its communique of 9 February 2026](<https://www.enqueteplus.com/content/cybersécurité-sénégal-numérique-signale-des-tentatives-de-cyberattaques-et-restreint>).

**A technology body for government.** [SENUM SA's own page presents TeleDAc as a platform offering the whole Administration dematerialised procedures, with dozens online across urbanism, tourism, justice, education, labour, civil service, interior and other sectors](<https://www.adie.sn/projets/teledac>). [DGPSN says SENUM-SA, the national company managing the state's digital infrastructure, hosts and secures the RNU and PNBSF information systems](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** [The e-Sénégal single-window platform and mobile app, developed by state company Sénégal Numérique SA, was launched on 24 March 2026 with judicial-record and nationality-certificate procedures](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** [The e-Senegal single-window platform and app were developed by the state company Senegal Numerique SA](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>). [Senegal Numerique SA, a state company, runs the government messaging service and its collaboration and video-conference tools](<https://www.presidence.sn/wo/assets/documents/Communication-SENUM-CAMP-2025.pdf>). [ANACIM's own technology and climate data teams have deployed and operated the AgData Hub end to end since September-October 2025](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>). [CINERI, a state body, manages the SIGESR higher-education system and its CAMPUSEN module](<https://aps.sn/le-supercalculateur-taouey-un-outil-au-service-de-la-recherche-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>).
