# Senegal

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Sénégal Numérique SA said its own Cyberdefense, Systems and Networks teams were mobilised to protect its information systems after attempted cyberattacks, per its communique of 9 February 2026](<https://www.enqueteplus.com/content/cybersécurité-sénégal-numérique-signale-des-tentatives-de-cyberattaques-et-restreint>). [Production of Senegal's biometric identity cards is done under contract by a Malaysian firm, and the Interior Minister said production would move progressively to national companies as the contract ends](<https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html>).

**A technology body for government.** [Sénégal Numérique SA, the state operator, says on its own page that the Diamniadio national Tier 3 datacenter hosts and stores the state's data, and that its Espaces Sénégal Services are connected to all administrations](<https://senegalnumeriquesa.sn/en/projets/smart-s%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal>). [DGPSN says SENUM-SA, the national company managing the state's digital infrastructure, hosts and secures the RNU and PNBSF information systems](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** [The e-Sénégal single-window platform and mobile app, developed by state company Sénégal Numérique SA, was launched on 24 March 2026 with judicial-record and nationality-certificate procedures](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>).
