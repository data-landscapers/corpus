# Zimbabwe

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Zimbabwe Republic Police ICT Directorate, headed by a Director ICT, is stated to be responsible for all ICT matters in the force](<https://zrp.gov.zw/?p=7187>). [It is stated to manage the force's ICT infrastructure and personnel through provincial ICT officers and systems administration](<https://zrp.gov.zw/?p=7187>). [A Professional Software Development Team is stated to sit under it](<https://zrp.gov.zw/?p=7187>).

**A technology body for government.** [The Government Internet Service Provider is a government body formed in 1998](<http://www.gisp.gov.zw/about/>). [It is stated to provide internet connectivity, email and web services to all government ministries, departments and parastatals](<http://www.gisp.gov.zw/about/>). [It is also stated to administer the .gov.zw domain](<http://www.gisp.gov.zw/about/>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff, not by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023. The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.gisp.gov.zw/about/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work. The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.gisp.gov.zw/about/>).

***Also on file***. [Citizen Engagement and Scoring Platform](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2026/06/19/president-mnangagwa-launches-citizen-engagement-and-scoring-platform-built-by-hits-lads-africa/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
