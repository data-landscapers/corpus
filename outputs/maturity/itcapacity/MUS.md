# Mauritius

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** The ministry's Central Information Systems Division (CISD) has an Application Development Unit that [fixes bugs and upgrades the applications it develops for ministries](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Pages/Application-Development.aspx>). CISD officers [work on site as database, system and network administrators for ministries' systems](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/customercharter24.pdf>). In 2024 CISD [counted 32 application systems under maintenance and four gone live](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** CISD is a division of the Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation. Its Application Development Unit [designs, develops, deploys and maintains applications for ministries and departments on the GOC Cloud](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Pages/Application-Development.aspx>).

**Built in-house.** CISD's new Government payroll system was [developed entirely in-house and took over payroll processing in July 2024](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>). All finance sections across government use it.

**Systems run wholly in-house.** Besides payroll, CISD's own unit [fully rolled out the Bad Weather Allowance System for the fisheries ministry](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>). It also [released the Flight Clearance system, whose three phases have gone live](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>). No contractor is named for any of the three. Government email, by contrast, [was awarded to Mauritius Telecom with Lingora](<https://cisd.govmu.org/Documents/Newsletter/Enewsletter%20issue%20No.6.pdf>).
