# Mozambique

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INTIC says Mozambican professionals implement and run its digital signature service](<https://intic.gov.mz/sistema-de-certificacao-digital-mocambique-registou-crescimento-na-adesao-de-assinaturas-electronicas-em-2024/>), which it offers free to government and which more than ten public institutions, among them the Supreme Court and the National Press, used by the end of 2024. [INAGE, the national e-government institute, states it developed the CloudGov file storage and sharing system for public administration (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.inage.gov.mz/?p=2697>).

**A technology body for government.** [INAGE's service catalogue, retrieved 2026-08-08, lists server rental, application hosting, application development and government network connection (GovNET, MoRENet) as services for public bodies](<https://www.inage.gov.mz/?p=2697>). [INAGE and CEDIMO staff make up the team that develops and implements e-SNGD, the state's document-management system, and INAGE trained 47 provincial officials on it in June 2025](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/inage-ip-gov-518591357_inage-instituto-nacional-de-governo-electr%C3%B3nico-activity-7337779452975726593-DWfE>).

**Built in-house.** [INAGE states it recently developed CloudGov and that it is in the implementation phase across institutions, with no go-live date given (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.inage.gov.mz/?p=2697>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.inage.gov.mz/?p=2697>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Huawei-built data centre (Mozambique)](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a system built by government staff went live since October 2023.
