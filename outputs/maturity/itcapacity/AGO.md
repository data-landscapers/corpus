# Angola

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [INE's head said its own technical staff built the Census 2024 Portal and the INE Angola app](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). [The tax authority AGT is building its own AI risk-analysis system](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>). It has been tested since 2024 and is not stated to be live. [The Finance Ministry's technology service SETIC-FP built its document management system, SIGD, in-house](<https://www.menosfios.com/setic-fp-lanca-i-lab-para-acelerar-transformacao-digital-das-financas-publicas/>). It had about 200 users in March 2026.

**A technology body for government.** [INFOSI manages the government's Data Center and Cloud, opened in April 2026](<https://pti.ao/mario-oliveira-inauguracao-do-data-center-e-cloud-do-governo-constitui-um-passo-gigante-rumo-a-soberania-digital/>). It offers more than 80 technology services. The state network it completes links every ministerial department.

**Built in-house.** [INE launched the census portal and app on 2 July 2026 in Uíge](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). Its head said INE's own staff built them.

**Systems built or run in-house.** Government staff built three named systems, none naming a contractor. [INE's own staff built the Census 2024 Portal and the INE Angola statistics app](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). [SETIC-FP built SIGD for the Finance Ministry](<https://www.menosfios.com/setic-fp-lanca-i-lab-para-acelerar-transformacao-digital-das-financas-publicas/>). AGT's risk-analysis system is not yet live.
