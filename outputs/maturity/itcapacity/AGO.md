# Angola

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [Angop reports INE's head said the Census 2024 Portal and the 'INE Angola' mobile app were developed by INE's own technical staff](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). [AGT's board chairman said on 11 February 2026 that the tax authority is developing its own AI risk-analysis system, tested since 2024 and fed with internal data; it is not stated to be live](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>).

**Built in-house.** [INE launched the Census 2024 Portal and the 'INE Angola' app on 2 July 2026 in Uíge; its head said they were developed by INE's own technical staff](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [SIGFE](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>).

***Not held***. No source held says the government has adopted a cadre or scheme of service for its IT staff.
