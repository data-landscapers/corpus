# Algeria

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The Haut Commissariat a la Numerisation states its own services designed and developed the mechanism for verifying electronic documents issued through the Dzair Digital Services portal, which it trained sector information-system managers to deploy and operate](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>). [Interlignes reports the health minister said the electronic medical record was entirely designed and developed by engineers and staff of the ministry's information systems directorate](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>).

**A technology body for government.** No source held from 2023 or later says a government agency or state company builds, hosts or runs systems for ministries and the rest of government; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>).

**A career structure for IT staff.** No source held says the government has adopted an ICT cadre, a scheme of service or an establishment for its IT staff; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Dzair Digital Services](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>); [National Identification Number (NIN)](<https://mail.webservices.dz/images/faq/hcn/SNTN-En.pdf>); [Chifa card / E-Chifa](<https://mail.webservices.dz/images/faq/hcn/SNTN-En.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held from 2023 or later says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government.
