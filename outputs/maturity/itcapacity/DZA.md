# Algeria

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [The digitisation commission HCN says its own services built the checker for documents issued on Dzair Digital Services](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>). It trained the ministries' systems managers to run it.

**A technology body for government.** [Dzair Digital Services, launched on 5 July 2026, groups the online services of several ministries](<https://www.algerie360.com/dzair-digital-services-documents-en-ligne/>). The HCN develops it.

**Built in-house.** [The health minister launched the national rollout of the electronic medical record on 25 July 2026](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>). He said the ministry's own engineers designed and built it. More than 2,500 health facilities in all 69 wilayas are to use it. On 11 January 2026 the state property directorate [extended Amlak to all its cadastre and land offices](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-vers-le-livret-foncier-electronique-avec-amlak/>). Its own staff built Amlak in a year. In March 2025 the Interior Ministry [launched a police-services platform built by the national police's own engineers](<https://www.lactualgerie.com/divers/lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-pour-les-services-de-police/>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** Three are named. [The HCN built the document checker](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/info-en-continu/portail-national-des-services-numeriques-le-hcn-va-assurer-des-formations-33381>). [The health ministry built the electronic medical record](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>). [The Interior Ministry's own engineers run the identity, travel-document and civil-status platforms](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).
