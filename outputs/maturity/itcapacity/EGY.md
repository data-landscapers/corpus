# Egypt

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [NOSI states its own specialists are working round the clock, with the implementing company, the project consultant and the data centre, to fix slow performance in its digital transformation system, live since 24 February 2026](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>). [The World Bank says the Egyptian government assigned the Administrative Control Authority to develop and manage the Unified National Registry, which connects 52 databases covering over 100 million people (no event date given (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [The World Bank says e-Finance, a state-owned enterprise set up in 2005 to build, operate and manage the government's financial payments network, processes payroll for 4.7 million government employees and runs card issuance for Takaful and Karama, pensions and subsidies (no event date given (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** No source held says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source held since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Takaful and Karama beneficiary database](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held from 2023 or later says a government body builds or runs systems for the rest of government.
