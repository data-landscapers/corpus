# Egypt

## In-house capacity

**Government staff.** [In April 2026 NOSI said its own specialists were working round the clock on its digital transformation system](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>). [They worked with the implementing company, the consultant and the data centre to fix slow performance](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>). [The system went live on 24 February 2026](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>). [In June 2021 the World Bank said the Administrative Control Authority develops and manages the Unified National Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>). [It connects 52 databases covering over 100 million people](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>).

**A technology body for government.** [In June 2021 the World Bank said e-Finance runs the government's financial payments network](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>). [It is a state-owned enterprise set up in 2005](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>). [It processed payroll for 4.7 million government employees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>). [It ran card issuance for Takaful and Karama, pensions and subsidies](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/533491624466169827/pdf/G2P-Payment-Arrangements-in-Egypt-and-COVID-19.pdf>).

**Built in-house.** No source found says a system built by government staff, rather than by a contractor or vendor, went live since October 2023; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>).

**Systems run wholly in-house.** No source found since October 2023 says government staff run, maintain or built a named system with no contractor doing the work; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2026-04-22.aspx>).
