# Somalia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** As of September 2023 the property tax work was [run by the UN Joint Programme on Local Governance with state ministries of interior](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-05/annex_vii_jplg_2023.pdf>). In Puntland the system was [implemented through the Ministry of Interior with the telecom firm Golis, on programme support](<https://unhabitat.org/news/16-aug-2021/mobile-taxation-expanded-to-four-districts-of-somalia-after-a-successful-pilot>), in August 2021.

**What is recorded.** In Garowe, Qardho and Galkacyo [properties were mapped and registered in the local government financial system, which generates the tax invoices and receipts payments made through mobile money](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>), on fieldwork of October 2022. The system, which the local governments call FMIS, [replaced printed receipts and books](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>), from 2019 in Garowe and from 2021 in three others.

**Where the record is held.** In June 2021 the programme described the Puntland districts' system as [moving to a coherent financial system from district to state level](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/40000/semi-annual_report_2021.pdf>), deployed through the state Ministries of Interior and Finance.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is in the period to September 2023, when [three of five targeted Puntland districts had completed the demonstration phase of a mobile-payment tax collection system](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-05/annex_vii_jplg_2023.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** [Four Puntland local governments, Galkacyo north, Garowe, Qardho and Bosaso, used the system to collect taxes](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>) in October 2022. In Galmudug and Benadir [no local government or district had a financial management system; Benadir's districts collect no taxes, and the regional administration's agents collect property taxes, permits and licences](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>), in November 2022.

***Noted, not assessed***. Hirshabelle State's [SUMER](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/hirshabelles-digital-fix-for-somalias-oldest-tax-problem>), a point-of-sale system at its border checkpoints; South West State's [Financial Management Information System of the State](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/40000/semi-annual_report_2021.pdf>); the federal [Somalia Financial Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>); Somaliland's [SLFMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>); and the [Integrated Tax Administration System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>) of the federal and Puntland tax administrations.

***Not held***. No total of local governments is given, so the four counted are a floor and not a share. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months, and whether district records reach the state system since 2021 is not established.
