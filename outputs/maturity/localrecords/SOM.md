# Somalia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** As of September 2023 the property tax work was [run by the UN Joint Programme on Local Governance with state ministries of interior](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-05/annex_vii_jplg_2023.pdf>). In August 2021 the Puntland system was [implemented through the Ministry of Interior with the telecom firm Golis](<https://unhabitat.org/news/16-aug-2021/mobile-taxation-expanded-to-four-districts-of-somalia-after-a-successful-pilot>). This was done with programme support.

**What is recorded.** In Garowe, Qardho and Galkacyo [properties were mapped and registered in the local government financial system](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>), on fieldwork of October 2022. The system generates the tax invoices and receipts payments made through mobile money. The local governments call it FMIS. It [replaced printed receipts and books](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>), from 2019 in Garowe and from 2021 in three others.

**Where the record is held.** In June 2021 the programme described the Puntland districts' system as [moving to a coherent financial system from district to state level](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/40000/semi-annual_report_2021.pdf>). It was deployed through the state Ministries of Interior and Finance.

**Last twelve months.** In the period to September 2023, [three of five targeted Puntland districts had completed the demonstration phase of a mobile-payment tax collection system](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-05/annex_vii_jplg_2023.pdf>). No later event has been found.

**Local governments recording.** [Four Puntland local governments, Galkacyo north, Garowe, Qardho and Bosaso, used the system to collect taxes](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>) in October 2022. In November 2022 [no local government or district in Galmudug and Benadir had a financial management system](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>). [Benadir's districts collect no taxes, and the regional administration's agents collect property taxes, permits and licences](<https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Hirshabelle State's [SUMER](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/hirshabelles-digital-fix-for-somalias-oldest-tax-problem>) is a point-of-sale system at its border checkpoints. South West State has a [Financial Management Information System of the State](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/40000/semi-annual_report_2021.pdf>). The federal government has the [Somalia Financial Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>), and Somaliland has [SLFMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>). The federal and Puntland tax administrations have the [Integrated Tax Administration System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/422841595537391953/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Second-Public-Financial-Management-Capacity-Strengthening-Project-P151492-Sequence-No-08.pdf>).

***Not found***. No total of local governments is given, so the four counted are a floor and not a share. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months, and whether district records reach the state system since 2021 is not established.
