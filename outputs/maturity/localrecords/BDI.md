# Burundi

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Interior ministry's 2025/26 budget carries [2,000,000,000 BIF from domestic revenue for digitalising communal revenue collection](<https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CANEVAS-RAPPORT_MIDCSP_2025-2026_T3.xlsx>). The ministry [developed E-taxe, with CRDB Bank as collection interface](<https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/lancement-officiel-de-la-digitalisation-dans-la-collecte-des-recettes-communales-e-taxe/>). The digitalisation master plan of May 2023 [includes communal tax collection](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf>).

**What is recorded.** In Kayanza in June 2025, [48 of 54 collectors used point-of-sale machines](<https://burundi-eco.com/recettes-communales-la-commune-kayanza-plus-productive-au-cours-de-lexercice-2024-%e2%80%90-2025/>), paying into the commune's account. In September 2026 VNG International said [Nyanza had put its taxpayer information into its accounting software](<https://www.vng-international.nl/project/sdlg-burundi/>). It is the only commune shown with a register of payers.

**Where the record is held.** In February 2026 the Interior ministry said [E-TAXE had centralised the data of its twelve pilot communes](<https://acoburundi.bi/communes-bubanza-bukinanyana-kayanza-ngozi-kirundo-muyinga-cankuzo-ruyigi-rutana-gitega-makamba-et-nyanza-pilotes-pour-la-digitalisation-fiscale-e-taxe-un-tournant-majeur-pour-la-gestion-d/>). Nyanza's register [sits in the commune's own software](<https://www.vng-international.nl/project/sdlg-burundi/>).

**Last twelve months.** Butihinda commune [trained its staff on IBB M+ on 5 May 2026](<https://communebutihinda.gov.bi/butihinda-en-marche-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-communales-grace-a-la-plateforme-ibb-m/>), for use from 2026-2027. In February 2026 E-TAXE was [still called a pilot, with national extension to prepare](<https://acoburundi.bi/communes-bubanza-bukinanyana-kayanza-ngozi-kirundo-muyinga-cankuzo-ruyigi-rutana-gitega-makamba-et-nyanza-pilotes-pour-la-digitalisation-fiscale-e-taxe-un-tournant-majeur-pour-la-gestion-d/>).

**Local governments recording.** The Interior ministry's third-quarter report for 2025/26 [lists 30 communes digitalised in communal revenue](<https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CANEVAS-RAPPORT_MIDCSP_2025-2026_T3.xlsx>). It gives no total. E-taxe [began in 12 pilot communes on 23 August 2024](<https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/lancement-officiel-de-la-digitalisation-dans-la-collecte-des-recettes-communales-e-taxe/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. A register of payers beyond Nyanza, and the total of communes against which the 30 can be set.
