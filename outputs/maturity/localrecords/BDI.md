# Burundi

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Interior ministry's revised budget for 2025/26 carries [a line of 2,000,000,000 BIF from the domestic state budget for extending the digitalisation of revenue collection in the communes](<https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CANEVAS-RAPPORT_MIDCSP_2025-2026_T3.xlsx>). The digitalisation master plan adopted on 10 May 2023 [includes an initiative to digitalise communal tax collection](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-DIRECTEUR-DE-DIGITALISATION-2023-2033.pdf>). One platform in use, IBB M+, is [run by Interbank Burundi, a bank](<https://communebutihinda.gov.bi/butihinda-en-marche-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-communales-grace-a-la-plateforme-ibb-m/>).

**What is recorded.** In Kayanza in June 2025, [48 of the commune's 54 collectors used point-of-sale machines, with the revenue transferred digitally to the commune's account](<https://burundi-eco.com/recettes-communales-la-commune-kayanza-plus-productive-au-cours-de-lexercice-2024-%e2%80%90-2025/>). In May 2026 IBB M+ [took payments from traders by phone or through agents, notified a commune officer of each transaction and kept the data to follow revenue](<https://communebutihinda.gov.bi/butihinda-en-marche-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-communales-grace-a-la-plateforme-ibb-m/>).

**Where the record is held.** In 2021 the Direction of Communal Finances was [considering an IT solution to replace the manual collection of communal taxes](<https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=261606>).

**Last twelve months.** Butihinda commune [trained its planning and accounting staff on IBB M+ on 5 May 2026, for use from the 2026–2027 budget year](<https://communebutihinda.gov.bi/butihinda-en-marche-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-communales-grace-a-la-plateforme-ibb-m/>).

**Local governments recording.** The Interior ministry's third-quarter execution report for 2025/26 [lists 30 communes digitalised in communal revenue](<https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CANEVAS-RAPPORT_MIDCSP_2025-2026_T3.xlsx>). A UNDP mission of June 2025 [reported the E-Taxe system operational in communes such as Kayanza](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/le-pnud-et-le-gouvernement-evaluent-conjointement-les-interventions-sur-terrain>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No register of payers is described in any system, and where the electronic record is held is not stated. The 30 communes carry no total, so no share of communes is given.
