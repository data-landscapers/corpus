# Burkina Faso

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. [The DGI's software for registering micro-enterprises and taking their payments, whose tax is passed back to the collectivités territoriales](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>); [the chaîne fiscale of census, enrolment, billing and collection, run by the State's deconcentrated tax and treasury services](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any collectivité territoriale keeps its own digital revenue record. No source on local government revenue records is held.
