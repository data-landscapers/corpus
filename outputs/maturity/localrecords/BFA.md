# Burkina Faso

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The commune of Ouagadougou [runs SYSCOTAX for local taxes through its tax base office](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2024/03/23/paiement-digital-des-taxes-ouagadougou-lance-la-syscotax-dans-4-marches/>), Digital Magazine Burkina reported in March 2024. No plan or budget line for it is given.

**What is recorded.** In November 2025 the commune's tax-base director said SYCOTAX [records market traders by phone number and tracks who has paid what](<https://libreinfo.net/ouagadougou-la-commune-sensibilise-les-commercants-au-paiement-electronique-des-taxes/>). Each payment brings a paper receipt and an electronic message. Card terminals take the payments.

**Where the record is held.** No source says where the SYCOTAX record is kept. The nearest fact is that [the commune collects market taxes through it with payment terminals](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2024/03/23/paiement-digital-des-taxes-ouagadougou-lance-la-syscotax-dans-4-marches/>), reported in March 2024.

**Last twelve months.** On 19 November 2025 Ouagadougou [briefed traders in the Gounghin and Koukin Yaar markets on electronic payment](<https://libreinfo.net/ouagadougou-la-commune-sensibilise-les-commercants-au-paiement-electronique-des-taxes/>). Extension to 23 more markets is planned.

**Local governments recording.** Only the capital is named. On 19 March 2024 Ouagadougou [launched SYSCOTAX in four pilot markets](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2024/03/23/paiement-digital-des-taxes-ouagadougou-lance-la-syscotax-dans-4-marches/>), saying collection rose above 1.2 billion FCFA. No other commune is reported.

***Not found***. Any commune outside Ouagadougou recording its revenue digitally, and where the capital's record is kept.
