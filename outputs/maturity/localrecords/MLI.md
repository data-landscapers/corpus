# Mali

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** ReCoDe, the communes' software, was [chosen by the national local finance committee and rolled out by the DGCT and the treasury](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>), a Pro-ARIDES note of July 2024 says. The Koutiala project launched on 6 March 2026 is [financed by the Embassy of the Netherlands and carried with the NGO ID Sahel Afrique](<https://www.idsahel.com/2026/08/13/koutiala-un-projet-de-recensement-fiscal-et-de-digitalisation-pour-renforcer-la-gestion-des-ressources-communales/>).

**What is recorded.** ReCoDe's local tax module [follows a census of taxable matter, with an electronic ticket coded for each taxpayer and a payment terminal that feeds the town hall's server](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>); Bamako's Commune VI collected 250 million CFA francs with it in 2023. In a test at Koulikoro reported in June 2023, [about 100 vendors were registered and each ticket was entered at once in the town hall's database](<https://www.undp.org/fr/mali/actualites/innovation-et-finance-locale-digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-ressources-communales>).

**Where the record is held.** In July 2024 the data of the 13 Pro-ARIDES communes using ReCoDe [were centralised on a server at the DGCT](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>). Market fees were [collected by hand, the agent writing each ticket](<https://www.undp.org/fr/mali/actualites/innovation-et-finance-locale-digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-ressources-communales>), UNDP Mali said in June 2023.

**Last twelve months.** A project launched on 6 March 2026 is [to give pilot communes a digital solution and equipment for financial management and the collection of communal taxes, starting with Koutiala](<https://www.idsahel.com/2026/08/13/koutiala-un-projet-de-recensement-fiscal-et-de-digitalisation-pour-renforcer-la-gestion-des-ressources-communales/>).

**Local governments recording.** ReCoDe was [deployed in 680 communes for testing](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>), the July 2024 note says. After training in 27 of the programme's communes, [only Kava was willing to carry tax digitalisation through](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Koutiala has a [fiscal census, the recensement de la matière imposable](<https://www.idsahel.com/2026/08/13/koutiala-un-projet-de-recensement-fiscal-et-de-digitalisation-pour-renforcer-la-gestion-des-ressources-communales/>), and a [ten-day census of market equipment launched on 12 March 2026](<https://www.studiotamani.org/200598-koutiala-lancement-du-recensement-des-equipements-marchands-pour-ameliorer-la-fiscalite-locale>). The tax directorate runs [Sigtas](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>) and [E-Impot](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>). Payment channels are the [digital payment of taxes and dues made obligatory by the Ministry of Economy and Finance](<https://afrikinfos-mali.com/2024/07/23/mali-le-gouvernement-devoile-la-liste-des-premieres-obligations-de-paiement-numerique-dans-les-services-publics/>), the [digital payment arrangement at civil registry centres](<https://fassoactu.com/2026/07/31/transparence-financiere-les-collectivites-sommees-daccelerer-la-digitalisation-des-paiements/>) and [Kava's Orange merchant account for communal taxes](<https://proarides.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Pro-ARIDES_2024_NT_Mali_Digitalisation-taxes.pdf>). The DGCT has [ReCoDe as the communes' budget software](<https://dgct.gouv.ml/dgct-reunion-de-coordination-et-de-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-activites-du-plan-daction-2025/>) and [OISE, its monitoring tool for decentralisation](<https://dgct.gouv.ml/dgct-reunion-de-coordination-et-de-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-activites-du-plan-daction-2025/>).

***Not held***. Whether any commune outside Bamako uses the local tax module as routine, and how many of the 680 communes use it at all. No plan for the system is stated.
