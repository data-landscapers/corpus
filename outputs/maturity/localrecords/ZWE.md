# Zimbabwe

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In May 2023 the Deputy Minister of Local Government said [LADS would become a business unit of her ministry](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2023/05/09/hit-hosts-lads-digital-transformation-and-sensitisation-workshop/>). The Harare Institute of Technology was to be its consultant. Further development was to be [paid for from a government devolution grant](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2023/05/09/hit-hosts-lads-digital-transformation-and-sensitisation-workshop/>).

**What is recorded.** Kwekwe City Council [bills on the Promun package, with sections for utility billing, accounts receivable, cashiering and credit control](<https://kwekwecity.org.zw/departments/finance/>) (August 2026). Mutare's finance department [runs the LADS debt management module](<https://www.clgf.org.uk/whats-new/news/digital-transformation-of-zimbabwe-local-government/>) (October 2021). At Gwanda, the Auditor-General reported for 2024, [27 stands sold in 2019 were still not billed](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20Local%20Authorities.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The Harare Institute of Technology says LADS [links councils' financials and billing to the national financial management system](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/>) (November 2024). For 2024 the Auditor-General found [most local authorities without an ERP system for revenue collection](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20Local%20Authorities.pdf>). Their processes were manual or partly automated.

**Last twelve months.** In June 2026 Mutare's Town Clerk said [LADS digital billing had lifted collection efficiency from 33 per cent in 2019 to 65 per cent](<https://www.clgf.org.uk/whats-new/news/cscn-peer-learning-clinic-highlights-mutares-digital-transformation-journey-to-strengthen-municipal-finance/>). That month LADS mobile collection was [reported in 53 councils](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>).

**Local governments recording.** [Around 83 of Zimbabwe's 92 local authorities use at least one LADS module](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/>), the institute told the Harare Commission of Inquiry in November 2024. In June 2026 Gemnation reported [LADS mobile revenue collection deployed to 53 councils](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>).
