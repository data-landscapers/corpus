# Zimbabwe

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In May 2023 the Deputy Minister of Local Government and Public Works said [LADS would become a business unit of her ministry, with the Harare Institute of Technology as consultant](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2023/05/09/hit-hosts-lads-digital-transformation-and-sensitisation-workshop/>), and that its further development would be [paid for from a government devolution grant](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2023/05/09/hit-hosts-lads-digital-transformation-and-sensitisation-workshop/>).

**What is recorded.** Kwekwe City Council [bills on the Promun package, with sections for utility billing, accounts receivable, cashiering and credit control](<https://kwekwecity.org.zw/departments/finance/>) (August 2026). Mutare's finance department [runs the LADS debt management module](<https://www.clgf.org.uk/whats-new/news/digital-transformation-of-zimbabwe-local-government/>) (October 2021). At Gwanda, the Auditor-General reported for 2024, [27 stands sold in 2019 were still not billed](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20Local%20Authorities.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The Harare Institute of Technology says LADS [links local authorities' financials and billing to the national public financial management system](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/>) (November 2024). The Auditor-General's report on 2024 says [most local authorities had no ERP system for revenue collection and ran processes that were manual or partly automated](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Report%20%20of%20the%20Auditor-General%20for%20the%20Year%20Ended%2031%20Dec%202024%20on%20Local%20Authorities.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In June 2025 the institute said [its InstaPark parking system was in use in 15 municipalities](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hits-erp-systems-boost-for-councils/>).

**Local governments recording.** [Around 83 of Zimbabwe's 92 local authorities use at least one LADS module](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/>), the institute told the Harare Commission of Inquiry in November 2024.
