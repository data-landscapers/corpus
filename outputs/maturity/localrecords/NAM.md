# Namibia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The City of Windhoek's billing system is its own, with [the vendor BCX supplying the financial management software](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>), The Namibian reported for February 2024.

**What is recorded.** The City of Windhoek [bills water accounts for named customers in the system it calls Solar](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>), with payments and arrears recorded against each account. A city memo of February 2024 found [accounts imported from the financial system with wrong customer information](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>), and a payment history missing from one account.

**Where the record is held.** In February 2024 the billing system [ran on the city's own servers](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 2 July 2024 the City of Windhoek [confirmed irregularities in its water billing system](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>), with forensic investigators appointed and nine accounts found with wrong figures. The system keeps its logs for 30 days.

**Local governments recording.** The Namibian's report of July 2024 covers [the City of Windhoek's billing system](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-in-water-billing-system-cover-up/>), the capital's, and names no other local authority.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Any local authority outside Windhoek, a total of those recording, and any of the city's accounts other than water. Nothing on who plans or pays for the systems, and no event in the last twelve months.
