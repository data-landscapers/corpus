# Kenya

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** On 7 February 2019 the President [directed a task force to deliver one revenue management system for all 47 counties](<https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560>). None had been delivered by September 2020. A 2018 policy brief says [most counties that had automated did not own their system](<https://cegkenya.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Policy-Brief-Brochure2.pdf>). Standards for county revenue automation, [gazetted on 27 July 2026, say counties are to own their systems and revenue data](<https://cra.go.ke/download/standards-and-guidelines-on-the-automation-of-county-own-source-revenue/?wpdmdl=5257>). An October 2026 commentary, on the writer's own figures, puts [about 70 per cent of counties on contracts paying a vendor up to 15 per cent of collections](<https://nation.africa/kenya/blogs-opinion/blogs/counties-must-rethink-costly-revenue-automation-5626512>).

**What is recorded.** A 2020 paper describes automation as [the move from paper receipts to an electronic system, with point-of-sale machines](<https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560>). In 2018, [97 per cent of the 34 counties with a system used only its collection module](<https://cegkenya.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Policy-Brief-Brochure2.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** A 2020 survey of 24 counties found that [most could not host their revenue data, which the vendor hosted](<https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560>). In 2018, [14 of the 34 counties used one vendor's cloud service](<https://cegkenya.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Policy-Brief-Brochure2.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Vision 2030 Delivery Secretariat's review of all 47 counties, dated 23 February 2026, names [five with well-established systems and cashless collection](<https://vision2030.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/ASSESSMENT-REPORT-AUTOMATION-EMPLOYMENT-IN-COUNTIES-23-02-2026.pdf>). They are Murang'a, Nairobi City, Machakos, Laikipia and Nyeri.

**Local governments recording.** [Thirty-three of the 47 counties, 70 per cent, had automated their revenues, some in part](<https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560>). The figure is from a September 2020 paper in the European Scientific Journal. In February 2026 [six of the 47 relied heavily on manual and cash-based systems](<https://vision2030.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/ASSESSMENT-REPORT-AUTOMATION-EMPLOYMENT-IN-COUNTIES-23-02-2026.pdf>), by the Secretariat's review.

***Also on file***. Counties are moving their payrolls to the national government's [Human Resource Information System](<https://cob.go.ke/download/county-governments-budget-implementation-review-report-for-the-financial-year-2025-26/?wpdmdl=16482>). The National Treasury runs [IFMIS](<https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560>). Kiambu County's [CountyPro](<https://cegkenya.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Policy-Brief-Brochure2.pdf>) is its accounting and reporting system.

***Not found***. No source says counties hold a register of payers, and no count of counties recording is dated after 2020. No source says any county has complied with the July 2026 standards.
