# Senegal

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The municipal software named in Senegal's communes is [a product of the vendor Linking Development, supplied to communes](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>), on the vendor's own account in November 2025.

**What is recorded.** The vendor says the software, which it calls Terlink, [includes accounting and taxation modules covering the recovery and management of communal taxes](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>). [Its other modules](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>) are for civil status and for planning and land.

**Where the record is held.** Linking Development describes Terlink as [all-in-one municipal management software, supplied together with a website for the commune](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>).

**Last twelve months.** In November 2025 the vendor [published its account of the software and of the communes it had equipped with it](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>).

**Local governments recording.** Linking Development, the vendor, states that [more than 20 communes in Senegal are equipped with Terlink](<https://www.linking-development.fr/realisations/projet-terlink-la-digitalisation-des-communes-senegalaises/>), as at November 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. The Treasury's [Koom Com and GFILOC](<https://www.sentresor.org/fidak-2025-dgcpt/>), its [Observatoire des finances locales](<https://aps.sn/la-direction-generale-du-tresor-met-en-place-une-plateforme-de-gestion-des-donnees-financieres-des-collectivites-territoriales/>) and its [Système d'information bancaire and Télépaiement platform](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2021-03/Guide%20pour%20la%20digitalisation%20des%20paiements%20des%20Etats%20membres%20de%20l%27UEMOA.pdf>); the tax directorate's [cadastre and NICAD parcel identifier](<https://www.geosenegal.gouv.sn/IMG/pdf/geoflash_sept25.pdf>) and its [property tax application for Dakar](<https://fundinnovation.dev/en/projects/assessing-the-impact-of-a-new-digital-tool-for-property-tax-management-in-dakar>); [Adressage Numérique National](<https://senego.com/adressage-numerique-le-senegal-se-donne-pour-objectif-de-boucler-la-phase-pilote-en-un-an_1990855.html>); [TeleDAc](<https://www.adie.sn/projets/teledac>); [SenGec](<https://senegalnumeriquesa.sn/actualites/l%E2%80%99ams-et-s%C3%A9n%C3%A9gal-num%C3%A9rique-veulent-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-l%E2%80%99informatisation-des-mairies-0>).
