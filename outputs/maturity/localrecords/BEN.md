# Benin

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The PAACOL project, which equips communes with cash-desk software, is [coordinated by the AIMF and implemented with the ANCB, the communes' association](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>). In September 2019 its [743 million CFA francs came from the European Union, the AIMF and the ANCB](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>).

**What is recorded.** The project's single cash window for non-tax revenue has [software that records each payment](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>), as described in September 2019.

**Where the record is held.** The software is [networked within the commune, so that information is shared among the communal actors concerned](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The project, begun in 2017 for three years, [was to equip its selected communes during 2019/2020](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>).

**Local governments recording.** In September 2019 the project had [selected 12 of Benin's 77 communes to be equipped with the cash windows](<https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The tax directorate levies the [Taxe Foncière Unique for the communes, 90 per cent going to them, and takes payment at its 77 tax centres](<https://logri.org/fr/notre-travail/ou-nous-travaillons/benin/>); it runs [an electronic tax system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092325063510323/pdf/P176763-2035557c-fe0f-4162-a072-04b417dfb137.pdf>), [an e-services platform](<https://api.impots.bj/media/68fa7fde68130_LE-MESSAGER-DES-IMPOTS-N%C2%B047.pdf>) and [SIGIBé, its integrated tax management system](<https://api.impots.bj/media/65e65d552449f_Rapport%20Final%20Evaluation%20TADAT-DGI%20BENIN%202023.PDF>). The [tax chain of census, enrolment, billing and collection is run by the state's tax and treasury services](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>). Abomey commune has built [an urban land register, the Registre foncier urbain, with a private firm](<https://lanation.bj/index.php/actualites/urbanisation-abomey-prepare-sa-revolution-fonciere-numerique>).

***Not held***. Whether the 12 selected communes were equipped and their cash desks went into use. Nothing dated after September 2019.
