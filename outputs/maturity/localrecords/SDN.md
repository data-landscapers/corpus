# Sudan

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In Sennar State, Isali electronic collection is [run by the state finance ministry's unified collection department](<https://almndranews.com/88116/>), as reported in May 2026.

**What is recorded.** The Sennar state finance ministry [launched Isali electronic collection on 12 May 2026 at the transport and petroleum department and Sinja locality, with collectors, auditors and accountants equipped and trained](<https://almndranews.com/88116/>).

**Where the record is held.** The state's governor described Isali as [a national programme covering all Sudanese states](<https://almndranews.com/88116/>).

**Last twelve months.** The launch of 12 May 2026 [followed preparation from January 2026, in stages of networking, account opening and training](<https://almndranews.com/88116/>).

**Local governments recording.** Sinja locality was [one of two model sites in Sennar State at the launch](<https://almndranews.com/88116/>). The state's finance director general said all state institutions would be covered at the start of the second half of 2026.

[Bankak, a national banking app used for payments](<https://www.medameek.com/?p=203706>); [the Khartoum State government portal, with downloadable forms for business licences and property tax and limited online payment of specific fees and fines](<https://learning-gate.com/index.php/2576-8484/article/download/3004/1130/4518>); Isali as a state system, [launched in Gedaref on 26 April 2025 as the fourth state to apply it](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1642534>), [rolled out to White Nile State's ministries by December 2025, with localities scheduled next](<https://arabic.sudanspost.com/article/9575>), and [awaiting launch in Khartoum State in April 2026, with localities named among the bodies to apply it](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39689>).
