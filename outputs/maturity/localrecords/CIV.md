# Côte d'Ivoire

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Directorate General of Decentralisation and Local Development (DGDDL) [developed the SYGIDAN Recettes software in its own services and directs the PAMREC project](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-fiscales-des-communes/>). In April 2024 the project was [financed by the African Development Bank and the Ivorian State, together 3,003,901,293 FCFA](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-fiscales-des-communes/>).

**What is recorded.** In April 2024 the DGDDL's director described SYGIDAN Recettes, then at pre-validation, as [digitalising all of a commune's revenues, with traceability each time a tax is collected](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-fiscales-des-communes/>). The project's design of July 2021 [registers taxpayers and records payments, balances, arrears and penalties](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/cote_divoire_-_pilot_support_for_the_mobilisation_of_municipal_revenue_pamrec_-_project_appraisal_report.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The tool is [to centralise the data from all the actors collecting local revenue](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-fiscales-des-communes/>). The 2021 design was [to connect it to the systems of the tax directorate, the Treasury, the DGDDL or the Court of Auditors](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/cote_divoire_-_pilot_support_for_the_mobilisation_of_municipal_revenue_pamrec_-_project_appraisal_report.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 9 December 2025 the DGDDL [handed the commune of Assinie-Mafia its taxpayer database, within the PAMREC pilot](<https://mairieassinie.com/web/ceremonie-de-restitution-des-bases-de-donnees-des-contribuables/>).

**Local governments recording.** The African Development Bank's appraisal of July 2021 places the pilot in [22 of 201 municipalities](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/cote_divoire_-_pilot_support_for_the_mobilisation_of_municipal_revenue_pamrec_-_project_appraisal_report.pdf>). In April 2024 the tool was [to be deployed to the 22 communes and then distributed to all from January 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-fiscales-des-communes/>).
