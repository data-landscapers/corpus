# Uganda

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Local Government [runs E-LogRev](<https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/>) (March 2024), under [a contract with Tucksee Pty Limited that does not clearly require the source code to pass to government](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>), the Auditor General reported in December 2024. The system is [funded by both the finance ministry and URA TREP](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>), and the local government ministry [has no IT strategic plan](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>).

**What is recorded.** In IRAS, the mayor of Nansana said in March 2024, [the system fixes the assessment and records payments against a catalogue of fees](<https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/>).

**Where the record is held.** The Auditor General found [no formal access policy for the E-LogRev database](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>) (December 2024).

**Last twelve months.** At the half-year of 2024/25 local governments' own revenue [reached 37 per cent of the annual budget, against 24.35 per cent at the same point of 2019/20](<https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Domestic%20Revenue%20Mobilisation%20Strategy%20Semi%20Annual%20Report%20FY%202024-25.pdf>), which the finance ministry attributes to the IRAS rollout.

**Local governments recording.** The finance ministry's monitoring [covered IRAS in nine cities in the first half of 2024/25, with the tenth, Jinja, still on E-LogRev](<https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Domestic%20Revenue%20Mobilisation%20Strategy%20Semi%20Annual%20Report%20FY%202024-25.pdf>). In March 2024 IRAS was reported [rolled out in 98 districts, 21 municipalities and nine cities](<https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/>) and E-LogRev [operational in 48 local governments](<https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/>). The Auditor General's report on 2022/23 counted [124 activated local governments, 39 of them without the equipment to run the system](<https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The Uganda Revenue Authority's [Instant TIN](<https://doi.org/10.1017/dap.2025.10043>), [EFRIS](<https://eagle.co.ug/2026/09/14/ura-unveils-data-driven-tax-compliance-strategy-for-fy2026-27/>) and [TIN system](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/369514/ura-explains-use-of-nins-to-replace-tins>).

***Not held***. Where the record is held is not stated, and no total of local governments is given against which the counts can be set. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months.
