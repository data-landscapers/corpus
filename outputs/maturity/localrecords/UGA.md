# Uganda

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** IRAS was [developed by the Ministry of Local Government through the Local Government Finance Commission](<https://nbrb.go.ug/bims-and-iras-integrated-to-boost-compliance-and-revenue-generation-in-local-governments/>). Its older E-LogRev system is [funded by the finance ministry and URA TREP](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>). The local government ministry [has no IT strategic plan](<https://www.oag.go.ug/storage/reports/PSM_CG_MS_2023_24_1740644791.pdf>), the Auditor General reported in December 2024.

**What is recorded.** In October 2026 the finance ministry said [IRAS holds 3,101,290 registered taxpayers, with bills, payments and arrears](<https://habaridaily.com/the-grizzly-wall-behind-governments-heavy-burden-in-funding-local-governments/>). The finance commission [describes it as registration, assessment, billing and payment](<https://thecooperator.news/kikuube-district-leaders-urged-to-utilise-automated-revenue-collection-system/>).

**Where the record is held.** IRAS gives local governments [a common database that reports to the centre](<https://habaridaily.com/the-grizzly-wall-behind-governments-heavy-burden-in-funding-local-governments/>), the finance ministry said in October 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In the year to mid-2026 IRAS [reached all 176 local governments and their own revenue rose 15% to Shs442.64 billion](<https://habaridaily.com/the-grizzly-wall-behind-governments-heavy-burden-in-funding-local-governments/>). In a sample of 30, the Auditor General [found staff using it but short of skills, connectivity and devices](<https://www.independent.co.ug/system-failures-blamed-for-shs52-billion-local-revenue-shortfall/>).

**Local governments recording.** In April 2025 the National Building Review Board said [IRAS ran revenue collection and payment in 166 vote-level local governments](<https://nbrb.go.ug/bims-and-iras-integrated-to-boost-compliance-and-revenue-generation-in-local-governments/>). The country has 176. In October 2026 the finance ministry said [it had been rolled out to all of them](<https://habaridaily.com/the-grizzly-wall-behind-governments-heavy-burden-in-funding-local-governments/>).
