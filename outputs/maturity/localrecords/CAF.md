# Central African Republic

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Under an agreement signed with the Mairie de Bangui in December 2025, [BMI-WFS, a firm under Ivorian law, is to run the city's digital collection system](<https://acap-rca.com/2025/12/18/bangui-signature-dune-convention-pour-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>).

**What is recorded.** In late 2020 municipal agents [began a door-to-door census of traders in central Bangui, to build a taxpayer database with an individual sheet showing what each owes](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20201201-centrafrique-la-mairie-de-bangui-modernise-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupr%C3%A8s-des-commer%C3%A7ants>).

**Where the record is held.** In December 2020 a city-centre trader told RFI that [Bangui town hall had no computerised file of taxpayers and that its collectors issued paper receipts](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20201201-centrafrique-la-mairie-de-bangui-modernise-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupr%C3%A8s-des-commer%C3%A7ants>).

**Last twelve months.** The agreement to digitise Bangui's municipal revenue was [signed on 12 December 2025 in the presence of the Minister of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation](<https://acap-rca.com/2025/12/18/bangui-signature-dune-convention-pour-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>).

**Local governments recording.** The [agreement of 12 December 2025 covers the municipal revenue of Bangui, the capital](<https://acap-rca.com/2025/12/18/bangui-signature-dune-convention-pour-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>), and names no other commune.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Bangui town hall's census of traders and taxpayer database](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20201201-centrafrique-la-mairie-de-bangui-modernise-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupr%C3%A8s-des-commer%C3%A7ants>).

***Not held***. What the system agreed for Bangui will record, where it will be held and whether it is in use. No commune outside the capital is named, and no plan or finance is stated.
