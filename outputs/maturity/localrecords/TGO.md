# Togo

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The municipal tax project in Lacs 1 is [worked on with UNDP, which says it equipped about sixty communes with office furniture and IT equipment from 2020](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>), as of May 2021.

**What is recorded.** After a visit on 27 May 2021, [UNDP and the commune of Lacs 1, at Aného, were said to have initiated a project to digitalise the registration of municipal taxes](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>), alongside one for civil-status acts.

**Where the record is held.** The project to digitalise the registration of municipal taxes [belongs to the commune of Lacs 1 and UNDP](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>), on UNDP's account of May 2021.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is [the visit of 27 May 2021, after which the tax project was initiated](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>).

**Local governments recording.** One commune, [Lacs 1, had the tax digitalisation project in May 2021](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>), among the about sixty that UNDP says it equipped from 2020.
