# Nigeria

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. [The Local Addressing Database, which local government areas feed into state and national addressing units under NIPOST's standard](<https://nipost.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NIGERIAN-NATIONAL-ADDRESSING-STANDARD-AND-GUIDELINES.pdf>); [the Anambra State Internal Revenue Service's IGR portal, paid through POS and online, under a 2024 state law merging state and local government levies into one demand notice](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/STATE-BUSINESS-ENABLING-REFORM-ACTION-PLANS-BERAP-FY-2023.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any local government area keeps its own digital revenue record. The only revenue system named is one state's.
