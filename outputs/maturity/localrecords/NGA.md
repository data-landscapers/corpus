# Nigeria

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In March 2017 [the Edo State government introduced an automated revenue collection system under the Edo Jobs Initiative](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>). It did so with the local councils.

**What is recorded.** A journal article of November 2018 describes an app for local government revenue agents. In it, [agents register payers, businesses and vehicles and collect and print receipts](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>). Before it, the authors say, [revenue from drivers, traders and others was collected on manual receipts](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>). There was no data of collection.

**Where the record is held.** The agents' entries go onto [a taxpayers' database](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The system in the two local government areas of Edo State [was introduced in March 2017](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** The authors say [two of Edo State's local government areas use the system](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>). The two are Oredo and Egor, as at November 2018.

***Also on file***. [The Local Addressing Database, which local government areas feed into state and national addressing units under NIPOST's standard](<https://nipost.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NIGERIAN-NATIONAL-ADDRESSING-STANDARD-AND-GUIDELINES.pdf>). [The Anambra State Internal Revenue Service's IGR portal is paid through POS and online](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/STATE-BUSINESS-ENABLING-REFORM-ACTION-PLANS-BERAP-FY-2023.pdf>). The portal operates under a 2024 state law that merges state and local government levies into one demand notice.

***Not found***. Whether the two local government areas still use the system, and how many local government areas across the country keep a digital revenue record. Nothing on a local government area's own record is dated after 2018.
