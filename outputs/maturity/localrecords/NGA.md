# Nigeria

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In March 2017 [the Edo State government, with the local councils, introduced an automated revenue collection system under the Edo Jobs Initiative](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**What is recorded.** A journal article of November 2018 describes an app in which [local government revenue agents register payers, businesses and vehicles and collect and print receipts](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>). Before it, the authors say, [revenue from drivers, traders and others was collected on manual receipts, with no data of collection](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The agents' entries go onto [a taxpayers' database](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The system in the two local government areas of Edo State [was introduced in March 2017](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** The authors say [two of Edo State's local government areas, Oredo and Egor, use the system](<https://www.akamai.university/uploads/1/2/7/7/127725089/pjst19_2_242.pdf>), as at November 2018.

***Also on file***. [The Local Addressing Database, which local government areas feed into state and national addressing units under NIPOST's standard](<https://nipost.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NIGERIAN-NATIONAL-ADDRESSING-STANDARD-AND-GUIDELINES.pdf>); [the Anambra State Internal Revenue Service's IGR portal, paid through POS and online, under a 2024 state law merging state and local government levies into one demand notice](<https://anambrastate.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/STATE-BUSINESS-ENABLING-REFORM-ACTION-PLANS-BERAP-FY-2023.pdf>).

***Not found***. Whether the two local government areas still use the system, and how many local government areas across the country keep a digital revenue record. Nothing on a local government area's own record is dated after 2018.
