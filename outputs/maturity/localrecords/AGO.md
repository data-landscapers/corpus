# Angola

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Finance [launched and runs the Portal do Munícipe under Presidential Decree 47/18](<https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/>). In November 2021 its Secretary of State presented it as [a ministry tool, run with the ministry's SETIC-FP IT service, that the municipalities use](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/portal-do-municipe-em-risco-por-falta-de-infra-estruturas>).

**What is recorded.** In November 2021 the Portal [held the records of residents and their households and tracked the whole cycle of fees, licences and fines billed by local administrations, with payment by the state's single payment reference](<https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/>).

**Where the record is held.** The ministry describes the Portal as [a centralised system reached over the web, with revenue deposited in a Treasury sub-account](<https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/>). In August 2019 it [let central bodies see the amounts local administrations collect](<https://correiokianda.info/administracoes-municipais-vao-gerir-taxas-cobrados-localmente/>).

**Last twelve months.** The ministry [officially launched the Portal on 10 November 2021 across the 164 municipalities](<https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/>).

**Local governments recording.** The Secretary of State for Finance said on 10 November 2021 that the Portal was [implemented in all 164 municipalities, with telecommunications problems in some](<https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/>). In August 2019 the ministry said it had [created the technical conditions for it in 152 of 164](<https://correiokianda.info/administracoes-municipais-vao-gerir-taxas-cobrados-localmente/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [SIGT, the tax authority's integrated tax management system](<https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw41/mzu0/~edisp/minfin5354980.pdf>); [SIGFE, to be adopted for municipal budget preparation and execution, and SNCPE, to digitalise municipal procurement](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111623071533799/pdf/P17804001b89bf0080b90d0bd546f011451.pdf>); [RUPE, the national payment reference](<https://www.angolex.com/paginas/decreto-presidencial/regime-juridico-aplicavel-as-taxas-cobradas-pelos-orgaos-da-administracao-local-do-estado-176a-24a.html>); [the tax authority's Front Office for internet payments](<https://correiokianda.info/administracoes-municipais-vao-gerir-taxas-cobrados-localmente/>).

***Not held***. No share or count of the municipalities whose staff record in the Portal. Nothing dated after November 2021, so the system's current plan, finance and progress are not shown.
