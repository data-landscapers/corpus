# Digitalisation of local government records: revenue records

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The frame's anchor (`lookups/maturity-norms.csv`) is the African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy, with the decentralisation charter's Article 16. The Strategy asks states to "establish electronic government registers or digitalise existing ones, starting with an electronic population registry, eBusiness register and Land Use register", and to let organisations "reuse core registers".

**Neither measures what the sub-indicator measures.** The Strategy names national registers and no local government; Article 16 sets a duty and no measure. The ladder takes one thing from the Strategy: stage 5 needs `shared`, a record the centre can reuse. *(Read 2026-10-10.)*

## The ladder

| Stage | What is recorded | Where held | Local governments |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that local governments keep their revenue records on paper | `paper` | |
| 2 Preparing | A system is being prepared or piloted; or a `survey` only; or only the capital city or the upper tier records | Any, or not stated | Pilot local governments, or none |
| 3 Establishing | `register` or `receipts`: past its pilot and live in some local governments outside the capital | `shared` or `local` | A minority, a count with no total, or not published |
| 4 Operating | `register` | `shared` or `local` | More than half, on a share or a count with a denominator |
| 5 Leading | `register` | `shared` | 90 per cent or more, on a figure published within two years |

## Countries

### Tanzania: 4 Operating

By February 2023, 169 of 184 councils had started using a centrally hosted billing system; the share recording today is not known.

*Centrally collected.*

- The Controller and Auditor General reviewed bills issued through the TAUSI system to 30 June 2025 and found TZS 1.22 billion issued by seven LGAs unpaid, and cites the revenue administration manual requiring system bills to update taxpayer records on payment ([source, 2025-06-30](https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf)).
- The Controller and Auditor General's audit of FY2024/25 lists TAUSI as a revenue system managed by PMO-RALG as a centrally hosted application used by user public entities ([source, 2025](https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf)).
- PMO-RALG instructed LGAs on 31 January 2023 to install TAUSI by 31 March 2023 and stop collecting on LGRCIS; the audit draws TAUSI collection reports for 184 LGAs' own-source revenue (TZS 1.46 trillion in 2024/25) through PMO-RALG ([source, 2025-06-30](https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf)).
- PO-RALG's minister told the National Assembly that by February 2023, 169 councils had started to use TAUSI, which replaced LGRCIS in 2022/23; no total of councils is given in this document ([source, 2023-02](https://dailynews.co.tz/how-usaid-ps3-project-increased-efficiency-in-govt-systems/)).
- The CAG says there is no budget for system implementation in 137 LGAs and recommends PO-RALG secure funding for the full rollout of TAUSI in all 184 LGAs ([source, 2024](https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2023-24.pdf)).

### Angola: 3 Established

A finance ministry system recording municipal payers and fees was put in all 164 municipalities by 2021; how many record in it is not known.

- The Portal do Munícipe holds the records and management of residents and their households and tracks the whole cycle of fees, licences and fines billed by local administrations; payment is by RUPE ([source, 2021-11-10](https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/)).
- The Ministry of Finance describes the Portal as a centralised technological solution, web-accessed, with revenue deposited in a Treasury sub-account (SUB-CUT) ([source, 2021-11-10](https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/)).
- The Secretary of State for Finance said the Portal was implemented in all 164 municipalities (164 of 164, Ministry of Finance), with some telecommunications challenges for some administrations ([source, 2021-11-10](https://radionova.co.ao/ministerio-das-financas-lanca-portal-para-aumentar-nivel-de-arrecadacao-de-receitas-das-administracoes-locais/)).

### Burundi: 3 Established

Thirty communes collect local revenue electronically by 2026, recording payments only; where the record is held and the share of communes are not known.

*Advancing.*

- The catalogue note on the Interior ministry's Q3 FY2025/26 execution report lists 30 communes digitalised in communal revenue, with the digitalisation of collection being extended; no total of communes is given and the document dates only by its 2026-07 publication ([source, 2026-07](https://finances.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CANEVAS-RAPPORT_MIDCSP_2025-2026_T3.xlsx)).
- IBB M+ takes payments from traders by phone or from agents who convert cash to electronic money paid into the commune's account, with a notification per transaction to a commune officer, and keeps the data to follow revenue ([source, 2026-05-05](https://communebutihinda.gov.bi/butihinda-en-marche-vers-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-communales-grace-a-la-plateforme-ibb-m/)).
- Kayanza's accountant said 48 of the commune's 54 collectors use POS machines to collect, with the revenue transferred digitally to the commune's account and staff only monitoring ([source, 2025-06](https://burundi-eco.com/recettes-communales-la-commune-kayanza-plus-productive-au-cours-de-lexercice-2024-%e2%80%90-2025/)).
- UNDP's mission of 16-20 June 2025 reported the E-Taxe system operational in communes such as Kayanza; only Kayanza is named and no total is given ([source, 2025-06](https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/le-pnud-et-le-gouvernement-evaluent-conjointement-les-interventions-sur-terrain)).

### Eswatini: 3 Established

One council, Manzini, billed ratepayers by account and took payment by phone in 2024; other local governments' records are not known.

- Manzini Municipal Council issued 2024/2025 rates bills to individual ratepayers (by post, email and bulk SMS of the payable amount), keeps ratepayer contact and ownership details, and lets ratepayers check rates balances and pay on USSD *440#; payments carry the plot or account number ([source, 2024-04](https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/)).
- Only the Municipal Council of Manzini is described; the post gives no count of other Eswatini local governments ([source, 2024-04](https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/)).

- The ministry of finance's second and third quarter 2025 PFM report names one council, Basse Area Council, as operating a Digital Tax Collection System; no total of councils is given in the passage (document dated September 2025 and read by searching revenue terms) ([source, 2025-09](https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf)).
- Kanifing Municipal Council's Rates Manager says the council has integrated Google Plus Codes into its property rates database, and collectors carry tablets to navigate to registered properties (article of 29 June 2026; no date given for the integration) ([source, 2026-06](https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/)).
- The property rates database and the compliance mapping system are described as the Kanifing Municipal Council's own, with no national system or reporting to the centre mentioned (article of 29 June 2026) ([source, 2026-06](https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/)).
- Only the Kanifing Municipal Council is described as using the system; the article gives no total of local governments (article of 29 June 2026) ([source, 2026-06](https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/)).

### Ghana: 3 Established

Some assemblies record payers and bills in a shared national system, nine of 260 using it in 2019; how many do now is not known.

*Advancing.*

- dLRev holds a fiscal cadastre of businesses and properties (owner, type, location) linked to a digital address map, and manages billing and collection of property rates and business operating permits; the paper's 2019 data covers nine assemblies using it ([source, 2019](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/sites/default/files/gp_a4_digitization_final.pdf)).
- From 2017 dLRev has been hosted on the National IT Agency (NITA) national platform, so districts are set up remotely and there are no standalone operations ([source, 2017](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/sites/default/files/gp_a4_digitization_final.pdf)).
- Nine of Ghana's 260 MMDAs (3.5%) used dLRev throughout 2019 to collect property and business rates, per the GIZ-commissioned paper ([source, 2019](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/sites/default/files/gp_a4_digitization_final.pdf)).
- In February 2020 dLRev showed 47 of the 90 GIZ partner districts with properties registered for rates and 45 with businesses registered for operating permits; 60 had spatial data and 49 had fee-fixing loaded; no national total is given ([source, 2020-02](https://copenhagenconsensus.com/sites/default/files/gp_a4_digitization_final.pdf)).

### Kenya: 3 Established

Thirty-three of 47 counties had automated revenue collection by 2020, issuing electronic receipts; whether they hold a register of payers is not known.

- The paper (European Scientific Journal, September 2020) says 33 of the 47 county governments had automated their revenues, though some only partially and with structured revenue streams still manual ([source, 2020](https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560)).
- Automation is described as the move from paper receipts to an electronic system for data entry, processing and reporting, with point-of-sale machines in use; counties mostly collect unstructured revenues (markets, parking, cess), and a taxpayer database linked to the national identifier is listed only as a feature of the proposed integrated system ([source, 2020](https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560)).
- The survey of 24 of 47 counties found most counties cannot host their revenue data, so it is hosted by the vendor, and a majority of counties still use LAIFOMS in parallel instead of a system integrated with IFMIS ([source, 2020](https://eujournal.org/index.php/esj/article/view/13419/13560)).
- The counties' systems are used almost wholly for the revenue collection module (97 per cent of the 34 counties with systems); Nairobi uses E-Jiji Pay for electronic revenue collection and LAIFOMS to generate bill numbers, and the brief does not describe a payer register (document gives no event date, brief dated 2018) ([source, 2018](https://cegkenya.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Policy-Brief-Brochure2.pdf)).

### Malawi: 3 Established

Five of 35 local governments recorded revenue digitally by 2025, three with registers of payers; the cities' position since 2022 is not known.

- The IGC study says the revenue management systems in Blantyre, Lilongwe and Mzuzu consolidated scattered Excel and Word registers of property and business owners, with their payments and liabilities, into one database per city, used to produce demand notices and business licences ([source, 2022](https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf)).
- The IGC study says each of the four city councils runs its own cloud-hosted revenue system from a different vendor, with no national push or coordination, and that planned integration with the IFMIS, banks and mobile money had not been achieved in any of the four cities, so receipts were uploaded separately to both systems ([source, 2022](https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf)).
- The IGC study says revenue management systems were introduced in all four of Malawi's city councils (Blantyre, Lilongwe, Mzuzu, Zomba) since 2017, out of 35 local governments (28 districts, 4 cities, 3 municipal councils); it names none in the other 31 ([source, 2022](https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf)).
- UNDP Malawi reports that Mwanza District Council uses LORMIS to issue electronic tickets for market fees, vehicle parking and border entry fees and has a business registration component; the story says the system tracks who has paid and who has not, but does not describe a register of payers with bills against them ([source, 2025-02](https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue)).
- UNDP Malawi describes LORMIS as a system built and run by the Mwanza District Council's own systems analyst, working on an Android application and a second platform, with supervisors at the council office tracing transactions; it states no reporting to a national system ([source, 2025-02](https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue)).
- UNDP Malawi states that Mwanza is using all components of LORMIS; its developer hopes more districts will adopt it, and no other council or total of councils is given ([source, 2025-02](https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue)).

### Morocco: 3 Established

Communes issue local tax bills in a national system, in more than 100 large municipalities in August 2025; the share recording is not known.

*Centrally collected.*

- The World Bank mission found the Integrated Revenue Management System for Local Authorities (GIR-CT) operational at the national level, with its functions interfaced with local systems so municipalities manage the whole local taxation chain; verification of the result was still pending ([source, 2025-08-06](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf)).
- The World Bank status report says GIR-CT operationalisation is complete in more than 100 large municipalities (target of at least 100 large municipalities), still to be verified; it gives no total of municipalities ([source, 2025-08-06](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf)).
- Hespress, citing unnamed informed sources, reports that communal tax officials issue annual orders de recettes (bills) for local taxes in GIR-CT and, in a next phase, recovery of those orders is to run through the same system, sent electronically to the Treasury accountants; payments are collected by the accountants, not recorded in the commune's system ([source, 2025-05-29](https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html)).
- The Local Finance Directorate of the Interior Ministry's DGCT instructed prefecture and province services to have the collectivities' authorising officers use GIR-CT exclusively to upload revenue orders, and to abandon software the collectivities had bought or built; the orders pass electronically to the Treasury's accountants ([source, 2025-05-29](https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html)).

### Niger: 3 Established

The capital registers its taxpayers digitally and one commune outside it began in December 2025; how many communes record is not known.

*Advancing.*

- UNDP Niger says the commune urbaine of Diffa put Naneye Yarda into service on 11 December 2025, a system covering the whole tax cycle: a digital census of taxable establishments by agents with tablets, automatic tax calculation, tax notices with QR codes, and payment by electronic terminals carried by tax collectors ([source, 2025-12-11](https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda)).
- UNDP Niger says Diffa is the first region outside the capital to deploy the full digitalisation of municipal taxation; it gives no count of communes in the country, and the document names no other commune outside Niamey ([source, 2025-12-11](https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda)).
- Le Sahel quotes the City of Niamey's delegated administrator saying 50 field agents with mobile devices had geolocated over 2,000 taxpayers, and that municipal tax payments through Al Izza and Nita were integrated with real-time revenue tracking and an automated audit of each transaction; the article is dated 2025-01-29 and gives the launch as a Monday ([source, 2025-01](https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/)).
- Le Sahel reports the platform launched in the City of Niamey only; it names no other commune ([source, 2025-01](https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/)).

### Somalia: 3 Established

Four Puntland local governments kept a digital register of payers and payments in 2022; the share of all local governments is not known.

*Centrally collected.*

- The JPLG III report (to 30 September 2023) says a new property registration system, built on a GIS property survey, gives districts and taxpayers real-time tax payment tracking; the district is not named in the passage and the document gives no date for it, so its period end is used ([source, 2023-09](https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-05/annex_vii_jplg_2023.pdf)).
- Somali Public Agenda reports that in Garowe, Qardho and Galkacyo 20,000, 12,500 and 5,452 properties were GPS-mapped and registered with plate numbers embedded in the local government financial system, which generates the tax invoices and receipts payments made through mobile money (fieldwork October 2022) ([source, 2022-10](https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf)).
- The brief says the FMIS is used by four major Puntland local governments (Galkacyo north, Garowe, Qardho, Bosaso) to collect taxes and record local expenditure, and that Puntland local governments are the only ones in the study (Puntland, Galmudug, Benadir) using one; no national total of local governments is given ([source, 2022-10](https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf)).
- The FMIS was first introduced in Garowe local government in 2019 and in three other Puntland local governments in 2021, replacing printed receipts and books; payers now pay through mobile money accounts and not in cash to collectors ([source, 2021](https://somalipublicagenda.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/SPA_Governance_Briefs_26_2023_ENGLISH.pdf)).
- The report describes FMIS in Puntland districts as moving to a coherent financial system from district to state level, deployed through the Ministries of Interior and Finance; it also says the state-level FMIS is active and use had not yet been rolled out to district level in the national sense ([source, 2021-06](https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/40000/semi-annual_report_2021.pdf)).

### South Africa: 3 Established

Municipalities bill property rates per property and owner from their own billing systems, as described in 2023; how many do so is not known.

- National Treasury's circular says every municipality's property-rates billing report holds, per property, market value, category, amount billed, a unique property identifier linked to the valuation roll, owner and rebate, exemption and reduction values, because all financial systems use the valuation roll as the basis; it gives no count of municipalities ([source, 2023-12-07](https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf)).
- The circular has each municipality reconcile its valuation roll (Part A register) to its own billing system and send the reconciliation, not the billing record, to National Treasury quarterly through the GoMuni upload portal, within 10 working days of quarter end ([source, 2023-12-07](https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf)).

### Tunisia: 3 Established

Communes entered their business-tax rolls in a system shared with the Ministry of Finance in 2022; how many communes do so is not known.

*Centrally collected.*

- The GIZ study (March 2022) says that for the TCL business tax the commune president draws up a document listing the taxable persons and amounts due, enters it in the commune's GRB system and transmits it to the regional treasurer and the municipal receiver, who follow collection on the GRB network ([source, 2022-03](https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/els-document/2025-08/giz-idm-2203-les-revenus-municipaux-et-les-circuit-paiements-fr.pdf)).
- The GRB entry made by the commune is visible on the GRB network to the regional treasurer and the municipal receiver of the Ministry of Finance, who follow collection ([source, 2022-03](https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/els-document/2025-08/giz-idm-2203-les-revenus-municipaux-et-les-circuit-paiements-fr.pdf)).
- The study describes the GRB system as the communes' system and recommends adapting it to hold more types of municipal revenue and digital payment, but gives no count or share of communes using it ([source, 2022-03](https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/els-document/2025-08/giz-idm-2203-les-revenus-municipaux-et-les-circuit-paiements-fr.pdf)).

### Uganda: 3 Established

A revenue system was in use in nine cities in late 2024; the share of local governments recording in it is not known.

- Nansana's mayor says the system fixes assessment so that no assessor discretion enters, helps taxpayers keep records and records payments against a catalogue of fees, with an undefined 'other revenue' line open to under-recording; article of March 2024 ([source, 2024-03](https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/)).
- MoFPED's monitoring covered IRAS in nine cities, the tenth city, Jinja, still using e-LogRev, in the first half of FY2024/25; the report also says IRAS is being rolled out in all LGs but gives no count ([source, 2024-12](https://finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Domestic%20Revenue%20Mobilisation%20Strategy%20Semi%20Annual%20Report%20FY%202024-25.pdf)).
- The Independent, citing the IRAS website, says IRAS was rolled out in 98 districts, 21 municipalities and 9 cities (128 local governments) at a cost of UGX 20.07 billion to date; no national total is given. Date is the article's March 2024 ([source, 2024-03](https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/)).
- The article says the Ministry of Local Government's E-LogRev was operational in 48 local governments; no total is given. Date is the article's March 2024 ([source, 2024-03](https://www.independent.co.ug/shift-to-online-payment-boosts-local-revenue-by-nearly-80/)).

### Zimbabwe: 3 Established

In 2024 about 83 of 92 local authorities used a shared digital system; whether most record revenue payers in it is not known.

- HIT told the Harare Commission of Inquiry that around 83 of Zimbabwe's 92 local authorities use at least one LADS module; which modules, and whether any records revenue, is not stated ([source, 2024-11-25](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/)).
- HIT says LADS links local authorities' services, including financials and billing, to the national public financial management system ([source, 2024-11-25](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/hit-offers-affordable-erp-system-to-harare/)).
- Kwekwe City Council's finance department lists under Revenue the sections Utility Billing and Accounts Receivable, Cashiering and Credit Control, and states that its billing runs on the Promun ERP package; the page is undated, so the capture date 2026-08-08 is used ([source, 2026-08-08](https://kwekwecity.org.zw/departments/finance/)).
- CLGF reports the City of Mutare's Finance Department runs LADS debt management, procurement and budgeting modules, and that its revenue collection rose from 35% to nearly 72%; the document gives no date for the change ([source, 2021-10-05](https://www.clgf.org.uk/whats-new/news/digital-transformation-of-zimbabwe-local-government/)).

### Benin: 2 Nascent

*Register survey; Centrally collected.*

- The PAACOL project (started 2017, three years) selected 12 communes of Benin's 77 to be equipped with single cash windows for non-tax revenue during 2019/2020; the page is undated, so its 2019-09-19 publication date is used ([source, 2019-09-19](https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html)).

- The software is networked within the commune among its actors; no link to a national system is stated ([source, 2019-09-19](https://www.ancb.bj/index.php/projet/paacol.html)).

### Congo: 2 Nascent

A state platform for local governments' revenue was validated in February 2026; whether any local government yet records on it is not known.

*Advancing; Centrally collected.*
