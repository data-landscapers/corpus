# Gabon

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Libreville's pilot platform [is developed by the company GTI Gabon for the city hall](<https://www.gabonreview.com/libreville-enclenche-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>), and the mayor said on 3 July 2026 that the city's system [was developed with the public Treasury](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). Digitax [is the platform of the *Direction générale des Impôts*](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>).

**What is recorded.** At its launch on 29 July 2025 the Libreville pilot platform was described as [resting on a geolocated taxpayer file, online declaration and payment, automatic tax and penalty amounts and a dashboard showing who is up to date on which taxes](<https://www.gabonreview.com/libreville-enclenche-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>).

**Where the record is held.** Under the Owendo agreement of 29 April 2026 the tax directorate [will align the municipal agents' taxpayer directory with its own](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>).

**Last twelve months.** The commune of Owendo [signed an agreement with the tax directorate on 29 April 2026 to implement Digitax for municipal taxes](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>). A consultancy [presented the Colloc-Pay platform to the Ntoum commune and the Komo-Mondah departmental council on 19 June 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-ntoum-numerique-colloc-pay-un-nouvel-outil-pour-renforcer-les-recettes-des-collectivites-locales/>).

**Local governments recording.** The mayor of Libreville said on 3 July 2026 that [the city has its own operational system digitalising municipal revenue and securing collection](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). The city hall had [launched a pilot of digitised revenue collection on 29 July 2025](<https://www.gabonreview.com/libreville-enclenche-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Web: the Ministry of Digital Economy's [Madigipaie](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>), a QR code payment platform.

***Not held***. No local government outside Libreville is stated as recording, and where the record is held is not stated. Whether the system the mayor calls operational is the 2025 pilot is not held, and no plan or finance is stated.
