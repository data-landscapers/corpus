# Gabon

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Libreville's pilot platform [is developed by the company GTI Gabon for the city hall](<https://www.gabonreview.com/libreville-enclenche-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>). On 3 July 2026 the mayor said the city's system [was developed with the public Treasury](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). Digitax [is the platform of the tax directorate](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>).

**What is recorded.** The Libreville pilot platform launched on 29 July 2025 was designed to [rest on a geolocated taxpayer file](<https://www.gabonreview.com/libreville-enclenche-la-digitalisation-des-recettes-municipales/>), with online declaration and payment. A dashboard was to show who is up to date on which taxes.

**Where the record is held.** E-Tax payments [go directly to the municipal Treasury](<https://medias241.ga/e-tax-la-mairie-de-libreville-dresse-un-premier-bilan-de-la-collecte-numerique-des-taxes-de-marche/>). In Owendo the tax directorate [will align the municipal taxpayer directory with its own](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>), under an agreement of April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** On 29 April 2026 Owendo [signed an agreement with the tax directorate to use Digitax for municipal taxes](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitax-convention-de-partenariat-entre-la-direction-generale-des-impots-et-la-mairie-dowendo/>). On 19 June 2026 a consultancy [presented its Colloc-Pay platform to Ntoum commune](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-ntoum-numerique-colloc-pay-un-nouvel-outil-pour-renforcer-les-recettes-des-collectivites-locales/>). By October 2026 Libreville's E-Tax had recorded over 4,000 transactions.

**Local governments recording.** The mayor of Libreville said on 3 July 2026 that [the city has its own operational system digitalising municipal revenue and securing collection](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). On 30 October 2026 the city hall presented [e-Taxe municipale, a platform to declare and pay municipal taxes and fees online](<https://fr.infosgabon.com/gabon-libreville-digitalise-la-collecte-de-ses-taxes/>). The platform has been in service since 12 June 2026. By late October 2026 [E-Tax had enrolled nearly 2,000 traders in 15 Libreville markets](<https://medias241.ga/e-tax-la-mairie-de-libreville-dresse-un-premier-bilan-de-la-collecte-numerique-des-taxes-de-marche/>), with payments going straight to the municipal Treasury.

***Also on file***. Web: the Ministry of Digital Economy's [Madigipaie](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>), a QR code payment platform.
