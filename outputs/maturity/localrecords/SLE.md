# Sierra Leone

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Finance [runs the rollout with the Ministry of Local Government](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>), as at May 2026. It works under [a 2025-2029 strategy for property tax roll-out](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Fiscal-Decentralization-Policy-2025.pdf>). The rollout is [financed by a US$50 million IDA grant and GEF co-financing](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>). By 2025 [Freetown was running Moptax itself](<https://logri.org/story/impact-in-sierra-leone/>), LoGRI says.

**What is recorded.** The system [covers a georeferenced property roll, valuation, billing, demand notices and payment reconciliation](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>), the ministry said in May 2026. LoGRI reports [Kenema's property roll on Moptax doubled to 27,545](<https://logri.org/story/impact-in-sierra-leone/>), with compliance at 45%. Freetown's register holds about 110,000 properties.

**Where the record is held.** The ministry's notice of 20 May 2026 says the system is [integrated with the Central Government Monitoring and Reporting System](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>). Council records therefore report to the centre.

**Last twelve months.** Freetown City Council was [upgraded to MOPTAX 2.0 in December 2025](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>). In May 2026 the ministry [invited firms to roll the system out in Bo and Koidu New Sembehun](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>). Kenema [added mobile money payment](<https://logri.org/story/impact-in-sierra-leone/>).

**Local governments recording.** LoGRI says [Moptax runs in Freetown, Kenema and Makeni](<https://logri.org/story/impact-in-sierra-leone/>), Makeni since 2024. The ministry counts [three of 22 local councils with the upgraded system](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>) in December 2025.
