# Sierra Leone

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Finance [runs the rollout through its Fiscal Decentralization Division, with the Ministry of Local Government](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>), as at May 2026, under [a 2025-2029 strategy for property tax roll-out pilots and scaling-up plans](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Fiscal-Decentralization-Policy-2025.pdf>). The rollout is [financed by a US$50 million IDA grant and US$6.73 million of GEF co-financing](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>) under the Resilient Urban Sierra Leone Project.

**What is recorded.** The Municipal Property Tax System [covers a georeferenced property roll, valuation, billing, demand notices and payment reconciliation](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>), the ministry said on 20 May 2026. Freetown City Council's database [held more than 160,000 properties and billed each a Rate Demand Notice](<https://fcc.gov.sl/property-reform-faq/>) in 2020.

**Where the record is held.** The ministry's notice of 20 May 2026 says the system, which it calls MOPTAX 2.0, is [integrated with the Central Government Monitoring and Reporting System](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Freetown City Council was [upgraded to MOPTAX 2.0 in December 2025](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>). On 20 May 2026 the ministry [invited firms to roll the system out in Bo and Koidu New Sembehun City Councils over fifteen months](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** In December 2025 Freetown became [the third local council, after Kenema and Makeni, to have the upgraded system, of 22](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>), the Ministry of Finance says: 14 per cent of local councils given the system. Makeni's [was deployed in April 2025](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>), and the ministry called Kenema's [a pilot](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>) in May 2026.

***Not held***. Whether Kenema or Makeni bills from the system each was given, and any council recording outside Freetown past a pilot. The ministry's 2025 progress report still expected in 2026 the integration that its May 2026 notice describes as made.
