# Algeria

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. Central: the tax directorate's [Jibayatic](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>) and [Moussahamatic](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-orders-fast-tracking-of-digital-tax-data-integration>), the [online tax identification number platform, Tabioukom and Qassimatouka](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>), and the [general rolls for the *taxe foncière* and TEOM, drawn up by the tax directorate's regional centre at Constantine and sent to communal treasurers, who notify and collect](<https://www.ccomptes.dz/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Annuel-2020-F-1.pdf>). Survey: the interior ministry's [*Référentiel National d'Adressage*](<https://dtpsetif.bevrd.dz/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Guide_Procdures_dadressage_NA_00-08_2022.pdf>), a national addressing exercise launched in 2014. Accounts: the interior ministry's [computerised system for following each commune's financial position, in service from 11 December 2022](<https://algerie-eco.com/2022/12/19/suivi-de-la-situation-financiere-des-communes-mise-en-service-dun-systeme-informatise/>). Web: Algérie Poste's [Posty Mob](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2504/algeria-advances-digital-payment-ecosystem-through-post-energy-partnership>).

***Not held***. No source says how communes keep their revenue records, and none names a revenue system in preparation or on pilot. The one fact held is an audit of 22 communes over 2014 to 2018, which found most had no updated census of their taxable base and does not speak for the country.
