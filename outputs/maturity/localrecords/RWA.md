# Rwanda

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. [The local government tax register, kept by the Rwanda Revenue Authority, which collects on the districts' behalf, with 1,347,846 active taxpayers in June 2024](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Reports/Governance_and_Decentralization/Impact_Assessment_of_Decentralization_in_Rwanda_over_the_Last_25_Years__2000-2025__Full_Report.pdf>); [the authority's automated Local Government Tax Management system, which it calls LGT, for local governments' property tax](<https://www.addistaxinitiative.net/sites/default/files/resources/3.1.%20Presentation%20Karasira_IT%20Rwanda.pdf>); [CAMA, its computer-assisted mass appraisal model, being rolled out for property tax valuation](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Reports/Governance_and_Decentralization/Impact_Assessment_of_Decentralization_in_Rwanda_over_the_Last_25_Years__2000-2025__Full_Report.pdf>); [IFMIS, the integrated financial management information system, in local governments](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Policies/Governance___Decentralisation/Governance___Decentralisation_SSP.pdf>); [the Building Permit Management Information System, introduced in 2024](<https://minaloc.prod.risa.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Reports/Governance_and_Decentralization/Impact_Assessment_of_Decentralization_in_Rwanda_over_the_Last_25_Years__2000-2025__Full_Report.pdf>); [Irembo, the national service portal](<https://includeplatform.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Digital-divides-or-dividends-Assessing-the-inclusiveness-of-basic-services-in-Rwandas-digitalisation-agenda.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any district keeps a revenue record of its own. What is held is the national revenue authority's record, kept on the districts' behalf.
