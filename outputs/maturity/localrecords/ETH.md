# Ethiopia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** LoGRI's project page describes an assessment of the Computerized Property Tax Administration (CPTA) platform. The assessment is [run with the Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure's Urban Revenue Reform Project Office](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>). The Ministry of Finance's Tax Policy Department also takes part, with UNECA support.

**What is recorded.** CPTA is [a platform for administering property tax, developed in 2016](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>).

**Where the record is held.** The platform [was never fully implemented](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>), LoGRI says.

**Last twelve months.** The Addis Ababa City Government Revenues Bureau [began a property-tax reform pilot in July 2026, covering property identification and valuation](<https://birrmetrics.com/foreign-ministry-asks-diplomatic-missions-for-staff-and-property-records/>). The pilot moves from the land-rent and building-tax arrangement to a value-based property tax.

**Local governments recording.** CPTA [was developed for three pilot cities in 2016](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>), and [a national scale-up is planned](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>).

***Also on file***. Survey: the [Addis Ababa property-tax reform pilot](<https://birrmetrics.com/foreign-ministry-asks-diplomatic-missions-for-staff-and-property-records/>), which identifies and values properties.

***Not found***. Nothing has been found on what the platform records or where the record is held. Nor has anything been found on whether any of the three pilot cities still uses it. No plan or finance for it has been found. Nothing on it is dated within the last twelve months.
