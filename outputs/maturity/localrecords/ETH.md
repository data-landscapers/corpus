# Ethiopia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** An assessment of the Computerized Property Tax Administration (CPTA) platform is [run with the Ministry of Urban and Infrastructure's Urban Revenue Reform Project Office and the Ministry of Finance's Tax Policy Department, with UNECA support](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>), according to LoGRI's project page.

**What is recorded.** CPTA is [a platform for administering property tax, developed in 2016](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>).

**Where the record is held.** The platform [was never fully implemented](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>), LoGRI says.

**Last twelve months.** The Addis Ababa City Government Revenues Bureau [began a property-tax reform pilot in July 2026, covering property identification and valuation](<https://birrmetrics.com/foreign-ministry-asks-diplomatic-missions-for-staff-and-property-records/>), in a move from the land-rent and building-tax arrangement to a value-based property tax.

**Local governments recording.** CPTA [was developed for three pilot cities in 2016](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>), and [a national scale-up is planned](<https://logri.org/project/ethiopia-property-tax-system-review/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Survey: the [Addis Ababa property-tax reform pilot](<https://birrmetrics.com/foreign-ministry-asks-diplomatic-missions-for-staff-and-property-records/>), which identifies and values properties.

***Not held***. What the platform records and where the record is held are not held, nor whether any of the three pilot cities still uses it. No plan or finance for it is held, and nothing on it is dated within the last twelve months.
