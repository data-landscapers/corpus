# Tanzania

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The local government ministry, PMO-RALG, [manages TAUSI, the revenue system the councils use](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>), the Controller and Auditor General recorded for 2024/25, and the 2026/27 budget speech [assigns its development to that ministry](<https://www.mof.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/GOVERNMENT%20BUDGET%20%20SPEECH---ENGLISH%20VERSION_1781243659.pdf>). In his report on 2023/24 the auditor [recommended that the ministry secure funding for TAUSI's full rollout in all 184 local government authorities](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2023-24.pdf>).

**What is recorded.** The auditor [reviewed the bills issued through TAUSI to 30 June 2025 and found TZS 1.22 billion issued by seven authorities unpaid](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf>); the revenue manual he cites requires a bill to update the taxpayer's record on payment. A ministry official said on 10 September 2025 that [some councils issue receipts that do not come from the system](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/10/07/wataalamu-waeleza-changamoto-zitokanazo-na-halmashauri-kutotumia-mfumo-wa-tausi-kikamilifu-huweza-kusababisha-mapato-kupotea/>).

**Where the record is held.** TAUSI is [a centrally hosted application](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>) (audit of 2024/25). Some councils and village governments [still keep forest-product revenue in books and hard-copy documents](<https://dailynews.co.tz/government-moves-to-integrate-forest-revenue-systems/>), the forestry division said in August 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The budget speech of 11 June 2026 [commits the ministry and the revenue authority to finish linking TAUSI to the national tax system, and to add modules for buildings and for registering traders](<https://www.mof.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/GOVERNMENT%20BUDGET%20%20SPEECH---ENGLISH%20VERSION_1781243659.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** By February 2023 [169 councils had started to use TAUSI](<https://dailynews.co.tz/how-usaid-ps3-project-increased-efficiency-in-govt-systems/>), the minister told the National Assembly; the auditor counts [184 local government authorities](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf>). In 2023/24 [16 of them collected through the older LGRCIS instead](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2023-24.pdf>), and by December 2024 the property tax module was [in 47 of the 184](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2023-24.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Property rates collected centrally by [deduction through TANESCO electricity meters](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf>); the Tanzania Revenue Authority's [IDRAS](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tra-welcomes-taxpayers-at-the-official-launch-of-the-idras-system/>) and [Electronic Fiscal Device](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2023-24.pdf>); the Zanzibar Revenue Authority's [electronic receipt system](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-cuts-fines-for-businesses-that-fail-to-issue-e-receipts-5565968>); the payment gateway [GePG](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>); the accounts systems [MUSE](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>), [GAMIS](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>), [HCMIS](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>), [FFARS](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_Information_Systems_FY_2024-25.pdf>), [GoT-HoMIS](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf>) and [PlanRep](<https://dailynews.co.tz/how-usaid-ps3-project-increased-efficiency-in-govt-systems/>); and the forest licensing system [TAFFMIS](<https://dailynews.co.tz/government-moves-to-integrate-forest-revenue-systems/>).

***Not held***. No share of local government authorities recording in TAUSI has been published since February 2023; the newer figures concern one module or the authorities still on the older system. No domestic budget line for the system is stated.
