# Morocco

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** GIR-CT, the integrated revenue management system for local authorities, is [run by the Interior Ministry's directorate for local authorities with the national treasury](<https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html>), as reported on 29 May 2025. The minister's circular of 19 May 2020 [told authorising officers to equip their revenue offices and work with the treasury on extending it](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>).

**What is recorded.** Communal tax officials [issue the annual bills for local taxes in GIR-CT, and the treasury's accountants take the payments](<https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html>), as reported on 29 May 2025.

**Where the record is held.** The World Bank's status report on its mission of August 2025 [has GIR-CT operating at national level, interfaced with local systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf>). In May 2025 the ministry [told local authorities to upload their revenue orders in GIR-CT alone and to drop software they had bought or built](<https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html>). The treasury [collects all municipal revenues except the professional tax](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/308971641932185582/pdf/Morocco-Public-Sector-Performance-ENNAJAA-Program-for-Results.pdf>), the World Bank said in November 2021.

**Last twelve months.** On 29 May 2025 taxpayers were [being urged to declare and pay local taxes through the online platform set up with the treasury](<https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html>).

**Local governments recording.** The World Bank's status report of August 2025 [puts GIR-CT in place in more than 100 large municipalities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf>) and gives no total of municipalities. The Interior Ministry's circular [had it deployed in 969 of 1,590 local authorities on 19 May 2020](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The tax directorate runs the [Système intégré de taxation](<https://www.tadat.org/content/dam/tadat/en/assessments/Morocco%20-French%202025.pdf>) and [manages the base of the communal services, housing and professional taxes for the municipalities, with the treasury collecting](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/308971641932185582/pdf/Morocco-Public-Sector-Performance-ENNAJAA-Program-for-Results.pdf>). The State has [an online platform to fill and pay local taxes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf>), which the treasury and the Interior Ministry call the [plateforme électronique de déclaration et de paiement des taxes des collectivités territoriales](<https://fr.hespress.com/426411-casablanca-settat-mobilisation-des-autorites-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-des-taxes-locales-et-eviter-la-prescription.html>). Spending and accounts run in [GID](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf>) and [GID-CT](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/308971641932185582/pdf/Morocco-Public-Sector-Performance-ENNAJAA-Program-for-Results.pdf>), and staff records in [Indimaj](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/strategie-de-transformation-digitale-des-collectivites-territoriales>) and [@ujour-idm@j](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>). Permits go through [Rokhas](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/strategie-de-transformation-digitale-des-collectivites-territoriales>) and correspondence through the [Bureau d'Ordre Digital](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>).

***Not held***. How many local authorities record in GIR-CT, as a share: the newest count has no total and the only figure with one is a deployment figure of 2020. No plan or finance for the system, and nothing dated in the twelve months to September 2026.
