# Madagascar

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. The Ministry of Economy and Finance runs [SIGRNF, its information system for the State's non-tax revenues](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1642>). The tax directorate's [SAFI, the Système d'Administration Fiscale Intégré, manages State taxes and local taxes](<https://portal.impots.mg/textes/textes/2024-07-08--N-1253-2024-MEF-Ref-76VMQ8-QXPXJH.pdf>). The national decentralisation plan provides for connecting communes to [SIFC, the State's Système d'Information Financière des Communes](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing dated on or before 30 September 2026 shows a commune recording its payers or their payments in a system.
