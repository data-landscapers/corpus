# Madagascar

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** [In July 2024 the PIC project was funding the G-IFPB property-tax software for Antsiranana commune](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2024/07/antsiranana-des-efforts-pour-booster.html>) and the training of its staff. [UNDP developed the software and installed it in Toamasina in 2021](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>), with the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

**What is recorded.** [In February 2024 Mahajanga commune put two programs into use](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2024/02/commune-urbaine-de-mahajanga-logiciel.html>), SIGRL for local revenue and one for registering taxpayers. They hold each taxpayer's registration, payments and transfer orders. [In October 2026 Antananarivo registered its market traders on tablets](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610080562.html>) and began debiting their daily dues from chip cards.

**Where the record is held.** [Mahajanga's software was set up by the commune's own IT staff](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2024/02/commune-urbaine-de-mahajanga-logiciel.html>). [In Toamasina the property-tax software was installed at the commune and the regional tax office](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>) in 2021.

**Last twelve months.** [Antananarivo launched the digital collection of market dues on 7 October 2026](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610080562.html>). Markets are its third source of revenue, at 7 to 9 billion ariary a year.

**Local governments recording.** [Mahajanga is the one commune outside the capital shown recording its taxpayers](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2024/02/commune-urbaine-de-mahajanga-logiciel.html>) in its own software. Its staff were trained before the software went into use.

***Also on file***. The Ministry of Economy and Finance runs [SIGRNF, its information system for the State's non-tax revenues](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1642>). The tax directorate's [SAFI, the Système d'Administration Fiscale Intégré, manages State taxes and local taxes](<https://portal.impots.mg/textes/textes/2024-07-08--N-1253-2024-MEF-Ref-76VMQ8-QXPXJH.pdf>). The national decentralisation plan provides for connecting communes to [SIFC, the State's Système d'Information Financière des Communes](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>).

***Not found***. No share or total of communes recording their payers has been published.
