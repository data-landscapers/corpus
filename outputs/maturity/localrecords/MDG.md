# Madagascar

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In September 2021 [UNDP, through its GBCI project with the Ministry of Economy and Finance, supported and developed the G-IFPB property-tax software and installed it](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>).

**What is recorded.** UNDP states that G-IFPB, [fed by a 2019 taxpayer census, was handed over on 13 September 2021 to manage collection of the built-property tax in Toamasina](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>).

**Where the record is held.** The software was [installed at the Commune Urbaine de Toamasina and at the Direction Régionale des Impôts Atsinanana](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>), the regional tax office.

**Last twelve months.** UNDP reported [the software's operational launch and official handover in Toamasina on 13 September 2021](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>).

**Local governments recording.** In September 2021 [only Toamasina commune used the software, and staff were trained to replicate the process in the city's other arrondissements](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/g-ifpb-le-logiciel-de-gestion-de-limpot-foncier-sur-la-propriete-batie-operationnel-toamasina>).

***Also on file***. The Ministry of Economy and Finance runs [SIGRNF, its information system for the State's non-tax revenues](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1642>). The tax directorate's [SAFI, the Système d'Administration Fiscale Intégré, manages State taxes and local taxes](<https://portal.impots.mg/textes/textes/2024-07-08--N-1253-2024-MEF-Ref-76VMQ8-QXPXJH.pdf>). The national decentralisation plan provides for connecting communes to [SIFC, the State's Système d'Information Financière des Communes](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>).

***Not found***. Whether Toamasina went on to bill from the software after the handover, and whether any other commune was equipped. Nothing dated between September 2021 and 30 September 2026 shows a commune recording its payers or their payments in a system.
