# South Africa

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** National Treasury's budget circular of 7 December 2023 [has each municipality reconcile its valuation roll to its billing system and send the reconciliation to the Treasury every quarter](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>), within 10 working days of the quarter's end.

**What is recorded.** The circular says every municipality's property-rates billing report [holds, for each property, the market value, the category, the amount billed, the owner and any rebate, exemption or reduction](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>), with a unique identifier that ties the property to the valuation roll (December 2023).

**Where the record is held.** The billing record [stays in the municipality's own billing system](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>); what goes to National Treasury, through its GoMuni upload portal, is the reconciliation (December 2023).

**Last twelve months.** National Treasury [set the quarterly reconciliation in its circular of 7 December 2023](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>).

**Local governments recording.** The circular [describes the billing report of every municipality](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>), on the ground that all municipal financial systems take the valuation roll as their basis (December 2023).

***Noted, not assessed***. National Treasury's [GoMuni portal and Local Government Database and Reporting System](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>); the municipalities' [mSCOA-enabled ERP systems](<https://mfma.treasury.gov.za/Circulars/2024_25%20MTREF%20Budget%20Circular%20No.%20126%20-%2007%20December%202023%20%28final%29.pdf>) and [core financial systems](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/publications/other/MinAnsw/2020/PQ%201683%20-%20Brink%20-%20NW2073E.pdf>); [Blu Label's prepaid electricity vending and metering](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/26/after-the-cell-c-divorce-blu-label-is-making-a-play-for-electricity/>); and Hessequa Municipality's [Collab Citizen App](<https://www.hessequa.gov.za/129336-2/>).

***Not held***. No count or share of municipalities billing from a system is given. Nothing is held on who owns, plans or pays for the billing systems, and nothing is dated inside the last twelve months.
