# Zambia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Smart Zambia, the state digital agency, [announced the e-Council system in March 2026 as part of the national e-government plan](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>).

**What is recorded.** The e-Council system is planned to [let councils identify their revenue streams and track collections in real time](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>) (March 2026). A policy brief of May 2024 found councils' property tax administration [weakened by outdated cadastres and poor record keeping, with many tax maps covering a fraction of rateable properties](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The same brief says [most local councils still run on manual systems, and the few partly digitalised work in silos at department or section level](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>) (May 2024).

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 the government's chief technical officer said [all 116 local authorities were connected to the government network and had solar power](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>), ahead of an e-Council rollout planned for 2026.

**Local governments recording.** The first phase is [a proof of concept in eight councils, among them Ndola, Kitwe, Solwezi, Chingola, Kalulushi, Kalumbila and Chilanga](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>), with a demonstration of the system expected by 19 June 2026 before it is extended to the rest (March 2026).

***Noted, not assessed***. The [Lusaka digital base map of 2017](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>), a one-off survey of the city's properties; the payment channels [Government Service Bus](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>), [Government Electronic Payment Gateway and ZamPay](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>), [ZamPortal](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/05/29/councils-to-go-digital-as-lcc-becomes-first-on-zamportal/>), Lusaka City Council's [e-Council platform](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/08/03/lcc-urges-businesses-to-embrace-e-council-platform/>) and the [e-Council Digitisation Project](<https://www.facebook.com/MLGZambia/posts/press-releasefor-immediate-release2nd-july-2026eight-local-authorities-go-digita/1021935170223449/>); and the [Constituency Development Fund management information system](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>).

***Not held***. Nothing shows the e-Council system live in any council, or that the June 2026 demonstration took place. No plan period and no finance are stated, and the statement that most councils work manually dates from May 2024.
