# Zambia

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Smart Zambia is the state digital agency. It [announced the e-Council system in March 2026 as part of the national e-government plan](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>).

**What is recorded.** The e-Council system is planned to [let councils identify their revenue streams and track collections in real time](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>) (March 2026). A policy brief of May 2024 found councils' property tax administration [weakened by outdated cadastres and poor record keeping](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>). It found many tax maps covering a fraction of rateable properties.

**Where the record is held.** The same brief says [most local councils still run on manual systems](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>) (May 2024). The few partly digitalised councils work in silos at department or section level.

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 the government's chief technical officer said [all 116 local authorities were connected to the government network and had solar power](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>). An e-Council rollout was planned for 2026.

**Local governments recording.** The first phase is [a proof of concept in eight councils, among them Ndola, Kitwe, Solwezi, Chingola, Kalulushi, Kalumbila and Chilanga](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>). A demonstration of the system was expected by 19 June 2026. The system is then to be extended to the rest (March 2026).

***Also on file***. The [Lusaka digital base map of 2017](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-08/Kaputula%20and%20Oliveira-Cunha%20Policy%20brief%20May%202024_1.pdf>) was a one-off survey of the city's properties. Payment channels are the [Government Service Bus](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>), the [Government Electronic Payment Gateway and ZamPay](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>) and [ZamPortal](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/05/29/councils-to-go-digital-as-lcc-becomes-first-on-zamportal/>). Further channels are Lusaka City Council's [e-Council platform](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/08/03/lcc-urges-businesses-to-embrace-e-council-platform/>) and the [e-Council Digitisation Project](<https://www.facebook.com/MLGZambia/posts/press-releasefor-immediate-release2nd-july-2026eight-local-authorities-go-digita/1021935170223449/>).
