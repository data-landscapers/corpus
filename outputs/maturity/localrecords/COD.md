# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The digital tools for the decentralised territorial entities are [supported by USAID's Good Governance Activity, launched in July 2023, whose July 2024 workshop was co-hosted with COREF, the national public finance reform committee](<https://cd.usembassy.gov/les-etats-unis-appuient-les-efforts-visant-a-ameliorer-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques-des-entites-territoriales-decentralisees-etd/>).

**What is recorded.** A workshop of 23–24 July 2024 was [to standardise and assess tools for tracking revenue, registering taxpayers and budgeting](<https://cd.usembassy.gov/les-etats-unis-appuient-les-efforts-visant-a-ameliorer-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques-des-entites-territoriales-decentralisees-etd/>).

**Where the record is held.** The tools under assessment in July 2024 were [for the public financial management of the decentralised territorial entities themselves](<https://cd.usembassy.gov/les-etats-unis-appuient-les-efforts-visant-a-ameliorer-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques-des-entites-territoriales-decentralisees-etd/>).

**Last twelve months.** The Good Governance Activity [held its workshop on the tools on 23–24 July 2024](<https://cd.usembassy.gov/les-etats-unis-appuient-les-efforts-visant-a-ameliorer-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques-des-entites-territoriales-decentralisees-etd/>).

**Local governments recording.** In July 2024 the activity [worked with 20 decentralised territorial entities in four provinces: Haut Katanga, Lualaba, Tshopo and Haut Uele](<https://cd.usembassy.gov/les-etats-unis-appuient-les-efforts-visant-a-ameliorer-la-gestion-des-finances-publiques-des-entites-territoriales-decentralisees-etd/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Three provincial systems: [the city-province of Kinshasa's digital collection of property tax and rental income tax, with the water utility's REGI-PAY offered as a payment channel](<https://numerico.cd/2026/04/25/signature-dune-convention-historique-entre-la-ville-de-kinshasa-et-la-regideso-ladn-saluee-pour-son-role-moteur-et-pivot-dans-la-mutualisation-des-donnees-pour-loptimisati/>); [the Tshopo provincial revenue directorate's computerised one-stop counter](<https://acp.cd/province/tshopo-inauguration-dun-guichet-unique-des-recettes-pour-le-developpement-de-la-province/>); and [Moptax, which the Kasaï Central provincial government runs for Kananga's property tax](<https://logri.org/2025/04/building-local-government-capacity-for-property-tax-reform-in-kananga-drc/>).

***Not held***. What the tools record, where the record is held and whether any entity uses one. The tier of the 20 entities, the tools' owner and any plan are not stated.
