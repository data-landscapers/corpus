# Mauritania

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** El Mouhassil was [tested and provided to communes by the Interior Ministry's directorate for local authorities, with support from Germany, France and the World Bank](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>), the directorate's report of November 2022 says.

**What is recorded.** In Sebkha a seven-week census [found 10,564 taxpayers against the 2,000 officially recognised](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>), as reported in April 2020. The software, launched there in April 2019, [issues numbered, barcoded fiscal stamps and tracks each from stock to sale to payment into the commune's Treasury account](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>); agents with tablets scan a trader's stamp to see whether it is paid or overdue.

**Where the record is held.** Sebkha's mayor [describes the system as completely independent and run by the commune with its own stamps](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>), as reported in April 2020.

**Last twelve months.** On 11 July 2023 the commune of Rosso [held a training session on integrating civil-status records into its El Mouhassil programme](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/225638>).

**Local governments recording.** In April 2020 El Mouhassil was [live in one commune, Sebkha in Nouakchott, and being installed in eight others](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>). On 23 May 2023 the commune of Zouerate [launched its own digitised tax collection programme, built on a census of the town's commercial activities](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/222389>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Local accounts run in [SIGeL, the Système Intégré de Gestion Locale](<https://tresor.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/note-de-conjoncture2025.pdf>), which integrates [Elbeledi](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>) and [Alhazen](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>). The directorate's [Kachel toolbox](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>) covers communal acts, assets, staff, mail and archiving. The tax directorate has [a digital system for taxpayer registration, declarations and payments](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>), and communes have used [tablets for a census of fiscal potential](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any commune other than Sebkha records in El Mouhassil, and how many communes use it. Nothing is dated after July 2023.
