# Mauritania

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Interior Ministry's decentralisation directorate [validated the El Mouhassil user guide at a workshop in April 2025](<https://www.rimtoday.net/?q=node%2F45609>). The World Bank-backed Mudun project paid for training, equipment and collectors' bonuses. No plan naming the system is given.

**What is recorded.** The mayor of Ksar said in December 2023 that [El Mouhassil runs a full taxpayer census whose data go into the municipal database](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/235987>). The commune's office then applies the council's tax decisions to each taxpayer. In Sebkha a census [found 10,564 taxpayers against the 2,000 officially recognised](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>), as reported in April 2020.

**Where the record is held.** Each commune keeps its own record. Ksar's data are [entered in the municipal database, run by an office the commune created](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/235987>). Sebkha's mayor [called the system completely independent and run by the commune](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>).

**Last twelve months.** No event is dated inside the twelve months. The newest is the [April 2025 workshop on extending El Mouhassil](<https://www.rimtoday.net/?q=node%2F45609>) to the remaining municipalities.

**Local governments recording.** In April 2025 the directorate said the application, [in use since 2020, now serves about 30 municipalities](<https://www.rimtoday.net/?q=node%2F45609>). It gave no total of communes. In 2020 El Mouhassil was [live in Sebkha and being installed in eight other communes](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=735039>).

***Also on file***. Local accounts run in [SIGeL, the Système Intégré de Gestion Locale](<https://tresor.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/note-de-conjoncture2025.pdf>), which integrates [Elbeledi](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>) and [Alhazen](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>). The directorate's [Kachel toolbox](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>) covers communal acts, assets, staff, mail and archiving. The tax directorate has [a digital system for taxpayer registration, declarations and payments](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>), and communes have used [tablets for a census of fiscal potential](<https://www.uclg.org/sites/default/files/rapport_atelier_regional_dechanges_dispositifs_de_financement_des_ct.pdf>).
