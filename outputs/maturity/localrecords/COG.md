# Congo

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The *Plateforme de dématérialisation des recettes des collectivités locales* (PDRCL) was [given to the state's *Agence congolaise des systèmes d'information* (ACSI) by the 2025 finance law](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/systemes-dinformation-de-letat-les-differentes-plateformes-presentees-aux-deputes-171166>). ACSI's procurement notice of 4 September 2025 [lists equipment for it from the agency's 2025 investment budget, with the Ministry of the Interior as client](<https://armp.cg/download-bulletin.php?id=12&lang=en>).

**What is recorded.** The finance ministry said in February 2026 that the platform has [a back-office module for local government agents and a market-collector module, *Zando collecte*, that enrols taxpayers, records revenue by payment mode and tracks each taxpayer's operations](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/atelier-pdrcl_120226>).

**Where the record is held.** The ministry said on 14 February 2026 that the platform [brings the revenues of local governments into one information system](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226>).

**Last twelve months.** The platform [passed final validation at Kintélé on 14 February 2026](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226>) and ACSI [presented it to the Mayor of Brazzaville on 30 April 2026](<https://acsi.cg/actualites/presentation-de-la-plateforme-pdrcl-a-la-mairie-de-brazzaville-par-lacsi/>).

**Local governments recording.** The platform was validated after [workshops with the communes of Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226>). On 24 February 2026 ACSI [was preparing its deployment](<https://acsi.cg/actualites/reunion-strategique-sur-lavancement-des-projets-curs-et-pdrcl-au-ministere-des-finances/>), and in 2026 a pilot [was to follow at the Ndjindji central market in Pointe-Noire once municipal staff had been trained](<https://www.vox.cg/lacsi-deploie-la-plateforme-de-dematerialisation-des-recettes-au-marche-central-de-ndjindji/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Central: the [*Caisse unique des recettes de service* (CURS)](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/systemes-dinformation-de-letat-les-differentes-plateformes-presentees-aux-deputes-171166>), for state service revenue, and [FOUTA](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24.pdf>), the Treasury's payment platform for all public revenue, that of local governments included.

***Not held***. No local government is stated as recording on the platform, in Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire or elsewhere. No plan naming the platform is held.
