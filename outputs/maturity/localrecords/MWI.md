# Malawi

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In 2022 the four city councils' systems were [supplied by vendors under revenue-share contracts](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>), Blantyre's and Mzuzu's by Technobrain and Lilongwe's and Zomba's by Nitel. Mwanza District Council [adopted its system with the support of the United Nations Development Programme](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue>), as stated in February 2025. In August 2024 Lilongwe City Council said the United States had [agreed US$24 million in grant funding](<http://lcc.mw/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Lilongwe__City_Council_Policy_Statement_-_Sept_2024.pdf>) for property records and billing in four cities.

**What is recorded.** In 2022 the systems in Blantyre, Lilongwe and Mzuzu [held property and business owners with their payments and liabilities in one database per city](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>), used to produce demand notices and business licences. Zomba's [covered market rates, bus depots and parking only](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>) in March 2022. In February 2025 Mwanza [issued electronic tickets for market fees, vehicle parking and border entry fees](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue>).

**Where the record is held.** In 2022 each of the four city councils [ran its own cloud-hosted system from a different vendor](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>), none of them integrated with the treasury's system, banks or mobile money. Mwanza's is [built and run by the council's own systems analyst](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue>), as stated in February 2025.

**Last twelve months.** In February 2025 Mwanza District Council [was using every component of the system it calls LORMIS](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue>).

**Local governments recording.** Revenue management systems had been [introduced in all four city councils since 2017, of 35 local governments](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>), the International Growth Centre's study found in 2022. The United Nations Development Programme named [one district council, Mwanza](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/digital-tool-improves-how-mwanza-collects-revenue>), in February 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. [IFMIS](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>); the [New Local Authority Integrated Financial Management Information System](<https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ADVERT-EOI-LA%20IFMIS%202_FF.pdf>); [Blantyre City Council's digital payment system with NBS Bank](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawis-second-city-digitises-tax-collection/GxwQDq1DYWeMlPVo>).

***Not held***. No figure for the four cities after 2022, and no system named in the other 30 local governments. No current plan naming the systems, and no event in the last twelve months.
