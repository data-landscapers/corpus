# Guinea

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The president of Kindia's special delegation said on 25 March 2025 that [financing for the tool remained to be found](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>).

**What is recorded.** The tool presented on 25 March 2025 is one for [digitalising the management of communes' revenue](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>).

**Where the record is held.** The tool [comes from Benin](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>), where it was in use before its presentation in Guinea in March 2025.

**Last twelve months.** On 25 March 2025 the tool was [presented at the Conakry governorate](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>) to the communes that are to receive it and to officials of the ministry of territorial administration and decentralisation, ahead of a pilot.

**Local governments recording.** The pilot presented in March 2025 is for [Conakry's 13 urban communes, with Coyah and Kindia](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>), which are to experiment with the tool.

***Noted, not assessed***. [SIGIF](<https://www.anafic-gn.org/2024/12/19/recrutement-dun-expert-en-communication-et-accompagnement-du-changement-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation-de-la-gestion-budgetaire-et-comptable-des-collectivites-locales/>), the budget and accounting software the ministry of territorial administration and decentralisation is to acquire for local authorities.

***Not held***. Whether the pilot began, what the tool records and where its record is held. No owner or plan is named, and nothing is dated after March 2025.
