# Guinea

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** [In November 2025 Enabel, the Belgian aid agency, awarded the REVIS project in Mamou](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/le-partenariat-centre-ga%C3%AFa_revis-atelier-de-capitalisation-sur-la-activity-7490784325500710912-r9-L>) to a consortium of Global Itec and Le Partenariat. In March 2025 Kindia's special delegation said [financing for another revenue tool remained to be found](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>).

**What is recorded.** [REVIS digitised the census of market traders and motorcycle taxis in Mamou](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/le-partenariat-centre-ga%C3%AFa_revis-atelier-de-capitalisation-sur-la-activity-7490784325500710912-r9-L>), and the collection of their taxes. Taxi parking taxes have been collected through it since June 2026.

**Where the record is held.** No source found says where the REVIS record is held. [The tool presented in Conakry in March 2025 came from Benin](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>), where it was already in use.

**Last twelve months.** [The consortium reported in August 2026 that Mamou's market revenue had risen fourfold](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/le-partenariat-centre-ga%C3%AFa_revis-atelier-de-capitalisation-sur-la-activity-7490784325500710912-r9-L>). A week of taxi parking taxes now brings in what a year did before. Enabel held a workshop on 16 July 2026 on repeating the project in other local authorities.

**Local governments recording.** [Mamou is the one town where REVIS ran](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/le-partenariat-centre-ga%C3%AFa_revis-atelier-de-capitalisation-sur-la-activity-7490784325500710912-r9-L>), and the project is close to its end. The March 2025 tool was presented for [a pilot in Conakry's 13 communes, Coyah and Kindia](<https://guineematin.com/2025/03/25/guinee-vers-une-digitalisation-des-services-communaux-de-conakry-coyah-et-kindia/>).
