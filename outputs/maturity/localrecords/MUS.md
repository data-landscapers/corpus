# Mauritius

## Revenue records

***Noted, not assessed***. [The National Electronic Licensing System, for building and land-use permits through the local authorities](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/5-1%20Local%20Government/Annual%20Report%202024-2025.pdf>); [I-Council, a system built in-house by the District Council of Moka, piloted there in 2018 and in 12 local authorities by 2023-2024](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>); [the Mauritius Revenue Authority's Tax Payer Portal](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/Progress_Update_DGTS_Mauritius.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any council keeps a digital revenue register or receipt record. Nothing held says the national tax portal collects any council revenue.
