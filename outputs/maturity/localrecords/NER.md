# Niger

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The City of Niamey launched Naneye Yarda [under its 2025-2027 strategic plan](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>). The national information society agency built its architecture. The city's administrator called it [the main pillar of that plan](<https://anp.ne/niger-ceremonie-de-lancement-de-la-plate-forme-digitale-naney-yarda/>) at the launch on 3 October 2024.

**What is recorded.** In Diffa agents with tablets [census taxable establishments, and the system issues QR-coded notices paid by terminal](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>), from December 2025. In January 2026 Niamey's administrator said the project [had enumerated 35,000 taxpayers and follows each notice to payment](<https://www.lesahel.org/journee-portes-ouvertes-a-lhotel-de-ville-de-niamey-faire-decouvrir-linstitution-et-expliquer-le-fonctionnement-du-projet-naneye-yarda/>). It had assessed 8.08 billion FCFA and collected 2 billion.

**Where the record is held.** In Niamey, tax payments through Al Izza and Nita are [tracked in real time, with each transaction audited automatically](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>). The city's administrator said so in January 2025.

**Last twelve months.** The commune urbaine of Diffa [put the system into service on 11 December 2025](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>). It replaced manual collection with incomplete taxpayer files.

**Local governments recording.** The platform [launched in the City of Niamey](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>) in January 2025. The United Nations Development Programme named Diffa in December 2025 as [the first outside the capital to deploy it in full](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>). On 21 August 2025 Niamey [received 100 electronic payment terminals](<https://www.journalduniger.com/100-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-pour-niamey/>).
