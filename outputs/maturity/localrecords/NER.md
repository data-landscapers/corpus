# Niger

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The City of Niamey launched the platform it calls Naneye Yarda [under its 2025-2027 strategic plan, with the national information society agency building the architecture](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>), Le Sahel reported in January 2025. The city's delegated administrator called it [the main pillar of that plan](<https://anp.ne/niger-ceremonie-de-lancement-de-la-plate-forme-digitale-naney-yarda/>) at the launch on 3 October 2024.

**What is recorded.** In Diffa the system covers [a digital census of taxable establishments by agents with tablets, automatic tax calculation, tax notices with QR codes and payment by electronic terminals](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>), from 11 December 2025. In Niamey [50 field agents with mobile devices had geolocated over 2,000 taxpayers](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>) by January 2025.

**Where the record is held.** In Niamey municipal tax payments through Al Izza and Nita were [integrated with real-time revenue tracking and an automated audit of each transaction](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>), the city's administrator said in January 2025.

**Last twelve months.** The commune urbaine of Diffa [put the system into service on 11 December 2025](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>), replacing manual collection with incomplete taxpayer files.

**Local governments recording.** The platform [launched in the City of Niamey](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>) in January 2025. The United Nations Development Programme named Diffa in December 2025 as [the first outside the capital to deploy it in full](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>). On 21 August 2025 Niamey [received 100 electronic payment terminals](<https://www.journalduniger.com/100-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-pour-niamey/>).
