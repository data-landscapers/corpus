# Mozambique

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** CEDSIF, the state's information systems centre, [specifies e-SGA, a municipal management system of 22 modules, with ANAMM as client](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>). Its page was published on 8 August 2026. No plan or budget line is given.

**What is recorded.** Beira [linked its digitised land registry to its tax collection system](<https://partnersforwater.nl/news/beiras-land-registry-the-key-to-the-system-change-that-will-support-a-climate-resilient-city/>), Partners for Water reported in March 2025. The municipality now knows who owns what and who must pay property tax. In Nampula a 2025 case study found [no electronic system for the personal municipal tax](<https://doi.org/10.54751/revistafoco.v18n9-049>).

**Where the record is held.** Beira's [registry and tax collection run in the municipality itself](<https://partnersforwater.nl/news/beiras-land-registry-the-key-to-the-system-change-that-will-support-a-climate-resilient-city/>). The e-SGA specification [includes modules for municipal revenue, markets and commercial rents](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>).

**Last twelve months.** CEDSIF's page [setting out the e-SGA modules](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>) was published on 8 August 2026. It does not say e-SGA is in use.

**Local governments recording.** Beira is [the one municipality running the linked registry and tax system](<https://partnersforwater.nl/news/beiras-land-registry-the-key-to-the-system-change-that-will-support-a-climate-resilient-city/>). Other municipalities showed interest at a December 2024 seminar. Maputo had [a property tax system with a taxpayer register](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>) on a 2020 assessment.

***Also on file***. The Ministry of Finance's [SESPE, its service for payments to the state, takes in the municipalities](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/imprensa/discursos/2455-cge-2024-discurso-inicial-sexcia-mf-20-11-2025/file>). Budgets and accounts have [M17, a budget management system](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>), [SIGEF](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>) and the [e-SISTAFE Autárquico component of the SGA, launched as a pilot in three municipalities on 21 February 2020](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/lancado-o-sistema-de-gestao-autarquico-sga-na-componente-e-sistafe-autarquico/>). Municipalities are to have [virtual secretariats](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/digitalizacao-moderniza-gestao-municipal-no-pais/>) and to adopt the [Sistema Nacional de Gestão Documental](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/digitalizacao-moderniza-gestao-municipal-no-pais/>).

***Not found***. How many of the country's municipalities record their revenue digitally, and whether e-SGA is in use anywhere. No plan or finance is stated.
