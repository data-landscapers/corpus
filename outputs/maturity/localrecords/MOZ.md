# Mozambique

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** CEDSIF, the state's information systems centre, [specifies e-SGA, a single integrated municipal management system of 22 modules, with ANAMM named as client](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>), on a page published on 8 August 2026.

**What is recorded.** A September 2020 assessment for the Ministry of Economy and Finance [describes SISPGIA, the Maputo municipality's property tax system, built in 2009, with modules for a taxpayer register, taxpayer queries and the payment of taxes and fees](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>).

**Where the record is held.** The e-SGA specification [includes modules for municipal revenue management, markets and street trading, and commercial rents](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>).

**Last twelve months.** CEDSIF's page [setting out the e-SGA modules](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/sga/>) was published on 8 August 2026.

**Local governments recording.** The September 2020 assessment describes a property tax system [in one municipality, Maputo](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The Ministry of Finance's [SESPE, its service for payments to the state, takes in the municipalities](<https://www.mef.gov.mz/index.php/imprensa/discursos/2455-cge-2024-discurso-inicial-sexcia-mf-20-11-2025/file>). Budgets and accounts have [M17, a budget management system](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>), [SIGEF](<https://www.pdul.gov.mz/content/download/513/2779/file/Avaliacao%20Rapida%20do%20SIGEF%20Municipal.pdf>) and the [e-SISTAFE Autárquico component of the SGA, launched as a pilot in three municipalities on 21 February 2020](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/lancado-o-sistema-de-gestao-autarquico-sga-na-componente-e-sistafe-autarquico/>). Municipalities are to have [virtual secretariats](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/digitalizacao-moderniza-gestao-municipal-no-pais/>) and to adopt the [Sistema Nacional de Gestão Documental](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/digitalizacao-moderniza-gestao-municipal-no-pais/>).
