# South Sudan

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Stateman Software & Telecom Ltd is named as the developer, implementer and managing company](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>) of what Juba City Council calls its Digital Revenue Collection & Management System, working in partnership with the council, in October 2022. In September 2026 [the Central Equatoria State Government launched a digital revenue collection system and e-services for the council](<https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/>); no vendor or funder is named.

**What is recorded.** A council notice of 21 October 2022 [invites all businesses within the council and its blocks to register their data on the new system](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>). Registration and access to services had started, and manual payments for council services are to end.

**Where the record is held.** Businesses [register their data at the council's block headquarters offices](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), by the notice of October 2022.

**Last twelve months.** The state government launched the council's digital revenue system in September 2026, and [the council's first executive meeting under its caretaker mayor, reported on 8 October 2026, put the move from manual to digital collection at the centre of its priorities](<https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/>).

**Local governments recording.** [The roll-out covers Juba City Council and its blocks](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), and was announced as just started in October 2022. In October 2026 [the council was still described as moving from manual collection to a digital system](<https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Payroll software](<https://csps.org.ss/new/uploads/pubrp6.pdf>), named among the tools in place at states and local government institutions.

***Not held***. Where the record is held is not stated, and no local government other than Juba City Council is named. Whether the system of October 2022 stayed in use is not established, and no figure for collection on the system launched in September 2026 is held.
