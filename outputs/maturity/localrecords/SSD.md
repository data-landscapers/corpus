# South Sudan

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Stateman Software & Telecom Ltd is named as the developer, implementer and managing company](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>) of what Juba City Council calls its Digital Revenue Collection & Management System, working in partnership with the council, in October 2022.

**What is recorded.** A council notice of 21 October 2022 [invites all businesses within the council and its blocks to register their data on the new system](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>). Registration and access to services had started, and manual payments for council services are to end.

**Where the record is held.** Businesses [register their data at the council's block headquarters offices](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), by the notice of October 2022.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is of 4 October 2022, when [the mayor of Juba issued an office order to roll out digitalised revenue collection in the city and its block councils](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>).

**Local governments recording.** [The roll-out covers Juba City Council and its blocks](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), and was announced as just started in October 2022.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Payroll software](<https://csps.org.ss/new/uploads/pubrp6.pdf>), named among the tools in place at states and local government institutions.

***Not held***. Where the record is held is not stated, and no local government other than Juba City Council is named. Whether the system was still in use after October 2022 is not established.
