# South Sudan

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In October 2022 [Stateman Software & Telecom Ltd was named as developer, implementer and manager](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>) of Juba City Council's digital revenue system. It worked in partnership with the council. In September 2026 [the Central Equatoria State Government launched a digital revenue collection system and e-services for the council](<https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/>). No vendor or funder of that system is named.

**What is recorded.** A council notice of 21 October 2022 [invited all businesses in the council and its blocks to register their data on the new system](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>). Registration had started. Manual payments for council services were to end.

**Where the record is held.** Businesses [registered their data at the council's block headquarters offices](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), by the notice of October 2022.

**Last twelve months.** The state government launched the council's digital revenue system in September 2026. On 8 October 2026 [the council's first executive meeting under its caretaker mayor put the move to digital collection at the centre of its priorities](<https://radioyei.org/news/states/central-equatoria/juba-city/juba-city-council-discusses-digital-revenue-transition/>). In late October 2026 the caretaker mayor said [the council had moved all its revenue operations from manual forms to a digital payment system](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-city-council-launches-digital-payment-system-to-improve-transparency>). He said this followed the passing of a digitalisation law.

**Local governments recording.** [The roll-out of October 2022 covered Juba City Council and its blocks](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2022/10/21/advertisement-by-juba-city-council-jcc/>), and was then just starting. The reports of [October 2026](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-city-council-launches-digital-payment-system-to-improve-transparency>) also name only Juba City Council, the capital's council.

***Also on file***. [Payroll software](<https://csps.org.ss/new/uploads/pubrp6.pdf>), named among the tools in place at states and local government institutions.

***Not found***. Where the record is held has not been found. No local government other than Juba City Council is named. No figure for collection on the system launched in September 2026 has been found.
