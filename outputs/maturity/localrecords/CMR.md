# Cameroon

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The AIMF, the association of French-speaking mayors, reports that [FEICOM presented Inform'All City, a platform for collecting local tax from informal traders, on 21 March 2023](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actualite/au-cameroun-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-faciliter-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupres-des-commercants-informels/>).

**What is recorded.** On the platform, as presented in March 2023, [informal traders register and pay their local tax, against a site the commune assigns them](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actualite/au-cameroun-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-faciliter-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupres-des-commercants-informels/>).

**Where the record is held.** The platform is built so that, with traders registered and paying on it, [councils collect remotely](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actualite/au-cameroun-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-faciliter-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupres-des-commercants-informels/>).

**Last twelve months.** The platform, to be deployed first in two councils, [was presented on 21 March 2023](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actualite/au-cameroun-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-faciliter-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupres-des-commercants-informels/>).

**Local governments recording.** In March 2023 the platform [was to be deployed first in the councils of Yaoundé II and Douala III](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actualite/au-cameroun-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-faciliter-la-collecte-des-taxes-aupres-des-commercants-informels/>), in the political and economic capitals, with no wider count given.

***Also on file***. [Sim-Ba, public accounting software for the communes](<https://www.revue-irs.com/index.php/home/article/download/397/261>); [Mobile Tax, the tax directorate's payment channel, named as a payment mode replacing cash for local taxes under the new local tax law](<https://minfi.gov.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DGI_EXPOSE_CONFERENCE_ANNUELLE_2025.pdf>).

***Not found***. Whether the platform was deployed in the two councils or in any other, and where its record is held. Nothing dated after March 2023 shows a commune keeping a digital revenue record.
