# Eswatini

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The rates are billed and collected by [the Municipal Council of Manzini](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/>), on its notice of April 2024.

**What is recorded.** For 2024/2025 the council [issued rates bills to individual ratepayers by post, email and bulk SMS, and keeps their contact and ownership details](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/>); payments carry the plot or account number. In December 2020 it said [rates had been billed in April 2020 and some property owners had not paid since](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/you-can-now-pay-rates-using-e-mali/>).

**Where the record is held.** Ratepayers [check their rates balance and pay on the USSD code \*440#](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/>), as of April 2024.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is of April 2024, when the council [introduced the USSD code for checking balances and paying, alongside bulk SMS of bill amounts](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/>), for the 2024/2025 billing year.

**Local governments recording.** One council, the Municipal Council of Manzini, [billed its ratepayers for 2024/2025](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/rates-bills-for-2024-2025-financial-year-now-available/>), as it [did in April 2020](<https://manzinicity.co.sz/you-can-now-pay-rates-using-e-mali/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Where the record is held, who finances the billing, and whether any local government other than Manzini keeps such a record are not established; no total of local governments is given. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months.
