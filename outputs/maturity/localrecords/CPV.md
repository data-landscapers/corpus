# Cabo Verde

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The finance ministry's special projects unit [issued terms of reference in 2025 for the Territory Management modules of SIMple](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>), a planned successor to the municipal information system. The work is [funded from a US$15 million World Bank credit for the Harmonization and Improvement of Statistics in West Africa Project](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**What is recorded.** The planned modules [cover the cadastre, property assessment and property tax collection](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Where the record is held.** The system the modules are to succeed, SIM 2.0, [handles accounting, financial management and budget execution, the allocation of revenues and expenses](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Last twelve months.** The terms of reference for the property tax modules [were issued in 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Local governments recording.** The modules are [planned for the municipalities, all of which run SIM 2.0](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Accounts: [SIM 2.0, the *Sistema de Informação Municipal*](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>), its update [SIMple](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/politica/2025/11/11/governo-investe-125-mil-contos-na-modernizacao-digital-dos-municipios-para-reforcar-descentralizacao/99887>), and [SIMple's administrative and financial modules](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/TdR+SIMple+Administrative+and+Financial+Modules.pdf/88112f19-03b8-7b99-b9f4-435554ea2109?t=1714124769360>). Central: the State Treasury's [*Documento Único de Cobrança* (DUC)](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/92878/122657/8.BO+n%C2%BA+35+Portaria+n%C2%BA+28+de+2012++DUC.pdf/203ac883-ed8c-304f-80f1-fc1c1699ebe2>).

***Not held***. What municipalities record of their revenue, where they hold it and how many of them record are not held. No source says the property tax modules are piloted or live.
