# Cabo Verde

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The decentralisation ministry [is bringing a new municipal information system, Simpol, to the municipalities](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/municipios-ganham-novas-ferramentas-para-arrecadar-mais-impostos>) through its Directorate-General for Decentralisation. The finance ministry's projects unit [issued terms of reference in 2025 for property tax modules of SIMple](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>), a planned successor to the municipal system. That work is [funded from a US$15 million World Bank credit](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**What is recorded.** Simpol is [to support the preparation and collection of the new property tax from 2027](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/municipios-ganham-novas-ferramentas-para-arrecadar-mais-impostos>), the Directorate-General says. The planned SIMple modules [cover the cadastre, property assessment and property tax collection](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Where the record is held.** The modules are to succeed SIM 2.0, the municipal system now in use. [It handles accounting, budget execution and the allocation of revenues and expenses](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Last twelve months.** On 28 October 2026 the Directorate-General said [Simpol would be applied in all 22 municipalities from that day](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/municipios-ganham-novas-ferramentas-para-arrecadar-mais-impostos>). It was presented to municipal staff at a two-day event.

**Local governments recording.** [Municipal staff were trained on Simpol](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/municipios-ganham-novas-ferramentas-para-arrecadar-mais-impostos>), which is to be applied in all 22 municipalities. No municipality is yet shown recording payers or payments in it.

***Also on file***. Accounts: [SIM 2.0, the *Sistema de Informação Municipal*](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>). Its update [SIMple](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/politica/2025/11/11/governo-investe-125-mil-contos-na-modernizacao-digital-dos-municipios-para-reforcar-descentralizacao/99887>). And [SIMple's administrative and financial modules](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/TdR+SIMple+Administrative+and+Financial+Modules.pdf/88112f19-03b8-7b99-b9f4-435554ea2109?t=1714124769360>). Central: the State Treasury's [*Documento Único de Cobrança* (DUC)](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/92878/122657/8.BO+n%C2%BA+35+Portaria+n%C2%BA+28+de+2012++DUC.pdf/203ac883-ed8c-304f-80f1-fc1c1699ebe2>).

***Not found***. What municipalities record of their revenue, and where they hold it, has not been found. No source says Simpol or the property tax modules record payments yet.
