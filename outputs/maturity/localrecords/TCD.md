# Chad

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The taxpayer census is [run by the commune of Mongo in partnership with the firm IT Solution Center](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>), each enumerator accompanied by a collector and a municipal police officer, in April 2025.

**What is recorded.** The census [covers shops, bakeries and restaurants, and is described as the start of a digital tax system in which payments are to be recorded directly](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>), as of April 2025.

**Where the record is held.** Payments are to be recorded in [the commune's management system](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>), which it calls the *système de gestion communal*, by the account of April 2025.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is of 2 April 2025, when [Mongo launched the ten-day taxpayer census](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>), to move local tax collection to a digital system.

**Local governments recording.** One commune, [Mongo, began the census in April 2025 as the start of its digital tax system](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The [*adressage de N'Djaména*](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/ndjamena-mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere/>), a street-addressing and enumeration exercise of the city with the AIMF and the European Union, 2013 to 2016; and [SIM_ba](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/ndjamena-mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere/>), the AIMF's budget and accounting software.

***Not held***. Whether any commune records its payers or their payments in a system, and where such a record is held, is not established. No commune other than Mongo is named, and nothing is dated inside the last twelve months.
