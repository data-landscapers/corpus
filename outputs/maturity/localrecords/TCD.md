# Chad

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** Mongo's taxpayer census is [run by the commune with the firm IT Solution Center](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>). Each enumerator works with a collector and a municipal police officer. No plan or budget line is given.

**What is recorded.** Mongo's census of April 2025 [covers shops, bakeries and restaurants](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>). It is described as the start of a digital tax system recording payments directly. N'Djamena's census of April 2026 [enrolled the first traders of the Central Market](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-la-commune-de-ndjamena-digitalise-la-collecte-des-recettes/>).

**Where the record is held.** Payments are to be recorded in [the commune's management system](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>), by the account of April 2025.

**Last twelve months.** On 21 April 2026 the capital [launched digital collection of municipal revenue](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-la-commune-de-ndjamena-digitalise-la-collecte-des-recettes/>), starting at the Central Market.

**Local governments recording.** One commune, [Mongo, began the census in April 2025 as the start of its digital tax system](<https://tchadvision.com/mongo-le-recensement-fiscal-se-modernise-pour-optimiser-la-gestion-des-contribuables-decouvrez-les-enjeux-cles-aujourdhui/>). On 21 April 2026 N'Djamena [began a digital taxpayer census and collection at its Central Market](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-la-commune-de-ndjamena-digitalise-la-collecte-des-recettes/>), with other markets to follow.

***Also on file***. The [*adressage de N'Djaména*](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/ndjamena-mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere/>), a street-addressing and enumeration exercise of the city with the AIMF and the European Union, 2013 to 2016; and [SIM_ba](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/ndjamena-mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere/>), the AIMF's budget and accounting software.
