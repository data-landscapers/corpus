# Libya

## Revenue records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Local Government of the Government of National Unity [runs the National Programme for Activating Local Revenues](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>). Its [action plan for municipalities covers economic-activity revenue, electronic collection, revenue regulations and a forum](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>).

**What is recorded.** The programme's projects include [an electronic collection system and a database of municipal economic activities](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>), as reported in June 2024.

**Where the record is held.** The local revenue system and the electronic collection project both sit [under the ministry's national programme](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>).

**Last twelve months.** On 10 June 2024, [27 municipalities signed a sponsorship document committing them to implement the programme](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>).

**Local governments recording.** The programme director said in June 2024 that it [targets both municipalities where the local revenue system is already activated and those where it still needs activating](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/441789>), and gave no count of either.

***Also on file***. [The Ministry of Local Government's local correspondence system and local employment system, both for municipal mayors](<https://alsabaah.ly/%d9%85%d8%ac%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a8%d9%84%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a9/>).

***Not found***. What the local revenue system records, where its record is held, and how many municipalities use it or which they are. The 27 signatories are not said to use it; 21 municipalities were given access and training in October 2025.
