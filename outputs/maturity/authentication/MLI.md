# Mali

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Mali launched on 10 February 2026 two online platforms, for the criminal record and the nationality certificate, where citizens apply using their NINA and civil-status details (Maliweb)](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/le-ministere-de-la-justice-a-lere-du-numerique-voici-le-casier-et-la-nationalite-en-ligne-3113977.html>). [Civil servants who had not validated their biometric physical check in the SIGRH payroll and HR system had to produce papers including the carte Nina, under a grace period from 8 September 2025 extended to 30 January 2026 (L'Essor)](<https://lessor.ml/posts/sigrh-au-dela-du-delai-de-grace-69981e06e8d50>). [DPI Map records that NINA has no digital authentication function and no authentication through a government portal](<https://dpimap.org/mali/>).

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/le-ministere-de-la-justice-a-lere-du-numerique-voici-le-casier-et-la-nationalite-en-ligne-3113977.html>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/le-ministere-de-la-justice-a-lere-du-numerique-voici-le-casier-et-la-nationalite-en-ligne-3113977.html>).
