# Mali

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [DPI Map records that NINA, Mali's national identification number, has no digital authentication function and no authentication through a government portal](<https://dpimap.org/mali/>), in its record dated 17 June 2025. In December 2023 the head of the electronic certification and signature service said [electronic signature had not officially started in Mali](<https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/certification-et-signature-electronique-la-4eme-session-ordinaire-du-comite-fait-letat-des-lieux-3045750.html>).

**Used across services.** [Mali launched two online platforms, ecasierjudiciaire.gouv.ml and enationalite.gouv.ml, on 10 February 2026, for the criminal record and the nationality certificate, where citizens apply online with their NINA and civil-status details](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/le-ministere-de-la-justice-a-lere-du-numerique-voici-le-casier-et-la-nationalite-en-ligne-3113977.html>).

**Remote proof of identity.** The nearest held fact is a paper requirement: [Mali's civil servants who had not validated the biometric physical check of the SIGRH payroll and HR system had to produce papers including the carte Nina, the national identity card, under a grace period running from 8 September 2025, later extended to 30 January 2026](<https://lessor.ml/posts/sigrh-au-dela-du-delai-de-grace-69981e06e8d50>).
