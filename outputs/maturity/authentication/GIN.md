# Guinea

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [On 22 January 2026 the DGE and ONECI signed a convention under which the national register of persons (RNPP) is to be the sole official source of voter identification data for the biometric electoral file](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [The digital agency ANDE is developing a public key infrastructure to secure electronic signatures, and nine draft texts on the state's electronic signature were under review on 21 October 2026](<https://guineenews.org/2026/10/21/signature-electronique-de-letat-la-guinee-accelere-sa-marche-vers-le-zero-papier-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>); [ANSSI experts were being mobilised](<https://vision224.com/administration-publique-la-guinee-accelere-le-deploiement-de-la-signature-electronique-securisee>) to deploy it for the administration in place of scanned signatures.
