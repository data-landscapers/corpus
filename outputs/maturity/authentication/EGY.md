# Egypt

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Egypt's Public Prosecution limited its online personal-status document service to users verified through the Digital Egypt portal's digital identity on 10 September 2026](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%88%D8%AB%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7/7541763>). [Investors incorporating a company on the GAFI portal must hold a valid electronic signature token](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5259847.aspx>). [Mobile operators must compare an applicant's selfie with the photograph tied to the national ID before issuing a SIM](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/egypt-expands-biometric-sim-registration-after-identity-misuse-case>).

**Used across services.** The same digital identity serves more than one body: [the Public Prosecution's family-case enquiry service, from 1 September 2026](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/1/النيابة-العامة-تتيح-خدمة-الاستعلام-الإلكترونى-عن-دعاوى-الأسرة-والأحكام/7533251>), [its criminal-judgment enquiry service](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>), and [the Ministry of Social Solidarity's foster-family services, whose first phase launched on 29 August 2026 with sign-in through the digital identity system](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/29/التضامن-تطلق-أولى-مراحل-منظومة-الخدمات-الرقمية-الخاصة-بالأسر-البديلة/7529204>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Interior Ministry announced in March 2026 a pilot of MOIEG-PASS, an app in which users scan their national ID and face to sign in to a few government services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/egypt-pilots-selfie-biometrics-to-streamline-access-to-digital-govt-services>). [A biometric verification and e-signature system for telecom companies' apps opened for a trial period in September 2026](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>).
