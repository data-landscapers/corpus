# Egypt

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [On 10 September 2026 the Public Prosecution limited its online personal-status document service to users verified through the Digital Egypt portal's digital identity](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%88%D8%AB%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7/7541763>). [Investors incorporating a company on the GAFI portal must hold a valid electronic signature token](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5259847.aspx>). On 28 October 2026 the Ministry of Justice said [citizens can register property remotely, through to e-signature, on one platform](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%85%D8%B9/7597491>).

**Used across services.** The Digital Egypt identity serves more than one body. [From 1 September 2026 the Public Prosecution's family-case enquiry service required it](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/1/النيابة-العامة-تتيح-خدمة-الاستعلام-الإلكترونى-عن-دعاوى-الأسرة-والأحكام/7533251>). [Its criminal-judgment enquiry service has checked enquirers the same way since July 2026](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>). [On 29 August 2026 the Ministry of Social Solidarity launched the first phase of its foster-family services](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/29/التضامن-تطلق-أولى-مراحل-منظومة-الخدمات-الرقمية-الخاصة-بالأسر-البديلة/7529204>). Users sign in through the digital identity system.

**Remote proof of identity.** [In March 2026 the Interior Ministry announced a pilot of MOIEG-PASS](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/egypt-pilots-selfie-biometrics-to-streamline-access-to-digital-govt-services>). Users scan their national ID and face to sign in. [In September 2026 a biometric verification and e-signature system for telecom companies' apps opened for a trial period](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>). In August 2026 Enterprise reported that [some Digital Egypt services need the companion Digital Identity app](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/02/your-guide-to-navigating-egypts-online-government-services/>) to complete biometric checks. In October 2026 Badawi said [digital identity and biometric checks were still being developed](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8381060>) with the Interior Ministry.
