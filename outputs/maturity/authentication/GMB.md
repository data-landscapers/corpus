# Gambia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The diagnostic says the Drivers License System uses the National ID system for biometric verification of applicants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). The aim is to prevent identity fraud and duplication. [The World Bank ID4D diagnostic says the national ID's NIN was populated into the eCRVS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>) to cross-verify parents' IDs. This was done when new NINs were issued after the mass enrolment. [The government says the National Identity Management System, launched 30 June 2026](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>), combines a national identity register with biometric technology and real-time verification. Its purpose is to give a verifiable digital identity.

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest fact: [in 2025 a World Bank diagnostic said the driving licence system uses the National ID for biometric checks on applicants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest fact: [in April 2025 testing of an integrated eCRVS and electronic ID for health insurance cards was completed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).
