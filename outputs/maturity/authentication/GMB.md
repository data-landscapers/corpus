# Gambia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The diagnostic says the Drivers License System uses the National ID system for biometric verification of applicants to prevent identity fraud and duplication](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). [The World Bank ID4D diagnostic says the national ID's NIN was populated into the eCRVS to cross-verify parents' IDs when new NINs were issued after the mass enrolment](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). [The government says the National Identity Management System, launched 30 June 2026, combines a national identity register with biometric technology and real-time verification to give a verifiable digital identity](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).
