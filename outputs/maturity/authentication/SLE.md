# Sierra Leone

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [NCRA Director General Massaquoi said on 10 August 2026 that NCRA has established an identity verification platform in use](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>). [Citizens' Barray, a civil-society account, states that NatCA has implemented NIN-SIM integration, meant to strengthen identity verification and reduce fraud](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/citizens-barray_freeid-identity-telecommunications-activity-7492303807360679936-U3Br>). [The NCRA Director-General said the NCRA operates an eKYC platform integrated with multiple institutions (unnamed, uncounted) enabling identity verification against the NIN, which covers about 93 per cent of the population](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [Minister Salima Bah told Parliament on 4 August 2026 that the Government remains committed to completing the nationwide SIM registration and National Identification Number linkage exercise](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-bah-government-on-mobile-money-fraud/>).

**Used across services.** [The same NCRA identity verification platform is used by institutions including financial and telecommunications companies, per the Director General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>). [NCRA's Deputy Director of ICT named banking, public education and employment as requiring the NIN](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sierra-leone-reaches-93-percent-national-id-coverage-80-percent-services-digitized>). [The Act's eKYC fee schedule lists users in banking, telecoms (mobile company), tax, social security, education, lands and licensing (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/The-Finance-Act-2020.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>).
