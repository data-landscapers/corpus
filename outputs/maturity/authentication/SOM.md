# Somalia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** In late October 2026 [NIRA announced that its Hubiye platform is in operation](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/somalia-puts-hubiye-national-id-verification-platform-into-operation>). Service providers check an ID against its database by a one-time password sent to the holder. [The HSC service checks students against the national ID](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>), GovStack says. In August 2026 the Accountant General [said the government payroll will be checked against NIRA's ID system](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>), with a pilot from 1 October 2026.

**Used across services.** [UNDP Somalia reports Hubiye checks across 25 services](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>), public and private, including every commercial bank. [NIRA says over 100 services use the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>). They include banks, SIM registration, the Accountant General's online signatures and the education and transport bodies.

**Remote proof of identity.** [NIRA's eAqoonsi app carries the national ID on a phone](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/somalia-pushes-for-stronger-adoption-of-eaqoonsi-digital-id-app>). The digital credential has the same legal standing as the card. By August 2026 the app had passed 50,000 downloads. [NIRA lists mobile ID among its new components](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).
