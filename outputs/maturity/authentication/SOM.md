# Somalia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The Office of the Accountant General announced in a letter of 29 August 2026 that the government payroll and SFMIS will be integrated with NIRA's national identification system to verify public employees' identity, with a pilot from 1 October 2026 and nationwide completion by 31 December 2027](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>). [The eAqoonsi digital ID app, launched in 2025 by NIRA, is in use with more than 50,000 downloads](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/somalia-pushes-for-stronger-adoption-of-eaqoonsi-digital-id-app>). [The HSC service checks students' identity against the eAqoonsi/NIRA ID through GovStack's Identity Verification and Consent block, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>). [The article lists the HUBIYE Verification Platform for real-time authentication among NIRA's new components](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).

**Used across services.** [UNDP Somalia reports Hubiye eKYC verification across 25 integrated services spanning government and private sector platforms, including the whole commercial banking sector](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [NIRA says 100+ services are integrated with the national ID, among them the Accountant General's online signature services, banks, telecoms SIM registration and the education, transport and civil service bodies](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [NIRA's eAqoonsi app, on Google Play and the Apple app store, lets citizens carry their national ID on a smartphone as a digital credential with the same legal validity as the physical card; it has passed 50,000 downloads](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/somalia-pushes-for-stronger-adoption-of-eaqoonsi-digital-id-app>). [Credentials may be provided as a digital mobile ID, with the e-Aqoonsi Mobile App among NIRA's new components](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).
