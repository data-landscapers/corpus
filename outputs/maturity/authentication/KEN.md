# Kenya

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The civil registration system is linked to the national ID system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [Parents' ID details are checked when a birth is registered](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [The IPRS Secretary said over 800 organisations are integrated for citizen verification](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>). [The government has piloted the Maisha Digital ID, a virtual ID for smartphone users, since November 2023](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/state-revs-up-maisha-namba-digital-id-awareness-drive/>). [Starlink Kenya now checks subscribers' IDs to meet the regulator's KYC rules](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/06/19/starlink-hits-25000-subscribers-in-kenya/>).

**Used across services.** [Banks, insurers, SACCOs, telecoms and employment agencies are among the 800 organisations verifying citizens](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>), the IPRS Secretary said. [The Maisha Card is checked for SIM registration and bank account opening](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-2-Kenya-Final.pdf>). In October 2025 the Communications Authority [required every critical information infrastructure system to use digital certificates only from the certification service providers it licenses](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/technology/222141/ca-orders-telecom-operators-to-adopt-licensed-digital-certification-services-or-face-penalties>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>).
