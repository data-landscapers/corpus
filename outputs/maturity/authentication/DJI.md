# Djibouti

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The director general says the directorate is working on an interoperability system so that administrations, banks and social protection bodies can verify the authenticity of a national ID card in real time; the document speaks of this in the future tense (they 'will be able to verify')](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [The Interior Ministry says Mobile ID, launched officially on 10 February 2026, lets citizens authenticate electronically to reach public services and do procedures remotely](<https://interieur.gouv.dj/forum-sur-lidentite-numerique-et-lancement-du-projet-mobile-id/>). [TechHive Advisory says a Mobile ID is being rolled out in Djibouti and relies on the national identification number](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>).

**Used across services.** [The Interior Ministry says partner administrations use Mobile ID's authentication services, without direct access to the databases](<https://interieur.gouv.dj/forum-sur-lidentite-numerique-et-lancement-du-projet-mobile-id/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Interior Ministry says Mobile ID, a smartphone digital identity, lets citizens carry out administrative procedures remotely](<https://interieur.gouv.dj/forum-sur-lidentite-numerique-et-lancement-du-projet-mobile-id/>).
