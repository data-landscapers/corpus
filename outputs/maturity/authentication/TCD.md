# Chad

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [On 8 May 2026 ANATS and Airtel Tchad signed a partnership, Tchadinfos reported](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/>). [Under it ANATS will verify Airtel's customer data and interconnect the two systems to identify mobile subscribers](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/>). [The enrolment campaign was due to start in the following days](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [on 10 March 2026 the finance ministry and ANATS signed a memorandum on consulting the national biometric population register](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/03/10/tchad-le-ministere-des-finances-et-lanats-signent-un-accord-pour-securiser-et-partager-les-donnees-didentite/>). [It aims to improve identification, verification and authentication in administrative and financial procedures](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/03/10/tchad-le-ministere-des-finances-et-lanats-signent-un-accord-pour-securiser-et-partager-les-donnees-didentite/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [in May 2026 ANATS agreed to identify mobile subscribers by linking its system to Airtel's](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/>).
