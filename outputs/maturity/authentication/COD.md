# DR Congo

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [RDC-PASS, a digital identifier to authenticate Congolese for digital services, was activated on 13 June 2026](<https://oeildafrique.com/2026/09/17/rdc-pass-identite-numerique/>). [It runs under a 20-year public-private partnership](<https://oeildafrique.com/2026/09/17/rdc-pass-identite-numerique/>). [The minister said in September 2026 that it had entered a first operational phase, with 50 enrolment agents trained](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>). [A pilot in Kinshasa was to follow](<https://oeildafrique.com/2026/09/17/rdc-pass-identite-numerique/>). [A ministerial order of August 2024 gave the regulator ARPTC electronic certification on a provisional basis](<https://arptc.gouv.cd/app/uploads/2025/03/Discours-Pr-Katende-2-ans-du-Code-Numerique.pdf>). [In October 2026 another order named the heads of the electronic certification authority, ANCE](<https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-serge-kaozi-lubamba-nomme-a-la-tete-des-missions-de-l-ance>).
