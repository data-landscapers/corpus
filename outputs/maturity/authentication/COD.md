# DR Congo

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Agence Ecofin reports Minister Kibassa Maliba said on 28 September 2026 that RDC-PASS, launched in June to identify and authenticate Congolese for digital services, entered a first operational phase in September with 50 enrolment agents trained](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>). [Oeil d'Afrique reports that RDC-PASS, a digital identifier meant to provide biometric authentication for SIM registration, unified access to administrative platforms and automated customer identity checks, was activated on 13 June 2026 under a 20-year public-private partnership, with 50 enrolment agents trained on 15 September 2026 and a pilot phase in Kinshasa to follow](<https://oeildafrique.com/2026/09/17/rdc-pass-identite-numerique/>). On 19 October 2026 a ministerial order [named a head and a deputy for the electronic certification authority, ANCE](<https://numerico.cd/fr/article/rdc-serge-kaozi-lubamba-nomme-a-la-tete-des-missions-de-l-ance>), as part of setting it up.

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>).
