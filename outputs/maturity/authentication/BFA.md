# Burkina Faso

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** On 15 September 2026 the Prime Minister [launched FasoSign](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>), the national e-signature platform, which ANSSI says authenticates the signer and guarantees the document's integrity, with keys held in the country. It followed the installation of the public key infrastructure platform and the [training of about thirty staff in April 2025](<https://prod.cybastiontech.com/blog/newsroom-4/cybastion-trains-the-it-teams-of-the-government-of-burkina-faso-in-the-electronic-signature-system-348>).

**Used across services.** ANSSI's director general said FasoSign is [already operational on three government systems](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>): SIGED for official correspondence between institutions, CIMEXT for mission orders and SYC@D for land cession orders. The Prime Minister told every ministry to set a timetable to make it the ordinary way of signing.

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact is [FasoSign's launch](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>), which signs documents rather than proving a person's identity online.
