# Lesotho

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Digital identity cards in Lesotho are listed among domestic eKYC frameworks that have financial services use cases](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>). [The 2026 MICSTI-UNICEF work plan continues deployment of MOSIP-based infrastructure to enable secure authentication across government and private-sector services, including mobile-based verification](<https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/>). [Co-Develop says FinMark Trust's pilot between Lesotho and South Africa demonstrated real-time cross-border identity verification, with Lesotho migrants accessing formal remittance services in South Africa using their national digital IDs](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). [A real-time cross-border ID-verification-for-payments pilot between South Africa and Lesotho preceded the August 2025 SADC regional DPI framework announcement](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>).
