# Lesotho

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Digital identity cards in Lesotho are listed among domestic eKYC frameworks](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>) with uses in financial services. [The 2026 MICSTI-UNICEF work plan continues building MOSIP-based identity infrastructure](<https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/>). It aims at secure authentication across government and private services, including checks by phone. [A FinMark Trust pilot let Lesotho migrants use their national digital IDs](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>) to reach formal remittance services in South Africa. [That cross-border verification pilot came before the SADC regional DPI framework of August 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/regional-dpi-initiative-supports-kyc-digital-id-interoperability-across-16-countries>). [In June 2026 the finance ministry sought bids for a recovery site for the identity systems](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00452505>), including a hardware security module for PKI.

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 FinMark Trust listed digital identity cards among eKYC frameworks used in financial services](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 FinMark Trust listed Lesotho's digital identity cards among domestic eKYC frameworks](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>).
