# South Africa

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Minister Leon Schreiber unveiled South Africa's first working digital identity credential, bound to his own smartphone, as a prototype](<https://www.citizen.co.za/news/end-of-the-line-heres-when-the-old-sa-green-id-book-will-be-discontinued/>). [Minister Leon Schreiber demonstrated a working MyMzansi digital ID credential issued through the population register, but gave no public date; the annual performance plan targets hosting by 31 March 2027 and citizen credentials in a mobile wallet in 2027/2028](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [Mobile operators and the Department of Justice propose reforms to SIM registration under RICA that add real-time checks against the Home Affairs database; ACT has not disclosed how the new process will work](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>). On 1 October 2026 Minister Schreiber [showed the digital ID in the MyMzansi app on his phone](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-digital-id-could-change-how-you-pay/287578/>), with an operational digital ID targeted for 2027/2028.

**Used across services.** [Mobile network operators verify identities against the Home Affairs database and that Home Affairs verification systems are already used by banks, so telecoms and banking both check against it](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>). [The article names banks (TymeBank, Capitec), mobile networks (Vodacom, MTN) and insurers as users of the NPR verification check](<https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** In October 2026 Research ICT Africa's Drew Haller wrote that [Home Affairs' digital ID had reached a public trial in the MyMzanzi app](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>).
