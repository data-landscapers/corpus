# Rwanda

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** NIDA's new digital identity [will let citizens show their ID by a QR code on a phone or computer](<https://umunota.com/en/over-10-million-rwandans-registered-for-digital-id/>). A national ID deck of May 2026 says [IN Groupe was procured to supply authentication and a PKI for digital signatures](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>). Digitised birth registration linked to the national ID [allows real-time identity checks across sectors](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>), Vital Strategies says. NIDA [expects to issue digital IDs in July 2027](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>).

**Used across services.** [Vital Strategies says Rwanda's digital system connects with hospitals, government agencies and businesses and supports access to around 200 essential services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).
