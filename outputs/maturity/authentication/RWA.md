# Rwanda

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [NIDA says the new digital identity system will let citizens use a digital version of their ID through a QR code on a phone or computer and a token, separate from the national ID number, to access personal information](<https://umunota.com/en/over-10-million-rwandans-registered-for-digital-id/>). [Vital Strategies says Rwanda's digitised birth registration integrated with the national ID system has led to real-time identity verification across previously siloed sectors](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>). [NIDA is enrolling people for the Digital ID, with Digital IDs expected in July 2027, and describes technology replacing the physical card as the main means of authentication](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>).

**Used across services.** [Vital Strategies says Rwanda's digital system connects with hospitals, government agencies and businesses and supports access to around 200 essential services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).
