# Uganda

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** NITA-U says the [UGHub platform lets institutions verify citizens against NIRA's registry](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>) in real time. Its August 2026 terms of reference say it [operates a national public key infrastructure](<https://nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-08/PKI%20Audit%20TORs-%20Final%20-%2019-08-26.pdf>) with a national root certification authority and a document-signing service.

**Used across services.** NITA-U said in October 2026 that UGHub [connects NIRA's registry to more than 150 ministries, departments and agencies](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>), plus banks and telecoms. [URSB verifies identity in business registration over a real-time link with NIRA](<https://ursb.go.ug/2025/09/02/ursbs-digital-transformation-enhances-service-access-and-transparency/>), its ICT commissioner said in August 2025. It told ID4Africa in 2025 that [UgPass has over 10 million subscribers and more than 35 ministries and agencies](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS3-5-NITA-DigitalTrust-FINAL.pdf>), and hundreds of service providers.

**Remote proof of identity.** NITA-U's 2025 slides show the [UgPass app verifying users of e-services without passwords](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS3-5-NITA-DigitalTrust-FINAL.pdf>), with digital signature by PKI certificates; a digital credential wallet and signing on the phone are listed for UgPass 2.0. In August 2025 the Daily Monitor reported UgPass was [to be extended to all ministries, departments and agencies](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-introduces-e-signatures-to-curb-fraud-in-public-offices-5171052>).
