# Congo

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [In October 2025 the coordinator of the NIU project said his team was building a platform to check NIU numbers online](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>). [It is to tell real numbers from false ones](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. No verification service used by several bodies, and no licensed trust service provider, has been reported. The nearest fact found is [the online NIU check being built in 2025](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. No app lets people prove who they are online with the NIU. The nearest fact found is [the same NIU project](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>).
