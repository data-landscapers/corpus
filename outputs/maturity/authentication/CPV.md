# Cape Verde

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** By January 2026 the national identity system, SNIAC, [had issued about 1,291,910 digital certificates](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-24>) for authentication and qualified signature, the justice IT institute IMIJ says. The national ID card carries [a chip for secure authentication and digital signature](<https://portalconsular.mnec.gov.cv/en/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao>) once its digital functions are switched on. In March 2026 SNIAC [published version 1.9.9 of its card middleware](<https://sniac.cv/mw/>). Its kit lets public or private services check the holder, read their data and take a signature. [By December 2025 all professionals in criminal courts used qualified digital signatures](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/juvenal-pereira-presidente-do-imij-sij-j%C3%A1-chegou-a-todas-as-comarcas.-n%C3%A3o-encontr%C3%A1mos-nenhuma-resist%C3%AAncia>) in the justice information system, the IMIJ president said.

**Used across services.** The state platform Autentika [gives single sign-on across public and private applications](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/autentika>). It accepts the ID card with a PIN, the Mobile Digital Key, or an email and password. SNIAC [serves dozens of public and private bodies](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-24>), banks and insurers among them. It runs more than 134 configured services and has answered over 30 million requests. The Justice Portal, launched in December 2024, [signs users in through Autentika](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** Holders of the ID card can [obtain the Mobile Digital Key online](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/cabo-verde-mobile-digital-key>). A legislative decree created it in 2020. They sign in with a PIN and a one-time code. The code comes by SMS or through the key's mobile app, and they can sign documents too. In July 2026 Caboverde24 reported [the ID.GOV.CV digital ID wallet](<https://caboverde24.info/en/2026/07/22/ja-sabe-como-aceder-aos-servicos-publicos-sem-sair-de-casa-analisamos-o-site/>). It is a government app for iOS and Android that keeps a person's digital identity on the phone.
