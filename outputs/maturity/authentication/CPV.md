# Cape Verde

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [SNIAC published version 1.9.9 of its national ID card (CNI) middleware, whose SDK lets public or private services authenticate the holder, read their data and collect a digital signature, with front-office readers for counter services](<https://sniac.cv/mw/>). [The PDEX page says all integrations of the Consular Portal with the civil identification system run through PDEX, giving access to citizens' data; it does not say the portal verifies people](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>). [The Autentika page says a citizen may authenticate with the CNI at any Cabo Verdean public administration portal that has joined Autentika, and that any duly authorised application may integrate by OpenID Connect](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/>).

**Used across services.** [The CMDCV holder can access various digital services on public portals and sign documents with a single login, and that applications can apply to use Autentika](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/cabo-verde-mobile-digital-key>). [The Autentika portal says the platform gives single sign-on across multiple public and private applications, and that any duly authorized application can use it](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/autentika>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [Citizens holding the National ID Card can obtain the CMDCV online and authenticate with a PIN plus a one-time code received by SMS or through the CMDCV mobile application, with a qualified electronic signature](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/cabo-verde-mobile-digital-key>).
