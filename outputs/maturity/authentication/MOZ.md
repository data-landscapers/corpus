# Mozambique

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** Mozambique runs a state digital signature service, the Digital Certification System (SCDM), launched in 2023. [INTIC says accredited users sign and verify electronic documents on it free of charge](<https://intic.gov.mz/sistema-de-certificacao-digital-mocambique-registou-crescimento-na-adesao-de-assinaturas-electronicas-em-2024/>). [In November 2025 the minister said it had 500 active digital signatures](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>). [Decree 40/2026 lets acts in the low-risk business procedure be validated by electronic signature](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/atividades-economicas-de-baixo-risco-em-mocambique-com-certidao-em-duas-horas-e-assinatura-digital/>), Lusa reported in August 2026. It takes effect within 90 days.

**Used across services.** The SCDM serves public and private bodies. [INTIC says more than ten public institutions used it by the end of 2024](<https://intic.gov.mz/sistema-de-certificacao-digital-mocambique-registou-crescimento-na-adesao-de-assinaturas-electronicas-em-2024/>), among them the Supreme Court and the one-stop business office. [In November 2025 the Attorney General's Office, the Ministry of Education and Culture and Eduardo Mondlane University joined](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>). The same report says more than 500 institutions sign documents through it.

**Remote proof of identity.** No mobile ID or digital ID wallet is in service. [The ATDI tendered a national mobile digital ID and signature infrastructure in May 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mozambique-seeks-partner-for-mobile-digital-id-e-signature-rollout>), funded by the World Bank. [By September 2026 a shortlist had gone to the World Bank, with contracting planned for that month](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).

***Also on file***. [Bilhete de Identidade (BI)](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>); [National identity card (DNIC)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/mozambique-approves-new-national-id-framework-to-speed-card-issuance>); [Centro Pronto](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-centro-pronto-document-application-service-to-expand-to-diaspora/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service.
