# Mozambique

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Mozambique's ATDI says procurement of a national mobile digital ID and digital signature infrastructure, for identification, authentication and electronic signatures under the World Bank-financed EDGE project, has a shortlist submitted to the World Bank, with contracting planned for September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). [Biometric Update reports Mozambique recently tendered a mobile ID and digital signature infrastructure project](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/mozambique-approves-new-national-id-framework-to-speed-card-issuance>). [Biometric Update reports Mozambique's ATDI has called for expressions of interest, World Bank funded, for a mobile digital ID and digital signature infrastructure with PKI/CA for qualified certificates](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mozambique-seeks-partner-for-mobile-digital-id-e-signature-rollout>). [Minister Americo Muchanga said the Mozambique Digital Certification System (SCDM), launched in 2023, has 500 active digital signatures](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>).

**Used across services.** [The SCDM covers public and private institutions, with the Attorney General's Office, the Ministry of Education and Culture and Eduardo Mondlane University newly joined, and more than 500 institutions signing documents through it](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-reforca-estrategia-de-digitalizacao-com-500-assinaturas-electronicas-e-expansao-da-infra-estrutura-tecnologica/>).
