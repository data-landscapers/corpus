# Seychelles

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Government e-services that require SeyID need a 60% validation level, reached by checking the holder's NIN card or passport by video against the Civil Status System](<https://seyid.gov.sc>). [The Seychelles Licensing Authority's business and vehicle licence services moved to monServis in 2023 and require a SeyID account, which needs a valid National Identity Number](<https://www.sla.gov.sc/online-services/>).

**Used across services.** [The Agency for Social Protection's ASP Connect platform, launched internally on 15 October 2025, is reached through the SeyID portal, which hosts all government entities' systems](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The SeyID ID-Wallet holds a Digital Driving License issued with the Seychelles Licensing Authority, which requires a SeyID and a licence from the authority](<https://www.seyid.gov.sc/IDWallet>). [The digital licence began as a pilot on 15 December 2023, activated in the SeyID app's ID-Wallet](<https://seyid.gov.sc>).
