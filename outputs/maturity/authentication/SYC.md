# Seychelles

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Government e-services that require SeyID need a 60% validation level](<https://seyid.gov.sc>), reached by checking the holder's NIN card or passport by video against the Civil Status System. WISeKey, which built it, says SeyID [supports online KYC onboarding, one-time passwords, QR-code login and document signing](<https://www.wisekey.com/press/wisekey-awarded-project-by-government-of-seychelles-to-deliver-seyid-a-digital-identity-platform/>).

**Used across services.** The Agency for Social Protection's ASP Connect platform, launched internally on 15 October 2025, is [reached through the SeyID portal](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>), which hosts all government entities' systems. The Seychelles Licensing Authority's online services [require a SeyID account](<https://www.sla.gov.sc/online-services/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** WISeKey said in April 2024 that the SeyID app [gives passwordless access and holds the National ID and driving licence in an e-wallet](<https://www.wisekey.com/press/wisekey-awarded-project-by-government-of-seychelles-to-deliver-seyid-a-digital-identity-platform/>), generating a digital ID card with a QR code that third parties can check. No state source says the authorities accept that card, so it sets no stage here. The [ID-Wallet also holds a digital driving licence](<https://www.seyid.gov.sc/IDWallet>) issued with the Seychelles Licensing Authority.
