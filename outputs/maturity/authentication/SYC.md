# Seychelles

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Seychelles Nation reports the Agency for Social Protection's ASP Connect platform, launched internally on 15 October 2025, is accessible through the SeyID portal, which hosts all government entities' systems; the free text gives no detail of what SeyID checks](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>). [The SeyID page says government e-services that require SeyID need a 60% validation level, which is reached by checking the holder's NIN card or passport video against the Civil Status System](<https://seyid.gov.sc>). [At the SeyID launch on 1 November 2022 the platform was in its final phases of integration with eGovernment services and financial institutions, per the article citing Seychelles News Agency (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://idtechwire.com/seychelles-makes-digital-id-mandatory-for-work-permit-holders>).

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).
