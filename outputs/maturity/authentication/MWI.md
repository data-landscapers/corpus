# Malawi

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** The National Registration Bureau's 2025 slides show [the national ID checked online and offline through NRB middleware and a signed QR code](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-5-Malawi-final.pdf>). Since September 2026 [verification relies on real-time biometrics against the NRB database](<https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/>), the Homeland Security Minister said. The Nzika Digital ID Wallet [was launched in Lilongwe on 16 September 2026](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-nzika-digital-id-wallet-and-removes-expiry-date-national-id>). [More than 22,000 people had signed up by launch day](<https://malawi24.com/2026/11/09/malawis-nzika-digital-id-bounced-at-the-bank/>).

**Used across services.** The 2026 NRB fee regulations [charge for e-KYC checks of mobile money, SIM, bank and utility customers](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>). The NRB [lists ID linkages with the revenue authority, immigration, the electoral commission and other public bodies](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>), as well as telecoms and banks. Business registration [checks each applicant's ID number against the NRB database](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>), its registrar said in May 2026.

**Remote proof of identity.** The Nzika Wallet [lets citizens present verified credentials and reach services before their physical card arrives](<https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/>), the Homeland Security Minister said in September 2026. It [shows credentials, the national ID among them, by QR code from a phone](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/malawi-drops-expiry-dates-on-national-ids/>). By November 2026 [some banks refused it, and the bankers' association said it was piloted with one bank](<https://malawi24.com/2026/11/09/malawis-nzika-digital-id-bounced-at-the-bank/>).
