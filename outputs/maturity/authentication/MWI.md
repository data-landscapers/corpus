# Malawi

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [IANS reports that on 29 September 2026 India's High Commissioner met UNDP and UNCDF to review the e-KYC and Digital Identity Project in Malawi, funded through the India-UN Development Partnership Fund and implemented with the Reserve Bank of Malawi; the report states no verification service in use](<https://ianslive.in/india-un-agencies-review-e-kyc-and-digital-identity-project-in-malawi--20260929225238>). [The column says World Bank-supported programmes include plans involving digital identity authentication, electronic signatures and electronic know-your-customer services](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>). [The Nzika Digital ID Wallet was launched in Lilongwe on 16 September 2026 by the National Registration Bureau with partners including UNDP, with more than 22,000 reportedly enrolled by launch, per the Maravi Post column](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>).

**Used across services.** [The 2026 NRB fee regulations name e-KYC verification charges for mobile money subscribers, SIM card holders, bank account holders and utility services, per Malawi24](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>). [The NRB homepage lists National ID linkages with public bodies (Malawi Revenue Authority, Immigration, Electoral Commission, Financial Intelligence Authority, Department of Human Resource, Ministry of Agriculture) and private telecoms and banks](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>). [Business registration checks each applicant's national registration number against the NRB database, its registrar said in May 2026](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Homeland Security Minister said citizens can adopt a digital credential through the Nzika Digital ID Wallet as an option alongside the physical card](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/17/digital-gap-challenges-nzika-id-rollout/>). [The Nzika Wallet lets citizens present verified credentials digitally and can be used to access services before receiving a physical card, per the Homeland Security Minister](<https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/>).
