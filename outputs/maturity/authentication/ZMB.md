# Zambia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** The farm-input subsidy's e-voucher system [uses electronic know-your-customer checks to cut duplicate and ghost beneficiaries](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>), the Presidential Delivery Unit says. [It ran in all 116 districts, with redemption closing on 15 January 2026](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>). In September 2026 Altron Security [completed a key stage of the national PKI](<https://businessexplainer.co.za/deals/2026/09/10/zambia-taps-sa-company-to-build-national-digital-id/>), under a ZICTA contract won three years earlier. In May 2026 the Bank of Zambia said [a central eKYC platform was being built](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>), checking Home Affairs digital IDs by API and fingerprint.

**Used across services.** Co-Develop, a funder, says [an eKYC platform keyed on the national registration card number passed a proof of concept in April 2024](<https://www.codevelop.fund/zambia>). It says the platform was then extended to drought relief and health records. In January 2025 [the health record link was still being prepared](<https://idtechwire.com/zambia-advances-digital-id-system-with-mosip-training-for-healthcare-integration>). No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels.

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact is the [central eKYC platform the Bank of Zambia said was being built in May 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>).
