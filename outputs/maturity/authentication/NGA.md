# Nigeria

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [NIMC's director general says NINAuth, its authentication service, processes over 1.3 million identity verifications a day for banks, fintechs and government agencies](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>). [Banks, telecom operators, insurers and fintechs authenticate customers by fingerprint or face against NIN records through NIMC's real-time verification platform, Vanguard reported in July 2026](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/why-nigerias-national-database-s-still-fragmented-despite-nimcs-claims/>). [The NIMC Act 2026 makes NIMC the national root certification authority](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>).

**Used across services.** [About 63 government agencies, in examinations, student finance, consumer credit and health insurance among others, had integrated with NIMC's verification API by October 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [SIM registration, tax filing and banking run through the same NINAuth identity rail, alongside police, road safety and immigration services](<https://techmoonshot.com/2026/10/05/nimcs-ninauth-mandate-and-nigerias-180m-nin-push/>). [The Corporate Affairs Commission's company registration portal, piloted in July 2025, checks a director's NIN against NIMC in real time](<https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/national/cac-unveils-ai-powered-portal-for-faster-company-registrations/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [In March 2026 NIMC urged Nigerians to download the NINAuth app for digital verification](<https://www.arise.tv/nimc-intensifies-ward-level-nin-enrolment-nationwide/>). [NIMC says individuals can use NINAuth to verify their identity for SIM registration, immigration and passports, tax filing and financial transactions, with their consent required before data is shared](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2026/09/23/nimc-unveils-consent-based-nin-verification-to-authenticate-identity>). [TechCabal reported that NINAuth launched in 2025 and was still being refined](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).
