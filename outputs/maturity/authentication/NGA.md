# Nigeria

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [NIMC's director general says NINAuth processes over 1.3 million identity checks a day](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>). They are made for banks, fintechs and government agencies. [In July 2026 Vanguard reported that banks, telecom operators, insurers and fintechs check customers' fingerprints or faces against NIN records](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/why-nigerias-national-database-s-still-fragmented-despite-nimcs-claims/>). They use NIMC's real-time platform. [The NIMC Act 2026 makes NIMC the national root certification authority](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>). [In December 2024 the World Bank's ID4D project tendered an $83 million upgrade of the identity system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/nigeria-tenders-83m-digital-identity-system-upgrade-and-mosip-integration>), partly to enable authentication services.

**Used across services.** [About 63 government agencies had integrated with NIMC's verification API by October 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). They work in examinations, student finance, consumer credit and health insurance, among others. [SIM registration, tax filing and banking run through the same NINAuth identity rail](<https://techmoonshot.com/2026/10/05/nimcs-ninauth-mandate-and-nigerias-180m-nin-push/>), as do police, road safety and immigration services. [The Corporate Affairs Commission's registration portal checks a director's NIN against NIMC in real time](<https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/national/cac-unveils-ai-powered-portal-for-faster-company-registrations/>). It was piloted in July 2025.

**Remote proof of identity.** [In March 2026 NIMC urged Nigerians to download the NINAuth app for digital verification](<https://www.arise.tv/nimc-intensifies-ward-level-nin-enrolment-nationwide/>). [NIMC says individuals can use NINAuth to verify their identity for SIM registration, immigration and passports, tax filing and financial transactions, with their consent required before data is shared](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2026/09/23/nimc-unveils-consent-based-nin-verification-to-authenticate-identity>). [TechCabal reported that NINAuth launched in 2025 and was still being refined](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).
