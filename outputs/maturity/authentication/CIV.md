# Cote d'Ivoire

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [A feasibility study for a national authentication platform and digital identity wallet, with verifiable credentials in scope, was launched on 5 October 2026 by PARAE with AfDB support, running to end January 2027](<https://startupmedias.africa/articles/cote-ivoire-plateforme-nationale-authentification-identite-numerique>). [A feasibility study for a national digital authentication platform and digital identity access point was launched on 5 October 2026 under the AfDB-cofinanced PARAE, with results due by January 2027, per the government and PARAE coordinator](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). [BCPFS and ONECI signed a partnership convention on 2 April 2026 to interconnect the RSU with ONECI databases, identifying and verifying beneficiaries through the Registre National des Personnes Physiques](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). [Ecofin reports that a public key infrastructure was still being developed in 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0905-55432-cote-divoire-is-betting-big-on-ai-and-facing-bigger-cyber-risks>).

**Used across services.** [The DMOA says it is expanding electronic signature use across public administration, now reaching municipal officials in Marcory with support from the DGDDL and ANSSI](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/18/cote-divoire-expands-electronic-signature-paperless-administration/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/18/cote-divoire-expands-electronic-signature-paperless-administration/>).
