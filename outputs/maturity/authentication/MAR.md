# Morocco

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** The digital transition ministry says [more than 34 institutions use the digital identity service](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). About 100 others are preparing to join. An Interior Ministry deck of 2026 [counts over 70 million authentications by the population register](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>). The DGSN [announced a Mon e-ID app to hold a digital copy of the national ID card](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process>). It works on phones without NFC.

**Used across services.** [The ministries of Justice, Habitat and Youth, CNSS, DGI and TGR have adopted Mon e-ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks>), with banks and telecoms following. Six social programmes [check applicants against the population register's authentication service](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The ministry announced the launch of the unified public digital services project through the IDARATI X.0 app, based on a national digital wallet linked to the electronic national identity card](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). [Mon e-ID is already in use to let citizens carry the digital version of their national ID card, per DGSN officials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>).
