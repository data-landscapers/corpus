# Morocco

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Al3omk reports that the Ministry of Digital Transition says more than 34 institutions currently benefit from the digital identity service, with about 100 others preparing to join](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). [The DGSN announced the upcoming launch of a "Mon e-ID" mobile app to hold a digital copy of the national ID card, including on phones without NFC](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process>). [Biometric Update reports the DGSN digital ID project is extending the national ID card system to the digital world and a digital wallet super app is being worked on](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens>).

**Used across services.** [The ministry says more than 34 institutions currently benefit from the digital identity service, with about 100 more preparing to join](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). [Biometric Update reports that the ministries of Justice, Habitat and Youth, CNSS, DGI and TGR have adopted Mon e-ID, with banks and telecoms following](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The ministry announced the launch of the unified public digital services project through the IDARATI X.0 app, based on a national digital wallet linked to the electronic national identity card](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). [Mon e-ID is already in use to let citizens carry the digital version of their national ID card, per DGSN officials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>).
