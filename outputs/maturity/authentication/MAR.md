# Morocco

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The DGSN announced the upcoming launch of a "Mon e-ID" mobile app to hold a digital copy of the national ID card, including on phones without NFC](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process>). [Biometric Update reports the DGSN digital ID project is extending the national ID card system to the digital world and a digital wallet super app is being worked on](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens>). [The Mon e-ID mobile application is already in use, per DGSN officials at the May 2026 DGSN Open Days in Rabat](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>). [Biometric Update reports the Idarati X.0 national digital wallet project, for which eight partnership conventions were signed in February](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/morocco-approves-new-legal-framework-for-more-secure-biometric-passport>).

**Used across services.** [Biometric Update reports that the ministries of Justice, Habitat and Youth, CNSS, DGI and TGR have adopted Mon e-ID, with banks and telecoms following](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks>). [The DGSN says its verification platform has 17 framework agreements covering Bank Al-Maghrib, banking groups and healthcare providers, and authorised institutions in criminal records, prison administration and foreign trade](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [Mon e-ID is already in use to let citizens carry the digital version of their national ID card, per DGSN officials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>). [Biometric Update reports Morocco is working on a digital wallet super app, with work ongoing (underway, not in service)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens>).
