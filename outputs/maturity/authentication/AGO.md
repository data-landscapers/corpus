# Angola

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Justice Minister Marcy Lopes says the Interior Ministry, prison services and the migration service are integrated with the ID database to confirm citizens' identity and check the validity of the Bilhete de Identidade for passport issuance](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>). [Tech By Tech's CEO says AngolaID has been in operation since 2024 and offers API and SDK verification, authentication and liveness checks to institutions](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>). [Tech By Tech, a private company, says its AngolaID digital identity wallet and verification service is available on Google Play and the App Store in test mode](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>).

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).
