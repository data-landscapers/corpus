# Angola

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Justice Minister Marcy Lopes says the Interior Ministry, prison services and the migration service are integrated with the ID database to confirm citizens' identity and check the validity of the Bilhete de Identidade for passport issuance](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>). A presidential order of 13 August 2026 [authorised USD 28 million for a digital identity and electronic signature platform](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>) for up to 30 million identities. Tech By Tech, a private company, says its [AngolaID verification service has operated since 2024](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>) and its wallet is in test mode; the state is not shown to accept it, so it sets no stage here.

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).
