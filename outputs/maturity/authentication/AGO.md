# Angola

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** In October 2026 INFOSI, the state's information-society agency, [certified and approved the AngolaID platform for use nationwide](<https://pti.ao/infosi-certifica-e-aprova-utilizacao-da-plataforma-angolaid/>). AngolaID is Tech By Tech's identity verification and authentication service, offered as a free app. [The company says it has run since 2024](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>). Justice Minister Marcy Lopes says [the Interior Ministry, prisons and the migration service check the ID database](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>) when issuing passports. A presidential order of 13 August 2026 [authorised USD 28 million for a national digital identity and e-signature platform](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>), for up to 30 million identities.

**Used across services.** No source since October 2023 names several services or bodies using one authentication service. The nearest fact is the [ID database checks by the Interior Ministry, prisons and migration service](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>). INFOSI [approved AngolaID for use across the country](<https://pti.ao/infosi-certifica-e-aprova-utilizacao-da-plataforma-angolaid/>) in October 2026.

**Remote proof of identity.** After a face match and a proof-of-life check, [AngolaID users get a digital identity wallet](<https://pti.ao/infosi-certifica-e-aprova-utilizacao-da-plataforma-angolaid/>). The wallet authenticates them and carries a QR code others can verify. INFOSI [certified it for use nationwide](<https://pti.ao/infosi-certifica-e-aprova-utilizacao-da-plataforma-angolaid/>) in October 2026.
