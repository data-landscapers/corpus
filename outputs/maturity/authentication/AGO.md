# Angola

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Tech By Tech's CEO says AngolaID has been in operation since 2024 and offers API and SDK verification, authentication and liveness checks to institutions](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>). [Tech By Tech, a private company, says its AngolaID digital identity wallet and verification service is available on Google Play and the App Store in test mode](<https://pti.ao/tech-by-tech-disponibiliza-versao-de-testes-do-angolaid/>). [Presidential Despacho 300/26 of 13 August 2026 authorised USD 28 million and opened the public tender for a national digital identity and e-signature platform meant to let citizens and companies electronically verify the identity of their customers, per PTI](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>).
