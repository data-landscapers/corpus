# Botswana

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Business Tech Africa reports that the Bankers' Association of Botswana has launched a national eKYC project](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). [ The project lets banks verify customer identities against the national citizen database](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). [A P423 million public key infrastructure for digital identity and electronic signatures was funded](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/>). [ It was handed to the Directorate of Intelligence and Security](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/>). [The Permanent Secretary told the Public Accounts Committee it remains incomplete and not in operation, Weekend Post reported](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/>). [The speech reports the Health Information Exchange's Client Registry as ready for deployment](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). [ The registry identifies patients uniquely by Omang or passport number](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>).

**Used across services.** [In September 2015 a World Bank diagnostic said the national identity system was linked to the electoral system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/864651486101539760/pdf/Botswana-ID4D-DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf>). [ It was also linked to social benefits (SOBERS), government payroll and the transport system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/864651486101539760/pdf/Botswana-ID4D-DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 Business Tech Africa said banks could verify customer identities against the national citizen database](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>).
