# Madagascar

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The government told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025 that a digital signature regulation and a public key infrastructure are in place](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [The same answer says they are not yet used](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It names no service built on them and no date for their launch. No source has been found showing a digital way for people to prove who they are online.

**Used across services.** The nearest fact on file is [the same 2025 survey answer](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It names no public service that accepts a digital signature or a login. No source has been found counting the services that do.

**Remote proof of identity.** The nearest fact on file is again [the 2025 survey answer](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It says nothing about proving identity remotely. No source has been found on remote enrolment or remote identity checks.

***Also on file***. [IDMS](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says an authentication service is in use.
