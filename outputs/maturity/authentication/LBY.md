# Libya

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [In April 2026 the General Information Authority and the Central Bank of Libya reviewed a link for eKYC](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [It would connect the Level 3 biometric digital identity to banking systems](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [Technical trials with the Central Bank and a number of banks had begun, ahead of launch and integration](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [in April 2026 trials of the digital identity covered banks](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [in February 2025 an NESDB summary proposed a digital ID wallet for each citizen](<https://nesdb.ly/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf>). [The General Information Authority is the body authorised to issue and verify it, under NESDB supervision](<https://nesdb.ly/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf>).
