# Ethiopia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Biometric Update, citing Faydaverse, reports 150 integrated partners and over 208 million eKYC verifications on Fayda as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Addis Fortune reports banks retrieve KYC information and obtain biometric authentication from Fayda, and FaydaVerse has sent them a contract charging 10-20 Br per retrieval and 20-40 Br per authentication](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [Commercial Bank of Ethiopia customers can link their accounts to Fayda online at cbefayda.cbe.com.et with a Fayda number, and the restriction is lifted once identity is verified and linked, per Birr Metrics reporting the bank's notice](<https://birrmetrics.com/cbe-gives-customers-until-november-9-to-link-bank-accounts-with-fayda-id/>). [FaydaVerse says its authentication service has handled about 200 million operations and about two million verification requests a day, and has charged banks and other institutions for it since August 2026](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).

**Used across services.** [One hundred and fifty enterprises have integrated Fayda to carry out biometric KYC checks, per the article](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market>). [NIDP says Fayda authentication is used for harmonisation with banks, telcos (SIMs, mobile wallets) and the Ministry of Revenue](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market>).
