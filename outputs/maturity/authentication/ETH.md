# Ethiopia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [FaydaVerse, which runs the national digital ID, reports about 200 million one-time-password authentications](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>). [It handles about two million verification requests a day, and charges banks for them](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>). [In December 2025 the central bank told all banks to open new accounts through the VeriFayda 2 eKYC system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/ethiopia-banks-harmonize-customer-account-information-with-fayda-digital-id>). [Ethio Telecom's teleSign app, launched in March 2026, checks users online against Fayda](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8578538>). [INSA launched a national root certificate authority on 9 December 2025](<https://www.insa.gov.et/blog/Launch-Ceremony-Held-for-the-Digital-Certificate-Service-Ensuring-Fayda-Digital-ID-Security>). [In October 2026 FaydaVerse said it was adding verification by decentralised identifiers](<https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026102909031800756>).

**Used across services.** [By August 2026, 150 enterprises had integrated Fayda for biometric KYC checks](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market>). [The national ID programme reports Fayda authentication in use with banks, with telecoms for SIMs and mobile wallets, and with the Ministry of Revenue](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [MESOB, the one-stop government service platform, uses Fayda to authenticate citizens](<https://mosip.io/news/how-fayda-powers-ethiopia-mesob>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Cooperative Bank of Oromia was the first to open accounts through the Fayda Wallet](<https://idtechwire.com/ethiopia-launches-fayda-digital-id-system-to-cover-90-million-citizens-by-2027/>), without paper documents, in May 2025. [In April 2026 Dashen Bank let customers open accounts remotely with Fayda credentials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/dashen-bank-launches-ethiopias-first-digital-id-based-accounts>). [In August 2026 officials said the wallet was being linked to tax administration](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>). [In October 2026 FaydaVerse agreed with DSRV to turn its PhaidaPass wallet into a verifiable-credentials wallet](<https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026102909031800756>).
