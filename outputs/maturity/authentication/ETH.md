# Ethiopia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** Fayda, the national digital ID, carries a verification service charged to banks and other institutions: [FaydaVerse reports about 200 million authentication operations by one-time password and about two million verification requests a day](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>). [The National Bank of Ethiopia directed on 10 December 2025 that all banks open new accounts through the VeriFayda 2 eKYC system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/ethiopia-banks-harmonize-customer-account-information-with-fayda-digital-id>). [Ethio Telecom's teleSign app, launched in March 2026, checks users online against Fayda with video liveness detection](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8578538>). On 9 December 2025 INSA [launched a national root certificate authority](<https://www.insa.gov.et/blog/Launch-Ceremony-Held-for-the-Digital-Certificate-Service-Ensuring-Fayda-Digital-ID-Security>) issuing certificates that secure the Fayda ID.

**Used across services.** [By August 2026, 150 enterprises had integrated Fayda for biometric KYC checks](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market>). [The national ID programme reports Fayda authentication in use with banks, with telecoms for SIMs and mobile wallets, and with the Ministry of Revenue](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [MESOB, the one-stop government service platform, uses Fayda to authenticate citizens](<https://mosip.io/news/how-fayda-powers-ethiopia-mesob>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Cooperative Bank of Oromia was the first financial institution to integrate with the Fayda Wallet, opening accounts by biometric eKYC without physical documents, ID Tech reported in May 2025](<https://idtechwire.com/ethiopia-launches-fayda-digital-id-system-to-cover-90-million-citizens-by-2027/>). [In April 2026 Dashen Bank let customers open accounts remotely with a mobile number and Fayda credentials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/dashen-bank-launches-ethiopias-first-digital-id-based-accounts>). [Officials said in August 2026 that the wallet was being integrated with tax administration](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>).
