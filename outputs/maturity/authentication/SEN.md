# Senegal

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [IPRES opened its proof-of-life campaign for pensioners on 1 July 2026 on the Maa ngi nii platform](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>), which needs the ECOWAS national ID card. [The e-Sénégal app, launched on 24 March 2026, signs users in on the NIN of the biometric ID card](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>). Judicial records and nationality certificates were among its first procedures. [ARCOP says the APPEL e-procurement platform, opened on 14 October 2025, uses strong authentication and e-signature](<https://arcop.sn/2025/10/17/appel-le-clic-de-la-transparence-le-senegal-entre-dans-lere-numerique-de-la-commande-publique/>). [The company register's Seninfogreffe platform went online with strong authentication and e-signature in December 2023](<https://ods.sn/portfolio/projet-2/>). [In March 2026 the justice minister said the courts' e-justice platform carries the e-signatures of magistrates and clerks](<https://senego.com/digitalisation-de-la-justice-des-services-desormais-accessibles-en-ligne-pour-les-citoyens_1941148.html>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>).
