# Senegal

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [IPRES, the pension institution, opened its three-month periodic proof-of-life campaign on 1 July 2026 on the Maa ngi nii platform, where Senegalese pensioners must hold their ECOWAS national identity card to complete the proof of life](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>). [The e-Sénégal single-window app, launched 24 March 2026 and built by SENUM SA, gives access through authentication on the NIN carried on the biometric identity card, with judicial-record, nationality-certificate and similar procedures live at launch](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>). [ARCOP says the APPEL e-procurement platform, inaugurated on 14 October 2025, combines strong authentication, data encryption and electronic signature and timestamping](<https://arcop.sn/2025/10/17/appel-le-clic-de-la-transparence-le-senegal-entre-dans-lere-numerique-de-la-commande-publique/>). [ODS says the company register's Seninfogreffe platform, in December 2023, took formalities online with strong authentication and electronic signature](<https://ods.sn/portfolio/projet-2/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>).
