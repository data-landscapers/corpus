# Benin

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The World Bank feature says the WURI project supports the government's safety net programmes and the operationalisation of the Single Social Registry, without saying that the registry checks people against the ID](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [The World Bank feature says holders of the 'C'est Moi' ID card have used it to open bank accounts and a Mobile Money account and to take loans, and ANIP's director general says the ID lets people open an account and own a mobile phone; it gives no figure and does not describe the check itself](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [Since 15 October 2026 the public-sector pension fund's Preuve de Vie app checks retirees by fingerprint against their NPI, and its kiosks do so with the biometric national identity card](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>). [A 2025 paper lists a public key infrastructure among the digital infrastructure Benin has built since 2016](<https://council.science/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/AI-Paper-Case-Study-Benin_V1.pdf>). ANIP reported [27 million identity verification requests in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>), and the WURI coordinator [more than 15.7 million digital authentications through X-Road](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>).

**Used across services.** [The playbook names phone-number acquisition (telecoms) and public service platforms as conditioned on NPI authentication](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [The World Bank blog names microcredit (finance) and the education system among the programmes using the QR-code authentication app for fID certificates, and lists social protection, health, education, finance and SIM providers as services the fID enables](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/identification-system-in-benin-increasing-access-to-services>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
