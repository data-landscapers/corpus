# Benin

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** The identification agency ANIP [reported 27 million identity verification requests in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). Its 2025 presentation [put biometric authentications at 200,000 a day on average](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-3-Benin-final.pdf>) and listed the launch of a national PKI. The WURI coordinator counted [more than 15.7 million digital authentications through X-Road](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>). Since 15 October 2026 the public-sector pension fund's Preuve de Vie app [checks retirees by fingerprint against their NPI](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>). Holders of the C'est Moi card [have used it to open bank and mobile money accounts](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>), the World Bank says.

**Used across services.** The 2026 playbook says [getting a phone number and using public service platforms both depend on NPI authentication](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). The World Bank names [microcredit and the education system among users of the QR-code app that checks fID certificates](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/identification-system-in-benin-increasing-access-to-services>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact is the [pension fund's app that checks retirees by fingerprint remotely](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1710-59862-benin-launches-app-letting-public-sector-retirees-provide-proof-of-life-remotely>), which serves one procedure.
