# Namibia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Namibia launched its national public key infrastructure under DigiNam in August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>), said the Home Affairs minister, Lucia Iipumbu. [The government has saved about N$16 million on social grants through electronic identity checks](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), an OPM director said, partly by stopping payments to the dead. [Officials said the E-ID will let government systems share verified identity data](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). Tools and standards for businesses to use it were being built, for rollout in September 2026. [In November 2025 CRAN said the infrastructure was ready for the PKI rollout](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/11/namibia-moves-closer-to-secure-digital-identity-and-online-verification-system/>), with CRAN as Root Certification Authority.

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>).
