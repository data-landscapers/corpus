# Namibia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Namibia launched its national public key infrastructure under the DigiNam initiative in August 2026, per Home Affairs Minister Lucia Iipumbu](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>). [OPM's Director of Architecture said government has already saved about N$16 million on social grants through electronic identity verification, including preventing payments to deceased beneficiaries](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). [Officials said the E-ID will let different government systems share verified identity information and that government is developing authentication tools, standards and support so businesses including SMEs can use the E-ID infrastructure, with rollout set for September 2026](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>).
