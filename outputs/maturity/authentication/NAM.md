# Namibia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The government has saved about N$16 million on social grants through electronic identity checks](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), an OPM director said in July 2026, partly by stopping payments to the dead. [Officials said the E-ID will let government systems share verified identity data](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). Tools and standards for businesses to use it were being built, for rollout in September 2026. [The Home Affairs minister, Lucia Iipumbu, said Namibia launched its national public key infrastructure under DigiNam in August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>). [In November 2026 the ICT ministry told a financial-sector advisory body it was ready to launch a PKI for legally recognised e-signatures](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8326/namibia-highlights-digital-public-infrastructure-progress-in-financial-sector-transformation>). [In November 2025 CRAN said the infrastructure was ready for the PKI rollout](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/11/namibia-moves-closer-to-secure-digital-identity-and-online-verification-system/>), with CRAN as Root Certification Authority.

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest fact: [social grants are paid after electronic identity checks](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), one service.

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest fact is a plan. [In March 2026 the home affairs minister said an electronic ID would launch in 2026/27](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-expedites-electronic-ids-issuance/j5alr7QApo87pYQk>). It would come as an app or smart card.
