# Sao Tome and Principe

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [India's Ministry of External Affairs says Ooru Digital is implementing a MOSIP-based National Digital Identity system and a National Citizen Portal for São Tomé and Príncipe, enabling access to public services through National ID-based login, and that the portal was launched on 27 March 2026](<https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-SaoTome26.pdf>). [The World Bank mission reports a contracted System Integrator (Ooru) building a digital ID with working prototypes of a Verifiable Credentials Infrastructure and an electronic Signature service, with e-signature planned for May 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>). [The registry and notary directorate is running a pilot of a digital identity platform, for which it sought 1,000 volunteer testers](<https://rdpafrica.rtp.pt/noticias-africa/sao-tome-lanca-sistema-integrado-de-registo-civil/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-SaoTome26.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mea.gov.in/Portal/ForeignRelation/India-SaoTome26.pdf>).
