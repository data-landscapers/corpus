# Mauritania

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Tech Rek states the Khadamati app, which groups more than 15 digital public services, relies on Houwiyeti](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>) to verify users' national identity. [The WARDIP-Mauritania tender AP-01_DP-15_WARDIP-2026 seeks a national public key infrastructure (Lot 01)](<https://beta.mr/files/appls/177.pdf>). It also seeks a certificate management system with e-signature integrated with the ANRPTS PKI (Lot 02). The tender is World Bank financed. [The Houwiyeti digital ID and digital-government platform, launched in 2023, handles 70 percent of passport requests](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges>), per the ANRPTS director at ID4Africa 2025. [TECH5 says the Mauritanian government has released the Houwiyeti app](<https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/>), used to access services through biometric identification.

**Used across services.** [Tech Rek states Houwiyeti is the gateway public and private services use to verify citizens' identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). Private financial apps use the identity verification system at user registration, alongside the public Khadamati services. [Houwiyeti is used for passport requests and for more than 622,000 document extracts processed in the year](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [TECH5 says a digital ID is created from a passport or ID card and stored on the holder's phone](<https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/>). It is verified by selfie to log in to the e-government portal. In August 2026 an op-ed said Houwiyeti [issues signed digital birth, marriage, divorce and death documents that can be checked by QR code](<https://www.elmourageb.com/node/25951>).
