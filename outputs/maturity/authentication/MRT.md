# Mauritania

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Tech Rek states the Khadamati app, which groups more than 15 digital public services, relies on Houwiyeti to verify users' national identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [The WARDIP-Mauritania tender AP-01_DP-15_WARDIP-2026 seeks a national public key infrastructure (Lot 01) and a certificate management system with e-signature integrated with the ANRPTS PKI (Lot 02), World Bank financed](<https://beta.mr/files/appls/177.pdf>). [The Houwiyeti digital ID and digital-government platform, launched in 2023, handles 70 percent of passport requests, per the ANRPTS director at ID4Africa 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges>). [TECH5 says the Mauritanian government has released the Houwiyeti app, used to access services through biometric identification](<https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/>).

**Used across services.** [Tech Rek states Houwiyeti is the gateway public and private services use to verify citizens' identity, with private financial apps using the identity verification system at user registration alongside the public Khadamati services](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [Houwiyeti is used for passport requests and for more than 622,000 document extracts processed in the year](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [TECH5 says a digital ID is created from a passport or ID card, stored on the holder's phone and verified by selfie to log in to the eGovernment portal; the app has been released](<https://tech5.ai/mauritania-digital-id-undp-funding/>).
