# Niger

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [In July 2026 the WURI Niger project said it was developing a National Population Register to identify and authenticate people seeking services](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>). [A first enrolment phase in Niamey's five arrondissements was to start within weeks](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>). [Niger signed an MoU on 27 June 2025 with Libyan firm Al Itissal Al Jadeed for a new electronic national ID card enabling secure online identification](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/niger-moves-to-modernize-id-system-with-biometric-e-id-and-e-passport>). [ The card is aimed at online authentication, e-government, banking and social protection](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/niger-moves-to-modernize-id-system-with-biometric-e-id-and-e-passport>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the planned register was meant to serve both public and private services](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>).
