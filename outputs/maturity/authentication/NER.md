# Niger

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The WURI Niger project says it is developing a National Population Register meant to identify and authenticate anyone requesting public or private services that require prior identification, with a first enrolment phase in Niamey's five arrondissements to start in the coming weeks](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>). [Niger signed an MoU on 27 June 2025 with Libyan firm Al Itissal Al Jadeed for a new electronic national ID card enabling secure online identification, aimed at online authentication, e-government, banking and social protection](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/niger-moves-to-modernize-id-system-with-biometric-e-id-and-e-passport>).
