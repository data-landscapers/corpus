# Gabon

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The Gabon Digital programme, World Bank financed and run by the digital economy ministry, includes a national digital identity system meant to modernise identification and access to public services](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>). [Gabonreview reports that on 28 April 2026 the DGI, CNSS and CNAMGS launched work on the Declaration unique des salaires, in which the NIF for employers and the NIP for employees becomes compulsory, with operation targeted for July 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>). [GabonReview says the SNID will give every resident a digital identity with the same legal value as physical identity, with signatures, payments and validations authenticated](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identite-digitale-le-gabon-franchit-un-cap-vers-une-gouvernance-inclusive-et-securisee/>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>).
