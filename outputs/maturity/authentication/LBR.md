# Liberia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The National Identification Registry says an MoU with the Civil Service Agency (May 2024) allows national ID numbers to be used for payroll verification, helping identify ghost workers](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>). [The Civil Service Agency uses the NIR's e-verification portal under a May 2024 MOU, per NIR media relations head Sargbe](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>). [FrontPage Africa reports that the signed OeSD concession, not yet in force, would include secure identity verification among its components, per NIR director Andrew Peters](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/>). [The National Identification Registry says an MoU with the Liberia Telecommunications Authority (October 2024) makes the national ID a requirement for SIM registration](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>). A concession with OeSD International for digital identity credentials and authentication [awaited ratification by the legislature in September 2026](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>).

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>).
