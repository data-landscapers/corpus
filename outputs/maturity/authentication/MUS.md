# Mauritius

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Since 29 September 2026 about 884,000 licence holders can view a digital driving licence on the KOREK! app](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>), L'Express reports. MauPass handles identification and authentication. [The Prime Minister's Office says citizens must register through MauPass to use KOREK!](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20%20Friday%20%2013%20February%202026.pdf>), which is in service. [The 2024 regulations provide for the MauSign Certification Authority](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>). It issues certificates stored on the ID card, and one-time certificates for MauPass users to sign on the mobile ID. [The Registrar General plans to extend MAUPASS to give companies a secure identifier](<https://registrar.govmu.org/rgd/index.php/upcoming-projects/>) for its online systems.

**Used across services.** [The Unit says MauPass gives access to government services online](<https://mnis.govmu.org/>). Registered public and private bodies will be allowed to read card and mobile ID data.

**Remote proof of identity.** [Biometric Update says Mauritius launched a mobile digital ID system in 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>).
