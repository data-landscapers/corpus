# Mauritius

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [L'Express reports that since 29 September 2026 licence holders (about 884,000) can view their digital driving licence on the KOREK! app, with MauPass providing the identification and authentication and police systems the data](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>). [The Prime Minister's Office states that citizens must register through the MauPass authentication platform to use the KOREK! driving licence points app, which is in service](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20%20Friday%20%2013%20February%202026.pdf>). [The 2024 regulations provide for the MauSign Certification Authority system to issue digital certificates stored on the identity card, and one-time certificates for MauPass users to sign electronically on the mobile ID](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>).

**Used across services.** [The Unit says MauPass gives access to government services online, and registered public and private entities will be authorised to read card and mobile ID data](<https://mnis.govmu.org/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [Biometric Update says Mauritius launched a mobile digital ID system in 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>).
