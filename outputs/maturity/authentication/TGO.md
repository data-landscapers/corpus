# Togo

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Décret n° 2024-071/PR of 26 December 2024 requires telecom operators to record the ANID-issued NIU of every national and resident subscriber at SIM registration and to see the ANID identification card, giving existing operators 12 months from signature to comply; the text states no query of ANID's database](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Used across services.** No source held since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>).
