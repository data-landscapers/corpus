# Togo

## Authentication

***Noted, not assessed***. [Identification systématique et obligatoire des abonnés (SIM registration)](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>); [NIU / carte d'identification unique](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>); [e-ID Togo](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>); [MOSIP-based digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/why-decentralized-digital-identity-looks-different-in-every-country>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 names a digital authentication service. SIM registration records the ID number, and no query of ANID's database is held.
