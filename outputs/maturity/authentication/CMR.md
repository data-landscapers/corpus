# Cameroon

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Cameroon's Ministry of Public Contracts said users of the COLEPS e-procurement platform acquire an electronic certificate, used mainly to authenticate the individuals and legal persons who use the platform, and trained them on it from 10 October 2024](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>). [Secure electronic signature is operational on the AIGLE platform, per figures given at an e-government summit in October 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>).

**Used across services.** [The AIGLE secure electronic signature is live in 37 of the 40 administrations, per the same summit figures](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>). The COLEPS certificate serves public procurement.

**Remote proof of identity.** The nearest held fact is a card requirement: [the Finance Ministry made the national ID card mandatory, with birth certificates, for the second phase of its public-servant family-allowance census, launched in June 2026 and due to end on 15 August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist>).
