# Cameroon

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Cameroon's Finance Ministry made national ID cards a mandatory requirement, with birth certificates, of its public-servant family-allowance census, whose second phase launched in June 2026 and was due to end on 15 August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist>). [Cameroon's Ministry of Public Contracts said an Electronic Certificate, used mainly to authenticate the identity of individuals or legal persons using the COLEPS e-procurement platform, is acquired by users, who were being trained on it from 10 October 2024](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>). [Secure electronic signature is operational in 37 of 40 administrations on the AIGLE platform, per figures given at the summit](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>).

**Used across services.** [Secure electronic signature on AIGLE is live across 37 of 40 administrations, per the summit figures](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist>).
