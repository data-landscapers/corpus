# Cameroon

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Users of the COLEPS e-procurement platform acquire an electronic certificate to authenticate themselves](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>). [The procurement ministry trained them on it from 10 October 2024](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>). [Secure electronic signature is in operation on the AIGLE platform](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>), per figures given in October 2026. [A decree of February 2025 put the national ID production centre in charge of the certificates its public key infrastructure issues](<https://www.prc.cm/files/7f/4b/5b/2ce7f303c8e3bd44fa83c05acf9ddbf9.pdf>).

**Used across services.** [The AIGLE secure electronic signature is live in 37 of the 40 administrations, per the same summit figures](<https://leconomie.info/article/digitalisation-e-gov-africa-mise-sur-lia-pour-moderniser-les-services-publics>). The COLEPS certificate serves public procurement.

**Remote proof of identity.** The nearest fact found is a card requirement. [In June 2026 the finance ministry made the national ID card mandatory for its census of public-servant family allowances](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist>). [Birth certificates were required too](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-when-birth-certificates-represent-children-who-do-not-exist>).
