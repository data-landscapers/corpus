# Algeria

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** Algeria's digital identity rests on the biometric ID card. In May 2026 the Interior Ministry said [the card carries an electronic authentication certificate](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>), backed by a public key infrastructure. Its use to sign users in to the platforms for secure documents and civil status records had been launched. Electronic signature [was launched in January 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-adopts-electronic-signatures-and-certification>), with the regulator ARPCE named as the national certification authority. The public procurement portal [offers electronic signature of documents](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>) under the trust services law of February 2026.

**Used across services.** The Interior Ministry says the national digital identity was [launched at scale in 2025 and joined to the national services portal](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). In July 2026 the High Commission for Digitalisation said [the portal's first 21 services are reached through it](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lancement-du-portail-national-des-services-numeriques-la-e-gouvernance-en-marche/>). They come from the Interior, Labour and National Solidarity sectors. Citizens pre-register online with their national identification number, then confirm by biometrics at their commune. From August 2026 [the Sports Ministry's WADHIFATI platform accepts it](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>) without a separate account.

**Remote proof of identity.** In July 2026 the minister, Meriem Benmouloud, said [the portal's digital identity links each user's personal and biometric data](<https://lechodalgerie.dz/transformation-numerique-des-services-publics-le-portail-national-passera-de-21-a-350-services-dici-2028/>). Users reach the portal securely without signing in again each time. In April 2024 the Interior Ministry [planned to give citizens a digital identity reached from their phones](<https://www.algerie360.com/lancement-des-identites-numeriques-des-citoyens-avant-fin-2024/>) by the end of that year.
