# Algeria

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The High Commission for Digitalisation presented an SDK for secure biometric documents (passport, national ID card, driving licence) to the Ministry of Knowledge Economy, Startups and Micro-enterprises, as components for digital solutions that require identity verification](<https://news.prixalgerie.com/identite-numerique-algerie-gouvernance-donnees/>). [Echorouk reports CNEP-Banque, Housing Bank for Trade and Finance and Banque Nationale d'Algerie are verifying customers' identity and updating files, which can require a valid identity card; it does not say checks are made against the national ID register](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/توضيحات-أكبر-لـالبنوك-الجزائرية-حول>). [TSA reports that the newly launched public procurement portal offers electronic signature of documents under law 26-02 of 17 February 2026 on trust services](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>).

**Used across services.** [About a month before the launch, the National People's Assembly and the Governmental Electronic Certification Authority (AGCE) signed a framework agreement to integrate electronic certification and signature services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-adopts-electronic-signatures-and-certification>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-adopts-electronic-signatures-and-certification>).
