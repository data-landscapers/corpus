# Tunisia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [Tunisia's Ministry of Agriculture says it is integrating e-Houwiya into its electronic services, to verify the identity of users, with a deadline of 15 November 2026](<https://directinfo.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/18/tunisie-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-adopte-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya/>). [A circular of the Ministry of Agriculture, Water Resources and Fisheries requires the bodies under its tutelage to integrate E-Houwiya, the standard for authenticating users, into their platforms and online services by 15 November 2026, building on a pilot that put authorisations for the public hydraulic domain through E-Houwiya](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). [The Ministry of Communication Technologies launched the first version of the Khadamet mobile app giving citizens access to electronic administrative services through their digital identity](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/31/services-administratifs-numeriques-lancement-de-lapplication-mobile-khadamet/>).

**Used across services.** [Khadamet, accessed via digital identity, reaches CNAM, CNSS, tax, traffic and civil-status services, per the ministry](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/31/services-administratifs-numeriques-lancement-de-lapplication-mobile-khadamet/>). [The playbook lists E-Houwiya as integrated by services of many bodies, including the transport and communications ministry portals, education, CNAM, CNSS, the business register, Tunisie Telecom, five named banks and La Poste](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The playbook says the Mobile ID gives simple, secure access to digital services, and gives +450K Mobile IDs linked to a phone number and +35K linked to devices, with electronic signature of documents and contracts in the citizen portal](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
