# Tunisia

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** Since March 2024 the E-Houwiya mobile identity app [can be downloaded from app stores and signs with the citizen's own certificate](<https://radioexpressfm.com/ar/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d8%ae%d8%a8%d8%a7%d8%b1/%d9%85%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%b1%d8%a9-%d8%a8%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%b1%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d9%83%d9%86%d9%88%d9%84%d9%88%d8%ac%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%aa%d8%b5%d8%a7%d9%84-120-%d8%a3%d9%84%d9%81/>), the communication technologies ministry said. An agriculture ministry circular of September 2026 [requires its bodies to sign users in through E-Houwiya by 15 November](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). [A pilot already ran water-domain permits through it](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). In August 2026 the communication technologies ministry [launched the Khadamet app](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/31/services-administratifs-numeriques-lancement-de-lapplication-mobile-khadamet/>), which opens administrative services through the digital identity.

**Used across services.** [Khadamet, accessed via digital identity, reaches CNAM, CNSS, tax, traffic and civil-status services, per the ministry](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/31/services-administratifs-numeriques-lancement-de-lapplication-mobile-khadamet/>). [The playbook lists E-Houwiya as integrated by services of many bodies, including the transport and communications ministry portals, education, CNAM, CNSS, the business register, Tunisie Telecom, five named banks and La Poste](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Remote proof of identity.** The 2026 playbook counts [over 450,000 Mobile IDs linked to a phone number and 35,000 linked to devices](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). Holders use them to reach online services and to sign documents and contracts in the citizen portal.
