# Sudan

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The digital ministry and the Central Bank are linking the remaining banks one by one](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>), SUNA reports, each announcing its own go-live. [Omdurman National Bank was the first to switch on the link](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>). Its customers at home and abroad update their data on its digital channels, with SudaPass checking who they are. [The Cybersecurity Authority calls SUDAPASS the national digital identity platform](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/sudapass>). It verifies identity with several factors: biometrics, a PIN code and a QR code.

**Used across services.** [Digital identity is issued through certifiers licensed by the National Electronic Authentication Authority](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>). It opens government and commercial services. [The digital ministry platform Baldna lets users sign in with the SudaPass digital ID](<https://baldna.gov.sd/>), as its login page showed in November 2026.

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Sudanese Digital Identity smartphone app lets a user hold an account and sign electronic transactions](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>). Those transactions are binding and count as evidence in court.
