# Sudan

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [SUNA reports the Ministry of Digital Transformation, with the Central Bank of Sudan, is connecting the remaining banks one by one as each is technically ready, each to announce its own go-live](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>). [SUNA reports Omdurman National Bank became the first bank in Sudan to activate the link, so customers inside and outside Sudan update their data on its digital channels with SudaPass verifying their identity](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>). [The Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority's page describes SUDAPASS as the national digital identity platform providing secure identity verification with multi-factor authentication (biometrics, PIN code, QR code)](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/sudapass>).

**Used across services.** [Digital identity is issued through certifiers licensed by the National Electronic Authentication Authority and gives access to governmental and commercial services](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>).

**Remote proof of identity.** [The Sudanese Digital Identity application for smartphones lets a user hold an account and sign electronic transactions that are valid and binding with evidential power before courts](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>).
