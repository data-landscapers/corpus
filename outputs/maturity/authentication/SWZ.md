# Eswatini

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** In July 2024 APC said [commercial banks could check the civil registry to verify identity](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>). It added that the national ID card did not yet meet the criteria for digital identification. In March 2025 the central bank [said financial institutions verify customers against the civil registry](<https://www.bis.org/review/r250311k.htm>). In October 2025 it [launched an e-KYC registry](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0110-49197-eswatini-central-bank-launches-e-kyc-platform-to-support-financial-inclusion>). Banks, financial institutions and certain public administrations can use it to verify clients.

**Used across services.** No source found since October 2023 says authentication is used across services or channels. The e-KYC registry [is open to certain public administrations](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0110-49197-eswatini-central-bank-launches-e-kyc-platform-to-support-financial-inclusion>), but none is named using it.

**Remote proof of identity.** No source found since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact is [the central bank's e-KYC registry](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0110-49197-eswatini-central-bank-launches-e-kyc-platform-to-support-financial-inclusion>), which institutions use to check clients.
