# Ghana

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** The National Identification Authority runs an [Identity Verification System Platform](<https://nia.gov.gh/institutions-urged-to-use-nia-ivsp-for-authentic-ghana-card-verification/>). In July 2025 it said this is the only approved way to verify a Ghana Card in real time. Its [biometric verification](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>) matches fingerprints, iris and a live face against the NIA database. [In November 2026 the NIA said regulation L.I. 2523 requires identity to be checked biometrically against the national register](<https://nia.gov.gh/>). Institutions join by a service agreement, and a taskforce checks compliance.

**Used across services.** [About 284 institutions used the NIA verification platform by August 2026](<https://today.com.gh/muntaka-nia-clears-ghana-card-backlog-registers-418000-children/>), the interior minister said. [It ran more than 125 million biometric checks in 2025](<https://today.com.gh/muntaka-nia-clears-ghana-card-backlog-registers-418000-children/>). In February 2024 [there had been 191](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/191-financial-institutions-now-have-seamless-access-to-National-Identification-Authority-Data-1914728>). In September 2025 the NIA's chief said [GRA, NHIS, the Passport Office and banks all use its biometric checks](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>). Since January 2024 [the state payroll checks employees' Ghana Cards against the NIA database](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** On 7 October 2024 the Vice President [launched the CitizenApp (CADIS)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>). People scan their Ghana Card and enter a phone number to create a digital ID from NIA data. A biometric single sign-on then gives access to services from birth registration and passports to tax filing and health insurance. The NIA said in September 2026 that a [Ghana Card e-wallet would be activated in phases](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/ghana-card-digital-wallet-to-facilitate-bank-transfers-mobile-money-payments>).
