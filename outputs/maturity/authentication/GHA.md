# Ghana

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [NIA Deputy Executive Secretary Fred Bedzrah said Ghana Card biometric verification (fingerprints, iris, live facial recognition with liveness detection) matches a person present against the biometrics stored in the NIA database](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>). [The NIA Executive Secretary said the Ghana Card e-wallet, letting holders link bank accounts and load mobile money, will be activated in phases, with consultations including the Bank of Ghana at an advanced stage](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/ghana-card-digital-wallet-to-facilitate-bank-transfers-mobile-money-payments>). [The Communications Minister said on 7 September 2026 that Parliament had passed the LI for SIM registration and a biometric verification phase would be introduced before end-2026 so SIMs are linked to verified identities](<https://asaaseradio.com/the-resolve-of-this-ministry-is-unshaken-sam-george-on-biometric-sim-verification/>). [Regulations in force from 9 June 2026, L.I. 2523, replace visual inspection of the Ghana Card with biometric checks by NIA-approved methods](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/ghana-s-biometric-sim-plan-fixes-the-right-gap-but-the-data.html>).

**Used across services.** [The NIA statement says it supports banking, healthcare and taxation services, as well as telecoms, in verifying identity against the Ghana Card](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-card-non-negotiable-for-sim-registration-nia-boss-warns.html>). [The NIA chief says GRA, NHIS, the Passport Office and commercial banks all use NIA biometric verification, with the first three owing $457 million for it](<https://myrepubliconline.com/nia-threatens-service-cut-over-gra-nhia-and-passport-office-457m-debt/>).

**Remote proof of identity.** No source held since October 2023 says a mobile ID, ID wallet or signed credential is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-card-non-negotiable-for-sim-registration-nia-boss-warns.html>).
