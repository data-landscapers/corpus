# Zimbabwe

## Authentication

**Authentication service.** [The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe shut down ghost, fictitious and duplicate mobile money wallets](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/rbz-shuts-down-ghost-wallets/>). It did so after linking the Civil Registry's national database with mobile financial services subscriber records. [The Justice Minister said the Zimbabwe Electoral Commission was already using the Registrar-General's national population database](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>). The commission used it to verify voter information and clean up the voters' roll. [Police said logistics for implementing the Electronic Traffic Management System were being finalised](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/electronic-traffic-system-being-finalised/>), with its first phase awaiting commissioning. Police said the system is integrated with the Central Vehicle Registry and the Civil Registry to identify offenders electronically.
