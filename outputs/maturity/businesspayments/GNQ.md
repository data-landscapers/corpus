# Equatorial Guinea

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Law 11/2025 of 12 December 2025 brings in GE Compras, an electronic procurement information system for submitting and evaluating bids, according to a law firm's January 2026 article, which does not say it is live](<https://jonesandsacristan.es/blog-single.php?id=72>). [TACD CARTENA says the finance ministry mandated it, with AfDB support, to design an e-GP system and prepare a readiness report and roadmap](<https://www.tacdcartena.org/actualite/tacd-cartena-prepare-la-strategie-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-dun-systeme-electronique-de-passation-des-marches-en-guinee-equatoriale/>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 public-sector contracts law that brings in GE Compras for electronic bids and their evaluation](<https://jonesandsacristan.es/blog-single.php?id=72>).
