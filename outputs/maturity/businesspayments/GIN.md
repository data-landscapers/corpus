**E-procurement in service.** [The ARMP states that the national e-GP platform Telemo entered a new phase of use in September 2026, with companies registering on it and the system described by its Director General as in place and under way](<https://armpguinee.org/commande-publique-larmp-la-dgcmp-et-la-banque-mondiale-font-le-point-sur-levolution-telemo/>). [Guinea rolled out TELEMO, a national e-procurement platform from a Guinea-Rwanda South-South partnership, officially launched on 17 November 2025 on behalf of the Head of State, per Smart Africa](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>).
