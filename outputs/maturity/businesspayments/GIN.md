# Guinea

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Guinea rolled out TELEMO, a national e-procurement platform born of a Guinea-Rwanda South-South partnership, officially launched on 17 November 2025 on behalf of the Head of State](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>). [The procurement regulator ARMP says Télémo entered a new phase of use in September 2026, with companies registering on it, and its Director General describes the system as in place and under way](<https://armpguinee.org/commande-publique-larmp-la-dgcmp-et-la-banque-mondiale-font-le-point-sur-levolution-telemo/>).

**End to end.** [The ARMP's account of September 2026 describes the national e-GP platform as entering a new phase of use, with firms registering](<https://armpguinee.org/commande-publique-larmp-la-dgcmp-et-la-banque-mondiale-font-le-point-sur-levolution-telemo/>). [The platform is national in scope, built through a South-South partnership with Rwanda and launched in Conakry in November 2025 on behalf of the Head of State](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>).

**Use reported.** [Companies were registering on Télémo in its new phase of use from September 2026, according to the ARMP](<https://armpguinee.org/commande-publique-larmp-la-dgcmp-et-la-banque-mondiale-font-le-point-sur-levolution-telemo/>).
