# Kenya

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Star reports the e-GP system has been rolled out and covers tender advertisement, bid submission, evaluation and contract award](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-06-11-all-government-procurement-to-go-through-e-gp-from-july-1>). [Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi says the e-GP system, launched on 1 July 2025, is operational across ministries, departments, agencies, county governments and state corporations](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/treasury-to-fast-track-rollout-of-electronic-procurement-system/>). [Kenya's National Treasury Cabinet Secretary launched the e-GP system on 7 April 2025, with suppliers to submit bids online and manage contracts through a digital module](<https://www.treasury.go.ke/launch-electronic-government-procurement-system>).

**End to end.** [Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi said in the 2026/27 Budget Statement that all procurement by government entities will go through e-GP from 1 July 2026, a platform covering the whole cycle from tender advertisement to contract management](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-06-11-all-government-procurement-to-go-through-e-gp-from-july-1>). [Mbadi says there will be no exemptions in use of the e-GP system across ministries, departments, agencies, counties and state corporations](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/treasury-to-fast-track-rollout-of-electronic-procurement-system/>).
