# Kenya

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Star reported in June 2026 that the e-GP system has been rolled out and covers tender advertisement, bid submission, evaluation and contract award](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-06-11-all-government-procurement-to-go-through-e-gp-from-july-1>). [Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi said in August 2025 that e-GP, launched on 1 July 2025, is operational across ministries, agencies, counties and state corporations](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/treasury-to-fast-track-rollout-of-electronic-procurement-system/>). [K24 Digital reported on 21 October 2025 that 1,477 procuring entities were listed on e-GP and that no tender had yet been awarded through it](<https://k24.digital/411/new-procurement-system-yet-to-award-a-single-tender-4-months-after-launch>).

**End to end.** [Mbadi said in the 2026/27 Budget Statement that all procurement by government entities goes through e-GP from 1 July 2026, covering the cycle from tender advertisement to contract management](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-06-11-all-government-procurement-to-go-through-e-gp-from-july-1>). [He said there would be no exemptions](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/treasury-to-fast-track-rollout-of-electronic-procurement-system/>).

**Use reported.** [K24 Digital read off the e-GP website that 402 tenders had been uploaded by 21 October 2025](<https://k24.digital/411/new-procurement-system-yet-to-award-a-single-tender-4-months-after-launch>).
