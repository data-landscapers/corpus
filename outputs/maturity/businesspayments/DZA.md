# Algeria

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [In March 2026 TSA reported that Algeria has officially launched an electronic public procurement portal](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>). [The portal operates under law 23-12 of 5 August 2023](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>). [Firms download tender documents free and submit bids on it](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/lalgerie-lance-un-portail-electronique-dedie-au-marches-publics/>). [In December 2021 the finance ministry had launched a portal for exchanging procurement documents and information](<https://www.mf.gov.dz/index.php/fr/activites-2/808-lancement-officiel-du-portail-electronique-dedie-aux-marches-publics>). [Procurement was to be dematerialised progressively](<https://www.mf.gov.dz/index.php/fr/activites-2/808-lancement-officiel-du-portail-electronique-dedie-aux-marches-publics>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [in December 2021 the finance ministry said procurement would be dematerialised progressively](<https://www.mf.gov.dz/index.php/fr/activites-2/808-lancement-officiel-du-portail-electronique-dedie-aux-marches-publics>).
