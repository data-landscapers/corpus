# Uganda

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Ministry of Finance and PPDA rolled out eGP 2.0 to another 100 public entities on 20 July 2026, bringing the total using the platform to 136, and entities already migrated may not advertise tenders in newspapers (Daily Monitor, citing PPDA and the Acting Accountant General)](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/firms-to-lose-out-as-govt-procurement-goes-online-5547270>). [PPDA Executive Director Benson Turamye says Uganda already has an Electronic Government Procurement system, run end to end by 36 of more than 400 procurement entities](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-moves-to-fully-automate-procurement-to-cut-graft-delays-5427820>).

**End to end.** [The Acting Accountant General said under eGP 2.0 suppliers submit bids, receive notifications and sign contracts electronically, with registration mandatory for firms seeking government business and all 441 procuring entities to migrate by 2029](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/firms-to-lose-out-as-govt-procurement-goes-online-5547270>).

**Use reported.** [Turamye says the 36 entities running EGP have done more than 38,000 contracts on it, with a target of 113 entities by June 2026](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-moves-to-fully-automate-procurement-to-cut-graft-delays-5427820>).
