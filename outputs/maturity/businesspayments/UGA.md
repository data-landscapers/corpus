# Uganda

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Ministry of Finance and PPDA rolled out eGP 2.0 to another 100 public entities on 20 July 2026, bringing the total to 136](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/firms-to-lose-out-as-govt-procurement-goes-online-5547270>). [PPDA's Benson Turamye said in April 2026 that 36 of more than 400 procuring entities run e-GP end to end](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-moves-to-fully-automate-procurement-to-cut-graft-delays-5427820>). [The Ministry of Finance's procurement commissioner said in May 2025 that the home-grown system was piloted in 2020 and rolled out in 2021](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Dr-Isaac-Kyaligonza-MAPS-CASE-FOR-UGANDA-FOR-APPN.pdf>).

**End to end.** [The Acting Accountant General said under eGP 2.0 suppliers bid, receive notifications and sign contracts electronically, with all 441 procuring entities to migrate by 2029](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/firms-to-lose-out-as-govt-procurement-goes-online-5547270>). [PPDA says e-GP covers the cycle from planning to award, invoicing and payment](<https://www.ppda.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/PRESS-STATEMENT.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [Turamye says the 36 entities on e-GP have done more than 38,000 contracts on it](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/govt-moves-to-fully-automate-procurement-to-cut-graft-delays-5427820>).
