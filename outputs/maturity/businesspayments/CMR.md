# Cameroon

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The public-contracts minister told the National Assembly's finance committee that Coleps, launched in 2018, published 2,334 tender notices in 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>). [The second version of COLEPS, launched on 25 June 2025, handles tender notices and online bid submission](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/71737/fr.html/dematerialising-public-contracts-procedures-merits-of-e-procurement>). [A communique of 7 April 2025 requires certain contracting authorities to award all their contracts through Coleps](<https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/69967/fr.html/details_2>). [COLEPS, piloted from 2019, was in use in October 2024, with a nationwide training campaign](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>).

**End to end.** [The minister said Coleps now has modules for online contract signing and tracking of execution, and an order of December 2024 sets a calendar for exclusive electronic procurement](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>). [Cameroon Tribune says COLEPS runs the process from tender notice to monitoring of execution, with exclusive electronic award compulsory from the 2027 financial year, regional and local authorities included](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/71737/fr.html/dematerialising-public-contracts-procedures-merits-of-e-procurement>).

**Use reported.** [The minister reported 1,173 contracts awarded through Coleps in 2025 from 2,334 notices, against 365 awards from 458 notices in 2024](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>). [More than 8,143 contracts worth 1,745 billion FCFA had been programmed and published on Coleps for 2025 by January 2025](<https://cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/69967/fr.html/details_2>).
