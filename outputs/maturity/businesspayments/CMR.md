# Cameroon

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [EcoMatin reports the public-contracts minister told the National Assembly finance committee that Coleps, the Cameroon Online E-Procurement System launched in 2018, published 2,334 tender notices in 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>). [Minister Ibrahim Talba Malla said Cameroon's online e-procurement system COLEPS, piloted from 2019, is in use, with a nationwide stakeholder training campaign launched on 10 October 2024 and awards given to ministries for using it](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/4439/en/homesensitise-train-stakeholders-on-e-procurement-system-home>).

**End to end.** [The minister said Coleps now has modules for online contract signing and tracking of physical and financial execution, and an arrete of 24 December 2024 sets a calendar for exclusive electronic procurement, with generalisation to all public institutions planned for 2026](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>).

**Use reported.** [The minister reported 1,173 contracts awarded through Coleps in 2025 from 2,334 notices published, against 365 awards from 458 notices in 2024](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-lattribution-des-marches-publics-en-ligne-bondit-de-221-a-1-173-contrats-en-un-an>).
