# Madagascar

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [PREA says on 17 February 2026 that the e-Government Procurement strategy is validated and an implementation firm is about to be deployed, with iteration 1 of the e-GP going live in 2026](<https://prea.gov.mg/banque-mondiale/%f0%9d%97%b2-%f0%9d%97%9a%f0%9d%97%bc%f0%9d%98%83%f0%9d%97%b2%f0%9d%97%bf%f0%9d%97%bb%f0%9d%97%ba%f0%9d%97%b2%f0%9d%97%bb%f0%9d%98%81-%f0%9d%97%a3%f0%9d%97%bf%f0%9d%97%bc%f0%9d%97%b0%f0%9d%98%82/>). [ARMP and UNDP trained 1,039 central actors on the new e-GP platform and 460 on its e-Planning module, which is due to be functional for the 2023 budget year, as e-GP progressively replaces the SIGMP (2022, before the three-year window)](<http://www.armp.mg/site/A%20la%20une/eGP_Operationnel.php>). [UNDP formally handed the e-GP online procurement platform to Madagascar's procurement regulator ARMP; until then 70% of the process was manual and 30% ran on the SIGMP software (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/communiques/e-gp-ameliorer-le-processus-de-passation-et-dexecution-des-marches-publics>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies.
