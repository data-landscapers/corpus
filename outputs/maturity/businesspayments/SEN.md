# Senegal

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [ARCOP says it is continuing to deploy the APPEL e-procurement platform, with generalisation targeted from January 2027, a 1 December 2026 deadline to load 2027 procurement plans onto it, and training for more than 325 contracting authorities](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>). [ARCOP says the APPEL e-procurement platform, launched in pilot on 14 October 2025, has had 15 procedures launched, nine with electronic opening of bids, and publishes procurement plans, tenders and bid openings online](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>). [ARCOP says it inaugurated the APPEL electronic public procurement platform on 14 October 2025, where tender notices are published, tender files sent to the DCMP and bids submitted online](<https://arcop.sn/2025/10/17/appel-le-clic-de-la-transparence-le-senegal-entre-dans-lere-numerique-de-la-commande-publique/>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [ARCOP gives figures for APPEL: nearly 400 registered suppliers, over 80 procurement plans published for 2025 and since January 2026, and 15 procedures launched, nine with electronic opening of bids](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>).
