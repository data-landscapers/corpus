# Senegal

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [ARCOP says the APPEL platform has been in service since 14 October 2025, its first phase covering publication, submission and opening of bids](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/06/larcop-et-la-banque-mondiale-font-le-point-sur-lavancee-de-la-dematerialisation-la-plateforme-appel-au-coeur-du-suivi-des-reformes-conditionnant-les-financements-du-programme-pforr/>). [ARCOP's pilot report says APPEL runs tender publication, electronic bid submission and electronic bid opening](<https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Rapport_achevement_APPEL.pdf>). [ARCOP says it is extending APPEL, with generalisation targeted from January 2027 and training for more than 325 contracting authorities](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [generalisation of APPEL is targeted from January 2027](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>).

**Use reported.** [ARCOP gives figures for APPEL: nearly 400 registered suppliers, over 80 procurement plans published and 15 procedures launched, nine with electronic opening of bids](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>). [Its pilot report counts 15 electronic procedures, nine of them open tenders](<https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Rapport_achevement_APPEL.pdf>).
