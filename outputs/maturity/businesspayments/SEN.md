# Senegal

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** Senegal's public procurement regulator, ARCOP, is [still rolling out APPEL](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>), its electronic public procurement platform, and aims to make it general from January 2027. By 1 December 2026 contracting authorities are to load their 2027 procurement plans onto it, and ARCOP is training more than 325 of them, from central ministries and public bodies to state companies, agencies and local authorities. In October 2026 it showed APPEL's bid submission, electronic signature, encryption and time-stamping to digital-sector firms, so that they are ready to bid on it.

**End to end.** ARCOP's [stated aim](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>) is for most public purchasing to run through APPEL from January 2027, from preparing procedures through to conducting them.

**Use reported.** ARCOP [gives figures for its training](<https://arcop.sn/2026/10/16/generalisation-appel-et-acces-aux-opportunites-de-la-plateforme-larcop-accelere-la-preparation-des-entreprises/>), which covers more than 325 contracting authorities. It gives no count of tenders, value or share of purchasing on APPEL.
