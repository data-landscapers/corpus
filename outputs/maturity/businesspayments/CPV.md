# Cape Verde

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** The government said in March 2026 that [electronic public procurement has existed in Cabo Verde since 2020](<https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/>). Central State Administration procedures are carried out progressively through the official public purchasing portal. In the same note it said that [a new e-procurement platform was awarded to ACIN-iCloud Solutions for CVE 70,000,000 over four years](<https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/>). It is to extend the platform to local administration and to autonomous services and funds.

**End to end.** The portal serves the Central State Administration. [The new platform is to carry electronic procurement to local administration and to the autonomous services and funds](<https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/>), which the government gave as the reason for the award.

**Use reported.** The government note puts [the contract for the new platform at CVE 70,000,000, spread over four years](<https://www.governo.cv/nota-de-esclarecimento-publico-plataforma-eletronica-de-contratacao-publica/>). ACIN-iCloud Solutions is the supplier.
