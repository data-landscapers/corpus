# Burundi

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [PAFEN issued on 23 September 2026 a World Bank-financed request for proposals to design, build and deploy an end-to-end electronic public procurement (e-GP) system for Burundi, proposals due 13 November 2026](<https://armp.gov.bi/archives/12100>). [The finance minister said the e-GP e-procurement platform, set up by the Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy, is due to enter into force from 2027](<https://indundi.com/le-burundi-sachemine-vers-la-digitalisation-des-marches-publics-a-travers-la-plateforme-e-gp/>). [The public procurement regulator's director general says at least seven of fourteen planned activities for the e-GP system are done and first tests are expected early 2027](<https://ejoheza.bi/fr/at193/Burundi_:_Vers_la_digitalisation_des_march%C3%A9s_publics>).
