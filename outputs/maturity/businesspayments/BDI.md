# Burundi

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [PAFEN issued a World Bank-financed request for proposals on 23 September 2026 to design, build and deploy an end-to-end electronic public procurement (e-GP) system for Burundi](<https://armp.gov.bi/archives/12100>). Proposals were due 13 November 2026. [The finance minister said the e-GP e-procurement platform is due to enter into force from 2027](<https://indundi.com/le-burundi-sachemine-vers-la-digitalisation-des-marches-publics-a-travers-la-plateforme-e-gp/>). The Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy set it up. [The public procurement regulator's director general says at least seven of fourteen planned activities for the e-GP system are done](<https://ejoheza.bi/fr/at193/Burundi_:_Vers_la_digitalisation_des_march%C3%A9s_publics>). First tests are expected early 2027.
