# Morocco

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [An Al3omk op-ed says the Treasury suspended its procurement portal in late October 2026](<https://al3omk.com/1205193.html>). [Tender procedures were rescheduled and bid deadlines put back as a result](<https://al3omk.com/1205193.html>). [A ministerial order in force from September 2023 puts bid filing on the portal](<https://lematin.ma/express/2023/marches-publicslessentiel-larrete-relatif-dematerialisation/393581.html>). [A World Bank blog says the portal opened in 2007](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/fr/arabvoices/greening-moroccan-economy-through-e-procurement>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs from tender to award or payment. None says it is required of all public bodies. The nearest fact found: [the 2023 order puts bid filing, opening and evaluation on the portal](<https://lematin.ma/express/2023/marches-publicslessentiel-larrete-relatif-dematerialisation/393581.html>).

**Use reported.** No figure for e-procurement since October 2023 has been found. [The World Bank blog says 68% of bids were filed online in June 2022](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/fr/arabvoices/greening-moroccan-economy-through-e-procurement>). [The share was 15% in December 2019](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/fr/arabvoices/greening-moroccan-economy-through-e-procurement>).

***Also on file***. [Fatourati](<https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/le-cmi-lance-fatourati-agregateur-et-ouvre-sa-plateforme-aux-fintechs-et-editeurs-de>); [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement covers the whole cycle. None gives a figure for its use.
