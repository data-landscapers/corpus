# Eswatini

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Auditor General told the Public Accounts Committee that of E18.8 million appropriated for the Eswatini Electronic Government Procurement System only E1.1 million was released and E500 000 spent, while the Finance Principal Secretary said the project is now implemented](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/pac-questions-e41-5-million-under-expenditure-on-key-finance-ministry-projects/>). [The AfDB appraisal report lists lack of adoption of the e-GP system among Eswatini's main procurement constraints, says the 2020 e-GP strategy is finalised and Cabinet-approved but not adopted, and says the Government Tender Board and ESPPRA lack IT tools and operate manually (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/01/AFDB-P-SZ-KZ0-001.pdf>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/pac-questions-e41-5-million-under-expenditure-on-key-finance-ministry-projects/>).
