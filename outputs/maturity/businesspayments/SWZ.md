# Eswatini

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Auditor General told the Public Accounts Committee in June 2026 that of E18.8 million appropriated for the national e-GP system only E500,000 was spent, while the Finance Principal Secretary said the project is implemented](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/pac-questions-e41-5-million-under-expenditure-on-key-finance-ministry-projects/>). [The Central Bank of Eswatini's own e-procurement system, where suppliers register and bid online, went live in October 2025 for the Bank's purchasing](<https://www.insidebiz.co.sz/central-bank-unveils-new-e-procurement-system/>). [An AfDB appraisal of 2022 found the e-GP strategy approved but not adopted](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/01/AFDB-P-SZ-KZ0-001.pdf>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies.
