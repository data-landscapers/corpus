# Malawi

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The PPDA reports 562 tenders published on MANePS and 560 procurement notices by 5 October 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/89>). [It says the first rollout brought supplier registration, planning, requisition, an e-marketplace and e-tendering, with Phase II features in testing with 18 entities for November 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/84>). [Nation Online reports MANePS was formally launched with 174 procuring entities onboarded](<https://mwnation.com/govt-rolls-out-digital-procurement-system/>).

**End to end.** [A Treasury circular makes MANePS mandatory for all procuring entities from 1 April 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/58>), and [the Finance Minister told Parliament that all MDAs must procure through it](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/57>). [Nation Online says it manages the cycle from planning and tendering to award and payment](<https://mwnation.com/govt-rolls-out-digital-procurement-system/>).

**Use reported.** [The PPDA reports MANePS had 7,417 registered suppliers, 216 of 250 targeted 2026/27 procurement plans and 562 tenders by 5 October 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/89>).
