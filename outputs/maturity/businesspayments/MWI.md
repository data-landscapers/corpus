# Malawi

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The PPDA reports that 562 tenders have been published on MANePS to date and 560 procurement notices recorded, as of 5 October 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/89>).

**End to end.** [The Finance Minister told Parliament in the 2026/27 budget that all Government MDAs are required to procure goods and services through MANePS from 1 April 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/57>). [A Treasury circular directs all Procuring and Disposing Entities to use MANePS mandatorily from 1 April 2026 under Section 71 of the 2024 Act](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/58>).

**Use reported.** [The PPDA e-Government Procurement Manager says 232 PDEs uploaded their Annual Procurement Plans out of the 250 targeted in the first phase](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/58>). [The PPDA reports MANePS had 7,417 registered suppliers, 216 of 250 targeted 2026/27 procurement plans published (86 percent), 560 procurement notices and 562 tenders as of 5 October 2026](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/89>).
