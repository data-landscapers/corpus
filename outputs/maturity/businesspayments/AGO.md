# Angola

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The SNCP director says public contracting entities publish their procedures and annual plans on the Portal de Compras Públicas, per ANGOP](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/sncp-perspectiva-plataforma-para-facilitar-contratacao-publica/>). [Angola's national procurement service director said the Sistema Nacional da Contratação Pública Electrónica, launched in April 2018, has processed public purchases online, with over 400 state suppliers on the system (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>). [Presidential Decree 202/17 created the Sistema Nacional da Contratação Pública Electrónica and its regulation for a single state-run electronic platform, to be implemented in phases under a later ministerial decree, with paper and electronic procedures coexisting (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2017/decreto-presidencial-n-o-202-17-de-06-de-setembro/>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/sncp-perspectiva-plataforma-para-facilitar-contratacao-publica/>).

**Use reported.** [The Serviço Nacional da Contratação Pública director said SNCPE has run 1,959 tender procedures since 2018 and saved Kz 110 billion (25%) against estimated values (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>).
