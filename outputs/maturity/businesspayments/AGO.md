# Angola

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The SNCP director says public contracting entities publish their procedures and annual plans on the Portal de Compras Públicas, per ANGOP](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/sncp-perspectiva-plataforma-para-facilitar-contratacao-publica/>). [In June 2022 the national procurement service director said the Sistema Nacional da Contratação Pública Electrónica, launched in April 2018, processed public purchases online](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>). [Over 400 state suppliers were on it](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>). [In September 2017 Presidential Decree 202/17 created the system as a single state-run electronic platform](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2017/decreto-presidencial-n-o-202-17-de-06-de-setembro/>). [It was to be brought in by phases, with paper and electronic procedures side by side](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2017/decreto-presidencial-n-o-202-17-de-06-de-setembro/>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 contracting entities published their procedures on the Portal de Compras Públicas](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/sncp-perspectiva-plataforma-para-facilitar-contratacao-publica/>).

**Use reported.** [In June 2022 the director said SNCPE had run 1,959 tender procedures since 2018](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>). [It had saved Kz 110 billion, 25% against estimated values](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/estado-poupa-kz-110-mil-milhoes-em-compras-publicas-online/>).
