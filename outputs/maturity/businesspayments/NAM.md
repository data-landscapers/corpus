# Namibia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Namibia's Procurement Policy Unit says the government has developed an e-GP Portal through which all public entities are required to upload procurement plans, bid adverts, bidding documents, bid opening reports and award notices](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [World Bank procurement expert Devesh Mishra said Namibia's existing e-procurement portal is in use but only by a few public entities (less than 5%)](<https://www.namibian.com.na/world-bank-recommends-end-to-end-e-government-procurement-system/>). [The Ministry of Finance announced on 13 October 2022 that the E-GP portal is operational, publishing bid advertisements, documents, opening reports and notices of award online (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>).

**End to end.** [The Procurement Policy Unit says all public entities are required to upload their procurement documents to the e-GP Portal; an end-to-end platform is planned as phase two](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [The Ministry of Finance said all public entities are required by the E-GP guidelines to use the portal for open national and international bidding, sealed quotations and requests for proposals (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>).

**Use reported.** [Mishra gave a figure for use: the existing e-procurement portal is used by less than 5% of public entities](<https://www.namibian.com.na/world-bank-recommends-end-to-end-e-government-procurement-system/>).
