# Namibia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Namibia's Procurement Policy Unit says the government has developed an e-GP Portal](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [All public entities must upload plans, bid adverts, bidding documents, opening reports and award notices to it](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [World Bank expert Devesh Mishra said fewer than 5% of public entities use the portal](<https://www.namibian.com.na/world-bank-recommends-end-to-end-e-government-procurement-system/>). [On 13 October 2022 the Ministry of Finance announced the E-GP portal was operational](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>). [It published bid advertisements, documents, opening reports and notices of award](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>).

**End to end.** [The Procurement Policy Unit says all public entities must upload their procurement documents to the e-GP Portal](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [An end-to-end platform is planned as phase two](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/05/Mr.-Phineas-Nsundano-PRESENTATION-on-the-Impact-of-MAPS-AND-E-GP-Namibia.pdf>). [In October 2022 the E-GP guidelines required all public entities to use the portal](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>). [This covered open bidding, sealed quotations and requests for proposals](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-to-promote-accountability-transparency-through-e-procurement/>).

**Use reported.** [Mishra gave a figure for use: the existing e-procurement portal is used by less than 5% of public entities](<https://www.namibian.com.na/world-bank-recommends-end-to-end-e-government-procurement-system/>).
