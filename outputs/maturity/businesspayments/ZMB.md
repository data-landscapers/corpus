# Zambia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [ZPPA acting director-general Justin Matimuna said 14,732 tenders from 431 procuring entities were published on the E-GP system in the third quarter of 2024](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/procurement-authority-threatens-sanctions-against-officers-who-fail-to-implement-e-gp-system/>). [The Zambia Public Procurement Authority's circular says procurement is carried out on the e-GP system from 16 April 2024](<https://www.zppa.org.zm/documents/20182/196222/CIRCULAR+NO+13+OF+2024_GUIDANCE+ON+THE+MANDATORY+USAGE+OF+THE+ELECTRONIC+GOVERNMENT+PROCUREMENT+%28e-GP%29+SYSTEM/0e2aa3aa-a7a8-4185-9c8e-bbbb0862cb7f>).

**End to end.** [ZPPA said use of the E-GP system is mandated under Section 16 of the Public Procurement Act and directed that all public procurement be conducted exclusively on the platform](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/procurement-authority-threatens-sanctions-against-officers-who-fail-to-implement-e-gp-system/>). [The Zambia Public Procurement Authority directs all procuring entities to run all procurements of goods, works or services on e-GP, except listed methods](<https://www.zppa.org.zm/documents/20182/196222/CIRCULAR+NO+13+OF+2024_GUIDANCE+ON+THE+MANDATORY+USAGE+OF+THE+ELECTRONIC+GOVERNMENT+PROCUREMENT+%28e-GP%29+SYSTEM/0e2aa3aa-a7a8-4185-9c8e-bbbb0862cb7f>).

**Use reported.** [ZPPA said 14,732 tenders from 431 procuring entities were published on E-GP in the third quarter of 2024](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/procurement-authority-threatens-sanctions-against-officers-who-fail-to-implement-e-gp-system/>).
