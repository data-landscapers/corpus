# Mozambique

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Ministry of Economy page of 3 April 2025 says the government launched the e-GP electronic public procurement project, to be delivered in phases](<https://economia.gov.mz/governo-lanca-projecto-de-contratacao-publica-electronica-e-gp/>). [The government launched the project to set up an e-GP platform under the GEPRES project, said to cover planning to payment; Finance Minister Carla Louveira launched it](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-e-procurement-system/>). [UFSA says the Ministry of Economy and Finance launched the Concurso por Lances e-auction procurement mode on 12 March 2021, run on the e-SISTAFE platform, with a first live tender of nine bidders (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.ufsa.gov.mz/lancconcursolances.php>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://economia.gov.mz/governo-lanca-projecto-de-contratacao-publica-electronica-e-gp/>).
