# Mozambique

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [European Dynamics says it won the contract to supply and install Mozambique's national e-GP system](<https://www.eurodyn.com/the-government-of-mozambique-selects-epps-for-its-national-eprocurement-system/>), after an international tender. It includes three years of support after the pilot year. [The government launched the e-GP project in April 2025, to be delivered in phases](<https://economia.gov.mz/governo-lanca-projecto-de-contratacao-publica-electronica-e-gp/>). In September 2026 [O Economico tied the finance ministry's procurement reform to the e-CP system](<https://www.oeconomico.com/estado-centraliza-compras-publicas-e-coloca-nova-estrutura-sob-auditoria-externa-anual/>), not said to be in service. In December 2024 the CEDSIF chair [said most electronic procurement in e-SISTAFE's state asset module was operational](<https://www.jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/e-sistafe-passa-a-ser-ferramenta-de-auditoria/>).

**End to end.** The CEDSIF chair said in December 2024 that [e-SISTAFE's procurement is linked to its budget and payment modules](<https://www.jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/e-sistafe-passa-a-ser-ferramenta-de-auditoria/>). The [2025 e-GP project is said to cover planning to payment](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-e-procurement-system/>). No source found says e-procurement is required of all public bodies.

**Use reported.** No figure for e-procurement has been found. The nearest held fact: [the contract for the national e-GP system](<https://www.eurodyn.com/the-government-of-mozambique-selects-epps-for-its-national-eprocurement-system/>).
