# Benin

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Treasury (DGTCP) announced on 19 December 2025 that a new 'e-Procurement' platform will replace SIGMAP and the public procurement web portal](<https://www.tresorbenin.bj/actualite/seizieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-19-decembre-2025>). It comes under decree 2025-169 of 9 April 2025, which sets up the electronic public procurement information system. Its modules were presented, and implementation was at a progress-review stage. [ARMP says the e-procurement platform is still being acquired and implemented, under Decree 2025-169 of 9 April 2025](<https://armp.bj/le-benin-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-dans-la-dematerialisation-de-la-commande-publique/>). Training of public buyers ran from 6 October to 28 November 2025.

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest fact found is [a 2024 evaluation cited by ARMP](<https://armp.bj/le-benin-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-dans-la-dematerialisation-de-la-commande-publique/>). Of 757 calls published on SIGMaP in 2023, it found only 21 reached the automatic contract page.

**Use reported.** [A 2024 evaluation cited by ARMP looked at 757 calls published on SIGMaP in 2023](<https://armp.bj/le-benin-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-dans-la-dematerialisation-de-la-commande-publique/>). It found only 21 of them reached the automatic contract page.
