# Benin

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Treasury (DGTCP) announced on 19 December 2025 that a new 'e-Procurement' platform will replace SIGMAP and the public procurement web portal, under decree 2025-169 of 9 April 2025 setting up the electronic public procurement information system, with its modules presented and implementation at a progress-review stage](<https://www.tresorbenin.bj/actualite/seizieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-19-decembre-2025>). [ARMP says the e-procurement platform is still being acquired and implemented, under Decree 2025-169 of 9 April 2025, with training of public buyers running 6 October to 28 November 2025](<https://armp.bj/le-benin-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-dans-la-dematerialisation-de-la-commande-publique/>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [A 2024 evaluation cited by ARMP found that of 757 calls published in 2023 on SIGMaP only 21 reached the automatic contract page](<https://armp.bj/le-benin-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-dans-la-dematerialisation-de-la-commande-publique/>).
