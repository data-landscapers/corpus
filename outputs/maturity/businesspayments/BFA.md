# Burkina Faso

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The finance ministry's 2025 public-finance report, cited by OuagaNews, says the procurement-planning module was fully configured on the E-Burkindi platform and piloted with ministries and institutions in 2025](<https://ouaganews.net/burkina-759-des-marches-publics-par-procedures-exceptionnelles-en-2025/>). [A World Bank-backed project set out in 2022 to acquire, adapt and deploy an online e-GP solution for Burkina Faso, with the supplier tender for the solution due that year](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099730003022212412/pdf/Burkina0Faso000t000Procurement0Plan.pdf>).

**End to end.** The nearest held fact is the planning module, [piloted on E-Burkindi with ministries and institutions in 2025](<https://ouaganews.net/burkina-759-des-marches-publics-par-procedures-exceptionnelles-en-2025/>); nothing found says tenders are advertised, bids taken or contracts awarded on it.

**Use reported.** The nearest held fact is the 2025 pilot of [E-Burkindi's procurement-planning module](<https://ouaganews.net/burkina-759-des-marches-publics-par-procedures-exceptionnelles-en-2025/>), which gives no count of tenders, contracts or value passing through the platform.
