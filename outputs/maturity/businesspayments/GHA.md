# Ghana

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Public Procurement Authority says 3205 tenders had been published on GHANEPS by 23 April 2024](<https://ppa.gov.gh/clarification-of-claims-made-by-mr-bright-simons/>). [The Public Procurement Authority says GHANEPS, launched 30 April 2019, had been rolled out to 869 public entities in all 16 regions by September 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://ppa.gov.gh/mandatory-use-of-the-ghana-electronic-procurement-system-ghaneps-for-all-government-procurement/>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**End to end.** [The Public Procurement Authority says the grace period has ended and it is now mandatory for all public entities to use GHANEPS for their procurement activities](<https://ppa.gov.gh/mandatory-use-of-the-ghana-electronic-procurement-system-ghaneps-for-all-government-procurement/>).

**Use reported.** [The Public Procurement Authority says 5048 contracts had been awarded in GHANEPS from 3205 published tenders as of 23 April 2024, 64 per cent of the tenders being requests for quotations](<https://ppa.gov.gh/clarification-of-claims-made-by-mr-bright-simons/>).
