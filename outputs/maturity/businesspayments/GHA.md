# Ghana

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Public Procurement Authority's chief executive said in October 2025 that tenders are submitted online through GHANEPS, with manual submission to be phased out](<https://icnghana.com/finance-ministry-reaffirms-commitment-to-transparent-procurement-ghaneps-to-become-mandatory-from-2026/>). [The Authority says 3,205 tenders had been published on GHANEPS by 23 April 2024](<https://ppa.gov.gh/clarification-of-claims-made-by-mr-bright-simons/>). [It says GHANEPS, launched on 30 April 2019, had reached 869 public entities in all 16 regions by September 2023](<https://ppa.gov.gh/mandatory-use-of-the-ghana-electronic-procurement-system-ghaneps-for-all-government-procurement/>).

**End to end.** [The Authority said in November 2023 that the grace period had ended and all public entities must use GHANEPS for their procurement](<https://ppa.gov.gh/mandatory-use-of-the-ghana-electronic-procurement-system-ghaneps-for-all-government-procurement/>).

**Use reported.** [The Authority says 5,048 contracts had been awarded in GHANEPS from 3,205 published tenders by 23 April 2024](<https://ppa.gov.gh/clarification-of-claims-made-by-mr-bright-simons/>). [Its board chairman said in October 2025 that utilisation of GHANEPS is about 45%](<https://icnghana.com/finance-ministry-reaffirms-commitment-to-transparent-procurement-ghaneps-to-become-mandatory-from-2026/>).
