# Gabon

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [On 15 January 2026 the Ministry of Economy and Finance launched SIGFiP](<https://lejmp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/JMP_JanvFev210326.pdf>). [It handles public procurement among all revenue and spending operations](<https://lejmp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/JMP_JanvFev210326.pdf>). [Some procurement modules work and others are still being built](<https://lejmp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/JMP_JanvFev210326.pdf>). [On 27 December 2021 the Budget Minister launched the digital Journal des marchés publics at lejmp.ga](<http://news.alibreville.com/h/104877.html>). [It publishes tender notices and results online and offers tender documents for download](<http://news.alibreville.com/h/104877.html>).
