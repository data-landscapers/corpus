## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance launched SIGFiP on 15 January 2026, to handle public procurement among all revenue and spending operations; some procurement modules are functional and development of the others continues](<https://lejmp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/JMP_JanvFev210326.pdf>). [Gabon's Budget Minister launched on 27 December 2021 the digital Journal des marchés publics at lejmp.ga, which publishes tender notices and results online and offers tender document downloads (2021, before the three-year window)](<http://news.alibreville.com/h/104877.html>).
