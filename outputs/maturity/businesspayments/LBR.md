# Liberia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Liberian Observer reports the national e-GP system was launched in February 2025 after a six-institution pilot, and that businesses register, bid and manage contracts on it](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-s-e-gp-system-becomes-regional-model/article_578f3d8d-5076-43c2-8d63-594282d1476a.html>). [PPCC says 56 procurement entities were onboarded as Phase II of the rollout began in May 2026](<https://www.ppcc.gov.lr/media/press-release/liberia-advances-digital-procurement-ppcc-begins-phase-ii-e-gp-training>). [President Boakai launched e-GP on 6 February 2025 for online bidding](<https://gnnliberia.com/president-boakai-launches-mandatory-e-gp-system-to-boost-transparency-in-public-procurement/>).

**End to end.** [Tech Africa News reports e-GP lets government institutions plan, advertise, evaluate and award contracts digitally, in place of the manual process](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/liberia-registers-over-1200-vendors-on-electronic-government-procurement-platform/>). [President Boakai made its use mandatory for all government procuring entities at the launch](<https://gnnliberia.com/president-boakai-launches-mandatory-e-gp-system-to-boost-transparency-in-public-procurement/>). [The PPCC head says every stage from planning to contract award is tracked digitally](<https://ppcc.gov.lr/media/press-release/ppcc-and-usaid-spotlight-investment-reforms>).

**Use reported.** [PPCC executive director Bodger Scott Johnson said in September 2025 that contracts worth US$12,219,000 had been awarded through e-GP](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberian-companies-benefit-contracts-valued-over-us12-million/>). [He said in October 2025 that more than US$28 million in contracts had gone to Liberian-owned businesses through it](<https://ppcc.gov.lr/media/press-release/ppcc-and-usaid-spotlight-investment-reforms>). [PPCC reported 127 live processes on e-GP in August 2025](<https://www.ppcc.gov.lr/multimedia/photo-album/ppcc-set-onboard-50-macs-liberias-e-gp-platform>), and [132 procurement transactions in October 2025](<https://www.micat.gov.lr/office/news/newsroom/press-release/overview-public-procurement-and-concessions-commission-ppcc-0>).
