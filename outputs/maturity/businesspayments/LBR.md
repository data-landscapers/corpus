# Liberia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Liberia's President formally launched the national e-GP system in February 2025, after a pilot with six institutions; businesses register, bid and manage contracts on it, and Sierra Leone's procurement authority studied it in August 2026 as a working, deployed system](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-s-e-gp-system-becomes-regional-model/article_578f3d8d-5076-43c2-8d63-594282d1476a.html>).

**End to end.** [The Public Procurement and Concessions Commission's e-GP system lets government institutions plan, advertise, evaluate and award procurement contracts digitally, in place of the manual process](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/liberia-registers-over-1200-vendors-on-electronic-government-procurement-platform/>). [Phase II training, begun in May 2026, covers provider registration, annual procurement planning, e-notification, invitations and electronic bidding](<https://www.ppcc.gov.lr/media/press-release/liberia-advances-digital-procurement-ppcc-begins-phase-ii-e-gp-training>).

**Use reported.** [By May 2026, 56 government procurement entities had been onboarded onto the e-GP platform](<https://www.ppcc.gov.lr/media/press-release/liberia-advances-digital-procurement-ppcc-begins-phase-ii-e-gp-training>). [More than 1,200 vendors are registered on it, and it has raised more than US$400,000 in fees](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/liberia-registers-over-1200-vendors-on-electronic-government-procurement-platform/>).
