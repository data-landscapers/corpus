# Mauritius

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Procurement Policy Office says the e-PS expert recommended procuring a new enhanced e-PS, and preparatory action on replacing it with an electronic Government Procurement System (e-GP) is the next phase, with AfDB-approved terms of reference](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [The Procurement Policy Office says the web-based e-Procurement System (e-PS), launched in September 2015, covers the procurement process, with use made mandatory by government from July 2020, and 116 of 205 public bodies used it in 2023/24](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [Procurement Policy Office Directive No 47 says the 55 listed public bodies must undertake all their procurement through the e-Procurement System (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%20No%2047.pdf>). [In September 2025 government tender documents were published online on the Public Procurement Portal](<https://publicnotice.govmu.org/publicnotice/?p=28880>).

**End to end.** [Mauritian law mandates the e-PS for all open advertised bidding and requires all public bodies to use it, while finding use selective and bid evaluation and award often offline](<https://www.mapsinitiative.org/content/dam/maps-initiative/en/assessments/mauritius/maps-assessment-mauritius-e-proc-report.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [The Procurement Policy Office reports that in 2023/24 public bodies published 7,681 electronic invitations for bids, received 18,599 electronic bid submissions, and awarded 4,938 contracts above Rs 100,000 through the e-PS against 3,819 paper-based](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>).
