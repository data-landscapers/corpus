# Mauritius

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Procurement Policy Office says the web-based e-Procurement System (e-PS) was launched in September 2015](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [Government made its use mandatory from July 2020](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [In 2023/24, 116 of 205 public bodies used it](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [An expert recommended a new system, and replacing it with an e-GP system is the next phase](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [The AfDB approved the terms of reference](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). [In August 2020 Directive No 47 required 55 listed public bodies to do all their procurement through the e-PS](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%20No%2047.pdf>). [In September 2025 tender documents were published on the Public Procurement Portal](<https://publicnotice.govmu.org/publicnotice/?p=28880>).

**End to end.** [Mauritian law mandates the e-PS for all open advertised bidding and requires all public bodies to use it, while finding use selective and bid evaluation and award often offline](<https://www.mapsinitiative.org/content/dam/maps-initiative/en/assessments/mauritius/maps-assessment-mauritius-e-proc-report.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [The Procurement Policy Office reports that in 2023/24 public bodies published 7,681 electronic invitations for bids, received 18,599 electronic bid submissions, and awarded 4,938 contracts above Rs 100,000 through the e-PS against 3,819 paper-based](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Annual%20Reports/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>).
