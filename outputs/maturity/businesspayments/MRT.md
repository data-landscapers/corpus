# Mauritania

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [ARMP and the digital ministry launched the national public procurement portal on 3 December 2024, carrying procurement plans, tender announcements and results](<https://emergence.mr/2024/12/05/lancement-du-portail-national-des-marches-publics-en-mauritanie/>). [The ministry reports SIGMAP used by 200 contracting authorities and 91 procurement commissions with over 600 users, and 274 annual procurement plans and 179 annual purchasing plans published on it](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>). [The digital transformation ministry's communique, reported 22 July 2026, says SIGMAP's programming phase is operational since early 2026 while tender-file management and electronic bid submission are in testing](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://emergence.mr/2024/12/05/lancement-du-portail-national-des-marches-publics-en-mauritanie/>).
