# Mauritania

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The digital transformation ministry's communique, reported 22 July 2026, says SIGMAP's programming phase is operational since early 2026 while tender-file management and electronic bid submission are in testing](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>). [The digital ministry's steering committee reviewed progress on SIGMAP, the national public procurement system, which it says will start taking ministries' procurement plans on 15 December 2025, with full launch planned for early 2026](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/seance-du-comite-de-pilotage-dediee-au-projet-sigmap-lancement-prevu-en-2026/>). [ARMP and the digital ministry launched the national public procurement portal on 3 December 2024, carrying procurement plans, tender announcements and results](<https://emergence.mr/2024/12/05/lancement-du-portail-national-des-marches-publics-en-mauritanie/>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [The ministry reports SIGMAP used by 200 contracting authorities and 91 procurement commissions with over 600 users, and 274 annual procurement plans and 179 annual purchasing plans published on it](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>).
