# Mauritania

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [ARMP and the digital ministry launched the national public procurement portal on 3 December 2024, carrying procurement plans, tender announcements and results](<https://emergence.mr/2024/12/05/lancement-du-portail-national-des-marches-publics-en-mauritanie/>). [The digital ministry said in July 2026 that SIGMAP's programming phase has run since early 2026, while tender-file management and electronic bid submission are in testing](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>). [The World Bank approved US$50 million in June 2025 that will support deployment of the e-GP system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/03/world-bank-supports-mauritania-with-50-million-to-enhance-public-spending-efficiency>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [electronic bid submission on SIGMAP is in testing](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>).

**Use reported.** No figure for tenders or value has been found. [The ministry counts 200 contracting authorities, over 600 users and 274 procurement plans on SIGMAP](<https://trustmag.net/article/hDaLLNiaP>).
