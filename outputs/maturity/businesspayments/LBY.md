# Libya

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The General Information Authority announced that Attaat, the Tripoli government's tendering and procurement website, is operational, with entities publishing tenders through it](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/tripoli-libyan-governments-official-tendering-and-procurement-website-attaat-is-operational>). [It says 189 government entities published tenders on Attaat, the official platform for government tenders and purchases, in July 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/08/03/6665/>). [The Authority's head announced in February 2024 that the updated platform had started operating](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/2024/03/11/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%a6%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%86-%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1/>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [189 government entities published tenders on Attaat, the official platform for government tenders and purchases, in July 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/08/03/6665/>).

**Use reported.** [The Authority reports 626 tenders announced on Attaat during July 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/08/03/6665/>), [published by 189 government entities, with 840 entities registered on the platform](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/tripoli-libyan-governments-official-tendering-and-procurement-website-attaat-is-operational>).
