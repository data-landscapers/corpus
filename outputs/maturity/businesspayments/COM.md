# Comoros

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [In September 2025 a consultant report said Comoros is pursuing a reform to set up an e-Government Procurement system](<https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf>). [It specifies an online tendering platform of two modules, with hosting](<https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf>). [A draft e-GP implementing decree was under review](<https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf>).

**End to end.** No source found since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the e-GP implementing decree was still a draft under review](<https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for e-procurement. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 a consultant report described the specification for an online tendering platform](<https://armpcomores.km/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Rapport_final_Diagnostic_institutionnel_MP_Comores_Clean1.pdf>).
