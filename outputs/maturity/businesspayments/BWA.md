# Botswana

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The PPRA said in December 2025 that it is working with the Ministry of Finance to develop a national e-procurement system for real-time tracking of tenders](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89320>). [Its chief executive told the National Public Procurement Pitso in March 2026 that Botswana has no fully integrated e-procurement platform and runs manual processes, with a national system promised by 2027](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/botswanas-procurement-system-at-crossroads-amid-reform-efforts/>). [He said in December 2025 that procurement records are kept manually](<https://thevoicebw.com/the-citizen-cut-2/>), and [the Finance Minister told Parliament in October 2025 that the manual system would give way to e-procurement](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88940>). [The PPADB launched an Integrated Procurement Management System with online submission in July 2016](<https://www.sundaystandard.info/ppadb-launches-a-web-based-bidding-system/>).

**End to end.** Nothing found says e-procurement runs end to end; the PPRA said in March 2026 that [procurement runs on manual processes](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/botswanas-procurement-system-at-crossroads-amid-reform-efforts/>).

**Use reported.** No figure for e-procurement has been found; the nearest held fact is the [December 2025 statement that records are kept manually](<https://thevoicebw.com/the-citizen-cut-2/>).

***Also on file***. [national payment switch](<https://www.sundaystandard.info/finance-ministry-bets-p8-8bn-on-digital-discipline/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says an e-procurement system is in service; the PPRA said in 2025 and 2026 that procurement runs manually.
