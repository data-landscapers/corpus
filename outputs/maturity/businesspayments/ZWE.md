# Zimbabwe

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Herald reports the eGP system, launched in October 2023, became operational on 1 January 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/egp-system-upgraded/>). [It lets all public entities and bidders run procurement electronically](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/egp-system-upgraded/>). [PRAZ said by March 2024 that it automates bid submission, evaluation and communication](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/10-000-register-on-e-government-procurement-platform/>). [Over 9,900 suppliers and 380 procuring entities had registered by 31 March 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/10-000-register-on-e-government-procurement-platform/>). In November 2026 PRAZ's ICT director said [procuring entities prepare plans and bids are evaluated on e-GP](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/praz-moves-to-integrate-ai-into-e-procurement-system/>). PRAZ's board chair and chief executive said [public procurement now runs through eGP](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/praz-pushes-for-unified-technology-driven-procurement-oversight-system/>). They urged all procuring entities to use it fully.

**End to end.** [PRAZ upgraded eGP to Phase 1.3 in early 2025, for all public entities](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/egp-system-upgraded/>). The upgrade added e-evaluation, e-award, e-contract management, framework agreements and management reports.
