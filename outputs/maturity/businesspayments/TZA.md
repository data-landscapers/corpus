# Tanzania

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Public Procurement Regulatory Authority says NeST, introduced in 2023, replaced paper tendering and takes tenders online](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/procurement-through-nest-saves-over-35bn-cuts-carbon-emissions>). In February 2026 its director general, Dennis Simba, [told Parliament's Public Accounts Committee that NeST has six modules](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/nest-to-tackle-payment-delays-for-tenderers-ppra-tells-parliamentary-committee>). They run from e-registration and e-tendering to e-contract management and e-payment. [A directive required all procurement on the Mainland to go through NeST from 1 October 2023](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/jail-time-and-or-fine-await-public-officials-who-will-procure-outside-nest>).

**End to end.** [Simba said procuring entities can run the whole process within NeST up to payment](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/nest-to-tackle-payment-delays-for-tenderers-ppra-tells-parliamentary-committee>). [The Public Procurement Act of 2023, in force from 17 June 2024, mandates NeST for all procurement](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/new-public-procurement-law-to-take-effect-on-june-17-2024>). The finance ministry's budget papers for 2026/27 say [its MUSE accounting system is linked to NeST](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-push-toward-paperless-public-services-hits-heights-in-six-document-heavy-functions/>), so procurement payments feed into public accounts.

**Use reported.** By August 2026 [NeST had handled 397,395 procurement proceedings worth 40.4 trillion shillings](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-push-toward-paperless-public-services-hits-heights-in-six-document-heavy-functions/>), the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority said. [PPRA says more than 219,000 tenders went through NeST over financial years 2023/24 and 2024/25](<https://www.ppra.go.tz/news/procurement-through-nest-saves-over-35bn-cuts-carbon-emissions>). In May 2025 [Simba said NeST had handled 130,032 tenders worth Sh14.9 trillion](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/e-procurement-saves-tanzania-4-9-million-in-tender-document-costs-5038796>).
