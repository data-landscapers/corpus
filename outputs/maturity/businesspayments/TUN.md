# Tunisia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Government representatives told parliament's administration committee in November 2023 that public procurement runs online through the TUNEPS system](<https://arp.tn/blog/arp-blog-1/post/8172>). [The national public procurement observatory reports 1,536 public buyers and 17,835 suppliers registered on TUNEPS in 2024](<https://appn-racop.org/upidrots/2025/03/Tunisie-PPT-RACOP_26-Fevrier-2025_Professionnalisation.pdf>). [The OECD described TUNEPS in 2019 as running e-bidding, e-contracting, an e-catalogue and an e-shopping mall (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://institutdesfinances.gov.lb/sites/default/files/2024-06/improving-e-procurement-environment-tunisia-en.pdf>).

**End to end.** [The representatives said the online system was made general and compulsory for all ministries and public institutions in 2019](<https://arp.tn/blog/arp-blog-1/post/8172>). [The head of the e-administration unit said in 2021 that every public purchase must pass through TUNEPS (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2021/06/11/469188/a-partir-de-la-semaine-prochaine-la-signature-et-la-validation-des-decrets-gouvernementaux-seront-dematerialisees/>).

**Use reported.** [They told parliament that more than 27,700 bids had been registered online, with 15,500 suppliers and 1,441 public buyers](<https://arp.tn/blog/arp-blog-1/post/8172>). [The OECD counted 3,350 procurement procedures on TUNEPS over 2014-2019 (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://institutdesfinances.gov.lb/sites/default/files/2024-06/improving-e-procurement-environment-tunisia-en.pdf>).
