# Cote d'Ivoire

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The finance ministry told the Council of Ministers at the end of 2023 that SIGOMAP, its application for dematerialised contract award, is now the sole platform for managing public procurement, after a pilot with seven bodies and a wider extension](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>). [The Budget Ministry opened online procurement on SIGOMAP on 25 July 2023, where firms consult notices, buy tender files and submit bids online](<https://budget.gouv.ci/357-about-actualites.html>).

**End to end.** [The same communication says SIGOMAP already handles contract numbering and approval steps, and that its use extends to all contracting authorities of the ministries from 2024](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [The ministry says dematerialisation cut the time to process tender documents from 9.9 to 6.5 days in 2023](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>), a measure of speed rather than of tenders or value.
