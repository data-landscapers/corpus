# Cote d'Ivoire

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The finance ministry told the Council of Ministers at the end of 2023 that SIGOMAP is now the sole platform for managing public procurement](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>). SIGOMAP is the ministry's application for dematerialised contract award. It became the sole platform after a pilot with seven bodies and a wider extension. [The Budget Ministry opened online procurement on SIGOMAP on 25 July 2023](<https://budget.gouv.ci/357-about-actualites.html>). Firms consult notices, buy tender files and submit bids online there.

**End to end.** [The same communication says SIGOMAP already handles contract numbering and approval steps](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>). It says use extends to all contracting authorities of the ministries from 2024.

**Use reported.** [The ministry says dematerialisation cut the time to process tender documents from 9.9 to 6.5 days in 2023](<https://www.marchespublics.ci/uploads/doc/Projet_CCM_March%C3%A9s_2023.pdf>). This measures speed, not the number of tenders or their value.
