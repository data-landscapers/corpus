# Egypt

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** Egypt is building a national electronic procurement platform. [On 4 August 2025 Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said the platform was close to completion](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2025/08/04/electronic-public-procurement-platform-nears-completion-kouchouk/>). He said it was not yet in operation. No later source found says it has gone live, or that tenders are published or bid on it.

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs from tender to award or payment, or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact is [the minister's statement of August 2025 that the national platform was nearly complete](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2025/08/04/electronic-public-procurement-platform-nears-completion-kouchouk/>).

**Use reported.** No figure for e-procurement has been found: no count of tenders, no value and no share of purchasing. The nearest held fact is [the same statement](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2025/08/04/electronic-public-procurement-platform-nears-completion-kouchouk/>), which gives no figures.

***Not found***. No source found says an e-procurement system is in service. None gives a figure for its use.
