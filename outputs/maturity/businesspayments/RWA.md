# Rwanda

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Open Contracting Partnership says Rwanda's Umucyo e-procurement system is used by all national and local government agencies](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [The RPPA survey says Umucyo was rolled out to all government institutions in 2017 and is used by about 200 procuring entities and over 5,000 bidders (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>). [The RPPA Director General said the Umucyo e-procurement system was launched on 27 June 2016 in 8 pilot government institutions, with all government institutions to use it from April 2017 (2016, before the three-year window)](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/e-procurement>).

**End to end.** [Open Contracting Partnership says Umucyo is used by all national and local government agencies for almost all procurement procedures](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [The RPPA survey says Umucyo covers planning, bidding, awarding, contracting, purchasing and contract management, and law 62/2018 requires all public procurement to use it unless RPPA authorises otherwise (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>).

**Use reported.** [The RPPA survey reports about 200 procuring entities and over 5,000 bidders on Umucyo and an overall user satisfaction of 79.5% (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>).
