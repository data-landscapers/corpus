# Rwanda

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Open Contracting Partnership says Rwanda's Umucyo e-procurement system is used by all national and local government agencies](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [In November 2021 an RPPA survey said Umucyo had been rolled out to all government institutions in 2017](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>). [On 27 June 2016 the RPPA Director General launched Umucyo in 8 pilot institutions, with all to use it from April 2017](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/e-procurement>).

**End to end.** [Open Contracting Partnership says Umucyo is used by all national and local government agencies for almost all procurement procedures](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [In November 2021 the RPPA survey said Umucyo covered planning, bidding, awarding, contracting, purchasing and contract management](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>). [Law 62/2018 requires all public procurement to use it unless RPPA authorises otherwise](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>).

**Use reported.** [In November 2021 the RPPA survey counted about 200 procuring entities and over 5,000 bidders on Umucyo](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>). [User satisfaction was 79.5%](<https://www.rppa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=35424&t=f&token=eec54479804ff9695b9d7e0cbaea82472358c227>).
