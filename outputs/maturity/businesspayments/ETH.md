# Ethiopia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Federal Public Procurement and Property Authority said the electronic procurement system was implemented across 169 federal budgetary institutions and 93 branch offices in EFY 2017](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7113934>). [The US International Trade Administration reports Ethiopia's e-GP system, piloted since 2021, was expanded in 2023 to 74 federal agencies, and registered suppliers can submit bids online (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/ethiopia-e-procurement-platform-provides-easy-access-public-tenders>). [Addis Fortune reports the Public Procurement & Property Authority piloted electronic government procurement with nine federal agencies and made it compulsory for 72 agencies in August 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-banks-on-automated-public-procurement-process>).

**End to end.** [Addis Fortune reports the Authority is automating the end-to-end process, with procurement plans, tendering, bid evaluations and award notices communicated through the portal, compulsory for 72 of 174 federal agencies (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-banks-on-automated-public-procurement-process>).

**Use reported.** [The Authority said 308.75 billion Birr of procurement went through the electronic system in EFY 2017 (300 billion by bidding, 8.75 billion by price quotation), after 65 percent of procurement in 2016](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7113934>). [The US International Trade Administration, citing the PPPA, reports over 1,200 procurement opportunities posted on the e-GP in the past year and nearly 8,000 registered suppliers (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/ethiopia-e-procurement-platform-provides-easy-access-public-tenders>).
