# Ethiopia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Public Procurement and Property Administration Authority says its e-GP platform was used by 172 federal institutions and 196 branch offices in fiscal year 2025/26](<https://birrmetrics.com/public-procurement-authority-reports-597bn-birr-in-digital-transactions/>). [The Authority said the electronic system was implemented across 169 federal budgetary institutions and 93 branch offices in EFY 2017](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7113934>). [The US International Trade Administration reported in 2023 that registered suppliers can bid online on e-GP, expanded that year to 74 federal agencies](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/ethiopia-e-procurement-platform-provides-easy-access-public-tenders>).

**End to end.** [The Authority says the e-GP system automates bidding, evaluation, contract signing and payment](<https://birrmetrics.com/public-procurement-authority-reports-597bn-birr-in-digital-transactions/>). [Addis Fortune reported in 2022 that it was compulsory for 72 of 174 federal agencies](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-banks-on-automated-public-procurement-process>).

**Use reported.** [The Authority's Director General says more than 597 billion birr in completed transactions went through e-GP in fiscal year 2025/26](<https://birrmetrics.com/public-procurement-authority-reports-597bn-birr-in-digital-transactions/>). [The Authority said 308.75 billion birr went through it in EFY 2017](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7113934>).
