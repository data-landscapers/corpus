# South Africa

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [National Treasury's September 2026 deck says eTenders handles tender publication, online bid submission, online bid evaluation and publication of awards, for 892 organs of state](<https://www.gtac.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GTAC-EU-eProcurement-Dialog-NT-Presentation-draft.pdf>). [The MAPS assessment says organs of state publish tenders on eTenders, with 73% of registered organs publishing in 2023-2024](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/MAPS%20Assessment%20South%20Africa.pdf>).

**End to end.** [The deck lists eTenders functions from procurement planning to contract lifecycle management](<https://www.gtac.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GTAC-EU-eProcurement-Dialog-NT-Presentation-draft.pdf>). [A Treasury spokesperson told Business Day in October 2025 that procurement is still largely paper based and that an e-GP system is being developed in phases](<https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/national/2025-10-06-treasury-wants-state-tenders-automated-by-2030-to-rein-in-graft/>).

**Use reported.** [The deck gives eTenders an average of 3,300 tenders and 1,800 awards a month](<https://www.gtac.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GTAC-EU-eProcurement-Dialog-NT-Presentation-draft.pdf>). [The MAPS assessment found 12% of registered organs of state uploaded procurement plans and 3% published contract notices in 2023-2024](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/MAPS%20Assessment%20South%20Africa.pdf>).
