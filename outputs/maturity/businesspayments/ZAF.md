# South Africa

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The OECD, World Bank and AfDB MAPS assessment says South Africa's organs of state publish tenders on the eTenders e-procurement system, with 73% of registered organs of state publishing tenders in financial year 2023-2024](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/MAPS%20Assessment%20South%20Africa.pdf>). [National Treasury's SCM Instruction 09 of 2022/23 requires departments, constitutional institutions and Schedule 2 and 3 public entities to publish open competitive bids, amendments, cancellations and awards on the e-Tender Portal from 1 November 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://ocpo.treasury.gov.za/Legislation/Instruction%20Notes/PFMA%20SCM%20Instruction%20No%2009%20of%202022-23%20Mandatory%20Utilisation%20of%20the%20E-Tender%20Portal.pdf>).

**End to end.** No source held since October 2023 says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [The MAPS assessment gives eTenders use figures for 2023-2024: 73% of registered organs of state published tenders, 12% uploaded procurement plans and 3% published contract notices](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/MAPS%20Assessment%20South%20Africa.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [The MAPS assessment gives eTenders use figures for 2023-2024: 73% of registered organs of state published tenders, 12% uploaded procurement plans and 3% published contract notices](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2024/MAPS%20Assessment%20South%20Africa.pdf>).
