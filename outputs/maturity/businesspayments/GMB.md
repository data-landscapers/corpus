# Gambia

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The Gambia Public Procurement Authority's training of 8 to 10 October 2026 covered the rollout of the Electronic Government Procurement (E-GP) system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gppa-trains-procurement-cadre-on-new-act-regulations-and-standard-documents>). Its Board Vice Chairperson said the system will digitise the procurement cycle. In August 2025 [GPPA Director General Phoday Jaiteh said the authority had implemented a new e-procurement portal over the past two years](<https://foroyaa.net/procurement-drives-value-in-managing-resources-says-gppa-dg-jaiteh/>). The portal streamlined the business registration process, the Director General said.

**End to end.** [The E-GP system was being rolled out in October 2026](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/gppa-trains-procurement-cadre-on-new-act-regulations-and-standard-documents>). The GPPA Board Vice Chairperson said it is meant to digitise the procurement cycle.

**Use reported.** [The Director General described the new portal by what it does for firms: business registration streamlined and transaction times reduced](<https://foroyaa.net/procurement-drives-value-in-managing-resources-says-gppa-dg-jaiteh/>). It came alongside new regulations, a procurement manual and bidding documents.
