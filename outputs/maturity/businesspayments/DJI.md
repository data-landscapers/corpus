# Djibouti

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The state broadcaster RTD reported in October 2026 that the electronic public procurement portal, launched in May 2023, publishes tenders and their results](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>). It also lets users consult files and carries the annual reports of the national procurement commission, the CNMP. [Decree 2023-038/PRE of February 2023 instituted the portal, hosted by the CNMP](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-038-pre-instituant-le-portail-electronique-des-marches-publics/>). It requires contracting authorities to publish notices, tender documents and cancellations on it.

**End to end.** [RTD reported that electronic bid submission, e-signature and digital tracking of payments are planned over 2026-2028](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>). A platform covering the whole cycle from planning to payment is due by 2027.

**Use reported.** [RTD reported that the portal lists 1,300 published contracts and 1,227 recorded awards since 2021](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>). The figures were given at the second regional public-procurement colloquium in Dikhil.
