# Djibouti

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [The state broadcaster RTD reported in October 2026 that the electronic public procurement portal, launched in May 2023, publishes tenders and their results, lets users consult files and carries the annual reports of the national procurement commission, the CNMP](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>). [Decree 2023-038/PRE of February 2023 instituted the portal, hosted by the CNMP, and requires contracting authorities to publish notices, tender documents and cancellations on it](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-038-pre-instituant-le-portail-electronique-des-marches-publics/>).

**End to end.** [RTD reported that electronic bid submission, e-signature and digital tracking of payments are planned over 2026-2028, with a platform covering the whole cycle from planning to payment by 2027](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>).

**Use reported.** [RTD reported that the portal lists 1,300 published contracts and 1,227 recorded awards since 2021, figures given at the second regional public-procurement colloquium in Dikhil](<https://rtd.dj/le-premier-ministre-par-interim-participe-au-deuxieme-colloque-regional/>).
