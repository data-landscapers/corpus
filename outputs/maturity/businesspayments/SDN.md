# Sudan

## B2B and B2G functionality

**E-procurement in service.** [Minister of State at Finance Mohamed Nour Abdel-Daim announced on 17 March 2026 that design of the national electronic procurement system, with a government tender platform, is complete and its launch is being prepared](<https://sudanhorizon.com/government-announces-imminent-launching-of-national-electronic-procurement-system/>). [The National Medical Supplies Fund ran its first electronic tender for medicines since the war in November 2025, with 67 companies competing](<https://sudannabaa.com/en/news-en/first-electronic-tender-for-medical-supplies-since-the-war-draws-67-competitors/63060/>).

**End to end.** No source found says e-procurement runs end to end or is required of all public bodies. The nearest held fact: [the national system, with its government tender platform, is designed and its launch is being prepared](<https://sudanhorizon.com/government-announces-imminent-launching-of-national-electronic-procurement-system/>).

**Use reported.** No figure for e-procurement has been found. The nearest held fact: [67 companies, of 99 that bought the documents, competed in the Fund's electronic tender](<https://sudannabaa.com/en/news-en/first-electronic-tender-for-medical-supplies-since-the-war-draws-67-competitors/63060/>), which gives no count of tenders or value.
