# Morocco

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A Moroccan telecommunications company's terrestrial fibre cable [runs from Morocco to Niger](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>) through Mauritania, Mali and Burkina Faso. It is 5,700 km long and belongs to the company, which has subsidiaries in each of the four countries, and which invested about one billion dirhams in it between 2020 and 2022.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The cable [is recorded as completed in 2022](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). Morocco is the end of it that reaches the sea, and the four countries along it are those where the company's subsidiaries trade. The record of the investment names the route and its length and gives the year the work ended. It gives no figure for the traffic the cable carries into Morocco from the south, and names no operator outside the company's own group that uses it.
