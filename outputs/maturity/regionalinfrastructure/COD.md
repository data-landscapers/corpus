# DR Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Burundi's Council of Ministers [adopted a memorandum on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) for a fibre interconnection with the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It calls the project a joint initiative. Congo's SOCOF and Tanzania's TTCL [made the Kalemie to Kigoma lake cable official on 20 October 2025](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/rdc-tanzanie-interconnexion-fibre-optique-pourrait-transformer-region/>), with a Tanzanian ministry present. Works were due in early 2026 and service by the end of 2027. Tanzania [reaffirmed the cable across Lake Tanganyika in September 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-drc-agree-to-fast-track-sgr-link-and-fibre-optic-cable-across-lake-tanganyika/>). Ministers of the country and the Central African Republic [agreed in October 2026 on the need for a link](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says infrastructure shared with another country under an arrangement is in service. Paratus [switched on its Goma to Mombasa route in February 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>), but it is one company's network. Foreign networks [peer at the KINIX exchange in Kinshasa](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>) as members, which is commercial use. A [river cable from Brazzaville](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>) names no Congolese party on this side.
