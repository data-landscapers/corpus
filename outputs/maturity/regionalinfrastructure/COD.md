# DR Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Tanzania's foreign minister [reaffirmed on 5 September 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-drc-agree-to-fast-track-sgr-link-and-fibre-optic-cable-across-lake-tanganyika/>) that Tanzania and the Democratic Republic of the Congo are pursuing a fibre cable across Lake Tanganyika, from Kigoma to Kalemie, and agreed with the Congolese ambassador to speed it up. The digital ministers of the Central African Republic and the DRC [agreed on 8 October 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>) on the need for a fibre link between them to reach the Moanda cable landing. Burundi's Council of Ministers [adopted on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) the memorandum for a fibre interconnection with the DRC.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A company [activated a 2,000 km fibre route from Mombasa to Goma in February 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>), through Nairobi, Kampala and Kigali, giving the east of the country a direct path to the sea cables on the Kenyan coast. The company [said on 9 February 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>) that the route was already carrying traffic for its first wholesale customers, with partner companies in Uganda and Kenya. It [said again on 9 September 2026](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>) that the route was live and carrying traffic.
