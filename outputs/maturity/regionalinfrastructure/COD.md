# DR Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** [On 5 September 2026 Tanzania's foreign minister said Tanzania and the country are pursuing a fibre cable across Lake Tanganyika](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-drc-agree-to-fast-track-sgr-link-and-fibre-optic-cable-across-lake-tanganyika/>). It would run from Kigoma to Kalemie, and he agreed with the Congolese ambassador to speed it up. [On 8 October 2026 the digital ministers of the Central African Republic and the DRC agreed on the need for a fibre link between them](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>). It would reach the Moanda cable landing. [On 8 May 2024 Burundi's Council of Ministers adopted a memorandum for a fibre interconnection with the DRC](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** [A company activated a 2,000 km fibre route from Mombasa to Goma in February 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>). It runs through Nairobi, Kampala and Kigali to the sea cables on the Kenyan coast. [On 9 February 2026 the company said the route already carried traffic for its first wholesale customers](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>), with partners in Uganda and Kenya. [It said again on 9 September 2026 that the route was live and carrying traffic](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>). [In August 2026 Tech Africa said networks registered outside the country peer at KINIX, the Kinshasa internet exchange](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>). They include GVA of Côte d'Ivoire, Paratus and AFR-IX Telecom.
