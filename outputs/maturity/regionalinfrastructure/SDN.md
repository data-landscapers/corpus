# Sudan

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Sudatel, Djibouti Telecom and Ethio telecom [signed a tripartite agreement on 4 February 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>) for Horizon. It is a fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landings across Ethiopia to Sudan's. It follows a memorandum of December 2024. No source reports work begun on the Sudanese section.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Egypt's minister [launched a 400 km fibre cable to Sudan on 26 October 2004](<https://sudantribune.com/article/6899>), built to carry calls and internet between the two countries. Telecom Egypt and Sudatel [signed a cooperation agreement in April 2021](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html>) to make better use of that fibre for international traffic. In September 2026 Horizon was [still being presented to carriers before deployment](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>). No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
