# Sudan

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Sudatel [signed a tripartite agreement with Djibouti Telecom and Ethio telecom on 4 February 2026](<https://www.sudatel.sd/en/horizon-fibre-initiative/>) for Horizon, a terrestrial fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landing stations through Ethiopia to Sudan's, following a memorandum of December 2024. The corridor [is planned at about 2,800 km with 144 fibre pairs](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>), joining Djibouti, Addis Ababa and Port Sudan. Sudan's state news agency [reported the agreement the same day](<https://suna.sd/posts/atfaky-astratygy-byn-mgmoaa-sodatl-ogyboty-llatsalat-oalatsalat-alathyoby-ltdshyn-mbadr-horayzn-faybr>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The three operators [presented Horizon to large cloud companies and carriers in Addis Ababa on 7 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>), in a phase before deployment and commercial launch. The corridor [had no announced buyer of capacity and no date for service](<https://btw.media/ar/horizon-fiber-masar-badil-huquq-saa>) three days later.
