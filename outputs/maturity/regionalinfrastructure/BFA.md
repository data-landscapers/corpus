# Burkina Faso

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or contract for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Burkina Faso. [In November 2025 WACREN, the regional research and education network, held a workshop in Ouagadougou](<https://indico.wacren.net/event/256/>). Its aim was a roadmap and timetable for joining FasoREN, the national research network, fully to the regional backbone. A one-year transition run by WACREN, FasoREN and the virtual university was among the expected results.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** [WACREN said in 2025 that FasoREN's connection to its regional network was still partial](<https://indico.wacren.net/event/256/>). The connection runs over the national fibre that ANPTIC, the state digital agency, has laid. The regional network links universities and research centres across West and Central Africa, under the AfricaConnect project. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Maroc Telecom terrestrial cable](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>), which links its own subsidiaries.

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says the research network link is complete.
