# Djibouti

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Djibouti Telecom, Ethio telecom and Sudatel [signed an agreement in Djibouti on 4 February 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>) to build and maintain Horizon, a fibre corridor to Ethiopia and Sudan. It runs about 2,800 km with 144 fibre pairs. It follows [a memorandum signed in Addis Ababa in December 2024](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign/>). The Prime Minister [told the National Assembly on 1 March 2026](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-de-politique-generale-du-premier-ministre-abdoulkader-kamil-mohamed-a-loccasion-de-la-session-ordinaire-de-lassemblee-nationale-1er-mars-2026/>) that the agreements exist.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** In September 2026 the three operators [presented Horizon to carriers before deployment](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>). It is not yet in service. The DARE1 sea cable [has joined Djibouti to Somalia and Kenya since 2021](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>). The sources name no Somali or Kenyan party to it. A Djibouti Telecom profile [lists land cables to Somalia and Ethiopia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>). It names no party there.

***Also on file***. [DARE1](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>); [Djibouti Telecom land cables](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>).

***Not found***. No source found names the Somali, Kenyan or Ethiopian party to a cable already working. None names a shared exchange point or data centre.
