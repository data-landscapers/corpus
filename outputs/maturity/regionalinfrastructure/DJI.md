# Djibouti

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The heads of Djibouti Telecom, Ethio telecom and Sudatel [signed in Djibouti on 4 February 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/djibouti-telecom-ethio-telecomet-sudatel-telecom-group-signent-un-accord-strategique-pour-linitiative-horizon-fiber/>) the agreement for Horizon, a terrestrial fibre corridor of nearly 2,800 km and 144 fibre pairs joining Djibouti, Addis Ababa and Port Sudan. It [turned a memorandum of December 2024 into a binding agreement](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/28249-ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-sudatel-seal-binding-deal-on-cross-border-fiber-network/>). The three operators [presented the project to prospective carriers in Addis Ababa on 7 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>), ahead of deployment and commercial launch.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The DARE1 submarine cable [links Djibouti City with Bosaso and Mogadishu in Somalia and Mombasa in Kenya](<https://itweb.africa/article/djibouti-telecom-extends-dare1-subsea-cable-from-kenya-to-south-africa/Olx4z7kaW86q56km>), with 36 terabits a second over three fibre pairs, and [has been operational since 2021](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>). Djibouti Telecom announced in 2025 that the cable would be extended south from Mombasa.
