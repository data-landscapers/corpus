# Zambia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Zambia's science and technology minister [attended the launch of the interconnectivity project with Mozambique on 20 February 2026](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-e-zambia-avancam-com-interligacao-digital-transfronteirica-em-chifunde/>). It is a cross-border fibre partnership of Bayobab Zambia and Vodacom Moçambique, with both regulators involved. A World Bank document of January 2025 [said cross-border infrastructure was missing](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt>), hindering digital trade. Zambia's and Burundi's ministers [signed a memorandum in Lusaka in October 2024](<https://itweb.africa/article/burundi-zambia-agree-underwater-fibre-connection/kLgB17ezr59M59N4>) for a fibre cable under Lake Tanganyika. The report says an unnamed private firm was contracted.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Zambia and Mozambique [commissioned the Chanida to Cassacatiza fibre interconnector](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>), reported on 23 February 2026. It joins Zambia's national backbone to Mozambique's sea cable network. Zambia's technology minister and Mozambique's communications minister spoke at the ceremony. The link gives landlocked Zambia a route to the sea cables through its neighbour. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says building has started on the cable to Burundi.
