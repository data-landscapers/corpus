# Zambia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A development fund and a telecommunications group [announced in May 2023](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>) a partnership to build a terrestrial fibre network of some US$320 million, linking landlocked countries to the sea cables on Africa's east and west coasts, and listed Zambia among the countries it would cover.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Zambia and Mozambique [commissioned a cross-border fibre interconnector between Chanida and Cassacatiza](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>), reported on 23 February 2026. It joins Zambia's national backbone to Mozambique's submarine cable network, and so gives Zambia, which has no coast, a route to the sea. A World Bank project document [had said in January 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt>) that cross-border infrastructure between countries was missing in Zambia and that this held back digital trade.
