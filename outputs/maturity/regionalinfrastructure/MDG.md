# Madagascar

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The METISS cable [was built by a consortium of six Indian Ocean operators](<https://fibernet.ceb.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-17-MISE-EN-SERVICE-DU-CABLE-METISS.pdf>), Telma of Madagascar among them, with early support from the Indian Ocean Commission. It was laid in 2020 and lands at Fort Dauphin. Telma also [landed the 2Africa cable at Mahajanga in February 2023](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>), through a partnership with Vodafone.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** METISS [entered service on 5 March 2021](<https://fibernet.ceb.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-17-MISE-EN-SERVICE-DU-CABLE-METISS.pdf>), a 3,200 km cable joining Madagascar, Mauritius and Réunion to a landing near Durban in South Africa. The consortium said its first links to Africa were already used by its operators' customers. It gave no figure for traffic carried, and named no user in another country.

***Also on file***. [EASSy](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>); [LION](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>).
