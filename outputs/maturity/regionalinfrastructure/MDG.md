# Madagascar

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Madagascar. The arrangement on record is the METISS consortium. Telma is its Malagasy member, beside Mauritius's CEB and Emtel, the [consortium's 2021 statement](<https://fibernet.ceb.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-17-MISE-EN-SERVICE-DU-CABLE-METISS.pdf>) says.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The METISS consortium states that its 3,200 km sea cable [entered service on 5 March 2021](<https://fibernet.ceb.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-17-MISE-EN-SERVICE-DU-CABLE-METISS.pdf>). It links Mauritius, Réunion and Madagascar, landing at Fort Dauphin, to a landing near Durban. The operators' customers were already using its first links to Africa. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record. The 2Africa cable's landing at Mahajanga came through a partnership with Vodafone, not with an African country.

***Also on file***. [2Africa landing](<https://www.axian-telecom.com/ac-content/uploads/2023/02/AXIAN-Telecom-Press-release-2Africa-landing-in-Madagascar-VA.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says a new shared project was agreed in the last three years.
