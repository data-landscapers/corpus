# South Africa

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Google [said in May 2024 that the land section of its Umoja fibre route had been built](<https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/infrastructure/investing-in-connectivity-and-growth-for-africa>), working with a pan-African carrier. The route runs from Kenya through Uganda, Rwanda, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia and Zimbabwe to South Africa. Its crossing of the Indian Ocean to Australia had still to be laid.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The METISS submarine cable [entered service on 5 March 2021](<https://fibernet.ceb.mu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2021-03-17-MISE-EN-SERVICE-DU-CABLE-METISS.pdf>), joining Mauritius, Réunion and Madagascar to a landing near Durban. Liquid Dataport [launched a land-only fibre route from Mombasa to Johannesburg in May 2023](<https://liquid.tech/about-us/news/liquid-dataport-launches-the-first-terrestrial-data-superhighway-connecting-mombasa-to-johannesburg/>). It crosses Uganda, Rwanda, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Zambia and Zimbabwe. The state wholesaler Broadband Infraco [said in July 2019 that its network reached all six land neighbours](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>). It said its point of presence at Beitbridge carried Zimbabwe's broadband traffic to data centres in Johannesburg.
