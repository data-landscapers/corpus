# South Africa

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for South Africa. Google [built its Umoja route to South Africa with Liquid](<https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/infrastructure/investing-in-connectivity-and-growth-for-africa>) in 2024. That is a company's network, not an agreement between countries. No regional programme naming South Africa and a neighbour is on record either.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Mozambique's research network MoRENet [opened a border point in Cape Town](<https://africarxiv.ubuntunet.net/bitstreams/9427cb02-feef-4b3f-b03b-84cb4efea3cb/download>), interconnected with South Africa's research networks TENET and SANReN. It carries Mozambique's international capacity of 60 Gbps, and was described in October 2025 as operational. Networks from over 50 countries [peer at NAPAfrica](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>), which is commercial use. Broadband Infraco [said in 2019 that it reaches all six neighbours](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>), naming no party there.
