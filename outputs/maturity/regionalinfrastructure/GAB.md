# Gabon

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Gabon and Equatorial Guinea [signed an agreement on joining their fibre networks at Mongomo on 10 November 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/13/historic-fibre-roaming-and-frequency-deals-signed-between-gabon-and-equatorial-guinea/>), between the two ministers for the digital economy. The agreement and the inauguration of the link fell on the same day.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The two ministers [inaugurated the interconnection of the national fibre backbones on 10 November 2025](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>), at Meyo-Kyé on the Gabonese side and Ebebeyin on the Equatoguinean. Gabon's backbone company reports that tests confirmed traffic exchanged across the border. An earlier phase of the Central African Backbone programme [paid, from 2012, for a backbone from Libreville through Franceville to the Congolese border](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P122776>), on a World Bank loan of US$58 million.

***Also on file***. [ACE Consortium membership](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P122776>); [Africa Coast to Europe (ACE) cable](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>).

***Not found***. No source found reports the link or facility carrying another country's traffic in the last three years: capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a user from the other country named.
