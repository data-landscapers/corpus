# Gabon

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Gabon's and Equatorial Guinea's digital economy ministers [signed an agreement at Mongomo on 10 November 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/13/historic-fibre-roaming-and-frequency-deals-signed-between-gabon-and-equatorial-guinea/>) to interconnect the two countries' fibre networks. Gabon's backbone company SPIN and Cameroon's CAMTEL [signed a memorandum on CAB-Corridor](<https://spin.ga/cab-corridor-spin-camtel-connectivite-afrique-centrale/>), published in October 2026. It would join Cameroon, Gabon and Congo over the Central African Backbone. The World Bank [lent Gabon $58 million in 2012](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P122776>) for that backbone's fourth phase.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The two ministers [inaugurated the interconnection of the Gabonese and Equatoguinean backbones on 10 November 2025](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>), at Meyo-Kyé and Ebebeyin. Tests confirmed traffic flowing between Bitam and Ebebeyin, and between Libreville and Bata. A French Treasury sheet says [Congo has been joined to Gabon under the Central African Backbone since 2018](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>). No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
