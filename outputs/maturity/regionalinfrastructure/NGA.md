# Nigeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Nigeria's Backbone Connectivity Network and Equatorial Guinea's transport minister [signed a sea cable agreement in Malabo in February 2026](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/nigeria-equatorial-guinea-subsea-cable-to-strengthen-regional-connectivity>). Phase3, CSquared and Benin's SBIN [announced a fibre route from Lagos to Accra in May 2025](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>), tested but not yet open. Neither source reports works begun on the cable to Equatorial Guinea.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Phase3 and Senegal's Sonatel [activated a 3,500 km fibre route from Lagos to Dakar in May 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>). It runs through Benin, Togo and Ghana. The two operators' commercial packages were still to be announced. Nigeria and Senegal are the route's two ends; the countries between are crossed. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
