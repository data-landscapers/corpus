# Nigeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A Nigerian fibre company and Equatorial Guinea's minister for transport and telecommunications [signed an agreement in Malabo in February 2026](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/nigeria-equatorial-guinea-subsea-cable-to-strengthen-regional-connectivity>) for a direct submarine fibre cable between Nigeria and Equatorial Guinea, with a commercial data centre. It is [an agreement of principles](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/28289-nigeria-and-equatorial-guinea-sign-landmark-subsea-fiber-optic-agreement/>) that follows a state visit of August 2024, and its specifications, cost and timetable were not disclosed. CSquared, Phase3 and SBIN also [announced on 6 May 2025](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>) a terrestrial fibre route from Lagos to Accra through Benin, Togo and Ghana, with no date yet for it to be ready for service.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A Nigerian fibre company and a Senegalese operator [activated a 3,500 km terrestrial fibre route from Lagos to Dakar](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>), reported on 9 May 2025. The route runs through Benin, Togo and Ghana. Lagos and Dakar are its two ends. Commercial availability and service packages were still to be announced at that date, and no figure for traffic on the route has been published.
