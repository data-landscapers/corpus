# Liberia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Orange Liberia says it has [completed a new fibre link from Monrovia to Lugato](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/orange-liberia-celebrates-completion-of-ace-fiber-rerouting/article_7e13b2a8-da30-46a6-8b9b-68f96d819ff1.html>), reported in May 2025. The operator says the link joins Liberia to six other undersea cables by way of Côte d'Ivoire. The route is Liberia's connection to the cable systems of its neighbour, and it is built rather than planned, so no work is still pending on it.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The same [Monrovia-to-Lugato link](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/orange-liberia-celebrates-completion-of-ace-fiber-rerouting/article_7e13b2a8-da30-46a6-8b9b-68f96d819ff1.html>) is in service. Orange Liberia's chairman says it carried all of Liberia's international traffic during the outage on the Africa Coast to Europe cable in 2025. The traffic he names is Liberia's own, sent out through Côte d'Ivoire. He names no other country's operator on the link, and gives no figure for capacity sold across the border.
