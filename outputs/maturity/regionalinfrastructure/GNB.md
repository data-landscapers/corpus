# Guinea-Bissau

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Guinea-Bissau. An ECOWAS commissioner [said a protocol of April 2023 consolidated commitment to the Amilcar Cabral sea cable](<https://ang.gw/telecomunicacoes-ministro-diz-que-o-projeto-do-cabo-submarino-amilcar-cabral-vai-reforcar-a-integracao-da-guine-bissau-ao-mundo-global/>). It would run from Cabo Verde to Liberia, with a branch to Guinea-Bissau. Its study and its construction were still unfunded in June 2024.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Guinea-Bissau [joined the backbone of WACREN](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), the regional research and education network, in 2025. The expansion is supported by AfricaConnect projects co-funded by the European Commission. It brought six West African countries into the network together, for their universities and research institutes. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
