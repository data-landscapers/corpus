# Cape Verde

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The European Investment Bank [signed a EUR 37 million framework loan to Cabo Verde on 2 September 2024](<https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20220836>). It pays for a branch joining the national network to a new submarine cable from Europe to South Africa. It also pays for an upgrade of the national transmission network and of Cabo Verde Telecom's information systems. The bank's notes put the project's total cost at EUR 80 million. They name the cable as Equiano.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The bank's record of the loan, as accessed in June 2026, [lists it as active and shows nothing yet disbursed](<https://www.eib.org/en/projects/all/20220836>). The record gives no date for the branch to be built or brought into service.
