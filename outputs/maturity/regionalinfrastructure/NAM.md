# Namibia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Telecom Namibia [signed a master service agreement with Angola Telecom and a cable company on 12 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/15/telecom-namibia-angola-telecom-and-satcab-sign-sarssy-deal-to-deliver-1-tbps-connectivity-across-southern-africa/>) to join the Southern African Regional Submarine System to the Equiano cable at its Swakopmund landing station. Telecom Namibia hosts the landing and provides Angola Telecom with one terabit a second on Equiano. The agreement builds on a memorandum and commercial terms signed in April 2026. Telecom Namibia also [signed a pilot commercial agreement with Angola's space programme office](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/20382-telecom-namibia-signs-satellite-subsea-connectivity-deals-with-angola-firms.html>) that week, for a trial of the Angosat-2 satellite on its working network.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The [satellite pilot](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-angola-ink-satellite-deal-for-regional-connectivity/G98YdqLGjVoMX2PD>) is a trial, with a full commercial launch to follow it. It came after talks that began in June 2025 and [a proof of concept reported in October 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/namibia-taps-angosat-2-to-boost-connectivity>).
