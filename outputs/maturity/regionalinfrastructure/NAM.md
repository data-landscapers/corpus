# Namibia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Telecom Namibia, Angola Telecom and the cable company SATCAB [signed a master service agreement on 12 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/15/telecom-namibia-angola-telecom-and-satcab-sign-sarssy-deal-to-deliver-1-tbps-connectivity-across-southern-africa/>). It joins the Southern African Regional Submarine System to the Equiano cable at Swakopmund. Telecom Namibia hosts the landing and gives Angola Telecom one terabit a second. That week it also [signed a pilot agreement with Angola's space programme office](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/20382-telecom-namibia-signs-satellite-subsea-connectivity-deals-with-angola-firms.html>) to trial Angosat-2. MTC Namibia and Botswana's BoFiNet [signed a memorandum on 17 February 2026](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/index.php/news/article/bofinet-signs-mou-with-mtc-namibia-to-further-bolster-connectivity-reliability-for-botswana>) on cross-border fibre and transit capacity. BoFiNet says the two had recently completed interconnections at Mamuno to Charles Hill and at Ngoma.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says shared infrastructure is in service under an arrangement. The Angosat-2 work [began as a proof of concept in October 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/namibia-taps-angosat-2-to-boost-connectivity>) and is now a pilot. Paratus Africa, registered in Mauritius, [is a member of the Windhoek exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/368/>), which is commercial use. South Africa's Broadband Infraco [said in 2019 that it reaches Namibia](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>), naming no Namibian party.

***Also on file***. [Windhoek Internet Exchange Point](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/368/>); [Broadband Infraco regional network](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>).

***Not found***. No source found says building has begun on the cable work with Angola. None says the Botswana interconnections carry traffic. None says a shared link, exchange or data centre is in service.
