# Namibia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Telecom Namibia [signed a master service agreement with Angola Telecom and a cable company on 12 June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/15/telecom-namibia-angola-telecom-and-satcab-sign-sarssy-deal-to-deliver-1-tbps-connectivity-across-southern-africa/>). It joins the Southern African Regional Submarine System to the Equiano cable at Swakopmund. Telecom Namibia hosts the landing and gives Angola Telecom one terabit a second on Equiano. A memorandum and commercial terms signed in April 2026 came first. That week it also [signed a pilot agreement with Angola's space programme office](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/20382-telecom-namibia-signs-satellite-subsea-connectivity-deals-with-angola-firms.html>) to trial the Angosat-2 satellite.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** In August 2026 the Windhoek Internet Exchange Point [had six members, among them Paratus Africa, a network registered in Mauritius](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/368/>). Three Namibian and two US networks are the others. Broadband Infraco, South Africa's state wholesaler, [said in July 2019](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>) that its network reached all six neighbours, Namibia among them. The [Angosat-2 satellite pilot](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-angola-ink-satellite-deal-for-regional-connectivity/G98YdqLGjVoMX2PD>) with Angola is a trial, after [a proof of concept in October 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/namibia-taps-angosat-2-to-boost-connectivity>).
