# Central African Republic

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** The six CEMAC states [are developing the Central African Backbone](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>) to join their national fibre networks, an August 2026 report says. About $273 million from member states and partners backs it. The African Development Bank and the European Union [paid for the Central African Republic component](<https://www.jobrapide.org/offres/avis-appel-offres/avis-a-manifestation-dinteret-pour-le-recrutement-dune-firme-en-charge-de-lelaboration-dun-plan-national-strategique-pns-centrafrique-digitale-2025/>), a ministry notice of 2018 says.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The country [inaugurated its backbone on 6 February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>), with links to Cameroon at Gamboula and to Congo at Bomassa. A Congolese ministry official attended. The bank [closed the project in September 2023](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>) with both links built. In October 2026 the two routes [were still the country's fibre links abroad](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>). Vandalism in March 2026 [partly cut national service](<https://sunvimedia.info/vers-une-diversification-de-la-connectivite-en-centrafrique/>). No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
