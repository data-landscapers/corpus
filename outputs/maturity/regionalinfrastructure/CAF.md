# Central African Republic

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The six central African states [are developing the Central African Backbone](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>) to join their national fibre networks, with about $273 million from member states and partners, as reported in August 2026. The government [received grants from the African Development Bank and the European Union](<https://www.jobrapide.org/offres/avis-appel-offres/avis-a-manifestation-dinteret-pour-le-recrutement-dune-firme-en-charge-de-lelaboration-dun-plan-national-strategique-pns-centrafrique-digitale-2025/>) for its part of the project. A regional prospectus [lists the country among eleven in a USD 9 million project](<https://au-pida.org/prospectus/brochure-onepage.php?id=17>) for six new data centres, one of them here, which is still being defined.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The national fibre backbone [was inaugurated on 6 February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>): 935 km of fibre on land and under rivers, joined to Cameroon at Gamboula and to Congo at Bomassa. The project [closed in September 2023](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>) with both cross-border links installed. The authorities [judged in May 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1210-142321-internet-qu-est-ce-que-la-liaison-par-fibre-avec-la-rdc-peut-apporter-a-la-centrafrique>) that reaching the sea cables through the networks of coastal neighbours had markedly improved speeds.
