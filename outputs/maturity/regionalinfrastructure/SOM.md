# Somalia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Somalia is one of five recipients of the World Bank's Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project. A June 2026 status report says it [became effective for all five between 2023 and 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>). Consultancies have prepared fibre deployment in Somalia and Somaliland. Broadband deployment in Somaliland was expected to start in the second half of 2026. The [2023 grant agreement](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062123164542123/pdf/P17618108dc2460600945a0f83353acb3fb.pdf>) pays for cross-border links.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says infrastructure shared under an arrangement is in service. The DARE1 sea cable [has joined Bosaso and Mogadishu to Djibouti and Mombasa since 2021](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>), but no Somali party to it is named. A Djibouti Telecom profile [lists a land cable to Somalia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>), also naming no Somali party.

***Also on file***. [DARE1](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>); [Djibouti Telecom land cable to Somalia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>).

***Not found***. No source found says building has begun on the regional project's links. None names a Somali party to a working cable.
