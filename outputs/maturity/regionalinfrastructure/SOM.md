# Somalia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Somalia [signed a grant of SDR 59.5 million with the World Bank's fund for the poorest countries on 21 June 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062123164542123/pdf/P17618108dc2460600945a0f83353acb3fb.pdf>), to pay for cross-border terrestrial links and backbone in Somalia and Somaliland, under a regional project shared with South Sudan and two regional bodies. The project [had prepared the ground for fibre by 30 June 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>), with deployment in Somaliland expected to begin in the second half of the year.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The DARE1 submarine cable [has been operational since 2021](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>). It joins Bosaso and Mogadishu in Somalia to Djibouti City and to Mombasa in Kenya. Somalia has two landings on it, one on the northern coast and one at the capital.
