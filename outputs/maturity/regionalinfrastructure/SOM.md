# Somalia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** On 21 June 2023 Somalia [signed a World Bank grant of SDR 59.5 million](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062123164542123/pdf/P17618108dc2460600945a0f83353acb3fb.pdf>). It pays for cross-border land links and backbone in Somalia and Somaliland, under a project shared with South Sudan. By 30 June 2026 [the ground was prepared for fibre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>), and work in Somaliland was due to start later that year.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The DARE1 submarine cable [has been operational since 2021](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/dare1-subsea-cable-to-link-kenya-to-south-africa>). It joins Bosaso and Mogadishu in Somalia to Djibouti City and to Mombasa in Kenya. Somalia has two landings on it, one on the northern coast and one at the capital. A January 2026 profile of Djibouti Telecom [lists a land cable between Djibouti and Somalia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>) in its network.
