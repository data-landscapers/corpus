# Burundi

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Burundi's Council of Ministers [adopted a memorandum on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) for a fibre interconnection with the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The government calls the project a joint initiative of the two countries. The source names no contract, financing or start of works. Nor does it name the route or the operators who would run the link. Burundi's and Zambia's ministers [signed a memorandum in Lusaka in October 2024](<https://itweb.africa/article/burundi-zambia-agree-underwater-fibre-connection/kLgB17ezr59M59N4>) for a fibre cable under Lake Tanganyika. It would join Zambia's Northern province to Makamba province. The report says an unnamed private firm was contracted.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says a link, exchange point or data facility that Burundi shares with a neighbour is in service. The [memorandum with the Democratic Republic of the Congo](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) and the one with Zambia concern links still to be built. Nothing found describes how Burundi reaches the sea cables, or under what agreement.
