# Burundi

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Burundi's Council of Ministers [adopted a memorandum on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) for a fibre interconnection with the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The government calls the project a joint initiative of the two countries. The source names no contract, financing or start of works. Nor does it name the route or the operators who would run the link.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says a link, exchange point or data facility that Burundi shares with a neighbour is in service. The [May 2024 memorandum](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) is the only arrangement on record, and it concerns a link still to be built. Nothing found describes how Burundi reaches the sea cables, or under what agreement.
