# Burundi

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Burundi's Council of Ministers [analysed and adopted a memorandum of understanding on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) for the DRC-Burundi fibre-optic interconnection project. The government communique, reported by the Burundian press agency ABP, describes the project as a joint initiative of the two countries. The memorandum covers a cross-border fibre-optic link between Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and the Council of Ministers adopted it at its meeting of 8 May 2024.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The nearest held fact is the same [memorandum adopted on 8 May 2024](<https://abpinfo.bi/2024/05/15/un-memorandum-dentente-du-projet-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique-entre-la-republique-democratique-du-congo-et-le-burundi-a-ete-adopte-au-conseil-des-ministres/>) for the DRC-Burundi fibre-optic interconnection project. It is an agreement to build the link, as reported by ABP on 15 May 2024, and not a link in use.
