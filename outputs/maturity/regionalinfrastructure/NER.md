# Niger

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Niger's and Chad's ministers [signed a memorandum on 19 June 2025](<https://anp.ne/le-niger-et-le-tchad-bientot-interconnectes-via-la-dorsale-transharienne-a-fibre-optique-dts-fo/>) on interconnecting their sections of the trans-Saharan backbone. Niger's minister [received 1,031 km of that backbone on 14 November 2025](<https://anp.ne/niger-reception-provisoire-des-troncons-du-projet-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). It reaches the Algerian, Chadian, Nigerian, Beninese and Burkinabe borders, with African Development Bank support. Its steering committee [adopted a 2026 to 2027 work programme in March 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/session-ordinaire-du-comite-de-pilotage-copil-au-titre-de-lannee-2026-faire-le-point-des-progres-realises-et-prendre-des-orientations-pour-la-poursuite-des-activites/>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Niger's and Algeria's ministers [opened the trans-Saharan fibre link at Assamaka on 6 October 2026](<https://anp.ne/niger-algerie-lancement-a-assamaka-de-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). Niger's ministry [declared it officially in service](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>). Algeria reports a capacity of up to 100 gigabits a second from the start. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Maroc Telecom terrestrial cable](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>), which links its own subsidiaries.

***Not found***. No source found says the links to Chad, Nigeria or Benin are working. None says an exchange point or data centre is shared.
