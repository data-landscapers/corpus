# Gambia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for The Gambia. The arrangements on record are an operator link and a regional research network. Gamtel, the state operator, [describes its backup link with Senegal on its company profile](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>), captured in September 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Gamtel [holds 28 Gbps with the Senegalese operator Free on Cross Gambia, a backup link](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). Its traffic is moved onto that link automatically when the ACE sea cable fails. The page is undated, so the date it was captured stands. The Gambia also [joined the backbone of WACREN](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), the regional research and education network, in 2025. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record. Gamtel's ACE capacity is a cable landing with no Gambian arrangement with another African country named.
