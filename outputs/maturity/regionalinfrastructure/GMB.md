# Gambia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Gamtel, the national operator, [lists on its company profile a backup link with the Senegalese operator Free](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). The link, named Cross Gambia (CG2), has a capacity of 28 gigabits a second. The profile carries no date of its own. It was captured on 21 September 2026.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Gamtel [says that when the ACE submarine cable fails](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>), its traffic is rerouted automatically over the link with Free. Its main international capacity is 25 gigabits a second on ACE, through four separate gateways. That capacity is bought from the Portuguese and French operators. The company gives the link through Senegal as the basis of its 99.9 per cent redundancy.
