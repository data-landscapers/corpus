# Mauritania

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Mauritania. Its one shared link on record came through a regional research programme. Mauritania [joined the backbone of WACREN](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), the regional research and education network, in 2025, with five other West African countries.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** WACREN [says Mauritania is now on its high-speed network](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), alongside Senegal, Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau and Sierra Leone. The expansion is supported by AfricaConnect projects co-funded by the European Commission. It serves universities and research institutes. WACREN [also activated a link from Lagos to Cape Town](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>) in the same expansion, so its members can reach southern Africa's research networks without routing through Europe. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Maroc Telecom terrestrial cable](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>), which links its own subsidiaries.

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says a new shared project was agreed in the last three years.
