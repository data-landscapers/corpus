# Zimbabwe

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A Zimbabwean company and a regional fibre company [signed a partnership agreement in June 2025](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>) to invest together in long-distance fibre on a corridor joining Botswana, Zimbabwe and Zambia. The second phase, from Bulawayo to Livingstone in Zambia, [was being built in July 2026](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200057978/powertel-join-paratus-zimbabwe-to-launch-new-digital-highway>) and [was due to be lit in September 2026](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The first phase, from Plumtree on the Botswana border to Bulawayo, [was live and carrying traffic on 3 July 2026](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>), with 800 gigabits a second of equipped capacity. The companies call it the first active route of the regional company's network into Zimbabwe, linking the country to Botswana. A newspaper column of September 2026 [describes the same link as active](<https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/12/integrating-starlink-leo-infrastructure-with-zimbabwes-terrestrial-fibre-networks/>), and reports another operator [expanding capacity on the crossings to Zambia, Botswana and South Africa](<https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/12/integrating-starlink-leo-infrastructure-with-zimbabwes-terrestrial-fibre-networks/>).
