# Tunisia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Tunisian and Algerian delegations on their joint technical commission [agreed on 6 June 2026 to upgrade the land link between the two countries](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>). The source names no contract or start of works. Nor does it give the capacity planned or a date for the upgrade. The same commission [discussed a new sea cable to Europe](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says a link with Algeria, or any other facility, is in service under a named arrangement. The [June 2026 report](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>) calls the land link existing but gives no date or terms for it. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record. Tunisia's sea cables are noted elsewhere only as landings.
