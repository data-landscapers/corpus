## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Delegations of the Algeria-Tunisia Joint Technical Commission [agreed on 6 June 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>) to upgrade the existing terrestrial link between Algeria and Tunisia, as reported by Ecofin in June 2026. The agreement concerns a link that is already in place between the two countries, and it is the one shared digital link between Tunisia and another African country that the evidence names.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Nothing found gives capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a named user on the Algerian side, for any year. The nearest held fact is the [June 2026 agreement](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>) between the Algeria-Tunisia Joint Technical Commission delegations to upgrade the existing terrestrial link between the two countries.
