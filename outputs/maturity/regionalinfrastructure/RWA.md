# Rwanda

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Uganda and Rwanda [made the final fibre connection at the Mirama Hills border](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>) earlier in 2026, says the head of Uganda's Northern Corridor projects. The link was built under the Northern Corridor Integration Projects. Operators on both sides must still agree capacity before it is switched on. The same report says Uganda has completed a [second border connection with Kenya at Busia](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says the Mirama Hills link, or any other shared facility, is in service. The same report [names agreeing capacity as the step left](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>) before the link carries traffic. Foreign networks [are members of the Rwanda Internet Exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/401/>), which is commercial use. No data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Rwanda Internet Exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/401/>), whose foreign members are commercial users; [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a shared link, exchange or data centre is in service.
