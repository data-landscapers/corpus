# Rwanda

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** In May 2023 a development fund and a telecoms group [announced a terrestrial fibre network of some US$320 million](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>). It would link landlocked countries to sea cables on both coasts. Rwanda is among the twelve countries named. The announcement gave no start date for works. It named Rwanda as covered, not as the end of a particular link. No source found says building has started in Rwanda.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** In November 2026 the Rwanda Internet Exchange, RINEX, [had 17 members](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/401/>). They include networks registered in Mauritius, Côte d'Ivoire and Kenya. The members are counted from what each network declares. The source names none of the foreign members. It gives no figures for the traffic they exchange.

***Also on file***. [Rwanda Internet Exchange - RINEX](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/401/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a foreign network using RINEX. None gives figures for traffic carried across Rwanda's borders.
