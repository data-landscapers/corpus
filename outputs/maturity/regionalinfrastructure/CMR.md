# Cameroon

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Cameroon's and Chad's ministers [ended a four-day technical mission on 16 May 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2321>) that set the completion of the Nana-Mbéré fibre section, towards the Chadian border, for the following fourteen days, with targets running to July 2025. On the Chadian side, the memorandum between two operators for the route from N'Djamena to the Cameroon border [was suspended by Chad's regulator](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>), as reported on 9 February 2026, and Chad's government asked the operators to revise it before work could start.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The Central African Republic's fibre backbone [was inaugurated on 6 February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>) with 935 km of fibre installed and working. It joins the Central African Republic to Cameroon at Gamboula, and to Congo, as one part of the Central African Backbone project that the African Development Bank and the European Union paid for.
