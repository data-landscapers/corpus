# Cameroon

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Cameroon's and Chad's ministers [closed a technical mission on 16 May 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2321>). It settled the completion of the Nana to Mbere fibre section within fourteen days. Gabon's SPIN and Cameroon's CAMTEL [signed a memorandum on CAB-Corridor](<https://spin.ga/cab-corridor-spin-camtel-connectivite-afrique-centrale/>), published in October 2026. It would join Cameroon, Gabon and Congo. A planned operator route from N'Djamena [was suspended by Chad's regulator](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>) in February 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The Central African Republic [inaugurated its backbone in February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>), interconnected with Cameroon at Gamboula under the Central African Backbone. That regional programme is backed by the African Development Bank and the European Union. Chad told ITU-D in March 2026 that its [link to CAMTEL's network is working](<https://www.itu.int/md/D26-SG01-C-0040/en>). No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
