# Sierra Leone

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Sierra Leone and Guinea [signed an agreement in November 2024](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>) to join their backbones, the regulator records. Guinea's backbone company and Leoncom then [signed an interconnection at Pamelap](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/>), reported in June 2025. It starts at 42.5 gigabits a second, with 100 planned by 2026.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Michcom-IX [says its open exchange is live](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>), with points of presence in Freetown, Jui, Conakry and Monrovia. It joins 15 networks across Sierra Leone, Guinea and Liberia. Its PeeringDB record of June 2026 [describes it as serving the three countries](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4892>). All five peers listed there are Sierra Leonean networks. No source found says the Pamelap link with Guinea carries traffic yet.

***Also on file***. [Michcom-IX](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4892>).

***Not found***. No source found names a network from Guinea or Liberia using Michcom-IX. None gives figures for traffic carried across the border.
