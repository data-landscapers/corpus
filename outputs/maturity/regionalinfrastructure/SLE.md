# Sierra Leone

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Sierra Leone and Guinea [signed an agreement in November 2024](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>) to interconnect their national backbones, the regulator NatCA says. Guinea's SOGEB and Sierra Leone's Leoncom then [signed an interconnection agreement in Conakry](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/>), published in June 2025. It links the networks at Pamelap with 42.5 Gbps at first and 100 Gbps planned for 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Guinea's telecoms ministry [lists its interconnection with Sierra Leone among four working in 2025](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). No Sierra Leonean source on its opening has been found. Michcom-IX [runs an exchange with points of presence in Freetown, Conakry and Monrovia](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>). One company runs it, with no agreement between national bodies, so it is commercial. No data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Michcom-IX](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4892>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country under an agreement.
