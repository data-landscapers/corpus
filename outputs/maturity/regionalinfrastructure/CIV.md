# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Côte d'Ivoire. The country's place in shared links rests on RASCOM, the satellite organisation of African states. Its director general [spoke in Abidjan in July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>) about extending coverage of the country.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** RASCOM's director general [said on 31 July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>) that its services are already used in Côte d'Ivoire. He cited the state broadcaster RTI, which used RASCOM capacity during the 2023 Africa Cup of Nations. Guinea's ministry [lists an interconnection with Côte d'Ivoire among those working in 2025](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>), with no Ivorian party named. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
