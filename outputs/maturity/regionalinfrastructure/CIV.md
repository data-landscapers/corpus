# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Côte d'Ivoire's and Guinea's digital ministers [signed a protocol in Conakry on 11 October 2024](<https://www.gouv.ci/actualite/cooperation-deux-importants-protocoles-daccord-signes-entre-les-gouvernements-ivoirien-et-guineen-dans-le-secteur-numerique-2384>) to interconnect the two countries' fibre networks. No source reports works begun on it. The country also uses RASCOM, the satellite organisation of African states. Its director general [spoke in Abidjan in July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>) about extending coverage of the country.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** RASCOM's director general [said on 31 July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>) that its services are already used in Côte d'Ivoire. He cited the state broadcaster RTI, which used RASCOM capacity during the 2023 Africa Cup of Nations. Guinea's ministry [lists an interconnection with Côte d'Ivoire among those working in 2025](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>), with no Ivorian party named. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
