# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The pan-African satellite organisation RASCOM [counts Côte d'Ivoire among the countries that use its services](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>), as its director general said on 31 July 2026. The organisation serves the continent as a whole, and Côte d'Ivoire is one of the countries he named.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** RASCOM's director general [said on 31 July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643635/culture/communication-satellitaire-la-cote-divoire-mise-sur-rascom-pour-accelerer-la-couverture-numerique-du-territoire>) that the organisation's services are already in use in Côte d'Ivoire. His example was the national broadcaster, RTI, which used RASCOM satellite capacity during the Africa Cup of Nations in 2023. The user he names is Ivorian. He names no other country's operator using the same capacity, and gives no figure for what is carried.

***Also on file***. [SIPAO (Système d'information policière d'Afrique de l'Ouest)](<https://bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19393>).

***Not found***. No source found reports the link or facility carrying another country's traffic in the last three years: capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a user from the other country named.
