# Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** The Central African Backbone project [was still at work in January 2026](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>) extending Congo's fibre backbone to secondary towns and to neighbouring countries, as an analyst's brief of that month describes it.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The Central African Republic [inaugurated its fibre backbone on 6 February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>), and with it the interconnection with the Republic of Congo at Bomassa, in the presence of a representative of the Congolese telecommunications ministry. The 935 km of fibre were installed and working, and the African Development Bank said the component completed those of Cameroon and Congo. Cables [have also been laid across the Congo river](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>) to join Brazzaville to Kinshasa, as the January 2026 brief records.
