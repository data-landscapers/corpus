# Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** [A January 2026 analyst's brief says the Central African Backbone project was still extending Congo's fibre backbone](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>). The work reaches secondary towns and neighbouring countries. [The French Treasury's sheet of August 2024 says the World Bank finances the backbone](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** [The Central African Republic inaugurated its fibre backbone on 6 February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>), with the interconnection to Congo at Bomassa. [The French Treasury's sheet of August 2024 says Congo has been linked to Gabon since 2018](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>), under the Central African Backbone. The same sheet says Congo was linked to Cameroon in 2022. It also says a river cable has joined Brazzaville to Kinshasa since 2017. [The January 2026 brief also records cables laid across the Congo river](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>).
