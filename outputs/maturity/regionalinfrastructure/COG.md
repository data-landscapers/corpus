# Congo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** A January 2026 market brief [describes the Central African Backbone as ongoing](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>), extending Congo's fibre to secondary towns and to neighbours. Gabon's SPIN and Cameroon's CAMTEL [signed a memorandum on CAB-Corridor](<https://spin.ga/cab-corridor-spin-camtel-connectivite-afrique-centrale/>), published in October 2026, that would join Cameroon, Gabon and Congo. The Congolese party to that corridor is not named.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Under the Central African Backbone, Congo [has been interconnected with Gabon since 2018 and with Cameroon since 2022](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>), a French Treasury sheet says. The Central African Republic [inaugurated its interconnection with Congo at Bomassa in February 2023](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/infrastructures/0802-105283-inauguration-de-la-dorsale-optique-nationale-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-et-de-l-interconnexion-avec-le-congo>), with a Congolese ministry official present. A [river cable has joined Brazzaville to Kinshasa since 2017](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>), but no party on the Kinshasa side is named. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country under an agreement.
