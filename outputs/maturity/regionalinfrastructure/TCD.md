# Chad

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Chad's section of the trans-Saharan backbone [started works in 2023](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/11/projet-dorsale-transsaharienne-des-degradations-constatees-sur-le-site-technique-de-bol/>) with European Union money. It was [60% complete in February 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1302-52879-chad-plans-50-telecom-sites-50-km-of-fiber-in-n-djamena>). Chad told ITU-D in March 2026 that [its 503 km link to Niger is under construction](<https://www.itu.int/md/D26-SG01-C-0040/en>). A planned operator route to Cameroon [was suspended by the regulator](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>) in February 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Chad told ITU-D in March 2026 that its [35 km link from N'Djamena to Cameroon is working](<https://www.itu.int/md/D26-SG01-C-0040/en>). It is interconnected with the network of CAMTEL, Cameroon's state operator. Chad's regulator [also named a gateway with Sudan](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/connectivite-au-tchad-entre-vulnerabilite-et-ambitions-de-transformation-numerique/>), with no Sudanese party named. Libya [says its fibre will reach the Chadian border by the end of 2026](<https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/>), with no agreement named. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
