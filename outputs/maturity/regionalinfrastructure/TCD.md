# Chad

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Chad's part of the trans-Saharan fibre backbone, which is to join it to Niger and through Niger to the Mediterranean, [was 60 per cent complete in February 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1302-52879-chad-plans-50-telecom-sites-50-km-of-fiber-in-n-djamena>). Works [began in 2023 with €29 million from the European Union](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/11/projet-dorsale-transsaharienne-des-degradations-constatees-sur-le-site-technique-de-bol/>), [stood at 32 per cent in March 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/chad-urges-speed-on-delayed-trans-saharan-fiber-optic-project>), well behind a ten-month schedule, and [were found slowed, with one site vandalised, at an inspection in January 2026](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/11/projet-dorsale-transsaharienne-des-degradations-constatees-sur-le-site-technique-de-bol/>). [A Libyan project official said in October 2026 that Libya's fibre link to the Chadian border should be finished by the end of 2026, with the connection to Chad under way with Chadian authorities and operators](<https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The fibre route from N'Djamena to the Cameroon border [had not been started in February 2026](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>). The memorandum between two operators for it, signed in April 2025, was suspended by Chad's regulator over clauses touching sovereignty, and the government asked the operators on 9 February 2026 to reopen talks.
