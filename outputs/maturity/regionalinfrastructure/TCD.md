# Chad

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** [Chad's part of the trans-Saharan fibre backbone was 60 per cent complete in February 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1302-52879-chad-plans-50-telecom-sites-50-km-of-fiber-in-n-djamena>). The backbone is to join Chad to Niger and, through Niger, to the Mediterranean. [Works began in 2023 with €29 million from the European Union](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/11/projet-dorsale-transsaharienne-des-degradations-constatees-sur-le-site-technique-de-bol/>). [They stood at 32 per cent in March 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/chad-urges-speed-on-delayed-trans-saharan-fiber-optic-project>), well behind a ten-month schedule. [In March 2026 Chad told ITU-D that the 503 km link to Niger was under construction](<https://www.itu.int/md/D26-SG01-C-0040/en>). [In October 2026 a Libyan project official said Libya's fibre to the Chadian border should be finished by the end of 2026](<https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** [In March 2026 Chad told ITU-D that a 35 km fibre link from N'Djamena to Cameroon was operational](<https://www.itu.int/md/D26-SG01-C-0040/en>). It joins the CAMTEL network and the SAT3 cable, and Chad called it the country's only working link to the internet. [A separate route from N'Djamena to the Cameroon border at Mbéré had not been started in February 2026](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/chad-tells-operators-to-fast-track-fiber-link-to-cameroon>). Chad's regulator had suspended the operators' memorandum for it over clauses touching sovereignty.
