# Egypt

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Egypt. The arrangement on record is older. Telecom Egypt and Sudatel [signed a cooperation agreement in April 2021](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html>) to make better use of the fibre joining the two countries. They also planned to join Sudatel's Port Sudan landing to Telecom Egypt's Red Sea landings.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Egypt's telecommunications minister [launched a 400 km fibre cable to Sudan on 26 October 2004](<https://sudantribune.com/article/6899>). It was built by the Egyptian Telecommunications Company for $4 million to carry calls and internet between the two countries. The [2021 agreement with Sudatel](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html>) names the Sudanese operator using it. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says a new shared project was agreed in the last three years.
