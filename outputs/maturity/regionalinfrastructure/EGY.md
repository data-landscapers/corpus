# Egypt

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Telecom Egypt and Sudatel [signed a cooperation agreement in April 2021](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html>). It covers international traffic over the fibre that joins the two countries. The two operators also planned to link Sudatel's landing station at Port Sudan to Telecom Egypt's landing stations on the Red Sea.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Egypt's telecommunications minister [opened a 400 km fibre-optic cable to Sudan in October 2004](<https://sudantribune.com/article/6899>). The Egyptian Telecommunications Company built it in one year for US$4 million, to carry telephone and internet traffic between the two countries. Its president said the cable would serve millions of Sudanese and give Sudan a route to the world through Egypt. The [2021 agreement with Sudatel](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/10989-telecom-egypt-and-sudatel-plan-connectivity-cooperation.html>) describes the same fibre as connecting Egypt and Sudan.
