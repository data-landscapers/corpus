# Lesotho

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** South Africa's state wholesaler Broadband Infraco [said in July 2019](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>) that its regional network reached the borders of all six land neighbours. Lesotho is one of those six countries. The company was then upgrading its route to Botswana and adding redundancy to it. It said it expected to take a leading role in a planned regional fibre project, the SADC superhighway.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Broadband Infraco [said in July 2019](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/broadband-infraco-intensifies-capacity-to-platinum-belt/LPwQ57lynQmMNgkj>) that it provided connectivity to all six neighbouring countries, Lesotho included. Its chief executive said the company acted as the gateway to international capacity for South Africa's landlocked neighbours. Lesotho, surrounded by South Africa, is one of them.
