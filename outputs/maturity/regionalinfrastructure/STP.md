# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** São Tomé and Príncipe [joined the consortium of the Africa Coast to Europe cable in 2010](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), through a construction and maintenance agreement signed by its telecommunications company. The cable [arrived in the country in November 2011](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), on a World Bank grant that took effect that July.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The cable [was commercially launched in February 2013](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), after tests across the sixteen countries it connects. The segment from Côte d'Ivoire to São Tomé and Príncipe was among those in service in its first phase, and the landing station in São Tomé had been inaugurated in October 2012. The cable links Europe with the west and southern African coast, [Gabon and Equatorial Guinea among its other landings](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>).
