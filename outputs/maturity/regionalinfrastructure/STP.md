# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for São Tomé and Príncipe. The arrangement on record is the ACE cable consortium. The national operator CST [joined the consortium in 2010](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>) by signing its construction and maintenance agreement.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The ACE cable [was commercially launched in February 2013](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>) after tests across the 16 countries it connects. A World Bank report says so. The segment from Côte d'Ivoire to São Tomé was among those in service. The landing station was inaugurated in October 2012. The cable [arrived in São Tomé in November 2011](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), with a World Bank grant. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
