# Kenya

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Kenya's and Tanzania's governments [launched a cross-border project at the Lunga Lunga and Horohoro border on 20 July 2025](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/kenya-and-tanzania-launch-terrestrial-fiber-link-connection/>), to join Kenya's national fibre backbone and its submarine cable landings to Tanzania's network. Kenya's ICT Authority and Tanzania's state telecommunications company carry it out.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A company [completed a fibre network from Mombasa to Kampala in May 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/east-african-countries-to-launch-regional-satellite>), and Tanzania's state telecommunications company and Kenya's ICT Authority [put a terrestrial fibre link from Dar es Salaam to Mombasa into operation in July 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/east-african-countries-to-launch-regional-satellite>). A 2,000 km route from Mombasa to Goma, through Nairobi, Kampala and Kigali, [was activated in February 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>), and its owner [said it was already carrying traffic for its first wholesale customers](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>).
