# Kenya

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Kenya's and Tanzania's ministers [launched a cross-border project at the Lunga Lunga and Horohoro border on 20 July 2025](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/kenya-and-tanzania-launch-terrestrial-fiber-link-connection/>). It joins Kenya's national backbone and sea cables with Tanzania's. Kenya's ICT Authority and Tanzania's TTCL carry it out. Kenya and Ethiopia [committed on 5 June 2025, under IGAD](<https://igad.int/joint-communique-between-the-federal-democratic-republic-of-ethiopia-and-the-republic-of-kenya-on-the-horn-of-africa-gateway-development-project-hoagdp-convened-under-the-auspices-of-the-intergovern/>), to complete their cross-border fibre connection. Uganda and Kenya [have completed a fibre connection at Busia](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>), PML Daily reported in August 2026. An agreement between NITA-U and the ICT Authority is still to be finalised there. A World Bank report counts [a cross-border connectivity memorandum between Kenya and South Sudan](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>) as signed by June 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** TTCL and Kenya's ICT Authority [launched a fibre link between Dar es Salaam and Mombasa in July 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/east-african-countries-to-launch-regional-satellite>). Tanzania's [Daily News reported it running at 1.6 terabits a second](<https://dailynews.co.tz/new-fibre-link-to-boost-east-africas-digital-resilience/>), with Kenyan companies using it. Operators from Rwanda and Uganda [are members of the Kenya Internet Exchange Point](<https://ws.nog.ke/lib/exe/fetch.php?media=kixp_presentaion_-kenog_may_2024_-_compatibility_mode.pdf>), a 2024 deck shows. Foreign members joining an exchange are commercial use. No data facility run with another country is on record.
