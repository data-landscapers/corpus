# Kenya

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** On 20 July 2025 Kenya and Tanzania [launched a cross-border project at the Lunga Lunga and Horohoro border](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/kenya-and-tanzania-launch-terrestrial-fiber-link-connection/>). It joins Kenya's backbone and cable landings to Tanzania's network. Kenya's ICT Authority and Tanzania's state telecoms company carry it out.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** In May 2024 the Kenya Internet Exchange Point [listed operators from Rwanda and Uganda as members](<https://ws.nog.ke/lib/exe/fetch.php?media=kixp_presentaion_-kenog_may_2024_-_compatibility_mode.pdf>). BSC-Rwanda and Roke Telkom are among them. A fibre network [from Mombasa to Kampala was completed in May 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/east-african-countries-to-launch-regional-satellite>). A link from Dar es Salaam to Mombasa went into service in July 2025. In February 2026 Paratus [switched on a 2,000 km route from Mombasa to Goma](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). It carries traffic for its first wholesale customers. iColo says [Mombasa's cables and data centres serve Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and South Sudan](<https://www.icolo.io/news/locations/mba1/>).
