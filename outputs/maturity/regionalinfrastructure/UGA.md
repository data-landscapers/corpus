# Uganda

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** In May 2023 a development fund and a telecoms group [announced a terrestrial fibre network of some US$320 million](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>). It would link landlocked countries to sea cables on Africa's coasts. Uganda is among the countries named.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A subsidiary of that telecommunications group [completed a fibre network linking Mombasa, in Kenya, to Kampala in May 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/east-african-countries-to-launch-regional-satellite>). Uganda has no coast, and the network is a route to the Kenyan port owned by a single company from end to end. [A second account](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/partnerships/east-african-telcos-ink-cross-border-fiber-network-deal>) gives the same month and the same two cities. Neither gives the network's capacity, a figure for traffic, or the name of a Ugandan operator that buys capacity on it. In February 2026 Paratus [switched on its 2,000 km route from Mombasa to Goma through Kampala](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-lights-up-new-east-africa-fibre-highway-linking-goma-to-mombasa/wbrpOqg2aJQMDLZn>). It said the route already carried traffic for its first wholesale customers, who are not named.
