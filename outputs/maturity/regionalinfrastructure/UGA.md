# Uganda

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Uganda and Rwanda [made the final fibre connection at the Mirama Hills border](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>) earlier in 2026, under the Northern Corridor Integration Projects. The same report says Uganda and Kenya have completed a connection at Busia. An agreement between Uganda's NITA-U and Kenya's ICT Authority is still to be finalised there. NITA-U and Tanzania's TTCL [signed a memorandum on 29 September 2023](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/questions-swirl-over-internet-deal-between-uganda-tanzania-5654914>) to join Uganda's backbone to Tanzania's fibre loop. On 11 May 2026 Uganda's ICT ministry and the Congolese telecoms ministry [signed a memorandum](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/uganda-and-drc-deepen-digital-and-regional-cooperation>). One of its projects joins Uganda's backbone to Congolese fibre.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says a shared link is in service. Operators must still [agree capacity at Mirama Hills and finalise the Busia agreement](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/08/uganda-rwanda-complete-fiber-link-at-mirama-hills-border.html>) before commercial service. In November 2026 the Monitor [found little record of what the Tanzania memorandum led to](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/questions-swirl-over-internet-deal-between-uganda-tanzania-5654914>). No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
