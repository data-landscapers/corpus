# Ethiopia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Ethio telecom, Djibouti Telecom and Sudatel [signed a tripartite agreement on 4 February 2026](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign/>) for Horizon, a fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landings across Ethiopia to Sudan's. It builds on a memorandum signed in Addis Ababa on 3 December 2024. In August 2026 Ethio telecom [moved from planning into active deployment](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/>). Its design was final and site materials had been bought and delivered.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Horizon is not yet in service. In September 2026 the three operators [presented it to carriers in Addis Ababa ahead of commercial launch](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>). An analysis that month [found no capacity sale, customer or service date announced](<https://btw.media/ar/horizon-fiber-masar-badil-huquq-saa>). A Djibouti Telecom profile [lists a land cable to Ethiopia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>), but names no Ethiopian party to it. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Djibouti Telecom land cable to Ethiopia](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>).

***Not found***. No source found names an Ethiopian party to a cross-border link already working. None names a shared exchange point or data centre.
