# Ethiopia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Ethio telecom, Djibouti Telecom and Sudatel [signed a tripartite agreement in Djibouti on 4 February 2026](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign/>) for Horizon, a terrestrial fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landing stations across Ethiopia to Sudan's, [building on a memorandum signed in Addis Ababa on 3 December 2024](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign/>). Ethio telecom [had moved from planning to deployment by 20 August 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/>), with the design settled and materials bought and delivered to site.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The three operators [presented Horizon to large cloud companies, carriers and suppliers in Addis Ababa on 7 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>), in a phase before commercial launch, to gather interest before the corridor is deployed and opened. The corridor [had no announced buyer of capacity and no date for service](<https://btw.media/ar/horizon-fiber-masar-badil-huquq-saa>) three days later.
