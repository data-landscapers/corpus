# Ethiopia

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Ethio telecom, Djibouti Telecom and Sudatel [signed a tripartite agreement in Djibouti on 4 February 2026](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/ethio-telecom-djibouti-telecom-and-sudatel-group-sign/>). It is for Horizon, a land fibre corridor from Djibouti's cable landings across Ethiopia to Sudan. It built on a memorandum signed in Addis Ababa on 3 December 2024. By 20 August 2026 [Ethio telecom had moved from planning to deployment](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/>), with materials delivered to site.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A January 2026 profile of Djibouti Telecom [lists a land cable between Djibouti and Ethiopia in its network](<https://www.lanation.dj/du-hub-regional-au-catalyseur-continental-la-nouvelle-ere-de-djibouti-telecom/>). Horizon is not yet in service. The operators [presented it to cloud companies and carriers in Addis Ababa on 7 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/projet-de-fibre-optique-horizon-une-nouvelle-autoroute-numerique-pour-la-corne-de-lafrique/>), before its commercial launch. Three days later it [had no announced buyer of capacity and no service date](<https://btw.media/ar/horizon-fiber-masar-badil-huquq-saa>).
