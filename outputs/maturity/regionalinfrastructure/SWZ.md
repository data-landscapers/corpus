# Eswatini

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Eswatini's universal access fund and the national backbone operator [set up an alternative route to the east](<https://uasf.esccom.org.sz/programmes/connectivity-programmes/>), joining the national backbone to Maputo, in Mozambique, over the fibre strung on a regional power transmission line. The fund bought the equipment needed to connect to that fibre. Its account of the work was published on 3 October 2023.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The fund's [account of the Maputo route](<https://uasf.esccom.org.sz/programmes/connectivity-programmes/>) describes it as an alternative route for the national backbone, running east. Eswatini has no coast, and all its international traffic leaves through a neighbour. The fund reports the equipment bought and the route set up. It gives no date on which traffic began to run over it, no capacity, and no figure for what the route carries.
