# Botswana

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Namibia's MTC and Botswana's BoFiNet [signed a memorandum of understanding in Gaborone](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/02/mtc-bofinet-sign-agreement-to-strengthen-namibia-botswana-digital-connectivity/>), reported on 18 February 2026. It provides for cooperation on cross-border fibre interconnection, internet transit and wholesale capacity, and for the development of the corridor between Buitepos, on the Namibian border, and Ngoma. The memorandum is between the two companies, and names the corridor as work to be developed.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** A fibre route into Zimbabwe from the Botswana border [was live and carrying traffic on 3 July 2026](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>). The two companies behind it describe the section from Plumtree to Bulawayo as the first active route of its network into Zimbabwe and as linking Zimbabwe to Botswana.

***Also on file***. [BoFiNet fibre network](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>); [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>).

***Not found***. No source found reports the link or facility carrying another country's traffic in the last three years: capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a user from the other country named.
