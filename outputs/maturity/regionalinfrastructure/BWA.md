# Botswana

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Namibia's MTC and Botswana's state fibre company BoFiNet [signed a memorandum in February 2026](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/02/mtc-bofinet-sign-agreement-to-strengthen-namibia-botswana-digital-connectivity/>). It covers cross-border fibre interconnection, IP transit and wholesale capacity. It also covers developing the Buitepos to Ngoma corridor. BoFiNet [says the two had recently completed cross-border fibre interconnections](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/index.php/news/article/bofinet-signs-mou-with-mtc-namibia-to-further-bolster-connectivity-reliability-for-botswana>) at Mamuno to Charles Hill and at Ngoma when they signed. Both companies are their governments' fibre carriers, so the memorandum binds a body on each side.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** BoFiNet's acting chief executive [said in May 2025](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/05/how-bofinet-plans-to-transform-botswana.html>) that it has fibre connections to all of Botswana's neighbours. The source names no party in those countries. BoFiNet [reaches exchange points in London and Johannesburg](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bofinet-invests-p100m-in-upgrading-botswanas-internet-gateway/news>) as a customer. A [Paratus and Powertel route in Zimbabwe](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>) is described as linking to Botswana, with no Botswana party named.
