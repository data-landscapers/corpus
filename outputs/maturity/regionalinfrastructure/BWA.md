# Botswana

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** [Namibia's MTC and Botswana's BoFiNet signed a memorandum of understanding in Gaborone](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/02/mtc-bofinet-sign-agreement-to-strengthen-namibia-botswana-digital-connectivity/>), reported on 18 February 2026. It covers cross-border fibre, internet transit and wholesale capacity. It also covers a fibre corridor from Buitepos, on the Namibian border, to Ngoma, which is still to be developed.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** [In May 2025 BoFiNet's acting chief executive said it had fibre links to all of Botswana's neighbours](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/05/how-bofinet-plans-to-transform-botswana.html>). A major route runs through South Africa. [A fibre route into Zimbabwe from the Botswana border was live and carrying traffic on 3 July 2026](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-zimbabwe-and-powertel-switch-on-new-digital-highway/>). Its builders call the section from Plumtree to Bulawayo their first active route into Zimbabwe. They say it links Zimbabwe to Botswana.

***Also on file***. [BoFiNet fibre network](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>); [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>).

***Not found***. No source found reports a link or facility carrying another country's traffic in the last three years. That would be capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a named user in the other country.
