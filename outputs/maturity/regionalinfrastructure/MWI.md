# Malawi

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Africa50 and Bayobab (MTN Group) [partnered on 15 May 2023](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>) to develop Project East2West, a US$320 million to US$325 million terrestrial fibre network joining landlocked countries to the subsea cables on the east and west coasts. Malawi is among the countries the document names as covered, and it reports no start of construction.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** TNM, a Malawian mobile operator, [says its internet runs over cross-border fibre links](<https://malawi24.com/2024/05/21/tnm-invests-mk4-billion-restores-internet-disrupted-by-sea-cable-cuts/>) to Zambia, through Mchinji and Chipata, and to Mozambique, through Muloza and Mulanje. The operator restored or acquired both links in May 2024, after sea cable cuts disrupted its service, and the report does not say whether either link carries traffic for a user in Zambia or Mozambique.
