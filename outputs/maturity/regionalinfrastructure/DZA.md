# Algeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Algérie Télécom and Niger Télécom [signed a convention in Niamey on 8 September 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-telecom-algerie-telecom-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-des-infrastructures-telecoms-du-niger/>). Under it the Algerian operator gives fibre transmission equipment to set up a link from Agadez to Assamaka, on the Algerian border, with a first capacity of 100 gigabits a second, and the Nigerien operator took delivery of the equipment. On 5 October 2026 Niger's communications ministry [reported its minister and an Algerian technical delegation in Agadez](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/cooperation-niger-algerie-arrivee-du-ministre-hamani-kargne-a-agadez-pour-le-lancement-des-travaux-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique/>) to launch the works of the fibre interconnection. In June 2026 Algeria and Tunisia [agreed to upgrade the existing terrestrial link between them](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The two countries' ministers [launched the operation of the trans-Saharan fibre link at Assamaka on 6 October 2026](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/06/lalgerie-et-le-niger-lancent-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>), joining In-Guezzam in Algeria to the Arlit and Agadez region of Niger at 100 gigabits a second from the start, to carry data traffic between the two countries. Niger's ministry [called the link operational](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>) the same day, and the [national press agency](<https://anp.ne/niger-algerie-lancement-a-assamaka-de-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>) reported the launch.
