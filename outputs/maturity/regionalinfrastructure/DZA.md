# Algeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Algérie Télécom and Niger Télécom [signed a convention in Niamey on 8 September 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-telecom-algerie-telecom-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-des-infrastructures-telecoms-du-niger/>). Under it the Algerian operator gives fibre transmission equipment to set up a link from Agadez to Assamaka, on the Algerian border, with a first capacity of 100 gigabits a second, and the Nigerien operator took delivery of the equipment.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The two countries' ministers [launched the operation of the trans-Saharan fibre link at Assamaka on 6 October 2026](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/06/lalgerie-et-le-niger-lancent-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>), joining In-Guezzam in Algeria to the Arlit and Agadez region of Niger at 100 gigabits a second. Niger's ministry [called the link operational](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>) the same day. Both events fall after 30 September 2026.
