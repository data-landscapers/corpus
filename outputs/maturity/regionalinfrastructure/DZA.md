# Algeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Algérie Télécom and Niger Télécom [signed a convention in Niamey on 8 September 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-telecom-algerie-telecom-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-des-infrastructures-telecoms-du-niger/>). The Algerian operator gives the equipment for a link from Agadez to Assamaka, on the border, at 100 gigabits a second. On 5 October 2026 Niger's ministry [reported the launch of the works in Agadez](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/cooperation-niger-algerie-arrivee-du-ministre-hamani-kargne-a-agadez-pour-le-lancement-des-travaux-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique/>). In June 2026 Algeria and Tunisia [agreed to upgrade the land link between them](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** On 6 October 2026 the two ministers [opened the trans-Saharan fibre link at Assamaka](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/06/lalgerie-et-le-niger-lancent-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). It joins In-Guezzam in Algeria to Arlit and Agadez in Niger at 100 gigabits a second. Niger's ministry [called the link operational](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>) the same day, as did the [national press agency](<https://anp.ne/niger-algerie-lancement-a-assamaka-de-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). In November 2024 Algérie Télécom said [its network already joins neighbours such as Tunisia by land cable](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session3/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20Interconnexion%20UIT_MrAmirKahli.pdf>).
