# Algeria

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Algérie Télécom and Niger Telecom [signed a convention in Niamey on 8 September 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-telecom-algerie-telecom-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-des-infrastructures-telecoms-du-niger/>). Under it Algeria gives equipment for a 100 gigabit link from Agadez to Assamaka. Niger's minister [arrived in Agadez on 5 October 2026](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/cooperation-niger-algerie-arrivee-du-ministre-hamani-kargne-a-agadez-pour-le-lancement-des-travaux-dinterconnexion-par-fibre-optique/>) with Algeria's ambassador to launch the works. In June 2026 the Algeria and Tunisia joint commission [agreed to upgrade the land link between them](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0806-56258-algeria-tunisia-plan-new-submarine-cable-to-expand-digital-connectivity-with-europe>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** The ministers of Algeria and Niger [opened the trans-Saharan fibre link at Assamaka on 6 October 2026](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/10/06/lalgerie-et-le-niger-lancent-lexploitation-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). It joins In-Guezzam to the Arlit and Agadez region at up to 100 gigabits a second. Niger's ministry [declared it officially in service](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>). Algérie Télécom [has said its network reaches neighbours such as Tunisia by land cable](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session3/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20Interconnexion%20UIT_MrAmirKahli.pdf>). That source names no Tunisian party. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
