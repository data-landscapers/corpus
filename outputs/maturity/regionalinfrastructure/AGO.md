# Angola

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Angola and Mozambique [signed a memorandum in June 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) under which Mozambique's Tmcel and TVM would use Angola's Angosat-2 satellite. A commercial agreement was still to be formalised. Angola's space programme office also [signed a pilot agreement with Telecom Namibia](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) to test backhaul over the satellite. Neither source reports a contract for works or a start date for service.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says Angosat-2, or any other facility, carries another country's service under an arrangement. The Namibian pilot, [reported in August 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>), is a test. Angola is named among the countries an [East to West fibre partnership of 2023](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>) would cover. Its government is not a party to that partnership, which belongs to two companies.

***Also on file***. [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>); [Paratus data centre and fibre routes](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/paratus-group-to-build-third-angola-data-center/>).

***Not found***. No source found says building has begun on shared infrastructure. None says any is in service between Angola and another African country.
