# Angola

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Angola and Mozambique [signed a memorandum in June 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) under which Mozambique's state telecommunications operator and state broadcaster would carry their signals over Angola's ANGOSAT-2 satellite, with a commercial agreement still to be made final. Angola's space programme office [signed a commercial pilot agreement with Telecom Namibia](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>), reported in August 2026, to test the satellite as backhaul for the Namibian network. On land, a company [secured a US$31 million loan in November 2023](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/paratus-group-to-build-third-angola-data-center/>) to pay for a data centre in Luanda and three fibre routes, one of them from Luanda through Lusaka to Dar es Salaam.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The [pilot with Telecom Namibia](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) is a test of the satellite on a working network, and the [memorandum with Mozambique](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) awaits its commercial agreement.

***Also on file***. [WACS, SACS and MONET submarine cables](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fiber-networking/angola-cables-uniti-partnership-to-benefit-african-enterprises>); [Paratus Angola DC3](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/paratus-group-to-build-third-angola-data-center/>).
