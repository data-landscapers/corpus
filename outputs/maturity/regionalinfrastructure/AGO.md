# Angola

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Angola Telecom and Telecom Namibia [signed a memorandum in Swakopmund in March 2026](<https://pti.ao/angola-telecom-preve-operacao-do-cabo-submarino-sarssy-ate-2027/>) for the SARSSy sea cable. It would land at five Angolan points and join the Equiano cable in Namibia, with service due in June 2027. Angola's and Mozambique's ministers [signed a memorandum on 22 June 2026](<https://www.oeconomico.com/mocambique-e-angola-avancam-com-cabo-nacala-lobito-para-reforcar-corredor-digital-regional/>) for a sea cable from Nacala to Lobito, still at the concession stage. Another June 2026 memorandum [would let Mozambique's Tmcel and TVM use Angola's Angosat-2 satellite](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>). Angola's space programme office also [signed a pilot agreement with Telecom Namibia](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>) to test backhaul over the satellite. No contract for works on any of these is reported.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** A terrestrial fibre link already joins Santa Clara in Angola to Namibia, Angola Telecom's executive director [said in July 2026](<https://pti.ao/angola-telecom-preve-operacao-do-cabo-submarino-sarssy-ate-2027/>). He said Angola Telecom and Telecom Namibia carry services over it in both directions. The satellite pilot with Namibia, [reported in August 2026](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/tmcel-begins-angosat-2-satellite-trials/JN1gPvOAwolMjL6m>), is a test. Angola is named among the countries an [East to West fibre partnership of 2023](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>) would cover. Its government is not a party to that partnership, which belongs to two companies.
