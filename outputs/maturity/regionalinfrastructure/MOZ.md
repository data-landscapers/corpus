# Mozambique

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** In May 2023 a development fund and a telecoms group [announced a terrestrial fibre network of some US$320 million](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>). It would link landlocked countries to sea cables on both coasts. Mozambique is among the twelve countries named.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Zambia and Mozambique [commissioned a fibre interconnector between Chanida and Cassacatiza](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>), reported on 23 February 2026. It joins Zambia's backbone to the sea cables that land in Mozambique. For Zambia, which has no coast, it is a route to the sea. In October 2025 the research network MoRENet said [its international capacity rose from 5 to 60 gigabits a second](<https://africarxiv.ubuntunet.net/bitstreams/9427cb02-feef-4b3f-b03b-84cb4efea3cb/download>). The capacity runs through a new point of presence in Cape Town, linked to South Africa's research networks.
