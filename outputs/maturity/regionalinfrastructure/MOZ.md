# Mozambique

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Mozambique's communications minister [launched the interconnectivity project with Zambia at Cassacatiza on 20 February 2026](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-e-zambia-avancam-com-interligacao-digital-transfronteirica-em-chifunde/>). It is a cross-border fibre partnership of Vodacom Moçambique and Bayobab Zambia. Both countries' communications regulators are involved. Zambia's science and technology minister [spoke at the ceremony](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>). Mozambique's and Angola's ministers [signed a memorandum on 22 June 2026](<https://www.oeconomico.com/mocambique-e-angola-avancam-com-cabo-nacala-lobito-para-reforcar-corredor-digital-regional/>) for a sea cable from Nacala to Lobito, still at the concession stage.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Zambia and Mozambique [officially launched the Chanida to Cassacatiza fibre interconnector](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/zambia-and-mozambique-launch-fibre-optic-interconnector/>), reported on 23 February 2026. It joins Zambia's backbone to Mozambique's sea cable network. Mozambique's research network MoRENet [raised its international capacity from 5 to 60 Gbps](<https://africarxiv.ubuntunet.net/bitstreams/9427cb02-feef-4b3f-b03b-84cb4efea3cb/download>) through a new border point in Cape Town. That point is interconnected with South Africa's research networks TENET and SANReN. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None names a second kind of shared infrastructure in service.
