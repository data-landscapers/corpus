# Senegal

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** No agreement, memorandum or financing for new shared infrastructure, dated in the last three years, has been found for Senegal. Its links run under operator and backbone arrangements reported when they opened. Senegal's SENUM and Mali's SMTD [inaugurated their backbone interconnection in October 2026](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>), for example.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** SENUM and SMTD [joined their national backbones at the Kidira and Diboli border on 5 October 2026](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>). Gambia's Gamtel [holds 28 Gbps with the Senegalese operator Free on a backup link](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). Sonatel and Phase3 [activated a fibre route from Dakar to Lagos in May 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>). The ROA-BN backbone network's chairman [said Senegal's traffic can transit Mali](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>), with a backup through Guinea. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country. None says a new shared project was agreed in the last three years.
