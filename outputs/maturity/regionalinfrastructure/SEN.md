# Senegal

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Senegal and Guinea [signed a memorandum at the Transform Africa summit in Conakry in November 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3141/senegal-and-guinea-sign-digital-cooperation-agreement-at-transform-africa-summit-2025>). Under it they will work on joining their telecommunications networks and share expertise on data centres. No contract or works under it are reported. Senegal's other links run under operator and backbone arrangements reported when they opened.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** SENUM and SMTD [joined their national backbones at the Kidira and Diboli border on 5 October 2026](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>). Gambia's Gamtel [holds 28 Gbps with the Senegalese operator Free on a backup link](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>). Sonatel and Phase3 [activated a fibre route from Dakar to Lagos in May 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>). The ROA-BN backbone network's chairman [said Senegal's traffic can transit Mali](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>), with a backup through Guinea. Senegal also [joined the backbone of WACREN](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), the regional research and education network, in 2025. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
