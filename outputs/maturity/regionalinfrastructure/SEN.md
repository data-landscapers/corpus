# Senegal

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Sonatel and the Nigerian fibre company Phase3 [built a terrestrial fibre route of 3,500 km between Dakar and Lagos](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>), crossing Ghana, Togo and Benin. Senegal's state backbone company and Mali's transmission company [joined their national fibre backbones at the Kidira to Diboli bridge](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>). The link was inaugurated on 5 October 2026.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Gamtel, the Gambian national operator, [holds 28 gigabits a second with the Senegalese operator Free](<https://gamtel.gm/about-us/>) on a link named Cross Gambia (CG2). Gamtel's traffic is rerouted over it automatically when the ACE submarine cable fails. Its page carries no date and was captured on 21 September 2026. The Dakar to Lagos route [was activated in May 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>), and the link to Mali [was put into service at its inauguration](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>).
