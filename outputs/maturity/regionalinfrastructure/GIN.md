# Guinea

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Guinea and Sierra Leone [signed an agreement in November 2024](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>) to interconnect their national backbones. Guinea's backbone company SOGEB and Sierra Leone's Leoncom then [signed an interconnection agreement in Conakry](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/>), published in June 2025, to link at Pamelap. Guinea's and Côte d'Ivoire's ministers [signed a protocol in Conakry on 11 October 2024](<https://www.gouv.ci/actualite/cooperation-deux-importants-protocoles-daccord-signes-entre-les-gouvernements-ivoirien-et-guineen-dans-le-secteur-numerique-2384>) to interconnect their fibre networks. In June 2024 SOGEB and Mali's SMTD [agreed to interconnect the two countries' networks](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>).

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Guinea's telecoms ministry says [four cross-border interconnections were working in 2025](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>), with Mali, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire and Sierra Leone. The West African network of national backbones, ROA-BN, [opened its headquarters in Conakry on 10 November 2025](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>). Its chairman said Mali is connected to Guinea within the working eight-country network. Guinea also [joined the backbone of WACREN](<https://wacren.net/en/news/wacren-connects-six-new-countries-and-sets-foundation-for-climate-programme/>), the regional research and education network, in 2025. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.
