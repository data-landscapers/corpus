# Guinea

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** In June 2024 Guinea's backbone company and Mali's SMTD [agreed to join the two countries' fibre networks](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>). Guinea and Sierra Leone [signed an agreement in November 2024](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>) to join their backbones. Guinea's backbone company and its Sierra Leonean counterpart then [signed an interconnection at Pamelap](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/12188-guinea-and-sierra-leone-link-national-fibre-networks-for-regional-digital-integration/>), reported in June 2025, at 42.5 gigabits a second.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The telecoms ministry says [four cross-border interconnections were working by 2025](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>). They join Guinea to Mali, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire and Sierra Leone. On 10 November 2025 the West African backbone network [opened its headquarters in Conakry](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>). Its chairman said the network of eight countries is working and Mali is connected to Guinea.
