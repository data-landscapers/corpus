# South Sudan

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** A World Bank report [counts a cross-border connectivity memorandum between Kenya and South Sudan as signed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>), by June 2026. Under the regional integration project, South Sudan [was to tender fibre to the Kenyan and Sudanese borders](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099120225134529047/txt/P176181-10a2aeb5-d3fb-4c67-bc53-da109b62a9de.txt>), an August 2025 mission says. Bids for [397 km on the Juba to Nadapal route](<https://southsudanpress.com/analysis-south-sudan-fibre-juba-nadapal-procurement.html>) closed on 26 October 2026.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says shared infrastructure is in service. Bayobab [finished laying fibre on its Kenya and South Sudan segments in October 2026](<https://tech.africa/bayobab-east-west-fibre-south-sudan/>), with activation to come. It is one company's network, under a licence rather than an agreement with Kenya. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Bayobab East to West route](<https://tech.africa/bayobab-east-west-fibre-south-sudan/>); [Project East2West](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/africa50-and-mtn-globalconnect-announce-a-partnership-to-develop-a-us320-million-pan-african-terrestrial-fibre-optic-cable-connecting-10-countries-project-east2west/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a contract was awarded or works began on the regional project's fibre to Kenya.
