# South Sudan

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** South Sudan's ministry [invited international bids](<https://southsudanpress.com/analysis-south-sudan-fibre-juba-nadapal-procurement.html>), reported on 14 September 2026, for about 397 km of terrestrial fibre from Juba to Nadapal, towards the Kenyan border, paid for by a World Bank grant under a regional project. The same project [counts the first memorandum on cross-border digital connectivity between Kenya and South Sudan as signed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026190082191/txt/P176181-84d9770b-853c-4bb8-8e5a-996eac681382.txt>), in its report of June 2026. South Sudan also [gave a company a fifteen-year licence in October 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/27/why-connecting-landlocked-africa-requires-more-than-building-national-fibre-networks/>) to build and run national and cross-border fibre networks.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The [Juba to Nadapal route](<https://southsudanpress.com/analysis-south-sudan-fibre-juba-nadapal-procurement.html>) is at the stage of procurement, with bids closing on 26 October 2026. The works it would pay for have not been let.
