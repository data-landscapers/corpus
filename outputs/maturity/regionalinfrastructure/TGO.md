# Togo

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** CSquared, Phase3 and SBIN [announced on 6 May 2025](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>) a new terrestrial fibre route from Lagos to Accra through Ghana, Togo and Benin. The announcement describes the route as configured, activated and tested, and gives no date yet for it to be ready for service. It adds a path between Nigeria and Ghana that crosses Togo, announced jointly by CSquared with Phase3 and SBIN.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** CSquared's chief executive [said in March 2024](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/exclusive-equianos-togo-landing-station-sees-4x-increase-in-traffic/>) that the company had connected its cross-border circuits from Togo into Ghana and Benin. He said it had provided capacity to MTN Ghana and Vodafone Ghana through the Equiano landing station in Lomé, according to the March 2024 report in Capacity.
