# Benin

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** CSquared, Phase3 and SBIN [announced on 6 May 2025](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>) a new terrestrial fibre route from Lagos to Accra, running through Ghana, Togo and Benin. The three companies are the parties named to the route. They describe it as configured, activated and tested, and the 6 May 2025 announcement is the latest word held on the route.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The same [announcement](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>) says the date on which the route will be ready for service has not yet been announced, so the route stands tested and awaiting a service date, as that announcement states. The announcement reports no traffic carried across a border, no capacity sold and no user from another country.
