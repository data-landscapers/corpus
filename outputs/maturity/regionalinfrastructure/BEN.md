# Benin

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Benin's state infrastructure company SBIN, with CSquared and the Nigerian operator Phase3, [announced a fibre route from Lagos to Accra in May 2025](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>). It runs through Benin, Togo and Ghana. The partners said it was configured, activated and tested. They gave no date for it to enter service. SBIN, owned by the state, is Benin's party to the route, beside operators from Nigeria and Ghana.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** No source found says the [Lagos to Accra route](<https://csquared.com/csquared-phase3-and-sbin-commission-lagos-to-accra-terrestrial-fibre-route-strengthening-west-africas-digital-resilience/>) has entered service. No other link, exchange point or data facility run by Benin with another African country is on record. Benin lies on a [Lagos to Dakar route that Phase3 and Sonatel activated in 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/09/west-africas-digital-backbone-phase3-and-sonatel-launch-3500km-fibre-route/>), but no Beninese party to it is named.
