# Seychelles

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** Seychelles Cable Systems, owned by the government and two operators, [signed a contract in 2018 for a branch of the PEACE cable](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/16417/seychelles-achieves-security-in-international-connectivity-peace-operational-in-seychelles>) to reach the islands. Laying of the system was completed in October 2022. The company paid for the PEACE branch itself; the African Development Bank and the European Investment Bank had helped finance SEAS.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Operators in Seychelles [could carry traffic over PEACE from 16 November 2022](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/16417/seychelles-achieves-security-in-international-connectivity-peace-operational-in-seychelles>). The 15,000 km cable connects Pakistan, Seychelles, Kenya, Djibouti and France. The older SEAS cable [connects Seychelles and Tanzania](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/systems/asia-europe-africa/2africa/intelvision-lands-2africa-in-seychelles-the-third-subsea-cable-connecting-seychelles>) and was the country's first international cable; in May 2023 one operator still relied on it for its international traffic.
