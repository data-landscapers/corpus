# Mali

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** In June 2024 Mali's SMTD and Guinea's SOGEB [agreed to interconnect the two countries' fibre networks](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>). A telecommunications company based in Morocco [began investing about US$100 million in 2020](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>) in a terrestrial fibre cable linking Morocco, Mauritania, Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger. The cable is the company's own, and the investment ran from 2020 to 2022.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** On 5 October 2026 Senegal's and Mali's backbone companies [inaugurated the link between their networks](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>) at the Kidira-Diboli bridge. In November 2025 the chairman of the West African backbone network said [Mali is connected to Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea and Senegal](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>). The 5,700 km Morocco-to-Niger cable [was completed in 2022](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). It gives the owner's subsidiary in Mali international bandwidth. Afribone Mali [routes its international links by fibre](<https://afribone.com/a-propos/>) through Dakar and Abidjan.
