# Mali

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared infrastructure agreed and under construction.** Mali's SMTD and Guinea's SOGEB [agreed in June 2024 to interconnect the two countries' fibre networks](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/optical-fixed-networks/17258-guinea-adds-cote-d-ivoire-to-regional-fibre-interconnectivity-plan.html>), with ministry support. No source reports building begun on that link, or a contract for it. The backbone interconnections now working are older arrangements, or ones whose agreement is not on record.

**Shared infrastructure in service, by kind.** Senegal's SENUM and Mali's SMTD [inaugurated the interconnection of their national backbones at the Kidira and Diboli border on 5 October 2026](<https://aps.sn/bakel-inauguration-de-linterconnexion-des-backbones-du-senegal-et-du-mali/>). A speaker called it the putting into service of the link. The chairman of ROA-BN, the West African network of national backbones, [said in November 2025 that its network is working](<https://guineematin.com/2025/11/11/connectivite-regionale-le-siege-du-reseau-ouest-africain-des-backbones-ouvert-a-conakry/>). Mali is connected to Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea and Senegal within it. No exchange point or data facility run with another country is on record.

***Also on file***. [Maroc Telecom terrestrial cable](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>); [Afribone international links](<https://afribone.com/a-propos/>).

***Not found***. No source found says an exchange point or data centre is shared with another country under an agreement.
