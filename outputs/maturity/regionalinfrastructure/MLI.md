# Mali

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A telecommunications company based in Morocco [began investing about US$100 million in 2020](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>) in a terrestrial fibre cable linking Morocco, Mauritania, Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger. The cable is the company's own, built to join its subsidiaries in the four countries to its home network, and the investment ran from 2020 to 2022.

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** The 5,700 km cable [is recorded as completed in 2022](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>). It runs from Morocco to Niger through Mauritania, Mali and Burkina Faso, and lets the company's subsidiary in landlocked Mali reach international bandwidth over the parent's network. Mali has no coast, and the cable gives that subsidiary a route over land to the capacity its parent holds.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Mali IX (MLIX)](<https://sigtel.ecowas.int/regional-fiber-infrastructure/>).

***Not held***. No source held reports the link or facility carrying another country's traffic in the last three years: capacity sold or traffic carried across the border, or a user from the other country named.
