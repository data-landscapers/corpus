# Libya

## Digital links and facilities shared with other African countries

**Shared link or facility agreed and under construction.** A Libyan company [signed a contract in November 2023](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>) to connect Libya to the Medusa submarine cable, a system that the same account says links Libya with Morocco, Tunisia and Egypt among others. [A project official said in October 2026 that Libya's fibre link to the Chadian border, already built to the Egyptian border, should be finished by the end of 2026](<https://lendjampost.com/telecommunications-la-libye-prevoit-dachever-le-raccordement-de-la-fibre-optique-a-la-frontiere-tchadienne-dici-fin-2026/>).

**In service and carrying another country's traffic.** Libya's state telecommunications holding company [inaugurated the Medusa submarine cable at Misrata on 10 May 2025](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>). The system is 8,700 km long and joins Libya to other Mediterranean countries, Morocco, Tunisia and Egypt among them. The inauguration at Misrata is the Libyan landing; the account of it gives no figure for traffic between Libya and the three African countries, and names no operator in any of them that uses the Libyan landing.
