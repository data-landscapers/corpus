# Morocco

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [A proposed reform of the rules on payment systems and means of payment would broaden the Bank's prerogatives and require its prior approval of any payment, clearing or settlement system](<https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [Bank Al-Maghrib's payments department says the central bank has organised interoperability and framed access to payment infrastructures, per Le Matin's report of the 11th MFC Talks](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>). [Bank Al-Maghrib oversees Morocco's payment systems, including the SIMT retail clearing system, under article 10 of law 76-03 and a multilateral oversight convention signed by all infrastructure managers](<https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>).
