# Morocco

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Bank Al-Maghrib's 2025 supervision report lists the licences it granted that year](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/847887/9165409/Rapport%20DSB%202025.pdf>). Three licences created new payment institutions. A fourth widened an existing institution's licence to card-terminal acquiring. [A proposed reform would require its prior approval of any payment, clearing or settlement system](<https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Bank Al-Maghrib oversees Morocco's payment systems, including the SIMT retail clearing system](<https://www.bkam.ma/en/Systems-and-means-of-payment/Financial-markets-infrastructure-and-monitoring/Overview>). It does so under article 10 of law 76-03. A multilateral oversight convention signed by all infrastructure managers also applies. [Its payments department says it has organised interoperability and framed access to payment infrastructure](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact is [the central bank's work on interoperability](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>).
