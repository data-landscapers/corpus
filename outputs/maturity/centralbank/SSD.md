# South Sudan

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [In September 2025 the Bank of South Sudan issued a regulatory notice on digital financial service providers](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [It put them, mobile money operators included, under the same licensing-linked requirements as banks](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [The sanction is licence suspension or cancellation](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [In September 2026 the Bank required non-bank electronic money issuers to obtain its approval under the amended Electronic Money Regulation](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [It must assess applications within 60 days](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [On 24 November 2026 the Bank of South Sudan and the Ministry of Finance and Planning launched the South Sudan Interbank Payment and Settlement System](<https://eyeradio.org/central-bank-launches-electronic-payment-system-to-replace-manual-cheques/>). [Its real-time gross settlement system takes high-value payments and its automated clearing house takes low-value and bulk payments](<https://eyeradio.org/central-bank-launches-electronic-payment-system-to-replace-manual-cheques/>). [Both are linked to the government's financial management system](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/opinion-south-sudan-leaps-away-from-the-cheque-book>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest fact found: [in September 2025 the Bank put mobile money operators under bank-level licensing requirements](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>).
