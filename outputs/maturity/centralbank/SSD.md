# South Sudan

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of South Sudan requires non-bank electronic money issuers to obtain its regulatory approval under the amended Electronic Money Regulation, and must assess applications within 60 days](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [The Bank of South Sudan issued a regulatory notice putting digital financial service providers, including mobile money operators, under the same licensing-linked requirements as banks, with licence suspension or cancellation as the sanction](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).
