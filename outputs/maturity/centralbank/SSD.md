# South Sudan

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [In September 2025 the Bank of South Sudan issued a regulatory notice on digital financial service providers](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [It put them, mobile money operators included, under the same licensing-linked requirements as banks](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [The sanction is licence suspension or cancellation](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>). [In September 2026 the Bank required non-bank electronic money issuers to obtain its approval under the amended Electronic Money Regulation](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [It must assess applications within 60 days](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [in September 2026 the Bank of South Sudan set approval rules for electronic money issuers](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the Bank put mobile money operators under bank-level licensing requirements](<https://jakony.com/digital-money-operators-face-new-security-and-reporting-rules/>).

***Also on file***. [Interbank Payment and Settlement System](<https://ramdiet.com/writing/digital-payments-everyday-use>); [Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS)](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/05/20/south-sudan-platform-to-digitise-economy-pays-off/>).
