# Uganda

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [We Are Tech Africa reports NALA secured payment service provider and payment system operator licences from the Bank of Uganda](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/brief/brief-simple/tanzanian-fintech-nala-receives-uganda-payment-service-system-licenses>). [Daily Monitor reports the bank approved at least 17 payment licences in one year](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/business/technology/bou-s-switch-that-will-control-bank-mobile-money-and-fintech-charges--5221284>). They covered e-money issuers, payment service providers and aggregators.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Uganda runs the Real Time Gross Settlement and Automated Clearing House services](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>). On 3 November 2026 it said both would close from 5 to 9 November for an upgrade. It called them part of Uganda's national payments infrastructure.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says Ugandan banks send and receive payments through PAPSS. [The bank's annual report shows East African Payment System inflows of UGX 1.3 trillion in 2024/25](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>). A regional system does not count.

***Also on file***. [East African Payment System](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>), a regional system, which does not answer the ladder.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the country is live on PAPSS.
