# Uganda

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source found since October 2023 says whether the Bank of Uganda licenses payment providers. The nearest fact found: [its notice of 3 November 2026 on the national payments infrastructure](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Uganda runs the Real Time Gross Settlement and Automated Clearing House services](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>). [On 3 November 2026 it said both would close from 5 to 9 November for an upgrade](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>). [It said the upgrade supports the East African Community's cross-border payment plan](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>). [It called the two services part of Uganda's national payments infrastructure](<https://www.bou.or.ug/uploads/Public_Notice_on_RTGS_Temporary_Closure_f3e46d259e.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says Ugandan banks send and receive payments through PAPSS. [The bank's annual report shows East African Payment System inflows of UGX 1.3 trillion in 2024/25](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>). [That was 17% more than the year before](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>).

***Also on file***. [East African Payment System](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>), a regional system, which does not answer the ladder.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the central bank licenses payment providers. None says the country is live on PAPSS.
