# Guinea

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Guinea had 11 licensed electronic-money institutions in November 2025, up from six in 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0711-50255-guinea-plans-mobile-payment-system-to-ease-cash-shortage>), the central bank, BCRG, says. [Its own page says a World Bank-funded project is to draft a payment systems law](<https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/>) and set up a National Payment Council. In October 2026 [the BCRG was preparing that draft law and a national payments strategy for 2027-2030](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/2610-142742-guinee-nimbapay-traite-7-6-millions-de-transactions-et-connecte-7-etablissements-en-4-mois>), Agence Ecofin reports.

**Overseeing a switch.** NimbaPay, the national instant payment platform, was launched in July 2026. [A digital industry group congratulated the BCRG and Guinéenne de Monétique on its design](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>). [The BCRG's supervision director general said the central bank is putting NimbaPay into operation](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/paiements-digitaux-ofmg-la-cciag-et-la-bcrg-engagent-une-demarche-pour-encourager-les-transactions-electroniques-en-guinee/>). It links banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>).
