# Guinea

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Guinea has 11 licensed electronic-money institutions, up from six in 2024, per the central bank (BCRG)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-finances/0711-50255-guinea-plans-mobile-payment-system-to-ease-cash-shortage>). [The BCRG page says the World Bank-funded WARDIP project is to draft a payment systems law and set up a National Payment Council, awaiting World Bank non-objection on terms of reference](<https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [AGUIDUTEN congratulated the Banque Centrale de la Republique de Guinee and Guineenne de Monetique on the design and implementation of NimbaPay, the national instant payment platform whose launch it welcomed](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>). [The BCRG's supervision director general said the central bank is operationalising NimbaPay, a national instant payment platform interoperating banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/paiements-digitaux-ofmg-la-cciag-et-la-bcrg-engagent-une-demarche-pour-encourager-les-transactions-electroniques-en-guinee/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>).
