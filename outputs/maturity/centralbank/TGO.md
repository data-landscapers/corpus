# Togo

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Mixx holds an e-money institution licence (agrement) from the BCEAO](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>). [The BCEAO widened market access in Togo by licensing new fintech players, per Togo First](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1803-18503-togo-le-nombre-de-comptes-de-monnaie-electronique-a-progresse-de-76-87-en-un-an>). [TechCabal reports the BCEAO granted Togolese fintech Semoa its Level 3 payment institution accreditation on 29 January 2026, the first of its kind for a Togolese company](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>). [TechCabal says INTOUCH Togo obtained its BCEAO electronic money institution licence in June 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BCEAO launched the PI-SPI instant payment platform on 30 September 2025 and sets institutions' deadlines to connect](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [By 24 June 2026, 80 institutions were connected across the region](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [By the end of October 2026, 17 Togolese providers were connected and it had processed nearly CFAF 15 billion in Togo](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/economic-governance/0511-20563-togo-instant-payment-platform-processed-nearly-cfaf-15-billion-in-transactions-by-end-october>). [Six BCEAO-authorised institutions in Togo had earlier opened instant transfers around the clock](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/0603-18394-bceao-six-acteurs-autorises-a-ouvrir-au-public-les-services-de-paiement-instantane-au-togo>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count. [PI-SPI is the BCEAO's instant payment platform across the WAEMU, in service since 30 September 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [Mixx Togo has joined it, and Ecobank, Orabank and Bank of Africa in Togo are connected](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>). [Togo First calls adoption very gradual](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1803-18503-togo-le-nombre-de-comptes-de-monnaie-electronique-a-progresse-de-76-87-en-un-an>).
