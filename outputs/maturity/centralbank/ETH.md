# Ethiopia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Directive 1069/2025 defines payment service providers as those licensed or authorized by the National Bank of Ethiopia to issue payment instruments, and requires federal public bodies to accept payments from all of them](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>). [The National Bank of Ethiopia licenses ETTA's ZooryaPay as a Payment System Operator, per the Capital newspaper report](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Overseeing a switch.** No source held since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>).

**Linked regionally.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>).
