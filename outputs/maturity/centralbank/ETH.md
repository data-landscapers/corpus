# Ethiopia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The National Bank of Ethiopia licenses ETTA's ZooryaPay as a Payment System Operator, per the Capital newspaper report](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>). [Directive 1069/2025 defines payment service providers as those licensed or authorized by the National Bank of Ethiopia to issue payment instruments, and requires federal public bodies to accept payments from all of them](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>). The National Bank [gave Safaricom M-PESA a Payment Instrument Issuer licence in May 2023](<https://onafriq.com/press/article/m-pesa-safaricom-partners-with-onafriq-enabling-the-flow-of-remittances-to-ethiopia>), before the service went live that August.

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [ITWeb Africa reports Ethiopia has joined PAPSS, with NBE governor Eyob Tekalign saying the move deepens continental ties, enabling financial institutions in the country to connect for instant cross-border payments](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [ITWeb Africa reports Ethiopia has joined PAPSS, with NBE governor Eyob Tekalign saying the move deepens continental ties, enabling financial institutions in the country to connect for instant cross-border payments](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>). [The National Bank of Ethiopia and PAPSS announced on 15 October 2026 that Ethiopia has officially joined the PAPSS network, letting Ethiopian financial institutions connect for cross-border payments](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>).
