# Ethiopia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** In May 2023 the National Bank of Ethiopia [gave Safaricom M-PESA a Payment Instrument Issuer licence](<https://onafriq.com/press/article/m-pesa-safaricom-partners-with-onafriq-enabling-the-flow-of-remittances-to-ethiopia>). On 12 May 2025 it [raised the minimum capital of these issuers from 50 million to 100 million birr](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-doubles-capital-threshold-for-mobile-money-providers-as-central-bank-tightens-grip-on-digital-finance/>). [The amending Directive ONPS/10/2025, in force that day, also sets ownership and conduct rules for licensed issuers](<https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ONPS102025-Oversight-of-the-Natioanl-Payment-System-Licensing-and-Authorization-of-Payment-Instrument-Issuer-Amendment-Directive.pdf>). [Directive 1069/2025](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>) requires federal public bodies to accept payments from every provider the Bank licenses. In October 2026 the Capital newspaper reported that the Bank [licenses ETTA's ZooryaPay as a payment system operator](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** In May 2025 the National Bank [made interoperability among mobile wallets mandatory](<https://ethiopianmonitor.com/2025/05/27/nbe-mandates-interoperability-among-mobile-wallets/>). The same directive requires financial institutions to take part in the Ethiopian Instant Payment System.

**Live on PAPSS.** On 15 October 2026 the National Bank and PAPSS [announced that Ethiopia had joined the PAPSS network](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>). [ITWeb Africa reported](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>) that the step lets Ethiopian financial institutions connect for cross-border payments.
