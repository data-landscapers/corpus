# Burundi

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The BRB's 2024 report on payment systems names the e-money issuers it had licensed at end-September 2024](<https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20les%20syst%C3%A8mes%20et%20moyens%20de%20%20paiement%20et%20leur%20surveillance.pdf>). They are Saisai Fintech, Lumicash and Ellis Business Company.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BRB launched BurundiPay, the national instant payment system, on 23 April 2026](<https://www.brb.bi/node/3303>). It is the system's promoter. [Burundi Times reports BurundiPay connects banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money networks](<https://www.burunditimes.com/new-burundipay-platform-connects-banks-microfinance-and-mobile-money-networks/>). [By 4 August 2026, 17 institutions were live on it](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). [The BRB says BurundiPay complements its clearing house, RTGS and national switch](<https://www.brb.bi/node/3293>). [It moved its Automated Transfer System to ISO 20022 in February 2026](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-upgrades-payment-system-to-international-iso-20022-standard/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows Burundian banks sending and receiving through PAPSS. [The BRB has joined PAPSS, and four Burundian banks are authorised to take part](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). Joining is not a yes.
