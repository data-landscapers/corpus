# Burundi

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source found says the central bank's role over payments is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The BRB has acceded to PAPSS and four Burundian banks are authorised to take part, according to BRB's Nduwimana Libere](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of the Republic of Burundi pilots BurundiPay, the national instant payment system, with 17 institutions live on 4 August 2026 (BRB payment systems director Nduwimana Libere)](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). [Bank of the Republic of Burundi launched the national instant payments platform BurundiPay, unveiled on 23 April 2026, connecting banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money networks](<https://www.burunditimes.com/new-burundipay-platform-connects-banks-microfinance-and-mobile-money-networks/>).
