# Mauritius

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Mauritius's July 2026 guideline says it issues payment service provider licences](<https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/guideline_for_payment_aggregators_0.pdf>). It issues them under the National Payment Systems Act. Payment aggregators must now hold one. The guideline is the central bank's own, issued in July 2026.

**Overseeing a switch.** [Governor Thakoor says the bank's infrastructure includes the Mauritius Central Automated Switch](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>). Its QR payments now work in Mauritius and India. The governor named the switch as part of the bank's own infrastructure.

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count. [The governor says the bank is settlement bank for COMESA's REPSS](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>). REPSS is COMESA's regional system and is not PAPSS.
