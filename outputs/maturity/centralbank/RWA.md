# Rwanda

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The National Bank of Rwanda said in March 2026 that its passporting agreement with the Central Bank of Kenya [covers payment service providers licensed by their home central bank](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>), so a provider licensed in one country can offer its services in the other. The two central banks signed the agreement that month, at a ceremony where the National Bank's speech was given.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The National Bank of Rwanda says it monitors the eKash payment and transfer system, launched nationally on 14 July 2026, and works with providers to fix disruptions](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/rwandas-new-ekash-payment-system-wins-praise-but-users-report-lingering-transfer-glitches/>). [Taarifa reports that interoperability runs through the National Bank of Rwanda's eKash platform, rolled out months before July 2026](<https://taarifa.rw/2026/07/31/ekash-was-designed-to-bridge-the-digital-divide-but-airtel-moneys-struggles-risk-leaving-rural-users-behind/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing held says Rwandan banks or providers send and receive payments through PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact is the [national launch of eKash in July 2026](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/rwandas-new-ekash-payment-system-wins-praise-but-users-report-lingering-transfer-glitches/>), a domestic system.
