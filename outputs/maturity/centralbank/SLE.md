# Sierra Leone

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [A Bank of Sierra Leone supervisor's 2025 deck on digital financial services says eight fintechs hold a full BSL licence](<http://itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/qos/20250701/Documents/3-Esther%20Johnson_updated.pdf>). They graduated from the BSL's regulatory sandbox, where 19 firms remain.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Sierra Leone launched a national payment switch in April 2023](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/sierra-leone-launches-national-instant-payment-system>). It added an instant payment switch on 13 February 2025. That switch linked seven banks and two mobile money operators. [A Bank representative cited the National Switch among its inclusion efforts](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/mikashboks-and-ecobank-partner-to-deepen-financial-inclusion-in-sierra-leone/>). In January 2026 the head of its National Switch Department said it [runs the Salon Payment Switch, which now includes every commercial bank](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/sierra-leone-pushes-cash-lite-economy/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [PAPSS lists eight Sierra Leonean banks as live in its May 2025 update](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_19052025.pdf>). They include UBA, Access, Rokel and GTBank.
