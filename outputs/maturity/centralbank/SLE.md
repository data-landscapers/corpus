## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source held says the central bank's role over payments is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [PAPSS lists eight Sierra Leonean banks (UBA, SLCB, Access, Bloom, Rokel, GTBank, Vista, FBNBank) as live, update May 2025](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_19052025.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Sierra Leone's representative cited the central bank's launch of the National Switch among its inclusion efforts](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/mikashboks-and-ecobank-partner-to-deepen-financial-inclusion-in-sierra-leone/>). [The Bank of Sierra Leone launched a national payment switch in April 2023 and on 13 February 2025 a national instant payment switch linking seven banks and two mobile money operators, with full integration due by 1 April 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/sierra-leone-launches-national-instant-payment-system>). In January 2026 the head of the Bank's National Switch Department said it [runs the Salon Payment Switch, which now includes every commercial bank, and an instant payment system](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/sierra-leone-pushes-cash-lite-economy/>).
