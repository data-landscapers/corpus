# Botswana

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Botswana licenses electronic payment services: under the amended regulations in force since 2 April 2026 no one may operate one without its licence, on pain of a P1 million fine or five years in prison](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bob-electronic-money-regulations-gather-pace/news>). [The Bank of Botswana says the Electronic Payment Services (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, in effect since 2 April 2026, set out the entities that may be licensed to offer electronic payment services and the licensing and market-entry requirements](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>). [The Bank of Botswana said the National Payments Switch Act, enabling a national retail payments switch, has been drafted and has no tabling date](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/07/national-payments-switch-act-drafted.html>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bob-electronic-money-regulations-gather-pace/news>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bob-electronic-money-regulations-gather-pace/news>).
