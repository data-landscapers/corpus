# Senegal

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [BCEAO counted 31 licensed payment institutions in the UMOA at end of February 2026, 11 of them in Senegal, under its 23 January 2024 instruction on payment services](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>). [BCEAO lists 11 authorised electronic money initiatives in Senegal at 30 September 2024: three licensed e-money issuers and eight banks issuing through partners](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/List-of-electronic-money-issuing-institutions-as-of-september-30-2024.pdf>). Wave [holds an e-money issuer licence granted by the BCEAO in Senegal](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [BCEAO launched PI-SPI, the regional interoperable instant payment platform, on 30 September 2025, running 24/7; 80 participants were connected at 24 June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>). [Financial Afrik says BCEAO operates the PI-SPI instant-payment system, with 19 Senegal institutions authorised on its list, the most in the Union](<https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/05/instant-payments-pi-spi-in-the-waemu-updated-list-of-establishments-authorized-by-the-bceao/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing held shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform counts 175 authorised participants across the eight WAEMU countries, with Senegal holding the most at 43, and Wave was authorised to open its services in Senegal on the list updated 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). [Financial Afrik says 19 Senegal institutions are authorised by BCEAO to offer PI-SPI, the WAEMU regional real-time payment system](<https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/05/instant-payments-pi-spi-in-the-waemu-updated-list-of-establishments-authorized-by-the-bceao/>).
