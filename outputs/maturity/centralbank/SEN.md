# Senegal

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The BCEAO [listed 11 authorised e-money initiatives in Senegal at 30 September 2024](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/List-of-electronic-money-issuing-institutions-as-of-september-30-2024.pdf>): three licensed issuers and eight banks issuing through partners. At the end of February 2026 it [counted 11 licensed payment institutions in Senegal](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>), under its January 2024 instruction on payment services. Wave [holds a BCEAO e-money issuer licence](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** The BCEAO [launched PI-SPI, its interoperable instant payment platform, on 30 September 2025](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>). By April 2026 it [counted 80 connected participants, nine of them e-money institutions](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>). Since 2 November 2026 [bank-wallet transfers in Senegal must go through it](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/bank-to-wallet-et-wallet-to-bank-la-bceao-chamboule-les-regles-du-jeu/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional system, which does not count. At 30 September 2026 PI-SPI [had 175 authorised participants across the WAEMU, 43 of them in Senegal](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). In November 2026 Wave said [its customers in Senegal can use PI-SPI from its app](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>).
