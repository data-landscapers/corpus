# Malawi

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The Reserve Bank of Malawi licenses payment service providers. On 30 September 2026 it [granted the local fintech PayChangu Limited](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/17/payment-hurdles-ease-for-businesses/>) payment service provider licence PSP-003/26 for payment aggregation, letting it bring mobile money, card payments and bank transfers onto one platform for businesses. The Reserve Bank [issued the licence under the Payment Systems Act, 2016](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/17/payment-hurdles-ease-for-businesses/>), and the company's chief operating officer credited it with making room for local firms. In October 2023 it [granted Flutterwave an International Money Transfer Operator licence](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/reserve-bank-of-malawi-grants-flutterwave-license-to-process-international-transactions-301982699.html>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Reserve Bank of Malawi and the Bank of Zambia supervise the COMESA Digital Retail Payments Platform linking the two countries](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>). [The Reserve Bank of Malawi rolled out the National Switch in 2015, linking banks, mobile money platforms and ATMs into one network (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD>). [Malawi's 2025 ID4Africa deck says National Switch Limited, which connects all banks and mobile money operators for instant payments, is supervised by the Reserve Bank of Malawi](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [the Reserve Bank of Malawi supervises Malawi's participation in the COMESA cross-border payments platform](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>).
