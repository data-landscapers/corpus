# Burkina Faso

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source found says the central bank's role over payments is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [LeFaso.net reports that PI-SPI is a BCEAO instant payment platform across the UEMOA, in service since 30 September 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO, which sets the connection deadlines for banks, e-money and payment institutions and microfinance, launched the PI-SPI interoperable instant payment platform on 30 September 2025, with 80 institutions connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [LeFaso.net reports that PI-SPI is a BCEAO instant payment platform across the UEMOA, in service since 30 September 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [As of 24 February 2026 the BCEAO had authorised nine institutions in Burkina Faso (including Orange Money, Coris Bank International and Ecobank) to offer PI-SPI instant payment services to the public](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144889>).
