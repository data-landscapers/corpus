# Tanzania

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [As of March 2026, 113 service providers were licensed to operate payment system services, the finance minister told the National Assembly](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>). [The Bank of Tanzania licenses new players](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Tanzania launched the Tanzania Instant Payment System (TIPS) in 2019](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/tanzanias-ministry-of-communication-and-information-technology-partners-with-idea-to-launch-jamii-wallet-supported-by-co-develop>). [It is an interoperable platform across banks and mobile money providers](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/tanzanias-ministry-of-communication-and-information-technology-partners-with-idea-to-launch-jamii-wallet-supported-by-co-develop>). [Over 45 institutions take part](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/tanzanias-ministry-of-communication-and-information-technology-partners-with-idea-to-launch-jamii-wallet-supported-by-co-develop>). [In 2024 and 2025 the Bank capped the maximum cost of a single transaction at 2,000/-](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>). [The cap covers the Tanzania Automated Clearing House and the instant payment system, with a focus on TIPS](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/interoperability-costs-cut-while-113-agencies-operate-payments-systems-2026-04-28-111407>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system.
