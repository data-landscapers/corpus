# Mauritania

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Agence Ecofin reports Mattel Money is licensed by the Banque Centrale de Mauritanie as an e-money institution](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2307-140451-mauritanie-mattel-elargit-son-offre-aux-paiements-numeriques-avec-mechili>). Mattel Money, a subsidiary of the operator Mattel, launched the Mechili payment service on 22 July 2026. Mechili runs on an app and on USSD. It handles transfers, deposits, withdrawals and bill payments.

**Overseeing a switch.** [GIMTEL launched the interconnection of bank apps and e-wallets under the central bank's strategy](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/gimtel-annonce-le-debut-des-transactions-interbancaires-des-differentes-applications/>). The governor said oversight of payment systems had been activated. The launch came in October 2024.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. Nothing found names a regional system either. The nearest held fact is [GIMTEL's interconnection](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/gimtel-annonce-le-debut-des-transactions-interbancaires-des-differentes-applications/>).
