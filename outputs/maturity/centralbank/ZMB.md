# Zambia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Zambia issued fourteen designation certificates to payment service providers in 2024 (seven payment aggregators, six electronic money issuers, one money transfer operator) and revoked four](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Zambia says it oversees the national payment systems, including the National Financial Switch, which processed K131.9 billion and 140,496,750 transactions in 2024 with 34 participants live](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Zambian commercial banks process payments through the SADC Real Time Gross Settlement System (ZAR13.4 billion paid and ZAR13.9 billion received in 2024)](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>).
