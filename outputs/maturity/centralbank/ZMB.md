# Zambia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [In 2024 the Bank of Zambia issued fourteen designation certificates to payment service providers](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). [They went to seven payment aggregators, six electronic money issuers and one money transfer operator](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). [The Bank revoked four designations in the same year](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Zambia says it oversees the national payment systems, including the National Financial Switch](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). [In 2024 the National Financial Switch processed 140,496,750 transactions worth K131.9 billion](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). [It had 34 participants live](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country is live on PAPSS. [The Bank's report says that in 2024 Zambian commercial banks processed payments through the SADC Real Time Gross Settlement System](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>). [They paid ZAR13.4 billion and received ZAR13.9 billion through it that year](<https://www.boz.zm/sites/default/files/2026-01/2024NPSAnnualReport_0.pdf>).
