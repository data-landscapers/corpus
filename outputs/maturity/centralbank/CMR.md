# Cameroon

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC regulates payment market structures, including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [BEAC's rules apply to the six CEMAC states, including Cameroon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [BEAC launched the interoperable CEMAC QR code on 29 July 2026 in Douala](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>). [Regional interbank operations are routed and cleared via GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>). [BEAC publishes Gimacpay's figures](<https://lejour.cm/mobile-money-gimacpay-franchit-la-barre-des-600-milliards-fcfa-de-transactions/>). [It notes delays in applying the interoperability framework set by governor's instruction n°001/GR/2018 for card payment systems in the Cemac zone](<https://lejour.cm/mobile-money-gimacpay-franchit-la-barre-des-600-milliards-fcfa-de-transactions/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What has been found is a regional payment system, which does not count. [Cameroon is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [The UMAC ministerial committee validated the standard in April 2026, and it was launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [GIMACPAY has been in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC says the interoperable QR code covers the six CEMAC countries, cleared through GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>). [It rests on Regulation 02/26/CEMAC/UMAC/COREFI of 8 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).
