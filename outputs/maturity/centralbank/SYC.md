# Seychelles

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The Central Bank of Seychelles [gave Fusepay final approval as a licensed payment service provider](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>) in November 2025, and Fusepay now [runs a business-to-business payments platform under that licence](<https://dabafinance.com/en/news/fusepay-rolls-out-b2b-payments-platform>), with the central bank's approval.

**Overseeing a switch.** Governor Caroline Abel said in July 2025 that [banks interoperate fully on the country's most used payment systems](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>), the Seychelles Electronic Funds Transfer and electronic cheque clearing, under the Act that governs the operation and oversight of national payment systems. She was opening meetings of the payment system committees of the SADC central bank governors.

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing held says Seychelles takes part in PAPSS. The governor said cross-border payments are [processed through the central bank on the SADC-RTGS platform](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>), with volumes too small to bring in the commercial banks.

***Not held***. No source held says Seychelles banks or providers send and receive payments through PAPSS.
