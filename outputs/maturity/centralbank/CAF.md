# Central African Republic

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [In November 2023 AfricaNenda said the regional central bank BEAC regulates payment market structures](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [Its rules apply to the six CEMAC states, the Central African Republic among them](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [They include technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [In November 2023 AfricaNenda said BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY and is its overseer and settlement agent](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The switch serves payment providers in the Central African Republic](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [It provides interoperable payment services to providers in all six CEMAC states](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country is live on PAPSS. [PAPSS said BEAC, the central bank of the six CEMAC states, officially joined PAPSS on 9 July 2026](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [Afreximbank said CEMAC banks are to be connected by the end of 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>). [In 2025 GIMACPAY, the regional platform, processed 20.3 million transactions worth FCFA 863 billion](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>). [BEAC was among its 153 participants](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>). [In April 2026 the UMAC ministerial committee validated GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard for the six states](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [It was launched over GIMACPAY on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).
