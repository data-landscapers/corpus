# Gambia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source found says the central bank's role over payments is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [PAPSS lists Gambia as a country with live send-and-receive participants: seven banks (Access, AGIB, Bloom, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank, Vista)](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Central Bank of The Gambia, as lead regulator of the national payment system, launched the NPSAC after the launch of BANTABA 2.0, described as the country's first real-time, fully interoperable payment system](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-national-payment-systems-advisory-committee-npsac-to-strengthen-payment-system-governance>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [PAPSS lists Gambia as a country with live send-and-receive participants: seven banks (Access, AGIB, Bloom, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank, Vista)](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). [The PAPSS April 2026 coverage list shows Gambia as a country where PAPSS transfers are available, with live commercial banks AGIB Bank, Bloom Bank, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank and Vista Bank](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_APRIL2026.pdf>).
