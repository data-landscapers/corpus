# Gambia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Digital Cooperation Organization's 2025 Gambia report says mobile money providers must be authorised by the central bank](<https://dco.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Gambia-Report.pdf>). The central bank's mobile money regulations set the rules.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Central Bank of The Gambia, as lead regulator of the national payment system, launched an advisory committee after BANTABA 2.0 went live](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-national-payment-systems-advisory-committee-npsac-to-strengthen-payment-system-governance>). BANTABA 2.0 is the country's first real-time interoperable payment system. [The Point says the central bank co-led its launch in December 2025](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>). [Gamswitch says the central bank gave regulatory leadership and oversight for the switch](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-its-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>). Gamswitch operates it.

**Live on PAPSS.** [PAPSS lists the Gambia as a country with live send-and-receive participants](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). Seven banks are named, among them Access, Ecobank and Trust Bank. [The April 2026 PAPSS list also shows Gambian banks live](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_APRIL2026.pdf>).
