# Somalia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia licenses and supervises financial institutions, has introduced mobile-money regulation, and its website reports fifteen licensed commercial banks and six mobile-money service providers](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The House of the People passed the National Payment System Bill 154-2, empowering the Central Bank of Somalia to license, supervise and oversee payment systems and digital money platforms; it goes to the Senate](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia has launched the National Payment System, the Somali Payment Switch and the Somali Instant Payment System (SIPS), which the Bank states supports real-time transfers among banks, mobile wallets and points of sale](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>).

**Live on PAPSS.** The Central Bank of Somalia [joined PAPSS in March 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7444420816521412609-knRx>), but no Somali bank is reported live on it. No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system in service.
