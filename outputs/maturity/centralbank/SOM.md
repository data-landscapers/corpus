# Somalia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia licenses and supervises financial institutions and has introduced mobile-money regulation](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The bank's website reports fifteen licensed commercial banks and six mobile-money service providers](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The House of the People passed the National Payment System Bill 154-2](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). [The bill empowers the Central Bank of Somalia to license, supervise and oversee payment systems and digital money platforms](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). [It goes to the Senate](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia has launched the National Payment System](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [It has also launched the Somali Payment Switch and the Somali Instant Payment System (SIPS)](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The Bank states that SIPS supports real-time transfers among banks, mobile wallets and points of sale](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The Governor wrote in July 2026 that SIPS, launched in January 2025, is run by the Somalia Payment Switch](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/opinion-analysis/columnists/somalia-s-instant-payment-system-powering-economy-5535414>), a partnership of the central bank and 13 commercial banks.

**Live on PAPSS.** The Central Bank of Somalia [joined PAPSS in March 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7444420816521412609-knRx>), but no Somali bank is reported live on it. No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system in service.
