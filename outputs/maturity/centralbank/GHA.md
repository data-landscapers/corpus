# Ghana

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Ghana granted dLocal Ghana an Enhanced Payment Service Provider licence, reported 2026-09-09](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/09/dlocal-ghana-secures-enhanced-payments-licence/>). [The Bank of Ghana licenses and supervises Dedicated Electronic Money Issuers; the Governor launched SaoPay as a new DEMI on 7 October 2026](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>). A Bank of Ghana notice of March 2025 [names Brij Fintech Ghana as a licensed payment service provider](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NOTICE-NO.-BG-GOV-SEC-2025-07-Pilot-of-BRIJX-by-BRIJ-Fintech-Ghana-in-the-Bank-of-Ghana-Regulatory-Sandbox.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Ghana's head of fintech said in October 2026 that the Bank set up the Ghana Interbank Payment and Settlement Systems (GhIPSS) in 2007 as a publicly owned national payment infrastructure, which connects banks, mobile money operators and other payment providers](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [Ghana News Agency reports Bank of Ghana Governor Asiama saying the bank has extended PAPSS to mobile wallets in the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>). [Bank of Ghana Governor Asiama said PAPSS has been extended to mobile wallets along the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). In June 2025 the Bank approved [a six-month pilot in which Ghanaian banks and providers send and receive money through PAPSS](<https://onafriq.com/press/article/onafriq-partners-with-papss-to-launch-cross-border-payment-services-in-ghana>).
