# Ghana

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Ghana granted dLocal Ghana an Enhanced Payment Service Provider licence, reported 2026-09-09](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/09/dlocal-ghana-secures-enhanced-payments-licence/>). [The Bank of Ghana licenses and supervises Dedicated Electronic Money Issuers; the Governor launched SaoPay as a new DEMI on 7 October 2026](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>). A Bank of Ghana notice of March 2025 [names Brij Fintech Ghana as a licensed payment service provider](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NOTICE-NO.-BG-GOV-SEC-2025-07-Pilot-of-BRIJX-by-BRIJ-Fintech-Ghana-in-the-Bank-of-Ghana-Regulatory-Sandbox.pdf>). [In March 2025 the Bank warned that MTN MoMo Ghana was not licensed for transfers to Nigeria](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/africas-cross-border-payment-rail-faces-5bn-reality-check-as-telcos-pursue-own-networks/>). In November 2026 the Governor said [the Bank is developing a legal and regulatory framework for fintech companies](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/11/bog-develops-open-banking-framework-to-facilitate-customer-data-sharing/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Ghana's head of fintech said in October 2026 that the Bank set up GhIPSS in 2007](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>). [The Ghana Interbank Payment and Settlement Systems (GhIPSS) is a publicly owned national payment infrastructure](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>). [It connects banks, mobile money operators and other payment providers](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Bank of Ghana Governor Asiama said the bank has [extended PAPSS to mobile wallets in the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [Ghana News Agency reports the same statement](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>). In June 2025 the Bank approved [a six-month pilot](<https://onafriq.com/press/article/onafriq-partners-with-papss-to-launch-cross-border-payment-services-in-ghana>). In it, [Ghanaian banks and providers send and receive money through PAPSS](<https://onafriq.com/press/article/onafriq-partners-with-papss-to-launch-cross-border-payment-services-in-ghana>).
