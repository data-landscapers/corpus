# Gabon

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC, whose rules apply to the six CEMAC states including Gabon, regulates payment market structures including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [BEAC launched the interoperable CEMAC QR code on 29 July 2026 in Douala, with regional interbank operations routed and cleared via GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>). [AfricaNenda says BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY, is its overseer and settlement agent, and the switch serves payment providers in Gabon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Gabon is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard, validated by the UMAC ministerial committee in April 2026 and launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY, in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC says the interoperable QR code covers the six CEMAC countries, cleared through GIMAC, under Regulation 02/26/CEMAC/UMAC/COREFI of 8 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).
