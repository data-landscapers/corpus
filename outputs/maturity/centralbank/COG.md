# Congo

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC, whose rules apply to the six CEMAC states including the Republic of Congo, regulates payment market structures including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [AfricaNenda says BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY, is its overseer and settlement agent, and the switch serves payment providers in the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Congo is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard, validated by the UMAC ministerial committee in April 2026 and launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY, in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, provides interoperable payment services to providers in the six CEMAC states including the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
