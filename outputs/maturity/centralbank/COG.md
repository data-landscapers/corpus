# Congo

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC regulates payment market structures, including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [BEAC's rules apply to the six CEMAC states, including the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [AfricaNenda says BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY and is its overseer and settlement agent](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The switch serves payment providers in the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What has been found is a regional payment system, which does not count. [Congo is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [The UMAC ministerial committee validated the standard in April 2026, and it was launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [GIMACPAY has been in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, provides interoperable payment services to providers in the six CEMAC states](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The six include the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
