# Mozambique

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Mozambique granted PawaPay, through its subsidiary Quidexplus Mozambique, a Payment Service Provider licence, announced by PawaPay on 8 October 2026](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/10/pawapay-becomes-one-of-the-first-licensed-psps-in-mozambique/>). [The Bank of Mozambique's communiqué of 30 July 2026 says Metix integrates banks, microbanks, electronic money institutions and other payment service providers authorised by the central bank](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Bank of Mozambique launched the Metix instant payment system on 16 March 2026, run by SIMO, and reports its volume (over 11,000 transfers a day) in its Monetary Policy Committee communiqué](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>). [The National Single Network (National Switch) has been operated by SIMOrede since 2023 under the supervision and control of the Bank of Mozambique](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-financial-institutions-have-60-minutes-to-report-failures/>).

**Linked regionally.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>).
