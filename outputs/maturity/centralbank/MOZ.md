# Mozambique

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Bank of Mozambique granted PawaPay a Payment Service Provider licence through its subsidiary Quidexplus Mozambique](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/10/pawapay-becomes-one-of-the-first-licensed-psps-in-mozambique/>). [PawaPay announced it on 8 October 2026](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/10/pawapay-becomes-one-of-the-first-licensed-psps-in-mozambique/>). [The Bank of Mozambique's communiqué of 30 July 2026 says Metix integrates banks, microbanks and electronic money institutions](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>). [It also integrates other payment service providers authorised by the central bank](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Bank of Mozambique launched the Metix instant payment system on 16 March 2026, run by SIMO](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>). [It reports Metix's volume, over 11,000 transfers a day, in its Monetary Policy Committee communiqué](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>). [SIMOrede has operated the National Single Network (National Switch) since 2023](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-financial-institutions-have-60-minutes-to-report-failures/>). [It does so under the supervision and control of the Bank of Mozambique](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-financial-institutions-have-60-minutes-to-report-failures/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Mozambique [joined PAPSS in September 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7510672876509917184-5qEO>), with local banks invited to onboard, but none is reported live on it. No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system in service.
