# Algeria

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** In March 2026 [a banking consultant said](<https://care.dz/fr/espace-presse/prestataires-de-services-de-paiement-le-rappel-a-lordre-dun-banquier-art752>) that Bank of Algeria Regulation 25-02 sets the conditions for licensing payment service providers. He said the Bank grants the licences and had licensed none yet. On 23 September 2026 the Bank's Monetary and Banking Council [authorised Djezzy Payment Services to set up as a payment service provider](<https://ecotimesdz.com/djezzy-payment-services-psp-banque-algerie-paiement-mobile-2026/>). It must apply for its licence within twelve months.

**Overseeing a switch.** In August 2025 [Launchbase Africa reported](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/08/19/algeria-opens-for-fintech-new-psp-rules-create-a-playbook-for-payments-startups/>) that Instruction 06-2025 requires payment service providers to join the national payment and clearing systems. The report says the Bank of Algeria oversees those systems. It names no switch or retail clearing system.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest fact found is [the Bank's September 2026 authorisation of Djezzy Payment Services](<https://ecotimesdz.com/djezzy-payment-services-psp-banque-algerie-paiement-mobile-2026/>), a domestic provider.
