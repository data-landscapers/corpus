# Benin

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Bénin Web TV reports four payment institutions held a BCEAO licence in Benin at 31 August 2026 (Julaya Bénin, Open SI, FedaPay Bénin, QOS Integrated Center Bénin), per the BCEAO list](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BCEAO operates PI-SPI, the interoperable instant payment platform across the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [It now connects banks, microfinance institutions, e-money issuers and payment institutions, 24/7](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [BCEAO launched PI-SPI on 30 September 2025 as a 24/7 interoperable instant payment platform for the UEMOA](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>). [Its communique reports 80 participants connected on 2 April 2026](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>). From 2 November 2026 [MTN Mobile Money Benin routes its interoperable transfers through PI-SPI](<https://www.24haubenin.info/?Communique-de-MTN-Mobile-Money-Benin=>). [A BCEAO notice of 2 October 2026 makes PI-SPI compulsory for interoperable e-money transfers](<https://triomphemag.com/interoperabilite-du-mobile-money-au-benin-pi-spi-entre-en-vigueur-ce-qui-change-reellement-pour-les-utilisateurs-des-ce-2-novembre/>). [The rule applies from 2 November 2026](<https://triomphemag.com/interoperabilite-du-mobile-money-au-benin-pi-spi-entre-en-vigueur-ce-qui-change-reellement-pour-les-utilisateurs-des-ce-2-novembre/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [PI-SPI is a single regional instant payment infrastructure run by the BCEAO for the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [Benin is a UEMOA member state on the regional PI-SPI platform that BCEAO runs, with its institutions required to connect by 30 June 2026](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>).
