# Benin

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Bénin Web TV reports four payment institutions held a BCEAO licence in Benin at 31 August 2026 (Julaya Bénin, Open SI, FedaPay Bénin, QOS Integrated Center Bénin), per the BCEAO list](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BCEAO operates PI-SPI, the interoperable instant payment platform now connecting banks, microfinance institutions, e-money issuers and payment institutions across the eight UEMOA countries, 24/7](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [BCEAO launched PI-SPI on 30 September 2025, a 24/7 interoperable instant payment platform for the UEMOA, and reports 80 participants connected on 2 April 2026 (communique)](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [PI-SPI is a single regional instant payment infrastructure run by the BCEAO for the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [Benin is a UEMOA member state on the regional PI-SPI platform that BCEAO runs, with its institutions required to connect by 30 June 2026](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>).
