# Sudan

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Central Bank of Sudan says it licenses electronic payment system services providers, and it revoked Al-Asjad's licence on review by a technical and legal committee](<https://sudantribune.net/article/315827>). [The Central Bank of Sudan announced revocation of Al-Asjad's licence to provide electronic payment system services, and says licences are conditional on continuing compliance with its published standards and need its written approval for integration](<https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-officially-revokes-al-asjad-companys-license-following-review/>). [Sudan Horizon reports Bede Sudan launched its BBAN wallet service after receiving approval from the Central Bank of Sudan](<https://sudanhorizon.com/bede-sudan-launches-bban-service-to-strengthen-banking-integration-and-expand-digital-transaction/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sudantribune.net/article/315827>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sudantribune.net/article/315827>).
