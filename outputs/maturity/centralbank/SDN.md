# Sudan

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Central Bank of Sudan licenses electronic payment service providers](<https://sudantribune.net/article/315827>). It revoked Al-Asjad's licence after review by a technical and legal committee. [Licences depend on meeting its published standards](<https://sudanhorizon.com/cbos-officially-revokes-al-asjad-companys-license-following-review/>), and any integration needs its written approval. [Bede Sudan launched its BBAN wallet after the central bank approved it](<https://sudanhorizon.com/bede-sudan-launches-bban-service-to-strengthen-banking-integration-and-expand-digital-transaction/>), Sudan Horizon reports.

**Overseeing a switch.** In October 2026 the head of EBS said [it runs Sudan's national payments infrastructure under the central bank's supervision](<https://hawasmagazine.com/?p=23253>). That includes [the national switch, which connects all Sudanese banks](<https://hawasmagazine.com/?p=23253>), and settlement with the central bank.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source since October 2023 says Sudan's banks send or receive payments through PAPSS. The nearest fact is the [national switch EBS runs](<https://hawasmagazine.com/?p=23253>), which links banks inside the country.
