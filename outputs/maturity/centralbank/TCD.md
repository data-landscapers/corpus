# Chad

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Chad's payment-institution licences are granted by the Central African Banking Commission (Cobac) under the CEMAC regulation of 21 December 2018](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). [Airtel Money (2023), Konoom (2025) and Moov Money Tchad (2026) hold one](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC regulates payment market structures, including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [BEAC's rules apply to the six CEMAC states, including Chad](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [AfricaNenda says BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY and is its overseer and settlement agent](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The switch serves payment providers in Chad](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What has been found is a regional payment system, which does not count. [Tchadinfos reports that Chad's mobile money providers are interoperable via GIMACPAY in the CEMAC zone](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [Chad is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [The UMAC ministerial committee validated it in April 2026, and it was launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [GIMACPAY has been in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).
