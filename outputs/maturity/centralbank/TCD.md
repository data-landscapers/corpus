# Chad

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Chad's payment-institution licences are granted by the Central African Banking Commission (Cobac) under the CEMAC regulation of 21 December 2018; Airtel Money (2023), Konoom (2025) and Moov Money Tchad (2026) hold one](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). [AfricaNenda says the regional central bank BEAC, whose rules apply to the six CEMAC states including Chad, regulates payment market structures including technical approvals and authorisations of payment solutions](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [AfricaNenda says BEAC owns 99.2% of GIMACPAY, is its overseer and settlement agent, and the switch serves payment providers in Chad](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Chad's mobile money providers are interoperable via GIMACPAY in the CEMAC zone, per Tchadinfos](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [Chad is one of six CEMAC states covered by GIMAC's interoperable QR Code standard, validated by the UMAC ministerial committee in April 2026 and launched on 29 July 2026 over GIMACPAY, in service since 2021](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).
