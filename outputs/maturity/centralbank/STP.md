# Sao Tome and Principe

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The Banco Central de São Tomé e Príncipe regulates the financial system and licenses payment institutions. In a communiqué of 20 January 2025 it [eased some documentation requirements for their licences for twelve months](<https://rstp.st/2025/01/20/comunicado-banco-central-de-sao-tome-e-principe-bcstp/>). The same communiqué says it had been easing licensing requirements for payment institutions and bureaux de change since June 2023.

**Overseeing a switch.** In 2026 the central bank's director of financial inclusion [told the Alliance for Financial Inclusion](<https://afi-global.org/opinion/financial-inclusion-in-sao-tome-and-principe/>) that the bank is expanding Dobra24. Dobra24 is the country's domestic interoperable retail payment network. The bank is also setting interoperability requirements for all market players.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest fact found is [the central bank's 2026 account of its own payment network](<https://afi-global.org/opinion/financial-inclusion-in-sao-tome-and-principe/>), which names no link abroad.
