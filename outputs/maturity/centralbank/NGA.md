# Nigeria

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Under rules released on 5 November 2026, the Central Bank of Nigeria authorises firms to offer stablecoin payments](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/cbn-sec-divide-crypto-oversight/>). [CBN rules require mobile money and payment service bank licences to sit with the telco licence holder](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [In January 2026 the CBN upgraded the licences of Moniepoint, OPay, Kuda, PalmPay and Paga to national](<https://businesspost.ng/banking/cbn-upgrades-operating-licences-of-opay-moniepoint-others-to-national/>), after they met its requirements. [BusinessDay calls TeamApt a CBN-licensed switching and processing company](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/teamapt-gains-direct-mastercard-network-access-in-strategic-payments-expansion/>). On 10 November 2026 the Senate passed [a bill letting the CBN designate, register and supervise fintechs as systemically important](<https://naltf.gov.ng/senate-passes-bill-to-regulate-systemically-important-fintechs/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Premium Times reports NIBSS is owned by the CBN and all licensed deposit banks](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/918939-nibss-transactilitys-partnership-delivers-national-payment-stack.html>). NIBSS clears and settles instant interbank transfers. [The CBN's payments supervision director urged institutions to join its new National Payment Stack](<https://theeagleonline.com.ng/nibss-rolls-out-nps-as-nip-successor/>). The stack had carried 26.55 million transactions by August 2026.

**Live on PAPSS.** [In May 2025, 22 Nigerian banks were fully onboarded on PAPSS](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/27/cross-border-payments-22-nigerian-commercial-banks-join-papps-scheme/>). The CBN had told all banks to adopt it. [Remita's app, launched on 22 September 2026, sends transfers through PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>).
