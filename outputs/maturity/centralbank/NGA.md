# Nigeria

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [TechCabal reports that under Central Bank of Nigeria licensing requirements, mobile money and payment service bank licences must be held by an entity with the relevant telco licence, and that a CBN directive required Airtel Money to transfer its 25% SmartCash stake back to Airtel Networks](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [BusinessDay says TeamApt is a Central Bank of Nigeria-licensed switching and processing company](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/teamapt-gains-direct-mastercard-network-access-in-strategic-payments-expansion/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [PAPSS CEO Mike Ogbalu said Nigeria was the first chair of the PAPSS Governing Council, made up of central bank governors, and continues to lead its rollout](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [PAPSS CEO Mike Ogbalu said Nigeria was the first chair of the PAPSS Governing Council, made up of central bank governors, and continues to lead its rollout](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>). [Remita's consumer app, launched 22 September 2026 in Lagos, supports instant transfers from Nigerian bank accounts to other African countries through PAPSS, per Remita's managing director](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>). Nairametrics reported in May 2025 that [22 Nigerian commercial banks were fully onboarded on PAPSS](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/27/cross-border-payments-22-nigerian-commercial-banks-join-papps-scheme/>), after the Central Bank of Nigeria told all banks to adopt it and start sending payments.
