# Nigeria

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Under rules released on 5 November 2026, the Central Bank of Nigeria authorises firms to offer stablecoin payments, transfers and settlement](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/cbn-sec-divide-crypto-oversight/>). [Such firms need an extra NGN 1 billion in capital](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/cbn-sec-divide-crypto-oversight/>). [CBN rules require mobile money and payment service bank licences to sit with an entity holding the relevant telco licence](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [A CBN directive made Airtel Money return its 25% SmartCash stake to Airtel Networks](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [BusinessDay calls TeamApt a CBN-licensed switching and processing company](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/teamapt-gains-direct-mastercard-network-access-in-strategic-payments-expansion/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [Nigeria was the first chair of the PAPSS council of central bank governors](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [In May 2025, 22 Nigerian commercial banks were fully onboarded on PAPSS](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/27/cross-border-payments-22-nigerian-commercial-banks-join-papps-scheme/>). [The Central Bank of Nigeria had told all banks to adopt it and start sending payments](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/05/27/cross-border-payments-22-nigerian-commercial-banks-join-papps-scheme/>). [Remita's app, launched on 22 September 2026, sends instant transfers from Nigerian bank accounts to other African countries through PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>). [Nigeria was the first chair of the PAPSS Governing Council and still leads its rollout, the PAPSS chief executive said](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says the Central Bank of Nigeria runs a national switch.
