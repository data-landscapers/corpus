# Guinea-Bissau

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The BCEAO's list of payment institutions licensed under Instruction n°001-01-2024 shows one in Guinea-Bissau, INTOUCH Guinée-Bissau, licensed 25 June 2025](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>). [The UMOA Banking Commission and BCEAO register updated 29 June 2026 lists one authorised payment institution (Intouch Guinee-Bissau, EP.GB.001/2025) and one e-money institution (Finances Mobiles Bissau, EME.GB.021/2023) for Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/4%20-%20Liste%20Guin%C3%A9e-Bissau_2026.pdf>). [The BCEAO states that as at 28 February 2026 one payment institution is authorised in Guinea-Bissau under Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services in the UMOA](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-28-fevrier-2026>). [The BCEAO's register of payment institutions authorised in the UMOA as at 15 September 2025 lists one for Guinea-Bissau, INTOUCH Guinée-Bissau SARL, number EP.GB.001/2025](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2025-09/LISTE_DES_ETABLISSEMENTS_DE_PAIEMENT_AGREES_15_septembre_2025.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BCEAO list says the BCEAO builds and runs PI-SPI, an instant payment switch serving Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>). [BCEAO runs PI-SPI, the instant payment platform for UEMOA members including Guinea-Bissau, launched 30 September 2025 with 175 connected participants a year later](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [BCEAO runs the PI-SPI interoperable instant-payment platform, on which Guinea-Bissau has 5 authorised institutions](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Guinea-Bissau is a UEMOA member in the BCEAO's PI-SPI regional instant payment system, in service](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [The BCEAO list places Guinea-Bissau in the eight-country WAEMU PI-SPI rail run by the BCEAO](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>). [BCEAO says Guinea-Bissau has 5 institutions authorised on its regional PI-SPI platform at 31 July 2026](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>). [IAfrikan places Guinea-Bissau in the eight-country WAEMU PI-SPI rail run by the BCEAO](<https://iafrikan.com/tomorrow-west-africas-central-bank-finds-out-who-really-runs-its-payments-market>).
