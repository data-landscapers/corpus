# Cote d'Ivoire

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** In September 2025 the BCEAO [granted licence EME.CI.025/2025 to Djogana Pay for its Peya Pay e-money](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637099/economie/inclusion-financieremonnaie-electronique-la-cote-divoire-enregistre-un-4e-etablissement>). It became the fourth electronic-money institution in Cote d'Ivoire. Such activity needs the central bank's approval first.

**Overseeing a switch.** The BCEAO [launched its PI-SPI instant payment platform on 30 September 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>), with 80 institutions connected. By April 2026 it [ran around the clock with 80 participants, nine of them e-money institutions](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>). In October 2026 the BCEAO said [Cote d'Ivoire's Treasury was already connected](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>). Since 2 November 2026 [interoperable e-money transfers must route through it](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/11/03/failed-transfers-hidden-fees-and-phantom-aliases-inside-the-messy-birth-of-west-africas-unified-payments-network/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional system, which does not count. PI-SPI [serves banks, microfinance, e-money and payment institutions across the UEMOA](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>). In November 2026 Wave said [its customers in Cote d'Ivoire can use PI-SPI from its app](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>).
