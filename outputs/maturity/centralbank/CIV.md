# Cote d'Ivoire

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The BCEAO granted licence EME.CI.025/2025 to Djogana Pay, its Peya Pay e-money, as the fourth electronic-money institution in Cote d'Ivoire, activities being possible only after central bank approval](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637099/economie/inclusion-financieremonnaie-electronique-la-cote-divoire-enregistre-un-4e-etablissement>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The BCEAO announced that public Treasuries are being connected to its PI-SPI interoperable instant-payment platform, with Cote d'Ivoire's already in service](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>). [Fraternite Matin reports the BCEAO officially launched the PI-SPI interoperable instant payment platform on 30 September 2025, with 80 institutions already connected and connection mandatory for all UEMOA financial institutions from 30 June 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Fraternite Matin reports the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform covers banks, microfinance, e-money and payment institutions across the UEMOA, Cote d'Ivoire's union](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>).
