# Kenya

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The National Bank of Rwanda said in March 2026 that its passporting agreement with the Central Bank of Kenya [covers payment service providers licensed by their home central bank](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>), so a provider the CBK licenses can work in Rwanda. In September 2026 the CBK and the National Treasury [published a draft National Payment System Policy and Bill, 2026](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/>), which would replace the National Payment System Act.

**Overseeing a switch.** Nothing found says the Central Bank of Kenya runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact is the [draft National Payment System Bill, 2026](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/>), which would set its powers over payment systems afresh.

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found says Kenyan banks or providers send and receive payments through PAPSS. The nearest held fact is the [passporting agreement with Rwanda](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>), a bilateral arrangement for licensed providers.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the Central Bank of Kenya runs or oversees a national switch, or that Kenyan payments run on PAPSS.
