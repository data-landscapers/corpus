# Kenya

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** In March 2026 the National Bank of Rwanda described its passporting agreement with the Central Bank of Kenya. [It covers payment service providers licensed by their home central bank](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>). So a provider the CBK licenses can work in Rwanda. [The CBK's directory of 6 November 2025 lists 41 authorised payment service providers](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Directory-of-Authorized-Payment-Service-Providers-6-November-2025.pdf>). Among them are Safaricom, Airtel Money and Telkom Kenya. In September 2026 the CBK and the National Treasury [published a draft National Payment System Policy and Bill, 2026](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/>). It would replace the National Payment System Act.

**Overseeing a switch.** Kenswitch is a domestic payment switch [regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya](<https://www.business-standard.com/content/press-releases-ani/kenswitch-partners-with-payu-s-wibmo-to-strengthen-online-card-security-in-kenya-126110200460_1.html>), its November 2026 release says. It connects banks, SACCOs, microfinance institutions and mobile money providers. The [draft National Payment System Bill, 2026](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/2026/09/21/draft-national-payment-system-policy-and-national-payment-system-bill-2026/>) would reset the bank's powers.

**Live on PAPSS.** In November 2026 the PAPSS chief executive [said KCB Group and Equity Bank are connected to the network](<https://capitalfm.africa/papss-bets-on-local-currencies-to-unlock-kenyas-africa-trade/>). He called Kenya an early adopter. [He did not disclose transaction volumes](<https://capitalfm.africa/papss-bets-on-local-currencies-to-unlock-kenyas-africa-trade/>). The same month PesaLink's operator [said people can now send money into Kenya from other PAPSS countries through PesaLink](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/19/pesalink-kenya-on-cheaper-payments/>). Nothing found says Kenyan banks send payments out through it.
