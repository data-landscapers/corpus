# Niger

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The BCEAO's list of payment institutions licensed under Instruction n°001-01-2024 shows one in Niger, iFUTUR, licensed 6 May 2025](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>). [Bénin Web TV reports, from the BCEAO list, that Niger has one licensed payment institution at 31 August 2026](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>). [Airtel Money Niger SA is authorised by the BCEAO as an electronic money issuer under Décision n° 194-07-2022, per ActuNiger (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://actuniger.com/societe/22299-transfert-dargent-airtel-money-niger-lance-son-service-international-pour-des-operations-simples-et-securisees-au-dela-des-frontieres.html>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [BCEAO says it set up the PI-SPI instant-payment interoperability platform, allowing account-to-account transfers between different types of accounts across the UMOA, of which Niger is a member](<https://www.lesahel.org/digitalisation-et-services-financiers-numeriques-de-la-bceao-renforcer-linclusion-financiere-la-modernisation-des-services-financiers-et-la-resilience-du-systeme-financier-de-l/>). [BCEAO runs PI-SPI, the instant payment platform for UEMOA members including Niger, launched 30 September 2025 with 175 connected participants a year later](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). The BCEAO's list updated to 30 September 2026 [authorises 13 participants in Niger to open PI-SPI services to the public](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>): eleven banks, Airtel Money Niger and i-FUTUR.

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [Niger is a UEMOA member in the BCEAO's PI-SPI regional instant payment system, in service](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [BCEAO says it set up the regional payment systems STAR-UMOA, SICA-UMOA and SAGETIL-UMOA for the Union](<https://www.lesahel.org/digitalisation-et-services-financiers-numeriques-de-la-bceao-renforcer-linclusion-financiere-la-modernisation-des-services-financiers-et-la-resilience-du-systeme-financier-de-l/>). [Niger is a UEMOA member state on the regional PI-SPI platform that BCEAO runs, with its institutions required to connect by 30 June 2026](<https://ns2.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Communiqu%C3%A9_Connexion_%C3%A0_PI-SPI_2_avril_2026.pdf>).
