# Cape Verde

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Banco de Cabo Verde says payment institutions and e-money institutions must be authorised by it to provide payment services or issue e-money, under Decree-Laws 8/2018 and 9/2018 and Aviso 6/2019](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>). [The Banco de Cabo Verde continued in 2024 its revision of the legal framework for payment services and the issuance of electronic money, with changes to the earlier revision proposal](<https://www.bcv.cv/SiteCollectionDocuments/2025/Relat%C3%B3rio%20de%20Sistema%20de%20Pagamentos%202024.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Banco de Cabo Verde says it manages SICIL, the interbank clearing system for cheques, credit transfers and payment cards operated by SISP, and oversees the payment system under its oversight policy](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>).
