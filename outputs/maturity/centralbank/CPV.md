# Cape Verde

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Banco de Cabo Verde says payment institutions and e-money institutions must be authorised by it to provide payment services or issue e-money](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>). [The rules are Decree-Laws 8/2018 and 9/2018 and Aviso 6/2019](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>). [In 2024 the Banco de Cabo Verde continued revising the legal framework for payment services and the issuance of electronic money](<https://www.bcv.cv/SiteCollectionDocuments/2025/Relat%C3%B3rio%20de%20Sistema%20de%20Pagamentos%202024.pdf>). [The work made changes to the earlier revision proposal](<https://www.bcv.cv/SiteCollectionDocuments/2025/Relat%C3%B3rio%20de%20Sistema%20de%20Pagamentos%202024.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Banco de Cabo Verde says it manages SICIL, the interbank clearing system for cheques, credit transfers and payment cards](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>). [SISP operates SICIL](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>). [The bank oversees the payment system under its oversight policy](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest fact: [in 2025 the Banco de Cabo Verde managed SICIL, the domestic interbank clearing system](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>).
