# Lesotho

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Central Bank of Lesotho's list of licensed financial institutions names five mobile money issuers](<https://centralbank.org.ls/other-financial-institutions-2/wpbdp_category/mobile-money-issuers-in-lesotho/>). They include VCL Financial Services, Lesotho Postbank and Sasai Econet Financial Services. [In 2025 the finance minister moved a Payment System Bill to second reading](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/>). It would license all payment service providers under one law and repeal the Payment Systems Act 2014.

**Overseeing a switch.** [From 11 May 2025 the Central Bank of Lesotho and leading banks routed local card payments at shops through LeSwitch, the national switch](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>). ATM payments had moved to it in October 2024. [Standard Lesotho Bank says the central bank leads LeSwitch, whose fast-payments phase was launched on 20 March 2024](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says Lesotho's banks send or receive payments through PAPSS. The nearest fact is [LeSwitch](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>), which links banks inside the country.
