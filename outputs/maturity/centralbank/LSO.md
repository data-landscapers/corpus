# Lesotho

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The finance minister moved the Payment System Bill, 2025 to second reading in the National Assembly; it would repeal the Payment Systems Act 2014, license all payment service providers under one law and give the Central Bank of Lesotho powers to oversee the national payment system](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Central Bank of Lesotho, with leading banks, introduced localized POS transactions under the LeSwitch national switch from 11 May 2025, after ATM localization in October 2024, with all local card transactions handled in-country by early 2025](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>). [The Central Bank of Lesotho leads LeSwitch, whose fast-payments phase was launched on 20 March 2024, per Standard Lesotho Bank](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>).
