# Liberia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [CBL Directive 002/2025 applies to all licensed Mobile Money Providers and threatens sanctions up to licence revocation under the New Payment Systems Law 2014 and Mobile Money Regulations 2014](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [A Central Bank of Liberia speaker says the central bank built the country's instant payment system, launched in 73 days, with banks and mobile money operators integrated](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>). [The Central Bank of Liberia deputy governor says the CBL's reforms include the RTGS, the ACH and a recently launched instant and interoperable payment system operating across banks and mobile network operators](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-backs-lra-s-move-to-billion-revenue-drive/article_7ef5e649-7ccd-4187-91bc-d17d1a19505c.html>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [The Central Bank of Liberia's Executive Governor said PAPSS was officially launched in Liberia on 27 March 2025, with the central bank as settlement agent](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>).
