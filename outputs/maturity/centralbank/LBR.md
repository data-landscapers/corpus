# Liberia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [CBL Directive 002/2025 applies to all licensed Mobile Money Providers](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>). [It threatens sanctions up to licence revocation under the New Payment Systems Law 2014 and Mobile Money Regulations 2014](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The central bank launched Pay Na-Na, the country's first instant payment system, on 16 December 2025](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/west-africa-leans-towards-three-country-plan-for-regional-payment-system-liberia-central-bank-chief-says/>). It links the two mobile money operators. In November 2026 the Governor said [banks and fintechs were still being brought onto it](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-outlines-four-priorities-to-transform-liberia-s-digital-payment-system/article_39f669bb-c385-4c6e-a854-e9f6581fc1e6.html>). [A Central Bank of Liberia speaker says the central bank built the country's instant payment system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>). [It was launched in 73 days, with banks and mobile money operators integrated](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>). [The Central Bank of Liberia deputy governor says the CBL's reforms include the RTGS and the ACH](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-backs-lra-s-move-to-billion-revenue-drive/article_7ef5e649-7ccd-4187-91bc-d17d1a19505c.html>). [They also include a recently launched instant and interoperable payment system operating across banks and mobile network operators](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-backs-lra-s-move-to-billion-revenue-drive/article_7ef5e649-7ccd-4187-91bc-d17d1a19505c.html>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [The Central Bank of Liberia's Executive Governor said PAPSS was officially launched in Liberia on 27 March 2025](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>). [The central bank is its settlement agent](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>). In November 2026 he said [Liberian traders' payments to Nigeria and Ghana are settled through PAPSS](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/11/18/nigeria-ghana-proposed-as-hosts-for-ecowas-regional-payment-system/>).
