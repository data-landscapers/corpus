# South Africa

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The South African Reserve Bank's National Payment System Department says it regulates payment systems, third-party payment providers and e-money providers, with access to the payment system by designation or authorisation under the National Payment System Act 78 of 1998 and its directives](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>). [ASAPP says it has engaged the SARB on its Banks Act Exemption and Authorisation Framework, covering future authorisation of payment activities for regulated fintech payment providers](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [TechCentral reports the South African Reserve Bank took a 50% interest in PayInc, operator of PayShap, in late 2025, and the 2026 budget speech named PayInc the shared digital payments infrastructure supporting interoperability across providers](<https://techcentral.co.za/three-years-in-payshap-pivots-to-merchants/281687/>).

**Linked regionally.** [Notice 2024 of 2022 records the SARB revoking the designation of SIRESS and designating the SADC Real Time Gross Settlement System (SADC-RTGS) as a designated settlement system (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>).
