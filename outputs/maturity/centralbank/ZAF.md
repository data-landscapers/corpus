# South Africa

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The South African Reserve Bank's National Payment System Department says it regulates payment systems, third-party payment providers and e-money providers, with access to the payment system by designation or authorisation under the National Payment System Act 78 of 1998 and its directives](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>). [ASAPP says it has engaged the SARB on its Banks Act Exemption and Authorisation Framework, covering future authorisation of payment activities for regulated fintech payment providers](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). The Reserve Bank [published a draft framework for authorising non-bank payment and e-money providers in May 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/south-africa-fintech-payment-network/>), with the final framework due by the end of November 2026.

**Overseeing a switch.** [TechCentral reports the South African Reserve Bank took a 50% interest in PayInc, operator of PayShap, in late 2025, and the 2026 budget speech named PayInc the shared digital payments infrastructure supporting interoperability across providers](<https://techcentral.co.za/three-years-in-payshap-pivots-to-merchants/281687/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found shows the country live on PAPSS. What is held is a regional payment system, which does not count: [the Reserve Bank's governor said on 20 October 2026 that qualifying regional payments are carried on the SADC real-time gross settlement system and that those under R100,000 are to move to TCIB by the end of April 2027](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>). [In 2022 the Reserve Bank designated the SADC Real Time Gross Settlement System (SADC-RTGS) as a settlement system](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>).
