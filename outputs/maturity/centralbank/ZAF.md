# South Africa

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Reserve Bank's National Payment System Department says](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>) it regulates payment systems, third-party payment providers and e-money providers under the National Payment System Act of 1998. For 27 years the Payments Association of South Africa was the banks' management body for the payment system. When the Reserve Bank's recognition of the association lapsed on 2 September 2026, [licensing, authorisation and registration of payment institutions moved to the Reserve Bank](<https://eriinfo.com/end-of-an-era-for-south-africas-payments-system/>). In May 2026 the Reserve Bank [published a draft framework for authorising non-bank payment and e-money providers](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/south-africa-fintech-payment-network/>). The governor planned the final framework for the end of November 2026.

**Overseeing a switch.** In November 2025 the Reserve Bank [bought a 50% stake in BankservAfrica](<https://www.businessday.co.za/business-times/2026-08-01-no-plans-to-add-non-banks-to-payinc-shareholding-kganyago/>), the national automated clearing house, and renamed it PayInc. [PayInc runs low-value payments, including instant payments](<https://eriinfo.com/end-of-an-era-for-south-africas-payments-system/>). Since September 2026 the clearing houses for card and high-value payments have sat with the Reserve Bank itself.

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found shows the country live on PAPSS. Its regional payments run on SADC systems, which do not count here. On 20 October 2026 [the governor said](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>) that qualifying regional payments run on the SADC real-time gross settlement system. Those under R100,000 are to move to TCIB, the regional low-value scheme, by the end of April 2027.
