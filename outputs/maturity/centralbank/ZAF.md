# South Africa

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [ASAPP says it has engaged the SARB on its Banks Act Exemption and Authorisation Framework, covering future authorisation of payment activities for regulated fintech payment providers](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). [A proposed activity-based authorisation framework for non-banks performing payment activities is still being developed, with the draft in industry engagement and piloting and release expected in the first quarter of 2027 (Standard Bank's Nthabiseng Mohale)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [TechCentral reports the South African Reserve Bank took a 50% interest in PayInc, operator of PayShap, in late 2025, and the 2026 budget speech named PayInc the shared digital payments infrastructure supporting interoperability across providers](<https://techcentral.co.za/three-years-in-payshap-pivots-to-merchants/281687/>).

**Linked regionally.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcentral.co.za/three-years-in-payshap-pivots-to-merchants/281687/>).
