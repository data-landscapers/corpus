# Madagascar

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [In June 2026 2424.mg reported that the CSBF is attached to the Banky foiben'i Madagasikara](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>). [Under the e-money institutions law it can grant or withdraw licences](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>). [It can also issue regulatory instructions and audit e-money institutions](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>).

**Overseeing a switch.** No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch. The nearest held fact: [in June 2026 the CSBF held the power to audit e-money institutions](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [in June 2026 the CSBF licensed e-money institutions under national law](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the central bank runs or oversees a national switch.
