# Egypt

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Ahram Online says Instapay, a mobile payments application, is licensed by the Central Bank of Egypt](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [Six Egyptian banks have applied to the CBE for final approvals to integrate with PAPSS](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [In June 2025 the Central Bank of Egypt issued Rules for Licensing and Registration of Payment System Operators and Payment Service Providers](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>). [Existing institutions had to apply for a CBE licence within one year, to June 2026](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>). [The bank also issued governance, internal control and fit-and-proper rules for them under Law No. 194 of 2020](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>). In April 2025 the bank [licensed telecoms to send transfers abroad by e-wallet](<https://www.almezan.news/242191>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [Ahram Online says Instapay is licensed by the Central Bank of Egypt](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [It runs through the bank's Instant Payment Network, the national real-time payment infrastructure](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [The CBE says its InstaPay instant payments network had over 16 million users and 1.1 billion transactions by June 2025](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>). [The national Meeza card system runs alongside it](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335311.aspx>).

**Live on PAPSS.** [The Central Bank of Egypt agreed to join PAPSS in November 2024](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/23/instapay-to-connect-with-pan-african-payment-system-as-six-egyptian-banks-apply-for-integration/>). [Egypt is described as one of the largest economies in the PAPSS network](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/23/instapay-to-connect-with-pan-african-payment-system-as-six-egyptian-banks-apply-for-integration/>). [Daily News Egypt reports that the Central Bank of Egypt officially joined the PAPSS network in November 2024](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/09/19/central-bank-of-egypt-governor-discusses-african-payment-network-expansion-at-nairobi-meeting/>). [Its Governor has sat on the PAPSS Governing Council since January 2025](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/09/19/central-bank-of-egypt-governor-discusses-african-payment-network-expansion-at-nairobi-meeting/>).
