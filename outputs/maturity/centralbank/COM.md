# Comoros

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** No source found says the central bank's role over payments is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [Al-Watwan reports the Banque Centrale des Comores introduced and launched Komor Switch, a national platform connecting all banks and mobile money services for interoperability, and KomorPay, an automated interbank transfer system](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Overseeing a switch.** [Al-Watwan reports the Banque Centrale des Comores introduced and launched Komor Switch, a national platform connecting all banks and mobile money services for interoperability, and KomorPay, an automated interbank transfer system](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>). [The Banque Centrale des Comores says it is operator, supervisor and participant of KomorPay (ATS), the national interbank payment system operational since 23 April 2026](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret(3).pdf>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>).
