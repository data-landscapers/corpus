# Comoros

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Banque Centrale des Comores' financial inclusion strategy says its regulation sets the licensing terms for e-money issuers](<https://banque-comores.km/article/show/strategie-nationale-dinclusion-financiere-20252030>). It cites regulation no. 1 of 2017 on e-money institutions. It also says the payment services law has the BCC authorise digital transactions.

**Overseeing a switch.** [Al-Watwan reports the BCC launched Komor Switch, a national platform linking all banks and mobile money services](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>). [The BCC says it operates, supervises and takes part in KomorPay, the interbank payment system](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret(3).pdf>). KomorPay has run since 23 April 2026. [Mayotte Hebdo reports it cut cheque and transfer processing from three days to one working day](<https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/economie/comores-lancement-de-nouveaux-systemes-de-paiement/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact is [the launch of Komor Switch](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>).
