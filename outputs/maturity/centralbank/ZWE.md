# Zimbabwe

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Herald reports that the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe oversees mobile money by directive](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>). A 2021 directive enforced one line, one wallet for each customer, and the Monetary Policy Statement of February 2026 directed mobile money operators to verify their customers by 30 June 2026. The Reserve Bank sets these rules for the operators directly, through its own directives and policy statements.

**Overseeing a switch.** The nearest held fact is the Reserve Bank's [oversight of mobile money operators by directive](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>), including its order of February 2026 that every operator verify its customers by 30 June 2026.

**Live on PAPSS.** [Afreximbank says Zimbabwe is processing $5.8 million a month through PAPSS](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>), the Pan-African Payment and Settlement System, as reported in October 2026. [The same source says most banks in Southern Africa have joined the system](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>), which places Zimbabwe among the countries whose payments run on it. The Financial Gazette reported on 9 October 2026 that [CBZ Bank had gone live on PAPSS](<https://fingaz.co.zw/2026/10/09/cbz-links-new-trade-venture-to-system/>).
