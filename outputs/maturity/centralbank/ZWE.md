# Zimbabwe

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe's 2024 annual report says it licenses, oversees and supervises payment system providers](<https://www.rbz.co.zw/documents/ar/2024%20ANNUAL%20REPORT.pdf>). It reviewed 52 applications from them in 2024. [The Herald reports it also oversees mobile money by directive](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>). A 2021 directive enforced one line, one wallet. Operators had to verify their customers by 30 June 2026.

**Overseeing a switch.** [AfricaNenda's 2024 case study says ZIPIT, Zimswitch's instant payment system, is licensed by the Reserve Bank](<https://africanenda.org/wp-content/uploads/siips_2024_ZIPIT_CaseStudy_en.pdf>). The Reserve Bank shapes its governance, product approvals and pricing. It requires mobile money operators to join.

**Live on PAPSS.** [Afreximbank says Zimbabwe is processing $5.8 million a month through PAPSS](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>). This was reported in October 2026. The Financial Gazette reported on 9 October 2026 that [CBZ Bank had gone live on PAPSS](<https://fingaz.co.zw/2026/10/09/cbz-links-new-trade-venture-to-system/>).
