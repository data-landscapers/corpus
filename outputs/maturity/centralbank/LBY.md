# Libya

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Central Bank of Libya regulates mobile and e-wallet payment by circular: Circular 9/2026 of 11 March 2026 sets e-wallet rules for banks, Moamalat and e-payment companies, with compliance checked by field inspection by its banking and currency supervision department](<https://bankawy.net/banks/mssrf-lebea-almrkze-edta-dtwabtd-jdedtt-llm/bankawy/>). [Central Bank of Libya circular 9 of 2026 allows licensed electronic payment companies to issue e-wallets to legally resident foreigners, and its Banking and Monetary Control Department will inspect the companies for compliance](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/511044>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Central Bank of Libya directed commercial banks by letter of 4 March 2026 to activate the LYPay instant payment system in their banking apps for QR-code purchases at POS devices by 8 March](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>). [The Central Bank of Libya says it runs the LYPay and OnePay instant payment services, launched in 2024, and will raise their transfer and purchase limits](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/instant-payment-services-statistics/>).

**Linked regionally.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>).
