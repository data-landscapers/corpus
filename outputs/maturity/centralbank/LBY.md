# Libya

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** The Central Bank of Libya [regulates e-wallet payments by circular](<https://bankawy.net/banks/mssrf-lebea-almrkze-edta-dtwabtd-jdedtt-llm/bankawy/>). Circular 9 of 11 March 2026 sets e-wallet rules for banks, Moamalat and e-payment companies. It also [lets licensed payment companies issue e-wallets to legally resident foreigners](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/511044>), with compliance checked by inspection. Circular 6 of February 2026 [lets licensed exchange companies send direct foreign remittances](<https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/08/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-6-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2026.pdf>). The cap is USD 100,000 for firms and small traders.

**Overseeing a switch.** The central bank [runs the LYPay and OnePay instant payment services](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/instant-payment-services-statistics/>), launched in 2024. On 31 July 2025 it said it [was paying public salaries into commercial bank accounts over its own instant payment network](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/485074>). In March 2026 it [told banks to switch on LYPay QR payments at point-of-sale devices](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>) by 8 March.

**Live on PAPSS.** The central bank [joined PAPSS at the end of December 2025](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7412119263794208768-y9NP>). No Libyan bank is reported live on it.
