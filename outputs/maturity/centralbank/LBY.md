# Libya

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [The Central Bank of Libya regulates mobile and e-wallet payment by circular: Circular 9/2026 of 11 March 2026 sets e-wallet rules for banks, Moamalat and e-payment companies, with compliance checked by field inspection by its banking and currency supervision department](<https://bankawy.net/banks/mssrf-lebea-almrkze-edta-dtwabtd-jdedtt-llm/bankawy/>). [Central Bank of Libya circular 9 of 2026 allows licensed electronic payment companies to issue e-wallets to legally resident foreigners, and its Banking and Monetary Control Department will inspect the companies for compliance](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/511044>). Circular 6 of February 2026 [lets exchange companies the central bank has licensed send direct foreign remittances](<https://cbl.gov.ly/micifaf/2026/08/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-6-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2026.pdf>) of up to USD 100,000 for firms and small traders.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Central Bank of Libya directed commercial banks by letter of 4 March 2026 to activate the LYPay instant payment system in their banking apps for QR-code purchases at POS devices by 8 March](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>). [The Central Bank of Libya says it runs the LYPay and OnePay instant payment services, launched in 2024, and will raise their transfer and purchase limits](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/instant-payment-services-statistics/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** The Central Bank of Libya [joined PAPSS at the end of December 2025](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-papss-papss-activity-7412119263794208768-y9NP>), but no Libyan bank is reported live on it. No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system in service.
