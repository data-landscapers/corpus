# Namibia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Bank of Namibia Deputy Governor Leonie Dunn said licensing and supervisory responsibilities for payments are being transferred to the central bank](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>). [This is under a new Payments Association framework](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>). [The Bank of Namibia issued the PSD-1 determination under section 45 of the Payment System Management Act 2023](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>). [It sets the licensing and authorisation of payment service providers, including electronic money issuers](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>). [It took effect on Gazette publication on 6 January 2026](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>). [MTC holds an electronic money licence from the Bank of Namibia](<https://thebrief.com.na/2024/10/mtc-targets-over-50-of-namibians-who-are-unbanked-with-maris-offering/>), The Brief reported in October 2024. Its Maris service runs under the Bank's regulation.

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Namibia said its instant payments solution became operational in June 2026](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>). [The first live transactions ran between its Currency Management and Banking Operations Department and Bank Windhoek, Nampost and Letshego Bank Namibia](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** Nothing found says Namibian banks or providers send and receive payments through PAPSS. The Bank of Namibia issued a directive in March 2025. It [orders providers to move low-value transfers within the Common Monetary Area from SADC-RTGS to the TCIB scheme by April 2027](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/1b/1b0b2668-5468-400b-8285-c2e3fc386a03.pdf>).
