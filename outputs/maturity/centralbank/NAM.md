# Namibia

## Governance role of central bank

**Licensing providers.** [Bank of Namibia Deputy Governor Leonie Dunn said licensing and supervisory responsibilities for payments are being transferred to the central bank under a new Payments Association framework](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>). [The Bank of Namibia issued the PSD-1 determination under section 45 of the Payment System Management Act 2023, setting the licensing and authorisation of payment service providers, including electronic money issuers, effective on Gazette publication on 6 January 2026](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>).

**Overseeing a switch.** [The Bank of Namibia said its instant payments solution became operational in June 2026, with live transactions first processed between its Currency Management and Banking Operations Department and Bank Windhoek, Nampost and Letshego Bank Namibia](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>).

**Live on PAPSS.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/07/namibia-becomes-fourth-african-country-to-launch-instant-payments-system/>).
