# Nigeria

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** The government [released N-ATLAS, built by Awarri, an open-source language model for Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Nigerian English, in September 2025](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/nigeria-launches-landmark-ai-model-powered-by-awarri-to-advance-languages-and-ai-at-scale/>). [Its model card describes a Llama-3 8B fine-tune on about 392 million tokens](<https://huggingface.co/NCAIR1/N-ATLaS>). In November 2026 Data Science Nigeria [opened registration for contributors to a National AI Glossary Project](<https://www.nigeriastartupact.ng/google-opens-new-opportunity-for-nigerians-to-teach-ai-yoruba-igbo-hausa-and-pidgin/>), with Google support. It builds language resources in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa and Pidgin.

**AI computing.** [Udu Technologies runs its Africa GPU Hub through the AGH Cloud platform, with deployments in Nigeria](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/11/udutech-adds-baro-ai-to-expand-gpu-supply/>), TechCabal reported in September 2026. In June 2026 the government [launched shared AI computing through the Nigeria AI Scaling Hub](<https://techeconomy.ng/naish-tijani-unveils-ai-scaling-hub-to-power-startups-public-sector-innovation>), hosted by Galaxy Backbone. On 26 October 2026 INFRAGORA Global Capital said it [plans to start building two AI-ready data centres in 2027](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/infragora-1-billion-data-centres/>).

**Shared with users.** By November 2026 [N-ATLAS had more than 140 registered collaborators and 18 certified third-party applications](<https://von.gov.ng/nitda-seeks-stakeholders-input-to-review-digital-strategy/>). The figures were given at a NITDA consultation on its digital strategy. Commercial adoption was at an early stage.
