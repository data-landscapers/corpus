# Nigeria

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [TechCabal reports Udu Technologies operates its Africa GPU Hub through the AGH Cloud platform, with deployments in Nigeria](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/11/udutech-adds-baro-ai-to-expand-gpu-supply/>). [TechCabal reports Udu Technologies, founded in February 2025, is building a distributed GPU infrastructure business and added BARO AI Poseidon GPU servers to AGH Cloud under a partnership signed 2026-09-09](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/11/udutech-adds-baro-ai-to-expand-gpu-supply/>). [IAfrica reports that Nigerian startup Awarri developed African language datasets and speech technologies and was technical partner behind Nigeria's N-ATLAS national language model](<https://iafrica.com/nigeria-tops-africa-on-responsible-ai-governance-index-which-is-not-the-same-as-deploying-ai/>). [The communications ministry says N-ATLAS, a government-backed multilingual large language model, was launched in 2025](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/2025-year-in-review-of-activities-of-the-honourable-minister-dr-bosun-tijani/>), [supporting Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/11/05/funding-gap-threatens-nigerias-ai-growth-despite-strong-implementation-report/>). [In December 2025 a Gates-funded consortium validated the design of the AI-enabled DAES farm advisory platform, with pilots planned in three states](<https://businessday.ng/agriculture/article/nigeria-pilots-ai-enabled-digital-extension-system-to-bridge-farm-advisory-gap/>). [In June 2026 the government launched shared AI computing through the Nigeria AI Scaling Hub, hosted by Galaxy Backbone, for startups, researchers and public bodies](<https://techeconomy.ng/naish-tijani-unveils-ai-scaling-hub-to-power-startups-public-sector-innovation>). [In October 2026 minister Tijani said N-ATLAS was in use and that Nigeria was working with the Gates Foundation on a national shared computing facility](<https://newsghana.com.gh/nigerias-tijani-pitches-fibre-and-skills-drive-for-ai-in-seoul/>).

**AI computing.** [TechCabal reports Udu Technologies operates its Africa GPU Hub through the AGH Cloud platform, with deployments in Nigeria and the United States](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/11/udutech-adds-baro-ai-to-expand-gpu-supply/>).
