# Malawi

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Malawi under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [In August 2026 AGRA's MlimiVoice Malawi, an agriculture language-AI project, was among 26 projects selected under LINGUA Africa](<https://iafrica.com/lingua-africa-funds-26-projects-covering-50-african-languages-tied-to-health-farming-and-justice-use-cases/>). [Its funders are the Microsoft AI for Good Lab, Gates Foundation, Masakhane African Languages Hub and Google.org](<https://iafrica.com/lingua-africa-funds-26-projects-covering-50-african-languages-tied-to-health-farming-and-justice-use-cases/>). [Each awardee gets up to $250,000 cash and $400,000 compute credits](<https://iafrica.com/lingua-africa-funds-26-projects-covering-50-african-languages-tied-to-health-farming-and-justice-use-cases/>). [In June 2026 the government launched the Malawi Low Resource Language Data Trust Initiative to develop Chichewa AI models](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/>). [It assembles licensed Chichewa content, with World Bank and Gates Foundation support](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/>). [The Malawi Voice Data Commons fine-tunes a speech model on new Chichewa data and runs on local servers to transcribe emergency reports](<https://portulansinstitute.org/introducing-the-malawi-voice-data-commons/>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Malawi](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
