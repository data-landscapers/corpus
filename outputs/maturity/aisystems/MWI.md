# Malawi

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Malawi under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [IAfrica.com reports AGRA's MlimiVoice Malawi, an agriculture language-AI project, is among 26 projects selected under LINGUA Africa, funded by the Microsoft AI for Good Lab, Gates Foundation, Masakhane African Languages Hub and Google.org with up to $250,000 cash and $400,000 compute credits per awardee](<https://iafrica.com/lingua-africa-funds-26-projects-covering-50-african-languages-tied-to-health-farming-and-justice-use-cases/>). [TechAfrica News says the Malawi government has launched the Malawi Low Resource Language Data Trust Initiative to develop Chichewa AI models and assemble licensed Chichewa content, supported by the World Bank and the Gates Foundation](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/24/malawi-launches-chichewa-ai-initiative-to-drive-digital-inclusion-and-innovation/>). [The Malawi Voice Data Commons fine-tunes a speech model on new Chichewa data and runs on local servers to transcribe emergency reports](<https://portulansinstitute.org/introducing-the-malawi-voice-data-commons/>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Malawi](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
