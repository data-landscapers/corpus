# Mali

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [RobotsMali AI4D Lab in Bamako released 612 hours of transcribed Bambara speech, fine-tuned ASR models (Parakeet and QuartzNet based, deployed in the An Be Kalan app) and the 45-recording Nyana-Eval benchmark, as part of African Next Voices](<https://robotsmali.org/en/african-next-voices-robotsmali-releases-major-bambara-dataset-models-research-tools/>). [RobotsMali AI4D Lab published two open-source Bambara speech-recognition models, Soloni-114M TDT-CTC and QuartzNet-15x5, trained on about 35 hours of Bambara audio, with weights on Hugging Face](<https://robotsmali.org/en/robotsmali-ai4d-lab-devoile-deux-modeles-open-source-pour-la-reconnaissance-vocale-en-bambara/>). [The AI and robotics centre near Kati, whose first stone was laid in June 2023, was still unfinished in May 2025](<https://benbere.org/au-grin/intelligence-artificielle-mali-est-aujourdhui/>). [In October 2026 Tidiani Togola said RobotsMali and the MALIBA-AI community had gathered hundreds of hours of Bambara recordings and built speech-recognition tools for Bambara, Bomu, Dogon and Songhai](<https://amap.ml/2026/10/23/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainete-numerique-le-mali-face-aux-defis-de-la-gouvernance-et-de-la-desinformation/>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://robotsmali.org/en/african-next-voices-robotsmali-releases-major-bambara-dataset-models-research-tools/>).
