## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Communications minister Sam George said the government has committed US$250 million to the first phase of a national AI compute centre, within US$270 million for AI infrastructure and an AI fund](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-targets-5bn-in-annual-economic-gains-from-ai-sam-george/>). [Communications Minister Sam George tasked KNUST experts to lead development of a Twi language model, with other universities to lead models for other Ghanaian languages, as the government decided national language models must be built locally](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-tasks-knust-to-develop-twi-ai-model-as-ghana-plans-local-language-artificial-intelligence/>). [KNUST researchers developed SignTalk-Gh, an AI system trained on Ghanaian Sign Language datasets that translates sign to text and audio and back for doctor-patient consultations, funded via the RAIL AI4SD project](<https://www.knust.edu.gh/news/news-items/knust-researchers-develop-ai-system-bridge-ghanaian-sign-language-communication-gap-healthcare>).
