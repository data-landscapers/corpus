# Ghana

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Communications minister Sam George said the government has committed US$250 million to the first phase of a national AI compute centre](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-targets-5bn-in-annual-economic-gains-from-ai-sam-george/>). [Communications Minister Sam George tasked KNUST experts to lead development of a Twi language model, with other universities to lead models for other Ghanaian languages, as the government decided national language models must be built locally](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-tasks-knust-to-develop-twi-ai-model-as-ghana-plans-local-language-artificial-intelligence/>). [KNUST researchers developed SignTalk-Gh, an AI system trained on Ghanaian Sign Language datasets that translates sign to text and audio and back for doctor-patient consultations, funded via the RAIL AI4SD project](<https://www.knust.edu.gh/news/news-items/knust-researchers-develop-ai-system-bridge-ghanaian-sign-language-communication-gap-healthcare>). [Hubtel's chief executive says the company set up an AI lab in 2023 and built its own AI models, testing some with other businesses](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/hubtel-ceo-calls-for-national-data-sharing-framework-to-boost-ai-development/>). [In December 2025 Ghana and the UAE signed a US$1 billion agreement for an AI hub at Ningo-Prampram, including US$180 million for an AI compute hub to be built by G42](<https://african.business/2025/12/innov-africa-deals/ghana-and-uae-sign-1bn-deal-to-build-africas-largest-innovation-and-ai-hub>). [In October 2026 Bright Simons wrote that this centre, to host the national GhanaChat platform, was in procurement](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bright-simons-flags-computing-capacity-error-in-ghanas-ai-strategy-and-questions-ghanachat-costs/>).
