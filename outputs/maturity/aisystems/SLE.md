# Sierra Leone

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In March 2026 EDITH, built for the education ministry by Sierra Leonean AI specialist Mohamed Alpha, was being prepared for handover](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). [It had a GBP 50,000 EdTech Hub support package and was not yet released](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). [Farmerline signed a five-year MoU with the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation on 12 June 2024](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [It commits Farmerline to develop speech recognition and text-to-speech models for the agricultural sector](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [Farmerline claims a first-of-its-kind Krio language model](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [In June 2025 Africell launched AI-EVA, a voice assistant in several local languages that works on basic feature phones](<https://www.financialstandardsl.com/article.php?title=africell-unveils-ai-eva-and-afrogpt-at-summit>). [Its AfroGPT, an offline model for Sierra Leonean languages and curricula, was to be piloted in schools](<https://www.financialstandardsl.com/article.php?title=africell-unveils-ai-eva-and-afrogpt-at-summit>).
