# Sierra Leone

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [EDITH was developed by Sierra Leonean AI specialist Mohamed Alpha for the education ministry with a GBP 50,000 EdTech Hub support package, and is being prepared for handover, not yet released](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). [Farmerline says its five-year MoU of 12 June 2024 with the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation commits it to develop language and speech models (speech recognition, text-to-speech) for Sierra Leone's agricultural sector, and it claims a first-of-its-kind Krio language model](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [In June 2025 Africell launched AI-EVA, a voice assistant in several local languages that works on basic feature phones, and AfroGPT, an offline model for Sierra Leonean languages and curricula, to be piloted in schools](<https://www.financialstandardsl.com/article.php?title=africell-unveils-ai-eva-and-afrogpt-at-summit>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>).
