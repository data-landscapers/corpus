# Sierra Leone

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** In May 2025 the DSTI's Kuma page said it had [built initial Krio and Mende text-to-speech prototypes with audio samples in a playground, an initial Sierra Leonean language model and a Whisper-based speech-to-text pipeline](<https://kuma.dsti.gov.sl/research>), all below production quality. [In March 2026 EDITH, built for the education ministry by Sierra Leonean AI specialist Mohamed Alpha, was being prepared for handover](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). [It had a GBP 50,000 EdTech Hub support package and was not yet released](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). [In June 2025 Africell launched AI-EVA, a voice assistant in several local languages that works on basic feature phones](<https://www.financialstandardsl.com/article.php?title=africell-unveils-ai-eva-and-afrogpt-at-summit>). [Farmerline signed a five-year MoU with the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation on 12 June 2024 to develop speech recognition and text-to-speech models for agriculture](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>).

**AI computing.** [On 16 November 2026 Climate Home News reported that Emergent Grid, funded through the Mission300 Accelerator, will test small-scale AI data centres on existing mini-grids in Sierra Leone](<https://www.climatechangenews.com/2026/11/16/can-ai-data-centres-help-power-underserved-african-communities/>). No source found says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country.

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 Africell's AfroGPT model was to be piloted in schools and community centres](<https://www.financialstandardsl.com/article.php?title=africell-unveils-ai-eva-and-afrogpt-at-summit>).

***Also on file***. [Krio language model, Darli AI and Krio AI Agronomist](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>).

***Not found***. No source found says where Africell's AI-EVA was built. No source found since October 2023 says a GPU or AI-accelerator facility is in service in the country.
