# Cape Verde

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [On 19 November 2024 Cabo Verde launched an AI application for financial and accounting management](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/-/vice-primeiro-ministro-destaca-transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-como-pilar-do-desenvolvimento-de-cabo-verde>). [NOSi developed it with the national company SINTAXY](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/-/vice-primeiro-ministro-destaca-transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-como-pilar-do-desenvolvimento-de-cabo-verde>). [In April 2025 Uni-CV researcher Mateus Andrade presented acoustic AI models in Creole for health applications](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/eitec/2025/04/19/uni-cv-com-ciclo-de-palestras-sobre-inteligencia-artificial-e-quimica-computacional/96651>). [In 2026 the University of Cabo Verde was building AI and IoT water-management systems in the SSWAxC4V project](<https://www.unicv.edu.cv/pt/nots-geral/5824-unico-projeto-arise-em-cabo-verde-apresenta-resultados-a-african-academy-of-sciences>). [In March 2024 UNDP reported its smart-irrigation AI models, built with Primebotics, with a prototype on the campus](<https://www.undp.org/acceleratorlabs/blog/agricultural-transition-using-internet-things-and-artificial-intelligence>). [The University of Cabo Verde and Iscte began the ORAL project in 2025 to build Creole text and speech corpora and a first Creole language model](<https://www.iscte-iul.pt/noticias/2819/iscte-lanca-projeto-internacional-para-desenvolvimento-de-modelos-de-linguagem-crioulo-caboverdiano>).
