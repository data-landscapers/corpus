# Burkina Faso

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** On 15 October 2026 the CITADEL centre and the Data Lab [launched L'Essentiel.bf](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991>), a free platform that uses AI to summarise national news in French and Mooré. It serves the news as text and audio every 15 minutes. On 2 November 2026 Sahal Tech and Old Rock Group [agreed to build an African language data platform](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/06/burkina-fasos-sahal-tech-partners-with-old-rock-group-to-develop-african-language-ai/>), starting with Mooré, Dioula, Fulfulde and Bambara. In October 2026 an official said [the national broadcaster RTB had signed up to supply language data for AI models](<https://lefaso.net/?id_article=150234&page=impression>). In October 2025 CITADEL [showed a Mooré-French translation tool](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/>). In March 2025 the minister said [an AI engine and two chatbots were being finalised](<https://www.ledeputemetre.net/spip.php?article342=>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The minister said in 2025 that [the PACDIGITAL project funds AI centres of excellence](<https://an.bf/127>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. [L'Essentiel.bf is free to the public](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991>), but no user count is given.
