# Burkina Faso

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [On 15 October 2026 the CITADEL centre and the Data Lab launched L'Essentiel.bf in Ouagadougou, a free web and mobile platform that uses AI to gather, summarise and serve national news every 15 minutes in French and Mooré, as text and audio](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991>). [A Ministry of Digital Transition AI expert presented the national strategy for designing and deploying an AI model adapted to Burkinabe realities, on 12 August 2026](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/08/15/burkina-faso-et-nigeria-renforcent-a-ouagadougou-leur-cooperation-numerique-lia-et-la-connectivite/>). [Specialists at CITADEL, Virtual University of Burkina, developed a Mooré-to-French and French-to-Mooré translation tool, shown in a live demonstration on 21 October 2025](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/>). [The minister said the PACDIGITAL project, run by the Ministry of Digital Transition, provides substantial funding to set up AI centres of excellence and study grants](<https://an.bf/127>). [In March 2025 the minister told parliament the DJAM AI engine, for analysing threats to social cohesion in text and audio, and two chatbots were being finalised](<https://www.ledeputemetre.net/spip.php?article342=>). [In November 2025 the government said universities were studying over fifty AI solutions, including the LegiChat legal-text app](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cstd2025-26_ai_c05_burkina_faso_fr.pdf>).
