# Burkina Faso

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** On 15 October 2026 the CITADEL centre and the Data Lab [launched L'Essentiel.bf](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991>), a free platform that uses AI to summarise national news in French and Mooré. It serves the news as text and audio every 15 minutes. On 2 November 2026 Sahal Tech and Old Rock Group [agreed to build an African language data platform](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/06/burkina-fasos-sahal-tech-partners-with-old-rock-group-to-develop-african-language-ai/>), starting with Mooré, Dioula, Fulfulde and Bambara. In October 2026 an official said [the national broadcaster RTB had signed up to supply language data for AI models](<https://lefaso.net/?id_article=150234&page=impression>). In May 2026 the KIX-SEEDS project at Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo said it had [designed a machine-learning module that predicts school dropout](<https://seeds.bf/post_detail/4d824034-e6d1-4182-acf5-b8088d4a5a42>), used in two pilot communes. In July 2026 TIC Magazine BF reported that CITADEL had [built a French-Mooré voice and text translator and a corpus of 83,000 French-Mooré sentence pairs](<https://ticmagazine.bf/intelligence-artificielle-burkina-faso-2026/>). In April 2026 Digital Magazine Burkina reported that [its French-Mooré speech translation system, built on a 150-hour audio corpus with a 4.24% error rate, was already operational](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/04/06/intelligence-artificielle-le-centre-dexcellence-interdisciplinaire-en-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpement-citadel-au-cours-du-loreslm-2026-lia-burkinabe-parle-desorma/>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The minister said in 2025 that [the PACDIGITAL project funds AI centres of excellence](<https://an.bf/127>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. [L'Essentiel.bf is free to the public](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149991>), but no user count is given.
