# Kenya

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** In October 2026 [Teknobyte's Kenya Languages Lexicon held over 133,000 Kenyan English words](<https://capitalfm.africa/kenyas-language-data-holds-promise-for-ai-powered-services/>) for speech and translation tools. In September 2026 the Maseno Centre for Applied AI [prepared to collect Dholuo speech data](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7404/>). Kenya is one of five governments that [launched ATLAS Umoja AI with the GSMA](<https://iafrica.com/gsma-and-five-african-governments-launch-atlas-umoja-ai-to-scale-nigerias-language-model-template-across-the-continent/>) in July 2026, to build models for African languages. In November 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary [discussed a proposed US$180 million Chinese grant for an AI cloud data project at Eldoret](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/kenya-seeks-chinese-support-for-digital-economy-and-ai-development/>). In August 2026 the ICT Principal Secretary said [Oracle would establish its AI cloud platform in Kenya](<https://gaa.go.ke/ministry-urges-public-sector-adopt-ai-cloud-computing>).

**AI computing.** In November 2025 Atlancis Technologies said [it had deployed an NVIDIA GPU AI factory at iXAfrica's NBOX1 data centre](<https://atlancis.com/atlancis-technologies-ai-infra/>). In March 2026 [Servernah Cloud launched there with GPU capacity for AI](<https://atlancis.com/servernah-cloud-launch/>), which iXAfrica's chief executive called live for the market. In October 2026 Vodacom's technology chief said [Kenya will be the second node for its planned GPU service](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-10-18-vodacoms-technology-chief-outlines-regional-data-centre-strategy/>). That month the PPP Directorate [signed a funding agreement to prepare an expansion of the Konza National Data Centre](<https://peopledaily.digital/business/konza-data-centre-set-for-expansion-through-public-private-partnership>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named or counted users. [Servernah Cloud is offered commercially](<https://atlancis.com/servernah-cloud-launch/>), and no customer of it is named.
