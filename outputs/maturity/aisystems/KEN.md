# Kenya

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Maseno Centre for Applied AI, with the ACTS-AI4D team, did a pre-visit to Suba ahead of the main exercise collecting Dholuo speech data with the SmartVoice application to improve Dholuo representation in AI](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7404/>). [The GSMA and the governments of Benin, Kenya, Namibia, Nigeria and Togo launched ATLAS Umoja AI to build AI models for African languages, with private partners Awarri, Zindi, Pawa AI and Mozisha](<https://iafrica.com/gsma-and-five-african-governments-launch-atlas-umoja-ai-to-scale-nigerias-language-model-template-across-the-continent/>).

**AI computing.** [Atlancis Technologies said in November 2025 it had delivered and deployed an NVIDIA GPU AI factory at iXAfrica's NBOX1 data centre](<https://atlancis.com/atlancis-technologies-ai-infra/>), and [in March 2026 Servernah Cloud was launched there with GPU capacity for AI inference and model deployment, which iXAfrica's chief executive called live for the market](<https://atlancis.com/servernah-cloud-launch/>). [Vodacom's group technology chief told Business Day in October 2026 that Kenya will be the second node where Vodacom plans to build GPUs as a service](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-10-18-vodacoms-technology-chief-outlines-regional-data-centre-strategy/>).

**Shared with users.** No source held since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named or counted users. [Servernah Cloud is offered commercially](<https://atlancis.com/servernah-cloud-launch/>), and no customer of it is named.
