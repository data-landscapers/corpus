# DR Congo

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In April 2025 the Ministry of National Education and New Citizenship said it developed Snote Manager, grading software that uses AI](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/news/artificial-intelligence-at-the-heart-of-state-exam-correction>). [The software is in use at the ministry's grading centre](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/news/artificial-intelligence-at-the-heart-of-state-exam-correction>). [On 6 August 2026 the Ministry of Digital Economy, with UNDP support, launched a project to collect Lingala language data](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-la-campagne-de-vulgarisation-du-code-du-numerique-lancee-a-kinshasa/>). [The data are to build the databases needed for AI that understands national Congolese languages](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-la-campagne-de-vulgarisation-du-code-du-numerique-lancee-a-kinshasa/>). [On 27 June 2026 Université Nouveaux Horizons inaugurated the Lofoyi supercomputer in Lubumbashi](<https://www.unhorizons.org/inauguration-lofoyi-premier-supercalculateur-academique-rdc/>). [The university describes it as optimised for scientific computing, AI and big data](<https://www.unhorizons.org/inauguration-lofoyi-premier-supercalculateur-academique-rdc/>).

**AI computing.** [In June 2026 Université Nouveaux Horizons said Lofoyi is built from several servers linked by 100 Gbps InfiniBand, with 150 TFLOPS of computing power](<https://www.unhorizons.org/inauguration-lofoyi-premier-supercalculateur-academique-rdc/>). No source found since October 2023 names GPUs or other AI accelerators in it or elsewhere in the country. [In September 2025 Arab Founders reported that Kinshasa-based startup Yamify is building a GPU-powered AI cloud platform](<https://arabfounders.net/en/dr-congo-based-yamify-raises-100k-ai-cloud/>). [It is hosted in African data centres and was in private beta](<https://arabfounders.net/en/dr-congo-based-yamify-raises-100k-ai-cloud/>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 Yamify's AI cloud had a waitlist of over 1,500](<https://arabfounders.net/en/dr-congo-based-yamify-raises-100k-ai-cloud/>).
