# Uganda

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Ugandan developer Ernest Mwebaze built the Sunflower language model, supporting 31 Ugandan indigenous languages on Alibaba's Qwen 3, and that it provides Ugandan farmers with weather information and crop guidance in local languages](<https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026080708231865765>). [Mbarara University of Science and Technology leads the image-analysis and machine-learning work of the Wellcome-funded openScopes AIDDiA programme, which is building data pipelines and machine-learning tools for microscopy images](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/r101m-uct-project-brings-ai-microscopy-to-africa/LPp6V7rBYJb7DKQz>). [Makerere University launched its AI Health Lab in March 2025 for AI research and development, including locally made AI-powered health platforms, per CIPIT](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). [In September 2026 Sunbird AI released Sunflower v2, five open models with their datasets, covering speech recognition for 51 African languages](<https://sunbird.ai/sunbird-ai-launches-sunflower-v2-expanding-open-african-language-ai-from-31-ugandan-languages-to-67-across-the-continent/>).
