[Makerere University launched its AI Health Lab in March 2025 for AI research and development, including locally made AI-powered health platforms](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>).
