# Senegal

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [APS reports that Senegalese startup Andakia presented Allo Goor Gi, an AI platform answering pupils' questions by phone call in Wolof and French without internet, built with the Ministry of National Education and hosted on servers in Senegal, according to its designer](<https://aps.sn/allo-goor-gi-une-ia-avec-des-contenues-pedagogiques-certifies/>). [RTS says works began on 5 October 2026 on a locally run AI application for court archives, with an NVIDIA DGX Spark delivered, funded with UNDP and Korean support](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>).

**AI computing.** No source held since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/allo-goor-gi-une-ia-avec-des-contenues-pedagogiques-certifies/>).

**Shared with users.** No source held since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/allo-goor-gi-une-ia-avec-des-contenues-pedagogiques-certifies/>).
