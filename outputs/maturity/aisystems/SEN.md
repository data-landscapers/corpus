# Senegal

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In July 2026 the Senegalese startup Andakia presented Allo Goor Gi, which answers pupils' questions by phone in Wolof and French without internet](<https://aps.sn/allo-goor-gi-une-ia-avec-des-contenues-pedagogiques-certifies/>). It was built with the Ministry of National Education and runs on servers in Senegal, its designer says. [On 16 October 2026 AI Hub Senegal and the French embassy presented the Kiriku Voice results](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/kiriku-voice-challenge-des-modeles-dia-developpes-pour-les-langues-nationales/>). They comprise 210 hours of annotated Wolof, Pulaar and Serer speech and two models. [Works began on 5 October 2026 on a locally run AI application for court archives](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>), with an NVIDIA DGX Spark delivered. In August 2026 Senegal told the ITU that a laboratory at Cheikh Anta Diop University is [developing SENEGAL-AI](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-senegal-se-dote-dun-hub-pour-transformer-les-projets-d-ia-en-applications-concretes>). It is a language model built on the Taouey supercomputer.

**AI computing.** [The state bought the Taouey supercomputer from Atos and put it into service at Diamniadio in March 2024](<https://aps.sn/le-supercalculateur-taouey-un-outil-au-service-de-la-recherche-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>). APS describes it as increasingly needed for training AI models. [In April 2026 CINERI's director general said Taouey runs part-time](<https://letechobservateur.sn/pr-ousmane-thiare-directeur-general-de-la-cineri-le-niveau-dutilisation-est-encore-moyen-a-cause-des-dysfonctionnements-que-nous-avons-de-temps-en-temps/>). Its second cell, which holds the GPUs, is often idled by power instability.

**Shared with users.** [In May 2025 CINERI said Taouey had had more than a hundred users, public and private, including scientists from the sub-region](<https://aps.sn/le-supercalculateur-taouey-un-outil-au-service-de-la-recherche-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [In 2026 it said use has been free and open to all, naming UCAD's faculty of medicine among its users](<https://letechobservateur.sn/pr-ousmane-thiare-directeur-general-de-la-cineri-le-niveau-dutilisation-est-encore-moyen-a-cause-des-dysfonctionnements-que-nous-avons-de-temps-en-temps/>). [The Kiriku Voice data and models are published openly](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/kiriku-voice-challenge-des-modeles-dia-developpes-pour-les-langues-nationales/>).
