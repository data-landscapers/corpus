# Senegal

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [APS reports that Senegalese startup Andakia presented Allo Goor Gi, an AI platform answering pupils' questions by phone call in Wolof and French without internet, built with the Ministry of National Education and hosted on servers in Senegal, according to its designer](<https://aps.sn/allo-goor-gi-une-ia-avec-des-contenues-pedagogiques-certifies/>). [AI Hub Senegal and the French embassy presented on 16 October 2026 the results of the Kiriku Voice challenge: 210 hours of annotated Wolof, Pulaar and Serer speech and two models, M-Kiriku ASR and M-Kiriku TTS](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/kiriku-voice-challenge-des-modeles-dia-developpes-pour-les-langues-nationales/>). [RTS says works began on 5 October 2026 on a locally run AI application for court archives, with an NVIDIA DGX Spark delivered, funded with UNDP and Korean support](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>).

**AI computing.** [The state's Taouey supercomputer, bought from Atos, was put into service at Diamniadio in March 2024, with demand rising for training AI models](<https://aps.sn/le-supercalculateur-taouey-un-outil-au-service-de-la-recherche-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [In 2026 CINERI's director general said it runs part-time: its second cell, which holds the GPUs, is often idled by power instability](<https://letechobservateur.sn/pr-ousmane-thiare-directeur-general-de-la-cineri-le-niveau-dutilisation-est-encore-moyen-a-cause-des-dysfonctionnements-que-nous-avons-de-temps-en-temps/>).

**Shared with users.** [In May 2025 CINERI said Taouey had had more than a hundred users, public and private, including scientists from the sub-region](<https://aps.sn/le-supercalculateur-taouey-un-outil-au-service-de-la-recherche-et-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [In 2026 it said use has been free and open to all, naming UCAD's faculty of medicine among its users](<https://letechobservateur.sn/pr-ousmane-thiare-directeur-general-de-la-cineri-le-niveau-dutilisation-est-encore-moyen-a-cause-des-dysfonctionnements-que-nous-avons-de-temps-en-temps/>). [The Kiriku Voice data and models are published openly](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/kiriku-voice-challenge-des-modeles-dia-developpes-pour-les-langues-nationales/>).

***Also on file***. [local AI application for judicial archives](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>).
