## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In August 2026 Mauritius Telecom's CEO said the operator is investing in local data centres equipped with GPU infrastructure](<https://sundaytimesmauritius.com/interview-veemal-gungadin-ceo-of-mauritius-telecom-our-core-objective-through-our-partnership-with-aws-is-to-create-high-skilled-jobs-for-young-professionals/>). [They are to host sovereign AI applications locally](<https://sundaytimesmauritius.com/interview-veemal-gungadin-ceo-of-mauritius-telecom-our-core-objective-through-our-partnership-with-aws-is-to-create-high-skilled-jobs-for-young-professionals/>). [He said Kevin AI is the first model specifically designed to speak and understand Mauritian Creole](<https://sundaytimesmauritius.com/interview-veemal-gungadin-ceo-of-mauritius-telecom-our-core-objective-through-our-partnership-with-aws-is-to-create-high-skilled-jobs-for-young-professionals/>). [Partner startups already use it for automated bookings and for translating Creole interviews](<https://sundaytimesmauritius.com/interview-veemal-gungadin-ceo-of-mauritius-telecom-our-core-objective-through-our-partnership-with-aws-is-to-create-high-skilled-jobs-for-young-professionals/>). [In July 2026 Mauritius Telecom said NVIDIA B300 GPUs for its Mauritius data centre are due by the end of the year](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>). [Its sovereign MyTGPT, a local enterprise-grade equivalent of ChatGPT, is in use and integrates with Microsoft 365, Gmail and Shopify](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>).
