# South Africa

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in South Africa under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [The University of Cape Town released MzansiText and MzansiLM in May 2026](<https://www.news.uct.ac.za/article/-2026-05-04-uct-researchers-develop-ai-model-for-11-south-african-languages>). MzansiText is a dataset of the 11 official written languages, and MzansiLM is a language model trained on it. In October 2026 researchers including the University of Pretoria's Data Science for Social Impact group [released AfriScience-MT, a science translation corpus](<https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.757.pdf>). It covers Northern Sotho and isiZulu among six languages. [The science minister said in October 2025 that Lelapa AI's InkubaLM came from private or donor funding](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32962/>). The minister said no government-backed model is being built.

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in South Africa](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [The national CHPC offers an HPC and GPU cluster with 30 Nvidia V100 nodes](<https://www.chpc.ac.za/nicis-service-catalogue/>). On 23 November 2026 Business Day reported that [Africa Data Centres' CEO said the 3-megawatt facility, with just over 2,000 Nvidia GPUs, had been at full capacity since 1 November](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-11-23-strive-masiyiwas-ai-gamble-pays-off-as-cassava-factory-hits-full-capacity/>).

**Shared with users.** [The CHPC's 2024 service catalogue offers its HPC and GPU cluster free to academic and public-interest users for renewable six-month periods](<https://www.chpc.ac.za/nicis-service-catalogue/>). It says the cluster has had over 2,000 users. In March 2026 [Zindi's CEO said Cassava AI Factory compute is helping the Zindi developer community build AI solutions](<https://www.cassavatechnologies.com/cassava-scales-african-ai-infrastructure-with-nvidia-powered-ai-factories-to-accelerate-sovereign-data-capabilities/>). The CSIR's NICIS centre manager said it lets the CSIR extend partnership to South African research communities.
