# South Africa

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in South Africa under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [The University of Cape Town released MzansiText, a dataset of the 11 official written languages, and MzansiLM, a language model trained on it, in May 2026](<https://www.news.uct.ac.za/article/-2026-05-04-uct-researchers-develop-ai-model-for-11-south-african-languages>). In October 2026 researchers including the University of Pretoria's Data Science for Social Impact group [released AfriScience-MT, a science translation corpus covering Northern Sotho and isiZulu among six languages](<https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.757.pdf>). [The science minister said in October 2025 that Lelapa AI's InkubaLM came from private or donor funding and no government-backed model is being built](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32962/>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in South Africa](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Vodacom's group technology chief said in October 2026 that South Africa will be the last of its three large markets to get Vodacom GPUs, starting small and mainly for internal use](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-10-18-vodacoms-technology-chief-outlines-regional-data-centre-strategy/>). [The national CHPC offers an HPC and GPU cluster with 30 Nvidia V100 nodes](<https://www.chpc.ac.za/nicis-service-catalogue/>), and [HPE says Mzansi Mind runs on Nvidia Blackwell GPU servers in Johannesburg](<https://www.hpe.com/us/en/newsroom/press-release/2026/10/mzansi-mind-advances-south-africas-sovereign-ai-ambitions-with-hpe-infrastructure.html>).

**Shared with users.** [The CHPC's 2024 service catalogue offers its HPC and GPU cluster free to academic and public-interest users for renewable six-month periods, and says it has had over 2,000 users](<https://www.chpc.ac.za/nicis-service-catalogue/>).
