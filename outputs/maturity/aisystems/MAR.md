# Morocco

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** In September 2026 the digital ministry and Mistral AI [released in open source a classifier for Arabic dialects and a Darija speech model](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7504/morocco-mistral-ai-release-first-open-source-ai-tools-under-ai-made-in-morocco-r>). The ministry says it has [set up a joint laboratory with Mistral AI to build models together](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/un-open-source-week-2026-madame-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-intervient-lors-de-la-s%C3%A9ance-inaugurale-consacr%C3%A9e-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99open-source>). In June 2026 the minister [presented projects for models in Arabic, Darija and Amazigh](<https://laquotidienne.ma/article/economie/maroc-un-open-source-week-2026-souverainete-numerique-intelligence-artificielle>). LesEco says the [Sovereign Meeting Assistant, built in Morocco, is hosted on the sovereign marketplace](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>). In 2025 AtlasIA [published Atlaset, a Darija corpus, with the AlAtlas language model](<https://www.atlasia.ma/blog/al-atlas-pretraining>). An [AI factory at Nouaceur is planned at 16 MW in its first phase](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/data-centers-avec-quelle-electricite>).

**AI computing.** LesEco says the [Sovereign Meeting Assistant was trained on Moroccan GPUs](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>).

**Shared with users.** AtlasIA put [its Darija dataset on Hugging Face, with demos and code](<https://www.atlasia.ma/blog/al-atlas-pretraining>).
