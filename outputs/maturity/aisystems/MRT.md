# Mauritania

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [MWiML published an open CC BY 4.0 repository of NLP datasets for Pulaar, Soninke and Hassaniya Arabic (e.g. 991 Pulaar-French pairs, a 708-entry Soninke-French dictionary, 100 Hassaniya stories), with the Wolof folder still empty](<https://github.com/mwimlmauritania-byte/Open-Data-Mauritania>). [Sahara Media reports the Mauritanian digital transformation ministry and the World Bank agreed to mobilise additional funds for data infrastructure and data sharing to widen AI use and develop intelligent systems under WARDIP](<https://saharamedias.net/257839/>).
