# Mauritania

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In May 2026 MWiML published an open CC BY 4.0 repository of NLP datasets for Pulaar, Soninke and Hassaniya Arabic](<https://github.com/mwimlmauritania-byte/Open-Data-Mauritania>). [Among them are 991 Pulaar-French pairs, a 708-entry Soninke-French dictionary and 100 Hassaniya stories; the Wolof folder is still empty](<https://github.com/mwimlmauritania-byte/Open-Data-Mauritania>). [In June 2026 the digital transformation ministry and the World Bank agreed to mobilise more funds for data infrastructure and data sharing](<https://saharamedias.net/257839/>). [The aim is to widen AI use and develop intelligent systems under WARDIP](<https://saharamedias.net/257839/>). [In September 2026 researchers presented Enemray, a Hassaniya language model trained on a new Mauritanian corpus](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14829>).
