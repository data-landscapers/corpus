# Zambia

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Zambia under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [INFRATEL Corporation Limited advertised on the Zambia Public Procurement Authority e-GP platform an open national tender (INFRATEL-CAPEX-2026-029/35) for the supply, installation and commissioning of 150TB storage engines and an AI/ML GPU-accelerated server platform, with bids due 28 July 2026](<https://eprocure.zppa.org.zm/epps/cft/prepareViewCfTWS.do?resourceId=26844139>). [Larry RX AI, a drug interaction and compliance tool developed in Zambia, is part of a June 2025 pilot at 20 health posts](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-launches-nationwide-pilot-project-to-test-remote-health-diagnostics-ai-based-pharmacological-analysis-tools/>). [UNDP says Zambia is in the first cohort of its timbuktoo EDGE network of sovereign AI compute, with its node hosted at the University of Zambia AI UniPod](<https://www.undp.org/zambia/news/lusakas-water-about-get-smarter>). [In September 2024 the government said Google will set up an AI centre of excellence at ZAMREN in the University of Zambia](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/google-to-establish-ai-center-of-excellence-at-university-of-zambia/>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Zambia](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
