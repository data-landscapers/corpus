# Guinea

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In April 2025 Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said the science and innovation policy provides for AI laboratories and a sovereign data centre](<https://lelynx.net/2025/05/cite-des-sciences-de-guinee-une-conference-autour-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [They sit in the Cite des sciences et de l'innovation, with data processed by the 'projet de supercalculateur'](<https://lelynx.net/2025/05/cite-des-sciences-de-guinee-une-conference-autour-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [Nma Xui, a Pular and Soso conversation and translation prototype, was at an early stage in March 2024](<https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-les-langues-locales-se-mettent-a-lia_KM4QTYOVL5FMLFD4L4KR4EWNEE/>). [A five-person Guinean team built it](<https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-les-langues-locales-se-mettent-a-lia_KM4QTYOVL5FMLFD4L4KR4EWNEE/>). [In September 2026 the Guinean startup 224 N'Lambé said it offers translation APIs between French and Pular, Malinké and Soussou](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/ahmed-conscient-bald%C3%A9-655b85177_224-nlamb%C3%A9-api-linfrastructure-linguistique-activity-7503887516295430145-kypq>). [They serve developers and AI uses](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/ahmed-conscient-bald%C3%A9-655b85177_224-nlamb%C3%A9-api-linfrastructure-linguistique-activity-7503887516295430145-kypq>). [In November 2025 the higher education ministry signed a memorandum on sovereign green AI with a GenerIA-led consortium](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/afria-formation-recherche_communiqu%C3%A9-de-presse-conakry-14-novembre-activity-7395789206121467904-MlUx>). [Feasibility studies and pilots are to follow](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/afria-formation-recherche_communiqu%C3%A9-de-presse-conakry-14-novembre-activity-7395789206121467904-MlUx>).
