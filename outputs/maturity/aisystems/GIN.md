# Guinea

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said the science and innovation policy provides, through the Cite des sciences et de l'innovation, for AI laboratories and a sovereign data centre, whose data would be processed by the 'projet de supercalculateur'](<https://lelynx.net/2025/05/cite-des-sciences-de-guinee-une-conference-autour-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [Le360 reports Nma Xui, a Pular and Soso conversation and translation prototype built by a five-person Guinean team including an AI expert, is at an early stage](<https://afrique.le360.ma/societe/guinee-les-langues-locales-se-mettent-a-lia_KM4QTYOVL5FMLFD4L4KR4EWNEE/>). In September 2026 the Guinean startup 224 N'Lambé said it [offers translation APIs between French and Pular, Malinké and Soussou for developers and AI uses](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/ahmed-conscient-bald%C3%A9-655b85177_224-nlamb%C3%A9-api-linfrastructure-linguistique-activity-7503887516295430145-kypq>). [In November 2025 the higher education ministry signed a memorandum on sovereign green AI with a consortium led by GenerIA](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/afria-formation-recherche_communiqu%C3%A9-de-presse-conakry-14-novembre-activity-7395789206121467904-MlUx>), with feasibility studies and pilots to follow.

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lelynx.net/2025/05/cite-des-sciences-de-guinee-une-conference-autour-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named or counted users.
