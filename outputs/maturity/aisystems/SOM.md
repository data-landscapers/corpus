# Somalia

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Goobo Labs of Mogadishu released whisper-som-small, a Somali speech-recognition checkpoint fine-tuned on 320 hours of Somali audio from its SomNLP-STT-Corpus, reporting 11.4% word error rate on its own radio test set](<https://www.goobolabs.so/blog/whisper-som-small>). [The SIMAD AI Institute's Natural Language Processing for Somalia project is in phased development, including dataset preparation and prototype model development for Somali text and speech (SIMAD AI Institute)](<https://ai.simad.edu.so/building-the-foundations-of-somali-language-technologies/>). [In March 2025 Sykli Intelligence presented Himilo 7B, a Somali language model still under evaluation](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/adanmoh_aiforall-himilo7b-activity-7305950369514250241-zoUR>).
