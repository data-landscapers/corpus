# Burundi

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Burundian company Hyphen Tech developed the AgriHyphen AI app, a machine-learning tool that recognises 37 diseases across seven crops and is in use by farmers, according to Global South World](<https://www.globalsouthworld.com/article/ai-app-helps-burundian-farmers-detect-crop-diseases-early-video>). [The AI4Mpox Burundi team developed a machine-learning-ready epidemiological dataset and is piloting an AI early-warning dashboard](<https://www.ai4d.ai/fr/innovations/ai-powered-surveillance-and-response-network>). [In September 2026 the Ijwi ry'Ikirundi AI project published a free Kirundi dataset of 1,813 sentences, drawn from a private set of 4,809, and named its own Kirundi translation model](<https://github.com/Ijwi-ry-Ikirundi-AI/Kirundi_Dataset>).
