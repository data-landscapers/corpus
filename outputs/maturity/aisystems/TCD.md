**Model, dataset or computing.** [Arrete 095/2026, signed on 3 August 2026 by the higher education minister, creates the LIDIA AI research lab inside the CNRD](<https://atpe.td/intelligence-artificielle-le-tchad-cree-un-laboratoire-interdisciplinaire-de-recherche-au-cnrd/>).
