# Zimbabwe

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Econet Ai, a subsidiary of Econet Wireless Zimbabwe, runs the Shona-language chatbot Yamurai, which the company says already handles more than 70% of customer enquiries](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>). [Econet Ai is building a Ndebele-speaking chatbot trained on thousands of hours of recorded Ndebele speech, to launch in phases from September 2026](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>). [Techzim reports that Econet AI, launched in April 2026, is working on local language AI, including making its Yamurai assistant sound more Zimbabwean](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/>).
