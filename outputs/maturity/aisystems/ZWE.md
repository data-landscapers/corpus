# Zimbabwe

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Econet Ai, a subsidiary of Econet Wireless Zimbabwe, runs the Shona-language chatbot Yamurai, which the company says already handles more than 70% of customer enquiries](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>). [Econet Ai is building a Ndebele-speaking chatbot trained on thousands of hours of recorded Ndebele speech, to launch in phases from September 2026](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>). [Techzim reports that Econet AI, launched in April 2026, is working on local language AI, including making its Yamurai assistant sound more Zimbabwean](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/>). [In October 2026 the ICT minister said a Penguin AI project to create local datasets for AI would launch before December](<https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/>).

**AI computing.** [In March 2026 the government opened a US$350,000 National Information Access Centre at Zimbabwe Open University's Hatcliffe campus, equipped with an Nvidia GPU AI server for AI training and analytics](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>).
