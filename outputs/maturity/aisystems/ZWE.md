# Zimbabwe

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** Econet Ai, part of Econet Wireless Zimbabwe, [runs the Shona-language chatbot Yamurai](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>), which the company says handles more than 70% of customer enquiries. It is [building a Ndebele-speaking chatbot](<https://cite.org.zw/econet-ai-nears-completion-of-ndebele-speaking-chatbot/>), to launch in phases from September 2026. On 28 October 2026 the ICT minister said [Pangolin AI, a national platform of local computing and data, would be fully working by early 2027](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7993/zimbabwe-to-fully-operationalise-pangolin-ai-platform-by-q1-2027>). In October she also said a [project to create local datasets for AI would launch before December](<https://www.zimcitizennews.com/zimbabwe-approves-national-ai-charter-to-combat-surging-cyber-fraud/>).

**AI computing.** In March 2026 the government [opened a US$350,000 National Information Access Centre at Zimbabwe Open University](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>). It has an [Nvidia GPU server for AI training and analytics](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>).

**Shared with users.** The minister says [Pangolin AI is meant for startups, developers and researchers](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7993/zimbabwe-to-fully-operationalise-pangolin-ai-platform-by-q1-2027>). The minister expects it to be fully working by early 2027.

***Also on file***. The [University of Zimbabwe has a high-performance computer](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/04/econet-ai-is-here-and-its-more-than-those-yamurai-ads-weve-been-seeing/>).
