# Ethiopia

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Ethiopia, with UNDP and Addis Ababa University, has established an AI University Innovation Pod equipped with laboratories, robotics facilities and high-performance computing systems, per Birr Metrics](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-signs-up-to-new-global-artificial-intelligence-alliance/>). [Addis Fortune reports the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute developed automatic court transcription software with the Federal Supreme Court, delivered for 15.8 million Br](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>). [Ethiopia's 2026/27 federal budget proclamation, per the Ministry of Finance workbook, appropriates ETB 162,080,000 from the Treasury to the Artificial Intelligence Institution for National Artificial Intelligence Datacenter Deployment and ETB 170,000,000 for a National Artificial Intelligence Research Laboratory](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c1/de/c1de6f52-b57e-4a94-91ff-cc22a2c751b8/2019_e_c_budget_year_goe_federal_budget_proclamation_part_two_updated.xlsx>). [In October 2026 a WHO delegation reviewed tools developed at the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute, including speech-to-text and translation applications, predictive disease models and AI diagnostic tools for skin diseases](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/who-and-ethiopian-artificial-intelligence-institute-explore-strategic-collaboration-advance-ai>). [In November 2025 the Prime Minister said the AI Institute had released language tools for Amharic, Afan Oromo, Somali, Tigrinya and Afar](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7724441>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-signs-up-to-new-global-artificial-intelligence-alliance/>).
