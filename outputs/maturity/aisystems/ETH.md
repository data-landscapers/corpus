# Ethiopia

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute, UNDP and Addis Ababa University opened the AI UniPod on 16 February 2026](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9899270>). It has laboratories, robotics facilities and [high-performance computing systems](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-signs-up-to-new-global-artificial-intelligence-alliance/>). The institute [built court transcription software with the Federal Supreme Court](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>), delivered in 2025 for 15.8 million birr. In November 2025 the Prime Minister said it had [released language tools for Amharic, Afan Oromo, Somali, Tigrinya and Afar](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7724441>). In October 2026 a WHO delegation [reviewed its speech-to-text, translation and disease-model tools](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/who-and-ethiopian-artificial-intelligence-institute-explore-strategic-collaboration-advance-ai>). The [2026/27 federal budget gives it ETB 162,080,000 for a national AI data centre](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/c1/de/c1de6f52-b57e-4a94-91ff-cc22a2c751b8/2019_e_c_budget_year_goe_federal_budget_proclamation_part_two_updated.xlsx>) and ETB 170,000,000 for a research laboratory.

**AI computing.** UNDP's EDGE initiative [provides high-performance GPUs at the AI UniPod](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9899270>), a UNDP regional director said on 9 November 2026. Innovators use them to build AI products.

**Shared with users.** On 9 November 2026 [51 AI startup founders from 12 African countries began a 10-day residency at the AI UniPod](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9899270>). It offers training, mentorship and product development. Japan's ambassador put Japanese support for the UniPod at about US$1 million a year.
