## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Namibian reports that the National Artificial Intelligence Institute, launched in Windhoek on 23 September 2026 under NCRST, comprises a software development centre, datasets centre, AI innovation centre and R&D centre, and will develop and manage Namibian datasets](<https://www.namibian.com.na/national-ai-institute-launched/>). [New Era reports NCRST launched the National AI Institute in Windhoek, with research, software, datasets and AI innovation centres, and that it is working towards developing high-quality Namibian datasets](<https://neweralive.na/ncrst-opens-national-ai-institute/>). [The INCEIT page says C-DAC will set up a 5-node PARAM cluster (17.7 TF, including one NVIDIA Tesla V100 GPU node) to be deployed at NUST, Windhoek, Namibia](<https://inceit.nust.na/param-hpc>).
