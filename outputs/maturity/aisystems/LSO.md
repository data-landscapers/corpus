# Lesotho

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Government of Lesotho launched the Kobong hydropower and AI data-centre project on 2026-07-31](<https://www.gov.ls/energy/record-breaking-m100-billion-investment-secured-for-lesothos-hydropower-and-artificial-intelligence-data-centre/>). US company Convalt backs it with about US$6.2 billion in capital investment. [The National University of Lesotho is compiling a Sesotho word bank to train AI systems](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/sesotho-word-bank-being-compiled-to-train-artificial-intelligence-systems-in-lesotho/>). It held a follow-up meeting on this with UNDP and the Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation. [Authors at the National University of Lesotho and Botho University develop an AI diagnostic model for TB, HIV/AIDS and non-communicable diseases specific to Lesotho](<https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/buirc-25/126018805>). Their conference paper reports preliminary findings. [SeSoDa, a dataset of 1,966 Lesotho Sesotho-English prompt-completion pairs for fine-tuning language models, was published open source in 2025 with a baseline model](<https://doi.org/10.55492/v6i02.6742>). [Lesotho has begun the first phase of a national computing plan for 2026 to 2029](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says the country has a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators in service. [In October 2026 the ICT minister said the government was upgrading high-performance computing at the Mohale's Hoek Data Centre](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91936>). The minister said this was for AI development and research.

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named or counted users. [SeSoDa is published openly for researchers in Lesotho](<https://doi.org/10.55492/v6i02.6742>), and no user of it is named.
