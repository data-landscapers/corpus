# Lesotho

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Government of Lesotho launched on 2026-07-31 the Kobong hydropower and AI data-centre project, backed by US company Convalt with about US$6.2 billion in capital investment](<https://www.gov.ls/energy/record-breaking-m100-billion-investment-secured-for-lesothos-hydropower-and-artificial-intelligence-data-centre/>). [The National University of Lesotho is compiling a Sesotho word bank to train AI systems, in a follow-up meeting with UNDP and the Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/sesotho-word-bank-being-compiled-to-train-artificial-intelligence-systems-in-lesotho/>). [A conference paper by authors at the National University of Lesotho and Botho University develops an AI diagnostic model for TB, HIV/AIDS and non-communicable diseases specific to Lesotho, reporting preliminary findings](<https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/buirc-25/126018805>). [SeSoDa, a dataset of 1,966 Lesotho Sesotho-English prompt-completion pairs for fine-tuning language models, was published open source in 2025 with a baseline model](<https://doi.org/10.55492/v6i02.6742>). [Lesotho has begun the first phase of a national computing plan for 2026 to 2029](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>).

**AI computing.** No source held since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. [In October 2026 the ICT minister said the government was upgrading high-performance computing at the Mohale's Hoek Data Centre for AI development and research](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91936>).

**Shared with users.** No source held since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named or counted users. [SeSoDa is published openly for researchers in Lesotho](<https://doi.org/10.55492/v6i02.6742>), and no user of it is named.
