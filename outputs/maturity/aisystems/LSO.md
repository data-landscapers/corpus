# Lesotho

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Government of Lesotho launched on 2026-07-31 the Kobong hydropower and AI data-centre project, backed by US company Convalt with about US$6.2 billion in capital investment](<https://www.gov.ls/energy/record-breaking-m100-billion-investment-secured-for-lesothos-hydropower-and-artificial-intelligence-data-centre/>). [The National University of Lesotho is compiling a Sesotho word bank to train AI systems, in a follow-up meeting with UNDP and the Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/sesotho-word-bank-being-compiled-to-train-artificial-intelligence-systems-in-lesotho/>). [A conference paper by authors at the National University of Lesotho and Botho University develops an AI diagnostic model for TB, HIV/AIDS and non-communicable diseases specific to Lesotho, reporting preliminary findings](<https://www.atlantis-press.com/proceedings/buirc-25/126018805>).
