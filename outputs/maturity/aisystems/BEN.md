# Benin

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [GPT.BJ, a chatbot developed by the Benin Agency for Information Systems and Digital (ASIN), was launched in 2023](<https://council.science/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/AI-Paper-Case-Study-Benin_V1.pdf>). [It answers questions on the tax, digital, labour and penal codes](<https://council.science/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/AI-Paper-Case-Study-Benin_V1.pdf>). [In June 2026 it was described as a public-service chatbot trained on legal and administrative texts](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>). [In June 2025 IIDIA and ASIN launched a Regional Innovation and Digital Technology Lab in Cotonou](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/30/benin-launches-first-local-language-voice-ai-to-bridge-digital-divide>). [It is to develop the first voice-to-voice AI model in the Fon language, with a functional prototype due within 9 months](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/30/benin-launches-first-local-language-voice-ai-to-bridge-digital-divide>). [The model is trained on NVIDIA A100/H100 GPUs, with Gates Foundation support](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/30/benin-launches-first-local-language-voice-ai-to-bridge-digital-divide>).
