# Mozambique

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [ATDI, IP and Huawei Technologies Mozambique signed an MoU that provides for creating Portuguese-language AI models adapted to Mozambique, per ATDI's own note](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/atdi-e-huawei-firmam-parceria-estrategica-para-acelerar-a-inteligencia-artificial-em-mocambique/>). [On 22 October 2026 MAZA IA, an AI learning tool developed by MOZEDUCA with Vodacom Moçambique for students and teachers, was launched with the education minister, who urged national technology solutions suited to Mozambique's reality](<https://mznews.co.mz/governo-prepara-lei-para-regular-uso-da-inteligencia-artificial-na-educacao/>).

**AI computing.** No source held since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/atdi-e-huawei-firmam-parceria-estrategica-para-acelerar-a-inteligencia-artificial-em-mocambique/>).

**Shared with users.** No source held since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/atdi-e-huawei-firmam-parceria-estrategica-para-acelerar-a-inteligencia-artificial-em-mocambique/>).
