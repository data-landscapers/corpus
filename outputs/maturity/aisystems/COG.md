# Congo

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The Congo's AI strategy technical committee was launched on 6 May 2026 with UNDP technical and financial support](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/intelligence-artificielle-et-langues-nationales-le-congo-lance-les-travaux-de-sa-strategie>). Within its six-month mandate it is to produce a structured digital corpus for Lingala and Kituba. Les Depeches de Brazzaville reported that a budget of 150 to 200 million FCFA is sought. [Russia has committed to finance phases 3 and 4 of the African Center for Research in Artificial Intelligence (CARIA) in Congo](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/21/congo-and-russia-strengthen-digital-and-ai-cooperation-during-high-level-meeting/>). The Congolese minister says the money comes through a Sberbank debt-to-development conversion and has not yet been disbursed. In 2026 Congo Digital Services, working with UNDP and the digital ministry, published [an 884-pair Lingala image-text dataset for training a vision-language model](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/Congo-digital-service/dataset-qwen-vl-lingala-qlora-vf>). It also published [the weights of a Lingala-tuned Llama 3.1 8B model](<https://huggingface.co/Congo-digital-service/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-Lingala-QLoRA-merged-v2>). [RFI reported in November 2025 that the state was finishing a data centre in Brazzaville, largely financed by the African Development Bank](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20251119-le-congo-brazzaville-s-%C3%A9quipe-d-un-data-center-pour-r%C3%A9pondre-au-d%C3%A9fi-des-infrastructures-de-l-ia>). RFI said the centre could host computing to train AI models.

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest fact found: [the Brazzaville data centre being finished](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20251119-le-congo-brazzaville-s-%C3%A9quipe-d-un-data-center-pour-r%C3%A9pondre-au-d%C3%A9fi-des-infrastructures-de-l-ia>).

**Shared with users.** [The Lingala dataset is published under gated access](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/Congo-digital-service/dataset-qwen-vl-lingala-qlora-vf>); no user of it is named or counted.
