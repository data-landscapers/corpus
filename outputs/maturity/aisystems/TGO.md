# Togo

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute in Togo](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Togo First says Togo AI Lab is building models for several Togolese languages](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1009-20017-togo-ai-lab-seeks-technical-lead-for-public-sector-ai-projects>). [Togo is one of five governments that launched ATLAS Umoja AI with the GSMA in 2026](<https://iafrica.com/gsma-and-five-african-governments-launch-atlas-umoja-ai-to-scale-nigerias-language-model-template-across-the-continent/>). [In October 2026 the digital ministry launched Voix du Togo, collecting voice and text data in Ewe, Kabiye and Moba](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/1610-142485-le-togo-collecte-des-donnees-en-ewe-kabye-et-moba-pour-entrainer-ses-futurs-modeles-d-ia>). [In June 2026 a developer in Lomé published TogoLM, a corpus of 62,168 public documents and a model tuned on it](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7473279907129696256>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP Togo said in November 2026 that a GPU server it donated has been in service since July 2026](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>). [It sits at Lomé Data Centre and is run by Togo AI Lab](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>). [It handled about 50,200 requests between 26 August and 27 October 2026](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>).

**Shared with users.** [UNDP Togo names its users as the procurement control directorate, the security ministry and Togo AI Lab](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>). No source found since October 2023 says the server or a national model is open to outside researchers or startups.
