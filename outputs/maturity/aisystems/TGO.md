# Togo

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Togo under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Togo First says Togo AI Lab is developing models that understand and generate content in several Togolese languages, within a national local-languages project using LLMs, RAG and AI agents](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1009-20017-togo-ai-lab-seeks-technical-lead-for-public-sector-ai-projects>). [The GSMA and the governments of Benin, Kenya, Namibia, Nigeria and Togo launched ATLAS Umoja AI to build AI models for African languages, with private partners Awarri, Zindi, Pawa AI and Mozisha](<https://iafrica.com/gsma-and-five-african-governments-launch-atlas-umoja-ai-to-scale-nigerias-language-model-template-across-the-continent/>). [In October 2026 the digital ministry officially launched Voix du Togo, a three-year programme collecting voice and text data in Ewe, Kabiye and Moba to train AI models, aiming at 50 hours of validated audio and 6,000 translated sentence pairs a language](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/1610-142485-le-togo-collecte-des-donnees-en-ewe-kabye-et-moba-pour-entrainer-ses-futurs-modeles-d-ia>). [In June 2026 a developer in Lomé published TogoLM, a corpus of 62,168 Togolese public documents and a model tuned on it](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7473279907129696256>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Togo](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
