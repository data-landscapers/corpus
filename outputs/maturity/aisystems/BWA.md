# Botswana

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In February 2025 the Guardian Sun reported that two Batswana, Kitso Speaks and Glen Mokoka, built a Setswana AI](<https://guardiansun.co.bw/news/speaks-mokoka-the-brains-behind-setswana-artificial-intelligence/news>). [It understands and communicates in standard Setswana](<https://www.globalsouthworld.com/article/botswana-innovators-create-first-ai-that-speaks-setswana>). [They trained it on a dataset they collected from books, newspapers and recorded conversations](<https://guardiansun.co.bw/news/speaks-mokoka-the-brains-behind-setswana-artificial-intelligence/news>). [The work is self-funded, and mobile apps and dialects are still in development](<https://guardiansun.co.bw/news/speaks-mokoka-the-brains-behind-setswana-artificial-intelligence/news>). [In July 2025 Orion X, whose team first took root in Botswana, offered its Uhuru African language model through WhatsApp, SMS, USSD and a web app](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/lifestyle/how-uhuru-sets-to-resist-digital-imperialism>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [in March 2025 Tlou Energy said it signed a binding Heads of Agreement with Kala Data FZCO](<https://tlouenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Binding-HOA-with-Kala-Data-Final.pdf>). [The deal is to develop a containerised high-density computing facility for servers or GPUs at its Lesedi project](<https://tlouenergy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Binding-HOA-with-Kala-Data-Final.pdf>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 Uhuru had a free tier and a paid tier](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/lifestyle/how-uhuru-sets-to-resist-digital-imperialism>).
