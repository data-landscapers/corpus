# Djibouti

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [La Nation reports that HOOM, an AI-based education application conceived and developed by Djiboutians, is already used by more than 4,500 terminale pupils, about 57% of the year group, and includes the KékéBot teaching assistant](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-les-acteurs-dun-ecosysteme-en-construction/>). [Djibouti's labour minister met LinkWise on the planned AI compute infrastructure, projected at about 2,000 construction jobs and 500 to 700 skilled operating posts, still to be confirmed by studies (ADI)](<https://www.adi.dj/public/article/139002>). [Djibouti Ports Corridor Road developed CorridorVision AI, a road-defect detection model trained on road images its annotators labelled](<https://dpcr.dj/2026/01/15/corridorvision-ai-ai-powered-road-condition-monitoring-for-djiboutis-trade-corridors/>), [launched in 2024 as DPCR Vision AI, per the ports authority](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/djibouti-unveils-ai-tools-to-modernize-ports-and-logistics>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-les-acteurs-dun-ecosysteme-en-construction/>).

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-les-acteurs-dun-ecosysteme-en-construction/>).
