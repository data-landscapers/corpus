# Djibouti

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In September 2026 HOOM, an AI-based education application conceived and developed by Djiboutians, was used by more than 4,500 terminale pupils](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-les-acteurs-dun-ecosysteme-en-construction/>). [That is about 57% of the year group, and it includes the KékéBot teaching assistant](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-les-acteurs-dun-ecosysteme-en-construction/>). [In October 2026 Djibouti's labour minister met LinkWise on the planned AI compute infrastructure](<https://www.adi.dj/public/article/139002>). [It is projected at about 2,000 construction jobs and 500 to 700 skilled operating posts, still to be confirmed by studies](<https://www.adi.dj/public/article/139002>). [Djibouti Ports Corridor Road developed CorridorVision AI, a road-defect detection model trained on road images its annotators labelled](<https://dpcr.dj/2026/01/15/corridorvision-ai-ai-powered-road-condition-monitoring-for-djiboutis-trade-corridors/>). [It was launched in 2024 as DPCR Vision AI](<https://www.dawan.africa/news/djibouti-unveils-ai-tools-to-modernize-ports-and-logistics>).

**AI computing.** No source found since October 2023 says a GPU or AI-accelerator facility is in service in the country. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 LinkWise's AI compute infrastructure was still planned](<https://www.adi.dj/public/article/139002>).
