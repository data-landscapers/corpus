# Algeria

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** In September 2026 [a draft decree creating a national research centre for AI and data went forward for legal review](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=79285>). The same month [a committee set 17 infrastructure and 14 data operations for 2026](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=79285>) to localise AI applications. [The higher education ministry set up a commission on large language models](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-premiere-reunion-de-coordination-consacree-a-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelli/>). [The minister said a language model for university lecturers is being developed](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/إطلاق-المسار-التعليمي-الشخصي-للطالب-ب>) to set exercises and mark answers. [On 21 September 2026 the commission reported four large language models, two completed and two in development](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-troisieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-l/>). One is a tax-legislation chatbot prototype, [for which equipment was made available on 20 October 2026](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/10/m-kamel-baddari-preside-la-cinquieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-2/>), with delivery due in four months. [On 9 November 2026 the minister discussed an AI data centre at the national AI school with a Chinese delegation](<https://www.mesrs.dz/en/2026/11/minister-of-higher-education-and-scientific-research-discusses-with-chinese-delegation-the-project-to-establish-an-artificial-intelligence-data-centre-at-the-national-higher-school-of-artificial-intel/>) at Sidi Abdellah. [In October 2024 the National People's Assembly said it was working with CERIST on speech-to-text for Algerian dialects](<https://asgp.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wed-16-Oct-PM-Algerie-FR.pdf>). [In March 2025 work began in Oran on an AI computing centre](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/61579>), to have GPUs for researchers and startups.
