# Algeria

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [An updated draft decree creating a National Research Center for Artificial Intelligence and Data was approved for further legal procedures, and the Data and Infrastructure Committee set 17 infrastructure and 14 data operations for 2026 to localize AI applications (DZWatch)](<https://dzwatch.dz/?p=79285>). [The ministry's coordination meeting decided to create a national research centre for AI and data and a multisectoral Algerian system for AI tools, with a commission on large language models](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-premiere-reunion-de-coordination-consacree-a-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelli/>). [The higher education minister said a language model (LLM) for university lecturers, to generate exercises and assess student answers, is being developed](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/إطلاق-المسار-التعليمي-الشخصي-للطالب-ب>). [On 21 September 2026 the ministry's LLM commission reported four large language models, two completed and two in development, among them a tax-legislation chatbot prototype](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-troisieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-l/>); [on 20 October 2026 equipment was made available for the tax model, with delivery due in four months](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/10/m-kamel-baddari-preside-la-cinquieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-2/>). [The National People's Assembly said in October 2024 that it was working with CERIST on AI speech-to-text for Algerian dialects](<https://asgp.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wed-16-Oct-PM-Algerie-FR.pdf>). [In March 2025 the post and telecommunications minister laid the first stone in Oran of an AI high-performance computing centre, to have GPUs for researchers and startups](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/61579>).
