# Tanzania

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The e-Government Authority says Msaada AI, Muhtasari AI and Tafsiri AI were designed at its e-Government Research, Innovation and Development Centre, and that Msaada AI is in use by two institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>). [Muhimbili University researchers built a chest X-ray AI for TB in people with HIV, trained on about 3,000 patient images and reporting 92% accuracy, in trials ahead of TMDA approval in August 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/makala/afya/watafiti-waja-na-ai-kubaini-tb-kwa-wenye-vvu-5576206>). [Communications and ICT Minister Angellah Kairuki said on 18 July 2026 that Tanzania is building a National AI Data Centre, to hold the country's data onshore so Tanzanian and African researchers can train AI systems on it](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/tanzania-yaonesha-mafanikio-ya-utekelezaji-wa-akili-unde-ai-katika-mkutano-wa-kimataifa-wa-waico-2026>).

**AI computing.** [In April 2026 Ghent University installed a GPU workstation at Mzumbe University, as its Centre for AI Research and Innovation opened, and served language, image and speech models from it at a workshop](<https://airo.ugent.be/news/202604_workshop_at_mzumbe/>). [HostAfrica said in August 2026 it is rolling out Nvidia Blackwell GPUs in Tanzania](<https://hostingdiscussion.com/news/hostafrica-enters-tanzania-brings-africas-first-local-blackwell-gpus-with-it/>), and [the National AI Data Centre is being built](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/tanzania-yaonesha-mafanikio-ya-utekelezaji-wa-akili-unde-ai-katika-mkutano-wa-kimataifa-wa-waico-2026>).
