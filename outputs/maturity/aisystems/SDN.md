**Model, dataset or computing.** [Sudan-MM, a public multimodal dataset of 3,042 Sudanese Arabic images and videos with MSA and dialect captions, came from a 2025 shared task](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/IndabaXSudan/Sudan-MM>). [Five teams produced it, and it is released under Apache 2.0](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/IndabaXSudan/Sudan-MM>). [Researchers at Sudan University of Science and Technology document three Sudanese Arabic resources for language processing](<https://www.ijcstjournal.org/volume-14/issue-4/IJCST-V14I4P4.pdf>). [They are a 6,755,955-sentence social-media corpus, a 13,293-review labelled sentiment corpus and a 40,000-sentence hate-speech resource](<https://www.ijcstjournal.org/volume-14/issue-4/IJCST-V14I4P4.pdf>).
