# Sudan

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Sudan-MM, a public multimodal dataset of 3,042 Sudanese Arabic images and videos with MSA and dialect captions, came from a 2025 shared task](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/IndabaXSudan/Sudan-MM>). [Five teams produced it, and it is released under Apache 2.0](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/IndabaXSudan/Sudan-MM>). [Researchers at Sudan University of Science and Technology document three Sudanese Arabic resources for language processing](<https://www.ijcstjournal.org/volume-14/issue-4/IJCST-V14I4P4.pdf>). [They are a 6,755,955-sentence social-media corpus, a 13,293-review labelled sentiment corpus and a 40,000-sentence hate-speech resource](<https://www.ijcstjournal.org/volume-14/issue-4/IJCST-V14I4P4.pdf>). On 20 November 2026 Almandara News reported that the data and AI authority [was finishing a shared national AI lab, hosted inside Sudan and offline](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/11/20/%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85-100-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3-->). It is for about 100 concurrent users running mid-size models, and is expected to be fully operating within three months.
