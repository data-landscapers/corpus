# Cote d'Ivoire

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Under the PARAE e-government loan, the AfDB finances an artificial intelligence hub and will equip Cote d'Ivoire with AI digital infrastructure, including high-performance computing and GPUs, per AfDB deputy director for West Africa Joseph Ribero](<https://digitalmag.ci/services-publics-financements-bad-des-projets-numeriques-en-cote-divoire-appels-doffres-et-avis-a-manifestation-dinteret/>). [Minister Djibril Ouattara said on 18 May 2026 that Cote d'Ivoire has begun work on an Ivorian artificial intelligence](<https://www.lintelligentdabidjan.info/news/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-la-cote-divoire-prepare-son-cadre-sur-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [The national AI strategy unveiled on 13 March 2025 plans an AI laboratory in the Cite de l'innovation et de la culture, per the AfDB](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/press-releases/la-cote-divoire-fait-un-bond-en-avant-avec-de-nouvelles-initiatives-numeriques-pour-revolutionner-ladministration-publique-et-la-prestation-de-services-81910>). [FuturAfric's YIRI project ran a national campaign in 2025 to collect voice and image data in Ivorian languages, aiming at a first Ivorian language model](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-yiri-quand-le-numerique-parle-les-langues-locales/>), and [launched its platform in Abidjan in October 2026](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/126771-intelligence-artificielle-la-plateforme-yiri-entend-faciliter-l-acces-au-numerique-grace-aux-langues-locales>). [The national computing centre is to host the country's AI hub](<https://cncci.edu.ci/mercredi-30-juillet-2025-seance-de-travail-de-la-task-force-ia-au-centre-national-de-calcul-de-cote-divoire-cncci/>).
