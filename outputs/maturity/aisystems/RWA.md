## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Cenfri reports that NISR staff built a machine-learning model that predicts ISIC, ISCO and ISCED codes from Kinyarwanda, English or French descriptions, and that it is now used by analysts and monitors, with 96 percent of records auto-coded correctly in one test run](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Rwanda under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Tunga, a Kinyarwanda AI voice assistant drawing on an agricultural knowledge base developed by MINAGRI, RAB and partners, was being tested in September 2026](<https://visionmedia.rw/Rwanda-expands-use-of-AI-to-reach-millions-of-farmers-with-timely-advice.html>). [In October 2026 the national AI agency's AI Factory was set to build and test applications with private partners](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>).

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Rwanda](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
