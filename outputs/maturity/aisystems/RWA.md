## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In October 2026 NISR staff had built a machine-learning model that predicts ISIC, ISCO and ISCED codes from Kinyarwanda, English or French descriptions](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [Analysts and monitors now use it](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [In one test run 96 percent of records were auto-coded correctly](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Rwanda under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Tunga, a Kinyarwanda AI voice assistant drawing on an agricultural knowledge base developed by MINAGRI, RAB and partners, was being tested in September 2026](<https://visionmedia.rw/Rwanda-expands-use-of-AI-to-reach-millions-of-farmers-with-timely-advice.html>). [In October 2026 the national AI agency's AI Factory was set to build and test applications with private partners](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>).
