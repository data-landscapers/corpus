# Rwanda

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In October 2026 NISR staff had built a machine-learning model that predicts ISIC, ISCO and ISCED codes from Kinyarwanda, English or French descriptions](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [Analysts and monitors now use it](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [In one test run 96 percent of records were auto-coded correctly](<https://cenfri.org/articles/award-winner-ngirinshuti-on-an-ai-model-that-serves-rwandas-statistical-analysis/>). [UNDP says it deployed country-hosted AI compute capacity in Rwanda under its AI Infrastructure Activation programme](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>). [Tunga, a Kinyarwanda AI voice assistant drawing on an agricultural knowledge base developed by MINAGRI, RAB and partners, was being tested in September 2026](<https://visionmedia.rw/Rwanda-expands-use-of-AI-to-reach-millions-of-farmers-with-timely-advice.html>). [In October 2026 the national AI agency's AI Factory was set to build and test applications with private partners](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>). The agency's October 2026 plan says a [World Bank grant is to finance GPU compute equipment with co-location, hosting and maintenance](<https://raia.rw/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/RAIA-SEP-October-2026.pdf>), and consulting for AI-ready data assets and registries. On 18 August 2026 UNDP Rwanda [tendered a consultancy to prepare AI-ready Kinyarwanda text and speech datasets and a secure digital repository](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/311474>). In February 2026 Techinika reported that [Yali Labs had spent close to US$0.5 million training a Kinyarwanda large language model on external GPUs](<https://techinika.com/why-rwanda-ai-innovators-are-struggling-to-scale>), and had not yet got the accurate model it wanted.

**AI computing.** [UNDP's programme procured and deployed country-hosted GPU compute in Rwanda](<https://www.undp.org/africa/publications/local-ai-compute-infrastructure-africa-lessons-six-country-hosted-deployments-governance-and-effective-use>).
