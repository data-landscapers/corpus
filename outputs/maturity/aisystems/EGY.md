# Egypt

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The ministry's Applied Innovation Center built AI applications including the Karnak language model, Loghat and Bel Masry, the minister said](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [The ICT minister called Karnak the first national Arabic large language model](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5894042.aspx>). He said it tops Arabic models of its size on international evaluation platforms. [The ministry says Karnak was unveiled at the AI Everything MEA summit in Egypt](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/99046>). [In September 2026 Vodafone Business and Cassava Technologies agreed to build Egypt's first sovereign AI data centre](<https://www.maspero.eg/economy/2026/09/08/987328/%DA%A4%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%88%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%A7-%D8%AA%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1>). It is to offer Nvidia-based GPUs as a service, hosted in Egypt. [In November 2026 Vodacom's chief executive put its cost at about US$1 billion over the next couple of years](<https://techcentral.co.za/vodacom-ai-data-centre-hubs-south-africa-kenya-egypt/288232/>), built in modules with Elsewedy Electric, Vodafone Egypt owning about 30%. [In October 2025 the communications minister said the state is investing over US$3.3 billion over seven years in digital infrastructure for AI](<https://www.cairo24.com/2301925>).

**AI computing.** [In October 2026 Vodacom's technology chief said ground had been broken on Africa Data Centres Egypt](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-10-18-vodacoms-technology-chief-outlines-regional-data-centre-strategy/>), built with Elsewedy Electric and Cassava Technologies. It will run its own GPUs for internal and outside users. No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country.

**Shared with users.** No source found since October 2023 says national AI computing, models or datasets are open to named users. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>).
