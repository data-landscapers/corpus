# Egypt

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [The MCIT Applied Innovation Center built homegrown AI applications including the Karnak LLM, Loghat and Bel Masry, the minister said](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [Egypt's ICT minister said engineers and researchers at the ministry's Applied Innovation Center developed Karnak, the first national Arabic large language model, which according to international evaluation platforms tops Arabic models in the 30 and 40 billion parameter classes](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5894042.aspx>). [Vodafone Business and Cassava Technologies signed a partnership, attended by the communications minister, for Egypt's first sovereign AI data centre offering Nvidia-based GPU-as-a-Service hosted in Egypt](<https://www.maspero.eg/economy/2026/09/08/987328/%DA%A4%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%88%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%88%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%A7-%D8%AA%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A3%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1>). [MCIT says the AI Everything MEA summit in Egypt unveiled Egypt's Arabic LLM Karnak](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/99046>). [Communications minister Amr Talaat said in October 2025 that the state is investing over USD 3.3 billion over seven years in digital infrastructure for AI](<https://www.cairo24.com/2301925>).

**AI computing.** [Vodacom's group technology chief told Business Day in October 2026 that Vodafone Business has broken ground on Africa Data Centres Egypt with Elsewedy Electric and Cassava Technologies, a facility that will run its own GPUs for internal and outside users](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-10-18-vodacoms-technology-chief-outlines-regional-data-centre-strategy/>). No source found since October 2023 says a facility with GPUs or AI accelerators is in service in the country.
