# Eritrea

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [GeezLab's GLaRe programme lists open Tigrinya datasets](<https://research.geezlab.com/>). They include the TiNC24 named-entity corpus of more than 200,000 words and the TiPOSC24 part-of-speech corpus of more than 118,000 tokens. Students at Mai Nefhi College of Engineering led both. [The programme also lists Dahlak LMs, a family of language models trained on Eritrean languages, as in progress](<https://research.geezlab.com/>).

**AI computing.** No AI computing facility in the country is on record. The nearest facts found are [GeezLab's Dahlak LMs, a family of language models for Tigrinya, Tigre and related languages still in progress](<https://research.geezlab.com/>). [In 2021 six Eritrea Institute of Technology graduates built a Tigrigna speech-to-text prototype on almost 30 hours of new voice data](<https://shabait.com/2021/11/06/an-enormous-leap-towards-artificial-intelligence-tigrigna-speech-to-text/>).

**Shared with users.** [GeezLab says it publishes its datasets, models, code and tools openly, and lists TiNC24 and TiPOSC24 among them](<https://research.geezlab.com/>). No user of them is named or counted.
