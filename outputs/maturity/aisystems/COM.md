## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [Al-Watwan reports that Komori TTS, the first Shikomori text-to-speech application, was launched on 28 July 2026 by a Comorian graduate now studying in Morocco, with no such model existing before](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-«komori-tts-»,-la-première-synthèse-vocale-dédiée-au-shikomori.html>). [A CNDRS researcher presented two AI early-warning models for Comorian conditions, one for juvenile delinquency and one for Karthala seismovolcanic changes, simulated on real data and not deployed, per La Gazette des Comores](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/l%E2%80%99intelligence-artificielle-aux-comores-mariama-madi-%C3%A9veille-les-consciences-%C3%A0-lamerican-corner-.html>). [Komor-IA released a French-Shikomori parallel dataset of 1,424 sentence pairs in 2026](<https://huggingface.co/datasets/Komor-IA/French-ShiKomori-Parallel-Dataset>).
