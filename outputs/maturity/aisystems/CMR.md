# Cameroon

## AI built at home

**Model, dataset or computing.** [In July 2025 the Direction Generale du Budget said GOV-AI runs only on local servers, on CENADI's national infrastructure](<https://www.dgb.cm/gov-ai-lintelligence-artificielle-gouvernementale-en-vitrine-au-sago-2025/>). [It is available through the nGomna app](<https://www.dgb.cm/gov-ai-lintelligence-artificielle-gouvernementale-en-vitrine-au-sago-2025/>). [The National AI Strategy (SNIA), presented in July 2025 by the Minister of Posts and Telecommunications, plans a sovereign large language model, "GPT Cameroon"](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/>). [It is for local and national languages, with voice data collection](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/08/cameroon-unveils-2040-ai-roadmap-anchored-on-seven-strategic-pillars/>). [In February 2025 ST Digital announced GPU Cloud Africa, with NVIDIA GPU servers to be deployed in Douala](<https://itweb.africa/content/mYZRXv9g4nEMOgA8>). [It was to provide AI hosting from the second quarter of 2025](<https://itweb.africa/content/mYZRXv9g4nEMOgA8>). [Researchers at the universities of Ngaoundere and Dschang and ESSCA built a machine-learning tax fraud model for Cameroon](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2708-16627-cameroon-built-ai-claims-97-2-precision-in-tax-fraud-detection-but-revenue-impact-remains-unclear>). [In August 2026 it had been trained on 3.2 million records and piloted at three tax centres](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2708-16627-cameroon-built-ai-claims-97-2-precision-in-tax-fraud-detection-but-revenue-impact-remains-unclear>).
