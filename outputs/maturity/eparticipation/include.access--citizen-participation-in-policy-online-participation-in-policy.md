# Citizen participation in policy: online participation in policy

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Charter on Democracy, Elections and Governance asks for citizen participation in the development process; the UN E-Participation Index measures it online. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says laws and policies are adopted with no public consultation and no way for citizens to take part. |
| 2 Asked online | Yes to 1 |
| 3 Standing channel | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Answers published | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Required | Yes to 4 |

## Countries

### Senegal: 4 Operating

The government published what the public said in an online consultation in 2026; a rule requiring one is not on record.

- ARTP published on its website a synthesis report of the twenty-one contributions to the FTTx consultation, setting out points of convergence and the range of positions expressed ([source, 2026-08-10](https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx)).

### Angola: 3 Established

A standing online channel for public comment was in service in 2026; published answers are not on record.

- MINTTICS describes its Consulta Publica portal as a tool for the public to take part in drafting the sector's guiding documents, listing documents in consultation and contributions received ([source, 2026-08-27](https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/)).

### Zambia: 3 Established

A standing online channel for public comment was in service in 2026; published answers are not on record.

- The Zambia Bureau of Standards says stakeholders can access draft standards for public comment on its website, download supporting files and use an official public enquiry comments form, with 37 drafts currently uploaded for review ([source, 2026-08-29](https://www.zabs.org.zm/standards-development)).

### Botswana: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2025; a standing online channel is not on record.

- The Botswana Energy Regulatory Authority invited the public to send written comments by email by 30 January 2026 on BPC's 2026-27 tariff application, with the Public Consultation Paper requesting comments posted on the BERA website ([source, 2025-12-17](https://www.bera.co.bw/downloads/Electricity/BPC%202026-27%20Tariff%20Application%20-%20Public%20Notice/BERA%2028X5%20KO.pdf)).
- The notice says the draft Cybersecurity Bill is available on the BOCRA website and that written comments go by email to the ministry by 23 June 2025 ([source, 2025-06](https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf)).

### Cape Verde: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2026; a standing online channel is not on record.

- ARME, Cape Verde's multisectoral regulator, launched a public consultation on a proposed regulatory framework for satellite communication networks and services, with submissions open until 31 August 2026 ([source, 2026-08-05](https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/)).
- The Government of Cabo Verde put the Portal da Transparencia, a fiscal transparency portal, out to public consultation from 27 June to 17 July 2025 ([source, 2025-06-27](https://www.governo.cv/portal-da-transparencia-em-consulta-publica/)).

### Gambia: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2026; a standing online channel is not on record.

- PURA issued the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026 for public consultation on 2 April 2026, published on its website alongside six other draft instruments ([source, 2026-04-02](https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf)).
- The Public Utilities Regulatory Authority posted seven named draft regulatory instruments, including the Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026, on its website for download and invited the general public to send comments electronically to legal@pura.gm between 1 and 10 April 2026 ([source, 2026-04-01](https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/)).

### Ghana: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2025; a standing online channel is not on record.

### Mozambique: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2026; a standing online channel is not on record.

- Africa AI News reports INTIC opened a one-month public consultation on the first version of the draft National AI Strategy, running until 4 June 2026, with the full document on INTIC's website in editable and PDF formats and contributions sought from institutions, academia and citizens ([source, 2026-05](https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy)).
- INTIC says the first version of the proposed National Artificial Intelligence Strategy is on its official portal in Word and PDF for public consultation from 4 May to 4 June 2026, with comments sent by email ([source, 2026-05-04](https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/)).

### Nigeria: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2026; a standing online channel is not on record.

### Sao Tome and Principe: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2025; a standing online channel is not on record.

- AGER posted the National Cybersecurity Strategy of Sao Tome and Principe 2024-2028 and its action plan on its website for public consultation, with contributions to be sent within 30 days by the telephone and email contacts given ([source, 2025-09](https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe)).

### Tunisia: 2 Nascent

The government asked the public for views online in 2026; a standing online channel is not on record.

- The OGP Independent Reporting Mechanism says co-creation of Tunisia's 2023-2025 action plan collected over 320 proposals through the digital participation portal, workshops, email and public structures ([source, 2026-08-04](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/)).
- The Instance Nationale des Telecommunications (INT) opened a second public consultation on fibre access infrastructure sharing from 6 March to 2 April 2026, posted on its website with questions and responses accepted by email ([source, 2026-03-06](https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89)).
