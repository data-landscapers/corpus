# Algeria

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In January 2025 the National Observatory of Civil Society [opened a digital platform for associations](<https://www.aljazairalyoum.dz/%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ab%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%85%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%af%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%b9/>) to register and give their views on the draft organic law on associations. It stayed open until 30 January 2025. In April 2026 the Ministry of Youth [launched Sawtoukoum](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-sawtoukoum-platform-to-channel-youth-feedback-and-complaints/>), a digital platform where young people send proposals and observations to feed policy.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the launch of Sawtoukoum](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-sawtoukoum-platform-to-channel-youth-feedback-and-complaints/>). Young people can follow what happens to their submissions in real time. The platform takes their proposals and observations on public policy.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the observatory's call for views on the draft law](<https://www.aljazairalyoum.dz/%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ab%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%85%d9%87%d9%8a%d8%af%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%85%d8%b9/>). It was addressed to associations, which registered on the platform to take part, over about a week in January 2025.
