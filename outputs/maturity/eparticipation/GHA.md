# Ghana

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The Business Regulatory Reform unit of the Ministry of Trade and Industry [runs a Public Consultation Portal](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/transforming-governance-in-ghana-a-new-regulatory-service-model>), a World Bank blog said in March 2024. Civil society reads draft laws and regulatory proposals there. The unit's officials review the comments and poll results. In 2021 the MSME Classification Regulation went on the portal and drew over 1,000 views and suggestions. In November 2025 the National Information Technology Agency, NITA, [posted the draft Data Harmonisation Bill 2025 on its website](<https://nita.gov.gh/publicconsultation-3-2-2/>). Comments were taken from 19 to 27 November, by email.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the World Bank's account of the portal](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/transforming-governance-in-ghana-a-new-regulatory-service-model>). It says one of the suggestions made on the MSME regulation in 2021 was adopted in the final text. The unit's officials review the comments and poll results the portal collects.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the Graphic's report on the consultation](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanas-data-harmonisation-bill-2025-to-be-opened-for-public-consultation-from-nov-19-to-27-2025.html>). It said the draft would be open for public comment for nine days in November 2025. NITA is the government's agency for information technology. Its consultation page held the document.
