# DR Congo

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The telecoms regulator, ARPTC, [opened a public consultation on bringing in 5G](<https://arptc.gouv.cd/app/uploads/2025/03/Questionnaire_Consultation-publique_RDC_V7_010325.pdf>), from 3 March to 2 April 2025. Its questionnaire of 24 questions could be downloaded from the regulator's website. Replies were sent by email. The consultation asked how 5G should be introduced in the country. ARPTC regulates posts and telecommunications in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. In September 2025 the Minister of Youth [launched the National Youth Consultations 2025](<https://www.xtrafrica.com/news/grace-kutino-national-youth-consultations-2025>) with an online form for young people, to feed a revision of the National Youth Policy.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the 5G questionnaire itself](<https://arptc.gouv.cd/app/uploads/2025/03/Questionnaire_Consultation-publique_RDC_V7_010325.pdf>). It was posted on the regulator's website in March 2025, with a closing date of 2 April 2025.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the regulator's 5G consultation of March 2025](<https://arptc.gouv.cd/app/uploads/2025/03/Questionnaire_Consultation-publique_RDC_V7_010325.pdf>). It took written replies by email to a questionnaire of 24 questions, over one month. Replies went back to the regulator by email.
