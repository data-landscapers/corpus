# Mozambique

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** [Africa AI News reports INTIC opened a one-month public consultation on the first version of the draft National AI Strategy, running until 4 June 2026, with the full document on INTIC's website in editable and PDF formats and contributions sought from institutions, academia and citizens](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). [INTIC says the first version of the proposed National Artificial Intelligence Strategy is on its official portal in Word and PDF for public consultation from 4 May to 4 June 2026, with comments sent by email](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). The central bank, Banco de Moçambique, posted on its website [a draft revision of its mobile money customer-funds notice for public consultation on 22 April 2025](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>), and [a draft revision of its complaints-handling regulation on 6 June 2025](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/pt/media/noticias/consulta-p%C3%BAblica-sobre-a-proposta-de-revis%C3%A3o-do-aviso-n-%C2%BA-9-gbm-2020-de-31-de-dezembro-que-aprova-o-regulamento-de-tratamento-de-reclama%C3%A7%C3%B5es/>), with the documents linked.
