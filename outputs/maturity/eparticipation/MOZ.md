# Mozambique

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The ICT institute, INTIC, [posted the first draft of the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy on its portal](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>) for public consultation from 4 May to 4 June 2026. Comments went by email. [Africa AI News reported](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>) that contributions were sought from institutions, academia and citizens. The central bank, Banco de Moçambique, posted [a draft revision of its mobile money notice](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>) for consultation in April 2025. In June 2025 it posted [a draft revision of its complaints-handling rules](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/pt/media/noticias/consulta-p%C3%BAblica-sobre-a-proposta-de-revis%C3%A3o-do-aviso-n-%C2%BA-9-gbm-2020-de-31-de-dezembro-que-aprova-o-regulamento-de-tratamento-de-reclama%C3%A7%C3%B5es/>).

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [INTIC's notice on the AI strategy consultation](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). It offered the draft in Word and PDF and gave an email address for comments.
