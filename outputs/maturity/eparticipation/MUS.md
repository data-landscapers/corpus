# Mauritius

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** [The Government of Mauritius launched a public consultation on 10 April 2025 on a draft report on child online safety, posted on the ministry website, with views taken by email or the MAIA+ chatbot from 15 April 2025](<https://www.govmu.org/EN/newsgov/SitePages/Government-launches-public-consultation-to-beef-up-online-safety-of-children.aspx>). [The National Agency for Drug Control runs the National Submission Portal for Cannabis Policy Reform, collecting public comments and proposals online until 21 June 2026](<https://consultations.nadc.mu/>).

**Answers published.** No source found since October 2023 says the government published what it was told in an online consultation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.govmu.org/EN/newsgov/SitePages/Government-launches-public-consultation-to-beef-up-online-safety-of-children.aspx>).

**Required.** No source found since October 2023 says a rule requires drafts to be put online for public comment. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.govmu.org/EN/newsgov/SitePages/Government-launches-public-consultation-to-beef-up-online-safety-of-children.aspx>).
