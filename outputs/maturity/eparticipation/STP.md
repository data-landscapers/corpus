# Sao Tome and Principe

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In September 2025 the regulator AGER [posted the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2024-2028 and its action plan on its website](<https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe>) for public consultation. Contributions were to be sent within 30 days. The notice gave telephone and email contacts for them. AGER is the general regulatory authority of São Tomé and Príncipe. The consultation was announced among the regulator's notices.
