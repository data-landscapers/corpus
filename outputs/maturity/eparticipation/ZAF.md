# South Africa

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** Home Affairs [published the draft revised white paper on citizenship, immigration and refugee protection for public comment](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-leon-schreiber-invites-public-comment-draft-revised-white-paper-citizenship>). The draft was on the department's website from 12 December 2025 to 31 January 2026. Written comments went by email. [In September 2025 the Information Regulator published draft regulations on health and sex-life data](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202509/53426gon6673.pdf>), with written comments due by 10 October 2025. The Reserve Bank [lists a 2026 draft directive on payments for offshore merchants](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>), with a comment template and comments due on 17 July 2026.

**Answers published.** In 2023 the Reserve Bank's page [linked a feedback note answering the comments](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>) on a draft guidance note to Directive 1 of 2022. That was before October 2023. The note was hosted on the Financial Intelligence Centre's website.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the minister's invitation to comment on the white paper](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-leon-schreiber-invites-public-comment-draft-revised-white-paper-citizenship>). It set a closing date of 31 January 2026. Comments were accepted by email.
