# Tunisia

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The Open Government Partnership's review body [reported that Tunisia's 2023-2025 action plan drew over 320 proposals](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>). They came through the digital participation portal, workshops, email and public bodies. In March 2026 the telecoms regulator, INT, [opened a second consultation on sharing fibre access infrastructure](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89>). It ran to 2 April 2026, with replies by email. [Tunisia's government told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>) that e-people.gov.tn is a national platform allowing citizens to take part in policy decision-making.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the Open Government Partnership's results report](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>). It counted the proposals received while the action plan was drawn up. The plan covered 2023 to 2025 and drew over 320 proposals.
