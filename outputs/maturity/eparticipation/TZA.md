# Tanzania

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The Ministry of Finance [says its Maoni Portal is open to the public](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2025/06/tanzania-invites-public-input-on-tax.html>). Each December the ministry opens a window on the portal for views on improving the tax system. It said so in June 2025, when it invited the public to send views on tax policy through the portal.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the ministry's call of June 2025](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2025/06/tanzania-invites-public-input-on-tax.html>). It names the Maoni Portal as the channel for the public to send views on the tax system. The call was addressed to the public at large.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same call](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2025/06/tanzania-invites-public-input-on-tax.html>). It says the consultation window opens each December, on the portal, so that the public can propose changes to tax policy.
