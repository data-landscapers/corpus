# Zambia

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** On 21 October 2026 the telecoms regulator, ZICTA, [put its draft revised type approval guidelines on its website for comment](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>). Comments are taken through an online form until 20 November 2026. [The Zambia Bureau of Standards posts draft standards for comment on its website](<https://www.zabs.org.zm/standards-development>), with 37 drafts up for review in August 2026. Comments on those are made on the bureau's enquiry form.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [ZICTA's public notice](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>), which sets the closing date for comments on the guidelines. No source found since October 2023 says the government published the comments it received in an online consultation.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the government's answer to the World Bank's GovTech survey](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It names portal.citizensupport.gov.zm as a national platform for citizens to take part in policy decisions, as at 2025. No source found says a rule requires drafts to go online for comment.

***Also on file***. [Documentation and Information Centre (DIC)](<https://www.zabs.org.zm/standards-development>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows a portal taking comments online on more than one draft.
