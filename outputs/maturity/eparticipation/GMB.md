# Gambia

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In April 2026 the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority, PURA, [posted seven named draft regulatory instruments on its website](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>). The public was invited to send comments by email between 1 and 10 April 2026. One of the drafts was [the Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>), issued for consultation on 2 April. PURA regulates public utilities, broadcasting among them. In June 2025 PURA [posted the draft Gateway Traffic Measurement and Revenue Assurance Regulations 2025](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-on-the-draft-gateway-traffic-measurement-and-revenue-assurance-regulations-2025/>) for comment from 3 to 17 June, again by email.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [PURA's notice for the April 2026 consultation](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>). It gave a ten-day window and an email address for comments. Each of the seven drafts could be downloaded from the authority's website.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the draft broadcasting and online content regulations](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>). They were put out for consultation alongside six other draft instruments, on 2 April 2026.
