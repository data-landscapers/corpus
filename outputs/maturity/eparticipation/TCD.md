# Chad

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In January 2024 Prime Minister Succès Masra [asked Chadians on social media](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20240116-tchad-le-premier-ministre-succ%C3%A8s-masra-consulte-les-tchadiens-sur-les-r%C3%A9seaux-sociaux>) what they expected of his government. He also asked for the three sectors they would make priorities. He did so ahead of the government programme he was to present on 18 January 2024.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [RFI's report of the call](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20240116-tchad-le-premier-ministre-succ%C3%A8s-masra-consulte-les-tchadiens-sur-les-r%C3%A9seaux-sociaux>), published on 16 January 2024. It describes the prime minister asking the public for views on social networks, two days before he was due to present his programme. The report names no other channel for the replies.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same report](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20240116-tchad-le-premier-ministre-succ%C3%A8s-masra-consulte-les-tchadiens-sur-les-r%C3%A9seaux-sociaux>). The call came from the prime minister himself, and asked each reply to name three priority sectors for the government's work.
