# Benin

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In April 2025 the Open Government Partnership [reported that the BousProB application](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/stories/co-creation-in-action-how-benin-is-making-budgets-work-for-people/>) lets citizens in Benin follow the stages of the budget process and comment on them. The application belongs to an Open Budget Hub run by the budget directorate. The hub opens 13 of the 17 stages of the budget process to civil society.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the partnership's account of the hub](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/stories/co-creation-in-action-how-benin-is-making-budgets-work-for-people/>). It describes citizens commenting on the budget through the application, as the budget moves from one stage to the next.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same account](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/stories/co-creation-in-action-how-benin-is-making-budgets-work-for-people/>), published on 30 April 2025. It presents the hub as a way to open the making of the budget to civil society and to citizens.

***Also on file***. [229 Cadre de vie](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3436/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a portal takes comments online on more than one draft.
