# Senegal

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In March 2026 ARTP, the telecoms regulator, [launched a public consultation on deploying and sharing FTTx fibre networks](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>). It received twenty-one contributions from operators, public developers, equipment makers, consultancies and individuals. [The World Bank's GovTech record lists the Jubbanti platform](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>) at jubbanti.sec.gouv.sn, as at 2025, as a national platform allowing citizens to take part in policy decision-making. [The digital ministry's consultation on the 2025-2035 digital strategy drew more than 700 contributions on Jubbanti](<https://letechobservateur.sn/cloture-de-la-consultation-publique-pour-la-strategie-numerique-du-senegal-2025-2035-un-pas-vers-une-transformation-digitaleinclusive/>). It opened on 25 July 2024.

**Answers published.** In August 2026 ARTP [published on its website a synthesis report of the twenty-one contributions](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>). It sets out the points of convergence and the range of positions expressed. The report covers the consultation on FTTx fibre that ARTP launched in March 2026.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the GovTech record's entry for Jubbanti](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). It lists the platform as a national one for citizen participation in policy decision-making, as at 2025. No source found since October 2023 says a rule requires drafts to go online.
