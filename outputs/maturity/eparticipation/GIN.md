# Guinea

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The Secretariat General of the Government [keeps a web page of draft texts open for public comment](<https://sgg.gov.gn/document/textecom/1>). As retrieved in October 2026 it listed three drafts, among them the bill revising the labour code. Each draft carries a button to read the text and another to comment on it. The page shows how many comments each has received: 2 on one draft and 9 on another.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the secretariat's page of drafts for comment](<https://sgg.gov.gn/document/textecom/1>). It shows a count of comments for each draft. It does not show the comments.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same page](<https://sgg.gov.gn/document/textecom/1>), which presents the drafts as available for comment by the public. It gives the secretariat's address and telephone number.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the government published what it was told in an online consultation.
