# Rwanda

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In February 2026 the Capital Market Authority [invited the public to comment on a draft regulation](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>). The draft governs operators of intermediary service platforms. It was posted on the authority's website. Comments went by email, by 24 February 2026. The Capital Market Authority regulates Rwanda's capital market. In March 2025 the authority and the National Bank of Rwanda [published the draft law regulating virtual assets business](<https://bitcoinke.io/2025/03/rwanda-invites-public-comments-on-virtual-assets-draft-law/>). Comments went by email, and a consultation was held on Zoom on 17 March 2025.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the authority's call for public comments](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>). It gave the closing date and an email address for replies. It was issued as a press notice on 12 February 2026, twelve days before the closing date.
