## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In February 2026 the Capital Market Authority [invited the public to comment on a draft regulation](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>). The draft governs operators of intermediary service platforms. It was posted on the authority's website. Comments went by email, by 24 February 2026. The Capital Market Authority regulates Rwanda's capital market.
