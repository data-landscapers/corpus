# Egypt

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** IDSC [runs the Hiwar platform](<https://www.masrtimes.com/531613>). Citizens register there and comment on issues the government puts to them. In May 2025 it said two new issues go up every 30 days. That month they were aviation and water quality. In January 2026 the Egyptian Tax Authority [launched a consultation platform](<https://cairoscene.com/News/Tax-Authority-Launches-Consultation-Platform-for-Businesses>). Companies and business sectors send views and proposals there before tax decisions are issued.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [IDSC's account of Hiwar](<https://www.masrtimes.com/531613>). Citizens who register can comment on each issue posted on the platform, two of them every month. Registration is open to any citizen.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the Tax Authority's platform](<https://cairoscene.com/News/Tax-Authority-Launches-Consultation-Platform-for-Businesses>), launched in January 2026. It takes views from companies and their representatives before the authority issues tax decisions.
