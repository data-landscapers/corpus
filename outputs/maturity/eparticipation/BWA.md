# Botswana

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In June 2025 a notice [put the draft Cybersecurity Bill on the website of BOCRA](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>), the communications regulator. Written comments went to the ministry by email, by 23 June 2025. In December 2025 the energy regulator, BERA, [posted a consultation paper on the power utility's 2026-27 tariff application](<https://www.bera.co.bw/downloads/Electricity/BPC%202026-27%20Tariff%20Application%20-%20Public%20Notice/BERA%2028X5%20KO.pdf>). It asked the public for written comments by email by 30 January 2026.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the energy regulator's call for comments on the tariff application](<https://www.bera.co.bw/downloads/Electricity/BPC%202026-27%20Tariff%20Application%20-%20Public%20Notice/BERA%2028X5%20KO.pdf>). It set a closing date of 30 January 2026 and took replies by email.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the notice on the draft Cybersecurity Bill](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>). It named the ministry as the place to send written comments, and email as the way to send them.
