# Seychelles

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** [In February 2024 the Central Bank of Seychelles invited the public to comment on proposed amendments to its payment system licensing regulations](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/21119/cbs-opens-public-consultation-on-proposed-amendments-to-the-national-payment-system-licensing-and-authorisation-regulations-2014>). The consultation paper was posted on the bank's website. Comments were taken by email until 29 February 2024. The regulations govern licences for payment service providers and authorisation to run payment systems.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the central bank's February 2024 consultation](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/21119/cbs-opens-public-consultation-on-proposed-amendments-to-the-national-payment-system-licensing-and-authorisation-regulations-2014>). The bank said the amendments would give it more discretion over licence applications. No report of the comments it received has been found.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same consultation on payment licensing rules](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/21119/cbs-opens-public-consultation-on-proposed-amendments-to-the-national-payment-system-licensing-and-authorisation-regulations-2014>). The bank said the 2014 regulations had shown deficiencies since they came into force. No rule requiring drafts to be put online for comment has been found.

***Also on file***. [E-Portal](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Seychelles_Action-Plan_2023-2025_December.pdf>).

***Not found***. No standing online consultation portal has been found. Nor has a report of comments received, or a rule requiring online consultation.
