# Somalia

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** On 17 March 2024 the Speaker of the Lower House, Madobe, [launched a Digital Platform for Public Consultations](<https://radiodalsan.com/2024/03/18/speaker-madobe-launches-digital-platform-for-public-consultations-on-constitutional-review/>) on the proposed constitutional amendments. The Independent Constitutional Review and Implementation Commission built it to gather citizens' views. In July 2023 the National Communications Authority [invited comments by email on its draft strategic plan for 2023-2027](<https://nca.gov.so/strategic-plan-2023-207/>).

The platform was built to collect feedback from citizens on the amendments. It was the commission's own platform, built for this review.
