# Angola

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The ICT ministry, MINTTICS, [runs a public consultation portal](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). It describes the portal as a way for the public to help draft the sector's guiding documents. In August 2026 the portal [carried the draft law on artificial intelligence](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>), with 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and 5 from the general public. In April 2025 the regulator INACOM reported that [the ministry's draft cybersecurity laws were open for comment](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/04/15/sessao-tematica-aborda-programa-de-desenvolvimento-e-expansao-das-telecomunicacoes-e-tecnologias-de-informacao/>) on the same portal and by email. In December 2025 a central bank committee [put the draft financial inclusion strategy for 2025-2027 out for public consultation](<https://plataformacipra.gov.ao/public/ficheiros/arquivos/Draft_da_ENIF_Consulta_Publica.pdf>) on a government platform.

**Answers published.** The portal [shows how many contributions the draft AI law received](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>), counted by type of contributor. It lists the documents in consultation beside them.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the ministry's own account of the portal](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). It calls the portal a channel of dialogue between the administration and the public on the documents in consultation.
