# Angola

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** [MINTTICS describes its Consulta Publica portal as a tool for the public to take part in drafting the sector's guiding documents, listing documents in consultation and contributions received](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). [MINTTICS's consultation portal posts the Proposta de Lei sobre a Inteligencia Artificial as a PDF in consultation and shows 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and 5 from the general public](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). [The BNA-convened CCENIF put the draft Estrategia Nacional de Inclusao Financeira 2025-2027 out for public consultation as a PDF on a gov.ao platform, asking readers for their contribution](<https://plataformacipra.gov.ao/public/ficheiros/arquivos/Draft_da_ENIF_Consulta_Publica.pdf>).

**Answers published.** No source held since October 2023 says the government published what it was told in an online consultation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>).

**Required.** No source held since October 2023 says a rule requires drafts to be put online for public comment. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>).
