# Angola

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** The ICT ministry, MINTTICS, [runs a public consultation portal](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). It describes the portal as a way for the public to help draft the sector's guiding documents. In August 2026 the portal [carried the draft law on artificial intelligence](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). It showed 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and 5 from the general public. In April 2025 the regulator INACOM reported that [the ministry's draft cybersecurity laws were open for comment](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/04/15/sessao-tematica-aborda-programa-de-desenvolvimento-e-expansao-das-telecomunicacoes-e-tecnologias-de-informacao/>) on the same portal and by email. In December 2025 a central bank committee [put the draft financial inclusion strategy for 2025-2027 out for public consultation](<https://plataformacipra.gov.ao/public/ficheiros/arquivos/Draft_da_ENIF_Consulta_Publica.pdf>) on a government platform. INACOM [runs a consultation portal of its own](<https://consultapublica.inacom.gov.ao/>). Draft laws and regulations are posted there. The public replies through forms, polls and forums. In March 2025 the data protection agency [posted its draft revision of the data protection law](<https://www.consultapublica.gov.ao/consulta/100021>) on the national consultation portal. It was open for comment until 17 April 2025. In May 2026 the administrative modernisation institute IMA [put the draft Digital Government Law on the public consultations portal](<https://ima.gov.ao/web/noticias/ima-promove-sessao-de-consulta-publica-sobre-o-anteprojecto-de-lei-do-governo-digital>).

**Answers published.** The portal [shows how many contributions the draft AI law received](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>), counted by type of contributor. It lists the documents in consultation beside them.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the ministry's own account of the portal](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). It calls the portal a channel of dialogue between the administration and the public on the documents in consultation.
