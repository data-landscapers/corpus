# Malawi

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In February 2024 the Ministry of Information and Digitalization, through the regulator MACRA, [invited the public to a hybrid consultation meeting](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/invitation-to-a-hybrid-public-stakeholder-consultation-meeting-on-the-electronic-transactions-and-cyber-security-act-etca-regulations-bingu-international-conference-centre-bicc-in-lilong/>). The meeting was on three named draft regulations under the Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act. People could attend in Lilongwe or online through Teams. The drafts were linked from the invitation by QR code.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is [the invitation to the hybrid meeting](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/invitation-to-a-hybrid-public-stakeholder-consultation-meeting-on-the-electronic-transactions-and-cyber-security-act-etca-regulations-bingu-international-conference-centre-bicc-in-lilong/>). It gave the venue, the online link and the drafts to be discussed. The physical meeting was in Lilongwe.

**Required.** The nearest fact is [the same invitation](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/invitation-to-a-hybrid-public-stakeholder-consultation-meeting-on-the-electronic-transactions-and-cyber-security-act-etca-regulations-bingu-international-conference-centre-bicc-in-lilong/>). It was issued by the ministry through MACRA for the three cyber security regulations. The act governs electronic transactions and cyber security in Malawi.
