# Kenya

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In May 2026 the Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy [posted its draft National Data Governance Policy online for public comment](<https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/have-your-say-kenyas-draft-national-data-governance-policy-comments-due-5-june-2026/>). Comments were due by 5 June 2026, by email or post. KICTANet, a Kenyan policy network, relayed the call. In September 2026 the Social Health Authority [put its proposed 2026-2029 provider contracts on its website](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-08-sha-invites-views-on-new-healthcare-contracts>). It gave stakeholders an online feedback form, with comments due on 10 September.
