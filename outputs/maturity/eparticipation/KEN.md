# Kenya

## Online participation in policy

**Asked online.** In May 2026 the Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy [posted its draft National Data Governance Policy online for public comment](<https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/have-your-say-kenyas-draft-national-data-governance-policy-comments-due-5-june-2026/>). Comments were due by 5 June 2026, by email or post. KICTANet, a Kenyan policy network, relayed the call. In September 2026 the Social Health Authority [put its proposed 2026-2029 provider contracts on its website](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-08-sha-invites-views-on-new-healthcare-contracts>). It gave stakeholders an online feedback form, with comments due on 10 September. In November 2024 the Ministry of Health [posted three draft regulations under the Digital Health Act on its website](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/realtime/2024-11-21-moh-releases-venues-for-public-forums-on-digital-health-laws>) and invited written comments by 19 December 2024, alongside regional forums. [The government told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>) that Kenya has a national platform for citizen participation in policy decision-making, at publicparticipation.statelaw.go.ke.

**Answers published.** The nearest fact is the [data governance policy's call for comments](<https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/have-your-say-kenyas-draft-national-data-governance-policy-comments-due-5-june-2026/>). It asked for written replies by 5 June 2026, by email or post.

**Required.** The nearest fact is the [Social Health Authority's online call for views](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-08-sha-invites-views-on-new-healthcare-contracts>). It covered the authority's proposed provider contracts for 2026 to 2029. Stakeholders could reply through an online form.
