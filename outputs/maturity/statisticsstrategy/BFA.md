# Burkina Faso

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [On 1 December 2025 the national statistics council examined the draft fifth Schéma directeur de la statistique for 2026-2030](<https://yop.l-frii.com/burkina-faso-statistique-nationale-examen-dun-projet-estime-a-96689-milliards-de-f-cfa/>). The draft was costed at FCFA 96.689 billion. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the SDS 2026-2030 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [On 11 September 2026 the head of INSD, the statistics institute, called it the new framework for statistical work](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149178>). The 2027 national statistical programme is aligned with it. [It follows the SDS 2021-2025](<https://www.insd.bf/sites/default/files/2026-04/Plan%20strat%C3%A9gique_2026-2030%20de%20l%27INSD_0.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on the SDS 2026-2030 has been found. [The council's September 2026 session](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149178>) aligned the next year's programme with it.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the SDS 2026-2030 or of the SDS 2021-2025 has been found. [The 2026-2030 plan began after the council examined its draft in December 2025](<https://yop.l-frii.com/burkina-faso-statistique-nationale-examen-dun-projet-estime-a-96689-milliards-de-f-cfa/>).
