# Zambia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2023-2027](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [In July 2023 the Minister of Finance and National Planning's foreword presented the Second National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (NSDS2) for 2023-2027 as the national strategy for the statistical system](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>). [The first NSDS ended in 2018](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body. **Evaluated.** [In 2017 the African Development Bank, the UN Economic Commission for Africa, COMESA and PARIS21 evaluated NSDS1](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>). [They recommended a second NSDS](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>).
