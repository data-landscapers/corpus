# Cape Verde

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2022-2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [A Budget Support Group mission on 20-28 November 2025 noted progress in implementing the national statistics development strategy (ENDE)](<https://www.governo.cv/comunicado-de-imprensa-do-grupo-de-apoio-orcamental-20-28-de-novembro-de-2025/>). [In 2022 the fourth ENDE, for 2022-2026, was finalised after a five-phase process ending in validation](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ENDE-2022_2026-VF.pdf>). [It names approval by the State as a condition of its success](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ENDE-2022_2026-VF.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body.
