# Cape Verde

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2022-2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [The Budget Support Group mission of 20-28 November 2025 notes progress in implementing Cabo Verde's national statistics development strategy (ENDE), so it is in force; the document gives no period](<https://www.governo.cv/comunicado-de-imprensa-do-grupo-de-apoio-orcamental-20-28-de-novembro-de-2025/>). [The document presents itself as the fourth ENDE, covering 2022-2026, finalised after a five-phase process ending in validation, and lists its approval by the State as a condition of success, so approval is not stated (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ENDE-2022_2026-VF.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body. The nearest held fact: [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2022-2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Evaluated.** [The document summarises a completed evaluation of the previous ENDE 2017-2021 on three dimensions (execution, ownership, impact), reported as positive overall, with a financial execution rate of 83% of the estimated cost for actions (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ENDE-2022_2026-VF.pdf>).
