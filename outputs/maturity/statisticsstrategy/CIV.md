# Cote d'Ivoire

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [On 23 September 2025 the "Vision et stratégies" report of the SNDS 2026-2030 was adopted](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/744751/strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-2026-2030-le-rapport-vision-et-strategies-adopte>). [A workshop validated its action plan on 10 October 2025](<https://www.abidjaneconomie.net/2025/10/11/atelier-de-validation-du-rapport-de-la-snds-2026-2030-une-etape-cle-pour-moderniser-le-systeme-statistique-national/>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the SNDS 2026-2030 as awaiting adoption](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [Technical validation began on 30 April 2026, the last step before adoption](<https://plan.gouv.ci/actualite/960>). [On 8 June 2026 the planning minister presented the strategy as one of the pillars of the statistical system](<https://plan.gouv.ci/index.php/actualite/983>). It is aligned with the national development plan.

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on the SNDS 2026-2030 has been found. [The minister's June 2026 presentation](<https://plan.gouv.ci/index.php/actualite/983>) set out the statistical system, not the strategy's results.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the SNDS 2026-2030 has been found. [A diagnostic of the national statistical system was validated on 17 September 2025](<https://plan.gouv.ci/actualite/890>) as a step in drawing it up.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy in the last three years.
