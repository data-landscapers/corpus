# South Sudan

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In September 2025 the director general of the National Bureau of Statistics said the second National Strategy for the Development of Statistics, NSDS II, was being developed](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/acs/capacity-building-workshop-data-ethics-governance-quality/presentations/2025_da.14_session_3a_south_sudan_coordinating_national_systems.pdf>). It is to cover the Bureau and the other producers of official statistics. [In May 2026 FAO said the agricultural statistics plan SPARS 2026-2031 is being aligned with NSDS II](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/south-sudan-advances-national-agricultural-statistics-strategy-through-fao-supported-diagnostic-workshop/en>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the Master Plan for Statistics Development 2021-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). Its period has ended. [The first strategy, the NSDS 2014-2020, had a budget of US$58 million](<https://nbs.gov.ss/about-us/Strategic_Plan.php>).
