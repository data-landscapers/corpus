# South Sudan

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The director general of the National Bureau of Statistics said in September 2025 that the second National Strategy for the Development of Statistics, NSDS II, was being developed, covering the Bureau and other producers of official statistics](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/acs/capacity-building-workshop-data-ethics-governance-quality/presentations/2025_da.14_session_3a_south_sudan_coordinating_national_systems.pdf>). [FAO said in May 2026 that the agricultural statistics plan SPARS 2026-2031 is being aligned with NSDS II](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/south-sudan-advances-national-agricultural-statistics-strategy-through-fao-supported-diagnostic-workshop/en>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the Master Plan for Statistics Development in Southern Sudan 2021-2025 as the strategy being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>); its period has ended. [The Bureau's first strategy, the NSDS 2014-2020, had an estimated budget of US$58 million and a coordination unit at the Bureau](<https://nbs.gov.ss/about-us/Strategic_Plan.php>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body.
