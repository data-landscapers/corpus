# Algeria

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** In March 2025 the Prime Minister, in a written reply to a deputy, said [the National Statistics Council was drawing up the national statistics strategy](<https://elmaouid.dz/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7/>) through dedicated working committees. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the strategy for 2015 to 2025 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>), a period that has since ended. In November 2024 the head of the statistics office [spoke of a national statistics strategy for 2024 to 2028](<http://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/55477>), resting on technology, statistics training and wider cooperation.

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on a strategy since October 2023. The nearest fact: in 2025 [the Council's committees were drawing up a roadmap for economic, environmental and social statistics](<https://elmaouid.dz/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7/>) and revising the statistics law.

**Evaluated.** No source found reports a mid-term or final evaluation since October 2023. The nearest fact: [PARIS21 still listed the 2015 to 2025 strategy as being implemented in January 2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).
