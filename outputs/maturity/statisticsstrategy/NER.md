# Niger

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The government adopted SNDS III for 2022-2026 in 2022, the statistics institute says](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/rapport_enquete/Rapport_sur_etat_de_la_statistique_du_SSN_2024.pdf>). [Its May 2022 text costs the action plan at FCFA 85.89 billion](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SNDS-III_Niger.pdf>). At adoption 35.5% of that was secured and 64.5% was still to be found. [Coordination work in 2024 carried out the action plan](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/rapport_enquete/Rapport_sur_etat_de_la_statistique_du_SSN_2024.pdf>), the institute reported in December 2025. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists SNDS 2022-2026 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on SNDS III has been found since November 2023. The nearest fact is [the institute's December 2025 account of coordination work under the action plan in 2024](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/rapport_enquete/Rapport_sur_etat_de_la_statistique_du_SSN_2024.pdf>).

**Evaluated.** No mid-term or final evaluation of SNDS III has been found. [Its period runs to 2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).
