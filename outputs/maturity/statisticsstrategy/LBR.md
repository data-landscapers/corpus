# Liberia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In February 2024 Richard Ngafuan told the Senate that the strategy in force from 2008 expired in 2013](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-lisgis-director-designate-promises-annual-report-on-liberias-population-to-track-national-development/>). He was the incoming head of LISGIS, the statistics institute. No strategy had governed the system since. [In May 2025 UNFPA advertised a 60-working-day consultancy to draft and validate a new NSDS](<https://liberia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-national-strategy-development-statistics-nsds-liberia>). It was to align with the 2025-2029 development plan. [NSDS III for 2026-2030 was signed at Monrovia on 15 December 2025](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/nsds/nsdsIII.pdf>). Its chapter 6 says activities proceed once the LISGIS Board approves it. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table still listed the strategy as being designed or awaiting adoption](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [LISGIS launched the strategy officially on 18 February 2026](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-lisgis-launches-national-statistics-strategy-set-to-unveils-new-population-policy/>). [The first strategy, NSDS I, ran from 2008 to 2013](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/nsds/nsdsIII.pdf>). It guided post-war recovery of the statistical system.

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on NSDS III has been found. [Its launch in February 2026](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-lisgis-launches-national-statistics-strategy-set-to-unveils-new-population-policy/>) is the latest event found.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of NSDS III or of NSDS I has been found. [NSDS III's period runs to 2030](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/nsds/nsdsIII.pdf>).
