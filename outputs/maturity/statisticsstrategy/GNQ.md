# Equatorial Guinea

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The ENDE 2022-2026 is a five-year plan prepared by INEGE, the statistics institute](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). [On 14 July 2023 the Council of Ministers approved the draft decree on the strategy](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/ampliacion_de_referencia_del_consejo_de_ministros_de_14_de_julio_de_2023>). It has four axes and 218 activities. [In March 2024 the press office reported that Decree 103 approves the strategy for 2022-2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/inege_realiza_una_gira_sobre_la_estrategia_de_desarrollo_estadistico_2022-2026>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists it as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [In August 2026 the national statistics council took up an order creating the team to draft the successor, ENDE 2027-2031](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_consejo_nacional_de_estadistica_analiza_los_avances_de_las_principales_operaciones_estadisticas_del_pais>).

**Progress reviewed.** [In February 2025 the Statistical Programmes Committee adopted the 2024 implementation report, with an overall execution rate of 30%](<https://inege.org/?p=6815>). [The national statistics council heard it in March 2025](<https://inege.org/?p=6896>). [In April 2026 the committee took the 2025 implementation report and the 2026 action plan](<https://inege.org/?p=16157>). [The council had the 2025 report on its agenda for validation in August 2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_consejo_nacional_de_estadistica_analiza_los_avances_de_las_principales_operaciones_estadisticas_del_pais>).

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the ENDE 2022-2026 has been found. [The strategy reports that the ENDE 2016-2020 completed 47.8% of its activities](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>).
