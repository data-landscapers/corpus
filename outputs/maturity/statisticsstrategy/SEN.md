# Senegal

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [Senegal's authorities will pursue the IMF ROSC recommendations through the 2024-2028 national statistical development strategy, which is thus in force](<https://www.channelafrica.co.za/channelafrica/news/imf-calls-for-stronger-coordination-of-senegals-economic-data/>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2025-2029](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [The final report of the third strategy, SNDS III 2019-2023, was presented to the steering committee on 18 December and to the CTPS on 19 December 2019 after its action plans were validated by the CTPS on 4-5 December, and delivered to ANSD on 23 December 2019 (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>).
