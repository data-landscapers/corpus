# Senegal

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In September 2026 the authorities said they would pursue the IMF's statistics recommendations through the 2024-2028 strategy](<https://www.channelafrica.co.za/channelafrica/news/imf-calls-for-stronger-coordination-of-senegals-economic-data/>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2025-2029](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [In December 2019 the final report of the third strategy, SNDS III 2019-2023, went to its steering committee and the CTPS](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>). [It was delivered to ANSD on 23 December 2019](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body. The nearest held fact: [Senegal's authorities will pursue the IMF ROSC recommendations through the 2024-2028 national statistical development strategy, which is thus in force](<https://www.channelafrica.co.za/channelafrica/news/imf-calls-for-stronger-coordination-of-senegals-economic-data/>).

**Evaluated.** [In November 2017 AFRISTAT, PARIS21 and INSEE held a mid-term evaluation of the SNDS 2014-2019](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>). [It found 52% physical implementation after four of six years](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>).
