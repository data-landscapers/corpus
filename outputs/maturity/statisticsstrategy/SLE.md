# Sierra Leone

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [Statistics Sierra Leone issued the Extended Second National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (E-NSDS-II), dated November 2023](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/Extended-Second-National-Strategy-for-the-Development-of-Statistics_E-NSDSII.pdf>). It extends NSDS II (2016-2020) so that NSDS III can be designed later to match the next national development plan. [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2022-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body. The nearest fact: [in November 2023 Statistics Sierra Leone extended NSDS II (2016-2020) as the E-NSDS-II](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/Extended-Second-National-Strategy-for-the-Development-of-Statistics_E-NSDSII.pdf>). [NSDS III is to be designed later to match the next national development plan](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/Extended-Second-National-Strategy-for-the-Development-of-Statistics_E-NSDSII.pdf>).

**Evaluated.** [In 2018 a mid-term review of NSDS II found weaknesses in the Statistics Act](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/Extended-Second-National-Strategy-for-the-Development-of-Statistics_E-NSDSII.pdf>).
