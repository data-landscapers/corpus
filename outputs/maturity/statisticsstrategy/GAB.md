# Gabon

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In 2023 the statistical system worked on an interim strategy, SNDS-II for 2023-2025, whose validation was still awaited](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-bientot-un-institut-national-de-la-statistique/>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the SNDS 2023-2025 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). Its period ended in 2025. [In October 2026 INSTAT Gabon said it had begun drafting SNDS-III for 2027-2030](<https://new.instatgabon.org/fr/actualite/snds-2027-2030-l-instat-gabon-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-du-systeme-statistique-national>). The work is supported by the HISWACA-SOP 2 project. [An international expert's second mission on its strategic orientations was set for 1 to 13 November 2026](<https://new.instatgabon.org/fr/actualite/snds-2026-2030-deuxieme-mission-de-l-expert-international-alain-brilleau-a-l-instat-gabon>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on SNDS-II has been found since 2023. [The statistics office's site lists SNDS-II with a presentation of its roadmap](<https://statgabon.ga/donnee_a_telecharcher/>).

**Evaluated.** No completed evaluation of SNDS-II has been found. [INSTAT Gabon says the drafting of SNDS-III draws on an evaluation of SNDS-II](<https://new.instatgabon.org/fr/actualite/snds-2027-2030-l-instat-gabon-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-du-systeme-statistique-national>) and a diagnostic of the system.
