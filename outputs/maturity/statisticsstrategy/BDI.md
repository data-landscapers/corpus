# Burundi

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [INSBU refers to the SNDS-III as the statistics strategy in force](<https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists it as adopted: NSDS 2022-2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). INSBU's account gives no period, so PARIS21's dates are used. Four facts are held, all given here. No news story or notice read says more.

**Progress reviewed.** [The statutory Groupe Sectoriel Statistique met on 28 July 2026](<https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements>). It discussed the recommendations of the SNDS-III mid-term evaluation. The agenda also covered the agricultural survey, the census and data-collection methods.

**Evaluated.** [The statutory Groupe Sectoriel Statistique meeting of 28 July 2026 discussed the recommendations of the mid-term evaluation of the SNDS-III, which shows that evaluation had reported](<https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements>). The completion date is not given, so the meeting date stands for it.
