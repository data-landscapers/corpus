# Central African Republic

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [ICASEES refers to the second national statistics development strategy (SNDS II) as being implemented in June 2026](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/361-icasees-validation-du-plan-de-renforcement-des-capacites-des-cadres-du-systeme-statistique-national>). [ICASEES states the Central African Republic adopted its second national statistics development strategy (SNDS2, period 2024-2028, envelope of nearly FCFA 60 billion) in August 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** [ICASEES held a workshop on 7-9 October 2026 in Bangui, with members of the statistical programming committee (CTPAS), to review implementation of SNDS2 2024-2028 at mid-term against its commitments, funded by UNDP](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>).
