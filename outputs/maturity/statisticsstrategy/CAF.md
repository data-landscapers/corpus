# Central African Republic

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [ICASEES, the statistics institute, says SNDS2 for 2024-2028 was adopted in August 2023](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). [In July 2024 it gave the approval date as 31 August 2023](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/07/22/centrafrique-mise-a-jour-de-la-strategie-nationale-du-developpement-de-statistique-deuxieme-generation/>). [On African Statistics Day in November 2024 the strategy's cost was put at 58 billion, with 36 billion available](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/11/25/la-centrafrique-se-dote-dune-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-pour-repondre-aux-besoins-de-sa-population/>). The government pledged 0.15% of the state budget to statistical activity. [In July 2026 ICASEES said partners support the strategy's implementation](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/389-icasees-le-directeur-general-de-licasees-recoit-une-mission-devaluation-des-activites-du-pnud>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table still lists the earlier SNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** [On 18 July 2024 ICASEES held a workshop to update SNDS2](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/07/22/centrafrique-mise-a-jour-de-la-strategie-nationale-du-developpement-de-statistique-deuxieme-generation/>). Its director general said the system's members meet each year to assess what worked the year before. [On 7-9 October 2026 ICASEES reviewed SNDS2's implementation at mid-term with the statistical programming committee](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). UNDP funded the October workshop.

**Evaluated.** No completed evaluation of SNDS2 has been found. [The October 2026 mid-term workshop](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>) reviewed implementation and reported no finished evaluation.
