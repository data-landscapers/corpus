# Central African Republic

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [ICASEES, the national statistics institute, says the Central African Republic adopted its second national strategy for the development of statistics, SNDS2, in August 2023](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). It runs from 2024 to 2028, with an envelope of nearly FCFA 60 billion. [In July 2026 ICASEES said partners support the strategy's implementation](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/389-icasees-le-directeur-general-de-licasees-recoit-une-mission-devaluation-des-activites-du-pnud>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table still lists the earlier SNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** [On 7-9 October 2026 ICASEES held a workshop in Bangui with members of the statistical programming committee (CTPAS) to review SNDS2's implementation at mid-term against its commitments](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). UNDP funded it.

**Evaluated.** No completed mid-term or final evaluation of SNDS2 has been found. The nearest fact is [the October 2026 mid-term workshop, which reviewed implementation but did not report a finished evaluation](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>).
