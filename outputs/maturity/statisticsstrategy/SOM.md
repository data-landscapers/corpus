# Somalia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2024-2029](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [The Somalia National Bureau of Statistics unveiled the Second National Strategy for the Development of Statistics on 2025-12-07](<https://nbs.gov.so/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-statistics-nsdsii/>). It is NSDS 2, for 2024-2029 per the document's header note, and builds on the first NSDS. [The first NSDS was in effect from 2017 to 2023](<https://nbs.gov.so/national-strategy-for-the-development-of-statistics-nsdsii/>). It was developed under the Directorate of Statistics in the Ministry of Planning, Investment and Economic Development. It has ended and been succeeded by NSDS 2. [EGRISS says NSDS II, covering 2024-2029, was validated in October 2023](<https://egrisstats.org/implementation/case-studies/somalia/>). [PARIS21's September 2024 status table already listed it as in implementation](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>).
