# Gambia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2024-2028](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [STATAFRIC's announcement of the peer review report says The Gambia has adopted NSDS III 2024-2028](<https://statafric.au.int/en/news/press-releases/2025-11-17/peer-review-report-national-statistical-system-gambia>). [On 13 September 2023 the national statistical system technically approved NSDS III after a validation workshop in Banjul](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). [It went to the National Statistics Council and Cabinet for endorsement](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). [Its predecessor, NSDS II, ran from 2018 to 2022](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>).

**Progress reviewed.** No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy since October 2023, from an annual review, a progress report or a meeting of its steering body. **Evaluated.** [A final evaluation of NSDS II 2018-2022 was carried out in July 2022 with PARIS21 support](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). [Its report was validated at a national workshop on 13-15 February 2023](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>).
