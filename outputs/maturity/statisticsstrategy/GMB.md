# Gambia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2024-2028](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [STATAFRIC's announcement of the peer review report says The Gambia has adopted NSDS III 2024-2028](<https://statafric.au.int/en/news/press-releases/2025-11-17/peer-review-report-national-statistical-system-gambia>). [NSDS III 2024-28 was technically approved on 13 September 2023 by the national statistical system after a validation workshop in Banjul, and was submitted for endorsement by the National Statistics Council and Cabinet (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). [The Gambia implemented its second national strategy for the development of statistics, NSDS II 2018-2022, which has since come to term (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>).
