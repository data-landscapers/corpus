# Nigeria

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The government unveiled NSDS III for 2024-2028 on 15 December 2025](<https://nannews.ng/2025/12/15/fg-unveils-strategic-data-initiatives-for-evidence-based-governance-inclusive-development/>). The budget minister called it the blueprint for the national statistical system. [Its text, dated June 2024, puts the five-year cost at US$875 million](<https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/174/download/1324/NSDS_III_2025_REPORT.pdf>). Governments at federal, state and local level are to fund 51%. [In his 2026 message the Statistician-General said the Bureau would implement its reforms that year](<https://nigerianstat.gov.ng/page/sg-message>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists NSDS 2024-2028 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on NSDS III has been found since its [launch in December 2025](<https://nannews.ng/2025/12/15/fg-unveils-strategic-data-initiatives-for-evidence-based-governance-inclusive-development/>).

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of NSDS III has been found. [The statistics bureau says NSDS II ran from 2017 to 2021](<https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/174>), after which a national committee recommended a successor.
