# Eritrea

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the Statistical Master Plan 2023-2026 as the strategy being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [Its September 2024 table had listed only the master plan for 2010-14, as expired](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>). [In July 2026 UNICEF named an updated Statistical Master Plan among efforts to close gaps in data on children](<https://www.unicef.org/executiveboard/media/40026/file/2026-PL15-Eritrea-CPD-EN-ODS.pdf>). [In January 2007 a Norad review said the statistics office had no overall plan for its work](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>). It called such a master plan increasingly important.

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on the 2023-2026 plan has been found. [Its period ends in 2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the 2023-2026 plan has been found. [In September 2024 PARIS21 listed no successor planned to the 2010-14 plan](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Master Plan for economic statistics](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found dates a review of progress on the Statistical Master Plan 2023-2026.
