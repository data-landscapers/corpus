# Mauritania

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In 2021 Mauritania set out a ten-year national statistical development strategy, SNDDS 2021-2030](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>). [It carries an action plan for 2021-2025](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDDS 2021-2023](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Evaluated.** [In 2021 the new strategy set out a strategic evaluation and an operational review of the previous strategy, SNDS 2016-2020](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>).
