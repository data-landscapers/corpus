# Guinea

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [Guinea's INS says SNDS 2021-2025 is the country's third national statistics development strategy, after 2008-2013 (extended 2015) and 2016-2020](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/index.php/actu/382-atelier-de-restitution-et-de-validation-du-rapport-de-mise-a-jour-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-snds-2021-2025>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): SNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [The National Statistics Council, meeting on 10 August 2018, adopted the second national statistics development strategy, SNDS 2016-2020, costed at 444.2 billion GNF in its action plan (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/Textes/Rapport_1ere_Session_CNS.pdf>).
