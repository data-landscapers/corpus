# Uganda

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [UBOS launched the Fourth Plan for National Statistical Development (PNSD IV) in November 2026, during Africa Statistics Week](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/387592/ubos-unveils-new-data-strategy-to-support-ugandas-tenfold-economic-growth-ambition>). It is aligned with the Fourth National Development Plan, and no period is given for it. [The UBOS Board chairman said coordination was strengthened through the Plan for National Statistics Development (PNSD)](<https://www.finance.go.ug/media-center/news-and-updates/finance-minister-calls-stronger-evidence-based-statistics-support>). The plan is aligned with the National Development Plan. [UBOS handed over 4,050 tablets and six computers under the Fourth Plan for National Statistical Development (PNSD IV)](<https://chimpreports.com/ubos-donates-4050-census-tablets-to-strengthen-national-data-systems/>). The plan is therefore in operation. [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists a strategy as being designed or awaiting adoption: PNSD (successor to PNSD 2020-2025)](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).
