# Egypt

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In June 2024 the planning ministry said a national strategy for the statistical system had been in preparation for nearly two years](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=5528&lang=en>). Eighteen ministries and government and research bodies took part, and a first draft was expected the next month. [PARIS21's September 2024 table lists the NSDS 2017-2021 as expired and the next one as being finalised](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on a strategy has been found since 2023. [The ministry's June 2024 statement](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=5528&lang=en>) concerned the strategy's drafting.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the NSDS 2017-2021 has been found. [PARIS21 lists it as expired](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>).
