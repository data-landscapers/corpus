# Mali

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [On 19 November 2024 the national statistics council launched the drafting of the fourth Schéma Directeur de la Statistique](<https://www.instat-mali.org/fr/actualites-et-evenements/quatrieme-generation-du-schema-directeur-de-la-statistique-du-mali>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists the Schéma Directeur 2026-2030 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [In February 2026 INSTAT, the statistics institute, called SDS IV the roadmap for the national statistical system](<https://instat-mali.org/fr/actualites-et-evenements/session-ordinaire-du-conseil-national-de-la-statistique-cns->). [On 9 October 2026 the Council of Ministers took note of SDS IV and its 2026-2030 action plan](<https://icimali.com/mali-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-vendredi-9-octobre-2026-cm-n2026-40-sgg46239-2/>). Earlier plans were adopted in 2006, 2014 and 2020.

**Progress reviewed.** [In February 2026 a commission of the national statistics council validated the 2025 action-plan scorecard](<https://instat-mali.org/fr/actualites-et-evenements/session-ordinaire-du-conseil-national-de-la-statistique-cns->). It also updated the action-plan path for the whole of SDS IV. [INSTAT held the 17th technical session of the plan's annual review in Bamako on 13-14 August 2026](<https://instat-mali.org/fr/actualites-et-evenements/dix-septieme-session-technique-de-la-revue-annuelle-du-schema-directeur-de-la-statistique>). It assessed statistical activities and the follow-up of the 2025 review.

**Evaluated.** [On 6-7 February 2025 two commissions of the national statistics council validated the final evaluation report of the SDS 2020-2024](<https://www.instat-mali.org/fr/actualites-et-evenements/atelier-de-validation-du-rapport-devaluation-du-3eme-schema-directeur-de-la-statistique-sds-2020-2024>). [The 2020-2024 plan itself drew lessons from an evaluation of the 2015-2019 plan](<https://instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/sdsmli20-24_ca.pdf>).
