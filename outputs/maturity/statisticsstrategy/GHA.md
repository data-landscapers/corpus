# Ghana

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [On 18 March 2026 the Ghana Statistical Service held a workshop with chief directors and heads of public institutions to validate NSDS III for 2026-2030](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alhassan-iddrisu-464638394_nsdsiii-ghanadata-evidencebasedgovernance-activity-7440052863508201473-XCxg>). It rests on three pillars: integration, interoperability and data use. [The 2026 budget gave GH¢207 million to rebasing GDP and the consumer price index, in line with the new strategy](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/ghana-to-reset-economic-compass-in-2026-gh%c2%a2207-million-allocated-to-rebase-gdp-and-cpi/>). [PARIS21's January 2026 status table listed a successor to NSDS 2018-2022 as being designed or awaiting adoption](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on a current strategy has been found since November 2023. [The March 2026 workshop](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alhassan-iddrisu-464638394_nsdsiii-ghanadata-evidencebasedgovernance-activity-7440052863508201473-XCxg>) validated NSDS III before its period began.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of a strategy completed since November 2023 has been found. [NSDS 2018-2022, the last strategy PARIS21 names](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>), ended in 2022.
