# Ethiopia

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [On 1 February 2024 the planning ministry unveiled the Ethiopian Statistics Development Program, to run for three years](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/population-housing-census-to-be-conducted-in-forthcoming-triennium-ministry/>). It includes economic, agricultural and population and housing censuses. [It was put at about ETB 35 billion, running until 2026](<https://thereportermagazines.com/2586/>). [PARIS21's September 2024 table gives its period as 2023/24-2025/26](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>). [The same table lists the next strategy as being designed, with funding secured](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>). [PARIS21's January 2026 table lists an NSDS 2021-2026 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on the programme has been found since 2023. [PARIS21's January 2026 table](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>) gives its status only.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation of the current programme has been found. [In July 2016 NSDS II said NSDS I (2010/11-2014/15) had a mid-term and a final evaluation](<https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/Ethiopia-NSDS.pdf>). PARIS21, AfDB and ECA took part in them.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found dates a review of progress on the strategy in the last three years.
