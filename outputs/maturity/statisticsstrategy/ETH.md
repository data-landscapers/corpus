## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): NSDS 2021-2026](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [The Reporter says the Ministry of Planning and Development and the Ethiopian Statistics Service launched the Ethiopian Statistical Development Program, costing about ETB 35 billion and running until 2026](<https://thereportermagazines.com/2586/>). [The Ministry of Planning and Development unveiled the Ethiopian Statistics Development Program on 1 February 2024, to be executed over three years and including economic, agricultural and population and housing censuses](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/population-housing-census-to-be-conducted-in-forthcoming-triennium-ministry/>).
