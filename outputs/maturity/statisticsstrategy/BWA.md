# Botswana

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [Statistics Botswana and the University of Botswana launched the second Botswana Strategy for Development of Statistics (BSDS 2026-2030), a five-year national roadmap for the statistical system](<https://www.ub.bw/news/ub-joins-statistics-botswana-launch-second-bsds-2026-2030-strategy>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): BSDS 2026-2030](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [PARIS21 states Botswana validated, endorsed and launched its second Botswana Strategy for the Development of Statistics (BSDS II, covering 12 sectors) in October 2025](<https://www.paris21.org/news/botswana-launches-its-second-nsds>). [The first BSDS (BSDS I) covered 2015-2020 and was extended from April 2020 to March 2023, so it has ended (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>).
