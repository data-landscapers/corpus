# Tanzania

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The Tanzania Statistical Master Plan Phase Two (TSMP II) has been under implementation since 2022/23 and will end in June 2027](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/index.php/events/kamati-ya-uongozi-mof-yapitia-utekelezaji-mradi-wa-tsmp-ii-kupitisha-bajeti-mwaka-wa-fedha-2026-2027>). [PARIS21's January 2026 NSDS status table lists the strategy as implementing (adopted): Tanzania Statistical Master Plan 2022-2027](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>). [NBS and OCGS are implementing the second phase of the Tanzania Statistical Master Plan (TSMP II, 2022/23 - 2026/27), described as the operational roadmap for the National Statistical System](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1761214907-Assessment%20Report%20on%20Gaps%20and%20Opportunities%20using%20Big%20Data%20in%20Tanzania.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** [The fourth meeting of the TSMP II Steering Committee, chaired by the Ministry of Finance Permanent Secretary, met on 15 July 2026 and reviewed implementation for 2025/26, reporting 88.9% implementation since 2022/23, and approved the 2026/27 work plan and budget](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/index.php/events/kamati-ya-uongozi-mof-yapitia-utekelezaji-mradi-wa-tsmp-ii-kupitisha-bajeti-mwaka-wa-fedha-2026-2027>).

**Evaluated.** [TSMP I (2011/12 to 2017/18) was reviewed in 2019 and that its evaluation reports informed the design of TSMP II; the evaluation covers TSMP I, the predecessor strategy, and no TSMP II evaluation is reported as done (mid-term and end-term evaluations are only planned) (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1734514885-TSMP_II_Strategy.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Zanzibar Strategy for the Development of Statistics (ZSDS)](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1734514885-TSMP_II_Strategy.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found reports an evaluation of the strategy completed in the last three years.
