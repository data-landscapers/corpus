# Guinea-Bissau

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [In 2023 the statistics institute posted a draft strategic plan for the national statistical system over 2023-2028](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/IHPC/2023/Plano%20estrategico%202023-2028.pdf>). It sets a mission, values and three objectives, with no costing. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists ENDE 2021-2024 as the strategy being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>), though its period ended in 2024. [The ENDE 2018-2020 was validated at a workshop in October 2018](<https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf>), after drafting that began in April 2018.

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on a strategy has been found since November 2023. [PARIS21's September 2024 table](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2025-03/nsds-summary-table-2024.pdf>) listed ENDE 2021-2024 as being implemented.

**Evaluated.** No evaluation since November 2023 has been found. [The first strategy, ENDE-1 for 2015-2017, was evaluated in late 2017](<https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-10/Guine-Bissau_ENDE_Resumo_PT_print_final.pdf>), with an execution rate of 24.13%.
