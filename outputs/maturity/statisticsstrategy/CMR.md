# Cameroon

## Statistics strategy

**A strategy adopted or in preparation.** [The statistics institute says Cameroon adopted its third statistics strategy, SNDS30 for 2021-2030, in 2021](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/the-financing-of-the-snds30-in-question-in-yaounde/>). Its first action plan, for 2021-2025, was costed at about FCFA 63 billion and put to partners in April 2022. [In June 2024 the strategy's financing plan set a budget of FCFA 166.5 billion for 2024-2028](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-le-gouvernement-veut-mobiliser-166-milliards-de-fcfa-sur-05-ans-pour-developper-son-activite-statistique>). The state was to give FCFA 68.9 billion and the World Bank 63.3 billion, leaving a gap of 34.1 billion. [PARIS21's January 2026 status table lists SNDS 2021-2030 as being implemented](<https://www.paris21.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-02/nsds_summary_table_2026.pdf>).

**Progress reviewed.** No review of progress on SNDS30 has been found since November 2023. [In February 2026 the steering committee of a regional statistics project met to support the strategy's implementation](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/75463/en.html/statistiques-le-cameroun-mise-sur-la-qualite>).

**Evaluated.** [The statistics institute said in June 2023 that the strategy was evaluated in 2022](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/2024-2026-statistical-program-the-snis-validates/>). No evaluation completed since November 2023 has been found.
