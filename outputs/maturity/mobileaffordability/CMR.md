# Cameroon

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Cameroon. In 2025 it cost [5.72 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 9.29 per cent in 2022, 7.49 per cent in 2023 and 7.36 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 3.57 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 0.72 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person, divided by the 12 months of the year. The figure says nothing of what the poorest households pay for data against what they earn.
