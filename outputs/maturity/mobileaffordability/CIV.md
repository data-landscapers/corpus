# Cote d'Ivoire

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [On 11 November 2026 Tech Tools Afrique read Moov's tariffs](<https://tech-toolsafrique.com/meilleur-forfait-internet-cote-divoire/>): a 30-day plan of 7.4 GB cost FCFA 4,900. At that price a month of data comes to 3.51 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's income figure for 2025. [In October 2026 MTN's 30-day plan of 7.4 GB cost FCFA 5,000](<https://tech-toolsafrique.com/meilleur-forfait-internet-cote-divoire/>), or 3.58 per cent. The International Telecommunication Union's figures [put the cost at 3.02 per cent in 2024 and 3.04 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is within 5 per cent and 1.51 points above 2 per cent, the international target. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
