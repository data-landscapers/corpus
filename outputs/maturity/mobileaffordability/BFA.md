# Burkina Faso

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In 2025, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Burkina Faso cost [8.8 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>), on the International Telecommunication Union's price figures. The same figures [put the cost at 19.88 per cent in 2022, 19.62 per cent in 2023 and 11.1 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 11.08 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 3.8 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it. [Internet Society figures put a mobile data package of at least 1 GB in Burkina Faso at 0.2 per cent of average income in 2025, which brought to 5 GB is 1 per cent](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/05/local-infrastructure-lower-costs-how-peering-is-moving-the-needle-on-internet-affordability/>). [Internet Society figures put a mobile data package of at least 1 GB in Burkina Faso at 13 per cent of average income in 2020, which brought to 5 GB is 65 per cent](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/05/local-infrastructure-lower-costs-how-peering-is-moving-the-needle-on-internet-affordability/>).
