# Lesotho

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Lesotho. In 2025 it cost [8.35 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 9.13 per cent in 2022, 8.35 per cent in 2023 and 9.37 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 0.78 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 3.35 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest found for 2024 or later, whoever published it.

[The RIA Africa Mobile Pricing (RAMP) Index ranked Lesotho 22nd of 49 countries on 2016 data](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf>). [Econet Telecom Lesotho's Hoa Khonahala monthly bundle gives 4GB for M165, valid 1 month](<https://www.etl.co.ls/files/Hoa%20Khonahala%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf>). [It was promotional from October 2025 to February 2026](<https://www.etl.co.ls/files/Hoa%20Khonahala%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf>). [The Hoa Khonahala daily bundle is M10 for data or voice, valid 1 day, and promotional](<https://www.etl.co.ls/files/Hoa%20Khonahala%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf>). [The weekly bundle is M25 for data or voice, also stated as M20, valid 7 days and promotional](<https://www.etl.co.ls/files/Hoa%20Khonahala%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.pdf>).
