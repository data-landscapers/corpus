# Sudan

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [RT Media reported on 9 November 2026 that MTN Sudan had raised its 5 GB bundle to SDG 16,500](<https://rtmediasd.net/2026/11/09/%d8%aa%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%86/>). [On 29 August 2026 MTN's tariff page priced its monthly 5 GB bundle at SDG 12,500](<https://www.mtn.sd/bundle-type/20>). At the new price a month of data comes to 7.89 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's income figure for 2025. [Sudani's 5 GB bundle rose to SDG 25,000](<https://rtmediasd.net/2026/11/09/%d8%aa%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%82%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d9%86/>), the same report says. The operators blamed the dollar exchange rate and the cost of diesel for their towers. The International Telecommunication Union's figures [put the cost at 6.66 per cent in 2024 and 6.62 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12.
