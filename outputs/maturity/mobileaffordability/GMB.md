# Gambia

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [In January 2026 Comium, Africell and QCell charged D65 for 1 GB of standard data](<https://www.askanwi.com/news/after-askanwi-spotlight-all-telecoms-fall-in-line-with-d75-data-cap>), Askanwi reported. [Gamcel cut its price for 1 GB from D130 to D60](<https://www.askanwi.com/news/after-askanwi-spotlight-all-telecoms-fall-in-line-with-d75-data-cap>), under the regulator's ceiling of D75 a gigabyte. At D65 a gigabyte, 5 GB for a month comes to 5.99 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025. [The International Telecommunication Union put the cost at 8.68 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [Its figures were 22.02 per cent in 2022, 21.02 in 2023 and 10.26 in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost has fallen by more than two thirds. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. It says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
