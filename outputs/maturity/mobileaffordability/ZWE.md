# Zimbabwe

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Potraz announced a mobile data tariff of ZiG 92.8 a gigabyte in April 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>), as the Herald reported. At that price 5 GB for a month [comes to 5.87 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>) in 2024. [NetOne's monthly package of 1.5 GB cost ZiG 138 in April 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>). The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator at 50.28 per cent in 2022 and 12.92 per cent in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
