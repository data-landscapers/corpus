# Zimbabwe

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Potraz announced a mobile data tariff of ZiG 92.8 a gigabyte in April 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>), as the Herald reported. At that price 5 GB for a month [comes to 5.87 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>) in 2024. [NetOne's monthly package of 1.5 GB cost ZiG 138 in April 2024](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/debate-over-new-data-prices/>). The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator at 50.28 per cent in 2022 and 12.92 per cent in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it.
