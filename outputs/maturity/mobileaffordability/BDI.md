# Burundi

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Burundi. In 2025 it cost [25.18 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 25.24 per cent in 2022, 24.93 per cent in 2023 and 20.66 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 0.06 points. A month of data costs more than a tenth of a month's income, and is 15.18 points above 10 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
