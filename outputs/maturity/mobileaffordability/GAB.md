# Gabon

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Moov Africa Gabon Telecom's tariff page, captured in June 2026](<https://www.moov-africa.ga/particulier/internet/Internet-Mobile-4G/Forfaits/Pages/Pass_Waz.aspx>), lists a 30-day Pass Waz of 9 GB for FCFA 10,000. The data are released at 300 MB a day. At that price a month of data comes to 2.56 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's income figure for 2025. The International Telecommunication Union's figures [put the cost at 2.62 per cent in 2024 and 2.65 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is within 5 per cent and 0.56 points above 2 per cent, the international target. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
