# Malawi

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In July 2025 Macra, the regulator, said [one gigabyte of mobile data cost $0.38, down from $27.41 in 2020](<https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/>). On the International Telecommunication Union's price figures, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Malawi cost [8.45 per cent of average monthly income in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 23.6 per cent in 2022, 24.15 per cent in 2023 and 9.46 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost on those figures fell by 15.15 points. The regulator's figure is a price per gigabyte and the international figure is a share of income. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figures say nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
