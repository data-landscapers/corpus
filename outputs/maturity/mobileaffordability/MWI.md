# Malawi

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In 2025, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Malawi cost [8.45 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>), on the International Telecommunication Union's price figures. The same figures [put the cost at 23.6 per cent in 2022, 24.15 per cent in 2023 and 9.46 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 15.15 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 3.45 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it.

***Noted, not assessed***. [2027 cost of data target: around $2.30 (MACRA target, no basket stated)](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2023/1004/Documents/Daud%20Elvin%20Suleman.pdf>); [surtax on internet services: 10%](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A>); [VAT on smartphones: 17.5%](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-targets-smartphone-tax-relief/mQwkoq6Y9DpM3r9A>).

***Not held***. No figure of 5 per cent or less is held.
