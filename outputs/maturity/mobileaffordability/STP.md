# Sao Tome and Principe

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Sao Tome and Principe. In 2025 it cost [4.35 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 7.67 per cent in 2022, 6.97 per cent in 2023 and 5.49 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 3.32 points. The cost is within 5 per cent and 2.35 points above 2 per cent, the international target. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest found for 2024 or later, whoever published it.
