# Benin

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [In August 2026 the monthly 5,000 CFA franc bundles of MTN, Moov Africa and Celtiis gave over 11 GB](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/08/06/hausse-des-tarifs-data-au-benin-la-reponse-de-larcep-qui-pose-encore-question/>), La Marina reported. The regulator, ARCEP, said they stayed in the catalogues after the unlimited versions went on 4 August. At that price a month of data comes to 6.29 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025. [In June 2025 the regulator's observatory listed a 30-day Moov bundle of 5,120 MB at the same price](<https://arcep.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/2025_T2_-Tarifs-Internet.pdf>). [The International Telecommunication Union put the cost at 6.89 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [Its figures were 9.19 per cent in 2022, 8.93 in 2023 and 7.77 in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [The regulator said offers had reached 995 CFA francs a gigabyte in 2023](<https://fraternite.bj/internet-au-benin-acces-qualite-et-cout-ou-en-sommes-nous/>), from about 4,000 in 2016. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. It says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
