# Uganda

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [On 13 October 2026 the Uganda Communications Commission stated the average retail price of 1 GB from licensed operators at UGX 2,000](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/press-release-on-illegal-wi-fi-hotspots/>). [The price includes taxes and all costs](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/press-release-on-illegal-wi-fi-hotspots/>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 2.77 per cent of average monthly income in 2026. [A news report of the regulator's June 2026 figures works out about UGX 2,930 a gigabyte](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandans-spend-on-data-telecom-budget-shift/>). [The Commission's 2026 study on telecom taxes put it at about UGX 5,000](<https://hiascope.com/article/home-internet-still-a-luxury-for-most-ugandans-data-shows>). [The East African Communications Organisation's report, published in August 2026, puts mobile data at about USD 1.40 a gigabyte](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost of a month of data at 10.88 per cent in 2022 and 7.09 per cent in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [They put it at 6.43 per cent in 2024 and 6.07 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.

***Also on file***. [The combined tax on imported mobile data is 54%](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/ict-ministry-communication-stakeholders-call-NV_238288_082026>). [It is made up of VAT of 18%, withholding of 6%, excise of 12% and an 18% levy](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/ict-ministry-communication-stakeholders-call-NV_238288_082026>). [Internet Society Pulse says the average package cost fell over 70% since the ISOC Foundation grant](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/05/local-infrastructure-lower-costs-how-peering-is-moving-the-needle-on-internet-affordability/>). Another record is the [National IP Peering Exchange (NIPX)](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/nita-u-ministry-ict-national-guidance-announce-launch-national-ip-peering-exchange>). [The GSMA estimates the cheapest usable smartphone at about USD 39, or 39% of monthly GDP per capita](<https://pctechmag.com/2026/08/can-device-financing-solve-the-problem-of-smartphone-penetration/>). [The handset import tax stack is 10% import duty, 6% withholding tax and a 1.5% infrastructure levy](<https://pctechmag.com/2026/08/can-device-financing-solve-the-problem-of-smartphone-penetration/>). [The smartphone tax burden is 40%, from import duty and VAT under the Excise Duty Act](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). [Excise duty on internet data is 12%, with a proposed cut to 5%](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/ucc-proposes-major-telecom-tax-cuts-to-boost-ugandas-digital-economy/>). [VAT on mobile data is 18%, with a proposed cut to 14%](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/ucc-proposes-major-telecom-tax-cuts-to-boost-ugandas-digital-economy/>).
