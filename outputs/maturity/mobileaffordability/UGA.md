# Uganda

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [On 13 October 2026 the Uganda Communications Commission stated the average retail price of 1 GB from licensed operators at UGX 2,000, with taxes and all costs met](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/press-release-on-illegal-wi-fi-hotspots/>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 2.77 per cent of average monthly income in 2026. [A news report of the regulator's June 2026 figures works out about UGX 2,930 a gigabyte](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandans-spend-on-data-telecom-budget-shift/>), and [the East African Communications Organisation's report, published in August 2026, puts mobile data at about USD 1.40 a gigabyte](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost of a month of data at 10.88 per cent in 2022, 7.09 per cent in 2023, 6.43 per cent in 2024 and 6.07 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
