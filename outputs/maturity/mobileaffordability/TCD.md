# Chad

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [In June 2026 the government said a 5 GB data package cost 11.8 per cent of average monthly income](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>), Russell Southwood reported. [The International Telecommunication Union put the cost at 11.81 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [Its figures were 32.16 per cent in 2022, 32.69 in 2023 and 16.75 in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost has fallen by almost two thirds since 2022. [In December 2021 the government told Airtel and Moov to cut all internet offers by 30 per cent](<https://presidence.td/internetla-reduction-du-prix-actee-par-le-pcmt/12154/>). A month of data still costs more than a tenth of a month's income. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. It says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.

***Also on file***. [In 2020 internet cost a minimum-wage earner 17.8 per cent of salary](<https://presidence.td/internetla-reduction-du-prix-actee-par-le-pcmt/12154/>), against a target of 8 to 2 per cent. [The 30 per cent cut ordered in December 2021 reached 60 to 78 per cent on some offers](<https://presidence.td/internetla-reduction-du-prix-actee-par-le-pcmt/12154/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 10 per cent or less has been found.
