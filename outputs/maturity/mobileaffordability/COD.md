# DR Congo

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [The regulator's 2026 tariff page lists 30-day bundles of USD 10 for 10 GB from Orange and Airtel](<https://www.arptc-solution.cd/license_list/tarifview/1>). [Vodacom gives 13 GB and Africell 16 GB for the same price](<https://www.arptc-solution.cd/license_list/tarifview/1>). These are the cheapest 30-day plans of 5 GB or more. At USD 10, a month of data comes to 16.67 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's figure for 2025. [The International Telecommunication Union put the cost at 18.75 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [Its figures were 20.69 per cent in 2022, 20.34 in 2023 and 18.18 in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). A month of data still costs more than a tenth of a month's income. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. It says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
