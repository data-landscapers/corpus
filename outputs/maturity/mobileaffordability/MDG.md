# Madagascar

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In 2025, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Madagascar cost [12.49 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>), on the International Telecommunication Union's price figures. The same figures [put the cost at 21.76 per cent in 2022, 20.89 per cent in 2023 and 13.08 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 9.27 points. A month of data costs more than a tenth of a month's income, and is 2.49 points above 10 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it. [Ecofin, citing the ITU, reports monthly spending on mobile internet in Madagascar at 6.28% of gross national income per capita in 2023; the document does not state the basket size](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2410-49797-madagascar-presses-telecoms-to-slash-high-mobile-data-prices>).
