# Congo

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In 2025, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Congo cost [5.32 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>), on the International Telecommunication Union's price figures. The same figures [put the cost at 19.49 per cent in 2022, 10.82 per cent in 2023 and 6.67 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 14.17 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 0.32 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it. [The Congolese regulator's report for 2024 gives a weighted average mobile internet tariff of 0.70 CFA francs a megabyte, about 700 a gigabyte, across both operators](<https://arpce.org/api/publications/rapport-du-marche-linternet-mobile-2024/download>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 3.21 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025.

***Also on file***. [Weighted 4G/LTE mobile internet tariff: 2.15 FCFA per MB in 2024 (ARPCE weighted 4G tariff)](<https://arpce.org/api/publications/rapport-du-marche-linternet-mobile-2024/download>).

***Not found***. No figure of 5 per cent or less has been found.
