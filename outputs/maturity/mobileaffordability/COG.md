# Congo

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Congo. In 2025 it cost [5.32 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 19.49 per cent in 2022, 10.82 per cent in 2023 and 6.67 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 14.17 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 0.32 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. [The Congolese regulator's report for 2024 gives a weighted average mobile internet tariff of 0.70 CFA francs a megabyte, about 700 a gigabyte, across both operators](<https://arpce.org/api/publications/rapport-du-marche-linternet-mobile-2024/download>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 3.21 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025.

***Also on file***. [Weighted 4G/LTE mobile internet tariff: 2.15 FCFA per MB in 2024 (ARPCE weighted 4G tariff)](<https://arpce.org/api/publications/rapport-du-marche-linternet-mobile-2024/download>).

***Not found***. No figure of 5 per cent or less has been found.
