# Tanzania

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Tanzania's regulator reports the average bundle tariff across operators at 2.08 shillings a megabyte, about 2,080 a gigabyte, for the quarter to September 2025](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/Communication%20statistics%20report%20for%20quarter%20one%20ending%20September%202025_1760792820.pdf>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 3.81 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025. It is the newest figure held, and sets the stage. The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost at 5.53 per cent in 2022, 5.33 per cent in 2023, 5.08 per cent in 2024 and 4.79 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is within 5 per cent and 1.81 points above 2 per cent, the international target. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it.
