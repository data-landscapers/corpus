# Togo

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** The International Telecommunication Union's price figures cover the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in Togo. In 2025 it cost [9.46 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The same figures [put the cost at 13.59 per cent in 2022, 10.45 per cent in 2023 and 9.72 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2022 the cost fell by 4.13 points. The cost is within 10 per cent and 4.46 points above 5 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. [ARCEP Togo's comparative analysis gives an average price per gigabyte of 325 FCFA for Togo Cellulaire in the top-range (bundles over 5,000 FCFA) range; this is an average per-gigabyte price across bundles, not a single plan](<https://arcep.tg/la-baisse-des-forfaits-de-linternet-mobile-se-poursuit-au-togo/>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 2.42 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025.
