# Eritrea

## The price of mobile data against income

***Also on file***. [The ITU fixed broadband basket is 35% of GNI per capita](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/ind/D-IND-ICT_MDD-2023-PDF-E.pdf>). [The figure is stated only in a bracketed capture note](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/ind/D-IND-ICT_MDD-2023-PDF-E.pdf>). [A mobile prepaid SIM registration costs 550 NKF, plus 150 NKF for a Regional Administration letter](<https://eritel.com.er/apply_our_services.php?id=1037>). [The fixed dial-up internet tariff is 316 NKF per month](<https://www.eritel.com.er/faqs.php>). [Fixed ADSL line rent runs from 475 NKF (128 Mbps) to 3,062 NKF (2048 Mbps)](<https://www.eritel.com.er/faqs.php>). [Mobile prepaid recharge cards come in 110 NKF and 330 NKF denominations](<https://www.eritel.com.er/faqs.php>).
