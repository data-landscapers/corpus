# Guinea

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [On 4 September 2025 Orange Guinée enlarged its monthly pass of GNF 50,000 from 4 GB to 6 GB](<https://www.guinee360.com/12/09/2025/guinee-hausse-du-pass-internet-les-utilisateurs-partages/>), at an unchanged price, Guinee360 reported. At that price a month of data comes to 3.86 per cent of average monthly income, on the World Bank's income figure for 2025. The International Telecommunication Union's figures [put the cost at 5.85 per cent in 2023, 5.27 per cent in 2024 and 5.16 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is within 5 per cent and 1.86 points above 2 per cent, the international target. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.
