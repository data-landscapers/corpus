# Libya

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Almadar Aljadid's cheapest monthly plan of 5 GB or more is 6 GB, valid 30 days, for 20 Libyan dinars, on its tariff page as at 3 September 2026](<https://almadar.ly/ar/Pages/Tawasul/Services/Netpackages.aspx>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 0.68 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025. It is the newest figure found, and sets the stage. The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost at 1.07 per cent in 2022 and 0.74 per cent in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [They put it at 0.34 per cent in 2024 and 0.39 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). At 1 per cent or less, the cost is half the international target of 2 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest found for 2024 or later, whoever published it.
