# Central African Republic

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [The International Telecommunication Union is cited as putting 5 GB of mobile data at about 53.7 per cent of average monthly income in the Central African Republic in 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1703-136713-starlink-en-centrafrique-entre-couverture-nationale-et-defis-d-accessibilite>). It is the newest figure held, and sets the stage. The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost at 59.41 per cent in 2022, 68.01 per cent in 2023, 62.29 per cent in 2024 and 53.67 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). A month of data costs more than a tenth of a month's income, and is 43.7 points above 10 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it.
