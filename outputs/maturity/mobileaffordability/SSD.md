# South Sudan

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** In 2025, the cheapest 30-day plan of at least 5 GB from the largest operator in South Sudan cost [24.23 per cent of average monthly income](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>), on the International Telecommunication Union's price figures. The same figures [put the cost at 81.49 per cent in 2023 and 33.05 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). Since 2023 the cost fell by 57.26 points. A month of data costs more than a tenth of a month's income, and is 14.23 points above 10 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12, so the figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn. The stage rests on that one figure, the newest held for 2024 or later, whoever published it. [A newspaper editorial of November 2025 said 1 GB of mobile data in South Sudan costs about USD 23.70, naming no operator, plan or source](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/11/27/41522/>). No income figure has been found in that currency, so it is not set against income.
