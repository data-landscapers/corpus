# Angola

## The price of mobile data against income

**Cost of a month of mobile data against income, with its year.** [Africell Angola's cheapest 30-day prepaid plan of 5 GB or more is 7 GB for 5,000 kwanza, on its tariff page as at 23 October 2025](<https://www.africell.ao/afrinet/>). At that price 5 GB for a month comes to 1.89 per cent of average monthly income. That uses the World Bank's figure for income per person in 2025. It is the newest figure found. The International Telecommunication Union's price figures [put the cost at 4.49 per cent in 2022 and 4.32 per cent in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). [They put it at 3.91 per cent in 2024 and 3.05 per cent in 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Pages/ICTprices/default.aspx>). The cost is within 2 per cent and 0.89 points above 1 per cent. Average monthly income is gross national income per person divided by 12. The figure says nothing of what the poorest pay against what they earn.

***Also on file***. [INACOM's low-use basket with data (70 min + 20 SMS + 500 MB) cost 1.56% of GDP per capita in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>). [It cost 1.80% in 2022 and 1.73% in 2023](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>). [INACOM's low-use voice basket (70 min + 20 SMS) cost 1.07% of GDP per capita in 2024, against 1.80% in 2022](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>). [The Afrinet 30-day plan gives 4 GB for Kz 3000](<https://www.africell.ao/afrinet/>). [The 7-day plan gives 5 GB for Kz 2000](<https://www.africell.ao/afrinet/>). [The 1-day plans give 600 MB to 1.5 GB for Kz 200 to 800, valid 24 h](<https://www.africell.ao/afrinet/>). [The 60-day plans give 25 GB for Kz 15,000 and 50 GB for Kz 25,000](<https://www.africell.ao/afrinet/>).
