# Tanzania

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Education's Permanent Secretary directed institutions under the ministry on 13 July 2026 to fast-track integration of their digital platforms into the Education Sector Management Information System (ESMIS), so enrolment and other statistics need no manual requests](<https://dailynews.co.tz/schools-told-to-align-courses-with-market-needs/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/schools-told-to-align-courses-with-market-needs/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/schools-told-to-align-courses-with-market-needs/>).
