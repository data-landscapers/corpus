# Tanzania

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Teachers Service Commission said in August 2024 that its teacher information system, TSCMIS, is connected to the School Information System, the civil service HR system and the Treasury pensions system to exchange teacher data](<https://www.tsc.go.tz/news/tsc-imefanikiwa-kuunganisha-mfumo-wa-tscmis-na-mifumo-mingine-mitatu>). [On 13 July 2026 the Permanent Secretary told institutions under the ministry to speed up linking their platforms to the Education Sector Management Information System](<https://dailynews.co.tz/schools-told-to-align-courses-with-market-needs/>), so enrolment figures need no manual requests.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. [The commission's link carries teacher records, attendance and lessons taught](<https://www.tsc.go.tz/news/tsc-imefanikiwa-kuunganisha-mfumo-wa-tscmis-na-mifumo-mingine-mitatu>), not a learner number.

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. [The links reported are for teachers' records, with HR and pensions](<https://www.tsc.go.tz/news/tsc-imefanikiwa-kuunganisha-mfumo-wa-tscmis-na-mifumo-mingine-mitatu>).
