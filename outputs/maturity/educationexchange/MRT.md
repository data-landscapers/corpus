# Mauritania

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** No source found says education systems exchanging data is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The education ministry launched a service on the Houwiyati platform to extract and confirm national certificates, available for certificates issued after 2013 or already linked to the holder's national number (NNI) by the ministry; the AMI report gives no figures](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276019>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276019>).
