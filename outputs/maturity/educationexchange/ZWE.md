# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In April 2026 UNICEF sought a consultant to build a web-based school registration system for the education ministry](<https://jobs.unicef.org/en-us/job/592432/development-of-an-information-management-system-for-school-registration-at-the-ministry-of-primary-and-secondary-education-harare-zimbabve-5-months-nationals-only-home-based>). [The system is to be integrated with the EMIS](<https://jobs.unicef.org/en-us/job/592432/development-of-an-information-management-system-for-school-registration-at-the-ministry-of-primary-and-secondary-education-harare-zimbabve-5-months-nationals-only-home-based>). [In June 2026 the ministry's spokesperson said an Early Warning System is now part of the EMIS](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [It generates real-time lists of absent learners](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [In 2019 an ADEA peer review found no formal structures for sharing data among education stakeholders](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/zimbabwe_education_management_information_systems_peer_review_report.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the Early Warning System's lists of absent learners, described in June 2026](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. [The spokesperson said the tracing of absent learners runs under the National Case Management System](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [It runs alongside the Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>).
