# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The ministry spokesperson says the Early Warning System is now integrated into EMIS and runs under the National Case Management System alongside the Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare, generating real-time lists of absent learners](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [A UNICEF consultancy notice seeks a consultant to design, develop and deploy a web-based school registration Information Management System for the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education, including EMIS integration](<https://jobs.unicef.org/en-us/job/592432/development-of-an-information-management-system-for-school-registration-at-the-ministry-of-primary-and-secondary-education-harare-zimbabve-5-months-nationals-only-home-based>). [The ADEA peer review of September 2019 found no formal structures for data sharing among key education stakeholders in Zimbabwe, and recommended a data reporting and sharing platform (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/zimbabwe_education_management_information_systems_peer_review_report.pdf>).
