# Guinea

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Minister of Higher Education announced the Système Intégré de Gestion des Universités (SIGU) as a centralised digital tool interconnected with other national platforms, named as ANSAE, the National Education Ministry's Porte Educ platform and SIGOU](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>). [In March 2021 exam candidate lists passed between central level, IRE, DPE and schools by agents' personal email in Excel files](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>). [Teacher matricules were not shared automatically](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>). [Personnel data passed between the Budget and Fonction publique ministries on encrypted USB keys](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** [In September 2025 the minister said each 2025 bachelier gets a unique identifier for the whole university career](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>). [A single file holds CENOU matricule numbers](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>). [It serves enrolment, bursaries, housing, social aid and allowance payments](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [in September 2025 SIGU was interconnected with ANSAE, Porte Educ and SIGOU](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>).
