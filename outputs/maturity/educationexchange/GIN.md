# Guinea

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Minister of Higher Education announced the Système Intégré de Gestion des Universités (SIGU) as a centralised digital tool interconnected with other national platforms, named as ANSAE, the National Education Ministry's Porte Educ platform and SIGOU](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>). [Exam candidate lists pass between central level, IRE, DPE and schools by agents' personal email in Excel files, and that teacher matricules are not shared automatically between the actors; personnel data pass between the Budget and Fonction publique ministries semi-automatically on encrypted USB keys (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** [The Minister said each 2025 bachelier receives a unique identifier kept through the whole university career, and the CENOU director general said the single file with CENOU matricule numbers serves enrolment, bursaries, housing, social aid, SAMA Money allowance payment and tracking on CENOU KIBARU](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/>).
