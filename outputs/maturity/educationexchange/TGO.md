# Togo

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The implementing researchers and Ministry staff state Togo's ministry has developed an integrated education data system, StatEduc with DHIS2, connecting the Department of Planning, the Examinations Department and the General Inspectorate of Education, improving data sharing](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>). [The World Bank mission noted a non-appropriation of the project of interconnectivity of platforms and databases for more efficient education system management, under the basic education project P172674](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>). [The DHIS2 implementation profile says the ministry is piloting DHIS2 alongside StatEduc, with aggregate integration with StatEduc; the page is a living profile dated 2024-11-05 and gives its own start as 2019 at national scale](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>).
