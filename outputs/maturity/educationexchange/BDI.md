# Burundi

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [From 29 July to 2 August 2024 the ministry and UNESCO held a workshop on a human resources module for the central SIGE](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>). [The module is to work with the STATEDUC application and serve teacher recruitment, deployment and posting](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>). [The project was agreed in December 2022 and runs to 14 February 2026](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>). [The Panel says the ministry of education is developing a human resources module to be integrated in the education management information system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact: [the module planned in 2024 covers teachers, not learners](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [in August 2024 the human resources module was planned inside the education ministry's SIGE](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>).
