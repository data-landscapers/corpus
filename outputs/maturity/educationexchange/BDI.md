# Burundi

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The ministry and UNESCO held a workshop on 29 July to 2 August 2024 for the project, agreed in December 2022 and running 35 months to 14 February 2026, to build a human resources module integrated into the central SIGE and compatible with the STATEDUC application, for teacher recruitment, deployment and posting; activities were planned over 12 months and user profiles validated](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>). [The Panel says the ministry of education is developing a human resources module to be integrated in the education management information system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>).
