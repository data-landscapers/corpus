# Somalia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The High School Certificate System connects the MOECHE Examination System registry so exam results are retrieved automatically and verified digital certificates are issued online, per GovStack's showcase page](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>). [The World Bank letter of amendment, countersigned by Somalia's Finance Minister on 2 October 2025, finances expansion of the Teacher Management Information System and its integration with the EMIS under project part 1.3](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100225092557188/pdf/P172434-c7c7f078-1bc2-439f-a6a6-aa0c69ca52b6.pdf>). [The World Bank restructuring paper reports EMIS enhancement with integration of an enhanced Teacher Management Information System and an offline teacher attendance module, terms of reference approved and delivery planned for April 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099093025010039338/pdf/P172434-b9fc93cd-c0c2-4708-bb04-f2fb8990be32.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [The HSC verifies students' identity through the eAqoonsi/NIRA national ID as one of its integrated building blocks, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** [The HSC verifies students' identity through the eAqoonsi/NIRA national ID as one of its integrated building blocks, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>).
