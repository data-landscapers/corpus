# Somalia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The High School Certificate System connects the MOECHE Examination System registry, per GovStack's showcase page](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>). [Exam results are retrieved automatically and verified digital certificates are issued online](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>). [Somalia's Finance Minister countersigned the World Bank letter of amendment on 2 October 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100225092557188/pdf/P172434-c7c7f078-1bc2-439f-a6a6-aa0c69ca52b6.pdf>). [It finances expansion of the Teacher Management Information System and its integration with the EMIS under project part 1.3](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100225092557188/pdf/P172434-c7c7f078-1bc2-439f-a6a6-aa0c69ca52b6.pdf>). [The World Bank restructuring paper reports EMIS enhancement with integration of an enhanced Teacher Management Information System and an offline teacher attendance module](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099093025010039338/pdf/P172434-b9fc93cd-c0c2-4708-bb04-f2fb8990be32.pdf>). [Terms of reference were approved and delivery was planned for April 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099093025010039338/pdf/P172434-b9fc93cd-c0c2-4708-bb04-f2fb8990be32.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact found: [The HSC verifies students' identity through the eAqoonsi/NIRA national ID as one of its integrated building blocks, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** [The HSC verifies students' identity through the eAqoonsi/NIRA national ID as one of its integrated building blocks, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>).
