# Mali

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** On 5 June 2025 the PASCOFI project, run by EUMC and funded by Global Affairs Canada, [called for a consultant to strengthen the ministry's education management system so that JIGITUGU can be integrated with it](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>). JIGITUGU collects pupils' and teachers' attendance and pupils' results in real time. The work includes [a module to synchronise the ministry's education portal with JIGITUGU, by API or webhook](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>), and computers for the planning unit and the basic education directorate.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact is the [planned link between JIGITUGU and the education portal](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>), which names no identifier.

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact is the same [2025 call](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>), which concerns education systems only.
