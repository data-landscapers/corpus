# Gambia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the education and health ministries signed an MoU to link the EMIS with the HMIS](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [A joint technical working group oversees it](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [The link is planned, not yet running](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [In October 2022 a DHIS2 for Education use case found separate databases for HR, examinations and infrastructure](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>). [None brought them together](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the EMIS-HMIS link was planned, not yet running](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the education and health ministries agreed to link the EMIS with the HMIS](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).
