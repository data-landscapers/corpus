# Gambia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education and the Ministry of Health signed an MoU to link the EMIS with the HMIS, with a joint technical working group to oversee implementation; the story states the linkage is planned, not yet running](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>). [The University of Oslo DHIS2 for Education country use case says separate databases for HR, examinations and infrastructure exist, with no comprehensive database bringing different data sources together for cross-analysis and planning (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).
