# Sierra Leone

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [MBSSE has launched an initiative, funded by GPE with UNICEF supporting, to build a DHIS2-based national education data hub whose automated interoperability layer will connect the annual school census, teacher management system, learning assessments, school quality data and examination results, rolling out across all 16 districts](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [Sierra Leone is integrating multiple data systems into a national data warehouse; the systems are not named](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>). [The annual school census, teacher management records, school quality assurance data, learning assessments and examination results are collected and maintained in separate systems, with no reliable way to track a learner across them](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).
