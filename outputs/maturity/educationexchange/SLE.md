# Sierra Leone

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In April 2026 MBSSE launched work, funded by GPE with UNICEF support, on a DHIS2-based national education data hub](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [Its interoperability layer will connect the annual school census, teacher management, learning assessments, school quality data and examination results](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [It is rolling out across all 16 districts](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [Sierra Leone is integrating multiple data systems into a national data warehouse; the systems are not named](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>). [The annual school census, teacher management records, school quality assurance data, learning assessments and examination results are collected and maintained in separate systems, with no reliable way to track a learner across them](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 there was no reliable way to track a learner across the separate education systems](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [the data hub will connect education systems only](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).
