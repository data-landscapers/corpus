# South Sudan

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The ERICC working paper states that REMIS, the UNHCR refugee education data system, relies on data from the Ministry's EMIS to incorporate refugee hosting areas and compare quality indicators between refugee and host communities, and that only the Ministry receives REMIS enrolment and attendance data](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).
