# South Sudan

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In September 2025 an ERICC working paper said REMIS, UNHCR's refugee education data system, relies on data from the ministry's EMIS](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>). [REMIS uses it to cover refugee hosting areas and compare quality indicators between refugee and host communities](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>). [Only the ministry receives REMIS enrolment and attendance data](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 REMIS passed enrolment data to the ministry](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 REMIS drew on EMIS data for host communities](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).
