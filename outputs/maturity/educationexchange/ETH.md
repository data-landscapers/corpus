# Ethiopia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank Program Paper proposes US$69m additional financing, including US$750,000 for EMIS enhancement, to integrate all 57 refugee primary schools, which currently operate outside the Ministry of Education's EMIS, and their learners into the national EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>). [The World Bank deal record for ETOL additional financing (wb-eth-004) states the grants integrate all 57 refugee primary schools and their learners into the national EMIS, with US$750,000 earmarked for EMIS enhancement and disaggregated reporting, the schools currently being outside the Ministry's EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>).
