# Ethiopia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [From November 2025 a UNICEF-backed project has worked to connect local school, woreda and zone systems to the national DHIS2-Ed EMIS](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>). [It covers the SER, SWEPR, Oromia and Benishangul Gumuz regions, HISP Ethiopia reports](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>). [In March 2026 the World Bank proposed US$750,000 for EMIS enhancement](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>). [The money would bring all 57 refugee primary schools and their learners into the national EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>). [Those schools operate outside the ministry's EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank's plan to bring refugee learners into the national EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the regional project connecting local data systems to DHIS2-Ed](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>).
