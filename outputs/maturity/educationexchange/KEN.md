# Kenya

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [A Ministry of Education circular says the KEMIS Learner Validation Engine is operational and automatically compares learner records against official examination and assessment databases, marking learners Valid or Invalid](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>). [Nairobi Wire reports a State House meeting of the Head of Public Service with the Public Service Commission, the Kenya National Examinations Council and the Ministry of Education to integrate the systems that authenticate academic qualifications and link credential databases directly with hiring agencies, with no technological framework or timeline disclosed](<https://nairobiwire.com/2026/09/kenya-academic-certificate-verification-fake-certificates.html>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).
