# Kenya

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** The Principal Secretary for basic education said KEMIS would [go live on 9 January 2026 and integrate basic education, higher education and TVET with the examinations council and the Teachers Service Commission](<https://educationnews.co.ke/government-to-roll-out-kemis-on-january-9-2026-to-track-learners-nationwide/>). [A Ministry of Education circular says the KEMIS Learner Validation Engine is operational](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>) and compares learner records with the official examination and assessment databases, marking each learner valid or invalid. In September 2026 [a State House meeting with the Public Service Commission, the examinations council and the ministry](<https://nairobiwire.com/2026/09/kenya-academic-certificate-verification-fake-certificates.html>) agreed to integrate the systems that authenticate academic certificates, with no timeline given.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).
