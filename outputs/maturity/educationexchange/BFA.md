# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [La Tribune du Faso, citing the digital transition ministry, reports that on 25 June 2025 the digital transition and basic education ministers reviewed progress on the national pupil file, online enrolment and school identity cards, built with WURI support to track pupils from primary to university, with progressive enrolment due from the next school year](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). [The ministry's February 2024 report on the pilot phase of the national pupil file, online enrolment and school identity cards (FIUE-BF) records a pilot of the unique pupil identification file; the passages read give only the contents list, with no figures](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).
