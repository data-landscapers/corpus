# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [On 25 June 2025 the digital transition and basic education ministers reviewed progress on the national pupil file, online enrolment and school identity cards, La Tribune du Faso reports](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). [Built with WURI support, the file is to track pupils from primary to university](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). [Enrolment was to start progressively the next school year](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). [In February 2024 a ministry report recorded a pilot of the unique pupil identification file, FIUE-BF](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 the national pupil file was to track pupils from primary to university](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 the pupil file was built with WURI support](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).
