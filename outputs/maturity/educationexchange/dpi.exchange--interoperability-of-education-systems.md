# Interoperability of education systems: interoperability of education systems

*As at 2026-11-02.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Education Strategy asks for an individual, identifier-based education management information system linked to the national ID and other sectors. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the last three years says it is absent |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |
| 4 One learner ID | Yes to 3 |

## Countries

### Nigeria: 5 Leading

In 2026 the ministry said learner identification numbers were integrated with national identification numbers through the education data system.

- Biometric Update reports that the Ministry of Education and NIMC integrated the Learner's Identification Number of 80 million students with the NIN registry via the digital NEMIS, citing an announcement on X; 1.9 million LINs had been issued since April ([source, 2026-08-05](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/nigeria-integrates-national-student-ids-digital-identity-system)).
- Education Minister Alausa said the Ministry has integrated the National Learners' Identification Number (LIN) with the National Identification Number (NIN) through the Nigeria Education Management Information System (NEMIS) ([source, 2026-07-10](https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-to-extend-trusted-digital-identity-to-80-million-learners-as-education-ministry-nimc-deepen-partnership/)).

### Central African Republic: 3 Established

In 2026 the teacher records system was linked to the payroll, covering 98 percent of teachers; no shared learner identifier has been found.

- The World Bank ICR says the HRMIS built under the Education Sector Plan Support Project is now linked to the payroll, with 98.01 percent of teachers on payroll recorded in it with a certificate of presence (target 95 percent), and is used under the CARE project for teacher recruitment and deployment, while also saying it was developed, validated and piloted and that nationwide routine use is to be consolidated under CARE; the date is the document's published date, as it gives no event date ([source, 2026-06-15](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf)).

### Kenya: 3 Established

Since 2026 learner records are checked against the examination databases; no learner identifier shared across systems has been found.

- A Ministry of Education circular says the KEMIS Learner Validation Engine is operational and automatically compares learner records against official examination and assessment databases, marking learners Valid or Invalid ([source, 2026-08-17](https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/)).

### Somalia: 3 Established

Somalia's certificate system drew examination results from the exam registry in 2026; one learner identifier across education systems is not known.

- The High School Certificate System connects the MOECHE Examination System registry so exam results are retrieved automatically and verified digital certificates are issued online, per GovStack's showcase page; as_of is the publication date, the document gives none ([source, 2026-05-29](https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/)).

### South Africa: 3 Established

Since at least 2025 the national learner register has taken learner records from provincial school systems; no identifier shared across systems has been found.

- The Department of Basic Education says LURITS consolidates learner information submitted by schools via provincial school administration systems and provincial data warehouses ([source, 2025-12-19](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35150/)).

### South Sudan: 3 Established

In 2025 the refugee education data system drew on the ministry's records and fed enrolment data back; no shared learner identifier has been found.

- The ERICC working paper states that REMIS, the UNHCR refugee education data system, relies on data from the Ministry's EMIS to incorporate refugee hosting areas and compare quality indicators between refugee and host communities, and that only the Ministry receives REMIS enrolment and attendance data; no date is given, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2025-09-23](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664)).

### Tanzania: 3 Established

The teachers' commission's system exchanged teacher data with the school information system in 2024; no learner identifier shared across systems is known.

- The Teachers Service Commission's IT head says its teacher information system TSCMIS is now connected to e-Watumishi (HR), the Treasury pensioners payment system and the School Information System, to exchange teacher data ([source, 2024-08-29](https://www.tsc.go.tz/news/tsc-imefanikiwa-kuunganisha-mfumo-wa-tscmis-na-mifumo-mingine-mitatu)).

### Togo: 3 Established

In 2025 the ministry's education data system connected planning, examinations and the inspectorate; no shared learner identifier has been found.

- The implementing researchers and Ministry staff state Togo's ministry has developed an integrated education data system, StatEduc with DHIS2, connecting the Department of Planning, the Examinations Department and the General Inspectorate of Education, improving data sharing; as_of is the page's published year, the document gives no event date ([source, 2025](https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data)).

### Angola: 2 Nascent

In 2025 the contractor said the technical-vocational education system was designed to link with the ministry's records; no link in service has been found.

- LBC, the contractor, says the SIETP technical-vocational education system was designed for integration with SIGE, Angola's Education Information and Management System; no go-live of the link is stated ([source, 2025-05-09](https://lbc-global.com/en/retfop-um-ponto-de-viragem-no-ensino-tecnico-profissional-angolano/)).

### Burkina Faso: 2 Nascent

A national pupil file was piloted in 2024 and reviewed by ministers in 2025; no link in service has been found.

- La Tribune du Faso, citing the digital transition ministry, reports that on 25 June 2025 the digital transition and basic education ministers reviewed progress on the national pupil file, online enrolment and school identity cards, built with WURI support to track pupils from primary to university, with progressive enrolment due from the next school year ([source, 2025-06-25](https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538)).
- The ministry's February 2024 report on the pilot phase of the national pupil file, online enrolment and school identity cards (FIUE-BF) records a pilot of the unique pupil identification file; the passages read give only the contents list, with no figures ([source, 2024-02](https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf)).

### Burundi: 2 Nascent

A human resources module for the education data system was being built in 2024; no link in service has been found.

- The ministry and UNESCO held a workshop on 29 July to 2 August 2024 for the project, agreed in December 2022 and running 35 months to 14 February 2026, to build a human resources module integrated into the central SIGE and compatible with the STATEDUC application, for teacher recruitment, deployment and posting; activities were planned over 12 months and user profiles validated ([source, 2024-08](https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/)).
- The Panel says the ministry of education is developing a human resources module to be integrated in the education management information system; no completion is stated ([source, 2023-12-19](https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf)).

### Cameroon: 2 Nascent

A 2026 agreement with UNESCO includes interconnecting the education databases; no link in service has been found.

- UNESCO and Cameroon's PAEBC project unit signed an agreement on 2026-07-31 for 8 million euros of technical assistance over 36 months that includes updating the MINEDUB WebSIG platform and interconnecting the education databases ([source, 2026-07-31](https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base)).

### Ethiopia: 2 Nascent

From 2025 a project works to connect local school data systems in four regions to the national system; no link in service has been found.

- HISP Ethiopia and the HISP Centre report that from November 2025 a UNICEF-backed project in the SER, SWEPR, Oromia and Benishangul Gumuz regions works to connect local school, woreda and zone data systems to the Ministry's national DHIS2-Ed EMIS enterprise application ([source, 2025-11](https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/)).

### Gambia: 2 Nascent

In 2026 the education and health ministries agreed to link their data systems; no link in service has been found.

- In July 2026 the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education and the Ministry of Health signed an MoU to link the EMIS with the HMIS, with a joint technical working group to oversee implementation; the story states the linkage is planned, not yet running ([source, 2026-07](https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/)).

### Ghana: 2 Nascent

A 2024 ministry dashboard interface takes data from agencies; no source found says the link is in service.

- The Ministry of Education's PMDV Dashboard API documentation lists endpoints serving EMIS school statistics, enrolment rates, schools and NST test scores, and a POST endpoint that takes data from agencies by agency code into MongoDB, with development and production base URLs; it does not say which systems feed it or that it is in service ([source, 2024](https://pmdv.moedatadashboard.com/api_documentation)).

### Guinea: 2 Nascent

Guinea announced a university management system linked to other education platforms in 2025; two education systems exchanging data in service is not known.

- The Minister of Higher Education announced the Système Intégré de Gestion des Universités (SIGU) as a centralised digital tool interconnected with other national platforms, named as ANSAE, the National Education Ministry's Porte Educ platform and SIGOU ([source, 2025-09-09](https://guineejet.info/lancement-de-latelier-national-dimmatriculation-des-bacheliers-de-2025/)).

### Malawi: 2 Nascent

An education data portal joining ministry and partner datasets was being built in 2026; no link in service has been found.

- The Unlocking Data Initiative Malawi team is developing an Education Data Portal (EDaP) to bring together datasets from the education ministry (MoEST, which runs the EMIS), the gender and social welfare ministry, the National Statistics Office, development partners and academia, with hosting to pass to CERT at the University of Malawi ([source, 2026-07-06](https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/)).

### Mali: 2 Nascent

In 2025 a donor project began linking a school attendance and results tool to the ministry's education system; whether the link works is not known.

- EUMC's call for expressions of interest of 5 June 2025 seeks a consultant to build a module syncing the JIGITUGU attendance and performance solution with the ministry's education portal (API or webhook) ([source, 2025-06-05](https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/)).

### Rwanda: 2 Nascent

In 2026 the ministry and partners worked on linking its student and school data systems; no link in service has been found.

- HISP Rwanda reports a technical assistance mission with MINEDUC and partners focused on strengthening integration across SDMS, SEMIS and partner platforms, alongside the DHIS2-based student absence tracking system ([source, 2026-02-11](https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/)).

### Sierra Leone: 2 Nascent

In 2026 the ministry began building a hub to connect its separate education data systems; no link in service has been found.

- MBSSE has launched an initiative, funded by GPE with UNICEF supporting, to build a DHIS2-based national education data hub whose automated interoperability layer will connect the annual school census, teacher management system, learning assessments, school quality data and examination results, rolling out across all 16 districts (date is the document's published date, as it gives none) ([source, 2026-04-07](https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/)).
- The document says Sierra Leone is integrating multiple data systems into a national data warehouse; the systems are not named ([source, 2025-02](https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning)).

### Uganda: 2 Nascent

Exam results and a health link run through an education data platform in pilot sites in 2026; the national system lacks an exam-board link.

- The brief says examination data (the 2025 Primary Leaving Examination results from the UNEB portal) was integrated into DHIS2-Ed, which also collates the Termly School Report and other datasets, as used by the Makindye Sabagabo municipal education department ([source, 2025](https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf)).
- The brief says the DHIS2-Ed system was linked to the Ministry of Health's electronic Integrated Disease Surveillance Reporting (eIDSR) platform so both ministries see school-based surveillance indicators; no date given, so the published date of 2026-04-23 is used ([source, 2026-04-23](https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf)).
- The Assistant Commissioner said the ministries and UNEB are working to resolve the EMIS-UNEB integration gap so the affected students can be uploaded ([source, 2026-06-09](https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html)).
