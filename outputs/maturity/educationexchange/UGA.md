# Uganda

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Assistant Commissioner said the ministries and UNEB are working to resolve the EMIS-UNEB integration gap so the affected students can be uploaded](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>). [EMIS lacks integration with the Uganda National Examinations Board system, so details of all Senior One and Senior Five students are not yet fully captured, as acknowledged by the Ministry's Assistant Commissioner for Statistics](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>). [The APHRC KIX-SEEDS issue brief says Uganda introduced the School Report Card integrated with DHIS2 for Education, and that sustainability depends on system-wide integration, so the link between school-level data and the national platform is not yet national](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [The DHIS2-Ed system was linked to the Ministry of Health's electronic Integrated Disease Surveillance Reporting (eIDSR) platform so both ministries see school-based surveillance indicators](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** [The DHIS2-Ed system was linked to the Ministry of Health's electronic Integrated Disease Surveillance Reporting (eIDSR) platform so both ministries see school-based surveillance indicators](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).
