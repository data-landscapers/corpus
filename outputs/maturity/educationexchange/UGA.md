# Uganda

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In June 2026 the ministry's statistics lead said the EMIS lacks integration with the national examinations board's system](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>). [Not all Senior One and Senior Five students are captured as a result](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>). [He said the ministries and the board are working to close the gap](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>). [A HISP Uganda brief of April 2026 says 2025 primary leaving results were brought into DHIS2 for Education](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [The brief shows that use in the Makindye Sabagabo municipal education department](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [In 2023 that platform ran in 10 of 175 administrative sites](<https://education.dhis2.org/routine-data-collection-uganda/>). [APHRC said in December 2025 that its link with school-level data was not yet national](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [APHRC said in December 2025 that Uganda had introduced a School Report Card integrated with DHIS2 for Education](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. [The April 2026 brief says DHIS2 for Education was linked to the Ministry of Health's eIDSR platform](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [Both ministries see school-based surveillance indicators through it](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).
