# Senegal

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** No source says education systems exchange data. [The ministry's GREEN guide says every pupil and teacher has a national education number](<https://senprof.education.sn/GUIDES%20ET%20DOCUMENTATIONS/GUIDE%20GREEN>). GREEN issues it at enrolment.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** [The IEN is deployed nationwide](<https://senprof.education.sn/GUIDES%20ET%20DOCUMENTATIONS/GUIDE%20GREEN>), the ministry says. No source names a second system that uses it.

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [every pupil and teacher has a national education number, issued by GREEN at enrolment](<https://senprof.education.sn/GUIDES%20ET%20DOCUMENTATIONS/GUIDE%20GREEN>).
