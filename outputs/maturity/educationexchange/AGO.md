# Angola

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [LBC, the contractor, says the SIETP technical-vocational education system was designed for integration with SIGE, Angola's Education Information and Management System](<https://lbc-global.com/en/retfop-um-ponto-de-viragem-no-ensino-tecnico-profissional-angolano/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lbc-global.com/en/retfop-um-ponto-de-viragem-no-ensino-tecnico-profissional-angolano/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lbc-global.com/en/retfop-um-ponto-de-viragem-no-ensino-tecnico-profissional-angolano/>).
