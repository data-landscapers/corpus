# Cameroon

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [UNESCO and Cameroon's PAEBC project unit signed an agreement on 2026-07-31 for 8 million euros of technical assistance over 36 months that includes updating the MINEDUB WebSIG platform and interconnecting the education databases](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>).
