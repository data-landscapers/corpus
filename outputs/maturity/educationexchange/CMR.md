# Cameroon

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [On 31 July 2026 UNESCO and Cameroon's PAEBC project unit signed a technical assistance agreement](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>). [It is worth 8 million euros over 36 months](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>). [It includes updating the MINEDUB WebSIG platform and interconnecting the education databases](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 UNESCO agreed to help update the MINEDUB WebSIG platform](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the agreement covered interconnecting the education databases](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-et-le-cameroun-signent-un-accord-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dappui-leducation-de-base>).
