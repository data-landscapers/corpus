# DR Congo

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In August 2026 the SERNIE director wrote that other education departments collect teacher and school-infrastructure data](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). [They collect it separately from SERNIE's learner records](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). [The director stated no link between them](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [in August 2026 SERNIE held the learner records](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [in August 2026 SERNIE's learner records were kept apart from teacher data](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

***Also on file***. [Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20260512/au-iped-supports-education-data-systems-self-assessment-drc>); [e-SIGE-DRC](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121724060523706/txt/P172341-54806169-ac97-4144-8f26-c20d06662f3c.txt>); [EDUSER](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>); [HereMIS](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a link between education systems is under way.
