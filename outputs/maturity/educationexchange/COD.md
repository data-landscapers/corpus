# DR Congo

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The SERNIE director writes that other education departments in the DRC collect teacher and school-infrastructure data separately from SERNIE's learner records, with no link between them stated](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).
