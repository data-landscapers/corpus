# Libya

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Education says it launched an electronic platform for private education institutions that shows parents a private school's data is registered with the private education administration and that it is linked to the National Centre for Examinations](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362098&lang=ar>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362098&lang=ar>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362098&lang=ar>).
