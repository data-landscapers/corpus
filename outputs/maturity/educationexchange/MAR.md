# Morocco

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** No source found since October 2023 names two education systems exchanging data. [The education ministry's circular has school heads and provincial offices register and check pupils' records in Massar](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>), which also holds each pupil's results and school history.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. [The ministry's circular gives each pupil a digital identifier in Massar](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>), with a single national learner register as the aim.

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** [The ministry's circular says Massar's online enrolment services for parents rely on digital identity and can download the pupil's data held in the civil status register](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>). Each pupil record carries the civil status number.

***Also on file***. [E-government interoperability platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 names two education systems exchanging data, or one learner identifier used across them.
