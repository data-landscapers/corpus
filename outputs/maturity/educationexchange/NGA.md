# Nigeria

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [Nigeria's Minister of Education said more than 250 universities, polytechnics, monotechnics and colleges of education are integrated with the Nigeria Education Repository and Data Bank (NERD) for real-time credential verification, after four months of enforcement](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-deploys-national-education-data-repository-to-end-certificate-fraud-strengthen-academic-records-integrity/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** [The Federal Government says it launched the nationwide Learner Identification Number, and that over 1.9 million candidates registered for the 2026 WAEC and NECO examinations have been issued one in the first phase, building on DNEMIS](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-first-ever-nationwide-learner-identification-number-to-transform-education-system/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** [Biometric Update reports that the Ministry of Education and NIMC integrated the Learner's Identification Number of 80 million students with the NIN registry via the digital NEMIS, citing an announcement on X; 1.9 million LINs had been issued since April](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/nigeria-integrates-national-student-ids-digital-identity-system>).
