# Rwanda

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** In February 2026 HISP Rwanda [worked with the education ministry on integrating SDMS, SEMIS and partner platforms](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>). The work sat beside a DHIS2-based system that tracks student absence. No source found says which education systems already exchange data.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact is the [2026 integration work on SDMS and SEMIS](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** In May 2026 the national ID agency said [the ministry was integrating education data systems with the Single Digital Identity](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>). It named harmonising the EMIS with the population register for children under 16 as a challenge. No source found says learner records are already linked.

***Also on file***. [SDMS](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>), [SEMIS](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>) and a [DHIS2-based student absence tracking system](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>). The [School Data Management System (SDMS)](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/REPORTS/Education_Statistics/Education_statistical_yearbook/Education_Statistical_Year_Book_2024-2025.pdf>) is in the 2024-2025 yearbook. The sector plan names an [integrated EMIS](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/PLANS/Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan/The_Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan__ESSP__2024-2029.pdf>) and the [School Data Management Information System (SDMIS)](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/PLANS/Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan/The_Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan__ESSP__2024-2029.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says two education systems exchange data in use.
