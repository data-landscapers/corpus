# Rwanda

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [HISP Rwanda reports a technical assistance mission with MINEDUC and partners focused on strengthening integration across SDMS, SEMIS and partner platforms, alongside the DHIS2-based student absence tracking system](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector.
