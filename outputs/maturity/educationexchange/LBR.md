# Liberia

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [An AU-IPED peer review held on 1-2 September 2025 found Liberia's EMIS has no integration with WAEC exam data](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>). [It found no integration with teacher service records either](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>). [The exams, curriculum and teacher databases are not linked](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the exams, curriculum and teacher databases were unlinked](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the EMIS was not integrated with teacher records](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>).
