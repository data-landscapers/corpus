# Malawi

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the Unlocking Data Initiative Malawi team was developing an Education Data Portal (EDaP)](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>). [It is to bring together datasets from the education ministry, MoEST, which runs the EMIS](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>). [Other datasets come from the gender and social welfare ministry, the National Statistics Office, development partners and academia](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>). [Hosting is to pass to CERT at the University of Malawi](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the portal was to draw on the ministry's EMIS](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the portal was to add welfare and statistics datasets](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>).
