# Malawi

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The Unlocking Data Initiative Malawi team is developing an Education Data Portal (EDaP) to bring together datasets from the education ministry (MoEST, which runs the EMIS), the gender and social welfare ministry, the National Statistics Office, development partners and academia, with hosting to pass to CERT at the University of Malawi](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/07/06/unlocking-malawis-foundational-learning-data-for-better-learning-outcomes/>).
