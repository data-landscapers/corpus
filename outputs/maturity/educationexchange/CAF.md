# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [In June 2026 a World Bank completion report said the HRMIS built under the Education Sector Plan Support Project is linked to the payroll](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>). [It records 98.01 percent of teachers on payroll with a certificate of presence, against a 95 percent target](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>). [The CARE project uses it for teacher recruitment and deployment](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>). [The report also says it was piloted, and nationwide routine use is to be consolidated under CARE](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 the HRMIS held teachers' records, not learners'](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 the teachers' HRMIS was linked to the payroll](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).
