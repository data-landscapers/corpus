# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank ICR says the HRMIS built under the Education Sector Plan Support Project is now linked to the payroll, with 98.01 percent of teachers on payroll recorded in it with a certificate of presence (target 95 percent), and is used under the CARE project for teacher recruitment and deployment, while also saying it was developed, validated and piloted and that nationwide routine use is to be consolidated under CARE](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>).
