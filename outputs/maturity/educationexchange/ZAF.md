# South Africa

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** [Parliament's Higher Education and Training committee was briefed by MICT SETA that the Integrated Learner Management System, meant to integrate the various post-school entities' systems into one national shared platform for real-time data sharing, is being developed and is now piloted in a couple of SETAs, though an IP dispute with Tshwane University of Technology and weak DHET buy-in could delay it](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/dispute-over-intellectual-property-could-derail-leaner-management-system>).

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/dispute-over-intellectual-property-could-derail-leaner-management-system>).

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/dispute-over-intellectual-property-could-derail-leaner-management-system>).
