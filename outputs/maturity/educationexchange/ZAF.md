# South Africa

## Interoperability of education systems

**Education systems exchanging data.** The Department of Basic Education told Parliament in December 2025 that [LURITS, its learner register, consolidates the learner records schools submit](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35150/>). Schools submit them through provincial school administration systems and provincial data warehouses. MICT SETA told Parliament's Higher Education and Training committee in August 2026 that [an Integrated Learner Management System is being piloted in a couple of SETAs](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/dispute-over-intellectual-property-could-derail-leaner-management-system>). It is meant to join the post-school bodies' systems in one shared platform. An intellectual property dispute with Tshwane University of Technology could delay it.

**One learner identifier across education systems.** No source found since October 2023 says one learner identifier is used across education systems. The nearest held fact is that [LURITS holds a record for each learner](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35150/>) drawn from the provincial systems.

**Linked to the national ID or another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another sector. The post-school platform [is planned for real-time data sharing among education bodies](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/news/dispute-over-intellectual-property-could-derail-leaner-management-system>), not with other sectors.
