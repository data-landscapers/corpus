# Sudan

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Sudan Customs, in a press release carried by SUNA, says its advance cargo declaration system (ACD) was tried successfully at Port Sudan's South Port and will be extended from early October 2026 to the other ports and crossings](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/09/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1>). [Sudan's finance ministry says its Isali e-collection system is integrated with the ASYCUDA customs procedures system](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>). [Sudan's Customs Authority says it completed integration of the ASYCUDA customs management system with the civil registry database via the Baladna platform, and that customs risk officers across checkpoints will use it to prevent forged national ID numbers in declarations](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/>). [SUNA reports the ASYCUDA system was reactivated at the Hantoub customs yard in Gezira state after war damage, with equipment and network links restored, ahead of the yard starting official customs work](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/aaaad-tshghyl-ntham-alasykoda-balhthyr-algmrky-bgmark-olay-algzyr-astaadada-lantlak-alaaml-algmrky>).
