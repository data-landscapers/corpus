# Sudan

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Sudan Customs says its advance cargo declaration system was tried successfully at Port Sudan's South Port](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/09/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1>), and [was to reach the other ports and crossings from October 2026](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/09/30/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%83-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1>). [The finance ministry's Isali e-collection system is integrated with ASYCUDA](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>). In October 2025 customs [completed linking ASYCUDA with the civil registry through Baladna](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/>). [Risk officers at checkpoints use it to stop forged ID numbers in declarations](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/>). [ASYCUDA was restored at the Hantoub customs yard in Gezira after war damage](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/aaaad-tshghyl-ntham-alasykoda-balhthyr-algmrky-bgmark-olay-algzyr-astaadada-lantlak-alaaml-algmrky>), SUNA reports.

**Single window.** In December 2025 the government portal said [the first export contract was completed fully electronically on the Baladna platform](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b0-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%a5%d9%84/>). [It links trade, customs, standards, ports, border crossings, tax, Zakat and health clearances as a single window for foreign trade](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b0-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%a5%d9%84/>).
