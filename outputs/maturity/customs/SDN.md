# Sudan

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Sudan's Customs Authority says it completed integration of the ASYCUDA customs management system with the civil registry database via the Baladna platform, and that customs risk officers across checkpoints will use it to prevent forged national ID numbers in declarations](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/>). [SUNA reports the ASYCUDA system was reactivated at the Hantoub customs yard in Gezira state after war damage, with equipment and network links restored, ahead of the yard starting official customs work](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/aaaad-tshghyl-ntham-alasykoda-balhthyr-algmrky-bgmark-olay-algzyr-astaadada-lantlak-alaaml-algmrky>).
