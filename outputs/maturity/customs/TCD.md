# Chad

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Chad's customs director general said Chad's customs uses SYDONIA World, which Chad and Cameroon plan to interconnect with Cameroon's CAMCIS](<https://www.theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7286/en/homefluidify>). [Customs says the SYDONIA World platform is used by Chad customs, and Cameroon's CAMCIS is to be made interoperable with it](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7061/en/hometighten-cemac-transit-cut>). [Chad's customs directorate says its SYDONIA World system processes declarations, with e-payment of all duties and taxes linked to it since 21 February 2024](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>). [Chad's customs directorate opened an evaluation workshop on 22 April 2025 for the SYDONIA World automated system, which it says was put in place two years earlier under the PAGESE project](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/04/22/tchad-le-systeme-sydonia-world-soumis-a-evaluation/>). [The Chad customs directorate (DGDDI) says SYDONIA World was officially put into service on 15 October 2022, replacing SYDONIA++ in use since 2001 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.td/douane/historique-des-douanes-tchadiennes/>).
