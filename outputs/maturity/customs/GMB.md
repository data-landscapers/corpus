# Gambia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Gambia Revenue Authority and Senegalese Customs agreed to install the GAINDE customs system at Karang, Keur Ayib and Selety border posts](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gra-senegalese-customs-agree-to-eliminate-transit-impediments-through-ict>). The work is to be done within three months. [The Gambia Revenue Authority states that the rollout of ASYCUDA World has automated and streamlined clearance procedures](<https://www.gra.gm/news/26fd4242-17b6-11f1-b086-029254d29bb1>). [Its Commissioner of Customs is in discussions with UNCTAD on upgrading the existing ASYCUDA system](<https://www.gra.gm/news/5cc30201-b025-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>). The upgrade would be to the new-generation cloud-based version. [The authority's Commissioner General said on 30 May 2025 that ASYCUDA moved from version 2.7 to ASYCUDA++](<https://www.gra.gm/news/57d06877-3d50-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>). It then moved to ASYCUDA World in 2022.

**Single window.** [The president of the clearing and forwarding agents association says the Gambia National Single Window has helped improve vessel turnaround and clearance time](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/govt-promises-duty-waiver-decision-in-72-hrs-with-new-digital-system>). [The Gambia Revenue Authority states that strides in implementing the National Single Window have improved inter-agency coordination](<https://www.gra.gm/news/26fd4242-17b6-11f1-b086-029254d29bb1>). It says they have reduced the time and cost of doing business. [The authority lists a National Single Window for trade documentation among the digital initiatives it has implemented](<https://www.gra.gm/news/57d06877-3d50-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>).
