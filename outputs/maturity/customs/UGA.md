# Uganda

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Uganda Revenue Authority's Q&A column says importers lodge declarations in its customs system](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>), where controlled HS codes need prior authorisation. Air-cargo manifest data entry is still manual. [The URA says it is in advanced stages of automating and integrating systems with airlines at Entebbe](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>). This is for electronic submission of cargo manifests before arrival.

**Single window.** [Uganda's WTO notification G/TFA/N/UGA/4 of 12 June 2024 reports 22 of a target 45 agencies actively operational on the e-Single Window](<https://www.tfadatabase.org/en/members/uganda/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>). It reports interfaces to commercial banks and online permits and certificates. [The Uganda Revenue Authority's UESW page says the Uganda Electronic Single Window has automated 22 of 30 border agencies](<https://ura.go.ug/en/uesw/>). These agencies can issue permits and certificates electronically.

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa reports that the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi are interconnected](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>). They connect through the East African Community Single Customs Territory Centralised Platform. Scanner image sharing was first implemented between Kenya and Uganda in 2024. [A KRA officer writing in WCO News says the EAC Single Customs Territory Centralized Platform was built for six partner states](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-106-issue-1-2025/kenya-uganda-exchanging-images-scanned-cargo/>). It has shared customs declarations and scanned-cargo images since July 2024. Uganda has sent over 8,000 images to Kenya.
