# Uganda

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Uganda Revenue Authority's Q&A column says importers lodge declarations in its customs system, where controlled HS codes need prior authorisation, while air-cargo manifest data entry is still manual](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>). [The URA says it is in advanced stages of automating and integrating systems with airlines at Entebbe for electronic submission of cargo manifests before arrival](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>).

**Single window.** [Uganda's WTO notification G/TFA/N/UGA/4 of 12 June 2024 reports 22 of a target 45 agencies actively operational on the e-Single Window, with interfaces to commercial banks and online permits and certificates](<https://www.tfadatabase.org/en/members/uganda/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>). [The Uganda Revenue Authority's UESW page says the Uganda Electronic Single Window has automated 22 of 30 border agencies, which can issue permits and certificates electronically](<https://ura.go.ug/en/uesw/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa reports that the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi are interconnected through the East African Community Single Customs Territory Centralised Platform, with scanner image sharing first implemented between Kenya and Uganda in 2024](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>). [A KRA officer writing in WCO News says the EAC Single Customs Territory Centralized Platform, built for six partner states, has shared customs declarations and scanned-cargo images since July 2024, with Uganda sending over 8,000 images to Kenya](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-106-issue-1-2025/kenya-uganda-exchanging-images-scanned-cargo/>).
