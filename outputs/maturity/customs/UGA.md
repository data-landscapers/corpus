# Uganda

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Uganda Revenue Authority says ASYCUDA World is an online system used for processing all goods imported or exported, in which agents capture, validate and assess declarations and importers self-declare and pay taxes](<https://ura.go.ug/en/sycuda/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ura.go.ug/en/sycuda/>).
