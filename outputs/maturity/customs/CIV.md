# Cote d'Ivoire

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [GUCE CI's terms of use say its e-DAU module lets customs agents enter and submit detailed customs declarations through the GUCE platform, partially used since 2016 because operators may also lodge declarations directly with SYDAM (2016, before the three-year window)](<https://guce.gouv.ci/cgu/?lang=en>). [Ivorian customs says SYDAM World moved in 2014 to version 2, based on ASYCUDA World, with electronic payment, data exchange and interoperability services (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.ci/info/positionnement-de-l-informatique>).

**Single window.** [GUCE CI's terms of use describe GUCE as a transactional single-window web portal where operators meet import, export and transit formalities, with customs clearance modules (e-DAU, e-Risk) alongside technical-ministry pre-clearance modules (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://guce.gouv.ci/cgu/?lang=en>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says it launched deployment of SIGMAT between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia on 31 October 2025, exchanging electronic transit messages through interconnection of their national customs systems; Liberia joins Guinea and Cote d'Ivoire on the platform](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>).
