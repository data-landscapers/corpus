# Cote d'Ivoire

## Customs

**Automated customs.** In July 2024 GUCE CI's director said [traders had for several years lodged customs declarations and paid duties electronically through its portal](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>). The platform [includes pre-clearance and customs clearance modules used by customs brokers, importers and exporters](<https://www.225.ci/cote-d-ivoire/actualite/387856-Amelioration-des-systemes-d'information-L'application-''Guce-mobile''-du-commerce-exterieur-officiellement-mise-sur-pied>). In December 2025 [Ivorian customs described its Douane Intelligente modernisation project, backed by Huawei, as being deployed](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/modernisation-douaniere>).

**Single window.** GUCE CI, the foreign trade single window, has been [in service since 2013](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>). Traders file customs formalities, licences and declarations through it for the administrations involved. In May 2025 the commerce ministry said [traders complete import, export and transit formalities entirely online through it](<https://www.commerce.gouv.ci/actualite/133>). It links operators, public administrations and banks. [A mobile application, Guce mobile, was launched on 25 July 2024](<https://www.225.ci/cote-d-ivoire/actualite/387856-Amelioration-des-systemes-d'information-L'application-''Guce-mobile''-du-commerce-exterieur-officiellement-mise-sur-pied>) for traders, customs brokers, technical ministries and banks.

**Exchange with partners.** ECOWAS says [Côte d'Ivoire's customs was connected to SIGMAT, the community's transit data exchange system, in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>). On 31 October 2025 ECOWAS [launched SIGMAT between Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>), linking their national customs systems. On 24 and 25 September 2026 [Ivorian and Malian customs teams reviewed SIGMAT in operation on the Abidjan to Bamako corridor](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/cooperation-bilaterale>).
