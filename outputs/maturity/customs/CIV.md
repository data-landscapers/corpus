# Cote d'Ivoire

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Ivorian customs describes its Douane Intelligente modernisation project, backed by Huawei, as being deployed, with a 5-14 December 2025 training mission to China](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/modernisation-douaniere>). [GUCE CI's director says traders have for several years submitted customs declarations and paid duties and taxes electronically through the GUCE transaction portal](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>). [Ivorian customs says SYDAM World moved in 2014 to version 2, based on ASYCUDA World, with electronic payment, data exchange and interoperability services (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.ci/info/positionnement-de-l-informatique>).

**Single window.** [The Ministry of Commerce says the GUCE-CI single window lets traders complete import, export and transit formalities entirely online and is the central digital interface between operators, public administrations and banks, with ten years of service](<https://www.commerce.gouv.ci/actualite/133>). [GUCE CI, the foreign-trade single window implemented in 2013, lets traders file customs formalities, licences and declarations electronically for the administrations involved](<https://www.gucecotedivoire.ci/innovation-et-dematerialisation-dans-lecosysteme-du-commerce-exterieur-guce-ci-lance-sa-nouvelle-application-guce-mobile-tant-attendue-par-les-operateurs-economiques-les-acteurs-majeurs-e/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [Douanes Ivoiriennes report that Cote d Ivoire and Mali teams met on 24-25 September 2026 to assess SIGMAT in its operating phase on the Abidjan-Bamako transit corridor, sharing information between the two administrations](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/cooperation-bilaterale>). [ECOWAS says it launched deployment of SIGMAT between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia on 31 October 2025, exchanging electronic transit messages through interconnection of their national customs systems; Liberia joins Guinea and Cote d'Ivoire on the platform](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>).
