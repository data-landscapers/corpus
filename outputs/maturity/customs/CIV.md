# Cote d'Ivoire

## Customs

**Automated customs.** Ivorian customs runs [SYDAM World, which moved in 2014 to a version based on ASYCUDA World with electronic payment, data exchange and interoperability services](<https://www.douanes.ci/info/positionnement-de-l-informatique>). [Since 2016 customs agents can also lodge detailed declarations through the e-DAU module of the GUCE platform, while operators may still lodge them directly in SYDAM](<https://guce.gouv.ci/cgu/?lang=en>).

**Single window.** [GUCE CI's terms of use, from 2022, describe a transactional single-window portal where operators meet import, export and transit formalities, with customs clearance modules beside the technical ministries' pre-clearance modules](<https://guce.gouv.ci/cgu/?lang=en>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS launched SIGMAT between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia in Monrovia on 31 October 2025, a system that exchanges electronic transit messages between the countries' national customs systems](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>). [Ivorian customs had piloted an interconnection with Ghana's customs in July 2017, with a single declaration crossing the border at the pilot site](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-des-douanes-ivoiriennes-et-ghaneennes-une-realite>).
