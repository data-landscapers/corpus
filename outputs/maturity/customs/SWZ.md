# Eswatini

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Eswatini Revenue Service says customs payments made through FNB Eswatini now show almost instantly on ASYCUDA, so traders can process their customs declarations more quickly](<https://independentnews.co.sz/44878/business/ers-fnb-complete-real-time-customs-payment-integration/>). [The Eswatini Revenue Service says customs payments through FNB now reflect in near real time in its ASYCUDA customs system, completing the integration on 12 June 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>). [Trade Minister Manqoba Khumalo says a National Single Window System is under development to digitise customs procedures, with border posts at Mananga, Ngwenya and Lavumisa being upgraded](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/eswatini-pushes-for-full-implementation-of-wto-trade-facilitation-agreement/>). [The Eswatini Revenue Service says in a 3 February 2025 presentation that customs declaration data is automatically exchanged with the South African Revenue Service, and that it built its electronic certificate of origin on existing ASYCUDA World infrastructure](<https://storage.unitedwebnetwork.com/files/1223/2d6dba669eee8645059d5cb734d75d90.pdf>). [Times of Eswatini, reading the Eswatini Revenue Service 2024/25 annual report, says ERS made continued upgrades to ASYCUDA World for customs operations during 2024/25, with system availability averaging 99.9 per cent](<https://times.co.sz/30612/business/inside-erss-e14-6bn-2024-25-revenue-collections/>). [The World Customs Organization says on 18 October 2024 that the Eswatini Revenue Service runs an ASYCUDA World Risk Management Module, to be upgraded in 2025](<https://www.wcoomd.org/en/media/newsroom/2024/october/wco-workshop-empowers-eswatini-revenue-service-to-advance-customs-risk-management.aspx>).
