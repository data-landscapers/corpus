# Egypt

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Nafeza is the electronic system governing sea and air freight](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). Advance cargo information has been mandatory at seaports since October 2021 and on air freight from January 2026. [The Finance Ministry has begun surveying road-freight firms to register and link them electronically to Nafeza](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>), per three officials. It starts at Nuweiba and Safaga, with consolidated bills of lading and pre-arrival cargo data for land freight. [Arqam reports Egypt's customs authority will run its automated classification and valuation system at all customs sites as a test environment](<https://arqam.news/487753/>). Use is mandatory from 1 October 2025, with a link to E-Finance's database.

**Single window.** [Nafeza, described as Egypt's national single window for foreign trade, is live in production with the Ain Sokhna truck yard system](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>). It supplies cargo clearance and release status, per Egytrans Nosco's Abir Leheta. [EnterpriseAM says Nafeza was designed as a single window for the approval process, instead of separate portals](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). Over 30k registered importers and about 80k foreign exporters file information on it. [Egypt's progress report to the WTO of 3 May 2024 says the Nafeza national single window is activated in most customs ports](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/egypt/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>). It is electronically linked to most of the border agencies concerned.

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country.
