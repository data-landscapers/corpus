# Egypt

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Nafeza is the electronic system governing sea and air freight, with advance cargo information mandatory at seaports since October 2021 and on air freight from January 2026](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). [The Finance Ministry has begun surveying road-freight firms to register and link them electronically to Nafeza, starting at Nuweiba and Safaga, with consolidated bills of lading and pre-arrival cargo data for land freight, per three officials](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>).

**Single window.** [Nafeza, described as Egypt's national single window for foreign trade, is live in production with the Ain Sokhna truck yard system and supplies cargo clearance and release status, per Egytrans Nosco's Abir Leheta](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Nafith truck-management yard system, Ain Sokhna](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>); [TIR system](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>).
