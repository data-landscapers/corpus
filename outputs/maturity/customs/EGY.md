# Egypt

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Nafeza is the electronic system governing sea and air freight, with advance cargo information mandatory at seaports since October 2021 and on air freight from January 2026](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). [The Finance Ministry has begun surveying road-freight firms to register and link them electronically to Nafeza, starting at Nuweiba and Safaga, with consolidated bills of lading and pre-arrival cargo data for land freight, per three officials](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). [Arqam reports Egypt's customs authority will run its automated classification and valuation system at all customs sites as a test environment, with mandatory use from 1 October 2025 and a link to E-Finance's database](<https://arqam.news/487753/>).

**Single window.** [Nafeza, described as Egypt's national single window for foreign trade, is live in production with the Ain Sokhna truck yard system and supplies cargo clearance and release status, per Egytrans Nosco's Abir Leheta](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>). [EnterpriseAM describes Nafeza as Egypt's national single window for foreign trade, designed as a single window for the approval process rather than separate portals, with over 30k registered importers and about 80k foreign exporters filing information](<https://enterpriseam.com/logistics/2026/09/14/egypts-govt-is-bringing-overland-freight-into-nafeza-for-the-first-time-closing-a-gap-in-trade-digitization/>). [Egypt's progress report to the WTO of 3 May 2024 says the Nafeza national single window is activated in most customs ports and electronically linked to most of the border agencies concerned](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/egypt/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/28/ain-sokhnas-new-truck-yard-is-already-linking-with-nafeza/>).
