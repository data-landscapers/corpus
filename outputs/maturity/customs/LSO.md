# Lesotho

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [In August 2026 the report of the National Single Window's launch said it is linked to ASYCUDA World for customs declarations](<https://swazi24.com/49844/business/trade-investment/lesotho-launches-digital-trade-platform/>). [In November 2025 CAFI managing director Chaba Mokuku said the government is working with South Africa to remodel border infrastructure](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). [The work includes digital immigration and customs management tools](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>).

**Single window.** [Lesotho launched its National Single Window on 24 August 2026](<https://swazi24.com/49844/business/trade-investment/lesotho-launches-digital-trade-platform/>). [Traders submit certificate, licence and permit applications there for imports, exports and transit](<https://swazi24.com/49844/business/trade-investment/lesotho-launches-digital-trade-platform/>). [Revenue Services Lesotho and CAFI said 12 other agencies and 88 processes are automated](<https://swazi24.com/49844/business/trade-investment/lesotho-launches-digital-trade-platform/>). [In September 2026 Mokuku wrote that Revenue Services Lesotho runs the window as one online entry point](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 Mokuku said the government is working with South Africa on digital customs tools at the border](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>).
