# Senegal

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Port Autonome de Dakar and the customs directorate announced a pilot from 2 January 2026 of electronic exchange of Bons à Délivrer via the electronic port single window](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/dedouanement-le-port-de-dakar-entame-une-etape-decisive-de-sa-transformation-digitale/>). [It followed a June 2025 data-exchange protocol with GAINDE 2000](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/dedouanement-le-port-de-dakar-entame-une-etape-decisive-de-sa-transformation-digitale/>). [In June 2024 Senegal's customs director general said the GAINDE system had come into force that year at Rosso, Ziguinchor, Sénoba and Kaolack](<https://bie.cciad.sn/2024/06/05/20-ans-de-orbus-lheure-de-chanter-les-merites-de-la-dematerialisation/>). [He said all clearance offices would follow within six months](<https://bie.cciad.sn/2024/06/05/20-ans-de-orbus-lheure-de-chanter-les-merites-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Single window.** [GIE GAINDE 2000 marked 20 years of ORBUS, the single window for foreign-trade formalities, run with customs and clearance actors in Senegal](<https://bie.cciad.sn/2024/06/05/20-ans-de-orbus-lheure-de-chanter-les-merites-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest fact: [in November 2025 the port and customs announced electronic exchange of delivery orders under a protocol with GAINDE 2000](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/dedouanement-le-port-de-dakar-entame-une-etape-decisive-de-sa-transformation-digitale/>).
