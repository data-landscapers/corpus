# Senegal

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Port Autonome de Dakar and the Direction générale des Douanes announced a pilot phase from 2 January 2026 of electronic exchange of Bons à Délivrer via the electronic port single window, following a June 2025 data-exchange protocol with GAINDE 2000](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/dedouanement-le-port-de-dakar-entame-une-etape-decisive-de-sa-transformation-digitale/>). [Senegal's customs director general said in June 2024 that the GAINDE system has been in force since that year at Rosso, Ziguinchor, Sénoba and Kaolack, with all clearance offices to follow within six months](<https://bie.cciad.sn/2024/06/05/20-ans-de-orbus-lheure-de-chanter-les-merites-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Single window.** [GIE GAINDE 2000 marked 20 years of ORBUS, the single window for foreign-trade formalities, run with customs and clearance actors in Senegal](<https://bie.cciad.sn/2024/06/05/20-ans-de-orbus-lheure-de-chanter-les-merites-de-la-dematerialisation/>).
