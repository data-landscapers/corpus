# Guinea-Bissau

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme reports ASYCUDAWorld operational across all computerised customs offices, including the Port of Bissau, Bissau International Airport and the Sao Domingos, Gabu and Bafata offices, after migration from ASYCUDA++](<https://asycuda.org/en/guinea-bissau-launches-asycudaworld-to-strengthen-customs-digitalization/>). [Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate reviewed with an African Development Bank mission on 2026-10-08 its migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World, which the mission head said had passed 80% execution, with the remaining applications and modules still to be installed](<https://alfandegas.mef.gw/dga-e-missao-tecnica-do-bad-avaliam-a-execucao-dos-projetos-de-modernizacao-e-formacao-aduaneira/>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://asycuda.org/en/guinea-bissau-launches-asycudaworld-to-strengthen-customs-digitalization/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://asycuda.org/en/guinea-bissau-launches-asycudaworld-to-strengthen-customs-digitalization/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
