# Guinea-Bissau

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Guinea-Bissau's customs directorate reviewed its migration from SYDONIA++ to SYDONIA World with an African Development Bank mission on 2026-10-08](<https://alfandegas.mef.gw/dga-e-missao-tecnica-do-bad-avaliam-a-execucao-dos-projetos-de-modernizacao-e-formacao-aduaneira/>). The mission head said the migration had passed 80% execution, with the remaining applications and modules still to be installed. [The customs administration says it officially launched an air electronic manifest module in SYDONIA World on 17 September 2026](<https://alfandegas.mef.gw/dga-lanca-oficialmente-o-modulo-de-manifesto-eletronico-via-aerea-no-sydonia-world/>). It was built with IATA to receive advance cargo information. [Service order GDGA 10/26 of the Direcao-Geral das Alfandegas says declarations are registered in SYDONIA World](<https://alfandegas.mef.gw/ordem-de-servico-gdga-10-26-modulo/>). Its ASYVal valuation module takes effect on 1 May 2026, and value alerts are resolved in the system before clearance. [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme reports ASYCUDAWorld operational across all computerised customs offices after migration from ASYCUDA++](<https://asycuda.org/en/guinea-bissau-launches-asycudaworld-to-strengthen-customs-digitalization/>). These include the Port of Bissau, Bissau International Airport and the Sao Domingos, Gabu and Bafata offices. [The customs directorate says Guinea-Bissau formally presented SYDONIA World, migrating from SYDONIA++, with training of customs staff under way](<https://alfandegas.mef.gw/guine-bissau-adota-sistema-sydonia-world-para-modernizar-controlo-aduaneiro/>).
