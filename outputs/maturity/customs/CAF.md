# Central African Republic

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Investir en Centrafrique reports that, once the DPI fee is paid via Orange Money, the loading-intention declaration is generated automatically and data goes in real time to the SYDONIA customs system, on the eDouanes platform run by concessionaire Webb Fontaine](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>). [Customs says the SYDONIA World platform is used by Central African Republic customs, and Cameroon's CAMCIS is to be made interoperable with it](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7061/en/hometighten-cemac-transit-cut>). [The Ministry of Finance and Budget's proposed 2025 budget carries a line of XAF 115,000,000 titled 'Appui au deploiement de SYDONIA World', supporting the rollout of the customs system](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2024-12/3-%20PLF2025%2004%2012%202024.pdf>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>).
