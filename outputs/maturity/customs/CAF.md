# Central African Republic

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Investir en Centrafrique reports that once the DPI fee is paid via Orange Money, the loading-intention declaration is generated automatically](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>). Data then goes in real time to the SYDONIA customs system, on the eDouanes platform run by concessionaire Webb Fontaine. [Customs says Central African Republic customs uses the SYDONIA World platform](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7061/en/hometighten-cemac-transit-cut>), and Cameroon's CAMCIS is to be made interoperable with it. [The Ministry of Finance and Budget's proposed 2025 budget carries a line of XAF 115,000,000 titled 'Appui au deploiement de SYDONIA World'](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2024-12/3-%20PLF2025%2004%2012%202024.pdf>). The line supports the rollout of the customs system.

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 data from loading-intention declarations went in real time to the SYDONIA customs system on the eDouanes platform](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the eDouanes platform was run by the concessionaire Webb Fontaine](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>).
