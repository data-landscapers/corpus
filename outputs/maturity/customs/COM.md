# Comoros

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The finance ministry's notice of 26 June 2026 on the close of the AFD- and EU-backed PAGF says the project strengthened the interface between SYDONIA and SIGIT for smoother tax and customs data](<https://dncmp.km/official-journals/ceremonie-de-cloture-pagf-f916dcee>). [The Direction Generale des Douanes says SYDONIA World has been the central platform dematerialising customs procedures, in operation since 2018](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>). [The Direction Generale des Douanes says a UNCTAD expert's functional and technical audit of SYDONIA World defined the evolutions needed to strengthen its performance, interoperability and security, with the aim of evolving it into a more integrated platform](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>). [UNCTAD says Comoros customs procedures remain only partially digitalized despite the deployment of SYDONIAWorld, and its programme is to digitalize further customs procedures](<https://unctad.org/project/comoros-economic-integration-acceleration-programme-components-1-and-2a>). [The UN Global Survey 2025 records the Automated Customs System in Comoros as fully implemented, with e-submission of customs declarations partially implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=COM>). [TACD CARTENA says SYDONIA is one of the four main applications of the Comorian finance ministry's financial information system, alongside SIGIT, SIM-BA and GISE](<https://www.tacdcartena.org/actualite/comores-si/>).

**Single window.** [UNCTAD says the first phase of the Guichet Unique du Commerce Extérieur (GUCE) has been deployed, and its programme is to extend it to more border agencies](<https://unctad.org/project/comoros-economic-integration-acceleration-programme-components-1-and-2a>). [The same programme says customs procedures are still only partly digitised](<https://unctad.org/project/comoros-economic-integration-acceleration-programme-components-1-and-2a>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>).
