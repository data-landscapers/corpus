# Comoros

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Direction Generale des Douanes says SYDONIA World has been the central platform dematerialising customs procedures, in operation since 2018](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>). [La Gazette des Comores reports that the customs information system SYDONIA was interconnected with the tax system SIGIT in a launch on 30 December 2023, with customs operator data cross-checked against tax records](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/economie/interconnexion-sigit-/-sydonia-vers-la-modernisation-et-la-s%C3%A9curisation-de-la-gestion-fiscale-.html>). [The Direction Generale des Douanes says a UNCTAD expert's functional and technical audit of SYDONIA World defined the evolutions needed to strengthen its performance, interoperability and security, with the aim of evolving it into a more integrated platform](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.douane.gov.km/lire-actu.php?id=34&lang=fr>).
