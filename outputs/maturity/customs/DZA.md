# Algeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Algeria's customs director general says the new customs information system ALCES, in service since November 2023 at land, sea and air border customs offices, had recorded 168,000 detailed declarations by September 2024, 70% of filings, the rest on the old SIGAD system](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>). [CUPIA says it launched ALCES, an automated customs clearance system based on Korea's UNI-PASS, in major customs centres including the Port of Algiers and Algiers airport (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.unipass.or.kr/cupia-officially-launched-the-alces-algerian-customs-electronic-system-system/>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>).
