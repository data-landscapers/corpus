# Algeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [TSA Algerie reports that the customs information system ALCES lets customs formalities be done online](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-les-douanes-algeriennes-lancent-le-guichet-electronique-unique-ce-qui-change/>). [Finance Minister Abdelkrim Bouzred says the customs information system launched in early November 2023 is generalised to all customs offices](<https://algerie-eco.com/2025/05/25/douanes-plus-de-23-000-inscrits-sur-le-systeme-dinformation/>). [Customs files are processed electronically at all land, sea and air centres, and 23,402 traders were registered by end March 2025](<https://algerie-eco.com/2025/05/25/douanes-plus-de-23-000-inscrits-sur-le-systeme-dinformation/>). [In September 2024 the customs director general said ALCES, in service since November 2023 at border customs offices, had recorded 168,000 detailed declarations](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>). [These were 70% of filings, the rest on the old SIGAD system](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>).

**Single window.** [TSA Algerie reports a customs notice of 18 August 2026 saying the electronic single window module of ALCES was put in service](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-les-douanes-algeriennes-lancent-le-guichet-electronique-unique-ce-qui-change/>). [Commerce Ministry authorisations are now filed through it in parallel with paper, and several other bodies are to be covered](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-les-douanes-algeriennes-lancent-le-guichet-electronique-unique-ce-qui-change/>).
