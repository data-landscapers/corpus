# Algeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [TSA Algerie reports that the Algerian customs information system ALCES lets customs formalities be done online](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-les-douanes-algeriennes-lancent-le-guichet-electronique-unique-ce-qui-change/>). [Finance Minister Abdelkrim Bouzred says the customs information system launched in early November 2023 is generalised to all customs offices, with electronic processing of customs files at all land, sea and air centres, and 23,402 traders registered by end March 2025](<https://algerie-eco.com/2025/05/25/douanes-plus-de-23-000-inscrits-sur-le-systeme-dinformation/>). [Algeria's customs director general says the new customs information system ALCES, in service since November 2023 at land, sea and air border customs offices, had recorded 168,000 detailed declarations by September 2024, 70% of filings, the rest on the old SIGAD system](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>).

**Single window.** [TSA Algerie reports a customs notice of 18 August 2026 saying the electronic single window module of ALCES was put in service, with Commerce Ministry authorisations now filed through it in parallel with paper and several other bodies to be covered](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/importations-les-douanes-algeriennes-lancent-le-guichet-electronique-unique-ce-qui-change/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lalgerieaujourdhui.dz/les-douanes-optent-pour-un-nouveau-systeme/>).
