# Guinea

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Africa Guinee reports that Guinea's customs work on the SYDONIA customs platform, and that under an agreement signed on 28 August 2025 System FIRST Guinea will issue and manage the electronic cargo tracking note on it](<https://www.africaguinee.com/securite-et-transparence-les-douanes-guineennes-modernisent-la-gestion-des-cargaisons/>). [The UN Global Survey on Digital and Sustainable Trade Facilitation, in its 2025 round, rates Guinea's automated customs system as fully implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=GIN&year=2025>).

**Single window.** [Webb Fontaine reported in June 2026 that after seven years of operation the Guinean Single Window for Foreign Trade (GUCEG) connects commercial banks, the Central Bank, Customs, port authorities, land border posts and economic operators](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/guceg-impact-360-highlights-seven-years-of-digital-trade-transformation-in-guinea>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says it launched the deployment of SIGMAT, its interconnected system for goods in transit, between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia on 31 October 2025, the three exchanging electronic transit messages through the interconnection of their national customs systems](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>).
