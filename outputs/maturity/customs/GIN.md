# Guinea

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Africa Guinee reports that Guinea's customs work on the SYDONIA customs platform](<https://www.africaguinee.com/securite-et-transparence-les-douanes-guineennes-modernisent-la-gestion-des-cargaisons/>). Under an agreement signed on 28 August 2025, System FIRST Guinea will issue and manage the electronic cargo tracking note on it. [The UN Global Survey on Digital and Sustainable Trade Facilitation, in its 2025 round, rates Guinea's automated customs system as fully implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=GIN&year=2025>).

**Single window.** [Webb Fontaine reported in June 2026 that the Guinean Single Window for Foreign Trade (GUCEG) had operated for seven years](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/guceg-impact-360-highlights-seven-years-of-digital-trade-transformation-in-guinea>). It connects commercial banks, the Central Bank, Customs, port authorities, land border posts and economic operators.

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says it launched deployment of SIGMAT, its interconnected system for goods in transit, between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia on 31 October 2025](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>). The three exchange electronic transit messages through the interconnection of their national customs systems.
