# Equatorial Guinea

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [PAMFP states that phase II of the SYDONIA customs software is to be implemented at Bata, Mongomo, Ebebiyin, Corisco, Aconibe, Cogo and Rio Campo, with IT equipment and network to extend SYDONIA outside Malabo](<https://www.pamfp.org/en/support-for-the-reinforcement-of-the-mobilization-of-non-oil-revenues/>). [UNCTAD ASYCUDA reports in June 2025 that ASYCUDAWorld is fully operational in all Equatorial Guinea customs offices, with Bata live since November 2024, the Malabo port since March 2025 and the Malabo airport since 2020, enabling paperless clearance](<https://asycuda.org/en/equatorial-guinea-completes-nationwide-rollout-of-asycudaworld/>). [The Prime Minister officially launched the Sidunea World customs platform at Bata customs on 27 December 2024; the finance minister says it has been in use at Malabo customs since 2021](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/lanzamiento-oficial-de-la-plataforma-digital-sidunea-world-en-las-aduanas-de-bata/>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.pamfp.org/en/support-for-the-reinforcement-of-the-mobilization-of-non-oil-revenues/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.pamfp.org/en/support-for-the-reinforcement-of-the-mobilization-of-non-oil-revenues/>).
