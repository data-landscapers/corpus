# Cape Verde

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [UGPE reports an upgrade of SYDONIA World at Cape Verde's customs offices, including migration to version 4.4.0 and new customs modules, in a project running November 2023 to July 2026](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/en/noticia/cabo-verde-digital-project-supports-customs-modernization-through-an-upgrade-to-sydonia-world>). [ASYCUDA reports that Cabo Verde launched the ASYVAL valuation control module in ASYCUDAWorld in March 2025, which flags deviating import declarations automatically and raised customs revenue by 2.49% on average across 12 product categories](<https://asycuda.org/en/cabo-verde-embraces-asyval-to-strengthen-trade-integrity-and-revenue-mobilisation/>). [Cabo Verde hosted an ECOWAS mission on 18-21 November 2024 to implement the SIGMAT transit system, and customs chose the SIGMAT mobile version for inter-island transit](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/-/dire%C3%A7%C3%A3o-geral-das-alf%C3%A2ndegas-recebe-misso-da-cedeao-para-implementa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-do-sistema-sigmat>).
