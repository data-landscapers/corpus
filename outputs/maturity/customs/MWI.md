# Malawi

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Nation reports the Malawi Revenue Authority's upgraded ASYCUDA World processes import and export declarations and uses risk profiles to route them at Mchinji and Kamuzu International Airport](<https://mwnation.com/mra-hails-automation-of-cargo-clearance/>). [TradeMark Africa reports the Malawi Revenue Authority customs system processes export and import declarations, with the enhanced CCEDE live with the neighbouring authority since November 2025](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/>). [SATCP says the Malawi Revenue Authority is upgrading, testing and piloting ASYCUDA World from version 4.3.3 to 4.4.0, to complete by September 2025, with a scanner integration with ASYCUDA operational](<https://satcp.mw/2025/05/23/ict-projects-running-at-satcp/>).

**Single window.** [The Minister of Trade and Industry launched Malawi's National Single Window on 20 May 2025, live first for the trade ministry with seven MDAs at go-live and 22 agencies planned by September 2025, and SATCP's project manager says it integrates with the Malawi Revenue Authority's ASYCUDA World system](<https://satcp.mw/2025/05/20/malawi-launches-national-single-window-to-boost-trade-and-digital-economy/>).
