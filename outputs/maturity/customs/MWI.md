# Malawi

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Nation reports the Malawi Revenue Authority's upgraded ASYCUDA World processes import and export declarations](<https://mwnation.com/mra-hails-automation-of-cargo-clearance/>). It uses risk profiles to route them at Mchinji and Kamuzu International Airport. [TradeMark Africa reports the authority's customs system processes export and import declarations](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/>). The enhanced CCEDE has been live with the neighbouring authority since November 2025. [SATCP says the Malawi Revenue Authority is upgrading, testing and piloting ASYCUDA World from version 4.3.3 to 4.4.0](<https://satcp.mw/2025/05/23/ict-projects-running-at-satcp/>), to complete by September 2025. A scanner integration with ASYCUDA is operational.

**Single window.** [The Minister of Trade and Industry launched Malawi's National Single Window on 20 May 2025](<https://satcp.mw/2025/05/20/malawi-launches-national-single-window-to-boost-trade-and-digital-economy/>). It went live first for the trade ministry, with seven MDAs at go-live and 22 agencies planned by September 2025. SATCP's project manager says it integrates with the Malawi Revenue Authority's ASYCUDA World system.
