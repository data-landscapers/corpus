# Malawi

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Since February 2025 Kalondola has been linked to Asycuda, described as the customs clearance system, so imports in specified categories must meet stamp requirements at entry points](<https://mwnation.com/digital-tax-stamps-enhance-revenue-compliance-mra/>). [The Malawi Revenue Authority says it is upgrading its customs management system from ASYCUDA World 4.3.3 to 4.4, with migration scheduled for 11 January 2025](<https://www.mra.mw/press-releases/upgrading-of-customs-management-system-from-asycuda-world-version-433-to-asycuda-world-version-44>). [The Malawi Revenue Authority says it supports clearing agents, importers and exporters on its ASYCUDA World 4.3.3 system at all customs stations across the country](<https://www.mra.mw/press-releases/upgrading-of-customs-management-system-from-asycuda-world-version-433-to-asycuda-world-version-44>).

**Single window.** [The Minister of Trade and Industry launched Malawi's National Single Window on 20 May 2025, live first for the trade ministry with seven MDAs at go-live and 22 agencies planned by September 2025, and SATCP's project manager says it integrates with the Malawi Revenue Authority's ASYCUDA World system](<https://satcp.mw/2025/05/20/malawi-launches-national-single-window-to-boost-trade-and-digital-economy/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [The SARS-MRA arrangement, applicable on signature on 30 July 2026, provides for automatic exchange of customs declaration and transit data between the two authorities' automated customs systems, using a unique consignment reference](<https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Legal/Agreements/CMAA/Legal-IntA-CMAA-2026-01-Arrangement-between-SARS-and-Malawi-Revenue-Authority-for-the-Automatic-of-Exchange-of-Customs-Information-30-July-2026.pdf>).
