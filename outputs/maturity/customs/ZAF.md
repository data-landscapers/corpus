# South Africa

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [SARS told Freight News that the National Single Window is being introduced in phases, with an electronic inspection workflow implemented in September 2023 and selected importers in phased testing, and gave no completion date](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>). [SARS says it has an automated inspection and case management workflow that the Single Window extends to other government agencies](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>). [SARS says a new customs management system, replacing some 26 legacy and paper-based systems, was fully operational from 18 August 2013 for all commercial cross-border trade (2013, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/22-august-2013-implementation-of-the-new-customs-systems-modernisation-programme/>).

**Single window.** [SARS says its Single Window electronic inspection workflow, sharing inspection cases with the Border Management Authority, was implemented in September 2023 but is still in restricted testing with selected importers, and the full National Single Window is 'still some time away' (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data through a system open to its whole regional community. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>).
