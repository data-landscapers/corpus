# South Africa

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [SARS told Freight News that the National Single Window is being introduced in phases, and gave no completion date](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>). [An electronic inspection workflow was implemented in September 2023, and selected importers are in phased testing](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>). [SARS says it has an automated inspection and case management workflow that the Single Window extends to other government agencies](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>). [SARS says a new customs management system was fully operational from 18 August 2013 for all commercial cross-border trade](<https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/22-august-2013-implementation-of-the-new-customs-systems-modernisation-programme/>). [It replaced some 26 legacy and paper-based systems](<https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/22-august-2013-implementation-of-the-new-customs-systems-modernisation-programme/>).

**Single window.** [In September 2023 SARS implemented its Single Window electronic inspection workflow](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>). [The workflow shares inspection cases with the Border Management Authority](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>). [SARS said it was in restricted testing with selected importers](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>). [It said the full National Single Window was 'still some time away'](<https://www.sars.gov.za/customs-and-excise/customs-single-window-solution/>).
