# Namibia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [NamRA upgraded and rolled out ASYCUDAWorld version 4.4.1 on 5 May 2026, and Commissioner Sam Shivute says it processed N$18 billion of goods between 5 and 17 May and handles all trade-related transactions, with declarations submitted online](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>). [NamRA, reported by New Era on 2026-05-05, put the upgraded ASYCUDAWorld 4.4.1 into effect that day, with transit, warehousing, export and similar declarations accepted online only](<https://neweralive.na/namra-upgrades-asycudaworld-system-and-implements-new-procedures/>). [The Namibia Revenue Agency's notice of 1 April 2026 says the ASYCUDAWorld upgrade to version 4.4.1 has its go-live moved from 1 April to 5 May 2026 for training and stakeholder readiness](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice-asycudaworld-golive-date-extended-e62d0a7fe7.pdf>). [The Bank of Namibia says its Trade Verification System, live since 16 October 2023, is integrated with the Namibia Revenue Agency's Automated System for Customs Data (ASYCUDA), through which customs declarations are made](<https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/Media/Media-Releases/2023-Media-Releases/Articles/Bank-of-Namibia-and-Namibia-Revenue-Agency-activat.aspx>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>).
