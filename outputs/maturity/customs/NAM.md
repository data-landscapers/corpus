# Namibia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [NamRA upgraded and rolled out ASYCUDAWorld version 4.4.1 on 5 May 2026](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>). Commissioner Sam Shivute says it processed N$18 billion of goods between 5 and 17 May. He says it handles all trade-related transactions, with declarations submitted online. [New Era reported on 2026-05-05 that NamRA put the upgraded ASYCUDAWorld 4.4.1 into effect that day](<https://neweralive.na/namra-upgrades-asycudaworld-system-and-implements-new-procedures/>). Transit, warehousing, export and similar declarations are accepted online only. [The Namibia Revenue Agency's notice of 1 April 2026 says the go-live of the ASYCUDAWorld upgrade to version 4.4.1 moved from 1 April to 5 May 2026](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice-asycudaworld-golive-date-extended-e62d0a7fe7.pdf>). The delay was for training and stakeholder readiness. [The Bank of Namibia says its Trade Verification System, live since 16 October 2023, is integrated with the Namibia Revenue Agency's Automated System for Customs Data (ASYCUDA)](<https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/Media/Media-Releases/2023-Media-Releases/Articles/Bank-of-Namibia-and-Namibia-Revenue-Agency-activat.aspx>). Customs declarations are made through ASYCUDA.

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 the NamRA commissioner said ASYCUDAWorld handles all trade-related transactions, with declarations submitted online](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>).
