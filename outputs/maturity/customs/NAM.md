# Namibia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [NamRA upgraded and rolled out ASYCUDAWorld version 4.4.1 on 5 May 2026, and Commissioner Sam Shivute says it processed N$18 billion of goods between 5 and 17 May and handles all trade-related transactions, with declarations submitted online](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>). [NamRA, reported by New Era on 2026-05-05, put the upgraded ASYCUDAWorld 4.4.1 into effect that day, with transit, warehousing, export and similar declarations accepted online only](<https://neweralive.na/namra-upgrades-asycudaworld-system-and-implements-new-procedures/>). [The Bank of Namibia says its Trade Verification System, live since 16 October 2023, is integrated with the Namibia Revenue Agency's Automated System for Customs Data (ASYCUDA), through which customs declarations are made](<https://www.bon.com.na/Informations/Media/Media-Releases/2023-Media-Releases/Articles/Bank-of-Namibia-and-Namibia-Revenue-Agency-activat.aspx>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-says-old-customs-system-was-11-versions-behind/>).
