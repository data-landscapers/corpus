# Mali

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Mali's Director-General of Customs said the move to SYDONIAWorld in 2015 built an integrated system that now covers over 95 percent of customs business procedures](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/535>). [The Togolese Revenue Office announced a pilot interconnection of the Togo and Mali customs systems via Burkina Faso on the Lome-Bamako corridor](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). [It was operational from 10 March 2025](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). [The UN Global Survey 2025 round records Mali's automated customs system as fully implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=MLI>). [In 2017-2018 the customs directorate continued switching offices into production on SYDONIAWorld](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/migration-sydoniaworld>). [The international transit module and a direct-removal procedure were in operation](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/migration-sydoniaworld>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window joining customs with the other border agencies is in service. The nearest held fact is the Director-General's account of [SYDONIAWorld covering most customs procedures](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/535>).

**Exchange with partners.** [Douanes Ivoiriennes report that Cote d Ivoire and Mali teams met on 24-25 September 2026 to assess SIGMAT in its operating phase on the Abidjan-Bamako transit corridor, sharing information between the two administrations](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/cooperation-bilaterale>). [ECOWAS says Mali was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system in July 2024, though it also lists Mali among states preparing to put it into operation](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
