# Mali

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Mali's Director-General of Customs said in February 2026 that the move to SYDONIAWorld in 2015 built an integrated system that now covers over 95 percent of customs business procedures](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/535>). [The customs directorate reports that offices were switched into production on SYDONIAWorld through 2017 and 2018, with the international transit module and a direct-removal procedure in operation](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/migration-sydoniaworld>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window joining customs with the other border agencies is in service. The nearest held fact is the Director-General's account of [SYDONIAWorld covering most customs procedures](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/535>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says Mali's customs exchanges declaration or transit data electronically with another country's customs. The nearest held fact: [the transit module in operation since 2018](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/migration-sydoniaworld>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Sygce](<https://malijet.com/actualite_economique_du_mali/275483-amelioration-du-commerce-exterieur-du-mali-le-guichet-unique-de-.html>).

***Not held***. No source held says a trade single window is in service or that declaration data is exchanged with another country's customs.
