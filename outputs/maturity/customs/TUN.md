# Tunisia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [On 12 November 2025 customs spokesman Chokri Jarraya said the SINDA 2 customs system will be ready in 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/12/douane-le-systeme-informatique-sinda-2-operationnel-a-partir-de-2026-2/>). [He said its first applications would enter service at the end of 2025](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/12/douane-le-systeme-informatique-sinda-2-operationnel-a-partir-de-2026-2/>). [Decree 2023-160 of February 2023 created a finance-ministry unit to acquire, develop and put a new customs information system into operation](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-n-2023-160-du-17-fevrier-2023-portant-creation-dune-unite-de-gestion-par-objectifs-au-ministere-des-finances-pour-la-realisation-du-projet-dacquisition-de-developpement-et-de/>). [Its three-year first stage covers development and testing](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-n-2023-160-du-17-fevrier-2023-portant-creation-dune-unite-de-gestion-par-objectifs-au-ministere-des-finances-pour-la-realisation-du-projet-dacquisition-de-developpement-et-de/>).
