# Cameroon

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Cameroon customs has run the CAMCIS information system since April 2020](<https://bougna.net/2024/06/19/douanes/fongod-edwin-nuvaga-la-reforme-de-camcis-se-poursuit-inexorablement/>), with freight, clearance and revenue modules, its director general says. [He named linking CAMCIS with Chad's SYDONIA World as a priority](<https://www.theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7286/en/homefluidify>). [An AFRITAC-supported mission is examining how CAMCIS can work with SYDONIA World](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7061/en/hometighten-cemac-transit-cut>).

**Single window.** [The Chambre des comptes said in 2024 that the single window, Guce, centralises import and export operations](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/gestion-publique-forets-mines-douanes-et-guichet-unique-ne-se-parlent-pas/>). [Guce issues customs duty receipts and clears imports and vehicles within 48 hours](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/echanges-commerciaux-le-guichet-unique-de-troisieme-generation-se-devoile-et-promet-de-nombreuses-innovations-aux-agents-economiques/>), on a platform dating from 2012. In November 2025 its director general said [it had digitalised more than 35 trade procedures](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/1111-15330-cameroon-logistics-bodies-partner-to-accelerate-digital-trade-operations>). [With the shippers' council it had exchanged more than 2 million files and cargo tracking notes](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/1111-15330-cameroon-logistics-bodies-partner-to-accelerate-digital-trade-operations>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/gestion-publique-forets-mines-douanes-et-guichet-unique-ne-se-parlent-pas/>).
