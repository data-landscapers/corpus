# Cameroon

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Cameroon customs director general said the interconnection between CAMCIS and Chad's SYDONIA World remains a priority, naming CAMCIS as the system Cameroon customs uses](<https://www.theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7286/en/homefluidify>). [The article refers to Cameroon's customs system CAMCIS, whose interoperability with SYDONIA World is being examined by an AFRITAC-supported mission](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/7061/en/hometighten-cemac-transit-cut>). [Cameroon's Director General of Customs says the CAMCIS customs information system has been in operation since April 2020, with its freight, clearance and revenue-collection modules launched (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://bougna.net/2024/06/19/douanes/fongod-edwin-nuvaga-la-reforme-de-camcis-se-poursuit-inexorablement/>).

**Single window.** [The Chambre des comptes' 2024 certification work states that the Guichet unique des opérations du commerce extérieur (Guce) centralises import and export operations and serves as the reference for tracking foreign-trade financial flows, with customs revenue recorded against it](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/gestion-publique-forets-mines-douanes-et-guichet-unique-ne-se-parlent-pas/>). [The GUCE single window, a partnership of the State and operators on a platform dating from 2012, is described as issuing customs duty receipts and clearing imports and vehicles within 48 hours; a third-generation version is announced for Q1 2025](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/echanges-commerciaux-le-guichet-unique-de-troisieme-generation-se-devoile-et-promet-de-nombreuses-innovations-aux-agents-economiques/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.economieducameroun.info/gestion-publique-forets-mines-douanes-et-guichet-unique-ne-se-parlent-pas/>).
