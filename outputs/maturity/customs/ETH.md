# Ethiopia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Ethiopian Customs Commission says it has fully digitalized its services and established a digital system that lets importers and exporters execute their activities online (Deputy Commissioner Azezew Chane to ENA)](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>). [A Smart Border Custom Management System with AI-enabled cameras covering about 50 kilometers is being implemented at customs stations on the borders, per the Deputy Commissioner](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>). [Webb Fontaine says its EU-funded Webb Customs eCMS automates 27 Ethiopian customs offices, replacing the old Asycuda system, with declaration processing and airport manifests handled electronically](<https://webbfontaine.com/success-stories/ethiopia/customs-management-project>).

**Single window.** [The National Bank of Ethiopia and the Ethiopia Customs Commission issue NBE Account Numbers to newly licensed importers and exporters through the Customs Electronic Single Window (eSW), joining the central bank to customs (NBE statement)](<https://nbe.gov.et/nbe_news/national-bank-of-ethiopia-streamlines-nbe-account-number-issuance-for-new-importers-and-exporters-through-the-customs-electronic-single-window/>). [The Electronic Single Window (ESW) integrates more than 70 institutions and lets traders submit all import/export requirements in batch, per the Deputy Commissioner](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>).
