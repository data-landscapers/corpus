# Ethiopia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Ethiopian Customs Commission says it has fully digitalized its services](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>), Deputy Commissioner Azezew Chane told ENA. It says a digital system lets importers and exporters execute their activities online. [A Smart Border Custom Management System with AI-enabled cameras covering about 50 kilometers is being implemented](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>) at border customs stations, per the Deputy Commissioner. [Webb Fontaine says its EU-funded Webb Customs eCMS automates 27 Ethiopian customs offices, replacing the old Asycuda system](<https://webbfontaine.com/success-stories/ethiopia/customs-management-project>). Declaration processing and airport manifests are handled electronically.

**Single window.** [The National Bank of Ethiopia and the Ethiopia Customs Commission issue NBE Account Numbers to newly licensed importers and exporters](<https://nbe.gov.et/nbe_news/national-bank-of-ethiopia-streamlines-nbe-account-number-issuance-for-new-importers-and-exporters-through-the-customs-electronic-single-window/>). They do so through the Customs Electronic Single Window (eSW), which joins the central bank to customs (NBE statement). [The Electronic Single Window (ESW) integrates more than 70 institutions](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8388900>). Traders can submit all import/export requirements in batch, per the Deputy Commissioner.
