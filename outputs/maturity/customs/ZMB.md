# Zambia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Ecofin Agency reports the Zambia Revenue Authority is moving from ASYCUDA World to ASY5 under a UNCTAD customs modernisation project](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2010-59956-zambia-advances-customs-system-upgrade-to-improve-trade-processing>). Assessments, pilot testing and officer training are under way, per the authority's 19 October 2026 account. [On 19 October 2026 the authority outlined these next steps, under a project agreed with UNCTAD in December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2010-59956-zambia-advances-customs-system-upgrade-to-improve-trade-processing>). No completion date was set. [ZRA, UNCTAD and TradeMark Africa signed an agreement on 16 December 2025 for an 18-month, UK-funded project](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-sets-course-for-faster-trade-as-customs-system-upgrade-agreement-is-signed/>). It is to upgrade and stabilise the ASYCUDAWorld customs system.

**Single window.** [The Zambia Revenue Authority says the Zambian Electronic Single Window has an enhanced e-bond module](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-110-issue-2-2026/integrity-zambia/>). The 2025 BMTF Act mandates electronic information exchange among all participating agencies through it.

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data through a system open to its whole regional community. The closest fact found: [TradeMark Africa reports the Zambia Revenue Authority customs system processes export and import declarations](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/>). The enhanced CCEDE has been live with the neighbouring authority since November 2025.
