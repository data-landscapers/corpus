# Zambia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [A British High Commission official said the upgraded ASYCUDA Single Window will improve coordination across agencies and shorten clearance times](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-sets-course-for-faster-trade-as-customs-system-upgrade-agreement-is-signed/>). [ZRA Commissioner General Dingani Banda said ASYCUDA is deeply integrated with internal and external systems and is handling rising declaration volumes, with delays at borders](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-sets-course-for-faster-trade-as-customs-system-upgrade-agreement-is-signed/>). [ZRA, UNCTAD and TradeMark Africa signed an agreement on 16 December 2025 for an 18-month, UK-funded project to upgrade and stabilise the ASYCUDAWorld customs system](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-sets-course-for-faster-trade-as-customs-system-upgrade-agreement-is-signed/>). [The World Customs Organization says Zambia Revenue Authority and Tanzania Revenue Authority held a workshop on 2-6 September 2024 to integrate their automated Customs systems, which ZRA runs](<https://www.wcoomd.org/en/media/newsroom/2024/september/strengthening-bilateral-customs-connectivity-between-zambia-and-tanzania.aspx>).

**Single window.** [The Zambia Revenue Authority says the Zambian Electronic Single Window has an enhanced e-bond module and the 2025 BMTF Act mandates electronic information exchange among all participating agencies through it](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-110-issue-2-2026/integrity-zambia/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-110-issue-2-2026/integrity-zambia/>).
