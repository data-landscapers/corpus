# Zambia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Ecofin Agency reports the Zambia Revenue Authority is moving from ASYCUDA World to ASY5 under a UNCTAD customs modernisation project, with assessments, pilot testing and officer training under way per the authority's 19 October 2026 account](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2010-59956-zambia-advances-customs-system-upgrade-to-improve-trade-processing>). [The Zambia Revenue Authority outlined on 19 October 2026 next steps (system assessments, pilot testing, officer training) in moving from ASYCUDA World to ASY5 under an UNCTAD project agreed in December 2025, with no completion date set](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2010-59956-zambia-advances-customs-system-upgrade-to-improve-trade-processing>). [ZRA, UNCTAD and TradeMark Africa signed an agreement on 16 December 2025 for an 18-month, UK-funded project to upgrade and stabilise the ASYCUDAWorld customs system](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-sets-course-for-faster-trade-as-customs-system-upgrade-agreement-is-signed/>).

**Single window.** [The Zambia Revenue Authority says the Zambian Electronic Single Window has an enhanced e-bond module and the 2025 BMTF Act mandates electronic information exchange among all participating agencies through it](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-110-issue-2-2026/integrity-zambia/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data through a system open to its whole regional community. The nearest held fact: [TradeMark Africa reports the Zambia Revenue Authority customs system processes export and import declarations, with the enhanced CCEDE live with the neighbouring authority since November 2025](<https://trademarkafrica.com/zambia-and-malawi-live-customs-data-exchange-to-speed-up-trade-and-strengthen-compliance/>).
