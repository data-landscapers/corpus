# Botswana

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Finance minister Peggy Serame said BURS continues to optimise e-services including online customs declarations](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77665>). [BURS's Principal Officer for Customs Reforms says a new integrated Customs Management System replaced ASYCUDA++ in 2017 and clears declarations after online payment through the declaration processing platform (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>). 5 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Single window.** [BURS says the CMS provides a Single Window through its Other Government Agencies module, with the Botswana Bureau of Standards enrolled and other agencies at testing stage (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [Serame said BURS's Systems Connectivity and Data Exchange programme with Zambia, for customs and tax data, was completed in May 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77665>).
