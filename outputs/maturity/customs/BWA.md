# Botswana

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [In February 2024 finance minister Peggy Serame said BURS continues to optimise its e-services](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77665>). [These include online customs declarations](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77665>). [In 2022 BURS's Principal Officer for Customs Reforms said a new integrated Customs Management System replaced ASYCUDA++ in 2017](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>). [The system clears declarations after online payment through the declaration processing platform](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>).

**Single window.** [In February 2022 BURS said the Customs Management System provides a single window through its Other Government Agencies module](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>). [The Botswana Bureau of Standards was enrolled and other agencies were at testing stage](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-97-issue-1-2022/how-botswana-customs-is-leveraging-data-driven-technologies/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country.
