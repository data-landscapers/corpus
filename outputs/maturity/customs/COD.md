# DR Congo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Electronic transmission of the GUICE document bundle to the SYDONIA World customs system is deployed in Haut-Katanga (Lubumbashi, Kasumbalesa, Sakania), Lualaba and Kongo-Central, with N'djili airport from 1 July 2026](<https://lepoint.cd/commerce-exterieur-la-dematerialisation-douaniere-setend-a-trois-provinces-avant-kinshasa/>). [An interministerial circular of 17 Dec 2025 set phased trials of electronic transmission from S-ONE to SYDONIA World from 29 Dec 2025](<https://fec-rdc.com/note-circulaire-interministerielle-relative-a-la-transmission-obligatoire-de-la-liasse-documentaire-du-systeme-informatique-du-guichet-unique-integral-du-commerce-exterieur-vers-le-systeme-informatiqu/>). [Kinshasa came first, then Haut-Katanga and Lualaba, then Kongo Central](<https://fec-rdc.com/note-circulaire-interministerielle-relative-a-la-transmission-obligatoire-de-la-liasse-documentaire-du-systeme-informatique-du-guichet-unique-integral-du-commerce-exterieur-vers-le-systeme-informatiqu/>). [The circular generalised the obligation nationwide from 18 Feb 2026](<https://fec-rdc.com/note-circulaire-interministerielle-relative-a-la-transmission-obligatoire-de-la-liasse-documentaire-du-systeme-informatique-du-guichet-unique-integral-du-commerce-exterieur-vers-le-systeme-informatiqu/>). [The DRC foreign trade ministry says trade documents go electronically into the customs directorate's SYDONIA World system with the goods declaration](<https://commerce.gouv.cd/details_actualites?id=175>). [It says this is to be consolidated](<https://commerce.gouv.cd/details_actualites?id=175>).

**Single window.** [Minister Julien Paluku said 93 of 98 foreign-trade documents are dematerialised through the Guichet unique intégral du commerce extérieur, whose system is connected to DGDA's SYDONIA World customs system for electronic transmission of the documentary set](<https://desknews.cd/2026/09/17/rdc-julien-paluku-vante-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-commerciales-pour-lutter-contre-la-corruption/>). [The Guichet unique integral du commerce exterieur (GUICE) sends documents electronically to customs and the administrations involved in import and export, deployed in three provinces in June 2026, per SEGUCE RDC and DGDA](<https://lepoint.cd/commerce-exterieur-la-dematerialisation-douaniere-setend-a-trois-provinces-avant-kinshasa/>). [The DRC foreign trade ministry says 67 of the 77 items of the import, export and transit document bundle were already dematerialised through the GUCE integrated single window, linking customs and other agencies](<https://commerce.gouv.cd/details_actualites?id=175>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 the minister said the Guichet unique intégral du commerce extérieur is connected to DGDA's SYDONIA World system](<https://desknews.cd/2026/09/17/rdc-julien-paluku-vante-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-commerciales-pour-lutter-contre-la-corruption/>).
