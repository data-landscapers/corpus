# DR Congo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Prime Minister officially launched electronic transmission of the import, export and transit documentary set from SEGUCE's S-ONE platform into SYDONIA World, DGDA's automated system, and the Finance Minister said operators must now use this channel](<https://www.primature.gouv.cd/2025/12/29/lutte-contre-les-tracasseries-dans-limport-export-judith-suminwa-lance-la-digitalisation-de-la-liasse-documentaire-en-rdc/>). [An interministerial circular of 17 Dec 2025 says trials of electronic transmission of the documentary set from S-ONE to SYDONIA World roll out in phases from 29 Dec 2025 (Kinshasa, then Haut-Katanga and Lualaba, then Kongo Central), with the obligation generalised nationwide from 18 Feb 2026](<https://fec-rdc.com/note-circulaire-interministerielle-relative-a-la-transmission-obligatoire-de-la-liasse-documentaire-du-systeme-informatique-du-guichet-unique-integral-du-commerce-exterieur-vers-le-systeme-informatiqu/>).

**Single window.** [Minister Julien Paluku said 93 of 98 foreign-trade documents are dematerialised through the Guichet unique intégral du commerce extérieur, whose system is connected to DGDA's SYDONIA World customs system for electronic transmission of the documentary set](<https://desknews.cd/2026/09/17/rdc-julien-paluku-vante-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-commerciales-pour-lutter-contre-la-corruption/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://desknews.cd/2026/09/17/rdc-julien-paluku-vante-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-commerciales-pour-lutter-contre-la-corruption/>).
