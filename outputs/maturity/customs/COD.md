# DR Congo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Electronic transmission of the GUICE document bundle to the SYDONIA World customs system is deployed in Haut-Katanga (Lubumbashi, Kasumbalesa, Sakania), Lualaba and Kongo-Central, with N'djili airport from 1 July 2026](<https://lepoint.cd/commerce-exterieur-la-dematerialisation-douaniere-setend-a-trois-provinces-avant-kinshasa/>). [An interministerial circular of 17 Dec 2025 says trials of electronic transmission of the documentary set from S-ONE to SYDONIA World roll out in phases from 29 Dec 2025 (Kinshasa, then Haut-Katanga and Lualaba, then Kongo Central), with the obligation generalised nationwide from 18 Feb 2026](<https://fec-rdc.com/note-circulaire-interministerielle-relative-a-la-transmission-obligatoire-de-la-liasse-documentaire-du-systeme-informatique-du-guichet-unique-integral-du-commerce-exterieur-vers-le-systeme-informatiqu/>). [The DRC foreign trade ministry says trade documents are transmitted electronically into the customs directorate's SYDONIA World system with the goods declaration, and that this is to be consolidated](<https://commerce.gouv.cd/details_actualites?id=175>).

**Single window.** [Minister Julien Paluku said 93 of 98 foreign-trade documents are dematerialised through the Guichet unique intégral du commerce extérieur, whose system is connected to DGDA's SYDONIA World customs system for electronic transmission of the documentary set](<https://desknews.cd/2026/09/17/rdc-julien-paluku-vante-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-commerciales-pour-lutter-contre-la-corruption/>). [The Guichet unique integral du commerce exterieur (GUICE) sends documents electronically to customs and the administrations involved in import and export, deployed in three provinces in June 2026, per SEGUCE RDC and DGDA](<https://lepoint.cd/commerce-exterieur-la-dematerialisation-douaniere-setend-a-trois-provinces-avant-kinshasa/>). [The DRC foreign trade ministry says 67 of the 77 items of the import, export and transit document bundle were already dematerialised through the GUCE integrated single window, linking customs and other agencies](<https://commerce.gouv.cd/details_actualites?id=175>).
