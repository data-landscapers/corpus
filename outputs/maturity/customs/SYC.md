# Seychelles

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The SRC says traders lodge bills of entry in ASYCUDA, and once payment is confirmed automatic risk-based selectivity assigns red or green clearance lanes](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>). [The SRC launched an ePayment module in ASYCUDA for import and export declarations (bills of entry), and its Commissioner of Customs said manifest, courier and excise tax modules had gone online the year before](<https://src.gov.sc/seychelles-revenue-commission-launches-online-payment-for-customs-services/>). [The Seychelles Revenue Commission announced the first phase of the ASYCUDA World upgrade to version 4.4.1, conducted by UNCTAD, for 13-14 March 2026, adding trader self-assessment and paperless bill-of-entry processing](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>).
