# Seychelles

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The SRC says traders lodge bills of entry in ASYCUDA, and once payment is confirmed automatic risk-based selectivity assigns red or green clearance lanes](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>). [The Seychelles Revenue Commission announced the first phase of the ASYCUDA World upgrade to version 4.4.1, conducted by UNCTAD, for 13-14 March 2026, adding trader self-assessment and paperless bill-of-entry processing](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>). [UNDP says an AI-driven customs and logistics upgrade has been implemented through the Port Victoria Management Information System to streamline vessel clearance, without describing declaration processing](<https://sdglocalaction.org/how-cities-are-using-artificial-intelligence-to-deliver-services/>). [The SRC launched an ePayment module in ASYCUDA for import and export declarations (bills of entry), and its Commissioner of Customs said manifest, courier and excise tax modules had gone online the year before](<https://src.gov.sc/seychelles-revenue-commission-launches-online-payment-for-customs-services/>). [The WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement database record, from Seychelles' own extension request, says the Seychelles Revenue Commission runs ASYCUDA World (version 4.3.2), enhanced from September 2021 to May 2024 with express courier, manifest, excise bonded warehouse and tariff modules](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/seychelles/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>).
