# Seychelles

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The SRC says traders lodge bills of entry in ASYCUDA](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>). Once payment is confirmed, automatic risk-based selectivity assigns red or green clearance lanes. [The Seychelles Revenue Commission announced the first phase of the ASYCUDA World upgrade to version 4.4.1 for 13-14 March 2026](<https://src.gov.sc/the-seychelles-revenue-commission-prepares-to-upgrade-asycuda-world-to-version-4-4-1/>). UNCTAD conducts it, adding trader self-assessment and paperless bill-of-entry processing. [UNDP says an AI-driven customs and logistics upgrade has been implemented through the Port Victoria Management Information System](<https://sdglocalaction.org/how-cities-are-using-artificial-intelligence-to-deliver-services/>). It streamlines vessel clearance, and UNDP does not describe declaration processing. [The SRC launched an ePayment module in ASYCUDA for import and export declarations (bills of entry)](<https://src.gov.sc/seychelles-revenue-commission-launches-online-payment-for-customs-services/>). Its Commissioner of Customs said manifest, courier and excise tax modules had gone online the year before. [The WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement database record, from Seychelles' own extension request, says the SRC runs ASYCUDA World (version 4.3.2)](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/seychelles/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>). It was enhanced from September 2021 to May 2024 with express courier, manifest, excise bonded warehouse and tariff modules.
