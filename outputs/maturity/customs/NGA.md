# Nigeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Nigeria Customs Service presentation says a 20-year concession with Trade Modernisation Project Ltd is implementing a modernisation project to automate all NCS business processes, with scope including a Unified Customs Management System, cargo release system and technology upgrades](<https://tfadatabase.org/es/uploads/thematicdiscussiondocument/wto_ncs_digitalization_in_nigeria_customs_service_2025.03.12.pdf>). [The presentation's digital transformation timeline lists ASYCUDA e-Declaration, e-Payment and NICIS II among Nigeria Customs digital systems introduced through 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://tfadatabase.org/es/uploads/thematicdiscussiondocument/wto_ncs_digitalization_in_nigeria_customs_service_2025.03.12.pdf>).

**Single window.** [The National Single Window went live in March 2026 to unify trade processes across agencies, though lawyers say it lacks a statutory basis](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/stakeholders-seek-legal-framework-to-make-nigerias-digital-port-systems-work/>). [The National Single Window, launched 27 March 2026, had issued over 100,000 licences and permits to more than 1,000 registered users per its national coordinator, though licensed customs agents dispute that it works](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/customs-agents-disagree-over-national-single-window-operations/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/stakeholders-seek-legal-framework-to-make-nigerias-digital-port-systems-work/>).
