# Nigeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [BusinessDay reports the Nigeria Customs Service is fully integrated into the National Single Window, and a manual licence-upload option was added on customs' B'Odogwu platform to keep Form M and pre-arrival assessment processing moving](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/phase-2-of-national-single-window-to-go-live-november-2026-with-export-processing/>). [Punch reports that in recent years the Nigeria Customs Service has introduced digital reforms including automated cargo clearance systems and enhanced risk management frameworks](<https://punchng.com/customs-deploy-ai-to-curb-revenue-leakages-deepen-transparency/>). [Nigeria Customs Comptroller-General Adewale Adeniyi says the B'Odogwu customs system is a flagship project with phased improvements and continuous upgrades](<https://thenationonlineng.net/customs-b-odogwu-other-payment-platforms-to-link-with-national-single-window/>). [The presentation's digital transformation timeline lists ASYCUDA e-Declaration, e-Payment and NICIS II among Nigeria Customs digital systems introduced through 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://tfadatabase.org/es/uploads/thematicdiscussiondocument/wto_ncs_digitalization_in_nigeria_customs_service_2025.03.12.pdf>).

**Single window.** [The National Single Window, operational since 27 March 2026, had processed over 124,614 licences and permits, with the Nigerian Customs Service, SON, NAFDAC, Nigeria Quarantine Service and NESREA fully onboarded, per its Secretariat director and the trade minister](<https://punchng.com/fg-gives-mdas-november-deadline-to-remove-export-barriers/>). [The National Single Window, launched 27 March 2026, had issued over 100,000 licences and permits to more than 1,000 registered users per its national coordinator, though licensed customs agents dispute that it works](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/customs-agents-disagree-over-national-single-window-operations/>). [The National Single Window Secretariat's operations director said Phase 1 went live on 27 March 2026 with Customs, SON, NAFDAC, NAQS and NESREA fully integrated, having processed 114,112 licences and permits and N11.27 billion in payments](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/phase-2-of-national-single-window-to-go-live-november-2026-with-export-processing/>).
