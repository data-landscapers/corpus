# Nigeria

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Nigeria Customs Comptroller-General Adewale Adeniyi says the B'Odogwu customs system is a flagship project with phased improvements and continuous upgrades](<https://thenationonlineng.net/customs-b-odogwu-other-payment-platforms-to-link-with-national-single-window/>). [The Nigeria Customs Service presentation says a 20-year concession with Trade Modernisation Project Ltd is implementing a modernisation project to automate all NCS business processes, with scope including a Unified Customs Management System, cargo release system and technology upgrades](<https://tfadatabase.org/es/uploads/thematicdiscussiondocument/wto_ncs_digitalization_in_nigeria_customs_service_2025.03.12.pdf>). [The Nigeria Customs Service has introduced the web-based B'Odogwu Unified Customs Management System, and its PTML Command recorded a record N44.9 billion monthly revenue in November 2024, per the service's deputy comptroller-general Kikelomo Adeola](<https://businessday.ng/maritime/article/how-bodogwu-models-global-strategies-to-modernize-nigerian-trade/>).

**Single window.** [The National Single Window went live in March 2026 to unify trade processes across agencies, though lawyers say it lacks a statutory basis](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/stakeholders-seek-legal-framework-to-make-nigerias-digital-port-systems-work/>). [The National Single Window, launched 27 March 2026, had issued over 100,000 licences and permits to more than 1,000 registered users per its national coordinator, though licensed customs agents dispute that it works](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/customs-agents-disagree-over-national-single-window-operations/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/stakeholders-seek-legal-framework-to-make-nigerias-digital-port-systems-work/>).
