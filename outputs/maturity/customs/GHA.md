# Ghana

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The article says the GRA Customs Division operates the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS), which streamlines customs processes and improves trade facilitation](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [Graphic Online reports ICUMS is the main interface used for declarations, classification, valuation and clearing of goods at Ghana's ports of entry](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>). [Ghana Link says the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS), hosted in its new Kumasi Tier IV data centre, is relied on daily for trade facilitation and revenue processes by GRA Customs, clearing agents, importers, shipping lines and airlines](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-link-secures-iso-iec-270012022-certification-for-new-tier-iv-data-centre-supporting-icums/>). [Ghana Revenue Authority Commissioner-General Anthony Sarpong said the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS) has been the backbone of customs processing for over five years](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/01/ghana-to-plug-us127bn-revenue-leakages-with-new-ai-customs-tool/>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [The ECOWAS Commission says SIGMAT, its interconnected transit-goods system for electronic data exchange between Member States, has been in use by implementing states since 2019, and that a 21-25 July 2025 mission to Ghana resolved the technical and functional challenges hindering transit between Cote d'Ivoire and Ghana](<https://www.ecowas.int/technical-mission-to-ghana-on-the-deployment-of-sigmat-between-ghana-and-cote-divoire/>). [ECOWAS says Ghana was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
