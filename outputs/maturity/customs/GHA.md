# Ghana

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The GRA Customs Division operates the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS), which streamlines customs processes and improves trade facilitation](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [Ghana Link says the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS), hosted in its new Kumasi Tier IV data centre, is relied on daily for trade facilitation and revenue processes by GRA Customs, clearing agents, importers, shipping lines and airlines](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-link-secures-iso-iec-270012022-certification-for-new-tier-iv-data-centre-supporting-icums/>). [The Commissioner-General said in January 2026 that ICUMS has been the backbone of customs processing for over five years](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/01/ghana-to-plug-us127bn-revenue-leakages-with-new-ai-customs-tool/>). [It is the main interface for declarations, classification, valuation and clearance at the ports of entry (March 2026)](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>).
