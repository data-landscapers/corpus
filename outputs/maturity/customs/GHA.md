# Ghana

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The GRA Customs Division operates the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS), according to a 2026 article](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [Graphic Online reports ICUMS is the main interface for declarations, classification, valuation and clearing of goods at Ghana's ports of entry](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>). [Ghana Link says GRA Customs, clearing agents, importers, shipping lines and airlines rely on ICUMS daily](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-link-secures-iso-iec-270012022-certification-for-new-tier-iv-data-centre-supporting-icums/>).

**Single window.** [Ghana told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025 that its customs online portal is a single window](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). The answer linked it to the ICUMS external login. [The Ghana Revenue Authority's ICUMS user guide of October 2020 shows users applying through a Single Window tab for permits from other agencies](<https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/ICUMS-Manual-1.pdf-1.pdf-1.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Ghana was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>). [The ECOWAS Commission says a mission to Ghana on 21-25 July 2025 resolved technical and functional challenges](<https://www.ecowas.int/technical-mission-to-ghana-on-the-deployment-of-sigmat-between-ghana-and-cote-divoire/>). They had hindered transit between Cote d'Ivoire and Ghana.
