# Sierra Leone

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Customs declarations are processed in ASYCUDA World, where an Anti-Corruption Commission report found some deleted after payment](<https://www.thesierraleonetelegraph.com/over-300-billion-leones-missing-at-sierra-leones-national-revenue-authority/>). [The report notes real-time payment visibility on the platform](<https://www.thesierraleonetelegraph.com/over-300-billion-leones-missing-at-sierra-leones-national-revenue-authority/>). [In October 2026 the revenue authority, NRA, began consultations at the Jendema border crossing to launch the system there](<https://sierraloaded.sl/local/nra-launch-digital-customs-system-jendema/>). [In January 2026 UNCTAD committed to speed up an upgrade of the system](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/2026/01/21/unctad-secretary-general-rebecca-grynspan-commits-to-expediting-asycuda-system-upgrade-and-e-commerce-readiness-assessment-support/>).

**Single window.** [The NRA's ASYCUDA unit says the system connects several agencies through its modules](<https://sierraloaded.sl/local/nra-launch-digital-customs-system-jendema/>). [Each institution receives information according to its role](<https://sierraloaded.sl/local/nra-launch-digital-customs-system-jendema/>). [The WTO says Sierra Leone introduced an Electronic Single Window in 2020](<https://www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/tpr_e/s470_sum_e.pdf>), before the three-year window.

**Exchange with partners.** [In November 2024 ECOWAS noted that SIGMAT, the regional transit data system, was being tested in Sierra Leone](<https://www.ecowas.int/relevant-recommendations-to-ensure-the-success-of-sigmat-in-the-eco-was-region/>). [In October 2026 a Gambian member of the ECOWAS Parliament listed Sierra Leone among the few countries implementing it](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/ecowas-parliament-calls-for-expansion-of-sigmat-across-west-africa-cites-infrastructure-challenges/>). [The delegation's vice chair urged Sierra Leone to advance its implementation](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/ecowas-parliament-calls-for-expansion-of-sigmat-across-west-africa-cites-infrastructure-challenges/>).
