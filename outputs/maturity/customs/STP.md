# Sao Tome and Principe

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The General Customs Authority (AGA) officially launched Sydonia World 4.4.0 on 23 May 2025](<https://rstp.st/2025/05/27/autoridade-geral-aduaneira-avanca-na-reforma-digital-com-nova-versao-do-sydonia-world/>). [It was implemented across the whole national territory at once](<https://rstp.st/2025/05/27/autoridade-geral-aduaneira-avanca-na-reforma-digital-com-nova-versao-do-sydonia-world/>). [The AfDB financed it under the CAPEM project, with UNCTAD technical support](<https://rstp.st/2025/05/27/autoridade-geral-aduaneira-avanca-na-reforma-digital-com-nova-versao-do-sydonia-world/>). [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme said the new version went live on 1 May 2025](<https://asycuda.org/en/sao-tome-and-principe-leverages-asycuda-to-enhance-customs-efficiency-and-compliance/>). [It replaced ASYCUDAWorld 4.2, in use since 2011](<https://asycuda.org/en/sao-tome-and-principe-leverages-asycuda-to-enhance-customs-efficiency-and-compliance/>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service.
