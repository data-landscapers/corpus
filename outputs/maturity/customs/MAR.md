# Morocco

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance has rolled out the BADR system for customs clearance, and the customs IT system is integrated with key partners in the foreign trade chain, per the budget minister](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/08/12/minister-morocco-to-use-ai-to-enhanced-public-services/>). [Moroccan Customs says declarations have been lodged by computer since 1991 and clearance entered total dematerialisation in 2019, on the online clearance system BADR (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/fr/magazine/omd-actu-94/dematerialisation-douane-maroc/>). [ADII says detailed declarations are registered on its BADR system, which sends data automatically to the industry ministry through PortNet, now at all import clearance offices (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douane.gov.ma/dms/loadDocument?application=communiquesPresse&documentId=73198>).

**Single window.** [ADII and ANP say the electronic delivery order (BAD), dematerialised in 2020, is exchanged among port actors over PortNet, the Guichet Unique du Commerce Extérieur platform (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.anp.org.ma/fra/Documents%20partages/Note%20d'accompagnement%20ADII%20et%20ANP%20(BAD%20e%CC%81lectronique).pdf>). [ADII says the PortNet foreign-trade single window carries ministry authorisations and conformity-check results to customs by EDI, generalised to all import offices (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douane.gov.ma/dms/loadDocument?application=communiquesPresse&documentId=73198>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/08/12/minister-morocco-to-use-ai-to-enhanced-public-services/>).
