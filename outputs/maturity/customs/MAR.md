# Morocco

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [PortNet's monthly report says it processed 191,388 Single Goods Declarations (DUM) in June 2026, 115,180 import and 76,208 export declarations](<https://en.7news.ma/portnet-processes-more-nearly-200000-customs-declarations-in-june/>). [Express TV reports that the BADR system is the backbone of Morocco's digital customs transactions, with an audit begun in January 2025](<https://en.expresstv.ma/2026/03/7-2-million-dirhams-for-upgrading-the-badr-system-and-enhancing-cybersecurity-for-customs/>). [Morocco's customs directorate launched international tender No. 6/2026, about MAD 7.2 million, to carry out the BADR audit recommendations and move it to a cloud-native microservices architecture, with bids opening 16 April 2026](<https://en.expresstv.ma/2026/03/7-2-million-dirhams-for-upgrading-the-badr-system-and-enhancing-cybersecurity-for-customs/>). [The ADII 2024 activity report says the number of customs declarations rose 7.1% to pass two million for the first time, processed through its BADR system](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>).

**Single window.** [The 7News report describes PortNet as Morocco's National Single Window for Foreign Trade, connecting customs, ports, banks and businesses, with 95,753 active users in June 2026](<https://en.7news.ma/portnet-processes-more-nearly-200000-customs-declarations-in-june/>). [ADII and ANP say the electronic delivery order (BAD), dematerialised in 2020, is exchanged among port actors over PortNet, the Guichet Unique du Commerce Extérieur platform (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.anp.org.ma/fra/Documents%20partages/Note%20d'accompagnement%20ADII%20et%20ANP%20(BAD%20e%CC%81lectronique).pdf>). [ADII says the PortNet foreign-trade single window carries ministry authorisations and conformity-check results to customs by EDI, generalised to all import offices (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douane.gov.ma/dms/loadDocument?application=communiquesPresse&documentId=73198>).
