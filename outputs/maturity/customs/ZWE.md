# Zimbabwe

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Finance Permanent Secretary George Guvamatanga said that through ASYCUDA World upgrades ZIMRA has automated customs declarations, reducing paperwork](<https://newziana.co.zw/gvt-pledges-to-modernise-customs-operations/>). [ZIMRA says it is upgrading ASYCUDA from version 4.3.2 to 4.4.0 in phases, the first rolling out on 18 August 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4043%3Apublic-notice-62-of-2024-notice-to-all-clearing-agents-importers-and-exporters>). [ZIMRA says its ASYCUDA system is at version 4.3.2 and the first upgrade phase migrates the current operational modules](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4043%3Apublic-notice-62-of-2024-notice-to-all-clearing-agents-importers-and-exporters>). [ZIMRA says the Zimbabwe Electronic Single Window was introduced in 2022 with UNCTAD support](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2253-streamlining-trade-in-zimbabwe-introducing-the-zimbabwe-electronic-single-window-system>). Its first implementation stage was due by end-September 2023, and ZIMRA is activating its system for integration with other agencies.

**Single window.** [The Finance Permanent Secretary said an Electronic Single Window System integrates regulatory agencies in customs clearance](<https://newziana.co.zw/gvt-pledges-to-modernise-customs-operations/>). Port health, the National Ozone Unit and the Radiation Protection Authority of Zimbabwe have subscribed to it. [ZIMRA's Public Notice 77 of 2024 says the window rolled out its Port Health and Radiation Protection Authority modules on 9 October 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4103%3Apublic-notice-77-of-2024-zesw-roll-out>). The rollout covered seven border posts and two airports. [ZIMRA says the window processed well over a thousand transactions in the first quarter of 2024 at Forbes and Robert Gabriel Mugabe Airport](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2300-zimbabwe-electronic-single-window-transforming-cross-border-trade>). Beitbridge was completed in May 2024, and Plumtree was due by end June 2024.
