# Zimbabwe

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Finance Permanent Secretary George Guvamatanga said that through ASYCUDA World upgrades ZIMRA has automated customs declarations, reducing paperwork](<https://newziana.co.zw/gvt-pledges-to-modernise-customs-operations/>). [ZIMRA says it is upgrading ASYCUDA from version 4.3.2 to 4.4.0 in phases, the first phase rolling out on 18 August 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4043%3Apublic-notice-62-of-2024-notice-to-all-clearing-agents-importers-and-exporters>). [ZIMRA says its ASYCUDA system is at version 4.3.2 and the first upgrade phase migrates the current operational modules](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4043%3Apublic-notice-62-of-2024-notice-to-all-clearing-agents-importers-and-exporters>). [ZIMRA says the Zimbabwe Electronic Single Window, introduced in 2022 with UNCTAD support, has a first implementation stage due by end-September 2023 and that ZIMRA is activating its system for integration with other agencies](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2253-streamlining-trade-in-zimbabwe-introducing-the-zimbabwe-electronic-single-window-system>).

**Single window.** [The Finance Permanent Secretary said an Electronic Single Window System integrates regulatory agencies in customs clearance, and port health, the National Ozone Unit and the Radiation Protection Authority of Zimbabwe have subscribed to it](<https://newziana.co.zw/gvt-pledges-to-modernise-customs-operations/>). [ZIMRA's Public Notice 77 of 2024 says the Zimbabwe Electronic Single Window rolled out its Port Health and Radiation Protection Authority modules on 9 October 2024 at seven border posts and two airports](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4103%3Apublic-notice-77-of-2024-zesw-roll-out>). [ZIMRA says the Zimbabwe Electronic Single Window processed well over a thousand transactions in the first quarter of 2024 at Forbes and Robert Gabriel Mugabe Airport, with Beitbridge completed in May 2024 and Plumtree due by end June 2024](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2300-zimbabwe-electronic-single-window-transforming-cross-border-trade>).
