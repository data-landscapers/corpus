# Angola

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [CIAT authors say Asycuda World automates the customs clearance of goods from the cargo manifest to release, and is migrating to ASY5](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>). [The WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement Database, citing Angola's progress report of 21 May 2026, says risk management is implemented with ASYCUDA World, the customs declarations system, with the new ASY5 system's risk module expected fully implemented by 2027](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/angola/technical-assistance-projects/article-7-4>). [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme reports Angola launched ASY5 operationally on 5 January 2026 in Lobito, with a shipping agent lodging a cargo manifest and a customs broker lodging the first export declaration in the live system](<https://asycuda.org/en/angola-marks-the-operational-launch-of-asy5-the-5th-generation-of-asycuda-customs-management-software/>). [The UN Global Survey on Digital and Sustainable Trade Facilitation 2025 round reports Angola's automated customs system as fully implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=AGO>). [AGT is migrating to ASYCUDA World version 5 (ASY5), per its chairman José Leiria, after TIMS in 2002 and ASYCUDA in 2007](<https://pti.ao/agt-anuncia-migracao-para-novo-sistema-de-processamento-aduaneiro/>). [AGT's customs department says Angola uses the Sistema Automatizado de Procedimentos Aduaneiros, run on ASYCUDA World, from cargo manifest submission to release from temporary storage, including payments and refunds](<https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw40/mziw/~edisp/minfin4320326.pdf>).
