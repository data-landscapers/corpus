# Angola

## Customs

**Automated customs.** The tax administration AGT says Angola runs customs [on ASYCUDA World, from the cargo manifest to release from storage](<https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw40/mziw/~edisp/minfin4320326.pdf>), with payments and refunds included. In December 2024 its chairman said AGT [was moving to ASYCUDA World version 5](<https://pti.ao/agt-anuncia-migracao-para-novo-sistema-de-processamento-aduaneiro/>). The new system [went live at Lobito on 5 January 2026](<https://asycuda.org/en/angola-marks-the-operational-launch-of-asy5-the-5th-generation-of-asycuda-customs-management-software/>), where a broker lodged the first export declaration. AGT [launched it at the Port of Luanda on 29 June 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/agt-expande-sistema-automatizado-ao-porto-de-luanda/>), after Lobito, Namibe and Cabinda. The 2025 UN trade facilitation survey [rates the automated customs system as fully implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=AGO>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [customs runs on ASYCUDA World and its successor](<https://asycuda.org/en/angola-marks-the-operational-launch-of-asy5-the-5th-generation-of-asycuda-customs-management-software/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [risk management runs in ASYCUDA World](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/angola/technical-assistance-projects/article-7-4>), Angola told the WTO in May 2026.
