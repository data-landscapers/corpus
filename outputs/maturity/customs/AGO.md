# Angola

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme reports Angola launched ASY5 operationally on 5 January 2026 in Lobito, with a shipping agent lodging a cargo manifest and a customs broker lodging the first export declaration in the live system](<https://asycuda.org/en/angola-marks-the-operational-launch-of-asy5-the-5th-generation-of-asycuda-customs-management-software/>). [AGT's customs department says Angola uses the Sistema Automatizado de Procedimentos Aduaneiros, run on ASYCUDA World, from cargo manifest submission to release from temporary storage, including payments and refunds](<https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw40/mziw/~edisp/minfin4320326.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
