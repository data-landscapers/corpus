# Madagascar

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Customs Code (art. 87, as amended by the 2023 finance law) requires declarations at customs offices equipped with computer systems to be lodged electronically, and art. 88 refers to an automated clearance system](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/code/titre-iv-operations-de-dedouanement/chapitre-premier-declaration-en-detail/section-i-caractere-obligatoire-de-la-declaration-en-detail/>). [Madagascar Customs says paperless clearance, using a Midac web application alongside Sydonia, is under test at the Mamory Ivato office (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/nouvelle-technologie/>). [Madagascar Customs says Sydonia++ is its automated customs system, installed centrally, and that TradeNet electronically registers declarations and is operational at 11 offices, near-total coverage of clearance operations (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/nouvelle-technologie/>). [The Customs Directorate clears goods on Sydonia World and launched a pilot to improve its electronic clearance in July 2024](<https://new.moov.mg/actualite/economie/88024-douanes-malagasy-lancement-du-projet-pilote-de-modernisation-du-systeme-de-dedouanement>). [In December 2024 customs and the Korea Customs Service launched a project to modernise the electronic clearance system, with full deployment expected in 2026](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1550>).

**Single window.** [Madagascar Customs says TradeNet interconnects declarants, customs, banks and the ministries and control agencies that issue permits and licences for clearance, reinforcing the existing single window (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/nouvelle-technologie/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/code/titre-iv-operations-de-dedouanement/chapitre-premier-declaration-en-detail/section-i-caractere-obligatoire-de-la-declaration-en-detail/>).
