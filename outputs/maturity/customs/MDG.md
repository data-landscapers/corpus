# Madagascar

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [GasyNet's chief executive says the TradeNet electronic single window, with its Douar customs module, is in service and has cut average clearance time to three days](<https://www.2424.mg/commerce-exterieur-gasynet-rapporte-un-delai-moyen-de-dedouanement-reduit-a-trois-jours-grace-au-guichet-unique-electronique-tradenet>). [Madagascar's customs administration says it analyses millions of records from SYDONIA, its customs management platform, and that automated controls have reduced clearance times](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/la-douane-malagasy-un-modele-regional-dans-lusage-de-la-donnee-et-de-lia/>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says the customs directorate and the Korea Customs Service officially launched on 4 December 2024 a project to modernise the electronic customs clearance system, with a data warehouse and full deployment expected in 2026](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1550>).

**Single window.** [GasyNet's chief executive says TradeNet connects customs (Douar), control ministries and agencies (Midac) and shipping lines (Delivery Order) in one electronic single window](<https://www.2424.mg/commerce-exterieur-gasynet-rapporte-un-delai-moyen-de-dedouanement-reduit-a-trois-jours-grace-au-guichet-unique-electronique-tradenet>). [Madagascar Customs says TradeNet interconnects declarants, customs, banks and the ministries and control agencies that issue permits and licences for clearance, reinforcing the existing single window (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/nouvelle-technologie/>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/code/titre-iv-operations-de-dedouanement/chapitre-premier-declaration-en-detail/section-i-caractere-obligatoire-de-la-declaration-en-detail/>).
