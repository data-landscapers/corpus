# Rwanda

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [UNCTAD describes ASYCUDA World as Rwanda's system for processing international trade transactions](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>). [UNCTAD says it will work with MINICOM and Rwanda Revenue Authority to optimize ASYCUDA World's selectivity/risk modules and activate import clearance](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>). [Rwanda introduced the ASYCUDA++ customs management system in 2004 and upgraded it to ASYCUDA World in 2012, halving customs clearance times, according to TradeMark East Africa (2012, before the three-year window)](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/home?cHash=8f3d2c2f531584fb89dc7425813e1fbc&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2418>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>).

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa reports that the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi are interconnected through the East African Community Single Customs Territory Centralised Platform, unveiled in revamped form on 27 January 2025](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>).
