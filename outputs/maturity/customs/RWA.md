# Rwanda

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [In 2024 UNCTAD described ASYCUDA World as Rwanda's system for processing international trade transactions](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>). [UNCTAD said it would work with MINICOM and the Rwanda Revenue Authority to optimise the system's selectivity and risk modules](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>). [The work would also activate import clearance](<https://unctad.org/project/amplifying-digital-opportunities-cross-border-trade>). [In December 2024 the revenue authority said Rwanda introduced ASYCUDA++ in 2004 and upgraded to ASYCUDA World in 2012](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/home?cHash=8f3d2c2f531584fb89dc7425813e1fbc&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2418>). [TradeMark East Africa said the upgrade halved customs clearance times](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/home?cHash=8f3d2c2f531584fb89dc7425813e1fbc&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2418>).

**Single window.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says it facilitates trade through the Rwanda electronic Single Window (ReSW)](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>). [It also names one-stop border posts and electronic cargo tracking](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa reported that a revamped East African Community Single Customs Territory Centralised Platform was unveiled on 27 January 2025](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>). [It interconnects the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>).
