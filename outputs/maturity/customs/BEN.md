# Benin

## Customs

**Automated customs.** In April 2025 Webb Fontaine said [Benin's customs system had moved from ASYCUDA to Webb Customs](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-fontaine-successfully-completes-national-rollout-of-customs-management-systems-in-benin>). It runs at all sea ports, airports and land border offices, and has processed over 100,000 declarations. By June 2025 [Webb Customs had replaced Sydonia World, with customs procedures fully automated](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-customs-the-new-digital-weapon-against-customs-fraud-in-benin>). At the Hilla-Condji border all formalities are completed online. In May 2025 [ECOWAS said its transit system, SIGMAT, was installed in the customs systems of every member state](<https://www.ecowas.int/launch-of-the-interconnected-transit-goods-management-system-sigmat-between-benin-and-nigeria/>) except Guinea-Bissau and Cabo Verde.

**Single window.** Benin's foreign trade single window, GUCE, has been [in service since 2018](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/>). It handles import declarations and issues agencies' authorisations, licences and certificates online. Customs says it was [handed to the government on 21 July 2023](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/>). In February 2025 Webb Fontaine said [Webb Customs is linked to the National Single Window, the Port Community System and electronic cargo tracking](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/benin-customs-management-milestones>).

**Exchange with partners.** ECOWAS says [Benin's customs was connected to SIGMAT, the community's goods-in-transit data exchange system, in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>). In November 2026 ECOWAS [called Benin one of the first member states to deploy SIGMAT](<https://www.ecowas.int/the-third-group-of-the-ecowas-trade-liberalisation-scheme-etls-task-force-holds-its-inaugural-meeting-on-5-and-6-november-2026-in-cotonou/>). TradeMark Africa handed IT equipment to Benin customs that month to reduce downtime on its national node.
