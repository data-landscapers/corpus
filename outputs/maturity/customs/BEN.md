# Benin

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Webb Fontaine says Webb Customs has replaced Sydonia World in Benin, with fully automated customs procedures, and at the Hilla-Condji border all formalities are completed online](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-customs-the-new-digital-weapon-against-customs-fraud-in-benin>). [ECOWAS said its Interconnected System for the Management of Goods in Transit (SIGMAT) is installed in the customs IT systems of every ECOWAS member state except Guinea-Bissau and Cabo Verde, and launched its deployment between Benin and Nigeria at Seme-Kraké](<https://www.ecowas.int/launch-of-the-interconnected-transit-goods-management-system-sigmat-between-benin-and-nigeria/>).

**Single window.** [In February 2025 Webb Fontaine said Webb Customs interconnects trade platforms including the National Single Window, the Port Community System and electronic cargo tracking](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/benin-customs-management-milestones>). [Benin Customs says the GUCE single window, operational since 2018, handles import declarations with online issue of agencies' authorisations, licences and certificates, and was officially handed to government on 21 July 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/>). [Benin Customs says GUCE is the single platform for international trade operations, connecting all administrations and technical directorates, with more than 2,000 users (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/guce-le-benin-franchit-le-cap-du-milliard-en-transaction-commerciale-effectuee-sur-le-guce-a-travers-le-module-e-payement/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Benin was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
