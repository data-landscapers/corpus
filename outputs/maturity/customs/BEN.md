# Benin

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Webb Fontaine says it completed migration of Benin's national Customs Management System from ASYCUDA to Webb Customs, fully operational at all sea ports, airports and land border offices, with over 100,000 declarations processed](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-fontaine-successfully-completes-national-rollout-of-customs-management-systems-in-benin>). [Benin Customs says the GUCE lets it process customs declarations and electronic payment of import and export declarations (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/>). [Benin Customs says clearance at the port of Cotonou and at land borders now runs through GUCE, with Hilacondji clearance dematerialised and cut from 18 steps to 10 (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/guce-le-benin-franchit-le-cap-du-milliard-en-transaction-commerciale-effectuee-sur-le-guce-a-travers-le-module-e-payement/>).

**Single window.** [In February 2025 Webb Fontaine said Webb Customs interconnects trade platforms including the National Single Window, the Port Community System and electronic cargo tracking](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/benin-customs-management-milestones>). [Benin Customs says the GUCE single window, operational since 2018, handles import declarations with online issue of agencies' authorisations, licences and certificates, and was officially handed to government on 21 July 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/en/remise-officielle-du-guce-benin-au-gouvernement/>). [Benin Customs says GUCE is the single platform for international trade operations, connecting all administrations and technical directorates, with more than 2,000 users (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://douanes.gouv.bj/guce-le-benin-franchit-le-cap-du-milliard-en-transaction-commerciale-effectuee-sur-le-guce-a-travers-le-module-e-payement/>).
