# Tanzania

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Revenue Authority's delegation head says Tansis is the software used for declaring and clearing goods](<https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2617-l-interfacage-des-systemes-de-suivi-electronique-des-cargos-pour-la-securite-et-la-surete-des-biens-ou-des-marchandises>). [Its data go straight to the TECTS and RECTS cargo tracking systems](<https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2617-l-interfacage-des-systemes-de-suivi-electronique-des-cargos-pour-la-securite-et-la-surete-des-biens-ou-des-marchandises>). In early 2025 [the Revenue Authority rolled out a new version of its customs system, TANCIS, with clearance requests filed online](<https://dailynews.co.tz/port-customs-system-upgrade-speed-up-cargo-clearance/>), the ports authority said.

**Single window.** [e-GA said in December 2023 that the Tanzania Electronic Single Window System lets traders lodge data once for all border agencies](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). [Its first phase was in use at Dar es Salaam port and Julius Nyerere airport by all 1,496 clearing agents](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). [The system connects traders, customs and regulators on the mainland](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tra-rolls-out-zanzibar-single-window-to-streamline-trade-and-customs-procedures>). On 2 June 2026 [the Revenue Authority launched the Zanzibar Single Window System](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tra-rolls-out-zanzibar-single-window-to-streamline-trade-and-customs-procedures>), bringing several agencies under one gateway.

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa says the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi are linked](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>). [They connect through the EAC Single Customs Territory platform, as does Tanzania's ports authority](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>).
