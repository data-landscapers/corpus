# Tanzania

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Tanzania Revenue Authority's delegation head says Tansis is the software used in Tanzania for declaring and clearing goods, with its data shared directly to the TECTS and RECTS cargo tracking systems](<https://www.obr.bi/index.php/en/actualites/2617-l-interfacage-des-systemes-de-suivi-electronique-des-cargos-pour-la-securite-et-la-surete-des-biens-ou-des-marchandises>). [Daily News reports Tanzania Ports Authority's director of ICT saying the Tanzania Revenue Authority rolled out a new version of its customs system TANCIS in early 2025, with clearance requests filed online](<https://dailynews.co.tz/port-customs-system-upgrade-speed-up-cargo-clearance/>).

**Single window.** [The article says the Tanzania Electronic Single Window System has already been established on Mainland Tanzania to connect traders, customs authorities and regulatory agencies](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tra-rolls-out-zanzibar-single-window-to-streamline-trade-and-customs-procedures>). [The Tanzania Revenue Authority launched the Zanzibar Single Window System at a ceremony on 2 June 2026, a digital platform for customs clearance bringing various government agencies under one electronic gateway](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tra-rolls-out-zanzibar-single-window-to-streamline-trade-and-customs-procedures>).

**Exchange with partners.** [TradeMark Africa reports that the customs systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda and Burundi are interconnected through the East African Community Single Customs Territory Centralised Platform, with Tanzania's ports authority also connected](<https://trademarkafrica.com/one-stop-border-posts-save-eac-over-63m-annually/>).
