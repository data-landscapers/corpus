# Mauritania

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Mauritania's customs guide to clearance procedures for 2026 says import and export declarations are registered and printed in SYDONIA World, which also runs the risk circuits, the liquidation of duties and the payment receipts](<https://www.douanes.mr/uploads/files/P2026%20Guide%20de%20proc%C3%A9dures%20de%20d%C3%A9douanement%20des%20marchandises%20%C3%A0%20l'importation%20et%20%C3%A0%20l%E2%80%99exportation.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the system itself was not examined.

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the clearance guide given above](<https://www.douanes.mr/uploads/files/P2026%20Guide%20de%20proc%C3%A9dures%20de%20d%C3%A9douanement%20des%20marchandises%20%C3%A0%20l'importation%20et%20%C3%A0%20l%E2%80%99exportation.pdf>).
