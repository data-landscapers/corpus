# Gabon

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The DGDDI announced the e-DIEBS platform, attached to Sydonia World for centralised handling of import and export declarations, to start on 30 March 2026](<https://novaafriquemedia.com/?p=1616>). [The Libreville customs region was the pilot site, with a three-month transitional period and other regional directorates to follow](<https://novaafriquemedia.com/?p=1616>). [Gabonmediatime reports the migration of customs to SYDONIA World is being completed, with a backup site for continuity](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-vers-une-modernisation-des-douanes-pour-faciliter-le-commerce-et-le-soutien-aux-pme/>). [It says the system analyses declarations faster and has proved itself](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-vers-une-modernisation-des-douanes-pour-faciliter-le-commerce-et-le-soutien-aux-pme/>). [The DGDDI names SYDONIAWorld as its clearance system](<https://douanes.ga/medias/actualites/140/la-dgddi-accueille-une-mission-afritac-centre-fmi->). [An IMF AFRITAC Centre mission of 11 to 18 December 2024 was to help interconnect it with other CEMAC customs administrations](<https://douanes.ga/medias/actualites/140/la-dgddi-accueille-une-mission-afritac-centre-fmi->).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 the DGDDI said the e-DIEBS platform would centralise handling of import and export declarations](<https://novaafriquemedia.com/?p=1616>).
