# Gabon

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The DGDDI announced the e-DIEBS platform, attached to Sydonia World for centralised handling of import and export declarations, to start on 30 March 2026 with the Libreville customs region as pilot site and a three-month transitional period, other regional directorates to be integrated progressively](<https://novaafriquemedia.com/?p=1616>). [Gabonmediatime reports the migration of Gabon's customs to SYDONIA World is being completed, a system it says analyses declarations faster and has proved itself, with a backup site for continuity](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-vers-une-modernisation-des-douanes-pour-faciliter-le-commerce-et-le-soutien-aux-pme/>). [The Gabon customs directorate DGDDI refers to its clearance system SYDONIAWorld, which an IMF AFRITAC Centre mission of 11 to 18 December 2024 is to help interconnect with other CEMAC customs administrations](<https://douanes.ga/medias/actualites/140/la-dgddi-accueille-une-mission-afritac-centre-fmi->).
