# Liberia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Deputy Commerce Minister Wilmot A. Reeves said an upgrade of ASYCUDA World is among reforms under the Liberia Investment, Finance and Trade Project](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-digital-collateral-registry-business-credit/>). [The LRA Corporate Strategic Plan 2025-2029 says ASYCUDA has played a pivotal role in augmenting customs operations and tables its status as extended to Freeport of Liberia, RIA and Buchanan](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [The plan tables an ASYCUDA extension project with expected result of extension to five rural ports](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [LRA Commissioner General James Dorbor Jallah said ASYCUDA has been deployed to 11 of 17 Customs Business Offices](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/lra-unveils-new-strategic-plan-as-liberia-moves-toward-historic-us1-billion-revenue-mark/>).

**Single window.** [The Ministry of Commerce and Industry issued a World Bank-financed tender on 9 June 2026 for the ICT infrastructure of the National Single Window System, to be shared by the LRA, MOA, MOCI and LISA](<https://www.emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/Special%20Procurement%20Notice%20for%20ICT%20Infrastructure%20for%20the%20NSW.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says it launched deployment of SIGMAT between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia in Monrovia on 31 October 2025, exchanging electronic transit messages through interconnection of their national customs systems](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>). [The Liberia Revenue Authority says ECOWAS launched SIGMAT in Monrovia in late October 2025, a customs data exchange system connecting national customs systems of ECOWAS members, with Liberia, Cote d'Ivoire and Guinea forming the latest corridor](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/liberia-joins-regional-digital-trade-drive-as-ecowas-launches-sigmat-corridor/>).
