# Liberia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The LRA Corporate Strategic Plan 2025-2029 says ASYCUDA has played a pivotal role in augmenting customs operations and tables its status as extended to Freeport of Liberia, RIA and Buchanan](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [LRA Commissioner General James Dorbor Jallah said in August 2026 that ASYCUDA has been deployed to 11 of 17 Customs Business Offices, with solar power and connectivity being introduced at rollout locations outside Monrovia](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/lra-unveils-new-strategic-plan-as-liberia-moves-toward-historic-us1-billion-revenue-mark/>). [Deputy Commerce Minister Wilmot A. Reeves said an upgrade of ASYCUDA World is among reforms under the Liberia Investment, Finance and Trade Project](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-digital-collateral-registry-business-credit/>).

**Single window.** [The Ministry of Commerce and Industry issued a World Bank-financed tender on 9 June 2026 for the ICT infrastructure of the National Single Window System, to be shared by the LRA, MOA, MOCI and LISA](<https://www.emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/Special%20Procurement%20Notice%20for%20ICT%20Infrastructure%20for%20the%20NSW.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS launched SIGMAT between Cote d'Ivoire, Guinea and Liberia in Monrovia on 31 October 2025, a system that exchanges electronic transit messages between the countries' national customs systems](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-strengthens-intra-regional-trade-with-the-launch-of-sigmat-between-cote-divoire-guinea-and-liberia/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service; the national one is still being procured.
