# Niger

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [In March 2026 the customs director general said duty receipts are generated automatically in SYDONIA. SYDONIA is the automated customs system](<https://www.lesahel.org/point-de-presse-a-la-direction-generale-des-douanes-les-operations-de-paiement-electronique-en-banque-des-droits-et-taxes-liquides-en-douanes-reprennent-le-1er-avril-2026/>). [Users print from it the receipt that releases goods](<https://www.lesahel.org/point-de-presse-a-la-direction-generale-des-douanes-les-operations-de-paiement-electronique-en-banque-des-droits-et-taxes-liquides-en-douanes-reprennent-le-1er-avril-2026/>). [The statistics institute's 2024 trade bulletin says customs enters declarations and receipts in SYDONIA](<https://www.stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/annuaire_bulletin/bulletin/Bulletin_des_Statistiques_annuelles_definitives_2024_VF.pdf>). [In Lomé in September 2024 the customs heads of the Sahel states and Togo reviewed linking their customs systems](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>). [The instruments for it were signed at Niamey that July](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>).

**Single window.** [Webb Fontaine says the GUCE-Niger single window was launched in 2022](<https://webbfontaine.com/fr/success-stories-niger-is-transforming-foreign-trade-procedures-with-webb-single-window>). [It connects Customs, the finance, trade, agriculture, livestock and health ministries and the chamber of commerce](<https://webbfontaine.com/fr/success-stories-niger-is-transforming-foreign-trade-procedures-with-webb-single-window>). [They share one platform with banks and trade operators](<https://webbfontaine.com/fr/success-stories-niger-is-transforming-foreign-trade-procedures-with-webb-single-window>). [The Minister of Economy and Finance said the virtual transaction file runs through GUCE-Niger, the foreign trade single window](<https://www.lesahel.org/reformes-du-systeme-de-gestion-du-commerce-exterieur-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-dossier-virtuel-de-transaction-dvt/>). [It passes between operators and the administrations concerned](<https://www.lesahel.org/reformes-du-systeme-de-gestion-du-commerce-exterieur-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-dossier-virtuel-de-transaction-dvt/>). [Its control joined the processing of operations from 1 July 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/reformes-du-systeme-de-gestion-du-commerce-exterieur-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-dossier-virtuel-de-transaction-dvt/>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Niger was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
