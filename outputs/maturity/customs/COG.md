# Congo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [L'Economie reports that from 3 August 2026 importers declare on Sydonia and pay customs duties online via Fouta, doing declaration, payment and receipt online in under five minutes, per ministry IT director Elenga Ngaporo Okina](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Single window.** [The Congolese finance ministry says the Guichet unique des operations transfrontalieres (GUOT), with 78 staff, provides IT services for data exchange in dematerialised foreign-trade procedures and ensures interoperability of all trade actors' systems across ports, airports and land borders](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/visite-du-guichet-unique-des-op%C3%A9rations-transfrontali%C3%A8res-guot>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Fouta](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).
