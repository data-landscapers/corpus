# Somalia

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The finance ministry's 2024 to 2027 customs strategic plan says Mogadishu Customs implemented SOMCAS in February 2022](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somali%20Customs%20Strategic%20Plan2024-2027.pdf>). [SOMCAS runs alongside the current Port Customs Management Information System (PCMIS)](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somali%20Customs%20Strategic%20Plan2024-2027.pdf>). [In August 2025 the finance minister and the UK ambassador reviewed progress on digital customs systems at Mogadishu Port](<https://shabellemedia.com/somalia-finance-minister-uk-ambassador-visit-mogadishu-port-to-review-revenue-reform-efforts/>). [The work centres on the UK-supported SOMCAS online platform, Shabelle Media reported](<https://shabellemedia.com/somalia-finance-minister-uk-ambassador-visit-mogadishu-port-to-review-revenue-reform-efforts/>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the finance ministry said Mogadishu Customs runs SOMCAS alongside the port's PCMIS](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somali%20Customs%20Strategic%20Plan2024-2027.pdf>).
