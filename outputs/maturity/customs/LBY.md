# Libya

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Libya's Customs Authority announced on 21 August 2025 the full launch of the ASYCUDA customs system early the following month, after a two-week expert visit for the Customs Procedures Automation Project reviewed infrastructure readiness and implementation progress](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>). [The UN Global Survey 2025 records e-submission of customs declarations in Libya as not implemented, and the automated customs system and e-single window as at the planning stage](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=LBY>). [The IMF METAC says its August-September 2024 mission helped Libya Customs develop a National Standard Operating Procedure documenting the new automated clearing process using ASYCUDA World](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>). [Libya Herald reports the Customs Authority approved a pilot of an electronic customs declaration portal at Ras Jadir and the Khoms sea port, as a first phase to computerise all ports (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/09/customs-authority-implements-digital-transformation-through-pilot-project-for-customs-declaration-e-portal>). [An IMF METAC review says an ASYCUDA World 4.3.3 prototype is piloted only at the Port of Tripoli, used inconsistently for IM4 import declarations (four registered in May 2022, twelve in April) and not the primary declaration system (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2022/english/1lbyea2022001.pdf>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the 2024 clearance procedure given above](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 launch announcement given above](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>).
