# Libya

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The IMF METAC says its August-September 2024 mission helped Libya Customs develop a National Standard Operating Procedure documenting the new automated clearing process using ASYCUDA World](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>). [Libya's Customs Authority announced on 21 August 2025 the full launch of the ASYCUDA customs system early the following month, after a two-week expert visit for the Customs Procedures Automation Project reviewed infrastructure readiness and implementation progress](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>). [Libya Herald reports the Customs Authority approved a pilot of an electronic customs declaration portal at Ras Jadir and the Khoms sea port, as a first phase to computerise all ports (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/09/customs-authority-implements-digital-transformation-through-pilot-project-for-customs-declaration-e-portal>).
