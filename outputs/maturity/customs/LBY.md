# Libya

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Libya's Customs Authority announced on 21 August 2025 the full launch of the ASYCUDA customs system early the following month, after a two-week expert visit for the Customs Procedures Automation Project reviewed infrastructure readiness and implementation progress](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>). [An IMF METAC mission in August and September 2024 helped Libya Customs write a National Standard Operating Procedure documenting the new automated clearing process using ASYCUDA World](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>). [In September 2023 the Customs Authority approved a pilot of an electronic customs declaration portal at Ras Jadir and the Khoms sea port](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/09/customs-authority-implements-digital-transformation-through-pilot-project-for-customs-declaration-e-portal>), and [in 2022 the IMF found an ASYCUDA World prototype piloted only at the Port of Tripoli, not the primary declaration system](<https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2022/english/1lbyea2022001.pdf>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the 2024 clearance procedure given above](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 launch announcement given above](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 shows declarations lodged and processed in ASYCUDA World at a main port or border post.
