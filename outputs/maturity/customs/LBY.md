# Libya

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Libya's Customs Authority announced on 21 August 2025 the full launch of the ASYCUDA customs system early the following month](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>). [A two-week expert visit for the Customs Procedures Automation Project had reviewed infrastructure readiness and implementation progress](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>). [The UN Global Survey 2025 records e-submission of customs declarations in Libya as not implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=LBY>). [It records the automated customs system and e-single window as at the planning stage](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=LBY>). [The IMF METAC says its August-September 2024 mission helped Libya Customs develop a National Standard Operating Procedure for the new automated clearing process using ASYCUDA World](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>). [In September 2023 Libya Herald reported the Customs Authority approved a pilot of an electronic customs declaration portal at Ras Jadir and the Khoms sea port](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/09/customs-authority-implements-digital-transformation-through-pilot-project-for-customs-declaration-e-portal>). [It was a first phase to computerise all ports](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/09/customs-authority-implements-digital-transformation-through-pilot-project-for-customs-declaration-e-portal>). [In June 2022 an IMF METAC review said an ASYCUDA World 4.3.3 prototype was piloted only at the Port of Tripoli](<https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2022/english/1lbyea2022001.pdf>). [It was used inconsistently for IM4 import declarations, four in May 2022 and twelve in April, and was not the primary declaration system](<https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2022/english/1lbyea2022001.pdf>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the 2024 clearance procedure given above](<https://www.imfmetac.org/content/dam/METAC/Countries-Activities/FY25/Libya.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 launch announcement given above](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/08/customs-authority-announces-full-launch-of-asycuda-system-next-month>).
