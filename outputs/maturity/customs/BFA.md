# Burkina Faso

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Togolese Revenue Office announced the pilot phase of the interconnection of the Togo and Mali customs systems via Burkina Faso](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). It runs on the Lome-Bamako corridor and was operational from 10 March 2025. [Sylvie 2 improves interfacing with SYDONIA, the automated customs system, and facilitates customs clearance operations, per the customs director general](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>). [Customs directors general of the Sahel Confederation (AES) states and Togo reviewed the interconnection of their customs IT systems in Lome](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>). Instruments signed at Niamey on 23 July 2024 set its legal framework.

**Single window.** [The Direction generale des douanes and SOGESY launched Sylvie 2 on 17 February 2025](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>). It is the electronic single window for import and export clearance, running since 2016. It collects documents from technical administrations and private bodies.

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Burkina Faso was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>). The connection is for its customs administration.
