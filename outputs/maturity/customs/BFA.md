# Burkina Faso

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Sylvie 2 improves interfacing with SYDONIA, the automated customs system, and facilitates customs clearance operations, per the customs director general](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>). [L'Economiste du Faso reports Burkina Faso customs launched Sydonia World online on 13 November 2019, after use at pilot sites since 2 February 2015 (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://leconomistedufaso.com/2019/11/25/dedouanement-en-ligne-une-realite-avec-sydonia-world/>). [Togo's Office Togolais des Recettes says transit declarations from Lome to Bingo Burkina are registered on Togo's Sydonia World since 11 May 2018 (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/677-modernisation-des-procedures-douanieres-et-renforcement-de-la-cooperation-des-administrations-douanieres-du-togo-et-du-burkina-faso.html>).

**Single window.** [The Direction generale des douanes and SOGESY launched Sylvie 2 on 17 February 2025, the electronic single window for import and export clearance, running since 2016 and collecting documents from technical administrations and private bodies](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source held since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>).
