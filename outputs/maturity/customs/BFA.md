# Burkina Faso

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Togolese Revenue Office announced the pilot phase of the interconnection of the Togo and Mali customs systems via Burkina Faso on the Lome-Bamako corridor, operational from 10 March 2025](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). [Sylvie 2 improves interfacing with SYDONIA, the automated customs system, and facilitates customs clearance operations, per the customs director general](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>). [Customs directors general of the Sahel Confederation (AES) states and Togo reviewed in Lome the interconnection of their customs IT systems, under instruments signed at Niamey on 23 July 2024 that set the legal framework for it](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>).

**Single window.** [The Direction generale des douanes and SOGESY launched Sylvie 2 on 17 February 2025, the electronic single window for import and export clearance, running since 2016 and collecting documents from technical administrations and private bodies](<https://jumelages-partenariats.com/actualites.php?n=22833>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Burkina Faso was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
