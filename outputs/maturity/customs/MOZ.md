# Mozambique

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Centro de Integridade Pública says the current Janela Unica Electronica (electronic single window), implemented by MCNet under a concession, is in operation](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Ajuste-Directo-da-JUE-Estaria-o-Governo-a-Legitimar-Carteis-que-Lesam-o-Estado-2.pdf>). [It reports frequent breakdowns and hybrid digital and paper procedures](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Ajuste-Directo-da-JUE-Estaria-o-Governo-a-Legitimar-Carteis-que-Lesam-o-Estado-2.pdf>). [In March 2024 the economy and finance minister told parliament that MCNET holds the concession for the single electronic customs clearance system](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-state-collected-2-billion-meticais-from-kudumba-since-2006-carta/>). [He said the Janela Unica Electronica has operated since 2010](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-state-collected-2-billion-meticais-from-kudumba-since-2006-carta/>). [MCNet said the JUE had submitted and cleared 100,000 declarations between September 2011 and December 2012, at 52 customs offices, terminals and border posts](<https://jue.mcnet.co.mz/mcnet/portal/news/243/link>).

**Single window.** [In July 2017 customs official Abishek Dewan said the single window, introduced in 2011 and phased in, operated across the country as a centralised electronic trade platform](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/single-window-speeds-up-clearance-at-moz-borders>). [He said clearance was down to eight steps](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/single-window-speeds-up-clearance-at-moz-borders>). [In December 2012 MCNet said commercial banks took part through the JUE in collecting USD 450 million in customs duties](<https://jue.mcnet.co.mz/mcnet/portal/news/243/link>). [Trained users included clearing agents, shipping and port company staff and bank staff](<https://jue.mcnet.co.mz/mcnet/portal/news/243/link>).
