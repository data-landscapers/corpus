# Mozambique

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [The Centro de Integridade Pública says the current Janela Unica Electronica (electronic single window), implemented by MCNet under a concession, is in operation, though with frequent breakdowns and hybrid digital and paper procedures](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Ajuste-Directo-da-JUE-Estaria-o-Governo-a-Legitimar-Carteis-que-Lesam-o-Estado-2.pdf>). [Mozambique's economy and finance minister told parliament in March 2024 that MCNET is concessionaire of the single electronic system for customs clearance (Janela Unica Electronica), operating since 2010](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-state-collected-2-billion-meticais-from-kudumba-since-2006-carta/>). [MCNet says the JUE had by December 2012 submitted and cleared 100,000 declarations since starting in September 2011, at 52 customs offices, terminals and border posts (2012, before the three-year window)](<https://jue.mcnet.co.mz/mcnet/portal/news/243/link>).

**Single window.** [Mozambique customs official Abishek Dewan says the single window, introduced in 2011 and phased in, now operates across the country as a centralised electronic trade platform, with clearance down to eight steps (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/single-window-speeds-up-clearance-at-moz-borders>). [MCNet says commercial banks take part through the JUE in collecting USD 450 million in customs duties, and trained users include clearing agents, shipping and port company staff and bank staff (2012, before the three-year window)](<https://jue.mcnet.co.mz/mcnet/portal/news/243/link>).
