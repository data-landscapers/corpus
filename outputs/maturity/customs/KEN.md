# Kenya

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [President Ruto said AI-powered risk engines were piloted in KRA's Customs Department alongside re-engineering of its processes](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2329-president-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliancepresident-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliance>). [KRA Commissioner-General Humphrey Wattanga says KRA is upgrading and modernising iCMS from a back-end perspective, and is considering a move to the cloud](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>). [Business Daily reports the Integrated Customs Management System (iCMS), adopted in October 2021, is Kenya's sole clearance system, where traders lodge import and export documents at Mombasa, JKIA and inland depots](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>). [KenTrade says it achieved full integration with the Kenya Revenue Authority's Integrated Customs Management System (iCMS), giving full automation of the cargo release process](<https://kentrade.go.ke/site/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Revised-KenTrade-Strategic-Plan-20232024-To-20272028.pdf>).

**Single window.** [Business Daily says iCMS involves submitting export or import documents into a single-window system](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>). [KenTrade says its National Electronic Single Window System, a single entry point for lodging trade documents and paying fees, was upgraded and commissioned on 20 August 2021 with 43 stakeholder institutions onboarded (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://kentrade.go.ke/site/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Revised-KenTrade-Strategic-Plan-20232024-To-20272028.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** [Business Daily says under the EAC single customs territory, the importing country's revenue authority submits import documents electronically and the exporting country's authority issues a road manifest against them](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>).
