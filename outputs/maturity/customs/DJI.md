# Djibouti

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [In its 2025 to 2029 strategic plan, Djibouti Customs says it processed 205,678 declarations in 2023](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>). [It routed transit declarations to yellow, blue, green and red lanes](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>). [It planned to extend selectivity to other regimes in 2025](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>).

**Single window.** No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [in 2023 customs routed transit declarations through selectivity lanes](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>).

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [in 2023 transit declarations were among the 205,678 that customs processed](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Djibouti Port Community System (DPCS)](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-216-pr-mdeni-portant-instauration-de-la-plateforme-djibouti-port-community-system/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service.
