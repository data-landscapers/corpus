# Djibouti

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [Djibouti Customs says it processed 205,678 declarations in 2023, of which transit declarations were routed to yellow, blue, green and red lanes, with selectivity to be extended to other regimes in 2025](<https://douanes.gouv.dj/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Plan-Strategique-Djibouti_2025-29_Short_Web_Page.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
