# Togo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [An OTR notice says customs declarations (DDU) are entered in SYDONIA, where a blocking field now requires the certificate of origin to be uploaded for preferential-tariff codes](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1489-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-013-2025-otr-cg-cddi-relatif-a-la-creation-d-un-pour-champ-bloquant-pour-toute-utilisation-d-un-code-additionnel-donnant-droit-aux-tarifs-preferentiels-dans-sydonia.html>). [The Togolese Revenue Office notice requires Togo-to-Mali transit operations via Burkina Faso to be generated in SYDONIA World](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). [Customs directors general of the Sahel Confederation (AES) states and Togo reviewed in Lome the interconnection of their customs IT systems, under instruments signed at Niamey on 23 July 2024 that set the legal framework for it](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>).

**Single window.** No source held since October 2023 says a trade single window is in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1489-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-013-2025-otr-cg-cddi-relatif-a-la-creation-d-un-pour-champ-bloquant-pour-toute-utilisation-d-un-code-additionnel-donnant-droit-aux-tarifs-preferentiels-dans-sydonia.html>).

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Togo was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
