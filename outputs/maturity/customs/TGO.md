# Togo

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [A notice from the Togolese Revenue Office (OTR) says customs declarations (DDU) are entered in SYDONIA](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1489-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-013-2025-otr-cg-cddi-relatif-a-la-creation-d-un-pour-champ-bloquant-pour-toute-utilisation-d-un-code-additionnel-donnant-droit-aux-tarifs-preferentiels-dans-sydonia.html>). A blocking field there now requires the certificate of origin to be uploaded for preferential-tariff codes. [The Togolese Revenue Office notice requires Togo-to-Mali transit operations via Burkina Faso to be generated in SYDONIA World](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>). [Customs directors general of the Sahel Confederation (AES) states and Togo reviewed the interconnection of their customs IT systems in Lome](<https://douanes.gouv.ml/node/418>). They did so under instruments signed at Niamey on 23 July 2024, which set the legal framework for it.

**Single window.** From December 2025 [free-zone import manifests are processed through the foreign-trade single window, GUCE](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.com/gouvernance-economique/1112-11403-zone-franche-un-nouveau-code-pour-renforcer-le-controle-des-importations>). [GUCE handles foreign-trade formalities and GUFORD the port's](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/logistique/1911-20689-port-de-lome-un-nouveau-guichet-numerique-va-remplacer-les-plateformes-guce-et-guford>). An order of 14 October 2026 will replace both with one port system. No go-live date is set.

**Exchange with partners.** [ECOWAS says Togo was connected to the SIGMAT goods-in-transit data exchange system for its customs administration in July 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/for-better-knowledge-and-understanding-of-the-interconnected-system-for-the-management-of-goods-in-transit-sigmat/>).
