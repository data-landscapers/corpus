# Mauritius

## Customs

**Automated customs.** [A WCO Smart Customs Project mission of 12-16 January 2026 found the Mauritius Revenue Authority hosts its Customs Management System on a private cloud](<https://scp.wcoomd.org/study-mission-cloud-computing-hosted-mauritius-customs>). The system supports risk management and border control. [The authority's time release study says bills of entry lodged from 10 July to 6 August 2023 were processed in that system](<https://www.mra.mu/download/MauritiusTRS.pdf>).

**Single window.** [The authority's 2023 time release study says the TradeLink national single window was fully rolled out](<https://www.mra.mu/download/MauritiusTRS.pdf>). The study used it to measure the time other government agencies take on permits and clearances. The Customs Management System checks their clearances.

**Exchange with partners.** No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data electronically with another country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/download/MauritiusTRS.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [MauPass](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>); [Cargo Community System (CCS)](<https://www.mra.mu/download/MauritiusTRS.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says customs exchanges data through a system open to its whole regional community.
