# Eswatini

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Eswatini's [National Payments System Act, 2023](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/NATIONAL-PAYMENT-SYSTEM-ACT-2023.pdf>) regulates payment systems and payment service providers and repealed a 2011 clearing and settlement act. The central bank said in November 2025 that [licensing runs under it now](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/13/how-a-small-country-is-tackling-big-payments-challenges/>) and that it was [finalising regulations](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/13/how-a-small-country-is-tackling-big-payments-challenges/>) on timelines and requirements.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [A new mobile money provider must go through the central bank's licensing process](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/13/how-a-small-country-is-tackling-big-payments-challenges/>); three mobile money operators, MTN MoMo and Instacash among them, are in the interoperable system. At the launch of fast payments on the Eswatini Payment Switch on 11 December 2024 the Governor said [participation is mandated for all payment service providers](<https://www.bis.org/review/r250114f.htm>) offering local payments, with the remaining participants expected to be live by the end of January 2025. [A central bank directive binding all licensed payment service providers from 31 March 2025](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf>) gives them until 31 March 2027 to move low-value transfers within the Common Monetary Area to the TCIB scheme.
