# Gabon

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Gabon is bound by [CEMAC regulation n°04/18 of 21 December 2018 on payment services](<https://droitmediasfinance.com/index.php/actualites/droit-tech-fintech/1262-cemac-transferts-de-fonds-via-les-fintechs-une-nouvelle-instruction-beac-precise-les-diligences-relatives-au-traitement-et-suivi-des-prefinancements-des-operations-de-remittance-entrante-executees-par-le-canal-des-etablissements-de-paiement>), which a BEAC instruction of 5 May 2026 supplements. A national text is in preparation: the Council of Ministers [adopted a draft ordinance on digital payments](<https://gouvernement.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Communique-final-publiee-du-Conseil-du-29-janvier-2026-1.pdf>) on 29 January 2026, and on 22 May the digital economy minister [defended the bill ratifying it](<https://www.focusgroupemedia.com/gabon-modernisation-de-letat-le-cadre-juridique-des-paiements-digitaux-en-examen-a-lassemblee-nationale/>) before the National Assembly's finance committee.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The regional regulation governs payment institutions](<https://droitmediasfinance.com/index.php/actualites/droit-tech-fintech/1262-cemac-transferts-de-fonds-via-les-fintechs-une-nouvelle-instruction-beac-precise-les-diligences-relatives-au-traitement-et-suivi-des-prefinancements-des-operations-de-remittance-entrante-executees-par-le-canal-des-etablissements-de-paiement>) distinct from credit institutions, which must report monthly to the central bank. COBAC's 2025 report [names Airtel Money Gabon and Clikpay Gabon](<https://ecomatin.net/cemac-le-mobile-money-brasse-44-000-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-et-genere-47-milliards-de-benefices-aux-operateurs>) in Gabon's payment institution sector, which earned FCFA 24.7 billion in 2025. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other.

***Also on file***. [GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
