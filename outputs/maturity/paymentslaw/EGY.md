# Egypt

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Central Bank of Egypt issued [Rules for Licensing and Registration of Payment System Operators and Payment Service Providers](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>) in June 2025, with transitional provisions for existing payment institutions. In September 2025 it issued [regulations on governance, internal control and fit and proper criteria](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>), with which operators and providers must comply within a year.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2025 licensing rules](<https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news/2025/09/01/13/10/payment-system-operators-and-payment-service-providers>) apply to payment system operators and payment service providers. It is not said whether a company that is not a bank can be licensed under them, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Egypt therefore answers the first two questions only.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the law or rules in force license payment providers or e-money issuers that are not banks, or names one licensed under them.
