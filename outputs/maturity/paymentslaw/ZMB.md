# Zambia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Zambia's [National Payment Systems Act](<https://www.boz.zm/national-payment-systems-act.htm>) was enacted by parliament on 12 April 2007, to give legal backing to the operation of payment mechanisms in Zambia and to empower the Bank of Zambia to regulate them. Under it and the Bank of Zambia Act 2022 the bank [issued directives on e-money charges, binding on e-money institutions, banks and financial institutions from 2 August 2024, that prohibit some fees and cap others](<https://regalert.today/document/directives-on-unwarranted-charges-and-fees-on-electronic-money-services-bca3f339-2d82-4256-967c-905e4917c5f1>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2007 Act](<https://www.boz.zm/national-payment-systems-act.htm>) empowers the Bank of Zambia to regulate payment mechanisms. It is not said whether companies that are not banks can be licensed under it, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Zambia therefore answers the first two questions only: a law in force, with its reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated.
