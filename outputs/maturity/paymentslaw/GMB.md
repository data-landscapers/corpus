# Gambia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Gambia has two texts. The central bank's [Mobile Money Regulation, 2011](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/b2064e61-f37b-11e9-876c-02e599c15748>), made under the Central Bank of The Gambia Act 2005, took effect on 15 October 2011. The [Electronic Payments System Act 2013](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/448bc45a-06e7-11ea-876c-02e599c15748>) defines the establishment and operation of the electronic payments system.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The 2011 regulation provides for retail transfers by persons not licensed as banks](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/b2064e61-f37b-11e9-876c-02e599c15748>) or financial institutions. It has the central bank authorise such payment service providers. They must be limited liability companies registered in The Gambia, with core capital of GMD 10 million and a security deposit of GMD 5 million. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch.

***Also on file***. [GAMSWITCH](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/448bc45a-06e7-11ea-876c-02e599c15748>).

***Not found***. No rule found requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other. None requires them to connect to a national or regional switch.
