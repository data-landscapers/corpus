# Namibia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Namibia's [Payment System Management Act 14 of 2023](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/94/94193758-e15b-4af9-8321-4801142c82f7.pdf>) was promulgated on 28 July 2023 and commenced on 8 August 2023. The Bank of Namibia makes determinations under its section 45: [PSD-10 on fees and charges](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8816.pdf>) took effect on 24 December 2025, [PSD-1 on licensing](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>) on 6 January 2026 and [PSD-12 on operational and cybersecurity standards](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2026/8968.pdf>) on 3 July 2026.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [PSD-1 sets a licensing process for payment services by non-bank financial institutions](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/31/313b4ab5-b138-45d3-b526-28959686db96.pdf>) and defines an electronic money issuer as a licensed payment service provider. [PSD-10 applies to all payment service providers](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2025/8816.pdf>), defined as banking institutions and non-bank financial institutions, and binds e-money issuers and providers of e-money wallets and instant payments. [Directive PSDIR-11 of 16 June 2025](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/57/57989e72-2303-40db-9617-01d97d88abbf.pdf>) requires every provider issuing payment instruments to make e-money interoperable through the instant payment switch by 26 February 2026. [Directive PSDIR-10, in force from 31 March 2025, gives providers until 1 April 2027 to send low-value transfers within the Common Monetary Area through the regional TCIB scheme](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/1b/1b0b2668-5468-400b-8285-c2e3fc386a03.pdf>).
