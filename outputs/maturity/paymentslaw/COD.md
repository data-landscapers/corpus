# DR Congo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** [Loi n° 18-019 of 9 July 2018](<https://anapi.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/221.07.18-Loi-du-9-juillet-2018_Systemes-de-paiement-1.pdf>) on payment systems and securities settlement systems is the Democratic Republic of the Congo's payments law. It was published in the Journal Officiel on 23 July 2018. No regulation made under it by the central bank is named, and no amendment to it is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2018 law](<https://anapi.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/221.07.18-Loi-du-9-juillet-2018_Systemes-de-paiement-1.pdf>) defines electronic money and payment orders. It is not said whether companies that are not banks can be licensed under it, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. The Democratic Republic of the Congo therefore answers the first two questions only: a law in force, with its reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the law or rules in force license payment providers or e-money issuers that are not banks, or names one licensed under them.
