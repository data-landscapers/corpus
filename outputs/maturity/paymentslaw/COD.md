# DR Congo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** [Loi n° 18-019 of 9 July 2018](<https://anapi.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/221.07.18-Loi-du-9-juillet-2018_Systemes-de-paiement-1.pdf>) on payment systems and securities settlement systems is the Democratic Republic of the Congo's payments law. It was published in the Journal Officiel on 23 July 2018. No regulation made under it by the central bank is named, and no amendment to it is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The central bank's map of the payment system lists the providers it has approved](<https://www.bcc.cd/intermediaires-financiers/systemes-de-paiement/infrastructures>). At end-2023 they included five non-bank financial companies, e-money issuers among them, and 81 money-transfer firms. [Instruction No. 58 of September 2024](<https://regalert.today/document/instruction-no-58-on-the-interoperability-of-electronic-payment-systems-and-part-34b2c78f-c351-4d8d-a6ee-2eba0ba23e98>) obliges every regulated entity to join the National E-Payment Switch within six months. That covers banks, financial companies, postal services and aggregators, under sanction. Settlement runs through the central bank's SAREC system.
