# DR Congo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** [Loi n° 18-019 of 9 July 2018](<https://anapi.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/221.07.18-Loi-du-9-juillet-2018_Systemes-de-paiement-1.pdf>) on payment systems and securities settlement systems is the Democratic Republic of the Congo's payments law. It was published in the Journal Officiel on 23 July 2018. No regulation made under it by the central bank is named, and no amendment to it is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2018 law](<https://anapi.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/221.07.18-Loi-du-9-juillet-2018_Systemes-de-paiement-1.pdf>) defines electronic money and payment orders. The central bank's [Instruction No. 58 of 4 September 2024](<https://regalert.today/document/instruction-no-58-on-the-interoperability-of-electronic-payment-systems-and-part-34b2c78f-c351-4d8d-a6ee-2eba0ba23e98>) obliges every regulated entity, from credit institutions to financial companies, postal services and aggregators, to connect to the National E-Payment Switch within six months, under sanction, and covers wallet-to-bank transfers between e-money institutions. Settlement runs through the central bank's SAREC system.
