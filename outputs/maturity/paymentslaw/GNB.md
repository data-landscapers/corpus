# Guinea-Bissau

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Guinea-Bissau is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. Payment institutions are [licensed under its Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>), and 34 were approved across the union at 31 August 2026.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The banking commission's register [lists one payment institution and one e-money institution in Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/4%20-%20Liste%20Guin%C3%A9e-Bissau_2026.pdf>), INTOUCH Guinée-Bissau and Finances Mobiles Bissau, neither a bank; [the first was approved on 25 June 2025](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>). The BCEAO required every bank, e-money issuer and payment institution to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June 2026, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>); five institutions in Guinea-Bissau were authorised on the platform at 31 July 2026.
