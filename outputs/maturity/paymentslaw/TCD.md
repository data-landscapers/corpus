# Chad

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Chad is bound by [the CEMAC regulation of 21 December 2018 on payment services](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>), which replaced 2011 rules covering e-money issuance only and is the framework under which Chad's payment establishments are licensed. The regional central bank's register [lists it as a text for all six member states](<https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/instructions-circulaires-reglements/>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Moov Money Tchad, a telecom subsidiary, holds a payment establishment licence](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>) from the regional banking commission, COBAC, confirmed in July 2026; it is the third company that is not a bank so licensed in Chad, after [Airtel Money in 2023 and the fintech Konoom in 2025](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). In February 2026 the finance ministry [signed conventions with four such operators](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>) on mobile money for state payments. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other.

[Moov Money](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>); [Airtel Money](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>); [Konoom](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>); [GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
