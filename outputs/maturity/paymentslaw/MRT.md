# Mauritania

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Mauritania's [Loi n°2021-014 of 5 July 2021](<https://www.droit-afrique.com/uploads/Mauritanie-Loi-2021-14-services-moyens-paiement-electronique.pdf>) on electronic payment services and means regulates payment service providers and e-money issuers, and repealed a 2006 ordinance. Under it the central bank issued [Instruction no. 03/GR/2022 of 31 March 2022](<https://fintech.bcm.mr/download/instruction_03_gr_2022-2%20Portant%20reglementation%20des%20%C3%A9tablissements%20de%20paiement.pdf>) regulating payment institutions. The law has 113 articles and was published in Journal Officiel n°1490 of 30 July 2021.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The law bars anyone except banks](<https://www.droit-afrique.com/uploads/Mauritanie-Loi-2021-14-services-moyens-paiement-electronique.pdf>) from carrying on the business of a payment institution, or issuing and distributing e-money, without the central bank's prior licence. [The instruction defines payment institutions](<https://fintech.bcm.mr/download/instruction_03_gr_2022-2%20Portant%20reglementation%20des%20%C3%A9tablissements%20de%20paiement.pdf>) as legal entities licensed by the central bank other than credit institutions, with minimum capital and a guarantee deposit of 20 million ouguiya each. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
