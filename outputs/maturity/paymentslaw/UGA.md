# Uganda

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Uganda's law is the [National Payment Systems Act, 2020](<https://kikubolane.com/2026/09/15/ugandas-central-bank-to-probe-claims-mobile-money-users-are-paying-off-strangers-loans/>). Bank of Uganda officials told a parliamentary committee on 14 September 2026 that the bank's oversight of wallet providers, payment firms and deposit-taking institutions rests on it. On 16 October 2026 the Daily Monitor [reported that Uganda relies on a framework that includes the Act and guidelines issued by financial regulators](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/uganda-champions-au-single-digital-market-plan-to-boost-cross-border-trade-5634362>). The bank [is reviewing the National Payment Systems Regulations after industry feedback](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/can-ugandas-national-payment-switch-bring-down-digital-transaction-costs/>), with amendments due in FY2026/27.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The bank's payment systems director spoke at the same hearing. The director said that [the wallet providers it licenses and supervises are MTN and Airtel](<https://kikubolane.com/2026/09/15/ugandas-central-bank-to-probe-claims-mobile-money-users-are-paying-off-strangers-loans/>). These are mobile money operators that are not banks, under the 2020 Act. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other. Nor is one named for a national switch. Fintechs have since announced licences of their own: [NALA, a Tanzanian firm, payment service provider and payment system operator licences from the Bank of Uganda in December 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/brief/brief-simple/tanzanian-fintech-nala-receives-uganda-payment-service-system-licenses>), and [Payaza a payment system operator licence in February 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/11/payaza-expands-east-african-footprint-with-uganda-license/>). In FY2025/26 the bank [licensed ten institutions as payment service providers or payment system operators](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/can-ugandas-national-payment-switch-bring-down-digital-transaction-costs/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
