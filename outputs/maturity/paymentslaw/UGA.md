# Uganda

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Uganda's law is the [National Payment Systems Act, 2020](<https://kikubolane.com/2026/09/15/ugandas-central-bank-to-probe-claims-mobile-money-users-are-paying-off-strangers-loans/>). Bank of Uganda officials told a parliamentary committee on 14 September 2026 that the bank's oversight of wallet providers, payment firms and deposit-taking institutions rests on it. On 16 October 2026 the Daily Monitor [reported that Uganda relies on a framework that includes the Act and guidelines issued by financial regulators](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/uganda-champions-au-single-digital-market-plan-to-boost-cross-border-trade-5634362>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The bank's payment systems director said at the same hearing that [the wallet providers it licenses and supervises are MTN and Airtel](<https://kikubolane.com/2026/09/15/ugandas-central-bank-to-probe-claims-mobile-money-users-are-paying-off-strangers-loans/>), mobile money operators that are not banks, under the 2020 Act. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch, so Uganda answers the first three questions and not the fourth. The hearing was part of a committee inquiry into the central bank.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
