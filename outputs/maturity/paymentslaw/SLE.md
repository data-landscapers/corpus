# Sierra Leone

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Sierra Leone's [National Payment Systems Act, 2022](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23>) was assented to on 2 June 2022, commenced on 23 June 2022 and repealed the Payment Systems Act 2009. The Bank of Sierra Leone's [Guidelines for E-money](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323115036589/pdf/P16660104d3ecf01808eca0b695fa306106.pdf>) have been completed and gazetted, with guidelines on remittance business and directives for the national switch.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Section 6 of the Act bars anyone from providing payment services](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2022/8/eng@2022-06-23>) or operating a system without a Bank of Sierra Leone licence, and sets conditions for e-money issuers; section 8 exempts only banks and financial institutions from needing a new licence. The licence is therefore one for companies that are not banks, and the Act gives mobile money operators a seat on its payments council. Directives issued in December 2024 [require all commercial banks and mobile money operators](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/bank-of-sierra-leone-issues-national-payment-switch-directives-for-2024/>) to route instant, card and point-of-sale payments through the national switch from 1 April 2025.
