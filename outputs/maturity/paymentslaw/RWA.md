# Rwanda

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The National Bank of Rwanda issued [Directive No. 45/2026 on 9 January 2026](<https://rba.rw/a-fully-connected-rwanda-what-ekash-interoperability-means-for-the-future-of-financial-inclusion/>). It [required domestic retail payments between banks and electronic money issuers](<https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-migrates-retail-payments-to-ekash/rxP3jqBEnOoMA2ye>) to move to the eKash platform, the national instant payment rail. RSwitch gave the account of the directive that ITWeb Africa reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [From 14 July 2026 every transfer between bank accounts and electronic money issuers](<https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-migrates-retail-payments-to-ekash/rxP3jqBEnOoMA2ye>), account to wallet, wallet to account, account to account and wallet to wallet, is routed through eKash. The Rwanda Bankers' Association states that [the directive took effect on that date](<https://rba.rw/a-fully-connected-rwanda-what-ekash-interoperability-means-for-the-future-of-financial-inclusion/>), so that banks and e-money issuers all run on the single rail. The directive binds the e-money issuers with the banks; the law under which those issuers are licensed is not named.
