# Mali

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Mali is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. Payment institutions are [licensed under its Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>), which [sets them apart from banks](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>), microfinance institutions and e-money institutions.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Two payment institutions are licensed in Mali](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>): INTOUCH Mali, since 9 May 2025, and CAURIDOR, since 3 September 2025. The union's banking commission [counted two licensed e-money issuers](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/umoa-la-monnaie-electronique-gagne-du-terrain-les-encours-bondissent-de-352-en-2025/>) there at the end of 2025. In April 2026 the BCEAO told every bank, e-money issuer and payment institution to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June, and on 25 June [set 30 September 2026 as the new deadline](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/prolongation-du-delai-de-connexion-a-pi-spi>). L'Oeil du Sahel reports [a BCEAO communique of 2 October 2026 requiring interoperable e-money transfers in the union to pass through PI-SPI from 2 November 2026](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>), and the banking commission's listing of [Orange Finances Mobiles Mali and Sama Money among Mali's e-money institutions, with 162.7 billion FCFA outstanding at the end of 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>). Orange Finances Money Mali [is an e-money institution authorised by the BCEAO](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/orange-finances-money-mali-et-terrapay-etablissent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-des-paiements-transfrontaliers-fluides-302028891.html>), it said in January 2024.
