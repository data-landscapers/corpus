# Burkina Faso

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Burkina Faso is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. Payment institutions are licensed under [its instruction of 23 January 2024 on payment services](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>), which sets them apart from banks, microfinance institutions and e-money institutions; the BCEAO [counted 34 licensed across the union](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>) at 31 August 2026.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Two payment institutions are licensed in Burkina Faso](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>): INTOUCH Burkina, since 9 May 2025, and KERRY Payments Burkina, since 2 September 2025. The union's banking commission [counted one licensed e-money issuer](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/umoa-la-monnaie-electronique-gagne-du-terrain-les-encours-bondissent-de-352-en-2025/>) there at the end of 2025. In April 2026 the BCEAO told every bank, e-money issuer and payment institution to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). L'Oeil du Sahel reports [a BCEAO communique of 2 October 2026 requiring interoperable e-money transfers in the union to pass through PI-SPI from 2 November 2026](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>), and the banking commission's figure of [314.8 billion FCFA of e-money outstanding at Orange Money Burkina Faso at the end of 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).
