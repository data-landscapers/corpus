# Burundi

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Burundi's [Loi n°1/07 of 11 May 2018 on the national payment system](<https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-09/LOI%20N%201-07%20DU%2011%20MAI%202018%20PORTANT%20SYSTEME%20NATIONAL%20DE%20PAIMENT.pdf>) is in force. The central bank's oversight framework, published in November 2025, [cites it as the current law](<https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/CADRE%20DE%20SURVEILLANCE%20DE%20SYSTEME%20DE%20PAIEMENT%20%2020%20NOV%202025.pdf>). Its article 9 lets the bank issue regulations. Under it the bank made [Règlement n°002/2024](<https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/CADRE%20DE%20SURVEILLANCE%20DE%20SYSTEME%20DE%20PAIEMENT%20%2020%20NOV%202025.pdf>), revising a 2017 regulation on payment services and payment institutions. No one may provide payment services without the central bank's licence.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The central bank licenses payment institutions](<https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2025-11/CADRE%20DE%20SURVEILLANCE%20DE%20SYSTEME%20DE%20PAIEMENT%20%2020%20NOV%202025.pdf>) under the 2024 regulation. Its framework lists them apart from commercial banks and microfinance institutions as operators of e-money platforms. Every licensed payment service provider is to be inspected on site at least every two years. The regulation's date within 2024 is not given. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other.
