# Seychelles

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Seychelles' [National Payment System Act, 2014](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/2014/14/eng@2014-08-18>) commenced on 18 August 2014. Under it the Central Bank made the [Electronic Money Regulations, 2022](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf>), which commenced on 12 December 2022, and on 15 December 2025 [amended its licensing and authorisation regulations](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/SI%2099%202025%20No%202%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Licensing%20and%20Authorisation%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%202025.pdf>). The Governor said in July 2025 that [Seychelles had amended its main payment system Act](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The 2022 regulations set capital and liquidity rules for electronic money issuers other than banks](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-01/SI%20128%202022%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Electronic%20Money%29%20Regulations%2C%202022.pdf>), with initial capital of SCR 1,000,000, and treat issuers that are banks or credit unions as separate cases. [The 2025 amendment sets the conditions](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/SI%2099%202025%20No%202%20-%20National%20Payment%20System%20%28Licensing%20and%20Authorisation%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%202025.pdf>) on which the Central Bank may license any company incorporated under the Companies Act as a payment service provider. No rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other. The amended regulations exempt banks and credit unions from annual fees only.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
