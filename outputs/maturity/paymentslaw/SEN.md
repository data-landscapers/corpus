# Senegal

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Senegal is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. [Its instruction of 23 January 2024 on payment services](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>) is the rule under which payment institutions are licensed, and [obliges every fintech offering payment services](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>) in the union to obtain a licence or stop.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Eleven payment institutions were licensed in Senegal](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>) at 31 August 2026, Flutterwave Sénégal and Julaya Sénégal among them, and the banking commission [counted five licensed e-money issuers](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/umoa-la-monnaie-electronique-gagne-du-terrain-les-encours-bondissent-de-352-en-2025/>) at the end of 2025; Wave [has held an e-money licence since April 2022](<https://www.wave.com/en/blog/sn-emi/>). By a communiqué of 25 June 2026 the BCEAO [set 30 September 2026 as the deadline](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/Communiqu%C3%A9_Prolongation_d%C3%A9lai_Connexion_%C3%A0_PI-SPI-25062026.pdf>) for banks, e-money institutions and payment institutions to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI.
