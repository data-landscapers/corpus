# Libya

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Libya's [Law No. 6 of 2022 on Electronic Transactions](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-6-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2022-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83/>) defines electronic money and, in article 70, provides that it is issued by commercial banks and other financial institutions under controls set by the Central Bank of Libya.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The Central Bank's [circular 18 of 2025](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-18-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A/>) is addressed to electronic payment companies it has licensed, and binds them. It defines an electronic payment service provider as an entity licensed by the Central Bank to provide electronic payment services and e-wallets. The licence is therefore one a company that is not a bank can hold. No rule is named that requires those companies and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch.

***Also on file***. [LYPay](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>).

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
