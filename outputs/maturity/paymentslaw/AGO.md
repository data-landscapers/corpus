# Angola

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Angola's payments law is [Lei n.º 40/20 of 16 December 2020, the Lei do Sistema de Pagamentos de Angola](<https://lex.ao/docs/banco-nacional-de-angola/2022/aviso-n-o-2-22-de-02-de-fevereiro/>), and the Banco Nacional de Angola makes binding notices under its article 6. Aviso n.º 2/22 of February 2022 sets the rules for access to payment service activity, [Aviso n.º 4/25 of August 2025](<https://lex.ao/docs/banco-nacional-de-angola/2025/aviso-n-o-4-25-de-22-de-agosto/>) those for opening and running e-money accounts, and [Aviso n.º 1/26 of January 2026](<https://lex.ao/docs/banco-nacional-de-angola/2026/aviso-n-o-1-26-de-28-de-janeiro/>) those for acquirers that are not banks.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Aviso n.º 2/22](<https://lex.ao/docs/banco-nacional-de-angola/2022/aviso-n-o-2-22-de-02-de-fevereiro/>) lets non-bank financial institutions based in Angola provide payment services, e-money issuance among them, as payment service companies authorised by the central bank. Since January 2026 [only such authorised companies may act as non-bank acquirers](<https://lex.ao/docs/banco-nacional-de-angola/2026/aviso-n-o-1-26-de-28-de-janeiro/>) of payment terminals and cash machines.
