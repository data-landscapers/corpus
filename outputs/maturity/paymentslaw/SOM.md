# Somalia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Central Bank of Somalia made the [Mobile Money Regulations, Amended 2020](<https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf>) under its 2012 Act. The governor [dates the move to regulate mobile money providers to 2021](<https://www.african.business/2026/11/long-reads/meet-the-man-building-somalias-central-bank-from-the-ground-up>). A National Payment System Bill [would let the central bank license payment systems and digital money platforms](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). The House of the People [passed it on 8 November 2025](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>). The Upper House [passed its third reading on 11 January 2026](<https://sonna.so/en/somalias-upper-house-passes-national-payment-system-bill-receives-briefing-on-worsening-drought/>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2020 regulations](<https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf>) set out the licensing, conduct of business and supervision of mobile money service providers. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. [In October 2026 the House of the People approved a separate Digital Transactions Bill, framed around mobile money](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/somalias-house-of-the-people-approves-the-digital-transactions-bill-with-137-votes-in-favour>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule held makes mobile money providers and banks connect to each other or to a switch. The bill's signing is not reported.
