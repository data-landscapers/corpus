# Somalia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Central Bank of Somalia made the [Mobile Money Regulations, Amended 2020](<https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf>) under the Central Bank of Somalia Act 2012, setting out the licensing, conduct and supervision of mobile money service providers. A National Payment System Bill is before parliament: the House of the People [passed it on 8 November 2025](<https://golekaab.so/en/bill/15>) and the Upper House [passed its third reading on 11 January 2026](<https://sonna.so/en/somalias-upper-house-passes-national-payment-system-bill-receives-briefing-on-worsening-drought/>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2020 regulations](<https://centralbank.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Mobile-Money-Regulation-2020_amended_2021.pdf>) license mobile money service providers. It is not said that those providers are companies other than banks, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Whether the bill has been signed into law is not reported.
