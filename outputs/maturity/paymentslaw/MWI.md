# Malawi

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Malawi has a Payment Systems Act in force. On 6 August 2026 [Parliament passed an amendment to it](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/>) removing its provisions on cheques, and the Finance Minister said detailed regulations would follow. Because Parliament amended it, the Act was already law before August 2026. The report does not name the regulations to come.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [amendment](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/>) deals with cheques. It is not said whether companies that are not banks can be licensed under the Act, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Malawi therefore answers the first two questions only: a law in force, with its reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated. The year the Act was first passed is not given.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the law or rules in force license payment providers or e-money issuers that are not banks, or names one licensed under them.
