# Malawi

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Malawi's Payment Systems Act, 2016 is in force: the Reserve Bank of Malawi [issued a payment service provider licence under it](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/17/payment-hurdles-ease-for-businesses/>) on 30 September 2026. On 6 August 2026 [Parliament passed an amendment to the Act](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/07/mps-hailed-for-fast-tracking-digital-finance-reforms/>) removing its provisions on cheques, and the Finance Minister said detailed regulations would follow. [Malawi's 2025 ID4Africa deck lists the Payment Systems Act, 2022, a revision of the 2011 act, and the E-Money Regulation, 2019 among the legal frameworks for payments](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** Companies that are not banks are licensed under the Act. On 30 September 2026 the Reserve Bank [granted the local fintech PayChangu Limited](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/17/payment-hurdles-ease-for-businesses/>) licence PSP-003/26 to run payment aggregation across the country, bringing mobile money, card payments and bank transfers onto one platform for businesses. No rule has been found that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch.
