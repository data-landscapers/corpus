# Togo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Togo is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. A [BCEAO communique of 2 October 2026](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-10/Communiqu%C3%A9%20du%202%20octobre%202026.pdf>) sets binding terms for person-to-person e-money transfers through PI-SPI from 2 November: free sends up to FCFA 8,000 a day, a charge of 0 to 0.8% above that, and free receipt. A [BCEAO instruction of 8 October 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/1210-20312-bceao-to-double-mobile-money-account-limit-to-cfaf-4-million>) raises the e-money balance cap to CFAF 4 million per issuer, from CFAF 2 million since 2015, and cuts the demand-deposit float floor from 75% to 65%, both from 2 November 2026.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Tmoney was licensed as an e-money institution in September 2025](<https://cadreco.media/finance/2026/umoa-les-transactions-mobiles-atteignent-164169-milliards-de-francs-cfa-en-2025>). In April 2026 the BCEAO [told every bank, e-money issuer and payment institution in the eight states](<https://iafrikan.com/tomorrow-west-africas-central-bank-finds-out-who-really-runs-its-payments-market>) to be connected to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June 2026, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>), with microfinance institutions given until 30 June 2027. Nine institutions in Togo were authorised on the platform at 31 July 2026.
