# Togo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Togo is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union, which licenses e-money institutions across the union. The banking commission's 2025 report says the number of licensed e-money issuers [rose from 14 to 18 in 2025](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/umoa-la-monnaie-electronique-gagne-du-terrain-les-encours-bondissent-de-352-en-2025/>), Togo's Tmoney among the new approvals.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Tmoney was licensed as an e-money institution in September 2025](<https://cadreco.media/finance/2026/umoa-les-transactions-mobiles-atteignent-164169-milliards-de-francs-cfa-en-2025>). In April 2026 the BCEAO [told every bank, e-money issuer and payment institution in the eight states](<https://iafrikan.com/tomorrow-west-africas-central-bank-finds-out-who-really-runs-its-payments-market>) to be connected to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June 2026, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>), with microfinance institutions given until 30 June 2027. Nine institutions in Togo were authorised on the platform at 31 July 2026.
