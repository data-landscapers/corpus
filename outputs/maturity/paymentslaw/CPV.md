# Cape Verde

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Cabo Verde legislated on payments in three decree-laws published on 28 November 2018. [Decreto-legislativo n.º 7/2018](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2610>) sets the regime for regulating and operating the payment system, and [Decreto-legislativo n.º 8/2018](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>) regulates payment services and the issue, distribution and redemption of electronic money. All three appeared in the same issue of the Boletim Oficial, n.º 78, and the Banco de Cabo Verde is the regulator under each.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Payment institutions and e-money institutions](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>), financial institutions distinct from banks, are authorised by the Banco de Cabo Verde under the third, [Decreto-legislativo n.º 9/2018](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2610>), which governs access to their activity. The postal service, Correios de Cabo Verde, may also issue e-money. No licensed institution is named, and no rule is named that requires providers to connect to each other or to a national switch.

***Also on file***. [Sistema de Gestão de Depósitos e Liquidação (SGDL)](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>); [Sistema Integrado de Compensação Interbancária e Liquidação (SICIL)](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>); [Sistema de Gestão de Mercados e Ativos (SIGMA)](<https://www.bcv.cv/pt/SistemadePagamentos/Paginas/SistemadePagamentos.aspx>).

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
