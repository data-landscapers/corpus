# Guinea

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The central bank's licensing committee issued [Decision D/2015/003/CAM on 31 March 2015](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>). It governs e-money and the firms that issue it. It binds from its signature and was published in the Journal Officiel. The central bank reproduced it in its 2017 collection of banking texts. In October 2026 the central bank was [preparing a bill on payment systems and instruments](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/2610-142742-guinee-nimbapay-traite-7-6-millions-de-transactions-et-connecte-7-etablissements-en-4-mois>), with World Bank support. No text of it has been published.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The 2015 decision classes e-money firms as financial establishments, not banks. It [requires each to hold a central bank licence](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>), with a decision due within 90 days of a complete file. [A second decision of 3 June 2015](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>) sets their minimum capital at GNF 2 billion. The 2013 banking law also lets firms other than credit institutions be authorised to manage means of payment. At the July 2026 launch of the NimbaPay switch, the central bank's Vice-Governor [called it an opportunity, not a regulatory constraint](<https://guineenews.org/2026/07/22/guinee-la-bcrg-lance-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-qui-revolutionne-les-paiements-instantanes-et-linteroperabilite/>).

***Also on file***. [NimbaPay](<https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2026/07/guinea-launches-nimbapay-opening-a-new-era-of-instant-and-inclusive-digital-payments/>); [RTGS](<https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/>); [Switch national](<https://www.bcrg.gn/projets-de-reformes-en-cours-a-travers-la-dsmp/>); [ACH](<https://www.bcrg.gn/le-cadre-reglementaire-systemes-et-moyens-de-paiement-en-republique-de-guinee/>).

***Not found***. No rule is recorded that requires providers to connect to each other or to a switch.
