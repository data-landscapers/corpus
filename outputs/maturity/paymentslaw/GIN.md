# Guinea

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The BCRG's Comite des agrements issued [Decision D/2015/003/CAM of 31 March 2015](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>) on e-money issuance and e-money establishments. It binds from its date of signature and was published in the Journal Officiel, as reproduced in the BCRG's December 2017 compendium of banking texts.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The decision places e-money establishments among financial establishments, not banks, and constitutes them as Guinean limited companies. It [requires them to obtain a BCRG licence](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>), with a decision due within 90 days of a complete file. [Decision D/2015/008/CAM of 3 June 2015](<https://www.bcrg-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Recueil-des-textes-legaux-et-reglementaires-regissant-lactivite-bancaire-et-financiere-%E2%80%93-Dec-201.pdf>) sets their minimum share capital at GNF 2 billion, to be held in Guinean francs and employed in Guinea. Article 5 of banking law L/2013/060/CNT, promulgated by decree on 28 November 2013, allows service providers other than credit institutions to be authorised, in a limited way, to provide and manage means of payment under rules set by a Comite des agrements decision. At the 22 July 2026 NimbaPay launch the BCRG Vice-Governor [called NimbaPay a strategic opportunity, not a regulatory constraint](<https://guineenews.org/2026/07/22/guinee-la-bcrg-lance-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-qui-revolutionne-les-paiements-instantanes-et-linteroperabilite/>).

***Not found***. No rule has been found that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
