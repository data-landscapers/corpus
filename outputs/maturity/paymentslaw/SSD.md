# South Sudan

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** South Sudan's [Electronic Money Regulation 2017 Amendment 2025](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>) took effect as reported on 1 September 2026, issued under the Bank of South Sudan Act 2011 Amendment 2023 and binding licensed financial institutions and non-bank e-money issuers. A broader National Payment System Bill, 2025 is still a draft: the Bank of South Sudan [opened a five-day workshop on 10 August 2026](<https://www.eyeradio.org/boss-parliament-back-stronger-legal-framework-for-digital-payments/>) to validate it, and at its close the governor [called for faster progress](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/south-sudan-pushes-digital-payments-as-central-bank-calls-for-faster-payment-bill/>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** Under the amended regulation [non-bank e-money issuers are licensed](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>): standalone payment providers must set up a separate company, seek the central bank's approval and pay a filing fee of 65 million South Sudanese pounds. The same rules [require every digital payment platform to connect to the National Payment System through a licensed switch](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>), so that customers can send money between providers.
