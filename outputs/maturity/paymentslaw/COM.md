# Comoros

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Central Bank of the Comoros issued [Regulation n°01/2017/BCC/DSBR](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/Loi%20et%20textes%20reglementaire/REGLEMENTS/2017/R%C3%A8glement%20n%C2%B001_2017_BCC_DSBR%20sur%20la%20monnaie%20%C3%A9lectronique.pdf>) in 2017, setting the conditions for carrying on e-money activity. It rests on [decree n°15-026/PR of 3 March 2015](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/Loi%20et%20textes%20reglementaire/REGLEMENTS/2017/R%C3%A8glement%20n%C2%B001_2017_BCC_DSBR%20sur%20la%20monnaie%20%C3%A9lectronique.pdf>) on payment systems, means of payment and payment incidents. The regulation gives only the year it was made, and no later amendment is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2017 regulation](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/Loi%20et%20textes%20reglementaire/REGLEMENTS/2017/R%C3%A8glement%20n%C2%B001_2017_BCC_DSBR%20sur%20la%20monnaie%20%C3%A9lectronique.pdf>) sets the conditions for e-money activity. In December 2025 [the central bank listed Telco Money SA and Hury Money SA among the e-money institutions legally established in the Comoros](<https://finances.gouv.km/actualite_post/communique-de-la-banque-centrale-des-comores-sur-lexercice-illegal-dactivites-bancaires-offshores/>), beside its licensed banks. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to join a common switch.
