# Congo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Congo's payment institutions are governed by [CEMAC/UMAC/COBAC Regulation No. 04/18 of 21 December 2018 on payment services](<https://www.sgg.cg/textes-officiels/arretes/2025/congo-arrete-2025-1672.pdf>). The finance ministry cited it in orders of 20 June 2025. The regulation binds the six states of the Central African Economic and Monetary Community. COBAC is the region's banking commission. Its [2025 annual report covers the payment institutions licensed in the region](<https://ecomatin.net/cemac-le-mobile-money-brasse-44-000-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-et-genere-47-milliards-de-benefices-aux-operateurs>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The finance ministry [approved Mobile Money Congo SA as a payment institution by an order of 27 May 2022](<https://www.sgg.cg/textes-officiels/arretes/2025/congo-arrete-2025-1672.pdf>). [Mobile Money Congo SA and Mobile Commerce Congo SA](<https://ecomatin.net/cemac-le-mobile-money-brasse-44-000-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-et-genere-47-milliards-de-benefices-aux-operateurs>) are the payment institutions operating in Congo, neither of them a bank. Their revenues rose 28.1% to FCFA 20.6 billion in 2025. No rule has been found that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.

***Also on file***. [GIMACPAY](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>); [ARTF digital platform and electronic payment terminals (TPE)](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/transfert-de-fonds-lusage-des-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-entrera-en-vigueur-des>); [ARTF digital platform](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-finances-vers-la-digitalisation-du-secteur-des-transferts-de-fonds-pour-une-meilleure-transparence/>); [FOUTA](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf>).

***Not found***. No rule has been found that makes mobile money providers and banks connect. None has been found for a national or regional switch.
