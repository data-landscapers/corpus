# Congo

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Congo's payment institutions are licensed by COBAC, the banking commission of the six-state Central African Economic and Monetary Community, whose [2025 annual report covers the payment institutions licensed in the region](<https://ecomatin.net/cemac-le-mobile-money-brasse-44-000-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-et-genere-47-milliards-de-benefices-aux-operateurs>). The commission reports each year on the institutions it supervises in the six member states.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Mobile Money Congo SA and Mobile Commerce Congo SA](<https://ecomatin.net/cemac-le-mobile-money-brasse-44-000-milliards-fcfa-en-2025-et-genere-47-milliards-de-benefices-aux-operateurs>) are the payment institutions operating in Congo, neither of them a bank, and their revenues rose 28.1% to FCFA 20.6 billion in 2025. No law or regulation is named as the one they are licensed under, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch. Congo therefore reaches the third question on the licences alone.
