# Ethiopia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The National Bank of Ethiopia introduced [two sets of rules in 2020](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52044/>): the Payment Instrument Issuers directives and the Payment Systems Operators directive. Firms are licensed and operating under both. The Reporter gave this account of the two frameworks in July 2026. No act of parliament on payments is named.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The operators directive created four licence categories](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/52044/>), for switch, point-of-sale, cash machine and payment gateway operators, under which companies that are not banks, Chapa, Arifpay, Santimpay and Kacha among them, were licensed. The issuers directives let telecom operators launch mobile wallets. No rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch, so Ethiopia answers the first three questions and not the fourth.
