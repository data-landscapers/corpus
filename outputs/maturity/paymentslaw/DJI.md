# Djibouti

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** [Loi n° 118/AN/15/7ème L](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n118-an-15-7eme-l-portant-creation-dun-systeme-de-paiement-national-sa-reglementation-et-sa-surveillance/>), the law creating the National Payment System, was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated in 2016. It applies across Djibouti to every payment service and system. It is known as the Loi sur le Système de Paiement National. No regulation made under it by the central bank is named, and no amendment to it is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2016 law](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n118-an-15-7eme-l-portant-creation-dun-systeme-de-paiement-national-sa-reglementation-et-sa-surveillance/>) covers payment services and systems. It is not said whether companies that are not banks can be licensed under it, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Djibouti therefore answers the first two questions only: a law in force, with its reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the law or rules in force license payment providers or e-money issuers that are not banks, or names one licensed under them.
