# Liberia

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** The Central Bank of Liberia's [Mobile Money Regulations](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/MobileMoneyRegulations.pdf>) are the framework governing electronic money and mobile money operators in Liberia, where more than 11 million mobile wallets operate. The Central Bank of Liberia is the regulator.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [regulations](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/MobileMoneyRegulations.pdf>) govern mobile money operators. It is not said that those operators are companies other than banks, or that they are licensed under the regulations, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Liberia therefore answers the first two questions only: rules in force, with their reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated. The year the regulations were made is not given. Nor is any payments act of parliament named.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Pay Na-Na](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/MobileMoneyRegulations.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held says the law or rules in force license payment providers or e-money issuers that are not banks, or names one licensed under them.
