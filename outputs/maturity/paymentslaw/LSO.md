# Lesotho

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Lesotho's [Payment Systems Act, 2014](<https://centralbank.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/Payment-Systems-Act-2014-1.pdf>) was published in the Government Gazette on 12 September 2014 and came into operation that day. On 5 May 2026 the Minister of Finance [moved the Payment System Bill, 2025 for second reading](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/>) in the National Assembly, to repeal and replace it. The bill had [first been presented on 2 May 2025 and approved by the portfolio committee with ten amendments](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Report-Payment-Systems-Bill-2025.pdf>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [bill](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/finance-minister-presents-payment-system-bill/>) would license all payment service providers, fintech companies included. The 2014 Act frames interbank payment systems and the issue and use of payment instruments. It is not said that companies other than banks can be licensed under the Act in force, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch.
