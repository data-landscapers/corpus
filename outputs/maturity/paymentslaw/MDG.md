# Madagascar

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Madagascar's [Loi n°2016-056 on electronic money and electronic money institutions](<https://textes.lexxika.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Loi-2016-056-02-Fevrier-2016-Sur-la-Monnaie-Electronique-et-les-Etablissements-de-Monnaie-Electronique.pdf>) was adopted by both chambers in December 2016, cleared by the High Constitutional Court on 27 January 2017 and promulgated on 2 February 2017. The banking supervision commission, the CSBF, [applies it](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>): it issues instructions and audits the institutions.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The law requires any legal entity running e-money operations to be approved by the CSBF](<https://textes.lexxika.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Loi-2016-056-02-Fevrier-2016-Sur-la-Monnaie-Electronique-et-les-Etablissements-de-Monnaie-Electronique.pdf>) as an e-money institution, a limited company separate from credit institutions and microfinance institutions. The CSBF [grants and withdraws those licences](<https://2424.mg/mobile-money-les-autorites-annoncent-des-audits-du-systeme-des-etablissements-de-monnaie-electronique/>), and in June 2026 the central bank governor announced audits of the institutions. In October 2024 the central bank [made connection to its instant payment system compulsory](<https://2424.mg/adhesion-obligatoire-au-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-banque-centrale-pour-les-institutions-financieres/>) for banks and e-money institutions, with a deadline of 31 January 2025, and for the largest microfinance institutions.
