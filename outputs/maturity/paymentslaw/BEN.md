# Benin

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Benin is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. [Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>), applied since January 2024, obliges every company offering payment services in the union to hold a licence or stop, and [distinguishes payment institutions](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>) from banks, microfinance institutions and e-money institutions.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Four companies held payment institution licences in Benin](<https://beninwebtv.bj/benin-quatre-etablissements-paiement-agrees-aout-2026/>) at 31 August 2026: Julaya Bénin, Open SI, FedaPay Bénin and QOS Integrated Center Bénin. The union's banking commission [counted three licensed e-money issuers](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/umoa-la-monnaie-electronique-gagne-du-terrain-les-encours-bondissent-de-352-en-2025/>) there at the end of 2025. In April 2026 the BCEAO told every bank, e-money institution and payment institution to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September 2026](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/Communiqu%C3%A9_Prolongation_d%C3%A9lai_Connexion_%C3%A0_PI-SPI-25062026.pdf>), with microfinance institutions given until 30 June 2027.
