# Nigeria

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** [OPay's October 2026 US filing names the central bank's licensing frameworks](<https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2103076/000119312526418812/d56487df1.htm>) for payment service banks, mobile money operators and payment service providers. It says they opened payments to non-bank players. [A 2025 bill would create one regulator for all fintech services](<https://businesstech.ng/fintech-regulatory-commission-bill-nigeria/>). It passed second reading in the House of Representatives in October 2025. Until it passes, the central bank licenses and supervises payment firms.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Operators told a March 2026 hearing that the central bank licenses them](<https://businesstech.ng/fintech-regulatory-commission-bill-nigeria/>). They include mobile money operators, payment service providers and digital banks. No rule is named that binds them and banks to a national switch. Payments already clear through NIBSS Instant Payment, but joining it is not shown to be required.

***Also on file***. [NIBSS Instant Payment](<https://iafrikan.com/tomorrow-west-africas-central-bank-finds-out-who-really-runs-its-payments-market>).

***Not found***. No source found names a rule requiring mobile money providers and banks to connect to a national switch.
