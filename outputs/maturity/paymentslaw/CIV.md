# Cote d'Ivoire

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Côte d'Ivoire is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. [Its Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services has applied since January 2024](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>). [Every company offering payment services in the union must hold a licence under it or stop](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>). [Payment institutions are licensed under it](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>). [On 4 November 2026 a National Assembly committee adopted a 258-article banking bill](<https://www.linfodrome.com/economie/127262-banques-fintech-monnaie-electronique-la-cote-d-ivoire-prepare-une-nouvelle-reglementation>). [The bill brings fintech and e-money operations into national law](<https://www.linfodrome.com/economie/127262-banques-fintech-monnaie-electronique-la-cote-d-ivoire-prepare-une-nouvelle-reglementation>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Ten payment institutions were licensed in Côte d'Ivoire at 31 August 2026](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>), the newest on 27 April 2026. [In 2025 Djogana Pay became the country's fourth e-money institution](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637099/economie/inclusion-financieremonnaie-electronique-la-cote-divoire-enregistre-un-4e-etablissement>), after Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money and Moov Money. [In April 2026 the BCEAO told every bank and e-money and payment institution to connect to PI-SPI by 30 June](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>). [On 25 June it moved the deadline to 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
