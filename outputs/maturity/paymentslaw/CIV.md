# Cote d'Ivoire

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Côte d'Ivoire is bound by the rules of the BCEAO, the central bank of the eight-state West African Economic and Monetary Union. [Instruction n°001-01-2024 on payment services](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>), applied since January 2024, obliges every company offering payment services in the union to hold a licence or stop; [payment institutions are licensed under it](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [Ten payment institutions were licensed in Côte d'Ivoire](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/liste-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees-dans-lumoa-au-31-aout-2026>) at 31 August 2026, the newest on 27 April 2026, and in 2025 [Djogana Pay became the country's fourth e-money institution](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2637099/economie/inclusion-financieremonnaie-electronique-la-cote-divoire-enregistre-un-4e-etablissement>) after Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money and Moov Money. In April 2026 the BCEAO told every bank, e-money institution and payment institution to connect to its instant payment platform, PI-SPI, by 30 June, and on 25 June [moved the deadline to 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
