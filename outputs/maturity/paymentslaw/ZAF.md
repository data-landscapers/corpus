# South Africa

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** South Africa's [National Payment System Act of 1998](<https://facephi.com/observatory/en/national-payment-system-south-africa-psp-licences/>) regulates the payment system and gives the Reserve Bank responsibility for clearing and settlement; it is [twenty-eight years old and still the law](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). The Reserve Bank governor said on 1 September 2026 that [a National Payment System Bill](<https://techcentral.co.za/national-payment-system-bill-sarb-fintech/285597/>), moving regulation from the kind of entity to the kind of activity, would soon go out for public comment.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** South Africa [has no dedicated mobile money licence for companies that are not banks](<https://techcentral.co.za/national-payment-system-bill-sarb-fintech/285597/>), an MTN executive said in September 2026, so fintech companies must work through a licensed bank to move money. The Reserve Bank is preparing an equivalent through an authorisation framework and the bill.
