# South Africa

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** South Africa's [National Payment System Act of 1998](<https://facephi.com/observatory/en/national-payment-system-south-africa-psp-licences/>) regulates the payment system and gives the Reserve Bank responsibility for clearing and settlement. It is [twenty-eight years old and still the law](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). On 1 September 2026 the Reserve Bank governor said [a National Payment System Bill](<https://techcentral.co.za/national-payment-system-bill-sarb-fintech/285597/>) would soon go out for public comment. The bill moves regulation from the kind of entity to the kind of activity. On 20 October 2026 he said the Reserve Bank had [proposed an exemption notice and authorisation framework](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>) letting regulated non-banks carry out payment activities. It is to be published by the end of November 2026. Under the Act the Reserve Bank [issued Directive No. 1 of 2025, binding on settlement participants from 1 April 2025](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). In June 2026 it [published a draft directive on payments for offshore merchants](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/media-releases/2026/cross-border-regulation>). [Its May 2026 draft framework would let approved non-banks issue e-money](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/south-africa-fintech-payment-network/>).

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The Reserve Bank says it [regulates third-party payment providers and e-money providers](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>). It has designated companies that are not banks as clearing system participants under the 1998 Act, among them Yoco Technologies in 2021 and Payfast in 2024. An MTN executive said in September 2026 that South Africa [has no dedicated mobile money licence for non-banks](<https://techcentral.co.za/national-payment-system-bill-sarb-fintech/285597/>). The Reserve Bank is preparing one through an authorisation framework and the bill. No rule has been found that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a switch.

***Also on file***. [PayInc](<https://techcentral.co.za/national-payment-system-bill-sarb-fintech/285597/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a rule requires mobile money providers and banks to interconnect or join a switch.
