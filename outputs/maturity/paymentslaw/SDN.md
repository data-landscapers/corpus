# Sudan

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Sudan's central bank regulates payments under its [Regulation on Licensing and Organising Mobile Payment Financial Institutions 2020](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/content/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%B5-%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2020>), approved on 28 December 2020 under the Banking Business Organisation Act, and a Payment Systems Regulation of 2013. [Circular 7/2026 of 31 March 2026](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>) cites both as its basis and sets binding minimum rules for financial services over USSD.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** [The 2020 regulation has the central bank license companies, not banks](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/content/%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%B5-%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2020>), to run mobile payment and open e-money wallet accounts; their names may not include the word bank. [The 2026 circular lets those institutions](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>) and licensed electronic payment providers offer USSD financial services alongside banks. It asks providers to connect to a national switch once one is available, and sets no date.

***Also on file***. [USSD mobile financial services](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>); [central national switch](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/node/24567/>).

***Not found***. No rule held requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national or regional switch.
