# Zimbabwe

## Payments law

**Law or rules in force.** Zimbabwe's [National Payment Systems Act (Chapter 24:23)](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2001/21/eng@2016-12-31>), Act 21 of 2001, commenced on 16 November 2001. It provides for the recognition, operation, regulation and supervision of payment and clearing systems. The text held is consolidated to 31 December 2016. No regulation made under it is named, and no later amendment is reported.

**Non-banks licensed and providers required to connect.** The [2001 Act](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2001/21/eng@2016-12-31>) covers payment and clearing systems. It is not said whether companies that are not banks can be licensed under it, and no rule is named that requires mobile money providers and banks to connect to each other or to a national switch. Zimbabwe therefore answers the first two questions only: a law in force, with its reach to non-banks and to connection between providers not stated.
