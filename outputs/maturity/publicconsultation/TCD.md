# Chad

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The media regulator HAMA [held a day of public consultation at the Cefod on 22 November 2024](<https://lendjampost.com/media-le-projet-portant-cahier-de-charge-des-web-radios-et-televisions-presente-par-la-hama/>). It put its draft specifications for web radio and web television to the managers of private media. The telecommunications ministry, with ECA and GIZ, [held consultations in N'Djamena on 13 to 15 May 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-chad-developing-national-data-governance-strategy-advance-au-data-policy-framework>) for the National Data Governance Strategy. The private sector, academia, civil society and telecom operators were listed among the participants. A [three-day workshop opened on 18 May 2026](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/transition-numerique-le-tchad/>) with technology incubators and security specialists, to feed the drafting. On 7 October 2026 the ministry [held a national validation workshop on the draft strategy](<https://mten.gouv.td/discours-de-haliki-choua-mahamat-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees/>), with academics, civil society and the private sector invited. The Minister [asked the academic and civil society representatives to say frankly what they think](<https://camerpressagency.com/transformation-numerique-le-tchad-batit-sa-souverainete-de-la-donnee/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** HAMA's head of legal affairs said the November 2024 hearing [was required by article 43 of the audiovisual communication law](<https://lendjampost.com/media-le-projet-portant-cahier-de-charge-des-web-radios-et-televisions-presente-par-la-hama/>). That article has HAMA set such specifications after a public hearing. Civil society and other interested parties give their views at it.

***Also on file***. [IGF Tchad](<https://igf.td/>) is the national internet governance forum. Chad's minister attended the [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>) virtually. Officials, experts and technical partners attended a [pre-validation workshop on the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/news/chad-moves-towards-adopting-its-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy/>) in October 2026. An [agricultural data strategy went to a validation workshop](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-fao-la-strategie-de-collecte-de-traitement-et-de-diffusion-des-donnees-agricoles-en-phase-de-validation/>). A civil society group [petitioned the regulator ARCEP on Airtel Tchad's network quality](<https://flashtchad.com/telephonie-une-organisation-denonce-airtel-tchad-et-saisit-larcep/>). A [national internet governance forum was announced for 23 and 24 September 2026](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/08/25/gouvernance-dinternet-le-tchad-prepare-un-forum-national-consacre-a-lintelligence-artificielle/>).

***Not found***. No standing body with members from outside government has been found giving its view on a named digital law or policy. No report of the November 2024 hearing has been found either.
