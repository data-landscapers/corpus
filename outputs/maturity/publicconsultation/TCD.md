# Chad

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The media regulator HAMA [held a day of public consultation at the Cefod on 22 November 2024](<https://lendjampost.com/media-le-projet-portant-cahier-de-charge-des-web-radios-et-televisions-presente-par-la-hama/>) on its draft specifications for web radio and web television, with the managers of private media present. The Ministry of Telecommunications, Digital Economy and Digitalisation of Administration, with ECA and GIZ, held [consultations in N'Djamena on 13 to 15 May 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-chad-developing-national-data-governance-strategy-advance-au-data-policy-framework>) for the National Data Governance Strategy, with a workshop on 21 May to validate the findings. The private sector, academia, civil society organisations, telecom operators and fintech companies were listed among the participants. A [three-day workshop opened on 18 May 2026](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/transition-numerique-le-tchad/>) included technology incubators and information-security specialists, and its working groups were to produce contributions for the drafting. On [7 October 2026](<https://camerpressagency.com/transformation-numerique-le-tchad-batit-sa-souverainete-de-la-donnee/>) the Minister opened a national validation workshop on the draft and asked the academic and civil society representatives present to read it and say frankly what they think.

**Required consultation and standing body.** HAMA's head of legal affairs said the November 2024 hearing [was required by article 43 of the audiovisual communication law](<https://lendjampost.com/media-le-projet-portant-cahier-de-charge-des-web-radios-et-televisions-presente-par-la-hama/>), which has HAMA set such specifications after a public hearing at which civil society and other interested parties give their views. The hearing put the draft web radio and web television specifications to the managers of private media in N'Djamena.
