# Nigeria

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 23 July 2026 the Senate [held a public hearing on bill SB648, which amends the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/project-sprint-rallies-senate-support-for-data-protection-bill/>). A coalition of civil society and youth groups attended. On 4 March 2026 it [held a public hearing on bill SB. 959 on fintech regulation](<https://naltf.gov.ng/senate-passes-bill-to-regulate-systemically-important-fintechs/>), with support from agencies, regulators and industry. On 19 June 2026 the Nigerian Communications Commission [issued a consultation paper on zero-rated access to educational platforms](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-06/Consultation-Paper-on-Zero-Rated-Access-to-Educational-Platforms.pdf>). It asked for written comments by 9 July. It then [held a public forum on the paper in Abuja](<https://therevealerng.com/stakeholders-back-nccs-zero-rated-education-access-plan/>), with the operators' association ALTON and uLesson Group present. The Data Protection Commission [held a workshop in Lagos with companies on its e-commerce privacy framework](<https://www.cyberera.com.ng/building-trust-in-e-commerce-ndpc-partners-giz-to-strengthen-sectoral-compliance-with-ndp-act-2023/>), reported in September 2026. NITDA [took a draft innovation hubs framework to over 60 hub operators, founders and investors in Lagos](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/07/fg-reiterates-national-innovation-hubs-framework-will-strengthen-ecosystem-at-lagos-workshop/>). On 10 November 2025 the National Assembly [held a hearing on the Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/11/nigerias-digital-economy-bill-hands-sweeping-powers-to-nitda/>). The operators' association presented a position paper there.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The zero-rated access paper [came from a Joint NCC-Industry Committee](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-06/Consultation-Paper-on-Zero-Rated-Access-to-Educational-Platforms.pdf>), which the commission formed with industry. The Open Government Partnership's reviewers say [the 2023-2025 action plan was written through a steering committee of government and civil society](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/nigeria-results-report-2023-2025/>). The plan includes an open contracting portal.
