# Nigeria

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Senate [held a public hearing on 23 July 2026 on bill SB648, which amends the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, attended by a coalition of civil society and youth organisations](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/07/project-sprint-rallies-senate-support-for-data-protection-bill/>). The Nigerian Communications Commission [issued a consultation paper on 19 June 2026 on zero-rated access to educational platforms, asking for written comments by 9 July](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-06/Consultation-Paper-on-Zero-Rated-Access-to-Educational-Platforms.pdf>), and [held a public forum on it in Abuja with the telecom operators' association ALTON and uLesson Group among those present](<https://therevealerng.com/stakeholders-back-nccs-zero-rated-education-access-plan/>). The Nigeria Data Protection Commission [held a workshop in Lagos with private companies on its Data Privacy Framework for e-Commerce](<https://www.cyberera.com.ng/building-trust-in-e-commerce-ndpc-partners-giz-to-strengthen-sectoral-compliance-with-ndp-act-2023/>), reported in September 2026. The technology agency NITDA [took a draft National Innovation Hubs Standard Framework to more than 60 hub operators, startup founders and investors in Lagos](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/07/fg-reiterates-national-innovation-hubs-framework-will-strengthen-ecosystem-at-lagos-workshop/>), reported on 7 September 2026. The National Assembly [held a public hearing on 10 November 2025 on the National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill, where the telecom operators' association presented a position paper](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/11/nigerias-digital-economy-bill-hands-sweeping-powers-to-nitda/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The consultation paper on zero-rated access [came from a Joint NCC-Industry Committee, which the commission formed with industry players](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-06/Consultation-Paper-on-Zero-Rated-Access-to-Educational-Platforms.pdf>). The Open Government Partnership's reviewers say [the 2023-2025 action plan, which includes an open contracting portal, was written through a steering committee of government and civil society](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/nigeria-results-report-2023-2025/>).

***Also on file***. [NITDA's Multi-Stakeholder Coordinating Group on Digital Governance, which met with no named law or policy before it](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/nitda-calls-for-trust-transparency-accountability-digital-governance/>); [the National Advisory Committee for Community Networks, which backed a strategy led by civil society](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/strategy-action-nigerias-communities-push-bridge-digital-divide>); [a regional workshop in Abuja on a draft West African directive on electronic communications](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-commission-convenes-validation-workshop-for-the-draft-directive-governing-electronic-communications-in-west-africa/>); [a House committee public hearing on the Statistics Bill, 2025](<https://guardian.ng/featured/reps-begin-review-of-statistics-act-link-reform-to-1tn-economy-drive/>); [the identity commission's engagement on the new digital identity law, described only as a meeting with officials](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-seeks-support-to-operationalize-new-digital-id-law-as-nin-tops-136m>); [the National Privacy Summit of Privacy Week 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years.
