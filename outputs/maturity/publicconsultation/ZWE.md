# Zimbabwe

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The ICT ministry held a [national multi-stakeholder consultation on the draft AI strategy in Harare on 28 August 2025](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>). Sessions followed in Bulawayo and Masvingo in September 2025. They validated the Kadoma Draft of the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026-2030. The strategy [credits academia, the private sector, civil society and the diaspora](<https://t3n9sm.c2.acecdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Zimbabwe-National-Artificial-Intelligence-Strategy.pdf>) with contributions. The regulator POTRAZ says [seven more draft data protection guidelines were out for public consultation in 2025](<https://privacyscorecardreport.com/privacy-scorecard-report-2025/zimbabwe/zimbabwes-data-protection-regime/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026-2030](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) says a National AI Council is to be set up. It [names AI & Ethics Dare](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>), a public consultation forum, as a way citizens shape AI policy. Its appendix proposes a National Digital Regulatory Committee with members from several sectors.

***Also on file***. The [National AI Council](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) the strategy says is to be set up. [AI & Ethics Dare](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>), a public forum the strategy names. A [National Digital Regulatory Committee](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) proposed in the strategy's appendix. The regulator's [call for research proposals on digital technologies policy](<https://applynow.co.zw/2026/09/09/potraz-4/>). The [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>), a regional forum.

***Not found***. No consultation report from the last three years has been found. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them.
