# Zimbabwe

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of ICT, Postal and Courier Services held a [National Multi-Stakeholder Consultation in Harare on 28 August 2025](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>), followed by sessions in Bulawayo on 9 September and Masvingo on 11 September 2025. The sessions were held to interrogate and validate the Kadoma Draft of the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026-2030. In the strategy the Ministry [credits contributions from academia, the private sector, civil society and diaspora representatives](<https://t3n9sm.c2.acecdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Zimbabwe-National-Artificial-Intelligence-Strategy.pdf>), alongside government and development partners.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026-2030](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) says a National AI Council is to be established. It [names AI & Ethics Dare](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>), a public consultation forum, as a way citizens shape policy on artificial intelligence, and its appendix proposes a National Digital Regulatory Committee with members from several sectors.

***Also on file***. The [National AI Council](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) the strategy says is to be established; [AI & Ethics Dare](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>), a public forum the strategy names; a [National Digital Regulatory Committee](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Zimbabwe%20National%20Artificial%20Intelligence%20Strategy.pdf>) proposed in the strategy's appendix; the regulator POTRAZ's [call for research proposals on digital technologies policy](<https://applynow.co.zw/2026/09/09/potraz-4/>); the [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>), a regional forum.

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them from the last three years has been found.
