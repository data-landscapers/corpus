# Seychelles

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 20 December 2023 the Financial Services Authority, for the Ministry of Finance, [launched a public consultation on the proposed Virtual Asset Service Providers framework](<https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Virtual-Asset-Service-Providers-Framework-Response-to-Public-Consultation.pdf>). It invited written responses. It held a live session on 9 January 2024, in person and online, attended by about 50 to 60 people. Further sessions were held for Securities Act licensees, corporate service providers and virtual asset providers already operating. The Ministry and the Authority [published a consultation response dated 7 March 2024](<https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Virtual-Asset-Service-Providers-Framework-Response-to-Public-Consultation.pdf>). It summarises the consultation, which drew about 15 detailed written responses. A member of the drafting team says the [National Digital and AI Strategy 2026-2031 followed consultations with the private sector, academia and civil society](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/baidy-s-91972228_digitaltransformation-ai-digitalgovernment-activity-7481275398413152257-B36W>). They closed with a national validation workshop in mid-2026.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [National AML/CFT Committee approved and launched that consultation](<https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Virtual-Asset-Service-Providers-Framework-Response-to-Public-Consultation.pdf>). The Cabinet [approved the holding of a Digital Economic Transformation Forum with members from several sectors](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/cabinet-decisions/7247/cabinet-business-wednesday-09th-september-2026>), on record in September 2026.

***Also on file***. [The National AML/CFT Committee approved and launched the virtual asset consultation](<https://www.finance.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Virtual-Asset-Service-Providers-Framework-Response-to-Public-Consultation.pdf>). [The Digital Economic Transformation Forum was approved by Cabinet and has not yet been held](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/cabinet-decisions/7247/cabinet-business-wednesday-09th-september-2026>). [A committee of ministers, officials and civil society heads compiled the open government action plan for 2023 to 2025](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Seychelles_Action-Plan_2023-2025_December.pdf>). [The National Cybersecurity Coordination Committee presented the National Cybersecurity Baseline to banks at an awareness workshop](<https://www.mhsca.gov.sc/nccc/news>). Also on file is [the national multi-stakeholder group on fisheries transparency](<https://mofbe.gov.sc/fiti-nmsg-showcases-seychelles-leadership-in-fisheries-transparency-during-cote-divoire-ministerial-visit/>).
