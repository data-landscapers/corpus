# Kenya

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy, with the Communications Authority, [asked the public on 26 August 2025 for views](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>) on the draft Kenya Information and Communications Regulations, then [published a matrix of comments beside the revised drafts](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>) and [called a public validation forum for 15 to 17 September 2026](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>). The State Department for ICT and the Digital Economy [opened the draft Artificial Intelligence and Other Emerging Technologies Policy for written comment from 21 July to 4 August 2026](<https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/AI%20Policy%20Doc/draft-kenya-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy-2026.pdf>). The Communications Authority said on 22 September 2026 that it had [published the submissions on its draft internet telephony guidelines with its own position on them](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/22/kenya-ca-finalise-commercial-internet-telephony-guidelines/>), and it [answered in writing the public comments on its line deactivation and number recycling rules](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ca-to-set-up-central-database-to-track-recycled-phone-numbers-5628406>), reported on 12 October 2026. The Ministry [held a validation workshop on the draft National Data Governance Policy on 5 February 2026](<https://www.ict.go.ke/government-moves-strengthen-kenyas-data-governance-framework>) with the private sector, academia and civil society in the room.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Ministry said on 26 August 2026 that the public participation on the regulations [was in line with Article 10 of the Constitution and sections 5 and 8 of the Statutory Instruments Act](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>). Its notice of 21 July 2026 on the artificial intelligence policy said the process [followed the Constitution, which it said requires public participation in developing policies](<https://kahawatungu.com/govt-invites-public-submissions-on-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy/>).

***Also on file***. [The Kenya Internet Governance Forum, 19th edition held on 21 May 2026](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/navigating-digital-rights-in-africa-amidst-increasing-foreign-malign-influence/>); [a National Data Governance and Emerging Technologies Council, announced and not yet set up](<https://www.ict.go.ke/government-moves-strengthen-kenyas-data-governance-framework>); [the Presidential Advisory Council on Science and Technology, set up by executive order in 2023](<https://tracker.mzalendo.com/category/promise-category/ict/>); [the Universal Service Advisory Council](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/07/kenya-moves-to-boost-digital-inclusion-through-locally-assembled-devices/>); [a 100-day National Steering Committee on Digital Piracy](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/31/kenya-unveils-national-framework-to-combat-kes-92-billion-digital-piracy-losses/>); [the Social Health Authority's consultation on healthcare provider contracts](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-08-sha-invites-views-on-new-healthcare-contracts>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years says a standing body with members from outside government gave its view on a named digital law or policy.
