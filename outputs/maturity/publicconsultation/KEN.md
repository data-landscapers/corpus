# Kenya

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The ICT ministry and the Communications Authority [asked the public for views on 26 August 2025](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>) on the draft Kenya Information and Communications Regulations. The ministry later [published a matrix of the comments beside the revised drafts](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>). It [called a public validation forum for 15 to 17 September 2026](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>). The draft Artificial Intelligence and Other Emerging Technologies Policy [was open for written comment from 21 July to 4 August 2026](<https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/AI%20Policy%20Doc/draft-kenya-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy-2026.pdf>). The draft National Data Governance Policy [was open for public comment until 5 June 2026](<https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/have-your-say-kenyas-draft-national-data-governance-policy-comments-due-5-june-2026/>). It had been through a [validation workshop on 5 February 2026](<https://www.ict.go.ke/government-moves-strengthen-kenyas-data-governance-framework>) with the private sector, academia and civil society. On 22 September 2026 the Communications Authority [published the submissions on its internet telephony guidelines with its own position](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/22/kenya-ca-finalise-commercial-internet-telephony-guidelines/>). It also [answered in writing the comments on its line deactivation rules](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ca-to-set-up-central-database-to-track-recycled-phone-numbers-5628406>), reported on 12 October 2026.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The ministry said on 26 August 2026 that the regulations' consultation [followed the Constitution and the Statutory Instruments Act](<https://ict.go.ke/kica-regulations-documents-2026>). Its 21 July 2026 notice on the AI policy said [the Constitution requires public participation in policy-making](<https://kahawatungu.com/govt-invites-public-submissions-on-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy/>).

***Also on file***. The [Kenya Internet Governance Forum held its 19th meeting on 21 May 2026](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/navigating-digital-rights-in-africa-amidst-increasing-foreign-malign-influence/>). A [National Data Governance and Emerging Technologies Council was announced and not yet set up](<https://www.ict.go.ke/government-moves-strengthen-kenyas-data-governance-framework>). The [Presidential Advisory Council on Science and Technology was set up by executive order in 2023](<https://tracker.mzalendo.com/category/promise-category/ict/>). Also noted are [the Universal Service Advisory Council](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/07/kenya-moves-to-boost-digital-inclusion-through-locally-assembled-devices/>), [a 100-day steering committee on digital piracy](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/31/kenya-unveils-national-framework-to-combat-kes-92-billion-digital-piracy-losses/>) and [a health insurer's consultation on provider contracts](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-08-sha-invites-views-on-new-healthcare-contracts>).

***Not found***. No standing body with members from outside government has been found giving its view on a named digital law or policy. The search covered the last three years.
