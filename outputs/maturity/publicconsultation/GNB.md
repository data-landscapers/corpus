# Guinea-Bissau

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Board of Guinea-Bissau's national regulator, ARN-TIC, [opened a public consultation on the draft Electronic Transactions Law](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>) with a notice, Aviso n.º 01/CA/ARN/2026, published on 8 May 2026. The notice was addressed to "interested parties" and asked for their responses in writing, by email, by 28 May 2026. The regulator's own name for the draft is Projeto de Lei das Transações Eletrónicas.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The consultation on the draft law rests on [the notice of the regulator's Board](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>), which set its own terms for who could respond, how and by when. After the military takeover of 26 November 2025, citizens [reported restrictions across the country on Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>).
