# Guinea-Bissau

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** [On 8 May 2026 the regulator ARN-TIC opened a public consultation on the draft Electronic Transactions Law](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). [Its notice asked interested parties for written responses by email by 28 May 2026](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). [On 30 October 2025 a workshop presented the draft universal access and service law, decree and policy to stakeholders](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). [The consultants' final report answered the workshop's twelve comments](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** [The consultation on the draft law rests on a notice of the regulator's Board](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). The notice set its own terms for who could respond, how they should respond and by what date. [After the military takeover of 26 November 2025, citizens reported restrictions on social media across the country](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>).
