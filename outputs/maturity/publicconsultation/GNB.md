# Guinea-Bissau

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** [On 8 May 2026 the regulator ARN-TIC opened a public consultation on the draft Electronic Transactions Law](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). [Its notice asked interested parties for written responses by email by 28 May 2026](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). [On 30 October 2025 a workshop presented the draft universal access and service law, decree and policy to stakeholders](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). [The consultants' final report answered the workshop's twelve comments](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). On 17 July 2026 ARN [published its report on a consultation on the draft radio spectrum fees order](<https://arn.gw/activeapp/noticias/arn-publica-relatorio-da-consulta-publica-n-o-2-sobre-o-projeto-de-despacho-das-taxas-radioeletricas/>). [It set out Orange Bissau's contribution, the only one, and which proposals it accepted or refused](<https://arn.gw/activeapp/noticias/arn-publica-relatorio-da-consulta-publica-n-o-2-sobre-o-projeto-de-despacho-das-taxas-radioeletricas/>). On 29 November 2024 [ministries, companies and civil society validated the draft National Digital Transformation Strategy](<https://arn.gw/estrategia-nacional-para-a-transformacao-digital/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** [The consultation on the draft law rests on a notice of the regulator's Board](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=57171>). The notice set its own terms for who could respond, how they should respond and by what date. [After the military takeover of 26 November 2025, citizens reported restrictions on social media across the country](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing found says who responded to the notice, or who took part in the October 2025 workshop. The spectrum fees report cites a law but does not say it required the consultation. No standing body giving a view has been found.
