# Uganda

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance [held a validation workshop on 4 June 2025 on draft guidelines for ICT infrastructure in buildings, roads and transport](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/advancing-ict-infrastructure-standards-through-stakeholder-collaboration>). Private telecom companies and professional bodies took part, after stakeholder consultations during 2024/25. It is the only fact held on consultation: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and no report of the comments made or of the ministry's answers to them has been found.

**Required consultation and standing body.** No source found says a rule required that consultation, or that a standing body with members from outside government gave its view on a digital law or policy. The nearest held fact is [the validation workshop on the infrastructure guidelines](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/advancing-ict-infrastructure-standards-through-stakeholder-collaboration>), which the ministry convened for that draft.

[Uganda Internet Governance Week 2026](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/uganda-pushes-for-inclusive-safe-and-ai-ready-internet-5559810>), national internet governance forum events; [validation workshop on a digital entrepreneurship assessment](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/smart-africa-uganda-reforms-digital-entrepreneurship/>), held by Smart Africa and the Ministry of ICT; [validation workshop on a baseline study for a national ICT infrastructure data store](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/uganda-to-advance-digital-transformation-with-a-national-ict-infrastructure-platform>), with the private sector and universities; [joint submission on Uganda to the UN Human Rights Council](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Uganda_UPR_Review.pdf>) by CIPESA, APC and WOUGNET, not asked for by government; [East African Community survey of statistics users](<https://www.eac.int/press-releases/3590-eac-calls-for-public-participation-in-shaping-better-regional-statistics>), regional and not digital.
