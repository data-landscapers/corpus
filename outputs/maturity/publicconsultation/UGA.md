# Uganda

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 20 October 2026 the communications regulator, UCC, [met the Kampala City Traders Association and Wi-Fi hotspot operators](<https://publicisteastafrica.com/can-uganda-build-a-legal-public-wi-fi-revolution/>). It announced a multi-stakeholder committee on licensing for resellers. On 4 June 2025 the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance [held a validation workshop](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/advancing-ict-infrastructure-standards-through-stakeholder-collaboration>) on draft guidelines for ICT infrastructure in buildings, roads and transport. Private telecom companies and professional bodies took part, after stakeholder consultations in 2024/25.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest fact is the committee UCC announced in October 2026, [a multi-stakeholder committee on licensing for resellers](<https://publicisteastafrica.com/can-uganda-build-a-legal-public-wi-fi-revolution/>). It has been announced and has not yet given a view. Neither the UCC meeting nor the ministry's [workshop on infrastructure guidelines](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/advancing-ict-infrastructure-standards-through-stakeholder-collaboration>) is said to have been held under a rule that required it.
