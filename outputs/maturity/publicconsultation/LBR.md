# Liberia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Liberia Telecommunications Authority [put its draft Satellite Communications Guidelines out for comment](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CONSULTATION-DOCUMENT-SATELLITE-COMMUNICATIONS-GUIDELINES-FINAL-COPY.pdf>) from 23 February to 9 March 2026. It [held public forums on 5 March and 29 May 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>) with licensed providers. On 3 April 2026 it [published a Consultation Report](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>) answering the written submissions. In October 2026 it [held a public consultation on draft Infrastructure Regulations](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-holds-public-consultation-on-draft-infrastructure-regulations/article_f187f32e-dd53-4c5e-8645-7e26476124be.html>) in Paynesville. The Senate committees reviewing the draft Cybercrime Act [said a public hearing guided their review](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202511050108.html>) in November 2025. That month the ministry and the African Union Commission [held a two-day consultation](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-moves-to-strengthen-data-governance-under-au-led-initiative/>) on a National Data Policy.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Authority says the satellite consultation [was held under Part III, Section 11(4) of the Telecommunications Act of 2007](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>). Its consultation document says it [followed the Authority's Guidelines for Consultation Process of 2009](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CONSULTATION-DOCUMENT-SATELLITE-COMMUNICATIONS-GUIDELINES-FINAL-COPY.pdf>). [The 2007 Act](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LTA-Act-2007.pdf>) requires the ministry and the Authority to consult the public before policy or orders with substantial impact.

***Also on file***. [The Telecommunications Act 2007](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LTA-Act-2007.pdf>), which requires public consultation before major telecoms policy. [The Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>), held in Paynesville in September 2026. [The Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/ten-csos-form-digital-rights-coalition/article_af9fdbc9-c6ab-4b6a-b1f1-3981a3e53c4f.html>), a coalition of ten civil society groups. [University of Liberia consultations](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/building-liberias-ai-future-undp-ul-consult-stakeholders>) on a master's programme in AI. [The National Elections Commission's communications strategy](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/nec-approves-new-communications-strategy-boost-transparency-and-trust>), consulted with the media. [Planned consultations on an education information policy](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20EMIS%20Consultant_May%20ER%20LB.%202026_.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source reports a standing body with outside members giving its view. That covers any named digital law or policy in the last three years.
