# Liberia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Liberia Telecommunications Authority [put its draft Satellite Communications Guidelines out for written comment from 23 February to 9 March 2026](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CONSULTATION-DOCUMENT-SATELLITE-COMMUNICATIONS-GUIDELINES-FINAL-COPY.pdf>), asking licensed service providers, satellite operators and others for their views, and [held public forums on 5 March and 29 May 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>) with mobile operators, internet service providers and satellite companies. It [published a Consultation Report on 3 April 2026 giving its responses to the written submissions](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>). The Authority also [held a public consultation on its Draft Infrastructure Regulations in Paynesville](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-holds-public-consultation-on-draft-infrastructure-regulations/article_f187f32e-dd53-4c5e-8645-7e26476124be.html>), reported on 12 October 2026, with internet service providers, mobile network operators and licensees. The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, with the African Union Commission, [held a two-day consultation in Monrovia on 13 and 14 November 2025](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-moves-to-strengthen-data-governance-under-au-led-initiative/>) to prepare the National Data Policy, with civil society, academia, the private sector and the media.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Authority says the satellite consultation [was conducted under Part III, Section 11(4) of the Telecommunications Act of 2007](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/lta-signs-new-satellite-framework-targets-digital-access-across-liberia/article_e178022e-dcdf-47e5-a31b-9abefbb594c2.html>), and its consultation document states that it ran [in accordance with the Authority's published Guidelines for Consultation Process of 2009](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CONSULTATION-DOCUMENT-SATELLITE-COMMUNICATIONS-GUIDELINES-FINAL-COPY.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [The Telecommunications Act 2007, which requires the Ministry and the Authority to consult the public before telecommunications policy or orders with substantial impact](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/LTA-Act-2007.pdf>); [the Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum, held in Paynesville on 21 and 22 September 2026](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>); [the Digital Advocacy and Rights Alliance, a coalition of ten civil society organisations](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/ten-csos-form-digital-rights-coalition/article_af9fdbc9-c6ab-4b6a-b1f1-3981a3e53c4f.html>); [the University of Liberia's consultations on a master's programme in artificial intelligence](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/building-liberias-ai-future-undp-ul-consult-stakeholders>); [the National Elections Commission's communications strategy, consulted with the media](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/nec-approves-new-communications-strategy-boost-transparency-and-trust>); [consultations planned on a national education information system policy](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20EMIS%20Consultant_May%20ER%20LB.%202026_.pdf>).
