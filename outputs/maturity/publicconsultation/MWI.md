# Malawi

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** MACRA, acting as the Data Protection Authority, [held a consultation meeting in Lilongwe on 15 July 2026 on draft data protection regulations and proposed registration fees of K50,000 to K7 million by turnover](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/>), attended by companies such as United General Insurance. In February 2026 the Department of E-Government [opened a two-day national validation workshop in Lilongwe on the draft National Digital Transformation Strategy and the draft National AI Strategy, with government institutions, regulators, companies, civil society and academia](<https://leymanck.com/ministry-validates-digital-transformation-and-ai-strategies/>). A GSMA report says the ministry, supported by UNDP, [published the draft digital transformation strategy and a five-year roadmap for consultation that month](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>). [On 27 February 2024 the PPPC, the digitalisation ministry and MACRA held a hybrid public consultation in Lilongwe on the draft Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Regulations, with written submissions invited by 6 March 2024](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/government-engages-stakeholders-on-etca-regulations/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Department of E-Government [runs a public portal for people's feedback on the two draft strategies](<https://leymanck.com/ministry-validates-digital-transformation-and-ai-strategies/>), which it calls Shape Malawi's Digital and AI Future.
