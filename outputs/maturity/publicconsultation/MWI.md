# Malawi

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** MACRA, acting as the Data Protection Authority, [held a consultation meeting in Lilongwe on 15 July 2026](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/>). It covered draft data protection regulations and registration fees of K50,000 to K7 million by turnover. Companies such as United General Insurance attended. A later [validation workshop on the fees in Blantyre](<https://www.dpa.mw/dpa-reviews-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/>) brought in public and private sector representatives. In February 2026 the Department of E-Government [opened a two-day validation workshop in Lilongwe](<https://leymanck.com/ministry-validates-digital-transformation-and-ai-strategies/>). It covered the draft National Digital Transformation Strategy and the draft National AI Strategy. Regulators, companies, civil society and academia took part. A GSMA report says the ministry, with UNDP, [published the draft strategy and a five-year roadmap for consultation that month](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>). On 27 February 2024 the PPPC, the ministry and MACRA [held a public consultation on the draft Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Regulations](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/government-engages-stakeholders-on-etca-regulations/>). Written submissions were due by 6 March 2024. In October 2025 MACRA [took submissions from the Media Council and MISA Malawi on its review of the Communications Act](<https://mwnation.com/media-council-for-law-backed-self-regulation/>). [A Digital Laws Task Force is reviewing them before a first draft bill goes to stakeholders](<https://mwnation.com/media-council-for-law-backed-self-regulation/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Department of E-Government [runs a public portal for feedback on the two draft strategies](<https://leymanck.com/ministry-validates-digital-transformation-and-ai-strategies/>). It calls the portal Shape Malawi's Digital and AI Future.

***Also on file***. [Regional consultations on a draft Cyber Security Policy and Strategy](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jolly-chibwana-486620156_moderated-regional-stakeholder-consultations-activity-7402990349041872896-kMyt>) were held in Mzuzu, Lilongwe and Blantyre in December 2025. Also noted are the [open government action plan for 2025 to 2028](<https://www.ogp.gov.mw/index.php/documents/malawi-ogp-national-action-plan-2025-2028/download>). So is a [July 2026 workshop on AI readiness](<https://ncst.mw/ncst-takes-part-in-validation-workshop-for-the-malawi-artificial-intelligence-readiness-assessment-methodology-ram-report/>). So is an [open data policy for 2022 to 2027](<https://mweiti.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2024-11/malawi_open_data_policy%20approved%2023%20June%202022.pdf>). So are the [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>) and an [African Union workshop on data governance](<https://au.int/sw/node/45730>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them.
