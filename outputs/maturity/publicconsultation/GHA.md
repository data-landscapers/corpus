# Ghana

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The National Information Technology Agency [put out its draft Data Harmonisation Bill 2025 for public consultation](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanas-data-harmonisation-bill-2025-to-be-opened-for-public-consultation-from-nov-19-to-27-2025.html>), announced on 23 October 2025 and set to run from 19 to 27 November 2025. It invited citizens, universities, the private sector and the general public to make submissions through the consultation document on its website. The agency's own [invitation for comments](<https://nita.gov.gh/publicconsultation-3-2-2/>) also named ministries, assemblies, civil society and development partners. In 2023 Ghana's Open Government Partnership secretariat and Multi-Stakeholder Forum [wrote the fifth national action plan with civil society organisations and government agencies](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Ghana_Action-Plan_2024-2028_December.pdf>), in consultative meetings and a workshop in October 2023. The plan carries commitments on misinformation on digital platforms, on amending the Electronic Communications Act 2008, and on data sharing and open data.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Open Government Partnership Multi-Stakeholder Forum is [a steering body whose members include civil society and parliament](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Ghana_Action-Plan_2024-2028_December.pdf>).
