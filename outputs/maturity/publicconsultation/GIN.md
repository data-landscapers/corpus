# Guinea

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** ANSSI, an agency under Guinea's Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and the Digital Economy, [held a workshop to validate the draft State Information Systems Security Policy](<https://anssi.gov.gn/atelier-de-validation-de-la-politique-de-securite-des-systemes-dinformation-de-letat-pssie/>), which it calls the PSSIE, in April 2025. Representatives of public institutions, cybersecurity experts and people working in the digital sector attended. They analysed the draft's parts, added to them and validated them.

**Required consultation and standing body.** Guinea has [a multi-stakeholder group for transparency in the extractive industries](<https://eiti.org/documents/guinea-2026-validation-report>), covered in a validation report of 2026. Its steering committee has [an open data policy of its own](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>), which covers data on extractive revenue. The digital ministry and UNDP held [a validation workshop on a report assessing readiness for artificial intelligence](<https://guineematin.com/2025/12/20/intelligence-artificielle-guinee-rapport-national-feuille-de-route/>), reported in December 2025.

***Also on file***. The [third Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>), a regional forum held in Liberia on 21 and 22 September 2026 with Guinea among four states attending; a [validation workshop on the national and agricultural land policies](<https://agriculture.gov.gn/politique-fonciere-agricole-pfa-le-premier-ministre-lance-de-latelier-national-de-validation-des-avant-projets/>), which are not digital.

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years. Nothing found states a rule that required a consultation, or a standing body that gave a view on a digital law or policy.
