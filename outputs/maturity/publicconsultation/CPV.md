# Cape Verde

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** ARME, the country's regulator, [opened a public consultation on a proposed regulatory framework for satellite communication networks and services](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/>), which the trade press reported on 5 August 2026. Submissions were open until 31 August 2026.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Conselho Consultivo da Economia Digital is a consultative council on the digital economy. It [held its third ordinary meeting on 25 November 2025](<https://www.governo.cv/a-economia-digital-e-um-pilar-estrategico-para-o-crescimento-de-cabo-verde-ministro-olavo-correia/>), chaired by the minister. The digital economy strategy and the Morabeza project were presented at it. The country's open government action plan carries a [commitment on an open data policy and portal](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>). The commitment proposes a consultation with civil society. The Ministry of State Modernization and Public Administration had not held it when the commitment was recorded.

***Also on file***. The [sixth Internet Governance Forum of Cabo Verde](<https://inps.cv/inps-mobile-apresentado-no-6-o-forum-de-governacao-da-internet-igf-cabo-verde-2026/>) was held in 2026, with no named law or policy put to it. The [public consultation of titleholders on provisional land registry data](<https://ingt.gov.cv/ingt/Servi%C3%A7os/cadastro-predial/>) is not on a digital law or policy. The annual [Semana do Digital](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/eitec/2026/03/09/comeca-hoje-a-semana-do-digital/101778>) was [set up by Resolution 24/2026](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/eitec/2026/02/13/cabo-verde-passa-a-assinalar-anualmente-semana-do-digital/101395>), and is not a consultation.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them. Nothing found states who answered the satellite consultation. Nothing found states the instrument that set up the council or any view it gave, or a rule that required a consultation.
