# Senegal

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** In March 2026 the telecoms regulator ARTP [launched a public consultation on deploying and sharing fibre networks to the subscriber](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>). It asked operators, equipment makers, consultancies and individuals, among others, to contribute. On 10 August 2026 it [published a synthesis of the twenty-one contributions](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>). The synthesis sets out where they agreed and where they differed. From 25 July 2024 the digital ministry [ran a national public consultation on the Digital Strategy 2025-2035](<https://letechobservateur.sn/cloture-de-la-consultation-publique-pour-la-strategie-numerique-du-senegal-2025-2035-un-pas-vers-une-transformation-digitaleinclusive/>). [More than 700 contributions came in on the Jubbanti platform](<https://letechobservateur.sn/cloture-de-la-consultation-publique-pour-la-strategie-numerique-du-senegal-2025-2035-un-pas-vers-une-transformation-digitaleinclusive/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [Conseil national du Numérique is a consultative body of 20 public, private and civil-society members](<https://primature.sn/publications/actualites/installation-officielle-du-conseil-national-du-numerique-cnn-du-senegal>). It sits under the Prime Minister's office. The Presidency's [Jubbanti platform hosts draft reform bills for the public to read](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/actualites/communique-de-la-presidence-de-la-republique-sur-la-mise-a-disposition-publique-des-avant-projets-de-loi-de-reforme/>). It also [took citizens' contributions to the national dialogue on the political system](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/actualites/lancement-de-jubbanti-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-un-dialogue-national-inclusif>).
