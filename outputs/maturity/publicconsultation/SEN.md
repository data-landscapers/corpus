# Senegal

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The telecoms regulator, ARTP, [launched a public consultation in March 2026 on the deployment and sharing of fibre networks to the end subscriber](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>). It asked for contributions from operators, alternative infrastructure operators, public developers, equipment makers, integrators, consultancies and individuals. On 10 August 2026 it [published a synthesis of the twenty-one contributions it received](<https://artp.sn/partage-infrastructures/fttx>), setting out where they agreed and the differing positions expressed.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [Conseil national du Numérique is a consultative body of 20 public, private and civil-society members](<https://primature.sn/publications/actualites/installation-officielle-du-conseil-national-du-numerique-cnn-du-senegal>) under the Prime Minister's office. The Presidency's [Jubbanti platform hosts draft reform bills for the public to read](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/actualites/communique-de-la-presidence-de-la-republique-sur-la-mise-a-disposition-publique-des-avant-projets-de-loi-de-reforme/>), and it [took citizens' contributions to the national dialogue on the political system](<https://www.presidence.sn/fr/actualites/lancement-de-jubbanti-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-un-dialogue-national-inclusif>).
