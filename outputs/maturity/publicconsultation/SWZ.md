# Eswatini

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of ICT's Department of Research, Science, Technology and Innovation held [a two-day validation session in February 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministry-of-ict-eswatini_rsti-stipolicyreview-digitaltransformation-activity-7432119915588812800-2Nv->) on the draft review of the National STI Policy and the combined 4IR and 5IR Strategy, which covers artificial intelligence, automation and data governance. The department's director said the draft was brought back to those consulted to refine, and ESEPARC, an economic policy research centre, presented. The Ministry also held [a validation workshop at Ezulwini](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/mchumanisi-system-to-give-emaswati-one-stop-access-to-government-services/>), reported on 22 July 2026, on the draft National Data Governance Policy (2027-2031). It brought together government ministries, regulators, the private sector, civil society, academia and development partners.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The long title of the [Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act 2022](<https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/COMPUTER-CRIME--CYBERCRIME-ACT.pdf>) names a National Cybersecurity Advisory Council.

***Noted, not assessed***. The [National Cybersecurity Advisory Council](<https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/COMPUTER-CRIME--CYBERCRIME-ACT.pdf>) named in the 2022 act; a [Digital Eswatini Project consultation of 8 August 2025](<https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/Draft%20SEP.pdf>) with officials and World Bank consultants only; a [Skills for Youth validation workshop on the ICT workforce](<https://independentnews.co.sz/49335/news/ecot-missing-from-eswatinis-visible-ict-workforce/>) on 8 October 2026; a [rollout briefing in Matsapha on the National Postcode and Addressing Project](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/matsapha-first-to-roll-out-addressing-system-in-eight-weeks/>); a [meeting with special-school representatives on data capture for the education information system](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/towards-inclusive-education-data-systems-integration-disability-indicators-eswatinis-national>); the [National Advisory Council for Persons with Disabilities](<https://www.gov.sz/images/justice/GAZETTES/Extra-Ord-No.-109---Persons-with-Disabilities.pdf>) under the 2018 disability act.
