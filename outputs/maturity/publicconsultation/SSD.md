# South Sudan

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The regulator, the National Communication Authority, and Digital Rights Frontlines, a civil society group, [convened a two-day workshop on the Data Protection Bill 2025](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>), reported on 5 May 2026. More than 30 people took part, among them members of parliament and civil society groups. A [ministry workshop in Juba on 8 to 10 September 2026](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/south-sudan-data-governance-workshop-juba/>) prepared for the same bill. About 50 people attended, among them civil society groups and telecom operators. In October 2025 the chair of the Assembly's Information Committee said its work on the cybercrime bill [included public consultations and workshops](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-lawmakers-advance-cybercrime-bill-to-final-vote>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [South Sudan Internet Governance Forum](<https://ssigf.org.ss/about-ss-igf/>) is a national forum of government and people outside it, with a Multistakeholder Advisory Group. [Its 2026 meeting was held in Juba](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/04/south-sudan-internet-governance-forum-2026-charts-course-for-inclusive-digital-future/>), reported on 4 July 2026. The [National Constitutional Review Commission is consulting the public on the permanent constitution](<https://www.eyeradio.org/ncrc-says-public-views-will-form-basis-of-s-sudans-permanent-constitution/>), reported in August 2026.

***Also on file***. The [South Sudan Internet Governance Forum is a national forum with an advisory group](<https://ssigf.org.ss/about-ss-igf/>). A [meeting in Central Equatoria State introduced a digital skills programme](<https://www.sceniushub.com/post/scenius-hub-and-central-equatoria-state-ministry-of-information-and-communication-advance-implement>). The [East African Community surveyed users of regional statistics](<https://www.eac.int/press-releases/3590-eac-calls-for-public-participation-in-shaping-better-regional-statistics>). The public is being [consulted on the permanent constitution](<https://www.eyeradio.org/ncrc-says-public-views-will-form-basis-of-s-sudans-permanent-constitution/>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received, or a written answer to them.
