# Egypt

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** MP Sahar Albazar wrote in September 2025 about the artificial intelligence bill. The MP wrote that the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology had [hosted a listening session for the private sector on the bill](<https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/governing-ai-with-inclusion-an-egyptian-model-for-the-global-south>). More than 70 companies and experts attended and gave their recommendations directly to government officials.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The National Telecom Regulatory Authority has its own [framework of rules and measures for public consultations](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Regulatory-Frame-on-the-Rules-and-Measures-of-Public-Consultations-003.pdf>), dated 2021. Under it the authority issues a consultation when it judges one necessary. The ministry's session on the artificial intelligence bill was [a listening session for the private sector](<https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/governing-ai-with-inclusion-an-egyptian-model-for-the-global-south>). The ministry held it, not a council or committee with outside members.

***Also on file***. The telecoms regulator has a [framework of rules for its public consultations](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Regulatory-Frame-on-the-Rules-and-Measures-of-Public-Consultations-003.pdf>), dated 2021, with no consultation under it named. A [freedom of information bill](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33330>) was drafted by the Journalists Syndicate and submitted to parliament in August 2026. The prime minister's Media Development Committee adopted it. Also on file is the [same bill as tabled by an MP](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/8/9/3030353/>). In July 2026 the government made a [commitment](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/07/05/egypt-coordinates-to-issue-freedom-of-information-law-open-media-dialogue/>) to issue a freedom of information law, coordinated between ministries.

***Not found***. No report of the listening session has been found. No comments received and no written answer to them have been found. No consultation held under a rule that required it has been found.
