# Egypt

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** MP Sahar Albazar wrote in September 2025 that the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology had [hosted a listening session for the private sector on the artificial intelligence bill](<https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/governing-ai-with-inclusion-an-egyptian-model-for-the-global-south>). More than 70 companies and experts attended and gave their recommendations directly to government officials. In June 2026 People of Internet reported that the [Draft Artificial Intelligence Law of 2024 was moving through stakeholder consultation](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The National Telecom Regulatory Authority has its own [framework of rules and measures for public consultations](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Regulatory-Frame-on-the-Rules-and-Measures-of-Public-Consultations-003.pdf>), dated 2021, under which it issues a consultation when it judges one necessary. The ministry's session on the artificial intelligence bill was [a listening session for the private sector](<https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/governing-ai-with-inclusion-an-egyptian-model-for-the-global-south>), held by the ministry rather than by a council or committee with outside members.

***Also on file***. The telecoms regulator's [framework of rules for its public consultations](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Regulatory-Frame-on-the-Rules-and-Measures-of-Public-Consultations-003.pdf>), dated 2021, with no consultation under it named; a [freedom of information bill](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33330>) drafted by the Journalists Syndicate and submitted to parliament in August 2026, adopted by the prime minister's Media Development Committee; the [same bill as tabled by an MP](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/8/9/3030353/>); a [government commitment](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/07/05/egypt-coordinates-to-issue-freedom-of-information-law-open-media-dialogue/>) of July 2026 to issue a freedom of information law, coordinated between ministries.

***Not found***. No report of the listening session, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found, and no consultation held under a rule that required it.
