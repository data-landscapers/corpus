# Egypt

## Consultation on digital law and policy

***Noted, not assessed***. The telecoms regulator's [framework of rules for its public consultations](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Regulatory-Frame-on-the-Rules-and-Measures-of-Public-Consultations-003.pdf>), dated 2021, with no consultation under it named; a [freedom of information bill](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33330>) drafted by the Journalists Syndicate and submitted to parliament in August 2026, adopted by the prime minister's Media Development Committee; the [same bill as tabled by an MP](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/8/9/3030353/>); a [government commitment](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2026/07/05/egypt-coordinates-to-issue-freedom-of-information-law-open-media-dialogue/>) of July 2026 to issue a freedom of information law, coordinated between ministries.

***Not held***. Nothing held says people outside government were asked for their views on a digital law or policy, or that they were not.
