# Gambia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Public Utilities Regulatory Authority [published the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>) on 2 April 2026, one of seven draft instruments released together. It [invited licensees, sector ministries, industry, consumer groups, civil society organisations and the general public to comment](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>) between 1 and 10 April 2026. On 2 and 3 July 2025 [people from government, the private sector, civil society and universities gave inputs on the draft National Data Policy](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/gambia/gambia-moves-closer-stronger-data-governance-validating-its-national-data-policy-validated_en>) at a national validation workshop. Government and private institutions [reviewed the draft E-Commerce Policy and Strategy in groups](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministry-of-trade-industry-regional-integration-employment-the-gambia_national-validation-workshop-e-commerce-policy-activity-7376955606995234816-umtR>) at a workshop on 24 September 2025. In November 2025 [telecom executives, consultants and development partners reviewed the regulator's proposed tools for regulating telecom prices](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-races-toward-smarter-telecom-regulation>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The government's [National Policy Development Handbook 2025](<https://policies.gov.gm/f/1edfbb99-e652-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>) is a guide to the policy cycle, and it says it requires inclusive and participatory processes.

***Also on file***. The [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>), a regional forum held in Tanzania; the [Open Budget Survey 2025](<https://foroyaa.net/gambia-ranks-third-in-sub-saharan-africa-on-budget-transparency/>), on public participation in the budget, which is not digital; a [validation workshop on the National Digital ID and Digital Transformation Strategy](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/validation-of-the-gambia's-national-digital-id-and-digital-transformation-strategy>) on 16 and 17 May 2024, attended by officials only.

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years. Nothing found shows a consultation held because a rule required it, or a standing body that gave a view.
