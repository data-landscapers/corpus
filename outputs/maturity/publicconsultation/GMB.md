# Gambia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The regulator, PURA, [published the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>) on 2 April 2026, with six other draft instruments. It [invited licensees, industry, civil society and the general public to comment](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>) between 1 and 10 April 2026. In July 2025 [the private sector, civil society and universities gave inputs on the draft National Data Policy](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/gambia/gambia-moves-closer-stronger-data-governance-validating-its-national-data-policy-validated_en>) at a validation workshop. Private institutions [reviewed the draft E-Commerce Policy and Strategy](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministry-of-trade-industry-regional-integration-employment-the-gambia_national-validation-workshop-e-commerce-policy-activity-7376955606995234816-umtR>) at a workshop in September 2025. In November 2025 [telecom executives reviewed the regulator's proposed tools for regulating telecom prices](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-races-toward-smarter-telecom-regulation>). In December 2024 the minister said the data protection bill [had been through a consultation with civil society and private organisations](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/na-commits-data-protection-privacy-bill-to-committee>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The government's [National Policy Development Handbook 2025](<https://policies.gov.gm/f/1edfbb99-e652-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>) is a guide to the policy cycle. It says it requires inclusive and participatory processes.

***Also on file***. The [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>), a regional forum held in Tanzania. The [Open Budget Survey 2025](<https://foroyaa.net/gambia-ranks-third-in-sub-saharan-africa-on-budget-transparency/>), on public participation in the budget, which is not digital. A [validation workshop on the digital ID and digital transformation strategy](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/validation-of-the-gambia's-national-digital-id-and-digital-transformation-strategy>) in May 2024, attended by officials only.

***Not found***. No consultation report from the last three years has been found. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them. No consultation is shown to have been held because a rule required it. No standing body is shown to have given a view.
