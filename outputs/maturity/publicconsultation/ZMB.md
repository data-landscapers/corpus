# Zambia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 21 October 2026 the regulator ZICTA [opened a consultation on its draft revised ICT type approval guidelines](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>). [It invited operators, importers, civil society, academics and the public to comment until 20 November 2026](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>). The Global Network Initiative says the [Cyber Security Act and Cyber Crimes Act of 2025 were made with minimal transparency and public participation](<https://globalnetworkinitiative.org/gni-statement-on-zambias-new-cyber-laws-a-blow-to-freedom-of-expression-and-privacy/>). [It says concerns civil society raised after the bills were tabled in November 2024 were not met before enactment](<https://globalnetworkinitiative.org/gni-statement-on-zambias-new-cyber-laws-a-blow-to-freedom-of-expression-and-privacy/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is ZICTA's [public notice of 21 October 2026](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>), which the regulator issued itself. It set a window of about a month for written comments, [open to the public as well as to the industry](<https://ib-lenhardt.com/media/pages/news/consultation-on-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines/3220b471e8-1792662126/public-notice-draft-revised-type-approval-guidelines_20102026.pdf>).
