# Sudan

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** Skyline International for Human Rights says [the Cabinet approved amendments to the Cybercrime Law on 13 October 2025 without transparency or public consultation](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>). [The group calls the amendments a grave threat to digital freedoms](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>). [The Minister of Justice asked for them](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>). [They set fines of up to ten million Sudanese pounds and prison terms of up to ten years](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>). [Social media users and independent journalists are the target, Skyline says](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>). [It asks for a review of such laws in consultation with civil society, journalists' unions and legal experts](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the same: [the Cabinet approved the October 2025 cybercrime amendments](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>) with, Skyline says, no public consultation. No body from outside government is named as taking part.

***Also on file***. [Mersal](<https://howtobuildup.org/mersal-digital-consultation-report-october-2025/>), a consultation of Sudanese communities on ceasefire talks, was run by Build Up, not by government. [Zain Interactive Forum](<https://sudanhorizon.com/at-zain-interactive-forum-experts-call-for-accelerating-digital-transformation-in-sudan/>) was hosted by Zain Sudan on 31 May 2026. The [National Consultative Committee on Statistics](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/migration/arabstates/NSDS-Abridged-Version_Final_English_Nov-2012.pdf>) is a committee of agencies, and not digital.

***Not found***. No source says people outside government were asked about a digital law or policy. That covers the last three years.
