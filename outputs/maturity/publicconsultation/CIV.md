# Cote d'Ivoire

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 13 and 14 August 2026 ARTCI, the telecommunications regulator, [held a workshop at Assinie with telecom operators, internet service providers and consumer associations](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643946/economie/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-harmonise-la-procedure-de-traitement-des-plaintes>), who [examined and amended its draft decision](<https://www.afriksoir.net/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-veut-rendre-le-traitement-des-plaintes-plus-efficace/>) on one procedure for handling consumer complaints about electronic communications services. On 30 and 31 July 2025 it [held a workshop at Yamoussoukro to validate the draft national strategy and action plan for moving to IPv6](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635755/economie/cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-ladoption-de-lipv6-pour-un-internet-plus-moderne>), with about twenty public and private bodies, among them Orange CI, MTN CI, Moov Africa, Huawei and CODINORM.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The draft decision on consumer complaints [was drawn up under law no. 2024-352](<https://www.afriksoir.net/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-veut-rendre-le-traitement-des-plaintes-plus-efficace/>), and ARTCI [held the August 2026 meeting as a consultation workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6772/artci-consults-stakeholders-on-new-consumer-complaint-handling-procedure>) for operators, providers and consumer associations to contribute to it.

***Also on file***. The [Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum 2026](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>), a regional forum with no named law put to it; [Open Government Week on 18 to 22 May 2026](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/semaine-ogp-2026-ouverte/>), with in-person public consultations on access to information and no named digital law or policy; the [open data commitment CI0024](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cote-divoire/commitments/CI0024/>) of the 2018 to 2020 open government action plan, with no consultation stated.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no set of comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years. Nothing found states a rule that required a consultation, or a standing body with outside members giving a view.
