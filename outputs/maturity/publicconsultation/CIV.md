# Cote d'Ivoire

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 13 and 14 August 2026 the regulator ARTCI [held a workshop at Assinie with telecom operators, internet providers and consumer associations](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2643946/economie/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-harmonise-la-procedure-de-traitement-des-plaintes>). They [examined and amended its draft decision on handling consumer complaints](<https://www.afriksoir.net/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-veut-rendre-le-traitement-des-plaintes-plus-efficace/>). In July 2025 it [held a workshop at Yamoussoukro to validate the draft national IPv6 strategy](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635755/economie/cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-ladoption-de-lipv6-pour-un-internet-plus-moderne>). [About twenty public and private bodies took part, among them Orange CI, MTN CI and Moov Africa](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635755/economie/cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-ladoption-de-lipv6-pour-un-internet-plus-moderne>). In November 2024 [ARTCI opened a public consultation on a study of internet access pricing](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tarification_des_Services_dAcces_a_Internet_-_Novembre_2024_Reponse_OCI.pdf>). [Its website carries the written response Orange Cote d'Ivoire sent on 4 December 2024](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Tarification_des_Services_dAcces_a_Internet_-_Novembre_2024_Reponse_OCI.pdf>). From 6 July to 6 August 2026 ARTCI [held a public consultation on net neutrality guidelines](<https://artci.ci/en/public-consultation-on-the-development-of-guidelines-for-the-implementation-of-net-neutrality-in-cote-divoire/>), open to all. [It said it would publish the responses with a summary report](<https://artci.ci/en/public-consultation-on-the-development-of-guidelines-for-the-implementation-of-net-neutrality-in-cote-divoire/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** [The draft complaints decision was drawn up under law no. 2024-352](<https://www.afriksoir.net/protection-des-consommateurs-lartci-veut-rendre-le-traitement-des-plaintes-plus-efficace/>). ARTCI [held the August 2026 meeting as a consultation workshop](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6772/artci-consults-stakeholders-on-new-consumer-complaint-handling-procedure>) for operators, providers and consumer associations.

***Also on file***. The [Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum 2026](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>) was a regional forum with no named law put to it. [Open Government Week, 18 to 22 May 2026](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/semaine-ogp-2026-ouverte/>), held public consultations on access to information, not on a named digital law. The [open data commitment CI0024](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cote-divoire/commitments/CI0024/>) of the 2018 to 2020 open government action plan states no consultation.

***Not found***. No source found from the last three years says a consultation was held because a rule required it. None says a standing body with outside members gave a view.
