# Madagascar

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 11 September 2024 the telecoms regulator ARTEC [held a public consultation workshop on a draft decree on spectrum and numbering](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>). [Operators, frequency users, civil society and ministries took part](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>). [ARTEC says the workshop was held to finalise the text](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>). [The decree sets the rules for managing, allocating and controlling frequency and numbering resources in telecoms](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>). [It followed a workshop the week before on a draft text on USSD services](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>). [The director general said the consultation was part of a reform of the sector's regulations](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the [September 2024 workshop on the draft decree](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7239950779405524992>), which the regulator convened and ran. Those taking part came from both inside and outside government, among them operators and civil society.
