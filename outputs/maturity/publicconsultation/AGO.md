# Angola

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** In September 2025 the telecommunications ministry [put its 86-article draft Law on Artificial Intelligence on its public consultation portal](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/storage/docs/Proposta%20de%20Lei%20Sobre%20a%20Intelig%C3%AAncia%20Artificial.pdf>). The report attached to the draft says any public or private body or individual could take part. On 27 August 2026 [the portal showed 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and 5 from the general public](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>). On 6 May 2026 the Institute of Administrative Modernisation [held a public consultation session on the draft Digital Government Law](<https://ima.gov.ao/web/noticias/ima-promove-sessao-de-consulta-publica-sobre-o-anteprojecto-de-lei-do-governo-digital>), with experts, jurists and academics. In July 2026 an official said the [National Artificial Intelligence Plan was in public consultation](<https://www.menosfios.com/governo-reforca-estrategias-para-o-uso-das-tecnologias/>). In April 2025 the ministry [opened the draft cybersecurity law package to public consultation](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/04/15/sessao-tematica-aborda-programa-de-desenvolvimento-e-expansao-das-telecomunicacoes-e-tecnologias-de-informacao/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [report attached to the draft law says the consultation was held before the draft goes to the Council of Ministers](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/storage/docs/Proposta%20de%20Lei%20Sobre%20a%20Intelig%C3%AAncia%20Artificial.pdf>). A [coordination committee for the national financial inclusion strategy was set up by Despacho Presidencial 201/23](<https://plataformacipra.gov.ao/public/ficheiros/arquivos/Draft_da_ENIF_Consulta_Publica.pdf>).

[The fourth Lusophone Internet Governance Forum, held in Luanda](<https://www.menosfios.com/governo-anuncia-medidas-para-acelerar-a-transformacao-digital-em-angola/>). [The administrative procedure code, Lei 31/22](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2022/lei-n-o-31-22-de-30-de-agosto/>). [An analyst's review of the draft AI law](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-angolas-proposed-law-on-artificial-intelligence>).

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy since 2023 has been found. Nor have the comments received, or a written answer to them.
