# Mauritius

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 23 August 2024 the Information and Communication Technologies Authority [issued a consultation paper](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>) on more spectrum for mobile services. [Its response to the paper, dated 7 November 2024](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>), sits beside it on the Authority's public consultations register. On 15 April 2025 the ICT ministry [put its draft child online safety report out for comment until 30 April](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Public-Consultation-on-Child-Online-Safety-Draft-Report.pdf>). [It invited parents, educators, young people, civil society, technology firms and the public](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Public-Consultation-on-Child-Online-Safety-Draft-Report.pdf>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the Authority's [public consultations register](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>). It lists the August 2024 spectrum consultation paper and the November 2024 response to it. It names the Authority as the body that ran the consultation. It dates each document.

***Also on file***. [Mauritius Internet Governance Forum](<https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/blockchain-for-good-alliance-and-bybit-join-the-mauritius-internet-governance-forum-to-advance-digital-trust-for-small-island-states-812258483.html>), a national forum run by the Halley Movement. [Consultation on a proposed fisheries information system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/561717>), a grant project and not a law or policy. [AFRINIC consultation on its own bylaws](<https://afrinic.net/community-consultation-review-of-bylaws.html>), regional. [Consultation on a proposed African payment services directive](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/250311-RFP-Consultancy-for-Feasibility-Study-for-a-PSDA.pdf>), by the African Union Commission and AfricaNenda.

***Not found***. No source from the last three years says a consultation was held because a rule required it. That holds for any digital law or policy.
