# Mauritius

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Information and Communication Technologies Authority [issued a consultation paper on 23 August 2024](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>) on making available additional spectrum for IMT. It then [published its response to that consultation paper, dated 7 November 2024](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>), and lists both documents on its own public consultations register. The response came out in the same year as the paper, under the Authority's name, and both items carry their dates on the register.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the Authority's [public consultations register](<https://www.icta.mu/public-consultations/>), which lists the August 2024 spectrum consultation paper and the November 2024 response to it. It names the Authority as the body that ran the consultation and gives the date of each document.

[Mauritius Internet Governance Forum](<https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/blockchain-for-good-alliance-and-bybit-join-the-mauritius-internet-governance-forum-to-advance-digital-trust-for-small-island-states-812258483.html>), a national forum run by the Halley Movement; [consultation on a proposed fisheries information system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/561717>), a grant project and not a law or policy; [AFRINIC consultation on its own bylaws](<https://afrinic.net/community-consultation-review-of-bylaws.html>), regional; [consultation on a proposed African payment services directive](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/250311-RFP-Consultancy-for-Feasibility-Study-for-a-PSDA.pdf>), by the African Union Commission and AfricaNenda.
