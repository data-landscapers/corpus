# South Africa

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** Parliament's communications committee [took written submissions on the Electronic Communications Amendment Bill from the public, industry bodies and civil society until 21 September 2026](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/communications-committee-extends-deadline-public-input-electronic-communications-amendment-bill>). The communications regulator, ICASA, [published draft amendments to its radio spectrum regulations on 15 May 2026 and held public hearings on 19 and 20 August](<https://www.icasa.org.za/news/2026/icasa-to-hold-public-hearings-on-draft-amendments-to-radio-frequency-spectrum-regulations-and-fees-regulations>) for those who had sent written comments. The Department of Communications and Digital Technologies [put its draft national artificial intelligence policy out for public comment on 10 April 2026 and later withdrew it](<https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/cape-town-host-activate-sa-summit-ai-policy-reset-puts-spotlight-governance/>). The National Treasury and the Reserve Bank [asked for written comments by 30 September 2026 on a draft crypto asset manual, and said comments on the draft capital flow regulations published on 17 April were being considered](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-seeks-industry-input-on-crypto-asset-manual/o1Jr5MxPa6PMKdWL>). The universal service agency [opened a public consultation on the 2010 determination on universal access](<https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/business/2026-10-10-public-consultation-to-update-universal-access-policy/>), reported on 10 October 2026. The Bureau of Standards [invited comment on draft AI standards until 10 November 2026](<https://www.polity.org.za/article/sabs-publishes-draft-ai-standards-for-public-comment-2026-10-21>). [Home Affairs took written comments on draft digital identity regulations until 6 June 2026](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/have-your-say-digital-identity-draft-regulations>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** ICASA [gave notice on 4 September 2026 of an inquiry under section 4B of the ICASA Act into the affordability of telecommunications services](<https://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Notice-of-Intention-to-Conduct-a-Market-Inquiry-into-the-affordability-of-telecommunications-services-in-SA-0260409.pdf>), inviting written answers to a questionnaire and public comment on a discussion document. A [gazette of 30 September 2026 withdrew that notice and carried a new draft notice](<https://www.icasa.org.za/uploads/files/Notice-of-Withdrawal-of-Notice-of-Intention-to-Conduct-a-Market-Inquiry-Into-The-Affordability-of-Telecommunications-Services-in-South-Africa.pdf>) inviting comment on a questionnaire and a discussion document.

***Also on file***. [Section 34(11) of the Electronic Communications Act, on the minister approving the frequency plan or giving notice that further consultation is required](<https://insidepolitic.co.za/malatsi-approves-2026-spectrum-plan-to-expand-broadband-and-wi-fi-access/>); [the Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>); [the Human Rights Commission's call for submissions on data centres, closing 30 July 2026](<https://www.sahrc.org.za/index.php/sahrc-media/news-2/item/4479-media-advisory-south-african-human-rights-commission-calls-for-public-submissions-on-data-centres-and-human-rights-in-south-africa>); [road traffic regulations on driving licences, gazetted for public participation](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-select-committee-briefed-proposed-amendments-regulations-governing-driving-licences>); [a digital identity policy awaiting Cabinet approval, then public comment](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/government/626663-bad-news-about-south-africas-newest-id-book-plan.html>); [a draft anti-money-laundering bill published for public comment](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/2026011501%20Media%20Statement%20-%20General%20Laws%20Amendment%20Bill.pdf>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years.
