# South Africa

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 30 October 2026 ICASA, the communications regulator, [published its analysis of the 32 submissions to its inquiry into network licences](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/icasa-takes-aim-at-dormant-telecoms-licences/j5alrvQAdwJvpYQk>). It gave its preliminary answers to them and invited further written comment. Parliament's communications committee [took written submissions on the Electronic Communications Amendment Bill until 21 September 2026](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/communications-committee-extends-deadline-public-input-electronic-communications-amendment-bill>). ICASA [held public hearings on draft spectrum regulations in August 2026](<https://www.icasa.org.za/news/2026/icasa-to-hold-public-hearings-on-draft-amendments-to-radio-frequency-spectrum-regulations-and-fees-regulations>). The communications department [put a draft artificial intelligence policy out for comment on 10 April 2026](<https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/cape-town-host-activate-sa-summit-ai-policy-reset-puts-spotlight-governance/>), then withdrew it. [Home Affairs took comments on draft digital identity regulations](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/have-your-say-digital-identity-draft-regulations>) until 6 June 2026. The Treasury and the Reserve Bank [asked for comments on a draft crypto asset manual](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-seeks-industry-input-on-crypto-asset-manual/o1Jr5MxPa6PMKdWL>) by 30 September 2026.

**Required consultation and standing body.** On 4 September 2026 ICASA [gave notice of an inquiry into the affordability of telecommunications services](<https://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Notice-of-Intention-to-Conduct-a-Market-Inquiry-into-the-affordability-of-telecommunications-services-in-SA-0260409.pdf>) under section 4B of the ICASA Act. A [gazette of 30 September 2026 replaced that notice with a new draft](<https://www.icasa.org.za/uploads/files/Notice-of-Withdrawal-of-Notice-of-Intention-to-Conduct-a-Market-Inquiry-Into-The-Affordability-of-Telecommunications-Services-in-South-Africa.pdf>), again inviting comment.

***Also on file***. [Section 34(11) of the Electronic Communications Act, on the minister approving the frequency plan or giving notice that further consultation is required](<https://insidepolitic.co.za/malatsi-approves-2026-spectrum-plan-to-expand-broadband-and-wi-fi-access/>); [the Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>); [the Human Rights Commission's call for submissions on data centres, closing 30 July 2026](<https://www.sahrc.org.za/index.php/sahrc-media/news-2/item/4479-media-advisory-south-african-human-rights-commission-calls-for-public-submissions-on-data-centres-and-human-rights-in-south-africa>); [road traffic regulations on driving licences, gazetted for public participation](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-select-committee-briefed-proposed-amendments-regulations-governing-driving-licences>); [a digital identity policy awaiting Cabinet approval, then public comment](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/government/626663-bad-news-about-south-africas-newest-id-book-plan.html>); [a draft anti-money-laundering bill published for public comment](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/2026011501%20Media%20Statement%20-%20General%20Laws%20Amendment%20Bill.pdf>).

***Not found***. No consultation is known to have been held because a rule required it. No standing body with outside members is known to have given its view.
