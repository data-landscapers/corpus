# Niger

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 20 July 2026 ARCEP, the telecoms regulator, [launched a public consultation](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>) on reorganising the spectrum plan for 5G. [It ran to the end of August 2026](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>) and was open to anyone. On 18 and 19 May 2026 the national communication observatory [held a workshop in Maradi on the draft ordinance on the electronic press](<https://anp.ne/maradi-cloture-de-latelier-sur-la-presse-electronique-avec-adoption-de-plusieurs-recommandations/>). [Journalists, online media owners, web activists and influencers reviewed and amended the draft](<https://anp.ne/maradi-cloture-de-latelier-sur-la-presse-electronique-avec-adoption-de-plusieurs-recommandations/>). The draft covers social networks and platforms.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is ARCEP's [public consultation on the 5G spectrum plan](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>). It ran from 20 July to the end of August 2026. The regulator did the asking, and it asked the public, not a closed group.
