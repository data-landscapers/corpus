# Niger

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** ARCEP, Niger's telecoms regulator, [launched a public consultation on 20 July 2026](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>) on reorganising the country's spectrum plan for 5G. The consultation [ran to the end of August 2026](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>) and was open to the public, so operators, companies and anyone else outside government could send their views on how the spectrum should be rearranged. The subject was the spectrum plan for 5G, a digital policy that the regulator decides, and the consultation stayed open for about six weeks.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is ARCEP's [public consultation on the 5G spectrum plan](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20557-niger-kicks-off-public-consultation-on-5g-spectrum-plan.html>), launched on 20 July 2026 and open until the end of August 2026. The regulator did the asking, and it asked the public rather than a closed group.
