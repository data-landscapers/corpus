# Mauritania

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of Digital Transformation, Innovation and Administrative Modernisation [held a validation workshop in Nouakchott on the draft national e-commerce strategy for 2026 to 2030](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/281808>). The national news agency AMI reported it on 28 October 2025. The ministry's Secretary General [described the workshop as a consultation with those concerned](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/281808>). [Among them were representatives of companies, civil society and academia](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/281808>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The WARDIP-Mauritanie project is under the Ministry of Digital Transformation. It [published a provisional award notice on 7 May 2026 for technical assistance to build a national platform for citizen participation](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Attribution-provisoire_platforme-citoyenne.pdf>). It calls the platform the Plateforme de participation citoyenne. The notice is a procurement, and [names no digital law or policy put to the public](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Attribution-provisoire_platforme-citoyenne.pdf>).

***Also on file***. A [national platform for citizen participation, at the stage of a provisional contract award](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Attribution-provisoire_platforme-citoyenne.pdf>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them.
