# DR Congo

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** [In March 2025 the regulator ARPTC opened a public consultation on bringing 5G to the country](<https://actualite.cd/2025/03/18/larptc-lance-une-consultation-publique-sur-lintroduction-de-la-5g-en-rdc-communique>). [On 6 August 2026 the digital economy minister met people from the digital sector](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/>) about an order of 20 July 2026 setting the sector's duties and fees. [The meeting followed a public outcry over the order](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/>). [A validation workshop on the draft national AI strategy, closed on 27 July 2026, included the private sector, academia and civil society](<https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166058_economie_numerique_les_participants_a_l_atelier_de_validation_technique_approuvent_le_projet_de_la_strategie_nationale_de_l_intelligence_artificielle.html>). [In July 2026 the rights group ACEDH said rural and indigenous communities had not been consulted on the RDC-PASS digital ID project](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-loperationnalisation-des-autorites-competentes-recommandee-pour-le-projet-rdc-pass/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** [The Réseau des acteurs du numérique, an industry body, was among those the minister met on 6 August 2026](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/>). It is not a standing body set up to advise on digital law.

***Also on file***. The [fourth Youth Forum on Internet Governance](<https://acp.cd/anglais/drc-is-committed-to-support-initiatives-that-strengthen-young-peoples-digital-skills-dg-artpc/>) was run by Internet Society DRC. No named law or policy was put to it.

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, comments received or written answer has been found. Nothing found names who took part in the 5G consultation. No rule requiring a consultation, and no standing body with outside members giving a view, has been found.
