# DR Congo

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** ARPTC, the post and telecommunications regulator of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, [opened a public consultation on bringing 5G to the country](<https://actualite.cd/2025/03/18/larptc-lance-une-consultation-publique-sur-lintroduction-de-la-5g-en-rdc-communique>), announced in a communiqué dated 18 March 2025. On 6 August 2026 the digital economy minister [held a working session with people from the digital sector](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/>) on interministerial order no. 015 of 20 July 2026, which sets the duties, taxes and fees of the digital sector. The session followed a public outcry over the order and was reported by the state news agency the next day. The digital economy ministry's [validation workshop on the draft national artificial intelligence strategy](<https://www.mediacongo.net/article-actualite-166058_economie_numerique_les_participants_a_l_atelier_de_validation_technique_approuvent_le_projet_de_la_strategie_nationale_de_l_intelligence_artificielle.html>), closed on 27 July 2026, included the private sector, academia and civil society.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Réseau des acteurs du numérique, an industry body, [was among those the minister met on 6 August 2026](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-larrete-interministeriel-sur-la-fixation-des-taux-ne-concerne-nullement-les-startups-reseau-des-acteurs-du-numerique/>) about the order.

***Also on file***. The [fourth Youth Forum on Internet Governance](<https://acp.cd/anglais/drc-is-committed-to-support-initiatives-that-strengthen-young-peoples-digital-skills-dg-artpc/>), run by Internet Society DRC and the organisers of the national internet governance forum, with no named law or policy put to it.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no set of comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years. Nothing found states who took part in the 5G consultation, a rule that required a consultation, or a standing body with outside members giving a view.
