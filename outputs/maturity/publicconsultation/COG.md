# Congo

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 8 and 9 June 2026 the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy [held a workshop in Brazzaville with 60 participants](<https://www.uneca.org/fr/stories/la-r%C3%A9publique-du-congo-veut-%C3%A9tablir-les-fondements-d%E2%80%99un-%C3%A9cosyst%C3%A8me-de-donn%C3%A9es-fiable>). The ministry held it with the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa and GIZ. Its aim was to help draft the national data governance strategy. It followed a mission of 2 to 6 June that consulted 21 institutions. They came from government, regulators, universities, the private sector, civil society and development partners. The commission had [announced consultations in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>) with telecom operators, financial institutions, universities and digital businesses. [It also announced a workshop that would include civil society and the private sector](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** A [multi-party advisory council](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/evaluation-du-developpement-de-linternet-en-republique-du-congo>) was set up to guide UNESCO's assessment of internet development in the country, reported in April 2026. Its task is that assessment and no digital law or policy.

***Also on file***. A [national internet governance forum](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/evaluation-du-developpement-de-linternet-en-republique-du-congo>) had no named law or policy put to it. The [women's consultative council](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/promotion-de-la-femme-des-etudiantes-formees-aux-metiers-du-numerique-171593>) signed a digital skills scholarship partnership on 11 September 2026. The [consultative council of civil society and non-governmental organisations](<https://ekolo242.cg/economie/investissements/a-la-decouverte-des-depenses-en-investissements-du-budget-2024-en-republique-du-congo/>) is a line in the 2024 budget. A [February 2026 workshop on the civil status data system](<http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226>) and [another on the local revenue platform](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-atelier-PDRCL_140226>) each name only officials and elected local office holders. Also on file is an [election report by Avenir NEPAD Congo](<https://lasemaineafricaine.info/association-avenir-nepad-congo-un-rapport-appelle-a-renforcer-la-transparence-du-processus-electoral/>) that the government did not ask for.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them. Nothing found states a rule that required a consultation. Nor has a standing body with outside members been found giving a view on a digital law or policy.
