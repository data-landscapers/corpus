# Togo

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of Commerce [held a two-phase national stakeholder workshop on strengthening the e-commerce regulatory framework](<https://commerce.gouv.tg/renforcement-du-cadre-reglementaire-du-commerce-electronique-au-togo/>), in Kara on 21 January 2025 and in Lomé on 24 January 2025. Businesses, consumer associations, regulators and financial service operators took part in it. The ministry's page on the workshops is the only record of it held. On 19 December 2025 a ministry [workshop on the draft National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/numeriquetg_mesptn-atd-giz-activity-7407900229221638144-WE3d>) brought in the private sector and civil society and gathered their contributions.

**Required consultation and standing body.** No standing body with members from outside government has been found. The nearest held fact is the [Forum National sur la Gouvernance de l'Internet au Togo](<https://intgovforum.tg/>), a national internet governance forum run by Internet Society Togo and held each year since 2010. Its 12th edition was on 19 June 2026, and [the data protection authority sat on a panel](<https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-contribue-aux-reflexions-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-a-togo-igf-2026/>) at it.

***Also on file***.
