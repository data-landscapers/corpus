# Sierra Leone

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Minister of Information and Civic Education, Chernor Bah, [said in November 2025 that the Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2025 had finished its final national validation](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>), with drafting input from Parliament, local councils, security agencies, the Electoral Commission, civil society, media, academia and the private sector. Cabinet then [approved the National Data Protection Policy and a new Data Protection and Right to Access Information Act, reported on 22 April 2026](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>); that report says the reform builds on years of consultations with civil society groups, the media, security institutions and development partners. On 28 February 2024 MoCTI [held a validation workshop on the National Broadband Strategy with mobile operators and service providers](<https://gleanersl.com/communications-ministry-poise-to-address-broadband-connectivity-challenges/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** MoCTI [held the inaugural meeting of its Technology, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Advisory Council, reported on 5 February 2026](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-holds-its-inaugural-meeting-of-the-technology-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-advisory-council-tieac/>); the council has members from the private sector and academia.
