# Sierra Leone

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** In November 2025 the information minister said the [Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2025 had finished its final national validation](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>). Civil society, media, academia and the private sector gave drafting input alongside Parliament and state bodies. Cabinet then [approved the National Data Protection Policy and a new data protection and access to information law](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>), reported on 22 April 2026. MoCTI held a [validation workshop on the National Data Governance Strategy on 7 and 8 August 2025](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/sierra-leone-leads-bringing-the-au-data-policy-framework-to-life/>). Over 130 people attended, among them civil society, academia and the private sector. Civil society groups [helped validate a draft roadmap for linking public finance systems](<https://a-zsl.com/dfmst-and-stakeholders-conclude-two-day-validation-exercise-on-draft-interoperability-roadmap/>) in October 2025. On 28 February 2024 MoCTI [held a validation workshop on the National Broadband Strategy with mobile operators](<https://gleanersl.com/communications-ministry-poise-to-address-broadband-connectivity-challenges/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** MoCTI [held the first meeting of its Technology, Innovation and Entrepreneurship Advisory Council](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-holds-its-inaugural-meeting-of-the-technology-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-advisory-council-tieac/>), reported on 5 February 2026. The council has members from the private sector and academia.

***Also on file***. The [advisory council on technology, innovation and entrepreneurship has met](<https://mocti.gov.sl/mocti-holds-its-inaugural-meeting-of-the-technology-innovation-and-entrepreneurship-advisory-council-tieac/>). The [Mano River Union Internet Governance Forum 2026 was a regional forum held in Liberia](<https://owlpress-sl.com/hon-mariama-bangura-calls-for-stronger-digital-laws-at-mru-internet-governance-forum-2026/>). Ministries and regulators met in Abuja on a [draft ECOWAS directive on electronic communications](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-commission-convenes-validation-workshop-for-the-draft-directive-governing-electronic-communications-in-west-africa/>). Only government ICT officers attended the [validation of the Open-Source Software Policy for the Public Sector](<https://mocti.gov.sl/over-50-ict-officers-and-digital-leads-from-across-government-gathered-to-validate-sierra-leones-first-ever-open-source-software-policy-for-the-public-sector/>). A [Bank of Sierra Leone fintech strategy plans its consultation as consultant tasks](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Request-EXPRESSION-OF-INTEREST1-REOI-for-Fintech-Strategy.pdf>). A [2016 open data policy for the extractive industries](<https://raic.gov.sl/images/downloads/2016-final_sleiti_open_data_policy.pdf>) was agreed by a group of several parties.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years. Nor have the comments received, or a written answer to them.
