# Mozambique

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The National Institute of Information and Communication Technologies (INTIC) [published the first version of the proposed National Artificial Intelligence Strategy and invited comments by email from public and private institutions, academia, development partners and citizens, from 4 May to 4 June 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). It also [held consultation sessions in the provinces with people from the public sector, companies, civil society and academia](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). Earlier, INTIC [put its draft Regulation on Cloud Computing Service Providers to public consultation until 30 March 2025, with a companion draft regulation on data centres](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>). The central bank [opened a public consultation on 22 April 2025 on a draft revision of its notice protecting mobile money customer funds](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** A [National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, established by decree, is to assess the final strategy before it goes to the government](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). The minister also [convened a working group across sectors to review the strategy](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The [National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, set up by decree](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>); the [minister's working group on the strategy](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>); [consultations planned by consultants for a national data governance strategy and data policy](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>); the [national consultation on political reform, which the presidency calls the Diálogo Nacional Inclusivo](<https://presidencia.gov.mz/?p=5179>); a [consultation on the social security regulation](<https://www.inss.gov.mz/2026/03/23/inss-lanca-auscultacao-publica-sobre-revisao-do-regulamento-da-seguranca-social/>); the [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>).

***Not held***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy is held from the last three years, nor the comments received, nor a written answer to them.
