# Mozambique

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** From 4 May to 4 June 2026 the National Institute of Information and Communication Technologies (INTIC) [invited comments on the first version of the draft National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://intic.gov.mz/consulta-publica-da-proposta-da-estrategia-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/>). Public and private institutions, academia, development partners and citizens could reply by email. INTIC also [held consultation sessions in the provinces with the public sector, companies, civil society and academia](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). In February 2026 the ministry [launched a public consultation on the national digital transformation strategy](<https://www.oeconomico.com/governo-mobiliza-o-pais-para-definir-estrategia-nacional-de-transformacao-digital-e-aposta-na-interoperabilidade-como-eixo-estruturante/>), at a national conference in Maputo. In May 2026 it said [regional consultations on the draft national digital transformation strategy had drawn contributions from civil society, youth, women and persons with disabilities](<https://intic.gov.mz/intic-promove-workshop-de-validacao-publica-da-estrategia-nacional-de-transformacao-digital/>). Earlier, INTIC [put its draft Regulation on Cloud Computing Service Providers to public consultation until 30 March 2025](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>). A draft regulation on data centres went out with it. On 22 April 2025 the central bank [opened a public consultation on a draft notice protecting mobile money customer funds](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/public-consultation-on-the-proposed-revision-of-the-notice-that-establishes-measures-for-protecting-customer-funds-received-in-exchange-for-mobile-money/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** A [National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, established by decree, is to assess the final strategy before it goes to the government](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). The minister also [convened a working group across sectors to review the strategy](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>).

***Also on file***. The [National Commission for Artificial Intelligence, set up by decree](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>), and the [minister's working group on the strategy](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>). Consultants planned [consultations for a national data governance strategy and data policy](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). The presidency ran a [national consultation on political reform, which it calls the Diálogo Nacional Inclusivo](<https://presidencia.gov.mz/?p=5179>). Social security held a [consultation on its regulation](<https://www.inss.gov.mz/2026/03/23/inss-lanca-auscultacao-publica-sobre-revisao-do-regulamento-da-seguranca-social/>). Also found: the communique of the [Africa Internet Governance Forum 2025](<https://igf.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/AfIGF2025-Communique.pdf>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy from the last three years has been found. Nor have the comments received, or a written answer to them.
