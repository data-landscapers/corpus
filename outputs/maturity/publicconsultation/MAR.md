# Morocco

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 7 September 2026 the minister delegate for digital transition, Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni, [held a dialogue in Rabat with more than 100 participants from civil society, administrations, institutions and universities](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). They met in [three workshops, on digital training for young people, digital skills for women and access to digital public services for people with disabilities](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). Their proposals [are to feed the update of the third national Open Government action plan, for 2024 to 2028](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>), as LeNew reported on 8 September 2026.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administrative Reform [holds the action plan, which carries commitments on digital inclusion](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). A [preliminary draft law, number 62.25 on climate change and the carbon market, went to public consultation](<https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/un-avant-projet-de-loi-soumis-au-sgg-le-maroc-encadre-le-marche-carbone>), reported in September 2026; it is not a digital law.

***Also on file***. A [public consultation on the preliminary draft climate law 62.25](<https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/un-avant-projet-de-loi-soumis-au-sgg-le-maroc-encadre-le-marche-carbone>); the [Morocco AI 2030 national roadmap, announced with no consultation stated](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>); an [experts' call for a law on digital platforms](<https://www.hespress.com/%d8%a3%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%a7%d8%ab-%d8%b3%d8%a8%d8%aa%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d8%a4%d9%83%d8%af-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a3%d9%85%d8%a7%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-1792419.html>); a [Council of Europe committee's opinion of 2012 on Morocco joining Convention 108](<https://rm.coe.int/16806fdcb3>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years, nor the comments received, nor a written answer to them.
