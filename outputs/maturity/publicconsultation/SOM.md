# Somalia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The National Communications Authority [held a one-day consultation on 16 February 2026](<https://nca.gov.so/nca-holds-consultation-on-telecom-consumer-protection-regulation/>) on its draft Telecom Consumer Protection Regulation. Operators, internet providers, submarine cable operators and consumer representatives gave their views. It then [convened a national consultation meeting](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-holds-consultation-meeting-on-cybersecurity-risk-management-and-compliance-framework/>), reported on 25 April 2026, on its draft Cybersecurity Risk Management and Compliance Framework. Government institutions, private companies, the education sector, civil society and technical experts took part. The meeting gathered input before the framework is finished. A civil society brief of December 2025 says the government [launched national consultations on a social media policy](<https://sonsaplatform.org/policy-brief-strengthening-governance-and-responsible-use-of-social-media-in-somalia-advancing-digital-rights-and-safeguarding-civic-space-a-civil-society-perspective>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The ministry's [Digital Inclusion Policy, dated August 2025](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>), says a workshop of interested parties validated it. The Ministry of Communications and Technology issued it to widen access to ICT services for marginalised groups.
