# Somalia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The National Communications Authority of Somalia [held a one-day consultation on 16 February 2026 on its draft Telecom Consumer Protection Regulation](<https://nca.gov.so/nca-holds-consultation-on-telecom-consumer-protection-regulation/>), at which telecommunications operators, internet service providers, submarine cable operators and consumer representatives gave their views. It then [convened a national consultation meeting, reported on 25 April 2026, on its draft Cybersecurity Risk Management and Compliance Framework](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-holds-consultation-meeting-on-cybersecurity-risk-management-and-compliance-framework/>), with government institutions, private companies, the education sector, civil society and technical experts taking part, to gather input before the framework is finished.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Ministry of Communications and Technology's [Digital Inclusion Policy for Advancing Access to ICT Services for Marginalised Groups, dated August 2025](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>), says it was validated by a workshop of interested parties.

***Also on file***. [The Digital Inclusion Policy, which says a workshop validated it](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>); [the Ministry of Finance's consultation on the 2026 budget](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/2026%20Budget%20Consultation%20Report.pdf>); [an East African Community survey of users of regional statistics](<https://www.eac.int/press-releases/3590-eac-calls-for-public-participation-in-shaping-better-regional-statistics>); [a national consultation planned under UNESCO's assessment of readiness for artificial intelligence](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/call-expression-interest-lead-national-expert-somalia-somalia-unesco-regional-office-eastern-africa>); [a civil society position of July 2026 urging ratification of the Malabo Convention, which the government did not ask for](<https://sonsaplatform.org/somali-civil-society-issued-advocacy-position-on-the-ratification-of-the-malabo-convention>); [an academic survey of those involved in the education management information system](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43621-026-02703-1>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, the comments received or a written answer to them from the last three years is held.
