# Burkina Faso

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 17 June 2026 the Ministry of Digital Transition [held a national validation workshop](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147201=>) on its draft national artificial intelligence roadmap for 2026 to 2030, where [government, the private sector, universities, civil society organisations and technical partners reviewed the draft](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/burkina-faso-validates-national-ai-roadmap-2026-2030-to-accelerate-digital-transformation/>). The ministry had [drafted it with the private sector, universities, research centres, civil society and the diaspora](<https://www.sidwaya.info/intelligence-artificielle-au-burkina-faso-vers-la-validation-dune-feuille-de-route-nationale/>). On 27 August 2025 it [opened a workshop on the national artificial intelligence action plan](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=b9c89091470055a016f89f5176165793&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=1024>) with the same groups, whose conclusions feed the draft plan. On 11 June 2024 a committee of the transitional legislative assembly [heard FED-numérique, Internet Society Burkina Faso and two independent specialists](<https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportcdd/eXnOdb937ARewNMzS8rzUcHTYyWJnXuZ5Nv4UF8i.pdf>) on the information systems security bill, and some of them handed in written observations.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The committee's report of July 2024 says the government's draft of that bill [had earlier gone to a national validation workshop with other ministries, civil society and the private sector](<https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportcdd/eXnOdb937ARewNMzS8rzUcHTYyWJnXuZ5Nv4UF8i.pdf>).

***Also on file***. A [dialogue on digital citizenship and digital governance](<https://libreinfo.net/burkina-faso-un-dialogue-direct-pour-une-gouvernance-numerique-inclusive-et-vertueuse/>) convened by Balai Citoyen and the Kofi Annan Foundation on 28 July 2026, with no named law or policy put to it; an [open government commitment on digitising the civil registry](<http://ogp.gov.bf/ogpse/engagement-detail/91?plan_id=3>), with no consultation stated; the [government's technical committee for checking draft bills](<https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportcdd/eXnOdb937ARewNMzS8rzUcHTYyWJnXuZ5Nv4UF8i.pdf>), with no outside members stated; the [SIPEN-UEMOA digital economy fair](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147847>) in Ouagadougou on 14 to 16 July 2026, with no named law or policy put to it.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no set of comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years. Nothing found states a rule that required a consultation, or a standing body with outside members giving a view.
