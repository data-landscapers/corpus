# Tunisia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The telecoms regulator INT ran [a first public consultation on sharing fibre-optic access infrastructure](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89>) from 26 September to 26 November 2025. It opened [a second, on the rules for that sharing, from 6 March to 2 April 2026](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89>). Anyone could answer by email or post. On 27 January 2026 INT [published a synthesis of the responses and the full contributions](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1054&typeactu=89>) to its consultation on value-added telecoms services. The Presidency's E-Government Unit [drew up the 2023-2025 open government action plan](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>) through three regional workshops. Its commitments cover open data and digital administrative services. The Tunisian Civil Forum for OGP took part, and over 320 proposals were collected, a results report of 4 August 2026 says.

**Required consultation and standing body.** [Government decree 2018-328 of 29 March 2018](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.tn/fr/legislations/5950/download>) requires public consultation on public policies and draft texts. It also requires a published consultation report.

***Also on file***. [Government decree 2018-328 on the organisation of public consultations](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.tn/fr/legislations/5950/download>). The [action plan's steering committee](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>), which did not meet between May and December 2025.

***Not found***. No consultation held because a rule required it has been found. Nor has a standing body with outside members giving its view.
