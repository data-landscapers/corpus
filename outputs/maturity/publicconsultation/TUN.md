# Tunisia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Instance Nationale des Télécommunications, the telecommunications regulator, ran [a first public consultation from 26 September to 26 November 2025](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89>) on the conditions for sharing fibre-optic access infrastructure. It opened [a second from 6 March to 2 April 2026](<https://www.intt.tn/fr/index.php?actu=1060&typeactu=89>) on the rules for that sharing, which any contributor could answer by email or post. The E-Government Unit of the Presidency of the Government drew up the [2023-2025 open government action plan](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>), whose commitments cover open data and the digitalisation of administrative services, through three regional workshops in Gafsa, Kairouan and Kef. Over 320 proposals were collected through a digital participation portal, workshops, email and public structures, and the Tunisian Civil Forum for OGP took part, as a results report published on 4 August 2026 states.

**Required consultation and standing body.** [Government decree 2018-328 of 29 March 2018](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.tn/fr/legislations/5950/download>) requires public consultation on public policies and draft texts, with a published consultation report.

***Also on file***. [Government decree 2018-328 on the organisation of public consultations](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.tn/fr/legislations/5950/download>); the [action plan's steering committee](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>), which did not meet between May and December 2025.

***Not found***. No report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them from the last three years has been found.
