# Lesotho

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The ICT ministry [circulated the draft Cybersecurity Bill 2026 for stakeholder review](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>) in August 2026. On 23 September 2026 it opened a second two-day workshop on the bill in Maseru, with the Lesotho Communications Authority and UNDP. The regulator [published a consultation document](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PROPOSED-AMENDMENT-TO-LICENSING-FRAMEWORK_public-consultation-document.pdf>) on amendments to its licensing classification and fees rules in October 2024. MISA Lesotho says the minister [met it about three times on the Computer Crimes and Cyber Security Bill](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/06/10/big-brother-is-watching-you-bill-raises-dust/>) and took written submissions in 2024. It says none of its recommendations were taken up.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The ministry [launched a National Cybersecurity Forum in July 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>) with public bodies, companies, academics, civil society and the technical community. It [first met in Maseru on 29 July 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/ministry-of-information-communications-science-technology-and-innovation-launched-lescomcsirt-and-inaugural-national-cybersecurity-forum/>). The Computer Crime and Cyber Security Bill 2024 [would set up a National Cybersecurity Advisory Council](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/COMPUTER-CRIME-AND-CYBER-SECURITY-BILL-2024.pdf>) to advise the government on policy.
