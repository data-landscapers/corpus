# Lesotho

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Ministry of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation [circulated the draft Cybersecurity Bill 2026 for stakeholder review in August 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>). On 23 September 2026 it [opened a second consultation workshop on the bill in Maseru, lasting two days, with the Lesotho Communications Authority and UNDP](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>), as the Department of Information reports.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The ministry [launched a National Cybersecurity Forum in July 2026 that brings together public bodies, companies, academics, civil society and the technical community](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>). With the Lesotho Communications Authority it [first held the forum in Maseru on 29 July 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/ministry-of-information-communications-science-technology-and-innovation-launched-lescomcsirt-and-inaugural-national-cybersecurity-forum/>). The Computer Crime and Cyber Security Bill 2024 [would set up a National Cybersecurity Advisory Council to advise the government on cybersecurity policy](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/COMPUTER-CRIME-AND-CYBER-SECURITY-BILL-2024.pdf>).

***Also on file***. The [National Cybersecurity Forum, first held in Maseru on 29 July 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/ministry-of-information-communications-science-technology-and-innovation-launched-lescomcsirt-and-inaugural-national-cybersecurity-forum/>); the [National Cybersecurity Advisory Council proposed in the 2024 bill](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/COMPUTER-CRIME-AND-CYBER-SECURITY-BILL-2024.pdf>); a [two-day validation workshop on the Electronic Transactions and Communications Bill, 2026, held on 28 September 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/development/micsti-on-electronic-transactions-communications-bill/>); a [validation workshop on the draft National Data Value Creation Blueprint](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/the-government-of-lesotho-tabled-a-national-data-value-creation-blueprint-draft-to-position-lesotho-competitively-on-the-international-digital-landscape/>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy has been found from the last three years, nor the comments received, nor a written answer to them.
