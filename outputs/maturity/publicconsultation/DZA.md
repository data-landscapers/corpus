# Algeria

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 12 May 2025 High Commissioner for Digitalization Meriem Benmouloud unveiled the national digital transformation strategy. Le Chiffre d'Affaires reports it as [the result of broad consultation with ministries, experts, economic operators and digital actors](<https://lechiffredaffaires.dz/algerie-numerique-2030-une-strategie-ambitieuse-pour-un-virage-digital-inclusif-et-souverain/>). She called it the fruit of wide consultation. The report names no hearing, workshop or call for comment. No account of what was said has been found.

**Required consultation and standing body.** No rule requiring consultation on digital law has been found. The nearest fact found is [the government's account of consultation on the strategy in May 2025](<https://lechiffredaffaires.dz/algerie-numerique-2030-une-strategie-ambitieuse-pour-un-virage-digital-inclusif-et-souverain/>). On 23 October 2025 the High Commission for Digitalization [launched a Scientific and Technical Committee of experts](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-appoints-expert-panel-to-accelerate-digital-overhaul>) to advise it. No view it gave on a named digital law or policy has been found.

***Also on file***. The [Scientific and Technical Committee](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-appoints-expert-panel-to-accelerate-digital-overhaul>) of the High Commission for Digitalization is an advisory expert body launched on 23 October 2025. A [National Data Governance Framework](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>) was gazetted in early 2026, with no consultation stated. The ombudsman's [citizens' complaints register](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/developpement/mpjv5j1x-la-numerisation-du-registre-des-doleances-des-citoyens-un-pas-qualitatif-vers-la-modernisation-de-l-administration>) is being digitised.

***Not found***. No dated hearing or call for comment on the strategy has been found. No published report of a consultation has been found either.
