# Rwanda

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** The Capital Market Authority, a regulator, [put its draft regulation governing intermediary service platform operators out for written comment](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>) on 12 February 2026. The regulation covers fintech firms, and the Authority asked fintech experts, lawyers, bankers, the payment industry, capital market participants and investors to review the draft and send their views, with a physical consultation meeting to follow.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the Capital Market Authority's [call of 12 February 2026 for public comments](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>) on its draft fintech platform regulation. The Authority put the draft out itself, named the groups it wanted to hear from, and said a physical consultation meeting would follow the written comments.
