# Rwanda

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** On 12 February 2026 the Capital Market Authority [put its draft regulation on intermediary service platform operators out for comment](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>). The regulation covers fintech firms. The Authority asked fintech experts, lawyers, bankers, payment firms and investors for their views. A consultation meeting was to follow. In June 2025 the Data Protection and Privacy Office [began workshops with health, media and education stakeholders on sector data protection guides](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Newsletter_June_2025.pdf>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the Capital Market Authority's [call of 12 February 2026 for public comments](<https://www.cma.rw/news-publications/press-center/call-for-public-comments-on-the-draft-regulation-governing-intermediary-service-platform-operators>) on its draft fintech platform regulation. The Authority put the draft out itself and named the groups it wanted to hear from. It said a consultation meeting would follow the written comments.
