# Comoros

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** In August 2025 the information ministry [held a national validation workshop in Moroni on the new Code de l'information et de la communication](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>). The code [is to bring digital media and online platforms under the law, and to act against disinformation and hate speech](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>). [The national journalists' union, the SNJC, was in the room](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>). [Private, community and island media say they were not invited, and only the two state media attended](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>).

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest held fact is the [August 2025 validation workshop on the draft code](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>), which the information ministry convened. [Once finalised, the text goes forward for promulgation](<https://www.al-fajrquotidien.com/reforme-du-code-de-linformation-aux-comores-entre-modernisation-necessaire-et-exclusion-contestee/>). The journalists' union was the one body from outside government named as present.

Its plan states that no law requires consultation.
