# Namibia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** Namibia's Ministry of Information and Communication Technology [put its draft Data Protection Bill out for public comment in October 2022](<https://action-namibia.org/government-seeks-public-input-on-draft-data-protection-bill/>), asking for written submissions by 30 November 2022, with the draft available from its website and from regional council offices. The ACTION Namibia Coalition, a civil society group, [filed a 19-page submission with the ministry on that date](<https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ACTION-Data-Protection-Submission.pdf>), and IPPR says [a version of its own paper on the bill went in as the coalition's input](<https://ippr.org.na/publication/data-protection-bill-not-fit-for-purpose/>). The Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia later [invited written submissions by 29 August 2025 on the minister's proposed policy directive to raise the minimum broadband speed](<https://www.cran.na/notices/>), after consultations held by the ministry on 21 May 2025 and by the regulator on 11 July 2025.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The regulator and the chamber of commerce have [a three-year memorandum of understanding, reported in July 2026](<https://economist.com.na/108753/general-news/ncci-and-cran-partner-to-accelerate-digital-economy/>), with a joint steering committee that oversees the memorandum itself.

***Also on file***. [A validation workshop in Windhoek for an AfCFTA-backed assessment of digital trade rules, with no law or policy put to the room](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7567/x>); [the regulator's memorandum and joint steering committee with the chamber of commerce](<https://economist.com.na/108753/general-news/ncci-and-cran-partner-to-accelerate-digital-economy/>).

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, the comments received or a written answer to them from the last three years is held.
