# Namibia

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** In February 2026 the ICT ministry [held a public stakeholders' engagement in Windhoek](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/cybercrime-bill-in-the-pipeline-for-namibia/>) on the draft Cybercrime Bill. Members of the public attended. Consultations with industry on a review of the national broadband framework [are complete](<https://observer24.com.na/cybercrime-bill-heads-to-parliament/>), the Windhoek Observer reports from the 2026/27 budget estimates. In 2025 the regulator CRAN [invited written submissions by 29 August](<https://www.cran.na/notices/>) on the minister's proposed directive to raise the minimum broadband speed. That followed consultations by the ministry on 21 May and by CRAN on 11 July 2025.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The nearest fact is an agreement, not a standing body that gave a view. The regulator and the chamber of commerce [signed a three-year memorandum](<https://economist.com.na/108753/general-news/ncci-and-cran-partner-to-accelerate-digital-economy/>), reported in July 2026. Its joint steering committee oversees the memorandum itself. No law or policy is named among its work.

***Also on file***. [A validation workshop on digital trade rules](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7567/x>), backed by the AfCFTA, with no law or policy put to the room. [The regulator's joint steering committee with the chamber of commerce](<https://economist.com.na/108753/general-news/ncci-and-cran-partner-to-accelerate-digital-economy/>).

***Not found***. No consultation report from the last three years has been found. Nor have the comments received or a written answer to them.
