# Botswana

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** A published notice [announces public consultations on the draft Cybersecurity Bill on 20 June 2025](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>). The consultations were [open to all stakeholders](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>), and the notice [invites comments to the ministry by 23 June 2025](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>). That is the one consultation on a digital law or policy held for Botswana in the past three years. No report of what people said, and no written answer to them, has been found with it.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The [National Planning Act, 2024, section 6](<https://www.npc.gov.bw/sites/default/files/resources/NATIONAL%20PLANNING%20ACT%202024.pdf>), sets up an advisory council of the National Planning Commission. The council belongs to development planning, and the Act is not a digital law. No law or published procedure that requires consultation on a digital law or policy is held, and no body is named to give an opinion on one.

***Also on file***. A [National Data Strategy workshop](<https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement>) of 7 to 11 September 2026, with no participant from outside government named; a [UNESCO workshop](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/botswana-maps-its-creative-industries-shape-more-inclusive-digital-future>) of 25 and 26 August 2026 on the digital environment of creative industries, with no named law or policy put to it; a UNESCO [AI readiness assessment report](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/en/africa-education-knowledge-platform/botswana-artificial-intelligence-readiness-assessment-report>); the energy regulator's [call for comments](<https://www.bera.co.bw/downloads/Electricity/BPC%202026-27%20Tariff%20Application%20-%20Public%20Notice/BERA%2028X5%20KO.pdf>) on an electricity tariff; a [consultation with trade unions](<https://gov.bw/trade-unions-strategic-partners-constitutional-court-consultations>) on a proposed Constitutional Court; the [advisory council](<https://www.npc.gov.bw/sites/default/files/resources/NATIONAL%20PLANNING%20ACT%202024.pdf>) of the National Planning Commission, set up by the National Planning Act, 2024.

***Not found***. No published report of a consultation on a digital law or policy, no comments received and no written answer to them has been found from the last three years.
