# Burundi

## Consultation on digital law and policy

**Consultation and what was published of it.** A [nationwide workshop on 18 April 2025 brought together public bodies, companies and civil society to set Burundi's priorities for its National Data Governance Strategy](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy>), by the account of the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa. The strategy was [validated on 7 November 2025 at a workshop presided over by the Vice-Minister of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy>). The commission says companies, academics and civil society took part in the process. Its page is the published account of both workshops.

**Required consultation and standing body.** The Ministry of Finance, Budget and Digital Economy [hosted the Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in Bujumbura from 16 to 21 February 2026](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/02/19/le-burundi-accueille-pour-la-premiere-fois-le-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-en-afrique-centrale/>), a regional forum with no named national law or policy before it.

[An East African Community survey of users of regional statistics](<https://www.eac.int/press-releases/3590-eac-calls-for-public-participation-in-shaping-better-regional-statistics>); [a statistics institute workshop of 8 May 2026 on a communication strategy for the statistical system](<https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements>); [a consumer association's call for a national meeting on telecommunications](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/07/31/telecoms-en-crise-au-burundi-labuco-denonce-linaction-des-autorites-face-a-la-degradation-des-services/>).
