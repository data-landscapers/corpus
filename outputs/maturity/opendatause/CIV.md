# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The 2024-2026 national open government action plan lists an open data portal among the government's initiatives since joining the OGP](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plan-dAction-National-2024-2026-VF-signe.pdf>). [Under commitment 6 it speaks of the Côte d'Ivoire Open Data portal as running and to be enriched with datasets](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plan-dAction-National-2024-2026-VF-signe.pdf>). [The Government Information and Communication Centre (CICG) launched the open data portal data.gouv.ci under the 2016-2018 action plan](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cote-divoire/commitments/CI0024/>). [By June 2020, after training, the petroleum and renewable energy ministry had published around 50 new datasets on it](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cote-divoire/commitments/CI0024/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 the CICG's official open data portal listed 211 datasets across economy, society, education, energy and agriculture](<https://data.gouv.ci/datasets>).
