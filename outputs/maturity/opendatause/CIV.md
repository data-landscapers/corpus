# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The 2024-2026 national open government action plan lists the creation of an open data portal among the government's initiatives since joining the OGP and, under commitment 6, speaks of the Côte d'Ivoire Open Data portal as running and to be enriched with datasets](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plan-dAction-National-2024-2026-VF-signe.pdf>). [The OGP/IRM page reports that the Government Information and Communication Centre (CICG) launched the open data portal data.gouv.ci under the 2016-2018 action plan, and that after training the Ministry in charge of Petroleum and Renewable Energies published around 50 new datasets on it by June 2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cote-divoire/commitments/CI0024/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ogp.gouv.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plan-dAction-National-2024-2026-VF-signe.pdf>).
