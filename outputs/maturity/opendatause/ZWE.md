# Zimbabwe

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [ZIMSTAT's guide for its Open Data Portal (ODP 2.0) describes a public frontend at zw.dbr.afdb.org where published datasets and dashboards are visible, from a workshop of 13-17 October 2025](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>). [ZIMSTAT (Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency) states that the OpenSDG portal, a hub for national SDG indicators giving the public access to Zimbabwe's SDG progress, was officially launched on 9 July 2025](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>).
