# Zimbabwe

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [On 9 July 2025 ZIMSTAT, the national statistics agency, launched the OpenSDG portal](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>). [The hub gives the public access to Zimbabwe's progress on SDG indicators](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>). [In July 2025 the OpenSDG Data Portal offered downloadable datasets](<https://www.thescroll.co.zw/zim-launches-sdgs-open-data-portal/>). [In October 2025 ZIMSTAT's Open Data Portal (ODP 2.0) had a public frontend at zw.dbr.afdb.org showing published datasets and dashboards](<https://zimstat.co.zw/odp2-0-tutorial-guide/>). [In June 2026 ZIMSTAT's open data portal disseminated key indicators including GDP, inflation and trade statistics](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-committed-to-modernising-data-collection/>). [ZIMSTAT is moving official statistics to a single Open Data Portal, expected to launch in 2026](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-committed-to-modernising-data-collection/>).
