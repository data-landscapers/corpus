[INSEED, the national statistics institute, lists the RGA-2 2025 agricultural census (73,818 family holdings enumerated, 11,454-holding sample) as an entry in its NADA microdata catalogue](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/catalog/16>). [INSEED's NADA microdata catalogue lists the EHCVM 2024 household survey (5,487 households) with version 1.0 of the cleaned data files and a public anonymised version, dated April 2026](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/catalog/15>).
