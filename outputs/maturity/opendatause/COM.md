# Comoros

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [INSEED, the national statistics institute, runs a national microdata archive (NADA) whose catalogue held 15 datasets on 23 August 2026](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/home>). [They include the 2025 agricultural census and the 2024 household survey; no open licence is stated](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/home>). [The RGA-2 2025 agricultural census enumerated 73,818 family holdings, with an 11,454-holding sample](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/catalog/16>). [The EHCVM 2024 household survey of 5,487 households has cleaned data files and a public anonymised version, dated April 2026](<https://www.nada.inseed-comores.org/index.php/catalog/15>). [On 29 August 2024 the UN country team and national partners presented the Open Data Comores portal in Moroni, giving free access to development data](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9ration-des-odd-pr%C3%A9sentation-du-portail-des-donn%C3%A9es-.html>).
