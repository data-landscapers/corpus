# Rwanda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Rwanda Public Procurement Authority launched an open contracting portal with procurement data back to 2016, downloadable in spreadsheet formats or by API](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [The Open Contracting Partnership's registry shows the Umucyo data, December 2013 to April 2026 and updated daily, for download in JSON, Excel and CSV under a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licence](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/145>), which bars commercial reuse. Two facts are held on the country's published government data, both given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
