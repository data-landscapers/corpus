# Rwanda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Rwanda Public Procurement Authority launched an open contracting portal with procurement data back to 2016, downloadable in spreadsheet formats or by API](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined. The source does not say what licence the portal's data carry.
