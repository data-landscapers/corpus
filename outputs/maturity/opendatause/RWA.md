# Rwanda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In November 2024 the Rwanda Public Procurement Authority launched an open contracting portal with procurement data back to 2016](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [The data download in spreadsheet formats or by API](<https://www.open-contracting.org/2024/11/13/rwanda-a-new-open-contracting-portal-to-promote-effective-and-inclusive-procurement/>). [In October 2026 the authority published its Umucyo data on tenders, awards and contracts, December 2013 to April 2026, updated daily](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/145>). [They download in JSON, Excel and CSV](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/145>). [The licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/145>), which bars commercial reuse. No source found states an open licence that lets anyone reuse the data.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 Minister Iradukunda said an open data portal provides research datasets](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>).
