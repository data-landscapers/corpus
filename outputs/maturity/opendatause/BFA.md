# Burkina Faso

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Burkina Faso's INSD states it grants a worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence to copy, modify and distribute its data, including for commercial use, with attribution, for data on its website and open data portal](<https://www.insd.bf/fr/licence-data-insd>). [CITADEL (Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo) says its national data portal for public decision-making has 147 datasets available and 12 in validation, with no public API, as of 17 June 2026](<https://data.citadel.bf/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [CITADEL says its portal holds more than 145 datasets from more than 5 national agencies and official portals](<https://data.citadel.bf/>).

**Reuse on record.** In November 2025 the government [told the UN Commission on Science and Technology for Development](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cstd2025-26_ai_c05_burkina_faso_fr.pdf>) that the data.gov.bf portal holds 261 datasets on education, health, ICT and agriculture, and that four applications have been built reusing them. It named two from the Burkina Open Data Initiative run by ANPTIC: Open Election, built for the November 2015 elections, and Nos écoles, nos données, a map of school data. In 2019 the [BODI section of the ANPTIC website](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/members/burkina-faso/commitments/BF0011/>) linked to four reusable data applications.
