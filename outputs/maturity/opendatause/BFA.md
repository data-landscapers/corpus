# Burkina Faso

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [INSD, the statistics institute, licenses its data free of charge to anyone, worldwide](<https://www.insd.bf/fr/licence-data-insd>). The licence covers data on its website and open data portal, and allows copying, changes and commercial use with attribution. [CITADEL, at the Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo, listed 147 datasets on its national data portal in June 2026](<https://data.citadel.bf/>). Twelve more were in validation, and it has no public API.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [CITADEL says its portal holds more than 145 datasets from more than 5 national agencies and official portals](<https://data.citadel.bf/>). That count, of June 2026, meets the top rung: four or more public bodies publishing, named or counted since October 2024. CITADEL, at the Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo, gathers datasets from national agencies and official portals.
