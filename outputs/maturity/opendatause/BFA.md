# Burkina Faso

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Burkina Faso's INSD states it grants a worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence to copy, modify and distribute its data, including for commercial use, with attribution, for data on its website and open data portal](<https://www.insd.bf/fr/licence-data-insd>). [CITADEL (Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo) says its national data portal for public decision-making has 147 datasets available and 12 in validation, with no public API, as of 17 June 2026](<https://data.citadel.bf/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [CITADEL says its portal holds more than 145 datasets from more than 5 national agencies and official portals](<https://data.citadel.bf/>). That count, of June 2026, meets the top rung: four or more public bodies publishing, named or counted since October 2024. CITADEL, at the Université Joseph Ki-Zerbo, gathers datasets from national agencies and official portals.
