# Nigeria

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Anambra State Government launched a redesigned website in October 2026 whose Open Government section gives access to budgets, audit reports, procurement plans, contract awards and open data](<https://techtrends.africa/anambra-launches-digital-gateway-to-unify-government-services-open-data-and-citizen-engagement/>). No dataset count or licence is given. [The 2018 national open data policy names Data.gov.ng as the national catalogue of open data](<https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf>). It requires agencies to link their datasets to the portal and to post a rolling release schedule there. It names no publishing ministry and gives no dataset count.

**Several public bodies publishing.** Anambra State Government is the one publisher on record, at the level of one state. The nearest held fact is [the 2018 policy's requirement that agencies link their datasets to Data.gov.ng](<https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf>), which names no agency.
