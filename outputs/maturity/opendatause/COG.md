# Congo

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The SYNA-MNV forest monitoring programme of the Ministry of Forest Economy publishes five datasets for download, the newest from 2024](<https://syna-mnv.cg/donnees-ouvertes>). They cover land cover, forest inventory plots, emission factors, administrative boundaries and deforestation series. [The programme publishes them under an open licence, asking only that reusers credit the source](<https://syna-mnv.cg/donnees-ouvertes>). [On 5 June 2026 the director general of the Institut national de la statistique said anyone can download data from its Open Data Portal](<https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/>). The files come in Excel, PDF and Word formats.

**Several public bodies publishing.** Two public bodies offer data for download: [the forest ministry's SYNA-MNV programme](<https://syna-mnv.cg/donnees-ouvertes>) and [the Institut national de la statistique](<https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/>). Only the forest programme states an open licence.
