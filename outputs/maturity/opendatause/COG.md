# Congo

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The director general of the Institut national de la statistique said on 5 June 2026 that the Open Data Portal is at work and anyone can access and download data from it in Excel, PDF and Word formats](<https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source held since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/>).

**Reuse on record.** No source held since October 2023 names a use of the data or a usage figure. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vox.cg/le-congo-et-ses-partenaires-signent-un-accord-de-partenariat-pour-capitaliser-les-donnees-statistiques/>).
