# Tanzania

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The National Bureau of Statistics and the Office of the Chief Government Statistician run the Tanzania Integrated Statistical Portal, which lets users explore and analyse administrative, demographic and survey data](<https://tisp.nbs.go.tz/>). [The Public Procurement Regulatory Authority publishes procurement data from procuring entities through the NeST Open Contracting Data Portal (data.nest.go.tz), updated daily with bulk JSON, CSV and Excel downloads updated weekly](<https://data.nest.go.tz/assets/docs/OCDS_Publication_Policy_PPRA.pdf>). Four facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
