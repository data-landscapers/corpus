# Tanzania

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Public Procurement Regulatory Authority publishes its open contracting data under a CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, as listed in February 2026](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/152>). The data comes from its NeST e-procurement system and is updated daily. [The authority's publication policy says bulk downloads in JSON, CSV and Excel are updated weekly](<https://data.nest.go.tz/assets/docs/OCDS_Publication_Policy_PPRA.pdf>). [In August 2026 the National Bureau of Statistics and the Office of the Chief Government Statistician ran the Tanzania Integrated Statistical Portal](<https://tisp.nbs.go.tz/>). It lets users explore administrative, demographic and survey data.

**Several public bodies publishing.** Three public bodies publish data online: [the procurement authority](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/152>), [the National Bureau of Statistics](<https://tisp.nbs.go.tz/>) and [the Office of the Chief Government Statistician](<https://tisp.nbs.go.tz/>). Only the procurement authority states an open licence.
