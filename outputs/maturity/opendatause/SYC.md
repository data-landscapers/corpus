# Seychelles

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In June 2026 the Ministry of Fisheries, Agriculture and Blue Economy said its Fisheries Information Management System (FIMS) was running](<https://mofbe.gov.sc/fiti-nmsg-showcases-seychelles-leadership-in-fisheries-transparency-during-cote-divoire-ministerial-visit/>). [It gives the public fisheries statistics, licensing and export data, which the ministry calls open data](<https://mofbe.gov.sc/fiti-nmsg-showcases-seychelles-leadership-in-fisheries-transparency-during-cote-divoire-ministerial-visit/>).
