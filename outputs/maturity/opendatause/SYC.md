# Seychelles

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Ministry of Fisheries, Agriculture and Blue Economy describes its Fisheries Information Management System (FIMS) as running and giving the public access to fisheries statistics, licensing and export data, which it calls open data](<https://mofbe.gov.sc/fiti-nmsg-showcases-seychelles-leadership-in-fisheries-transparency-during-cote-divoire-ministerial-visit/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The fisheries ministry is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mofbe.gov.sc/fiti-nmsg-showcases-seychelles-leadership-in-fisheries-transparency-during-cote-divoire-ministerial-visit/>).
