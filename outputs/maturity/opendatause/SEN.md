# Senegal

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [ANSD, the statistics agency, licenses its open data to anyone, free and worldwide](<https://nso-senegal.opendataforafrica.org/hiwidxb>). Users may copy, change, publish and distribute it, commercially or not, citing the source. [In June 2026 ANSD trained about thirty regional staff to publish datasets on its Open Data Platform 2.0](<https://aps.sn/louga-lansd-mise-sur-lopen-data-pour-ameliorer-la-qualite-et-laccessibilite-des-statistiques-regionales/>). [The national Géoportail offers official geospatial data to the public, with download services](<https://www.geosenegal.gouv.sn/une-nouvelle-ere-pour-l-information-geospatiale-au-senegal.html>). [The mines ministry publishes production data, and an EITI open data portal runs beside it](<https://eiti.org/countries/senegal>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Géoportail carries data from at least two public bodies](<https://www.geosenegal.gouv.sn/une-nouvelle-ere-pour-l-information-geospatiale-au-senegal.html>). They are the Centre de suivi écologique, with 2023 bushfire areas, and ANSD, with 2023 school locations. [ANSD's own platform was being extended to the regions in June 2026](<https://aps.sn/louga-lansd-mise-sur-lopen-data-pour-ameliorer-la-qualite-et-laccessibilite-des-statistiques-regionales/>).

***Also on file***. [ANADS (Archivage national des données du Sénégal), ANSD catalogue](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>); [Digital Earth Africa](<https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/publicsector/digital-earth-africa-and-aws-are-making-satellite-data-work-for-africa/>).
