# Senegal

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [ANSD closed a four-day regional workshop at Louga on 24 June 2026 on operationalising its Open Data Platform (ODP) 2.0, training about thirty regional staff to create and publish datasets on it as the platform is extended to the regions](<https://aps.sn/louga-lansd-mise-sur-lopen-data-pour-ameliorer-la-qualite-et-laccessibilite-des-statistiques-regionales/>). [Senegal launched its national Géoportail (Géo Sénégal), giving the public official base and thematic geospatial data, with consultation and download services for institutions' maps](<https://www.geosenegal.gouv.sn/une-nouvelle-ere-pour-l-information-geospatiale-au-senegal.html>). No source found states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Four facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Géoportail carries data from at least two public bodies, the Centre de suivi écologique (bushfire areas 2023) and ANSD (school locations 2023)](<https://www.geosenegal.gouv.sn/une-nouvelle-ere-pour-l-information-geospatiale-au-senegal.html>).

***Also on file***. [ANADS (Archivage national des données du Sénégal), ANSD catalogue](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>); [Digital Earth Africa](<https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/publicsector/digital-earth-africa-and-aws-are-making-satellite-data-work-for-africa/>).
