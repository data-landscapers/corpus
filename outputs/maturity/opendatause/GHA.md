# Ghana

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [It ties the portal to the initiative and to the draft data sharing policy, not to an adopted open data policy](<https://data.gov.gh/about>). [The World Bank completion report (23 Dec 2024) says the eTransform project, implemented by the Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation, built the Ghana Open Data portal, to which 315 datasets had been uploaded since 2020, but that the count had not grown for 3 years and the portal was down at the time of writing](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122324145517808/txt/BOSIB1971c4e0d0a219e2b1e9f369f20cb7.txt>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://data.gov.gh/about>).
