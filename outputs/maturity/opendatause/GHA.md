# Ghana

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Bank of Ghana launched a beta database portal on 5 February 2024 for extracting macroeconomic data](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Press-Release-Launch-of-Beta-Version-BOG-Database-Portal-05-02-2024.pdf>). It held 255 monthly and 86 quarterly series across five sectors, with no licence stated. [Statistics Denmark says the Ghana Statistical Service's StatsBank has improved access to the country's data since 2023](<https://www.dst.dk/en/consulting/news-from-international-consulting/2025/27-05-2025-Ghana-and-Morocco-Advance-In-Open-Data>). [The World Bank said in December 2024 that 315 datasets had been uploaded to the Ghana Open Data portal since 2020](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122324145517808/txt/BOSIB1971c4e0d0a219e2b1e9f369f20cb7.txt>). It added that the count had not grown for three years and the portal was down.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Bank of Ghana](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Press-Release-Launch-of-Beta-Version-BOG-Database-Portal-05-02-2024.pdf>) and [the Ghana Statistical Service](<https://www.dst.dk/en/consulting/news-from-international-consulting/2025/27-05-2025-Ghana-and-Morocco-Advance-In-Open-Data>) each publish data on portals of their own. [The National Information Technology Agency runs the Ghana Open Data Initiative catalogue](<https://data.gov.gh/about>), described in August 2026. No one source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data.
