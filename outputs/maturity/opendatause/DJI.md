# Djibouti

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [On 12 June 2025 Djibouti launched its National Summary Data Page under the IMF e-GDDS at the Central Bank](<https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/>). [Central Bank, INSTAD and Budget Ministry staff built it with the IMF and AfDB](<https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/>). [It is publicly accessible worldwide and hosted on the Djibouti Open Data Platform](<https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/>).
