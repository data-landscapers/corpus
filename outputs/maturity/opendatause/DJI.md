# Djibouti

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Djibouti launched its National Summary Data Page (PNRD) under the IMF e-GDDS on 12 June 2025 at the Central Bank, built by Central Bank, INSTAD and Budget Ministry staff with the IMF and AfDB, publicly accessible worldwide and hosted on the Djibouti Open Data Platform](<https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The National Summary Data Page, hosted at the Central Bank, is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/systeme-general-de-diffusion-des-donnees-ameliore-sgdd-a-djibouti-lance-sa-page-nationale-recapitulative-des-donnees/>).
