# Benin

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Direction generale du Budget (Ministry of Economy and Finance) runs a public, unauthenticated open-data API at opendata.budgetbenin.bj serving budget classification data (113 aggregates, held extract 5,351 rows across seven datasets, covering 2008-2024 and some 2025)](<https://backdata.budgetbenin.bj/public/api/agregats>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The finance ministry's budget directorate is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://backdata.budgetbenin.bj/public/api/agregats>).

***Also on file***. [Open Data change-management technical assistance project (ASSI)](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/fr/projets/benin-open-data>); [Portail national de données ouvertes](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>); [AfDB data portal (INStaD datasets)](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>); [UXP Directory (Benin national data-exchange dashboard)](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it, and none since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
