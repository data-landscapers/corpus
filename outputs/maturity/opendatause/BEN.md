# Benin

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Direction generale du Budget, in the Ministry of Economy and Finance, runs a public, unauthenticated open-data API at opendata.budgetbenin.bj](<https://backdata.budgetbenin.bj/public/api/agregats>). [It serves budget classification data: 113 aggregates in seven datasets, covering 2008-2024 and some 2025](<https://backdata.budgetbenin.bj/public/api/agregats>). [INStaD, the statistics institute, runs the Open Data portal of the national statistical system](<https://www.instad.bj/actualites/731-diffusion-des-donnees-statistiques-au-benin-l-instad-et-la-commission-de-l-uemoa-se-donnent-la-main-pour-la-migration-vers-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-open-data>). [A July 2025 workshop prepared its migration to the SDMX 2.0 version](<https://www.instad.bj/actualites/731-diffusion-des-donnees-statistiques-au-benin-l-instad-et-la-commission-de-l-uemoa-se-donnent-la-main-pour-la-migration-vers-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-open-data>). [In 2025 ASIN ran the national portal data.gouv.bj](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/transformer-les-donnees-geospatiales-du-benin-en-levier-de-politique-publique/>).
