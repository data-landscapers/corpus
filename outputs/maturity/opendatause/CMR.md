# Cameroon

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [On 22 April 2026 the National Institute of Statistics (INS) unveiled platforms giving direct access to macroeconomic series and, through a national data archive, to survey microdata](<https://seasonmedia.net/cameroun-lins-simplifie-lacces-aux-donnees/>). No licence was stated. [The INS launched its Open SDG platform on 17 September 2025](<https://leconomie.info/open-sdg-une-plateforme-pour-le-suivi-des-odd-au-cameroun/>), set up in 2024 with UNECA support. [In November 2025 an INS officer presented its Portail Open Data, Open SDG and ANADOC platforms](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/35%E1%B5%89-journee-africaine-de-la-statistique-le-littoral-mise-sur-linnovation-pour-une-donnee-au-service-du-developpement/>) as the means of making national statistics accessible.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The National Institute of Statistics is the one publisher on record](<https://seasonmedia.net/cameroun-lins-simplifie-lacces-aux-donnees/>), with its data archive and its macroeconomic series. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. None since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
