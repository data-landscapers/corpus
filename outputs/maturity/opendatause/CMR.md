# Cameroon

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Cameroon's National Institute of Statistics (INS) officially launched the Open SDG platform on 17 September 2025 to give the public access to data on the Sustainable Development Goals, set up in 2024 with UNECA support; the document does not state a dataset count or a licence](<https://leconomie.info/open-sdg-une-plateforme-pour-le-suivi-des-odd-au-cameroun/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The National Institute of Statistics is the one publisher on record.
