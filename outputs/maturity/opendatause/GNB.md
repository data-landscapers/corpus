# Guinea-Bissau

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The IMF reports Guinea-Bissau launched the National Summary Data Page on its national Open Data Platform, giving macroeconomic data in human- and machine-readable form](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/07/08/pr24262-guinea-bissau-implements-imfs-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>). [The National Institute of Statistics says the page, hosted on the national Open Data Portal, was launched on 14 June 2024](<http://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/COMUNICADO/Comunicado_Divulga%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Dados_e_GDDS_INE_FMI.pdf>). [It gives public access to key economic information](<http://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/COMUNICADO/Comunicado_Divulga%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Dados_e_GDDS_INE_FMI.pdf>). [In April 2015 the National Assembly's open data page published 4 open datasets in JSON via APIs](<https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos>). [They cover the institution, the ombudsman e-SIC, the legislative process and transparency](<https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos>). No source found states an open licence.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [In July 2024 the IMF said the page's data comes from the statistics institute, the finance ministry and the BCEAO](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/07/08/pr24262-guinea-bissau-implements-imfs-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>). [The page was developed with the same three bodies](<http://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/COMUNICADO/Comunicado_Divulga%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Dados_e_GDDS_INE_FMI.pdf>).
