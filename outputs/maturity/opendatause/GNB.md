# Guinea-Bissau

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The IMF reports Guinea-Bissau launched the National Summary Data Page on its national Open Data Platform, giving macroeconomic data in human- and machine-readable form](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/07/08/pr24262-guinea-bissau-implements-imfs-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>). [The National Institute of Statistics communique says the National Summary Data Page, hosted on the national Open Data Portal, was launched on 14 June 2024 giving public access to key economic information](<http://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/COMUNICADO/Comunicado_Divulga%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Dados_e_GDDS_INE_FMI.pdf>). [The National Assembly's open data page says it publishes 4 open datasets in JSON via APIs (institution, ombudsman e-SIC, legislative process, transparency); dated to the page's last modification, 1 April 2015 (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://www.parlamento.gw/transparencia/dados-abertos>). No source found states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Five facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The IMF states the data on the page is provided by the National Institute of Statistics, the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank of West African States](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2024/07/08/pr24262-guinea-bissau-implements-imfs-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>). [The communique says the page was developed with the National Institute of Statistics, the Ministry of Finance and the BCEAO national directorate](<http://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/COMUNICADO/Comunicado_Divulga%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Dados_e_GDDS_INE_FMI.pdf>).
