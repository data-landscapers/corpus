# Libya

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The General Information Authority (GIA), which runs the Open Data Platform, had recently launched indicators covering 91 datasets on it; it does not tie the platform to an open data policy, and gives the month only (workshop of 15 February 2026)](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop>). [The GIA, which runs the platform, launched the Open Government Data Platform at opendata.gia.gov.ly on 27 November 2025, simultaneously with the draft national open data policy, with first-phase datasets, APIs and indicators but no dataset count or named publishing bodies](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/>). No source held states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Three facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The GIA, which runs the platform at opendata.gia.gov.ly, reported statistics to February 2026 of 91 datasets, 9 active government entities participating and 8 sectors covered; the document does not tie the platform to an open data policy](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/11/includes-91-datasets-the-general-authority-for-information-launches-new-indicators-through-the-open-data-platform/>).

**Reuse on record.** No source held since October 2023 names a use of the data or a usage figure. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/11/includes-91-datasets-the-general-authority-for-information-launches-new-indicators-through-the-open-data-platform/>).
