# Libya

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The General Authority for Communications and Informatics lists its 2025 ICT workforce indicator as a downloadable XLSX dataset on its open data platform, created 2026-09-29](<https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/432987>). [The General Authority for Communications and Informatics lists the 2025 gender comparison dataset as an XLSX resource with a download option on its open-data platform, created 29 September 2026](<https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/987456>). No source found states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Five facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The GIA, which runs the platform at opendata.gia.gov.ly, reported statistics to February 2026 of 91 datasets, 9 active government entities participating and 8 sectors covered; the document does not tie the platform to an open data policy](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/11/includes-91-datasets-the-general-authority-for-information-launches-new-indicators-through-the-open-data-platform/>).
