# Libya

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The General Information Authority launched the Open Government Data Platform on 27 November 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/>), with datasets, APIs and indicators. [Its terms let anyone use the datasets lawfully and republish them](<https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/terms.html>), crediting the platform and the dataset. The data is supplied as it is, and no standard licence is named. [In September 2026 the communications authority listed its 2025 ICT workforce figures there as a downloadable spreadsheet](<https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/432987>). [It added a 2025 gender comparison dataset the same day](<https://opendata.gia.gov.ly/dashboard/dataset/987456>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The authority reported 91 datasets on the platform in February 2026](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/11/includes-91-datasets-the-general-authority-for-information-launches-new-indicators-through-the-open-data-platform/>). Nine government bodies were taking part, across eight sectors. [The authority had launched indicators covering those 91 datasets by mid-February](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop>), it told a workshop on cloud computing.
