# Mozambique

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The IPDP portal of the Fundo de Estradas puts its data under a Creative Commons licence with attribution](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>). [The Fundo de Estradas, a Mozambican public fund, runs the portal for data on road, bridge and other public infrastructure projects](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>). [It was validated as an OC4IDS publisher in 2026](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the Fundo de Estradas published public infrastructure project data on its IPDP portal](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>).

***Also on file***. [Centralized open data portal administered by the NDGC (proposed)](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>).
