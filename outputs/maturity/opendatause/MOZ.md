# Mozambique

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The IPDP portal of the Fundo de Estradas states its data is available under a Creative Commons licence with attribution](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>). [The Fundo de Estradas, a Mozambican public fund, runs the Portal de Divulgacao de Dados de Infra-estruturas Publicas (IPDP), which publishes data on road, bridge and other public infrastructure projects and was validated as an OC4IDS publisher in 2026](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>).

***Also on file***. [Centralized open data portal administered by the NDGC (proposed)](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>).
