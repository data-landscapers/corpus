# Kenya

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In 2025 the civic technology group Pollicy said the Kenya Open Data Initiative portal gives free access to government data in machine-readable formats](<https://pollicy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Policy-brief-13-Mar.pdf>). The portal it names is kenya.opendataforafrica.org. [President Kibaki launched the first national portal, opendata.go.ke, on 8 July 2011](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2011/07/kenya-govt-data-released-on-internet/>). It was set up to put census, budget, school and health facility data online. [The Open Government Partnership's record of Kenya's commitments says that portal later went offline](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/kenya/commitments/KE0034/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [Pollicy names the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics as the body that supports the portal](<https://pollicy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Policy-brief-13-Mar.pdf>). It says Kenya was the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to launch such an initiative. [The partnership's record lists a budget data portal planned by the National Treasury for July 2025](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/kenya/commitments/KE0034/>).

***Also on file***. [Kenya national data marketplace (proposed)](<https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/econographics/kenya-wants-to-monetize-its-data-but-at-what-cost/>). [Budget Data portal (planned, National Treasury, due July 2025)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/kenya/commitments/KE0034/>). [Kenya GeoPortal](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/kenya/commitments/KE0034/>). [National digital knowledge repository (proposed)](<https://parliament.go.ke/node/26196>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 states an open licence for Kenya's government data. None names two or more public bodies publishing it.
