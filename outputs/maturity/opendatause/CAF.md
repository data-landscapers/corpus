# Central African Republic

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The ICASEES mid-term review of the national statistics strategy (7-9 October 2026) says the Open Data Portal of the Central African Republic, administered by an ICASEES webmaster, has changed the conditions of access to official statistics](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). [ICASEES's Director General described its digital dissemination ecosystem as including the Portail Open Data (ODP RCA) and the NADA data archive portal, and the ODIN/ODP administrator reported more than fifty indicators updated on ICASEES platforms](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/398-icasees-la-troisieme-phase-dapurement-du-rgph-4-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-vers-la-finalisation-des-resultats-du-recensement>). [ICASEES's education data page offers yearbook and key-indicator files for download, each marked licence CC BY 4.0](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/publications/tableau-de-bord/donnees-sectorielles/education-nationale>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source held since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data: the statistics institute's portal is the one publisher on record, and its per-file download counts are not evidence of use. In May 2025 the country [launched its National Summary Data Page](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>), a portal of key macroeconomic and financial data that the IMF says is [hosted by ICASEES with the regional central bank BEAC and the finance ministry](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2025/05/19/pr-25153-central-african-republic-car-implements-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>); no licence is stated for it.
