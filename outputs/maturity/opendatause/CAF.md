# Central African Republic

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [ICASEES confirmed at a regional meeting that a new open licence is integrated on its Open Data portal and institutional website](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/364-icasees-participation-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-a-la-reunion-regionale-de-suivi-des-mises-a-jour-odin-odp>). [The ICASEES mid-term review of the national statistics strategy (7-9 October 2026) says the Open Data Portal of the Central African Republic, administered by an ICASEES webmaster, has changed the conditions of access to official statistics](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). [ICASEES, the national statistics institute, runs an Open Data Portal (ODP) at work, with a national workshop planned for end-June 2026 to update its indicators ahead of an ODIN assessment in mid-August 2026](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/364-icasees-participation-de-la-republique-centrafricaine-a-la-reunion-regionale-de-suivi-des-mises-a-jour-odin-odp>).
