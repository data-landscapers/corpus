# Central African Republic

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [ICASEES, the statistics institute, administers the country's Open Data Portal](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). Its review of October 2026 says the portal has changed access to official statistics. [Its platforms also include the NADA data archive, and more than fifty indicators on them were updated in 2026](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/398-icasees-la-troisieme-phase-dapurement-du-rgph-4-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-vers-la-finalisation-des-resultats-du-recensement>). [Its education data page offers yearbook and key-indicator files for download, each under CC BY 4.0](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/publications/tableau-de-bord/donnees-sectorielles/education-nationale>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** In May 2025 the country [launched its National Summary Data Page](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>), a portal of key macroeconomic and financial data. It covers national accounts, prices, government operations, debt, and the monetary and external sectors. [The IMF says three public bodies publish on it: ICASEES, the regional central bank BEAC and the finance ministry](<https://www.imf.org/en/News/Articles/2025/05/19/pr-25153-central-african-republic-car-implements-enhanced-general-data-dissemination-system>). No source names or counts a fourth.
