# Morocco

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The portal's own documentation page says the Agence de Developpement du Digital (ADD) manages Morocco's national open data portal and monitors the national Open Data action plan, which the Open Data steering committee (set up December 2019) oversees; no published date, so the page's retrieval date of 2026-08-08 is used](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/documentations>). [The Agence de Développement du Digital (ADD) launched the new version of the national open data portal on 2021-12-13, in Arabic and French, as part of implementing the Open Data action plan and Morocco's open government commitments (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-des-donnees-ouvertes>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The commitment sets as baseline, doubling by 2020, 10 institutions adhering to open data and 100 datasets published on data.gov.ma (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouvernement-ouvert.ma/engagements.php?engagement=4&lang=fr>).
