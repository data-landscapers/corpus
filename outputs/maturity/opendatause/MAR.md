# Morocco

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Agence de Développement du Digital manages the national open data portal, data.gov.ma, as its documentation page said in August 2026](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/documentations>). [In December 2024 the agency said connected APIs now publish the Haut-Commissariat au Plan's employment, price and national accounts figures on the portal automatically](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/publication-automatiques-des-donnees-des-apis-du-hcp>). [The Open Government Partnership's review counted over 500 datasets and over 70,000 downloads by the end of 2023](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/morocco/commitments/MO0034/>). [In December 2025 the national open data steering committee discussed a 2026 action plan](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/11eme-reunion-du-comite-de-pilotage-national-open-data>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The same review says 37 institutions were updating datasets on data.gov.ma by the end of 2023](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/morocco/commitments/MO0034/>). Over 180 open data officers had been named in public bodies by then. [The commitment behind it had set a baseline of 10 institutions and 100 datasets in 2018](<https://www.gouvernement-ouvert.ma/engagements.php?engagement=4&lang=fr>).
