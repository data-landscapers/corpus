# Gambia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics publishes GDP, CPI, PPI, trade and survey reports with microdata for download at gbosdata.org/downloads](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads>). [On 2 July 2026 its microdata catalogue held three survey studies: MICS 2000, DHS 2019-2020 and the Labour Force Survey 2018](<https://microdata.gbosdata.org/index.php/home>). [The latest entries are dated June and July 2025](<https://microdata.gbosdata.org/index.php/home>).
