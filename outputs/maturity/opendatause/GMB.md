# Gambia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics publishes GDP, CPI, PPI, trade and survey reports with microdata for download at gbosdata.org/downloads](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads>). [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics runs a microdata catalogue that, as of 2 July 2026, holds three survey studies (MICS 2000, DHS 2019-2020, Labour Force Survey 2018), the latest entries dated June and July 2025](<https://microdata.gbosdata.org/index.php/home>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
