# Togo

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Open Data portal and the Geoportail were launched in December 2024](<https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/>), run by the Agence Togo Digital. [The portal's terms of use say its data is open-licensed unless marked otherwise](<https://opendata.gouv.tg/pages/legal/cgu>). They name no particular licence. [In May 2025 the digital ministry launched a government geoportal with an open version for the public](<https://cio-mag.com/au-togo-le-ministere-de-leconomie-numerique-lance-une-plateforme-geoportail/>). [In April 2026 INSEED trained about thirty staff to move datasets to its new open data platform](<https://inseed.tg/atelier-national-de-formation-sur-la-modelisation-sdmx-et-la-mise-en-place-dune-plateforme-odp2-0-au-profit-des-points-focaux-du-togo/>), which serves INSEED and sector ministries.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Geoportail centralises data from 23 ministries and over 550 administrative units](<https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/>), a January 2025 report says. The data was collected in 2021 and 2022 under the PRISE project. [A post on the portal announced the launch ceremony of both platforms for 19 December 2024](<https://opendata.gouv.tg/fr/posts/lancement-des-plateformes-open-data-et-geoportail-open-data-du-togo/>).
