# Togo

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [INSEED reports that in April 2026 a national workshop in Lome trained about thirty statistical-system staff to migrate datasets from Togo's existing Open Data Portal 1.0 to ODP 2.0, the national open data platform for INSEED and sector ministries](<https://inseed.tg/atelier-national-de-formation-sur-la-modelisation-sdmx-et-la-mise-en-place-dune-plateforme-odp2-0-au-profit-des-points-focaux-du-togo/>). [La Voix de la Nation reports that the Open Data portal (opendata.gouv.tg) and the Geoportail (geodata.gouv.tg) were officially launched in December 2024 by the digital ministry through its operational arm, the Agence Togo Digital; the article names no open data policy](<https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/>). No source found states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Five facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Geoportail centralises data from 23 ministries and more than 550 administrative units, drawn from a 2021-2022 collection under the PRISE project](<https://lavoixdelanation.tg/index.php/2025/01/08/democratisation-de-lacces-aux-donnees-publiques-au-togo/>).
