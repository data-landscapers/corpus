# Angola

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The statistics office INE launched the Portal do Censo 2024 and the INE Angola app](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). They give anyone online census indicators, tables and maps, with statistics in PDF and Excel. [INE's own site publishes the definitive 2024 census results for download](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/NDc0MTE%3D>), with a summary report and an Excel file. Ten per cent of the microdata is to follow six months later. [The Open Budget Survey 2025 says the finance ministry published its citizens budget and in-year reports online on time](<https://internationalbudget.org/sites/default/files/country-surveys-pdfs/2025/open-budget-survey-angola-2025-en.pdf>). It asks the ministry to publish them in open formats, not only as PDFs.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [INE](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>) and [the finance ministry](<https://internationalbudget.org/sites/default/files/country-surveys-pdfs/2025/open-budget-survey-angola-2025-en.pdf>) each publish data online, on their own sites.

***Also on file***. [Portal de Compras Publicas](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/minttics-ainda-nao-corrigiu-pac-2025---documento-mantem-se-com-zeros-a-mais-e-despesas-na-ordem-dos-bilioes-de-kwanzas-64204.html>); [portal de dados abertos (PADA Component 2)](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/20251122_Plano_EAS_AS_V5_Clean_Final_site_1_db036c6b62.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it, and none since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
