# Angola

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Angola's statistics office INE launched the Portal do Censo 2024 and the 'INE Angola' mobile app, which give access to census indicators, tables and maps and to statistics in PDF and Excel formats, open to any user with internet access](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/ine-lanca-portal-do-censo-2024-e-aplicacao-movel/>). [INE states the definitive 2024 census results are also available on the INE portal (ine.gov.ao); the document is dated 2025-11-20](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Relatorio_Sintese_CENSO_2024_INE_Nov_2025_c5fba11d70.pdf>). Three facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
