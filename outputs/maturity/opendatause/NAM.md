# Namibia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Namibia Statistics Agency runs a NADA microdata catalogue (microdata.nsanamibia.com) listing 16 Namibian studies, most marked Open data access, including the 2023 census PUMS last modified 21 July 2026; the document is a capture dated 9 August 2026](<https://nsa.org.na/data-portals/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The Namibia Statistics Agency is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nsa.org.na/data-portals/>).
