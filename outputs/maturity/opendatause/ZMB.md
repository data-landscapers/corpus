# Zambia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The ZNSDI article says 58 dataset layers (road network, power utility networks, Continuous Operation Reference System) have been published through the country's existing geoportal platform, and that a new national geoportal run by the SMART Zambia e-Government Division launched alongside the ZNSDI Policy](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>). No source found states an open licence; a later answer places the country above that step. Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The geoportal integrates datasets from custodians such as the Zambia Electricity Supply Corporation and the Road Development Agency](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>).
