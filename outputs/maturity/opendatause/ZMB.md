# Zambia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The article on the Zambia National Spatial Data Infrastructure (ZNSDI) policy says 58 dataset layers have been published through the country's existing geoportal platform](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>). [They include the road network, the power utility networks and the Continuous Operation Reference System](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>). [A new national geoportal run by the SMART Zambia e-Government Division launched alongside the ZNSDI Policy](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>). No source found states an open licence for the published data. No other news story or official notice on the country's published government data has been found. A later answer places the country above that step.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The geoportal integrates datasets from custodians such as the Zambia Electricity Supply Corporation and the Road Development Agency](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>).
