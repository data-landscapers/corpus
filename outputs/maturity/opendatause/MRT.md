# Mauritania

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The EITI Board decision of 12 November 2024 states that Mauritania EITI, the government-backed multi-stakeholder body set up by Decree 2018-135, makes extractive revenue, production and export data available on its Data Warehouse portal with data sets downloadable in CSV](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** Mauritania EITI is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>).
