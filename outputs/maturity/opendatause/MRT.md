# Mauritania

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In November 2024 the EITI Board said Mauritania EITI makes extractive revenue, production and export data available on its Data Warehouse portal](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>). [The data sets download in CSV](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>). [Mauritania EITI is a government-backed multi-stakeholder body set up by Decree 2018-135](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>).
