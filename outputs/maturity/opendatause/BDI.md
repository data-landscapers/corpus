# Burundi

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In June 2025 INSBU's Accord de Licence Ouverte granted a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to use, copy, modify and distribute the platform's data](<https://www.insbu.bi/useragreement>). [Derivative products are allowed, commercially or not, with attribution](<https://www.insbu.bi/useragreement>). [INSBU says it publishes data on its Open Data Platform for users to access and reuse](<https://www.insbu.bi/useragreement>). [Some survey-based indicators had not been updated in November 2023, its acting director-general said](<https://lerenouveau.bi/insbu-la-publication-des-donnees-statistiques/>).
