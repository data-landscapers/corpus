# Sierra Leone

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Statistics Sierra Leone says its publications, metadata and microdata are released free of charge on its website, including an Open Data Sierra Leone data portal, with unrestricted datasets available online to all](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/2023_Stats_SL_Proactive_Publication_Scheme.pdf>). [The OGP National Action Plan III describes the government Open Data Portal as being revamped and as hosted at the Ministry of Information and Communications, managed by the National Open Data Council steered by the Right to Access Information Commission and Statistics Sierra Leone, as a one-stop shop for government documents (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Sierra-Leone_Action-Plan_2019-2021.pdf>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/2023_Stats_SL_Proactive_Publication_Scheme.pdf>).
