# Guinea

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [From 20 to 25 July 2026 the INS held a workshop, with African Development Bank support, on migrating to its Open Data Platform (ODP) 2.0](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/index.php/actu/418-atelier-national-de-renforcement-des-capacites-sur-la-modelisation-sdmx-et-la-migration-vers-odp-2-0>). [The platform is the system through which the INS disseminates official statistics](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/index.php/actu/418-atelier-national-de-renforcement-des-capacites-sur-la-modelisation-sdmx-et-la-migration-vers-odp-2-0>). [Its participants recommended feeding the platform regularly](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/index.php/actu/418-atelier-national-de-renforcement-des-capacites-sur-la-modelisation-sdmx-et-la-migration-vers-odp-2-0>). No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the INS data.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [In 2017 the ITIE-Guinee steering committee's policy said five bodies publish revenue data on their own sites: the Ministry of Mines, CPDM, SOGUIPAMI, the Chambre des Mines and the central bank (BCRG)](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>). [The data include extractive revenues](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>). [The Ministry of Mines publishes at www.mines.gov.gn](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>). [Mining contracts are online at www.contratsminiersguinee.com](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [ITIE-Guinee website (Excel and PDF publication)](<https://opendataitie-guinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Politique-GMP-en-Matiere-de-donnees-Ouvertes.pdf>); [World Bank Microdata Library (Global Findex 2025 Guinea file, GIN_2024_FINDEX_v02_M)](<https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/7911>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it. None since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
