# Somalia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [SNBS states that all data and datasets published on its Open Data Portal are provided under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC-BY 4.0), allowing users to download, copy, redistribute and make derivative works with attribution](<https://nbs.gov.so/terms-of-use-for-open-data-portals-and-datasets/>). [The Somali National Bureau of Statistics (SNBS) states that data and datasets are published on its Open Data Portal, free of charge and available in human- and machine-readable formats](<https://nbs.gov.so/terms-of-use-for-open-data-portals-and-datasets/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
