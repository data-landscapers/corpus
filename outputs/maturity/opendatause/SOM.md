# Somalia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Somali National Bureau of Statistics (SNBS) publishes data free of charge on its Open Data Portal, in human- and machine-readable formats](<https://nbs.gov.so/terms-of-use-for-open-data-portals-and-datasets/>). [All data and datasets there are under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC-BY 4.0)](<https://nbs.gov.so/terms-of-use-for-open-data-portals-and-datasets/>). [Users may download, copy, redistribute and make derivative works with attribution](<https://nbs.gov.so/terms-of-use-for-open-data-portals-and-datasets/>). [In March 2025 the Federal Ministry of Finance and Puntland's Ministry of Finance publicly released income and expenditure reports](<https://bareedo.org/somalia-open-data-day-2025-unlocking-transparency-for-a-better-future/>). [In November 2026 the federal finance ministry's transparency portal offered budget, fiscal, debt, procurement and audit publications as downloadable files](<https://mof.gov.so/>).
