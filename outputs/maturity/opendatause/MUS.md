# Mauritius

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Bank of Mauritius launched BOMStats, its statistical data platform at https://bomstats.bom.mu, on 3 September 2026, giving access to the Bank's official statistics with self-service analytics, an API and SDMX-format data sharing; the release gives no dataset count and no licence](<https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/bank-mauritius-expands-access-official-statistics-rollout-bomstats-platform>). [UNDP reports that Statistics Mauritius, with UNDP, officially launched the MauStats web-based data platform on 22 October 2025, a one-stop hub to collect, analyse and share data through interactive dashboards, built to SDMX data exchange standards](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-and-statistics-mauritius-launch-maustats-platform-world-statistics-day>).
