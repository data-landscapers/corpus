# Mauritius

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [On 3 September 2026 the Bank of Mauritius launched BOMStats, its statistical data platform at https://bomstats.bom.mu](<https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/bank-mauritius-expands-access-official-statistics-rollout-bomstats-platform>). [It gives access to the Bank's official statistics with self-service analytics, an API and SDMX-format data sharing](<https://www.bom.mu/media/media-releases/bank-mauritius-expands-access-official-statistics-rollout-bomstats-platform>). [On 22 October 2025 Statistics Mauritius and UNDP launched the MauStats data platform](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-and-statistics-mauritius-launch-maustats-platform-world-statistics-day>). [It is a one-stop hub to collect, analyse and share data through interactive dashboards, built to SDMX standards](<https://www.undp.org/mauritius-seychelles/news/undp-and-statistics-mauritius-launch-maustats-platform-world-statistics-day>). [The National Open Data Portal, set up under the May 2017 National Open Data Policy, releases government datasets as open data in CSV and XML](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/NDS_final.pdf>).
