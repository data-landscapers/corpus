# Gabon

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In February 2025 the download page of the Direction Générale de la Statistique offered files for anyone to download](<https://statgabon.ga/donnee_a_telecharcher/>). They include monthly consumer price indices, foreign trade bulletins, population projections and census results. The page also links to an open data database. Its reports include waves of a survey on the impact of COVID-19 on households in 2021 and 2022. The newest files on it date from April 2022. The page gives no terms of reuse for the files.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The statistics office's download page](<https://statgabon.ga/donnee_a_telecharcher/>) is the one place found where a Gabonese public body offers data for download. [In September 2026 Gabon hosted a CEMAC and African Development Bank workshop on regional statistics platforms](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-atelier-bad-cemac-14-recommandations-formulees/>). Those platforms are listed below.

[CEMAC-Data](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-ouverture-de-latelier-bad-cemac-sur-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-sdmx-et-limplementation-de-lopen-data-platform/>). [Open Data Platform (ODP 1.0/2.0)](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/09/20/statistiques-et-donnees-les-pays-de-lafrique-centrale-sur-la-meme-longueur-dondes/>). ***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 states an open licence for Gabon's government data. None names two or more public bodies publishing it.
