# DR Congo

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Ministry of Mines publishes validated mining statistics files for 2017 to 2025 on its statistics page](<https://www.mines.gouv.cd/public/statistique/pdf>), each stated to be open access and downloadable. No licence is named. [ITIE-RDC publishes extractive revenue, production, mining-rights and contract datasets in CSV, Excel and JSON as at November 2026](<https://www.itierdc.net/open-data/>), with a public API. [The National Institute of Statistics (INS) launched data.gouv.cd on 23 February 2026](<https://data.rhinocerosoftware.co/posts/lancement-de-la-plateforme-des-donnees-ouvertes-de-la-rdc-1/>) as the national open data platform. [Its statistical portal offers 15 indicators to explore and export, the newest dated February 2026](<https://www.ins.gouv.cd/portail-statistique>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Ministry of Mines](<https://www.mines.gouv.cd/public/statistique/pdf>), [ITIE-RDC](<https://www.itierdc.net/donnees/>) and [the INS](<https://www.ins.gouv.cd/portail-statistique>) each publish data on their own sites. [The Banque Centrale du Congo's new site of August 2026 shows exchange rates, inflation and other indicators](<https://numerico.cd/2026/08/17/la-bcc-a-desormais-un-site-internet-2-0-new-look-plus-fonctionnel>), with no download stated. No one source found since October 2023 names two or more of these bodies publishing data.
