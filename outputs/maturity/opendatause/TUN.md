# Tunisia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In March 2025 the government's Open Government Partnership office called the national portal data.gov.tn a major advance](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/fr/index.php/2025/03/19/lancement-de-lenquete-publique-en-ligne-sur-lelaboration-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-promotion-de-louverture-et-de-la-reutilisation-des-donnees-publiques/>). It said the portal is linked to several sector portals. The same notice cites government decree 2021-03 and circular 4 of 2024 as the legal basis for open data. It gives no terms of reuse for the portal's data.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The notice names the E-Government Unit at the Presidency of the Government as the body leading the open data work](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/fr/index.php/2025/03/19/lancement-de-lenquete-publique-en-ligne-sur-lelaboration-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-promotion-de-louverture-et-de-la-reutilisation-des-donnees-publiques/>). From 19 March to 20 April 2025 the unit ran a public survey of data users. The survey was the first step towards a national strategy on opening and reusing public data.
