# South Sudan

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In August 2026 the National Bureau of Statistics described an operational Open Data Portal with publicly accessible datasets, CSV and Excel downloads and API access](<https://www.nbs.gov.ss/resources/Data_Portal.php>). [Its Public Data tier is freely available to access, use and share without restrictions for any purpose](<https://www.nbs.gov.ss/resources/Data_Portal.php>). [On 21 April 2026 the bureau and UNICEF released the results of the 2025 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS)](<https://www.unicef.org/southsudan/press-releases/new-national-survey-shows-serious-challenges-children-south-sudan-also-clear-areas>). [Full results and detailed data are available on the NBS and UNICEF websites](<https://www.unicef.org/southsudan/press-releases/new-national-survey-shows-serious-challenges-children-south-sudan-also-clear-areas>).
