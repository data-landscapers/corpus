# Niger

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Under a licence dated 30 October 2024, Niger's Institut National de la Statistique lets anyone use, modify and distribute its data](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?page_id=4877>). [The worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence covers commercial and non-commercial use, with attribution](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?page_id=4877>). [In May 2025 the World Bank said the national Open Data Portal, with some of its data, is accessible on the statistics office's website](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/improving-access-to-quality-data-in-fragility-and-conflict-conte>). [On 18 September 2025 the national nutrition information platform PNIN launched its renovated portal, with a download space](<https://pnin-niger.org/web/?p=3641>). [The INS demonstrated it](<https://pnin-niger.org/web/?p=3641>).
