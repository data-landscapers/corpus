# Niger

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [Niger's Institut National de la Statistique grants, under a licence dated 30 October 2024, a worldwide, free, non-exclusive licence to use, modify and distribute its data for commercial and non-commercial purposes with attribution](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?page_id=4877>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The Institut National de la Statistique is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?page_id=4877>).

***Also on file***. [Illimi Niger multisectoral data platform](<https://www.lesahel.org/renforcement-de-capacites-en-intelligence-artificielle-appliquee-a-la-securite-humaine-le-bureau-de-la-coordination-des-nations-unies-au-niger-perfectionne-les-connaissances-de-140-cadres-des-s/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
