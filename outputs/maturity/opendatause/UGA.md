# Uganda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The procurement authority PPDA publishes open contracting data from July 2015 to October 2026, updated monthly](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/130>). The files can be downloaded as JSON, Excel or CSV. [They carry the Open Data Commons Attribution Licence, which lets anyone share and adapt the data with credit](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/130>). [In July 2026 a working group chaired by the Uganda Bureau of Statistics reported all 155 SDG indicators it reports, of 201, on the National SDG Open Data Portal](<https://sdgs.opm.go.ug/2026/07/17/july-2026-national-sdg-task-force-accelerated-action-as-uganda-enters-final-stretch-to-2030/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** Two public bodies publish data for download. [The bureau hosts the SDG Open Data Portal, where users can download the indicator datasets](<https://sdgs.opm.go.ug/2025/08/11/ugandas-sdg-open-data-portal-advancing-transparency-and-sustainable-development/>). [It also names its own open data portal, a spatial data portal and a gender statistics portal](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/meetings/2025/genderstat-forum-10/docs/Session8.2-Uganda-Johnstone-updated.pdf>). The other is [the procurement authority](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/130>). Only the procurement authority states an open licence.

***Also on file***. [Open data portal](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>).
