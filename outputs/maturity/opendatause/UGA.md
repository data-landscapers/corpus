# Uganda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** A working group chaired by the Uganda Bureau of Statistics [reported in July 2026 that all 155 SDG indicators it reports, of 201, are on the National SDG Open Data Portal](<https://sdgs.opm.go.ug/2026/07/17/july-2026-national-sdg-task-force-accelerated-action-as-uganda-enters-final-stretch-to-2030/>) with their metadata. The bureau also [names its own open data portal, a spatial data portal and a gender statistics portal](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/meetings/2025/genderstat-forum-10/docs/Session8.2-Uganda-Johnstone-updated.pdf>). The procurement authority [publishes machine-readable procurement data on the Government Procurement Portal](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/why-govt-is-moving-to-upgrade-procurement-por-NV_222664_022026>). CoST Uganda says [a project with the authority has worked since July 2025 to upgrade the portal's infrastructure data](<https://www.cost.or.ug/2025/12/24/prioritized-data-points-cost-uganda-leads-major-upgrade-to-prioritize-sustainability-and-climate-finance-data-on-gpp/>). No source says under what licence any of this data is published.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sdgs.opm.go.ug/2026/07/17/july-2026-national-sdg-task-force-accelerated-action-as-uganda-enters-final-stretch-to-2030/>).

***Also on file***. [Open data portal](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it, and none since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
