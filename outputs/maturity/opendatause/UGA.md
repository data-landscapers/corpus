# Uganda

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Data Technical Working Group chaired by the Uganda Bureau of Statistics reported that all 155 reported SDG indicators and their metadata have been uploaded to the National SDG Open Data Portal, of 201 SDG indicators](<https://sdgs.opm.go.ug/2026/07/17/july-2026-national-sdg-task-force-accelerated-action-as-uganda-enters-final-stretch-to-2030/>). [CoST Uganda says that since July 2025 a project with the PPDA has worked to reverse the decline in infrastructure data disclosures on the Government Procurement Portal and to upgrade it](<https://www.cost.or.ug/2025/12/24/prioritized-data-points-cost-uganda-leads-major-upgrade-to-prioritize-sustainability-and-climate-finance-data-on-gpp/>). Four facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
