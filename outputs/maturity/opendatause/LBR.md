# Liberia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [LISGIS, the statistics office, adopted CC BY 4.0 as its licence standard from 8 June 2026](<https://liberia.opendataforafrica.org/atrwrfc>). [Its notice grants anyone a free, worldwide licence to use and redistribute its data, including on its open data portal](<https://liberia.opendataforafrica.org/atrwrfc>). Commercial use is allowed with attribution. [Its microdata catalogue lists the 2024 agricultural survey as a public use file, open to all for research](<https://microdata.lisgislr.org/index.php/catalog/43>). [The EITI said in July 2026 that LEITI's online data portal had made extractive data much more open](<https://eiti.org/documents/liberia-2026-validation-report>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The EITI report names two public sources of production and export data by company](<https://eiti.org/documents/liberia-2026-validation-report>). They are the LEITI Data Warehouse and the Ministry of Mines and Energy's online cadastre. No count of the bodies publishing on a portal is given.
