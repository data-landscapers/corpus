# Chad

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The customs directorate's public data page lists two 2025 trade statistics documents for download](<https://douanes.td/douane/donnees-publiques/>). One covers exports and one imports through Douala and Kribi. Both are by the Chad Shippers' Council, and no licence is stated. [The page sorts the directorate's statistics, studies, activity reports and customs revenue under four headings](<https://douanes.td/douane/donnees-publiques/>). It describes itself as the home of all the statistics, reports and studies the directorate produces.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The customs directorate is the one public body found publishing data for download](<https://douanes.td/douane/donnees-publiques/>), as at 2025. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. None since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
