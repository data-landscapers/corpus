# Lesotho

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho publications page states, as of April 2025, that all listed reports can be downloaded, with Excel tables for national accounts, CPI and GDP](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data, from a single page of the Bureau: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The Bureau of Statistics is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).

***Also on file***. [Lesotho Open Data Initiative (LODI)](<https://pub.sbc4d.com/2022/lesotho_full_final.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it, and none since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
