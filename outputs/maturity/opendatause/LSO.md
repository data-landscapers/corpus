# Lesotho

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho publications page states, as of April 2025, that all listed reports can be downloaded, with Excel tables for national accounts, CPI and GDP](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data, from a single page of the Bureau: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source held since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).

**Reuse on record.** No source held since October 2023 names a use of the data or a usage figure. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Lesotho Open Data Initiative (LODI)](<https://pub.sbc4d.com/2022/lesotho_full_final.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 states an open licence for the data.
