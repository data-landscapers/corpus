# Botswana

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The article cites Statistics Botswana's own microdata portal as visible evidence of the office moving toward publishing datasets for reuse, though it names no dataset count and the licensing is described as still being explored (Creative Commons rather than non-commercial clauses)](<https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/data-botswanas-new-diamond-taavi-erkkola-gdowf/>). It is the only fact held on the country's published government data: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** Statistics Botswana is the one publisher on record. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data, and none since October 2024 names or counts the four that the top rung asks for. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/data-botswanas-new-diamond-taavi-erkkola-gdowf/>).
