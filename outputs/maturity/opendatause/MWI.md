# Malawi

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The OGP Malawi Secretariat announced on 2025-09-03 that Malawi had officially launched its new Open Data Portal, giving citizens access to government budget, development project and public service delivery data through dashboards and downloadable datasets, as part of the Third OGP National Action Plan; the document names no publishing ministry or dataset count and does not tie the portal to an open data policy](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>).
