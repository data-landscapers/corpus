# Malawi

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [On 3 September 2025 the OGP Malawi Secretariat announced the launch of Malawi's new Open Data Portal](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>). [It gives citizens budget, development project and public service delivery data through dashboards and downloadable datasets](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>). [It is part of the Third OGP National Action Plan](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>).
