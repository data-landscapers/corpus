# Mali

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [INSTAT's open data licence grants a free, worldwide, non-exclusive licence to use, copy, modify and distribute its datasets, tables, indicators and dashboards](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/doc/conditions-utilisation-donnees-ouvertes_doc.pdf>). [Derived products and commercial use are allowed, with source attribution](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/doc/conditions-utilisation-donnees-ouvertes_doc.pdf>). [In August 2026 INSTAT's data catalogue on microdata.instat.ml listed 107 studies, including the 2022 census (RGPH5) and agricultural surveys](<https://microdata.instat.ml/index.php/catalog/>). [In November 2026 FAO said INSTAT and the rural development planning unit CPS/SDR had published the 2023-24 agricultural survey results](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/agrisurvey/news/news-detail/mali-releases-2023-24-agricultural-data-under-the-50x2030-initiative/en>). [Interactive tables are on the national open data portal](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/agrisurvey/news/news-detail/mali-releases-2023-24-agricultural-data-under-the-50x2030-initiative/en>).
