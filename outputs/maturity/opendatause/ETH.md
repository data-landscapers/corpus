# Ethiopia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Ministry of Agriculture's National Agri Data Hub listed 1,910 datasets in RDF and JSON-LD in June 2026](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>). No data licence is stated. [The Ethiopian Statistics Service says in 2026 that it runs STATBANK, microdata repositories and web portals](<https://ess.gov.et/about-ess/departments/statistical-data-dissemination-and-access/>), and provides public use files. [A vendor case study of July 2025 says the Ministry of Innovation and Technology's National Open Data Exchange offers bulk downloads](<https://360ground.com/project-case-study/building-ethiopias-national-open-data-exchange-platform/>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The agriculture data hub lists datasets from two public bodies in June 2026](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>): the Ministry of Agriculture and the Ethiopian Institute of Agricultural Research. An international research centre also publishes there. [In 2018 the communications ministry and the Central Statistics Agency had posted 29 datasets on the national portal](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt>), before the three-year window.
