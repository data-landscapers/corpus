# Ethiopia

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Ethiopian Statistics Service says its dissemination office manages the national data dissemination platforms including STATBANK, microdata repositories and web portals, and provides Public Use Files for public access](<https://ess.gov.et/about-ess/departments/statistical-data-dissemination-and-access/>). [The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the Central Statistics Agency, under a signed memorandum of understanding, had posted 29 datasets in open format on the national open data portal, with three CSA experts trained to upload data (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt>). Three facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ess.gov.et/about-ess/departments/statistical-data-dissemination-and-access/>).
