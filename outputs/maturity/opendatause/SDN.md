# Sudan

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Sea Ports Corporation lists annual port statistics for 2007 to 2025 as downloadable files on its website](<https://sudanports.gov.sd/portal/statistics/statistics_list.php?menuItemId=2&q=%28Type%7Eequals%7EStatistics%29>). The latest was posted on 2 April 2026. [The files for 2019 to 2021 went up together in April 2023, and those for 2022 and 2023 in October 2024](<https://sudanports.gov.sd/portal/statistics/statistics_list.php?menuItemId=2&q=%28Type%7Eequals%7EStatistics%29>). Most files are in Arabic, and no licence is stated. It is the one publisher found on the country's government data.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [The Sea Ports Corporation is the one public body found publishing data for download](<https://sudanports.gov.sd/portal/statistics/statistics_list.php?menuItemId=2&q=%28Type%7Eequals%7EStatistics%29>), as at April 2026. No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. None since October 2024 names or counts four or more.

***Also on file***. [Open Data Inventory (ODIN)](<https://mokoro.co.uk/land-rights-article/state-of-land-information-in-sudan-an-open-data-assessment/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 states an open licence for the data or names two or more public bodies publishing it. None since October 2024 names or counts four or more.
