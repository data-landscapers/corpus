# Cape Verde

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The government launched the Portal da Transparência on 21 January 2026](<https://www.governo.cv/o-lancamento-do-portal-da-transparencia-representa-o-compromisso-do-governo-com-a-responsabilidade-publica-e-com-a-participacao-cidada-na-construcao-de-uma-sociedade-mais-justa-e-aberta/>). The minister said it shows in real time how public money is raised, planned and spent. [The OGP review says the finance ministry publishes budget execution and spending data on its website](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>). Other open data is spread across government sites and seldom updated. [Since January 2026 the fisheries directorate has published fisheries data on its Fisheries Information System](<https://fiti.global/cabo-verde-transforms-fisheries-transparency-with-new-fisheries-information-system-fis>), FiTI reports. It covers the vessel registry, catches, access agreements, trade and subsidies.

**Several public bodies publishing.** [Focal points from several bodies update the fisheries system](<https://fiti.global/cabo-verde-transforms-fisheries-transparency-with-new-fisheries-information-system-fis>). They include the fisheries directorate, the Maritime and Port Institute and the National Institute of Statistics. [The finance ministry publishes on its own site](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>).
