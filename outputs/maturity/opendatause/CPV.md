# Cape Verde

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Government of Cabo Verde launched the Portal da Transparência on 21 January 2026, which the Minister for State Modernisation said gives citizens open, real-time information on how public money is raised, planned and spent, for academic or informational analysis](<https://www.governo.cv/o-lancamento-do-portal-da-transparencia-representa-o-compromisso-do-governo-com-a-responsabilidade-publica-e-com-a-participacao-cidada-na-construcao-de-uma-sociedade-mais-justa-e-aberta/>). [The OGP IRM review says open data is available but dispersed across government websites and not often updated, with the Ministry of Finance publishing updated budget execution and expenditure data on its website](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** No source found since October 2023 names two or more public bodies publishing data. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.governo.cv/o-lancamento-do-portal-da-transparencia-representa-o-compromisso-do-governo-com-a-responsabilidade-publica-e-com-a-participacao-cidada-na-construcao-de-uma-sociedade-mais-justa-e-aberta/>).
