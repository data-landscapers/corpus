# South Africa

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The author, writing for the OGP Results Report, says South Africa has national open government portals, Vulekamali, Municipal Money and GoMuni, that aggregate public data, and that more data is being published than ever](<https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/when-open-data-misses-streetlight-rethinking-impact-joanne-parker-tq84f/>). [The City of Tshwane's mayor said its new Enterprise Data and Analytics Platform supports the continued development of the city's Open Data Portal; the document gives no dataset count and does not describe its contents](<https://www.citizen.co.za/rekord/news-headlines/2026/10/02/tshwane-launches-data-platform-with-csir-to-boost-service-delivery/>). [National Treasury's procurement office publishes eTenders data from February 2017 to September 2026, 35,309 tenders and 9,551 awards, for download in JSON, Excel and CSV under the Open Data Commons PDDL](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/143>). Three facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.
