# South Africa

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [National Treasury's transparency portal offers monthly eTender procurement data from May 2021 in CSV, JSON and Excel under CC BY 4.0, as at November 2026](<https://data.etenders.gov.za/home/index>). It also has a JSON API. [The Open Contracting Partnership lists the same Treasury data under the Open Data Commons PDDL](<https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/143>), 35,309 tenders and 9,551 awards to September 2026. [The South African National Biodiversity Institute publishes its 2025 national species checklist under CC BY 4.0](<https://ipt.sanbi.org.za/resource?r=zanationaltaxon>), 99,254 accepted names. [The environment department offers free geospatial data for download, with quarterly releases set for 2026/27](<https://www.dffe.gov.za/egis>).

**Several public bodies publishing.** Four public bodies publish data. Three are [National Treasury](<https://data.etenders.gov.za/home/index>), [the biodiversity institute](<https://ipt.sanbi.org.za/resource?r=zanationaltaxon>) and [the environment department](<https://www.dffe.gov.za/egis>). [The fourth, the public service department, leads the National Data Portal](<https://pollicy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Policy-brief-13-Mar.pdf>). [In July 2026 an OGP writer named Vulekamali, Municipal Money and GoMuni as public data portals](<https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/when-open-data-misses-streetlight-rethinking-impact-joanne-parker-tq84f/>). [The City of Tshwane runs an open data portal, its mayor said in October 2026](<https://www.citizen.co.za/rekord/news-headlines/2026/10/02/tshwane-launches-data-platform-with-csir-to-boost-service-delivery/>).
