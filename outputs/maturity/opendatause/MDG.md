# Madagascar

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [The Assembly's data is published under Licence Ouverte v2.0 (compatible CC-BY), free to reuse including commercially with attribution](<https://parlement.mg/opendata/>). [The Assemblée nationale de Madagascar publishes five parliamentary datasets (deputies, legislative texts, sittings, commissions, parliamentary groups) as JSON and CSV on its open data page and read-only REST API](<https://parlement.mg/opendata/>). Two facts are held on the country's published government data, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the portals themselves were not examined.

**Several public bodies publishing.** The National Assembly is the one publisher on record.
