# Madagascar

## Use of open data

**Data published for download, and under an open licence.** [In August 2026 the Assemblée nationale de Madagascar published five parliamentary datasets as JSON and CSV on its open data page and a read-only REST API](<https://parlement.mg/opendata/>). [They cover deputies, legislative texts, sittings, commissions and parliamentary groups](<https://parlement.mg/opendata/>). [The data is under Licence Ouverte v2.0 (compatible CC-BY), free to reuse including commercially with attribution](<https://parlement.mg/opendata/>). [In February 2025 the Ministry of Economy and Finance launched PortES, a socio-economic data portal with six components, giving data access to all development actors](<https://www.undp.org/fr/madagascar/actualites/portes-transparence-des-donnees-et-informations-socioeconomiques-madagascar>).
