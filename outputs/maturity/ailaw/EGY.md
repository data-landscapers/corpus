# Egypt

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** [FRA Board Decree No. 57 of 2024](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) regulates robo-advisors for investment and is AI-specific. It requires client suitability assessments and gives clients a right to discontinue automated transactions. A 2026 Legal 500 guide by a law firm also says the [Government is preparing a draft AI law](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) with the House of Representatives' Communications and IT Committee, and elsewhere calls it the draft currently before Parliament. [MP Amira Saber submitted a 24-article bill on governing AI applications to the House in June 2024](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3202754>), and [in October 2025 the House ICT committee chair said the ministry's comprehensive draft would go to the House in January 2026](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8148341>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** The same 2026 Legal 500 guide states that [no binding AI-specific statute is in force](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) in Egypt. The one binding AI-specific measure it names is the 2024 decree of the Financial Regulatory Authority board on robo-advisors for investment, which reaches that sector and no other. The decree dates from 2024 and the draft law from the guide's 2026 imprint.

***Also on file***. [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law on AI itself, applying across sectors, is in force.
