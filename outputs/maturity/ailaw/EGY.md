# Egypt

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** [FRA Board Decree No. 57 of 2024](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) regulates robo-advisors for investment and is AI-specific. It requires client suitability assessments and gives clients a right to discontinue automated transactions. A 2026 Legal 500 guide by a law firm says the [Government is preparing a draft AI law](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) with the House of Representatives' Communications and IT Committee. Elsewhere the guide calls it the draft currently before Parliament. [MP Amira Saber submitted a 24-article bill on governing AI applications to the House in June 2024](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3202754>). [In October 2025 the House ICT committee chair said the ministry's comprehensive draft would go to the House in January 2026](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8148341>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** The same 2026 Legal 500 guide states that [no binding AI-specific statute is in force](<https://www.legal500.com/guides/chapter/egypt-artificial-intelligence/?export-pdf=>) in Egypt. The one binding AI measure it names is the 2024 robo-advisor decree of the Financial Regulatory Authority. That decree reaches one sector only. [In October 2026 the House ICT committee chair said the Ministry of Justice had written over 80% of a draft AI law](<https://www.egyin.com/article/153991/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%85%D9%86-80-%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%87-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D9%85%D9%87%D8%AF>). It goes next to the Cabinet.

***Also on file***. [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law on AI itself, applying across sectors, is in force.
