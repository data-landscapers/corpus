# Congo

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** On 8 April 2026 the Congolese National Assembly [adopted a bill on the orientation and development of scientific research](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-recherche-scientifique-une-nouvelle-loi-pour-structurer-le-secteur-et-encadrer-lia/>). The bill introduces an ethical framework for AI and institutes a national committee to oversee responsible AI use. The report of the adoption, published by the Congolese news agency ACI, does not say the bill is in force. The bill is a law on scientific research, and AI is one of the matters it regulates.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The only measure held is [the research bill](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-recherche-scientifique-une-nouvelle-loi-pour-structurer-le-secteur-et-encadrer-lia/>), adopted by the National Assembly on 8 April 2026. Its AI provisions, the ethical framework and the national committee on responsible AI use, sit within a bill on scientific research. They cover AI within scientific research and not across sectors. No other law, decree or regulation on AI has been found for the Republic of the Congo.
