# Ethiopia

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Ethiopia has a draft and no rule in force. Writing in The Conversation in June 2026, two scholars say [Ethiopia has tabled a draft AI law that follows the EU's risk-based approach and bans unacceptable-risk AI systems](<https://theconversation.com/ai-regulation-in-africa-why-copying-the-european-model-wont-work-283524>). UNESCO's readiness assessment profile of the same month says the country's [legal framework is not AI-specific](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>) and that it has no binding legislation on the public procurement of AI systems, on algorithmic transparency or on liability for harm caused by AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With a [legal framework that is not AI-specific](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>) in June 2026, Ethiopia has no general AI law to apply. The [tabled draft](<https://theconversation.com/ai-regulation-in-africa-why-copying-the-european-model-wont-work-283524>) is not yet law, and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country.

***Also on file***. [National Artificial Intelligence Policy (Ethiopia)](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the draft has been passed, or that a rule on AI is in force.
