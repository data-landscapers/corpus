# Ethiopia

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Ethiopia has no binding rule on AI. UNESCO's readiness assessment profile of June 2026 says the country's [legal framework is not AI-specific](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>) and that it has no binding legislation on the public procurement of AI systems, on algorithmic transparency or on liability for harm caused by AI. The profile names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With a [legal framework that is not AI-specific](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>) in June 2026, Ethiopia has no general AI law to apply. UNESCO's profile describes the laws that shape the framework as general ones, and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country.

***Noted, not assessed***. [National Artificial Intelligence Policy (Ethiopia)](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>).

***Not held***. No bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI is held.
