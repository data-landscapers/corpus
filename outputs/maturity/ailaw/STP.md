# Sao Tome and Principe

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Sao Tome and Principe has no law on AI. UNESCO's country profile, published in January 2026, states that [the country has no AI-specific laws or regulations governing the development, use or deployment of AI](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>). The minister of education wrote the same in the preface to [the country's AI readiness assessment of 2025](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>). Neither names a bill or a draft.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no law or regulation specific to AI in January 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>), there is no general AI law to apply. The profile and [the readiness assessment](<https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000394953>) both treat the data protection law and its authority as the nearest rules the country has, and neither records a rule for one sector or one use that names AI.
