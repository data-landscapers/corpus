# Niger

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Niger has a draft in preparation and no rule in force. [The head of the data protection authority (HAPDP) said in July 2025 that it had been asked to present a draft AI regulation](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). Its peers in Burkina Faso and Mali gave it that task within the Alliance of Sahel States. The text would be a shared one for the three countries. The interview gives no date for the task and no timetable for the draft. No law, decree or regulation setting a rule for AI is recorded for Niger.

**General AI law and the law applied.** Niger has no AI law in force to apply. [The same interview](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) names the HAPDP as the body preparing the draft. It does not say which body would enforce it. No body holds powers under an AI law today. No rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded.
