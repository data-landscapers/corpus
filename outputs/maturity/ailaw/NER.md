# Niger

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Niger's data protection law sets a rule for AI. [Article 57 of Law 2022-59, in the authority's consolidated text of April 2026](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>), defines artificial intelligence. It requires AI developers, makers and service providers to comply with the law. Those operating from Niger must declare to the data protection authority, the HAPDP. Article 52 says a person must be told the criteria and data behind a processing that uses AI. [In September 2025 a jurist said the law places this declaration duty on AI developers](<https://www.lesahel.org/lintelligence-artificielle-un-outil-benefique-avec-diverses-contraintes/>), and that Niger has no framework specific to AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI itself has been found in force. [In July 2025 the head of the HAPDP said its peers in Burkina Faso and Mali had asked it to present a draft text regulating AI](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). The task came within the Alliance of Sahel States. The interview gives no date for the task and no timetable for the draft.

***Not found***. No published draft of the AI regulation has been found. No decision made under article 57 has been found.
