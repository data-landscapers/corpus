# Eritrea

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Eritrea has no law on AI. [A legal-status page last reviewed on 8 June 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>) states that Eritrea has no dedicated AI law and no AI-specific legislation, and that AI is not governed by any specific national framework. It names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation, and no ministry or body asked to prepare one.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no AI-specific legislation in June 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>), there is no general AI law in force and nothing to apply. The same page says no national framework of any kind governs AI in the country, so no regulator holds a duty to oversee it. No rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded either.
