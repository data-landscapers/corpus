# Rwanda

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Rwanda has no law on AI. A university centre wrote in September 2026 that [there is no AI Act in Rwanda](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). It said the country's framework for AI rests on the National AI Policy of April 2023. The laws on personal data and privacy support it. [A New Times opinion piece in November 2026 said Rwanda had no binding AI rules](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/40360/opinions/rwandas-skills-first-path-to-ai-governance>). It called for a standalone AI Act. Neither names a bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no AI Act in September 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), there is no general AI law to apply. The 2023 policy states the government's aims and binds no one. The data protection and privacy laws apply to AI only as they apply to any processing of personal data. No rule for one sector that names AI is recorded.
