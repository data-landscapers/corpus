# Rwanda

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Rwanda has no law on AI. A university centre wrote in September 2026 that [there is no AI Act in Rwanda](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), and that the country's framework for AI rests on the National AI Policy of April 2023, with the laws on personal data and privacy as supplementary support. It names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no AI Act in September 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), there is no general AI law to apply. The policy of 2023 that carries the field is a statement of the government's aims and binds no one; the data protection and privacy laws beside it apply to AI only as they apply to any processing of personal data. No rule for one sector that names AI is recorded.
