# Ghana

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Ghana has a draft and no rule in force. A report of 26 May 2026 says the proposed [National Information Technology Authority bill](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/what-are-they-hiding-tech-consultant-questions-rush-for-15-digital-bills/>), written to replace the 2008 law on the authority, includes regulation of emerging technologies such as AI and blockchain. It is one of 15 digital bills the government put out together, and the report records a consultant's objection to the speed of the consultation.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. The [draft bill](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/what-are-they-hiding-tech-consultant-questions-rush-for-15-digital-bills/>) treats AI as one of several emerging technologies an information technology regulator would oversee, and is not a law on AI itself. No source dated to September 2026 says it has gone to cabinet or to parliament, and none names a separate AI bill.

***Also on file***. [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree or regulation that sets a rule for AI is in force.
