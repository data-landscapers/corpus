# Sierra Leone

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Sierra Leone has no law on AI. The attorney-general and justice minister told a UNESCO meeting in Riyadh on 16 September 2026 that [the country has established a position on AI but has not yet enacted specific AI legislation](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-unesco-governance-saudi-arabia/>). The report of his remarks names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI, and no date by which one is to be written.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no specific AI legislation enacted by September 2026](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-unesco-governance-saudi-arabia/>), there is no general AI law to apply. The minister set out a national position on AI to UNESCO without naming a law to carry it, and the report gives no account of what that position requires of anyone. No rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded.
