# Mauritius

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Mauritius has binding rules on AI in two sectors. The [Higher Education (Use of Artificial Intelligence) Regulations 2026](<https://supremecourt.govmu.org/downloadPDF/legislation/18106230>), Government Notice 39 of 2026, were made by the minister under the Higher Education Act 2017 and came into operation on 31 March 2026. They oblige higher education institutions to adopt policies on AI and require the use of AI tools to be disclosed. The Financial Services Commission's [Robotic and Artificial Intelligence Enabled Advisory Services Rules of 2021](<https://www.orisonlegal.com/ai-legislation-for-mauritius/>) apply to licensees offering investment and portfolio management through AI-enabled algorithms.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The [higher education regulations](<https://supremecourt.govmu.org/downloadPDF/legislation/18106230>) and the Financial Services Commission's rules each reach one sector. Mauritius has no law on AI itself applying across sectors in any source dated to September 2026, and no bill or draft of one is named. Its AI strategy and its guidelines on the development and use of AI are not binding. No decision or penalty under the regulations is on record.
