# Mauritius

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Mauritius has a binding rule on AI in one sector. The [Higher Education (Use of Artificial Intelligence) Regulations 2026](<https://supremecourt.govmu.org/downloadPDF/legislation/18106230>), Government Notice 39 of 2026, were made by the minister under the Higher Education Act 2017 and came into operation on 31 March 2026. They oblige higher education institutions to adopt policies on AI and require the use of AI tools to be disclosed.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The [regulations](<https://supremecourt.govmu.org/downloadPDF/legislation/18106230>) apply to higher education only. Mauritius has no law on AI itself applying across sectors in any source dated to September 2026, and no bill or draft of one is named. Its AI strategy and its guidelines on the development and use of AI are not binding. No decision or penalty under the regulations is on record.
