# Malawi

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Malawi has no binding rule on AI. The Minister of Higher Education, Jessie Kabwila, said in June 2025 that the country [has not yet passed a dedicated AI law](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-higher-education-minister-dr-kabwila-reaffirms-govt-commitment-on-ethical-inclusive-ai-development/>). No rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country, and no published bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI has been found.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no dedicated AI law passed](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-higher-education-minister-dr-kabwila-reaffirms-govt-commitment-on-ethical-inclusive-ai-development/>) in June 2025, Malawi has no general AI law to apply. An AI bill has been announced alongside the national AI strategy, but no draft text of it has been found, and no regulation made for one sector, such as higher education, is recorded either.

***Not found***. No published bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI has been found.
