# Lesotho

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** No law or rule on AI has been found in force. [An analyst wrote in May 2025 that Lesotho has a proposed Artificial Intelligence Bill, cited to the government, that has not yet been tabled](<https://www.meig.ch/highlight-26-2025-why-strengthening-information-governance-is-crucial-how-good-intentions-can-compromise-personal-data-and-human-rights/>). [The analyst urged the government to table it](<https://www.meig.ch/highlight-26-2025-why-strengthening-information-governance-is-crucial-how-good-intentions-can-compromise-personal-data-and-human-rights/>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI itself is in force. [The proposed bill had not been tabled in May 2025](<https://www.meig.ch/highlight-26-2025-why-strengthening-information-governance-is-crucial-how-good-intentions-can-compromise-personal-data-and-human-rights/>), so there is no law yet to apply.

***Also on file***. [Artificial Intelligence Policy](<https://www.gov.ls/development/country-statement-of-the-kingdom-of-lesotho-at-the-united-nations-global-dialogue-on-artificial-intelligence-governance-delivered-by-the-honourable-deputy-prime-minister-in-palexpo-international-exhib/>).

***Not found***. No source says the proposed bill has been published or tabled, or that any rule on AI is in force.
