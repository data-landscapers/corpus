# Libya

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Libya has no binding rule on AI. An article in the Journal of the Academic Forum, published in November 2025, says the country has [a legislative vacuum on AI-related crimes](<https://journals.asmarya.edu.ly/jaf/index.php/jaf/article/view/803>), including deepfakes, and that its legislation needs updating to cover them. It names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI, and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [a legislative vacuum on AI](<https://journals.asmarya.edu.ly/jaf/index.php/jaf/article/view/803>) in November 2025, Libya has no general AI law to apply, and no body has been given powers under one. The national AI strategy and ethics charter the country has published are policy documents, not law.
