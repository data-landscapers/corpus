# Djibouti

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Djibouti has no law on AI. [La Nation, reporting the country's AI readiness assessment in August 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-ou-en-est-reellement-djibouti/>), said that no law specifically addresses artificial intelligence to date. It describes the Digital Code as a framework for data protection only. The article names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI, and no ministry asked to write one.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no law that addresses AI in August 2026](<https://www.lanation.dj/intelligence-artificielle-ou-en-est-reellement-djibouti/>), there is no general AI law to apply. The article reports the readiness assessment carried out with UNESCO's support, and sets the missing law beside it as part of where the country stands on AI. The Digital Code it names is the nearest rule the country has, and it covers personal data and not AI.
