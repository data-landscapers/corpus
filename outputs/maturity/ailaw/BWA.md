# Botswana

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** In August 2026 The Probe Journal wrote that [Botswana has no enacted AI-specific legislation](<https://theprobe.co.bw/the-ai-question-botswana-cannot-avoid/>). The essay says the ministry has described a National AI Policy as in its final stage for some time. It names no bill or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force, so none has been applied. The [same August 2026 essay](<https://theprobe.co.bw/the-ai-question-botswana-cannot-avoid/>) says AI is governed only indirectly, through the data protection, digital services and cybersecurity laws.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI has been found.
