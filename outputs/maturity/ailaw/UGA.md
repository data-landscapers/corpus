# Uganda

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Uganda has no law on AI. A university centre wrote in September 2026 that [Uganda has no AI laws in place and uses data protection laws in place of a dedicated framework](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>). An assessment by the ministry responsible for information and communication technology with the International Telecommunication Union, dated 2025, lists [the lack of an AI policy and of a legal and regulatory framework](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/uga/D-UGA-NAAILA-2025-PDF-E.pdf>) among its main findings.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no AI laws in place in September 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/artificial-intelligence-in-east-africas-public-sector-ai-adoption-and-regulatory-preparedness-within-east-africa/>), there is no general AI law to apply. The [2025 assessment](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/uga/D-UGA-NAAILA-2025-PDF-E.pdf>) recommends that Uganda develop a policy and a legal framework for AI. Neither source names a bill or a draft, or a rule for one sector that names AI.
