# Somalia

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Somalia has no binding rule on AI in force. Ecofin Agency, writing on 17 July 2026 about the launch of a university AI center, [cites an analysis published in May 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>) that reports Somalia has no national AI law. The same report says the country has no dedicated AI legislation. Nothing found records a draft, a bill or a decree on AI, whether published, out for consultation or before cabinet or parliament, so the position reported is that no rule on AI exists at the national level at all, and none is in preparation in any source found.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With no law on AI, there is no general AI law that applies across sectors, and none has been applied. The nearest fact held is the [May 2026 analysis reported by Ecofin Agency](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1707-57474-somalia-launches-university-ai-center-to-tackle-local-development-challenges>), which states that Somalia has no national AI law and no dedicated AI legislation. No regulation or order has been made, and no body has registered, licensed, decided or penalised under such a law.

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether a sector law or a draft on AI exists, or whether any source later than May 2026 changes the position reported.
