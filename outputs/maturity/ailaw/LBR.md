# Liberia

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Liberia has no binding rule on AI. A memorandum by Abdullah Kamara, reported in March 2026, says the country [lacks clear guardrails on how AI should be used or governed](<https://oraclenewsdaily.com/2026/03/29/liberia-tech-expert-issues-early-warning-as-ai-threatens-liberias-job-market-security/>) and recommends legislation to regulate high-risk AI applications. It says the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications, the telecoms regulator and the National Security Agency have yet to set AI-specific frameworks, and it names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no guardrails on AI in place](<https://oraclenewsdaily.com/2026/03/29/liberia-tech-expert-issues-early-warning-as-ai-threatens-liberias-job-market-security/>) in March 2026, Liberia has no general AI law to apply, and no body has been given powers under one. The memorandum asks for legislation; it does not say one is being drafted.
