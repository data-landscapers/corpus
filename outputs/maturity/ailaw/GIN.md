# Guinea

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** On 14 October 2025 the secretary-general of the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy [said Guinea lacks a legislative and regulatory framework on AI](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>) at the opening of a workshop to diagnose the country's position on AI. The official added that Guinea has not yet adopted a specific AI law.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The same official, speaking on 14 October 2025 at the opening of the diagnostic workshop, said Guinea has [not adopted an AI-specific law](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>), unlike other countries of the sub-region. The statement covers the whole legislative and regulatory framework on AI, and no law on AI of any scope is named. The statement is the ministry official's own account of the legal position on AI.
