# Burundi

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Burundi has no binding rule on AI. A review by Levellers dated 8 June 2026 says the country has [no dedicated AI law and no AI regulator](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>). It names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI, and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country. The review gives [the personal data protection law of 10 March 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>) as the main hard law bearing on AI; it is a data protection law, not a law on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no dedicated AI law in June 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>), Burundi has no general AI law to apply, and no body holds powers under one. The [data protection law of March 2026](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-burundi>) is the nearest instrument in force, and it applies to personal data across sectors rather than to AI.
