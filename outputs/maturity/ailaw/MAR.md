# Morocco

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Morocco has a binding rule on one use of AI. An interview published in July 2026 says [the revised electoral law for the 2026 legislative elections makes it a crime to use AI and digital means to alter the truth or to harm the private lives of individuals and candidates](<https://www.hespress.com/%d8%a8%d9%86-%d8%ad%d9%85%d8%b2%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d8%ac%d8%b1%d9%8a%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%84%d9%8a%d9%84-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b0%d9%83%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%b5%d8%b7%d9%86%d8%a7-1784231.html>). The government's [draft organic law 53.25](<https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf>), laid before the House of Representatives in October 2025, set two to five years' imprisonment and a fine of 50,000 to 100,000 dirhams. The broadcasting regulator's [decision 50-26 binds licensed radio and television not to broadcast misleading AI-generated or AI-altered election content](<https://www.theleveragedyears.com/ai-regulation-news/morocco-haca-decision-50-26-electoral-ai-content-2026>); it does not reach online platforms. [In June 2026 the digital transition minister said her ministry was preparing a digital administration bill with provisions specific to AI](<https://al3omk.com/1167498.html>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** The rule covers elections and not AI across sectors. A [private member's bill on regulating the use of AI](<https://www.hespress.com/%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-1494587.html>), of 17 articles, was received by the bureau of the House of Representatives in January 2025; no source dated to September 2026 says it has been debated or passed.
