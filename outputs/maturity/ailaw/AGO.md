# Angola

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Angola has a draft law and no rule in force. The ministry for telecommunications and information technology lists a [proposed law on artificial intelligence](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>) on its public consultation portal, which in August 2026 recorded 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and five from the public. A review published in December 2025 describes [the draft as a comprehensive framework](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-angolas-proposed-law-on-artificial-intelligence>) for developing, supplying and using AI, built on levels of risk and reaching providers outside the country.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. The [draft under consultation](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>) would apply to AI across sectors and not to one sector or one use. It has not been sent to the Council of Ministers or the National Assembly in any source dated to September 2026.

***Also on file***. [Revised Personal Data Protection Law (2011 law under revision)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/19/why-data-protection-has-become-africas-default-ai-policy-tool/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree or regulation that sets a rule for AI is in force.
