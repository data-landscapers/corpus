# Angola

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Angola has a draft law and no rule in force. The ministry for telecommunications and information technology lists a [proposed law on artificial intelligence](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>) on its public consultation portal. In August 2026 the portal recorded 18 contributions from companies, 18 from specialists and five from the public. A review published in December 2025 describes [the draft as a comprehensive framework](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-angolas-proposed-law-on-artificial-intelligence>) for developing, supplying and using AI. It is built on levels of risk and reaches providers outside the country. [The draft has 86 articles in nine chapters](<https://pti.ao/governo-apresenta-proposta-de-lei-sobre-inteligencia-artificial/>), and [sets prison terms of one to 12 years and fines of up to Kz 1.5 billion for firms](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/lei-sobre-inteligencia-artificial-preve-multas-de-ate-1500-milhoes-kz-e-prisao-ate-12-anos-68260.html>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. The [draft under consultation](<https://consultapublica.minttics.gov.ao/>) would apply to AI across sectors and not to one sector or one use. [The Council of Ministers had the proposed law on its agenda on 29 April 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/celebracao-do-contrato-de-producao-petrolifera-no-bloco-kon-4-em-destaque-na-4a-sessao-do-conselho-de-ministros/>). [On 30 April 2026 the ministry's national director of telecommunications said the proposed law, with complementary diplomas, was under review by the Council of Ministers](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/minttics-destaca-potencial-do-data-center-e-cloud-nacional-para-reforcar-a-adopcao-da-ia-no-pais/>). No source found shows it before the National Assembly.

***Also on file***. [Revised Personal Data Protection Law (2011 law under revision)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/19/why-data-protection-has-become-africas-default-ai-policy-tool/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree or regulation that sets a rule for AI is in force.
