# Cameroon

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** [Levellers said in June 2026 that Cameroon has no AI-specific statute and no bill creating an AI regulator](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-cameroon>). It says AI is governed indirectly, through the data protection law of December 2024 and the cybersecurity laws of 2010.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No general AI law has been found. [The national AI strategy of July 2025 proposes a framework law and an AI authority](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-cameroon>), and Levellers says neither has an enacted text.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names a draft law on AI.
