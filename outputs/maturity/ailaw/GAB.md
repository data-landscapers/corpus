# Gabon

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Gabon has a binding rule on one use of AI. A report of August 2026 cites [an ordinance of April 2026 governing AI-generated content](<https://www.echosdeleco.com/articles/ia-le-gabon-dans-le-top-13-africain-mais-le-fosse-entre-les-engagements-officiels-et-la-realite-reste-beant>), which among other things prohibits deepfakes. The report gives no number for the ordinance and quotes none of its articles. It sets the ordinance beside a national technical committee on AI and a national AI agenda for 2026 to 2036.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The [ordinance of April 2026](<https://www.echosdeleco.com/articles/ia-le-gabon-dans-le-top-13-africain-mais-le-fosse-entre-les-engagements-officiels-et-la-realite-reste-beant>) covers content generated by AI and not AI across sectors. Gabon has no law on AI itself in any source dated to September 2026, and no bill or draft of one is named. No prosecution, order or penalty under the ordinance is on record, and the body that enforces it is not named.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a law on AI itself, applying across sectors, is in force.
