# Zimbabwe

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Zimbabwe has no AI-specific rule in force. On 27 July 2026 Tashinga Magaya of African Law Matters wrote that the [National AI Strategy is not a law](<https://www.africanlawmatters.com/blog/strategy-without-statute-reading-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy>), that the proposed regulator has no enabling Act, and that the Cyber and Data Protection Act is the law that applies to AI today. She urged that a draft AI Act be introduced within twelve to eighteen months.

**General AI law and the law applied.** As at 27 July 2026, [no AI act exists](<https://www.africanlawmatters.com/blog/strategy-without-statute-reading-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy>) in Zimbabwe. The National Digital Regulatory Committee named in the strategy has no enabling legislation, and a draft AI Act is still to be introduced to Parliament. The strategy is the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2026-2030, and the Cyber and Data Protection Act is the law that applies to AI today.
