# Mozambique

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Mozambique has a draft for one sector and no rule in force. The education and culture minister said on 16 September 2026 that [a law to regulate the use of AI in education is almost ready](<https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/69322>) and will go to the Council of Ministers for approval before the end of 2026. It would cover every institution from primary school to higher education.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. A month earlier the chairman of the national institute for information and communication technologies said that [Mozambique does not yet have specific legislation governing AI](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/intic-drafts-copyright-and-ai-legislation-to-protect-digital-creators/>) and called for a dedicated legal framework. The education law would cover one sector, and no draft of a law on AI itself is named.
