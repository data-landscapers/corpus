# Mozambique

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** No law or rule on AI has been found in force. In August 2026 the chairman of the national institute for information and communication technologies said that [Mozambique does not yet have specific legislation governing AI](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/intic-drafts-copyright-and-ai-legislation-to-protect-digital-creators/>) and called for a dedicated legal framework. [UNESCO's profile of December 2025 also says Mozambique has no specific AI legislation](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/mozambique>). On 16 September 2026 the education and culture minister said that [a law to regulate the use of AI in education is almost ready](<https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/69322>). She said the text was still being written and would go to the Council of Ministers for approval. [In October 2026 the education ministry's coordinating council appraised a proposed 19-article text of guidelines on AI use in schools, excluding higher education](<https://moztoday.com/arquivo/7986>). [The text is still to be refined before it is formalised as a Joint Ministerial Diploma with the labour, gender and social action ministry](<https://moztoday.com/arquivo/7986>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI itself is in force. The institute's chairman [named no draft of one in August 2026](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/intic-drafts-copyright-and-ai-legislation-to-protect-digital-creators/>). The education law the minister described would cover every institution from primary school to higher education.
