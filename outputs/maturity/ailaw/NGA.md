# Nigeria

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Nigeria has a binding rule that names AI, inside its data protection rules. The data protection commission's [General Application and Implementation Directive of 2025](<https://theliberalist.org/nigeria-needs-no-ai-law-it-already-has-one/>) requires an organisation deploying AI to document its parameters, assess unequal outcomes and test the system, under article 43, and to stop using AI systems that pose undue risks to human rights, under article 44. In October 2026 the Centre for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics [described the proposed Artificial Intelligence Bill](<https://von.gov.ng/nigerias-ai-bill-targets-sovereignty-investment-power/>) as covering sovereignty, investment and risk management.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI itself is in force. [Four AI bills passed second reading in the House of Representatives on 3 December 2024](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17191>), and a proposed [Artificial Intelligence Bill with a licensing regime and an AI council](<https://techcabal.com/2025/11/20/nigerias-ai-bill-puts-control-first-but-at-what-cost-to-innovation/>) was under debate in November 2025. In January 2026 the government was [preparing to regulate AI through the National Digital Economy and E-Governance Bill](<https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-plans-to-introduce-ai-regulations/DZQ587V8lQDqzXy2>), with yearly impact assessments for high-risk systems.

***Also on file***. [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](<https://theliberalist.org/nigeria-needs-no-ai-law-it-already-has-one/>); [National Artificial Intelligence Strategy (NAIS)](<https://techtrends.africa/how-african-governments-are-regulating-ai-and-who-is-getting-it-right/>); [National AI strategy](<https://itweb.africa/article/nigeria-takes-new-measures-to-regulate-ai/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law on AI itself, applying across sectors, is in force.
