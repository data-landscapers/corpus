# Algeria

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Algeria has a bill and no rule in force. The secretary general of the National People's Assembly told an inter-parliamentary meeting in October 2024 that [a bill by a group of deputies to regulate the development and use of AI had recently been registered and would be examined by the Assembly's bureau](<https://asgp.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wed-16-Oct-PM-Algerie-FR.pdf>). Digital Policy Alert's digest of April 2025 states that the country [has not yet adopted laws or regulations specifically governing the development and use of AI](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>), and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country.

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no law or regulation specifically governing AI](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>) in April 2025, Algeria has no general AI law to apply. The rules the digest describes on personal data, the hosting of cloud services on Algerian territory and online content are general ones that do not name AI, and no body has been given powers under an AI law.

***Also on file***. [Law No. 25-11](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/19/why-data-protection-has-become-africas-default-ai-policy-tool/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the deputies' 2024 bill has been examined, adopted or withdrawn, or that a rule on AI is in force.
