# Gambia

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** The Gambia has no law on AI. [An assessment by the Oxford Institute of Technology and Justice](<https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/thegambia-3447303d-c9d6-4476-963c-02898588dc35>) covers the position as at June 2026. It states that there is no dedicated legislation regulating the use of AI in The Gambia and no proposed legislation on AI. It is the one source found that says in terms that no draft exists as well as no law. [A security practitioner wrote in November 2026 that The Gambia still has no law written for AI](<https://alkambatimes.com/ai-is-arriving-in-the-gambia-are-we-ready-for-the-risks/>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no legislation on AI in force or proposed in June 2026](<https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/thegambia-3447303d-c9d6-4476-963c-02898588dc35>), there is no general AI law to apply. The assessment looks at AI in the justice system. There it describes an automated system for recording and transcribing court proceedings, in use without any rule written for AI. No rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded.
