# Benin

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Benin has no binding rule on AI. The Forum on Information and Democracy and ALCRER stated in April 2025 that, [outside the national AI and big-data strategy 2023-2027, there is no specific and sufficiently binding regulation](<https://informationdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FID_Plaquette-IA-BENIN_VDEF-1.pdf>) on developing, deploying or using AI systems in Benin. Their statement names no bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI. [Levellers said in June 2026 that Benin has no dedicated AI law, bill or statute, and that AI is governed indirectly through the Digital Code's book on personal data](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-benin>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** With [no specific binding regulation on AI outside the strategy](<https://informationdemocracy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/FID_Plaquette-IA-BENIN_VDEF-1.pdf>) as of April 2025, Benin has no general AI law to apply. The national AI and big-data strategy for 2023 to 2027 is a strategy, not a law, and no rule for one sector or one use that names AI is recorded for the country.
