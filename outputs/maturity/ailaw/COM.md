# Comoros

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** The [Code of Information and Communication](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>), adopted by Law 25-021/AU of 26 December 2025 and promulgated by decree on 2 February 2026, requires any content generated or heavily modified by AI to be labelled as such, under its article 231. The media regulator, the Conseil national de la presse et de l'audiovisuel, can impose penalties of up to 1,000,000 Comorian francs. Al-watwan reported on 2 October 2026 the UNESCO finding that the Comoros has [legislation with several provisions on artificial intelligence in the media](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** The rule in force covers one use, [AI-generated content in the media](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>), and sits in a code that governs the press and broadcasting. The same UNESCO assessment, reported by Al-watwan on 2 October 2026, finds [no AI-specific legal instrument and a framework that is mainly sectoral](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-le-diagnostic-ram-2.0-r%C3%A9v%C3%A8le-les-d%C3%A9fis-des-comores.html>) rather than cross-sector.
