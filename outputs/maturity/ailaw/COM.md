# Comoros

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** The [Code of Information and Communication](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>), adopted by Law 25-021/AU of 26 December 2025 and promulgated by decree on 2 February 2026, requires any content generated or heavily modified by AI to be labelled as such, under its article 231. The media regulator, the Conseil national de la presse et de l'audiovisuel, can impose penalties of up to 1,000,000 Comorian francs.

**General AI law and the law applied.** The rule in force covers one use, [AI-generated content in the media](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>), and sits in a code that governs the press and broadcasting. The Comoros has no law on AI itself applying across sectors, and no bill or draft of one appears in any source dated to September 2026. No penalty imposed under article 231 is on record either.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a law on AI itself, applying across sectors, is in force.
