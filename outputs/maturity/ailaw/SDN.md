# Sudan

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Sudan has a draft and no rule in force. On 6 August 2026 the [Council of Ministers discussed a draft Data and Artificial Intelligence Law](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) put forward by the ministry for digital transformation, and directed that it be studied further before going for approval to the interim parliament, a joint sitting of the Sovereignty Council and the Council of Ministers.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. Five days later the undersecretary of the justice ministry said the ministry was preparing, with the digital transformation ministry, [three draft laws for 2026, one of them the law regulating data and AI](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>). The draft joins AI to data in one law across sectors; nothing on its content is recorded.
