# Sudan

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Sudan has a draft and no rule in force. [On 6 August 2026 the Council of Ministers discussed a draft Data and Artificial Intelligence Law from the digital transformation ministry](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>). [It sent the draft for further study before approval by the interim parliament](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>). [On 1 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved the draft Data and Artificial Intelligence Regulation Bill 2026, SUNA reports](<https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya>).

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. In August 2026 the justice ministry's undersecretary said it was preparing [three draft laws for 2026, one of them the law regulating data and AI](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>). The draft joins data and AI in one law across sectors. Nothing on its content is recorded. [In July 2026 the Sudanese Centre for Policy Studies wrote that the Data and AI Authority had issued no binding rules](<https://sudancps.com/2026/07/20/%d9%81%d8%ac%d9%88%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%88%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b2%d9%86%d8%a9-%d8%a8%d9%8a%d9%86/>). The authority was created in November 2025.
