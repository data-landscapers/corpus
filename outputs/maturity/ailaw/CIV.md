# Cote d'Ivoire

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** On 11 March 2026 a digital-law expert told a conference at the Université Polytechnique de Bingerville that [Cote d'Ivoire has no specific law on AI](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-et-regulation-de-la-technologie-de-lintelligence-artificielle-ia-en-cote-divoire/>). He said AI is covered only by soft law resting on existing digital statutes. He named no bill or draft regulation on AI.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force, so none has been applied. The [same March 2026 report](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-et-regulation-de-la-technologie-de-lintelligence-artificielle-ia-en-cote-divoire/>) lists the 2013 electronic transactions and cybercrime laws and the 2017 information society law as the texts that apply.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No bill, draft decree or draft regulation on AI has been found.
