# Chad

## AI law or binding rule

**Binding rule on AI in force.** Chad has no binding rule on AI and no draft. On 27 April 2026 the telecoms minister said that [existing digital texts no longer allow AI to be regulated](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-la-refonte-du-cadre-juridique-du-numerique-pour-encadrer-lia-et-renforcer-la-souverainete-digitale/>). He installed a 34-member committee and gave it 45 days to propose updated texts for the digital legal framework.

**General AI law and the law applied.** No law on AI is in force. The [article of 27 April 2026](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-la-refonte-du-cadre-juridique-du-numerique-pour-encadrer-lia-et-renforcer-la-souverainete-digitale/>) describes the current legal vacuum on AI and names no draft text. The telecoms minister installed a committee of 34 members and set it 45 days to propose updated texts, after saying that the existing digital texts no longer allow AI to be regulated. No draft text on AI is named in the article.
