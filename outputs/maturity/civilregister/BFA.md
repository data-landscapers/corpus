# Burkina Faso

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Direction générale de la modernisation de l'état civil trained delegated officers and transcribing agents in Pouni on the VENEEM platform](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>). The platform is for real-time entry of births, marriages and deaths, and the session was funded by PREGOLS for 2026. [The Minister of Territorial Administration said 30 communes of Burkina Faso now hold fully digitised civil-status registers](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>), as reported by the Primature. [AMADE Mondiale lists deployment of a digital birth registration platform and health-registry interoperability among completed activities](<https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html>). They are activities of its 2025-2028 project in Kaya, Tenkodogo and Koupela.

**Deaths digital.** [The same session covered real-time entry of deaths on VENEEM, alongside births and marriages](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>). Entry is at central and secondary registration offices, per the Agence d'Information du Burkina.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 officers in Pouni were trained to enter births in real time on VENEEM](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>).
