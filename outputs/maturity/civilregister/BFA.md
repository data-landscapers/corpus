# Burkina Faso

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Direction générale de la modernisation de l'état civil trained delegated officers and transcribing agents in Pouni on the VENEEM platform for real-time entry of births, marriages and deaths, funded by PREGOLS for 2026](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>). [The Minister of Territorial Administration said 30 communes of Burkina Faso now hold fully digitised civil-status registers, reported by the Primature](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144154>). [AMADE Mondiale lists deployment of a digital birth registration platform and health-registry interoperability among completed activities of its 2025-2028 project in Kaya, Tenkodogo and Koupela](<https://www.amade.org/en/missions/protection/civil-status-for-all/civil-registration-for-children-in-burkina-faso-oe-phase-ii.html>).

**Deaths digital.** [The same session covered real-time entry of deaths, alongside births and marriages, on VENEEM at central and secondary registration offices, per the Agence d'Information du Burkina](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>).
