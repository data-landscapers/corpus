# Sierra Leone

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [NCRA says over 80% of integrated CRVS and ID services, including birth registration, are digitized and decentralized across regional and district offices](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>). [The Calabash Newspaper reports a one-year Vital Strategies-funded NCRA project integrating digital technologies into birth and death registration, with 48 laptops and modems for 48 health facilities in 16 districts](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/45757>). [UNICEF's 6 October 2026 story says the NCRA registered a baby's birth in Bombali District on a portable digital device through Sierra Leone's digital birth registration system, which NCRA's Bombali District Registration Officer calls an integral part of the civil registration system](<https://www.unicef.org/digitalimpact/stories/putting-children-heart-sierra-leones-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>).
