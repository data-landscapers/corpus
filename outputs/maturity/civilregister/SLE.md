# Sierra Leone

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Sierra Leone registers births digitally in its district offices. [In 2025 the registration authority said over 80% of its registration and ID services are digitised](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>). [An official said in March 2025 that fully electronic birth registration began in 2022](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/african-civil-registration-leaders-acsa-point-direction-for-needed-acceleration>). [In October 2026 UNICEF described a birth in Bombali registered on a portable device](<https://www.unicef.org/digitalimpact/stories/putting-children-heart-sierra-leones-digital-public-infrastructure>). [In August 2026 the authority said a new integrated eCRVS platform had been developed with EU support](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/93-of-sierra-leone-registered-with-unique-nins-2/>). No source since 2023 says births are registered on it nationwide.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. [A 2024 project gave 48 health facilities laptops for digital birth and death registration](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/45757>).

**Births at 90%.** Most births are registered, though not shown to be digitally. [In March 2025 an official put civil registration at 90%, up from about 7% a decade before](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/african-civil-registration-leaders-acsa-point-direction-for-needed-acceleration>). The rate is not tied to the digital system.

***Also on file***. [NIN system](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>); [National Inmates Identity Management System](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
