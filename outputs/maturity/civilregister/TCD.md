# Chad

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [An ATPE opinion piece says the TASDJIL system of Technidev and ANATS already digitally registers births at a hospital desk at Koukou Angarana in Sila province, with experiences still to be scaled up](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). [UNICEF's national sub-office head says Chad has worked since 2021 to modernise civil registration through digitalisation and deployment of the TASDJIL application](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/08/08/sarh-un-atelier-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-et-la-transmission-des-donnees-detat-civil/>). [ANATS and the OIF discussed digitalisation and interoperability of civil registration services between communes and the SIGPTS, with ANATS issuing secure birth certificates in several provinces](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-lanats-et-loif-sunissent-pour-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat-civil/>). [In December 2023 TASDJIL registration desks were in more than 50 maternity units, with the aim of covering every health facility](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>).
