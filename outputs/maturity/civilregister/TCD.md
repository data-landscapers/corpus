# Chad

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Chad registers births digitally at maternity desks, not yet nationwide. [In December 2023 the TASDJIL system had desks in more than 50 maternity units](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>). The aim is to cover every health facility. [In July 2025 a desk at Mongo hospital served 18 health centres and logged 10 to 15 births a day](<https://ndarason.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-accelere-lenregistrement-des-naissances-a-mongo/>). [An OIF-backed project to June 2026 is strengthening TASDJIL in N'Djamena, Batha and Hadjer Lamis](<https://www.francophonie.org/tchad-lancement-de-la-phase-2-du-projet-etat-civil-8217>). [In August 2026 a writer said these desks were still to be scaled up](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a digital system. The nearest fact is [the hospital birth desk at Koukou Angarana described in August 2026](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**Births at 90%.** About one birth in four is registered. [In October 2025 a registration official put the rate at about 26%](<https://www.francophonie.org/tchad-lancement-de-la-phase-2-du-projet-etat-civil-8217>). The rate is not tied to a digital system.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
