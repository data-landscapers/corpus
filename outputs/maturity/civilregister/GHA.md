# Ghana

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Ghana's Births and Deaths Registry registers births on a digital platform. [At a March 2024 launch the Vice President said it allowed real-time registration in all regions](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth/>). [In June 2025 the registry's reform head described a central digital database deployed in 261 districts](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/bdr-at-the-centre-of-reform-push-as-minister-for-public-sector-reforms-charts-path-for-improved-data-systems/>). The move from paper is not finished. [In August 2025 a former Registrar wrote that the registry is still leaving paper behind](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2025/08/counting-every-ghanaian-how-digitalising-births-and-deaths-fuels-ghanas-inclusive-future/>). [In September 2026 officers in the Eastern Region were still taking digital registration into communities](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1530759/unicef-bdr-and-kuami-eugene-push-for-improved.html>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest fact is [the central birth database described in June 2025](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/bdr-at-the-centre-of-reform-push-as-minister-for-public-sector-reforms-charts-path-for-improved-data-systems/>).

**Births at 90%.** About seven births in ten are registered. [In February 2024 the registry said national coverage had been over 70%](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/02/births-and-deaths-registry-records-663223-births-in-2023/>). The figure is not tied to the digital system.
