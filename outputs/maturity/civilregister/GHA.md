# Ghana

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Eastern Region Registrar says officers are expected to complete digital registrations in communities, and an Assembly Member says birth registration 'has been digitalised'](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1530759/unicef-bdr-and-kuami-eugene-push-for-improved.html>). [Graphic Online's opinion piece by two CRVS experts refers to Ghana's rollout of the Harmonised Births and Deaths Registry Portal (HBDRP) as an example of decentralised digital registration](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/features/opinion/building-africas-architecture-of-visibility-the-next-crvs-decade.html>). [In June 2025 the registry's reform project head described a centralised digital database with district-level deployments in 261 areas](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/bdr-at-the-centre-of-reform-push-as-minister-for-public-sector-reforms-charts-path-for-improved-data-systems/>). [At the March 2024 launch of the Ghana Card number at birth, Vice President Bawumia said the Births and Deaths Registry had moved to an automated platform with real-time birth registration in all regions](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth/>). [In August 2025 a former Registrar wrote that the registry is still moving from paper to a digital platform](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2025/08/counting-every-ghanaian-how-digitalising-births-and-deaths-fuels-ghanas-inclusive-future/>).
