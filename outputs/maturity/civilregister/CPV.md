# Cape Verde

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The registry directorate's services have been computerised with NOSi since 2004](<https://bdmi.ine.cv/site_deploy_api/Uploads/20251205_142245_cc7f58f7-2417-444c-9c21-07f670b19a7f.pdf>), the statistics institute says. Its count of 6,760 births registered in 2023 comes from that registration database. The system was built to digitise the archive too, and it links to social security and health.

**Deaths digital.** [Deaths are drawn from the same registration database](<https://bdmi.ine.cv/site_deploy_api/Uploads/20251205_142245_cc7f58f7-2417-444c-9c21-07f670b19a7f.pdf>), and checked against the health ministry's information system. The institute codes and analyses births, deaths and marriages itself, from the directorate's data.

**Births at 90%.** [Biometric Update reports, citing the IMIJ presentation at ID4Africa 2026, that Cabo Verde reached 99 percent birth registration, up from about 91 percent before the reforms](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). [IMIJ's ID4Africa 2026 deck gives birth registration in Cabo Verde as about 99-100% (presenter's approximation)](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-1-Cape-Verde-FINAL.pdf>).
