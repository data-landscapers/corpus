# Cape Verde

## Civil register

**Births digital.** No source held says digital civil registration is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [Biometric Update reports, citing the IMIJ presentation at ID4Africa 2026, that Cabo Verde reached 99 percent birth registration, up from about 91 percent before the reforms](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). A later answer places the country above that step. It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).

**Births at 90%.** [Biometric Update reports, citing the IMIJ presentation at ID4Africa 2026, that Cabo Verde reached 99 percent birth registration, up from about 91 percent before the reforms](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).
