# Morocco

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Ministry of the Interior says a new information system for modernising civil status is being installed, after an electronic database of civil-status records since 1915 was generalised to all civil-status offices](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/programme-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil>). [Interior Minister Laftit ordered the walis of ten regions to speed the digitisation of paper civil-status records](<https://hnews.ma/92028/>). [About 20 million records had been digitised in Casablanca-Settat and Rabat-Sale-Kenitra](<https://hnews.ma/92028/>). [Staff were trained on the new information system ahead of its official application](<https://hnews.ma/92028/>).

**Deaths digital.** [The Interior Ministry says the SGEC handles death declarations as well as births, marriages and divorces](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in October 2024 the Interior Ministry said over 1,300,000 acts had been declared or issued through the SGEC](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>).
