# Cameroon

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [BUNEC director-general Alexandre Marie Yomo said on 14 September 2026 that the digitisation pilot begun in 2020 has digitised 60 of Cameroon's 432 civil-registration centres (13.9%), up from 42 in April 2022, with full completeness targeted for 2029-2030 if funded](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>). [UNDP says the Système Intégré de Gestion de l'État Civil (SIGEC), with the SANEC digital archive, is being rolled out under its Legal Identity II project with BUNEC in six East Region communes including Bertoua, Batouri and Abong-Mbang, with Bertoua 1er already in production producing birth and marriage acts](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>).
