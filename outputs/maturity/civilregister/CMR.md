# Cameroon

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Cameroon's civil register is still mostly on paper. [In September 2026 the registration bureau BUNEC said 60 of 432 civil-registration centres were digitised](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>). That is 13.9%, up from 42 in April 2022. It aims to finish by 2029-2030 if funded. [UNDP says its integrated civil-status system is in production at Bertoua 1er, issuing birth and marriage acts](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>). It is being rolled out to six East Region communes. [In March 2026 NRC was piloting a digital office in Logone-Birni, in the Far North](<https://reliefweb.int/report/cameroon/securing-every-childs-identity-nrcs-efforts-strengthen-birth-registration-cameroon-nrc>). [In April 2026 the statistics institute said a December 2024 law creates a national civil-status file](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202604210117.html>). A test linking the register with health and statistics systems is under way.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a digital system. The nearest fact is [BUNEC's count of 60 digitised centres in September 2026](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** Fewer than half of births are registered. [UNICEF estimates that 43.77% of the 560,000 births in health facilities in 2023 were registered](<https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/5/a-name-a-document-a-future-cameroons-fight-to-register-every-child>). [The 2018 MICS survey put registration at 62%](<https://lejour.cm/enregistrement-des-naissances-seules-135-communes-sur-les-374-dans-le-train/>). Neither figure is tied to a digital system.
