# Angola

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Angola's presentation to the registrars' meeting says amendments to the civil registry and identity card laws led to operations merging the two systems, with a multifunctional database under the Ministry of Justice built on the existing Sistema do Registo Civil](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). [Novo Jornal reports that identification and civil registration posts in Luanda and nearly all the country were without their system for months, which the Justice Ministry blamed on telecom breakdowns, showing a computerised registry in use at posts](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/minjusdh-diz-que-falhas-do-sistema-nos-postos-de-identificacao-e-registo-civil-que-ja-duram-ha-meses-se-devem-as-quebras-de-telecomunicacoes-71601.html>).
