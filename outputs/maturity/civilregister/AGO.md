# Angola

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Angola keeps its civil register on computer at registration posts. [In May 2026 posts in Luanda and most of the country had been without their system for months](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/minjusdh-diz-que-falhas-do-sistema-nos-postos-de-identificacao-e-registo-civil-que-ja-duram-ha-meses-se-devem-as-quebras-de-telecomunicacoes-71601.html>). The Justice Ministry blamed telecom breakdowns. [In July 2026 Angola told a registrars' meeting it is merging the civil register and the identity card system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). The shared database is to be built on the existing civil registry system. No source says births are registered digitally across the country.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest fact is [the merger of the civil and identity registers described in July 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** Fewer than four in ten young children are registered. [The statistics institute's 2023-2024 survey put registration of children under five at 38%](<https://novojornal.co.ao/sociedade/detalhe/no-pais-menos-de-quatro-criancas-em-cada-10-estao-registadas-65688.html>). The figure is not tied to any digital system.
