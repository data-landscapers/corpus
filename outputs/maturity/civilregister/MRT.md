# Mauritania

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In February 2025 Law 2025-011 provided that digital civil-status acts can be issued through the adopted information systems](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>). [Under it civil-status acts are signed electronically](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>). [The Centres d'Accueil des Citoyens receive declarations](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>). [In March 2025 a Justice Ministry official said the national population register agency has developed a modern biometric civil-status system](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/267622>). [In June 2026 the interior minister listed modernising the civil-status system and digitising its services among the reforms](<https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/>). [The minister named mobile field missions alongside them](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261951>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [in February 2025 Law 2025-011 provided for electronic signing of civil-status acts](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank put birth registration completeness at 44.8% in 2021](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [That is a national rate, not tied to a digital system](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).
