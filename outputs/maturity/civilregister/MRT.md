# Mauritania

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The interior minister listed modernising the civil-status system and digitising its services among the reforms, alongside mobile field missions](<https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/>). [A Justice Ministry official says the national population register agency has developed a modern biometric civil-status system](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/267622>). [Law 2025-011 of 24 February 2025 provides that, through the adopted information systems, digital civil-status acts can be issued and civil-status acts are signed electronically, with the Centres d'Accueil des Citoyens receiving declarations](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
