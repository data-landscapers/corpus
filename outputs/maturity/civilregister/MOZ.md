# Mozambique

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Direccao Nacional dos Registos e Notariado portal describes the e-RCEV electronic civil registration and vital statistics system, using USSD, SMS and web to register births and deaths, with proposed coverage of all civil registration centres](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>). [Jornal Notícias reports the Government intends to digitise civil registration through the Digital Economy and E-Government sub-projects, with the Ministry of Justice responsible for birth registration, and registrars can enter data on the digital platform once online](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>). [Kabum Digital says the SRN online portal lets users request certificates and carry out civil registrations, including birth registration (2023)](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>). [UNICEF's 2024 birth registration brief says all 165 conservatories are now digitised on the national eCRVS](<https://www.unicef.org/mozambique/en/birth-registration-brief>), with 152 of 300 eligible health facilities linked to it.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>).

***Also on file***. [MGDH-Birth](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country, or that deaths are registered in a digital system.
