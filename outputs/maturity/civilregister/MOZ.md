# Mozambique

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Direccao Nacional dos Registos e Notariado portal describes the e-RCEV electronic civil registration and vital statistics system, using USSD, SMS and web to register births and deaths, with proposed coverage of all civil registration centres](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>). [Jornal Notícias reports the Government intends to digitise civil registration through the Digital Economy and E-Government sub-projects, with the Ministry of Justice responsible for birth registration, and registrars can enter data on the digital platform once online](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>). [The CRVS Law and Policy Compendium says the revised Civil Registration Code (Law No. 12/2018) established the e-SIRCEV electronic system, integrated with national ID and health systems (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.crvslaws.org/mozambique/summary>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>).
