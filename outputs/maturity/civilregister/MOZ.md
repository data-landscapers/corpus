# Mozambique

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Mozambique's electronic civil register, e-SIRCEV, has run since 2018 but does not yet cover the country. [The registry's portal gives coverage of all registration centres as a proposal](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>). [UNICEF's 2024 brief says all 165 registry offices are digitised](<https://www.unicef.org/mozambique/en/birth-registration-brief>). It adds that 152 of 300 eligible health facilities are linked. [In July 2024 hospital births in some Inhambane districts were linked to e-SIRCEV](<https://www.jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/aprimorados-mecanismos-do-registo-de-nascimento/>), with national expansion launched. [By March 2026 the database held 4.57 million people, against a target of 13 million](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a digital system. [The registry's portal says e-SIRCEV is designed to register deaths](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>), but not that it does.

**Births at 90%.** Few births are registered. [The statistics institute says the system registered 8.8% of births in 2025](<https://dossiersefactos.com/2026/08/28/mocambique-falha-registo-de-9-em-cada-10-bebes-que-nascem/>). It was 8.9% in 2024. The figure is not tied to the digital system.

***Also on file***. [MGDH-Birth](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country. None says deaths are registered in a digital system.
