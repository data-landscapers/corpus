# Togo

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The MESPTN, with KfW financing, tendered technical assistance for a pilot of the PDiMa project to digitalise civil registration services in sixteen communes, including supervision of the civil registration information system deployment, before national roll-out](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MESPTN-Consultant-international-2.pdf>). [The territorial administration ministry delivered computers and printers on 8 April 2026 to five pilot communes (Golfe 1, Agoe-Nyive 1, Lacs 1, Ave 2, Zio 2) in the pilot of digitalised civil-status services, ahead of national extension](<https://territoire.gouv.tg/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-des-equipements-informatiques-remis-aux-communes-pilotes-pour-accelerer-le-processus/>).
