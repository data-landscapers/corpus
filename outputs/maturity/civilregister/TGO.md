# Togo

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The MESPTN, with KfW financing, tendered technical assistance for a pilot of the PDiMa project](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MESPTN-Consultant-international-2.pdf>). The project is to digitalise civil registration services in sixteen communes before national roll-out. The assistance includes supervision of the civil registration information system deployment. [The territorial administration ministry delivered computers and printers on 8 April 2026 to five pilot communes](<https://territoire.gouv.tg/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-des-equipements-informatiques-remis-aux-communes-pilotes-pour-accelerer-le-processus/>). They are Golfe 1, Agoe-Nyive 1, Lacs 1, Ave 2 and Zio 2, in the pilot of digitalised civil-status services, ahead of national extension.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. [In November 2026 the health ministry described its e-Vital pilot, begun in August 2026 in four communes](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>). [Health facilities there enter births and deaths and send them electronically to civil-status services](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 a KfW-financed pilot in sixteen communes was to precede national roll-out](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MESPTN-Consultant-international-2.pdf>).
