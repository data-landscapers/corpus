# Ethiopia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Ethiopia's national eCRVS is being rolled out in phases. [A July 2026 presentation describes a pilot in Addis Ababa, then Amharic-speaking regions, then the rest](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>). [In December 2025 Addis Ababa launched a digital civil registration system in 50 of its woredas](<https://www.ebc.et/english/Home/NewsDetails?NewsId=3422>). It replaced 14 years of manual work. The city's City Net initiative, [worth over 280 million birr across 120 districts](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_5608429>), aims to modernise registration in the capital. [A government statement the same month still called the Addis system a pilot](<https://www.gcs.gov.et/en/2025/12/22/digital-ethiopia-2025-has-brought-about-a-significant-change-in-improving-access-to-civil-registration-and-citizenship-services/>).

**Deaths digital.** The eCRVS handles deaths too. [The July 2026 presentation lists death registration and notice of deaths from health facilities among its services](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** Most births are not registered. [In March 2025 the government put birth registration at 37% complete and death registration at 16%](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/news/new-initiative-launched-in-ethiopia-to-strengthen-health-data-and-save-lives/>). The figures are not tied to a digital system.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/school-registration-adds-up-to-expanding-use-cases-of-ethiopias-digital-id>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
