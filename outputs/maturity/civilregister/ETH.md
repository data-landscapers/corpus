# Ethiopia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [An APAI-CRVS/UNECA presentation (July 2026) describes Ethiopia's national eCRVS as a phased rollout: pilot in Addis Ababa, scale-up to Amharic-speaking regions, then national expansion to non-Amharic regions](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>). [The Government Communication Service quotes the Ethiopian AI Institute saying that in Addis Ababa, as a pilot, family registration and vital events registration are being implemented through a digital system connected from central level to woreda level](<https://www.gcs.gov.et/en/2025/12/22/digital-ethiopia-2025-has-brought-about-a-significant-change-in-improving-access-to-civil-registration-and-citizenship-services/>). [Addis Ababa Mayor Adanech Abiebie said on 2025-12-05 that the city has launched an integrated digital civil registration and residency system, now operational and replacing previously paper-based processes (city only, no national rollout stated)](<https://www.amn.gov.et/en/mayor-launches-new-digital-civil-registration-and-residency-service-platform/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Deaths digital.** [The same presentation lists death registration and certificate, with health-facility death notification, among the services of the national eCRVS system, which it says improved CRVS operations](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>).
