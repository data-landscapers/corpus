# Liberia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNICEF says the Birth Registration Information Management System (BRIMS) on the DHIS2 platform has been implemented, with birth registration decentralized to all 15 counties and 58 centers](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>).
