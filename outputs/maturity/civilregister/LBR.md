# Liberia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNICEF said in August 2024 that birth registration reaches all 15 counties and 98% of health districts and is digitised, with tablets, online registration and digital birth certificates](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/unicef-oic-urges-stronger-collaboration-to-overcome-birth-registration-challenges-in-liberia/>). [UNICEF says the Birth Registration Information Management System (BRIMS) on the DHIS2 platform has been implemented, with birth registration decentralised to all 15 counties and 58 centres](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>). A proposed concession with OeSD International for a new digital civil registration system awaited ratification in October 2026.
