# Liberia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Liberian Observer reports a proposed concession agreement between Liberia's government and OeSD International GmbH for a digital civil registration system recording births, deaths and marriages, referred to a joint committee and not yet ratified](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). [UNICEF says the Birth Registration Information Management System (BRIMS) on the DHIS2 platform has been implemented, with birth registration decentralized to all 15 counties and 58 centers](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
