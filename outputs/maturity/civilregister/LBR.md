# Liberia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Liberia registers births digitally in every county. [In August 2024 UNICEF said registration reaches all 15 counties and 98% of health districts](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/unicef-oic-urges-stronger-collaboration-to-overcome-birth-registration-challenges-in-liberia/>). It uses tablets, online registration and digital certificates. [UNICEF says the system runs on DHIS2 through 58 centres](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>). A new system may replace it. [The government signed a concession with OeSD International in April 2026 for a digital civil registry](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>). [In October 2026 it was still before the legislature](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. [The proposed concession would record deaths as well as births](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>), but it is not ratified.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of births registered. The nearest fact is [UNICEF's 2024 statement that registration reaches 98% of health districts](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/unicef-oic-urges-stronger-collaboration-to-overcome-birth-registration-challenges-in-liberia/>), which counts places, not births.
