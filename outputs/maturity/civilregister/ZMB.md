# Zambia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [INRIS, the civil registration and legal identity information management system, has been rolled out in 79 districts, covering all city and municipal councils, with some district councils pending, per the INRIS deputy project manager](<https://dailynationzambia.com/2023/10/govt-digitises-nrcs/>). [The Lavushimanda district registration officer said digital civil registration, covering birth registration, runs on INRIS, which was deployed to 26 district national registration offices in March 2022 (Lusaka Times, 3 September 2026) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Deaths digital.** [The same official said the exercise run on INRIS covers securing death certificates as well as birth registration (Lusaka Times, 3 September 2026)](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Sample Based Registration System with Vital Statistics (SRS-VS)](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-launches-sample-based-registration-system-to-capture-silent-deaths-outside-health-facilities/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more.
