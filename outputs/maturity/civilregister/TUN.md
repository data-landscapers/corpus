# Tunisia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The playbook names Madania as the national civil status system that feeds the citizen identifier register, and says the citizen portal issues the birth act for the account owner and for children](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [The deck says Tunisia has had a centralised national civil status management system since 2004, shown as the information system Madania, and lists its overhaul as part of the strategy](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>). [The Tunisian cabinet council of 15 August 2026 listed among 114 projects under way the electronic exchange of civil-status annotations (mentions d'etat civil) between consulates abroad and municipalities, with several projects past 90% progress](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
