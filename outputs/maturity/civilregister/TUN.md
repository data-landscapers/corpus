# Tunisia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Tunisian cabinet council of 15 August 2026 listed among 114 projects under way the electronic exchange of civil-status annotations (mentions d'etat civil) between consulates abroad and municipalities, with several projects past 90% progress](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>). [Al Chourouk reports that Tunisia's Ministry of Health has begun the first pilot of a digital birth-declaration system, described as a civil registration and vital statistics digitisation step](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%B0-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%89-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA>). [UNFPA Tunisie reports that three workshops (20 Nov, 4 Dec, 11 Dec 2025) involving 72 municipalities gave civil registrars intensive training on the digital system Medania for accurate and fast declarations in the civil-registration and vital-statistics system](<https://tunisia.unfpa.org/fr/news/%C3%A9tat-civil-72-municipalit%C3%A9s-tunisiennes-sunissent-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-des-donn%C3%A9es>).
