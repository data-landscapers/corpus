# Tunisia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The playbook names Madania as the national civil status system that feeds the citizen identifier register](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). It says the citizen portal issues the birth act for the account owner and for children. [The deck says Tunisia has had a centralised national civil status management system since 2004](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>). It shows it as the information system Madania and lists its overhaul as part of the strategy. [The Tunisian cabinet council of 15 August 2026 listed 114 projects under way](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>). They include the electronic exchange of civil-status annotations (mentions d'etat civil) between consulates abroad and municipalities. Several projects are past 90% progress.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 Madania was named as the national civil status system feeding the citizen identifier register](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 the citizen portal issued birth acts for account owners and their children](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
