# Libya

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [LANA reports a ministerial meeting on 9 July 2026 reviewing completion phases and obstacles to finishing Project Intilaqa and developing the civil registry system, including data cleaning and electronic linkage with other bodies](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=361315&lang=ar>). [LANA reports the Attorney General discussed with the head of the Civil Registry Authority the latest completed phase of the project to create the electronic civil registry database](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351962&lang=ar>). [Libya Herald reports the Civil Registry Authority says 257 offices have completed matching and scanning under Project Intilaqa, which matches electronic Civil Registry data with paper records, with 1.8 million families' data printed and 97% completion](<https://libyaherald.com/2024/09/pm-aldabaiba-follows-up-on-intilaqa-project-for-matching-civil-registry-electronic-data-with-paper-records>).
