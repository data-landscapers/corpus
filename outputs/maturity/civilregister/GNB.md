# Guinea-Bissau

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNDP says the Santa Luzia Civil Registry Office in Bissau is the first in the country to operate entirely digitally on SIREC, replacing paper registry books, and over 2.4 million records (11,745 registers) are digitised](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). [UNICEF says birth registration is now done digitally on a tablet platform in hospitals and health centres in three regions (Bissau, Tombali, Quinara), with over 20,000 children aged 0 to 7 registered from October 2024 to June 2025](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>).

**Deaths digital.** [UNDP says officials at Santa Luzia record births, marriages and deaths directly on the national platform SIREC](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). [The SIREC page says the system registers deaths (obito) alongside births and marriages, with fully digital storage of registration acts](<https://sirec.web.ua.pt/Home/About>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>).
