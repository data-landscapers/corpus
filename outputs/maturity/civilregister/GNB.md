# Guinea-Bissau

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNDP says the Santa Luzia Civil Registry Office in Bissau is the first in the country to operate entirely digitally on SIREC](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). It has replaced paper registry books, and over 2.4 million records (11,745 registers) are digitised. [UNICEF says birth registration is now done digitally on a tablet platform in hospitals and health centres in three regions](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>). The regions are Bissau, Tombali and Quinara. Over 20,000 children aged 0 to 7 were registered from October 2024 to June 2025.

**Deaths digital.** [UNDP says officials at Santa Luzia record births, marriages and deaths directly on the national platform SIREC](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). [The SIREC page says the system registers deaths (obito) alongside births and marriages, with fully digital storage of registration acts](<https://sirec.web.ua.pt/Home/About>).
