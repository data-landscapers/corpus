# Sudan

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The South Gezira locality civil registry centre (Al-Hosh office) announced it issued the first electronic birth certificate, on 17 May 2026, for a child born in August 2023, after a suspension caused by war and displacement (Al-Nawrs News)](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>). [SUNA reports the Ministries of Health and Digital Transformation launched on 2026-05-11 phase one of the HMS hospital-management programme, whose integrated systems include a civil registration and vital statistics (CRVS) system, starting at Al-Nao hospital in Khartoum State and the diagnostic centre at Gedaref hospital](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>).
