# Sao Tome and Principe

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The chief conservator of the Registo Civil told RSTP that an electronic system now lets births be entered directly into the database from computers and printers at the maternity points, eliminating paper](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). [The PReSE coordinator says São Tomé and Príncipe has a completely computerised information system of birth and death records](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>). [The registry and notary directorate launched a new integrated management and identification system for civil registry and notary services, under a project to digitalise them, built with Cabo Verdean technical consultants](<https://rdpafrica.rtp.pt/noticias-africa/sao-tome-lanca-sistema-integrado-de-registo-civil/>).

**Deaths digital.** [The PReSE coordinator says the computerised civil-registry system covers deaths as well as births](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>).
