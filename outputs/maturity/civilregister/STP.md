# Sao Tome and Principe

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The chief conservator of the Registo Civil told RSTP that an electronic system now lets births be entered directly into the database from computers and printers at the maternity points, eliminating paper](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). [The PReSE coordinator says São Tomé and Príncipe has a completely computerised information system of birth and death records](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Deaths digital.** [The PReSE coordinator says the computerised civil-registry system covers deaths as well as births](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>).
