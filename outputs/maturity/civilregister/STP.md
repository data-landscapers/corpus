# Sao Tome and Principe

## Civil register

**Births digital.** São Tomé and Príncipe keeps births in a computerised register. [In February 2026 an electoral official said birth and death records are fully computerised](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>). [In March 2026 the civil registry's chief said births are entered directly from maternity wards](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). Paper is no longer used there. [A new system for civil registry and notary services was launched in March 2025](<https://rdpafrica.rtp.pt/noticias-africa/sao-tome-lanca-sistema-integrado-de-registo-civil/>).

**Deaths digital.** [The PReSE coordinator says the computerised civil-registry system covers deaths as well as births](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>).

**Births at 90%.** Most births are registered, though not shown to be digitally. [The Mo Ibrahim Foundation's 2023 index lists the country among ten in Africa registering at least 90% of births](<https://www.rtp.pt/noticias/mundo/sao-tome-e-principe-entre-paises-africanos-com-melhores-registos-de-nascimento_n1546795>). It does not say the register is digital. No newer share of births is on file.

***Also on file***. [MOSIP-based national digital ID pilot](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sao-tome-and-principe-pilots-mosip-based-national-digital-id-system>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more.
