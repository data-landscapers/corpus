# Gambia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Registrar General CRVS says an eCRVS system was deployed in August 2022 and that over 1.4 million people were registered and issued a birth certificate as of June 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>). [President Barrow's 2025 State of the Nation Address said ID cards will be integrated with the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics database and that digitalisation of national documents is in progress](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>). [The World Bank says nearly 1.17 million people were registered and issued birth certificates between 1 August 2022 and 28 February 2023 using an electronic CRVS (eCRVS) system that replaced the paper process (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).
