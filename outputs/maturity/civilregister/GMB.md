# Gambia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Registrar General's slides report 1,238,662 births in the eCRVS digital cumulative registration from August 2022 to March 2025, shown for every local government area, equal to 53% of a 2.4m population](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P2-S1-3-Gambia_FINAL.pdf>). [The Registrar General CRVS says an eCRVS system was deployed in August 2022 and that over 1.4 million people were registered and issued a birth certificate as of June 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>). [President Barrow's 2025 State of the Nation Address said ID cards will be integrated with the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics database and that digitalisation of national documents is in progress](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P2-S1-3-Gambia_FINAL.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P2-S1-3-Gambia_FINAL.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [eID system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system.
