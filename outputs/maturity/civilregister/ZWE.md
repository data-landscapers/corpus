# Zimbabwe

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Zimbabwe is computerising its civil registry office by office. [In July 2026 the Deputy Registrar General said all 10 provinces and 63 districts were computerised](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/>). Only about 20 of 209 sub-offices were. [A China-funded UNICEF project will computerise 10 sub-offices in eight districts](<https://humanitarianpost.co.zw/unicef-boosts-zims-civil-registry-efficiency-in-eight-districts/>). [New Ziana reports these sub-offices are not yet computerised](<https://newziana.co.zw/unicef-government-move-to-digitise-birth-registration-services/>). [In September 2026 the home affairs minister said work to digitalise civil registration was ongoing](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [In February 2026 he called the population registry system an integrated digital platform](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/registrar-general-to-get-voters-roll-custody/>). He said it updates birth and death records in real time. [In November 2023 the Registrar-General said the register was being linked to hospitals](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/issuance-of-id-cards-going-on-smoothly-rg/>).

**Deaths digital.** [In February 2026 the home affairs minister said death records enter the population registry system automatically](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/registrar-general-to-get-voters-roll-custody/>). No other source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in it.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>).
