# Zimbabwe

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Home affairs minister Kazembe Kazembe said ongoing efforts to digitalise civil registration systems are complemented by improved mobility, with 30 vehicles commissioned for the Civil Registry Department](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [UNICEF's chief of child protection says a China-funded project will computerise, digitalise and solar-power 10 decentralised Civil Registry sub-offices across eight districts, some of which are not computerised now](<https://humanitarianpost.co.zw/unicef-boosts-zims-civil-registry-efficiency-in-eight-districts/>). [New Ziana reports UNICEF and the Government are to computerise, digitalise and solar-power 10 Civil Registry sub-offices across eight districts under a China-funded project, and says these sub-offices are not currently computerised](<https://newziana.co.zw/unicef-government-move-to-digitise-birth-registration-services/>). [In November 2023 the Registrar-General said all provincial civil registration stations and some district offices were computerised, and that the population register was being linked to hospitals for instant registration of births and deaths](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/issuance-of-id-cards-going-on-smoothly-rg/>).
