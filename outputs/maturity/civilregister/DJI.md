# Djibouti

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The DGPF director general says birth and marriage certificates are progressively being integrated into a digital database as part of an archive digitisation programme](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [Ville de Djibouti says its civil-status events have been recorded on the SIGEC software since 2011, with the database fed continuously, and a diagnostic of it began on 28 April 2024](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>). [Ville de Djibouti says civil-status events including deaths are recorded in Djibouti city on the SIGEC software, in use since 2011](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system.
