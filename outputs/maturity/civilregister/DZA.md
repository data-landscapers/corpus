# Algeria

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Interior Minister Said Sayoud says administrations can now consult civil status data directly in the Registre National Automatise de l'Etat Civil, set up under Executive Decree 15-204 of 27 July 2015](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>). [The Algerian Interior Ministry's digitisation of the national civil-status register has digitised more than 100 million marriage, death and birth acts, per La Sentinelle](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2024/04/04/une-revolution-dans-le-service-public/>). [The Ministry of the Interior says a computerised civil-status system and a centralised database, the Registre National d'Etat Civil (RNEC), were set up in an operation launched in 2010, with birth-record registers archived digitally (2010, before the three-year window)](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>).
