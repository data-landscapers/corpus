# Civil register: civil register

*As at 2026-10-24.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy and the Africa Programme on Accelerated Improvement of CRVS ask for digital civil registration; the SDG target is every birth and 80% of deaths registered. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Paper | A source from the past three years says civil registration is on paper with no digital register. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 Births digital | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Deaths digital | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Births at 90% | Yes to 4 |

## Countries

### Rwanda: 5 Leading

90% or more of births were registered in 2025.

- NISR says the system shows 356,838 births occurred and registered nationally in 2025 ([source, 2025](https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/vital-statistics/rwanda-vital-statistics-report-2025)).
- NISR says birth registration completeness against estimated live births was 92.9% in 2025, up from 90.3% in 2024 ([source, 2025](https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/vital-statistics/rwanda-vital-statistics-report-2025)).

### Benin: 4 Operating

Births and deaths are registered digitally in the national civil status file, said in 2026; no source found reports the share of births registered.
