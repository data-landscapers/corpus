# Burundi

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Thirteen communes already have a biometric registration system for civil events, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>). Burundi is extending digitisation and decentralisation of civil registration. [The Directorate General for territorial administration says civil registration is digitised in pilot communes](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). UNICEF says thirteen communes already have a biometric registration system, per RTNB. [The Ministry of the Interior presented a digital platform for managing civil registration, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative>). Its infrastructure is nearly complete and a decree governing its use is awaited. [ID Tech reported in August 2026 that 13 municipalities have biometric civil registration systems](<https://idtechwire.com/burundi-extends-biometric-civil-registration-toward-national-digital-id/>). Vital events are registered electronically in pilot municipalities.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 thirteen communes had a biometric system for registering civil events](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 Burundi was extending digital civil registration beyond thirteen communes](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>).
