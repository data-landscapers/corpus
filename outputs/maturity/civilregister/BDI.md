# Burundi

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Thirteen communes already have a biometric registration system for civil events as Burundi extends digitisation and decentralisation of civil registration, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>). [The Directorate General for territorial administration says civil registration is digitised in pilot communes, and UNICEF says thirteen communes already have a biometric registration system, per RTNB](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). [The Ministry of the Interior presented a digital platform for managing civil registration, with infrastructure nearly complete and a decree governing its use awaited, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative>). [ID Tech reported in August 2026 that 13 municipalities have biometric civil registration systems, with vital events registered electronically in pilot municipalities](<https://idtechwire.com/burundi-extends-biometric-civil-registration-toward-national-digital-id/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>).
