# Egypt

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Egypt's FY2026/27 economic and social development plan, reported by Youm7, says the Ministry of Health and Population and partner bodies developed the electronic registration system for births and deaths at national (republic) level](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/2/%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-2026-2027-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9/7497444>). [Egypt's own country presentation (July 2026) states full nationwide automation of civil registration at 4,571 health offices across 27 governorates, feeding a central births and deaths database](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** [Egypt's FY2026/27 development plan, reported by Youm7, says deaths are registered electronically nationwide alongside births, developed by the Ministry of Health and Population and partner bodies](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/2/%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-2026-2027-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9/7497444>). [Egypt's own country presentation (July 2026) states a central database for births and deaths is in service with nationwide automation at 4,571 health offices](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** [Egypt's own UNSD vital statistics questionnaire (2024) reports estimated completeness of live birth registration of 96.6 per cent, referring to 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/documents/metadata/2024/Egypt-2024-VT-Metadata.pdf>), and a national rate that may include paper registration.

***Also on file***. [automated civil-registry machines](<https://blogs.realestate.gov.eg/civil-registry-machines-in-egypt>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more.
