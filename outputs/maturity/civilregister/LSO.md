# Lesotho

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Lesotho registers births digitally in three districts. [In August 2026 UNICEF said 30 registration stations run on an SMS-based platform](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>). They sit in government and church-run health facilities in Maseru, Berea and Leribe. [A tablet system in Ha-Tankele feeds the social assistance database](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>). [The civil registry plans to take digital birth registration nationwide in 2026-2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a digital system. The nearest fact is [the 30 birth registration stations reported in August 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>).

**Births at 90%.** About six births in ten are registered. [A ministry official said registration rose from 21% in 2019 to 61% in 2023](<https://swazi24.com/25962/news/lesotho-marks-identity-day-with-progress-on-registration/>). The rate is not tied to a digital system, which covers three districts.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
