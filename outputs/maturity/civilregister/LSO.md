# Lesotho

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNICEF Lesotho says 30 birth registration stations supported by an SMS-based platform have been established in government and CHAL health facilities across Maseru, Berea and Leribe](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>). [Lesotho's NICR presentation to UNECA's 2026 expert group meeting lists 'Scale Up Digital Birth Registration Nationwide' for 2026-2027 and names digital birth registration as the solution for remote communities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [UNICEF's Lesotho office reports the Ntlafatsa Bana project launched a tablet-based digital birth registration system in Ha-Tankele, run by Village Health Workers and Civil Registration Officers and integrated with the NISSA social assistance database](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>).
