# Nigeria

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Nigeria moved birth registration onto VitalReg, a digital platform, nationwide in July 2026. [The population commission launched VitalReg in November 2023](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/president-tinubu-accurate-population-data-essential-for-tailored-innovative-and-permanent>). It is run with the firm Barnksforte and UNICEF support. [In June 2026 UNICEF said it ran in 14 states and the capital](<https://data.unicef.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/A-Statistical-Profile-of-Birth-Registration-in-Nigeria_final-June-2026.pdf>). [The commission's chairman said digital registration began in all 36 states and the capital on 1 July 2026](<https://guardian.ng/news/npc-flags-off-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>). Paper forms are no longer used. [In August 2026 a Lagos man found a birth wrongly listed under his name on VitalReg](<https://fij.ng/article/lagos-man-visited-birth-registration-website-he-finds-strangers-listed-as-son-wife/>).

**Deaths digital.** Deaths are registered on the same platform. [The commission said death registration on VitalReg also began on 1 July 2026](<https://guardian.ng/news/npc-flags-off-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>).

**Births at 90%.** Just over half of births are registered. [In July 2026 the chairman put birth registration at about 57% and death registration below 20%](<https://guardian.ng/news/npc-flags-off-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>). The rates are not tied to the digital system.
