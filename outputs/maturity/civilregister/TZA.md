# Tanzania

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [ZCSRA says a web-based eCRVS platform and Sheha Mobile App, redeveloped from 2022, now carry birth registration in all 11 Zanzibar districts down to Sheha level](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>). [NIDA says eRITA issues a birth certificate in 48 hours, with online applications and digitized records, and 9.4 million under-5s registered](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [Tanzania upgraded the RITA system so users can apply online for, track and receive birth registration documents via the eRITA platform, with certificates issued within 48 hours, per the Constitutional and Legal Affairs Minister](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/tanzania-working-on-legislative-framework-for-infant-id-registration>).

**Deaths digital.** [ZCSRA says death registration, with births, marriages and divorces, is decentralised to Sheha level through the eCRVS platform in all 11 Zanzibar districts](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>). [RITA says deaths are among the vital events registered on the eRITA single digital platform](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Patricia-Tanzania-PPT-RITA-presentation.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>).
