# Tanzania

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Tanzania registers births on one digital platform on the mainland and another in Zanzibar. [The registration agency RITA says eRITA is the only system it uses, across all 26 mainland regions](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Patricia-Tanzania-PPT-RITA-presentation.pdf>). [Since December 2025 it has issued birth certificates within 48 working hours](<https://www.rita.go.tz/news.php?n=464>). [The identity agency says 9.4 million children under five are registered](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [Zanzibar's eCRVS and a mobile app carry registration in all 11 of its districts](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** [ZCSRA says death registration, with births, marriages and divorces, is decentralised to Sheha level through the eCRVS platform in all 11 Zanzibar districts](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>). [RITA says deaths are among the vital events registered on the eRITA single digital platform](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Patricia-Tanzania-PPT-RITA-presentation.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** About two births in three are registered. [The minister said coverage rose from 55% in 2020 to 68% in 2025](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/birth-registration-rises-as-tanzania-targets-universal-coverage-by-2030-2026-08-12-122049>). The target is all births by 2030. The rate is not tied to the digital system.
