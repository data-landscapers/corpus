# Cote d'Ivoire

## Civil register

**Births digital.** ONECI's director general said [the national civil-status system runs in 699 civil-registry centres](<https://www.koaci.com/index.php/article/2026/08/12/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-operation-zero-enfant-invisible-loneci-deploie-283-terminaux-numeriques-dans-le-tonkpi-pour-garantir-lidentite-des-la-naissance_199500.html>), with over four million records digitised. In August 2026 ONECI handed 283 digital birth-registration terminals to health workers in Tonkpi. [All centres in the Gôh region had Citiweb by the end of 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>), and 44 agents were trained in Gagnoa from July 2026, AIP reports. [In February 2026 ONECI said Cityweb was in all 692 civil registration centres](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/02/10/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-le-delai-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-ramene-a-moins-de-deux-semaines-perspectives-de-la-structure-pour-2026_194217.html>). In October 2026 it said [CityWeb had been deployed in all the country's centres](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/etat-civil-le-cameroun-s-inspire-de-la-cote-d-ivoire-pour-interconnecter-ses-donnees>), and its director general [noted connectivity problems in some](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/cote-divoire-le-logiciel-national-detat-civil-deploye-la-connectivite-reste-un-defi_65266>). Use is uneven. In September 2026 Abobo [recorded 5,453 acts on CityWeb, while up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-trois-outils-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>).

**Deaths digital.** [AIP reports that Citiweb, in every mairie and sous-préfecture centre, registers births, marriages and deaths](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>). [The centres were equipped by the end of 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>). ONECI says [registrars create, process and follow death acts in CityWeb](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-trois-outils-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>), as they do births and marriages.

**Births at 90%.** Figures disagree on how many births are registered, and none is tied to the digital system. [The Interior Ministry says the annual registration rate rose from 54.7% in 2022 to 57.6% in 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-taux-denregistrement-annuel-des-naissances-progresse-a-576-en-2025-contre-547-en-2022-officiel/>). [UNICEF put registration of children under five above 90% in December 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/african-civil-registration-leaders-acsa-point-direction-for-needed-acceleration>).

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP)](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more.
