# Cote d'Ivoire

## Civil register

**Births digital.** ONECI's director general said [the national civil-status system runs in 699 civil-registry centres](<https://www.koaci.com/index.php/article/2026/08/12/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-operation-zero-enfant-invisible-loneci-deploie-283-terminaux-numeriques-dans-le-tonkpi-pour-garantir-lidentite-des-la-naissance_199500.html>), with over four million records digitised. In August 2026 ONECI handed 283 digital birth-registration terminals to health workers in Tonkpi. [All centres in the GÃ´h region had Citiweb by the end of 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>), and 44 agents were trained in Gagnoa from July 2026, AIP reports. In October 2026 ONECI said [CityWeb had been deployed in all the country's centres](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/etat-civil-le-cameroun-s-inspire-de-la-cote-d-ivoire-pour-interconnecter-ses-donnees>). Use is uneven. In September 2026 Abobo [recorded 5,453 acts on CityWeb, while up to 62.5% of centres in Moronou were inactive](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-trois-outils-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>).

**Deaths digital.** [AIP reports that Citiweb, in every mairie and sous-préfecture centre, registers births, marriages and deaths](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>). [The centres were equipped by the end of 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>). ONECI says [registrars create, process and follow death acts in CityWeb](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-trois-outils-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>), as they do births and marriages.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>).
