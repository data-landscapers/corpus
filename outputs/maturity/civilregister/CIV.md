# Cote d'Ivoire

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [ONECI's director general said the national civil-status management system is deployed in 699 civil-registry centres and over four million civil-status records are digitised, and 283 digital birth-registration terminals were handed to health workers in Tonkpi for progressive rollout](<https://www.koaci.com/index.php/article/2026/08/12/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-operation-zero-enfant-invisible-loneci-deploie-283-terminaux-numeriques-dans-le-tonkpi-pour-garantir-lidentite-des-la-naissance_199500.html>). [AIP reports ONECI deployed the Citiweb software in the mairies and sous-préfectures of the Gôh region, with all centres equipped at the end of 2025 and agent training under way since then, 44 agents trained in Gagnoa from 27 July 2026](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>).

**Deaths digital.** [AIP reports the Citiweb software deployed in all mairie and sous-préfecture civil-registry centres is for digital registration of births, marriages, deaths and other acts](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>). [AIP reports the Citiweb software deployed by ONECI captures births, marriages and deaths in all mairie and sous-préfecture centres, equipped at the end of 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>).
