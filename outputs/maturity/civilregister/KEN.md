# Kenya

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The Principal Secretary said electronic birth notification, a reform introduced by government, lets parents generate birth certificates using the Unique Personal Identifier](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ngao-trained-on-revised-national-id-registration-system/>). [Interior CS Kipchumba Murkomen said on 2026-06-22 that Kenyans can now download and print birth certificates online, with applications already available through the eCitizen portal](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/124508-kenyans-download-and-print-birth-certificates-online>). [Immigration PS Belio Kipsang said on 2026-10-06 that the digital birth e-notification system will be extended to all Social Health Authority hospitals within three months, by December 2026, with the civil registration system being integrated with hospital management systems](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ngao-trained-on-revised-national-id-registration-system/>).
