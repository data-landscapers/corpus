# Kenya

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Identity Week reported that the digital birth e-service had recorded nearly 300,000 births by October 2026](<https://identityweek.net/birth-registration-e-service-reduces-admin-from-months-to-days/>). [It recorded over 270,000 in the two months to September](<https://identityweek.net/birth-registration-e-service-reduces-admin-from-months-to-days/>), and is expanding across the country. In October 2026 the Immigration Principal Secretary said [e-notification would reach all Social Health Authority hospitals by December](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>). In November 2026 he told MPs that [electronic birth notification had been piloted in Nairobi and Bomet counties](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/how-kenya-s-digital-birth-registration-system-will-work--5663174>). [A nationwide rollout is to follow](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/how-kenya-s-digital-birth-registration-system-will-work--5663174>). In June 2026 the Interior Cabinet Secretary said [Kenyans can download and print birth certificates online](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/124508-kenyans-download-and-print-birth-certificates-online>), through eCitizen. [KIPPRA said in 2024 that more than 107 civil registration centres had been digitised](<https://kippra.or.ke/leveraging-on-digitalization-to-promote-public-service-delivery-in-kenya/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. [The Principal Secretary said in June 2026 that electronic birth notification lets parents generate birth certificates](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ngao-trained-on-revised-national-id-registration-system/>), and said nothing of deaths.

**Births at 90%.** About three births in four are registered. [The Civil Registration Services put birth registration at about 77% in 2023-24 and death registration at about 45%](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/african-civil-registration-leaders-acsa-point-direction-for-needed-acceleration>). [Kenya's ID4Africa deck of May 2026 gave birth registration as 70%](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>). Neither rate is tied to the digital system.

***Also on file***. [Unique Personal Identifier (UPI)](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>). [Live Capture Units](<https://peopledaily.digital/news/parliament-probes-delays-in-issuance-of-ids-birth-certificates-in-elgeyo-marakwet>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
