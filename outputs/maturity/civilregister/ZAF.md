# South Africa

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In November 2023 the Minister of Home Affairs said the department had rolled out online birth registration in 161 health facilities](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/23930/>). [These covered about 68.35% of births delivered](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/23930/>). [In August 2024 the minister said a rollout strategy connects health facilities to Home Affairs systems to automate birth registration](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/25667/>). [It runs under an MOU with the Department of Health, staggered over five years to 2024/25](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/25667/>). [In July 2025 Deputy Minister Nzuza said Home Affairs has invested in building an e-birth registration platform](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-njabulo-nzuza-home-affairs-dept-budget-vote-202526-08-jul-2025>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [in August 2024 Home Affairs was connecting health facilities to its systems](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/25667/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts the share of births registered in the digital system at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [in its 2024 round the World Bank's SPI scored South Africa 1 on SDG 17.19.2](<https://github.com/worldbank/SPI>). [That means birth registration at least 90% and death registration at least 75% complete, nationally](<https://github.com/worldbank/SPI>). [The World Bank put national birth registration completeness at 88.6% in 2017](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).
