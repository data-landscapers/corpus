# Somalia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [OpenCRVS says its production-level Unified Digital Civil Registration System operates in 30 Somali districts, is linked to 160 hospitals and has completed 28,391 birth registrations](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>). [Ecofin Agency reports the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation piloted a Unified Digital Civil Registration Service in 19 districts on 10 August 2025, with national rollout planned in phases](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). [Ecofin Agency says the pilot platform enables local administrations to digitally register births, marriages, divorces and deaths, stored in a central national database](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).
