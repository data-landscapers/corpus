# Somalia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [OpenCRVS says its production-level Unified Digital Civil Registration System operates in 30 Somali districts, is linked to 160 hospitals and has completed 28,391 birth registrations](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>). [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system, piloted in 19 districts in 2023 and scaled to 48 districts in 2025, is used daily to register births, though not all regions have it](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>). [The Ministry says the Unified Digital Civil Registration Service is operational in 19 districts across five Federal Member States, with plans to expand to all regions and districts](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Deaths digital.** [The Ministry says the service in those 19 districts registers deaths digitally, with records stored in a central national database](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>). [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system is deployed with full operational capability for daily registration of vital events such as births and deaths](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>).
