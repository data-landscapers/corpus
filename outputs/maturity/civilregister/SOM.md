# Somalia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [OpenCRVS says its production-level Unified Digital Civil Registration System operates in 30 Somali districts, is linked to 160 hospitals and has completed 28,391 birth registrations](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>). [Ecofin Agency reports the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation piloted a Unified Digital Civil Registration Service in 19 districts on 10 August 2025, with national rollout planned in phases](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system, piloted in 19 districts in 2023 and scaled to 48 districts in 2025, is used daily to register births, though not all regions have it](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>).

**Deaths digital.** [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system is deployed with full operational capability for daily registration of vital events such as births and deaths](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>). [The Ministry says the service in those 19 districts registers deaths digitally, with records stored in a central national database](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).
