# Somalia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [OpenCRVS says its production-level Unified Digital Civil Registration System operates in 30 Somali districts](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>). It is linked to 160 hospitals and has completed 28,391 birth registrations. [Ecofin Agency reports the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation piloted a Unified Digital Civil Registration Service in 19 districts on 10 August 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). National rollout is planned in phases. [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system is used daily to register births, though not all regions have it](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>). It was piloted in 19 districts in 2023 and scaled to 48 districts in 2025.

**Deaths digital.** [UNICEF Somalia says the digital CRVS system is deployed with full operational capability](<https://www.unicef.org/somalia/stories/legal-identity>). It is used for daily registration of vital events such as births and deaths. [The Ministry says the service in those 19 districts registers deaths digitally](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>). Records are stored in a central national database.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 OpenCRVS said the digital system had completed 28,391 birth registrations in 30 districts](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).
