# Madagascar

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Midi Madagasikara reports that Madagascar is deploying phase II of the Izaho Tokana 2.0 birth-registration operation, backed by the OIF Etat civil project and relying on biometrisation](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/etat-civil-le-projet-de-loif-en-renfort-d-izaho-tokana-2-0/>). [Intelligent CIO reports that Studia Inc digitised and indexed nearly 10 million civil registration records across 1,695 communes in 11 priority regions under the national EC-MADA programme, using OCR and AI indexing](<https://www.intelligentcio.com/africa/2026/05/08/ai-powered-digitisation-project-positions-madagascar-as-a-model-for-african-digital-identity-modernisation/>). [A Studia Inc press release says it digitised and indexed nearly 10 million civil registration records in 1,695 communes across 11 priority regions under the national EC-MADA programme (vendor-claimed)](<https://afroasianews.com/studia-inc-strengthens-its-african-expansion-and-concludes-a-strategic-partnership-with-d-ia-advisory/>).
