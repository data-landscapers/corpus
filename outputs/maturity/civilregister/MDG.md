# Madagascar

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Madagascar is digitising its old registers and running registration drives. [In May 2026 Intelligent CIO reported nearly 10 million civil records digitised](<https://www.intelligentcio.com/africa/2026/05/08/ai-powered-digitisation-project-positions-madagascar-as-a-model-for-african-digital-identity-modernisation/>). They cover 1,695 communes in 11 regions. [The contractor, Studia, made the same claim](<https://afroasianews.com/studia-inc-strengthens-its-african-expansion-and-concludes-a-strategic-partnership-with-d-ia-advisory/>). [In September 2026 the second phase of the Izaho Tokana 2.0 drive was under way, using biometrics](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/etat-civil-le-projet-de-loif-en-renfort-d-izaho-tokana-2-0/>). No source says births are registered digitally across the country.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a digital system. The nearest fact is [the Izaho Tokana birth drive of September 2026](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/etat-civil-le-projet-de-loif-en-renfort-d-izaho-tokana-2-0/>).

**Births at 90%.** About three births in four are registered. [UNICEF says the rate fell from 83% in 2012 to 74% in 2021](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/communiqu%C3%A9s-de-presse/si-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-progresse-r%C3%A9guli%C3%A8rement-%C3%A0-l%C3%A9chelle>). The rate is not tied to a digital system.
