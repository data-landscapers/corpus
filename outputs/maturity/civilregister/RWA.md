# Rwanda

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Rwanda registers births in one national digital system. [The statistics institute counts 356,838 births registered in it in 2025](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/vital-statistics/rwanda-vital-statistics-report-2025>). [The identity agency says births have been registered at health facilities since 2020, and at cell offices otherwise](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/over-7-2-million-rwandans-complete-biometric-enrolment-for-electronic-ids/>). [In January 2026 the local government ministry said all nine modules of the system were digitised](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-completes-digitalisation-of-crvs-modules>). [In June 2026 it launched a new centralised system and was training 2,148 cell officials to use it](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launches-new-integrated-civil-registration-system>).

**Deaths digital.** [Vital Strategies says Rwanda's CRVS, covering births and deaths, is now decentralized and digitalized](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>). [MINALOC says the death module is among the nine modules of the National Digital Civil Registration and Vital Statistics system now digitalised](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-completes-digitalisation-of-crvs-modules>). [NISR says 39,355 deaths occurred and were registered in 2025 and the CRVS system collects cause of death for them](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/vital-statistics/rwanda-vital-statistics-report-2025>).

**Births at 90%.** More than nine births in ten are registered in the system. [The statistics institute puts completeness at 92.9% of estimated live births in 2025](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/vital-statistics/rwanda-vital-statistics-report-2025>). It was 90.3% in 2024. [In September 2026 the identity agency put birth registration at about 96%](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/over-7-2-million-rwandans-complete-biometric-enrolment-for-electronic-ids/>).
