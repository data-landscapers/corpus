# Niger

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Niger's director general of territorial administration set out the reform workstreams on 2026-09-03](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>). They include progressive computerisation of communes and declaration centres and a sovereign platform for a National Civil Status Register. A first registration phase is due soon in Niamey. [UNICEF's Tahoua office chief said UNICEF contributed to computerising 68% of communes nationwide](<https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/>). The ministry cited introduction of digital technologies with computerisation of civil registration. [The Diffa regional civil-status directorate (DRECM-R) reports that four communes in the region have been computerised](<https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/>). Staff were trained in digital tools, as part of modernising the civil-status system.
