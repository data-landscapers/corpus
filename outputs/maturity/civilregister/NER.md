# Niger

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Niger's director general of territorial administration said on 2026-09-03 that progressive computerisation of communes and declaration centres and a sovereign platform for a National Civil Status Register are among the reform workstreams, with a first registration phase soon in Niamey](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>). [UNICEF's Tahoua office chief said UNICEF contributed to computerising 68% of communes nationwide, and the ministry cited introduction of digital technologies with computerisation of civil registration](<https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/>). [The Diffa regional civil-status directorate (DRECM-R) reports that four communes in the region have been computerised and staff trained in digital tools, as part of modernising the civil-status system](<https://anp.ne/mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-la-refondation-a-diffa-plus-de-173-000-actes-de-naissance-delivres-entre-juillet-2023-et-juillet-2026/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>).
