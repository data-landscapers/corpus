# DR Congo

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [A joint UN pilot (UNICEF, BCNUDH, UNHCR, UNFPA, funded by the Joint SDG Fund) is under way in Masina commune, Kinshasa, deploying DHIS2 to digitally record births, marriages and deaths](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>). [Actualite.cd reports that the December 2023 contract with Afritech and Idemia, worth $1.2 billion, covered biometric ID cards and a civil registration system, but it was cancelled by mutual consent on 12 August 2024](<https://actualite.cd/2024/09/04/rdc-le-contrat-carte-didentite-annule-apres-les-revelations-dactualitecd-lighthouse>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>).
