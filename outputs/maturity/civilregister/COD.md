# DR Congo

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [A joint UN pilot (UNICEF, BCNUDH, UNHCR, UNFPA, funded by the Joint SDG Fund) is under way in Masina commune, Kinshasa, deploying DHIS2 to digitally record births, marriages and deaths](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>).
