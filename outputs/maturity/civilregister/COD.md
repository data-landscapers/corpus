# DR Congo

## Civil register

**Births digital.** The DR Congo has only a pilot of digital civil registration. [In August 2025 four UN agencies were piloting DHIS2 in Masina commune, Kinshasa](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>). It records births, marriages and deaths. [A national contract with Afritech and Idemia covered a civil registration system](<https://actualite.cd/2024/09/04/rdc-le-contrat-carte-didentite-annule-apres-les-revelations-dactualitecd-lighthouse>). It was signed in December 2023 and cancelled in August 2024. Nothing has replaced it.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in a national digital system. The nearest fact is [the Masina pilot, which records deaths as well as births](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** About four births in ten are registered. [Official statistics cited in August 2024 put the rate at 40%](<https://ouragan.cd/2024/08/lunicef-plaide-pour-la-numerisation-des-procedures-de-letat-civil>). The figure is not tied to a digital system.

***Also on file***. [Afritech/Idemia ID card and civil registration contract](<https://actualite.cd/2024/09/04/rdc-le-contrat-carte-didentite-annule-apres-les-revelations-dactualitecd-lighthouse>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country.
