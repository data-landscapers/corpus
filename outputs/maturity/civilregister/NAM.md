# Namibia

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Namibia's Chief Registrar said it is working to strengthen timely birth and death registration by monitoring notification and registration through its e-notification system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). [A speaker at the Africa CDC learning visit said the e-birth notification system, introduced in 2017, has been implemented nationwide and connects 153 state and private facilities, per Home Affairs Minister Iipumbu's account](<https://www.nbcnews.na/node/115695>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says 126 state and private hospitals are now equipped with the electronic birth notification system that lets nurses record births instantly into the national system](<https://www.namibian.com.na/home-affairs-deploys-staff-to-24-hospitals-for-instant-birth-registration/>).

**Deaths digital.** [Home Affairs Minister Iipumbu said the e-death notification, introduced in 2018, now operates in 153 state and private facilities](<https://www.nbcnews.na/node/115695>). [The Ministry says its e-death notification system, electronic notification of deaths in line with births, was implemented at 26 named sites in 2019/2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mhaiss.gov.na/documents/292728/537582/HAI+Annual+Report+2019-2020+web-+23+November+2023-1.pdf/36f0e050-9a59-a5db-7c2a-13373d1502a2?download=true&t=1701160032086>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found puts births registered in the digital system at 90% or more. [A speaker at the Africa CDC learning visit said Namibia records birth registration coverage of close to 99%, in March 2026](<https://www.nbcnews.na/node/115695>), a national rate that may include paper registration.
