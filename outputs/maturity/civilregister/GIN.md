# Guinea

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [The MATD minister told the CNT commission that, through ONECI, it is digitising civil status and setting up the Registre national de l'Etat civil, with digital solutions deployed in 28 communes in 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>). [ONECI and NG Tech Innovation signed an MoU on 24 July 2026 on the digital transformation of identification and civil status services in Guinea, as reported by Mediaguinee](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/>). [In Ratoma, a civil-registry officer says birth certificates are issued on secure digital paper, and registration continued through a two-week paper stockout in September 2026](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country, or that deaths are registered in a digital system.
