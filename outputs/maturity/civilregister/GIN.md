# Guinea

## Civil register

**Births digital.** The MATD minister told the CNT that [ONECI is setting up a national civil register, with digital solutions in 28 communes in 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>). [ONECI and NG Tech Innovation signed an MoU on digital identification and civil status on 24 July 2026](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/>). In Ratoma, a civil-registry officer says [birth certificates are issued on secure digital paper](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). Registration continued through a two-week paper stockout in September 2026. On 28 October 2026 [the ministry launched RENEC, the national civil register project](<https://guistat.com/guinee-le-matd-lance-via-loneci-le-projet-renec-pour-moderniser-et-securiser-letat-civil/>). Its first centres open in 2026 and it runs to 2028. [An EU-funded pilot run by Enabel reached 10 communes and was extending to 28 in July 2025](<https://planete7.info/modernisation-de-letat-civil-en-guinee-enabel-remet-un-important-lot-de-materiel-informatique-aux-autorites-guineennes/>). [In October 2025 officers from Conakry's 13 communes were shown the first functions of the electronic national register](<https://ravec.gov.gn/constitution-du-registre-national-de-letat-civil-rnec-presentation-des-premieres-fonctionnalites-du-registre-electronique/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 gives a national registration rate. [In December 2025 an official said birth registration remains low and only about 2% of deaths are declared](<https://www.lindependant.org/actualites-etat-civil-guineen-la-refondation-en-marche-lidentification-au-coeur-du-futur-fichier-electoral/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births are registered digitally across the country. None says deaths are registered in a digital system.
