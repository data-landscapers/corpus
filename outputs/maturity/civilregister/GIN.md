# Guinea

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [Ratoma's delegated civil-registry officer Amadou Konia Camara says secure digital paper is used to issue digital birth certificates in all communes of Guinea, and that registrations continue despite a two-week paper stockout](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). [ONECI and NG Tech Innovation signed an MoU on 24 July 2026 on the digital transformation of identification and civil status services in Guinea, as reported by Mediaguinee](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/>). [The MATD minister told the CNT commission that, through ONECI, it is digitising civil status and setting up the Registre national de l'Etat civil, with digital solutions deployed in 28 communes in 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).
