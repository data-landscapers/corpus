# Benin

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [ANIP says secure birth certificates are now issued from data in the RNPP and the national civil-status file, per a note signed 5 May 2026](<https://lepotentiel.bj/2026/05/06/enrolement-au-rnpp-et-delivrance-des-actes-de-naissance-securises-au-benin-lanip-precise-les-nouvelles-directives/>). [ANIP's director general says the single-window birth declaration and registration is in a pilot in ten health facilities, with health agents now using new internet-connected terminals for declarations and registration feeding the national register of natural persons (RNPP), with generalisation to all facilities planned](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). [Law 2020-34 of 6 January 2021 dematerialised civil status and abolished paper civil registers, so every birth is registered in the RNPP, which counts as the civil-status declaration, per ANIP (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lepotentiel.bj/2026/05/06/enrolement-au-rnpp-et-delivrance-des-actes-de-naissance-securises-au-benin-lanip-precise-les-nouvelles-directives/>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lepotentiel.bj/2026/05/06/enrolement-au-rnpp-et-delivrance-des-actes-de-naissance-securises-au-benin-lanip-precise-les-nouvelles-directives/>).
