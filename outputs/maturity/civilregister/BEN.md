# Benin

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Benin's civil register has been digital since 2021. [ANIP says Law 2020-34 of January 2021 abolished paper civil registers](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). Every birth is now registered in the national register of persons, the RNPP. [The old paper registers have been fully digitised](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Since May 2026 secure birth certificates are issued from RNPP data](<https://lepotentiel.bj/2026/05/06/enrolement-au-rnpp-et-delivrance-des-actes-de-naissance-securises-au-benin-lanip-precise-les-nouvelles-directives/>). Registration is now moving into maternity wards. [A single desk there was piloted from August 2025 in ten public health facilities](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/le-b%C3%A9nin-modernise-lenregistrement-des-naissances-avec-un-guichet-unique-digital>). [ANIP says national scale-up is still to come](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/b%C3%A9nin-journ%C3%A9e-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-2026>).

**Deaths digital.** Deaths go into the same digital file. [ANIP says a death declared by a health worker or local chief is registered automatically](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). The person's status in the identity system changes to deceased at the same time.

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [ANIP gives nearly 94% for births declared on time, which counts declarations, not registrations](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
