# Uganda

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [NIRA says it developed and integrated the NSIS national ID system with a birth and death registration module built on open source OpenCRVS, with automatic NIN issuance on birth notification and immediate certification, and lists deploying crvs.nsis.nira.go.ug for birth registration as a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).
