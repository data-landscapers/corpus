# Uganda

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In July 2026 NIRA said it had integrated a birth and death registration module into the NSIS national ID system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [The module is built on open-source OpenCRVS](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [It issues a NIN automatically on birth notification, with immediate certification](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [NIRA lists deploying crvs.nsis.nira.go.ug for birth registration as a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 NIRA's OpenCRVS module covered death registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank put birth registration completeness at 32.2% in 2016](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [That is a national rate, not tied to a digital system](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).
