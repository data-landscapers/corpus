# Senegal

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [ANEC director-general Matar Ndao says modernisation covers 77% of Senegal's 629 civil registration centres, deployed in Dakar, Thies, Louga, Diourbel, Fatick and Kaolack regions, and maternity units can register births directly in ANEC's system](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>). [ANEC director-general Matar Ndao says the civil registration management software (LGEC) is deployed in 74% of civil registration centres and the Sama Etat civil online request platform is operational in 189 communes](<https://aps.sn/la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-une-veritable-politique-publique-et-strategique-selon-le-directeur-general-de-lanec/>). [RTS reports that the Registre national de l'etat civil (RNEC) is now operational, with 20 million civil-status acts digitised (2025)](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/vers-une-identite-numerique-fiable-le-senegal-franchit-un-cap-dans-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).
