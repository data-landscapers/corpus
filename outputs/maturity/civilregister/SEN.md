# Senegal

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In September 2026 the civil registration agency ANEC said its modernisation covers 77% of the 629 civil registration centres](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>), in the Dakar, Thiès, Louga, Diourbel, Fatick and Kaolack regions. Maternity units there can register births directly in its system. [In August 2026 it put its registration software in 74% of centres](<https://aps.sn/la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-une-veritable-politique-publique-et-strategique-selon-le-directeur-general-de-lanec/>), with the Sama Etat civil online request platform working in 189 communes. [RTS reported in July 2025 that the national civil register was operational](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/vers-une-identite-numerique-fiable-le-senegal-franchit-un-cap-dans-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>), with 20 million civil-status acts digitised. [In January 2026 ANEC's director general said the register gives real-time statistics only in connected communes](<https://www.sunugox.info/securisation-des-donnees-de-letat-civil-lanec-et-lansd-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-un-systeme-plus-fiable/>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the national civil register holds 20 million digitised civil-status acts](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/vers-une-identite-numerique-fiable-le-senegal-franchit-un-cap-dans-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [ANEC's software covers 77% of centres](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>).
