# Senegal

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [ANEC director-general Matar Ndao says modernisation covers 77% of Senegal's 629 civil registration centres, deployed in Dakar, Thies, Louga, Diourbel, Fatick and Kaolack regions, and maternity units can register births directly in ANEC's system](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>). [ANEC director-general Matar Ndao says the civil registration management software (LGEC) is deployed in 74% of civil registration centres and the Sama Etat civil online request platform is operational in 189 communes](<https://aps.sn/la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-une-veritable-politique-publique-et-strategique-selon-le-directeur-general-de-lanec/>). [ANEC states that 437 of Senegal's 629 civil registration centres are fully deployed on the LGEC, a 70% national deployment rate](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/anec-senegal_%C3%A9tat-davancement-au-18-juillet-2026-lanec-activity-7485345706107625472-_yhI>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>).
