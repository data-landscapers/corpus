**Births digital.** [UNICEF's Mali representative says a project for a platform digitising births, deaths and marriages has been approved, and the National Directorate of Civil Status has identified ten pilot areas](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>).
