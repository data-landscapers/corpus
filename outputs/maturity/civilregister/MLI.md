# Mali

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In December 2024 UNICEF's Mali representative said a project for a platform digitising births, deaths and marriages had been approved](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>). [The National Directorate of Civil Status has identified ten pilot areas](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>). [In July 2026 the directorate, DNEC, said the community birth declaration chain is still on paper](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>). [Village notebooks are passed onward](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>). [DNEC lists complete digitalisation of the system among its persisting priorities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [in December 2024 the approved platform was to digitise deaths too](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank put birth registration completeness at 86.7% in 2018](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [That is a national rate, not tied to a digital system](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).
