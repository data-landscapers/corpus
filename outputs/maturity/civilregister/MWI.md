# Malawi

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNDP Malawi reports an NRB official briefed an EU delegation on the real-time birth registration system at Bwaila Hospital in Lilongwe](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/eu-optimistic-about-progress-phase-iii-birth-registration-exercise>). [The National Registration Bureau says over 970,400 child records from Phase One of its birth registration campaign had synchronised with the NRB Database by 6 February 2026](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw/news/nrb-completes-phase-one-of-national-birth-registration-campaign/>). [UNDP says the Nzika registration application, which connects civil registration with national identity management, has been piloted in 87 locations across five districts, with expansion to 250 locations planned by 2028](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration>).

**Deaths digital.** No source held since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/eu-optimistic-about-progress-phase-iii-birth-registration-exercise>).

**Births at 90%.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/eu-optimistic-about-progress-phase-iii-birth-registration-exercise>).
