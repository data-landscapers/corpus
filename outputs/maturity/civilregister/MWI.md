# Malawi

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [UNDP says the Nzika registration application, which connects civil registration with national identity management, has been piloted in 87 locations across five districts, with expansion to 250 locations planned by 2028](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration>). [UNDP handed over 152 printers (100 to NRB offices at Traditional Authority level, 52 to health facilities) to support digitalisation of birth, marriage and death registration, and minister Peter Mukhito said 180 health facilities have already been digitalised](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/09/printers-to-bring-registration-services-closer-to-malawians/>). [UNDP Malawi reports an NRB official briefed an EU delegation on the real-time birth registration system at Bwaila Hospital in Lilongwe](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/eu-optimistic-about-progress-phase-iii-birth-registration-exercise>).

**Deaths digital.** [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo said NRB had integrated electronic birth and death registration systems with the national ID system by February 2025](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [EGPAF says electronic birth and death registration software is installed at 173 sites](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>). [The National Registration Bureau reports 179,000 deaths recorded in its registration system and electronic death registration in 142 health facilities in 2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-5-Malawi-final.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>).
