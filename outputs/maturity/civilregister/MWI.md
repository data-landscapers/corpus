# Malawi

## Civil register

**Births digital.** Malawi's central civil registration system runs in every district. [In 2024 EGPAF said it operated in all 28 district registration offices and in district and central hospitals](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>). [In October 2026 the minister said 180 health facilities had been digitalised](<https://malawi24.com/2026/10/09/printers-to-bring-registration-services-closer-to-malawians/>). [A new app, Nzika, linking registration to the national ID, was piloted in 87 places](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration>). It is to reach 250 by 2028.

**Deaths digital.** [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo said NRB had integrated electronic birth and death registration systems with the national ID system by February 2025](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [EGPAF says electronic birth and death registration software is installed at 173 sites](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>). [The National Registration Bureau reports 179,000 deaths recorded in its registration system and electronic death registration in 142 health facilities in 2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-5-Malawi-final.pdf>).

**Births at 90%.** About two births in three are registered. [The registration bureau said 67% of births were registered in 2023, and 27% of deaths](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/malawi-pushes-for-universal-birth>). The rates are not tied to the digital system.

***Also on file***. [National Registration and Identification System (NRIS)](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more.
