# Comoros

## Civil register

**Births digital.** [In October 2025 La Gazette des Comores reported the AMECC project coordinator saying civil registration is still largely manual](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/vers-un-%C3%A9tat-civil-num%C3%A9rique-moh%C3%A9li-ouvre-la-voie-%C3%A0-la-modernisation-nationale-.html>). [The coordinator cited frequent errors, falsification and lost documents](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/vers-un-%C3%A9tat-civil-num%C3%A9rique-moh%C3%A9li-ouvre-la-voie-%C3%A0-la-modernisation-nationale-.html>). [A digitisation study was launched on 10 October 2025](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/vers-un-%C3%A9tat-civil-num%C3%A9rique-moh%C3%A9li-ouvre-la-voie-%C3%A0-la-modernisation-nationale-.html>). [In March 2026 Comores Droit said births, marriages and deaths have been recorded in paper registers for decades](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>). [A preliminary study found internet access at only one in five of 37 surveyed civil-status centres](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>). [In August 2026 La Gazette des Comores reported that the system still largely depends on old paper registers](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/%C3%A9tat-civil-l%E2%80%99amecc-veut-enterrer-les-registres-sur-papier-.html>). [People in rural areas still held no birth extract in 2026](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/%C3%A9tat-civil-l%E2%80%99amecc-veut-enterrer-les-registres-sur-papier-.html>).

**Deaths digital.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths are registered in the digital system. The nearest held fact: [in March 2026 Comores Droit said deaths are recorded in paper registers](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>).

**Births at 90%.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at 90% or more. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank put birth registration completeness at 87.3% in 2012](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [That is a national rate, not tied to a digital system](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).
