# Ghana

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** On 16 March 2026 the Minister for Transport [launched the Passenger Complaints Portal at the Ghana Civil Aviation Authority](<https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection>). Air passengers report, track and resolve complaints on the web or by QR codes in the terminal. They are acknowledged by email and SMS. In March 2024 the World Bank described [Have Your Say, where companies complain about regulatory bottlenecks](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/transforming-governance-in-ghana-a-new-regulatory-service-model>). It was then in the first stages of implementation.

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches the services of most ministries. The nearest held fact: [the passenger portal covers air transport alone](<https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection>).

**Results published.** No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled. The nearest held fact: [passengers can track their complaints to resolution](<https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection>).
