# Ghana

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** On 16 March 2026 the Minister for Transport [launched the Passenger Complaints Portal at the Ghana Civil Aviation Authority](<https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection>). Air passengers report and track complaints on the web or by QR codes in the terminal. [In June 2025 a UN peacebuilding project in the Upper West Region introduced a mobile app for complaints about security, justice, social and health services](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/06/unfpa-undp-pbf-project-introduces-mobile-app-to-enhance-public-service-delivery/>). [The same month CHRAJ said the rollout of its electronic case management system had stalled](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/06/chraj-appeals-for-government-support-to-complete-case-management-system/>). [In November 2024 the Sekondi-Takoradi Metropolitan Assembly launched Twin-City Connect](<https://www.stma.gov.gh/STMA-Launches-Twin-City-Connect-An-Electronic-Government-Platform.php>), which takes citizens' complaints. [In January 2024 the Ghana Statistical Service piloted an app and USSD code for feedback on education, health and ID services](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/01/pilot-phase-of-the-public-services-satisfaction-survey-app-launched/>) in two districts.

**Across government.** Each channel held serves one sector, region or town: [the passenger portal covers air transport alone](<https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection>), and [Twin-City Connect one metropolitan assembly](<https://www.stma.gov.gh/STMA-Launches-Twin-City-Connect-An-Electronic-Government-Platform.php>). No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches the services of most ministries.

**Results published.** No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
