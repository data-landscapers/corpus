# Morocco

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [Statistics on Chikaya.ma show over 1,677,000 complaints filed since the January 2018 launch, 111,945 of them from January to 3 September 2025](<https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/chikaya-ma-plus-de-16-million-de-plaintes-deposees-jusqua-present>). [Minister Seghrouchni told parliament the national complaints portal Chikaya.ma recorded about 176,000 complaints in 2024 and over 186,000 in 2023](<https://h24info.ma/maroc/chikaya-ma-reclamations-seghrouchni/>). [Morocco World News reports citizens filed 163,694 complaints against public administrations through the national Chikaya.ma platform in 2025, and that it was launched in 2018](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/01/273688/moroccan-citizens-filed-163694-complaints-against-public-administrations-in-2025/>).

**Across government.** [The portal gives access to 38 government departments, 116 public institutions and 1,590 local authorities](<https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/chikaya-ma-plus-de-16-million-de-plaintes-deposees-jusqua-present>). [The minister said the portal gives access to 38 government departments, 116 public institutions and companies, and 1,590 local authorities](<https://h24info.ma/maroc/chikaya-ma-reclamations-seghrouchni/>).

**Results published.** [Morocco World News reports Chikaya.ma processed 66.14% of 2025 complaints, with a 58.69% satisfaction rate, an average processing time of 16 days and more than 14,000 cases reopened](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/01/273688/moroccan-citizens-filed-163694-complaints-against-public-administrations-in-2025/>). [Chikaya.ma statistics give 1,406,000 complaints processed (65.46%), an average processing time of 12 days and 60.94% positive feedback](<https://aujourdhui.ma/societe/chikaya-ma-plus-de-16-million-de-plaintes-deposees-jusqua-present>).
