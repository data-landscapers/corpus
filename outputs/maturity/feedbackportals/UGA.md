# Uganda

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [GIZ said in 2025 that the Citizen Feedback Platform lets Ugandans report concerns about service delivery to the Office of the Auditor General](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2025-09/giz2025-en-gcsp-factsheet_0.pdf>). Since 2022, 2,300 citizens had registered on it and on two other apps. These are the procurement authority's Contract Monitoring System and the Uganda Human Rights Commission's app. Some 900 cases reported through the three had been resolved.

**Across government.** [The platform sends reports to the Office of the Auditor General](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2025-09/giz2025-en-gcsp-factsheet_0.pdf>), by GIZ's account. No source found says a national channel takes complaints about most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [GIZ's factsheet gives 900 cases resolved since 2022](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2025-09/giz2025-en-gcsp-factsheet_0.pdf>), across the three apps together. No resolution rate, response time or case report has been found.

***Also on file***. [Contract Monitoring System](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2025-09/giz2025-en-gcsp-factsheet_0.pdf>), [UHRC App](<https://www.giz.de/sites/default/files/media/pkb-document/2025-09/giz2025-en-gcsp-factsheet_0.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national channel takes complaints across government. No resolution rate or case report has been found.
