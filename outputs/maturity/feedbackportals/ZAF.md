# South Africa

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Minister in the Presidency said the Presidential Hotline received 1,832 complaints in April to June 2025](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32718/>). [In January 2025 DPME said the hotline had received 339,579 cases since September 2009](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>), including calls, emails and walk-ins. Of these, 242,636 were complaints, logged in a web-based case system. [DPME also said its Khawuleza app received no cases in a Limpopo pilot](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>), because of technical faults. [In April 2025 the Mpumalanga government said it would launch the Livi Lemphakatsi app](<http://www.mpumalanga.gov.za/media/statements/otp_/2025/29042025.htm>) for residents to submit and follow complaints. [In July 2025 the City of Tshwane launched an app for residents to log and track service faults](<https://www.citizen.co.za/rekord/news-headlines/2025/07/11/mobile-app-to-bring-service-delivery-closer-to-residents/>).

**Across government.** [In January 2025 DPME said hotline complaints are assigned through its case system to departments and municipalities](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>). Each holds a licence to view and close its cases. [National and provincial departments are expected to resolve hotline complaints within 25 working days](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32718/>), the Minister said in September 2025.

**Results published.** [The Minister said 991 of the 1,832 complaints of April to June 2025 were resolved](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32718/>), a rate of 55 per cent. [In January 2025 DPME said 96.7% of complaints since 2009 had been resolved](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>). It said quarterly, mid-year and annual hotline reports are available.
