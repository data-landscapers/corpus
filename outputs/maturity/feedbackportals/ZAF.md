# South Africa

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [DPME says the Khawuleza app received no cases after being piloted in Limpopo owing to technical glitches, and benchmarking is under way for digitalising the hotline](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>). [The Department of Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation says the Presidential Hotline received 339,579 cases since September 2009, including calls, emails and walk-ins, of which 242,636 were complaints, logged in a web-based ITSM system](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Across government.** [DPME says hotline complaints are assigned through ITSM directly to stakeholders, municipalities and departments, each with a licence to view and close cases](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>).

**Results published.** [DPME says 234,752 of 242,636 complaints (96.7%) were resolved as at 17 December 2024 and that quarterly, mid-year and annual hotline reports are available](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW727-2025-01-06.pdf>).
