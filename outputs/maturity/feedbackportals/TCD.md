# Chad

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In June 2024 [Chad's Ministry of Justice and Human Rights, with UNDP, was preparing an online platform for filing complaints and requests](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-le-ministere-de-la-justice-compte-creer-une-plateforme-virtuelle-de-depot-de-plaintes-et-requetes/>). Its committee for mapping the procedures met for the first time on 6 June 2024. Nothing had yet been built when the committee first met, and no date was given for the platform.

**Across government.** The platform is planned for [complaints and requests to the justice ministry](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-le-ministere-de-la-justice-compte-creer-une-plateforme-virtuelle-de-depot-de-plaintes-et-requetes/>). Its committee was mapping procedures in June 2024. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The report of the committee's first meeting](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-le-ministere-de-la-justice-compte-creer-une-plateforme-virtuelle-de-depot-de-plaintes-et-requetes/>) gives no number of complaints, as nothing was yet built. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
