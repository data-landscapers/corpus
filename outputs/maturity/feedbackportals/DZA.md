# Algeria

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Mediator of the Republic's online grievance-register portal had received 52,262 grievances by 22 April 2024](<https://www.dknews-dz.com/flash/4133-mediature-de-la-republique-plus-de-40000-doleances-traitees-par-linstance.html>), counted from October 2023. [Its Annaba delegation received 55 requests through the Wassit.dz app in 2025](<https://leprovincial.dz/bilan-de-la-mediation-de-la-republique-plus-de-2-000-requetes-etudiees/>). [In February 2025 the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications launched a portal for complaints about postal and telecom services](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/02/22/un-portail-electronique-pour-prendre-en-charge-les-requetes-des-citoyens/>). [In April 2026 the Ministry of Youth launched the Sawtoukoum platform](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-sawtoukoum-platform-to-channel-youth-feedback-and-complaints/>), where users report shortcomings and follow each case.

**Across government.** [The Mediator's portal serves all administrations and public establishments](<https://www.dknews-dz.com/flash/4133-mediature-de-la-republique-plus-de-40000-doleances-traitees-par-linstance.html>), its digitisation director said in April 2024. By then 43,870 accounts had been opened for 21,953 public administrations. [In April 2024 the Mediator also presented Tansik](<https://www.dknews-dz.com/article/184987-mediature-de-la-republique-de-nouvelles-plateformes-pour-une-meilleure-prise-en-charge-des-preoccupations-des-citoyens.html>), a platform for the grievance registers of local and central administrations.

**Results published.** [The Mediator's office said 40,145 of the 52,262 grievances had been handled and closed by 22 April 2024](<https://www.dknews-dz.com/flash/4133-mediature-de-la-republique-plus-de-40000-doleances-traitees-par-linstance.html>). That is 76.81 per cent of them.

***Also on file***. [registre des doléances des citoyens](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/developpement/mpjv5j1x-la-numerisation-du-registre-des-doleances-des-citoyens-un-pas-qualitatif-vers-la-modernisation-de-l-administration>).

***Not found***. No resolution figures newer than April 2024 have been found.
