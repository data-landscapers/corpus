# Mauritius

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In May 2026 the ICT minister told parliament that [the Korek app takes online complaints about water leaks](<https://lexpress.mu/s/application-korek-462-310-enregistrements-depuis-janvier-2026-557967>). The app was launched on 30 January 2026. The Citizen Support Unit has run [an online complaints portal at csu.mu since April 2017](<https://www.mazavaroo.mu/pravind-jugnauth-835-des-demandes-et-plaintes-au-csu-traitees-en-3-ans/>), where citizens register and track complaints.

**Across government.** In July 2020 the Prime Minister said [the Citizen Support Unit portal links the public to about 320 institutions](<https://www.mazavaroo.mu/pravind-jugnauth-835-des-demandes-et-plaintes-au-csu-traitees-en-3-ans/>). They include ministries, departments, local authorities and parastatals. No source since October 2023 describes its reach.

**Results published.** The latest results found date from 2020. The Prime Minister told Parliament that [83.5% of requests and complaints to the Citizen Support Unit were handled in three years](<https://www.mazavaroo.mu/pravind-jugnauth-835-des-demandes-et-plaintes-au-csu-traitees-en-3-ans/>). No count of complaints made through Korek has been found.
