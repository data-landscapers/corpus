# Mauritania

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In May 2025 the Minister of Digital Transformation said [the Ain app had recorded about 9,000 requests and complaints on access to basic services](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/271580>). Ain was launched in October 2024. That month the Council of Ministers [adopted a draft decree on the Khadamati portal](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/257680>), which is to take citizens' grievances, reports and complaints.

**Across government.** The minister presented Ain as [a national app for complaints on access to basic services](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/271580>), run by the digital transformation ministry. No source found names the ministries whose services it covers.

**Results published.** In May 2025 the minister said [96% of the requests and complaints on Ain had been processed](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/271580>), the other 4% being in progress. Of the citizens who rated the service, 52% called it good.
