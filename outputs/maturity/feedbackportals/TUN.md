# Tunisia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Presidency of the Government says it launched the online platform e-Mouaten (e-people.gov.tn), through which citizens report in real time obstacles met with the administration and follow the handling of their requests, with no case figures given](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>). [The Médiateur Administratif says citizens can submit petitions through its website, a mobile app and its social network, and that 95 petitions came in through electronic channels in 2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>). [Tunisia's e-people civil petition and corruption reporting platform was in a pilot phase linked to ten government bodies, with no reports yet available, per the IRM (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/tunisia/commitments/TN0030/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>).

**Results published.** [The Médiateur Administratif publishes statistics from its 2020 annual report on files handled centrally, with 51% answered, 49% unanswered and 38% still being followed up, broken down by type of body (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>).
