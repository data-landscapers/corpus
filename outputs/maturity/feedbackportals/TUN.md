# Tunisia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In July 2025 the Presidency of the Government launched the online platform e-Mouaten](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>). [Citizens use it to report obstacles met with the administration](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>). [They can follow the handling of their requests](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>). [The Médiateur Administratif takes petitions through its website, a mobile app and its social network](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>). [In 2020, 95 petitions came in through those electronic channels](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>). [In 2019 the IRM said the e-people petition platform was a pilot linked to ten government bodies](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/tunisia/commitments/TN0030/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 e-Mouaten let citizens report obstacles met with the administration and follow their requests](<https://letemps.news/2025/07/24/plateforme-e-mouaten-pour-la-participation-citoyenne-une-democratie-en-construction/>).

**Results published.** [In its 2020 annual report the Médiateur Administratif said 51% of the files it handled centrally were answered and 49% unanswered](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>). [It broke them down by type of body](<https://www.mediateur.tn/category/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%a7%d8%aa/>).
