# Sierra Leone

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In April 2026 the Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation, the electricity distributor EDSA and the Ministry of Energy launched a WhatsApp chatbot](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-directorate-of-science-technology-and-innovation_edsa-whatsapp-chatbot-official-launch-the-activity-7445754881841471488-7rs2>). Citizens use it to report electricity faults and share their location. Each report gets a ticket number for tracking, and the system assigns the case. It replaced paper complaint forms and was launched for nationwide use.

**Across government.** [The chatbot takes reports on electricity supply alone](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-directorate-of-science-technology-and-innovation_edsa-whatsapp-chatbot-official-launch-the-activity-7445754881841471488-7rs2>), for one utility. No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches the services of most ministries or of local governments.

**Results published.** [The launch post says the chatbot records fault patterns and response times for EDSA](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/the-directorate-of-science-technology-and-innovation_edsa-whatsapp-chatbot-official-launch-the-activity-7445754881841471488-7rs2>), and gives no number of reports. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
