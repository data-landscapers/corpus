# Libya

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In November 2026 the economy ministry launched a platform for enquiries and complaints about obstacles to trade](<https://alwasat.ly/news/484587>). [On 31 August 2026 the health ministry put a link on its website for complaints, comments and reports](<https://alwasat.ly/news/476453>), to be referred to the bodies concerned. [On 7 April 2026 the Minister of Agriculture and Livestock launched a platform for complaints and inquiries from farmers and citizens](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/513847>). [The communications authority announced Tawasul, a platform for citizens' complaints about telecom services](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=321836&lang=ar>). It tracks them with the operators, and citizens can follow their resolution. In June 2025 [the Central Bank of Libya opened an online form for complaints about its LYPay payment service](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-officially-launches-the-website-for-the-instant-payment-service-lypay/>). [The Citizen Service Centre received over 4,000 requests and complaints on its first day, 12 October 2021](<https://csc.gov.ly/en/home/>). Thirty agents took them through Facebook.

**Across government.** [In 2021 the Citizen Service Centre referred each complaint to the delegate of the ministry concerned](<https://csc.gov.ly/en/home/>). Most first-day requests concerned finance and health.
