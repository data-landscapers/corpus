# Libya

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Minister of Agriculture and Livestock of the Government of National Unity announced on 7 April 2026 the launch of a platform for complaints, comments and inquiries from farmers and citizens, with follow-up of action taken](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/513847>). [The General Authority for Communications and Informatics announced the Tawasul platform to receive citizens' complaints about telecom services, count and track them with operators and let citizens follow resolution](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=321836&lang=ar>). On 18 June 2025 [the Central Bank of Libya launched the website of its LYPay instant payment service with an online platform for complaints and inquiries](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-officially-launches-the-website-for-the-instant-payment-service-lypay/>), giving no case count. [The Citizen Service Centre's own page says it received over 4,000 requests and complaints on its first working day, 12 October 2021, taken via Facebook by thirty agents (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://csc.gov.ly/en/home/>).

**Across government.** [The Citizen Service Centre's own page says tickets are verified by an agent and referred to the delegate of the ministry concerned, with first-day requests mostly on Finance and Health and the rest distributed among the other ministries (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://csc.gov.ly/en/home/>).
