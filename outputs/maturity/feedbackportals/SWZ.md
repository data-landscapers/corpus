# Eswatini

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [On 21 September 2026 the National Road Transport Council announced BIKA, a platform for reporting problems on public and freight transport](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-rolls-out-bika-platform-to-combat-corruption-abuse-on-public-transport-in-real-time/>). The public can report corruption and abuse there. Reports can be anonymous and are tracked by a reference number. [In June 2020 the Ministry of Health and UNICEF launched a feedback mechanism for health clients](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/stories/unicef-and-ministry-health-launch-client-satisfaction-and-feedback-mechanism>). It asks them about satisfaction and waiting times.

**Across government.** BIKA, run by the National Road Transport Council, covers [reports on public and freight transport](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-rolls-out-bika-platform-to-combat-corruption-abuse-on-public-transport-in-real-time/>) only.
