# Lesotho

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In August 2024 the Ministry of Communications said [the National Contact Centre takes complaints by telephone and email](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/national-contact-centre-pro-workshop/>). Its agents pass each one to the ministry concerned. That ministry's public relations officer must report back within three days. The centre will also serve as a feedback mechanism for government.

**Across government.** The centre's agents [pass complaints to the public relations officers of each ministry](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/national-contact-centre-pro-workshop/>). The ministry said the centre was still being established. Nothing found since October 2023 shows it in service across government.

**Results published.** [The ministry's account of the centre](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/national-contact-centre-pro-workshop/>) gives no number of complaints taken or answered. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
