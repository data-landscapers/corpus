# Seychelles

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [On 18 February 2025 the Seychelles Revenue Commission opened a complaints email address and hotline](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-approach-for-handling-feedback/>). Taxpayers use it to comment on the service they received from its officers and the time it took. At weekends they can send feedback by WhatsApp on the same number. The Commission said it would use the feedback to find what needs improving.

**Across government.** [The lines serve the Revenue Commission alone](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-approach-for-handling-feedback/>). [In April 2023 the finance ministry set up an online feedback form for itself and its agencies](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/18555/Seychelles'+finance+ministry+launches+customer+feedback+service>). No source found since October 2023 says a channel reaches most ministries.

**Results published.** [The Commission's announcement](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-approach-for-handling-feedback/>) gives no number of complaints received or resolved. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
