# Benin

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [La Marina reported that the telecoms regulator ARCEP handled about a hundred formal consumer complaints in 2025](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/06/17/telecoms-comment-larcep-benin-compte-automatiser-la-gestion-des-litiges/>). They came by green line, counter, email and social networks, and 67 per cent were found justified. [In June 2026 ARCEP was preparing to link these channels to one automated back-office](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/06/17/telecoms-comment-larcep-benin-compte-automatiser-la-gestion-des-litiges/>), by the paper's account. [Benin's Treasury (DGTCP) says its Centre d'Ecoute Clients has been in a pilot phase since July 2024](<https://tresorbenin.bj/actualite/douzieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-28-fevrier-2025-1>). It takes complaints by phone, email, web form and at a counter. [On 14 February 2025 the commune of Adjarra launched the Doton app for complaints about communal services](<https://dpi.africa/fr/doton-une-plateforme-mobile-rapproche-ladministration-des-citoyens/>).

**Across government.** [ARCEP's channels take complaints about telecoms](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/06/17/telecoms-comment-larcep-benin-compte-automatiser-la-gestion-des-litiges/>), one sector. No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches the services of most ministries or of local governments.

**Results published.** [La Marina gave the 2025 complaint count from an unnamed source](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/06/17/telecoms-comment-larcep-benin-compte-automatiser-la-gestion-des-litiges/>), not from a published report. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
