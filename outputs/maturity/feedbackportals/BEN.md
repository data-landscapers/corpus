# Benin

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [Benin's Treasury (DGTCP) says its Centre d'Ecoute Clients has been in a pilot phase since July 2024, taking complaints by phone, email, web form and a physical counter, with the IT complaints-management system still in production and to be deployed soon](<https://tresorbenin.bj/actualite/douzieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-28-fevrier-2025-1>). [The commune of Adjarra launched the Doton mobile app on 14 February 2025 for citizens to file complaints and suggestions about communal services, with status tracking and a dedicated unit in the town hall, but the article gives no count of complaints received](<https://dpi.africa/fr/doton-une-plateforme-mobile-rapproche-ladministration-des-citoyens/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tresorbenin.bj/actualite/douzieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-28-fevrier-2025-1>).
