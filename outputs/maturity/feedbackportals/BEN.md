# Benin

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [Benin's Treasury (DGTCP) says its Centre d'Ecoute Clients has been in a pilot phase since July 2024](<https://tresorbenin.bj/actualite/douzieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-28-fevrier-2025-1>). It takes complaints by phone, email, web form and at a counter, and its complaints-management software is still being built. [On 14 February 2025 the commune of Adjarra launched the Doton app for complaints and suggestions about communal services](<https://dpi.africa/fr/doton-une-plateforme-mobile-rapproche-ladministration-des-citoyens/>). People can follow their complaint, and a unit in the town hall handles them.

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tresorbenin.bj/actualite/douzieme-edition-du-vendredi-des-reformes-a-la-dgtcp-ce-28-fevrier-2025-1>).
