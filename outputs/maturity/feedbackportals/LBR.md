# Liberia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Cabinet Director General said citizens would submit feedback on government performance through a short SMS reporting system alongside the new Performance Management Information System](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/boakai-government-performance-tracking-system/>). [Cabinet Director Nathaniel Kawbo announced President Boakai's 'Citizens Feedback' platform under the 2026 Performance Contract, taking public reports on officials and stalled projects by SMS to shortcode 2424 on Orange and Lonestar](<https://geezliberia.com/2026/04/president-boakai-launches-citizens-feedback-initiative-invites-direct-public-reporting-on-government-officials/>). [FrontPageAfrica reports IDAD, with UNDP funding, began a ten-county awareness rollout of the Citizens Feedback Mechanism, a national SMS (short code 2424) and web platform for citizens to report on public service delivery (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-idad-rolls-out-initiative-to-get-citizens-feedback-on-public-service-delivery/>).

**Across government.** [The article describes the Citizens Feedback Mechanism as a national platform through which citizens' messages are channelled to the concerned Ministries, Agencies or Commissions (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-idad-rolls-out-initiative-to-get-citizens-feedback-on-public-service-delivery/>).
