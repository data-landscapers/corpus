# Liberia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In April 2026 the Cabinet Director General said citizens would give feedback on government performance by SMS](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/boakai-government-performance-tracking-system/>). [The SMS system sits beside the new Performance Management Information System](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/boakai-government-performance-tracking-system/>). [Cabinet Director Nathaniel Kawbo announced President Boakai's 'Citizens Feedback' platform](<https://geezliberia.com/2026/04/president-boakai-launches-citizens-feedback-initiative-invites-direct-public-reporting-on-government-officials/>). [It takes reports on officials and stalled projects by SMS to shortcode 2424](<https://geezliberia.com/2026/04/president-boakai-launches-citizens-feedback-initiative-invites-direct-public-reporting-on-government-officials/>). [In May 2023 IDAD, with UNDP funding, began a ten-county rollout of the Citizens Feedback Mechanism](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-idad-rolls-out-initiative-to-get-citizens-feedback-on-public-service-delivery/>). [It is a national SMS and web platform for reporting on public services](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-idad-rolls-out-initiative-to-get-citizens-feedback-on-public-service-delivery/>).

**Across government.** [In May 2023 the Citizens Feedback Mechanism channelled citizens' messages to the ministries, agencies or commissions concerned](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-idad-rolls-out-initiative-to-get-citizens-feedback-on-public-service-delivery/>).
