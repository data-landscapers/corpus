# Egypt

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Cabinet's Unified Government Complaints System received and logged 195,000 complaints, appeals, requests and enquiries in September 2025, per the Cabinet assistant secretary-general for complaints](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Across government.** [The system routed 153,000 items in September 2025 to competent bodies, 64% to ministries, 22% to governorates and 14% to linked authorities and universities, with the Central Bank overseeing routing to banks](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>).

**Results published.** [The monthly report to the Prime Minister gives complaints resolved per ministry and body, for example 15.1 thousand in health and 13.6 thousand at the Interior Ministry, for September and earlier periods](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>).
