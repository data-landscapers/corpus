# Egypt

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Cabinet's Unified Government Complaints System received and handled 206,000 complaints, requests and enquiries in May 2026](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/571908/Egypt/Society/Egypt-moves-citizen-complaints-beyond-hotlines-wit.aspx>), per the State Information Service. It takes them by the 16528 hotline, a portal, the Fi Khedmetak app and WhatsApp. [In September 2025 it logged 195,000 items](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>), per the Cabinet's assistant secretary-general for complaints. [The Cabinet said the system handled 1.46 million items in 2023](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/74891>), 15% more than in 2022.

**Across government.** [In September 2025 the system routed 153,000 items to the competent bodies](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>). Ministries took 64%, governorates 22%, and linked authorities and universities 14%. [In May 2026 it referred 172,000 of 206,000 submissions to the competent authorities](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/571908/Egypt/Society/Egypt-moves-citizen-complaints-beyond-hotlines-wit.aspx>), per the State Information Service.

**Results published.** [The monthly report to the Prime Minister gives complaints resolved per ministry and body](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>). In September 2025 health resolved 15,100 and the Interior Ministry 13,600. [The Cabinet's report on 2023 gives cases and what was done](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/74891>). For example, 72,000 of 78,000 complaints of encroachment on land were settled with legal action.
