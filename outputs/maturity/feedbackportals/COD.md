# DR Congo

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In May 2026 the Ministry of National Economy launched the 'Loba' chatbot for consumer complaints in regulated sectors](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-loba-pour-la-gestion-des-plaintes-des-consommateurs/>). They include public health and electricity. Its competition commission, COMCO, runs the chatbot. It is piloted in Kinshasa before reaching the provinces.

**Across government.** Loba is run by the economy ministry's competition commission. It takes [consumer complaints in the sectors it regulates](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-loba-pour-la-gestion-des-plaintes-des-consommateurs/>), among them health and electricity. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The ministry's announcement of the Kinshasa pilot](<https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-loba-pour-la-gestion-des-plaintes-des-consommateurs/>) gives no number of complaints received or resolved. The pilot runs in Kinshasa first. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
