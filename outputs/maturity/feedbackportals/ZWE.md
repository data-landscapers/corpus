# Zimbabwe

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In July 2025 [Zimbabwe's Ministry of Health and Child Care launched a Digital Grievance Redress Mechanism](<https://www.theanchor.co.zw/ministry-of-health-launches-digital-complaint-system/>), with support from the World Bank and Cordaid. Patients can rate services, write their own feedback, use mobile apps or go to public feedback booths. The mechanism covers the health services that the ministry runs. [In April 2026 the Ministry of Public Service presented progress on a proposed Grievance Redress Feedback Mechanism](<https://zimbabwenow.co.zw/articles/22060/zim-tests-grievance-system-model-abroad>) in Stockholm. [In August 2026 the City of Bulawayo launched a digital Citizen Engagement Scorecard](<https://cite.org.zw/bulawayo-launches-citizen-engagement-scorecard-to-improve-service-delivery/>), with the local government ministry, for residents to rate services and give feedback.

**Across government.** The mechanism covers [health services, run by the Ministry of Health and Child Care](<https://www.theanchor.co.zw/ministry-of-health-launches-digital-complaint-system/>) with apps and feedback booths. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The report of the launch in July 2025](<https://www.theanchor.co.zw/ministry-of-health-launches-digital-complaint-system/>) gives no number of complaints or ratings received. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
