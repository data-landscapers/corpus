# Citizen feedback portals: citizen feedback channel

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The AU Charter on Decentralisation asks that ICT be used so residents can give feedback to their representatives; the UN E-Participation Index ranks e-information, e-consultation and e-decision-making. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## Countries

### Egypt: 5 Leading

Results of the national complaints channel were published in 2025.

- The monthly report to the Prime Minister gives complaints resolved per ministry and body, for example 15.1 thousand in health and 13.6 thousand at the Interior Ministry, for September and earlier periods ([source, 2025-09](https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705)).

### Nigeria: 4 Operating

A national complaints channel covered services across government in 2026; its results are not published.

- SERVICOM's acting National Coordinator says its Ministerial and Parastatal SERVICOM Units across MDAs let complaints be received, escalated, tracked and resolved across all tiers of government through the platform ([source, 2026-03-05](https://servicom.gov.ng/servicom-presidency-office-launches-the-nigeria-egm-electronic-grievance-management-platform/)).

### Comoros: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- CCARDESA reports that a national consultation in Moroni on 23 March 2026, with nearly 60 participants, worked to operationalise the World Bank-supported Food Systems Resilience Programme grievance redress mechanism, which takes complaints through in-person submissions, digital platforms and formal channels ([source, 2026-03-23](https://www.ccardesa.org/comoros-strengthening-accountability-food-systems-through-stakeholder-consultations)).

### DR Congo: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- The Ministry of National Economy, through the Commission de la Concurrence (COMCO), launched the 'Loba' chatbot from the week of 1 May 2026 to help consumers file complaints in regulated sectors including public health and electricity, with a pilot phase in Kinshasa before extension to the provinces, per the ministry communique reported by ACP ([source, 2026-05](https://acp.cd/economie/rdc-lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-loba-pour-la-gestion-des-plaintes-des-consommateurs/)).

### Djibouti: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- ARULoS, the urban rehabilitation and social housing agency, runs an online complaints platform (E-DOLÉANCES) that routes recovery problems to the DOCR division and technical problems to the DLS directorate; no case numbers are given ([source, 2026-08-31](https://www.doleances.arulos.dj/)).

### Ghana: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- The Minister for Transport launched the Passenger Complaints Portal at the Ghana Civil Aviation Authority, a web portal and terminal QR-code channel for air passengers to report, track and resolve complaints, with email and SMS acknowledgement ([source, 2026-03-16](https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/335/minister-for-transport-launches-passenger-complaints-portal-to-enhance-passenger-protection)).

### Rwanda: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- The Ministry of Local Government states that the revised Citizens' Assembly guidelines have assembly reports supported by the electronic software e-Citizen Complaints, which is used to receive and solve citizens' complaints online ([source, 2026-06-03](https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/citizens-assembly-guidelines-revised)).

### Senegal: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- The Ministry of Energy, Petroleum and Mines held the official launch workshop on 17 March 2026 for a complaints management mechanism for the oil and gas sector, with reception channels WhatsApp, e-mail, a web form and physical boxes ([source, 2026-03-17](https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/mise-en-place-dun-mecanisme-de-gestion-des-plaintes-le-mepm-reaffirme-son-engagement-pour-une-gouvernance-responsable-participative-et-apaisee-des-ressources-naturelles/)).

### Somalia: 2 Nascent

A digital channel for complaints about public services was under way in 2026; one in service is not on record.

- The Somali Ministry of Communication and Technology's EA-RDIP project unit sets out in its January 2026 manual a grievance mechanism whose channels will include telephone hotline, SMS and WhatsApp, email and online platforms, plus suggestion boxes, for complaints about the project; no case numbers are given ([source, 2026-01](https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/GRM-Manual-Final-clean-copy.pdf)).
