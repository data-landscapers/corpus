# Togo

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [On 27 March 2026 the national health insurance institute INAM announced an online platform, plaintes.inam.tg](<https://auxnouvelles.tg/2026/03/27/sante-inam-lance-une-plateforme-numerique/>). [Insured people and partners file and track complaints on it](<https://auxnouvelles.tg/2026/03/27/sante-inam-lance-une-plateforme-numerique/>). [It sits beside counters and a toll-free line, 8220](<https://auxnouvelles.tg/2026/03/27/sante-inam-lance-une-plateforme-numerique/>). [In January 2025 the Ministry of Security launched an online platform for citizens to report abuses by security forces](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.com/securite/0901-10169-une-plateforme-en-ligne-desormais-pour-denoncer-les-abus-des-forces-de-l-ordre>). [Complainants are told of the follow-up](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.com/securite/0901-10169-une-plateforme-en-ligne-desormais-pour-denoncer-les-abus-des-forces-de-l-ordre>).
