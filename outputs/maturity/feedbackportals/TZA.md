# Tanzania

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The e-Government Authority's e-Mrejesho system works by USSD, mobile app and website](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [Citizens send feedback, complaints or compliments to government institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [In December 2022 e-GA said citizens could send complaints straight to the institution concerned](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-mrejesho-ni-suluhisho-la-kero-za-wananchi>). [They can follow them up, anonymously if they choose](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-mrejesho-ni-suluhisho-la-kero-za-wananchi>).

**Across government.** [The e-Government Authority says e-Mrejesho lets citizens reach all government institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [It passes a misdirected complaint to the right institution and allows appeal to a higher level](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [In December 2022 e-GA's director general said all government institutions were connected to it](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-mrejesho-ni-suluhisho-la-kero-za-wananchi>).
