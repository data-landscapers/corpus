# Tanzania

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The e-Government Authority says the e-Mrejesho system is available by USSD, mobile app and website for citizens to send feedback, complaints or compliments to government institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [E-GA states its e-Mrejesho system, on web, app and USSD *152*00#, lets citizens send complaints and comments directly to the relevant institution or ministry and follow them up, anonymously if they choose (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-mrejesho-ni-suluhisho-la-kero-za-wananchi>).

**Across government.** [The e-Government Authority says e-Mrejesho lets citizens reach all government institutions, transfers a misdirected complaint between institutions and allows appeal to a higher level](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/mfumo-wa-e-mrejesho-washinda-tuzo-ya-umoja-wa-mataifa-ya-ubunifu-katika-huduma-za-umma>). [E-GA's director general says e-Mrejesho connects all government institutions, which can exchange the complaints and opinions citizens submit (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-mrejesho-ni-suluhisho-la-kero-za-wananchi>).
