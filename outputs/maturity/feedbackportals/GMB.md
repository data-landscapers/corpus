# Gambia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In February 2015 the Office of the Ombudsman launched a website that let citizens file a complaint online for the first time](<https://www.theioi.org/ioi-news/current-news/office-of-the-ombudsman-launches-new-website>). [In April 2026 NAWEC, the national water and electricity company, launched a mobile app](<https://mansabanko.gm/nawec-launches-mobile-app-to-digitize-utility-services-in-the-gambia/>). [Customers can lodge complaints and report faults on it in real time, with photos](<https://mansabanko.gm/nawec-launches-mobile-app-to-digitize-utility-services-in-the-gambia/>).
