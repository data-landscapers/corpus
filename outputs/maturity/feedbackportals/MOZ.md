# Mozambique

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In June 2026 AURAS, Mozambique's regulator of water and sanitation services, [presented a new digital complaints platform, RECO](<https://www.aura.org.mz/noticias/51>). Any citizen can use it to submit a complaint or an appeal online about the regulated water and sanitation services. The platform covers only the water and sanitation services that AURAS regulates.

**Across government.** RECO takes [complaints and appeals about the water and sanitation services that AURAS regulates](<https://www.aura.org.mz/noticias/51>). It covers that one sector and no other. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The regulator's presentation of RECO](<https://www.aura.org.mz/noticias/51>) gives no number of complaints received or settled. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
