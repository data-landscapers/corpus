# Namibia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In November 2025 Walvis Bay Municipality [launched Ovakalimo, an online platform for reporting problems with municipal services](<https://futuremedianews.com.na/2025/11/18/walvis-bay-launches-online-reporting-tool-to-strengthen-local-accountability/>). A resident sends a report with a photo. The platform passes it to the department responsible, and the resident can track it. [In July 2024 the City of Windhoek launched a mobile app](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-of-windhoek-launches-mobile-app-for-clients-to-manage-accounts/>). Residents use it to report potholes and broken traffic lights.

**Across government.** Ovakalimo [routes each report to the Walvis Bay department responsible](<https://futuremedianews.com.na/2025/11/18/walvis-bay-launches-online-reporting-tool-to-strengthen-local-accountability/>), in one town. [Windhoek's app serves that city alone](<https://www.namibian.com.na/city-of-windhoek-launches-mobile-app-for-clients-to-manage-accounts/>). No source found since October 2023 says a channel reaches most ministries or local governments nationwide.

**Results published.** [The municipality's spokesperson](<https://futuremedianews.com.na/2025/11/18/walvis-bay-launches-online-reporting-tool-to-strengthen-local-accountability/>) said residents can track the status of a report, and gave no number of reports. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
