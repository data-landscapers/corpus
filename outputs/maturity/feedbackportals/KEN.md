# Kenya

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In November 2023 [the Commission on Administrative Justice was developing an app, the PASHA Ombudsman platform, for complaints about public services](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ombudsman-set-to-revamp-complaints-filing-system/>). It works on it with the Ministry of Public Service. [Makueni County launched a project-tracking dashboard in March 2024 with a feedback tool for residents](<https://umanyi.makueni.go.ke/storage/success_stories/68b949613618a_Success_Story.pdf>). It takes their concerns and comments. [In May 2022 the Commission launched the public portal of its complaints system](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/office-of-the-ombudsman-launches-cmis/>). It then held over 6,000 active complaints and had processed over 493 through it.

**Across government.** In May 2022 [the Commission's chair said complainants could lodge complaints against any public entity through its system, from anywhere in the country](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/office-of-the-ombudsman-launches-cmis/>). Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.
