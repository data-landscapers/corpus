# Djibouti

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** ARULoS, Djibouti's agency for urban rehabilitation and social housing, [runs an online complaints platform, E-DOLÉANCES](<https://www.doleances.arulos.dj/>). People with a complaint about the agency's housing file it there online. The platform sends problems with the recovery of payments to the agency's DOCR division and technical problems to its DLS directorate.

**Across government.** E-DOLÉANCES belongs to [one agency, ARULoS, and routes complaints between two of its own units](<https://www.doleances.arulos.dj/>). It covers the services of that agency and of no other body. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel reaching most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The platform's own page](<https://www.doleances.arulos.dj/>) describes how complaints are filed and routed, and gives no number of them. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
