# Ethiopia

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In October 2026 the Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman's home page offered a Submit Complaint service](<http://ethombudsman.gov.et/>). [Citizens can file administrative grievances online there](<http://ethombudsman.gov.et/>). [In January 2026 Ethiopian Investment Holdings launched Adera, an online platform](<https://birrmetrics.com/eih-introduces-online-platform-adera-to-rein-in-corruption-in-the-wake-of-leadership-overhaul/>). [Citizens use it to report complaints and corruption at the state-owned enterprises the holding company manages](<https://birrmetrics.com/eih-introduces-online-platform-adera-to-rein-in-corruption-in-the-wake-of-leadership-overhaul/>). [Mobile apps were planned for later](<https://birrmetrics.com/eih-introduces-online-platform-adera-to-rein-in-corruption-in-the-wake-of-leadership-overhaul/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 the government told the World Bank's GovTech survey it has a platform for feedback on service delivery](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [It named the e-complaint portal ecompliant.gov.et](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>).

**Results published.** No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled. The nearest held fact: [in October 2026 the Ombudsman's service let complainants track the status of a response](<http://ethombudsman.gov.et/>).
