# Cape Verde

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In May 2024 the minister said [the E-participa platform takes citizens' complaints, reports and suggestions on public services](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-tem-dado-passos-consistentes-que-demonstram-o-compromisso-do-governo-e-do-pais-numa-gestao-publica-mais-aberta-transparente-responsavel-e-participativa-ministr/>). She gave no count of cases. Since January 2021 [the Ombudsman has also taken complaints through eparticipa.gov.cv](<http://www.provedordejustica.cv/index.php?Itemid=105&catid=26&id=151%3Aprovedor-de-justica-e-casa-do-cidadao-assinam-acordo-de-nivel-de-servico&option=com_content&view=article>), with a free phone line. In February 2017 the Casa do Cidadão [launched the Participa Cidadão app](<https://www.governo.cv/casa-do-cidadao-lanca-dois-aplicativos-moveis-para-melhorar-o-servico-prestado-pela-administracao-publica/>) for complaints, suggestions and praise.

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches the services of most ministries. The nearest held fact: [E-participa takes complaints on the conduct of public services](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-tem-dado-passos-consistentes-que-demonstram-o-compromisso-do-governo-e-do-pais-numa-gestao-publica-mais-aberta-transparente-responsavel-e-participativa-ministr/>), by the minister's account.

**Results published.** No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled. The nearest held fact: [the Ombudsman's agreement with the Casa do Cidadão set service levels](<http://www.provedordejustica.cv/index.php?Itemid=105&catid=26&id=151%3Aprovedor-de-justica-e-casa-do-cidadao-assinam-acordo-de-nivel-de-servico&option=com_content&view=article>) for the platform.
