# Cote d'Ivoire

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** In November 2026 the Ministry of Digital Transition [sought a firm to digitalise the National Barometer of Public Service Quality](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00480535>), under a project financed by the World Bank. The plan is to collect people's ratings of public services through web forms, tablets used offline, phone surveys, USSD and SMS. The 2024-2025 round of the barometer was run largely by hand. [The Miliê citizen portal, launched in February 2019](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/652356/modernisation-de-ladministration-le-premier-ministre-ivoirien-amadou-gon-coulibaly-prone-un-service-public-de-qualite-au-benefice-des-populations>), was built to collect and handle users' concerns about the administration. [A government release in January 2022 still named it](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/702934/loutil-numerique-un-levier-indispensable-a-la-modernisation-et-a-lefficacite-de-ladministration-publique>).

**Across government.** The barometer is [a national survey of the quality of public services](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00480535>), run by the observatory of public service. Nothing found since October 2023 shows a channel that takes complaints about most ministries or routes them across government.

**Results published.** [The tender notice](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00480535>) gives no count of responses to the 2024-2025 survey. In 2022 the government said [the public service observatory had recorded over 30,000 user concerns from 2017 to 2021](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/702934/loutil-numerique-un-levier-indispensable-a-la-modernisation-et-a-lefficacite-de-ladministration-publique>), without tying that count to the portal. No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled.
