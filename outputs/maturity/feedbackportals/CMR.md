# Cameroon

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In December 2024 the Public Independent Conciliator for the North-West and South-West trained its staff on an online complaints platform](<https://opicswr.cm/opic-to-launch-digital-platform-to-manage-complaints/>). The office was about to launch the platform, built on SharePoint. [In June 2022 the public service ministry said it handled users' concerns by phone and WhatsApp on the green number 1522](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-le-minfopra-annonce-la-mise-en-place-prochaine-dune-plateforme-web-centralisant-lensemble-des-services-publics/>).

**Across government.** The platform belongs to [the Conciliator's office for two regions](<https://opicswr.cm/opic-to-launch-digital-platform-to-manage-complaints/>), the North-West and the South-West. No source found since October 2023 says a channel takes complaints about most ministries or local governments.

**Results published.** [The Conciliator's announcement of December 2024](<https://opicswr.cm/opic-to-launch-digital-platform-to-manage-complaints/>) gives no number of complaints received or handled. The platform had not yet been launched when it was published. No resolution rates, response times or case reports have been found.
