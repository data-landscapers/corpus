# Nigeria

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [SERVICOM Presidency, with the World Bank's ESF Unit, launched the Nigeria Electronic Grievance Management platform in Abuja to receive and manage complaints and feedback on World Bank-supported projects and government institutions](<https://servicom.gov.ng/servicom-presidency-office-launches-the-nigeria-egm-electronic-grievance-management-platform/>). [SERVICOM Presidency's National Coordinator announced that its Citizens E-Feedback portal, built into the SERVICOM website for complaints and commendations about MDA services, is now operational](<https://fmino.gov.ng/servicom-launches-an-e-citizens-feedback-portal-to-enhance-citizens-focused-service-delivery-across-mdas/>). In Edo State, a [Citizens' Feedback System](<https://datasciencenigeria.org/edo-data-for-governance-project/>) built on a DHIS2 tracker lets residents report service gaps in schools, clinics and roads, and had handled over 12,000 reports by June 2025.

**Across government.** [SERVICOM's acting National Coordinator says its Ministerial and Parastatal SERVICOM Units across MDAs let complaints be received, escalated, tracked and resolved across all tiers of government through the platform](<https://servicom.gov.ng/servicom-presidency-office-launches-the-nigeria-egm-electronic-grievance-management-platform/>).
