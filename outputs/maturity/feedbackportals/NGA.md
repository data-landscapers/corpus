# Nigeria

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In March 2026 SERVICOM Presidency launched the Nigeria Electronic Grievance Management platform](<https://servicom.gov.ng/servicom-presidency-office-launches-the-nigeria-egm-electronic-grievance-management-platform/>) with the World Bank's ESF Unit. It takes complaints about World Bank-supported projects and government institutions. [In March 2025 SERVICOM said its Citizens E-Feedback portal for complaints about ministries and agencies was operational](<https://fmino.gov.ng/servicom-launches-an-e-citizens-feedback-portal-to-enhance-citizens-focused-service-delivery-across-mdas/>). [ReportGov.ng, described as the official public service complaint website, logged complaints against federal agencies in November 2024](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/12/01/nitda-bpp-and-frsc-emerge-worst-agencies-for-customer-complaints-resolution-in-november-2024/>). In Edo State, [a Citizens' Feedback System had handled over 12,000 reports by June 2025](<https://datasciencenigeria.org/edo-data-for-governance-project/>). [In October 2025 the Public Complaints Commission said it would introduce an online portal](<https://www.radionigeriakaduna.gov.ng/2025/10/24/public-complaints-commission-to-launch-online-portal-for-case-reporting/>), with no launch date.

**Across government.** [SERVICOM said in March 2026 that its platform tracks complaints across all tiers of government](<https://servicom.gov.ng/servicom-presidency-office-launches-the-nigeria-egm-electronic-grievance-management-platform/>). Its units in ministries and agencies receive, escalate and resolve them. [ReportGov.ng's monthly report covers many federal agencies](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/12/01/nitda-bpp-and-frsc-emerge-worst-agencies-for-customer-complaints-resolution-in-november-2024/>). Among them are NITDA, the FRSC, the ports authority and Customs. [In August 2024 it was described as taking complaints about any federal agency](<https://nigerianobservernews.com/2024/08/nerc-leads-53-mdas-on-complaint-resolution/>), with 53 ranked.

**Results published.** [ReportGov.ng's report for November 2024 gives the complaints received and resolved for each agency](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/12/01/nitda-bpp-and-frsc-emerge-worst-agencies-for-customer-complaints-resolution-in-november-2024/>). The airports authority resolved four of four, and NITDA none of two. [PEBEC's compliance report for the first half of 2024 ranked 53 agencies and found the electricity regulator fully compliant with the 72-hour deadline for resolving complaints](<https://nigerianobservernews.com/2024/08/nerc-leads-53-mdas-on-complaint-resolution/>).
