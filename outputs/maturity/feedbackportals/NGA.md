[SERVICOM Presidency's National Coordinator announced that its Citizens E-Feedback portal, built into the SERVICOM website for complaints and commendations about MDA services, is now operational](<https://fmino.gov.ng/servicom-launches-an-e-citizens-feedback-portal-to-enhance-citizens-focused-service-delivery-across-mdas/>).
