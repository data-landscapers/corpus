# Burkina Faso

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Commission de Régulation des Dysfonctionnements launched the DenonciationPro online platform, announced 23 February 2026, for citizens to report confidentially dysfunctions in public administration in about five minutes](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/25/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-de-soumission-des-denonciations-par-la-commission-de-regulation-des-dysfonctionnements/>). [Burkina Faso's public-service ministry officially launched on 5 March 2019 a complaints and suggestions system for users of ministries, including the online form at plaintes.gov.bf, with a 10-day response deadline (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2019/03/05/la-plateforme-pour-les-recriminations-des-usagers-des-services-publics/>). [Burkina Faso's 2017-2019 OGP commitment BF0005 plans complaint-recording mechanisms in 5 ministerial departments and an online platform to collect complaints directly; the IRM review lists completion as pending (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/burkina-faso/commitments/BF0005/>).
