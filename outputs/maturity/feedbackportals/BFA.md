# Burkina Faso

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [In February 2026 the Commission de Régulation des Dysfonctionnements launched DenonciationPro](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/25/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-de-soumission-des-denonciations-par-la-commission-de-regulation-des-dysfonctionnements/>), an online platform for reporting dysfunctions in public administration. [In November 2026 the Ministry of Health launched Lafiako](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150463>), a platform for complaints and suggestions about public and private health facilities. It sets a ten-day target for handling them. [In March 2019 the public-service ministry launched a complaints system with an online form](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2019/03/05/la-plateforme-pour-les-recriminations-des-usagers-des-services-publics/>).

**Across government.** No source found since October 2023 says a complaints channel reaches services across government. The nearest fact: [DenonciationPro, launched in February 2026, takes reports on public administration as a whole](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/25/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-de-soumission-des-denonciations-par-la-commission-de-regulation-des-dysfonctionnements/>).

**Results published.** No source found since October 2023 gives resolution rates, response times or a report of the cases handled. The nearest fact: [in February 2026 DenonciationPro let citizens report dysfunctions confidentially in about five minutes](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/25/burkina-faso-lancement-dune-plateforme-de-soumission-des-denonciations-par-la-commission-de-regulation-des-dysfonctionnements/>).
