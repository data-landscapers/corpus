# Botswana

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [Mmegi reported that Parliament launched BW Speaks on 28 July 2022](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/parly-launches-bwspeaks/news>). It is an online platform for citizens to voice issues to MPs and be responded to. [The government told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>) that it has a platform for citizens to give feedback on service delivery, at botswanaspeaks.gov.bw. The survey record gives no launch date or case count.
