# Rwanda

## Citizen feedback channel

**Feedback channel.** [The Ministry of Local Government states that the revised Citizens' Assembly guidelines have assembly reports supported by the electronic software e-Citizen Complaints, which is used to receive and solve citizens' complaints online](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/citizens-assembly-guidelines-revised>). [MINALOC, which runs the contact centre where citizens report issues to local leaders, began designing the Mbaza AI agent for complaints in October 2025, aiming to deploy it in early 2026 with the technology company Proto as partner](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>).
