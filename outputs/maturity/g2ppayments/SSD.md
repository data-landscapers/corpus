# South Sudan

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In September 2024 the Ministry of Finance and Planning and the Bank of South Sudan [asked for civil servants' salaries to be paid cashless](<https://nation.africa/africa/news/south-sudan-tests-cashless-pay-to-civil-servants-4767684>). The finance minister said salaries would be paid through bank accounts. In May 2025 the ministry [told every public employee to open a bank account by 27 May](<https://www.sudanspost.com/civil-servants-ordered-to-open-bank-accounts-amid-cash-crisis/>). In August 2025 it [met in Juba on paying all civil servant salaries through individual bank accounts](<https://radioyei.org/news/business/banking-and-finance/government-races-to-end-cash-salary-payments/>). Officials said the shift was already under way in many institutions. No mandatory date was set.
