# South Sudan

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Ministry of Finance and Planning held a meeting in Juba on implementing a government resolution requiring all civil servant salaries to be paid through individual bank accounts, with senior officials saying the shift is already underway in many institutions but no mandatory date set](<https://radioyei.org/news/business/banking-and-finance/government-races-to-end-cash-salary-payments/>). [The Ministry of Finance and Planning and the Bank of South Sudan issued a circular asking that civil servants' salaries and payments to departmental vote heads be made cashless, and the finance minister said salaries and operating costs of ministries, departments and agencies will be paid through bank accounts](<https://nation.africa/africa/news/south-sudan-tests-cashless-pay-to-civil-servants-4767684>). [In May 2025 the ministry directed every public employee to open a bank account by 27 May 2025 so that salaries could move off cash](<https://www.sudanspost.com/civil-servants-ordered-to-open-bank-accounts-amid-cash-crisis/>).
