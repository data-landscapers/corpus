# Congo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The World Bank says the government-launched Lisungi project introduced digital payments via mobile money for its cash transfers, and that its delivery systems were institutionalized in the National Safety Net Program](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>). [The World Bank appraisal says Lisungi cash transfer beneficiaries are as of now paid in cash at Banque Postale du Congo branches, with no ATM card provided (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/898141592839567766/pdf/Republic-of-Congo-Lisungi-Emergency-COVID-19-Response-Project.pdf>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>).
