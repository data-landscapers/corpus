# Congo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The World Bank says the government-launched Lisungi project introduced digital payments via mobile money for its cash transfers](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>). [It says its delivery systems were institutionalized in the National Safety Net Program](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>). [In June 2020 a World Bank appraisal said Lisungi beneficiaries were paid in cash at Banque Postale du Congo branches](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/898141592839567766/pdf/Republic-of-Congo-Lisungi-Emergency-COVID-19-Response-Project.pdf>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 the World Bank said Lisungi cash transfers were paid by mobile money](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 the World Bank said Lisungi's delivery systems had been built](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>). [They were built into the National Safety Net Program](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/03/27/afw-refugee-women-in-congo-are-planting-the-seeds-for-change-with-cash-transfers>).
