# Malawi

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Social cash transfers are paid by e-payment. In December 2023 UNICEF reported that [10 districts had moved to a harmonised e-payment system through banks and mobile money operators](<https://www.unicef.org/malawi/stories/social-cash-transfers-spur-financial-inclusion>). In Ntcheu 14,378 beneficiaries were paid via FDH Bank, and in Balaka 10,070 by mobile money. [From April 2024 to March 2025, 95,520 Social Cash Transfer and 195,520 public works beneficiaries were paid into e-wallets](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>), the World Bank said. In May 2026 the Ministry of Gender said [TNM Mpamba was paying about K6 billion to over 27,760 beneficiaries in Mangochi](<https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/05/01/gender-ministry-hails-tnm-mpamba-for-transforming-social-cash-transfers-in-mangochi/>).

**Main streams.** The [Social Cash Transfer and public works payments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>) are social transfers, one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** [Malawi's 2025 ID4Africa deck says the National Registration Bureau verifies beneficiaries of social protection payments](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>). It maps digital identities to bank accounts and mobile wallets.
