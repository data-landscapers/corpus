# Malawi

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Ministry of Gender says TNM Mpamba mobile money is disbursing about K6 billion in social cash transfers to over 27,760 beneficiaries in Mangochi District](<https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/05/01/gender-ministry-hails-tnm-mpamba-for-transforming-social-cash-transfers-in-mangochi/>). [During April 2024 to March 2025, 95,520 Social Cash Transfer Programme and 195,520 public works beneficiaries in 10 World Bank-funded districts were enrolled on e-payments and received MWK 4.57 billion and MWK 21.08 billion in e-wallets, per the World Bank aide memoire citing NLGFC](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.malawivoice.com/2026/05/01/gender-ministry-hails-tnm-mpamba-for-transforming-social-cash-transfers-in-mangochi/>).

**Linked to ID.** [Malawi's 2025 ID4Africa deck says the National Registration Bureau provides beneficiary verification for social protection G2P programmes and maps digital identities to bank accounts and mobile wallets](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>). The same deck says the national payment switch, supervised by the Reserve Bank, is working to bring government payments onto it.
