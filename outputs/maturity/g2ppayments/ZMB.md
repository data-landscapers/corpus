# Zambia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In August 2026 the Ministry of Community Development and Social Services [began paying the July-August Social Cash Transfer cycle](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/govt-claims-k1-4-billion-to-be-spent-in-july-august-under-social-cash-transfer-programme/>). It covers over 1.5 million households and more than K1.4 billion, through beneficiaries' payment service providers. Recipients were told to guard their SIM cards, phones and PINs. [Co-Develop says Zambia has rolled out OpenG2P to manage government payments to people](<https://www.codevelop.fund/zambia>). A [December 2024 World Bank procurement notice](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00327850>) says ZamPay, which takes payments to government, is to become a government-to-people platform too.

**Main streams.** The [Social Cash Transfer](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/govt-claims-k1-4-billion-to-be-spent-in-july-august-under-social-cash-transfer-programme/>) is one main stream. The electronic Farmer Input Support Programme [delivers input subsidies in Chongwe District through digital vouchers](<https://agritechinsights.com/index.php/2026/11/08/digital-tools-transform-zambias-farm-inputs-but-gaps-remain/>), a University of Zambia study reported in November 2026. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [the OpenG2P rollout](<https://www.codevelop.fund/zambia>).

***Also on file***. [CIPS](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-says-rmb-payment-system-can-cut-trade-costs-deepen-china-investment/>); [Social Cash Transfer](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/civil-society-condemns-alleged-sms-linking-cash-transfers-to-voting-for-ruling-upnd/>).
