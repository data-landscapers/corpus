# Zambia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [Zambia's Ministry of Community Development and Social Services began disbursing more than K1.4 billion in the July-August 2026 Social Cash Transfer cycle to over 1.5 million households, redeemable through beneficiaries' payment service providers, with recipients told to safeguard SIM cards, phones and PINs](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/govt-claims-k1-4-billion-to-be-spent-in-july-august-under-social-cash-transfer-programme/>). [A December 2024 procurement notice for the World Bank-financed Digital Zambia Acceleration Project says it will support upgrading ZamPay from payments to government into an integrated government-to-people payment platform](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00327850>).
