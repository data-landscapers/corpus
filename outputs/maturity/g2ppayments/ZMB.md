# Zambia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In August 2026 the Ministry of Community Development and Social Services [began paying the July-August Social Cash Transfer cycle](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/govt-claims-k1-4-billion-to-be-spent-in-july-august-under-social-cash-transfer-programme/>). It covers over 1.5 million households and more than K1.4 billion. Payment runs through beneficiaries' payment service providers. Recipients were told to guard their SIM cards, phones and PINs. A [December 2024 World Bank procurement notice](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00327850>) says the Digital Zambia Acceleration Project will upgrade ZamPay. ZamPay takes payments to government, and is to become a government-to-people platform as well.
