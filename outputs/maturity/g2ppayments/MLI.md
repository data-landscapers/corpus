# Mali

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Caisse Malienne de Securite Sociale pays pensions by mobile payment, which the article calls a success, and is now digitalising bank payment of pensions](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [The Caisse malienne de sécurité sociale has paid pensions by mobile since 2019, and its application pays pensions below FCFA 50,000 by mobile; a bank-payment pilot with the Banque de Développement du Mali was judged conclusive in July 2026](<https://www.africascoop.net/index.php/2026/07/30/sante-le-mali-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services-publics/>). [The CMSS pilot of fully digital bank payment of pensions with the Banque de Developpement du Mali was conclusive and is to be rolled out progressively to other partner banks](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>).
