# Mali

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The Caisse malienne de sécurité sociale (CMSS) [has paid pensions by mobile since 2019](<https://www.africascoop.net/index.php/2026/07/30/sante-le-mali-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services-publics/>). Its application pays pensions below FCFA 50,000 by mobile. In July 2026 [a pilot of fully digital bank payment of pensions with the Banque de Développement du Mali was judged conclusive](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). It is to reach other partner banks step by step. Safety-net transfers are paid digitally too. [Recipients of the Jigisèmèjiri safety net told the World Bank they were paid into mobile money accounts](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/cash-vs-digital-how-do-digital-government-person-payments-ease-lives-recipients>), in a blog of March 2024. [The programme had piloted mobile money transfers to almost 3,000 households in Mopti in 2021](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/improving-cash-transfer-process-mobile-technologies-mali>).

**Main streams.** Two main streams are paid digitally: [CMSS pensions](<https://www.africascoop.net/index.php/2026/07/30/sante-le-mali-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services-publics/>) and [Jigisèmèjiri transfers](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/cash-vs-digital-how-do-digital-government-person-payments-ease-lives-recipients>). In November 2023 [the World Bank said Mali was committed to social payments through an account](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/digital-financial-services-can-bring-inclusion-and-growth-mali>). No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [the CMSS mobile pension payments](<https://www.africascoop.net/index.php/2026/07/30/sante-le-mali-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services-publics/>), reported in July 2026.
