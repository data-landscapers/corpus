# Chad

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In February 2026 the finance ministry [signed partnership conventions with Moov Money, Airtel Money, Konoom and Cashi](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>), all mobile money operators. [The five-year framework lets the state pay salaries, bonuses or pensions by mobile money](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/tchad-letat-sallie-aux-operateurs-mobile-money-pour-encaisser-les-impots-et-payer-les-salaires_59931>). The minister said every civil servant could take salary free by bank or wallet. In January 2025 [the Cellule Filets sociaux said safety-net transfers go through microfinance institutions and mobile operators](<https://ndjamenahebdo.net/le-transfert-monetaire-aux-menages-vulnerables/>). In Bahr El Gazal, Express Union pays them in cash. No source found since October 2023 says a payment reached a wallet or account.

**Main streams.** The [February 2026 conventions](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/tchad-letat-sallie-aux-operateurs-mobile-money-pour-encaisser-les-impots-et-payer-les-salaires_59931>) cover salaries and pensions, and the [safety-net contracts](<https://ndjamenahebdo.net/le-transfert-monetaire-aux-menages-vulnerables/>) social transfers. No source found since October 2023 shows any of them paid digitally in service.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [finance ministry's conventions with mobile money operators](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>), signed in February 2026.
