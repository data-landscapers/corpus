# Chad

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In February 2026 the finance ministry [signed partnership conventions with Moov Money, Airtel Money, Konoom and Cashi](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>), Tchadinfos reported. All four are mobile money operators. The minister said the conventions cover settling the salaries of all civil servants. No source found since October 2023 says salaries are now paid through these operators.

**Main streams.** The [February 2026 conventions](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>) concern civil servants' salaries, which is one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [finance ministry's conventions with mobile money operators](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/02/18/digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-le-ministere-des-finances-signe-des-partenariats-avec-des-operateurs-de-mobile-money/>), signed in February 2026.
