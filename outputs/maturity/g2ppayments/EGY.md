# Egypt

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Government salaries are paid electronically. [Finance Minister Mohamed Maait said e-payment of state payrolls grew 24% to EGP 299.4 billion](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>) in July 2023 to May 2024. [The cabinet credited the figure to the finance ministry's e-payment and collection centre](<https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/76152>). The Ministry of Social Solidarity pays Takaful and Karama cash support digitally. [From 15 August 2024 it paid 5.2 million families](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/530140/Egypt/Society/,-new-families-added-to-Takaful-amp;-Karama-cash-s.aspx>), some through Meeza bank cards at ATMs. In May 2026 [it paid the support monthly into a mobile wallet on request](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8292004>), beside Meeza cards and the post.

**Main streams.** Two main streams are paid digitally: [state salaries](<https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/76152>) and [Takaful and Karama transfers](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8292004>). In June 2022 [NOSI paid 10.7 million pensioners, 5.5 million of them on Meeza cards](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/50/1202/467201/AlAhram-Weekly/Economy/Egypt%E2%80%99s-pensioners-enter-the-digital-age.aspx>). No source found since October 2023 shows pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [Takaful and Karama's wallet payment](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8292004>), from May 2026.
