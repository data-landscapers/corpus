# Egypt

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [Finance Minister Mohamed Maait says the e-payment service for government employees' payrolls grew 24% to EGP 299.4 billion in July 2023 to May 2024](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>). [Al-Ahram reports NOSI pays about LE22 billion a month to 10.7 million pensioners, with 5.5 million already using new Meeza cards and pensions transferable to bank accounts or mobile e-wallets (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/50/1202/467201/AlAhram-Weekly/Economy/Egypt%E2%80%99s-pensioners-enter-the-digital-age.aspx>). [The cabinet credited the payroll figure to the Ministry of Finance's government e-payment and collection centre](<https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/76152>).

**Main streams.** No source held since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).
