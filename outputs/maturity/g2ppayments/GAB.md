# Gabon

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [On 19 December 2025 the CNSS began paying 3,010 maternity and old-age single-entitlement benefits](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2025/12/19/paiement-des-prestations-cnss-3-010-droits-uniques-en-cours-de-reglement-a-travers-le-pays/>). [All were paid without cash, by bank transfer or Mobile Money](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2025/12/19/paiement-des-prestations-cnss-3-010-droits-uniques-en-cours-de-reglement-a-travers-le-pays/>). [In late October 2025 the government began a pilot to digitise social and tax payments, Ecofin Agency reported](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1112-51310-gabon-speeds-up-digital-payments-rollout-with-madigipaie-program>). [Full deployment was scheduled for January 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1112-51310-gabon-speeds-up-digital-payments-rollout-with-madigipaie-program>).
