# Sao Tome and Principe

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The Programa Família Vulnerável is paid by bank transfer. [In 2025 the DPSSF paid 5,000 households in five cycles through the Ké Non payments module](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>), over STN 35.75 million by October. [The World Bank's September 2025 project paper continues this electronic payment system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>), built with a financial institution. [openIMIS said in February 2024 that Ké Non's payments module works with Banco Internacional de São Tomé e Príncipe](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>). The government adopted Ké Non in May 2023.

**Main streams.** The [family programme](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>) is a social transfer, one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** [Ké Non holds the Unique Social Registry and the payments module, and identifies clients against the national civil registry](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>), openIMIS said in February 2024. The civil registry is the Ministry of Justice's.

***Also on file***. [Unique Social Registry / Ke-Non MIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.
