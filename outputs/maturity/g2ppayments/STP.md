# Sao Tome and Principe

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The DPSSF states that Programa Família Vulnerável transfers to 5,000 households were paid by bank transfer through the payments module of the Ké Non system, with five payment cycles completed in 2025 through October totalling over STN 35.75 million](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>). [The World Bank project paper says the additional financing continues an electronic cash-transfer payment system, built through a partnership with a financial institution, for the Vulnerable Family Program (5,000 to 6,000 households)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).
