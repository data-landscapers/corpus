# Ethiopia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In July 2026 the finance ministry, the central bank and EthSwitch [convened government technical teams on interoperable government payments](<https://www.findevgateway.org/news/ethiopia-advances-nationwide-rollout-of-interoperable-digital-government-payments>), FinDev Gateway reported. Their framework covers payments from people to government and from government to people. In March 2024 [the World Bank said it was supporting a government pilot of safety-net payments through Telebirr](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>) in two regions. In May 2026 [the World Bank said its G2Px programme had helped 1.3 million Ethiopian households move to digital payments](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/06/global-digital-public-infrastructure-program>). It named no programme. In December 2022, before the three-year window, [almost 1.5 million safety-net households were paid through bank accounts](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>).

**Main streams.** The [rural and urban safety nets](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>) are social transfers. No source found since October 2023 shows them, civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally in service.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [July 2026 work on interoperable government payments](<https://www.findevgateway.org/news/ethiopia-advances-nationwide-rollout-of-interoperable-digital-government-payments>).
