# Burundi

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The World Bank says Merankabandi, a government cash transfer programme, is nationwide since 2022, has reached over 1.5 million people, and is backed by investments in digital payments](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>). [SOS Médias Burundi reports that Merankabandi, a joint programme of the Government of Burundi, the World Bank and WFP, paid selected refugees in Ruyigi (Buhumuza) 73,600 BIF every two months to their mobile phones for two years, to nearly 8,000 households in five camps, ending in September 2025](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2025/09/22/buhumuza-clap-de-fin-pour-merankabandi-deux-annees-de-transferts-monetaires-qui-ont-transforme-la-vie-des-refugies/>).
