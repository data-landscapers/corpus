**Paid digitally.** In May 2026 the World Bank [said Merankabandi, a government cash transfer programme, has been nationwide since 2022 and has reached over 1.5 million people](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>). It said the programme is backed by investments in digital payments. Merankabandi, run by the government with the World Bank and WFP, [paid selected refugees in Ruyigi 73,600 BIF every two months to their mobile phones](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2025/09/22/buhumuza-clap-de-fin-pour-merankabandi-deux-annees-de-transferts-monetaires-qui-ont-transforme-la-vie-des-refugies/>). Nearly 8,000 households in five camps were paid this way for two years, until September 2025.
