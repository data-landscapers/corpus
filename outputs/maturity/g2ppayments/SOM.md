# Somalia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The central bank's instant payment system carries government payments to people. In July 2026 [the Central Bank said SIPS supports government disbursements to citizens](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>). [SONNA reported that SIPS currently carries them](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>). [The Central Bank's payment system page says its ACH supports bulk salary processing](<https://centralbank.gov.so/nps/>) for the public workforce. The Baxnaano programme [planned in 2025 to pay 50,000 households USD 240 by mobile money](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). USD 60 a quarter follows in 2026 and 2027, to the phone number registered at enrolment.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 names the stream SIPS carries or shows salaries paid through the ACH. [Baxnaano's payments](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>) are a social transfer, stated as planned.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [SIPS disbursements to citizens](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>), from July 2026.
