# Ghana

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** LEAP, the government's cash grant, is paid digitally. [Its 100th payment cycle in February 2026 reached all 216 district assemblies and over 350,000 households](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>). In March 2025 [the minister announced cycles 94 and 95, GHS 265.22 million paid through GhIPSS to E-Zwich accounts](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/ministers-press-briefing-on-the-94th-and-95th-leap-disbursement/>). In September 2026 [LEAP's page said grants are paid every two months on e-zwich](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/eligibility.php>). In October 2025 [the ministry said it was adding the Ghana Pay mobile money system with GhIPSS](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/government-through-mogcsp-commences-98th-cycle-of-leap-payments/>). LEAP [first moved to electronic payment in 2013](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>).

**Main streams.** Beside LEAP, [government employees' salaries are paid from the payroll into bank accounts](<https://osp.gov.gh/media/Resources/Government_Payroll_Administration_-_Phase_I_Vol_1.pdf>). The Office of the Special Prosecutor and the Controller and Accountant-General's Department report it. Banks were told to withhold irregular salaries from January 2024.

**Linked to ID.** [In April 2026 LEAP used the Ghana National Household Registry to find 400,000 newly eligible households](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>). [Its grants are paid with biometric fingerprint verification](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/eligibility.php>) through GhIPSS and PayCode.
