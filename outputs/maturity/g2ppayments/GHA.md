# Ghana

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [LEAP says its 100th payment cycle in February 2026 reached all 216 MMDAs and over 350,000 households](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>). [The Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection says LEAP cash-grant disbursement, a government social transfer paying about 350,580 households in its 99th cycle from 22 December 2025, increasingly runs on mobile-money-enabled G2P rails](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/government-begins-99th-cycle-of-leap-cash-grant-payments>). [The Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection said on 9 October 2025 it has a partnership with GhIPSS to implement the Ghana Pay mobile money payment system for LEAP, giving beneficiaries multiple cash-out options](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/government-through-mogcsp-commences-98th-cycle-of-leap-payments/>).

**Main streams.** Beside the LEAP transfers, [the Office of the Special Prosecutor and the Controller and Accountant-General's Department report that government employees' salaries are paid from the payroll into their bank accounts, and that banks were told to withhold the salaries of those found on the payroll irregularly from January 2024](<https://osp.gov.gh/media/Resources/Government_Payroll_Administration_-_Phase_I_Vol_1.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** [LEAP says that in April 2026 it used the Ghana National Household Registry to identify 400,000 newly eligible households for enrolment](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ghana National Household Registry](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>); [GhIPSS](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>).
