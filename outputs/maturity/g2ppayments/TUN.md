# Tunisia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The World Bank says 44.4% of the cash transfers under Tunisia's AMEN Program have been digitalized](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). It says a convention between the Ministry of Social Affairs and the Tunisian Post continues digital payment cards tied to an e-wallet for AMEN beneficiaries. [La Poste Tunisienne provides students with e-Dinar wallets and electronic payment cards for university financial operations](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/09/numerisation-la-poste-tunisienne-valide-plus-de-18-million-dinscriptions-scolaires-et-universitaires-en-ligne/>). These include the payment of university scholarships and loans. [In November 2026 Tunisia Post held about 970,000 accounts for poor families and retirees, and 400,000 e-wallets were active](<https://en.africanmanager.com/minister-hemissi-presents-his-record-and-it-was-anything-but-super/>).

**Main streams.** Two main streams are paid into accounts. [The World Bank said in April 2024 that 44.4% of AMEN cash transfers had been digitalised](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [About 65% of pensions are withdrawn by ATM from Tunisia Post accounts](<https://en.africanmanager.com/minister-hemissi-presents-his-record-and-it-was-anything-but-super/>), per a November 2026 report.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest fact: [in April 2024 AMEN beneficiaries held payment cards tied to an e-wallet from the Tunisian Post](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>).
