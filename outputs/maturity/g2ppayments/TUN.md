# Tunisia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The World Bank says 44.4% of the cash transfers under Tunisia's AMEN Program have been digitalized, and a convention between the Ministry of Social Affairs and the Tunisian Post continues digital payment cards tied to an e-wallet for AMEN beneficiaries](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [La Poste Tunisienne provides students with e-Dinar wallets and electronic payment cards for university financial operations including the payment of university scholarships and loans](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/09/numerisation-la-poste-tunisienne-valide-plus-de-18-million-dinscriptions-scolaires-et-universitaires-en-ligne/>).
