# Mauritius

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority pays the Rs 20,000 Independence Allowance to eligible youths](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/financial-assistance/independence-allowance>). The money goes into the bank account the youth gives in a one-time electronic application. The MRA, the tax authority, runs the payment.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally. The nearest held fact is [the Independence Allowance paid by bank transfer](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/financial-assistance/independence-allowance>). That allowance is a social transfer to young people, one stream of the three the question asks about.

**Linked to ID.** [Only youths registered on the Social Register of Mauritius are eligible for the allowance](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/financial-assistance/independence-allowance>). The application must give the youth's National Identity Card number. The payment is thus tied to both the social register and the national ID.
