# Liberia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The speaker says civil servants' salaries were pushed to wallets through the instant payment system, with government payments as its first use case](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>). [The Government of Liberia's REALISE Social Cash Transfer programme pays extremely poor families quarterly by MTN mobile money, with all transfers delivered digitally (REALISE Project, Government of Liberia)](<https://realise.liberiasp.gov.lr/2026/05/18/government-of-liberia-presents-crisis-response-plan-to-world-bank/>). [FrontPage Africa says the IIPS pilot phase currently supports government-to-people transactions](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/business/economy/liberia-cbl-launches-inclusive-instant-payment-system-ends-era-of-mobile-money-silos/>). [Governor Saamoi says the IIPS pilot phase offers person-to-person and government-to-people real-time transactions, and that salaries, pensions and social transfers will be delivered through it](<https://cbl.org.lr/index.php/media/features-articles/special-remark-executive-governor-henry-f-saamoi-launch-iips-december-16>).

**Main streams.** [The directive names salaries, pensions, social benefits and other transfers as the government payments by mobile money routed through NEPS/IIPS](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** [The Household Social Registry supplies the families enrolled in the Social Cash Transfer](<https://realise.liberiasp.gov.lr/2026/05/18/government-of-liberia-presents-crisis-response-plan-to-world-bank/>). It holds over 442,000 households, ranked by poverty. That is enrolment, not a check of the payment. No source refers to the national ID.
