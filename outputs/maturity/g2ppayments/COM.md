# Comoros

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The PFSS-RRC says the World Bank-backed Mayendeleyo social cash transfer phase launched on 20 September 2026](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-%C3%A9largit-la-protection-sociale-%C3%A0-55-000-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables>). It pays 55,000 households through the mobile-banking operators Mvula and Huri Money. [Africa24 TV says the Msaanda social cash transfers under the World Bank-financed PFSS-RRC (2023-2027) pay each beneficiary up to 75,000 KMF via mobile banking](<https://africa24tv.com/comores-financement-des-filets-sociaux-plus-de-40-000-menages-vulnerables-beneficiaires/>). They reach 40,010 households. [The World Bank/Government mission aide-memoire on the PFSS-RRC social safety net project credits electronic payments](<https://pfss-comores.org/ressources/download/9>). It says their use, with digitalised operations and a digitised monitoring system, contributed to the success of the project's interventions.
