## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Fonds d'Intervention pour le Développement, under the World Bank-financed Projet Filets de Sécurité et de Résilience, opened a consultation on 30 July 2025 (bids due 19 August 2025) for payment services for the FSP cash transfers by QR code on the PGP platform, in two lots covering six communes of Benenitra district](<https://www.fid.mg/services-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-du-programme-fsp-par-qr-code-sur-la-plateforme-pgp-n03-dc-fid-dru-fsr-25-19-08-2025/>). [The FID technical sheet says the government's FIAVOTA cash transfer pays bimonthly transfers of 40,000 to 60,000 Ar to 64,987 FSS and 3,537 RPI households through the payment management platform using QR codes and mobile money (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.fid.mg/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/FICHE_TECHNIQUE_FIAVOTA_VF_Sep_2022.pdf>).
