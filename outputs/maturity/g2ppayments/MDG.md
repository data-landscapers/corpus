# Madagascar

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 21 June 2024 the Fonds d'Intervention pour le Développement [opened a national tender for payment of cash transfers by mobile money](<https://www.fid.mg/service-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-par-le-mobile-money-aaon-05-2024-aon-fid-dirgen-pfsr-23-07-2024/>), in 67 lots of communes. On 30 July 2025 [it sought payment services for FSP transfers by QR code](<https://www.fid.mg/services-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-du-programme-fsp-par-qr-code-sur-la-plateforme-pgp-n03-dc-fid-dru-fsr-25-19-08-2025/>) on the PGP platform. Both serve the World Bank-financed Projet Filets de Sécurité et de Résilience. No source found since October 2023 says payments under them have been made. In September 2022 [the FIAVOTA cash transfer paid 64,987 FSS and 3,537 RPI households by QR code and mobile money](<https://www.fid.mg/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/FICHE_TECHNIQUE_FIAVOTA_VF_Sep_2022.pdf>).

**Main streams.** The [FSP tenders](<https://www.fid.mg/service-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-par-le-mobile-money-aaon-05-2024-aon-fid-dirgen-pfsr-23-07-2024/>) concern social transfers only. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [the FID's July 2025 call for QR-code payment services](<https://www.fid.mg/services-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-du-programme-fsp-par-qr-code-sur-la-plateforme-pgp-n03-dc-fid-dru-fsr-25-19-08-2025/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government transfer, salary or pension is paid digitally in service.
