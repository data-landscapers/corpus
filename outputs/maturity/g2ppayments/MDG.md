# Madagascar

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 30 July 2025 the Fonds d'Intervention pour le Développement [sought payment services for FSP cash transfers by QR code](<https://www.fid.mg/services-pour-le-paiement-des-transferts-monetaires-du-programme-fsp-par-qr-code-sur-la-plateforme-pgp-n03-dc-fid-dru-fsr-25-19-08-2025/>). Payment runs on the PGP platform. The FSP is the World Bank-financed Projet Filets de Sécurité et de Résilience. Bids were due on 19 August 2025, in two lots covering six communes of Benenitra district. In 2022 [the FIAVOTA cash transfer paid 64,987 FSS and 3,537 RPI households by QR code and mobile money](<https://www.fid.mg/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/FICHE_TECHNIQUE_FIAVOTA_VF_Sep_2022.pdf>), the FID said. That was before the three-year window.
