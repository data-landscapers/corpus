# Cote d'Ivoire

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The BCEAO says the Treasury of Cote d'Ivoire has met all PI-SPI requirements and regularly makes bulk payments through the platform](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>). The platform lets Treasuries send salaries, grants and subsidies to any account. [APIF's 2024 report says the government's Programme Filets Sociaux Productifs pays quarterly allowances to rural households by mobile phone](<https://apif.finances.gouv.ci/images/app/publications/364/rapport-if-2024com.pdf>). The payments go into e-money accounts. There were 230,000 active beneficiaries, and 130,000 new mobile money accounts were opened in 2024.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 the BCEAO said the Treasury regularly makes bulk payments through PI-SPI](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>).
