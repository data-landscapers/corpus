# Cote d'Ivoire

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The BCEAO says the Treasury of Cote d'Ivoire, having met all PI-SPI requirements, regularly makes bulk payments through the platform, which lets Treasuries send salaries, grants and subsidies to any account](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>). [APIF's 2024 report says the government's Programme Filets Sociaux Productifs pays quarterly allowances to rural households by mobile phone into e-money accounts, with 230,000 active beneficiaries and 130,000 new mobile money accounts opened in 2024](<https://apif.finances.gouv.ci/images/app/publications/364/rapport-if-2024com.pdf>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry.
