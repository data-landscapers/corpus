# Algeria

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 26 June 2024 the social insurance fund CNAS [signed a convention with Algérie Poste to digitise benefit transfers](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/06/26/signature-dune-convention-sur-la-numerisation-des-virements-entre-la-cnas-et-algerie-poste/>). The benefits go to insured people's CCP postal accounts. The two are to set up remote processing of the transfers on a secure electronic platform. No source found since October 2023 says a payment under it has been made. In April 2023 the labour minister said [1.9 million jobseekers get a monthly allowance paid into CCP accounts](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/versement-sur-les-comptes-ccp-le-ministre-devoile-un-nouveau-calendrier-pour-lallocation-chomage/>).

**Main streams.** The [CNAS convention](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/06/26/signature-dune-convention-sur-la-numerisation-des-virements-entre-la-cnas-et-algerie-poste/>) concerns social-insurance benefits only. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [June 2024 convention for an electronic data exchange between CNAS and Algérie Poste](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/06/26/signature-dune-convention-sur-la-numerisation-des-virements-entre-la-cnas-et-algerie-poste/>).

***Also on file***. [Minha](<https://lechodalgerie.dz/140-000-demandes-en-traitement-via-minha-lanem-accelere-les-procedures/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government transfer, salary or pension is paid digitally in service.
