# Sierra Leone

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** NaCSA pays social safety net transfers by mobile money. In September 2024 [the labour ministry and NaCSA began paying NLe1,018 to 13,091 people aged 60 and over by Orange Money](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>), in 10 districts. On 15 April 2025 [NaCSA signed with Afrimoney to pay over 11,600 elderly beneficiaries](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/afrimoney-partners-nacsa-cash-transfers-elderly/>). In May 2026 [officials said retired citizens can receive NaCSA support in mobile money wallets](<https://sierraloaded.sl/business/afrimoney-prepaid-visa-card-launched-sierra-leone/>). In July 2026 [Economic Inclusion Grants were paid through Afrimoney in Falaba District](<https://www.nacsa.gov.sl/post/economic-inclusion-grants-empower-falaba-sle-3-7-million-worth-of-businesses-created-and-recalibrat>), under the PSSNYE project.

**Main streams.** These [NaCSA payments](<https://www.nacsa.gov.sl/post/economic-inclusion-grants-empower-falaba-sle-3-7-million-worth-of-businesses-created-and-recalibrat>) are social transfers, one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [NaCSA's July 2026 grants paid through Afrimoney](<https://www.nacsa.gov.sl/post/economic-inclusion-grants-empower-falaba-sle-3-7-million-worth-of-businesses-created-and-recalibrat>).
