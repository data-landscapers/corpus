# Senegal

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 2 June 2025 the Treasury directorate (DGCPT) [launched SenTrésor, a digital platform to pay state expenditure](<https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/>). It pays instantly. On 1 October 2026 [the family minister relaunched payment of the bourses de sécurité familiale by mobile money](<https://aps.sn/bourses-de-securite-familiale-le-paiement-des-allocations-officiellement-relance-apres-deux-ans/>), after two years' interruption. Each household receives CFA 140,000 for 2026. In September [the ministry said 469,497 families would be paid through La Poste and mobile money operators](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/La-Poste-et-mobile-money-comment-seront-versees-les-bourses-de-securite-familiale_a46531.html>). [The Caisse de Sécurité Sociale announced that social benefits will be paid into Wave accounts](<https://aps.sn/la-css-annonce-le-paiement-des-prestations-sociales-par-un-operateur-de-monnaie-electronique/>), with no start date.

**Main streams.** [The DGCPT says SenTrésor pays salaries and allowances, pensions, scholarships and social aid](<https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/>). It names village chiefs, students and salaried staff as beneficiaries. An economist says [the state uses Orange Money to distribute some public credit programmes](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [SenTrésor's June 2025 launch](<https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/>).
