# Senegal

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The state has chosen Orange Money as the distribution channel for certain public credit programmes, directing aid flows through it, according to economist Seydou Bocoum](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>). [Senegal's Family, Social Action and Solidarity minister launched on 1 October 2026 payment of the bourses de securite familiale after two years' interruption, CFA140,000 per beneficiary household for 2026's four quarters, paid via mobile money operators (APS)](<https://aps.sn/bourses-de-securite-familiale-le-paiement-des-allocations-officiellement-relance-apres-deux-ans/>). [The Caisse de Sécurité Sociale announced on 2 September 2026 that social benefits will be paid into beneficiaries' Wave accounts, with enrolment by form and no start date given](<https://aps.sn/la-css-annonce-le-paiement-des-prestations-sociales-par-un-operateur-de-monnaie-electronique/>).

**Main streams.** [The DGCPT says SenTrésor pays salaries and allowances, pensions, scholarships and social aid, and names village chiefs, students and salaried staff as beneficiaries](<https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sentresor.org/sentresor-la-plateforme-de-paiements-digitaux-lancee/>).
