# Burkina Faso

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In January 2024 [the CNSS said it had moved many of its benefits from counter payment to bank transfer](<https://cnssbf.org/?p=8268>). It had [signed with Orange Money in May 2023](<https://cnssbf.org/?p=8268>) to pay pensions, family allowances and annuities to phones, step by step. In August 2024 [CARFO, the civil servants' pension fund, announced mobile money payment of pensions](<https://burkina24.com/2024/08/21/communique-carfo-a-partir-du-4eme-trimestre-2024-paiement-par-mobile-money-des-arrerages-de-pension-ou-de-rente-6/>) from the last quarter of 2024. Bank transfer remained the alternative. Since 2018 [the Projet Filets Sociaux has paid its transfers to mobile money accounts](<https://www.findevgateway.org/fr/blog/2023/12/linclusion-financiere-les-paiements-sociaux-numeriques-un-objectif-atteignable>), FinDev Gateway said.

**Main streams.** The [CNSS benefits](<https://cnssbf.org/?p=8268>) and [CARFO's pensions](<https://burkina24.com/2024/08/21/communique-carfo-a-partir-du-4eme-trimestre-2024-paiement-par-mobile-money-des-arrerages-de-pension-ou-de-rente-6/>) are social security payments, which make one stream. No source found since October 2023 shows CARFO's mobile payments made, or civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [the CNSS move to bank transfer](<https://cnssbf.org/?p=8268>), reported in January 2024.
