# Guinea

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The CNSS, the public social security fund, pays pensions digitally. [It launched payment of retirement pensions by bank transfer or mobile money on 5 December 2023](<https://guineenews.org/2023/12/05/reformes-et-innovations-a-la-cnss-vers-la-la-dematerialisation-dans-le-paiement-des-pensions-de-retraite/>), through Orange Money and MTN Mobile Money. An awareness campaign for pensioners ran to 14 December. [From July 2025 it ended cash payment of retirement, survivor and invalidity pensions](<https://cnss.gov.gn/digitalisation-des-pensions-vers-le-0-especes-dans-le-paiement-des-retraites/>). Bank transfer, mobile wallets and rechargeable cards replaced it. [The January 2026 pensions were paid from 23 December 2025 by bank transfer and mobile telephony](<https://cnss.gov.gn/fin-dannee-la-cnss-anticipe-le-versement-des-pensions-de-retraite/>).

**Main streams.** [CNSS pensions](<https://cnss.gov.gn/fin-dannee-la-cnss-anticipe-le-versement-des-pensions-de-retraite/>) are one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [CNSS move to cash-free pension payment](<https://cnss.gov.gn/digitalisation-des-pensions-vers-le-0-especes-dans-le-paiement-des-retraites/>), from July 2025.
