# Angola

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [In August 2023 FAS director-general Jelembi said Kwenda payments over three years went 77% in cash and 23% by electronic card](<https://angop.ao/es/noticias/economia/programa-kwenda-entrega-mais-de-akz-44-mil-milhoes-as-familias/>). [Mobile money carried 0.4%](<https://angop.ao/es/noticias/economia/programa-kwenda-entrega-mais-de-akz-44-mil-milhoes-as-familias/>). [From 10 January 2025, FAS said, Kwenda cash transfers were paid into the personal bank accounts of 91 households in Cabinda](<https://fasangola.com/pt/2025/01/11/kwenda-avanca-com-transferencia-monetaria-as-contas-pessoais-dos-beneficiarios-em-cabinda/>). [The households collect the money at ATMs and partner bank branches](<https://fasangola.com/pt/2025/01/11/kwenda-avanca-com-transferencia-monetaria-as-contas-pessoais-dos-beneficiarios-em-cabinda/>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [from January 2025 one programme, Kwenda, paid some households into bank accounts](<https://fasangola.com/pt/2025/01/11/kwenda-avanca-com-transferencia-monetaria-as-contas-pessoais-dos-beneficiarios-em-cabinda/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [in January 2025 Kwenda transfers went into beneficiaries' personal bank accounts](<https://fasangola.com/pt/2025/01/11/kwenda-avanca-com-transferencia-monetaria-as-contas-pessoais-dos-beneficiarios-em-cabinda/>).
