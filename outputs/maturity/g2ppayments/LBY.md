# Libya

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [In August 2025 the Government of National Unity began paying public sector salaries through the unified treasury account, straight to bank accounts](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-starts-paying-public-sector-wages-through-new-treasury-system>). [The central bank's instant-salary system, Ratibak Lahzi, cut salary payment time to under an hour](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>). [About 1.75 million of 2.2 million public employees had registered for it by September 2026](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>). [That month salary orders from the Ministry of Finance went through it to commercial banks for immediate processing](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-the-commencement-of-september-salary-payments-through-the-your-salary-instantly-system/>).

**Main streams.** [Public sector salaries are paid through the central bank's instant-salary system](<https://cbl.gov.ly/en/the-central-bank-of-libya-announces-the-commencement-of-september-salary-payments-through-the-your-salary-instantly-system/>). [In June 2026 the chairman of the General Authority for Information and Communication said the children's allowance was paid electronically within a short period](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358533&lang=en>). [He said integrated government databases made that possible](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358533&lang=en>).
