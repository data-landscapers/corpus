# Togo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Retirement payments are moving off cash. [CGAR paid its first retirement allowances in July 2026 by bank transfer and mobile money](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>). Beneficiaries had until 31 August to give payment details. [The Caisse de Retraites du Togo said it would end cash pension payments at its counters from July 2026](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/social/1606-11993-pensions-de-retraite-la-crt-met-fin-aux-paiements-en-especes>). It pays by Mixx by Yas, Flooz or bank transfer instead. In April 2020 [the Novissi programme paid over 600,000 informal workers monthly by mobile money](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [The government targeted those transfers with AI](<https://news.berkeley.edu/2025/07/25/togo-and-uc-berkeley-a-data-science-alliance-to-build-a-dynamic-west-african-nation/>), Berkeley News said in 2025.

**Main streams.** Two streams are shown. [CGAR's allowances](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>) and [the CRT's pensions](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/social/1606-11993-pensions-de-retraite-la-crt-met-fin-aux-paiements-en-especes>) are pensions. [The World Bank said in March 2026 that the Novissi platform serves ANADEB's cash transfer programmes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032526174532318/pdf/P169594-5fd6786a-2b45-425b-95b6-0e79d792a6fb.pdf>). No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** [The World Bank says the e-ID is used to authenticate beneficiaries of ANADEB's cash transfers on Novissi](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032526174532318/pdf/P169594-5fd6786a-2b45-425b-95b6-0e79d792a6fb.pdf>). It says Togo's social payments platform uses the foundational ID.
