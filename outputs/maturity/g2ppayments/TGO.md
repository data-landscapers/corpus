# Togo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Retirement payments are moving off cash. [CGAR paid its first retirement allowances in July 2026 by bank transfer and mobile money](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>). Beneficiaries had until 31 August to give payment details. [The Caisse de Retraites du Togo said it would end cash pension payments at its counters from July 2026](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/social/1606-11993-pensions-de-retraite-la-crt-met-fin-aux-paiements-en-especes>). It pays by Mixx by Yas, Flooz or bank transfer instead. In 2020, before the three-year window, [the Novissi programme paid over 600,000 informal workers monthly by mobile money](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [The government targeted those transfers with AI](<https://news.berkeley.edu/2025/07/25/togo-and-uc-berkeley-a-data-science-alliance-to-build-a-dynamic-west-african-nation/>), Berkeley News said in 2025.

**Main streams.** [CGAR's allowances](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>) and [the CRT's pensions](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/social/1606-11993-pensions-de-retraite-la-crt-met-fin-aux-paiements-en-especes>) are pensions, one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [CGAR's July 2026 payments](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>).
