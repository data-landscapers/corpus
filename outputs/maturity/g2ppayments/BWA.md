# Botswana

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [In May 2025 food-voucher beneficiaries of a Ministry of Local Government programme received a monthly allowance on a SmartSwitch biometric smart card](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/28/smartswitch-botswana-pioneers-dignified-digital-aid-for-75000-beneficiaries/>). [The card is used at over 1,200 shops](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/28/smartswitch-botswana-pioneers-dignified-digital-aid-for-75000-beneficiaries/>). [It replaced a paper-based food basket system](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/28/smartswitch-botswana-pioneers-dignified-digital-aid-for-75000-beneficiaries/>). [More than 75,000 beneficiaries had made digital purchases at local stores with it](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/biometric-payment-card-system-pushing-financial-inclusion-in-botswana>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [in May 2025 the food-voucher programme paid its allowance by smart card](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/28/smartswitch-botswana-pioneers-dignified-digital-aid-for-75000-beneficiaries/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [in May 2025 the food-voucher card was biometrics-enabled](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/biometric-payment-card-system-pushing-financial-inclusion-in-botswana>).
