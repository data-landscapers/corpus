# Mauritania

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [Taazour launched its first cash distribution by electronic transfer on 9 September 2024, in Nouakchott, to Tekavoul families](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/253780>). Nationally, 140,297 families were in the regular transfer operation.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest fact found is [Decree 2024-073 (Article 11 and Article 13)](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229659.pdf>). It makes every poor or vulnerable household enrolled in the Registre Social eligible for Tekavoul regular and shock-response cash transfers. Eligibility is within a national quota set by the Government.

**Linked to ID.** [Decree 2024-073 makes households on the Registre Social eligible for Tekavoul transfers](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229659.pdf>). A national quota set by the government caps their number. That is enrolment, not a check of the payment against the national ID.

***Also on file***. [Registre Social](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229659.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 shows salaries or pensions paid digitally. None shows payments checked against the national ID.
