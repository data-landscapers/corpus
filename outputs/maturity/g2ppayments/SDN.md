# Sudan

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Public workers are paid through banks. In February 2024 The New Humanitarian reported that [nearly all public sector workers are paid digitally](<https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news-feature/2024/02/07/sudan-war-digital-money-online-banking-apps>). In April 2026 [the finance ministry said Council of Ministers decisions 149 and 150 have been put into effect](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>). They require workers' entitlements to be paid through bank accounts.

**Main streams.** Salaries are one main stream. In March 2021, before the three-year window, [the Thamarat family support programme paid its first transfers to about 79,142 households through cash cards](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2021/03/18/world-bank-reaffirms-commitment-to-sudan-s-family-support-program>). No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [the finance ministry's April 2026 statement that workers' entitlements are paid through bank accounts](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>).
