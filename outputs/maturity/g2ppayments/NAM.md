# Namibia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [Minister Emma Kantema told the National Assembly on 2026-09-08 that 10,066 of 15,785 suspended beneficiaries had been reinstated and paid through the government payment system](<https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/5-700-grant-beneficiaries-still-locked-out-of-payment-system-nmh017779-1-21902>). They held old-age, child-welfare and other social grants. [Bank Windhoek processed the first live government Marginalised Beneficiaries grant payments over Namibia's national instant payment network on 17 June 2026](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/06/bank-windhoek-processes-first-govt-grant-payments-on-instant-payment-network/>). They were paid in near real time. [Namibia Economist reports Bank Windhoek processed the first batch of live government grant payments to Marginalised Beneficiaries](<https://economist.com.na/107579/retail/government-to-person-disbursements-go-live-under-the-instant-payment-platform/>). It did so over the national instant payment network, on Wednesday 17 June 2026.
