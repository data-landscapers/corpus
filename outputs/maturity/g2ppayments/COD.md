# DR Congo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In August 2025 the Ministry of Public Service said [civil servants' pay is delivered through bank accounts](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.cd/fonction-publique-jean-pierre-lihau-trace-la-voie-dune-paie-modernisee-et-securisee/>). Payroll bancarisation began ten years earlier. Gaps remain: some areas have no bank branch, and accounts are slow to open. On 23 July 2026 CADECO, the public savings bank, and the Comité de suivi de la paie [validated the last adjustments for proximity pay of state agents in rural DRC](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>). The first operations were to cover several rural zones.

**Main streams.** [Civil servants' pay](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.cd/fonction-publique-jean-pierre-lihau-trace-la-voie-dune-paie-modernisee-et-securisee/>) is one main stream, and the [CADECO arrangement](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>) extends it to rural areas. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [ministry's August 2025 account of salaries paid into bank accounts](<https://fonctionpublique.gouv.cd/fonction-publique-jean-pierre-lihau-trace-la-voie-dune-paie-modernisee-et-securisee/>).

***Also on file***. [CADECO](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>); [FlashApp](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.
