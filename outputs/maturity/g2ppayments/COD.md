# DR Congo

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 23 July 2026 CADECO, the public savings bank, and the Comité de suivi de la paie [validated the final technical and organisational adjustments for proximity pay of state agents and civil servants in rural DRC](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>). The first operations were to cover several rural zones. No source found since October 2023 says salaries are now paid this way.

**Main streams.** The [CADECO arrangement](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>) concerns the pay of state agents and civil servants in rural areas, which is one main stream. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [July 2026 preparation of rural proximity pay](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>).

***Also on file***. [CADECO](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/24/paie-des-agents-publics-la-cadeco-et-le-csp-prets-lancer-la-paie-de-proximite-en-milieu>); [FlashApp](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/>).
