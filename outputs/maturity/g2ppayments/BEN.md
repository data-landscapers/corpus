# Benin

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [Benin's Ministry of Economy and Finance circular of 25 September 2026 institutes electronic payment of official-mission expenses in all public administrations, with the SEMO 2 modules ready for domestic implementation](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3677/finances-publiques-nouvelle-etape-franchie-dans-digitalisation-paiement-frais-mission/>). [The government says GBESSOKE began monthly cash transfers on 18 September 2025, paying 20,621 beneficiaries (85% women) a first instalment of 208,272,100 FCFA, received on their mobile phones via secured SIM cards](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>). [The government says the GBESSOKE productive social safety-net programme is in a pilot phase across 12 communes, with electronic transfers to households received on secured SIM cards and extension planned to all 77 communes](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3677/finances-publiques-nouvelle-etape-franchie-dans-digitalisation-paiement-frais-mission/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3677/finances-publiques-nouvelle-etape-franchie-dans-digitalisation-paiement-frais-mission/>).
