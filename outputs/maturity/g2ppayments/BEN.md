# Benin

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Since 18 September 2025 [GBESSOKE has paid monthly cash transfers to households' secured SIM cards](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>). The first instalment went to 20,621 beneficiaries, 85% of them women. The programme was then [a pilot in 12 communes, to be extended to all 77](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>). Pensions are paid digitally too. [Pensions under 50,000 FCFA go to mobile money accounts and larger ones by bank transfer](<https://matinlibre.com/2023/12/05/dematerialisation-du-paiement-des-pensions-de-retraite-au-benin-un-veritable-soulagement-malgre-des-couacs/>), Matin Libre reported in December 2023. A [September 2026 finance ministry circular](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3677/finances-publiques-nouvelle-etape-franchie-dans-digitalisation-paiement-frais-mission/>) moved official-mission expenses to electronic payment.

**Main streams.** Two main streams are paid digitally: [GBESSOKE's social transfers](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>) and [retirement pensions](<https://matinlibre.com/2023/12/05/dematerialisation-du-paiement-des-pensions-de-retraite-au-benin-un-veritable-soulagement-malgre-des-couacs/>). Pensioners of the Fonds national des retraites have been paid by bank transfer for some years, and those of the social security fund now are. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [GBESSOKE's payment to secured SIM cards](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3265/mise-oeuvre-programme-gbessoke-transferts-monetaires-demarre-plus-mille-menages-deja-touches-/>), from September 2025.
