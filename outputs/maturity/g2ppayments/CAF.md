# Central African Republic

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Civil servants' salaries are paid digitally. [The World Bank said in December 2024 that more civil servants had enrolled on Patapaye](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>), the government's mobile salary payment system. [Recipients told the World Bank in 2024 they were satisfied with mobile money salaries](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/cash-vs-digital-how-do-digital-government-person-payments-ease-lives-recipients>). Before 2020 some travelled up to two days to collect cash. In August 2025 RFI reported that [administrations pay monthly salaries into bank accounts](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20250825-le-calvaire-de-ces-centrafricains-de-province-contraints-de-se-rendre-%C3%A0-bangui-pour-retirer-leur-salaire>). Provincial staff must still travel to Bangui to draw them. In July 2026 [an Orange network outage in Batangafo kept some civil servants from collecting their pay](<https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/>).

**Main streams.** The facts found concern salaries, one main stream. A [March 2026 notice for the NDOYE safety-net project](<https://tenders.indexbox.io/tenders/selection-d-un-consultant-cabinet-pour-la-realisation-de-l-etude-de-faisabilite-et-b-op00430153>) seeks a firm to set up a payment platform for its transfers. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [clean payroll database used to pay civil servants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>), reported in December 2024.
