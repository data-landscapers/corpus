# Cameroon

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** On 17 July 2026 the national coordinator of the MINEPAT Adaptive Social Safety Nets and Economic Inclusion Project (PFS-AIE) [awarded framework contracts for digital payments to the project's beneficiaries](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=36442&type_publication=COMM>). Mmockmbie Credit Union holds zone 1 and People Finance SA zone 3. The contracts cover cycle 8 of the TMO and phases 7 and 8 of the TMU. No source found since October 2023 says a payment under them has been made. In 2020, before the three-year window, [the government paid emergency COVID-19 transfers to 80,000 informal workers' mobile money accounts](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/fr/africacan/renforcer-la-resilience-comment-argent-mobile-transforme-la-reponse-aux-crises-au-cameroun>).

**Main streams.** The [PFS-AIE contracts](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=36442&type_publication=COMM>) concern social safety net transfers only. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [PFS-AIE payment contracts of July 2026](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=36442&type_publication=COMM>).
