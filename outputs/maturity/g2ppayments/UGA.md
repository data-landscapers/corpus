# Uganda

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In October 2026 the gender ministry [said it had begun piloting mobile money payment of the SAGE older-persons grant](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). It is an alternative to the existing payment points. The ministry would extend it nationwide if the pilot succeeds. In November 2025 a minister [said SAGE paid older people a monthly cash transfer into bank accounts](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/304189/govt-lowers-sage-eligibility-age-to-65-injects-shs-373bn-to-expand-support-for-older-persons>).

**Main streams.** Only the SAGE grant is described. The newest fact on it is the [mobile money pilot](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>), reported in October 2026. Salaries and pensions are not covered.

**Linked to ID.** SAGE eligibility is read from the date of birth on the national ID card. In October 2026 the programme head [said 43,000 eligible older people had complained of refusals](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). Their ID cards put them below the grant's age-80 threshold. In November 2025 the minister [said SAGE payments need a bank account linked to the National Identification Number](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/304189/govt-lowers-sage-eligibility-age-to-65-injects-shs-373bn-to-expand-support-for-older-persons>).
