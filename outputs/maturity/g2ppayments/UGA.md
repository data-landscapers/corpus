# Uganda

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Parish Development Model's Wendi system, managed by Pearl Bank, disburses the government programme's loans directly to beneficiaries' mobile phones, reaching 3,571,108 beneficiaries, as reported by the Ministry of Finance (14,133 Wendi agents recruited)](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Main streams.** No source held since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>).

**Linked to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>).
