# Uganda

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Parish Development Model's Wendi system, managed by Pearl Bank, disburses the government programme's loans directly to beneficiaries' mobile phones, reaching 3,571,108 beneficiaries, as reported by the Ministry of Finance (14,133 Wendi agents recruited)](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>). [The gender ministry said in October 2026 it had begun piloting mobile money payment of the SAGE older-persons grant as an alternative to the existing payment points, to be extended nationwide if the pilot succeeds](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>).

**Linked to ID.** [SAGE eligibility is read from the date of birth on the national ID card, and 43,000 people refused on that basis had complained by October 2026](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>).
