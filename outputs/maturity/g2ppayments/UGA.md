# Uganda

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In October 2026 the gender ministry [said it had begun piloting mobile money payment of the SAGE older-persons grant](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>), as an alternative to the existing payment points. It would extend the arrangement nationwide if the pilot succeeds. No source found since October 2023 shows a government transfer, salary or pension paid digitally in service.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more of social transfers, civil service salaries and pensions paid digitally. The nearest fact found is the [SAGE mobile money pilot](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>), reported in October 2026.

**Linked to ID.** SAGE eligibility is read from the date of birth on the national ID card. In October 2026 the programme head [said 43,000 eligible older people had complained of refusals](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). Their ID cards put them below the grant's age-80 threshold.
