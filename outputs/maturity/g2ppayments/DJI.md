# Djibouti

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [In March 2024 UNHCR said 158 refugee teachers from Ali Addeh and Holl-Holl are paid their salaries by bank transfer](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/cdde8002-a416-4ea1-a6f9-05412c05d89c/HCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_Mars%202024%20.pdf>). The education ministry had helped them open bank accounts. [In November 2025 the Ministry of Budget said it had signed a convention with the bank BCI to digitise the payment of civil servants' salaries](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-du-budget-de-djibouti_budget-digitalisation-gouvernance-activity-7396655456540086272-U-c_>). The ministry gave no date for the first salaries paid under it. It did not state the convention's terms.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. [The refugee teachers' bank salaries](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/cdde8002-a416-4ea1-a6f9-05412c05d89c/HCR%20Djibouti_Operational%20Update_Mars%202024%20.pdf>) are one, and [the November 2025 convention on civil servants' salaries](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-du-budget-de-djibouti_budget-digitalisation-gouvernance-activity-7396655456540086272-U-c_>) gives no go-live.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact is [the November 2025 convention between the Ministry of Budget and BCI on paying civil servants' salaries digitally](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-du-budget-de-djibouti_budget-digitalisation-gouvernance-activity-7396655456540086272-U-c_>).
