# Djibouti

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [In November 2025 the Ministry of Budget said it had signed a convention with the bank BCI to digitise the payment of civil servants' salaries](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-du-budget-de-djibouti_budget-digitalisation-gouvernance-activity-7396655456540086272-U-c_>). The ministry gave no date for the first salaries paid under it and did not state the convention's terms.

**Main streams.** No source held since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact is [the November 2025 convention between the Ministry of Budget and BCI on paying civil servants' salaries digitally](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-du-budget-de-djibouti_budget-digitalisation-gouvernance-activity-7396655456540086272-U-c_>).
