# Guinea-Bissau

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In September 2026 the prime minister [said the government would make every effort to pay each teacher at their workplace, through bank branches or Orange Money](<https://ang.gw/pm-promete-elevar-para-15-por-cento-do-orcamento-geral-do-estado-os-fundos-destinados-ao-setor-do-ensino-nacional/>). The education minister listed dematerialised salary payment as a pillar of a strategy being implemented. No source found since October 2023 says teachers' salaries are paid digitally in service.

**Main streams.** The [September 2026 statements](<https://ang.gw/pm-promete-elevar-para-15-por-cento-do-orcamento-geral-do-estado-os-fundos-destinados-ao-setor-do-ensino-nacional/>) concern teachers' salaries only, which the government pays as part of civil service pay. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [prime minister's September 2026 promise on teachers' pay](<https://ang.gw/pm-promete-elevar-para-15-por-cento-do-orcamento-geral-do-estado-os-fundos-destinados-ao-setor-do-ensino-nacional/>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>); [Orange Money](<https://ang.gw/pm-promete-elevar-para-15-por-cento-do-orcamento-geral-do-estado-os-fundos-destinados-ao-setor-do-ensino-nacional/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government transfer, salary or pension is paid digitally in service.
