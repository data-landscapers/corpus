# Lesotho

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Two social grants are paid by mobile money. In July 2026 UNICEF [said 25,575 households receive Child Grants Programme payments by mobile cash transfer](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>), through the Ntlafatsa Bana programme. In January 2026 the Lesotho Times reported that [over 40,000 elderly people, about 53% of old-age pension beneficiaries, are paid electronically](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89428>). Most use M-Pesa and EcoCash. In Quthing [23 of 30 cash pay-points closed after over 4,800 pensioners moved to mobile money](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/quthing-shuts-23-pension-pay-points/>), Lesotho News Agency said. Over 1,000 there were still paid in cash.

**Main streams.** The [child grants](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>) and the [old-age pension](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89428>) are both social transfers, which make one stream. In January 2025 the UN in Lesotho said [the government was moving child grant payments from cash-in-transit to mobile networks](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/291096-policy-watch-issue-5-january-2025>). No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or contributory pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [UNICEF's July 2026 account of child grants paid by mobile transfer](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>).
