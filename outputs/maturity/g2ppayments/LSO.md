# Lesotho

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In July 2026 UNICEF [said 25,575 households now receive Child Grants Programme payments by digital mobile cash transfer](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>), through the Ntlafatsa Bana programme. In January 2025 the UN in Lesotho [said the government was moving these payments from third-party cash-in-transit to selected mobile network providers](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/291096-policy-watch-issue-5-january-2025>). The Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development leads the move, with EU, UNICEF and World Bank support. The [EU and UNICEF-backed Ntlafatsa Bana project (2023-2026)](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>) aims to digitalise payments to beneficiaries.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>).
