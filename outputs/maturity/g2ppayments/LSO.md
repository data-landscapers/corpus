# Lesotho

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [UNICEF says that through the Ntlafatsa Bana programme 25,575 households now receive Child Grants Programme payments through digital mobile cash transfers](<https://www.unicef.org/lesotho/stories/how-birth-certificate-opened-door-support>). [The UN in Lesotho says the Government, led by the Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development with EU, UNICEF and World Bank support, is transitioning Child Grants Programme payments from third-party cash-in-transit to mobile payments through selected mobile network providers](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/291096-policy-watch-issue-5-january-2025>). [The EU/UNICEF-backed Ntlafatsa Bana project (2023-2026) aims to improve Child Grants Programme delivery by shifting towards digital payments and digitalising payments to beneficiaries, and the Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development is transitioning to digital payments](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>).
