# Zimbabwe

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Pensions and a social cash transfer are paid by mobile money. In May 2026 the National Social Security Authority (NSSA) [said pensioners can now receive their pensions in a mobile money wallet](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>). In July 2024 Gemnation reported that [the Harmonised Social Cash Transfer pays through EcoCash, not cash in transit](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>). It had 75,000 households registered in 30 districts. In September 2024 [cabinet said NetOne had been contracted to pay urban beneficiaries of the Urban-Cash-for-Cereal Programme](<https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/TWENTY-EIGHTH%20Post-Cabinet%20brief%202024.pdf>). In October 2024 [the minister said money for 1.7 million urban people had been loaded with NetOne](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/urban-cash-transfers-to-benefit-17m/>). It was to reach their phones that week.

**Main streams.** Two main streams are paid digitally: [NSSA pensions](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>) and the [Harmonised Social Cash Transfer](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>). No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [NSSA's May 2026 notice on wallet pensions](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>).
