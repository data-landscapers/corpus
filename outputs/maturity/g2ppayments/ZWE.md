# Zimbabwe

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In May 2026 the National Social Security Authority (NSSA) [said pensioners can now receive their pensions in a mobile money wallet](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>). No source found since October 2023 says how many have taken up the option. No source says how the other pensioners are paid.

**Main streams.** [NSSA's pensions](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>) are one stream, paid by the national social security authority to its pensioners. No source found since October 2023 shows social transfers or civil service salaries paid digitally, so a second main stream has not been found.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [NSSA's May 2026 notice on pensions paid to mobile wallets](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/national-social-security-authority-nssa_nssaheroes-convenience-digitalservices-activity-7463468848600715264-CH3I>).
