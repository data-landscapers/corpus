# South Africa

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Minister of Social Development says SRD grant payments run through beneficiary bank accounts](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>). Accounts lacking biometric FICA verification are placed in a 'referred' status that blocks further payments. [The Portfolio Committee on Social Development says SASSA administers the SRD application and payment system](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-committee-welcomes-findings-sassas-srd-grant-payment-system>). The system uses a mobile money service to pay grants. [SASSA's 2026 grant booklet says it requires applicants to give an active personal bank account in their own name](<https://sassa.gov.za/documents/uploads/SASSA_Grant_Booklet_-_2026_compressed.pdf>). The account must support Electronic Fund Transfers, for payment of social assistance benefits.

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the minister said SRD grants were paid into beneficiary bank accounts](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the minister said accounts without biometric FICA verification were blocked from further SRD payments](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>).
