# South Africa

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [The Minister of Social Development says SRD grant payments run through beneficiary bank accounts, with accounts lacking biometric FICA verification placed in a 'referred' status that blocks further payments](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>). [The Portfolio Committee on Social Development says SASSA administers the SRD application and payment system, which uses a mobile money service to pay grants](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-committee-welcomes-findings-sassas-srd-grant-payment-system>). [SASSA's 2026 grant booklet says it requires applicants to give an active personal bank account in their own name, supporting Electronic Fund Transfers, for payment of social assistance benefits](<https://sassa.gov.za/documents/uploads/SASSA_Grant_Booklet_-_2026_compressed.pdf>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the ID or a social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31120/>).
