# Kenya

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Inua Jamii, the government's cash transfer for older people, orphans and people with severe disabilities, is paid digitally. [From July 2023 it paid monthly into bank accounts through six contracted payment providers](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/882c0c1238394fef0d3a308d9d47946f-0380022024/related/2-1-Digital-Payments-Kenya-Case-ASP-Rome-Course.pdf>). [A December 2023 mandate added mobile money, piloted for two of its programmes](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/882c0c1238394fef0d3a308d9d47946f-0380022024/related/2-1-Digital-Payments-Kenya-Case-ASP-Rome-Course.pdf>). [From January 2025 all beneficiaries moved to the eCitizen-M-Pesa platform](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/press%20release/Press%20release%20for%20Inua%20Jamii%20rollout%20-%20JAN.%202025.pdf>), collecting funds from M-Pesa agents by USSD.

**Main streams.** [Inua Jamii](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/press%20release/Press%20release%20for%20Inua%20Jamii%20rollout%20-%20JAN.%202025.pdf>) is one main stream, social transfers. In August 2025 the National Treasury [said it would pay civil servants' salaries and pensions through its payroll database](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/treasury-shifts-salary-pension-payments-to-new-online-system-5152690>). No source found since October 2023 shows salaries or pensions now paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** [Every Inua Jamii beneficiary must hold a registered Safaricom line that matches their ID card](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/press%20release/Press%20release%20for%20Inua%20Jamii%20rollout%20-%20JAN.%202025.pdf>) to receive funds. The State Department for Social Protection set the rule in January 2025.
