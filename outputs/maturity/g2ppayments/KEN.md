# Kenya

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** In August 2025 the National Treasury [said it would pay civil servants' salaries and pensions through the Integrated Personnel Payroll Database](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/treasury-shifts-salary-pension-payments-to-new-online-system-5152690>). The change was to apply from the end of that month. It extended the database, in use since 1998, to pensioners. No source found since October 2023 says salaries or pensions are now paid digitally through it.

**Main streams.** The [Treasury's August 2025 statement](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/treasury-shifts-salary-pension-payments-to-new-online-system-5152690>) names two streams, salaries and pensions. No source found since October 2023 shows either of them, or social transfers, paid digitally in service.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [Treasury's August 2025 payroll change](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/treasury-shifts-salary-pension-payments-to-new-online-system-5152690>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government transfer, salary or pension is paid digitally in service.
