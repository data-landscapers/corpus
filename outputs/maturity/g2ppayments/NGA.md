# Nigeria

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** [NSIPA lists digital payments via bank accounts and wallets, with payment duration cut from 6 months to 3](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [The World Bank reports 4.4 million households had received at least one tranche of the federal government cash transfer paid directly into bank accounts, and 0.8 million a second, as of 8 October 2024](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/10/20/over-4-million-households-benefit-from-fgs-cash-transfer-lowest-in-southeast-report/>). [The Central Bank of Nigeria pays Conditional Cash Transfer beneficiaries directly, with bulk payments to five million households due to begin the week after the article and verified households already receiving payment alerts, says the minister's media aide](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>).

**Main streams.** No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally. The nearest held fact: [The World Bank says payments to recipients' bank accounts are made after validating individuals through the NIN or BVN, and low NIN/BVN coverage among the poor slowed the rollout](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/10/20/over-4-million-households-benefit-from-fgs-cash-transfer-lowest-in-southeast-report/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The World Bank says payments to recipients' bank accounts are made after validating individuals through the NIN or BVN, and low NIN/BVN coverage among the poor slowed the rollout](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/10/20/over-4-million-households-benefit-from-fgs-cash-transfer-lowest-in-southeast-report/>). [Beneficiaries are matched on NIN and BVN with in-person verification and biometrics, and those without a NIN and BVN are disqualified, says the minister's media aide](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>).
