# Nigeria

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Federal cash transfers are paid into accounts and wallets. In October 2023 [the minister's media aide said the central bank was paying Conditional Cash Transfer beneficiaries directly](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>). By 8 October 2024 [4.4 million households had received at least one tranche in their bank accounts](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/10/20/over-4-million-households-benefit-from-fgs-cash-transfer-lowest-in-southeast-report/>), the World Bank said. In September 2025 [the finance minister said N330 billion had gone to 8.5 million households](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/09/18/fg-moves-to-include-conditional-cash-transfer-in-national-budgets-disburses-n330bn-to-poor-households/>), by bank account or mobile wallet. On 30 October 2026 [the humanitarian affairs minister began N50,000 payments to mobile wallets under NERSI](<https://punchng.com/how-fg-selects-beneficiaries-for-n50000-relief/>).

**Main streams.** [NSIPA lists payment through bank accounts and wallets](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>) for its programmes, which are social transfers. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** [Beneficiaries are verified through their National Identity Number, and payments are tied to NIN-linked accounts](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/09/18/fg-moves-to-include-conditional-cash-transfer-in-national-budgets-disburses-n330bn-to-poor-households/>), the finance minister said in September 2025. [NERSI selects beneficiaries from verified identity records and the National Social Register](<https://punchng.com/how-fg-selects-beneficiaries-for-n50000-relief/>), with the NIN linked to the receiving wallet. In 2023 [those without a NIN and BVN were disqualified](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>).
