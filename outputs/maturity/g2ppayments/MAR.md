# Morocco

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Morocco's direct social aid [has been paid since the end of December 2023](<https://medias24.com/2024/02/20/aides-sociales-directes-les-premiers-retours-dexperience-du-secteur-des-etablissements-de-paiement/>), Médias24 reported. The government and the central bank require it to be paid into payment or bank accounts, and not in cash. Between 2023 and 2025 [the National Social Support Agency paid 51 billion dirhams to nearly four million families](<https://telquel.ma/2026/10/02/digital-payment-the-failed-revolution-against-the-reign-of-cash_2008499>), monthly into payment accounts. [Recipients of the DAAM programme told the World Bank they valued payment into an account](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/cash-vs-digital-how-do-digital-government-person-payments-ease-lives-recipients>), with ATM withdrawals at any hour.

**Main streams.** [Direct social support](<https://telquel.ma/2026/10/02/digital-payment-the-failed-revolution-against-the-reign-of-cash_2008499>) is one social transfer. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** In November 2025 the government said in a written answer that [Direct Social Support eligibility rests on household scoring in the Unified Social Register](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). Monthly electronic data exchanges check that households remain eligible.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.
