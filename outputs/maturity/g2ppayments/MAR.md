# Morocco

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** Morocco's direct social aid [has been paid since the end of December 2023](<https://medias24.com/2024/02/20/aides-sociales-directes-les-premiers-retours-dexperience-du-secteur-des-etablissements-de-paiement/>), Médias24 reported in February 2024. The government and the central bank require it to be paid only into payment or bank accounts, and not in cash. A recipient is therefore paid through an account held with a bank or a payment institution.

**Main streams.** [Direct social aid](<https://medias24.com/2024/02/20/aides-sociales-directes-les-premiers-retours-dexperience-du-secteur-des-etablissements-de-paiement/>) is one social transfer. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally, so a second main stream has not been found.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is the [account-only payment of direct social aid from December 2023](<https://medias24.com/2024/02/20/aides-sociales-directes-les-premiers-retours-dexperience-du-secteur-des-etablissements-de-paiement/>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows two or more main government payment streams paid digitally.
