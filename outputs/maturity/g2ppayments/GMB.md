# Gambia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The Nafa cash transfer is run by the National Nutrition Agency under the RISE project, which the World Bank and the government fund. In January 2026 two NaNA officials said [Nafa pays households bi-monthly transfers of D3,000 through the Wave fintech service](<https://standard.gm/rise-project-boosts-rural-livelihoods-with-nafa-cash-transfers/>). In July 2026 The Standard reported [a Nafa round paid electronically through QMoney's platform](<https://standard.gm/thousands-of-beneficiaries-receive-nafa-cash-transfer/>). Provincial beneficiaries received their fifth round, and Banjul, Kanifing and parts of Kombo their first.

**Main streams.** [Nafa](<https://standard.gm/thousands-of-beneficiaries-receive-nafa-cash-transfer/>) is a social transfer. The Department of Community Development and the Directorate of Social Welfare take part in it. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [Nafa's July 2026 round paid through QMoney](<https://standard.gm/thousands-of-beneficiaries-receive-nafa-cash-transfer/>).
