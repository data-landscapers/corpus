# Gambia

## G2P functionality

**Paid digitally.** The Nafa cash transfer programme is run by the National Nutrition Agency under the RISE project. The World Bank and the Gambian government fund RISE. In January 2026 two NaNA officials said [Nafa pays households bi-monthly transfers of D3,000 through the Wave fintech service](<https://standard.gm/rise-project-boosts-rural-livelihoods-with-nafa-cash-transfers/>). The transfers are now paid without cash.

**Main streams.** [Nafa](<https://standard.gm/rise-project-boosts-rural-livelihoods-with-nafa-cash-transfers/>) is a social transfer. No source found since October 2023 shows civil service salaries or pensions paid digitally, so a second main stream has not been found.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says government payments are checked against the national ID or a social registry. The nearest fact found is [Nafa's move to cashless payment through Wave](<https://standard.gm/rise-project-boosts-rural-livelihoods-with-nafa-cash-transfers/>), reported in January 2026.
