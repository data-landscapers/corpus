# Mauritius

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 3.39 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by ILOSTAT's labour force series. The same series [gives 3.03 in 2021, 3.18 in 2022 and 2.95 in 2023](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the figure rose by 0.36 points. At 1 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 0.3, 0.6 and 1 per cent of employment, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and IT services, not graduates alone.

**Youth unemployment.** No source found since October 2023 gives youth unemployment of 14 per cent or less on the national labour force survey. The figure [alone](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) set the stage.
