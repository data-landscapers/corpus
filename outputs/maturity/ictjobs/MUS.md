# Mauritius

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 3.39 per cent of all workers in Mauritius](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 3.03 per cent in 2021, 3.18 per cent in 2022 and 2.95 per cent in 2023](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication rose by 0.36 points. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No figure for graduate unemployment since October 2023 has been found. Statistics Mauritius's labour force survey [puts youth unemployment at 18.4 per cent in 2025](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1925/LF_Emp_Unemp_Yr25_190526.pdf>), up from 17.7 in 2024, a figure for all young people rather than graduates. [In 2024 information and communication employed 3.39 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>).
