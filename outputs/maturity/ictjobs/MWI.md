# Malawi

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 0.23 per cent of all workers in Malawi](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The series gives no figure for the years just before, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is 0.07 points short of 0.3 per cent of all workers. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** It is asked about only once information and communication employs 1 per cent of workers. [The figure](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) is below that line, so none was looked for.
