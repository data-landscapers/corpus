# Lesotho

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 0.31 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by ILOSTAT's labour force series. The series gives no figure for the years just before, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is 0.29 points short of 0.6, the next line. The study's lines are 0.3, 0.6 and 1 per cent of employment, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and IT services, not graduates alone.

**Youth unemployment.** Youth unemployment is asked about only once information and communication employs 1 per cent of workers, so with [the figure below that line](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) none was looked for in this first pass.
