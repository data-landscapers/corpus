# Eswatini

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2023, [information and communication employed 1.56 per cent of all workers in Eswatini](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 1.32 per cent in 2021](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication rose by 0.24 points. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No figure for graduate unemployment since October 2023 has been found. [In 2023 information and communication employed 1.56 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>).

***Also on file***. None found.
