# Ghana

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 0.31 per cent of all workers in Ghana](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 0.45 per cent in 2021, 0.29 per cent in 2022, 0.2 per cent in 2023 and 0.21 per cent in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication fell by 0.13 points. The share was highest in 2021, at 0.45 per cent. The share was lowest in 2023, at 0.2 per cent. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** [In 2025 information and communication employed 0.31 per cent of all workers in Ghana](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). No figure for graduate unemployment in Ghana has been found.
