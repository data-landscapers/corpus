# Angola

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 0.44 per cent of all workers in Angola](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 0.31 per cent in 2021, 0.42 per cent in 2022, 0.43 per cent in 2023 and 0.44 per cent in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication rose by 0.12 points. The share was lowest in 2021, at 0.31 per cent. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services in Angola, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** [In 2025 information and communication employed 0.44 per cent of all workers in Angola](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). No figure for graduate unemployment in Angola has been found.
