# Senegal

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 0.596 per cent of all workers in Senegal](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 0.79 per cent in 2022 and 0.61 per cent in 2023](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2022 the share of workers in information and communication fell by 0.2 points. The share fell in each year given, from 0.79 per cent in 2022 to 0.596 per cent in 2024. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services in Senegal, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** [In 2024 information and communication employed 0.596 per cent of all workers in Senegal](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). No figure for graduate unemployment in Senegal has been found.
