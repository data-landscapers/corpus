# Djibouti

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 1.26 per cent of all workers in Djibouti](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The series gives no figure for the years just before. No source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is above 1 per cent of all workers. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No credible statistic found since October 2023 gives graduate unemployment of 14 per cent or less. The figure [alone](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) set the stage.
