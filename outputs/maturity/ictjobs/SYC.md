# Seychelles

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 2.19 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by ILOSTAT's labour force series. The same series [gives 1.65 in 2023 and 0.82 in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2023 the figure rose by 0.54 points. At 1 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 0.3, 0.6 and 1 per cent of employment, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and IT services, not graduates alone.

**Youth unemployment.** A World Bank report of September 2026 [puts youth unemployment at around 9 per cent](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/9264a4f5-29c4-40ff-903b-9891303c089a>), three times the national rate. The figure [alone](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) set the stage.
