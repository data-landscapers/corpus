# Seychelles

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 2.19 per cent of all workers in Seychelles](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 1.65 per cent in 2023 and 0.82 per cent in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2023 the share of workers in information and communication rose by 0.54 points. It is above 1 per cent of all workers. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No credible statistic found since October 2023 gives graduate unemployment of 14 per cent or less. A World Bank report of September 2026 [puts youth unemployment at around 9 per cent](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/9264a4f5-29c4-40ff-903b-9891303c089a>), three times the national rate, a figure for all young people rather than graduates. The figure [alone](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) set the stage.
