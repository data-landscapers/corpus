# Equatorial Guinea

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2023, [information and communication employed 1.29 per cent of all workers in Equatorial Guinea](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The series gives no figure for the years just before. No source found says whether it is rising or falling. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No figure for graduate unemployment since October 2023 has been found. [In 2023 information and communication employed 1.29 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>).
