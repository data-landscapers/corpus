# Mali

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2024, [information and communication employed 0.19 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by ILOSTAT's labour force series. The same series [gives 0.22 in 2022 and 0.18 in 2023](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2022 the figure fell by 0.03 points. It is 0.11 points short of 0.3, the next line. The study's lines are 0.3, 0.6 and 1 per cent of employment, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and IT services, not graduates alone.

**Youth unemployment.** Youth unemployment is asked about only once information and communication employs 1 per cent of workers, so with [the figure below that line](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) none was looked for in this first pass.

***Not found***. No figure of 0.3 per cent of employment or more is held.
