# Tanzania

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 0.15 per cent of all workers in Tanzania](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 0.19 per cent in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2024 the share of workers in information and communication fell by 0.03 points. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** [In 2025 information and communication employed 0.15 per cent of all workers](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). No figure for graduate unemployment has been found.
