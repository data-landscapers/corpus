# Tunisia

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2023, [information and communication employed 1.26 per cent of all workers in Tunisia](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 1.14 per cent in 2021 and 1.32 per cent in 2022](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication rose by 0.11 points. The share was lowest in 2021, at 1.14 per cent. The share was highest in 2022, at 1.32 per cent. No figure for 2024 or later has been found. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services in Tunisia, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** No figure for graduate unemployment since October 2023 has been found. [In 2023 information and communication employed 1.26 per cent of all workers in Tunisia](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>).
