# Rwanda

## ICT employment

**Employment in information and communication.** In 2025, [information and communication employed 0.27 per cent of all workers in Rwanda](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>), by the International Labour Organization's labour force series, ILOSTAT. The same series [gives 0.31 per cent in 2021, 0.19 per cent in 2022, 0.24 per cent in 2023 and 0.25 per cent in 2024](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>). Since 2021 the share of workers in information and communication fell by 0.03 points. It is 0.03 points short of 0.3 per cent of all workers. The figure counts all employment in publishing, broadcasting, telecommunications and information technology services, not graduates alone.

**Graduate unemployment.** It is asked about only once information and communication employs 1 per cent of workers. [The figure](<https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/>) is below that line, so none was looked for.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No figure of 0.3 per cent of employment or more is held.
