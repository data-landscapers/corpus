# Sierra Leone

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The national digital identity system was launched in August 2019](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2506-56815-sierra-leone-trains-1-000-civil-servants-to-modernize-public-services>). [The NCRA registered 100 inmates for National Identification Numbers and National ID cards, issued free by Government](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>). [NCRA Director General Massaquoi said NCRA has strengthened the national identity infrastructure and issued secure identity documents, and the Bank of Sierra Leone referred to the unique National Identification Number](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>). [Citizens' Barray says Sierra Leone's National ID system is in use for NIN-SIM telecom registration and that NCRA lists the National ID card at NLe140](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/citizens-barray_freeid-identity-telecommunications-activity-7492303807360679936-U3Br>). [Biometric Update says Sierra Leone's national ID system is based on MOSIP and is the bedrock of its digital transformation](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework>).

**Biometric checks.** [The system is biometric and the NINs are described as secure and non-duplicable, per the Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).
