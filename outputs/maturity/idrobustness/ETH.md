# Ethiopia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Fayda, launched at scale in 2023, has recorded more than 52 million registrations and over 208 million eKYC verifications as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Fayda has registered more than 51 million citizens, issued nearly 14 million printed cards and integrated about 150 institutions, each person assigned a unique identification number (Addis Fortune)](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [FaydaVerse says the national ID had registered 52.4 million people by 15 October 2026 and printed over 14.5 million credentials](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>). [Fayda is a requirement for opening new bank accounts and customers hold Fayda numbers, with the National ID Programme enrolling people at branches, per Birr Metrics](<https://birrmetrics.com/cbe-gives-customers-until-november-9-to-link-bank-accounts-with-fayda-id/>).
