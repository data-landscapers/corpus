# Ethiopia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Fayda, launched at scale in 2023, has recorded more than 52 million registrations and over 208 million eKYC verifications as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Fayda has registered more than 51 million citizens, issued nearly 14 million printed cards and integrated about 150 institutions, each person assigned a unique identification number (Addis Fortune)](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [President Taye Atske Selassie described Fayda as the national digital identification programme that enables electronic verification and access to banking, mobile money and telecoms](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9786837>). [FaydaVerse says the national ID had registered 52.4 million people by 15 October 2026 and printed over 14.5 million credentials](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).

**Biometric checks.** In March 2025 the national ID programme said Fayda [takes iris, ten fingerprints and a face image to identify each person uniquely](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>). It works on a principle of one person, one ID, and gives each registrant a 12-digit number. Biometrics are mandatory for everyone over five.

**Security certified or audited.** No certification, audit or assurance level has been reported for the system. The nearest held fact is [the programme's account of biometric enrolment in March 2025](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>).
