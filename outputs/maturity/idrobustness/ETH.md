# Ethiopia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Fayda is a requirement for opening new bank accounts and customers hold Fayda numbers, with the National ID Programme enrolling people at branches, per Birr Metrics](<https://birrmetrics.com/cbe-gives-customers-until-november-9-to-link-bank-accounts-with-fayda-id/>). [The National ID Program's own page lists the documents accepted for Fayda registration, including a witness route through an already registered resident and a USSD line 9779](<https://id.gov.et/proof>). [Fayda, launched at scale in 2023, has recorded more than 52 million registrations and over 208 million eKYC verifications as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Fayda has completed 49.1 million enrolments on 12,000 biometric registration kits, with over 194 million eKYC verifications, per the National ID Program](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/cbe-gives-customers-until-november-9-to-link-bank-accounts-with-fayda-id/>).
