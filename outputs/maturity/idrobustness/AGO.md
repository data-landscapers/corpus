# Angola

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Angola's National Director of Registries and Notary Services Aristides Marques said 17,166,000 national identity cards (BI) have been issued at home and in the diaspora](<https://angolanewswire.com/angolan-government-issues-more-than-17-million-citizens-identity-cards/>). [The Benguela justice delegation issued and delivered more than 4,000 Bilhetes de Identidade in 16 days in Egito Praia under the BI universalisation programme](<https://minjusdh.gov.ao/web/noticias/programa-de-universalizacao-do-bi-reforca-acesso-a-documentacao-em-egito-praia>). [Intelwatch reports a EUR 130 million contract with Hungary's ANY Security Printing for biometric passports and identity documents, part of contracts exceeding US$250 million](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [Angop reports Cabinda province now issues more than 200 Bilhete de Identidade cards a day, delivered in under 24 hours since printing was decentralised in June 2026](<https://angop.ao/en/noticias/sociedade/municipes-destacam-impacto-do-servico-desconcentrado-do-bi-em-cabinda/>). [A presidential order of August 2026 authorised US$28 million for a digital identity platform with capacity for up to 30 million digital identities](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angolanewswire.com/angolan-government-issues-more-than-17-million-citizens-identity-cards/>).
