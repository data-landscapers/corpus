# Ghana

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The National Identification Authority [issues the Ghana Card as the national identity card](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/nia-adopts-three-shift-system-for-24-hour-ghana-card-registration/>). In August 2026 its Labone centre registered around the clock in three shifts. It had [registered 17.8 million by March 2024](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). By September 2025 it had [registered 18,955,360 Ghanaians and issued 17,855,708 cards](<https://kessbenonline.com/2025/09/16/nia-to-mandate-biometric-verification-for-public-services/>). It [has started registering children aged six to 14](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1528541/ghana-card-to-become-e-wallet-for-electronic-trans.html>). In February 2026 the communications minister called the card [the key unique identifier for all Ghanaians](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/KEYNOTE-ADDRESS-FOR-THE-NATIONAL-DATA-PROTECTION-CONFERENCE-2026-_-updated.pdf>).
