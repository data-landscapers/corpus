## Robustness of system

[The NIA issues the Ghana Card as the country's national identification card and runs registration at its Labone centre round the clock in three shifts (NIA Executive Secretary, media tour 2026-08-12)](<https://thehighstreetjournal.com/nia-adopts-three-shift-system-for-24-hour-ghana-card-registration/>). [NIA says the Ghana Card, with fingerprint, iris and facial biometrics held in the NIA database, is in use for verifying individuals](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).
