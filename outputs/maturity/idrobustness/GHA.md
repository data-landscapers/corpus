[The analyst says Ghana has built a foundational biometric identity, the Ghana Card, managed by the National Identification Authority](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).
