# Togo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [ANID has produced about 5.9 million identification cards, nearly 80% reportedly distributed, and each person registered receives a Unique Identification Number (NIU), per data reported on 28 September 2026](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). [ANID is enrolling residents, Togolese or foreign, for its card and a Numero d'Identification Unique across four regions, the Plateaux joining with fifty centres at Atakpame, says the government portal](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/se-faire-enregistrer-c-est-compter-c-est-exister-c-est-participer>). [Biometric Update says MOSIP has MoUs with 29 countries for rollouts and pilots, including Togo](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/why-decentralized-digital-identity-looks-different-in-every-country>). [Togo's presentation says the e-ID programme launched in 2023 assigns a unique biometric identifier to each resident, led by the National Identity Agency](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>).
