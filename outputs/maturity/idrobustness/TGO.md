# Togo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The ANID gives each registered person a Unique Identification Number (NIU)](<https://www.lacinquieme.tg/identification-biometrique-au-togo-118-million-de-cartes-attendent-encore-leurs-titulaires/>). [By 21 October 2026 it had produced 5.9 million cards and distributed 4.72 million, leaving about 1.18 million awaiting collection](<https://www.lacinquieme.tg/identification-biometrique-au-togo-118-million-de-cartes-attendent-encore-leurs-titulaires/>). [The e-ID Togo system bases the number on fingerprints, iris and photo](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [The government portal says residents, Togolese or foreign, are enrolled region by region; the Plateaux joined with fifty centres at Atakpame](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/se-faire-enregistrer-c-est-compter-c-est-exister-c-est-participer>). [Togo is among 29 countries with a MOSIP agreement for rollouts or pilots](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/why-decentralized-digital-identity-looks-different-in-every-country>).

**Biometric checks.** [The deck says cards are printed after deduplication of the biometric enrolment](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).
