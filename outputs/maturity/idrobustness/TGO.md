# Togo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The deck says the e-ID Togo system provides unique identification numbers based on fingerprints, iris and photo, with cards printed after deduplication](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [ANID has produced about 5.9 million identification cards, nearly 80% reportedly distributed, and each person registered receives a Unique Identification Number (NIU), per data reported on 28 September 2026](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). [Biometric Update says MOSIP has MoUs with 29 countries for rollouts and pilots, including Togo](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/why-decentralized-digital-identity-looks-different-in-every-country>). [ANID is enrolling residents, Togolese or foreign, for its card and a Numero d'Identification Unique across four regions, the Plateaux joining with fifty centres at Atakpame, says the government portal](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/se-faire-enregistrer-c-est-compter-c-est-exister-c-est-participer>).

**Biometric checks.** [The deck says cards are printed after deduplication of the biometric enrolment](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).
