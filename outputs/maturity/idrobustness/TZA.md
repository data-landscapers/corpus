# Tanzania

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The National Identification Authority (NIDA) issues a National Identification Number and an identity card. [On 22 October 2026 the Deputy Minister for Home Affairs told a parliamentary committee that NIDA had generated 23,718,060 numbers](<https://dailynews.co.tz/nida-produces-23718060-identification-numbers-21526594-national-identity-cards-for-tanzanians/>). It had produced 21,526,594 cards, and 21,515,793 had been distributed. [In July 2026 NIDA counted 27,458,882 people registered](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>). [FinScope Tanzania 2023 found that 57% of adults held a number](<https://www.fsdt.or.tz/2023/11/23/universal-id-for-financial-inclusion-and-economic-empowerment/>), the Financial Sector Deepening Trust reports. [The NIDA Director General said in September 2026 that the ID will be upgraded under the name Jamii Namba](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>), linking nine services instead of four.

**Biometric checks.** [The Deputy Minister said the centre can verify applicants and match fingerprints so that each applicant is uniquely identified](<https://dailynews.co.tz/national-ids-to-stay-under-centralised-printing/>).
