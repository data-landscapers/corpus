# Tanzania

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [On 22 October 2026 the Deputy Minister for Home Affairs told a parliamentary committee that NIDA had generated 23,718,060 National Identification Numbers and produced 21,526,594 identity cards, 21,515,793 of them distributed](<https://dailynews.co.tz/nida-produces-23718060-identification-numbers-21526594-national-identity-cards-for-tanzanians/>). [Home Affairs Minister Patrobas Katambi said NIDA has registered 943,284 Tanzanians, that identity cards have been printed (6,308 uncollected in Zanzibar) and that NIDA numbers are accepted for some services](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/6308-nida-cards-remain-uncollected-in-zanzibar-2026-10-12-135944>). [Minister Katambi said 23,515,667 people have been issued a National Identification Number and 21,526,635 cards have been produced, with 2,027,103 finished cards uncollected](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [The NIDA Director General said the current ID has four parts and will be upgraded to nine under the name Jamii Namba, with four services available now](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>). [NIDA says it has issued 23,515,667 National Identification Numbers and produced 21,526,635 identity cards](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [NIDA reports 22.6 million national identification numbers generated and more than 22 million ID cards produced](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Biometric checks.** [The Deputy Minister said the centre can verify applicants and match fingerprints so that each applicant is uniquely identified](<https://dailynews.co.tz/national-ids-to-stay-under-centralised-printing/>).
