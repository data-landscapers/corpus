# DR Congo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [ONIP's legal expert described the FGP as holding biographic and biometric identity data and as the single source for producing all secure titles and documents](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). [Communication minister Patrick Muyaya said a population identification process is under way that will end in the grant of a national identity card](<https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir>). [Agence Ecofin reports the ministry presents RDC-PASS, launched in June by President Tshisekedi, as a tool to identify Congolese, now in a first operational phase since September 2026 with 50 enrolment agents trained and rollout to widen gradually](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).
