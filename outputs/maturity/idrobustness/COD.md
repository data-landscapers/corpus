# DR Congo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [ONIP resumed issuing the biometric national ID card in April 2024, in four pilot Kinshasa communes](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). [By September 2026 mass enrolment had not reached provinces such as Tshopo, a civil-society group said](<https://lumiernews.net/carte-didentite-nationale-en-rdc-ou-en-est-le-projet-trois-ans-apres-la-premiere-carte-remise-a-felix-tshisekedi/>). [ONIP describes its population file as holding biographic and biometric data](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). [It is the single source for all secure titles](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). [The communication minister said the identification under way will end in a national identity card](<https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir>). [RDC-PASS, launched in June 2026, entered a first operational phase that September](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>). [Fifty enrolment agents were trained and the rollout is to widen gradually](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>). On 11 September 2026 the interior minister [told the Council of Ministers that ONIP's mass identification would launch in mid-December](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/16/rdc-lidentification-de-masse-de-la-population-annoncee-pour-mi-decembre-2026>), funding permitting.

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest fact: [in August 2024 ONIP described its population file as holding biographic and biometric data](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest fact: [in August 2024 ONIP described its population file, the Fichier general de la population](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).
