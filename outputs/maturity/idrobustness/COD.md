## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Communication minister Patrick Muyaya said a population identification process is under way that will end in the grant of a national identity card](<https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir>). [Agence Ecofin reports the ministry presents RDC-PASS, launched in June by President Tshisekedi, as a tool to identify Congolese, now in a first operational phase since September 2026 with 50 enrolment agents trained and rollout to widen gradually](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3009-142019-rdc-strategies-identite-numerique-et-cybersecurite-au-bilan-d-un-an-de-reformes>). [ONIP handed the first national ID card to the President on 2023-06-30 as the start of a national process; a civil-society group says mass enrolment has not reached provinces such as Tshopo and demands a delivery timetable](<https://lumiernews.net/carte-didentite-nationale-en-rdc-ou-en-est-le-projet-trois-ans-apres-la-premiere-carte-remise-a-felix-tshisekedi/>).
