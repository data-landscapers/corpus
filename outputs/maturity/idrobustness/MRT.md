# Mauritania

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Tech Rek reports that the Houwiyeti app of the national population registry and secure titles agency has about 1.5 million users and serves as the national digital identity infrastructure that public and private services use to verify citizens' identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [The Interior Minister told the National Assembly that under the 2025 legal reform enrolment in the biometric population register is permanent and open, with 36,516 citizens registered in under a year](<https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/>). [UNHCR says registered refugees in Hodh Chargui receive a National Identification Number, enabling access to essential services with ANRPTS](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/a6553726-0803-553f-8c28-d41f409e0d65/UNHCR%20Mauritania_Hodh%20Chargui%20Factsheet%20-%20January%202026%20%281%29.pdf>). [The agency ANRPTS said the Houwiyeti system began operating on 2 January 2024, letting citizens and resident foreigners open an account and issue a digital identity, while ID-card services stay blocked until regulatory texts are aligned](<https://alwiam.info/ar/34035>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>).
