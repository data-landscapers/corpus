# Niger

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Niger launched a new biometric national identity card in 2026, with President Tiani enrolled first](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1526691/from-ecowas-to-aes-niger-completes-its-passport.html>). [Enrolment for the card, the CNIB AES, opened in Agadez, with fingerprints taken locally and cards printed in Niamey](<https://www.journalduniger.com/agadez-lancement-enrolement-carte-identite-biometrique-cnib-aes/>). [The card is valid for five years](<https://www.journalduniger.com/agadez-lancement-enrolement-carte-identite-biometrique-cnib-aes/>). [In October 2026 the director general of territorial administration said fewer than 3 million people held the biometric card](<https://www.lesahel.org/sensibilisation-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-la-caravane-nationale-fait-halte-a-niamey-pour-cloturer-sa-campagne/>). [Decree 2026-242 created ANEI and announced a National Population Register giving each citizen a unique identifier](<https://www.lesahel.org/dosso-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-letat-civil/>). [In June 2026 the WURI-Niger project said it was preparing to buy an integrated biometric identification system for the register](<https://www.wuri.ne/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Early_Market_Engagement_Notice_WURI_Niger_EN.pdf>).

**Biometric checks.** On 25 April 2026 the interior ministry [opened general enrolment for the AES biometric card at Yantala police station in Niamey](<https://anp.ne/niger-lancement-officiel-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-aes-a-niamey/>). Enrolment is to spread to all 20 Niamey stations and then the country. It [captures fingerprints, face, voice and iris](<https://anp.ne/niger-lancement-officiel-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-aes-a-niamey/>), which the authorities say prevents identity theft and guarantees each citizen's uniqueness.

**Security certified or audited.** No certification, audit or assurance level has been reported for the system. The nearest held fact is [the April 2026 account of biometric enrolment](<https://anp.ne/niger-lancement-officiel-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-aes-a-niamey/>).
