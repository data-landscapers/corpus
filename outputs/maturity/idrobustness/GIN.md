# Guinea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** Guinea is enrolling people for biometric documents. In March 2026 ID Tech Wire reported that [enrolment for the biometric national ID card had begun](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>) in Sanoyah commune. It was free for first-time applicants aged 16 and over, on MOSIP and TECH5 infrastructure. In July 2026 the technical partner, MGG, said [enrolment centres of the civil status reform were operating](<https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/>). The civil registry office, ONECI, [is setting up a national register of natural persons](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>). In September 2026 the territorial administration minister [opened work on extending the register](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>) and on a platform to verify identities.

**Biometric checks.** In April 2025 the government [received 3,000 biometric enrolment kits](<https://gouvernement.gov.gn/le-premier-ministre-receptionne-les-kits-denrolement-biometrique/>) for the register and for ID cards. It said the kits, with fingerprint sensors and iris recognition, guarantee that each citizen is unique. Enrolment of people aged 10 and over [was launched on 15 April 2025](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/04/en-avant-pour-le-recensement-biometrique-par-sayon-mara/>), in a first phase of 45 days.

**Security certified or audited.** The nearest fact is [the decree of April 2025 on the register](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>). It defines the register as a database of biographic and biometric data, fingerprints and photographs, kept to identify people.

***Also on file***. [FUGAS](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3011-50979-guinea-claims-26-9m-saved-since-2024-with-biometric-audit-of-public-workforce>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the system checks fingerprints, face or iris to stop one person enrolling twice. None reports a certification, audit or assurance level for it.
