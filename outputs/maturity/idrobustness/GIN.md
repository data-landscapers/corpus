# Guinea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Enrolment centres of the biometric civil status reform are operating in Guinea, with MGG as the state's technical partner, and users are being enrolled for biometric documents, which the authorities announced as free, per MGG](<https://www.infosbruts.com/etat-civil-biometrique-en-guinee-mgg-denonce-des-pratiques-de-racket-et-appelle-a-une-reaction-des-autorites/>). [ID Tech Wire reports that biometric national ID card enrolment, free for first-time applicants aged 16 and over, began in Sanoyah commune on MOSIP and TECH5 infrastructure](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>). [A workshop on extending the RNPP and building an identity verification and validation platform was launched by the territorial administration minister, who asked for its first phase before end-2026](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>). [Jurist Sayon Mara writes that PN-RAVEC biometric enrolment of people aged 10 and over was officially launched on 15 April 2025, in a first phase of 45 days, to build the RNPP register of biographic and biometric data](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/04/en-avant-pour-le-recensement-biometrique-par-sayon-mara/>).
