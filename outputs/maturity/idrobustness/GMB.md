# Gambia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The government says a mass registration exercise with mobile teams is enrolling Gambians on new 10-year national ID cards, said Information Minister Ceesay](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-steps-up-to-tackle-id-card-passport-delays>). [Public registration for the new biometric national ID card opened on 4 August 2026 in Greater Banjul under the National Identity Management System launched 30 June 2026, with phased expansion to other regions from 7 September 2026](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>). [The Ministry of Interior launched nationwide issuance of a new 10-year biometric National Identity Card on 2026-08-04, with enrolment at six centres on the first day](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gambia-rolls-out-new-10-year-national-identity-cards/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The government says the system combines a national identity register with biometric technology to reduce duplicate identities and fraud](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>).
