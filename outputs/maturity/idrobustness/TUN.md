# Tunisia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [A ministry circular makes the unique identifier of the national digital identity system E-Houwiya the exclusive reference for new digital services, after a pilot dematerialising public hydraulic domain authorisations through it](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). [The playbook describes the unique citizen identifier register as in operation, holding biographic data, civil status and CIN data, with a stated maturity of consolidation phase](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [The deck presents the IUC register as Tunisia's national unique citizen identifier system under the Ministry of the Interior, used by services with Mobile ID](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>). [The minister said in April 2025 that work on the biometric identity card and passport was being accelerated](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/sofiane-hmissi-annonce-le-lancement-du-portail-unique-des-services-administratifs-au-debut-du-mois-de-mai/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system.
