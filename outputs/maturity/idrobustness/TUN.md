# Tunisia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [A ministry circular makes the unique identifier of the national digital identity system E-Houwiya the exclusive reference for new digital services, after a pilot dematerialising public hydraulic domain authorisations through it](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). [The law states the national ID card, carrying a card number, is issued by Ministry of the Interior services after fingerprint and photo capture, with a new encrypted-chip card replacing earlier cards on renewal](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>). [ID Tech reports a ministerial working session set a target to start the biometric ID card rollout in the first half of 2025, made possible by Organic Laws 22 and 23 of 2024](<https://idtechwire.com/tunisia-to-get-biometric-ids-in-h1-of-2025>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>).
