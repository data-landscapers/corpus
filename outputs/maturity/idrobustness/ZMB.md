# Zambia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [About 1.3 million citizens have been enrolled onto the National Registration Information System (INRIS), per the Home Affairs spokesperson](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>). [ITWeb says biometric enrolment stands at roughly 1.5 million people and the Smart Zambia Institute has said digital identity cards will be issued by the end of 2026, with digital identity still in its implementation journey](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [The Zambian government through the Smart Zambia Institute is procuring a system integrator to deploy, customise and integrate a MOSIP-based digital ID system, with full rollout targeted by the end of 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).
