# Zambia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [About 1.3 million citizens have been enrolled onto the National Registration Information System (INRIS), per the Home Affairs spokesperson](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>). [ITWeb says biometric enrolment stands at roughly 1.5 million people and the Smart Zambia Institute has said digital identity cards will be issued by the end of 2026, with digital identity still in its implementation journey](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [The Zambian government through the Smart Zambia Institute is procuring a system integrator to deploy, customise and integrate a MOSIP-based digital ID system, with full rollout targeted by the end of 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>). [The Smart Zambia Institute's request for proposals, issued 3 December 2025 under the IDA-financed Digital Zambia Acceleration Project, procures a system integrator to develop and deploy the foundational digital ID, backend infrastructure and enrolment kits](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00412626>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).
