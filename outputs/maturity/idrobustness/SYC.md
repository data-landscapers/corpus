# Seychelles

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Department of Immigration and Civil Status issues National Identity Cards valid for ten years, free on first issue for Seychellois and SCR300 for expatriates, per its FAQ](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>). [The Seychelles ICS assigns a National Identity Number and, from 17 September 2024, issues permit holders a virtual ID card on the SeyID platform; Seychellois abroad can also register for it](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/launch-of-new-digital-solutions-for-public-services>). [The Civil Status Division prepares and issues Identity Cards to all residents under the Civil Status Act and the ID Card Act](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>).

***Also on file***. [SeyID virtual ID](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/civil-status/national-identity-card>); [SEYID](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/public-announcement>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics.
