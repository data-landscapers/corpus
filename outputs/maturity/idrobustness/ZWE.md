# Zimbabwe

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Civil Registry Department issues national identity documents, per the home affairs minister](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [The Civil Registry Department issues national identity cards, per the Home Affairs minister](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/civil-registry-department-to-computerise-sub-offices/>). [Biometric Update reports the government concluded an upgrade of the national population registry as the base for a national digital ID, with issuance announced but no confirmed start date](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>). [Registrar-General Henry Machiri said the Civil Registry Department was issuing national ID cards from computerised offices, with machines each able to produce at least 200 cards a day, and that the ZPRS database holds each individual's national ID number](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/issuance-of-id-cards-going-on-smoothly-rg/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>).
