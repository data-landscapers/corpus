# Namibia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs says the new electronic ID card is being rolled out nationwide and issued free to first-time applicants and renewals](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>). [Existing identity cards stay legally valid until withdrawn by gazette, and the E-ID is built on the national population register](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). [The planned digital ID rollout, set for September 2026, was delayed because the legal framework needed for implementation was not yet finalized](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>). [The home affairs ministry's deputy executive director said the new e-ID, with a chip, thumb print and facial image, is to be rolled out in September 2026, renewable after 10 years](<https://www.namibian.com.na/new-e-id-expiry-date-to-boost-security/>).
