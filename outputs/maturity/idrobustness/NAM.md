# Namibia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs says the new electronic ID card is being rolled out nationwide](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>). It is free to first-time applicants and for renewals. [Existing identity cards stay legally valid until withdrawn by gazette, and the e-ID is built on the national population register](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). [The ministry said the e-ID, with a chip, thumb print and facial image, was to be rolled out in September 2026](<https://www.namibian.com.na/new-e-id-expiry-date-to-boost-security/>). [In October 2026 Biometric Update reported that the September rollout was delayed for lack of a final legal framework](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/namibia-mps-press-government-on-security-access-and-reach-of-planned-digital-id>). [Cabinet briefs published in November 2026 say the launch awaits regulations under the Civil Registration and Identification Act of 2024](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>).
