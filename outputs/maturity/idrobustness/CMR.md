# Cameroon

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Yaounde 5 mayor handed out 4,000 free national identity cards on 27 July 2026, after DGSN police officers worked on site to clear double-identity cases some people had been blocked by for over fifteen years](<https://tribunedelinfo.com/de-la-promesse-a-la-cni-en-main-4000-beneficiaires-soulages-a-yaounde-5/>). [Issuance of the new biometric national ID card, with an embedded chip, began on 3 March 2025 after pre-enrolment from 17 February and biometric capture from 24 February; applicants report collecting cards within about 48 hours](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/cameroonians-ecstatic-as-issuance-of-new-national-id-card-takes-off>). [Cameroon launched its new biometric national ID card system in February 2025, and production centres in Douala and Garoua issue cards, about 2,000 a day at Garoua](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tribunedelinfo.com/de-la-promesse-a-la-cni-en-main-4000-beneficiaires-soulages-a-yaounde-5/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tribunedelinfo.com/de-la-promesse-a-la-cni-en-main-4000-beneficiaires-soulages-a-yaounde-5/>).
