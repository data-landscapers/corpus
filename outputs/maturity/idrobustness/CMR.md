# Cameroon

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Yaounde 5 mayor handed out 4,000 free national identity cards on 27 July 2026, after DGSN police officers worked on site to clear double-identity cases some people had been blocked by for over fifteen years](<https://tribunedelinfo.com/de-la-promesse-a-la-cni-en-main-4000-beneficiaires-soulages-a-yaounde-5/>). [VOA Afrique reports that production of the new biometric national ID cards within 48 hours is effective in Cameroon, with the first applicants having received their cards, under a presidential decree of 28 February 2025](<https://www.voaafrique.com/a/les-cartes-d-identit%C3%A9-biom%C3%A9triques-d%C3%A9livr%C3%A9es-en-48h-d%C3%A9sormais-au-cameroun-/8004266.html>). [Cameroon launched its new biometric national ID card system in February 2025, and production centres in Douala and Garoua issue cards, about 2,000 a day at Garoua](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/new-biometric-id-card-center-opens-for-citizens-in-northern-cameroon>). [Biometric Update reports Cameroon's new national ID card system went live on 17 February 2025 with online pre-enrolment, biometric capture a week later and the first cards given to applicants on 3 March 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/cameroon-opens-new-id-card-production-centre-as-issuance-gains-traction>).
