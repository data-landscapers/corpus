# Sao Tome and Principe

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [A campaign running until 15 March 2026 gave a free Bilhete de Identidade to citizens over 17 without a valid one, implemented by the Direcao-Geral dos Registos e Notariado with PReSE support](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe>). [The Direcao-Geral dos Registos e Notariado is issuing the free Bilhete de Identidade through a brigade in Porto Alegre, Caue district, after Ilheu das Rolas, with EU-funded PReSE supplying Starlink and a generator](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/01/22/51507/campanha-de-atribuicao-gratuita-do-bilhete-de-identidade-iniciou-se-no-ilheu-das-rolas-e-decorre-em-porto-alegre/>). [A tender notice of 14 May 2026 by AFAP seeks servers for the data centre and disaster recovery of the digital identity system](<https://www.telanon.info/anuncios/2026/05/14/52921/afap-fornecimento-de-servidores-para-o-data-center-e-recuperacao-de-desastre-para-o-sistema-de-identidade-digital/>).
