# Rwanda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [A March 2025 deck lists NIDA's common identification system as live](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Talking-Interoperability-18-Rwanda_vFinal_Updated.pdf>). It covers above 98% of the eligible population aged 16 and over. [NIDA's director general referred to the current physical ID cards](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>). They are to be phased out in June 2027. [The FY2026/27 Finance Law appropriates RWF 2 287 611 846 for MINICT programme B9 National Identification](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>). That includes RWF 1 245 513 610 for Identity Card Production and Distribution. It also includes RWF 925 913 239 for National ID System Infrastructure and Security. [IremboGov's help article says Rwanda is preparing to roll out a new Digital National ID system](<https://support.new.irembo.gov.rw/en/support/solutions/articles/47001283560-how-to-apply-for-single-digital-id-adult-self-pre-enrollment->). It requires pre-enrolment before the official launch. Biometric capture and issue are still to follow. [NIDA resumed Single Digital ID registration in all districts on 17 November 2026, to run to 16 January 2027](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/40514/news/technology/digital-id-registration-resumes-in-all-districts>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 photo, fingerprint and iris data for the new card were being collected into a central database](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/06/17/rwanda-la-carte-biometrique-qui-pourrait-changer-la-vie-des-refugies/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest fact: [by mid-August 2026 NIDA had registered more than 8 million people for the Digital ID](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>).
