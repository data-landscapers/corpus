# Rwanda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [NIDA's director general referred to the current physical ID cards, which are to be phased out in June 2027](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>). [The FY2026/27 Finance Law appropriates RWF 2 287 611 846 for MINICT programme B9 National Identification, including RWF 1 245 513 610 for Identity Card Production and Distribution and RWF 925 913 239 for National ID System Infrastructure and Security](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>). [IremboGov's help article says Rwanda is preparing to roll out a new Digital National ID system and requires pre-enrolment before the official launch, with biometric capture and issue still to follow](<https://support.new.irembo.gov.rw/en/support/solutions/articles/47001283560-how-to-apply-for-single-digital-id-adult-self-pre-enrollment->).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>).
