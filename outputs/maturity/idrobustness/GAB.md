# Gabon

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Interior Minister told the National Assembly that 164,944 CNIE cards have been produced and 147,224 collected by holders](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>). They come from the enrolment campaign begun in 2024. [Gabon launched a new generation national digital ID system in July 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/gabon-ramping-up-digital-id-interoperability-infrastructure-implementation>), which the government says it is now moving to operationalise. [Gabon Digital, financed by the World Bank, plans a biometric register of persons covering the whole territory](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-le-gabon-face-au-defi-de-la-modernisation/>). It also plans a national digital identification system, per the workshop's component 2 coordinator. [The sixth e-Gabon-SIS steering committee on 8 September 2026 reviewed the deployment of a National Digital Identity System](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>), per ANINF. [The governor of Ogooué-Maritime launched issuance of the chip-based electronic national identity card (CNIE) in Port-Gentil on 13 December 2024](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-lancement-de-letablissement-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-electronique-dans-logooue-maritime/>). Applications, made through the DGDI, require a Personal Identification Number (NIP) attestation.
