# Gabon

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Interior Minister told the National Assembly that 164,944 CNIE cards have been produced and 147,224 collected by holders, from the enrolment campaign begun in 2024](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>). [Gabon launched a new generation national digital ID system in July 2024, which the government says it is now moving to operationalise](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/gabon-ramping-up-digital-id-interoperability-infrastructure-implementation>). [Gabon Digital, financed by the World Bank, plans a biometric register of persons covering the whole territory and a national digital identification system, per the workshop's component 2 coordinator](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-le-gabon-face-au-defi-de-la-modernisation/>). [The sixth e-Gabon-SIS steering committee on 8 September 2026 reviewed the deployment of a National Digital Identity System, per ANINF](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>). [The governor of Ogooué-Maritime launched issuance of the chip-based electronic national identity card (CNIE) in Port-Gentil on 13 December 2024, applications requiring a Personal Identification Number (NIP) attestation, through the DGDI](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-lancement-de-letablissement-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-electronique-dans-logooue-maritime/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>).
