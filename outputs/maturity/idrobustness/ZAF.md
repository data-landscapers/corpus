# South Africa

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The physical smart ID card is available, with 515 bank branches already offering walk-in smart ID replacements, and it will remain available beside the digital ID](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [The minister says a working prototype of the smartphone digital ID is built, the first credential was issued to him at the demonstration, and pilot work is underway](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-unveils-digital-id-prototype-issues-first-credential>). [Home Affairs says the Smart ID card is in issue and 515 bank branches take replacement applications, 822,500 people having used the service (Minister Schreiber)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-unveils-digital-id-prototype-issues-first-credential>).

**Biometric checks.** [Minister Schreiber said HANIS and ABIS support the integrity of the National Population Register through biometric verification and the detection of duplicate, fraudulent, cancelled, blocked or otherwise invalid identity records](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>).
