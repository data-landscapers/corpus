# South Africa

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Home Affairs and Sars signed a digital pact in April 2025, TechCentral reports](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-digital-id-could-change-how-you-pay/287578/>). [The digital ID's core infrastructure will be built in Sars's hosting environment](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-digital-id-could-change-how-you-pay/287578/>). [Treasury has set aside R150-million for the MyMzansi app, with a working digital ID due in 2027/2028](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-digital-id-could-change-how-you-pay/287578/>). [The physical smart ID card stays available beside it, with 515 bank branches offering replacements](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [Home Affairs had delivered Smart ID cards to 1,002,773 citizens through 669 bank branches by 26 October 2026](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-milestone-one-million-smart-ids-delivered-seven-months-26-oct>). [By 11 November 2026 applications were taken at 753 bank branches, 967 sites in all](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-leon-schreiber-announces-expansion-smart-id-replacement-services-11>). [A working prototype of the smartphone digital ID is built, and the minister holds the first credential](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-unveils-digital-id-prototype-issues-first-credential>). [Pilot work is under way](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-unveils-digital-id-prototype-issues-first-credential>).
