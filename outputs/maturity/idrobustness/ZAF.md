# South Africa

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [TechCentral reports that home affairs and Sars signed a digital pact in April 2025, the digital ID's core infrastructure will be built in Sars's hosting environment, and Treasury has set aside R150-million for the MyMzansi app, with an operational digital ID targeted for 2027/2028](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-digital-id-could-change-how-you-pay/287578/>). [The physical smart ID card is available, with 515 bank branches already offering walk-in smart ID replacements, and it will remain available beside the digital ID](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [Home Affairs said on 26 October 2026 that it had delivered Smart ID cards to 1,002,773 citizens through 669 bank branches since 9 March 2026](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-milestone-one-million-smart-ids-delivered-seven-months-26-oct>). [The minister says a working prototype of the smartphone digital ID is built, the first credential was issued to him at the demonstration, and pilot work is underway](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/south-africa-unveils-digital-id-prototype-issues-first-credential>).

**Biometric checks.** [Minister Schreiber said HANIS and ABIS support the integrity of the National Population Register through biometric verification and the detection of duplicate, fraudulent, cancelled, blocked or otherwise invalid identity records](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>).
