# Mali

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** Mali gives each person a national identification number, NINA. In 2025 the civil status directorate, DNEC, said [more than 20 million NINA had been attributed](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>), from a biometric file built from civil status. On 13 February 2024 President Goïta [launched Mali Kura Biométrie](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>), a national system replacing the old RAVEC file. The old system had been blocked from April 2023, which slowed the production of biometric ID cards. In October 2026 [a report on cleaning the electoral roll](<https://bamada.net/fichier-electoral-lheure-du-grand-nettoyage>) named NINA as each voter's unique identifier.

**Biometric checks.** In January 2025 L'Essor [described the biometric civil status database](<https://lessor.ml/posts/loi-electorale-les-articles-52-57-et-70-modifies-677641a527512>) behind NINA. It holds photographs and fingerprints. The service that manages the database assigns the definitive NINA.

**Security certified or audited.** The nearest fact is [DNEC's 2025 presentation](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). It gives the count of NINA attributed and names the biometric file they come from.

***Also on file***. [E-AES biometric passport](<https://idtechwire.com/mali-launches-e-aes-biometric-passport-taps-chinas-emptech-in-digital-sovereignty-move>); [e-Passeport AES](<https://www.securite.gouv.ml/index.php/e-passeport-aes-securise-le-ministre-de-la-securise-et-de-la-protection-civile-lance-officiellement-la-reprise-de-la-production>); [AES common biometric passport](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/sahel-states-under-military-rule-unveil-common-biometric-passport-jan-29>); [RAVEC / NINA biometric file](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the system checks fingerprints, face or iris to stop one person enrolling twice. None reports a certification, audit or assurance level for it.
