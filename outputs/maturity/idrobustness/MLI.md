# Mali

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [DNEC says more than 20 million NINA have been attributed to Malians through the CTDEC, from a biometric file built from civil status](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). [The government says President Goita officially launched the new sovereign Mali Kura Biométrie system replacing the old RAVEC file on 13 February 2024, after the old system's blockage from April 2023 had slowed biometric ID card production](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
