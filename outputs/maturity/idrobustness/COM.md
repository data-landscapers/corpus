# Comoros

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Al-Watwan reports that GenKey took over production of Comorian biometric documents, passports and national identity cards, on 31 March 2026 after Semlex held the concession from 2007](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/production-de-documents-biom%C3%A9triques-genkey-succ%C3%A8de-officiellement-%C3%A0-semlex.html>). [Biometric Update reports Comoros selected GenKey to produce biometric passports and national ID cards, replacing Semlex, which produced biometric IDs from 2007; issuance suspended until 26 March 2026 during the change](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/genkey-takes-over-biometric-passport-national-id-card-production-in-comoros>). [On 25 March 2026 Comoros authorities validated preparatory studies in Moroni for digitising the civil registry, introducing a unique Personal Identification Number as the cornerstone of citizen identity, under the Amecc programme funded by the French Embassy with UNICEF support](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-advances-digital-identity-reform-with-nationwide-registry-overhaul>).
