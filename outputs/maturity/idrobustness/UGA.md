# Uganda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) keeps the National Identification Register](<https://www.independent.co.ug/all-set-as-33-million-ugandan-await-mass-national-id-enrollment-renewal/>). [In May 2025 the minister said it held biometric and biographic data on over 27.7 million Ugandans](<https://www.independent.co.ug/all-set-as-33-million-ugandan-await-mass-national-id-enrollment-renewal/>). [NIRA was then preparing a mass enrolment and card renewal](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-prepares-for-mass-registration-id-renewal/>). [National Identification Numbers are backed by the biometric data NIRA holds](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). [The SAGE social pension uses the date of birth on the card to decide eligibility](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). [In October 2026 Lwengo district leaders closed a NIRA office over alleged extortion](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379038/lwengo-leaders-close-nira-office-over-extortion-claims>).

**Biometric checks.** [NIRA deduplicated multiple biometric databases during the migration and found about 200,000 duplicates over about seven months](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).
