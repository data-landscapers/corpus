# Uganda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Uganda Business News reports National Identification Numbers are in use and backed by biometric information held by the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA)](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). [Nile Post reports that residents of Lwengo District collect national identity cards at a National Identification and Registration Authority office, which district leaders closed on 2026-10-16 over alleged extortion](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379038/lwengo-leaders-close-nira-office-over-extortion-claims>). [NIRA runs a national identification registry issuing National Identification Numbers and identity cards, free of charge, and it announced a December 2026 exercise to register school-going children without NINs](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [NIRA issues National Identification Numbers, free for eligible applicants, and keeps the National Identification Register](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-intensifies-birth-registration-drive-lau-NV_234727_062026>).

**Biometric checks.** [NIRA deduplicated multiple biometric databases during the migration and found about 200,000 duplicates over about seven months](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system.
