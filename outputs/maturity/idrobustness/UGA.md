# Uganda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [NIRA runs a national identification registry issuing National Identification Numbers and identity cards, free of charge, and it announced a December 2026 exercise to register school-going children without NINs](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [New Vision reports that the Uganda Security Printing Company prints Uganda's National Identification cards, which carry more than 20 security features in seven layers, with the features supplied by NIRA](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/secure-documents-to-be-printed-locally-NV_241521_092026>). [NIRA, mandated to maintain the National Identification Register, has printed about 15.4 million new ID cards and issued about 10 million, its executive director told Parliament](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/>). [NIRA issues National Identification Numbers, free for eligible applicants, and keeps the National Identification Register](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-intensifies-birth-registration-drive-lau-NV_234727_062026>).

**Biometric checks.** [NIRA deduplicated multiple biometric databases during the migration and found about 200,000 duplicates over about seven months](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system.
