# Uganda

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Development and the SAGE head describe national identity cards, registered through NIRA, as carrying each person's recorded date of birth and being used to decide SAGE eligibility](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). [Uganda Business News reports National Identification Numbers are in use and backed by biometric information held by the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA)](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). [Nile Post reports that residents of Lwengo District collect national identity cards at a National Identification and Registration Authority office, which district leaders closed on 2026-10-16 over alleged extortion](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379038/lwengo-leaders-close-nira-office-over-extortion-claims>).

**Biometric checks.** [NIRA deduplicated multiple biometric databases during the migration and found about 200,000 duplicates over about seven months](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).
