# Central African Republic

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Corbeau News Centrafrique reports that national identity cards (CNI) are being issued through police and registry offices in Ndele and other towns including Bangui, with applicants demanding payment of 100,000 to 200,000 CFA francs](<https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-business-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-reprend-de-plus-belle-a-ndele/>). [Journal de Bangui reports that Al-Madina's team is enrolling applicants for the secure national identity card in Birao, capturing biometric data, photos and personal information, after Bouar and Berberati](<https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-carte-nationale-didentite-la-societe-al-madina-deploie-sa-mission-a-birao>). [The $13m SNID, built around a unique identification number, has feasibility studies done and an international tender and procurement ahead, with implementation 2027-2032](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>).
