# Central African Republic

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Corbeau News Centrafrique reports that national identity cards (CNI) are being issued through police and registry offices in Ndele and other towns, including Bangui](<https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-le-business-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-reprend-de-plus-belle-a-ndele/>). It reports applicants demanding payment of 100,000 to 200,000 CFA francs. [Journal de Bangui reports that Al-Madina's team is enrolling applicants for the secure national identity card in Birao](<https://www.journaldebangui.com/rca-carte-nationale-didentite-la-societe-al-madina-deploie-sa-mission-a-birao>), after Bouar and Berberati. It captures biometric data, photos and personal information. [The $13m SNID, built around a unique identification number, has had feasibility studies done](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>). An international tender and procurement are ahead, with implementation in 2027-2032.

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics.
