# Chad

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [SIGPTS assigns a unique National Identification Number to registered individuals after enrolment and deduplication, per We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [The ANATS site states that after enrolment and deduplication the SIGPTS assigns every individual a unique randomly generated NNI](<https://www.anats.td/>). This includes refugees and resident or transiting foreigners, and the site lists 29 enrolment centres. [Tchadinfos reports the national secure-titles agency ANATS said each beneficiary would receive a secure document tied to the Numero national d'identification (NNI)](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/07/27/societe-lancement-dune-operation-denrolement-des-personnes-handicapees-exercant-des-activites-generatrices-de-revenus-sur-laxe-ndjamena-kousseri/>). The operation was launched on 27 July 2026. [An ANATS circular of 26 September 2025 says school leavers are enrolling to obtain their NNI, which the agency's centres issue](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/09/26/lanats-simplifie-lacces-aux-nni-pour-les-bacheliers-tchadiens-a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-universitaire/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The SIGPTS assigns each person a unique NNI only after enrolment and deduplication, the ANATS site says in 2026](<https://www.anats.td/>). [The system launched in 2020 able to deduplicate biometrics against 15 million records](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help>). [Each search took ten seconds, Biometric Update reported in 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help>).
