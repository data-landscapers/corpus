# Chad

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [SIGPTS assigns a unique National Identification Number to registered individuals after enrolment and deduplication, per We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [The ANATS site states that the SIGPTS, after enrolment and deduplication, assigns every individual, including refugees and resident or transiting foreigners, a unique randomly generated NNI, with 29 enrolment centres listed](<https://www.anats.td/>). [Tchadinfos reports the national secure-titles agency ANATS said each beneficiary would receive a secure document tied to the Numero national d'identification (NNI), in an operation launched 27 July 2026](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/07/27/societe-lancement-dune-operation-denrolement-des-personnes-handicapees-exercant-des-activites-generatrices-de-revenus-sur-laxe-ndjamena-kousseri/>). [ANATS circular of 26 September 2025 says school leavers are enrolling to obtain their NNI, which the agency's centres issue](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/09/26/lanats-simplifie-lacces-aux-nni-pour-les-bacheliers-tchadiens-a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-universitaire/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The system launched in 2020 able to deduplicate biometrics against a database of 15 million people within 10 seconds, per ChadInfos (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/chad-issues-biometric-ids-to-thousands-of-refugees-with-idemia-tech-unhcrs-help>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>).

***Also on file***. [biometric ID cards for refugees](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/chad-uses-national-biometric-registry-to-boost-govt-systems-interoperability>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics.
