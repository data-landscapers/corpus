# Kenya

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The National Registration Bureau issues national identity cards. Live capture units [send applicants' data and photographs to Nairobi](<https://peopledaily.digital/news/parliament-probes-delays-in-issuance-of-ids-birth-certificates-in-elgeyo-marakwet>), a parliamentary committee heard in October 2026. The principal secretary said [600 such devices let a new card be issued in ten days](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>), or one day in Nairobi. First applications are free. The bureau [registered over 600,000 people once extra vetting in border counties ended](<https://radiogeneration.co.ke/news/114038/600000-secure-ids-after-government-drops-extra-vetting-in-border-counties>). On 5 November 2026 the immigration department [told parliament that at least 557,000 printed cards were uncollected](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/state-turns-to-chiefs-e-citizen-to-trace-owners-of-uncollected-ids-5660290>). It said it can print 20,000 cards a day. [Over 6 million Maisha cards had been produced since October 2023](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>), the deck says.

**Biometric checks.** The Maisha system issues a unique lifetime number and a card on top of the IPRS-AFIS infrastructure. In May 2026 The Elephant reported that [AFIS checks fingerprints and flags duplicate or fraudulent registrations](<https://www.theelephant.info/investigations/2026/05/28/project-profile-the-in-between-lives-of-the-double-registered/>) as ID applications are processed. An upgrade adding face and iris was under way. The government's deck for ID4Africa 2026 lists [AFIS deduplication in the identity lifecycle](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>), with two fingerprints held on the card chip.

**Security certified or audited.** No certification, audit or assurance level has been reported for the system.
