# Eswatini

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs says national ID card production and issuance are taking place at ten service centres](<https://times.co.sz/31079/news/id-card-services-resume-in-boost-for-citizens/>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says production of national ID cards has resumed and services are available at ten government service centres](<https://independentnews.co.sz/41508/news/home-affairs-resumes-id-card-production/>). [The World Bank concept note for Digital Eswatini (US$65m) proposes deploying digital ID as a DPI building block and buying biometric equipment for government e-services, calling digital ID currently underdeveloped](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says national identity cards were introduced in 2005 under the Identification Order of 1998 and the systems were later automated (2005, before the three-year window)](<https://40years.gov.sz/home-affairs-at-the-heart-of-the-nation/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://times.co.sz/31079/news/id-card-services-resume-in-boost-for-citizens/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://times.co.sz/31079/news/id-card-services-resume-in-boost-for-citizens/>).
