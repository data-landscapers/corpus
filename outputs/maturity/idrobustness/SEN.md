# Senegal

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Vipeoples reports that Senegal's ECOWAS biometric identity card is produced under a contract with a Malaysian firm, with bill 21/2026 amending the founding law before the National Assembly on 29 September 2026](<https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html>). [Bill 21-2026 amending the law that instituted the ECOWAS biometric identity card, to extend validity of cards in circulation, was sent back to committee by the National Assembly](<https://senego.com/assemblee-coup-de-theatre-le-projet-de-loi-sur-la-carte-didentite-retourne-en-commission_2007288.html>). [Senegal's DAF director Ibrahima Dieng said national identity-card production resumed after the February 2026 cyberattack and that the databases used to produce national identity cards are available, intact and unchanged](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/senegal-daf-acknowledges-cyberattack-assures-no-data-was-compromised/>).
