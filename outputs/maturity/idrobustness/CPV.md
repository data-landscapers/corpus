# Cape Verde

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Biometric Update reports Cabo Verde issues a Unique Identification Number to every child born, within a national ID system its presenters call the backbone of future authentication](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). [IMIJ states SNIAC has contributed to issuing about 631,720 national identity cards (CNI), 513,862 electronic passports and 14,235 residence permits since its implementation](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-24>). [The Autentika portal says citizens authenticate to services with the smart-card Cartão Nacional de Identificação (CNI) and its PIN, and that the CNI is activated in person at registry offices, the Casa do Cidadão and consulates](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).
