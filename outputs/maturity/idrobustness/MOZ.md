# Mozambique

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The government extended free issuance of the national identity card (BI), with the birth certificate and personal registration booklet, to 2027 for children and adults, as announced by the President](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>). [Biometric Update, citing the National Directorate of Civil Identification (DNIC), reports 856,101 national ID cards issued between January and June 2026, with biometric data collected for issuance](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/mozambique-approves-new-national-id-framework-to-speed-card-issuance>). [ATDI is developing a national digital ID project as part of the digital government architecture, and the EDGE mobile digital ID procurement has a shortlist submitted to the World Bank with contracting planned for September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>).
