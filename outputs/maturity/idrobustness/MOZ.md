# Mozambique

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** By 20 March 2026, the World Bank said, [11,038,940 people were in the civil identification database](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>). About 11 million IDs had been issued, against a target of 17 million. The civil identification directorate [issued 856,101 cards from January to June 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/mozambique-approves-new-national-id-framework-to-speed-card-issuance>), taking biometric data for each. The President [extended free issue of the identity card to 2027](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>), for children and adults. A mobile digital ID is also being bought: [a shortlist went to the World Bank, with a contract planned for September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>).
