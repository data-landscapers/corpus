# Libya

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [In October 2026 the Attorney General's Office found 315,385 National Numbers granted contrary to law](<https://alwasat.ly/news/481677>). [It suspended 8,662 of them](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/libyas-attorney-general-reveals-315000-unlawfully-issued-national-id-numbers-citizenship-data>). [The Civil Status Authority has issued national numbers through a unified electronic system since 2011](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). [Each is meant as a unique reference for one citizen](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). [In 2026 officials were still arresting people for forging them](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [The Digital Identity Management System for Citizens has a Level 3 identity based on biometric data, described as the highest level of digital verification](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

**Security certified or audited.** [The Digital Identity Management System for Citizens has a Level 3 identity based on biometric data](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [The General Information Authority calls it the highest level of digital verification](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [In April 2026 it was in technical trials with the Central Bank of Libya and several banks](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).
