# Libya

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Attorney General's Office says a review found 315,385 National Numbers granted contrary to legislation and 8,662 National Numbers suspended, showing the national number system in operation](<https://alwasat.ly/news/481677>). [Libya's Attorney General Al-Siddiq Al-Sour said a review found more than 315,000 national identification numbers issued unlawfully, with 8,662 numbers suspended](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/libyas-attorney-general-reveals-315000-unlawfully-issued-national-id-numbers-citizenship-data>). [Al-Wasat reports that Libya's national number project, aimed at a unique reference number for each citizen, has issued and circulated national numbers through a unified electronic system run by the Civil Status Authority since 2011, and that officials in 2026 were still arresting people for forging them](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>).
