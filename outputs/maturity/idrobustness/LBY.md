# Libya

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [In October 2026 the Attorney General's Office found 315,385 National Numbers granted contrary to law](<https://alwasat.ly/news/481677>). [It suspended 8,662 of them](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/libyas-attorney-general-reveals-315000-unlawfully-issued-national-id-numbers-citizenship-data>). [The Civil Status Authority has issued national numbers through a unified electronic system since 2011](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). [Each is meant as a unique reference for one citizen](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). [In 2026 officials were still arresting people for forging them](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). In 2024 the Libya Herald said [the Civil Registry Authority issues a unique National ID Number to all Libyan nationals](<https://libyaherald.com/2024/09/pm-aldabaiba-follows-up-on-intilaqa-project-for-matching-civil-registry-electronic-data-with-paper-records>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the national numbers are checked by biometrics. The nearest held fact is a digital ID in trials. [Its Level 3 identity is based on biometric data](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

**Security certified or audited.** [The Digital Identity Management System for Citizens has a Level 3 identity based on biometric data](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [The General Information Authority calls it the highest level of digital verification](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). [In April 2026 it was in technical trials with the Central Bank of Libya and several banks](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

***Also on file***. [Civil Registry Authority database](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/libyas-attorney-general-reveals-315000-unlawfully-issued-national-id-numbers-citizenship-data>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the national numbers are checked for duplicates by biometrics. The assurance level is set for a digital ID still in trials, not one issued to the population.
