# Burkina Faso

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The Office national d'identification (ONI) [issues the AES biometric identity card, the CIB-AES, and during a campaign to 16 September 2026 delivered it within 48 hours at nine sites in Ouagadougou](<https://www.fasoamazone.net/2026/09/15/id-day-2026-loni-ouvre-plusieurs-sites-denrolement-a-ouagadougou-face-a-laffluence-pour-la-cib-aes/>). [It ran special enrolment operations in every region that month](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-internationale-de-lidentite-la-securisation-des-diplomes-au-coeur-de-la-viie-edition/>). [Its director general says enrolment is still mostly on paper](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-les-operations-detablissement-lancees-a-koudougou/>), with about 500 centres due mobile digital kits by the end of 2026. [From 16 October 2026 the Gendarmerie's brigades also produce the card](<https://aconews.net/burkina-faso-desormais-la-gendarmerie-nationale-produit-les-cib-aes-et-legalise-les-documents-administratifs/>). [In August 2026 the digital minister said mass enrolment for a unique electronic personal identifier would begin in the second half of the year](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/08/17/transformation-digitale-au-burkina-faso-le-secteur-injecte-142-milliards-de-fcfa-dans-le-plan-relance-pour-une-souverainete-numerique-accrue/>). [On 11 November 2026 President Traoré launched that mass enrolment](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/11/operation-denregistrement-de-masse-de-lidentifiant-unique-le-capitaine-ibrahim-traore-lance-loperation-denrolement/>). Each person receives a free, random 12-digit number, through 160 enrolment sites and mobile teams. [A pilot had enrolled over 16,000 people](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150706=>).

**Biometric checks.** [The ONI system checks for duplicate identities by matching fingerprints, the national police's 2026 CNIB page says](<https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib>). [It has matched faces too since 2009](<https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib>). Enrolment for the new identifier [takes a photograph, fingerprints and iris to eliminate multiple records](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/11/operation-denregistrement-de-masse-de-lidentifiant-unique-le-capitaine-ibrahim-traore-lance-loperation-denrolement/>), the Presidency says.
