# Burkina Faso

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Office national d'identification ran special enrolment operations in all regions around 2026-09-16, and its director general said it has a process to deliver the cards in a shorter time](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-internationale-de-lidentite-la-securisation-des-diplomes-au-coeur-de-la-viie-edition/>). [The ONI is enrolling citizens for the AES biometric identity card (CIB-AES) at a main centre and nine sites in Ouagadougou and delivering the card within 48 hours during the campaign to 16 September 2026](<https://www.fasoamazone.net/2026/09/15/id-day-2026-loni-ouvre-plusieurs-sites-denrolement-a-ouagadougou-face-a-laffluence-pour-la-cib-aes/>). [The ONI director general says the office is switching to a new platform, while enrolment is mostly on paper for now, with about 500 centres to get mobile digital kits by end 2026](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-les-operations-detablissement-lancees-a-koudougou/>). [From 16 October 2026 the Gendarmerie's territorial brigades also produce the CIB-AES; enrolment still requires a birth-certificate extract or nationality certificate](<https://aconews.net/burkina-faso-desormais-la-gendarmerie-nationale-produit-les-cib-aes-et-legalise-les-documents-administratifs/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The ONI system checks for duplicates by fingerprint matching and, since 2009, facial biometrics, to detect identity fraud (2009, before the three-year window)](<https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-internationale-de-lidentite-la-securisation-des-diplomes-au-coeur-de-la-viie-edition/>).
