# Somalia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The NIRA national identity card is issued at NIRA registration centres, and from 2026-10-01 the federal government required it for travel between regions, though it is not issued in Puntland where NIRA has no registration offices](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Oct/206408/puntland_passengers_blocked_from_flights_over_nira_id_requirement.aspx>). [Federal authorities have made the NIRA card mandatory for domestic air travel and NIRA centres issue it in Mogadishu, but there is no NIRA office in Puntland (Warsame)](<https://www.eurasiareview.com/03102026-from-e-visas-to-nira-mogadishus-bureaucratic-war-on-ordinary-travelers-oped/>). [Biometric Update reports that Somalia launched its biometric national ID system in 2023 and NIRA says about two million people have been enrolled (October 2026)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/somalia-enrolls-citizens-returning-from-yemen-for-biometric-national-ids>).

**Biometric checks.** [Fingerprints and a photograph are captured and all information is deduplicated before the NIN is issued, using fingerprint and facial recognition](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>). [NIRA lists fraud reduction via biometric de-duplication among the early results of the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).
