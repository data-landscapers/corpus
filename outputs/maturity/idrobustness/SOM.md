# Somalia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Somalia launched its biometric national ID in 2023, and NIRA says about two million people were enrolled by October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/somalia-enrolls-citizens-returning-from-yemen-for-biometric-national-ids>). [Citing the World Bank, the EU asylum agency says NIRA had issued about 700,000 cards by 31 March 2026](<https://www.euaa.europa.eu/somalia-displacement-mogadishu/42-cards-issued-and-nira-offices>). [It says over 600,000 more enrolments awaited deduplication in June 2026](<https://www.euaa.europa.eu/somalia-displacement-mogadishu/42-cards-issued-and-nira-offices>). [From 1 October 2026 the federal government required the card for travel between regions](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Oct/206408/puntland_passengers_blocked_from_flights_over_nira_id_requirement.aspx>). [NIRA has no registration office in Puntland, so the card is not issued there](<https://www.eurasiareview.com/03102026-from-e-visas-to-nira-mogadishus-bureaucratic-war-on-ordinary-travelers-oped/>).

**Biometric checks.** [Fingerprints and a photograph are captured and all information is deduplicated before the NIN is issued, using fingerprint and facial recognition](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>). [NIRA lists fraud reduction via biometric de-duplication among the early results of the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).
