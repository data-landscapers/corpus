# Somalia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The NIRA national identity card is issued at NIRA registration centres, and from 2026-10-01 the federal government required it for travel between regions, though it is not issued in Puntland where NIRA has no registration offices](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Oct/206408/puntland_passengers_blocked_from_flights_over_nira_id_requirement.aspx>). [Federal authorities have made the NIRA card mandatory for domestic air travel and NIRA centres issue it in Mogadishu, but there is no NIRA office in Puntland (Warsame)](<https://www.eurasiareview.com/03102026-from-e-visas-to-nira-mogadishus-bureaucratic-war-on-ordinary-travelers-oped/>). [The Somali Digest reports that the National Identification and Registration Authority issues an identity number, which the Deputy Prime Minister said Somalis should be required to use when opening a social media account](<https://thesomalidigest.com/the-ministry-of-information-wants-a-name-behind-every-somali-account/>).

**Biometric checks.** [Fingerprints and a photograph are captured and all information is deduplicated before the NIN is issued, using fingerprint and facial recognition](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).
