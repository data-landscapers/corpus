# Burundi

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The FY2026/27 procurement plan of Burundi's finance and digital economy ministry lists recruiting a contractor to set up a national digital identity system, BIF 8,902,999,980 on the state budget, by international open tender](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf>). [Burundi is setting up a national digital identity system that is to assign a unique identification number, backed by fingerprint and facial biometrics, according to We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>). [The Directorate General for territorial administration says the State is working on a national digital identity system with a unique identification number, using fingerprints and facial recognition](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). [President Evariste Ndayishimiye announced in December 2025 that the digital ID system is being tested in Zone Matara, Gitega province, with nationwide rollout to follow if the pilot succeeds](<https://communityvoice.bi/the-lack-of-digital-id-systems-threatens-the-digital-economy-of-burundi/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf>).
