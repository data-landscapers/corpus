# Burundi

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** Burundi is building a national digital identity system. It is to [assign a unique number backed by fingerprints and face](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>), says We Are Tech Africa. The territorial administration [describes the same system](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). Burundi Eco reported in March 2025 that [a pilot of biometric ID cards had begun in eleven communes](<https://burundi-eco.com/carte-didentite-biometrique-cette-fois-sera-la-bonne/>), including Gitega, Ngozi and Kayanza. The interior minister expected the card before the 2027 elections. In December 2025 the President said [the system was being tested in Zone Matara, Gitega province](<https://communityvoice.bi/the-lack-of-digital-id-systems-threatens-the-digital-economy-of-burundi/>). Nationwide rollout is to follow if the pilot succeeds. In May 2026 an interior ministry official said [Burundi was technically ready to produce the cards, with issuance waiting on a law](<https://lionweek.com/burundi-seeks-saudi-support-for-digital-id-and-data-centre-at-doha-telecoms-summit/>). [On 6 May 2026 the ministry presented the card and its unique number as a project not yet issued](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/2026/05/11/burundi-la-carte-didentite-biometrique-encore-loin-detre-une-realite/>), SOS Médias Burundi reports. The finance ministry's [procurement plan for 2026/27 lists a contractor to set up the system](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf>). It carries BIF 8,902,999,980 on the state budget, by international open tender.

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the finance and digital economy ministry planned to recruit a contractor to set up a national digital identity system](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf>).
