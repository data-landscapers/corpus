# Benin

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [ANIP says the biometric national identity card (CNIB) is issued, certifying identity from the holder's personal identification number, and renewal can now be started online](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-la-carte-didentite-biometrique-peut-desormais-etre-renouvelee-en-ligne/>). [ANIP director Aristide Adjinacou refers to the Registre national des personnes physiques and the personal identification number (NPI), with parents already holding NPIs and the "C'est moi" card certificate accepted as a supporting document](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). [Benin's director of emigration and immigration said Benin has issued a biometric national identity card meeting ECOWAS and international standards since 2020](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-carte-didentite-biometrique-de-la-cedeao-les-medias-appeles-a-jouer-un-role-cle-dans-la-sensibilisation>). [In October 2026 pensioners proved they were alive at biometric kiosks and in an app, using their biometric identity card and NPI](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/10/benin-15-189-retraites-ont-deja-effectue-leur-preuve-de-vie-la-campagne-se-poursuit/>).
