# Nigeria

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [NIMC says it has enrolled 142 million Nigerians and issued about 100 million National Identification Numbers](<https://guardian.ng/business-services/nimc-onboards-63-biometric-agents-reaffirms-local-hosting-of-national-database/>). [NIMC director-general said the national register now holds the identities of more than 140 million Nigerians and legal residents, enrolled through the Ward-to-Ward project in 8,809 wards](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>). [NIMC says 142 million people have registered for National Identification Numbers as of September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [NIMC's director of research and strategy said the National Identity Number total had reached about 140 million and enrolment time had fallen from seven days to one hour](<https://businessandtransport.com/how-nimc-emerges-nigerias-national-trust-authority-under-new-act-2026/>). [NIMC says 136 million Nigerians are registered in the National Identity Database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/nigeria-integrates-national-student-ids-digital-identity-system>). [The Director General of NIMC says NIMC had enrolled 126,717,316 individuals, about 25 million of them in the last two years](<https://statehouse.gov.ng/credible-inclusive-national-identity-management-system-fundamental-to-national-development-goals-president-tinubu/>).

**Biometric checks.** [TechCabal reports that enrolment captures demographic and biometric data and that issuance follows processing, validation and deduplication against the national database, as explained by two commentators](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).
