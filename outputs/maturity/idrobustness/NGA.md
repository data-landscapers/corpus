# Nigeria

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [NIMC director-general said the national register now holds the identities of more than 140 million Nigerians and legal residents, enrolled through the Ward-to-Ward project in 8,809 wards](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>). [NIMC says 142 million people have registered for National Identification Numbers as of September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [NIMC's director of research and strategy said the National Identity Number total had reached about 140 million and enrolment time had fallen from seven days to one hour](<https://businessandtransport.com/how-nimc-emerges-nigerias-national-trust-authority-under-new-act-2026/>). [The man told FIJ his first daughter has a National Identification Number (NIN), so the system has issued a number to her](<https://fij.ng/article/lagos-man-visited-birth-registration-website-he-finds-strangers-listed-as-son-wife/>).

**Biometric checks.** [TechCabal reports that enrolment captures demographic and biometric data and that issuance follows processing, validation and deduplication against the national database, as explained by two commentators](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).
