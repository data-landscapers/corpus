# Nigeria

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) issues the National Identification Number. A World Bank paper [records about 121 million numbers generated from about 145 million enrolments by 14 September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/nigeria-seeks-digital-id-project-extension-to-roll-out-mosip-based-infrastructure>). NIMC [put enrolment at 142 million in September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). Its director-general [said the register held more than 140 million identities](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>), enrolled ward by ward. Its director of research [said enrolment time had fallen from seven days to one hour](<https://businessandtransport.com/how-nimc-emerges-nigerias-national-trust-authority-under-new-act-2026/>). Since 2024 [a number can be generated at birth registration](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>). A replacement system built on MOSIP is under way: [its contract was signed on 15 September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/nigeria-seeks-digital-id-project-extension-to-roll-out-mosip-based-infrastructure>), with full operation expected by December 2027.

**Biometric checks.** Enrolment captures demographic and biometric data. The World Bank paper says [numbers are generated only after biometric and demographic validation and de-duplication](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/nigeria-seeks-digital-id-project-extension-to-roll-out-mosip-based-infrastructure>). TechCabal [described the same checks against the national database in September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>).

**Security certified or audited.** In December 2025 NIMC [said it had held ISO 27001 certification since 2014](<https://techcabal.com/2025/12/10/inside-nigerias-83m-bet-on-mosip-for-its-national-id/>). It said the certificate had been upgraded to the 2022 version of the standard.
