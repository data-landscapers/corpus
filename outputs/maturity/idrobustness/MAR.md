# Morocco

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Le Matin reports that Morocco's 2026-27 university grant eligibility requires applicants to hold an electronic national identity card (CNIE)](<https://lematin.ma/enseignement/bac-2026-les-demandes-de-bourse-sur-minhaty-sont-ouvertes/356597>). [Biometric Update says the DGSN issues the country's national identity cards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/morocco-approves-new-legal-framework-for-more-secure-biometric-passport>). [The document describes the electronic national identity card (CNIE) as in use, with renewal ending in an in-person appointment for biometric verification, per the DGSN](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process>). [Biometric Update quotes the DGSN digital ID head saying the national ID card management system has been used since the 1950s](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/building-digital-id-systems-that-last-african-countries-share-experiences-as-id4africa-2026-opens>). [Morocco's biometric national ID card (CNIE) is renewed through DGSN enrolment offices where biometrics are captured, and the system is being upgraded](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>).

**Biometric checks.** [IDAKTO says the system uses server-side 1:N biometric matching for proofing and native device biometrics for 1:1 verification](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system.
