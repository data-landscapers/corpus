# Liberia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [About 720,000 people are enrolled in the NBIS, the presidential proclamation says](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [Every person in Liberia must enrol, with citizen or foreign-national status recorded](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [Citizens outside Monrovia must travel to the capital to register and collect a card](<https://knewsonline.com/liberia-plans-nationwide-same-day-national-id-service-seeks-us39-million-for-rollout>). [The registry's director wants to replace its technology under a proposed US$39 million plan](<https://knewsonline.com/liberia-plans-nationwide-same-day-national-id-service-seeks-us39-million-for-rollout>). [A US$54.7 million concession with OeSD International GmbH covers biometric enrolment, deduplication and digital credentials](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>). It was signed and awaits ratification, NIR Executive Director Andrew Peters said on 4 September 2026. [The House sent it to a joint committee on 13 October 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>), the Liberian Observer reports. [In June 2025 Peters told the House the registry processed 5,000 ID cards a day](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/house-turns-heat-on-national-id-chief-andrews-peter/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>).
