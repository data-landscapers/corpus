# Liberia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The proclamation says about 720,000 people are enrolled in the NBIS and that every person in Liberia is required to enrol, with citizen or foreign-national status recorded](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [Citizens outside Monrovia must travel to the capital to register and obtain national ID cards from the National Identification Registry, whose director says it wants to replace its current technology under a proposed US$39 million plan](<https://knewsonline.com/liberia-plans-nationwide-same-day-national-id-service-seeks-us39-million-for-rollout>). [The National Identification Registry says a proposed US$54.7 million investment by OeSD International for nationwide biometric enrolment and national identity infrastructure has reached the Legislature and awaits ratification](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/nir-seeks-legislative-approval-for-us54-7m-identity-deal/>).
