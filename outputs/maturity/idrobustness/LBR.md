# Liberia

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The proclamation says about 720,000 people are enrolled in the NBIS and that every person in Liberia is required to enrol, with citizen or foreign-national status recorded](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [Citizens outside Monrovia must travel to the capital to register and obtain national ID cards from the National Identification Registry, whose director says it wants to replace its current technology under a proposed US$39 million plan](<https://knewsonline.com/liberia-plans-nationwide-same-day-national-id-service-seeks-us39-million-for-rollout>). [The Liberian Observer reports a proposed concession agreement with OeSD International GmbH for a biometric national ID system issuing unique numbers and digital cards, referred to a House joint committee on 2026-10-13 and not yet ratified](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). [A US$54.7 million concession with OeSD International GmbH for biometric enrolment, deduplication and digital credentials has been signed but awaits legislative ratification, NIR Executive Director Andrew Peters said on 4 September 2026](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>).
