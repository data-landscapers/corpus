# Madagascar

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Madagascar's interior ministry launched mass biometric enrolment, Izaho Tokana 2.0, to give each person a unique identification number](<https://www.moov.mg/article/121321-enregistrement-biometrique-izaho-tokana-20-entre-dans-sa-deuxieme-phase>). The World Bank finances it through the DECIM project. [The DECIM project says over six million people aged 18 and over were in the national base by 30 June 2026](<https://decim.mg/actualites/enrolement-biometrique-a-madagascar-capitaliser-les-acquis/>). [Phase II aims to enrol over 10 million adults by July 2027](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>). [In November 2026 the interior minister said just under seven million adults had been enrolled](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-pres-de-sept-millions-de-personnes-enrolees-dans-le-cadre-du-projet-izaho-tokana>). [On 13 May 2026 the High Constitutional Court upheld law 2026-001](<https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9952>), which allows late birth registration during the enrolment.

**Biometric checks.** [The World Bank says teams collect fingerprints and iris scans](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar>). Identity is confirmed against a de-duplicated biometric database. [TechAfrica News reported in January 2026 that the programme captures fingerprints, face and iris to remove duplicate identities](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/30/national-biometric-enrolment-begins-paving-way-for-digital-inclusion-in-madagascar/>).
