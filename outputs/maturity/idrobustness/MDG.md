# Madagascar

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Ministry of Interior and Decentralisation launched mass biometric enrolment (Izaho tokana 2.0) on 16 September, to record biometric data and give each person a unique identification number, financed by the World Bank through the DECIM project](<https://www.moov.mg/article/121321-enregistrement-biometrique-izaho-tokana-20-entre-dans-sa-deuxieme-phase>). [The Ministry of Interior and Decentralisation launched phase II of Izaho tokana 2.0 biometric enrolment, targeting over 10 million citizens aged over 18 by July 2027 after about 5 million in phase I, with 1,000 extra kits](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>). [The High Constitutional Court's decision of 13 May 2026 found law 2026-001 conforming, a law authorising retroactive birth registration within the mass biometric enrolment operation](<https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9952>). [The DECIM project page says the Izaho Tokana mass biometric enrolment, begun in early 2026 by the CNECI with PRODIGY support, had registered over six million people aged 18 and over in the national base by 30 June 2026, within a modernised identity system still being built](<https://decim.mg/actualites/enrolement-biometrique-a-madagascar-capitaliser-les-acquis/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The World Bank says teams collect fingerprints and iris scans, and identity is confirmed against a de-duplicated biometric database](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar>). [The programme captures fingerprints, facial recognition and iris scans to ensure the uniqueness of each record and eliminate duplicate identities, TechAfrica News reports](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/30/national-biometric-enrolment-begins-paving-way-for-digital-inclusion-in-madagascar/>).
