# Malawi

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Malawi's national identity system contains information on more than 13.5 million registered people, including names, photographs and fingerprints, per current reporting cited by the column](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>). [More than 13.5 million Malawians are registered in the national ID system, with fingerprints and faces captured at registration, per Malawi24](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/>). [UNDP says expiry dates were removed from National Identity Cards from 2026-09-16 and national ID registration is near universal among eligible Malawians aged 16 and over](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-nzika-digital-id-wallet-and-removes-expiry-date-national-id>). [Malawi24 reports the National Registration Bureau issues national identity cards, now without expiry dates from 2026-09-16 on a directive of the Homeland Security Minister](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/malawi-drops-expiry-dates-on-national-ids/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>).
