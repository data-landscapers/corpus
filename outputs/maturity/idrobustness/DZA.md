# Algeria

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The High Commission for Digitalisation set out how Algeria's digital identity works: registration and appointment at the commune via dzds.dz, activation, then verification against the holder's biometric data, tied to legal and biometric identity](<https://news.prixalgerie.com/identite-numerique-algerie-gouvernance-donnees/>). [Citizens holding the national digital identity issued by the Haut Commissariat a la Numerisation can now access the WADHIFATI platform without a separate account](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>). [The Ministry of the Interior says the biometric electronic national ID card (CNIBE) has been issued since January 2016, with more than 40 million cards in circulation, on a unique national identification number (NIN)](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).

**Biometric checks.** [The Ministry of the Interior says delivery of identity and travel documents is conditional on fingerprint matching and that the issuance system is connected to AFIS for fingerprint identification](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). [The presentation says the NIN is tied to each person's biometric data to guarantee definitive uniqueness (2010, before the three-year window)](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).
