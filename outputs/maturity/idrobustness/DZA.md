# Algeria

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Citizens holding the national digital identity issued by the Haut Commissariat a la Numerisation can now access the WADHIFATI platform without a separate account](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>). [El Moudjahid reports the vehicle-registration file requires copies of the biometric national identity card of both parties, so a biometric national ID card is in service](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/immatriculation-des-vehicules-passage-au-tout-numerique-des-aujourd-hui-250065>). [The High Commission for Digitalisation set out how Algeria's digital identity works: registration and appointment at the commune via dzds.dz, activation, then verification against the holder's biometric data, tied to legal and biometric identity](<https://news.prixalgerie.com/identite-numerique-algerie-gouvernance-donnees/>).

**Biometric checks.** [The presentation says the NIN is tied to each person's biometric data to guarantee definitive uniqueness (2010, before the three-year window)](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source held since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Patient national identification number (electronic patient record)](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics.
