# Cote d'Ivoire

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [ONECI manages the RNPP and assigns the national identification number, per the PARAE coordinator](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). [ONECI enrols applicants and issues the national identity card, with a stamp bought online, enrolment at an approved centre and a notification to collect the produced card](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/124119-grand-bassam-le-calvaire-des-demandeurs-de-la-carte-nationale-d-identite>). [The RNPP holds citizens' biometric and civil data and assigns each person a national identification number (NNI), according to the article](<https://digitalmag.ci/identite-numerique-la-cote-divoire-pays-connecte-mais-pas-encore-pleinement-identifie/>). [Linfodrome reports ONECI's director general presenting Cote d'Ivoire's identification system, run by the single office ONECI, as a strategic state infrastructure](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/121311-id4africa-2026-l-oneci-presente-les-avancees-du-systeme-ivoirien-d-identification-numerique>). [ONECI's director general says the RNPP gives each citizen an NNI based on biometric data](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1768657500>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>).
