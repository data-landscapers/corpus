# Lesotho

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The director of the National Identity and Civil Registry says an integrated system launched in 2013 gives each person in the register a unique number](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/stories/linking-civil-registration-vital-statistics-and-legal-identity-system-essential-inclusive>). [In a pilot, Lesotho migrants in South Africa used their national digital IDs for formal remittances](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). [The IDs were verified in real time across the border, Co-Develop and FinMark Trust state](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). [Pangea claims in court that Home Affairs relied on the identification systems it built until the December 2024 termination](<https://lestimes.com/?p=88892>), the Lesotho Times reports. [Lesotho's action timeline lists an Integrated National Digital Identity Ecosystem for 2026-2030, with MICSTI and UNICEF](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [The 2026 work plan supports it, with biometric registration and continued MOSIP-based deployment](<https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/>).
