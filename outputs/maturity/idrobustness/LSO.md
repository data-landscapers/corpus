# Lesotho

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Co-Develop and FinMark Trust state that in a pilot Lesotho migrants in South Africa accessed formal remittance services using their national digital IDs, verified in real time across the border](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). [The Lesotho Times reports Pangea's court claim that the Ministry of Home Affairs kept relying on the national identification systems Pangea implemented through to the December 2024 termination](<https://lestimes.com/?p=88892>). [Lesotho's action timeline lists implementing an Integrated National Digital Identity Ecosystem for 2026-2030 with MICSTI and UNICEF](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [The 2026 work plan supports development of an integrated national digital identity ecosystem, with biometric registration and continued MOSIP-based deployment](<https://www.gov.ls/government/micsti-and-unicef-signed-digital-id-work-plan/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>).
