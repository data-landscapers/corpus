# Lesotho

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Co-Develop and FinMark Trust state that in a pilot Lesotho migrants in South Africa accessed formal remittance services using their national digital IDs, verified in real time across the border](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). [Lesotho's action timeline lists implementing an Integrated National Digital Identity Ecosystem for 2026-2030 with MICSTI and UNICEF](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs engaged AAM Consultancy to assess existing systems and develop a systems architecture and modernization framework for an integrated digital identity system, presented at a workshop from 29 June to 3 July 2026, per the Government of Lesotho](<https://www.gov.ls/development/ministry-of-home-affairs-advances-digital-transformation-of-national-identity-system/>).

**Biometric checks.** No source held since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>).
