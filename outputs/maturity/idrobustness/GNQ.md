# Equatorial Guinea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [CNEDOGE issues national identity documents (DIP) with a biometric security system](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>). [Each takes about 30 working days, by an unwritten norm](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>). [On 15 October 2026 the Vice-President ordered its technology, mostly installed in 2017 and 2018, assessed for modernisation](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [The equipment shows wear and limited capacity, the government's press office says](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [CETIS, its supplier, is to say what can be kept and what replaced](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [It is also to propose new designs for the identity document and passport](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [Backups are to be improved so that failures do not halt issuance in Malabo, Bata and the embassies](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest fact: [in 2024 CNEDOGE issued national identity documents carrying a biometric security system](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest fact: [in 2024 CNEDOGE took about 30 working days to issue each identity document](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>).
