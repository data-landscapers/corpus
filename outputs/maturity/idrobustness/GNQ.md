# Equatorial Guinea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [CNEDOGE issues national identity documents (DIP) carrying a biometric security system, with an unwritten norm of about 30 working days per document](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>). [On 15 October 2026 the Vice-President ordered CNEDOGE's technology, largely installed in 2017 and 2018, assessed for modernisation](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [The government's press office says the equipment shows wear and limited capacity; CETIS, the supplier of that technology, is to assess what can be kept and what must be replaced, and to propose new designs for the identity document and the passport](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>). [Backup systems are to be improved so that technical failures do not halt issuance in Malabo, Bata and the embassies](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_vicepresidente_de_la_republica_impulsa_la_modernizacion_del_sistema_informatico_de_cnedoge>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.hayderechoenguinea.com/noticias/sociedad/sobre-el-funcionamiento-del-centro-nacional-de-emision-de-documentos-oficia>).
