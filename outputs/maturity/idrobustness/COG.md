# Congo

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** The [finance ministry issues the NIU](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>), a unique identification number, to every resident from age 6. It is valid for ten years and free for individuals. In October 2025 its [database held over 600,000 people](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>), and card production had restarted in September 2025. In Pointe-Noire, [25,549 NIU cards issued from 2020 to 2024 had not been collected](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913>). [Decree 2024-2692](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>) creates a secure national identity card with a national number, for citizens aged 16 and over.

**Biometric checks.** Enrolment in the NIU [takes iris and fingerprints, in person](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>), the ministry said in October 2025. The finance minister said the aim is one identity for each taxpayer, to limit duplicates and fraud. The biometric NIU dates from [an order of 2020](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>).

**Security certified or audited.** No certification, audit or assurance level has been reported for the system. The nearest held fact is [the ministry's account of enrolment in October 2025](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>).
