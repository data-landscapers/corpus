# Djibouti

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [At enrolment the system automatically assigns each citizen a National Identification Number (NNI) held in the national register, per the DGPF](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). [The Interior Ministry says it officially launched Mobile ID on 10 February 2026 as the national secure digital identity system, using biometrics](<https://interieur.gouv.dj/forum-sur-lidentite-numerique-et-lancement-du-projet-mobile-id/>). [Djibouti's President launched the Mobile ID digital identity platform on 9 February 2026, with biometric data held by the Interior Ministry](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/djibouti-officially-launches-mobile-id-digital-identity-platform>). [Biometric Update reports a biometrics-based mobile ID launched on 9 February 2026 at the end of a national forum on digital identification](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/djibouti-unveils-biometric-mobile-id-to-enhance-access-to-public-services>).

**Biometric checks.** [Fingerprints, iris and photograph are captured and automatically compared with the database, which has detected duplicates and people using a dead person's identity, says the head of the NNI service](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).
