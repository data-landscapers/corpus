# Botswana

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Bankers' Association of Botswana launched a national eKYC project letting banks verify customer identities against the national citizen database](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). A 2021 system verified identity cards the same way. [The permanent secretary says the national ID, the Omang, continues to be hacked](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202608260258.html>). The government is targeting a computer-generated Omang with distinct features, and the minister says migrants hold fake Omang cards. [The minister's electoral reform package proposes maintaining Omang as the primary form of ID](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202608190305.html>). [P42 million of the development budget goes to computerisation projects including the Biometric Omang system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>), alongside a wider Digital ID project. [Minister Pius Mokgware told Parliament the ministry is developing an Electronic Identification system as a single source of identity](<https://www.ngamitimes.co.bw/electronic-id-system-aims-to-improve-efficiency/>) for citizens and residents. It integrates the Omang, passports and driver's licences, with a pilot by end-2025 and full rollout by end-2026. [In October 2026 the minister said nearly half of the 45,000 Omang cards needed to clear a production backlog had been made](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93807>).
