# Eritrea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The UK Home Office guidance says Eritrea issues national ID cards, which are required for an Eritrean passport](<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile>). [The Eritrean embassy in Washington says Eritreans aged 18 or above must obtain a national ID and describes how new and replacement IDs are issued](<https://us.embassyeritrea.org/national-id/>). [The profile states Eritrea has no operational foundational digital ID and no foundational ID exists yet](<https://globaldigitalidentityindex.com/eritrea-population-digital-identity>). [The US State Department says Eritrea began issuing new plastic national identity cards with the holder's fingerprint in late 2015 for people over 18, though very few are in circulation (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile>).
