# Eritrea

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The UK Home Office guidance says Eritrea issues national ID cards, which are required for an Eritrean passport](<https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile/eritrea-knowledge-base-profile>). [The Eritrean embassy in Washington says Eritreans aged 18 or above must obtain a national ID and describes how new and replacement IDs are issued](<https://us.embassyeritrea.org/national-id/>). [The profile states Eritrea has no operational foundational digital ID and no foundational ID exists yet](<https://globaldigitalidentityindex.com/eritrea-population-digital-identity>). [In late 2015 Eritrea began issuing plastic national identity cards carrying the holder's fingerprint, the US State Department says](<https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html>). [It said very few were in circulation](<https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics.
