# Egypt

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Public Prosecution's online service verifies enquirers through the digital identity system](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>). [Its records are keyed to the national number](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5838334.aspx>). [The National ID System runs a card production centre and issuing centres](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>). [Its databases hold citizens' images and fingerprints](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>). [The communications ministry is building a unified national 'digital identity' for the whole state, says the NTRA's chief](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5887018.aspx>). [Full operation of that national digital identity is still to come](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5887018.aspx>).
