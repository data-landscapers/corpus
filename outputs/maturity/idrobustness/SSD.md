# South Sudan

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [The Directorate is registering examination candidates for National IDs in schools, launched 4 August 2026, with expansion to more schools planned](<https://catholicradionetwork.org/2026/08/04/immigration-directorate-launches-school-based-national-id-registration-for-examination-candidates/>). [The Directorate launched school-based registration to provide National ID cards to Primary Eight and Senior Four candidates, starting in Juba and to expand to other states](<https://www.eyeradio.org/school-based-national-id-registration-launched-for-exam-candidates-in-juba/>). [Authorities began distributing more than 4,000 newly processed South Sudan national identity cards in Yei River County, reported by the Directorate of Nationality, Passports and Immigration](<https://radioyei.org/news/local/yei-residents-begins-collecting-nationality-cards/>). [DNPI says it has resumed issuing personal ID cards (Butaka Shakhsia), holds 380,000 cards, and processing takes no more than two days](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://catholicradionetwork.org/2026/08/04/immigration-directorate-launches-school-based-national-id-registration-for-examination-candidates/>).
