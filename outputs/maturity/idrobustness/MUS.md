# Mauritius

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [Government Notice 28 of 2024 regulates application for, issuance and use of the Mauritius Mobile ID of the National Identity Card (MNIC), backed by a mID Certification Authority System](<https://mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/28_The-NIC_Mobile-ID_Reg-2024-.pdf>). [Phase 1 of the national ID rollout, a new physical smart ID card with anti-fraud security features, launched in February 2024 under the National Identity Card Regulations 2024](<https://www.lemauricien.com/le-mauricien/smart-and-mobile-id-in-mauritius-recommendations-for-a-balanced-approach/664957>). [L'Express reports that MauPass is the government's national identification system giving citizens a single secure account, and that minister Avinash Ramtohul said 500,000 people have already registered on it](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>).

**Biometric checks.** No source found since October 2023 says the system checks people by biometrics. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/28_The-NIC_Mobile-ID_Reg-2024-.pdf>).

**Security certified or audited.** No source found since October 2023 reports a certification, audit or assurance level for the system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/28_The-NIC_Mobile-ID_Reg-2024-.pdf>).
