# Sudan

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** [SudaPass, the national digital identity, is in use: Omdurman National Bank became the first bank to activate the link, verifying customers' identity with it, per SUNA](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>). [Sudan's Central Bank said on 23 August 2026 that registering on the SudaPass national digital identity platform establishes identity](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317788>). [The Central Bank of Sudan says the SudaPass digital identity is being introduced to facilitate bank clients' data updates](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/central-bank-of-sudan-extends-deadline-for-bank-clients-to-update-their-data-until-year-end/>). [The terms govern use of the Sudanese Digital Identity, which gives each registered user a unique digital account after registration and verification](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>).
