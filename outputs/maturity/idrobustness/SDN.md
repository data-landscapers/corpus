# Sudan

## Robustness of system

**National ID in service.** SudaPass is Sudan's national digital identity. Its terms say [each registered user gets a unique digital account after verification](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>). On 23 August 2026 the Central Bank said [registering on SudaPass establishes identity](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317788>). It is [introducing SudaPass for bank clients' data updates](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/central-bank-of-sudan-extends-deadline-for-bank-clients-to-update-their-data-until-year-end/>). Omdurman National Bank [was the first bank to verify customers with it](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>), per SUNA. In November 2026 Pakistan's interior minister said [Sudan had issued nine million national identity cards with NADRA's support](<https://thenews.pk/print/1447584-pakistan-sudan-to-strengthen-security-digital-registration-efforts>).
