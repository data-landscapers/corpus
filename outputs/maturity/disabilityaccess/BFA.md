# Burkina Faso

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [A project was launched on 8 August 2025 by Light for the World, UN-ABPAM and the Ministry of Secondary Education](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>). [It aims to produce accessible digital lessons and exams](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>). [It serves 48 visually impaired pupils and 34 teachers](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the 2025 project for accessible digital lessons and exams](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>). [It is run with the Ministry of Secondary Education for 48 visually impaired pupils](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same project, which produces accessible exams for visually impaired pupils](<https://www.sidwaya.info/promotion-de-leducation-inclusive-un-projet-lance-pour-optimiser-lapprentissage-des-eleves-handicapes-visuels-par-le-numerique/>).
