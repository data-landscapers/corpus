# Kenya

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** On 30 October 2026 officials [validated a roadmap for digital and ICT accessibility](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-31-state-plans-digital-accessibility-audits-to-improve-services-for-pwds>). Public institutions are to audit their websites and online platforms and plan to remove barriers for persons with disabilities. The disability office is drafting guidelines on sign language, captioning and accessible documents.

**Delivered.** In September 2026 Nakuru County [opened a digital platform with a disability module](<https://www.nakuru.go.ke/new-digital-platform-launched-in-nakuru-county-to-improve-support-for-survivors-of-gender-based-violence-and-persons-with-disabilities/>). Residents apply through it, online or by phone, for assistive devices, empowerment programmes and tax relief. It had received 216 applications. The Universal Service Fund's portfolio [includes an assistive technology project](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/major-usf-transparency-update-kenyas-communications-authority-responds-to-public-inquiry/>), giving content and devices to learners with print disabilities, as listed in August 2025.

**Audited.** Audits of public websites and platforms [are planned under the October 2026 roadmap](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-31-state-plans-digital-accessibility-audits-to-improve-services-for-pwds>). No results have been published.

***Also on file***. [Digital Platform for Gender-Based Violence and Persons with Disabilities Case Management and Reporting](<https://www.nakuru.go.ke/new-digital-platform-launched-in-nakuru-county-to-improve-support-for-survivors-of-gender-based-violence-and-persons-with-disabilities/>).

***Not found***. No results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services have been published since October 2023.
