# Mozambique

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Lei 10/2024, published in the Boletim da Republica on 7 June 2024, gives persons with disabilities a right of access to information and communication technologies and systems (Article 16) and says public service entities should provide service information in accessible formats, with sign-language training for State communicators (Article 17)](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>). [The ITU-EU VaMoz Digital project, supporting Mozambique's government 2023-2027, completed a digital accessibility assessment with special focus on persons with disabilities and ran digital accessibility awareness and training (5 webinars, 4 workshops, 3 focus groups) reaching 320+ stakeholders, according to ITU](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>).

**Delivered.** No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>).
