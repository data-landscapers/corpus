# Mozambique

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Lei 10/2024 of 7 June 2024 gives persons with disabilities a right of access to information technologies](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>). [It says public services should give information in accessible formats](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>). [It also asks for sign-language training for State communicators](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>). [The ITU-EU VaMoz Digital project supports the government from 2023 to 2027](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>). [It completed a digital accessibility assessment focused on persons with disabilities](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>). [It trained more than 320 stakeholders, according to ITU](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>).

**Delivered.** No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/moz228091.pdf>).
