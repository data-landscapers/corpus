# South Africa

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [In August 2026 the disability minister proposed a seven-point action plan with China](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>). [It includes accessible websites and apps, captioning, Braille, Easy Read and South African Sign Language](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>). [USAASA is consulting on its 2010 universal service determination](<https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/business/2026-10-10-public-consultation-to-update-universal-access-policy/>). [Accessibility for people with disabilities is among the issues](<https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/business/2026-10-10-public-consultation-to-update-universal-access-policy/>).
