# South Africa

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In August 2026 the disability minister [proposed a seven-point action plan with China](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>). It includes accessible websites and apps, captioning, Braille, Easy Read and South African Sign Language. In October 2026 USAASA [opened a consultation on its 2010 universal service determination](<https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/business/2026-10-10-public-consultation-to-update-universal-access-policy/>). Accessibility for people with disabilities is among the issues.

**Delivered.** In July 2025 the Department of Basic Education told Parliament that [406 of 461 public special schools had received ICT equipment, connectivity and assistive devices](<https://www.education.gov.za/ArchivedDocuments/ArchivedArticles/ProgressinInclusiveEducation.aspx>). It put the share at 88%. The devices came under obligations set by ICASA, and numbered 12,180 for learners. In August 2025 the deputy science minister [launched assistive technologies funded through the Technology Innovation Agency](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/deputy-minister-nomalungelo-gina-innovations-assist-people-disabilities-south>). They include a smartphone for blind users and a sign-language app.

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. The nearest fact found is [the minister's plan of August 2026](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>), which proposes accessible websites and apps.

***Also on file***. [Virecom](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/deputy-minister-nomalungelo-gina-innovations-assist-people-disabilities-south>), a video sign-language interpreting service funded through the agency.

***Not found***. No rule in force requiring accessible public information or digital services is found. No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services.
