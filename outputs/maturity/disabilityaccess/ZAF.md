**Accessibility rule.** [The Minister in the Presidency for Women, Youth and Persons with Disabilities proposed an approach with South Africa and China that includes accessible websites and mobile applications, captioning, Braille, Easy Read and South African Sign Language services, within a seven-point action plan on accessibility and assistive technology](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>).
