# South Africa

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** A 2025 legal analysis by CIPESA covers the [Electronic Communications Act 2005](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Legal_Analysis_of_Technology_and_DisabilityinAfrica.pdf>). It says the Act requires network and service providers to offer services accessible to persons with disabilities. In October 2026 USAASA [opened a consultation on its universal service determination](<https://www.sundaytimes.timeslive.co.za/business/2026-10-10-public-consultation-to-update-universal-access-policy/>). Accessibility for people with disabilities is among the issues.

**Delivered.** In July 2025 the Department of Basic Education told Parliament that [406 of 461 public special schools had received ICT equipment and assistive devices](<https://www.education.gov.za/ArchivedDocuments/ArchivedArticles/ProgressinInclusiveEducation.aspx>). The devices came under obligations set by ICASA. In August 2025 the deputy science minister [launched assistive technologies funded through the Technology Innovation Agency](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/deputy-minister-nomalungelo-gina-innovations-assist-people-disabilities-south>). They include a smartphone for blind users and a sign-language app.

**Audited.** No published audit of public websites or digital services has been found since October 2023. In August 2026 the disability minister [proposed a plan with China](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-china-deepen-cooperation-disability-inclusion>) that includes accessible websites and apps.

***Also on file***. [Virecom](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/deputy-minister-nomalungelo-gina-innovations-assist-people-disabilities-south>), a video sign-language interpreting service funded through the agency.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services.
