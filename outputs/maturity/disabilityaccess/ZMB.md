# Zambia

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** A 2025 legal analysis by CIPESA covers [the Persons with Disabilities Act 2012](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Legal_Analysis_of_Technology_and_DisabilityinAfrica.pdf>). It says section 49 requires television stations to provide a sign language inset or subtitles. This applies to all newscasts and educational programmes. In May 2025 ZAPD said [draft national accessibility standards had been developed](<https://zapd.org.zm/zapd-observes-14th-global-accesibility-awareness-day/>). They cover built and digital environments.

**Delivered.** In 2024 [ZICTA gave Chinsali Special School 15 desktop computers](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/zicta-assesses-uasf-ict-projects-to-expand-digital-inclusion-across-rural-zambia/>). Five carry JAWS screen-reading software. A braille printer and internet connectivity came with them. The school has 768 learners, 213 of them with disabilities.
