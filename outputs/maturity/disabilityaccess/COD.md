# DR Congo

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The tribune by Noemie Kilembe says the DRC has ratified the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, which makes accessibility of information and communication a condition of exercising public freedoms](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/04/dialogue-national-en-rdc-sans-les-personnes-sourdes-linclusion-restera-un-slogan-tribune>). [G3ict's DARE Index (2020 tab) records policies and programmes for web, TV and multimedia, mobile telephony and e-books and digital content accessibility in the DRC, each at implementation level 5 of 5 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of>).

**Delivered.** [The WSIS Forum 2026 session description says HandiConnect, backed by the DRC Ministry of Telecommunications, the Ministry delegated to Persons with Disabilities and FONA, lets users access state services and G2P social-protection payments on basic feature phones via USSD](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>).
