# DR Congo

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The DRC has ratified the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, Noemie Kilembe writes](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/04/dialogue-national-en-rdc-sans-les-personnes-sourdes-linclusion-restera-un-slogan-tribune>). [It makes accessible information and communication a condition of exercising public freedoms](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/04/dialogue-national-en-rdc-sans-les-personnes-sourdes-linclusion-restera-un-slogan-tribune>). [In 2020 G3ict's DARE Index recorded DRC policies on accessible web, TV, mobile telephony and digital content](<https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of>). [It rated each at implementation level 5 of 5](<https://g3ict.org/country-profile/congo-democratic-republic-of>).

**Delivered.** [The WSIS Forum 2026 session description says HandiConnect, backed by the DRC Ministry of Telecommunications, the Ministry delegated to Persons with Disabilities and FONA, lets users access state services and G2P social-protection payments on basic feature phones via USSD](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 HandiConnect let users reach state services on basic feature phones](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>).
