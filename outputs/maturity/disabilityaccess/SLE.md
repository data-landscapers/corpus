# Sierra Leone

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** On 22 October 2026 [the Directorate of Science, Technology and Innovation and the Ministry of Social Welfare validated Leh Wi Tok, a UNICEF-sponsored communication app for speech- and hearing-impaired people](<https://www.sierraleonepress.com/dsti-and-social-welfare-validate-new-app-for-speech-and-hearing-impaired-in-sierra-leone/>), with a first phase for school pupils; the Social Welfare minister plans to present it for cabinet approval. No law or standard in force requiring accessible public websites or services has been found.

**Delivered.** [MoCTI, with World Bank-funded SLDTP support, handed over a second set of assistive devices (computers with NVDA screen-reader software, Braille note takers and embossers, scanners and book readers, braillers, digital recorders) to four disability institutions in Makeni, Kenema and Freetown, after an early 2024 phase to two Freetown schools](<https://sldtp.gov.sl/2025/11/12/mocti-hands-over-assistive-devices-to-disability-institutions-with-support-from-sldtp/>).
