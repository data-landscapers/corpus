# Sierra Leone

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** On 22 October 2026 [two government bodies validated Leh Wi Tok, a communication app for speech- and hearing-impaired people](<https://www.sierraleonepress.com/dsti-and-social-welfare-validate-new-app-for-speech-and-hearing-impaired-in-sierra-leone/>). [UNICEF sponsors it, and its first phase is for school pupils](<https://www.sierraleonepress.com/dsti-and-social-welfare-validate-new-app-for-speech-and-hearing-impaired-in-sierra-leone/>). [The Social Welfare minister plans to take it to cabinet](<https://www.sierraleonepress.com/dsti-and-social-welfare-validate-new-app-for-speech-and-hearing-impaired-in-sierra-leone/>). No law or standard in force requiring accessible public websites has been found.

**Delivered.** [In 2025 MoCTI handed assistive devices to four disability institutions in Makeni, Kenema and Freetown](<https://sldtp.gov.sl/2025/11/12/mocti-hands-over-assistive-devices-to-disability-institutions-with-support-from-sldtp/>). [They include screen-reader computers, Braille note takers and embossers](<https://sldtp.gov.sl/2025/11/12/mocti-hands-over-assistive-devices-to-disability-institutions-with-support-from-sldtp/>). [A first set went to two Freetown schools in 2024](<https://sldtp.gov.sl/2025/11/12/mocti-hands-over-assistive-devices-to-disability-institutions-with-support-from-sldtp/>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is MoCTI's 2025 handover of assistive devices](<https://sldtp.gov.sl/2025/11/12/mocti-hands-over-assistive-devices-to-disability-institutions-with-support-from-sldtp/>).
