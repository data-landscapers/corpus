# Malawi

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Section 30 of the Persons with Disabilities Act 2024 has been in force since April 2024](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>). [It requires anyone publishing information to the public to publish it in accessible formats](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>). [It names sign language and braille](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>). [The National Disability Policy 2025 commits the government to information in accessible formats](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/250812084349_NATIONAL%20DISABILITY%20POLICY.pdf>). [Its plan runs from 2025 to 2028](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/250812084349_NATIONAL%20DISABILITY%20POLICY.pdf>). [In 2020 MACRA proposed a fund project on assistive technologies for persons with disabilities](<https://macra.mw/universal-service-stakeholder-consultations/>).

**Delivered.** No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered. The nearest fact: [since April 2024 the Act has given persons with disabilities a right to access information and communication technologies](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services. The nearest fact: [section 30 of the 2024 Act requires public information in accessible formats](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>).
