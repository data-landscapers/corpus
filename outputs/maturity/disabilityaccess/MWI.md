# Malawi

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Malawi's Persons with Disabilities Act 2024 (Act No. 4 of 2024, assented 31 January 2024, commenced 5 April 2024 per the file's note) section 30 gives persons with disabilities a right to access information and communication technologies and requires anyone publishing information to the public to also publish it in an accessible format, including sign language and braille](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>). [Malawi's MACRA proposed a Connectivity for Persons with Disability Project under the Universal Service Fund for 2020/21, to promote internet access and use of assistive technologies, and called for stakeholder input by 28 August 2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://macra.mw/universal-service-stakeholder-consultations/>).

**Delivered.** No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://macoda.mw/public/assets/uploads/publications/240812090242_Persons%20with%20Disabilities%20Act,%202024.pdf>).
