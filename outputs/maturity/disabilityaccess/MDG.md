# Madagascar

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [On 3 December 2025 the Prime Minister sent Parliament draft law 017/2025 on access to public information](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>). [It says public information must be adapted for persons with disabilities](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>). [It names specialised media, adapted technologies and human assistance](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is draft law 017/2025 on access to public information](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>). [The Prime Minister sent it to Parliament by decree in December 2025](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same draft law](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>). [It would require public information adapted for persons with disabilities](<https://pub-7e0d3a35c1b949698a7ced344fa56252.r2.dev/textes/017-2025/PROJET_LAICP___PR_SENT_E_AU.PDF>).
