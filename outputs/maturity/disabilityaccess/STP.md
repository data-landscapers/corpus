# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The regulator AGER found in November 2025 no specific action on accessibility in digital policy](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>). [A 2025 country report says the 2012 disability law leaves accessibility out of its rights](<https://www.adry.up.ac.za/country-reports-2025/sao-tome-and-principe>). [It says a national disability strategy is being adopted](<https://www.adry.up.ac.za/country-reports-2025/sao-tome-and-principe>). [The strategy puts accessibility among its legal reforms](<https://www.adry.up.ac.za/country-reports-2025/sao-tome-and-principe>).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is AGER's 2025 finding](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>). [It saw no specific action on accessibility in digital policy](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same finding by the regulator, set out in an ITU project document of November 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says work is under way on accessible information or digital services.
