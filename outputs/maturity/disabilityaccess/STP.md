# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The African Disability Rights Yearbook report says an ongoing process of adopting a five-year National Strategy for the Inclusion and Participation of People with Disabilities, developed with the UN Country Team, which prioritises accessibility among its legal reforms](<https://www.adry.up.ac.za/country-reports-2025/sao-tome-and-principe>). [AGER observes an absence of specific actions on accessibility in São Tomé and Príncipe's digital policy actions](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>). [The 2012 Basic Law for Persons with Disabilities excludes accessibility among its rights, and that the Labour Code (Law 6/2019) has no specific provisions on accessibility measures](<https://www.adry.up.ac.za/country-reports-2025/sao-tome-and-principe>).
