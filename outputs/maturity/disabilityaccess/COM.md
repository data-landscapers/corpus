# Comoros

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [In January 2026 a civil-society submission said Comoros's 2014 disability law, no. 14-037/AU, does not take up accessibility (CRPD article 9)](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/JS3_UPR46_COM_F_Main.pdf>). [Nor does it take up reasonable accommodation](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/JS3_UPR46_COM_F_Main.pdf>). [The law also lacks provisions on freedom of expression and access to information](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/JS3_UPR46_COM_F_Main.pdf>).

**Delivered.** No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the 2014 law had no provision on access to information](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/JS3_UPR46_COM_F_Main.pdf>).
