# Zimbabwe

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [In July 2026 the Herald said the National ICT Policy (2022-2027) requires government digital platforms to progressively meet international accessibility standards](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [In the same report advocates said few standards are enforceable](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [Permanent Secretary Kunaka said the government's target is that by 2027 all new public service digital platforms meet minimum accessibility standards](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [Pre-procurement accessibility assessments were under discussion](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [In May 2025 ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said the National Disability Policy and National ICT Policy would be reviewed to add accessibility guidelines](<https://technomag.co.zw/government-urges-telecom-operators-to-prioritise-accessibility-standards/>). [In May 2024 the Ministry of Health and Child Care launched the National Assistive Technology Strategy and the Assistive Products Priority List](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>).

**Delivered.** [In March 2026 the Herald reported that the government-commissioned National Information Access Centre at Zimbabwe Open University includes an inclusive library with assistive technologies](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>). [The vice chancellor said it has smart boards and assistive devices for students with disabilities](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>). [In July 2025 POTRAZ's director general said it had donated 567 computers to disability-focused institutions](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/potraz-trains-visually-impaired-professionals-in-masvingo/>). [They carry adaptive software for the visually impaired and audio equipment for those with hearing impairments](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/potraz-trains-visually-impaired-professionals-in-masvingo/>). [In June 2026 Harare Institute of Technology hosted a workshop training librarians from more than 22 state universities on assistive technologies](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2026/06/10/opening-doors-to-every-learner-inside-hits-national-assistive-technologies-training-workshop/>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services. The nearest held fact: [in August 2025 Potraz ran a programme on digital exclusion that followed a nationwide survey](<http://www.businessdaily.co.zw/index-id-home-zk-51057.html>).
