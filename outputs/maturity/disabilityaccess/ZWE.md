# Zimbabwe

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In July 2026 the Herald reported on the National ICT Policy (2022-2027). [The policy requires government digital platforms to progressively meet international accessibility standards](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [Advocates said few standards are enforceable](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). [Permanent Secretary Kunaka said the target date is 2027](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusion-the-next-frontier-for-disability-rights-in-zimbabwe/>). By then all new public service digital platforms should meet minimum accessibility standards. [In May 2025 ICT minister Tatenda Mavetera said two policies would be reviewed to add accessibility guidelines](<https://technomag.co.zw/government-urges-telecom-operators-to-prioritise-accessibility-standards/>). They are the National Disability Policy and the National ICT Policy.

**Delivered.** In March 2026 the Herald reported on the government-commissioned National Information Access Centre at Zimbabwe Open University. [It includes an inclusive library with assistive technologies](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/minister-shava-launches-ai-hub-at-zimbabwe-open-university/>). In July 2025 POTRAZ's director general reported a donation. [POTRAZ had given 567 computers with adaptive software and audio equipment to disability-focused institutions](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/potraz-trains-visually-impaired-professionals-in-masvingo/>). [In June 2026 Harare Institute of Technology trained librarians from more than 22 state universities on assistive technologies](<https://www.hit.ac.zw/2026/06/10/opening-doors-to-every-learner-inside-hits-national-assistive-technologies-training-workshop/>).
