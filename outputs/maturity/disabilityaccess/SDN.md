# Sudan

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** No rule or programme since October 2023 has been found. [Sudan's Persons with Disability National Act 2017](<https://www.apminebanconvention.org/fileadmin/_APMBC-DOCUMENTS/StatePlans-policies/Sudan_Disability_Law_Translated_2017.pdf>) provides for sign language with on-screen text in media programmes, and for access to electronic services.

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology since October 2023 has been found. In December 2017 the telecoms regulator [opened the first phase of its Ibsar project](<https://tpra3.onespace.sd/en/projects/ibsar-project/>), training visually impaired people in computer and internet use.

**Audited.** A study published in April 2026 [tested seven Sudanese academic, government and service websites against WCAG 2.1 AA](<https://doi.org/10.56961/mejeit.v5i1.1442>). It used WAVE, axe DevTools and Lighthouse. Lighthouse scores ran from 76 to 88. The authors found critical barriers on every site, such as poor colour contrast and missing alternative text.

***Also on file***. [Inter-Agency Community Feedback Mechanism](<https://humanitarianaction.info/plan/1514/document/sudan-humanitarian-needs-and-response-plan-2026/article/22-implementing-humanitarian-reset-sudan>).

***Not found***. No rule, programme or delivery since October 2023 has been found. The audit found is an academic study of seven sites.
