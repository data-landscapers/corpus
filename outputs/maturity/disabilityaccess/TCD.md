# Chad

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Chad's 2025 report to the UN disability committee cites Act No. 007/PR/2007](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>). [Its article 32 requires public and private bodies to adapt communication and information systems for persons with disabilities](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>). [A 2019 decree requires public transport agencies to give information in braille](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>). [It also requires sign-language support for hard-of-hearing travellers](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the 2007 Act's duty to adapt information systems](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>). [Chad reported it to the UN in 2025](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same report](<https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/g25/194/41/pdf/g2519441.pdf>).
