# Uganda

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [In February 2024 the Uganda Communications Commission's universal service fund ran an ICT for Persons with Disabilities programme](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/UCC-UCUSAF-TELECOM-INFRASTRUCTURE-LAUNCHED-IN-AGAGO-06.FEB_.2024.pdf>). No rule in force is known to require accessible public information or digital services.

**Delivered.** [A UCC study of February 2026 says the programme gave assistive technologies to secondary schools and regional hubs](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/ucc-study-highlights-strong-impact-of-ict4pwd-programme/>). It covered 73 of 135 districts. [In November 2024 the ICT ministry said four regional resource centres had assistive technology](<https://businessfocus.co.ug/manifesto-week-ict-ministry-reveals-key-achievements-as-internet-penetration-increases-to-67/>). [In August 2026 the National Council for Persons with Disabilities opened an ICT hub in Kasese with accessible ICT facilities](<https://elixnews.co.ug/new-ict-hub-launched-in-kasese-to-skill-pwds/>), funded by IDI Uganda and KOICA. [In November 2026 the ICT minister said the government's e-Booster Programme backs Blind Assistant](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/uganda/>). The locally built AI app reads learning materials aloud to visually impaired learners.

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites are is known since October 2023. The nearest held fact is [the UCC's study of its own programme](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/ucc-study-highlights-strong-impact-of-ict4pwd-programme/>). It measured the programme's impact, not public services.
