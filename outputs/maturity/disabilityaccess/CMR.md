# Cameroon

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [In February 2024 PATNUC, at MINPOSTEL, sought a consultant to update the 2020 digital plan](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=787&type_publication=DP>). [The update adds accessibility and gender equality](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=787&type_publication=DP>). [In June 2025 a CIPESA legal analysis found no national law or policy on ICT access for persons with disabilities](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Legal_Analysis_of_Technology_and_DisabilityinAfrica.pdf>). [Only general disability laws apply](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Legal_Analysis_of_Technology_and_DisabilityinAfrica.pdf>). [On 27 April 2021 Cameroon ratified the Marrakesh Treaty](<https://cbchealthservices.org/first-accessible-digital-library-for-print-disabled-learners-in-cameroon-underway/>). [It lets organisations convert published works into accessible formats for learners with vision impairments](<https://cbchealthservices.org/first-accessible-digital-library-for-print-disabled-learners-in-cameroon-underway/>). [CBC Health Services relied on it](<https://cbchealthservices.org/first-accessible-digital-library-for-print-disabled-learners-in-cameroon-underway/>).
