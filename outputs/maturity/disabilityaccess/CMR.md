# Cameroon

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Cameroon's PATNUC project unit at MINPOSTEL, financed by IDA credit 69870-CM, sought a consultant to update the 2020 digital plan with accessibility and gender-equality considerations](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=787&type_publication=DP>). [CIPESA's June 2025 legal analysis, by Cameroonian law firm Zangue & Partners, states there are no specific national laws or policies that govern or facilitate access to and use of ICT by persons with disabilities, only general disability laws](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Legal_Analysis_of_Technology_and_DisabilityinAfrica.pdf>). [Cameroon ratified the Marrakesh Treaty on 27 April 2021, which authorizes organizations to convert published works into accessible formats for learners with vision impairments, and CBC Health Services relied on it (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://cbchealthservices.org/first-accessible-digital-library-for-print-disabled-learners-in-cameroon-underway/>).
