# Cape Verde

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** No law, policy, standard or programme on digital accessibility for persons with disabilities has been found. [The nearest fact found is the labour inspectorate's accessible portal of November 2026](<https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/ilo-supports-digital-transformation-cabo-verde%E2%80%99s-general-labour>).

**Delivered.** [The labour inspectorate's portal, launched on 3 November 2026, has text-to-speech and keyboard navigation](<https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/ilo-supports-digital-transformation-cabo-verde%E2%80%99s-general-labour>). [It also works with screen readers and has better colour contrast, the ILO reports](<https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/ilo-supports-digital-transformation-cabo-verde%E2%80%99s-general-labour>).
