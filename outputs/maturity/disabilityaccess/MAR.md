# Morocco

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In June 2026 the broadcasting regulator HACA issued [decision 50-26 on the 2026 elections](<https://www.haca.ma/sites/default/files/upload/CSCA_D%C3%A9cision%20n%C2%B050-26_Elections%202026%20VF.pdf>). It requires audiovisual services to give persons with disabilities access to election programmes. Sign language, subtitling or audio description are the means, where the programme allows. In July 2024 ESCWA and the Agency of Digital Development [held a workshop on a national digital accessibility plan](<https://www.unescwa.org/news/concepts-and-policies-digital-accessibility-morocco>).

**Delivered.** In September 2026 the digital transition ministry [launched a new version of the Idarati portal](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/idarati-vers-une-administration-plus-proche-proactive-et-centr%C3%A9e-sur-le-citoyen>). Its navigation, search and content were redesigned for digital accessibility. The ministry says it paid particular attention to persons with disabilities.

**Audited.** A joint study by the Mediator, the National Observatory of Human Development and the Secretariat of State for Social Inclusion [was presented in March 2026](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/personnes-en-situation-de-handicap-seules-25-des-administrations-marocaines-sont-inclusives-le_IH7JZFEDLJHUTK5V2MM3YSNIFY/>). It found 47.5% of public institutions offer inclusive digital solutions. It rated 25% of administrations inclusive overall.

***Also on file***. In September 2026 the minister [held a consultation on digital public services for persons with disabilities](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>).
