# Morocco

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In September 2026 digital transition minister Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni [held a consultation on access of persons with disabilities to digital public services](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). [Its recommendations go to the update of the 2024-2028 Open Government plan](<https://h24info.ma/economie/inclusion-numerique-amal-seghrouchni-societe/>). In July 2024 ESCWA and the Agency of Digital Development [held a workshop in Rabat to develop a national digital accessibility plan](<https://www.unescwa.org/news/concepts-and-policies-digital-accessibility-morocco>). A 2024 study of public-sector web teams [reports a lack of legal framework and guidelines](<https://doi.org/10.11591/ijict.v14i1.pp77-90>) as a barrier to accessible e-services.

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found. The nearest fact found is [the minister's consultation of September 2026](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>).

**Audited.** A joint study by the Mediator, the National Observatory of Human Development and the Secretariat of State for Social Inclusion [was presented in March 2026](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/personnes-en-situation-de-handicap-seules-25-des-administrations-marocaines-sont-inclusives-le_IH7JZFEDLJHUTK5V2MM3YSNIFY/>). It found 47.5% of public institutions offer inclusive digital solutions. It rated 25% of administrations inclusive overall, and 35% weak. Only 26% had formal rules on access for persons with disabilities.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No rule in force is found: a 2024 study of public-sector web teams reports no legal framework or guidelines (MAR-005). No delivery of accessible digital services by a government body is found.
