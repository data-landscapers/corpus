# Morocco

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Digital transition minister Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni held a consultation on access of persons with disabilities to digital public services](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). [Its proposals are to feed the third Open Government action plan, per LeNew](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>). [H24Info reports a workshop on the same subject](<https://h24info.ma/economie/inclusion-numerique-amal-seghrouchni-societe/>). [Its recommendations go to the update of the 2024-2028 Open Government plan](<https://h24info.ma/economie/inclusion-numerique-amal-seghrouchni-societe/>).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the minister's 2026 consultation on digital public services for persons with disabilities](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same consultation](<https://lenew.ma/inclusion-numerique-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-les-jeunes-les-femmes-et-les-personnes-en-situation-de-handicap/>), which fed the Open Government action plan.
