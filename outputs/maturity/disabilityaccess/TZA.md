# Tanzania

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The Tanzania Bureau of Standards recognised TCRA for implementing the newly introduced Tanzania Standard on accessibility of communications and ICT products and services for persons with disabilities](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>). [TCRA says its iPRIS Change Initiative on adopting assistive technologies for persons with disabilities is 80 percent complete and due to be finalised within six months](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>). [The Tanzania Bureau of Standards circulated for stakeholder comment, by 2025-04-14, a draft Tanzania standard on requirements for communications and ICT products and services accessibility to persons with disabilities (EDC 6, 3227)](<https://www.tbs.go.tz/uploads/publications/en-1739785468-Stakeholders'%20Comments%20Letter%20Feb%202025.pdf>).

**Delivered.** [TCRA has added assistive technologies to its website](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>). [Its digital skills campaigns used sign language and braille in three cities](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-advances-digital-inclusion-for-pwds-through-assistive-technologies/>).

**Audited.** [A 2025 study tested 42 e-government websites against WCAG 2.2](<https://journals.out.ac.tz/index.php/huria/article/view/1737>). [It found accessibility errors on 3.64 pages a site on average](<https://journals.out.ac.tz/index.php/huria/article/view/1737>).
