# Mauritius

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The UN Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities says Mauritius's Protection and Promotion of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act of 2024 does not cover the right to information in an accessible format, with only limited measures to promote accessible printed and electronic information, and recommends that it establish accessibility standards for information, communication technologies and websites](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). [Mauritius's report to the UN disability committee says Section 23 of the Copyright Act 2014 permits reproduction of published works in accessible format for people with visual impairment and the print disabled, incorporating the Marrakesh Treaty provisions (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CRPD_C_MUS_2-3_7812_E-2nd-3rd-reports.pdf>).

**Delivered.** [The Government of Mauritius says its Ministry of Social Integration set up a disability website, disability.govmu.org, where persons with disabilities can apply online for ministry services, with special features for persons with visual impairment (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CRPD_C_MUS_2-3_7812_E-2nd-3rd-reports.pdf>).
