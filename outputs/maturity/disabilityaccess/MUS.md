# Mauritius

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In 2024 the UN disability committee said [Mauritius's new disability Act does not cover information in accessible formats](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). It recommended accessibility standards for information technologies and websites. In May 2026 the AI Unit of the Ministry of ICT and Innovation said [its website is working to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>), and is not yet fully there.

**Delivered.** In May 2026 [the AI Unit's website offered built-in accessibility tools](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>). They include read-aloud, text scaling, high contrast, a dyslexia-friendly font, keyboard navigation and a simple language mode. In September 2025 [the Mauritian Sign Language electronic dictionary was launched at the Beau-Bassin school for the deaf](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/societe/ecole-des-sourds-lancement-dun-dictionnaire-electronique-de-langue-des-signes/683699/>). Social integration minister Ashok Subron praised his ministry's work on it with the Society for the Welfare of the Deaf.

**Audited.** The AI Unit says [it fixed all critical issues found in a May 2026 accessibility audit of its website](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>). It says some PDFs may still not read correctly in a screen reader. It publishes no other findings.

***Not found***. No rule in force requiring accessible public websites has been found. No published audit results for public websites have been found.
