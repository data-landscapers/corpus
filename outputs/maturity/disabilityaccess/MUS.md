# Mauritius

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The UN disability committee said in 2024 that Mauritius's new disability Act does not cover information in accessible formats](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). [It found only limited measures for accessible printed and electronic information](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). [It recommended accessibility standards for information technologies and websites](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). [The Copyright Act 2014 permits accessible copies of published works for print-disabled people](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CRPD_C_MUS_2-3_7812_E-2nd-3rd-reports.pdf>) (2020, before the three-year window).

**Delivered.** [The government's disability website lets persons with disabilities apply online](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CRPD_C_MUS_2-3_7812_E-2nd-3rd-reports.pdf>). [It has features for visually impaired users](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/CRPD_C_MUS_2-3_7812_E-2nd-3rd-reports.pdf>) (2020, before the three-year window).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the UN disability committee's 2024 review](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>). [It recommended standards for accessible information technologies and websites](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>).
