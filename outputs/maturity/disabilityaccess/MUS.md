# Mauritius

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In August 2026 the ICT ministry released its [Digital Service Framework](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Digital-Service-Framework.pdf>). It says ministries must adhere to it. It requires all digital services to comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. In 2024 the UN disability committee had said [the new disability Act does not cover information in accessible formats](<https://disability.govmu.org/disabilities/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Concluding-Observations-Final-2024.pdf>).

**Delivered.** In May 2026 [the AI Unit's website offered built-in accessibility tools](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>). They include read-aloud, text scaling, high contrast, a dyslexia-friendly font and keyboard navigation. In September 2025 [a Mauritian Sign Language e-dictionary was launched at the Beau-Bassin school for the deaf](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/societe/ecole-des-sourds-lancement-dun-dictionnaire-electronique-de-langue-des-signes/683699/>). The social integration minister praised his ministry's work on it.

**Audited.** The AI Unit says [it fixed all critical issues found in a May 2026 audit of its website](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>). It publishes no other findings.

***Also on file***. The AI Unit says [its website is working to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA](<https://aim.govmu.org/aim/accessibility>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services.
