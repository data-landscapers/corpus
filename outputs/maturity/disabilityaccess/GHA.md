# Ghana

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The digital minister promised to make all government digital platforms accessible, following standards such as WCAG](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/NCA-and-GFD-Lead-National-Celebration-of-IDPD-2025-on-Assistive-Technology-and-Inclusion-2.pdf>). [The Bank of Ghana's January 2025 directive requires financial providers to make websites and digital channels disability friendly](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Financial-Inclusion-for-Persons-with-Disabilities-Directive.pdf>). [It names voice and audio controls, larger font and sign-language videos](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Financial-Inclusion-for-Persons-with-Disabilities-Directive.pdf>). [It sets administrative penalties for providers that do not comply](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Financial-Inclusion-for-Persons-with-Disabilities-Directive.pdf>).

**Delivered.** [Ghana says the National Communication Authority, in its move to digital terrestrial television, has taken measures to make GTV news broadcasts accessible to the deaf](<https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=u%2F7HeWsLeAGf4bOeOKu3awum%2BSewPd75x9YtJIsnfd7wWVeIkx7T8atLYZizIHJzk1touKDXHXm66ALasS%2BmMw%3D%3D>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the NCA's work to make GTV news accessible to deaf viewers](<https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=u%2F7HeWsLeAGf4bOeOKu3awum%2BSewPd75x9YtJIsnfd7wWVeIkx7T8atLYZizIHJzk1touKDXHXm66ALasS%2BmMw%3D%3D>). [That work came with the move to digital terrestrial television](<https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=u%2F7HeWsLeAGf4bOeOKu3awum%2BSewPd75x9YtJIsnfd7wWVeIkx7T8atLYZizIHJzk1touKDXHXm66ALasS%2BmMw%3D%3D>).
