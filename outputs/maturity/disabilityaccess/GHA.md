# Ghana

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The Minister of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations promised to improve accessibility on all government digital platforms by following standards such as WCAG](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/NCA-and-GFD-Lead-National-Celebration-of-IDPD-2025-on-Assistive-Technology-and-Inclusion-2.pdf>). [The Bank of Ghana's Financial Inclusion for Persons with Disabilities Directive of January 2025 requires regulated financial service providers to make websites and digital service channels disability friendly and to add features such as voice and audio controls, larger font and sign language videos to online platforms, with administrative penalties for non-compliance](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Financial-Inclusion-for-Persons-with-Disabilities-Directive.pdf>).

**Delivered.** [Ghana says the National Communication Authority, in its move to digital terrestrial television, has taken measures to make GTV news broadcasts accessible to the deaf](<https://docstore.ohchr.org/SelfServices/FilesHandler.ashx?enc=u%2F7HeWsLeAGf4bOeOKu3awum%2BSewPd75x9YtJIsnfd7wWVeIkx7T8atLYZizIHJzk1touKDXHXm66ALasS%2BmMw%3D%3D>).

**Audited.** No source found since October 2023 gives the results of an accessibility audit of public websites or digital services.
