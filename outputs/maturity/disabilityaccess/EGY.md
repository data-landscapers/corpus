# Egypt

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The Ministry of Transport says it continues to make its websites inclusive](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/17/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B0%D9%88%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%85%D9%85-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%88%D9%82%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%A7/7549148>). [A presidential initiative aims to spread accessibility standards across government websites](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5882651.aspx>). [The National Academy of IT for Persons with Disabilities implements it](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5882651.aspx>). [NTRA and the Central Bank required mobile operators to make e-wallets accessible](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-instructions-to-facilitate-the-use-of-mobile-e-wallet-services-for-people-of-determination/>). [The rules ask for sign language, Braille contracts and apps that support screen readers](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-instructions-to-facilitate-the-use-of-mobile-e-wallet-services-for-people-of-determination/>).

**Delivered.** [Egypt's Ministry of Transport added accessibility tools to its website, including text-to-speech, a sign-language interpreter and keyboard navigation, with the ICT ministry](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/17/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B0%D9%88%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%85%D9%85-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%88%D9%82%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%A7/7549148>). [NTRA previously established a sign language complaint service via video calls on its website](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-instructions-to-facilitate-the-use-of-mobile-e-wallet-services-for-people-of-determination/>).
