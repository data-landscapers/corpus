# Egypt

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The Ministry of Transport says it continues to develop its website and the websites of its affiliated authorities and companies for inclusion](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/17/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B0%D9%88%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%85%D9%85-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%88%D9%82%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%A7/7549148>). [A presidential initiative to strengthen digital accessibility of government websites, implemented by the National Academy of IT for Persons with Disabilities, aims to generalise accessibility standards across government bodies' sites](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5882651.aspx>). [Egypt's NTRA, with the Central Bank, issued instructions requiring mobile operators to make e-wallet services accessible to people with disabilities, with sign language interpreters, Braille contracts and mobile apps with audio interfaces, voice dictation and screen reader support](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-instructions-to-facilitate-the-use-of-mobile-e-wallet-services-for-people-of-determination/>).

**Delivered.** [Egypt's Ministry of Transport added a digital accessibility service to its website mot.gov.eg with text-to-speech, speech-to-text, a sign-language interpreter, dyslexia fonts, a reading mask and keyboard navigation, working with the ICT ministry and the National Academy of IT for Persons with Disabilities](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/17/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B0%D9%88%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%85%D9%85-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%88%D9%82%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%A7/7549148>). [NTRA previously established a sign language complaint service via video calls on its website](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/en/ntra-issues-instructions-to-facilitate-the-use-of-mobile-e-wallet-services-for-people-of-determination/>).
