# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [CIPESA says the Ministry of Digital Transition and Digitalization is reviewing its digital transition policy with a focus on prioritising persons with disabilities](<https://cipesa.org/2025/11/100-activists-trained-to-advance-digital-inclusion-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-cote-divoire/>). [A ministry speaker said Law No 2024-352 of 6 June 2024 on electronic communications contains measures to enable inclusive access for persons with disabilities, though implementation has been slow](<https://cipesa.org/2025/11/100-activists-trained-to-advance-digital-inclusion-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-cote-divoire/>).

**Delivered.** [On 26 June 2025 the Ministry of Employment and Social Protection formally received, under a 13 May 2025 convention with the MTN Foundation, a job-matching website for job-seekers with disabilities, multimedia rooms at the Ivorian School for the Deaf and Felix Houphouet-Boigny University, and a YouTube channel for ECIS pupils](<https://www.emploi.gouv.ci/uploads/publications/17509697364.pdf>).
