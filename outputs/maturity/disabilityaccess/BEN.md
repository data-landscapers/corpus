# Benin

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** No rule or programme for digital accessibility has been found. [The government set up three digital classrooms for visually impaired learners](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>), in Cotonou-Sègbèya, Parakou and one other town. [They have screen readers, braille printers and braille displays](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>).

**Delivered.** [La Nation reports three government digital classrooms for visually impaired learners, in Cotonou-Sègbèya, Parakou and a third town in Benin](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>). [Each has screen readers, braille printers and braille displays](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>). [In 2023-2024, 31 pupils trained at Sègbèya](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the government's digital classrooms for visually impaired learners](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>). [Their screen readers and braille displays served 31 pupils at Sègbèya in 2023-2024](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>).
