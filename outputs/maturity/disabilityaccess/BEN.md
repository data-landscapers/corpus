# Benin

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** No source found says digital accessibility for persons with disabilities is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [La Nation reports the government set up three digital classrooms for visually impaired learners (Cotonou-Sègbèya, Parakou, and one other) with screen readers, braille printers and braille displays, and 31 pupils of CEG Sègbèya trained at Sègbèya in 2023-2024](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/inclusion-des-personnes-handicapees-au-benin-une-avancee-grace-aux-classes-numeriques>). A later answer places the country above that step.
