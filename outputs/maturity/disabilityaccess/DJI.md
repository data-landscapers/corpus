# Djibouti

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In July 2024 the National Agency for Persons with Disabilities (ANPH) [held a workshop on its survey of sign language use](<https://www.lanation.dj/anph-mieux-comprendre-lusage-et-la-connaissance-des-langues-des-signes-parmi-les-personnes-malentendantes/>). The survey is to digitise the Djiboutian sign language dictionary. The agency was finishing a Play Store app to learn the language. In October 2024 [an ANPH concept note presented a hackathon to build Mobile ID apps](<https://evenement.anph.dj/pdf/note.pdf>) that improve access to public services for persons with disabilities. [Law 151/AN/22 of July 2022](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/113314/DJI-113314.pdf>) asks the State to ensure access to information and electronic services for persons with disabilities.

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found. The nearest fact found is [the sign language app the agency was finishing in July 2024](<https://www.lanation.dj/anph-mieux-comprendre-lusage-et-la-connaissance-des-langues-des-signes-parmi-les-personnes-malentendantes/>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found. The nearest fact found is [the agency's 2024 survey of sign language use](<https://www.lanation.dj/anph-mieux-comprendre-lusage-et-la-connaissance-des-langues-des-signes-parmi-les-personnes-malentendantes/>), which is not a test of websites.

***Also on file***. The [Agence Nationale des Personnes Handicapées (ANPH)](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/113314/DJI-113314.pdf>). Its [2022 convention with the national IT agency ANSIE](<https://www.anph.dj/2022/01/18/signature-de-la-convention-entre-lanph-ansie/>) covers information systems for disability.

***Not found***. No rule in force since October 2023 has been found. No delivery or audit has been found either.
