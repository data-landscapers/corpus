# Lesotho

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** No rule or programme for digital accessibility has been found. [The ICT ministry and Vodacom opened a digital library for persons with disabilities at the State Library in Maseru](<https://www.gov.ls/education/4g-enabled-digital-library-unveiled-for-persons-with-disability-in-maseru/>). [It has a Braille printer and eye-tracking technology](<https://www.gov.ls/education/4g-enabled-digital-library-unveiled-for-persons-with-disability-in-maseru/>).

**Delivered.** [The ICT ministry and Vodacom unveiled the Insight Centre, a 4G-enabled digital library, at the State Library in Maseru](<https://www.gov.ls/education/4g-enabled-digital-library-unveiled-for-persons-with-disability-in-maseru/>). [It has 10 computers, a Braille printer and eye-tracking technology for persons with disabilities](<https://www.gov.ls/education/4g-enabled-digital-library-unveiled-for-persons-with-disability-in-maseru/>).
