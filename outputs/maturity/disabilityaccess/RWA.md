# Rwanda

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [RISA's Government of Rwanda Websites Guideline says all government websites must meet or exceed WCAG 2.2](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>). [It asks for code that supports assistive technologies, keyboard access and captions](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>). [The ICT ministry's National Digital Inclusion Strategy of September 2022 targets digital exclusion of persons with disabilities](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/The_Rwanda_National_Digital_Inclusion_Strategy.pdf>) (2022, before the three-year window).

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is RISA's websites guideline](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>). [It requires all government websites to meet WCAG 2.2](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>).

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. [The nearest held fact is the same guideline](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>), which sets the WCAG 2.2 test.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says accessible digital services or assistive technology were delivered.
