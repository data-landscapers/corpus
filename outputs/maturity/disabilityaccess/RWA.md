# Rwanda

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** In 2025 RISA's Government of Rwanda Websites Guideline said [all government websites must meet or exceed WCAG 2.2](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>). It asks for code that supports assistive technologies, keyboard access and captions. [The ICT ministry's National Digital Inclusion Strategy of September 2022 targets digital exclusion of persons with disabilities](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/The_Rwanda_National_Digital_Inclusion_Strategy.pdf>).

**Delivered.** GPE reports that [the Rwanda Basic Education Board had recorded 96 edutainment episodes by September 2024](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/results/country-journeys/rwanda-education-goes-digital>). They carry sign language for deaf children and audio for blind children. They are broadcast on radio and TV and online.

**Audited.** No published audit of how accessible public websites or digital services are has been found since October 2023. The nearest fact found is [RISA's websites guideline](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>), which sets the WCAG 2.2 test.
