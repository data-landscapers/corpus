# Rwanda

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The Rwanda Information Society Authority's Government of Rwanda Websites Guideline states that all government websites must meet or exceed WCAG 2.2, with semantic HTML and ARIA roles supporting assistive technologies, keyboard access and captions](<https://guidelines.risa.gov.rw/books/government-of-rwanda-websites-guideline/page/accessibility>). [The Ministry of ICT and Innovation's National Digital Inclusion Strategy, finalised in September 2022 with RISA and the National Council of Persons with Disabilities on the steering committee, targets digital exclusion of persons with disabilities and calls for reasonable accommodation in solution design (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/The_Rwanda_National_Digital_Inclusion_Strategy.pdf>).
