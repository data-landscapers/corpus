# Libya

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** A September 2026 paper says [Libya issued its National Digital Accessibility Policy in December 2024](<https://journal.utripoli.edu.ly/index.php/Alqalam/article/download/1972/1479/4024>). It describes the policy as mandatory, based on WCAG 2.2, with a timetable from 2025 to 2030. The General Information Authority dates [its adoption to April 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>). On 28 January 2026 the Authority and ESCWA [began putting the policy into effect](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>), and named mandatory compliance as the next phase. [The Authority held a national workshop on the draft policy in June 2024](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2024/06/26/the-public-information-authority-organizes-a-national-consultative-workshop-on-the-preparation-of-the-national-policy-for-digital-accessibility-for-persons-with-disabilities/>). In October 2026 two ministers [launched the Wasl initiative](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367274&lang=ar>), one of whose tracks eases access of persons with disabilities to services.

**Delivered.** No delivery of accessible digital services or assistive technology by a government body has been found. The nearest fact found is [the start of work on the policy in January 2026](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>).

**Audited.** The September 2026 paper reports [observing more than 20 government and private platforms](<https://journal.utripoli.edu.ly/index.php/Alqalam/article/download/1972/1479/4024>). It found that most fail the policy's accessibility standards. Of the designers and users it asked, 70% knew the principles but not the policy.

***Also on file***. [The Wasl initiative](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367274&lang=ar>) of the social affairs ministry and the digital economy minister.

***Not found***. No delivery of accessible services by a government body has been found. The September 2026 audit is an academic paper, which does not count.
