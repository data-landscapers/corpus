# Libya

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [The National Digital Accessibility Policy was adopted in April 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>). [On 28 January 2026 the General Information Authority and ESCWA began putting it into effect](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>). [Mandatory compliance was named as the next phase](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/04/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-actual-implementation-path-of-the-national-digital-accessibility-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>). [The Authority's chairman said the policy will bring services for people with disabilities up to international standards](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/04/24/prime-minister-of-the-government-of-national-unity-approves-the-national-strategy-for-government-digital-transformation/>). [In October 2026 two ministers launched the Wasl initiative](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367274&lang=ar>). [One of its tracks eases access of persons with disabilities to services and knowledge](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367274&lang=ar>).
