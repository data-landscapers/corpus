# Algeria

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Executive decree 26-98 of 31 January 2026 requires ministries to make information accessible to persons with specific needs](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026013.pdf>). [It names braille, adapted computing, sign language and subtitling](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026013.pdf>). [It also requires adapted ICT equipment for pupils, students and trainees](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026013.pdf>). [The Ministry of Post and Telecommunications ran app contests for people with specific needs in 2021 and 2022](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/coordination-qualitative-avec-differents-secteurs/autonomisation-des-personnes-a-besoins-specifiques/>). [Sign-language apps were among them (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/coordination-qualitative-avec-differents-secteurs/autonomisation-des-personnes-a-besoins-specifiques/>).
