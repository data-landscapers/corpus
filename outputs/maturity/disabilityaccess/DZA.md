# Algeria

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [Executive decree 26-98 of 31 January 2026 (Arts 15-18, Journal officiel no. 13) requires ministries and bodies to take measures for access to information and communication for persons with specific needs, using braille press, adapted computing tools, sign language and subtitling, and to provide adapted ICT equipment and technical aids to pupils, students and trainees](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026013.pdf>). [The Ministry of Post and Telecommunications says it launched national contests in 2021 and 2022 for software and mobile apps for people with specific needs (sign-language apps in 2022) and gives technical support to the winning projects so they can be offered free (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/coordination-qualitative-avec-differents-secteurs/autonomisation-des-personnes-a-besoins-specifiques/>).
