# Nigeria

## Digital accessibility

**Accessibility rule.** [NITDA said it is looking to advance national accessibility standards and that accessibility considerations are already incorporated into its revised website guidelines for government institutions](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/16/nitda-strengthens-digital-accessibility-persons-with-disabilities/>). [NITDA said it is considering national accessibility standards and has incorporated accessibility requirements into revised website guidelines for government institutions](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/nigerian-govt-platforms-fail-access-test/KA3Ww7dzPjkqrydZ>). [NITDA's Standards and Guidelines for Government Websites require government institutions to adopt WCAG and to test accessibility-related applications and downloads with assistive technologies, and NITDA says it monitors compliance](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/31/nigeria-wants-assistive-tech-who-is-fixing-its-inaccessible-digital-platforms/>). In October 2026 GovInsider reported that an Open Government Partnership group [working with NITDA plans to pilot standards](<https://govinsider.asia/intl-en/article/the-jargon-problem-keeping-public-participation-out-of-dpi-design-1>) making digital public infrastructure usable by rural, non-literate and visually impaired people, with 10 federal agencies within two years.
