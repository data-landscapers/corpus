# Mauritius

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In May 2025 the IT minister launched the first Community Digital Empowerment Programme in Bel Air](<https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/index.php/community-digital-empowerment-programme/>). [It ran over two days, on 10 and 11 May](<https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/index.php/community-digital-empowerment-programme/>). [Similar events are planned in other regions](<https://mdpa.govmu.org/mdpa/index.php/community-digital-empowerment-programme/>). In November 2026 Mauritius Telecom and AWS [launched a free portal of 16 digital and AI courses](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8162/mauritius-telecom-launches-free-digital-and-ai-skills-training-platform>), toward the government's target of 50,000 people trained. [The 2024 ICT household indicators give 62.2 per cent of people aged five and over able to use a computer](<https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218>).

**Share measured.** [The Mauritius Digital Promotion Agency publishes household ICT indicators](<https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218>). [Its table gives 62.2 per cent of persons aged five and over able to use a computer in 2024](<https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218>).

**Half or more.** [In 2024, 62.2 per cent of people aged five and over could use a computer](<https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218>), more than half. The agency calls it the [digital literacy rate](<https://indicators.govmu.org/ictindicators/?page_id=3218>). The figure covers children as well as adults.
