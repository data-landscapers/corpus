# South Sudan

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [MTN South Sudan launched the Rumbek ICT Hub in Lakes State with the Lakes State Government on 8 September 2026 to provide ICT training for young people, welcomed by the national ICT minister](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/mtn-south-sudan-launches-rumbek-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-innovation/>). [MTN South Sudan launched the Aweil ICT Hub offering digital skills training and scholarships, in partnership with the Northern Bahr el Ghazal State government, per the state Information Minister and MTN's CEO](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/mtn-south-sudan-launches-aweil-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-youth-innovation/>). [The Ministry of ICT and Postal Services lists its Girls in ICT initiative, which gives girls and young women opportunities to gain ICT knowledge and skills, as part of its youth digital programme](<https://mictps.gov.ss/projects/>).
