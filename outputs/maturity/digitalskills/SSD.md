# South Sudan

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In November 2025 the ICT ministry opened an MTN-financed ICT hub in Bor for young people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/06/mtn-partners-with-government-to-launch-bor-ict-hub-and-online-learning-programs/>). [MTN opened further hubs in Rumbek in September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/mtn-south-sudan-launches-rumbek-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-innovation/>), with the Lakes State government. [Another opened in Aweil with the Northern Bahr el Ghazal government](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/mtn-south-sudan-launches-aweil-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-youth-innovation/>). [Two ministries and MTN launched the Junub Academy e-learning platform in June 2026](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/data-free-junub-academy-brings-ai-supported-education-to-south-sudan/>). [The ICT ministry lists a Girls in ICT initiative in its youth programme](<https://mictps.gov.ss/projects/>).
