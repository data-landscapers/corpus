# Zambia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The SMART Zambia Institute's digital literacy programme for citizens trained 243 people in six localities in 2024](<https://academy.itu.int/about/news/itu-and-norway-support-dtc-zambia-equip-more-240-cooperative-members-across-6-localities-basic-and>). [The ITU and Norway funded it](<https://academy.itu.int/about/news/itu-and-norway-support-dtc-zambia-equip-more-240-cooperative-members-across-6-localities-basic-and>). [In April 2025 the institute launched a three-week Learn-A-Thon for girls in all provinces](<https://theagencymedia.co.zm/smart-zambia-institute-launches-national-learn-a-thon-to-equip-girls-with-21st-century-digital-skills/>). [With the ITU and the youth ministry, it runs Digital Transformation Centres for young people and women](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/smart-zambia-and-itu-equip-mpika-youth-resource-centre-to-boost-digital-skills/>). [In August 2026 it equipped a youth centre in Kalomo as one](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/12/smart-zambia-and-itu-equip-kalomo-youth-centre-to-advance-digital-inclusion/>). [The World Bank-financed Digital Zambia Acceleration Project includes basic digital literacy for underserved citizens](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00327850>).
