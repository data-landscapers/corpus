# Zambia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [SMART Zambia Institute with the ITU handed ICT equipment to the Mukwela Youth Resource Centre in Kalomo to become a Digital Transformation Centre for digital-skills training of young people](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/12/smart-zambia-and-itu-equip-kalomo-youth-centre-to-advance-digital-inclusion/>). [SMART Zambia Institute, with the ITU and the Ministry of Youth, Sport and Arts, runs the Digital Transformation Centres Initiative to equip young people and women with digital skills, and has handed ICT equipment to the Mpika Youth Resource Centre with more handovers planned](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/05/smart-zambia-and-itu-equip-mpika-youth-resource-centre-to-boost-digital-skills/>). [A December 2024 procurement notice for the World Bank-financed Digital Zambia Acceleration Project includes a component on basic digital literacy for underserved citizens](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00327850>).
