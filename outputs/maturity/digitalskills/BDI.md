# Burundi

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The ministry in charge of the digital economy, with UN Women Burundi and Belgian funding, launched phase III of the AGCCI coding camps on 25 September 2025, training 102 girls aged 17-25 from all provinces in digital literacy and coding, with telecentres supported at Bubanza, Kirundo and Ruyigi](<https://abpinfo.bi/2025/09/30/la-promotion-des-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes-filles-burundaises-une-priorite/>). [The PAFEN project launched 12 September 2024 includes development of digital skills for the population among its priority components, per its coordinator](<https://en.abpinfo.bi/official-launch-of-the-project-to-support-the-foundations-of-the-digital-economy-in-burundi/>). In September 2026 PAFEN's coordinator [said a basic digital skills programme to train 10,000 young people within a year would start soon](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7509922575322566656>).
