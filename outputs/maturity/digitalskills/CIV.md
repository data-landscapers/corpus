# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Ministry of Digital Transition presented digital-training infrastructure investments in the PND 2026-2030, with an estimated budget of 95 billion FCFA, aiming to train tens of thousands of digital talents across the territory, under a strategy pillar on developing digital skills](<https://www.afriqueeconomie.net/2026/09/pnd-2026-2030-5413-milliards-de-fcfa-dopportunites-dinvestissement-presentees-au-secteur-prive-dans-le-numerique/>). [Cote d'Ivoire's digital minister and ITU discussed efforts to expand digital skills programmes for workforce development and digital inclusion](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6194/cote-divoire-and-itu-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-development-priorities>). The Ministry of Digital Transition's [draft 2025 budget](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/173214119048.pdf>) lists digital skills for women, young people and vulnerable people among the activities of a digital support project funded at FCFA 1.5 billion. [CIPESA says ANSUT's universal access efforts include digital literacy and skills programs](<https://cipesa.org/2025/11/100-activists-trained-to-advance-digital-inclusion-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-cote-divoire/>).
