# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [A youth ministry adviser said in July 2025 that the government's youth programme had given digital skills to 51,601 young people since 2023](<https://digitalmag.ci/journee-mondiale-des-competences-des-jeunes-51601-jeunes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-et-ia-en-cote-divoire/>). Twenty-one ministries run it. [In February 2025 the Prime Minister launched the GENIE project](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/02/27/cote-divoire/technologie/cote-divoire-projet-genie-installation-de-35-centres-numeriques-dans-des-colleges-lycees-universites-et-mairies-pour-la-formation-de-plus-de-45000-jeunes-en-competences-digitales_184828.html>). [Its 35 centres in Abidjan and Grand-Bassam are to train over 45,000 young people](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/02/27/cote-divoire/technologie/cote-divoire-projet-genie-installation-de-35-centres-numeriques-dans-des-colleges-lycees-universites-et-mairies-pour-la-formation-de-plus-de-45000-jeunes-en-competences-digitales_184828.html>). [The 2026-2030 national plan sets out digital-training investments of about 95 billion FCFA](<https://www.afriqueeconomie.net/2026/09/pnd-2026-2030-5413-milliards-de-fcfa-dopportunites-dinvestissement-presentees-au-secteur-prive-dans-le-numerique/>). [An IDA loan of October 2025 funds digital skills for young people and women](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19406>). [The universal access agency ANSUT also runs digital literacy programmes, CIPESA says](<https://cipesa.org/2025/11/100-activists-trained-to-advance-digital-inclusion-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-cote-divoire/>). In November 2026 the digital minister [set a target of 200,000 people trained in cybersecurity, networking and AI by 2030](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/127288-cybersecurite-abidjan-devient-le-qg-americain-pour-securiser-l-afrique-de-l-ouest>), under a programme of the TIA Foundation and Cisco.
