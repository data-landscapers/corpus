# Senegal

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Universite numerique Cheikh Hamidou Kane and the Mastercard Foundation launched the FORCE-N programme in 2021](<https://aps.sn/force-n-plus-de-100-mille-jeunes-formes-dans-les-metiers-du-numerique-en-cinq-ans-responsable/>). [In June 2026 its director said it had trained more than 100,000 young people in certified digital-trade courses across the 14 regions](<https://aps.sn/force-n-plus-de-100-mille-jeunes-formes-dans-les-metiers-du-numerique-en-cinq-ans-responsable/>). [On 8 September 2025 the university and the digital ministry launched the first Vacances citoyennes numeriques](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/senegal-lancement-vacances-citoyennes-numeriques/>). [The programme ran in 18 Espaces numeriques ouverts to train over 1,500 young people](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/senegal-lancement-vacances-citoyennes-numeriques/>). [AITN reported 1,869 enrolled in software and cybersecurity tracks](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/senegal-lancement-vacances-citoyennes-numeriques/>).
