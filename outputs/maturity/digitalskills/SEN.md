# Senegal

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The FORCE-N programme, launched in 2021 by the Universite numerique Cheikh Hamidou Kane and the Mastercard Foundation, has according to its director trained more than 100,000 young people in certified digital-trade courses across the 14 regions](<https://aps.sn/force-n-plus-de-100-mille-jeunes-formes-dans-les-metiers-du-numerique-en-cinq-ans-responsable/>). [The Universite numerique Cheikh Hamidou Kane, with the telecommunications and digital ministry, launched on 8 September 2025 the first Vacances citoyennes numeriques, a programme in 18 Espaces numeriques ouverts to train over 1,500 young people, with 1,869 enrolled in software and cybersecurity tracks, per AITN](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/senegal-lancement-vacances-citoyennes-numeriques/>).
