# Chad

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** In September 2024 the World Bank [approved the Chad Digital Transformation Project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/09/25/world-bank-increases-access-to-broadband-connectivity-in-chad>). It is to train about 40,000 people in digital skills. At least 25,000 of them are to be women and girls. [In July 2026 a World Bank-financed project handed 3,000 payment terminals to the finance ministry](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-3-000-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-remis-au-ministere-des-finances-pour-moderniser-la-collecte-des-recettes-publiques/>). The project provides for financial and digital literacy programmes. [UNHCR and the government set up the Iridimi Digital Learning Center](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/arizona-state-university-and-unhcr-offer-online-training-400-refugees-and>). It offers online training to refugees and young people from host communities. [A 2025-2029 digital inclusion project led by ADPV with the education ministry trained over 150 young people in its pilot](<https://www.unesco.org/sdg4education2030/en/knowledge-hub/inclusion-numerique-et-competences-professionnelles-autonomiser-les-jeunes-et-renforcer-les>). [UNESCO's 2024-2025 project on digital skills and media literacy](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>) allocates 15% of its USD 166,800 to Chad.
