# Chad

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [UNESCO's project 3210652071 (USD 166,800, 66% progress, 2024-2025) on digital skills and media and information literacy for populations is on going, with an allocation to this country (Tchad 15%)](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [UNHCR and the Government of Chad established the Iridimi Digital Learning Center, which with Arizona State University offers online training to refugees and host-community youth, with certified office tools and digital literacy training to follow](<https://www.unhcr.org/africa/news/press-releases/arizona-state-university-and-unhcr-offer-online-training-400-refugees-and>). [UNESCO's hub records a 2025-2029 digital inclusion project led by ADPV with the education ministry, whose pilot trained over 150 young people](<https://www.unesco.org/sdg4education2030/en/knowledge-hub/inclusion-numerique-et-competences-professionnelles-autonomiser-les-jeunes-et-renforcer-les>).
