# Cameroon

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In July 2024 the national ICT agency ANTIC launched the sixth Cameroon Digital Skills Campaign in Douala](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/3519/en/homeat-launch-of-cameroon-digital-skills-campaign-antic-dg-calls-for-investments-in-digital-education-of-youth>). [The three-day event drew over 100 students](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/3519/en/homeat-launch-of-cameroon-digital-skills-campaign-antic-dg-calls-for-investments-in-digital-education-of-youth>). [In June 2025 the posts and telecommunications minister launched a Digital Citizenship Festival](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/en/actualites/508-national-campaign-to-promote-cybersecurity>). [It aims to train 50,000 young people through regional and national events](<https://www.minpostel.gov.cm/index.php/en/actualites/508-national-campaign-to-promote-cybersecurity>). [A UNESCO project on digital skills allocates 35 per cent of its funds to Cameroon](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).
