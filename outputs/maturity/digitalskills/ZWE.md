# Zimbabwe

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [TechnoMag reports that UNDP, Zimbabwe's Ministry of Youth and Uncommon, with ICT ministry support, are launching a government VTC-based rural AI, digital and innovation hub at Nyamuroro, Gokwe, with a free one-year digital skills bootcamp for 50 youths from September 2026 and coding outreach targeting 3,000 learners and 300 teachers, in a model meant to be replicated at other VTCs](<https://technomag.co.zw/undp-partners-gvt-to-launch-first-rural-ai-digital-and-innovation-hub-at-nyamuroro-vtc-in-gokwe/>). [Minister Mavetera said the government is implementing digital skills programmes targeting 1.5 million citizens through Digital Ambassadors and self-learning portals](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>). [POTRAZ's digital centres gave nearly 15,000 people ICT training in 2025, with more than 257,000 accessing services, MISA Zimbabwe reports](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2026/08/16/internet-affordability-and-access-in-zimbabwe/>).

**Share measured.** [The 2025 ZIMSTAT and POTRAZ ICT household survey found 43.7 percent of Zimbabweans have basic digital skills](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/ai-gains-ground-as-zimbabweans-turn-to-digital-learning/>).
