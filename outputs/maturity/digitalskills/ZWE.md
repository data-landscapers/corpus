# Zimbabwe

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The regulator POTRAZ's digital centres gave nearly 15,000 people ICT training in 2025](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2026/08/16/internet-affordability-and-access-in-zimbabwe/>), MISA Zimbabwe reports. [In March 2026 the ICT minister said the Digital Skills Ambassadors Programme had trained over 2,300 people in one district in a week](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimbabwe-steps-up-digital-literacy-drive/>). [Minister Mavetera said the government aims to reach 1.5 million citizens](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>), through ambassadors and self-learning portals. [UNDP and the youth ministry are opening a rural digital hub at Nyamuroro, Gokwe](<https://technomag.co.zw/undp-partners-gvt-to-launch-first-rural-ai-digital-and-innovation-hub-at-nyamuroro-vtc-in-gokwe/>). [It offers a free one-year bootcamp for 50 young people from September 2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/undp-partners-gvt-to-launch-first-rural-ai-digital-and-innovation-hub-at-nyamuroro-vtc-in-gokwe/>).

**Share measured.** [The 2025 ZIMSTAT and POTRAZ ICT household survey found 43.7 percent of Zimbabweans have basic digital skills](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/ai-gains-ground-as-zimbabweans-turn-to-digital-learning/>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/ai-gains-ground-as-zimbabweans-turn-to-digital-learning/>).
