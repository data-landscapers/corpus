# Morocco

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The ST Foundation's Digital Unify programme, run with the youth ministry, trained 25,765 people in 45 labs in 2025](<https://www.stfoundation.org/morocco-2/>). [It works in 17 cities](<https://www.stfoundation.org/morocco-2/>). [The digital transition ministry says its JobIntech programme had trained 1,000 people by August 2024](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>). [In January 2026 the minister told parliament that two national digital-skills and AI programmes for ages 8 to 18 had begun](<https://fr.alyaoum24.com/numerique-et-innovation/53166.html>). [Youth-centre workshops had reached over 740 children since October 2025](<https://fr.alyaoum24.com/numerique-et-innovation/53166.html>). [The Classes Connectées programme is adding connected rooms to 40 more rural primary schools](<https://lematin.ma/societe/classes-connectees-dir-iddik-inwi-lance-un-appel-national-au-benevolat/331445>), with the education ministry.

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>).
