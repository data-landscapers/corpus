# Morocco

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The MTNRA brochure states that 1000 talents had been trained through the JobIntech programme by the end of August 2024](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>). [Le Matin reports that Fondation Al Mada with inwi, Managem, Nareva and Injaz Al Maghrib, in collaboration with the Ministry of National Education, is launching a new phase of the Classes Connectees Dir iddik programme equipping 40 more rural primary schools with connected multimedia rooms, with teachers trained in coding, robotics and AI under the ministerial Coding pour Tous programme to run workshops for sixth-year pupils](<https://lematin.ma/societe/classes-connectees-dir-iddik-inwi-lance-un-appel-national-au-benevolat/331445>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>).

**Half or more.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Coding pour Tous](<https://lematin.ma/societe/classes-connectees-dir-iddik-inwi-lance-un-appel-national-au-benevolat/331445>); [Oracle online certifying digital training platform](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/BrochureTalentNum%C3%A9rique_24092024_Fra.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills.
