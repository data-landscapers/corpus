# Eswatini

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [More than 100 participants completed computer literacy, Microsoft Office and AI training in the Macetjeni iHub pilot phase before the official launch](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-launches-macetjeni-ihub-to-drive-digital-innovation/>). [The hub enrolled a first cohort of 15 in a basic computer skills programme in June 2026 and completed the two-month course by end of July, per its manager](<https://www.undp.org/eswatini/news/macetjeni-ihub-opens-expand-digital-opportunities>). [The Government of Eswatini and UNDP launched the Macetjeni iHub on 6 August 2026, a centre offering digital skills training to young people, women, small business owners and persons with disabilities](<https://www.undp.org/eswatini/news/macetjeni-ihub-opens-expand-digital-opportunities>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-launches-macetjeni-ihub-to-drive-digital-innovation/>).
