# Gambia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The trade ministry signed agreements with three training institutions in September 2026](<https://newglobalmedia.net/motie-signs-mous-to-boost-digital-labour-skills/>) for a three-year WARDIP digital skills programme. It is meant for thousands of Gambians, mainly young people, women and people with disabilities. [The government launched it in November 2026 as a D40 million programme](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-launches-d40m-digital-skills-programme-for-1280-youths>) to train 1,280 young Gambians over three years. [The communications ministry and Smart Africa agreed in December 2025 to set up a digital academy](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/the-gambia-partners-with-smart-africa-to-launch-digital-skills-academy/>) for young people and women among others. [The same ministry launched the WE-Elevate Project in October 2025](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/government-launches-we-elevate-project-to-empower-women-in-the-digital-economy/>) for women-owned small businesses across the country. [An AI education app built with the basic education ministry was launched in November 2025](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/africa-missions-global-rolls-out-ai-education-for-gambia-students>). Africa Missions Global paid for course places for 1,000 students.
