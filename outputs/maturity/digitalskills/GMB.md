# Gambia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [MoTIE signed MoUs with three training institutions to deliver a three-year WARDIP digital skills programme for thousands of Gambians, particularly youth, women and persons with disabilities](<https://newglobalmedia.net/motie-signs-mous-to-boost-digital-labour-skills/>). [The Gambia's Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy and Smart Africa signed a framework agreement to implement the Smart Africa Digital Academy, which will provide digital training for youth and women among others, per TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/17/the-gambia-partners-with-smart-africa-to-launch-digital-skills-academy/>). [The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy, with the Digital Cooperation Organization and national partners, launched the WE-Elevate Project to improve digital skills of women-owned micro, small and medium enterprises across the country](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/government-launches-we-elevate-project-to-empower-women-in-the-digital-economy/>).
