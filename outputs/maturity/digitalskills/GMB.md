# Gambia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [MoTIE signed MoUs with three training institutions to deliver a three-year WARDIP digital skills programme for thousands of Gambians, particularly youth, women and persons with disabilities](<https://newglobalmedia.net/motie-signs-mous-to-boost-digital-labour-skills/>). [The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy, with the Digital Cooperation Organization and national partners, launched the WE-Elevate Project to improve digital skills of women-owned micro, small and medium enterprises across the country](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/government-launches-we-elevate-project-to-empower-women-in-the-digital-economy/>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newglobalmedia.net/motie-signs-mous-to-boost-digital-labour-skills/>).

**Half or more.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newglobalmedia.net/motie-signs-mous-to-boost-digital-labour-skills/>).
