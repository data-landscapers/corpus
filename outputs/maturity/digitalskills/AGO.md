# Angola

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** In October 2026 President João Lourenço [authorised US$20 million](<https://poligrafoafrica.com/fact-check/joao-lourenco-autoriza-despesa-de-20-milhoes-usd-para-capacitacao-digital-em-angola/>) for specialised centres, hired by public tender, to raise the digital skills of the working population at three levels, especially young people and public servants. [Presidential Despacho n.º 324/26 authorised US$5 million, World Bank-financed under PADA, for the National Programme for Training Local Agents in Basic Digital Skills, Inclusion and Digital Citizenship, implemented by IMA with FAS support](<https://pti.ao/formacao-de-agentes-locais-o-passo-seguinte-ao-investimento-em-infra-estrutura-digital/>). [The PADA terms of reference say Subcomponent 3.2 supports the Government in developing and implementing a national strategy for basic, intermediate and advanced digital skills for young and underemployed people, starting with a needs assessment](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/PADA_Td_R_Especialista_em_Competencias_Digitais_1_c2f8985540.pdf>). A May 2024 memorandum [plans a Digital Governance Academy that will also run digital literacy programmes for citizens](<https://ega.ee/mou-angola/>).
