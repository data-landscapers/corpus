# Angola

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In October 2026 President João Lourenço authorised US$20 million for digital-skills centres](<https://poligrafoafrica.com/fact-check/joao-lourenco-autoriza-despesa-de-20-milhoes-usd-para-capacitacao-digital-em-angola/>), hired by public tender. [They are to train the working population, especially young people](<https://poligrafoafrica.com/fact-check/joao-lourenco-autoriza-despesa-de-20-milhoes-usd-para-capacitacao-digital-em-angola/>). [A presidential order of August 2026 gave US$5 million to a national programme in basic digital skills](<https://pti.ao/formacao-de-agentes-locais-o-passo-seguinte-ao-investimento-em-infra-estrutura-digital/>), financed by the World Bank. [The World Bank-funded PADA project is to support a national digital-skills strategy](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/PADA_Td_R_Especialista_em_Competencias_Digitais_1_c2f8985540.pdf>). [In June 2024 the interbank services company EMIS said its Cidadão Digital project had trained over 500,000 young people](<https://www.angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/programa-cidadao-digital-investe-mais-de-400-milhoes-de-kwanzas/>). [The training was in Luanda and Benguela](<https://www.angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/programa-cidadao-digital-investe-mais-de-400-milhoes-de-kwanzas/>). [By December 2024 it reported over 600,000 people reached since 2022](<https://www.menosfios.com/projeto-cidadao-digital-alcancou-mais-de-600-mil-pessoas-revela-emis/>).
