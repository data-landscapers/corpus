# Mozambique

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The ITU-EU VaMoz Digital project, sponsored by Mozambique's Ministry of Communications and Digital Transformation, ran a Matola pilot training programme that trained 50 youth and 5 teachers, after a National Digital Skills Assessment](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>). [The World Bank says the Mozambique Digital Acceleration Project, under implementation in the country, has promotion of digital skills among its priorities](<https://www.oeconomico.com/mocambique-cria-mapa-nacional-da-banda-larga-para-orientar-investimento-e-reduzir-assimetrias-digitais/>). [The Ministry of Youth and Sports signed an MoU with MyRide Technologies and SoulCode for a national digital-capacity programme to train about 40,000 young people (36,000 in digital literacy, 3,660 in advanced-technology bootcamps), piloting in five provinces](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-lanca-programa-para-capacitar-40-mil-jovens-em-competencias-digitais/>).
