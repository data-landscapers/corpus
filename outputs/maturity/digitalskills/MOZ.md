# Mozambique

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In June 2026 the youth ministry signed for a national programme to train about 40,000 young people](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-lanca-programa-para-capacitar-40-mil-jovens-em-competencias-digitais/>). [It is piloting in five provinces](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-lanca-programa-para-capacitar-40-mil-jovens-em-competencias-digitais/>). [An ITU-EU pilot in Matola trained 50 young people and 5 teachers](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>). [In May 2025 the education ministry signed to expand the DiGi school project](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/machel-fidus-vodacom-foundation-and-huawei-sign-protocol-with-government-to-expand-digi-project/>). [The Machel Fidus Association runs it in 24 secondary schools and has trained over 900 students](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/machel-fidus-vodacom-foundation-and-huawei-sign-protocol-with-government-to-expand-digi-project/>). [The Nampula technology incubator trained about 1,300 young people and startups in the first half of 2026](<https://idolo.co.mz/mctd-aposta-no-polo-digital-de-nampula/>). [The World Bank-funded Digital Acceleration Project lists digital skills among its priorities](<https://www.oeconomico.com/mocambique-cria-mapa-nacional-da-banda-larga-para-orientar-investimento-e-reduzir-assimetrias-digitais/>).

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the ITU-EU VaMoz Digital project followed a National Digital Skills Assessment with a pilot in Matola](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest fact: [the August 2026 Matola pilot trained 50 youth and 5 teachers](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Pages/projects/2023/mozambique-vamoz-digital.aspx>).
