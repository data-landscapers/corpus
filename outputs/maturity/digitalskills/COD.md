## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Femintech, an institution launched in 2017 with partners including Unicef, teaches digital literacy, AI and programming, and about a thousand girls have received the training since its start (Femintech's outgoing president)](<https://actualite.cd/2025/11/05/formation-numerique-en-rdc-femintech-octroie-41-brevets-aux-nouveaux-formes-en>). [UNESCO's project 3210652071 (USD 166,800, 66% progress, 2024-2025) on digital skills and media and information literacy for populations is on going, with an allocation to this country (DR Congo 20%)](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).
