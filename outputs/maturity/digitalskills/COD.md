# DR Congo

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In October 2025 the youth ministry signed an accord with Cisco and Cybastion to train 250,000 young people in digital skills](<https://acp.cd/nation/rdc-le-programme-national-de-formation-numerique-vecteur-dautonomisation-de-la-jeunesse-congolaise-ministre-de-la-jeunesse/>). [The first train-the-trainer phase, with 500 trainees, ended in Kinshasa in February 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1802-53044-facing-youth-job-pressures-congo-launches-plan-to-train-250-000-in-digital-skills>). [On 28 February 2026 the digital economy minister gave the trainers their certificates](<https://www.zolanews.net/250-000-jeunes-vises-pour-le-programme-national-de-formation-numerique-augustin-kibassa-maliba-remet-les-brevets-aux-formateurs/>). [They are to be deployed nationwide over five years](<https://www.zolanews.net/250-000-jeunes-vises-pour-le-programme-national-de-formation-numerique-augustin-kibassa-maliba-remet-les-brevets-aux-formateurs/>). [Femintech, with Unicef, says it has trained about a thousand girls since 2017](<https://actualite.cd/2025/11/05/formation-numerique-en-rdc-femintech-octroie-41-brevets-aux-nouveaux-formes-en>).
