# Niger

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In August 2026 the governor of Tahoua launched free digital training for 140 young people](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>). It was a leg of the Niger Digital Day tour, under the communications minister's patronage. [The 2026 tour is to cover all eight regions and over 800 young people](<https://anp.ne/maradi-la-caravane-niger-digital-day-lancee-pour-renforcer-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes/>). [Its organisers said it trained over 300 young people in six regions in 2025](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>). [In September 2026 Algérie Télécom visited ESCEP in Niamey to plan a skills centre](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-algerie-une-delegation-algerienne-visite-lescep-dans-le-cadre-dun-partenariat-axe-sur-le-numerique/>).
