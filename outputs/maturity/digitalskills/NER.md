# Niger

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The World Bank-funded Smart Villages project, under the information society agency ANSI, trained nearly 38,000 rural people in its first phase](<https://www.lesahel.org/atelier-de-formation-et-de-recyclage-lancement-de-la-deuxieme-phase-de-la-campagne-nationale-dalphabetisation-numerique-et-deducation-financiere-des-populations-en-milieu-rura/>). [By February 2025 its coordinator put the number trained at 350,000, with digital centres in all eight regions](<https://anp.ne/tic-le-projet-villages-intelligents-ambitionne-dassurer-linclusion-numerique-et-financiere-des-ruraux/>). [In August 2026 the governor of Tahoua launched free digital training for 140 young people](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>). It was a leg of the Niger Digital Day tour, under the communications minister's patronage. [The 2026 tour is to cover all eight regions and over 800 young people](<https://anp.ne/maradi-la-caravane-niger-digital-day-lancee-pour-renforcer-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes/>). [Its organisers said it trained over 300 young people in six regions in 2025](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>). [In September 2026 Algérie Télécom visited ESCEP in Niamey to plan a skills centre](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-algerie-une-delegation-algerienne-visite-lescep-dans-le-cadre-dun-partenariat-axe-sur-le-numerique/>).

**Share measured.** No survey since October 2023 has been found that gives the share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest fact found is [the Tahoua leg of August 2026](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>).
