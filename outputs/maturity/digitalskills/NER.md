# Niger

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Niger Digital Day's focal point says it trained more than 300 young people in 6 regions in 2025 and will cover all 8 regions this year](<https://anp.ne/niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-de-140-jeunes-a-tahoua/>). [An Algerie Telecom delegation visited ESCEP in Niamey on 9 September 2026 to study rapid deployment of a digital Skills Center, under a July 2026 partnership accord, to train people in ICT, cybersecurity, AI and robotics](<https://www.lesahel.org/cooperation-niger-algerie-une-delegation-algerienne-visite-lescep-dans-le-cadre-dun-partenariat-axe-sur-le-numerique/>). [The Niger Digital Day national caravan, launched at its Diffa leg on 3 September 2026 with about 60 participants, trains young people in digital tools and trades and is to tour all eight regions of Niger](<https://anp.ne/diffa-le-niger-digital-day-renforce-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes/>).
