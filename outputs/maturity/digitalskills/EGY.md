# Egypt

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology says its "Digital Egypt Generations" initiatives (Cubs, Juniors, Pioneers, Builders) trained about 156,000 people in 2025 and about 277,000 since launch through the end of 2025](<https://egyptinnovate.com/en/news/eng-raafat-hendy-277000-trainees-empowered-through-digital-egypt-generations-initiatives>). [MCIT's Decent Life digital knowledge and competencies programme has improved the digital skills of 164,840 citizens of a 218,155 target (85.80%)](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [MCIT and the Ministry of Labour agreed digital literacy and progressive digital-skills training for graduates, job seekers and labour office visitors](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7663/x>). [In October 2026 Minister Raafat Hindy said the ministry's digital-skills programmes run in all governorates and that over 2 million citizens benefited over ten years](<https://www.tafsela.net/98622>). [In November 2024 the deputy minister said the ministry's digital literacy initiatives had reached 1,122,837 citizens, about 93,494 of them in Decent Life villages](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7692723>).
