# Guinea

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Guinea inaugurated the Simandou Academy in November 2025 as a hub for digital skills](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/major-ai-dpi-initiatives-to-support-africa-single-digital-market-unveiled>). [The universal service agency ANSUTEN launched the third edition of its R-CUN digital training programme on 7 October 2025](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/10/3e-edition-r-cun-ansuten-insere-plus-de-1-500-beneficiaires-dont-60-de-femmes/>). [Its two earlier editions trained 450 people, and the third is for over 1,500](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/10/3e-edition-r-cun-ansuten-insere-plus-de-1-500-beneficiaires-dont-60-de-femmes/>). [In September 2026 Guinea approved the feasibility study for a National Digital Academy](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-advances-plans-for-national-digital-academy>).
