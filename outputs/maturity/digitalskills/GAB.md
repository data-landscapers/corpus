# Gabon

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [UNESCO's project 3210652071 (USD 166,800, 66% progress, 2024-2025) on digital skills and media and information literacy for populations is on going, with an allocation to this country (Gabon 10%)](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [The digital economy ministry, UNESCO and two universities launched the Global Skills Academy in October 2024, offering 2,000 free certified digital courses](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-academie-mondiale-des-competences/>), and [a March 2026 agreement with Smart Africa is to bring the Smart Africa Digital Academy to Gabon](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-gabon-sallie-a-smart-africa-pour-former-les-talents-du-numerique/>).
