# Gabon

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In January 2026 the digital infrastructure agency ANINF and Cybastion launched DigiEmpower, free training for 1,000 young people](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gabon-launches-digital-skills-program-for-1-000-youths-with-u-s-firm-cybastion>). [Its first cohort of 150 began in Libreville in April 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-ouverture-de-formations-gratuites-pour-150-jeunes-gabonais/>). [The Smart Africa Youth Chapter Gabon, launched online in May 2026, enrolled over 2,000 young people](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/05/04/lancement-du-programme-smart-africa-youth-chapter-gabon-sayc-gabon-en-faveur-de-la-jeunesse/>). [In October 2024 the digital economy ministry and UNESCO launched a Global Skills Academy](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-officiel-de-la-campagne-academie-mondiale-des-competences/>), with 2,000 free courses. [A March 2026 agreement is to bring the Smart Africa Digital Academy to Gabon](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-gabon-sallie-a-smart-africa-pour-former-les-talents-du-numerique/>). [A UNESCO digital-skills project allocates 10 per cent of its funds to Gabon](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).
