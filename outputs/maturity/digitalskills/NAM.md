# Namibia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [ITWeb Africa reports Huawei launched the Women in Tech Namibia initiative and the 2026 National Talent Cultivation Project (about 1,000 students), which the ICT minister welcomed as supporting government efforts on digital inclusion and ICT skills](<https://itweb.africa/article/huawei-launches-namibia-digital-skills-drive/mQwkoM6YbmR73r9A>). [NUST says Huawei and partners launched the Women in Tech Namibia National Talent Cultivation Programme at the 13 May 2026 Huawei ICT Congress, committing to support 1,000 women in technology over five years with women-focused digital skills training](<https://www.nust.na/huawei-ict-congress-2026-spotlights-ai-talent-and-smart-campus-innovation-namibia>). [NDP6 includes gender-responsive and youth-focused digital training programmes as part of its digital package](<https://neweralive.na/namibia-targets-full-digital-transformation/>). [In October 2026 the ICT minister highlighted Project 1000, which aims to build young people's digital skills and link them to jobs](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/digital-infrastructure-investment-a-path-to-new-opportunities/>).
