# Namibia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The ICT ministry's free Digital Literacy Programme, launched in July 2023, had certified over 900 people by January 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/01/27/mict-empowers-over-900-namibians-through-free-digital-literacy-programme/>). [Its courses ran in all fourteen regions](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/01/27/mict-empowers-over-900-namibians-through-free-digital-literacy-programme/>). [In July 2026 its Hardap office ran a two-week course for 20 people in Maltahohe](<https://neweralive.na/maltahohe-participants-urged-to-embrace-digital-learning/>). [In October 2026 the ICT minister highlighted Project 1000](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/digital-infrastructure-investment-a-path-to-new-opportunities/>), for young people's digital skills. [The national development plan of 2025 includes digital training for women and young people](<https://neweralive.na/namibia-targets-full-digital-transformation/>). [In May 2026 Huawei launched a Women in Tech programme](<https://itweb.africa/article/huawei-launches-namibia-digital-skills-drive/mQwkoM6YbmR73r9A>), which the minister welcomed.
