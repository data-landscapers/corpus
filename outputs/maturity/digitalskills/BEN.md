# Benin

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In October 2025 the government opened a national call for digital skills training for young people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/benin-accelerates-digital-transformation-with-new-oif-d-clic-training-program>). [The training is the OIF's D-CLIC programme, run through the School of Digital Professions](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/benin-accelerates-digital-transformation-with-new-oif-d-clic-training-program>). [In November 2026 Benin became the first pilot country of the SNAEIA network's AI initiative](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3738/intelligence-artificielle-innovation-benin-designe-premier-pays-pilote-initiative-reseau-consacree-ia/>). [Its programme trains young people in digital tools and skills](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3738/intelligence-artificielle-innovation-benin-designe-premier-pays-pilote-initiative-reseau-consacree-ia/>).
