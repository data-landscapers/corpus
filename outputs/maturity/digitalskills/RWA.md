# Rwanda

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** The World Bank-funded Digital Acceleration Project [will take the Digital Ambassadors Programme to all 2,148 cells](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/27947/news/technology/inside-rwandas-plan-to-fast-track-digital-transformation-by-2026>), The New Times reports. The ambassadors teach citizens to use digital devices and services. In October 2026 the education and ICT ministries, with Huawei, [launched the DigiTruck, a mobile classroom for free digital skills training](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-marks-completion-of-smart-education-project-phase-i-and-launches-the-digitruck-initiative>). It is to reach more than 5,000 young people, women and farmers in all 30 districts over three years. It forms part of a goal to train one million citizens. No count of people trained so far is on record.
