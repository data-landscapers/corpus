# South Africa

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The communications department says its NEMISA programme trained 296,203 people in basic digital literacy from 2020/21 to late 2024](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RNW1084-2025-06-23_removed.pdf>). [It ran in all nine provinces](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RNW1084-2025-06-23_removed.pdf>). [The Collective X, which coordinates the National Digital Skills Plan, reports 4,000 beneficiaries across all provinces](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/16/south-africa-152m-digital-skills-jobs/>). [Microsoft says it trained over 150,000 people in digital and AI skills in 2024](<https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/microsoft-invests-zar-5-4bn-in-south-africa/>). [In September 2026 NEMISA trained over 100 women in Tzaneen](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/south-africa-subsea-cable-ai-digital-skills-connectivity/>).
