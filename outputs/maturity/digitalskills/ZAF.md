# South Africa

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Microsoft says more than 150,000 people in South Africa were trained in digital and AI skills in 2024 through its Skills for Jobs programme](<https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/microsoft-invests-zar-5-4bn-in-south-africa/>). [More than 100 women in Tzaneen, Limpopo received digital skills training from the Film and Publications Board and NEMISA under the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies' Gender-based Violence and Femicide Awareness Programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/south-africa-subsea-cable-ai-digital-skills-connectivity/>). [MTN South Africa says its Digital Skills for Digital Jobs programme, run with the MTN SA Foundation, MTN Skills Academy, government and other partners, graduated 80 Western Cape youth and has trained more than 1,600 young South Africans over two years](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/30/mtn-south-africa-graduates-80-youth-in-digital-skills-programme/>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/microsoft-invests-zar-5-4bn-in-south-africa/>).
