# South Africa

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Collective X, which coordinates South Africa's National Digital Skills Plan, said its board chair reports 600 member organisations, R600 million mobilised in its Digital Skills Impact Fund and 4,000 beneficiaries across all nine provinces](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/16/south-africa-152m-digital-skills-jobs/>). [Microsoft says more than 150,000 people in South Africa were trained in digital and AI skills in 2024 through its Skills for Jobs programme](<https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/microsoft-invests-zar-5-4bn-in-south-africa/>). [More than 100 women in Tzaneen, Limpopo received digital skills training from the Film and Publications Board and NEMISA under the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies' Gender-based Violence and Femicide Awareness Programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/south-africa-subsea-cable-ai-digital-skills-connectivity/>).
