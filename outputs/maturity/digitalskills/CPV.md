# Cape Verde

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Resolução 72/2026 recognises as progress digital-inclusion and skills-training initiatives developed for citizens and public servants](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>). [The Cabo Verde government launched the Skodji Digital programme to train up to 3,000 young people in digital skills, with 1,050 participants in the first phase and applications open to 25 February](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cabo-verde-bets-on-digital-skills-to-tackle-youth-unemployment>). [The Secretary of State for the Digital Economy said a national strategy of skills and digital readiness, part of the World Bank-funded Digital Cabo Verde project, is being prepared after a workshop in Praia](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/>). [The regulator ARME says it runs digital literacy actions, notably in schools, and set a mentoring session for 60 girls from 10 Praia lyceums in April 2026](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/cabo-verde-tem-sido-um-exemplo-naquilo-que-e-a-governacao-da-internet-a-nivel-regional-destaca-pca-da-arme/>).
