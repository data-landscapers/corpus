# Cape Verde

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In January 2026 the government launched Skodji Digital to train up to 3,000 young people](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cabo-verde-bets-on-digital-skills-to-tackle-youth-unemployment>). [Its first phase took 1,050 participants](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cabo-verde-bets-on-digital-skills-to-tackle-youth-unemployment>). [In April 2026 a 15-station TechLab opened in Brava as a national pilot of the programme](<https://www.digital.cv/blog/brava-como-piloto-nacional-de-inclusao-digital-com-implementacao-do-techlab-no-ambito-do-skodji-digital>). [A national digital-skills strategy is being prepared under the World Bank-funded Digital Cabo Verde project](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/>). [The regulator ARME runs digital literacy actions, notably in schools](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/cabo-verde-tem-sido-um-exemplo-naquilo-que-e-a-governacao-da-internet-a-nivel-regional-destaca-pca-da-arme/>). [A 2026 resolution records skills training for citizens and public servants as progress](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101313>).
