# Uganda

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Uganda Communications Commission's universal service fund, UCUSAF, says its programmes trained more than 13,000 women and over 11,000 rural community members in digital skills between 2020 and 2026, and more than 5,000 small businesses](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/10/ugandas-digital-skills-gap-threatens-jobs-despite-expanding-connectivity-ucc.html>). [The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance, with MTN Foundation Uganda and Centenary Technology Services, is implementing a Shs4 billion, three-year programme of four regional digital innovation hubs at public universities plus the Nakawa hub, expected to give over 20,000 students and community members digital skills training; the second hub was handed over at Busitema University](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/371109/digital-hubs-must-equip-youth-for-jobssays-ps-zawedde>). [The National Council for Persons with Disabilities and IDI Uganda, with KOICA funding, set up an ICT hub in Kasese that will provide computer literacy and vocational digital training to persons with disabilities and the wider community](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/360591/new-ict-hub-in-kasese-to-empower-persons-with-disabilities-with-digital-skills>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/371109/digital-hubs-must-equip-youth-for-jobssays-ps-zawedde>).

**Half or more.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/371109/digital-hubs-must-equip-youth-for-jobssays-ps-zawedde>).
