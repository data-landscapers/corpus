# Uganda

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Digi-Truck mobile classroom, run by the gender ministry with Huawei since March 2023, had trained 8,192 people by September 2025](<https://observer.ug/technology/in-amuria-digital-lessons-on-wheels-offer-new-hope-for-ugandans/>). [The ICT ministry says over 10,000 Ugandans have learned digital skills through government programmes](<https://ict.go.ug/media/articles/digital-skilling-powering-ugandas-future>). [In November 2026 the ICT minister told the ITU conference that over 100,000 young people had been trained in three years](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/uganda/>). [The ministry also counts over 160,000 citizens and small businesses trained, and 58 sites in over 25 districts visited by the mobile classroom](<https://pmldaily.com/news/world/2026/11/what-dr-aminah-zawedde-leaves-behind-at-the-ict-ministry.html>). [The regulator's universal service fund says it trained over 13,000 women and 11,000 rural residents between 2020 and 2026](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/10/ugandas-digital-skills-gap-threatens-jobs-despite-expanding-connectivity-ucc.html>). [The ICT ministry, with MTN Foundation, is opening four regional digital hubs at public universities](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/371109/digital-hubs-must-equip-youth-for-jobssays-ps-zawedde>). [They are to train over 20,000 students and community members](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/371109/digital-hubs-must-equip-youth-for-jobssays-ps-zawedde>). [A new hub in Kasese is to teach computer literacy to people with disabilities](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/360591/new-ict-hub-in-kasese-to-empower-persons-with-disabilities-with-digital-skills>).
