# Togo

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In April 2025, 244 young Togolese received certificates after five months of D-CLIC training in Lome, Aneho, Kara and Dapaong, per Togo First](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/0110-20219-francophonie-organization-s-investment-in-aneho-digital-training-program-reaches-nearly-cfaf-300m>). [A second cohort of 50 young people began D-CLIC digital-skills training at Aneho in August 2026, backed by nearly CFAF 300 million from OIF since 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/0110-20219-francophonie-organization-s-investment-in-aneho-digital-training-program-reaches-nearly-cfaf-300m>). [Togo's digital transformation minister launched the Campus 42 coding and AI peer-learning programme, financed by Axian Group through Yas Togo with French support, to train hundreds of young Togolese a year](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2804-18842-university-of-lome-joins-campus-42-digital-training-network>).
