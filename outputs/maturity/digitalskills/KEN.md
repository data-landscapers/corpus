# Kenya

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In September 2026 the State Department of ICT ran digital-skills training from the Huawei DigiTruck](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/kenya-trains-330-young-people-in-digital-skills-through-huawei-digitruck/>). [In Keiyo South, 330 young people completed the course](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/kenya-trains-330-young-people-in-digital-skills-through-huawei-digitruck/>). [In 2026 Garissa county and its partners began a 25-week course for about 1,700 young people](<https://cioafrica.co/garissa-refugee-youth-get-digital-skills-training/>). Over 700 had enrolled. No national count of people trained has been found.
