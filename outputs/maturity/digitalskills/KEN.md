# Kenya

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In September 2026 the State Department of ICT ran digital-skills training from the Huawei DigiTruck](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/kenya-trains-330-young-people-in-digital-skills-through-huawei-digitruck/>). [In Keiyo South, 330 young people completed the course](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/11/kenya-trains-330-young-people-in-digital-skills-through-huawei-digitruck/>). [In 2026 Garissa county and its partners began a 25-week course for about 1,700 young people](<https://cioafrica.co/garissa-refugee-youth-get-digital-skills-training/>). Over 700 had enrolled. In August 2026 [Nyandarua county and KICTANet trained about 100 trainers](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/sheconnects-nyandarua-county-digital-skills-training/>), each to train 50 more. [In mid-2024 the ICT Cabinet Secretary said the free government digital-skills programme had trained 490,000 young people in a year](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/governments-digital-literacy-program-creates-149-000-online-jobs/>). [The Jitume and Ajira programmes had 182,568 beneficiaries in 2025](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/youth-urged-to-utilize-jitume-program-to-acquire-online-jobs/>). [In May 2025 the government launched a National Digital Literacy Skills Curriculum](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kenya-launches-digital-literacy-curriculum-at-connected-africa-summit/>), aiming at 20 million citizens by 2032. [In October 2026 the UK said joint UK-Kenya programmes had trained over 1.2 million citizens](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-10-31-state-plans-digital-accessibility-audits-to-improve-services-for-pwds>).
