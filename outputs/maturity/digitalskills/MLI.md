# Mali

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The communications and digital economy ministry, through DNEN, and Kabakoo Academies announced the launch of a first cohort of 1,000 young people in a programme to train 15,000 youth aged 18-35 in digital skills under a three-year convention signed 31 January 2026](<https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/kabakoo-academies-et-la-dnen-lancent-la-premiere-cohorte-de-leur-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes-au-mali-3119774.html>). [The Minister of Communication, Digital Economy and Modernisation of Administration launched phase III of the African Girls Can Code Initiative (AGCCI) with UN Women Mali and Belgian funding, to train 100 young women aged 17 to 25 in coding, with 51 already in training](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/305718-le-mali-lance-la-phase-iii-du-projet--les-filles-africaines-peuv.html>).
