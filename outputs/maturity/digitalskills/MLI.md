# Mali

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In January 2026 the digital economy ministry, through DNEN, signed a three-year agreement with Kabakoo Academies](<https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/kabakoo-academies-et-la-dnen-lancent-la-premiere-cohorte-de-leur-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes-au-mali-3119774.html>). [It is to train 15,000 people aged 18 to 35, starting with a cohort of 1,000](<https://www.maliweb.net/technologie/kabakoo-academies-et-la-dnen-lancent-la-premiere-cohorte-de-leur-partenariat-pour-renforcer-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes-au-mali-3119774.html>). [The minister also launched phase III of the African Girls Can Code Initiative](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/305718-le-mali-lance-la-phase-iii-du-projet--les-filles-africaines-peuv.html>), with UN Women. [It is to train 100 young women in coding, and 51 had begun](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/305718-le-mali-lance-la-phase-iii-du-projet--les-filles-africaines-peuv.html>). [An ITC project with the technology agency AGETIC trained two cohorts of 60 young people in Bamako in 2023 and 2024](<https://www.intracen.org/news-and-events/news/malian-youth-take-up-the-challenge-of-digitalization>).
