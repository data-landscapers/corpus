# Tanzania

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The government, through the regulator TCRA, has set up 800 digital clubs across the country](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tcra-ups-support-for-mathematics-digital-clubs/>), from nursery school to university. [The 2025 WSIS country report counts 711 clubs with 766 teachers and 1,878 students](<https://www.itu.int/net/wsis/review/inc/docs/reports/country/Tanzania__20YearCountryReport_WSISOutcomes.pdf>). [Zanzibar's ministry trained 1,015 citizens in digital skills between July 2025 and March 2026](<https://www.eastafricanherald.com/business/2026/6/2/tanzania-isles-sets-ambitious-digital-agenda-with-5339bn-ict-budget>). [It will continue ICT training through community centres](<https://www.eastafricanherald.com/business/2026/6/2/tanzania-isles-sets-ambitious-digital-agenda-with-5339bn-ict-budget>). A government programme running to August 2027 [aims to improve the digital literacy of 500,000 farmers](<https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/6/tanzanias-smallholder-farmers-struggle-to-benefit-from-digital-agriculture>). [In September 2026 the ICT minister cited the same 800 clubs](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-tells-wtpf-session-how-its-citizens-enjoy-access-to-inclusive-secure-communication-services/>).

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzania-tells-wtpf-session-how-its-citizens-enjoy-access-to-inclusive-secure-communication-services/>).
