# Somalia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** In September 2025 the Ministry of Communications and Technology and UNDP [held a one-day digital-skills workshop in Mogadishu for 73 young people](<https://undpinsomalia.medium.com/empowering-somali-youth-through-digital-skills-e1626efde8ee>). It was part of a project that aims to train 1,000 young people under the Enhancing SDG Localization and Digital Transformation project. [In April 2026 UNDP said it had trained 80 young Somalis with the ministry](<https://undpinsomalia.medium.com/promoting-innovation-and-digital-transformation-in-somalia-db2d48bc9643>), in cybersecurity, data analysis, digital marketing and online business. Neither count names a second location.

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. [The project's target of 1,000 young people](<https://undpinsomalia.medium.com/empowering-somali-youth-through-digital-skills-e1626efde8ee>) is a count of people to train, not a share of the population.

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a share of half or more of adults with digital skills.
