# Congo

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** The EU part of the government's digital transformation project, [launched on 17 June 2025](<https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-250-pour-un-avenir-numerique-et-inclusif-au-service-de-la-gouvernance-de-l-economie-et-de-la-jeunesse-l-union-europeenne-renforce-son-engagement-en-faveur-de-l-acceleration-de-la-transformation-numerique-au-congo>), aims to develop the digital skills of the population, especially young people and women. [UNESCO's project 3210652071 (USD 166,800, 66% progress, 2024-2025) on digital skills and media and information literacy for populations is on going, with an allocation to this country (Congo 10%)](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [The Congo Digital Transformation Project, launched by the government in January 2023, provides for 3,000 people to acquire certified digital skills from basic to advanced (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux>).
