# Congo

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** The EU part of the government's digital transformation project [was launched on 17 June 2025](<https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-250-pour-un-avenir-numerique-et-inclusif-au-service-de-la-gouvernance-de-l-economie-et-de-la-jeunesse-l-union-europeenne-renforce-son-engagement-en-faveur-de-l-acceleration-de-la-transformation-numerique-au-congo>). [It aims to develop the digital skills of the population, especially young people and women](<https://www.eib.org/en/press/all/2025-250-pour-un-avenir-numerique-et-inclusif-au-service-de-la-gouvernance-de-l-economie-et-de-la-jeunesse-l-union-europeenne-renforce-son-engagement-en-faveur-de-l-acceleration-de-la-transformation-numerique-au-congo>). [UNESCO's project 3210652071, worth USD 166,800 for 2024-2025, covers digital skills and media and information literacy](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [It allocates 10% to Congo](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [In January 2023 the government launched the Congo Digital Transformation Project](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux>). [It provides for 3,000 people to acquire certified digital skills from basic to advanced](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux>).

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest fact: [in 2024-2025 UNESCO's project on digital skills for populations allocated 10% to Congo](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest fact: [at the end of 2025 that UNESCO project stood at 66% progress](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).
