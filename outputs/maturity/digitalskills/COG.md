# Congo

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [UNESCO's project 3210652071 (USD 166,800, 66% progress, 2024-2025) on digital skills and media and information literacy for populations is on going, with an allocation to this country (Congo 10%)](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>). [The Congo Digital Transformation Project, launched by the government in January 2023, provides for 3,000 people to acquire certified digital skills from basic to advanced (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).

**Half or more.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://core.unesco.org/fr/project/3210652071>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Projet de transformation numerique du Congo](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/congo-l-aden-s-associe-a-la-societe-anglaise-regalli-pour-deployer-un-portail-numerique-des-services-gouvernementaux>).
