# Djibouti

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The government launched a feasibility study (statement of 13 May 2026) for Digital Houses in five inland regions under the E-SKILLS programme, which aims to train at least 3,000 young people and women by 2029 for about EUR 7 million](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/djibouti-seeks-to-narrow-digital-divide-with-digital-houses-initiative>). [The Ministry of Digital Economy and Innovation launched work on École 42 Djibouti, a project-based coding school for young people in software development, cybersecurity and data, with EU E-Skills programme and Expertise France support, per Tech Review Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/public/news/4771/djibouti-launches-ecole-42-project-to-promote-technological-innovation>). [Djibouti's government launched the EU-funded E-SKILLS programme to train at least 3,000 youths and women in digital skills by 2029](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2511-50808-djibouti-launches-8-million-e-skills-project-to-train-3-000-youths-and-women>).

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/djibouti-seeks-to-narrow-digital-divide-with-digital-houses-initiative>).
