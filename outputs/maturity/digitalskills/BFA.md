# Burkina Faso

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** Burkina Faso's digital skills work runs through PACTDIGITAL, a World Bank-financed project. [Its appraisal document of 22 December 2023](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>) gives US$22 million to a digital skills component. [It aims to widen access to digital skills programmes](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>). Agence Ecofin says the digital ministry's 2025 results included [training 169 young girls in digital skills](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3112-134607-le-burkina-faso-prevoit-109-millions-de-dollars-au-developpement-du-numerique-en-2026>). On 9 November 2026 the ministry's secretary general [announced a free ICT training campaign of about five weeks](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150638>). [It covers office software, AI, cybersecurity, networks and web and mobile development](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150638>).
