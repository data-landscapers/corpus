# Burkina Faso

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** Burkina Faso's digital skills work runs through PACTDIGITAL, a World Bank-financed project. [Its appraisal document of 22 December 2023](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>) gives US$22 million to a component on developing digital skills, and sets the project the aim of widening access to digital skills programmes. Agence Ecofin reports that the digital ministry's results for 2025, set out with its plans for 2026, included [strengthening the digital skills of 169 young girls](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3112-134607-le-burkina-faso-prevoit-109-millions-de-dollars-au-developpement-du-numerique-en-2026>).

**Share measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>).
