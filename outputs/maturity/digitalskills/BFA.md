# Burkina Faso

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The World Bank appraisal document (2023-12-22) for PACTDIGITAL includes a US$22.00m component on developing digital skills and a project aim to increase access to digital skills programs](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Share measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>).

**Half or more.** No source held since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P177022>).
