# Ethiopia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Ministry of Education's CEO says the nationwide International Certification of Digital Literacy initiative has equipped over 12,000 academic staff and nearly 200,000 students with digital skills and reached close to 3,000 community members](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/30/empowering-ethiopians-by-laying-the-digital-foundations-for-afe-economic-growth>). [The Government Communication Service statement points to free coding training for millions of young people as one of the government's investments preparing the new generation](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopian-government-declares-technological-knowledge-cybersecurity-and-data-sovereignty-essential-to-the-countrys-future/>). [The Digital Ethiopia Skills and TVET in Action (DESTA) project, EUR 21m, launched with the Ministry of Labor and Skills, Germany, the EU and Finland, aims to equip youth with digital and entrepreneurial skills in five regions, per GIZ](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/ethiopia/news/advancing-digitalised-tvet-ethiopia-launch-desta-initiative>). The government's [5 Million Ethiopian Coders initiative](<https://addisfortune.news/civil-servants-rush-to-secure-digital-certificates-skills-take-a-back-seat>), announced with the UAE in July 2024, [offers free digital training to five million citizens](<https://www.pps.gov.et/pps-leadership-and-staff-successfully-complete-5-million-coders-training-1>).
