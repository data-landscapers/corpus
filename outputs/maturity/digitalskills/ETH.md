# Ethiopia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The innovation ministry said in July 2025 that its 5-Million Coders programme had certified 900,000 young people](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6969056>). [It had 1.6 million enrolled](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_6969056>). [In Oromia alone 956,000 residents had completed it by December 2025](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7899684>). [The government announced the programme with the UAE in July 2024](<https://addisfortune.news/civil-servants-rush-to-secure-digital-certificates-skills-take-a-back-seat>). [The education ministry's digital literacy certification has reached nearly 200,000 students and close to 3,000 community members](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/30/empowering-ethiopians-by-laying-the-digital-foundations-for-afe-economic-growth>). [The EUR 21 million DESTA project, with the labour ministry, is to train young people in five regions](<https://www.giz.de/en/regions/africa/ethiopia/news/advancing-digitalised-tvet-ethiopia-launch-desta-initiative>).
