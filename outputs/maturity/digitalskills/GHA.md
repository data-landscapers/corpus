## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [More than 12,000 course completions were recorded in the early weeks of Phase Two of the One Million Coders Programme, per Sukparu](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ghana-opens-2026-national-ict-week-ai-digital-skills/>). [Minister Samuel Nartey George said the One Million Coders Programme had recorded 5,812 course completions and 11,683 cumulative attendances across 118 reporting centres between 1 June and 15 August 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/one-million-coders-programme-records-141954-registrations-27782-active-learners/>). [Tech Review Africa reports the government is restructuring the One Million Coders Programme, which originally targeted first-year students, to focus on third- and final-year students preparing for the job market](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7709/x>).
