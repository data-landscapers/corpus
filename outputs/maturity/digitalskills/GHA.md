# Ghana

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [More than 12,000 course completions were recorded early in Phase Two of the One Million Coders Programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ghana-opens-2026-national-ict-week-ai-digital-skills/>). [Between June and August 2026 the minister reported 5,812 completions across 118 centres](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/one-million-coders-programme-records-141954-registrations-27782-active-learners/>). [The government aims to train 400,000 people in 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/01/one-million-coders-programme-government-targets-400000-beneficiaries-this-year/>), after a 2025 pilot trained 859. [It is refocusing the programme on third- and final-year students](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7709/x>). [In July 2024 the ministry said its Digital Transformation Centre project had trained over 22,000 people since 2020](<https://moc.gov.gh/2024/07/02/communications-and-digitalisation-ministry-trains-22000-people-in-ict/>).
