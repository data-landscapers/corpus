# Madagascar

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In January 2026 the technical education ministry and the OIF began a D-CLIC phase to train 1,000 young people](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/madagascar-to-train-1-000-young-people-in-digital-skills-by-june-under-d-clic-program>). [The ministry targets 40,000 trained by 2028](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2309-48937-madagascar-targets-40-000-digital-skills-trainees-by-2028>). [The free Skills4Job programme began in Toamasina in October 2025 with 51 people](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/skills4job-madagascar-mise-sur-le-digital-pour-renforcer-l-employabilite-des-jeunes>). [The digital ministry is building a Digital Center with skills training in each of six provinces](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-stephanie-delmotte-minister-of-of-digital-development-posts-and-telecommunications-of-madagascar-mndpt>). [In September 2026 the ministry agreed with GIP Pix to certify young people's digital skills](<https://www.moov.mg/article/121011-metfp-et-gip-pix-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-et-certifier-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes>). [Asan'ai, launched in January 2026, is to train 1,300 young people in AI and data trades by May 2027](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/11/telecommunications-les-reformes-au.html>). [A World Bank project funds training for 5,700 people](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf>).
