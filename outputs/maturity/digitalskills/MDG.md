# Madagascar

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Madagascar's Ministry of Technical Education and Vocational Training signed a partnership with GIP Pix on 11 September 2026 to prepare young people for, and certify, digital skills, across eight axes including exam centres and trainer training](<https://www.moov.mg/article/121011-metfp-et-gip-pix-un-partenariat-pour-renforcer-et-certifier-les-competences-numeriques-des-jeunes>). [Madagascar's Ministry of Technical Education and Vocational Training and the OIF launched on 19 January 2026 a new phase of D-CLIC to train 1,000 young people free in digital skills by mid-2026, within a goal of 40,000 by 2028](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/madagascar-to-train-1-000-young-people-in-digital-skills-by-june-under-d-clic-program>). [The World Bank records DECIM digital-skills sub-component 2.2 (USD 15m) including training of 5,700 people in advanced digital skills and basic digital literacy](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf>).
