# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In November 2025 the regulator AGER found no specific digital-literacy action in the digital policy](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>). It listed a national digital-literacy programme as a recommendation. [In June 2025 SEGIB reported a project for Principe island](<https://segib.org/pt/digitalizacao-que-cruza-oceanos-a-cooperacao-triangular-que-impulsiona-a-digitalizacao-em-uma-pequena-ilha-africana/>). Portugal, Argentina and Principe's regional government back it. Digital classrooms in schools and adult centres are two of its four plans.

**Share measured.** No survey since October 2023 has been found that gives the share of people with basic digital skills. The nearest fact found is [AGER's review of November 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).

**Half or more.** No survey since October 2023 has been found that gives the share of adults with basic digital skills, so none shows half or more. The nearest fact found is [AGER's review of November 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).
