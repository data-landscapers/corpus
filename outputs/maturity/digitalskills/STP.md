# Sao Tome and Principe

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [SEGIB reports a triangular cooperation project (Portugal, Argentina, the Autonomous Government of Principe) has started for Principe island, with digital classrooms in schools and adult training centres as two of four identified projects; Principe only, not stated as delivered](<https://segib.org/pt/digitalizacao-que-cruza-oceanos-a-cooperacao-triangular-que-impulsiona-a-digitalizacao-em-uma-pequena-ilha-africana/>). [AGER observes an absence of specific actions on digital literacy in São Tomé and Príncipe's digital policy, and lists a national digital literacy programme only as a 1-5 year recommendation](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).
