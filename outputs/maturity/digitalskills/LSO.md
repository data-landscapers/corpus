# Lesotho

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The LCA strategic plan says the Authority, through its Universal Service Fund projects, contributes to improving digital literacy in unserved and underserved areas](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/STRATEGIC-PLAN-2026-2029.pdf>). [Information Minister Moorosi said digital ambassadors are deployed across local councils to help rural communities acquire internet access and computer literacy skills](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/un-resident-coordinator-pays-coutersy-call-on-information-minister/>). [UNDP says Lesotho's 40 Digital Ambassadors will take Intel's 'AI for Citizens' training into their local areas, building on 40 Digital Skills Learning Labs](<https://www.undp.org/digital-innovation/stories/global-partnerships-local-impact-undp-and-intel-join-hands-strengthen-ai-capacity-lesotho-and-liberia>). [Lesotho's Community Digital Inclusion Model, launched with UNDP and now run through the Universal Service Fund, deploys village digital ambassadors to train citizens in essential digital skills, per the Minister](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/22/84_WTDC-25-HLS-Statement-Moorosi-Lesotho-1.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Share measured.** [The LCA and Bureau of Statistics household survey of 2023 found that 35.1% of individuals in Lesotho had ICT skills, against 58.4% who use the internet](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>).
