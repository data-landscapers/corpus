# Lesotho

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In August 2025 the government and UNDP launched a digital inclusion initiative with 40 community digital ambassadors](<https://www.gov.ls/economy/lesotho-moves-forward-with-digital-inclusion/>). [Community learning centres in Mafeteng had trained 164 people at three schools by September 2025](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/09/16/tech-initiative-graduates-first-cohort/>). [The model now runs through the Universal Service Fund](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/22/84_WTDC-25-HLS-Statement-Moorosi-Lesotho-1.pdf>), the minister said. [In April 2026 he said digital ambassadors work across local councils](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/un-resident-coordinator-pays-coutersy-call-on-information-minister/>). [UNDP says they will also teach AI skills from 40 learning labs](<https://www.undp.org/digital-innovation/stories/global-partnerships-local-impact-undp-and-intel-join-hands-strengthen-ai-capacity-lesotho-and-liberia>).

**Share measured.** [A 2023 household survey by the regulator and the statistics bureau found 35.1 per cent of individuals had ICT skills](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>). [In the same survey 58.4 per cent used the internet](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills.
