# Nigeria

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In October 2026 NITDA's National Digital Literacy Dashboard recorded about 2.76 million digital-literacy training achievements from 248 reporting organisations since January 2022, across all 36 states and the FCT](<https://von.gov.ng/agency-unveils-dashboard-to-track-national-digital-literacy-progress/>); [Tech Africa News puts it at 2,769,512 across all 774 local government areas](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/21/nitda-digital-literacy-dashboard-2-7-million-training-achievements/>). [The communications ministry says the 3MTT programme received a N1 billion Airtel Africa Foundation grant in 2025](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/2025-year-in-review-of-activities-of-the-honourable-minister-dr-bosun-tijani/>). [The Federal Government launched the Digital Training Academy on 14 September 2026, committing over N10.5 billion to train more than 32,000 young Nigerians, per Education Minister Tunji Alausa](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/14/fg-invests-n10-5bn-in-digital-training-academy-to-equip-over-32000-nigerians/>). [Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Education admitted 16,000 people to the first cohort of the free Digital Training Academy, with national launch set for 14 September 2026 and training via Coursera and Pluralsight](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/09/fg-enrols-16000-nigerians-in-maiden-digital-training-academy-cohort/>). [UNDP and Cisco signed an MoU giving up to 50,000 Nigeria Jubilee Fellows Programme fellows and alumni free Cisco Networking Academy digital-skills learning on a rolling basis](<https://www.undp.org/nigeria/press-releases/cisco-and-undp-partner-advance-youth-digital-skills-and-employment-through-eu-funded-njfp-programme>).
