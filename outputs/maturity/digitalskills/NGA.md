# Nigeria

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Federal Government launched the Digital Training Academy on 14 September 2026, committing over N10.5 billion to train more than 32,000 young Nigerians, per Education Minister Tunji Alausa](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/14/fg-invests-n10-5bn-in-digital-training-academy-to-equip-over-32000-nigerians/>). [Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Education admitted 16,000 people to the first cohort of the free Digital Training Academy, with national launch set for 14 September 2026 and training via Coursera and Pluralsight](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/09/fg-enrols-16000-nigerians-in-maiden-digital-training-academy-cohort/>). [UNDP and Cisco signed an MoU giving up to 50,000 Nigeria Jubilee Fellows Programme fellows and alumni free Cisco Networking Academy digital-skills learning on a rolling basis](<https://www.undp.org/nigeria/press-releases/cisco-and-undp-partner-advance-youth-digital-skills-and-employment-through-eu-funded-njfp-programme>).
