# Nigeria

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [In October 2026 NITDA's National Digital Literacy Dashboard recorded about 2.76 million training achievements since January 2022](<https://von.gov.ng/agency-unveils-dashboard-to-track-national-digital-literacy-progress/>). [They came from 248 reporting organisations in all 36 states and the FCT](<https://von.gov.ng/agency-unveils-dashboard-to-track-national-digital-literacy-progress/>). [President Tinubu said in December 2025 that the 3MTT programme had trained 30,000 young people](<https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-we-are-laying-the-foundation-for-a-digital-workforce-to-power-nigerias-economic-transformation/>). [It had reached all 36 states and the FCT](<https://statehouse.gov.ng/president-tinubu-we-are-laying-the-foundation-for-a-digital-workforce-to-power-nigerias-economic-transformation/>). [The Federal Government launched a Digital Training Academy on 14 September 2026](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/14/fg-invests-n10-5bn-in-digital-training-academy-to-equip-over-32000-nigerians/>), to train over 32,000 young people. [Its first cohort admitted 16,000](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/09/fg-enrols-16000-nigerians-in-maiden-digital-training-academy-cohort/>). [UNDP and Cisco offer free online courses to up to 50,000 Jubilee Fellows](<https://www.undp.org/nigeria/press-releases/cisco-and-undp-partner-advance-youth-digital-skills-and-employment-through-eu-funded-njfp-programme>).
