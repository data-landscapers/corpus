# Sierra Leone

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The ITU's Digital Transformation Centre in Sierra Leone trained 600 citizens in a 20-day basic skills course](<https://academy.itu.int/about/news/bdd-sl-scales-and-extends-basic-digital-skills-training-600-sierra-leonean-citizens-underserved>). [The communications ministry lists World Bank-supported training for 1,500 people across 16 Digital Learning Hubs in 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-innovation/>). [In September 2025 the science and innovation directorate graduated the first cohort of its Tech 101 course](<https://sierraloaded.sl/technology/youths-graduate-dsti-tech-101-training/>). [DIAL says the national effort included digital literacy campaigns for the public](<https://dial.global/work/digital-transformation-sierra-leone/>).

**Share measured.** [UNCDF's national baseline survey scores adult digital literacy at 26 out of 100](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/8994/national-digital-financial-literacy-baseline-survey-sierra-leone>). [It found 25 per cent of adults could save or delete phone contacts](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/8994/national-digital-financial-literacy-baseline-survey-sierra-leone>).
