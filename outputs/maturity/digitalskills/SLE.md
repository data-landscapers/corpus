# Sierra Leone

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [DIAL says the Sierra Leone effort involved widespread digital literacy campaigns for the public](<https://dial.global/work/digital-transformation-sierra-leone/>). [Bridge the Digital Divide Sierra Leone, the ITU Digital Transformation Centre, trained 600 citizens in a 20-day basic digital skills course, funded by ITU and Norway, per ITU Academy](<https://academy.itu.int/about/news/bdd-sl-scales-and-extends-basic-digital-skills-training-600-sierra-leonean-citizens-underserved>). [The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation lists as an active 2025 programme World Bank-supported digital skills training for 1,500 Sierra Leoneans across 16 Digital Learning Hubs](<https://mocti.gov.sl/directorate-of-innovation/>).

**Share measured.** [UNCDF's national baseline survey gives adult digital literacy at 26 of 100 points, with 25% of adults able to save or delete phone contacts and 21.5% able to send text messages](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/8994/national-digital-financial-literacy-baseline-survey-sierra-leone>).

**Half or more.** No source found since October 2023 gives a measured share of half or more of adults with basic digital skills. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/8994/national-digital-financial-literacy-baseline-survey-sierra-leone>).
