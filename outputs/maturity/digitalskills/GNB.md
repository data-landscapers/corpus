# Guinea-Bissau

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Council of Ministers approved a draft decree on a National Digital Skills Policy](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/scan_20260519162259.pdf>), to equip citizens with digital skills. [Fifty women and girls completed a seven-day trainer course on digital skills](<https://ang.gw/impact-hub-bissau-reforca-competencias-digitais-de-50-mulheres-e-raparigas-na-guine-bissau/>). [UNDP and the government ran it with EU funding](<https://ang.gw/impact-hub-bissau-reforca-competencias-digitais-de-50-mulheres-e-raparigas-na-guine-bissau/>). [In April 2026 the government and Telecel agreed to launch the STARTOCODE platform](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=56945>). [The government is to train 1,000 young people a year on it](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=56945>).
