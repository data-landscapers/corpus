# Guinea-Bissau

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The Council of Ministers approved a draft decree on a National Digital Skills Policy whose purpose is to train Guinean citizens in digital skills, per the cabinet communique reported by ANG](<https://ang.gw/conselho-de-ministros-aprova-diplomas-sobre-concorrencia-estrategia-para-desenvolvimento-do-caju-e-politica-de-competencias-digitais/>). [Fifty women and girls completed a seven-day trainer course on digital skills, digital security and financial education under the Justiça pa Tudu Djintis and Empoderamento da Mulher projects, run by UNDP and the Government of Guinea-Bissau with EU and UNDP funding](<https://ang.gw/impact-hub-bissau-reforca-competencias-digitais-de-50-mulheres-e-raparigas-na-guine-bissau/>). [The Council of Ministers approved, with amendments, a draft decree on the National Digital Skills Policy, aimed at equipping national citizens with digital skills](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/scan_20260519162259.pdf>).
