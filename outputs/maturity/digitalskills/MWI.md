# Malawi

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The government's World Bank-funded Digital Malawi Project had taught digital skills to over 19,000 people by August 2024](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [The training ran at 10 tech hubs](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>). [Its Luntha ndi Chuma initiative works through nine tech hubs](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digmap-luntha-ndi-chuma-initiative-for-human-capital-development/>). [One of them had trained 3,371 young people and women in five cities by January 2024](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digmap-luntha-ndi-chuma-initiative-for-human-capital-development/>). [In February 2026 UNDP launched a programme for 30 students to train 3,000 people](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/lilongwe-lupaso-how-pemphero-kanyenda-taking-digital-skills-back-home>), with the ICT minister present.

**Share measured.** [A study by the National Statistical Office and the regulator MACRA found that 1.4 per cent of Malawians had digital skills](<https://mbc.mw/1-4-malawians-possess-digital-expertise-nso-macra/>). [It was reported in May 2024](<https://mbc.mw/1-4-malawians-possess-digital-expertise-nso-macra/>).

**Half or more.** [The 2024 National Statistical Office study put the share with digital skills at 1.4 per cent](<https://mbc.mw/1-4-malawians-possess-digital-expertise-nso-macra/>), far below half.

***Also on file***. [ICBVI](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/beyond-access-advancing-digital-agency-people-visual-impairments-malawi>).

***Not found*** No survey has been found that shows half or more of adults with basic digital skills.
