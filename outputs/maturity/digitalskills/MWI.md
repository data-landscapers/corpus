# Malawi

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** The government's World Bank-funded Digital Malawi Project had, by August 2024, taught [digital skills to over 19,000 youths, women and elderly people](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/digital-malawi-project-a-game-changer-for-malawis-digital-transformation/>) at 10 tech hubs, its Secretary for Information and Digitalization said. [UNDP and ICTAM, with the ICT Minister present, launched the Digital Literacy Advocates programme: 30 certified university students are each to train 100 community members, 3,000 Malawians in all](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/stories/lilongwe-lupaso-how-pemphero-kanyenda-taking-digital-skills-back-home>). [The University of Malawi and UNDP launched Nzeru Hub, a free digital learning platform for students and underserved communities offering digital skills courses, per UNIMA and UNDP](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/unima-and-undp-launch-nzeru-hub-driving-digital-learning-in-malawi-14-10-2025>).
