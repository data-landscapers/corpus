# Liberia

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [Orange Digital Center Liberia says it trained 3,035 people in 44 sessions in 2025](<https://thenewsnewspaperonline.com/orange-digital-center-releases-2025-report-exceeds-targeted-impact-goals-across-liberia/>). [It works in all 15 counties](<https://thenewsnewspaperonline.com/orange-digital-center-releases-2025-report-exceeds-targeted-impact-goals-across-liberia/>). [In September 2026 the regulator LTA and the youth ministry agreed to set up 15 Youth Digital Skills Hubs](<https://lta.gov.lr/lta-ministry-of-youth-sports-launch-nationwide-digital-skills-initiative-for-liberian-youth/>). [The same month the gender ministry signed with Cybastion and Cisco to train thousands of women in five counties](<https://elbcnews.com/2026/09/23/gender-minister-signs-partnership-with-cybastion-institute-of-technology-and-cisco-system/>). [Cyber Liberia 2030 is to train 20,000 people in digital literacy and cybersecurity from October 2026](<https://development.mfdp.gov.lr/activities/2513>).
