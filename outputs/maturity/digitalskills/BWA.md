# Botswana

## Digital skills of the public

**Training for the public.** [The EU, Expertise France and HAUS Finland project, ongoing 2024-2027 with Botswana's Digital and Innovation Hub, supports and expands digital innovation programmes to enhance digital skills, in particular for women and youth entrepreneurs](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/en/projects/digital-transformation-botswana>). [BOCRA's incident response team is running ICT training and cybersecurity awareness sessions for students and teachers at four named schools, following its Digital Centres handover](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/3680/bocra-expands-ict-training-to-support-digital-inclusion-in-botswana-schools>). [SmartBots (Government of Botswana) says the ongoing EU-funded programme, started August 2024, includes targeted programmes that enhance digital skills for women and youth entrepreneurs](<https://smartbots.org.bw/projects/eu-digital-transformation>). A later answer places the country above that step.
