# Cameroon

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In June 2026 the urban development ministry [sought a firm for a land information system for Yaoundé and Douala](<https://www.armp.cm/details?id_publication=6404&type_publication=AMI>), with the digitising of cadastral and land archives. [A September 2026 call under the same World Bank project covers supervising that work](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=6610&type_publication=AMI>). The 2026 finance bill [proposes FCFA 874 million for a programme whose aim is a digital national cadastre](<https://rfp.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/PROJET-DE-LOI-FINANCES-2026_FR_26112025.pdf>). The budget directorate says [no commune had a digital cadastral plan in 2024](<https://www.dgb.cm/mindcaf-engager-les-reflexions-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-progressive-de-la-digitalisation-des-procedures-foncieres/>), against a target of 0.25.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the 2024 budget report that no commune had a digital cadastral plan](<https://www.dgb.cm/mindcaf-engager-les-reflexions-pour-la-mise-en-oeuvre-progressive-de-la-digitalisation-des-procedures-foncieres/>).
