# Uganda

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** Uganda's finance ministry says [UgNLIS was operational in 22 zonal offices in March 2026](<https://doingbusiness.finance.go.ug/reforms/expand-ugnlis-to-all-land-offices-by-2026>), with expansion to all land offices in progress. In October 2025 the Lands PS said [more than 1.5 million titles had been digitised](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/ugandans-abroad-welcome-digitalisation-of-lan-NV_221485_102026>). [Applicants enter their data at district level and have their title printed](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/377216/government-moves-to-reduce-cost-of-obtaining-land-titles>), Minister Ntabazi said. In Jinja [old records were still being digitised in August 2026](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/jinja-land-office-backlog-exposes-staffing-infrastructure-gaps-despite-digital-gains-5577014>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [UgNLIS in 22 zonal offices, with the rest to follow](<https://doingbusiness.finance.go.ug/reforms/expand-ugnlis-to-all-land-offices-by-2026>).

**Online services.** [Citizens can check land status and confirm ownership on the UgNLIS public portal](<https://media.gcic.go.ug/landowners-to-receive-freehold-titles-in-government-initiative/>), the lands minister said. [Landowners can verify their land online or by mobile app for 10,000 shillings](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/ugandans-abroad-welcome-digitalisation-of-lan-NV_221485_102026>).
