# Uganda

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Minister Ntabazi said that with the new digitalisation system applicants enter their data in the computer at district level and have their title printed](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/377216/government-moves-to-reduce-cost-of-obtaining-land-titles>). [Lands Minister of State Harriet Ntabazi said the ministry's survey, mapping and registration departments have been equipped with upgraded digital systems, a blockchain-based land records system has been developed, and satellite and drones will be used to capture parcels](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/377216/government-moves-to-reduce-cost-of-obtaining-land-titles>). [The Uganda Land Commission is preparing a nationwide GIS-based digital registry of all government-owned land, with ministries asked to submit records and city and municipal authorities verifying boundaries](<https://ubc.go.ug/2026/03/10/govt-to-roll-out-digital-land-registry-to-protect-public-land-and-boost-revenue/>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country.
