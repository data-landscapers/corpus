# Uganda

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Minister of Lands, Housing and Urban Development, Judith Nabakooba, described the Uganda National Land Information System (UGNLIS) as a public portal where citizens can check land status, confirm ownership and track transactions](<https://media.gcic.go.ug/landowners-to-receive-freehold-titles-in-government-initiative/>). [Minister Ntabazi said that with the new digitalisation system applicants enter their data in the computer at district level and have their title printed](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/377216/government-moves-to-reduce-cost-of-obtaining-land-titles>). [Lands Minister of State Harriet Ntabazi said the ministry's survey, mapping and registration departments have been equipped with upgraded digital systems, a blockchain-based land records system has been developed, and satellite and drones will be used to capture parcels](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/377216/government-moves-to-reduce-cost-of-obtaining-land-titles>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://media.gcic.go.ug/landowners-to-receive-freehold-titles-in-government-initiative/>).
