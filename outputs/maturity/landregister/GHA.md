# Ghana

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ELIS remains the backbone of the Lands Commission's online services, with manual processes coexisting, and progress is anchored largely in Accra](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>). [A digital Client Service Access Unit inaugurated at the Volta Regional Lands Commission office in Ho enabled the deployment of ELIS in the Volta Region](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-client-service-access-unit-inaugurated-at-volta-regional-office-of-lands-commission.html>). [The Minister for Lands said the Lands Commission's ongoing ELIS digitization would eliminate manual inefficiencies, and the article says full migration from manual operations is still in progress](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

**Online services.** [Then-Lands Minister Jinapor acknowledged that ELIS had enabled consolidated searches, stamp duty assessment and plan approvals to be completed online](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).
