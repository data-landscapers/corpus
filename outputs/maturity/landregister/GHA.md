# Ghana

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The Lands Commission [runs its records on ELIS after scanning and geo-referencing them](<https://www.eis.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/THE-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-OF-GHANA_S-LANDS-COMMISSION-BENJAMIN-QUAYE.pdf>), and file retrieval fell from months to days. In October 2026 its Executive Secretary said [ELIS is operational in Greater Accra and nine other regions](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/lands-commissions-digital-reform-gamechanger-for-land-administration-in-ghana/>). [A digital service unit in Ho brought it to the Volta Region in June 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-client-service-access-unit-inaugurated-at-volta-regional-office-of-lands-commission.html>). [Manual processes still run beside it](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the Commission's target of nationwide rollout by the end of 2027](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/lands-commissions-digital-reform-gamechanger-for-land-administration-in-ghana/>).

**Online services.** In July 2026 the Lands Minister said [the public can apply online for official land searches in the nine regions where ELIS runs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/lands-commission-targets-one-month-land-registration.html>). [The Commission's portal takes searches, first registrations and transfers, with online payment](<https://www.eis.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/THE-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-OF-GHANA_S-LANDS-COMMISSION-BENJAMIN-QUAYE.pdf>). [ELIS had already put consolidated searches and stamp duty assessment online](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

***Also on file***. [Digital Land Acquisition and Management Platform (DILAP)](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).
