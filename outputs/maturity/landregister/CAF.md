# Central African Republic

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [On 8 April 2024 UNDP and the finance ministry signed a US$1.6 million agreement supporting the e-cadastre pilot](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/04/14/le-pnud-et-le-gouvernement-centrafricain-signent-un-accord-de-1-6-millions-de-dollars-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation/>). [The pilot is an electronic land management system, the UNDP representative said](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/04/14/le-pnud-et-le-gouvernement-centrafricain-signent-un-accord-de-1-6-millions-de-dollars-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation/>). [It is to digitise the cadastre service, which is kept on paper](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/04/14/le-pnud-et-le-gouvernement-centrafricain-signent-un-accord-de-1-6-millions-de-dollars-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation/>).
