# Zimbabwe

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Zimbabwe launched the Digital Land Administration Platform in May 2025 to process title deeds electronically](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>). [All titles must be digitised by July 2027](<https://openparly.com/unvalidated-title-deeds-to-lose-formal-recognition-in-11-months/>), when the platform becomes the sole title register. In August 2026 the lands minister said [three manual land databases had been merged into one digital platform](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/land-tenure-reform-ensures-bankability-and-agricultural-investment/>). The lands ministry [is building a single national land registry to replace records kept in Excel and Access](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-land-system-to-boost-transparency/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>).

**Online services.** [The Permanent Secretary said conveyancers submit the title deed information through the DLAP system to the Deeds Registry for processing, after which a securitised title deed is issued](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200054925/title-deeds-validation-programme-goes-nationwide>). [The eCadastre portal states property can be searched online from anywhere, with property cards available after payment, and surveyors can e-lodge surveys](<http://www.zimcadastre.co.zw/>).
