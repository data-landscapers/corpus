# Zimbabwe

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Zimbabwe launched the Digital Land Administration Platform for electronic processing of title deeds, per a Government Gazette notice announced by Justice Minister Ziyambi Ziyambi](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>). [Justice Minister Ziyambi told Parliament that the programme begun in July 2025 requires all urban freehold stands, sectional titles and agricultural land to be digitised, with the Digital Land Administrative Platform to become the sole authoritative title register after the 24-month validation period ends in July 2027 (Open Parly)](<https://openparly.com/unvalidated-title-deeds-to-lose-formal-recognition-in-11-months/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>).

**Online services.** [The Permanent Secretary said conveyancers submit the title deed information through the DLAP system to the Deeds Registry for processing, after which a securitised title deed is issued](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200054925/title-deeds-validation-programme-goes-nationwide>). [The eCadastre portal states property can be searched online from anywhere, with property cards available after payment, and surveyors can e-lodge surveys](<http://www.zimcadastre.co.zw/>).
