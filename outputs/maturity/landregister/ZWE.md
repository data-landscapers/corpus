# Zimbabwe

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Zimbabwe launched the Digital Land Administration Platform in May 2025 to process title deeds electronically](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>). [All titles must be digitised by July 2027](<https://openparly.com/unvalidated-title-deeds-to-lose-formal-recognition-in-11-months/>), when the platform becomes the sole title register. In August 2026 the lands minister said [three manual land databases had been merged into one digital platform](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/land-tenure-reform-ensures-bankability-and-agricultural-investment/>). The lands ministry [is building a single national land registry to replace records kept in Excel and Access](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-land-system-to-boost-transparency/>).

**Nationwide.** [In November 2026 The Herald reported that the One National e-Cadastre held over 80% of an estimated 1.3 to 1.4 million property records](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/new-digital-land-registry-captures-1m-properties-nears-launch/>). Its spatial records cover about 60% of the country. Its official launch is set for the first quarter of 2027.

**Online services.** [The Permanent Secretary said conveyancers submit the title deed information through the DLAP system to the Deeds Registry](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200054925/title-deeds-validation-programme-goes-nationwide>). A securitised title deed is issued after processing. [The eCadastre portal states property can be searched online from anywhere](<http://www.zimcadastre.co.zw/>), with property cards available after payment. Surveyors can e-lodge surveys there.
