# Zimbabwe

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Justice Minister Ziyambi told Parliament that the programme begun in July 2025 requires all urban freehold stands, sectional titles and agricultural land to be digitised, with the Digital Land Administrative Platform to become the sole authoritative title register after the 24-month validation period ends in July 2027 (Open Parly)](<https://openparly.com/unvalidated-title-deeds-to-lose-formal-recognition-in-11-months/>). [The Masvingo Mirror reports title deeds are being digitised under the Presidential Deeds Digitisation Programme at US$200 each, with submissions since April 2026 and a digitisation deadline of mid-2027, though lawyers say no deed submitted has been returned](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/presidential-deeds-digitisation-programme-a-scam-lawyers/>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [The Permanent Secretary said conveyancers submit the title deed information through the DLAP system to the Deeds Registry for processing, after which a securitised title deed is issued](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200054925/title-deeds-validation-programme-goes-nationwide>).
