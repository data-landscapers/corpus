# Sierra Leone

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In February 2024 [land and house ownership was recorded by hand](<https://sierraloaded.sl/feature/what-biometric-property-agreement/>), and a 2021 deal for biometric property registration had not been carried out. [A World Bank report of October 2024 found no land rights yet recorded under the Land Administration Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102924175012156/pdf/P177031-6773547a-5b87-4c2c-9ac7-3f49fd446856.pdf>), with digitisation still in procurement. In July 2025 the Ministry of Lands said [a firm was building a land management information system](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-05/5.jobo_samba.pdf>), and about 60,000 records were being digitised. [A boundary harmonisation pilot ended in March 2026](<https://ayvnews.com/government-concludes-boundary-harmonisation-pilotsets-stage-for-nationwide-land-title-registration/>). [The minister spoke of digital records and title registration in September 2026](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/land-reform-digital-titles-planning/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the system under construction in July 2025](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-05/5.jobo_samba.pdf>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. In June 2026 the minister said [an online portal will let anyone verify ownership records](<https://moice.gov.sl/civic-day-london-minister-of-lands-highlights-major-reforms-in-land-sector/>).
