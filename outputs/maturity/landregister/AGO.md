# Angola

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Government spokesman Paulo Tecas announced the creation of a digital platform with the tax authority AGT to link IGCA, AGT, the matrix cadastre and the Property Registry Conservatoria, to speed up property legalisation and registration, with a target of 40,000 properties by end of 2026](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/executivo-promete-legalizar-40-mil-imoveis-ate-ao-final-de-2026-71209.html>). [Secretary of State for Justice Osvaldo Amaro said the Executive is creating computer systems to ensure no conflicts between holders of property titles, as part of the property registration services](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Nationwide.** [Esri says GEODATA implemented the property registration system in all 22 registry offices in all 18 provinces of Angola](<https://www.esri.com/en-us/lg/industry/government/stories/angola-modernizes-land-administration-gis-cadastre-management>).
