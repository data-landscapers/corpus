# Angola

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Government spokesman Paulo Tecas announced a digital platform built with the tax authority AGT](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/executivo-promete-legalizar-40-mil-imoveis-ate-ao-final-de-2026-71209.html>). [It links IGCA, AGT, the matrix cadastre and the Property Registry Conservatoria](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/executivo-promete-legalizar-40-mil-imoveis-ate-ao-final-de-2026-71209.html>). [It is to register 40,000 properties by the end of 2026](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/executivo-promete-legalizar-40-mil-imoveis-ate-ao-final-de-2026-71209.html>). [Secretary of State for Justice Osvaldo Amaro said the Executive is building systems to prevent conflicting property titles](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>). [They form part of the property registration services](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Nationwide.** [Esri says GEODATA implemented the property registration system in all 22 registry offices in all 18 provinces of Angola](<https://www.esri.com/en-us/lg/industry/government/stories/angola-modernizes-land-administration-gis-cadastre-management>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest fact: [in 2024 GEODATA's cadastre, holding 450,000 urban parcel boundaries, was linked to the registry offices' system](<https://www.esri.com/en-us/lg/industry/government/stories/angola-modernizes-land-administration-gis-cadastre-management>).
