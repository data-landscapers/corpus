# Namibia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [NCLAS 1 was rolled out to all Communal Land Boards in 2008](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [The ministry introduced web-based NCLAS 2 in 2014](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [It registers customary and leasehold rights in communal areas](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [In May 2023 a FIG paper said the cadastral LIS had crashed the year before](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>). [It was being redeveloped, and its planned spatial website had not materialised](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>). [By then NCLAS had registered over 193,000 communal land rights, all mapped and digitised](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>). [That was about 79% of the estimated total](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [NCLAS 1 was rolled out to all Communal Land Boards in 2008](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>).
