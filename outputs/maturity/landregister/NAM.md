# Namibia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The presentation says NCLAS 1 was rolled out to all Communal Land Boards in 2008 and web-based, server-based NCLAS 2 was introduced in 2014 by the ministry, to register customary and leasehold rights in communal areas](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [The paper says the Digital Cadastral Information System's LIS crashed the previous year and is under further development, with the planned spatially enabled website not yet materialised (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>). [The FIG paper by NUST authors says the Namibia Communal Land Administration System has registered over 193,000 communal land rights, all mapped and digitised, about 79% of the estimated total (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2023/papers/ts08i/TS08I_MABAKENG.pdf>).
