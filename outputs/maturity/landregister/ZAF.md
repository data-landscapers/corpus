# South Africa

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Minister's reply says 16,911,328 deeds documents have been digitised and made publicly accessible through the eDRS Information Provisioning Module, live since 1 April 2025, and all deeds registered after 2014 are in electronic format](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34341/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34341/>).
