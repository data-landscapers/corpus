# South Africa

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The minister said in November 2025 that [16,911,328 deeds documents are digitised and open to the public through eDRS](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34341/>), live since 1 April 2025. Every deed registered since 2014 is electronic. In July 2026 [the minister gave a budget of R215 million for eDRS and R13.3 million to revamp the cadastral system](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/39709/>). SITA is to host eDRS.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the 16.9 million deeds documents open through eDRS](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34341/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the public access to deeds documents through eDRS since April 2025](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/34341/>).
