# Kenya

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ArdhiSasa is the government's digital platform for land information and land services](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The Law Society of Kenya says essential land services have moved to it](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>). In August 2026 the Lands PS said [it runs in Nairobi, Murang'a, Mombasa Island and Isiolo](<https://radiogeneration.co.ke/why-ardhisasa-expansion-has-fallen-behind-ps-korir_43436>). The Auditor-General found only Nairobi and Murang'a fully migrated, of 83 registries. [An FAO programme is scanning records in 10 registries in three counties](<https://www.fao.org/kenya/news/newsdetails/from-congestion-to-faster-services--how-digitization-is-transforming-kenya-s-land-registries/en>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the Auditor-General's count of 2 registries fully migrated out of 83](<https://radiogeneration.co.ke/why-ardhisasa-expansion-has-fallen-behind-ps-korir_43436>).

**Online services.** [Users can search land records, transfer property and register titles and leases on ArdhiSasa](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). They also pay stamp duty and land rent there. [Advocates carry out searches, transfers, registrations and valuations on it](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>), says the Law Society of Kenya. Its outage from 1 September 2026 held this work up.
