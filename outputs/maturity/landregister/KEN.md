# Kenya

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Kenyans.co.ke reports ArdhiSasa is the government's digital platform for land information and land services, with an outage announced on 2026-09-21 affecting land searches, transfers, title and lease registration and other transactions](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The Law Society of Kenya says Ardhisasa has become a central platform for delivery of land administration services in Kenya, with essential land services migrated to it](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>). [Kenyans.co.ke reports the FAO and the Council of Governors run a digital land governance programme targeting all 47 counties to strengthen use of digital land information and geospatial technologies](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>).

**Online services.** [Kenyans.co.ke reports ArdhiSasa lets users conduct land searches, property transfers, title and lease registration, stamp duty and land-rent payments without relying entirely on physical government offices](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The Law Society of Kenya says advocates' land searches, transfers, registrations and valuations are carried out on Ardhisasa and were disrupted by its unavailability from 2026-09-01](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>).
