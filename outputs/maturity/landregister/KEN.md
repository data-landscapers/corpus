# Kenya

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ArdhiSasa is the government's digital platform for land information and land services](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). An outage in September 2026 disrupted searches, transfers and title registration. [The Law Society of Kenya says essential land services have moved to it](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>). [The FAO and the Council of Governors run a digital land governance programme](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>) aimed at all 47 counties. [In November 2026 Vihiga County said its own land information system was 70 percent complete](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/from-paper-files-to-fingertips-vihiga-unleashes-digital-revolution/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>).

**Online services.** [Users can search land records, transfer property and register titles and leases on ArdhiSasa](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). They also pay stamp duty and land rent there. [Advocates carry out searches, transfers, registrations and valuations on it](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>), says the Law Society of Kenya. Its outage from 1 September 2026 held this work up.
