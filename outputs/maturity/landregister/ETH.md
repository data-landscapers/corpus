## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The eLand platform, built on CRPRS, is stated to be in use in the Ethiopian cities of Bishoftu, Adama and Shagar for digital land registration, transfers and title records](<https://land.et>). [The Ministry of Agriculture describes NRLAIS as a computer-based central repository of rural land transactions, land use and ownership records](<https://www.moa.gov.et/e-services/>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Nationwide.** [The Ministry of Agriculture states NRLAIS facilitates land administration in rural areas across the country](<https://www.moa.gov.et/e-services/>).

**Online services.** [The eLand platform states citizens can submit online applications for ownership transfer, lease, mortgage and parcel subdivision, search property with map integration, pay fees online and download title certificates](<https://land.et>).
