# Ethiopia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Ministry of Agriculture describes NRLAIS as a central computer record of rural land transactions and ownership](<https://www.moa.gov.et/e-services/>). Finland says [certificates had been issued for 34 million parcels in 562 woredas by the end of June 2026](<https://um.fi/results-of-development-cooperation/-/asset_publisher/o2A1dCQquJyC/content/miljoonille-etiopialaisille-oikeus-omaan-maahan/35732>). [The eLand platform runs digital registration, transfers and title records in Bishoftu, Adama and Shagar](<https://land.et>). In Addis Ababa [more than 700,000 land titles had been digitised by March 2024](<https://addisfortune.news/south-korea-funds-land-title-digitisation-in-four-cities>). [INSA says its cadastral registry system is in use in government agencies](<https://ethiopianbusinessreview.net/innovation-wears-netela-ethiopia-to-build-its-own-versions-of-whatsapp-gmail-and-zoom-citing-tired-of-data-leaks/>).

**Nationwide.** [The Ministry of Agriculture says NRLAIS serves rural areas across the country](<https://www.moa.gov.et/e-services/>). It holds rural land only, and eLand runs in three cities.

**Online services.** [The eLand platform states citizens can submit online applications for ownership transfer, lease, mortgage and parcel subdivision, search property with map integration, pay fees online and download title certificates](<https://land.et>).
