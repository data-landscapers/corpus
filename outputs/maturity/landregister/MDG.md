# Madagascar

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [A kiosk at the Tana-ville land office has issued registration certificates and plans from dematerialised records since 7 July 2025](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/lancement-du-distributeur-automatique-dinformation-au-sein-de-la-circonscription-fonciere-de-tana-ville-et-remise-de-materiel-pour-lapipa/>). The CASEF project [certified 1.1 million parcels with QR-coded certificates](<https://www.fao.org/newsroom/story/a-more-secure-future-from-a-single-document/>), to be stored in a national register once it is built. [The land ministry said in April 2025 that its land information system would roll out in five pilot regions](<https://newsmada.com/2025/04/17/mdat-deploiement-progressif-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere/>). In January 2026 Newsmada wrote that [land records still rest on old, often damaged archives](<https://newsmada.com/2026/01/23/madagascar-coree-du-sud-la-digitalisation-du-foncier-comme-levier-strategique-de-developpement/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the five-region pilot of the land information system](<https://newsmada.com/2025/04/17/mdat-deploiement-progressif-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the Tana-ville kiosk](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/lancement-du-distributeur-automatique-dinformation-au-sein-de-la-circonscription-fonciere-de-tana-ville-et-remise-de-materiel-pour-lapipa/>).
