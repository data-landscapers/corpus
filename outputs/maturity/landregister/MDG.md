# Madagascar

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [PRODUIR reports that after dematerialising legal-status certificates and topographic plans for Antananarivo, an automatic information kiosk entered public service on 7 July 2025 at the Tana-ville land office, issuing registration and legal-status certificates and individual plans](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/lancement-du-distributeur-automatique-dinformation-au-sein-de-la-circonscription-fonciere-de-tana-ville-et-remise-de-materiel-pour-lapipa/>). [Newsmada reports ProPFR digitised land data in the Analamanga region and piloted an interconnected land information system linking topographic circumscriptions and land offices](<https://newsmada.com/2026/01/05/propfr-giz-dix-ans-pour-instaurer-une-politique-fonciere-responsable/>). [The DGSF says PNF II aims at digitising and centralising land documents and data nationwide, in a land information system that will be operational soon](<https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/reformes-foncieres-vers-un-systeme-transparent-et-inclusif/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country.
