# Mauritania

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The land directorate said citizens can now access their secured land documents, starting in Nouakchott and then regional capitals](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/250018>). [Kassataya's opinion column says the government has a project to digitise the cadastre, including a national digital cadastral reference and a unique parcel identifier](<https://kassataya.com/mauritanie-le-numerique-comme-instrument-de-depossession-silencieuse-du-foncier/>). [AMI reports the land minister presented a draft decree creating a national digital reference of cadastral data as the State's official source, with a unique permanent digital identifier for each parcel, overseen by the land ministry](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/301506>). [The land minister said LEEGOU services will be extended by progressive installation of its branches in all 54 moughataas by 2027](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/250018>).
