# Mauritania

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The land directorate said citizens can now access their secured land documents, starting in Nouakchott and then regional capitals](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/250018>). [The Administration Générale des Domaines says it registers all contracts digitally on the Lagoud platform for real estate documents, with final copies delivered by e-mail or WhatsApp](<https://fr.madar.mr/actualites/mauritanie-procedures-pour-faciliter-lenregistrement-des-contrats-a-travers-une-plateforme-numerique/>). [Kassataya's opinion column says the government has a project to digitise the cadastre, including a national digital cadastral reference and a unique parcel identifier](<https://kassataya.com/mauritanie-le-numerique-comme-instrument-de-depossession-silencieuse-du-foncier/>). [The land minister said LEEGOU services will be extended by progressive installation of its branches in all 54 moughataas by 2027](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/250018>).
