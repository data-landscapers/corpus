# Gambia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The Gambia's land policy for 2026-2035 [names the absence of a central land information system and of cadastral data](<https://policies.gov.gm/f/6d4b253b-ef9d-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>). In January 2026 [nine firms bid for the National Land Information System](<https://gambiana.com/land-reforms-gather-pace-as-government-weighs-new-agency-and-sweeping-changes/>). In September the Lands Minister said [the system is being built with World Bank-supported consultants](<https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/>), and archives are being digitised. On 9 November 2026 the ministry said [its alpha version is complete and in testing](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>). A firm is setting up a centre to scan old land records.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the alpha version under test in November 2026](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the same alpha version](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>).
