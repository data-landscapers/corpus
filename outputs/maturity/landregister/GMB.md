# Gambia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Lands Minister Hamat Bah said on 4 September 2026 that the government is developing a Land Information System to hold digital records of landholdings across the country, with World Bank-supported consultants working on it and a draft due by end-September, and that land-record archives are being digitised](<https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/>). [The National Land Policy 2026-2035 states that land administration suffers from the absence of a centralised Land Information System and a lack of cadastral data, and calls for a digitalised National Land Administration System](<https://policies.gov.gm/f/6d4b253b-ef9d-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/>).
