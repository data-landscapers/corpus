# Lesotho

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** Land Equity International said in March 2025 that [Community Council land allocation records are still kept by hand and often lost](<https://www.landequity.com.au/news/land_registration_lesotho_ntsebo/>). Each council records allocation and ownership by hand, and lost records lead to duplicate allocations and disputes. [The councils are not linked to the Land Administration Authority](<https://www.landequity.com.au/news/land_registration_lesotho_ntsebo/>), which issues leases, the strongest title. Its offices are all in towns.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the paper records of the Community Councils, each of which works on its own](<https://www.landequity.com.au/news/land_registration_lesotho_ntsebo/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the same report](<https://www.landequity.com.au/news/land_registration_lesotho_ntsebo/>).
