# Comoros

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In April 2026 the head of the Cartography Unit said [a land information system is being set up as a national priority](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/nair-aboubacar-2265a092_un-moment-historique-pour-les-comores-au-activity-7449676142535692288-noIc>), with World Bank funding. The unit was training in GIS at the regional mapping centre in Nairobi. In February 2024 an opinion piece said [the country had no national online land register](<https://www.habarizacomores.com/2024/02/les-litiges-fonciers-et-les-arnaques.html>). It urged that land registers be digitised to curb fraud and land disputes.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the system being set up in April 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/nair-aboubacar-2265a092_un-moment-historique-pour-les-comores-au-activity-7449676142535692288-noIc>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the 2024 statement that no online register exists](<https://www.habarizacomores.com/2024/02/les-litiges-fonciers-et-les-arnaques.html>).
