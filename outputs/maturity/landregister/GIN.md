# Guinea

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** On 24 November 2025 the urbanism ministry's secretary general said [the system for issuing land titles has been digitised](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2025/11/guinee-la-digitalisation-du-titre-foncier-met-fin-aux-ventes-multiples-dun-meme-terrain-sg-muh/>). He said a parcel can no longer be titled twice. That ends the sale of one plot to several buyers. In October 2025 [the government began mapping state farmland in four regions](<https://www.agronewsguinee.net/2025/10/reforme-fonciere-lancement-de-la-cartographie-des-terres-agricoles-en-guinee/>). It is to feed a future rural land platform.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the digitised title system of November 2025](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2025/11/guinee-la-digitalisation-du-titre-foncier-met-fin-aux-ventes-multiples-dun-meme-terrain-sg-muh/>).
