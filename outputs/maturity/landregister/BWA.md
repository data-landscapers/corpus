# Botswana

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In August 2026 the Assistant Minister of Lands told Parliament that [Land Board officers process Secure Land Title transactions on the web-based Land Information System](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92827>). In 2024 the ministry [described the system as its database of land records](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77810>). [The 2026/27 budget funds a four-year upgrade of the system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89882>). In October 2025 [the deeds registry was still largely manual](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90382>).

**Nationwide.** [Land Board officers across the country use the system, so work is no longer all done in Gaborone](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92827>). Only the Gaborone registry prints the deeds. In February 2024 the minister said [every residential plot in the country now has a plot number](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/77810>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the web-based system used by Land Board officers](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92827>).
