# Benin

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Matin Libre reports Parakou has full cadastral coverage with 196,474 parcels registered in the national cadastre on the e-Foncier Bénin platform, with over 11,500 registration certificates (CEC) distributed by 1 September 2026](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>). [La Nation reports over 53,000 parcels registered across Porto-Novo in the e-Foncier Bénin digital cadastral database, with Cadastre registration certificates being distributed and 8,777 updates integrated](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/mise-en-place-du-cadastre-national-plus-de-53-000-parcelles-deja-enregistrees-a-porto-novo-les-pays-bas-satisfaits-des-resultats-obtenus>). [Municipal officials say cadastral data updating continues and new e-Foncier Bénin functions for recasement attestations, mutation certificates and compulsions are to be developed, while the PPMEC project aims for a complete cadastre](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>).
