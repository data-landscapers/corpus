# Benin

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The land agency ANDF runs the national cadastre on its e-Foncier Bénin platform. On 6 November 2026 its project chief [put the database at 643,993 parcels in 12 communes, 3 of them fully covered](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>). Parakou alone [had 196,474 parcels registered by September 2026](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>), and Porto-Novo [over 53,000 by April](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/mise-en-place-du-cadastre-national-plus-de-53-000-parcelles-deja-enregistrees-a-porto-novo-les-pays-bas-satisfaits-des-resultats-obtenus>). Officials say [the data is still being updated and new functions are planned](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. In November 2026 [9 of the 12 communes it reached were only partly covered](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is that [e-Foncier Bénin holds the cadastral records](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>).
