# Benin

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The land agency ANDF runs the national cadastre on its e-Foncier Bénin platform. On 6 November 2026 its project chief [put the database at 643,993 parcels in 12 communes, 3 of them fully covered](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>). Parakou alone [had 196,474 parcels registered by September 2026](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>). [The Council of Ministers found Cotonou fully covered in April 2025](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/cm/2025-04-09/download>). ANDF says [it has digitised all the paper files of the former land directorate](<https://academie-sciences.bj/actualite/tribune-de-haut-niveau-des-experts-du-numerique-et-du-foncier>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. In November 2026 [9 of the 12 communes it reached were only partly covered](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>). The government launched two programmes that month: PPMEC II continues in the same 12 communes, and TERRA BENIN [aims to register 1.5 million parcels, starting in 14 communes](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3734/modernisation-fonciere-benin-deux-nouveaux-programmes-accelerer-deploiement-cadastre-national/>).

**Online services.** ANDF [put five e-services on service-public.bj on 5 June 2024](<https://andf.bj/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/13_Communique_au_public_E_SERVICE-1-1.pdf>). They include requests for a land title. In July 2026 it said [E-TERRE takes certain land procedures online](<https://academie-sciences.bj/actualite/tribune-de-haut-niveau-des-experts-du-numerique-et-du-foncier>). It is linked to the national public services portal.
