# Morocco

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ANCFCC states it holds 7.7 million land titles and 260 million land and cadastral documents digitised](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). [It created over 373,000 land titles in 2024, and its whole map archive is digitised and georeferenced](<https://www.btpnews.ma/lancfcc-fer-de-lance-dun-cadastre-intelligent-au-service-dun-etat-plus-performant/>). [Notaries have consulted its electronic database since 15 January 2024](<https://medias24.com/2024/01/17/documents-fonciers-la-consultation-en-ligne-desormais-ouverte-aux-notaires/>). [Its board forecast 2,000,000 digital property certificates in 2025](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/media/ametop/Communiqu%c3%a9%2025%20decembre%202024%20VF.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** [ANCFCC states national territory is covered by digital cadastral maps](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). [ANCFCC states its digital cadastral maps cover the entire national territory](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/pr%C3%A9sentation/chiffres-cl%C3%A9s>).

**Online services.** [ANCFCC states 86% of its 3 million users are online](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>), including 76% of notarial deed filings and 95% of certificates issued. Through [its e-conservation and e-cadastre platforms users consult titles, follow files, pay and request certificates](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/agence-nationale-de-la-conservation-fonciere-du-cadastre-et-de-la-cartographie-la-transformation-numerique-au-service-dune-gouvernance-fonciere-durable.html>). [Notaries consult land documents online for a fee](<https://medias24.com/2024/01/17/documents-fonciers-la-consultation-en-ligne-desormais-ouverte-aux-notaires/>).
