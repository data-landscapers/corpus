# Morocco

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ANCFCC states it holds 7.7 million land titles and 260 million land and cadastral documents digitised](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). [ANCFCC's page lists digital cadastral maps covering the whole national territory among its information assets, alongside 6,750,000 land titles and cadastral plans at end 2021](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/pr%C3%A9sentation/chiffres-cl%C3%A9s>). [The ANCFCC board communique says the project to digitalise services for citizens and institutional partners continues, with a forecast of 2,000,000 digital property certificates and an information-system overhaul in 2025](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/media/ametop/Communiqu%c3%a9%2025%20decembre%202024%20VF.pdf>). [ANCFCC director general's circular says notaries can consult documents in the agency's electronic database from 15 January 2024](<https://medias24.com/2024/01/17/documents-fonciers-la-consultation-en-ligne-desormais-ouverte-aux-notaires/>).

**Nationwide.** [ANCFCC states national territory is covered by digital cadastral maps](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). [ANCFCC states its digital cadastral maps cover the entire national territory](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/pr%C3%A9sentation/chiffres-cl%C3%A9s>).

**Online services.** [Notaries consult land documents online through a dedicated space on the ANCFCC electronic platform, for a fee](<https://medias24.com/2024/01/17/documents-fonciers-la-consultation-en-ligne-desormais-ouverte-aux-notaires/>). [ANCFCC states 86% of its 3 million users are online, including 76% of notarial deed filings and 95% of certificates issued](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>).
