# Chad

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ATPE reports the Direction générale des Domaines put E-Conservation (SIDCF) into production on 2 October 2023, with the first 5,000 secured land deeds distributed and a database for managing land files](<https://atpe.td/actes-fonciers-la-conservation-electronique-est-lancee/>). [Portail de la Renaissance du Tchad reports Chad's land ministry signed an accord with IGNFI covering modernisation of geographic information systems and digitalisation of cadastral data](<https://portail.td/cooperation-tchad-france-un-accord-signe-pour-la-modernisation-du-foncier-et-la-numerisation-des-archives-cadastrales/>). [ATPE reports the land ministry signed a partnership with Géo Consult on 5 December 2025 with priorities of modernising the cadastre and developing an urban and land information system](<https://atpe.td/modernisation-fonciere-le-ministere-de-lamenagement-du-territoire-signe-un-accord-avec-geo-consult/>).
