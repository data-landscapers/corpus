# Chad

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The domains directorate put its E-Conservation system into production on 2 October 2023](<https://atpe.td/actes-fonciers-la-conservation-electronique-est-lancee/>), with the first 5,000 secured deeds. In November 2026 the Cadastre director said [regularisation files are checked in the GIRAF database and archived digitally](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/11/08/foncier-au-tchad-la-regularisation-pour-prevenir-les-conflits/>). [A parcel and land-right recording pilot was set for N'Djamena's 7th district and rural Linia in March 2026](<https://www.kadaster.com/-/from-cocreation-to-action-in-chad>). [The land ministry signed with IGNFI to digitise cadastral data](<https://portail.td/cooperation-tchad-france-un-accord-signe-pour-la-modernisation-du-foncier-et-la-numerisation-des-archives-cadastrales/>), and [with Géo Consult in December 2025](<https://atpe.td/modernisation-fonciere-le-ministere-de-lamenagement-du-territoire-signe-un-accord-avec-geo-consult/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://atpe.td/actes-fonciers-la-conservation-electronique-est-lancee/>).
