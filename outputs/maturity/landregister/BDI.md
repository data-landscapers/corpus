# Burundi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [We Are Tech Africa reports the land directorate DTFCN runs a Building Permit System for initial titles and a Property Management System for transfers, corrections and mortgages, joined by an EDRMS launched 17 June 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>). [The DTFCN uses a Property Management System that handles updates to existing titles (transfers, corrections, mortgages) and a Building Permit System that handles issuing first titles, both interconnected with the new EDRMS](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>). [ABP reports the Prime Minister launched on 14 July 2026 the RPPCUB-2026 urban owner census to build a digitised, geo-referenced land register, starting in Muha, Mukaza, Ntahangwa, Gitega, Ngozi and Rumonge](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/07/16/lancement-officiel-du-recensement-des-proprietaires-des-proprietes-ou-parcelles-dans-les-centres-urbains-du-burundi/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>).
