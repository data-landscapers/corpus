# Burundi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The land directorate DTFCN runs a Building Permit System for first titles and a Property Management System for transfers, corrections and mortgages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>). [An electronic records system joined them on 17 June 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>). The 2025-2026 Finance Law [required every titleholder to apply for a secure electronic land title](<https://burundi-eco.com/titres-fonciers-electroniques-securises-entre-avancee-technologique-et-inquietudes-sociales/>), with files taken at Bujumbura, Gitega, Makamba and Ngozi. [The 2026-2027 budget law moved the deadline to 31 March 2027](<https://cn-law.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/EN-Budget-Law-2026-2027-CN-Law.pdf>). In July 2026 the Prime Minister [launched a census of urban owners to build a digitised, geo-referenced land register](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/07/16/lancement-officiel-du-recensement-des-proprietaires-des-proprietes-ou-parcelles-dans-les-centres-urbains-du-burundi/>), starting in six towns.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>).
