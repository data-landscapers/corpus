# Burundi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [We Are Tech Africa reports the land directorate DTFCN runs a Building Permit System for initial titles and a Property Management System for transfers, corrections and mortgages, joined by an EDRMS launched 17 June 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>). [ABP reports the Prime Minister launched on 14 July 2026 the RPPCUB-2026 urban owner census to build a digitised, geo-referenced land register, starting in Muha, Mukaza, Ntahangwa, Gitega, Ngozi and Rumonge](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/07/16/lancement-officiel-du-recensement-des-proprietaires-des-proprietes-ou-parcelles-dans-les-centres-urbains-du-burundi/>). [The Ministry of Finance posted joint ordinance 550/540/998 of 6 February 2026 setting the terms for acquiring the secured electronic land title](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/02/09/ordonnance-ministerielle-conjointe-n550-540-998-du-06-02-2026-portant-determination-des-modalites-dacquisition-du-titre-foncier-electronique-securise-en-application-de-larticl/>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>).

**Online services.** No source held since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-s-land-services-go-digital-with-new-integrated-platform>).
