# Mali

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** A July 2026 review of the land reforms says [over 1.5 million parcels in Bamako, Kati and several towns have a unique cadastral number](<https://www.maliweb.net/habitationsparcelles/gestion-fonciere-et-domaniale-le-bilan-des-reformes-phares-de-la-transition-3118561.html>). The digitised-documents module was updated at the Bamako and Kati land offices. [One million Bamako parcels had been digitised by March 2024](<https://www.maliweb.net/habitationsparcelles/problematique-de-la-gestion-du-foncier-letat-a-la-recherche-de-solutions-perennes-3058808.html>). [A land information system and one-stop land office was presented in March 2025](<https://www.ortm.ml/digitalisation-de-ladministration-deux-nouvelles-plateformes-de-modernisation-lancees>), to run in Bamako's six communes.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the July 2026 count of 1.5 million parcels](<https://www.maliweb.net/habitationsparcelles/gestion-fonciere-et-domaniale-le-bilan-des-reformes-phares-de-la-transition-3118561.html>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the one-stop land office planned for Bamako](<https://www.ortm.ml/digitalisation-de-ladministration-deux-nouvelles-plateformes-de-modernisation-lancees>).
