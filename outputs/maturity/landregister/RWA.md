# Rwanda

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The National Land Authority lists a National Registry system, LAIS, with a spatial database](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>). More than 14 systems connect to it. [The Ministry of Environment says every land record is now kept in a digital registry](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>), and the e-Title system issues digital titles. [Over 10 million paper titles were converted to digital form in 2023](<https://www.houseinrwanda.com/news/digital-land-titles-are-changing-land-ownership-and-verification-rwanda>), and more than 866,000 e-titles have been downloaded. In October 2026 the Governance Scorecard said [LAIS is linked to IFMIS, the Rwanda Revenue Authority and Irembo, putting 12 land services online](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>).

**Nationwide.** [The Ministry of Environment says land tenure regularisation in 2009-2013 digitised parcels across the country](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>). It says every land record is now held in a digital registry.

**Online services.** [The Ministry of Environment says land transactions that need no notary are fully online through Irembo](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>). E-Titles can be downloaded at landinformation.lands.rw.
