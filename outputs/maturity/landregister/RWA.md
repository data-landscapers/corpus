# Rwanda

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The National Land Authority presentation lists the establishment of a National Registry system (LAIS) with a spatial database, with more than 14 systems connected to LAIS](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>). [The Ministry of Environment says that today every land record is kept in a digital registry, and that the e-Title system issues digital land titles](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Nationwide.** [The Ministry of Environment says the nationwide land tenure regularisation of 2009-2013 digitised parcels and that today every land record is kept in a digital registry](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>).

**Online services.** [The Ministry of Environment says land transactions that need no notary are now fully online via Irembo and e-Titles can be downloaded at landinformation.lands.rw](<https://www.environment.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launched-electronic-land-registration-certificate-system>).
