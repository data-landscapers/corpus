# Tanzania

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The Ministry of Lands' [e-Ardhi system is in use in Dodoma, Arusha, Tanga, Mbeya and Mwanza regions and several councils](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>), TanzaniaInvest reports. It covers planning, surveying, allocation, registration and valuation. In Dodoma the deputy minister said [the National Land Data Infrastructure project is building a national land database](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/economy/national-land-database-digital-land-services>). It will centralise official land records and geographic information. [Zanzibar launched its LARIS land project on 27 July 2026, starting with aerial mapping](<https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/>). [LARIS runs to May 2028 and will digitise land documents in an integrated land information system](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>).

**Online services.** A Ruvuma lands official said [citizens can sign their title deed through e-Ardhi from any region](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>). They no longer travel to the region where the service began.
