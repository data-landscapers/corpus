# Tanzania

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** On 30 October 2026 the Lands PS said [e-Ardhi is operational in 17 regions, with land records kept in a central digital register](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/tanzania-targets-nationwide-rollout-of-e-ardhi-land-system-by-june-2027-2026-10-30-124103>). [The deputy minister told Parliament's lands committee the same on 21 October](<https://www.lands.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-bunge-yasisitiza-wananchi-kuelimishwa-umuhimu-wa-hatimiliki-za-ardhi>). The system covers planning, surveying, allocation, registration and valuation. [The National Land Data Infrastructure project is building a national land database](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/economy/national-land-database-digital-land-services>). [Zanzibar's LARIS project runs to May 2028 and will digitise land documents](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the target of e-Ardhi in every region by June 2027](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/tanzania-targets-nationwide-rollout-of-e-ardhi-land-system-by-june-2027-2026-10-30-124103>).

**Online services.** A Ruvuma lands official said [citizens can sign their title deed through e-Ardhi from any region](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>). They no longer travel to the region where the service began.
