# Tanzania

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Ministry of Lands' e-Ardhi system, covering planning, surveying, allocation, registration and valuation, is already in use in Dodoma, Arusha, Tanga, Mbeya and Mwanza regions and several municipal and district councils, per TanzaniaInvest](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>). [Deputy Minister Kaspar Mmuya said the National Land Data Infrastructure project is building a national land database to centralise official land records and geographic information, during an inspection visit in Dodoma](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/economy/national-land-database-digital-land-services>). [IGN FI reports the Land Administration and Registration Information System (LARIS) project was officially launched in Zanzibar on 27 July 2026, with aerial mapping starting to provide the foundation for Zanzibar's Digital Land Information System](<https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>).

**Online services.** [A Ruvuma assistant commissioner for lands said citizens can sign their title deed through e-Ardhi while in any region, without travelling to the region where the service originated](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/construction/e-ardhi-digital-land-system>).
