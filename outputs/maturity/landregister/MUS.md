# Mauritius

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The government's list of operational systems, dated November 2024, [names the Land Administration and Valuation Information Management System (LAVIMS)](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>) of the housing and land ministry. The Registrar General [calls its e-Registry System the platform for land and property registration](<https://registrar.govmu.org/rgd/index.php/upcoming-projects/>). In May 2026 the ministry [tendered consultancy for LAVIMS 2.0](<https://housing.govmu.org/Communique/Clarification%20No%201.pdf>), to be linked to the Registrar General's systems.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the November 2024 listing of LAVIMS](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). It does not say which land offices use the system.

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the same listing of LAVIMS](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>), which says nothing of a public portal.
