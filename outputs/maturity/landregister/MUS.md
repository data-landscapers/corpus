# Mauritius

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The government's list of operational systems, dated November 2024, [names the Land Administration and Valuation Information Management System (LAVIMS)](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). The list places it under the Ministry of Housing and Land Use Planning. Its name covers both land administration and valuation. The list gives no date for its launch and no count of parcels or titles it holds.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the November 2024 listing of LAVIMS](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). It does not say which land offices use the system.

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the same listing of LAVIMS](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>), which says nothing of a public portal.
