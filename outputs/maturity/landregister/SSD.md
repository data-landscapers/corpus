# South Sudan

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Central Equatoria's Minister of Information says engineers are developing a unified digital land registration system integrating applications, registration and transfers, beginning with Juba County](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/08/central-equatoria-state-advances-digital-land-registration/>). [IOM says the EU-funded Catalysing Durable Solutions programme, begun 1 January 2026 in Rubkona and Yei River counties, will advance the digitalization of land records](<https://southsudan.iom.int/news/eu-iom-launch-new-initiative-help-displacement-affected-communities-rebuild-south-sudan>). [Undersecretary Louis Kwot says the Ministry of Lands, which took custody of the Land Registry from the judiciary, is in the process of implementing a digitalised land registry](<https://www.eyeradio.org/ministry-of-lands-set-to-finalise-key-land-managment-policies/>). [Policy expert Alimure Ali Awuda says South Sudan's current land registration system is manual and urges introducing a digital registration process](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/06/18/digital-land-register-key-in-fighting-fraud-expert/>).
