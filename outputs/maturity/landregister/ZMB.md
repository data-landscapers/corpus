# Zambia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [A Ministry of Lands ICT officer says ZILAS, deployed by the SMART Zambia Institute and hosted on the Government Service Bus, is in use and issuance of title deeds is now digitally processed](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=3769>). [SMART Zambia Institute says the Zambia Integrated Land Administration System (ZILAS) is deployed and 100% digital, handling land administration in Zambia](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=3792>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [SMART Zambia Institute says users can apply for any land-related matter through ZILAS web and mobile interfaces, accessed via the Government Service Bus](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=3792>).
