# Nigeria

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** Lagos State's [e-GIS portal went live in January 2024 with the state's digitised land titles and records](<https://businessday.ng/life/article/lagos-revolutionises-land-acquisition-as-sanwo-olu-launches-automated-application-portal/>). In Cross River [the 360Lands registry had processed 330 title and consent applications by June 2026](<https://guardian.ng/technology/tech-firm-to-tackle-delays-in-c-of-o-processing/>). In June 2026 the housing minister [inspected a federal Centralised Land Repository built ahead of its launch](<https://guardian.ng/news/fed-govt-reaffirms-commitment-to-digitalization-of-land-administration/>). [He said over 90 per cent of land is unregistered and untitled](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/03/20/with-300-billion-in-dead-capital-fg-plans-digitisation-of-land-information-system-nationwide>) in March 2025.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the federal repository awaiting launch in June 2026](<https://guardian.ng/news/fed-govt-reaffirms-commitment-to-digitalization-of-land-administration/>).

**Online services.** [Anyone can search, verify and apply for land titles on the Lagos portal](<https://businessday.ng/life/article/lagos-revolutionises-land-acquisition-as-sanwo-olu-launches-automated-application-portal/>). [In Cross River applications for a Certificate of Occupancy can be made online](<https://guardian.ng/technology/tech-firm-to-tackle-delays-in-c-of-o-processing/>).
