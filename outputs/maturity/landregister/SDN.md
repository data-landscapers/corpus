# Sudan

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In August 2025 the Registrar General of Lands [ordered registry offices to check paper records against the electronic record](<https://almndranews.com/71664/>). The check comes before a search certificate is issued. [Registries had closed when armed groups took parts of four states](<https://almndranews.com/71664/>), and some records were lost. In October 2026 an Omdurman registry employee said [the office checks ownership in an electronic database](<https://3ayin.com/real-estate-ownership-dispute-/>). It keeps backup copies.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the Registrar General's order to all registry offices](<https://almndranews.com/71664/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the Omdurman office's electronic database](<https://3ayin.com/real-estate-ownership-dispute-/>).
