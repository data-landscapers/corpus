# Cape Verde

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** A joint order of 17 March 2025 [declared the island of São Vicente a cadastred area](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=74399>), with property registration compulsory. It follows a project that built a property rights information system and cadastred Sal, Boa Vista and Maio. [Mindelo was declared cadastred on 19 March 2025](<https://www.governo.cv/cidade-do-mindelo-declarada-area-cadastrada/>). [Survey work resumed in Tarrafal and São Salvador do Mundo in October 2026](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-11888>). [The finance ministry is procuring cadastral modules for municipalities](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/ToR_SIMple_Territory_Management.pdf/fe03f815-0452-ffd6-e948-2b00d5623dc3>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. In October 2026 the minister put [national cadastral coverage at about 33%](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-11888>).

**Online services.** [The PEO platform takes property transfers](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). The notary, the land registry, the council and the cadastre work through it.
