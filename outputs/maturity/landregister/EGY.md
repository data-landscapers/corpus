# Egypt

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The property registry's New Alamein office is linked by a digital hub to the New Urban Communities Authority](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentMulti/549747/Multimedia.aspx>). [All data on land and units in the new communities can be retrieved through it](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentMulti/549747/Multimedia.aspx>). [In July 2025 the Prime Minister opened the link in 16 registry offices in the new cities](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>). [In October 2026 the justice ministry launched a network linking the property registry with four ministries and the survey bodies](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%85%D8%B9/7597491>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentMulti/549747/Multimedia.aspx>).

**Online services.** [In October 2026 the justice ministry said a unified digital platform lets a citizen complete property registration remotely](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%85%D8%B9/7597491>). [It covers the whole cycle, from application to payment and e-signature](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/10/28/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%85%D8%B9/7597491>). [Electronic applications were already possible in 2022](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/3458304.aspx>).
