# Land register: land register

*As at 2026-11-11.*

## The norm

The AU Framework and Guidelines on Land Policy asks for land rights, customary tenure included, to be registered and tracked in a computerised land information system with public access. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Paper | A source from the past three years says land rights are recorded on paper or not recorded. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Nationwide | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Online | Yes to 4 |

- The minister of urbanism and housing said a single cadastre is being built through the E-Cadastre project developed with ANSI and financed by the World Bank PVI, with cadastral digitalisation the priority ([source, 2026-06-22](https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-et-de-lhabitat-le-colonel-major-abdoulkadri-amadou-dresse-le-bilan-des-reformes-et-des-chantiers-en-cours/)).
- ANSI says Niger is engaged in the progressive deployment of a national E-Cadastre, steered by the newly installed CONPEC, with ANSI building the NIPU parcel-identification platform as its infrastructure ([source, 2025-12-17](https://ansi.ne/?p=1143)).

### Senegal: 2 Nascent

Digital land registration was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- PROCASEF's steering committee records that SIFCOM development is complete and the system is in its deployment phase, with 55 communes integrated in preproduction at 30 June 2026 ([source, 2026-06-30](https://procasef.com/comite-de-pilotage-du-procasef-des-resultats-historiques-salues-et-un-ptba-2026-revise-adopte/)).
- The World Bank reports 351,899 occupancy titles with NICAD recorded in the Land Information System at 21 November 2025, and zero municipal land bureaux yet equipped and operational with LIS access ([source, 2025-11-21](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122530674/pdf/P172422-ab8683a5-9e88-4886-8cab-aa59ce4e604d.pdf)).

### Sierra Leone: 2 Nascent

Digital land registration was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Minister Turad Senesi said Sierra Leone is advancing reforms to modernise land administration through digital records and is moving towards title registration ([source, 2026-09-08](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/land-reform-digital-titles-planning/)).
- The World Bank status report says the Land Administration Project's Land Information System component will start new procurements to fast-track digitisation of land records and development of a digital land information system, with 0 of 200,000 land records digitised at 15 March 2024 ([source, 2024-03-15](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041024065516011/pdf/P1770311f0362d0d01b4bf1fa651ae664b7.pdf)).

### South Sudan: 2 Nascent

Digital land registration was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Central Equatoria's Minister of Information says engineers are developing a unified digital land registration system integrating applications, registration and transfers, beginning with Juba County ([source, 2026-07-08](https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/08/central-equatoria-state-advances-digital-land-registration/)).
