# Togo

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The OTR says it is georeferencing land titles created before 2007, with surveyors measuring parcels and the data entered into a digital database, to modernise the cadastre; the Directorate of Cadastre and Land Conservation leads the operation](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1544-georeferencement-des-titres-fonciers-une-revolution-pour-la-securite-et-la-transparence-au-togo.html>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
