# Togo

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Togo Officiel says land title procedures are fully dematerialised](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>). On 3 December 2025 [contractors delivered the full digitisation of the land books](<https://lomegazette.com/transformation-numerique-au-togo-lotr-et-le-pagdsp-saluent-lexcellence-operationnelle-de-cagecfi-et-sesa-technologies-lors-de-la-reception-des-livres-fonciers-numerises/>), and the land directorate had stopped manual titles two weeks before. [The tax office is georeferencing titles issued before 2007](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1544-georeferencement-des-titres-fonciers-une-revolution-pour-la-securite-et-la-transparence-au-togo.html>), and [every parcel has had a unique number since August 2024](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/2605-16413-togo-operation-de-mise-a-jour-des-anciens-titres-fonciers-non-georeferences>). In May 2026 it [sought a firm to build the modules of its land information system](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/ami-togo-recrutement-dun-cabinet-charge-du-developpement-de-lapplication-du-systeme-dinformations-du-foncier-sif-loffice-togolais-des-recettes-otr-pagdsp>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [in September 2024 land title procedures were fully dematerialised](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>).

**Online services.** [Togo Officiel says citizens can now carry out all cadastre procedures for a land title remotely through an online portal](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>). [Togo-Presse reports that, through an online portal, citizens can now carry out all Cadastre procedures for a land title remotely, and that a unique parcel number (NUP) has been introduced](<https://togopresse.tg/une-plateforme-numerique-creee-pour-rendre-les-services-gouvernementaux-plus-accessibles/>).
