# Togo

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Togo Officiel says land title procedures are fully dematerialised, with all cadastre procedures done remotely via an online portal](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>). [The OTR says it is georeferencing land titles created before 2007, with surveyors measuring parcels and the data entered into a digital database, to modernise the cadastre; the Directorate of Cadastre and Land Conservation leads the operation](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1544-georeferencement-des-titres-fonciers-une-revolution-pour-la-securite-et-la-transparence-au-togo.html>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>).

**Online services.** [Togo Officiel says citizens can now carry out all cadastre procedures for a land title remotely through an online portal](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>). [Togo-Presse reports that, through an online portal, citizens can now carry out all Cadastre procedures for a land title remotely, and that a unique parcel number (NUP) has been introduced](<https://togopresse.tg/une-plateforme-numerique-creee-pour-rendre-les-services-gouvernementaux-plus-accessibles/>).
