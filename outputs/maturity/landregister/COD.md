**Digital register in service.** [APA reported on 2026-08-11 that the Ministry of Land Affairs launched training for 250 officials, including 50 Land Title Registrars, with INACO, followed by scanning and indexing of archives in seven pilot land districts as the opening phase of the national Land Registry's digital transformation](<https://apanews.net/dr-congo-land-registry-begins-digitalisation-process/>).
