# DR Congo

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** In June 2026 the land ministry said [a pilot is making digital archiving work in seven Kinshasa land districts](<https://www.affairesfoncieres.gouv.cd/actualites/rdc-le-gouvernement-adopte-le-plan-foncier-national-2026-2040-tournant-historique-pour-la-gestion-des-terres>). It [trained 250 officials, 50 of them registrars, in August 2026](<https://apanews.net/dr-congo-land-registry-begins-digitalisation-process/>), before scanning archives in those districts. On 5 August it [signed a protocol with INaCo to digitise land administration](<https://www.affairesfoncieres.gouv.cd/actualites/refo-onei-lan-sation-de-ladmin>). [A UN-Habitat study found digitisation still embryonic in December 2025](<https://drcongo.un.org/fr/308407-des-donn%C3%A9es-fonci%C3%A8res-pour-orienter-les-choix-techniques-institutionnels-et-financiers-la>), with under 15% of plots holding a legal document. [A land atlas study counted 151 land offices across 26 provinces](<https://fonaredd-rdc.org/rdc-restitution-des-etudes-sur-levaluation-des-initiatives-de-numerisation-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere-et-de-la-mise-en-place-de-latlas-des-circonscriptions-foncieres/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the UN-Habitat finding that digitisation was embryonic in December 2025](<https://drcongo.un.org/fr/308407-des-donn%C3%A9es-fonci%C3%A8res-pour-orienter-les-choix-techniques-institutionnels-et-financiers-la>).
