# Algeria

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The General Directorate of National Land Domain announced on 11 January 2026 the nationwide rollout of the AMLAK information system to gradually replace the paper land title booklet with an electronic title, with all cadastral and land registry offices to be connected ultimately](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-moves-to-electronic-land-titles-with-amlak-system>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
