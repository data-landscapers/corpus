# Algeria

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** On 11 January 2026 the national domain directorate [generalised Amlak, one system with a central database for cadastre, land conservation and state domains](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/01/27/algerie-publication-dun-decret-officialisant-la-mise-en-place-du-livret-foncier-electronique/>). [Decree 26-15 of 7 January 2026 created the electronic land booklet](<https://ecotimesdz.com/numerisation-prestations-cadastrales-livret-foncier-electronique/>) that Amlak is to issue. [The Fadaeldjazair portal draws on more than 27 million documents from 217 land conservations](<https://www.algerie360.com/fadaeldjazair-livret-foncier-cadastre-en-ligne/>), and over 14 million properties are digitised. In Guelma [rural cadastre is complete and urban cadastre about 98%](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2026/11/04/guelma-cadastre-et-conservation-fonciere-accelerent-leur-mue-numerique/>), La Sentinelle reported in November 2026.

**Nationwide.** [The director general said in March 2025 that the database covers 217 land conservations across the country](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/03/11/un-livret-foncier-electronique-sous-forme-de-carte-electronique-en-2025/>). [Amlak reached every local directorate of cadastre, land conservation and state domains in January 2026](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-vers-le-livret-foncier-electronique-avec-amlak/>).

**Online services.** [The Fada El Djazair portal gives access to cadastral and land information](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/03/11/un-livret-foncier-electronique-sous-forme-de-carte-electronique-en-2025/>). It takes fees electronically. Users [request the land booklet and cadastral extracts online and pay by card](<https://www.algerie360.com/fadaeldjazair-livret-foncier-cadastre-en-ligne/>). Some steps still need an office visit.
