# Algeria

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Executive decree 26-15 of 7 January 2026 instituted the electronic land booklet, and the Direction generale du Domaine national announced Amlak would be generalised to all cadastre and land conservation directorates in preparation for issuing it](<https://ecotimesdz.com/numerisation-prestations-cadastrales-livret-foncier-electronique/>). [The General Directorate of National Land Domain announced on 11 January 2026 the nationwide rollout of the AMLAK information system to gradually replace the paper land title booklet with an electronic title, with all cadastral and land registry offices to be connected ultimately](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-moves-to-electronic-land-titles-with-amlak-system>).

**Nationwide.** [The director general says the database covers 217 land conservations across the country](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/03/11/un-livret-foncier-electronique-sous-forme-de-carte-electronique-en-2025/>).

**Online services.** [The director general says the Fada El Djazair portal, launched May 2024, gives access to cadastral and land information and offers over 6,500 electronic services, plus electronic payment of land service fees](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/03/11/un-livret-foncier-electronique-sous-forme-de-carte-electronique-en-2025/>).
