# Senegal

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The World Bank reports 351,899 occupancy titles with NICAD recorded in the Land Information System at 21 November 2025, and zero municipal land bureaux yet equipped and operational with LIS access](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122530674/pdf/P172422-ab8683a5-9e88-4886-8cab-aa59ce4e604d.pdf>). [PROCASEF reports 10,318 titres d'affectation approved in the NICAD system were handed over in Dodel commune, with a land office and SIFCOM still to be deployed there](<https://procasef.com/vallee-du-fleuve-senegal-et-ferlo-la-commune-de-dodel-franchit-une-etape-majeure-dans-la-securisation-fonciere-avec-la-remise-officielle-de-10-318-titres-daffectation/>). [PROCASEF's steering committee records that SIFCOM development is complete and the system is in its deployment phase, with 55 communes integrated in preproduction at 30 June 2026](<https://procasef.com/comite-de-pilotage-du-procasef-des-resultats-historiques-salues-et-un-ptba-2026-revise-adopte/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122530674/pdf/P172422-ab8683a5-9e88-4886-8cab-aa59ce4e604d.pdf>).
