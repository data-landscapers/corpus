# Senegal

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The World Bank counted 351,899 occupancy titles recorded in the land information system on 21 November 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122530674/pdf/P172422-ab8683a5-9e88-4886-8cab-aa59ce4e604d.pdf>), and no municipal land office yet working on it. In June 2026 PROCASEF said [830,000 parcels are mapped in 138 communes and 128 land offices built for SIFCOM](<https://aps.sn/securisation-fonciere-le-procasef-plaide-pour-une-reforme-denvergure-nationale/>). [SIFCOM was in preproduction in 55 communes at 30 June 2026](<https://procasef.com/comite-de-pilotage-du-procasef-des-resultats-historiques-salues-et-un-ptba-2026-revise-adopte/>). The tax directorate's Suigil 2 [added over 38,000 parcels to the tax base in pilot zones](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/la-taxe-fonciere-un-levier-strategique-pour-le-senegal/>). Its Cadastre director says no full cadastre covers the country.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the Cadastre director's statement that no full cadastre covers the country](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/la-taxe-fonciere-un-levier-strategique-pour-le-senegal/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. In April 2026 [a platform to track communal land files from request to title was being deployed](<https://aps.sn/vers-la-digitalisation-de-toutes-les-procedures-foncieres-dans-les-communes-responsable/>), with agents trained in Thiès.
