# Tunisia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The land registry office ONPF says consulting titles, certified copies and ownership searches can already be done remotely](<https://news.gnet.tn/vers-une-administration-sans-papier-la-propriete-fonciere-tunisienne-passe-a-la-vitesse-numerique/>). [A land conservator said the digital land register is in service](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-vers-l-adoption-de-la-signature-et-du-contrat-electroniques-dans-le-registre-foncier/222152/>). The cadastre office [signed a USD 50m contract with a Samsung-led consortium in May 2025](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/05/26/546769/tunisie-lancement-du-projet-de-systeme-dinformations-foncieres-avec-un-consortium-sud-coreen-mene-par-samsung-ct/>) for a land database and cadastral system. [The state domains ministry has digitised over 10,000 state properties](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/biens-publics-4000-proprietes-detrangers-regularisees-et-une-carte-fonciere-numerique-en-preparation/387548/>). In November 2026 its minister said [a cadastral geoportal is to be finished within two years](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/07/terres-domaniales-11-000-hectares-mis-a-la-disposition-des-societes-communautaires/>).

**Nationwide.** [The Minister says the ONPF's IT system has been generalised to all its regional administrations](<http://www.domainetat.tn/?p=14628>).

**Online services.** [The minister says ONPF services are delivered online through cpf.gov.tn](<http://www.domainetat.tn/?p=14628>), including certificates of ownership and registration. [Users consult land titles remotely](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-vers-l-adoption-de-la-signature-et-du-contrat-electroniques-dans-le-registre-foncier/222152/>). In May 2025 ONPF said [a second phase aims at a paperless office within three years](<https://news.gnet.tn/vers-une-administration-sans-papier-la-propriete-fonciere-tunisienne-passe-a-la-vitesse-numerique/>).
