# Tunisia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Minister of State Domains and Land Affairs said the ministry has completed digitisation of over 10,000 state properties into a unified database, with the Office of Topography and Cadastre and the National Remote Sensing Centre](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/biens-publics-4000-proprietes-detrangers-regularisees-et-une-carte-fonciere-numerique-en-preparation/387548/>). [The OTC signed a USD 50m, three-year contract with the Samsung C&T Consortium for the Tunisian land information system, to digitise all OTC archives, build a land database and an integrated cadastral information system, with infrastructure in the OTC's 24 arrondissements, per OTC head Ahmed El Kamel](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/05/26/546769/tunisie-lancement-du-projet-de-systeme-dinformations-foncieres-avec-un-consortium-sud-coreen-mene-par-samsung-ct/>). [Land conservator Wadii Rhouma said the digital land register is already operational](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-vers-l-adoption-de-la-signature-et-du-contrat-electroniques-dans-le-registre-foncier/222152/>).

**Nationwide.** [The Minister says the ONPF's IT system has been generalised to all its regional administrations](<http://www.domainetat.tn/?p=14628>).

**Online services.** [Land conservator Wadii Rhouma said the operational digital land register lets users consult land titles remotely](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/tunisie-vers-l-adoption-de-la-signature-et-du-contrat-electroniques-dans-le-registre-foncier/222152/>). [The Minister says ONPF publicity services are now delivered online, including certificates of ownership, co-ownership, non-ownership and registration, via the updated cpf.gov.tn site](<http://www.domainetat.tn/?p=14628>).
