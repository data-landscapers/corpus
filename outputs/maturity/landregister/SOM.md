# Somalia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The World Bank says that [the municipal land registries of Baidoa and Garowe registered each plot with GIS](<https://worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/05/21/371-000-households-in-somalia-find-respite-from-drought-afe>) for resettled families, who received title deeds. [The Nagaad project resettled 1,150 families in Baidoa and 1,000 in Garowe](<https://worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/05/21/371-000-households-in-somalia-find-respite-from-drought-afe>). It is run by the Ministry of Public Works with the municipalities of Garowe, Baidoa and Mogadishu.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the registries of Baidoa and Garowe](<https://worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/05/21/371-000-households-in-somalia-find-respite-from-drought-afe>).
