# Cote d'Ivoire

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The finance ministry's Livre Foncier Electronique lets notaries consult land titles](<https://finances.gouv.ci/gouvernance-et-reformes/fiscalite-interieure>). In February 2025 LoGRI said [the construction ministry's SIGFU is digitising all existing paper maps](<https://logri.org/2025/02/expanding-the-property-tax-base-innovations-in-property-identification-and-challenges-in-cote-divoire/>) to modernise titling. In October 2026 the director of the Cadastre said [the tax administration's e-Cadastre platform is dematerialising cadastre procedures step by step](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/10/09/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-journees-foncieres-le-cadastre-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-reforme-fiscale_201459.html>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the e-Cadastre platform, which was dematerialising cadastre procedures step by step in October 2026](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/10/09/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-journees-foncieres-le-cadastre-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-reforme-fiscale_201459.html>).

**Online services.** [Notaries consult land titles online through the Livre Foncier Electronique](<https://finances.gouv.ci/gouvernance-et-reformes/fiscalite-interieure>), and the finance ministry plans to open it to banks. [LoGRI says applications for land titles can be made online through SIGFU](<https://logri.org/2025/02/expanding-the-property-tax-base-innovations-in-property-identification-and-challenges-in-cote-divoire/>).
