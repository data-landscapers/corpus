# Burkina Faso

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [ANSSI's director general says FasoSign is operational on SYC@D, the system managing the cadastre, state domain and land, for issuing signed provisional cession orders](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>). [Sidwaya reports the Direction générale des impôts officially launched SYC@D, a cloud platform integrating cadastre, domain and land processes, on 21 March 2025 in Ouagadougou, accessible at sif.bf](<https://www.sidwaya.info/foncier-au-burkina-sycd-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-des-terres/>). [The LuxDev project sheet BKF/027 (Nov 2021 to Apr 2024, EUR 1.8m, at the Direction générale des impôts) says 13,293 land titles and 89,131 survey files were digitised in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, towards a multipurpose cadastre](<https://luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2025-04/Fiche_BKF027.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Online services.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says users can apply for plot allocation, obtain a cadastral extract and receive provisional and definitive transfer orders online through SYC@D](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/forum/detail-actualites>). [The DGI says SyC@D offers online request and issue of the parcel attribution certificate, provisional and definitive cession decrees and the cadastral extract](<https://dgi.bf/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-fonciere-la-plateforme-sycd-en-marche/>).
