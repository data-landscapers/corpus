# Burkina Faso

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The LuxDev project sheet BKF/027 (Nov 2021 to Apr 2024, EUR 1.8m, at the Direction générale des impôts) says 13,293 land titles and 89,131 survey files were digitised in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso, towards a multipurpose cadastre](<https://luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2025-04/Fiche_BKF027.pdf>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says it launched SYC@D, the integrated cadastre and domain management system, on 21 March 2025 at sif.bf to process and issue land-occupation titles](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/forum/detail-actualites>). [The Direction Générale des Impôts says the Prime Minister launched SyC@D, the integrated cadastre and domain management system, on 21 March 2025 at sif.bf](<https://dgi.bf/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-fonciere-la-plateforme-sycd-en-marche/>). [In September 2026 the director general of ANSSI said the FasoSign signature service is running on SYC@D, the system that manages the cadastre, state land and land titles, to issue signed provisional cession orders](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Nationwide.** No source held since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [Sidwaya reports the Prime Minister said SYC@D lets citizens and investors carry out land procedures online and track their files in real time, with cadastral extract requests handled in one day and definitive transfer orders (land titles) in 60 days at most](<https://www.sidwaya.info/foncier-au-burkina-sycd-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-des-terres/>).
