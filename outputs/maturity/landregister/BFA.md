# Burkina Faso

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The tax directorate [launched SYC@D, a cloud platform for cadastre, state domain and land, on 21 March 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/foncier-au-burkina-sycd-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-des-terres/>). [A one-month pilot at Ouagadougou land offices had begun in January](<https://www.horonyafinance.com/burkina-dematerialisation-des-actes-administratifs-la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-lapplication-sycd-pour-une-phase-pilote-dun-mois/>). ANSSI says [signed provisional cession orders are now issued on it](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>). In May 2025 a project coordinator said [a national land information system was being deployed, with a pilot of systematic rights registration](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article138383=>). [A LuxDev project digitised 13,293 land titles and 89,131 survey files in Ouagadougou and Bobo-Dioulasso by April 2024](<https://luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2025-04/Fiche_BKF027.pdf>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 SYC@D managed the cadastre, state domain and land, with FasoSign signing provisional cession orders](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Online services.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says users can apply for plot allocation, obtain a cadastral extract and receive provisional and definitive transfer orders online through SYC@D](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/forum/detail-actualites>). [The DGI says SyC@D offers online request and issue of the parcel attribution certificate, provisional and definitive cession decrees and the cadastral extract](<https://dgi.bf/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-fonciere-la-plateforme-sycd-en-marche/>).
