# Malawi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Malawi launched LIMS in February 2024](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). The web system links Lands, Surveys and Physical Planning, and registered users can view land parcels. [The Ministry of Lands is moving the remaining manual land transactions onto it](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). [The rollout contract was signed on 10 May 2021 under the World Bank's AGCOM project](<https://lands.gov.mw/index.php/our-projects/lims>), for 20 months. In October 2026 the Lands Minister said [hackers had breached the system, which holds title deeds](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/>), about two weeks earlier.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).

**Online services.** [Registered LIMS users can submit documents, receive notifications and make payments, including ground rent and application fees](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).
