# Malawi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Malawi launched LIMS in February 2024](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). The web system links Lands, Surveys and Physical Planning, and registered users can view land parcels. [The Ministry of Lands is moving the remaining manual land transactions onto it](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). In June 2025 [the ministry launched LIMS in Blantyre to replace paper records of land transactions](<https://www.247malawi.com/malawi-takes-a-leap-forward-in-land-management-with-digital-platform/>). In August 2026 the minister said [a land audit matched paper files against the records in LIMS](<https://malawicables.com/speech-by-minister-of-lands-chimwemwe-chipungu-on-the-land-ownership-audit/>). In October 2026 he said [hackers had breached the system, which holds title deeds](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).

**Online services.** [Registered LIMS users can submit documents, receive notifications and make payments, including ground rent and application fees](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).
