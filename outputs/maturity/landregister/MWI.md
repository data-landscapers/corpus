# Malawi

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Malawi launched LIMS in February 2024, a web-based system linking Lands, Surveys and Physical Planning where registered users can view land parcels and assets](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). [The Ministry of Lands is reviewing LIMS workflows and developing digital procedures to move the remaining manual land transactions onto the platform](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). [The Ministry of Lands, with the World Bank's AGCOM project, is implementing the Land Information Management System, with a contract signed 10 May 2021 for a 20-month rollout including data capture and go-live phases](<https://lands.gov.mw/index.php/our-projects/lims>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).

**Online services.** [Registered LIMS users can submit documents, receive notifications and make payments, including ground rent and application fees](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).
