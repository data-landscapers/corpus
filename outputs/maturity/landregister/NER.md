# Niger

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The urbanism minister said a single cadastre is being built through the E-Cadastre project with ANSI](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-et-de-lhabitat-le-colonel-major-abdoulkadri-amadou-dresse-le-bilan-des-reformes-et-des-chantiers-en-cours/>), financed by the World Bank. [ANSI says the national E-Cadastre is being deployed step by step](<https://ansi.ne/?p=1143>). In March 2026 it [sought equipment for the national data centre to host the platform](<https://www.nigertenders.com/tender/acquisition-equipment-which-will-be-installed-national-datacenter-host-e-cadastre-platform-83605d0.php>). In 2026 it also [sought a firm to collect cadastral data on a 15,000-plot pilot site at Bangoula](<https://www.nigermarches.com/appel_d_offre/recrutement-dun-cabinet-consortium-charge-de-la-collecte-des-donnees-cadastrales-et-urbanistiques-numeriques-georeferencees-sur-le-site-pilote-des-15-000-parcelles-de-bangoula/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the 15,000-plot pilot at Bangoula](<https://www.nigermarches.com/appel_d_offre/recrutement-dun-cabinet-consortium-charge-de-la-collecte-des-donnees-cadastrales-et-urbanistiques-numeriques-georeferencees-sur-le-site-pilote-des-15-000-parcelles-de-bangoula/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the national data centre that is to host the platform](<https://www.nigertenders.com/tender/acquisition-equipment-which-will-be-installed-national-datacenter-host-e-cadastre-platform-83605d0.php>).
