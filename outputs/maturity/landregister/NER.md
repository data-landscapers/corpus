# Niger

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The minister of urbanism and housing said a single cadastre is being built through the E-Cadastre project developed with ANSI and financed by the World Bank PVI, with cadastral digitalisation the priority](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-ministre-de-lurbanisme-et-de-lhabitat-le-colonel-major-abdoulkadri-amadou-dresse-le-bilan-des-reformes-et-des-chantiers-en-cours/>). [ANSI says Niger is engaged in the progressive deployment of a national E-Cadastre, steered by the newly installed CONPEC, with ANSI building the NIPU parcel-identification platform as its infrastructure](<https://ansi.ne/?p=1143>). [The Minister chaired a meeting on the E-Cadastre project, run with ANSI, which aims to set up a national digital cadastral system; a steering committee is to be created](<https://nouvelledafrique.tg/e-cadastre-le-niger-digitalise-son-foncier-pour-plus-de-transparence-et-defficacite/>).
