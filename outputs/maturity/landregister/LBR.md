# Liberia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Liberia Land Authority and Liberia Revenue Authority are using a Real Property Valuation Software and Mobile App for GPS mapping and coding of properties in Nimba County, with over 12,000 properties mapped and data feeding a national cadastre](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-land-authority-launches-real-property-valuation-training-in-nimba-ushering-in-digital-era-for-land-governance/>). [The Liberia Land Authority signed a three-year MOU with ALLFORLAND and LTMETRIC to develop the Liberia Land Information System, with a pilot in Montserrado and Margibi Counties expected within a few months](<https://lla.gov.lr/index.php/publications/news-article/item/65-liberia-land-authority-and-south-korean-partners-signed-landmark-mou-to-develop-liberia-land-information-system>).
