# Liberia

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The Liberia Land Authority signed a three-year MOU in October 2025 to develop the Liberia Land Information System](<https://lla.gov.lr/index.php/publications/news-article/item/65-liberia-land-authority-and-south-korean-partners-signed-landmark-mou-to-develop-liberia-land-information-system>), with a pilot planned in Montserrado and Margibi. [Over 12,000 properties in Nimba County were mapped for a national cadastre](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-land-authority-launches-real-property-valuation-training-in-nimba-ushering-in-digital-era-for-land-governance/>) by early 2026. A Swedish-funded project [digitised the Monrovia Atlas and 232 archived maps](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/swedish-funded-land-reform-project-strengthens-liberia-land-governance/>) before closing in June 2026. It also built an online tool for the administration of customary land.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [by February 2026 over 12,000 properties had been mapped in Nimba County for a national cadastre](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-land-authority-launches-real-property-valuation-training-in-nimba-ushering-in-digital-era-for-land-governance/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest fact: [in February 2026 the land and revenue authorities mapped properties with valuation software and a mobile app](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-land-authority-launches-real-property-valuation-training-in-nimba-ushering-in-digital-era-for-land-governance/>).
