# Gabon

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** Gabon's cadastre and digital economy ministers met to speed up [the digitalisation of the cadastre and land procedures](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-foncier-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-du-cadastre/>). [Work begun on 5 May 2026 under the Gabon Digital programme aims to modernise the cadastre](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitalisation-le-gouvernement-echange-avec-le-snedai/>). In September 2026 the government [signed a memorandum with SNEDAI for a land registry pilot in Estuaire, Greater Libreville included](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/gabon-steps-up-land-registry-overhaul-with-greater-libreville-pilot>), with specifications still to be set. [ANUTTC has spent nearly six months scanning all freehold-transfer land files](<https://www.union.sonapresse.com/fr/anuttc-lancement-de-la-numerisation-de-tous-les-dossiers-fonciers>). The government's Gabon Connect calendar [expects ePropriété, the land-book service, in September 2027](<https://gabonmediatime.com/digitalisation-le-gouvernement-annonce-le-deploiement-progressif-de-gabon-connect/>).

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 the housing and digital economy ministers reviewed deployment of the 'Ogooué' cadastre application](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-foncier-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-du-cadastre/>).
