# Gabon

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [Gabon's Housing, Habitat, Urbanism and Cadastre minister and the Digital Economy minister met on accelerating the digitalisation of the cadastre and land procedures, reviewing advances including deployment of the 'Ogooué' application and online case-tracking, per the Agence Gabonaise de Presse](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-foncier-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-du-cadastre/>). [The government says work begun on 5 May 2026 under the Gabon Digital programme aims to modernise the cadastre and structure land data, and a 14 September 2026 session with SNEDAI continued it on digitalising land tenure](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitalisation-le-gouvernement-echange-avec-le-snedai/>). [ANUTTC has for nearly six months been scanning all freehold-transfer land files, with its IT directorate building applications alongside the Gargantua software](<https://www.union.sonapresse.com/fr/anuttc-lancement-de-la-numerisation-de-tous-les-dossiers-fonciers>).
