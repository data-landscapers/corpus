# Gabon

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** Gabon's cadastre minister and digital economy minister met to speed up [the digitalisation of the cadastre and land procedures](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-foncier-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-du-cadastre/>). They reviewed the 'Ogooué' application and online case-tracking, the Agence Gabonaise de Presse says. [Work begun on 5 May 2026 under the Gabon Digital programme aims to modernise the cadastre](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digitalisation-le-gouvernement-echange-avec-le-snedai/>), and a session with SNEDAI on 14 September 2026 continued it. [ANUTTC has spent nearly six months scanning all freehold-transfer land files](<https://www.union.sonapresse.com/fr/anuttc-lancement-de-la-numerisation-de-tous-les-dossiers-fonciers>). The government's Gabon Connect calendar [expects ePropriété, the land-book service, in September 2027](<https://gabonmediatime.com/digitalisation-le-gouvernement-annonce-le-deploiement-progressif-de-gabon-connect/>), with full rollout in December 2027.
