# Congo

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** The state-domain directorate said in November 2025 that [field patrols starting in Brazzaville will feed a national digital register of state land](<https://congolive11.com/ngouedy-makita-transparence-fonciere-en-marche/>). Each parcel and building is to be geolocated, photographed and given a unique number. [The patrols are to extend step by step to the twelve departments](<https://congolive11.com/ngouedy-makita-transparence-fonciere-en-marche/>). The directorate expects close to 35,000 titles to be issued or updated by the end of 2026.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest held fact is [the state-land register begun in Brazzaville](<https://congolive11.com/ngouedy-makita-transparence-fonciere-en-marche/>).

**Online services.** No source found since October 2023 says land can be searched or registered online. The nearest held fact is [the same register](<https://congolive11.com/ngouedy-makita-transparence-fonciere-en-marche/>).
