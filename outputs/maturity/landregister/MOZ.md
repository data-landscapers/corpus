# Mozambique

## Land register

**Digital register in service.** [The World Bank found SIGIT, the land information system, operational when its project closed in 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041025095533783/txt/P164551-e911de3f-ad72-4e08-b0e6-7bbb6c628183.txt>), with 1,283,171 land use rights recorded. Cities keep their own records too. [Beira scanned and registered 40,000 plots into a new database](<https://partnersforwater.nl/news/beiras-land-registry-the-key-to-the-system-change-that-will-support-a-climate-resilient-city/>), and [Nampula registered 44,706 properties between February and May 2025](<https://ikweli.co.mz/2025/05/13/14621/>). An MCC evaluation found that [in 2023 many land offices still relied on paper](<https://www.mcc.gov/resources/doc/evalbrief-052824-moz-land-tenure-regularization/>) because the system was down or only a backup.

**Nationwide.** No source found since October 2023 says the digital land register covers the whole country. The nearest fact: [in March 2024 the World Bank said 1,283,171 land use rights (DUATs) were recorded in SIGIT](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041025095533783/txt/P164551-e911de3f-ad72-4e08-b0e6-7bbb6c628183.txt>).

**Online services.** [The World Bank IEG review says SIGIT included a web portal that enabled public access to the national cadastre land data](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041025095533783/txt/P164551-e911de3f-ad72-4e08-b0e6-7bbb6c628183.txt>).
