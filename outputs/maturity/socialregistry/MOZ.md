# Mozambique

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank PID says Additional Financing 1 supports the legal and technical establishment of the National Social Registry, built by consolidating the current database through data cleaning and collection and registering new beneficiaries](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>).
