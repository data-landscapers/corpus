# Mozambique

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In May 2026 a World Bank project document said Additional Financing 1 supports the legal and technical establishment of the National Social Registry](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). [The registry is to be built by consolidating the current database through data cleaning and collection](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). [New beneficiaries are to be registered in it](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>).
