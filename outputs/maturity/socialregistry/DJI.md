# Djibouti

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [President Guelleh said a mapping of poverty and precariousness was established in particular thanks to the social registry (ADDS report)](<https://www.adds.dj/en/president-guelleh-reiterates-his-commitment-to-dedicating-a-central-component-of-his-policy-to-the-fight-against-poverty/>). He said State responses were tailored using this methodology. [On the Ministry of Social Affairs and Solidarity page, President Guelleh said that for the first time such a mapping was established notably thanks to the registre social](<https://sociales.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/452>). He said it enabled targeted public policies. [The World Bank project record says Djibouti's national Social Registry is housed at the Ministry of Social Affairs and Solidarities](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>). It had 515,730 people registered as of February 2025, with a target of 600,000.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest fact: [in January 2026 President Guelleh said State responses were tailored using a poverty mapping built from the social registry](<https://www.adds.dj/en/president-guelleh-reiterates-his-commitment-to-dedicating-a-central-component-of-his-policy-to-the-fight-against-poverty/>).
