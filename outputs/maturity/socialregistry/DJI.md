# Djibouti

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [President Guelleh said a mapping of poverty and precariousness was established in particular thanks to the social registry, and that State responses were tailored using this methodology (ADDS report)](<https://www.adds.dj/en/president-guelleh-reiterates-his-commitment-to-dedicating-a-central-component-of-his-policy-to-the-fight-against-poverty/>). [President Guelleh said that for the first time a mapping of poverty and precariousness was established notably thanks to the registre social, enabling targeted public policies (Ministry of Social Affairs and Solidarity page)](<https://sociales.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/452>). [The World Bank project record says Djibouti's national Social Registry, housed at the Ministry of Social Affairs and Solidarities, had 515,730 people registered as of February 2025, with a target of 600,000](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>).
