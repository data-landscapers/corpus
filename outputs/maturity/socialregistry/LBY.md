# Libya

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** In October 2023 [UNICEF Libya announced that the Tripoli-based government had launched the Unified Social Registry](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/10/unicef-helps-launch-libyas-unified-social-registry-for-children>), with UN agencies, to target people in need. On 22 June 2025 [Prime Minister Dabaiba approved the draft Unified Social Registry document](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=335044&lang=en>). It sets out a unified database of citizens' social and economic data, classified by income, health and family size, with periodic updates. [LANA described it on 23 June 2025 as a project](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?lang=en&id=335111>) to identify those eligible for support.

**Several programmes.** No source found since November 2023 names a programme that selects its recipients from the registry. [The approved document says the registry is meant to identify those eligible for support](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?lang=en&id=335111>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the registry holds national ID numbers or exchanges records with the identity system. [The June 2025 document describes a unified database of citizens' information](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=335044&lang=en>).
