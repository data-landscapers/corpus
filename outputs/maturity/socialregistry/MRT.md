# Mauritania

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** Taazour runs the social registry. Its coordinator says [352,000 households are registered in it](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261586>). Taazour says [2,057,006 citizens in the registry can check their eligibility on its platform](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>). A decree of October 2025 [makes the registry a source for the disaster-household database BDS-MAC](<https://www.informea.org/en/content/legislation/decret-ndeg2025-162-du-15-octobre-2025-portant-creation-dune-base-de-donnees>). Nouakchott's wilaya authorities are [to use its platform to process claims](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/294304>).

**Several programmes.** Decree 2024-073 of 15 May 2024 sets the rules. [Poor households in the registry are eligible for Tekavoul regular transfers](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229659.pdf>). Vulnerable households in it are eligible for shock-response transfers. The two rules are articles 11 and 13 of the decree.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID. Taazour's own platform [lets citizens check their eligibility](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>).

***Also on file***. [BDS-MAC](<https://www.informea.org/en/content/legislation/decret-ndeg2025-162-du-15-octobre-2025-portant-creation-dune-base-de-donnees>); [TEKAVOUL](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau229659.pdf>); [Registre biométrique national (ANRPTS)](<https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105>).
