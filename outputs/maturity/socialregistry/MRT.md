# Mauritania

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Taazour says the AOUN operation targets 2,057,006 citizens registered in the social registry database nationwide, who verify eligibility on its platform](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>). [The national social registry coordinator at Taazour says the AOUN operation covers all 352,000 households registered in the social registry database](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261586>). [AMI reports Taazour announced that the Nouakchott wilaya authorities will access the social registry platform to receive and process claims, during ongoing registry updating](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/294304>). [Decree 2025-162 names the Registre Social de Taazour as a source of the disaster-household database BDS-MAC and as the place where affected households' data get socio-economic analysis](<https://www.informea.org/en/content/legislation/decret-ndeg2025-162-du-15-octobre-2025-portant-creation-dune-base-de-donnees>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>).
