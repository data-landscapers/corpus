# Zambia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank project paper says a Cabinet Memorandum for developing and implementing the Zambia Social Registry was approved in April 2025, a concept note was endorsed by the Government in September 2025, and design of the registry architecture is ongoing, due by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). [The Minister of Finance told the National Assembly the Government is developing a national social registry to streamline social protection and human capital development and to help avoid double-dipping by beneficiaries](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12586>).
