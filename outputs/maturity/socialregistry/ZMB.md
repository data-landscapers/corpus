# Zambia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [A World Bank paper says Cabinet approved a memorandum to develop the Zambia Social Registry in April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). [The government endorsed a concept note in September 2025, and design of the registry was due by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). [The Minister of Finance told the National Assembly the Government is developing a national social registry to streamline social protection and human capital development and to help avoid double-dipping by beneficiaries](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12586>).
