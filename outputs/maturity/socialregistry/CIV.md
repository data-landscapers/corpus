# Cote d'Ivoire

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Registre Social Unique was set up in 2019. The Bureau de Coordination des Programmes Filets Sociaux says it [holds more than 1.45 million households and 8.8 million people](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/elaboration-et-validation-des-indicateurs-du-registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu/>). In April 2025 the same office said [more than 200,000 households were being registered](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/operationnalisation-du-registre-social-unique-rsu-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-plus-de-200-000-menages-en-cours-denregistrement>), through the SIG-RSU system.

**Several programmes.** No source found names the programmes that select from the registry, or says how many there are. The office that runs it [coordinates the social safety nets programme](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).

**Linked to ID.** On 2 April 2026 the office and the identity agency ONECI [signed a convention interconnecting the registry with ONECI's databases](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). Beneficiaries are to be identified through the national register of persons. The aim is to remove duplicates, so that no household is counted twice.

***Also on file***. [ONECI](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/atelier-sur-linteroperabilite-des-systemes-dinformation-pour-renforcer-la-protection-sociale-en-cote-divoire/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 names the programmes that select from the registry. None says benefits are paid digitally.
