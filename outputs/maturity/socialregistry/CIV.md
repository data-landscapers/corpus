# Cote d'Ivoire

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Bureau de Coordination des Programmes Filets Sociaux says the RSU, set up in 2019, has more than 1.45 million households and 8.8 million individuals registered](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/elaboration-et-validation-des-indicateurs-du-registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu/>). [BCP Filets Sociaux says more than 200,000 households were in the process of registration in the RSU, managed through the SIG-RSU system, as of April 2025](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/operationnalisation-du-registre-social-unique-rsu-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-plus-de-200-000-menages-en-cours-denregistrement>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026 interconnecting the Registre Social Unique with ONECI's databases, with beneficiaries identified through the national register of persons to remove duplicates](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).
