# Equatorial Guinea

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Ley 5/2026, promulgated in April 2026, creates a Registro Único Social within the Instituto de Protección Social](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg248009.pdf>), to hold data on people who receive or could receive non-contributory benefits. [The law takes effect six months after publication](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg248009.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says any programme uses the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the 2026 law gives the registry the data on beneficiaries of non-contributory benefits](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg248009.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [under the 2026 law other public and private bodies must supply the registry data on request](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/eqg248009.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the social registry has been built, has enrolled households or is in service.
