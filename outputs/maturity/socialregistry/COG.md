# Congo

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Registre Social Unique has been in place since 2015. [Its headquarters opened on 10 April 2026](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>), the social affairs ministry says. The World Bank counted [947,013 households in it at 30 November 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122225174011681/pdf/P174178-d3f57ca8-c3c8-481f-b065-df5bd1e79798.pdf>). It is [the central tool for targeting vulnerable people after a crisis](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>), with households ranked by a proxy means test.

**Several programmes.** In April 2026 the minister said [the assistance programmes now share it as a common source of information](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>). He named universal health insurance, the social safety nets and insertion projects. In May 2025 the national safety net programme [selected the recipients of its conditional cash transfers from the registry](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/affaires-sociales-des-menages-vulnerables-recoivent-des-transferts-monetaires-164790>), over 10,000 households in Brazzaville.

**Linked to ID.** An arrêté of 20 August 2024 governs the registry. Its article 8 says [the registry holds the unique identification number](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>), with data from the civil-status systems. The same arrêté says [it generates a code for each household and extracts lists of potential beneficiaries](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>) for programmes.

***Also on file***. [Registre général des bénéficiaires](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a programme drawing on the registry pays benefits digitally.
