# Congo

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Congo ministry of social affairs reports the Registre Social Unique headquarters was inaugurated on 10 April 2026 and, in the minister's words, programmes now have a common source of information through it](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>). [Les Dépêches de Brazzaville reports the Registre social unique, in place since 2015 under the safety nets, is used as the central tool for targeting vulnerable people after a crisis, with households categorised by Proxy Means Test](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>). [Les Depeches de Brazzaville reports that the Registre social unique is a national digital database of vulnerable households, being updated by the Talangai pilot that began 15 January 2026](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/talangai-des-documents-didentite-remis-des-menages-vulnerables-168666>).

**Several programmes.** [The minister says that thanks to the RSU the assistance programmes, universal health insurance, social safety nets and insertion projects, now have a common source of information](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>).

**Linked to ID.** [Article 8 of the arrete says the registre social unique holds data from the civil-status information systems and the unique identification number, among other sources](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>).
