# Gabon

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Ministry of Social Affairs presented the first deliverable of the unified social registry (RSUG) on 29 July 2026, with the stated aim of setting up the registry](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-protection-sociale-presentation-du-premier-livrable-du-registre-social-unifie-et-du-programme-dacces-aux-agr/>). [The Minister of Social Affairs officially received the strategic deliverables of the Registre social unique du Gabon on 29 July 2026, under the Gabon Digital programme, marking the start of setting up the registry](<https://www.union.sonapresse.com/index.php/fr/protection-sociale-le-gabon-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape>). [The ministry and World Bank experts reviewed progress on implementing the Registre social unique, a centralised national database meant to identify the most vulnerable households, at a 7 May 2026 meeting](<https://financesao.com/gabon-le-registre-social-unique-avance-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-mondiale/>).
