# DR Congo

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Decree n°25/041 of 4 December 2025 creates the Registre Social Unique (RSU), a socio-economic database to harmonise targeting of poor and vulnerable households](<https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d>). It centralises beneficiaries' biographic, demographic and biometric data. A steering committee and a technical management unit manage it. [MINAS says the Registre Social Unique, created by Decree 25/041 of 4 December 2025, has a functional management information system (MIS-DRC)](<https://minas.gouv.cd/registre-social-unique-rsu-un-levier-essentiel-pour-la-protection-sociale-en-rdc/>). It registered about 465,883 households in 2020-2025, 267,015 of them in 2025.

**Several programmes.** [MINAS says the RSU provides lists of eligible households to the different social programmes, including State ones](<https://minas.gouv.cd/registre-social-unique-rsu-un-levier-essentiel-pour-la-protection-sociale-en-rdc/>). It does so under data-sharing protocols.

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID.
