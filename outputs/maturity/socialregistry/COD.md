# DR Congo

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Decree n°25/041 of 4 December 2025 creates the Registre Social Unique (RSU), a socio-economic database to harmonise targeting of poor and vulnerable households, centralising beneficiaries' biographic, demographic and biometric data, managed by a steering committee and a technical management unit](<https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Several programmes.** No source held since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leganews.pro/articles/019d2bef-1d56-70af-a0b8-79e76597706d>).
