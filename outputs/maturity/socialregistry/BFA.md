# Burkina Faso

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank says the earlier Burkina Naong Sa Ya project helped establish a national social registry of vulnerable households, a platform for expanding and targeting social protection programs, and the new project will support its expanded coverage and use](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [The Registre Social Unique database holds 362,698 households (2,217,518 people) across 99 communes in 10 regions, per the Ministry of Humanitarian Action's representative](<https://www.sidwaya.info/registre-social-unique-le-burkina-faso-renforce-la-protection-sociale/>). [The World Bank SASPP page says the social registry held 344,400 poor households as of June 2025, with roll-out continuing after the safety-net project closed](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

**Several programmes.** No source held since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>).

**Linked to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>).
