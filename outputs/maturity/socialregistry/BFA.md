# Burkina Faso

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Registre Social Unique [held 362,698 households, about 2.2 million people, in 99 communes in December 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/registre-social-unique-le-burkina-faso-renforce-la-protection-sociale/>). By 30 June 2026 the solidarity minister put it at [433,888 households, over 2 million people, in 110 communes across 12 regions](<https://aesinsider.com/burkina-faso-plus-de-433-000-menages-recenses-dans-le-registre-social-unique/>). That is 30% of the target. The World Bank's new project, approved in June 2026, [continues its roll-out](<https://www.banquemondiale.org/fr/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

**Several programmes.** The World Bank describes the registry as [a platform for expanding and targeting social protection programmes](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). It names no programme that selects its beneficiaries from the registry.

**Linked to ID.** The World Bank's programme page [names a unique identifier system under the WURI project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>), alongside the registry. It does not say the registry is linked to that identifier or checked against it.

***Also on file***. [Unique Identifier System (WURI project)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the registry. None says it is linked to the national ID.
