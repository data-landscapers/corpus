# Cameroon

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank paper says RESUC, established under the Ministry of Social Affairs, had 198,140 households covered by July 2025 and has operational systems providing a foundation for targeting and coordination across programmes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>). [Cameroon Tribune reports that in the Ouest region only 55,097 vulnerable people in eight communes are registered in the Unified Social Registry of Cameroon (RESUC), and the Minister of Social Affairs said a regional session aimed to extend registration to at least ten new communes](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76019/en.html/registre-social-unifie-les-acteurs-de-louest-sensibilises>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).
