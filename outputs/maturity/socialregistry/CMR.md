# Cameroon

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank says RESUC, under the Ministry of Social Affairs, covered 198,140 households by July 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>). [It said RESUC's operational systems give a foundation for targeting and coordination across programmes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>). [Cameroon Tribune reported that in the Ouest region only 55,097 vulnerable people in eight communes were registered in RESUC](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76019/en.html/registre-social-unifie-les-acteurs-de-louest-sensibilises>). [The Minister of Social Affairs said a regional session aimed to extend registration to at least ten new communes](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/76019/en.html/registre-social-unifie-les-acteurs-de-louest-sensibilises>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the World Bank said RESUC gives a foundation for coordination across programmes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest fact: [in August 2025 the World Bank said RESUC was strengthening its MIS toward 300,000 people by 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).
