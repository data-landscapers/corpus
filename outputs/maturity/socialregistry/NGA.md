# Nigeria

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** NASSCO runs the National Social Register. Its programme manager said in May 2026 that it [covers over 20 million households and more than 77 million people](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/77-million-nigerians-now-on-fgs-safety-net-register-nassco/>), in all 36 states and the FCT. NSIPA lists it as [built and operational](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). The World Bank says [over 6 million households were selected from it for shock-response cash transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>).

**Several programmes.** Two transfer programmes draw on the register, the World Bank says in 2025. [Shock-response transfer households are identified from it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>), and the Extended Regular Cash Transfer counts households from it too.

**Linked to ID.** In May 2026 NASSCO said [12.3 million national ID numbers had been captured into the register](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/77-million-nigerians-now-on-fgs-safety-net-register-nassco/>), and over 11.5 million validated. NSIPA says [ID and bank verification numbers are integrated into the register](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [Entries without them are disqualified](<https://punchng.com/cash-transfer-fg-disqualifies-nigerians-without-nin-bvn-cbn-begins-payment/>), the minister's media aide said in 2023.
