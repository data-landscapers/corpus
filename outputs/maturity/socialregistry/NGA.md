# Nigeria

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** NASSCO runs the National Social Register. In October 2026 it said the register [held 21.1 million households and 83.2 million people](<https://punchng.com/fg-links-15-6m-nigerians-to-nin-in-social-register/>), and that 13.3 million households had been updated. NSIPA lists it as [built and operational](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). The World Bank says [over 6 million households were selected from it for shock-response cash transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>). Since November 2026 mobile units [enrol displaced people in the register](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/11/10-states-get-mobile-centres-to-tackle-documentation-crisis-among-3-7m-displaced-nigerians/>) in 10 states.

**Several programmes.** Two transfer programmes draw on the register, the World Bank says in 2025. [Shock-response transfer households are identified from it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>), and the Extended Regular Cash Transfer counts households from it too.

**Linked to ID.** In October 2026 NASSCO said [the national ID numbers of 15.65 million people had been integrated into the register](<https://punchng.com/fg-links-15-6m-nigerians-to-nin-in-social-register/>). NSIPA says [ID and bank verification numbers are integrated into the register](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). In 2025 the World Bank said [the register's cash transfers are paid directly into verified, beneficiary-owned digital accounts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>). In November 2026 the humanitarian ministry said [relief recipients drawn from ID records and the register receive N50,000 into a mobile wallet](<https://punchng.com/how-fg-selects-beneficiaries-for-n50000-relief/>).
