# Nigeria

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [NASSCO's national programme manager said the National Social Register covers over 20 million households and more than 77 million individuals across all 36 states and the FCT](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/77-million-nigerians-now-on-fgs-safety-net-register-nassco/>). [The World Bank report says over 6 million poor and vulnerable households were selected from the Social Register to receive shock responsive cash transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>). [Minister Yilwatda said the federal government expanded the social register from 13 million to 19.7 million people using an AI system to identify urban poor](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/nigeria-amplifies-efforts-to-id-prisoners-expand-social-register>).

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank report says ESR-CT households are identified from the Social Registers (National Social Register and Rapid Response Register), and the Extended Regular Cash Transfer also counts households from it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>).

**Linked to ID.** [NASSCO said 12.3 million NINs have been captured into the National Social Register and over 11.5 million validated, with NIN integration ongoing](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/77-million-nigerians-now-on-fgs-safety-net-register-nassco/>). [The World Bank report says a NIN validation drive for households in the Social Register began April 7 with NIMC, with over 6.7 million households visited and over 9 million NINs being verified](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>).
