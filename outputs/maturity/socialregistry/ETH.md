# Ethiopia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** OpenG2P told ID4Africa in 2025 that [the Ministry of Women and Social Affairs is setting up a National Social Registry](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>). [A pilot had registered 821 people in Addis Ababa](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes choose recipients from a social registry. The nearest fact found: [in 2025 the National Social Registry was at a pilot of 821 people](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** OpenG2P says [it seeded its social registry with Fayda IDs issued by the National ID Program](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a social registry is in service. None says two or more programmes choose recipients from one.
