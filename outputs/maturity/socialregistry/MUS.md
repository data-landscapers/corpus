# Mauritius

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Mauritius Revenue Authority's FAQ of January 2026 says [people on the Social Register of Mauritius keep receiving CSG income allowances at the June 2025 rates](<https://www.mra.mu/download/FAQs-SocialContributionIncomeAllowance.pdf>). People not on it receive lower rates. The register sets who is paid what. [A November 2024 government list of operational systems names the Social Register of Mauritius system](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>), run from the benefits local offices.

**Several programmes.** Two payments are tied to the register. The CSG income allowance rate depends on it, as above. Since the 2025-26 budget, [youths on the Social Register receive the Rs 20,000 Independence Allowance at 18](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/financial-assistance/independence-allowance>). The revenue authority pays it, on an application carrying the youth's identity card number.

**Linked to ID.** Applicants for the Independence Allowance [give the youth's National Identity Card number](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/financial-assistance/independence-allowance>). The authority does not say the register itself is checked against the identity system.
