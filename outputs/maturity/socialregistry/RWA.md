# Rwanda

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** Rwanda launched the Imibereho Dynamic Social Registry [on 29 February 2024, replacing the Ubudehe classification](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>). The World Bank says it [covers 13.8 million people as a national platform](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). A ministry official says [360,000 appeals have been handled since 2024](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/37878/news/rwanda/360000-claimsin-new-social-registryhandled-since-2024>), with 93 per cent of disputed classifications corrected.

**Several programmes.** The World Bank says [Imibereho supports safety nets, economic inclusion, skills and health insurance programmes](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). It [targets households for the Vision Umurenge Programme](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>) and sets payments for community health insurance, says BMZ. Over 11 million people have health insurance, the World Bank adds.

**Linked to ID.** The World Bank says Rwanda [moved to a national digital platform linked to ID and administrative data](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). BMZ says [its data are cross-checked with the National ID Agency](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>).
