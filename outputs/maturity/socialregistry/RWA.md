# Rwanda

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The New Times reports the Imibereho Social Registry System has classified households since the digital registry was introduced in early 2024, with 360,000 appeals handled and 93 per cent of disputed classifications corrected, per a Ministry of Local Government official](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/37878/news/rwanda/360000-claimsin-new-social-registryhandled-since-2024>). [The World Bank states Rwanda's Imibereho Social Registry now covers 13.8 million people as a national platform](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). [BMZ reports the Rwandan government launched the Imibereho Dynamic Social Registry System on 29 February 2024, replacing the Ubudehe classification](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>).

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank states the Imibereho registry supports safety nets, economic inclusion, skills and health insurance programmes, with over 11 million people covered by health insurance](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). [BMZ reports Imibereho targets households for the Vision Umurenge Programme and determines payments for Community-based Health Insurance](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The World Bank states Rwanda moved to a national digital platform linked to ID and administrative data](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/11/19/social-registries-digital-platforms-inclusive-service-delivery>). [BMZ reports data in Imibereho is validated through cross-checks with other government databases including the National ID Agency (NIDA)](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/rwandan-government-launches-imibereho-dynamic-social-registry/>).
