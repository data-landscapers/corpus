# Madagascar

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Newsmada reports that Madagascar's Registre social unique already covers about 2.7 million households, close to 12 million people](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/10/protection-sociale-le-rsu-elargit-son-filet/>). [L'Express reports that, per the Ministry of Population and Solidarities, over 2.6 million households are in the Registre social unique, up from 990,000 in February 2023](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/05/recensement-26-millions-de-menages.html>). [Newsmada reports eight more districts are joining the 20 already covered, and a new survey is under way to update the data and extend the registry into urban areas](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/10/protection-sociale-le-rsu-elargit-son-filet/>). [L'Express reports a mass registration campaign in 99 districts until December 2026, aiming at national coverage](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/05/recensement-26-millions-de-menages.html>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/10/protection-sociale-le-rsu-elargit-son-filet/>).
