# Senegal

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In 2026 the director of social safety nets said the Registre national unique held 1,000,649 households after recertification](<https://senego.com/bourses-familiales-le-rnu-compte-1-000-649-menages-apres-la-recertification_2000852.html>). [It was set up in 2015 and is used to identify beneficiaries of the family security grants](<https://senego.com/bourses-familiales-le-rnu-compte-1-000-649-menages-apres-la-recertification_2000852.html>). APS reported a Council of Ministers communique. [It says the update and extension of the Registre National Unique to one million households was completed early in 2026](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>). [It covered 996,194 households against 545,000 before](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>). [ANSD's catalogue says the DGPSN has been extending the RNU database to one million households since 2023](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>). [The March 2025 survey had a pre-identified sample of 1,024,411 households](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>). [The catalogue says decree 2021-1052 of 2 August 2021 institutionalised the Registre national unique](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>). [The 2025 update survey covered all regions in March 2025](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>).

**Several programmes.** [DGPSN says RNU data lowered data-collection costs for one-time interventions (exceptional cash transfers, Covid-19 assistance) beside the PNBSF cash transfer programme it serves](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest fact: [in November 2023 DGPSN said the Registre National Unique covered 541,192 households](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).
