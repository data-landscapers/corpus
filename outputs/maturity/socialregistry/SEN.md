# Senegal

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Council of Ministers communique reported by APS says the update and extension of the Registre National Unique to one million households was completed early in 2026, covering 996,194 households against 545,000 before](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>). [ANSD's catalogue says the DGPSN has been extending the RNU database to one million households since 2023, with a pre-identified sample of 1,024,411 households in the March 2025 survey](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>). [ANSD's catalogue says the Registre national unique was institutionalised by decree 2021-1052 of 2 August 2021, and the 2025 update survey covered all regions in March 2025](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>).

**Several programmes.** [DGPSN says RNU data lowered data-collection costs for one-time interventions (exceptional cash transfers, Covid-19 assistance) beside the PNBSF cash transfer programme it serves](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).
