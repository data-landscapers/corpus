# Senegal

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Council of Ministers communique reported by APS says the update and extension of the Registre National Unique to one million households was completed early in 2026, covering 996,194 households against 545,000 before](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>). [ANSD's catalogue says the Registre national unique was institutionalised by decree 2021-1052 of 2 August 2021, and the 2025 update survey covered all regions in March 2025](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>). [ANSD's catalogue says the DGPSN has been extending the RNU database to one million households since 2023, with a pre-identified sample of 1,024,411 households in the March 2025 survey](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/337>).

**Several programmes.** No source held since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>).
