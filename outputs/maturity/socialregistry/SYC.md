# Seychelles

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In May 2025 a World Bank status report put the social registry indicator at 0%](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>). [The target was 60% of existing social protection beneficiaries by September 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>). [Progress was waiting on the ASP Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>). [The programme was being restructured to put more emphasis on the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>).
