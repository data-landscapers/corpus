# Seychelles

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank's ISR reports the social registry indicator (target 60% of existing social protection beneficiaries by Sep 2025) at 0% on 30-May-2025, pending the ASP Management Information System, with the program being restructured to put more emphasis on the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
