# Chad

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank says 1,094,784 people were in Chad's Unified Social Registry by 22 December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010226092029201/pdf/P164748-cddb7c2b-8bd1-4c60-a944-e149b1b37d6e.pdf>). [Of these, 283,448 were refugees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010226092029201/pdf/P164748-cddb7c2b-8bd1-4c60-a944-e149b1b37d6e.pdf>). [Almost all beneficiaries of its Refugees and Host Communities Support Project were targeted through the registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010226092029201/pdf/P164748-cddb7c2b-8bd1-4c60-a944-e149b1b37d6e.pdf>). [In January 2026 INSEED and WFP briefed authorities of N'Djamena's 9th arrondissement](<https://www.inseed.td/index.php/component/content/article/79-actualites/517-registre-social-unifie-l-inseed-et-le-pam-sensibilisent-les-autorites-du-9eme-arrondissement-de-n-djamena?Itemid=437>). [The briefing preceded a household enrolment operation to extend the Registre Social Unifié](<https://www.inseed.td/index.php/component/content/article/79-actualites/517-registre-social-unifie-l-inseed-et-le-pam-sensibilisent-les-autorites-du-9eme-arrondissement-de-n-djamena?Itemid=437>). [In March 2026 INSEED said 37,449 pastoral and agro-pastoral households in 13 provinces had been enrolled](<https://praps2tchad.org/vers-une-cartographie-exhaustive-de-la-vulnerabilite-pastorale-grace-au-registre-social-unifie/>). [The livestock minister ordered the registry extended to areas not yet covered](<https://praps2tchad.org/vers-une-cartographie-exhaustive-de-la-vulnerabilite-pastorale-grace-au-registre-social-unifie/>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest fact: [in December 2025 the World Bank said one project's beneficiaries were almost all targeted through the registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010226092029201/pdf/P164748-cddb7c2b-8bd1-4c60-a944-e149b1b37d6e.pdf>).
