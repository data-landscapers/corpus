# Ghana

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Ghana National Household Registry is [a single national database of household data and the main platform for targeting social protection](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>). In November 2026 the gender ministry said it [holds about three million households across all 16 regions](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gender-ministry-swears-in-data-review-committee-to-strengthen-household-registry/>).

**Several programmes.** The registry [targets beneficiaries of LEAP, school feeding and national health insurance](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>), and its site counts 15 programmes using its data over 2019-2026. Its July 2026 privacy policy [names the same three programmes](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>). LEAP households [are selected by the registry's proxy-means score](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/eligibility.php>).

**Linked to ID.** The registry's privacy policy of July 2026 says it [collects each person's Ghana Card number and links its data with the identity authority's databases](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>). It also names the health insurance and education service databases.
