# Ghana

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Ghana National Household Registry (GNHR) site describes the GNHR Registry as a single national database capturing household-level data across Ghana and the primary platform for identifying and targeting social protection beneficiaries](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Several programmes.** [The GNHR site says the registry targets beneficiaries of LEAP, school feeding and national health insurance (NHIS), and that its data is used by 15 programmes and 8 organisations over 2019-2026](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>).
