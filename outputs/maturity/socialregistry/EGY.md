# Egypt

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** The Takaful and Karama programme runs the social registry. It [held about 11.3 million households, 40.5 million people, in May 2024](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>), the World Bank says. In November 2026 the Ministry of Social Solidarity's Raafat Shafik said [the national social registry holds data on 13 million households, about half of Egypt's](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). In August 2026 the ministry said [Social Security Law 12 of 2025 made the registry the single source for every cash-transfer beneficiary database](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4343435>).

**Several programmes.** Both components of Takaful and Karama [select beneficiaries from the registry](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). So do the FORSA, Two is Enough, Waii and 1,000 days interventions, on the World Bank's account of May 2024.

**Linked to ID.** Shafik said in November 2026 that [the registry is linked to 27 government databases through the national ID number](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). He said the links are used to verify applicants' data and target support more accurately. In 2025 the World Bank's Stephane Guimbert said [Takaful and Karama pays benefits digitally through Meeza cards](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx>).

***Also on file***. [Unified National Registry (UNR)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says what share of Takaful and Karama payments go to cards. None says whether the other programmes using the registry pay digitally.
