# Zimbabwe

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [ZBC News reports the government is developing the Zimbabwe Social Registry (ZISO), a database to identify households eligible for social assistance, to be rolled out across the 65 districts, per Minister Edgar Moyo](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>). [The Herald reports the 2025/2026 Zimbabwe Social Registry Survey was launched in Nkayi district by ZimStat with the Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare, collecting household data for a national social registry and spreading progressively across 65 districts](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-rolls-out-nationwide-survey-to-strengthen-social-protection-systems/>).
