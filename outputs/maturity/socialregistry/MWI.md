# Malawi

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In March 2025 a World Bank mission said the Unified Beneficiary Registry, also called the Malawi Social Registry, held 4.2 million households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [That was 17 million people across 27 of 28 rural districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [In June 2026 a Ministry of Finance principal economist called the Malawi Social Registry a vital tool for delivering social programmes](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>). [He spoke at a workshop to move it from a static to a dynamic database](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>).

**Several programmes.** [In March 2025 the World Bank mission said five programmes use the registry's data for targeting and research](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [in March 2025 the registry held 4.2 million households, or 17 million people](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).
