# Angola

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [MASFAMU reports the First Lady symbolically activated the Cadastro Social Único platform on 20 July 2026, the registry instituted by Presidential Decree 136/19 of 10 May](<https://masfamu.gov.ao/home>). [Angola's Ministry of Social Action opened the pilot launch of the Cadastro Social Unico in Sumbe, Cuanza-Sul, training 79 participants on the platform, to test the methodology before progressive expansion to other provinces (Angop)](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/ministra-orienta-lancamento-piloto-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). [The pilot phase of Angola's Cadastro Social Unico began in Sumbe municipality, Cuanza-Sul, registering households, with the minister saying the first operational stage will inform progressive national expansion (Angop)](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cadastro-social-nico-inicia-fase-piloto-no-sumbe/>).
