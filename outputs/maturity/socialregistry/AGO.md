# Angola

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** On 20 July 2026 [the First Lady symbolically activated the Cadastro Social Único platform](<https://masfamu.gov.ao/home>), the registry set up by Presidential Decree 136/19. The same week [the Ministry of Social Action launched it as a central database for social support](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/lancamento-do-cadastro-social-unico-marca-a-semana-social/>), [with teams to register households using biometrics](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/angola-lanca-cadastro-social-unico-para-gestao-de-accoes-sociais/>). On 24 August 2026 [its pilot phase began in Sumbe municipality, Cuanza-Sul](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cadastro-social-nico-inicia-fase-piloto-no-sumbe/>). [The ministry trained 79 people to test the method before expansion to other provinces](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/ministra-orienta-lancamento-piloto-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). In June 2024 [the World Bank counted 1,595,496 households registered for cash transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102524042530084/pdf/P169779177dc6e0f518d2610fb44ed3049d.pdf>), while the unified registry was not yet operational.

**Several programmes.** No source found since November 2023 names a programme that selects its recipients from the Cadastro Social Único. [The ministry describes it as a database to manage and target social support](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/lancamento-do-cadastro-social-unico-marca-a-semana-social/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the registry holds national ID numbers or exchanges records with the identity system. [Its enumerators collect biometric and facial data from households](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/angola-lanca-cadastro-social-unico-para-gestao-de-accoes-sociais/>).
