# Somalia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In November 2023 Somalia's Prime Minister unveiled the Social Welfare System of the Unified Social Registry](<https://www.somalidispatch.com/latest-news/somalia-feds-to-launch-social-welfare-system-and-unified-social-registry/>). [He urged aid agencies to use the government's registry to avoid duplication](<https://www.somalidispatch.com/latest-news/somalia-feds-to-launch-social-welfare-system-and-unified-social-registry/>). [A 2025 page on unconditional cash transfers says their beneficiaries are identified through the Unified Social Registry](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [It calls the registry a national database of socio-economic data on vulnerable households](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [The data are used to assess eligibility for social support](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [In October 2025 the World Bank said the BOOST-You project will improve the existing Unified Social Registry](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/10/28/over-738000-somalis-to-benefit-from-world-bank-supported-initiative-for-jobs-social-protection-and-climate-resilience>). [A National Socio-economic Survey covering 12 million people goes with it](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/10/28/over-738000-somalis-to-benefit-from-world-bank-supported-initiative-for-jobs-social-protection-and-climate-resilience>).
