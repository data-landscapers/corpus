# Niger

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The Registre Social Unifié-Niger, run from the Prime Minister's office, is described as an information system holding household and individual data and delivering dashboards on social protection actions, and as the entry point for programmes](<https://rsu.gouv.ne/presentation>). [UNICEF's social policy section chief said more than 500,000 vulnerable households have been registered in Niger's Registre social unifié (RSU) and now receive adapted services, with targeting to be extended to the worst-affected zones](<https://www.lesahel.org/formation-des-cadres-nationaux-du-systeme-de-protection-sociale-du-niger-pour-la-mise-en-place-dun-systeme-durable-coherent-et-fonde-sur-les-droits-humains/>). [The Registre Social Unifié (RSU), although progressing, is not yet deployed across the whole country and suffers update delays](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21295-protection-sociale-au-niger-lancement-dune-feuille-de-route-pour-un-systeme-plus-juste-inclusif-et-resilient.html>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://rsu.gouv.ne/presentation>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://rsu.gouv.ne/presentation>).
