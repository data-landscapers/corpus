# Botswana

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [A World Bank completion report says that by March 2025, 98 percent of beneficiaries of the five main social assistance programmes were in the Single Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>). [Those programmes target poor households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>). [In his State-of-the-Nation Address in November 2025, President Boko said the government is putting the Single Social Registry into operation for beneficiary registration](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89025>). [Proxy means testing goes with it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89025>).
