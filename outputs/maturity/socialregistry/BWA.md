# Botswana

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** In September 2026 the Ministry of Local Government's social protection director gave an update. [The director said the Single Social Registry was 96 per cent complete and had captured 18 social protection programmes](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93447>). In October 2026 a reform official spoke of it. [The official said the government was establishing the registry to identify and target the most vulnerable](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93635>). [In the February 2026 budget speech the finance minister called for its roll-out to be accelerated](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89878>). [In November 2025 President Boko said the government is putting the registry into operation for beneficiary registration](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89025>). A World Bank completion report covers March 2025. [By then 98 percent of beneficiaries of the five main social assistance programmes were in it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>).
