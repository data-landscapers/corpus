# Mali

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [WFP says it is supporting the Commissariat a la Securite Alimentaire in a lessons-learned exercise on the 2025 use of the RSU (Unified Social Registry) for targeting within the national food insecurity response](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172581/download/>). [WFP says it is financing data collection in 14 chronically food-insecure communes to expand the RSU with an additional 3,000 vulnerable households, and that RSU-based targeting is to be scaled up progressively nationwide](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172581/download/>). [The World Bank SASPP page says its FY26 support will go to implementing the RSU (Unified Social Registry) development plan, finalizing the RSU operational manual and digitalizing historical archives](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/mali>).
