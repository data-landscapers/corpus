# Mali

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In April 2024 the World Bank said the RSU covered 1.2 million households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031425101035349/pdf/P500480-8589b096-9644-4127-863d-00895a97c3d8.pdf>). [In 2025 it was used to target the national food insecurity response, WFP says](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172581/download/>). [A 30-day household enrolment campaign ran in communes of Mopti, Bandiagara and Douentza from November 2025](<https://apidev.org/campagne-denrolement-au-registre-social-unifie-rsu/>). [In February 2026 WFP was financing data collection in 14 food-insecure communes to add 3,000 households](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172581/download/>), and RSU-based targeting is to be scaled up nationwide. [The World Bank's FY26 support goes to the RSU development plan, its operational manual and digitised archives](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/mali>).
