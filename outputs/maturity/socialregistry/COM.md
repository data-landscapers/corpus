# Comoros

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In November 2026 the PFSS-RRC said the Registre social unique held 81,075 households](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-et-la-banque-mondiale-renforcent-leur-partenariat-autour-du-projet-mayendeleyo>). [It operated in 152 of the country's 319 localities](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-et-la-banque-mondiale-renforcent-leur-partenariat-autour-du-projet-mayendeleyo>). It called the registry [a targeting instrument for public aid](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-et-la-banque-mondiale-renforcent-leur-partenariat-autour-du-projet-mayendeleyo>). [In July 2026 the World Bank representative had put the households registered at 62,000](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/registre-social-unique-62-000-m%C3%A9nages-d%C3%A9j%C3%A0-enregistr%C3%A9s-.html>). [In September 2026 the Msaanda cash transfer phase was said to rest on putting the registry into operation](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/programme-de-transferts-mon%C3%A9taires-sociaux-msaanda-23-483-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables-cibl%C3%A9s-%C3%A0-ngazidja-.html>). The registry was to identify and target households.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes choose recipients from the social registry. The nearest fact found is from September 2026. [The PFSS-RRC tied its extension of cash transfers to putting the Registre Social Unique into operation](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-%C3%A9largit-la-protection-sociale-%C3%A0-55-000-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID.
