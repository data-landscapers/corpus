## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In July 2026 La Gazette des Comores reported that the Registre social unique was still being set up](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/registre-social-unique-62-000-m%C3%A9nages-d%C3%A9j%C3%A0-enregistr%C3%A9s-.html>). [The World Bank representative put the households registered at 62,000](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/registre-social-unique-62-000-m%C3%A9nages-d%C3%A9j%C3%A0-enregistr%C3%A9s-.html>). [In September 2026 the PFSS-RRC said its extension aims to improve crisis response by putting the Registre Social Unique into operation](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-%C3%A9largit-la-protection-sociale-%C3%A0-55-000-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables>).
