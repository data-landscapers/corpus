# Liberia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The REALISE Project page says the Liberia Household Social Registry, managed by the Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection, holds verified, poverty-ranked records for over 442,000 households (35 percent of the population) and is operational](<https://realise.liberiasp.gov.lr/2026/05/18/government-of-liberia-presents-crisis-response-plan-to-world-bank/>). [The World Bank blog says more than 250,000 households, about 20 percent of the population, are enrolled in the Liberia Household Social Registry, which the Cabinet endorsed in January 2020 as the primary tool for targeting social protection programmes](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/a-strong-foundation-for-adaptive-social-protection-in-liberia>).

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank blog says the LHSR and the MIS are being used by three government-led social protection programmes](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/a-strong-foundation-for-adaptive-social-protection-in-liberia>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/a-strong-foundation-for-adaptive-social-protection-in-liberia>).
