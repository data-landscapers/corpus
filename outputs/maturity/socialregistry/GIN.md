# Guinea

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In July 2026 the director general of the FDSI said teams had covered the whole country](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [The Registre Social Unifié (RSU) then held 977,610 households and 4,860,075 individuals](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [The minister called it the single entry point of national social protection programmes](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>).

**Several programmes.** [In July 2026 the FDSI director general said RSU data had served the Guichet Choc Alimentaire project](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [ANIES and the IMF run that project](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [It had also served UNICEF's follow-up of malnourished children and the family strengthening project of SOS Villages d'Enfants](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>).
