# Sao Tome and Principe

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Sao Tome and Principe's Cadastro Social Unico was set up by Decreto-Lei 16/2018 and regulated by Decreto-Lei 3/2023](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/26/pm-defende-reforco-da-protecao-social-para-apoiar-mais-pessoas-em-situacao-de-vulnerabilidade/>). [The prime minister said a data update expanded it from 8,000 to more than 12,500 households (RSTP)](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/26/pm-defende-reforco-da-protecao-social-para-apoiar-mais-pessoas-em-situacao-de-vulnerabilidade/>). [The Direcao da Protecao Social says about 12,500 households, 25% of the population, are registered in it](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/03/direcao-da-protecao-social-esclarece-sobre-cadastro-social-unico-stp/>). [It is a system built to identify and target support to vulnerable families](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/03/direcao-da-protecao-social-esclarece-sobre-cadastro-social-unico-stp/>). [The DPSSF reports a 2026 recertification and expansion from 8,000 to 12,000 households](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/06/26/53407/resultados-da-2a-recertificacao-e-expansao-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). [It updated 12,560 households and 52,808 individuals in the Cadastro Social Unico](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/06/26/53407/resultados-da-2a-recertificacao-e-expansao-do-cadastro-social-unico/>).

**Several programmes.** [In February 2026 the DPSSF said the Cadastro Social Único is the reference instrument for several programmes](<https://rstp.st/2026/02/17/protecao-social-lanca-inquerito-piloto-para-a-ii-recertificacao-e-atualizacao-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). [Among them are the Programa Família Vulnerável and a self-employment support component](<https://rstp.st/2026/02/17/protecao-social-lanca-inquerito-piloto-para-a-ii-recertificacao-e-atualizacao-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). [In August 2026 the fourth Programa Família Vulnerável payment went to 6,000 families](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/31/dpssf-inicia-4-o-pagamento-a-6-mil-familias-vulneraveis-em-stp/>). [It was the first after the registry's recertification and expansion](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/31/dpssf-inicia-4-o-pagamento-a-6-mil-familias-vulneraveis-em-stp/>).
