# Sao Tome and Principe

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Sao Tome and Principe's Cadastro Social Unico, set up by Decreto-Lei 16/2018 and regulated by Decreto-Lei 3/2023, was expanded by a data update from 8,000 to more than 12,500 households, the prime minister said (RSTP)](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/26/pm-defende-reforco-da-protecao-social-para-apoiar-mais-pessoas-em-situacao-de-vulnerabilidade/>). [The Direcao da Protecao Social says about 12,500 households, 25% of the population, are registered in the Cadastro Social Unico, a system built to identify and target support to vulnerable families](<https://rstp.st/2026/08/03/direcao-da-protecao-social-esclarece-sobre-cadastro-social-unico-stp/>). [The DPSSF reports it updated 12,560 households and 52,808 individuals in the Cadastro Social Unico in a 2026 recertification and expansion from 8,000 to 12,000 households](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/06/26/53407/resultados-da-2a-recertificacao-e-expansao-do-cadastro-social-unico/>).
