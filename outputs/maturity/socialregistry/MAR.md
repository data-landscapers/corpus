# Morocco

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [ANSS says a household needs an RSU score at or under 9.743001, computed on rsu.ma, to receive direct social aid](<https://www.anss.gov.ma/fr/conditions-d-eligibilite>). [The government said in November 2025 that enrolment yields a household score from 35 urban or 28 rural variables, under Law 72.18](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). [It added that household data are updated at least once a year](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). [Le Matin reports that 2026-27 university grants are also awarded on the RSU household score](<https://lematin.ma/enseignement/bac-2026-les-demandes-de-bourse-sur-minhaty-sont-ouvertes/356597>).

**Several programmes.** [The government said in November 2025 that the RSU is the reference for access to social support programmes](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). [It named direct cash transfers and basic health insurance for those unable to pay contributions](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). [The Ministry of Interior said in 2024 that the RSU is used to target social protection programmes](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the register is linked to the national ID. [ANSS's report for 2025 says direct aid is targeted by crossing the RSU with the national population register (RNP)](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>). [It puts 84% of the aid in the poorest three deciles](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>).

***Also on file***. [Registre national de la population (RNP)](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>); [Minhaty](<https://lematin.ma/enseignement/bac-2026-les-demandes-de-bourse-sur-minhaty-sont-ouvertes/356597>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID.
