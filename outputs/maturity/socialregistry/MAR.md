# Morocco

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The author says the Registre social unifié processes social and economic data to calculate an index used in targeting beneficiaries of social programmes](<https://medias24.com/chronique/droits-humains-maroc-defi-espace-numerique/>). [The Head of Government's department says 5.3 million families were enrolled in the Registre Social Unifie as of mid-September 2025, and 1.8 million RSU families received a supplementary school-year aid](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [The World Bank project description says it supports the implementation of the Unified Social Registry (RSU) as a unique gateway for all social safety net programmes, including in rural and remote areas](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>).

**Several programmes.** [The Ministry of Interior document says the RSU is used to improve targeting of social protection programmes (plural) and that the RNP is used by social protection programmes](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>). [The author says the Registre social unifié index is used in targeting beneficiaries of social programmes (plural, unnamed)](<https://medias24.com/chronique/droits-humains-maroc-defi-espace-numerique/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Registre national de la population (RNP)](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID.
