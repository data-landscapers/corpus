# South Africa

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In August 2024 DSD News reported that the Department of Social Development had signed MoUs with government departments](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>). [The MoUs are to develop and implement the National Integrated Social Protection Information System (NISPIS)](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>). [NISPIS is an electronic data management system integrating data from several sectors](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>). [Its purpose is better targeting of social protection](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>).

The nearest held fact: [in August 2024 DSD News said NISPIS would integrate data from several sectors](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>).
