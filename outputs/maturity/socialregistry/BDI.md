# Burundi

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Merankabandi II coordinator Michel Nyabenda said registration into the Registre social unique continues, with 249,671 households already enrolled against a final target of 290,000](<https://lerenouveau.bi/projet-merankabandi-ii-bilan-a-mis-parcours/>). [President Ndayishimiye highlighted progress made in setting up the registre social unique at the opening of the ninth CNPS general assembly](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/06/16/lancement-de-la-9eme-assemblee-generale-de-la-cnps/>). [The World Bank says investments in a Unified Social Registry and CORE-MIS enable transparent targeting and have established the foundations of Burundi's national social protection system, used to identify over 100,000 flood- and food-crisis-affected households](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>).

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank says Merankabandi's Social Registry is also used by the Soleil-Nyakiriza solar energy project to reach vulnerable households, and that the systems are increasingly used across sectors and by other programmes](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>).
