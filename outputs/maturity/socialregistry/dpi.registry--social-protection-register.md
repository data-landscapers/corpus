# Social protection register: social protection register

*As at 2026-11-13.*

## The norm

The African Union's Protocol on Social Protection asks states for a social registry and integrated beneficiary registry, interoperable with civil registration and payments. This study asks the five questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 None | A source from the past three years says no social registry exists and programmes hold their own lists. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Several programmes or linked | Yes to 3, or yes to 4 |
| 5 Linked and paying | Yes to 4 and yes to 5 |

### Zambia: 2 Nascent

A social registry was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.

- The World Bank project paper says a Cabinet Memorandum for developing and implementing the Zambia Social Registry was approved in April 2025, a concept note was endorsed by the Government in September 2025, and design of the registry architecture is ongoing, due by December 2025 ([source, 2025-09](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf)).
- The Minister of Finance told the National Assembly the Government is developing a national social registry to streamline social protection and human capital development and to help avoid double-dipping by beneficiaries ([source, 2025-09-26](https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/12586)).

### Zimbabwe: 2 Nascent

A social registry was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- ZBC News reports the government is developing the Zimbabwe Social Registry (ZISO), a database to identify households eligible for social assistance, to be rolled out across the 65 districts, per Minister Edgar Moyo ([source, 2026-07-20](https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/)).
- The Herald reports the 2025/2026 Zimbabwe Social Registry Survey was launched in Nkayi district by ZimStat with the Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare, collecting household data for a national social registry and spreading progressively across 65 districts ([source, 2026-03-11](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-rolls-out-nationwide-survey-to-strengthen-social-protection-systems/)).
