# Eswatini

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Deputy Prime Minister Thulisile Dladla said the National Dynamic Social Registry (Imbeleko) is being built, with Lubombo household data collection scheduled for 2026-10-12 to 2026-11-27 after the Shiselweni enumeration of June to July 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/lubombo-mps-join-dpm-to-make-every-vulnerable-household-count/>). [The Observer reports Shiselweni Region pilot results of the National Dynamic Social Registry (Imbeleko), covering 47,766 households with 44,683 registered and ready for targeting, which government intends to use to target social protection](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/2-304-shiselweni-residents-have-difficulty-walking/>). [Social Registry official Nosizo Mtfupha said the Shiselweni pilot registered 44,683 of 47,766 households in the Social Registry database, and the World Bank contributed US$2.5 million (about E42.5 million) to the programme](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/world-bank-injects-e42-5-million-into-social-registry-project-to-deliver-homes-and-jobs/>).
