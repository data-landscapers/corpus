# Uganda

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [WFP says its 2025 support included advancing integration of the Karamoja Social Registry into the National Single Registry, with discussions continuing with the Office of the Prime Minister and the World Bank's NUSAF team](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172882/download/>). [The FY2026/27 Public Investment Plan lists NUSAF IV, running from July 2025 to June 2030, with UShs 44.798bn in FY2026/27](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Public%20Investment%20Plan%20FY%202067-27.pdf>). [One of its outputs is a National Social Registry with beneficiary data, to cover the whole country](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Public%20Investment%20Plan%20FY%202067-27.pdf>). [MGLSD leads the registry's technical development](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/National%20Budget%20docs/Public%20Investment%20Plan%20FY%202067-27.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use the social registry. The nearest fact: [in 2025 WFP worked on integrating the Karamoja Social Registry into the National Single Registry](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000172882/download/>).
