# Benin

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [Le Matinal reports the Conseil des ministres created the Registre Social Unique, an information system for eligibility decisions and household data, described as a system 'en cours de mise en place'](<https://lematinal.bj/registre-social-unique-au-benin-la-protection-des-plus-vulnerables-renforcee/>). [ANIP/WURI-Benin reports a launch and scoping workshop was opened for setting up the Registre Social Unique, financed by the World Bank WURI project and due to be effective in six months](<https://anip.bj/le-registre-social-unique-rsu-bientot-une-realite/>). [The social affairs ministry opened the kick-off workshop for building the Registre Social Unique, World Bank-financed through WURI, with a DW/Technologies Wevioo/Stratec-ARC consortium and completion due in six months](<https://social.gouv.bj/index.php/actualite/actualites-du-ministere/le-registre-social-unique--rsu--bientot-une-realite>).
