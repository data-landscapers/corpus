# Sudan

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In April 2026 SUNA reported that two ministers discussed progress in creating the unified social registry system](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/lkaaa-ozary-yaazz-althol-alrkmy-oaslah-alkhdm-almdny-nho-dol-mossat-hdyth>). [They were the ministers of human resources and social welfare and of digital transformation](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/lkaaa-ozary-yaazz-althol-alrkmy-oaslah-alkhdm-almdny-nho-dol-mossat-hdyth>). [The system is meant to improve targeting of poor and vulnerable groups](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/lkaaa-ozary-yaazz-althol-alrkmy-oaslah-alkhdm-almdny-nho-dol-mossat-hdyth>). [In February 2025 the Solidarity and Poverty Reduction Council approved the registry, with a plan to launch it as a pilot in Gedaref state](<https://alakhbar-alsudan.com/?p=29265>).
