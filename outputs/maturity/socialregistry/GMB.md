# Gambia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In April 2024 the World Bank said the Gambia Social Registry (GamSR), begun in 2021, covered 203,022 households](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [They were in 40 of 43 districts, about 70% of the population](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).

**Several programmes.** [In April 2024 the World Bank said GamSR data underpinned the Nafa Quick COVID-19 response, for 78,422 households](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [They also underpinned the Nafa cash-transfer programme, for 16,966 households](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [The National Disaster Management Agency, the ROOTS project and the World Food Programme also use the registry](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).
