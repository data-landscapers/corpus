# Gambia

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank says the Gambia Social Registry (GamSR), initiated in 2021, now covers 203,022 households in 40 of 43 districts, about 70% of the population](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank says GamSR data underpinned the Nafa Quick COVID-19 response (78,422 households) and the Nafa cash-transfer programme (16,966 households), and is also used by the National Disaster Management Agency, the ROOTS project and the World Food Programme](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).
