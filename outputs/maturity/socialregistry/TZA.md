# Tanzania

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In August 2022 NRD Companies said TASAF's Unified Registry of Beneficiaries had been built](<https://www.nrdcompanies.com/case-studies/consultancy-services-to-develop-unified-registry-of-beneficiaries-system/>). [All targeting information had been migrated from the cash-transfer MIS](<https://www.nrdcompanies.com/case-studies/consultancy-services-to-develop-unified-registry-of-beneficiaries-system/>). [In November 2025 the Prime Minister's Office listed Tanzania's challenges](<https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/ClosingRemark_PMO_LYED_SocialProtection_Tanzania.pdf>). [Among them was the absence of an integrated social protection system, a Unified Social Registry](<https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/ClosingRemark_PMO_LYED_SocialProtection_Tanzania.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes use a social registry. The nearest fact: [in November 2025 the Prime Minister's Office said Tanzania had no Unified Social Registry](<https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/ClosingRemark_PMO_LYED_SocialProtection_Tanzania.pdf>). [In August 2022 NRD Companies said users from different organisations could extract beneficiary lists from TASAF's registry](<https://www.nrdcompanies.com/case-studies/consultancy-services-to-develop-unified-registry-of-beneficiaries-system/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says a social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest fact: [in November 2025 the Prime Minister's Office said Tanzania had no integrated social protection system](<https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/ClosingRemark_PMO_LYED_SocialProtection_Tanzania.pdf>). [In August 2022 NRD Companies said TASAF's registry was linked with NIDA to cross-check identity](<https://www.nrdcompanies.com/case-studies/consultancy-services-to-develop-unified-registry-of-beneficiaries-system/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a social registry is under way.
