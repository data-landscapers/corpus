# Kenya

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [The World Bank's 29 June 2026 press release says the programme supports Kenya's Enhanced Single Registry as the main platform for identifying social-assistance beneficiaries, reducing duplication](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/30/world-bank-supports-kenyas-push-for-stronger-accountability-and-social-protection>). [The KSEIP 2 environmental and social management plan says the Enhanced Single Registry is a national database for poor and vulnerable households used by the government's National Safety Net Program](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Several programmes.** [All National Safety Net Programme cash transfers (HSNP, OPCT, CT-OVC, PwSD-CT) use the Enhanced Single Registry, with poverty targeting for all except the OPCT, which is not poverty-targeted](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).
