# Kenya

## Social protection register

**Registry in service.** [In June 2026 the World Bank said its programme supports Kenya's Enhanced Single Registry as the main platform for identifying social-assistance beneficiaries](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/30/world-bank-supports-kenyas-push-for-stronger-accountability-and-social-protection>). [The Bank said this reduces duplication](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/30/world-bank-supports-kenyas-push-for-stronger-accountability-and-social-protection>). [In August 2025 the KSEIP 2 environmental and social management plan called the Enhanced Single Registry a national database for poor and vulnerable households](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [The government's National Safety Net Programme uses it](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**Several programmes.** [The August 2025 plan says all National Safety Net Programme cash transfers use the Enhanced Single Registry](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [These are the HSNP, OPCT, CT-OVC and PwSD-CT](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [All but the OPCT are poverty-targeted](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [In October 2025 Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale said households to be sponsored under the Social Health Authority were identified through the Enhanced Single Registry](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-10-30-sha-system-stopped-theft-of-millions-in-fake-claims-duale>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the social registry is linked to the national ID.
