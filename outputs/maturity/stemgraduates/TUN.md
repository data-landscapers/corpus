# Tunisia

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2023, [STEM graduates were 37.9 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's tertiary graduates series. The same series [gives 38.3 in 2021 and 37.9 in 2022](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the figure fell by 0.37 points. It is 2.07 points short of 40, the next line. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent, so with [the figure below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>) none was looked for in this first pass.
