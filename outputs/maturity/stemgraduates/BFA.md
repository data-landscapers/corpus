# Burkina Faso

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 61.4 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's tertiary graduates series. The same series [gives 20.7 in 2021 and 25.3 in 2022](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the figure rose by 40.7 points. At 40 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.
