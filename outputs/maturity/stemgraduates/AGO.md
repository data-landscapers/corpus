# Angola

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** The higher education ministry's 2023-2024 statistical yearbook counts [3,076 of the 31,503 graduates of academic year 2022/23 in STEM, 9.8 per cent](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). Economia & Mercado, reporting the ministry's yearbook, gave [about 11 per cent of 29,302 graduates for 2022/23](<https://economiaemercado.com/artigo/apenas-11-dos-novos-licenciados-em-angola-formam-se-em-areas-de-tecnologicas-e-de-engenharia>). Both figures come from the ministry's 2023-2024 yearbook. [UNESCO's tertiary graduates series gave 12 per cent for 2015](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them. It says nothing of where the graduates find work.

**Research and education network.** In academic year 2022/23, [STEM graduates were 9.8 per cent of graduates, by the ministry's yearbook](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). No research and education network has been found in Angola.
