# Angola

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2022/23, [about 11 per cent of Angola's 29,302 tertiary graduates were in science, technology, engineering and mathematics](<https://economiaemercado.com/artigo/apenas-11-dos-novos-licenciados-em-angola-formam-se-em-areas-de-tecnologicas-e-de-engenharia>), by the higher education ministry's statistical yearbook as reported by Economia & Mercado. [UNESCO's tertiary graduates series gave 12 per cent for 2015](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). The 2022/23 figure is 2 points short of 13, the next line. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent, so with [the figure below that line](<https://economiaemercado.com/artigo/apenas-11-dos-novos-licenciados-em-angola-formam-se-em-areas-de-tecnologicas-e-de-engenharia>) none was looked for.
