# Angola

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** The higher education ministry's 2023-2024 statistical yearbook counts [3,076 of the 31,503 graduates of academic year 2022/23 in STEM, 9.8 per cent](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). Economia & Mercado, reporting the ministry's yearbook, gave [about 11 per cent of 29,302 graduates for 2022/23](<https://economiaemercado.com/artigo/apenas-11-dos-novos-licenciados-em-angola-formam-se-em-areas-de-tecnologicas-e-de-engenharia>); both put the share below 13 per cent. [UNESCO's tertiary graduates series gave 12 per cent for 2015](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). The yearbook's figure is about 3 points short of 13, the next line. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent, so with [the figure below that line](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>) none was looked for.
