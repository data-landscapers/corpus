# Rwanda

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 29.5 per cent of tertiary graduates in Rwanda](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 31 per cent in 2022 and 28.8 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2022 the STEM share of tertiary graduates fell by 1.5 points. The share was highest in 2022, at 31 per cent. The share was lowest in 2023, at 28.8 per cent. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 29.5 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No research and education network has been found in Rwanda.
