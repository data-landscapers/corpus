# Egypt

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2025, [STEM graduates were 14.1 per cent of tertiary graduates in Egypt](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 16.9 per cent in 2022, 16.9 per cent in 2023 and 14.1 per cent in 2024](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2022 the STEM share of tertiary graduates fell by 2.83 points. It is 12.9 points short of 27 per cent of tertiary graduates. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent. [The figure is below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), so none was looked for.
