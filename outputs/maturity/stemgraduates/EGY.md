# Egypt

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2025, [STEM graduates were 14.1 per cent of tertiary graduates in Egypt](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 16.9 per cent in 2022, 16.9 per cent in 2023 and 14.1 per cent in 2024](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2022 the STEM share of tertiary graduates fell by 2.83 points. The share was 16.9 per cent in both 2022 and 2023. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** In 2025, [STEM graduates were 14.1 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No research and education network has been found in Egypt.
