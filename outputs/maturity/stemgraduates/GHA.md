# Ghana

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 18.7 per cent of tertiary graduates in Ghana](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 16.7 per cent in 2021, 14.3 per cent in 2022 and 16.6 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the STEM share of tertiary graduates rose by 1.99 points. The share was lowest in 2022, at 14.3 per cent. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 18.7 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No research and education network has been found in Ghana.
