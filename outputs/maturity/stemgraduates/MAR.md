# Morocco

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 29.7 per cent of tertiary graduates in Morocco](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 27.2 per cent in 2021, 27.2 per cent in 2022 and 24.6 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the STEM share of tertiary graduates rose by 2.52 points. The share was lowest in 2023, at 24.6 per cent. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 29.7 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No research and education network has been found in Morocco.
