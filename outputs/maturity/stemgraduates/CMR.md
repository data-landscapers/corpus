# Cameroon

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 27.7 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's tertiary graduates series. The same series [gives 37.9 in 2021, 31.7 in 2022 and 31 in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the figure fell by 10.2 points. It is 12.3 points short of 40, the next line. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent, so with [the figure below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>) none was looked for in this first pass. [Scoop Afrique, citing higher education ministry communiques, said in August 2026 that the China-backed E-National Higher Education Network has given the state universities ten digital development centres linked by fibre](<https://www.scoop-afrique.com/articles/ia-et-education-le-pari-camerounais-presente-a-pekin>).
