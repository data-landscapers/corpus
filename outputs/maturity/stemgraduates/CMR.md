# Cameroon

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 27.7 per cent of tertiary graduates in Cameroon](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 37.9 per cent in 2021, 31.7 per cent in 2022 and 31 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the STEM share of tertiary graduates fell by 10.2 points. It is 12.3 points short of 40 per cent of tertiary graduates. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent. [The figure is below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), so none was looked for. [Scoop Afrique, citing higher education ministry communiques, said in August 2026 that the China-backed E-National Higher Education Network has given the state universities ten digital development centres linked by fibre](<https://www.scoop-afrique.com/articles/ia-et-education-le-pari-camerounais-presente-a-pekin>).
