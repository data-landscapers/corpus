# Cameroon

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 27.7 per cent of tertiary graduates in Cameroon](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 37.9 per cent in 2021, 31.7 per cent in 2022 and 31 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the STEM share of tertiary graduates fell by 10.2 points. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them. It says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 27.7 per cent of tertiary graduates](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). [Scoop Afrique, citing higher education ministry communiques, reported on the China-backed E-National Higher Education Network in August 2026](<https://www.scoop-afrique.com/articles/ia-et-education-le-pari-camerounais-presente-a-pekin>). It has given the state universities ten digital development centres linked by fibre.
