# Zimbabwe

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 23.8 per cent of tertiary graduates in Zimbabwe](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The series gives no figure for the years just before. No source found says whether it is rising or falling. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them. It says nothing of where they find work.
