# Algeria

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In 2024, [STEM graduates were 30.9 per cent of tertiary graduates in Algeria](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by the tertiary graduates series of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO). The same series [gives 30.1 per cent in 2021, 29.9 per cent in 2022 and 31.1 per cent in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the STEM share of tertiary graduates rose by 0.77 points. It is 9.1 points short of 40 per cent of tertiary graduates. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent. [The figure is below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), so none was looked for.
