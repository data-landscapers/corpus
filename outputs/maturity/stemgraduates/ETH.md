# Ethiopia

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In June 2026, [UNESCO's AI readiness profile of Ethiopia put STEM graduates at 11.2 per cent of graduates](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>). No earlier figure has been found, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is 1.8 points short of 13, the next line. The study's lines are 13, 27 and 40 per cent of tertiary graduates, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, ICT among them; it says nothing of where they find work.

**Research and education network.** A research and education network is asked about only once STEM graduates reach 40 per cent, so with [the figure below that line](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>) none was looked for.
