# Ethiopia

## STEM graduates

**STEM graduates, as a share of tertiary graduates.** In June 2026, [UNESCO's AI readiness profile of Ethiopia put STEM graduates at 11.2 per cent of graduates](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/ethiopia>). No earlier figure has been found. No source found says whether it is rising or falling. The figure counts graduates in science, technology, engineering and mathematics, information and communication technology among them. It says nothing of where they find work.
