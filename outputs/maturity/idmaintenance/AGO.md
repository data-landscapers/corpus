# Angola

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Angola's National Director of Registries said all 21 provinces can now issue and print identity cards locally](<https://angolanewswire.com/angolan-government-issues-more-than-17-million-citizens-identity-cards/>). Daily production rose from 12,000 to 60,000 cards after technical upgrades. He said so in September 2026. [The Ministry of Justice manages the multifunctional database at the centre of digital identity](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). Card printing has moved from the centre to the provinces.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The September 2026 report credits technical upgrades for the rise in output](<https://angolanewswire.com/angolan-government-issues-more-than-17-million-citizens-identity-cards/>). It does not say whether national staff or a supplier carried them out.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The Ministry of Justice's 2026 account names the database it manages](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). It does not say who holds the code.
