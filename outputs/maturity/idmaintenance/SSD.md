# South Sudan

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Directorate of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passports and Immigration runs national ID registration](<https://catholicradionetwork.org/2026/08/04/immigration-directorate-launches-school-based-national-id-registration-for-examination-candidates/>). In August 2026 it launched a school-based campaign in Juba. [It worked with the Central Equatoria education ministry](<https://www.eyeradio.org/school-based-national-id-registration-launched-for-exam-candidates-in-juba/>). [Its Yei office issues the cards, with European Union support](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/>). Students sitting Primary Eight and Senior Four exams were registered first.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The 2026 reports describe registration campaigns](<https://catholicradionetwork.org/2026/08/04/immigration-directorate-launches-school-based-national-id-registration-for-examination-candidates/>). They do not say whether national staff or a supplier maintain the system. No supplier is named. [In October 2025 the directorate's head said a contracted company maintains the computers behind passport and ID issuance](<https://eyeradio.org/revenue-sharing-plan-under-discussion-to-address-passport-id-shortages-official/>), and is owed over $800,000. [A journalist wrote that issuance stopped in August 2024 when the German supplier's printing software expired](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/opinion-south-sudanese-lose-opportunities-as-passport-crisis-remains-unresolved>).

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The Yei report credits European Union support](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/>). It does not say who holds the code. No supplier is named either.
