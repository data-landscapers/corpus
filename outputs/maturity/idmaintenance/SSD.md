# South Sudan

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Directorate of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passports, and Immigration runs a school-based National ID registration campaign for Primary Eight and Senior Four candidates, launched 4 August 2026 at Juba Day Secondary School](<https://catholicradionetwork.org/2026/08/04/immigration-directorate-launches-school-based-national-id-registration-for-examination-candidates/>). [The Directorate of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passport and Immigration with the Central Equatoria State Ministry of General Education launched school-based registration to issue National ID cards on 4 August 2026](<https://www.eyeradio.org/school-based-national-id-registration-launched-for-exam-candidates-in-juba/>). [The Directorate of Nationality, Passports and Immigration processed the national identity cards and issues them from its Yei office, with European Union support](<https://radioyei.org/news/local/yei-residents-begins-collecting-nationality-cards/>).
