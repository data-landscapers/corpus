# Sierra Leone

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Civil Registration Authority registered 100 inmates for National Identification Numbers and ID cards, with mobile registration teams and decentralised services](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>). [The NCRA has developed the National Biometric Identity Management System and a Tier III data centre and operates an eKYC platform, per its Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [The MoU stresses institutional capacity building and knowledge transfer, with governance so the platform can be sustainably managed and evolved by national institutions over time, while Sign leads system design and technical implementation](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/sierra-leone-partners-with-bhutan-sign-on-digital-identity-platform>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [Biometric Update says Sierra Leone's national ID system is based on MOSIP, an open-source platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework>).
