# Sierra Leone

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Civil Registration Authority manages the integrated registration and identity system in its entirety](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/93-of-sierra-leone-registered-with-unique-nins-2/>), its Director-General said in August 2026. [The NCRA developed the National Biometric Identity Management System and a Tier III data centre](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). It also operates an eKYC platform. [In September 2026 it registered 100 inmates for ID numbers and cards](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>).

**Maintained nationally.** [A July 2026 MoU with Sign stresses knowledge transfer so national institutions can manage and evolve the platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/sierra-leone-partners-with-bhutan-sign-on-digital-identity-platform>). The NCRA runs the system under the 2016 Act. No source found names a foreign firm maintaining the national ID system.

**Platform owned.** [Biometric Update reported in August 2026 that Sierra Leone's national ID system is based on MOSIP](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework>). MOSIP is an open-source identity platform. The system in service therefore runs on code no supplier owns.
