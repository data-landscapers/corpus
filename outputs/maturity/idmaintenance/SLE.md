# Sierra Leone

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** In August 2026 the head of the National Civil Registration Authority said [it manages the integrated registration and identity system in its entirety](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/93-of-sierra-leone-registered-with-unique-nins-2/>). Earlier that month he said [it had built the biometric identity system and a Tier III data centre](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). It also runs an eKYC platform. In September 2026 its [mobile teams registered 100 inmates for identity numbers and cards](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>). A July 2026 agreement with Bhutan's Sign [stresses knowledge transfer to national institutions](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/sierra-leone-partners-with-bhutan-sign-on-digital-identity-platform>). Sign leads the system's design.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [Biometric Update says Sierra Leone's national ID system is based on MOSIP, an open-source platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework>).

**Platform owned.** [Biometric Update says Sierra Leone's national ID system is based on MOSIP, an open-source platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/sierra-leone-consults-private-sector-on-trusted-digital-id-framework>).
