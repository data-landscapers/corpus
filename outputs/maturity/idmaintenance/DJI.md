# Djibouti

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The DGPF's own agents run enrolment, checks and duplicate resolution on the national register](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>), the head of its central NNI service said in July 2026. [The DGPF has issued the biometric national ID card across the territory since 2025](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). It runs the enrolment centres and mobile biometric teams itself. No foreign operator is named in either account.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The DGPF's 2026 account covers its agents' daily work on the register](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). It does not say who maintains the software.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The DGPF's director described its card operations in 2025](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). He did not say who holds the code. The card has been issued nationwide since 2025.
