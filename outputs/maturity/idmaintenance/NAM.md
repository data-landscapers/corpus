# Namibia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs, through its civil registration department, runs the e-ID card application and issuing, including amendments and replacements](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>). [The Office of the Prime Minister says it provides oversight, technology support, quality assurance and management of key components of the national population register on which the E-ID is built, and the Ministry of Home Affairs runs the existing registration infrastructure](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs itself takes applications, prints and distributes national identity documents, printing 166,237 between 1 April 2025 and 30 June 2026, per its spokesperson](<https://neweralive.na/ministry-takes-id-registration-to-khomas-schools/>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ministry-of-home-affairs-clarifies-e-id-costs/>).
