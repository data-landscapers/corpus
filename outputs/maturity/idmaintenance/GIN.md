# Guinea

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** Guinea's national ID programme had enrolment in service at Sanoyah in March 2026. [NG Tech Innovation says its teams are in Conakry to train national cadres and transfer skills](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/identite-numerique-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-scellent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-le-rnpp/>). The same July 2026 report says Guinean engineers developed the register's infrastructure.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system day to day. [The programme runs on the open-source MOSIP platform with TECH5 biometric infrastructure](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>), so its code is not a supplier's. The training of national cadres, reported in July 2026, is the nearest step towards national upkeep.

**Platform owned.** [ID Tech Wire reported in March 2026 that Guinea's national ID is built on MOSIP](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>). MOSIP is an open-source identity platform. TECH5 supplies the biometric part. Enrolment was in service at Sanoyah when the report appeared.
