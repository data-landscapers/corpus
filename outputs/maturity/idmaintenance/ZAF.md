# South Africa

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Department of Home Affairs runs the smart ID card programme](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/online-tool-finds-nearest-smart-id-issuing-bank/nWJadvbNDZDvbjO1>). With banking partners it issues smart IDs through 372 bank branches. This account dates from August 2026. The department runs the programme itself and works with banks to reach more people.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The department's 2026 account covers issuing through bank branches](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/online-tool-finds-nearest-smart-id-issuing-bank/nWJadvbNDZDvbjO1>). It does not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain the system. No supplier is named in the account. The banks act as issuing points, not as operators of the system.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2026 account names the department as the programme's owner](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/online-tool-finds-nearest-smart-id-issuing-bank/nWJadvbNDZDvbjO1>). It does not say who holds the code. No supplier is named either.
