# Chad

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [ANATS, the National Agency for Secure Documents, already operates SIGPTS, the Integrated Population and Secure Document Management System, per We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [ANATS, a public establishment created by Ordonnance N°001/PR/2016, states it is responsible for producing, managing and securing identity cards and other secure titles, and that it set up the SIGPTS](<https://www.anats.td/>). [ANATS's director general instructs its user reception centres and the Système Intégré Sécurisé director to enrol school leavers for the NNI with priority, so the national agency runs enrolment](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/09/26/lanats-simplifie-lacces-aux-nni-pour-les-bacheliers-tchadiens-a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-universitaire/>).
