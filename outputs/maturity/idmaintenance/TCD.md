# Chad

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [ANATS, the National Agency for Secure Documents, operates SIGPTS, the integrated population and secure document system](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [It is a public body that produces, manages and secures identity cards](<https://www.anats.td/>). [In September 2025 its director general had its centres enrol school leavers first](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/09/26/lanats-simplifie-lacces-aux-nni-pour-les-bacheliers-tchadiens-a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-universitaire/>). The agency was created by ordinance in 2016.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [ANATS says it set up SIGPTS](<https://www.anats.td/>). It does not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain it. No supplier is named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2026 report says only that ANATS operates SIGPTS](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). It does not say who holds the code. No supplier is named either.
