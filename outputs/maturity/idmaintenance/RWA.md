# Rwanda

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [NIDA leads nationwide enrolment for the new digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/rwanda-accelerates-biometric-enrollment-ahead-of-digital-id-switch>). It has registration teams in the provinces and will place biometric kits in sector offices. [In October 2025 its field teams ran enrolment in Nyanza, Huye and Gisagara](<https://www.nida.gov.rw/news-details/nida-has-officially-launched-digital-id-activities-in-the-southern-province-beginning-with-nyanza-huye-and-gisagara-districts>). District administrations worked with them. The agency runs the rollout itself. Biometric kits are to stay in sector offices for good.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [NIDA's 2026 plan covers enrolment and equipment](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/rwanda-accelerates-biometric-enrollment-ahead-of-digital-id-switch>). It does not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain the system. No supplier is named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns.
