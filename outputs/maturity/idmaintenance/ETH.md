# Ethiopia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [In August 2026 the national ID authority became Faydaverse, a government-owned enterprise](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). It sits under Ethiopian Investment Holdings. [Faydaverse runs the ID, the wallet and credential verification](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>). [It bills banks for record retrievals and authentications](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [It also runs the Fayda partner portal and the VeriFayda authentication platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>).

**Maintained nationally.** [The ID programme said in 2024 that local engineering teams provide Fayda's technical solutions](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>), with an in-house cybersecurity team. [It relies on local engineers and in-house technical capability](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>). [A May 2026 deck says the programme office and the new enterprise would run in parallel](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Platform owned.** [Fayda runs on MOSIP, the open-source identity platform](<https://kanatelevision.com/news/what-is-faydaverse-the-ethiopian-national-id-program-that-became-a-company-and-how-it-plans-to/>), Kana TV News reported in August 2026. [Faydaverse's mandate is to develop, operate and scale the digital public infrastructure](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/faydaverse-officially-enters-africas-dpi-market>).
