# Ethiopia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The national ID authority became the government-owned enterprise Faydaverse in August 2026, mandated to develop, operate and scale the digital public infrastructure](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). [The National ID program (Fayda) has become the state-owned enterprise Faydaverse under Ethiopian Investment Holdings and runs the ID, wallet and credential verification, per its stakeholder engagement director](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>). [Biometric Update says Ethiopia's National ID Program, re-established in August as the public entity Faydaverse Digital Solutions, runs the Fayda Partner Portal sandbox and the VeriFayda authentication platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [The state-owned enterprise FaydaVerse, under Ethiopian Investment Holdings, runs the national digital ID system and bills banks for record retrievals and authentications](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [National ID Ethiopia's deck says the Program Office and the planned Fayda state enterprise will operate in parallel to ensure operational continuity, with home-grown capacity](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).
