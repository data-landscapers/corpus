# Mauritania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Tech Rek reports that the national population registry and secure documents agency runs its own information system, including the Houwiyeti app and citizen reception centres, and took it offline for a day for preventive maintenance](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [UNDP says the National Agency for People Registry and Secure Documents, under the Ministry of Home Affairs, developed and manages Mauritania's national biometric system](<https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Maintained nationally.** [Tech Rek reports that the agency carried out and announced the preventive maintenance of its information system, and that periodic maintenance was generally done on Saturdays, with no foreign supplier named](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>).
