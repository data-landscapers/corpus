# Mauritania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [UNDP says the National Agency for People Registry and Secure Documents, under the Ministry of Home Affairs, manages the national biometric system](<https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app>). [Tech Rek reports that the agency runs its own information system, including the Houwiyeti app and citizen reception centres](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). About 1.5 million people use Houwiyeti.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The agency took the system offline for a day of preventive maintenance in August 2026](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). It announced the outage in advance. The report does not say whether agency staff or a supplier did the work.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [UNDP wrote in 2024 that the agency developed the national biometric system](<https://www.undp.org/blog/mauritania-pilots-digital-id-app>). It did not say who wrote the code.
