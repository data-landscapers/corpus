# Botswana

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Minister said in July 2026 that the ministry runs card production with its own personnel and extended hours](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439>). [The Department of Civil and National Registration issues the Omang through its own offices and a central production centre](<https://www.gov.bw/civil-registration/national-identity-card-application>). [It received P105 million in 2026 to produce Omang cards](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>). The department runs head, regional and district offices.

**Maintained nationally.** [The ministry engages suppliers only for equipment maintenance](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439>), the Minister said in July 2026. No source found says national staff maintain the system itself. The suppliers are not named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2026 budget line covers card production and the births and deaths system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>). It does not say who holds the code.
