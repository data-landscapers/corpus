# Botswana

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Minister said the ministry runs card production with its own personnel and extended hours, engaging suppliers only on equipment maintenance](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439>). [The ministry's Civil and National Registration department is funded with P105 million to produce Omang cards and maintain the Births and Deaths Registration System](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>). [The Department of Civil and National Registration registers applicants and issues the Omang through its own head, regional and district offices and a centralized production center](<https://www.gov.bw/civil-registration/national-identity-card-application>).
