# DR Congo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Under the 20-year contract the IDEMIA/Afritech consortium produces the cards, manages the general population file and builds and operates the identification centres, per BBC News Afrique](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>).

**Platform owned.** No source found since October 2023 says the system runs on code the state owns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>).
