# DR Congo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The IDEMIA and Afritech consortium produces the cards and manages the general population file](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). It also builds and operates the identification centres. It does so under a 20-year contract, as BBC News Afrique reported in May 2024. No national body is named as running the system.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [The consortium's 20-year contract covers the cards, the population file and the centres](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). No transfer to national staff is reported. The consortium remains in charge for the life of the contract.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2024 contract leaves the population file with the consortium](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). Nothing says the code passes to the state. No national body is named as owner.
