# DR Congo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The IDEMIA and Afritech consortium produces the cards and manages the general population file](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). It also builds and operates the identification centres. It does so under a 20-year contract, as BBC News Afrique reported in May 2024. No national body is named as running the system. [Trident says its subsidiary develops and operates the RDC-PASS digital ID under a 20-year partnership with the government](<https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/18/3314133/0/en/Trident-Digital-Tech-Holdings-Nasdaq-TDTH-Activates-National-RDC-PASS-Digital-Identity-Ecosystem-in-the-Democratic-Republic-of-Congo-Under-20-Year-Public-Private-Partnership.html>). [In March 2024 ONIP said it would manage the national data centre and population file](<https://scooprdc.net/2024/03/27/rdc-lonip-presente-le-plan-de-mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-fichier-general-de-la-population-et-de-la-delivrance-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-aux-congolais/>), while its partner installs and maintains the system and trains ONIP's trainers.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [The consortium's 20-year contract covers the cards, the population file and the centres](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). No transfer to national staff is reported. The consortium remains in charge for the life of the contract.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2024 contract leaves the population file with the consortium](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). Nothing says the code passes to the state. No national body is named as owner.
