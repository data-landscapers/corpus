# Malawi

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Registration Bureau manages the national civil registration and digital identity system](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>). It is a government department. [In December 2025 its Principal Secretary said it would renew, print and distribute about two million expired ID cards](<https://malawi24.com/2025/12/25/government-extends-validity-of-expired-national-id-cards-to-2027/>). The Bureau runs card printing and distribution across the country.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The Bureau's 2025 plan covers renewing cards](<https://malawi24.com/2025/12/25/government-extends-validity-of-expired-national-id-cards-to-2027/>). It does not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain the system. No foreign operator is named.

**Platform owned.** [UNDP handed the system's source code to the Bureau in October 2018](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/>). That gave the Bureau ownership of the core software. No source from the last three years confirms it. That handover is older than the three-year window this study reads.

***Also on file***. [Nzika Digital ID Wallet](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/16/malawi-drops-expiry-dates-on-national-ids/>).

***Not found***. No source found says who maintains the system. The code handover dates from 2018.
