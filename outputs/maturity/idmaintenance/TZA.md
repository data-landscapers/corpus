# Tanzania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** In September 2026 NIDA said [it registers people, keeps the national register and runs a system connected to 157 institutions](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>). That month the minister said [NIDA serves 156 offices that print ID cards electronically](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). NIDA's 2026 deck [names it as the implementer for registration, issuance and data management](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). It lists in-house technical capacity among the system's supports. [NIDA sits under the Ministry of Home Affairs](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>).
