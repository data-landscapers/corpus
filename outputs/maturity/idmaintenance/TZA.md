# Tanzania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Minister Katambi said NIDA now serves 156 offices, with machines and equipment that print ID cards electronically](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [NIDA, a government institution under the Ministry of Home Affairs, says it registers people, issues national IDs and maintains the national digital registry](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). [NIDA's deck names NIDA as the implementer for registration, ID issuance and data management, and lists in-house technical capacity among the supports of the system](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended.
