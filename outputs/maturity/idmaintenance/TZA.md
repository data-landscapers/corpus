# Tanzania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [NIDA registers people, issues national IDs and maintains the national digital registry](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). It is a government institution under the Ministry of Home Affairs. [In September 2026 it served 156 offices with equipment that prints ID cards](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [Its system connects to 157 institutions](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>). NIDA runs registration and card printing itself. [In early 2024 NIDA kept the central register and ran registration through 153 district offices linked to its data centre](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>).

**Maintained nationally.** [NIDA said in early 2024 that its in-house experts had built 24 of 42 planned systems](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>). [They provide technical support, ending dependence on foreign contractors](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>). [Its 2026 deck lists in-house technical capacity among the system's supports](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Platform owned.** [NIDA says that by building in-house it owns the program copies of the national ID system](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>). [It is free of foreign contractors' licence limits](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>).
