# Tanzania

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [NIDA registers people, issues national IDs and maintains the national digital registry](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). It is a government institution under the Ministry of Home Affairs. [In September 2026 it served 156 offices with equipment that prints ID cards](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [Its system connects to 157 institutions](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>). NIDA runs registration and card printing itself.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains and upgrades the software. [NIDA's 2026 deck lists in-house technical capacity among the supports of the system](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). It does not say that NIDA's staff maintain it.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [NIDA's deck names it as implementer for registration and data management](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). It does not say who holds the code. No supplier is named.
