# Tunisia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [A unit within the Interior Ministry manages the citizen identifier system](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). The Centre National Informatique operates the interoperability platform. [The CNI is in charge of the national unique identifier register and digital identity](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-le-centre-national-de-linformatique-celebre-50-ans-au-coeur-de-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat/>). [The Interior Ministry's own services issue the national ID card](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>). The Interior Ministry takes fingerprints and a photo for each card.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The CNI is a national operator](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-le-centre-national-de-linformatique-celebre-50-ans-au-coeur-de-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat/>). Its January 2026 communiqué does not say who maintains the identifier system. No supplier is named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2026 playbook shows who manages each part](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). It does not say who holds the code. No supplier is named either.
