# Liberia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The proclamation says the National Identification Registry, set up by 2011 legislation, owns and operates the National Biometric Identification System](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [The signed OeSD build-operate-transfer concession includes training, technology transfer and knowledge-sharing for Liberian institutions, while OeSD serves as technology partner and NIR administers the system, NIR Executive Director Andrew Peters said on 4 September 2026](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>). [The Liberian Observer reports the proposed Build-Operate-Transfer concession has OeSD International GmbH operate and maintain the system before transferring it to the Liberian government, with training and expertise transfer to be examined by a joint committee](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>).
