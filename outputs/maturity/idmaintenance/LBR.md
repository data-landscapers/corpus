# Liberia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The proclamation says the National Identification Registry, set up by 2011 legislation, owns and operates the National Biometric Identification System](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [The Liberian Observer reports the proposed Build-Operate-Transfer concession has OeSD International GmbH operate and maintain the system before transferring it to the Liberian government, with training and expertise transfer to be examined by a joint committee](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). [The National Identification Registry says the proposed US$54.7 million Build, Operate and Transfer agreement would have OeSD International operate the system for an agreed period to recover its investment, then transfer infrastructure and operations to the Government of Liberia](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/nir-seeks-legislative-approval-for-us54-7m-identity-deal/>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>).
