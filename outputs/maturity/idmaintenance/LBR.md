# Liberia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Identification Registry owns and operates the national biometric identification system](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>), set up by 2011 legislation. [Its Executive Director said in September 2026 that the NIR administers the system, with OeSD as technology partner](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [A proposed US$54.7 million concession would have OeSD operate the system to recover its investment](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/nir-seeks-legislative-approval-for-us54-7m-identity-deal/>). [OeSD would operate and maintain it before handing it to the government](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). The concession was still awaiting ratification in late 2026.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The concession includes training and technology transfer, to avoid permanent dependence on one company](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/>). [The system would pass to the government only at its end](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202611/revenue-sharing-data-sovereignty-concerns-arise-from-liberia-id-card-concession>).

***Also on file***. [NIDCRVS](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>).

***Not found***. No source found says national staff maintain the system. The OeSD concession awaited ratification in late 2026.
