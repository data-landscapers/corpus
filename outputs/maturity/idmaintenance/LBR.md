# Liberia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The proclamation says the National Identification Registry, set up by 2011 legislation, owns and operates the National Biometric Identification System](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [The National Identification Registry says the proposed US$54.7 million Build, Operate and Transfer agreement would have OeSD International operate the system for an agreed period to recover its investment, then transfer infrastructure and operations to the Government of Liberia](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/nir-seeks-legislative-approval-for-us54-7m-identity-deal/>). [FrontPage Africa reports NIR says the proposed US$54.7 million Build, Operate and Transfer concession with OeSD International includes training and technology transfer to avoid permanent dependence on an external company, while NIR keeps statutory responsibility for administration](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended.
