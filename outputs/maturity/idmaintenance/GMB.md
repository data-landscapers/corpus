# Gambia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The new biometric card is rolled out by the Gambia Immigration Department, whose officers run the enrolment centres, under the system launched through the Ministry of Interior with Margins ID Group](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>). [Subsequent reporting found immigration officials complained of being denied access to systems including the Orbis administrative database and firewall infrastructure, which reportedly remained under Semlex's control](<https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/>). [Margins ID Systems Ltd will design, build, co-operate and fund the new ID system for eight years under a build, co-operate and transfer model, sharing revenue 70/30 with the Government, with all assets transferred to the Government at the end](<https://standard.gm/new-id-card-to-get-10-years-validity/>).

**Maintained nationally.** [A Gambia Immigration Department officer testified on 6 October 2026 that the department no longer has access to the Semlex data because its access ended when the Semlex contract ended](<https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/>).

**Platform owned.** No source held since October 2023 says the system runs on code the state owns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>).
