# Gambia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Gambia Immigration Department rolls out the new biometric card, and its officers run the enrolment centres](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>). The system was launched in 2026 with Margins ID Group. [Margins' subsidiary MIDSA won the contract in January 2026 to design, deploy and operate the system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/the-gambia-launches-sovereign-digital-id-system-built-by-margins-id-group>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [Margins will co-operate the system for eight years and transfer all assets at the end](<https://standard.gm/new-id-card-to-get-10-years-validity/>). [Officials said in October 2026 that parts of the older system remained under Semlex's control](<https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/>).

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [Margins designs, builds and funds the new system](<https://standard.gm/new-id-card-to-get-10-years-validity/>). Its assets pass to the government only when the eight years end.
