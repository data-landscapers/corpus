# Gambia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The new biometric card is rolled out by the Gambia Immigration Department, whose officers run the enrolment centres, under the system launched through the Ministry of Interior with Margins ID Group](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>). [The National Identity System is operated by the Department of Immigration](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>). [Subsequent reporting found immigration officials complained of being denied access to systems including the Orbis administrative database and firewall infrastructure, which reportedly remained under Semlex's control](<https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/>). [MIDSA, a subsidiary of Ghana's Margins ID Group, was awarded the contract in January 2026 to design, deploy and operate the National Identity Management System under a Build, Operate and Transfer agreement](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/the-gambia-launches-sovereign-digital-id-system-built-by-margins-id-group>).
