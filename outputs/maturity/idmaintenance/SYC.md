# Seychelles

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Department of Immigration and Civil Status issues, replaces and amends the National Identity Card at its ID Card Offices in Victoria and Grand Anse Praslin, per its FAQ](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>). [The Department of Immigration & Civil Status, with the Department of Information Communications Technology, issues the NIN and ID letters and from 17 September 2024 issues virtual ID cards on the SeyID platform instead of physical cards for permit holders](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/launch-of-new-digital-solutions-for-public-services>). [The Civil Status Division of the Department of Immigration and Civil Status prepares and issues Identity Cards to all residents and holds the National Population Database](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>).
