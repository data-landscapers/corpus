# Seychelles

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Civil Status Division prepares and issues identity cards and holds the National Population Database](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>). It sits in the Department of Immigration and Civil Status. [The department issues and replaces cards at offices in Victoria and Grand Anse Praslin](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/faq/civil-services/id-section>). [Since September 2024 permit holders get a virtual ID on SeyID](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/launch-of-new-digital-solutions-for-public-services>). It also issues the National Identity Number.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The ICT department works with the department on the SeyID platform](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/launch-of-new-digital-solutions-for-public-services>). Nothing says who maintains the identity system itself. No supplier is named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2024 notice covers the move to virtual cards](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/public-announcement>). It does not say who holds the code.
