# Comoros

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Dutch firm GenKey took over production of ID cards and passports on 31 March 2026](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/production-de-documents-biom%C3%A9triques-genkey-succ%C3%A8de-officiellement-%C3%A0-semlex.html>). The interior minister said this would bring a transfer of skills to national staff. [A week earlier Semlex, the producer since 2007, handed the data to the government](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html>). The data are now hosted in Comoros. Comoros thereby took back its own data.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [GenKey takes over production and management of the documents](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html>). [Semlex had held the biometric data in Belgium](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/impression-des-documents-biom%C3%A9triques-i-genkey-remplacera-semlex-%C3%A0-partir-du-mois-d%E2%80%99avril-prochain.html>).

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The Interior Ministry calls the handover control of the state's identification resources](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html>). Nothing says who holds the code.
