# Comoros

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Al-Watwan reports that the Comoros interior minister said GenKey took over production of biometric passports and national identity cards on 31 March 2026 from Semlex, with a transfer of skills to national staff and progressive national appropriation of biometric data](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/production-de-documents-biom%C3%A9triques-genkey-succ%C3%A8de-officiellement-%C3%A0-semlex.html>). [Al-Watwan reports that on 24 March 2026 Semlex, producer of Comoros' biometric ID cards and passports since 2007, handed the data to the government, that Dutch firm GenKey takes over production and management of the documents, and that the Interior Ministry calls the handover control of the state's identification resources, with data hosted in Comoros](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html>).
