# Eswatini

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs spokesperson says Chief Codes are updated in the ministry's own system before a replacement ID card is applied for](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/home-affairs-ministry-urges-emaswati-to-verify-chief-codes-2/>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says national ID card production and issuance are taking place at ten service centres after a suspension for technical upgrades of the production system](<https://times.co.sz/31079/news/id-card-services-resume-in-boost-for-citizens/>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says it has resumed national ID card production after a temporary suspension over technical challenges, with services at ten government service centres](<https://independentnews.co.sz/41508/news/home-affairs-resumes-id-card-production/>).
