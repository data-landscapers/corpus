# Togo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The national identification agency ANID's teams carried out biometric registration across the regions to build the national identification register](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). [The national biometric identification agency ANID opened fifty enrolment centres at Atakpame, bringing the Plateaux region in after the Savanes, Grand Lome and Maritime, says the government portal](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/se-faire-enregistrer-c-est-compter-c-est-exister-c-est-participer>). [The deck says the e-ID Togo system is based on the open source MOSIP platform, with no vendor lock-in](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [Togo chose MOSIP, an open-source identity platform, to build the infrastructure, which the article says lets the country keep control of it](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>).

**Platform owned.** [Togo chose MOSIP, an open-source identity platform, to build the infrastructure, which the article says lets the country keep control of it](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). [Togo's national digital ID system is MOSIP-based, an open-source platform, under a 2021 MoU; an Atos and Idemia consortium implements mostly the technical aspects](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses>).
