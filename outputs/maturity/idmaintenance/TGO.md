# Togo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [ANID, the national identification agency, opened fifty enrolment centres at Atakpame in March 2026](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/se-faire-enregistrer-c-est-compter-c-est-exister-c-est-participer>). That brought the Plateaux region in after the Savanes, Grand Lomé and Maritime. [ANID's teams carried out biometric registration across the regions in 2026](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). The agency runs enrolment itself, region by region. [The identification agency runs e-ID enrolment, with field work entrusted to the statistics institute INSEED](<https://www.toutafrica.com/2025/08/25/e-id-togo-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-togo-a-fait-le-choix-strategique-dutiliser-mosip/>). [Togo's ID project was developed and managed mainly by Togolese expertise](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/30/senegal-and-togo-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-identity-systems/>).

**Maintained nationally.** [An Atos and Idemia consortium implements mostly the technical aspects of the system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses>), under a 2021 MoU. No source found says who maintains it day to day. Its open-source base means no supplier holds the code.

**Platform owned.** [Togo's national digital ID is built on MOSIP, an open-source identity platform](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). A September 2026 article says this choice lets the country keep control of it. [A 2026 deck says the e-ID Togo system has no vendor lock-in](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [The agency's head said in August 2025 that Togo chose MOSIP to keep sovereignty over its system](<https://www.toutafrica.com/2025/08/25/e-id-togo-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-togo-a-fait-le-choix-strategique-dutiliser-mosip/>).
