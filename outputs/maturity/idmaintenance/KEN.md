## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Registration Bureau produces identity cards at its Nairobi headquarters](<https://www.vividvoicenews.com/2026/10/03/bungoma-id-registration-hampered-by-staff-vehicle-and-equipment-shortages/>). Cards take three weeks to a month to return to the county. A county registration officer gave this account in October 2026. The Bureau is a national body, and production is centralised in Nairobi.
