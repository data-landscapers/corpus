# Kenya

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Registration Bureau produces identity cards at its Nairobi headquarters](<https://www.vividvoicenews.com/2026/10/03/bungoma-id-registration-hampered-by-staff-vehicle-and-equipment-shortages/>). Cards take three weeks to a month to return to the county. A county registration officer gave this account in October 2026. The Bureau is a national body, and production is centralised in Nairobi. [In June 2024 the National Registration Bureau was printing ID cards at its own Nairobi centre in 24-hour shifts](<https://www.tv47.digital/govt-adopts-24-hour-ids-printing-to-clear-backlog-59752/>). [In November 2023 a principal secretary said the government had declined foreign firms' offers to build Maisha Namba](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/business-rivalry-blamed-for-digital-id-misinformation/>), preferring local experts.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. It does not say whether national staff or a supplier maintain the system. No supplier is named in the report. The delay reported lies in production and delivery.
