# Somalia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The federal National Identification and Registration Authority runs the NIRA card, which a Mogadishu resident can obtain at a NIRA centre, with no NIRA registration office in Puntland (op-ed by Ismail H Warsame)](<https://www.eurasiareview.com/03102026-from-e-visas-to-nira-mogadishus-bureaucratic-war-on-ordinary-travelers-oped/>). [Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA) runs the registration and issuance of the national ID, which it launched as eAqoonsi in September 2023, says the Hiiraan report](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Sep/206316/somalia_registers_15_million_citizens_under_national_id_system.aspx>). [NIRA, the national authority, runs registration: 59 centres, 17 mobile teams and 31 embassy centres, per its director general](<https://sonna.so/en/article/National-Identification-Week-2026-Concludes-in-Mogadishu>). [NIRA, the national authority, operates three registration centres and issues the National Identification Number](<https://wardheernews.com/somalias-digital-id-a-milestone-on-the-roadmap-towards-effective-governance-and-service-delivery>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the system or the foreign contract has ended. The nearest held fact: [NIRA's Director General says the DPI systems were entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, reflecting national ownership; the World Bank's Scaled-UP project supported the build](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/somalia-unveils-dpi-tools-for-biometric-verification-id-issuance-and-service-access>).

**Platform owned.** [NIRA's Director General says the DPI systems were entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, reflecting national ownership; the World Bank's Scaled-UP project supported the build](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/somalia-unveils-dpi-tools-for-biometric-verification-id-issuance-and-service-access>). [NIRA's Director General says the three launched systems (HUBIYE, e-Aqoonsi, CDS) were entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA, reflecting national ownership](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>).
