# Somalia

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Somalia's National Identification and Registration Authority runs registration and issues the national ID](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Sep/206316/somalia_registers_15_million_citizens_under_national_id_system.aspx>). It launched the card as eAqoonsi in September 2023. [By September 2026 NIRA ran 59 centres, 17 mobile teams and 31 embassy centres](<https://sonna.so/en/article/National-Identification-Week-2026-Concludes-in-Mogadishu>). Pakistan's NADRA and the World Bank supported the build. [In February 2026 NIRA agreed a framework with China that includes transfer of technical skills to Somali staff](<https://sonna.so/en/article/director-of-nira-meets-with-chinese-ambassador-to-advance-national-id-system-and-digital-cooperation>).

**Maintained nationally.** In May 2025 [NIRA added a verifier, a digital ID app and a certificate system to the ID system](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). Its own Somali engineers built all three, its Director General said. [NIRA has used partnerships with NADRA and the World Bank for knowledge transfer and training](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>). NIRA reported that in 2026.

**Platform owned.** [The three launched systems, HUBIYE, e-Aqoonsi and CDS, were entirely developed by Somali engineers at NIRA](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-key-digital-public-infrastructure-for-national-id-system>). NIRA's Director General presented this in May 2025 as national ownership. The World Bank's Scaled-UP project funded the work.
