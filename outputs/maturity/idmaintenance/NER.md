# Niger

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [Niger launched its new biometric ID card on 25 April 2026](<https://anp.ne/niger-lancement-officiel-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-aes-a-niamey/>). A Libyan company, Alitisal Aljadeed, produces it, the national press agency reports. The project is a partnership with a local company. [In March 2026 the Libyan firm's engineer said it trains local teams to take over](<https://www.lesahel.org/projet-des-nouveaux-passeports-et-cartes-biometriques-du-niger-le-secretaire-general-du-ministere-de-linterieur-visite-les-services-concernes-pour-apprecier-letat-de-mise-en-o/>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says national staff maintain the ID system, or that the supplier's contract has ended. The nearest held fact is [the April 2026 report that the Libyan company provides card production](<https://anp.ne/niger-lancement-officiel-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-biometrique-aes-a-niamey/>). It says nothing of who maintains or upgrades the system behind the card.

**Platform owned.** No source found since October 2023 says the ID system runs on code the state owns. The nearest held fact is [the March 2026 account of the card and passport project](<https://www.lesahel.org/projet-des-nouveaux-passeports-et-cartes-biometriques-du-niger-le-secretaire-general-du-ministere-de-linterieur-visite-les-services-concernes-pour-apprecier-letat-de-mise-en-o/>). It describes training for local teams, but not who holds the code.

***Not found***. No source found says a national authority runs the ID system day to day.
