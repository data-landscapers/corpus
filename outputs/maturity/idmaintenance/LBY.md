# Libya

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Civil Registry Authority, a national body, issues the unique National ID Number to all Libyan nationals, per the Libya Herald](<https://libyaherald.com/2024/09/pm-aldabaiba-follows-up-on-intilaqa-project-for-matching-civil-registry-electronic-data-with-paper-records>). [Al-Wasat reports that national numbers have been issued since 2011 through a unified electronic system supervised by the Civil Status Authority (Libya's own authority), with no foreign operator named (2011, before the three-year window)](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
