# Cape Verde

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** Cabo Verde is moving production of its identity documents home. [In September 2025 the government's Resolution 95/2025 authorised the national press, INCV, to take over buying, personalising and issuing the electronic ID card](<https://tiver.cv/incv-inicia-producao-de-documentos-eletronicos-de-identificacao/>), with the residence permit and e-passport. The press built a security printing plant for the work, financed with a state-backed loan. The change is meant to reduce dependence on outside suppliers.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the national ID system. The nearest held fact is [the resolution moving card production to the national press](<https://tiver.cv/incv-inicia-producao-de-documentos-eletronicos-de-identificacao/>), which says nothing of the system's upkeep.

**Platform owned.** No source found says whether the state owns the ID system's code. The nearest held fact is [the same resolution](<https://tiver.cv/incv-inicia-producao-de-documentos-eletronicos-de-identificacao/>).
