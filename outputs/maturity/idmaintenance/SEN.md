# Senegal

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The DAF, a national directorate, resumed making national identity cards on 1 April 2026](<https://aps.sn/la-daf-annonce-la-reprise-de-la-confection-des-cartes-nationales-didentite-depuis-le-1er-avril/>). Production had stopped after a cyberattack in February. [Its director said the identity-card databases had been preserved](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/senegal-daf-acknowledges-cyberattack-assures-no-data-was-compromised/>). [Its technical services restored the system](<https://aps.sn/piratage-informatique-la-daf-annonce-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-production-de-cartes-nationales-didentite/>). Card production and enrolment resumed together. [A former consul general wrote in November 2026 that card production has been centralised in Dakar at the DAF since 2017](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Contribution/cartes-nationales-didentite-a-letranger-quand-le-senegal-renouera-t-il-avec-la-production-dans-ses-consulats-par-elhadji-amadou-ndao_n_999003261.html>), while consulates take applications.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [The Malaysian operator Iris has produced the cards since 2016, under a rider ending on 21 May 2027](<https://senegaldirect.com/cartes-didentite-mouhamadou-moukhtar-cisse-explique-la-prorogation-exceptionnelle/>). The interior minister wants Senegalese firms to take over that skill.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [In September 2026 the Law Committee chair called for national control over citizens' data held by foreign operators](<https://senego.com/assemblee-coup-de-theatre-le-projet-de-loi-sur-la-carte-didentite-retourne-en-commission_2007288.html>). Nothing says who holds the code.
