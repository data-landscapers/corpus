# Nigeria

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [NIMC says the identity database is hosted in Nigeria and managed by NIMC employees, not vendors](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [Its own self-service platform had processed close to two million record updates by September 2026](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/>). [NIMC was enrolling 1.5 to 1.8 million people a month in mid-2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigeria-needs-62-5-million-nins-in-six-months-to-meet-target/>). NIMC runs the national ID programme under the ID4D project. [NIMC had issued about 124 million NINs by October 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2025/12/10/inside-nigerias-83m-bet-on-mosip-for-its-national-id/>).

**Maintained nationally.** [NIMC's Alvan Ikoku said in October 2026 that NIMC maintains its core database internally with its own staff](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). He said the database is hosted in Nigeria. No foreign supplier is named in its upkeep. [In April 2024 NIMC renewed Idemia's three-year contract to upgrade the biometric system and provide maintenance and support](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202404/idemia-upgrading-nigerias-national-biometric-system-to-handle-250m-ids>).

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [NIMC's October 2026 account covers hosting and staff](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). It does not say who holds the code. [In May 2026 the director-general said NIMC was moving from a vendor-locked system to an open-source, locally driven one](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/id4africa-fg-targets-cheaper-digital-identity-system-with-local-solution-nimc-dg/>). [The integrator for the move to MOSIP is to work with NIMC's technical team on transfer](<https://techcabal.com/2025/12/10/inside-nigerias-83m-bet-on-mosip-for-its-national-id/>).
