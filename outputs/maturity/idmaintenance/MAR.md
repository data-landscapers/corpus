# Morocco

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The DGSN issued nearly 4.7 million electronic national ID cards in 2024](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). It did so at its centres in Rabat and Marrakech. The DGSN is Morocco's national security directorate. It also ran the second phase of the electronic identity system that year. No foreign operator is named in the report.

**Maintained nationally.** [A Moroccan university unit, UM6P's MOSIP Centre of Excellence, holds the interior ministry's contract for support and code changes to the national population register](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>). [It has 17 staff and is moving the register to MOSIP's long-term-support version](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>). [The ministry seeks technological autonomy for the register through training in India and knowledge transfer](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>).

**Platform owned.** [The national population register runs on the open-source MOSIP platform](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>). [It was extended to all 83 provinces in October 2022](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>).
