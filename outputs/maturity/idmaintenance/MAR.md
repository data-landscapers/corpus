# Morocco

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The DGSN issued nearly 4.7 million electronic national ID cards in 2024](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). It did so at its centres in Rabat and Marrakech. The DGSN is Morocco's national security directorate. It also ran the second phase of the electronic identity system that year. No foreign operator is named in the report.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The DGSN's 2024 annual report counts cards issued](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). It does not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain the system. The report gives no maintenance contract either.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2024 report describes the second phase of the system](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). It does not say who holds the code.
