# Cameroon

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [A February 2025 decree gives the head of the national identity-title production centre the administration of the system](<https://www.prc.cm/files/7f/4b/5b/2ce7f303c8e3bd44fa83c05acf9ddbf9.pdf>). That covers its network, databases, applications and security. The centre is attached to the national police. [Its chief says the new card is now delivered within 48 hours](<https://www.voaafrique.com/a/les-cartes-d-identit%C3%A9-biom%C3%A9triques-d%C3%A9livr%C3%A9es-en-48h-d%C3%A9sormais-au-cameroun-/8004266.html>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says national staff maintain the system. [Augentic signed a 15-year build-operate-transfer contract with the police in May 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202405/cameroon-hands-15-year-biometric-id-card-contract-to-augentic>). It will hand the equipment and infrastructure over once its operating period ends.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [Augentic finances, designs, develops and installs the system](<https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-lentreprise-allemande-augentic-gmbh-va-produire-les-cni-en-48-heures/>), with about 70 million euros of investment. Nothing says the code passes to the state.
