# Congo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Finance Ministry relaunched production of the NIU card in September 2025](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>). Biometric data are captured with the holder present. [A 2024 decree has the cards produced only by national production centres at Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire and Oyo](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). Departmental section heads under the Interior Ministry issue them. The state therefore keeps card production in its own centres.

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The 2024 decree names the state's production centres](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). It does not say whether national staff or a supplier maintain the system. No foreign operator is named.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The Finance Ministry's 2025 article covers card production](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>). It does not say who holds the code.
