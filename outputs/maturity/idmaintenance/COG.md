# Congo

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Finance Ministry relaunched production of the NIU card from September 2025, with biometric capture in physical presence, per the ministry's own article](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>). [The decree has the cards produced only by the national production centres at Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire and Oyo and issued by departmental section heads under the Interior Ministry](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
