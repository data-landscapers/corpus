# Algeria

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** The Interior Ministry runs Algeria's identity and travel document platforms itself. [Its own engineers administer the platforms that collect and centrally process identity data](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). The ministry gave this account in May 2026. No foreign operator is named for the day-to-day running of the system.

**Maintained nationally.** [The ministry says its strategy was to act as integrator of solutions rather than buy turnkey ones](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). Under that approach its own engineers administer the platforms. It reported this in May 2026. The upkeep of the system therefore sits with ministry staff.

**Platform owned.** [The central collection and processing systems were developed by Algerian engineers](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>), the ministry said in May 2026. The state therefore holds a system built in-house. No foreign supplier is named as the owner of its code.
