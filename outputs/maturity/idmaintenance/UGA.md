# Uganda

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [NIRA, the national authority, operates the register and card issuance, with 418 of 571 posts filled](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/>). [The Registration of Persons Act, 2015 mandates NIRA to create, manage, maintain and operate the National Identification Register and issue national IDs, as the Independent reports](<https://www.independent.co.ug/new-id-rollout-hits-qr-code-verification-hurdle/>). [The Prime Minister's plan to expand NIRA's staff from 571 to 997 employees, three officers per district; NIRA itself acknowledged the QR code had not been integrated into its digital operational system](<https://techbuild.africa/uganda-national-ids-broken-qr-codes-fix-is-five/>). [NIRA, the national authority, created NSIS in 2014/15 and developed its birth and death registration module itself, using open source OpenCRVS, keeping core personnel throughout](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**Maintained nationally.** [NIRA itself built into the system the capability to issue two distinct ID cards, one for citizens and one for foreign nationals, to comply with Ugandan law](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).

**Platform owned.** [NIRA has completed migration of its national ID records to the open-source MOSIP platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/brownfield-implementations-represent-mosips-next-wave-of-national-digital-ids>).
