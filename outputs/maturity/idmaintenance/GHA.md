# Ghana

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The National Identification Authority sets fees and runs first-time registration, card replacement and record updates](<https://nia.gov.gh/fees-and-charges-2026/>). It also runs mobile registration at its district offices and Premium Centres. It set out these services in January 2026. The authority runs these services through its own offices. [In September 2025 the NIA cleared a backlog of 640,000 cards by printing at its district offices](<https://metrotvonline.com/640000-ghana-card-backlog-cleared-after-fresh-talks-with-private-partner-nia-boss/>), with blank cards released by its private partner. [Margins Group told MPs in 2024 that it built the registration system while the NIA operated it](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/politics/ghana-news-parliament-cttees-commend-margins-id-group-for-ghana-card-investment.html>). [Its homepage describes it as the statutory body, under Act 707, that creates and maintains the Ghana Card](<https://nia.gov.gh/>).

**Maintained nationally.** [The NIA's own PKI team carried out updates to the Ghana Card registration system in 2026](<https://www.classfmonline.com/news/NIA-announces-temporary-nationwide-service-disruption-for-system-updates-74362>). Services were due to resume on 10 May 2026 after the work. No foreign supplier is named in the account.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The 2026 update notice names the NIA's own PKI team](<https://www.classfmonline.com/news/NIA-announces-temporary-nationwide-service-disruption-for-system-updates-74362>). It does not say who holds the code. No foreign supplier is named as owner of the platform.
