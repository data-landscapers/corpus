# Cote d'Ivoire

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [ONECI, under the Interior Ministry, manages the national population register and assigns the identification number](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). The PARAE coordinator said so in October 2026. [ONECI is the only body authorised to issue the secure national identity card](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/124119-grand-bassam-le-calvaire-des-demandeurs-de-la-carte-nationale-d-identite>). It does so through enrolment centres such as Grand-Bassam. [ONECI said in May 2026 that the RNPP has run since 2019 as a public-private partnership with Semlex as operator](<https://sitetest.oneci.ci/actualites/2026/05/07/1778155208-mise-en-uvre-du-rnpp-loneci-et-ses-partenaires-dfinissent-les-orientations-stratgiques-2026>). [It is described as the authority for enrolment, card production and identity management](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-2-Cote-d-Ivoire-FINAL.pdf>). [MOSIP trained Ivorian staff in 2025 for a pilot](<https://www.mosip.io/pdf/MOSIP-Rewind-2025.pdf>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [ONECI's director general said in May 2026 that the national databases are maintained with money from ONECI's own budget](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/121311-id4africa-2026-l-oneci-presente-les-avancees-du-systeme-ivoirien-d-identification-numerique>). He did not say whether ONECI's own staff or a supplier carry out the work.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The director general's account of May 2026 covers funding and upkeep only](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/121311-id4africa-2026-l-oneci-presente-les-avancees-du-systeme-ivoirien-d-identification-numerique>). It says nothing of who holds the code.
