# Cote d'Ivoire

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [ONECI, under the Interior Ministry, manages the RNPP and attributes the national identification number, per the PARAE coordinator](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). [ONECI, the Office national de l'etat civil et de l'identification, is the only body authorised to issue the secure national identity card, through enrolment centres such as Grand-Bassam](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/124119-grand-bassam-le-calvaire-des-demandeurs-de-la-carte-nationale-d-identite>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Maintained nationally.** [Linfodrome quotes ONECI's director general saying the national databases are maintained with an investment taken from ONECI's own budget](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/121311-id4africa-2026-l-oneci-presente-les-avancees-du-systeme-ivoirien-d-identification-numerique>).
