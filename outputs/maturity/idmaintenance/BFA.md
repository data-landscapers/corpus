# Burkina Faso

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The Office national d'identification (ONI) runs the enrolment of the AES biometric identity card (CIB-AES), opening nine extra sites in Ouagadougou beside its main centre and setting delivery at 48 hours, per an ONI communiqué](<https://www.fasoamazone.net/2026/09/15/id-day-2026-loni-ouvre-plusieurs-sites-denrolement-a-ouagadougou-face-a-laffluence-pour-la-cib-aes/>). [The ONI, the national identification office, runs CIB-AES enrolment and production, with its own production centres in regional capitals](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-les-operations-detablissement-lancees-a-koudougou/>). [The Police Nationale page says the ONI runs the CNIB checking and issuing system, with its own production centre and intermediate data-processing centres](<https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib>). [The ONI will begin mass production of the CIB-AES card from January 2026](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-au-burkina-faso-le-capitaine-ibrahim-traore-pour-commencer/>).
