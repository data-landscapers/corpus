# Burkina Faso

## National maintenance of id and credentials systems

**Operated nationally.** [The national identification office, ONI, runs enrolment for the AES biometric identity card](<https://www.fasoamazone.net/2026/09/15/id-day-2026-loni-ouvre-plusieurs-sites-denrolement-a-ouagadougou-face-a-laffluence-pour-la-cib-aes/>). In September 2026 it opened nine extra sites in Ouagadougou and set delivery at 48 hours. [It runs its own production centres in regional capitals](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-les-operations-detablissement-lancees-a-koudougou/>). [It also runs the checking and issuing system for the older CNIB card](<https://www.police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/cnib>). [A new ONI antenna at Koudougou opened at the end of 2023 with 30 production agents](<https://oni.bf/antenne-centre-ouest/>).

**Maintained nationally.** No source found says who maintains the system. [The ONI's 2026 communiqués describe enrolment and production only](<https://www.fasoamazone.net/2026/09/15/id-day-2026-loni-ouvre-plusieurs-sites-denrolement-a-ouagadougou-face-a-laffluence-pour-la-cib-aes/>). They do not say whether its own staff or a supplier maintain it.

**Platform owned.** No source found says the system runs on code the state owns. [The ONI's production centres are its own](<https://www.sidwaya.info/carte-didentite-biometrique-aes-les-operations-detablissement-lancees-a-koudougou/>). Nothing says who holds the code.
