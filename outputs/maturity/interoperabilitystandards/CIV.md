# Cote d'Ivoire

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Côte d'Ivoire's general interoperability referential is [annexed to decree 2021-913 of 22 December 2021](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/28/décret_2021-913_du_22_décembre_2021_adoption_référentiel_général_dinteropérabilité_systèmes_dinformation.pdf>), which adopts it. The referential is a list of reference standards for the administration's electronic systems. It covers network and messaging protocols, SOAP and REST web services, data formats and encodings including XML, JSON, XSD, PDF/A and UTF-8, geospatial formats, pivot data formats and a set of semantic key terms.

**Standards required and checked.** [Article 3 of the decree](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/28/décret_2021-913_du_22_décembre_2021_adoption_référentiel_général_dinteropérabilité_systèmes_dinformation.pdf>) says public bodies are bound to conform to the referential. That makes its technical, syntactic and semantic interoperability standards compulsory for the information systems those bodies run. The duty dates from the decree itself, 22 December 2021, and applies to the standards listed in its annex.
