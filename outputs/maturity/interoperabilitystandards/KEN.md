# Kenya

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Kenya's standards are in draft. [The ICT Authority's second edition of the Government Enterprise Architecture and Government Interoperability Framework](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/GEA%20-%20GIF%202nd%20Edition.pdf>), dated 2023, sets out an interoperability charter and data, technical and metadata standards for exchanging data between government agencies. Its approval date and document history are blank. [At a workshop with ministries, departments and agencies on 6 and 7 November 2025](<https://kdeap.icta.go.ke/kenya-advances-digital-government-gea-interoperability-workshop-held-at-kicc/>), the Authority said it would develop and finalise the national enterprise architecture standards and the interoperability framework.

**Standards required and checked.** [The second edition](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/GEA%20-%20GIF%202nd%20Edition.pdf>) says in its scope that it is to be adopted by the whole of government: ministries, counties and agencies. The workshop of November 2025 was held under the Authority's KDEAP programme, and brought those ministries, departments and agencies together for two days on the architecture and the framework.
