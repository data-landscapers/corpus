# DR Congo

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo has draft standards for the public sector. [A ministry workshop on 2 and 3 February 2022](<https://medd.gouv.cd/atelier-sur-lelaboration-et-adoption-des-referentiels-numeriques-du-secteur-public-en-rdc/>) validated five books of digital standards for the public sector, among them a framework for a common data reference architecture and a general interoperability framework meant to let public information systems exchange data in real time. The books were still to be tidied and promulgated. [The government adopted the draft digital norms and standards on 15 April 2022](<https://kt.cd/2022/04/16/adoption-du-projet-des-normes-et-standards-du-numerique-applicable-au-secteur-public/>), in the same five books.

**Standards required and checked.** [The report of the April 2022 adoption](<https://kt.cd/2022/04/16/adoption-du-projet-des-normes-et-standards-du-numerique-applicable-au-secteur-public/>) says legal force was still to be given to the standards by ministerial order. Until that order is made the five books bind no public body.
