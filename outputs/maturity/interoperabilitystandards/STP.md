# Sao Tome and Principe

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** [Lei 1/2024, passed by the National Assembly on 3 November 2023, promulgated on 30 November and gazetted on 29 January 2024](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26>), institutes the National Interoperability Framework (QNI). The framework is a minimum set of premises, policies and technical specifications governing how ICT is used to make e-government services interoperate. It applies to all public institutions and took effect 30 days after publication. [Its annex sets the specifications out by segment, from interconnection to security, at version 1.2](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26>). It names the reference standard for each component, such as the open cloud computing interface (OCCI).

**Standards required and checked.** [The law grades each specification as mandatory or recommended](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26>). A mandatory item has been adopted by the government as a norm after formal approval and must be adopted in every new IT product or project; a recommended item is suggested but not compulsory. [A transitional article keeps existing public-service systems valid until they are updated or replaced to conform to the framework](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=26>).
