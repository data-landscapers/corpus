# Angola

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Angola has a draft and no published standards. [Terms of reference issued by the government agency IMA on 9 February 2026](<https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf>) report that a seven-month study with 25 public institutions produced a Framework and Roadmap of Interoperability for the public administration, with organisational, semantic, technical and infrastructure guidelines and a phased roadmap. The document names no instrument adopting the framework. [The same study](<https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf>) found that 27% of the institutions' systems were interconnected, mostly point to point, that governance was fragmented and that the institutions shared no common standards.

**Standards required and checked.** [The terms of reference](<https://sys.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_R_Consultoria_para_Implementacao_da_Interoperabilidade_da_Administracao_Publica_1_13f0252ef0.pdf>) name no decree making the framework compulsory. They were issued to hire a consultant to put interoperability into effect across the public administration, on the basis of the framework and its roadmap.

***Also on file***. [Interoperability Framework and roadmap (Quadro e Roteiro de Interoperabilidade)](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>), an interoperability framework that no source found says sets technical standards.

***Not found***. No source found says the standards have been published.
