# Mauritania

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Mauritania is writing its standards. [The digital transformation ministry held a three-day workshop, reported on 27 March 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/mauritania-moves-to-standardize-public-digital-services-with-national-framework-initiative/>), to develop a unified framework of standards for public digital services, intended to make those services more interoperable. The ministry presented a technical study and a mechanism for putting the framework into effect, and asked for stakeholders' comments before full adoption.

**Standards required and checked.** [The framework](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/27/mauritania-moves-to-standardize-public-digital-services-with-national-framework-initiative/>) had not been adopted when the workshop was reported. The ministry described full adoption as the step that follows the stakeholders' comments on the technical study and on the mechanism for putting the framework into effect. The workshop ran for three days, and the report of 27 March 2026 does not give its dates.
