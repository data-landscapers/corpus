# Mozambique

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Mozambique's Electronic Government Systems Interoperability Regulation sets standards for integrating and managing public information systems, [the chairman of the national ICT institute INTIC said in a report of 5 December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/05/mozambique-prioritizes-data-governance-as-cornerstone-of-digital-sovereignty/>), when he announced that a thorough review of the regulation was complete. [Ecofin Agency reported on 24 September 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2409-59214-mozambique-plans-unified-digital-public-services-platform-by-2029>) that the country was revising its 2017 interoperability framework to strengthen technical standards, the governance of data exchange, data protection and cybersecurity.

**Standards required and checked.** [The revision reported in September 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2409-59214-mozambique-plans-unified-digital-public-services-platform-by-2029>) was still in progress. It is aimed at the technical standards as well as at the governance of data exchange, data protection and cybersecurity, and it follows the review that INTIC's chairman announced nine months earlier.
