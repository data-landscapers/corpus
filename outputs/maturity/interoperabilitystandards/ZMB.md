# Zambia

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Zambia's e-Government Division, the Smart Zambia Institute, issued [the Public Service ICT Electronic Government Interoperability Standard, first edition, in February 2019](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/ZMB588.pdf>). The standard is meant to enable the exchange of data between the government information systems of different public bodies. It sets principles and names data interoperability and technical interoperability guidelines within its scope.

**Standards required and checked.** [The standard](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/ZMB588.pdf>) is at version 1.0, marked final, and is issued for the public service. It is the division's own document, and it sets out what government information systems should do in order to exchange data with one another. The text describes itself as the electronic government interoperability framework for all ministries, provinces and spending agencies, and the first edition of February 2019 is the one it issued.

***Also on file***. [National Public Key Infrastructure (NPKI)](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-national-digital-trust-infrastructure/VgZeyqJlpzp7djX9>).

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree, regulation or circular requires public bodies to use the standards.
