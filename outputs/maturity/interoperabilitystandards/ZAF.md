# South Africa

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** South Africa is updating its Minimum Interoperability Standards. [The Operation Vulindlela progress report for the first quarter of 2026/27](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf>), published on 31 July 2026, says work progressed between April and June 2026 on updating the standards, which it describes as the common standards needed to connect public-sector systems. The report does not list the data formats or interfaces the standards cover.

**Standards required and checked.** [The same report](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/Operation%20Vulindlela%20Progress%20Report%20Q1%20-%202026.pdf>) says the updated standards were due to be tested in priority digital service initiatives by September 2026. That testing is of the updated standards themselves, in the services chosen to try them. The report comes from the programme run by the National Treasury and the Presidency, and covers the quarter from April to June 2026.

***Not held***. No source held says the standards have been published.
