# Guinea-Bissau

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Guinea-Bissau's National Regulation on Interoperability of Digital Systems and Platforms was [published in the Boletim Oficial on 31 October 2025 as Decree 27/2025](<https://wardip.gw/governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-para-acelerar-a-transformacao-digital-na-guine-bissau/>). The digital integration programme WARDIP, which reported the decree, lists among the regulation's objectives the establishment of technical standards for developing interoperable digital solutions and the harmonising of communication between systems.

**Standards required and checked.** [The regulation](<https://wardip.gw/governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-para-acelerar-a-transformacao-digital-na-guine-bissau/>) is a decree of the government and covers the digital systems and platforms of both public and private services. WARDIP's report gives the regulation's objectives and its publication in the official bulletin, and does not list the standards themselves. The decree was approved by the government and published in the bulletin on the same date the report gives, 31 October 2025.
