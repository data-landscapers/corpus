# Senegal

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Senegal's standards are being prepared. [The digital ministry announced that on 5 May 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_dpi-newdealtechnologique-activity-7457180609757528065-UqRT>) it would present the draft decree governing the interoperability platform and introduce the national interoperability framework, with its data-exchange architectures, technical standards and security requirements. Both the decree and the framework were at the stage of presentation.

**Standards required and checked.** [The draft decree](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_dpi-newdealtechnologique-activity-7457180609757528065-UqRT>) had not been adopted when the ministry made its announcement. The ministry gave 5 May 2026 as the day of the presentation. The decree is the instrument that would govern the platform through which public bodies exchange data, and the framework introduced beside it carries the technical standards. The ministry presented the two together, on the same day, as the foundations of the country's digital public infrastructure.
