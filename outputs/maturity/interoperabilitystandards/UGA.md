# Uganda

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** [The national IT authority's e-Government Interoperability Framework, e-GIF-2026, dated 15 April 2026](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>), sets the prerequisites and guidelines for integrating government systems, offering services online and sharing data across government; it is the country's second national e-GIF. [Among its standards are DCAT for metadata, so that systems describe data the same way, machine-readable ODRL policies stating how, when and by whom data may be used, and the reuse of data meanings from the base registries](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>). It covers all ministries, departments, agencies and local governments, and states that interoperability is a prerequisite of every new IT project.

**Standards required and checked.** [The framework calls itself mandatory for all public-sector institutions and says adherence to its standards and guidelines is mandatory](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>), but that is the text speaking of itself: no law, decree, regulation or circular making the standards compulsory has been found. No source found names a system tested or certified against them.
