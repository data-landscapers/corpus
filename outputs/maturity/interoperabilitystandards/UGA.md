# Uganda

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** [The national IT authority's e-Government Interoperability Framework, e-GIF-2026, dated 15 April 2026](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>), sets the prerequisites and guidelines for integrating government systems, offering services online and sharing data across government. It is the country's second national e-GIF. [Among its standards is DCAT for metadata, so that systems describe data the same way](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>). It also sets machine-readable ODRL policies stating how, when and by whom data may be used. It provides for the reuse of data meanings from the base registries. It covers all ministries, departments, agencies and local governments, and states that interoperability is a prerequisite of every new IT project.

**Standards required and checked.** [The framework calls itself mandatory for all public-sector institutions and says adherence to its standards and guidelines is mandatory](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Uganda%20e-Government%20Interoperability%20Framework%20%28e-GIF%29-2026.pdf>). That is the text speaking of itself. No law, decree, regulation or circular making the standards compulsory has been found. No source found names a system tested or certified against them.
