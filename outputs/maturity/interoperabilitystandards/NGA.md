# Nigeria

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Nigeria is writing a data exchange standard. [In September 2025 NITDA held a stakeholders' review of the draft Technical Standard for Nigeria Data Exchange](<https://thenationonlineng.net/fg-to-begin-implementation-of-digital-public-infrastructure-data-exchange-standards/>), known as NGDX. The Nigeria Governors Forum was among the reviewers, so that states and local governments could align with it. The same workshop, held in Abuja, reviewed draft regulations for digital public infrastructure. NITDA said the regulators were working together to finish the drafts so that they would be jointly owned.

**Standards required and checked.** [NITDA's Director General said implementation of the standard could begin in the first quarter of 2026](<https://thenationonlineng.net/fg-to-begin-implementation-of-digital-public-infrastructure-data-exchange-standards/>), once the review was done. He wanted states and the federal government to work to one common standard. The data protection commissioner said the standard would let data be exchanged securely between public and private sectors.
