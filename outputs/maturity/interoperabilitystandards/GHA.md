# Ghana

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Ghana's standards are a draft. [Version 2.0 of the eGovernment Interoperability Framework](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>), from the National Information Technology Agency and dated 2022 by its publication, is labelled a draft for stakeholder review. It sets standards for government systems in technical annexes on the interoperability of data, networks, service-oriented architecture, channels, business processes, security and applications.

**Standards required and checked.** [Version 2.0](<https://nita.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Ghana-eGovernment-Interoperability-Framework-eGIF-Version-2.0_v.Draft-for-Stakeholder-Review-1.pdf>) is a text for stakeholders to review. Its annexes set out the standards government systems would meet in each of the seven areas, and the text was circulated for comment before any final version. The draft updates the framework the agency issued earlier, and it is the agency's text, published on its own website for that review. The framework it updates, adopted in 2008, was the government's first.
