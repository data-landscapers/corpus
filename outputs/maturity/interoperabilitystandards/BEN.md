# Benin

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Benin's interoperability framework, [published by the digital agency ASIN and instituted by decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020](<https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download>), sets recommendations and names the technical standards government information systems are to use, among them W3C web service and XML standards, the ODF document format and the JPEG and PNG image formats, with a catalogue of interoperability solutions. [A legal note from ASIN in July 2026](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>) treats the framework as in place and says it sets the registration, technical and contractual conditions for connecting to the government's exchange platform. [A consultation workshop in Cotonou on 30 June 2026](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/transformation-numerique-de-letat-un-cadre-commun-pour-ses-systemes-dinformation-en-preparation>) examined a draft policy on the architecture of the State's information systems, which sets technical rules and standards for making public systems interoperable.

**Standards required and checked.** [The framework](<https://api.xroad.bj/documents/10/download>) was instituted by decree in 2020 and is addressed to administrations that provide a public service.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree, regulation or circular requires public bodies to use the standards. The decree that instituted the framework is not held.
