# Libya

## Technical standards for government systems

**Standards published.** Libya has no common technical standards for its government systems. [A paper published on 30 June 2026](<https://cjos.histr.edu.ly/index.php/journal/en/article/view/2167>) sets out a national government interoperability platform, which it calls Nawa. Its abstract says that Libyan government institutions lack unified interoperability standards and that their information systems are fragmented. The statement is dated by the paper's publication.

**Standards required and checked.** With no standards in place, there is nothing public bodies are required to use and nothing against which a system could be checked. [The paper](<https://cjos.histr.edu.ly/index.php/journal/en/article/view/2167>) presents its platform as a step towards a national means of exchange between government institutions, and gives the lack of unified standards and the fragmentation of systems as the state of things in which it is proposed.
