# Niger

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's monitoring guide of December 2023 [makes its statistics directorate responsible for the monthly health information reports](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>). Written for the health plan 2022-2026, the guide [names DHIS2 as the base for routine health data](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>). The 2019 e-health strategy [expected USD 1.00 million of the system's USD 1.20 million from partners](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>). It planned nothing from the ministry.

**Tiers in use.** The guide [has every health establishment key its monthly report into DHIS2](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>). It names integrated health centres (CSI) and hospitals as sources. Health posts [send their data to their CSI, where it is checked and keyed](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** Under the guide's circuit of December 2023, [each establishment validates its monthly report and keys it into DHIS2](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>). The CSI does so by the 15th of the following month. DHIS2 [has been online since September 2016](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** OpenFn reports that [workflows moving STELab laboratory results and CommCare community data into DHIS2 were running by May 2026](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/connecting-nigers-health-information-systems-with-datafi>). They sit on a ministry-hosted server, and ministry staff were trained to run them that month.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The guide gives [CSI report completeness of 97.90% and timeliness of 54.63% for 2021](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>), as reports transmitted over reports expected. In September 2018 the ministry [counted 1,026 integrated health centres and 2,443 health huts](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker for the Men5CV coverage survey](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>). Single-function: [Estelab](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>), also written [STELab](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/connecting-nigers-health-information-systems-with-datafi>), and the [weekly notifiable-disease reporting in DHIS2](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>). Also the [DHIS2 campaign monitoring module](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>) and the ministry's [e-surveillance platform](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>). Community: [CommCare and the eCHIS](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/connecting-nigers-health-information-systems-with-datafi>), whose data now flows into DHIS2. Also [mHealth](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-est-utilise-a-goure-a-plus-de-1000-km-de-niamey/>) and its [mHealth Monitor dashboard](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-est-utilise-a-goure-a-plus-de-1000-km-de-niamey/>) in Gouré. Two mobile follow-up projects ran from 2013 to 2016, [one for child health](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>) and [one for acute malnutrition](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>). Enabling: the [maintenance management system (GMAO)](<https://joghep.scholasticahq.com/article/144373-integrating-specialised-services-into-primary-health-care-in-niger-from-fragmentation-to-coordination>) and [telemedicine at referral hospitals](<https://joghep.scholasticahq.com/article/144373-integrating-specialised-services-into-primary-health-care-in-niger-from-fragmentation-to-coordination>). Also the [national telemedicine project](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>) and the [national government network](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>).

***Not found***. No count or share of integrated health centres keying their own reports is published; the 2021 rates count reports transmitted. No financing dated within the last three years has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The mid-term evaluation for Enabel, of November 2024, states that [the Enabel project PASS Sutura started the digitalisation with recruited consultancy firms, with the Ministry of Health's digital health directorate involved](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/19/download/81>). PASS Sutura is [a Belgian bilateral project with EUR 14.5 million for health over 2022-2026, and a Netherlands co-financing of EUR 3.2 million to 31 March 2025 puts particular emphasis on digitalisation](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/19/download/81>). On 8 July 2026 the health minister [opened the workshop validating the costed roadmap of the Schéma Directeur National de la e-Santé 2025-2029](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>).

**Tiers in use.** By November 2024 the project [had set up digitalisation, including computerised medical records and billing, in the integrated health centres (CSI) and district hospitals of Dioundiou, Gothèye and Gaya](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/19/download/81>).

**Where data is digitised.** Enabel states that [a hospital information system built around a computerised medical record was put in place at the district hospitals of Gaya and Gothèye after field missions in February and April 2024](<https://open.enabel.be/en/NER/2570/2751/u/le-niger-avance-vers-la-digitalisation-des-services-de-sant-avec-le-projet-pass-sutura.html>).

**Last twelve months.** At the workshop of 8 July 2026 the Directorate of e-Santé [presented OpenClinic, its project for a hospital information system and computerised patient record, as already under way](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The evaluation of November 2024 [places the digitalised health centres in the project's three target health districts only](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/19/download/81>). In September 2018 the ministry's strategy [reported electronic patient record activity in two health districts, Gaya and Gothèye, of 72](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker module for the Men5CV post-campaign coverage survey](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>). Single-function: [Estelab](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>), the [weekly notifiable-disease reporting in DHIS2](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/02/Guide-de-suivi-evaluation-du-PDSS_2022-2026_MoH_2023.x69485.pdf>), the [DHIS2 intra-campaign monitoring module](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>) and the ministry's [plateforme e-surveillance](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>). Community: [mHealth](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-est-utilise-a-goure-a-plus-de-1000-km-de-niamey/>) and its [mHealth Monitor dashboard](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-est-utilise-a-goure-a-plus-de-1000-km-de-niamey/>) in Gouré, the [World Vision and University of Toronto mobile child-health follow-up](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>) of 2014 to 2016 and the [Save the Children and USAID mobile follow-up of acute malnutrition](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>) of 2013 to 2016. Enabling: the [Computerised Maintenance and Management System (GMAO)](<https://joghep.scholasticahq.com/article/144373-integrating-specialised-services-into-primary-health-care-in-niger-from-fragmentation-to-coordination>), [telemedicine at referral hospitals](<https://joghep.scholasticahq.com/article/144373-integrating-specialised-services-into-primary-health-care-in-niger-from-fragmentation-to-coordination>), the [projet national de télémédecine](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>) and the [Réseau Informatique National de l'Administration](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/NER_Niger_National-eHealth-Strategy_2019-2023.pdf>).

***Not found***. No count of health centres using the computerised record has been found, and no denominator. Nothing found says whether the record is made at the point of care or keyed afterwards, and no sites or counts are given for OpenClinic.
