# Malawi

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [has collected aggregated facility data since 2002 through a unified HMIS under its Central Monitoring and Evaluation Division, which runs on DHIS2](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>), on a study published in December 2024. The Global Fund's Inspector General reported in late 2023 that [the Fund had invested US$21 million in the system since Grant Cycle 4](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** As of January 2023, [1,266 health facilities, government-owned and private, were reporting in the HMIS on DHIS2](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>). Countdown to 2030, with the ministry, puts [completeness of monthly facility reporting in DHIS2 at 97% of expected reports nationally in 2025, up from 83% in 2020](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Malawi.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** An August 2023 survey of six facilities and three district health offices found that [facilities compile monthly reports on paper and deliver them to the district health office, where the data are entered on computer](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12539031/>). A study published in December 2024 states that [paper forms are compiled at facilities and entered into DHIS2 at the district HMIS office](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>).

**Last twelve months.** A project that [began in December 2025 and runs to March 2027 will deploy DHIS2 climate and health tools on the national HMIS, first in four districts](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [expanding DHIS2 Mobile to the 24 remaining districts in 2023 allows direct data entry into DHIS2 by all health centres](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Annual%20Report%202023-24%20FY_FINAL_06122024docx%201.pdf>). Neno district [began moving DHIS2 data entry to its health centres in November 2022](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [TB Electronic Medical Record system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>) and the HIV programme's [Open EMR](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>). Single-function: the [national disease surveillance and reporting systems](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/ichis-transforming-community-health-service-delivery-through-innovation-21-06-2026>), the [One Health Surveillance Platform (OHSP)](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>), the [Early Warning, Alert and Response (EWARS) system](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>), the [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) weekly report](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>), [OpenLMIS](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Annual%20Report%202023-24%20FY_FINAL_06122024docx%201.pdf>), [iHRIS](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Annual%20Report%202023-24%20FY_FINAL_06122024docx%201.pdf>), the [Electronic Health Information Network (eHIN)](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>) and the [warehouse management system at the Central Medical Stores Trust](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>). Community: the [integrated Community Health Information System (iCHIS)](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/ichis-transforming-community-health-service-delivery-through-innovation-21-06-2026>). Enabling: the [Government Wide Area Network (GWAN)](<https://health.bmz.de/stories/linking-rural-health-centres-up-with-malawis-digital-health-architecture/>).

***Not held***. No count or share of health centres entering their own reports is published, and whether health centres key their reports in practice is not established. No current plan naming the system is held.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and Sanitation [has rolled out the Malawi Health Care Information System, which it calls Mahis](<https://mwnation.com/digital-health-records-system-in-pilot-phase/>), as reported on 3 September 2026. The Digital Health Governance Framework 2025-2030, signed in June 2025, [sets the system's nationwide scale-up across public and private facilities](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Digital%20Health%20Governance%20Framework%20Malawi%20Signed.pdf>) and states that [digital health in Malawi continues to rely heavily on donor funding](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Digital%20Health%20Governance%20Framework%20Malawi%20Signed.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** On 3 September 2026 Mahis was [in pilot at Queen Elizabeth Central Hospital in Blantyre and at selected health centres nationwide](<https://mwnation.com/digital-health-records-system-in-pilot-phase/>).

**Where data is digitised.** A newspaper's check of the hospital's outpatient department on 3 September 2026 found that [registered patients no longer produced health passport books, and first-time patients had their details captured at the registry and were issued a barcode](<https://mwnation.com/digital-health-records-system-in-pilot-phase/>). The ministry says [each patient gets a unique identifier held on a server that facilities reach through an interoperability layer](<https://mwnation.com/digital-health-records-system-in-pilot-phase/>). In Zomba District in September 2024, [most facilities, particularly rural government clinics, kept no out-patient files, so the paper Health Passport was the only medical record for most people](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0>).

**Last twelve months.** In April 2026 [the link between the iCHIS community module for non-communicable diseases and MaHIS at health facilities was being finalised](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Mahis was [under pilot at selected health centres nationwide](<https://mwnation.com/digital-health-records-system-in-pilot-phase/>) on 3 September 2026. In 2025 the ministry [piloted linking the National ID to electronic medical record systems in five health facilities in Karonga District](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

Trackers: the [TB Electronic Medical Record system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>) and the HIV programme's [Open EMR](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>). Single-function: the [national disease surveillance and reporting systems](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/ichis-transforming-community-health-service-delivery-through-innovation-21-06-2026>), the [One Health Surveillance Platform (OHSP)](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>), the [Early Warning, Alert and Response (EWARS) system](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>), the [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR) weekly report](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622671/>), [OpenLMIS](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Annual%20Report%202023-24%20FY_FINAL_06122024docx%201.pdf>), [iHRIS](<https://health.gov.mw/storage/resources/Annual%20Report%202023-24%20FY_FINAL_06122024docx%201.pdf>), the [Electronic Health Information Network (eHIN)](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>) and the [warehouse management system at the Central Medical Stores Trust](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/13744/oig_gf-oig-24-007_report_en.pdf>).
