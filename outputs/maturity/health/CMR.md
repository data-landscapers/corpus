# Cameroon

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Public Health's digital health plan for 2026 to 2030 [calls DHIS2 the ministry's national health information system, adopted in 2017](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>), and [costs work on its management plan and interoperability framework](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>). The ministry states that under the 2020-2024 plan [DHIS2 was financed through partner conventions, with no dedicated national digital health budget](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The national statistics institute states that [DHIS2 carries aggregate data from the monthly activity reports of all health facilities across the national territory](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>), and used those reports for 2018 to 2022. The ministry's DHIS2 plan of April 2022 [counts 197 health districts and 6,200 facilities](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/7f4eeb8b-67fe-4692-b639-3c43e8a3580c/content>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's DHIS2 plan of April 2022 states that [a nationwide HMIS is in place for aggregate data, with data entry at the facility level](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/7f4eeb8b-67fe-4692-b639-3c43e8a3580c/content>). Facilities [still fill paper registers and monthly reporting booklets, which the plan budgets to reprint for 6,200 facilities](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/7f4eeb8b-67fe-4692-b639-3c43e8a3580c/content>).

**Last twelve months.** The ministry [published its digital health plan for 2026 to 2030 in March 2026, with 2026 budget lines for DHIS2 deployment, maintenance, servers and interoperability](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's family health directorate gives [completeness of monthly activity reports in DHIS2 as 96% nationally for October to December 2025, and timeliness as 89%](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/2bc60008-7482-4a7c-842d-15f112a2efd7/content>), read from DHIS2 on 23 February 2026.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Public Health's digital health plan for 2026 to 2030 [budgets for 2026 the selection of a partner and a public-private partnership contract to deploy an electronic medical record](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>), and names the record among [projects that drew on partner financing under the 2020-2024 plan](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>). SmartMobileClinic, launched in 2022, [is run by the German association Mapubi e.V. with the Health and Research Organisation Cameroon](<https://diasporafordevelopment.eu/cpt_actions/smartmobileclinic-expanding-telehealth-services-in-rural-communities-in-cameroon/>).

**Tiers in use.** At Yaoundé Central Hospital, a national referral hospital, [a clinical information system built on MediBoard was deployed in the gynaecology-obstetrics department and counted 113 users and 1,278 patient records after six months](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10620639/>), on its authors' account published in October 2023. Staff [stopped entering data when the per-entry payment ended](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10620639/>).

**Where data is digitised.** In that department [data were entered on computers in consultation and treatment rooms, with paper service registers kept in parallel](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10620639/>). EcoMatin reported in September 2023 that [in almost all public hospitals patient files are kept on paper](<https://ecomatin.net/le-gouvernement-en-quete-dune-expertise-etrangere-pour-operationnaliser-la-digitalisation-des-dossiers-medicaux>).

**Last twelve months.** From September 2025 [SmartMobileClinic was extended to further rural communities, with 12 rural campaigns between November 2025 and January 2026](<https://diasporafordevelopment.eu/cpt_actions/smartmobileclinic-expanding-telehealth-services-in-rural-communities-in-cameroon/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [its electronic medical record, which it calls the DME and runs on Bahmni, was deployed in about fifty pilot health facilities during the 2020-2024 plan](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>). The SmartMobileClinic project [runs campaigns with local health facilities in 12 rural communities](<https://diasporafordevelopment.eu/cpt_actions/smartmobileclinic-expanding-telehealth-services-in-rural-communities-in-cameroon/>).
