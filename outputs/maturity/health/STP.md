# São Tomé and Príncipe

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [launched DHIS2 as the health information system on 18 December 2019](<https://minsaude.st/sis/>). Its National Health Development Plan 2023-2032, dated September 2022, [carries a costed health information pillar that names DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>) and states that the system [has the support of WHO, Gavi, UNFPA and UNICEF](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan of September 2022 states that the ministry approved DHIS2 for nationwide use and that, two years after implementation, [its use covered only the vaccination, reproductive health, tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS and malaria programmes](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's page on the 2019 launch describes DHIS2 as [a web platform for digital forms and indicators, with data stored at national level](<https://minsaude.st/sis/>).

**Last twelve months.** The WHO country office reports that during the 2024-2025 biennium [DHIS2 was implemented in all districts and district health managers were trained on the platform, on data quality and on report generation](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/report_2024-25_WHO_STP_0.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The plan of September 2022 lists [focal points in health units and equipment for health centres to run DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>) among its actions, and states that [health professionals were not adhering to the system even where it was available](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The ministry's [DHIS2 Tracker](<https://minsaude.st/sis/>), the [HIV Patient Tracking, HIV testing (DIPS) and TB Patient Tracking modules](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>), the [Malaria Notification and Investigation, COVID-19 surveillance and Death Registry modules](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>), [integrated disease surveillance and response data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-08/WHO%20Cooperation%20Strategy%202023-2027%20with%20Sao%20Tome%20and%20Principe_July%202023_1.pdf>), [mSupply](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-08/WHO%20Cooperation%20Strategy%202023-2027%20with%20Sao%20Tome%20and%20Principe_July%202023_1.pdf>), the laboratory system the plan calls [SIDLAB](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>), the [surveillance monitoring dashboard](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/report_2024-25_WHO_STP_0.pdf>), [Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources (EIOS)](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/report_2024-25_WHO_STP_0.pdf>), [Zzapp Malaria](<https://digitalx.undp.org/zzapp-malaria_1.html>), [telemedicine between health units](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>), and the [Piauí Saúde Digital teleconsultation platform](<https://rstp.st/2026/06/15/stp-lanca-plataforma-de-teleconsulta-com-medicos-brasileiros-para-reforcar-cuidados-de-saude-em-caue/>).

***Not held***. Which tiers use DHIS2 routinely, where the data is keyed and how many primary clinics enter their own reports are not established. No statement of who finances the system is held from the last three years, and the all-district implementation of 2024-2025 carries no month.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Health Development Plan 2023-2032, dated September 2022, [sets a strategic axis on expanding what it calls the single clinical process to the whole national health system](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>). The Joint SDG Fund programme's final report of 26 July 2022 states that [the Single Process module for patient files was developed inside DHIS2](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>), the Ministry of Health's main information software, and that [rolling it out beyond the pilot will be done with funding from other donors](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In September 2022 the plan placed the record in [a pilot phase at the Ayres de Menezes central hospital and the Água Grande Health Area](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The report of July 2022 states that [patient data is entered from reception through the nurses and doctors, each patient's file reached by a bar code sticker and scanner](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The plan of September 2022 states that the pilot is [to be evaluated before expansion to the whole national health system](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** At 26 July 2022 the Single Process [had not been rolled out to other districts and still needed consolidating in the first pilot district, Água Grande](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>). The plan states that computerised clinical records are [to be introduced gradually through a pilot model of health centres and the central hospital](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The ministry's [DHIS2 Tracker](<https://minsaude.st/sis/>), the [HIV Patient Tracking, HIV testing (DIPS) and TB Patient Tracking modules](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>), the [Malaria Notification and Investigation, COVID-19 surveillance and Death Registry modules](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2023-05/sao_tome_and_principe_final_report.pdf>), [integrated disease surveillance and response data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-08/WHO%20Cooperation%20Strategy%202023-2027%20with%20Sao%20Tome%20and%20Principe_July%202023_1.pdf>), [mSupply](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-08/WHO%20Cooperation%20Strategy%202023-2027%20with%20Sao%20Tome%20and%20Principe_July%202023_1.pdf>), the laboratory system the plan calls [SIDLAB](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>), the [surveillance monitoring dashboard](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/report_2024-25_WHO_STP_0.pdf>), [Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources (EIOS)](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/report_2024-25_WHO_STP_0.pdf>), [Zzapp Malaria](<https://digitalx.undp.org/zzapp-malaria_1.html>), [telemedicine between health units](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/STP_Sao-Tome-and-Principe_National-Health-Development-Plan_2023-2032.pdf>), and the [Piauí Saúde Digital teleconsultation platform](<https://rstp.st/2026/06/15/stp-lanca-plataforma-de-teleconsulta-com-medicos-brasileiros-para-reforcar-cuidados-de-saude-em-caue/>).

***Not held***. Nothing is dated after September 2022: whether the pilot was evaluated, consolidated or extended beyond Água Grande is not established. The health area's facilities are not named and no count of facilities using the record is given.
