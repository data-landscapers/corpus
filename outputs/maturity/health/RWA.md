# Rwanda

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [upgraded the HMIS, established in 1998, to web-based DHIS2 from 2012](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0235823>). The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda describes the system in 2025 as [operating countrywide under the ministry's management](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Global Fund's Inspector General states, for the period to December 2023, that DHIS2 [has been rolled out at all health facilities, a network of referral, provincial and district hospitals, 505 health centres and 1,250 health posts](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/15329/oig_gf-oig-24-020_report_en.pdf>). The ministry's booklet for fiscal year 2023/24 [reports HMIS counts by facility level for referral, provincial and district hospitals, medicalized health centres and medical clinics](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Moh/Publications/Reports/new_annual_Report/Annual_Statistical_Booklet_Fiscal_Year_2023-2024_Final_10102025.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** Data [is reported into DHIS2 at the health facilities, after monthly validation meetings led by the heads of facilities](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/15329/oig_gf-oig-24-020_report_en.pdf>), on the Inspector General's account of the period to December 2023. In 2017 [heads of service filled paper forms from registers and the facility's own data manager uploaded the data into DHIS2 by the 5th of the month](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0235823>).

**Last twelve months.** The National Institute of Statistics describes the system as it stood in 2025 as [still capturing aggregated births and deaths to check the civil registration system](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Inspector General states that [DHIS2 is rolled out at all health facilities](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/15329/oig_gf-oig-24-020_report_en.pdf>), in the audit covering July 2021 to December 2023. Authors at the University of Rwanda and the Rwanda Biomedical Center [held HMIS immunisation reports for October to December 2022 for all 520 primary health facilities](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/articles/10.3389/frhs.2026.1816682/full>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [e-Tracker electronic immunization registry](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/articles/10.3389/frhs.2026.1816682/full>), [DHIS2 HIV case-based surveillance](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rwandas-eBuzima-EMR_KP-and-PrEP-Modules_Zephanie_Final.pdf>) and the [2005 electronic record for tuberculosis and HIV care](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4763866/v1>). Single-function: the [pharmacy management system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), the [national laboratory information management systems](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), the [national health commodity inventory management system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), [DHIS2 for the Marburg outbreak response](<https://dhis2.org/invest-in-routine-systems/>), [eLMIS](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>) and the [laboratory data systems for HIV viral load](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/15329/oig_gf-oig-24-020_report_en.pdf>). Community: the [community health workers' standardized forms and mobile tools](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Moh/Publications/Reports/new_annual_Report/Annual_Statistical_Booklet_Fiscal_Year_2023-2024_Final_10102025.pdf>). Enabling: the planned [virtual hospital](<https://techcabal.com/2025/04/24/rwanda-e-ubuzima-rollout/>).

***Not held***. No plan or financing for the HMIS dated within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months. No measured share of clinics entering their own reports is held, and whether the health posts key theirs is not established.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Rwanda Biomedical Centre slides of April 2026 present eBuzima as [the national system for all public health facilities, served from the Ministry of Health's own domain](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rwandas-eBuzima-EMR_KP-and-PrEP-Modules_Zephanie_Final.pdf>). In a memorandum signed on 5 December 2025 and running from April 2026 to December 2030, the government [commits to expand national EMR systems across all health facilities](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>). The United States [plans to allocate US$6,806,292 over 2026 and 2027 to the e-Buzima rollout, and Rwanda commits to pay the ongoing licensing, cloud and hardware costs the United States does not](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** A survey of April and May 2023 found [all 42 district hospitals using an electronic medical record system, 35 of them OpenMRS](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4763866/v1>). King Faisal Hospital Rwanda, a referral hospital in Kigali, [runs an Integrated Health Management Information System that gives each patient a single record](<https://institute.global/insights/public-services/how-rwanda-is-using-data-to-deliver-better-health-care>), the Tony Blair Institute reported in July 2026.

**Where data is digitised.** The April 2026 slides describe eBuzima as [centralised, web-based and synchronising records across public facilities](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rwandas-eBuzima-EMR_KP-and-PrEP-Modules_Zephanie_Final.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** By 8 April 2026 eBuzima [had been rolled out to the majority of public health facilities, with its HIV key-population and PrEP modules running in one Kigali pilot site](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rwandas-eBuzima-EMR_KP-and-PrEP-Modules_Zephanie_Final.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Tom Frieden wrote in a piece published in September 2026 that eBuzima [is in more than two thirds of health centres](<https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/make-every-life-count-dr-tom-frieden-72x6c/>). The 2023 survey found [71 of 215 randomly sampled public health centres (33.0%) using an electronic medical record system](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4763866/v1>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [e-Tracker electronic immunization registry](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/health-services/articles/10.3389/frhs.2026.1816682/full>), [DHIS2 HIV case-based surveillance](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Rwandas-eBuzima-EMR_KP-and-PrEP-Modules_Zephanie_Final.pdf>) and the [2005 electronic record for tuberculosis and HIV care](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4763866/v1>). Single-function: the [pharmacy management system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), the [national laboratory information management systems](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), the [national health commodity inventory management system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/Rwanda_Us_Afghs_Mou.pdf>), [DHIS2 for the Marburg outbreak response](<https://dhis2.org/invest-in-routine-systems/>), [eLMIS](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>) and the [laboratory data systems for HIV viral load](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/15329/oig_gf-oig-24-020_report_en.pdf>). Community: the [community health workers' standardized forms and mobile tools](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Moh/Publications/Reports/new_annual_Report/Annual_Statistical_Booklet_Fiscal_Year_2023-2024_Final_10102025.pdf>). Enabling: the planned [virtual hospital](<https://techcabal.com/2025/04/24/rwanda-e-ubuzima-rollout/>).

***Not held***. No unique patient identifier and no record retrieved at another facility is established. No count of facilities using eBuzima is held, nor who enters the record at the health centres or where it is keyed.
