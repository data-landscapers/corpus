# Mozambique

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's own page states that [its Departamento de Informação para Saúde manages SIS-MA, the ministry's aggregate health information system](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>). The ministry's [Plano de Melhoria de Qualidade de Dados 2025-2029, dated January 2025, is a national plan for the quality of data in SIS-MA](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** A 2019 assessment by the University of Oslo, Eduardo Mondlane University and the ministry found that [SISMA's users, services and health facilities were registered and configured centrally at the ministry, with support passing from district and provincial statisticians to its technical teams](<https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/research/groups/is/ifip-94/proceedings-virtual-conference-2021/all-papers/collinsonetal.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same assessment, covering 14 districts, found that [facilities' monthly data reached the district health office on paper forms and were entered into SISMA there](<https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/research/groups/is/ifip-94/proceedings-virtual-conference-2021/all-papers/collinsonetal.pdf>). A study led with the ministry found in 2016-17 that [public primary care facilities submitted monthly paper forms to the district management office, where the data were entered](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).

**Last twelve months.** The data quality plan of January 2025 followed [the ministry's 2022-23 assessment of 175 facilities in all 11 provinces, which found that only 9% of records matched exactly between facility registers and SIS-MA](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [None of the 16 randomly selected public primary care facilities visited in four districts had electronic data collection in place in 2016-17](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>), the study led with the ministry reports.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [Electronic Patient Tracking System (SESP)](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), [MGDH-Birth](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>) and [MozART](<https://ons.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/INS-Mapeamento-das-bases-de-dados_Marco_2022.pdf>). Single-function: [Ferramenta Central](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>), the [SIS-RME pharmacy module](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), [iDMED](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), the [Laboratory Information System (SIS-LAB)](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), [DISA](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), the [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response Platform (eVIDR)](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), [e-IDSR and BES](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>) and [nSIMAM](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health memorandum signed with the United States on 15 December 2025 states that [the Integrated Electronic Medical Record System (SIS-RME), as it names it, is owned and operated by the Government of Mozambique](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>), and [names it the core product of the national digital health platform](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>). The United States [plans to fund SIS-RME's software development and equipment within US$83,072,079 of data-systems funding over 2026-2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Jembi Health Systems states that [in December 2024 Mavalane General Hospital in Maputo became Mozambique's first digital hospital, its Hospital Health Information System (SIS-H) digitising admissions and inpatient care and replacing paper records](<https://www.jembi.org/case-study/mozambique>).

**Where data is digitised.** At the eight health units visited in a 2019 assessment with the ministry, [daily registries were kept in paper books and charts, beside tablets supplied by particular programmes](<https://www.mn.uio.no/ifi/english/research/groups/is/ifip-94/proceedings-virtual-conference-2021/all-papers/collinsonetal.pdf>). In 2016-17 [patient data at 16 public primary care facilities were recorded daily in paper registers and tally sheets](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).

**Last twelve months.** The memorandum of 15 December 2025 [targets a minimum viable version of SIS-RME for September 2026 and a scannable data-capture solution for low-burden health facilities by the end of June 2027](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0008QN-Mozambique-Health-12.15.2025.pdf>). The ministry states that it [singles out implementation of the Electronic Medical Record starting from the health units](<https://misau.gov.mz/mocambique-e-estados-unidos-assinam-acordo-de-17-mil-milhoes-de-dolares-para-reforcar-o-sector-da-saude/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [None of the 16 public primary care facilities selected for the 2016-17 study had an electronic system](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).
