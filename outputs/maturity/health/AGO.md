# Angola

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's Office of Studies, Planning and Statistics, GEPE, [oversees the health information system and has been moving it onto DHIS2 since 2015](<https://doi.org/10.5937/smclk6-58193>), as an article of June 2025 states. WHO [supported the drafting in 2024 of a strategic plan for the system running to 2030](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/WHO%20Angola%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf>), and the [European Union finances that plan's preparation](<https://doi.org/10.5937/smclk6-58193>). The World Bank's appraisal of 4 November 2025 [proposes US$5 million to extend DHIS2's functions and geographic coverage](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110425174012650/pdf/BOSIB-af99fa45-c63f-4604-8c90-af595e9db762.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** A technical mission in March 2026 found the DHIS2-based national system had [more than 4,700 registered users](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>).

**Where data is digitised.** Health facilities collect and report their data on paper, and the figures are [aggregated and entered into DHIS2 at municipal or provincial level](<https://doi.org/10.5937/smclk6-58193>), as the June 2025 article states.

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 a joint WHO Angola and HISP UiO mission with Saudigitus assessed the platform, and the ministry and its partners [agreed to upgrade it, harmonise data across information flows, build dashboards for decision-makers and train staff at all levels](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Health facilities [report in paper format, and entry into DHIS2 is made at municipal or provincial level](<https://doi.org/10.5937/smclk6-58193>), on the June 2025 article's account.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [ReDIV](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>), as the ministry names its vaccination register, the [TB EMR system](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-03270-7>) piloted in Luanda and the [immunization registry system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110425174012650/pdf/BOSIB-af99fa45-c63f-4604-8c90-af595e9db762.pdf>). Single-function: the ministry's stock system [IOTA](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>), [eLMIS](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/angola-to-partner-with-hisp-uio-and-who/>), the [health logistics and supply chain management platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110425174012650/pdf/BOSIB-af99fa45-c63f-4604-8c90-af595e9db762.pdf>), [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110425174012650/pdf/BOSIB-af99fa45-c63f-4604-8c90-af595e9db762.pdf>), the [neglected tropical diseases programme's DHIS2-based reporting](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44162-026-00229-8>), the [drug dependence data held on DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/WHO%20Angola%20Annual%20Report%202024.pdf>) and the [National TB Program's digital database](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2022.745928/full>). Enabling: [telemedicine](<https://angop.ao/noticias/saude/angola-reafirma-compromisso-com-a-inovacao-digital-no-sistema-de-saude/>).

***Not held***. No source names the tiers using the system, and no count or share of primary clinics entering their own reports is published. The strategic plan is not reported as adopted, and no domestic budget line is tied to the system by name.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The electronic obstetric record prototype at the Irene Neto Maternity Hospital in Lubango [was part of a doctoral research project at the University of Beira Interior, Portugal, adapting ObsCare, an application owned by Virtual Care](<http://www.academicstar.us/UploadFile/Picture/2020-7/2020716175015266.pdf>), as its authors stated in November 2019.

**Tiers in use.** The prototype ran at [one 180-bed maternity hospital](<http://www.academicstar.us/UploadFile/Picture/2020-7/2020716175015266.pdf>) in November 2019.

**Where data is digitised.** At that hospital the clinical processes of deliveries from the fourth quarter of 2016 were [being entered retrospectively in the admission, archive and medical statistics section](<http://www.academicstar.us/UploadFile/Picture/2020-7/2020716175015266.pdf>) in November 2019. Luanda's two reference tuberculosis facilities [kept clinical and surveillance data entirely on paper records and registers](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2022.745928/full>) between March and September 2019.

**Last twelve months.** The prototype was [in its test phase](<http://www.academicstar.us/UploadFile/Picture/2020-7/2020716175015266.pdf>) in November 2019.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Anti-Tuberculosis Dispensary of Luanda [recorded on paper medical records and registers](<https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2022.745928/full>) in 2019.
