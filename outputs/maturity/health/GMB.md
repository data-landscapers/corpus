# Gambia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's National Health Sector Policy 2021-2030, published in February 2024, [calls DHIS2 the central reporting system for all national health information](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>) and puts the HMIS programme under the ministry's Directorate of Planning and Information. The policy [sets the strategy of migrating fully from paper recording to a computerised system at all levels](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>) and lists a costed strategic plan for 2021-2025 as its implementing document.

**Tiers in use.** The policy [places the electronic HMIS at the seven regional health directorates and the ministry](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>). The ministry's service statistics report for 2021, drawn from DHIS2, [carries data from referral hospitals, community clinics and the village health service](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2022-07/Final%20Service%20Statistic%20Report%2C%202021.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The policy states that the HMIS is [paper at the facility, in registers and monthly return forms, and electronic at the regional health directorates and the centre](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>). From 1 October 2023 one secondary-level hospital, Bundung Maternal and Child Health Hospital, [had its monthly indicators integrated into DHIS2 automatically](<https://journals.lww.com/pidj/fulltext/2025/02001/toward_an_electronic_pregnancy_registry_in_the.30.aspx>) from scanned forms, in a pilot.

**Last twelve months.** Between June and November 2024 the 11 sites of a tuberculosis pilot [kept generating their monthly reports for the DHIS2 aggregate system from paper registers](<https://journals.theunion.org/content/ijtldo/3/3/138.full.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The policy gives [electronic entry only at the regional health directorates and centrally](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>). A World Bank assessment of July 2023 states that [many lower-level facilities continue to collect HMIS data on paper](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194133413/pdf/P1598650585e0e01c0b8ae0dcc1f1cc826c.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Smart Paper Technology](<https://journals.lww.com/pidj/fulltext/2025/02001/toward_an_electronic_pregnancy_registry_in_the.30.aspx>), the [TB e-Tracker](<https://journals.theunion.org/content/ijtldo/3/3/138.full.pdf>), the immunisation programme's [MyChild Solution](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>) and [Vaccine Visibility System](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>), the pharmaceutical directorate's [Logistics Management Information System](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>), the [electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>), [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194133413/pdf/P1598650585e0e01c0b8ae0dcc1f1cc826c.pdf>), and [community health workers' service delivery reports, integrated into the HMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is published, and no dated event falls in the twelve months to 30 September 2026. No statement of who finances the HMIS is held from the last three years.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Health Sector Policy 2021-2030, published in February 2024, [states the need to establish an Electronic Medical Record System across all levels](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>) and for the Ministry of Health to integrate existing databases into one system. In June 2023 the World Bank stated that the Bluesquare contract for the electronic National Health Insurance System, under the Bank's Essential Health Services Strengthening Project, [was amended to include an electronic health records system using OpenClinic](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>).

**Tiers in use.** The Point reported in August 2023 [the pilot launch of an Electronic Medical Records system at Edward Francis Small Teaching Hospital](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/efsth-launches-emr-system>), the country's main referral hospital, developed by the Medical Research Council Unit The Gambia. The policy of February 2024 states that the health sector [does not have a robust electronic medical record system in place](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The policy [sets the strategy of migrating fully from paper-based recording to a computerised system at all levels of care](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's identity diagnostic of 2025 describes what it calls the Health Management Information System as [managing patient records and issuing health cards](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>), with the national identification number of the civil registration system identifying each patient.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In June 2023 the World Bank described the OpenClinic system as [still to be piloted, in four health facilities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Smart Paper Technology](<https://journals.lww.com/pidj/fulltext/2025/02001/toward_an_electronic_pregnancy_registry_in_the.30.aspx>), the [TB e-Tracker](<https://journals.theunion.org/content/ijtldo/3/3/138.full.pdf>), the immunisation programme's [MyChild Solution](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>) and [Vaccine Visibility System](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>), the pharmaceutical directorate's [Logistics Management Information System](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>), the [electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>), [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194133413/pdf/P1598650585e0e01c0b8ae0dcc1f1cc826c.pdf>), and [community health workers' service delivery reports, integrated into the HMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062323150011889/txt/BOSIB0afda2c060e808f83098c6dd3a423e.txt>).

***Not held***. Whether the teaching hospital's pilot of 2023 or the contracted pilot in four facilities was running at 30 September 2026 is not established, and no primary clinic figure is held. No facility is named for the patient record system described in 2025.
