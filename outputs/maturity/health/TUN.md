***Not held***. Nothing is held on routine aggregate reporting, at any tier. The ministry's performance report for 2021 names a unified national health information system being put in place and says nothing of its use.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** WHO Tunisia stated in April 2024 that [the Ministry of Health's Information Technology Centre develops and implements the hospital information management system, with a focus on electronic medical records](<https://tunisia.un.org/fr/download/159284/267554>). The draft National Health Policy of 2019 [calls for the generalisation of a single, digitised medical record](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/06/PNS-Tunisia.x71134.pdf>). The first-line record, which the programme calls the DMI, [was developed under Essaha Aziza, a Ministry of Health and European Union programme](<https://www.essaha-aziza.tn/resultat-5-digitalisation>).

**Tiers in use.** The World Bank's project document of 2 April 2025 gives the record as [fully installed and used in 50 per cent of university hospitals and partially used in the other 50 per cent](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040225000532312/txt/P507209-7bb7c459-f516-4ffe-a638-bc2583cfd224.txt>), and as [fully operational in 32 per cent of regional hospitals, partially used in 52 per cent and absent from 16 per cent](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040225000532312/txt/P507209-7bb7c459-f516-4ffe-a638-bc2583cfd224.txt>). In district hospitals it is [neither installed nor used](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040225000532312/txt/P507209-7bb7c459-f516-4ffe-a638-bc2583cfd224.txt>).

**Where data is digitised.** Of primary health care, the same document states that [the health system remains largely paper-based and that the DMI pilot faces connectivity and functionality issues](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040225000532312/txt/P507209-7bb7c459-f516-4ffe-a638-bc2583cfd224.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** The Ministry of Health confirmed on 21 July 2026 that [the Sahetna.tn portal, through which citizens are to reach their medical records, had completed technical validation and a cybersecurity audit](<https://tmo-mag.com.tn/tunisia-finalizes-sahetna-tn-digital-health-portal-as-national-id-rollout-gains-steam/>); [it was not yet in service](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/21/numerisation-de-la-sante-le-ministere-sapprete-a-lancer-le-portail-dacces-unique-sahetna-tn/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Essaha Aziza states that it [monitored the DMI's deployment on pilot sites in Nabeul](<https://www.essaha-aziza.tn/resultat-5-digitalisation>). DH Arab, on the ministry's account at 25 September 2026, has [99 healthcare facilities at various stages of implementing electronic medical records](<https://dharab.com/tunisia-bets-on-telehealth-as-it-prepares-to-host-telehealth-connect-2026/>).
