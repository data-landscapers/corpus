# Algeria

## HMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [weekly notification forms for priority diseases and events](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>) and [Go.Data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>) in the Sahrawi refugee camps at Tindouf, the [GIS for surveillance of immunisation and polio indicators](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>), and the laboratory system [IKOLAB](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) and radiology system [RISPACKS](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) at the Tizi-Ouzou university hospital. Enabling: the [fibre-optic connection of health structures](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>) in the wilaya of Annaba.

***Not held***. The Ministry of Health, with WHO support, [trained a first team of 20 on DHIS2 in December 2023](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>), and the national digital health strategy [was a draft in November 2023](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>). Nothing held says which tiers report through the platform, where data is keyed, how many primary clinics enter their own reports or how it is financed.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [updated the patient's electronic record itself, drawing on nearly 700 proposals from the sector](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>), and its minister presented the new version on 26 February 2024. In Annaba the ministry's services [coordinate preparation of what Seybouse Times calls the Dossier médical électronique with the wilaya health directorate and Algérie Télécom](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>), as of 16 August 2026.

**Tiers in use.** An El Moudjahid editorial of 8 September 2026 [places the electronic medical record system in the country's hospital establishments](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/editorials/a-l-ere-du-tout-numerique-4195>). From 2018 the ministry [put an earlier record, DEMDZ, into certain pilot hospitals, the Tizi-Ouzou university hospital among them](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>). At that hospital, Salmi, Bouchelaghem and Mohellebi wrote in 2022, [the use of DEMDZ failed and only the laboratory and radiology parts of a computerised patient record were in use](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The version presented to hospital directors on 26 February 2024 [carries a national identification number for each patient and holds all the patient's personal and medical data](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>). The minister said [any hospital establishment in the country can access it](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 16 August 2026 the Annaba health directorate, the Ministry of Health and Algérie Télécom [met to speed up the fibre-optic connection of health structures, in preparation for the record's implementation in the wilaya](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>), Seybouse Times reported.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Seybouse Times describes [the record's progressive roll-out in the health structures of the wilaya of Annaba as still forthcoming in mid-August 2026](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>).
