# Algeria

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health, with WHO support, [began training health practitioners and IT staff to use the DHIS2 platform, adapted to the national context](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>), the WHO Algeria office reports. The national digital health strategy under which the training began [was still a draft in November 2023, when a pre-validation workshop of sixty participants worked on it ahead of its submission to the national authorities](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The training is meant, the WHO office states, [to make the national health information system yield information for decision-makers at every level](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The first session, [five days in December 2023 led by an international expert](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>), taught its participants to use the platform.

**Last twelve months.** The WHO office reports that [further training sessions were to be programmed in the 2024-2025 work plan](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The first session [trained a first team of 20 health practitioners and IT staff](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Single-function: the [weekly notification forms for priority diseases and events](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>) and [Go.Data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>) in the Sahrawi refugee camps at Tindouf, the [GIS for surveillance of immunisation and polio indicators](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>), and the laboratory system [IKOLAB](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) and radiology system [RISPACKS](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) at the Tizi-Ouzou university hospital. Enabling: the [fibre-optic connection of health structures](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>) in the wilaya of Annaba.

***Not found***. Nothing found says which tiers report through the platform, where data is keyed, how many primary clinics enter their own reports or how it is financed. No use of the platform after the first training has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health minister said in July 2026 that [the ministry's information systems directorate designed and developed the Dossier médical électronique (DME) itself](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>), as the ministry [launched a national deployment plan aiming at over 2,500 health structures in all 69 wilayas](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>). In September 2026 he [announced an additional budget for digitising the record in public health facilities](<https://elwatan.dz/sante-alger-fixe-au-15-novembre-lecheance-finale-pour-la-numerisation-totale-des-hopitaux/>).

**Tiers in use.** An El Moudjahid editorial of 8 September 2026 [places the record in the country's hospitals](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/editorials/a-l-ere-du-tout-numerique-4195>). From 2018 the ministry [put an earlier record, DEMDZ, into certain pilot hospitals, the Tizi-Ouzou university hospital among them](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>). At that hospital, a 2022 study found, [the use of DEMDZ failed and only laboratory and radiology records were computerised](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The version presented on 26 February 2024 [carries a national identification number for each patient](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>). The minister said [any hospital establishment in the country can access it](<https://algerie-eco.com/2024/02/27/sante-dossier-electronique-patient-dote-numero-identification-national/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 April 2026 the minister said [a platform for remote follow-up of patient records had recently been set up](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/algerie/sante-et-environnement/mnun8thn-achevement-de-la-mise-en-place-d-une-plateforme-numerique-de-suivi-a-distance-des-dossiers-medicaux-des-patients>). He [opened the first training week of the deployment plan in Algiers in July 2026](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>). On 16 August 2026 Annaba's health directorate [met the ministry and Algérie Télécom to speed up fibre connection of health structures](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In September 2026 the record [was under way in several pilot facilities, with 15 November 2026 set as the deadline for all public health establishments](<https://elwatan.dz/sante-alger-fixe-au-15-novembre-lecheance-finale-pour-la-numerisation-totale-des-hopitaux/>). The first phase [trained over 200 trainers from ten pilot wilayas](<https://interlignes.dz/sante-lancement-du-deploiement-du-dossier-medical-electronique-a-travers-les-69-wilayas/>).

***Also on file***. Single-function: the [weekly notification forms for priority diseases and events](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-09/1%20OMS%20ALGERIE%20RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%202022%202023.pdf%20%287%29.pdf>) and [Go.Data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>) in the Sahrawi refugee camps at Tindouf, the [GIS for surveillance of immunisation and polio indicators](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-05/RAPPORT%20BIENNAL%20V.Finale%2029.03.2023.pdf>), and the laboratory system [IKOLAB](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) and radiology system [RISPACKS](<https://dspace.univ-mila.dz/jspui/bitstream/123456789/1787/1/690-Texte%20de%20l%27article-2080-1-10-20220611.pdf>) at the Tizi-Ouzou university hospital. Enabling: the [fibre-optic connection of health structures](<https://seybousetimes.dz/actualite/region/2026/08/17/annaba-le-raccordement-des-structures-de-sante-a-la-fibre-optique-au-coeur-dune-reunion-avec-le-ministere/>) in the wilaya of Annaba.

***Not found***. No count or share of hospitals or primary clinics using the record has been found, and where it is keyed is not stated.
