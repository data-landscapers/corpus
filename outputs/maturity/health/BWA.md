# Botswana

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** WHO's country office report for 2024-2025 describes DHIS2 as [the Government's selected "gold standard" platform for aggregate data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>). The Ministry of Health and Wellness's roadmap, dated 2019, [set one integrated DHIS2 as the default reporting system for the sector, under the ministry's stewardship](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Country_documents/Botswana/BHDC_Roadmap_2020.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** MEASURE Evaluation's country profile of August 2019 records [a national deployment of DHIS2 aggregating health data from all facilities in the country](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>).

**Where data is digitised.** WHO's report for 2024-2025 [lists facilities' heavy reliance on paper-based reporting as a challenge and recommends revising the DHIS2 dashboards and trackers "to enable onsite data capturing"](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's report for 2024-2025 has DHIS2 [used to monitor HIV, immunisation and reproductive, maternal and child health across districts](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** MEASURE Evaluation gives [100% of public health facilities as reporting through DHIS2, for 2016 to 2018](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>), a share of all public health facilities.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 trackers](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>) and the ministry's [open-source cancer registry](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>). Single-function: [VigiFlow](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>), the [Electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS)](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>), the [electronic Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response System (eIDSR)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099541410302526068/pdf/IDU-b74a99ca-88c5-43e8-aba0-34a34d67e29f.pdf>), [Botswana Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR)](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>), the [disease surveillance system](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Country_documents/Botswana/BHDC_Roadmap_2020.pdf>) of the ministry's 2019 roadmap, the [central medical stores warehouse management system](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>) and the [Laboratory Information Management System (Senaite)](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>) of one private clinic. Community: [PaperEMR](<https://aiconference.cipit.org/documents/the-state-of-ai-in-africa-report.pdf>). Enabling: the [Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS)](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>), the [village connectivity programme](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>), the [Gabriel telemedicine platform](<https://aiconference.cipit.org/documents/the-state-of-ai-in-africa-report.pdf>), the telemedicine application [Kgonafalo](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>) and the [Government Data Network](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>).

***Not held***. The level at which facilities' paper reports are keyed is not established, and no share or count of clinics entering their own reports is held; the 100% is of facilities reporting, and carries no numerator or denominator. No plan or financing dated within the last three years is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's budget speech of 5 March 2026 states that [it and the Ministry of Local Government and Traditional Affairs will roll out the completed EMR to the remaining 29 districts in 2026/27](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). The 2026/27 development budget request [allocates P62,534,000 to the ministry's headquarters, naming the Digitalization of Health Services Project](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). A World Bank technical note published on 30 October 2025 [costs the electronic medical record at US$1.531 million, from an IBRD loan](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099541410302526068/pdf/IDU-b74a99ca-88c5-43e8-aba0-34a34d67e29f.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** MEASURE Evaluation's profile of August 2019 states that [all hospitals are on a centralised Integrated Patient Management System](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>), the ministry's name for it. A 2019-20 survey [names 13 public hospitals using it, from referral to primary hospitals](<https://noyam.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/EHASS20234416.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The National eHealth Strategy adopted in March 2020 is quoted as saying [the public hospitals' electronic medical record is real time](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>). A survey of 2019-20 found [most eRecord systems not interoperable within or between facilities, and no national unique patient identifier](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8379582/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 19 November 2025 the Minister of Local Government told Parliament that [the Electronic Medical Records system was being piloted in two districts](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). The March 2026 budget speech reports [21 more facilities in Okavango due by the end of that month](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Assistant Minister of Health told Parliament on 5 March 2026 that [Phase 1 of the ministry's centralised Electronic Medical Record had been implemented in 22 health facilities in Ngami](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>).
