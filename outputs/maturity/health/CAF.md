# Central African Republic

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** A DHIS2 Community poster of May 2026 describes the Ministry of Health and Population's work. The ministry [undertook the reform of the national health information system (SNIS) and adopted DHIS2 as the national platform](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). The ministry's immunisation recovery plan dates from November 2020. It [gives its SNIS service the administration of DHIS2](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>). The World Bank's appraisal of May 2022 states that [the SENI-Plus project will finance the transition to DHIS2 in all 35 districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/810311653593644795/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Health-Service-Delivery-and-System-Strengthening-Project-SENI-PLUS.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's plan of November 2020 [names 5 central hospitals with data managers keying SNIS data](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>). It also names the [35 district and 7 regional health offices](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** By 2025 DHIS2 [had been deployed in all 35 health districts, with data managers recruited and offline and VSAT connections](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). The plan of November 2020 [has facilities' SNIS reports arriving at the district, where data managers key them](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>). The IMPULSE study found [a functional electronic system in 83.3% of the 6 district health offices it assessed](<https://jogh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/jogh-15-04295n.pdf>) to July 2024.

**Last twelve months.** Report completeness in DHIS2 was 52% in 2020 across the 35 health districts. It [rose to more than 85% in 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The IMPULSE study assessed 14 facilities, on data collected to July 2024. They were hospitals and health centres in Bangui and three health regions chosen for access and security. The study found that [21.4% of them used a functional electronic system for routine data entry](<https://jogh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/jogh-15-04295n.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** HISP Rwanda reported on 18 March 2026 that it [is supporting deployment of the HISP EMR](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). The system is known in the country as e-SENI. HISP Rwanda works with the Ministry of Health and the Susan Buffett Foundation under the SENI project.

**Tiers in use.** HISP Rwanda's team [supervised implementation of e-SENI at hospital and health centres](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), on its account of March 2026.

**Where data is digitised.** HISP Rwanda described e-SENI in March 2026. It [gives each patient a unique identification and a centralised record](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). The record is captured in real time across clinical units, from registration and consultation to laboratory and pharmacy. It is [shared within the facility and synchronised to a central EMR server](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Last twelve months.** HISP Rwanda reported on 18 March 2026 [a phased deployment under way](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). It covered site assessments, configuration in French and staff training. An EMR showroom and a [central synchronisation server were set up](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** HISP Rwanda states that [medical records in many facilities are still kept in paper registers](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), as of March 2026.
