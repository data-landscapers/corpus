# Central African Republic

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and Population [undertook the reform of the national health information system (SNIS) and adopted DHIS2 as the national platform](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>), on a DHIS2 Community poster of May 2026. The ministry's immunisation recovery plan of November 2020 [gives its SNIS service the administration of DHIS2](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>). The World Bank's appraisal of May 2022 states that [the SENI-Plus project will finance the transition to DHIS2 in all 35 districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/810311653593644795/pdf/Central-African-Republic-Health-Service-Delivery-and-System-Strengthening-Project-SENI-PLUS.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's plan of November 2020 [names 5 central hospitals with data managers keying SNIS data, alongside the 35 district and 7 regional health offices](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** By 2025 DHIS2 [had been deployed in all 35 health districts, with data managers recruited and offline and VSAT connections](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). The plan of November 2020 [has facilities' SNIS reports arriving at the district, where data managers key them](<http://sante.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2020-12/15.Plan%20de%20relance%20de%20la%20vaccination%20de%20routine%20en%20RCA_Bangui12112020.pdf>). The IMPULSE study found [a functional electronic system in 83.3% of the 6 district health offices it assessed](<https://jogh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/jogh-15-04295n.pdf>) to July 2024.

**Last twelve months.** Report completeness in DHIS2 [rose from 52% in 2020 to more than 85% in 2025 across the 35 health districts](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The IMPULSE study found that [21.4% of the 14 facilities it assessed, hospitals and health centres in Bangui and three health regions chosen for access and security, used a functional electronic system for routine data entry](<https://jogh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/jogh-15-04295n.pdf>), on data collected to July 2024.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** HISP Rwanda [is supporting deployment of the HISP EMR, known in the country as e-SENI, in partnership with the Ministry of Health and the Susan Buffett Foundation under the SENI project](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), as it reported on 18 March 2026.

**Tiers in use.** HISP Rwanda's team [supervised implementation of e-SENI at hospital and health centres](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), on its account of March 2026.

**Where data is digitised.** e-SENI [gives each patient a unique identification and a centralised record captured in real time across clinical units, from registration and consultation to laboratory and pharmacy](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). The record is [shared within the facility and synchronised to a central EMR server](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), as HISP Rwanda described it in March 2026.

**Last twelve months.** HISP Rwanda reported on 18 March 2026 [a phased deployment under way, with site assessments, configuration in French, staff training, an EMR showroom and a central synchronisation server set up](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** HISP Rwanda states that [medical records in many facilities are still kept in paper registers](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>), as of March 2026.
