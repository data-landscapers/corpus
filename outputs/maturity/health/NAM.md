# Namibia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Managers were interviewed for a study published in March 2026. They said that [the Ministry of Health and Social Services manages DHIS2 centrally, on a server at headquarters](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>). National staff alone change its design, add indicators and run backups. They said that [external donors pay for customising DHIS2, fund the training and buy the computers](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>). On WHO's account, Namibia [launched a National Digital Health Policy for 2026 to 2036](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>) in February 2026. It is a ten-year framework to strengthen health information systems.

**Tiers in use.** The same managers said that [DHIS2 is used at district, regional and national levels](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>).

**Where data is digitised.** Health facilities send in monthly paper summary forms, on the managers' account of March 2026. District HIS officers [key them into the DHIS2 aggregate module at the district office](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>).

**Last twelve months.** The Ministry of Health and Social Services [launched the National Digital Health Policy (2026-2036)](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>) in February 2026. WHO says it is to address fragmentation across health information systems.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The health minister spoke to the National Assembly on 6 May 2025. The minister said [health data collection had expanded to over 90 clinics beyond district and hospital levels](<https://www.namibian.com.na/strengthening-namibias-health-system-through-investment-in-infrastructure-digitalisation-and-equity/>). In the study of March 2026 the managers said [primary health facilities still report on paper tools](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker module](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), the [Electronic Patient Management Information System (EPMIS)](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), the [Electronic Tuberculosis Register (ETR)](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), [PTracker](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), the [Electronic Patient Monitoring System (EPMS)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>) and the [Electronic Dispensing Tool (EDT)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>). Single-function: the [Pharmacy Management Information System (PMIS)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), [Meditech](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), [SysPro](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>) and the [COVID-19 electronic data system and dashboards](<https://dev2.restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/global-health/media/pdfs/2024/11/Namibia-2024-Fact-Sheet.pdf>). Community: the ministry's [Digital Community System (DCS)](<https://neweralive.na/health-prepares-for-digital-system-rollout/>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and Social Services [launched its Integrated Health Care Information Management System (IHCIMS) in Windhoek in 2011](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>). Its own IT department supports the ministry's information systems. Managers interviewed for a study of March 2026 said the ministry [has developed an e-Health Strategy, cited as the National eHealth Strategy 2021-2025](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>). It is to guide a digital platform for capturing patient data. The minister's budget speech of 6 May 2025 [asked N$62.5 million for health data and policy support](<https://www.namibian.com.na/strengthening-namibias-health-system-through-investment-in-infrastructure-digitalisation-and-equity/>). Expanding the e-Health network was among its priorities. Namibia [launched a National Digital Health Policy for 2026 to 2036](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/namibia/news/namibia-launches-national-digital-health-policy-advance-equitable-people-centred-healthcare>) in February 2026.

**Tiers in use.** IHCIMS was launched for every facility. On the study of September 2025, it [was fully deployed only at Windhoek Central Hospital and Oshakati State Hospital](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>).

**Where data is digitised.** Practitioners [reach a patient's record only through the paper health passport and manual records, and there is no unique patient identifier](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), the same study states. The minister said in March 2026 that [the system still faces paper-based records](<https://www.we.com.na/health-we/fragmented-systems-still-hinder-healthcare-as-namibia-pushes-digital-remodel-nmh008500-4-9782>).

**Last twelve months.** The managers spoke in the study of March 2026. They said [the platform was starting with a billing module piloted at Katutura and Windhoek Central hospitals](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** On 6 May 2025 the health minister said [five health centres and 18 clinics had the e-Health system's network in operation](<https://www.namibian.com.na/strengthening-namibias-health-system-through-investment-in-infrastructure-digitalisation-and-equity/>). The September 2025 study says [the ministry still relies on paper records in most hospitals and clinics](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker module](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), the [Electronic Patient Management Information System (EPMIS)](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), the [Electronic Tuberculosis Register (ETR)](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2060/3547>), [PTracker](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), the [Electronic Patient Monitoring System (EPMS)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>) and the [Electronic Dispensing Tool (EDT)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>). Single-function: the [Pharmacy Management Information System (PMIS)](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), [Meditech](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>), [SysPro](<https://journal-isi.org/index.php/isi/article/download/1185/612>) and the [COVID-19 electronic data system and dashboards](<https://dev2.restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/global-health/media/pdfs/2024/11/Namibia-2024-Fact-Sheet.pdf>). Community: the ministry's [Digital Community System (DCS)](<https://neweralive.na/health-prepares-for-digital-system-rollout/>).

***Not found***. No count or share of primary clinics keeping an electronic record has been found: the five health centres and 18 clinics of May 2025 are sites with the network in place. No budget line names the record system itself.
