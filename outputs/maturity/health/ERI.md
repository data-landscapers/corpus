# Eritrea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [implemented DHIS2 as its health information system, installed on the ministry's own local area networks, with HISP Tanzania supporting customisation, upgrades and training](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621>), as a DHIS2 Community post of March 2024 described it. The ministry's sector plan, HSSDP III, [covers 2022 to 2026 and treats the HMIS and DHIS2 in its Data and Information section](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/ERI_Eritrea_Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20and%20Development%20Plan%20%28HSSDP%20III%29_2022-2026.pdf>). It [places what it calls the National Health Information System under the Minister's Office, with HMIS, ICT and System Development units](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/ERI_Eritrea_Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20and%20Development%20Plan%20%28HSSDP%20III%29_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** HSSDP III, dated October 2021, states that [DHIS2 had been rolled out to about 40 per cent of facilities at secondary level and above](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/ERI_Eritrea_Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20and%20Development%20Plan%20%28HSSDP%20III%29_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** WHO Eritrea states that [by 2019 DHIS 2 was installed and rolled out in all zobas, the regional level, in its offline version](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/countries/all/Eritrea/Who%20Eritrea%20Bienniun%20Report%20WHO%202020%20-%202021.pdf>). In 2022 it reported [a functional DHIS-2 at national level, at sub-national level and in 44% of the districts](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>). A DHIS2 Community post of March 2024 states that [data enters the national DHIS2 from zones and sub-zones over the ministry's local networks, running offline](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO Eritrea reports that [in 2022 the DHIS 2 server was upgraded to the latest version, with new data analysis and data quality applications](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** HSSDP III states that, as of October 2021, [data is collected from all health facilities through paper and electronic channels](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/ERI_Eritrea_Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20and%20Development%20Plan%20%28HSSDP%20III%29_2022-2026.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the Ministry of Health's [DHIS2 tracker program](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/overcoming-connectivity-barriers-the-role-of-dhis2-platform-in-enhancing-health-data-management-in-eritrea/57621>). Single-function: [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response (IDSR)](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>), and two systems the joint external evaluation of August 2025 recommended, a [USSD-based national electronic reporting system](<https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/1c6a1499-7f49-4b88-930a-a83b18c5d55e/content>) and a [mobile-based data system for essential health service use during emergencies](<https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/1c6a1499-7f49-4b88-930a-a83b18c5d55e/content>).

***Not held***. Nothing held shows a primary clinic entering its own reports, and no share or count of clinics is held. How the system is financed is not stated, and no event dated within the last twelve months is held.
