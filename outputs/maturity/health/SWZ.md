# Eswatini

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** WHO's country office places DHIS2 [within the Ministry of Health's Strategic Health Information Department, run by its M&E unit](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-01/4th%20quarter%20newsletter.pdf>), and reports that in 2024 it was [hosted on UNICEF servers](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Eswatini%202024%20Annual%20Report%20.pdf>). MEASURE Evaluation's country profile of August 2019 records [the Health Information System Strategic Plan 2010-2014 as not current](<https://measureevaluation.cpc.unc.edu/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/swaziland.html>).

**Tiers in use.** The Central Statistical Office's vital statistics report for 2022 shows [an HMIS officer at the hospital or maternity recording birth and death outcomes in the Ministry of Health's inpatient system](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same report shows [the record made at the hospital or maternity itself, producing the ministry's hospital birth and death statistics](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>), and HMIS [supplied it with 28,541 health-institution births and 1,354 health-institution deaths for 2022](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's country office reports that [DHIS2 was introduced nationally in 2024 as a single platform for health data that had sat in separate programme databases](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Eswatini%202024%20Annual%20Report%20.pdf>). The ministry's HMIS unit says that in the first quarter of 2026 [reporting from CMIS, the national electronic medical record, to DHIS2 was strengthened with automated, FHIR-aligned data flows](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** MEASURE Evaluation's country profile of August 2019 records [95% of facilities as represented in HMIS information, and no electronic system for aggregating routine facility or community service data](<https://measureevaluation.cpc.unc.edu/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/swaziland.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [eLMIS](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>), the [Integrated Disease Notification System](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20HEALTH%202025.pdf>), [EIOS](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Eswatini%202024%20Annual%20Report%20.pdf>), [DATIM](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>) and the [integrated laboratory diagnostics system](<https://www.acceleratehss.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Accelerating-the-Achievement-of-Health-Equity-Goals-through-a-One-Record-per-Citizen-Electronic-Medical-Record-System-in-Eswatini.pdf>). Community: the [Community-Based Health Information System (CBHIS)](<https://www.ihmafrica.org/?page_id=1854>), the [Digital Community Health System](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>) and the [CMIS community module](<https://www.acceleratehss.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Accelerating-the-Achievement-of-Health-Equity-Goals-through-a-One-Record-per-Citizen-Electronic-Medical-Record-System-in-Eswatini.pdf>). Enabling: the Eswatini Digital Project's [broadband connectivity for health centres](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>) and MTN Eswatini's [TruMD](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5994/eswatini-rolls-out-digital-platforms-to-expand-access-to-health-agriculture-and-creative-economy>).

***Not held***. Nothing says which tiers send routine aggregate reports, where clinics' reports are keyed, or how many primary clinics enter their own. No plan or financing dated within the last three years is held.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Data.FI described the Client Management Information System (CMIS) in April 2026 as [an open-source, government-owned electronic medical record](<https://datafiproject.substack.com/p/even-on-the-rainiest-afternoons-in>). The Ministry of Health's HMIS unit [describes itself as custodian of the digital health systems, with its own in-country developers working on CMIS](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>). Data.FI, a PEPFAR-funded USAID agreement, reported for the period ending March 2024 that [it funds and carries out CMIS system development, security assessment and facility network support](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Data.FI described CMIS in April 2026 as in use [in the outpatient department of Piggs Peak Government Hospital and at local health clinics](<https://datafiproject.substack.com/p/even-on-the-rainiest-afternoons-in>).

**Where data is digitised.** Data.FI said in April 2026 that [clinicians open a client's full record on a unique ID number at their point of service, and that the record is available to clinicians at another clinic if the patient moves district](<https://datafiproject.substack.com/p/even-on-the-rainiest-afternoons-in>).

**Last twelve months.** The HMIS unit reported in June 2026 that [the integration of CMIS with eLMIS runs for ART medicines only and is ready for a facility-level pilot](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>). In the first quarter of 2026 its developers [completed training that produced automated, FHIR-aligned data flows from CMIS to DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_digitalhealth-eswatini-healthsystems-activity-7444746957228687361-maAL>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Data.FI said in April 2026 that [CMIS is used in 73% of the country's health facilities, with 233 live sites](<https://datafiproject.substack.com/p/even-on-the-rainiest-afternoons-in>). For the period ending March 2024 it reported CMIS [in use at 75% (247 of 327) of healthcare facilities](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [eLMIS](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hmis-eswatini_eswatini-integration-cmis-activity-7471108127233417216-53Hw>), the [Integrated Disease Notification System](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20HEALTH%202025.pdf>), [EIOS](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Eswatini%202024%20Annual%20Report%20.pdf>), [DATIM](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>) and the [integrated laboratory diagnostics system](<https://www.acceleratehss.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Accelerating-the-Achievement-of-Health-Equity-Goals-through-a-One-Record-per-Citizen-Electronic-Medical-Record-System-in-Eswatini.pdf>). Community: the [Community-Based Health Information System (CBHIS)](<https://www.ihmafrica.org/?page_id=1854>), the [Digital Community Health System](<https://datafi.thepalladiumgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Data.FI-2024-SAPR_Final-for-dissemination_508.pdf>) and the [CMIS community module](<https://www.acceleratehss.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Accelerating-the-Achievement-of-Health-Equity-Goals-through-a-One-Record-per-Citizen-Electronic-Medical-Record-System-in-Eswatini.pdf>). Enabling: the Eswatini Digital Project's [broadband connectivity for health centres](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>) and MTN Eswatini's [TruMD](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5994/eswatini-rolls-out-digital-platforms-to-expand-access-to-health-agriculture-and-creative-economy>).

***Not held***. No current plan naming CMIS and no domestic budget line for it is held. The 73% is a share of all health facilities with no denominator given, and no count of primary clinics alone is held.
