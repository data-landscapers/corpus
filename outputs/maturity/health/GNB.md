# Guinea-Bissau

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The DHIS2 project's country profile, updated July 2026, describes Guinea-Bissau's DHIS2 systems as [locally owned, with the Ministry of Health leading the work](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>) on them with Saudigitus (HISP Mozambique). The Ministry of Public Health's PNDS III, of June 2023, [places the national health information system, the SNIS, under INASA](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf>) and names [a strategic plan for it, PENSIS 2022-2026](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** INASA's health statistics yearbook for 2022 tabulates [the monthly reports of health centres entered in DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-05/Anu%C3%A1rio%20estat%C3%ADstico%202022_web.pdf>) by region and health area: 1,747 of 2,073 expected reports were entered, a completeness of 82.2 per cent.

**Where data is digitised.** PNDS III, of June 2023, states that [all the SNIS paper forms and reports are digitised in the 11 health directorates](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf>), on the DHIS2 platform.

**Last twelve months.** On 22 December 2025 the DHIS2 project announced that the HISP network, selected by the Global Fund, [will give technical assistance to seven African countries including Guinea-Bissau](<https://dhis2.org/global-fund-climate-health-catalytic/>) to integrate climate data into their national HMIS. By July 2026 [climate and modelling apps were installed on a DHIS2 testing instance](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>), not the national production system.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** INASA reported in January 2019 that [tablet entry of data directly from the health areas entered a pilot phase in 2018](<http://vida.org.pt/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Apresentacao_JulioVieira-INASA.pdf>). The 2022 yearbook gives the public network as [111 type C, 19 type B and 5 type A health centres](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-05/Anu%C3%A1rio%20estat%C3%ADstico%202022_web.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. On DHIS2, [disease surveillance](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>), [bed-net campaign planning and management](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>), [Tracker registries for maternal and child health and immunisation](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>), [the COVID-19 surveillance packages](<https://dhis2.org/guinea-bissau-covid-surveillance/>) and [individual tuberculosis data](<http://vida.org.pt/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Apresentacao_JulioVieira-INASA.pdf>). Beside them, [an eLMIS](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>), [an Excel and Access laboratory data tool](<https://dhis2.org/guinea-bissau-covid-surveillance/>) and [an epidemiological surveillance data flow](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GNB_Guinea-Bissau_Plano-Nacional-de-Desenvolvimento-Sanitario-PNDSIII_2023-2028.pdf>). For community health workers, [UNICEF's mWater](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>), [the concept named Rota Segura](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>) and what INASA calls [GBSIS Comunitário](<http://vida.org.pt/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Apresentacao_JulioVieira-INASA.pdf>). Also the [National Telemedicine Center](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>).

***Not held***. No number or share of health centres keying their own reports is published, and no later fact follows the 2018 tablet pilot. No coverage fact is dated after June 2023, and how the system is financed is not stated.
