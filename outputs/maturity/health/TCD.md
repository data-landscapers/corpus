# Chad

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Public Health and Prevention [runs DHIS2 through its Direction des Statistiques et du Système d'Information Sanitaire](<https://dhis2.org/fr/chad-hmis-transition/>), as the DHIS2 programme stated in November 2022. The ministry's PNDS 4 for 2022-2030, dated November 2022, [sets the target of DHIS2 operational in all health districts by 2030](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/TCD_Chad_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire-%28PNDS4%29_2022-2030.pdf>). The World Bank's diagnostic states that [DHIS2 was implemented in July 2021 with the financial support of the Global Fund and the GAVI Alliance](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>).

**Tiers in use.** The Countdown to 2030 country team states that [hospitals' migration from the old health information system to DHIS2 is very slow, with support to them running since 2022](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CHARTBOOK-TCHAD-2024.pdf>), as of 2023.

**Where data is digitised.** Gavi's programme audit, on site in August 2024, records that [in one district the immunisation officer keys the monthly reports of 16 health centres into DHIS2 himself, and that entry for N'Djamena Centre district is likewise done at the district](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/document/audit/Programme-audit-report_Chad-2024-FRENCH.pdf>). The DHIS2 programme reports [96.4% of districts entering their data directly into DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/chad-hmis-transition/>) after the national launch of November 2022. PNDS 4, of November 2022, [lists extending DHIS2 to the health centres as an action still to be carried out](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/TCD_Chad_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire-%28PNDS4%29_2022-2030.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's Chad office reports that during the 2024-2025 biennium its support contributed to [the updating of the reporting tools and their integration into DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/Rapport%20des%20Resultats%20Biennal%202024%20-%202025_VFINALE.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Countdown to 2030 country team reports [facility reporting rates above 90% since 2020](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CHARTBOOK-TCHAD-2024.pdf>), as of 2023.
