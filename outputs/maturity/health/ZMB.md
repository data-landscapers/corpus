# Zambia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Gavi's programme audit of April 2024 calls DHIS2 [the Ministry of Health's own system](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>). It also calls it ["the approved country data management system"](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>). The National Health Strategic Plan 2022-2026 was revised in February 2023 and costed at ZMW 158.3 billion. It [names the HMIS as a basis for monitoring the plan](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/ZMB_Zambia_National-Health-Strategic-Plan_2022-2026.pdf>). It sets out to integrate health information systems at different levels. The Global Fund's case study of April 2025 covers its 2023-2025 grant cycle. In it the Fund [finances the upgrade and maintenance of the DHIS2-based health management information system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Interviews were held in May and June 2019. They found the HMIS [collecting monthly reports from rural health posts, rural and urban health centres, and district and provincial hospitals](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>).

**Where data is digitised.** Gavi's audit of April 2024 states that [data entry into DHIS2 is done monthly at the district level](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>). A few health facilities report directly. The 2019 interviews describe [facilities aggregating patient data onto paper reports each month](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>). District information officers [key them into DHIS2](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>).

**Last twelve months.** The Countdown to 2030 country team is from the University of Zambia and the Ministry of Health. It states that [99% of expected monthly facility reports were in DHIS2 in 2024, against 93% in 2019](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Gavi's audit of April 2024 [says that a few health facilities report directly into DHIS2](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>). It cites 3,320 health facilities countrywide from a 2022 cold chain inventory.

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [Zambia Immunisation Electronic Register (ZIER)](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>) and the ['Smart Card' project for HIV continuity of care](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>). Single-function: the [electronic integrated disease surveillance and response system (eIDSR)](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>), the [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response system (IDSR)](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>) and the [electronic logistics management information system (eLMIS)](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). Also single-function: the [Logistimo eLMIS](<https://www.gavi.org/sites/default/files/about/governance/audit/Programme-audit-report-Zambia-April-2024.pdf>) for vaccine stores, the [laboratory information system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) and the laboratory system [DISA](<https://smartcarezambia.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Smart-CarePro-Program-Managers-Presentation-final_kpp_mkm_2.pdf>). Also single-function: the [Laboratory Information Management System](<https://dspace.unza.zm/server/api/core/bitstreams/d584c52e-dcba-4dec-bd58-910fe11976cb/content>) and [national malaria data reporting by mobile phone](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>). Community: the [electronic community health information system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) and the [digitized community health package](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). Enabling: the [GovLink private 4G network](<https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-govlink-private-4g-network/G98YdMLGBZw7X2PD>), the [Government Wide Area Network (GWAN)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/08/smart-zambia-expands-digital-infrastructure-to-reach-every-corner-of-the-country/>) and [health facility solar power and internet connectivity](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

***Not found***. No count or share of facilities entering their own reports has been found. The 99% is a completeness rate and does not say where the data are keyed. The account of the tiers dates from 2019. No dated event in the last twelve months has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Global Fund's case study of April 2025 states that [the Ministry of Health developed SmartCare](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). It describes it as [co-financed by the Global Fund](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). SMART Zambia's national coordinator spoke on 7 July 2026. The coordinator said [government aims at full national coverage of SmartCare Pro](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/08/smart-zambia-expands-digital-infrastructure-to-reach-every-corner-of-the-country/>).

**Tiers in use.** [SmartCare 2.0 Hospital Edition went live as a pilot at Ndola Teaching Hospital on 27 April 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/zambia-targets-nationwide-health-connectivity-as-smartcare-2-0-pilot-goes-live-in-ndola/>). Interviews of May and June 2019 covered select districts. They found [some larger health centres and hospitals collecting data in the electronic health record](<https://www.ovid.com/journals/hpop/fulltext/10.1093/heapol/czad077~identifying-barriers-to-the-production-and-use-of-routine>).

**Where data is digitised.** The programme managers' presentation of March 2025 describes SmartCare Pro as [capturing the patient encounter at facility level](<https://smartcarezambia.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Smart-CarePro-Program-Managers-Presentation-final_kpp_mkm_2.pdf>). A central server identifies each client uniquely and tracks patients between facilities. In January 2026 the implementers described [a nurse at a rural clinic in Kafulafuta identifying a patient by fingerprint to reach his record](<https://dpi.africa.com/how-digital-public-infrastructure-is-transforming-health-and-identity-in-rural-zambia>).

**Last twelve months.** ITWeb Africa reported on 16 July 2026 that [SmartCare Pro and SmartCare 2.0 will be migrated to the government-owned GovLink network](<https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-launches-govlink-private-4g-network/G98YdMLGBZw7X2PD>). Three Copperbelt hospitals are to be connected first.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** At April 2025 the Global Fund states that [SmartCare Pro is in use at 877 health facilities](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). Zambia has 3,540 health facilities. The March 2025 presentation puts [SmartCare Legacy and SmartCare Plus together in approximately 1,800 healthcare facilities](<https://smartcarezambia.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Smart-CarePro-Program-Managers-Presentation-final_kpp_mkm_2.pdf>). On 7 July 2026 SMART Zambia's national coordinator said [SmartCare Pro has been deployed in approximately 80% of health facilities nationwide](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/08/smart-zambia-expands-digital-infrastructure-to-reach-every-corner-of-the-country/>).
