# Côte d'Ivoire

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's annual report for 2020 [calls DHIS2 the national database of routine data and places its checking with the ministry's own information directorate](<https://www.snisdiis.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Rapport-Annuel-sur-la-Situation-Sanitaire-RASS-2020-VF.pdf>). The ministry's costed health plan for 2021 to 2025, dated October 2021, [carries 30 million FCFA a year to make DHIS 2 interoperable with four other systems](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/CIV_Cote-d-Ivoire_Plan-National-de-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_2021-2025.pdf>). USAID's President's Malaria Initiative [financed support to routine reporting and the use of DHIS2 data in 20 health districts](<https://measuremalaria.cpc.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/CI-Qualite-des-Donnees-et-Util_FS-23-619-PMI_FR_508.pdf>) in 2022.

**Tiers in use.** The October 2021 plan states that DHIS 2 [is deployed in all regional and departmental health directorates, all regional hospital centres and general hospitals, and in a few first-contact health establishments](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/CIV_Cote-d-Ivoire_Plan-National-de-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_2021-2025.pdf>). In 2020 the general hospitals and regional hospital centres [had their data keyed into DHIS2 by their own data managers](<https://www.snisdiis.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Rapport-Annuel-sur-la-Situation-Sanitaire-RASS-2020-VF.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** In 2020 each first-contact establishment drew up a monthly report from its registers, and [the district's epidemiological surveillance officer keyed those reports into DHIS2 at the departmental health directorate](<https://www.snisdiis.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Rapport-Annuel-sur-la-Situation-Sanitaire-RASS-2020-VF.pdf>). PMI Measure Malaria reported for 2022 that [health centres reporting on paper had no access to the information in DHIS2](<https://measuremalaria.cpc.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/CI-Qualite-des-Donnees-et-Util_FS-23-619-PMI_FR_508.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 September 2025 the ministry's Health Information Directorate [organised a DHIS2 technical working group meeting on managing the national DHIS2 database, which it calls SIGSANTE](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** By the ministry's account for 2020, [first-contact establishments did not enter their own reports](<https://www.snisdiis.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Rapport-Annuel-sur-la-Situation-Sanitaire-RASS-2020-VF.pdf>). District reports [reached 100% completeness and 98.23% timeliness in 2020](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/CIV_Cote-d-Ivoire_Plan-National-de-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_2021-2025.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's digital health directorate [deployed its patient record, the DPI, in August 2024](<https://disd.ci/deploiement-reussi-du-dossier-patient-informatise-a-lhg-de-ferkessedougou-une-etape-cruciale-vers-la-digitalisation-des-soins/>). It presents the DPI as a ministry platform. In March 2025 the project's steering committee [set itself to write a plan for digitalising health](<https://disd.ci/digitalisation-du-systeme-de-sante-en-cote-divoire-le-comite-de-pilotage-du-projet-pape-digitalisation-fait-le-point/>). In September 2025 the World Bank [put EUR 128.6 million of IDA credit behind a patient records system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The DPI [has run in every service of the Hôpital Général de Ferkessédougou since 16 August 2024](<https://disd.ci/deploiement-reussi-du-dossier-patient-informatise-a-lhg-de-ferkessedougou-une-etape-cruciale-vers-la-digitalisation-des-soins/>). In November 2025 the directorate said [the record had been extended to first-contact health establishments](<https://disd.ci/himss-eurasia-2025-franck-simon-blehiri-expose-le-succes-du-modele-ivoirien-de-sante-digitale/>). In October 2026 Afrimag reported [the DPI deployed in 268 establishments, 9 of the 15 national hospitals among them](<https://afrimag.net/cote-divoire-administration-ouattara-fixe-le-cap-dune-economie-numerique-dici-2029/>).

**Where data is digitised.** At Ferkessédougou the platform [sits on the hospital's local server](<https://disd.ci/deploiement-reussi-du-dossier-patient-informatise-a-lhg-de-ferkessedougou-une-etape-cruciale-vers-la-digitalisation-des-soins/>). Every service registers and follows patients on it.

**Last twelve months.** On 22 April 2026 the health ministry [noted the DPI's deployment as under way](<https://www.sante.gouv.ci/index.php/actualite/2050>). It also prioritised 150 first-contact establishments for connectivity in 2026. A national workshop closing on 4 September 2026 [named the patient record among ten quick wins due by the end of 2026](<https://afriksoir.net/transformation-numerique-les-travaux-retiennent-59-services-prioritaires-et-dix-quick-wins-attendus-pour-la-fin-2026/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In March 2025 the project unit said [268 establishments were connected to the DPI](<https://disd.ci/digitalisation-du-systeme-de-sante-en-cote-divoire-le-comite-de-pilotage-du-projet-pape-digitalisation-fait-le-point/>). In October 2026 the directorate's officials said [258 health facilities use it](<https://digitalmag.ci/sante-numerique-vers-une-interoperabilite-dans-le-systeme-de-sante-en-cote-divoire/>). They did not say of which type.
