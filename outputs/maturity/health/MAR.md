# Morocco

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Health Compact of 6 December 2025 [makes the Ministry of Health and Social Protection and the territorial health groups accountable for deploying an integrated health information system, which it calls the SIIS, in all primary health care establishments and all university hospitals](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Morocco.pdf>). The World Bank [ties US$37.5 million of its US$450 million loan to health data, with nothing disbursed against it at 20 June 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025131517704/pdf/P179014-d9407c9f-bfb0-46b2-b320-48bebe0df550.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The World Bank's report of June 2025 gives as its baseline that [an integrated and digitalised health information system was deployed at hospitals and that deployment had begun in primary health care centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025131517704/pdf/P179014-d9407c9f-bfb0-46b2-b320-48bebe0df550.pdf>). The World Bank reports an earlier system, which the ministry calls the SMIPF-SC, [operational in public health centres in all regions by December 2019](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The SMIPF-SC [gave service-utilisation data at facility level by December 2019, aggregated through dashboards at provincial, regional and central levels](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf>), the World Bank reports. Results data [came from a paper-based system until the SMIPF-SC supplied them from 2018](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Compact gives the SIIS, at 6 December 2025, as [being deployed in primary health care establishments and in progress in university hospitals](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Morocco.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank's completion report gives the SMIPF-SC as [operational in 100% of public health centres by December 2019](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/998811657067132849/pdf/Morocco-Health-Sector-Support-Project.pdf>). Its report of June 2025 has deployment of the integrated system [begun in primary health care centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025131517704/pdf/P179014-d9407c9f-bfb0-46b2-b320-48bebe0df550.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [NADIS](<https://wd.fmpm.uca.ma/biblio/theses/annee-htm/FT/2024/these258-24.pdf>), a record for HIV patients at the CHU Mohammed VI. Single-function: [radiology information and picture archiving systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>), [laboratory information management systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>), [pharmacy information systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>) and the [maternal and neonatal death surveillance system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025131517704/pdf/P179014-d9407c9f-bfb0-46b2-b320-48bebe0df550.pdf>). Enabling: the [AI-assisted ultrasound pilot](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hopitaux-publics-recours-lia-pour-renforcer-le-diagnostic-precoce>), [AI-assisted breast cancer screening](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hopitaux-publics-recours-lia-pour-renforcer-le-diagnostic-precoce>), the [national telemedicine pilot of October 2018](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>) and [peritoneal dialysis telemonitoring at the CHU Hassan II in Fez](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>).

***Not held***. No share or count of health centres entering their own reports is held, and no source says who keys the data; the 100% is of centres where the system was operational, in 2019. No domestic budget line for the system is named.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Framework law 06-22 of 9 December 2022 [creates a shared medical record within the national health information system](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor227130.pdf>). Nichan reported on 23 July 2024 that the health ministry [was preparing to buy a national system built on a shared patient file, at an estimated 189,999,000 dirham](<https://nichan.ma/257314/>).

**Tiers in use.** A government document published in December 2024 states that [the integrated hospital information system has been deployed in all public hospitals of the twelve regions](<https://www.maroc.ma/sites/default/files/2024-12/sante-fr.pdf>). A purchase notice of 23 April 2024 [places the HOSIX patient-file database in the primary health care establishments of Taounate province](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma/bdc/entreprise/consultation/show/52009>).

**Where data is digitised.** At the CHU Mohammed VI in Marrakech, [doctors write observations and diagnoses in HOSIX and order laboratory tests there, with the paper record kept alongside](<https://wd.fmpm.uca.ma/biblio/theses/annee-htm/FT/2024/these258-24.pdf>), a thesis defended on 29 May 2024 reports. The Taounate notice [buys 19,200 working hours of contracted staff to key patient files into HOSIX](<https://www.marchespublics.gov.ma/bdc/entreprise/consultation/show/52009>).

**Last twelve months.** Medias24 reported in July 2026 that the Government Council had [adopted draft law 52.26, which creates a shared medical record for every patient](<https://medias24.com/2026/07/23/sante-numerique-ce-que-le-projet-de-loi-changer-dans-votre-parcours-de-soins-1728651/>). The health minister said on 1 June 2026 that [the ministry is still working to put the unified medical record in place](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hopitaux-publics-recours-lia-pour-renforcer-le-diagnostic-precoce>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The same document has [an information system for primary health care establishments in the course of deployment](<https://www.maroc.ma/sites/default/files/2024-12/sante-fr.pdf>). The ministry said on 5 April 2026 that [work continues with the generalisation of the electronic medical record](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/achevement-du-programme-gouvernemental-de-rehabilitation-de-1400-etablissements-de-soins-de-sante>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [NADIS](<https://wd.fmpm.uca.ma/biblio/theses/annee-htm/FT/2024/these258-24.pdf>), a record for HIV patients at the CHU Mohammed VI. Single-function: [radiology information and picture archiving systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>), [laboratory information management systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>), [pharmacy information systems](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>) and the [maternal and neonatal death surveillance system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025131517704/pdf/P179014-d9407c9f-bfb0-46b2-b320-48bebe0df550.pdf>). Enabling: the [AI-assisted ultrasound pilot](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hopitaux-publics-recours-lia-pour-renforcer-le-diagnostic-precoce>), [AI-assisted breast cancer screening](<https://www.maroc.ma/fr/actualites/hopitaux-publics-recours-lia-pour-renforcer-le-diagnostic-precoce>), the [national telemedicine pilot of October 2018](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>) and [peritoneal dialysis telemonitoring at the CHU Hassan II in Fez](<https://www.mdpi.com/2673-7426/6/2/22>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics using a patient record is held, and no count of hospitals using the hospital system; primary-tier use is held for one province. No shared record is in service, and the financing of the planned national system is not stated.
