# DR Congo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** DHIS2.org in December 2025 calls the national DHIS2 [locally owned and managed infrastructure of the Ministry of Health, in use at national scale since 2013](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>), and [names Gavi, the Global Fund and US CDC among its funders](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). The ministry's health informatics plan for 2020 to 2024 [states that DHIS2 has been adopted as the software of its Système National d'Information Sanitaire (SNIS)](<https://anicns.gouv.cd/restapi/uploads/documents/pndisii_2020_2024.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Sud-Kivu provincial health division's report of December 2021 lists [paper reporting forms for health centres, health posts and general referral hospitals, and DHIS2 data sets for basic, secondary and hospital services in all 34 health zones](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Fonctionnalite_Q4-2021_Sud-Kivu.pdf>). Nationally, the SNIS division [counted 52,625 basic-services reports in DHIS2 of 53,736 expected in the second quarter of 2020](<https://www.diffusion-snis-dhis2.itconnexion.net/index.php/actualites/189-retro-information-globale-aux-dps-a-l-issue-d-analyse-des-donnees-du-snis-au-2eme-trimestre-2020>).

**Where data is digitised.** A HISP evaluation that visited five health zones in Kinshasa and Haut-Katanga in late 2021 found that [facilities send their reports on paper to the health zone office, where they are entered into DHIS2; some large facilities enter their own](<https://dhis2.org/drc-data-use/>).

**Last twelve months.** In February 2026 [Lualaba became the first province to make the HIV Insight Dashboard, an analysis application built inside the national DHIS2, its primary tool for provincial meetings and data validation](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Ministry of Public Health's Countdown to 2030 report, signed on 14 August 2022, states that [12,050 of the 16,010 health facilities listed in DHIS2, 75.3% of all health facilities, report in it](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Rapport-technique-actualise-RDC_Atelier-danalyse-donnees-SRMNEA-Nut_14.08.2022.pdf>).
