# DR Congo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** DHIS2.org in December 2025 calls the national DHIS2 [locally owned and managed infrastructure of the Ministry of Health](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). It has run nationally since 2013. Its [funders include Gavi, the Global Fund and US CDC](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). The ministry's 2020-2024 health informatics plan [names DHIS2 as the software of its Système National d'Information Sanitaire (SNIS)](<https://anicns.gouv.cd/restapi/uploads/documents/pndisii_2020_2024.pdf>). ICTworks in July 2026 [lists that plan as expired, its successor unpublished](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>).

**Tiers in use.** A GFF Countdown analysis for 2019 to 2024 [says public primary facilities offering the minimum package report more reliably in DHIS2](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>). Sud-Kivu's health division lists paper forms for health centres, health posts and referral hospitals. It also lists [DHIS2 data sets in all 34 health zones](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Fonctionnalite_Q4-2021_Sud-Kivu.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** A HISP evaluation of five health zones in Kinshasa and Haut-Katanga in late 2021 found that [facilities send paper reports to the zone office for DHIS2 entry](<https://dhis2.org/drc-data-use/>). Some large facilities enter their own. [PATH in October 2025](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>) and [a vendor in January 2026](<http://snapiform.com/use-case/>) say health zone staff key the paper reports of 30 to 150 facilities monthly into DHIS2.

**Last twelve months.** The HIV Insight Dashboard is built inside the national DHIS2. In February 2026 [Lualaba became the first province to make it its primary tool for provincial data validation](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Ministry of Public Health's Countdown to 2030 report of 14 August 2022 states that [12,050 of the 16,010 health facilities listed in DHIS2 report in it](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Rapport-technique-actualise-RDC_Atelier-danalyse-donnees-SRMNEA-Nut_14.08.2022.pdf>). That is [75.3% of all health facilities](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Rapport-technique-actualise-RDC_Atelier-danalyse-donnees-SRMNEA-Nut_14.08.2022.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 Tracker configuration for the Ebola response](<https://dhis2.org/drc-uganda-africa-cdc-ebola-outbreak-response/>), RTI International's [Ebola outbreak surveillance and response platform](<https://www.rti.org/news/ebola-drc-surveillance-response-activated>) and the [DHIS2 Ebola tracking tool used in Bulape and Mweka](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). Also trackers: [DHIS2 Tracker in several provinces](<https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1669745>) and the Salvation Army's [patient-record digitisation project on Visa Médical](<https://www.sanru.org/actualites/66/lancement-officiel-du-projet-de-renforcement-des-capacites-et-numerisation-du-dossier-des-patients-en-rdc>). Single-function: the [HIV Insight Dashboard](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>), [DHIS2 for disease surveillance](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>) and [DHIS2 mpox surveillance and vaccination](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). Also single-function: the [DHIS2 animal health surveillance system](<https://dhis2.org/drc-uganda-africa-cdc-ebola-outbreak-response/>) and the [mpox surveillance application integrated with DHIS2](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>) piloted in three Sud-Kivu health zones. Also single-function: [SORMAS](<https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1669745>), [EWARS](<https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1669745>) and [eIDSR on DHIS2](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>) in Haut Katanga. Also single-function: the weekly surveillance data set [Relevé Epidémiologique Hebdomadaire](<https://www.diffusion-snis-dhis2.itconnexion.net/index.php/actualites/189-retro-information-globale-aux-dps-a-l-issue-d-analyse-des-donnees-du-snis-au-2eme-trimestre-2020>) and the facility stock data set [SIGL FOSA](<https://www.diffusion-snis-dhis2.itconnexion.net/index.php/actualites/189-retro-information-globale-aux-dps-a-l-issue-d-analyse-des-donnees-du-snis-au-2eme-trimestre-2020>). Also single-function: the [HIV, tuberculosis, malaria sentinel-site and blood bank reporting modules](<https://www.diffusion-snis-dhis2.itconnexion.net/index.php/actualites/189-retro-information-globale-aux-dps-a-l-issue-d-analyse-des-donnees-du-snis-au-2eme-trimestre-2020>), the [SURVEPI data set](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Appui-technique-a-la-revue-ZS-M1T4-2021_Kasai-Central.pdf>) and the malaria programme's [analysis database and dashboard](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Appui-technique-a-la-revue-ZS-M1T4-2021_Kasai-Central.pdf>). Also single-function: [computerised medication management](<https://www.scirp.net/journal/paperinformation?paperid=136674>) in seven Kananga hospitals. Community: the [SSC App](<https://www.openiaso.com/digitalization-community-care-site-supervision-drc-ssc-app/>). Its monthly variables are [exported to the SNIS communautaire](<https://www.openiaso.com/digitalization-community-care-site-supervision-drc-ssc-app/>), the ministry's community instance of DHIS2. Also community: [community-level capture synchronised with DHIS2](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>) in the Sud-Kivu mpox pilot and [community health workers' paper reporting](<https://dhis2.org/drc-data-use/>). Enabling: the computers, tablets and routers ANICNS supplies as a [kit digital](<https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/kasai-oriental-un-lot-dequipements-numeriques-offert-a-la-division-provinciale-de-la-sante/>) and [VSAT and 3G modem links for Sud-Kivu's health zones](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Fonctionnalite_Q4-2021_Sud-Kivu.pdf>).

***Not found***. No share or count of facilities entering their own reports has been found, and no coverage figure dated after 2022; the 75.3% counts facilities whose reports are in DHIS2. No current plan has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Actualite.cd reported on the national agency ANICNS on 23 November 2024. ANICNS [leads the health-sector digital transformation project](<https://actualite.cd/2024/11/23/rdc-lanicns-dote-les-hopitaux-de-mbuji-mayi-et-kalemie-dequipements-numeriques-pour-leur>). It approves the hospital information system to be put into service. The project [is financed entirely by the Congolese government](<https://actualite.cd/2024/11/23/rdc-lanicns-dote-les-hopitaux-de-mbuji-mayi-et-kalemie-dequipements-numeriques-pour-leur>). Guinéenews reported on 13 August 2026 on the private firm Kouma Academy. It [has begun deploying its Visa Medical patient record in the country, in partnership with Sanofi](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>).

**Tiers in use.** On 12 July 2024 ANICNS [handed digital kits and three hospital information systems to three sites in Kinshasa](<https://actualite.cd/2024/07/13/kinshasa-lanicns-dote-trois-etablissements-hospitaliers-de-kits-medicaux-digitalises>). They were the Lufungula general hospital, the Ngaba mother-and-child centre and the Lufungula health centre. The handover was to allow the first modules to be implemented.

**Where data is digitised.** A University of Kananga survey covered seven hospitals in that city in 2023. It [found 4.7% of 86 staff managing patient information electronically](<https://www.scirp.net/journal/paperinformation?paperid=136674>).

**Last twelve months.** On 16 June 2026 ANICNS [opened a call for the now-mandatory approval of hospital information systems](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>). Each is [to include a complete computerised patient record](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** ACP reported on 13 February 2025 on equipment for the Kasaï-Oriental provincial health division. ANICNS [handed it 516 computers, 206 tablets and 206 routers to equip 206 care establishments](<https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/kasai-oriental-un-lot-dequipements-numeriques-offert-a-la-division-provinciale-de-la-sante/>).
