# DR Congo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** DHIS2.org in December 2025 calls the national DHIS2 [locally owned and managed infrastructure of the Ministry of Health, in use at national scale since 2013](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>), and [names Gavi, the Global Fund and US CDC among its funders](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). The ministry's 2020-2024 health informatics plan [names DHIS2 as the software of its Système National d'Information Sanitaire (SNIS)](<https://anicns.gouv.cd/restapi/uploads/documents/pndisii_2020_2024.pdf>). ICTworks in July 2026 [lists that plan as expired, its successor unpublished](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>).

**Tiers in use.** A GFF Countdown analysis for 2019 to 2024 [says public primary facilities offering the minimum package report more reliably in DHIS2](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>). Sud-Kivu's health division lists [paper forms for health centres, health posts and referral hospitals, and DHIS2 data sets in all 34 health zones](<https://pnlprdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Fonctionnalite_Q4-2021_Sud-Kivu.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** A HISP evaluation of five health zones in Kinshasa and Haut-Katanga in late 2021 found that [facilities send paper reports to the health zone office for entry into DHIS2; some large facilities enter their own](<https://dhis2.org/drc-data-use/>). [PATH in October 2025](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>) and [a vendor in January 2026](<http://snapiform.com/use-case/>) say health zone staff key the paper reports of 30 to 150 facilities monthly into DHIS2.

**Last twelve months.** In February 2026 [Lualaba became the first province to make the HIV Insight Dashboard, built inside the national DHIS2, its primary tool for provincial data validation](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icaps-hiv-insight-dashboard-streamlines-data-analysis-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Ministry of Public Health's Countdown to 2030 report, signed on 14 August 2022, states that [12,050 of the 16,010 health facilities listed in DHIS2, 75.3% of all health facilities, report in it](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Rapport-technique-actualise-RDC_Atelier-danalyse-donnees-SRMNEA-Nut_14.08.2022.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The national agency ANICNS [leads the health-sector digital transformation project and approves the hospital information system to be put into service](<https://actualite.cd/2024/11/23/rdc-lanicns-dote-les-hopitaux-de-mbuji-mayi-et-kalemie-dequipements-numeriques-pour-leur>), Actualite.cd reported on 23 November 2024, and the project [is financed entirely by the Congolese government](<https://actualite.cd/2024/11/23/rdc-lanicns-dote-les-hopitaux-de-mbuji-mayi-et-kalemie-dequipements-numeriques-pour-leur>). Separately, the private firm Kouma Academy [has begun deploying its Visa Medical patient record in the country, in partnership with Sanofi](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>), Guinéenews reported on 13 August 2026.

**Tiers in use.** On 12 July 2024 ANICNS [handed digital kits and three hospital information systems to the Lufungula general hospital, the Ngaba mother-and-child centre and the Lufungula health centre in Kinshasa](<https://actualite.cd/2024/07/13/kinshasa-lanicns-dote-trois-etablissements-hospitaliers-de-kits-medicaux-digitalises>), to allow the first modules to be implemented.

**Where data is digitised.** A University of Kananga survey of seven hospitals in that city, covering 2023, [found 4.7% of 86 staff managing patient information electronically](<https://www.scirp.net/journal/paperinformation?paperid=136674>).

**Last twelve months.** On 16 June 2026 ANICNS [opened a call for the now-mandatory approval of hospital information systems, each to include a complete computerised patient record](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/16/actualite-rdc-homologation-sih-rdc/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** ACP reported on 13 February 2025 that ANICNS [handed the Kasaï-Oriental provincial health division 516 computers, 206 tablets and 206 routers to equip 206 care establishments](<https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/kasai-oriental-un-lot-dequipements-numeriques-offert-a-la-division-provinciale-de-la-sante/>).
