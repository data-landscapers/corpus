# Zimbabwe

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The U.S. President's Malaria Initiative (PMI), in a profile published in 2023, [describes DHIS2 as the Ministry of Health and Child Care's system, to which donors and partners have very limited access](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>). The ministry's National Health Strategy 2021-2025 [names DHIS2 among its routine aggregate systems for monitoring and evaluation](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/ZWE_Zimbabwe_National-Health-Strategy-for-Zimbabwe_2021-2025.pdf>). The Global Fund said in August 2025 that [its financing supports data analysis and capacity building at sub-national levels](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) of the national health management information system.

**Tiers in use.** PMI's 2023 profile states that [the HMIS collects routine aggregate information from four levels: over 1,700 primary health centres, clinics and rural hospitals, about 140 district-level hospitals, eight provincial hospitals and six quaternary hospitals](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same profile states that [monthly collection is on paper at village, health facility and hospital levels, and that the district health information officer enters the paper records into the Zimbabwe DHIS2](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>). A 2025 abstract on the DHIS2 community site states that [facilities running Impilo, the ministry's patient record system, send data to DHIS2 by real-time, mobile-backup and manual-backup pipelines](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).

**Last twelve months.** The Countdown to 2030 synthesis report, written with ministry staff, states that [the national reporting rate of health facility data in DHIS2 was above 92% in 2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Zimbabwe-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** PMI's routine table gives [1,776 health facilities reporting into the HMIS/DHIS2 in 2022, with 96% of expected monthly reports received](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the Zimbabwe National AIDS Council's [DHIS2 Tracker for HIV prevention](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) and the malaria programme's [DHIS2 Tracker Capture case-based surveillance system](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>). Single-function: the [Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-takes-diagnostics-closer-to-rural-communities/>), the [electronic logistics management information system (eLMIS)](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>), the [Zimbabwe Assisted Pull System (ZAPS) ordering system](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>), the [Rapid Disease Notification System and Weekly Disease Surveillance Database](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>), the [eSample transport system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>), the National Pharmaceutical Company's [warehouse management system](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) and PMI's [Malaria Data Integration and Visualization for Eradication (MDIVE)](<https://mesamalaria.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ZIMZABWE-Malaria-Profile-PMI-FY-2024.pdf>). Enabling: the [Starlink kits at rural health facilities](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058417/starlink-kits-gather-dust-at-government-rural-hospitals-as-parent-ministry-dithers-on-subscription>), the [Starlink Pilot Program](<https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijrsi/uploads/vol12-iss15-pg2476-2481-202511_pdf.pdf>) in Matabeleland South, [Solar for Health](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) and the [medical school's telemedicine application](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/digital-health-documents/global-observatory-on-digital-health/zwe_ehealth.pdf>).

***Not held***. No count or share of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the 96% counts reports received. No strategy current at 30 September 2026 is held, and no dated event in the last twelve months.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Global Fund said in August 2025 that [the Government of Zimbabwe has been developing a national EMR system since February 2015, culminating in Impilo, for use in public health facilities](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). The ministry's National Health Strategy 2021-2025 [commits to supporting the deployment of what it calls the electronic health record (Impilo) system](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/ZWE_Zimbabwe_National-Health-Strategy-for-Zimbabwe_2021-2025.pdf>). The Global Fund [has put over US$43.1 million into digital health in Zimbabwe since 2014, US$9.85 million of it in the 2024-2026 grant cycle](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Permanent Secretary for Health and Child Care told The Sunday Mail on 17 November 2024 that [the facilities with Impilo are spread across provinces and districts and include clinics, hospitals and other health centres](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/1-322-facilities-to-get-electronic-health-record-system/>).

**Where data is digitised.** A 2025 abstract on the DHIS2 community site states that [Impilo captures real-time clinical and administrative data for services including HIV testing, antiretroviral therapy and maternal and child health, stored in centralised repositories and a national data warehouse](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).

**Last twelve months.** The Herald on 23 September 2026 [described Impilo as the Ministry of Health's own electronic health records platform](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-takes-diagnostics-closer-to-rural-communities/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Global Fund reports [1,254 health facilities implementing Impilo as of August 2025, with deployment planned for all 1,900 health facilities](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). The Permanent Secretary [put deployment at 1,178 health facilities](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/1-322-facilities-to-get-electronic-health-record-system/>) on 17 November 2024. The Matabeleland South provincial health team reported [110 of the province's 153 health facilities (71.9%) with Impilo fully functional](<https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijrsi/uploads/vol12-iss15-pg2476-2481-202511_pdf.pdf>) in May 2025.
