# South Sudan

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In the ministry's report for 2024-25 the Minister of Health [calls DHIS2 the ministry's official national health information platform](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). The Health Sector Strategic Plan 2023-2027 sets targets for health facilities reporting directly through DHIS2. They are [55% by 2025 and 90% by 2027](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records [HMIS strengthening as financed by IDA grants and a multi-donor trust fund](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020926085056524/pdf/P181385-60d1e87d-2a49-4159-807e-05791fd83204.pdf>). [WHO implements it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020926085056524/pdf/P181385-60d1e87d-2a49-4159-807e-05791fd83204.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO states that [monthly reporting vouchers for hospitals, primary health care centres and primary health care units were configured in DHIS2 in the third quarter of 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-02/WHO%20South_Sudan_Annual_Report_2025_DigitalLow_v6_1_compressed.pdf>). The ministry finds for 2024-25 that [many hospitals submit DHIS2 reports regularly](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry states for 2024-25 that [hospitals and primary health care centres often lack the computers, stable internet and trained staff](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>) to enter data directly into DHIS2. As a result [entry is frequently centralised or delayed](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). Its assessment with WHO of February 2025 finds [DHIS2 implemented at national and sub-national or district levels](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records that [facilities in DHIS2 were verified with State Ministries of Health](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020926085056524/pdf/P181385-60d1e87d-2a49-4159-807e-05791fd83204.pdf>). Closed or non-existent ones were removed. FLLOW-M is a malaria and climate dashboard inside the national DHIS2 instance. [A pilot of it was launched in July 2026](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry reports that [30% of all health facilities reported directly through DHIS2 in 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). They were those with tablets and computers. The [plan's target was 55%](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>).

Also single-function: [NIS](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>) for nutrition, the [IDSR reporting system](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-06/WHO%20South%20Sudan%202023%20Annual%20Report.pdf>) and the [ODK mobile data collection tool](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2024-06/WHO%20South%20Sudan%202023%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNDP South Sudan [issued a tender on 11 March 2026](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/293539>) for an OpenMRS pilot. A 2019 electronic record at two hospitals [was PEPFAR-supported](<https://icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/ICAP_SouthSudan_eRegister_Dissemination-Report-21April2026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO's September 2026 series [names Lafon PHCC, a primary health care centre, among the facilities using the Digital Care Companion](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>). The 2019 record at [Juba Teaching Hospital and Nimule Hospital](<https://icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/ICAP_SouthSudan_eRegister_Dissemination-Report-21April2026.pdf>) did not reach routine clinical use.

**Where data is digitised.** The Digital Care Companion is a clinical decision support system. WHO says it [records each patient encounter in the facility and links a patient's visits](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A UNDP tender of March 2026 [seeks an OpenMRS pilot at two Juba facilities](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/293539>). They are Al Sabah Children's Hospital and Munuki primary health care centre.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In September 2026 WHO [counted 12 facilities in 10 states and administrative areas logging visits on the Digital Care Companion](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>). It does not say how many are primary clinics.
