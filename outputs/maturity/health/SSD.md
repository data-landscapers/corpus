# South Sudan

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In the ministry's report for 2024-25 the Minister of Health [calls DHIS2 the ministry's official national health information platform](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). The Health Sector Strategic Plan 2023-2027 [sets targets of 55% of health facilities reporting directly through DHIS2 by 2025 and 90% by 2027](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records [HMIS strengthening as financed by IDA grants and a multi-donor trust fund and implemented by WHO](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020926085056524/pdf/P181385-60d1e87d-2a49-4159-807e-05791fd83204.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO states that [monthly reporting vouchers for hospitals, primary health care centres and primary health care units were configured in DHIS2 in the third quarter of 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-02/WHO%20South_Sudan_Annual_Report_2025_DigitalLow_v6_1_compressed.pdf>). The ministry finds for 2024-25 that [many hospitals submit DHIS2 reports regularly](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry states for 2024-25 that [hospitals and primary health care centres often lack the computers, stable internet and trained staff to enter data directly into DHIS2, so entry is frequently centralised or delayed](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>). Its assessment with WHO of February 2025 finds [DHIS2 implemented at national and sub-national or district levels](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's mission of November 2025 records that [facilities in DHIS2 were verified with State Ministries of Health and closed or non-existent ones removed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020926085056524/pdf/P181385-60d1e87d-2a49-4159-807e-05791fd83204.pdf>). [A pilot of the FLLOW-M malaria and climate dashboard inside the national DHIS2 instance was launched in July 2026](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry reports that [30% of all health facilities, those equipped with tablets and computers, reported directly through DHIS2 in 2025, against the plan's target of 55%](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>).
