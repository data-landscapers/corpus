# Guinea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene issued a performance bulletin for the first quarter of 2026. On it, the ministry's Bureau de Stratégie et de Développement [runs the national health information system (SNIS) and DHIS2 Guinée](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>). The bureau publishes the quarterly SNIS bulletin. The bulletin [places the SNIS under strategic orientation 3 of the PNDS 2025-2034](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>). It [lists GFF/R4D as the partner supporting DHIS2 in that quarter](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry reports on the first quarter of 2026. [All regions and all levels of the health pyramid transmitted their data on time](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>). National completeness was [100% and promptness 99%](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry published its PASA2 programme page in 2025. It states that [health posts, health centres and prefectural hospitals collect SNIS data on paper primary tools](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). The data are keyed into DHIS2. A CSEA policy brief of November 2025 describes weak rural connectivity. It states that this [forces local health centres to rely on manual reporting and paper records](<https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/47281988.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In the first quarter of 2026 the SNIS [reached what the ministry calls a historic record of 100% completeness](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>). WASH indicators were integrated into DHIS2. Prefectural [DHIS2 dashboards were in development](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's statistical yearbook for 2021 covers first-level health centres and improved health centres. It says [their monthly reports were either entered in DHIS2 or sent to the district team for entry](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/sante/Annuaire_2021_draft0_30_06_2022_VF.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Visa Medical is a digital medical record. It [is built and run by the private start-up Kouma Academy with the Ministry of Health's support](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>), as reported in August 2026. The OpenClinic hospital information system [was installed in five facilities with the support of the Belgian agency Enabel](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The non-profit Clinic+O [runs its record in collaboration with the Ministry of Health](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>). [The Swiss Re Foundation funds the project from November 2025 to December 2026](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Tiers in use.** By August 2026 Visa Medical [was deployed in 19 public establishments, Dubréka hospital among them](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>). A ministry mission of 17 to 22 August 2026 [reviewed five digitised facilities in the Mamou and Dalaba districts running OpenClinic](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The Dalaba prefectural hospital was among them. The Swiss Re Foundation published a page in March 2026. It has the Clinic+O record [in use in rural communities and at Community Health Centers](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Where data is digitised.** At Dubréka hospital in August 2026 [clinicians retrieved a patient's consultations and tests in real time from the Visa Medical record](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>). In the five Mamou and Dalaba facilities [data are entered directly into OpenClinic](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). Clinic+O's [community health workers capture patient information on tablets in an offline-first electronic medical record](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The Dalaba prefectural hospital [deployed OpenClinic in January 2026](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The [Clinic+O project began in November 2025](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Swiss Re Foundation described Guinea's rural Community Health Centers in March 2026. It stated that they [are often understaffed and rely on paper-based systems](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).
