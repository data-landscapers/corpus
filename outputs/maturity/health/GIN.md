# Guinea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene's Bureau de Stratégie et de Développement [runs the national health information system (SNIS) and DHIS2 Guinée and publishes the quarterly SNIS bulletin](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>), on its performance bulletin for the first quarter of 2026. The bulletin [places the SNIS under strategic orientation 3 of the PNDS 2025-2034](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>) and [lists GFF/R4D as the partner supporting DHIS2 in that quarter](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry reports that in the first quarter of 2026 [all regions and all levels of the health pyramid transmitted their data on time, with national completeness at 100% and promptness at 99%](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's PASA2 programme page, published in 2025, states that [health posts, health centres and prefectural hospitals collect SNIS data on paper primary tools, which are keyed into DHIS2](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). A CSEA policy brief of November 2025 states that [weak rural connectivity forces local health centres to rely on manual reporting and paper records](<https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/47281988.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In the first quarter of 2026 the SNIS [reached what the ministry calls a historic record of 100% completeness, WASH indicators were integrated into DHIS2 and prefectural DHIS2 dashboards were in development](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's statistical yearbook for 2021 says that [the monthly reports of first-level health centres and improved health centres were either entered in DHIS2 or sent to the district team for entry](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/sante/Annuaire_2021_draft0_30_06_2022_VF.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [SPAQ](<https://www.guinee7.com/2026/06/16/conakry-le-japon-debloque-pres-de-400-000-dollars-pour-numeriser-les-soins/>), [REC maternité](<https://guineenews.org/2025/11/27/interoperabilite-tenue-dun-atelier-cle-pour-la-modernisation-du-systeme-dinformation-sanitaire-en-guinee>) and [DHIS2 Tracker](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). Single-function: [DHIS2 Surveillance Guinée](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>), the logistics system [eSIGL](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/sante/Annuaire_2021_draft0_30_06_2022_VF.pdf>) and the epidemiological surveillance [Tracker](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf>). Community: [Kendeya](<https://sante.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bulletin_Performance_Sanitaire_N1_T1_28052026.pdf>). Enabling: the [planned Réseau National Numérique de la Santé](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf>), the [planned Réseau national de télémédecine](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf>), the [telemedicine unit at Donka hospital](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf>) and the [telemedicine platform at Faranah regional hospital](<https://p4h.world/app/uploads/2024/09/Strategie-Nationale-Sante-Numerique_2021-2025_Guinee.x73677.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of health centres or health posts entering their own reports is held; the 100% completeness is data transmitted. No domestic budget line for the SNIS is named.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Visa Medical, a digital medical record, [is built and run by the private start-up Kouma Academy with the Ministry of Health's support](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>), as reported in August 2026. The OpenClinic hospital information system [was installed in five facilities with the support of the Belgian agency Enabel](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The non-profit Clinic+O [runs its record in collaboration with the Ministry of Health](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>), and [the Swiss Re Foundation funds the project from November 2025 to December 2026](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Tiers in use.** By August 2026 Visa Medical [was deployed in 19 public establishments, Dubréka hospital among them](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>). A ministry mission of 17 to 22 August 2026 [reviewed five digitised facilities in the Mamou and Dalaba districts running OpenClinic, the Dalaba prefectural hospital among them](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The Swiss Re Foundation's page of March 2026 has the Clinic+O record [in use in rural communities and at Community Health Centers](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Where data is digitised.** At Dubréka hospital in August 2026 [clinicians retrieved a patient's consultations and tests in real time from the Visa Medical record](<https://guineenews.org/2026/08/13/financer-la-digitalisation-des-hopitaux-sans-endetter-letat-le-pari-reussi-dune-healthtech-guineenne-devenue-panafricaine/>). In the five Mamou and Dalaba facilities [data are entered directly into OpenClinic](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). Clinic+O's [community health workers capture patient information on tablets in an offline-first electronic medical record](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The Dalaba prefectural hospital [deployed OpenClinic in January 2026](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). The [Clinic+O project began in November 2025](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Swiss Re Foundation stated in March 2026 that [Guinea's rural Community Health Centers are often understaffed and rely on paper-based systems](<https://www.swissrefoundation.org/what-we-do/projects/access-to-health/Clinic-O--Strengthening-community-based-primary-healthcare-in-rural-Guinea.html>).
