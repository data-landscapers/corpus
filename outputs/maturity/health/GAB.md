# Gabon

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Decree 0292/PR/MS of 21 July 2024](<https://journal-officiel.ga/21149-0292-pr-ms-/>) charges the Ministry of Health's planning directorate-general with health information. The directorate-general collects, processes and disseminates it nationwide. The national health development plan was adopted on 23 February 2024. It [sets a DHIS2 platform integrating all health programmes for the end of December 2028](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>). It costs its health information axis at 3,509,631,187 FCFA. DHIS2 was [launched nationally on 6 December 2024](<https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/>), as HISP Rwanda reported. WHO Gabon and the Global Fund gave financial and technical support.

**Tiers in use.** WHO Gabon describes DHIS2 at its December 2024 launch. It [integrated all functional public health facilities](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>). Private facilities were [to follow in 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** WHO Gabon reports on those trained to use DHIS2 in 2024. They were [122 data managers and 10 regional health directors](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>). They came [from all 51 health departments and 10 regions](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO Gabon [directed its 2025 support to data quality, reporting completeness and timeliness, training more than 76 professionals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The 2024-2028 plan [provides for training data managers at the three levels of the health pyramid](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>).

Single-function: the [DHIS2 logistics indicators and dashboards](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SIMR weekly notification of deaths in hospitals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>) and [ODK Compagnon](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Gabonactu reported on 25 October 2025 on the health information system Gabon calls eGabon-SIS. [The state digital agency ANINF implements it](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>). A project technical committee follows it. Its supplier [won a tender launched by the Ministry of Health](<https://www.gabonreview.com/egabon-sis-vers-un-hopital-sans-papier/>). The contract was handed over on 16 February 2024. The World Bank [approved a US$56 million loan on 3 June 2016](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P132824>). Of it, [US$40.08 million was for the national health information system](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P132824>). The project [was officially closed on 24 June 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-sante-cloture-du-projet-egabon-sis/>).

**Tiers in use.** On 11 February 2026 the system [worked in 13 pilot sites across Grand Libreville](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/02/11/projet-egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-satisfaite-du-bon-fonctionnement-du-systeme-dans-les-hopitaux-pilotes/>), on Gabonactu's account. The Egypt-Gabon cooperation hospital was among them. The Libreville university hospital and the regional hospitals were not yet covered.

**Where data is digitised.** The chief doctor of the Lalala health centre in Libreville spoke on 23 October 2025. The doctor said [a patient can now be found in different facilities under a unique identifier](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 February 2026 [the health minister asked the World Bank to bring the system into the Libreville university hospital](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/02/11/projet-egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-satisfaite-du-bon-fonctionnement-du-systeme-dans-les-hopitaux-pilotes/>). The World Bank agreed. The project closed on 24 June 2026. The authorities then [named maintenance, cybersecurity, training and extension to the whole territory as the next priorities](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-sante-cloture-du-projet-egabon-sis/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [Two health centres, Lalala and Glass, were confirmed using the system on 23 October 2025](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>). They are among 13 pilot sites in Grand Libreville.

Single-function: the [DHIS2 logistics indicators and dashboards](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SIMR weekly notification of deaths in hospitals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>) and [ODK Compagnon](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>).
