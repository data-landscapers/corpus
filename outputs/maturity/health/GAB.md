# Gabon

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Decree 0292/PR/MS of 21 July 2024 charges the Ministry of Health's planning directorate-general with collecting, processing and disseminating health information nationwide](<https://journal-officiel.ga/21149-0292-pr-ms-/>). The national health development plan adopted on 23 February 2024 [sets a DHIS2 platform integrating all health programmes for the end of December 2028, and costs its health information axis at 3,509,631,187 FCFA](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>). DHIS2 was [launched nationally on 6 December 2024 with financial and technical support from WHO Gabon and the Global Fund](<https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/>), as HISP Rwanda reported.

**Tiers in use.** WHO Gabon states that at its December 2024 launch DHIS2 [integrated all functional public health facilities, with private facilities to follow in 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** WHO Gabon reports that those trained to use DHIS2 in 2024 were [122 data managers and 10 regional health directors, from all 51 health departments and 10 regions](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO Gabon [directed its 2025 support to data quality, reporting completeness and timeliness, training more than 76 professionals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The 2024-2028 plan [provides for training data managers at the three levels of the health pyramid](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 Tracker for hypertension and diabetes follow-up](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 logistics indicators and dashboards](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SIMR weekly notification of deaths in hospitals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), [ODK Compagnon](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), [ESPEN Collect](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SRMNIA scorecard](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>) and, planned for 2024 to 2028, the [e-SIMR notification platform](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>) and [computerised management tools for medicines and health products](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>). Enabling: [tele-echography under eGabon-SIS in four health departments of Ngounié](<https://agpgabon.ga/ngounie-sante-extension-du-projet-egabon-sis-dans-quatre-departements/>), [telemedicine in six selected medical centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052125123532941/pdf/P132824-c6a10e3c-f5f5-41bc-ab52-9252846fc3f2.pdf>) and the 2024-2028 plan's [tele-echography pilot](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and no source names the tiers that report. The published completeness figures disagree, and no dated event in the last twelve months is held.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** [The state digital agency ANINF implements the health information system Gabon calls eGabon-SIS, with a project technical committee following it](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>), as Gabonactu reported on 25 October 2025. Its supplier [won a tender launched by the Ministry of Health, the contract handed over on 16 February 2024](<https://www.gabonreview.com/egabon-sis-vers-un-hopital-sans-papier/>). The World Bank [approved a US$56 million loan on 3 June 2016, US$40.08 million of it for the national health information system](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P132824>). The project [was officially closed on 24 June 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-sante-cloture-du-projet-egabon-sis/>).

**Tiers in use.** On 11 February 2026 the system [worked in 13 pilot sites across Grand Libreville, the Egypt-Gabon cooperation hospital among them, with the Libreville university hospital and the regional hospitals not yet covered](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/02/11/projet-egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-satisfaite-du-bon-fonctionnement-du-systeme-dans-les-hopitaux-pilotes/>), on Gabonactu's account.

**Where data is digitised.** On 23 October 2025 the chief doctor of the Lalala health centre in Libreville said [a patient can now be found in different facilities under a unique identifier](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 February 2026 [the health minister asked the World Bank, which agreed, to bring the system into the Libreville university hospital](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/02/11/projet-egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-satisfaite-du-bon-fonctionnement-du-systeme-dans-les-hopitaux-pilotes/>). At the project's closing on 24 June 2026 [the authorities named maintenance, cybersecurity, training and extension to the whole territory as the next priorities](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-sante-cloture-du-projet-egabon-sis/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [Two health centres, Lalala and Glass, were confirmed using the system on 23 October 2025](<https://www.gabonactu.com/blog/2025/10/25/egabon-sis-la-banque-mondiale-touche-du-doigt-leffectivite-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-projet-dans-le-grand-libreville/>), among 13 pilot sites in Grand Libreville.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 Tracker for hypertension and diabetes follow-up](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 logistics indicators and dashboards](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SIMR weekly notification of deaths in hospitals](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), [ODK Compagnon](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), [ESPEN Collect](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%20OMS%20Gabon%202025.pdf>), the [SRMNIA scorecard](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Annual%20Report%202024%20Verso%20V.Final_.pdf>) and, planned for 2024 to 2028, the [e-SIMR notification platform](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>) and [computerised management tools for medicines and health products](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>). Enabling: [tele-echography under eGabon-SIS in four health departments of Ngounié](<https://agpgabon.ga/ngounie-sante-extension-du-projet-egabon-sis-dans-quatre-departements/>), [telemedicine in six selected medical centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052125123532941/pdf/P132824-c6a10e3c-f5f5-41bc-ab52-9252846fc3f2.pdf>) and the 2024-2028 plan's [tele-echography pilot](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/GAB_Gabon_Plan-national-de-d%C3%A9veloppement-sanitaire_2024-2028.pdf>).
