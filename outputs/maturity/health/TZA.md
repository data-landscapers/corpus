# Tanzania

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Monitoring and Evaluation Unit [coordinated the HMIS data drawn from DHIS2 for 2024, with the University of Dar es Salaam and HISP Tanzania giving technical support in maintaining the platform](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>). The Health Sector Strategic Plan V, for July 2021 to June 2026, [carried strategic directions for strengthening the HMIS](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>). Informants interviewed in April and May 2022 said [most money for the HMIS was pooled from development partners in the Basket Fund and managed by government](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's guidelines of May 2019 describe MTUHA, its name for the HMIS, as [collecting routine data from dispensaries, health centres and council hospitals through the councils, while national, zonal, specialised and private hospitals use different platforms](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's methodology for its 2024 statistics states that [each facility aggregates its registers and tally sheets monthly and the figures are entered into DHIS2 at council level, for 11,805 public and private health facilities](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** No dated event falls inside the twelve months. The latest is of 1 October 2025, when the University of Dar es Salaam's DHIS2 Lab reported that a workshop led by the Ministry of Health Zanzibar [validated data flows from programme and point-of-service systems into ZHMIS, Zanzibar's HMIS, and moved the integration towards the live environment](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The guidelines of May 2019 state that [aggregated data is entered into DHIS in some health facilities, and at council level where facilities use paper](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [biometric fingerprint identification at HIV care and treatment sites](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>) and the [HIV and tuberculosis and leprosy programme systems](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). Single-function: [eLMIS](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [eIDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [HRHIS3](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>), [MPDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/news-single/tanzania-kuendelea-kutumia-takwimu-za-kitaifa-na-kimataifa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya>), [M-Mama](<https://www.moh.go.tz/index.php/news-single/matumizi-ya-data-na-teknolojia-yaimarisha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>), the [electronic prescription system](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-south-korea-explore-ai-to-improve-healthcare-5578968>) under development, the [vaccine management digital systems](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Digital%20Square%20-%20Connecting%20the%20World%20for%20Better%20Health%20Factsheet.%201.pdf>) and the [Laboratory Management Information System](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). Community: [Afya-Tek](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-health-push-puts-ai-hospital-technology-on-procurement-agenda-5579848>) and the [Unified Community System (UCS)](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). Enabling: the [telemedicine and diagnostics programme of Tanzania Data Lab with UNICEF and PATH](<https://aiconference.cipit.org/documents/the-state-of-ai-in-africa-report.pdf>) and [TeleHealth](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held. No financing for the HMIS dated within the last three years is held, and no plan period after June 2026.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The mid-term review of the Health Sector Strategic Plan V, published in March 2025, states that the Ministry of Health [developed and launched Afya eHMS for all regional hospitals](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>) and [plans to phase out the vendor-dependent Medipro and E-Medical used in zonal and specialised hospitals](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). It records that [the Digital Health Strategy 2019-2024 had run out and its 2025-2030 successor was still being developed](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Citizen reported on 1 September 2026 that [electronic medical record coverage had reached 100 percent of national, specialised, zonal and regional referral hospitals, and that adoption had expanded across district hospitals, health centres and dispensaries](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-health-push-puts-ai-hospital-technology-on-procurement-agenda-5579848>). The mid-term review of March 2025 states that [all 28 regional hospitals use Afya eHMS](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's guidelines of May 2019 state that [most confidential health data is held in health facilities in paper-based registers](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Health said on 1 October 2025 that the country [has an integrated electronic medical records system linking a patient's information from community level through health centres and district hospitals to national level](<https://www.moh.go.tz/index.php/news-single/matumizi-ya-data-na-teknolojia-yaimarisha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The mid-term review, citing the SARA survey of 2023, gives [23.25% of all health facilities using GOT-HoMIS, the government's facility system, against 44.9% of health centres and 10.63% of dispensaries](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). In March 2025 it states that [more than 3,000 primary health care facilities use it as their electronic medical record system](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).

Single-function: [eLMIS](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [eIDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [HRHIS3](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>), [MPDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/news-single/tanzania-kuendelea-kutumia-takwimu-za-kitaifa-na-kimataifa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya>), [M-Mama](<https://www.moh.go.tz/index.php/news-single/matumizi-ya-data-na-teknolojia-yaimarisha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>), the [electronic prescription system](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-south-korea-explore-ai-to-improve-healthcare-5578968>) under development, the [vaccine management digital systems](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Digital%20Square%20-%20Connecting%20the%20World%20for%20Better%20Health%20Factsheet.%201.pdf>) and the [Laboratory Management Information System](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).
