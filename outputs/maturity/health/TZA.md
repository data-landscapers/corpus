# Tanzania

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Monitoring and Evaluation Unit [coordinated the HMIS data drawn from DHIS2 for 2024](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>). The University of Dar es Salaam and HISP Tanzania gave technical support in maintaining the platform. The Health Sector Strategic Plan V, for July 2021 to June 2026, [carried strategic directions for strengthening the HMIS](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>). Informants interviewed in April and May 2022 said [most money for the HMIS was pooled from development partners in the Basket Fund](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>). It was [managed by government](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's guidelines of May 2019 describe MTUHA, its name for the HMIS, as [collecting routine data from dispensaries, health centres and council hospitals through the councils](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). National, zonal, specialised and private hospitals [use different platforms](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's 2024 statistics methodology covers 11,805 public and private health facilities. It states that [each facility aggregates its registers and tally sheets monthly](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>). The figures are [entered into DHIS2 at council level](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** No dated event falls inside the twelve months. The latest is of 1 October 2025. The University of Dar es Salaam's DHIS2 Lab then reported on a workshop led by the Ministry of Health Zanzibar. It [validated data flows from programme and point-of-service systems into ZHMIS, Zanzibar's HMIS](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>). It [moved the integration towards the live environment](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The guidelines of May 2019 state that [aggregated data is entered into DHIS in some health facilities](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). Where facilities use paper, [it is entered at council level](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: [biometric fingerprint identification at HIV care and treatment sites](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>) and the [HIV and tuberculosis and leprosy programme systems](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). Single-function: [eLMIS](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [eIDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/337/ef7/698337ef72583788742967.pdf>), [HRHIS3](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>), [MPDSR](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/news-single/tanzania-kuendelea-kutumia-takwimu-za-kitaifa-na-kimataifa-kuboresha-huduma-za-afya>), [M-Mama](<https://www.moh.go.tz/index.php/news-single/matumizi-ya-data-na-teknolojia-yaimarisha-huduma-za-afya-nchini>), the [electronic prescription system](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-south-korea-explore-ai-to-improve-healthcare-5578968>) under development, the [vaccine management digital systems](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Digital%20Square%20-%20Connecting%20the%20World%20for%20Better%20Health%20Factsheet.%201.pdf>) and the [Laboratory Management Information System](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>). Community: [Afya-Tek](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-health-push-puts-ai-hospital-technology-on-procurement-agenda-5579848>) and the [Unified Community System (UCS)](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). Enabling: the [telemedicine and diagnostics programme of Tanzania Data Lab with UNICEF and PATH](<https://aiconference.cipit.org/documents/the-state-of-ai-in-africa-report.pdf>) and [TeleHealth](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10617168/>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The mid-term review of the Health Sector Strategic Plan V was published in March 2025. It states that the Ministry of Health [developed and launched Afya eHMS for all regional hospitals](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). Zonal and specialised hospitals use the vendor-dependent Medipro and E-Medical. The ministry [plans to phase them out](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). The review records that [the Digital Health Strategy 2019-2024 had run out](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). Its [2025-2030 successor was still being developed](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Citizen reported on 1 September 2026 that [electronic medical record coverage had reached 100 percent of national, specialised, zonal and regional referral hospitals](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-health-push-puts-ai-hospital-technology-on-procurement-agenda-5579848>). It said [adoption had expanded across district hospitals, health centres and dispensaries](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-s-digital-health-push-puts-ai-hospital-technology-on-procurement-agenda-5579848>). The mid-term review of March 2025 states that [all 28 regional hospitals use Afya eHMS](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's guidelines date from May 2019. They state that [most confidential health data is held in health facilities in paper-based registers](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/661/fe3/71d/661fe371de96f490413146.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The integrated electronic medical records system was introduced in October 2025. The Ministry of Health said in October 2026 that [it now connects 222 public facilities](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-connects-222-public-health-facilities-to-digital-records-system-5630008>). District, regional referral, zonal, specialised and national hospitals are among them.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** GOT-HoMIS is the government's facility system. The mid-term review cites the SARA survey of 2023. It gives [23.25% of all health facilities using GOT-HoMIS, against 44.9% of health centres and 10.63% of dispensaries](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>). In March 2025 it states that [more than 3,000 primary health care facilities use it as their electronic medical record system](<https://ihi.or.tz/media/List_and_report/MTR_Data_Management_-_REPORT_iGUVS7W.pdf>).
