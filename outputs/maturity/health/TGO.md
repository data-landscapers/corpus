# Togo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's monitoring and evaluation plan of October 2023, for the national health development plan 2023-2027, [names DHIS2 as the platform of what the ministry calls the SNIS, the national health information system](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>). The ministry's directorate DSNISI coordinates it. The Global Fund's Office of the Inspector General reported in June 2025 on Grant Cycle 7, 2024 to 2026. It found [Global Fund investment in the health management information system and related systems is EUR 9.8 million](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>). It found that [Togo has no coordinated national digitalisation strategy for health](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan names [the peripheral care units (USP) and the CHP, CHR and CHU hospitals](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>) as producing and keying SNIS reports. The April 2022 procedures manual states that [DHIS2 was scaled up to all health facilities from 2018](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/MOP_SNIS_TOGO_2%C3%A8me_Edition_VF.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The plan [requires each health facility to key its validated monthly or quarterly activity report into DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>). The deadline is [the 5th of the following month](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** HISP's July 2026 round-up reports [a field mission to integrate the eRDQA data quality app into national DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). It was [tested at the regional hospitals of Sokodé and Dapaong](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>) and the health centres of Kadambara, Ataloté and Kpessidè.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's plan [gives 96.5% for 2021 as the share of all health facilities due to report that keyed a complete SNIS report into DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>). WHO Togo's 2024 annual report [gives 89.2% completeness for 2021](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: [Digital Vaccine Passports through DHIS2](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). The Togo Digital 2025 strategy plans [a digital vaccination booklet and mother and child health and vaccination follow-up](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>). Single-function: the national malaria repository and [DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance and non-communicable disease monitoring](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). Others are the [DHIS2 immunization package](<https://dhis2.org/togo-immunization-package/>), which replaced DVD-MT, and [DHIS2 for dengue surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>). Others are [ODK forms for surveillance of vaccine-preventable diseases and EIOS](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>). Others are [DHIS2 in the 2023 mosquito net campaign](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>), the paper-based LMIS and the planned eLMIS. Others are EDT Web, the medical store's ERP and the [weekly epidemiological surveillance report](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>). Community: [SanteComTogo](<https://medic.org/stories/how-santecomtogo-is-transforming-community-health-insights-from-the-field/>), the [community health worker's monthly report keyed into DHIS2](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/MOP_SNIS_TOGO_2%C3%A8me_Edition_VF.pdf>) and [community DHIS2](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>).

***Not found***. No share for primary clinics alone has been found, nothing newer than 2021, and the 96.5% is printed without its counts. No domestic budget line is stated, and no count of community health workers keying their reports into DHIS2.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank's additional-financing paper is dated 24 December 2025. It states that [since October 2025 the health ministry has been responsible for the health and insurance systems layer](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). The layer includes the electronic health record (EHR). The financing [pays EUR 866,112.50 on establishment of the system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). It pays [EUR 173,223 for every 10 contracted facilities with an EHR](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The paper [records "No" at December 2025 for the establishment of an electronic health record system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The Togo Digital 2025 strategy dates from April 2021. It [plans a digital and centralised medical record, as something to be developed](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>).

**Last twelve months.** On 24 December 2025 the World Bank [put forward additional financing, a EUR 60.6 million credit and US$11 million in grants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). It set new conditions for establishing an EHR in facilities contracted under the national health insurance scheme. The [work starts with a pilot](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The paper's results framework [gives a baseline of 0 facilities contracted with the national health insurance scheme holding an EHR at December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). About [335 facilities are contracted](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: [Digital Vaccine Passports through DHIS2](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099010826100581635/pdf/BOSIB-d027e46d-cef1-4596-b8c9-b647398e3408.pdf>). The Togo Digital 2025 strategy plans [a digital vaccination booklet and mother and child health and vaccination follow-up](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>). Single-function: the national malaria repository and [DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance and non-communicable disease monitoring](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). Others are the [DHIS2 immunization package](<https://dhis2.org/togo-immunization-package/>), which replaced DVD-MT, and [DHIS2 for dengue surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>). Others are [ODK forms for surveillance of vaccine-preventable diseases and EIOS](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>). Others are [DHIS2 in the 2023 mosquito net campaign](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>), the paper-based LMIS and the planned eLMIS. Others are EDT Web, the medical store's ERP and the [weekly epidemiological surveillance report](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Final_Plan%20de%20suivi%20%26%20%C3%A9valuation%20PNDS%202023-2027.pdf>). Community: [SanteComTogo](<https://medic.org/stories/how-santecomtogo-is-transforming-community-health-insights-from-the-field/>), the [community health worker's monthly report keyed into DHIS2](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/MOP_SNIS_TOGO_2%C3%A8me_Edition_VF.pdf>) and [community DHIS2](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/gbqltprg/oig_gf-oig-25-005_report_en.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source states in terms that patient records are on paper at every tier, and the baseline does not cover facilities outside the insurance scheme. Where records are digitised is not held.
