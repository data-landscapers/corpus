# Somalia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's bulletin for July to September 2023 states that [the ministry decided to use DHIS2 as the exclusive national HMIS platform, run by its HMIS Section](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>), and that [a five-year costed Health Information Strategy was prepared and finalised](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). Under a three-year agreement signed with UNICEF in August 2025, [HISP maintains the DHIS2 platform and trains ministry staff](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry reports [579 public health facilities reporting to DHIS2 in July to September 2023, on data sets for health-centre outpatients and hospital outpatients and inpatients](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The WHO and ministry assessment of September 2022 found that [most facilities record on paper registries and send monthly summary forms to the district HMIS officer, who enters them into DHIS2](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>). The ministry's case study of 2023 to 2025 reports that [in the 111 primary facilities running RAAD, its electronic health record, routine care documentation generates the DHIS2 aggregate reports automatically](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Last twelve months.** ISDP [ran a three-day HMIS training of trainers in Bosaso on 3 to 5 February 2026 for 16 health workers from the health centres and primary health units it supports](<https://isdpsom.org/health-management-information-systems-hmis-training/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's case study of 2023 to 2025 reports that [111 public primary healthcare facilities across six states produce their DHIS2 aggregate reports digitally from the RAAD record](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). A commentary of August 2026 by ministry HMIS staff says [DHIS2-based reporting remains uneven across facilities and administrative levels](<https://www.dovepress.com/strengthening-district--and-regional-level-supportive-supervision-for--peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-JHL>).

Single-function: the [Pharmaceutical Information Management System (PIMS)](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>), the [Electronic Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS)](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/ab35c724-c83d-4a74-b39e-35c38dd2dcbc/download>), the [Nutrition Module](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>), the [IDSR data set on DHIS2](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [Early Warning, Alert and Response Network (EWARN)](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), [HeRAMS](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [logistic management information system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [laboratory data set under DHIS2](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [nutrition reporting system on ONA](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [TB surveillance system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [malaria surveillance system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), the [HIV reporting system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>), [AFP polio surveillance](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>) and [measles surveillance](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** By its own account the Ministry of Health [developed RAAD as a national electronic health record for primary healthcare, implemented between 2023 and 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). Its bulletin for July to September 2023 says [the pilot was supported by the Government of Sweden through SPIDER](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). The Caafimaad+ Digitalized Data System (C+ DDS) is [run by the CaafimaadPlus consortium of ten NGOs, in partnership with the Federal Ministry of Health](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>), and [funded by EU Humanitarian Aid](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>), as the consortium stated in April 2026.

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's case study places [RAAD in public primary healthcare facilities only, between 2023 and 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Where data is digitised.** RAAD [captures clinical data as routine care documentation, in place of an average of 15 paper forms](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>), on the ministry's case study of 2023 to 2025. The consortium said in April 2026 that C+ DDS [replaces manual reports with real-time entry and makes a family's record available at any facility on the system](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Last twelve months.** The consortium reports [growth from a five-facility pilot in mid-2024 to over 40 facilities by April 2026](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In April 2026 the consortium reported C+ DDS [operational in over 40 facilities across 9 regions and more than 25 districts of South-Central Somalia](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). The ministry's case study reports RAAD [deployed in 111 public primary healthcare facilities across six states between 2023 and 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).
