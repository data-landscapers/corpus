# Somalia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's bulletin for July to September 2023 states that [the ministry decided to use DHIS2 as the exclusive national HMIS platform](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). The ministry's HMIS Section runs it. The bulletin states that [a five-year costed Health Information Strategy was prepared and finalised](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). Under a three-year agreement with UNICEF of August 2025, [HISP maintains the DHIS2 platform and trains ministry staff](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry reports [579 public health facilities reporting to DHIS2 in July to September 2023](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). They reported on [data sets for health-centre outpatients and hospital outpatients and inpatients](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The WHO and ministry assessment of September 2022 found that [most facilities record on paper registries](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742188-eng.pdf>). They send monthly summary forms to the district HMIS officer, who enters them into DHIS2. RAAD is the ministry's electronic health record. Its case study of 2023 to 2025 covers 111 primary facilities running it. There, [routine care documentation generates the DHIS2 aggregate reports automatically](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Last twelve months.** ISDP [ran a three-day HMIS training of trainers in Bosaso on 3 to 5 February 2026](<https://isdpsom.org/health-management-information-systems-hmis-training/>). It was for [16 health workers from the health centres and primary health units it supports](<https://isdpsom.org/health-management-information-systems-hmis-training/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's case study covers 2023 to 2025. It reports that [111 public primary healthcare facilities across six states produce their DHIS2 aggregate reports digitally from the RAAD record](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). A commentary of August 2026 by ministry HMIS staff says [DHIS2-based reporting remains uneven across facilities and administrative levels](<https://www.dovepress.com/strengthening-district--and-regional-level-supportive-supervision-for--peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-JHL>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** By its own account the Ministry of Health [developed RAAD as a national electronic health record for primary healthcare](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). It was implemented between 2023 and 2025. The ministry's bulletin for July to September 2023 says [the pilot was supported by the Government of Sweden through SPIDER](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). The CaafimaadPlus consortium of ten NGOs described its system in April 2026. The Caafimaad+ Digitalized Data System (C+ DDS) is [run by the consortium, in partnership with the Federal Ministry of Health](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). It is [funded by EU Humanitarian Aid](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's case study places [RAAD in public primary healthcare facilities only, between 2023 and 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Where data is digitised.** On the ministry's case study of 2023 to 2025, RAAD [captures clinical data as routine care documentation](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). It replaces an average of 15 paper forms. The consortium said in April 2026 that C+ DDS [replaces manual reports with real-time entry](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). It [makes a family's record available at any facility on the system](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Last twelve months.** The consortium reports [growth from a five-facility pilot in mid-2024 to over 40 facilities by April 2026](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In April 2026 the consortium reported C+ DDS [operational in over 40 facilities](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). They lie across 9 regions and more than 25 districts of South-Central Somalia. The ministry's case study reports RAAD [deployed in 111 public primary healthcare facilities across six states between 2023 and 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).
