# Congo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry published its national health development plan for 2023 to 2026 in September 2024. It [names DHIS2 as the principal platform for collecting and processing health information](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). The ministry calls the system the Système national d'information sanitaire (SNIS). The plan [lists a health information directorate in the ministry and a database manager in every health district](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). It says the platform [was built with Global Fund and Gavi support](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan of September 2024 states that standard collection tools exist in health centres, district referral hospitals and private facilities. It states that [general hospitals' forms are configured in DHIS2](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). Their [monthly reporting is still to begin](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same plan states that nearly all public, private and faith-based facilities compile their reports on paper. They [send them to the health districts for entry](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). All [12 departments and 52 districts have been trained on DHIS2](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). WHO reports [data completeness of 87% and timeliness of 47.9% in 2024](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-04/9789290314417-fre.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's annual report for 2025 describes work done with its support during the year. [401 data collectors were equipped for electronic transmission of data](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>). The [national list of health indicators was drawn up](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The plan of September 2024 states that facilities produce their reports on paper. The health districts key them into DHIS2. Paper collection tools remain much used, the plan says. The [computer tools provided are not often used](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

Single-function: the logistics system [e-LMIS](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-sante-le-pnud-offre-plus-de-49-millions-de-fcfa-dequipements-informatiques/>) and the [epidemic prevention and health information component](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-44.pdf>) of the Projet d'accélération de la transformation numérique. Also single-function: [routine immunisation data in DHIS2](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>) and the weekly surveillance system [EWARS](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). Also single-function: the Covid-19 response system [SIRCOV](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>) and the stock system [SAGE](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>) at the medicines store CAMEPS. Also single-function: [epidemiological surveillance](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>) and [sentinel influenza surveillance](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>).
