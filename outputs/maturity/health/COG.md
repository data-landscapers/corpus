# Congo

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's national health development plan for 2023 to 2026, published in September 2024, [names DHIS2 as the principal platform for collecting and processing health information](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>), within what the ministry calls the Système national d'information sanitaire (SNIS). The plan [lists a health information directorate in the ministry and a database manager in every health district](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>), and says the platform [was built with Global Fund and Gavi support](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan of September 2024 states that [standard collection tools exist in health centres, district referral hospitals and private facilities, and that general hospitals' forms are configured in DHIS2 with their monthly reporting still to begin](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same plan states that [nearly all public, private and faith-based facilities compile their reports on paper and send them to the health districts for entry, all 12 departments and 52 districts having been trained on DHIS2](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>). WHO reports [data completeness of 87% and timeliness of 47.9% in 2024](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-04/9789290314417-fre.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's annual report for 2025 says that during the year, with its support, [401 data collectors were equipped for electronic transmission of data and the national list of health indicators was drawn up](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The plan of September 2024 states that [facilities produce their reports on paper and the health districts key them into DHIS2, and that paper collection tools remain much used while the computer tools provided are not often used](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the logistics system [e-LMIS](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-sante-le-pnud-offre-plus-de-49-millions-de-fcfa-dequipements-informatiques/>), the [epidemic prevention and health information component](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-44.pdf>) of the Projet d'accélération de la transformation numérique, [routine immunisation data in DHIS2](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>), the weekly surveillance system [EWARS](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>), the Covid-19 response system [SIRCOV](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>), the stock system [SAGE](<https://sante.gouv.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PLAN-NATIONAL-DE-DEVELOPPEMENT-SANITAIRE-DU-CONGO-2023-2026_VERSION-OFFICIELLE.pdf>) at the medicines store CAMEPS, [epidemiological surveillance](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>) and [sentinel influenza surveillance](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Rapport%20Annuel%202025%20OMS_Congo.pdf>).

***Not held***. No count or share of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and nothing on health posts. The tier, total number and software of the 401 data collectors equipped in 2025 are not stated, and no domestic budget line for the system is named.

## EMR

***Not held***. No statement of whether patient records are kept on paper or digitally, at any tier, is held. A public hospital's organisation decree names a hospital information system without saying that it holds the clinical record.
