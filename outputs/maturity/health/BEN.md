# Benin

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's 2022 statistical yearbook says the ministry [adopted DHIS2 from 2014](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>) as its platform for managing health data. It calls the system the Système National d'Information et de Gestion Sanitaires (SNIGS). The ministry's programme budget for 2024-2026 [puts its Direction des Systèmes d'Information in charge](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>) of the information systems action. It [funds that action from domestic resources alone](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>), at 590,406,313 FCFA in 2024. The same document sets targets for health facilities whose statistics are integrated in SNIGS. The [targets are 80 per cent in 2024 and 85 per cent in 2025 and 2026](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In 2019 SNIGS [collected a monthly return from health centres and another from hospitals](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>). Both were on standard paper forms. SNIGS also reported indicators for the national teaching hospital.

**Where data is digitised.** In 2022 a facility in every zone sanitaire [sent its paper report to the zone before the 5th of each month](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>). The zone keyed it into DHIS2 before the 20th. [Certain equipped facilities and community relays keyed their own monthly forms](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>) directly into the data warehouse.

**Last twelve months.** The programme budget is dated December 2023. It [allocates the information systems action 590,406,313 FCFA in 2026](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>). That is the same as in 2024 and 2025.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In November 2023 Abt Global [named one private facility, the Minonkpo Clinic in Cotonou](<https://www.abtglobal.com/insights/impact-briefs/pshpa-trains-private-health-facilities-to-submit-rmnch-data-for-dhis2>). It said the clinic had recorded its data directly into DHIS2 since 2020. The country has 752 private health facilities.

The health ministry has a [Sous-système de surveillance épidémiologique et de détection des épidémies](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>). It also has a [Sous-système d'information à assise communautaire](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>). Community health workers use the [plateforme Alafia.com](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2025/11/telemedecine-et-donnees-le-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-nouvelle-proximite-sanitaire/>).
