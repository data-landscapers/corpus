# Benin

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's 2022 statistical yearbook says the ministry [adopted DHIS2 from 2014](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>) as its platform for managing health data, under what it calls the Système National d'Information et de Gestion Sanitaires (SNIGS). The ministry's programme budget for 2024-2026 [puts its Direction des Systèmes d'Information in charge](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>) of the information systems action and [funds that action from domestic resources alone](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>), at 590,406,313 FCFA in 2024. The same document [sets targets of 80 per cent in 2024 and 85 per cent in 2025 and 2026](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>) for health facilities whose statistics are integrated in SNIGS.

**Tiers in use.** In 2019 SNIGS [collected a monthly return from health centres and another from hospitals](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>), on standard paper forms, and reported indicators for the national teaching hospital.

**Where data is digitised.** In 2022 a facility [sent its paper report to the zone sanitaire before the 5th of each month and the zone keyed it into DHIS2 before the 20th](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>), in every zone sanitaire. [Certain equipped facilities and community relays keyed their own monthly forms](<https://s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/front-office-resources/production/uploads/publication/attachment/46e5f76a-5f8f-4780-9d32-d51df5c239c2/02be6fdb-3d72-4984-adba-f696163051fd.pdf>) directly into the data warehouse.

**Last twelve months.** The programme budget of December 2023 [allocates the information systems action the same 590,406,313 FCFA in 2026](<https://budgetbenin.bj/storage/2024/01/DPPD-MS-2024-2026-LF-1.pdf>) as in 2024 and 2025.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Abt Global, in November 2023, [names one private facility, the Minonkpo Clinic in Cotonou](<https://www.abtglobal.com/insights/impact-briefs/pshpa-trains-private-health-facilities-to-submit-rmnch-data-for-dhis2>), as recording its data directly into DHIS2 since 2020, against 752 private health facilities in the country.

***Noted, not assessed***. The health ministry's [Sous-système de surveillance épidémiologique et de détection des épidémies](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>) and its [Sous-système d'information à assise communautaire](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>), and the [plateforme Alafia.com](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2025/11/telemedecine-et-donnees-le-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-nouvelle-proximite-sanitaire/>), used by community health workers.

***Not held***. No number or share of primary clinics entering their own reports is published, and no tier below the health centre is named. No dated event falls in the last twelve months.

## EMR

***Noted, not assessed***. The health ministry's [Sous-système de surveillance épidémiologique et de détection des épidémies](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>) and its [Sous-système d'information à assise communautaire](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-publications/annuelles/AS-INSAE/Archive/Annee_2019/Annuaire_Statistique_Sant%C3%A9_2019.pdf>), and the [plateforme Alafia.com](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2025/11/telemedecine-et-donnees-le-numerique-au-coeur-de-la-nouvelle-proximite-sanitaire/>), used by community health workers.

***Not held***. No source held states a tier, a place of digitisation or a clinic count for an electronic medical record, or that patient records are on paper at every tier. A digital health booklet is held only as a pledge.
