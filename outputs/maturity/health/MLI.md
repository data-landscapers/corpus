# Mali

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The DHIS2 platform [is coordinated by a technical team and a steering committee chaired by the Secretary General of the Ministry of Health and Social Development](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/PS-SNISS_2020-2024_VF_11fev2021.pdf>), by the ministry's plan of February 2021, which [records no budget line for the national health information system](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/PS-SNISS_2020-2024_VF_11fev2021.pdf>). The digital health plan for 2024 to 2028 [bases that system on DHIS2 and schedules a DHIS2 test server and an interoperability framework](<https://oncd-mali.com/media/PSNSN_Mali.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Since 2016 [the routine data of community health centres (CSCom), reference health centres (CSRéf) and hospitals has been on the DHIS2 platform](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/PS-SNISS_2020-2024_VF_11fev2021.pdf>), the same plan states, with figures for 2019.

**Where data is digitised.** In four sub-districts of Mopti in September and October 2018, [the technical director of each CSCom entered community health workers' paper monthly reports into DHIS2](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Community_HIS_country_case_studies/Case_studies/CS_Health_Data_Reporting_Challenges_in_Mali.pdf>), the Population Council found. The ministry's agency ANTIM [places mobile terminals carrying a form at CSCom and CSRéf](<https://snisi.sante.gov.ml/about>) for its SNISI platform, by the platform's own page as read on 3 September 2026.

**Last twelve months.** At a workshop of 30 and 31 July 2025 [the ministry's Directorate General of Health and Public Hygiene reviewed community indicators for alignment with DHIS2, and a deputy director said interoperability between DHIS2 and DISC-Mali was established](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>), Muso reports.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** By September 2019 the health ministry [had deployed DHIS-2 in 98 percent of community health centers](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Mali%20DECA_English.pdf>), USAID's assessment of April 2023 states.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's agency ANTIM [introduced and runs the OpenClinic GA deployments in community health centres, reference health centres and hospitals](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Mali%20DECA_English.pdf>), it told USAID in July 2022; those in community health centres [were launched with support from Global Affairs Canada](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Mali%20DECA_English.pdf>). The ministry's digital health plan for 2024 to 2028 [sets 75% of health establishments using a harmonised, computerised or shared medical record by 2028](<https://oncd-mali.com/media/PSNSN_Mali.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** On 27 July 2026 the Hôpital dermatologie de Bamako [ran a fully digitised consultation circuit, from registration through consultation and laboratory to pharmacy](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>), the ministry's communication network reports. In July 2022 ANTIM [had digitalized a dozen of the 65 reference health centers and was working to digitalize 5 of the 13 hospitals](<https://www.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2023-05/Mali%20DECA_English.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** At that hospital on 27 July 2026 [registration, consultation, laboratory and pharmacy each worked in the system on the patient's unique number as the patient passed through](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>).

**Last twelve months.** OpenClinic [was presented to the Prime Minister on 16 September 2025 as the health ministry's tool centralising patient records](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1709-48777-mali-rolls-out-new-digital-tools-to-modernize-public-administration>), Ecofin Agency reports.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The SanDi project of 2021 to 2022 [deployed OpenClinic in 27 community health centres](<https://www.usherbrooke.ca/medecine/fileadmin/sites/medecine/documents/CIDIS/Affiches/3_Acfas.pdf>), a University of Sherbrooke poster of 2024 says. Pilots to digitise patient files in a few community health centres [did not succeed, for lack of funding and institutional support](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099320408092229246/pdf/P17487603465eb02082e70a588acd95033.pdf>), the World Bank's diagnostic of 2023 states.
