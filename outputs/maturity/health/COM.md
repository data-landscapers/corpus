# Comoros

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's digital health strategy for 2022 to 2026 is dated July 2021. It [puts the deployment of DHIS2 under its Direction de l'Information et des Statistiques Sanitaires](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). It [schedules the extension of the national health information system with DHIS2 at US$0.60 million](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). The work runs from January 2022 to December 2025. The cost is [all set against technical and financial partners](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). The World Bank reported in November 2024 that DHIS2 [was deployed under an IDA-financed project implemented by the ministry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO Comoros reports that [DHIS2 was extended to all 91 public-level health structures in 2025](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>). It calls this a national extension.

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's strategy of July 2021 describes the national health information system, then on GESIS. It calls it [semi-electronic and computerised at the level of the regions](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). Data [passed from districts to regions by hand or by e-mail](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** During 2025, with WHO support, validation rules were put into DHIS2. A data quality self-assessment was run. A [manual of standard data-management procedures was written](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** DHIS2 states that [almost 99% of all public health facilities used DHIS2 for health service reporting in 2024](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>). The World Bank [counted 69 public health facilities reporting data in DHIS2 at 31 August 2024, against a target of 80 by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>). WHO Comoros [gives the 91 structures reached in 2025 as 100% of all public-level structures](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [registre électronique de vaccination (REV)](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>). Single-function: [DHIS2 disease surveillance](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>), the [DHIS2 Climate & Health instance for malaria early warning](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>) and the [National Malaria Control Programme's weekly malaria data collection](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>). Also single-function: the eLMIS tools [DHIS2 Real-Time Stock Management](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>) and [Open mSupply](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>), and the vaccination campaign platform WHO Comoros calls [ODK/PADACOR](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>). Also single-function: [Surveillance Intégrée de la Maladie et la Riposte (SIMR) data entry in DHIS2 and KoboCollect](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>), [EIOS](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>) and the [système d'information des ressources humaines en santé](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>). Also single-function: [GLASS](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>), the [ODK geolocation of suspected vaccine-preventable disease cases and adverse events](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>) and [Kobo Collect in the 2024 cholera vaccination campaign](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>). Also single-function: the [cholera digital case register and dashboard](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>) and the planned [Système National Automatisé de Collecte et de Saisie des naissances et des décès](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). Community: [DHIS2 community health](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>). Enabling: the planned [Réseau National Numérique de la Santé (RNNS)](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>) and the planned [Réseau national de télémédecine](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says where DHIS2 reports are keyed. Nothing says what share of primary clinics enter their own. The 99%, the 69 and the 100% count all public facilities. No tier is named. No event dated within the last twelve months has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's digital health strategy for 2022 to 2026 is dated July 2021. It calls the electronic patient record the Dossier Electronique du Patient. It [lists the record among the components absent from the country](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). It [plans a modular application for reception, consultations, tests, hospitalisation and billing](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). The target is [more than 40 structures by the end of 2024](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In July 2021 the ministry stated that [the electronic patient record does not exist in the country](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same strategy [lists telemedicine, m-health applications and online training as absent alongside the patient record](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank [recorded a hospital management information system as not functional at 31 August 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>). The target was [functional by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In July 2021 [only CHN El Maarouf, CHRI Hombo and the medical imaging centre had internet](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). District health centres [were rarely connected](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).
