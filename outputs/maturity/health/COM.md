# Comoros

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's digital health strategy for 2022 to 2026, dated July 2021, [puts the deployment of DHIS2 under its Direction de l'Information et des Statistiques Sanitaires](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). It [schedules the extension of the national health information system with DHIS2 for January 2022 to December 2025 at US$0.60 million](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>), [all of it set against technical and financial partners](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). The World Bank reported in November 2024 that DHIS2 [was deployed under an IDA-financed project implemented by the ministry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO Comoros reports that [DHIS2 was extended to all 91 public-level health structures in 2025](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>), which it calls a national extension.

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's strategy of July 2021 describes the national health information system, then on GESIS, as [semi-electronic and computerised at the level of the regions, with data passing from districts to regions by hand or by e-mail](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** During 2025, with WHO support, [validation rules were put into DHIS2, a data quality self-assessment was run and a manual of standard data-management procedures was written](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** DHIS2 states that [almost 99% of all public health facilities used DHIS2 for health service reporting in 2024](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>). The World Bank [counted 69 public health facilities reporting data in DHIS2 at 31 August 2024, against a target of 80 by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>). WHO Comoros [gives the 91 structures reached in 2025 as 100% of all public-level structures](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/OMS%20Comores%20_%20Rapport%20annuel%20des%20r%C3%A9sultats%202025%20%20PUBLICATION.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's digital health strategy for 2022 to 2026, dated July 2021, [lists the electronic patient record, which it calls the Dossier Electronique du Patient, among the components absent from the country](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>). It [plans a modular application for reception, consultations, tests, hospitalisation and billing, in more than 40 structures by the end of 2024](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In July 2021 the ministry stated that [the electronic patient record does not exist in the country](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same strategy [lists telemedicine, m-health applications and online training as absent alongside the patient record](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank [recorded a hospital management information system as not functional at 31 August 2024, against a target of functional by December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111924122078581/pdf/P175840-1a3a0438-2d5c-487f-8960-7e60d83dfb5b.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In July 2021 [only CHN El Maarouf, CHRI Hombo and the medical imaging centre had internet, and district health centres were rarely connected](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>).
