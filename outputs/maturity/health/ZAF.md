# South Africa

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Under the District Health Management Information System Policy, [the Director-General has overall ownership of the District Health Information System and the Department of Health owns all its data](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), in the National Department of Health's bid terms of 24 July 2026. The department [invites the bids itself, under National Treasury prescripts and priced in rand, for three years of hosting, maintenance, support and enhancement](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The department described the system in July 2026 as [used at facility, district, province and national level, reporting by clinic, community health centre and hospital](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** [Nine provincial instances capture the routine data and the national instance is filled by synchronisation](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), on the department's account of July 2026. The same bid plans training for [920 data capturers at primary health care clinics and 400 at district hospitals over three years](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 24 July 2026 the department [issued bid NDOH 09/2025-2026 for a provider to host, maintain, support and enhance the system for three years, covering nine provincial DHIS2 instances, the national data warehouse and training of about 6,000 officials](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Thabethe and Mathews state that [all public health facilities in Eastern Cape province used DHIS2, with capture by staff in the facility](<https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S1560-683X2024000100055&script=sci_arttext>), over April 2017 to March 2020.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [Tier.net](<https://medinform.jmir.org/2026/1/e73831>) and the [Malaria Information System](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>). Single-function: [ART Quarterly Reporting](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), the [National Institute for Communicable Diseases system](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), the [Integrated School Health Programme instance](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), the [Patient Experience of Care instance](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Advert-NDOH-09-2026-27.pdf>), the [laboratory information system](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NDoH04-20252026-Final-BSC-Invitation-to-Bid-EMR-implementation-03-03-2026.pdf>), the [Stock Visibility System](<https://knowledgehub.health.gov.za/system/files/elibdownloads/2023-04/national-digital-strategy-for-south-africa-2019-2024-b.pdf>), the [COVID-19 Tracing Database](<https://datascience.codata.org/articles/10.5334/dsj-2026-031>) and the proposed [Live Healthcare Console](<https://sacj.org.za/article/view/23106>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Minister of Health told Parliament on 1 August 2025 that [the National Department of Health is developing a single common Electronic Medical Record for primary health care facilities](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>). The department's bid document of March 2026 says [it developed the EMR itself and that its Digital Health Unit directs implementation](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NDoH04-20252026-Final-BSC-Invitation-to-Bid-EMR-implementation-03-03-2026.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The same reply counts [Clinicom in 53 Western Cape hospitals](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>), [the eHealth system in 42 KwaZulu-Natal public hospitals](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>) and [HMS2 in 16 Free State hospitals](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>). It states that [Mpumalanga, North West, Limpopo and Northern Cape are not implementing such systems at hospitals](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>).

**Where data is digitised.** The department describes its EMR, in March 2026, as [capturing clinical information at the point of care, verified against the patient's identifier in the Health Patient Registration System](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NDoH04-20252026-Final-BSC-Invitation-to-Bid-EMR-implementation-03-03-2026.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 the department [invited bids to scale up its EMR to all fixed primary health care facilities in eight provinces within 18 months](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NDoH04-20252026-Final-BSC-Invitation-to-Bid-EMR-implementation-03-03-2026.pdf>). The Free State Department of Health [won the Public Sector Digital Innovator Award for deploying its primary health care EMR](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/free-state-dept-health-scoops-innovator-award>), SAnews reported in May 2026.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Minister's reply states that [the National EMR beta ran in 36 primary health care facilities between 22 October 2024 and 13 February 2025](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>) and that [Western Cape's Primary Health Care Information System is implemented in 308](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>). It counts [3,333 primary health care facilities reporting to the 2024/25 Ideal Clinic Assessment](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31734/>).
