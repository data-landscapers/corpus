# Ethiopia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [holds full ownership of DHIS2's customisation and implementation](<https://medinform.jmir.org/2024/1/e50375>), as assessed in June 2022, under [a budgeted health information strategy and a 10-year strategic plan](<https://medinform.jmir.org/2024/1/e50375>). The memorandum Ethiopia signed with the United States on 23 December 2025 [names DHIS2 as the country's HMIS](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), and the United States [plans US$18,850,149 of data-systems funding over 2026 to 2030, across all the systems it lists](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In facility surveys of 2019 and 2020, [305 public hospitals and health centres reported a functional HMIS, and health posts have none of their own](<https://www.joghr.org/article/75141->). The Ministry of Health counted [more than 33,000 health facilities on DHIS2](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>) in 2024.

**Where data is digitised.** [Health information technicians key routine reports into DHIS2 at their own facilities](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0314505>), on interviews held from October 2023 to March 2024. Health posts [record largely on paper and send monthly to their health centre, which compiles and submits](<https://www.joghr.org/article/75141->), as surveyed in 2019 and 2020.

**Last twelve months.** The share of expected monthly facility reports received in DHIS2 [rose from 90% in 2024 to 93% in 2025](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ethiopia.pdf>), on Countdown to 2030's figures.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** A study published in May 2023 states that [more than 95% of all health facilities used DHIS-2, online or offline](<https://www.joghr.org/article/75141->). A 2025 study puts [over 4,000 primary health care institutions, 95% of those eligible, on DHIS2](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13690-025-01641-0>), counting district hospitals, health centres, health posts and district health offices.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [ART EMR](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the [PMTCT EMR](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>) and the [HIV case-based surveillance system (CBSS)](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>). Single-function: the [National Laboratory Information System](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the laboratory database [ETTORS](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the logistics system [Dagu](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the pharmaceutical transaction service [eAPTS](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the [DHIS2 routine surveillance system](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), the [DHIS2 tracker for all-cause mortality surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), the [Electronic Public Health Emergency Management system (ePHEM)](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), [Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources (EIOS)](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>) and the [Excel-based claim management for Community-Based Health Insurance](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>). Community: the [electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS)](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>) and the paper [Community Health Information System (CHIS)](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>). Enabling: [HealthNet](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>) and [telemedicine services](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held: both 95% figures count all or eligible health facilities, not primary clinics. Nothing dated after 2020 shows health posts entering their own data, and no exchange between eCHIS and DHIS2 is established.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [presents electronic medical records as a milestone of its own digital health work](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>) in December 2024. The memorandum signed with the United States on 23 December 2025 [schedules a national multi-tenant EMR: piloting in 2026, 48 facilities by 2027 and 438 by 2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>). The United States [plans to support its customisation and roll-out, Ethiopia paying the licensing, cloud and hardware costs the United States does not](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** EMR systems [are implemented in health centres and hospitals](<https://cerhb.gov.et/digital-health-systems/>), as the Central Ethiopia Regional Health Bureau states. In June 2023 the Bahmni EMR [was in use at one general hospital and three health centres in Addis Ababa](<https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.1177/20552076241301946>).

**Where data is digitised.** Providers at those four facilities [entered consultations, prescriptions and laboratory orders in the EMR during care](<https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.1177/20552076241301946>) in June 2023, and [the record was used across the departments of the one facility](<https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.1177/20552076241301946>). The Ministry of Health states that [10 of the facilities with an EMR operate entirely paperless](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>) in December 2024.

**Last twelve months.** On 23 December 2025 Ethiopia and the United States [signed the memorandum, with activities from 1 April 2026 to 31 December 2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Ministry of Health states that [electronic medical records were introduced in over 70 health facilities in the three years to December 2024](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>). The memorandum of 23 December 2025 gives [a baseline of 0% of hospitals and health centres implementing the full national EMR](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [ART EMR](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the [PMTCT EMR](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>) and the [HIV case-based surveillance system (CBSS)](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>). Single-function: the [National Laboratory Information System](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the laboratory database [ETTORS](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the logistics system [Dagu](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the pharmaceutical transaction service [eAPTS](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0004QN-Ethiopia-Health-12.23.2025.pdf>), the [DHIS2 routine surveillance system](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), the [DHIS2 tracker for all-cause mortality surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), the [Electronic Public Health Emergency Management system (ePHEM)](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>), [Epidemic Intelligence from Open Sources (EIOS)](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ethiopia/news/ethiopia-digitalizes-and-modernizes-its-health-emergency-information-systems>) and the [Excel-based claim management for Community-Based Health Insurance](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>). Community: the [electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS)](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>) and the paper [Community Health Information System (CHIS)](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>). Enabling: [HealthNet](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12403253/>) and [telemedicine services](<https://www.moh.gov.et/sites/default/files/2024-12/REQUEST%20OF%20EXPRESSION%20OF%20INTEREST%20.pdf>).

***Not held***. The tier of the 70-plus facilities and the total they are drawn from are not held, and the 0% counts the full national system only. Neither most referral and district hospitals nor primary clinics across any district are shown using an EMR; the named primary sites are three health centres in Addis Ababa.
