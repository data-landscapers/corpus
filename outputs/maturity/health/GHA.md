# Ghana

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** GIZ published a mapping with the Ghana Health Service in December 2023. On it, the service's Policy, Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation Division [owns DHIMS2](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>). DHIMS2 is the service's District Health Information Management System, and runs on a national scale. The Ministry of Health's Health Information System Strategic Plan 2022-2025 dates from June 2022. It [budgets GHS 31,354,750, of which GHS 28,219,275 is from development partners](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). The service's [Policy and Strategy on Digital Health 2023-2027 lists DHIMS II first among its digital health solutions](<https://ghs.gov.gh/api/media/file/POLICY_AND_STRATEGY_ON_DIGITAL_HEALTH-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** A PLOS One survey of March 2024 covered 86 CHPS zones across six Northern Region districts. Community health officers there [reported monthly into DHIMS 2 at CHPS level](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0341176>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's plan of June 2022 states that [most facilities validate and enter their data into DHIMS2 themselves](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). Those without computers or internet submit manually to the sub-district for entry. CHPS staff in Karaga, Mion and Sagnarigu spoke in March 2024. They said [their compounds lacked login details and computers](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0341176>). Paper reports [went to the sub-district or the district office for upload](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0341176>).

**Last twelve months.** Donor-funded projects paid for the servers hosting DHIMS2. They [had ended by December 2023, leaving the annual server costs unfunded](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** A WHO-led study visited 16 public primary facilities in four districts of Ghana in 2016-2017. It [found no electronic data collection in any of them](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).

Single-function: the [Ghana Integrated Logistics Management Information System (GhiLMIS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>), the [Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>) and the [KoboCollect interface for nutrition data](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GHANA-HSMTDP-MTR-REPORT_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health issued a directive on 28 October 2025. It [named its Chief Director to lead the national rollout of the Ghana Health Information Management System (GHIMS)](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/health-ministry-rolls-out-new-digital-system-to-fix-hospital-service-disruptions.html>). The ministry's Health Sector Medium-Term Development Plan 2026-2029 dates from October 2025. It [carries a national indicator for the percentage of health facilities with functional EMR systems in use](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/HSMTDP_Final-Draft_compiled_latest_signed-copy-EDITED-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Vice President said on 31 October 2024 that [the Lightwave system LHIMS was deployed in all six teaching hospitals, 10 regional hospitals and 243 district hospitals](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/veep-launches-national-e-health-project-to-transform-ghanas-healthcare-system/>). Its vendor's contract [expired on 31 December 2024](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-launches-new-digital-system-to-replace-failing-lhims-platform/>).

**Where data is digitised.** OneHealthEHR Inc. described its platform, in use in Liberia and Ghana, in September 2026. It said [clinicians record patient information during care and synchronise it when a connection returns](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The directive of 28 October 2025 [ordered GHIMS rolled out nationwide in November 2025 as the only authorised platform for patient registration](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/health-ministry-rolls-out-new-digital-system-to-fix-hospital-service-disruptions.html>). A Ghana Medical Journal editorial of January 2026 [says the ministry discontinued LHIMS after its contract expired in December 2024](<https://doi.org/10.4314/gmj.v59i4.1>). OneHealthEHR says its platform [went into live clinical use in Liberia and Ghana on 1 September 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's plan of October 2025 has an indicator for the percentage of all health facilities with functional EMR systems in use. It [gives 15% as the first value](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/HSMTDP_Final-Draft_compiled_latest_signed-copy-EDITED-2.pdf>). By late September 2026 OneHealthEHR [reported seven active clinics and health centres](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>). The figure is for Liberia and Ghana together.
