# Ghana

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ghana Health Service's Policy, Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation Division [owns DHIMS2, the service's District Health Information Management System, which runs on a national scale](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>), on the mapping GIZ published with the service in December 2023. The Ministry of Health's Health Information System Strategic Plan 2022-2025, of June 2022, [budgets GHS 31,354,750, of which GHS 28,219,275 is from development partners](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). The service's [Policy and Strategy on Digital Health 2023-2027 lists DHIMS II first among its digital health solutions](<https://ghs.gov.gh/api/media/file/POLICY_AND_STRATEGY_ON_DIGITAL_HEALTH-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Community health officers in 86 CHPS zones across six Northern Region districts [reported monthly into DHIMS 2 at CHPS level](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0341176>), in a PLOS One survey of March 2024.

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's plan of June 2022 states that [most facilities validate and enter their data into DHIMS2 themselves, while those without computers or internet submit manually to the sub-district for entry](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/HISSP_2022-2025_FINALVERSION-1.pdf>). CHPS staff in Karaga, Mion and Sagnarigu said in March 2024 that [their compounds lacked login details and computers, and that paper reports went to the sub-district or the district office for upload](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0341176>).

**Last twelve months.** The donor-funded projects that paid for the servers hosting DHIMS2 [had ended by December 2023, leaving the annual server costs unfunded](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** A WHO-led study [found no electronic data collection in any of the 16 public primary facilities it visited in four districts of Ghana in 2016-2017](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [E-Tracker](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>) and the [rsLog application](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GHANA-HSMTDP-MTR-REPORT_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf>). Single-function: the [Ghana Integrated Logistics Management Information System (GhiLMIS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>), the [Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>) and the [KoboCollect interface for nutrition data](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GHANA-HSMTDP-MTR-REPORT_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the one figure on clinics is from 16 facilities in 2016-2017. No plan dated within the last three years is held, and no dated event on DHIMS2 in the last twelve months.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** By a directive of 28 October 2025 the Ministry of Health [named its Chief Director to lead the national rollout of the Ghana Health Information Management System (GHIMS), with Axon Information Systems implementing it under government oversight](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/health-ministry-rolls-out-new-digital-system-to-fix-hospital-service-disruptions.html>). The ministry's Health Sector Medium-Term Development Plan 2026-2029, of October 2025, [carries a national indicator for the percentage of health facilities with functional EMR systems in use](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/HSMTDP_Final-Draft_compiled_latest_signed-copy-EDITED-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Vice President said on 31 October 2024 that [the Lightwave system LHIMS was deployed in all six teaching hospitals, 10 regional hospitals and 243 district hospitals](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/veep-launches-national-e-health-project-to-transform-ghanas-healthcare-system/>). Its vendor's contract [expired on 31 December 2024](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/health-ministry-launches-new-digital-system-to-replace-failing-lhims-platform/>).

**Where data is digitised.** OneHealthEHR Inc. said in September 2026 that on its platform, in use in Liberia and Ghana, [clinicians record patient information during care and synchronise it when a connection returns](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The directive of 28 October 2025 [ordered GHIMS rolled out nationwide in November 2025 as the only authorised platform for patient registration and clinical documentation](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/health-ministry-rolls-out-new-digital-system-to-fix-hospital-service-disruptions.html>), and [cited nationwide disruptions to the existing clinical information and billing systems](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/health-ministry-rolls-out-new-digital-system-to-fix-hospital-service-disruptions.html>). OneHealthEHR says its platform [went into live clinical use in Liberia and Ghana on 1 September 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's plan of October 2025 [gives 15% as the first value against its indicator for the percentage of all health facilities with functional EMR systems in use](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/HSMTDP_Final-Draft_compiled_latest_signed-copy-EDITED-2.pdf>). OneHealthEHR [reported seven active clinics and health centres across Liberia and Ghana together](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>) by late September 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [E-Tracker](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>) and the [rsLog application](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GHANA-HSMTDP-MTR-REPORT_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf>). Single-function: the [Ghana Integrated Logistics Management Information System (GhiLMIS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>), the [Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS)](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/GHANA-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>) and the [KoboCollect interface for nutrition data](<https://www.moh.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/GHANA-HSMTDP-MTR-REPORT_FINAL_SIGNED.pdf>).

***Not held***. Which tiers and how many facilities use GHIMS is not established, nor whether LHIMS was shut down outright or disrupted in some hospitals. The 15% has no numerator, denominator or baseline year, GHIMS's financing is not stated, and the OneHealthEHR figures give no count for Ghana.
