# Lesotho

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Health Management Information System [has run on DHIS2 since February 2015](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). A workshop was reported on 23 September 2026. At it an officer of the HMIS Division [presented how health data is collected from communities and facilities](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/unicef-calls-for-stronger-data-systems-to-protect-health-rights-in-lesotho/>). In 2025 Lesotho [endorsed its National Digital Health Strategy 2025-2030](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>), WHO's country office reports.

**Tiers in use.** A BMJ Open study covers [monthly routine reporting by 60 health centres and 6 hospitals](<https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/13/5/e071414>) over July 2013 to June 2017. They were in the four pilot districts of Berea, Butha Buthe, Leribe and Mohale's Hoek.

**Where data is digitised.** A primary health care reform pilot ran from July 2014 to June 2017. Under it, [each health facility nurse in charge was given a computer and submitted the monthly report from the facility](<https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/13/5/e071414>). The registers stayed on paper. In November 2025 the ministry's HIV programme stated that [facility reports are automated from its electronic medical record into DHIS2](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO's country office reports that during 2025 Lesotho [advanced interoperability across its HMIS, HRH, LMIS and surveillance platforms](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>). The HIV programme states that [231 facilities report on HIV indicators](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>). It says [reporting rates fell from 97% in January to 95% in September 2025](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** To June 2017, facilities in four pilot districts out of 10 submitted monthly reports electronically. The districts [held 66 facilities, 60 health centres and 6 hospitals](<https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/13/5/e071414>). They held about 40% of the population.

Trackers: the Ministry of Health's [electronic registers](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/unicef-calls-for-stronger-data-systems-to-protect-health-rights-in-lesotho/>), the EpiC project's [DHIS2 Tracker](<https://selibeng.com/epic-lesotho-consultancy-digital-health-and-data-analytics/>) systems, the [eRegister](<https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093878>) and the [electronic medical record for HIV-positive patients](<https://www.mcc.gov/blog/entry/blog-121123-digital-health-lesotho/>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The ministry's HIV programme stated in November 2025 that [the plan is for the government to completely own the EMR, using domestic resources](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>). After the US stop-work orders of 2025 the government [hired 10 IT and 10 HMIS personnel to support districts](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>). The MCC compact of 12 May 2022 says the e-Register is limited to HIV and tuberculosis patients. It [funds the expansion of the e-Register into a national electronic medical record system](<https://assets.mcc.gov/content/uploads/compact-lesotho-health-and-horticulture.pdf>) for all primary health care patients.

**Tiers in use.** A hospital-based EMR [was introduced in 2013 across 16 public hospitals in the 10 districts](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). The ministry's assessment of 18 October 2017 found it [discontinued in several facilities and in substandard use in others](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-025-02858-3>). The HMIS Strategic Plan 2018-2022 records it [at six hospitals](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/cef30410-108d-4f8a-a725-2842e5baaf84/content>). There, [clinicians and pharmacists used it inadequately](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/cef30410-108d-4f8a-a725-2842e5baaf84/content>).

**Where data is digitised.** MCC wrote on 11 December 2023 about a rural health centre. There, [a pregnant woman who tests negative for HIV or TB has her care recorded on paper, in a health booklet](<https://www.mcc.gov/blog/entry/blog-121123-digital-health-lesotho/>). An [HIV-positive patient's care is recorded in an electronic medical record](<https://www.mcc.gov/blog/entry/blog-121123-digital-health-lesotho/>).

**Last twelve months.** The HIV programme states that [the EMRs stayed in place through the 2025 US funding cuts](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>). It also says [suboptimal use of the eRegister led to under-reporting of new ART initiations to September 2025](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The HIV programme stated in November 2025 that what it calls [the OpenMRS/Bahmni-based EMR/eRegister is in 197 public facilities](<https://cquin.icap.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CQUIN-9th-Annual-Meeting_Lesotho-Country-updates-FINAL.pdf>).
