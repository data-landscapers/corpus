# Madagascar

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's plan for its health information system is the PSRSIS. It [assigns routine data to the ministry's DEPSI directorate](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). It records that the ministry adopted DHIS2 as its data warehouse under the 2018-2022 plan. The plan [runs from 2023 to 2027 and carries a costed budget by strategy](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). It [names DHIS2 throughout](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan states that [DHIS2 was scaled to all 22 regions and 114 districts in January 2019](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). That was for the basic health centres' monthly activity report. The district hospitals' and community reports were integrated from July 2020. What the plan calls the CHU/CHRR/ES report [was last updated in 2011](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). DHIS2 is [only to be brought forward for it](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** As at the 2023 plan, [the basic health centres' data go up to the districts on the integrated monthly report in paper form](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). The report [is keyed into the DHIS2 Routine platform](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO Madagascar lists the key actions of 2024-2025. They include [an audit of the DHIS2 platform and secured cloud hosting](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/rapport%20biennal%202025%20-%20web%20optimis%C3%A9%202.pdf>). They include the deployment of electronic registers, among them the monthly activity report. They also include [the distribution of tablets with DHIS2 training](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/rapport%20biennal%202025%20-%20web%20optimis%C3%A9%202.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The 2023 plan covers an electronic monthly report keyed directly at health facilities. It [has been piloted since 2021 and is available in 10 pilot districts](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). [Scale-up is being planned](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's PSRSIS 2023-2027 [lists an activity for all care structures](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). It is the development of a patient management and electronic reporting system. Community medical centres use computerised patient record software. It [is Datasanté's, under a partnership with the community doctors' association AMC-MAD](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>). A convention signed on 6 November 2024 renewed the partnership. It [provides for Datasanté's continued logistical and financial support](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>).

**Tiers in use.** The health ministry attended a round table on 5 November 2024. There, [the electronic personal medical record was described as functional in some private establishments and some basic health centres](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>).

**Where data is digitised.** Datasanté equipped a community doctor at Matielona in November 2019. She [states that she keeps no paper register](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/mada-realisation>). Everything is in each patient's computerised record, entered on a tablet. A 2021 assessment covered 30 health centres in two regions. It [names paper registers as their primary documents for recording patient information](<https://doi.org/10.4269/ajtmh.23-0224>).

**Last twelve months.** In January 2025, 2424.mg reported that [two pilot sites had been chosen to start the hospital digitalisation project](<https://www.2424.mg/news/deux-sites-pilotes-ont-ete-choisis-pour-demarrer-le-projet-de-digitalisation-des-hopitaux/>). They are the CHU Ravoahangy Andrianavalona and the CHUSSPA at Analakely. The project was [initiated by the digital development ministry with the Ministry of Public Health](<https://www.2424.mg/news/deux-sites-pilotes-ont-ete-choisis-pour-demarrer-le-projet-de-digitalisation-des-hopitaux/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's digital health strategy dates from January 2023. It states that [management of clinical data and of the hospital information system remains at an experimental and local stage](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/Strat%C3%A9gie_Nationale_en_Sant%C3%A9_Digitale_2023_2027_Sign%C3%A9e.pdf>). It says [patient-management applications are used in private practices and private health centres](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/Strat%C3%A9gie_Nationale_en_Sant%C3%A9_Digitale_2023_2027_Sign%C3%A9e.pdf>).
