# Madagascar

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's plan for its health information system, the PSRSIS, [assigns routine data to the ministry's DEPSI directorate and records that the ministry adopted DHIS2 as its data warehouse under the 2018-2022 plan](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). The plan [runs from 2023 to 2027, carries a costed budget by strategy and names DHIS2 throughout](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The plan states that [DHIS2 was scaled to all 22 regions and 114 districts in January 2019 for the basic health centres' monthly activity report, and that the district hospitals' and community reports were integrated from July 2020](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). What the plan calls the CHU/CHRR/ES report [was last updated in 2011, and DHIS2 is only to be brought forward for it](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** As at the 2023 plan, [the basic health centres' data go up to the districts on the integrated monthly report in paper form, which is keyed into the DHIS2 Routine platform](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO Madagascar lists among the key actions of 2024-2025 [an audit of the DHIS2 platform and secured cloud hosting, the deployment of electronic registers including the monthly activity report, and the distribution of tablets with DHIS2 training](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/rapport%20biennal%202025%20-%20web%20optimis%C3%A9%202.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The 2023 plan states that an electronic monthly report keyed directly at health facilities [has been piloted since 2021 and is available in 10 pilot districts, with scale-up being planned](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's PSRSIS 2023-2027 [lists as an activity the development of a patient management and electronic reporting system for all care structures](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/PSRSIS_2023-2027.pdf>). The computerised patient record software used in community medical centres [is Datasanté's, under a partnership with the community doctors' association AMC-MAD renewed by a convention signed on 6 November 2024](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>), which [provides for Datasanté's continued logistical and financial support](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>).

**Tiers in use.** At a round table on 5 November 2024 attended by the health ministry, [the electronic personal medical record was described as functional in some private establishments and some basic health centres](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/congres/seconde-edition-des-journees-internationales-de-la-medecine-generale-communautaire-et-du-numerique-dans-la-sante-madagascar-du-25-au-30-octobre-2022>).

**Where data is digitised.** A community doctor at Matielona, equipped by Datasanté in November 2019, [states that she keeps no paper register and that everything is in each patient's computerised record, entered on a tablet](<https://www.datasante.org/par-pays/a-madagascar/mada-realisation>). A 2021 assessment of 30 health centres in two regions [names paper registers as their primary documents for recording patient information](<https://doi.org/10.4269/ajtmh.23-0224>).

**Last twelve months.** In January 2025, 2424.mg reported that [two pilot sites had been chosen to start the hospital digitalisation project, the CHU Ravoahangy Andrianavalona and the CHUSSPA at Analakely](<https://www.2424.mg/news/deux-sites-pilotes-ont-ete-choisis-pour-demarrer-le-projet-de-digitalisation-des-hopitaux/>), a project [initiated by the digital development ministry with the Ministry of Public Health](<https://www.2424.mg/news/deux-sites-pilotes-ont-ete-choisis-pour-demarrer-le-projet-de-digitalisation-des-hopitaux/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry's digital health strategy of January 2023 states that [management of clinical data and of the hospital information system remains at an experimental and local stage, and that patient-management applications are used in private practices and private health centres](<https://www.msanp.gov.mg/upload/documents/Strat%C3%A9gie_Nationale_en_Sant%C3%A9_Digitale_2023_2027_Sign%C3%A9e.pdf>).
