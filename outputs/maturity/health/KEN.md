# Kenya

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [uses the Kenya Health Information System (KHIS) to collect health data from facilities nationwide](<https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Financial-Non-Financial-Information-Flow-Kenya-Health-Sector.pdf>), as IDinsight reported in October 2024. The ministry's strategic plan for July 2023 to June 2027 [takes most of its indicator baselines and targets from KHIS](<https://health.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-08/KHSSP%202023-2027%20signed%20copy.pdf>). USAID's HealthIT grant [committed US$22,528,318 over 2016 to 2021 to DHIS2 implementation and use](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=US-GOV-1-AID-615-A-16-00013>).

**Tiers in use.** KHIS Aggregate [collects data from health facilities nationwide and is designed for every level of the health system, from the national down to the community](<https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Financial-Non-Financial-Information-Flow-Kenya-Health-Sector.pdf>), as IDinsight described it in October 2024.

**Where data is digitised.** Facilities [record services in paper daily registers, summarise them at the end of the week or month, and enter and submit the summaries online in KHIS](<https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Financial-Non-Financial-Information-Flow-Kenya-Health-Sector.pdf>), on IDinsight's account of October 2024.

**Last twelve months.** Legal Notice 77 of 2025, in force from 11 April 2025, [made the Digital Health Agency responsible for maintaining the Health Management Information Services Platform for reporting](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Ministry of Health's mid-term review reports that [94% of all health facilities submitted reports within KHIS in 2019/20, up from 89% in 2017/18](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/KHSSP-MTR-Statistical-report.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The US-Kenya Cooperation Framework signed on 4 December 2025 describes TaifaCare HMIS, a hospital management system with clinical modules, as [the Government of Kenya's flagship system for health facilities, deployed and maintained by the government](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0007QN-Kenya-Health-12.4.2025.pdf>). It [sets deployment at 2,000 public and faith-based facilities by 2026, 4,000 by 2027 and 8,000 by 2028](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0007QN-Kenya-Health-12.4.2025.pdf>). The United States [intends to provide US$175 million for data systems](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0007QN-Kenya-Health-12.4.2025.pdf>), and Kenya [intends to pay the ongoing licensing, cloud and hardware costs the United States does not](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0007QN-Kenya-Health-12.4.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** By 30 September 2026 [all public Level 2 to 4 facilities were reported migrated from the Social Health Authority's provider portal to HMIS](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001559265/why-sha-is-shifting-hospitals-from-portal-to-hmis-duale-explains>), the government's name for the facility system.

**Where data is digitised.** At the private network Penda Health, [clinical officers recorded consultations directly in a cloud-based EMR at the point of care](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04503-6>) between April and July 2025. Regulations in force from 11 April 2025 [require certified systems to query and update a shared health record within twenty-four hours of each encounter](<https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/77/eng@2025-04-11>).

**Last twelve months.** The government [set 30 September 2026 for facilities to move to HMIS, then extended the deadline to 30 October 2026, with 28 private platforms certified by the Digital Health Agency](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001559265/why-sha-is-shifting-hospitals-from-portal-to-hmis-duale-explains>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Framework states that [TaifaCare HMIS was deployed in 1,500 public health facilities](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0007QN-Kenya-Health-12.4.2025.pdf>) by 4 December 2025. Penda Health [ran the same EMR in its 16 primary care clinics](<https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04503-6>) in Nairobi and Kiambu counties in 2025.
