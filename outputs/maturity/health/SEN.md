# Senegal

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministère de la Santé et de l'Action sociale [has run the DHIS2 implementation since 2013](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>), on a DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021. The ministry's sector policy letter of 25 March 2025 states that [a national strategic plan 2022-2026 for the health and social information system is in force](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Lettre%20de%20Politique%20Sectorielle%20du%20MSAS%202025-2029%20Signe%CC%81.pdf>). The NGO PATH [contributes technical and financial assistance](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>).

**Tiers in use.** DHIS2 was [scaled nationally between 2015 and 2016 and is used at all levels of the health pyramid: hospitals, health centres and health posts](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>), as the DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021 states.

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's IT master plan of 31 December 2021 states that [DHIS2 runs from the health districts up to the minister's cabinet](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>). In the Thiès, Mbour, Kédougou and Saraya districts in 2018, [health-post head nurses entered monthly reports drawn from paper registers into DHIS2](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12889-024-21241-x>), on a BMC Public Health study.

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's programme document of 11 December 2025 states that [health worker strikes over the past 2.5 years have left gaps in data collection, storage and reporting across DHIS2](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121125054535615/pdf/P508171-8e51d04a-cf96-498a-8664-a7ca29962a42.pdf>). The national malaria programme put [national completeness of the data entered in DHIS2 for 2023 at 26.66%, against 97.8% in 2021](<https://pnlp.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bulletin-Epidemiologique-ANNUEL-2023-du-Paludisme-au-SENEGAL-vf21032024-1.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The DHIS2 Community post of 2 March 2021 states that [DHIS2 is used in health centres and health posts for data entry and analysis](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 Tracker for HIV patients](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>), the [DHIS2 Tracker COVID-19 package](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/harmoniser-la-collecte-de-donnees-pour-une-meilleure-riposte-contre-la-covid-19-au-senegal/42145>), the malaria programme's [DHIS2 Tracker](<https://pnlp.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bulletin-Epidemiologique-ANNUEL-2023-du-Paludisme-au-SENEGAL-vf21032024-1.pdf>), [e Vaccin](<https://paens.sn/digitalisation-de-certains-systemes-et-services-de-sante/>), the planned [carnet électronique de santé de la mère et de l'enfant](<https://paens.sn/digitalisation-de-certains-systemes-et-services-de-sante/>) and the World Bank project's [electronic maternal records](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112125172011631/pdf/P172524-657f27b1-2c02-496e-a944-90d4ab9bfee7.pdf>). Single-function: [ANACoD3](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/sante/renforcement-informations-sanitaires-anacod3-lance-afin-dameliorer-la-qualite-des-donnees-de-mortalite/>), [ERPX3](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), [Parsyl](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), [Coach2PEV](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), [DannCovid](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), the planned [SIPMED](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), the malaria programme's [data dashboard](<https://pnlp.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Bulletin-Epidemiologique-ANNUEL-2023-du-Paludisme-au-SENEGAL-vf21032024-1.pdf>), the [logistics management information systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121125054535615/pdf/P508171-8e51d04a-cf96-498a-8664-a7ca29962a42.pdf>) and the planned [regional early warning system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121125054535615/pdf/P508171-8e51d04a-cf96-498a-8664-a7ca29962a42.pdf>). Community: the planned [DHIS2 mobile module for community health actors](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>). Enabling: the [Starlink satellite kits](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/new-deal-technologique-alioune-sall-lance-loffensive-contre-la-fracture-numerique-a-kolda/>) of the universal connectivity programme, the [telemedicine platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112125172011631/pdf/P172524-657f27b1-2c02-496e-a944-90d4ab9bfee7.pdf>), the planned [Télé@ccesSanté](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Plan%20Directeur%20Informatique%20MSAS.pdf>), the [governmental intranet's broadband links to public health facilities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112125172011631/pdf/P172524-657f27b1-2c02-496e-a944-90d4ab9bfee7.pdf>) and the planned [datacentre and data warehouse](<https://paens.sn/digitalisation-de-certains-systemes-et-services-de-sante/>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held, and no coverage fact is dated after 2021. No approved financing for the system dated within the last three years is held.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank, in a paper of January 2026, names [the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene as lead agency, deploying the electronic medical record](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099013026050538641/pdf/P172524-9a862e0b-dfb7-46ab-8c90-3096cadd5e1a.pdf>). The ministry's sector policy letter of 25 March 2025 [sets the generalisation of the computerised patient record under its Health System Digitalisation Programme 2023-2029](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/sites/default/files/Lettre%20de%20Politique%20Sectorielle%20du%20MSAS%202025-2029%20Signe%CC%81.pdf>). The record is [a core activity of the US$46 million health component of the IDA-financed Senegal Digital Economy Acceleration Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099013026050538641/pdf/P172524-9a862e0b-dfb7-46ab-8c90-3096cadd5e1a.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The health ministry states that the record, which it calls the Dossier Patient Informatisé, [has been in use since November 2024 at the Thierno Birahim Ndao regional hospital in Kaffrine](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/Actualites/digitalisation-hospitali%C3%A8re-le-centre-hospitalier-r%C3%A9gional-thierno-biraahim-ndao-de>). We Are Tech Africa reported on 30 April 2025 [a completed pilot at two hospitals, the Khadim Rassoul health centre and the HLM Fass health post](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-adopts-digital-health-policy-drafts-data-law>).

**Where data is digitised.** At the Kaffrine hospital [the patient is created in the system at registration and the doctor enters observations directly into it after the consultation](<https://www.sante.gouv.sn/Actualites/digitalisation-hospitali%C3%A8re-le-centre-hospitalier-r%C3%A9gional-thierno-biraahim-ndao-de>), on the ministry's account, since November 2024.

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank records that [a pilot of the record across 51 health centres had not started as of December 2025, delayed by disagreement between the health and digital ministries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099013026050538641/pdf/P172524-9a862e0b-dfb7-46ab-8c90-3096cadd5e1a.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank's results framework gives [1 per cent of the 102 district-level health centres as using electronic medical records at 31 October 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099013026050538641/pdf/P172524-9a862e0b-dfb7-46ab-8c90-3096cadd5e1a.pdf>). An electronic record [was available for 200,000 primary care patients at the same date](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112125172011631/pdf/P172524-657f27b1-2c02-496e-a944-90d4ab9bfee7.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***.
