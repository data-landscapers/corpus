# Cabo Verde

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's terms of reference published in February 2024 [name DHIS-2 as one of the three main information systems in use by the ministry](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>), whose Health Technology, Communication and Information Office leads the digital health work.

**Tiers in use.** The same terms of reference state that DHIS-2 is [in operation in all structures of the national health service](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>), from the two central hospitals and four regional hospitals to health centres, health posts and basic health units. It has been [implemented for routine epidemiological management since November 2018](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's terms of reference published in May 2023 state that DHIS2, used to manage aggregated data, is [in production in all structures of the national health service and for all public health programmes](<https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** As at February 2024 DHIS-2 [managed aggregated health information, mainly for epidemiological surveillance](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The February 2024 terms of reference list [32 health centres, 34 health posts and 114 basic health units](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>) among the structures in which DHIS-2 is in operation.

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: [SIMFAR](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/07/30/mundo/africa/cabo-verde-lanca-plataforma-para-detectar-entrada-ilegal-de-medicamentos-e-rupturas-de-stock/>), the [laboratory information management platform of Uma Só Saúde](<https://ugpe.gov.cv/uploads/TDR_OP_3_28_04_2026_vf_1_f29bd63f85.pdf>), the [receita eletrónica](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/ministro-da-saude-aponta-avanco-da-digitalizacao-do-sistema-nacional-de-saude/>), the [national electronic prescription repository](<https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf>), [DHIS2 death certification and cause-of-death notification](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/PT_Relatorio_Bianual_OMS_Cabo_Verde_2024_25.pdf>), [EIOS](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-06/PT_Relatorio_Bianual_OMS_Cabo_Verde_2024_25.pdf>), the [Food and Medication Management System](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>), the [SIS Laboratory Management module](<https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf>) and the [Medicines and Equipment Stock Management module](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>). ## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's terms of reference published in February 2024 [name Medicine One as one of the three main information systems in use by the ministry](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>), a proprietary product licensed per workstation and per period. They state that the government's digital governance strategy [takes health as a priority and includes a single-platform health project](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>), under which the ministry plans what it calls a single electronic clinical process for all health structures, public and private.

**Tiers in use.** As at February 2024 Medicine One was [in use only in Praia's health facilities, namely its health centres and the central hospital](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>). The ministry gives the software's complexity, per-workstation licence costs and bandwidth needs as the reasons.

**Where data is digitised.** Medicine One was [brought in with the Electronic Prescription Project to centralise clinical history in a single electronic repository](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>), on the ministry's account of February 2024.

**Last twelve months.** On 18 December 2025 the Minister of Health, Jorge Figueiredo, announced that [each citizen will get a unique patient code giving access to a digital clinical history](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/ministro-da-saude-aponta-avanco-da-digitalizacao-do-sistema-nacional-de-saude/>), with [digital medical records centralising clinical data across facilities](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/article-6732>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Praia's health centres were [the only ones using Medicine One](<https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf>) in May 2023, and [the position was the same in February 2024](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Terms%2Bof%2BReference%2B-ugpe.pdf/b0d39856-c19f-5ca9-2081-a9cdeb683c0a?t=1706880078938>).
