# Sudan

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Federal Ministry of Health [established an integrated health management information system in 2013 and deployed DHIS2 from 2016](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), WHO's 2020 assessment records. Its strategic plan for 2022-2024 [names DHIS2 in three strategic interventions](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>). Participants at the ministry's review meeting of February 2026 [recommended continued technical and financial support from partners, the Global Fund and UNICEF among them](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO's 2020 assessment states that [DHIS2 had not been implemented in most hospitals](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>). The ministry's plan [gives 86.40 per cent of selected hospitals as using DHIS2 in 2021](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** WHO's 2020 assessment states that [facility data are entered into DHIS2 at the locality level](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), and that [the platform covered 144 of 189 localities in 17 states, of which 122 reported in May 2020](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Federal Minister of Health told the 2025 annual health information performance review that [data reporting coverage rose from 13 per cent at the onset of the crisis to more than 56 per cent by the end of 2025](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>). At that meeting, in February 2026, its Director General of Health Information [called for a full transition from paper-based to digital reporting through DHIS2](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Global Fund's Inspector General [found in 2018 that DHIS2 was not used in health facilities, their results being consolidated on paper and keyed above them](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/8433/oig_gf-oig-19-010_report_en.pdf>). The ministry's plan [gives the DHIS2 reporting rate at primary health care level as 46.90 per cent in 2021](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker module for notifiable diseases](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>) and [Tier.Net](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>). Single-function: the [Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS)](<https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/herams-sudan-health-services-across-states-2026-01>), the [laboratory, pharmacy and disease surveillance components of the Hospital Management System](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>), the [national maternal death surveillance and response system](<https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/board-documents/main-document/DP.FPA_.CPD_.SDN_.8%20-%20Sudan%20CPD%20-%20Final%20-%205Dec25.pdf>), the [gender-based violence information management system](<https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/board-documents/main-document/DP.FPA_.CPD_.SDN_.8%20-%20Sudan%20CPD%20-%20Final%20-%205Dec25.pdf>), the [integrated disease surveillance and response system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [DHIS2 COVID-19 module](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [National Medical Supplies Fund's ERP and electronic logistics management information systems](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [TB reporting system and the separate MDR-TB data collection system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>) and [eVIN](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/8433/oig_gf-oig-19-010_report_en.pdf>). Community: the [community-based health information system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>). Enabling: [IConnect](<https://sudan.un.org/en/301575-rebuilding-sudan%E2%80%99s-health-system-through-digital-innovations>), [Care Connect](<https://sudan.un.org/en/301575-rebuilding-sudan%E2%80%99s-health-system-through-digital-innovations>) and the ministry's [tele-health services for maternal and child health providers](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing dated after April 2023 says where data is keyed, which localities still enter it or whether any facility enters its own reports. The 56 per cent has no numerator or denominator, and whether hospitals key their own reports is not established.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health and the Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications [launched the hospital management system (HMS) on 11 May 2026 as their own programme](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>), on the Sudan News Agency's report. ICTworks' directory, as of July 2026, [lists the Sudan eHealth Strategy of the WHO Global Observatory's 2014 cycle as the operative digital health instrument and states that no replacement has been promulgated](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>).

**Tiers in use.** Phase one of the HMS [started on 11 May 2026 at two hospital sites, Al-Nao hospital in Khartoum state and the diagnostic centre of Gedaref hospital, ahead of gradual expansion to health institutions in other states](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Where data is digitised.** An MSc thesis at Sudan University of Science and Technology, of March 2021, states that [hospitals in Sudan had no electronic medical records and kept patients' health records on paper](<https://repository.sustech.edu/jspui/handle/123456789/26207>).

**Last twelve months.** A directive published on 30 March 2026 has the Prime Minister [ordering hospital systems digitised within two weeks through a first pilot phase at Soba teaching hospital and Gedaref hospital](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%8c-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b3%d9%88%d8%b1-%d9%83%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84-%d8%a5%d8%af%d8%b1%d9%8a%d8%b3%d8%8c/>). The HMS launched on 11 May 2026 [includes an electronic patient record and an electronic medical record](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The first phase directed in March 2026 [covers two hospitals only, with wider expansion left to a later phased plan](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%b1%d8%a6%d9%8a%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%8c-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d8%b1%d9%88%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b3%d9%88%d8%b1-%d9%83%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84-%d8%a5%d8%af%d8%b1%d9%8a%d8%b3%d8%8c/>). The thesis of March 2021 states that [government clinics had no electronic medical record and depended wholly on paper records](<https://repository.sustech.edu/jspui/handle/123456789/26207>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 tracker module for notifiable diseases](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>) and [Tier.Net](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>). Single-function: the [Health Resources and Services Availability Monitoring System (HeRAMS)](<https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/herams-sudan-health-services-across-states-2026-01>), the [laboratory, pharmacy and disease surveillance components of the Hospital Management System](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>), the [national maternal death surveillance and response system](<https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/board-documents/main-document/DP.FPA_.CPD_.SDN_.8%20-%20Sudan%20CPD%20-%20Final%20-%205Dec25.pdf>), the [gender-based violence information management system](<https://www.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/board-documents/main-document/DP.FPA_.CPD_.SDN_.8%20-%20Sudan%20CPD%20-%20Final%20-%205Dec25.pdf>), the [integrated disease surveillance and response system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [DHIS2 COVID-19 module](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [National Medical Supplies Fund's ERP and electronic logistics management information systems](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>), the [TB reporting system and the separate MDR-TB data collection system](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>) and [eVIN](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/8433/oig_gf-oig-19-010_report_en.pdf>). Enabling: [IConnect](<https://sudan.un.org/en/301575-rebuilding-sudan%E2%80%99s-health-system-through-digital-innovations>), [Care Connect](<https://sudan.un.org/en/301575-rebuilding-sudan%E2%80%99s-health-system-through-digital-innovations>) and the ministry's [tele-health services for maternal and child health providers](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Sudan%20Health%20Sector%20Strategic%20Plan%202022%20-%202024.pdf>).
