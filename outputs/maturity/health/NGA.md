# Nigeria

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Federal Ministry of Health [issues the monthly bulletin of the National Health Management Information System (NHMIS)](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). Its annual report for 2021 places the single national DHIS2 instance in the ministry. It sits in the [Department of Health Planning, Research and Statistics](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/NHMIS-Annual-Report-2021-Designed.pdf>). In a health memorandum with the United States of 19 December 2025, Nigeria [commits to use DHIS2 as its national health data warehouse](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>). The United States [plans to fund it over 2026-2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry's bulletin gives the national health facility reporting rate for the NHMIS monthly summary on DHIS-2. It [puts the rate at 91.5% for June 2025](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). It [charts the rate for primary, secondary and tertiary facilities, public and private](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's annual report for 2021 has [facilities submit a paper monthly summary form to the LGA office by the 7th](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/NHMIS-Annual-Report-2021-Designed.pdf>). It is entered into DHIS2 there by the 14th. Gavi's Zero-Dose Learning Hub published an assessment in November 2024. It states that [monthly reports are entered manually into DHIS2 at LGA offices](<https://zdlh.gavi.org/sites/default/files/2024-12/Nigeria_Political_Economy_Assessment_ZDLH_November%202024.pdf>). In June 2025 the ministry [encouraged states to deploy mobile devices and computers for direct reporting to DHIS-2](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The memorandum of 19 December 2025 [expects DHIS2 to collect and report all of its outcome and process metrics](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** A WHO-led study visited 16 randomly selected public primary health care facilities in Nigeria in 2016-2017. It found that [none of them had electronic data collection](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8436492/>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare issued the Nigeria Digital Health Architecture in December 2025. It [sets interoperable electronic health record systems across all levels of care as its first pillar](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>). In a memorandum with the United States of 19 December 2025, Nigeria [commits to roll digital medical records out in 20% of facilities by 2026 and 100% by 2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>). The United States [plans approximately US$28 million for an enhanced electronic medical record system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>). The money covers [2026-2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0009QN-Nigeria-Health-12.19.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The Minister of State for Health spoke to the National Council on Health on 20 November 2025. The minister said [over 200 hospitals are integrated with electronic medical records systems](<https://health.gov.ng/opening-remarks-by-the-honourable-minister-of-state-for-health-and-social-welfare-dr-iziaq-adekunle-salako-delivered-at-the-national-council-on-health-nch-2025/>). In Lagos State [an EMR was in use in primary health care centres](<https://lagosministryofhealth.org/lagos-drives-digital-revolution-in-primary-healthcare/>) in May 2025.

**Where data is digitised.** In May 2025 the Lagos State Primary Health Care Board described [its EMR as working "at the point of care"](<https://lagosministryofhealth.org/lagos-drives-digital-revolution-in-primary-healthcare/>). The federal architecture of December 2025 describes health data systems. It states that they [remain largely siloed and paper based, especially in public facilities](<https://www.digitalhealth.gov.ng/_files/ugd/83cfc4_4e435ffb27084421b62605872634624b.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 16 September 2026 the minister of state for health said [the electronic medical record systems deployed in health facilities are fragmented](<https://health.gov.ng/fg-moves-to-accelerate-digital-health-transformation-with-national-health-technology-and-data-analytics-office/>). The minister said [minimum standards would be set and enforced](<https://health.gov.ng/fg-moves-to-accelerate-digital-health-transformation-with-national-health-technology-and-data-analytics-office/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Lagos State Ministry of Health reported on 22 May 2025. It had [the EMR platform deployed in 100 of the state's 326 primary health care facilities](<https://lagosministryofhealth.org/lagos-drives-digital-revolution-in-primary-healthcare/>).
