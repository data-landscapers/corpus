# Burkina Faso

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Direction générale des études et des statistiques sectorielles [coordinates the national health information system](<https://www.sante.gov.bf/fileadmin/manuel_procedure_gestion_info_sanitaire_avril_2025.pdf>), under its procedures manual of July 2023. Enabel reported in July 2026 that [a strategic plan for the system for 2026-2030 is being implemented](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>). The ministry's digital health strategy of January 2025 [is costed at 42.6 billion FCFA, against 2.8 billion FCFA of available funds](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** In 2024 Endos-BF, the ministry's name for its health data warehouse, [took the monthly activity reports of public and private health facilities and of the units of the regional and university hospitals](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). The Countdown to 2030 country team states that [83% of expected monthly facility reports were received nationally through DHIS2 in 2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Burkina-Faso_Full-Report_CAM2025_LAST-FINAL.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** In 2024 [health facilities sent their monthly reports to the district, where data managers keyed them into Endos-BF](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). The ministry's strategy of January 2025 [lists the scaling-up of decentralised entry of routine data in health facilities among the health system's innovations](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Enabel reports that [district management teams and head nurses of health posts were trained in data analysis and use, including quality control of DHIS2 data, at Dédougou and Tenkodogo in July 2026](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In 2024, [in the health districts implementing decentralised entry, the heads of health facilities entered their own monthly activity reports](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). In 2021 [entry by the primary health centres themselves was operational in the Boucle du Mouhoun, one of 13 regions](<https://www.communityhealthdeliverypartnership.org/media/1021/file/BurkinaFaso_Roadmap_FR_Final_20211228-B.pdf.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 that the Ministry of Health, through its Direction des systèmes d'information, [trains the administrators of OpenClinic, a tool chosen at a workshop with the directors general of all university and regional hospitals](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>). A paper on the 2024-2025 pilot describes OpenClinic GA as [a nationally governed open-source hospital information system, customised under a national reference framework](<https://doi.org/10.3233/shti260468>). ICTworks lists the [Plan stratégique de la santé numérique 2025-2029, signed in January 2025, as the operative strategy](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>).

**Tiers in use.** OpenClinic, a hospital management system with clinical and nursing records, [is deployed at pilot sites, the CHUR of Ouahigouya and the CHR of Manga](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>), NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025. At Manga the 2024-2025 pilot [ended in full digitisation of admissions and use of the electronic patient record across multiple departments](<https://doi.org/10.3233/shti260468>). A study with fieldwork from September 2022 to April 2023 found [one patient-record package fully deployed in a public university hospital](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>).

**Where data is digitised.** A study with fieldwork from September 2022 to April 2023 found that [the predominant data collection instruments in health facilities were physical consultation registers](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>).

**Last twelve months.** NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 [the ministry's second training session for OpenClinic administrators, in the Nazinon region](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** By April 2023 [the public health system lacked computerised patient records, those in existence being electronic registers or isolated specialty records in a hospital](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>), a study of 35 digital health interventions found.
