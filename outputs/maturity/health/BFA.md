# Burkina Faso

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Direction générale des études et des statistiques sectorielles [coordinates the national health information system](<https://www.sante.gov.bf/fileadmin/manuel_procedure_gestion_info_sanitaire_avril_2025.pdf>). Its procedures manual of July 2023 sets this. Enabel reported in July 2026 that [a strategic plan for the system for 2026-2030 is being implemented](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>). The ministry's digital health strategy of January 2025 [is costed at 42.6 billion FCFA, against 2.8 billion FCFA of available funds](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** Endos-BF is the ministry's name for its health data warehouse. In 2024 it [took the monthly activity reports of public and private health facilities](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). It also took those of regional and university hospital units. The Countdown to 2030 country team states that in 2024 [83% of expected monthly facility reports were received through DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Burkina-Faso_Full-Report_CAM2025_LAST-FINAL.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** In 2024 [health facilities sent their monthly reports to the district](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). Data managers there keyed them into Endos-BF. The ministry's strategy of January 2025 [lists among its innovations the scaling-up of decentralised entry of routine data in health facilities](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burkina%20Faso/Strategiceskij%20plan%20razvitia%20cifrovogo%20zdravoohranenia%20na%202025-2029%20gg.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Enabel reports training at Dédougou and Tenkodogo in July 2026. District management teams and head nurses of health posts were trained in data analysis and use. The [training included quality control of DHIS2 data](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In 2024, in districts with decentralised entry, [the heads of health facilities entered their own monthly activity reports](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>). In 2021 [entry by the primary health centres themselves was operational in the Boucle du Mouhoun](<https://www.communityhealthdeliverypartnership.org/media/1021/file/BurkinaFaso_Roadmap_FR_Final_20211228-B.pdf.pdf>), one of 13 regions.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 on the Ministry of Health's Direction des systèmes d'information. It [trains the administrators of OpenClinic](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>). The tool was chosen at a workshop with the directors general of all university and regional hospitals. A paper on the 2024-2025 pilot describes OpenClinic GA as [a nationally governed open-source hospital information system](<https://doi.org/10.3233/shti260468>). It is customised under a national reference framework. ICTworks lists the [Plan stratégique de la santé numérique 2025-2029 as the operative strategy](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>). The plan was signed in January 2025.

**Tiers in use.** OpenClinic is a hospital management system with clinical and nursing records. It [is deployed at pilot sites, the CHUR of Ouahigouya and the CHR of Manga](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>), NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025. At Manga the 2024-2025 pilot [ended in full digitisation of admissions](<https://doi.org/10.3233/shti260468>). It also ended in use of the electronic patient record across multiple departments. A study with fieldwork from September 2022 to April 2023 found [one patient-record package fully deployed in a public university hospital](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>).

**Where data is digitised.** The same study found that [the predominant data collection instruments in health facilities were physical consultation registers](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>).

**Last twelve months.** NetAfrique reported on 18 July 2025 [the ministry's second training session for OpenClinic administrators, in the Nazinon region](<https://netafrique.net/digitalisation-de-la-gestion-des-hopitaux-au-burkina-des-administrateurs-en-formation-dans-la-region-du-nazinon/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** By April 2023, a study of 35 digital health interventions found [the public health system lacked computerised patient records](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12911-024-02574-4>). Those in existence were electronic registers or isolated specialty records in a hospital.
