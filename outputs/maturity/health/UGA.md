# Uganda

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's Division of Health Information [manages DHIS2, which the ministry rolled out as the national reporting system to 112 districts in 2011](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12512544/>), according to a study published in October 2025. The National Health Compact 2025-2030, adopted in December 2025, [commits to scale DHIS2 up to all facilities, with 100% of hospitals and 80% of Health Centre IVs and IIIs reporting in real time by 2028](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compacts-Uganda.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry [coded 6,546 health facilities in DHIS2 in FY 2023/24 and publishes DHIS2 figures by level, from national referral hospitals to Health Centre IIs and clinics](<https://library.health.go.ug/sites/default/files/resources/Annual%20Health%20Sector%20Performance%20Report%20%20FY%202023_24.pdf>). A census of all 265 government Health Centre IVs and hospitals, published in October 2025, [found DHIS2 in use in 98.8% of them](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12512544/>).

**Where data is digitised.** At a launch on 11 August 2026 the Uganda Muslim Medical Bureau said [many Health Centre IIIs lack computers and connectivity to submit reports, and Ziiru Health Centre III carries its report to Buvuma district headquarters](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/ucc-launches-digital-skilling-for-health-cent-NV_239098_082026>). In Health Centre IVs and hospitals, [paper registers capture patient data, which is later aggregated and entered into DHIS2](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12512544/>), on the October 2025 study's account.

**Last twelve months.** Makerere University's METS programme reported in April 2026 that [reporting through DHIS2 rose from 58 percent in 2020 to 98 percent in 2025](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [decentralising data entry to facilities continued in FY 2023/24](<https://library.health.go.ug/sites/default/files/resources/Annual%20Health%20Sector%20Performance%20Report%20%20FY%202023_24.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's guidelines of 18 September 2024 [name the ministry as owner of the Electronic Medical Records System](<https://library.health.go.ug/file-download/download/public/1887>). The US-Uganda memorandum signed on 10 December 2025 [names eAfya and ClinicMaster as the Government of Uganda's electronic medical records](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0012QN-Uganda-Health-12.10.2025.pdf>) and [sets a functional EMR in all facilities down to Health Centre III by 2028](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0012QN-Uganda-Health-12.10.2025.pdf>). Under it the United States [plans US$113,102,289 for data systems over 2026-2030](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/2026-0012QN-Uganda-Health-12.10.2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry says that as of 2025 [the EMR is in referral and general hospitals and in some health centres](<https://health.go.ug/electronic-medical-records-rollout-transforms-patient-care-and-data-management/>). In Kiryandongo district it was [in use in September 2026 at the general hospital, Nyakadoti Health Centre III and Mpumwe and Apodorwa Health Centre IIs](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/kiryandongo-health-facilities-struggle-with-e-NV_241031_092026>).

**Where data is digitised.** The in-charge of Nyakadoti Health Centre III said on 19 September 2026 that [patient data must be captured in the EMR at every stage of the visit](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/kiryandongo-health-facilities-struggle-with-e-NV_241031_092026>). A census of all 265 government Health Centre IVs and hospitals, published in October 2025, [found 94.3% keeping both paper and digital patient records](<https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12512544/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 31 March 2026 Makerere University's METS programme [handed the UgandaEMR repositories to the Ministry of Health](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>). Health workers told a baraza on 19 September 2026 that [the EMR was slowing care in Kiryandongo facilities because of poor connectivity, unstable power and too few computers](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/kiryandongo-health-facilities-struggle-with-e-NV_241031_092026>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry [counts 5 Health Centre IVs and 2 Health Centre IIIs using the EMR as of 2025](<https://health.go.ug/electronic-medical-records-rollout-transforms-patient-care-and-data-management/>).
