## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Seychelles Nation reports the health minister speaking on 17 October 2023. The minister said [the Ministry of Health is investing in an Electronic Health Information System encompassing Electronic Medical Records](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->). That is the ministry's name for it. The National Health Strategic Plan 2022-2026 sets [specific objective 6.8, "Implement eHIS"](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SD6-Specific-Objectives.pdf>). It [names the Health Care Agency as lead entity](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SD6-Specific-Objectives.pdf>). The Ministry of Health and the Public Health Authority contribute. Seychelles News Agency reports that [the software is funded through a line of credit from the Indian government](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/18825/Digitisation+of+Seychelles'+public+health+system+nearly+complete>). The credit is [around USD 4 million, under a contract of December 2019](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/18825/Digitisation+of+Seychelles'+public+health+system+nearly+complete>).

**Tiers in use.** The minister spoke in October 2023. The minister said the system is [to be accessible to all health professionals throughout the health system](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->).

**Where data is digitised.** The Health Care Agency's chief executive said in June 2023 that [some doctors still use handwritten notes](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/18825/Digitisation+of+Seychelles'+public+health+system+nearly+complete>). The ministry held a digital health visioning meeting on 22 January 2024. It said it was [trying to link the system with laboratories and X-ray](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20833/leaders-scrutinize-seychelles-digital-health-transformation>). The aim is that [doctors get results on their computers](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20833/leaders-scrutinize-seychelles-digital-health-transformation>).

**Last twelve months.** Seychelles Nation carried a ministry press release in January 2024. It states that the system [has been operational since the end of 2021](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20833/leaders-scrutinize-seychelles-digital-health-transformation>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Health Care Agency spoke in June 2023. It said it was [training doctors to use the electronic medical records clinic by clinic](<https://www.seychellesnewsagency.com/public/articles/18825/Digitisation+of+Seychelles'+public+health+system+nearly+complete>).
