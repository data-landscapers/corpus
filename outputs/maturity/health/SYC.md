# Seychelles

## HMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the Ministry of Health's [disease surveillance system](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->), its [laboratory digital platforms](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->), its [imaging department digital platforms](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->) and its [stock management digital platform](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->).

***Not held***. No tier reporting digitally and no primary clinic entering its own reports is held. The ministry's 2022 plan describes data capture as primarily paper-based and its electronic system work as stalled, which is neither a dated statement of no digital entry at any level nor a contracted rollout.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Seychelles Nation reports the health minister saying on 17 October 2023 that [the Ministry of Health is investing in an Electronic Health Information System encompassing Electronic Medical Records](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->), the ministry's name for it. The National Health Strategic Plan 2022-2026 sets [specific objective 6.8, "Implement eHIS"](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SD6-Specific-Objectives.pdf>), and [names the Health Care Agency as lead entity, with the Ministry of Health and the Public Health Authority contributing](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SD6-Specific-Objectives.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The minister said in October 2023 that the system is [to be accessible to all health professionals throughout the health system](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->).

**Where data is digitised.** At its digital health visioning meeting of 22 January 2024 the ministry said it was [trying to link the system with laboratories and X-ray so that doctors get results on their computers](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20833/leaders-scrutinize-seychelles-digital-health-transformation>).

**Last twelve months.** A ministry press release carried by Seychelles Nation in January 2024 states that the system [has been operational since the end of 2021](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20833/leaders-scrutinize-seychelles-digital-health-transformation>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The 2022-2026 plan sets [three go-live phases, the first being client registration and appointments across all relevant units](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SD6-Specific-Objectives.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the Ministry of Health's [disease surveillance system](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->), its [laboratory digital platforms](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->), its [imaging department digital platforms](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->) and its [stock management digital platform](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/19775/digital-maturity-of-seychelles-health-sector-in-the-spotlight--by-sunny-esparon->).

***Not held***. The tiers using the system, where the record is entered and the primary clinics using it are not established. Finance is not stated, and nothing dated inside the last twelve months is held.
