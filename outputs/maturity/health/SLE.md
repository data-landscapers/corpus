# Sierra Leone

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's National Digital Health Roadmap 2024-2026 [lists DHIS2 as the health management information system](<https://mohs.gov.sl/download/68/digital-health/18150/sierral-leone-digital-health-roadmap-2024-2026-2.pdf>). The Ministry has deployed it in all districts. GIZ and the Ministry made a landscape assessment dated January 2023. It states that [the Ministry's 2022 statutory budget made no provision for digital health](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The January 2023 assessment surveyed 34 secondary and 10 tertiary facilities. It states that [all of them had M&E Officers](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>). Their [role was predominantly data capture and upload on DHIS2](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The Ministry's HMIS procedures manual dates from April 2020. Under it, [peripheral health units compile monthly reports from paper registers](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>). They send hard copies to the District Health Management Team. A data entry officer there keys them into DHIS2. [A hospital's M&E officer enters the hospital's data](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>). The January 2023 assessment found [M&E Officers at district level capturing and uploading data on DHIS2](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Statistics Sierra Leone, the Ministry of Health and APHRC give DHIS2 completeness of expected monthly facility reports. They put it at [98% nationally in 2024, with all districts at 90% or more](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The April 2020 manual [has no peripheral health unit entering its own data](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>). It notes a plan for 2020 to move electronic entry to the community health centres. The January 2023 assessment surveyed 246 health facilities across the 16 districts, 202 of them primary. It states that [DHIS2 was deployed in 96% of them](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** OpenMRS's case study of April 2026 describes the PIH EMR as [Partners In Health's own OpenMRS distribution](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The National Health Compact of 6 December 2025 [commits the Government to roll out Electronic Medical Records to all medical facilities by 2030](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>). It [names a Ministry of Health directorate as lead](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>). The Ministry's request for expressions of interest of 18 December 2025 says [a donor grant finances the initial phase](<https://mohs.gov.sl/jobs/hiring-of-a-consulting-firm-to-develop-customize-deploy-manage-and-maintain-an-electronic-medical-records-system/>).

**Tiers in use.** The case study states that [Koidu Government Hospital, a referral centre, uses the PIH EMR](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). It states that [the EMR has run at Wellbody Clinic since 2015](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The Ministry reported on 11 October 2023 that it and Smart Systems Sierra Leone Ltd [had launched an EMR pilot at Connaught Hospital in Freetown](<https://mohs.gov.sl/emr-system-to-improve-health-care-delivery-in-sierra-leone/>).

**Where data is digitised.** The case study covers the EMR at the Maternal Center of Excellence in Koidu. It states that [the EMR is entered at the point of care, with retrospective entry also possible](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>).

**Last twelve months.** The Maternal Center of Excellence [opened on 14 February 2026 with the EMR covering all its inpatient wards](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). On 18 December 2025 the Ministry's project unit [asked firms to express interest](<https://mohs.gov.sl/jobs/hiring-of-a-consulting-firm-to-develop-customize-deploy-manage-and-maintain-an-electronic-medical-records-system/>). The work is [deploying an OpenMRS-based EMR across 300 health facilities](<https://mohs.gov.sl/jobs/hiring-of-a-consulting-firm-to-develop-customize-deploy-manage-and-maintain-an-electronic-medical-records-system/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The case study [names one clinic using the PIH EMR, Wellbody Clinic](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The Compact of 6 December 2025 [gives a baseline of 5 health facilities implementing the system](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>). It [puts the network at about 1,463 public facilities](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>).
