# Sierra Leone

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's National Digital Health Roadmap 2024-2026 [lists DHIS2 as the health management information system, deployed by the Ministry in all districts](<https://mohs.gov.sl/download/68/digital-health/18150/sierral-leone-digital-health-roadmap-2024-2026-2.pdf>). The landscape assessment by GIZ and the Ministry, dated January 2023, states that [its 2022 statutory budget made no provision for digital health](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The January 2023 assessment states that [all 34 secondary and all 10 tertiary facilities it surveyed had M&E Officers whose role was predominantly data capture and upload on DHIS2](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** Under the Ministry's HMIS procedures manual of April 2020, [peripheral health units compile monthly reports from paper registers and send hard copies to the District Health Management Team, where a data entry officer keys them into DHIS2](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>), and [a hospital's M&E officer enters the hospital's data](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>). The January 2023 assessment found [M&E Officers at district level capturing and uploading data on DHIS2](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Statistics Sierra Leone, the Ministry of Health and APHRC give [DHIS2 completeness of expected monthly facility reports as 98% nationally in 2024, with all districts at 90% or more](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The April 2020 manual [has no peripheral health unit entering its own data, and notes a plan for 2020 to move electronic entry to the community health centres](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Working_Groups/RHIS/B._Country-specific_documentation__case_studies__best_practices_/Data_collection_and_management/Official_RHIS_reports/Sierra_Leone_RHIS_SOPs_-_volume_1_.pdf>). The January 2023 assessment states that [DHIS2 was deployed in 96% of the 246 health facilities it surveyed across the 16 districts, 202 of them primary](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SIERRA-LEONE-Health-Data-Ecosystem-Mapping-DIPC.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** OpenMRS's case study of April 2026 describes the PIH EMR as [Partners In Health's own OpenMRS distribution](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The National Health Compact of 6 December 2025 [commits the Government to roll out Electronic Medical Records to all medical facilities by 2030](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>) and [names a Ministry of Health directorate as lead](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>). The Ministry's request for expressions of interest of 18 December 2025 states that [a donor grant finances the initial phase](<https://mohs.gov.sl/jobs/hiring-of-a-consulting-firm-to-develop-customize-deploy-manage-and-maintain-an-electronic-medical-records-system/>).

**Tiers in use.** The case study states that [Koidu Government Hospital, a referral centre, uses the PIH EMR](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>) and that [the EMR has run at Wellbody Clinic since 2015](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The Ministry reported on 11 October 2023 that [it and Smart Systems Sierra Leone Ltd had launched an EMR pilot at Connaught Hospital in Freetown](<https://mohs.gov.sl/emr-system-to-improve-health-care-delivery-in-sierra-leone/>).

**Where data is digitised.** The case study states that [the EMR at the Maternal Center of Excellence in Koidu is entered at the point of care, with retrospective entry also possible](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>).

**Last twelve months.** The Maternal Center of Excellence [opened on 14 February 2026 with the EMR covering all its inpatient wards](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). On 18 December 2025 the Ministry's project unit [asked firms to express interest in deploying an OpenMRS-based EMR across 300 health facilities](<https://mohs.gov.sl/jobs/hiring-of-a-consulting-firm-to-develop-customize-deploy-manage-and-maintain-an-electronic-medical-records-system/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The case study of April 2026 [names one clinic using the PIH EMR, Wellbody Clinic](<https://openmrs.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/docs/pages/947060773/PIH+EMR+Maternal+Center+of+Excellence+Case+Study>). The Compact of 6 December 2025 [gives a baseline of 5 health facilities implementing the system, in a network it puts at about 1,463 public facilities](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0273f33ab6ee48c5d842108b9b55c789-0140022025/related/National-Health-Compact-Sierra-Leone.pdf>).
