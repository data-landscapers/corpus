# Djibouti

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNFPA Djibouti states that the Ministry of Health [ensures national governance of the integrated DHIS2 and GBVIMS-Health platform, which its Health Information Directorate administers](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>), at a workshop dated 2 February 2025. The ministry [began integrating gender-based violence data into what UNFPA calls the National Health Information System (DHIS2) with UNFPA's technical and financial support](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>). The national health development plan for 2020 to 2024 [committed to implementing DHIS2 across the whole national territory](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/DJI_Djibouti_Plan-National-de-Developpement-Sanitaire_2020-2024.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** WHO's country office, on 14 April 2025, [listed the review and enhancement of DHIS2 as implemented at primary health care level as an action in progress](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/evaluation-office/management-response_evaluation-of-who-contribution-in-djibouti.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=c96230e4_3>).

**Where data is digitised.** The World Bank states that at 30 June 2025 [some health facilities use DHIS-2 directly while others track data on paper and submit it immediately](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102225172035856/pdf/P178033-79193a4a-bd86-4d36-b863-cdbc43bde6c7.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's results table shows [the share of facilities with DHIS-2 data entry at the facility level rising from 69% at 31 December 2024 to 84% at 30 June 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102225172035856/pdf/P178033-79193a4a-bd86-4d36-b863-cdbc43bde6c7.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank records [84% of all health facilities with DHIS-2 data entry at the facility level at 30 June 2025, against an end target of 65% for May 2027](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102225172035856/pdf/P178033-79193a4a-bd86-4d36-b863-cdbc43bde6c7.pdf>). Its [baseline was 35% in May 2022](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102225172035856/pdf/P178033-79193a4a-bd86-4d36-b863-cdbc43bde6c7.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: [GBVIMS-Health](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>) and the [dossier patient électronique de planification familiale](<https://djibouti.un.org/fr/294824-djibouti-am%C3%A9liore-ses-services-de-planification-familiale-gr%C3%A2ce-au-dossier-patient>), as the United Nations in Djibouti names it. Single-function: [mSupply](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/djibouti-completes-nationwide-rollout-of-msupply-software-to-modernize-healthcare>) and the [National Logistics Management Information System (eLMIS)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102225172035856/pdf/P178033-79193a4a-bd86-4d36-b863-cdbc43bde6c7.pdf>). Community: the planned [système d'information à base communautaire](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/DJI_Djibouti_Plan-National-de-Developpement-Sanitaire_2020-2024.pdf>) of the 2020 to 2024 health plan. Enabling: the [Djibouti Fondations Numériques fibre connection](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>), the [digital health equipment handed over by IGAD](<https://igad.int/igad-hands-over-digital-health-equipment-to-djiboutis-ministry-of-health/>), the [Medyc online platform](<https://www.gavi.org/fr/vaccineswork/doucement-mais-surement-sante-devient-numerique-djibouti>) and the planned [Starlink teleconsultation pilot](<https://www.gavi.org/fr/vaccineswork/doucement-mais-surement-sante-devient-numerique-djibouti>).

***Not held***. No current plan naming the system and no domestic budget line dated within the last three years is held. The 84% is of all health facilities, with no numerator or denominator, and no dated event in the last twelve months is held.
