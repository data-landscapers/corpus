# Equatorial Guinea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry [announced the conclusion of the DHIS2 pilot on 25 November 2025](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). It presents the platform as its tool for the national health system. It states that [the pilot ran under a primary-care agreement](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). The Spanish cooperation agency AECID financed the agreement. UNDP, on 1 March 2025, quotes the health minister. The minister placed [the digitalisation within the national health development plan PNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). On 16 November 2026 [UNDP handed DHIS2 over to the health ministry](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/el-ministerio-de-sanidad-e-infraestructuras-sanitarias-refuerza-su-gestion-digital-con-las-plataformas-transdoc-y-dhis2>).

**Tiers in use.** EHAS describes the first pilot, in 2025. It [let health facilities in two districts enter real data](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). The districts were [insular Baney and continental Añisok](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Where data is digitised.** EHAS, in 2025, states that [health facilities filled paper register sheets](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). Ministry staff periodically collected them and transferred them to spreadsheets. In the Baney and Añisok pilot [data is entered from the health facility with the DHIS2 Android or web app](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). That is where digital means are available. Otherwise [data is entered at the District Office](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 25 November 2025 the ministry [declared the pilot in Baney and Añisok successfully completed](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). It said the pilot [would be the reference for a national expansion of DHIS2 foreseen for 2026](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [the DHIS2 pilot ran in 2025 in two health districts, Baney and Añisok](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). In it, [104 health professionals were trained](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 individual register and follow-up of routine vaccination](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the [DHIS2 follow-up of HIV patients](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 data on health workforce availability and qualification](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the [DHIS2 inventory control in medicine dispensaries](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Also single-function: the [DHIS2 malaria eLMIS of the Bioko Island Malaria Elimination Project](<https://dhis2.org/equatorial-guinea-malaria-lmis/>) and the [Sistema Nacional de Vigilancia Epidemiológica](<https://guineasalud.org/archivos/Protocolos/0567FolletoPlanEstrategicoSNIS_0303.pdf>), as the ministry names it. Enabling: the [Data Center at the ministry's Malabo headquarters](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Also enabling: the proposed [GALE digital health pilot of eClinics telemedicine stations](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_explora_alianzas_estrategicas_en_ee_uu_para_reforzar_proteger_y_modernizar_su_sistema_nacional_de_salud>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Vannin Group is the vendor, and calls its product Greencube. It states that [it deployed Greencube at Hospital Nuevo INSESO in Bata in 2018](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>).

**Tiers in use.** The vendor published a case study dated 2018. It describes Greencube as [the unified electronic health record platform of Hospital Nuevo INSESO](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>). The hospital has 85 beds and is in Bata.

**Where data is digitised.** Vannin Group counts [600 active users of its Greencube platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>), in 2018. It calls the platform a unified electronic health record platform.

**Last twelve months.** Vannin Group dates [the deployment of its platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO in Bata to 2018](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>). It then counted 600 active users and 60,000 patient records there.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Vannin Group reports [60,000 patient records held on its Greencube platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>) in 2018. It is the one hospital the vendor names, and has 85 beds.
