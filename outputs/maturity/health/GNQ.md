# Equatorial Guinea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry [announced the conclusion of the DHIS2 pilot on 25 November 2025 and presents the platform as its tool for the national health system](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). It states that [the pilot ran under a primary-care agreement financed by the Spanish cooperation agency AECID](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). UNDP, on 1 March 2025, quotes the health minister placing [the digitalisation within the national health development plan PNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>).

**Tiers in use.** EHAS states that [the first pilot, in 2025, let health facilities in two districts, insular Baney and continental Añisok, enter real data](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Where data is digitised.** EHAS, in 2025, states that [health facilities filled paper register sheets, which ministry staff periodically collected and transferred to spreadsheets](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). In the Baney and Añisok pilot [data is entered from the health facility with the DHIS2 Android or web app where digital means are available, and at the District Office otherwise](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 25 November 2025 the ministry [declared the pilot in Baney and Añisok successfully completed and said it would be the reference for a national expansion of DHIS2 foreseen for 2026](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [the DHIS2 pilot ran in 2025 in two health districts, Baney and Añisok, with 104 health professionals trained](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 individual register and follow-up of routine vaccination](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the [DHIS2 follow-up of HIV patients](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 data on health workforce availability and qualification](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 inventory control in medicine dispensaries](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 malaria eLMIS of the Bioko Island Malaria Elimination Project](<https://dhis2.org/equatorial-guinea-malaria-lmis/>) and the [Sistema Nacional de Vigilancia Epidemiológica](<https://guineasalud.org/archivos/Protocolos/0567FolletoPlanEstrategicoSNIS_0303.pdf>), as the ministry names it. Enabling: the [Data Center at the ministry's Malabo headquarters](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the proposed [GALE digital health pilot of eClinics telemedicine stations](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_explora_alianzas_estrategicas_en_ee_uu_para_reforzar_proteger_y_modernizar_su_sistema_nacional_de_salud>).

***Not found***. No source names the tiers or counts the facilities entering data in the Baney and Añisok pilot, and nothing dated after November 2025 shows the national expansion foreseen for 2026 under way. No plan naming DHIS2 and no domestic budget line dated within the last three years has been found.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** Vannin Group, the vendor, states that [it deployed Greencube, its name for the product, at Hospital Nuevo INSESO in Bata in 2018](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>).

**Tiers in use.** The vendor describes Greencube as [the unified electronic health record platform of Hospital Nuevo INSESO, a hospital of 85 beds in Bata](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>), in a case study dated 2018.

**Where data is digitised.** Vannin Group counts [600 active users of its Greencube platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>), in 2018, on what it calls a unified electronic health record platform.

**Last twelve months.** Vannin Group dates [the deployment of its platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO in Bata to 2018](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>), when it counted 600 active users and 60,000 patient records there.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Vannin Group reports [60,000 patient records held on its Greencube platform at Hospital Nuevo INSESO, the one hospital it names](<https://vanningroup.com/case-studies/hospital-nuevo-inseso-bata-equatorial-guinea>), in 2018, at a hospital of 85 beds.

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 individual register and follow-up of routine vaccination](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the [DHIS2 follow-up of HIV patients](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 data on health workforce availability and qualification](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 inventory control in medicine dispensaries](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 malaria eLMIS of the Bioko Island Malaria Elimination Project](<https://dhis2.org/equatorial-guinea-malaria-lmis/>) and the [Sistema Nacional de Vigilancia Epidemiológica](<https://guineasalud.org/archivos/Protocolos/0567FolletoPlanEstrategicoSNIS_0303.pdf>), as the ministry names it. Enabling: the [Data Center at the ministry's Malabo headquarters](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the proposed [GALE digital health pilot of eClinics telemedicine stations](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_explora_alianzas_estrategicas_en_ee_uu_para_reforzar_proteger_y_modernizar_su_sistema_nacional_de_salud>).

***Not found***. Nothing dated after 2018 shows the record at the hospital in Bata still in use, and no other facility keeping electronic patient records has been found. Who owns and pays for the system, where the record is entered and any use in primary clinics are not held.
