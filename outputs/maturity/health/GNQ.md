# Equatorial Guinea

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry [announced the conclusion of the DHIS2 pilot on 25 November 2025 and presents the platform as its tool for the national health system](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). It states that [the pilot ran under a primary-care agreement financed by the Spanish cooperation agency AECID](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). UNDP, on 1 March 2025, quotes the health minister placing [the digitalisation within the national health development plan PNDS 2021-2025](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>).

**Tiers in use.** EHAS states that [the first pilot, in 2025, let health facilities in two districts, insular Baney and continental Añisok, enter real data](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Where data is digitised.** EHAS, in 2025, states that [health facilities filled paper register sheets, which ministry staff periodically collected and transferred to spreadsheets](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). In the Baney and Añisok pilot [data is entered from the health facility with the DHIS2 Android or web app where digital means are available, and at the District Office otherwise](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 25 November 2025 the ministry [declared the pilot in Baney and Añisok successfully completed and said it would be the reference for a national expansion of DHIS2 foreseen for 2026](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The ministry states that [the DHIS2 pilot ran in 2025 in two health districts, Baney and Añisok, with 104 health professionals trained](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 individual register and follow-up of routine vaccination](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the [DHIS2 follow-up of HIV patients](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). Single-function: the [DHIS2 data on health workforce availability and qualification](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 inventory control in medicine dispensaries](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>), the [DHIS2 malaria eLMIS of the Bioko Island Malaria Elimination Project](<https://dhis2.org/equatorial-guinea-malaria-lmis/>) and the [Sistema Nacional de Vigilancia Epidemiológica](<https://guineasalud.org/archivos/Protocolos/0567FolletoPlanEstrategicoSNIS_0303.pdf>), as the ministry names it. Enabling: the [Data Center at the ministry's Malabo headquarters](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>) and the proposed [GALE digital health pilot of eClinics telemedicine stations](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/guinea_ecuatorial_explora_alianzas_estrategicas_en_ee_uu_para_reforzar_proteger_y_modernizar_su_sistema_nacional_de_salud>).
