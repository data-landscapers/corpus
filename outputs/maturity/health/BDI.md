# Burundi

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's Direction du Système National d'Information Sanitaire [manages the health information system, and its IT cell administers DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>). The ministry's statistical yearbook of July 2024 states this. The government signed a memorandum with the United States on 6 February 2026. In it the government [commits to use DHIS2 as its national health data warehouse](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>). The United States [plans $188,526 for it in 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry [collected its 2023 data through the national DHIS2 platform](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>) from health centres, district hospitals and national hospitals. Report forms are set for the community, the health centre and the hospital.

**Where data is digitised.** Facilities record by hand in paper registers, the World Bank reported in March 2023. [The entry into DHIS2 is made at the facility](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>). The deputy head of a health centre makes it, as does a hospital's information officer. Community health workers' monthly reports [go to the health centre for entry](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>), by the method note of the ministry's 2023 yearbook.

**Last twelve months.** The [memorandum of 6 February 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>) runs to 31 December 2030. It costs United States support for DHIS2 at about $609,872 over the term. The support is for linking DHIS2 with SIDAinfo and other systems. It is also for bringing in supply chain and laboratory data.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank published an assessment in March 2023. In it, reports in DHIS2 were [more than 95% complete for health centres at 30 June 2022](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** OpenClinic [sits under Enabel and PROGISSA](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), the World Bank reported in March 2023. PROGISSA is the health informatics programme the ministry created in 2020. In a memorandum signed on 6 February 2026 the government [commits to use SIDAinfo as its electronic medical records system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>). The United States [intends to fund it with $565,579 in 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** [Forty-one of 71 public and faith-based hospitals are computerised](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), patient health records included, and 39 of them run OpenClinic, the World Bank reported in March 2023, citing PROGISSA. They are not interconnected and each has its own patient identifier.

**Where data is digitised.** The ministry's plan drawn up in 2019 describes the computerised hospitals. In them OpenClinic GA [runs on a local server in each hospital](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/BDI_Burundi_National-plan-development-digital-health_2020-2024.pdf>). Data passes between clinical services, laboratory, imaging and pharmacy in real time. No further manual entry is needed.

**Last twelve months.** The memorandum is dated 6 February 2026. It [commits Burundi to roll SIDAinfo out in 460 facilities by 2026 and 572 by 2028](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [Six health centres are computerised under a pilot financed by Enabel](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), the World Bank reported in March 2023. Another 29 were planned during that year on Enabel's financing. Four more were planned on the government's. The ministry's 2019 plan [puts first-level facilities at more than 1,000](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/BDI_Burundi_National-plan-development-digital-health_2020-2024.pdf>).
