# Burundi

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The health ministry's Direction du Système National d'Information Sanitaire [manages the health information system, and its IT cell administers DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>), as the ministry's statistical yearbook of July 2024 states. In a memorandum signed with the United States on 6 February 2026 the government [commits to use DHIS2 as its national health data warehouse](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>), and the United States [plans $188,526 for it in 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The ministry [collected its 2023 data through the national DHIS2 platform](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>) from health centres, district hospitals and national hospitals, with report forms set for the community, the health centre and the hospital.

**Where data is digitised.** Facilities record by hand in paper registers and [the entry into DHIS2 is made at the facility](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), by the deputy head of a health centre and by a hospital's information officer, the World Bank reported in March 2023. Community health workers' monthly reports [go to the health centre for entry](<https://extranet.who.int/uhcpartnershiplivemonitoring/system/files/doc_attachments/Annuaire%20des%20Statistiques%20Sanitaires%202023.pdf>), by the method note of the ministry's 2023 yearbook.

**Last twelve months.** The [memorandum of 6 February 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>) runs to 31 December 2030 and costs United States support for DHIS2 at about $609,872 over the term, for linking it with SIDAinfo and other systems and for bringing in supply chain and laboratory data.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** Reports in DHIS2 were [more than 95% complete for health centres at 30 June 2022](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), in the World Bank's assessment of March 2023.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** OpenClinic [sits under Enabel and PROGISSA](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), the health informatics programme the ministry created in 2020, the World Bank reported in March 2023. In a memorandum signed on 6 February 2026 the government [commits to use SIDAinfo as its electronic medical records system](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>), and the United States [intends to fund it with $565,579 in 2026](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** [Forty-one of 71 public and faith-based hospitals are computerised](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), patient health records included, and 39 of them run OpenClinic, the World Bank reported in March 2023, citing PROGISSA. They are not interconnected and each has its own patient identifier.

**Where data is digitised.** In the computerised hospitals OpenClinic GA [runs on a local server in each hospital](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/BDI_Burundi_National-plan-development-digital-health_2020-2024.pdf>), with data passing between clinical services, laboratory, imaging and pharmacy in real time without further manual entry, the ministry's plan drawn up in 2019 states.

**Last twelve months.** The memorandum of 6 February 2026 [commits Burundi to roll SIDAinfo out in 460 facilities by 2026 and 572 by 2028](<https://www.citizen.org/wp-content/uploads/BURUNDI_FL-2026-00021_August-2026-Production_RELEASE.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** [Six health centres are computerised under a pilot financed by Enabel](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050923073520082/pdf/P17512808c1cbf01009a830d30eb4cb8732.pdf>), the World Bank reported in March 2023, with 29 more on Enabel's financing and four on the government's planned during that year. The ministry's 2019 plan [puts first-level facilities at more than 1,000](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/BDI_Burundi_National-plan-development-digital-health_2020-2024.pdf>).
