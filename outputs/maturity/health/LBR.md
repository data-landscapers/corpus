# Liberia

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health's report for 2024 states that [its HMIS runs on DHIS2 and is managed within its Department of Policy, Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>). The ministry's Data Quality Improvement Plan of June 2022 [runs 2022 to 2026 and is costed at US$7,312,887.50](<https://publications.jsi.com/JSIInternet/Inc/Common/_download_pub.cfm?id=25312&lid=3>). The memorandum signed with the United States on 9 December 2025 [plans US$1,338,372 of US funding for HMIS/DHIS2 over 2026 to 2030](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** [Every health facility, public and private, is required to report service data monthly, and counties submit to the central ministry by the 17th of the following month](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), on the ministry's account for 2024.

**Where data is digitised.** Evidence Action's survey found that [all 48 facilities surveyed in Montserrado, Margibi and Grand Bassa filled paper HMIS forms monthly, which district or county data clerks entered into DHIS2](<https://files.givewell.org/files/DWDA%202009/Evidence_Action/Facility_Survey_Report_Liberia_Maternal%20Syphilis_2022.pdf>), in records for May to July 2022. The Liberian team's Countdown to 2030 synthesis of June 2022 says [facilities' monthly reports are normally collected by the County Health Team for entry into DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Liberia-draft-report.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** WHO reports that in 2025, with Gavi support, it [delivered 31 laptops with statistical software for data analysis in the health management information and surveillance systems](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20%20Liberia%20Annual%20Report%202025%20%20.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The Countdown to 2030 synthesis counts [856 functional health facilities submitting reports through DHIS2, of 964 on the Master Facility List](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Liberia-draft-report.pdf>), for data of January 2017 to December 2021.

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 e-tracker](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>), the [DHIS2 Tracker for HIV client-level data](<https://dhis2.org/liberia-hiv-tracker/>) and the [DHIS2 Tracker of the Key Population program](<https://dhis2.org/liberia-hiv-tracker/>). Single-function: [eIDSR](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20%20Liberia%20Annual%20Report%202025%20%20.pdf>), the [Lab Information System](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>), [mSupply and eLMIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>) and the pharmacy system [sLMIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>). Community: the [Community Based Information System (CBIS) and its electronic form, eCBIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>).

***Not held***. No national figure for clinics entering their own reports is held. Nothing on where data is keyed is dated after July 2022, and no event dated inside the last twelve months is held.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** [OneHealthEHR Inc. deploys and runs its platform with a consortium of European firms and research bodies, with the National Public Health Institute of Liberia as surveillance partner](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>), as reported in September 2026. In the memorandum signed with the United States on 9 December 2025, Liberia [commits to an open-source EMR in clinics, health centers and hospitals, with targets of 20% of all public health facilities in 2026 and 100% in 2030](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>), and the United States [plans US$2,676,744 for EMR systems over 2026 to 2030](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The OneHealthEHR platform [is in use at clinics and health centers](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>), as reported in September 2026.

**Where data is digitised.** The company said in September 2026 that [clinicians, nurses and registration staff record patient information in the platform during routine care, offline, with synchronisation when a connection returns](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The company says its platform [went into live clinical use on 1 September 2026 at four facilities across Liberia and Ghana](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>). The Ministry of Health, through its National Health Intelligence Center, [launched enumerator training on 8 May 2026 for a nationwide EMR readiness assessment](<https://informernewspaperliberia.com/ministry-of-health-launches-nationwide-enumerator-training-for-major-emr-readiness-assessment/>). UNFPA [issued a request for quotation dated 27 October 2025 for an EMR at Careysburg and Duport Road Health Centers](<https://liberia.unfpa.org/sites/default/files/vacancies/RFQ%20Template%20-%20Complex%20Services%2C%20Hiring%20of%20an%20ICT%20Firm%20to%20Develop%20Application.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** By the company's account, [the deployment grew from four to seven active clinics and health centers, counted across Liberia and Ghana together, within about three weeks of 1 September 2026](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/onehealthehr-goes-live-in-liberia-ghana/article_778165ac-8dad-4c41-bf63-9413eb2c46a7.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [DHIS2 e-tracker](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>), the [DHIS2 Tracker for HIV client-level data](<https://dhis2.org/liberia-hiv-tracker/>) and the [DHIS2 Tracker of the Key Population program](<https://dhis2.org/liberia-hiv-tracker/>). Single-function: [eIDSR](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20%20Liberia%20Annual%20Report%202025%20%20.pdf>), the [Lab Information System](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>), [mSupply and eLMIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>) and the pharmacy system [sLMIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>). Community: the [Community Based Information System (CBIS) and its electronic form, eCBIS](<https://healthpolicy-watch.news/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/US-Liberia-MOU-2025.pdf>).

***Not held***. The number of Liberian facilities live is not held: the count of seven covers Liberia and Ghana together. Whether the OpenMRS system launched at J.J. Dossen Hospital in 2020 still runs is not on record.
