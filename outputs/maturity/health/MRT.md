# Mauritania

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The SNIS is the Ministry of Health's name for its information system. Arrêté n° 1496 of 14 December 2021 [assigns collection and analysis of SNIS data to the ministry's regional and circonscription services](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau217409.pdf>). The ministry's PNDS 2021-2030 [commits to a SNIS strategic plan to 2030](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). It requires all its indicators in DHIS2. The European Commission's 2025 action document for PASS IV [sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for interoperable government-owned digital solutions](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>). The [information system is among them](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The World Bank's PHCPI assessment of 2023 states that [health posts and health centres collect SNIS data and send them to the moughataa health centre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>). This holds across all health districts. A ministry and Countdown to 2030 analysis of June 2025 covers institutional mortality. It states that [only the hospitals of Hodh Gharbi and Guidimagha have data integrated in DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MAURITANIE-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The World Bank assessment states that [health posts report on paper to health centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>). The information is made digital there. DHIS2 takes the districts' data. Article 18 of the 2021 arrêté [charges the circonscription sanitaire's service with entering its health statistics reports into the database](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau217409.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Countdown to 2030 reports a national workshop from 3 to 7 November 2025. It [trained district-level focal points to use routine SNIS data](<https://www.countdown2030.org/countdown-news/strengthening-the-use-of-srmnia-nutrition-data-for-action-in-mauritania>). It [included DHIS2 practical sessions](<https://www.countdown2030.org/countdown-news/strengthening-the-use-of-srmnia-nutrition-data-for-action-in-mauritania>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank's 2023 assessment gives [92 percent coverage of monthly summary reports from basic health facilities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Single-function: the [registre de consultation électronique](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/mauritania/news/mauritanie-des-donnees-fiables-pour-mieux-repondre-aux-epidemies-dans-le-hod-el-charghi-et-hod-el>), an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: [mHealth pour la santé communautaire](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). Enabling: the ministry's [centre numérique](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>), the [réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), the [télé-radiologie pilot projects](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) and [télémédecine](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

***Not found***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports has been found. The 92 percent is report coverage, given without a numerator or denominator. Nothing found shows a health post entering its own data. Nothing shows hospitals beyond the two named integrated.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The European Commission's 2025 action document says [Mauritania's digital health strategy 2024-2030 is to be carried out partly through electronic medical records](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>). It [sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for procurement under the output that names the patient record](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>). The digital agenda of June 2022 [makes the Ministry of Health carrier of the portal meant to hold the shared medical record](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>). On 21 February 2026 [the health minister said the electronic medical record would start two days later](<https://ar.taqadoumy.mr/archives/69133>). Each patient's file is to sit on the national ID number.

**Tiers in use.** The state news agency AMI reported in 2022 that [one health centre had digitised its patients' records](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>). It was Sebkha, in Nouakchott-Ouest. The digital agenda put [the share of people with a shared medical record at 0% in 2021](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>). Its 2025 target was 50%.

**Where data is digitised.** At Sebkha in 2022 the ministry's secretary general said [the whole patient pathway would be digital by 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2026 Bluesquare said [12 of 21 health centres assessed in Nouakchott, Trarza and Brakna were confirmed as pilot sites for OpenClinic GA](<https://www.bluesquarehub.com/health-system-interoperability-governance-technical-lessons/>). That is the shared primary care record it recommends. The first deployment is planned at the Dar Naim health centre.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** AMI's 2022 report [called Sebkha a pilot centre and a model for the country's facilities](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>). The 12 OpenClinic GA sites of October 2026 [are pilot sites still awaiting the first deployment](<https://www.bluesquarehub.com/health-system-interoperability-governance-technical-lessons/>).

***Also on file***. Single-function: the [registre de consultation électronique](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/mauritania/news/mauritanie-des-donnees-fiables-pour-mieux-repondre-aux-epidemies-dans-le-hod-el-charghi-et-hod-el>), an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: [mHealth pour la santé communautaire](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). Enabling: the ministry's [centre numérique](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>), the [réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), the [télé-radiologie pilot projects](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) and [télémédecine](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing after 2022 shows whether the Sebkha record still runs, and no OpenClinic GA site is shown in use yet. Where the record is keyed is not stated.
