# Mauritania

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Arrêté n° 1496 of 14 December 2021 [assigns collection and analysis of data for the SNIS, the Ministry of Health's name for its information system, to its regional and circonscription services](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau217409.pdf>). The ministry's PNDS 2021-2030 [commits to a SNIS strategic plan to 2030 and requires all its indicators to be included in DHIS2](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). The European Commission's 2025 action document for PASS IV [sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for interoperable government-owned digital solutions, the information system among them](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

**Tiers in use.** The World Bank's PHCPI assessment, published in 2023, states that [health posts and health centres collect SNIS data and send them to the moughataa health centre, across all health districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>). A ministry and Countdown to 2030 analysis of June 2025 states that, for institutional mortality, [only the hospitals of Hodh Gharbi and Guidimagha have data integrated in DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MAURITANIE-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf.pdf>).

**Where data is digitised.** The World Bank assessment states that [health posts report on paper to health centres, where the information is made digital, and DHIS2 takes the districts' data](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>). Article 18 of the 2021 arrêté [charges the circonscription sanitaire's service with entering its health statistics reports into the database](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mau217409.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Countdown to 2030 reports that from 3 to 7 November 2025 [a national workshop trained district-level focal points to use routine SNIS data, with practical sessions in DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/countdown-news/strengthening-the-use-of-srmnia-nutrition-data-for-action-in-mauritania>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The World Bank's 2023 assessment gives [a coverage rate of 92 percent for monthly summary reports from basic health facilities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121523194016438/pdf/P1598650398a4706d09a340d1a66200239.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [registre de consultation électronique](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/mauritania/news/mauritanie-des-donnees-fiables-pour-mieux-repondre-aux-epidemies-dans-le-hod-el-charghi-et-hod-el>), an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: [mHealth pour la santé communautaire](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). Enabling: the ministry's [centre numérique](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>), the [réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), the [télé-radiologie pilot projects](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) and [télémédecine](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary clinics entering their own reports is held; the 92 percent is report coverage, given without a numerator or denominator. Nothing held shows a health post entering its own data, or hospitals beyond the two named integrated.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** The European Commission's 2025 action document for PASS IV states that [Mauritania's digital health strategy 2024-2030 is to be deployed through telemedicine, electronic medical records and integrated management systems](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>), and [sets aside EUR 3,000,000 for procurement under the output that names the patient record](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>). The National Digital Transformation Agenda of June 2022 [names the Ministry of Health as carrier of the Digital Health Portal project that is to hold the shared medical record](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), the DMP.

**Tiers in use.** The state news agency AMI, in a report published in 2022, states that [patients' health records have been digitised at one health centre, that of Sebkha in Nouakchott-Ouest](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>). The Agenda gives [the share of the population holding a shared medical record as 0% in 2021, against a 2025 target of 50%](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), attributing the figure to the Ministry of Health.

**Where data is digitised.** At Sebkha in 2022 the Ministry of Health's secretary general said that [the whole patient pathway, from consultation to discharge, would be fully digital by 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>).

**Last twelve months.** The European Commission's 2025 action document [counts interoperable digital services, the patient record among them, at 0 in 2024 against a target of 5](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In AMI's 2022 report the secretary general [called Sebkha a pilot centre and a model for all the country's health and hospital facilities](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/209725>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [registre de consultation électronique](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/mauritania/news/mauritanie-des-donnees-fiables-pour-mieux-repondre-aux-epidemies-dans-le-hod-el-charghi-et-hod-el>), an electronic consultation register for epidemic data. Community: [mHealth pour la santé communautaire](<https://extranet.who.int/countryplanningcycles/sites/default/files/public_file_rep/MRT_Mauritania_Plan-National-De-D%C3%A9veloppement-Sanitaire_PNDS_2021-2030.pdf>). Enabling: the ministry's [centre numérique](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/288124>), the [réseau d'interconnexion du Ministère de la Santé](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>), the [télé-radiologie pilot projects](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Agenda-Numerique-2022-25-MTNIMA.pdf>) and [télémédecine](<https://statewatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/C-2025-8951-Decision-dexecution-financement-plan-daction-Mauritanie-ANNEX-2.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing held after 2022 shows whether the Sebkha record still runs or has spread to another facility, and where the record is digitised is not stated. No event within the last twelve months is held.
