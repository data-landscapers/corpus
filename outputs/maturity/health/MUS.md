# Mauritius

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** DHIS2's own country story of January 2022 states that [the government, through the Ministry of Health and Wellness, has begun implementing DHIS2 as the national health information system, the work done by the health ministry and the Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation with support from WHO, Gavi, the Global Fund, HISP UiO and HISP Uganda](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). The implementation [is carried out under the Health Sector Strategic Plan 2020-2024](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>).

**Tiers in use.** In January 2022 DHIS2 [was in use at regional and national level](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), on the same account.

**Where data is digitised.** WHO's SCORE assessment, on data from 2013 to 2018, [rates a standardised system of electronic data entry for aggregate reporting at district or comparable level as 'not there', and completeness of reporting above 75% for public primary care facilities, public hospitals and private facilities](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>). The ministry's strategic plan of 2020 states that [the health information system in Rodrigues is largely manual](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Decentralising DHIS2 further [will need more training, staff and IT infrastructure](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), the story of January 2022 states.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In January 2022 the health authorities [planned to decentralise DHIS2 to district and primary health care level, and to Rodrigues and Agalega](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [Electronic Immunization Registry and AEFI Tracker programmes on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). Single-function: [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response on DHIS2](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/18-1%20Health/Final%20MOHW%20Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20FY%202024-2025%2030.10.25.pdf>), the laboratory system [OpenELIS](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>), the [laboratory information management system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>) of e-Health phase 1, [VigiFlow](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), the [national human resources health information system (HRHIS)](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>), [DHIS2 for case detection and surveillance of notifiable diseases](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>), the [Laboratory Management and Information System (LMIS)](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>) and the [paper-based inventory management system for medicines in Rodrigues](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing on the HMIS dated after January 2022 is held, so whether the planned decentralisation to district and primary level has happened is not established. Nor is which tiers use DHIS2 routinely, or whether it carries routine reporting of patients treated.

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNDP's case study of February 2025 states that [UNDP Mauritius ran the procurement that selected TrioTree Technologies and its consortium for the project it names One Patient, One Record](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>), and that [the project has been allocated MUR 600 million, divided into phases](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>). allAfrica reported on 8 January 2026 that [Digital Health Company Ltd is a public company under the Ministry of Health and Wellness](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601080233.html>).

**Tiers in use.** L'Express reported in May 2026 the health minister saying that the system, which he calls e-Health, [is operational at New Flacq Hospital, where testing began on 23 August 2025, and in several primary health care structures of the same region](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>).

**Where data is digitised.** The system [covers the full outpatient cycle from registration to follow-up, with nursing, doctor and pharmacy modules added to phase 1](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>), the minister said. By 30 April 2026 it [had recorded more than 126,000 patient registrations, 225,000 visits and 221,000 medical consultations](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>).

**Last twelve months.** L'Express reported in May 2026 the minister telling Parliament that [deployment is scheduled at Jawaharlal Nehru Hospital by September 2026, Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam National Hospital in March 2027 and Dr A.G. Jeetoo and Victoria hospitals no earlier than December 2027](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The primary structures [using the system in May 2026 are in the New Flacq region](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>). The ministry's Health Sector Strategic Plan 2020-2024 [gave the share of all health facilities using EMR as 0 in 2019, against a target of 100% by 2024](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Trackers: the [Electronic Immunization Registry and AEFI Tracker programmes on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). Single-function: [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response on DHIS2](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/18-1%20Health/Final%20MOHW%20Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20FY%202024-2025%2030.10.25.pdf>), the laboratory system [OpenELIS](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>), the [laboratory information management system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>) of e-Health phase 1, [VigiFlow](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), the [national human resources health information system (HRHIS)](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>), [DHIS2 for case detection and surveillance of notifiable diseases](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>), the [Laboratory Management and Information System (LMIS)](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>) and the [paper-based inventory management system for medicines in Rodrigues](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

***Not held***. The primary structures using the system are neither named nor counted, so no share or count of primary clinics is established. Who pays the MUR 600 million, a current plan naming the system, and whether a record can be retrieved at another facility are not stated.
