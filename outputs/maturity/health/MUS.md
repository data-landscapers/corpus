# Mauritius

## HMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** DHIS2's own country story of January 2022 states that [the government, through the Ministry of Health and Wellness, has begun implementing DHIS2 as the national health information system, the work done by the health ministry and the Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation with support from WHO, Gavi, the Global Fund, HISP UiO and HISP Uganda](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). The implementation [is carried out under the Health Sector Strategic Plan 2020-2024](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>).

**Tiers in use.** In January 2022 DHIS2 [was in use at regional and national level](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), on the same account.

**Where data is digitised.** WHO's SCORE assessment, on data from 2013 to 2018, [rates a standardised system of electronic data entry for aggregate reporting at district or comparable level as 'not there', and completeness of reporting above 75% for public primary care facilities, public hospitals and private facilities](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>). The ministry's strategic plan of 2020 states that [the health information system in Rodrigues is largely manual](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Decentralising DHIS2 further [will need more training, staff and IT infrastructure](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), the story of January 2022 states.

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** In January 2022 the health authorities [planned to decentralise DHIS2 to district and primary health care level, and to Rodrigues and Agalega](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>).

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [Electronic Immunization Registry and AEFI Tracker programmes on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). Single-function: [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response on DHIS2](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/18-1%20Health/Final%20MOHW%20Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20FY%202024-2025%2030.10.25.pdf>), the laboratory system [OpenELIS](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>), the [laboratory information management system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>) of e-Health phase 1, [VigiFlow](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>), the [national human resources health information system (HRHIS)](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>), [DHIS2 for case detection and surveillance of notifiable diseases](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>), the [Laboratory Management and Information System (LMIS)](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>) and the [paper-based inventory management system for medicines in Rodrigues](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).

## EMR

**Governance, planning, finance.** [UNDP Mauritius ran the procurement that chose TrioTree Technologies](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>), its February 2025 case study says. [The project has been allocated MUR 600 million, in phases](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>). [Digital Health Company Ltd, a public company under the health ministry, runs it](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601080233.html>), allAfrica reported in January 2026.

**Tiers in use.** In May 2026 the health minister said the system, which he calls e-Health, [is operational at New Flacq Hospital](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>). It also runs [in several primary health care structures of the same region](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>), L'Express reported. By October 2026 it was [in pilot in about thirty public health facilities of the East and Centre](<https://defimedia.info/dossier-medical-numerique-e-health-plus-de-150000-patients-deja-enregistres>), Le Defi Media reports.

**Where data is digitised.** [E-Health holds one national electronic record per patient](<https://defimedia.info/dossier-medical-numerique-e-health-plus-de-150000-patients-deja-enregistres>), a ministry adviser said in October 2026. Staff at any other connected public facility find it by the patient's identity card. The system [covers the outpatient cycle from registration to follow-up](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>), the minister said in May 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The ministry says [over one million electronic transactions were made from January to September 2026](<https://defimedia.info/dossier-medical-numerique-e-health-plus-de-150000-patients-deja-enregistres>). It plans acceptance of phase 1 by December 2026. In May 2026 the minister [set the national hospital's turn for March 2027](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>).

**Primary clinics doing the digital input.** The primary structures [using the system in May 2026 were in the New Flacq region](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>). In October 2026 [more than 150,000 patients were registered](<https://defimedia.info/dossier-medical-numerique-e-health-plus-de-150000-patients-deja-enregistres>). The ministry's strategic plan [gave the share of all health facilities using EMR as 0 in 2019](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>), against a target of 100% by 2024.

***Also on file***. Trackers: the [Electronic Immunization Registry and AEFI Tracker programmes on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). Single-function: [Integrated Disease Surveillance and Response on DHIS2](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/18-1%20Health/Final%20MOHW%20Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20FY%202024-2025%2030.10.25.pdf>). The laboratory system [OpenELIS](<https://digitalhealthfordevelopment.undp.org/upload/documents/Roadmap_for_Digital_Health_Transformation_in_Mauritius.pdf>). The [laboratory information management system](<https://lexpress.mu/node/557690>) of e-Health phase 1, [VigiFlow](<https://dhis2.org/fr/integration-des-donnees-dhis2-avec-vigiflow-pour-le-signalement-des-cas-daefi-a-maurice/>). The [national human resources health information system (HRHIS)](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_mus_en.pdf>). [DHIS2 for case detection and surveillance of notifiable diseases](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>). The [Laboratory Management and Information System (LMIS)](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>). The [paper-based inventory management system for medicines in Rodrigues](<https://health.govmu.org/health/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Health-Sector-Strategic-Plan-2020-2024.pdf>).
