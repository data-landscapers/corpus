# Seychelles

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The National Bureau of Statistics released Population and Vital Statistics December 2025 (catalogue Population 2026/1) on 31 March 2026, compiled from Civil Status registered births and deaths and covering 2025](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1924-end-of-year-population-2025/download>). Three facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** [The National Bureau of Statistics released Population and Vital Statistics December 2025 on 31 March 2026, reporting 1,293 births and 845 deaths registered in 2025, with figures taken from the Civil Status office](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1924-end-of-year-population-2025/download>). [Seychelles Nation stated on 10 August 2018 that vital statistics compiled by the NBS from Civil Status records are published by the NBS on a bi-annual basis in electronic publications (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years.
