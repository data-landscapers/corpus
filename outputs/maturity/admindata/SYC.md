# Seychelles

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Seychelles Nation reports, from a ministry press release, that the Public Enterprise Monitoring Commission and the National Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum of understanding to share information and streamline data collection from public enterprises](<https://nation.sc/articles/31387/pemc-and-nbs-partner-for-better-service-delivery>). Five facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [The National Bureau of Statistics released Population and Vital Statistics December 2025 on 31 March 2026, reporting 1,293 births and 845 deaths registered in 2025, with figures taken from the Civil Status office](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1924-end-of-year-population-2025/download>). [The Ministry of Health published its Annual Vital Statistics Report 2024 in March 2025 (figures cover 2024), drawing on Department of Civil Status registrations (1,333 live births, 850 deaths, named as the primary source and legal authority) cross-verified with hospital, maternity, mortuary and Statistics Unit records](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Vital-Statistics-Report-2024.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [Seychelles Nation stated on 10 August 2018 that vital statistics compiled by the NBS from Civil Status records are published by the NBS on a bi-annual basis in electronic publications (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>).
