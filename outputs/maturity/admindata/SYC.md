# Seychelles

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In July 2026 the Public Enterprise Monitoring Commission and the National Bureau of Statistics signed a memorandum of understanding](<https://nation.sc/articles/31387/pemc-and-nbs-partner-for-better-service-delivery>). [It is to share information and streamline data collection from public enterprises](<https://nation.sc/articles/31387/pemc-and-nbs-partner-for-better-service-delivery>).

**Sources published from.** [On 31 March 2026 the National Bureau of Statistics released Population and Vital Statistics December 2025, with figures from the Civil Status office](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1924-end-of-year-population-2025/download>). [It reports 1,293 births and 845 deaths registered in 2025](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1924-end-of-year-population-2025/download>). [In March 2025 the Ministry of Health published its Annual Vital Statistics Report 2024, drawing on Department of Civil Status registrations](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Vital-Statistics-Report-2024.pdf>). [It counts 1,333 live births and 850 deaths, cross-verified with hospital, maternity, mortuary and Statistics Unit records](<https://www.health.gov.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Vital-Statistics-Report-2024.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [In August 2018 Seychelles Nation said the NBS publishes vital statistics from Civil Status records on a bi-annual basis](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>). [They appear in electronic publications](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>).
