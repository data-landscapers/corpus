# Guinea

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The INS published the Annuaire statistique 2024 in October 2025, compiled from data collected by forms filled in by sector ministries' structures and from their national databases](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/INS/annuelles/annuaire/Annuaire_Statistique_2024_INS_VF1.pdf>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [The INS Annuaire statistique 2024 publishes tables sourced to ministry statistics bureaux (BSD) for education, health, justice and security, to the CNSS social security fund, and to transport bodies including the customs directorate](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/INS/annuelles/annuaire/Annuaire_Statistique_2024_INS_VF1.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/INS/annuelles/annuaire/Annuaire_Statistique_2024_INS_VF1.pdf>).
