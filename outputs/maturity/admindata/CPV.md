# Cape Verde

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 14-16 April 2025, with WHO support and Portuguese Cooperation funding, Cabo Verde assessed its death certification and cause-of-death notification process and trained health professionals as trainers, to improve the quality of vital statistics](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/cabo-verde/news/cabo-verde-takes-historic-step-mortality-management-support-who-and-portuguese-cooperation>). [The profile states that the collaboration protocol between INE and the civil registry service was updated in 2014 and a cooperation protocol signed with the Directorate of Registries, Notaries and Identification to transmit birth data to INE (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>). Four facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [The Instituto Nacional de Estatistica released its fifth Estatisticas Vitais report on 1 October 2025, covering 2023 (6,760 births, 2,925 deaths, 2,435 marriages), compiled from administrative records of the Registos, Notariado e Identificacao](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>). [INE released the Estatisticas Vitais 2023 report on 1 October 2025, built on civil register administrative records and covering 2016-2023](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>).
