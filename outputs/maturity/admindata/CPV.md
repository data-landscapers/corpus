# Cape Verde

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 14-16 April 2025, with WHO support and Portuguese Cooperation funding, Cabo Verde assessed its death certification and cause-of-death notification process](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/cabo-verde/news/cabo-verde-takes-historic-step-mortality-management-support-who-and-portuguese-cooperation>). [It trained health professionals as trainers, to improve the quality of vital statistics](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/cabo-verde/news/cabo-verde-takes-historic-step-mortality-management-support-who-and-portuguese-cooperation>). [In September 2024 the coordinator of Cabo Verde's ADA/Master Data project visited Statistics Portugal on modernising the use of administrative data for statistics](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n26/24/>).

**Sources published from.** [On 1 October 2025 the Instituto Nacional de Estatistica released its fifth Estatisticas Vitais report, covering 2016-2023](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>). [Its 2023 figures, 6,760 births, 2,925 deaths and 2,435 marriages, come from the administrative records of the Registos, Notariado e Identificacao](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>).
