# Guinea-Bissau

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [UNFPA, the National Institute of Statistics (INE) and the Ministry of Justice and Human Rights ran a five-day descriptive statistics training (4-8 August 2025, Bissau) for 42 professionals, including civil registry technicians from all nine regions, to improve the accuracy of vital statistics](<https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-guinea-bissaus-future-civil-registry-professionals-master-data-skills>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
