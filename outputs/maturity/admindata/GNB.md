# Guinea-Bissau

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [From 4 to 8 August 2025 UNFPA, the National Institute of Statistics (INE) and the justice ministry ran a descriptive statistics training in Bissau](<https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-guinea-bissaus-future-civil-registry-professionals-master-data-skills>). [It trained 42 professionals, civil registry technicians from all nine regions among them](<https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-guinea-bissaus-future-civil-registry-professionals-master-data-skills>). [The aim was to improve the accuracy of vital statistics](<https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-guinea-bissaus-future-civil-registry-professionals-master-data-skills>).

**Sources published from.** [In 2022 INE's yearbook of vital statistics for 2018-2022 drew on the justice ministry's paper civil-registration records of births, marriages and deaths](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf>). [The ministry sends INE the databases each year in Excel](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf>). [Deaths and causes also come from the DHIS2 health information system, and births from the RapidPro platform](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf>).
