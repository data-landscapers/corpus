# Botswana

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Statistics Botswana reports that by March 2024 its review of administrative data had reached twelve sectors](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>). [It gave tablets to four ministries and agencies for digital data capture](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [Statistics Botswana released its Vital Statistics Report 2024 in August 2026](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [It is compiled from the Births and Deaths Registration System and civil registration records, and counts 49,180 births and 14,070 deaths in 2024](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [Its Statistical Year Book 2020/21, dated November 2023, draws its vital statistics from the same registration department](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/The%20Statistical%20Year%20Book%202020-21.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** Civil registration is the one source published twice since October 2023: [in the November 2023 yearbook](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/The%20Statistical%20Year%20Book%202020-21.pdf>) and [in the August 2026 report](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). No other administrative source has been found published twice in that time.
