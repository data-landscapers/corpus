# Mozambique

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In August 2026 the statistics institute INE published its vital statistics report for 2024-2025](<https://dossiersefactos.com/2026/08/28/mocambique-falha-registo-de-9-em-cada-10-bebes-que-nascem/>). It gives the births and deaths registered in 2025, among them 38,032 deaths. [In May 2026 the Ministry of Justice and INE agreed to link the electronic civil registration system to INE's statistical systems](<https://aimnews.org/2026/05/18/mjcr-e-ine-ligam-sistemas-para-modernizar-estatisticas-vitais/>). The link is to share data on births, deaths, marriages and other events. [In 2021 Canada committed US$5.2 million to UNICEF to expand the civil registration system and improve vital statistics](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=CA-3-P001300002>), in a project that ran to the end of 2023.

**Sources published from.** [The report draws its births and deaths from the records of the civil registration system](<https://dossiersefactos.com/2026/08/28/mocambique-falha-registo-de-9-em-cada-10-bebes-que-nascem/>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that the report is published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the 2024-2025 report](<https://dossiersefactos.com/2026/08/28/mocambique-falha-registo-de-9-em-cada-10-bebes-que-nascem/>).
