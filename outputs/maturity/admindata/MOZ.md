# Mozambique

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 18 May 2026 the Ministry of Justice (MJCR) and the National Statistics Institute (INE) signed an MoU linking the electronic civil registration and vital statistics system to INE's statistical systems to share birth, death, marriage and other event data](<https://aimnews.org/2026/05/18/mjcr-e-ine-ligam-sistemas-para-modernizar-estatisticas-vitais/>). [Global Affairs Canada committed US$5,187,000 to UNICEF Mozambique in 2021 (to end 2023) for a project that expands civil registration digital infrastructure and increases vital statistics data quality (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=CA-3-P001300002>).
