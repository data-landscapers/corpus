# Eswatini

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The 2025 CRVS Annual Report (figures for 2025) was launched at the Africa CRVS Day commemoration hosted by the Central Statistical Office](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatinis-crvs-gains-put-every-citizen-on-the-map/>). [The Eswatini Observer reports the Minister of Home Affairs launched the 2024 Annual Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Report, with deaths by cause](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/hiv-leading-killer-among-eswatini-men-diabetes-among-women/>). [The 2021 Eswatini Vital Statistics Report, compiled from civil registration, was presented on 10 August 2022; it covers 2021 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icap-supports-eswatini-to-strengthen-countrys-civil-registration-process-birth-deaths-and-marriage-records/>).

**Vital statistics published.** [The 2025 CRVS Annual Report, covering 2025 and giving birth registration completeness of 61% and death registration of 62%, was launched at the 9th Africa CRVS Day event](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatinis-crvs-gains-put-every-citizen-on-the-map/>). [The Eswatini Observer reports the Minister of Home Affairs launched the 2024 Annual Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Report, with deaths by cause](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/hiv-leading-killer-among-eswatini-men-diabetes-among-women/>).

**Published every year.** [The Eswatini Observer reports the Central Statistical Office has produced the Annual Vital Statistics Report consistently since 2016, the 2024 edition launched in August 2025](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/hiv-leading-killer-among-eswatini-men-diabetes-among-women/>).
