# Eswatini

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The 2025 CRVS Annual Report (figures for 2025) was launched at the Africa CRVS Day commemoration hosted by the Central Statistical Office](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatinis-crvs-gains-put-every-citizen-on-the-map/>). [The 2021 Eswatini Vital Statistics Report, compiled from civil registration, was presented on 10 August 2022; it covers 2021 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icap-supports-eswatini-to-strengthen-countrys-civil-registration-process-birth-deaths-and-marriage-records/>).

**Vital statistics published.** [The 2025 CRVS Annual Report, covering 2025 and giving birth registration completeness of 61% and death registration of 62%, was launched at the 9th Africa CRVS Day event](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatinis-crvs-gains-put-every-citizen-on-the-map/>). [The 2021 Eswatini Vital Statistics Report (figures for 2021, including over 10,000 births registered late) was presented at the CRVS commemoration day launch on 10 August 2022, per ICAP (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://icap.columbia.edu/news-events/icap-supports-eswatini-to-strengthen-countrys-civil-registration-process-birth-deaths-and-marriage-records/>).

**Published every year.** [The 2022 report is stated to be the seventh in a series of annual CRVS reports produced since 2016, released October 2023, and the foreword says producing the annual report is a continuous process](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>).
