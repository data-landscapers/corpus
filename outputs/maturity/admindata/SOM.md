# Somalia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** Somalia publishes statistics drawn from administrative records. [In April 2026 UNDP said the statistics bureau had delivered administrative data reports across key sectors](<https://undpinsomalia.medium.com/promoting-innovation-and-digital-transformation-in-somalia-db2d48bc9643>). It did not name the records used. [The health ministry's bulletin of December 2023 drew on routine DHIS2 data](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The statistics bureau's Statistical Abstract 2024 draws on records from line ministries and agencies](<https://nbs.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Statistical-Abstract-2024.pdf>). It names school enrolment, court cases, passenger movements, finance and livestock. Its figures cover mostly 2018 to 2023. [The health bulletin used reports from 579 health facilities](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The health ministry issues its bulletin every quarter from routine health data](<https://moh.gov.so/so/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Quarterly-Healtrh-Statistics-Bulletin.-final_signed.pdf>). The edition found covers July to September 2023. No other series published twice since October 2023 has been found.
