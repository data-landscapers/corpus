# Cameroon

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In August 2024 the national statistics institute published the North-West regional statistical yearbook, 2023 edition](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-du-nord-ouest-edition-2023/>). [It says its data come mainly from local administrative sources and from national surveys](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-du-nord-ouest-edition-2023/>). [The yearbook serves the region and its communes, and feeds the national statistical yearbook](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-du-nord-ouest-edition-2023/>). [In August 2023 the institute published a first report on civil-registration statistics for 2018-2022, with the civil registration bureau and the health ministry](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>). [Many of its key tables are partly or wholly empty for lack of data](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The regional yearbook does not name the administrative sources it draws on](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-du-nord-ouest-edition-2023/>). [The civil-registration report of August 2023 draws on civil-registration centre records for 2018](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>). [It adds health-information data for 2018 to 2022](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The institute calls the regional yearbook an annual compilation](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-du-nord-ouest-edition-2023/>). No source found dates a second edition since November 2023.
