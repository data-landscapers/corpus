# Cameroon

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [INS Cameroon published the Adamaoua regional statistical yearbook, 2025 edition, on 7 August 2026, compiling statistics from sectoral administrations, local authorities and enterprises across 22 chapters; its regional agencies produce one every year](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-de-ladamaoua-edition-2025/>). [The INS, with BUNEC and the Ministry of Public Health, published the first edition of the report on civil-registration statistics in August 2023, covering 2018-2022; many key tables are left partly or wholly empty for lack of data (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>). Three facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [The INS report on civil-registration statistics 2018-2022, published August 2023 (the dates 2018-2022 are the years its figures cover), draws on civil-registration centre records for 2018 and on health-information (DHIS2) data for 2018-2022, alongside the 2018 demographic and health survey (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/statistique/annuaire-statistique-de-la-region-de-ladamaoua-edition-2025/>).
