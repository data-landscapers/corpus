# Mauritius

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In October 2023 two Statistics Finland experts reviewed the data sources used to update the Statistical Business Register](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>). [They worked under the EU-funded PAS2 project](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>). [A study tour in Oslo on 21-24 May 2024 covered using administrative data and quality control of the business register](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [On 28 August 2026 Statistics Mauritius published Population and Vital Statistics for January-June 2026, drawn from the Civil Status Division's computerised system](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>). [It reports 6,386 live births and 6,333 deaths registered in those six months](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 Statistics Mauritius published half-year vital statistics from the Civil Status Division's system](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>).
