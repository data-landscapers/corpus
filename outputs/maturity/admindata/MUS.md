# Mauritius

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In October 2023 two Statistics Finland experts reviewed the data sources used to update the Statistical Business Register](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>). [They worked under the EU-funded PAS2 project](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>). [A study tour in Oslo on 21-24 May 2024 covered using administrative data and quality control of the business register](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_US/Annual_Report/Annual_Report_yr23-yr24_Statistics_Mauritius_031224.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [On 28 August 2026 Statistics Mauritius published Population and Vital Statistics for January-June 2026, drawn from the Civil Status Division's computerised system](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>). [It reports 6,386 live births and 6,333 deaths registered in those six months](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>). [In March 2026 it published Population and Vital Statistics for 2025, counting 13,250 live births and 12,685 deaths registered that year](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Pages/Statistics/ESI/Population/Pop_Vital_Yr25.aspx>).

**Published regularly.** [Statistics Mauritius published annual vital statistics of registered events for 2023 in March 2024](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Pages/Statistics/ESI/Population/Pop_Vital_Yr23.aspx>). [It published the 2025 edition in March 2026](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Pages/Statistics/ESI/Population/Pop_Vital_Yr25.aspx>), [and a half-year issue for January to June 2026 in August 2026](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Fisheries Information System (FIS)](<https://lexpress.mu/node/561717>); [computerised system at the Civil Status Division](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1949/Pop_Vital_Midyr26_280826.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found shows statistics from three administrative sources, each published twice since November 2023.
