**Sources published from.** [The education ministry's statistical yearbook for 2024/25, drawn from its central database covering all elements of the education process, reports schools, classes, pupils, exam results and teachers](<https://emis.gov.eg/statistics/books/2024-2025/main_book2024.html>).

**Published regularly.** [Egypt's presentation states CAPMAS, the national statistics office, directly updates the monthly and annual reports of births and deaths from the central civil registration database](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). [The presentation states CAPMAS produces annual as well as monthly reports of births and deaths](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).
