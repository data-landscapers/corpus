# Egypt

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Egypt's Financial Regulatory Authority and CAPMAS signed their first data-sharing protocol on 2026-06-23](<https://en.amwalalghad.com/egypts-fra-capmas-sign-first-data-sharing-pact-on-insurance-brokerage-sectors/>). Under it CAPMAS receives data on insurance, reinsurance and securities brokerage firms for official statistics. No other statistics drawn from the country's administrative records have been found.

**Sources published from.** [The education ministry's statistical yearbook for 2024/25 reports schools, classes, pupils, exam results and teachers](<https://emis.gov.eg/statistics/books/2024-2025/main_book2024.html>). It draws on the ministry's central database, which covers all elements of the education process.

**Published regularly.** [Egypt's presentation states CAPMAS, the national statistics office, directly updates the monthly and annual reports of births and deaths](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). It does so from the central civil registration database. [The presentation states CAPMAS produces annual as well as monthly reports of births and deaths](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

***Also on file***. The [central births and deaths database (4,571 health offices)](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>) and the [Egyptian Birth and Death Registration System (EBDRS)](<http://www.au.int/sites/default/files/newsevents/workingdocuments/37474-wd-crvs-v-the_electronic_birth_and_death_registration_system_in_egypt.pdf>) are on file. So are the [Digital Labour Platform (منصة العمل الرقمية)](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/7/28/3024561/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%82>) and a [proposed national labour-market data and systems platform](<https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/7/28/3024561/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%AB-%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%82>). The [Statistical Yearbook 2024 (115th edition)](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5084208.aspx>) is also on file.

***Not found***. No source found shows statistics from three administrative sources, each published twice since October 2023.
