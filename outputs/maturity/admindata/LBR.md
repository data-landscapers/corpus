# Liberia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [LISGIS published the 2023 Socioeconomic Statistical Bulletin (Vol. 4 No. 01, December 2023), compiled mainly from ministry, agency and commission administrative data (education, health, security, trade, transport and others), with most data covering 2016-2022](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/statisticalbulletines/socialstatisticsBulletin2023.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** No source held reports vital statistics from civil registration published since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [LISGIS published the 2023 Socioeconomic Statistical Bulletin (Vol. 4 No. 01, December 2023), compiled mainly from ministry, agency and commission administrative data (education, health, security, trade, transport and others), with most data covering 2016-2022](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/statisticalbulletines/socialstatisticsBulletin2023.pdf>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/uploads/statisticalbulletines/socialstatisticsBulletin2023.pdf>).
