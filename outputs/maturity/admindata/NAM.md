# Namibia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Namibia Statistics Agency told the SADC workshop that an MoU with the labour ministry (MLIREC) provides for a steering committee and technical working group on data sharing, but no law or MoU yet covers access to immigration records and no administrative statistics are published from them](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/meetings/2025/migration-wk-sadc-20250317/Country%20-%20Namibia%20-%20SADC%20presentations_rev_17%20March%202025_updated.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [Its national institutional maternal and stillbirth rates are consistent with the 2023 vital statistics report](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). [The Namibia Statistics Agency published the Namibia Vital Statistics Report 2018-2021 in April 2024 (figures cover births, deaths and marriages registered 2018-2021), stating its vital events are administrative data from the Civil Registration System, extracted from the National Population Registration System held by the Office of the Prime Minister up to 17 February 2022](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The first Vital Statistics Report from civil registration data was published in 2020 (events of 2016 and 2017) and this second edition appeared in April 2024 (events of 2018-2021), made with the Ministries of Home Affairs and of Health](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>).
