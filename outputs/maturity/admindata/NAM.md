# Namibia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** No source held reports statistics from administrative records published, or work towards them, since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [Its national institutional maternal and stillbirth rates are consistent with the 2023 vital statistics report](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** [Its national institutional maternal and stillbirth rates are consistent with the 2023 vital statistics report](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). The source names the report in passing and gives no release date; the report itself was not read for this study.

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).
