# Lesotho

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Bureau of Statistics lists vital statistics reports of births and deaths for 2020, 2021 and 2022](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). [In July 2024 the Bureau said it had held a workshop on modernising administrative data for official statistics](<https://archive.uneca.org/archive2/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/acs/sixth-africa-gender-statistics-forum/7._nsds-ii_funding_strategy_of_lesotho.pdf>). [The Economic Commission for Africa financed and supported it](<https://archive.uneca.org/archive2/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/acs/sixth-africa-gender-statistics-forum/7._nsds-ii_funding_strategy_of_lesotho.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists a Vital Statistics Report 2022 and Vital Statistics (Births & Deaths) 2021 and 2020](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). [The Bureau of Statistics published the 2022 Vital Statistics Report (Statistical Report No. 04, 2024)](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/lesotho-2022-vital-statistics-report.pdf>). It covers births, deaths, marriages and divorces registered in 2022. It was disseminated on the BOS website and MOHA dashboard. Its release date is given only as 2024.

**Published regularly.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists vital statistics reports of births and deaths for 2020, 2021 and 2022](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).
