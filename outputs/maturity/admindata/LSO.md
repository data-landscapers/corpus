# Lesotho

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho publications page lists downloadable Crime Statistics 2022, Education Statistical Report 2023 and Annual Foreign Trade Statistics Report 2024](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). [The Bureau of Statistics published Statistical Report No. 04, the 2022 Vital Statistics Report (figures cover 2022), compiled from civil registration records collected by Ministry of Home Affairs staff; release date given only as 2024](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/lesotho-2022-vital-statistics-report.pdf>). Five facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists a Vital Statistics Report 2022 and Vital Statistics (Births & Deaths) 2021 and 2020](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). [The Bureau of Statistics published the 2022 Vital Statistics Report (Statistical Report No. 04, 2024) on births, deaths, marriages and divorces registered in 2022, disseminated on the BOS website and MOHA dashboard; release date given only as 2024](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/lesotho-2022-vital-statistics-report.pdf>).

**Published every year.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists vital statistics reports of births and deaths for 2020, 2021 and 2022](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).
