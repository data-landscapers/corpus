# Zimbabwe

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [ZIMSTAT's Transport and Infrastructure page says its quarterly statistics are compiled from administrative data obtained from relevant institutions, and lists Transport Statistics Reports for 2023 Q3 and Q4, 2024 Q4, and 2025 Q1, Q2 and Q4](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [ZIMSTAT's NSDS III reports that statistical products were produced from administrative records and civil and vital registration during the NSDS II period (2016-2020); the document was published 2024-01-25 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/NSS/National_stategy_for_the_development_of_statistics_Final_25012024.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [ZIMSTAT's Transport and Infrastructure page says its quarterly statistics are compiled from administrative data obtained from relevant institutions, covering air, inland water, rail and road transport and also energy, ICT and water infrastructure](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [ZIMSTAT published the Vital Statistics Report 2023-2024 on registered births and deaths (including causes of death) for 2023 and 2024, compiled by the Civil Registry Department and processed by ZIMSTAT](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [ZIMSTAT's Transport and Infrastructure page says its quarterly statistics are compiled from administrative data obtained from relevant institutions; Transport Statistics Reports are listed for 2023 Q3, 2023 Q4, 2024 Q4, 2025 Q1, 2025 Q2 and 2025 Q4](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>).
