# Zimbabwe

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [ZIMSTAT's NSDS III reports that statistical products were produced from administrative records and civil and vital registration during the NSDS II period (2016-2020); the document was published 2024-01-25 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/NSS/National_stategy_for_the_development_of_statistics_Final_25012024.pdf>). Four facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [ZIMSTAT announced it had published the Vital Statistics Report 2023-2024, compiled from Civil Registry Department registrations, covering registered births, deaths, marriages and divorces for 2023 and 2024](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>). [ZIMSTAT published the Vital Statistics Report 2023-2024 on registered births and deaths (including causes of death) for 2023 and 2024, compiled by the Civil Registry Department and processed by ZIMSTAT](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>). [NSDS III states that statistical products of the NSDS II period (2016-2020) were drawn from civil and vital registration, among administrative records (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/NSS/National_stategy_for_the_development_of_statistics_Final_25012024.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>).
