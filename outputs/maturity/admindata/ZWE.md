# Zimbabwe

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [ZIMSTAT's quarterly transport and infrastructure statistics are compiled from administrative data obtained from relevant institutions](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [Transport Statistics Reports are listed for 2023 Q3 and Q4, 2024 Q4, and 2025 Q1, Q2 and Q4](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [In the 2016-2020 period ZIMSTAT produced statistical products from administrative records and civil and vital registration](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/NSS/National_stategy_for_the_development_of_statistics_Final_25012024.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [ZIMSTAT's quarterly statistics are compiled from administrative data on air, inland water, rail and road transport and energy, ICT and water infrastructure](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [In June 2026 ZIMSTAT published the Vital Statistics Report 2023-2024, on registered births and deaths with causes of death](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>). [The Civil Registry Department compiled it and ZIMSTAT processed it](<https://www.zimstat.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/demography/vital/2023_2024_Vital_Statistics_Report.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [ZIMSTAT's quarterly transport statistics are compiled from administrative data](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>). [Transport Statistics Reports are listed for 2023 Q3, 2023 Q4, 2024 Q4, 2025 Q1, 2025 Q2 and 2025 Q4](<https://zimstat.co.zw/transport-and-infrastructure/>).
