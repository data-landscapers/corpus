# Cote d'Ivoire

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Ministry of the Interior and Security presented the civil status yearbook for 2024 on 30 June 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635039/economie/annuaire-statistique-detat-civil-de-lannee-2024-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-dans-les-delais-en-nette-regression>). An analysis report was presented with it. It covers births, adoptions, marriages, divorces and deaths registered that year. [On 31 July 2026 the ministry's statistics directorate presented the yearbook for 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-taux-denregistrement-annuel-des-naissances-progresse-a-576-en-2025-contre-547-en-2022-officiel/>). It did so with the statistics institute ANSTAT and UNICEF.

**Sources published from.** [The 2024 yearbook is compiled from civil registration records](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635039/economie/annuaire-statistique-detat-civil-de-lannee-2024-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-dans-les-delais-en-nette-regression>). [The 2025 edition is built from the birth, adoption, marriage, divorce and death records collected in civil status offices](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-taux-denregistrement-annuel-des-naissances-progresse-a-576-en-2025-contre-547-en-2022-officiel/>).

**Published regularly.** [The yearbook has appeared twice in thirteen months](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-taux-denregistrement-annuel-des-naissances-progresse-a-576-en-2025-contre-547-en-2022-officiel/>), for 2024 and for 2025. Each edition counts the events registered in the year before. No source found states that it is published every year.
