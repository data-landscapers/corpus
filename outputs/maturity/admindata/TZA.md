# Tanzania

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [NBS and OCGS Zanzibar published the 2024 Updated Statistical Business Register report, compiled from Tanzania Revenue Authority and Zanzibar Revenue Authority tax records plus establishment surveys, giving 257,405 establishments in 2024 against 219,371 in 2023 (+17.3%); figures cover 2024, release date taken from the source's published date as the report itself is dated September 2025](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1761505497-2024%20Updated%20Statistical%20Business%20Register%20using%20Administrative%20Data,%20Tanzania.pdf>).
