# Tanzania

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [NBS and the Zanzibar Office of the Chief Government Statistician, under TSMP II (2022/23-2026/27), developed the first manual for integrating administrative data into official statistics, after assessing how sector bodies produce administrative data](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1719651054-Manual%20for%20strengthening%20and%20integrating%20administrative%20data%20into%20National%20-%202024.pdf>). Four facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [NBS and OCGS Zanzibar published the 2024 Updated Statistical Business Register report, compiled from Tanzania Revenue Authority and Zanzibar Revenue Authority tax records plus establishment surveys, giving 257,405 establishments in 2024 against 219,371 in 2023 (+17.3%); figures cover 2024, release date taken from the source's published date as the report itself is dated September 2025](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1761505497-2024%20Updated%20Statistical%20Business%20Register%20using%20Administrative%20Data,%20Tanzania.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The Ministry of Health states that, since 2009, its Monitoring and Evaluation Unit has consistently published the Annual Health Statistical Tables and Figures report each year for the preceding calendar year; this edition was published September 2025](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Crime Statistics Management Information System (CSMIS)](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1734514885-TSMP_II_Strategy.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found shows statistics from three administrative sources, each published twice since October 2023.
