# Tanzania

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [NBS and the Zanzibar Office of the Chief Government Statistician developed the first manual for integrating administrative data into official statistics](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1719651054-Manual%20for%20strengthening%20and%20integrating%20administrative%20data%20into%20National%20-%202024.pdf>). They did so under TSMP II (2022/23-2026/27), after assessing how sector bodies produce administrative data.

**Sources published from.** [NBS and OCGS Zanzibar published the 2024 Updated Statistical Business Register report](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1761505497-2024%20Updated%20Statistical%20Business%20Register%20using%20Administrative%20Data,%20Tanzania.pdf>). It is compiled from Tanzania Revenue Authority and Zanzibar Revenue Authority tax records plus establishment surveys. It gives 257,405 establishments in 2024 against 219,371 in 2023 (+17.3%). The figures cover 2024. The release date is taken from the source's published date, as the report itself is dated September 2025.

**Published regularly.** [The Ministry of Health states that, since 2009, its Monitoring and Evaluation Unit has consistently published the Annual Health Statistical Tables and Figures report each year](<https://www.moh.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/698/4e8/b7b/6984e8b7b067c606076637.pdf>). Each report covers the preceding calendar year. This edition was published in September 2025.
