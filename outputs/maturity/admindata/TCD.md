# Chad

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Ministry of National Education's planning, analysis and school map directorate held a validation workshop on 7 October 2025 in N'Djamena for the 2024-2025 school statistical yearbook](<https://www.ndjamenaactu.com/2025/10/07/education-la-statistique-scolaire-2024-2025-en-validation/>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more. Statistical products held that do not say their figures are drawn from administrative records, rather than estimates, surveys or censuses, are not counted.
