[The Ministry of National Education's planning, analysis and school map directorate held a validation workshop on 7 October 2025 in N'Djamena for the 2024-2025 school statistical yearbook](<https://www.ndjamenaactu.com/2025/10/07/education-la-statistique-scolaire-2024-2025-en-validation/>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
