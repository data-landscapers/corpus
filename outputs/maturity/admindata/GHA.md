# Ghana

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 22 July 2026 the Ghana Statistical Service met the Education Ministry, Ghana Education Service and National Identification Authority on the Pilot Register-Based Education Statistics Project, and the Education Minister agreed to chair its steering committee](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1512867/ghana-to-replace-periodic-education-surveys-with.html>). [The Ghana Statistical Service signed a memorandum of understanding with 25 ministries, departments and agencies, including the Births and Deaths Registry, Health Service and Police Service, to coordinate and share administrative data for official statistics; about 17 MDAs had draft data-sharing policies](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/03/gss-25-mdas-sign-data-sharing-agreements/>). By October 2026 the Government Statistician said [the Service held memoranda with 26 institutions](<https://thevaultznews.com/2026/10/23/ghana-pledges-to-link-official-statistics-with-maps/>), and committed to turn them into data flowing daily.

**Sources published from.** [The Births and Deaths Registry published its 2022 Statistical Report: Death Registration, which presents statistics on deaths registered in 2022 drawn from all 261 districts; the preface calls it the inaugural statistical report by the Registry](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>). [The Births and Deaths Registry published its 2022 Statistical Report Birth Registration, with sections on birth registration process, data compilation and data source and figures on registered births in 2022 by region, sex, place of birth and mother's age](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-BIRTH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>).
