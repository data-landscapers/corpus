# Ghana

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 22 July 2026 the Ghana Statistical Service met the Education Ministry, Ghana Education Service and National Identification Authority on a pilot register-based education statistics project](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1512867/ghana-to-replace-periodic-education-surveys-with.html>). [The Education Minister agreed to chair its steering committee](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1512867/ghana-to-replace-periodic-education-surveys-with.html>). [In March 2026 the Service signed a memorandum with 25 ministries, departments and agencies to coordinate and share administrative data for official statistics](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/03/gss-25-mdas-sign-data-sharing-agreements/>). [They include the Births and Deaths Registry, Health Service and Police Service; about 17 had draft data-sharing policies](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/03/gss-25-mdas-sign-data-sharing-agreements/>). By October 2026 the Government Statistician said [the Service held memoranda with 26 institutions](<https://thevaultznews.com/2026/10/23/ghana-pledges-to-link-official-statistics-with-maps/>), and committed to turn them into data flowing daily.

**Sources published from.** [In 2024 the Births and Deaths Registry published its 2022 Statistical Report: Death Registration, on deaths registered in all 261 districts](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>). [The preface calls it the Registry's inaugural statistical report](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>). [Its 2022 Statistical Report Birth Registration gives registered births in 2022 by region, sex, place of birth and mother's age](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-BIRTH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>).
