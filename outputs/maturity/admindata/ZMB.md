# Zambia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [ZamStats says administrative data, the information people give government when they use public services, is used to produce statistics about the population](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/faqs/>). Its question-and-answer page was captured in November 2023. The agency was set up under the Statistics Act No. 13 of 2018 to compile and publish official statistics.

**Sources published from.** [The page gives annual births and deaths statistics as the example](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/faqs/>), drawn from civil registration. [Most ZamStats products are free on its website and data portal](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/faqs/>), the page says, and an archive of past publications is kept there.

**Published regularly.** No source found dates two editions of statistics from one administrative source since November 2023. The nearest fact is [the agency's statement that births and deaths statistics are annual](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/faqs/>).
