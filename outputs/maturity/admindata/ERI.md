# Eritrea

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In 2022 WHO supported the Ministry of Health to set up a National Health Observatory drawing on population surveys, CRVS and DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>). [Its indicators were compiled and verified, but the data were to be uploaded only once the ministry approved and launched it](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>). [In January 2007 a mid-term review of the NSO and Statistics Norway project found foreign trade statistics compiled for 2003 and 2004 but not yet published](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>). [NSO publication was largely missing](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Education's 'Eritrea: Basic Education Statistics 2023/24' (Asmara, December 2024) draws on annual school censuses run by Zoba education offices](<https://www.ecss-online.com/2013/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Basic-Education-Statistics-2023-24.pdf>). [They are normally 100% collected](<https://www.ecss-online.com/2013/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Basic-Education-Statistics-2023-24.pdf>). [In 2022 the Ministry of Health's HMIS unit produced an Annual Health Service Activity Report from routine health and disease records reported through DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>). [DHIS2 was functional in 44% of districts](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The December 2024 abstract is the Ministry of Education's official annual education publication, the 26th report of its Department of Research and Human Resource Development](<https://www.ecss-online.com/2013/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Basic-Education-Statistics-2023-24.pdf>). [In 2022 the HMIS unit produced the Annual Health Service Activity Report every year](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>).
