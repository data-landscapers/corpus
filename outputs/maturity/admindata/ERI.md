# Eritrea

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [WHO supported the Ministry of Health to set up a National Health Observatory drawing on population surveys, CRVS and DHIS2; indicators were compiled and verified but the data were to be uploaded only once the ministry approved and launched it (2022) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>). [The mid-term review of the NSO and Statistics Norway project states foreign trade statistics were compiled for 2003 and 2004, would soon cover 2005 and were ripe for publication but not yet published, with NSO publication largely missing (2007, before the three-year window)](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Education's 'Eritrea: Basic Education Statistics 2023/24' (Asmara, December 2024) draws on annual school censuses run by Zoba education offices through standard questionnaires 7, 8 and 9, reported as normally 100% collected](<https://www.ecss-online.com/2013/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Basic-Education-Statistics-2023-24.pdf>). [The Ministry of Health's HMIS unit produces an Annual Health Service Activity Report with statistics from routine health and disease records (health services, communicable diseases, mother and child data) reported through DHIS2, which is functional in 44% of districts (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The abstract calls itself the official annual education publication of the Ministry of Education and the 26th report produced by its Department of Research and Human Resource Development](<https://www.ecss-online.com/2013/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Basic-Education-Statistics-2023-24.pdf>). [The HMIS unit produces the Annual Health Service Activity Report annually (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-02/WHO%20Annual%20Report%20-%202022.pdf>).
