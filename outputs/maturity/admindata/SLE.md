# Sierra Leone

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 10-11 September 2026 NCRA held a stakeholder validation meeting on its draft 2025 Vital Statistics Report, compiled from civil registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-validation-sierra-leones-statistics/>). [Fertility and mortality data were the focus; the report was not yet final](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-validation-sierra-leones-statistics/>). [On 8 April 2026 NaCSA and Statistics Sierra Leone signed an amended MoU on data collaboration for the PSSNYE project](<https://www.nacsa.gov.sl/post/nacsa-ancollaborationd-statistics-sierra-leone-sign-amended-mou-to-strengthen-data-for-pssnye-proje>). [It is to expand the SPRINT social protection registry to national coverage and support targeting, monitoring and impact evaluation](<https://www.nacsa.gov.sl/post/nacsa-ancollaborationd-statistics-sierra-leone-sign-amended-mou-to-strengthen-data-for-pssnye-proje>).

**Sources published from.** [The NCRA report compiles births, deaths, marriages, divorces and adoptions registered under the 2016 National Civil Registration Act](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/ncra-releases-provisional-statistics/>). [The 2024 provisional vital events report draws on events registered by the National Civil Registration Authority, with changes stated against 2023](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/ncra-releases-2024-provisional-report-on-vital-statistics/>). [The Countdown to 2030 synthesis report, by Statistics Sierra Leone, the Ministry of Health and APHRC, publishes RMNCAH indicators for 2019-2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [They are computed from routinely reported health facility data in DHIS2](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [National reporting completeness was 93-99 percent](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest fact: [at the end of 2025 the NCRA published its provisional annual report on vital events](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/ncra-releases-provisional-statistics/>).
