# Kenya

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [KNBS published the Economic Survey 2025 (figures for 2024), whose statistics draw on administrative data collected by ministries, departments, agencies and county governments](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Economic-Survey.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** No source held reports vital statistics from civil registration published since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [KNBS published the Economic Survey 2025 (figures for 2024), whose statistics draw on administrative data collected by ministries, departments, agencies and county governments](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Economic-Survey.pdf>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Economic-Survey.pdf>).
