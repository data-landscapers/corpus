# Kenya

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS) published the Economic Survey 2025, with figures for 2024](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Economic-Survey.pdf>). Its statistics draw on administrative data collected by ministries, departments, agencies and county governments. No other statistics drawn from the country's administrative records have been found.

**Sources published from.** [The Civil Registration Services' Kenya Vital Statistics Report 2023 states that it presents 2023 vital statistics extracted from the civil registration system](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2023-Kenya-Vital-Statistics-Report.pdf>). It covers births, deaths and causes of death registered in health facilities and the community.

**Published regularly.** [The report's foreword says the 2023 edition is the eleventh in a series of annual CRVS reports produced since 2010](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2023-Kenya-Vital-Statistics-Report.pdf>). It says production of an annual vital statistics report is a continuous process.
