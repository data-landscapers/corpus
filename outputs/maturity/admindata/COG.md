# Congo

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 10 November 2025 the Institut national de la statistique, customs, the BEAC, the national oil company, the ports and the single window agreed at a workshop to share their databases for foreign trade statistics](<https://congo-b.com/statistiques-export-lins-muscle-ses-outils/>). [They adopted a draft decree on data-transmission obligations](<https://congo-b.com/statistiques-export-lins-muscle-ses-outils/>). [A pilot collection into a data warehouse at the Institute is planned from January](<https://congo-b.com/statistiques-export-lins-muscle-ses-outils/>).
