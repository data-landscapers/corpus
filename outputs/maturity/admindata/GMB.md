# Gambia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Since 2014 the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education has published education data in its Statistical Yearbook](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>), DHIS2 for Education reported in 2026. The ministry has also opened a public Education Data Centre. It combines school census, attendance and learner records in the months between yearbooks. Researchers, journalists and head teachers can search it directly.

**Sources published from.** [The yearbook is compiled from questionnaires collected at schools each November](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>) and is published the following May. The portal draws on the annual school census and attendance reporting.

**Published regularly.** [The yearbook appears every year](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>). The minister, Habibatou Drammeh, wrote the preface to the 2026 edition. The Permanent Secretary called it the cornerstone of basic education statistics.

***Also on file***. [eCRVS](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found shows statistics from health, civil registration or any other administrative source.
