# Gambia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Since 2014 the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education has published education data in its Statistical Yearbook](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>), DHIS2 for Education reported in 2026. The ministry has also opened a public Education Data Centre. It combines school census, attendance and learner records in the months between yearbooks. Researchers, journalists and head teachers can search it directly.

**Sources published from.** [The yearbook is compiled from questionnaires collected at schools each November](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>) and is published the following May. The portal draws on the annual school census and attendance reporting. [In June 2026 the Gambia Bureau of Statistics published its Transport Statistics 2025 Summary Report, compiled from administrative data of the civil aviation, ports, ferry and police services](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/639-transport-statistics-summary-report-2025>).

**Published regularly.** [The yearbook appears every year](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>). The minister, Habibatou Drammeh, wrote the preface to the 2026 edition. The Permanent Secretary called it the cornerstone of basic education statistics. [The report gives five-year series for 2021 to 2025, including police-recorded road accident cases, 1,001 in 2025](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/639-transport-statistics-summary-report-2025>).
