# Gambia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics downloads page lists The 2024 Gambia Statistical Abstract, a Transport Statistics Summary Report 2025 and International Merchandise Trade Statistics 2015 to 2025 for download](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
