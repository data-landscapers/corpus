# Uganda

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The CRVS Technical Working Group, led by NIRA with UBOS present, met on 30 April 2026 with development of the 2025 Vital Statistics Report and data sharing agreements between UBOS and CRVS data-generating institutions among its focus areas](<https://uniph.go.ug/civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-technical-crvs-technical-working-group-convenes-to-advance-vital-statistics-and-data-integration/>). [UBOS reports online training of 750 district officials on compiling local government community and administrative data in FY2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>). [UBOS reports in its FY2025/26 budget performance (to end December 2025) that it disseminated the 2024 UBOS Annual Statistical Abstract](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Uganda Police Force published its Annual Crime Report 2025 on 2026-04-02 (figures cover 2025), reporting 196,405 cases reported to Police, 115,301 submitted to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions, 79,291 taken to court and 24,899 cases with convictions](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>). [UBOS reports that it finalized and disseminated the Vital Statistics Report 2024 (and a CRVS report) during FY2025/26 to end December 2025](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>).
