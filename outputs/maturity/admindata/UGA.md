# Uganda

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 30 April 2026 the CRVS Technical Working Group, led by NIRA with UBOS present, met on the 2025 Vital Statistics Report](<https://uniph.go.ug/civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-technical-crvs-technical-working-group-convenes-to-advance-vital-statistics-and-data-integration/>). [Data sharing agreements between UBOS and CRVS data-generating institutions were also among its focus areas](<https://uniph.go.ug/civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-technical-crvs-technical-working-group-convenes-to-advance-vital-statistics-and-data-integration/>). [UBOS reports online training of 750 district officials on compiling local government community and administrative data in FY2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>). [On 12-14 December 2024 NIRA and UBOS held a workshop to review the first draft of the first annual Vital Statistics report](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRVS-Quartely-report-Data-for-Health-Initiative-Oct-Dec-2024-for-AFENET.pdf>). [Completion was targeted for January 2025](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRVS-Quartely-report-Data-for-Health-Initiative-Oct-Dec-2024-for-AFENET.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [On 2 April 2026 the Uganda Police Force published its Annual Crime Report 2025](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>). [It reports 196,405 cases reported to Police and 115,301 submitted to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>). [Of these, 79,291 were taken to court and 24,899 ended in convictions](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>).
