# Comoros

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [A preliminary mission looked at improving the production of foreign and domestic trade statistics from information exchanged between Comoros customs services, and lists the 2023 health statistical yearbook as still to be drafted and validated; the report was posted in June 2024](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-06/comoros_sids.pdf>). Five facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [WHO Comoros reports having supported the Ministry of Health's Direction de l'Information et des Statistiques Sanitaires in producing the health statistical yearbooks for 2021 and 2022, which it describes as health-sector information; the report was published 2025-07-10 and the date the yearbooks came out is not given](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>). [The Joint SDG Fund annual report for 2023 says the programme supported the printing and dissemination of the health statistics yearbook 2021 and 2022 and of the quarterly statistical health information bulletins](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-06/comoros_sids.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [WHO Comoros reports having supported the first quarterly health information bulletins (bulletins trimestriels d'information sanitaire), a quarterly series from the health information system](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Rapport%20annuel%20_%20OMS%20Comores%20_%20PUBLICATION%2010%2007%202025.pdf>).
