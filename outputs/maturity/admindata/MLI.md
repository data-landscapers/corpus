# Mali

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [UNESCO and IIPE, funding and supporting the education ministry's planning and statistics unit (CPS-SE), held a workshop at Fana on 11-15 March 2026 to analyse national SIGE school-health data for 2023-2024 and 2024-2025, after building the unit's capacity since 2021 to add routine school-health indicators to the SIGE](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>). [The World Bank MIQRA project report says publication of Mali's education statistical yearbooks for 2023/24 and 2024/25 was delayed and is now being supported by the project, with the latest indicator data still from 2022/23](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925133063670/pdf/P164032-3dbd8877-99ba-4d00-8051-52eac68d5d74.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The health and social sector planning unit (CPS/SSDSPF) published the Annuaire 2023 of the SNISS, compiled from the 2023 SISo, SIH and SLIS yearbooks and validated documents of the sector's structures, with tables on CANAM insurance, pensions and mutuelle coverage](<https://bibliostat.instat.ml/greenstone3/library/collection/sant/document/HASH01915e0cf9b388acc96f8e9e?ed=1>).

**Published regularly.** [The CPS health sector plan states the health ministry has regularly published the Annuaire statistique du SNIS since 2009, built from the SLIS, hospital and surveillance data, with a hospital yearbook since 2003 (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/PS-SNISS_2020-2024_VF_11fev2021.pdf>).
