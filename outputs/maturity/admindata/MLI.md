# Mali

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [INSTAT's publications page of October 2026 lists its quarterly foreign trade bulletins](<https://www.instat-mali.org/fr/publications/statistiques-du-commerce-exterieur>) up to the first quarter of 2026. It also lists trade yearbooks for 2017 to 2025. [The World Bank reported in December 2025 that the education yearbooks for 2023/24 and 2024/25 were delayed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925133063670/pdf/P164032-3dbd8877-99ba-4d00-8051-52eac68d5d74.pdf>). A World Bank project is supporting their publication.

**Sources published from.** [INSTAT values the goods in its trade bulletin from customs declarations](<https://www.instat-mali.org/fr/publications/statistiques-du-commerce-exterieur>). [The health and social sector planning unit published the SNISS yearbook for 2023](<https://bibliostat.instat.ml/greenstone3/library/collection/sant/document/HASH01915e0cf9b388acc96f8e9e?ed=1>), compiled from the social, hospital and local health information systems. It carries tables on health insurance, pensions and mutual cover.

**Published regularly.** [INSTAT's trade bulletins appear every quarter and its trade yearbook every year](<https://www.instat-mali.org/fr/publications/statistiques-du-commerce-exterieur>), with editions for 2023, 2024 and 2025.
