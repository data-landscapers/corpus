# Mali

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The KHI-DEUX bulletin lists the Annuaire statistique de l'environnement et des changements climatiques, edition 2025, among the national statistical system's publications of the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/353/khideux53_pub.pdf>). [INSTAT lists the Annuaire du commerce international des marchandises 2022 and 2023 (exports and imports by chapter and country) among the national statistical system's publications of the fourth quarter of 2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>). [The Observatoire du Transport's steering committee examined and validated the transport statistical yearbooks 2023 and 2024 on 29 December 2025, and they are listed among the publications of the fourth quarter of 2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>).

**Vital statistics published.** No source held reports vital statistics from civil registration published since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [The KHI-DEUX bulletin lists the Annuaire statistique de l'environnement et des changements climatiques, edition 2025, among the national statistical system's publications of the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/353/khideux53_pub.pdf>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/353/khideux53_pub.pdf>).
