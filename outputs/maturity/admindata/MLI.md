# Mali

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The KHI-DEUX bulletin lists the Annuaire statistique de l'environnement et des changements climatiques, edition 2025, among the national statistical system's publications of the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/353/khideux53_pub.pdf>). [UNESCO and IIPE, funding and supporting the education ministry's planning and statistics unit (CPS-SE), held a workshop at Fana on 11-15 March 2026 to analyse national SIGE school-health data for 2023-2024 and 2024-2025, after building the unit's capacity since 2021 to add routine school-health indicators to the SIGE](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>).

**Sources published from.** [INSTAT lists the Annuaire du commerce international des marchandises 2022 and 2023 (exports and imports by chapter and country) among the national statistical system's publications of the fourth quarter of 2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>). [The Observatoire du Transport's steering committee examined and validated the transport statistical yearbooks 2023 and 2024 on 29 December 2025, and they are listed among the publications of the fourth quarter of 2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The CPS health sector plan states the health ministry has regularly published the Annuaire statistique du SNIS since 2009, built from the SLIS, hospital and surveillance data, with a hospital yearbook since 2003 (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://files.aho.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/PS-SNISS_2020-2024_VF_11fev2021.pdf>).
