**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Health's Annuaire statistique 2024 was published in April 2025](<https://www.wakatsera.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/annuaire-statistique-2024-du-ministere-burkinabe-de-la-Sante.pdf>), with figures for 2024. It states its data come from the monthly activity reports of public and private health facilities. These are entered in the Endos-BF health data warehouse. Districts, regions and the ministry's statistics directorate validate them.
