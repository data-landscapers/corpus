# Tunisia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** In September 2025 the INS director general said [INS had begun preparing the next census, to be based on administrative registers](<https://africanmanager.com/le-prochain-recensement-sera-base-sur-les-registres-administratifs-2/>) instead of face-to-face collection, building the registers from the 2024 census results. Three facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [INS published a bulletin on social protection combining RGPH 2024 census results with CNSS, CNRPS, CNAM and Amen Social scheme records, giving 2.43 million occupied people effectively covered (66.3% of 3.66 million occupied aged 18+) and 82.1% health coverage](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). [INS published Statistiques issues du Repertoire National des Entreprises (edition 2024, figures 2005-2023), compiled from tax (DGI) and social security (CNSS) files plus APII and customs data](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [RGPH 2024](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>); [Repertoire National des Entreprises (RNE)](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>).
