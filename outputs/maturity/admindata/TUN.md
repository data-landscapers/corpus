# Tunisia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [INS published a bulletin on social protection combining RGPH 2024 census results with CNSS, CNRPS, CNAM and Amen Social scheme records, giving 2.43 million occupied people effectively covered (66.3% of 3.66 million occupied aged 18+) and 82.1% health coverage](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). [INS published Statistiques issues du Repertoire National des Entreprises (edition 2024, figures 2005-2023), compiled from tax (DGI) and social security (CNSS) files plus APII and customs data](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
