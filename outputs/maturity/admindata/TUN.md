# Tunisia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In September 2025 INS director general Bouzid Nsiri said INS had begun preparing the next census, to be based on administrative registers](<https://africanmanager.com/le-prochain-recensement-sera-base-sur-les-registres-administratifs-2/>). [It will replace face-to-face collection, with registers built from the 2024 census results](<https://africanmanager.com/le-prochain-recensement-sera-base-sur-les-registres-administratifs-2/>).

**Sources published from.** [In September 2025 INS published a bulletin on social protection combining 2024 census results with CNSS, CNRPS, CNAM and Amen Social scheme records](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). [It gives 2.43 million occupied people effectively covered, 66.3% of 3.66 million aged 18 and over, and 82.1% health coverage](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). [In June 2025 INS published statistics from the national business register, edition 2024](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>). [They were compiled from tax, social security, APII and customs files](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 INS published the 2024 edition of its business register statistics, covering 2005-2023](<https://www.ins.tn/publication/statistiques-issues-du-repertoire-national-des-entreprises-2023>).
