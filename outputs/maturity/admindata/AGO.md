# Angola

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Ministry of Higher Education (MESCTI) officially presented statistical yearbooks of higher education for 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24, with figures such as about 8% gross enrolment](<https://mescti.gov.ao/web/noticias/mescti-apresenta-anuario-estatistico-do-ensino-superior>). [The Anuario Estatistico Sanitario 2023, compiled by the health ministry's planning and statistics office from the Sistema de Informacao Sanitaria records, was released through INE on 6 April 2026, covering 2023](<https://ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/NTM0NjQ%3D>). [The Ministry of Higher Education's planning and statistics office published the 7th edition of the Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior in December 2023, covering academic year 2020-21 (graduates of 2019), compiled from seven administrative primary registers supplied by higher-education institutions](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>). Five facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
