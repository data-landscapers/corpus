# Angola

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In October 2024 MESCTI's Gabinete de Estudos, Planeamento e Estatistica published the 10th Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). [The yearbook covers 2023-2024](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). [Its figures on students, graduates, staff and grants come from the higher education institutions and INAGBE](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). [On 16 September 2025 Statistics Portugal, INE Angola and the World Bank met on a World Bank statistical capacity project](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [The project's first component inventories and evaluates data held in public administration](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [In September 2026 the World Bank reported a cut to the project's administrative data pilot](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092526002549347/pdf/P178043-e43e72a0-0ef6-4b41-b196-f41e92b8c8f9.pdf>). [INE and the Bank had agreed to reduce it from five ministries to three](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092526002549347/pdf/P178043-e43e72a0-0ef6-4b41-b196-f41e92b8c8f9.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Education's 2022/2023 education yearbook draws its figures on pupils, teachers and infrastructure from school-level data collected through SIGE](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>). [In December 2023 the Ministry of Higher Education's planning and statistics office published the 7th Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior, covering academic year 2020-21](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>). [It was compiled from seven administrative primary registers supplied by higher-education institutions](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly.
