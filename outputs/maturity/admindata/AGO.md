# Angola

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 16 September 2025 Statistics Portugal, INE Angola and the World Bank held an exploratory meeting on a World Bank statistical capacity project](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [Its first component includes an inventory and evaluation of data held in public administration and better administrative-based statistical products](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [In October 2024 MESCTI's Gabinete de Estudos, Planeamento e Estatistica published the 10th Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior, for 2023-2024](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>). [Its figures on students, graduates, staff and grants come from the higher education institutions and INAGBE](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Education's 2022/2023 education yearbook draws its figures on pupils, teachers and infrastructure from school-level data collected through SIGE](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>). [In December 2023 the Ministry of Higher Education's planning and statistics office published the 7th Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior, covering academic year 2020-21](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>). [It was compiled from seven administrative primary registers supplied by higher-education institutions](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest fact: [in 2025 the Ministry of Education's planning office published the Anuario Estatistico da Educacao 2022/2023, from SIGE data](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>).
