# Angola

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Statistics Portugal, INE Angola and the World Bank held an exploratory meeting on 16 September 2025 on the first component of a World Bank statistical capacity project, which includes an inventory and evaluation of data held in public administration and improvement of administrative-based statistical products](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [The World Bank approved on 2022-03-25 a US$60 million project, active to 2027, for Angola's INE whose scope covers household surveys, administrative data systems and statistical data platforms (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P178043>). Five facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more.

**Sources published from.** [The Ministry of Higher Education (MESCTI) officially presented statistical yearbooks of higher education for 2021-22, 2022-23 and 2023-24, with figures such as about 8% gross enrolment](<https://mescti.gov.ao/web/noticias/mescti-apresenta-anuario-estatistico-do-ensino-superior>). [The Ministry of Higher Education's planning and statistics office published the 7th edition of the Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior in December 2023, covering academic year 2020-21 (graduates of 2019), compiled from seven administrative primary registers supplied by higher-education institutions](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mescti.gov.ao/web/noticias/mescti-apresenta-anuario-estatistico-do-ensino-superior>).
