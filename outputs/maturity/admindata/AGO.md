# Angola

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Statistics Portugal, INE Angola and the World Bank held an exploratory meeting on 16 September 2025 on the first component of a World Bank statistical capacity project, which includes an inventory and evaluation of data held in public administration and improvement of administrative-based statistical products](<https://www.ine.pt/ine_novidades/Coopstat_n28/7/>). [MESCTI's Gabinete de Estudos, Planeamento e Estatistica published the 10th edition of the Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior 2023-2024 in October 2024, with figures on students, graduates, staff and grants based on information supplied by the higher education institutions and INAGBE](<https://mescti.gov.ao/media/uploads/ANUARIO_ESTATISTICO_2023_2024_5824af246e.pdf>).

**Sources published from.** [The 2022/2023 education yearbook of Angola's Ministry of Education draws its figures on pupils, teachers and infrastructure from school-level data collected through SIGE; the page gives 19 February with no year, so the year is inferred](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>). [The Ministry of Higher Education's planning and statistics office published the 7th edition of the Anuario Estatistico do Ensino Superior in December 2023, covering academic year 2020-21 (graduates of 2019), compiled from seven administrative primary registers supplied by higher-education institutions](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Publicacao_638494608243198556_f5b5215e8a.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>).
