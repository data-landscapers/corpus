# Burundi

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** No source found reports work under way, or a product that leaves its sources unnamed, since October 2023. The closest fact found: the health information directorate [states it produces a health statistics yearbook every year](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>). The 2024 edition was published in August 2025, after validation by the statistical information technical committee (CTIS), and follows a 2023 edition. Products that do not say their figures come from administrative records, as opposed to estimates, surveys or censuses, are not counted.

**Sources published from.** [The trade, transport, industry and tourism ministry published its sectoral statistical yearbook, edition 2024, in January 2026](<https://api.insbu.bi/storage/documents/01KRXTD00HD7QH9TWKPF45K76J.pdf>). It gives figures for 2024 and tabulates licences, permits, import-export documents, port and airport traffic and revenue from the ministry's own records. [The education ministry's planning and statistics bureau (BPSE) published the education statistical yearbook 2024-2025, Tome 2, in December 2025](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Tome-2_Annuaire__2024_2025.pdf>). It covers post-fundamental, technical and vocational schools, with data at 15 November 2024, compiled from the annual school census.

**Published regularly.** The Ministry's health information directorate [states it produces a health statistics yearbook every year](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>). The 2024 edition was published in August 2025, after validation by the statistical information technical committee (CTIS). It follows a 2023 edition.
