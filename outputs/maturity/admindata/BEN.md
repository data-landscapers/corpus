The closest fact found: [INStaD states it schedules a yearbook of civil-status statistics in its work plan every year](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-statistiques/Etat-civil/ANNUAIRE_DES_STAT_DE_L'ETAT_CIVIL_2024.pdf>). Since 2023 its data come from the ANIP database, and the 2024 edition is dated November 2025.

**Sources published from.** [INStaD published its yearbook of civil-status statistics for 2024](<https://www.instad.bj/images/docs/insae-statistiques/Etat-civil/ANNUAIRE_DES_STAT_DE_L'ETAT_CIVIL_2024.pdf>), compiled from the civil-registration database of ANIP. It covers births, marriages and deaths for all 77 communes. It records, for example, 468,570 births registered, 3,228 marriages and 9,707 deaths. The figures cover 2024, and the yearbook is dated November 2025.
