# South Sudan

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Ministry of Health, with WHO technical support, produced and launched the Health Statistics Abstract (2025) at the Fourth South Sudan Health Summit, 2-5 December 2025, launched by the Vice President](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-02/WHO%20South_Sudan_Annual_Report_2025_DigitalLow_v6_1_compressed.pdf>). [The Ministry of Health, with the National Bureau of Statistics, published the inaugural 2025 Health Statistics Abstract covering 2021 to 2024, drawing routine facility data from DHIS2 alongside surveys and modelled estimates](<http://www.nbs.gov.ss/assets/images/2025%20Health%20Statistical%20Abstract.pdf>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
