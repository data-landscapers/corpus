# Ethiopia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [The Ministry of Education's EMIS and ICT Executive Office released the Education Statistics Annual Abstract 2022/2023 (2015 E.C.), dated October 2023, covering general education from records collected by EMIS; the foreword text names the 2021/22 year, but the cover says 2022/23](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the country's statistics from administrative records: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** No source held reports vital statistics from civil registration published since October 2023. The nearest held fact: [The Ministry of Education's EMIS and ICT Executive Office released the Education Statistics Annual Abstract 2022/2023 (2015 E.C.), dated October 2023, covering general education from records collected by EMIS; the foreword text names the 2021/22 year, but the cover says 2022/23](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>).
