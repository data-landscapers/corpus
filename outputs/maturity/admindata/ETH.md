# Ethiopia

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In August 2025 the Ethiopian Statistics Service ran a three-day training in Semera for Afar regional sector offices](<https://ess.gov.et/%e1%8b%a8%e1%8a%a0%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b0%e1%8b%b3%e1%8b%b0%e1%88%ab%e1%8b%8a-%e1%88%98%e1%8b%9b%e1%8c%8d%e1%89%a5%e1%89%b5-%e1%88%98%e1%88%a8%e1%8c%83-%e1%8a%a0%e1%88%b0%e1%89%a3%e1%88%b0/>). [It covered collecting, managing and analysing administrative records so they can be used as official statistics](<https://ess.gov.et/%e1%8b%a8%e1%8a%a0%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b0%e1%8b%b3%e1%8b%b0%e1%88%ab%e1%8b%8a-%e1%88%98%e1%8b%9b%e1%8c%8d%e1%89%a5%e1%89%b5-%e1%88%98%e1%88%a8%e1%8c%83-%e1%8a%a0%e1%88%b0%e1%89%a3%e1%88%b0/>). [In February 2025 the Service released the 2015 E.C. Statistical Abstract in print and on its website](<https://ess.gov.et/%e1%8b%93%e1%88%98%e1%89%b3%e1%8b%8a-%e1%8b%a8%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b3%e1%89%b2%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b2%e1%8a%ad%e1%88%b5-%e1%88%98%e1%8c%bd%e1%88%94%e1%89%b5-%e1%88%88%e1%89%b0%e1%8c%a0%e1%89%83%e1%88%9a/>). [Its Director General said administrative records from various institutions are one source for the figures](<https://ess.gov.et/%e1%8b%93%e1%88%98%e1%89%b3%e1%8b%8a-%e1%8b%a8%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b3%e1%89%b2%e1%88%b5%e1%89%b2%e1%8a%ad%e1%88%b5-%e1%88%98%e1%8c%bd%e1%88%94%e1%89%b5-%e1%88%88%e1%89%b0%e1%8c%a0%e1%89%83%e1%88%9a/>).

[It covers general education from records collected by EMIS](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>).
