## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Stats SA published its first Statistics of insolvencies release (P0043.2) on 21 July 2026, covering June 2026 with a series from January 2023, compiled from insolvency data supplied by the Department of Justice and Constitutional Development (320 insolvencies in Q2 2026)](<https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19751>). [In a reply to parliamentary question NW6007, the Acting Minister of Police states that SAPS compiles quarterly crime statistics from dockets registered on its CAS/ICDMS case systems and releases them, while having halted the women-and-children breakdown over data inconsistencies](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.
