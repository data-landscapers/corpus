# Togo

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [From 28 August to 5 September 2025 the Ministry of Territorial Administration trained communal civil-registry officials, with UNFPA and INSEED as partners](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gestion-publique/2908-16989-togo-vers-une-modernisation-du-systeme-de-collecte-des-statistiques-d-etat-civil>). [The training is part of a reform to improve the collection and use of civil-registration statistics](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gestion-publique/2908-16989-togo-vers-une-modernisation-du-systeme-de-collecte-des-statistiques-d-etat-civil>). [In February 2025 the ministry asked the mayors of the 117 communes for the births, supplementary judgments, marriages and deaths registered from 2019 to 2024](<https://togobreakingnews.info/etat-civil-maires-chiffres-de-2019-a-2024/>). [The figures are to build national statistics on civil-status events](<https://togobreakingnews.info/etat-civil-maires-chiffres-de-2019-a-2024/>).

**Sources published from.** [In August 2026 INSEED released the revised Annuaire statistique national 2024, covering 2020 to 2024](<https://inseed.tg/download/7668/>). [It draws mainly on ministries' administrative reports and sectoral information systems, with chapters on education, health, justice, social security and more](<https://inseed.tg/download/7668/>). [Of 67 targeted structures, 63 returned data](<https://inseed.tg/download/7668/>).

**Published regularly.** No source found states that statistics from administrative records are published regularly. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 INSEED revised the national statistical yearbook first released in December 2025](<https://inseed.tg/download/7668/>).
