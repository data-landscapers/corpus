# Togo

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Togo's Ministry of Territorial Administration began training sessions on 28 August 2025, running to 5 September, for communal civil-registry officials as part of a reform to improve the collection and use of civil-registration statistics, with UNFPA and INSEED as partners](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gestion-publique/2908-16989-togo-vers-une-modernisation-du-systeme-de-collecte-des-statistiques-d-etat-civil>). [Togo's territorial administration ministry asked the mayors of the 117 communes to send the numbers of births, transcribed supplementary judgments, marriages and deaths registered from 2019 to 2024, by 21 February 2025, to build national statistics on civil-status events](<https://togobreakingnews.info/etat-civil-maires-chiffres-de-2019-a-2024/>).
