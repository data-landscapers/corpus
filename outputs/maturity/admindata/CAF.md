# Central African Republic

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 18 February 2026 the World Bank-financed HISWACA-SOP 2 project handed 300 motorcycles (357 million FCFA) to school sector chiefs to supervise and speed the collection and transmission of school data for the education statistics system, so that statistical yearbooks can be produced regularly](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/322-icasees-hiswaca-sop-2-ceremonie-officielle-de-remise-des-motos-aux-chefs-de-secteur-scolaire-de-leducation-nationale>). [A 2023 diagnostic of the civil-status system, launched by the territorial administration ministry and CIVIPOL under the EU-funded modernisation project, analysed the system's strengths and weaknesses including the production of civil-status statistics; no statistics are said to be published](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/05/02/centrafrique-validation-technique-du-document-de-politique-nationale-detat-civil/>). A World Bank mission to ICASEES on [7 and 8 October 2026 discussed developing administrative statistics of education and higher education](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/427-icasees-la-mission-dappui-de-la-banque-mondiale-au-projet-hiswaca-sop2>) under HISWACA-SOP2. Four facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: nothing else read for this study, including the yearbooks and reports the rebuild of 2026-10-19 found held, says more. Statistical products held that do not say their figures are drawn from administrative records, rather than estimates, surveys or censuses, are not counted.
