# Central African Republic

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [On 18 February 2026 the World Bank-financed HISWACA-SOP 2 project handed 300 motorcycles, worth 357 million FCFA, to school sector chiefs](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/322-icasees-hiswaca-sop-2-ceremonie-officielle-de-remise-des-motos-aux-chefs-de-secteur-scolaire-de-leducation-nationale>). [In 2023 the territorial administration ministry and CIVIPOL launched a diagnostic of the civil-status system under the EU-funded modernisation project](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/05/02/centrafrique-validation-technique-du-document-de-politique-nationale-detat-civil/>). [It analysed the system's strengths and weaknesses, the production of civil-status statistics among them](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2024/05/02/centrafrique-validation-technique-du-document-de-politique-nationale-detat-civil/>).
