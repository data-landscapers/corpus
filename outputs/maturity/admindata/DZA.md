# Algeria

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [Econostrum reports that Algeria's national statistics office (ONS) published a demography report for 2020-2023 built on an exhaustive survey of the communes' civil-status services covering births, deaths, stillbirths and marriages](<https://econostrum.info/algerie/demographie-natalite-esperance-de-vie/>). Two facts are held on the country's statistics from administrative records, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the yearbooks and reports themselves were not read.

**Vital statistics published.** [The ONS demography report for 2020-2023 gives births registered (under 900,000 in 2023) and deaths (192,000 in 2023) from the communes' civil-status services, per Econostrum](<https://econostrum.info/algerie/demographie-natalite-esperance-de-vie/>).

**Published every year.** No source held reports vital statistics published for two successive years. The nearest held fact: [The ONS demography report for 2020-2023 gives births registered (under 900,000 in 2023) and deaths (192,000 in 2023) from the communes' civil-status services, per Econostrum](<https://econostrum.info/algerie/demographie-natalite-esperance-de-vie/>).
