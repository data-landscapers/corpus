# Madagascar

## Statistics from administrative data

**Statistics from records published or in preparation.** [In February 2024 the Ministry of Public Health published the 26th edition of its health sector statistics yearbook](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [The yearbook covers health data for 2023](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [It was posted by the national statistics institute, INSTAT](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [It reports on the health of the population, morbidity and mortality, and on the curative and preventive activities of health facilities](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>).

**Sources published from.** [The yearbook says its data come from health facilities' monthly activity reports](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [The reporting sites are community sites, basic health centres, and district, regional and university hospitals](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>).

**Published regularly.** [The 2023 yearbook is the 26th edition since 1998](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [That was the year the health information management system was set up](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>). [The yearbook is drawn from the facilities' monthly activity reports each year](<https://instat.mg/p/msanp-annuaire-des-statistiques-du-secteur-sante-de-madagascar-2023>).
