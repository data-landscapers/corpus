# Ethiopia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 and the Ministry of Health say the DHIS2-based national HMIS reached all 30,000+ public health facilities by 2024, after full national implementation began in 2017, and held over 1 billion records by 2025](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use as the routine health information system in central and subnational data offices in Ethiopia](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). [A cross-sectional survey of 220 staff in public health facilities in Harari Regional State and Dire Dawa City Administration (June-July 2020) found DHIS2 in use there, with about 45% of staff using its data (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-15620-w>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>).
