# Ethiopia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 and the Ministry of Health say the DHIS2-based national HMIS reached all 30,000+ public health facilities by 2024](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [Full national implementation began in 2017](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [The system held over 1 billion records by 2025](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use in central and subnational data offices](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). [A 2020 survey of 220 staff in Harari and Dire Dawa found DHIS2 in use there (before the three-year window)](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-15620-w>).

**Linked.** [Weekly notifiable disease surveillance data are integrated into the DHIS2-based public health emergency system](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/ethiopia/>). DHIS2's country profile says so, as of 2024.

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>).

***Also on file***. [eCHIS](<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0350270>); [DHIS2 (education data, TaRL)](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/ethiopia/>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 names a separate system that exchanges data with the national system. None gives its reporting rate.
