# Ethiopia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 and the Ministry of Health say the DHIS2-based national HMIS reached all 30,000+ public health facilities by 2024](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [Full national implementation began in 2017](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>). [A case study says the ministry has used an HMIS since 2008 and moved to a unified DHIS2 in 2017 to integrate data across all regions and programmes](<https://ejhd.org/index.php/ejhd/article/view/6302>). [The IMPULSE study, run from November 2022 to July 2024, found DHIS2 in use in central and subnational data offices](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). [In 2020 a survey of 220 staff in Harari and Dire Dawa found DHIS2 in use there](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-15620-w>). [About 45% of those staff used its data](<https://doi.org/10.1186/s12913-026-15620-w>). [In November 2023 the Gates Foundation granted USD 4,798,310 for 2023-2027 to help the ministry upgrade and maintain the national HMIS](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=9PRIV1601.ETH.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2023%2C2023&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>).

**Linked.** [In 2024 DHIS2's country profile said weekly notifiable disease data are integrated into the DHIS2-based emergency system](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/ethiopia/>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [by 2025 the national HMIS held over 1 billion records](<https://dhis2.org/ethiopia-healthcare-performance>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 names a separate system that exchanges data with the national system. None gives its reporting rate.
