# Liberia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A 2026 Vaccines article by CDC, Ministry of Health and UNICEF authors describes Liberia's vaccination data](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>). [Monthly datasets from all facilities offering vaccination are aggregated at county level](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>). [Data officers enter them into the DHIS2-based HMIS the Ministry of Health manages](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>). [The system supplies national reports](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>). [In March 2026 a West African Health Organisation pilot aligned regional indicators with Liberia's national DHIS2](<https://atlanticinfos.tg/2026/03/08/benin-waho-initie-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-partage-automatise-des-donnees-de-sante-de-routine-phase-1/>). It is the first phase of automated sharing of routine health data.

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 CDC, ministry and UNICEF authors said vaccination data is entered into the DHIS2-based system](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 they said monthly data from all vaccinating facilities is aggregated by county and entered into DHIS2](<https://www.mdpi.com/2076-393X/14/4/323>).
