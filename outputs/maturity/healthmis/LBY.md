## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Libya Review reports that the Ministry of Health and WHO met in November 2025 to assess DHIS2 digital reporting](<https://libyareview.com/60731/libya-who-review-expansion-of-national-digital-health-system/>). [They plan to expand it](<https://libyareview.com/60731/libya-who-review-expansion-of-national-digital-health-system/>). [The Ministry stressed continuous data entry from all health institutions](<https://libyareview.com/60731/libya-who-review-expansion-of-national-digital-health-system/>). [DHIS2 indicators are used nationally and regionally](<https://libyareview.com/60731/libya-who-review-expansion-of-national-digital-health-system/>). It is the only fact held on the subject.
