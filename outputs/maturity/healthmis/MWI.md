# Malawi

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 states Malawi's DHIS2-based national health information system, in use since around 2000, has incorporated a new NCD screening algorithm](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). [A DHIS2 country profile calls it the ministry's main data collection tool and national repository for health data, covering more than 60 health programmes](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/malawi/>). [DHIS2 states the project deploys climate tools on Malawi's DHIS2-based national health management information system (HMIS), with initial implementation in four districts](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>). [The University of Malawi says HISP UNIMA is supporting the Ministry of Health in implementing DHIS2 systems and the national Health Management Information System (HMIS)](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/ichis-transforming-community-health-service-delivery-through-innovation-21-06-2026>). [The BMC Health Services Research article says data clerks transcribe patient records into facility registers that are compiled and reported via Malawi's DHIS1 electronic aggregation system](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0>).
