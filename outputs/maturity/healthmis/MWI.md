# Malawi

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 says Malawi's DHIS2-based national health information system has added a new NCD screening algorithm](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>). [A DHIS2 profile calls it the ministry's national repository for health data](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/malawi/>). [It covers more than 60 health programmes](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/malawi/>). [A climate project is deploying tools on the HMIS, starting in four districts](<https://dhis2.org/malawi-project-to-strengthen-surveillance-climate-sensitive-diseases/>). [The University of Malawi says HISP UNIMA supports the ministry with the national HMIS](<https://www.unima.ac.mw/news/single-post/ichis-transforming-community-health-service-delivery-through-innovation-21-06-2026>). [A BMC article says facility registers are reported through Malawi's DHIS1 aggregation system](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12913-025-12844-0>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/fighting-ncds-in-malawi-and-sri-lanka-with-the-diabetes-compass-project/>).
