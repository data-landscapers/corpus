# Equatorial Guinea

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [EHAS says a first DHIS2 pilot ran from February to May 2025 in two districts, Baney and Añisok](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). [Health facilities or district offices entered real data there, centralised on a ministry server](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). [UNDP reports the official launch of the DHIS2 platform at Baney on 1 March 2025](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). [More than 100 digital agents were trained](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>). [The health ministry says the pilot finished satisfactorily, with national expansion foreseen for 2026](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>).
