# Equatorial Guinea

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The health ministry (MINSABSIS) says the DHIS2 pilot run in June 2025 in the Baney and Añisok health districts finished satisfactorily, with national expansion of DHIS2 foreseen for 2026](<https://guineasalud.com/finaliza-con-exito-la-prueba-piloto-de-la-plataforma-dhis2-en-baney-y-anisok/>). [EHAS says a first DHIS2 pilot ran from February to May 2025 in two districts, Baney and Añisok, where health facilities or district offices enter real data, centralised on a server at the ministry](<https://ehas.org/que-hacemos/sis/sis-guinea-ecuatorial/>). [UNDP reports the official launch at Baney on 1 March 2025 of the national health system's digital tool, the DHIS2 platform, giving the ministry district-level aggregated care data, with more than 100 digital agents trained](<https://www.undp.org/es/equatorial-guinea/noticias/lanzamiento-de-la-herramienta-digital-en-el-sistema-sanitario-de-guinea-ecuatorial>).
