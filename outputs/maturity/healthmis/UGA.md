# Uganda

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health built an HMIS e-register for Health Centre II and III facilities](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uganda-CRVS-quartely-report-January-March-2026-1.pdf>). [In March 2026 a training of trainers in Arua was to cascade to 19 facilities](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uganda-CRVS-quartely-report-January-March-2026-1.pdf>). [The system is being tested for national rollout](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uganda-CRVS-quartely-report-January-March-2026-1.pdf>). [A March 2026 report says the ministry's DHIS2 carries the national HMIS data](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRVS-D4H-quartely-Report-October-December-2025.pdf>). [Hospitals in Kampala, eastern and western Uganda enter mortality data into its MCCD application](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRVS-D4H-quartely-Report-October-December-2025.pdf>). [The e-register is integrated with the HMIS](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uganda-CRVS-quartely-report-January-March-2026-1.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [DHIS2 reporting reached 98 percent in 2025](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [Makerere University News says DHIS2 reporting rose from 58 percent in 2020](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>). [It reached 98 percent in 2025](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).
