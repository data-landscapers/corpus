# Uganda

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health developed an integrated HMIS digital register (e-register) for Health Centre II and III facilities, with a training of trainers in Arua district in March 2026 to cascade to 19 facilities and system testing for national rollout, per the CRVS Q1 2026 report](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Uganda-CRVS-quartely-report-January-March-2026-1.pdf>). [The report, dated 10 March 2026, describes the Ministry of Health's District Health Information Software (DHIS2) as carrying the national HMIS data and a national MCCD application, with hospitals in Kampala, eastern and western Uganda entering mortality data into it](<https://uniph.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRVS-D4H-quartely-Report-October-December-2025.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [DHIS2 reporting rose from 58 percent in 2020 to 98 percent in 2025, per Makerere University News on the MakSPH-METS programme](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2026/04/mak-transitions-100-million-digital-health-systems-assets-to-health-ministry/>).
