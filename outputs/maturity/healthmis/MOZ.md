# Mozambique

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [MISAU's 2022-23 data-quality assessment visited 175 of 180 facilities in all 11 provinces](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>). [It checked facility registers against what SIS-MA, the national HMIS, reported](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>). [DHIS2 says the ministry adopted DHIS2 in 2015 as SISMA, covering 161 districts](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/climate/country-profiles/mozambique/>). [Malaria Consortium says the community system upSCALE is being scaled to all 10 provinces from January 2025](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=GB-CHC-1099776-2U-MZ>). [A US$4.6m grant funds it](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=GB-CHC-1099776-2U-MZ>). [The assessment compared four reporting tiers: facility, district, province and central](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>).

**Linked.** [MISAU's interoperability page says SIS-MA exchanges data with the medicines logistics platform of CMAM](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>). [It also exchanges data with the Ministry of Justice's SIRCEV death-certificate system](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>).
