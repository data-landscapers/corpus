# Mozambique

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Malaria Consortium's IATI record says upSCALE, a mobile electronic community health information system used by community health workers, is being institutionalised into the Ministry of Health and scaled to all 10 provinces under a US$4.6m grant from January 2025](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=GB-CHC-1099776-2U-MZ>). [MISAU's 2022-23 national data-quality assessment visited 175 of 180 facilities across all 11 provinces and compared facility registers with what SIS-MA, the national health management information system, reported, using four reporting tiers (facility, district, province, central)](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Plano-de-Melhoria-de-Qualidade-de-Dados-2025-2029.pdf>). [DHIS2 says the Ministry of Health adopted DHIS2 in 2015 as SISMA, the integrated platform for routine data of all health programmes, covering 161 districts](<https://dhis2.org/pt-pt/climate/country-profiles/mozambique/>).

**Linked.** [MISAU's SISMA interoperability page says SIS-MA exchanges data with the medicines logistics platform Ferramenta Central (CMAM) and with the Ministry of Justice's SIRCEV death-certificate system](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>).
