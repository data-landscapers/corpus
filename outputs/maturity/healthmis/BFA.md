# Burkina Faso

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Enabel's article states Burkina Faso has a structured, decentralised national health information system (SNIS) that collects routine data across the whole territory, with district data quality control done in DHIS2](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>). [Africa Frontline First says Burkina Faso's national health information system runs on DHIS2 as the National Health Management Information System known as ENDOS, into which community health worker data is to be integrated](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
