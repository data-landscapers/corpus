# Burkina Faso

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [In July 2026 Enabel said Burkina Faso has a decentralised national health information system (SNIS)](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>). [It collects routine data across the whole territory](<https://open.enabel.be/en/BFA/2798/3690/u/analyse-et-utilisation-des-donnees-sanitaires-les-districts-de-dedougou-et-de-tenkodogo-renforcent-leurs-capacites-pour-mieux-decider-et-mieux-agir.html>). [In September 2025 Africa Frontline First said the SNIS runs on DHIS2 as the national HMIS known as ENDOS](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 community health worker data was to be integrated into ENDOS](<https://africafrontlinefirst.org/2025/09/29/burkina-faso-is-scaling-up-digital-community-health-systems-through-a-national-roadmap/>).
