# Mauritania

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Countdown 2030 chartbook assesses national DHIS2 facility data for 2019-2024 by district and region](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MAURITANIE-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf.pdf>). [It reports that district report completeness is low against target](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MAURITANIE-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf.pdf>). [In November 2025 Countdown to 2030 and APHRC trained district focal points to use SNIS data](<https://www.countdown2030.org/countdown-news/strengthening-the-use-of-srmnia-nutrition-data-for-action-in-mauritania>). [The workshop had practical sessions in DHIS2](<https://www.countdown2030.org/countdown-news/strengthening-the-use-of-srmnia-nutrition-data-for-action-in-mauritania>). [In July 2025 the health ministry said DHIS2 is used nationally to collect and analyse SNIS health data](<https://elhadeth.mr/node/31262>).
