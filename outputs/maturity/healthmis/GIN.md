# Guinea

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health's PASA2 page says SNIS data is entered on the DHIS2 platform](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). [It installed 77 solar systems in 7 regions and 33 health districts to power data entry](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). [It gave 21 facilities internet and computers](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). [The ministry says OpenClinic now runs in five facilities in Mamou and Dalaba districts](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [The software can exchange data with DHIS2](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>). [Paper reporting into the SNIS continues alongside it](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>).
