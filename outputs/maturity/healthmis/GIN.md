# Guinea

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health's PASA2 page says SNIS data is entered on the DHIS2 platform, with 77 solar systems installed in 7 regions and 33 health districts to power data entry and 21 facilities given internet and computers](<https://pasa2.sante.gov.gn/activite-cle/gestion-analyse-et-validation-des-donnees-snis-dhis2/>). [The Ministry of Health says five health facilities in Mamou and Dalaba districts were digitalised with OpenClinic software, which offers interoperability with the national system (DHIS2), and that paper reporting into the SNIS continues alongside it](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2026/08/26/guinee-a-mamou-et-dalaba-le-pari-de-la-reforme-sanitaire-prend-une-nouvelle-dimension/>).
