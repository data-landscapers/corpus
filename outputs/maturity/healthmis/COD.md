# DR Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The author states that the national DHIS2 system is available in the DRC and that health facilities transmit data through it, though often incomplete or late](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>). [The DHIS2 organisation states the DRC has used DHIS2 as a national-scale HMIS since 2013, for health programme planning and management at zonal, provincial and national levels](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). [The Countdown analysis says DHIS2 data on facility reporting across the DRC's health zones and districts cover 2019-2024 and are of generally high quality](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Linked.** [RTI states the eIDSR disease surveillance system is integrated in the national DHIS2 platform and connects directly to the national health information system](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The analysis gives DHIS2 reporting completeness rising to 99% in 2024, with about 90% for six key indicators](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>).
