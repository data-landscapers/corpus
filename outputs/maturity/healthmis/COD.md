# DR Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [An April 2026 article says the national DHIS2 system is available in the DRC](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>). [Health facilities send data through it, though often incomplete or late](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>). [The DHIS2 organisation says the DRC has used DHIS2 as a national-scale HMIS since 2013](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). [PATH says health zone staff enter about half a million pages of HMIS reports into DHIS2 a month](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>). [A Countdown analysis says DHIS2 data on facility reporting across the health zones cover 2019-2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [It calls them generally of high quality](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Linked.** [RTI says the eIDSR disease surveillance system is integrated in the national DHIS2 platform](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>). [It connects directly to the national health information system](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The analysis gives DHIS2 reporting completeness rising to 99% in 2024, with about 90% for six key indicators](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/DRC--COUNTDOWN.pdf>).
