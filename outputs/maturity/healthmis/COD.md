# DR Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The author states that the national DHIS2 system is available in the DRC and that health facilities transmit data through it, though often incomplete or late](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/surveillance-mpox-integree-a-dhis2-au-sud-kivu/71886>). [The DHIS2 organisation states the DRC has used DHIS2 as a national-scale HMIS since 2013, for health programme planning and management at zonal, provincial and national levels](<https://dhis2.org/drc-ebola-response-2025/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Linked.** [RTI states the eIDSR disease surveillance system is integrated in the national DHIS2 platform and connects directly to the national health information system](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source held since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.rti.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-07/Implementing_Real-Time_Disease_Stolka.pdf>).
