## Health information system

**Health information system.** [On 4 September 2026 the project coordination centre CCIP asked for expressions of interest](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/09/1788545549351.pdf>). [The work falls under the USD 29 million IDA-financed health security project P179078](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/09/1788545549351.pdf>). [A firm is to train health staff and managers over six months](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/09/1788545549351.pdf>). [It is to support use of the health information system's digital tools, SIS/PORTAL and DHIS2](<https://static.expressodasilhas.cv/media/pub/v2/pdf/2026/09/1788545549351.pdf>). [In December 2023 the health ministry received 62 computers financed by the Global Fund](<https://minsaude.gov.cv/noticias/ccssida-entrega-a-direcao-nacional-da-saude-equipamentos-informaticos-financiados-pelo-fundo-global-para-reforcar-o-sis/>), to strengthen DHIS2 in health delegations and centres.
