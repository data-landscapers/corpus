# Gabon

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [ICTworks lists an eGabon-SIS pilot in 13 facilities, the 2017-2022 health information system master plan having expired](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>). [Gabon Media Time reports the DHIS2 Tracker has been piloted in several health facilities, with extension to four health districts by September 2026 and progressive national rollout in 2027, feeding patient data into the national DHIS2 system](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>). [HISP Rwanda reports Gabon officially launched the DHIS2 health information platform at national scale on 6 December 2024, with WHO and the Global Fund supporting national deployment](<https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/>).
