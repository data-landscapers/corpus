# Gabon

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [ICTworks lists an eGabon-SIS pilot in 13 facilities](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>). [The 2017-2022 health information system master plan has expired](<https://www.ictworks.org/updated-every-african-countrys-national-digital-health-strategy-in-2026/>). [Gabon Media Time says the DHIS2 Tracker has been piloted in several health facilities](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>). [It is to reach four health districts by September 2026, with national rollout in 2027](<https://gabonmediatime.com/sante-numerique-le-gabon-premier-pays-dafrique-francophone-a-deployer-le-dhis2-tracker/>). [HISP Rwanda says Gabon launched the DHIS2 platform at national scale on 6 December 2024](<https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/>). [WHO and the Global Fund support national deployment](<https://hisprwanda.org/dhis2-officially-launched-in-gabon-hisp-rwandas-pivotal-role-in-transforming-health-information-management/>).
