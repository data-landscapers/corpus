# Sudan

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The ministries of health and digital transformation launched a hospital management system with an HMIS component](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [Phase one runs at Al-Nao hospital in Khartoum and a Gedaref diagnostic centre](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [Expansion to other states is planned](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1784876>). [In February 2026 the ministry's health information director called for a full move from paper to DHIS2](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>). [A 2025 study found DHIS2 is the only approved system for collecting health information](<https://doi.org/10.59992/ijci.2025.v4n12p2>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the minister said reporting coverage passed 56 percent by the end of 2025](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The Federal Health Minister said national teams raised data reporting coverage from 13 percent](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>). [It reached more than 56 percent by the end of 2025](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202602100399.html>).
