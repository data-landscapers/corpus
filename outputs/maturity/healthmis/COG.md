# Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [HISP West and Central Africa says Congo's national health information system runs on DHIS2, which gives real-time data feed from 52 health districts to the central level](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>). [A Les Dépêches de Brazzaville report says DHIS-2 has been put in place across the national health information system since October 2018 and has proven itself in all 52 health districts for passing health data up to the central level, per the ministry's health information director (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.santetropicale.com/sites_pays/actus.asp?action=lire&id=32973&rep=congo>).
