# Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [HISP West and Central Africa says Congo's national health information system runs on DHIS2](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>). [It feeds data in real time from 52 health districts to the central level](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>). [In 2022 the ministry's health information director said DHIS-2 had been in place across the system since October 2018](<https://www.santetropicale.com/sites_pays/actus.asp?action=lire&id=32973&rep=congo>). [He said it had proven itself in all 52 districts (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.santetropicale.com/sites_pays/actus.asp?action=lire&id=32973&rep=congo>). [HISP West and Central Africa reported this in October 2023](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>).
