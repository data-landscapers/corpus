# Congo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [In October 2023 HISP West and Central Africa said Congo's national health information system runs on DHIS2](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>). [It feeds data in real time from 52 health districts to the central level](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>). [In August 2022 the ministry's health information director said DHIS-2 had been in place across the system since October 2018](<https://www.santetropicale.com/sites_pays/actus.asp?action=lire&id=32973&rep=congo>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [in October 2023 DHIS2 passed district data to the central level in real time](<https://hispwca.org/hispwca/?p=996>).
