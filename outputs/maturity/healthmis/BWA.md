# Botswana

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [WHO's 2024-2025 Botswana report calls DHIS2 the Government's selected gold standard platform for aggregate data](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>). [It says all districts now report IPV2 with monitoring through DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>). [The minister told parliament that District Health Information System 2 is active and in use in health facilities](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). [He said it is not interfaced with the other systems to share data across all facilities](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). [In November 2024 WHO trained the ministry's informatics staff to run DHIS2, its main aggregate reporting tool](<https://afro.who.int/countries/botswana/news/botswana-strengthens-malaria-surveillance-systems-accelerate-elimination-efforts>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest fact: [in 2025 WHO said all districts report IPV2 vaccination with monitoring through DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>).
