# Botswana

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [WHO's 2024-2025 Botswana report says DHIS2 is the Government's selected gold standard platform for aggregate data, enabling rapid response capacity across districts, and that all districts now report IPV2 with monitoring through DHIS2](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-05/WHO%20Botswana%20Biennial%20Report%202024-2025_web.pdf>). [The minister told parliament that District Health Information System 2 is active and in use in health facilities, though not interfaced with the other systems to permit data sharing across all facilities](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89169>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
