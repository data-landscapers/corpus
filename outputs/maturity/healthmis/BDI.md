# Burundi

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNDP Burundi says the DSNIS uses DHIS2 as the sole system for collecting and sending health data](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-verification-et-validation-des-donnees-tuberculose-pour-lamelioration-des-performances-des-services-et-soins-de-sante>). [Data are validated at district, province and DSNIS level](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-verification-et-validation-des-donnees-tuberculose-pour-lamelioration-des-performances-des-services-et-soins-de-sante>). [Tuberculosis data checks ran in 16 provinces on 3-22 June 2024](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-verification-et-validation-des-donnees-tuberculose-pour-lamelioration-des-performances-des-services-et-soins-de-sante>). [The health ministry's Annuaire 2024 says health facilities and community groups enter monthly reports in DHIS2](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>). [District and province offices validate them, and the DSNIS compiles a national database](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>).

**Linked.** [In June 2026 OpenFn said a scheduled workflow takes HIV indicators from SIDAInfo, the HIV electronic medical record, and pushes them to DHIS2](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>). [OpenFn says DHIS2 carries Burundi's aggregate national health reporting and programme monitoring](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>).

**Reporting rate published.** [Countdown 2030 gives national completeness of monthly facility reports as 96% in 2025](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Burundi.pdf>), with 84% of districts at 90% or more. [The Annuaire 2024 gives 99.6 percent completeness for community monthly reports in DHIS2](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>), with a table by district.
