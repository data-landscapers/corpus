# Burundi

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNDP Burundi says DSNIS uses DHIS2 as the sole system for collecting and transmitting health data in real time, validated at district, province and DSNIS level, and tuberculosis data checks ran in 16 provinces on 3-22 June 2024](<https://www.undp.org/fr/burundi/actualites/pnud-verification-et-validation-des-donnees-tuberculose-pour-lamelioration-des-performances-des-services-et-soins-de-sante>). [The Burundi health ministry's Annuaire 2024 states that monthly reports from health facilities and community groups are entered in DHIS2, validated by district and province offices, and compiled by the DSNIS into a national database](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>).

**Linked.** [In June 2026 OpenFn said a scheduled workflow takes HIV indicators from SIDAInfo, the HIV electronic medical record, and pushes them to DHIS2](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>). [OpenFn says DHIS2 carries Burundi's aggregate national health reporting and programme monitoring](<https://www.openfn.org/spotlight/automating-national-hiv-health-information-exchange-in-burundi>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The Annuaire 2024 publishes a national average completeness of 99.6 percent for community (GASC) monthly reports entered in DHIS2 in 2024, with a district-by-district table](<https://ahofiles.afro.who.int/afahobckpcontainer/production/files/Annuaire_des_Statistiques_Sanitaires_2024_VF.pdf>).
