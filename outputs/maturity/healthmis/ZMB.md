# Zambia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Global Fund case study says the Government of Zambia and partners have strengthened the national health management information system for routine reporting, a DHIS2-based system the Global Fund supports with upgrades and maintenance](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [The Countdown to 2030 / Global Financing Facility synthesis report analyses routinely reported national health facility data from DHIS2 for 2019-2024, with facility reports received from districts across the country](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The Ministry of Health's ZM-Core FHIR guide defines a facility profile for exchanging health information across systems, carrying identifiers issued by the Master Facility List, HMIS, DHIS2, SmartCare, eLMIS and iHRIS](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>). [The Global Fund case study says approximately 90% of health facilities use the national DHIS2 platform for integrated disease surveillance and response reporting](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The report gives national completeness of monthly facility reporting in DHIS2 as 99% of expected reports in 2024 (93% in 2019), with 99% of districts at or above 90% completeness in 2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).
