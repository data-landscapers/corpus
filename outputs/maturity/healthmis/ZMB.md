# Zambia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Global Fund says Zambia has strengthened its national HMIS for routine reporting](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [It is a DHIS2-based system the Global Fund helps upgrade and maintain](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [A Countdown to 2030 report analyses national facility data from DHIS2 for 2019-2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [Reports came from districts across the country](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The Global Fund says about 90% of health facilities use the national DHIS2 for disease surveillance reporting](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [The ministry's ZM-Core FHIR guide sets a facility profile for exchanging data across systems](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>). [It carries identifiers from the facility list, HMIS, DHIS2, SmartCare, eLMIS and iHRIS](<https://github.com/MOH-Zambia/ZM-Core/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [Countdown to 2030 gives national completeness of monthly facility reporting in DHIS2 as 99% in 2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [It was 93% in 2019](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [In 2024, 99% of districts were at or above 90% completeness](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/Zambia-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).
