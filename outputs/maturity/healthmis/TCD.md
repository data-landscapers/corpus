## Health information system

**Health information system.** [On 22 September 2026 the health ministry began validating its 2025 health statistics yearbook](<https://lesdepechesdutchad.com/lannuaire-des-statistiques-sanitaires-du-tchad-2025-en-validation/>). It rests on the national base of the DHIS2 platform. [On 2 April 2026 the ministry launched the Vacci360 pilot in Lac, Kanem and Hadjer Lamis](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/02/vacci360-projette-de-renforcer-la-couverture-vaccinale/>). It adds an analysis layer to the existing DHIS2 to raise vaccination coverage.
