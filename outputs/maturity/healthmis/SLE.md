# Sierra Leone

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The HISP network has supported Sierra Leone's DHIS2-based HMIS since 2007, across all 16 districts](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [The UN is preparing a programme to help build a nationally owned health information system](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [A Countdown analysis uses facility data reported through DHIS2 for 2019-2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [It assesses them at national and district level](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [The Health Information Hub is to play a central role in it](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>).

**Linked.** [The Ministry of Health says its new Health Information Hub draws on several platforms, including DHIS2](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>). [It brings together facility, staffing, supply chain and referral data](<https://mohs.gov.sl/moh-launches-health-information-hub/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The Countdown report gives national completeness of monthly facility reporting in DHIS2 for 2019 to 2024](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>). [It was 99, 95, 95, 93, 94 and 98 percent of expected reports](<https://data.gffportal.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/SierraLeone-COUNTDOWN.pdf>).
