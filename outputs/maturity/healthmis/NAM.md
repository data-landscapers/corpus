# Namibia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The US CDC says it supports the health ministry with the national system, DHIS2](<https://dev2.restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/global-health/media/pdfs/2024/11/Namibia-2024-Fact-Sheet.pdf>). [It collects aggregated data and gives staff from facilities to national programmes access to them](<https://dev2.restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/global-health/media/pdfs/2024/11/Namibia-2024-Fact-Sheet.pdf>). [Countdown to 2030 reports national facility data from DHIS2 for 2019-2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). [In 2024, 96% of expected monthly facility reports arrived nationally](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). [93% of districts reached 90% completeness or better](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).

**Linked.** [PTracker, the patient-level tracker for mothers and infants, submits its data to DHIS2 automatically](<https://informaticshub.ucsf.edu/node/116>). It also feeds the National Data Warehouse, UCSF's technical assistance page says. The warehouse combines data from DHIS2 and the electronic patient management system.

**Reporting rate published.** [Countdown to 2030 gives national DHIS2 completeness of monthly facility reporting as 86% in 2019](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>). [It rose to 96% of expected monthly reports in 2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).
