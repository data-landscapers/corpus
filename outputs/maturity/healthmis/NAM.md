# Namibia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The US CDC says it supports the health ministry with the national health information management system (DHIS2), which collects aggregated data and produces summary reports, giving staff from health facilities up to national programmes access to information](<https://dev2.restoredcdc.org/www.cdc.gov/global-health/media/pdfs/2024/11/Namibia-2024-Fact-Sheet.pdf>). [Countdown to 2030 reports national health facility data from DHIS2 for 2019-2024, with 96% of expected monthly facility reports received nationally in 2024 and 93% of districts at 90% completeness or better](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [Countdown to 2030 gives national DHIS2 completeness of monthly facility reporting as 86% in 2019 rising to 96% of expected monthly reports in 2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).
