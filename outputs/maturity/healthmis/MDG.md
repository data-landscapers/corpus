# Madagascar

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [MTOMADY says DHIS2 is the ministry's national health data system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mtomady_digitalhealth-interoperability-dhis2-activity-7381257757754150912-s2DE>). [In October 2026 HISP and the Data.FI project finished five months of work strengthening the national DHIS2 platform](<https://dhis2.org/hisp-centre-completes-dhis2-system-strengthening-support-in-madagascar-with-data-fi/>). It hosts the national routine and surveillance systems. [Community health volunteers' monthly activity reports also reach the national system](<https://msh.org/story/mobile-app-technology-improves-health-delivery-in-rural-madagascar/>), USAID ACCESS says.

**Linked.** [CommCare covers care, surveillance and reporting, including COVID-19 and plague tracking](<https://msh.org/story/mobile-app-technology-improves-health-delivery-in-rural-madagascar/>). [USAID ACCESS and Dimagi say it feeds its data into the national health information management system](<https://msh.org/story/mobile-app-technology-improves-health-delivery-in-rural-madagascar/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [Countdown 2030 gives monthly facility reporting completeness of 98% in 2024 and in 2025](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Madagascar.pdf>). [The share of districts with complete reporting rose from 92% to 94%](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Madagascar.pdf>). [Health ministry staff presented these Countdown 2030 figures for 2020 to 2025](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Madagascar.pdf>).
