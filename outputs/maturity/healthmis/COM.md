# Comoros

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The DHIS2 Climate & Health country profile, updated in July 2026, says Comoros has used DHIS2 as its national health management information system since 2022](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>). It covers routine data on a range of health programmes. [In October 2025 DHIS2 said almost 99 percent of public health facilities in Comoros reported through it](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>).

**Linked.** [DHIS2 says the eLMIS project, launched in 2024, links stock records kept in DHIS2 at facilities with Open mSupply at central and regional warehouses](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>). The ministry can follow stock from end to end, DHIS2 says. [La Gazette des Comores reports that a coordination meeting on 27 October 2026 presented the electronic vaccination register as integrated or interoperable with DHIS2](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/sant%C3%A9/sant%C3%A9-et-num%C3%A9rique-onze-projets-pour-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-la-transformation-du-syst%C3%A8me-sanitaire-.html>). The same was said of the medicines and supplies management system.
