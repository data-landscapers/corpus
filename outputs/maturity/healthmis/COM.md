# Comoros

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The DHIS2 Climate & Health country profile (updated July 2026) says DHIS2 has been used as Comoros's national health management information system since 2022, covering routine data on a range of health programmes](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/comoros/>). [DHIS2 says almost 99 percent of Comoros public health facilities already used DHIS2 for health service reporting](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Linked.** [DHIS2 says the eLMIS project, launched in 2024, links facility-level stock recording in the national DHIS2 platform with Open mSupply at central and regional warehouses, giving the Ministry end-to-end stock visibility](<https://dhis2.org/supply-chain-management-dhis2-open-msupply-comoros/>).
