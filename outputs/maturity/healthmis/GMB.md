# Gambia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 has served as the Gambia's national health management information system since 2009, DHIS2 said in September 2024](<https://dhis2.org/strengthening-school-management-gambia-eswatini/>). [The education ministry has also run DHIS2 for school data since 2018](<https://dhis2.org/strengthening-school-management-gambia-eswatini/>). [Smart Paper Technology, a paper-to-digital tool the Ministry of Health launched in 2017, scans health forms and syncs them with DHIS2](<https://articles.nigeriahealthwatch.com/smart-paper-technology-is-transforming-community-health-data-capture-in-the-gambia>). [It was piloted at Bundung and Fajikunda and was expanding in December 2025, Nigeria Health Watch reports](<https://articles.nigeriahealthwatch.com/smart-paper-technology-is-transforming-community-health-data-capture-in-the-gambia>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the national system is linked to disease surveillance, the medicines supply chain or a patient register. [The nearest held fact is the paper-to-digital forms that feed DHIS2 at pilot sites](<https://articles.nigeriahealthwatch.com/smart-paper-technology-is-transforming-community-health-data-capture-in-the-gambia>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the national system's reporting completeness or timeliness. [The nearest held fact is DHIS2's use as the national system since 2009](<https://dhis2.org/strengthening-school-management-gambia-eswatini/>).
