# Togo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Togo's Ministry of Health has used DHIS2 as its HMIS since 2016, the DHIS2 profile says](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). [It is the main tool for collecting and aggregating data and the national repository](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). [The ministry owns it, with HISP WCA support](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). [In July 2026 HISP helped the ministry add the eRDQA app to the national DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). [It was tested in hospitals and health centres in three regions](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). [The July 2026 mission went to the Savanes, Kara and Centrale regions](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>). [The profile was updated in July 2026](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>).

**Linked.** [The profile says Togo also uses DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance, alongside its HMIS role](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>).
