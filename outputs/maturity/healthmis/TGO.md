# Togo

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 has been used by Togo's Ministry of Health since 2016 as the country's HMIS, its main data collection and aggregation tool and national repository for health data, owned and maintained by the ministry with HISP WCA support (DHIS2 profile, updated July 2026)](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>). [HISP says it supported Togo's national health information systems directorate on a July 2026 field mission in the Savanes, Kara and Centrale regions to integrate the eRDQA app into the national DHIS2 platform, testing it in regional hospitals and health centres](<https://dhis2.org/fr/hisp-highlights-july-2026/>).

**Linked.** [The profile says Togo also uses DHIS2 for infectious disease surveillance, alongside its HMIS role](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/togo/>).
