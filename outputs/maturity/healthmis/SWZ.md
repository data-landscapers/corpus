# Eswatini

## Health information system

**Health information system.** On 16 November 2026 [the health ministry launched a National Health Intelligence Platform](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-health-platform-to-help-govt-anticipate-medicine-stockouts/>). The minister said it will bring clinical, epidemiological, supply-chain and community data into one system. [Harmonised reporting tools and automated aggregation are expected](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-health-platform-to-help-govt-anticipate-medicine-stockouts/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says a national system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest fact found: [the platform launched in November 2026 is meant to draw in supply-chain data](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-health-platform-to-help-govt-anticipate-medicine-stockouts/>).
