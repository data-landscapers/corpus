# Central African Republic

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A DHIS2 Community poster by the CAR health ministry's SNIS team states DHIS2 was adopted as the national platform for the Système National d'Information Sanitaire and deployed in all 35 health districts](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use as the routine health information system in central and subnational data offices in the Central African Republic](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Linked.** [HISP Rwanda says clinical data from the e-SENI hospital EMR is automatically structured and integrated into national health information systems, including DHIS2-powered platforms, with automated reporting to DHIS2](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The poster states report completeness rose from 52% in 2020 to over 85% in 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>).
