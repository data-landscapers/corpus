# Central African Republic

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A poster by the health ministry's SNIS team says DHIS2 was adopted as the national platform for the Système National d'Information Sanitaire](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). [It was deployed in all 35 health districts](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use in central and subnational data offices](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). 4 facts are held on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Linked.** [HISP Rwanda says clinical data from the e-SENI hospital record are integrated into national systems, including DHIS2](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>). [The record reports to DHIS2 automatically](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-emr-digitizing-hospital-care-in-the-central-african-republic/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The poster states report completeness rose from 52% in 2020 to over 85% in 2025](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/dhis2-pour-la-reconstruction-du-snis-cas-de-la-rca/72669>).
