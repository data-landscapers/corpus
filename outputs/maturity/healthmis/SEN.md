# Senegal

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Countdown to 2030 report presented by Senegal's health ministry directorates analyses facility data reported through DHIS2 for 2019-2024, including by district and region](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SENEGAL-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf-FINAL.pdf>). [The health ministry told the World Health Assembly in May 2026 that it runs a unified national DHIS2 platform for health information](<https://apps.who.int/gb/statements/WHA79/PDF/Senegal-12.10.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The ministry said in May 2026 that the national DHIS2 platform has been extended to epidemiological surveillance, human resources, the supply chain and maternal and perinatal death surveillance](<https://apps.who.int/gb/statements/WHA79/PDF/Senegal-12.10.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The share of districts with report completeness of 90% or more was 57% in 2024 for reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health (antenatal care and delivery) in DHIS2, and gives an overall data-quality score of 63% in 2024](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SENEGAL-Chartbook-Report-CAM-2025.pdf-FINAL.pdf>).
