# Djibouti

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNFPA Djibouti says the Ministry of Health, with UNFPA support, is integrating gender-based violence case data into the National Health Information System (DHIS2), whose operational administration is run by the Health Information Directorate](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>). [WHO Djibouti's management response of 14 April 2025 says DHIS2 is implemented at primary health care level, with challenges in its use, and that the DHIS2 system is to be reviewed and enhanced and resources sought to revamp the data system and ensure data connectivity at national level](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/evaluation-office/management-response_evaluation-of-who-contribution-in-djibouti.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=c96230e4_3>).
