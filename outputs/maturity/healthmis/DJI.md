# Djibouti

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNFPA Djibouti says the Ministry of Health is adding gender-based violence case data to the National Health Information System (DHIS2)](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>). [The Health Information Directorate runs the system](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>). [WHO's management response of 14 April 2025 says DHIS2 is implemented at primary health care level](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/evaluation-office/management-response_evaluation-of-who-contribution-in-djibouti.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=c96230e4_3>). [It notes challenges in its use](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/evaluation-office/management-response_evaluation-of-who-contribution-in-djibouti.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=c96230e4_3>). [It says DHIS2 is to be reviewed, and resources sought to ensure data connectivity at national level](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/evaluation-office/management-response_evaluation-of-who-contribution-in-djibouti.pdf?download=true&sfvrsn=c96230e4_3>).
