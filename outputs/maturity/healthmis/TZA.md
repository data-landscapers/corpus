# Tanzania

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health service page lists DHIS2 as the system used for health information collection, storage, analysis, reporting and dissemination, with an HMIS data warehouse accessible through DHIS2 HMIS Data and a public data portal](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use as the routine health information system in central and subnational data offices in Tanzania](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). [The UDSM DHIS2 Lab page says Zanzibar is channelling data from vertical programme systems into the national HMIS (ZHMIS) through the Zanzibar Health Information Layer, led by the Ministry of Health Zanzibar, with a workshop validating end-to-end flows into ZHMIS](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>).
