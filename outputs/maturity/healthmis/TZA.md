# Tanzania

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health lists DHIS2 as its system for collecting, storing, analysing and reporting health information](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>). [An HMIS data warehouse and a public data portal sit on it](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>). [The IMPULSE study (Nov 2022-Jul 2024) found DHIS2 in use in central and subnational data offices](<https://researchonline.lshtm.ac.uk/id/eprint/4679144/>). [Zanzibar is channelling programme data into its HMIS, ZHMIS, through a health information layer](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>). [A workshop validated the data flows](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>). [The UDSM DHIS2 Lab reports the Zanzibar work](<https://dhis2.udsm.ac.tz/advancing-tanzanias-information-systems-interoperability-agenda/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moh.go.tz/en/services/18>).
