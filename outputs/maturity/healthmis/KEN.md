# Kenya

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health says 6,427 public facilities have migrated from the SHA Provider Portal to the Health Management Information System](<https://www.health.go.ke/cs-duale-calls-timely-transition-hmis-and-sha-contracting>). [So have 38 private and 38 of 518 faith-based facilities](<https://www.health.go.ke/cs-duale-calls-timely-transition-hmis-and-sha-contracting>). [The Council of Governors committed to help Level 5 hospitals complete the move](<https://www.health.go.ke/duale-and-governors-set-14-october-deadline-sha-facility-contracts>). [Duale says 4 Level 5 hospitals have migrated and 23 are in progress](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001559265/why-sha-is-shifting-hospitals-from-portal-to-hmis-duale-explains>). [The Digital Health Agency has certified 28 HMIS platforms](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001559265/why-sha-is-shifting-hospitals-from-portal-to-hmis-duale-explains>).
