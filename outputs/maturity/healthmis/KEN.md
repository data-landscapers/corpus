# Kenya

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Ministry of Health says 6,427 public facilities, 38 DHA-certified private facilities and 38 of 518 faith-based facilities have completed migration from the SHA Provider Portal to the Health Management Information System](<https://www.health.go.ke/cs-duale-calls-timely-transition-hmis-and-sha-contracting>). [The Council of Governors committed to support Level 5 hospitals to complete their transition to the Health Management Information System, per the Ministry of Health](<https://www.health.go.ke/duale-and-governors-set-14-october-deadline-sha-facility-contracts>). [Duale says HMIS is part of the government's ongoing rollout, with all 6,427 public Level 2 to 4 facilities transitioned, 4 Level 5 migrated and 23 in progress, and 28 HMIS platforms certified by the Digital Health Agency](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001559265/why-sha-is-shifting-hospitals-from-portal-to-hmis-duale-explains>).
