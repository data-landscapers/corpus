# Mali

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Muso describes DHIS2 as Mali's official national health data management system](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>). [A DGSHP deputy director says DHIS2 and DISC-Mali now exchange data](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>). [Community health workers in four districts reported births and perinatal deaths into DHIS2 by smartphone](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). [Over eight months they reported 729 live births and 35 perinatal deaths](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). [They used DISK-Mali, which INSP and Vital Strategies say is integrated into DHIS2](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). [The districts were Diéma, Kita, Sikasso and Kignan](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 a health ministry deputy director said DHIS2 had been made interoperable with DISC-Mali](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>).
