# Mali

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Muso describes DHIS2 as the official national health data management system of Mali, and a DGSHP deputy director says interoperability between DHIS2 and DISC-Mali has been established](<https://www.musohealth.org/post/towards-an-integrated-and-efficient-health-information-ecosystem>). [Community health workers in the health districts of Diéma, Kita, Sikasso and Kignan reported births and perinatal deaths by smartphone into DHIS2 over eight months (729 live births, 35 perinatal deaths), via the DISK-Mali tool that the abstract says is integrated into DHIS2, per INSP and Vital Strategies](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>).
