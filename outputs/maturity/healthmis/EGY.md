# Egypt

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [In January 2026 the Ministry of Health and Population launched DHIS2 as part of the national health information system](<https://dhis2.org/egypt-launches-dhis2-in-health-information-system>). A national DHIS2 training programme began at the launch, per DHIS2 and the HISP Centre. [In July 2026 the Egyptian Healthcare Authority said its health information system ran in 265 primary care units](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4321204>) and 166 other facilities. These are in the first universal health insurance governorates, with a pilot starting in Minya.
