# Nigeria

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Federal Ministry of Health's June 2025 NHMIS Bulletin reports national health facility data](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). [Facilities across the states and the FCT are shown reporting](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). [States are encouraged to report directly into DHIS-2](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). [The bulletin presents data reported through the National Health Management Information System](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the ministry put the national facility reporting rate at 91.5% for June 2025](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The Federal Ministry of Health put the national health facility reporting rate at 91.5% for June 2025](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). [Monthly rates ran from 90.9% to 93.4% for January to June 2025](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>). [The June figure was the rate at the time of the report](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).
