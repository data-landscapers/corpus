# Nigeria

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Federal Ministry of Health's June 2025 NHMIS Bulletin reports national health facility data through the National Health Management Information System, with states encouraged to report directly into DHIS-2 and facilities across states and the FCT shown reporting](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [The Federal Ministry of Health says the national health facility reporting rate for June 2025 was 91.5% at the time of the report, with monthly rates of 90.9% to 93.4% for January to June 2025](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NHMIS-June-2025-Bulletin-1.pdf>).
