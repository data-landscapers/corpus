# Sao Tome and Principe

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A University of Sao Tome and Principe master's dissertation, reported by Tela Non, used national DHIS2 system data for 2022-2023 to compare hypertension, diabetes and stroke incidence across districts including Agua Grande, Lemba, Me-Zochi, Lobata and Principe](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [UNDP Sao Tome and Principe published a notice (UNDP-STP-00136) on 29 April 2026 for a one-month independent evaluation of the country's health digitisation project, covering the effectiveness and sustainability of the DHIS2, mSupply and Instalacoes Inteligentes systems](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says the system is linked to surveillance, supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).

The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).
