# Sao Tome and Principe

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Tela Non reports a 2023 master's dissertation that used national DHIS2 data for 2022-2023](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [It compared hypertension, diabetes and stroke across districts, Principe included](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [On 29 April 2026 UNDP sought an evaluation of the health digitisation project](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [It covers the DHIS2, mSupply and Instalacoes Inteligentes systems](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [The dissertation's districts included Agua Grande, Lemba, Me-Zochi and Lobata](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/07/29/53782/estudo-revela-elevada-carga-de-hipertensao-diabetes-e-avc-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [The notice is UNDP-STP-00136](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>). [The evaluation was to last one month](<https://undpjobs.net/vacancy/ic-para-realizar-a-avaliacao-do-sistema-de-informacao-na-saude-em-stp-842416>).
