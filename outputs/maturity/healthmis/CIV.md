# Cote d'Ivoire

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [In September 2025 mHealth Africa said DHIS2, the national database known as SIGSANTE, is the backbone of the National Health Information System](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [It has centralised routine health data since its deployment in 2015](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [In October 2026 Ecofin Agency reported a National Health Data Platform giving one access point, with an API, to the system's official indicators](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0510-59476-nigeria-builds-national-digital-health-architecture-to-connect-fragmented-systems>).

**Linked.** [Since 2018 the national DHIS2 has taken in vaccination, epidemiological surveillance and community data flows](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The mHealth community platform sends data to it through HL7 FHIR APIs](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).
