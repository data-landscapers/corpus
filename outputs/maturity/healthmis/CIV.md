# Cote d'Ivoire

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [In September 2025 mHealth Africa said DHIS2, the national database known as SIGSANTE, is the backbone of the National Health Information System](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [It has centralised routine health data since its deployment in 2015](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).

**Linked.** [Since 2018 the national DHIS2 has taken in vaccination, epidemiological surveillance and community data flows](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>). [The mHealth community platform sends data to it through HL7 FHIR APIs](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/mhealth-et-dhis2-vers-une-interoperabilite-renforcee-en-cote-divoire/>).
