# Somalia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Somalia Federal Ministry of Health poster says DHIS2 functions as the national HMIS for aggregate reporting in Somalia](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [HISP UiO says DHIS2 serves as the backbone of Somalia's national health management information system, supporting data collection across disease surveillance, HIV monitoring and immunization](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>).

**Linked.** [The CaafimaadPlus Consortium says its C+ Digitalized Data System, operating in over 40 facilities across 9 regions, pushes data in real time into the government's DHIS2 platform](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [The poster says RAAD (BAMNI), the national primary-care electronic health record deployed in 111 facilities across six states, automatically generates DHIS2-compliant aggregate reports from routine care records](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).
