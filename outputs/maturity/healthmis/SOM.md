# Somalia

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A Federal Ministry of Health poster says DHIS2 is Somalia's national HMIS for aggregate reporting](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [HISP UiO calls DHIS2 the backbone of the national HMIS](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>). [It supports data collection for disease surveillance, HIV monitoring and immunisation](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>).

**Linked.** [The CaafimaadPlus Consortium says its data system, in over 40 facilities in 9 regions, pushes data into DHIS2](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [The data arrive in real time](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>). [The ministry poster says RAAD, the national primary-care health record, runs in 111 facilities](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [It generates aggregate reports for DHIS2 from routine care records](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/a-standards-based-approach-to-dhis2-integration/72690>). [HISP UiO says Somalia has adopted DHIS2 Tracker for disease surveillance, HIV monitoring and immunisation records](<https://dhis2.org/unicef-and-hisp-uio-expand-partnership-somalia/>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://caafimaad-plus.org/partnering-for-a-data-driven-somali-health-system/>).
