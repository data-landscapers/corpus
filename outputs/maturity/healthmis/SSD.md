# South Sudan

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNESCO says DHIS2 is widely used by the Government of South Sudan for routine health information and has supported the Ministry of Health in collecting data across states, counties, payams and bomas since 2018](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/south-sudan-launches-new-education-emergencies-data-system-strengthen-crisis-education-response>). [The DHIS2 Climate & Health country profile says DHIS2 has been used as South Sudan's national health management information system since 2020, covering routine data on a range of health programs](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>). [The WHO/MoH maturity assessment says South Sudan has implemented DHIS 2 at both the national and sub-national/district levels](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The profile says the FLLOW-M dashboard within the national DHIS2 integrates historical malaria surveillance data with climate indicators, and DHIS2 is also used for disease surveillance](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The WHO/MoH maturity assessment records the completeness rate for routine reports at 62% and for indicator data at 57%](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).
