# South Sudan

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [UNESCO says the Government of South Sudan widely uses DHIS2 for routine health information](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/south-sudan-launches-new-education-emergencies-data-system-strengthen-crisis-education-response>). [Since 2018 it has collected data across states, counties, payams and bomas](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/south-sudan-launches-new-education-emergencies-data-system-strengthen-crisis-education-response>). [The DHIS2 country profile says DHIS2 has been the national HMIS since 2020](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>). [A WHO and ministry assessment says DHIS 2 runs at national and district levels](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The profile says the FLLOW-M dashboard in the national DHIS2 joins malaria surveillance data with climate data](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>). [DHIS2 is also used for disease surveillance](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/south-sudan/>). [A UNDP brief says the mSupply eLMIS at the Gumbo Warehouse exchanges data with DHIS2](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>). [It reaches 105 of 145 health facilities](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-06/undp-ss-rsshbrief-2026.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The WHO/MoH maturity assessment records the completeness rate for routine reports at 62% and for indicator data at 57%](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20Maturity%20of%20the%20Health%20Information%20System%20of%20South%20Sudan%20_%20progress%2C%20gaps%20and%20priorities%20for%20investment.pdf>).
