# Zimbabwe

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The Global Fund case study says the Government of Zimbabwe and partners have strengthened the national health management information system for routine reporting of key health metrics, including HIV, TB and malaria, with data used at national and sub-national levels](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/qksld2tg/publication_zimbabwe-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [The Countdown to 2030 synthesis report analyses national health facility data from DHIS2 for 2019-2024 across provinces and districts, and refers to the MoHCC HMIS](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Zimbabwe-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).

**Linked.** [The DHIS2 Community of Practice post says the Impilo electronic health record, deployed across over 700 facilities, feeds DHIS2 in real time over FHIR, reporting over 150 indicators and 15 facility registers](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/integration-and-interoperability-of-impilo-electronic-health-record-ehr-system-with-dhis2/64859>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The Countdown to 2030 report says the national reporting rate for 2024 was above 92%, with only Harare and Bulawayo provinces just below the threshold](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Zimbabwe-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).
