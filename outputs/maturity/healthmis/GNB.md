# Guinea-Bissau

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 says it has been Guinea-Bissau's national health management information system since 2011](<https://dhis2.org/hisp-to-strengthen-guinea-bissaus-national-dhis2-system/>). [INASA manages it](<https://dhis2.org/hisp-to-strengthen-guinea-bissaus-national-dhis2-system/>). [The DHIS2 country profile says it covers routine data on a range of health programmes](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>). [DHIS2 and HISP UiO say climate data will be added to the national HMIS under the Global Fund Climate x Health Catalytic Fund](<https://dhis2.org/global-fund-climate-health-catalytic/>). [The DHIS2 country profile dates its use as the national HMIS from 2013](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>).

**Linked.** [The DHIS2 country profile says Guinea-Bissau also uses DHIS2 for disease surveillance and ITN campaign planning](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>). [DHIS2 Tracker programmes serve as electronic registries for maternal and child health and immunisation](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 DHIS2 said its platform had been the national health information system since 2013](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>).
