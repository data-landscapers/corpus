# Guinea-Bissau

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [DHIS2 has been Guinea-Bissau's national health management information system since 2011, managed by INASA and including data from a wider range of health programs, according to DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/hisp-to-strengthen-guinea-bissaus-national-dhis2-system/>). [The DHIS2 country profile says DHIS2 has been used as Guinea Bissau's national health management information system since 2013, covering routine data on a range of health programs](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>). [DHIS2 and HISP UiO say Guinea-Bissau, among seven countries, has an existing national Health Management Information System into which climate data will be integrated under the Global Fund Climate x Health Catalytic Fund](<https://dhis2.org/global-fund-climate-health-catalytic/>).

**Linked.** [The DHIS2 country profile says DHIS2 is also used in Guinea Bissau for disease surveillance and ITN campaign planning, and DHIS2 Tracker programs serve as electronic registries for maternal and child health and immunization](<https://dhis2.org/climate/country-profiles/guinea-bissau/>).
