# Niger

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Data.FI Project says several digital health platforms are now connected to Niger's national DHIS2-based system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [The health minister said health centres, districts and hospitals enter notifiable-disease data weekly into DHIS2](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>). [A Niamey regional director says health workers use DHIS2 regularly as part of the national HMIS](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>). [The Ministry of Health's Directorate of Statistics hosts it](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>).

**Linked.** [Data.FI Project says a national interoperability layer based on OpenFn links three health platforms to the national DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [Data move between them automatically](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [The minister said data from the Estelab disease surveillance platform now reach DHIS2 automatically](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>).
