# Niger

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [Data.FI Project refers to the national DHIS2-based health information system of Niger, to which several digital health platforms have now been connected](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [The health minister said health agents in integrated health centres, health districts and hospitals entered notifiable-disease data weekly into DHIS2 (ANP)](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>). [DHIS2 is used by health workers regularly as part of Niger's national routine health management information system, hosted in the Ministry of Health Directorate of Statistics (DHIS2 article, quoting the Niamey Public Health Regional Director)](<https://dhis2.org/niger-meningitis-vaccination/>).

**Linked.** [Data.FI Project says a national interoperability layer based on OpenFn enables automated data exchange between three health platforms and the national DHIS2](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>). [The minister said that via the national interoperability layer data from the Estelab disease surveillance platform now transfers automatically to DHIS2 without human intervention (ANP)](<https://anp.ne/sante-numerique-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-de-la-feuille-de-route-budgetisee-du-schema-directeur-national-de-la-e-sante-2025-2029/>).

**Reporting rate published.** No source found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting completeness or timeliness. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/data-fi-project_niger-dhis2-interoperability-activity-7493677620434288642-RoBC>).
