# Rwanda

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The DPGA report says Rwanda adopted DHIS2 in 2012 and it covers 700+ facilities](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/dpgs-for-dpi-building-open-digital-states.pdf>). [The DPGA blog says data completeness rose from 88% to 95%](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/blog/building-open-digital-states-dpgs-create-value-dpi>). [The Ministry of Health's Health Intelligence Center takes in DHIS2, its HMIS, among its sources](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [Its data run from village health workers to national referral hospitals](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>).

**Linked.** [The ministry says the Health Intelligence Center joins DHIS2 with eLMIS, CRVS and medical records](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [It uses the data for disease surveillance and outbreak early warning](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [The Global Fund says the community system eCHIS is linked to the national HMIS](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). [Laboratory systems also exchange data with it](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The DPGA report says DHIS2 data completeness rose from 88% to 95% and timeliness from 60% to 90%+](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/dpgs-for-dpi-building-open-digital-states.pdf>). [The DPGA blog says DHIS2 data completeness rose from 88% to 95% and timeliness from 60% to 90%+](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/blog/building-open-digital-states-dpgs-create-value-dpi>).
