# Rwanda

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [The DPGA report says Rwanda adopted DHIS2 in 2012 and it covers 700+ facilities](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/dpgs-for-dpi-building-open-digital-states.pdf>). [The DPGA blog says Rwanda's DHIS2 improved data completeness from 88% to 95% and timeliness from 60% to 90%+](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/blog/building-open-digital-states-dpgs-create-value-dpi>). [The Ministry of Health says its national Health Intelligence Center takes in DHIS2, described as the Health Management Information System, among its sources, with data from village health workers to national referral hospitals](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>).

**Linked.** [The Ministry of Health says the Health Intelligence Center integrates DHIS2 with eLMIS (logistics), CRVS, eBuzima and cEMR medical records, and uses the data for disease surveillance and outbreak early warning](<https://www.moh.gov.rw/news-detail/new-health-intelligence-center-to-drive-real-time-evidence-based-decisions>). [The Global Fund says the community health information system eCHIS is linked to the national HMIS for malaria, TB and HIV surveillance, and laboratory systems are interoperable with it](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** [The DPGA report says DHIS2 data completeness rose from 88% to 95% and timeliness from 60% to 90%+](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/dpgs-for-dpi-building-open-digital-states.pdf>). [The DPGA blog says DHIS2 data completeness rose from 88% to 95% and timeliness from 60% to 90%+](<https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/blog/building-open-digital-states-dpgs-create-value-dpi>).
