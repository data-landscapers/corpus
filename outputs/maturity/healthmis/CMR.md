# Cameroon

## Health information system

**Health information system.** [A 2026 preprint describes Cameroon's national DHIS2 instance](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11014186/v1>), into which facility or district staff enter monthly malaria reports. [It covers all ten regions and 202 health districts](<https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11014186/v1>), and its malaria section was changed in 2024. [The health ministry's 2022 bulletin reported weekly facility reports from across the regions received in DHIS2](<http://onsp.minsante.cm/sites/default/files/publications/345/20230320_BEC2022_FR.pdf>).

**Linked.** Nothing found since October 2023 names a link to surveillance, the supply chain or a register. The nearest held fact: [the 2022 bulletin, which reports 94% of detected surveillance signals entered in DHIS2](<http://onsp.minsante.cm/sites/default/files/publications/345/20230320_BEC2022_FR.pdf>).

**Reporting rate published.** Nothing found since October 2023 gives the system's reporting rate. The nearest held fact: [the 2022 bulletin's DHIS2 completeness of 86.25% and timeliness of 76.6%](<http://onsp.minsante.cm/sites/default/files/publications/345/20230320_BEC2022_FR.pdf>), up from 77.37% and 62.1% in 2021.
