# Nigeria

## Business register

**Online registration.** No source held says a digital business register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [Registrar-General Hussaini Magaji says Nigeria operates a CAC beneficial ownership register, with free access to the data, used by ICPC, EFCC and NDLEA to trace ownership structures](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Shared number.** No source held since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [Registrar-General Hussaini Magaji says Nigeria operates a CAC beneficial ownership register, with free access to the data, used by ICPC, EFCC and NDLEA to trace ownership structures](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>). [The OGP Independent Reporting Mechanism says Nigeria launched its Beneficial Ownership Register in 2023, accessible to government institutions, CSOs, journalists and citizens, and used by regulatory and enforcement agencies](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/nigeria-results-report-2023-2025/>).
