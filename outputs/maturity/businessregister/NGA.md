# Nigeria

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Registrar-General said in February 2026 that the Corporate Affairs Commission runs a fully digital, end-to-end registry open around the clock, receiving close to 10,000 registration requests a day](<https://punchng.com/cac-processes-10000-daily-registrations-after-ai-rollout/>). [The Commission says companies register on its Company Registration Portal](<https://guardian.ng/news/cac-warns-newly-registered-companies-against-paying-for-tin-others/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [Registrar-General Hussaini Magaji says Nigeria operates a CAC beneficial ownership register, with free access to the data, used by ICPC, EFCC and NDLEA to trace ownership structures](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>). [The OGP Independent Reporting Mechanism says Nigeria launched its Beneficial Ownership Register in 2023, accessible to government institutions, CSOs, journalists and citizens, and used by regulatory and enforcement agencies](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/nigeria-results-report-2023-2025/>).

***Also on file***. [NEPZA free trade zone beneficial ownership information](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>); [NEITI extractive industries beneficial ownership information](<https://von.gov.ng/cac-seeks-unified-beneficial-ownership-register/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. Nothing found says the beneficial owners of every company are recorded.
