# Liberia

## Business register

**Online registration.** In August 2026 the Ministry of Commerce, through the Liberia Business Registry, [launched a business registration digitalisation and decentralisation project in Ganta, Nimba County](<https://dnnewsliberia.com/liberia-business-registry-launches-digitalization-and-decentralization-project-in-nimba/>). The project is piloted in seven counties. The ministry says full digital filing will follow, so that firms need not travel to Monrovia. Later that month the deputy commerce minister [listed online business registration among the reforms of the Liberia Investment, Finance and Trade Project](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-digital-collateral-registry-business-credit/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest fact is [the August 2026 launch of registration in the counties](<https://dnnewsliberia.com/liberia-business-registry-launches-digitalization-and-decentralization-project-in-nimba/>).
