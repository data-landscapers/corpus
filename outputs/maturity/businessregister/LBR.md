# Liberia

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Deputy Commerce Minister Wilmot Reeves said online business registration is among the reforms under the Liberia Investment, Finance and Trade Project, alongside the new collateral registry](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/liberia-digital-collateral-registry-business-credit/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.
