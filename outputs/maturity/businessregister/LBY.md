# Libya

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In November 2026 the Ministry of Economy and Trade began a pilot of electronic registration for branches of foreign companies](<https://www.alwasat.ly/news/485008>). Full operation is to follow. [In July 2026 the head of the Commercial Registry Authority said the plan was still being completed](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=361401&lang=ar>). The plan links all regional branches electronically and archives company files. [In September 2025 the economy minister reviewed the registry's electronic system](<https://libyaobserver.ly/inbrief/economy-minister-reviews-digital-transformation-and-e-payment-project>). It brings together data on public, private and joint companies and foreign branches. [In February 2023 a UNCTAD-built e-single window for company registration was being developed](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/commercial-registry-and-general-information-authority-receive-unctad-training-on-digital-transformation>). Online trade-name search was the first of its seven procedures.
