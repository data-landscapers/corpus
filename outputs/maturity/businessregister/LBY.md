# Libya

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Libya Observer reports the economy minister and the head of the Commercial Registry Authority reviewed the electronic system for the commercial registry, including integration of data on public, private and joint companies and foreign branches](<https://libyaobserver.ly/inbrief/economy-minister-reviews-digital-transformation-and-e-payment-project>). [The Libya Herald reports a UNCTAD-built e-single window for company registration, of which online trade-name search is the first of seven procedures, is being developed with EU and Expertise France support (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/commercial-registry-and-general-information-authority-receive-unctad-training-on-digital-transformation>). [The Libya Herald reports the Tripoli Ministry of Economy launched its electronic commercial (company) registry system, linked to regional registers (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://libyaherald.com/2021/02/tripoli-economy-ministry-launches-e-company-registry>).
