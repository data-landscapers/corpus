# Libya

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In September 2025 the economy minister and the Commercial Registry Authority reviewed the registry's electronic system](<https://libyaobserver.ly/inbrief/economy-minister-reviews-digital-transformation-and-e-payment-project>). [It brings together data on public, private and joint companies and foreign branches](<https://libyaobserver.ly/inbrief/economy-minister-reviews-digital-transformation-and-e-payment-project>). [In February 2023 a UNCTAD-built e-single window for company registration was being developed](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/commercial-registry-and-general-information-authority-receive-unctad-training-on-digital-transformation>). [The EU and Expertise France support it](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/commercial-registry-and-general-information-authority-receive-unctad-training-on-digital-transformation>). [Online trade-name search is the first of its seven procedures](<https://libyaherald.com/2023/02/commercial-registry-and-general-information-authority-receive-unctad-training-on-digital-transformation>). [In February 2021 the Tripoli Ministry of Economy launched an electronic company registry linked to regional registers](<https://libyaherald.com/2021/02/tripoli-economy-ministry-launches-e-company-registry>).
