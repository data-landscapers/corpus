# Angola

## Business register

**Online registration.** [An executive decree of 4 December 2025 states that the Guiché Único da Empresa runs an online company-incorporation system with national reach](<https://lex.ao/docs/ministerio-da-justica-e-dos-direitos-humanos/2025/decreto-executivo-n-o-734-25-de-04-de-dezembro/>). Online incorporation through the counter's portal dates from 2020, when [an executive decree entered into force carrying company incorporation and registration certificates online at www.gue.gov.ao](<https://www.iflr.com/article/2gjw2n5ocpr2orkl4miq9/iflr1000/angola-regulations-on-online-commercial-registration-and-publications>), and [a presidential decree created a single incorporation form for companies formed there in person or online](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2020/decreto-presidencial-n-o-64-20-de-04-de-marco/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives a firm one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the December 2025 decree on the counter's online incorporation](<https://lex.ao/docs/ministerio-da-justica-e-dos-direitos-humanos/2025/decreto-executivo-n-o-734-25-de-04-de-dezembro/>), which says nothing of tax numbers.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact is [the same decree](<https://lex.ao/docs/ministerio-da-justica-e-dos-direitos-humanos/2025/decreto-executivo-n-o-734-25-de-04-de-dezembro/>), which concerns which registry office handles commercial registration.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security, or that the register collects beneficial owners.
