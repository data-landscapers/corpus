# Burundi

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Agence de Développement du Burundi describes company and sole-trader registration, with commercial register fees of 100,000 FBU for a person and 200,000 FBU for a company, done online through the easybusiness.bi platform](<https://investburundi.bi/procedure-de-la-creation/>). [The Burundi Development Agency, with UNCTAD and the World Bank Group, launched Easybusiness.bi, a fully online single-window platform for business registration](<https://www.businessfacilitation.org/academy/projects/burundi/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
