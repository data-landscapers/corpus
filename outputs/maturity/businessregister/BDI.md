# Burundi

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In July 2024 the Agence de Développement du Burundi described company and sole-trader registration done online through the easybusiness.bi platform](<https://investburundi.bi/procedure-de-la-creation/>). [Commercial register fees were 100,000 FBU for a person and 200,000 FBU for a company](<https://investburundi.bi/procedure-de-la-creation/>). [A 2026 project page says the Burundi Development Agency, with UNCTAD and the World Bank Group, launched Easybusiness.bi](<https://www.businessfacilitation.org/academy/projects/burundi/>). [It is a fully online single-window platform for business registration](<https://www.businessfacilitation.org/academy/projects/burundi/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [Easybusiness.bi is a single window for business registration](<https://www.businessfacilitation.org/academy/projects/burundi/>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [companies and sole traders register on the commercial register](<https://investburundi.bi/procedure-de-la-creation/>).
