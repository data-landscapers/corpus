# Gambia

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In 2026 the Single Window Registry portal listed digital services for registering a sole proprietorship, company, partnership, external company and non-profit](<https://easybusiness.gov.gm/>). [It also lists user accounts and trading licences](<https://easybusiness.gov.gm/>). [In August 2026 Minister Ceesay said the government is finalising the Digital Single Window Business Registration System](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/trans-gambia-economic-zone-set-to-create-3400-jobs-as-investor-interest-grows>). [It is meant to simplify registration and encourage formalisation, especially outside the Greater Banjul Area](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/trans-gambia-economic-zone-set-to-create-3400-jobs-as-investor-interest-grows>).
