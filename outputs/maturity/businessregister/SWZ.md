# Eswatini

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Eswatini Trade Portal sets out company registration as six steps, from name search to certificate](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=23&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>). Each is done online through the government portal online.gov.sz and processed within a day. [In January 2026 the Commerce Minister said the business registry had been digitised and a Business One-Stop Shop set up](<https://times.co.sz/29818/business/eswatinis-business-reforms-deliver-top-10-b-ready-ranking/>). The minister said this cut the time and cost of registering. [The ICT ministry says its Government-In-Your-Hand app, launched in May 2025, lets entrepreneurs register and manage businesses from home](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>). [The commerce ministry's report for the quarter to September 2026 says 632 applicants used online company registration](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/digital-company-registrations-plunge-70/>), against 2,127 a year earlier.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the minister's January 2026 account of the one-stop shop](<https://times.co.sz/29818/business/eswatinis-business-reforms-deliver-top-10-b-ready-ranking/>), which does not mention tax numbers.
