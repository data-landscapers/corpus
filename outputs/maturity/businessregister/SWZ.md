# Eswatini

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Eswatini Trade Portal sets out company registration as six steps, from name search to certificate, done online through the government portal online.gov.sz and processed within a day](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=23&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>). [In January 2026 the Commerce Minister said the business registry had been digitised and a Business One-Stop Shop set up, cutting the time and cost of registering](<https://times.co.sz/29818/business/eswatinis-business-reforms-deliver-top-10-b-ready-ranking/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the minister's January 2026 account of the one-stop shop](<https://times.co.sz/29818/business/eswatinis-business-reforms-deliver-top-10-b-ready-ranking/>), which does not mention tax numbers.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact is [the trade portal's registration procedure](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=23&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>), which lists no ownership filing.
