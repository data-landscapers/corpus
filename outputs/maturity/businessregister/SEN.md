# Senegal

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Seninfogreffe, the commercial registry's online service, lets users carry out formalities online](<https://ods.sn/portfolio/projet-2/>). It gives access to register information on all companies and has digitised the archives. [In January 2025 the Economy Minister inaugurated a digital platform for obtaining the NINEA](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/senegal-launches-new-platform-to-streamline-business-registration>), to speed up business registration. [The investment agency's company-creation office formalises a firm in 48 hours](<https://investinsenegal.sn/services/creation-dentreprise/>). It brings the justice, finance, statistics and labour services together. [On 10 November 2026 the Justice Minister agreed to deploy the agency's digital company-creation platform in the courts](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/senegal-la-justice-accelere-la-digitalisation-des-entreprises/>). It is to link the court registries that keep the commercial register.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. [The company-creation office issues the commercial register entry and the tax identifier](<https://investinsenegal.sn/services/creation-dentreprise/>) without saying they are one number.

**Beneficial owners.** [EITI's country page, updated in 2026, says the commercial register records beneficial ownership information, available on request to authorities, courts and people with a legitimate interest](<https://eiti.org/countries/senegal>). [The latest registry extract it cites covered 262 companies](<https://eiti.org/countries/senegal>).
