# Namibia

## Business register

**Online registration.** The Business and Intellectual Property Authority (BIPA) [takes company forms on paper at its offices](<https://www.bipa.na/business-registration/>), and records are fetched from off-site storage in three to five days. In December 2025 it [announced a N$40 million registration platform built by CSS-CA Technology](<https://theissue.com.na/namibia-wallows-in-digital-sovereignty-illusion/>), meant as a one-stop registration centre. In January 2026 BIPA [held the kick-off meeting with the developer](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/business-and-intellectual-property-authority-bipa_ibrs-bipa-innovation-activity-7419737447879602177-EQNb>), aiming for incorporation within 60 minutes. It has not gone live.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the January 2026 start of the new registration system](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/business-and-intellectual-property-authority-bipa_ibrs-bipa-innovation-activity-7419737447879602177-EQNb>).

**Beneficial owners.** The deputy trade minister [told the National Assembly that BIPA has collected beneficial owners at registration since 7 August 2023](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Ministerial-Statement-on-BO-Penalties.pdf>). By 30 June 2024, 64,356 of the 221,338 entities on the register had filed them.

***Also on file***. [ITAS](<https://thebrief.com.na/2024/02/namra-bipa-explore-integrating-business-registration-and-tax-systems/>).

***Not found***. Online registration is not established: forms were filed on paper in 2024 and the new system began development in January 2026. Nothing found says registration gives one number shared with tax. Nothing found says the beneficial owners of every company are recorded.
