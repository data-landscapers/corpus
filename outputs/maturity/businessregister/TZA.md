# Tanzania

## Business register

**Online registration.** [BRELA, the business registrations and licensing agency, said in May 2026 that all new company registrations, annual returns, updates and closures now go through its upgraded BRELA Online Service](<https://dailynews.co.tz/brela-rolls-out-an-upgraded-online-system-to-speed-up-company-registration/>). [Its service page sets out a ten-step online company registration, paid by mobile money or bank](<https://www.brela.go.tz/services/registration-of-companies>), and [the national identity authority lists online company and tax registration among the services using its identity checks](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the identity authority's 2025 listing of company and tax registration as separate services](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

**Beneficial owners.** [A BRELA public notice of 6 January 2026 says every registered company must file its beneficial owners through BRELA's electronic system at bo.brela.go.tz](<https://www.brela.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1767859137-TAARIFA%20KWA%20UMMA%20BO%20-%20BRELA%202.pdf>), and that companies which have not filed are barred from any transaction with the Registrar until they do.
