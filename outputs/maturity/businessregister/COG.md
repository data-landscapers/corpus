# Congo

## Business register

**Online registration.** [On 5 December 2025 the minister for small and medium enterprises launched an online company-creation platform in Brazzaville, at www.acpce.cg](<https://labreveonline.com/lancement-dune-plateforme-de-creation-dentreprise-en-ligne-au-congo/>). [The ACPCE director general said some promoters created their companies online during the launch](<https://labreveonline.com/lancement-dune-plateforme-de-creation-dentreprise-en-ligne-au-congo/>). [The platform launched by Minister Lydia Mikolo is 100 percent online: entrepreneurs file, track and pay fees there](<https://24newsagency.com/brazzaville-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-creation-dentreprises-en-ligne-du-ministere-des-pme-et-de-lartisana/>). [More than 5,300 firms were created in 2025](<https://24newsagency.com/brazzaville-lancement-officiel-de-la-plateforme-de-creation-dentreprises-en-ligne-du-ministere-des-pme-et-de-lartisana/>). [In January 2026 RFI reported that a company can now be created in 24 hours from anywhere](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/podcasts/afrique-%C3%A9conomie/20251231-congo-brazzaville-cr%C3%A9er-une-entreprise-en-quelques-clics>).
