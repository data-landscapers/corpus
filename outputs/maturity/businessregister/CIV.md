# Cote d'Ivoire

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CEPICI states that firms can be created online through the E-Entreprise platform on the 225Invest portal, where creators complete their formalities](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>). [CEPICI says it launched the online company-creation service E-ENTREPRISE in October 2018, via the 225invest.ci portal (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.225invest.ci/fr/actualite/details_actualite.xhtml?id=706>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared number.** [CEPICI states that since 2018 the Identifiant Unique (IDU) is operational, one number registering a firm with the commercial register, the tax authority and the social security fund (CNPS) (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source held since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security.
