# Cote d'Ivoire

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CEPICI states that firms can be created online through the E-Entreprise platform on the 225Invest portal, where creators complete their formalities](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>). [CEPICI launched the online company-creation service E-ENTREPRISE in October 2018, on the 225invest.ci portal](<https://www.225invest.ci/fr/actualite/details_actualite.xhtml?id=706>), and the agency's current reform page presents the same service as the way a firm is created.

**Shared number.** [CEPICI's reform page says the Identifiant Unique (IDU), operational since 2018, is now the one number a firm needs for all its dealings with the administrations, and that with it the firm is registered at once with the commercial register, the tax authority and the social security fund (CNPS)](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>). The number is issued at creation, so the register, the tax office and CNPS hold the firm under one identifier.

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing held says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [CEPICI's account of what the Identifiant Unique registers](<https://cepici.africa/reforme>), which names the commercial register, tax and social security and not ownership.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners.
