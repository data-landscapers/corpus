# Ghana

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Registrar-General officials told AmCham Ghana that an end-to-end online business registration platform, from name search to certification and integrated with national identification, is currently being piloted](<https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/23/amcham-ghana-engages-registrar-general-on-digital-transformation-and-business-registration-reforms/>). [At the launch of the CitizenApp Data Interoperability System (CADIS) on 7 October 2024, Vice President Mahamudu Bawumia listed business registration among the services the app gives access to](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>). The app verifies users with the Ghana Card and a phone number, and its other listed services include birth and death registration, passport applications, tax filing and vehicle registration. The Registrar-General's officials described their own end-to-end platform as a pilot nearly two years later.

**Shared number.** No source held since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security.
