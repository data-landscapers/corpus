# Ghana

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Registrar-General officials told AmCham Ghana in July 2026 that an end-to-end online business registration platform, from name search to certificate and linked to national identification, was being piloted](<https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/23/amcham-ghana-engages-registrar-general-on-digital-transformation-and-business-registration-reforms/>). A year earlier [the acting Registrar of Companies said the office was testing a platform for registering businesses remotely, aiming to roll it out by the end of 2025](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/08/orc-to-implement-key-reforms-toreset-ghanas-business-environment/>), and [EITI said a new digital registration system was expected to be fully working in 2026](<https://eiti.org/impact-story/ghana-unlocking-ownership-data-accountability-extractive-sector>). In October 2024 [the Vice President listed business registration among the services on the CADIS app](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the July 2026 account of the pilot platform's link to national identification](<https://amchamghana.org/2026/07/23/amcham-ghana-engages-registrar-general-on-digital-transformation-and-business-registration-reforms/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [EITI says 175,984 companies had filed beneficial ownership data with the Office of the Registrar of Companies by 30 September 2025, up from 24,256 in 2021](<https://eiti.org/impact-story/ghana-unlocking-ownership-data-accountability-extractive-sector>). [An assessment reported in March 2025 found the information held in the Registrar's central register, available to authorities and the public on request](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-be-diligent-with-financial-accounts-of-businesses-chraj-to-registrar-of-companies.html>).

***Also on file***. [Ekumfi District Assembly business database](<https://edacentral.gov.gh/ekumfi-district-assembly-launches-major-business-registration-drive-across-all-communities-in-the-district/>).

***Not found***. Online business registration in service is not established: the Registrar-General called its end-to-end platform a pilot in July 2026. Nothing found says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. Nothing found says the beneficial owners of every company are recorded.
