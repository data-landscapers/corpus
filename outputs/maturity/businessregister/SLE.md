# Sierra Leone

## Business register

**Online registration.** In November 2026 the National Investment Board [demonstrated its newly developed Online Business Registration System](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-nib-online-business-registration/>) to the communications minister. It is designed to let applicants reserve a name, register, upload documents and print certificates online. In October 2026 the Chief Minister said [the board's one-stop digital business platform would be operational by November 2026](<https://ayvnews.com/sengeh-highlights-bios-business-transformation/>). In May 2026 Parliament [ratified a 15-year concession for a private firm to build and run the system](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>). When the WanGov app was launched in February 2025, [the ministry said it offered business registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. In November 2026 the board was [working with the civil registration authority to bring the National Identification Number into registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-nib-online-business-registration/>).

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the concession for the Online Business Registration System](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>).
