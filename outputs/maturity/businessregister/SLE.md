# Sierra Leone

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation says the launched WanGov app offers businesses services including business registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>). [Chief Minister Sengeh said the National Investment Board's One-Stop Digital Business Service Platform, built through a joint venture, is nearly complete and expected to be operational by November 2026, cutting business registration from about 24 hours to minutes](<https://ayvnews.com/sengeh-highlights-bios-business-transformation/>). [Parliament ratified a 15-year build-operate-transfer concession under which Multi GIS E-Services SL Ltd will develop, operate and maintain the Online Business Registration System, per Sierraloaded](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>).
