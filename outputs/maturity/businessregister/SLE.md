# Sierra Leone

## Business register

**Online registration.** On 10 November 2026 President Bio [launched the National Investment Board's Online Business Registration System](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1111-60716-sierra-leone-digitizes-business-registration-as-75-of-firms-remain-unregistered>). Applicants can apply, upload documents, track their file and obtain certificates online. The board says [records it held before have been moved into the system](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/bio-launches-online-business-registration/>). In May 2026 Parliament [ratified a 15-year concession for a private firm to build and run it](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>). When the WanGov app was launched in February 2025, [the ministry said it offered business registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. In November 2026 the board was [working with the civil registration authority to bring the National Identification Number into registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-nib-online-business-registration/>).

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest fact on file is [the launch of the online system](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/bio-launches-online-business-registration/>).
