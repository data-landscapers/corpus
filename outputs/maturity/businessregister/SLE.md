# Sierra Leone

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Chief Minister Sengeh said in October 2026 that the National Investment Board's One-Stop Digital Business Service Platform, built through a joint venture, is nearly complete and expected to be operational by November 2026, cutting business registration from about 24 hours to minutes](<https://ayvnews.com/sengeh-highlights-bios-business-transformation/>). [In May 2026 Parliament ratified a 15-year build-operate-transfer concession under which Multi GIS E-Services SL Ltd will develop, operate and maintain the Online Business Registration System](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>). [When the WanGov app was launched in February 2025, the communications ministry said it offered businesses services including business registration](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [the Chief Minister's account of the platform](<https://ayvnews.com/sengeh-highlights-bios-business-transformation/>), which brings the registration unit and the investment agencies together under the NIB.

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the concession for the Online Business Registration System](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/parliament-online-business-registration/>).

***Also on file***. [WanGov](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>).

***Not found***. Online registration in service is not established: the WanGov app listed it in February 2025, but in 2026 the online registration system was still being built.
