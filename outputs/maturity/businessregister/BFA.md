# Burkina Faso

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CEFORE's portal, creerentreprise.me.bf, takes business-creation requests online, with the fee paid by Orange Money or Moov Money](<https://creerentreprise.me.bf/>). [It reports 6,916 requests from 1 January to 13 November 2026](<https://creerentreprise.me.bf/>). [The Ministry of Justice launched the e-RCCM platform on 15 December 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>), which issues register extracts, registration certificates and non-bankruptcy certificates online. [Libreinfo.net reports the same launch by the government](<https://libreinfo.net/burkina-trois-plateformes-numeriques-lancees-pour-faciliter-les-demarches-en-justice/>), with the register's documents obtainable online.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives a firm one number shared with tax or social security. [CEFORE's online portal](<https://creerentreprise.me.bf/>) takes requests and fees and names no tax number.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register records who owns a firm. [The e-RCCM platform launched in December 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>) issues certificates of registration and non-bankruptcy.
