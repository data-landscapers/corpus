# Burkina Faso

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Ministry of Justice launched the e-RCCM platform, e-actes-rccm.gov.bf, on 15 December 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>). Users can get register extracts, registration certificates and non-bankruptcy certificates online. [Libreinfo.net reported the same launch](<https://libreinfo.net/burkina-trois-plateformes-numeriques-lancees-pour-faciliter-les-demarches-en-justice/>), for the trade and movable credit register. The platform serves records already in the register. No source found says a firm can be created on it.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives a firm one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the December 2025 launch of e-RCCM](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>). It issues extracts and certificates and names no tax number.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. [The e-RCCM platform launched in December 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>) issues certificates of registration and non-bankruptcy. No source found says it records who owns a firm.
