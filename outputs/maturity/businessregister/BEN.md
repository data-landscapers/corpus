# Benin

## Business register

**Online registration.** [APIEx's guide says companies are registered in the RCCM entirely online through www.monentreprise.bj, with QR-coded electronic certificates issued in three working hours](<https://monentreprise.bj/APIEX_Guide_pratique_v2_3e1993847e.pdf>). [The government says online company creation is effective on monentreprise.bj through APIEx, with 59 firms created online on 3 March 2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/534/investir-au-benin-les-facilites-de-creation-dentreprise-exposees-aux-acteurs-du-prive/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared number.** [APIEx's guide lists among the certificates the platform issues the RCCM extract, the IFU tax identifier attestation and the CNSS employer number notification](<https://monentreprise.bj/APIEX_Guide_pratique_v2_3e1993847e.pdf>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source held since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://monentreprise.bj/APIEX_Guide_pratique_v2_3e1993847e.pdf>).
