# Algeria

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In 2026 the CNRC said a firm can register in the commerce register online, through its company-creation portal](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/creation-d-entreprise-personne-morale>). [Fees are paid online by CIB or Edahabia card](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/creation-d-entreprise-personne-morale>). [A firm can also ask online to reserve its name](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/creation-d-entreprise-personne-morale>). [The portal lets entrepreneurs create a company without visiting the administrations, with one registration to all of them](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).

**Shared number.** [In 2026 the CNRC said the single registration gives the firm a common identification number](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [It is issued within three days of approval by CNRC, the tax directorate (DGI), CNAS, CASNOS and ONS](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [the CNRC keeps the commerce register in which firms register online](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/creation-d-entreprise-personne-morale>).
