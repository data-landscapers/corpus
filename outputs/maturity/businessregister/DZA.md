# Algeria

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The CNRC's company-creation page says a firm's registration in the commerce register can be made online through its dedicated company-creation portal, with fees paid online by CIB or Edahabia card, and name reservation can also be requested online](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/creation-d-entreprise-personne-morale>). [The CNRC portal lets entrepreneurs create a company online without visiting the administrations, with a single registration to all of them](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared number.** [The CNRC says the single registration gives the firm a common identification number, issued within three days of approval by CNRC, the tax directorate (DGI), CNAS, CASNOS and ONS](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).
