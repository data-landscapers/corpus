# Uganda

## Business register

**Online registration.** In June 2026 the Uganda Registration Services Bureau [unveiled its e-Registry](<https://dailyexpress.co.ug/2026/06/30/ursb-launches-unified-e-registry-replacing-obrs/>), one online portal for business registration that replaced its earlier system. In November 2026 the bureau said [the e-Registry is running and a new URSB Connect app adds company and business-name registration](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/11/ursb-launches-mobile-app-for-business-registration-services.html>). The Auditor General found the [earlier online system, launched in December 2022, slow and prone to outages](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ursbs-online-business-registration-system-still-slow-despite-ugx-6-5b-investment/>) in the year to 2025. In May 2026 officials said [a central register giving each entity a unique registration number](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/centralised-digital-register-to-streamline-bu-NV_233277_052026>) was in final testing.
