# Uganda

## Business register

**Online registration.** In June 2026 the Uganda Registration Services Bureau [unveiled its e-Registry](<https://dailyexpress.co.ug/2026/06/30/ursb-launches-unified-e-registry-replacing-obrs/>). It is one online portal for business registration and replaced the earlier system. In November 2026 the bureau said [the e-Registry is running](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/11/ursb-launches-mobile-app-for-business-registration-services.html>). A new URSB Connect app adds company and business-name registration. The Auditor General found the [earlier online system slow and prone to outages](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ursbs-online-business-registration-system-still-slow-despite-ugx-6-5b-investment/>) in the year to 2025. In May 2026 officials said [a central register giving each entity a unique number](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/centralised-digital-register-to-streamline-bu-NV_233277_052026>) was in final testing.

**Shared number.** The revenue authority URA said in September 2026 that [the number URSB issues to a firm will serve as its tax identifier](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-37/>), under section 4 of the Tax Procedures Code Act. It said the move away from the separate tax number had begun. The old tax numbers stop working only at full rollout.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact is that [the e-Registry handles business registration](<https://dailyexpress.co.ug/2026/06/30/ursb-launches-unified-e-registry-replacing-obrs/>).
