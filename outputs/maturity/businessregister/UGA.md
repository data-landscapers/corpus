## Business register

**Online registration.** [URSB unveiled its e-Registry, an online platform replacing the Online Business Registration System and giving access to business registration services through a single portal](<https://dailyexpress.co.ug/2026/06/30/ursb-launches-unified-e-registry-replacing-obrs/>). [The Auditor General's report for the year ended 2025 describes URSB's Online Business Registration System, launched in December 2022, as a digital business registration platform that remains slow, with frequent outages](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ursbs-online-business-registration-system-still-slow-despite-ugx-6-5b-investment/>). [URSB's centralised digital Non-Individual Register, issuing each entity a unique Entity Registration Number, is in its final stages of testing with full rollout expected by June 2026, URSB officials say](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/centralised-digital-register-to-streamline-bu-NV_233277_052026>).
