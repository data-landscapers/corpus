# Ethiopia

## Business register

**Online registration.** In September 2025 [the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration, with GIZ, launched the Integrated Company Creation Journey](<https://birrmetrics.com/motri-launches-online-business-registration-and-licensing-platform/>). [It is a fully digital system for registration and licensing, cutting 11 steps over 32 days to two steps in two days](<https://birrmetrics.com/motri-launches-online-business-registration-and-licensing-platform/>). In June 2026 [the ministry moved import-export registration to the Online Trade Registration and Licensing System, which links 745 registration offices](<https://birrmetrics.com/import-export-licensing-centralized-as-government-targets-regional-loopholes/>). In October 2026 [the ministry reported 60,409 new business registrations among 1,088,787 services delivered online over three months](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9819771>).
