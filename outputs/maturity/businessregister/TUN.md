# Tunisia

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Registre National des Entreprises completed its full digitisation in July 2026, so filing, registration and updates can all be done remotely on its platform](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/16/depot-immatriculation-mise-a-jour-le-registre-national-des-entreprises-digitalise-tous-ses-services/>), and [it took no more paper at its counters from 1 July 2026](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>). [A ministerial council on 15 August 2026 counted the full digitisation of the register's dealings with firms among the projects completed that half-year](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>). In April 2025 [its director general said more than 65,000 firms used its online company creation and filing of accounts](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/04/18/le-rne-accelere-la-digitalisation-des-entreprises-tunisiennes/>).

**Shared number.** [The register gives each firm the unique identifier required for all its administrative and tax procedures](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/04/18/le-rne-accelere-la-digitalisation-des-entreprises-tunisiennes/>), and [ties each firm's electronic account to that identifier, the matricule fiscal](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [The register also manages the register of beneficial owners](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/16/depot-immatriculation-mise-a-jour-le-registre-national-des-entreprises-digitalise-tous-ses-services/>). [A government order of 13 January 2026 sets how it makes that information available in real time to competent authorities, through its platform to firms with checking duties, and to the public on a legitimate interest](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Arrete2026_35.pdf>).
