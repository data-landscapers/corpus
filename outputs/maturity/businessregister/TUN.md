# Tunisia

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Registre National des Entreprises finished going digital in July 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/16/depot-immatriculation-mise-a-jour-le-registre-national-des-entreprises-digitalise-tous-ses-services/>), so filing, registration and updates can all be done remotely. [It took no more paper at its counters from 1 July 2026](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>). [A ministerial council on 15 August 2026 listed this among the half-year's completed projects](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>). In April 2025 [its director general said more than 65,000 firms used its online company creation and filing of accounts](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/04/18/le-rne-accelere-la-digitalisation-des-entreprises-tunisiennes/>). [In November 2026 the ICT minister said the business licence could be declared online from August](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/06/sofiene-hemissi-annonce-la-numerisation-de-plusieurs-services-administratifs-avant-fin-2026/>).

**Shared number.** [The register gives each firm the unique identifier it needs for all its administrative and tax procedures](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/04/18/le-rne-accelere-la-digitalisation-des-entreprises-tunisiennes/>). [It ties each firm's electronic account to that number, the matricule fiscal](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [The register also keeps the register of beneficial owners](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/16/depot-immatriculation-mise-a-jour-le-registre-national-des-entreprises-digitalise-tous-ses-services/>). [A government order of 13 January 2026 sets how it shares that data](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Arrete2026_35.pdf>). Competent authorities see it in real time, firms with checking duties through its platform, and the public on a legitimate interest.
