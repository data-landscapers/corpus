# Tunisia

## Business register

**Online registration.** [A ministerial council on 15 August 2026 reviewed projects completed in the first half of 2026, including the complete digitisation of transactions between the Registre national des entreprises and economic operators](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/conseil-ministeriel-restreint-consacre-au-suivi-de-lavancement-des-projets-de-transformation-numerique/>). [Nessma TV reports the Ministry of Employment launched the national register of community companies, a digital platform for creating and following them, accessible from that day at alahlia.tn](<https://www.nessma.tv/fr/economie/actu/lancement-du-registre-national-des-societes-communautaires-pour-impulser-le-developpement-local/560812>). [The Registre National des Entreprises says it is moving to full digitalisation, with all procedures online only from 1 July 2026 and no more physical filing at counters](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

**Shared number.** [The Registre National des Entreprises says its electronic safe account for each registered firm is tied to the firm's unique identifier, the matricule fiscal](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source held since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [e-industrie.gov.tn](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/28/tunisie-lancement-dune-plateforme-numerique-unifiee-pour-les-services-du-ministere-de-lindustrie/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners.
