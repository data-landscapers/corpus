# Malawi

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Minister of Justice said in May 2026 that the Malawi Business Registration System lets businesses register and keep their records online](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>), and the registry estimates that about 20,000 companies register each year. [President Chakwera launched the system on 29 February 2024, saying it would streamline business registration](<https://www.247malawi.com/chakwera-launches-land-and-business-systems-a-leap-forward-in-governance/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the minister's May 2026 account of online registration](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>), which does not mention tax numbers.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners in service. The nearest held fact is [the same May 2026 account](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>), which covers registration and record-keeping only.
