# Malawi

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Minister of Justice said in May 2026 that the Malawi Business Registration System lets firms register and keep their records online](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>). The registry puts new companies at about 20,000 a year. [President Chakwera launched the system on 29 February 2024](<https://www.247malawi.com/chakwera-launches-land-and-business-systems-a-leap-forward-in-governance/>), to streamline registration. [The state broadcaster said it would let people register, update records and get certificates online](<https://mbc.mw/all-set-for-launch-of-lims-mbrs/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the minister's May 2026 account of online registration](<https://mwnation.com/minister-of-justice-urges-professionalism/>), which does not mention tax numbers.
