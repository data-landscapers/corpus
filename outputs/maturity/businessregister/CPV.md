# Cape Verde

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In March 2024 Lusa reported a government tender for an internet portal covering the whole life cycle of a company](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/atualidade/cabo-verde-prepara-portal-para-criacao-de-empresas-na-internet/>). [Its first phase was due by the end of that year, with links to INPS, IGT, INCV and Pro-Empresa](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/atualidade/cabo-verde-prepara-portal-para-criacao-de-empresas-na-internet/>). [In April 2026 the Ministry of Justice's Portaria 33/2026 set fees for incorporating, amending and dissolving companies electronically](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739>). [The fees are paid only online, through the single Plataforma Empresa Online](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739>). [The portaria takes effect when the platform becomes publicly available](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739>). [In 2026 the government's Plataforma Empresa Online (PEO) offered company creation online as one package](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). [The package includes formalisation with the Commercial Registry (DGRNI)](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). [The PEO also takes changes to company data and requests for dissolution](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>).

**Shared number.** [In 2026 the PEO package covered commercial registry formalisation, a Tax Identification Number (NIF) from DNRE and enrolment with social security (INPS)](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). [All three are done in one process](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [companies are incorporated, amended and dissolved through the Plataforma Empresa Online](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739>).
