## Business register

**Online registration.** [Portaria 33/2026 of the Ministry of Justice sets fees for incorporating, amending and dissolving companies electronically through the single Plataforma Empresa Online, payable only online, in force on the platform's public availability](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=100739>). [The Plataforma Empresa Online (PEO), of the Cabo Verde government, offers company creation online as one package, including formalisation with the Commercial Registry (DGRNI), plus changes to company data and requests for dissolution](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
