# Djibouti

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Prime Minister says a person can create a company entirely online on the platform launched on 26 January 2026](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>). [The entreprendre.dj guide says firms register in the commercial register through the ANPI one-stop counter](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). [It takes 72 hours and costs 23,000 FDJ](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). [In April 2018 Decree 2018-146 made the ANPI one-stop shop the sole place for company creation](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2018-146-pr-mapci-portant-reglementation-des-formalites-de-creation-dentreprises/>). [It runs on an integrated information system with a single payment](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2018-146-pr-mapci-portant-reglementation-des-formalites-de-creation-dentreprises/>). [Registrations are published on the ODPIC and one-stop websites](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2018-146-pr-mapci-portant-reglementation-des-formalites-de-creation-dentreprises/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest fact: [from 26 January 2026 a person could create a company entirely online](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest fact: [the online company platform was launched on 26 January 2026](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>).
