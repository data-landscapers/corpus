# Djibouti

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Prime Minister states that with the platform launched on 26 January 2026 a person can create a company entirely online](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>). [The entreprendre.dj guide says firms register in the commercial register (RCS) through the ANPI one-stop counter (Guichet Unique), in 72 hours at 23,000 FDJ; it does not say registration is online](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). [Decree 2018-146 makes the ANPI one-stop shop the sole place for company creation, required to run on an integrated information system and single payment, with registrations published on the ODPIC and one-stop websites (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2018-146-pr-mapci-portant-reglementation-des-formalites-de-creation-dentreprises/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://primature.gouv.dj/discours-du-premier-ministre-9/>).
