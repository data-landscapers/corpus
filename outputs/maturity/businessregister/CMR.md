# Cameroon

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In April 2024 OHADA reported an AFD-financed project to computerise the national commerce and movable credit register (RCCM) in Cameroon](<https://www.ohada.org/informatisation-du-rccm-cameroun-les-parties-prenantes-font-le-point/>). [It also covers 30 sites, the greffes and business formality centres](<https://www.ohada.org/informatisation-du-rccm-cameroun-les-parties-prenantes-font-le-point/>). [Equipment had been acquired, sites wired, and administrators and users trained](<https://www.ohada.org/informatisation-du-rccm-cameroun-les-parties-prenantes-font-le-point/>). [Official handover to the state was planned for June 2024](<https://www.ohada.org/informatisation-du-rccm-cameroun-les-parties-prenantes-font-le-point/>).
