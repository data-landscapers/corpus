# Sao Tome and Principe

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The registries directorate (DGRN) presents a one-stop company counter, the Guiche Unico para Empresa, which handles company formation, alteration and dissolution, branch registration and sole-trader registration, with its fees set in dobras](<https://dgrn.gov.st/reg_gue.php>). [Forming a company there takes three days at the normal fee of 5,190 dobras, or one day at the urgent fee of 10,190 dobras, paid into the counter's account](<https://dgrn.gov.st/reg_gue.php>). The page describes a counter that firms attend, and says nothing of registering online.

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [the DGRN's description of the one-stop counter](<https://dgrn.gov.st/reg_gue.php>).

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the same page](<https://dgrn.gov.st/reg_gue.php>), which lists the counter's registration services.
