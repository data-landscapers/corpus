# Equatorial Guinea

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In July 2024 Real Equatorial Guinea reported the commerce minister demanding faster incorporation at the Ventanilla Unica Empresarial](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/gobierno-muestra-su-descontento-por-la-tardanza-para-crear-una-empresa-en-la-ventanilla-unica-empresarial/>). [It is the country's one-stop company-creation counter](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/gobierno-muestra-su-descontento-por-la-tardanza-para-crear-una-empresa-en-la-ventanilla-unica-empresarial/>). [The minister asked for incorporation in three days at most](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/destacado/politica/gobierno-muestra-su-descontento-por-la-tardanza-para-crear-una-empresa-en-la-ventanilla-unica-empresarial/>).
