# Lesotho

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In November 2025 CAFI's managing director, Chaba Mokuku, spoke on a Business Facilitation Single Window](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). [It had been deployed at the One Stop Business Facilitation Centre (OBFC)](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). [It is to simplify registration and licensing, and was due to launch by early 2026](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>). [The centre, in the trade ministry, helps businesses register with Revenue Services Lesotho](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>). [Companies register first with the relevant ministries and then obtain a TIN from RSL](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest fact: [the single window covers business registration and licensing](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest fact: [in November 2025 the single window was deployed but not yet launched](<https://lestimes.com/?p=89016>).
