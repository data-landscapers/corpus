# Comoros

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In January 2026 the national investment promotion agency, ANPI, was about to launch the Guichet générique diaspora, a platform built with the French development agency and Expertise France](<https://www.comoresinfos.net/bientot-depuis-letranger-la-diaspora-pourra-ouvrir-son-entreprise-aux-comores/>) so that Comorians abroad can assemble a company file and send documents online. Most of the procedure moves online, but the official signing of the deeds and entry in the commercial register still take place in person in the Comoros.

**Shared number.** No source found says registration gives a firm one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the January 2026 report on the diaspora platform](<https://www.comoresinfos.net/bientot-depuis-letranger-la-diaspora-pourra-ouvrir-son-entreprise-aux-comores/>), which does not mention tax registration.

**Beneficial owners.** No source found says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact is [the same report](<https://www.comoresinfos.net/bientot-depuis-letranger-la-diaspora-pourra-ouvrir-son-entreprise-aux-comores/>), which covers company creation only.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says firms can register fully online; the 2026 platform prepares the file online, but signing and the commercial-register entry stay in person.
