# Madagascar

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The online company-creation platform Orinasa has been accessible in Manakara since July 2025, per the head of the EDBM antenna there](<https://newsmada.com/2026/05/26/investissement-prive-ledbm-sinstalle-a-manakara/>). [EDBM opened that regional antenna in May 2026, and company-registration services there rest on collaboration between the Tribunal de Premiere Instance, Instat, the regional tax directorate and EDBM](<https://newsmada.com/2026/05/26/investissement-prive-ledbm-sinstalle-a-manakara/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [the account of the Manakara antenna](<https://newsmada.com/2026/05/26/investissement-prive-ledbm-sinstalle-a-manakara/>), where the court, Instat and the tax directorate each take part in registering a firm.

**Beneficial owners.** [The tax directorate (DGI) launched its Registre Central des Beneficiaires Effectifs (RCBE) on 16 June 2026 to identify the beneficial owners of legal persons and legal arrangements](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1781>). [On 18 September 2026 the DGI signed four data-sharing protocols, including one with the justice ministry for the trade and companies register (RNCS), so the RCBE's data can be cross-matched with the register's records of partners and directors](<https://www.2424.mg/transparence-le-rcbe-entre-dans-sa-phase-dexploitation-avec-le-croisement-des-donnees>). The beneficial-owner register is the tax directorate's, kept apart from the trade and companies register.

***Also on file***. [RCBE](<https://www.2424.mg/transparence-le-rcbe-entre-dans-sa-phase-dexploitation-avec-le-croisement-des-donnees>).

***Not found***. No source found says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security, or that the business register itself collects beneficial owners.
