# Togo

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Full-News article says firms can check name availability, file statutes, pay fees and receive registration documents online through the CFE site cfetogo.tg, in under four hours, with online payment since 2019](<https://full-news.tg/promotion-infrastructures-publiques-numeriques-cfetogo-tg-plateforme-cle-entrepreneurs/>). [Togo Officiel says the DGDN, the business formalities centre (CFE) and the commercial register (RCCM) are now connected so users can do several procedures through one channel, notably company creation](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>). [Togo-Presse reports that in 2024 the CFE and the RCCM were connected with other administrative platforms and that a citizen can create a company account online through the Services publics portal](<https://togopresse.tg/une-plateforme-numerique-creee-pour-rendre-les-services-gouvernementaux-plus-accessibles/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [Togo Officiel's account of the connected CFE and RCCM](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>), which speaks of one channel, not one number.

**Beneficial owners.** [Togo Officiel says a database of companies' beneficial owners has been created to identify who really owns a company](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>), and [Togo-Presse repeats it](<https://togopresse.tg/une-plateforme-numerique-creee-pour-rendre-les-services-gouvernementaux-plus-accessibles/>). Neither says who holds the database.

***Also on file***. [Services publics](<https://togopresse.tg/une-plateforme-numerique-creee-pour-rendre-les-services-gouvernementaux-plus-accessibles/>).

***Not found***. No source found says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security, or that the beneficial-owner database is part of the business register and in service.
