# Central African Republic

## Business register

**Online registration.** [On 2 February 2026 GUFE RCA opened its third one-stop company counter at Berberati, after Bangui and Bambari](<https://gufe-rca.org/antenne-gufe-berberati-operationnelle/>). [It offers company creation, modification and dissolution on the spot](<https://gufe-rca.org/antenne-gufe-berberati-operationnelle/>). [In May 2026 the counter launched a website with World Bank support, while still working largely by hand](<https://lengosongo.cf/2026/05/14/centrafrique-le-gufe-rca-lance-un-site-internet-pour-moderniser-la-creation-dentreprises/>). [The ministry says dematerialisation continues so that companies can be created remotely](<https://lengosongo.cf/2026/05/14/centrafrique-le-gufe-rca-lance-un-site-internet-pour-moderniser-la-creation-dentreprises/>). [In October 2025 a ministry workshop validated the specification for digitising the manual commercial register](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/10/29/centrafrique-vers-un-rccm-numerise-pour-booster-leconomie-nationale/>).
