# Central African Republic

## Business register

**Online registration.** [GUFE RCA states it officially launched its third operational branch of the one-stop company formalisation counter at Berberati on 2 February 2026, after Bangui and Bambari, offering company creation, modification and dissolution on the spot](<https://gufe-rca.org/antenne-gufe-berberati-operationnelle/>). [The counter launched a website in May 2026 with World Bank support while still working largely by hand, and the ministry says dematerialisation continues so that companies can be created remotely](<https://lengosongo.cf/2026/05/14/centrafrique-le-gufe-rca-lance-un-site-internet-pour-moderniser-la-creation-dentreprises/>), and [in October 2025 a ministry workshop validated the specification for digitising the manual commercial register](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/10/29/centrafrique-vers-un-rccm-numerise-pour-booster-leconomie-nationale/>).
