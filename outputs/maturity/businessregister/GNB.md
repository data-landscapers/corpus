# Guinea-Bissau

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Centro de Formalização de Empresas describes itself as an autonomous public one-stop shop under the Ministry of Economy for company creation and registration formalities, with online company registration still only planned](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>). The centre's own [company-creation platform](<https://cfegb.com/>) now takes founders' files online or through an agent, with validation and payment at the counter, and counts more than 7,666 firms created. [The centre succeeds the investment-promotion directorates DPIP and DGPIP, and was formally established by presidential and government decrees between 2010 and 2012 on the one-stop model](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>).
