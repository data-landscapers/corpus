# Guinea-Bissau

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Centro de Formalização de Empresas describes itself as an autonomous public one-stop shop under the Ministry of Economy for company creation and registration formalities, with online company registration still only planned](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>). Firms register at the centre's counter. [The centre succeeds the investment-promotion directorates DPIP and DGPIP, and was formally established by presidential and government decrees between 2010 and 2012 on the one-stop model](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>).

**Shared number.** Nothing held says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [the centre's own description](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>), which covers creation and registration formalities.

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing held says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the same description of the centre](<https://cfe.gw/sobre-nos/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says firms can register online; the centre says online registration is planned.
