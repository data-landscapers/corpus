# Mauritius

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CBRD's annual report counts 8,565 companies incorporated online in 2024/25 (for example 868 online against 318 at the office in July 2024)](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/7-5%20CBRD/Annual%20report%2024%2025.pdf>). [The Corporate and Business Registration Department's April 2025 online guide describes incorporating companies and registering businesses through online forms with documents uploaded](<https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/GUIDELINES-FOR-INCORPORATION-REGISTRATION-OF-COMPANIES-AND-REGISTRATION-OF-BUSINESSES_04_04_25.pdf>). [The Corporate and Business Registration Department portal offers online 'Register your business', incorporation of a company, a business registration number and online trade fee payment](<https://companies.govmu.org/Pages/default.aspx>).

**Shared number.** [Since May 2021 VAT registration has been done through CBRIS at company incorporation, where it applies](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/7-5%20CBRD/Annual%20report%2024%2025.pdf>). [The Corporate and Business Registration Department runs CBRIS](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/7-5%20CBRD/Annual%20report%2024%2025.pdf>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest fact: [in 2024/25, 8,565 companies were incorporated online through CBRIS](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/7-5%20CBRD/Annual%20report%2024%2025.pdf>).
