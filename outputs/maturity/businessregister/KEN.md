# Kenya

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Business Registration Service says name reservation and registration are one step, with the full application submitted online](<https://brs.go.ke>), and [its services are run with eCitizen, where applications are filed, fees paid and documents retrieved online](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-la-fintech-kenyane-pesaflow-prospecte-le-marche-de-la-govtech>). [On 16 February 2026 it launched a second version of its portal on eCitizen for company changes, and downloadable certificates replaced paper ones](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/120891-huduma-kenya-clarifies-end-physical-business-certificates-after-ecitizen-integration>). [The service says all Company Registry records have been digitised](<https://brs.go.ke/development-partners/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact is [the service's account of joining the companies registry with its collateral, hire purchase and insolvency registries](<https://brs.go.ke/development-partners/>), which does not mention tax.

**Beneficial owners.** [The Business Registration Service's partners page, as captured in June 2026, lists an online Beneficial Ownership Information Register for private companies as operational](<https://brs.go.ke/development-partners/>), built with German development cooperation (GIZ) support.

***Also on file***. [E-Colateral Registry (MPSR)](<https://brs.go.ke>); [Hire Purchase Registry](<https://brs.go.ke>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. Nothing found says the beneficial owners of every company are recorded.
