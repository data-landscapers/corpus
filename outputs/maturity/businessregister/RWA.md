# Rwanda

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Rwanda Development Board's Office of the Registrar General says business registration is 100% an online process and free of charge, with the certificate downloadable from the applicant's account in the registry](<https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/>). [The Norebase report, via TechCabal, says firms can incorporate in Rwanda within 24 to 72 hours through digital registration, and that the Rwanda Development Board's digital business registration platform moved registration entirely online in January](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/22/norebase-report-nigeria-rwanda-lead-africas-fastest-incorporation-markets/>). [The Rwanda Revenue Authority page says individual enterprises and companies are registered online through the Rwanda Development Board portal, immediately and free of charge](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).

**Shared number.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority says the RDB and RRA systems are integrated so registration gives a single, unique Taxpayer Identification Number](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the Registrar General's description of the online registry](<https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/>), which covers registration and certificates.
