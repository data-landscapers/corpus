# Rwanda

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Rwanda Development Board's Office of the Registrar General says business registration is 100% an online process and free of charge](<https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/>). [The certificate can be downloaded from the applicant's account in the registry](<https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/>). [The Norebase report, via TechCabal, says firms can incorporate in Rwanda within 24 to 72 hours through digital registration](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/22/norebase-report-nigeria-rwanda-lead-africas-fastest-incorporation-markets/>). [It says the Rwanda Development Board's digital business registration platform moved registration entirely online in January](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/22/norebase-report-nigeria-rwanda-lead-africas-fastest-incorporation-markets/>). [The Rwanda Revenue Authority page says individual enterprises and companies are registered online through the Rwanda Development Board portal](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>). [It says this is immediate and free of charge](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).

**Shared number.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority says the RDB and RRA systems are integrated](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>). [It says registration therefore gives a single, unique Taxpayer Identification Number](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest fact found is [the Registrar General's description of the online registry](<https://org.rdb.rw/business-registration/>), which covers registration and certificates.
