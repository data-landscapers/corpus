# Mozambique

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Kabum Digital reports that the registry and notary service (SRN) launched an online platform in November 2023](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>). On the platform, utente.srn.gov.mz, companies can be registered remotely. Payment is by M-Pesa or bank. [In September 2026 the BAU one-stop business registration centre had 129 service points nationwide](<https://360mozambique.com/business/facim-2026-bau-pushes-digitalisation-to-ease-small-business-formalisation/>). Its director-general gave the figure.

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [the BAU registers firms at one counter, at 129 service points in September 2026](<https://360mozambique.com/business/facim-2026-bau-pushes-digitalisation-to-ease-small-business-formalisation/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [Kabum Digital says users of the SRN portal can submit a list of beneficial owners](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>). It is one of the filing actions. [A 2024 decree-law requires legal entities to register owners of 10 percent or more](<https://www.rsm.global/mozambique/en/news/registo-do-beneficiario-efectivo>). They register with the legal entities registry. Filing is online on the same portal and completed in person.
