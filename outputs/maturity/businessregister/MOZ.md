# Mozambique

## Business register

**Online registration.** [Kabum Digital reports that Mozambique's registry and notary service (SRN) launched an online platform, utente.srn.gov.mz, where companies can be registered remotely, with payment by M-Pesa or bank](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [Kabum Digital says users of the SRN portal can submit a list of beneficial owners (lista de beneficiários efectivos) as one of the filing actions](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [Kabum Digital says users of the SRN portal can submit a list of beneficial owners (lista de beneficiários efectivos) as one of the filing actions](<https://kabum.digital/mocambique-lanca-plataforma-para-registo-de-empresas/>). [A 2024 decree-law requires legal entities to register owners of 10 percent or more with the legal entities registry, submitted online on the same portal and completed in person](<https://www.rsm.global/mozambique/en/news/registo-do-beneficiario-efectivo>).
