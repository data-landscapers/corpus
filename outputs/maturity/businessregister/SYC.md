# Seychelles

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Registration Division of Seychelles publishes an online business registration form on registry.gov.sc](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), held as captured in 2026. The form takes the entity's name, registration type, business type and activity, its physical and postal addresses, its partners, nominees or owners, supporting documents, tax-related information and, for companies, the nominal share capital, and closes with a verification step.

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest held fact is [the online registration form](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), which asks for tax-related information but does not say a number is shared.

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest held fact is [the same form](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), which records the partners, nominees and owners named by the applicant.
