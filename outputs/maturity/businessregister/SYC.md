# Seychelles

## Business register

**Online registration.** [The Registration Division of Seychelles publishes an online business registration form on registry.gov.sc](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), as captured in 2026. The form takes the entity's name, registration type, business type and activity, and its physical and postal addresses. It also takes its partners, nominees or owners, supporting documents and tax-related information. For companies it takes the nominal share capital. The form closes with a verification step.

**Shared number.** Nothing found says registration gives a firm one number shared with the tax authority or social security. The nearest fact found is [the online registration form](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), which asks for tax-related information but does not say a number is shared.

**Beneficial owners.** Nothing found says the register collects the beneficial owners of the firms it records. The nearest fact found is [the same form](<https://www.registry.gov.sc/BizRegistration/WebBusinessRegsitration.aspx>), which records the partners, nominees and owners named by the applicant.
