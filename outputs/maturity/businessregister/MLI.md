# Mali

## Business register

**Online registration.** [In 2025 API-Mali said its Guichet Unique brings company registration, operating authorisations and Investment Code approvals together in one place](<https://apimali.gov.ml/en/creation-entreprise/>). [In July 2026 Moctar Ousmane Sy wrote that firms can pre-register online there and upload scanned documents](<https://croissanceafrique.com/mali-cette-belle-proposition-citoyenne-de-moctar-ousmane-sy-pour-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>). [They must still go to Bamako in person to file originals, pay and collect official documents](<https://croissanceafrique.com/mali-cette-belle-proposition-citoyenne-de-moctar-ousmane-sy-pour-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [the Guichet Unique handles company registration alongside operating authorisations](<https://apimali.gov.ml/en/creation-entreprise/>).

**Beneficial owners.** No source found since October 2023 says the register collects beneficial owners. The nearest held fact: [firms file their original documents in person in Bamako](<https://croissanceafrique.com/mali-cette-belle-proposition-citoyenne-de-moctar-ousmane-sy-pour-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>).
