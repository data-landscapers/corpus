# South Africa

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CIPC states external company registration is available only on its e-Services platform, with no manual or e-mail applications accepted, and is within 20 business days of starting activity](<https://www.cipc.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/External-Company-Registration-v_1_2-31-Mar-2026.pdf>).

**Shared number.** No source found since October 2023 says registration gives one number shared with tax or social security. The nearest held fact: [The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission launched an upgraded Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Module giving law enforcement agencies real-time access to company ownership information, first launched in April 2023, per the dtic spokesperson](<https://www.ewn.co.za/new-tools-from-cipc-to-strengthen-sas-fight-against-money-laundering-terrorist-financing/>).

**Beneficial owners.** [The Companies and Intellectual Property Commission launched an upgraded Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Module giving law enforcement agencies real-time access to company ownership information, first launched in April 2023, per the dtic spokesperson](<https://www.ewn.co.za/new-tools-from-cipc-to-strengthen-sas-fight-against-money-laundering-terrorist-financing/>). [The CIPC says the register had received more than 3.2 million filings by 27 May 2026](<https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/cipc-puts-spotlight-on-its-beneficial-ownership-register-and-requirements-for-companies-2026-06-15>), and [the trade minister said filings covered 1,139,011 of 2,297,242 active private companies, 49.58 percent, on 22 September 2025](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33890/>).
