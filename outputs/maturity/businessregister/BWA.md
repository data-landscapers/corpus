# Botswana

## Business register

**Online registration.** [CIPA says its Online Business Registration System, acquired in 2019, lets investors register companies online 24/7 and cut registration from over five days to one](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/news/cipa-rots-from-the-head-splashes-p15m-on-bad-it-system>). [CIPA says that before the system it relied on a semi-automated process with no online functionality, manual searches in paper files and long queues of clients who travelled far](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/news/cipa-rots-from-the-head-splashes-p15m-on-bad-it-system>). The same February 2025 report puts the system's cost at the centre of a dispute over its performance.
