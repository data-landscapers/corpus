# Madagascar

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Orange Madagascar inaugurated on 1 December 2025 a 558 km fibre link from the capital Antananarivo to Mahajanga, which it calls a national backbone (2424.mg, quoting the operator)](<https://2424.mg/telecommunications-mahajanga-desservie-par-un-reseau-fibre-optique-dorange-madagascar/>). [Orange Madagascar's Antananarivo-Toamasina fibre link was put in place in 2024 (works launched August 2024), per 2424.mg](<https://2424.mg/telecommunications-mahajanga-desservie-par-un-reseau-fibre-optique-dorange-madagascar/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://2424.mg/telecommunications-mahajanga-desservie-par-un-reseau-fibre-optique-dorange-madagascar/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
