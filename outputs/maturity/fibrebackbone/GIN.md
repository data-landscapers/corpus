# Guinea

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The minister's 2025 sector review, as reported by WeAreTech Africa, says nearly 12,000 km of fibre optic was deployed to connect all administrative regions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026>). [Agence Ecofin reports that SOGEB, the national backbone operator, announced on 20 May 2026 that the first loop of Guinea's national fibre-optic backbone was raised from 200 to 400 Gb/s with Huawei's technical support, after a 50 to 200 Gb/s step in December 2024; SOGEB says it improves interurban communications; the article gives no towns or km](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2205-138703-la-guinee-augmente-la-capacite-de-son-reseau-internet-de-700-en-un-an-et-demi>). [ANSUTEN's director general said on 26 December 2025 that 13 of 17 universities and institutes are already connected to the national fibre-optic network (national backbone and Conakry metropolitan network), four more being connected; the document names no regions or km](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/12/la-ministre-pricemou-lance-un-vaste-projet-de-connexion-numerique-pour-17-universites-et-institut/>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
