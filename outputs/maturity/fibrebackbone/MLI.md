# Mali

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In January 2024 a $117.3 million contract was approved to extend fibre to Mopti, Koro and Timbuktu over 24 months](<https://cioafrica.co/landed-not-delivered/>), CIO Africa reports. No source found says backbone fibre is in service between Bamako and another region.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [the fibre under contract](<https://cioafrica.co/landed-not-delivered/>). Nor does any source give its terms.

***Also on file***. These include the [Maroc Telecom transafrican terrestrial cable](<https://leseco.ma/business/maroc-telecom-plus-de-454-mmdh-investis-en-afrique.html>) and the [Trans-Sahara Optical Fibre Backbone Project (TSB)](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/niger-takes-major-step-towards-high-speed-connectivity-handover-over-1000-km-fibre-optic-cable-88768>). They also include [Moov Africa Malitel FTTH](<https://www.maliweb.net/communique-391/moov-africa-malitel-tourne-la-page-de-ladsl-la-migration-vers-la-fibre-optique-ftth-lancee-dici-fin-2026-3118639.html>) and [Moov Africa Malitel fibre plans](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1506-56460-moov-africa-upgrades-fiber-service-amid-rising-internet-use-in-mali>).

***Not found***. No source found says backbone fibre is in service between the capital and another region.
