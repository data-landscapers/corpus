# Namibia

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In its 2025/26 budget speech the ICT ministry put Namibia's fibre network at 18,719.5 km](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>). [It gave the longest regional stretches as //Karas (3,340 km), Otjozondjupa (2,624 km), Erongo (2,548 km) and Hardap (2,232 km)](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>). [Khomas, the capital's region, had 1,776 km](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>), and Zambezi, Kavango West and Omusati each had between 539 and 572 km. [In May 2026 Telecom Namibia said it was laying new long-distance routes](<https://www.telecom.na/media-centre/902-telecom-namibia-affirms-commitment-to-network-modernisation-amid-ongoing-service-improvements>) to give redundant paths between key cities and regions.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In March 2025 the competition commission said NamPower offers competitors access to its fibre through its Grid Online service](<https://www.namibian.com.na/nampower-telecom-mtc-given-30-days-to-revisit-unfair-agreement/>). [It found three of the six fibre pairs on that network leased exclusively to MTC and Telecom Namibia](<https://www.namibian.com.na/nampower-telecom-mtc-given-30-days-to-revisit-unfair-agreement/>).
