# South Sudan

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** In June 2022 the World Bank found that [South Sudan had no national fibre backbone](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Digital-Economy-SouthSudan-jun22.pdf>). [In February 2026 Africa50 said fibre construction had begun](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africa50_connectingafrica-digitalafrica-easttowestconnectivity-activity-7431791734906458112-_7PC>) on a route from Nadapal on the Kenyan border through Kapoeta, Torit, Juba, Rumbek and Wau to Tambura. It is part of the East2West project, built with Bayobab. NCT South Sudan and Bayobab [launched the national backbone project that month](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project>), starting with the Juba to Nadapal segment.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators in South Sudan buy or lease capacity on [this fibre](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. The nearest held fact is that [works on the route had begun by February 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africa50_connectingafrica-digitalafrica-easttowestconnectivity-activity-7431791734906458112-_7PC>).
