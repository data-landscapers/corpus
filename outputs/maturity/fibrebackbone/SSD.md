# South Sudan

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Tech Review Africa reports NCT South Sudan and Bayobab moved into the execution phase, with official kickoff of the National Backbone Fiber Network Project about one month before the 2026-03-04 article, including the Juba to Nadapal eastern segment at the Kenya border](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project>). [World Bank Digital Economy Assessment states South Sudan lacks a national fiber backbone](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Digital-Economy-SouthSudan-jun22.pdf>). These say a national backbone is being built under a contract or with works begun, which answers the first of the study's four questions. That any of it is in service between the capital and another region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
