# Benin

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** Benin Telecom signed a contract with Huawei in 2014 for [rehabilitating a 1,060 km fibre backbone from Cotonou to Malanville](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>). China Eximbank financed this first phase of the National Broadband Network Project with [an RMB 496 million concessional loan signed in April 2015](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>). [Implementation began in December 2016](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>). The deal record gives the project as [completed, with 2018 as its end year](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>). The rehabilitated route is [a single long-distance link of 1,060 km](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** The backbone belongs to [Benin Telecom, the state operator that also took a metropolitan fibre loop around Cotonou under the same loan](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>). Huawei, the contractor, supplied [the equipment for what the project calls the National Backbone](<https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/ce/cebenin/chn/zbgx/t1239853.htm>).
