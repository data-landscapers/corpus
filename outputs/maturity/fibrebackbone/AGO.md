# Angola

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** In May 2023 the ministry's director of telecommunications said [Angola's terrestrial fibre exceeds 22,000 km and connects all provincial and communal capitals](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/matias-borges-dois-novos-projectos-vao-garantir-a-expansao-dos-servicos-de-comunicacoes-nos-municipios-e-nas-comunas/>). In November 2025 a secretary of state put the network at [more than 30,000 km, with 1,400 km more under construction](<https://angop.ao/en/noticias/economia/angola-presente-na-conferencia-mundial-das-telecomunicacoes/>). The first link of a new national broadband backbone, from Luanda to Benguela, [opened at Lobito in September 2026](<https://www.angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/rede-de-banda-larga-de-fibra-optica-ja-funciona-em-benguela/>). It is meant to reach all 21 provinces. In November 2026 the minister said the national broadband network is [still being extended across the country](<https://pti.ao/angola-apresenta-a-uit-projectos-de-fibra-optica-satelite-e-cloud/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** The ministry's 2023 account of [the national fibre network](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/matias-borges-dois-novos-projectos-vao-garantir-a-expansao-dos-servicos-de-comunicacoes-nos-municipios-e-nas-comunas/>) does not say other operators buy capacity on it. Nor does it say the network runs on open-access or wholesale terms. Angola Telecom [built the new backbone's first link with Mercury](<https://www.angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/rede-de-banda-larga-de-fibra-optica-ja-funciona-em-benguela/>).
