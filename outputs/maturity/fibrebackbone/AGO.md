# Angola

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Matias Borges, national director of telecommunications at MINTTICS, said in a May 2023 interview that Angola's terrestrial fibre network exceeds 22,000 km and connects all provincial and communal capitals](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/matias-borges-dois-novos-projectos-vao-garantir-a-expansao-dos-servicos-de-comunicacoes-nos-municipios-e-nas-comunas/>). [PTI reported on 21 September 2026 that the section of the Rede Nacional de Fibra Óptica to the city of Lobito, in Benguela province, was completed, with capacity of about 400 Gb, per the ministry](<https://pti.ao/fibra-optica-de-400-gb-chega-ao-lobito-e-tudo-nosso-da-angola-telecom-avanca-na-baia-farta/>). [Angola Telecom's operations executive Rock Dinis said the first link of the national fibre-optic broadband backbone, DWDM-based and built with Mercury, was inaugurated at Lobito on a route from Luanda through Bengo and Cuanza Sul to Benguela, with initial capacity reported as 200 Gigabytes; the project is meant to reach all 21 provinces, Huambo, Namibe and Cabinda being next (plan only)](<https://www.angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/rede-de-banda-larga-de-fibra-optica-ja-funciona-em-benguela/>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/matias-borges-dois-novos-projectos-vao-garantir-a-expansao-dos-servicos-de-comunicacoes-nos-municipios-e-nas-comunas/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
