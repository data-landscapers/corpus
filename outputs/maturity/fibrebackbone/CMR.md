# Cameroon

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The telecoms regulator ART reported that in 2024 Camtel's national fibre backbone served all ten regional capitals](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-camtel-a-gagne-34-milliards-fcfa-grace-a-la-fibre-optique-en-2024-en-hausse-de-17>). It also reached 51 of 58 divisional capitals, with about 12,000 km of fibre. [ART's 2024 observatory puts the national fibre network at nearly 12,000 km](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/09/cameroons-telecom-sector-grows-18-in-2024-driven-by-mobile-and-digital-services/>), run mainly by the state operator Camtel. [In March 2026 ITWeb Africa reported about 812 km more of overhead fibre on electricity poles](<https://itweb.africa/article/cameroon-seeks-195-million-to-expand-optic-fibre-backbone/WnxpEv4Y28g7V8XL>). [NewsWatch put Camtel's backbone at over 15,000 km in July 2026](<https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/>). Camtel's [2026 product sheet for operators](<https://carrier.camtel.cm/documentation/download/2>) describes a national network of nearly 10,000 km covering the whole territory. In November 2026 ART said its audit of [the 112 sections of Camtel's network found serious degradation](<https://art.cm/sites/default/files/documents/COMMUNIQUE%20ART%2010%20NOV%202026.pdf>), with repeated cuts and some sections out of service.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [ART reported that Camtel's transport network revenue rose in 2024 with demand for bandwidth from mobile operators and internet providers](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-camtel-a-gagne-34-milliards-fcfa-grace-a-la-fibre-optique-en-2024-en-hausse-de-17>). [NewsWatch reported in July 2026 that Camtel leases capacity on the backbone to private mobile operators](<https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/>). The same [2026 product sheet](<https://carrier.camtel.cm/documentation/download/2>) offers operators and internet providers leased lines between cities on the backbone. Camtel also [carries Chad's traffic to the sea cables over its fibre](<https://obs-cc.org/infrastructures-reseaux/>), and a deal of 15 May 2025 raised that capacity to over 100 Gbit/s. Camtel holds exclusive rights over the backbone itself.
