# Cameroon

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [NewsWatch states that CAMTEL owns and operates Cameroon's national fibre-optic backbone of over 15,000 km](<https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/>). [ITWeb Africa reports Cameroon currently operates about 12,000 km of terrestrial fibre-optic network plus about 812 km of overhead fibre on electricity poles, all managed by state operator Camtel; it does not list regions served](<https://itweb.africa/article/cameroon-seeks-195-million-to-expand-optic-fibre-backbone/WnxpEv4Y28g7V8XL>). [The deal record puts CAMTEL's existing national fibre at about 12,000 km terrestrial plus about 812 km overhead on electricity poles, as reported on 2026-03-09; it names no regions or towns](<https://www.prc.cm/files/c3/68/8c/dc79325e0fb9f1c5354d4abbe5c4a2b7.pdf https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2026/03/10/cameroon-secures-us195-million-to-enhance-fibre-optic-network/>). [Reporting ART's 2024 Annual Observatory, TechAfrica News says transport, mainly by the incumbent CAMTEL, relies on a national fibre optic network of nearly 12,000 km](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/09/cameroons-telecom-sector-grows-18-in-2024-driven-by-mobile-and-digital-services/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [NewsWatch states that CAMTEL leases capacity on its national fibre-optic backbone to private mobile network operators, while holding exclusive rights over the backbone itself](<https://newswatchcameroon.com/cameroon-starlink-licensing-stalls-over-security-sovereignty-concerns/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
