# Guinea-Bissau

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Agence Ecofin reported in August 2025 that Guinea-Bissau's international connectivity is confined to the coast](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>). [It said the country still needs a national backbone to link its main cities](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>). Nothing dated later says one is being built or is in service. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts as open where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
