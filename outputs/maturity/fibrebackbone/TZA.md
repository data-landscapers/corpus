# Tanzania

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Minister says the backbone links all mainland regional headquarters (also 122 of 139 districts), as of December 2025](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>). [Liquid Kenya's CEO says Liquid's East African footprint, including Tanzania, is connected through its own metro and national long-distance fibre networks](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>). [TM Africa says the NICTBB is expanding toward about 16,280 km in total, with an extension to the DRC in the 2026/27 work programme](<https://www.tmafrica.co.ug/news/feature-phone-economy-tanzania-built-rails-and-hit-device-ceiling>). [African Business (Neil Ford) states the TTCL-managed NICTBB fibre network now stretches more than 15,000 km and connects the main urban centres](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, the fourth of the study's questions after it being built and being in service, and the stage rests on that and on the sale of capacity below. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [TTCL, through NICTBB, agreed with Burundi Backbone System to raise capacity to Burundi from 4 Gbps to 10 Gbps, i.e. capacity supplied to another operator](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/ttcl-bbs-increase-tanzania-burundi-connectivity-capacity-10gbps/>). [TTCL signed a five-year capacity agreement worth USD 3.3 million with Burundi Backbone System to supply capacity over the National ICT Broadband Backbone, TTCL's Director General said](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-nictbb-expands-connectivity-to-burundi/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
