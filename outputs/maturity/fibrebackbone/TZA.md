# Tanzania

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** The National ICT Broadband Backbone already [linked the headquarters of all 25 mainland regions by 2016](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/pages/completed-project>), at 7,910 km. In December 2025 the minister said it [links every mainland regional headquarters and 122 of 139 districts](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>). It then spanned [15,167 km at 2,000 Gbps](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>), reaching seven neighbouring countries. In April 2026 operators [handed over about 1,193 km of new backbone routes](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/business/yas-tanzania-partners-hand-over-major-digital-infrastructure-to-government-5420804>) to the government. The network is [to grow to about 16,280 km](<https://www.tmafrica.co.ug/news/feature-phone-economy-tanzania-built-rails-and-hit-device-ceiling>), with a link to the DRC planned for 2026/27.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** TTCL [manages the backbone](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>) and sells capacity on it. In February 2024 it [signed a five-year deal worth USD 3.3 million](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-nictbb-expands-connectivity-to-burundi/>) to supply capacity to Burundi Backbone System. In September 2026 it [raised that capacity from 4 to 10 Gbps](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/ttcl-bbs-increase-tanzania-burundi-connectivity-capacity-10gbps/>). Tanzania also [signed agreements in 2023 to connect Malawi and Uganda](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/telecom/2602-116544-la-tanzanie-signe-un-nouvel-accord-de-cinq-ans-avec-le-burundi-pour-lui-fournir-des-capacites-telecoms-a-haut-debit>) to the backbone.
