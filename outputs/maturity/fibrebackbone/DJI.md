# Djibouti

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Ministry of Communication (MCPT) says DFN has deployed over 200 km of fibre in Djibouti-ville and the interior regions, connecting hospitals, schools and public bodies; fibre use is institutional, so the backbone reading is uncertain)](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>). [The Ministry of Communication (MCPT) says the World Bank-financed DFN and EARDIP projects are deploying fibre optic networks in Djibouti-ville, Balbala and the five interior regions, with EARDIP developing terrestrial fibre along the road corridor north of Djibouti](<https://www.lanation.dj/transition-numerique-les-parties-prenantes-mobilisees-autour-des-projets-dfn-et-eardip/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
