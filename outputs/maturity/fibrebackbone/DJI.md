# Djibouti

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Ministry of Communication (MCPT) says DFN has deployed over 200 km of fibre in Djibouti-ville and the interior regions](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>). [The fibre connects hospitals, schools and public bodies; its use is institutional, so the backbone reading is uncertain](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>). [MCPT says the World Bank-financed DFN and EARDIP projects are deploying fibre optic networks in Djibouti-ville, Balbala and the five interior regions](<https://www.lanation.dj/transition-numerique-les-parties-prenantes-mobilisees-autour-des-projets-dfn-et-eardip/>). [EARDIP is developing terrestrial fibre along the road corridor north of Djibouti](<https://www.lanation.dj/transition-numerique-les-parties-prenantes-mobilisees-autour-des-projets-dfn-et-eardip/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region. That it reaches every province or region is not stated.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.lanation.dj/ali-sabieh-le-dfn-propulse-lhopital-regional-dans-une-nouvelle-ere-numerique/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts as open where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
