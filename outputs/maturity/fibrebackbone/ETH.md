# Ethiopia

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Ethio telecom says that in its reporting period it deployed 793 km of new backbone fibre, bringing its national backbone network to 23,442.1 km (regions served not listed)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/ethio-telecom-active-deployment-africa-middle-east-fibre-corridor/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.

***Not found***. No source found says the backbone reaches every province or region.
