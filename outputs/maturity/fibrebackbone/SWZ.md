# Eswatini

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** The regulator, ESCCOM, names [EPTC as the sole national backbone operator](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). Its Notice 5/2026 finds [EPTC's existing national backbone an essential facility](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). The ruling, in force from 1 July 2026, directs EPTC to [share its existing connections and fibre topology with internet service providers](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). The Ministry of ICT said in February 2026 that [optical fibre is being rolled out across the country as part of a nationwide deployment](<https://times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Optical+fibre+rollout+strengthens+business+ecosystem&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2892>). In August 2025 the minister reported [a nationwide fibre rollout supported by the World Bank](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/04/eswatini-ramps-up-tech-reform-with-new-legislation-5g-rollout-and-coding-training-for-all/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** ESCCOM ordered EPTC to make its backbone [available to Jenny Internet on non-discriminatory terms](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). It ordered Jenny Internet, which had built [its own cross-border fibre link at the Malutha border post](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>), to [use EPTC backbone connectivity](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). EPTC must also give internet service providers [its fibre topology on non-discriminatory terms](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>).
