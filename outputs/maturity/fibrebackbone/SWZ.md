# Eswatini

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Ministry of ICT confirmed that optical fibre is being rolled out across the country as part of a nationwide deployment; Dated by the article's published date](<https://times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Optical+fibre+rollout+strengthens+business+ecosystem&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=2892>). [Minister of ICT Maziya reported a nationwide rollout of fibre optic cable supported by the World Bank; Dated by the article's published date](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/04/eswatini-ramps-up-tech-reform-with-new-legislation-5g-rollout-and-coding-training-for-all/>). These say a national backbone is being built under a contract or with works begun, which answers the first of the study's four questions. That any of it is in service between the capital and another region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [ESCCOM's Notice 5/2026 found EPTC's national backbone an essential facility and ordered EPTC to make it available to Jenny Internet and to share fibre topology with ISPs on non-discriminatory terms, and ordered Jenny to use EPTC backbone connectivity; decision effective 1 July 2026](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.

***Also on file***. [Jenny Internet Malutha Border Post cross-border fibre link](<https://esccom.org.sz/publications/notices/docs/EPTC%20AND%20JENNY%20FINAL%20%20DECISION%20Notice%205.pdf>); [UASF Backbone Network redundancy route to Maputo via MOTRACO powerline fibre](<https://uasf.esccom.org.sz/programmes/connectivity-programmes/>); [Digital Eswatini Project fibre component](<https://independentnews.co.sz/46514/business/aviation/digital-eswatini-project-hinges-on-leadership-ps-warns/>).

***Not found***. No source found says any of the backbone is in service between the capital and another region.
