# Burkina Faso

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Minister Aminata Zerbo/Sabane told parliament the national backbone gives robust connectivity across the whole territory](<https://an.bf/127>). [SMART Burkina, about 800 km of fibre linking 22 cities, was completed in August 2025](<https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2022/11/GCI_PB_012_EN_FIN.pdf>). [The ministry's 2025 results put the national fibre network at over 11,292 km](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/burkina-faso-earmarks-109-4mln-for-digital-projects-in-2026>). [PBNT Phase 1, 307 km from Ouagadougou to Po, was inaugurated on 26 October 2021](<https://www.jeuneafrique.com/593265/politique/ambassade-de-chine-au-burkina-pekin-resserre-sa-cooperation-avec-ouagadougou/>). It is part of a planned 5,740 km programme costing EUR 175 million. [The project began on 8 July 2021 and was due to finish within 54 months](<https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/CR/Issues/2019/01/22/Burkina-Faso-2018-Article-IV-Consultation-First-Review-Under-the-Extended-Credit-Facility-46533>). [Its operator says the network has run commercially since March 2023 and covers almost the whole territory](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/08/18/interview-avec-sagadibwaoga-michel-ouedraogo-dg-de-bfs-le-backbone-national-a-tenu-ses-promesses-trois-ans-dexploitation-de-la-fibre-optique-publique-burkinabe/>). A 43 km Ouarkoye-Mana segment was added in 2026. So the fibre was built, is in service and reaches every region. Length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Bridge Fiber Solutions, which runs the state backbone, sold capacity on it to 44 customers in August 2026](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/08/18/interview-avec-sagadibwaoga-michel-ouedraogo-dg-de-bfs-le-backbone-national-a-tenu-ses-promesses-trois-ans-dexploitation-de-la-fibre-optique-publique-burkinabe/>). It had 7 in 2023. They include all three mobile operators, internet providers, state firms and banks. It sells capacity, dark fibre and wavelengths. Its prices follow a catalogue that ARCEP and the ministry validate each year, on equal terms for all buyers.
