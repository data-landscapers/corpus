# Burkina Faso

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Minister Aminata Zerbo/Sabane told parliament the national backbone now offers robust connectivity across the whole territory](<https://an.bf/127>). [The SMART Burkina project, about 800 km of fibre connecting 22 major cities across Burkina Faso, was completed in August 2025, citing a SASAC report](<https://www.bu.edu/gdp/files/2022/11/GCI_PB_012_EN_FIN.pdf>). [The ministry's 2025 results say the national fibre-optic network was expanded to more than 11,292 km (We Are Tech Africa reporting)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/burkina-faso-earmarks-109-4mln-for-digital-projects-in-2026>). [PBNT Phase 1, a 307 km fibre backbone linking Ouagadougou and Po (capacity 1,600 Gbit/s, serving ten municipalities), was completed and officially inaugurated on 26 October 2021; it is part of a planned 5,740 km, EUR 175 million programme](<https://www.jeuneafrique.com/593265/politique/ambassade-de-chine-au-burkina-pekin-resserre-sa-cooperation-avec-ouagadougou/>). [The project officially commenced on 8 July 2021, with completion expected within 54 months (8 January 2026)](<https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/CR/Issues/2019/01/22/Burkina-Faso-2018-Article-IV-Consultation-First-Review-Under-the-Extended-Credit-Facility-46533>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://an.bf/127>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
