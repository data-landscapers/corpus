# Togo

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The government's ICT specifications guide of 2023 says Togocom has deployed a backbone of about 1,500 km](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html>), running from the coast to the far north. [Its axes are Lomé–Atakpamé–Cinkassé, Lomé–Kpalimé–Atakpamé and Lomé–Aného, with 24 fibres a link](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html>). [The links around Lomé, from Atakpamé to Kara and from Kara to Cinkassé are doubled](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In January 2025 the regulator ARCEP capped the wholesale prices operators charge each other for access to national transmission infrastructure](<https://arcep.tg/larcep-encadre-a-la-baisse-les-tarifs-sur-le-marche-de-gros/>). The decision is numbered 003/ARCEP/DG/25. [The cap cut the monthly rent for dark fibre from 75 to 30 FCFA a metre](<https://arcep.tg/larcep-encadre-a-la-baisse-les-tarifs-sur-le-marche-de-gros/>), a fall of 60 per cent. [ARCEP set the caps after auditing the costs of the operators Togocom and Moov Africa Togo](<https://arcep.tg/larcep-encadre-a-la-baisse-les-tarifs-sur-le-marche-de-gros/>).
