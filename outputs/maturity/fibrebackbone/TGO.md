# Togo

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Togo government ICT specifications guide states Togocom has deployed a backbone of about 1,500 km north to south on the axes Lome-Atakpame-Cinkasse, Lome-Kpalime-Atakpame and Lome-Aneho, 24 fibres per link, doubled around Lome and on Atakpame-Kara and Kara-Cinkasse](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://ressources.digital.gouv.tg/shares/documentations/Specifications_tic_administrations/html/internet.html>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
