# Rwanda

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [TechTrends KE reports Liquid's East African footprint, including Rwanda, is connected through the company's own metro and national long-distance fibre networks](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>). [Ministerial Instructions 001/2012 (Ministry in the Office of the President in charge of ICT) recite that the national fibre optic backbone has been completed, and provide for government bandwidth to be distributed to institutions across the country over the National Backbone Optic Fiber](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/min/2012/1/eng@2012-03-12>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
