# Rwanda

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In August 2025 btw.media reported that KT Rwanda Networks runs a fibre backbone of over 4,000 km](<https://btw.media/en/kt-rwanda-networks-rwandas-wholesale-4g-fibre-backbone>). It connects all districts and border areas. The network has redundant transmission and power systems. The company is a partnership between the government and Korea Telecom. [Ministerial Instructions 001/2012 state that the national fibre backbone had been completed](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/min/2012/1/eng@2012-03-12>). [Liquid said in September 2026 that its own national long-distance fibre serves Rwanda](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [KT Rwanda Networks sells wholesale connectivity to other mobile and internet providers](<https://btw.media/en/kt-rwanda-networks-rwandas-wholesale-4g-fibre-backbone>). btw.media reported this in August 2025. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
