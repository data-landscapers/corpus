# Malawi

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [ESCOM states its national fibre backbone covers all districts of Malawi including Likoma Island (coverage extended through investment in 2017 and 2021)](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ESCOM-Presentation-v.02-ITU-2025-Africa-BoradBand-Mapping.pdf>). [Airtel Malawi says its proprietary fibre backbone has been reinforced with new routes from Lilongwe to Salima and Blantyre to Mangochi](<https://www.maraviexpress.com/airtel-malawi-plc-brings-new-era-of-digital-connectivity-with-volte-and-5g-services/>). [ESCOM's optic fibre business states its national fibre backbone network is 7000 km, built on its electricity poles, with transport and MPLS backbone running in two rings centred on Lilongwe and Blantyre and linking Mzuzu, Kasungu, Salima, Zomba and Mangochi](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ESCOM-Presentation-v.02-ITU-2025-Africa-BoradBand-Mapping.pdf>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/ESCOM-Presentation-v.02-ITU-2025-Africa-BoradBand-Mapping.pdf>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
