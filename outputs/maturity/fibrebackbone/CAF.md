# Central African Republic

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Minister of Digital Economy praised Telecel Centrafrique for connecting to fibre optic in Bangui and in several interior towns, and said the first fibre deployments have begun while national connectivity remains fragmented](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/02/02/presentation-de-voeux-le-ministere-de-leconomie-numerique-devoile-ses-grandes-orientations-pour-lannee-2026/>). [The Minister and Africa24 state 1,200 km of fibre optic is in place in CAR, with cross-border fibre taken from Congo at Ouesso and Cameroon at Kenzo](<https://africa24tv.com/republique-centrafricaine-le-taux-de-penetration-dinternet-atteint-30/>). [AfDB says that at project close in September 2023 the first national optical backbone was installed: 900 km of fibre with eleven technical sites, a network operations centre in Bangui and two community digital centres, linking CAR to Cameroon and Congo](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/les-jeunes-centrafricains-formes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-grace-la-fibre-optique-installee-avec-lappui-de-la-banque-africaine-de-developpement-et-de-lunion-europeenne-69893>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/02/02/presentation-de-voeux-le-ministere-de-leconomie-numerique-devoile-ses-grandes-orientations-pour-lannee-2026/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
