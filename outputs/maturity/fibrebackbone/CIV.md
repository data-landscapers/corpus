# Cote d'Ivoire

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In June 2026 ANSUT's infrastructure director said its national broadband network has more than 6,600 km of fibre](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>). ANSUT is the universal service agency. [ANSUT had completed 5,476 km of a planned 7,000 km by 2023](<https://digitalmag.ci/services-telecoms-les-acquis-du-desenclavement-numerique-de-la-cote-divoire-ansut/>), with 66 access points. Of the 7,000 km planned, about 1,500 km had still to be laid in 2023. By 2026 the network was over 1,100 km longer than in 2023.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In 2023 Orange CI, MTN CI and Moov Africa CI operated 2,245 km of the ANSUT network](<https://digitalmag.ci/services-telecoms-les-acquis-du-desenclavement-numerique-de-la-cote-divoire-ansut/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
