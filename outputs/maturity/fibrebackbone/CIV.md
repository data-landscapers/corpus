# Cote d'Ivoire

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In June 2026 ANSUT's infrastructure director said its national broadband network has more than 6,600 km of fibre](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>). ANSUT is the universal service agency, and [calls the network the Réseau National Haut Débit](<https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/le-r%C3%A9seau-national-haut-d%C3%A9bit-rnhd-un-pilier-essentiel-pour-la-transformation-tiuce>). [In April 2025 it said it had laid 5,207 km of the network's planned 6,632 km](<https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/le-r%C3%A9seau-national-haut-d%C3%A9bit-rnhd-un-pilier-essentiel-pour-la-transformation-tiuce>). [In 2023 the network had 66 access points](<https://digitalmag.ci/services-telecoms-les-acquis-du-desenclavement-numerique-de-la-cote-divoire-ansut/>), and its plan then ran to 7,000 km. By 2026 the length laid was close to the whole planned length.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In April 2025 ANSUT said the network carries traffic for Orange CI, MTN CI and Moov Africa CI, and for internet providers](<https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/le-r%C3%A9seau-national-haut-d%C3%A9bit-rnhd-un-pilier-essentiel-pour-la-transformation-tiuce>). [The three operators ran 2,245 km of it](<https://fr.linkedin.com/pulse/le-r%C3%A9seau-national-haut-d%C3%A9bit-rnhd-un-pilier-essentiel-pour-la-transformation-tiuce>), the length [reported for 2023](<https://digitalmag.ci/services-telecoms-les-acquis-du-desenclavement-numerique-de-la-cote-divoire-ansut/>).
