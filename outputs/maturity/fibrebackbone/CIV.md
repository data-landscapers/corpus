# Cote d'Ivoire

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [ANSUT's RNHD infrastructure director states the RNHD totals more than 6,600 km of optical fibre across the country](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
