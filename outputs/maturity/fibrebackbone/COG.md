# Congo

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In August 2024 the French Treasury said the national coverage project had laid about 3,000 km of fibre](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>). It links Pointe-Noire, Dolisie, Brazzaville, Oyo and Ouesso. Congo Telecom operates and markets it, beside a 1,100 km secondary network. [Xalam Analytics described the project as still under way in January 2026](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>). It put the fibre at about 3,000 km connecting key towns.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [the national coverage project's fibre](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>). None says it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
