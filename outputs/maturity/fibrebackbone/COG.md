# Congo

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Xalam Analytics says the Projet de Couverture Nationale fibre project is underway, about 3,000 km of terrestrial fibre connecting key towns; document gives no event date, so its published date (January 2026) is used](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>). These say a national backbone is being built under a contract or with works begun, which answers the first of the study's four questions. That any of it is in service between the capital and another region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/Data-Center-Market-Briefs-Congo-Republic-2026.pdf>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
