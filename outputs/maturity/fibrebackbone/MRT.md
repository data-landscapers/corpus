# Mauritania

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** In December 2025 the director general of the state company SDIN [said it operates a national fibre network of more than 1,760 km on three main axes linking the country's regions](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/285647>). The World Bank says [five fibre links totalling 1,710 km were laid and in service by 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099915008262277976/pdf/BOSIB0ef45e1210300bb6f0e08fc7c02a45.pdf>) under WARCIP APL2, carrying international capacity from the ACE cable landing to most provincial capitals and to the borders with Mali, Niger, Burkina Faso and Morocco. In August 2025 a ministry representative said [over 4,000 km of the national fibre network had been completed, with over 3,000 km more built by operators](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276833>), and tenders prepared for another 3,000 km.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [The World Bank says the backbone was built under a PPP and open-access model giving operators fair access to the infrastructure, with state vehicle SDIN established and an affermage contract with IMT signed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099915008262277976/pdf/BOSIB0ef45e1210300bb6f0e08fc7c02a45.pdf>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
