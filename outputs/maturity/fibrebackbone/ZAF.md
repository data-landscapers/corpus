# South Africa

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In March 2025 MyBroadband reported that the state-owned Broadband Infraco's network covers all nine provinces](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/587054-the-technically-insolvent-state-owned-broadband-company-that-lost-r459-million.html>). Its corporate profile gives 15,000 km of fibre and 156 points of presence. [In September 2026 Telkom's Openserve completed a third long-haul fibre route between Cape Town and Pretoria](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-09-07-openserve-completes-third-fibre-route-to-reduce-network-outage-risk/>), via Springbok and Mahikeng.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [Broadband Infraco's licence lets it sell long-distance transmission to licensed fixed and mobile operators and internet providers](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/587054-the-technically-insolvent-state-owned-broadband-company-that-lost-r459-million.html>). [TechCentral called it a carrier of carriers in April 2026](<https://techcentral.co.za/state-broadband-merger-limps-into-a-second-decade/280723/>). It had since lost its largest public-sector customers, SITA and Eskom. [Openserve, Telkom's wholesale unit, sells capacity to licensed operators on about 180,000 km of fibre](<https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-09-07-openserve-completes-third-fibre-route-to-reduce-network-outage-risk/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
