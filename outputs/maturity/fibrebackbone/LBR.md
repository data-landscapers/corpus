# Liberia

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [LTA Chairman Massaquoi says the fibre line had already reached Loguatuo from Monrovia; no length or date given, so dated by publication](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html>). [LTA Chairman Massaquoi says private firms are helping extend the fibre-optic network from Buchanan to Rivercess, Sinoe, Maryland, Grand Gedeh and Nimba counties, with work ongoing to interconnect all regions; no contract is described](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing held says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/liberia-on-the-verge-of-5g-revolution/article_992b027e-4c8a-4578-bf70-baf2e2eabeeb.html>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
