# Equatorial Guinea

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Gabon's state infrastructure holder SPIN reports that on 10 November 2025 the Gabonese and Equatorial Guinean ministers inaugurated the interconnection of the two national fibre backbones](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [The physical and logical interconnection is at Meyo-Kyé/Ebebeyin](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [Tests confirmed stable traffic exchange between Bitam and Ebebeyin and between Libreville and Bata](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). [The document names no regions or length for the Equatorial Guinean network and does not say Malabo is on it](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region. That it reaches every province or region is not stated.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts as open where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.

***Also on file***. These include the [Backbone National Gabonais (BNG)](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>) and the [Central African Fibre Optic Backbone (CAB), Gabon component](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=46002-P-Z1-GZ0-001>). They also include the [red nacional de fibra óptica de SEGESA (2,000 km, along electricity infrastructure)](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/segesa_estudia_la_posibilidad_de_comercializar_su_red_de_fibra_optica>). Others are [ACE](<https://www.larazon.es/sociedad/cables-submarinos-que-conectan-africa-resto-mundo_202606056a227fb59c159d030fd0e65c.html>), [Medusa (GNQ landing)](<https://www.larazon.es/sociedad/cables-submarinos-que-conectan-africa-resto-mundo_202606056a227fb59c159d030fd0e65c.html>) and [Via Africa](<https://www.larazon.es/sociedad/cables-submarinos-que-conectan-africa-resto-mundo_202606056a227fb59c159d030fd0e65c.html>).
