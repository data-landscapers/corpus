# Equatorial Guinea

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Gabon's state infrastructure holder SPIN reports that on 10 November 2025 the Gabonese and Equatorial Guinean ministers inaugurated the physical and logical interconnection of the two national fibre backbones at Meyo-Kyé/Ebebeyin, with tests confirming stable traffic exchange between Bitam and Ebebeyin and between Libreville and Bata; the document names no regions or length for the Equatorial Guinean network and does not say Malabo is on it](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://spin.ga/alliance-historique-numerique-gabon-guinee-equatoriale/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
