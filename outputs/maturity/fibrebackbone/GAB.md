# Gabon

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In October 2026 the head of SPIN, the state infrastructure holding, said its backbone runs about 2,000 km](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-numerique-interview-bertrand-mahoukou-bitchinda-expose-la-strategie-de-la-spin-pour-etendre-la-fibre-optique/>). It follows the railway from Libreville to Franceville and reaches Makokou and Cameroon. Two of the nine provinces are not yet connected. [On 14 September 2026 SPIN signed a 20-year partnership with Gabon Fiber](<https://spin.ga/backbone-national-gabonais-spin-gabon-fiber/>). Gabon Fiber takes over the existing 1,782 km and is to build 1,651 km of extensions. [A 196 km section to Mouila was commissioned on 6 October 2026](<https://spin.ga/backbone-national-gabonais-196-km-de-fibre-optique/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [SPIN says Gabon Fiber sells only wholesale, on open and non-discriminatory terms for operators and internet providers](<https://spin.ga/backbone-national-gabonais-spin-gabon-fiber/>). [GabonReview reported in September 2026 that the contract requires open access](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-fiber-vers-une-connectivite-plus-etendue-plus-performante-et-plus-accessible/>).
