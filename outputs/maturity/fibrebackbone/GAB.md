# Gabon

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [L'Union reports the 196 km Fougamou-Mouila section (Ngounie province), built and pre-financed by Moov Africa Gabon Telecom, now connects Fougamou and Mouila to the national backbone; it is the first segment of phase 1 of the extension, ceded to the State by a convention signed at Mouila (dated 'yesterday' in a 7 Oct 2026 article)](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/telecommunications-magt-et-letat-signent-une-convention-strategique>). [SPIN states the 196 km Lambarene-Mouila fibre section, first segment of phase 1 of the national backbone extension, was commissioned on 6 October 2026, connecting Fougamou and Mouila to the national network](<https://spin.ga/backbone-national-gabonais-196-km-de-fibre-optique/>). [The same report says extension works on the national fibre are under way](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-seminaire-sur-levolution-des-communications-spatiales-11-recommandations-adoptees/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [GabonReview reports the contract imposes 'open and non-discriminatory' access for operators on the backbone, with performance and cybersecurity obligations](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-fiber-vers-une-connectivite-plus-etendue-plus-performante-et-plus-accessible/>). [GabonReview reports Gabon Fiber, concessionaire of the backbone, will operate exclusively on the wholesale market and not compete with telecom operators and ISPs; it will market capacity](<https://www.gabonreview.com/backbone-national-gabon-fiber-prend-les-commandes-et-promet-1-651-km-de-fibre-en-plus/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
