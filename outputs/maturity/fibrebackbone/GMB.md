# Gambia

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [WBBA states GAMTEL's National Broadband Network was deployed in 2019, with 420 km of fibre-optic links and around 31 central office sites](<https://worldbroadbandassociation.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/WBBA-GAMTEL_Final.pdf>). [It extends the 2015 ECOWAN backbone and 947 km of legacy fibre in four rings](<https://worldbroadbandassociation.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/WBBA-GAMTEL_Final.pdf>). [WBBA calls it a national backbone serving all key areas of the country](<https://worldbroadbandassociation.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/WBBA-GAMTEL_Final.pdf>). [The World Broadband Association's case study states the National Broadband Network was deployed in 2019](<https://www.dropbox.com/s/9yvhtd9i4i0vrs0/cr18197.pdf?dl=0>). [It has 420 km of fibre-optic links and access networks around 31 central office sites, extending the 2015 ECOWAN backbone](<https://www.dropbox.com/s/9yvhtd9i4i0vrs0/cr18197.pdf?dl=0>). [GAMTEL owns and operates it, and in July 2020 the minister said the network is earning](<https://www.dropbox.com/s/9yvhtd9i4i0vrs0/cr18197.pdf?dl=0>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region. That it reaches every province or region is not stated.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [WBBA states the national backbone (ECOWAN, NBN, legacy fibre) is accessible to private service providers](<https://worldbroadbandassociation.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/WBBA-GAMTEL_Final.pdf>). [They use lit or dark fibre or buy capacity from GAMTEL, and all MNOs and ISPs use GAMTEL's network](<https://worldbroadbandassociation.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/WBBA-GAMTEL_Final.pdf>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it. It also counts as open where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
