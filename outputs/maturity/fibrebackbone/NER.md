# Niger

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [AidData says over 2,275 km of fibre was installed on three sections (Niamey-Tillabery, Zinder-Diffa, Agadez-Tahoua), construction ended in Q1 2017 and the project was completed and handed over to local authorities; it does not use the word lit or give a date of traffic](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>). [AidData says over 2,275 km of fibre was installed on three sections (Niamey-Tillabery, Zinder-Diffa, Agadez-Tahoua), 95% complete in November 2016, construction ended Q1 2017 and the project was completed and handed over to local authorities](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>). [Steering committee on 13 Mar 2026 recalls provisional acceptance of the fibre works on 14 Nov 2025; the project coordinator says the fibre component's provisional acceptance is still under way after years of delays; AfDB financing over 30 billion FCFA (16.00 loan, 12.76 grant, 2.17 national counterpart)](<https://www.lesahel.org/session-ordinaire-du-comite-de-pilotage-copil-au-titre-de-lannee-2026-faire-le-point-des-progres-realises-et-prendre-des-orientations-pour-la-poursuite-des-activites/>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
