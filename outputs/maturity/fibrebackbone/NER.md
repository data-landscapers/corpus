# Niger

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [AidData says over 2,275 km of fibre was laid on three sections of Niger's national backbone](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>): Niamey-Tillabéry, Zinder-Diffa and Agadez-Tahoua. Construction ended early in 2017 and the network was handed over to the authorities. [China Eximbank lent RMB 623.7 million for it in 2014](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>). [On 14 November 2025 the communications minister provisionally received 1,031 km of fibre laid under the trans-Saharan backbone project](<https://www.latribune.fr/article/afrique/6030907088105514/le-niger-aborde-une-nouvelle-ere-de-connectivite-avec-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique>). [It runs on five routes from Niamey and other towns to the borders of Algeria, Chad, Nigeria, Benin and Burkina Faso](<https://www.afdb.org/fr/news-and-events/press-releases/le-niger-franchit-une-etape-majeure-vers-la-connectivite-haut-debit-avec-la-reception-de-plus-de-1-000-kilometres-de-fibre-optique-88765>). [The AfDB called its commissioning forthcoming](<https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/niger-takes-major-step-towards-high-speed-connectivity-handover-over-1000-km-fibre-optic-cable-88768>). [In March 2026 the project's coordinator said its provisional acceptance was still under way](<https://www.lesahel.org/session-ordinaire-du-comite-de-pilotage-copil-au-titre-de-lannee-2026-faire-le-point-des-progres-realises-et-prendre-des-orientations-pour-la-poursuite-des-activites/>), after years of delay.
