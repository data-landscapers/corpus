# Botswana

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In May 2025 BoFiNet's acting chief executive said it had laid about 12,400 km of fibre](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/05/how-bofinet-plans-to-transform-botswana.html>). He said it covers over 80% of the population, and about 2,000 km more is needed to reach the remaining villages. [In March 2026 the communications minister said about 16,000 km of fibre is laid across Botswana](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90463>). It links the country to South Africa, Namibia, Zimbabwe and Zambia. [BoFiNet reported 99.7% availability across its backbone at its August 2026 annual meeting](<https://na.co.bw/bofinet-hosts-agm-showcasing-enhanced-accountability-and-value-creation/>). [The 2018 National Broadband Strategy describes BoFiNet's backbone as a ring linking all urban centres and major villages](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [BoFiNet holds a wholesale-only licence and sells capacity to internet providers and mobile operators, its acting chief executive said in May 2025](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/05/how-bofinet-plans-to-transform-botswana.html>). [In August 2026 it described its mandate as wholesale open-access provider](<https://na.co.bw/bofinet-hosts-agm-showcasing-enhanced-accountability-and-value-creation/>).
