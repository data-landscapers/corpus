# Botswana

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [BoFiNet reported at its 13 August 2026 AGM 99.7% service availability across its national fibre backbone (News&All Botswana report, no regions or km given)](<https://na.co.bw/bofinet-hosts-agm-showcasing-enhanced-accountability-and-value-creation/>). [Communications minister David Tshere said approximately 16,000 km of fibre optic cable is already laid across Botswana, linking the country internally and to South Africa, Namibia, Zimbabwe and Zambia](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90463>). [The Ministry of Transport and Communications' National Broadband Strategy says Botswana has a fibre optic backbone network in the form of a ring owned by BoFiNet, with private operators such as Mascom also having deployed national backbone infrastructure](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [BoFiNet's national mandate as the country's wholesale open-access fibre and broadband infrastructure provider; buyers are not named](<https://na.co.bw/bofinet-hosts-agm-showcasing-enhanced-accountability-and-value-creation/>). A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
