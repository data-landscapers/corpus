# Kenya

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** The National Optical Fibre Backbone Infrastructure reached [Nairobi and 28 counties in its first phase, in 2012 and 2013](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). The Ministry of ICT says [phase 2 connected the remaining 18 counties and 3 towns in 2017, giving all 47 counties a connection](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). A project record gives phase 2 as [2,100 km of backbone through eight major towns and 36 districts, completed on 7 November 2020](<http://e-promis.treasury.go.ke/e-promis-de/frame.jsp?src=deView.faces?sessionid=14980499248632094&MasterID=1&SubModuleID=1&Hierarchy=A1_RT_OP_PROJECT&UserID=13&iLanguageID=1&jsf_sequence=1&projectid=55855&beansKey=14980499248632094_1498054039500>). ODI wrote in 2025 that [phase 2 was still incomplete](<https://odi.org/en/publications/chinas-infrastructure-finance-in-africa-impacts-and-lessons/>). By March 2022 the government had built [about 9,000 km of backbone fibre, reaching sub-county level](<https://kippra.or.ke/leveraging-on-digitalization-to-promote-public-service-delivery-in-kenya/>). Treasury figures put [the public-sector fibre network at 30,454 km, up from 22,486 km in 2022](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-opens-fibre-backbone-tender/KjlyrvwBxmGqk6am>). In October 2026 the ICT principal secretary said [a 17,000 km project to link all public institutions is under way](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-targets-grassroots-internet-connectivity-to-open-up-global-digital-market-for-youth/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** The backbone is [the government's, run through the Ministry of ICT](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). In September 2026 the ICT Authority [opened a tender for national backbone links](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/kenya-opens-fibre-backbone-tender/KjlyrvwBxmGqk6am>). Operators run their own long-distance fibre beside it: [Liquid Intelligent Technologies](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/10/interview-liquid-bets-on-ai-as-next-growth-frontier-for-africas-fibre-network/>) and [Paratus, whose Nairobi-Mombasa route is live](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>). In August 2024 Bayobab [completed a second route of over 1,000 km from Mombasa to Malaba and Busia](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-kenya-to-boost-connectivity-with-mombasa-to-malaba-fibre-project/>). It [offers that route to internet providers on open-access terms](<https://bayobab.africa/bayobab-kenya-to-boost-connectivity-with-mombasa-to-malaba-fibre-project/>).
