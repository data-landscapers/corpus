# National fibre backbone: long-distance fibre across the country

*As at 2026-10-18.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy 2020–2030 and the Programme for Infrastructure Development in Africa ask member states to build national backbones that reach every region and join their neighbours' networks, and to share infrastructure among operators. This study asks whether a backbone is being built, is in service, reaches every region, and carries other operators' traffic; it does not measure length, capacity or price.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says the country has no national fibre backbone |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: being built |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 2: in service between the capital and other regions |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 3: reaching every province or region |
| 5 Leading | Yes to 4: every region reached, and capacity sold to other operators |

- The Minister of Finance told Parliament that optical fibre deployment is being implemented, with a 96km stretch from Roma to Thaba-Tseka underway; the speech names no contract and gives no completion date, and as_of is the speech date (19 February 2025) ([source, 2025-02-19](https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf)).

### South Sudan: 2 Nascent

A national fibre backbone was being built in 2026; none is known to be in service.

- Tech Review Africa reports NCT South Sudan and Bayobab moved into the execution phase, with official kickoff of the National Backbone Fiber Network Project about one month before the 2026-03-04 article, including the Juba to Nadapal eastern segment at the Kenya border ([source, 2026-02](https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project)).

### Guinea-Bissau: 1 Absent

A 2025 source says the country has no national fibre backbone.

- Agence Ecofin reports in August 2025 that Guinea-Bissau's international connectivity is confined to the coast and the country still needs a national backbone to link its main cities ([source, 2025-08-29](https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad)).
