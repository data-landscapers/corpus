# Nigeria

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Nairametrics, from NCC data, says the 101,148.36 km of fibre is deployed across all 36 states and the FCT, though concentrated in Lagos and Abuja](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/09/fg-incorporates-company-to-deliver-90000km-nationwide-fibre-network/>). [The 101,000 km network stretches across all 36 states and the FCT; NCC state data lists fibre in every state (lowest Ebonyi 586.92 km, Bayelsa 656.87 km)](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/how-nigeria-built-101000km-of-fibre-that-bypasses-130m-nigerians/>). [WTES COO Chidi Ajuzie, presenting at the 7th Telecom Sector Sustainability Forum, put Nigeria's current baseline at approximately 68,000 km of terrestrial fibre-optic backhaul against 120,000 km needed for complete national coverage](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigerias-52000km-fibre-gap-drives-sovereign-bond-plan/>). [TechCabal reports MTN Nigeria's national terrestrial fibre network spans more than 43,000 km and links tens of thousands of base stations](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/14/mtn-controls-55-of-nigerias-tiny-fibre-market/>). [Nigeria's existing fibre backbone is roughly 30,000 to 35,000 km, to be extended by Project BRIDGE](<https://techsoma.africa/nigeria-project-bridge-fibre-network-2028/>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/09/fg-incorporates-company-to-deliver-90000km-nationwide-fibre-network/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
