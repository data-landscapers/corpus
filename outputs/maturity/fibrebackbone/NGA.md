# Nigeria

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** The regulator's state data put [101,148 km of fibre across all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/09/fg-incorporates-company-to-deliver-90000km-nationwide-fibre-network/>) in August 2026. [Lagos and the capital territory hold 18% of it](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/09/fg-incorporates-company-to-deliver-90000km-nationwide-fibre-network/>). [The smallest totals are Ebonyi's 587 km and Bayelsa's 657 km](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/how-nigeria-built-101000km-of-fibre-that-bypasses-130m-nigerians/>). The figure counts every operator's fibre, and [MTN Nigeria alone runs more than 43,000 km](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/14/mtn-controls-55-of-nigerias-tiny-fibre-market/>). The World Bank said in September 2025 that [Nigeria has no national backbone network, so operators duplicate one another's routes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091125121538069/txt/BOSIB-f3e6411b-ac7d-490f-a0ef-af1e4a6d1a28.txt>). It put [deployed terrestrial fibre at 30,000 km](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091125121538069/txt/BOSIB-f3e6411b-ac7d-490f-a0ef-af1e4a6d1a28.txt>). The state-owned Galaxy Backbone says [its network covers 27 state capitals and links Abuja to Lagos](<https://technologytimes.ng/galaxy-backbone-lights-up-fibre-in-3-cities/>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** The government plans Project BRIDGE to take [the national network from 35,000 km to about 125,000 km](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/10/07/world-bank-approves-new-financing-to-enhance-nigeria-digital-infrastructure-and-health-security>). It is to be built through [a company named Bridge Open Access](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/nigerias-52000km-fibre-gap-drives-sovereign-bond-plan/>). In August 2026 the minister said [contracting for the 90,000 km network was complete and laying would start within weeks](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/fg-to-rollout-90000km-fibre-optic-network-tijani/>).
