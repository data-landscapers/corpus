# DR Congo

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications told the Council of Ministers that 7,000 km of fibre is currently deployed, to reach over 18,500 km once 11,500 km more is added under the Digital Transformation Project begun a year earlier](<https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de>). [Minister Augustin Kibassa Maliba said about 12,000 km of a planned 55,000 km national fibre backbone was completed, mainly linking major urban hubs](<https://reg-tech.co/2025/05/06/drc-e-government-initiative-blockchain>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://deskeco.com/2026/08/03/transformation-numerique-en-rdc-encore-11500-km-de-fibre-optique-deployer-pour-atteindre-un-total-de>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
