# Algeria

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In April 2025 Algérie360 reported a national fibre optic network of 260,000 km](<https://www.algerie360.com/zerrouki-devoile-la-feuille-de-route-de-la-5g-et-les-avancees-du-service-universel/>). [It covers 95 per cent of the territory](<https://www.algerie360.com/zerrouki-devoile-la-feuille-de-route-de-la-5g-et-les-avancees-du-service-universel/>). [In February 2025 Algérie Télécom and Huawei launched a national 400G WDM transmission project](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/25/algeria-telecom-and-huawei-ignite-a-new-era-of-high-speed-connectivity/>). [It is to cover the whole country](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/25/algeria-telecom-and-huawei-ignite-a-new-era-of-high-speed-connectivity/>), per a vendor release. No source names the provinces the network reaches, or the towns its main links join.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [the national network](<https://www.algerie360.com/zerrouki-devoile-la-feuille-de-route-de-la-5g-et-les-avancees-du-service-universel/>). Nothing found says it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
