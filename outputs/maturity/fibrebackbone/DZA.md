# Algeria

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In 2026 Algérie Télécom's chief executive said the group had finished nearly 300,000 km of backbone fibre covering every wilaya](<https://alger16.dz/?p=60388>). [In October 2026 L'Echo d'Algérie reported the same length of cable laid across all wilayas](<https://lechodalgerie.dz/fibre-optique-en-lalgerie-4-millions-de-foyers-raccordes/>), from the cities to remote areas. [In April 2025 Algérie360 had reported a national network of 260,000 km covering 95 per cent of the territory](<https://www.algerie360.com/zerrouki-devoile-la-feuille-de-route-de-la-5g-et-les-avancees-du-service-universel/>). [In February 2025 Algérie Télécom and Huawei launched a national 400G WDM transmission project](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/25/algeria-telecom-and-huawei-ignite-a-new-era-of-high-speed-connectivity/>). A vendor release says it is to cover the whole country.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In November 2024 Algérie Télécom told an ITU meeting that more than 40,000 km of its fibre was available to other operators](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session3/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20Interconnexion%20UIT_MrAmirKahli.pdf>). [It sells that fibre as leased lines and as irrevocable rights of use](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session3/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20Interconnexion%20UIT_MrAmirKahli.pdf>). [In 2026 its chief executive said the backbone gives the mobile operators the capacity their networks need](<https://alger16.dz/?p=60388>).
