# Algeria

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Algerie Telecom and Huawei announced the official launch of the national 400G WDM project, building an all-optical transmission foundation covering the whole country (vendor/PR-wire copy](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/25/algeria-telecom-and-huawei-ignite-a-new-era-of-high-speed-connectivity/>). These say a national backbone is being built under a contract or with works begun, which answers the first of the study's four questions. That any of it is in service between the capital and another region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/02/25/algeria-telecom-and-huawei-ignite-a-new-era-of-high-speed-connectivity/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
