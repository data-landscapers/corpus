# Lesotho

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Minister of Finance told Parliament that optical fibre deployment is being implemented, with a 96km stretch from Roma to Thaba-Tseka underway](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>). These say a national backbone is being built under a contract or with works begun, which answers the first of the study's four questions. That any of it is in service between the capital and another region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
