# Chad

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Minister Haliki Choua Mahamat said on 2026-10-09 that Chad has about 6,000 km of optical fibre, with a target of 14,000 km; the text does not say which regions it links](<https://www.tachad.com/tchad-le-ministre-des-telecommunications-echange-avec-les-partenaires-europeens-sur-la-transformation-numerique/>). [Minister Haliki Choua Mahamat told the World Bank on 2026-09-15 that national fibre coverage is incomplete, with six provinces still not connected by fibre, and about 8,000 km still to deploy](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1709-141662-infrastructure-numerique-le-tchad-a-encore-besoin-de-8000-km-de-fibre-optique>). [At a ministerial review on 19 May 2026 the PMICE coordinator reported 1,275 km of fibre deployed so far on the national backbone works, with Huawei as contractor under Gulf Consultancy supervision; the document does not say it is in service](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/pmice-le-tchad-accelere-les-verifications-autour-du-data-center-et-des-infrastructures-numeriques/>). [DCD reports the Ministry announced commencement on 20 December 2025 of PMICE Phase I (EUR 175.1m), which includes deploying 1,200 km of fibre optic cabling](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/chad-invests-175m-in-national-data-center-and-networks>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.tachad.com/tchad-le-ministre-des-telecommunications-echange-avec-les-partenaires-europeens-sur-la-transformation-numerique/>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
