# Chad

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In November 2026 Minister Haliki Choua Mahamat put completed fibre at 4,400 km](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/chad/>), against a target of 14,000 km by 2030. A further 2,700 km were being built and 6,900 km had no funding. [In September 2026 he told the World Bank that six provinces still had no fibre](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1709-141662-infrastructure-numerique-le-tchad-a-encore-besoin-de-8000-km-de-fibre-optique>), with about 8,000 km left to lay. [Russell Southwood reported in November 2026 that Safitel's fibre connects 26 cities and towns](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>). It runs from N'Djamena to Moundou, Doba and Abéché, while routes in the south-east are unbuilt. [The ministry said a 1,200 km national backbone was provisionally accepted at Sarh on 20 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51602-chad-completes-first-phase-of-205mln-digital-connectivity-project>) and is now operational. [By May 2026 the PMICE coordinator counted 1,275 km of fibre laid](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/pmice-le-tchad-accelere-les-verifications-autour-du-data-center-et-des-infrastructures-numeriques/>), with Huawei as contractor.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy capacity on [the national fibre](<https://www.tachad.com/tchad-le-ministre-des-telecommunications-echange-avec-les-partenaires-europeens-sur-la-transformation-numerique/>), or that it runs on wholesale terms. [In September 2025 the government ordered Airtel and Moov Africa to connect to the national fibre network](<https://afriqueitnews.com/tech-media/tchad-ordonne-operateurs-connecter-reseau-national-fibre-optique/>) within seven days.
