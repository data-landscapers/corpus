# Sierra Leone

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** Sierra Leone's backbone [reached 14 of its 16 districts by April 2026](<https://www.slena.gov.sl/post/sierra-leone-s-president-julius-maada-bio-commissions-natca-hq-reinforces-commitment-to-digital-inn>), Communications Minister Salima Bah said. [In January 2025 she put the growth at 8 to 14 districts, through 1,641 km of new fibre](<https://moice.gov.sl/minister-salima-outlines-accomplishments-in-the-tech-sector/>). [On 29 April 2026 she told Parliament's ICT committee that work was under way to reach Falaba and Karene](<https://libertyonlinetv.com/2026/04/30/15-million-world-bank-grant-secured-for-second-fibre-cable-landing-says-minister-of-communications-salima-bah/>), the two districts left. Works on the second phase, [721.5 km of fibre to 15 cities in the north, east and south, had begun by September 2020](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Address-by-His-Excellency-Julius-Maada-Bio-President-of-the-Republic-and-Commander-in-Chief-of-the-Republic-of-Sierra-Leone-Armed-Forces-on-the-Occasion-of-the-State-Opening-of-Parliament.pdf>), under a Huawei contract financed by China Eximbank.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [the national backbone](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/sierra-leone-invests-400m-in-telecoms-60-usage-gap-remains>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. The nearest held fact is the minister's May 2026 count of 14 districts connected.
