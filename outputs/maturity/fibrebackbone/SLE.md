# Sierra Leone

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [Minister of Communication, Technology and Innovation Salima Bah said the national fibre backbone had been expanded to 14 of the country's 16 districts (article of 2026-05-05](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/sierra-leone-invests-400m-in-telecoms-60-usage-gap-remains>). [Minister Salima Bah said at the NatCA headquarters commissioning that 14 of 16 districts are now connected by fibre (SLENA report of 2026-04-17](<https://www.slena.gov.sl/post/sierra-leone-s-president-julius-maada-bio-commissions-natca-hq-reinforces-commitment-to-digital-inn>). [Construction of Phase 2 of the National Fibre Optic Backbone Infrastructure Project, a 721.5 km fibre network covering 15 major cities in the northern, eastern and southern regions, had commenced by September 2020 under a Huawei contract signed 11 April 2019 and financed by a China Eximbank RMB 207 million loan (AidData record)](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Address-by-His-Excellency-Julius-Maada-Bio-President-of-the-Republic-and-Commander-in-Chief-of-the-Republic-of-Sierra-Leone-Armed-Forces-on-the-Occasion-of-the-State-Opening-of-Parliament.pdf>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/sierra-leone-invests-400m-in-telecoms-60-usage-gap-remains>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
