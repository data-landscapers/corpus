# Zambia

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** In November 2026 a ministry official said [the national fibre backbone had grown from about 15,000 km in 2021 to more than 42,000 km](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/11/03/comesa-world-bank-back-regional-drive-to-connect-180-million-people-through-digital-inclusion/>). A 2022 gap analysis by the regulator ZICTA found [backbone points of presence in 83 of 116 districts](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/committee_reports/REPORT%20ON%20THE%20COMMMITTEE%20ON%20MEDIA%2C%20INFORMATION%20AND%20COMMUNICATION%20TECHNOLOGIES.pdf>). Another 11 had transit fibre without a point of presence, and 22 had neither. It put the links needed to close the gap at 18, totalling 1,685 km. In January 2025 a World Bank project document [referred to 10,000 km of fibre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt>), to be extended by at least 2,000 km.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** No source found says other operators buy or lease capacity on the national backbone. None says it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank project plans to extend the backbone and regional network](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt>).
