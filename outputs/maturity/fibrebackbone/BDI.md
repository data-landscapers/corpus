# Burundi

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [ARCT states BBS's fibre (1,750 km) covers all provinces of Burundi and that the national territory is now covered by fibre optic, as at 31 December 2024](<https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RAPPORT-DES-INDICATEURS-DU-SECTEUR-DES-TIC-EDITION-2024-VALIDE-ET-PUBLIE-1.pdf>). [ARCT's 2024 ICT indicators report lists BBS (Burundi Backbone System) as a fibre infrastructure operator with 1,750 km of fibre deployed at 31 December 2024, covering all provinces](<https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RAPPORT-DES-INDICATEURS-DU-SECTEUR-DES-TIC-EDITION-2024-VALIDE-ET-PUBLIE-1.pdf>). Together these say the fibre reaches every province or region, which answers the first three of the study's four questions: it was built, it is in service between the capital and other regions, and it reaches them all. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing found says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://arct.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/RAPPORT-DES-INDICATEURS-DU-SECTEUR-DES-TIC-EDITION-2024-VALIDE-ET-PUBLIE-1.pdf>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
