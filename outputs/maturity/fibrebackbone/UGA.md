# Uganda

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [In November 2024 the ICT ministry said 4,387 km of government fibre reached 57 of 135 districts](<https://businessfocus.co.ug/manifesto-week-ict-ministry-reveals-key-achievements-as-internet-penetration-increases-to-67/>). It connected 1,567 government sites. [NITA-U says the first four phases of the National Backbone Infrastructure linked 53 district headquarters and 11 border stations](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-12/Press%20Release%20Phase%205%20Launch-1.pdf>). [On 4 December 2024 President Museveni launched the fifth phase, financed by China Exim Bank and built by Huawei](<https://english.news.cn/africa/20241206/328b1ba0410f42f0954291b9efdcfed6/c.html>). [In July 2026 NITA-U said fibre had reached 41 more districts](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>), with work continuing toward national coverage. [In May 2025 Bayobab opened a 260 km route from Kampala to Malaba on the Kenyan border](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/kampala-malaba-get-new-fibre-optic-route-NV_210273_052026>).

**Capacity sold to other operators.** [In April 2025 the ICT minister told Parliament that government agencies are the main users of the backbone](<https://www.parliament.go.ug/news/3663/utel-take-operationalisation-national-backbone-infrastructure>). It has few private-sector clients, and its sale to them is to pass to Uganda Telecom.
