# Uganda

## Long-distance fibre across the country

**Built, in service and reaching every region.** [The Ministry of ICT's FY2025/26 policy statement reports 1,567 sites connected to the National Backbone Infrastructure and 4,387 km of optical fibre cable laid in total, as the position at the time of writing (March 2025, the document's published date); it names no regions](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/ministerial-policy-statement-mps-20252026.pdf>). [Bayobab Uganda (MTN Group subsidiary) launched on 6 May 2025 a 260 km fibre route along the Uganda Railway line from Kampala to Malaba on the Kenyan border, built December 2024 to February 2025, which Bayobab says completes the Kampala-Mombasa backbone and carries over 1 Tbit/s](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/business/kampala-malaba-get-new-fibre-optic-route-NV_210273_052026>). [Nile Post describes the NBI as Uganda's government-owned national fibre-optic network connecting public institutions and offering access to a nationwide network; Dated by the article (2026-10-09)](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/376831/museveni-orders-cancellation-of-utel-deal-calls-investors-bafere-over-missing-25-million>). These place backbone fibre in service between the capital and at least one other region, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. That it reaches every province or region is not stated. A length in kilometres is given where a source states one; length sets no stage. By July 2026 NITA-U reported [fibre extended to 41 more districts under the National Backbone Infrastructure](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>), with work continuing towards nationwide coverage.

**Capacity sold to other operators.** Nothing held says other operators buy capacity on [this fibre](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/ministerial-policy-statement-mps-20252026.pdf>), or that it runs on open-access or wholesale terms. A backbone counts as open where other operators buy or lease capacity on it, or where it runs on open-access or wholesale terms, whoever owns it.
