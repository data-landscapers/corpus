# Rwanda

## Land

**System in service.** [In February 2026 Environment Minister Bernadette Arakwiye said Rwanda's Land Administration Information System (LAIS) holds all land and owner data](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>). [She said it handles land registration, boundary correction and transfers](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>). [It processes over 800,000 files a year and received 864,000 in 2025](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>). [In January 2026 the National Land Authority's head of land use and mapping said more than 14 systems are connected to LAIS](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>). [The same presentation describes a functioning land administration system](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>).

**Maps or valuation.** [In January 2026 the National Land Authority said LAIS is a national registry with a spatial database](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>).

**Linked to tax.** [In February 2026 Minister Arakwiye said LAIS is connected to the tax payment system](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>). [She said it is also connected to Irembo, among other national systems](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>).
