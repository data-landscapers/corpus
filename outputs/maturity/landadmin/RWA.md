# Rwanda

## Land

**System in service.** [Minister of Environment Bernadette Arakwiye said Rwanda's LAIS electronic system holds all land and owner data and handles land registration, boundary correction and transfers, with over 800,000 files a year processed and 864,000 files received in 2025](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>). [The National Land Authority's land use and mapping head lists LAIS among Rwanda's digital systems, with more than 14 systems connected to it and a functioning land administration system established](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Maps or valuation.** [The National Land Authority presentation says Rwanda established a National Registry system (LAIS) with a spatial database](<https://www.un.org/globalgeospatial/sites/default/files/2026-04/rwanda_land_administration_case_study_unggkic.pdf>).

**Linked to tax.** [Minister Arakwiye said LAIS has been connected to other national systems including Irembo and the tax payment system](<https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/>).
