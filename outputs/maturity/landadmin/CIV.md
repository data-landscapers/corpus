# Cote d'Ivoire

## Land

**System in service.** [AFOR reports that the Bongouanou sub-prefectural land committee examined and validated five rural land certificate files on 12 March 2026 using digital maps and geospatial data, and the certificates were signed electronically](<https://afor.ci/news/nos-actualites/105>). [The World Bank aide-memoire of the 21-31 January 2025 mission says AFOR's DIGIFOR and SCCARTO applications are functional and that an ordinance to make SIFOR the single digital rural land register was still to be re-presented in mid-February 2025, with cadastre title data still awaited](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021325040027199/pdf/P179338-ea58f66e-1b11-40a4-a9ed-c2be7b9f4434.pdf>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://afor.ci/news/nos-actualites/105>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://afor.ci/news/nos-actualites/105>).
