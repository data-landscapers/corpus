# Cote d'Ivoire

## Land

**System in service.** [On 12 March 2026 a land committee in Bongouanou validated five rural land certificates](<https://afor.ci/news/nos-actualites/105>). [It used digital maps and geospatial data, and the certificates were signed electronically](<https://afor.ci/news/nos-actualites/105>). [In January 2025 a World Bank mission found AFOR's DIGIFOR and SCCARTO applications working](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021325040027199/pdf/P179338-ea58f66e-1b11-40a4-a9ed-c2be7b9f4434.pdf>). [An ordinance making SIFOR the single digital rural land register was still pending](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021325040027199/pdf/P179338-ea58f66e-1b11-40a4-a9ed-c2be7b9f4434.pdf>). [Cadastre title data were still awaited](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021325040027199/pdf/P179338-ea58f66e-1b11-40a4-a9ed-c2be7b9f4434.pdf>).
