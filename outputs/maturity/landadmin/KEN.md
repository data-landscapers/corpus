# Kenya

## Land

**System in service.** [Kenyans.co.ke reports that Ardhisasa processes land searches, transfers and title and lease registration](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [PS Nixon Korir told Parliament in August 2026 that it runs in Nairobi, Murang'a, Mombasa Island and Isiolo](<https://radiogeneration.co.ke/why-ardhisasa-expansion-has-fallen-behind-ps-korir_43436>). Machakos, Kajiado and Baringo were being digitised. [Lands Secretary Alice Wahome said in August 2025 that fifteen more counties will join](<https://capitalfm.africa/govt-to-spend-sh35bn-to-expand-land-record-digitization-to-15-more-counties/>), at a cost of at least Sh35 billion. [The Law Society says searches, transfers and registrations stopped when Ardhisasa went down in September 2026](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>).

**Maps or valuation.** [Kenyans.co.ke lists survey, mapping and valuation among Ardhisasa's services](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The State Department for Lands says standardised valuation data in the National Stamp Duty Module sets stamp duty](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent>).

**Linked to tax.** [The State Department says stamp duty is assessed and paid through the Module within Ardhisasa, via Ardhipay](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent>). It collected KES1.946 billion in two months. [Korir said in August 2026 that the Module supports transactions across the country](<https://radiogeneration.co.ke/why-ardhisasa-expansion-has-fallen-behind-ps-korir_43436>). [The Ngong registrar says online stamp-duty payment has made transactions easier to trace](<https://www.fao.org/kenya/news/newsdetails/from-congestion-to-faster-services--how-digitization-is-transforming-kenya-s-land-registries/en>).

***Also on file***. [Ardhipay](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent>).

***Not found***. Ardhisasa is still being rolled out section by section, so not every county's transactions are on it.
