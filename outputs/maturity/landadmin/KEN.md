# Kenya

## Land

**System in service.** [Kenyans.co.ke reports ArdhiSasa is the government's digital platform for processing land services, including land searches, property transfers and title and lease registration, which an outage notice said were affected](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The Law Society of Kenya says Ardhisasa is a central platform for delivering land administration services, with searches, transfers and registrations disrupted when it was unavailable from 2026-09-01](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/396368/lsk-demands-urgent-restoration-of-ardhisasa-platform-amid-land-transaction-delays>). [The State Department for Lands and Physical Planning says it is progressively expanding the National Land Information Management System (Ardhisasa) to more registration sections and remains committed to a nationwide rollout](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-expands-digitization-mombasa-bringing-more-land-transactions-ardhisasa>).

**Maps or valuation.** [Kenyans.co.ke lists survey and mapping and valuation among the services offered through ArdhiSasa alongside land administration](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The State Department for Lands says standardized valuation data in the National Stamp Duty Module ensures stamp duty is assessed accurately](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent>).

**Linked to tax.** [Kenyans.co.ke lists stamp duty payments and land-rent payments among the services offered through ArdhiSasa](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services>). [The State Department for Lands says stamp duty is assessed and paid digitally through the NSDM within ArdhiSasa via Ardhipay, with KES1.946 billion collected in two months](<https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent>).
