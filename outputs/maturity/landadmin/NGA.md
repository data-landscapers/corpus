# Nigeria

## Land

**System in service.** [Kwara State launched its KW-GIS land information system](<https://von.gov.ng/kwara-government-launches-digital-land-administration-process/>), where investors and titleholders register land and process certificates of occupancy online. [Lagos moved land title applications from paper to its e-GIS portal in January 2024](<https://businessday.ng/life/article/lagos-revolutionises-land-acquisition-as-sanwo-olu-launches-automated-application-portal/>). [Cross River State's 360Lands registry has issued 188 certificates of occupancy](<https://guardian.ng/technology/tech-firm-to-tackle-delays-in-c-of-o-processing/>), its developer said in June 2026. [The federal housing minister said in July 2026 that a central lands repository system is being built](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fgs-land-reform-initiative-to-drive-increased-delivery-of-affordable-housing-for-low-income-informal-workers-darma/>).

**Maps or valuation.** [The KW-GIS project manager lists digital parcel mapping and land records integration among its features](<https://von.gov.ng/kwara-government-launches-digital-land-administration-process/>). [Lagos's e-GIS joins archived records with titles and holds each parcel's history in the state cadastre](<https://businessday.ng/life/article/lagos-revolutionises-land-acquisition-as-sanwo-olu-launches-automated-application-portal/>). [360Lands links the Cross River lands ministry, the surveyor general and physical planning](<https://guardian.ng/technology/tech-firm-to-tackle-delays-in-c-of-o-processing/>), with parcels on satellite maps.

**Linked to tax.** [The Kwara revenue service says its payment platform is integrated with the land system](<https://von.gov.ng/kwara-government-launches-digital-land-administration-process/>). Land payments are made online.
