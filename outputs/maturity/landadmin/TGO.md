# Togo

## Land

**System in service.** [Togo Officiel said in 2024 that obtaining a land title is fully dematerialised](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/tic/1609-9722-services-publics-zoom-sur-les-reformes-initiees-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation>), through an online portal for cadastre procedures. [Togo First reports the land process digitised through the Guichet unique du foncier](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/1510-20352-foncier-apres-la-nomination-de-son-dg-l-andf-face-au-defi-de-son-operationnalisation>). Since 17 August 2026 surveyors must send georeferenced parcel plans to it online. [A May 2026 notice seeks a firm to build a land information system at the revenue office](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/ami-togo-recrutement-dun-cabinet-charge-du-developpement-de-lapplication-du-systeme-dinformations-du-foncier-sif-loffice-togolais-des-recettes-otr-pagdsp>), with links to tax and civil status.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [surveyors' parcel plans, sent online since August 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/1510-20352-foncier-apres-la-nomination-de-son-dg-l-andf-face-au-defi-de-son-operationnalisation>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the planned tax links of the new system](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/ami-togo-recrutement-dun-cabinet-charge-du-developpement-de-lapplication-du-systeme-dinformations-du-foncier-sif-loffice-togolais-des-recettes-otr-pagdsp>).
