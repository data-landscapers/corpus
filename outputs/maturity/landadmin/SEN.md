# Senegal

## Land

**System in service.** [PROCASEF says it has built 128 land offices fitted with SIFCOM, a communal land information system](<https://procasef.com/financement-additionnel-du-procasef-des-rencontres-dinformation-avec-60-communes-preselectionnees-pour-consolider-la-securisation-fonciere-au-senegal/>). [In December 2025 it said SIFCOM is planned to run in the project's 136 communal land offices](<https://procasef.com/fidak-2025-panel-sur-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-foncieres-le-procasef-au-coeur-de-la-modernisation-du-foncier-senegalais/>). An electronic land book is to replace the paper registers.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [SIFCOM's planned rollout to 136 communal land offices](<https://procasef.com/fidak-2025-panel-sur-la-dematerialisation-des-procedures-foncieres-le-procasef-au-coeur-de-la-modernisation-du-foncier-senegalais/>), announced at the FIDAK fair in December 2025.

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the 128 land offices fitted with SIFCOM](<https://procasef.com/financement-additionnel-du-procasef-des-rencontres-dinformation-avec-60-communes-preselectionnees-pour-consolider-la-securisation-fonciere-au-senegal/>), which PROCASEF also furnished.
