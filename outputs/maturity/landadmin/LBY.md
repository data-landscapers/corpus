# Libya

## Land

**System in service.** [In April 2025 the Real Estate Registration Authority issued its first electronic real estate certificate, LANA reported](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331206&lang=ar>). [The certificate carries an encrypted QR code and was issued at the South Tripoli registration office](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331206&lang=ar>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [in April 2025 LANA described the certificate as a step towards building a real estate database](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331206&lang=ar>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [LANA called the certificate a model for moving real estate registration to digital form](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331206&lang=ar>).
