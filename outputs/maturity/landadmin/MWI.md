# Malawi

## Land

**System in service.** [ITWeb Africa reports that Malawi launched its web-based Land Information Management System in February 2024](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). Registered users submit documents and pay ground rent and fees on it, while some transactions remain manual. [It tracks land transactions in Blantyre](<https://www.247malawi.com/malawi-takes-a-leap-forward-in-land-management-with-digital-platform/>), under the Digital Malawi project. [About 130,000 customary parcels had been registered in it by July 2026](<https://www.ednews.africa/p/new-hope-for-malawis-farmers-as-agcom>). [The Lands Minister said in October 2026 that lease approvals are now digital](<https://www.247malawi.com/one-year-of-dpp-how-chipungu-is-securing-land-for-every-malawian-exclusive-interview/>). The ministry is checking its paper files against the system.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the digital lease approvals of 2026](<https://www.247malawi.com/one-year-of-dpp-how-chipungu-is-securing-land-for-every-malawian-exclusive-interview/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [ground rent and fees paid on the system](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>).
