# Malawi

## Land

**System in service.** [ITWeb Africa reports Malawi launched its web-based Land Information Management System in February 2024, where registered users submit documents, receive notifications and pay ground rent and application fees, while some transactions remain manual](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). [The Ministry of Lands is reviewing LIMS workflows and developing digital procedures to move the remaining manual land transactions onto the platform, per a ministry deputy director of LIMS](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m>). [The Ministry of Lands says it is implementing the Land Information Management System with the World Bank's AGCOM project, with a contract signed 10 May 2021 for a 20-month rollout through to go-live](<https://lands.gov.mw/index.php/our-projects/lims>).
