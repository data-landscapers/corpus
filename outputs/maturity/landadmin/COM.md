# Comoros

## Land

**System in service.** [The head of the Comoros Cartography Unit said in April 2026 that a land information system is being set up](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/nair-aboubacar-2265a092_un-moment-historique-pour-les-comores-au-activity-7449676142535692288-noIc>). He called it a national priority. The World Bank finances it through a regional climate resilience project, and the unit is building it.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the cartography unit's account of April 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/nair-aboubacar-2265a092_un-moment-historique-pour-les-comores-au-activity-7449676142535692288-noIc>), which names the system and its funding but not its functions.

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the system's World Bank funding](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/nair-aboubacar-2265a092_un-moment-historique-pour-les-comores-au-activity-7449676142535692288-noIc>), through a regional project on climate resilience.
