# DR Congo

## Land

**System in service.** [Afrique-sur7 reports that the DRC relaunched e-Foncier, its cadastre digitisation project, with the Dubai firm Oxinus](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance>). The 2022 contract with eProseed had failed. [A workshop on 26 February 2026 evaluated the pilots of the land information system](<https://fonaredd-rdc.org/rdc-restitution-des-etudes-sur-levaluation-des-initiatives-de-numerisation-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere-et-de-la-mise-en-place-de-latlas-des-circonscriptions-foncieres/>). It proposed an e-SIF 2.0 of digitised archives, a GIS cadastre and digitised transactions, still to be deployed. [On 28 August 2026 the land minister told the Council of Ministers](<https://beto.cd/cadastre-numerisation-archives-foncieres-rntfi/>) that 250 staff had sorted 16,622 paper land files in 17 Kinshasa districts. She presented this as the step before any digitisation of the cadastre. The ministry still has 146 land offices to equip before the national register of land titles can operate.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the proposed e-SIF 2.0, with a GIS cadastre](<https://fonaredd-rdc.org/rdc-restitution-des-etudes-sur-levaluation-des-initiatives-de-numerisation-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere-et-de-la-mise-en-place-de-latlas-des-circonscriptions-foncieres/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the relaunch of e-Foncier](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance>).
