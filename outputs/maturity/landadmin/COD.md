# DR Congo

## Land

**System in service.** [Afrique-sur7 reports that the DRC relaunched e-Foncier, its cadastre digitisation project, with the Dubai firm Oxinus](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance>). The 2022 contract with eProseed had failed. [A workshop on 26 February 2026 evaluated the pilots of the land information system](<https://fonaredd-rdc.org/rdc-restitution-des-etudes-sur-levaluation-des-initiatives-de-numerisation-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere-et-de-la-mise-en-place-de-latlas-des-circonscriptions-foncieres/>). It proposed an e-SIF 2.0 of digitised archives, a GIS cadastre and digitised transactions, still to be deployed. [The relaunch followed a meeting that folded e-Foncier into the country's land management](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the proposed e-SIF 2.0, with a GIS cadastre](<https://fonaredd-rdc.org/rdc-restitution-des-etudes-sur-levaluation-des-initiatives-de-numerisation-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere-et-de-la-mise-en-place-de-latlas-des-circonscriptions-foncieres/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the relaunch of e-Foncier](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance>).
