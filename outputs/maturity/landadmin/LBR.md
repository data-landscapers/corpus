# Liberia

## Land

**System in service.** [The Liberia Land Authority signed a three-year agreement on 16 October 2025 to develop the Liberia Land Information System](<https://analystliberiaonline.com/lla-deepens-land-reforms-launches-new-deed-form-guidelines-digital-system/>). [Pilots in Montserrado and Margibi were to follow](<https://lla.gov.lr/index.php/publications/news-article/item/65-liberia-land-authority-and-south-korean-partners-signed-landmark-mou-to-develop-liberia-land-information-system>). [The Swedish-funded project's manager said in June 2026 that it had introduced a Digital Land Administration System at the Authority](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/swedish-funded-land-reform-project-strengthens-liberia-land-governance/>). [The Authority's chairman listed making the older Land Administration System operational among the project's results](<https://analystliberiaonline.com/lla-deepens-land-reforms-launches-new-deed-form-guidelines-digital-system/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the agreement of October 2025](<https://analystliberiaonline.com/lla-deepens-land-reforms-launches-new-deed-form-guidelines-digital-system/>).
