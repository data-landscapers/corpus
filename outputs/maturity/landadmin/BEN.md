# Benin

## Land

**System in service.** [Land transactions in Benin are done in e-Foncier Bénin](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>), and a parcel with a cadastre certificate is sold at the mairie. [The national land agency said in November 2026](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>) that the platform registers parcels, issues cadastral certificates and updates records. It then held 643,993 parcels in 12 communes. [La Nation reported the platform in service from 1 January 2025](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/reforme-du-e-foncier-au-benin-plus-de-491-000-parcelles-enregistrees-dans-12-communes>) with mairies, notaries and surveyors. From May 2022 to December 2024 it took 11,418 online requests, property transfers and mortgage registration among them. [Parakou officials named further functions as next steps in September 2026](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>).
