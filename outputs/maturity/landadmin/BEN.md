# Benin

## Land

**System in service.** [Fraternité states that with the cadastre registration certificate a parcel can be sold legally at the mairie and that all operations are done directly in e-Foncier Bénin](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>). [La Nation reports that the e-Foncier platform has been operational since 1 January 2025 with mairies, notaries and surveyors, and that nine e-Services on service-public.bj included 163 property transfer requests and mortgage registration, with 11,418 online requests from May 2022 to December 2024 (ANDF)](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/reforme-du-e-foncier-au-benin-plus-de-491-000-parcelles-enregistrees-dans-12-communes>). [Parakou municipal officials named formalising land transactions through e-Foncier Bénin and adding platform functions for recasement attestations, mutation certificates and compulsions as next steps (reported by Matin Libre)](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/09/04/cadastre-a-parakou-196-474-parcelles-enregistrees-les-pays-bas-saluent-les-avancees/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>).
