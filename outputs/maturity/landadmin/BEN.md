# Benin

## Land

**System in service.** [Land transactions in Benin are done in e-Foncier Bénin](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>), and a parcel with a cadastre certificate is sold at the mairie. [La Nation reported the platform in service from 1 January 2025](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/reforme-du-e-foncier-au-benin-plus-de-491-000-parcelles-enregistrees-dans-12-communes>), with mairies, notaries and surveyors. From May 2022 to December 2024 it took 11,418 online requests, transfers and mortgages among them. [The land agency said in November 2026](<https://fraternite.bj/terra-benin-et-ppmec-ii-pour-accelerer-la-securisation-fonciere/>) that it held 643,993 parcels in 12 communes. [Its E-TERRE platform manages the issue of land titles](<https://academie-sciences.bj/actualite/tribune-de-haut-niveau-des-experts-du-numerique-et-du-foncier>), the agency's systems director said in July 2026.

**Maps or valuation.** [MDF, which built e-Foncier Bénin with the land agency, says it maps plot boundaries and links them to legal records](<https://www.mdf.nl/assignments/modernising-land-administration-in-benin>). It counts 491,293 parcels mapped from 2021 to 2025. [By its account 222,463 people hold recorded rights](<https://www.mdf.nl/assignments/modernising-land-administration-in-benin>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [e-Foncier Bénin's parcel maps, linked to legal records](<https://www.mdf.nl/assignments/modernising-land-administration-in-benin>).
