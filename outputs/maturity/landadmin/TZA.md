# Tanzania

## Land

**System in service.** Zanzibar's Land Administration and Registration Information System, LARIS, was [officially launched on 27 July 2026](<https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/>). BPI and CRDB Bank fund it. Aerial mapping began at the launch, as the base for Zanzibar's Digital Land Information System. The firm IGN FI is [building LARIS from June 2026 to May 2028](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>), its coordinator said in October 2026.

**Maps or valuation.** The coordinator says LARIS [will bring together land ownership, surveying, urban planning, registration and land-use records](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>) in one system. The join is part of the build to 2028.

**Linked to tax.** The nearest fact found is the [launch of LARIS in July 2026](<https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/>), with aerial mapping as its first step. Neither the launch report nor the coordinator mentions land or property tax.
