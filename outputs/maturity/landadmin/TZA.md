# Tanzania

## Land

**System in service.** [A 2025 journal study of 350 respondents in Dar es Salaam finds ILMIS has cut transaction times](<https://doi.org/10.51867/ajernet.6.4.23>) where it is fully in place. Zanzibar's LARIS was [officially launched on 27 July 2026](<https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/>). BPI and CRDB Bank fund it, and aerial mapping began at the launch. The firm IGN FI is [building LARIS from June 2026 to May 2028](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>), its coordinator said in October 2026.

**Maps or valuation.** The coordinator says LARIS [will bring together land ownership, surveying, urban planning, registration and land-use records](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/zanzibar/zanzibar-to-link-identity-card-with-land-ownership-records-to-curb-disputes-5650378>) in one system. The join is part of the build to 2028.

**Linked to tax.** [The same study says ILMIS is integrated with tax administration](<https://doi.org/10.51867/ajernet.6.4.23>), among other government functions. Neither Zanzibar source mentions land or property tax.
