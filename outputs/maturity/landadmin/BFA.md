# Burkina Faso

## Land

**System in service.** [The Prime Minister launched SYC@D, Burkina Faso's land and cadastre system, on 21 March 2025](<https://www.minute.bf/gestion-du-foncier-le-burkina-se-dote-du-sycd-pour-digitaliser-lacces-aux-titres-de-propriete/>). The tax director general said users can request and receive cadastral extracts, land allocation certificates and transfer orders online. [In May 2025 the land project's coordinator said a national land information system was being developed and deployed](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article138383=>). The land services of 20 communes were already linked up.

**Maps or valuation.** [The tax director general said at the launch that the cadastral and geographic databases had been integrated into SYC@D](<https://www.minute.bf/gestion-du-foncier-le-burkina-se-dote-du-sycd-pour-digitaliser-lacces-aux-titres-de-propriete/>). The tax directorate runs the system.

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the launch of SYC@D in March 2025](<https://www.minute.bf/gestion-du-foncier-le-burkina-se-dote-du-sycd-pour-digitaliser-lacces-aux-titres-de-propriete/>), presented by the tax director general.
