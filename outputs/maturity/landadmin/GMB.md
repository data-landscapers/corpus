# Gambia

## Land

**System in service.** [The Land Policy Implementation Unit said on 9 November 2026 that the alpha version of the National Land Information System is complete](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>). It is in testing before full rollout. [The Ministry of Lands said in September 2026 that a contracted firm is building a digital land registry](<https://standard.gm/lands-policy-implementation-committee-inaugurated>), due in 2027. [Nine firms bid for the system's tender](<https://gambiana.com/land-reforms-gather-pace-as-government-weighs-new-agency-and-sweeping-changes/>), Minister Hamat Bah said in January 2026. [World Bank-supported consultants were working on it](<https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/>), he said that September.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the alpha version in testing in November 2026](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the same update of November 2026](<https://senigambia.com/ministry-updates-public-on-national-land-policy-implementation/>).
