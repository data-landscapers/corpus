# Morocco

## Land

**System in service.** [Morocco's land agency, ANCFCC, says in its key figures for 2026 that 76% of notarial deed filings with it are made online](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). [In 2013 its e-Foncier service offered online registrations, certificates and titles](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>). [e-Foncier was built on the agency's systems for land titles, LOGICF, and for requisitions, SIGEST](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>).

**Maps or valuation.** [In 2013 e-Foncier was also built on SICAD, which holds the cadastral registers](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>). [CADGIS, which holds the cadastral plans, was another of its systems](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [ANCFCC's online filings in 2026 are notarial deeds](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>).

***Also on file***. [ANCFCC e-services](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>).

***Not found***. Nothing since 2023 says how deed filings meet the cadastral maps or valuation. Nothing says land tax is paid through the land system.
