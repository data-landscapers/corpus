# Morocco

## Land

**System in service.** [Morocco's land agency, ANCFCC, says 76% of notarial deed filings with it are made online](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>). The figure is from its key figures for 2026. Its e-Foncier service [offered online registrations, certificates and titles in 2013](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>). It runs on the agency's systems for land titles and requisitions, LOGICF and SIGEST. Two facts are held on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read. The systems themselves were not examined.

**Maps or valuation.** e-Foncier was [built in 2013 on the agency's cadastral systems](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/archive/stocktaking/Project/Details?projectId=1270127883>). SICAD holds the cadastral registers. CADGIS holds the cadastral plans.

**Linked to tax.** The nearest fact is the [share of notarial deed filings made online in 2026](<https://www.ancfcc.gov.ma/chiffrecles/>): 76%.
