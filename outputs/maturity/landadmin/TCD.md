# Chad

## Land

**System in service.** [A Land at Scale pilot with Oxfam, Kadaster International and FAO equips the cadastre directorate with tablets and GNSS receivers in two zones, N'Djamena 7th arrondissement and Linia](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-modernisation-de-ladministration-fonciere-le-mathu-en-oeuvre/>). [The data will feed the base of the land one-stop shop, GIRAF](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-modernisation-de-ladministration-fonciere-le-mathu-en-oeuvre/>). [The ministry, MATUH, says the pilot is not a land registration](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-modernisation-de-ladministration-fonciere-le-mathu-en-oeuvre/>). [On 21 December 2022 the land minister, Mahamat Assileck Halata, said GIRAF, the online land-document system, was fully restored](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-guichet-unique-des-affaires-foncieres-a-nouveau-operationnel/>). [The land one-stop shop could reopen](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-guichet-unique-des-affaires-foncieres-a-nouveau-operationnel/>).
