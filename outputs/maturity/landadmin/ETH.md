# Ethiopia

## Land

**System in service.** [The eLand platform's site says citizens apply online for ownership transfer, lease registration and mortgage registration](<https://land.et>). It names Bishoftu, Adama and Shagar in Oromia as the cities using it. [Finland's foreign ministry says more than 5 million land transactions had been recorded in the digital rural land registry by June 2026](<https://um.fi/results-of-development-cooperation/-/asset_publisher/o2A1dCQquJyC/content/miljoonille-etiopialaisille-oikeus-omaan-maahan/35732>). [The rural system, NRLAIS, ran in 450 woredas in October 2024](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_5369855>), with some 30 million parcels certified. [A $30 million contract of January 2024 is building an urban system](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_3840048>) in Bahir Dar, Adama, Hawassa and Sodo.

**Maps or valuation.** [The eLand site says its property search has map integration, with automated valuation tools](<https://land.et>). [In September 2026 the urban ministry and INSA agreed to combine legal, physical and cadastral data in one land system](<https://www.ameco.et/english/ethiopia-turns-to-3d-digital-mapping-and-cybersecurity-to-secure-property-rights-and-curb-land-corruption/>).

**Linked to tax.** [The eLand site says it calculates land taxes](<https://land.et>). It takes payment of taxes, fees and charges. It issues receipts and revenue reports.
