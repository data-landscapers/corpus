# Mozambique

## Land

**System in service.** [A 2021 paper said SiGIT, Mozambique's land information system, had recorded 1,375,586 parcels by March 2020](<https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/10/8/818>). [The MCC's 2024 evaluation brief says use of the system has largely not been sustained](<https://www.mcc.gov/resources/doc/evalbrief-052824-moz-land-tenure-regularization/>). In many land offices it does not work or serves only as a back-up database. Those offices still rely on paper records, and getting a certificate remains long and costly.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the MCC evaluation of 2024](<https://www.mcc.gov/resources/doc/evalbrief-052824-moz-land-tenure-regularization/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. [The 2021 paper said SiGIT carried land processes from registration to taxation](<https://www.mdpi.com/2073-445X/10/8/818>).
