# Sierra Leone

## Land

**System in service.** [The World Bank's April 2024 status report says the land administration project would advertise the land information system consultancy that month](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041024065516011/pdf/P1770311f0362d0d01b4bf1fa651ae664b7.pdf>). It would also speed up the digitising of land records. [Its October 2024 report says procurement was still open](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102924175012156/pdf/P177031-6773547a-5b87-4c2c-9ac7-3f49fd446856.pdf>), with no transactions yet registered in a system. [The Chief Minister said in April 2025 that the lands ministry expects to launch a land management information system](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sengeh-highlights-progress-land-reforms-bio/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the launch expected in 2025](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sengeh-highlights-progress-land-reforms-bio/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the procurement of October 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102924175012156/pdf/P177031-6773547a-5b87-4c2c-9ac7-3f49fd446856.pdf>).
