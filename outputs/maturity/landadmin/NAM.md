# Namibia

## Land

**System in service.** [The presentation says NCLAS 1 was rolled out to all Communal Land Boards in 2008 and web-based, server-based NCLAS 2 was introduced in 2014 by the ministry, to register customary and leasehold rights in communal areas](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [The Ministry of Agriculture, Water and Land Reform said that since 2003 it had run manual and automated (Computerized Deeds Registration System) systems in parallel, and on 20 July 2022 the automated system was offline after a malfunction (2003, before the three-year window)](<https://economist.com.na/deeds-registration-system-down-system-to-remain-offline-until-further-notice-official/>).

**Maps or valuation.** [The presentation says NCLAS has two components, communal deeds and communal cadastre (parcel geometries), linked via a Unique Parcel Identifier](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>).
