# Namibia

## Land

**System in service.** [NCLAS 1 was rolled out to all Communal Land Boards in 2008](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [The ministry introduced web-based NCLAS 2 in 2014](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [It registers customary and leasehold rights in communal areas](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [From 2003 the land ministry ran manual and automated deeds systems in parallel](<https://economist.com.na/deeds-registration-system-down-system-to-remain-offline-until-further-notice-official/>). [The automated one is the Computerized Deeds Registration System](<https://economist.com.na/deeds-registration-system-down-system-to-remain-offline-until-further-notice-official/>). [On 20 July 2022 it was offline after a malfunction](<https://economist.com.na/deeds-registration-system-down-system-to-remain-offline-until-further-notice-official/>).

**Maps or valuation.** [The presentation says NCLAS has two components, communal deeds and communal cadastre (parcel geometries), linked via a Unique Parcel Identifier](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system.
