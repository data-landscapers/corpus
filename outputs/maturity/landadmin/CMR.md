## Land

**System in service.** [The housing ministry was selecting firms on 2 June 2026 to design, supply and install a land information system in Yaoundé and Douala](<https://www.armp.cm/details?id_publication=6404&type_publication=AMI>). The World Bank finances it under the Sustainable Cities and Land Management Project. [A further call of 11 September 2026 seeks a firm to supervise the provider](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=6610&type_publication=AMI>). The system is for the Ministry of State Property, Surveys and Land Tenure.
