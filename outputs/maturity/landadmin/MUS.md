# Mauritius

## Land

**System in service.** [The Registrar-General's Department registers deeds of sale, mortgages and discharges, and leases online](<http://registrar.govmu.org/rgd/index.php/faqs/>). They are submitted and tracked through the Mauritius eRegistry System, MeRS, or at the counter. Users sign in with a MauPASS login. The department's guidance on this is from 2026. [The Ministry of Housing and Lands computerised the whole land transaction process](<https://housing.govmu.org/Pages/Dept%20and%20Org/Divisions/Survey/The-Cadastre-Unit.aspx>) in its LAVIMS system. Its Cadastre Unit has run since April 2012.

**Maps or valuation.** [LAVIMS joined a digital cadastral database, a valuation of all residential and commercial sites and deeds registration by parcel](<https://housing.govmu.org/Pages/Dept%20and%20Org/Divisions/Survey/The-Cadastre-Unit.aspx>). Registered deeds were tied to cadastre parcels. The ministry has described it so since 2012.

**Linked to tax.** [Registration duty is paid online through MeRS](<http://registrar.govmu.org/rgd/index.php/faqs/>). The system calculates the amount payable. It works out any penalty automatically. This is from the department's 2026 guidance.
