# Land: land

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The AU Framework and Guidelines on Land Policy asks for a computerised land information system through which rights are registered and tracked across the administration's functions. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Paper | A source from the past three years says land transactions, valuation and planning are run on paper. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |

## Countries

### Ethiopia: 5 Leading

The land system was linked to land or property tax in 2025.

- The eLand platform's own site says it has tax calculation and an integrated payment system for land taxes, fees and charges, with receipts and revenue reports ([source, 2025](https://land.et)).

### Ghana: 5 Leading

The land system was linked to land or property tax in 2024.

- Then-Lands Minister Jinapor said in 2024 that ELIS had enabled stamp duty assessment to be completed online ([source, 2024](https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/)).

### Kenya: 5 Leading

The land system was linked to land or property tax in 2026.

- Kenyans.co.ke lists stamp duty payments and land-rent payments among the services offered through ArdhiSasa ([source, 2026-09-21](https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127225-ardhisasa-goes-down-again-users-warned-disrupted-land-services)).
- The State Department for Lands says stamp duty is assessed and paid digitally through the NSDM within ArdhiSasa via Ardhipay, with KES1.946 billion collected in two months ([source, 2026-02-16](https://lands.go.ke/state-department-lands-rolls-out-nsdm-countrywide-making-land-transactions-faster-and-transparent)).

### Nigeria: 5 Leading

The land system was linked to land or property tax in 2025.

- The KW-IRS chairman says the KW-IRS online payment platform is integrated with the new land system, so land and related payments are made online ([source, 2025-10-20](https://von.gov.ng/kwara-government-launches-digital-land-administration-process/)).

### Rwanda: 5 Leading

The land system was linked to land or property tax in 2026.

- Minister Arakwiye said LAIS has been connected to other national systems including Irembo and the tax payment system ([source, 2026-02](https://ickjournalism.com/impamvu-u-rwanda-rukeneye-abakozi-3000-bo-gutanga-serivisi-zubutaka/)).

### Benin: 3 Established

Land transactions were processed electronically in 2026; a link to survey maps or valuation is not known.

- Fraternité states that with the cadastre registration certificate a parcel can be sold legally at the mairie and that all operations are done directly in e-Foncier Bénin ([source, 2026-03-26](https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/)).
- La Nation reports that the e-Foncier platform has been operational since 1 January 2025 with mairies, notaries and surveyors, and that nine e-Services on service-public.bj included 163 property transfer requests and mortgage registration, with 11,418 online requests from May 2022 to December 2024 (ANDF) ([source, 2025-01-01](https://lanation.bj/actualites/reforme-du-e-foncier-au-benin-plus-de-491-000-parcelles-enregistrees-dans-12-communes)).

### Eswatini: 3 Established

Land transactions were processed electronically in 2026; a link to survey maps or valuation is not known.

- The Ministry of Natural Resources and Energy says the Deeds Registry has computerised its land, debts and miscellaneous contract registers since 1995, updated daily as ownership changes hands, with scanned records on-line on a local network ([source, 2026-08-29](https://gov.sz/index.php/departments-sp-623334762/deeds)).

### Malawi: 3 Established

Land transactions were processed electronically in 2024; a link to survey maps or valuation is not known.

- ITWeb Africa reports Malawi launched its web-based Land Information Management System in February 2024, where registered users submit documents, receive notifications and pay ground rent and application fees, while some transactions remain manual ([source, 2024-02](https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-reviews-digital-land-workflows/rW1xLv5ngAp7Rk6m)).

### Botswana: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Acting Minister Dikoloti told Parliament that under NDP 12 the Ministry of Lands and Agriculture will upgrade the Land Information System and the Integrated Geographic Information System (IGIS), and announced a planned Electronic Deeds Registration Systems Act to give a legal framework for digital registration ([source, 2026-03-02](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90382)).
- Vice President and Finance Minister Gaolathe said in the 2026/27 Budget Speech that Government will begin, through the Geospatial Information Centre, the first phase of a four-year programme to upgrade the Land Information System and strengthen the Integrated Geographic Information System ([source, 2026-02-09](https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89882)).

### DR Congo: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.

- Afrique-sur7 reports that the DRC relaunched its land cadastre digitisation project, e-Foncier, with Dubai firm Oxinus after the 2022 eProseed contract failed, and that a meeting led to e-Foncier being integrated into the country's land management ([source, 2025-02-04](https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/rdc-projet-de-numerisation-du-foncier-relance)).

### Gambia: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Lands Minister Hamat Bah said on 4 September 2026 that the government is developing a Land Information System to digitise records of landholdings, with World Bank-supported consultants working on it and a draft expected by end-September 2026 ([source, 2026-09-04](https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/)).

### Guinea: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.

- Agronews Guinée reports that on 6 October 2025 the government launched mapping of State agricultural land in Boké, Conakry, Kankan and Kindia, to centralise the data in a national database connected to the future 'e-Foncier rural' digital platform, with a dematerialised agricultural land cadastre among the reform's components ([source, 2025-10-06](https://www.agronewsguinee.net/2025/10/reforme-fonciere-lancement-de-la-cartographie-des-terres-agricoles-en-guinee/)).

### Liberia: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.

- The Liberia Land Authority signed a three-year MOU with ALLFORLAND and LTMETRIC to develop the Liberia Land Information System, with pilot activities in Montserrado and Margibi Counties expected within a few months ([source, 2025-10-16](https://lla.gov.lr/index.php/publications/news-article/item/65-liberia-land-authority-and-south-korean-partners-signed-landmark-mou-to-develop-liberia-land-information-system)).

### Senegal: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- PROCASEF states it has built 128 land offices equipped with furniture and fitted with a Système d'Information Foncière Communal (SIFCOM) ([source, 2026-05-13](https://procasef.com/financement-additionnel-du-procasef-des-rencontres-dinformation-avec-60-communes-preselectionnees-pour-consolider-la-securisation-fonciere-au-senegal/)).

### Sierra Leone: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2024; none in service is on record.

- The World Bank status report says Component 2 of the Sierra Leone Land Administration Project will start new procurements to fast-track digitization of land records and development of a digital land information system, with the system development consultancy to be advertised in April 2024 ([source, 2024-04-10](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041024065516011/pdf/P1770311f0362d0d01b4bf1fa651ae664b7.pdf)).

### Tanzania: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- IGN FI reports the official launch on 27 July 2026 of the Land Administration and Registration Information System (LARIS) project in Zanzibar, with aerial mapping starting to provide the foundation for Zanzibar's Digital Land Information System, funded by BPI and CRDB Bank ([source, 2026-07-27](https://ignfi.fr/en/official-launch-ceremony-for-the-land-administration-modernization-project-in-zanzibar-laris/)).

### Uganda: 2 Nascent

A land information system was under way in 2023; none in service is on record.

- The Ministry of Lands says the UgNLIS is being enhanced under the NaLISEP project run from October 2023 to May 2024 by IGN FI with World Bank support ([source, 2023-10](https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/)).
