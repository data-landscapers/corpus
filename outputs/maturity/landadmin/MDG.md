# Madagascar

## Land

**System in service.** [Newsmada reports that the GIZ ProPFR project piloted a land information system linking topographic and land offices](<https://newsmada.com/2026/01/05/propfr-giz-dix-ans-pour-instaurer-une-politique-fonciere-responsable/>). [The land ministry said in April 2025 that the system would be deployed in stages in five pilot regions](<https://newsmada.com/2025/04/17/mdat-deploiement-progressif-du-systeme-dinformation-fonciere/>). The African Development Fund supports it under the second National Land Programme. [The land services director general said in September 2025 that a national system would be operational soon](<https://newsmada.com/2025/09/13/reformes-foncieres-vers-un-systeme-transparent-et-inclusif/>). [In December 2023 the land offices of 291 communes received computers and solar kits](<https://newsmada.com/2023/12/13/redynamisation-des-guichets-fonciers-vers-la-mise-en-place-dun-systeme-dinformation-fonciere/>) to set it up.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [the pilot linking topographic and land offices](<https://newsmada.com/2026/01/05/propfr-giz-dix-ans-pour-instaurer-une-politique-fonciere-responsable/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the same pilot](<https://newsmada.com/2026/01/05/propfr-giz-dix-ans-pour-instaurer-une-politique-fonciere-responsable/>).
