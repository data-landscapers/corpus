# Eswatini

## Land

**System in service.** [The Ministry of Natural Resources and Energy says the Deeds Registry has computerised its land, debts and miscellaneous contract registers since 1995, updated daily as ownership changes hands, with scanned records on-line on a local network](<https://gov.sz/index.php/departments-sp-623334762/deeds>). [Kenya's State Department for Lands reports that Registrar of Deeds Nozipho Vilakati said Eswatini is working to upgrade its manual registration and storage of deeds into a fully electronic system, after two benchmarking visits to Kenya's Ardhisasa](<https://lands.go.ke/eswatini-delegation-returns-kenya-benchmarking-lands-digitization>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gov.sz/index.php/departments-sp-623334762/deeds>).
