# Mauritania

## Land

**System in service.** [In April 2024 Mauritania's Administration Générale des Domaines said it had begun registering all contracts through the Lagoud platform](<https://fr.madar.mr/actualites/mauritanie-procedures-pour-faciliter-lenregistrement-des-contrats-a-travers-une-plateforme-numerique/>). [Final copies of registered contracts are put online](<https://fr.madar.mr/actualites/mauritanie-procedures-pour-faciliter-lenregistrement-des-contrats-a-travers-une-plateforme-numerique/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the Domaines administration registers contracts and puts their final copies online through Lagoud](<https://fr.madar.mr/actualites/mauritanie-procedures-pour-faciliter-lenregistrement-des-contrats-a-travers-une-plateforme-numerique/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [since April 2024 registration fees have been paid by mobile apps](<https://fr.madar.mr/actualites/mauritanie-procedures-pour-faciliter-lenregistrement-des-contrats-a-travers-une-plateforme-numerique/>).
