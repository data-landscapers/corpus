# South Sudan

## Land

**System in service.** [FAO sought consultants on 23 September 2025 to develop a Land Information System for South Sudan](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/279257>). Bids closed on 30 October 2025. [Central Equatoria State launched a Digital Land Registry for Juba on 11 August 2023](<https://www.eyeradio.org/ces-introduces-digital-land-registry-system/>). The governor said it would reach Yei, Terekeka and perhaps Kajo-Keji and Kaya.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [FAO's call for consultants](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/279257>), a request for proposal posted on the UN procurement portal under reference 2025/FRSSD/FRSSD/133933.

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the same call of September 2025](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/279257>).
