# Uganda

## Land

**System in service.** [The Ministry of Lands says the UgNLIS is being enhanced under the NaLISEP project run from October 2023 to May 2024 by IGN FI with World Bank support](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>). [The Ministry of Lands says the UgNLIS, rolled out nationally between February 2015 and February 2020, is a computerized system through 22 Ministry Zonal Offices that reduces the time to check, update and transfer land (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>).

**Maps or valuation.** [The Ministry of Lands says the DeSINLISI national roll-out fully integrated land registration, land administration, surveying and mapping, physical planning and property valuation in the UgNLIS (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>).
