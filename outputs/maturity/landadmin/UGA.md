# Uganda

## Land

**System in service.** [The Ministry of Lands says UgNLIS cuts the time needed to check, update and transfer land](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>). It was rolled out nationally from 2015 to 2020 and runs through 22 zonal offices. [The finance ministry's reform tracker said in August 2026 that it is being extended to every land office](<https://doingbusiness.finance.go.ug/reforms/expand-ugnlis-to-all-land-offices-by-2026>). [The Jinja zonal office had registered 12,748 transactions on it by August 2026](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/jinja-land-office-backlog-exposes-staffing-infrastructure-gaps-despite-digital-gains-5577014>), with thousands pending.

**Maps or valuation.** [The ministry says UgNLIS joins registration, surveying and mapping, physical planning and valuation](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>). [Jinja's registrar says digital mapping links titles to surveys](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/jinja-land-office-backlog-exposes-staffing-infrastructure-gaps-despite-digital-gains-5577014>), though maps still need checking on the ground.

**Linked to tax.** [IGAD said in February 2024 that Uganda's land system integrates land records with the Uganda Revenue Authority](<https://igad.int/igad-land-governance-twinning-exercise-between-uganda-and-ethiopia/>). Ethiopian land experts studied the link on a visit to Kampala.
