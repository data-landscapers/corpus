# Uganda

## Land

**System in service.** [The Ministry of Lands says UgNLIS cuts the time needed to check, update and transfer land](<https://mlhud.go.ug/ugnlis/>). It was rolled out nationally from 2015 to 2020. It runs through 22 zonal offices. IGN FI enhanced it from October 2023 to May 2024, with World Bank support. Three facts are held on the subject.
