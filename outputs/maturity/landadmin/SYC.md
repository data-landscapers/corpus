# Seychelles

## Land

**System in service.** [On 22 January 2025 the Lands Department invited tenders for an online Land Valuation System](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/22.01.2025_Advert_for_LVS_003.pdf>). [The Department of Information Communications Technology joined in the tender](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/22.01.2025_Advert_for_LVS_003.pdf>). [It is to interface with SeyID and the National Population Database](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/22.01.2025_Advert_for_LVS_003.pdf>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [the January 2025 tender is for land valuation and asks for an interface with the Seychelles Planning Authority](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/22.01.2025_Advert_for_LVS_003.pdf>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the tendered system is to interface with IFMIS and the Registration Division](<https://www.ntb.sc/media/tender-files/22.01.2025_Advert_for_LVS_003.pdf>).
