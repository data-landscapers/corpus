# Ghana

## Land

**System in service.** [A digital Client Service Access Unit inaugurated in Ho on 3 June 2026 enabled deployment of the Enterprise Land Information System in the Volta Region, where clients can submit land applications and track progress](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-client-service-access-unit-inaugurated-at-volta-regional-office-of-lands-commission.html>). [Lands Minister Emmanuel Armah-Kofi Buah said in August 2025 that the Commission's ongoing ELIS digitization would eliminate manual inefficiencies and reduce corruption](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>). [Then-Lands Minister Jinapor said in 2024 that ELIS had enabled selected services, including consolidated searches and plan approvals, to be completed online, with full migration from manual operations still in progress](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).
