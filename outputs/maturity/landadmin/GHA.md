# Ghana

## Land

**System in service.** [The Lands Commission's Executive Secretary said in October 2026 that ELIS runs in Greater Accra and nine other regions](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/lands-commissions-digital-reform-gamechanger-for-land-administration-in-ghana/>). Official searches now take seven working days, not a month. [In July 2026 the Lands Minister said the commission's major services were processed digitally in nine regional offices](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/lands-commission-targets-one-month-land-registration.html>). [The commission describes ELIS as one workflow platform, with an online portal for searches, registration, transfers and mortgages](<https://www.eis.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/THE-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-OF-GHANA_S-LANDS-COMMISSION-BENJAMIN-QUAYE.pdf>). It names incomplete deployment and remaining manual work as gaps. [A Volta regional unit opened in June 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-client-service-access-unit-inaugurated-at-volta-regional-office-of-lands-commission.html>), where clients submit applications and track them.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. [In September 2025 President Mahama told the commission to integrate its Registries, Valuation and Survey departments](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/09/mahama-directs-lands-commission-to-undertake-audit-of-state-lands-allocated-from-2017-2024/>), building on ELIS.

**Linked to tax.** [The commission's portal listed stamp duty assessment among its online services in November 2025](<https://www.eis.africa/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/THE-DIGITAL-TRANSFORMATION-OF-GHANA_S-LANDS-COMMISSION-BENJAMIN-QUAYE.pdf>). [The former Lands Minister said in 2024 that stamp duty could be assessed online](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

***Also on file***. [Digital Land Acquisition and Management Platform (DILAP)](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-has-spent-years-trying-to-digitise-its-land-registry-system-progress-is-still-underwhelming/>).

***Not found***. Nothing says ELIS joins land records with survey maps or valuation.
