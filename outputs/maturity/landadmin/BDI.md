# Burundi

## Land

**System in service.** [Mediabox announced on 17 June 2025 the commissioning of an EDRMS at the land titles directorate DTFCN, interconnected with the Building Permit System (first titles) and the Property Management System (title updates: transfers, corrections, mortgages), forming an integrated digital ecosystem](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
