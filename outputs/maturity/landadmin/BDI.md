# Burundi

## Land

**System in service.** [In June 2025 Mediabox said it had commissioned an electronic records system, an EDRMS, at Burundi's land titles directorate, the DTFCN](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>). [The EDRMS is interconnected with the Building Permit System, which handles first titles](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [in June 2025 the DTFCN's records system was joined to the Property Management System](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the Property Management System handles title updates, including transfers, corrections and mortgages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-mediabox-deploie-un-systeme-interconnecte-pour-les-services-fonciers>).
