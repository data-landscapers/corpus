# Algeria

## Land

**System in service.** [Algeria's finance minister told the National People's Assembly on 12 December 2024 that digitising the land file, archives and documents in the land conservation offices has given automated, secured management of all land transactions](<https://elwatan.dz/conservations-foncieres-instructions-pour-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique/>). [The Direction generale du Domaine national announced the generalisation of the Amlak information system to all cadastre, land conservation and State Domains directorates, ahead of issuing the digital land booklet (Eco Times, 25 February 2026)](<https://ecotimesdz.com/numerisation-prestations-cadastrales-livret-foncier-electronique/>).
