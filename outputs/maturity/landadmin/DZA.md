# Algeria

## Land

**System in service.** [Algeria's finance minister told the National People's Assembly on 12 December 2024](<https://elwatan.dz/conservations-foncieres-instructions-pour-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique/>) that the land conservation offices manage all land transactions digitally. [On 11 January 2026 the national domain directorate generalised the Amlak information system](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-vers-le-livret-foncier-electronique-avec-amlak/>). It now runs in every cadastre, land conservation and state property office. [Eco Times reported that the move prepares the digital land booklet](<https://ecotimesdz.com/numerisation-prestations-cadastrales-livret-foncier-electronique/>). [The directorate's head says cadastre and land conservation services are being fully digitised](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2025/03/11/un-livret-foncier-electronique-sous-forme-de-carte-electronique-en-2025/>), with new services due on the Fada El Djazair portal.

**Maps or valuation.** [Algérie Zoom reports that Amlak is one national system joining the cadastre, land conservation and state property](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-vers-le-livret-foncier-electronique-avec-amlak/>). It rests on a central national database.

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [Amlak's generalisation in January 2026](<https://algeriezoom.com/algerie-vers-le-livret-foncier-electronique-avec-amlak/>).
