# Zambia

## Land

**System in service.** [The Law Association of Zambia's president described ZILAS in March 2025 as the Ministry of Lands system](<https://buyingpropertyinzambia.com/2025/03/12/the-zambia-integrated-land-administration-system-progress-or-setback/>), where land documents are submitted, tracked and processed online. It produces digital title deeds. [Zambian Business Times reported in October 2026 that 982 property records went missing](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/982-properties-vanish-after-zilmis-to-zilas-transition/>) when the old ZILMIS system was moved to ZILAS. Users face intermittent outages.

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest fact: [ZILAS as the Law Association described it](<https://buyingpropertyinzambia.com/2025/03/12/the-zambia-integrated-land-administration-system-progress-or-setback/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the move to ZILAS reported in October 2026](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/982-properties-vanish-after-zilmis-to-zilas-transition/>).
