# Zimbabwe

## Land

**System in service.** [A Government Gazette notice launched the Digital Land Administration Platform](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>), for online processing of documents and deeds. [The Lands Permanent Secretary said in July 2026 that a land information management system is being rolled out](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-land-system-to-boost-transparency/>), to take effect within six months. [He told Parliament in September 2026 that a National Cadastre System is being built](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/land-reform-enters-new-era-of-consolidation-productivity/>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. [The planned National Cadastre System is to bring land records, mapping and survey data onto one platform](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/land-reform-enters-new-era-of-consolidation-productivity/>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest fact: [the platform's launch in May 2025](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>).

***Also on file***. [Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/314282>); [Impilo](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-takes-diagnostics-closer-to-rural-communities/>).

***Not found***. Nothing says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation.
