# Zimbabwe

## Land

**System in service.** [Justice Minister Ziyambi Ziyambi announced in a Government Gazette notice the launch of the Digital Land Administration Platform (DLAP), described as a computerized land administration system for online processing of documents and deeds](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
