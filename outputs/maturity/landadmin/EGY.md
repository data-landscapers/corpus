# Egypt

## Land

**System in service.** [In July 2025 Egypt's prime minister opened the Real Estate Registry and Notarization Complex in New Alamein, Ahram Online reported](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>). [Sixteen registry offices in the new cities register land, building and unit transactions with electronic tools](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>).

**Maps or valuation.** No source found since October 2023 says the land system joins records to survey maps or valuation. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 an integration system joined the Justice and Housing ministries in those 16 offices](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>).

**Linked to tax.** No source found since October 2023 says land or property tax is assessed or paid through the land system. The nearest held fact: [the New Alamein complex, opened in July 2025, combines real estate registration and notarisation](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>).
