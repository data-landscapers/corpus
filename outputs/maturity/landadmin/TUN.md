## Land

**System in service.** [The Minister of State Domains says the national land office has installed its IT system and rolled it out to all its regional offices](<http://www.domainetat.tn/?p=14628>). [A Tunis workshop in November 2023 discussed setting up the Tunisian Land Information System](<https://managers.tn/2023/11/14/la-tunisie-va-enfin-se-doter-dun-systeme-foncier-digitalise/>). Its parts include a digital land database and scanned land archives.
