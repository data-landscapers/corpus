# Malawi

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Malawi told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025 that e-filing is in use for tax and customs declarations](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [The same answer linked the Malawi Revenue Authority's Msonkho Online portal, a system the authority owns](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [In October 2026 the authority's representative said it is developing ITAS II, its second Integrated Tax Administration System, in-house](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>). The authority is still seeking funding for it.

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest fact: [in 2025 the government told the World Bank that e-filing was in use through the MRA's Msonkho Online portal](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in 2025 the government told the World Bank that e-filing was in use for tax declarations](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>).

***Also on file***. [Electronic Invoicing System](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/mra-credits-ataf-support-for-tax-administration-reforms-data-improvements/article_46cb9644-8810-4649-be0a-d358dbf91606.html>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system.
