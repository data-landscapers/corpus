# South Sudan

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Commissioner of Domestic Taxes said the SSRA was rolling out an e-tax system to automate tax filings and payments](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/03/10/south-sudan-tax-digital-first-strategy-to-combat-revenue-leakages/>). [Seven state head offices had been digitised, with others to follow, One Citizen Daily reported](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/03/10/south-sudan-tax-digital-first-strategy-to-combat-revenue-leakages/>). [One Citizen Daily also reported the SSRA's review of its E-Tax reform, noting limited infrastructure and low taxpayer awareness](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/09/26/ssra-pushes-digital-reforms-to-boost-transparency-efficiency/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The SSRA Commissioner General directed that all government revenues, including taxes, be paid exclusively through the National e-Tax System, with electronic receipts as proof of payment, per Sudans Post](<https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-bans-manual-revenue-collection-orders-mandatory-e-tax-system-usage/>). [Commissioner General Rizig Dominic Samuel says that under the electronic system taxpayers pay directly through authorised commercial banks, with transactions recorded in the system; e-Tax was demonstrated to the Zambian delegation](<https://radioyei.org/news/business/ssra-hosts-zambian-delegation-on-digital-tax-systems/>).
