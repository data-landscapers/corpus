# Eritrea

## Tax

**Online filing.** [A firm was hired on 9 October 2024 to supply, install and configure the Eritrea Integrated Tax Administration System, EriTAS](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_strengthening_tax_administration_project_estap_-_p-er-k00-002_-_ipr_may_2025.pdf>). [By May 2025 it had delivered the software licences](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_strengthening_tax_administration_project_estap_-_p-er-k00-002_-_ipr_may_2025.pdf>), the African Development Bank reported. The Bank's grant was [to replace the home-grown ERITAS with an e-services portal for taxpayers](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_strengthening_tax_administration_project_estap_-_project_appraisal_report.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest fact: [in May 2025 EriTAS was still being installed](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_strengthening_tax_administration_project_estap_-_p-er-k00-002_-_ipr_may_2025.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in May 2025 EriTAS was still being installed](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/eritrea_-_strengthening_tax_administration_project_estap_-_p-er-k00-002_-_ipr_may_2025.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says tax returns can be filed online or taxes paid electronically.
