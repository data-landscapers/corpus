# Guinea

## Tax

**Online filing.** [We Are Tech reported in May 2026 that the government has launched eTax for filing tax returns online](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-guinee-lance-neuf-chantiers-numeriques-pour-moderniser-l-administration>). [The Budget Minister said in September 2020 that pilot firms had begun filing on the eTax portal, launched that month](<https://guineenews.org/2020/09/14/innovation-dans-la-gouvernance-financiere-le-premier-ministre-kassory-lance-le-portail-e-tax/?amp=1>). The same report names it among the platforms of the national e-government drive, beside the public services portal.

**Electronic payment.** [eTax also takes tax payments online](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-guinee-lance-neuf-chantiers-numeriques-pour-moderniser-l-administration>), We Are Tech reported in May 2026. [In September 2020 the Budget Minister said pilot firms had declared and paid GNF 45 billion through it](<https://guineenews.org/2020/09/14/innovation-dans-la-gouvernance-financiere-le-premier-ministre-kassory-lance-le-portail-e-tax/?amp=1>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. [The 2020 figures cover pilot firms only](<https://guineenews.org/2020/09/14/innovation-dans-la-gouvernance-financiere-le-premier-ministre-kassory-lance-le-portail-e-tax/?amp=1>). [Nor does the May 2026 report give a count of users](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-guinee-lance-neuf-chantiers-numeriques-pour-moderniser-l-administration>).
