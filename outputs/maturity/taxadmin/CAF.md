# Central African Republic

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Ministry of Finance and Budget's e-Finances note lists accelerating implementation of the e-Tax software package as a priority action](<https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-03/Plan%20e-Finances%20publiques-Note%20de%20cadrage-Pr%C3%A9sentation%2026%20mars%202025%20(1).pdf>). [Agence Ecofin reports the DGID launched the e-Tax platform on 24 March 2025, providing online tax declaration](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [ITWeb Africa quotes the director general of taxes saying the e-tax registration and e-filing modules were fully operational; the platform allows electronic tax filing](<https://itweb.africa/article/central-african-republic-advances-with-tax-digitalisation/rW1xL75n43RMRk6m>). [We Are Tech Africa reports the e-Tax platform has 301 large and 325 medium firms registered and offers online filing (télédéclaration) of taxes](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-centrafrique-adopte-le-tax-pour-mieux-percevoir-les-impots>). [Ecomatin reports the DGID began in April 2025 its official switch to general use of e-Tax for tax returns](<https://ecomatin.net/le-fmi-annonce-un-nouveau-decaissement-de-334-milliards-fcfa-au-profit-de-la-centrafrique>).

**Electronic payment.** [Agence Ecofin reports the DGID launched the e-Tax platform on 24 March 2025 for online payment of taxes](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [ITWeb Africa states the e-tax platform allows electronic tax payments, backed by the EU under a 24-month service contract](<https://itweb.africa/article/central-african-republic-advances-with-tax-digitalisation/rW1xL75n43RMRk6m>). [We Are Tech Africa reports the e-Tax platform introduces online payment of taxes (télépaiement), with 301 large and 325 medium firms registered](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-centrafrique-adopte-le-tax-pour-mieux-percevoir-les-impots>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>).
