# Central African Republic

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The finance ministry's e-Finances note of March 2025 made faster rollout of the e-Tax software a priority](<https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2025-03/Plan%20e-Finances%20publiques-Note%20de%20cadrage-Pr%C3%A9sentation%2026%20mars%202025%20(1).pdf>). [In January 2025 the director general of taxes said e-Tax's registration and e-filing modules were fully operational](<https://itweb.africa/article/central-african-republic-advances-with-tax-digitalisation/rW1xL75n43RMRk6m>). [The tax directorate (DGID) launched e-Tax for online returns on 24 March 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [From 22 March 2025 returns were to be filed on it, the finance minister said](<https://ecomatin.net/centrafrique-malgre-la-mauvaise-connectivite-du-pays-les-autorites-optent-pour-le-paiement-des-impots-en-ligne>).

**Electronic payment.** [E-Tax has taken online payment of taxes since its launch on 24 March 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [Its EU-funded service contract runs for 24 months](<https://itweb.africa/article/central-african-republic-advances-with-tax-digitalisation/rW1xL75n43RMRk6m>). [In August 2025 the finance minister told late taxpayers to file their July returns and pay the taxes due through e-Tax by 15 August](<https://ecomatin.net/fiscalite-bangui-menace-de-sanctionner-les-grandes-entreprises-qui-boudent-la-plateforme-e-tax>).

**Mostly online.** [In a communiqué of 7 August 2025 the finance minister required large taxpayers to file on e-Tax](<https://ecomatin.net/fiscalite-bangui-menace-de-sanctionner-les-grandes-entreprises-qui-boudent-la-plateforme-e-tax>). [Those who did not faced systematic audits from early September 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/fiscalite-bangui-menace-de-sanctionner-les-grandes-entreprises-qui-boudent-la-plateforme-e-tax>). [He said more than half of the firms concerned still did not use the platform](<https://ecomatin.net/fiscalite-bangui-menace-de-sanctionner-les-grandes-entreprises-qui-boudent-la-plateforme-e-tax>).

***Also on file***. [SYSTEMIF](<http://news.abangui.com/h/88755.html>); [eTVA](<https://africa24tv.com/reportage-centrafrique-la-digitalisation-pour-stimuler-les-finances-publiques>).
