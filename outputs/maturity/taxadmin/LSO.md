# Lesotho

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Revenue Services Lesotho's guide says taxpayers can complete and submit CIT, IIT, RIIT, T&E, VAT and PAYE returns online through its e-Taxation platform (e-Filing)](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Electronic payment.** [Revenue Services Lesotho's guide says tax payments can be made through its e-Payments platform using mobile money (EcoCash and M-Pesa) and by bank EFT, with Standard Lesotho Bank payments via the Unayo app reflected immediately in the RSL system](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source held since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>).
