# Lesotho

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Revenue Services Lesotho says taxpayers register on its eTax platform, log in with a username and password and file their returns there](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>). [Revenue Services Lesotho's guide says taxpayers can complete and submit CIT, IIT, RIIT, T&E, VAT and PAYE returns online through its e-Taxation platform (e-Filing)](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>). [LAA's Director General says the Automatic Compliance Project is already in use, with RSL retrieving PAYE files as early as August 2024, and that taxpayers can file returns from home on pre-populated PAYE forms](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/rsl-doubles-down-on-enhancing-tax-compliance/>).

**Electronic payment.** [Revenue Services Lesotho says taxes can be paid through its e-Payments platform by mobile money and by bank electronic transfer](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>). [Revenue Services Lesotho's guide says tax payments can be made through its e-Payments platform using mobile money (EcoCash and M-Pesa) and by bank EFT, with Standard Lesotho Bank payments via the Unayo app reflected immediately in the RSL system](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Guide%20on%20E-Taxation%20Platform%20%28E-Filing%29.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>).
