# Eswatini

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Finance Minister Neal Rijkenberg said the Eswatini Revenue Service (ERS) introduced its TaxEase platform in March 2024](<https://swazidailynews.com/2024/09/19/ers-collects-e-e6-02-billion-13-increase-minister-neal-launches-2024-tax-filing-season-and-introduces-taxease/>). [It replaced the legacy eTax system](<https://swazidailynews.com/2024/09/19/ers-collects-e-e6-02-billion-13-increase-minister-neal-launches-2024-tax-filing-season-and-introduces-taxease/>). [From 4 March 2024 the portal offered online filing for several types of tax](<https://eswatinipositivenews.com/ers-launches-tax-portal/>). [It auto-populates returns and takes monthly payroll uploads, Eswatini Positive News reported](<https://eswatinipositivenews.com/ers-launches-tax-portal/>).

**Electronic payment.** [In March 2024 Eswatini Positive News reported that TaxEase provides integrated payments and simplified payment referencing](<https://eswatinipositivenews.com/ers-launches-tax-portal/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [by 12 September 2024 taxpayers had filed 54,842 returns on TaxEase, the Finance Minister said](<https://swazidailynews.com/2024/09/19/ers-collects-e-e6-02-billion-13-increase-minister-neal-launches-2024-tax-filing-season-and-introduces-taxease/>). [They comprised 38,900 PAYE, 11,466 VAT, 368 corporate income tax and 3,108 personal income tax returns](<https://swazidailynews.com/2024/09/19/ers-collects-e-e6-02-billion-13-increase-minister-neal-launches-2024-tax-filing-season-and-introduces-taxease/>).
