# Madagascar

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In September 2025 finance minister Rindra Rabarinirinarison said the new Integrated Tax Administration System (SAFI) takes tax returns and payments online at any hour](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-rindra-rabarinirinarison-minister-of-economy-and-finance-of-madagascar>). [The tax administration's steering committee reported SAFI, which covers online declaration and several means of payment, tested and ready for production from the end of September 2025](<https://new.moov.mg/actualite/106644-modernisation-fiscale-la-plateforme-safi-prete-pour-la-mise-en-production>), and [the tax administration confirmed it went into production after the committee met on 18 September 2025, with existing tax services moving onto it](<https://cabinet-fenitra.mg/actualites/safi-cap-sur-le-deploiement-effectif-a-madagascar/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The minister said the e-Hetra system for declaring and paying taxes online has been extended nationwide](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-rindra-rabarinirinarison-minister-of-economy-and-finance-of-madagascar>).

**Mostly online.** No source held since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-rindra-rabarinirinarison-minister-of-economy-and-finance-of-madagascar>).
