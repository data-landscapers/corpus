# Burkina Faso

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In November 2026 the tax directorate's eSINTAX JAVA portal let taxpayers make their declarations remotely](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [The same portal also handles taxpayers' requests for tax certificates, leFaso.net reported that month](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [Its eSINTAX platform has been online since April 2018 for large and mid-size enterprises](<https://burkina24.com/2019/12/30/communique-du-directeur-general-des-impots-4/>).

**Electronic payment.** [In November 2026 eSINTAX JAVA let taxpayers pay their taxes remotely, from the same portal they file on](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150547>). [Since 2018 large and mid-size enterprises have been able to pay taxes online through eSINTAX](<https://burkina24.com/2019/12/30/communique-du-directeur-general-des-impots-4/>). [Online payment became compulsory for large enterprises from 1 January 2020](<https://burkina24.com/2019/12/30/communique-du-directeur-general-des-impots-4/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. [Large enterprises have had to file through the portal since 1 January 2019](<https://burkina24.com/2019/12/30/communique-du-directeur-general-des-impots-4/>), the tax directorate said that year.
