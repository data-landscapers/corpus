# Togo

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The OTR launched the e-TPU platform on 17 September 2026 for online declaration and assessment of the Taxe Professionnelle Unique by micro and small enterprises, per Togo-Presse](<https://togopresse.tg/faciliter-lacces-aux-services-administratifs-et-le-paiement-en-ligne-la-plateforme-e-tpu-de-lotr-lancee/>). [The OTR statistics page of 20 February 2026 reports VAT returns filed electronically (teledeclaration) at 100% for the DGE and DME directorates and 26% for DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR in 2025](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).

**Electronic payment.** [The OTR's e-TPU platform, launched 17 September 2026, lets taxpayers pay the Taxe Professionnelle Unique online from a phone, tablet or computer, per the OTR commissioner general quoted by Togo-Presse](<https://togopresse.tg/faciliter-lacces-aux-services-administratifs-et-le-paiement-en-ligne-la-plateforme-e-tpu-de-lotr-lancee/>). [The OTR statistics page of 20 February 2026 reports VAT paid electronically (telepaiement) at 100% for the DGE and DME directorates and 24% for DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR in 2025](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).

**Mostly online.** [The OTR statistics page of 20 February 2026 puts the share of VAT returns filed online at 100% in 2025 for the DGE and DME directorates, and 26% for the DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR directorates](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).
