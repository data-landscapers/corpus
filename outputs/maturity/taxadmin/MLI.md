# Mali

## Tax

**Online filing.** [A DGI communiqué of 22 April 2026 says taxpayers can file IS and IBIC returns online through the E-Impôt platform, entering the balance sheet and profit-and-loss account and attaching financial statements, or upload statements through E-Liasse](<https://bamada.net/mali-la-direction-generale-des-impots-ouvre-trois-options-pour-la-declaration-fiscale-et-le-depot-des-etats-financiers>). [The DGI director-general says telefiling through the E-Impôt module, launched January 2019, now covers all taxpayers and all taxes except a few spontaneous ones such as registration duties and vignettes](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>). [Mali's finance ministry project unit (PASG, AfDB-funded) sought a consultancy firm to extend DGI tele-services to all taxes, add a USSD app and APIs, and connect all tax centres to the SIGTAS network](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/project-related-procurement/ami_assistance_technique_telepaiement_dgi.aib_-rev_vf_ano.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** [The DGI director-general says telepayment, launched December 2021, is operational for all taxpayers of the computerised centres, who can pay taxes by bank transfer, with no mobile money or card payment yet](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
