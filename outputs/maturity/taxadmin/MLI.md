# Mali

## Tax

**Online filing.** Mali's tax directorate DGI runs online filing through its E-Impôt platform, launched in January 2019. In November 2025 its director-general said [telefiling covers all taxpayers and all taxes](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>), except a few such as registration duties and vignettes. In March 2025 the finance minister [said tele-declaration was in place](<https://www.ortm.ml/digitalisation-de-ladministration-deux-nouvelles-plateformes-de-modernisation-lancees>), ORTM reported. An April 2026 notice says companies [can file corporate and business income tax returns online](<https://bamada.net/mali-la-direction-generale-des-impots-ouvre-trois-options-pour-la-declaration-fiscale-et-le-depot-des-etats-financiers>) with their accounts attached. In 2024 a finance ministry project [sought consultants to extend the services](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/project-related-procurement/ami_assistance_technique_telepaiement_dgi.aib_-rev_vf_ano.pdf>) to every tax and tax centre.

**Electronic payment.** Telepayment started in December 2021. In November 2025 the director-general said [taxpayers of the computerised centres can pay by bank transfer](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>). Mobile money and card payment were not yet offered. The finance minister [said in March 2025 that tele-payment was in place](<https://www.ortm.ml/digitalisation-de-ladministration-deux-nouvelles-plateformes-de-modernisation-lancees>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact is the [director-general's November 2025 account](<https://www.maliweb.net/interview/hamadou-fall-dianka-dg-des-impots-le-montant-des-recettes-electroniques-a-atteint-300-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2024-3111560.html>). It says who may file online, not how many do.
