# Zimbabwe

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In 2025 the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) said taxpayers can submit tax returns online on its E-Taxes platform](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2239:e-taxes-online-platform-2>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 ZIMRA said it had launched E-Taxes alongside its E-Services online filing platform](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2239:e-taxes-online-platform-2>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 ZIMRA said online returns on E-Taxes cover selected tax heads](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2239:e-taxes-online-platform-2>).
