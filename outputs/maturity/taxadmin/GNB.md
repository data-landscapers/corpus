# Guinea-Bissau

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In July 2024 O Democrata GB reported that Kontaktu, the platform of the tax directorate (DGCI), lets taxpayers declare their taxes online](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=49407>). [The IMF says Kontaktu, an electronic filing and payment platform, was launched in 2021](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>).

**Electronic payment.** [Taxes can be paid through Kontaktu by bank transfer, Orange Money and Mobile Money, the paper said](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=49407>). [The IMF describes Kontaktu as a platform for paying taxes as well as filing them](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>). [The IMF counted six electronic payment options in April 2024, up from none in March 2022, offered by banks and telecoms firms](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>).

**Mostly online.** [An IMF report published in April 2024 says all large and medium taxpayers have had to file electronically since March 2022](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>). [That was the year after Kontaktu launched](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>).
