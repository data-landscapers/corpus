# Morocco

## Tax

**Online filing.** The DGI says its [Simpl modules for VAT, corporate tax and income tax](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/e-services-la-dgi-etoffe-son-systeme-simpl>) let taxpayers file returns online. In 2016 it [counted 15,200 taxpayers enrolled](<https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/35106>) in the SIMPL teleservices. In May 2026 it [opened a platform called Taxation on Digital Services](<https://fr.hespress.com/489385-publicite-meta-applique-la-tva-au-maroc-des-octobre.html>). Foreign providers of remote services use it to register, declare their Moroccan turnover and pay VAT.

**Electronic payment.** The DGI says [Simpl lets taxpayers pay taxes online](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/e-services-la-dgi-etoffe-son-systeme-simpl>), by direct debit and other channels. [Corporate tax, income tax and VAT](<https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/35106>) were already paid through the SIMPL teleservices in 2016.

**Mostly online.** [Decree 2.25.862 of 27 November 2025](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>) requires non-resident suppliers of digital services to register on the DGI's platform. They must file VAT returns electronically each quarter and pay electronically. The decree took effect six months after publication. In 2017 the DGI [made electronic filing and payment mandatory for all firms](<https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/35106>), except those on the flat-rate regime.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No share of returns filed online has been found. The mandate found in the last three years covers non-resident suppliers of digital services only.
