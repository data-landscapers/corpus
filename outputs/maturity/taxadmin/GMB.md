# Gambia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The GRA Commissioner General said the new Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) will let taxpayers register, file returns, pay and access services online, with stakeholder workshops held ahead of rollout](<https://www.voicegambia.com/2026/06/17/gra-says-new-itas-will-transform-tax-administration-in-the-gambia/>). [A joint Ministry of Finance and GRA press release says the World Bank-supported ITAS, to provide online services, is being implemented, with an Arabsoft and Tunisie Micro-Informatique consortium selected at USD 7,270,192.20 after the first awardee went bankrupt](<https://www.gra.gm/news/4f0f3845-b049-11f0-b086-029254d29bb1>). [That first contract, signed with Sogema Technologies in May 2024, was for a web-based ITAS with online filing to replace GAMTAXNET](<https://standard.gm/gra-signs-agreement-to-improve-efficiency-transparency/>).
