# Senegal

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In September 2026 the DGID said SENTAX is its online filing platform, replacing ETAX for large-enterprise taxpayers](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>). [The DGID says SENTAX now replaces ETAX for online filing of returns by Large Enterprises Directorate taxpayers](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/communique-sentax-1790185473.pdf>). [From September 2023 regulated-professions centre users had to file all returns on Etax, the DGID said](<https://www.leral.net/Souscription-obligatoire-a-la-plateforme-Etax-Les-precisions-de-la-Direction-generale-des-impots-et-domaines_a353801.html>).

**Electronic payment.** [In September 2026 the DGID said SENTAX handles online payment of taxes and levies](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>). [The DGID says SENTAX lets Large Enterprises Directorate taxpayers pay taxes online (telepaiement)](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/communique-sentax-1790185473.pdf>). [From September 2023 regulated-professions centre users had to pay on Etax, the DGID said](<https://www.leral.net/Souscription-obligatoire-a-la-plateforme-Etax-Les-precisions-de-la-Direction-generale-des-impots-et-domaines_a353801.html>).

**Mostly online.** [The DGID says use of SENTAX is mandatory and paper returns are no longer accepted for Large Enterprise Directorate taxpayers from 1 September 2026, and for the Dakar medium-enterprise centres and regulated professions centre from 1 October 2026](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>).
