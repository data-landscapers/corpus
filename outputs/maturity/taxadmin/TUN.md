# Tunisia

## Tax

**Online filing.** In 2026 the tax administration [opened filing of the employer's declaration on its TEJ platform](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/10/27/575585/tunisie-la-digitalisation-fiscale-permet-deconomiser-6-000-journees-de-travail/>), according to the report on the 2027 State budget bill. More than 85,000 taxpayers filed it there by the end of July 2026. They had filed it before on magnetic media at tax control offices. The report says the change saved the administration about 6,000 working days.

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid through the tax system online. The nearest fact found is older. The tax directorate's [August 2023 guide](<https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/guide-adhesion-en-ligne-min.pdf>) describes its tele-payment system. Payment there is by bank or postal account.

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or gives the share of returns filed online. The nearest fact found is [the count of more than 85,000 employers filing on TEJ by July 2026](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/10/27/575585/tunisie-la-digitalisation-fiscale-permet-deconomiser-6-000-journees-de-travail/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically. No share of returns filed online has been found.
