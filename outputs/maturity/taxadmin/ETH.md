# Ethiopia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The ICTD paper uses tax administrative records to examine the adoption rate trend of the e-filing system among large and medium taxpayers in Ethiopia, including timeliness of VAT and corporate income tax return filing](<https://www.ictd.ac/publication/e-tax-system-adoption-tax-compliance-ethiopia>). [Ministry of Revenue re-advertised on 8 October 2026 for a Technical Lead/Chief Architect to govern design of the Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) and oversee vendor activities, financed by the World Bank](<https://www.getchereta.com/tender/85995>). [The Executive Budget Proposal for 2024/25 lists a Ministry of Revenue capital project, Integrated Tax Adminstration System-ITAS, at ETB 26,671,900](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/4f/f4/4ff40d12-0261-4680-9e1a-06dc7b964cd8/executive_budget_proposal_efy_2017_-202425__budget_year_goe_federal_budget_proclamation-proposal.xlsx>). [In January 2024 the Ministry of Revenues announced it would extend its e-tax system across tax bureaus, including some document filing, Addis Fortune reports](<https://addisfortune.news/ministry-embarks-on-e-tax-services>).
