# Sierra Leone

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The National Revenue Authority (NRA) runs an Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS)](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>). [In August 2025 it said ITAS has been fully operational since 2021](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>). [In December 2025 Commissioner General Jeneba Bangura said taxpayers can file returns electronically through ITAS](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-digital-tax-mobile-payments-modernise/>). [A Fair Tax Monitor study of May 2022 said budget speeches had promised ITAS from 2015](<https://maketaxfair.net/assets/2024/01/FTM-STUDY-FOR-SIERRA-LEONE-FINAL-.pdf>). [Work on it began only in 2019, the study said](<https://maketaxfair.net/assets/2024/01/FTM-STUDY-FOR-SIERRA-LEONE-FINAL-.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** [In December 2025 Bangura said taxpayers can pay through ITAS, by wallet or mobile money](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-digital-tax-mobile-payments-modernise/>). [In August 2025 the NRA said all excise taxpayers file and pay electronically](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>).

**Mostly online.** [In August 2025 the Commissioner General said everyone who files income tax and GST returns does so on ITAS](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>). [Manual filing has ended, the Commissioner General added](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>).
