# Seychelles

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In March 2024 the Seychelles Revenue Commission (SRC) said taxpayers lodge monthly returns on dedicated paper forms, one for each tax](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [It said its e-service platform had closed to taxpayers lodging returns from January 2024 onwards](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [in March 2024 the SRC said taxpayers lodge their returns with a payment slip](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [in March 2024 the SRC said its new online tax portal was not yet live](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [It had started migration to the new platform](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>).
