# Burundi

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In November 2023 the revenue authority (OBR) said large and medium taxpayers file returns online](<https://abpinfo.bi/2023/12/01/lancement-officiel-des-activites-de-celebration-de-la-8eme-edition-de-la-journee-dediee-aux-contribuables/>). [They have done so on its Umutangakori portal since 1 August 2023](<https://abpinfo.bi/2023/12/01/lancement-officiel-des-activites-de-celebration-de-la-8eme-edition-de-la-journee-dediee-aux-contribuables/>). [In January 2026 Burundi Times reported that the OBR had begun implementing E-KORI](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>). [It is a new platform for online declaration and payment, with World Bank support](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>). [The Finance Ministry says taxpayers will declare and pay remotely once E-KORI is fully operational](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The OBR said in November 2023 that taxes are paid online through Umutangakori, with receipts issued the same way](<https://abpinfo.bi/2023/12/01/lancement-officiel-des-activites-de-celebration-de-la-8eme-edition-de-la-journee-dediee-aux-contribuables/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
