# Burundi

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In January 2026 Burundi Times reported that the revenue authority (OBR) had begun implementing E-KORI](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>). [E-KORI is an integrated digital platform for online declaration and payment of taxes and duties, with World Bank technical and financial support](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>).
