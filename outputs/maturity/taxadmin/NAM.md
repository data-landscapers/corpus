# Namibia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [NamRA says taxpayers file returns through its ITAS portal, which has faults but is partly restored](<https://economist.com.na/tax-deadline-extended-to-august-as-namra-battles-continued-itas-portal-glitches/>). [It extended the filing deadline from 30 June to 31 August 2026](<https://economist.com.na/tax-deadline-extended-to-august-as-namra-battles-continued-itas-portal-glitches/>). [The Namibia Revenue Agency's e-portal is its Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS)](<https://www.itas.namra.org.na/>). [It states that once logged in, taxpayers can submit their returns online](<https://www.itas.namra.org.na/>). [By 31 March 2025 ITAS had 575,136 active e-filers](<https://observer24.com.na/namra-registers-over-1-million-taxpayers/>), the Windhoek Observer reported. [In April 2025 NamRA set a four-year target to digitise its services fully](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/04/namra-sets-four-year-target-to-fully-digitize-services/>). [It has World Bank support for a digital tax ecosystem](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/04/namra-sets-four-year-target-to-fully-digitize-services/>).
