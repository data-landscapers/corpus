# Namibia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [NamRA says taxpayers file returns through its ITAS portal, which has faults but is partly restored, and it extended the filing deadline from 30 June to 31 August 2026](<https://economist.com.na/tax-deadline-extended-to-august-as-namra-battles-continued-itas-portal-glitches/>). [The Namibia Revenue Agency's Integrated Tax Administration System e-portal (ITAS) states that once logged in, taxpayers can submit their returns online](<https://www.itas.namra.org.na/>). [In April 2025 NamRA set a four-year target to digitise its services fully, with World Bank support for a digital tax ecosystem](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/04/namra-sets-four-year-target-to-fully-digitize-services/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://economist.com.na/tax-deadline-extended-to-august-as-namra-battles-continued-itas-portal-glitches/>).
