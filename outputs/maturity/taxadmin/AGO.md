# Angola

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The 2025 state budget allots Kz 23 billion to the tax authority AGT, according to the news outlet reading the budget](<https://pti.ao/oge-transformacao-digital-da-agt-vai-custar-23-mil-milhoes-kz-ao-estado/>). [The money is for its SIGT/SIIAT digital transformation project](<https://pti.ao/oge-transformacao-digital-da-agt-vai-custar-23-mil-milhoes-kz-ao-estado/>). [In September 2026 CIAT authors described AGT's Integrated Tax Management System (SIGT)](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>). [It is meant to take tax procedures off paper](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>). [That runs from registration and the submission of returns to the payment of taxes](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>).

**Electronic payment.** [In July 2026 AGT ruled that taxes are, as a general rule, paid electronically through its Portal do Contribuinte](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/fim-do-papel-agt-exige-via-digital-para-pagamento-de-impostos-e-reclamacoes>). [CIAT counts the payment of taxes within SIGT's scope](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 CIAT authors said the SIGT system aims to move the submission of returns online](<https://www.ciat.org/modernisation-of-general-tax-administration-over-the-last-10-years-a-tax-and-customs-analysis/?lang=en>).
