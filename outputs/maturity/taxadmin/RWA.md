# Rwanda

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says taxpayers declare taxes online or by phone](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>). They use E-Tax, M-Declaration and the Local Government Taxes (LGT) system. E-Tax was introduced in 2012. [The authority's 7th edition tax statistics report electronic filing, meaning all filing made online, for excise, corporate income tax, PAYE, personal income tax and VAT in 2023/24](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RRA_Tax_Statistics_2023-2024.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says that after declaring, taxes are paid using E-Payment, Online Banking, MTN Mobile Money and Mobicash](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** [The same tax statistics put electronic filings at about 96 to 100 percent of filings](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RRA_Tax_Statistics_2023-2024.pdf>). That covers excise, corporate income tax, PAYE, personal income tax and VAT. It holds for each year from 2020/21 to 2023/24.

***Also on file***. [Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) / EBM](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory.
