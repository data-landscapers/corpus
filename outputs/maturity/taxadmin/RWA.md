# Rwanda

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says taxpayers declare taxes online or by phone using E-Tax, M-Declaration and the Local Government Taxes (LGT) system, and that E-Tax was introduced in 2012](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Electronic payment.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority's 2025 Tax Handbook says that after declaring, taxes are paid using E-Payment, Online Banking, MTN Mobile Money and Mobicash](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/Folder_for_2025/RRA_Tax_Handbook_2025_Final.pdf>).
