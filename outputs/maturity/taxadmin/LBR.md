# Liberia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The plan says LITAS registration, return processing and payment modules are operational and rolled out to one Tax Business Office, and lists e-filing among the LRA's strengths](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [The plan tables LITAS with an expected result of all modules functional and rolled out to all Tax Business Offices](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [The joint press conference says the LRA is expanding LITAS across regional tax offices to strengthen domestic tax administration](<https://mfdp.gov.lr/index.php/media-center/press-release/joint-press-conference-by-the-ministry-of-finance-and-development-planning-liberia-revenue-authority-and-central-bank-of-liberia-on-gac-s-compliance-audit-report-on-the-government-of-liberia-revenue-collection-and-reconciliation-processes-for-the-period-july-1-2018-to-december-31-2024>). [The LRA said in August 2025 that LITAS handles online filing, assessments and payments](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The plan says a growing number of taxpayers use direct transfer and mobile money to pay taxes, and lists e-payments among the LRA's strengths](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>). [In August 2025 the LRA said taxes are paid through LITAS by mobile money and through seven of the nine commercial banks connected to it, with electronic receipts](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/LRA-CSP-2025-Final.pdf>).
