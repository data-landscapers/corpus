# Congo

## Tax

**Online filing.** [L'Economie reports taxpayers declare on the E-Tax platform, and the Fouta pilot was run among firms already familiar with online declaration](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). [The Ministry of Finance says the FOUTA pilot phase was engaged with a number of enterprises that already file returns electronically (telediclaration), with the declaration made on E-Tax](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/pr%C3%A9sentation-du-switch-mon%C3%A9tique-national-onec-c_030726>). [The Ministry of Finance says the FOUTA online tax payment platform is being deployed in three phases, with a pilot phase engaged, large and medium enterprises next, and all taxpayers by the end of 2026](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/pr%C3%A9sentation-du-switch-mon%C3%A9tique-national-onec-c_030726>).

**Electronic payment.** [L'Economie reports that since 3 August 2026 taxpayers pay taxes and levies online through the Fouta national switch after declaring on E-Tax, with an electronic Treasury receipt, rollout to all taxpayers by end of 2026](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).
