# Nigeria

## Tax

**Online filing.** [TaxPro Max, the FIRS digital tax administration platform, enables taxpayer registration and filing of returns in a fully automated environment](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [FIRS says TaxPro-Max enables registration, filing and payment of taxes (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>). 5 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Electronic payment.** [FIRS directed that all tax payments must originate from assessments on TaxPro Max, which the article says handles payment processing](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [FIRS says TaxPro-Max enables payment of taxes and that taxpayers cannot pay without the Document Identity Number it generates (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** [FIRS says that from 7 June 2021 TaxPro-Max is the channel for filing all Naira-denominated tax returns, with manual returns uploaded by FIRS staff (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Case Management System (Office of the Tax Ombud)](<https://taxombud.gov.ng/tax-ombud-launches-website-call-centre-and-case-management-portal-to-enhance-taxpayer-services/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
