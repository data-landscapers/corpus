# Nigeria

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In April 2025 The Economic Times described TaxPro Max as the digital tax administration platform of the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [It said the platform enables taxpayer registration and filing of returns in a fully automated environment](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [In June 2021 FIRS said TaxPro-Max enables registration, filing and payment of taxes](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** [In April 2025 FIRS directed that all tax payments must originate from assessments on TaxPro Max](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [The Economic Times said the platform handles payment processing](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [In June 2021 FIRS said taxpayers cannot pay without the Document Identity Number that TaxPro-Max generates](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** [FIRS said that from 7 June 2021 TaxPro-Max is the channel for filing all Naira-denominated tax returns](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>). [FIRS staff upload manual returns to it](<https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/tax/firs-public-notice-on-taxpro-max.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Case Management System (Office of the Tax Ombud)](<https://taxombud.gov.ng/tax-ombud-launches-website-call-centre-and-case-management-portal-to-enhance-taxpayer-services/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
