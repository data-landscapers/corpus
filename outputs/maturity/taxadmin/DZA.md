# Algeria

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In May 2026 the tax directorate (DGI) extended its Jibayatic tele-declaration and tele-payment system to all local tax offices](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [It did so under DGI instruction 31/2026 of 6 May 2026, AlgerieZoom reported](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [Until that instruction, only large-enterprise (DGE) taxpayers had to file electronically](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>).

**Electronic payment.** [From May 2026 taxpayers of tax centres, proximity centres and inspections can pay taxes remotely through Jibayatic](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [It is the first time they can do so, and the method is optional](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [The DGI said in July 2026 that payment through Jibayatic is by CIB or Edahabia card or bank transfer](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/direction-generale-des-impots-lancement-du-paiement-electronique-a-distance-des-impots-via-le-portail-jibayatic/>).

**Mostly online.** [In 2026 AlgerieZoom reported that electronic filing is mandatory for DGE taxpayers](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [Instruction 31/2026 also makes it mandatory for real-profit regime taxpayers and simplified non-commercial professions](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>). [The 2026 Finance Law makes electronic filing mandatory for real-regime taxpayers, payroll returns and wealth-tax filers in tax offices equipped for it](<https://www.britacom.org/zt/BRPolicies/Algeria/Booklets/202601/P020260126579296296093.pdf>). [Returns for 2025, other than series G n°50, may still be filed on paper](<https://algeriezoom.com/impots-algerie-5-changements-generalisation-jibayatic/>).
