# Chad

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In August 2025 the Director General of Taxes said taxpayers can declare their taxes online through E-Tax](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [E-Tax was launched in 2022](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [The DGI says E-Tax is a platform for online registration, filing (teledeclaration) and payment](<https://tchadinfos.com/2024/03/20/vulgarisation-de-e-tax-la-direction-des-impots-echange-avec-les-experts-comptables-et-les-conseillers-fiscaux/>). [Returns, payment orders and receipts can be issued and downloaded online](<https://tchadinfos.com/2024/03/20/vulgarisation-de-e-tax-la-direction-des-impots-echange-avec-les-experts-comptables-et-les-conseillers-fiscaux/>). [The DGI portal dates the launch of online filing on eTAX to 30 January 2023](<https://dgi.td/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The Director General of Taxes said that through E-Tax taxpayers can pay their taxes online, with banks and mobile operators facilitating electronic payments](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [The DGI says E-Tax allows taxpayers to pay online all taxes they owe, and it is interconnected with eight banks](<https://tchadinfos.com/2024/03/20/vulgarisation-de-e-tax-la-direction-des-impots-echange-avec-les-experts-comptables-et-les-conseillers-fiscaux/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in August 2025 the Director General of Taxes said banks and mobile operators facilitate online payment](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [The payment goes through E-Tax](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>).
