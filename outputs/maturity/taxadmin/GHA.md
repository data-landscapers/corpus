# Ghana

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The GRA is deploying its Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) as a single platform covering registration, filing, payment and accounting](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>). [Commissioner Yamborigya says some capabilities are operational and more will be rolled out progressively](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/gras-itas-and-other-digital-platforms-set-to-strengthen-tax-compliance/>). [MyJoyOnline reports the ITAS pilot currently covers filing of PAYE, VAT, VAT Withholding and Withholding VAT for selected taxpayers](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [Graphic Online reports GRA began onboarding taxpayers onto ITAS](<https://graphiconline.com/news/general-news/ghana-news-gra-activates-new-tax-administration-platform-over-400-taxpayers-onboarded.html>). [Over 400 from the Accra West Area Office were onboarded in phase one, with large taxpayers next](<https://graphiconline.com/news/general-news/ghana-news-gra-activates-new-tax-administration-platform-over-400-taxpayers-onboarded.html>).

**Electronic payment.** [MyJoyOnline reports the ITAS pilot currently covers payment of PAYE, VAT, VAT Withholding and Withholding VAT](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [Taxpayers can pay electronically through portals and mobile channels](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>). [The GRA says tax payments initiated through ITAS and GITMIS are processed on the Ghana.gov payment platform](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/gra-working-to-resolve-intermittent-tax-payment-challenges-on-ghana-gov-platform/>). [The platform was hit by intermittent faults from 14 August 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/gra-working-to-resolve-intermittent-tax-payment-challenges-on-ghana-gov-platform/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the ITAS pilot covered filing of PAYE and VAT returns for selected taxpayers](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/understanding-ghanas-new-integrated-tax-administration-system-itas/>).
