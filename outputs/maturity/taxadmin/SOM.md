# Somalia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The World Bank ISR (archived 26 Nov 2025) says the contract for the Integrated Tax Administration System was signed after the September 2025 mid-term review, with online filing by large taxpayers still at 0% awaiting ITAS implementation](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>).
