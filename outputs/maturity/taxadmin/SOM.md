# Somalia

## Tax

**Online filing.** [A World Bank project status report archived in November 2025 says the Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) contract was signed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>). [It was signed after the project's September 2025 mid-term review](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>). [The report puts online filing by large taxpayers at 0%, awaiting ITAS implementation](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the World Bank said the ITAS contract had been signed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the World Bank said large taxpayers were filing none of their returns online](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112625052026899/pdf/P177298-a188364c-8fe9-4a23-a8d1-afb09ca7c17a.pdf>).
