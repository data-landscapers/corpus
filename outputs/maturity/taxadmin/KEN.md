# Kenya

## Tax

**Online filing.** Kenyans file tax returns on the revenue authority's iTax portal. Since mid-August 2026 [VAT returns are filed there](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>), after the offline Excel template was dropped, The Star reported. From May 2026 [export values from the customs system are prefilled](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>) in the VAT return. Returns can also be filed [through a WhatsApp service run by an AI chatbot](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>), and [by USSD code](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2329-president-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliancepresident-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliance>), the Commissioner General said. Since July 2023 the government payroll system [has been linked to iTax](<https://techweez.com/2024/06/14/kenya-uhr-egp-under-imf-agreement/>) for employees' PAYE.

**Electronic payment.** Capital gains tax [is paid through the iTax system](<https://anoadvocates.com/insights/stamp-duty-payments-simplified-by-ardhipay>), the law firm ANO Advocates wrote in July 2024. Since a change to iTax, stamp duty can be paid without first quoting the capital gains payment number. Stamp duty on land deals is paid through the lands ministry's Ardhisasa portal, by M-PESA or bank transfer.

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact is the [Business Daily report](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>) that iTax crashed at the June 2025 filing deadline.

***Also on file***. [iCMS](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>); [TIMS/eTIMS](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>).
