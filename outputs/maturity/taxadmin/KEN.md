# Kenya

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Star column says KRA's iTax portal takes electronically submitted returns, with VAT returns filed on iTax from mid-August 2026 after moving off the offline Excel template](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>). [KRA's public notice of 23 April 2026 says that from May 2026 validated export values from iCMS are prefilled in the VAT return in iTax, which taxpayers file there](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>). [The Kenya Revenue Authority says taxpayers can file returns through its WhatsApp-based tax filing service, powered by the Shuru AI chatbot](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>). [KRA Commissioner General Humphrey Wattanga said a Kenyan can file returns through the USSD code *222*5# and KRA services are also on WhatsApp](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2329-president-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliancepresident-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliance>). [The Treasury Cabinet Secretary said in June 2024 that the government's UHR payroll system has been linked to iTax since July 2023 for filing employees' PAYE](<https://techweez.com/2024/06/14/kenya-uhr-egp-under-imf-agreement/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source held since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>).

**Mostly online.** No source held since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system.
