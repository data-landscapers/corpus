# Kenya

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Star column says KRA's iTax portal takes electronically submitted returns, with VAT returns filed on iTax from mid-August 2026 after moving off the offline Excel template](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>). [KRA's public notice of 23 April 2026 says that from May 2026 validated export values from iCMS are prefilled in the VAT return in iTax, which taxpayers file there](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>). [KRA's November/December 2025 release notes describe iTax handling taxpayer income tax returns, with returns validated against eTIMS invoices and customs import records, and a fix letting taxpayers amend monthly rental income returns](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** No source held since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>).

**Mostly online.** No source held since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/opinion/star-blogs/2026-09-01-monari-when-itax-fails-the-taxpayer-still-pays>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [iCMS](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>); [TIMS/eTIMS](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2368-integration-of-the-export-process-in-icms-with-the-vat-return-in-itax>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system.
