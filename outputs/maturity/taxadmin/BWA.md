# Botswana

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In July 2025 BURS board chairperson Lesedi Senatla encouraged taxpayers to file 2025 individual returns through the e-services portal and Mtax mobile app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>). [The filing season ran from 1 July to 30 September 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>). [In August 2020 BURS's acting commissioner general urged individuals to e-file on its website or the MTax app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/57896>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 BURS urged taxpayers to use its e-services portal and Mtax mobile app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>).

**Mostly online.** [In April 2020 a BURS official said the new Lekgetho Live system took manual schedules only for fewer than 10 payees](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/55086>). [Submissions for more than 10 payees had to be online](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/55086>).
