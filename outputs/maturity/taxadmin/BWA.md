# Botswana

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In July 2025 BURS board chairperson Lesedi Senatla urged taxpayers to file 2025 individual returns through the e-services portal and Mtax mobile app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>). [The season ran from 1 July to 30 September 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>). [In August 2020 BURS's acting commissioner general urged individuals to e-file on its e-services website or MTax app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/57896>). [In July 2024 Commissioner General Janet Makgolo said individual returns are filed online](<https://www.gabzfm.com/post/burs-encourages-online-filing-after-falling-short-of-tax-return-target>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 BURS urged taxpayers to use its e-services portal and Mtax mobile app](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>).

**Mostly online.** [Makgolo said 77% of the 38,000 individual returns filed in the previous financial year were submitted online](<https://www.gabzfm.com/post/burs-encourages-online-filing-after-falling-short-of-tax-return-target>). [From April 2020 BURS's Lekgetho Live system required schedules for more than 10 payees to be filed online](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/55086>).
