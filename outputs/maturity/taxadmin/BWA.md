## Tax

**Online filing.** [BURS board chairperson Lesedi Senatla encouraged taxpayers to file 2025 individual returns through BURS' e-services portal and Mtax mobile app, with the filing season running 1 July to 30 September 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87418>). [BURS's acting commissioner general urged individuals required to file returns to e-file on its e-services website or mobile app (MTax), with the 2020 filing season running to 30 November (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/57896>).
