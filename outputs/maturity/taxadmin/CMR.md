# Cameroon

## Tax

**Online filing.** [LeFisk reported in August 2026 that the tax directorate takes returns online through ATOM](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/enregistrement-actes-en-ligne-dgi-cameroun-registrations-guide>). It has since put the registration of deeds on a new platform. [Tele-declaration began in 2016 for the largest taxpayers and was extended in 2022 to the local and individual taxpayer centres](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>). [A 4.7 billion FCFA project to modernise the integrated tax management system was announced in May 2026](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).

**Electronic payment.** [Registration duties on public-procurement contracts are assessed and paid online on registrations.dgi.cm, by Mobile Money, Orange Money or a DGI code](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/enregistrement-actes-en-ligne-dgi-cameroun-registrations-guide>), LeFisk reported in August 2026. Only these three deeds of the 120 listed were open. [Tele-payment had been extended to the local and individual taxpayer centres in 2022](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. [The 2022 extension of tele-declaration gives no share of returns filed online](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).
