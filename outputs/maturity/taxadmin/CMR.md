# Cameroon

## Tax

**Online filing.** [A new project to modernise the Integrated Tax and Duty Management System (SIGIT), estimated at about 4.7 billion FCFA and backed by technical partners, aims to strengthen interconnection of tax services and automate procedures, per L'Economie](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>). [Tele-declaration was introduced progressively from fiscal year 2016 for taxpayers of the Large Taxpayers Directorate (DGE) and extended from 2022 to the Local and Individual Taxpayer Centres, per L'Economie (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).

**Electronic payment.** [Tele-payment was introduced progressively from fiscal year 2016 for DGE taxpayers and extended from 2022 to the Local and Individual Taxpayer Centres, per L'Economie (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leconomie.info/digitalisation-fiscale-au-cameroun-la-direction-generale-des-impots-opere-sa-mue/>).
