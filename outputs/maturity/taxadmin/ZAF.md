# South Africa

## Tax

**Online filing.** [ITWeb reports SARS's 2026 filing season ran auto assessments from 1 to 12 July, with non-provisional taxpayers submitting returns from 13 July to 23 October and some completing a manual eFiling submission](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/ai-becomes-taxmans-little-helper/KA3Wwqdzpgb7rydZ>). [SARS says individual taxpayers file returns via eFiling and the SARS MobiApp from 21 July to 20 October 2025, with an outcome in under five seconds for most online returns](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/over-5-million-taxpayers-auto-assessed-2025-filing-season-gets-underway>). [ITWeb reports that SARS had auto-assessed more than 6.9 million returns by 30 September 2026, after improving eFiling for that season](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sarss-expanded-auto-assessments-pay-off/kYbe9MXbjykvAWpG>).
