# Mauritius

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority says foreign suppliers of digital services can register for VAT and submit VAT returns online through its VAT filing portal](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [The Mauritius Revenue Authority says individuals file their 2025 income tax returns electronically with a User Id (NID, NCID or TAN) and password, due 15 October 2025](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Electronic payment.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority says VAT due from foreign digital-service suppliers must be remitted electronically to it, in several approved foreign currencies](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

**Mostly online.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority says all VAT returns from foreign digital-service suppliers must be submitted electronically through its systems](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).
