# Mauritius

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority (MRA) says individuals file their 2025 income tax returns electronically, due by 15 October 2025](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>). [They log in with a User Id (NID, NCID or TAN) and a password](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>). [In 2026 the MRA said foreign suppliers of digital services can register for VAT and submit VAT returns through its VAT filing portal](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [Its 2022/23 annual report lists e-filing among its services, with an income-returns season from August to October 2022](<https://www.mra.mu/download/AnnualReport22-23.pdf>). [A Taxpayer Portal gives individuals online services 24 hours a day, seven days a week](<https://www.mra.mu/download/AnnualReport22-23.pdf>).

**Electronic payment.** [The MRA's 2022/23 annual report says it collected 98% of tax receipts through e-payment](<https://www.mra.mu/download/AnnualReport22-23.pdf>). [In 2026 it said foreign digital-service suppliers must remit VAT to it electronically, in several approved foreign currencies](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

**Mostly online.** [In 2026 the MRA said all VAT returns from foreign digital-service suppliers must be submitted electronically through its systems](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [Its 2022/23 annual report says tax returns were e-filed in almost all cases](<https://www.mra.mu/download/AnnualReport22-23.pdf>).
