# Djibouti

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The World Bank implementation status report for the Economic and Financial Governance Project sets a PDO indicator on the percentage of tax returns filed and paid on time via ITAS, at 0 as of 9 January 2026 against a 70% target for December 2029, so the system is not yet in use](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012726115520907/pdf/P180696-28ecf2f4-34e9-4559-9695-f14bd83f8798.pdf>). [The World Bank project record for the US$20m Djibouti Economic and Financial Governance Project says it includes tax administration system modernization and revenue administration digitalization](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P180696>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012726115520907/pdf/P180696-28ecf2f4-34e9-4559-9695-f14bd83f8798.pdf>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012726115520907/pdf/P180696-28ecf2f4-34e9-4559-9695-f14bd83f8798.pdf>).
