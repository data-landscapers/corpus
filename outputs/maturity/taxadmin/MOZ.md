# Mozambique

## Tax

**Online filing.** [PwC's page, last reviewed 11 August 2026, says the eDeclaração platform takes online income tax returns](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>). [The Contribuinte Portal takes monthly VAT returns, and manual filing is also allowed](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>). [Registered taxpayers generate a reference number online and pay VAT, PIT and CIT through their bank](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>). [In March 2022 the tax authority said e-Declaracao took VAT, IRPC, IRPS and other returns](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2022/03/22/economia/financas/autoridade-tributaria-lanca-portal-do-contribuinte/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 PwC said taxpayers generate a payment reference online and pay through their commercial bank](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 PwC said manual filing was still allowed alongside the online platforms](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>).
