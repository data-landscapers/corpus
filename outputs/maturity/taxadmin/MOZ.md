# Mozambique

## Tax

**Online filing.** [PwC's page, last reviewed 11 August 2026, says the Mozambique Tax Authority's eDeclaração platform takes online income tax returns and the Contribuinte Portal takes monthly VAT returns, with manual filing also allowed](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>). [PwC says taxpayers registered on the online platform generate a reference number there and pay VAT, employment-income PIT and CIT through their commercial bank](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>). [The tax authority says its recently introduced e-Declaracao system allows submission of VAT, ISPC, IRPC, IRPS and other tax returns, and its new taxpayer portal also allows electronic submission of returns (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2022/03/22/economia/financas/autoridade-tributaria-lanca-portal-do-contribuinte/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mozambique/individual/tax-administration>).
