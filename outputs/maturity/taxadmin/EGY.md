# Egypt

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Egyptian Tax Authority head Rasha Abdel Aal said taxpayers have responded to the electronic systems and the mobile app](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [The head said they have already started creating accounts and submitting returns electronically](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [In October 2025 the authority's head said employers compute wage tax on its unified platform](<https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/mnzwmt-twhyd-ass-wmayyr-ahtsab-drybt-alajwr-walmrtbat-ysahm-fy-thqyq-aldalt-aldrybyt>). [They do so by payroll integration, spreadsheet upload or manual entry](<https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/mnzwmt-twhyd-ass-wmayyr-ahtsab-drybt-alajwr-walmrtbat-ysahm-fy-thqyq-aldalt-aldrybyt>). [An employee portal now issues payslips](<https://eta.gov.eg/ar/news/mnzwmt-twhyd-ass-wmayyr-ahtsab-drybt-alajwr-walmrtbat-ysahm-fy-thqyq-aldalt-aldrybyt>).

**Electronic payment.** [Finance minister Ahmed Kouchouk said real estate transactions tax can be paid electronically by bank cards or direct-debit cards](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [A final clearance is issued online in five steps](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the Egyptian Tax Authority's head said taxpayers had started submitting returns electronically](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>).
