# Mauritania

## Tax

**Online filing.** [In September 2026 the tax directorate (DGI) said taxpayers can declare their taxes online through its STT system](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). [The system runs at stt.e-tax.impots.gov.mr](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>).

**Electronic payment.** [In September 2026 the DGI said STT lets taxpayers pay their taxes online and add their means of payment](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [STT offers online declaration of taxes, the DGI said in September 2026](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>).
