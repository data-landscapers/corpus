# Benin

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Direction générale des impôts says its e-services portal, launched 23 May 2024 in Cotonou for small businesses and individuals, lets taxpayers declare income online](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/le-benin-permet-desormais-aux-contribuables-et-aux-particuliers-de-declarer-et-de-payer-leurs-impots-en-ligne/>). [The government says a single online return for payroll tax and social security contributions was launched on 3 February 2020 on the eservices.impots.bj platform (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/493/direction-generale-des-impots-lancement-du-formulaire-unique-de-declaration-des-impots-sur-salaires-et-des-cotisations-de-securite-sociale/>).

**Electronic payment.** [The Direction générale des impôts describes the e-services portal as a portal for online declaration and payment (télépaiement) of taxes, with land taxes payable online, since 23 May 2024](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/le-benin-permet-desormais-aux-contribuables-et-aux-particuliers-de-declarer-et-de-payer-leurs-impots-en-ligne/>). [The government says the single payroll tax and social security contribution return is followed by online payment on the e-services platform, launched 3 February 2020 (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/493/direction-generale-des-impots-lancement-du-formulaire-unique-de-declaration-des-impots-sur-salaires-et-des-cotisations-de-securite-sociale/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
