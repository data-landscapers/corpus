# Benin

## Tax

**Online filing.** [On 23 May 2024 the Direction générale des impôts launched its e-services portal in Cotonou](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/le-benin-permet-desormais-aux-contribuables-et-aux-particuliers-de-declarer-et-de-payer-leurs-impots-en-ligne/>). It lets small businesses and individuals declare income online. [In February 2020 the government launched a single online return for payroll tax and social security contributions](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/493/direction-generale-des-impots-lancement-du-formulaire-unique-de-declaration-des-impots-sur-salaires-et-des-cotisations-de-securite-sociale/>), on the same platform.

**Electronic payment.** [The tax directorate describes the e-services portal as a place to declare and pay taxes online, land taxes included](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/le-benin-permet-desormais-aux-contribuables-et-aux-particuliers-de-declarer-et-de-payer-leurs-impots-en-ligne/>). [A circular of 16 September 2025 made tax stamps and deed registration online only](<https://africaho.bj/article/benin-la-dgi-dematerialise-les-timbres-fiscaux-et-lenregistrement-des-actes/>). Payment goes through e-services.impots.bj, and a QR-coded electronic stamp replaces the paper one.

**Mostly online.** [The September 2025 circular requires tax stamps to be paid online](<https://africaho.bj/article/benin-la-dgi-dematerialise-les-timbres-fiscaux-et-lenregistrement-des-actes/>). No source found since October 2023 says online filing of returns is mandatory or used for half or more of returns.
