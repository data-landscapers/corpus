# Tanzania

## Tax

**Online filing.** [Tanzania told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025 that e-filing for tax and customs declarations is available and in use](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [The Tanzania Revenue Authority's new IDRAS system was scheduled to launch on 9 February 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/how-new-tax-system-enhances-compliance/>). The Commissioner for Domestic Revenue said it replaces separate VAT and other platforms. It handles registration, return filing, payments, objections and sales monitoring. [The Daily News reports IDRAS began operation in January 2025, starting with vehicle registration and driver's licence systems in February 2025](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tax-administration-driver-licence-goes-digital/>).

**Electronic payment.** No source found since October 2023 says taxes can be paid electronically through the tax authority's system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dailynews.co.tz/how-new-tax-system-enhances-compliance/>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>).
