# Gabon

## Tax

**Online filing.** [The Gabon DGI announced on 18 August 2025 a new e-tax portal for online tax declaration, so taxpayers can file returns online](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Electronic payment.** [The Gabon DGI announced on 18 August 2025 that the e-tax portal also takes online payment of taxes](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>).

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>).
