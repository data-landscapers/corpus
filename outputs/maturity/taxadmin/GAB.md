# Gabon

## Tax

**Online filing.** The tax directorate [announced an e-tax portal for online tax returns on 18 August 2025](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>), so taxpayers can file online. In October 2026 [eTax was being brought into the Gabon Connect portal](<https://dgi.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Flyer-Gabon-Connect-%E2%80%93-Rencontre-FEG.pdf>). [A single online monthly salary declaration opened there on 21 October for the 440 firms of the large-enterprises directorate](<https://dgi.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Flyer-Gabon-Connect-%E2%80%93-Rencontre-FEG.pdf>), the directorate's flyer says.

**Electronic payment.** [The e-tax portal also takes online payment of taxes](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>). The tax directorate announced it on 18 August 2025, with online filing on the same portal.

**Mostly online.** No source found since October 2023 says online filing is mandatory or used for half or more of returns. The nearest held fact: [the salary declaration for the large firms opened online in October 2026](<https://dgi.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Flyer-Gabon-Connect-%E2%80%93-Rencontre-FEG.pdf>).
