# Liberia

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The Liberia Revenue Authority says it launched an Online Duty-Free Application Platform in July 2025, where qualified individuals and institutions submit their duty-free requests electronically](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-digitizes-rural-customs-offices-rolls-out-online-duty-free-platform/>). [Liberia's National ePortal, eLiberia.gov.lr, was launched on 23 July 2018 as a one-stop shop for government information, the posts and telecommunications minister said](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-posts-telecommunications-calls-for-use-of-internet-to-access-govt-information/>).

**Single gateway.** No portal held since October 2023 carries the services of several ministries with sign-in by the national ID. The nearest held fact is [the revenue authority's own duty-free platform](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-digitizes-rural-customs-offices-rolls-out-online-duty-free-platform/>), which serves one agency's applicants.

**Use published.** No count of users, applications or transactions through a national portal has been found. The nearest held fact is [the 2025 launch of the duty-free platform](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-digitizes-rural-customs-offices-rolls-out-online-duty-free-platform/>), which gives no figures for its use.
