# Algeria

## Government services online

**Services online.** In July 2026 the High Commission for Digitalisation said [the national portal, Dzair Digital Services, carried 21 digital services](<https://elhiwar.dz/event/335541/>), with 62 planned by the end of 2027. They include [civil status, residence and family certificates, social security attestations and unemployment benefit](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>). The portal [was piloted in March and April 2026 across seven ministerial sectors](<https://iafrica.com/algeria-reviews-national-ai-strategy-progress-and-approves-launch-of-dzair-digital-services-portal/>). In October 2026 the housing minister said [four housing-ministry services had been added](<https://www.expressdz.dz/2026/10/22/habitat-inscription-de-545-000-logements-dans-le-plf-2027/>).

**Single gateway.** The [one national portal carries services of the interior, transport, labour and social security sectors](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>). Users sign up for a digital identity with their national ID number and personal details.

**Use published.** The vocational training minister said [82,383 candidates had registered through the national portal by 23 August 2026](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%B6%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9>). Registration for training intake [opened on the portal for the first time on 8 August 2026](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%B6%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9>).
