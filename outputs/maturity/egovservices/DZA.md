# Algeria

## Government services online

**Services online.** In July 2026 the High Commission for Digitalisation said [the national portal, Dzair Digital Services, carried 21 digital services](<https://elhiwar.dz/event/335541/>), with 62 planned by the end of 2027. They include [civil status, residence and family certificates, social security attestations and unemployment benefit](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>). By 10 November 2026 [the portal offered 68 services](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/portail-numerique-les-documents-en-ligne-desormais-reconnus>), and public offices accept the signed documents it issues. On 19 November 2026 a High Commission for Digitalisation expert [put it at 91 public services](<https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/99407>). [Twenty tourism and handicraft services were added on 16 November](<https://www.algerie360.com/dzair-digital-services-tourisme-artisanat-nouveaux-services/>). Earlier, [the Fada El Djazair platform has run digital services since 14 March 2024](<https://www.elkhabar.com/fr/economie/recettes-record-et-services-numeriques-l-etat-modernise-la-gestion-fonciere-263223>), with a pilot of online cadastral extracts.

**Single gateway.** The [one national portal carries services of the interior, transport, labour and social security sectors](<https://elmassar-elarabi.dz/152310>). Users sign up for a digital identity with their national ID number and personal details. In November 2026 a High Commission expert said [one account reaches services of the interior, local government, employment, social security, tax and justice sectors](<https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/99407>).

**Use published.** In November 2026 the minister said [the portal had over two million registered users](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/portail-numerique-les-documents-en-ligne-desormais-reconnus>) and had issued over 800,000 signed documents. On 19 November 2026 a High Commission expert [gave about two million registered citizens and nearly 11 million visits](<https://news.radioalgerie.dz/ar/node/99407>). El Khabar reports that [nearly 7 million users were served through Fada El Djazair](<https://www.elkhabar.com/fr/economie/recettes-record-et-services-numeriques-l-etat-modernise-la-gestion-fonciere-263223>) from March 2024 to April 2026. The vocational training minister said [82,383 candidates had registered through the national portal by 23 August 2026](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%B6%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9>).
