## Government services online

**Services online.** [Echaab reports that administrative services have expanded through the dzair digital services portal, simplifying procedures in vital sectors, citing the High Commission for Digitalisation's achievements](<https://www.echaab.dz/2026/09/20/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A9/>).
