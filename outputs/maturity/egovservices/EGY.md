# Egypt

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Communications minister Raafat Hindy says the Digital Egypt platform currently provides more than 240 digital government services in 29 service bundles, with a target of 400 services in 2030](<https://www.alnaharegypt.com/1196568>). [Vetogate reports the Digital Egypt platform offers real estate registration and notarisation services online, including powers of attorney, transaction lookup and office booking, some still needing a visit](<https://www.vetogate.com/5748525>). [Veto Gate's analysis says the Digital Egypt platform lets citizens complete many government procedures online, from registration and document upload to fee payment, without visiting the agency](<https://www.vetogate.com/5741296>). [The government launched the Egypt Digital Platform in 2022, which delivers about 170 government services (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/DocumentLibraryIssues/AttachmentA/9154/Digitalization%20in%20Egypt.pdf>). [The foreign ministry began piloting a consular-services app for Egyptians abroad on 21 October 2026, covering 17 missions](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/foreign-ministry-launches-pilot-operation-of-e-platform-for-consular-services-for-egyptians-abroad/>). [In October 2026 the platform's 250 services were to reach 500 by the end of the year](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/150233/Egypt-Forum-2026-outlines-sweeping-development-agenda-to-raise-living>).
