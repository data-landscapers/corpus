# Egypt

## Government services online

**Services online.** In September 2026 communications minister Raafat Hindy said [the Digital Egypt platform provides more than 240 government services in 29 bundles](<https://www.alnaharegypt.com/1196568>). The target is 400 services by 2030. In October 2026 [a report put the number at 250, to rise to 500 before the end of 2026](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/150233/Egypt-Forum-2026-outlines-sweeping-development-agenda-to-raise-living>). On 21 October 2026 the foreign ministry [began piloting a consular app covering 17 missions](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/foreign-ministry-launches-pilot-operation-of-e-platform-for-consular-services-for-egyptians-abroad/>).

**Single gateway.** [Digital Egypt offers real estate registration and notarisation services](<https://www.vetogate.com/5748525>), among those of other bodies. No source found says how users sign in, or that one account reaches every ministry's services.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through Digital Egypt has been found. The nearest fact: [citizens register, upload documents and pay fees on it without visiting the agency](<https://www.vetogate.com/5741296>).
