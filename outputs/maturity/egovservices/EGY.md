# Egypt

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Communications minister Raafat Hindy says the Digital Egypt platform currently provides more than 240 digital government services in 29 service bundles, with a target of 400 services in 2030](<https://www.alnaharegypt.com/1196568>). [Veto Gate's analysis says the Digital Egypt platform lets citizens complete many government procedures online, from registration and document upload to fee payment, without visiting the agency](<https://www.vetogate.com/5741296>). [Egypt's Ministry of Social Solidarity, with the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, launched the first phase of a digital services system for foster families and care institutions on its social-care services platform, where citizens apply and track requests online](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/29/التضامن-تطلق-أولى-مراحل-منظومة-الخدمات-الرقمية-الخاصة-بالأسر-البديلة/7529204>).
