# Egypt

## Government services online

**Services online.** In September 2026 communications minister Raafat Hindy said [the Digital Egypt platform provides more than 240 government services in 29 bundles](<https://www.alnaharegypt.com/1196568>). The target is 400 services by 2030. In October 2026 [a report put the number at 250, to rise to 500 before the end of 2026](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/150233/Egypt-Forum-2026-outlines-sweeping-development-agenda-to-raise-living>). On 1 November 2026 [environmental impact assessments moved wholly online](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-launches-digital-environmental-impact-assessment-system-ends-paper-based-applications/>). In November 2026 Minister Mohamed Farid said [new digital services were being opened to exporters with the ICT ministry and e-Finance](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>). In November 2026 Vetogate reported [about 22 notarial and real estate registry services on the platform](<https://www.vetogate.com/5767586>), with fees paid online.

**Single gateway.** Enterprise reports that [one Digital Egypt account, opened with the 14-digit national ID and a mobile number](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/02/your-guide-to-navigating-egypts-online-government-services/>), works across civil status, traffic, real estate, court and commercial registry services. Fees are paid online.

**Use published.** In October 2026 assistant ICT minister Mahmoud Badawi said [Digital Egypt had about 13.5 million users](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8381060>). He put their growth at about 30% a year, with about 90% of use on mobile phones.
