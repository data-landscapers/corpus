# DR Congo

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The WSIS Forum 2026 session description says DRC's HandiConnect lets users reach state services and receive government-to-person social protection payments over USSD on basic phones, backed by the Ministry of Telecommunications, the Ministry delegated to Persons with Disabilities and FONA](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>). [Biometric Update, citing the national news agency, reports the DRC launched the RDC-Pass national digital identity platform in Kinshasa, which supports unified access to e-government services via a single login, with a phased rollout beginning with pilot testing](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Single gateway.** [Biometric Update reports RDC-Pass, the national digital ID platform, gives unified access to e-government services via a single login and is described as a central gateway for service delivery across government agencies](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>).
