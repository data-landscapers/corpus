# DR Congo

## Government services online

**Services online.** In June 2026 the government [launched RDC-Pass, a national digital identity platform, in Kinshasa](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>). Its rollout began with pilot testing. A WSIS Forum 2026 session described HandiConnect, which [lets users reach state services and receive social protection payments over USSD](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2026/Agenda/Session/220>). It is backed by the telecoms ministry and the ministry for persons with disabilities. From 1 March 2024 [applications for vehicle plates and registration cards were made and paid online](<https://www.africanewsrdc.net/actu/nicolas-kazadi-plateforme-numerique-plaques-cartes-roses/>), the finance minister said. On 19 November 2026 the civil service ministry [announced E-Services RDC, a portal and app publishing the procedures and official fees of public services](<https://acp.cd/nation/rdc-le-gouvernement-annonce-la-creation-de-la-plateforme-numerique-e-services-rdc/>) in six languages. The President said [it also allows some formalities and payments to be made online](<https://acp.cd/nation/modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-president-tshisekedi-demande-de-renforcer-la-relation-etat-administres/>). The migration directorate [runs an official e-visa application portal](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-accelere-son-projet-de-plateforme-integree-pour-les-visas-electroniques>). In July 2026 the business one-stop shop GUCE [presented a site that tracks company files in real time and an app for formalising informal operators](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/28/formalisation-des-entreprises-en-rdc-daniel-mukoko-samba-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour>). Since June 2025 passport applicants [register on passeport.gouv.cd and can pay online](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance>).

**Single gateway.** Biometric Update describes RDC-Pass as [a central gateway, with one login, for services across government agencies](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>). No source found names a ministry's service that users already reach through it, or says how many services it will carry.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through a national portal has been found. The nearest fact: [RDC-Pass began a phased rollout with pilot testing in 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>).
