# Equatorial Guinea

## Government services online

**Services online.** Equatorial Guinea's [e-visa service opened on 1 July 2023](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_analiza_el_contrato_de_vfs_global_para_reforzar_el_sistema_de_visados_en_linea_y_potenciar_la_promocion_turistica>), run for the government by the company VFS Global. Travellers apply for an entry visa online. In October 2026 the Vice-President [said it had processed about 35,127 e-visas since its launch](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_analiza_el_contrato_de_vfs_global_para_reforzar_el_sistema_de_visados_en_linea_y_potenciar_la_promocion_turistica>), against 200,000 a year expected. He asked the ministries concerned for a report on the system before the contract is formalised.

**Single gateway.** No source found since October 2023 says one national portal carries the services of several ministries. The nearest fact found is [the e-visa platform run by VFS Global](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_analiza_el_contrato_de_vfs_global_para_reforzar_el_sistema_de_visados_en_linea_y_potenciar_la_promocion_turistica>), which serves one service.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through a national portal has been found. The one usage figure found is [the e-visa count of about 35,127 since July 2023](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_analiza_el_contrato_de_vfs_global_para_reforzar_el_sistema_de_visados_en_linea_y_potenciar_la_promocion_turistica>), given in October 2026.
