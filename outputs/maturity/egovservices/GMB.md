# Gambia

## Government services online

**Services online.** In September 2024 the minister said [the government was building a one-stop platform for its services](<https://standard.gm/dr-ceesay-says-govt-committed-to-building-capacities/>). It starts, through an app, with ID cards, birth certificates, business registration, passports and driving licences. In 2025 the communications ministry [presented MYGOV, a one-stop platform starting with five pilot services](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). It is modelled on Bangladesh's platform, with UNDP and ECOWAS support. In 2025 the National Records Commission [opened a portal for information requests](<https://informationcommission.gm/en/news/3923befe-e60f-4ba7-8369-95f1f7ca7059>), tracked online.

**Single gateway.** [MYGOV is planned as one platform for citizens to reach government services](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). No source found says it is in service, or that one sign-in, by the national ID or a shared account, reaches several ministries' services.

**Use published.** No count of users or applications has been found. The nearest fact: [MYGOV begins with five pilot services](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>), so no use of it can yet be counted.
