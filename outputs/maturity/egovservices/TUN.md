# Tunisia

## Government services online

**Services online.** The national playbook of May 2026 [lists online services for citizens, businesses, transport, education, justice and finance](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). Citizens get birth records, driving licence and vehicle data online. The Khadamet app, launched in late August 2026, [gives access to more than forty administrative services through the digital identity](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/11/06/passeport-carte-didentite-permis-les-demarches-a-distance-promises-avant-fin-2026/1427550/>), the minister said. Passport renewal [could be applied for remotely from June 2026](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/11/06/passeport-carte-didentite-permis-les-demarches-a-distance-promises-avant-fin-2026/1427550/>). For 2023/24, [2,279,086 public-school pupils were registered online](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). The ministry [targets 150 services on Khadamet](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0911-143235-tunisie-356-8-millions-pour-la-transformation-numerique-en-2027-face-a-des-defis-persistants>). In January 2025 [the medicines agency opened a platform for its administrative services](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/la-tunisie-lance-une-plateforme-numerique-innovante-pour-lagence-nationale-des-medicaments/>).

**Single gateway.** The playbook [presents e-bawaba.tn as the citizen's access point to services](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). Users sign in through E-Houwiya, the mobile identity, which services of many ministries and bodies use. The [Khadamet app also uses the digital identity for sign-in](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/11/06/passeport-carte-didentite-permis-les-demarches-a-distance-promises-avant-fin-2026/1427550/>).

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through e-bawaba.tn has been found. The nearest figure: [2,279,086 pupils registered online for 2023/24, through the education system](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>).
