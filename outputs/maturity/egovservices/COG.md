# Congo

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The Ministry's page says citizens can use the e-Citizen Portal, from computer or mobile, to file administrative procedures, pay taxes and fees, and track their files in real time](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). [The Budget ministry's third steering committee on 6 June 2024 reviewed the E-Bourses project, under execution, to let students apply online for national and cooperation scholarships](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/gouvernance-le-ministere-du-budget-temoigne-la-bonne-execution-de-trois-projets-157749>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Single gateway.** [The Ministry's page describes the e-Citizen Portal as a single entry point to all digital public services, with each citizen's unique biometric-authenticated digital identifier as the gateway to all online public services](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).

**Use published.** No source found since October 2023 gives users, applications or transactions through the national portal. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).
