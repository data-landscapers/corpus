# Congo

## Government services online

**Services online.** The digital economy ministry says citizens can use the e-Citizen Portal [to file administrative procedures, pay taxes and fees, and track their files in real time](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). They reach it from a computer or a phone. In June 2024 the budget ministry [reviewed E-Bourses, a project to let students apply online for scholarships](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/gouvernance-le-ministere-du-budget-temoigne-la-bonne-execution-de-trois-projets-157749>). It was then under way.

**Single gateway.** The ministry [describes the e-Citizen Portal as the single entry point to all digital public services](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). Each citizen signs in with a unique digital identifier checked by biometrics. That identifier is the gateway to every online public service.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through the e-Citizen Portal has been found. The nearest fact: [taxes and fees are paid through the portal](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).
