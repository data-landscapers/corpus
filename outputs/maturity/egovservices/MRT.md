# Mauritania

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The tax directorate (DGI) states taxpayers can declare and pay taxes online through its STT system and consult their tax file through its teleservices portal](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). [Ecofin Agency, citing Mauritanian authorities, reports that services including driver's licence applications, the road traffic digital system, criminal record issuance, vehicle registration documents and loss certificates for official documents have already been digitised, and that national electricity company services were integrated into the "Khdamati" digital platform](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2703-54171-mauritania-introduces-national-standards-for-digital-public-services>). [ID Tech Wire reports Mauritania launched the Houwiyeti digital identity app giving access to government e-services, with biometric login to the e-services portal demonstrated in the March 2023 pilot](<https://idtechwire.com/mauritania-launches-digital-id-for-e-services-built-on-tech5-platform>).

**Single gateway.** No source held since October 2023 says one national portal carries several ministries' services with shared sign-in. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>).
