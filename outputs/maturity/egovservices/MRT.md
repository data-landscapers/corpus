# Mauritania

## Government services online

**Services online.** In October 2026 a Mauritanian official [put the transactional services online at about forty, of nearly 900 targeted](<https://transcontinents.africa/fr/article/e-gouvernance-le-benin-et-la-mauritanie-a-lecole-estonienne>). [Taxpayers can declare and pay taxes online through the tax directorate's STT system](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). Ecofin Agency reports that [driving licences, criminal records, vehicle papers and loss certificates have been digitised](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2703-54171-mauritania-introduces-national-standards-for-digital-public-services>). The national portal Khidmaty took [university registration in September 2025](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/239312>), [electricity subscriptions in December 2025](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/247224>) and [health fund affiliation in May 2026](<https://cnass.mr/ar/%d8%a5%d8%b7%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%82-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ae%d8%af%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%84%d9%84%d8%b5%d9%86%d8%af%d9%88%d9%82-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b7%d9%86%d9%8a/>). On 4 April 2025 the government [launched the Mes services portal and app with two services](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>), company creation and investment-code accreditation. Since March 2025 [citizens can request and obtain judicial records online](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2210-49751-mauritania-signs-2-year-deal-to-strengthen-courts-with-moroccan-digital-expertise>), Ecofin reports.

**Single gateway.** The [Mes services portal and app](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>) opened in April 2025 with two services, both for businesses. In 2024 the [Houwiyeti digital identity app was launched to give access to e-services](<https://idtechwire.com/mauritania-launches-digital-id-for-e-services-built-on-tech5-platform>), with a biometric login piloted in 2023. [Decree 2024-154 of November 2024 sets how the Khidmaty portal is managed](<https://keyveille2.net/upload/text/40911/D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B0%202024-154.pdf>). A cell at the digital ministry runs it, and each sub-platform is created by joint ministerial order.

**Use published.** The tax directorate's [teleservices portal lets taxpayers consult their tax file](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). It gives no count of users.
