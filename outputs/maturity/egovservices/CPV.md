# Cape Verde

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 18 December 2024 the government [launched the Portal de Justiça](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>). It offers land, commercial, vehicle and criminal-record certificates online, with civil-registry certificates and ID card renewal. The World Bank-funded Consular Portal [put consular services for the diaspora online in 2024](<https://www.caboverdeexpert.com/cabo-verde-digital-operational-nucleus-for-the-information-society-nosi/>), cutting processing times by 85%. In February 2026 the government launched [Gov.cv, a single portal for public services](<https://caboverde24.info/en/2026/07/22/ja-sabe-como-aceder-aos-servicos-publicos-sem-sair-de-casa-analisamos-o-site/>). It takes criminal record, nationality and higher education applications and tracks each request.

**Single gateway.** Users of the Portal de Justiça [sign in through Autentika, the state's authentication platform](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>). [Gov.cv is organised by life event rather than by ministry](<https://caboverde24.info/en/2026/07/22/ja-sabe-como-aceder-aos-servicos-publicos-sem-sair-de-casa-analisamos-o-site/>). No source found says how users sign in to it.
