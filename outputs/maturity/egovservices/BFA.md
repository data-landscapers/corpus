# Burkina Faso

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The portal provides 95 dematerialized procedures and 1,672 information sheets, and integrates 183 public institutions, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>). [The government launched a national portal at www.service-public.gov.bf on 5 May 2026 in Ouagadougou to centralize online public services, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>). [The government announced on 20 February 2026 the official launch of version 2 of the Service Public BF portal, with search covering e-services, step-by-step life-event guides and an administration directory](<https://service-public.gov.bf/actualites/lancement-du-portail-service-public-bf-v2>).
