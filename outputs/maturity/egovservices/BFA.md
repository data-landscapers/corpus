# Burkina Faso

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 5 May 2026 the government [launched service-public.gov.bf to bring public services online in one place](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>). The portal [offers 95 procedures completed online and links 183 public institutions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>). In February 2026 the government [launched version 2 of the Service Public BF portal](<https://service-public.gov.bf/actualites/lancement-du-portail-service-public-bf-v2>), with search across e-services and life-event guides. Since June 2025 the finance ministry's SECOP portal [lets firms request tax, social security and trade-register certificates remotely](<https://faso7.com/2025/06/23/economie-la-plateforme-secop-lancee-pour-faciliter-les-formalites-dobtention-des-pieces-administratives-fonctionnelles-au-burkina-faso/>).

**Single gateway.** The national portal [brings together services from 183 public institutions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>). No source found says how users sign in to it, or that one account reaches them all.

**Use published.** No count of users or requests through the national portal has been found. The nearest fact: [the portal carried 95 online procedures at its launch in May 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-launches-central-portal-to-streamline-e-services>).
