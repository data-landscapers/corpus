# Seychelles

## Government services online

**Services online.** The government's monServis portal [lets users access, communicate and transact with government services online](<https://monservis.gov.sc>). On 1 May 2026 [import and export permits moved to monServis](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/30391/import-and-export-services-to-transition-to-monservis-portal-on-may-1-2026>) from the older E-Services Gateway. In June 2026 [a revamped gov.sc, the official government portal, was launched](<https://nation.sc/articles/31239/seychelles-launches-revamped-govsc-portal-to-strengthen-digital-access>). The Seychelles Pension Fund [lets members view their statements through My e-Services, and employers submit contribution records on its e-Contribution Platform](<https://pensionfund.sc/2026/07/seychelles-pension-fund-showcases-digital-innovation-during-productivity-awareness-week-2026/>).

**Single gateway.** [monServis carries e-services from several providers](<https://monservis.gov.sc>). Individuals sign in with SeyID, the national digital identity, and organisations join through Biz Auth. One sign-in therefore reaches the services of several government providers.
