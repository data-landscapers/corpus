# South Africa

## Government services online

**Services online.** [DCDT says the National e-Government Portal currently allows access to 134 e-Services, including licensing, e-recruitment and exam queries, out of 255 services identified for digitisation](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>). [Home affairs minister Schreiber said the electronic travel authorisation (ETA) system, launched by President Ramaphosa on 12 August, is live for short-stay visa applications from 53 countries, at four international airports](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [The Gauteng Department of e-Government said its 2025/26 annual report records 33 e-Services developed and enhanced during the year](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/gauteng-e-government-accelerates-digital-transformation-e-gov-delivers-more>). [DCDT says it and SITA jointly developed the National e-Government Portal, hosted by SITA at eservices.gov.za, which gives access to e-Services](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>).
