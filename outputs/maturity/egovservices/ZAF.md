# South Africa

## Government services online

**Services online.** [DCDT says the National e-Government Portal currently allows access to 134 e-Services, including licensing, e-recruitment and exam queries, out of 255 services identified for digitisation](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>). [The Gauteng Department of e-Government said its 2025/26 annual report records 33 e-Services developed and enhanced during the year](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/gauteng-e-government-accelerates-digital-transformation-e-gov-delivers-more>). [The Minister of Communications and Digital Technologies said in a written reply that SITA delivered 117 e-Services through 35 digital products to more than 30 government departments and public entities in 2025/26](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/41100/>). [DCDT says it and SITA jointly developed the National e-Government Portal, hosted by SITA at eservices.gov.za, which gives access to e-Services](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>).
