# South Africa

## Government services online

**Services online.** In October 2024 the communications department said [the National e-Government Portal gives access to 134 e-services](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>), of 255 identified for digitisation. They include licensing, recruitment and exam queries. In 2025/26 SITA [delivered 117 e-services through 35 digital products to more than 30 departments](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/41100/>). On 12 August 2026 [the electronic travel authorisation went live for short-stay visas](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). In November 2026 Home Affairs [opened an online portal for asylum applications to its first test cases](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-launches-first-phase-digital-asylum-system-cut-delays-clear>).

**Single gateway.** [The national portal at eservices.gov.za was built by the department and SITA](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>). No source found says how users sign in. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** In April 2024 the minister gave [over 899,394 logins to the portal's services in a year](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/index.php/south-africa/government-accelerates-programme-connect-citizens-internet>). It had over 1.4 million registered users. SITA's chair [put users at 1.3 million in October 2023](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sita-to-ramp-up-access-to-digitised-govt-services/j5alr7QAN157pYQk>). That was 2.4% of the population.
