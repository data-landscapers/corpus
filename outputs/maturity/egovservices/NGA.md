# Nigeria

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 4 August 2026 Galaxy Backbone and the communications ministry [held the soft launch of the Government Service Portal, services.gov.ng](<https://spacewatchafrica.com/galaxy-backbone-unveils-government-service-portal-to-simplify-access-to-public-services/>). It is to be a one-stop gateway for the services of ministries and agencies, starting with pioneer agencies. In August 2026 [it was still in a pilot phase, with more agencies to join](<https://techsoma.africa/nigeria-launches-services-gov-ng-a-single-digital-gateway-for-government-services/>). The interior ministry opened [online passport applications in January 2024](<https://punchng.com/immigration-unveils-online-passport-application-portal-today/>) and [a citizenship and business platform in May 2025](<https://interior.gov.ng/fg-launches-new-citizenship-business-management-platform/>). NIMC [offers a self-service portal and an app for updating identity records](<https://thetrumpet.ng/nigerians-recount-frustrating-experiences-with-nimc-over-data-correction-failed-verification/>).

**Single gateway.** [services.gov.ng is planned as one gateway for the services of ministries, departments and agencies](<https://spacewatchafrica.com/galaxy-backbone-unveils-government-service-portal-to-simplify-access-to-public-services/>). No source found says how users sign in, or that it is in service beyond the pioneer agencies.
