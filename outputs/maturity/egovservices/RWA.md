# Rwanda

## Government services online

**Services online.** [UN DESA states Irembo has grown to provide access to more than 90 government services online](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>). [DIAL reports IremboGov is a one-stop portal for over 240 public services that citizens can access digitally and remotely](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>). [The Internet Society says a Karongi father renewed his family's health insurance and could request a marriage certificate copy through Rwanda's Irembo platform on his own phone, and that government initiatives are moving essential services online](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/09/empowering-parents-in-rwanda-through-digital-literacy-training/>). [UN DESA states Irembo is Rwanda's national online service portal, born of a partnership between RwandaOnline Platform Ltd and the Government of Rwanda, in operation since 2015](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>). [The Rwanda Governance Scorecard of October 2026 put satisfaction with Irembo at 81.8%, down 5.6 points](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>).
