# Rwanda

## Government services online

**Services online.** [UN DESA says Irembo now gives access to more than 90 government services online](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>). It calls it the national portal, run with RwandaOnline since 2015. [DIAL says IremboGov is a one-stop portal for over 240 public services](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>). In October 2026 KT Press said [citizens apply for documents and pay fees on Irembo, and 12 land services are online](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>). [A Karongi father renewed his family's health insurance on Irembo from his phone](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/09/empowering-parents-in-rwanda-through-digital-literacy-training/>), the Internet Society says. [The October 2026 Governance Scorecard put satisfaction with Irembo at 81.8%](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>), down 5.6 points.
