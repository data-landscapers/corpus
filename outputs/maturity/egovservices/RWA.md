# Rwanda

## Government services online

**Services online.** In May 2025 DIAL reported that [IremboGov is a one-stop portal for over 240 public services](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>). UN DESA [put the count at more than 90 in 2025](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>). Ministry data cited by the Governance Scorecard show that [only 11% of government services were fully digitised in 2024](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>). In October 2026 KT Press reported that [12 land services are online](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>).

**Single gateway.** [Irembo is Rwanda's national online service portal, in operation since 2015](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>), built by RwandaOnline with the government. No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through Irembo has been found. The nearest figure: [satisfaction with Irembo was 81.80% in October 2026](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>).
