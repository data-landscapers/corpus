# Rwanda

## Government services online

**Services online.** In November 2025 ICT minister Paula Ingabire told the Chamber of Deputies that [Irembo offers 240 government services](<https://rwandadispatch.com/irembo-platform-transforms-access-to-public-services/>). A national ID deck of May 2026 [puts them at over 247](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>). Ministry data cited by the Governance Scorecard show that [only 11% of government services were fully digitised in 2024](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>). In October 2026 KT Press reported that [12 land services are online](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>).

**Single gateway.** [Irembo is Rwanda's national online service portal, in operation since 2015](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/good-practices-for-digital-government/compendium/irembo-rwandan-government-e-service-portal>), built by RwandaOnline with the government. No source found says how users sign in to it.

**Use published.** The ICT ministry says [citizens submitted over 5 million applications through Irembo in 2023 and 2024](<https://rwandadispatch.com/irembo-platform-transforms-access-to-public-services/>). More than 400,000 people had opened personal accounts by March 2025. [Satisfaction with Irembo was 81.80% in October 2026](<https://rwandadispatch.com/rwandans-report-lower-satisfaction-with-public-services-new-scorecard-shows/>).
