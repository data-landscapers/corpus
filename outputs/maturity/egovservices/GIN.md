# Guinea

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 7 May 2026 the minister [launched the deployment of the national e-Services platform](<https://ande.gov.gn/rencontre-du-ministre-avec-lequipe-e-services-vers-le-lancement-de-e-services-une-solution-innovante-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>), built inside the digital agency ANDE. Several digitised public services are targeted by the end of its first phase. In October 2026 the livestock ministry [launched Ngaynaako, a portal for livestock requests](<https://laguinee.info/2026/10/22/guinee-le-ministere-de-lelevage-lance-ngaynaako-son-guichet-unique-numerique/>). On 26 October 2026 the fisheries ministry [launched Konkoe, where fishers apply for licences and track their files](<https://guineenews.org/2026/10/26/peche-la-guinee-lance-le-sigpem-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives/>).
