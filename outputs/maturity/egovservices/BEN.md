# Benin

## Government services online

**Services online.** In November 2026 [service-public.bj listed 1,208 services, 131 of them available online](<https://www.nasuba.info/politique-societe/benin-comment-fonctionne-le-portail-numerique-des-services-publics/>), by its own count. [Cybernetica says over 50 government institutions offered around 270 services through the national portal and gateway in February 2025](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/cybernetica-and-benin-continue-enhancing-the-country-s-digitalisation/>). Citizens [retrieve birth, marriage and nationality certificates online through eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ app](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). Since 1 November 2026 [a criminal record certificate is issued at once on the portal](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/digitization-in-benin-criminal-records-now-issued-instantly-and-without-supporting-documents/>), for 1,900 CFA francs.

**Single gateway.** [Users of service-public.bj create an account or sign in to a personal space](<https://www.nasuba.info/politique-societe/benin-comment-fonctionne-le-portail-numerique-des-services-publics/>). They submit and track requests from it. [Over 50 institutions offer services through the portal](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/cybernetica-and-benin-continue-enhancing-the-country-s-digitalisation/>), Cybernetica said in February 2025.

**Use published.** In November 2024 the digital minister gave [counts of requests and payments made through service-public.bj](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20241123-e-services-publics-au-benin-ces-chiffres-qui-temoignent-d-une-vision-salutaire-et-juste>). They included over 1 million water bill payments and over 500,000 criminal record requests. In November 2026 [the portal showed more than 2.5 million requests](<https://www.nasuba.info/politique-societe/benin-comment-fonctionne-le-portail-numerique-des-services-publics/>).
