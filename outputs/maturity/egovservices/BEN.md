# Benin

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Fraternite reports over 1,000 services digitised in Benin since 2016, more than 210 of them fully paperless, through the official portal service-public.bj](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). [The ANIP playbook lists eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ mobile app as access channels to online services, alongside USSD *151# and in-person](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Fraternite reports citizens can retrieve birth, marriage and nationality certificates online through eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ mobile app](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). [ANIP data released 26 January 2026 say identification procedures were handled online in 2025, 37% through eservices.anip.bj, 30% through the ANIP BJ mobile app and 8% by USSD](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/benin-advances-e-government-with-mass-digital-id-adoption>).

**Single gateway.** No source held since October 2023 says one national portal carries several ministries' services with shared sign-in. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>).

**Use published.** No source held since October 2023 gives users, applications or transactions through the national portal. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>).
