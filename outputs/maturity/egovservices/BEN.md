# Benin

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Fraternite reports over 1,000 services digitised since 2016, more than 210 of them fully paperless](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>), through the portal service-public.bj. [Cybernetica says over 50 government institutions offered around 270 services through the national portal and gateway in February 2025](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/cybernetica-and-benin-continue-enhancing-the-country-s-digitalisation/>). Citizens [retrieve birth, marriage and nationality certificates online through eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ app](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). [ANIP says its portal, app and USSD menus issued over 10 million documents in 2025, 75% of them online](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). The Cotonou Commercial Court [took applications and fees for three services online only from 16 February 2026](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/02/13/benin-trois-nouveaux-e-services-desormais-operationnels-au-tribunal-de-commerce-de-cotonou/>).
