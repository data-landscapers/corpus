# Benin

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Fraternite reports over 1,000 services digitised in Benin since 2016, more than 210 of them fully paperless, through the official portal service-public.bj](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). [The ANIP playbook lists eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ mobile app as access channels to online services, alongside USSD *151# and in-person](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [La Marina BJ reports the Cotonou Commercial Court made three services (writ-of-order requests, opposition to payment order, enforcement formula) available online, with applications and fees online only from 16 February 2026](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/02/13/benin-trois-nouveaux-e-services-desormais-operationnels-au-tribunal-de-commerce-de-cotonou/>). [Fraternite reports citizens can retrieve birth, marriage and nationality certificates online through eservices.anip.bj and the ANIP BJ mobile app](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>). [ANIP says its portal, app and USSD menus issued over 10 million documents in 2025, 75% of them online](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Single gateway.** No source found since October 2023 says one national portal carries several ministries' services with shared sign-in. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>).
