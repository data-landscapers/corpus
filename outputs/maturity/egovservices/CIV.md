# Cote d'Ivoire

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Applications for nationality certificates can be made online on the e-Justice platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/ivory-coast-strengthens-e-services-with-digital-nationality-certificate>). A June 2025 decree to digitise the certificate builds on it. In May 2025 the trade ministry said the GUCE-CI single window [lets businesses complete import, export and transit formalities online](<https://www.commerce.gouv.ci/actualite/133>). Users follow their files in real time on its website and on the GuceMobil app. On 10 November 2026 the tourism ministry [opened a digital one-stop shop for tourism operators](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/127482-administration-touristique-les-operateurs-pourront-desormais-effectuer-leurs-demarches-en-ligne>). They file procedures, pay and track applications online, first in four regional offices.
