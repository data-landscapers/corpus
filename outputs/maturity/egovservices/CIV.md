# Cote d'Ivoire

## Government services online

**Services online.** SNDI, the state IT company, said [315 administrative procedures had been digitised and put online by 31 December 2025](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>), on the national public services portal. [Applications for nationality certificates can be made online on the e-Justice platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/ivory-coast-strengthens-e-services-with-digital-nationality-certificate>). A June 2025 decree to digitise the certificate builds on it. In May 2025 the trade ministry said the GUCE-CI single window [lets businesses complete import, export and transit formalities online](<https://www.commerce.gouv.ci/actualite/133>). Users follow their files in real time on its website and on the GuceMobil app. On 10 November 2026 the tourism ministry [opened a digital one-stop shop for tourism operators](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/127482-administration-touristique-les-operateurs-pourront-desormais-effectuer-leurs-demarches-en-ligne>). They file procedures, pay and track applications online, first in four regional offices.

**Single gateway.** No source found says how users sign in to the national services portal, by the national ID or one shared account. The nearest fact: [SNDI runs the portal that carries the 315 procedures](<https://ivoirexpress.ci/2026/07/28/digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-cote-divoire-les-applications-metiers-developpees-par-la-sndi/>). [The GUCE-CI single window serves traders' import, export and transit formalities](<https://www.commerce.gouv.ci/actualite/133>) for the trade ministry.
