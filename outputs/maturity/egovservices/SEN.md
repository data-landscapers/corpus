# Senegal

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 24 March 2026 Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko [launched e-Senegal, a one-stop portal](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/senegal-launches-digital-government-portal-to-ease-access-to-public-services>). The presidency says citizens complete passport applications, business creation, police clearance and nationality certificates on it. In August 2025 Polygon reported that [the Service Public portal lists nearly 700 procedures, with information and forms](<https://www.polygon.sn/anta-lia-au-service-du-citoyen/>). In October 2026 the interior ministry [asked NGOs and associations to register on the new e-OSC platform](<https://aps.sn/les-ong-et-associations-invitees-a-sinscrire-sur-la-plateforme-numerique-e-osc/>). The justice ministry's [e-service portal, linked to e-Senegal, issues criminal-record extracts and nationality certificates](<https://senego.com/digitalisation-de-la-justice-des-services-desormais-accessibles-en-ligne-pour-les-citoyens_1941148.html>) in its pilot phase.

**Single gateway.** At the March 2026 launch the Prime Minister [presented a single citizen window for applying for and following public services](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>). No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or applications through e-Senegal has been found. The nearest fact: [the presidency calls e-Senegal operational](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/senegal-launches-digital-government-portal-to-ease-access-to-public-services>).
