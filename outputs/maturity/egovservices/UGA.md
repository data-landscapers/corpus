# Uganda

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 12 August 2026 NITA-U [launched the U-Learn platform](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/12/uganda-launches-two-new-e-services-to-digitise-teacher-training-human-rights-cases/>), one of 14 e-services being rolled out under UDAP-GovNet. Over 6,600 teachers have enrolled. The Judiciary [has rolled its case system out to 42 court stations](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>), where cases are filed and paid for electronically. Uganda also [has sector e-services in revenue, company registration and procurement](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>).
