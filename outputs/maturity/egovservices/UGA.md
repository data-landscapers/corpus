# Uganda

## Government services online

**Services online.** In November 2026 the registration bureau [launched its URSB Connect app](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/11/ursb-launches-mobile-app-for-business-registration-services.html>). It offers company and business-name registration, name searches, annual returns, trademarks and the chattels registry. On 12 August 2026 NITA-U [launched the U-Learn platform](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/12/uganda-launches-two-new-e-services-to-digitise-teacher-training-human-rights-cases/>), one of 14 e-services being rolled out under UDAP-GovNet. The Judiciary [has rolled its case system out to 42 court stations](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>), where cases are filed and paid for electronically. Uganda also [has sector e-services in revenue, company registration and procurement](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>). NITA-U [lists six government services online](<https://nita.go.ug/projects/national-backbone-infrastructure-project-nbiegi>), among them E-Visa, E-Tax, the voters register and the investment one-stop centre.
