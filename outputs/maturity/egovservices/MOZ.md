# Mozambique

## Government services online

**Services online.** In September 2026 Biometric Update reported that [the Citizen Portal, before its formal launch, provides about 26 services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). They include [driving licences, tax-number services and one-stop centre services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). The digital agency ATDI [presented the portal at FACIM 2026](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/atdi-ip_atdi-apresenta-portal-do-cidad%C3%A3o-e-coloca-activity-7501551667520368640-SmSp>), where citizens submit applications, track cases and obtain documents remotely. The World Bank reported in March 2026 that [the portal's earlier version, with 25 information services and one transactional service, was discontinued](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>). In October 2026 ATDI and VFS Global [opened Centro Pronto](<https://bnews.co.mz/artigo/governo-digitaliza-servi-os-de-identifica-o-e-reduz-burocracia>) for passport, ID card and licence applications.

**Single gateway.** No source found says how users sign in to the Citizen Portal. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in. The nearest fact: [it carries services of the transport authority, the tax authority and the one-stop centres](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).

**Use published.** No count of users or applications through the Citizen Portal has been found. The nearest fact: [it was still before its formal launch in September 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).
