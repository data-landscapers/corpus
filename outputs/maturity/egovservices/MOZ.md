# Mozambique

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Biometric Update reports the pre-launch Citizen Portal provides about 26 services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). [MZNews says ATDI officially presented the Portal do Cidadão at FACIM 2026, where citizens submit applications, track case status and obtain official documents remotely](<https://mznews.co.mz/atdi-lanca-portal-do-cidadao-na-facim-2026-para-digitalizar-mais-de-140-servicos-publicos-em-mocambique/>). [ATDI says ATDI officially presented the Portal do Cidadão at FACIM 2026, where citizens submit applications, track case status and obtain official documents remotely](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/atdi-ip_atdi-apresenta-portal-do-cidad%C3%A3o-e-coloca-activity-7501551667520368640-SmSp>). [Biometric Update reports the Citizen Portal is in a pre-launch phase and already provides services including INATRO driving licences, NUIT tax services and One-Stop Service Centre (BAU) services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). [In October 2026 ATDI and VFS Global opened the Centro Pronto in Maputo, whose portal and kiosks are to let citizens prepare passport, ID card, driving licence and residence permit applications, pay electronically and book appointments, with services added in phases, BNEWS reports](<https://bnews.co.mz/artigo/governo-digitaliza-servi-os-de-identifica-o-e-reduz-burocracia>).
