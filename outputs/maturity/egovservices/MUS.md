# Mauritius

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority offers simplified VAT registration as an online application on its portal, accessed with the National Identity Card, Business Registration Number or Tax Account Number and a password](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/vat-eservices/simplified-vat-registration>). [The Corporate and Business Registration Department portal offers online business registration and incorporation, annual return filing, trade fee payment by companies and individuals, and login applications](<https://companies.govmu.org/Pages/default.aspx>). [The OECD OPSI case says MauPass was launched in December 2020 and that e-services such as payment of Motor Vehicle Licence online, application for a learner's driving licence, and MoKloud digital safe access use it (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>). [In August 2025 an online driving licence system had recently been launched, with more e-services to follow](<https://maurice-info.mu/2025/08/14/maupass-au-service-de-linclusion-numerique.html>).

**Single gateway.** [The OECD OPSI case says MauPass is a single window of authentication for government e-services of several ministries, with one account and user data validated against the Central Population Database including the ID number (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>).

**Use published.** [Government Online Centre statistics, cited in the OECD OPSI case, show hits on the MoKloud digital safe rising from 2,067 in January 2022 to 12,172 in September 2022 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>).
