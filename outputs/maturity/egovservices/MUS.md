# Mauritius

## Government services online

**Services online.** In May 2026 the ICT minister told parliament that [a 2023-24 audit counted 123 online government services](<https://lexpress.mu/s/application-korek-462-310-enregistrements-depuis-janvier-2026-557967>), fewer than ten of them used. The Mauritius Revenue Authority [offers VAT registration as an online application](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/vat-eservices/simplified-vat-registration>). On 17 August 2026 [taxi operators could renew licences and pay road tax online](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/online-motor-vehicle-licence-system-launched-for-taxi-operators/>). The Registrar General's Department is [testing an extended online search of the land registry](<https://registrar.govmu.org/rgd/index.php/upcoming-projects/>), paid by e-voucher.

**Single gateway.** The Korek app, launched on 30 January 2026, [gathers government services in one place](<https://lexpress.mu/s/application-korek-462-310-enregistrements-depuis-janvier-2026-557967>). They include penalty points, driving licence details and a certificate of character. In October 2026 the Cabinet noted that [a new Unified Government Web Portal was still being developed](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20-%20Friday%2030%20October%202026.pdf>).

**Use published.** No source since October 2023 publishes use of a national portal. In May 2026 the ICT minister said [Korek had 462,310 registered and 413,409 active users](<https://lexpress.mu/s/application-korek-462-310-enregistrements-depuis-janvier-2026-557967>). In October 2026 he put registrations at [nearly 500,000, with 250,000 identity-checked](<https://topfm.mu/actualites/services-publics-numeriques-pres-dun-demi-million-de-mauriciens-desormais-enregistres-sur>). By June 2025 [300,000 people had registered on the MauPass sign-in](<https://topfm.mu/au-1er-juin-2025-300-000-personnes-se-sont-enregistrees-sur-maupass-le-service-en-ligne-des>).
