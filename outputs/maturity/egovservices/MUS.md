# Mauritius

## Government services online

**Services online.** The Mauritius Revenue Authority [offers VAT registration as an online application](<https://www.mra.mu/index.php/eservices1/vat-eservices/simplified-vat-registration>), signed in with the national identity card or a tax number. The companies registry [offers online business registration, annual returns and trade fee payment](<https://companies.govmu.org/Pages/default.aspx>). In August 2025 [an online driving licence system was launched](<https://maurice-info.mu/2025/08/14/maupass-au-service-de-linclusion-numerique.html>). The Cabinet expected [online payment of fines through the Supreme Court website from 30 October 2026](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20-%20Friday%2030%20October%202026.pdf>).

**Single gateway.** In October 2026 the Cabinet noted that [a new Unified Government Web Portal was still being developed](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20-%20Friday%2030%20October%202026.pdf>). MauPass was launched in December 2020 as [one sign-in for e-services of several ministries](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>). No source since October 2023 describes it.

**Use published.** The latest use figure found dates from 2022. [Visits to the MoKloud digital safe rose to 12,172 in September 2022](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>).
