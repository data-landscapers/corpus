# Togo

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Togo First reports that the ministry's two procedures, five services of state broadcaster TVT and five of Radio Lomé, twelve in all, became available through the national public services portal from early the week of 28 September 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/2909-20205-togo-adds-more-administrative-procedures-to-online-public-services-portal>). [Togo's justice ministry launched on 2 July 2026 an online portal through which litigants, lawyers and bailiffs pay fees for nearly forty services of the Tribunal de Grande Instance de Lomé](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>). [The CNSS reports that its online registration portal, open since 2018, lets employees and retirees, and since October 2025 non-salaried workers, affiliate and manage their rights online](<https://cnss.tg/linam-emboite-le-pas-a-la-cnss/>).

**Single gateway.** [The national identification agency's 2026 deck describes service-public.gouv.tg as a fully integrated one-stop portal for public services, powered by the e-ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>), with sign-in by the national identification number or an email address and a one-time password sent to the user's registered phone.
