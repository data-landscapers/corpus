## Government services online

**Services online.** [Togo First reports that the ministry's two procedures, five services of state broadcaster TVT and five of Radio Lomé, twelve in all, became available through the national public services portal from early the week of 28 September 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/2909-20205-togo-adds-more-administrative-procedures-to-online-public-services-portal>). [Togo First reports that the Ministry of Territorial Planning and Urban Development put two procedures, architect certification on a building permit application and application for and issuance of building permits, online on the national public services portal from early the week of 28 September 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/2909-20205-togo-adds-more-administrative-procedures-to-online-public-services-portal>).
