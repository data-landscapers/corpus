# Togo

## Government services online

**Services online.** In December 2025 [the national portal service-public.gouv.tg offered 101 online public services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/togo-adds-three-new-digital-administrative-procedures-to-national-e-service-platform>), among them passports, residence permits and nationality certificates. In September 2025 Togo First [counted 98 services across commerce, security, energy, civil status, transport and education](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/gouvernance-economique/0509-17029-togo-une-centaine-de-services-desormais-disponibles-sur-la-plateforme-services-publics>). From late September 2026 [twelve more services were added](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/public-services/2909-20205-togo-adds-more-administrative-procedures-to-online-public-services-portal>). On 2 July 2026 the justice ministry [opened payment online for nearly forty court services in Lomé](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>).

**Single gateway.** The national identification agency's 2026 deck describes service-public.gouv.tg as [one integrated portal for public services, powered by the e-ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). Users sign in with the national identification number or an email address. A one-time password is sent to their registered phone.

**Use published.** No source found since October 2023 gives users, applications or transactions through the national portal.
