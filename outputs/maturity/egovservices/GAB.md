# Gabon

## Government services online

**Services online.** In November 2023 the immigration directorate DGDI [opened an e-services portal](<https://techies.ga/article/la-dgdi-lance-un-portail-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives>) for renewing passports, ID cards and residence permits. Since July 2024 a holder of a valid passport [can apply online for the national electronic ID card](<https://techies.ga/article/identite-numerique-quand-pourra-t-on-faire-une-demarche-sans-redonner-ses-papiers>). In October 2025 the minister said [companies have been created online at ANPI for four or five years](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>). The Gabon Connect platform [was set up in March 2026 to put tax, customs, land and social security procedures online](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-connect-le-ministere-de-leconomie-sollicite-lexpertise-de-lapdpvp/>). On 5 November 2026 the civil-service pension fund CPPF [began collecting school certificates online for its school allowance](<https://www.gabonreview.com/allocation-de-rentree-scolaire-2027-2028-la-cppf-accelere-sa-revolution-numerique/>), through its BAANA platform.

**Single gateway.** On 22 October 2026 the vice-president of the government [presented Gabon Connect as a single digital window](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-connect-tresorpay-dus-le-gabon-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-mieux-encaisser/>). Its first phase is due to finish in December 2027. The Digital Gabon loan, amended in September 2026, [finances a one-stop shop for public e-services for people and businesses](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>).

**Use published.** No count of users of a national portal has been found. The nearest figure: [close to 50,000 companies have been created online at ANPI](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>).
