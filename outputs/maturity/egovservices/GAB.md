# Gabon

## Government services online

**Services online.** In November 2023 the immigration directorate DGDI [opened an e-services portal](<https://techies.ga/article/la-dgdi-lance-un-portail-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives>) for renewing passports, ID cards and residence permits. Since July 2024 a holder of a valid passport [can apply online for the national electronic ID card](<https://techies.ga/article/identite-numerique-quand-pourra-t-on-faire-une-demarche-sans-redonner-ses-papiers>). In October 2025 the minister said [companies have been created online at ANPI for four or five years](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>). The Gabon Connect platform [was set up in March 2026 to put tax, customs, land and social security procedures online](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-connect-le-ministere-de-leconomie-sollicite-lexpertise-de-lapdpvp/>).

**Single gateway.** On 22 October 2026 the vice-president of the government [presented Gabon Connect as a single digital window](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-connect-tresorpay-dus-le-gabon-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-mieux-encaisser/>). Its first phase is due to finish in December 2027.

**Use published.** No count of users of a national portal has been found. The nearest figure: [close to 50,000 companies have been created online at ANPI](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>).
