# Gabon

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Minister Doumba says companies have been created online at ANPI for 4 or 5 years, with close to 50,000 companies created, and that CNSS and CNAMGS declarations are expected to follow online](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>). [Techies reports Gabon's DGDI inaugurated an e-services portal where citizens can renew passports, national ID cards and residence permits online (three services named)](<https://techies.ga/article/la-dgdi-lance-un-portail-pour-simplifier-les-demarches-administratives>). [Since July 2024 a citizen holding a valid passport can apply online for the national ID card on the DGDI site using their personal identification number, Techies reports](<https://techies.ga/article/identite-numerique-quand-pourra-t-on-faire-une-demarche-sans-redonner-ses-papiers>). [On 22 October 2026 the government presented Gabon Connect, a single digital window whose first portals, for salary declarations and TrésorPay, are deployed in a phase due to end in December 2027](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-connect-tresorpay-dus-le-gabon-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-mieux-encaisser/>).
