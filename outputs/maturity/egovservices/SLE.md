# Sierra Leone

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 10 November 2026 the President launched the National Investment Board's [online business registration system, which puts 66 of the board's services online](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1111-60716-sierra-leone-digitizes-business-registration-as-75-of-firms-remain-unregistered>). Applicants submit, upload documents, track their status and obtain certificates online. [Complete applications are to be processed within 24 hours](<https://www.sierraleonemonitor.com/bio-launches-online-business-registration/>). In February 2025 the communications ministry [launched the WanGov app](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-launches-wangov-app/>). Users apply for services, submit documents, track applications and pay. Business registration, tax filing, permits, driving licences and electricity meters are named. Since 30 July 2026 [all police clearance applications go through WanGov](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-police-clearance-applications/>).

**Single gateway.** In June 2025 the minister said the ministry [had created the One Government Platform, a one-stop portal for all government services and information](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/2025/06/04/sierra-leones-president-bio-champions-technological-innovation-at-the-2025-digital-innovation-summit/>). A 2024 government presentation [listed a central government service portal among its e-government work](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2024/presentation%204%29%20UN-DESA-ADGF-Pretoria-2024.pdf>). No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or payments through WanGov has been found. The nearest fact: [WanGov takes payment for services at the border post](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-digital-platform-border-trade-guinea/>).
