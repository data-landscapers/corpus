# Cameroon

## Government services online

**Services online.** In August 2026 the tax directorate launched [registrations.dgi.cm for registering deeds online](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/enregistrement-actes-en-ligne-dgi-cameroun-registrations-guide>). Three public procurement acts could be registered and paid for by mobile money. More than 120 other acts in its catalogue, among them property sales, leases and company statutes, were shown as unavailable. Processing was announced at three days after payment.

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries with one sign-in. The nearest held fact: [the deed registration platform belongs to the tax directorate alone](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/enregistrement-actes-en-ligne-dgi-cameroun-registrations-guide>).

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions through a national portal has been found. The nearest held fact: in August 2023 the finance ministry [unveiled Tresor Pay for paying non-tax public services online](<https://cameroonnewsagency.com/government-modernizes-revenue-collection-system-with-the-introduction-of-tresor-pay-platform/>), by mobile money, post office or card. No figure for its use has been found.
