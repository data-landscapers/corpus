# Zambia

## Government services online

**Services online.** In September 2024 ZamPortal's director, Beaton Sibulowa, said [it hosts 322 services that citizens can access and apply for online](<https://zamportal.gov.zm/director-sibulowa-introduces-the-gsb/>). According to the director, citizens can both reach and apply for these services online through the one portal. ZamPortal runs on the Government Service Bus.

**Single gateway.** [ZamPortal is the government platform on the Government Service Bus](<https://zamportal.gov.zm/director-sibulowa-introduces-the-gsb/>), through which citizens reach services of many bodies. No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** In September 2024 the director said [ZamPortal had recorded over 3.7 million transactions and 5 billion Kwacha since its start](<https://zamportal.gov.zm/director-sibulowa-introduces-the-gsb/>). No later figure for users or transactions through ZamPortal has been found.
