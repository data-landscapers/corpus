# Morocco

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The ministry says the Idarati portal gives access to 874 online services, organised around priority user journeys, and 2,533 published administrative acts](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>). [The relaunched Idarati portal gives access to 874 public e-services (the article elsewhere says 784), up from 605 at the end of 2023 per the Court of Audit, according to analyst Slimane El Omrani](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-1814104.html>). [The Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform launched the new version of the national portal of administrative procedures Idarati on 18 September 2026, per analyst Slimane El Omrani](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-1814104.html>). [The US Country Commercial Guide says Morocco has 300+ citizen and 200+ business digital services](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy>).
