# Morocco

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 18 September 2026 the digital transition ministry [relaunched the national Idarati portal](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>). The ministry says [Idarati gives access to 874 online services and 2,533 published administrative acts](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>). That is [up from 605 at the end of 2023, as counted by the Court of Audit](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-1814104.html>). In June 2026 the ministry said [its national reference listed more than 600 services online](<https://al3omk.com/1166931.html>). From 9 November 2026 [Moroccans abroad can apply online for criminal record certificates](<https://en.hespress.com/148811-moroccans-abroad-can-now-apply-online-for-criminal-record-certificates.html>) through the police's Epolice portal.

**Single gateway.** The ministry [presents Idarati e-Services as one access point to the administrations' digital services](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>). No source found says how users sign in. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or requests through Idarati has been found. The nearest fact: [874 services were reachable through it in September 2026](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>).
