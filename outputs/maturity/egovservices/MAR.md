# Morocco

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The digital transition ministry relaunched the national Idarati portal on 18 September 2026](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-1814104.html>). [It says the portal gives access to 874 online services and 2,533 published administrative acts](<https://fr.assahafa.com/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-idarati-pour-une-administration-plus-proche-et-plus-intelligente/>). [The Court of Audit counted 605 online services at the end of 2023, analyst Slimane El Omrani notes](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-1814104.html>). [The US Country Commercial Guide counted over 300 citizen and 200 business services in 2025](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/morocco-digital-economy>). [In April 2024 the education ministry's Massar system let parents apply online for a first school place](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>).
