# Chad

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 3 December 2024 the finance minister [launched e-Notaire, an online platform for notarial services](<https://portail.td/lancement-de-notaire-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-moderniser-les-services-notariaux-au-tchad/>), with demonstrations. On 11 December 2024 the minister [launched the e-Visa.td portal, putting visa applications online](<https://libertechnologies.lu/lancement-officiel-de-e-visa-au-tchad/>), the vendor Liber Technologies says. No other government service in Chad has been found online since October 2023.

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries. None says one account signs users in to them. The nearest fact: [e-Notaire is a finance ministry platform for notarial services](<https://portail.td/lancement-de-notaire-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-moderniser-les-services-notariaux-au-tchad/>). No source found says how users sign in to e-Notaire or to e-Visa.td.

**Use published.** [No usage figures were given at the e-Notaire launch](<https://portail.td/lancement-de-notaire-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-moderniser-les-services-notariaux-au-tchad/>). No count of users of the e-Visa portal, or of any national portal, has been found either.
