# Ghana

## Government services online

**Services online.** The Interior Ministry [opened an online services portal, fully working from 15 December 2025](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-interior-ministry-launches-online-digital-services-portal.html>). It takes applications for immigrant quota permits, citizenship renunciation, dual nationality, naturalisation and licences. Applicants track their files and pay online. In 2025 DIAL reported that Ghana.gov is [where users find and pay for government services, and that most of a licence or passport renewal is done online](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>). In October 2026 the Lands Commission's portal [let buyers pay for a land search and receive the report by email](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/10/lands-commission-outlines-four-steps-for-conducting-online-land-searches/>). It was live in 10 of 16 regions.

**Single gateway.** The digital economy policy [describes Ghana.gov as the portal that centralises access to government services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>), such as tax filing, business registration and passports. No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or payments through Ghana.gov has been found. The nearest fact: [an e-Payment portal takes payment for government services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>).
