# Ghana

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The Ministry of the Interior launched an online digital services portal, fully operational from 15 December 2025, hosting applications for immigrant quota permits, citizenship renunciation, dual nationality, naturalisation and licensing, with tracking and automated payments, as the Interior Minister said](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-interior-ministry-launches-online-digital-services-portal.html>). [The policy states the Ghana.gov portal centralizes access to government services such as tax filings, business registrations and passport applications, and an e-Payment portal supports payment for government services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ghana-Digital-Economy-Policy-Strategy.pdf>).
