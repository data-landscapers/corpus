# Eswatini

## Government services online

**Services online.** In May 2026 a Digital Landscape Assessment Report [found 31 of 419 government services available online](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). The other 388 remain paper-based and manual. On 23 October 2026 the ICT ministry said [13 online services had been restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>) after a data centre incident. In November 2026 the home affairs ministry said [eVisa issuance was ready](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/home-affairs-goes-digital-to-cut-costs/>) as it moves passports online. Online company registration had [632 applicants in the quarter to September 2026](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/digital-company-registrations-plunge-70/>), against 2,127 a year earlier.

**Single gateway.** The ICT ministry launched [the Government in Your Hand app in May 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>). Users register with the national ID and a one-time code. It carries ID and passport status checks, business registration, education loans and utility payments.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions has been found. The nearest fact: [the app had over 2,000 downloads in its first month](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>), a ministry engineer said.
