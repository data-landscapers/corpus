# Eswatini

## Government services online

**Services online.** [The Digital Landscape Assessment Report, as reported, found only 31 of 419 government services currently available online, the rest remaining paper-based and manual](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). [The Times reports that a National Data Centre incident disrupted at least 12 government digital platforms including the Government in Your Hand application, e-Visa, company registration and the Elderly Grant Application and Payment system, with company registration online services affected per the ministry](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>). [On 23 October 2026 the ICT ministry said 13 online services, including Government in Your Hand and e-Visa, had been restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>).

**Single gateway.** No source found since October 2023 says one national portal carries several ministries' services with shared sign-in. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>).
