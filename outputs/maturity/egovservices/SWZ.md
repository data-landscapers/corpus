# Eswatini

## Government services online

**Services online.** In May 2026 a Digital Landscape Assessment Report [found 31 of 419 government services available online](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). The other 388 remain paper-based and manual. In September 2026 [a National Data Centre incident disrupted at least 12 government platforms](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>), among them e-Visa and company registration. On 23 October 2026 the ICT ministry said [13 online services had been restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>).

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries with one sign-in. The nearest fact: [the Government in Your Hand platform is among the 13 services restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>). No source found says how users sign in to it.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions has been found. The nearest fact: [the elderly grant application and payment system was among the platforms disrupted](<https://times.co.sz/44621/news/national-data-centre-hit-by-incident/>).
