# Eswatini

## Government services online

**Services online.** In May 2026 a Digital Landscape Assessment Report [found 31 of 419 government services available online](<https://independentnews.co.sz/43652/news/critical-state-information-at-risk-in-9-govt-ministries/>). The other 388 remain paper-based and manual. In the first quarter of 2026/27 [six payment services were added to the Government in Your Hand app](<https://swazidailynews.com/9096/business/economy/ict-ministry-records-strong-start-as-digital-rollout-gains-momentum/>). On 23 October 2026 the ICT ministry said [13 online services had been restored](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/govt-restores-13-online-services-website-still-down/>) after a data centre incident.

**Single gateway.** The ICT ministry launched [the Government in Your Hand app in May 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>). Users register with the national ID and a one-time code. It carries ID and passport status checks, business registration, education loans and utility payments.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions has been found. The nearest fact: [the app had over 2,000 downloads in its first month](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>), a ministry engineer said.
