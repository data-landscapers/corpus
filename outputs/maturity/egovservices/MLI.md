# Mali

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 19 July 2024 the President [launched the digitalisation of public services](<https://bamada.net/nouvelles-technologies-le-president-de-la-transition-lance-la-digitalisation-des-moyens-de-paiement-et-des-services-publics-de-letat>). ID card fees, police fines and civil-status acts can be paid by mobile phone, first in Bamako. On 10 February 2026 two portals [opened for criminal record and nationality certificate requests](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/mali-launches-online-criminal-record-and-nationality-certificate-services>), with applications tracked online. In May 2025 the Prime Minister said [the land information system would offer land procedures online from July](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-quatre-nouvelles-plateformes-accelerent-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration/>), with a pilot in Bamako.

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries. The nearest fact: [mobile payment of several services began in Bamako in July 2024](<https://bamada.net/nouvelles-technologies-le-president-de-la-transition-lance-la-digitalisation-des-moyens-de-paiement-et-des-services-publics-de-letat>).

**Use published.** No count of payments or applications has been found. The nearest fact: [the land system's pilot was planned for Bamako's seven arrondissements](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-quatre-nouvelles-plateformes-accelerent-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration/>).
