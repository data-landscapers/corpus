# Mali

## Government services online

**Services online.** [APAnews reports the Prime Minister said on 27 May 2025 that the land information system SIF/GUF, being finalised, would from July offer fully dematerialised land procedures including registration, transfers, mortgages and online payments, with a pilot planned in Bamako's seven arrondissements](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/mali-quatre-nouvelles-plateformes-accelerent-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration/>). [The Bamada.net report says that on 19 July 2024 the President launched digitalisation of public services in Mali, so that ID card fees, police fines and civil-status acts can be paid by mobile phone, first in Bamako, with extension to the regions and embassies and later to taxes and public procurement](<https://bamada.net/nouvelles-technologies-le-president-de-la-transition-lance-la-digitalisation-des-moyens-de-paiement-et-des-services-publics-de-letat>).
