# South Sudan

## Government services online

**Services online.** [One Citizen Daily reports the launch of the National Communication Authority e-Services Platform, on which licensing and payments that once required office visits can now be completed online](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/13/south-sudan-digitizes-regulation-to-strengthen-revenue-collection/>). [The Ministry of Justice's Business Registration Service portal lets users apply online to validate a business, submit the application and receive approval notification](<https://brs.eservices.gov.ss>). [The eServices portal offers online applications for permits and clearances from the revenue authorities, the Bureau of Standards accreditation permit, TIN registration on eTax and business registration, with payment online and permit delivered as PDF](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Single gateway.** [One eServices.gov.ss account, created with an ID for citizens and residents or a passport number for foreigners, gives access to all government services online including BRS, with fees payable by mobile money, cards and online banking](<https://brs.eservices.gov.ss>).
