# Lesotho

## Government services online

**Services online.** Revenue Services Lesotho [offers an online taxpayer registration form on its eServices site](<https://eservices.rsl.org.ls/ereg/public/individualform>). In June 2024 UNDP and the communications ministry [launched the E-Service Outlet Model](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/news/undp-launched-e-service-outlet-model>). Under it, internet and print shops and mobile money agents help people use government e-services, such as registering a business or obtaining a trader's licence. In May 2026 a minister said [an integrated online services platform with a payment gateway was close to launch](<https://lestimes.com/?p=90492>). Also in May 2026 the ministry [validated the first stage of automating e-services with Home Affairs, Public Service, Public Works and Finance](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-eservices-to-undergo-evolution-as-part-of-digital-transformation-agenda/>). Back-end integration is to follow.
