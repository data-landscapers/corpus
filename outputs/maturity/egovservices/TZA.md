## Government services online

**Services online.** [e-GA says citizens obtain various government services by phone through mGov, by dialling *152*00#](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yatoa-elimu-ya-serikali-mtandao-kwa-viziwi>). BRELA's Online Services system [had five of its seven modules running in October 2026](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/govt-launches-business-law-reforms-to-drive-investment-tech-innovation-2026-10-12-115945>), the deputy trade minister said. [Tanzania recruits through the Ajira Portal and buys through NeST, with GePG processing payments](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>), the Daily Monitor reports. [In October 2026 the education minister launched a national digital library system that takes member registration and ISBN and ISSN applications](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>).
