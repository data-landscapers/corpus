# Tanzania

## Government services online

**Services online.** [E-GA says citizens can obtain various government services through the mGov mobile system by dialling *152*00#, and a CHAVITA board member says it let Deaf people pay electricity and water bills by phone](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yatoa-elimu-ya-serikali-mtandao-kwa-viziwi>). [Deputy Minister for Industry and Trade Denis Londo commended BRELA for upgrading its Online Services (BOS) system, noting that five of its seven modules were operational, and directed that the remaining upgrades be completed on schedule](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/govt-launches-business-law-reforms-to-drive-investment-tech-innovation-2026-10-12-115945>). [The Daily Monitor reports Tanzania recruits through the Ajira Portal and runs digital procurement through NeST, alongside GePG payment processing](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>). [The education minister launched the Integrated National Digital Library System in October 2026, taking member registration, ISBN and ISSN applications, lending and billing on web and mobile](<https://thechanzo.com/2026/10/21/tanzania-launches-digital-library-system-as-mkenda-urges-board-to-prioritise-books-over-buildings/>).
