# Tanzania

## Government services online

**Services online.** e-GA says [citizens can obtain various government services through the mGov system by dialling *152*00#](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yatoa-elimu-ya-serikali-mtandao-kwa-viziwi>). On 8 April 2026 BRELA [put new business name and industrial licence applications online](<https://www.brela.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1775729096-TAARIFA%20KWA%20UMMA%20-%20MATUMIZI%20YA%20MFUMO%20ULIOBORESHWA%20%20WA%20UTOAJI%20HUDUMA.pdf>). The procurement authority's [NeST app lets users register, apply for tenders and track payments](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-shift-boosts-access-to-sh42-6-trillion-tender-market-5651820>). Public service recruitment [runs through the Ajira Portal](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>).

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries with one sign-in. The nearest fact: [mGov is linked to all mobile operators to deliver government services by phone](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). It gives no count of the services it carries.

**Use published.** No count of users or transactions has been found. The nearest fact: [five of BRELA's seven online modules were working in October 2026](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/govt-launches-business-law-reforms-to-drive-investment-tech-innovation-2026-10-12-115945>).
