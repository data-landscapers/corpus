# Comoros

## Government services online

**Services online.** In June 2024 the national digital agency ANADEN [held a technical meeting to present e-Bourse, a national portal for online scholarship applications](<https://anaden.org/realisations>). ANADEN lists the portal among its projects and marks it as in progress. Students are to apply for national scholarships through it. In June 2025 ANADEN's procurement notice [listed an upgraded government portal with e-services and digital payments](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/project-related-procurement/agpm_padec-comores_180625.pdf>), funded by the AfDB. No other government service in the Comoros has been found online since October 2023.

**Single gateway.** No source found says one national portal carries the services of several ministries. None says one account signs users in to them. The nearest fact: [e-Bourse, a single-purpose scholarship portal, was presented in June 2024](<https://anaden.org/realisations>).
