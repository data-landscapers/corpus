# Central African Republic

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 14 October 2026 the government [launched a national digital portal for public services](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republics-digital-public-services-face-connectivity-barriers/>), Africa Business Insight reports. On it people can apply for driving licences and urban transport passes. They can also request criminal record extracts and nationality certificates. School attendance and baccalaureate certificates can be obtained through it too.

**Single gateway.** The new portal [carries services of the transport, justice and education sectors](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republics-digital-public-services-face-connectivity-barriers/>). No source found says how users sign in to it. Neither the national ID nor one shared account is named as its sign-in.

**Use published.** No count of users or applications through the portal has been found. The nearest fact: [the portal opened on 14 October 2026](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/central-african-republics-digital-public-services-face-connectivity-barriers/>), with six services named at its launch.
