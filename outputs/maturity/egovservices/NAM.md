# Namibia

## Government services online

**Services online.** [New Era says that only 13 public institutions currently offer online services, and NDP6 targets 100% digitalisation of government services by 2029/30](<https://neweralive.na/namibia-targets-full-digital-transformation/>). [NBC reports Namibia's Ministry of Home Affairs launched online applications, payment and SMS tracking for passports and short-term work visas at eservices.mhaiss.gov.na](<https://www.nbcnews.na/node/100218>). [Prime Minister Saara Kuugongelwa-Amadhila told Parliament she had launched an online death-registration service under Home Affairs, and listed eleven other implemented online systems including online visa and permits, business registration and deeds registration, at various stages of implementation (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://neweralive.na/govt-moves-to-curb-bureaucracy/>).
