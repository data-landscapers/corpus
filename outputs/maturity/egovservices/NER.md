# Niger

## Government services online

**Services online.** In July 2025 the national agency ANSI [upgraded the Portail Public du Niger](<https://nigerdiaspora.net/politique/services-en-ligne-au-niger-le-portail-public-inaugure-une-nouvelle-ere-de-gouvernance-numerique>), adding administrative services online step by step. Several functions were already available, with more due in the following months. Numéricité says that [company creation and building permit applications feed NigerOnline, the national e-government portal](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>). Service-public.ne, [Niger's first public services portal, opened in January 2021](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>).

**Single gateway.** No source found says one portal carries the services of several ministries with one sign-in. The nearest fact: [NigerOnline, the national e-government portal, carries services from the EIG programme](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>), such as company creation.
