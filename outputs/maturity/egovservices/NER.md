# Niger

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Nigerdiaspora reports that ANSI, under the Presidency, evolved the Portail Public du Niger on 16 July 2025, integrating administrative services online gradually, with several functions already available and more to come in the following months](<https://nigerdiaspora.net/politique/services-en-ligne-au-niger-le-portail-public-inaugure-une-nouvelle-ere-de-gouvernance-numerique>). [Numéricité states that services from the EIG programme, such as company creation and building permit applications, feed NigerOnline, the national e-gov portal, today](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>). [Numéricité states that Service-public.ne, Niger's first public services portal, was launched on 28 January 2021 and that since then Nigeriens can carry out administrative procedures online (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://numericite.eu/realisations/definir-la-strategie-e-gov-et-accompagner-la-mise-en-oeuvre-des-services-publics-numeriques-du-niger/>).
