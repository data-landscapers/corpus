# Libya

## Government services online

**Services online.** On 3 March 2024 the interior ministry of the Government of National Unity [announced a package of government e-services](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/431469>), including the start of an e-government portal project. The Hawiyati portal was still to be launched. The Passports, Nationality and Foreigners Affairs Authority runs an electronic visa unit. It [received 773 e-visa applications in the week of 23 to 29 October 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368841&lang=ar>) and approved 610 of them.
