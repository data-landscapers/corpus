# Ethiopia

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Ethio Telecom launched the teleSign app on 30 March 2026 with the foreign and justice ministries and the documents authentication service, for applying online for document authentication, powers of attorney and professional licences, paid by telebirr](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8578538>). [The labour ministry put ten services on the Ethiopian e-Services portal in 2020, when the portal carried 36 government institutions](<https://addisinsight.net/2020/08/11/ministry-digitizes-services-joins-e-service-platform/>).

**Single gateway.** [National ID Ethiopia's May 2026 deck describes MESOB as a digital one-stop shop for e-government services built on the Fayda ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>), listed beside farmer and land records and community health insurance. [Prime Minister Abiy inaugurated the Unified MESOB Application, which ENA says brings 27 services from across institutions onto one platform](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>).

**Use published.** No source found gives users, applications or transactions through a national portal. The nearest held fact: [MESOB one-stop service had reached 70 centres within a year](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>).
