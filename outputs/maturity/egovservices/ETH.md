# Ethiopia

## Government services online

**Services online.** Ethio Telecom launched the teleSign app on 30 March 2026 with the foreign and justice ministries. It lets people [apply online for document authentication, powers of attorney and professional licences, paid by telebirr](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8578538>). In October 2026 the trade ministry said [1,088,787 business registration and licensing services were delivered online in three months](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9819771>). They ran across federal, regional and city offices. [The labour ministry put ten services on the e-Services portal in 2020](<https://addisinsight.net/2020/08/11/ministry-digitizes-services-joins-e-service-platform/>), when it carried 36 institutions.

**Single gateway.** [National ID Ethiopia's May 2026 deck calls MESOB a digital one-stop shop for e-government services built on the Fayda ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). It lists it beside farmer and land records and health insurance. Prime Minister Abiy [inaugurated the Unified MESOB Application, which brings 27 services from several institutions onto one platform](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>), ENA says.

**Use published.** No source found gives users, applications or transactions through a national portal. The nearest held fact: [MESOB one-stop service had reached 70 centres within a year](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>).
