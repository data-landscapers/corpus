# Ethiopia

## Government services online

**Services online.** [Ethio Telecom launched the teleSign app on 30 March 2026 with the foreign and justice ministries and the documents authentication service, for applying online for document authentication, powers of attorney and professional licences, paid by telebirr](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8578538>). [The labour ministry put ten services on the Ethiopian e-Services portal in 2020, when the portal carried 36 government institutions](<https://addisinsight.net/2020/08/11/ministry-digitizes-services-joins-e-service-platform/>).

**Single gateway.** [The national ID programme describes MESOB as a one-stop shop for e-government services built on the Fayda ID](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [Prime Minister Abiy inaugurated the Unified MESOB Application, which ENA says brings 27 services from across institutions onto one platform](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>).

**Use published.** No source held gives users, applications or transactions through a national portal. The nearest held fact: [MESOB one-stop service had reached 70 centres within a year](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9070888>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [National Rural Land Administration Information System (NRLAIS)](<https://www.moa.gov.et/e-services/>); [Fayda](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source dated to the day or month since October 2023 counts ten or more services online; the MESOB application's launch is dated only to 2026.
