# Guinea

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [82.2 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Guinea owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 17.8 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 79.3 per cent in 2021, 80.1 per cent in 2022 and 81 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.9 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past four in five and 12.8 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
