# Ghana

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [73.1 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Ghana owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 26.9 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 69.8 per cent in 2021, 70.7 per cent in 2022 and 71.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.3 points. The last survey figure in the series is [69.8 per cent, for 2021](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The share is past two in three and 6.9 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
