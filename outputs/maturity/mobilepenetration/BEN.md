# Benin

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [65 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Benin owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 35.0 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 57 per cent in 2021, 64.2 per cent in 2022 and 64.6 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past two in three and 15 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
