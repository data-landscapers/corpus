# Cape Verde

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [76.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Cabo-Verde.pdf>) in Cape Verde owned a mobile phone, by INE's continuous multi-purpose household survey. That leaves 23.7 per cent without a phone of their own. The International Telecommunication Union's estimate for the same year is higher, [81.4 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Its series [put the share at 81.1 per cent in 2021, 79.6 per cent in 2022 and 80.6 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). The survey figure is past two in three and 3.7 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold. It counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
