# Cape Verde

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2025, [78.2 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/utilizacao-da-internet-em-cabo-verde-sobe-ligeiramente-para-773-em-2025-revelam-dados-do-ine/>) in Cape Verde owned a mobile phone, by the national statistics institute INE's survey. That leaves 21.8 per cent without a phone of their own. INE's survey [put the share at 76.3 per cent in 2024](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Cabo-Verde.pdf>). The International Telecommunication Union's estimate for 2024 is higher, [81.4 per cent](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). The survey figure is past two in three and 1.8 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold. It counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
