# Mali

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [69.2 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Mali owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 30.8 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 66.4 per cent in 2021, 68.5 per cent in 2022 and 68.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past two in three and 10.8 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.

***Also on file***. [EMOP 2025 (INSTAT) household phone ownership: 88.1% of households own a mobile phone, 2025 (66.7% in 2011)](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/345/ranuel25_eq.pdf>); [ITU mobile-cellular subscriptions: 114 mobile-cellular subscriptions per 100 inhabitants, 2022](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MLI.pdf>); [ITU mobile network coverage: 100% population covered by mobile-cellular network, 70% 3G, 53% 4G, 2022](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MLI.pdf>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
