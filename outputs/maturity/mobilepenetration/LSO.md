# Lesotho

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [75.2 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Lesotho owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 24.8 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 69.3 per cent in 2021, 71.7 per cent in 2022 and 70.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5.9 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past two in three and 4.8 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use. [The Lesotho Communications Authority's national survey of late 2016 found 78.7 per cent of residents owning a mobile phone](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/2334/Final%20Integrated%20Report%2013022018.pdf>).
