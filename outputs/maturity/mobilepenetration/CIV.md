# Cote d'Ivoire

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [65.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Cote d'Ivoire owned a mobile phone, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 34.7 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 87.9 per cent in 2021, 63.1 per cent in 2022 and 66.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share fell by 22.6 points. The share is past two in three and 14.7 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
