# Kenya

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [53.7 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Kenya owned a mobile phone, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 46.3 per cent without a phone of their own. The series gives no figure for 2021, 2022 or 2023, so no source found says whether the share is rising or falling. The share is past half and 11.3 points short of 65 per cent, which is two in three. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use. [Kenya's 2023/24 Housing Survey, published by the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics and the Communications Authority, found 53.7 per cent of individuals owning a mobile phone: 54.5 per cent of men and 52.9 per cent of women](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-08/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20ICT%20Analytical%20Report%20Based%20on%20Kenya%20Housing%20Survey%202023-2024%20Final%20WEB%20Version.pdf>).
