# Mozambique

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [79.9 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Mozambique owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 20.1 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 64.5 per cent in 2021, 77.3 per cent in 2022 and 78.6 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 15.4 points. The last survey figure in the series is [77.3 per cent, for 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The share is past two in three and 0.1 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
