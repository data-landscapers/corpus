# Mauritania

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [83.2 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Mauritania owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 16.8 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 76 per cent in 2021, 77.5 per cent in 2022 and 81.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 7.2 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past four in five and 11.8 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.

***Also on file***. [Mobile-cellular subscriptions per 100 inhabitants: 113 per 100 inhabitants (2022), ITU Digital Development Dashboard](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MRT.pdf>).

***Not found***. No figure of 95 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
