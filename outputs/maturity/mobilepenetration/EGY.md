# Egypt

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [97.4 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Egypt owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 2.6 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 98.4 per cent in 2021, 97.4 per cent in 2022 and 97.4 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share fell by 1 point. The last survey figure in the series is [97.4 per cent, for 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. At 95 per cent or more, nearly everyone aged 10 and over has a phone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
