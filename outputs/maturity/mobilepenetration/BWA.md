# Botswana

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [68.7 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Botswana owned a mobile phone, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 31.3 per cent without a phone of their own. The series gives no figure for 2021, 2022 or 2023, so no source found says whether the share is rising or falling. The share is past two in three and 11.3 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
