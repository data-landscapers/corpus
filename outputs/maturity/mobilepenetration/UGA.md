# Uganda

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [43.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Uganda owned a mobile phone, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union; the 2024 census [put it at 53.7 per cent in towns and 37 per cent in rural areas](<https://pmldaily.com/news/2026/10/ugandas-digital-skills-gap-threatens-jobs-despite-expanding-connectivity-ucc.html>). That leaves 56.7 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 65 per cent in 2021, 65 per cent in 2022 and 62 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share fell by 21.7 points. Fewer than half of people own a phone, and the share is 6.7 points short of half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
