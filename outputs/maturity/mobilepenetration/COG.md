# Congo

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [69.1 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Congo owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 30.9 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 63 per cent in 2021, 69.2 per cent in 2022 and 69.1 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 6.1 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past two in three and 10.9 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.

***Also on file***. [Arpce mobile telephony penetration rate: 94%](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-telecommunications-larpce-presente-la-2e-edition-du-livre-blanc-et-le-projet-dadressage-postal-numerique/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
