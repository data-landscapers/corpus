# Tanzania

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [85.3 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Tanzania owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 14.7 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 78.2 per cent in 2021, 82.5 per cent in 2022 and 83.9 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 7.1 points. The last survey figure in the series is [82.5 per cent, for 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The share is past four in five and 9.7 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
