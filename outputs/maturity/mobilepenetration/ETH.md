# Ethiopia

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [52.7 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Ethiopia owned a mobile phone, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 47.3 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 45.2 per cent in 2021, 49.6 per cent in 2022 and 51.2 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 7.5 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 12.3 points short of 65 per cent, which is two in three. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.

***Also on file***. [Unique mobile subscribers, 33 percent of the population in 2024 (26 percent in 2020): unique mobile subscribers 33% of population, 2024](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/30/empowering-ethiopians-by-laying-the-digital-foundations-for-afe-economic-growth>).

***Not found***. No figure of 65 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
