# Morocco

## People who own a mobile phone

**Share of people who own a mobile phone, with its year.** In 2024, [97 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) in Morocco owned a mobile phone, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 3.0 per cent without a phone of their own. The same series [put the share at 96.2 per cent in 2021, 96.5 per cent in 2022 and 96.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.8 points. At 95 per cent or more, nearly everyone aged 10 and over has a phone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of SIM cards they hold, and counts a phone as owned where the person has it for their own use.
