# Liberia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Liberia's printed [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>), approved on 9 March 2026 and printed on 23 March 2026, takes effect on publication into handbills (s.44), and existing businesses have one year from the effective date to comply (s.41).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Liberia.

**Authority appointed.** Sections 8 and 10 of the [Act](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>) give data protection functions to the Independent Information Commissioner, the oversight body created by the Freedom of Information Act of 2010; the Act does not state that the Commissioner has begun acting under it.

**Implementing and acting.** On 13 September 2026 the Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection [said](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/mgcsp-admits-authentic-beneficiary-data-leaked-launches-internal-investigation/>) that it is aware of its responsibilities under Liberia's Data Protection and Privacy Act, and that it took part in validating the legal framework.
