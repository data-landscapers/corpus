# Comoros

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [TechHive Advisory states](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) the Union of Comoros enacted its Protection of Personal Data Law on 29 June 2021 as a comprehensive framework. The [Assembly adopted Loi n°14-029/AU](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>) on personal data protection, 68 articles, in plenary session on 26 June 2014.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Comoros signed the Malabo Convention on 29 January 2018 and had not ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** [TechHive Advisory states](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) the law establishes a National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data but that as of mid-2024 it had not been formally constituted or made operational. [Article 19 of the 2014 law](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>) institutes a Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés whose nine members are named by decree under Article 24.

**Implementing and acting.** As of mid-2024, [TechHive Advisory states](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) the authority had not been formally constituted or made operational.

***Also on file***. [National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>) and [Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>).

***Not found***. The evidence does not establish whether the law has commenced, whether the authority has been constituted since mid-2024, or any ruling against an organisation.
