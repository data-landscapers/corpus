# Sierra Leone

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone has a Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, which the Information Minister said in [November 2025](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>) had finished its final national validation, with Cabinet and Parliament still to come. In [April 2026](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>) Cabinet approved a National Data Protection Policy and authorised a new Data Protection and Right to Access Information Act.

**Malabo Convention.** Sierra Leone signed the Malabo Convention on 29 January 2016, and the [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that it had not ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** The [2025 Bill](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>) would establish for the first time a single independent authority for both data protection and access to information, and that authority is not yet constituted because the Bill is unenacted.

**Implementing and acting.** [Cabinet's April 2026 decision](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>) left the Ministry of Information and Civic Education and the Attorney-General's office to finalise the Bill and table it in Parliament.

***Also on file***. [Right to Access Information Commission (RAIC)](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>); [National Data Protection Policy](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>).

***Not found***. Whether any authority is constituted or acting is not held, and neither is any ruling against an organisation.
