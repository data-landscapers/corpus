# Mauritius

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection Office [reports on its 2024 enforcement](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) of the Data Protection Act 2017. It cites the Act's sections on complaints, transfers, registration, certification and appeals. [Commissioner Madhub describes](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>) the 2017 regime as the current law. A first law of 2004 was partly implemented in 2009. The Office has also [sent the ministry a draft bill](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) to amend the Act for European Commission adequacy.

**Malabo Convention.** Mauritius [ratified the Malabo Convention on 6 March 2018](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>). It deposited its instrument on 14 March 2018, the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026 records.

**Authority appointed.** [Drudeisha Madhub is named as Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>). The Data Protection Office has been [operational since 16 February 2009](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). In January 2026 it [co-led the launch of the National Data Strategy 2025-2029](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553938>) with the ministry. Regulations of June 2026 [give it roles](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf>): receiving notice of data protection officers within 14 days, and certifying them.

**Implementing and acting.** For 2024 the Office [reports](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) 179 new complaints and 39 closed. [It also counts](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) 105 breaches notified, 112 transfers authorised and 12 rejected, and four inspections. It [issued guidelines for the insurance sector](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) and 3,076 registration certificates in 2024. In September 2026 it [told management companies to register as controllers and name their own officers](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/Advice%20to%20%20MCs%20on%20Registration.pdf>). It said it may make site visits. In August 2026 it [issued guidance on designating and certifying officers](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Communique/DPO_Guidance_Notes_Designation_Certification_DPOs.pdf>). Controllers must keep their officer's details current on its e-DPO Portal.
