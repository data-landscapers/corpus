# Mauritius

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection Office [reports on its enforcement in 2024](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) of the Data Protection Act 2017, citing its sections on complaints, transfers, registration, certification and appeals. [Commissioner Madhub describes](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>) the 2017 regime as the country's current law, after a first law in 2004 partly implemented in 2009. The office also [submitted a draft Data Protection Bill](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) to the ministry to amend the Act for European Commission adequacy.

**Malabo Convention.** Mauritius [ratified the Malabo Convention on 6 March 2018](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 14 March 2018, according to the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** [Drudeisha Madhub is named as Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>), and the Data Protection Office has been [operational since 16 February 2009](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). In January 2026 the Office [co-led the launch of the National Data Strategy 2025-2029](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553938>) with the ministry, and a June 2026 set of [regulations give it roles](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf>) in receiving data protection officer notifications within 14 days and certifying officers.

**Implementing and acting.** For January to December 2024 the Office [reports](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) 179 new complaints, 39 closed, 105 personal data breaches notified, 112 cross-border transfers authorised and 12 rejected, and four compliance inspections. It [issued guidelines for the insurance sector](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) and 3,076 registration certificates in 2024.
