# Madagascar

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Loi n° 2014-038 on the protection of personal data was [promulgated on 9 January 2015](<https://digital.gov.mg/en/2022/07/05/loi-n-2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>), and the [procedures manual of the data protection commission](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf>), dated December 2025, treats it as the operative legal basis for its work.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union status list of 2 February 2026 records that [Madagascar ratified the Malabo Convention on 17 July 2025](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 15 September 2025.

**Authority appointed.** Seven members of the Commission Malagasy de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CMIL) were [sworn in at the Cour suprême on 4 August 2025](<https://assemblee-nationale.mg/article/prestation-de-serment-des-membres-de-la-commission-malagasy-de-linformatique-et-des-libertes-cmil/>). Its [bureau was elected on 14 August 2025](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>), and in March 2026 the Unité de Gouvernance Digitale [announced the CMIL is now effectively in place](<https://digital.gov.mg/en/2026/03/27/rapport-annuel-sur-la-confidentialiteet-la-protection-des-donnees-a-madagascar/>).

**Implementing and acting.** The CMIL's [annual report of January 2026](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>) says no processing declaration has been registered yet, that citizens' complaints went untreated for ten years, and that the commission has no premises or staff, with 104 posts targeted in the 2026 Finance Law.

***Also on file***. [Loi n° 2014-038 sur la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://digital.gov.mg/en/2022/07/05/loi-n-2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>); [Commission Malagasy de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CMIL)](<https://digital.gov.mg/en/2026/03/27/rapport-annuel-sur-la-confidentialiteet-la-protection-des-donnees-a-madagascar/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows the authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
