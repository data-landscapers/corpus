# Zambia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 is the law a [World Bank tender notice of 10 August 2026](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461500>) treats as being implemented. A [July 2026 report](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/zambia-begins-enforcing-controversial-cyber-crimes-act-amid-digital-economy-growth/>) describes it as part of the operating legal framework beside the Cyber Security Act and Cyber Crimes Act.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Zambia as having ratified the Malabo Convention on 15 December 2020 and deposited its instrument on 24 March 2021.

**Authority appointed.** The first Data Protection Commissioner was [appointed in June 2023](<https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/>). In September 2025 the Commissioner and a Deputy Commissioner [took part in a review of a draft health data statutory instrument](<https://www.cidrz.org/2025/09/23/cidrz-and-moh-lead-stakeholders-in-reviewing-draft-health-data-statutory-instrument-to-strengthen-health-data-management/>).

**Implementing and acting.** The Commissioner's office [opened online registration](<https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2025/04/zambias-data-protection-commissioner-fully-operational-enforcing-privacy-compliance/>) for controllers and processors, with a 30 April 2025 deadline. [UNDP](<https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration>) lists training staff in over 145 offices across 10 provinces on the Act as a requirement of the digital ID rollout.
