# Ethiopia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Ethiopia published its [Personal Data Protection Proclamation](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>) in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 24 July 2024. [Shega reported in November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) that it took effect on publication.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature of the Malabo Convention by Ethiopia. It records no ratification either.

**Authority appointed.** [The Tech Hive review](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>) says the Ethiopian Communications Authority is the regulator under Article 5 of the Proclamation. In [November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) its Director General said data processors and collectors now need the Authority's permission. The Director General also said staffing and technical capacity are being expanded.

**Implementing and acting.** The Authority told Shega it had received about 10 requests to transfer data abroad. It had approved them as a temporary measure. Officials previewed a registration portal for data controllers and processors at a [workshop](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) co-hosted with Huawei.
