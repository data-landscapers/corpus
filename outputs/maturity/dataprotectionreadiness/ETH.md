# Ethiopia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Ethiopia published its [Personal Data Protection Proclamation](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>) in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 24 July 2024, and [Shega reported in November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) that it took effect on publication.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Ethiopia.

**Authority appointed.** The Ethiopian Communications Authority is the regulator under Article 5 of the Proclamation, [per the Tech Hive review](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-ethiopias-data-protection-act>); its Director General said in [November 2025](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) that data processors and collectors now need the Authority's permission and that staffing and technical capacity are being expanded.

**Implementing and acting.** The Authority told Shega it had received about 10 requests to transfer data abroad and approved them as a temporary measure, and officials previewed a registration portal for data controllers and processors at a [workshop](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>) co-hosted with Huawei.
