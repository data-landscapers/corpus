# Côte d'Ivoire

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [law of 19 June 2013 on personal data protection](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) is the framework under which a 2015 decree sets filing fees, prior authorisations and fines of up to FCFA 500 million on repeat breach. The digital minister [cited the same law](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>) as the country's existing framework on 18 May 2026.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union Commission's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Côte d'Ivoire as having ratified or acceded to the Convention on 8 March 2023, with the instrument deposited on 3 April 2023.

**Authority appointed.** ARTCI, styling itself the Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles, [issued a communique on 3 August 2026](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>) inviting controllers to use CERTINUM. A [fact sheet of 23 May 2022](<https://dataprotection.africa/cote-divoire/>) says a data protection authority is appointed, with 7 members serving up to 6 years.

**Implementing and acting.** ARTCI [launched Certinum on 2 July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642985/economie/ntic/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-certinum-entre-en-service-pour-accelerer-la-conformite-numerique-en-cote-divoire>), an online platform for compliance and processing-authorisation requests, replacing about 100 paper files handled each month. The minister listed [compliance control operations carried out in 2022](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>) and national awareness campaigns launched from 2015.

***Also on file***. [ARTCI (Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles)](<https://artci.ci/lartci-invite-les-entreprises-a-privilegier-la-plateforme-certinum/>); [Certinum](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642985/economie/ntic/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-certinum-entre-en-service-pour-accelerer-la-conformite-numerique-en-cote-divoire>); [Loi du 19 juin 2013](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>).

***Not found***. No ruling by the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
