# Benin

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Personal data protection in Benin sits in Book V of the Digital Code, [law no. 2017-20 of 20 April 2018](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>), which the APDP's 2015-2020 report gives as its legal framework. The code is [amended by law no. 2020-35 of 6 January 2021](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2022-107/>), and [La Nation of 5 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.bj/societe/societe-votre-patron-peut-il-lire-vos-messages-whatsapp-professionnels>) treats it as the current framework.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Benin ratified the Malabo Convention on 14 May 2024 and deposited its instrument on 12 November 2024.

**Authority appointed.** The Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles is established by [Articles 462 to 473 of the Digital Code](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>). Its [president, Me Luciano Hounkponou, spoke at a forum in Johannesburg on 1 to 3 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/ia-et-flux-transfrontaliers-en-afrique-lappel-de-lapdp-benin-a-une-gouvernance-fiable-des-donnees>), and its [2026 budget programme is XOF 738,277,000](<https://budgetbenin.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Tableaux-de-Classifications-croisees-des-depenses-de-l-Etat-sur-la-periode-pluriannuelle-2022-2028-budget-LF-2026.xlsx>).

**Implementing and acting.** The APDP's [2025 report](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite>) gives 907 files examined, 64 inspection missions, 20 complaints treated and 75 compliance certificates issued. Its [2015-2020 report](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>) records 347 decisions rendered over 2016 to 2020, including the CNIL's refusal on 18 August 2016 of Spacetel Benin authorisation to collect biometric data.
