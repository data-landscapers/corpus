# Egypt

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Shura Law Firm](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>) says Law No. 151 of 2020 entered into force in October 2020 and its regulations' transitional period expires on 31 October 2026. [Baker McKenzie](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>) says all data users must be in full compliance by that date, and [Access Partnership](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>) says enforcement is expected from 1 November 2026.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records no signature and no ratification of the Convention by Egypt.

**Authority appointed.** A [7 August 2026 report](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/Massai/News/5884624.aspx>) says the Personal Data Protection Centre, affiliated to the ICT ministry, is acting under Law 151 of 2020 with an acting chief executive. [Baker McKenzie](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>) says it launched its electronic portal in early October 2026 for accreditation, licence and permit applications.

**Implementing and acting.** [Tech Hive Advisory](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>) says the Centre published guidelines on consent, lawful bases, direct marketing and licences, and breach-notification templates, in January and February 2026. [Baker McKenzie](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>) says its portal also publishes guidelines and frameworks setting out requirements for controllers and processors.

***Also on file***. The [Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>), the [Executive Regulations of the PDPL (Decree No. 816 of 2025)](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>), the [Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC)](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335073.aspx>) and the [PDPC electronic portal](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).

***Not found***. Any ruling by the Centre against an organisation, and any inspection it has carried out.
