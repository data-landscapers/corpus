# South Sudan

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** A two-day workshop of over 30 stakeholders, convened by Digital Rights Frontlines with the NCA, reviewed the [Data Protection Bill, 2025](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>) in a post dated 5 May 2026. The [ICT Minister said in December 2025](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister>) the government will introduce its first Data Protection Bill in 2026. The [World Bank's 2026 diagnostic](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>) states there is no national data protection or privacy law.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by South Sudan.

**Authority appointed.** [Digital Rights Frontlines](<https://www.digitalrights.ngo/post/an-independent-data-protection-act-a-critical-part-to-south-sudan-s-digital-future>) states in February 2026 that the country lacks a fully operational, independent Data Protection Authority. [Radio Tamazuj](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>) reports the draft bill, once passed, will establish a Data Protection Commission.

**Implementing and acting.** In October 2025 Paradigm Initiative and Koneta Hub held a [two-day Juba convening](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-partners-with-koneta-hub-to-drive-dialogue-on-data-protection-law-in-south-sudan>) of lawmakers, civil society and officials to seek parliamentary commitment to table the draft Bill.

***Also on file***. [National Communication Authority (NCA)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>); [Cybercrime and Abuse of Computer Bill 2025](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister>); [Data Protection Commission (proposed)](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>); [Transitional Constitution of South Sudan (2011), Article 22](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>); [Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Bill 2024](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>); [Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Provisional Order 2021](<https://dataprotection.africa/south-sudan/>); [National Civil Registry and Identity Regulations (2024)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

***Not found***. No enacted data protection law, no operating authority and no ruling against an organisation has been found.
