# Niger

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The president of the High Authority for the Protection of Personal Data, HAPDP, said in an interview published on 28 July 2025 that [law no. 2022-59 of 16 December 2022](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) repealed and replaced the 2017 law as amended in 2019. The authority's [consolidated text of April 2026](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>) presents the law as the operative general law, amended by Law 2023-31 of 4 July 2023.

**Malabo Convention.** Niger [ratified the Malabo Convention on 22 February 2022](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 16 March 2022, according to the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** [Africa Data Protection reports](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>) that the government has by decree abolished the HAPDP and split its powers between the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior, in a report published on 13 June 2026. In July 2025 the HAPDP president said the authority had [ten members](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) and that its president took office on 18 September 2024.

**Implementing and acting.** The HAPDP president said in July 2025 that the authority [processed 139 compliance files](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) since September 2024, covering declarations, video-surveillance declarations, and processing and transfer authorisations, and ran four control missions in 2025 on mobile operators.

***Also on file***. [Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP)](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>).

***Not found***. No authority appointed after the decree has been found, and none registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
