# Niger

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The president of the High Authority for the Protection of Personal Data, HAPDP, gave an interview published on 28 July 2025. The president said [law no. 2022-59 of 16 December 2022](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) repealed and replaced the 2017 law as amended in 2019. The authority's [consolidated text of April 2026](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>) presents the law as the operative general law. It records the amendment by Law 2023-31 of 4 July 2023.

**Malabo Convention.** Niger [ratified the Malabo Convention on 22 February 2022](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 16 March 2022. That is the record on the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** [Africa Data Protection reports](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>) that the government has abolished the HAPDP by decree. It split the HAPDP's powers between the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior. The report was published on 13 June 2026. In July 2025 the HAPDP president said the authority had [ten members](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). The president had taken office on 18 September 2024.

**Implementing and acting.** The HAPDP president said in July 2025 that the authority had [processed 139 compliance files](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) since September 2024. They covered declarations, video-surveillance declarations, and processing and transfer authorisations. The authority also ran four control missions on mobile operators in 2025.

***Also on file***. [Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP)](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>).

***Not found***. No authority appointed after the decree has been found. None has been found registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
