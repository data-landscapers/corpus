# National data protection readiness: data protection system

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Union's Data Policy Framework, endorsed by the Executive Council in February 2022, asks each member state for a law, an independent authority and working practice, and asks it to ratify the Malabo Convention. It sets the parts and no measure of them. This study puts the ability to act above ratification.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source shows no data protection law is in force: there is none, or it is still a bill or not yet commenced |
| 2 Preparing | A law is in force |
| 3 Establishing | A law is in force, and the country has ratified the Malabo Convention or appointed an authority |
| 4 Operating | The law is being implemented: the authority registers organisations, takes breach reports, issues guidance or inspects |
| 5 Leading | The authority is acting: it has ruled against a named organisation, or publishes a count of its rulings |

### Angola: 5 Leading

Law in force since 2011; the authority is appointed and working, and fined a hotel company in August 2026.

- Angola's Agência de Protecção de Dados fined Lizíria, Lda. - The World Bank's ID4D diagnostic states South Sudan does not have a national data protection or privacy law and has no national data protection and privacy regime; no event date is given, so the published date of 2026-05-01 is used ([source, 2026-05-01](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf)).

### Sudan: 1 Absent

Sudan has no data protection law in force in 2026; two authorities set up by decree in 2025 have issued no binding rules.

- On 11 August 2026 the Justice Ministry's under-secretary said the ministry is preparing three draft laws with the digital transformation ministry, among them a 2026 Law on Regulating Data and Artificial Intelligence, to provide the legislative framework including data protection ([source, 2026-08-11](https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1)).
- On 6 August 2026 the Council of Ministers discussed the digital transformation ministry's draft bills, including a data and artificial intelligence bill, and directed further study before approval by the interim parliament, a joint sitting of the Sovereignty and Ministers councils; the article does not describe the bill's content ([source, 2026-08-06](https://sudantribune.net/article/317108)).
