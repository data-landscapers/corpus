# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Digital Code, Ordinance-Law No. 23/010 of 13 March 2023](<https://droitnumerique.cd/arptic-arptc-investie-des-missions-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-en-rd-congo/>), entered into force in 2023 and provides for the Autorité de Protection des Données.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that the Democratic Republic of the Congo ratified the Malabo Convention on 6 March 2025 and deposited its instrument on 27 June 2025.

**Authority appointed.** [Beto.cd reported on 13 September 2026](<https://beto.cd/onip-rgph2-deux-comptages-population-autorite-protection-donnees/>) that the Autorité de protection des données is not installed. A [ministerial order of 17 August 2024](<https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/>) provisionally gave its missions to ARPTIC pending its establishment. Its organisation and operation await a Prime Minister's decree.

**Implementing and acting.** On 12 March 2026 the Ministry of Digital Economy [launched a national network of data protection officers](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>) in Kinshasa, backed by RUDI International and Droit-Numérique.cd, to standardise personal data practice and support compliance. [No sanction or decision is reported](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/17/drc-launches-data-protection-officers-network-to-strengthen-digital-governance/>).

***Also on file***. [Code du numérique](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/17/drc-launches-data-protection-officers-network-to-strengthen-digital-governance/>), [Autorité de protection des données](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>) and [ARPTIC](<https://droitnumerique.cd/arptic-arptc-investie-des-missions-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-en-rd-congo/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows an appointed authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
