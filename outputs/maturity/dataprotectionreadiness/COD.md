# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Digital Code, Ordinance-Law No. 23/010 of 13 March 2023](<https://droitnumerique.cd/arptic-arptc-investie-des-missions-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-en-rd-congo/>), entered into force in 2023. It provides for the Autorité de Protection des Données. [In August 2025 a UNESCO workshop in Kinshasa reviewed the data protection texts in force, the Code among them](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/droits-numeriques-en-rdc-entre-protection-et-regulation>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records ratification on 6 March 2025. The instrument was deposited on 27 June 2025.

**Authority appointed.** A [ministerial order of 17 August 2024](<https://droitnumerique.cd/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees/>) gave the authority's missions to the telecoms regulator, ARPTC, until the authority is set up. Africa Data Protection reports that [the ARPTC has exercised those missions since March 2023](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/rdc-publication-au-journal-officiel-de-la-decision-fixant-la-procedure-des-formalites-prealables.html>). [Beto.cd reported on 13 September 2026](<https://beto.cd/onip-rgph2-deux-comptages-population-autorite-protection-donnees/>) that the Autorité de protection des données itself is not installed.

**Implementing and acting.** On 12 March 2026 the Ministry of Digital Economy [launched a national network of data protection officers](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>) in Kinshasa. The ARPTC's [decision on prior formalities under the Code was published in the Journal officiel on 23 April 2026](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/rdc-publication-au-journal-officiel-de-la-decision-fixant-la-procedure-des-formalites-prealables.html>). It sets how organisations declare their processing.
