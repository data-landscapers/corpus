# Morocco

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The president of the CNDP, interviewed on 4 August 2026, [speaks of Law 09-08](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>) as in force for more than fifteen years, with its paradigms still applicable.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Morocco.

**Authority appointed.** The CNDP, Morocco's national commission for the control of personal data protection, [validated five new deliberations](<https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/>) on 28 November 2025, acting as the supervisory body.

**Implementing and acting.** In a communiqué dated 28 August 2026, the CNDP [gave guidance](<https://fr.walaw.press/country/cirdi/QWSP/articles/legislatives_2026_la_cndp_encadre_l_usage_des_donnees_personnelles_et_de_l_ia_pendant_la_campagne/GPFLSXGWQQFM>) on Law 09-08 to political parties and voters for the 23 September 2026 elections, and opened a 3020 information line. It [published a national register of notified processing operations](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf>) in January 2023.

***Also on file***. [CNDP](<https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/>); [Loi 09-08](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
