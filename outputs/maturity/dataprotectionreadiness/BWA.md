# Botswana

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Data Protection Act No. 18 of 2024](<https://itlawco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/1736836435941.pdf>) came into operation on 14 January 2025. It applies to all organisations processing personal data, including public entities, [Mmegi states](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Convention by Botswana.

**Authority appointed.** [Mmegi reports](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>) Kepaletswe Somolekae as the newly appointed Information and Data Protection Commissioner on 11 February 2026. The Commission was [established in 2022](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87795>), and in July 2025 it had three contract officers, with the Deputy Commissioner and two director posts unfilled.

**Implementing and acting.** On 17 July 2026 the Commission [issued a Determination and an Enforcement Notice](<https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/>) against DPSM, ordering the ePayslip portal suspended and a DPIA and revised privacy notice submitted within one month. [Mmegi reports](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news>) DPSM suspended the portal on 22 July 2026.
