# Mali

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Law no. 2013-015 of 21 May 2013, as amended, is the basis the authority's president [cited for its sanctions when opening its session on 3 August 2026](<https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/>). A [decree of 14 May 2024](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2024/mali-jo-2024-11.pdf>) is made under it.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union status list of 2 February 2026 [records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Mali](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>).

**Authority appointed.** The Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (APDP) held its [second ordinary session on 3 August 2026](<https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/>) under president Pr Mamoudou Samassekou with all commissioners present. It [opened regional offices at Kayes, Sikasso, Segou and Mopti](<https://saheltribune.com/donnees-personnelles-lalerte-du-regulateur-malien/>) and a [Ségou antenna on 4 December 2025](<https://lessor.ml/posts/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-region-de-segou-a-son-antenne-6932a107b56bd>).

**Implementing and acting.** In 2025 the APDP [fined a Bamako clinic 5 million FCFA](<https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/>) for obstructing a verification mission and [fined Société Aminata Konaté SARL 5 million F CFA](<https://malijet.com/les_faits_divers_au_mali/303061-sanction-pour-non-respect-des-donnees-personnelles--penalite-de-.html>) for refusing one. Sahel Tribune, summarising the 2025 annual report, [reports fines totalling 22 million FCFA](<https://saheltribune.com/donnees-personnelles-lalerte-du-regulateur-malien/>).
