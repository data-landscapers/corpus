# Cameroon

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 governs personal data protection. The [Direction Generale du Budget states](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>) that its deadline for entry into force was reached on 23 June 2026. [ACTPOL](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) says the law gave organisations 18 months to comply, a period that ended that day. In October 2026 [CLG Global](<https://clgglobal.com/cameroons-ai-data-centre-five-legal-questions-before-the-first-server-is-installed/>) advised operators to assume its obligations now apply.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union Commission's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Cameroon signed the Convention on 12 August 2021. It gives no ratification or deposit date. A [Direction Generale du Budget article of 24 September 2024](<https://www.dgb.cm/le-cenadi-sensibilise-les-administrations-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) also says ratification is awaited.

**Authority appointed.** The [law](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) makes the Autorité an independent public body with supervision and enforcement powers. [ACTPOL](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) found no decree creating it as of July 2026. On 29 October 2026 [CLG Global said](<https://clgglobal.com/cameroons-ai-data-centre-five-legal-questions-before-the-first-server-is-installed/>) it was not yet operational and awaited a presidential decree, with no head or members appointed.

**Implementing and acting.** The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications [told the National Assembly](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>) on 8 April 2025 that Cameroon had adopted the law. She said it sets principles, controller obligations and data subject rights.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No ratification of the Malabo Convention has been found, and no authority has been appointed. Nothing is held on how the law is applied or on any ruling.
