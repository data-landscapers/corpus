# Cameroon

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Direction Generale du Budget (Ministry of Finance) states](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>) that the deadline set for the entry into force of Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on personal data protection was reached on 23 June 2026, and that the law is now officially in force. The authors of an [African Law & Business article of 17 July 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-cameroons-new-data-protection-law/>) say the law becomes enforceable after an 18-month grace period from enactment.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union Commission's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Cameroon as having signed the Convention on 12 August 2021, with no ratification or deposit date. A [Direction Generale du Budget article of 24 September 2024](<https://www.dgb.cm/le-cenadi-sensibilise-les-administrations-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) likewise says Cameroon signed on 12 August 2021 and that ratification is awaited.

**Authority appointed.** The [law](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) defines the Autorité as an independent public body in charge of data protection and gives it supervision, notification and enforcement functions. The [African Law & Business authors](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-cameroons-new-data-protection-law/>) said in July 2025 that the authority the law provides for was yet to be established.

**Implementing and acting.** The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications [told the National Assembly](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>) on 8 April 2025 that Cameroon had adopted the law of 23 December 2024, setting principles, controller obligations and data subject rights.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No ratification of the Malabo Convention has been found, and no source found says an authority has been appointed. Nothing is held on how the law is applied or on any ruling.
