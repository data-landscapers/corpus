# Eritrea

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Law Lab Africa](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) states that no comprehensive data protection law is in force in Eritrea, as of a verification pass on 1 August 2026. A June 2025 [Horn of Africa Initiative update](<https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf>) says Eritrea remains without data protection legislation.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Eritrea.

**Authority appointed.** [Law Lab Africa](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) states that no supervisory authority has been established in Eritrea, as of 1 August 2026. [Levellers](<https://www.levellers.ai/what-is/ai-regulation-eritrea>), reviewed on 8 June 2026, states that no data protection authority has been appointed.

**Implementing and acting.** [Law Lab Africa](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) states that no enforcement decisions could be found for Eritrea and none are published, as of the 1 August 2026 verification pass by that source.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Any draft data protection law, and any body with data protection duties.
