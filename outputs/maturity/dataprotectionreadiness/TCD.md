# Chad

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** A digital law covering data protection is still being finalised. [Minister Boukar Michel announced on 9 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>) that authorities are finalizing a digital law incorporating international standards on cybersecurity, data protection and internet governance. [Ecofin reports](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) the Digital Code has been under development since December 2025 and incorporates international standards on cybersecurity and data protection.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Chad signed the Malabo Convention on 14 June 2015 and had not ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** [Ecofin reported on 16 March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) that a reform law ratified by the Senate on 13 March 2026 broadens the mandate of ANSICE, the National Agency for Information Security and Electronic Certification, to personal data protection, cybersecurity, cybercrime and AI oversight.

**Implementing and acting.** The [Minister's announcement of 9 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>) concerned a digital law still being finalised, with data protection among the standards it incorporates. [Ecofin's report of 16 March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) still describes the Code as under development.

***Noted, not assessed***. ***Not held***. The evidence does not show a data protection law in force or any ruling.
