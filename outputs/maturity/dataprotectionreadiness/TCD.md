# Chad

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Chad's personal data law of 2015 is treated as in force. [A Chadian law firm's analysis published on 6 July 2026 applies Loi n°007/PR/2015 to personal data](<https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/>). The analysis covers its articles 29 to 32 on transfers abroad. A replacement is being written. [The minister announced on 9 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>) that authorities were finalising a digital law. It would incorporate international standards on cybersecurity, data protection and internet governance. [Ecofin reported in March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) that the Digital Code was still under development.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Chad signed the Malabo Convention on 14 June 2015. Chad had not ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** [Ecofin reported on 16 March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) that the Senate ratified a reform law on 13 March 2026. The law broadens the mandate of ANSICE, the National Agency for Information Security and Electronic Certification. The new mandate covers personal data protection, cybersecurity, cybercrime and AI oversight.

**Implementing and acting.** [The reform of March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) gave the security agency data protection duties. No source found shows it registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting. No ruling against a named organisation has been found.
