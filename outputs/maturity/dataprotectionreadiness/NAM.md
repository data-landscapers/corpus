# Namibia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Minister Theofelus said in July 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A>) that Namibia is finalising the Data Protection Bill. In January 2026 she said the bill had been through consultation and drafting and was [ready for resubmission to the Cabinet Committee on Legislation](<https://neweralive.na/data-legislations-under-final-polishing/>) for onward processing by Parliament. An opposition MP told the Windhoek Observer in August 2026 that [Namibia has no data protection statute](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>).

**Malabo Convention.** Namibia [ratified the Malabo Convention on 25 January 2019](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 1 February 2019, according to the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** The ministry's [revised draft Data Protection Bill of 2022](<https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Data-Protection-Draft-Bill-March-2022....pdf>) provides in its long title for establishing a Data Protection Supervisory Authority and its powers, duties and functions. [Section 25 of the 2020 draft](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf>) establishes the Data Protection Supervisory Authority of Namibia, with Part VII running to section 37.

**Implementing and acting.** [Minister Theofelus said on 14 August 2025](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/08/namibias-data-protection-bill-to-be-tabled-in-september/>) that the ministry would table a Data Protection Bill in September 2025. In 2018 the [Minister of ICT told Parliament](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/STA2CF1-min.pdf>) that Namibia had embarked on developing Personal Data Protection and Cyber Security Bills.
