# Malawi

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024) was [assented to on 31 January 2024 and published on 2 February 2024](<https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf>). The Authority's [compliance guideline](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>) says it applies at once to non-Malawi controllers and to Malawi controllers of significant importance, and that other Malawi controllers and processors must comply from 3 June 2026.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Malawi.

**Authority appointed.** The [guidelines state](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818>) that the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority, MACRA, is the designated Data Protection Authority under the Act. [Nyasa Times](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/>) reports it convened a stakeholder consultation on draft regulations in Lilongwe in July 2026, with Daniel Chiwoni as Head of Data Protection. The World Bank said in November 2025 that MACRA [had appointed an acting Data Protection Commissioner](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/>).

**Implementing and acting.** In October 2025 the Authority published [guidelines on personal data breach notifications](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818>), with a 72-hour notice to the Authority and to data subjects, and [a compliance guideline and checklist](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>) covering the Act's duties, data subject rights and cross-border transfers. [GIZ ran a training-of-trainers programme](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7293/giz-supports-malawi-data-protection-authority-with-training-of-trainers-programme>) with the Authority in September 2026.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Act, 2024](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1714974153599cf41tfelimj.pdf>); [Data Protection Authority](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818>).
