# Central African Republic

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Data Protection Africa states](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>) in its factsheet of 28 January 2026 that the Central African Republic has a data protection law in place. The [promulgated text of Loi n° 24.001](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>), adopted in January 2024, gives no commencement date and no compliance period. Jurist Zengue [writes in September 2025](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/%EF%81%9Bcentrafrique%EF%81%9D-les-huit-forces-loi-relative-protection-des-donnees,54519.html>) that the law is promulgated and applied, with a compliance period running and no suspension of its execution.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Convention by the Central African Republic.

**Authority appointed.** [Data Protection Africa states](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>) the data protection authority had not yet been appointed as of 28 January 2026. [Article 57](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>) gives the ministry in charge of the digital economy twelve months from promulgation to set up the data protection agency, and names no composition, seat or financing for it.

**Implementing and acting.** The [law's text](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>) vests the agency's missions in the ministry in charge of the digital economy until the agency is set up.

***Also on file***. [Loi n° 24.001 portant protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>); [Agence en charge de la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>), run by the digital economy ministry in the interim.

***Not found***. No source found says the Malabo Convention has been signed or ratified, or that an authority has been appointed. Nothing is held on how the law is applied or on any ruling.
