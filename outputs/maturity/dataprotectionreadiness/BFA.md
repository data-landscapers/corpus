# Burkina Faso

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Law no. 001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021 [abrogates law no. 010-2004/AN](<https://www.fonction-publique.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/22-08-2021-C2-LOI-N__001-2021_AN_portant_protection_des_personnes.pdf>) and gives one year from entry into force for all processing to comply. The CIL [invoked it in a communiqué of 24 December 2024](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>), and the CIL president [recalled on 1 July 2025 that data protection is compulsory under it](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Burkina Faso.

**Authority appointed.** The Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CIL) is [led by its president, Dr Nassa](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-nationale-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-cil-sollicite-laccompagnement-du-premier-ministre/>), and was [funded in 2024 under its own budget programme, XOF 857,062,000 appropriated and 93.3 percent executed](<https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/loi-de-finances-2024-2026-vf2.pdf>). On [18 November 2025](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/>) its president signed the operational roadmap of the AES data protection authorities' coordination framework with Mali and Niger.

**Implementing and acting.** By [communiqué of 24 December 2024](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>) the CIL warned that repeated posting of people's images on social media without consent violates the law. On [1 July 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>) the Prime Minister launched CIL awareness sessions on personal data protection for ministries and public institutions, and on [26 June 2025](<https://cil.bf/?p=1506>) the CIL validated its public report for 2024.
