# Burkina Faso

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Law no. 001-2021/AN of 30 March 2021 [abrogates law no. 010-2004/AN](<https://www.fonction-publique.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/22-08-2021-C2-LOI-N__001-2021_AN_portant_protection_des_personnes.pdf>). It gives all processing one year from entry into force to comply. The CIL [invoked it in a communiqué of 24 December 2024](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). On 1 July 2025 the CIL president [said data protection is compulsory under it](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>). On 3 August 2026 deputies [adopted a new data protection bill](<https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification by Burkina Faso.

**Authority appointed.** The Commission de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CIL) is [led by its president, Dr Nassa](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-nationale-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-cil-sollicite-laccompagnement-du-premier-ministre/>). Its 2024 budget programme [appropriated XOF 857,062,000, of which 93.3 per cent was executed](<https://budgetouvert.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/loi-de-finances-2024-2026-vf2.pdf>). On [18 November 2025](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cybersecurite-les-pays-de-laes-signent-une-feuille-de-route-sur-la-protection-des-donnees/>) its president signed a roadmap of the AES data protection authorities with Mali and Niger. On 3 August 2026 deputies [adopted the law creating ARCOD](<https://www.aib.media/burkina-les-deputes-adoptent-la-loi-portant-creation-de-larcod-issue-de-la-fusion-du-csc-et-de-la-cil/>), which merges the CIL and the CSC. By decree of 16 October 2026 [the CSC's president, Wendingoudi Louis Modeste Ouedraogo, was named president of ARCOD](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150089>).

**Implementing and acting.** By [communiqué of 24 December 2024](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>) the CIL warned against posting people's images on social media without consent. On [26 June 2025](<https://cil.bf/?p=1506>) it validated its public report for 2024. On [1 July 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>) the Prime Minister launched CIL awareness sessions for ministries and public institutions. The CIL [publishes a form for authorising transfers of personal data abroad](<https://cil.bf/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/FORMULAIRE_ANNEXE-TRANSFERT-DE-DONNEES-A-LETRANGER-1.pdf>).
