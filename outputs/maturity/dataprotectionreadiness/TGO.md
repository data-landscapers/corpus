# Togo

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Loi n° 2019-014 of 29 October 2019](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) on personal data protection was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated. In September 2026 the IPDCP [briefed ministry cabinet directors and secretaries-general on their obligations as data controllers under it](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>), and in June 2026 it said [35 data protection correspondents had been designated under the law](<https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-lance-la-premiere-session-de-formation-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Togo ratified or acceded to the Malabo Convention on 30 September 2021 and deposited its instrument on 19 October 2021.

**Authority appointed.** The IPDCP [became operational in 2024 and, per its head, holds powers of control, injunction and sanction](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>). It [officially launched its activities in Lomé on 28 March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>), and in 2026 it [ran the June correspondent training](<https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-lance-la-premiere-session-de-formation-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>) and [signed a cooperation agreement with Mali's APDP on 23 September](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/2509-20170-togo-mali-data-regulators-agree-to-share-expertise>).

**Implementing and acting.** The IPDCP [ran a three-day training for 35 data protection correspondents from 22 to 24 June 2026](<https://ipdcp.tg/lipdcp-lance-la-premiere-session-de-formation-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>) and opened [its first evaluation and certification session for correspondents in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>), with 32 professionals. On 15 September 2026 it [convened a compliance meeting for public administrations](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>). It [launched a public-awareness campaign in March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>). On 19 October 2026 it [placed a team in the media regulator's monitoring service](<https://ipdcp.tg/medias-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-lipdcp-et-la-harc-renforcent-leur-partenariat-2/>).
