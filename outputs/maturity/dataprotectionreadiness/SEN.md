# Senegal

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** A [reference summary](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Senegal-Factsheet.pdf>) states that Law No. 2008-12 on the protection of personal data was enacted on 25 January 2008 and has been in force since 2014. In April to June 2026 the Commission issued receipts, authorisations and a rejection [under that law](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). An expert told [APS](<https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/>) in March 2026 that a bill to replace it is reportedly in the circuit.

**Malabo Convention.** Senegal [ratified the Malabo Convention on 3 August 2016](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 16 August 2016, according to the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** The Commission de protection des données personnelles (CDP) is an [independent administrative authority](<https://aps.sn/plus-de-300-dossiers-traites-par-la-commission-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-au-premier-trimestre-2026-officiel/>) set up by Law No. 2008-12. It [held two plenary sessions](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>) in April to June 2026, and its president [signed a partnership convention](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-benin-et-le-senegal-scellent-une-alliance-strategique-a-cotonou>) with Benin's authority in April 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** On 12 March 2026 the CDP, after a complaint, [inspected Digicommunication International unannounced and ordered the meeting-room camera uninstalled](<https://aps.sn/plus-de-300-dossiers-traites-par-la-commission-de-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-au-premier-trimestre-2026-officiel/>). For April to June 2026 it [reported 195 files handled](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>), 39 authorisations issued and one processing rejected. In 2024 it [served a formal notice on SOLID SA](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>), operator of the Kay Voter platform.
