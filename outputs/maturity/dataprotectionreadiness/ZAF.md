# South Africa

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [ITWeb reports](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ>) that the Protection of Personal Information Act came into force on 1 July 2021 after a year-long grace period, with [fines up to R10 million and up to 10 years' imprisonment](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/popia-grace-period-limits-regulators-fines/P3gQ2qGAlAk7nRD1>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records South Africa as having signed on 16 February 2023, with no ratification or accession date.

**Authority appointed.** The Information Regulator was [established on 1 December 2016](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ>) and its chairperson Pansy Tlakula spoke for it in September 2026. Parliament's Justice Committee [published candidate names for vacant positions](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-justice-committee-calls-public-input-candidate-cvs-information-regulator>) on 11 September 2026. National Treasury [appropriated R135.7m for 2025/26](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/National%20Budget/2025May/ene/FullENE.pdf>) to the Regulator.

**Implementing and acting.** The Regulator [received over 8,000 security-compromise reports to date](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-concerned-by-alarming-rate-of-data-breaches/rxP3jqBE2ZLMA2ye>), 1,220 of them since April 2026. It issued the South African Bureau of Standards an [enforcement notice in August 2026](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-concerned-by-alarming-rate-of-data-breaches/rxP3jqBE2ZLMA2ye>) giving it 90 days to revise policies and implement security measures. Its [first fine, R5 million, was imposed](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/popia-grace-period-limits-regulators-fines/P3gQ2qGAlAk7nRD1>) on the Department of Justice and Constitutional Development in July 2023.
