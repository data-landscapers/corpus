# Nigeria

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/donnees-nigeria-letat-reprend-la-ndpc-apres-plus-de-9-millions-de-recettes-generees-en-3-ans/>) was signed by President Tinubu on 12 June 2023. In July 2026 the [Federal High Court treated the Act as operative](<https://techtrends.africa/federal-high-court-affirms-ndpcs-authority-to-register-data-controllers-and-processors-of-major-importance/>) in a case against the Commission, and a [federal circular dated 27 July 2026](<https://www.thecable.ng/fg-directs-mdas-to-appoint-data-protection-officers-comply-with-ndp-act/>) directed all federal ministries, departments and agencies to comply with it.

**Malabo Convention.** Nigeria [signed the Malabo Convention on 23 January 2024](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and had not ratified it at the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** The Nigeria Data Protection Commission is a [full data protection authority under the Act](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/nigeria-targets-200-telecom-towers-by-december-plans-new-satellite-beyond-fibre-coverage/>), according to its National Commissioner on 14 September 2026, when its new Abuja headquarters was commissioned. [Voice of Nigeria](<https://von.gov.ng/ndpc-chief-calls-for-stronger-data-protection-practices/>) names Vincent Olatunji as National Commissioner and CEO.

**Implementing and acting.** The Commission [fined Multichoice Nigeria N766,242,500](<https://www.rapdp.org/index.php/en/node/222>) in 2025 for violating the Act. [Premium Times](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/913097-court-dismisses-25-million-privacy-suit-against-meta-in-imo.html>) reports its Final Order against Meta imposed a $32.8 million fine, later set aside when the Federal High Court adopted a settlement. In August 2026 it [opened an investigation](<https://dailytrust.com/ndpc-investigates-varsity-bank-over-data-violations/>) into the University of Lagos, Lotus Bank and Hackerbella Ltd. In February 2026 it [served a compliance notice on 649 higher-education institutions](<https://businessday.ng/bd-weekender/article/nigerias-data-protection-audit-return-blessing-udo-advocates-emulation/>). In October 2026 its National Commissioner said [41,000 organisations had registered with the Commission in 2026 and 362 compliance organisations were licensed](<https://businessday.ng/interview/article/protection-of-personal-privacy-and-rights-is-central-to-ndpcs-mandate-olatunji/>).
