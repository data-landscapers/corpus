# Somalia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Somalia's Data Protection Act, Law No. 005 of 2023, was [enacted on 23 March 2023](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations>) and became effective immediately, according to TechHive Advisory. In [June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>) the Authority's notice required registration under Article 32 of the Act from all entities that collect, store, process or share personal data in Somalia.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Somalia.

**Authority appointed.** Somalia [inaugurated its Data Protection Authority on 24 February 2024](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>). Its [own service page](<https://dpa.gov.so/services/registration-licensing/>) of 2 June 2026 lists the registration forms it issues, and its January 2026 [Registration Guidelines](<https://dpa.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Registration-Guidelines.pdf>) set fees and a 30-day approval timeline.

**Implementing and acting.** On 2 June 2026 the Authority [began accepting registration applications](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>) from controllers and processors and said it will issue certificates only to those meeting the conditions. In February 2024 it [published guidelines](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>) for implementing the law.
