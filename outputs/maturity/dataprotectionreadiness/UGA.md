# Uganda

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap 97, was [applied by the Personal Data Protection Office in its decision of 18 July 2025](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>) as law in operation, including its reach over foreign entities handling Ugandans' personal data. The [PDPO director says the Act requires registration](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>) of data collectors and processors and makes failure to register a criminal offence.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Uganda. [Police forensic-services director Andrew Mubiru said on 12 August 2026](<https://observer.ug/technology/african-cybersecurity-solutions-akin-to-glass-house-with-fancy-curtains-uganda-police/>) that Uganda has not ratified it and needs to domesticate it.

**Authority appointed.** The Personal Data Protection Office is [headed by director Baker Birikujja](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>), who addressed an ISACA Kampala breakfast on 18 August 2026. It [briefed government IT officers](<https://techreviewafrica.purplealmondconsulting.com/public/news/6726/uganda-personal-data-protection-office-engages-information-technology-officers-from-government-ministries>) on data protection on 13 August 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** The [PDPO's decision of 20 February 2026](<https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/>) partly upheld a complaint against WhatsApp LLC and Meta Platforms and issued corrective orders; on 17 July 2026 it confirmed WhatsApp had implemented them. On [18 July 2025 it found Google in breach](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>) and ordered it to register within 30 days. In July 2026 the Office [supervised more than 13,400 registered data controllers and processors](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>), and carried out inspections and investigations.
