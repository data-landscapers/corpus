# Eswatini

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Data Protection Act, 2022](<https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf>) was gazetted on 4 March 2022. The Ministry of ICT [referred to its implementation and enforcement as current](<https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/>) in August 2026, and [enforcement notices under section 41 of the Act](<https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html>) run to March 2025.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Eswatini. In July 2026 the [Minister of ICT presented to a Senators' workshop on the importance of ratifying it](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministry-of-ict-eswatini_the-minister-of-information-communications-activity-7481421459043770369-IcuH>).

**Authority appointed.** The [notice of 2024](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/EDPA%20NOTICE%201%202024%20-%20FINAL%20DECISION%20ON%20THE%20REGISTRATION%20OF%20DATA%20CONTROLLERS%20AND%20DATA%20PROCESSORS.pdf>) states the Act designates the Eswatini Communications Commission as the Eswatini Data Protection Authority. The Ministry of ICT [vests oversight of compliance in it](<https://times.co.sz/40740/news/new-regulations-to-crack-whip-on-online-media/>), and it has [powers to investigate breaches, resolve complaints and impose sanctions](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>). In September 2026 it was [led by an acting chief executive](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/edpa-takes-data-protection-services-closer-to-emaswati/>).

**Implementing and acting.** The Authority issued [enforcement notices under section 41 in June 2024 to MTN Eswatini and Liberty Life Swaziland Limited](<https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html>); [the notice to MTN Eswatini](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/EDPA%20NOTICE%203%202024%20-%20ENFORCEMENT%20NOTICE-MTN%20ESWATINI%20Final%20(002).pdf>) was a warning with eleven directed measures and [in March 2025 to the Royal Science Technology Park](<https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html>). Registration of controllers and processors [became mandatory on 1 March 2024](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/EDPA%20NOTICE%201%202024%20-%20FINAL%20DECISION%20ON%20THE%20REGISTRATION%20OF%20DATA%20CONTROLLERS%20AND%20DATA%20PROCESSORS.pdf>). Its [transfer form](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/APPLICATION%20FORM_%20TRANSFER.pdf>) bars unregistered organisations from transferring personal data. In June 2026 it [opened a formal investigation into the Ministry of Home Affairs](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>).
