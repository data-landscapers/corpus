# Guinea

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [2016 cybersecurity and personal data protection law, Loi L/2016/037/AN](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l2016037an_relative_a_la_cybersecurite_et_protection_des_do.pdf>), was adopted and promulgated by President Alpha Condé, and [the ministry's secretary-general said in October 2025](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>) that it is in place. At the [closing of the Transform Africa Summit on 19 November 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>), Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said Guinea is strengthening its regulatory framework with a new data-protection law.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Guinea ratified the Malabo Convention on 31 July 2018 and deposited its instrument on 16 October 2018. A [presidential decree of 26 July 2017](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ratification.jpg>) had ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** The 2016 law [defines an Autorité de Protection and sets its functions](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>), including declarations, transfer approval and investigation. The CNT reported on [20 June 2024](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>) that its commissions debated a bill to establish the authority, with the designation of members and their mandates still contested.

**Implementing and acting.** The bill to establish the authority was awaiting plenary adoption, the [CNT reported on 20 June 2024](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>).

***Also on file***. [Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (APDP)](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>); [Loi L/2016/037/AN relative à la cybersécurité et la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>); [Autorité de Protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>); [Centre de sécurité des systèmes d'information (CERT)](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

***Not found***. No authority constituted or appointed has been found, and no source dated in the last three years shows one registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
