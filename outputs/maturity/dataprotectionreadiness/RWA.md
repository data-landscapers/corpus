# Rwanda

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Rwanda's [Data Protection Act was introduced on 15 October 2021](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>), and its two-year grace period ended on 15 October 2023, marking the transition to enforcement and accountability, according to a legal adviser writing in The New Times.

**Malabo Convention.** Rwanda [ratified the Malabo Convention on 14 November 2019](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 21 November 2019, according to the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** The National Cyber Security Authority [acts as the supervisory authority](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) for personal data protection and privacy under the Data Protection Act, as Rwanda observed Data Privacy Week in January 2026. In July 2026 [CIPESA](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>) stated that Rwanda's AI policy positions the Authority to oversee data protection compliance relevant to AI systems. Template clauses published in [February 2024](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>) name it the supervisory authority under Law No 058/2021, receiving breach reports and settling disputes.

**Implementing and acting.** [The New Times](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) reported in January 2026 that over the last four years the Authority has registered data controllers and processors with registration certificates, trained over 100 public sector data protection officers, and run sector workshops for pharmacists and local government entities. In [February 2024 its Data Protection and Privacy Office published template standard contractual clauses](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>) for transfers abroad under Article 49 of Law No 058/2021, which also set 72-hour breach reporting to the authority.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Act (introduced 15 October 2021)](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>); [National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA)](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
