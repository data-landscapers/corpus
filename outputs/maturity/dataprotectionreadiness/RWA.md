# Rwanda

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Rwanda's [Data Protection Act was introduced on 15 October 2021](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). Its two-year grace period ended on 15 October 2023, a legal adviser wrote in The New Times. [Brookings also dates the law to 15 October 2021](<https://www.brookings.edu/articles/rwandas-data-governance-navigating-data-governance-in-the-public-sector/>).

**Malabo Convention.** Rwanda [ratified the Malabo Convention on 14 November 2019](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>). It deposited its instrument on 21 November 2019, the African Union's status list shows.

**Authority appointed.** The National Cyber Security Authority [acts as the supervisory authority](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) for data protection under the Act. [Brookings described its role in enforcing data protection law in 2024](<https://www.brookings.edu/articles/rwandas-data-governance-navigating-data-governance-in-the-public-sector/>). In July 2026 [CIPESA](<https://cipesa.org/2026/07/rethinking-africas-approach-to-the-politics-of-ai-governance-and-regulation/>) said Rwanda's AI policy gives it oversight of data protection in AI systems. Template clauses published in [February 2024](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>) name it the supervisory authority under Law No 058/2021. It receives breach reports and settles disputes.

**Implementing and acting.** [The New Times](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) reported in January 2026 that the Authority has registered data controllers and processors over four years. It has trained over 100 public sector data protection officers. It has also run workshops for pharmacists and local government. In [February 2024 its Data Protection and Privacy Office published template contractual clauses](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/DPO/ComplianceTools/-_Standard_Contractual_Clauses_for_Transfer_Personal_Data_Outside_Rwanda_.pdf>) for transfers abroad. Law No 058/2021 also sets 72-hour breach reporting to the Authority.
