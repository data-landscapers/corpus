# Lesotho

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Cenfri says](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) that Lesotho's Data Protection Act has been law for over a decade, in a document published on 23 March 2026.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records that Lesotho ratified or acceded to the Malabo Convention on 5 February 2025 and deposited its instrument on 16 February 2025.

**Authority appointed.** [Cenfri says](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) that the Data Protection Commission under Lesotho's Data Protection Act has remained unappointed, in its document published on 23 March 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** The law firm Z. Mayet and Associates [states](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) that the Act establishes a Data Protection Commission, but that the Commission and its Commissioner have not been appointed, in a document published on 31 October 2025.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Commission](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows an appointed authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
