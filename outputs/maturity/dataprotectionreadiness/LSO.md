# Lesotho

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Lesotho [enacted its Data Protection Act in 2011](<https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/exploring-the-data-protection-and-data-governance-challenges-and-opportunities-in-lesotho/>), and a March 2026 article calls it the cornerstone of the framework. [Cenfri says](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) the Act has been law for over a decade.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records that Lesotho ratified or acceded to the Malabo Convention on 5 February 2025. It deposited its instrument on 16 February 2025.

**Authority appointed.** The Act provides for a Data Protection Commission. [Cenfri says](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>) it had not been appointed by 23 March 2026. An [article of 27 March 2026](<https://digitalrightsalliance.africa/exploring-the-data-protection-and-data-governance-challenges-and-opportunities-in-lesotho/>) says no independent body exists to enforce the law.

**Implementing and acting.** The law firm Z. Mayet and Associates [states](<https://zmayetlaw.co.ls/lesothos-outdated-data-protection-act-no-commissioner-no-teeth-what-should-change-next/>) that the Commission had not been appointed by 31 October 2025. Nor had its Commissioner.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection Commission](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows an appointed authority at work. A March 2026 article says Lesotho has signed the Malabo Convention but not ratified it, against the African Union's record.
