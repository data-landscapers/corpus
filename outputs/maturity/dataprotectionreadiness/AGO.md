# Angola

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Lei n.º 22/11 on personal data protection was [published on 17 June 2011](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/>) and enters into force on the date of its publication. In [June 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/parlamento-rejeita-proposta-sobre-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>) the Assembleia Nacional rejected a bill to amend it.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Angola ratified the Convention on 21 February 2020 and deposited its instrument on 11 May 2020.

**Authority appointed.** The Agência de Protecção de Dados was set up by [Decreto Presidencial n.º 214/16](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/>) and [Decreto Presidencial n.º 277/19 of 6 September 2019](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2019/decreto-presidencial-n-o-277-19-de-06-de-setembro/>) appointed a president and two executive administrators to its board. In [August 2026](<https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b>) its board took a sanction decision.

**Implementing and acting.** On [10 August 2026](<https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b>) the authority fined Lizária, Lda., owner of Hotel Diamante, the kwanza equivalent of USD 115,000 under Deliberação n.º 006/2026. Its [register of fines](<https://apd.ao/ao/gca/index.php?id=218&preview=1>) lists 15 fines for 2022 to 2026, of US$75,000 to US$525,000 each.
