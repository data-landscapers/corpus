# The Gambia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** President Barrow [assented the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025 on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>), with no grace period before it becomes enforceable and no commencement date given.

**Malabo Convention.** The Gambia [signed the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection on 2 December 2022](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>) and the minister reiterated the government's commitment to ratify it. In April 2024 the minister told a UN commission that [the government ratified it in March 2024](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf>); the [African Union's list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no ratification.

**Authority appointed.** The [Information Commission of The Gambia is to develop a communication strategy](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>) raising awareness of the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025, per a notice published on 17 August 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** [Paradigm Initiative says the Gambia enacted data protection legislation in September 2025](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-applauds-countries-with-data-protection-laws/>) and calls for implementation and enforcement by the relevant authorities, in a press release dated 29 January 2026.
