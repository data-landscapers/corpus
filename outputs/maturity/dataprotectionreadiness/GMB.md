# The Gambia

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Data Protection and Privacy Bill, 2024 was [committed to the National Assembly's ICT committee](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/na-commits-data-protection-privacy-bill-to-committee>) on 10 December 2024. President Barrow [assented to the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025 on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>). No grace period and no commencement date are given.

**Malabo Convention.** The Gambia [signed the Convention on 2 December 2022](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>). In April 2024 the minister told a UN commission that [the government ratified it in March 2024](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf>). In December 2024 a minister [told the Assembly the Gambia is a signatory](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/na-commits-data-protection-privacy-bill-to-committee>). The [African Union's list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records signature and no ratification.

**Authority appointed.** A notice of 17 August 2026 says the [Information Commission of The Gambia is to develop a communication strategy](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>). The strategy is to raise awareness of the 2025 Act.

**Implementing and acting.** In January 2026 [Paradigm Initiative said the Gambia enacted data protection legislation in September 2025](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-applauds-countries-with-data-protection-laws/>). It called on the relevant authorities to implement and enforce it.

***Also on file***. [Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act 2025](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>). [Commission under the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>). [Information Commission of The Gambia](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>).

***Not found***. A commencement date for the Act, and any ruling against an organisation.
