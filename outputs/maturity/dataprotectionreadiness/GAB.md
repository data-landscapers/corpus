# Gabon

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Loi n°025/2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>) of 12 July 2023 amended the 2011 data protection law. It added rights, controller duties, data protection officers, breach notification and sanctions. It was published in the Journal Officiel on 15 July 2023.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature of the Malabo Convention by Gabon. It records no ratification either.

**Authority appointed.** The APDPVP's president, vice-president and six other commissioners were [appointed by the Council of Ministers on 12 July 2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>). Its president, Joel Dominique Ledaga, [gave its review of 2025](<https://www.gabonreview.com/journee-mondiale-de-la-protection-des-donnees-le-gabon-affirme-sa-strategie-face-aux-defis-du-numerique/>) on 27 January 2026. He [spoke as its sitting head](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>) on 4 September 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** In April 2024 the APDPVP [fined Citibank Gabon S.A. 90,000,000 FCFA](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>) for misusing employee data. Its president [counted 12 administrative and financial sanctions](<https://www.gabonreview.com/journee-mondiale-de-la-protection-des-donnees-le-gabon-affirme-sa-strategie-face-aux-defis-du-numerique/>) on controllers in breach in 2025. In December 2025 he [demanded the suspension of a data-sharing operation](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) between the social security fund, the health insurance fund and the digital infrastructure agency. By September 2026 it had logged [about 715 declarations of conformity and about 30 complaints](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).

***Also on file***. [Loi relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).

***Not found***. Nothing is outstanding on the four questions.
