# Gabon

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Loi n°025/2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>) of 12 July 2023 amended the 2011 data protection law with rights, controller duties, data protection officers, breach notification and sanctions, and was published in the Journal Officiel on 15 July 2023.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Gabon.

**Authority appointed.** The APDPVP sat in plenary on 25 April 2024 with a named president, vice-president and six other permanent commissioners, [appointed by a Council of Ministers decision of 12 July 2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>); its president, Joel Dominique Ledaga, [spoke as its sitting head](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>) on 4 September 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** By deliberation n°073 of 25 April 2024, the APDPVP [imposed a sanction of 90,000,000 FCFA on Citibank Gabon S.A.](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>) for diverting employee data from its declared purpose and processing ethnic-origin data. Its president said in September 2026 it had logged [about 715 declarations of conformity and about 30 complaints from individuals](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).

***Also on file***. [Loi relative à la protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).

***Not found***. Nothing is outstanding on the four questions.
