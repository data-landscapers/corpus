# Mauritania

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Law 2017-020 of 22 July 2017 on personal data protection is applied in the authority's [deliberation 002/2026 of 24 March 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>). [Deliberation 005/2025 of 30 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/>) is issued under it and requires controllers using foreign cloud services to seek authorisation.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union status list of 2 February 2026 records that [Mauritania ratified the Malabo Convention on 19 April 2023](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 9 May 2023.

**Authority appointed.** The Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (APD) held its [ordinary monthly session on 30 June 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-18/>) under its president, with its members and the Government Commissioner present. Its [site lists monthly meetings of 25 March, 29 April and 6 May 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/>), and its [president was sworn in for a second term on 7 October 2026](<https://rimweb.net/2026/10/08/le-president-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-prete-serment-pour-un-second-mandat/>).

**Implementing and acting.** On 24 March 2026 the APD [gave a favourable opinion, conditional on its remarks](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>), on the joint decision authorising Maaden Mauritanie's Maadeni system. At its [29 April 2026 meeting](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-16/>) it approved amendments to its transfer and cloud deliberations, and on [19 November 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-13/>) it examined complaints 004-2025, 006-2025 and 007-2025.
