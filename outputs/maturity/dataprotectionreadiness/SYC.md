# Seychelles

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>) was assented to on 19 December 2023 and bears the operative date 22 December 2023. [The Commission's page](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection/>) states the Act came into effect, and [Appleby reports](<https://www.applebyglobal.com/publications/data-protection-law-now-in-force-in-seychelles>) an 18-month transition for controllers and processors from that date.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Seychelles.

**Authority appointed.** The [Information Commission](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>) is named in section 5 as the competent authority, with powers to investigate, audit, issue enforcement notices and impose administrative fines up to SCR200,000. In 2025 it had [a Chief Information Commissioner, two Information Commissioners and a CEO in post](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>), and it [took on the mandate at the end of December 2023](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>).

**Implementing and acting.** The Commission [handled 14 cases under the Act in 2025](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>): 6 on data transfers, 5 breaches, 2 refused deletion requests and 1 consultation. In 2024 it [received four data protection cases](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Information-Commission-Annual-Report-2024.pdf>), mostly from the non-banking financial sector. In 2026 it [marked Data Protection Day with guidance to organisations and the public](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection-day-2026-building-trust-in-a-digital-seychelles/>).

***Also on file***. [Information Commission Seychelles](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/publications/reports/information-commission-seychelles-annual-report-2025>); [Data Protection Act, 2023](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection/>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
