# Cabo Verde

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [The CNPD president says](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) the base data protection law dates from 2001. It was amended in 2013 and again [on 17 March 2021](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>). The [IBA chapter](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde>) names Law 133/V/2001 as the general regime. It applies to processing in the country and to data of people located there.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Cabo Verde ratified the Malabo Convention on 13 November 2020. It deposited its instrument on 5 February 2022.

**Authority appointed.** The CNPD's members [took office on 24 April 2015](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>), and it was declared installed on 10 December 2015. It is [led by president Faustino Varela](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). [Resolução n.º 160/X/2024](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>) gives it CVE 36,313,115 for 2025, up from CVE 35,255,451 in 2024.

**Implementing and acting.** The CNPD says that [from 2015 to 2025 it issued thousands of authorisations](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>) and hundreds of opinions, and received hundreds of complaints. [Its president says](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) cases on AI and Big Data have arrived, two of them on generative-AI image manipulation. Cases that may be criminal go to the Ministério Público. Its [2024 activity plan](<https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNPD-Plano-de-Atividades-2024.pdf>) covers planned inspections and guidance.

***Also on file***. [Regime Jurídico Geral de Protecção de Dados](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). [Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD)](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=CV&t=law>). [Lei de Proteção de Dados (2001/2021)](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/opiniao/2025/04/07/revolucao-tecnologica/96431>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
