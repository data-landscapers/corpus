# Cabo Verde

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [The CNPD president says](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) the base data protection law dates from 2001 and was amended in 2013 and in 2021, in an interview published 2 February 2025. The [IBA chapter](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde>) states Law 133/V/2001 is Cabo Verde's general data protection regime, applying within the country and to processing of data of people located in it.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Cape Verde ratified the Malabo Convention on 13 November 2020 and deposited its instrument on 5 February 2022.

**Authority appointed.** The CNPD, [led by president Faustino Varela, started in 2015-2016](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) and completes a decade of existence in 2025. [Resolução n.º 160/X/2024](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>) appropriates CVE 36,313,115 for the CNPD in the Assembly's own budget for FY2025, up from CVE 35,255,451 in FY2024.

**Implementing and acting.** [The CNPD president says](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) cases have arrived involving AI and Big Data, including two generative-AI image manipulation cases, with cases that may be criminal sent to the Ministério Público; no decision or count is given. The CNPD's [2024 activity plan](<https://www.cnpd.cv/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/CNPD-Plano-de-Atividades-2024.pdf>), dated 27 December 2023, covers planned inspections, guidance and international fora.

***Also on file***. [Regime Jurídico Geral de Protecção de Dados](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>), [Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD)](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=CV&t=law>) and [Lei de Proteção de Dados (2001/2021)](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/opiniao/2025/04/07/revolucao-tecnologica/96431>).

***Not found***. No ruling of the authority against a named organisation in the last three years has been found.
