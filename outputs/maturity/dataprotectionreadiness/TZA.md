# Tanzania

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Personal Data Protection Act, Chapter 44, commenced on 1 May 2023](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>) and applies to Mainland Tanzania and, for union matters, Zanzibar. The Citizen [reported on 28 August 2026](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>) that the Commission will inspect compliance with the Act from 31 August 2026.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Tanzania.

**Authority appointed.** [Section 6 of the Act](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>) establishes the Personal Data Protection Commission as a body corporate. [Director General Dr Emmanuel Mkilia announced on-site inspections](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>) in Dodoma on 28 August 2026. The Commission [ran the eighth phase of its DPO training](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2026/10/pdpc-bolsters-safeguards-as-personal.html>) in Morogoro on 7-8 October 2026.

**Implementing and acting.** [PDPC figures cited in October 2026](<https://www.therespondents.co.tz/2026/10/pdpc-bolsters-safeguards-as-personal.html>) show 15,146 institutions registered and 445 complaints received by August 2026. A [ruling reported in August 2025](<https://www.rive.co.tz/landmark-ruling-first-pdpc-monetary-award-signals-new-era-for-personal-data-in-tanzania/>) awarded TZS 20,000,000 against Mi Casa Company Limited and ordered deletion of personal data. [FB Attorneys reports](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/instagram-photo-triggers-payment-of-fine/>) a ruling of 19 August 2025 with TZS 20 million damages, a TZS 5 million fine and deletion of a newborn's photos within 14 days.
