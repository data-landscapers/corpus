# Zimbabwe

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] is applied through SI 155 of 2024, which [took effect in September 2024](<https://techunzipped.co.zw/2026/08/potraz-to-begin-mandatory-data-protection-compliance-inspections-from-1-september-2026/>) and made a Data Controller Licence mandatory. [Business Times](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/>) states the Act provides criminal sanctions for accountable officers.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Zimbabwe.

**Authority appointed.** [POTRAZ states](<https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/08/17/potraz-sets-september-deadline-for-data-protection-compliance>) it is the designated Data Protection Authority under section 5 of the Act and is acting as such. [Section 6(2)](<https://www.ictministry.gov.zw/assets/documents/Cyber-and-Data-Protection-Act-Chapter-1207.pdf>) says it is not subject to the direction or control of any person in exercising its functions.

**Implementing and acting.** POTRAZ [handed over the first Data Controller Licences to 570 companies](<https://technomag.co.zw/570-institutions-receive-data-controllers-licences-to-bolster-digital-trust-and-compliance/>) in June 2025. In December 2025 it [reported 831 organisations recognised as compliant](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/zimbabwe-to-guide-southern-africa-in-data-protection-capacity-building>). Its [Regulatory Notice 2 of 2026](<https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/08/17/potraz-sets-september-deadline-for-data-protection-compliance>) announces mandatory compliance inspections from 1 September 2026. A [September 2026 account](<https://sirdc.ac.zw/the-inaugural-africa-data-protection-day/>) says POTRAZ held a national consultative workshop on guidelines under the Act and on amendments to SI 155 of 2024 on administrative fines.
