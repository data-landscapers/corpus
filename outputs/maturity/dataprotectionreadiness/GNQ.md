# Equatorial Guinea

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Ley Núm. 1/2016 of 22 July 2016](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>) sets rules for personal data processing in the public and private sectors and enters into force twenty days after its publication in the Boletín Oficial del Estado.

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union's Infrastructure and Energy Department reports that [Equatorial Guinea's foreign minister signed the Malabo Convention in Addis Ababa on 28 July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI>), as a step towards ratification.

**Authority appointed.** [Articles 15 and 29 name the Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales as the supervisory body](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>), to be created by a decree that will fix its structure.

**Implementing and acting.** [Data Protection Africa (ALT Advisory) states that Law No. 1/2016 creates the Governing Body for the Protection of Personal Data as the data protection authority, but that it is not yet appointed or operational](<https://dataprotection.africa/equatorial-guinea/>).

***Also on file***. [Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales](<https://dataprotection.africa/equatorial-guinea/>); [Ley Núm. 1/2016, de Protección de Datos Personales](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>).

***Not found***. Ratification of the Malabo Convention is not held, nor is any appointment to the authority or any ruling against an organisation.
