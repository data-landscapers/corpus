# Djibouti

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The National Assembly passed Djibouti's first [Digital Code](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>), with a data-protection regime embedded in it, on 30 June 2025. It was still to be promulgated by the President and published in the Journal Officiel. [TechHive Advisory](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>) describes the regime as 156 articles in all, within the Code.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) of 2 February 2026 records that Djibouti signed the Convention on 12 May 2023 and had not ratified it.

**Authority appointed.** The Code creates the [National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>), independent with administrative and financial autonomy. [Jones Day](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>) frames the competent authorities as still to be operationalised.

**Implementing and acting.** [TechHive Advisory](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>) states that the regime was enacted in June 2025 as part of the Digital Code, and gives no promulgation or commencement date.

***Also on file***. The [Digital Code](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>) and the [National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>).

***Not found***. The date the Code was promulgated or took effect, whether the commission has been constituted, and any ruling by it.
