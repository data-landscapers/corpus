# Kenya

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner [enforces the Data Protection Act and Regulations](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). It has issued fines under them since 2022. In a [Senate statement on 13 May 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary said the Data Protection Act, 2019 is being enforced](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>).

**Malabo Convention.** [Citizen Digital reported on 8 September 2026 that Kenya had not yet formally joined the Malabo Convention](<https://citizen.digital/article/stakeholders-urge-ratification-of-malabo-convention-for-effective-digital-governance-in-kenya-n389789>). Cabinet approved it in 2025. Ratification awaits National Assembly approval, and the [outgoing Data Commissioner named it as unfinished business in November 2026](<https://capitalfm.africa/kassait-making-data-protection-a-national-conversation-was-my-key-achievement/>).

**Authority appointed.** The Office was [established under the Data Protection Act, 2019, with Immaculate Kassait as its first commissioner from 2020](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>). [Capital FM reported on 10 November 2026 that she is leaving after six years](<https://capitalfm.africa/kassait-making-data-protection-a-national-conversation-was-my-key-achievement/>). The [ODPC bulletin of March 2025 shows the Office registering data handlers](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>) from seven regional offices, Nairobi among them.

**Implementing and acting.** By March 2025 the Office had [received 7,128 complaints, issued 215 determinations and penalised 10 entities](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>). [Cadmus Cyber Solutions counts KES 26,275,000 in fines on at least nine businesses by September 2024](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>).

***Also on file***. [ODPC draft guidance note on AI and cross-border transfers](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/regulator-plans-tighter-data-shields-on-offshore-ai-platforms-5546966>). [Data Protection Act, 2019](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>). [Office of the Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AI-Guidance-Note.July-2026.pdf>). [Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-28-odpc-gives-data-handlers-14-days-to-renew-expired-certificates>). [Data Protection Regulations (2021)](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>).

***Not found***. A completed ratification of the Malabo Convention.
