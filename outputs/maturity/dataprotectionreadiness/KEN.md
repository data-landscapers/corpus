# Kenya

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner [enforces the Data Protection Act and Regulations](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>) and has issued fines under it since 2022, and in a [Senate statement on 13 May 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary said the Data Protection Act, 2019 is being enforced](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>).

**Malabo Convention.** [Citizen Digital reported on 8 September 2026 that Kenya had not yet formally joined the Malabo Convention](<https://citizen.digital/article/stakeholders-urge-ratification-of-malabo-convention-for-effective-digital-governance-in-kenya-n389789>): Cabinet approved it in 2025 and ratification awaits National Assembly approval.

**Authority appointed.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was [established under the Data Protection Act, 2019, with inaugural commissioner Immaculate Kassait in office since 2020](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>); the [ODPC bulletin of March 2025 shows the Office registering data handlers](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>) from offices in Nairobi, Kisumu, Mombasa, Eldoret, Machakos, Nyeri and Nakuru.

**Implementing and acting.** The [ODPC bulletin states that as of March 2025 the Office had received 7,128 complaints, issued 215 determinations and imposed penalties on 10 entities](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>), and [Cadmus Cyber Solutions states the Office had fined at least nine businesses KES 26,275,000 in total by September 2024](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>).

***Also on file***. [ODPC draft guidance note on AI and cross-border transfers](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/regulator-plans-tighter-data-shields-on-offshore-ai-platforms-5546966>); [Data Protection Act, 2019](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>); [Office of the Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/AI-Guidance-Note.July-2026.pdf>); [Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-28-odpc-gives-data-handlers-14-days-to-renew-expired-certificates>); [Data Protection Regulations (2021)](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>).

***Not found***. A completed ratification of the Malabo Convention.
