# Mozambique

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [DLA Piper states](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=MZ>), in a guide last modified on 17 February 2026, that Mozambique has no specific data protection legislation. The World Bank says the Data Protection Act had [not been approved by Cabinet or submitted to Parliament as of October 2025](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>), and on 1 October 2026 the Assembly's Standing Commission [placed a draft law on the agenda of the session opening 28 October](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-convenes-extraordinary-and-ordinary-sessions-for-october/>).

**Malabo Convention.** The African Union status list of 2 February 2026 records that [Mozambique ratified the Malabo Convention on 2 December 2019](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and deposited its instrument on 21 January 2020.

**Authority appointed.** A June 2025 inception report states that [Mozambique has no data regulatory authority](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). A [review of the draft law](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-mozambiques-proposed-personal-data-protection-law>) says it designates the existing Regulatory Authority for ICT as the National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data.

**Implementing and acting.** The World Bank's October 2025 paper says the [Data Protection Office is not yet established](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>), which prevents its operationalisation, and that the project envelope for it was cut from US$18m to US$11m.

***Also on file***. [Personal Data Protection draft law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-convenes-extraordinary-and-ordinary-sessions-for-october/>); [National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data (ANPD)](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-mozambiques-proposed-personal-data-protection-law>); [Data Protection Office](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>); [Electronic Transactions Law (Law 3/2017)](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).

***Not found***. A law in force, an appointed authority and any ruling are not held.
