# Sudan

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** On 6 August 2026 the Council of Ministers [discussed a draft data and artificial intelligence bill](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) and directed further study before approval by the interim parliament. On 11 August 2026 the Justice Ministry's under-secretary [said the ministry is preparing a 2026 Law on Regulating Data and Artificial Intelligence](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>) with the digital transformation ministry. The [Sudanese Center for Policy Studies](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>) says Sudan has no privacy law governing personal data and that a 2018 draft bill was never enacted.

**Malabo Convention.** Sudan [signed the Malabo Convention on 15 March 2023](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) and had not ratified it at the African Union's status list of 2 February 2026.

**Authority appointed.** The [Sudanese Center for Policy Studies](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>) says the Sudanese Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority and the Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority were both established by a November 2025 decree.

**Implementing and acting.** According to the [same Center](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>), citing the AI Regulation Tracker, neither of those two authorities had issued binding rules as of mid-2026.
