# Ghana

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-begins-checks-on-data-protection-compliance/>) is the law the Data Protection Commission enforces in 2026. A [Circuit Court in Accra applied it](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>) in a judgment of 18 September 2024.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Ghana ratified the Malabo Convention on 13 May 2019. It deposited its instrument on 3 June 2019.

**Authority appointed.** Executive Director Dr Arnold Kavaarpuo leads the Data Protection Commission, [according to the Ghana News Agency in October 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-begins-checks-on-data-protection-compliance/>). A [new Governing Board](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/>) chaired by Teki Akuetteh was sworn in in August 2025. The [2026 budget estimates](<https://mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/pbb-estimates/2026/2026-PBB-MOCD.pdf>) appropriate GHS 9,720,400 to the Commission, which has 39 staff.

**Implementing and acting.** In [August 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/data-protection-commission-acquires-vehicles-to-boost-nationwide-enforcement/>) the Commission declared 2026 the "Year of Enforcement" and said enforcement actions had begun. It [registered 2,575 data controllers in 2026](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-strengthens-enforcement-after-registering-2575-data-controllers/>) and is running inspections. On 12 October 2026 it [directed Nyaho Medical Centre to report to the Police CID](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/nyaho-medical-centre-referred-to-cid-over-allegedly-expired-data-protection-registration/>) over an expired registration certificate. The Communications Minister reported a [98.4 per cent rise in newly registered data controllers](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-records-sharp-rise-in-regulatory-activity-sam-george/>) in the first half of 2026. On 20 October 2026 the Commission [warned a ministry and media houses](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-warns-against-disclosure-of-evacuated-ghanaians-personal-data/>) over disclosing evacuees' personal data.
