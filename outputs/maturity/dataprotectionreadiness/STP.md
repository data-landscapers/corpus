# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** The [IBA chapter of 2024](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>) names the Personal Data Guarantee and Protection Law, Law 03/2016, as the primary instrument, covering processing by controllers headquartered or established in the country. [Lei n.º 03/2016](<https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/STP%20-%20Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2003_2016%20-%20Lei%20de%20Garantia%20e%20Protec%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20dos%20Dados%20Pessoais%20d....pdf>) was published in the Diário da República of 10 May 2016.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that São Tomé and Príncipe ratified or acceded on 25 September 2023 and deposited its instrument on 15 February 2024.

**Authority appointed.** The Agência Nacional de Protecção de Dados Pessoais has a president, José Manuel Costa Alegre. In April 2026 he [met the National Assembly president](<https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>) to plan oversight of personal data in the electoral roll, and on 1 June 2026 the agency [signed a cooperation memorandum with Macau](<https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/>).

**Implementing and acting.** The [IBA chapter](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>) describes the fines and criminal sanctions the law provides and says the agency imposes fines and ancillary measures. In January 2025 its president [described working with Facebook](<https://rstp.st/2025/01/29/agencia-de-protecao-de-dados-trabalha-com-o-facebook-para-eliminar-perfis-falsos-em-stp/>) on fake profiles.

***Also on file***. [Agência Nacional de Protecção de Dados Pessoais (ANPDP)](<https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows the authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting. No ruling against an organisation has been found.
