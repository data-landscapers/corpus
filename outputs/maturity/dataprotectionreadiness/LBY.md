# Libya

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Libya's National AI Strategy, adopted on 1 June 2026, concedes that the Data Protection and Privacy Law is still in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), and sets its adoption as a 2026 target under the strategy's second pillar. Until it passes, [no general law governs the collection and use of personal data](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification by Libya, so the Convention's data protection rules do not bind it either.

**Authority appointed.** [SMEX reported in July 2026 that Libya has no functioning data protection authority](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). The draft law, [not yet adopted](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), is the instrument that would create one.

**Implementing and acting.** None: with the law [still a draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>) and [no authority in place](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), no body registers organisations, takes breach reports, issues guidance or rules on complaints.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No data protection law in force has been found, and no authority.
