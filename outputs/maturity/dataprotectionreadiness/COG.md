# Congo

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** [Law No. 29-2019 of 10 October 2019 on personal data protection](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>) is in force as of 22 January 2026, and Decree No. 2025-538 completes the institutional framework it established.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records that Congo ratified the Malabo Convention on 24 September 2020 and deposited its instrument on 23 October 2020.

**Authority appointed.** The National Personal Data Protection Commission was inaugurated in January 2026 after its members were [appointed under Decree No. 2025-538 of 31 December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>). Its President, Vice President and Rapporteur were elected at the inaugural meeting.

**Implementing and acting.** On 22 January 2026 [ITWeb Africa reported](<https://itweb.africa/article/congo-brazzaville-intros-data-protection-commission/>) that Congo-Brazzaville has established the Commission, whose members were installed by ICT minister Leon Juste Ibombo, who described it as an independent body able to advise, warn and sanction.

***Also on file***. [National Personal Data Protection Commission](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>).

***Not found***. No source dated in the last three years shows an appointed authority registering organisations, taking breach reports, issuing guidance or inspecting.
