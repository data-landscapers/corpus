# Burundi

## Data protection system

**Law in force.** Burundi's National Assembly unanimously [adopted a bill on personal data protection on 15 January 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates>), covering the collection and processing of personal data across public and private sectors. It is [structured in eight chapters](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/>), and the head of state [promulgated the law on 10 March 2026](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>), per Community Voice.

**Malabo Convention.** The [African Union's status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records no signature and no ratification of the Malabo Convention by Burundi.

**Authority appointed.** The law [establishes an independent administrative authority](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>) to oversee data protection, [responsible for enforcement](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-lawmakers-approve-data-protection-framework-as-digitalisation-accelerates>), and [a dedicated body to implement and monitor data protection regimes](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/>).

**Implementing and acting.** [DLA Piper's guide of 17 January 2024](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?c=BI&t=law>) described only sector confidentiality provisions in Burundi, before the 2026 law was adopted, and stated that no national data protection authority existed then.

***Also on file***. [Burundi personal data protection law](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>); [independent data-protection authority](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/16/burundi-adopts-landmark-personal-data-protection-law-to-safeguard-citizens-in-the-digital-era/>).

***Not found***. Whether the law has entered into force, whether the authority has been constituted and whether it has ruled on any organisation are not established.
