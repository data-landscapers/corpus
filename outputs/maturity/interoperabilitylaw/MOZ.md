# Mozambique

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Mozambique's [Decree 67/2017 of 1 December 2017](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>) approves the regulation of the e-government interoperability framework. The Council of Ministers made it under the Electronic Transactions Law. It applies to every institution of the public administration and took effect 180 days after publication. The regulator [described a review in December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/05/mozambique-prioritizes-data-governance-as-cornerstone-of-digital-sovereignty/>). A revised regulation [went to its final consultation on 11 June 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/>). On 23 October 2026 the regulator [presented the proposed revision of the decree](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/intic-instituto-nacional-de-tecnologias-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-e-comunica%C3%A7%C3%A3o_revis%C3%A3o-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-refor%C3%A7a-activity-7520792996766699521-sTTA>), still not adopted.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The decree [obliges public administration bodies to share the data they hold and to reuse data held by other state bodies](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>). They must reuse data a citizen has already supplied through the interoperability platform, and must use the single platform. The revised regulation had not been adopted by late October 2026. No rule made under the decree since 2023 is recorded. No body is named as exchanging data because the decree requires it.
