# Mozambique

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Mozambique's [Decree 67/2017 of 1 December 2017](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>), made by the Council of Ministers under the Electronic Transactions Law, approves the regulation of the e-government interoperability framework. It applies to every institution of the public administration and took effect 180 days after publication. A revised regulation [went to its final consultation at a national workshop on 11 June 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/decorre-a-fase-final-da-revisao-do-regulamento-do-quadro-de-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-de-governo-digital-em-mocambique/>), and the regulator [described the review in December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/05/mozambique-prioritizes-data-governance-as-cornerstone-of-digital-sovereignty/>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The decree [obliges public administration bodies to share the data they hold and to reuse data held by other state bodies](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_188_I_SERIE_2017.pdf>). They must reuse data a citizen has already supplied through the interoperability platform, and must use the single platform. The revised regulation had not been approved by the end of September 2026, so no rule made under the decree since 2023 is recorded, and no body is named as exchanging data because it requires them to.
