# Benin

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Benin's [Decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>), issued by the Presidency, sets up XRoad BJ, the national interoperability platform through which public bodies exchange data. The decree is the founding instrument of the platform, and no later text is recorded as amending or replacing it.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The [2020 decree](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>) is recorded as setting up the platform, with nothing on an obligation for ministries and agencies to connect to it or to exchange their data through it. No regulation or order made under the decree since 2023 is recorded, and no public body is named as exchanging data because the decree requires it. The platform is run for the Presidency by the agency for information systems and digital technology.

***Also on file***. [XRoad BJ](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
