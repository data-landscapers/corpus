# Benin

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Benin's [Decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>) was issued by the Presidency. It sets up XRoad BJ, the national interoperability platform through which public bodies exchange data. The decree is the founding instrument of the platform. No later text is recorded as amending or replacing it.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The [2020 decree](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>) is recorded as setting up the platform. Nothing in it is recorded on an obligation for ministries and agencies to connect to it or to exchange their data through it. No regulation or order made under the decree since 2023 is recorded. No public body is named as exchanging data because the decree requires it. The agency for information systems and digital technology runs the platform for the Presidency.

***Also on file***. [XRoad BJ](<https://api.xroad.bj/media/617ac98a74baa_D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02020-209%20du%2018%20mars%202020%20portant%20mise%20en%20place%20de%20la%20Plateforme%20d%27Interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9%20XRoad%20BJ%20(1).pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data. None found says it requires them to connect to the government's exchange platform. None found says it bars them from asking a person for what another public body already holds.
