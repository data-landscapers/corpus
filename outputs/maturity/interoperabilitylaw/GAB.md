# Gabon

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Gabon's [Ordinance 0006/PR/2025 regulating digitalisation](<https://journal-officiel.ga/21995-0006-pr-2025-/>) was signed by the President on 12 August 2025 and published in the Official Journal on 30 August 2025. It applies to all services of the state and to public and private bodies, and [sets a legal framework for e-government for the first time](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The ordinance [makes it compulsory for administrations to interconnect](<https://www.gabonreview.com/e-gouvernement-le-gabon-se-dote-dun-cadre-juridique-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-de-son-administration/>), so that they exchange information and procedures are simpler, and puts the interoperability of information systems at its centre. It [also requires public administrations to digitise their services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/gabon-s-2026-digital-budget-soars-by-156-to-fund-tech-overhaul>). No decree or order applying the ordinance is recorded, and no administration is named as exchanging data because the ordinance requires it. The ordinance was published in the Official Journal within three weeks of its signature.
