# Somalia

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** In September 2024 GovStack [stated that Somalia has no enabling legislation for data sharing and data analysis](<https://govstack.global/showcase/digital-leaders-spotlight-rwanda/>). No law, decree or regulation on public bodies sharing data is recorded as in force, and no draft of one is recorded. The statement is dated 29 September 2024 and comes from GovStack's spotlight on Somalia's digital leaders, which sets out the country's position on data exchange.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** With no such law recorded, nothing requires public bodies to exchange data, to connect to a government exchange platform or to stop asking people for what another body holds. No regulation or order is recorded as made under such a law, and no public body is named as exchanging data because a law requires it. The nearest held fact is the same [2024 statement that no enabling legislation exists yet](<https://govstack.global/showcase/digital-leaders-spotlight-rwanda/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found shows whether a data sharing law, decree or draft has appeared since September 2024, or whether public bodies are required to share data.
