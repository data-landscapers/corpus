# Angola

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Angola's [Presidential Decree 46/18 of 14 February 2018](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-46-18-de-14-de-fevereiro/>) approves the national plan for a global interoperability architecture across the central and local administration of the state. It took effect on publication and is a binding act of the President. The plan it approves says that the measures it needs at the level of law are still to be legislated.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The 2018 decree states that [adherence to the plan is mandatory for the services of the public administration](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-46-18-de-14-de-fevereiro/>). What is made mandatory is the plan and its architecture; the decree is not recorded as obliging a public body to hand data to another, to connect to an exchange platform, or to stop asking people for what the state already holds. No regulation or order made under it since 2023 is recorded.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
