# Cote d'Ivoire

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Cote d'Ivoire's [Ordinance 2017-500 of 2 August 2017](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/22/Ordonnance_2017-500_02_ao%C3%BBt_2017_sur_les_eservices.pdf>), made by the President in the Council of Ministers, governs electronic exchanges between users and administrative authorities and between the administrative authorities themselves. It has a chapter on the interoperability of electronic services, and no repeal is recorded.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** Chapter V of the ordinance says the state sets up an interoperability platform for electronic services [to which every administrative authority is bound to connect](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/22/Ordonnance_2017-500_02_ao%C3%BBt_2017_sur_les_eservices.pdf>), and that the authorities must follow the general interoperability framework. The ordinance also bars an authority from asking a user again for information the administration already holds. No decree or order applying the ordinance since 2023 is recorded, and no authority is named as connected to the platform because the ordinance requires it.
