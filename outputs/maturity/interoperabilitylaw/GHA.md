# Ghana

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Ghana has a bill and no law in force. The National Information Technology Agency's [draft Data Harmonisation Bill 2025](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanas-data-harmonisation-bill-2025-to-be-opened-for-public-consultation-from-nov-19-to-27-2025.html>) would give a legal basis for data sharing, common standards and the integration of systems across public and private institutions. Public consultation was set for 19 to 27 November 2025, with Cabinet and Parliament to follow. In July 2026 a representative of the communications minister said [a Data Exchange Act was also proposed](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The proposed Data Exchange Act is described as a law to [improve interoperability and allow secure data sharing among government agencies](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>). Neither text had been passed by the end of September 2026. With no law in force, nothing has been made under one and no public body is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
