# Ghana

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Ghana has a bill and no law in force. The National Information Technology Agency's [draft Data Harmonisation Bill 2025](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghanas-data-harmonisation-bill-2025-to-be-opened-for-public-consultation-from-nov-19-to-27-2025.html>) would give a legal basis for data sharing, common standards and the integration of systems across public and private institutions. The agency [put it out for public consultation](<https://nita.gov.gh/publicconsultation-3-2-2/>) on 19 November 2025, with Cabinet and Parliament to follow. In July 2026 a representative of the communications minister said [a Data Exchange Act was also proposed](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The proposed Data Exchange Act is described as a law to [improve interoperability and allow secure data sharing among government agencies](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/government-rolls-out-10-year-ai-implementation-roadmap-to-drive-jobs-economic-growth/>). The draft bill would set a framework for data sharing, standardisation and interoperability [through a National Data Exchange Platform](<https://nita.gov.gh/publicconsultation-3-2-2/>). With no law in force, nothing has been made under one and no public body is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
