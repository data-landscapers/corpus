# Algeria

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Algeria's [Presidential Decree 25-320 of 30 December 2025](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>), published in Official Journal no. 87, sets up a national data governance system. Within it, a national interoperability system secures the exchange of data between public institutions and administrations. In September 2026 [a further presidential decree on a secure digital interoperability plan was reported as being drawn up](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/guessoum-sur-lelaboration-dun-decret-sur-linteroperabilite-numerique-permettre-aux-avancees-numeriques-de-produire-pleinement-leurs-effets/>) on the President's instruction. An earlier measure, [Executive Decree 23-316 of 6 September 2023](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>), is also in force.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The 2025 decree makes the national interoperability system [the exclusive framework for exchanging digital data between public institutions and administrations](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>), over a secure national interconnection network set aside for the purpose. Each body must classify and catalogue its data to gain access. No order made under the decree is recorded, and no institution is named as exchanging data through the system because the decree requires it. Separately, the Interior Minister says public bodies are [required under Executive Decree 23-316 to connect their IT systems to the automated national identification number generation system](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>).
