# Mauritania

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Mauritania's [Decree 2022-009](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2024-02/D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02022-009%20Relatif%20aux%20%C3%A9changes%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20entre%20les%20usagers%20et%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives%20et%20entre%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives_%5BJ.O%20n%C2%B01505%5D.pdf>), made by the Prime Minister and published in Official Journal no. 1505, governs electronic exchanges between users and administrative authorities and between the administrative authorities themselves. The Council of Ministers heard it on 3 November 2021. It applies to any information exchanged electronically between administrative authorities.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The decree [sets a general security reference that the authorities' information systems must meet](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2024-02/D%C3%A9cret%20n%C2%B02022-009%20Relatif%20aux%20%C3%A9changes%20%C3%A9lectroniques%20entre%20les%20usagers%20et%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives%20et%20entre%20les%20autorit%C3%A9s%20administratives_%5BJ.O%20n%C2%B01505%5D.pdf>) when they exchange information electronically. It governs how exchanges are made; it is not recorded as obliging an authority to share its data, to connect to an exchange platform, or to stop asking users for what another authority already holds. No order made under it since 2023 is recorded, and no authority is named as exchanging data because the decree requires it.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
