# Mauritius

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** On 15 April 2022 the Bank of Mauritius Governor said, in opening remarks at the signing of memoranda of understanding, that [legislative amendments to the Civil Status Act](<https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/opening_remarks_governor_harvesh_seegolam_signing_of_mous_15_april_2022.pdf>) enable the Bank to share information retrieved from the Central Population Database with Central KYC participants. [The amendment is limited to the Central KYC purpose](<https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/opening_remarks_governor_harvesh_seegolam_signing_of_mous_15_april_2022.pdf>), and the Governor named the Central Population Database as the source of the information shared.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [speech](<https://www.bom.mu/sites/default/files/opening_remarks_governor_harvesh_seegolam_signing_of_mous_15_april_2022.pdf>) of 15 April 2022, in which the Governor of the Bank of Mauritius says the amendment to the Civil Status Act enables sharing between the Bank and Central KYC participants. The Governor describes the amendment as enabling sharing from the Central Population Database for Central KYC.
