# Ethiopia

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Ethiopia has a bill and no law in force. On 28 September 2026 the Council of Ministers [referred a draft National Data Governance Proclamation to the House of Peoples' Representatives](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9629721>), a text designed to make government services work through interoperable systems. The same meeting is reported as [approving a draft National Data Management Proclamation](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopias-council-of-ministers-advances-new-legal-frameworks-for-state-institutions-data-sovereignty-defence-and-economy/>) meant to ease data sharing, interoperability and reuse.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The draft is described as [designed to facilitate data sharing, interoperability and the reuse of data](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopias-council-of-ministers-advances-new-legal-frameworks-for-state-institutions-data-sovereignty-defence-and-economy/>) across government. Whether it would oblige public bodies to share is not stated in either report, and the House had not passed it by the end of September 2026. With no proclamation in force, nothing has been made under one and no public body is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
