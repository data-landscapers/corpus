# Cape Verde

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Cape Verde has a government resolution and no law. [Council of Ministers Resolution 54/2020](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) was approved on 19 March 2020 and published in Boletim Oficial no. 37 of 27 March 2020. It took effect the next day and applies to all direct and indirect State administration services. Its [measure e](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) orders a legal diploma on the interoperability of public administration information systems to be drafted and approved. Measure f orders an interoperability framework.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** [Measure g of the resolution](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) says public administration services and bodies must share among themselves the data and documents a process or service needs. That duty is subject to personal data protection rules. [Measure r](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) orders steps to put the once-only principle into practice, with the citizen's consent. These are the terms of a resolution that a law is meant to follow, and no law is reported to have followed it.

***Also on file***. [Gov.cv](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/02/24/governo-lanca-portal-govcv-modernizar-os-servicos-publicos/101584>).

***Not found***. No law, decree or regulation on public bodies sharing data is in force. No source says the legal diploma the 2020 resolution orders has been drafted or passed.
