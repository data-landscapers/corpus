# Cape Verde

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** [Council of Ministers Resolution 54/2020](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>), approved on 19 March 2020 and published in Boletim Oficial no. 37 of 27 March 2020, took effect the day after publication and applies to all direct and indirect State administration services. It is a government resolution, not a law. Its [measure e](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) orders a legal diploma on interoperability of public administration information systems to be drafted and approved, and its measure f orders an interoperability framework.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** [Measure g of the resolution](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) establishes that public administration services and bodies must share among themselves the public data and documents needed for a given process or service, subject to personal data protection rules. [Measure r](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/3048284/Medidas%20de%20Acelera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Transforma%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Digital%20na%20AP%20%282020%29.pdf/c556d060-0549-84e6-f11d-9eaee3e3f9d3>) orders steps to operationalise the once-only principle, with the citizen's consent. The resolution was published on 27 March 2020.
