# Sao Tome and Principe

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Sao Tome and Principe has dealt with data sharing and governance in public institutions through a national framework for interoperability, which a legal survey of 2024 [cites as Law 1/2024](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). The survey gives it as an act and gives no date of promulgation beyond the year in its number.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The survey says the framework [addresses data sharing and data governance in public institutions](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). It does not say that the law obliges public bodies to share data, to connect to an exchange platform, or to stop asking people for what another body already holds. No regulation or order made under the law is recorded, and no public body is named as exchanging data because the law requires it. Nothing later than the 2024 survey is recorded on the law.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
