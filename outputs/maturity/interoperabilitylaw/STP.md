# Sao Tome and Principe

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Sao Tome and Principe deals with data sharing in public institutions through a national framework for interoperability. A legal survey of 2024 [cites it as Law 1/2024](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). The survey gives it as an act and gives no date of promulgation beyond the year in its number.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The survey says the framework [addresses data sharing and data governance in public institutions](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). It does not say that the law obliges public bodies to share data or to connect to an exchange platform. Nor does it say the law bars asking people for what another body already holds. No regulation or order made under the law is recorded. No public body is named as exchanging data because the law requires it. In October 2026 the justice minister [cited Law 1/2024 among the country's legislative initiatives](<https://rafa.tl/pt/sao-tome-e-principe-defende-mecanismos-mais-rapidos-para-obter-provas-digitais-entre-paises-da-cplp/>).
