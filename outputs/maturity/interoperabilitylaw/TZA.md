# Tanzania

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Tanzania's [e-Government Act, No. 10 of 2019](<https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/revised/559/download>), started on 19 September 2019 by Government Notice 964 of that year, and applies to all public institutions. Part VIII of the Act deals with the management of electronic government data, and its section 48 is headed *Data sharing and exchange*. The Act defines interoperability as systems exchanging data.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The Act [provides for data sharing and exchange between public institutions in its section 48](<https://oagmis.oag.go.tz/portal/acts/revised/559/download>). [The e-Government Authority says section 48(2) requires it to set up and run a system through which government ICT systems exchange data](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). Nothing found says the Act obliges other public institutions to share data or connect to that system. Nothing found says it stops them asking people for what another institution already holds. No regulation or order on data sharing made under the Act since 2023 is recorded. No institution is named as exchanging data because the Act requires it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
