# Zambia

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Zambia's [Electronic Government Act, No. 41 of 2021](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%2041%20OF%202021%2C%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20GOVERNMENT%20ACT%2C%202021_0.pdf>), was assented to on 19 May 2021. It provides for public bodies sharing data: one public body may retrieve a document from the body that issued it, with the consent of the person concerned, and public bodies may run joint automated procedures. In January 2026 [an amendment bill was presented to stakeholders](<https://itweb.africa/article/zambia-begins-amending-electronic-government-act/>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The Act [allows sharing between public bodies and does not require it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%2041%20OF%202021%2C%20THE%20ELECTRONIC%20GOVERNMENT%20ACT%2C%202021_0.pdf>): each of the two provisions says a public body may act, and neither obliges it to exchange data or to connect to a platform. The amendment bill is described as giving legal standing to electronic submissions and digital authentication. No regulation made under the Act since 2023 is recorded, and no public body is named as exchanging data because the Act requires it.
