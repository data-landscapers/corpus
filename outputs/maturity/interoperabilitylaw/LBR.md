# Liberia

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Liberia has an executive order and no law. [Executive Order No. 163](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/boakai-s-executive-order-163-signals-digital-government-shift/article_7a31a87b-4fe2-4578-9ff7-b0224f3bf36a.html>), signed by President Boakai on 22 April 2026, creates a national digitalisation and modernisation initiative and an office of technology, digitalisation and innovation. Draft legislation to make the arrangement permanent is due within twelve months of the order.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The order [mandates interoperable digital systems across ministries, agencies and commissions](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/boakai-s-executive-order-163-signals-digital-government-shift/article_7a31a87b-4fe2-4578-9ff7-b0224f3bf36a.html>) and introduces a government interoperability framework. It provides that data sharing between them be governed by formal agreements on access, use and accountability. These are the terms of an order that legislation is meant to replace. With no law in force, nothing has been made under one and no ministry is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
