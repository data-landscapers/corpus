# Uganda

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Uganda's [National Information Technology Authority-Uganda (E-Government) Regulations, 2015](<https://pmlawhub.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NITA-U-E-Government-Regulations-2015-SI-No.-27-of-2015.pdf>) (S.I. 2015 No. 27) were made by the ICT Minister on 4 May 2015 and gazetted on 10 June 2015. They bind all public bodies in Uganda and are a statutory instrument issued by the ICT Minister. They provide for inter-operable systems, a national data centre, mandatory use of the shared government backbone and e-government infrastructure, and information sharing in e-government services.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The [2015 regulations](<https://pmlawhub.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/NITA-U-E-Government-Regulations-2015-SI-No.-27-of-2015.pdf>) make use of the shared government backbone and e-government infrastructure mandatory for public bodies (reg. 10). On information sharing, regulation 13 says public and private bodies may share. No source found says they must share data, connect to an exchange platform, or stop asking a person for what another public body holds.

***Also on file***. [national databank](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2025-06/NITA-U%20Act%20%28Act%20No.%204%20of%202009%29.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
