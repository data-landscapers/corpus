# Burkina Faso

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Burkina Faso adopted its general interoperability reference, the *Référentiel général d'interopérabilité*, [by an order of the Prime Minister dated 23 May 2018](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>). The order fixes the rules that make the information systems of the different public administrations compatible with each other. It is a regulation of the head of government and not an act of parliament or a decree.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The reference was [the subject of a training seminar for the IT directorates of ministries from 15 to 17 October 2019](<https://burkina24.com/2019/10/15/burkina-un-referentiel-pour-moderniser-ladministration-publique/>), a year after the order was signed. Nothing recorded says the order obliges administrations to exchange data or to connect to a common platform, and no regulation or order made under it since 2023 is recorded. No administration is named as exchanging data because the order requires it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
