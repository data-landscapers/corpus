# Senegal

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Senegal has a draft decree and no law in force. The digital ministry said on 26 March 2026 that the first phase of its interoperability work had [delivered a national interoperability framework, a draft decree and a platform](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_newdealtechnologique-interop%C3%A9rabilit%C3%A9-activity-7443068023738900480-ejtp>). On 4 May 2026 it announced that [the draft decree governing the interoperability platform would be presented the next day](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_dpi-newdealtechnologique-activity-7457180609757528065-UqRT>). No adoption is recorded.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The decree is described only as [the text that will govern the interoperability platform](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/mctnsn_dpi-newdealtechnologique-activity-7457180609757528065-UqRT>). The ministry's posts do not say that it would oblige public bodies to connect to the platform or to exchange data through it. It had not been adopted by the end of September 2026. With nothing in force, no rule has been made under it and no public body is named as exchanging data because a decree requires it.
