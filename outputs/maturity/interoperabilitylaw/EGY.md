# Egypt

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Egypt has a bill and no law in force. In February 2024 the deputy communications minister told the House ICT committee that [a bill on data exchange between government bodies had been drafted](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2024/2/12/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%86%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%8A%D9%84-%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%84/6477784>). It had gone to the Justice Ministry. In December 2025 the committee's chair said the government had [finished a 35-article bill on classifying and circulating information](<https://www.dostor.org/5338473>). It was to go to parliament at the start of its next session. The communications minister said the same month that the draft [would go to Cabinet and then the House](<https://www.fintechgate.net/184454>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The 35-article bill [governs data exchange between the state's executive bodies](<https://www.dostor.org/5338473>), leaving out classified information. It has not been passed. Nothing has been made under it, and no public body is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
