# Tunisia

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Tunisia's Decree-Law 2020-31 of 10 June 2020 on the electronic exchange of data between public structures, and between them and their users, [was approved by Law 14 of 7 April 2021](<https://www.pm.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2026-09/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B4%D9%88%D8%B1%20%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF04%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202026_0_0.pdf>). [Government Decree 2020-777 of 5 October 2020](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-777-du-5-octobre-2020-fixant-les-conditions-les-modalites-et-les-procedures-dapplication-du-decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-31-du-10-juin/>) sets the conditions and procedures for applying it, and took effect on 1 January 2021. The Presidency of the Government [gave the decree-law as in force in October 2026](<https://www.alchourouk.com/index.php/article/%D8%B1%D8%A6%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%85-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The 2020 decree says [public structures must use electronic exchange among themselves and with their users](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-777-du-5-octobre-2020-fixant-les-conditions-les-modalites-et-les-procedures-dapplication-du-decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-31-du-10-juin/>). They must give the interoperability operator the list of the data flows they can supply, and may not ask for more than one electronic copy of a document. The Presidency says the decree-law [sets the principle that users are not asked for data or documents public bodies already hold](<https://www.alchourouk.com/index.php/article/%D8%B1%D8%A6%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%85-%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%83%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9>). The decree itself [is the rule made under the decree-law](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2020-777-du-5-octobre-2020-fixant-les-conditions-les-modalites-et-les-procedures-dapplication-du-decret-loi-du-chef-du-gouvernement-n-2020-31-du-10-juin/>), and sets up a strategic committee and a coordination committee; it dates from 2020. No rule made under the decree-law since 2023 is recorded.
