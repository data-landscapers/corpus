# Guinea-Bissau

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Guinea-Bissau's national regulation on the interoperability of the digital systems and platforms of public and private services was [approved by Decree 27/2025 and published in the Official Bulletin of 31 October 2025](<https://wardip.gw/governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-para-acelerar-a-transformacao-digital-na-guine-bissau/>). The Council of Ministers had [approved it in draft on 10 July 2025](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/>). The regional digital project again [announced the regulation's approval in August 2026](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/wardip-guin%C3%A9-bissau_governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-activity-7491768929355194368-Vk5Q>).

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The regulation is described as one that [lets public bodies share information securely](<https://wardip.gw/governo-aprova-regulamento-nacional-de-interoperabilidade-para-acelerar-a-transformacao-digital-na-guine-bissau/>). The reports do not say that it obliges public bodies to share data, to connect to an exchange platform, or to stop asking people for what another body already holds. No order made under the regulation is recorded, and no public body is named as exchanging data because the regulation requires it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force requires public bodies to share data, to connect to the government's exchange platform, or not to ask a person for what another public body already holds.
