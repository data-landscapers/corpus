# Botswana

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Botswana's [Digital Services Act, 2025 (Act No. 22 of 2025)](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>) was passed by the National Assembly on 14 August 2025 and assented to on 17 October 2025. It provides for public bodies exchanging data through an information gateway and under a framework for the secure exchange of information, and for one body to retrieve data from another. The Act starts on a date the minister sets by order in the Gazette.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The Act says [a public or private body shall avoid collecting information that another body already holds](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>), and that citizens and businesses are to give the same information only once, to be shared and reused as needed. A body that does not comply with the directions of the authority the Act sets up can be fined up to P500,000. No regulation or order made under the Act is recorded.
