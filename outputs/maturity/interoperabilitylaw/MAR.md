# Morocco

## Data sharing law

**Law or decree in force.** Morocco has a bill and no law in force. A framework bill known as Digital X.0, covering artificial intelligence, data governance, digital identity and interoperability, was [under review by the General Secretariat of the Government in November 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-drafts-landmark-law-to-govern-ai-data-and-digital-identity>). It was [still being worked on before going to parliament](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/morocco-prepares-new-legislation-to-accelerate-digital-transformation>), and the minister delegate for digital transition said it clarifies how data moves between government institutions.

**Sharing required and the law applied.** The bill is described as setting a model in which [any exchange of information between public agencies is authorised by the user, through consent that can be traced](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-drafts-landmark-law-to-govern-ai-data-and-digital-identity>). That is a condition on sharing and not a duty to share. The bill had not been passed by the end of September 2026. With no law in force, nothing has been made under one and no agency is named as exchanging data because a law requires it.
