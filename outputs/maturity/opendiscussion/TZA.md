# Tanzania

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Nothing is held on public criticism of policy. The nearest held fact is that the Legal & Human Rights Centre [filed a case at the East African Court of Justice on 8 December 2025](<https://www.eacj.org/?cases=reference-no-56-of-2025-legal-human-rights-centre-v-the-attorney-general-of-the-united-republic-of-tanzania>) alleging a nationwide internet shutdown by the government.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Access Now reports [a five-day nationwide internet blackout during the 29 October 2025 election](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>), and counts [8 internet shutdowns in Tanzania in 2025](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>). The Legal & Human Rights Centre alleges [a total shutdown from 29 October to 4 November 2025](<https://www.eacj.org/?cases=reference-no-56-of-2025-legal-human-rights-centre-v-the-attorney-general-of-the-united-republic-of-tanzania>). Amnesty International says the authorities imposed [a partial internet shutdown during the October 2025 general elections](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/>), and the Southern Africa Litigation Centre says [the network was switched off around the election and the protests that followed](<https://www.southernafricalitigationcentre.org/oral-statement-under-item-3-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa/>). The Internet Governance Project puts the cost at [an estimated US$250 million in losses during the 2025 election period](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority [suspended the online content licence of JamiiForums for 90 days from 6 September 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tcra-suspends-jamiiforums-licence-over-misleading-insulting-content--5183050>) over content it called misleading and insulting to the Government and the President, and [blocked access to the platform](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tcra-suspends-jamiiforums-licence-over-misleading-insulting-content--5183050>); [Amnesty International](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/>) also reports the ban. A complaint filed in August 2026 [alleges the leader of the opposition was jailed on capital charges in 2025](<https://unicourt.com/case/ca-sm-casebm9eb4fc6d8dcd-1031959>) for calling for electoral reforms.

***Also on file***. [The Online Content Regulations of 2020, amended 2022 and 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tcra-suspends-jamiiforums-licence-over-misleading-insulting-content--5183050>); [online content licensing](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>) and [VPN registration](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>), as the Internet Governance Project describes them; [Meta account suspensions on Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/01/tanzanian-influence-mange-kimambi-sues-meta-facebook/>) and [on Instagram](<https://unicourt.com/case/ca-sm-casebm9eb4fc6d8dcd-1031959>); [the licensing of online content by the Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/ai-changing-habits-reshape-online-media-5562974>).

***Not found***. Nothing found shows a twelve-month stretch with no cut or block of the internet or a service, or who ordered the October 2025 blackout.
