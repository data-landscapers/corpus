# Libya

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [Article 14 of the Constitutional Declaration of 2011](<https://security-legislation.ly/ar/latest-laws/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2011-%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87/>), which guarantees freedom of opinion, expression and press in accordance with the law.

***Not held***. Nothing held says government policy is criticised in public inside the country, that the internet was cut, or that anyone was punished for what they said.
