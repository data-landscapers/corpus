# Comoros

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** [In December 2025 the Comorian press mobilised against a ministry and CNPA proposal to amend article 169 of the Information Code on source protection, and the journalists' union SNJC obtained its withdrawal](<https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/societe/comores-une-journaliste-entendue-par-la-police-a-cause-dun-post-facebook/>). [CIVICUS Monitor reports that the union, media and rights defenders opposed the draft, which would have let a judge compel journalists to reveal sources, and that MPs rejected it on 26 December 2025](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/comoros-right-of-journalists-to-keep-sources-secret-is-upheld-after-pressure-arrests-of-protesters/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found. The nearest held fact is [law 25-021/AU, promulgated by decree on 2 February 2026, whose article 124 lets the regulator order internet providers](<https://munganyo.km/public/storage/Décret N°26-023PR du 02 fév 2026 portant promul Loi N°25-021AU modifiant et completant le Code Information et de la  Com.pdf>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** [Mayotte Hebdo says police arrested Al-Watwan correspondent Abdou Moustoifa on 17 November 2025 over a Facebook post about migrants held in a Moroni police station, and questioned him for over two hours to learn his source](<https://www.mayottehebdo.com/actualite/societe/comores-une-journaliste-entendue-par-la-police-a-cause-dun-post-facebook/>). [Article 361 of the penal code makes defamation a criminal offence](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_COM_E_Main.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [the information and communication code, with the media regulator CNPA](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>); [the criminal defamation offence in article 361 of the penal code](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_COM_E_Main.pdf>); [law 25-021/AU, whose article 124 lets the regulator order internet providers and which treats false news as a content breach](<https://munganyo.km/public/storage/Décret N°26-023PR du 02 fév 2026 portant promul Loi N°25-021AU modifiant et completant le Code Information et de la  Com.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says a year has passed with no one arrested, charged or jailed, and no outlet suspended or closed, for what was said or published. Nothing found says the internet was cut or a service blocked.
