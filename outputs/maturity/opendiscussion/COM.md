# Comoros

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [the information and communication code, with the media regulator CNPA](<https://www.cnpa-comores.org/un-nouveau-souffle-pour-le-cnpa/>); [the criminal defamation offence in article 361 of the penal code](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_COM_E_Main.pdf>); [law 25-021/AU, whose article 124 lets the regulator order internet providers and which treats false news as a content breach](<https://munganyo.km/public/storage/Décret N°26-023PR du 02 fév 2026 portant promul Loi N°25-021AU modifiant et completant le Code Information et de la  Com.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing held says government policy is criticised in public inside the country, that the internet was cut, or that anyone was punished for what they said.
