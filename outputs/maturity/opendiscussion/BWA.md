# Botswana

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 9 February 2026 [the chairperson of the civil society body BOCONGO told the minister](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89903>) that the Constitutional Court process needs meaningful, inclusive participation. Consultations, the chairperson said, must go beyond formality. The chairperson also asked for clear public information and more time.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing on a cut or block is held. The nearest held fact is the [dialogue between civil society and the minister on the Constitutional Court process, reported on 10 February 2026](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89903>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In July 2022 Tshepo Sethibe, director of Moeladilotlhoko News Boiler, [was charged with publishing “alarming information” on Facebook](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/82198>) over posts on the police handling of a disappearance. The state withdrew the charge in October 2024.
