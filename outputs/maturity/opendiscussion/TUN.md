# Tunisia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The National Union of Tunisian Journalists, with Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International and RSF, [denounced Decree-Law 2022-54 as a tool of repression](<https://nawaat.org/2026/07/16/liberte-dexpression-en-tunisie-recul-alarmant-sous-le-joug-du-decret-54-et-de-larticle-86/>) in a report dated 16 July 2026, and the union [called for the annulment of the El Mekki judgment](<https://nawaat.org/2026/07/16/liberte-dexpression-en-tunisie-recul-alarmant-sous-le-joug-du-decret-54-et-de-larticle-86/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing is held on cuts or blocks. The nearest held fact is the RSF [2026 index, which ranks Tunisia 137th of 180](<https://rsf.org/en/country/tunisia>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 15 July 2026 the Sfax Court of Appeal [sentenced journalist Haythem El Mekki to one year in prison](<https://nawaat.org/2026/07/16/liberte-dexpression-en-tunisie-recul-alarmant-sous-le-joug-du-decret-54-et-de-larticle-86/>) under Article 86 of the Telecommunications Code over a social-media post about a Sfax hospital, after a first-instance court had dismissed the case. Journalists Borhen Bsaies and Mourad Zeghidi were [sentenced to one year, reduced to eight months, under Decree-Law 54](<https://nawaat.org/2026/07/16/liberte-dexpression-en-tunisie-recul-alarmant-sous-le-joug-du-decret-54-et-de-larticle-86/>); RSF also [reports the El Mekki sentence](<https://rsf.org/en/country/tunisia>).

***Also on file***. [Lawyer and commentator Sonia Dahmani, arrested in May 2024 and later convicted several times](<https://nawaat.org/2026/07/16/liberte-dexpression-en-tunisie-recul-alarmant-sous-le-joug-du-decret-54-et-de-larticle-86/>); [RSF's 2024 count of over ten journalists facing legal proceedings](<https://rsf.org/en/country/tunisia>); [Diwan FM, which RSF says keeps a balanced, critical approach](<https://rsf.org/en/country/tunisia>); Decree-Law 54 of 2022 [before the Council of Ministers](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%86%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%85-54-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%AD-3-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%85>) and [in parliament](<https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4139>).

***Not found***. Nothing found shows a twelve-month stretch in which no one was arrested, charged or jailed, and no outlet suspended or closed, for what was said or published.
