# Eritrea

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** UNESCO states, in a document dated January 2026, that the Ministry of Information [runs all television, radio and newspapers in Eritrea](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>), and that [privately owned outlets effectively ceased to exist after a government ban in 2001](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>). It presents that position as continuing, so no outlet independent of the state has been found as carrying public criticism.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing is held on a cut to internet access or a block on a platform or outlet in Eritrea, or on any bar against such a cut. The nearest held fact is the [2001 government ban on privately owned media, which UNESCO states ended private outlets](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** Nothing is held on a person arrested or an outlet punished for what was said, nor on a law repealed. The nearest held fact is again the [2001 ban, after which UNESCO says the Ministry of Information runs all television, radio and newspapers](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [the RSF World Press Freedom Index 2026, which ranked Eritrea 180th of 180](<https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low>); [the 1996 Press Proclamation, owned by the Government of Eritrea](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>); [Penal Code Article 154 on false information discrediting state bodies](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source says whether anyone in Eritrea has been cut off, arrested or charged for speech in the last twelve months, or whether any law has been repealed.
