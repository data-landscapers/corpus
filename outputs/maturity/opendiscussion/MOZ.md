# Mozambique

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In March 2026 civil society bodies and opposition parties attacked the new telecommunications regulations. They said the rules allowed [internet shutdowns on national security grounds](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>). The journalist Tomas Vieira Mario [condemned the minister's defence on Facebook](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>) and called for the rules to be overhauled.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** After the October 2024 election, OONI measurements show [internet providers blocked Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp](<https://explorer.ooni.org/findings/2025-mozambique-blocked-facebook-instagram-and-whatsapp-amid-pro>). The block ran from 29 October to 14 November 2024. Human Rights Watch says [mobile and internet access was restricted from 25 October](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/11/06/mozambique-post-election-internet-restrictions-hinder-rights>), and operators said it was restored on 18 November. No cut is on record from the last twelve months. In March 2026 the minister [denied any attempt to restrict use of the internet](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or sentence for speech has been found. The nearest held fact is that [a named journalist publicly condemned the minister's defence of the regulations in March 2026](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>), the day after the minister spoke.
