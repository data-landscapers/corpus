# Mozambique

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Civil society bodies and opposition parties strongly criticised the government's new telecommunications regulations in March 2026 as allowing [internet shutdowns on national security grounds](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>), and journalist and press freedom activist Tomas Vieira Mario [condemned the minister's defence on Facebook and called for the regulations to be overhauled](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found. The nearest held fact, from a Club of Mozambique report, is that [the March 2026 criticism concerned regulations said to allow shutdowns](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>), and that the minister denied any attempt to restrict use of the internet.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or sentence for speech has been found. The nearest held fact is that [a named journalist publicly condemned the minister's defence of the regulations in March 2026](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>), the day after the minister spoke.

***Also on file***. [Telecommunications Traffic Control Regulation, Decree 48/2025, of the National Communications Institute](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Regulamento-de-Controle-de-Trafego-de-Telecomunicacoes-Revisto-2025-Decreto-48_2025.pdf>); [the Constitutional Council ruling of 29 July 2026, Acórdão 6/CC/2026](<https://cconstitucional.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Acordao-no-6-CC2026-de-29-de-Julho-2026.pdf>); [the government's response to the ruling](<https://mznews.co.mz/chumbo-do-regulamento-de-telecomunicacoes-pelo-cc-deixa-governo-de-boca-aberta-mas-recusa-engolir-sapos/>); [the minister's statement that the government respects the ruling](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-respects-ruling-striking-down-telecom-traffic-control-regulations-minister/>); [the minister's denial of an attempt to restrict the internet](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-minister-denies-attempt-to-restrict-use-of-internet/>); [a CIPESA account of the shutdown case before African courts](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>).

***Not found***. No source says the country went the last twelve months with no one punished for what they said or published.
