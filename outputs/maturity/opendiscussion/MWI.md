# Malawi

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Civil society groups [told parliamentary committees on 9 March 2026 that the proposed 2026/27 education budget falls short of the 20 percent UNESCO benchmark](<https://csecmalawi.com/2026/03/10/civil-society-coalition-engages-parliamentarians-to-lobby-for-increased-education-financing-in-the-2026-27-national-budget/>). A group calling itself the Concerned Citizens of Malawi People Power Movement [publicly criticised the communications regulator MACRA by January 2026](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>) for not briefing the public on its social media monitoring system procurement.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found. The nearest held fact is that civil society groups [challenged the legality of MACRA's $1.5 million social media monitoring contract and called for its suspension](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/questions-grow-over-malawis-social-media-monitoring-system-amid-oversight-concerns/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest or charge in the last twelve months has been found. The nearest held fact is that [journalist Joshua Chisa Mbele was arrested in January 2022](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>) after sharing on Facebook a document alleging corruption involving government officials.

***Also on file***. [MACRA's social media monitoring system, built by Hashcom of Ghana](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>); [the Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act 2016](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>), under which [Tumpale Mwakibinga was charged in 2019](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>); [activist Manes Hale, detained in 2018 over Facebook posts](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>); [Ignatius Kamwanje, sentenced to 18 months in May 2021 over a Facebook post](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>).

***Not found***. No source says the country went the last twelve months with no one punished for what they said or published.
