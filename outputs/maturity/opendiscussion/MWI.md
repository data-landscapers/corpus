# Malawi

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 9 March 2026 civil society groups [told parliamentary committees the 2026/27 education budget falls short of a UNESCO benchmark](<https://csecmalawi.com/2026/03/10/civil-society-coalition-engages-parliamentarians-to-lobby-for-increased-education-financing-in-the-2026-27-national-budget/>). By January 2026 a group calling itself the Concerned Citizens of Malawi People Power Movement had [publicly criticised the communications regulator MACRA](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>). It faulted MACRA for not briefing the public on a social media monitoring system.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found. The nearest fact found is that civil society groups [challenged the legality of MACRA's $1.5 million social media monitoring contract and called for its suspension](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/questions-grow-over-malawis-social-media-monitoring-system-amid-oversight-concerns/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 5 December 2025 police [arrested Maravi Post contributor Jones Gadama](<https://www.maravipost.com/maravi-post-contributor-jones-gadama-in-court-over-mbc-employee-mwandiras-cooked-cybercrime-claims/>) over two documents posted online. The documents alleged misconduct by an employee of the state broadcaster MBC. He was placed on fortnightly police bail. In July 2025 the High Court, sitting as a Constitutional Court, [declared criminal defamation under section 200 of the Penal Code unconstitutional](<https://ifex.org/malawi-moves-forward-on-law-stalls-on-protecting-media-freedom/>), MISA Malawi reports.

[Ignatius Kamwanje, sentenced to 18 months in May 2021 over a Facebook post](<https://moz24h.co.mz/part-2-a-ghanaian-contractor-a-1-5-million-contract-and-a-surveillance-system-that-remains-unexplained/>).

***Not found***. No source says the government is barred from cutting the internet.
