# Senegal

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 10 March 2026, 23 MPs [lodged a constitutional challenge to the media regulator law](<https://paradigmhq.org/paradigm-initiative-welcomes-landmark-ruling-against-repressive-media-legislation-in-senegal/>), citing risks of administrative censorship. APPEL, the Senegalese online press body, backed it with Paradigm Initiative. The Conseil constitutionnel [ruled on the law on 7 April 2026](<https://conseilconstitutionnel.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Decision-N%C2%B0-2C2026.pdf>), in Decision n° 2/C/2026. In October 2026 Sen TV columnist Mamadou Cissé [criticised the police handling of an influencer's arrest](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-columnist-facing-prosecution-for-disseminating-false-news/>) on YouTube.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** CIPESA says [mobile data was blocked nationwide in early February 2024](<https://cipesa.org/2025/06/will-the-ecowas-judgment-on-senegal-redefine-digital-rights-in-francophone-africa/>) after the presidential election was postponed. On 14 May 2025 the ECOWAS Court [ruled that the shutdowns of June and July 2023 violated fundamental rights](<https://cipesa.org/2025/06/will-the-ecowas-judgment-on-senegal-redefine-digital-rights-in-francophone-africa/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 16 October 2026 Cissé was [charged with disseminating false news](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-columnist-facing-prosecution-for-disseminating-false-news/>) over those comments. On 5 August 2026 a Dakar court [sentenced three commentators for offending the head of state](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-three-supporters-of-opposition-party-sentenced-for-offending-the-president/>), over a video criticising President Faye. In February 2026 the Sen TV columnist Abdou Nguer was [charged with spreading false information and remanded in custody](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/senegal-tv-commentator-detained-for-disputing-prosecutors-statement/>). The deputies [adopted the media regulator bill on 3 March 2026](<https://aps.sn/conseil-national-de-regulation-des-medias-le-texte-adopte-par-les-deputes/>) as [Loi n° 2026-09](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/be7f281f-9d15-46c2-aedf-1b5213032b17/loi-2026-09-creation-conseil-national-regulation-medias-cnrm-senegal.pdf>).

[RSF on the shutdowns of early 2024](<https://rsf.org/en/country/senegal>). ***Not found***. No law repealing a speech offence, and no bar on cutting the internet, has been found.
