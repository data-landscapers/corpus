# Senegal

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 10 March 2026, 23 MPs [lodged a constitutional challenge to the media regulator law](<https://paradigmhq.org/paradigm-initiative-welcomes-landmark-ruling-against-repressive-media-legislation-in-senegal/>), citing risks of administrative censorship. APPEL, the Senegalese online press body, backed it with Paradigm Initiative. The Conseil constitutionnel [ruled on the law on 7 April 2026](<https://conseilconstitutionnel.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Decision-N%C2%B0-2C2026.pdf>), in Decision n° 2/C/2026.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** [RSF says internet shutdowns in early 2024 undermined the right to inform](<https://rsf.org/en/country/senegal>). It does not say who ordered them or for how long. [CIPESA says shutdowns were imposed during the 2023 unrest](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>). A court later found they violated freedom of expression.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In July 2025, Le Soleil reports, [journalist Bachir Fofana was tried for spreading false news](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/division-speciale-de-cybersecurite-police-des-reseaux-sociaux-gardien-des-moeurs/>). The commentator Badara Gadiaga [was jailed over remarks about Ousmane Sonko](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/division-speciale-de-cybersecurite-police-des-reseaux-sociaux-gardien-des-moeurs/>). No arrest for speech is on record from the last twelve months. The deputies [adopted the media regulator bill on 3 March 2026](<https://aps.sn/conseil-national-de-regulation-des-medias-le-texte-adopte-par-les-deputes/>) as [Loi n° 2026-09](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/be7f281f-9d15-46c2-aedf-1b5213032b17/loi-2026-09-creation-conseil-national-regulation-medias-cnrm-senegal.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [The Conseil national de Régulation des Médias law](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/be7f281f-9d15-46c2-aedf-1b5213032b17/loi-2026-09-creation-conseil-national-regulation-medias-cnrm-senegal.pdf>). [The Commission nationale d'Accès à l'Information (CONAI)](<https://primature.sn/publications/conseil-des-ministres/conseil-des-ministres-du-29-octobre-2025>). [Loi n° 2025-15 on access to information](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/e37fee2b-7841-437a-ae96-b7f969b6a9ac/loi-2025-15-accees-a-l-information.pdf>). The [shutdowns of early 2024](<https://rsf.org/en/country/senegal>) and [of 2023](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>).

***Not found***. No source says the country went twelve months with no one punished for speech.
