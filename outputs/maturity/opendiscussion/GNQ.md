# Equatorial Guinea

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Nothing found says a newspaper, party or group inside the country criticised a government policy in public. The nearest fact is [the arrest of a man over a viral video](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Afrique/guinee-equatoriale-coupure-de-facebook-et-tiktok_n_504430.html>). The video accused the vice-president of corruption, AFP reported on 16 September 2026.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Access Now says the island of Annobón [has had no internet or phone service since July 2024](<https://www.ambolegadu.com/en/annobon-sin-internet-access-now-reune-testimonios-y-reclama-el-fin-del-apagon/>), and puts the blackout down to the government. Its report of 20 October 2026 [lists Annobón among places under indefinite blackouts](<https://www.accessnow.org/indefinite-internet-shutdowns/>), with testimonies from 2024 to 2026. Nothing found says the government is barred from cutting the internet.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** [The author of a viral video accusing the vice-president of corruption was arrested by the authorities](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Afrique/guinee-equatoriale-coupure-de-facebook-et-tiktok_n_504430.html>), AFP reported on 16 September 2026. The report names neither the person nor the law. [Ley 7/2024 on social networks and cybercrime](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>) is [in full force, the government said on 1 July 2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_recuerda_la_plena_vigencia_de_la_ley_que_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>). No repeal of a law that punishes speech has been found.

***Also on file***. The same AFP report is [headed as a cut of Facebook and TikTok](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Afrique/guinee-equatoriale-coupure-de-facebook-et-tiktok_n_504430.html>) and names WhatsApp and Instagram. [The office acting for Nguema Obiang Mangue explained its actions against Diario Rombe and three other people](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas>). A press freedom bill [reached the Senate floor on 8 October 2026](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/10/08/la-nueva-ley-de-libertad-de-prensa-avanza-al-pleno-del-senado-tras-el-consenso-alcanzado-por-la-comision/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says government policy is criticised in public inside the country. Nor does anything show a bar on cutting the internet, or a law punishing speech repealed.
