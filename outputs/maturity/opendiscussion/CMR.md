# Cameroon

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 8 and 9 June 2026 Civic Watch Cameroon's head and others met at [a workshop in Yaounde on the 2010 cybercrime law](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). They said the law contains provisions limiting free expression. Legal experts and civil society called the October 2025 election-time internet restrictions a rights violation.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** OONI's measurements suggest Cameroon [blocked TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and Telegram from 31 October to 5 November 2025](<https://explorer.ooni.org/findings/2025-cameroon-blocked-social-media-amid-post-election-protests>), during post-election protests. VPN sites were blocked too, mainly on Orange and CAMTEL. Le Jour reports that on 23 October 2025, during the election, [internet restrictions blocked a blogger's access to Facebook](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). Access Now's #KeepItOn report [counts four internet shutdowns in Cameroon in 2025](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest or charge for speech has been found. The nearest fact found is the [2010 cybercrime law, law n°2010/012 of 21 December 2010](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). Workshop participants said its provisions limit free expression.

***Also on file***. [Law n°2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybercrime](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). Global Voices says [the Anglophone regions lost internet access for more than a year from January 2017](<https://globalvoices.org/2026/10/07/africas-governments-already-treat-the-internet-as-protest-infrastructure-its-courts-have-not-caught-up/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found names who ordered the 2025 blocks. No source says the government is barred from such cuts.
