# Mali

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [The cybercrime tribunal sentenced the journalist Abdrahamane Keita of Le Temoin on 14 September 2026 to two years in prison and 1 million CFA francs damages](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>) over a remark about Kidal on a television programme, after [his detention in June 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); [Chahana Takiou of 22 Septembre was sentenced on 3 August 2026 to 12 months in prison, six suspended](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>), after remarks at the Pan-African Media Forum in June 2026; [Mohamed Youssouf Bathily (Ras Bath) was arrested in March 2023](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>) over comments on a death in custody, and [his trial began on 10 August 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); the cybercrime provisions of [the Penal Code amendment of December 2024](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); the [Pole national de lutte contre la cybercriminalite](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>); [a ministerial instruction of 29 July 2026 applying the cybercrime provisions to filming, publication and commentary of sexual images](<https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs>).

***Not held***. No source dated in the last three years says a newspaper, broadcaster, party or group inside the country criticised a government policy in public, and none says the internet was cut in the last twelve months.
