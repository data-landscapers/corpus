# Mali

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In June 2026 the Maison de la Presse [sharply criticised the detention of two journalists](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>), Reporters Without Borders says. The private print publishers' association [denounced the 5 February 2026 arrest of the editor Youssouf Sissoko](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/02/09/press-freedom-under-fire-in-mali>). In June 2026 the editor Chahana Takiou [criticised the authorities for trying journalists under the cybercrime law](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/08/04/journalist-in-mali-sentenced-to-a-year-in-prison>). He said [the press-offence law should apply instead](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** In January 2026 the government [banned the distribution, sale and online circulation of the magazine Jeune Afrique](<https://mfwa.org/issues-in-focus/malis-shrinking-civic-space-imprisonments-bans-and-the-silencing-of-independent-media/>). It accused the magazine of apology for terrorism over its coverage of the fuel crisis. Readers [report its website blocked in Mali since late January 2026](<https://www.jeuneafrique.com/1762043/politique/jeune-afrique-suspendu-au-mali-comment-contourner-la-censure/>), and the authorities have not announced the block.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The cybercrime tribunal [sentenced the journalist Abdrahamane Keita to two years in prison](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>) on 14 September 2026. On 17 August 2026 the Bamako appeal court [sentenced radio commentator Ras Bath and activist Rose Vie Chère to ten years, three suspended](<https://mfwa.org/issues-in-focus/malis-shrinking-civic-space-imprisonments-bans-and-the-silencing-of-independent-media/>), over broadcast comments on inflation and power cuts. Takiou, [arrested on 8 June 2026](<https://cpj.org/2026/06/in-mali-2-more-journalists-arrested-under-cybercrime-law-for-criticizing-authorities/>), was [sentenced on 3 August to 12 months, six suspended](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>). In March 2026 a court [sentenced Sissoko to two years in prison](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610080295.html>) over an article doubting statements by Niger's president. The HAC [suspended the radio programme Allo Kledu for two months from 19 June 2026](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>).
