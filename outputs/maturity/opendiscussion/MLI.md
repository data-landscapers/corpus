# Mali

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In June 2026 the Maison de la Presse [sharply criticised the detention of two journalists](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>), according to Reporters Without Borders. The Malian Association of Private Print Media Publishers [denounced the 5 February 2026 arrest of the editor Youssouf Sissoko](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/02/09/press-freedom-under-fire-in-mali>). At the Pan-African Media Forum in June 2026 the editor Chahana Takiou [said journalists should be tried under the press-offence law rather than the cybercrime law](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found for Mali. The restrictions held run through [the cybercrime provisions of the Penal Code amendment of December 2024](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>), not through the network.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The cybercrime tribunal [sentenced the journalist Abdrahamane Keita on 14 September 2026 to two years in prison](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>). Takiou was [sentenced on 3 August 2026 to 12 months, six suspended](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>). [Sissoko was held in Bamako's central prison](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/02/09/press-freedom-under-fire-in-mali>), and the HAC [suspended the radio programme Allo Kledu for two months from 19 June 2026](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>).

***Also on file***. [The cybercrime tribunal sentenced the journalist Abdrahamane Keita of Le Temoin on 14 September 2026 to two years in prison and 1 million CFA francs damages](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>) over a remark about Kidal on a television programme, after [his detention in June 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); [Chahana Takiou of 22 Septembre was sentenced on 3 August 2026 to 12 months in prison, six suspended](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>), after remarks at the Pan-African Media Forum in June 2026; [Mohamed Youssouf Bathily (Ras Bath) was arrested in March 2023](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>) over comments on a death in custody, and [his trial began on 10 August 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); the cybercrime provisions of [the Penal Code amendment of December 2024](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>); the [Pole national de lutte contre la cybercriminalite](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>); [a ministerial instruction of 29 July 2026 applying the cybercrime provisions to filming, publication and commentary of sexual images](<https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether anyone in Mali went twelve months without being punished for what they said or published, or that the internet was cut.
