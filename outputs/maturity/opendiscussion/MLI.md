# Mali

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In June 2026 the Maison de la Presse [sharply criticised the detention of two journalists](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>), Reporters Without Borders says. The private print publishers' association [denounced the 5 February 2026 arrest of the editor Youssouf Sissoko](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/02/09/press-freedom-under-fire-in-mali>). In June 2026 the editor Chahana Takiou [criticised the authorities for trying journalists under the cybercrime law](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/08/04/journalist-in-mali-sentenced-to-a-year-in-prison>). He said [the press-offence law should apply instead](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found for Mali. The restrictions held run through [the cybercrime provisions of the Penal Code amendment of December 2024](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>), not through the network.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The cybercrime tribunal [sentenced the journalist Abdrahamane Keita to two years in prison](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>) on 14 September 2026. Takiou, [arrested on 8 June 2026](<https://cpj.org/2026/06/in-mali-2-more-journalists-arrested-under-cybercrime-law-for-criticizing-authorities/>), was [sentenced on 3 August to 12 months, six suspended](<https://lessor.ml/posts/mali-le-journaliste-chahana-takiou-ecope-dune-peine-de-12-mois-dont-6-avec-sursis-6a70f92a500ac>). [Sissoko was held in Bamako's central prison](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/02/09/press-freedom-under-fire-in-mali>). The HAC [suspended the radio programme Allo Kledu for two months from 19 June 2026](<https://rsf.org/en/broadcast-suspension-and-arrests-journalists-mali-nearly-six-years-transition-marked-repression>). On 27 October 2025 the former prime minister Moussa Mara [got two years, one firm](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/mali-former-prime-minister-sentenced-to-two-years-imprisonment/>), over posts in support of political prisoners.

***Also on file***. [Keita's sentence of 14 September 2026 also carried 1 million CFA francs in damages](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>), over a remark about Kidal on television. He had been [detained in June 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>). [Mohamed Youssouf Bathily (Ras Bath) was arrested in March 2023](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>) over comments on a death in custody. [His trial began on 10 August 2026](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>). The cybercrime provisions of [the Penal Code amendment of December 2024](<https://ipi.media/mali-military-junta-uses-cybercrime-provisions-to-target-journalists/>). The [Pole national de lutte contre la cybercriminalite](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/justice/cybercriminalite-le-journaliste-abdrahamane-keita-condamne-a-deux-ans-de-prison-ferme-3119923.html>). [A ministerial instruction of 29 July 2026 on sexual images](<https://bamada.net/communique-officiel-tolerance-zero-contre-les-reseaux-de-depravation-de-pedopornographie-et-de-traite-de-mineurs>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says the internet was cut in Mali. Nothing found shows twelve months with no one punished for speech.
