# Rwanda

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Also on file***. RSF says journalist Dieudonné Niyonsenga of Ishema TV was [sentenced to seven years in prison in 2021 for 'humiliating' state officials](<https://rsf.org/en/rwanda-rsf-sounds-alarm-president-kagame-s-horrific-press-freedom-record-election-looms>). He was still jailed in July 2024. Théoneste Nsengimana, director of Umubavu TV, was [arrested in October 2021](<https://rsf.org/en/rwanda-rsf-sounds-alarm-president-kagame-s-horrific-press-freedom-record-election-looms>) while organising an event with an opposition leader.

***Not found***. Nothing found says government policy is criticised in public inside the country. Nor does anything found show the internet cut, or anyone punished for speech, in the last twelve months.
