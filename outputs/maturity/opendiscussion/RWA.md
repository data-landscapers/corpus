# Rwanda

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** No criticism of a government policy from inside the country has been found. On 27 November 2025 ARTICLE 19, an international group, [told a pre-session of the Universal Periodic Review that speech offences were being used to suppress dissent](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/Rwanda_Article_19_statement.pdf>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** On 27 November 2025 ARTICLE 19 [said independent online news sites, including The Rwandan and Le Prophete, remained blocked](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/Rwanda_Article_19_statement.pdf>). It did not say who ordered the block. An ARM project brief says [26 online radio stations and websites of exiled critics were made inaccessible in 2019](<https://www.arm-project.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/ARM_Brief_09_2026.pdf>), after the Senate accused them of genocide denial. No law or court ruling barring such blocks has been found.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** ARTICLE 19 [said in November 2025 that journalists and online commentators continued to face arrests and prosecutions](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/Rwanda_Article_19_statement.pdf>), and that several YouTube journalists had been detained or intimidated. RSF says journalist Dieudonné Niyonsenga of Ishema TV was [sentenced to seven years in prison in 2021 for 'humiliating' state officials](<https://rsf.org/en/rwanda-rsf-sounds-alarm-president-kagame-s-horrific-press-freedom-record-election-looms>). He was still jailed in July 2024. Théoneste Nsengimana, director of Umubavu TV, was [arrested in October 2021](<https://rsf.org/en/rwanda-rsf-sounds-alarm-president-kagame-s-horrific-press-freedom-record-election-looms>) while organising an event with an opposition leader.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No criticism of a government policy published inside the country has been found. No source found names the authority that ordered the blocks.
