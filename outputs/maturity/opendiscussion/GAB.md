# Gabon

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Nine civil-society organisations, including ROLBG, SOS Prisonniers, Tournons la Page Gabon and the Ligue nationale des droits humains, [met in Libreville on 11 July 2026 and called the suspension unconstitutional and disproportionate](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-cinq-mois-de-silence-numerique-quand-le-remede-devient-le-poison/>), demanding its repeal. On 25 July 2026 the group APSAD [presented a report finding self-censorship among public-debate actors](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/>) and urged an end to blanket suspensions.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** The communications regulator, the HAC, suspended social media in Gabon by [decision no. 0002/HAC/2026, until further notice](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-cinq-mois-de-silence-numerique-quand-le-remede-devient-le-poison/>). The Presidency's spokesman [defended the suspension as temporary and conservatory on 17 July 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/suspension-des-reseaux-sociaux-la-presidence-defend-une-mesure-temporaire/>), with no lifting date given. [The HAC lifted the suspension, ordered on 17 February 2026, on 24 October 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-la-hac-leve-la-suspension-des-reseaux-sociaux/>), citing cooperation with TikTok, Meta and YouTube. [Maroc Telecom lists temporary restrictions on certain internet uses in Gabon for February 2026](<https://www.iam.ma/documents/66341/0/Maroc+Telecom+-+Rapport+financier+S1+2026+%282%29.pdf/261de29d-cee8-407f-1e28-dbb3a53f985f>). No bar on such cuts is held. [The communication authority suspended Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp across Gabon in February 2026 without express legal basis, Village de la Justice says](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/gabon-dote-droit-des-reseaux-sociaux-analyse-des-ordonnances-fevrier-2026,57009.html>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** Nothing is held on a person arrested or an outlet closed for what was said, nor on a law repealed. The nearest held fact is the [APSAD finding of self-censorship among public-debate actors and rules short of international standards](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/07/26/reseaux-sociaux-reguler-dans-le-respect-des-libertes-apsad/>).
