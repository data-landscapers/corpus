# Namibia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 5 May 2026 the director of the NMT Media Foundation [criticised in The Namibian the government's failure to bring the Access to Information Act into force](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-press-freedom-reputation-is-realbut-the-fine-print-tells-a-different-story/>). The director wrote as rapporteur of the 2026 African Media Barometer for Namibia. In December 2022 the Institute for Public Policy Research [called the draft Data Protection Bill 'not fit for purpose'](<https://ippr.org.na/publication/data-protection-bill-not-fit-for-purpose/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut of the internet or block of a service has been found for Namibia, and no bar on one. The nearest fact found is [a Cybercrime Bill, reported in January 2026 as in final polishing](<https://neweralive.na/data-legislations-under-final-polishing/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or closure for speech has been found, and no repeal of a speech offence. The nearest fact found is the director's account, in May 2026, of [an Access to Information Act passed but not in force](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-press-freedom-reputation-is-realbut-the-fine-print-tells-a-different-story/>).
