# Republic of the Congo

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The platform Congo Droit et Justice pour Tous et par Tous [ran a campaign denouncing how hard it is for citizens to obtain a passport](<https://www.fidh.org/fr/themes/defenseurs-des-droits-humains/congo-poursuite-du-harcelement-judiciaire-et-ouverture-du-proces-de-m-33088>). Its members spread it on social media. It announced a press conference on the causes for 29 May 2026.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** On 15 March 2026, the day of the presidential election, NetBlocks [recorded a nation-scale shutdown of the internet in Congo](<https://www.telecoms.com/telecoms-law/internet-goes-down-in-congo-in-time-for-the-election>). ISS Africa [calls it an ad hoc shutdown during the polls](<https://issafrica.org/iss-today/internet-shutdowns-won-t-solve-central-africa-s-political-crises>). It says the government's reasons do not appear to meet the UN's tests of legality and necessity. The UNESCO assessment says [access to the internet and to social networks was restricted nationwide during the election of 21 March 2021](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/evaluation-du-developpement-de-linternet-en-republique-du-congo>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** Police in plain clothes [arrested the human rights defender Ghislain Ondele Kanga at his home on 30 May 2026](<https://www.fidh.org/fr/themes/defenseurs-des-droits-humains/congo-poursuite-du-harcelement-judiciaire-et-ouverture-du-proces-de-m-33088>). They took him to the intelligence service in Brazzaville. He was referred to the prosecutor 56 days later and held in pre-trial detention. FIDH says he was charged with false dissemination and with declaring an unauthorised demonstration.

***Also on file***. [Loi n° 8-2001 du 12 novembre 2001 sur la liberté de l'information et de la communication](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/sicom-2026-necessite-de-faire-progresser-le-cadre-juridique-des-medias-au-congo-171648>).
