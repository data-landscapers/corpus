# Ethiopia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 31 October 2026 The Reporter [published an editorial, 'Press Freedom Is Not A Luxury!'](<https://cpj.org/2026/11/ethiopia-suspends-the-reporter-indefinitely-in-escalating-assault-on-independent-media/>). It criticised the authorities for treating independent reporting as a threat to national security. It called for the release of journalists held without charge.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** In October 2026 Access Now reported [new internet shutdowns in Tigray](<https://www.accessnow.org/indefinite-internet-shutdowns/>). Freedom House says [connectivity restrictions continued in Amhara Region](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/ethiopia/freedom-net/2026>), citing phone and internet outages in North Shewa Zone in February 2026. OONI data shows [Facebook, Telegram, TikTok and YouTube blocked from 9 February to July 2023](<https://explorer.ooni.org/findings/186069533301>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 2 November 2026 the Ethiopian Media Authority [suspended the registration of The Reporter's publisher indefinitely](<https://cpj.org/2026/11/ethiopia-suspends-the-reporter-indefinitely-in-escalating-assault-on-independent-media/>). It said the paper's reports undermine national interests. Amnesty International says Addis Standard's managing editor, Million Beyene, was [abducted by intelligence agents on 15 April 2026](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/>) and held for two weeks. Freedom House says the authorities [revoked Addis Standard's licence in February 2026](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/ethiopia/freedom-net/2026>). In December 2025 the Authority [permanently suspended two Deutsche Welle correspondents](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/12/15/another-blow-to-media-freedom-in-ethiopia>).

***Also on file***. [Proclamation No. 590/2008 on Freedom of the Mass Media and Access to Information](<https://justice.gov.et/en/law/a-proclamation-to-provide-for-freedom-of-the-mass-media-and-access-to-information/>).

***Not found***. No source names who ordered the 2026 outages in Tigray and Amhara. No law repealing a speech offence, and no bar on cutting the internet, has been found.
