# South Sudan

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 28 January 2025 [AMDISS, a South Sudanese media body, criticised the block on Facebook and TikTok](<https://www.africafex.org/digital-rights/amdiss-welcomes-lifting-of-the-ban-on-facebook-and-tiktok-and-calls-on-the-authorities-to-keep-the-platforms-accessible>). It said the block restrained press freedom and public debate.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** [On 22 January 2025 the National Communication Authority directed all internet service providers to block all social media accounts for 30 to 90 days](<https://cpj.org/2025/01/south-sudan-blocks-social-media-access-amid-unrest/>). Zain and MTN told users Facebook and TikTok were no longer accessible, and CPJ's sources said both were unreachable without a VPN on 23 January. [AMDISS urged the regulator in January 2025 to expedite lifting the ban](<https://www.africafex.org/digital-rights/amdiss-welcomes-lifting-of-the-ban-on-facebook-and-tiktok-and-calls-on-the-authorities-to-keep-the-platforms-accessible>). CPJ says that [in 2025 the authorities also suspended social media to block news from neighbouring Sudan](<https://cpj.org/2026/04/south-sudans-new-cybercrime-law-ramps-up-threat-of-jail-for-journalists/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No punishment for speech is on record. The nearest fact is the law. [President Kiir signed the Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2026 on 18 February 2026](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-offensives-and-penalties>).

***Also on file***. [On 22 January 2025 the National Communication Authority directed internet providers to block all social media accounts for 30 to 90 days, and Facebook and TikTok were unreachable without a VPN on 23 January](<https://cpj.org/2025/01/south-sudan-blocks-social-media-access-amid-unrest/>). [The Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2026, signed on 18 February 2026](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-offensives-and-penalties>). [The Cybercrimes and Computer Misuse Provisional Order 2021](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/briefs/Analysis-of-the-South-Sudan-Cyber-Crimes-and-Computer-Misuse-Provisional-Order-2021.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether anyone was punished for speech in the last twelve months. Nothing found says when the social media block ended.
