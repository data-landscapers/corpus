# Burkina Faso

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In May 2026 the General Student Union of Burkina Faso (UGEB) [issued a statement criticising the government's security record](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/>) and the military authorities' failure to restore security, as the CIVICUS Monitor reports. No other criticism of a government policy inside the country has been found.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** The Centre national de presse Norbert Zongo reported that in 2024 [the online platforms of RFI, France 24, Jeune Afrique and Le Monde, among others, remained inaccessible in Burkina Faso](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). No law or court ruling barring such cuts has been found.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 5 May 2026 the Higher Council for Communication [indefinitely suspended TV5 Monde broadcasts](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/>), and Human Rights Watch says the regulator [accused the broadcaster of disinformation and apology of terrorism](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/05/06/burkina-fasos-junta-escalates-attack-on-information>). On 26 May 2026 the Minister of Territorial Administration [suspended UGEB for three months after its statement, and the Ouagadougou prosecutor opened an investigation into its authors](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/military-authorities-continue-to-suspend-csos-including-largest-student-union/>). An August 2026 report relayed by The Nation also [cites the suspension of international broadcasters over alleged bias](<https://thenationonlineng.net/mfwa-sahel-juntas-weaponising-cybercrime-laws-to-silence-journalists/>).

***Also on file***. [The cybercrime brigade of the Ministry of Security (BCLCC)](<https://nouvellesafrique.com/burkina-faso-la-bclcc-renforce-sa-lutte-contre-les-fake-news/>); [the Conseil supérieur de la communication, which works with it against false information](<https://www.sidwaya.info/lutte-contre-la-desinformation-sensibiliser-les-internautes-pour-une-utilisation-responsable-des-reseaux-sociaux/>); [the regulator ARCOD, created by an organic law voted on 3 August 2026](<https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/>); [organic law 041-2023/ALT, which gives the Conseil supérieur de la communication powers over social media users with at least 5,000 followers](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says who ordered the block on foreign media websites, or whether the internet was cut in the last twelve months. Nothing found says whether anyone was arrested or jailed for what they said or published in that time.
