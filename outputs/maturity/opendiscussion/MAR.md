# Morocco

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [The draft organic law no. 53.25](<https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf>), whose articles 39, 40, 51, 51 bis and 53 set prison terms and fines for false news, rumours and synthetic content about elections, and for paid political advertising on foreign platforms.

***Not held***. Nothing held says government policy is criticised in public inside the country, that the internet was cut, or that anyone was punished for what they said.
