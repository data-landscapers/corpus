# Morocco

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Human Rights Watch says the youth movement GenZ212 [called nationwide protests from 27 September 2025](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/10/15/morocco-protests-met-with-repression-violence>). The movement criticised public spending on mega-sporting events and demanded more for health care and education. No other criticism of a government policy inside the country has been found. [Opposition members proposed deleting part of Article 51 bis of Organic Law 53.25 as a threat to free expression](<https://euromedai.org/en/ai-morocco-2026-elections-legal-framework/>). The government rejected the amendment (December 2025).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No internet cut, no blocked service and no law or court ruling barring one has been found. The nearest fact found is the set of nationwide protests that began on 27 September 2025, [as Human Rights Watch reports](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/10/15/morocco-protests-met-with-repression-violence>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 4 October 2025 [the Agadir Court of First Instance sentenced a man to four years in prison and a 50,000 dirham fine](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/10/15/morocco-protests-met-with-repression-violence>). Human Rights Watch reports the case. The sentence was for "inciting minor offenses and crimes via social media". No repeal of an offence of criminal defamation, false news, sedition or insulting the head of state has been found.

***Also on file***. [The draft organic law no. 53.25](<https://www.chambredesrepresentants.ma/sites/default/files/loi/Projet_loi_53.25_0.pdf>) sets prison terms and fines in articles 39, 40, 51, 51 bis and 53. They cover false news, rumours and synthetic content about elections, and paid political advertising on foreign platforms.

***Not found***. Nothing found says the internet was cut in the last twelve months. Nothing found says the country went a year with no one punished for what they said or published.
