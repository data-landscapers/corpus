# Côte d'Ivoire

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The opposition party PPA-CI, speaking through Justin Kone Katinan, [publicly denounced the arrest of one of its activists as a violation of freedom of expression](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20250903-c%C3%B4te-d-ivoire-un-cyberactiviste-interpell%C3%A9-apr%C3%A8s-avoir-%C3%A9voqu%C3%A9-les-d%C3%A9lib%C3%A9rations-du-conseil-constitutionnel>) on 3 September 2025. The activist had spoken in a social-media video about the Constitutional Council's deliberations on the candidate list.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found. The nearest held fact is the Ministry of Communication's [national campaign against disinformation and its local information integrity committee in Tonkpi](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-man-le-ministere-de-la-communication-installe-un-relais-pour-lintegrite-de-linformation-dans-le-tonkpi/>), reported in September 2026.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** [RFI reports that PPA-CI activist Mamadou Zigui was arrested on 2 September 2025](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20250903-c%C3%B4te-d-ivoire-un-cyberactiviste-interpell%C3%A9-apr%C3%A8s-avoir-%C3%A9voqu%C3%A9-les-d%C3%A9lib%C3%A9rations-du-conseil-constitutionnel>) and that the Abidjan court sentenced activist Gala Kolebi on 30 June 2025 to 18 months and a 500,000 CFA franc fine for spreading false news. The prosecutor says a nurse [received 36 months and a 5 million fine on 18 July 2025](<https://www.afriksoir.net/cote-divoire-diakite-moussa-auteur-menaces-contre-opposition-defere-le-procureur-met-en-garde/>) over a social-media comment about a state institution.

***Also on file***. [the Ministry of Communication's national campaign against disinformation and its local information integrity committee in Tonkpi](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-man-le-ministere-de-la-communication-installe-un-relais-pour-lintegrite-de-linformation-dans-le-tonkpi/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says the internet was cut, or that any offence of speech was repealed.
