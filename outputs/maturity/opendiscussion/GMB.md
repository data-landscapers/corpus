# Gambia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [The Access to Information Act](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>); [the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>) and the draft Journalists Registration Guidelines, 2026, both of the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority; [its public consultation on seven draft instruments, 1 to 10 April 2026](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>); [a draft framework on broadcasting and online content with child-protection provisions](<https://www.voicegambia.com/le-ministre-de-linformation-exhorte-les-medias-a-proteger-les-enfants-vulnerables-dans-leurs-reportages/>), from the Ministry of Information, Media and Broadcasting Services.

***Not held***. Nothing held says government policy is criticised in public inside the country, that the internet was cut, or that anyone was punished for what they said.
