# Gambia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 14 May 2026 the Gambia Press Union, in a statement to the African Commission's 87th session in Banjul, [criticised the Cybercrime Bill 2023, the Communications Bill 2025 and the Criminal Offences Act 2025 as threats to media freedom](<https://gambiaj.com/society/human-rights/gpu-warns-press-freedom-gains-mask-growing-threats-against-journalists-in-the-gambia/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing is held on a cut to internet access, a block on a platform, or a bar against either in the Gambia. The nearest held fact is the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority's [draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>), put out for public consultation in April 2026.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The Gambia Press Union said in May 2026 that [criminal defamation and false publication online had previously been struck down by the Supreme Court](<https://gambiaj.com/society/human-rights/gpu-warns-press-freedom-gains-mask-growing-threats-against-journalists-in-the-gambia/>), and gave no date for the ruling.

***Also on file***. [The Access to Information Act](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>); [the draft Broadcasting and Online Content Regulations, 2026](<https://pura.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Broadcasting-and-Online-Content-Regulations-2026-.pdf>) and the draft Journalists Registration Guidelines, 2026, both of the Public Utilities Regulatory Authority; [its public consultation on seven draft instruments, 1 to 10 April 2026](<https://pura.gm/public-consultation-guidelines-and-notice/>); [a draft framework on broadcasting and online content with child-protection provisions](<https://www.voicegambia.com/le-ministre-de-linformation-exhorte-les-medias-a-proteger-les-enfants-vulnerables-dans-leurs-reportages/>), from the Ministry of Information, Media and Broadcasting Services.

***Not found***. Nothing found says the internet was cut, or that anyone was arrested or punished for what they said or published in the last twelve months. Nothing found says a law, a ruling of the country's own court or an undertaking given in court in the last three years bars the government from cutting the internet.
