# Mauritius

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Civil society groups and businesses [opposed the November 2024 social media suspension](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/mauritius-social-media-suspension-reversed-after-backlash/>), and members of the party Linion Reform protested in front of the National Assembly on 1 November 2024. Human Rights Watch [reports swift and widespread outcry](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/11/07/mauritius-ends-social-media-ban-ahead-elections>) from business, civil society and opposition politicians inside Mauritius.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** On 1 November 2024 the Information and Communications Authority [directed internet service providers to suspend access to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X](<https://monitor.civicus.org/explore/mauritius-social-media-suspension-reversed-after-backlash/>); the ban was lifted on 2 November after 24 hours. Human Rights Watch [says the suspension was to run until after the 10 November elections](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/11/07/mauritius-ends-social-media-ban-ahead-elections>) and the government rescinded it a day later.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In August 2026 the ministry [reminded the public of the sanctions for unwanted content online](<https://lexpress.mu/s/le-ministere-rappelle-les-sanctions-contre-les-contenus-indesirables-en-ligne-561596>).

***Also on file***. [Sections 16, 17 and 23 of the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021](<https://lexpress.mu/s/le-ministere-rappelle-les-sanctions-contre-les-contenus-indesirables-en-ligne-561596>); [a planned takedown agreement between CERT-MU and Meta](<https://lexpress.mu/s/avinash-ramtohul-il-faut-apporter-un-peu-de-discipline-dans-les-reseaux-sociaux-563444>) and [a planned banned-word list for Meta platforms](<https://lexpress.mu/s/avinash-ramtohul-il-faut-apporter-un-peu-de-discipline-dans-les-reseaux-sociaux-563444>); [a planned identity-verification mechanism for social media](<https://lexpress.mu/node/558887>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says the country went the last twelve months with no one punished for what they said or published.
