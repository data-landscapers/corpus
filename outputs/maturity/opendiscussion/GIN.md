# Guinea

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The Union pour la defense des consommateurs, through its president Bany Sidibe, [denounced the social media restrictions on 4 August 2026 as a state decision harming the right to information](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/08/04/guinee-bany-sidibe-denonce-les-restrictions-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux-et-la-degradation-de-linternet/>). It and the Association des blogueurs de Guinee [demanded the return of access to all social networks](<https://www.jeuneafrique.com/1830582/politique/guinee-des-reseaux-sociaux-coupes-alors-que-mamadi-doumbouya-est-a-letranger/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Facebook, YouTube and TikTok were [blocked from 28 July 2026, according to the bloggers association ABLOGUI](<https://www.guinee360.com/28/07/2026/perturbation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-guinee-lablogui-denonce-un-blocage-cible-de-facebook-youtube-et-tiktok/>), and Jeune Afrique [reported WhatsApp difficulties too](<https://www.jeuneafrique.com/1830582/politique/guinee-des-reseaux-sociaux-coupes-alors-que-mamadi-doumbouya-est-a-letranger/>). On 17 September 2026 the HAC [ordered the telecoms regulator ARPT to block all France 24 digital access points and Canal+ Guinée to cut its signal](<https://www.africaguinee.com/la-chaine-france-24-interdite-de-diffusion-en-guinee-larpt-et-canal-requisitionnes-par-la-hac/>). [The ECOWAS Court found that restrictions on internet access from October to December 2020 violated rights to information and expression](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 17 September 2026 the HAC [revoked the national broadcasting authorisation of France 24 and withdrew its correspondents accreditations](<https://www.africaguinee.com/la-chaine-france-24-interdite-de-diffusion-en-guinee-larpt-et-canal-requisitionnes-par-la-hac/>). The Media Foundation for West Africa report, as reported on 14 August 2026, [cites the suspension of critical media outlets in Guinea](<https://thenationonlineng.net/mfwa-sahel-juntas-weaponising-cybercrime-laws-to-silence-journalists/>). No repeal has been found.
