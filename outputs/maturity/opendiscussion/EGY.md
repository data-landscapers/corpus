# Egypt

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [Freedom House reports the news site Cairo 24 was blocked in November 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>) and [the outlet Zawia3 in February 2025](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>), within a wider blocking of news websites by the Supreme Council for Media Regulation; [more than 100 people were arbitrarily detained in June and July 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>) after using social media to call for protests over power cuts and rising prices; [the sentence of the blogger Alaa Abdel Fattah was extended by two years in September 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>); [the Supreme Council for Media Regulation refused a licence to Fakartany in October 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>); [the Freedom on the Net 2025 scores](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>) of 14 of 25, 9 of 35 and 5 of 40.

***Not held***. No source dated in the last three years says a newspaper, broadcaster, party or group inside the country criticised a government policy in public, and none says the internet was cut in the last twelve months.
