# Egypt

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Khaled Elbalshy, head of the Egyptian Journalists Syndicate, was [quoted by Al Manassa on 13 April 2026 rejecting all blocking of news websites](<https://almanassa.com/en/news/31271>). He also rejected the licensing justification the regulator gave for its move against the news site Egyptke.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Freedom House reports that [the news site Cairo 24 was blocked in November 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>) and [the outlet Zawia3 in February 2025](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>), within a wider blocking of news websites by the Supreme Council for Media Regulation. In February 2026 Access Now and other groups said [the authorities were still blocking independent media sites, at least 630 websites in all](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/human-rights-abuses-facilitated-by-applogic-networks-in-egypt/>). No bar on cuts or blocks has been found.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** [Human Rights Watch says security forces arrested the six journalists of the Matsda2sh newsroom on 28 and 29 September 2026](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/02/egypt-authorities-arrest-entire-investigative-newsroom>), and the Interior Ministry said they were suspected of preparing fabricated news. Al Manassa reports that [security forces arrested Egyptke's editor-in-chief and CEO in December 2025](<https://almanassa.com/en/news/31271>) after a complaint over a poultry story. Freedom House, citing Amnesty International and Human Rights Watch, reports [more than 100 people detained in June and July 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>) after calling for protests on social media, and [two years added to the sentence of the blogger Alaa Abdel Fattah in September 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>).

***Also on file***. [Freedom on the Net 2025 scores, Freedom House (A 14/25, B 9/35, C 5/40)](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>); [the refusal of a licence to Fakartany by the Supreme Council for Media Regulation in October 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether the journalists arrested in 2025 and 2026 were charged or jailed, or whether any outlet was suspended or closed for what it published.
