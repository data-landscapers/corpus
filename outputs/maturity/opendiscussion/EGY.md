# Egypt

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 13 April 2026 Al Manassa [quoted Khaled Elbalshy, head of the Egyptian Journalists Syndicate, rejecting all blocking of news websites](<https://almanassa.com/en/news/31271>). He also rejected the licensing reason the regulator gave for its move against the news site Egyptke.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** In February 2026 Access Now and other groups said [the authorities were still blocking independent media sites, at least 630 websites in all](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/human-rights-abuses-facilitated-by-applogic-networks-in-egypt/>). In November 2026 CPJ said [the site of the outlet Zawia3 is blocked inside Egypt](<https://cpj.org/2026/11/how-egypts-laws-are-used-to-choke-its-independent-press/>). Freedom House reports that [the news site Cairo 24 was blocked in November 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>). The Supreme Council for Media Regulation blocked [Zawia3 in February 2025](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>), in a wider blocking of news websites. No bar on cuts or blocks has been found.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 27 October 2026 a Cairo court [fined Mada Masr editor Lina Attalah EGP 3 million and ordered the news website closed](<https://manassa.news/en/news/34822>). The charge was operating a website without a licence. Human Rights Watch says security forces [arrested the six journalists of the Matsda2sh newsroom on 28 and 29 September 2026](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/02/egypt-authorities-arrest-entire-investigative-newsroom>). On 10 November 2026 the UN human rights office said [they remained in detention](<https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168778>). In December 2025 security forces [arrested Egyptke's editor-in-chief and chief executive](<https://almanassa.com/en/news/31271>) after a complaint over a poultry story.

***Also on file***. [Freedom on the Net 2025 scores, Freedom House (A 14/25, B 9/35, C 5/40)](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>). [The refusal of a licence to Fakartany by the Supreme Council for Media Regulation in October 2024](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>). In September 2024 [two years were added to the sentence of the blogger Alaa Abdel Fattah](<https://freedomhouse.org/country/egypt/freedom-net/2025>).

***Not found***. No law repealing a speech offence has been found.
