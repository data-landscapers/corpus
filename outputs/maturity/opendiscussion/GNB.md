# Guinea-Bissau

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** No source found reports public criticism of government policy in the window. The nearest held fact is that on 26 November 2025, [the day the military seized power, citizens reported restrictions on social platforms](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** On 26 November 2025, [citizens reported widespread restrictions on Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>), which the Media Foundation for West Africa says [the military imposed](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>). The source gives no end date, and no held source names a bar on such cuts.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No source found reports a person punished for speech, or a punishing law removed. The nearest held fact is the same report, which says the military imposed [restrictions on social platforms on the day it took power](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/guinea-bissau-military-takeover-triggers-media-suspension-online-restrictions/>).

***Also on file***. [The Digital Integrity Law, passed by the Conselho Nacional de Transição, on online falsehoods](<https://ultimahoragb.com/2026/05/23/cnt-aprova-a-lei-de-integridade-digital-para-travar-falsas-informacoes-e-atingir-cidadaos-que-destilam-o-odio-no-pais/>); [the Comissão de Verificação Digital it created, which refers cases to the Ministério Público](<https://maisafrika.com/noticias/ultimahora/guine-bissau-aprova-lei-contra-fake-news/>).

***Not found***. No source found says when the social media restrictions ended, or that anyone criticised policy in public or was punished for speech.
