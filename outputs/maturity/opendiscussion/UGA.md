# Uganda

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The Uganda Law Society [condemned the shutdown, its vice president saying no state of emergency had been declared so the order was illegal and unconstitutional](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>). At a Kampala briefing on 27 February 2026, UGANET and other civil society organisations [criticised blanket internet shutdowns](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/csos-raise-alarm-over-digital-violations-affe-NV_229061_092026>) and urged legal safeguards against them.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** The Uganda Communications Commission [directed all mobile operators and internet providers to suspend public internet access from 18:00 on 13 January 2026](<https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/314425/govt-orders-temporary-public-internet-shutdown>), two days before the presidential election. Access Now [describes it as a cut of internet and social media](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>); [the blackout lasted four days](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>). On 18 January the Commission's executive director said [social media and messaging applications stayed restricted](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PRESS-BRIEFING-BY-THE-EXECUTIVE-DIRECTOR-UGANDA-COMMUNICATIONS-COMMISSION-ON-THE-RESTORATION-OF-PUBLIC-INTERNET-ACCESS-%E2%80%93-18TH-JANUARY-2026-.pdf>) after general access returned, and they were still blocked [more than a week later](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** Nothing is held on anyone punished for speech. The nearest held fact is that a CIPESA submission to Uganda's fourth-cycle Universal Periodic Review [names sections 24 and 25 of the Computer Misuse Act](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Uganda_UPR_Review.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [The Communications Act, Cap 103](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Uganda_UPR_Review.pdf>); [the Computer Misuse (Amendment) Act, 2022](<https://cipesa.org/2026/08/uganda-and-zimbabwes-fourth-cycle-uprs-must-turn-digital-progress-into-stronger-rights-protections/>); [the 2021 near-total blackout and social media block](<https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/314425/govt-orders-temporary-public-internet-shutdown>); [the 2016 election social media block](<https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/314425/govt-orders-temporary-public-internet-shutdown>); [the Electronic Court Case Management System](<https://mulengeranews.com/uls-slams-court-restrictions-after-besigye-collapsed-during-treason-trial/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found shows a twelve-month stretch with no cut or block of the internet or a service.
