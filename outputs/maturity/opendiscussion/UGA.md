# Uganda

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The Uganda Law Society [condemned the shutdown as illegal and unconstitutional](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>). Its vice president said no state of emergency had been declared. On 27 February 2026 UGANET and other groups [criticised blanket internet shutdowns](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/health/csos-raise-alarm-over-digital-violations-affe-NV_229061_092026>) and urged legal safeguards. Unwanted Witness and Amnesty International [criticised the continued Facebook suspension](<https://observer.ug/news/uganda-to-maintain-facebook-ban-ict-minister/>) in March 2026.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** The Uganda Communications Commission [ordered operators to suspend public internet access from 18:00 on 13 January 2026](<https://nilepost.co.ug/2026-election-watch/314425/govt-orders-temporary-public-internet-shutdown>). That was two days before the presidential election. Access Now [describes it as a cut of internet and social media](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>). [The blackout lasted four days](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>). On 18 January the Commission said [social media and messaging stayed restricted](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PRESS-BRIEFING-BY-THE-EXECUTIVE-DIRECTOR-UGANDA-COMMUNICATIONS-COMMISSION-ON-THE-RESTORATION-OF-PUBLIC-INTERNET-ACCESS-%E2%80%93-18TH-JANUARY-2026-.pdf>). They were still blocked [more than a week later](<https://www.independent.co.ug/the-cost-of-ugandas-internet-shutdown/>). On 27 March 2026 the ICT minister of state said [the Facebook suspension, in place since January 2021, would stay](<https://observer.ug/news/uganda-to-maintain-facebook-ban-ict-minister/>) until Meta meets the government's conditions.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In December 2025 a student [was jailed for six months for TikTok posts claiming President Museveni was ill](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/student-sentenced-to-6-months-in-prison-for-spreading-false-info-on-tiktok-5292430>). In August 2025 a magistrate [convicted NUP member Juma Musuuza of hate speech and malicious information](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tiktoker-madubarah-convicted-of-hate-speech-malicious-information-5169640>). His TikTok posts were about the President, his son and the Speaker. A CIPESA submission to the UN review [names sections 24 and 25 of the Computer Misuse Act](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Uganda_UPR_Review.pdf>).
