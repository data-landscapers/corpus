# Benin

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 3 November 2026 [the media professionals' union UPMB and Amnesty International Bénin called for a review of articles 550 and following of the Code du numérique](<https://quotidienlexpress.bj/2026/11/04/code-du-numerique-lupmb-et-amnesty-international-benin-plaident-pour-une-relecture-des-textes/>). They cited what the articles mean for journalists, bloggers and citizens.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut to internet access or block on a platform in Benin has been found. No bar against one has been found either. The nearest held fact is [the authorisation of 89 online media outlets by the Haute autorité de l'audiovisuel et de la communication](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/regulation-des-medias-en-ligne-89-organes-autorises-par-la-haac>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 1 September 2026 CPJ said journalists Comlan Hugues Sossoukpè and Ali Moumouni [remained in prison without trial](<https://cpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/EN-CPJ-Letter-to-His-Excellency-the-President-of-Benin-September-1-2026.pdf>). They face charges including harassment through electronic communications, which CPJ says answer their critical reporting. Journalist Angela Kpeidja was [detained on 16 December 2025 over two Facebook posts](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/benin-prosecutor-seeks-two-year-prison-term-for-journalist-over-facebook-posts/>) questioning the security authorities on the day of the attempted coup. CPJ says she [was sentenced to 12 months in prison](<https://cpj.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/EN-CPJ-Letter-to-His-Excellency-the-President-of-Benin-September-1-2026.pdf>). Editor Olivier Allochémé was [placed in pre-trial detention on 16 October 2025](<https://mfwa.org/country-highlights/benin-journalist-detained-for-publishing-false-news-via-social-media-about-president-talon/>) for publishing false news on social media, over a Facebook editorial about President Talon.

***Also on file***. [the authorisation of 89 online media outlets by the Haute autorité de l'audiovisuel et de la communication](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/regulation-des-medias-en-ligne-89-organes-autorises-par-la-haac>); [Cloudwards' 2026 internet freedom ranking of Africa](<https://fraternite.bj/liberte-internet-en-afrique-le-benin-dans-le-top-5-du-classement-2026/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says the internet was cut or a platform blocked in Benin in the last twelve months.
