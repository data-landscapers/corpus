# Somalia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In August 2026 the National Union of Somali Journalists [criticised the draft Penal Code adopted by the Council of Ministers in December 2025](<https://rsf.org/en/somalia-new-penal-code-risks-further-criminalising-journalism-under-guise-reform>). It said the code risks a more restrictive climate for independent journalism and was drawn up without meaningful consultation. In a UN review submission of 28 October 2025, Defenders Coalition Somalia [criticised the Official Information Bill approved in March 2024](<https://defenddefenders.org/federal-republic-of-somalia-joint-submission-to-the-un-universal-periodic-review-52nd-session-of-the-upr-working-group/>). It objected to the bill's broad exemptions and its lack of independent oversight.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut of a connection has been found since March 2025. On 2 March 2025 WardheerNews says [its site was blocked on Hormuud Telecommunications' network](<https://wardheernews.com/somali-government-launches-brutal-crackdown-on-free-speech-journalists-arrested-websites-blocked/>) across Mogadishu and four member states. It puts the block down to the government. In 2026 the government [closed 82,000 social media pages it called extremist-linked](<https://www.hiiraan.com/security4/2026/Sep/206174/somali_government_closes_82000_extremistlinked_social_media_pages_in_2026.aspx>), Hiiraan reported on 4 September 2026.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 25 June 2026 police [arrested journalist Abdukadir Abukar Ali](<https://geeskafreedomcentre.org/en/escalating-crackdown-on-freedom-of-expression-in-somalia-as-journalists-and-social-media-users-arbitrarily-detained/>) after he published a video questioning the fairness of a trial. They accused him of insulting judicial institutions. On 30 June 2026 intelligence officers detained Mascuud Warsame, founder of the outlet Xogbile, which has criticised the South West State administration. On 18 March 2025 [armed police took the Risaala Media station in Mogadishu off air and arrested five journalists](<https://defenddefenders.org/federal-republic-of-somalia-joint-submission-to-the-un-universal-periodic-review-52nd-session-of-the-upr-working-group/>).
