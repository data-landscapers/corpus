# Somalia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Defenders Coalition Somalia, a Somali civil-society group, with DefendDefenders, [criticised the Official Information Bill approved by the Council of Ministers in March 2024](<https://defenddefenders.org/federal-republic-of-somalia-joint-submission-to-the-un-universal-periodic-review-52nd-session-of-the-upr-working-group/>) in a joint UN review submission dated 28 October 2025. They objected to its broad confidentiality and national-security exemptions, its lack of independent oversight and the secrecy in which it was drafted.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut of a connection has been found. The nearest held fact is that the government [closed 82,000 social media pages it called extremist-linked in 2026](<https://www.hiiraan.com/security4/2026/Sep/206174/somali_government_closes_82000_extremistlinked_social_media_pages_in_2026.aspx>), as Hiiraan reported on 4 September 2026.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 18 March 2025 [armed police stormed the Risaala Media station in Mogadishu, took it off air and arrested five named journalists without a court warrant](<https://defenddefenders.org/federal-republic-of-somalia-joint-submission-to-the-un-universal-periodic-review-52nd-session-of-the-upr-working-group/>), after it was first to report the bombing of the president's convoy. Amnesty International said in May 2026 that [journalists in Somalia were subjected to arbitrary arrests and prosecution](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2026/05/east-and-southern-africa-media-freedom-under-attack-amid-prevailing-impunity/>) as the government stepped up attempts to censor reporting on insecurity, corruption and forced evictions.
