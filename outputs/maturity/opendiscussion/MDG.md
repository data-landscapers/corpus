# Madagascar

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 3 September 2026 Madagascar-Tribune [questioned the government's cybercrime bill n°042/2026](<https://www.madagascar-tribune.com/Loi-sur-la-cybercriminalite-l-Etat-renforce-les-controles-alors-que-l-acces-a-l.html>). It said the permanent access powers of the bill's new digital police raise issues of public freedoms and of freedom of expression. It contrasted the bill with the stalled access-to-information bill.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut of internet access, and no bar on one, has been found. The nearest fact found is the [Council of Ministers decision of 8 July 2026 to block pornographic websites](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes>). That was a block of named sites, not of access.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 8 January 2026 the Presidency said [people and pages were being prosecuted for insulting senior officials online](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601090348.html>). Amnesty International says [at least nine Gen Z leaders were arrested in April 2026](<https://www.amnesty.org/fr/latest/campaigns/2026/11/a-year-after-the-military-coup-authoritarian-practices-dash-gen-z-aspirations-for-a-new-madagascar/>). It says they were held for being in a WhatsApp group or messaging an opposition figure. In January 2025 journalist Fernand Cello was [charged with false news over a Facebook post](<https://cpj.org/2025/02/in-madagascar-journalist-detained-on-false-news-charge-over-facebook-post/>).

***Also on file***. [The Upin, the digital protection and investigation unit in bill n°042/2026](<https://www.madagascar-tribune.com/Loi-sur-la-cybercriminalite-l-Etat-renforce-les-controles-alors-que-l-acces-a-l.html>); [the blocking of websites carrying obscene content](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/madagascar-annonce-le-blocage-des-sites-diffusant-des-contenus-obscenes>).

***Not found***. No law repealing a speech offence, and no bar on cutting the internet, has been found.
