# Eswatini

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** An opinion piece in Independent News Eswatini says that [the Swazi Bridge outlet published an investigation on 12 April 2026](<https://independentnews.co.sz/42365/opinion/the-real-google-problem-in-eswatini-is-not-the-mou/>) holding the ICT ministry to account for how its memorandum of understanding with Google Cloud EMEA was communicated, which the piece calls a failure of communication.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block of a connection or a site has been found. The nearest held fact is that MISA, the media body, [engaged the Eswatini government on expression and media freedom](<https://misa.org/blog/misa-engages-eswatini-government-on-expression-and-media-freedom/>), in a blog dated 27 February 2026.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The Southern Africa Litigation Centre says that [Mphandlana Shongwe was charged under the Suppression of Terrorism Act in 2009 for shouting political slogans at a meeting](<https://www.southernafricalitigationcentre.org/oral-statement-under-item-3-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa/>), and that the charge still stands untried. No removal of such an offence has been found.

***Also on file***. [An access to information law, promised](<https://misa.org/blog/misa-engages-eswatini-government-on-expression-and-media-freedom/>).

***Not found***. No source says the country went the last twelve months with no one punished for what they said or published.
