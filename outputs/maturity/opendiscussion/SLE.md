# Sierra Leone

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In July 2025 the regulator NaTCA [withdrew broadcast frequencies from Truth Media and G8 Media Group](<https://africapublicity.com/sierra-leones-press-freedom-under-pressure-mrcg-flags-arrests-assaults-and-digital-threats-against-journalists/>). The Sierra Leone Association of Journalists raised concerns with the information minister and NaTCA's director general. It warned that such actions could undermine press freedom.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut of the internet or block of a service has been found for Sierra Leone, and no bar on one. The nearest fact found is the same [withdrawal of two broadcasters' frequencies in July 2025](<https://africapublicity.com/sierra-leones-press-freedom-under-pressure-mrcg-flags-arrests-assaults-and-digital-threats-against-journalists/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The TV personality Hawa Hunt was [arrested on 22 December 2024](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/01/sierra-leone-fair-trial-hawa-hunt/>) and held for over a month. She was charged with insulting the President and First Lady in a video. In June 2025 police [arrested Citizens Radio reporter Hassan Kamara](<https://africapublicity.com/sierra-leones-press-freedom-under-pressure-mrcg-flags-arrests-assaults-and-digital-threats-against-journalists/>) after a post on the football association election. [The Public Order (Amendment) Act, 2020 repealed the part of the 1965 Act on defamatory and seditious libel](<https://www.parliament.gov.sl/uploads/bill_files/The%20Public%20Order%20Amendment%20Act,%202020.pdf>).
