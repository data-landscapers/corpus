# Cabo Verde

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Policy is criticised in public in Cabo Verde. [On 10 November 2026 the opposition party UCID questioned the government's digital transition](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/parlamento-ucid-alerta-para-riscos-da-digitalizacao-nas-micro-pequenas-e-medias-empresas>). Its deputy Zilda Oliveira feared a higher tax burden on small firms. [In August 2025 the RECADDH human-rights network denounced prosecutions and intimidation of journalists](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>). It called for the cases to be dropped.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block of the internet, a social media service or a news site has been found for Cabo Verde. No law or ruling barring one has been found either. [The RECADDH statement of August 2025 names prosecutions of journalists, not any cut](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** [RECADDH says two journalists were accused of qualified disobedience over reports on a death](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>). In August 2025 it named them as Herminio Silves and Daniel Almeida. No repeal of an offence of defamation, false news or sedition has been found.

[The open government forum, under the modernisation ministry](<https://www.governo.cv/queremos-um-forum-mais-representativo-e-mais-plural-em-materia-de-governacao-aberta-em-cabo-verde-eurico-monteiro/>). [eParticipa, the Casa do Cidadão's participation platform](<https://eparticipa.gov.cv/sobre/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether anyone was punished for speech in the last twelve months. The accusations of August 2025 are the latest found.
