# Cabo Verde

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The RECADDH human-rights network [publicly denounced an escalation of judicial prosecutions and intimidation of journalists in Cabo Verde](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>) on 8 August 2025. It called for the cases against the journalists to be dropped, so criticism of how the state treats the press is voiced openly inside the country.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block of the internet, a social media service or a news site has been found for Cabo Verde, and no law or ruling barring one. The nearest held fact is the same RECADDH statement, which [names prosecutions and intimidation of journalists, not any cut](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** RECADDH says journalists Herminio Silves and Daniel Almeida are [accused of qualified disobedience over reports on a death](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/recaddh-contra-escalada-de-perseguicoes-judiciais-e-ataques-a-jornalistas-em-cabo-verde/>), as of 8 August 2025, and demands that the cases be dropped. No repeal of an offence of defamation, false news, sedition or insulting the head of state is held.

***Not found***. Nothing found says whether anyone in Cabo Verde was punished for what they said or published in the last twelve months beyond the accusations of August 2025, or that no one was.
