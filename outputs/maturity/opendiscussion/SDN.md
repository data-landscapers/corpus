# Sudan

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The Sudanese Journalists Syndicate [condemned the detention of journalist Gurashi Awad on 14 August 2026](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>). It [called for the repeal of the 2007 Cybercrime Act](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>). In October 2026 it [rejected a decree extending a Criminal Act provision to electronic publishing](<https://en.sudannow.net/sudanese-journalists-syndicate-rejects-criminal-law-amendment-and-cybercrime-mechanism/>). In October 2025 its secretary general [called the cabinet's changes to the Informatics Crimes Law a threat to press freedom](<https://www.dabangasudan.org/ar/all-news/article/%d8%aa%d8%b9%d8%af%d9%8a%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a6%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d9%85>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** MTN Sudan [suspended internet service in the afternoons of 13 to 15 April 2026](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2026/04/its-exam-season-in-sudan-and-that-means-internet-shutdowns/>), on government directives. Those were the days of the national school examinations. The source calls it the sixth year running of exam-period shutdowns. Access Now says the government [ordered 10 internet shutdowns during exams between 13 and 23 April 2026](<https://www.accessnow.org/noexamshutdown-one-step-forward-another-step-back/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In August 2026 a criminal court in El Obeid [sentenced pharmacist Ahmed Suleiman Ahmed al-Waqi to six years in prison](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudan-court-jails-pharmacist-for-six-years-over-anti-war-message/>). His offence was a private phone conversation opposing the war. He was tried under the Criminal Act and the Cybercrime Law.

[Freedom House's Freedom on the Net 2025 score of 27 of 100](<https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025>). Older: [a four-year sentence in February 2025 for insulting and defaming state leaders](<https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025>). [A journalist arrested in July 2024 for criticising a governor](<https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025>). [Journalist Abdul Jalil Mohammed Abdul Jalil, arrested in May 2025](<https://freedomhouse.org/index.php/country/sudan/freedom-net/2025>). [Internet blackouts in June 2019, June 2022, April 2023 and February 2024](<https://timep.org/2024/09/19/internet-in-conflict-sudans-battle-for-connection/>).
