# Liberia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 24 September 2026 the Press Union of Liberia, the Center for Media Studies and several civil society groups [called the Cybercrime Act's provisions on false information and offensive communication vague and a weapon to silence critics](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609240628.html>). On 31 August 2026 the Center for Transparency and Accountability in Liberia and other groups [pressed the Liberia Telecommunications Authority chairman to disclose revenue from the gateway monitoring system](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/pressure-mounts-on-massaquoi-to-disclose-revenue/>), which he has refused to do. In October 2026 the Press Union [called for the withdrawal of the Civil Service Agency's confidentiality agreement for civil servants](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/24/pul-condemns-governments-assault-on-freedom-of-information-and-transparency-calls-for-immediate-withdrawal-of-confidentiality-policy-undermining-the-foi-act/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No held source reports a cut or block of internet access, or a law or ruling that bars one. The nearest held fact is [the Liberia Telecommunications Authority's temporary management of the International Gateway Monitoring System](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/pressure-mounts-on-massaquoi-to-disclose-revenue/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** In September 2026 [police arrested Jerry Nyanti Wratto in Monrovia, charged him and sent him to court under the Cybercrime Act](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609240628.html>) over Facebook and TikTok posts about the Vice President. [The Vice President asked for his release](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/koung-seeks-release-of-ai-image-suspect/>), and [no court outcome is given](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberias-cybercrime-law-faces-its-first-real-trial-as-vp-koung-case-exposes-liberias-widening-fault-lines/>). A 2019 law, [the KAK Act, decriminalised sedition and criminal libel against the President](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberias-cybercrime-law-faces-its-first-real-trial-as-vp-koung-case-exposes-liberias-widening-fault-lines/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [The Cybercrime Act of 2025](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609240628.html>); [the International Gateway Monitoring System](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/pressure-mounts-on-massaquoi-to-disclose-revenue/>).

***Not held***. No source held gives the outcome of the Wratto case, a cut or block of internet access, or a bar on one.
