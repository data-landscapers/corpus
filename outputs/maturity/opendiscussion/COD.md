# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

***Noted, not assessed***. [an internet shutdown at Goma in 2025, listed by Access Now among that year's shutdowns, with no orderer or end date given](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>); [a Senate bill to bar social media for children under 13](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/drc-senate-considers-social-media-ban-for-children-under-13>); [the 2023 digital code and the interministerial task force on the information space](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/30/rdc-le-gouvernement-renforce-son-dispositif-de-protection-de-lespace-informationnel-face>); [article 360 of the digital code, on false information](<https://kinshasamagazine.cd/rdc-le-parquet-mobilise-les-opj-contre-les-infractions-commises-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

***Not held***. No source dated in the last three years says a newspaper, broadcaster, party or group inside the country criticised a government policy in public, and none says the internet was cut in the last twelve months.
