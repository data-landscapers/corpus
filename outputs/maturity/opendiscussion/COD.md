# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** At a side event of the African Commission's 89th session in October 2026, Fidèle Mbilizi Mutimanwa of the National League of Indigenous Pygmy Associations of the Congo (LINAPYCO) [criticised the state for leaving the Commission's 2022 Batwa decision and national law 22/030 on Pygmy peoples' rights largely unapplied](<https://ishr.ch/latest-updates/achpr89-side-event-protecting-our-land-our-environment-and-digital-space-in-africa/>), and condemned the clearance operations in national parks.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut in the last twelve months is counted. The nearest held fact is that [Access Now lists an internet shutdown at Goma, in the M23 conflict area, among the shutdowns of 2025](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>), without saying who ordered it, when it began or whether it ended.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The Committee to Protect Journalists says journalist Tresor Muntu was [detained on 15 September 2026 and charged with defamation and publishing false news](<https://cpj.org/2026/09/drc-journalist-detained-prosecuted-for-sharing-x-post-on-whatsapp/>) for sharing a post criticising a provincial politician, and was jailed the next day.

***Also on file***. [a Senate bill to bar social media for children under 13](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/drc-senate-considers-social-media-ban-for-children-under-13>); [the 2023 digital code and the interministerial task force on the information space](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/30/rdc-le-gouvernement-renforce-son-dispositif-de-protection-de-lespace-informationnel-face>); [article 360 of the digital code, on false information](<https://kinshasamagazine.cd/rdc-le-parquet-mobilise-les-opj-contre-les-infractions-commises-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

***Not found***. A journalist was jailed for what he shared inside the last twelve months; no law repealing a speech offence, and no bar on cutting the internet, has been found.
