# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** In October 2026 Fidèle Mbilizi Mutimanwa of the indigenous peoples' league LINAPYCO spoke at a side event of the African Commission. He [criticised the state for leaving the Commission's 2022 Batwa decision and law 22/030 largely unapplied](<https://ishr.ch/latest-updates/achpr89-side-event-protecting-our-land-our-environment-and-digital-space-in-africa/>). He also condemned clearance operations in national parks.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** The press freedom group OLPA says [the main mobile networks in Fizi have been cut since July 2026](<https://www.opinion-info.cd/societe/2026/09/21/rdc-lolpa-alerte-sur-la-multiplication-des-atteintes-la-liberte-de-la-presse-et>). Access Now [lists an internet shutdown at Goma, in the M23 conflict area, among the shutdowns of 2025](<https://www.accessnow.org/internet-shutdowns-2025/>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** The Committee to Protect Journalists says journalist Tresor Muntu was [detained on 15 September 2026 and charged with defamation and publishing false news](<https://cpj.org/2026/09/drc-journalist-detained-prosecuted-for-sharing-x-post-on-whatsapp/>). He had shared a post criticising a provincial politician. OLPA says intelligence agents [held journalist Michael Tenende from 30 August to 8 September 2026](<https://www.opinion-info.cd/societe/2026/09/21/rdc-lolpa-alerte-sur-la-multiplication-des-atteintes-la-liberte-de-la-presse-et>) over a message calling for a boycott of a governor's rally. Two Radio Ndenga News technicians in Kalemie were held from 14 to 16 September 2026. They had refused to stop rebroadcasting a programme on protests against the constitutional change project.

***Also on file***. [A Senate bill to bar social media for children under 13](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/drc-senate-considers-social-media-ban-for-children-under-13>). [The 2023 digital code and the interministerial task force on the information space](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/30/rdc-le-gouvernement-renforce-son-dispositif-de-protection-de-lespace-informationnel-face>). [Article 360 of the digital code, on false information](<https://kinshasamagazine.cd/rdc-le-parquet-mobilise-les-opj-contre-les-infractions-commises-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

***Not found***. No source names who ordered the cuts in Fizi or at Goma. No law repealing a speech offence, and no bar on cutting the internet, has been found.
