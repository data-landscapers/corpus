# Zambia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 8 August 2026 the Net Rights Coalition said [arrests of journalists under the Cyber Crimes Act signal a chilling climate](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/fourteen-internet-rights-groups-urge-zambia-to-protect-digital-rights-ahead-of-electiona/>). It called for the cyber laws to be amended. In March 2025 eleven groups, MISA among them, said [the cyber bills still lacked safeguards against abuse](<https://tizambia.org.zm/2025/03/joint-civil-society-press-statement-on-the-cyber-bills-26th-march-2025/>). In July 2026 the Law Association of Zambia and Chapter One Foundation [asked the court to strike down parts of both laws](<https://cpj.org/2026/07/zambian-journalist-detained-for-audio-leak-under-cyber-laws-weeks-before-elections/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block is on record from the last twelve months. The nearest fact is older. Access Now says the authorities [reportedly blocked WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter and Instagram in the August 2021 elections](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>). The block was lifted after civil society went to court.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 15 September 2026 a Lusaka court [sentenced vlogger Joseph Chishimba to nine months](<https://diggers.news/courts/2026/09/15/vlogger-gets-9-months-for-sharing-screenshot-about-mwanawasas-false-pregnancy/>) over a screenshot about a presidential aide. Journalist MacPherson Mukuka [was detained from 17 July 2026 over an audio recording of a campaign committee](<https://cpj.org/2026/07/zambian-journalist-detained-for-audio-leak-under-cyber-laws-weeks-before-elections/>). Police in Mansa [arrested Kennedy Mwansa for insulting the president on TikTok](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/09/12/mansa-tailor-nabbed-for-insulting-hh-on-tiktok/>). The EU observers said the August 2026 elections [took place amid arrests for online speech](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026/documents/EU%20EOM%20Zambia%202026_Preliminary%20Statement%2015_8_FINAL_website2.pdf>).
