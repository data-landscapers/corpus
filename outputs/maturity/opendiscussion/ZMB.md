# Zambia

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** The Net Rights Coalition said on 8 August 2026 that [arrests, detention and summoning of journalists under the Cyber Crimes Act No. 4 of 2025 signal a chilling environment](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/fourteen-internet-rights-groups-urge-zambia-to-protect-digital-rights-ahead-of-electiona/>) and called for amendments to the cyber laws. Eleven civil society organisations, including Transparency International Zambia and MISA, said on 26 March 2025 that [the Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Bills still needed substantial safeguards against abuse and overreach](<https://tizambia.org.zm/2025/03/joint-civil-society-press-statement-on-the-cyber-bills-26th-march-2025/>). The Christian Churches Monitoring Group said the electoral system change and delimitation [were done with insufficient consultation](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/churches-group-rates-conduct-of-august-general-elections/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing is held on a cut or block in the last twelve months. The nearest held fact is that Access Now says authorities [reportedly blocked WhatsApp, Facebook, Twitter and Instagram during the 12 August 2021 elections](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>), lifted after civil society challenged the order in court.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 15 September 2026 the Lusaka Magistrates Court [sentenced vlogger Joseph Chishimba to nine months](<https://diggers.news/courts/2026/09/15/vlogger-gets-9-months-for-sharing-screenshot-about-mwanawasas-false-pregnancy/>) under section 54 of the Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act No. 2 of 2021 over a WhatsApp screenshot about a presidential aide, a conviction [also reported on 11 September 2026](<https://www.mwebantu.com/court-convicts-vlogger-chishimba-over-mwanawasa-allegations/>). Police in Mansa [arrested Kennedy Mwansa for allegedly insulting the president in a TikTok video](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/09/12/mansa-tailor-nabbed-for-insulting-hh-on-tiktok/>), charging him under the Cyber Crimes Act. The EU observation mission said the August 2026 elections [took place amid arrests for online speech offences](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026/documents/EU%20EOM%20Zambia%202026_Preliminary%20Statement%2015_8_FINAL_website2.pdf>), mostly over criticism of the president.

***Not found***. Nothing found shows a twelve-month stretch in which no one was arrested, charged or jailed for what was said or published.
