# Chad

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Internet Society Chad, a civil-society chapter, [called on the authorities in a communique of 11 February 2025 to restore access in Lac province](<https://lendjampost.com/coupure-dinternet-dans-le-lac-internet-society-tchad-tire-la-sonnette-dalarme/>) and said alternatives to a total cut exist. The Association des Médias en Ligne du Tchad (AMET) said on 5 August 2026 that the regulator HAMA's suspension of applications to create new media [must respect press freedom and citizens' right to pluralistic information](<https://lendjampost.com/medias-lamet-appelle-a-concilier-assainissement-du-secteur-et-liberte-de-la-presse/>), while backing action against fake pages.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** [Internet access in Lac province has been cut since 28 October 2024](<https://lendjampost.com/coupure-dinternet-dans-le-lac-internet-society-tchad-tire-la-sonnette-dalarme/>) and was still off on 11 February 2025, with security reasons mentioned as possible. A citizen's open letter to the president, [reported on 10 September 2026, says connectivity in Lac has been heavily limited for nearly two years and was still limited](<https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/lac-un-citoyen-appelle-a-un-reexamen-de-la-connectivite-internet/>). Neither source names who ordered it or gives an end date.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or sanction for speech has been found. The nearest held fact is that HAMA [suspended the examination of applications to create online newspapers](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/>), reported on 4 August 2026.

***Also on file***. [HAMA's suspension of applications to create new media](<https://lendjampost.com/medias-lamet-appelle-a-concilier-assainissement-du-secteur-et-liberte-de-la-presse/>), and [its suspension of the examination of online newspaper applications of 4 August 2026](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/>).

***Not found***. Who ordered the cut of internet access in Lac province, and when it ends, are not held.
