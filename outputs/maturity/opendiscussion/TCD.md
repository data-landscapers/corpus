# Chad

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Internet Society Chad is a civil-society chapter. In a communique of 11 February 2025 it [called on the authorities to restore access in Lac province](<https://lendjampost.com/coupure-dinternet-dans-le-lac-internet-society-tchad-tire-la-sonnette-dalarme/>). It said alternatives to a total cut exist. The Association des Médias en Ligne du Tchad (AMET) spoke on 5 August 2026 about the regulator HAMA's suspension of applications to create new media. AMET said the suspension [must respect press freedom and citizens' right to pluralistic information](<https://lendjampost.com/medias-lamet-appelle-a-concilier-assainissement-du-secteur-et-liberte-de-la-presse/>), while backing action against fake pages.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** [Internet access in Lac province has been cut since 28 October 2024](<https://lendjampost.com/coupure-dinternet-dans-le-lac-internet-society-tchad-tire-la-sonnette-dalarme/>). It was still off on 11 February 2025, with security reasons mentioned as possible. [A citizen's open letter to the president, reported on 10 September 2026, says connectivity in Lac has been heavily limited for nearly two years](<https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/lac-un-citoyen-appelle-a-un-reexamen-de-la-connectivite-internet/>). The letter says it was still limited. Neither source names who ordered it or gives an end date.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or sanction for speech has been found. The nearest held fact is that HAMA [suspended the examination of applications to create online newspapers](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/>), reported on 4 August 2026.

***Also on file***. [HAMA's suspension of applications to create new media](<https://lendjampost.com/medias-lamet-appelle-a-concilier-assainissement-du-secteur-et-liberte-de-la-presse/>). [Its suspension of the examination of online newspaper applications of 4 August 2026](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/tchad-la-hama-suspend-lexamen-des-demandes-de-creation-des-journaux-en-ligne/>).

***Not found***. Who ordered the cut of internet access in Lac province, and when it ends, are not held.
