# Angola

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** Leaders of the Sindicato dos Jornalistas Angolanos, the Comissao de Carteira e Etica and the Forum das Mulheres Jornalistas [called the penalties of the new Law Against False Information on the Internet excessive and inhibiting](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/jornalistas-advertem-que-lei-vai-inibir-jornalismo-de-cidadania-erro-jornalistico-dependera-da-interpretacao-dos-tribunais-e-do-lesado-74134.html>), reported on 22 August 2026. UNITA, the largest opposition party, [voted against the bill in the National Assembly on 21 May 2026](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/lei-contra-fake-news-aprovada-definitivamente-com-votos-do-mpla-prs-e-pha-unita-votou-contra-diploma-que-abre-espaco-para-criminalizar-jornalistas-e-utilizadores-71959.html>), and the bar association had told the Assembly president on 27 January 2026 that the bill [threatens free expression and needs reformulation](<http://oaa.ao/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OAA-PARECER-SOBRE-AS-PROPOSTAS-DE-LEI-R.pdf>). A jurist of the civic movement Mudei [said the law's definition is unclear](<https://www.rfi.fr/fr/afrique/20260810-angola-une-loi-sur-les-fausses-informations-suscite-des-craintes-pour-la-libert%C3%A9-d-expression>) and several NGOs plan a court challenge with the bar association.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut or block has been found for Angola. On 23 January 2026 UNITA abstained when the National Assembly approved the cybersecurity bill in general, [saying it feared internet cuts on election and protest days](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/proposta-de-lei-de-ciberseguranca-aprovada-mas-com-a-abstencao-da-unita-que-receia-que-governo-recorra-ao-corte-de-internet-nos-dias-das-eleicoes-e-das-manifestacoes-69381.html>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest or charge for speech has been found. The Law Against False Information on the Internet, Lei n.o 6/26, was [published in the Diario da Republica on 4 August 2026 with prison of up to 10 years](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/publicada-em-diario-da-republica-a-lei-que-pune-a-disseminacao-de-informacoes-falsas-na-internet-com-ate-10-anos-de-prisao-73765.html>), and the bar association cited [sanctions of 2 to 10 years for executives](<http://oaa.ao/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/OAA-PARECER-SOBRE-AS-PROPOSTAS-DE-LEI-R.pdf>).
