# Zimbabwe

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 23 June 2026 MISA Zimbabwe, a media-freedom body inside the country, [published an analysis criticising the Zimbabwe National AI Strategy 2026-2030](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2026/06/23/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>), including its proposed National Reality Check Platform for truth-verification, as lacking legal safeguards for freedom of expression.

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** CIPESA says the state security minister [ordered an internet shutdown in January 2019](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>) amid nationwide fuel-price protests, through an intercept directive to mobile network operators, and that the High Court [ruled that month that the minister had no authority under the Interception of Communications Act](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>) to order it.

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** No arrest, charge or sentence for speech is held in the evidence for this country. CIPESA's note on the fourth-cycle review [lists offences of false information, cyberbullying, incitement, insulting the President, sovereignty and national interest](<https://cipesa.org/2026/08/uganda-and-zimbabwes-fourth-cycle-uprs-must-turn-digital-progress-into-stronger-rights-protections/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [The January 2019 internet shutdown and the High Court ruling on it](<https://cipesa.org/2026/09/mozambiques-internet-shutdown-case-african-courts-draw-a-line-on-executive-power/>); [network degradation during the 2023 elections](<https://cipesa.org/2026/08/uganda-and-zimbabwes-fourth-cycle-uprs-must-turn-digital-progress-into-stronger-rights-protections/>); [section 164C of the Cyber and Data Protection Act, on false data messages](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf>); [the Freedom of Information Act, 2020](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2020/1/eng@2020-07-01>).

***Not held***. No source says the country went the last twelve months with no one punished for what they said or published.
