# Ghana

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 19 September 2026 Democracy Hub, a civil-society group, through its adviser Oliver Barker-Vormawor, [criticised on JoyNews the police detention, the denial of bail and the evidence-gathering in the Salomey Awiti Bafoh prosecution](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>). The same day the MP John Ntim Fordjour [said that Section 208 is applied selectively against government critics](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-jollof-case-does-section-208-of-criminal-code-only-apply-to-npp-ntim-fordjour-questions/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** Nothing is held on a cut to internet access or a block on a platform in Ghana, or on any bar against one. The nearest held fact is the Presidency's letter on a [national stakeholder engagement on hate speech and abusive online content](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/OPS308.3.26.1386-NATIONAL-STAKEHOLDER-ENGAGEMENT-ON-HATE-SPEECH-ABUSIVE-ONLINE-CONTENT-RESPONSIBLE-DIGITAL-COMMUNICATION.pdf>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** Senior nursing officer Salomey Awiti Bafoh was [arrested on 13 September 2026 and charged at the Adentan Circuit Court with abetment of publishing false news under sections 20(1) and 208 of the Criminal Offences Act](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>), for allegedly helping redistribute videos from a UK-based TikTok account. She [pleaded not guilty and was denied bail and remanded](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>). The National Commission for Civic Education chairperson said, as reported on 1 September 2026, that [the repeal of criminal libel and seditious laws in 2001 strengthened democratic development](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/misinformation-deepfakes-pose-serious-threat-to-ghanas-democratic-stability-ncce/>).

***Also on file***. [the National Stakeholder Engagement on Hate Speech, Abusive Online Content and Responsible Digital Communication, from the Office of the President](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/OPS308.3.26.1386-NATIONAL-STAKEHOLDER-ENGAGEMENT-ON-HATE-SPEECH-ABUSIVE-ONLINE-CONTENT-RESPONSIBLE-DIGITAL-COMMUNICATION.pdf>); [Section 208 of the Criminal and Other Offences Act on false publication, enforced by the Ghana Police Service CID](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-jollof-case-does-section-208-of-criminal-code-only-apply-to-npp-ntim-fordjour-questions/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says an outlet was suspended or closed for what it published in the last twelve months, or that any access to a platform was cut.
