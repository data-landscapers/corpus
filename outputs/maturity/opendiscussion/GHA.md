# Ghana

## Freedom to discuss policy online and in the press

**Public criticism of government policy.** On 7 November 2026 Minority Leader Alexander Afenyo-Markin [called for repeal of Section 208 of the Criminal Offences Act](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/11/afenyo-markin-calls-for-repeal-of-section-208-of-criminal-offences-act/>). That is the false-news offence. On 21 May 2026 the Media Foundation for West Africa [criticised the use of the false-news laws and called for their repeal](<https://demo.mfwa.org/issues-in-focus/mfwa-decries-wanton-and-discriminatory-use-of-false-news-laws/>). On 19 September 2026 Democracy Hub's adviser [criticised the police handling of a false-news prosecution](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>) on JoyNews. The same day the MP John Ntim Fordjour [said Section 208 is applied selectively against government critics](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-jollof-case-does-section-208-of-criminal-code-only-apply-to-npp-ntim-fordjour-questions/>).

**Cuts and blocks, and a bar on them.** No cut to internet access or block on a platform in Ghana has been found. No bar against one has been found either. The nearest fact found is the Presidency's letter on [hate speech and abusive online content](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/OPS308.3.26.1386-NATIONAL-STAKEHOLDER-ENGAGEMENT-ON-HATE-SPEECH-ABUSIVE-ONLINE-CONTENT-RESPONSIBLE-DIGITAL-COMMUNICATION.pdf>).

**Punishment for speech, and its removal.** On 13 September 2026 senior nursing officer Salomey Awiti Bafoh was [arrested and charged with abetment of publishing false news](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>). She was said to have helped redistribute videos from a UK-based TikTok account. She [pleaded not guilty and was remanded without bail](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/barker-vormawor-urges-courts-to-scrutinise-rights-violations-in-ghana-jollof-case/>). On 13 April 2026 police [arrested opposition figures Kwame Baffoe and Abubakar Yakubu over online publications](<https://demo.mfwa.org/issues-in-focus/mfwa-decries-wanton-and-discriminatory-use-of-false-news-laws/>). MFWA [counted 14 arrests under the false-news laws in the 16 months from 7 January 2025](<https://demo.mfwa.org/issues-in-focus/mfwa-decries-wanton-and-discriminatory-use-of-false-news-laws/>). In September 2026 the civic education commission's chair said [criminal libel and sedition laws were repealed in 2001](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/misinformation-deepfakes-pose-serious-threat-to-ghanas-democratic-stability-ncce/>).
