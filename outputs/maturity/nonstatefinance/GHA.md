# Ghana

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Africa50, Oikocredit and three other funds put USD 14 million of equity into Zeepay Ghana, [closing in April 2024](<https://www.africa50.com/media/news/article/zeepay-raises-equity-from-africa50-and-other-leading-institutions-to-support-ongoing-expansion/>). Google committed [USD 37 million to an AI research centre in Accra](<https://managingghana.com/2025/07/27/google-unveils-37m-investment-and-ai-hub-in-accra-to-boost-africas-ai-growth/>) from 2025. In 2026, IFC put a [US$2.4 million convertible loan into Complete Farmer](<https://www.ifc.org/en/pressroom/2026/ifc-partners-with-absa-bank-ghana-and-complete-farmer-to-expand-agricultural-finan>), and Village Capital's facility put [USD 500,000 into three startups](<https://newsandviews.vilcap.com/press-releases/africa-ecosystem-catalysts-facility-announces-usd-500k-in-new-investments-in-ghana>). MTN committed [US$1.1 billion over three years](<https://mtn.com.gh/newsabout/advancing-leading-digital-solutions-for-africas-progress-mtn-group-ceo-visits-ghana/>) and Telecel [US$200 million](<https://techfocus24.com/telecel-group-announces-200-million-network-modernization-for-telecel-ghana/>) to their networks.

**Money raised inside the country.** Nothing found names it. [Scancom PLC (MTN Ghana) invested GHS 1.9 billion in its network and digital platforms in the first half of 2026](<https://mtn.com.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Scancom-PLC-MTN-Ghana-H1-2026-Results.pdf>).
