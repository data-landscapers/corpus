# Malawi

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2025 TNM plc, a Malawian mobile operator, [invested MK30.87 billion of its own money in network infrastructure](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/24/can-tnms-mk30-87bn-investment-meet-demand/>), mainly modernisation, 4G site expansion and 5G rollout. In August 2026 TNM [said it had invested US$31 million of its own money](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/tnm-invests-us31-million-to-expand-network-coverage-across-malawi/>) in network capacity and coverage across Malawi, with no date given for the spending. In both cases the company is its own financier, and neither report says where its money comes from.

**Money raised inside the country.** TNM, a Malawian operator, [paid for its 2025 network spending itself](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/24/can-tnms-mk30-87bn-investment-meet-demand/>), covering modernisation, 4G site expansion and the 5G rollout, with no bank, fund or share issue named behind it.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** Both network investments were [TNM's own money](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/tnm-invests-us31-million-to-expand-network-coverage-across-malawi/>), with no state partner named and no public digital project attached to either amount.

[Digital Malawi Acceleration Project (DMAP)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap>), run by the Ministry of Information and Communications Technology with World Bank IDA financing; [Mastercard equity in Airtel Mobile Commerce BV](<https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/mastercard-invest-100-mln-airtel-africas-mobile-money-unit-2021-04-01/>), held through Airtel Africa; [PPP Connectivity initiative (GWAN)](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/pppc-e-govt-sensitize-mwanza-mdas-on-ppp-connectivity-initiative/>), the Government Wide Area Network.
