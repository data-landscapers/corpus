# Malawi

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2025 TNM plc, a Malawian mobile operator, [invested MK30.87 billion of its own money in network infrastructure](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/24/can-tnms-mk30-87bn-investment-meet-demand/>). This was mainly modernisation, 4G site expansion and 5G rollout. In August 2026 TNM [said it had invested US$31 million of its own money](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/tnm-invests-us31-million-to-expand-network-coverage-across-malawi/>) in network capacity and coverage across Malawi. It gave no date for the spending. In both cases the company is its own financier, and neither report says where its money comes from.

**Money raised inside the country.** TNM, a Malawian operator, [paid for its 2025 network spending itself](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/24/can-tnms-mk30-87bn-investment-meet-demand/>). The spending covered modernisation, 4G site expansion and the 5G rollout. No bank, fund or share issue is named behind it.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** Both network investments were [TNM's own money](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/17/tnm-invests-us31-million-to-expand-network-coverage-across-malawi/>). No state partner is named, and no public digital project is attached to either amount.

***Also on file***. The [Digital Malawi Acceleration Project (DMAP)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap>) is on file, run by the Ministry of Information and Communications Technology with World Bank IDA financing. So is [Mastercard equity in Airtel Mobile Commerce BV](<https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/mastercard-invest-100-mln-airtel-africas-mobile-money-unit-2021-04-01/>), held through Airtel Africa. The [PPP Connectivity initiative (GWAN)](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/pppc-e-govt-sensitize-mwanza-mdas-on-ppp-connectivity-initiative/>), the Government Wide Area Network, is also on file.

***Not found***. No amount from 2024 on has been found from a Malawian bank, pension fund, insurer or investor, or raised on Malawi's own market. No private partner has been found putting a named amount into a public digital project.
