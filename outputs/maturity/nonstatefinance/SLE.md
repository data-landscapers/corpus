# Sierra Leone

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Orange Sierra Leone committed [USD 50 million in 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/orange-sierra-leone-expands-coverage-with-launch-of-new-network-site-in-mathonkra-tonkolili-district/>), self-funded and put into its own business, for a network modernisation toward 4G and 5G readiness. The commitment is described as active. It is the only private amount held dated 2024 or later, so the page names one deal and one year.

**Money raised inside the country.** Nothing found shows money raised inside Sierra Leone. There is the same [Orange Sierra Leone commitment of USD 50 million in 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/orange-sierra-leone-expands-coverage-with-launch-of-new-network-site-in-mathonkra-tonkolili-district/>), which the company funded itself and which is stated as a commitment, not as a raise.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** Nothing found shows an amount the government brought in from private sources. There is the [November 2025 opening of the Orange Sierra Leone data centre in Bo](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/2025/11/29/sierra-leones-president-julius-maada-bio-opens-orange-sierra-leone-data-centre-reaffirms-governments-commitment-to-enabling-private-investment/>), where the President reaffirmed the government's commitment to enabling private investment.

***Also on file***. The [Sierratel-Africell MVNO partnership](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/2026/05/06/sierra-leones-president-julius-maada-bio-relaunches-sierratel-commits-to-stronger-public-private-partnership-in-telecoms-sector/>), a public-private arrangement between state-owned Sierratel and Africell with no private amount stated; the [Orange Sierra Leone data centre in Bo](<https://statehouse.gov.sl/2025/11/29/sierra-leones-president-julius-maada-bio-opens-orange-sierra-leone-data-centre-reaffirms-governments-commitment-to-enabling-private-investment/>); and the [OPIC USD 100 million Africell Holding telecom expansion guarantee or loan](<https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/2019-08/9000042686.pdf>) from 2018.

***Not found***. No source found says whether private money came in more than one year, because only one amount dated 2024 or later is held. No money raised inside the country and no private money brought in by the government has been found.
