# Namibia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In the year to September 2025 Paratus Group [invested a further N$600 million (US$33.7 million)](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/paratus-launches-namibia-s-first-private-mobile-network>) in its new private mobile network in Namibia, one-third of it for a digital technology stack. That follows N$1.42 billion (US$79.8 million) put into network infrastructure since 2018.

**Money raised inside the country.** On 25 March 2026 RMB Namibia, a Namibian bank, [announced a seven-year N$405 million social loan](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22896359>) to Telecom Namibia, the state-owned operator, for network modernisation and broadband expansion.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The nearest case is the 2026 [loan to the state-owned Telecom Namibia](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22896359>), which is a bank loan to an operator and not a partnership with a stated private contribution.

***Also on file***. [Central Securities Depository (CSD)](<https://www.namibiansun.com/mw-main/n250m-bond-moves-online-NMH017110-3015-21006>); [Telecom Namibia social loan, 2026](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22896359>).

***Not found***. No public-private partnership, concession or joint venture since 2024 is known in which a private partner puts a named amount into a public digital project, and no state fund that private investors paid into.
