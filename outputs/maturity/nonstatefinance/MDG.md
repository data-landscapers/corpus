# Madagascar

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** The African Development Bank [approved a $160 million senior loan to AXIAN Telecom in January 2025](<https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/african-development-bank-partners-with-axian-telecom-to-accelerate-africas-digital-transformation?lang=en>), with over $10 million for 22,000 women entrepreneurs in Madagascar through the MVola platform. The European Investment Bank [announced $100 million to AXIAN Telecom on 10 April 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/11/eib-grants-100m-to-axian-telecom-for-4g-5g-rollout-in-tanzania-and-madagascar/>) for 4G and 5G, $40 million of it for Madagascar and $60 million for Tanzania. Both lenders are development finance institutions, and both loans went to the same private telecoms group.

**Money raised inside the country.** Nothing found shows money raised inside Madagascar. The African Development Bank's January 2025 loan went [to a Mauritius-headquartered company working across nine countries](<https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/african-development-bank-partners-with-axian-telecom-to-accelerate-africas-digital-transformation?lang=en>), so the money came from outside the country.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** Nothing found shows the government bringing private money into a public digital project. The Economic Development Board of Madagascar, the state investment agency, [has had a regional antenna at Manakara since July 2025](<https://newsmada.com/2026/05/26/investissement-prive-ledbm-sinstalle-a-manakara/>), and no amount is stated.
