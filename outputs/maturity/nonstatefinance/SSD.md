# South Sudan

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Finnfund, Finland's development finance institution, put [USD 5 million in 2024](<https://www.finnfund.fi/en/news/finnfund-invests-in-crei-to-improve-mobile-connections-in-south-sudan/>) into Communication and Renewable Energy Infrastructure (CREI), which supplies solar-hybrid power to MTN South Sudan's telecom sites. A [follow-on of USD 5 million](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-green-network-gets-5m-boost/>) started in 2025 and was reported in February 2026, bringing Finnfund's [total in the company to USD 10 million](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/eu-backed-investment-boosts-green-telecom-expansion-in-south-sudan>), 40% of the USD 25 million project financing.

**Money raised inside the country.** CREI, the company Finnfund financed, is [registered in Mauritius](<https://www.finnfund.fi/en/hankkeet/communication-and-renewable-energy-infrastructure-crei-2/>), and its investor is Finnish.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** NCT South Sudan and Bayobab are rolling out a [national backbone fibre network](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4204/nct-south-sudan-and-bayobab-collaborate-to-roll-out-national-backbone-fiber-network-project>), announced in March 2026, with no private amount given.

***Also on file***. [Bayobab Infra Solutions Ltd's Project East 2 West fibre network](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/25/mtn-digital-infrastructure-secures-15-year-license-to-expand-fibre-network-in-south-sudan/>), held by MTN Digital Infrastructure.

***Not found***. No amount from a bank, pension fund, insurer, investor or company of South Sudan itself since 2024 is known, nor any raised on its own market. No private amount in the government's fibre project is known.
