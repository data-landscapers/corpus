# Tunisia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In December 2024 [a bank consortium led by Attijari lent Ooredoo TND 310 million](<https://irbe7.com/actualites/articles/attijari-bank-en-chef-de-file-dun-financement-de-310-millions-de-dinars-pour-la-5g-dooredoo/6806623203cffa19f5fb1c7e>) for its 5G licence and national 5G network. On 18 February 2025 [the startup Arcube announced a USD 1.5 million round](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>). [The fintech Konnect Networks raised USD 1.5 million](<https://afriqueitnews.com/finance/konnect-fintech-tunisienne-leve-15-m-soutien-dattijariwafa-ventures/>) in May 2025, from investors including Attijariwafa Ventures and Visa. [The Franco-Tunisian startup Thunder Code closed a USD 9 million seed round](<https://managers.tn/2025/06/04/la-startup-franco-tunisienne-thunder-code-leve-9-millions-de-dollars/>) in June 2025, led by Silicon Badia.

**Money raised inside the country.** [Attijari Bank Tunisie led the bank consortium](<https://irbe7.com/actualites/articles/attijari-bank-en-chef-de-file-dun-financement-de-310-millions-de-dinars-pour-la-5g-dooredoo/6806623203cffa19f5fb1c7e>) that made the Ooredoo loan. [The Arcube round was co-led by 216 CAPITAL](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>), a fund founded in Tunis in 2021. Its foreign co-investors were Fuel Ventures and Oxford Capital Partners.
