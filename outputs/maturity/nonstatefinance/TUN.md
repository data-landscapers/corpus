# Tunisia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** On 18 February 2025 [the startup Arcube announced a funding round of USD 1.5 million (TND 4.7 million)](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>). The round was [co-led by the Tunis-based fund 216 CAPITAL, with Fuel Ventures and Oxford Capital Partners](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>). It is the one named amount held for a Tunisian digital business from 2024 on, and it was announced in a single month of 2025.

**Money raised inside the country.** [216 CAPITAL, an investment fund based in Tunis and founded there in 2021, co-led the USD 1.5 million Arcube round announced on 18 February 2025](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>). A Tunisian financier is therefore named in the round, alongside the two foreign investors, [Fuel Ventures and Oxford Capital Partners](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2025/02/18/541011/la-start-up-arcube-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-15-million-de-dollars-co-dirigee-par-216-capital/>), that took part in it.
