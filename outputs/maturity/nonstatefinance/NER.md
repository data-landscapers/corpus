# Niger

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Telecom operators awarded rural connectivity contracts under the World Bank-backed Smart Villages project had put in [US$7,522,666 of private sector investment by 30 June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>), against a closing target of US$25 million for December 2025. It is the only private amount for Niger from 2024 on.

**Money raised inside the country.** The Smart Villages money came from [telecom operators awarded rural connectivity contracts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>) under the project, and no other private digital investment in Niger since 2024 is recorded.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The government's public project, [Smart Villages for rural growth and digital inclusion (P167543)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>), counts the operators' investment as private money raised through the project, measured at mid-2024 against its US$25 million target.

[ETC Services Centres](<https://itweb.africa/article/ericsson-luxembourg-sweden-support-boosts-sahel-digital-connectivity/Pero3MZ3ozJqQb6m>) and [iPayMoney](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9055/how-uncdfs-catalytic-finance-is-driving-digital-finance-solutions-for-smarter-university-enrollment-in-niger>).
