# Comoros

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** The International Finance Corporation, the World Bank Group's private-sector lender, agreed in June 2025 to lend EUR 25 million to Yas Comoros, an AXIAN Telecom subsidiary, to pay for [5G, fibre-to-the-home, fibre-to-the-office and inter-island connectivity](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/20/axian-telecoms-yas-comoros-secures-e25m-loan-to-boost-digital-transformation/>). There is an [IFC commitment of USD 27 million](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/20/axian-telecoms-yas-comoros-secures-e25m-loan-to-boost-digital-transformation/>) to Yas Comoros, a commercial loan approved and starting in 2025, the same year as the June agreement.

**Money raised inside the country.** The 2025 lender, the IFC, is [not a Comorian body](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/20/axian-telecoms-yas-comoros-secures-e25m-loan-to-boost-digital-transformation/>), so the money in this loan came from outside the country.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The 2025 loan went to [Yas Comoros, an AXIAN Telecom subsidiary](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/20/axian-telecoms-yas-comoros-secures-e25m-loan-to-boost-digital-transformation/>), for network connectivity, and it is a private firm's loan rather than a government project.
