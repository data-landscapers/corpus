# Guinea

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2024 [MIGA committed USD 373 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) in a guarantee to Orange Guinee SA, a telecoms operator. In the same year [MIGA committed USD 27 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) in a second guarantee, to Orange Finances Mobiles Guinee SA for mobile money. MIGA lists both under Sonatel equity. In January 2025 [TechCrunch reported that the Guinea-based fintech Cauridor had raised USD 3.5 million](<https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/cauridor-has-a-fix-for-cross-border-payments-issues-in-francophone-africa/>) in seed funding, led by Oui Capital.

**Money raised inside the country.** [MIGA's two 2024 guarantees](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>), of USD 373 million and USD 27 million, went to Orange Guinee SA and Orange Finances Mobiles Guinee SA. Neither names a Guinean financier.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** [MIGA's 2024 guarantee of USD 373 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) went to Orange Guinee SA, a telecoms operator. The record names no public project.
