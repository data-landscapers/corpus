# Guinea

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2024 [MIGA committed USD 373 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) in an active guarantee to Orange Guinee SA, a telecoms operator, under a project listed as Sonatel equity. In the same year [MIGA committed USD 27 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) in a second active guarantee, to Orange Finances Mobiles Guinee SA for mobile money, also listed as Sonatel equity.

**Money raised inside the country.** [MIGA's two 2024 guarantees](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>), of USD 373 million and USD 27 million, name Orange Guinee SA and Orange Finances Mobiles Guinee SA as the companies they were committed to.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** [MIGA's 2024 guarantee of USD 373 million](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>) was committed to Orange Guinee SA, a telecoms operator, and the record names no public project.
