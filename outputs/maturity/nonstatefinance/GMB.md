# The Gambia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In December 2025 the state telecoms operator GAMTEL [signed a US$50 million public-private partnership with SYSROAD SA HOLDING](<https://gamtel.gm/gamtel-and-syroad-project/>) to raise its national backbone from 40Gbps to 800Gbps. In July 2026 the financier YCELL Co. Ltd. [committed GMD 6.1 billion to modernise the network of GAMCEL](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gamtel-ycell-sign-landmark-share-investment-and-network-modernization-agreement>), the state mobile operator, under a Share Investment and Network Modernization Agreement that also gives it an 80% equity stake, about US$83.6 million in all.

**Money raised inside the country.** The GAMTEL backbone partner, [SYSROAD SA HOLDING, is a Senegal-based systems integrator](<https://gamtel.gm/gamtel-and-syroad-project/>).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** [GAMTEL is the public partner in the national broadband partnership](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/05/gamtel-and-sysroad-sign-50-million-ppp-to-modernize-the-gambias-broadband-network/>), and the private partner finances the upgrade of the national broadband network. The US$50 million agreement was signed in December 2025.
