# Lesotho

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Convalt Energy, a US company, signed a binding agreement with Lesotho's Ministry of Energy on 4 June 2026 for Project Kobong, a hydropower and AI data centre valued at about US$6.2 billion ([Lesotho Tribune](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/>)). The Tribune calls it the largest foreign investment commitment in Lesotho's history, with construction targeted for 2029 subject to feasibility. Its chief executive says the project was launched on 31 July 2026 ([TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/03/lesotho-approves-us6-2-billion-kobong-hydropower-and-ai-data-centre-project/>)).

**Money raised inside the country.** None found; Project Kobong's investor is Convalt Energy, a US company ([TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/09/lesotho-unveils-mega-hydropower-and-ai-data-center-project-under-6-2-billion-agreement/>)).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** None found; The Kobong agreement was signed with the Ministry of Energy ([Lesotho Tribune](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/>)).

***Also on file***. The [WIOCC Group](<https://www.africafc.org/news-and-insights/news/wiocc-group-secures-strategic-investment-from-afc-and-vision-invest-to-accelerate-its-digital-infrastructure-expansion-across-africa>) equity investment, and the [Kobong memorandum of agreement](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/lesotho-signs-m98-billion-deal-for-1200mw-hydropower-plant-and-ai-data-centre-in-mokhotlong/>) between the Ministry of Energy and Convalt Energy.
