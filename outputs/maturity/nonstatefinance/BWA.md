# Botswana

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In April 2026 Pula Investments, the family office of UK financier Stephen Lansdown, anchored Botswana Tech Fund, which [TechCabal reports](<https://techcabal.com/2026/04/27/botswana-tech-fund/>) has GBP 10 million (USD 13.5 million) in committed capital, with a first close of GBP 5 million (USD 6.7 million). The fund is run from Botswana with Launch Africa.

**Money raised inside the country.** Nothing from 2024 on is held; In May 2021 members of Angel Network Botswana, a Botswana investor network, put P250,000 (USD 22,000) into the online marketplace Ponatshego, [per the World Bank](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060525055531820/pdf/P172044-49042033-5f47-4802-91a1-c2307e59bb84.pdf>).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** There is a government-backed startup programme launched in March 2026 with [Scalar International](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/17/botswana-to-support-clean-energy-startups/>), which states no private amount.

***Also on file***. [Scalar Botswana Innovation Program (SBIP)](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/botswana-launches-scalar-programme-to-bridge-innovation-funding-gap/news>).

***Not found***. Only one amount is held from 2024 on, so no source found says whether private money came in more than one year.
