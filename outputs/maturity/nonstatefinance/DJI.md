# Djibouti

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [Wingu Africa is investing USD 20 million in its new Djibouti City 2 data centre](<https://magazinedelafrique.com/african-business/vers-un-hub-numerique-mondial/>), African Business reported in May 2024. The centre was then about to open. The investment is the company's own and goes into a single site in the capital.

**Money raised inside the country.** No source found says where any of this money was raised. [The report on the USD 20 million data centre](<https://magazinedelafrique.com/african-business/vers-un-hub-numerique-mondial/>) names Wingu Africa as the investor and no Djiboutian backer.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money in a government digital project is reported. [Wingu Africa's 2024 data centre investment](<https://magazinedelafrique.com/african-business/vers-un-hub-numerique-mondial/>) is the company's own, with no state partner named.

***Also on file***. The [Fonds de Fonds](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n179-an-25-9eme-l-portant-startup-act-djibouti/>), funded by the state with partners and donors under the Startup Act. The [Fonds de Garantie de Djibouti](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n179-an-25-9eme-l-portant-startup-act-djibouti/>), a state guarantee fund.

***Not found***. Only one amount is reported from 2024 on. No source found says whether private money came in other years.
