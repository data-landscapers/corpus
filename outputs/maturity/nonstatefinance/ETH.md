# Ethiopia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Safaricom PLC of Kenya [injected KES 21.3 billion into Safaricom Ethiopia in the year to March 2026](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/14/safaricom-ethiopia-passes-15-million-subscribers-five-years-after-getting-its-licence/>). That lifted its stake from 51.3% to 54.1%. [It added Sh1.4 billion in the quarter to June 2026](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/safaricom-invests-extra-sh1-4bn-in-ethiopia-unit-5541682>). The International Finance Corporation [committed a USD 200 million loan to Safaricom Ethiopia in 2024](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/49097/safaricom-ethiopia-debt-ii>). Dodai, an Addis Ababa e-mobility company, [closed a USD 13 million Series A in April 2026](<https://dodai.co/news/ibewjbxwi18ja2uwk542o8peqyqppj>). British International Investment was among its investors.

**Money raised inside the country.** [About 45,000 shareholders paid some ETB 3 billion for Ethio Telecom shares](<https://africancapitalmarketsnews.com/ethio-telecom-lists-on-ethiopian-securities-exchange/>) in its public offering of October 2024 to February 2025. The offering sought ETB 30 billion for 10% of the company. The shares were listed on the Ethiopian Securities Exchange on 26 May 2026.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No such project has been found. [The state-owned Ethio Telecom extended its share offering in January 2025](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/43299/>); the offering sold part of the company.
