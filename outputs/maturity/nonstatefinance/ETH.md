# Ethiopia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Safaricom PLC of Kenya [injected KES 21.3 billion into Safaricom Ethiopia in the year to March 2026](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/14/safaricom-ethiopia-passes-15-million-subscribers-five-years-after-getting-its-licence/>), lifting its stake from 51.3% to 54.1%, and [added Sh1.4 billion in the quarter to June 2026](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/companies/safaricom-invests-extra-sh1-4bn-in-ethiopia-unit-5541682>). The International Finance Corporation [committed a USD 200 million commercial loan to Safaricom Ethiopia in 2024](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/49097/safaricom-ethiopia-debt-ii>). Dodai, an Addis Ababa e-mobility company, [closed a USD 13 million Series A in April 2026](<https://dodai.co/news/ibewjbxwi18ja2uwk542o8peqyqppj>), USD 8 million equity and USD 5 million debt, with British International Investment among the investors. FMO [committed USD 15.7 million in a commercial loan to Cooperative Bank of Oromia in 2024](<https://www.iatistandard.org/en/>).

**Money raised inside the country.** [Safaricom PLC of Kenya holds 54.1% of Safaricom Ethiopia](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/14/safaricom-ethiopia-passes-15-million-subscribers-five-years-after-getting-its-licence/>).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** [Ethio telecom, which is state-owned, extended the deadline of its public share offering in January 2025](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/43299/>).
