# Benin

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [On 29 July 2024 BOAD, MTN Bénin and Banque Atlantique Bénin signed a FCFA 20 billion financing agreement](<https://journaldubenin.com/mtn-benin-un-reseau-encore-plus-performant-a-lhorizon/>). The money is for MTN Bénin's 5G, fibre and platform upgrade. In 2025 [MTN Group committed over USD 215 million of its own money in Benin through 2026](<https://www.mtn.com/mtn-group-backs-benin-investment-forum-2025-driving-digital-and-economic-progress-in-west-africa/>), for 5G, fibre and a new headquarters. [Partech counts one USD 1 million equity deal in Benin in 2025](<https://lamarinabj.com/index.php/2026/01/26/classement-partech-2025-une-transaction-historique-place-le-benin-sur-la-carte-du-capital-risque/>), which places it 23rd of 28 African venture markets.

**Money raised inside the country.** [Banque Atlantique Bénin was MTN Bénin's mandated lead arranger for the 2024 loan](<https://journaldubenin.com/mtn-benin-un-reseau-encore-plus-performant-a-lhorizon/>), and signed it with BOAD. The report does not give each lender's share of the FCFA 20 billion.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No such project has been found. [The MTN commitment of 2025 was self-funded](<https://www.mtn.com/mtn-group-backs-benin-investment-forum-2025-driving-digital-and-economic-progress-in-west-africa/>).
