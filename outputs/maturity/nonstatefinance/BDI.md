# Burundi

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026 Lumitel Burundi, a subsidiary of Vietnam's Viettel group, signed a [USD 10 million contract with the PAFEN project to extend rural 4G](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>), and [financed 56 per cent of it itself, about USD 5.6 million](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>). That is the only amount held from 2024 onward, and it is dated to the publication on 3 March 2026, as the page itself carries no date.

**Money raised inside the country.** The money came from [a foreign group's subsidiary, Lumitel, which pays 56 per cent of the rural 4G contract from its own funds](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>), and no local raise is stated in that source.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The government of Burundi, through the PAFEN project, [co-finances the remaining 44 per cent of the USD 10 million rural 4G contract](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>), and so brought Lumitel's private 56 per cent, about USD 5.6 million, into a public connectivity project in 2026, with the state and the company sharing the cost of the same contract.

***Also on file***. [ONATEL rural coverage contract (PAFEN)](<https://setic.gov.bi/pafen-onatel-couverture-reseau-et-connectivite-en-milieu-rural/>), [Lumitel investment increase](<https://lerenouveau.bi/le-premier-ministre-burundais-recoit-en-audience-la-delegation-du-groupe-viettel/>).

***Not found***. Only one amount is held from 2024 on, so no source found says whether private money came in more than one year.
