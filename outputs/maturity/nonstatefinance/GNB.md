# Guinea-Bissau

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2025 MIGA committed [USD 25 million as a guarantee to Orange Bissau S.A.](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>), a telecoms operator. The guarantee is listed as active in the MIGA project listing, and it is tied to Sonatel equity in the company, so the cover rests on a shareholder's stake in the operator.

**Money raised inside the country.** The 2025 guarantee is tied to [Sonatel equity in Orange Bissau S.A.](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>), a stake held by the operator's shareholder, and that stake is what the guarantee covers.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The 2025 guarantee went to [Orange Bissau S.A.](<https://www.miga.org/project/orange-guinea-and-guinea-bissau>), a telecoms operator, which is a company and not a state project; MIGA's cover for it is active and started in 2025.
