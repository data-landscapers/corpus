# DR Congo

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026 [EAAIF, a PIDG fund managed by Ninety One in London, signed a USD 32.8 million loan](<https://www.eaif.com/eaaif-accelerates-africas-digital-transformation-with-a-combined-usd-82-8-million-commitment-to-eastcastle-drc-and-liquid-intelligent-technologies/>) to Eastcastle Infrastructure DRC. It sits within a USD 179 million facility to build 728 towers. On 31 March 2026 [Helios Towers DRC signed an investment agreement for more than USD 100 million](<https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios>) in shared towers. The company says it is investing the money itself. In September 2025 [Trident Digital Tech Holdings said it had raised USD 2.6 million](<https://bankable.africa/fr/numerique/2601-2297-identification-numerique-97-1-millions-d-investissements-pour-deployer-le-drcpass>), mainly to expand its DRCPass service in the DRC.

**Money raised inside the country.** No source found says where any of this money was raised. [Vodacom Congo says it spends about USD 140 million a year on its DRC network](<https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-vodacom-veut-porter-a-670-millions-usd-ses-investissements-reseau-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/>). It plans a USD 670 million long-term programme, it said on 8 October 2026.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No amount has been found. [The national investment promotion agency ANAPI was the counterparty to the Helios tower agreement](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios) in March 2026.
