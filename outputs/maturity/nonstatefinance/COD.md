# DR Congo

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026, [EAAIF, a PIDG fund managed by Ninety One in London, signed a USD 32.8 million senior secured loan](https://www.eaif.com/eaaif-accelerates-africas-digital-transformation-with-a-combined-usd-82-8-million-commitment-to-eastcastle-drc-and-liquid-intelligent-technologies/) to Eastcastle Infrastructure DRC, within an upsized USD 179 million facility, to build 728 towers. On 31 March 2026, [Helios Towers DRC signed an investment agreement for more than USD 100 million](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios) in shared telecom towers, which the company says it is investing itself.

**Money raised inside the country.** No fact held says where any of this money was raised. [Vodacom Congo says it spends on average USD 140 million a year on its network in the DRC and plans a USD 670 million long-term programme](https://zoom-eco.net/autres-actualites/rdc-vodacom-veut-porter-a-670-millions-usd-ses-investissements-reseau-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/), stated on 8 October 2026.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No amount has been found. [The national investment promotion agency ANAPI was the counterparty to the Helios tower agreement](https://actualite.cd/2026/03/31/rdc-signature-dun-investissement-de-plus-de-100-millions-usd-entre-lanapi-et-helios) in March 2026.

[The national identification programme run by ONIP](https://actualite.cd/2026/05/29/identification-nationale-de-la-population-en-rdc-lonip-annonce-le-demarrage-des), [RDC-PASS, a 20-year public-private partnership with Trident Digital Tech](https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/19/trident-launches-rdc-pass-dr-congos-sovereign-digital-identity-ecosystem/), [WIOCC's data centres](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-infrastructures/1905-55714-wiocc-quietly-becomes-european-dfis-chosen-vehicle-in-africas-data-centre-race) and [the 2018 OPIC loan to Africell Holding](https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/2019-08/9000042686.pdf).
