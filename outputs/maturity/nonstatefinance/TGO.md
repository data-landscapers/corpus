# Togo

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2024 the International Finance Corporation committed [USD 54 million to Togocom, an Axian Group subsidiary](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44666/axian-togo-project>) for a telecom expansion loan, the earliest of the four deals. In February 2025 Gozem, a Lomé-based super app, [closed a USD 30 million Series B round](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>) with MSC Group and Al Mada Ventures. In 2026 the IFC [committed a further USD 10 million to Gozem](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>), and BGK [committed USD 28 million to Cyber Defense Africa](<https://www.gov.pl/web/cyfryzacja/wspolpraca-rozwojowa-bgk-wspiera-polska-inwestycje-technologiczna-w-afryce>).

**Money raised inside the country.** The Togo-based companies that took this money include [Gozem, based in Lomé](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>), and [Togocom](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44666/axian-togo-project>), a subsidiary of Axian Group.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The lenders and investors above dealt directly with companies, not with a government body: [Togocom](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44666/axian-togo-project>), [Gozem](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>) and [Cyber Defense Africa](<https://www.gov.pl/web/cyfryzacja/wspolpraca-rozwojowa-bgk-wspiera-polska-inwestycje-technologiczna-w-afryce>).

***Also on file***. [World Bank IDA $150 million development policy operation](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/12/15/togo-world-bank-provides-150-million-to-boost-private-investment-and-promote-job-creation>), owned by the World Bank and the Government of Togo.

***Not found***. No amount from 2024 on is named from a bank, pension fund, insurer, investor or company of Togo itself, or raised on Togo's own market. No private money the government brought into a public digital project is shown.
