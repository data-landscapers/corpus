# Senegal

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** US company Cybastion announced a [$300 million investment](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/transformation-numerique-cybastion-annonce-un-investissement-de-300-millions-de-dollars-au-senegal/>) in September 2026, and Helios Towers a [$150 million plan](<https://itweb.africa/article/helios-towers-commits-150m-to-senegal-expansion/rW1xLv5n1aO7Rk6m>) of its own money in July 2026. British International Investment committed [$32 million to Wave](<https://www.bii.co.uk/en/our-impact/investment/wave-mobile-money-sa-investment-01/>) in 2025, and in 2024 [$27 million](<https://www.proparco.fr/en/actualites/ifc-british-international-investment-and-proparco-partner-support-sonatels-telecoms>) to Sonatel, where the IFC committed $40 million. The US DFC committed [$5 million to QuantumID](<https://www.dfc.gov/investment-story/helping-american-it-business-expand-sub-saharan-africa>) in 2024. Tanel raised a [$2.1 million seed round](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/02/alan-tanel/>) in 2024 from Alan and Ventures Platform.
