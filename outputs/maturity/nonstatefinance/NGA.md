# Nigeria

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Terra Industries closed its [seed round at USD 52 million in 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/17/defence-tech-startup-terra-industries-closes-seed-round-at-52-million/>), with Norleo Space Investments among the new investors. Moove raised [USD 250 million in a Series C in August 2026](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/investment/moove-raises-250m-in-series-c-round-valuation-hits-2-1b>). Standard Bank Group [agreed in October 2026 to buy shares in OPay for up to USD 200 million](<https://techcentral.co.za/standard-bank-opay-stake-ipo/287154/>), subject to OPay's US listing. In 2024 Development Partners International [led a USD 110 million Series C in Moniepoint](<https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/google-dpi-backs-moniepoint-in-110m-round/>), and in 2025 Visa [put USD 10 million into Moniepoint](<https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/23/african-fintech-moniepoint-gets-visa-backing/>).

**Money raised inside the country.** ThriveAgric raised [N5.3 billion in August 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/25/thriveagric-commercial-paper-raise-5-3-billion-naira/>) from institutional investors in the first series of a commercial paper programme approved by Nigeria's SEC. Dimension Data Limited raised [N4.05 billion in July 2026](<https://tech.africa/dimension-data-nigeria-fibre-bond/>) in a naira bond from high-net-worth investors to expand its fibre network.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** In 2026 JICA committed [USD 10.6 million as a grant](<https://www.jica.go.jp/english/information/press/2025/20260309_11.html>) to the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority for a project supporting startups that address social challenges.
