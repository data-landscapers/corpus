# Uganda

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In January 2024 [Gateway Partners lent USD 15 million to Watu Credit Uganda](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandan-startups-raise-usd-19m-in-2024/>), a two- and three-wheeler asset-finance company, through Alpen Capital's Africa Credit Opportunities Fund. In 2024 the [Belgian Investment Company for Developing Countries committed USD 12 million](<https://www.bio-invest.be/en/investments/towerco-of-africa>) to TowerCo Of Africa Uganda for rural telecom towers, and [ElectriFI put USD 1.6 million into the e-bike start-up Gogo Electric](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandan-startups-raise-usd-19m-in-2024/>). In September 2024 [Rexial, a direct-debit payments start-up, raised a USD 420,000 seed round](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandan-startups-raise-usd-19m-in-2024/>).

**Money raised inside the country.** No money raised inside Uganda has been found; [Rexial, a Ugandan start-up, raised a USD 420,000 seed round](<https://www.ceo.co.ug/ugandan-startups-raise-usd-19m-in-2024/>) in September 2024, from investors the source does not name.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money brought in by the government has been found; The [Belgian Investment Company for Developing Countries loan of USD 12 million went to a private tower company](<https://www.bio-invest.be/en/investments/towerco-of-africa>), starting 2024.
