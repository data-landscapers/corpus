# Morocco

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [The Moroccan superapp Ora Technologies raised a USD 7.5 million Series A](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/07/15/moroccos-ora-technologies-raises-7-5m-series-a-to-expand-superapp-ecosystem/>) in July 2025, after a USD 1.9 million pre-Series A in March. [The UM6P startup report counts USD 108.44 million raised by Moroccan startups in 48 disclosed deals in 2025](<https://leseco.ma/business/startups-au-maroc-un-record-historique-de-1084-millions-de-dollars-leves-en-2025.html>). [TechCabal counts more than USD 25 million in the first half of 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/27/african-venture-capital-is-backing-fewer-founders-than-ever/>), a total across deals.

**Money raised inside the country.** [Ora's Series A was led by the Moroccan venture firm Azur Innovation Management](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/07/15/moroccos-ora-technologies-raises-7-5m-series-a-to-expand-superapp-ecosystem/>), with three Moroccan strategic investors, in July 2025. [The UM6P report says 67.92% of the 2025 total came from foreign funds](<https://leseco.ma/business/startups-au-maroc-un-record-historique-de-1084-millions-de-dollars-leves-en-2025.html>).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money in a government digital project is reported for 2024 or later. In December 2025 [Proparco and IFC committed USD 40 million to Emtech II](<https://www.challenge.ma/emtech-ii-demergingtech-ventures-les-40-millions-de-dollars-de-proparco-et-difc-prets-a-etre-boucles-314197/>), a Moroccan-law venture fund for tech startups run by EmergingTech Ventures.

***Also on file***. [Bidra Innovation Ventures](<https://techtrends.africa/the-rise-of-african-corporate-venture-capital/>), the venture arm of the fertiliser group OCP. The [Startup Catalytic Fund](<https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/>), run by the digital ministry and managed by Tamwilcom.

***Not found***. No source found from 2024 on shows the state bringing private money into a public digital project.
