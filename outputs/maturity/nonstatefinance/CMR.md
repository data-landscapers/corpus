# Cameroon

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In September 2026 the US company Cybastion [committed $75 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/the-next-bottleneck-for-african-digital-identity-is-infrastructure-not-software>) to digital infrastructure in Cameroon, including an AI-ready data center in Douala with a dedicated power plant, as Biometric Update reports. In 2025 MTN Cameroon [said it is investing US$225 million](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-cameroon-works-with-camusat-to-expand-network/xnklOqz1wVYM4Ymz>) over three years in its own operations, chiefly its fintech division and national network coverage.

**Money raised inside the country.** No amount raised on a Cameroonian market or put in by a Cameroonian bank, fund or investor is held for 2024 or later. The MTN Cameroon pledge is [financed by the company itself](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-cameroon-works-with-camusat-to-expand-network/xnklOqz1wVYM4Ymz>), in its own network and fintech operations, with no separate recipient named.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No public digital project with a stated private amount has been found for 2024 or later, and no government co-investment is recorded. There is the [private data center commitment in Douala](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/the-next-bottleneck-for-african-digital-identity-is-infrastructure-not-software>), a US company's investment in which no public project is named and no government money is stated.
