# Cameroon

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In September 2026 the US company Cybastion [committed $75 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/the-next-bottleneck-for-african-digital-identity-is-infrastructure-not-software>) to digital infrastructure in Cameroon. It includes an AI-ready data center in Douala with its own power plant, Biometric Update reports. In 2025 MTN Cameroon [said it is investing US$225 million](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-cameroon-works-with-camusat-to-expand-network/xnklOqz1wVYM4Ymz>) over three years in its own operations. The money goes chiefly to its fintech division and national network. In January 2024 [the Cameroonian fintech Koree closed a US$200,000 pre-seed round](<https://techcabal.com/2024/01/03/cameroonian-fintech-koree-200k-pre-seed-round/>), backed by Cameroon Angels Network and others.

**Money raised inside the country.** No amount raised on a Cameroonian market or from a Cameroonian bank, fund or investor is reported for 2024 or later. The MTN Cameroon pledge is [financed by the company itself](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-cameroon-works-with-camusat-to-expand-network/xnklOqz1wVYM4Ymz>), in its own network and fintech arm.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No public digital project with a stated private amount has been found for 2024 or later. The [Douala data center commitment](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/the-next-bottleneck-for-african-digital-identity-is-infrastructure-not-software>) is a US company's own investment. It names no public project and no government money.
