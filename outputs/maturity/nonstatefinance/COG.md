# Congo

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In June 2024 [Uma Ventures, a Hong Kong-based venture capital firm, led a USD 3 million seed round for Noki Noki](<https://trendtype.com/news/brazzaville-based-delivery-platform-noki-noki-secures-3m-in-seed-funding/>), a delivery platform based in Brazzaville. The money went into a privately owned company that operates in the Congolese capital, and the investor who led the round is based abroad. Trendtype reported the round on 24 June 2024. In December 2025 [Fondation BantuHub and L'Archer Group committed EUR 1 million in equity to Congolese startups and small firms](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/12/22/congos-e1m-slow-build-vc-fund-aims-to-defy-central-africas-startup-odds/>), about USD 1.1 million, to be invested during 2026 through a 24-month incubation and acceleration programme in fintech, artificial intelligence and business services.

**Money raised inside the country.** The one 2024 round, [Noki Noki's USD 3 million seed round, was led by Uma Ventures of Hong Kong](<https://trendtype.com/news/brazzaville-based-delivery-platform-noki-noki-secures-3m-in-seed-funding/>), so that money came from outside Congo and was not raised on the Congolese market.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The one 2024 round, [Noki Noki's USD 3 million seed round led by Uma Ventures](<https://trendtype.com/news/brazzaville-based-delivery-platform-noki-noki-secures-3m-in-seed-funding/>), went to a private Brazzaville delivery platform, not to a project of the Congolese state.
