# Libya

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [Technext says the startup Mataa raised Libya's first venture funding round in July 2025](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/libya-modern-people-but-archaic-laws/>). The round was reported as above USD 100,000. Technext says it can confirm the sum was more than USD 1 million. No other private round for a Libyan digital business is reported from 2024 on.

**Money raised inside the country.** No source found says where the money was raised. [The report on Mataa's July 2025 round](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/libya-modern-people-but-archaic-laws/>) names no Libyan investor.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money in a government digital project is reported. The one amount held is [Mataa's 2025 venture round](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/libya-modern-people-but-archaic-laws/>), a private raise with no state partner named. The state's own startup fund, set up in 2025, is listed below.

***Also on file***. [Sunduq Intilaqa (Intilaqa Fund)](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-23-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7/>), created in 2025. It is the Council of Ministers' fund to support and guarantee finance for young innovation companies.

***Not found***. Only one amount is reported from 2024 on. No source found says whether private money came in other years.
