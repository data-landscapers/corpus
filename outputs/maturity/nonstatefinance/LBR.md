# Liberia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026 the Austrian company OeSD International is to [finance about US$54.7 million of nationwide ID enrolment and infrastructure](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/>) under a Build, Operate and Transfer concession, which the National Identification Registry says is signed and awaits legislative ratification. In 2024 MIGA committed [USD 81 million as a guarantee to Orange Liberia Inc.](<https://www.miga.org/press-release/miga-facilitates-expansion-telecom-services-liberia>) for its telecom expansion.

**Money raised inside the country.** [MIGA's USD 81 million guarantee in 2024 went to Orange Liberia Inc.](<https://www.miga.org/press-release/miga-facilitates-expansion-telecom-services-liberia>), and the source names no local lender or investor.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The government of Liberia brought OeSD International into the national ID and civil registration upgrade through [a signed public-private partnership described as about US$54 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>), reported in September 2026 and still needing parliamentary approval.
