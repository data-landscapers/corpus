# Liberia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2024 MIGA committed [a USD 81 million guarantee to Orange Liberia Inc.](<https://www.miga.org/press-release/miga-facilitates-expansion-telecom-services-liberia>) for its telecom expansion. In 2026 the Austrian company OeSD International agreed to [finance about US$54.7 million of national ID enrolment and infrastructure](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-nir-us54-7m-digital-id-deal-awaits-legislative-approval/>). The deal awaits ratification by the legislature.

**Money raised inside the country.** The [2024 MIGA guarantee went to Orange Liberia Inc.](<https://www.miga.org/press-release/miga-facilitates-expansion-telecom-services-liberia>). The source names no Liberian lender or investor.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** In April 2026 the government [signed a build-operate-transfer concession with OeSD International](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>). It covers a national ID and civil registration system, put at US$52 million. In September 2026 [the deal was put at about US$54 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>). It still needed parliamentary approval.
