# Zambia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026, Acumen's Hardest-to-Reach Catalyze initiative [closed a US$5 million equity investment](<https://www.techtrends.co.zm/acumen-invests-5-million-in-sun-king-to-scale-pay-as-you-go-solar-energy-access-in-zambia/>) in Sun King's Zambian subsidiary, a pay-as-you-go solar company whose customers repay by mobile money, a business built on digital payments.

**Money raised inside the country.** In July 2026, Lupiya, a Zambian digital lender, [said it will put a further K500 million](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/lupiya-commits-k500-million-to-expand-financial-inclusion-over-next-decade/>) into loans to Zambian small businesses, farmers, traders and women entrepreneurs over the next decade, from its own money.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The nearest private money to a state-linked sector is [IFC's US$75 million senior loan to the tower operator IHS Zambia in 2021](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44235/ihs-zambia-covid>), lent to a private company and not to a public project, and made before the period counted.
