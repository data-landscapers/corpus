# Zambia

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In 2026 Acumen [closed a US$5 million equity investment](<https://www.techtrends.co.zm/acumen-invests-5-million-in-sun-king-to-scale-pay-as-you-go-solar-energy-access-in-zambia/>) in Sun King's Zambian subsidiary. Sun King sells pay-as-you-go solar power that customers repay by mobile money. In May 2024 [the Zambian construction e-commerce startup Bosso raised a US$400,000 pre-seed round](<https://disruptafrica.com/2024/05/31/zambian-construction-e-commerce-startup-bosso-raises-400k-pre-seed-funding/>). Its backers include Leonard by Vinci and Launch Africa Ventures.

**Money raised inside the country.** In July 2026 Lupiya, a Zambian digital lender, [said it will put a further K500 million](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/lupiya-commits-k500-million-to-expand-financial-inclusion-over-next-decade/>) into loans over the next decade. The money is its own and goes to small businesses, farmers, traders and women entrepreneurs.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money in a public digital project is reported. The nearest is [IFC's US$75 million loan to the tower operator IHS Zambia in 2021](<https://disclosures.ifc.org/project-detail/SII/44235/ihs-zambia-covid>). It went to a private company and came before 2024.
