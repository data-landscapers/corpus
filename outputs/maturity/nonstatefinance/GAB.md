# Gabon

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In October 2025 [Gabonese fleet-management startup POZI announced a EUR 650,000 funding round](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0510-49294-gabon-s-pozi-raises-foreign-investment-in-first-ever-vc-deal-for-local-startup>), led by Saviu Ventures with Emsy Capital and Chazai Wamba taking part. It is the only amount held from 2024 on. In 2022 [MIGA committed a US$27.63 million guarantee](<https://www.miga.org/project/airtel-mobile-commerce>) covering an equity investment in the Airtel Money mobile money business in Gabon.

**Money raised inside the country.** No money raised inside Gabon has been found. The nearest held fact is the [POZI round of EUR 650,000, led by Saviu Ventures](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0510-49294-gabon-s-pozi-raises-foreign-investment-in-first-ever-vc-deal-for-local-startup>), announced on 3 October 2025.
