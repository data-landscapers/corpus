# Angola

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [In August 2025 Valor Económico reported that Elsewedy Electric of Egypt had taken over 60% of the mobile operator Movicel](<https://diariodosnegocios.com/egipcios-da-elsewedy-electric-injectam-400-milhoes-de-dolares-para-salvar-operadora-movicel/>). Its agreement provides for USD 400 million in phases, the first under way in Luanda. [In December 2025 CAP Capital, a European holding, bought all of TSMARTS](<https://diariodosnegocios.com/cap-capital-investe-50-milhoes-tsmarts-infraestruturas-angola/>), an Angolan telecom infrastructure firm. It announced EUR 50 million of investment over three years. In March 2026 BFA Asset Management [put $1.2 million from its Kimbo Fund into Anda](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/30/angola-bfa/>), a motorcycle-taxi start-up. In 2026 [EXIM Bank of the United States committed $100 million to Africell](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/economia/detalhe/exim-bank-dos-eua-anuncia-emprestimo-de-996-milhoes-de-dolares-a-africell-para-investimento-em-tecnologia-em-angola-74587.html>).

**Money raised inside the country.** The $1.2 million for Anda came from [BFA Asset Management of Luanda](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/30/angola-bfa/>), a firm spun off from Banco de Fomento Angola. [Breega, Speedinvest and 4DX Ventures, investors from outside Angola, had put $3.4 million into Anda](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/30/angola-bfa/>).

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No such project has been found. [The state is selling a 15% stake in the mobile operator Unitel](<https://angop.ao/en/noticias/economia/estado-encaixa-kz-300-mil-milhoes-com-venda-de-15-da-unitel/>) in 2026.

***Also on file***. [Angola Digital Acceleration Project (PADA)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/factsheet/2024/06/27/inclusive-digitalization-in-eastern-and-southern-africa-program-afe-angola>), run by the Instituto de Modernização Administrativa; [Unitel](<https://itweb.africa/article/unitel-opens-angola-share-offering/dgp45MaB8JaqX9l8>), whose share offering opened in July 2026; [Anda](<https://www.africaprivateequitynews.com/p/proparco-invests-in-angolas-anda>), a mobility and fintech app with drive-to-own vehicle financing.

***Not found***. No source found says the government brought private money into a public digital project.
