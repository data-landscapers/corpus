# Côte d'Ivoire

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [The Abidjan fintech Djamo raised $17 million in equity led by Janngo Capital](<https://partechpartners.com/news/djamo-hits-1m-active-customers-and-raises-17m-of-equity-to-double-down-on-digital-first-banking-services-for-users-across-francophone-africa>) in April 2025, which Partech calls the largest venture round in the country. The money serves its business in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal. The Dutch development bank FMO [approved an equity investment of EUR 2.40 million in the fintech Hub2](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>), effective August 2024. Invest International [approved a USD 0.325 million concessional loan to PAIX Indigo B.V.](<https://investinternational.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Invest-International-transaction-PAIX-Indigo-BV-Ivory-Coast.pdf>) in 2024, for a data centre feasibility study.

**Money raised inside the country.** The Hub2 equity came from outside the country. [FMO is a Dutch development bank](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>), and it invested under its own Ventures Program 2.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The 2024 money went to [a payments fintech](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>) and [a data centre developer](<https://investinternational.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Invest-International-transaction-PAIX-Indigo-BV-Ivory-Coast.pdf>). Both are private companies, and neither is a government project.
