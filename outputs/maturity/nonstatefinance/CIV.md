# Côte d'Ivoire

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** The Dutch development bank FMO [approved an equity investment of EUR 2.40 million in Hub2 (Connekt 4 SAS)](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>), effective August 2024. Hub2 is a fintech providing mobile wallet and payment services in Francophone Africa, and the investment sits under FMO's Ventures Program 2. Invest International [approved a concessional loan of USD 0.325 million to PAIX Indigo B.V.](<https://investinternational.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Invest-International-transaction-PAIX-Indigo-BV-Ivory-Coast.pdf>), part of the Paix Group, in 2024. The loan paid for a feasibility study for a data centre in Côte d'Ivoire.

**Money raised inside the country.** The equity in Hub2 came from outside the country: [FMO is a Dutch development bank](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>), and the investment was made under its own Ventures Program 2.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** The 2024 money went to [a payments fintech](<https://www.fmo.nl/project-detail/63830>) and [a data centre developer](<https://investinternational.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Invest-International-transaction-PAIX-Indigo-BV-Ivory-Coast.pdf>), both private companies, and not to a government project.
