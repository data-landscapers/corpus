## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** Fusepay, a Seychelles-founded fintech, closed a [$350,000 pre-seed round in August 2025](<https://dabafinance.com/en/news/fusepay-rolls-out-b2b-payments-platform>), backed by Hustle Fund, Everywhere Ventures, First Check Ventures, Startup Istanbul and an angel investor. That makes five named backers in one round, and the amount is the only one held for 2024 or later.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** There is the [Fusepay pre-seed round](<https://dabafinance.com/en/news/fusepay-rolls-out-b2b-payments-platform>), a private raise by a payments business, with no government role or public project named, and no private money alongside a state-led digital project has been found.
