# Mauritius

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** In October 2025 [Kuunda, a Mauritius-based fintech, raised USD 7.5 million in a pre-Series A round](<https://impactalpha.com/kuunda-lands-7-5-million-to-provide-short-term-financing-for-small-businesses/>), backed by Seedstars Africa Ventures, Accion Ventures, Nedbank, E4E Africa, 4Di Capital and Portugal Gateway Fund. It is the only private amount held for the country from 2024 on.

**Money raised inside the country.** The nearest held fact is older: in 2017 [Proparco committed USD 5 million in equity to TIDE Africa Fund I](<https://www.proparco.fr/en/carte-des-projets/tide-africa-fund-2017>), a venture fund registered in Mauritius that invests in start-ups across Sub-Saharan Africa. [Kuunda's 2025 round](<https://impactalpha.com/kuunda-lands-7-5-million-to-provide-short-term-financing-for-small-businesses/>) lists its backers but does not say where each is based.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money brought in by the government has been found. The nearest held fact is [Kuunda's USD 7.5 million round in 2025](<https://impactalpha.com/kuunda-lands-7-5-million-to-provide-short-term-financing-for-small-businesses/>), which was raised by a private company.
