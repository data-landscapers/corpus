# Eswatini

## Private money for digital businesses

**Private money put into a digital business, with its year.** [The Eswatini-based food delivery startup Swoop raised USD 7.3 million in seed funding](<https://weetracker.com/2026/04/24/swoop-7-3m-seed-nigeria-food-delivery-expansion/>) in April 2026, WeeTracker reports. Its backers include Long Journey, Variant and Version One, and the money is to take it into Nigeria.

**Money raised inside the country.** No source found says any of this money was raised in Eswatini. [The investors named in the April 2026 round](<https://weetracker.com/2026/04/24/swoop-7-3m-seed-nigeria-food-delivery-expansion/>) are not described as Eswatini firms.

**Private money the government brought into a public digital project.** No private money in a government digital project is reported.
