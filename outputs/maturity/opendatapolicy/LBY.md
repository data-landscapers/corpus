# Libya

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The General Information Authority presented a draft national policy for open government data on 27 November 2025, with a technical guide, and said both would go to government bodies for official adoption](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/>). No source held says the policy has been adopted. No source held names the government bodies it was sent to, or gives a date by which they are to adopt it.

**Portal run and data published.** [The authority opened the Open Government Data Platform on the same day, with a first set of datasets](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/>). [By February 2026 it reported 91 datasets from nine government bodies in eight sectors](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/11/includes-91-datasets-the-general-authority-for-information-launches-new-indicators-through-the-open-data-platform/>). The policy the platform came with is still a draft. [In February 2026 the authority launched new indicators across the 91 datasets](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Government Data Governance Policies](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/02/16/the-general-authority-for-information-presents-its-vision-on-government-data-governance-during-a-cloud-computing-workshop>); [National Data Distributor (LY-NDEX)](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/>); [LY-GOVREP](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/07/20/the-general-authority-for-information-begins-the-preliminary-operation-of-the-national-platform-for-libyan-government-reports>).

***Not held***. No source held says an open data policy is adopted and still running.
