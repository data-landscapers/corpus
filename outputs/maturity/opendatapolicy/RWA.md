# Rwanda

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [Rwanda's National Data Sharing Policy for the public sector is in place and makes government data open by default, subject to classification and access controls, as reported in May 2026](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>). The rule on open data is part of that policy; no separate open data policy is on record. No source found gives the date the rule on open data took effect.

**Portal run and data published.** No source found names a government open data portal running under the policy, or a public body that has released data under it. [The policy sets classification and access controls on what is opened](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>). No source found says which body decides what is classified, or how a public body is to publish what is open.
