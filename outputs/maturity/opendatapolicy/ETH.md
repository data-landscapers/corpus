# Ethiopia

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A national open data policy guideline had been drafted by April 2018, when it was put to consultation workshops of more than 200 government and other representatives, and was expected by September 2018](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt>). No source found says it was adopted. No source found from after 2018 mentions the guideline, or any open data policy written since.

**Portal run and data published.** [The national open data portal, data.gov.et, opened in March 2016 under a project run by the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt>). [By June 2018 the ministry and the Central Statistics Agency had posted 29 datasets on it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt>). Both came before any adopted policy, and no source found from the last three years says the portal is still running.
