# Gambia

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy lists the Government Open Data Strategy 2024-2027 among The Gambia's policies in a 2025 presentation, and says it was written with Estonia's e-Governance Academy under a joint programme of the African Union and the European Union](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). No source found gives the date the strategy was approved. [The presentation gives the strategy's years as 2024 to 2027 and, in one heading, as 2023 to 2026](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>).

**Portal run and data published.** No source found names a government open data portal running under the strategy, or a ministry that has released data under it. [The ministry's presentation names the strategy and no portal](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). No source found says which body is to carry the strategy out.

***Also on file***. [Government Web Portal](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>); [MYGOV - Digital Gambia Platform](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a government open data portal is running under the policy.
