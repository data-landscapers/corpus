# Uganda

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance reported for the year to June 2022 that a draft Open Data Policy had been circulated to its staff](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>). No later source found says the draft has gone to cabinet or been adopted. No source found names the body that would approve it, or gives a date for its adoption.

**Portal run and data published.** [The same report says work continued on getting an open data portal running, with staff reviewing it and giving their views](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>). No source found says the portal has opened to the public, or that any ministry has released data on it. The report does not tie the portal to the draft policy, and no source found from after 2022 mentions either.
