# Nigeria

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Federal Ministry of Communications issued the Nigeria Open Data Policy in July 2018; it applies to every federal ministry, department and agency and requires them to release their data open by default](<https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf>). No source held says it has been replaced or withdrawn.

**Portal run and data published.** [The policy says Data.gov.ng has been built as the national catalogue of open data, and requires agencies to link their datasets to it and to post a schedule of releases there](<https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf>). No source held from the last three years names an agency that has released data on it, or counts its datasets. No source held names the body that runs the catalogue, reports it at work after 2018 or says how many agencies have posted a release schedule.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source from the last three years naming a ministry or agency that has released data under the policy, or counting the datasets or the bodies publishing, is held.
