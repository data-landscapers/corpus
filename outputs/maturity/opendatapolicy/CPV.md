# Cape Verde

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A draft open data policy had been discussed with government agencies by March 2023 and was to be shared with civil society under the 2023-2025 open government action plan, which commits the government to prepare a law on open data](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>). No source held says the policy or the law has been adopted. No source held gives a date for either.

**Portal run and data published.** [The open data portal promised in the previous action plan was never made public and was under development at the Ministry of State Modernisation and Public Administration, with completion set for January 2025](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>). [The minister told a parliamentary committee in November 2023 that the portal was a 2024 project financed by the World Bank](<https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/>). No source held says it has opened.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Ministry of Finance budget and accounts open data page](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>); [Public bodies database](<https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/>); [Integrated public services portal](<https://www.governo.cv/ministra-edna-oliveira-ouvida-na-1a-comissao-especializada-no-ambito-da-discussao-na-especialidade-oe2024/>).

***Not held***. No source held says an open data policy is adopted and still running.
