# Tunisia

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [Government decree 2021-3 of 6 January 2021, issued by the Head of Government after the Council of Ministers deliberated, organises the publication of public data on the principle that it is open by default](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2021-3-du-6-janvier-2021-relatif-aux-donnees-publiques-ouvertes/>). [A national strategy for governing, opening and using public data, written under the decree, was presented in final draft at a workshop with the PAGOF programme on 26 March 2026](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>) and [still awaited the government's endorsement in August 2026](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>).

**Portal run and data published.** [A new version of the national open data portal, built to the decree's specifications, opened in early March 2023, and in April 2024 it carried datasets from 23 data producers across six themes](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/members/tunisia/commitments/TN0053/>), according to the Open Government Partnership's review. [The e-administration unit of the Presidency of the Government said in July 2024 that it had built the national public data register in application of the decree and of Circular 4 of 2024 on open public data](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/fr/index.php/2024/07/01/developpement-du-registre-national-des-donnees-publiques/>).
