# Tunisia

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [Government decree 2021-3 of 6 January 2021, issued by the Head of Government after the Council of Ministers deliberated, organises the publication of public data on the principle that it is open by default](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2021-3-du-6-janvier-2021-relatif-aux-donnees-publiques-ouvertes/>). [A national strategy for governing, opening and using public data, written under the decree, was presented in final draft in March 2026](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>) and [still awaited the government's endorsement in August 2026](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>).

**Portal run and data published.** [The decree applies to public bodies and sets no end date](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2021-3-du-6-janvier-2021-relatif-aux-donnees-publiques-ouvertes/>). No source held reports a national open data portal at work under it, or names a public body that has published data under it in the last three years. No source held says how many public bodies have complied with the decree.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Mizaniatouna](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>); [National Open Data Registry](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/documents/tunisia-results-report-2023-2025/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a government open data portal is running under the policy.
