# Benin

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** In December 2022 a World Bank open data readiness assessment, written for the Government of Benin, said the [draft access to information bill and its Open Data decree](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>) were under discussion within the government, with no clear date for sending them to parliament and little progress in over 12 months. It named adoption of the bill and decree as the first priority. From January 2022 to September 2024, [Expertise France ran a technical assistance project](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/fr/projets/benin-open-data>) supporting the national agency ASSI in setting up Benin's open data initiative, with a first axis of putting in place an open data regulation.

**Portal run and data published.** The same assessment said the [national open data portal](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>), built on CKAN and developed by the digital agency ASIN in 2021, was being finalised and was due to be officially launched in 2023. It reported no data published on it, and said [no open data licence or law yet existed](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>).
