# Malawi

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A CSEA policy brief of November 2025](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>) finds that Malawi's open data vision is spread across other instruments. It finds no dedicated open data implementation framework and no central authority to enforce open standards. The brief calls for a standardised framework and a unified portal to close that gap. The vision rests on those other instruments alone.

**Portal run and data published.** [The OGP Malawi Secretariat announced on 3 September 2025](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>) that Malawi had officially launched its new Open Data Portal. The launch was part of the Third OGP National Action Plan. The portal gives citizens access to government budget, development project and public service delivery data. It does so through dashboards and downloadable datasets. In [November 2025 the CSEA brief](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>) states that most government datasets stay in PDF reports.

***Also on file***. These include the [MWEITI Open Data Policy 2022 to 2027](<https://mweiti.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2024-11/malawi_open_data_policy%20approved%2023%20June%202022.pdf>) and the [Malawi National Electronic Procurement System (MANePS)](<https://www.ppda.mw/newsdetail/58>). Another is the [CDF dashboard](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/ben-phiri-unveils-malawis-real-time-cdf-dashboard-as-minister-flags-erosion-of-trust-risk-in-councils/>). They also include the [Digital Transformation Strategy 2021-2026](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>), the [National ICT Policy (2013)](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>) and the [Malawi OGP National Action Plan 2025-2028](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>).
