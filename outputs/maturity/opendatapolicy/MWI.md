# Malawi

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A CSEA policy brief of November 2025](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>) finds that Malawi's open data vision is spread across other instruments, with no dedicated open data implementation framework and no central authority to enforce open standards. The brief calls for a standardised framework and a unified portal to close that gap, since the vision rests on those other instruments alone.

**Portal run and data published.** [The OGP Malawi Secretariat announced on 3 September 2025](<https://ogp.gov.mw/events/malawi-launches-new-open-data-portal>) that Malawi had officially launched its new Open Data Portal as part of the Third OGP National Action Plan, giving citizens access to government budget, development project and public service delivery data through dashboards and downloadable datasets. In [November 2025 the CSEA brief](<https://cpanel.cseaafrica.org/images/posts/62181895.pdf>) states that most government datasets stay in PDF reports.
