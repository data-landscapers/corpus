# Ghana

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A draft National Data Sharing policy, with open data in it, was a product of the rebuilding of the Ghana Open Data Initiative between July 2018 and April 2019](<https://data.gov.gh/about>). No source found says the draft has been adopted. No source found from after 2019 says what became of it, or names another open data policy written since.

**Portal run and data published.** [The Ghana Open Data Initiative is a government platform run by the National Information Technology Agency under the communications ministry, with a data catalogue portal rebuilt in 2018 and 2019](<https://data.gov.gh/about>). No policy has been adopted for it to run under, and no source found names a ministry that has released data on it in the last three years.
