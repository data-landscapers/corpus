# Open data policy: national open data policy

*As at 2026-10-23.*

## The norm

The African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights' Declaration of Principles on Freedom of Expression and Access to Information in Africa, adopted in November 2019, asks public bodies to publish information of public interest without being asked, including by digital means (Principle 29). The African Union's Data Policy Framework, of 2022, asks states to open government data. This study asks whether a policy exists, is adopted, has a portal and has data published under it; it does not measure the policy against either.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source says the country has no open data policy and none is being written |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: a policy being written, or one that has run out with no successor adopted |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: a policy adopted and still running |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: a government portal is running under it |
| 5 Leading | Yes to all four: public bodies have published data under it in the last three years |

## Countries

### Tunisia: 5 Leading

Tunisia's 2021 open data decree is in force and its portal carried data from 23 public producers in 2024; the follow-on strategy is not adopted.

- Government decree no. 2021-3 of 6 January 2021, issued by the Head of Government after deliberation of the Council of Ministers, organises the publication of public data under the open-by-default principle for public bodies; the document states no end or replacement ([source, 2021-01-06](https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-gouvernemental-n-2021-3-du-6-janvier-2021-relatif-aux-donnees-publiques-ouvertes/)).
- The IRM 2021-2023 results text says a new version of the national open data portal, built to the decree's specifications, was launched in early March 2023 ([source, 2023-03](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/members/tunisia/commitments/TN0053/)).
- The IRM text says the portal carried datasets from 23 data producers across 6 themes (consulted April 2024), and that the e-government unit reported about 296 datasets identified by over 40 public entities in the inventory system by end 2023 ([source, 2024-04](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/members/tunisia/commitments/TN0053/)).

### Nigeria: 4 Operating

Nigeria issued an open data policy in 2018 with a national data catalogue; no source found says whether agencies have published on it since 2023.

- The policy says Data.gov.ng has been developed as the national catalogue of open data, requires agencies to link their datasets to it and to post a Rolling Release Schedule on it; the document does not name who runs the portal, and ties it to the policy; it names no publishing ministry and gives no dataset count (document published 2018-07) ([source, 2018-07](https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf)).
- The Federal Ministry of Communications issued the Nigeria Open Data Policy in July 2018, applying to all federal ministries, departments and agencies and requiring them to release data open by default ([source, 2018-07](https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/nigeria-open-data-policy/nigeria-open-data-policy.pdf)).

### Egypt: 3 Established

Egypt adopted an open data policy in August 2025; no source found says whether a government portal is running under it.

- Al-Ahram Gate reports the minister of communications and information technology, Amr Talaat, listing the launch of the open data policy among the supportive policies and legislation Egypt has adopted for digital transformation; the article gives no launch date, so the publication date is used ([source, 2025-12-21](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5356100.aspx)).
- The National Council for Artificial Intelligence, chaired by the communications and IT minister, formally adopted Egypt's Open Data Policy, drafted by MCIT, as a reference document for all government entities; it entered into force in August 2025 as a transitional framework pending a data governance law ([source, 2025-08](https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/08/egypt-adopts-first-national-open-data-policy-to-drive-digital-transformation/)).

### Gambia: 3 Established

The Gambia has an open data strategy for 2024 to 2027; no source found says whether a government portal is running under it.

- A Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy presentation lists the Government Open Data Strategy 2024-2027 among The Gambia's policies, strategies and legal frameworks, and says it was developed with the e-Governance Academy of Estonia under the AU-EU D4D Hub; it gives no approval date and also headlines it as 'Open Data Strategy (2023-2026)', and the document's date is its published year 2025 ([source, 2025](https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf)).

### Mauritius: 3 Established

Mauritius issued a national open data policy in 2017; no source found says whether a portal is running under it.

- The Ministry of Technology, Communication and Innovation issued the National Open Data Policy in May 2017, prepared with the Central Informatics Bureau, the Data Protection Office and the Attorney General's Office, setting 'open by default' for public datasets and adopting the CC BY 4.0 licence; the document carries no end date ([source, 2017-05](https://bot.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf)).

### Rwanda: 3 Established

Rwanda's data sharing policy, in place in 2026, makes government data open by default; no source found says whether a portal is running under it.

- Cenfri reports the public sector data sharing policy is in place and positions government data as open by default, subject to classification and access controls; the document gives no adoption date, so this is dated to its publication ([source, 2026-05](https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/)).

### Benin: 2 Nascent

Benin had a draft open data decree under discussion in December 2022 and no portal data published; no adopted policy is known.

- The World Bank open data readiness assessment, written for the Government of Benin, says the draft access to information bill and its Open Data decree were under discussion within the government, with no clear date for sending them to parliament and little progress in over 12 months; it names adoption of the bill and decree as the first priority ([source, 2022-12-23](https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf)).

### Botswana: 2 Nascent

Botswana's national open data policy was in draft in March 2026, awaiting adoption by Parliament; no source found says whether it has since been adopted.

- HAUS's Phase 2 terms of reference for the EU-funded open data hackathons programme in Botswana refer to the anticipated adoption of the national Open Data Policy by Parliament, so the policy is written and not yet adopted; the document states no approval or launch ([source, 2026-03-14](https://haus.fi/en/topical/terms-of-reference-support-for-open-data-hackathons-and-forum-in-botswana-phase-2/)).

### Cape Verde: 2 Nascent

Cabo Verde had a draft open data policy under discussion in 2023; no source found says whether it has been adopted.

- The OGP Independent Reporting Mechanism review says an open data policy draft was discussed with government agencies, per the National Director of State Modernization interviewed on 20 March 2023, and is to be shared with civil society during the 2023-2025 action plan; the plan's commitment CV0004 is to prepare the law establishing the policy between January 2023 and January 2024 ([source, 2023-03-20](https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/)).

### Ethiopia: 2 Nascent

Ethiopia drafted an open data policy guideline in 2018; no source found says whether it was adopted.

- The World Bank status report says the national open data policy guideline had been drafted, was put to consultation workshops of over 200 government and non-government representatives on 24-26 April 2018, and was expected to be delivered by September 2018 ([source, 2018-04](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/557611531038811117/txt/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Ethiopia-Supporting-Open-Data-Initiative-P156400-Sequence-No-03.txt)).

### Ghana: 2 Nascent

Ghana had a draft data sharing policy covering open data in 2019; no source found says whether it was adopted.

- The Ghana Open Data Initiative's history page lists a draft National Data Sharing (Open Data incorporated) policy as a deliverable of the portal revamp that ran from July 2018 to April 2019, and states no adoption of it ([source, 2019-04](https://data.gov.gh/about)).

### Libya: 2 Nascent

Libya presented a draft open government data policy in November 2025 and opened a portal with it; no source found says whether the policy is adopted.

- The GIA revealed the draft national policy for open government data on 27 November 2025, with a national technical guide issued, and said it would distribute both to government entities for official adoption ([source, 2025-11-27](https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/27/the-general-authority-for-information-launches-the-open-government-data-platform/)).

### Mauritania: 2 Nascent

Mauritania wrote a national open data strategy in 2023; no source found says whether the government has approved it.

- The Ministry of Digital Transformation, Innovation and Administration Modernisation (MTNIMA) strategy document, dated June 2023, sets out a national open data and interoperability strategy with three axes and a roadmap, including open by default for public bodies' data; the passages state no cabinet approval, launch or issue date, and say the open data law and the portal's implementing decree are still to be drafted ([source, 2023-06](https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf)).

### Morocco: 2 Nascent

Morocco's ministry was preparing a draft open data decree in 2025, beside a portal running since 2011; no source found reports its adoption.

- The minister said the ministry is preparing a draft decree on open public data under the Maroc Digital 2030 strategy ([source, 2025-10-13](https://leseco.ma/maroc/connectivite-5g-et-e-gouvernement-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-fait-le-bilan-de-la-transformation-numerique.html)).

### Mozambique: 2 Nascent

Mozambique's draft data governance policy of 2025 would require ministries to publish data openly; no source found says whether it has been approved.

- Zero Draft 2 of the National Data Governance Policy and Implementation Strategy, drafted as a Council of Ministers Resolution with number and approval date left blank, has an open data section requiring all ministries, agencies and parastatals to proactively release non-sensitive datasets; the document has no face date, so its published date (2025-11, an upload-folder proxy) is used ([source, 2025-11](https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf)).

### South Africa: 2 Nascent

An open data policy framework was developed for South Africa by 2026; no source found says whether the government has adopted it.

### Tanzania: 2 Nascent

Tanzania's open data policy was still a draft in June 2017, when its portal held 163 datasets; no source found says whether it was adopted.

- The World Bank's SOGDAT year 3 report (period February 2016 to June 2017) says the National Open Data Policy was drafted by the Open Data Task Force in 2016, consulted on in April 2016, handed to the Ministry of Finance for submission to Cabinet in December 2016 and presented to the Permanent Secretary of Finance in February 2017, but by June 2017 it had not been officially adopted ([source, 2017-06](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt)).
