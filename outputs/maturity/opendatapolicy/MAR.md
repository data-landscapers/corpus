# Morocco

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** Morocco has no adopted open data policy on record. [In October 2025 the minister for digital transition said the ministry was preparing a draft decree on open public data under the Maroc Digital 2030 strategy](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/connectivite-5g-et-e-gouvernement-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-fait-le-bilan-de-la-transformation-numerique.html>). No source found says the draft has gone to the government council, been adopted or been published, or sets a date for it.

**Portal run and data published.** [The national portal data.gov.ma has run since 2011](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>), and [the Agence de Développement du Digital launched a new version in Arabic and French in December 2021](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-des-donnees-ouvertes>). [The agency manages the portal and monitors the national Open Data action plan, which a steering committee set up in December 2019 oversees](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/documentations>), and [that committee debated a 2026 action plan that includes maintaining and enriching the portal](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/11eme-reunion-du-comite-de-pilotage-national-open-data>). [The agency itself publishes datasets there, such as one listing 1,028 Moroccan startups, updated in January 2025](<https://data.gov.ma/data/dataset/donnees-sur-les-startups>). None of these ties the portal to a policy, since none has been adopted.

***Also on file***. [Loi n°31-13 relative au droit d'accès à l'information](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>); [Open Data Readiness Assessment (ODRA)](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>); [Plan d'actions Open Data](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-version-du-portail-national-des-donnees-ouvertes>).

***Not found***. No source found says the draft open data decree has been adopted, so the portal does not yet run under a policy.
