# Morocco

## Open data policy

***Noted, not assessed***. [The national open data portal, data.gov.ma, opened in 2011 and run by the Agence de Développement du Digital](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>); [a national open data steering committee with an action plan for 2026](<https://data.gov.ma/fr/actualites/11eme-reunion-du-comite-de-pilotage-national-open-data>); [Loi n°31-13 relative au droit d'accès à l'information](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>); [Open Data Readiness Assessment (ODRA)](<https://www.data.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2025-01/Manuel_Standards_Donn%C3%A9es_Open_Data_1.1.pdf>).

***Not held***. A portal is named with no open data policy behind it. No policy or draft is on record, and no source dated in the last three years says the country has neither.
