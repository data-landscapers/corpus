# Mozambique

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A second draft of Mozambique's National Data Governance Policy and Implementation Strategy, written as a resolution of the Council of Ministers with its number and approval date left blank, has an open data section requiring every ministry, agency and state company to release its non-sensitive datasets; the draft was held in November 2025](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>). No source found says it has been approved. No source found gives the date the draft was written, or says when it is to go to the Council of Ministers.

**Portal run and data published.** No source found names a government open data portal in Mozambique, or a ministry that releases its data openly. [The draft sets the duty to publish and has not come into effect](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Portal de Divulgação de Dados de Infra-estruturas Públicas (IPDP)](<https://www.dadosinfra.co.mz/>); [Centralized open data portal (planned)](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says an open data policy is adopted and still running.
