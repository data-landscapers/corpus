# Mauritania

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The digital transformation ministry's national strategy for open and interoperable data is dated June 2023](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>). It makes public bodies' data open by default and sets out three lines of work and a roadmap. On 29 May 2025 the minister [told the National Assembly that the open data and interoperability strategies had been adopted](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/272230>). He named them among several complementary national strategies.

**Portal run and data published.** [The strategy said in June 2023 that the open data law and the decree creating a national portal were still to be written](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>). In 2025 the government [told the World Bank's GovTech survey that it had no open data portal](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). No source names a ministry that has released its data openly under the strategy.

***Also on file***. [EITI Data Warehouse portal](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>); [2019 EITI disclosure decree](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>); [planned open data portal](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source says a national open data portal runs under the strategy.
