# Mauritania

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The digital transformation ministry's national strategy for open and interoperable data, dated June 2023, makes public bodies' data open by default and sets out three lines of work and a roadmap](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>). No source held says the government has approved or launched it. No source held from after June 2023 says what became of the strategy.

**Portal run and data published.** [The strategy says the law on open data and the decree setting up the national portal were still to be written in June 2023](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>). No source held says either has been issued, or that a national portal has opened. No source held names the body that is to run the portal, or a ministry that has released its data openly.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Data Warehouse portal (Mauritania EITI)](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>); [2019 decree on systematic disclosure of EITI data](<https://eiti.org/board-decision/2024-57>); [Portail des données ouvertes de la Mauritanie (planned)](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Strategie-de-donnees.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held says an open data policy is adopted and still running.
