# Egypt

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The National Council for Artificial Intelligence, chaired by the communications minister, adopted Egypt's Open Data Policy, drafted by the communications ministry, as a reference for all government bodies; it took effect in August 2025, pending a data governance law](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/08/egypt-adopts-first-national-open-data-policy-to-drive-digital-transformation/>). [In December 2025 the minister listed its launch among the policies Egypt has adopted for digital transformation](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5356100.aspx>).

**Portal run and data published.** [The policy took effect as a transitional framework until a data governance law is passed](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/08/egypt-adopts-first-national-open-data-policy-to-drive-digital-transformation/>). No source found says a government open data portal has opened under it, or names a ministry that has released data under it. No source found says which body is to run a portal, when the data governance law is expected or which government bodies will publish first.
