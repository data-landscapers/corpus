# Mauritius

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Ministry of Technology, Communication and Innovation issued the National Open Data Policy in May 2017, written with the Central Informatics Bureau, the Data Protection Office and the Attorney General's Office; it makes public datasets open by default and has no end date](<https://bot.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf>). No source found says it has been replaced or withdrawn.

**Portal run and data published.** [The government told the World Bank's GovTech survey in 2025 that data.govmu.org is its open data portal](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [Under the May 2017 policy, public bodies release their datasets under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence, open by default](<https://bot.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf>). No source found names a ministry that has released data on the portal in the last three years. None counts its datasets either.
