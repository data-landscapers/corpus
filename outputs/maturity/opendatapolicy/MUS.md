# Mauritius

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Ministry of Technology, Communication and Innovation issued the National Open Data Policy in May 2017, written with the Central Informatics Bureau, the Data Protection Office and the Attorney General's Office; it makes public datasets open by default and has no end date](<https://bot.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf>). No source held says it has been replaced or withdrawn.

**Portal run and data published.** [The policy adopts the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence for the datasets public bodies release](<https://bot.govmu.org/Documents/Strategies/Mauritius%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20May%202017.pdf>). No source held names the government portal running under the policy, or a ministry that has released data under it in the last three years. No source held from after 2017 reports how the policy has been carried out, or which ministries have named officers for it.
