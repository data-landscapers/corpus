# Tanzania

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The World Bank's SOGDAT year 3 report](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt>), covering February 2016 to June 2017, says the National Open Data Policy was drafted by the Open Data Task Force in 2016 and consulted on in April 2016. It was handed to the Ministry of Finance for submission to Cabinet in December 2016 and presented to the Permanent Secretary of Finance in February 2017. By June 2017 it had not been officially adopted.

**Portal run and data published.** [The same report](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt>) says the national open data portal opendata.go.tz, launched in September 2015 and managed by the eGovernment Agency, held 163 datasets (755 resources) from 11 institutions in 4 sectors by June 2017, double the year before. The portal ran under the Chief Secretary's January 2015 circular as the interim directive.
