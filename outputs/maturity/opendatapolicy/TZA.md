# Tanzania

## Open data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The World Bank's SOGDAT year 3 report](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt>) covers February 2016 to June 2017. It says the Open Data Task Force drafted the National Open Data Policy in 2016 and consulted on it in April 2016. It was handed to the Ministry of Finance for submission to Cabinet in December 2016. It was presented to the Permanent Secretary of Finance in February 2017. By June 2017 it had not been officially adopted.

**Portal run and data published.** [The same report](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt>) says the national open data portal opendata.go.tz was launched in September 2015 and managed by the eGovernment Agency. By June 2017 it held 163 datasets (755 resources) from 11 institutions in 4 sectors, double the year before. The portal ran under the Chief Secretary's January 2015 circular as the interim directive.

***Also on file***. These include the [Tanzania Integrated Statistical Portal (TISP)](<https://tisp.nbs.go.tz/>), the [NeST Open Contracting Data Portal](<https://data.nest.go.tz/assets/docs/OCDS_Publication_Policy_PPRA.pdf>) and the [National e-Procurement System of Tanzania (NeST)](<https://data.nest.go.tz/assets/docs/OCDS_Publication_Policy_PPRA.pdf>). They also include the [Tanzania Open Data Portal (opendata.go.tz)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/933101529919350715/txt/SOGDAT-Year-3-Report.txt>) and the [TEITI Open Data Policy](<https://www.teiti.go.tz/storage/app/uploads/public/643/fb8/35d/643fb835dd1b9116456168.pdf>).

***Not found***. Whether the National Open Data Policy was adopted after June 2017, and whether it is still running.
