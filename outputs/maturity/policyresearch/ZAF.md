# South Africa

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** In September 2026 [Research ICT Africa, the Centre for Analytics and Behavioural Change and Moxii Africa launched the findings of a joint national study on the governance of harmful online content](<https://researchictafrica.net/2026/09/18/when-harm-goes-viral-multistakeholder-forum-launches-timely-report-on-south-africas-social-media-problem/>), built on legal analysis, social media monitoring and over 30 key informant interviews. In June 2025 Stellenbosch University's Policy Innovation Lab [updated its searchable catalogue of AI tools in government](<https://policyinnovationlab.sun.ac.za/update-to-our-catalogue-of-ai-public-sector-use-cases/>), adding 23 South African tools.

**Feeding policy.** Research ICT Africa [submitted a response on the proposed amendments to the Identification Act](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>), which would provide for a digital ID, naming preconditions it found missing, among them a digital identity policy and a privacy impact assessment. It is also [running a study of digital ID in the Western Cape, Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>), one of its researchers wrote in October 2026.

**Standing role.** The nearest held fact is Research ICT Africa's [submission on the Identification Act amendments](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>), a contribution to one consultation.
