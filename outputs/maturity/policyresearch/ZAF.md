# South Africa

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** In September 2026 [Research ICT Africa, the Centre for Analytics and Behavioural Change and Moxii Africa launched a joint national study](<https://researchictafrica.net/2026/09/18/when-harm-goes-viral-multistakeholder-forum-launches-timely-report-on-south-africas-social-media-problem/>) on the governance of harmful online content. It drew on legal analysis, social media monitoring and over 30 interviews. In April 2026 Trade & Industrial Policy Strategies [published a briefing note on trade in telecommunications](<https://www.tips.org.za/manufacturing-data/the-real-economy-bulletin/quarterly-bulletin/item/download/2831_eb2c166ca4903dfdab4b8e54235778cc>). In June 2025 Stellenbosch University's Policy Innovation Lab [updated its catalogue of AI tools in government](<https://policyinnovationlab.sun.ac.za/update-to-our-catalogue-of-ai-public-sector-use-cases/>), adding 23 South African tools. [In April 2026 the University of Johannesburg secured a UNESCO Chair on digital media in Africa](<https://news.uj.ac.za/news/uj-anchors-national-digital-strategy-with-unesco-chair-and-government-partnership/>). It runs to 2030 and researches digital inclusion.

**Feeding policy.** Research ICT Africa [responded to the proposed amendments to the Identification Act](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>), which would provide for a digital ID. It named preconditions it found missing, among them a digital identity policy and a privacy impact assessment. One of its researchers wrote in October 2026 that it is also [studying digital ID in the Western Cape, Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>).

**Standing role.** The nearest held fact is Research ICT Africa's [submission on the Identification Act amendments](<https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-10-26-drew-haller-millions-risk-exclusion-as-smart-id-delays-persist/>), a contribution to one consultation.
