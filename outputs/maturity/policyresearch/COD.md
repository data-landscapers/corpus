# DR Congo

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [On 6 August 2024 Think Tank Law and Technologies, a Kinshasa study bureau, published its first study, on the DRC's law on personal data protection](<https://www.africanewsrdc.net/actu/law-and-technologies-fait-des-recommandations-au-gouvernement-et-au-secteur-prive/>). It made recommendations to the government and to private firms. On 26 July 2024 [the CRESH research centre held a conference on digital technology and development](<https://www.reporter.cd/le-numerique-et-le-developpement-de-la-rdc-au-coeur-dun-debat-scientifique-au-centre-de-recherche-en-sciences-humaines-cresh/>), digital sovereignty included. On 11 September 2025 [the CES-UDPS held a scientific morning in Kinshasa](<https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/kinshasa-sengager-dans-la-numerisation-des-services-publics-une-recommandation-dune-matinee-scientifique/>) on using Congolese data for local AI.

**Feeding policy.** [The study asks the government to issue the decree that sets up the data protection authority created by the Code du numérique](<https://www.africanewsrdc.net/actu/law-and-technologies-fait-des-recommandations-au-gouvernement-et-au-secteur-prive/>). It also asks it to ratify the African Union's Malabo convention. The minister for posts, telecommunications and digital affairs, Augustin Kibassa Maliba, launched the bureau at the same event.

**Standing role.** [In July 2023 a colloquium at the Université panafricaine de gouvernance et innovations recommended a national digital policy](<https://acp.cd/nation/kinshasa-une-politique-nationale-numerique-recommandee-au-colloque-dune-universite-locale/>). The University of Kinshasa and a governance research centre took part.
