# Think tanks and academic departments contributing to DT policy: digital policy research

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

STISA-2034 and Agenda 2063's second ten-year plan ask for research systems funded toward 1 per cent of GDP and contributing to policy; neither counts think tanks. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## Countries

### Kenya: 4 Operating

Local research fed a digital policy process in 2026; a standing role is not on record.

- KICTANet submitted a memorandum to the Senate of Kenya on 5 May 2026 as a public comment on the Artificial Intelligence Bill, 2026, proposing a statutory AI Authority, a National Council and a ring-fenced fund ([source, 2026-05-05](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-BILL-2026-Memorandum.docx.pdf)).
- The CEO's letter in KICTANet's Annual Report 2024 says its evidence-based advocacy contributed to policy reforms, including reviews of ICT legislation and roundtables on digital taxation and cybersecurity ([source, 2024](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/?mdocs-file=52063)).

### Uganda: 4 Operating

Local research fed a digital policy process in 2026; a standing role is not on record.

- CIPESA submitted recommendations to the national taskforce developing Uganda's AI and Emerging Technologies Strategy, in response to the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance process ([source, 2026-05-11](https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/)).

### Cameroon: 3 Established

A local institution published research on digital policy in 2026; a part in a policy process is not on record.

- Digital Empowerment Gateway published Policy Brief 01 from its ongoing research on digital governance and evidence ecosystem development in Cameroon ([source, 2026-09-30](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/beyond-infrastructure-why-cameroons-digital-challenge-frankline-ojong-0mxqf/)).
- Nkafu Policy Institute published a policy brief on AI and the future of work in Cameroon, recommending four pillars of action to the Government of Cameroon ([source, 2026-01-06](https://nkafu.org/ai-and-the-future-of-work-in-cameroon-preparing-the-workforce-for-emerging-opportunities/)).

### Nigeria: 3 Established

A local institution published research on digital policy in 2026; a part in a policy process is not on record.

- Paradigm Initiative published a policy brief on disability policy and practice in Nigeria's digital ecosystem, informed by desk research and a 25 May 2026 policy dialogue it convened in Abuja with 34 participants ([source, 2026-09-10](https://paradigmhq.org/report/assessing-disability-policy-and-practice-in-nigerias-digital-ecosystem-a-policy-brief/)).

### Sierra Leone: 3 Established

A local institution published research on digital policy in 2024; a part in a policy process is not on record.

- MRCG National Coordinator Francis Sowa said the group has produced reports on women's rights online, gendered disinformation, media freedom and freedom of expression in Sierra Leone, with initiatives running 2020 to 2024 ([source, 2024](https://sierraloaded.sl/local/stakeholders-women-facing-online-abuse/)).

### South Africa: 3 Established

### Sudan: 3 Established

### Zambia: 3 Established

A local institution published research on digital policy in 2026; a part in a policy process is not on record.

- MISA Zambia launched its Digital Rights Report 2026 and Media Development Report 2026, which its chairperson says give evidence-based analysis and recommendations to government and regulators ([source, 2026-09-28](https://register.misa.org/2026/09/28/government-reaffirms-commitment-to-digital-rights-and-media-development/)).
- MISA Zambia launched its 2025 Digital Rights Report in Lusaka, officiated for the Ministry of Information and Media by the Director for Press and Media, with government, media and civil society present ([source, 2026-07-30](https://youthvillagezm.com/2026/07/zambia-reaffirms-commitment-to-digital-rights-as-technology-drives-national-development/)).

### Zimbabwe: 3 Established

A local institution published research on digital policy in 2026; a part in a policy process is not on record.

### Benin: 2 Nascent
