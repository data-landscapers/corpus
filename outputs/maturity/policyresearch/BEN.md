# Benin

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** On 28-30 July 2026 [the Réseau des Femmes Leaders pour le Développement held a West Africa Feminist Data Summit](<https://rflgd.org/2026/08/02/thanks-to-sida-l-west-africa-feminist-data-summit-2026-%c2%b7-one-hundred-delegates-%c2%b7-twenty-one-countries/>) in Porto-Novo. About 100 delegates attended, researchers among them. [The programme of the West African Forum on Digital Transformations in Cotonou, 7-9 July 2026, had LASDEL Benin presenting research on digitised public services](<https://ferdi.fr/dl/df-MVdqDUNqMCiL26jATkxWaYWM/telecharger-le-programme-du-forum.pdf>). [The Global Development Network lists five Beninese research bodies, LASDEL among them, in consortia on digital transformation](<https://gdn.int/digitalisation/programmes/digital-transformation-in-west-africa-and-the-sahel/>).

**Feeding policy.** [On 12 January 2024 Dr Julien Hounkpe of the University of Abomey-Calavi presented a critique of the Code du numérique to members of parliament](<https://julienhounkpe.info/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Contribution0.pdf>). He spoke at a National Assembly workshop in Porto-Novo on revising the code. He is a researcher at the university's law research centre, CREDIJ.

**Standing role.** [Dr Hounkpe was formerly a legal adviser to the President of the National Assembly](<https://julienhounkpe.info/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Contribution0.pdf>).
