# Namibia

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** Namibia opened a public institute for research on artificial intelligence in September 2026. [The National Commission on Research, Science and Technology launched the National AI Institute in Windhoek on 23 September 2026](<https://neweralive.na/ncrst-opens-national-ai-institute/>). It has a research development centre. Its chief executive, Anicia Peters, said it will support research on how to govern AI. No research from it is on file yet. [Earlier, in August 2023, the Institute for Public Policy Research published a piece on data protection and surveillance](<https://ippr.org.na/blog/data-protection-in-the-age-of-mass-surveillance-part-1/>). It had commissioned a review of digital rights in Namibia in October 2022.

**Feeding policy.** [In April 2026 the Institute for Public Policy Research sent the ICT ministry its analysis of the cybercrime bill](<https://www.namibian.com.na/problems-with-the-cybercrime-bill/>), The Namibian reports. No research it published since October 2023 has been found.

**Standing role.** No source found says a local research body holds a standing advisory role in digital policy. [The new AI institute sits under the national research commission](<https://neweralive.na/ncrst-opens-national-ai-institute/>), but no advisory role is stated.
