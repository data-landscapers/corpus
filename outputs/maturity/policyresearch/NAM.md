# Namibia

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [In July 2026 the Institute for Public Policy Research published a briefing on the draft Cybercrime Bill](<https://www.namibian.com.na/cybercrime-bill-must-protect-women-reporters/>). It called the bill incompatible with the Constitution in numerous respects, The Namibian reports. [In August 2023 the institute published a piece on data protection and surveillance](<https://ippr.org.na/blog/data-protection-in-the-age-of-mass-surveillance-part-1/>). [The National Commission on Research, Science and Technology launched the National AI Institute in Windhoek on 23 September 2026](<https://neweralive.na/ncrst-opens-national-ai-institute/>). It has a research development centre, and its chief executive, Anicia Peters, said it will support research on how to govern AI.

**Feeding policy.** [In April 2026 the institute sent its analysis of the cybercrime bill to the ICT ministry](<https://www.namibian.com.na/problems-with-the-cybercrime-bill/>). The Namibian reported the submission.

**Standing role.** No source found says a local research body holds a standing advisory role in digital policy. [The new AI institute sits under the national research commission](<https://neweralive.na/ncrst-opens-national-ai-institute/>), but no advisory role is stated.
