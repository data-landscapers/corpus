# Uganda

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [The Economic Policy Research Centre published a think-tank blog on Uganda's AI opportunities and fears, covering access divides, the Ministry of ICT's promised AI strategy and recommendations for it](<https://eprcug.org/blog/for-uganda-ai-brings-opportunities-fears/>). [CIPESA's report 'Navigating the Implications of AI on Digital Democracy in Uganda' and its policy brief 'An Artificial Intelligence Eco-System for Uganda' are cited as its published work on AI in Uganda](<https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/>). [EPRC's strategic plan 2022/23-2026/27 states its research agenda will cover six thematic areas, one of them Digitalisation](<https://eprcug.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/EPRC-STRATEGIC-PLAN-2022-23-2026-27_.pdf>).

**Feeding policy.** [CIPESA submitted recommendations to the national taskforce developing Uganda's AI and Emerging Technologies Strategy, in response to the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance process](<https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/>).
