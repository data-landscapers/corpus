# Uganda

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [In October 2026 the Economic Policy Research Centre (EPRC) published a blog on AI in Uganda](<https://eprcug.org/blog/for-uganda-ai-brings-opportunities-fears/>). [It covered access divides and the promised AI strategy, with recommendations](<https://eprcug.org/blog/for-uganda-ai-brings-opportunities-fears/>). [In October 2026 the Daily Monitor reported EPRC research finding that high upfront digital fees burden households with cost and exclusion](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/business/finance/exim-bank-terrapay-partner-to-cut-cross-border-remittance-costs-5653342>). [In May 2026 CIPESA cited its own report and policy brief on AI in Uganda](<https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/>). [In January 2025 the Centre for Multilateral Affairs, a Kampala think tank, published a policy brief on inclusive AI governance](<https://thecfma.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Policy-brief-AI-governance_CfMA_2025_Final.pdf>).

**Feeding policy.** [In May 2026 CIPESA sent recommendations to the taskforce drafting the AI and Emerging Technologies Strategy](<https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/>). [The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance runs that process](<https://cipesa.org/2026/05/cipesa-urges-rights-centred-approach-to-ugandas-ai-strategy/>).

**Standing role.** No source found since October 2023 says a local research institution holds a standing advisory role in digital policy.
