# Think tanks and academic departments contributing to DT policy: digital policy research

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

STISA-2034 and Agenda 2063's second ten-year plan ask for research systems funded toward 1 per cent of GDP and contributing to policy; neither counts think tanks. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

### Kenya: 4 Operating

Local research fed a digital policy process in 2026; a standing role is not on record.

- KICTANet submitted a memorandum to the Senate of Kenya on 5 May 2026 as a public comment on the Artificial Intelligence Bill, 2026, proposing a statutory AI Authority, a National Council and a ring-fenced fund ([source, 2026-05-05](https://www.kictanet.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-BILL-2026-Memorandum.docx.pdf)).
