# Egypt

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [On 4 May 2025 the Access to Knowledge for Development Center (A2K4D) at the American University in Cairo held a roundtable on responsible AI in Egypt](<https://menaobservatory.ai/en/blogs/84>). It ran the meeting through its MENA Observatory on Responsible AI. Experts from business, academia, government and parliament took part. [A2K4D had launched the observatory at a workshop on 4 February 2024](<https://business.aucegypt.edu/news/artificial-intelligence-data-and-platform-work-mena-cases-studies-and-ai-observatory>), where it shared a year of its research on data and AI governance. [In March 2025 the prime minister appointed a new board for the Information and Decision Support Centre, the cabinet's think tank](<https://en.amwalalghad.com/egypts-pm-overhauls-decision-making-centre-with-high-profile-expert-board/>). The board is to guide a research agenda that stresses digital transformation and AI-driven policy analysis.

**Feeding policy.** [The first roundtable in A2K4D's series, on 14 January 2025, discussed Egypt's proposed AI bill and made recommendations](<https://menaobservatory.ai/en/blogs/84>). The second compared Egypt's national AI strategies of 2019 and 2025 and its charter for responsible AI with global principles.

**Standing role.** [The Information and Decision Support Centre is attached to the cabinet](<https://en.amwalalghad.com/egypts-pm-overhauls-decision-making-centre-with-high-profile-expert-board/>). Its new board includes Cairo University professors and officials of five ministries, communications and IT among them.
