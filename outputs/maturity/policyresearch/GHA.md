# Ghana

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [The African Center for Economic Transformation in Accra published a working paper on digitalisation in Ghana in 2024](<https://www.swp-berlin.org/assets/afrika/publications/MTA_working_paper/MTA_WP_2024_ACET_Digitalization_in_Ghana.pdf>). It makes recommendations to policymakers. [IMANI Africa's Centre for Science, Technology and Innovation Policy published an analysis of Ghana's internet exchange and peering policy in October 2025](<https://imaniafrica.org/2025/10/the-hidden-tax-on-ghanas-digital-future-a-path-to-faster-cheaper-internet/>). [The University of Ghana School of Law has run an Africa Centre of Excellence on Data Governance and Digital Rights since 2024](<https://law.ug.edu.gh/research/ongoing-projects/africa-Centre-of-excellence-on-data-governance-and-digital-rights>). [The Africa Digital Rights Hub, a Ghana-based think tank on digital rights research, received a USD 500,000 Hewlett Foundation grant in 2024](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=9PRIV1624.F_X.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>). [In June 2026 GIMPA launched a Centre for West African Studies for Digital Public Governance with China's UESTC](<https://gimpa.edu.gh/2026/06/02/chief-of-staff-calls-for-research-driven-digital-transformation-at-launch-of-gimpas-centre-for-digital-public-governance/>), as a platform for research and policy-relevant knowledge.
