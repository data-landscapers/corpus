# Seychelles

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [On 22 October 2026 the Education and Socio-economic Research Institute of the University of Seychelles held a one-day workshop on AI in education](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/32910/ai-in-education-takes-centre-stage-at-national-workshop>). The institute organised it with the Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development and the Seychelles Institute for Teacher Education. It met at the teacher education institute's auditorium. The workshop discussed the role of AI in shaping educational policy, teaching practice and learning.

**Feeding policy.** [Policymakers from the Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development attended the institute's workshop](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/32910/ai-in-education-takes-centre-stage-at-national-workshop>). School leaders and teachers from government schools also took part. The workshop aimed to encourage discussion and reflection among them.

**Standing role.** [The research institute is part of the University of Seychelles](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/32910/ai-in-education-takes-centre-stage-at-national-workshop>). It works on education and socio-economic questions.
