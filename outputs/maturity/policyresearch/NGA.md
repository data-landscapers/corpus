# Nigeria

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** In September 2026 [Paradigm Initiative published a policy brief on disability in the digital ecosystem](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/assessing-disability-policy-and-practice-in-nigerias-digital-ecosystem-a-policy-brief/>). It drew on desk research and a policy dialogue in Abuja on 25 May 2026. On 8 August 2026 [CITAD launched a project to track data-privacy violations during elections](<https://www.nationalrecord.com.ng/citad-launches-pawsom-monitor-to-track-digital-rights-breaches-ahead-of-2027-elections/>). [In September 2025 the Federal University of Technology, Minna, held a workshop on AI and data governance for consumer protection](<https://news.futminna.edu.ng/?p=1605>). It ran the workshop through its NCC professorial chair and launched a consumer protection handbook there.

**Feeding policy.** [In April 2024 the minister for communications, innovation and digital economy announced an AI Collective at the close of the national AI strategy workshop](<https://luminategroup.com/posts/news/partnerships-nigeria-ai-strategy>). [Lagos Business School, the Centre for Journalism, Innovation and Development and Data Science Nigeria run it, charged with supporting the strategy's development](<https://luminategroup.com/posts/news/partnerships-nigeria-ai-strategy>). It is housed in the National Center for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

**Standing role.** [The foundation Luminate funds the collective with $1.5 million over three years](<https://luminategroup.com/posts/news/partnerships-nigeria-ai-strategy>). Lagos Business School leads its research work.

***Also on file***. [Ripoti](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/nhrc-accountabilitylab-seek-coordinated-response-to-digital-rights-violations/>). [PAWSOM-Monitor](<https://www.nationalrecord.com.ng/citad-launches-pawsom-monitor-to-track-digital-rights-breaches-ahead-of-2027-elections/>).
