**Research published.** [Paradigm Initiative published a policy brief on disability policy and practice in Nigeria's digital ecosystem, informed by desk research and a 25 May 2026 policy dialogue it convened in Abuja with 34 participants](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/assessing-disability-policy-and-practice-in-nigerias-digital-ecosystem-a-policy-brief/>). [The Centre for Information Technology and Development (CITAD) launched the PAWSOM-Monitor project on 2026-08-08 to track data-privacy violations and platform restrictions during elections, piloted at the Osun governorship election on 15 August 2026](<https://www.nationalrecord.com.ng/citad-launches-pawsom-monitor-to-track-digital-rights-breaches-ahead-of-2027-elections/>). [CITAD's executive director said CITAD researched Facebook pages bearing the names of politicians about four years earlier, finding for example 126 pages for Ganduje (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nationalrecord.com.ng/citad-launches-pawsom-monitor-to-track-digital-rights-breaches-ahead-of-2027-elections/>).
