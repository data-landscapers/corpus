# Somalia

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** In October 2026 the Somalia Non-State Actors platform, SONSA, [published a report on artificial intelligence and digital rights in Somalia](<https://sonsaplatform.org/artificial-intelligence-and-digital-rights-in-somalia>), an analysis of government policies and private sector practices carried out by Capital University of Somalia. It finds the country's AI governance at an early stage, with data protection law giving general privacy safeguards but none specific to AI, and too little institutional capacity to regulate it.

**Feeding policy.** The [report proposes](<https://sonsaplatform.org/artificial-intelligence-and-digital-rights-in-somalia>) a rights-based approach for Somalia: AI-specific safeguards added to existing law, including algorithmic accountability and protection against deepfakes, clearer standards for private-sector data handling, and support for civil society monitoring.

**Standing role.** The [report names](<https://sonsaplatform.org/artificial-intelligence-and-digital-rights-in-somalia>) civil society organisations as critical actors in ethical AI governance and asks for sustained support for their monitoring, advocacy and public education.
