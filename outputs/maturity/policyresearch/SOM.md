# Somalia

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [SONSA published a 20-page research report on AI and digital rights in Somalia carried out by Capital University of Somalia, analysing government policies and private sector practices](<https://sonsaplatform.org/artificial-intelligence-and-digital-rights-in-somalia>). [SIDRA Institute, a Garowe-based think tank, published Policy Brief 26 on cybersecurity in Somalia by its researcher Abdi Omar Bile](<https://sidrainstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/SIDRA-Cyberescurity-Policy-brief-1.pdf>). [SONSA Platform, Mogadishu, published a policy brief evaluating Somalia's National ICT Policy and Strategy and recommending a 2025-2030 revision](<https://sonsaplatform.org/integrating-eu-digital-principles-into-the-next-national-ict-policy>). [The Heritage Institute for Policy Studies published in May 2023 'Digital ID: Prospects and Challenges for Somalia', an interview- and document-based study finding legal and infrastructure readiness inadequate (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://heritageinstitute.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Digital-ID-Report-English-1.pdf>).
