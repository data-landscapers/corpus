# Kenya

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [KICTANet, the Kenya ICT Action Network, launched its report 'Many Microphones, Little Accountability' on 2026-09-09, based on three months of platform monitoring, a national survey and interviews, on how alternative media shape political news ahead of the 2027 election](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kenya-alternative-media-misinformation-report-2027-election/>). [KICTANet, with Meedan, ran a year-long study of over a thousand Swahili-language content items, finding over half involved gendered targeting and about one in eight involved doxxing, per the KICTANet-sourced article](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kensafespace-project-impact-digital-rights-kenya/>). [KICTANet's Annual Report 2024 lists policy briefs and reports among its publications and describes the network as an institution dedicated to ICT policy, research, advocacy and capacity building](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/?mdocs-file=52063>).

**Feeding policy.** [KICTANet submitted a memorandum to the Senate of Kenya on 5 May 2026 as a public comment on the Artificial Intelligence Bill, 2026, proposing a statutory AI Authority, a National Council and a ring-fenced fund](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-BILL-2026-Memorandum.docx.pdf>).
