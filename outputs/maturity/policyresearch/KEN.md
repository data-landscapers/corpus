# Kenya

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [KICTANet launched a report on how alternative media shape political news on 9 September 2026](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kenya-alternative-media-misinformation-report-2027-election/>). It drew on three months of platform monitoring, a national survey and interviews. [With Meedan, KICTANet studied over a thousand Swahili-language content items over a year](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kensafespace-project-impact-digital-rights-kenya/>). Over half involved gendered targeting. [Its Annual Report 2024 lists policy briefs and reports among its publications](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/?mdocs-file=52063>). [Strathmore University's CIPIT has done foundational research on the African AI ecosystem, and from 2024 holds an IDRC grant for AI policy research](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=CAN.KEN.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>). [CIPIT at Strathmore University held the first forum of its East African AI Policy Hub in Kigali in May 2026](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/east-african-ai-policy-hub/>).

**Feeding policy.** [KICTANet sent the Senate a memorandum on the Artificial Intelligence Bill, 2026, on 5 May 2026](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-BILL-2026-Memorandum.docx.pdf>). It proposed a statutory AI Authority, a National Council and a ring-fenced fund. [CIPIT says it contributed to the national AI strategy and worked with the Judiciary on an AI framework](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/east-african-ai-policy-hub/>).
