## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [The AI Living Lab in Dakar was doing scientific work on responsible AI in 2024](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=CAN.SEN.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>). IDRC money reached it through Université numérique Cheikh Hamidou Kane, a Senegalese university. [In 2023 IPAR, a Senegalese think tank, said its DUA project had produced a policy paper proposing AI regulation](<https://grain-africa.org/en/ipar-takes-part-in-two-working-groups-on-the-development-of-the-national-strategy-on-artificial-intelligence/>). The same project produced a mapping report on AI governance. [In March 2024 the Max Planck Research Partner Group at Université numérique Cheikh Hamidou Kane published a report on Senegal's rules for data access and sharing](<https://preprodsite.unchk.sn/le-rapport-de-la-conference-atelier-2022-du-max-planck-research-partner-group-de-lun-chk-disponible/>), with a focus on agriculture and financial services. The group researches the regulation of the digital economy.

**Feeding policy.** [IPAR joined the ministry's workshops on the national AI strategy on 21-22 June 2023](<https://grain-africa.org/en/ipar-takes-part-in-two-working-groups-on-the-development-of-the-national-strategy-on-artificial-intelligence/>). It sat in two working groups and helped draft the strategy's summary document. No later contribution by a local institution to a digital policy, law or strategy is on record.
