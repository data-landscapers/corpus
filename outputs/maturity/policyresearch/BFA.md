# Burkina Faso

## Digital policy research

**Research published.** [In April 2025 CITADEL, at the Université Virtuelle du Burkina Faso, began a second phase of research and action](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article137461>). [IDRC funds the phase with about CAD 1 million](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article137461>). [On 2 November 2026 the Africa Multiple centre of Joseph Ki-Zerbo University launched the Digit OACS research project](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150452=>). [The four-year project studies the digital transition in universities](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150452=>). [German cooperation funds it with about 200 million FCFA](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150452=>). [In July 2026 GDN listed CIFOEB, CARDES and CERTIS in research consortia on digital transformation in West Africa and the Sahel](<https://gdn.int/digitalisation/programmes/digital-transformation-in-west-africa-and-the-sahel/>).
