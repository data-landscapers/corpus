# Botswana

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Botswana's law is [the Access to Information Act, Act 16 of 2024, dated 26 July 2024](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act>), which creates a general right of access to information held by public authorities and starts on a date to be set by notice. [The President's State of the Nation Address in November 2025 counted the Act among the laws Parliament had passed](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89031>). The date it starts is not recorded.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [the Access to Information Act of 2024](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Botswana.

***Also on file***. [Information and Data Protection Commission](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act>).

***Not found***. No source found names rules made under the law or says its oversight body is set up.
