# Botswana

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Botswana's law is [the Access to Information Act, Act 16 of 2024, dated 26 July 2024](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act>). It creates a general right of access to information held by public authorities. It starts on a date to be set by notice. [The President's State of the Nation Address in November 2025 counted the Act among the laws Parliament had passed](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/89031>). The date it starts is not recorded.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest fact found is the law itself, [the Access to Information Act of 2024](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/acts-on-notice/2024/access-to-information-act>). A decree, regulation or order made under it would put it to work. So would an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. A decision, order or penalty on a request for information would show it applied. So would figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Botswana.
