# Tunisia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Tunisia's law is [Organic Law no. 22 of 2016 on the right of access to information, promulgated on 24 March 2016](<https://inai.tn/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/loi-22-inai.pdf>). It gives every person a right to ask public bodies for information and took effect one year after publication.

**Law put to work and applied.** The law's oversight body is set up and has handled appeals, but has since stalled. [The government's 2024 return to UNESCO says a national information commission oversees the law, and reports 7,347 appeals received in 2023 and 6,375 requests received in 2021](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/11/Tunisia_SDG16102_2024.pdf>). [The government told a deputy, as reported on 30 November 2025, that the access to information body, nine members elected by Parliament for six years, has been working without its president and deputy, which has blocked legally valid meetings](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/01/donnees-personnelles-et-acces-a-linformation-lexecutif-engage-une-mise-a-jour-urgente-du-cadre-juridique/>).
