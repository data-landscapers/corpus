# Benin

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Benin's access provisions sit in Loi n° 2015-07, the Code de l'information et de la communication of 2015. The World Bank's [December 2022 open data readiness assessment](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>) calls them extremely limited. It finds that articles 84 and 90 restrict access rights. It finds no request procedure, no designated contact point and no annual report on requests. The same assessment says the BAI presidential reform office was drafting a new access to information law, with no adoption timetable.

**Law put to work and applied.** [Benin's government told UNESCO, in a survey return published in October 2024](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/10/Benin_SDG16102_2023.pdf>), that three bodies oversee the law. They are the national human rights commission, a communications regulator and a ministry or agency. It said they published an annual report, trained officials and kept statistics in 2022. They received 12 appeals that year. The World Bank's [December 2022 assessment](<https://opendatatoolkit.worldbank.org/content/dam/sites/data/odk/documents/odra-benin.pdf>) says the Haute Autorité de l'Audiovisuel et de la Communication (HAAC) does not appear to have undertaken any initiative to promote access to information. Civil society organisations found the right little known and little used. Their test letters to administrations were generally ignored.
