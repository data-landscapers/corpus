# Kenya

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Kenya's law is the Access to Information Act, 2016. [It gives effect to Article 35 of the Constitution](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-27-state-vows-proactive-disclosure-as-kenya-marks-access-to-information-day>), and [the press describes it as the guarantee of the right to information](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/new-bill-sharing-state-secrets-on-whatsapp-emails-could-land-you-in-jail-5516338>). It has stood since 2016 with no repeal recorded.

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Access to Information Regulations of 2023, made under the Act, define proactive disclosure and set what public bodies must publish](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-27-state-vows-proactive-disclosure-as-kenya-marks-access-to-information-day>). [A forum in September 2026 assessed how public institutions carry the Act out](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-27-state-vows-proactive-disclosure-as-kenya-marks-access-to-information-day>). The Act [gives oversight and enforcement to the Commission on Administrative Justice](<https://www.rti-rating.org/wp-content/uploads/Kenya.pdf>). In August 2026 the commission [said it would engage 54 public institutions on compliance](<https://kahawatungu.com/ombudsman-raises-alarm-over-delays-in-resolving-public-complaints/>). It said many do not routinely publish what the Act requires. A separate bill of 2026 on classified information is noted and does not change the Act's standing.
