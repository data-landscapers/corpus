# South Sudan

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** South Sudan's access to information law is not itself named in what is recorded; its commission is. [The Minister of Information's paper of 6 September 2023 lists the Information Commission among six bodies under the ministry](<https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/MinistryofInformation-Hon.MichaelMakuei.pdf>), and a commission set up shows a law in force behind it.

**Law put to work and applied.** The oversight body exists and is funded through the budget. [The budget performance report for 2024/25 lists the Access for Information Commission among the agencies with an allocation](<https://mofp.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2024_25_Execution-Report_v3.pdf>). [On 4 September 2025 the Ministry of Information said it had estimated a budget for the Commission's operations and described it as facing operating challenges](<https://mictps.gov.ss/moictps-settles-usaf-information-commission-operation-challenges/>). A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.
