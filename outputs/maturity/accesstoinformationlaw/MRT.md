# Mauritania

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mauritania has a bill and no law. [Reporters Without Borders reported on 23 June 2026 that a bill drafted by the government on access to and use of public information had been under discussion since April 2026](<https://rsf.org/fr/mauritanie-rsf-salue-le-projet-de-loi-sur-l-acces-et-l-utilisation-de-l-information-publique>) and had yet to go through parliament. No law is in force.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest fact found is the draft, [the government bill under discussion since April 2026](<https://rsf.org/fr/mauritanie-rsf-salue-le-projet-de-loi-sur-l-acces-et-l-utilisation-de-l-information-publique>). A bill makes no rules and sets up no oversight body until it is passed, promulgated and in force. After that, a decree or regulation made under it, or an information commission or commissioner appointed, would put it to work. A decision or penalty on a request for information, or published figures of requests, would show it applied.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says an access to information law is in force.
