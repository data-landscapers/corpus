# Morocco

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Morocco's law is [Law no. 31-13 on the right of access to information, promulgated on 22 February 2018 and published on 3 May 2018](<https://www.oc.gov.ma/sites/default/files/loi%2031-13/1.%20BO%20Loi%2031.13%20en%20francais.pdf>), in force one year after publication. [The Ministry of Justice says requests and complaints are filed and handled under it](<https://justice.gov.ma/2026/03/31/%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The law's oversight body is set up. [The Commission on the Right of Access to Information, led by its president, signed a partnership agreement with the Minister of Justice on 31 March 2026](<https://justice.gov.ma/2026/03/31/%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7/>), and it runs the national portal through which requests are made. A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.
