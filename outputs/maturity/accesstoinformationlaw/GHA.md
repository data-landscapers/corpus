# Ghana

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Ghana's law is the Right to Information Act, 2019 (Act 989). [It puts the constitutional right to information to work and sets the procedure for asking public institutions for what they hold](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/09/rti-act-public-institutions-urged-to-provide-accurate-timely-information-to-citizens/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Right to Information Commission, with a board and chairman, oversees and enforces the Act](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/09/rti-act-public-institutions-urged-to-provide-accurate-timely-information-to-citizens/>). [The 2026 budget votes it GHS 20,413,271](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-statements/Appropriation%20%28%20No.%202%29%20Act%2C%202025%20Act%201163_0.pdf>). [On 5 August 2026 the Commission imposed penalties of GHS 20,000 each on 254 public institutions](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/upsa-37-military-hospital-among-254-public-institutions-fined-by-rti-commission-over-report-delays.html>). The institutions had not filed their 2025 annual reports under the Act. A newspaper count puts the practice lower: [21 institutions failed to release information in 24 requests made between January 2025 and July 2026](<https://thefourthestategh.com/2026/09/transparency-in-words-secrecy-in-practice-how-mdas-are-betraying-govts-transparency-pledge>). Those penalties are for missed reports. [On 5 February 2026 the Commission fined four public institutions GH₵220,000 in total after hearings and determinations](<https://rtic.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RTIC-IMPOSES-FINES-AND-DIRECTIVE.pdf>). [Its technical advisor reports 819 applications for information in 2025 and 119 applications for review, of which the Commission had resolved 57 by the end of that year](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/features/opinion/ghana-news-right-to-information-commission-at-six.html>).
