# Somalia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Somalia has a bill and no law. [The Council of Ministers approved the Official Information Bill in March 2024 and sent it to the House of the People](<https://defenddefenders.org/federal-republic-of-somalia-joint-submission-to-the-un-universal-periodic-review-52nd-session-of-the-upr-working-group/>). [A Ministry of Information official said on 28 September 2025 that the Cabinet had passed the access to information law and that it awaited presentation to the Lower House](<https://soma.org.so/soma-commemorates-international-day-for-universal-access-to-information/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the draft, [the bill awaiting the Lower House](<https://soma.org.so/soma-commemorates-international-day-for-universal-access-to-information/>). A bill makes no rules and sets up no oversight body until it is passed, promulgated and in force. Once it is, what would put it to work is a decree or regulation made under it, or an information commission or commissioner appointed, and what would show it applied is a decision or penalty on a request for information, or published figures of requests.
