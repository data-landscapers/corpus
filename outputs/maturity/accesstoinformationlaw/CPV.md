# Cape Verde

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Cabo Verde's law is [Law no. 10/X/2022 on access to and reuse of administrative documents, approved on 8 April 2022, promulgated on 9 May 2022 and published on 16 May 2022](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/4230>). It gives everyone a right to consult and copy administrative documents and to be told what they contain.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [Law no. 10/X/2022](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/4230>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Cape Verde.

***Also on file***. [Open Data Policy and Open Data Portal (commitment CV0004)](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/members/cabo-verde/commitments/CV0004/>).

***Not found***. No source found names rules made under the law or says its oversight body is set up.
