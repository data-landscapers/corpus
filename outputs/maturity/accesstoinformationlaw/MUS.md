# Mauritius

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mauritius has no access to information law. [A Defi Media blog of 14 February 2026 says successive political parties have promised Freedom of Information and failed to deliver](<https://defimedia.info/blog-enough-promises-rama-valayden-urges-action-freedom-information-unveils-proposed-constitutional>). It says Senior Counsel Rama Valayden urges the government to introduce it. His 2022 draft is a constitutional amendment to section 12. [On 28 October 2025 the Prime Minister told parliament that exploratory drafting of a bill had begun](<https://lexpress.mu/s/le-freedom-of-information-bil-en-preparation-551179>). He said an older bill was under review. [In November 2026 Defimedia reported that the Act promised in the 2025-2029 programme had not been delivered](<https://defimedia.info/programme-2025-2029-ces-grandes-promesses-pas-encore-tenues>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest fact found is the same [February 2026 blog](<https://defimedia.info/blog-enough-promises-rama-valayden-urges-action-freedom-information-unveils-proposed-constitutional>). It describes a promise not yet delivered by any of the successive political parties. The 2022 draft it mentions amends section 12 of the constitution. Nothing found shows a rule made, an oversight body set up or a request decided under an access law.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No access to information bill has been found before cabinet or parliament.
