# Zimbabwe

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Zimbabwe's law is [the Freedom of Information Act, 2020, published and commenced on 1 July 2020](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2020/1/eng@2020-07-01>). It gives a right to ask public bodies for information and repealed the Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act. [MISA Zimbabwe's chairperson says Zimbabweans are increasingly using the Act to claim their right to information](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2025/04/26/misa-zimbabwe-2024-annual-report-now-available/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** Rules have been made under the Act and its oversight body issues orders. [A joint submission to the United Nations of July 2026 says the Act was put to work through the Freedom of Information (General) Regulations, 2021](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>), and [recommends that the Act be fully carried out](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>). [MISA Zimbabwe's 2024 annual report says the Zimbabwe Media Commission has issued release orders to public institutions that must provide information requested under the Act](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2025/04/26/misa-zimbabwe-2024-annual-report-now-available/>).

***Also on file***. [Freedom of Information (General) Regulations 2021, SI 229 of 2021](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>); [Cyber and Data Protection Act](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>).
