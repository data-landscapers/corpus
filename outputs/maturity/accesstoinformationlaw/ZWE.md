# Zimbabwe

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Zimbabwe's law is [the Freedom of Information Act, 2020, published and commenced on 1 July 2020](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2020/1/eng@2020-07-01>). It gives a right to ask public bodies for information and repealed the Access to Information and Protection of Privacy Act.

**Law put to work and applied.** Rules have been made under the Act. [A joint submission to the United Nations of July 2026 says the Act was put to work through the Freedom of Information (General) Regulations, 2021](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>), and [recommends that the Act be fully carried out](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>). The regulations are older than three years. A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Freedom of Information (General) Regulations 2021, SI 229 of 2021](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>); [Cyber and Data Protection Act](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Zimbabwe_UPR_Review.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held dates a decision, order or penalty on a request, published figures of requests, or a rule made under the law, in the last three years.
