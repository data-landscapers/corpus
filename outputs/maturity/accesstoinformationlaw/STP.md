# Sao Tome and Principe

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Civil society organisations (CIP-STP, FONG-STP, PDHEG and Wêbeto) submitted a [draft Lei de Acesso aos Documentos e Informações Administrativas](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>) to the President of the National Assembly in 2018. It was written by a national consultant under the Pro PALOP project. In November 2021 those organisations were still calling for the draft to be adopted, and [STP Digital reported on 1 December 2021](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>) that it was not yet adopted. [UNESCO's contribution to the 51st Universal Periodic Review, of 2026, states that there is no legislation on freedom of information and recommends introducing an access to information law](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-02/UNESCO_org_UPR51_STP_E_Main.pdf>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [STP Digital report](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>), which concerns a draft only: the calls made during the Quinzena da Cidadania, 15 to 30 November 2021. Nothing found shows a rule made, an oversight body set up or a request decided under an access law.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing found says the draft was adopted since 2021, or that any access law is in force or applied.
