# Sao Tome and Principe

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Civil society organisations (CIP-STP, FONG-STP, PDHEG and Wêbeto) submitted a [draft Lei de Acesso aos Documentos e Informações Administrativas](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>) to the President of the National Assembly in 2018. It was written by a national consultant under the Pro PALOP project, and the 2018 submission is reported by STP Digital. In November 2021 those organisations were still calling for the draft to be adopted during the Quinzena da Cidadania, and [STP Digital reported on 1 December 2021](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>) that it was not yet adopted.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [STP Digital report](<https://stpdigital.net/sociedade/lei-de-acesso-a-documentos-e-informacoes-administrativas>), which concerns a draft only: the calls made during the Quinzena da Cidadania, 15 to 30 November 2021. Nothing held shows a rule made, an oversight body set up or a request decided under an access law.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Nothing held says the draft was adopted since 2021, or that any access law is in force or applied.
