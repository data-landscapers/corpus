# Ethiopia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** [Proclamation No. 590/2008](<https://justice.gov.et/en/law/a-proclamation-to-provide-for-freedom-of-the-mass-media-and-access-to-information/>) on Freedom of the Mass Media and Access to Information, dated 4 December 2008 and proclaimed under Article 55(1) of the Constitution, is published by the Ministry of Justice and affirms in its preamble individuals' right to access information held by public bodies and a duty on public officials to facilitate it. A [June 2023 study](<https://www.cardeth.org/sites/default/files/New%20Media%20Legislations%20and%20their%20Implementations%20in%20Ethiopia.pdf>) lists it among Ethiopia's laws, and a draft Access to Information Proclamation prepared by a media law working group had not been passed as of March 2023.

**Law put to work and applied.** The same [study](<https://www.cardeth.org/sites/default/files/New%20Media%20Legislations%20and%20their%20Implementations%20in%20Ethiopia.pdf>) says the Ethiopian Institution of the Ombudsman was designated the organ responsible for implementing freedom of information. The study is dated 1 June 2023. It reports studies finding the Ombudsman ineffective for lack of resources to evaluate public bodies, and it gives no date for that finding.

***Not found***. Nothing found names rules made under the proclamation, says its oversight body is set up as such, or shows requests or decisions under it.
