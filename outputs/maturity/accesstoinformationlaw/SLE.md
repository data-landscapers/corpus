# Sierra Leone

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone's law is [the Right to Access Information Act, 2013, signed by the President on 31 October 2013](<https://raic.gov.sl/images/downloads/Right-access-information-act-2013.pdf>). A replacement is being written: [a Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill was validated on 7 November 2025](<https://moice.gov.sl/moice-concludes-final-national-validation-of-the-data-protection-and-right-to-access-information-bill-2025/>), and [Cabinet approved repealing the 2013 Act and enacting the new one, announced on 21 April 2026](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is long set up and has fined public bodies. [The Right to Access Information Commission presented its sixth annual report to the Minister of Information and Civic Education on 17 November 2025](<https://moice.gov.sl/raic-presents-6th-annual-report-to-minister-chernor-bah-highlighting-significant-progress-in-access-to-information/>). [In September 2024 it fined the Corporate Affairs Commission NLe80,000 for missing the 15-working-day deadline on a request of 18 June 2024, acting under the Act and the Right to Access Information Regulations 2022](<https://www.raic.gov.sl/index.php/all-categories-list/press-release/raic-fines-corporate-affairs-commission-nle80-000-for-non-compliance>). [On 13 October 2025 it ordered Freetown City Council to release requested documents within seven days and fined it Le 70,000](<https://www.raic.gov.sl/index.php/all-categories-list/news/raic-fines-freetown-city-council-le-70-000-for-failing-to-release-information>).
