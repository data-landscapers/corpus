# Sierra Leone

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone's law is [the Right to Access Information Act, 2013](<https://raic.gov.sl/images/downloads/Right-access-information-act-2013.pdf>), signed by the President on 31 October 2013. A replacement is under way. [A Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill was validated on 7 November 2025](<https://moice.gov.sl/moice-concludes-final-national-validation-of-the-data-protection-and-right-to-access-information-bill-2025/>). [Cabinet approved repealing the 2013 Act for the new law, announced on 21 April 2026](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>). [The Information Minister tabled the 2026 Bill in Parliament, where it passed first reading](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/information-tables-data-protection-bills/>), as reported on 6 November 2026.

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is long set up and has fined public bodies. [The Right to Access Information Commission gave its sixth annual report to the Minister](<https://moice.gov.sl/raic-presents-6th-annual-report-to-minister-chernor-bah-highlighting-significant-progress-in-access-to-information/>) on 17 November 2025. [In September 2024 it fined the Corporate Affairs Commission NLe80,000](<https://www.raic.gov.sl/index.php/all-categories-list/press-release/raic-fines-corporate-affairs-commission-nle80-000-for-non-compliance>) for missing the 15-working-day deadline on a request. It acted under the Act and [the Right to Access Information Regulations 2022](<https://www.raic.gov.sl/index.php/all-categories-list/press-release/raic-fines-corporate-affairs-commission-nle80-000-for-non-compliance>). [On 13 October 2025 it fined Freetown City Council Le 70,000](<https://www.raic.gov.sl/index.php/all-categories-list/news/raic-fines-freetown-city-council-le-70-000-for-failing-to-release-information>) and ordered it to release documents within seven days.
