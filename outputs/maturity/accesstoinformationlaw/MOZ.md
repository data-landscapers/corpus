# Mozambique

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mozambique's law is [Law no. 34/2014 of 31 December 2014, the Right to Information Law](<https://cscs.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lei-de-Direito-a-Informacao.pdf>), passed by the Assembly of the Republic to set how the right to information is exercised. It applies to the bodies of the State and to the public administration.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [Law no. 34/2014](<https://cscs.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lei-de-Direito-a-Informacao.pdf>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Mozambique.
