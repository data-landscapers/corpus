# Mozambique

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mozambique's law is [Law no. 34/2014 of 31 December 2014, the Right to Information Law](<https://cscs.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lei-de-Direito-a-Informacao.pdf>). The Assembly of the Republic passed it to set how the right to information is exercised. It applies to the bodies of the State and to the public administration. [The Ombudsman's annual report to the Assembly, reported in November 2025, says the law is being carried out](<https://cartamz.com/destaque/47502/provedor-de-justica-garante-haver-melhorias-na-implementacao-da-lei-do-direito-a-informacao/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Ombudsman's annual report, presented to the Assembly of the Republic in November 2025, says public bodies received 60,433 requests for information in 2024](<https://cartamz.com/destaque/47502/provedor-de-justica-garante-haver-melhorias-na-implementacao-da-lei-do-direito-a-informacao/>). Of these, 57,998 were granted, 1,589 refused and 846 still pending, against 23,808 requests in 2023. The report says the national archives body compiles the monitoring report from public bodies' own reports on the law. By the deadline of 30 December 2024, only Manica and Maputo provinces had sent theirs. The Ombudsman says a lack of funds and of provincial offices held back its monitoring and technical help to public bodies.
