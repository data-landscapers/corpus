# Mozambique

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mozambique's law is [Law no. 34/2014 of 31 December 2014, the Right to Information Law](<https://cscs.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lei-de-Direito-a-Informacao.pdf>), passed by the Assembly of the Republic to set how the right to information is exercised. It applies to the bodies of the State and to the public administration. [The Ombudsman's annual report to the Assembly, reported in November 2025, says the law is being carried out](<https://cartamz.com/destaque/47502/provedor-de-justica-garante-haver-melhorias-na-implementacao-da-lei-do-direito-a-informacao/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The law is applied and its use is counted. [The Ombudsman's annual report says public bodies received 60,433 requests for information in 2024, of which 57,998 were granted, 1,589 refused and 846 still pending](<https://cartamz.com/destaque/47502/provedor-de-justica-garante-haver-melhorias-na-implementacao-da-lei-do-direito-a-informacao/>), so about 95 per cent of requests that year were granted. The Ombudsman, who monitors the law, reports these figures to the Assembly of the Republic in an annual report.
