# Liberia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Liberia's law is [the Freedom of Information Act of 2010](<https://gnnliberia.com/iic-cental-step-up-transparency-anti-corruption-drive/>). [The Press Union of Liberia cited it in August 2026 as law a government agency's staff confidentiality agreement must fit](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/csa-launches-monthly-e-newsletter-to-boost-transparency-public-engagement/article_3f277f5c-da8f-43c6-91b4-405f0c91c464.html>). The Act gives a right to ask public bodies for information and has stood since 2010.

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is set up. [The Independent Information Commission ran a compliance campaign across public bodies from 11 August to 2 September 2026](<https://gnnliberia.com/iic-cental-step-up-transparency-anti-corruption-drive/>). [In October 2026 it ordered the Environmental Protection Agency to give The DayLight newspaper the records it had requested](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202610130246.html>). It fined the agency L$9,000. [The Press Union said in October 2026 that the Commission had publicly opposed the Civil Service Agency's new confidentiality policy](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/24/pul-condemns-governments-assault-on-freedom-of-information-and-transparency-calls-for-immediate-withdrawal-of-confidentiality-policy-undermining-the-foi-act/>).
