# Eritrea

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Eritrea has no access to information law and no draft of one. [UNESCO's contribution to the country's Universal Periodic Review, published in January 2026, states that no freedom of information law has been adopted and recommends that the government introduce one](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>). The contribution names no bill in preparation.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the statement that there is no law, [UNESCO's contribution of January 2026](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2026-01/UNESCO_org_UPR46_ERI_E_Main.pdf>). With no law and no draft there are no rules, no information commission or commissioner and no decisions on requests to record. A bill published, put out for consultation or sent to cabinet or parliament would be the first step, and a law in force the second.
