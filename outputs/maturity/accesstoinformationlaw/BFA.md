# Burkina Faso

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Burkina Faso's law is [Law no. 051-2015/CNT on the right of access to public information and administrative documents, which the government named to UNESCO as its legal guarantee for 2023](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/11/BurkinaFaso_SDG16102_2024.pdf>). [The Centre national de presse Norbert Zongo's digital rights report, published in February 2025, records no change to the law and says it has fallen into oblivion](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). The report names no repeal and no replacement, so the law stands.

**Law put to work and applied.** Existing bodies have taken up the law's oversight. [The government told UNESCO that the national data protection commission, the national human rights commission, a ministry and the Conseil Supérieur de la Communication oversee it, and that in 2023 they published an annual report, gave guidance and training to officials, raised awareness and kept statistics on requests and appeals](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/11/BurkinaFaso_SDG16102_2024.pdf>). A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by those bodies, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.

***Also on file***. [loi organique N°041-2023/ALT (Conseil supérieur de la communication)](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>).
