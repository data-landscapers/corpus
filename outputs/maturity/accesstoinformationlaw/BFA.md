# Burkina Faso

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Burkina Faso's law is [Law no. 051-2015/CNT on the right of access to public information and administrative documents](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/11/BurkinaFaso_SDG16102_2024.pdf>). The government named it to UNESCO as its legal guarantee for 2023. [The Centre national de presse Norbert Zongo published a digital rights report in February 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). It records no change to the law and says the law has fallen into oblivion. The report names no repeal and no replacement, so the law stands.

**Law put to work and applied.** Existing bodies have taken up the law's oversight. [The government told UNESCO that the national data protection commission, the national human rights commission, a ministry and the Conseil Supérieur de la Communication oversee it](<https://www.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2024/11/BurkinaFaso_SDG16102_2024.pdf>). It said that in 2023 they published an annual report, gave guidance and training to officials, raised awareness and kept statistics on requests and appeals. No decision, order or penalty on a request for information in the last three years is recorded. No figures of requests published by those bodies in that period are recorded.
