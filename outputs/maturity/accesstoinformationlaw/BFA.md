# Burkina Faso

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Burkina Faso has a law on access to public information and administrative documents. [The Centre national de presse Norbert Zongo's digital rights report, published in February 2025, records no change to the law and says it has fallen into oblivion](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). The report names no repeal and no replacement, so the law stands.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [the law the 2025 report describes as forgotten](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Burkina Faso.

***Noted, not assessed***. [loi organique N°041-2023/ALT (Conseil supérieur de la communication)](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>).

***Not held***. No source held names rules made under the law or says its oversight body is set up.
