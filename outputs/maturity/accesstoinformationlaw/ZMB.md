# Zambia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Zambia's law is [the Access to Information Act, 2023 (Act No. 24 of 2023)](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%2024%20of%202023%2C%20The%20Access%20to%20Information%20Act%2C%202023.pdf>). It was assented to on 22 December 2023. It gives citizens and residence permit holders a right to information held by public and private bodies. [The Human Rights Commission says it came into force on 25 June 2024](<https://hrc.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026.01.08-HRC-Statement-on-ATI.pdf>), under Statutory Instrument No. 35 of 2024.

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Minister of Information and Media made the Access to Information (General) Regulations, 2025, setting request forms, fees and procedures, signed on 20 August 2025](<https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/si/2025/56/eng@2025-08-29/source>). [The Human Rights Commission, which the Act names as its oversight body, issued guidelines under it on 5 December 2025](<https://hrc.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Access-To-Information-Guidelines_2025.pdf>). [On 23 June 2026 the Commission said it had written to the Ministry of Health directing it to comply with the law on requests from MakanDay](<https://makanday.org/access-denied-two-years-after-zambias-ati-law-citizens-still-struggle-for-public-information/>). The Ministry had not replied by 23 July 2026. [Court rules under the Act were still being finalised in July 2026](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/25/were-in-the-final-stage-of-operationalising-the-ati-act-govt/>).
