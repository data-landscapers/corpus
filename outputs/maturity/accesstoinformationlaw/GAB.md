# Gabon

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Gabon has no access to information law and no draft of one. [UNESCO's contribution to the country's Universal Periodic Review, published in December 2025, states that Gabon has no access to information law and encourages it to adopt one](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/UNESCO_org_UPR42_GAB_F_Main.pdf>). The contribution names no bill in preparation, in cabinet or before parliament.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the statement that there is no law, [UNESCO's contribution of December 2025](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/UNESCO_org_UPR42_GAB_F_Main.pdf>). With no law and no draft there are no rules, no information commission or commissioner and no decisions on requests to record. A bill published, put out for consultation or sent to cabinet or parliament would be the first step, and a law in force the second.
