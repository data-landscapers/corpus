# South Africa

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** South Africa's law is [the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000](<https://www.mondaq.com/southafrica/contracts-and-commercial-law/1797238/landmark-ruling-for-private-bodies-under-paia-information-regulators-enforcement-notice-against-mining-house>), the law under which the Information Regulator acts on complaints about refused requests. [It was amended on 17 April 2025](<https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/16116767.pdf>), and [a public service data governance directive of 2 April 2025 says it facilitates implementation of the Act](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/dpsa2g/documents/km/2025/Determination%20and%20Directive%20on%20the%20Implementation%20of%20Data%20Governance%20in%20the%20Public%20Service.pdf>). [A parliamentary committee approved the publication of proposed amended court rules under it in September 2026](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-justice-committee-approves-publication-proposed-amended-paia-court-rules-enhance-transparency-and-access-justice>), which would repeal the existing rules.

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Information Regulator, established on 1 December 2016, monitors and enforces the Act alongside the data protection law](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-unpacks-regulatory-complexities-posed-by-ai/KA3WwMdzPryvrydZ>), and [Pansy Tlakula chairs it](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/inforeg-concerned-by-alarming-rate-of-data-breaches/rxP3jqBE2ZLMA2ye>). [On 22 May 2026 it issued an enforcement notice under the Act directing the mining company Sibanye-Stillwater to give a complainant access to compliance reports](<https://www.mondaq.com/southafrica/contracts-and-commercial-law/1797238/landmark-ruling-for-private-bodies-under-paia-information-regulators-enforcement-notice-against-mining-house>), after a complaint lodged in September 2023. [The Regulator briefed the press on its enforcement of the Act on 31 August 2026](<https://werksmans.com/the-regulator-is-watching-new-enforcement-signals-for-popia-and-paia-compliance/>).
