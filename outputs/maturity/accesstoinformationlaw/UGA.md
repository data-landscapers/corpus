# Uganda

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Uganda's law is [the Access to Information Act, 2005, assented to on 7 July 2005](<https://www.judiciary.go.ug/files/downloads/access%20to%20informatioinformation%20Act2005.pdf>). It gives every citizen a right of access to the records of public bodies. [On 4 December 2025 the Constitutional Court struck down its exemption for court records before a case ends](<https://ulii.org/en/judgments/UGCC/2025/12/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [The Africa Freedom of Information Centre says the Act's Regulations of 2011](<https://www.africafoicentre.org/access-to-information-in-uganda-prospects-and-hurdles/>) set out what citizens can request and the exemptions. The courts decide disputed requests. [On 7 August 2026 the High Court allowed the national examinations board's appeal against a magistrate's order](<https://www.mmaks.co.ug/articles/2026/08/21/examination-scripts-are-personal-data-high-court-sets-aside-order-compelling>). That order, made on a complaint under the Act, had compelled release of a candidate's examination script. [The judgment applies the Act's section 4 right of access](<https://www.mmaks.co.ug/articles/2026/08/21/examination-scripts-are-personal-data-high-court-sets-aside-order-compelling>). The Act sets up no information commission.

***Also on file***. [Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap 97](<https://www.mmaks.co.ug/articles/2026/08/21/examination-scripts-are-personal-data-high-court-sets-aside-order-compelling>).

***Not found***. No source found names an information commission for the Act or gives published figures of requests.
