# Seychelles

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Seychelles' law is [the Access to Information Act, enacted on 20 July 2018](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>). [The Information Commission was created under it](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa>), and the Commission proposed amendments to the Act in a paper of November 2025.

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is set up. [The Information Commission was created under the Act and was later named the data protection authority as well](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa>). [Its paper of November 2025 says it has issued orders and decisions under the Act since 2019 and that information holders have ignored them, the Act giving no clear way to enforce them](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>). No decision is dated, and no figures of requests are recorded. A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.

***Noted, not assessed***.
