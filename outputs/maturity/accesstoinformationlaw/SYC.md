# Seychelles

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Seychelles' law is [the Access to Information Act, enacted on 20 July 2018](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>). [The Information Commission was created under it](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa>), and the Commission proposed amendments to the Act in a paper of November 2025.

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is set up and rules on appeals. [The Information Commission was created under the Act and was later named the data protection authority as well](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-seychelles-dpa>). [It found the Planning Authority non-compliant with sections 11 and 17 of the Act and ordered full access to the requested files within five days, in a notice of May 2024](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/17.-Decision-Notice-Ms.-Karen-Domingue-v.-Seychelles-Planning-Authority.pdf>), and [found the Agriculture Department in breach of sections 11(1), 12 and 21, closing that case on 15 November 2024](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/20.-Decision-Notice-Ms.-Rassool-v.-Agriculture-Department-002-002.pdf>). [Its paper of November 2025 says information holders have ignored its orders, the Act giving no clear way to enforce them](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>).
