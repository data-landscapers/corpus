# Gambia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** The Gambia's law is the Access to Information Act, 2021, cited as [Act No. 10 of 2021 in the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2025](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>). [The Gambia Press Union said in August 2026 that it had been in force for more than a year](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>), and [the Immigration Department reaffirmed its commitment to it in October 2026](<https://gambiana.com/gid-denies-losing-access-to-national-id-card-data/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is set up. [The Information Commission, with a deputy chairperson, was implementing the Act in September 2026](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/information-commission-trains-csos-on-access-to-information-data-protection>). [The Press Union sits on technical committees with it](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>), [a United Nations agency tendered in August 2026 for a communication strategy for the Commission](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>), and [the 2025 data protection Act defines the Commission as established under the Access to Information Act](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>).
