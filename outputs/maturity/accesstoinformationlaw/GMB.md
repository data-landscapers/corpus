# Gambia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** The Gambia's law is the Access to Information Act, 2021. [The Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2025 cites it as Act No. 10 of 2021](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>), and [the Gambia Press Union said in August 2026 that it had been in force for more than a year](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The Act's oversight body is set up. [The Information Commission, with a deputy chairperson, was implementing the Act in September 2026](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/information-commission-trains-csos-on-access-to-information-data-protection>). [The Press Union sits on technical committees with it](<https://www.voicegambia.com/gpu-says-access-to-information-law-yet-to-be-fully-implemented/>), and [a United Nations agency tendered in August 2026 for a communication strategy for the Commission](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>). A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.
