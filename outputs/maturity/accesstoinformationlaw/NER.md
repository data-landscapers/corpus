# Niger

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Niger's law is [Ordinance no. 2011-22 of 23 February 2011 on the charter of access to public information and administrative documents](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566209433658g62junbhzjn.pdf>). It is an ordinance with the force of law that gives any person a right to ask public bodies for information. [The Minister of Communication cited it on 22 October 2025 as standing](<https://anp.ne/le-niger-veut-se-doter-dune-politique-de-communication-et-dune-strategie-nationale-de-lutte-contre-la-desinformation/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [Transparency International's Niger chapter said on 10 November 2025 that the ordinance's implementing decree had still not been adopted](<https://transparencyniger.org/?p=243>). The chapter said the ordinance was adopted nearly fifteen years ago and that its implementation remains limited. Its workshop took up the adoption of the decree. Nothing found shows a rule made under the law, a decision on a request or published figures of requests.
