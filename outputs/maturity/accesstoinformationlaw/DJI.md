# Djibouti

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** The Council of Ministers, chaired by the head of state, examined and approved a draft law on the [Digital Code](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/850>) in June 2023. Book VII of the draft provides a general right of access to administrative documents and public information, with rules on how it is exercised. The report was published on 1 June 2023, and the draft is titled Projet de loi portant Code du Numérique. Its owner is the Ministère délégué chargé de l'Économie numérique et de l'Innovation.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [Council of Ministers report](<https://diplomatie.gouv.dj/Articles/DetailArticles/850>), which concerns a draft only: the Code du Numérique, examined and approved by the Council of Ministers under the head of state in June 2023. Nothing found shows a rule made, an oversight body set up or a request decided under an access law.

***Also on file***. [Loi n°2/AN/92/2ème L du 15 septembre 1992 sur la liberté de communication](<https://statemediamonitor.com/djibouti/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says the draft was passed, or that any access law is in force or applied.
