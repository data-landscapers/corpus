# Senegal

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Senegal's law is [Law no. 2025-15 on access to information, adopted by the National Assembly on 26 August 2025, promulgated on 4 September 2025 and published on 25 September 2025](<https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/e05027c9-f6f2-4f70-a313-b0ee81cf2c08>). [It is the first law to set how the right of access is exercised](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/e37fee2b-7841-437a-ae96-b7f969b6a9ac/loi-2025-15-accees-a-l-information.pdf>). [ARTICLE 19 and AFIC say it guarantees a right to ask public bodies for information](<https://www.article19.org/resources/senegal-access-to-information-law-will-support-transparency-and-accountability/>), and [Reporters Without Borders says it covers public institutions and certain private bodies performing a public service](<https://rsf.org/en/country/senegal>). [An October 2026 public note to the Prime Minister says law no. 2025-15 of 4 September 2025 gives citizens, legal residents and legal persons a right of access to information held by public bodies, with a reply due in eight clear days, extendable to fifteen, and an appeal to the national access to information commission](<https://www.osiris.sn/note-publique-au-premier-ministre-identite-numerique-et-e-gouvernement-les.html>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [ARTICLE 19 and AFIC say the law sets up an independent body to oversee it](<https://www.article19.org/resources/senegal-access-to-information-law-will-support-transparency-and-accountability/>). [The Open Government Partnership's review of May 2026 reports that Senegal created the Commission nationale d'accès à l'information under the law](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/documents/senegal-results-report-2023-2025/>), and that [the law carries sanctions for bodies that do not comply](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/fr/documents/senegal-results-report-2023-2025/>).
