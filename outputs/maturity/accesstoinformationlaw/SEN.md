# Senegal

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Senegal's law is [Law no. 2025-15 on access to information](<https://www.archives.sn/api/fichiers/e05027c9-f6f2-4f70-a313-b0ee81cf2c08>). It was adopted by the National Assembly on 26 August 2025, promulgated on 4 September 2025 and published on 25 September 2025. [It is the first law to set how the right of access is exercised](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/docs/e37fee2b-7841-437a-ae96-b7f969b6a9ac/loi-2025-15-accees-a-l-information.pdf>). [ARTICLE 19 and AFIC say it guarantees a right to ask public bodies for information](<https://www.article19.org/resources/senegal-access-to-information-law-will-support-transparency-and-accountability/>). [Reporters Without Borders says it covers public institutions and certain private bodies performing a public service](<https://rsf.org/en/country/senegal>). [An October 2026 public note to the Prime Minister says the law gives citizens, legal residents and legal persons a right of access to information held by public bodies](<https://www.osiris.sn/note-publique-au-premier-ministre-identite-numerique-et-e-gouvernement-les.html>). The note says a reply is due in eight clear days, extendable to fifteen, with an appeal to the national access to information commission.

**Law put to work and applied.** [ARTICLE 19 and AFIC say the law sets up an independent body to oversee it](<https://www.article19.org/resources/senegal-access-to-information-law-will-support-transparency-and-accountability/>). [The decree on the organisation and functioning of the Commission nationale d'accès à l'information was signed in October 2025](<https://www.ouestaf.com/senegal-acces-a-linformation-la-loi-votee-peu-deffet/>). [Implementing decree no. 2025-1837 requires a complaint to the commission before any court action](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Justice/la-conai-toujours-pas-installee-un-an-apres-son-adoption-un-blocage-juridique-concret-selon-el-amath-thiam_n_500254.html>). [On 3 August 2026 Justice Sans Frontière said neither the commission's president nor its twelve members had been appointed](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Justice/la-conai-toujours-pas-installee-un-an-apres-son-adoption-un-blocage-juridique-concret-selon-el-amath-thiam_n_500254.html>). [By ordinance no. 11/2026 of 16 April 2026 the Supreme Court declared inadmissible a request for the amounts of the political funds, because the applicant had not first gone to the commission](<https://www.vie-publique.sn/documents/11628/ordonnance-11-2026-cour-supreme-rejet-requete-fonds-politiques-2025-2026>).
