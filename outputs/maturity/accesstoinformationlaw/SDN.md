# Sudan

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Sudan's law is [the Right to Access Information Law of 2015](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566991640451x872tchicz.pdf>), in effect from signing. The National Assembly ratified it on 27 January 2015 and the President signed it on 22 February 2015. [The National Information Center listed it in 2016 among the laws governing the sector](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2016/Content/AgendaFiles/document/14d93639-cc47-4322-b464-5d97518a6653/NIC_SUDAN.pdf>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest fact found is the law itself, [the Right to Access Information Law of 2015](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566991640451x872tchicz.pdf>). A decree, regulation or order made under it would put it to work. So would an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. A decision, order or penalty on a request for information would show it applied. So would figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Sudan.

***Also on file***. [Commission for the Right to Access Information](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566991640451x872tchicz.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found names rules made under the law or says its oversight body is set up.
