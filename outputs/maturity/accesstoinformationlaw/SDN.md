# Sudan

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Sudan's law is [the Right to Access Information Law of 2015](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1566991640451x872tchicz.pdf>), in effect from signing. The National Assembly ratified it on 27 January 2015 and the President signed it on 22 February 2015. [The National Information Center listed it in 2016 among the laws governing the sector](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2016/Content/AgendaFiles/document/14d93639-cc47-4322-b464-5d97518a6653/NIC_SUDAN.pdf>). [A November 2025 opinion piece in Sudafax says the law covers federal and state bodies, and companies in which the state holds more than 51%](<https://sudafax.com/516941/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%ad%d9%82-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ad%d8%b5%d9%88%d9%84-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%81%d9%88%d8%b6>).

**Law put to work and applied.** [The same piece said on 27 November 2025 that the Act's general information commission is still absent](<https://sudafax.com/516941/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%ad%d9%82-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ad%d8%b5%d9%88%d9%84-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%88%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%81%d9%88%d8%b6>). Nothing found shows a rule made under the law. Nothing found shows a decision on a request or published figures of requests.
