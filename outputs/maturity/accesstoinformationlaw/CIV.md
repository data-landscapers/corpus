# Cote d'Ivoire

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Côte d'Ivoire's law is [Law no. 2013-867 of 23 December 2013 on access to information of public interest](<https://data.gouv.ci/pages/open-data>). [The president of the commission that oversees it describes it as guaranteeing every citizen the right of access to public information](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635603/culture/access-caidp-un-tournant-pour-la-transparence-et-lacces-a-linformation-en-cote-divoire>).

**Law put to work and applied.** The law's oversight body is in place. [The Commission d'Accès à l'Information d'intérêt public et aux Documents Publics, the CAIDP, was created in 2013](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/uploads/thematiques/175877989036.pdf>). [Its president presented a programme named ACCESS CAIDP on 25 July 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635603/culture/access-caidp-un-tournant-pour-la-transparence-et-lacces-a-linformation-en-cote-divoire>), and [in September 2026 the commission began a national register of public documents with about 200 information officers](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644667/culture/acces-a-linformation-et-aux-documents-publics-la-caidp-engage-le-chantier-du-referentiel-des-documents-publics>). A decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the oversight body, dated in the last three years, is not recorded.
