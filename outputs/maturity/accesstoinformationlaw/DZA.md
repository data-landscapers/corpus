# Algeria

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Algeria has no access to information law. [A 2025 thesis at Souk Ahras University says the law called for by the constitutional right of 2016 is still absent](<https://dspace.univ-soukahras.dz/items/68f7c21e-45fe-462c-9afb-5b78ddea0dda/full>). [ARTICLE 19 said in September 2025 that Algeria has no effective right to information legislation, despite years of discussion of draft laws](<https://www.article19.org/resources/mena-setbacks-and-persistent-challenges-to-the-right-to-information/>). Neither names a bill or a published draft.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest fact found is [ARTICLE 19's statement of September 2025](<https://www.article19.org/resources/mena-setbacks-and-persistent-challenges-to-the-right-to-information/>). With no law in force there are no rules, no information commission or commissioner and no decisions on requests to record.

***Also on file***. [Loi organique n° 23-14 du 27 août 2023 relative à l'information](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2023/F2023056.pdf>).

***Not found***. No bill or published draft has been found.
