# Namibia

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Namibia's law is [the Access to Information Act, 2022 (Act No. 8 of 2022)](<http://www.lac.org.na/laws/2022/7986.pdf>), published in the Government Gazette on 28 December 2022. It gives a right of access to information held by public and private bodies. It starts on a date the Minister sets by notice. In May 2026 the African Media Barometer's rapporteur wrote that [the Act remains inoperative](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-press-freedom-reputation-is-realbut-the-fine-print-tells-a-different-story/>).

**Law put to work and applied.** On 9 December 2024 [regulations made under the Act were gazetted](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2024/8530.pdf>). They set the terms of the selection committee and the process for appointing the Information Commissioner. In May 2026 the same rapporteur wrote that [the information commissioner had not been appointed](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibias-press-freedom-reputation-is-realbut-the-fine-print-tells-a-different-story/>). The op-ed adds that the Act's proactive disclosure duties and fee protections had no legal force.
