# Rwanda

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Rwanda's law is [Law no. 04/2013 of 8 February 2013 relating to access to information, published on 11 March 2013](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2013/4/eng@2013-03-11/source>). It is the country's access to information law and has stood since 2013, with no repeal or replacement recorded.

**Law put to work and applied.** [ARTICLE 19 told a Universal Periodic Review session on 27 November 2025 that no independent body oversees the law](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/Rwanda_Article_19_statement.pdf>). It said the Ombudsman's access to information mandate had been removed. ARTICLE 19 says compliance with the law is not independently monitored. It recommended that Rwanda establish an independent oversight body to monitor and enforce the law. Nothing found shows a rule made under the law, a decision on a request or published figures of requests.
