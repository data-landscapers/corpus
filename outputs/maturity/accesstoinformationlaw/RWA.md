# Rwanda

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Rwanda's law is [Law no. 04/2013 of 8 February 2013 relating to access to information, published on 11 March 2013](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2013/4/eng@2013-03-11/source>). It is the country's access to information law and has stood since 2013, with no repeal or replacement recorded.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [Law no. 04/2013](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/law/2013/4/eng@2013-03-11/source>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Rwanda.
