# DR Congo

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo has a bill and no law. [On 27 March 2026 the National Assembly declared receivable a private member's bill on access to information and transparency of public life](<https://actualite.cd/2026/03/28/assemblee-nationale-la-proposition-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation-et-la-transparence-de>) and sent it to a joint committee with ten days to report. The right of access written in the constitution has no law behind it until the bill passes.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the draft, [the bill declared receivable on 27 March 2026](<https://actualite.cd/2026/03/28/assemblee-nationale-la-proposition-de-loi-sur-lacces-linformation-et-la-transparence-de>). A bill makes no rules and sets up no oversight body until it is passed, promulgated and in force. Once it is, what would put it to work is a decree or regulation made under it, or an information commission or commissioner appointed, and what would show it applied is a decision or penalty on a request for information, or published figures of requests.
