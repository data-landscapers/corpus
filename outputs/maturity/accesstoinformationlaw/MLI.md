# Mali

## Access to information law

**Law in force.** Mali has no single access to information law. [A study published in April 2026 finds the legal framework for the right spread across several laws, among them Law no. 98-012 on relations between the administration and its users, and calls for a specific law](<https://www.revue-irs.com/index.php/home/article/view/1637>). The existing laws are counted here for the part that gives the right; the study names no draft of a single law.

**Law put to work and applied.** The nearest held fact is the law itself, [the laws the 2026 study describes](<https://www.revue-irs.com/index.php/home/article/view/1637>). What would put it to work is a decree, regulation or order made under it, an information commission or commissioner appointed, or an existing body such as an ombudsman taking up its oversight. What would show it applied is a decision, order or penalty on a request for information, or figures of requests published by the body that oversees it. Neither is recorded for Mali.
