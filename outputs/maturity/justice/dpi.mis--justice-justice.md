# Justice: justice

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Commission's Fair Trial Principles ask for systems recording proceedings and storing court information, with public access to decisions. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|

| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |

## Countries

### Algeria: 5 Leading

Court judgments or case status were open online in 2026.

- Algeria's Minister of Justice announced on 22 September 2026 that the national electronic window for administrative jurisdictions now covers the administrative courts of appeal and the Council of State, letting litigants and their lawyers consult and track their cases and read the operative parts of judgments and decisions online from any administrative jurisdiction ([source, 2026-09-22](https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/)).

### Zimbabwe: 5 Leading

Court judgments or case status were open online in 2026.

- The Minister says IECMS incorporates case tracking for litigants, and the JSC says court users can monitor cases electronically ([source, 2026-09-26](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/)).

### DR Congo: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2024; online filing is not on record.

- UNDP says SIGAJ, a system for managing judicial cases, was among four digital systems introduced in DRC's justice sector over 2020-2024 ([source, 2024](https://www.undp.org/fr/drcongo/actualites/transition-de-la-justice-congolaise-lalliance-onu-suede-au-service-de-letat-de-droit)).

### Egypt: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2026; online filing is not on record.

- Ministry of Justice technical office head Wadie Hanna Nashed said a lawyers' platform gives digital PDF copies of court case files sent electronically, free hearing-date enquiries and SMS notice of court decisions ([source, 2026-09-14](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/7546339)).
- Al Manassa reports the Ministry of Justice launched the E-Court platform on 31 August 2026, with a lawyers' services platform for first-instance criminal courts giving certified digital case-file copies, online fee payment and SMS of hearing decisions and dates ([source, 2026-08-31](https://manassa.news/en/news/33836)).

### Malawi: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2024; online filing is not on record.

- The Judiciary of Malawi's concept note says digital case management platforms "have already been introduced" as part of the e-court's digitisation of court proceedings ([source, 2024-04](https://judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf)).

### Nigeria: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2026; online filing is not on record.

- The Chief Justice of Nigeria launched the Nigerian Case Management System at the Supreme Court on 1 July 2026, with phase one requiring counsel to upload electronic copies of processes and records for pending appeals set for hearing September to December 2026 ([source, 2026-07-01](https://tribuneonlineng.com/supreme-court-launches-digital-case-management-system/)).

### Rwanda: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2025; online filing is not on record.

- The New Times reports that from January 2025 Rwanda's justice sector and its stakeholders will begin using an upgraded Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS) ([source, 2025-01](https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/22773/news/law/justice-whats-new-in-upgraded-integrated-case-management-system)).

### Senegal: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2026; online filing is not on record.

- Ecofin reported that the programme's final report records an e-Justice platform deployed in one court and five judicial procedures digitised ([source, 2026-08-25](https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot)).

### Somalia: 3 Established

Courts managed cases electronically in 2025; online filing is not on record.

- Goobjoog reports the Federal Government launched the Justice Sector Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS) in Mogadishu on 9 October 2025, linking the CID, the Office of the Prosecutor, the courts and the prison service in one electronic workflow that officials say replaces the paper-based process ([source, 2025-10-09](https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-launches-digital-case-management-system-to-modernize-justice-sector/)).

### Botswana: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Minister of Justice Nelson Ramaotwana told Parliament in the 2026/27 budget debate that the Industrial Court had developed an electronic Case Management System to digitise operations, with a soft launch planned within Q1 2026/27 after procurement of necessary software ([source, 2026-03-16](https://allafrica.com/stories/202603170027.html)).

### Chad: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.

- The Ministry of Justice and Human Rights, with UNDP, launched a digital exchange platform between litigants, lawyers and the judicial system, with a database of judicial activity including decisions, and the N'Djamena jurisdiction as its pilot phase, per Tribune Echos ([source, 2025-10-23](https://tribuneechos.com/e-justice-tchad-le-ministere-de-la-justice-lance-une-plateforme-dechange-numerique/)).

### Cote d'Ivoire: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- The Justice Minister announced on 29 June 2026 that all 39 common-law first-instance courts are connected to the e-Justice platform, completing the first national phase, which began in January 2025 with two pilot courts ([source, 2026-06-29](https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642911/societe/e-justice-les-39-tribunaux-de-premiere-instance-desormais-connectes-au-reseau-numerique-ivoirien)).
- TechAfrica News reports that sixteen more courts joined the national e-justice platform on 20 April 2026, bringing connected jurisdictions to 28, with further services to be added progressively ([source, 2026-04-20](https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/22/ivory-coast-expands-e-justice-platform-to-28-courts-nationwide/)).

### Djibouti: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2024; none in service is on record.

- UNDP reports that a pilot digital case management system for minor offenses is under construction in Djibouti, designed with judges and court administrators, following an assessment of digital readiness, process mapping and a vision for e-justice ([source, 2024](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/undp_djibouti_annual_report_en_v2.pdf)).

### Eswatini: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- The Minister of Justice said at a 20 May 2026 workshop that the Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS), launched 18 August 2025, is being rolled out and links the Judiciary, Ministry of Justice, Law Society, police, Anti-Corruption Commission and Correctional Services ([source, 2026-05-20](https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-digital-court-system-to-end-case-delays-prince-simelane/)).
- UNDP donated equipment worth USD 10,000 to the Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs in August 2025 to aid the rollout of the Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS), which aims to digitalise court operations and case tracking ([source, 2025-08-16](https://independentnews.co.sz/21715/news/undp-donates-equipment-to-support-digital-justice-system/)).

### Ghana: 2 Nascent

A court case management system was under way in 2025; none in service is on record.
