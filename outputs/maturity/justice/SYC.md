# Seychelles

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Seychelles' Judiciary is building its systems in phases. [It launched phase one of an e-Service Platform in November 2025](<https://www.judiciary.sc/news/judiciary-launches-new-e-service-platform-to-streamline-access-to-justice/>). Later phases are to bring more digital tools into court administration. No source says the courts manage their cases on a system. Phase one went live with a demonstration to the bench and bar.

**Online filing.** [Legal practitioners file written submissions online](<https://www.judiciary.sc/news/judiciary-launches-new-e-service-platform-to-streamline-access-to-justice/>) through the platform, with card payment and digital receipts. [Rules of September 2025](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/sites/default/files/2025-11/SI%2072%202025%20-%20Electronic%20Filing%20of%20Written%20Submissions%20%28Civil%20Matters%29%20Regulations%202025_0.pdf>) allow electronic filing of written submissions in Supreme Court civil cases. Switch built the platform for the Judiciary.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. Later phases [are to open the platform to the wider public](<https://www.judiciary.sc/news/judiciary-launches-new-e-service-platform-to-streamline-access-to-justice/>). The first phase serves legal practitioners.

***Also on file***. [Electronic Case Management System (ECMS)](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7053/president-herminie-officially-launches-electronic-case-management-system-new-digital-platform-marks-significant-step-forward-for-seychelles-legal-affairs-sector>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court. Lawyers file written submissions online through the e-Service Platform, which counts only once case management is shown in service.
