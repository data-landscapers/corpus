# Cape Verde

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Cabo Verde's courts handle some cases on the Justice Information System. [Law 38/X/2024 says](<https://imij-cms.gov.cv/uploads/SIJ_Aprovado_Lei_38_IX_2024_28_de_marco_eff33d2257.pdf>) the system has been phased in for over a decade to support electronic case handling. The same law says its mass use in the courts has been held back. The 2024 law sets rules for the system's use in the courts.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the Justice Information System](<https://imij-cms.gov.cv/uploads/SIJ_Aprovado_Lei_38_IX_2024_28_de_marco_eff33d2257.pdf>). The law describes electronic handling of cases inside the courts, not filing by lawyers.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same 2024 law](<https://imij-cms.gov.cv/uploads/SIJ_Aprovado_Lei_38_IX_2024_28_de_marco_eff33d2257.pdf>). It does not say whether decisions are published. Cabo Verde's courts are described only through this law.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says lawyers or litigants can file with the courts online.
