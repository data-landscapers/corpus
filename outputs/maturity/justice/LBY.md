# Libya

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [In November 2026 Khalifa Ashour of the Attorney General's Office said](<https://www.vetogate.com/5764136>) more than 1.2 million cases had been entered in the office's digital system. They date back to 1994 and carry all their documents. Since 2025 the office has also entered each court hearing, each judgment and the appeals against it. He said a search by name now returns a person's previous cases since 1994. He coordinates the office's digital unit. He said it drew on the Egyptian public prosecution's experience.

**Online filing.** No source found since November 2023 says cases can be filed with Libya's courts online. The nearest fact is that [the Attorney General's Office enters hearings and judgments in its own system](<https://www.vetogate.com/5764136>), as Khalifa Ashour said in November 2026.

**Open online.** No source found since November 2023 says Libyan judgments or case status are published online. [In the same interview](<https://www.vetogate.com/5764136>) Khalifa Ashour said the system serves the oversight of investigations and the enforcement of judgments.
