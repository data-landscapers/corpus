# Burkina Faso

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Burkina Faso is building a system for criminal and administrative cases. [The digital transition ministry said in June 2025](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=c0bdd7c3c4556fca9f1b0e43d15a0137&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=974>) that a 2025-2027 project to digitise both judicial chains is under way. It runs under PACT DIGITAL. A development team is at work and the first works are in progress. No source says it is in service in any court.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the 2025-2027 digitisation project](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=c0bdd7c3c4556fca9f1b0e43d15a0137&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=974>). The project covers both the criminal chain and the administrative chain of judicial procedure.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same project](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=c0bdd7c3c4556fca9f1b0e43d15a0137&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=974>). The digital transition ministry reported it in June 2025, with first works in progress.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court.
