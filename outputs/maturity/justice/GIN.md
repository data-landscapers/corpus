# Guinea

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Guinea's work is at the Conakry commercial court. A [12-month consultancy signed on 7 September 2026](<http://www.ucepguinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AVIS-PUBLICATION-DE-LATTRIBUTION-INFORMATISATION-TCC.pdf>) is to set up its integrated management, archive and communication system. A private firm, Judicalex, [says it has digitised the court's decisions](<https://judicalex-gn.org/blog/post/tribunal-du-commerce-le-ministre-de-la-justice-ibrahima-sory-2-tounkara-decouvre-les-installations-de-judicalex/>) and put its hearing roll online. No source says a case management system is in service.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the 2026 consultancy](<http://www.ucepguinee.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AVIS-PUBLICATION-DE-LATTRIBUTION-INFORMATISATION-TCC.pdf>). The consultancy runs for 12 months at USD 26,400 and includes a website and case-law database.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says the courts publish judgments or case status online. [Judicalex's hearing roll](<https://judicalex-gn.org/blog/post/tribunal-du-commerce-le-ministre-de-la-justice-ibrahima-sory-2-tounkara-decouvre-les-installations-de-judicalex/>) is a private firm's service. Guinea's procurement unit published the award on 5 October 2026.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court.
