# Algeria

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Algeria's administrative courts run their cases through a national electronic window. [On 22 September 2026 the Minister of Justice extended it](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/>) to the administrative courts of appeal and the Council of State. Parties can follow their cases from any administrative court in the country. [In November 2025 the Algiers court began using electronic exchange of civil requests and documents](<https://lechodalgerie.dz/reforme-judiciaire-la-numerisation-au-coeur-de-la-modernisation-de-la-justice/>), on the ministry's instruction.

**Online filing.** [From 18 January 2026 lawyers at the courts of justice must exchange civil requests and reply briefs electronically](<https://fildalgerie.dz/justice-numerique-lechange-electronique-des-documents-et-le-paiement-en-ligne-obligatoires-des-janvier-2026/>). Fildalgerie reported the rule in December 2025, after wide uptake by lawyers.

**Open online.** [The national window lets litigants and their lawyers follow their cases online](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/>). They can also read the operative part of judgments and decisions. It covers the administrative courts, the administrative courts of appeal and the Council of State.
