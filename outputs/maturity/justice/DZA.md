# Algeria

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Algeria's administrative courts run their cases through a national electronic window. On 22 September 2026 [the Minister of Justice extended it](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/>) to the administrative courts of appeal and the Council of State. Parties can follow their cases from any administrative court in the country.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the electronic window](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/>), which serves case tracking and copies of judgments. It covers the administrative courts, the administrative courts of appeal and the Council of State.

**Open online.** [The same window lets litigants and their lawyers](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/extension-des-services-du-guichet-electronique-national-aux-cours-administratives-dappel-et-au-conseil-detat/>) follow their cases online. They can also read the operative part of judgments and decisions. It covers the administrative courts only. Parties can also obtain ordinary copies of judgments and decisions.
