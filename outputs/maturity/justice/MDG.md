# Madagascar

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Madagascar's courts use several case tools. [The Justice Ministry's secretary general said in June 2026](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/06/justice-numerique-la-gestion-des.html>) that InfoTA, InfoTF, LogiComm, LogiCivil and LogiPénal are deployed in the courts. In 2019 [such software ran in six first-instance courts](<https://prea.gov.mg/realisations/informatisation-des-juridictions-tatpitf/>) and in administrative and financial courts. The 2019 rollout was paid for by NFD funding.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the tools now deployed](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/06/justice-numerique-la-gestion-des.html>). The 2026 statement lists tools for administrative, land, commercial, civil and criminal cases. None of them is described as open to lawyers.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the June 2026 statement](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/06/justice-numerique-la-gestion-des.html>). The secretary general said the tools are already in the courts.
