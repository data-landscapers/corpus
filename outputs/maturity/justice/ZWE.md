# Zimbabwe

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Zimbabwe's courts run the Integrated Electronic Case Management System, begun in 2022. [It now serves the Constitutional, Supreme, Labour and High Courts](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>). From October 2026 [it is reaching Magistrates' Courts](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>), with Harare and Bulawayo planned by year-end. [Rural courts in Chipinge and Chimanimani joined in January 2026](<https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/01/22/a-new-electronic-court-system-can-bridge-the-divide-for-people-in-the-countryside/>). [In June 2026 it reached 16 more magistrates' courts in Mashonaland East and Manicaland](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/justice-gwaunza-launches-phase-4-3-iecms/>).

**Online filing.** [Litigants and legal practitioners file documents electronically](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>). E-filing offices and internet hubs help users. The Minister of State for Midlands described the filing service in September 2026.

**Open online.** [Court users can track their cases electronically](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>), the minister and the Judicial Service Commission said in September 2026. The Judicial Service Commission ran awareness meetings in eight provinces in October 2026.
