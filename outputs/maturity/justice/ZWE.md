# Zimbabwe

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [The JSC is rolling IECMS out to Magistrates' Courts in Midlands and Mashonaland West from 1 October 2026 (Phase 4.4), with Harare and Bulawayo planned by year-end](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>). [The JSC's IECMS, begun in 2022 with the first paperless court, has been extended to the Constitutional Court, Supreme Court, Labour Court and all divisions of the High Court, per JSC deputy secretary Katsande](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>). [The Ministry of Justice's Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS) was introduced in the Chipinge and Chimanimani districts on 13 January 2026, with all court records kept in digital form, per Post On Sunday](<https://postonsunday.co.zw/2026/01/22/a-new-electronic-court-system-can-bridge-the-divide-for-people-in-the-countryside/>).

**Online filing.** [The Minister of State for Midlands and the JSC say IECMS includes electronic filing so litigants and legal practitioners can file documents electronically, with e-filing offices and internet hubs set up to help users](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>).

**Open online.** [The Minister says IECMS incorporates case tracking for litigants, and the JSC says court users can monitor cases electronically](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/electronic-case-management-system-iecms-brings-justice-closer-to-gweru-residents/>).
