# Ethiopia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Ethiopia's Federal Supreme Court is building an eCourt system. [Addis Fortune reported in July 2025](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>) that pilots were planned from September 2025, with full rollout by August 2026. INSA is building the court management information system and a records system for about 2.2 billion Br. No source says it went live. No source says the pilots began.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the planned eCourt system](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>). The plan also covers an Electronic Records Management System, built by INSA.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same plan](<https://addisfortune.news/supreme-court-sets-bold-course-toward-digital-justice>). The Federal Supreme Court leads the project, due for full rollout by August 2026.
