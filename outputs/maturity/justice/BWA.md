# Botswana

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [In May 2025 the Chief Registrar told Parliament the Court Record Management System was obsolete everywhere but Gaborone](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/86398>). The judiciary was hiring a firm to expand it. [In March 2026 the justice ministry said its Case Management System had not worked, because of system errors](<https://www.thegazette.news/news/paper-shortage-crisis-hits-courts/>). It was built to register cases and check their status. [The same month the minister said the Industrial Court had built its own electronic system](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202603170027.html>). A soft launch was planned within the first quarter of 2026/27.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. [The Chief Registrar said in May 2025 that the e-filing part of the record system was not working](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/86398>). The firm being hired was to build it.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the ministry's March 2026 statement](<https://www.thegazette.news/news/paper-shortage-crisis-hits-courts/>). The system meant to let people check a case's status was not working.
