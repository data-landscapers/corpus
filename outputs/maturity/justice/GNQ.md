# Equatorial Guinea

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Equatorial Guinea's courts are starting to digitise. In October 2024 [the Supreme Court signed an agreement with UNDP](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2024/10/07/la-corte-suprema-de-justicia-de-guinea-ecuatorial-y-pnud-firman-un-acuerdo-en-materia-de-digitalizacion/>) for digital systems in three courts. They are the Supreme Court and the Malabo and Bata provincial courts. The court paid 90 million FCFA toward equipment to digitise case files. No source says the systems are in service.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the 2024 agreement](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2024/10/07/la-corte-suprema-de-justicia-de-guinea-ecuatorial-y-pnud-firman-un-acuerdo-en-materia-de-digitalizacion/>). The agreement covers digital governance systems in the three courts. UNDP is the partner.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same agreement](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2024/10/07/la-corte-suprema-de-justicia-de-guinea-ecuatorial-y-pnud-firman-un-acuerdo-en-materia-de-digitalizacion/>). The Supreme Court's first payment was for IT equipment. The courts are in Malabo and Bata.
