# Burundi

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Burundi is preparing an E-Justice platform. [Wearetech reported in October 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-la-numerisation-de-la-justice-face-au-defi-de-la-connectivite>) that it is to link the penal chain from judicial police to prisons. It is also to manage and keep judicial case files. [Burundi Times reported](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundis-e-justice-platform-to-allow-online-criminal-complaints-and-case-tracking/>) that the platform was presented at a justice-sector meeting on 2 October 2026, with UNDP support. No source says it is in use.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the planned E-Justice platform](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundis-e-justice-platform-to-allow-online-criminal-complaints-and-case-tracking/>). The platform is to connect judicial police, prosecutors, courts and prisons.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same planned platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-la-numerisation-de-la-justice-face-au-defi-de-la-connectivite>). UNDP supports the work, which national radio and television also reported.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court.
