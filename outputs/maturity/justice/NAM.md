# Namibia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [Chief Justice Shivute said the Supreme Court remains entirely paper-based and efforts to extend e-justice to it are under way, with a task force defining an integrated electronic case management system](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibia-to-implement-e-justice-system-in-supreme-court/>). [The Uganda Judiciary reports that Namibia is preparing to roll out its own Integrated Electronic Case Management System (IECMS), and a 15-member Namibian delegation benchmarked Uganda's ECCMIS in May 2025](<https://judiciary.go.ug/data/news/1683/Namibian%20Judiciary%20Team%20Concludes%20ECCMIS%20Benchmarking%20Visit%20to%20Uganda.html>). [The NACM nomination describes Namibia eJustice as a fully implemented end-to-end electronic filing and case management system for the Supreme Court and High Court civil stream (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://nacmnet.org/wp-content/uploads/NAMIBIA_NACM-Nomination-Form-Namibia-eJustice.pdf>).

**Online filing.** [The nomination says Namibia eJustice connects the Supreme Court and High Court with legal practitioners for e-filing and commencement of case files (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://nacmnet.org/wp-content/uploads/NAMIBIA_NACM-Nomination-Form-Namibia-eJustice.pdf>).
