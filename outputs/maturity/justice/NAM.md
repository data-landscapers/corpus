# Namibia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Namibia is extending e-justice to its Supreme Court. [The Chief Justice said in July 2025](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibia-to-implement-e-justice-system-in-supreme-court/>) that the court remains entirely on paper. A task force is defining an integrated case management system. In May 2025 [a Namibian delegation studied Uganda's system](<https://judiciary.go.ug/data/news/1683/Namibian%20Judiciary%20Team%20Concludes%20ECCMIS%20Benchmarking%20Visit%20to%20Uganda.html>). In 2018 [eJustice served the High Court civil stream](<https://nacmnet.org/wp-content/uploads/NAMIBIA_NACM-Nomination-Form-Namibia-eJustice.pdf>).

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. In 2018 [eJustice linked practitioners to the High Court](<https://nacmnet.org/wp-content/uploads/NAMIBIA_NACM-Nomination-Form-Namibia-eJustice.pdf>) for filing. The 2017 Supreme Court rules provide for e-filing from a date the Chief Justice sets.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the Chief Justice's July 2025 remarks](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibia-to-implement-e-justice-system-in-supreme-court/>). Namibia signed a knowledge-sharing agreement with Uganda on its system.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court. The eJustice system described in 2018 is outside the window.
