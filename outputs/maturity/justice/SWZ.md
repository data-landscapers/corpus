# Eswatini

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Eswatini is rolling out an Integrated Electronic Case Management System. [It was launched on 18 August 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-digital-court-system-to-end-case-delays-prince-simelane/>) with training for users across the justice sector. It links the Judiciary, the ministry, the Law Society, police, the Anti-Corruption Commission and prisons. [UNDP gave equipment for the rollout](<https://independentnews.co.sz/21715/news/undp-donates-equipment-to-support-digital-justice-system/>) in August 2025. [On 22 May 2026 the project team showed the system's criminal, land and civil workflows to MPs](<https://swazi24.com/46158/news/digital-eswatini-team-presents-e-justice-system-to-mps/>).

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. [The system is designed for electronic filing](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-digital-court-system-to-end-case-delays-prince-simelane/>), the minister said in May 2026. Hearings are also to be held virtually.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the system's planned case tracking](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/new-digital-court-system-to-end-case-delays-prince-simelane/>). [The Judiciary publishes notable decisions with links to full text on the Eswatini Legal Information Institute](<https://www.judiciary.org.sz/services>).
