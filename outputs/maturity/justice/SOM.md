# Somalia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Somalia launched a justice sector case management system in Mogadishu. [Goobjoog reported](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-launches-digital-case-management-system-to-modernize-justice-sector/>) the launch on 9 October 2025. It links the CID, the prosecutor's office, the courts and the prison service in one electronic workflow. Officials say it replaces the paper process. It is called the Justice Sector Integrated Electronic Case Management System.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the October 2025 launch](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-launches-digital-case-management-system-to-modernize-justice-sector/>). The system joins the CID, prosecutors, courts and prisons in one workflow.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same launch](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-launches-digital-case-management-system-to-modernize-justice-sector/>). The Federal Government launched it in Mogadishu. Goobjoog reported the launch on 10 October 2025.
