# Mauritania

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [On 4 February 2025 the justice minister chaired a meeting on the sector's digitisation projects](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/219925>). The digital transformation minister and the head of the population register agency attended. Their three bodies are digitising the criminal justice chain. It is to let lawyers and parties follow their files. They are also digitising commercial cases at every step, from registration to judgment, appeal and cassation. A third project digitises the criminal record certificate.

**Online filing.** No source found since November 2023 says cases can be filed with Mauritania's courts online. The nearest fact is [the plan to digitise commercial cases from registration onwards](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/219925>), reported in February 2025.

**Open online.** No source found since November 2023 says Mauritanian judgments or case status are published online. [The criminal chain project is to let parties and lawyers follow their files](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/219925>), the official news agency reported in February 2025.
