# Tanzania

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Tanzania's Judiciary runs its electronic case management system across its courts. [In May 2025 its ICT Centre director said](<https://dailynews.co.tz/parliamentary-budget-committee-applauds-judiciarys-ict-reforms/>) cases are tracked from the Court of Appeal to district courts. In February 2026 [the Chief Court Administrator said](<https://dailynews.co.tz/courts-adopt-ai-integrated-data-systems/>) the system is phasing out paper files and is being expanded. Its systems exchange data with 16 national institutions. [In March 2026 the Chief Registrar said it runs from District Courts to the Court of Appeal](<https://dailynews.co.tz/judiciary-reforms-boost-case-disposal-to-128pc/>), linked to ten other systems.

**Online filing.** [Litigants can file cases online from anywhere](<https://dailynews.co.tz/courts-adopt-ai-integrated-data-systems/>), the Chief Court Administrator said in February 2026. Remote filing, he said, also helps revenue collection.

**Open online.** [He said they can also track a case's progress online](<https://dailynews.co.tz/courts-adopt-ai-integrated-data-systems/>) and see which magistrate is assigned. He said this makes the courts more transparent and accountable. He spoke at the e-Government Annual Meeting in Arusha.
