# Togo

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [The National Assembly adopted a law on 14 August 2026 that aims to complete the digitalisation of judicial procedures through the commercial courts' electronic platform and online hearings](<https://assemblee-nationale.tg/lassemblee-nationale-adopte-une-loi-pour-moderniser-la-justice-commerciale/>). [The Lomé commercial court's president states its chambers have had the FORSETI COMMERCIAL platform for automated, dematerialised handling of commercial procedures for six years, renamed SYSCOM after World Bank-funded updates and extended to the commercial chambers of the Lomé Court of Appeal](<https://tribunaldecommercedelome.tg/actualites/le-tribunal-de-commerce-de-lome-innove-avec-syscom>). [Togo First reports the Lomé commercial court runs SYSCOM, a centralised system for commercial procedures covering case registration of summonses and filings](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/justice/0910-12669-la-cellule-climat-des-affaires-dote-le-tribunal-de-commerce-dun-kit-de-visioconference-des-audiences-desormais-possibles-en-ligne>).

**Online filing.** [The Lomé commercial court's president states SYSCOM has been open since December 2019 to lawyers, bailiffs and to some extent individuals, with an online module for external users to enrol summonses, submit requests and file pleadings and documents](<https://tribunaldecommercedelome.tg/actualites/le-tribunal-de-commerce-de-lome-innove-avec-syscom>).

**Open online.** [The justice ministry says the new online portal of the Tribunal de Grande Instance de Lomé, launched 2 July 2026, lets litigants, lawyers and bailiffs follow the progress of procedures remotely](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>).
