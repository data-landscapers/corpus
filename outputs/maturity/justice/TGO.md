# Togo

## Justice

**Case management in service.** The Lomé commercial court runs its cases on SYSCOM. [Its president said in February 2025](<https://tribunaldecommercedelome.tg/actualites/le-tribunal-de-commerce-de-lome-innove-avec-syscom>) that the court has handled commercial procedures on it for six years. SYSCOM now also serves the Lomé Court of Appeal's commercial chambers. [Togo First described it in 2023](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/justice/0910-12669-la-cellule-climat-des-affaires-dote-le-tribunal-de-commerce-dun-kit-de-visioconference-des-audiences-desormais-possibles-en-ligne>) as covering registration of summonses and filings. World Bank funding paid for its updates.

**Online filing.** [Lawyers, bailiffs and some individuals file online through SYSCOM](<https://tribunaldecommercedelome.tg/actualites/le-tribunal-de-commerce-de-lome-innove-avec-syscom>), open to them since December 2019. They enrol summonses and file pleadings and documents. SYSCOM also sends electronic notice of decisions to its users.

**Open online.** [The Lomé high court's portal, launched on 2 July 2026](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>), lets litigants, lawyers and bailiffs follow procedures remotely. The portal also takes court fee payments online.
