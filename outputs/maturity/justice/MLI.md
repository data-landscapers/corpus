# Mali

## Justice

**Case management in service.** In July 2026 the Ministry of Justice said its e-Justice platform, [built by Malian teams, was about to launch](<http://news.abamako.com/h/305410.html>). It would first issue nationality certificates and criminal records online. A second phase would manage court files digitally. On 5 November 2026 the minister said the ministry [is putting the platform into operation](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/modernisation-de-la-justice-renforcement-de-letat-de-droit-le-pnud-3121305.html>), with case processing to come later. The same day UNDP, with Dutch support, [handed over laptops, tablets and modems worth USD 493,193](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/modernisation-de-la-justice-renforcement-de-letat-de-droit-le-pnud-3121305.html>) to equip it.

**Online filing.** No source found since October 2023 says cases can be filed with the courts online. The nearest held fact: [the platform's first services are certificates and criminal records](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/modernisation-de-la-justice-renforcement-de-letat-de-droit-le-pnud-3121305.html>).

**Open online.** No source found since October 2023 says judgments are published online. None says the public can look up a case's status. The nearest held fact: [the platform is to manage court files in a later phase](<http://news.abamako.com/h/305410.html>).
