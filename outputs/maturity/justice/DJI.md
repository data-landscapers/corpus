# Djibouti

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Djibouti is building a pilot case system for minor offences. [UNDP reported for 2024](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/undp_djibouti_annual_report_en_v2.pdf>) that it was under construction, designed with judges and court administrators. It followed a review of digital readiness and a mapping of court processes. No source says it is in service. UNDP supported the readiness assessment and the design.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the pilot system](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/undp_djibouti_annual_report_en_v2.pdf>). The pilot covers minor offences only, and its design involved judges and court administrators. No online services for lawyers are described.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same pilot](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/undp_djibouti_annual_report_en_v2.pdf>). UNDP's 2024 annual report describes the pilot as still under construction at the year's end.

***Also on file***. [centralized judicial-statistics system](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/undp_djibouti_annual_report_en_v2.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court.
