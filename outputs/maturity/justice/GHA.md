# Ghana

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [The Labari Journal reports that Ghana's World Bank-funded eJustice system, meant to automate court registry filing through to execution of decisions, is still being rolled out and an IMF 2024 governance report says it has "stalled"](<https://labarijournal.com/is-ghanas-3-million-ejustice-system-is-underdelivering/>). [A Judicial Service of Ghana document from 2022, cited by The Labari Journal, put the number of cases on the eJustice system at 61,000+, up from 58,495 the previous year (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://labarijournal.com/is-ghanas-3-million-ejustice-system-is-underdelivering/>).
