# South Africa

## Justice

**Case management in service.** South Africa's High Courts are moving to Court Online. [The Minister of Justice said in September 2025](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/32920/>) that 19 courts had adopted it. The first two went live in September 2022. [It went live in Gqeberha in July 2025](<https://justice.gov.za/m_speeches/2025/20250716-CourtOnline-DMin.html>), described as end-to-end case management. Mahikeng and Free State High Courts were next, from October 2025.

**Online filing.** [A 2026 KwaZulu-Natal directive](<https://www.judiciary.org.za/images/Directives/Directives_2026/KZN%20Court%20Online%20Directives%20(Final%20June%202026).pdf>) requires practitioners and litigants to upload process for issue on Court Online. [The Deputy Minister said in July 2025](<https://justice.gov.za/m_speeches/2025/20250716-CourtOnline-DMin.html>) that it handles e-filing. The directive applies in the KwaZulu-Natal Division.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are open to the public online. The nearest fact is [the 2026 directive](<https://www.judiciary.org.za/images/Directives/Directives_2026/KZN%20Court%20Online%20Directives%20(Final%20June%202026).pdf>). Court Online and CaseLines hold virtual court files under the directive.
