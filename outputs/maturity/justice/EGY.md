# Egypt

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Egypt's Ministry of Justice [launched the E-Court platform on 31 August 2026](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33836>). It serves the first-instance criminal courts. Lawyers get certified digital copies of case files and pay fees online. [The ministry's technical office said](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/7546339>) case files are sent as PDF copies, and hearing decisions go out by SMS. [In November 2026 the Council of State widened its e-litigation pilot](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/8/%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9/7610749>) to the Education Administrative Court and the Health Disciplinary Court.

**Online filing.** [The Health Disciplinary Court is linked to the Administrative Prosecution Authority](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/8/%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%B3-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9/7610749>). A disciplinary suit can be filed fully electronically, with its documents. Hearing dates and judgments are notified the same way. [Lawyers can attend some criminal hearings remotely](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33836>) through the E-Court platform.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments are published online. [Lawyers can look up hearing dates free](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/7546339>) on their platform, which is not open to the public. Decisions at the end of a hearing reach lawyers by text message, for a fee.
