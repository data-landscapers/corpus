# Egypt

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Egypt's Ministry of Justice [launched the E-Court platform on 31 August 2026](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33836>). It serves the first-instance criminal courts. Lawyers get certified digital copies of case files and pay fees online. [The ministry's technical office said](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/7546339>) case files are sent as PDF copies, and hearing decisions go out by SMS. The new Criminal Procedure Law took effect on 1 October 2026.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. Lawyers [can attend some hearings remotely](<https://manassa.news/en/news/33836>) through the platform. The law lets courts hold some hearings remotely.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments are published online. [Lawyers can look up hearing dates free](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B6%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF/7546339>) on their platform, which is not open to the public. Decisions at the end of a hearing reach lawyers by text message, for a fee.
