# Mauritius

## Justice

**Case management in service.** A government list of operational systems from November 2024 [names a case management system for the Children's Court](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). It also lists a Supreme Court registry system. In July 2023 L'Express found [the e-judiciary project 40% done](<http://cloudbeta.lexpress.mu/s/article/425103/judiciary-dix-ans-apres-toujours-pas-reseau>), with its second phase unfinished. Some courts, among them the Family Division, have been computerised since 2021.

**Online filing.** Since January 2021 attorneys at the Commercial Division [must file written submissions online](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>) and apply online for subpoenas. No source since November 2023 says lawyers file online. The e-Judiciary system went live at the Supreme Court in 2013 for commercial cases.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments are published online or that the public can look up a case. The nearest fact is [the Commercial Division's e-filing rule](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). The e-filing system serves registered attorneys.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says lawyers or litigants can file with the courts online. The rule that attorneys file online at the Commercial Division dates from 2021, and the only e-filing notice since November 2023 concerns maintenance.
