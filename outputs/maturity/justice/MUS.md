# Mauritius

## Justice

**Case management in service.** A government list of operational systems from November 2024 [names a case management system for the Children's Court](<https://cib.govmu.org/Documents/Reports/List%20of%20Operational%20Systems.pdf>). It also lists a registry system for the Supreme Court. In July 2023 L'Express reported that [some courts, among them the Family Division, have been computerised since 2021](<http://cloudbeta.lexpress.mu/s/article/425103/judiciary-dix-ans-apres-toujours-pas-reseau>). The same report found [the e-judiciary project 40% done](<http://cloudbeta.lexpress.mu/s/article/425103/judiciary-dix-ans-apres-toujours-pas-reseau>), with its second phase unfinished.

**Online filing.** The Chief Justice [launched the first phase of e-judiciary at the Supreme Court in April 2013](<https://business.mega.mu/2013/04/05/e-judiciary-entry-commercial-litigation-supreme-court-now-available-online/>). Lawyers could then enter commercial cases and submit documents online. Since January 2021 attorneys at the Commercial Division [must file written submissions online and apply online for subpoenas](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). [The Judiciary's e-filing notices](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>) run to October 2025.

**Open online.** No source found since October 2023 says judgments are published online. None says the public can look up a case's status online. The nearest held fact: [the e-filing system serves registered attorneys](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>).
