# Tunisia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [In November 2026 the Administrative Tribunal's central chambers held their first hearings on its Selim e-justice system](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/04/justice-le-tribunal-administratif-passe-a-laudience-electronique-avec-le-systeme-selim/>), La Presse reported. Its regional chambers were already digitised. The tribunal says Selim now covers all its chambers and every phase of procedure. [In October 2026 a judicial council leader said digitising the Administrative Court's services had begun in part](<https://news-tunisia.tunisienumerique.com/justice-budget-digitalisation-court-delays-and-enforcement-of-rulings-at-the-centre-of-debate/>). [An African Development Bank notice of September 2025](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/aoi-tunisie-mise-en-place-dun-portail-de-services-e-justice-et-dune-application-metier-du-ministere-de-la-justice-tunisie-digitale-2020>) is for an e-Justice portal and business application for the Ministry of Justice.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the Selim system](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/04/justice-le-tribunal-administratif-passe-a-laudience-electronique-avec-le-systeme-selim/>), which runs the tribunal's hearings and procedure. The e-Justice portal in the bank's notice is to offer services to court users.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same report on Selim](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/04/justice-le-tribunal-administratif-passe-a-laudience-electronique-avec-le-systeme-selim/>). It names no public look-up of cases or judgments.
