# Tunisia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Tunisia is procuring an e-Justice system. [An African Development Bank notice of September 2025](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/aoi-tunisie-mise-en-place-dun-portail-de-services-e-justice-et-dune-application-metier-du-ministere-de-la-justice-tunisie-digitale-2020>) is for a contract to build a services portal and a business application for the Ministry of Justice. A 2023 pilot of [the Jshare exchange platform](<https://www.justice.gov.tn/index.php?cHash=888f48d092bdd6ed182973531d401f87&id=575&tx_ttnews=3095>) falls before the window. Jshare was for exchanging civil court documents in model courts.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the e-Justice portal tender](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/aoi-tunisie-mise-en-place-dun-portail-de-services-e-justice-et-dune-application-metier-du-ministere-de-la-justice-tunisie-digitale-2020>). The portal is to offer e-Justice services to users of the courts. No date is set for it to open.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the same tender](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/aoi-tunisie-mise-en-place-dun-portail-de-services-e-justice-et-dune-application-metier-du-ministere-de-la-justice-tunisie-digitale-2020>). The loan is from the African Development Bank.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court.
