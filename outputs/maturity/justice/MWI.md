# Malawi

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Malawi's courts have begun to manage cases digitally. [The Judiciary's April 2024 concept note says](<https://judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf>) digital case management platforms have already been introduced. Its e-court [linked ten courts, six prisons and four police stations](<https://judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf>) in a pilot. The pilot centres on virtual hearings. The e-court went live from the Lilongwe Chief Resident Magistrate Court.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. [The e-court plan includes electronic filing](<https://judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf>) within the pilot. The note says online portals are to replace paper-based processes.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the e-court's online portals](<https://judiciary.mw/sites/default/files/2024-04/E-COURT%20LAUNCH.pdf>), which are planned. The e-court also links prisons and police stations so detainees can apply for bail.
