# Senegal

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Senegal's e-Justice platform is in service in one court. [Ecofin reported in August 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>) that the programme's final report records it at the Pikine-Guédiawaye court, with five procedures digitised. [The justice ministry launched it officially on 10 September 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Justice/modernisation-de-la-justice-les-plateformes-e-justice-et-jokko-ak-yoon-officiellement-lancees_n_503904.html>). It is to carry penal, civil, commercial and social cases. UNDP supports the programme.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the September 2026 launch](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Justice/modernisation-de-la-justice-les-plateformes-e-justice-et-jokko-ak-yoon-officiellement-lancees_n_503904.html>). Pikine-Guédiawaye was the pilot court, with a roadmap for expansion. Extension to other courts depends on funding.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the pilot court's report](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>). The court is also piloting digitisation of judicial archives.
