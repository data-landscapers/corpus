# Senegal

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [Ecofin reported that the programme's final report records an e-Justice platform deployed in one court and five judicial procedures digitised](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>). [Leral reported that the Pikine-Guediawaye court is piloting, with UNDP support, digitisation of judicial archives for later integration into the E-Justice platform, with first results judged satisfactory by the justice ministry on 2026-10-09 and extension to other courts possible](<https://www.leral.net/Justice-senegalaise-Pikine-Guediawaye-teste-la-numerisation-des-archives-judiciaires_a408035.html>). [Ecofin reported that the Pikine-Guediawaye courthouse is a pilot site for the e-Justice programme supported by UNDP, with a roadmap developed for possible expansion and no extension to other courts announced](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>).

**Online filing.** No source found since October 2023 says cases can be filed with the courts online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>).

**Open online.** No source found since October 2023 says judgments are published or case status can be looked up online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2508-58308-senegal-advances-e-justice-rollout-with-dakar-area-pilot>).
