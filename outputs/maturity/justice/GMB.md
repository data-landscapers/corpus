# Gambia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** The Gambia's electronic case management system is in pilot. [The Office of the Chief Justice said so in August 2024](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/judiciary-receives-vital-support-from-usaid>). In January 2026 [the Chief Justice said lost donor funding had slowed it](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/judiciary-still-faces-shortage-of-courtrooms-and-office-space>), and he still awaited its implementation. In March 2026 UNDP [sought bids to build an integrated system](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/296129>) for the Judiciary and two ministries. ABA ROLI supported the pilot.

**Online filing.** [The NHRC's 2024 report says the system includes e-filing](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>). No source says lawyers or litigants file through it. The same report says the Judiciary has developed e-payment and e-assignment of cases.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the 2026 tender](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/296129>). The tender, UNDP-GMB-00558, closed on 13 April 2026.

***Also on file***. [Digital Case Management System (UNDP-GMB-00320)](<https://moj.gov.gm/consultancy-for-feasibility-study-and-implementation-of-a-digital-case-management-system/>); [Computer-Aided Transcription System (CATS)](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/296129>); [Computer Aided Recording and Transcription System (CATS)](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/judiciary-still-faces-shortage-of-courtrooms-and-office-space>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court. The NHRC's 2024 report says the system has e-filing, but not that anyone files through it.
