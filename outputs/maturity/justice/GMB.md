# Gambia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [UNDP Gambia issued request for proposal UNDP-GMB-00558 on 27 March 2026 (deadline 13 April 2026) to develop, deploy and operationalize an Integrated Digital Case Management System for the Ministry of Justice, Ministry of Interior and the Judiciary](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/296129>). [The Chief Justice said at the 2026 Legal Year opening that donor funding suspension slowed expansion of the judiciary's electronic case management system, whose implementation he looks forward to](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/judiciary-still-faces-shortage-of-courtrooms-and-office-space>). [The Office of the Chief Justice said the electronic Case Management System developed with ABA ROLI support is in its pilot phase; online payment of court fees is expected](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/judiciary-receives-vital-support-from-usaid>).

**Online filing.** [The NHRC's 2024 report says the Judiciary's Case Management System includes e-filing](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>).

**Open online.** No source found since October 2023 says judgments are published or case status can be looked up online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>).
