# Morocco

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Morocco is spreading the electronic case file. [The justice minister told MPs on 2 November 2026](<https://justice.gov.ma/2026/11/03/16889/>) that it is being generalised, with digital notification, at about MAD 2.5 billion. [A national enforcement platform is being extended to all courts](<https://www.hespress.com/%d9%88%d9%87%d8%a8%d9%8a-%d9%8a%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%ac%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d9%86%d9%88%d9%83-%d8%a8%d8%b3%d8%a8%d8%a8-%d8%b1%d9%81%d8%b6-%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%af-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d8%af-1834575.html>). It already handles enforcement files beyond Casablanca.

**Online filing.** [Lawyers file requests, briefs and appeals through a dedicated platform](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/01/30/justice-voici-les-grandes-lignes-du-projet-du-dossier-judiciaire-electronique_1915535/>), the minister said in January 2025. [The ministry's portal says](<https://mahakim.ma/>) pleadings go straight to the chosen court, and fees are paid online. The lawyers' platform first opened with the Casablanca courts in 2021.

**Open online.** [The Mahakim tracking service](<https://mahakim.ma/>) lets the public follow civil and criminal files and hearing dates by file number. The ministry describes it on its 2026 portal. Files can also be followed through enforcement and recovery.
