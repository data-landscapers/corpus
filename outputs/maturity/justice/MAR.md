# Morocco

## Justice

**Case management in service.** In January 2025 the justice minister [said an electronic case file for every type of case and court was being prepared](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/01/30/justice-voici-les-grandes-lignes-du-projet-du-dossier-judiciaire-electronique_1915535/>). In November 2026 he [told Parliament the electronic case file and digital notification are being made general](<https://justice.gov.ma/2026/11/03/16889/>), at a cost of about MAD 2.5 billion. He [told a parliamentary committee that a national enforcement platform has entered service](<https://www.hespress.com/%d9%88%d9%87%d8%a8%d9%8a-%d9%8a%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%ac%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a8%d9%86%d9%88%d9%83-%d8%a8%d8%b3%d8%a8%d8%a8-%d8%b1%d9%81%d8%b6-%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%af-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d8%af-1834575.html>). It lets enforcement files be handled nationally, not only in Casablanca, and is being extended to all courts.

**Online filing.** [Morocco's justice minister says lawyers can now file requests, briefs and appeal acts through a dedicated platform](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/01/30/justice-voici-les-grandes-lignes-du-projet-du-dossier-judiciaire-electronique_1915535/>). [The Ministry of Justice says its lawyer electronic-exchange platform lets lawyers file pleadings with the chosen court and pay court fees online, the filing going straight to the court](<https://mahakim.ma/>).

**Open online.** [The Mahakim file-tracking service lets the public follow civil and criminal files, notifications, enforcement and recovery files, and hearing dates by entering the file number at the court](<https://mahakim.ma/>).
