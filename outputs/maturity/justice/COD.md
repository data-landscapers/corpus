# DR Congo

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [UNDP says SIGAJ manages judicial cases](<https://www.undp.org/fr/drcongo/actualites/transition-de-la-justice-congolaise-lalliance-onu-suede-au-service-de-letat-de-droit>). It was one of four digital systems brought into the justice sector over 2020-2024. [The Ministry of Justice launched a network linking courts, prosecutors' offices and prisons on 5 November 2026](<https://actualite.cd/2026/11/05/rdc-lue-et-le-pnud-accompagnent-la-digitalisation-de-la-justice-pour-renforcer-la>). UNDP gives technical support and the EU pays for it. It is to carry the judicial and prison management applications, site by site. [In July 2026 Projet TRANSFORME validated 33 commercial-court procedure maps](<https://transforme.cd/activities/marche-vers-la-dematerialisation-de-la-justice-commerciale-grace-au-projet-transforme>). They are the first step towards a platform for commercial cases from filing to judgment.

**Online filing.** No source found since October 2023 says cases can be filed with the courts online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/fr/drcongo/actualites/transition-de-la-justice-congolaise-lalliance-onu-suede-au-service-de-letat-de-droit>).

**Open online.** No source found since October 2023 says judgments are published or case status can be looked up online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.undp.org/fr/drcongo/actualites/transition-de-la-justice-congolaise-lalliance-onu-suede-au-service-de-letat-de-droit>).
