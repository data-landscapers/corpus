# DR Congo

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [UNDP says SIGAJ](<https://www.undp.org/fr/drcongo/actualites/transition-de-la-justice-congolaise-lalliance-onu-suede-au-service-de-letat-de-droit>), a system for managing judicial cases, was one of four systems introduced in the justice sector between 2020 and 2024. On 5 November 2026 the Ministry of Justice [began linking courts, prosecutors and prisons](<https://actualite.cd/2026/11/05/rdc-lue-et-le-pnud-accompagnent-la-digitalisation-de-la-justice-pour-renforcer-la>) on a common digital network. The EU funds it and UNDP supports it.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. In July 2026 [consultants began to specify a platform](<https://transforme.cd/activities/marche-vers-la-dematerialisation-de-la-justice-commerciale-grace-au-projet-transforme>) to run commercial cases from filing to judgment. The platform is to cover 33 validated procedure maps for the commercial courts.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the planned commercial-court platform](<https://transforme.cd/activities/marche-vers-la-dematerialisation-de-la-justice-commerciale-grace-au-projet-transforme>). UNDP also names SIGAJ among four justice systems introduced over 2020-2024.
