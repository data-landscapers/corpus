# Nigeria

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Nigeria's Supreme Court launched the Nigerian Case Management System on 1 July 2026. [The Chief Justice said](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/supreme-court-launches-digital-case-management-system/>) it supports the whole life of an appeal. It is to expand each quarter until every pending appeal is on it. [It is designed to link the superior courts](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/supreme-court-launches-digital-case-management-system/>) to the Court of Appeal and the Supreme Court.

**Online filing.** [Practice directions in force from 1 July 2026](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/supreme-court-launches-digital-case-management-system/>) require counsel to upload processes and records of appeal to the system. They start with appeals heard from September 2026. Full electronic filing is the second phase.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are open to the public online. The nearest fact is [the system's launch](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/supreme-court-launches-digital-case-management-system/>). Phase two is to let litigants start appeals online.
