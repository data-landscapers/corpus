# Benin

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [La Marina BJ reports that Benin's e-justice project, an information system for managing criminal and civil procedures, was at the stage of a technical feasibility study due to start in October 2024, with a consultancy to be named by September 2024](<https://lamarina.bj/2024/08/29/ou-en-est-on-avec-linterconnexion-des-juridictions-au-benin/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Online filing.** [La Marina BJ reports that from 16 February 2026 requests and fee payments for three filings at the Cotonou Commercial Court are made exclusively online, with copies of rulings to follow from 1 March 2026](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/02/13/benin-trois-nouveaux-e-services-desormais-operationnels-au-tribunal-de-commerce-de-cotonou/>).

**Open online.** [La Nation reports the Cotonou commercial court platform lets the public check a case's status online and the site lets users download court decisions (2021, before the three-year window)](<http://news.acotonou.com/h/133135.html>).
