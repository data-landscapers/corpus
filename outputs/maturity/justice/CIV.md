# Cote d'Ivoire

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [The Justice Minister announced on 29 June 2026 that all 39 common-law first-instance courts are connected to the e-Justice platform, completing the first national phase, which began in January 2025 with two pilot courts](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642911/societe/e-justice-les-39-tribunaux-de-premiere-instance-desormais-connectes-au-reseau-numerique-ivoirien>). [TechAfrica News reports that sixteen more courts joined the national e-justice platform on 20 April 2026, bringing connected jurisdictions to 28, with further services to be added progressively](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/22/ivory-coast-expands-e-justice-platform-to-28-courts-nationwide/>). [Fraternité Matin reports that sixteen more courts were connected to the e-Justice.ci platform on 20 April 2026, bringing the network to 28 jurisdictions, with full national coverage as the stated goal](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641413/societe/e-justiceci-la-cote-divoire-connecte-16-nouvelles-juridictions-et-accelere-sa-revolution-digitale>).
