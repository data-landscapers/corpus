# Cote d'Ivoire

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Côte d'Ivoire's e-Justice platform now reaches every first-instance court. [The Justice Minister announced on 29 June 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642911/societe/e-justice-les-39-tribunaux-de-premiere-instance-desormais-connectes-au-reseau-numerique-ivoirien>) that all 39 common-law courts are connected. What it serves is nationality certificates and criminal-record extracts. In June 2025 [he said all judicial procedures would be digitised in the short term](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634927/politique/tribunal-de-1re-instance-dabidjan-le-ministre-sansan-kambile-lance-la-plateforme-digitale-e-justiceci>). No source says courts manage their cases on it.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. Citizens [apply and pay online for certificates](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634927/politique/tribunal-de-1re-instance-dabidjan-le-ministre-sansan-kambile-lance-la-plateforme-digitale-e-justiceci>) through the platform. The platform's new services in 2026 include criminal-record bulletins for administrations and authentication of acts.

**Open online.** No source since November 2023 says judgments or case status are online. The nearest fact is [the platform's spread to all 39 courts](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642911/societe/e-justice-les-39-tribunaux-de-premiere-instance-desormais-connectes-au-reseau-numerique-ivoirien>). It began with two pilot courts in January 2025 and reached 28 by April 2026.

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court. The e-Justice platform in all 39 first-instance courts issues nationality certificates and criminal records, not case management.
