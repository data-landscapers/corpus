# Zambia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Zambia's Judiciary is building an electronic case management system. [Its contractor said in January 2025](<https://www.synisys.com/news/zambia-electronic-case-management-system/>) that it is to link the Supreme, Constitutional, Appeal, High and Subordinate Courts. Treasury funds it, and the courts still worked by hand. In 2022 [the earlier system was in limbo for lack of funds](<https://judiciaryzambia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/The-Judiciary-of-Zambia-Annual-Report2022.pdf>). Registries were brimming with paper in 2022. Funds to customise the old software were not available.

**Online filing.** No source since November 2023 says cases can be filed online. The nearest fact is [the system under development](<https://www.synisys.com/news/zambia-electronic-case-management-system/>). The system is being developed by Synergy International Systems.

**Open online.** In 2022 [the Judiciary uploaded superior-court decisions](<https://judiciaryzambia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/The-Judiciary-of-Zambia-Annual-Report2022.pdf>) and cause lists to its website. No source since November 2023 says so. The website also carried notices and press statements in 2022.

***Also on file***. [HP Records Manager (Electronic Record Management System)](<https://judiciaryzambia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/The-Judiciary-of-Zambia-Annual-Report2022.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says a court case management system is in service in any court. The 2022 annual report on decisions uploaded to the Judiciary website is outside the window.
