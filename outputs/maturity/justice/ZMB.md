# Zambia

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [Synergy International Systems says the Judiciary of Zambia is developing, with Treasury funding, an Electronic Case Management System to connect the Supreme, Constitutional, Appeal, High and Subordinate Courts, and that it currently relies on manual processes](<https://www.synisys.com/news/zambia-electronic-case-management-system/>). [The Judiciary of Zambia's 2022 annual report says operationalisation of its case management system remained in limbo because funds for customising the software were unavailable, with registries brimming with paper (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://judiciaryzambia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/The-Judiciary-of-Zambia-Annual-Report2022.pdf>).

**Online filing.** No source found since October 2023 says cases can be filed with the courts online. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.synisys.com/news/zambia-electronic-case-management-system/>).

**Open online.** [The Judiciary's 2022 annual report says it continued uploading decisions of the superior courts, cause lists, notices and press statements to its website (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://judiciaryzambia.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/The-Judiciary-of-Zambia-Annual-Report2022.pdf>).
