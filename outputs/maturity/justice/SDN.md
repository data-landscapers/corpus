# Sudan

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [In July 2024 a former prosecutor told Atar](<https://atarnetwork.com/?p=2097>) that Sudan's judiciary had not adopted electronic archiving. It still kept paper registers and forms. A former judge said the courts held no scans of case files. They held only litigants' names and case numbers and types. Fighting had destroyed court buildings and files in Khartoum and Omdurman. The judge said all case files may be lost in states the Rapid Support Forces overran.

**Online filing.** No source found since November 2023 says cases can be filed with Sudan's courts online. The nearest fact is [the paper registers and forms the courts kept in 2024](<https://atarnetwork.com/?p=2097>).

**Open online.** No source found since November 2023 says Sudanese judgments or case status are published online. The nearest fact is [the loss of paper case files in courts burned or looted in the war](<https://atarnetwork.com/?p=2097>), reported in July 2024.
