# Uganda

## Justice

**Case management in service.** Uganda's courts run ECCMIS, live since 1 March 2022. [It was in 31 court stations by July 2025](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). In October 2026 [its project manager said it is in more than 56 courts](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/381759/judiciary-to-expand-digital-court-services-to-eight-courts-in-november>). [The Judiciary's 2024/25 report put it in 11.5% of courts](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). It replaced the former CCAS system. A Namibian delegation studied it in May 2025.

**Online filing.** [The Judiciary says cases are filed and fees paid online](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>), with e-filing open at any hour. [Training in 2026 covered case registration and e-payments](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>). The Permanent Secretary to the Judiciary described the service in June 2026.

**Open online.** [A litigant at Jinja High Court checks her case file on her phone](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>), the Judiciary reports. ECCMIS tracks each case through its whole life cycle.
