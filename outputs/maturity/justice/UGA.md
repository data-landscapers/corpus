# Uganda

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [Justice Musa Ssekaana said ECCMIS has been rolled out to 42 court stations within and around Kampala, with Cluster 8 courts including Mbarara High Court to follow in May 2026](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>). [The article describes Uganda's ECCMIS as a platform enabling real-time case tracking and data management, whose knowledge Namibia is sharing under an agreement](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibia-to-implement-e-justice-system-in-supreme-court/>). [The Judiciary of Uganda says its Electronic Court Case Management Information System (ECCMIS) had expanded to 31 court stations across five rollout clusters by July 2025, after going live on 1 March 2022](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>).

**Online filing.** [The Judiciary says ECCMIS gave litigants 24-hour access to e-filing and that cases are filed and fees paid online, per the Permanent Secretary to the Judiciary](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). [Justice Ssekaana said "cases are filed electronically" on ECCMIS, whose training covered case registration and filing and e-payments](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>).

**Open online.** [The Judiciary describes a litigant at Jinja High Court checking updates on her case file on her phone through ECCMIS](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>).
