# Uganda

## Justice

**Case management in service.** [The Judiciary says ECCMIS went live on 1 March 2022 and had reached 31 court stations by July 2025](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). In 2026 Justice Musa Ssekaana said [it had been rolled out to 42 court stations in and around Kampala](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>). In October 2026 the Judiciary's project manager said [ECCMIS had been introduced in more than 56 courts](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/381759/judiciary-to-expand-digital-court-services-to-eight-courts-in-november>). [Namibia is drawing on Uganda's system for real-time case tracking under an agreement](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibia-to-implement-e-justice-system-in-supreme-court/>).

**Online filing.** [The Judiciary says ECCMIS gives litigants round-the-clock e-filing, with cases filed and fees paid online](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). Justice Ssekaana said ["cases are filed electronically" on the system](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>). [Its training for judicial officers covered case registration, filing and e-payments](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/337016/justice-ssekaana-leads-judiciary-digital-push-as-eccmis-training-rolls-out-for-judicial-officers>).

**Open online.** [The Judiciary describes a litigant at Jinja High Court checking her case file through ECCMIS](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>). [She followed its updates on her phone](<https://judiciary.go.ug/files/publications/doc_1783075331_341.pdf>), the Judiciary reported in June 2026.
