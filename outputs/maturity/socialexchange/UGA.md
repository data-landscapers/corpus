# Uganda

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The PDM Information System registers eligible beneficiaries, the Integrated Financial Management Information System transfers funds to PDM SACCO accounts, and Wendi, managed by Pearl Bank, disburses loans to beneficiaries' mobile phones, with 183,430 of 222,389 enterprise groups profiled on PDMIS](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>). [Zombo district officials reported that 31 of 61 PDM tablets are inoperative, tablets that support biometric verification and integration with the Wendi digital wallet, forcing a return to manual workflows in half the district's 61 parishes](<https://chimpreports.com/alarm-raised-as-50-of-zombo-pdm-tablets-fail-to-function/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/2026091868307/ugandas-parish-development-model-reaches-3-5-million-beneficiaries-with-1-1-billion-government-investment.html>).
