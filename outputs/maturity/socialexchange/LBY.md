# Libya

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 10 July 2025 Libya's Minister of Social Affairs, Wafaa al-Kilani, followed up activation of an electronic link for the wife-and-children grant](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>). [The ministry built the link with the Civil Status Authority](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>). [It is meant to keep data accurate and stop delays and duplicate payments](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 the link served the wife-and-children grant](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 the grant's link ran to the Civil Status Authority](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>).
