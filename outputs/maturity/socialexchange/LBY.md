# Libya

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 10 July 2025 Libya's Ministry of Social Affairs (Minister Wafaa al-Kilani) followed up activation of the electronic link of the wife-and-children grant project, built with the Civil Status Authority, to keep data accurate and stop delays and duplicate payments](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=336411&lang=ar>).
