# Mali

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the Caisse Malienne de Sécurité Sociale (CMSS) presented a digital pension payment scheme](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [Its pilot with the Banque de Développement du Mali was judged conclusive](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [The scheme is ready for phased rollout to other partner banks, with payment orders issued digitally through to settlement](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [It follows the CMSS's mobile payment of pensions](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [On 7 May 2026 CANAM launched digital prior-authorisation requests under the AMO](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>). [Health facilities send scanned patient files to CANAM through the AMO Anywhere information system](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>). [CANAM doctors return their decisions through it](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>). [Several facilities have integrated it and others are being deployed](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In June 2023 a World Bank paper said the unified social registry built under the Jigiséméjiri project held nearly 1.2 million households at the project's close](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>). [Several programmes had used it as a common targeting tool](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>). [By March 2018 the RSU held almost 75,000 poor households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>). [It identified beneficiaries of the labour-intensive public works programme, launched in September 2018](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>). [It also identified beneficiaries of the income-generating activities launched in October 2017, with RSU households given priority](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in May 2026 CANAM began exchanging prior authorisations with health facilities](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>).
