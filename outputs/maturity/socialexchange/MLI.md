# Mali

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The document refers to the success of the CMSS's mobile payment of pensions, which the bank payment scheme follows](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>). [CANAM launched on 7 May 2026 digital prior-authorisation requests under the AMO, with health facilities transmitting scanned patient files to CANAM through the AMO Anywhere information system and CANAM doctors returning decisions through it; several facilities have integrated it and others are being deployed](<http://www.canam.ml/index.php/actualite>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank paper says the unified social registry built under the Jigiséméjiri project, holding nearly 1.2 million households at the project's close, has been used by several programs as a common targeting tool (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099515109162536671/pdf/IDU-20b55bfe-10dc-41b4-bcef-06b27137e3ce.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://maliemergenceinfo.com/2026/07/28/%f0%9d%90%8ba-digitalisation-des-services-de-la-sante-et-du-developpement-social-l%f0%9d%90%9a-%f0%9d%90%aa%f0%9d%90%ae%f0%9d%90%9a%f0%9d%90%a5%f0%9d%90%a2%f0%9d%90%ad%f0%9d%90%9e-%f0%9d%90%9d/>).
