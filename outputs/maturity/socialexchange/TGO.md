# Togo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of Planning reports that on 18 December 2025 the government launched the cash-transfer component of the Programme National de Protection Sociale, for more than 700,000 people targeted by INSEED and ANADEB, to be paid at 25,000 FCFA per household through mobile money (Mixx by Yas and Flooz)](<https://planification.gouv.tg/programme-national-de-la-protection-sociale-le-ministre-sandra-lance-loperationnalisation-des-transferts-monetaires/>). [The case study says Togo is building, under a US$72 million IDA-financed WURI programme, a foundational unique identification platform for all residents, to be the basis of a dynamic social registry of individuals and households, both listed as priorities of the 2020-2025 government roadmap (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099751009222330502/pdf/IDU0e83f857301ff1047bf082710a8d21ddf42c3.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [Decree 2023-043/PR of 24 April 2023 creates the RSPM and obliges administrations to base their aid programmes on it and its household scores (art. 14), as a legal duty with no statement that any programme yet draws on it (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source held since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://planification.gouv.tg/programme-national-de-la-protection-sociale-le-ministre-sandra-lance-loperationnalisation-des-transferts-monetaires/>).
