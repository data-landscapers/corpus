# Togo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [Togo's presentation says the RSPM was being integrated in 2025 to link socio-economic data across institutions through the e-ID, over the RSIA](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>). [The Ministry of Planning reports that on 18 December 2025 the government launched the cash-transfer component of the Programme National de Protection Sociale, for more than 700,000 people targeted by INSEED and ANADEB, to be paid at 25,000 FCFA per household through mobile money (Mixx by Yas and Flooz)](<https://planification.gouv.tg/programme-national-de-la-protection-sociale-le-ministre-sandra-lance-loperationnalisation-des-transferts-monetaires/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [Decree 2023-043/PR of 24 April 2023 creates the RSPM and obliges administrations to base their aid programmes on it and its household scores (art. 14), as a legal duty with no statement that any programme yet draws on it (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).
