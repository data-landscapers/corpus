# Togo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 the World Bank said Togo's e-ID is fully interoperable with the RSPM social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032526174532318/pdf/P169594-5fd6786a-2b45-425b-95b6-0e79d792a6fb.pdf>). The registry takes in biographic data and ID numbers from it automatically. The e-ID is also built into the Novissi platform, where it authenticates beneficiaries of ANADEB's cash transfers. [On 18 December 2025 the government launched cash transfers under the national social protection programme](<https://planification.gouv.tg/programme-national-de-la-protection-sociale-le-ministre-sandra-lance-loperationnalisation-des-transferts-monetaires/>), the Ministry of Planning reports. More than 700,000 people targeted by INSEED and ANADEB were to receive 25,000 FCFA per household by mobile money.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [A decree of 24 April 2023 created the RSPM and obliges administrations to base their aid programmes on it](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_04_2023%20-%2068%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B030%20BIS.pdf>) and its household scores. No source says which programmes now draw their beneficiaries from it.

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [The RSPM is linked to the e-ID](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032526174532318/pdf/P169594-5fd6786a-2b45-425b-95b6-0e79d792a6fb.pdf>), the World Bank says. [Togo's 2025 presentation described its integration to link socio-economic data across institutions](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>), but no source names a link to civil registration, health, education or tax.
