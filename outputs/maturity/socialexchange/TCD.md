# Chad

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 10 March 2026 ANATS and WFP discussed linking the RSU with the Registre National Biométrique des Populations](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>). [Every RSU entrant would be identified by the NNI; a memorandum of agreement was still to be signed](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>). [In September 2025 the World Bank described the RSU as interoperable with other systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>). [It listed coordination with UNHCR, CNARR and ANATS and the integration of refugees into the RSU](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>). [It counted 25,000 secure IDs issued to refugees and 684,500 households in the RSU](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 ANATS and WFP discussed identifying every RSU entrant by the NNI](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 a memorandum linking the RSU to the biometric population register was still to be signed](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>).
