# Chad

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 10 March 2026 the ANATS director-general and a WFP Africa Bureau delegation discussed interoperability between the RSU and the Registre National Biométrique des Populations so every RSU entrant is identified by the NNI, with a memorandum of agreement still to be signed](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>). [The World Bank's September 2025 factsheet describes the RSU as interoperable with other systems and lists coordination with UNHCR, CNARR and ANATS and integration of refugees into the RSU among the system-strengthening work, with 25,000 secure IDs issued to refugees and 684,500 households registered in the RSU](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://realitesinfo.com/2026/03/10/securisation-des-aides-sociales-lanats-et-le-pam-sengagent-pour-une-identification-unique-des-beneficiaires/>).
