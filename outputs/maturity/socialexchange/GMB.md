# Gambia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank's November 2025 status report on the RISE project says complementary systems strengthening, including social registry enhancements and interoperability efforts, progressed, without naming the systems linked](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [The framework says MCNHRP payments are made manually, although a pilot test was running to pay beneficiaries by mobile money through a private service provider, Qcell (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>). [The World Bank framework says Gambian social protection agencies operate in silos with little information shared across them, and that the Department of Social Welfare and Community Development hold no functional information systems (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank blog says the Gambia Social Registry (GamSR), covering 203,022 households in 40 of 43 districts, underpinned the Nafa Quick COVID-19 response (78,422 households) and the Nafa cash-transfer programme (16,966 households), and is also used by the National Disaster Management Agency, the ROOTS project and the World Food Programme](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).
