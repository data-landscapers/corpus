# Gambia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In November 2025 a World Bank status report on the RISE project said social registry enhancements and interoperability efforts had progressed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [In 2019 a World Bank framework said Gambian social protection agencies worked separately, with little information shared across them](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>). [The Department of Social Welfare and Community Development had no functional information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>). [MCNHRP payments were made by hand](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>). [A pilot was testing payment by mobile money through Qcell, a private provider](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In April 2024 a World Bank blog said the Gambia Social Registry (GamSR) covered 203,022 households in 40 of 43 districts](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [It underpinned the Nafa Quick COVID-19 response, which reached 78,422 households](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [It also underpinned the Nafa cash-transfer programme, with 16,966 households](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>). [The National Disaster Management Agency, the ROOTS project and the World Food Programme also use it](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/the-gambia-social-registry-a-success-story-in-systems-building>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the World Bank reported progress on interoperability work around the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>).
