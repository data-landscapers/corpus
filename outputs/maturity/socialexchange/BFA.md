# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank Board approved US$120 million on 15 June 2026 for a project that finances integration of the Registre Social Unique with the IDP, health-insurance and other key beneficiary databases](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [The World Bank approved on 15 June 2026 a US$120 million project (US$100m IDA credit, US$20m grant) that will promote integration of the social registry with other databases, including those for internally displaced persons and health insurance](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [The RSU and the internally displaced persons data are managed separately, while coordination efforts are under way](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The social registry is a gateway for various social programs to select their beneficiaries, and that PAMPV economic inclusion beneficiaries will be selected from the RSU database; it states the RSU covers about one-quarter of poor and vulnerable households and is rolled out in 10 regions](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>).
