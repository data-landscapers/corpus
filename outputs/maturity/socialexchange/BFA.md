# Burkina Faso

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 15 June 2026 the World Bank approved US$120 million for a project in Burkina Faso](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [Of that, US$100 million is an IDA credit and US$20 million a grant](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [The project finances integration of the Registre Social Unique with the IDP, health-insurance and other key beneficiary databases](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/07/15/burkina-faso-new-world-bank-financing-to-expand-economic-opportunities-and-strengthen-social-protection-systems>). [In May 2026 the World Bank's appraisal document said the RSU and the data on internally displaced persons are managed separately](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [Coordination efforts between them are under way](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [The project's second component supports interoperable systems, including social registries and government payment platforms](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [In July 2025 the World Bank said work had begun on interoperability between the Social Registry and the databases of internally displaced persons](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The May 2026 appraisal document calls the social registry a gateway for social programmes to select their beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [Beneficiaries of the PAMPV economic inclusion programme will be selected from the RSU database](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [The RSU covers about a quarter of poor and vulnerable households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>). [It is rolled out in 10 regions](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052726095539002/pdf/BOSIB-9b77474f-0575-4c43-a165-babff2ee4d7b.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 that work also covered the WURI unique identifier](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/sahel-adaptive-social-protection-program-trust-fund/country-work/burkina-faso>).
