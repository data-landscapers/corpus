# Seychelles

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [Seychelles Nation reports the Agency for Social Protection's ASP Connect system, launched internally on 15 October 2025, is accessible through the SeyID portal; the free text names no data exchanged with the ID, a payment provider or another programme](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>). [The World Bank reports three interagency information-sharing protocols signed by the Agency for Social Protection, against a baseline of zero in July 2019 and a target of three, with the social registry still at 0% of the 60% beneficiary target (pending the ASP MIS) as at 30 May 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).
