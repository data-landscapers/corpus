# Seychelles

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Agency for Social Protection launched its ASP Connect system internally on 15 October 2025, reached through the SeyID portal](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>). [By 30 May 2025 the agency had signed three interagency information-sharing protocols, meeting the World Bank target](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>). [The social registry stood at 0% of its 60% beneficiary target, pending the agency's MIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest fact: [in October 2025 the Agency for Social Protection launched ASP Connect internally](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [from October 2025 ASP Connect was reached through the SeyID portal](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).
