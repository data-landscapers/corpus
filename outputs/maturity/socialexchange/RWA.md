# Rwanda

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The New Times quotes a Ministry of Local Government official saying the Imibereho registry is integrated with other government databases, including land records and Rwanda Social Security Board salary data, and records tax paid through the Rwanda Revenue Authority](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/37878/news/rwanda/360000-claimsin-new-social-registryhandled-since-2024>). [The paper's results framework counts systems interlinked to the social registry, from 0 in July 2022 to a target of 10 by December 2028, and the additional financing funds improved interoperability of the registry with other systems and MEIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031926174531406/pdf/BOSIB-8f413a7a-fae6-43b1-bf08-93edd39faf53.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The paper says the share of VUP beneficiaries identified through the dynamic social registry rose from 0 to 100 percent by end-February 2026, and that the registry facilitates access to the Ejo Heza long-term savings scheme and community-based health insurance](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031926174531406/pdf/BOSIB-8f413a7a-fae6-43b1-bf08-93edd39faf53.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [The paper says the Imibereho platform is anchored in the national ID system and interoperates with more than 10 government databases, and that Smart Start targeting draws on both health MIS data and the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031926174531406/pdf/BOSIB-8f413a7a-fae6-43b1-bf08-93edd39faf53.pdf>).
