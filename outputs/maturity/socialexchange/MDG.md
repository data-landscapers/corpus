# Madagascar

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In January 2026 a World Bank appraisal-stage document proposed additional financing for Madagascar's Safety Nets and Resilience Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>). [It would fund further work on the FID's information systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>). [The work includes interoperability with the social registry, identification systems and the government interoperability platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the World Bank proposed linking the FID's systems with the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the World Bank proposed linking the FID's systems with identification systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>).
