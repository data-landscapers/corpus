# Madagascar

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank's appraisal-stage document says the proposed additional financing for Madagascar's Safety Nets and Resilience Project would fund continued strengthening of FID information systems, including interoperability with the social registry, identification systems and the government interoperability platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012326100017065/txt/P179466-b0e3c23f-4024-4dae-9418-88897afe4e50.txt>).
