# Malawi

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [A Ministry of Finance principal economist said at the Dynamism in MSR kick-off workshop in Blantyre that data integration will link the Malawi Social Registry directly with existing functional management systems to allow data exchange, with Dowa and Balaka as pilot districts](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>). [UNDP's IDT4M report says NRB generated 1,101,631 QR codes for the Farm Input Subsidy Programme, verified data was uploaded into the FISP application on 12 December 2025, and field testing confirmed readiness for pilot rollout](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank mission reports that as of March 2025 the Unified Beneficiary Registry held 4.2 million households (17 million people) in 27 of 28 rural districts, with five programmes using the data for targeting and research](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).
