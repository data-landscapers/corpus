# Malawi

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [A Ministry of Finance economist said data integration will link the Malawi Social Registry with existing management systems for data exchange](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>). [Dowa and Balaka are the pilot districts](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>). [UNDP says NRB generated 1,101,631 QR codes for the Farm Input Subsidy Programme](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>). [Verified data went into the FISP application on 12 December 2025, and field tests confirmed readiness for a pilot](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank mission reports that as of March 2025 the Unified Beneficiary Registry held 4.2 million households (17 million people) in 27 of 28 rural districts, with five programmes using the data for targeting and research](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 CORE-MIS let programme systems share core modules and beneficiary IDs, the World Bank said](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).
