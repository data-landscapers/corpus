# Malawi

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In May 2026 the government launched a data-sharing app for the Malawi Social Registry, for exchange between institutions](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/giz-malawi_socialprotection-digitaltransformation-activity-7462758236538957824-0WIo>). [WFP Malawi and the GIZ EnDeV programme already use registry data](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/giz-malawi_socialprotection-digitaltransformation-activity-7462758236538957824-0WIo>). [In June 2026 a Ministry of Finance economist said integration will link the registry with existing management systems, piloted in Dowa and Balaka](<https://mwnation.com/ministry-touts-new-social-registry-tool/>). [Verified national ID data went into the Farm Input Subsidy Programme application on 12 December 2025, ready for a pilot](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank mission reports that as of March 2025 the Unified Beneficiary Registry held 4.2 million households (17 million people)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [They were in 27 of 28 rural districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [Five programmes were using the data for targeting and research](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 CORE-MIS let programme systems share core modules and beneficiary IDs, the World Bank said](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).
