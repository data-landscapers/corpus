# Mauritania

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The national social registry coordinator said AOUN cash transfers will be paid under an agreement between Taazour, the Caisse de Depot et de Developpement and GIMTEL](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261586>). [Beneficiaries from the registry are notified electronically and paid to the number they choose](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261586>). [Taazour launched an AOUN verification platform, reached by SMS link from the social registry database](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>). [Registered citizens use it to check eligibility, see their collection code and verify their identity; 2,057,006 were targeted](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In March 2025 the World Bank reported 17 users with signed agreements with the Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>). [The figure was 7 in July 2020](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>). [At least 25 governmental and non-governmental users, including Elmaouna, WFP, UNHCR and UNICEF, use it to select beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 the government signed a convention](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>). [It is between the Social Registry and ANRPTS, the civil registry agency](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>).
