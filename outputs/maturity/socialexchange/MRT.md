# Mauritania

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The national social registry coordinator said the AOUN cash transfers will be paid under a partnership agreement between Taazour, the Caisse de Depot et de Developpement and GIMTEL, with beneficiaries from the registry notified electronically and paid to the number they choose](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/261586>). [Taazour's AOUN verification platform was launched: beneficiaries in the social registry database received SMS messages with a link to the platform to verify eligibility, see their collection code and complete identity verification, targeting 2,057,006 registered citizens](<https://taazour.mr/archives/7559>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank ISR reports 17 users with signed agreements with the Social Registry as of March 2025 (baseline 7 in July 2020), out of at least 25 governmental and non-governmental users, including Elmaouna, WFP, UNHCR and UNICEF, using it to select beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099033125121012030/txt/P171125-e7d2d9de-2891-420a-ac6a-0645e0ccb9c8.txt>).
