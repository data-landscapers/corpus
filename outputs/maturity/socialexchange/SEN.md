# Senegal

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [DGPSN says the information systems of the RNU and the PNBSF are interconnected, with SENUM-SA's technical support, using REST APIs](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>). [The minister of Family, Social Action and Solidarities said at a workshop in Saly that each structure in the social sector has its own information system, which does not always allow data to be shared with other actors, and that a unified system is still to be built ahead of a planned single social window](<https://aps.sn/vers-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique-pour-un-meilleur-acces-aux-services-sociaux/>). [DGPSN says interoperability of the RNU with the CMU and DGAS systems is in progress, with preliminary studies and an action plan done](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).
