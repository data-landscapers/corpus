# Senegal

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In November 2023 DGPSN said the RNU and PNBSF information systems were interconnected through REST APIs](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>). [Links from the RNU to the CMU and DGAS systems were then in progress](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>). [In January 2026 a pilot on the PINS X-Road platform linked the RNU with the CMU health-insurance system, civil status and the national identity card](<https://pins.sn/actualites/2026-01/plateforme-interministerielle-et-interoperable-dechange-de-donnees-du-senegal>). [The pilot enrols PNBSF families in community health insurance free of charge](<https://pins.sn/actualites/2026-01/plateforme-interministerielle-et-interoperable-dechange-de-donnees-du-senegal>). [In July 2026 the social action minister said each social-sector body still runs its own information system, not always sharing data](<https://aps.sn/vers-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique-pour-un-meilleur-acces-aux-services-sociaux/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked in service to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [a January 2026 pilot links the RNU to the national identity card, civil status and health insurance](<https://pins.sn/actualites/2026-01/plateforme-interministerielle-et-interoperable-dechange-de-donnees-du-senegal>).
