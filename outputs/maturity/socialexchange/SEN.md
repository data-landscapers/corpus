# Senegal

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank status report says vulnerable households registered to the RNU by the DGPSN will be targeted for free ReadyBoard connections under the energy access project, with connections expected to begin in early 2027; none connected by 4 December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122225105510242/pdf/P176620-541ea364-1f14-4fe8-8ea3-18cad42ba2cc.pdf>). [The minister of Family, Social Action and Solidarities said at a workshop in Saly that each structure in the social sector has its own information system, which does not always allow data to be shared with other actors, and that a unified system is still to be built ahead of a planned single social window](<https://aps.sn/vers-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique-pour-un-meilleur-acces-aux-services-sociaux/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source held since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122225105510242/pdf/P176620-541ea364-1f14-4fe8-8ea3-18cad42ba2cc.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source held since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122225105510242/pdf/P176620-541ea364-1f14-4fe8-8ea3-18cad42ba2cc.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Programme national de bourses de sécurité familiale](<https://aps.sn/le-premier-ministre-annonce-un-renforcement-des-initiatives-de-protection-sociale/>); [PNBSF beneficiary-management system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/362331655308211699/pdf/Senegal-Adaptive-Safety-Net-Project.pdf>); [Guichet social unique (planned single social window)](<https://aps.sn/vers-la-creation-dun-guichet-unique-pour-un-meilleur-acces-aux-services-sociaux/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says a registry or programme exchanges data in use.
