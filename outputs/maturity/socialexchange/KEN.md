# Kenya

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In February 2026 the cash transfer programme's information system was linked to Civil Registration Services, which flags deceased beneficiaries for exit](<https://chasp.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Feb-Newsletter.pdf>). [National Safety Net Programme payments go from the Enhanced Single Registry to bank accounts and mobile money](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [In September 2026 the State Department for Social Protection was hiring consultants to link the registry with SHA, NSSF, IPRS and civil registration](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127262-govt-moves-link-sha-nssf-social-protection-database>). [The upgrade is funded by the World Bank's KSEIP-2](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127262-govt-moves-link-sha-nssf-social-protection-database>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In August 2025 all four National Safety Net Programme cash transfers used the Enhanced Single Registry](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [They are the HSNP, OPCT, CT-OVC and PwSD-CT, which use it for transfers and, except OPCT, for targeting](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [In February 2026 the registry held data from the core cash transfers and the Hunger Safety Net Programme](<https://chasp.co.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Feb-Newsletter.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [In October 2025 the Health Cabinet Secretary said the registry identified 558,000 indigent households](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-10-30-sha-system-stopped-theft-of-millions-in-fake-claims-duale>). [The Social Health Authority sponsors them](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-10-30-sha-system-stopped-theft-of-millions-in-fake-claims-duale>). [The upgrade that would link the registry to the population register (IPRS) was still being procured in September 2026](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127262-govt-moves-link-sha-nssf-social-protection-database>).
