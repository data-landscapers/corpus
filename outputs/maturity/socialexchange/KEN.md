# Kenya

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [Kenyans.co.ke reported that the State Department for Social Protection and Senior Citizen Affairs invited consultants to upgrade the Enhanced Single Registry and the Community Development Management Information System so they exchange data with SHA, NSSF, IPRS, the National Registration Bureau, Civil Registration Services and cash transfer programmes, under the World Bank-funded KSEIP-2; the assignment is an individual consultancy still being procured](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127262-govt-moves-link-sha-nssf-social-protection-database>). [The plan says the National Safety Net Programme programmes use the Enhanced Single Registry for electronic transfers to bank accounts and mobile money, alongside secure management information systems](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The KSEIP 2 environmental and social management plan says all four National Safety Net Programme cash transfers (HSNP, OPCT, CT-OVC, PwSD-CT) use the Enhanced Single Registry, a national database of poor and vulnerable households, for targeting (except OPCT), transfers and management information systems](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source held since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).
