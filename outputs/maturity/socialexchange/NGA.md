# Nigeria

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2025 NSIPA said NIN and BVN are integrated into the National Social Register](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [NIMC had validated 1,628,587 NINs](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [In November 2026 the ministry said relief recipients are drawn from verified identity records and the register](<https://punchng.com/how-fg-selects-beneficiaries-for-n50000-relief/>). [They are paid to a mobile wallet linked to their NIN](<https://punchng.com/how-fg-selects-beneficiaries-for-n50000-relief/>). [On 25 March 2026 the ministry launched a response system centred on a Unified National Register](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-flags-off-one-humanitarian-one-poverty-response-system-with-un-and-other-partners/>). [It committed to bringing all existing programmes into it](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-flags-off-one-humanitarian-one-poverty-response-system-with-un-and-other-partners/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).
