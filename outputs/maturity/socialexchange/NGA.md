# Nigeria

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [NSIPA says NIN and BVN are integrated into the NSR database, with 1,628,587 NINs validated by NIMC](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>). [The Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction flagged off the One Humanitarian - One Poverty Response System on 25 March 2026, centred on a Unified National Register, and committed to integrating all existing programmes into it](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-flags-off-one-humanitarian-one-poverty-response-system-with-un-and-other-partners/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).
