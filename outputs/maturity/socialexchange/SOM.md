# Somalia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2025 the households for Baxnaano's unconditional cash transfer were identified from the Unified Social Registry](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [The registry selects them by an automated proxy means test](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [Each selected household is given a Social Registry Number, which the programme uses at enrolment](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [The programme thus takes its caseload from the registry's data, in use](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes draw their beneficiaries from one registry. [The unconditional cash transfer is the one programme named as drawing on the Unified Social Registry, in 2025](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and another sector. The nearest held fact: [households are selected from the registry and given a registry number at enrolment](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>).
