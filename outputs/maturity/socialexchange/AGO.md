# Angola

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The ministry (MASFAMU) says the modernisation and expansion phase of the Cadastro Social Único, launched 20 July 2026, promotes interoperability between public institutions and integration of databases](<https://masfamu.gov.ao/home>). [MASFAMU launched the Cadastro Social Único on 20 July 2026 and says it will allow articulation between social programmes, local administration, civil registries, public institutions and sectoral databases](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/angola-lanca-cadastro-social-unico-para-gestao-de-accoes-sociais/>). [The record, citing the World Bank status report of 25 October 2024, says the Cadastro Social Unico was still not operational as of mid-2024 and MASFAMU's SIGAS registry was available in all 54 CASIs in its place](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102524042530084/pdf/P169779177dc6e0f518d2610fb44ed3049d.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://masfamu.gov.ao/home>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://masfamu.gov.ao/home>).
