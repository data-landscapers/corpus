# Lesotho

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [UNICEF reports the tablet-based digital birth registration system under the Ntlafatsa Bana project, launched at Ha-Tankele, is integrated with NISSA, Lesotho's national social assistance database, in real time](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The management information system completed in January 2016 supported integrated social safety nets, naming the Child Grants Programme, the OVC Bursary and Public Assistance; this is the date given in the document for the system, with the programme list as the document's own examples (2016, before the three-year window)](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/274430-request-proposals-national-information-system-social-assistance-nissa-social-registry-and>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>).

***Also on file***. [Digital birth registration system (Ntlafatsa Bana)](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry.
