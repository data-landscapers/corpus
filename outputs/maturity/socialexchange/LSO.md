# Lesotho

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** NISSA, Lesotho's national social assistance database, takes data from birth registration. [In June 2025 UNICEF said a tablet-based birth registration system feeds NISSA in real time](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>). It runs under the Ntlafatsa Bana project, launched at Ha-Tankele. [In April 2024 the ministry sought a consultant to build and test a link between NISSA and the national identity and civil registry](<https://selibeng.com/eoi-lesotho-nissa-systems-development-enhancement-consultancy/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** The only source on shared use is old. [NISSA's system, completed in January 2016, served the Child Grants Programme, the OVC Bursary and Public Assistance](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/274430-request-proposals-national-information-system-social-assistance-nissa-social-registry-and>). Nothing since October 2023 says the same.

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found says NISSA is linked to the national ID and another sector. The nearest facts are [the real-time link with birth registration in 2025](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>) and the planned link to the identity registry.

***Also on file***. [Digital birth registration system (Ntlafatsa Bana)](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry.
