# Algeria

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In April 2026 the CNAS social insurance fund and the CNR pensions fund were exchanging data instantly through the national identification number](<https://www.algerie360.com/securite-sociale-la-cnas-lance-de-nouveaux-services-pour-les-employeurs/>). [The labour ministry said the link between their databases rests on that number](<https://www.elhayatalarabiya.net/fr/?p=23295>). [In March 2026 the Ministry of National Solidarity was testing a unified digital platform in Dar El Beida and Hussein Dey](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>). [The pilot covered the AFS allowance and the card for persons with special needs, with database links still being checked](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest fact found: [in March 2026 the pilot platform served both the AFS allowance and the card for persons with special needs](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact found: [in March 2026 the ministry was verifying the platform's database integration](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).

***Also on file***. [Dzaïr Digital Services](<https://www.algerie360.com/securite-sociale-la-cnas-lance-de-nouveaux-services-pour-les-employeurs/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. None says a registry is linked to another sector.
