# Algeria

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Ministry of National Solidarity said a pilot of its unified digital platform, covering electronic registration for the AFS allowance and the national digital card for persons with special needs, was being tested in Dar El Beida and Hussein Dey until 31 March 2026, with database integration and interconnectivity under verification](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).
