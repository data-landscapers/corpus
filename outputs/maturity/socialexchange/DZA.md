# Algeria

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In March 2026 the Ministry of National Solidarity was testing a pilot of its unified digital platform in Dar El Beida and Hussein Dey](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>). [The pilot covered electronic registration for the AFS allowance and the national digital card for persons with special needs](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>). [It ran until 31 March 2026, with database integration and interconnectivity under verification](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in March 2026 the pilot platform served both the AFS allowance and the card for persons with special needs](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in March 2026 the ministry was verifying the platform's database integration](<https://www.dzair-tube.dz/en/algeria-launches-pilot-digital-services-platform-for-national-solidarity-sector/>).
