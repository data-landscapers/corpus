# Congo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [A flood-preparedness workshop on 27-28 January 2026 reported that survey data collected in five Talangai districts were migrated to the PSIPJ general beneficiary register and that a test payment to a sample of beneficiaries was made through that register](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>). [The arrete states the registre social unique is an interoperable information system whose interoperability terms are set by separate texts, and it obliges social programmes to register beneficiaries served in it and share beneficiary information (articles 3, 17, 18)](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The social affairs minister said at the 10 April 2026 inauguration of the RSU headquarters that the universal health insurance, safety net and insertion programmes now have the RSU as a common source of information](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [Article 8 of the joint arrete of 20 August 2024 says the registre social unique holds data drawn from the civil-status information systems and the unique identification number, among other sources](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>).
