# Congo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [A flood-preparedness workshop on 27-28 January 2026 reported that survey data collected in five Talangai districts were migrated to the PSIPJ general beneficiary register and that a test payment to a sample of beneficiaries was made through that register](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The social affairs minister said at the 10 April 2026 inauguration of the RSU headquarters that the universal health insurance, safety net and insertion programmes now have the RSU as a common source of information](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source held since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>).
