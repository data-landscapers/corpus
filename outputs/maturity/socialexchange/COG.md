# Congo

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [A flood-preparedness workshop on 27-28 January 2026 reported that survey data collected in five Talangai districts were migrated to the PSIPJ general beneficiary register](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>). [It reported that a test payment to a sample of beneficiaries was made through that register](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601290559.html>). [The arrete states the registre social unique is an interoperable information system](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>). [Its interoperability terms are set by separate texts](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>). [It obliges social programmes to register beneficiaries served in it and share beneficiary information (articles 3, 17, 18)](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The social affairs minister spoke at the inauguration of the RSU headquarters on 10 April 2026](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>). [The minister said the universal health insurance, safety net and insertion programmes now have the RSU as a common source of information](<https://affaires-sociales.gouv.cg/2026/04/14/le-registre-social-unique-du-congo-dispose-dun-siege-approprie/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [Article 8 of the joint arrete of 20 August 2024 covers the data held in the registre social unique](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>). [They are drawn from the civil-status information systems and the unique identification number, among other sources](<https://liziba.cg/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Arrete-n-18503-du-20-aout-2024-fixant-les-regles-de-collecte-de-traitement-dechange-et-dactualisation-des-donnees-du-registre-social-unique.pdf>).
