# Zambia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In November 2025 a World Bank project paper said procurement is under way for a gender-based violence (GBV) management information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). [The system is to be integrated with the GBV call centre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). [It may also be integrated with other programme MISs and the national Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the planned GBV system might be joined to other programme MISs](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the GBV system might be joined to the national Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>).
