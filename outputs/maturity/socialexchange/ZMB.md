# Zambia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank project paper says procurement is ongoing for a GBV management information system to be integrated with the GBV call center and possibly other programme MISs and the national Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111025101519517/pdf/P181391-34653b79-f98e-4c0c-a842-18e4bab01cb6.pdf>).
