# Mozambique

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In August 2026 the disaster agency INGD said its WFP-funded beneficiary management system (SGBAH) was in field pilot ahead of an October 2026 launch](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/mozambiques-digital-humanitarian-response-system-set-for-october-launch>). [It will be interoperable with the database of INAS, the social action institute that supports vulnerable households](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/mozambiques-digital-humanitarian-response-system-set-for-october-launch>). [INGD said the register was being tested in Beira, at a cost WFP put at about 20 million meticais](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-aid-recipient-digital-register-to-be-launched-to-prevent-duplication/>). [Linked with INAS's database, the two will show who has received food aid](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-aid-recipient-digital-register-to-be-launched-to-prevent-duplication/>).
