# Mozambique

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [Mozambique's disaster agency INGD says its WFP-funded Beneficiary and Humanitarian Assistance Management System (SGBAH), in field pilot and validated ahead of an October 2026 national launch, will be interoperable with the database of the social action institute INAS, which runs support programmes for vulnerable households](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/mozambiques-digital-humanitarian-response-system-set-for-october-launch>). [Mozambique's INGD says its digital register of humanitarian aid recipients, being tested in Beira and due to launch in October 2026 at a WFP-stated cost of about 20 million meticais, is to interoperate with the database of the social action institute INAS so the two show who has received food aid](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-aid-recipient-digital-register-to-be-launched-to-prevent-duplication/>).
