# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In May 2026 the World Bank said the Emergency Food Crisis Response Project's registration system will consolidate the parent project's beneficiary databases](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>). [It works with the Ministry of Agriculture and the National Statistical Institute](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>). [It will cross-check the data against existing registries to reduce duplication](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>). [A World Bank appraisal says the Unified Social Registry (RSU), begun in 2018, is delayed](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/11/WB-P179211_5aXsjNN.pdf>). [The Safety Nets Project will fund its management information system](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/11/WB-P179211_5aXsjNN.pdf>). [Household data from a beneficiary operations system will feed the RSU, beside a digital payment platform for safety net programmes](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/11/WB-P179211_5aXsjNN.pdf>).
