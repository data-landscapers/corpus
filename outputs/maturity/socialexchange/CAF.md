# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank project paper says the Emergency Food Crisis Response Project's beneficiary registration system will consolidate and update the parent project's beneficiary databases, with the Ministry of Agriculture and the National Statistical Institute, and cross-check them against existing registries to reduce duplication](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>). [The World Bank appraisal says the Unified Social Registry (RSU), begun in 2018, is delayed, and the Safety Nets Project will fund its management information system, with a beneficiary operations system (BOMS) whose household data will be fed into the RSU and a digital payment platform for safety net programmes](<https://ewsdata.rightsindevelopment.org/files/documents/11/WB-P179211_5aXsjNN.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060226181018944/pdf/BOSIB-b67bf62d-5670-4d2e-9c1c-d85c2fdb544c.pdf>).
