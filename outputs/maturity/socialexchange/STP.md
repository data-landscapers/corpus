# Sao Tome and Principe

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2025 Programa Família Vulnerável paid 5,000 households by bank transfer through the Ké Non payments module](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>), the DPSSF says. [Over STN 35.75 million was paid through October, with BISTP as the bank](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>). [The World Bank said in 2025 that Ke-Non, deployed in 2024, is integrated with the Unique Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>). [The registry holds data on 8,740 families, about 16 percent of the population](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In 2025 the DPSSF described Kénon as one database on vulnerable families and the beneficiaries of its programmes](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/explorar/recursos-uteis/sobre-kenon>). [It produces reports for both PEP+ and Programa Família](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/explorar/recursos-uteis/sobre-kenon>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. [In 2024 openIMIS said Ke-Non uses the civil registry to identify clients and BISTP to pay them](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>).

***Also on file***. [Electronic cash transfer payment system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector.
