# Sao Tome and Principe

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The DPSSF states that Programa Família Vulnerável payments to 5,000 households in the first five cycles of 2025, over STN 35.75 million through October, were made by bank transfer using the payments module of the Ké Non system, with BISTP named as the bank](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/noticias-g-1/comunicados-oficiais/governo-de-stp-injeta-mais-de-35-milhoes-de-dobras-na-economia-nacional-atraves-do-programa-familias-vulneraveis-em-2025>). [The World Bank project paper states that the open-source social protection MIS Ke-Non, deployed in 2024, is integrated with the Unique Social Registry, which holds socio-demographic data on 8,740 families (about 16 percent of the population), and that the MIS tracks beneficiaries, services and payments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The DPSSF page describes Kénon as a unified database centralising data on vulnerable families and beneficiaries of programmes such as Programa Família, and as producing reports for both PEP+ and Programa Família; it names no exchange with the ID, civil registration or other government systems](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/explorar/recursos-uteis/sobre-kenon>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dpssf.gov.st/index.php/explorar/recursos-uteis/sobre-kenon>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector.
