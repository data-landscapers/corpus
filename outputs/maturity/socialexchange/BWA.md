# Botswana

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2022 the World Bank said the social registry is linked to OMANG, the national ID, for validation](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [By March 2025, 98 per cent of beneficiaries of the five main poverty-targeted social programmes had been updated in the Single Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>). [The World Bank's completion report set the target at 75.8 per cent](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>). [By May 2021 beneficiary data for at least five programmes had been digitised into the registry in four districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>). [It had been uploaded in six districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in 2022 the World Bank said the registry validates its records against OMANG](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [OMANG](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>); [Ipelegeng MIS (customised generic MIS)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>).
