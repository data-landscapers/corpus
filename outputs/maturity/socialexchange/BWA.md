# Botswana

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank review says the social registry is linked to the national ID system (OMANG) for validation (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank's completion report says 98 percent of beneficiaries of the five main poverty-targeted social programmes were updated in the Single Social Registry by March 2025, against a 75.8 percent target](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121825122532143/txt/BOSIB-35f31917-1454-433b-a021-4d7246073f71.txt>).
