# Cameroon

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** Cameroon's health ministry said in November 2026 that its interoperability platform [links universal health coverage enrolment and billing](<https://scores2000.info/2026/11/sante-numerique-le-cameroun-accelere-l-interconnexion-de-ses-systemes-d-information-sanitaires.html>). The platform runs OpenHIM and OpenFn on a CAMTEL server. In October 2025 Cameroon was [assessing its ID, civil registration and disability systems for linking to the Unified Social Registry](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>), the Digital Convergence Initiative said. A 2021 World Bank appraisal [plans that registry under the social affairs ministry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099150001062231657/pdf/Project0Inform0n0Project000P175363.pdf>). It is to merge four databases. They belong to the safety nets project, the cash for education programme, the social insurance fund and the World Food Programme.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes draw on one registry. The nearest held fact: the Unified Social Registry [is to merge four existing beneficiary databases](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099150001062231657/pdf/Project0Inform0n0Project000P175363.pdf>), a 2021 World Bank appraisal says.

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 links a registry to the ID and another sector. The nearest held fact: in October 2025 Cameroon was [assessing its ID and civil registration systems](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>) for linking to the registry.
