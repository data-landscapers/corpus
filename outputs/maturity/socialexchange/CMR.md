# Cameroon

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Digital Convergence Initiative says Cameroon is assessing its Common ID System, Civil Registration System and Disability Registry to explore their integration with the Unified Social Registry (RESUC) MIS](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>). [The World Bank appraisal document says the Unified Social Registry information system, to be built under MINAS with project funds, will consolidate the existing databases of the Social Safety Nets Project, the Cash for Education Program, the Caisse Nationale de Prevoyance Sociale and the World Food Programme, with a pilot registry running since 2015 in 15 communes and 64,424 individuals (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099150001062231657/pdf/Project0Inform0n0Project000P175363.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>).
