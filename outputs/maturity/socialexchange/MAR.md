# Morocco

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2024 the RSU checked each household's declared data against over 30 administrative databases](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>), the Ministry of Interior says. [Monthly electronic exchanges let managing bodies check that beneficiaries are still eligible](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>), the government said in November 2025. [The ANSS annual report says the RNP and the RSU were crossed in 2025 to target direct aid](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>). [A World Bank project for 2024 to 2028 funds RNP authentication for use across social programmes](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>). [A 2026 deck says the RNP's authentication service checks what applicants declare for six social programmes, the RSU among them](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In September 2025 the government reported 5.3 million families in the RSU](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [RSU families received the school-start aid, over 1.8 million of them, and are the base of direct social aid, about 4 million](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [In November 2025 the government called the register the reference framework for social support](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). [That covers direct cash transfers and basic health insurance for those unable to pay](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** The RSU is tied to the national population register, the RNP. [The two were crossed in 2025 to target direct aid](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>). No source found says the registry is linked to civil registration, health, education or tax records.
