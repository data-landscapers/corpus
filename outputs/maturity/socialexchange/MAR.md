# Morocco

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank-financed second project (2024-2028, US$70 million) supports RNP digital authentication and eKYC services to be used across social programmes and the national rollout of the RSU as the gateway for all social safety net programmes](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>). [The World Bank approved US$70 million for a second project extending the RNP and RSU to rural and remote areas through digital and remote applications](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/04/world-bank-greenlights-70m-boost-for-morocco-s-innovative-social-protection-delivery-systems>). [The RSU information system registers households through automated verification of the head of household's declared data against more than 30 administrative databases via the government interoperability platform, per the Ministry of Interior document](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The government commission reported 5.3 million families enrolled in the RSU, and that the RSU-registered families received the exceptional school-start aid (over 1.8 million families) and are the base for the direct social aid programme (about 4 million families)](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>).
