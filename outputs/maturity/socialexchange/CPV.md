# Cape Verde

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** No source found says social protection systems exchanging data is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The Government of Cabo Verde states that the Cadastro Social Único is the base of the country's social support system, and that a registered eligible household can access targeted support including cash transfers, food security support and resilience programmes](<https://www.governo.cv/apelo-ao-registo-no-cadastro-social-unico-csu-para-agregados-familiares-em-zonas-climaticamente-vulneraveis/>). A later answer places the country above that step.
