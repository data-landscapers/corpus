# Cape Verde

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** No source found says social protection systems exchanging data is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [in 2018 the ministry said the CSU will be the reference database for municipalities and utilities](<https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In June 2026 the government said the Cadastro Social Único is the base of the country's social support system](<https://www.governo.cv/apelo-ao-registo-no-cadastro-social-unico-csu-para-agregados-familiares-em-zonas-climaticamente-vulneraveis/>). [A registered eligible household can access cash transfers, food security support and resilience programmes](<https://www.governo.cv/apelo-ao-registo-no-cadastro-social-unico-csu-para-agregados-familiares-em-zonas-climaticamente-vulneraveis/>). [In February 2024 the minister said about 16 social programmes draw beneficiaries from the CSU](<https://www.governo.cv/governo-lanca-mecanismo-de-gestao-das-informacoes-e-reclamacoes-do-cadastro-social-unico/>). [Decreto Regulamentar 7/2018 created the CSU](<https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico>). [The ministry says registration is the prior condition for social water and energy tariffs, school transport and kits, higher-education fee support and health user-fee exemption](<https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in 2018 the CSU was to serve the health, education, housing and family ministries](<https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico>).
