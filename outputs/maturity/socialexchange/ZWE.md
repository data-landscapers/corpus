# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Minister of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare said ZISO, the social registry now being developed, will be used as part of the mechanism to identify beneficiaries of the BEAM education assistance programme, to be rolled out across 65 districts](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).
