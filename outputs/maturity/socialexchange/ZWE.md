# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In July 2026 the Minister of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare said ZISO, the social registry being developed, will help identify beneficiaries](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>). [It will serve the BEAM education assistance programme](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>). [BEAM is to be rolled out across 65 districts](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 ZISO was still being developed](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the minister tied ZISO to the education sector through BEAM](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).
