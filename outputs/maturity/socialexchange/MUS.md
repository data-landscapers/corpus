# Mauritius

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In February 2026 Cabinet said Internet Gratis, for households on the Social Register of Mauritius (SRM), is operational](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>). [A State Informatics platform lets the three internet service providers check whether an applicant is on the SRM database](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The same Cabinet record keys Internet Gratis to the Social Register](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>). [The measure reaches over 7,000 families and nearly 31,000 persons, at Rs85 million a year](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>). [A wastewater-fee exemption for subscribers using six cubic metres or less is also keyed to the register](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>). [In 2024-2025, 7,336 households eligible on the SRM drew the Subsistence Allowance and other schemes, the Social Integration Division reported](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/9-1%20Social%20Integration/SOCIAL%20INTEGRATION%20DIVISION%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%202024-2025.pdf>). [These households held 28,722 beneficiaries](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/9-1%20Social%20Integration/SOCIAL%20INTEGRATION%20DIVISION%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%202024-2025.pdf>). [The other schemes include a crèche allowance, a diaper grant, a school materials grant and a tertiary fee waiver](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/9-1%20Social%20Integration/SOCIAL%20INTEGRATION%20DIVISION%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%202024-2025.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in February 2026 a State Informatics platform linked the register to internet service providers](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>).
