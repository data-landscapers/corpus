# Mauritius

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [Cabinet notes the Internet Gratis measure for Social Register of Mauritius households is operational, with a State Informatics platform letting the three internet service providers verify whether an applicant is on the SRM database](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [Cabinet records Internet Gratis (over 7,000 families, nearly 31,000 persons, Rs85 million a year) and a wastewater-fee exemption for subscribers of six cubic metres or less as both keyed to the Social Register of Mauritius](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2006%20February%202026.pdf>).
