# Djibouti

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 14 March 2025 the World Bank approved the US$31 million Integrated Community Resilience Project](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>). [One component scales Djibouti's national Social Registry with an ANSIE interoperability upgrade](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>). [The registry held 515,730 people in February 2025, against a target of 600,000](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>). [In July 2026 a director general said an interoperability system is being built](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [It will let social protection bodies, among others, verify national ID cards in real time](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [Decree 2020-137, of July 2020, makes the Registre Social the sole targeting tool for beneficiaries of social programmes](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-137-pr-mass-portant-modification-du-decret-n2017-311-pr-seas-du-28-septembre-2017-portant-creation-organisation-et-fonctionnement-du-registre-social/>). [The registry is an information system holding data on poor households](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-137-pr-mass-portant-modification-du-decret-n2017-311-pr-seas-du-28-septembre-2017-portant-creation-organisation-et-fonctionnement-du-registre-social/>). [The decree's aim is to coordinate and monitor aid](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-137-pr-mass-portant-modification-du-decret-n2017-311-pr-seas-du-28-septembre-2017-portant-creation-organisation-et-fonctionnement-du-registre-social/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 a planned system would let social protection bodies check ID cards](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>).
