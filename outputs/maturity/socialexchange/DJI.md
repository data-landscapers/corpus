# Djibouti

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The director general says an interoperability system is being worked on that will let social protection bodies, among others, verify a national ID card's authenticity in real time](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [The World Bank's record of the US$31 million Integrated Community Resilience Project, approved 14 March 2025, funds a component scaling Djibouti's national Social Registry (515,730 people registered as of February 2025, target 600,000) with an ANSIE interoperability upgrade](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [Decree 2020-137 makes the Registre Social, an information system holding data on poor households, the sole targeting tool for beneficiaries of social programmes, to coordinate and monitor aid (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-137-pr-mass-portant-modification-du-decret-n2017-311-pr-seas-du-28-septembre-2017-portant-creation-organisation-et-fonctionnement-du-registre-social/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>).

***Also on file***. [ANSIE](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P506969>).
