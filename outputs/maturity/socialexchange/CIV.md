# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026 to interconnect the RSU with ONECI databases and the RNPP for unique identification of beneficiaries and removal of duplicates](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). [A 7-11 September 2025 workshop at Grand-Bassam, run by the Ministry of National Cohesion through the Filets Sociaux programme office, aims to update the Registre Social Unique's interoperability framework for data sharing between SIG-RSU and CNAM, CNPS, CGRAE, ONECI, WURI and DOB](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/atelier-sur-linteroperabilite-des-systemes-dinformation-pour-renforcer-la-protection-sociale-en-cote-divoire/>). [The national interoperability platform, begun in September 2025, is to connect 12 administrations in its first phase, among them the social security funds CNPS and CGRAE and the identity office ONECI; five had joined by July 2026](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).
