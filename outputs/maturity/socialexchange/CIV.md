# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). [It is to interconnect the RSU with ONECI databases and the RNPP for unique identification of beneficiaries and removal of duplicates](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). [In September 2025 a workshop at Grand-Bassam set out to update the Registre Social Unique's interoperability framework](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/atelier-sur-linteroperabilite-des-systemes-dinformation-pour-renforcer-la-protection-sociale-en-cote-divoire/>). [It covered data sharing between SIG-RSU and CNAM, CNPS, CGRAE, ONECI, WURI and DOB](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/atelier-sur-linteroperabilite-des-systemes-dinformation-pour-renforcer-la-protection-sociale-en-cote-divoire/>). [The national interoperability platform began in September 2025](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>). [Its first phase is to connect 12 administrations, among them the social security funds CNPS and CGRAE and the identity office ONECI](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>). [Five had joined by July 2026](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 BCPFS and ONECI agreed to interconnect the RSU with ONECI's databases to remove duplicate beneficiaries](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [on 2 April 2026 BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention to interconnect the RSU with the RNPP](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).
