# Ghana

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [MoGCSP's terms of reference say its social protection programmes and other systems operate in silos with data dispersed across systems, limited interoperability and each programme running its own servers, and seek a firm to build a unified data exchange platform linking them to the National ID Registry](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>). [At a Technical Working Group meeting on 29 January 2026, NDPC said fragmented data management limits sharing across Ghana's social protection systems, and the project consultants said the several platforms remain poorly linked with limited access across institutions, particularly at sub-national level](<https://ndpc.gov.gh/media_center/blog_details/ndpc-and-mogcsp-support-giz-convene-technical-working-group-strengthen-social-protection-data-coordination>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The Ghana National Household Registry page describes a single national database that serves as the primary platform for identifying and targeting beneficiaries of LEAP, school feeding and national health insurance (NHIS) enrolment](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>).
