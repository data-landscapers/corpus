# Ghana

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** The Ghana National Household Registry exchanges data with other government systems. [Its July 2026 privacy policy says it links its data with the identity, health insurance and education authorities](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>). It also shares anonymised data with other ministries. The programmes' own systems are less joined up. [In August 2026 the social protection ministry said its programmes run in silos, each on its own servers](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>). It sought a firm to build a common data exchange platform.

**One registry shared by programmes.** Several programmes draw their beneficiaries from the registry. [The registry describes itself as the main platform for targeting LEAP, school feeding and health insurance enrolment](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/registry.php>). [Its 2026 privacy policy names the same three programmes](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** The registry is tied to the national ID and to health and education. [It holds each person's Ghana Card number, the July 2026 policy says](<https://gnhr.mogcsp.gov.gh/privacy.php>). It links its data with the National Identification Authority, the National Health Insurance Authority and the Ghana Education Service.
