# Burundi

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In January 2026 a World Bank blog said the CORE-MIS of the Merankabandi (Cash for Jobs) programme connects with several payment service providers](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/burundi-cash-for-jobs-project-is-embracing-smart-it-for-social-impact>). [This eases payment processing and reconciliation](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/burundi-cash-for-jobs-project-is-embracing-smart-it-for-social-impact>). [Field data collected with KoboToolbox flows directly into the system](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/burundi-cash-for-jobs-project-is-embracing-smart-it-for-social-impact>). [It was used to deliver emergency cash transfers to over 30,000 beneficiaries](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/burundi-cash-for-jobs-project-is-embracing-smart-it-for-social-impact>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In May 2026 the World Bank said the Merankabandi social registry is also used by the Soleil-Nyakiriza solar energy project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>). [The project, which the World Bank finances, uses it to reach vulnerable households for subsidised solar kits](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>).
