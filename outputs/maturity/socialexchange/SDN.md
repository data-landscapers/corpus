# Sudan

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Sudan Family Support Project was approved on 8 October 2020 and closed on 30 June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>). [It disbursed US$181.4 million of the US$760 million committed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>). [A World Bank completion report of February 2025 records that the project's MIS had a data-sharing platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>). [The platform was linked to payment providers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the MIS served the Sudan Family Support Project, which closed in June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 the World Bank recorded a platform link to the National Civil Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).
