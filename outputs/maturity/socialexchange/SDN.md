# Sudan

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank's completion report dated 28 February 2025 is catalogued as stating that the project MIS held a data-sharing platform linked to the National Civil Registry via API and to payment providers, but the captured text stops before that narrative, so the date used is the report's published date](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>). [The record states the Sudan Family Support Project, approved 8 October 2020 and closed 30 June 2024 with US$181.4 million disbursed of US$760 million committed, had an MIS with a data-sharing platform linked to the National Civil Registry via API and to payment providers (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).
