# Ethiopia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In March 2024 the World Bank said it was supporting a government pilot paying safety-net benefits through Telebirr mobile money](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). The pilot ran in two selected regions. [The rural Productive Safety Net Program and the urban programme pay benefits into bank accounts](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). No source found says either programme's information system exchanges data with a bank, a mobile money provider or the national ID in use.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found says two or more programmes draw their beneficiaries from one shared social registry. [The nearest held fact is the Telebirr payment pilot](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>), which concerns how benefits are paid.

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found says a social registry is linked to the national ID or to civil registration, health, education or tax. [The nearest held fact is the Telebirr payment pilot](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). Fayda, the national ID, is on file separately.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123090519104/pdf/P1790401f9447f0c8185031717ad18a7a00.pdf>); [Productive Safety Net Program (PSNP)](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>); [Urban Productive Safety Net and Jobs Program (UPSNJP)](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>).

***Not found***. No source says a programme's information system exchanges data with a payment provider or the ID in use. Nothing is known of a shared registry or a link to other sectors.
