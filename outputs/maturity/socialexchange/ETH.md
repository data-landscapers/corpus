# Ethiopia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In 2025 OpenG2P said the relief programme's social registry, run for the Ethiopian Disaster Risk Management Commission, was seeded from Fayda, the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>). [Fayda is integrated with the registry to keep its records clean](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>). [In March 2024 the World Bank said it was supporting a government pilot paying safety-net benefits through Telebirr mobile money](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/leveraging-e-payments-financial-inclusion-ethiopia-afe-0324>). The pilot ran in two selected regions.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found says two or more programmes draw their beneficiaries from one shared social registry. [The nearest held fact is the relief programme's registry, which serves that one programme](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** [In 2025 the relief programme's registry drew on Fayda, the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S2-OpenG2P-Final.pdf>). No source found says a social registry is linked to civil registration, health, education or tax.
