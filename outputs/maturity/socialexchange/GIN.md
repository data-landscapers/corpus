# Guinea

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [UNICEF's representative said the RSU report is a step towards a progressively built integrated register of persons to be interoperable with other government databases and linkable to a unique national identification number](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [The FDSI director general said RSU data have already served as the beneficiary base for the ANIES and IMF Guichet Choc Alimentaire project, among other partnerships](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [On 27 June 2024 the ANIES and FDSI directors general signed a protocol for ANIES to use secured RSU data for the IMF-financed Guichet Choc Alimentaire project, aimed at 51,259 indigent households](<https://anies.gov.gn/guichet-choc-alimentaire-lanies-et-le-fdsi-signent-un-protocole-daccord/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [On 8 July 2026 the FDSI director general said RSU data had been used by the ANIES and IMF Guichet Choc Alimentaire project](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [UNICEF used them to follow malnourished children, and SOS Villages d'Enfants for family-strengthening](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [Under its decree the RSU is the single entry point of social interventions](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 UNICEF's representative said the register is to be linkable](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>). [The link is to a unique national identification number](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/guinee-le-fdsi-presente-le-rapport-danalyse-du-registre-social-unifie-qui-cartographie-977-610-menages-soit-4-860-075-individus-a-travers-le-pays/>).
