# Egypt

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank says all Takaful and Karama beneficiaries are automatically linked with the food subsidy programme and universal health insurance](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). [The World Bank says all TKP beneficiaries receive Meeza debit cards for digital payments or ATM cash-out, and 100 percent are paid by electronic means as of December 2023](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [The World Bank says cash-plus interventions (FORSA, family planning with UNFPA, Waii with UNDP, the 1,000 days programme with WFP) are built on and target beneficiaries from the TKP social registry, which holds about 11.3 million registered households (40.5 million individuals)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).
