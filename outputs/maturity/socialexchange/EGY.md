# Egypt

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** Egypt's national social registry exchanges data with other government systems. [In November 2026 a Ministry of Social Solidarity official said it draws on 27 government databases through the national ID number](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). The exchange checks applicants' data and sharpens targeting. [The World Bank says Takaful and Karama beneficiaries are linked automatically to the food subsidy and universal health insurance](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). [By December 2023 all of them were paid electronically, on Meeza debit cards](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** Several programmes draw their beneficiaries from one registry. [The World Bank says cash-plus schemes run with UNFPA, UNDP and WFP target people from the Takaful and Karama registry](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). In 2024 it held about 11.3 million households, or 40.5 million people.

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** The registry uses the national ID number to exchange data, [the ministry said in November 2026](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). It did not name the 27 databases. No source found says the registry is linked to civil registration, health, education or tax records.

***Also on file***. [Meeza card](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>); [TKP management information system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

***Not found***. No source found names a link from the registry to civil registration, health, education or tax records.
