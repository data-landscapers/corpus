# Eswatini

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The World Bank project information document says the Imbeleko registry will be financed with an MIS with interoperability mechanisms, and that registry data will target beneficiaries of the project's youth income component, starting with Shiselweni's first cycle](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>).
