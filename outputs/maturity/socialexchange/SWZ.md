# Eswatini

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In March 2025 a World Bank project information document said the project will finance an MIS with interoperability mechanisms for the Imbeleko registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>). [Registry data will target beneficiaries of the project's youth income component](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>). [Targeting starts with the first cycle in Shiselweni](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [in March 2025 the World Bank planned to target a youth income programme from the Imbeleko registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in March 2025 the World Bank said the Imbeleko MIS will have interoperability mechanisms](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031525010023201/pdf/P507360-3e4a9889-8b21-4fe3-a3f0-ed133ffbd8ff.pdf>).
