# Namibia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [In June 2026 the finance and gender ministries said 15,825 old-age grants were suspended](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>). [The beneficiaries' names had not been verified in the National Population Registry System](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>). [The grant list is checked against the population registry](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>). [On 17 June 2026 the first live government-to-person grant payments went through Namibia's instant payment network](<https://technews.com.na/2026/06/17/namibia-moves-government-grant-payments-onto-instant-payment-network/>). [Bank Windhoek paid beneficiaries in the Marginalised Beneficiaries grant category in a controlled go-live](<https://technews.com.na/2026/06/17/namibia-moves-government-grant-payments-onto-instant-payment-network/>). [Instant Payments Namibia, a Bank of Namibia subsidiary, operates the network](<https://www.marketwatch.com.na/market-watch/bank-windhoek-letshego-go-live-on-wayame-network-nmh012436-3015-15164>). [Letshego Bank Namibia was certified to receive grant payments on it](<https://www.marketwatch.com.na/market-watch/bank-windhoek-letshego-go-live-on-wayame-network-nmh012436-3015-15164>). [A gender ministry spokesperson said the ministry plans to join its system to the Ministry of Home Affairs' system, to improve reporting of deaths](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>). [By June 2022 the ministry had signed memoranda for data integration with GIPF, SSC, IFMS and the Veteran Affairs System](<https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/>). [In March 2021 Cabinet adopted a policy saying there was no integrated management information system for social protection](<https://www.civic264.org.na/images/pdf/2022/4/Social_Protection_Policy_2022_Final.pdf>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [In June 2022 the ministry said a central information management system held all social grant beneficiaries](<https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/>). [It was built to remove duplication, and the activity was marked complete](<https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [in June 2022 the ministry also listed a data integration agreement with the tax system](<https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/>).
