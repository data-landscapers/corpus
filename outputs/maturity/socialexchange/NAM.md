# Namibia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The finance and gender ministries confirmed in a joint notice that 15,825 old-age grants were suspended because beneficiary names were unverified in the National Population Registry System, so the grant beneficiary list is checked against the population registry](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>). [The first live government-to-person grant payments went through Namibia's instant payment network on 17 June 2026, to beneficiaries in the Marginalised Beneficiaries grant category, via Bank Windhoek, in a controlled go-live](<https://technews.com.na/2026/06/17/namibia-moves-government-grant-payments-onto-instant-payment-network/>), and [Letshego Bank Namibia was certified to receive grant payments on the same network, WayaMe](<https://www.marketwatch.com.na/market-watch/bank-windhoek-letshego-go-live-on-wayame-network-nmh012436-3015-15164>). [A gender ministry spokesperson said the ministry is planning to incorporate its system with that of the Ministry of Home Affairs for improved reporting of deaths](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** [A central information management system was developed holding all social grant beneficiaries, to remove duplication, with the activity at 100 percent and Complete (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://hppii.gov.na/activities/activity-2-consolidation-of-social-grants-and-the-food-bank/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>).
