# Tunisia

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [The Khadamet app, which works with the digital identity, lets users track CNAM reimbursements and prior approvals, view CNSS career records and get CNRPS attestations, as presented by the ministry's Ahmed Megdich on 2 September 2026; the article says real interconnection of administrations is yet to be shown](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/09/02/khadamet-ladministration-en-un-clic/>). [Per the ministry's Ahmed Megdich, the first version of Khadamet gives secured access via digital identity to CNAM reimbursement tracking and CNSS services](<https://radioexpressfm.com/ar/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%ae%d8%a8%d8%a7%d8%b1/%d8%ae%d8%af%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%aa/>). [The Ministry of Communication Technologies launched the first version of Khadamet on 31 August 2026, giving digital-identity access to CNAM reimbursement and CNSS career and declaration services among others](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%86%D9%88%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA>).

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/09/02/khadamet-ladministration-en-un-clic/>).
