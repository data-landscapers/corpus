# Gabon

## Interoperability of social protection systems

**Social protection systems exchanging data.** [On 28 April 2026 the tax office, CNSS and CNAMGS began work on one wage declaration](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>). All three would read the same form. It was to operate from July 2026. [Gabon's amended World Bank financing, from September 2026, pays to join the CNAM-GS registry to the interoperability platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>). The registry is also to be linked to the RBPP identity register.

**One registry shared by programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says two or more programmes share one registry. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>).

**Linked to the ID and another sector.** No source found since October 2023 says the registry is linked to the national ID and to another sector. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>).
