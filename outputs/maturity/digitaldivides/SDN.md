# Sudan

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [TPRA says the Universal Service Fund exists to provide telecom services in remote and rural areas](<https://newsite.tpra.gov.sd/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9/>). [It also spreads postal services in rural areas](<https://newsite.tpra.gov.sd/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9/>). [It may set its own plans and programmes to bridge the digital divide](<https://newsite.tpra.gov.sd/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9/>). [In 2022 Paradigm Initiative reported that the fund, set up in 2018, had agreements with Zain and MTN](<https://paradigmhq.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Sudan-Londa-2022.pdf>). [They were to cover 93 of 168 uncovered areas, with obstacles to delivery](<https://paradigmhq.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Sudan-Londa-2022.pdf>).
