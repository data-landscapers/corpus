# Sudan

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [TPRA states the Universal Service Fund exists to provide telecom services in remote areas, bridge the digital divide and spread communications and postal services in rural areas, with power to set plans and programmes](<https://newsite.tpra.gov.sd/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9/>). [Paradigm Initiative reports that Sudan's Universal Service Fund, set up in 2018, has TPRA framework agreements with Zain-SD to cover 83 uncovered areas and MTN-Sudan 10, out of 168 economically non-viable areas identified, though it faces implementation obstacles (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://paradigmhq.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Sudan-Londa-2022.pdf>).
