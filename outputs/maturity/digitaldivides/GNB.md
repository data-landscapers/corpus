# Guinea-Bissau

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In October 2025 consultants to the World Bank-financed WARDIP project delivered a strategic plan for the Universal Access and Service Fund](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). [It includes fibre links that extend the national backbone to less-served rural centres](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). [It also proposes a pilot to subsidise phones](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). [The fund is known by its Portuguese initials, FAU](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Documento-Final-de-Validacao-do-Workshop.pdf>). No site is yet reported connected under the plan or by the fund.
