# Niger

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [By 6 August 2025 the Smart Villages project had brought mobile broadband to 780 villages without coverage](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>). [That was 35.86 per cent of the 2,175 planned](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>). [On 13 October 2025 the minister installed a committee to draft the universal access strategy](<https://anp.ne/niger-installation-officielle-du-comite-charge-delaborer-la-strategie-dacces-universel-aux-services-numeriques-et-son-plan-daction/>). [It is to let the universal access fund serve rural localities without connectivity](<https://anp.ne/niger-installation-officielle-du-comite-charge-delaborer-la-strategie-dacces-universel-aux-services-numeriques-et-son-plan-daction/>). [Smart Villages, run with the government and financed by the World Bank, closes on 23 October 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>).
