# Niger

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The World Bank report says the Smart Villages project had provided mobile broadband to 780 villages previously without coverage as of 6 August 2025, 35.86 per cent of 2,175 planned](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082825090526309/pdf/P167543-81ad214e-cd4c-45e6-8ab4-493a1416e959.pdf>). [On 13 October 2025 the Minister of Communication installed the ad hoc committee (created by order of 13 December 2024) to draft the national universal access strategy and action plan, so the universal access fund can bring electronic communication services to rural localities still without connectivity](<https://anp.ne/niger-installation-officielle-du-comite-charge-delaborer-la-strategie-dacces-universel-aux-services-numeriques-et-son-plan-daction/>).
