# Ethiopia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** A new regulation [requires Ethio Telecom and Safaricom to pay 1.5% of revenue into the Universal Access Fund](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/49962/>), about 2.5 billion Birr a year. The Reporter Ethiopia dated the rule to March 2026. The World Bank says its [Digital Foundations Project will keep its focus on rural connectivity](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/30/empowering-ethiopians-by-laying-the-digital-foundations-for-afe-economic-growth>). It added that JICA planned to support the fund, which the Ethiopian Communications Authority manages. In October 2026 the innovation minister [directed the Authority to expand connectivity in rural and underserved communities](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/28/ethiopia-telecom-regulator-service-quality-affordability/>). It is to work with the operators and regional administrations, against measurable targets.

**Gap measured.** The nearest fact is the [levy for the Universal Access Fund](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/49962/>), set at 1.5% of the two operators' revenue.

**Gap narrow.** The nearest fact is the minister's [order to expand rural connectivity](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/28/ethiopia-telecom-regulator-service-quality-affordability/>), given in October 2026.

***Also on file***. Ethio Telecom's [Next Horizon: Digital & Beyond 2028 strategy includes a rural connectivity programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/31/ethio-telecom-extends-digital-access-to-1-53-million-people-in-rural-ethiopia/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives a count of rural sites, towers or people connected. None gives internet use in rural and in urban areas.
