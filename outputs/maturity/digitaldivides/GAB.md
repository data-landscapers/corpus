# Gabon

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Minister Marc Alexandre Doumba said the government intends to accelerate deployment of the universal service to open up rural zones and improve connectivity in the interior](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>). [Arcep announced on 16 February 2024 the launch of phase 2 of the Universal Service of electronic communications, funded by a telecom-operator fund, to extend coverage to 200 villages in all nine provinces](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>). [Arcep says phase 1 of the Universal Service (2016-2018) installed 18 radio stations benefiting 33 villages on the Makokou-Okondja and Okondja-Aboumi roads (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>).

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>).
