# Gabon

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Minister Marc Alexandre Doumba said the government intends to accelerate deployment of the universal service to open up rural zones and improve connectivity in the interior](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>). [Arcep announced on 16 February 2024 the launch of phase 2 of the Universal Service of electronic communications, funded by a telecom-operator fund, to extend coverage to 200 villages in all nine provinces](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>). [Digital economy minister Mark Alexandre Doumba said his ministry is working with ARCEP, under the amending finance law, on a programme for connectivity of public infrastructure in rural areas (schools, community centres, multimedia rooms, hospitals), to start from May 2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-education-renforcement-de-la-connectivite-et-de-la-digitalisation-des-ecoles/>). [In December 2024 ARCEP's chairman said three of 20 planned rural universal-service sites had been built and were to be put into service within days](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/42>). In October 2026 Doumba said [more than 200 villages were still not connected and a universal service fund was being set up](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digital-interview-notre-ambition-est-de-faire-de-libreville-la-capitale-africaine-du-numerique-dixit-mark-alexandre-doumba/>) to bring them access.
