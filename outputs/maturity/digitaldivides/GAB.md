# Gabon

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 16 February 2024 Arcep launched phase two of the universal service, paid for by an operator fund](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>). [It is to extend coverage to 200 villages in all nine provinces](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>). [Phase one, from 2016 to 2018, had put up 18 radio stations serving 33 villages](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/8>). [In December 2024 ARCEP's chairman said three of 20 planned rural sites had been built and were about to enter service](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/42>). [In January 2025 ARCEP handed Moov Africa equipment for 10 more rural sites](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/44>). [In May 2026 minister Marc Alexandre Doumba said the government would speed up the universal service](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>). In October 2026 he said [more than 200 villages were still not connected](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-digital-interview-notre-ambition-est-de-faire-de-libreville-la-capitale-africaine-du-numerique-dixit-mark-alexandre-doumba/>).
