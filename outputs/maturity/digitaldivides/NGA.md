# Nigeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In October 2026 minister Bosun Tijani commissioned the first tower of the national programme NUCAP, in Bende, Abia State](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>). [He said 3,700 towers will connect about 20 million unconnected Nigerians](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>). [BusinessDay reports a blended-funding plan for those towers, 200 of them by December 2026](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/fg-turns-to-blended-funding-for-community-owned-rural-telecom-networks/>). [The Federal Executive Council approved 4,000 rural towers in 2025](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/2025-year-in-review-of-activities-of-the-honourable-minister-dr-bosun-tijani/>). [UNICEF says a schools partnership with the education ministry and operators reached 1,187 underserved communities](<https://www.unicef.org/nigeria/stories/connecting-every-child-digital-learning>).
