# Nigeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Punch reports Hotspot Network Limited secured a World Bank-backed results-based facility under the DARES programme with the Rural Electrification Agency to deploy 2G/4G, broadband and community Wi-Fi in 500 unserved and underserved communities](<https://punchng.com/hotspot-secures-world-bank-facility-for-500-communities/>). [BusinessDay reports the federal government is preparing a blended-funding programme in which the USPF finances towers and backhaul, the REA solar power and states land, for zero-connectivity communities, with about 3,700 towers planned to start in October and 200 by December 2026](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/fg-turns-to-blended-funding-for-community-owned-rural-telecom-networks/>).

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://punchng.com/hotspot-secures-world-bank-facility-for-500-communities/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://punchng.com/hotspot-secures-world-bank-facility-for-500-communities/>).
