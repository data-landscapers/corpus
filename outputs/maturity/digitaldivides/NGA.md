# Nigeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [UNICEF says the GenU 9JA partnership with the Federal Ministry of Education, Airtel, ATC and IHS has extended internet infrastructure to 1,187 underserved communities](<https://www.unicef.org/nigeria/stories/connecting-every-child-digital-learning>). [Minister Bosun Tijani commissioned the first tower of the National Universal Communications Access Programme (NUCAP) in Bende, Abia State, and said 3,700 towers will connect about 20 million unconnected Nigerians](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>). [BusinessDay reports the federal government is preparing a blended-funding programme in which the USPF finances towers and backhaul, the REA solar power and states land, for zero-connectivity communities, with about 3,700 towers planned to start in October and 200 by December 2026](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/fg-turns-to-blended-funding-for-community-owned-rural-telecom-networks/>). [The communications ministry says the Federal Executive Council approved 4,000 telecom towers in 2025 to extend rural connectivity](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/2025-year-in-review-of-activities-of-the-honourable-minister-dr-bosun-tijani/>).
