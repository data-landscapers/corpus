# Nigeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Minister Bosun Tijani commissioned the first tower of the National Universal Communications Access Programme (NUCAP) in Bende, Abia State, and said 3,700 towers will connect about 20 million unconnected Nigerians](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>). [The Punch reports Hotspot Network Limited secured a World Bank-backed results-based facility under the DARES programme with the Rural Electrification Agency to deploy 2G/4G, broadband and community Wi-Fi in 500 unserved and underserved communities](<https://punchng.com/hotspot-secures-world-bank-facility-for-500-communities/>).

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/fg-to-deploy-3700-telecom-towers-to-connect-20m-nigerians/>).
