# Cote d'Ivoire

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [A government communique of 24 August 2026 says the FCFA 38 billion rural connectivity programme continues towards 575 localities](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cote-d-ivoire-68-millions-pour-connecter-575-localites-rurales>). [Phases two and three cover 400 more](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cote-d-ivoire-68-millions-pour-connecter-575-localites-rurales>). [In August 2026 the universal service agency ANSUT said it was working to connect 300 localities](<https://digitalmag.ci/parlons-projet-gilles-thierry-beugre-devoile-le-plan-strategique-2026-2030-de-lansut/>). [It said 70% of rural households have no internet](<https://digitalmag.ci/parlons-projet-gilles-thierry-beugre-devoile-le-plan-strategique-2026-2030-de-lansut/>). [A further 300 localities are to join in 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>). [Phase one, finished in 2024, built 160 radio sites in 175 localities for about 221,000 people](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cote-d-ivoire-68-millions-pour-connecter-575-localites-rurales>).

**Gap measured.** [Citing ITU DataHub, the article gives internet penetration of 52.7% in urban areas against 24.6% in rural areas](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/cote-d-ivoire-to-extend-broadband-to-30-rural-towns-by-end-september>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/cote-d-ivoire-to-extend-broadband-to-30-rural-towns-by-end-september>).
