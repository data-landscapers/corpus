# Cote d'Ivoire

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [A government communique of 2026-08-24 says Cote d'Ivoire is continuing its FCFA 38 billion Programme national de connectivite rurale to bring mobile network and internet access to 575 localities, with phase two (FCFA 18 billion, 240 localities) and phase three (FCFA 10 billion, 160 localities) following](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cote-d-ivoire-68-millions-pour-connecter-575-localites-rurales>). [ANSUT's director general said on 2026-08-11 that the universal service agency is working to connect 300 localities to reach 300,000 inhabitants, citing that 70% of rural households have no internet](<https://digitalmag.ci/parlons-projet-gilles-thierry-beugre-devoile-le-plan-strategique-2026-2030-de-lansut/>). [ANSUT's Honoré Aka said 300 new localities are to be added to the rural digital connectivity effort in 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642807/societe/forum-intech-sante-2026-lansut-presente-sa-feuille-de-route-pour-connecter-les-zones-rurales-et-moderniser-lacces-aux-soins>).

**Gap measured.** [Citing ITU DataHub, the article gives internet penetration of 52.7% in urban areas against 24.6% in rural areas](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/cote-d-ivoire-to-extend-broadband-to-30-rural-towns-by-end-september>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/cote-d-ivoire-to-extend-broadband-to-30-rural-towns-by-end-september>).
