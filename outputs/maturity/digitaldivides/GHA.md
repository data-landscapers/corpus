# Ghana

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The minister said about 1,400 Rural Telephony Project sites are carrying traffic, with another 300 complete awaiting connection and about 300 under construction towards a target of 2,016 sites](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>). [The NPP says 1,961 rural telephony sites had been constructed by the January 2025 handover, while alleging many are now non-operational](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/09/npp-gives-communications-ministry-30-days-to-disclose-rural-telephony-status/>). [Communications minister Samuel Nartey George said Ghana plans 800 new and 2,000 upgraded cell sites this year, with traditional leaders helping prioritise underserved areas, and he inspected a newly built Rural Telephony Project facility at Akoefe Gadza in the Volta Region](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/16/ghana-2800-telecom-site-builds-upgrades/>).
