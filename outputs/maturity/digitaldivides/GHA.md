# Ghana

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In September 2026 the minister said about 1,400 Rural Telephony Project sites carry traffic](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>). [Another 300 were complete and awaiting connection, and about 300 under construction, towards 2,016 sites](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>). [The opposition NPP says 1,961 sites had been built by the January 2025 handover, many now not working](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/09/npp-gives-communications-ministry-30-days-to-disclose-rural-telephony-status/>). [In October 2026 the minister said Ghana plans 800 new and 2,000 upgraded cell sites this year, with underserved areas prioritised](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/16/ghana-2800-telecom-site-builds-upgrades/>).
