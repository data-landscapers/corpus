# Ghana

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The minister said about 1,400 Rural Telephony Project sites are carrying traffic, with another 300 complete awaiting connection and about 300 under construction towards a target of 2,016 sites](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>). [The NPP says 1,961 rural telephony sites had been constructed by the January 2025 handover, while alleging many are now non-operational](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/09/npp-gives-communications-ministry-30-days-to-disclose-rural-telephony-status/>). [The government is working with MTN Ghana to deploy 800 new telecommunications sites nationwide, at locations identified through Members of Parliament as having persistent network problems, with Sekyere Afram Plains communities prioritised](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/14/ghana-gifec-rural-connectivity-network-upgrades/>).

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/govt-to-upgrade-all-rural-telephony-networks-to-4g-capability-sam-george/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas.
