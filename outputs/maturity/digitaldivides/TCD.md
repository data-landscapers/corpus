# Chad

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In June 2026 the telecoms secretary of state said about 500 communities lack connectivity](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0406-56180-chad-seeks-world-bank-support-to-bring-connectivity-to-500-underserved-areas>). [The authorities are seeking World Bank support to reach them, with no places or timeline given](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0406-56180-chad-seeks-world-bank-support-to-bring-connectivity-to-500-underserved-areas>). [In March 2026 the PATN project unit said its connectivity component aims to lay 200 km of fibre](<https://patn.td/2026/03/02/vision-strategique-du-patn/>). [It is to bring 3G and 4G to 500 localities, rural and peri-urban among them](<https://patn.td/2026/03/02/vision-strategique-du-patn/>).
