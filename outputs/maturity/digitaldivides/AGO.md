# Angola

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Conecta Angola, launched in June 2023 for remote areas operators do not serve, had reached more than 300,000 people by October 2024](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/angosat-2-alcanca-mais-de-300-mil-angolanos-sem-acesso-as-telecomunicacoes/>). [In October 2026 the President said rural connectivity is a priority for coming years](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>). [On 20 October 2026 he authorised US$50 million and a tender for the Programa Conectividade](<https://pti.ao/executivo-disponibiliza-50-milhoes-de-dolares-e-abre-concurso-para-execucao-do-programa-conectividade/>). It is part of the [World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P180693>).
