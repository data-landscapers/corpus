# Angola

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [MINTTICS says its Conecta Angola project, launched in June 2023 to bring voice and internet to remote areas operators do not serve, has reached more than 300,000 people previously without communications, per GGPEN director-general Zolana João](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/angosat-2-alcanca-mais-de-300-mil-angolanos-sem-acesso-as-telecomunicacoes/>). [President Lourenço says programmes such as N'gola Digital, Escola Conectada and Conecta Angola brought connectivity to communities including remote areas via ANGOSAT-2, and that strengthening connectivity in rural communities is a priority for coming years](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>).

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/angosat-2-alcanca-mais-de-300-mil-angolanos-sem-acesso-as-telecomunicacoes/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/angosat-2-alcanca-mais-de-300-mil-angolanos-sem-acesso-as-telecomunicacoes/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
