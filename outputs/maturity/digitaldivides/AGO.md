# Angola

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [MINTTICS says its Conecta Angola project, launched in June 2023 to bring voice and internet to remote areas operators do not serve, has reached more than 300,000 people previously without communications, per GGPEN director-general Zolana João](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/angosat-2-alcanca-mais-de-300-mil-angolanos-sem-acesso-as-telecomunicacoes/>). [President Lourenço says programmes such as N'gola Digital, Escola Conectada and Conecta Angola brought connectivity to communities including remote areas via ANGOSAT-2, and that strengthening connectivity in rural communities is a priority for coming years](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>). [On 20 October 2026 the President authorised US$50 million and a tender for the Programa Conectividade, which takes broadband to less-served areas, schools and community centres first](<https://pti.ao/executivo-disponibiliza-50-milhoes-de-dolares-e-abre-concurso-para-execucao-do-programa-conectividade/>), under the [World Bank-financed Angola Digital Acceleration Project](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P180693>).
