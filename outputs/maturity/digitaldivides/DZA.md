# Algeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In June 2024 two ministerial orders awarded the universal-service mobile lots for low-density localities to Wataniya Telecom Algerie and Optimum Telecom Algerie](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2024/F2024045.pdf>). [On 21 October 2024 the minister switched on 4G in Djelida and El Amra, in Ain Defla](<https://www.ooredoo.dz/w/tout-sur-ooredoo/communiques-de-presse/ooredoo-etend-son-reseau-dans-le-cadre-du-service-universel>). [They are part of 1,400 zones of under 2,000 people the programme covers](<https://www.algerie360.com/zerrouki-devoile-la-feuille-de-route-de-la-5g-et-les-avancees-du-service-universel/>). [In September 2026 the State gave Mobilis a universal service programme for 201 4G sites in under-covered zones](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>). [It is funded through the universal service support fund at over DZD 27.3bn](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>). [On 7 September 2026 the regulator ARPCE opened a tender to extend mobile coverage to 3,000 sparsely populated localities](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/algeria-tender-mobile-coverage-3000-underserved-areas/>).
