# Algeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Algerian Business reports a universal service programme for 201 4G sites given to ATM Mobilis, funded by the State through the universal service support fund at over DZD 27.3bn, for under-covered zones](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>). [ARPCE launched a competitive tender announced 7 September 2026 to extend mobile coverage to 3,000 sparsely populated areas under its universal service mandate, to improve internet access](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/algeria-tender-mobile-coverage-3000-underserved-areas/>). [Agence Ecofin reports ARPCE launched on 7 September 2026 a tender to extend mobile services to 3,000 localities under 2,000 inhabitants, fully state-funded through the universal service mechanism, with an 18-month maximum build](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0809-141386-algerie-un-nouveau-pas-vers-la-generalisation-des-services-telecoms>).
