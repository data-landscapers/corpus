# Algeria

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In September 2026 the State gave Mobilis a universal service programme for 201 4G sites in under-covered zones](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>). [It is funded through the universal service support fund at over DZD 27.3bn](<https://algerian-business.com/mobilis-4g-algerie-201-sites-27-milliards/>). [On 7 September 2026 the regulator ARPCE opened a tender to extend mobile coverage to 3,000 sparsely populated localities](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/algeria-tender-mobile-coverage-3000-underserved-areas/>). [Each has under 2,000 inhabitants, the State pays in full and building may take 18 months at most](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0809-141386-algerie-un-nouveau-pas-vers-la-generalisation-des-services-telecoms>).
