# Malawi

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Tech Africa News reports the government has switched on the 36th rural telecommunications tower under the Last Mile Rural Connectivity Project, at Kalira Village, covering about 10 square kilometres](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/03/malawi-activates-36th-rural-telecom-tower-digital-connectivity/>). [Tech Africa News reports the government plans 99 further towers under the rural connectivity programme, targeted for completion by June 2027](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/03/malawi-activates-36th-rural-telecom-tower-digital-connectivity/>). [Nyasa Times reports DMAP project manager Chimwemwe Matemba saying a plan to deploy 100 mobile phone towers in underserved areas, with the Universal Service Fund, has entered procurement preparation](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/world-bank-turns-heat-on-malawis-digital-transformation-project/>).
