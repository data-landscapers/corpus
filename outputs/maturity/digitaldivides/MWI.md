# Malawi

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In September 2026 the government switched on the 36th tower of the Last Mile Rural Connectivity Project, at Kalira](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/03/malawi-activates-36th-rural-telecom-tower-digital-connectivity/>). [It plans 99 more towers by June 2027](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/03/malawi-activates-36th-rural-telecom-tower-digital-connectivity/>). [A plan for 100 towers in underserved areas, with the Universal Service Fund, has entered procurement](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/world-bank-turns-heat-on-malawis-digital-transformation-project/>). [By August 2026 safeguards checks were done for over 500 public institutions to be connected](<https://dmap.pppc.mw/news/malawi-showcases-digital-transformation-and-ai-agenda-at-regional-digital-forum>). [MACRA will keep using the fund for further sites](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0409-58612-malawi-seeks-private-investment-to-close-digital-connectivity-gap>).
