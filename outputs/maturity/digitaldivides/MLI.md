# Mali

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 23 July 2026 the state fund AGEFAU adopted a revised programme to extend network coverage and internet access](<https://primature.ml/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel/>). [It adds universal access centres and Wi-Fi points](<https://primature.ml/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel/>). [Its revised 2026 budget of FCFA 27.89bn keeps coverage of 162 localities](<https://agefaumali.com/archives/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel-agefau/>). [It also funds 80 universal access centres and 100 Wi-Fi points](<https://agefaumali.com/archives/11e-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lagence-de-gestion-du-fonds-dacces-universel-agefau/>). [For 2025 AGEFAU had planned FCFA 43.35bn to extend coverage and universal access centres](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1703-126707-mali-72-millions-pour-ameliorer-lacces-aux-services-telecoms-en-2025>). No locality is yet counted as connected.
