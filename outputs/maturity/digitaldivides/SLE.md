# Sierra Leone

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In August 2025 the Universal Access Development Fund told parliament it had built five towers since 2018](<https://www.premiermedia-sl.com/uadf-proposes-1-levy-to-accelerate-rural-ict-expansion/>). [Three more were nearly finished, for underserved rural communities](<https://www.premiermedia-sl.com/uadf-proposes-1-levy-to-accelerate-rural-ict-expansion/>). [In July 2024 the fund said its Rural Telephony Project had connected about 47,700 people](<https://uadf.gov.sl/launch-of-the-rural-telephony-project/>). [They live in 114 rural and remote localities, and over 30 schools and about 10 health centres are served](<https://uadf.gov.sl/launch-of-the-rural-telephony-project/>). [The fund is moving its towers from 2G to 4G with World Bank support](<https://www.premiermedia-sl.com/uadf-proposes-1-levy-to-accelerate-rural-ict-expansion/>).
