# Sierra Leone

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Universal Access Development Fund's managing director Marie Momoh told parliament's committee that the fund has built five towers since 2018 with three more nearing completion, to bring ICT services to underserved rural communities, and is upgrading towers from 2G to 4G with World Bank support](<https://www.premiermedia-sl.com/uadf-proposes-1-levy-to-accelerate-rural-ict-expansion/>). [The Universal Access Development Fund's CEO says its Rural Telephony Project, launched across five rural communities, has connected approximately 47,700 people across 114 rural and remote localities, with over 30 schools and about 10 health centres served](<https://uadf.gov.sl/launch-of-the-rural-telephony-project/>).
