# Lesotho

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Lesotho Communications Authority's annual report 2024/25 says the Universal Service Fund pays to extend telecommunications to unserved and underserved areas](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4278/Annual-Report-2024-25-2-1.pdf>). [In May 2024 a Vodacom tower at Ha-Tsepe, Leribe, paid for by the fund and the AfDB, was inaugurated](<https://www.diaspora.thepost.co.ls/2024/05/24/new-tower-for-ha-tsepe/>). [It connects 10 villages](<https://www.diaspora.thepost.co.ls/2024/05/24/new-tower-for-ha-tsepe/>). [In August 2025 the Community Digital Inclusion Model was launched with UNDP](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/08/19/undp-powers-lesothos-digital-leap/>). [It turns school computer labs into centres for remote communities](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/08/19/undp-powers-lesothos-digital-leap/>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest fact: [the Lesotho Communications Authority's 2024/25 annual report says the Universal Service Fund funds extension to unserved areas](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4278/Annual-Report-2024-25-2-1.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest fact: [the fund was established under section 33 of the Communications Act 2012](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4278/Annual-Report-2024-25-2-1.pdf>).
