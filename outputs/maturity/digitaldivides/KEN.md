# Kenya

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Communications Authority's USF Strategy 2023-2027 funds 2,500 km of fibre in unserved and underserved areas](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [By September 2024 the authority had laid 1,300 km of it across 19 counties](<https://www.ca.go.ke/ca-lay-2500km-fibre-accross-19-counties>). [In August 2025 it said 155 of the 369 sub-locations targeted by its mobile phases 1-4 were connected](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/major-usf-transparency-update-kenyas-communications-authority-responds-to-public-inquiry/>). [In 2021 the count had been 76](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [In May 2025 its director general said the fund had connected nearly 800,000 people in underserved areas](<https://www.ca.go.ke/ca-launches-ambitious-ksh-40b-universal-service-fund-strategy-boost-digital-inclusion>). [It had also connected 886 secondary schools](<https://www.ca.go.ke/ca-launches-ambitious-ksh-40b-universal-service-fund-strategy-boost-digital-inclusion>). [In October 2026 the government asked counties to name unconnected wards for the fund to target](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/government-to-partner-with-counties-to-implement-ict-related-projects-across-the-country/>).

**Gap measured.** [The 2022 KDHS gives internet use in the past 12 months among people aged 15 to 49](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>). [It was 80.0% of urban and 40.7% of rural men](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>). [For women it was 67.7% urban and 27.9% rural, before the three-year window](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>).
