# Kenya

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Communications Authority's USF Strategy 2023-2027 funds 2,500 km of fibre in unserved and underserved areas](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [The plan for the period is Ksh 40.037 billion](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [Between 2015 and 2021 the fund paid for voice and data links in 76 sub-locations across 15 counties](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [Over 500,000 people in unserved areas gained access, before the three-year window](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>).

**Gap measured.** [The 2022 KDHS gives internet use in the past 12 months among people aged 15 to 49](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>). [It was 80.0% of urban and 40.7% of rural men](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>). [For women it was 67.7% urban and 27.9% rural, before the three-year window](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>).
