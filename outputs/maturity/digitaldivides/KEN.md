# Kenya

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Communications Authority's USF Strategy 2023-2027 says the Authority will give direct financial support to roll out 2,500 km of fibre in unserved and underserved areas, with a Ksh 40.037 billion plan for the period](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>). [The Communications Authority says its Universal Service Fund financed voice and data infrastructure in 76 sub-locations across 15 counties between 2015 and 2021, giving access to over 500,000 people in unserved areas (2021, before the three-year window)](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>).

**Gap measured.** [The 2022 KDHS fact sheet gives internet use in the past 12 months, aged 15-49, as 80.0% of urban and 40.7% of rural men and 67.7% of urban and 27.9% of rural women (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/USF%20Strategy/Universal%20Service%20Fund%20Strategy%202023-2027.pdf>).
