# South Sudan

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In July 2025 MTN South Sudan and the Universal Service and Access Fund launched a network site at Abuyong, in Lakes State](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/mtn-south-sudan-expands-its-network-to-rural-abuyong>). [Three more sites are planned in Greater Pibor and Cueibet](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/mtn-south-sudan-expands-its-network-to-rural-abuyong>). [In January 2026 the National Communication Authority announced its agenda for the year](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/01/05/south-sudan-targets-rural-connectivity-child-online-safety-in-2026-digital-transformation-push/>). [It aims to speed the roll-out of infrastructure that brings reliable connectivity to rural and under-connected areas](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/01/05/south-sudan-targets-rural-connectivity-child-online-safety-in-2026-digital-transformation-push/>).
