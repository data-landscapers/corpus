# Mauritania

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [ARE says the universal service fund's southern and south-eastern border project brought 144 new radio sites and benefits more than 400 localities, and the northern project 19 radio sites with 2G coverage over nearly 600 km](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>). [AMI reports phase 1 of the border-zone electronic communication coverage project, launched in December 2023 across 42 solar sites, benefited over 85,000 citizens](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/261984>). [AMI reports President Ghazouani laid the foundation stone on 28 December 2024 for phases 2 and 3 of the project, covering 964 km, 100 sites and 21 transport sites in the southern and south-eastern border zones](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/261984>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>).
