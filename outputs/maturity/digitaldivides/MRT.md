# Mauritania

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In February 2025 the regulator ARE said the universal service fund's southern border project had built 144 radio sites](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>). [They serve more than 400 localities, and a northern project added 19 sites over nearly 600 km](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>). [Phase one, launched in December 2023 with 42 solar sites, benefited over 85,000 citizens](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/261984>). [On 28 December 2024 the President launched phases two and three, with 100 sites over 964 km](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/261984>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Cheikh%20ARE%20Mauritania.pdf>).
