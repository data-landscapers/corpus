# Guinea

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [ANSUTEN, the national universal service agency, presented on 22 July 2025 a feasibility study (with SFM Technologies, since 2023) mapping white zones and aiming at coverage of 90% of the rural population by 2030, with an international tender for technical and financial partners to follow](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/guinee-vers-une-couverture-a-90-des-zones-rurales-dici-2030-une-initiative-de-lansuten/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/guinee-vers-une-couverture-a-90-des-zones-rurales-dici-2030-une-initiative-de-lansuten/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/guinee-vers-une-couverture-a-90-des-zones-rurales-dici-2030-une-initiative-de-lansuten/>).
