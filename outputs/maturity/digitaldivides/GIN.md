# Guinea

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 22 July 2025 the universal service agency ANSUTEN presented a study mapping white zones](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/guinee-vers-une-couverture-a-90-des-zones-rurales-dici-2030-une-initiative-de-lansuten/>). [It aims to cover 90% of the rural population by 2030, with a tender to follow](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/07/guinee-vers-une-couverture-a-90-des-zones-rurales-dici-2030-une-initiative-de-lansuten/>). It is the only fact held on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
