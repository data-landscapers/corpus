# Cape Verde

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** No source found says a programme to connect rural areas is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [INE's 2025 survey implies rural internet use of 68.8% (non-use 31.2%) against 80.0% urban (non-use 20.0%) among people aged 10 and over, and 69.3% of rural households with internet against 68.5% urban](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/28/internet-chega-a-mais-familias-rurais-do-que-urbanas-em-cabo-verde-em-2025-ine/102930>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Gap measured.** [INE's 2025 continuous multi-purpose survey (IMC) shows 22.7% of people aged 10 and over did not use the internet in the previous three months, 31.2% in rural areas against 20.0% in urban areas](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/28/internet-chega-a-mais-familias-rurais-do-que-urbanas-em-cabo-verde-em-2025-ine/102930>).

**Gap narrow.** [INE's 2025 survey implies rural internet use of 68.8% (non-use 31.2%) against 80.0% urban (non-use 20.0%) among people aged 10 and over, and 69.3% of rural households with internet against 68.5% urban](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/28/internet-chega-a-mais-familias-rurais-do-que-urbanas-em-cabo-verde-em-2025-ine/102930>).
