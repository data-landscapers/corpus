# Tanzania

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In April 2026 Helios Towers completed 133 sites under the government's universal service fund programme, UCSAF](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [The programme extends telecom infrastructure to areas where connectivity is limited or unprofitable](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [For 2026/27 the ministry proposed a budget of about $84 million, 94 per cent for development](<https://cioafrica.co/liquid-tanzania-government-align-on-digital-transformation-agenda/>). [Its key initiatives include connecting rural and underserved communities](<https://cioafrica.co/liquid-tanzania-government-align-on-digital-transformation-agenda/>). [The 133 sites reach rural and underserved communities, Tech Review Africa reports](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [By 23 October 2025 the UCSAF programme had activated 741 of 758 planned masts](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/10/28/tanzania-nears-universal-rural-connectivity-as-741-of-758-masts-go-live/>). [They connect about 1,300 of 1,407 target villages](<https://thebizlens.co.tz/2025/10/28/tanzania-nears-universal-rural-connectivity-as-741-of-758-masts-go-live/>). In November 2026 the fund's implementation report [showed 2,199 of 2,324 planned towers completed](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/ucsaf-to-improve-mobile-network-coverage-on-sgr-5657830>), reaching 2,053 of 2,306 target wards in 31 regions. Its director general [said 198 more locations are being contracted under Phase 11A](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/ucsaf-to-improve-mobile-network-coverage-on-sgr-5657830>).
