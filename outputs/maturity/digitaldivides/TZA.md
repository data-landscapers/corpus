# Tanzania

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In April 2026 Helios Towers completed 133 sites under the government's universal service fund programme, UCSAF](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [The programme extends telecom infrastructure to areas where connectivity is limited or unprofitable](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [For 2026/27 the ministry proposed a budget of about $84 million, 94 per cent for development](<https://cioafrica.co/liquid-tanzania-government-align-on-digital-transformation-agenda/>). [Its key initiatives include connecting rural and underserved communities](<https://cioafrica.co/liquid-tanzania-government-align-on-digital-transformation-agenda/>). [The 133 sites reach rural and underserved communities, Tech Review Africa reports](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>).
