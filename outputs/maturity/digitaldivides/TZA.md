# Tanzania

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Helios Towers completed 133 telecom sites under Tanzania's government-led Universal Communications Service Access Fund (UCSAF) programme to extend connectivity to rural and underserved communities, per Tech Review Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>). [For 2026/27 Tanzania's Ministry of Communication and Information Technology proposed a budget of about $84 million, about 94 per cent for development projects, with key initiatives including extending connectivity to rural and underserved communities](<https://cioafrica.co/liquid-tanzania-government-align-on-digital-transformation-agenda/>). [The UCSAF programme is described as a government-led initiative extending telecommunications infrastructure to areas where connectivity has been limited or commercially unviable, per Tech Review Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4919/>).
