## Rural internet gap

[They include using satellite as a source of connectivity](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
