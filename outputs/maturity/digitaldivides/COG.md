# Congo

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In January 2026 the universal service fund FASUCE connected two localities, Miaba and Ehota](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/congo-advances-digital-inclusion-with-new-connections-in-miaba-and-ehota/>). [Its committee chair said that in five years it deployed 254 sites in 244 localities](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/miaba-et-ehota-connectees-le-fasuce-poursuit-sa-mission-dinclusion-numerique-dans-la-cuvette>). [They connect more than 500,000 people](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/miaba-et-ehota-connectees-le-fasuce-poursuit-sa-mission-dinclusion-numerique-dans-la-cuvette>). [On 26 February 2026 the PATN steering committee adopted a 2026 budget of 21.872 billion FCFA](<https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-plus-de-21-milliards-fcfa-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/>). [Rural connectivity is a 2026 priority, and white zones are to be served by 2027](<https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-plus-de-21-milliards-fcfa-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/>).
