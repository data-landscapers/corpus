# Congo

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [FASUCE officially connected two localities, Miaba and Ehota, with inauguration ceremonies attended by the Minister of Posts and Telecommunications](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/28/congo-advances-digital-inclusion-with-new-connections-in-miaba-and-ehota/>). [FASUCE committee chair Luc Missidimbazi said that in five years the fund deployed 254 sites in 244 localities, connecting more than 500,000 people, and the minister said the government will continue the policy](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/miaba-et-ehota-connectees-le-fasuce-poursuit-sa-mission-dinclusion-numerique-dans-la-cuvette>). [The PATN steering committee adopted a 2026 budget of 21.872 billion FCFA on 26 February 2026 with rural-area connectivity as a 2026 axis, and the project plans to serve white zones by 2027](<https://www.journaldebrazza.com/congo-plus-de-21-milliards-fcfa-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-numerique/>).
