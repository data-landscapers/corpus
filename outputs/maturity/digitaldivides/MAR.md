# Morocco

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 20 April 2026 the minister told parliament that phase one of the national broadband plan is complete](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/04/21/plus-de-10-690-zones-couvertes-par-les-services-de-telecommunications-haut-debit-affirme-seghrouchni_1985310/>). [It covered more than 10,690 of 10,740 zones, and phase two targets 2,000 rural zones in 2026](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/04/21/plus-de-10-690-zones-couvertes-par-les-services-de-telecommunications-haut-debit-affirme-seghrouchni_1985310/>). [Over 10,640 rural zones gained 2G, 3G and 4G between 2018 and 2024, the minister said](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>). [Phase two is funded by the universal service fund](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>).

**Gap measured.** [The 2024 census found that 70.2% of urban residents aged 15 and over had used the internet in the previous three months](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>). [Among rural residents it was 40.4%](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 puts rural internet use at four-fifths of the urban rate or more.
