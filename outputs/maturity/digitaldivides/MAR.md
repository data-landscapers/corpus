# Morocco

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Minister Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni told the Chamber of Representatives on 20 April 2026 that phase one of the national high and very high speed broadband plan (PNHD) is complete, with more than 10,690 of 10,740 zones covered by 2G, 3G and 4G, and phase two is launched for 2,000 new rural zones in 2026](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2026/04/21/plus-de-10-690-zones-couvertes-par-les-services-de-telecommunications-haut-debit-affirme-seghrouchni_1985310/>). [The minister said over 10,640 rural zones were covered by 2G, 3G and 4G services between 2018 and 2024 under PNHD phase one, and that phase two will serve 1,800 poorly covered rural zones by 2026 with universal service fund support](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>).

**Gap measured.** [HCP reported from the 2024 census that 70.2% of urban and 40.4% of rural residents aged 15 and over used the internet in the three months before the census](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).
