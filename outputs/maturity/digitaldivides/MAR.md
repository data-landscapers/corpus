# Morocco

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The minister said over 10,640 rural zones were covered by 2G, 3G and 4G services between 2018 and 2024 under PNHD phase one, and that phase two will serve 1,800 poorly covered rural zones by 2026 with universal service fund support](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>). [The ministry states that the universal service programme PNHD1 had covered 10,620 localities with 4G by end March 2024, and that over 2,500 VSAT access points had been subsidised by end 2022](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/connectivit%C3%A9>).

**Gap measured.** [HCP reported from the 2024 census that 70.2% of urban and 40.4% of rural residents aged 15 and over used the internet in the three months before the census](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate.
