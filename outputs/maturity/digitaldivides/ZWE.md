# Zimbabwe

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [ICT Minister Mavetera said at Digital UNGA that more than 10,000 remote sites have been connected under Zimbabwe's digital inclusion initiatives](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>). [ICT Minister Mavetera said the government introduced infrastructure sharing and mandated tower relocation using the Universal Service Fund as interventions to bridge the digital divide for underserved rural and remote communities, and wants the fund to yield 100% coverage of marginalised wards](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-minister-mavetera-directs-potraz-to-establish-unit-to-police-operator-deals/>). [Free Wi-Fi has been rolled out at rural business hubs under the Presidential Internet Scheme, and Community Information Centres modernised into Digital Centres](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>). [MISA Zimbabwe states the Universal Service Fund finances network expansion into economically disadvantaged areas](<https://zimbabwe.misa.org/2026/08/16/internet-affordability-and-access-in-zimbabwe/>). On 23 October 2026 the minister [directed POTRAZ to set Universal Service Fund targets for schools, clinics, chiefs' courts and rural business centres](<https://newshour.co.zw/potraz-ordered-to-crack-down-on-poor-network-service-data-fraud/>), and to publish coverage and uptake figures every quarter.

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7395/unga81-zimbabwe-highlights-digital-inclusion-drive-at-unga81-outlines-ai-roadmap>).
