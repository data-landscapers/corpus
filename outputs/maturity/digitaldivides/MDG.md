# Madagascar

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Digital Development Minister says the government has already implemented solutions in 146 fokontany under its telecom expansion programme](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0707-57146-madagascar-s-parliament-calls-for-faster-network-rollout-in-underserved-areas>). [The Digital Development Minister says the government's telecom expansion programme targets 108 districts, 935 communes and 3,844 fokontany, prioritising locations with no or limited network, with rollout dependent on funding](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0707-57146-madagascar-s-parliament-calls-for-faster-network-rollout-in-underserved-areas>). [The World Bank restructuring paper says the Madagascar government's DECIM project is preparing the first batch of about a hundred cell towers to extend rural connectivity, with ARTEC drafting tenders for launch by end of June 2024, and a feasibility study for expanding rural digital connectivity at evaluation stage](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060524002530122/pdf/P17870115f5e7c0081aa5c18d94df04e422.pdf>).
