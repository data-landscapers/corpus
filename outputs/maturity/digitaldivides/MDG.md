# Madagascar

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In July 2026 the digital development minister said solutions were in place in 146 fokontany](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0707-57146-madagascar-s-parliament-calls-for-faster-network-rollout-in-underserved-areas>). [The programme targets 108 districts, 935 communes and 3,844 fokontany with no or limited network](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0707-57146-madagascar-s-parliament-calls-for-faster-network-rollout-in-underserved-areas>). [Roll-out depends on funding](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0707-57146-madagascar-s-parliament-calls-for-faster-network-rollout-in-underserved-areas>). [In May 2024 the World Bank said the DECIM project was preparing a first batch of about a hundred rural towers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060524002530122/pdf/P17870115f5e7c0081aa5c18d94df04e422.pdf>). [A feasibility study for wider rural connectivity was then being evaluated](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060524002530122/pdf/P17870115f5e7c0081aa5c18d94df04e422.pdf>).
