# Sao Tome and Principe

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** In September 2024 [the government and Huawei signed a memorandum of understanding](<https://www.stp-press.st/2024/10/07/governo-e-huawei-da-china-firmam-parceria-para-levar-internet-e-iluminacao-a-42-comunidades/>) to install solar power and internet in 42 communities. Prime Minister Patrice Trovoada announced it. No source found says any of the 42 communities has since been connected. [The same communities are to receive solar power under the memorandum](<https://www.stp-press.st/2024/10/07/governo-e-huawei-da-china-firmam-parceria-para-levar-internet-e-iluminacao-a-42-comunidades/>). No other programme to bring internet to rural areas has been found.

**Gap measured.** No survey giving internet use in rural and in urban areas has been found. The nearest held fact is [the memorandum with Huawei for 42 communities](<https://www.stp-press.st/2024/10/07/governo-e-huawei-da-china-firmam-parceria-para-levar-internet-e-iluminacao-a-42-comunidades/>), signed in September 2024.

**Gap narrow.** No figure for rural internet use against urban has been found.
