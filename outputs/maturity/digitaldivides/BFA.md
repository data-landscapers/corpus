# Burkina Faso

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The digital-transition minister said on 8 October 2026 that 750 villages would get calls, internet and mobile money for the first time by end-2026 and 750 more by 2028, with FCFA 25 billion mobilised by the State, under a zero-white-zone target for 2030](<https://refletinfo.net/telecommunications-750-villages-burkinabe-sortiront-bientot-des-zones-blanches/>). [PactDigital, a government digital project, is set to extend mobile and internet connectivity to 1,000 underserved communities in 2026 under the 'Zero White Zones' initiative, benefiting 1.5 to 2 million people, mostly rural, per TechAfrica News citing the project](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/13/burkina-faso-targets-1000-white-zones-in-2026-connectivity-drive/>). [The minister said the State had subsidised the commissioning of 223 sites since 2020](<https://www.sidwaya.info/services-de-communications-electroniques-750-zones-bientot-couvertes/>).

**Gap measured.** [Data presented at the National Forum on the Digitalization of Justice showed internet access of about 72% in urban areas and 27% in rural areas](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-issues-600-000-criminal-records-online-but-rural-connectivity-gap-persists>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate.
