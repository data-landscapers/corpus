# Burkina Faso

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The ministry reported covering more than 400 new white-zone localities in 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article145231>), after [138 in 2024, built with a State subsidy through the universal service fund](<https://www.sidwaya.info/couverture-des-zones-blanches-la-ministre-aminata-zerbo-inspecte-des-sites-de-telecommunications/>). [On 8 October 2026 the minister said 750 villages would get calls, internet and mobile money by end-2026](<https://refletinfo.net/telecommunications-750-villages-burkinabe-sortiront-bientot-des-zones-blanches/>). [A further 750 are due by 2028](<https://refletinfo.net/telecommunications-750-villages-burkinabe-sortiront-bientot-des-zones-blanches/>). [PactDigital aims to connect 1,000 underserved, mostly rural communities in 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/13/burkina-faso-targets-1000-white-zones-in-2026-connectivity-drive/>).

**Gap measured.** [Figures shown at a national justice forum in June 2026 put internet access at 72% in urban areas](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-issues-600-000-criminal-records-online-but-rural-connectivity-gap-persists>). [In rural areas it was 27%](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-issues-600-000-criminal-records-online-but-rural-connectivity-gap-persists>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-issues-600-000-criminal-records-online-but-rural-connectivity-gap-persists>).

***Also on file***. [FASU](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144560>).

***Not found***. No household survey or statistics office figure since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and urban areas.
