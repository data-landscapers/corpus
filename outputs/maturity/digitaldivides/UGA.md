# Uganda

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [TechReviewAfrica reports the UCUSAF Programme, funded by statutory 2% contributions handed to the Uganda Communications Commission, finances projects extending telecommunications infrastructure and connectivity in underserved and hard-to-reach communities](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6536/atc-uganda-contributes-to-universal-service-fund-to-expand-digital-access-across-uganda>). [Uganda operates the Uganda Communications Universal Service and Access Fund (formerly the Rural Communications Development Fund), financed by a 2 per cent operator revenue levy to support digital inclusion](<https://observer.ug/technology/digital-inclusion-what-ugandan-telecoms-can-learn-from-kenyas-safaricom-and-south-africas-vodacom/>). [The Uganda Communications Commission says its Universal Service and Access Fund (UCUSAF), financed by a 2% levy on operator revenue, supports communications services in unserved and underserved areas and will be used to expand ICT infrastructure there](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/mtn-contributes-ugx-54-27bn-to-support-digital-inclusion/>).
