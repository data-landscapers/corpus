# Eswatini

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [ESCCOM, through the Universal Access Service Fund, co-funded and launched the Macetjeni iHub in rural Lubombo Region on 6 August 2026 with SEDCO and UNDP, giving young people, women and others internet access and digital skills training; it follows a first iHub at Piggs Peak](<https://www.undp.org/eswatini/news/macetjeni-ihub-opens-expand-digital-opportunities>). [The Ministry of ICT is implementing the E1.2 billion (US$65 million) Eswatini Digital Project, with E648 million for Affordable Broadband including fibre expansion and last mile connectivity, and says it will extend connectivity to underserved rural areas](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>).
