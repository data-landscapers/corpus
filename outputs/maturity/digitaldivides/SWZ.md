# Eswatini

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 6 August 2026 the regulator ESCCOM, through the Universal Access Service Fund, co-funded a digital skills hub at Macetjeni](<https://www.undp.org/eswatini/news/macetjeni-ihub-opens-expand-digital-opportunities>). [The hub, in Lubombo, offers internet access and is the second of its kind](<https://www.undp.org/eswatini/news/macetjeni-ihub-opens-expand-digital-opportunities>). [The ministry's E1.2 billion Eswatini Digital Project gives E648 million to affordable broadband, fibre and last-mile links](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>). [It says connectivity will reach underserved rural areas](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/e1-2-billion-digital-project-to-connect-schools-clinics-and-communities/>). [In April 2026 the government commissioned a Starlink gateway meant to serve remote and underserved areas](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/28/eswatini-commissions-starlink-satellite-gateway-to-expand-nationwide-internet-connectivity/>).
