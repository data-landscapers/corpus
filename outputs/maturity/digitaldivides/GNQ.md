# Equatorial Guinea

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Vice-President ordered Huawei to draw up a project to improve telephone and internet coverage nationwide, including more interior coverage and a basic network covering 100% of the territory, and gave ministries and operators a week to assess it before approving a first phase; the press office links poor coverage to the urban-rural digital gap](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_calidad_de_la_red_telefonica_podria_mejorar_proximamente>). [IDENTIC News reports that GITGE, the state telecom infrastructure manager, connected the island province of Annobon to the national fibre-optic network by submarine cable from Sao Tome, announced 3 April 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://news.identicge.org/annobon-ya-dispone-de-internet-de-alta-velocidad/>).
