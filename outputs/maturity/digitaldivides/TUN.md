# Tunisia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In June 2025 the ministry launched tender 02/2025 to extend telecom and broadband coverage to 103 white zones](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [They lie in 15 governorates](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [In 2022 the ministry closed its earlier white zones project, funded with TND 38 million](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2022/06/24/le-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-annonce-la-cloture-du-projet-des-zones-blanches/>). [It covered 94 sectors and 180,000 inhabitants](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2022/06/24/le-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-annonce-la-cloture-du-projet-des-zones-blanches/>). [A 2017 white-zones project with Tunisie Telecom connected 94 white zones, 164 schools and 59 health centres](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [On 19 November 2026 the minister told parliament that TND 76 million funds a Tunisie Telecom contract to cover white zones, with works started](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D8%B3%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%B3%D9%8A-76-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%BA%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%AD%D8%A7%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84-2027>).
