# Tunisia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In June 2025 the ministry launched tender 02/2025 to extend telecom and broadband coverage to 103 white zones](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [They lie in 15 governorates](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [In 2022 the ministry closed its earlier white zones project, funded with TND 38 million](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2022/06/24/le-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-annonce-la-cloture-du-projet-des-zones-blanches/>). [It covered 94 sectors and 180,000 inhabitants](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2022/06/24/le-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-annonce-la-cloture-du-projet-des-zones-blanches/>). [In 2017, before the three-year window, the project had connected 94 white zones, 164 schools and 59 health centres](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>).
