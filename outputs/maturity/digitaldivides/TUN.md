# Tunisia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Webdo reports that the Ministry of Communication Technologies launched national tender 02/2025 to extend telecom and broadband coverage to 103 white zones in 15 governorates](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>). [The communications technologies ministry announced the close of its white zones project, in which Tunisie Telecom, funded by the Communications Development Fund with TND 38 million, covered 94 sectors in 47 delegations across 15 governorates and 180,000 inhabitants (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2022/06/24/le-ministere-des-technologies-de-la-communication-annonce-la-cloture-du-projet-des-zones-blanches/>). [Webdo reports that the 2017 white-zones project, with Tunisie Telecom alone, connected 94 white zones, 164 schools and 59 health centres, benefiting over 180,000 citizens (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/internet-pour-tous-letat-relance-la-couverture-des-zones-blanches/378539/>).
