# Central African Republic

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 15 April 2026 the regulator ARCEP named finishing the universal service fund a priority for 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>). [The fund is to extend broadband to uncovered areas through operators](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>). [In July 2025 the body that oversees the fund began work](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1107-130013-centrafrique-vers-la-mise-en-uvre-du-fonds-du-service-universel>). [It is funded by 2% of operator revenue](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1107-130013-centrafrique-vers-la-mise-en-uvre-du-fonds-du-service-universel>). [Operators are to build the infrastructure in uncovered areas](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2104-54854-central-african-republic-plans-2026-launch-of-telecom-universal-service-fund>). [The fund is meant to extend basic telecom access across the whole territory](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1107-130013-centrafrique-vers-la-mise-en-uvre-du-fonds-du-service-universel>).
