# South Africa

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In March 2024 the minister said SA Connect had reached 361,000 households through 2,502 Wi-Fi hotspots](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minster-mondli-gungubele-launch-national-digital-skills-forum-01-mar-2024>). [It aims to connect 5 million rural and township households](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minster-mondli-gungubele-launch-national-digital-skills-forum-01-mar-2024>). [By June 2025 Sentech and Broadband Infraco had installed more than 11,500 of 32,055 planned hotspots, the department said](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RNW1084-2025-06-23_removed.pdf>). [The minister approved the Universal Service and Access Fund manual, USAASA said in October 2026](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/usaasa-to-implement-approved-usaf-manual/O2rQGqAEg1Yqd1ea>). [In August 2026 the British High Commission began funding community networks in rural KwaZulu-Natal](<https://witness.co.za/news/2026/08/27/multi-million-rand-british-investment-to-bring-affordable-internet-access-to-rural-kzn/>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://witness.co.za/news/2026/08/27/multi-million-rand-british-investment-to-bring-affordable-internet-access-to-rural-kzn/>).
