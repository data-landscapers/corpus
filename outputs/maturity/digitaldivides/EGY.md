# Egypt

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [MCIT's Decent Life phase one reports 1,180,723 buildings covered by fibre, 1,409 new cell sites built and 576 upgraded, and mobile coverage completed in 901 of 1,164 villages](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>).
