# Egypt

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Under Decent Life phase one, MCIT reports 1,180,723 buildings covered by fibre](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [It built 1,409 new cell sites and upgraded 576](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [Mobile coverage was completed in 901 of 1,164 villages by September 2025](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>).
