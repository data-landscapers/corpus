# Egypt

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Under Decent Life phase one, MCIT reports 1,180,723 buildings covered by fibre](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [It built 1,409 new cell sites and upgraded 576](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [Mobile coverage was completed in 901 of 1,164 villages by September 2025](<https://www.comcec.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/4.-Egypt-Presentation-on-Rural-Accessibility.pdf>). [By end-December 2024 the planning ministry reported fibre extended to 409 villages in the first phase of Haya Karima, the Decent Life initiative for rural Egypt](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2042>). [The first-phase villages then had 371,600 internet subscribers](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2042>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas.
