# Cameroon

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [On 31 October 2025 the posts minister opened bidding for the rural part of the World Bank-backed PATNUC](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0911-15315-digital-divide-cameroon-aims-to-connect-328-rural-communities-by-2028>). [It has 55 billion XAF to connect 328 localities without 3G by 2028](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/0911-15315-digital-divide-cameroon-aims-to-connect-328-rural-communities-by-2028>). [A tender for 120 firm and 208 conditional sites was being prepared in July 2025, to finish by 30 December 2026](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/11/le-cameroun-engage-la-phase-operationnelle-de-plus-ou-moins-300-nouveaux-sites-3g-dans-les-zones-blanches/>). [On 8 July 2025 the posts ministry validated a national universal digital service strategy](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/11/numerique-pour-tous-le-cameroun-valide-sa-strategie-nationale-de-service-universel/>). [It aims to cover all underserved areas by 2030](<https://patnuc.cm/2025/07/11/numerique-pour-tous-le-cameroun-valide-sa-strategie-nationale-de-service-universel/>).
