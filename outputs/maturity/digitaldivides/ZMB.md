# Zambia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In 2025 ZICTA found the Lisomona tower, completed that year under the 51 Towers Project, delivering 2G, 3G and 4G](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [Its signal reaches 15 km](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [In September 2025 the Smart Zambia Institute installed satellite internet in Moomba Chiefdom, Kazungula District](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>). [It connected a rural health clinic and two primary schools](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>). [In June 2026 ZICTA's board toured fund projects in Muchinga, Northern and Luapula provinces](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/zicta-assesses-uasf-ict-projects-to-expand-digital-inclusion-across-rural-zambia/>). [The Machiya tower, built in 2021, covers over 9,500 people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>).
