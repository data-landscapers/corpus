# Zambia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Smart Zambia Institute's rollout connected three rural community institutions in Moomba Chiefdom: Moomba Rural Health Clinic, Choonzo Primary School and Kalamba Primary School](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>). [Machiya Tower in Mpongwe District, built in 2021 under the 24 Towers Project, provides broadband coverage to over 9,500 people, and Lisomona Tower in Masaiti District, completed in 2025 under the 51 Towers Project, delivers 2G, 3G and 4G within 15 km, per ZICTA's inspection](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [The Smart Zambia Institute's Electronic Government Division installed Starlink satellite internet kits in Moomba Chiefdom, Kazungula District, as part of the government's efforts to bring internet connectivity to rural communities](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>).
