# Zambia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In 2025 ZICTA found the Lisomona tower, completed that year under the 51 Towers Project, delivering 2G, 3G and 4G](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [Its signal reaches 15 km](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [In September 2025 the Smart Zambia Institute installed satellite internet in Moomba Chiefdom, Kazungula District](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>). [It connected a rural health clinic and two primary schools](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/29/smart-zambia-brings-starlink-internet-to-remote-moomba-chiefdom/>). [In June 2026 ZICTA's board toured fund projects in Muchinga, Northern and Luapula provinces](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/17/zicta-assesses-uasf-ict-projects-to-expand-digital-inclusion-across-rural-zambia/>). [The Machiya tower, built in 2021, covers over 9,500 people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/01/zicta-board-committee-reviews-uasf-projects-in-copperbelt-and-northwestern-provinces/>). [In September 2025 ZICTA said 43 of 51 remote-area towers were complete, within a roll-out of 152](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/airtel-ihs-build-152-telecom-towers-in-zambia>). [In November 2024 the ministry said the fund pays for about 30 towers a year against a deficit of about 900](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/usaf-inadequate-to-finance-major-rollout-of-mobile-towers-ps/>). [In November 2026 the Smart Zambia Institute said the World Bank-backed Digital Zambia Acceleration Project plans 300 towers](<https://zambia24.com/2026/11/09/zambia-strives-to-bridge-digital-gap/>).
