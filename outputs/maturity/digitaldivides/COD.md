# DR Congo

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The universal service fund (FDSU) signed subsidy agreements in August 2026 with Vodacom, Airtel, Africell and Orange for at least 40 towers in peri-urban and rural areas, per a ministry communique](<https://acp.cd/economie/telecoms-une-entreprise-beneficie-de-328-millions-usd-pour-implanter-728-pylones-en-rdc/>). [The FDSU signed its first subsidy agreements with Airtel, Vodacom, Orange and Africell for a first phase of 40 sites under the Universal Connectivity Project in rural and peri-urban areas, with about 300 sites next and 3,000 settlements by 2030](<https://the-port-brief.co.tz/article/fdsu-is-stepping-up-its-efforts-to-bridge-the-digital-divide>).
