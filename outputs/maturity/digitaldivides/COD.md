# DR Congo

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** On 12 February 2026 the universal service fund (FDSU) [presented its National Connectivity Strategy 2026-2035](<https://www.fdsu.cd/blog/le-fdsu-presente-la-strategie-nationale-pour-la-connectivite-universelle-2026-2035-modele-towerco-lead>). It plans a subsidised, TowerCo-led roll-out in five zones and three phases, to connect about 68 million rural Congolese. In August 2026 the FDSU [signed subsidy agreements with Vodacom, Airtel, Africell and Orange for at least 40 towers](<https://acp.cd/economie/telecoms-une-entreprise-beneficie-de-328-millions-usd-pour-implanter-728-pylones-en-rdc/>) in peri-urban and rural areas. [About 300 sites are to follow, and 3,000 settlements by 2030](<https://the-port-brief.co.tz/article/fdsu-is-stepping-up-its-efforts-to-bridge-the-digital-divide>). No count of sites already connected is on record.

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/economie/telecoms-une-entreprise-beneficie-de-328-millions-usd-pour-implanter-728-pylones-en-rdc/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/economie/telecoms-une-entreprise-beneficie-de-328-millions-usd-pour-implanter-728-pylones-en-rdc/>).
