# Rwanda

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** On 14 March 2024 the ICT ministry launched [phase 2 of Connect Rwanda](<https://www.topafricanews.com/2024/03/19/rwanda-launches-connect-rwanda-program-phase-2-encourages-responsible-technology-use/>) in Kirehe District. At the launch it announced [58 towers and 42 kilometres of fibre completed in Kirehe](<https://www.topafricanews.com/2024/03/19/rwanda-launches-connect-rwanda-program-phase-2-encourages-responsible-technology-use/>) to bring high-speed internet to the district. No national count of rural sites connected has been found.

**Gap measured.** The newest rural and urban figures found are from the [EICV5 household survey of 2016/17](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/ict-water-sanitation-energy-tourism-and-transport/ict>). It gave home internet access for 38.1% of urban households and 12.2% of rural households. No survey since October 2023 giving rural and urban internet use has been found. [The survey was run by the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/ict-water-sanitation-energy-tourism-and-transport/ict>).

**Gap narrow.** The nearest held fact is the [EICV5 survey of 2016/17](<https://statistics.gov.rw/statistical-publications/ict-water-sanitation-energy-tourism-and-transport/ict>). Rural home internet access was 12.2%. Urban access was 38.1%.

***Also on file***. [Connect Rwanda](<https://www.topafricanews.com/2024/03/19/rwanda-launches-connect-rwanda-program-phase-2-encourages-responsible-technology-use/>), the ICT ministry's programme.

***Not found***. No survey since October 2023 giving internet use in rural and in urban areas has been found.
