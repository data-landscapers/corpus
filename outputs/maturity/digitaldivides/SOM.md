# Somalia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The policy document says the absence of government intervention in expanding access to underserved areas means the digital divide remains high in rural areas](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [The Ministry of Communications and Technology's Digital Inclusion Policy recommends improving broadband connectivity with priority to unserved and underserved areas, with an implementation roadmap budgeted at about $32 million over five years](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Gap measured.** [The Somalia ICT Survey 2023 found internet access of 22.7% in rural areas and 47.1% in urban areas, per the Ministry of Communications and Technology's policy document](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>).
