# Somalia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The ministry's Digital Inclusion Policy of August 2025 gives priority to broadband in unserved and underserved areas](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [Its roadmap is budgeted at about $32 million over five years](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [The policy says the government has not intervened to widen access in underserved areas](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [So the digital divide remains high in rural areas, it says](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [The policy was issued by the Ministry of Communications and Technology](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>).

**Gap measured.** [The policy cites the Somalia ICT Survey 2023, run to inform it](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>). [In rural areas 22.7% had access to internet connections, against 47.1% in urban areas](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>).

**Gap narrow.** No source found since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>).
