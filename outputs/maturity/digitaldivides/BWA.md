# Botswana

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Universal Access and Service Fund had connected 279 villages to 4G by March 2025](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/UASF_Annual_Report_2024_25_WEB.pdf>). [Fifty-one of them were added in 2024-25, with 37 highway sites](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/UASF_Annual_Report_2024_25_WEB.pdf>). [The minister says the village connectivity project's first phase connected 1,138 public facilities in 144 villages](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81395>). [In May 2026 the ministry said the project still extends internet to rural communities](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-taps-huawei-for-ai-rural-connectivity/GxwQDq1DOk9MlPVo>). [The fund aims at full rural connectivity by 2029](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/UASF_Annual_Report_2024_25_WEB.pdf>). [In May 2025 the state fibre operator BoFiNet said it had connected 223 of 503 gazetted villages with fibre](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/05/how-bofinet-plans-to-transform-botswana.html>).
