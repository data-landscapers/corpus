# Botswana

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Universal Access and Service Fund reports 51 more villages given 4G mobile network connectivity in 2024-25, bringing 279 villages connected by March 2025, plus 37 highway sites](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/UASF_Annual_Report_2024_25_WEB.pdf>). [Minister Segokgo says the government's village connectivity project, aimed at over 500 villages, had in its first phase connected 1,138 public facilities across 144 villages](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81395>). [The Ministry of Communications and Innovation says the government continues to expand high-speed internet access to rural communities, schools and healthcare facilities through the Village Connectivity Project](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-taps-huawei-for-ai-rural-connectivity/GxwQDq1DOk9MlPVo>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/UASF_Annual_Report_2024_25_WEB.pdf>).
