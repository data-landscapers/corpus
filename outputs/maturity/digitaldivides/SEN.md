# Senegal

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Under the New Deal Technologique the State planned to buy 5,000 Starlink kits at a preferential rate](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>). [They are for white zones, community Wi-Fi, schools and local authorities](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>). [On 10 April 2026 the telecoms minister launched free satellite kits in Kolda](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/new-deal-technologique-alioune-sall-lance-loffensive-contre-la-fracture-numerique-a-kolda/>). [The programme aims to connect one million people in remote zones by end 2026](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/new-deal-technologique-alioune-sall-lance-loffensive-contre-la-fracture-numerique-a-kolda/>). [The universal service fund plans 4G for 1,550 localities over 2024-2028](<https://fdsut.sn/projet-dacces-universel-fdsut/>). In March 2026 the Prime Minister said [a universal connectivity programme for white zones was under way](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>).
