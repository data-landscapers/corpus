# Senegal

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [Le Soleil reports that the government's New Deal Technologique provided for the State to buy 5,000 Starlink kits at a preferential rate to serve white zones, community wifi networks and the connection of schools and local authorities](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>). [Le Soleil reports that the communications and telecoms minister launched deployment of free Starlink satellite kits in Kolda on 10 April 2026, a government programme to connect 1 million Senegalese free by end 2026 in remote zones, with a first lot of 200 of 1,000 kits ready](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/new-deal-technologique-alioune-sall-lance-loffensive-contre-la-fracture-numerique-a-kolda/>). [The universal service fund's Projet d'Accès Universel plans 4G for 1,550 localities over 2024-2028](<https://fdsut.sn/projet-dacces-universel-fdsut/>). In March 2026 the Prime Minister said [a universal connectivity programme for white zones was under way](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>), aiming at free access for more than one million people, notably in rural areas.

**Gap measured.** No source held since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>).

**Gap narrow.** No source held since October 2023 gives rural internet use of 80 per cent or more of the urban rate.
