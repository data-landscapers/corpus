# Liberia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** On 26 October 2026 the Liberia Telecommunications Authority said the [Universal Access Fund already supports 15 towers](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/telecoms-le-liberia-identifie-les-zones-mal-desservies-pour-etendre-son-reseau>). The fund serves areas where low commercial returns hold back the networks. The Authority has [found sites for 15 more towers](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/telecoms-le-liberia-identifie-les-zones-mal-desservies-pour-etendre-son-reseau>), which would double the fund's count. In October 2025 the fund [installed Starlink in Vahun District, Lofa County](<https://universalaccessfund.com/starlink-installed-in-vahun-district-to-boost-internet-access-in-rural-liberia/>). It said the link was part of its mission to bring internet to remote communities and underserved regions. [In May 2025 the fund began restoring rural sites after nine of its twelve towers were found inactive for almost two years](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/0505-46729-liberia-launches-national-effort-to-restore-mobile-service-in-remote-areas>). [In September 2026 the Authority reported a new fund tower at Vahun](<https://lta.gov.lr/boosting-connectivity-in-rural-liberia/>).

**Gap measured.** The nearest fact is the Authority's [search for sites for 15 new towers](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/telecoms-le-liberia-identifie-les-zones-mal-desservies-pour-etendre-son-reseau>) under the Universal Access Fund. It was announced on 26 October 2026.

**Gap narrow.** The nearest fact is the [Starlink link the fund installed in Vahun District](<https://universalaccessfund.com/starlink-installed-in-vahun-district-to-boost-internet-access-in-rural-liberia/>) in October 2025. The district is in Lofa County.
