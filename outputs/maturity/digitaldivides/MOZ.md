# Mozambique

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In August 2026 the government said it had connected 149 localities to the internet, and 418 schools](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1808-140924-inclusion-numerique-le-mozambique-veut-connecter-300-localites-aux-services-telecoms>). [Its target is at least 300 localities, through the Rural Connectivity Project and the universal access fund](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1808-140924-inclusion-numerique-le-mozambique-veut-connecter-300-localites-aux-services-telecoms>). [In August 2026 the regulator INCM said a July 2026 decree lets it turn operators' fines into universal-service duties](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2608-141049-mozambique-les-amendes-telecoms-financent-desormais-l-inclusion-numerique>). [In October 2025 the regulator awarded Vodacom 60 rural mobile stations with public Wi-Fi in nine provinces](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/20/mozambique-to-install-60-new-mobile-stations-to-boost-rural-connectivity/>).
