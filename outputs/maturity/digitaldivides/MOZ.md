# Mozambique

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The government says it has already connected 149 localities to the internet and 418 schools](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1808-140924-inclusion-numerique-le-mozambique-veut-connecter-300-localites-aux-services-telecoms>). [The regulator INCM reported in a communique of 21 August 2026 that a new licensing regulation, adopted by decree in July 2026, lets it convert operators' fines into universal-service obligations to fund actions improving access in universal-service areas](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2608-141049-mozambique-les-amendes-telecoms-financent-desormais-l-inclusion-numerique>). [The Ministry of Communications and Digital Transformation announced on 17 August 2026 a target of extending telecom services to at least 300 localities, with the Rural Connectivity Project run through the FSAU with operators](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1808-140924-inclusion-numerique-le-mozambique-veut-connecter-300-localites-aux-services-telecoms>).
