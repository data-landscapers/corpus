# Burundi

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [In August 2026 the regulator ARCT said a new universal service fund will help rural people reach the internet](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/englishnews/number-of-internet-users-in-burundi-is-low-says-arct/>). [SETIC signed a $5.9 million contract with ONATEL for 92 rural hills lacking 2G or 3G](<https://www.burunditimes.com/5-9m-onatel-deal-marks-new-push-for-digital-access-in-rural-burundi/>). [It is expected to serve about 372,000 people](<https://www.burunditimes.com/5-9m-onatel-deal-marks-new-push-for-digital-access-in-rural-burundi/>). [PAFEN and Lumitel signed a USD 10 million contract for rural 4G, 44 per cent paid by the government](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>).

**Gap measured.** No source found since October 2023 gives internet use in rural and in urban areas. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/englishnews/number-of-internet-users-in-burundi-is-low-says-arct/>).
