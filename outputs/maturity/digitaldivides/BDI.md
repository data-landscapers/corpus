# Burundi

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [ARCT acting director Hermalas Nahimana said the government has created a universal service fund that will help the rural population to have access to the internet](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/englishnews/number-of-internet-users-in-burundi-is-low-says-arct/>). [SETIC (also PAFEN coordinator) signed a $5,907,000 contract with ONATEL to deploy infrastructure across 92 rural hills lacking 2G or 3G coverage, expected to benefit about 372,035 people, said Burundi Times](<https://www.burunditimes.com/5-9m-onatel-deal-marks-new-push-for-digital-access-in-rural-burundi/>). [PAFEN, the government digital-inclusion programme, and Lumitel signed a USD 10 million contract to extend 4G broadband in rural areas, co-financed 44 per cent by the government through PAFEN, says SETIC](<https://setic.gov.bi/connectivite-numerique-haut-debit-4g-en-milieu-rural/>).
