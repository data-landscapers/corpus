# Namibia

## Rural internet gap

**Rural connectivity programme.** [The Kavango East regional education director told parliament's ICT committee the region had received only one tower through the Universal Service Fund](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/poor-network-coverage-leaves-kavango-schools-struggling/>). [CRAN states the Universal Service Fund tower at Epinga launched on 19 November 2025 and the one at Ehomba on 30 January 2026, two village towers](<https://www.cran.na/driving-access-to-information-in-the-digital-age/>). [Namibia Daily News (Xinhua) reports MTC was awarded N$32.3 million under Phase 2 of CRAN's Universal Service Fund programme to deploy nine network solutions, including 10 towers, to extend connectivity to underserved areas](<https://namibiadailynews.info/namibia-accelerates-network-modernization-to-meet-growing-connectivity-needs/>). [NDP6 says underserved areas will receive broadband infrastructure, ICT-equipped schools and community internet centres to reduce the urban-rural divide](<https://neweralive.na/namibia-targets-full-digital-transformation/>).
