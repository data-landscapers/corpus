# Somalia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** In January 2024 the U.S. International Trade Administration [put mobile money market penetration at an estimated 73 percent](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/somalia-information-communication-and-technology-ict>), with three licensed providers. Hormuud's CEO says [its EVC Plus is used by about 90% of the population](<https://itweb.africa/article/hormuud-anchors-somalias-digital-economy/Olx4zMkaZozv56km>) for retail payments, salaries and remittances. The Central Bank says [mobile money operators serve most Somalis in their daily transactions](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>). Money [largely stays within each operator's wallets](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/somalias-mobile-money-remains-locked-in-silos/mYZRXM9gbKXvOgA8>), ITWeb reports.

**Between providers.** The Central Bank says SIPS, launched in January 2025, [carries real-time transfers between banks, mobile wallets and point-of-sale terminals](<https://centralbank.gov.so/nps/>), whatever the provider. SONNA reports [it carries transfers between individuals and payments to merchants](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>).

**National switch.** The Central Bank [launched the national Somalia Instant Payment System in early 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2026/01/07/how-ethiopia-somalia-and-djibouti-building-africas-digital-powerhouses/>), TechCabal says. It links mobile wallets and banks. A 2026 column says the Central Bank [also launched the Somali Payment Switch](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). Banks, wallets and points of sale connect through them in real time.
