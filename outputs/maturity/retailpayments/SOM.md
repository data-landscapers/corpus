# Somalia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Money largely remains within individual operators' mobile money wallets, with Hormuud Telecom operating the EVC Plus platform](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/somalias-mobile-money-remains-locked-in-silos/mYZRXM9gbKXvOgA8>). [Guleid Osman writes that EVC Plus is Somalia's leading mobile-money platform, used by households to buy food, pay school fees and rent, and send money](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The Central Bank says mobile money operators serve most Somalis in their daily financial transactions](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>). [SONNA reports SIPS currently carries transfers between individuals and payments to merchants, and that mobile wallets carry most day-to-day transactions in Somalia](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>). [Hormuud's CEO says EVC Plus mobile money is used by approximately 90% of the population for retail payments, salaries and remittances](<https://itweb.africa/article/hormuud-anchors-somalias-digital-economy/Olx4zMkaZozv56km>).

**Between providers.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia states SIPS supports real-time transfers among banks, mobile wallets and points of sale](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [The Central Bank of Somalia says SIPS, launched January 2025, enables real-time 24/7 transfers between banks, mobile wallets and point-of-sale terminals regardless of provider](<https://centralbank.gov.so/nps/>).

**National switch.** [Guleid Osman writes that the Central Bank of Somalia launched the Somali Payment Switch and SIPS, which it states connects banks, mobile wallets and points of sale in real time](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Sep/206320/strengthening_somalias_central_bank_and_securing_the_future_of_digital_payments.aspx>). [TechCabal says the Central Bank of Somalia launched the national Somalia Instant Payment System in early 2025 to link mobile wallets and the banking sector](<https://techcabal.com/2026/01/07/how-ethiopia-somalia-and-djibouti-building-africas-digital-powerhouses/>).
