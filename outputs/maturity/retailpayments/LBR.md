# Liberia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Liberia's Inclusive Instant Payment System has processed more than 5.3 million transactions since its December 2025 launch, and 2025 mobile money transactions reached about L$585.88 billion and US$6.92 billion, per the Central Bank of Liberia governor](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-s-instant-payment-system-crosses-5-3m-transactions/article_b75bc316-97ae-459f-840d-c2822b8e7a84.html>). [The speaker says mobile money operators were integrated at the initial stage of the instant payment system and civil servants' salaries were paid to wallets](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>). [Lonestar Cell MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money Liberia operate mobile money services in Liberia](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/africa-s-digital-payment-revolution-why-liberia-must-move-faster-on-inclusive-instant-payment-systems/article_78def9c4-1c3a-4997-80e5-76002c64d288.html>). [Finance Minister Ngafuan says Liberia's mobile wallets now number more than 11 million](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-expands-digital-finance/>).

**Between providers.** [The Inclusive Instant Payment System lets customers of Lonestar MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money Liberia make real-time interoperable transactions across the two networks, per the Central Bank of Liberia governor](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-s-instant-payment-system-crosses-5-3m-transactions/article_b75bc316-97ae-459f-840d-c2822b8e7a84.html>). [The Central Bank of Liberia deputy governor says the recently launched instant and interoperable payment system enables payments across banks and mobile network operators](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-backs-lra-s-move-to-billion-revenue-drive/article_7ef5e649-7ccd-4187-91bc-d17d1a19505c.html>).
