# Liberia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Mobile money transactions reached about L$585.88 billion and US$6.92 billion in 2025](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-s-instant-payment-system-crosses-5-3m-transactions/article_b75bc316-97ae-459f-840d-c2822b8e7a84.html>), the central bank governor says. [Lonestar Cell MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money Liberia run the services](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/africa-s-digital-payment-revolution-why-liberia-must-move-faster-on-inclusive-instant-payment-systems/article_78def9c4-1c3a-4997-80e5-76002c64d288.html>). [In July 2026 the finance minister put mobile wallets at more than 11 million](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-expands-digital-finance/>). [Civil servants' salaries have been paid to wallets](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>), a speaker at an AfricaNenda event said.

**Between providers.** [The Central Bank of Liberia launched the Inclusive Instant Payment System on 16 December 2025](<https://cbl.org.lr/index.php/media/features-articles/special-remark-executive-governor-henry-f-saamoi-launch-iips-december-16>). Its pilot phase carried person-to-person transfers. [Customers of the two mobile money networks can now pay each other in real time](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-s-instant-payment-system-crosses-5-3m-transactions/article_b75bc316-97ae-459f-840d-c2822b8e7a84.html>), the governor says. [It had carried more than 5.3 million transactions since launch](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-s-instant-payment-system-crosses-5-3m-transactions/article_b75bc316-97ae-459f-840d-c2822b8e7a84.html>).

**National switch.** [In August 2026 the deputy governor said the system enables payments across banks and mobile network operators](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/cbl-backs-lra-s-move-to-billion-revenue-drive/article_7ef5e649-7ccd-4187-91bc-d17d1a19505c.html>). [The central bank gave banks and mobile money operators access to it](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/africanenda_bringing-every-stakeholder-to-the-table-isnt-activity-7494679350248820736--7a1>), the AfricaNenda speaker said.
