# Eritrea

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Himbol's terms of service, in force from November 2025, describe its M-Nakfa mobile money wallet](<https://erihimbol.com/assets/Himbol%20-%20Terms%20and%20Conditions%20of%20Service-DOgGzzwW.pdf>). It carries cash-in and cash-out, transfers between people, merchant payments and utility bills, under Bank of Eritrea oversight. [Himbol's customer guide describes the same services under Bank of Eritrea licensing](<https://erihimbol.com/assets/Himbol%20-%20Customers%20and%20Partners%20Guide-CrBqoCzf.pdf>). [The Fintech Times said in 2026 that wallets linked to the state-owned EriTel are the main entry point to digital financial services](<https://thefintechtimes.com/eritreas-fintech-ecosystem-in-2026-and-financial-inclusion/>). It called their functions still limited.

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in November 2025 Himbol's M-Nakfa wallet offered cash-in, cash-out and person-to-person transfers](<https://erihimbol.com/assets/Himbol%20-%20Terms%20and%20Conditions%20of%20Service-DOgGzzwW.pdf>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 mobile wallets linked to state-owned EriTel were the main entry point to digital financial services](<https://thefintechtimes.com/eritreas-fintech-ecosystem-in-2026-and-financial-inclusion/>).
