# Zambia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Times of Zambia says Lipila lets businesses and institutions accept Airtel Money, MTN Mobile Money, Zamtel Money and bank payments, and cites 15 million active mobile money wallets in 2026](<https://times.co.zm/cashless-shift-how-hobbiton-is-driving-fintech-agenda/>). [MTN MoMo has more than five million users in Zambia, according to the operator, and MoMo customers can pay at FNB point-of-sale terminals](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/momo-fnb-launch-zambia-atm-cash-out/G98YdqLGnQ9MX2PD>). [MTN MoMo's chief executive said the service had facilitated more than K7 billion in unsecured loans through its digital financial services ecosystem in Zambia](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/11/pdu-ppdf-engage-mtn-momo-on-financial-inclusion-and-economic-transformation/>). [GSMA reports Zambia taxes mobile money under its Levy Act 2023, with the levy doubled in 2025](<https://www.gsma.com/mobilefordevelopment/>).

**Between providers.** [An existing MTN MoMo and FNB partnership already lets customers transfer funds between MoMo wallets and FNB accounts, and the two launched MoMo cash-out at FNB ATMs without a card or FNB account](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/momo-fnb-launch-zambia-atm-cash-out/G98YdqLGnQ9MX2PD>). [The National Financial Switch supports wallet-to-bank and bank-to-wallet transfers as well as merchant payments, per ITWeb quoting a Sybrin manager](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).

**National switch.** [The National Financial Switch connects banks, non-bank financial institutions, payment service providers, fintechs and mobile money operators, per ITWeb quoting a Sybrin manager](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).
