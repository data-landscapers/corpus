# Guinea

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Conakry Terminal says [clients can pay online through MyTouchPoint, including MoMo, and through the Akiba Mobile app](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/09/conakry-terminal-de-nouveaux-paiements-en-ligne-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation-des-services/>). [Orange Finance Mobile Guinee presented its mobile money merchant payments in Conakry on 23 July 2026](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/paiements-digitaux-ofmg-la-cciag-et-la-bcrg-engagent-une-demarche-pour-encourager-les-transactions-electroniques-en-guinee/>). [BGB Solutions launched KS-WALLET, an e-money app for deposits, QR payments and transfers](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/04/monnaie-electronique-bgb-solutions-s-a-lance-son-application-ks-wallet/>), reported in April 2026. [Orange Money launched a bank access service with Banque Nationale de Guinee](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/03/orange-money-lance-le-service-acces-a-ma-banque-avec-la-banque-nationale-de-guinee-bng/>), reported in March 2026.

**Between providers.** [AGUIDUTEN welcomed the launch of NimbaPay, the national instant payment platform, which it says promotes interoperability between banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>). [The BCRG's supervision director general cited NimbaPay as a platform ensuring interoperability between banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/07/paiements-digitaux-ofmg-la-cciag-et-la-bcrg-engagent-une-demarche-pour-encourager-les-transactions-electroniques-en-guinee/>). [Mediaguinee says KS-WALLET sends money in Guinea and abroad directly to bank accounts and mobile wallets](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/04/monnaie-electronique-bgb-solutions-s-a-lance-son-application-ks-wallet/>). By 15 October 2026 [seven institutions were connected to NimbaPay, which had carried about 67,000 transactions worth over GNF 68 billion](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites/2610-142742-guinee-nimbapay-traite-7-6-millions-de-transactions-et-connecte-7-etablissements-en-4-mois>).

**National switch.** [AGUIDUTEN described NimbaPay as the national instant payment platform](<https://focusguinee.info/2026/07/26/laguiduten-salue-le-lancement-de-nimbapay-la-plateforme-nationale-de-paiement-instantane/>). It connects banks, e-money institutions and microfinance institutions. The central bank and Guineenne de Monetique launched it. [NimbaPay went live on 23 July 2026, linking banks and electronic money institutions](<https://www.itnewsafrica.com/2026/07/guinea-launches-nimbapay-opening-a-new-era-of-instant-and-inclusive-digital-payments/>).
