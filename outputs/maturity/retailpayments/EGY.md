# Egypt

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [An analyst says a mobile wallet transfer moves e-money within the service provider's ecosystem, and InstaPay moves value between existing bank accounts](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/15/the-digital-egp-wont-look-different-at-checkout-but-it-will-work-nothing-like-instapay/>). [Ahram Online describes Instapay as a state-backed mobile application for electronic payments, licensed by the Central Bank of Egypt and in operation](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [NTRA reports 57.01 million telecom-operator mobile wallets in Egypt in H1 2026, with 2.22 billion transactions worth EGP 2.96 trillion](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/ar/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%b2-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%82%d9%88%d9%85%d9%8a-%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d8%b8%d9%8a%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa%d8%b5%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b5%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%aa-58/>). [Youm7 reports 57.01 million telecom-operated mobile wallets in H1 2026 (up 23%), with 2.22 billion transactions worth EGP 2.96 trillion, per the NTRA H1 2026 report](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81-%D8%BA%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1/7540929>). [Orange Egypt says its Orange Cash wallet had about 10 million users in October 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/20/orange-egypt-botim-uae-egypt-remittances/>).

**Between providers.** [NTRA reports 78% of wallet deposit transactions in H1 2026 were made from the wallet holder's bank account via InstaPay](<https://www.tra.gov.eg/ar/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d9%87%d8%a7%d8%b2-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%82%d9%88%d9%85%d9%8a-%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d8%b8%d9%8a%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa%d8%b5%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%8a%d8%b5%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%aa-58/>). [Youm7, citing NTRA, says InstaPay bank-account-to-wallet transfers made up 78% of wallet deposits by count and 58% by value in H1 2026](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81-%D8%BA%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1/7540929>).

**National switch.** [The InstaPay instant payments network, launched March 2022, handles personal, commercial and account-to-wallet transfers, with over 1.1 billion operations, per banking expert Walid Adel](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8325979>).
