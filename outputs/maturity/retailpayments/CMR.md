# Cameroon

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Tech Africa News reports that Looping Binary has launched LBPay in Cameroon, a platform to send and receive money, manage wallets and pay bills and airtime](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/looping-binary-launches-lbpay-digital-payments-cameroon/>). [Cameroon has two licensed payment institutions, Orange Money Cameroun and Mobile Money Corporation (which runs MTN Mobile Money), per DGTCFM data updated July 2026, and mobile money had 30.99 million accounts in 2024 per BEAC](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1709-141667-cameroun-konoom-obtient-son-agrement-et-se-lance-sur-le-marche-du-paiement-mobile>). [MTN Cameroon's Mobile Money Corporation runs MoMo accounts on which it presented MoMo Helep Pro, offering employees digital advances up to 100,000 FCFA repayable in 30 days, and a Bulk Payment system for instant mass payments, per Le Jour](<https://lejour.cm/economie-numerique-momo-helep-pour-revolutionner-la-tresorerie-des-pme/>).

**Between providers.** [Tech Africa News reports that LBPay lets users transfer funds across different mobile money networks](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/looping-binary-launches-lbpay-digital-payments-cameroon/>). [BEAC says the QR code launched 29 July 2026 lets any bank or microfinance mobile app read any merchant's code regardless of institution or CEMAC country](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including Cameroon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
