# Botswana

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 Orange Money, Mascom's MyZaka and BTC's Smega operated as mobile money platforms](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/fnbb-muscles-into-mobile-money-race/news>). [Customers also receive money through FNBB's eWallet](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/fnbb-muscles-into-mobile-money-race/news>). [In August 2026 Mukuru launched a Visa-branded card linked to its Mukuru Wallet](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/mukuru-launches-mobile-wallet-linked-visa-card-in-botswana>). [Mukuru Wallet customers already manage money by phone, and Mukuru is a registered Electronic Payment Service Provider](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/mukuru-launches-mobile-wallet-linked-visa-card-in-botswana>). [Orange Money is Botswana's most dominant mobile money service and has added Visa 3D Secure for its virtual cards with Paymentology](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/orange-money-paymentology-strengthen-online-payment-security/news>). [FinScope 2024 found 75% of Batswana use mobile money, and 33% of unbanked adults already use it](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/16/botswana-future-of-banking-not-start-with-bank/>).

**Between providers.** [Mobile money in Botswana is now interoperable with traditional bank accounts](<https://thevoicebw.com/mobile-money-madness/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 mobile money in Botswana was interoperable with bank accounts](<https://thevoicebw.com/mobile-money-madness/>).
