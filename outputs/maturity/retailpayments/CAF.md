# Central African Republic

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Telecel Money mobile money is in service in the Central African Republic, with many ENERCA subscribers already paying electricity bills through it before the official launch on 4 August 2026, according to Telecel and ENERCA](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/08/07/centrafrique-telecel-et-enerca-signent-un-partenariat-pour-digitaliser-le-paiement-des-factures-delectricite/>). [Orange Centrafrique provides its Orange Money mobile payment infrastructure, described as widely used across the country, to pay the DPI import fee, per the finance ministry's chief of staff](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>). [Orange mobile money transfer and mobile payment services operate in Batangafo, but have been down for nearly a month, per local testimony reported by Corbeau News](<https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/centrafrique-a-batangafo-un-mois-sans-reseau-orange-bloque-les-communications-et-les-services-financiers/>).

**Between providers.** [The CEMAC QR Code standard, launched 29 July 2026 across six states including the Central African Republic, lets a client with a bank account, mobile money wallet or microfinance account pay by scan whatever their institution](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY gives all-to-all interoperability between banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money operators in the six CEMAC states including the Central African Republic](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including the Central African Republic](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
