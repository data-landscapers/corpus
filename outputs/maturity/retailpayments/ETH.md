# Ethiopia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Capital says Ethio Telecom's telebirr is the dominant mobile-money operator with more than 60 million subscribers, and Safaricom Ethiopia's M-Pesa has also expanded](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [President Taye Atske Selassie said mobile money and mobile banking accounts reached 257 million and carried more than ETB 24 trillion in the first nine months of 2026](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9786837>). [ZooryaPay, a licensed payment platform, processed over ETB 120 billion in merchant transactions in the past year, per the report](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>). [Team Shega's diagnostic says Ethiopia has opened more than 135 million mobile wallets and grown its agent network fifteen-fold in four years](<https://akofada.shega.co/insights/research-reports/mobile-money-in-ethiopia-a-diagnostic-report>). [M-PESA Ethiopia lets customers withdraw cash at supported Awash Bank ATMs from the M-PESA app, with Tech Africa News reporting real-time processing](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/m-pesa-ethiopia-cardless-atm-withdrawals-awash-bank/>). [NIDP names Telebirr and M-Pesa as mobile wallets harmonised with Fayda](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Between providers.** [Capital says telebirr and M-Pesa are both connected through EthSwitch, which provides interoperability linking banks, payment-service providers and merchants](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [The ETTA/Visa devices interoperate fully with Ethiopia's national switch and existing financial institutions](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>). [M-PESA says its wallet customers can withdraw at Awash Bank ATMs, and expects this to strengthen interoperability between financial services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/m-pesa-ethiopia-cardless-atm-withdrawals-awash-bank/>).

**National switch.** [Capital describes EthSwitch as Ethiopia's national payment switch linking banks, payment-service providers, merchants and the mobile-money platforms](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [EthSwitch is described as Ethiopia's national payment switch, linking banks, payment institutions and other participants with instant-payment infrastructure and interoperable QR payments](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7138/ethswitch-takes-ethiopias-digital-payments-push-to-global-fintech-stage>).
