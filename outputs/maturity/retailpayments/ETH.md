# Ethiopia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Ethio Telecom's Telebirr [had 60.6 million registered users and carried 2.61 billion transactions worth 4.19 trillion birr in 2025/26](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/09/ethiopia-gdp-rebase-captures-growth-in-digital-financial-services/>). Safaricom's [M-Pesa has also expanded](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). The President said [mobile money and mobile banking accounts reached 257 million in the first nine months of 2026](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9786837>), carrying more than ETB 24 trillion. Ethiopia [has opened more than 135 million mobile wallets](<https://akofada.shega.co/insights/research-reports/mobile-money-in-ethiopia-a-diagnostic-report>), a diagnostic report says. M-Pesa customers [can withdraw cash at Awash Bank ATMs](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/m-pesa-ethiopia-cardless-atm-withdrawals-awash-bank/>). [A National Bank directive of May 2025 caps person-to-person e-money transfers at Birr 75,000 a day and QR merchant payments at Birr 250,000](<https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ONPS102025-Oversight-of-the-Natioanl-Payment-System-Licensing-and-Authorization-of-Payment-Instrument-Issuer-Amendment-Directive.pdf>).

**Between providers.** [Capital says telebirr and M-Pesa are both connected through EthSwitch, which provides interoperability linking banks, payment-service providers and merchants](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [The ETTA/Visa devices interoperate fully with Ethiopia's national switch and existing financial institutions](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/07/ethiopias-commerce-goes-contactless-with-new-visa-and-etta-infrastructure-agreement/>). [M-PESA says its wallet customers can withdraw at Awash Bank ATMs, and expects this to strengthen interoperability between financial services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/m-pesa-ethiopia-cardless-atm-withdrawals-awash-bank/>).

**National switch.** [Capital describes EthSwitch as Ethiopia's national payment switch linking banks, payment-service providers, merchants and the mobile-money platforms](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [EthSwitch is described as Ethiopia's national payment switch, linking banks, payment institutions and other participants with instant-payment infrastructure and interoperable QR payments](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7138/ethswitch-takes-ethiopias-digital-payments-push-to-global-fintech-stage>).
