# Cape Verde

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Televinti4 mobile app, launched in 2021 and run by SISP, lets users make transfers, QR Code and Token payments, pay bills and top up phones, and about 15,600 people use it, under 5% of roughly 329,000 Vinti4 network users, per Expresso das Ilhas](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2024/03/03/app-coloca-vinti4-no-telemovel-mas-taxa-de-penetracao-continua-baixa/90231>). [A study presented at a Banco de Cabo Verde forum names one operator already in the e-money market, Banco BAI/Makeba, while recommending against building a new e-money entity from scratch (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/estudo-servicos-gratuitos-sao-maior-constrangimento-para-moeda-eletronica-em-cabo-verde/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2024/03/03/app-coloca-vinti4-no-telemovel-mas-taxa-de-penetracao-continua-baixa/90231>).
