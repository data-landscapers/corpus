# Cape Verde

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Banco de Cabo Verde's 2025 payment system report counts 50,866 users of televinti4, which puts the vinti4 card on a mobile phone](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/09/25/mais-cabo-verdianos-com-contas-bancarias-e-maior-uso-de-pagamentos-digitais/104797>). They made 1,284,291 transactions moving money in 2025, Expresso das Ilhas reports. [The app was launched in 2021, is run by SISP and carries transfers, QR code payments, bills and phone top-ups](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2024/03/03/app-coloca-vinti4-no-telemovel-mas-taxa-de-penetracao-continua-baixa/90231>). In March 2024 about 15,600 people used it, under 5% of some 329,000 Vinti4 network users. [A study presented at a Banco de Cabo Verde forum in 2021 named one e-money operator, Banco BAI/Makeba](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/estudo-servicos-gratuitos-sao-maior-constrangimento-para-moeda-eletronica-em-cabo-verde/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service.
