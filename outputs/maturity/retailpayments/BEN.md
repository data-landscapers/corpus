# Benin

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Arcep counted 11,649,735 active mobile financial services accounts at 31 December 2025](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/communications-electroniques-lessor-des-usages-numeriques-transforme-le-marche-beninois>). MTN Mobile Money held 51%, Celtiis Cash 27% and Moov Money 22%. [MTN Mobile Money changed its account ceilings from 2 November 2026](<https://www.24haubenin.info/?Communique-de-MTN-Mobile-Money-Benin=>). [Mobile money is described as the standard for transactions in 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/le-beninois-phital-azah-transforme-whatsapp-et-mobile-money-en-leviers-de-vente>), and the Xedo platform settles merchant sales through it. [Agents in Cotonou serve Moov Money and MTN MoMo customers](<https://fraternite.bj/mobile-money-au-benin-une-debrouille-qui-soulage-les-poches/>). [A World Bank rural project recorded 219,391 users of digital financial services through mobile money](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041725164525253/pdf/P162599-60aa9f82-56f6-4ce1-a739-80ee59eafd40.pdf>).

**Between providers.** [The BCEAO launched PI-SPI, its regional instant payment platform, on 30 September 2025](<https://lanation.bj/economie/lancement-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-interoperable-la-pi-spi-une-solution-rapide-et-inclusive-au-profit-des-citoyens-uemoa>). [Bank transfers can credit mobile money wallets in seconds through it, and the reverse](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [At 31 July 2026 nine of Benin's 15 banks and financial establishments were connected, with Moov Money and MTN, two of its three mobile money issuers](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/08/14/marche-beninois-du-pi-spi-qui-manque-encore-a-lappel-du-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao/>). [From 2 November 2026 MTN, Moov Money and UBA Benin route transfers between wallets, other operators and banks through PI-SPI](<https://triomphemag.com/interoperabilite-du-mobile-money-au-benin-pi-spi-entre-en-vigueur-ce-qui-change-reellement-pour-les-utilisateurs-des-ce-2-novembre/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 the BCEAO's PI-SPI platform moved money between bank accounts and mobile wallets in seconds](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>).
