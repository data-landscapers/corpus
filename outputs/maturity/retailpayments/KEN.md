# Kenya

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Communications Authority counted 54 million mobile money subscriptions in June 2026, M-PESA holding 88.8% and Airtel Money 11.1%](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/wayawaya-partners-choice-bank/>). [Safaricom says M-PESA scan-to-pay works at more than 60,000 merchant outlets](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/01/safaricom-taps-pesapals-30000-pos-terminals-to-take-m-pesa-contactless/>). [Pesapal's 30,000 terminals now take M-PESA tap-to-pay and QR payments](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/01/safaricom-taps-pesapals-30000-pos-terminals-to-take-m-pesa-contactless/>). [Equity Bank's fintech arm has launched the EMMA wallet for transfers, airtime, bills and savings](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/why-equity-bank-is-building-a-neo-wallet-for-everyday-cash/>). [Digital Banker Africa says a usage gap remains despite Kenya's lead in mobile money](<https://digitalbankerafrica.com/kenya-connects-digital-payments-and-trade-finance-in-push-for-global-commerce/>).

**Between providers.** [TechCabal says Kenyan commercial banks compete over how seamlessly customers can move funds between accounts and mobile wallets](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/27/kenyas-payments-revolution-cannot-end-with-mobile-money/>). [Pesalink, run by the Kenya Bankers Association's IPSL, connects banks, telcos and microfinance institutions (TechCabal)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/04/03/kenswitch-visa-deal-stakes-kenya-payments-overhaul/>). [Transfers between M-PESA, banks and other mobile money services such as Airtel Money pass through several platforms, per TechCabal](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/20/m-pesa-to-stop-sharing-full-phone-numbers-with-merchants-banks-by-end-of-2026/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/27/kenyas-payments-revolution-cannot-end-with-mobile-money/>).
