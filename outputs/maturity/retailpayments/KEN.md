# Kenya

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Communications Authority counted 54 million mobile money subscriptions in June 2026, M-PESA holding 88.8% and Airtel Money 11.1%](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/06/wayawaya-partners-choice-bank/>). [Safaricom says M-PESA scan-to-pay works at more than 60,000 merchant outlets](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/01/safaricom-taps-pesapals-30000-pos-terminals-to-take-m-pesa-contactless/>). [Pesapal's 30,000 terminals now take M-PESA tap-to-pay and QR payments](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/01/safaricom-taps-pesapals-30000-pos-terminals-to-take-m-pesa-contactless/>). [Equity Bank's fintech arm has launched the EMMA wallet for transfers, airtime, bills and savings](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/why-equity-bank-is-building-a-neo-wallet-for-everyday-cash/>). [Digital Banker Africa says a usage gap remains despite Kenya's lead in mobile money](<https://digitalbankerafrica.com/kenya-connects-digital-payments-and-trade-finance-in-push-for-global-commerce/>).

**Between providers.** Money moves between banks and wallets over more than one switch. [Pesalink, run by the Kenya Bankers Association's IPSL, connects banks, telcos and microfinance institutions](<https://techcabal.com/2026/04/03/kenswitch-visa-deal-stakes-kenya-payments-overhaul/>), TechCabal reports. [In November 2026 Kenswitch said it links banks, SACCOs, microfinance institutions and mobile money providers](<https://www.business-standard.com/content/press-releases-ani/kenswitch-partners-with-payu-s-wibmo-to-strengthen-online-card-security-in-kenya-126110200460_1.html>). [Transfers between M-PESA, banks and other wallets such as Airtel Money pass through several platforms](<https://techcabal.com/2026/03/20/m-pesa-to-stop-sharing-full-phone-numbers-with-merchants-banks-by-end-of-2026/>). [Banks compete over how easily customers move funds between accounts and wallets](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/27/kenyas-payments-revolution-cannot-end-with-mobile-money/>).

**National switch.** [Kenswitch calls itself Kenya's domestic payment switch](<https://www.business-standard.com/content/press-releases-ani/kenswitch-partners-with-payu-s-wibmo-to-strengthen-online-card-security-in-kenya-126110200460_1.html>), regulated by the Central Bank. It says its members reach more than 2,500 ATMs, 50,000 POS terminals and 80,000 agents. [Pesalink connects banks and telcos too](<https://techcabal.com/2026/04/03/kenswitch-visa-deal-stakes-kenya-payments-overhaul/>).
