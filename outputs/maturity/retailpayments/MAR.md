# Morocco

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Morocco has wallets and an interoperable mobile payment system, though Switch Al Maghrib's director general says traffic on it remains limited](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>). [CMI says about 10 million holders of MarocPay payment-account wallets can settle bills through Fatourati](<https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/fatourati-collect-le-cmi-ouvre-un-acces-simple-et-immediat-au-reglement-multicanal>). [Bank Al-Maghrib reports 13.7 million m-wallets outstanding in 2024, up from 6.3 million in 2021](<https://www.bkam.ma/content/download/844040/9139542/Rapport%20annuel%20sur%20l'Inclusion%20financière%202024.pdf>). [Bank Al-Maghrib reports 20 M-Wallet offers on the market at end-2023, 12 issued by payment institutions, with over 10 million wallets](<https://www.bkam.ma/fr/content/download/844036/9139518/Rapport%20SNIF%202023.pdf>).

**Between providers.** [Switch Al Maghrib's director general said Morocco laid early the foundations of an interoperable mobile payment system, now in place](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>). [CMI says its Fatourati platform connects 32 banks and payment institutions and takes payments through mobile banking, e-banking, wallets, branches and ATMs across more than 70 channels](<https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/le-cmi-lance-fatourati-agregateur-et-ouvre-sa-plateforme-aux-fintechs-et-editeurs-de>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lematin.ma/economie/paiement-instantane-les-rails-sont-la-les-usages-restent-a-construire/367355>).
