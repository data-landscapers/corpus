# Burundi

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [BurundiPay, the national instant payment system run by the BRB, had about 224,000 users and about BIF 4.29 billion in cumulative transactions on 4 August 2026, with customers paying from bank accounts or e-wallets](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). [BurundiPay lets users pay and transfer funds in real time from bank accounts and mobile wallets, the BRB says](<https://www.brb.bi/node/3293>). [Burundi Pay, the national instant payment system, has been operational since March 2026, per the central bank's deputy governor](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/news/east-africa/burundi-to-join-east-african-payment-network-by-december-2026-5562616>).

**Between providers.** [On BurundiPay 17 institutions (5 commercial banks, 10 microfinance institutions, 2 payment institutions) are live, and customers can transfer money and pay instantly from bank accounts or e-wallets, per the BRB](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). [The BRB says BurundiPay already allows exchanges between banks, microfinance institutions and mobile wallets locally](<https://www.brb.bi/node/3303>).

**National switch.** [BurundiPay is described as the national instant payment system run by the BRB, live since 5 March 2026 and launched publicly on 23 April 2026](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>). [BurundiPay is described as the national instant payment system connecting commercial banks, microfinance institutions and payment service providers in a single network, also reachable by USSD](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-real-time-payments-platform-backed-by-world-bank>).
