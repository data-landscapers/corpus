# Sierra Leone

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Orange Money SL and other mobile money providers operate in Sierra Leone, and the workshop sought to secure mobile money services there](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/sierra-leone-validates-national-roadmap-to-tackle-mobile-and-digital-fraud/>). [Orange Sierra Leone said Orange Money lets customers send and receive money, pay bills, and shop online and in stores](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/18/orange-sierra-leone-launches-scam-alert-tool-to-combat-fraud/>). [The Sierra Leone Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation says mobile money occupies a growing place in the country's financial transactions, including on Orange's network](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/finance/sierra-leone-vers-une-feuille-de-route-contre-la-fraude-liee-au-mobile-money>). [The Minister said the government has increased financial inclusion through mobile money services, and that Orange Sierra Leone recorded about 390 mobile money fraud complaints in 2026](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-bah-government-on-mobile-money-fraud/>).

**Between providers.** [Sierra Leone launched real-time transfers between mobile money operators, banks, and mobile money and bank accounts on 13 February 2025, with seven banks and two mobile money providers connected](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/sierra-leone-launches-national-instant-payment-system>). [The Bank of Sierra Leone says its Instant Payment System carried about 600,000 transactions between banks and mobile money platforms by late 2025](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/sierra-leone-pushes-cash-lite-economy/>).

**National switch.** [The Bank of Sierra Leone's national instant payment switch connects mobile money accounts and bank accounts, launched 13 February 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/finance/sierra-leone-launches-national-instant-payment-system>).
