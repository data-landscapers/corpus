# Djibouti

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Djibouti Telecom's D-Money launched its new smartphone application on 26 September 2024](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>). [Open to all Djiboutians since 21 September, it serves money transfers, bill payment and online purchases of services](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>). [In November 2020 the Ministry of Communication said D-Money, then recently launched, already had thousands of subscribers, three ministers among them](<https://communication.gouv.dj/d-money-mcpt-le-ministre-de-la-communication-lance-officiellement-le-nouveau-systeme-service-digital-mobile-money-appele-d-money/>). [It offered transfers, payments, bill payment and airtime through agents and an app](<https://communication.gouv.dj/d-money-mcpt-le-ministre-de-la-communication-lance-officiellement-le-nouveau-systeme-service-digital-mobile-money-appele-d-money/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 D-Money customers were sending money from its mobile wallet app](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/djiboutimoney_dmoney-transfertinternational-djibouti-activity-7355990440086515714-KwfX>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in September 2024 D-Money's app opened to all Djiboutians for transfers, bill payment and online purchases](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>).
