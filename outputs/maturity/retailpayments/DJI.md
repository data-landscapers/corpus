# Djibouti

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Djibouti Telecom's D-Money officially launched its new smartphone application on 26 September 2024, open to all Djiboutians since 21 September, for money transfers, bill payment and online purchases of services](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>). [The Ministry of Communication says Djibouti Telecom's D-Money, a recently launched mobile money service for transfers, payments, bill payment and airtime through agents and an app, already has thousands of subscribers, including three ministers who opened wallets (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://communication.gouv.dj/d-money-mcpt-le-ministre-de-la-communication-lance-officiellement-le-nouveau-systeme-service-digital-mobile-money-appele-d-money/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://d-money.dj/lancement-de-la-nouvelle-plateforme-d-money/>).
