# Ghana

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Bank of Ghana reports about 85.8 million registered mobile money accounts in August 2026, with about 26.4 million used in the previous ninety days](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>). [Governor Asiama said Ghana's mobile money transactions exceeded one billion in August 2026 for the first time, worth GH¢519 billion, up 46.5% on the previous year](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [The Bank of Ghana reports mobile money in August 2026 at 1,046 million transactions worth GHC 518.8 billion, with 85.8 million registered and 26.4 million active accounts](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Summary-of-Economic-and-Financial-Data-September-2026.pdf>). [Bank of Ghana data put mobile money transactions at GH¢4.54 trillion in 2025, up 50.8% on 2024](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/09/dlocal-ghana-secures-enhanced-payments-licence/>).

**Between providers.** [The Bank of Ghana reports mobile money interoperability in August 2026 at 35.7 million transactions worth GHC 6.5 billion](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Summary-of-Economic-and-Financial-Data-September-2026.pdf>). [The analyst says Ghana has built real-time interoperable payments, naming GhIPSS and Ghana.gov](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

**National switch.** [The Bank of Ghana's head of fintech described GhIPSS in October 2026 as the national interoperable payment infrastructure linking banks and mobile money operators](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>).
