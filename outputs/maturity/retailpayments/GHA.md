# Ghana

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** The Bank of Ghana [counted 85.8 million registered mobile money accounts in August 2026](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Summary-of-Economic-and-Financial-Data-September-2026.pdf>), 26.4 million of them active. That month they carried 1,046 million transactions worth GHC 518.8 billion. [Transactions passed one billion in a month for the first time](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>), up 46.5% on a year earlier, the governor said. [Mobile money transactions reached GHC 4.54 trillion in 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/09/dlocal-ghana-secures-enhanced-payments-licence/>). MTN Ghana [named Mobile Money among the sources of its 35.7% service revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/09/mtn-group-earnings-outpace-revenue-by-22-9-as-nigeria-and-ghana-drive-q3-growth/>).

**Between providers.** [The Bank of Ghana reports mobile money interoperability in August 2026 at 35.7 million transactions worth GHC 6.5 billion](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Summary-of-Economic-and-Financial-Data-September-2026.pdf>). [The analyst says Ghana has built real-time interoperable payments, naming GhIPSS and Ghana.gov](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>).

**National switch.** GhIPSS links banks and mobile money operators. In October 2026 the Bank of Ghana's head of fintech [called it the national interoperable payment infrastructure](<https://channelonenewsonline.com/2026/10/22/bank-of-ghana-pushes-for-seamless-african-cross-border-payments/>).
