# Chad

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Tchadinfos [counts four e-money providers: Airtel Money, Moov Money, Konoom and Nita](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). Moov Money, Airtel Money and Konoom [are licensed payment institutions](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). BEAC counted FCFA 526.7 billion of mobile transactions in Chad at the end of 2024. Findex 2024 put [adults holding a mobile money account at 13.3%](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>), Russell Southwood reports. Konoom, launched in 2025, [offers an e-wallet for transfers, merchant payments and bills](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/solutions/tchad-konoom-elargit-lacces-aux-paiements-numeriques-grace-a-ses-plateformes-web-et-mobiles>). Cashi and IFC [plan an interoperable digital payments platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/la-fintech-cashi-s-allie-a-la-sfi-pour-accelerer-les-paiements-numeriques-au-tchad>), first in Chad.

**Between providers.** Airtel Money and Moov Money [interoperate through GIMACPAY across the CEMAC zone](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). On 29 July 2026 [the CEMAC QR code standard was launched in six states, Chad among them](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). A client of any bank, wallet or microfinance account pays by scan.

**National switch.** AfricaNenda says [GIMACPAY connects banks and mobile money wallets for instant payments in each CEMAC state](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>), Chad included.
