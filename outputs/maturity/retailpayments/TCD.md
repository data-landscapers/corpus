# Chad

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Chad's electronic money market has four providers, Airtel Money, Moov Money, Konoom and Nita, per Tchadinfos](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [Moov Money Tchad, Airtel Money and Konoom are licensed payment institutions in Chad, and BEAC counted FCFA 526.7 billion of mobile transactions there at end-2024](<https://leconomie.info/tchad-moov-money-rejoint-le-club-restreint-des-etablissements-de-paiement-agrees/>). [Konoom, a N'Djamena fintech launched in 2025, offers an e-wallet for money transfers, merchant payments and bill payments by mobile app, web, USSD and WhatsApp, per We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/solutions/tchad-konoom-elargit-lacces-aux-paiements-numeriques-grace-a-ses-plateformes-web-et-mobiles>). [Cashi and IFC are to deploy an interoperable digital payments platform, reachable by mobile phone, payment terminals and SMS, first in Chad (We Are Tech Africa)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/la-fintech-cashi-s-allie-a-la-sfi-pour-accelerer-les-paiements-numeriques-au-tchad>).

**Between providers.** [Airtel Money and Moov Money offer interoperability via GIMACPAY across the CEMAC zone, per Tchadinfos](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [The CEMAC QR Code standard, launched 29 July 2026 across six states including Chad, lets a client with a bank account, mobile money wallet or microfinance account pay by scan whatever their institution](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including Chad](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
