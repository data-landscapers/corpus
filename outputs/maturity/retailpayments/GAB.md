# Gabon

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Gabon already has substantial mobile money use, according to Biometric Update](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/gabon-updates-digital-payment-laws-to-support-regional-interoperability>). [L'Union says Madigipaie is a national QR code digital payment platform for transactions between merchants and users, working with several banks, whose rollout to merchants the economy minister asked Libreville's mayor to support on 3 July 2026](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). [Moov Gabon issued 1,969,325 transactions worth 68.1bn FCFA and Airtel Gabon 1,950,401 worth 55.8bn FCFA in H1 2026, per GIMAC statistics](<https://leconomie.info/article/monetique-moov-gabon-devient-le-premier-emetteur-de-paiements-electroniques-de-la-cemac>). [Droit Medias Finance reports payment institutions in the CEMAC execute local transfers onto customers' e-money wallets for inbound remittances](<https://droitmediasfinance.com/index.php/actualites/droit-tech-fintech/1262-cemac-transferts-de-fonds-via-les-fintechs-une-nouvelle-instruction-beac-precise-les-diligences-relatives-au-traitement-et-suivi-des-prefinancements-des-operations-de-remittance-entrante-executees-par-le-canal-des-etablissements-de-paiement>). [The article refers to growing mobile money in Gabon, per Biometric Update](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/gabon-pens-deal-with-uncdf-to-accelerate-financial-inclusion>).

**Between providers.** [BEAC says the QR code launched 29 July 2026 lets any bank or microfinance mobile app read any merchant's code regardless of institution or CEMAC country](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>). [The CEMAC QR Code standard, launched 29 July 2026 across six states including Gabon, lets a client with a bank account, mobile money wallet or microfinance account pay by scan whatever their institution](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including Gabon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
