# Libya

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Central Bank of Libya says electronic payment transfers totalled about LYD 764.9 billion (USD 119.3 billion) from January to August 2026](<https://www.thepaypers.com/fintech/news/wahda-bank-selects-backbase-for-digital-banking-platform-in-libya>). [Digital-wallet transactions rose from 109 million dinars in October 2024 to 703 million in August 2026, the governor said](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>). [In January and February 2026 Libya had 187,335 electronic wallets and 6,096 acceptance points](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/510123>). [Over the same months the instant payment services LYPay and OnePay carried more than 44.6 billion dinars](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/510123>). [Circular 9/2026 lets licensed e-payment companies issue wallets, with a daily cap of 100,000 dinars between people](<https://bankawy.net/banks/mssrf-lebea-almrkze-edta-dtwabtd-jdedtt-llm/bankawy/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 digital-wallet transactions reached 703 million dinars, up from 109 million in October 2024](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>).
