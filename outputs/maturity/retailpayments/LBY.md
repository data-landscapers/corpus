# Libya

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Governor Issa said digital-wallet transactions rose from 109 million dinars in October 2024 to 703 million dinars in August 2026](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>). [Circular 9/2026 of the Central Bank of Libya lets licensed e-payment companies issue e-wallets, including to legally resident foreigners, with daily transfer caps of 100,000 dinars person to person, 500,000 person to company and 2 million company to company for citizens](<https://bankawy.net/banks/mssrf-lebea-almrkze-edta-dtwabtd-jdedtt-llm/bankawy/>). [Libya had 187,335 electronic wallets with 6,096 acceptance points in January-February 2026, and instant payment services LYPay and OnePay handled transactions worth over 44.6 billion dinars, per the Central Bank of Libya](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/510123>).
