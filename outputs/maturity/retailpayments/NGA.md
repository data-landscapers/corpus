# Nigeria

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Nigeria's earlier payment vision produced mobile money services and agency banking, alongside the NIP instant payment platform, as reported by The Nation](<https://thenationonlineng.net/payment-vision-2028-is-nigeria-ready-for-digital-economy/>). [Vanguard reports the federal government directed banks and fintechs to collect 7.5% VAT from 19 January 2026 on fees for services such as mobile money transfers and USSD transactions](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/01/fg-directs-banks-fintechs-to-collect-7-5-vat-on-electronic-banking-fees/>). [EFInA says Nigerians increasingly combine banks, mobile money, agents and informal mechanisms to manage their finances (A2F 2026 survey)](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>). [TechCabal reports that mobile money services operate in Nigeria through MTN MoMo PSB and Airtel's SmartCash, and that Nigeria's mobile money market processed 20.71 trillion naira in Q1 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [Premium Times reports NIBSS has rolled out the National Payment Stack, an ISO 20022 instant payments platform carrying account-to-account transfers, QR merchant payments and bulk payments](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/918939-nibss-transactilitys-partnership-delivers-national-payment-stack.html>).

**Between providers.** [TechCabal reports that SmartCash has integrated with multiple banks and financial institutions, allowing wallet transfers and interbank transactions across its network](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [NIBSS says its National Payment Stack is a single standardised rail across commercial and microfinance banks, fintechs, mobile money operators and payment service providers, with over 100 million successful transactions](<https://guardian.ng/technology/nibss-national-payment-stack-hits-100m-transactions/>).

**National switch.** [NIBSS says the National Payment Stack is one rail connecting banks and mobile money operators, and has passed 100 million successful transactions](<https://guardian.ng/technology/nibss-national-payment-stack-hits-100m-transactions/>).
