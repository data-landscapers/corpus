# Nigeria

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Mobile money and agency banking grew out of Nigeria's earlier payment vision, The Nation reports](<https://thenationonlineng.net/payment-vision-2028-is-nigeria-ready-for-digital-economy/>). [TechCabal names MTN MoMo PSB and Airtel's SmartCash as mobile money services](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [The market processed 20.71 trillion naira in the first quarter of 2025](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [Business Post reports the central bank upgraded the licences of OPay, Moniepoint, PalmPay, Kuda and Paga to national in January 2026](<https://businesspost.ng/banking/cbn-upgrades-operating-licences-of-opay-moniepoint-others-to-national/>). [From 19 January 2026 banks and fintechs must charge 7.5% VAT on mobile money transfer fees](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/01/fg-directs-banks-fintechs-to-collect-7-5-vat-on-electronic-banking-fees/>). [EFInA's 2026 survey finds people mixing banks, mobile money, agents and informal means](<https://a2f.ng/a2f-2026-making-financial-inclusion-work-harder-for-nigerians/>).

**Between providers.** [SmartCash has integrated with several banks, so wallet and interbank transfers run across its network](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/06/airtel-money-ipo-nigeria-smartcash/>). [NIBSS's National Payment Stack is one rail for banks, fintechs, mobile money operators and payment providers](<https://guardian.ng/technology/nibss-national-payment-stack-hits-100m-transactions/>). [It has carried over 100 million transactions](<https://guardian.ng/technology/nibss-national-payment-stack-hits-100m-transactions/>).

**National switch.** [Premium Times reported in October 2026 that NIBSS had rolled out the National Payment Stack, an ISO 20022 instant payments platform](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/918939-nibss-transactilitys-partnership-delivers-national-payment-stack.html>). [It connects banks and mobile money operators](<https://guardian.ng/technology/nibss-national-payment-stack-hits-100m-transactions/>). [It carries account transfers, QR payments and bulk payments](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/918939-nibss-transactilitys-partnership-delivers-national-payment-stack.html>).
