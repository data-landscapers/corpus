# Madagascar

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [L'Express de Madagascar reports Mvola, a mobile money service, has close to 4 million users, over 60,000 cashpoints and over 50,000 merchants accepting payments, per its CEO Alexandre Castel](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/10/inclusion-financiere-la-digitalisation.html>). [BGFIBank Madagascar's Gasy Money mobile-banking app is available on Google Play and the App Store, open to customers and non-customers on any network](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/21/gasy-money-bgfibank-madagascar-reinvente-le-mobile-banking/>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says users of mobile money report lost money and failed transactions, and that the CSBF supervises mobile money operators](<https://www.2424.mg/news/une-task-force-sera-mise-en-place-pour-ameliorer-le-traitement-des-reclamations-liees-au-mobile-money/>). [Newsmada reports that Jirama electricity bills are paid through the mobile money services Orange Money, Airtel Money and MVola](<https://newsmada.com/2026/08/26/payer-deux-fois-la-meme-facture-les-clients-de-la-jirama-peuvent-demander-un-remboursement/>).

**Between providers.** [Gasy Money users can transfer money to another Gasy Money account, a bank account or Airtel Money, per Newsmada](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/21/gasy-money-bgfibank-madagascar-reinvente-le-mobile-banking/>). [BGFIBank Madagasikara's Gasy Money app, presented on a Friday before 22 September 2026, currently allows national money transfers in partnership with the country's mobile phone operators, for bank clients, other banks' clients and people without an account](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/mobile-banking-gasy-money-entre-dans-la-course-de-la-finance-digitale/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/21/gasy-money-bgfibank-madagascar-reinvente-le-mobile-banking/>).
