# Madagascar

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In October 2026 Mvola, a mobile money service, had close to 4 million users, over 60,000 cashpoints and over 50,000 merchants, its CEO said](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/10/inclusion-financiere-la-digitalisation.html>). [BGFIBank Madagascar's Gasy Money app is open to customers and non-customers on any network](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/21/gasy-money-bgfibank-madagascar-reinvente-le-mobile-banking/>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says mobile money users report lost money and failed transactions](<https://www.2424.mg/news/une-task-force-sera-mise-en-place-pour-ameliorer-le-traitement-des-reclamations-liees-au-mobile-money/>). [The CSBF supervises mobile money operators](<https://www.2424.mg/news/une-task-force-sera-mise-en-place-pour-ameliorer-le-traitement-des-reclamations-liees-au-mobile-money/>). [Jirama electricity bills are paid through Orange Money, Airtel Money and MVola](<https://newsmada.com/2026/08/26/payer-deux-fois-la-meme-facture-les-clients-de-la-jirama-peuvent-demander-un-remboursement/>).

**Between providers.** [In September 2026 Gasy Money users could transfer money to another Gasy Money account, a bank account or Airtel Money](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/21/gasy-money-bgfibank-madagascar-reinvente-le-mobile-banking/>). [BGFIBank Madagasikara's Gasy Money app, presented in September 2026, allows national transfers in partnership with the mobile phone operators](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/mobile-banking-gasy-money-entre-dans-la-course-de-la-finance-digitale/>). [It serves bank clients, other banks' clients and people without an account](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/mobile-banking-gasy-money-entre-dans-la-course-de-la-finance-digitale/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 Airtel Money users could move funds from wallets to Baobab Bank accounts](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/17/airtel-money-madagascar-and-baobab-bank-partner-to-expand-mobile-banking-services/>).
