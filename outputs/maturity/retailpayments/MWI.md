# Malawi

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 GiveDirectly sent cash transfers of about US$550 per eligible adult in Malawi by mobile money](<https://www.ednews.africa/p/canvas-150m-boost-expands-givedirectlys>). [It had enrolled and paid 85,000 adults in TA Khongoni](<https://www.ednews.africa/p/canvas-150m-boost-expands-givedirectlys>). [The Malawi Police Service names TNM Mpamba and Airtel Money as channels for paying traffic fines](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2>). [In June 2026 Airtel Money Malawi and Admarc launched a digital payment platform in Blantyre](<https://itweb.africa/article/airtel-digitises-malawian-farmer-payments/JN1gPvOAE4DMjL6m>). [Customers pay instantly with Airtel Money accounts and farmers receive payments into mobile wallets](<https://itweb.africa/article/airtel-digitises-malawian-farmer-payments/JN1gPvOAE4DMjL6m>). [Cashing outlets for Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba have spread, a University of Malawi lecturer says](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>).

**Between providers.** [The Digital Retail Payments Platform integrates banks and non-bank players, mobile-money operators among them](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>). [FDH Bank, Finca Malawi and TNM Mpamba were early Malawian adopters](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>). [In July 2025 the Bankers Association of Malawi's chief executive said integration with the National Switch has enabled wallet-to-bank and bank-to-wallet transfers](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>). [The National Switch, rolled out in 2015, linked banks, mobile money platforms and ATMs into one network](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD>).

**National switch.** [In July 2025 the bankers' association said the National Switch connects banks and wallets for transfers both ways](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>). [Rolled out in 2015, the National Switch linked banks, mobile money platforms and ATMs into one network](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD>).
