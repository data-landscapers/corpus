# Malawi

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [GiveDirectly says it sends unconditional cash transfers of about US$550 per eligible adult to recipients in Malawi by mobile money, with 85,000 adults enrolled and paid in TA Khongoni](<https://www.ednews.africa/p/canvas-150m-boost-expands-givedirectlys>). [The article names TNM Mpamba and Airtel Money mobile money services as channels for paying traffic fines, per the Malawi Police Service](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2>). [Airtel Money Malawi and Admarc launched a digital payment platform in Blantyre letting customers pay instantly with Airtel Money accounts and farmers receive payments into mobile wallets, per ITWeb Africa](<https://itweb.africa/article/airtel-digitises-malawian-farmer-payments/JN1gPvOAE4DMjL6m>). [Cashing outlets for Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba have spread, per a University of Malawi lecturer](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>).

**Between providers.** [The platform integrates banks and non-bank players including mobile-money operators, with FDH Bank, Finca Malawi and TNM Mpamba as early Malawian adopters](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>). [The Bankers Association of Malawi's chief executive says integration with the National Switch has enabled wallet-to-bank and bank-to-wallet transfers](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>). [The National Switch rolled out in 2015 linked banks, mobile money platforms and ATMs into a unified network (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD>).

**National switch.** [The Bankers Association of Malawi's chief executive says the National Switch connects banks and wallets for transfers in both directions](<https://mwnation.com/banks-respond-to-digital-services-demand-bam/>). [The National Switch rolled out in 2015 linked banks, mobile money platforms and ATMs into a unified network (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-unveils-digital-payments-roadmap/KPNG8v8NQLZM4mwD>).
