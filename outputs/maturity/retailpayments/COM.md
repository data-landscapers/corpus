# Comoros

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The PFSS-RRC names Mvula and Huri Money as mobile-banking operators that make the transfer payments to households in Comoros](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-%C3%A9largit-la-protection-sociale-%C3%A0-55-000-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables>). [The central bank says Komor Switch connects banks, financial institutions and mobile money services in the Comoros](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret(3).pdf>). [AXIAN says it has an established presence in mobile financial services in the Comoros through MVola, separate from the newly licensed digital financial institution](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/24/axian-gains-central-bank-approval-to-launch-regulated-digital-finance-institution-in-comoros/>). [ITWeb reports AXIAN says it already has a presence in mobile financial services in the Comoros through MVola, separate from the newly licensed institution](<https://itweb.africa/article/axian-gets-digital-first-financial-license-in-the-comoros/xA9POvNElGOqo4J8>).

**Between providers.** [The central bank says Komor Switch allows instant real-time transfers between banks and mobile money](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret(3).pdf>). [Al-Watwan reports the president launched Komor Switch, a national platform connecting all banks and mobile money services and making interoperability between banks and mobile banking possible](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>).

**National switch.** [The central bank describes Komor Switch as the national payment infrastructure interconnecting all banks, financial institutions and mobile money services](<https://banque-comores.km/uploads/BCC-livret(3).pdf>). [Al-Watwan reports Komor Switch is a national platform connecting all banks and mobile money services, launched officially by the president](<https://alwatwan.net/economie/secteur-bancaire-i-les-comores-inaugurent-de-nouveaux-modes-et-syst%C3%A8mes-de-paiement.html>).
