# Seychelles

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Nebula FinTech's eboo e-money wallet went live on the iOS and Google Play stores on 30 and 31 January 2024](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20931/nebula-fintech-limited-launches-new-online-payment-platform>). It came in versions for individuals and businesses. The Central Bank of Seychelles governor spoke at its launch. [The governor said Airtel Seychelles had introduced e-money with a licensed mobile money transfer service in 2015](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20240130-launch-nebula-fintech-limited>). [In November 2025 Fusepay said it had received a payment service provider licence from the Central Bank of Seychelles](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>). It lets Fusepay run digital money accounts and instant payments. [Its first live transactions were still to come](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact found: [in November 2025 Fusepay was licensed to run instant payments](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact found: [in January 2024 the eboo wallet went live](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/20931/nebula-fintech-limited-launches-new-online-payment-platform>).
