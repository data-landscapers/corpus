# Seychelles

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In November 2025 Fusepay said it had received a payment service provider licence from the Central Bank of Seychelles](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>). [The licence lets it run digital money accounts and instant payments](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>). [Its first live transactions were still to come](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 Fusepay was licensed to run instant payments](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 Fusepay's digital money accounts had no live transactions](<https://www.fusepay.app/blog-posts/fusepay-receives-final-approval-to-operate-as-a-licensed-payment-service-provider-in-seychelles>).
