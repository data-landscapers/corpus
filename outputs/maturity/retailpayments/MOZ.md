# Mozambique

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [PawaPay says Mozambique's mobile money market exceeds 12 million active accounts across three operators: M-Pesa (Vodacom), e-Mola (Movitel) and mKesh (Tmcel)](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/10/pawapay-becomes-one-of-the-first-licensed-psps-in-mozambique/>). [Funds from the Adianta-La salary advance are paid into customers' M-Pesa accounts in Mozambique via the *150# menu, per TechReviewAfrica](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7081/m-pesa-maximo-microbanco-and-cedsif-open-formal-credit-access-to-380000-mozambican-civil-servants>). [M-Pesa's Paga Fácil merchant payment service is in use, and Absa and M-Pesa signed an MoU at FACIM 2026, reported 2 September 2026](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/02/negocios/empresas/facim-2026-absa-e-m-pesa-reforcam-parceria-para-facilitar-pagamentos-digitais-e-gestao-financeira-dos-negocios/>). [WHO Mozambique says it has used the Mobile Money (MOMO) digital payment system since 2024 to pay nearly 246,000 health workers and volunteers, over US$14.4 million so far](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/11/who-mozambique-channels-14-4m-through-mobile-money-for-health-campaigns/>).

**Between providers.** [Under the Absa-M-Pesa MoU, merchants transfer Paga Fácil receipts directly into Absa bank accounts, settled normally within minutes](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/02/negocios/empresas/facim-2026-absa-e-m-pesa-reforcam-parceria-para-facilitar-pagamentos-digitais-e-gestao-financeira-dos-negocios/>). [The Bank of Mozambique attributes growth partly to interoperability between electronic money institutions, banks, microbanks and other payment service providers through SIMOrede](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>).

**National switch.** [Metix, in production since March 2026 and run by SIMO, integrates banks, microbanks and electronic money institutions, with participation by digital wallet operators mandatory, and processes over 11,000 transfers a day](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>). [The Bank of Mozambique's Instant Payment System, operated by SIMO and running 24 hours a day, connects banks and mobile wallets nationally from 2026-03-02](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-banks-unveil-instant-payment-system/>).
