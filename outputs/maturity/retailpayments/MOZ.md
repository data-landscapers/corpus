# Mozambique

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** PawaPay says [Mozambique's mobile money market exceeds 12 million active accounts](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/10/pawapay-becomes-one-of-the-first-licensed-psps-in-mozambique/>), across M-Pesa, e-Mola and mKesh. In November 2025 Vodacom's chief executive [put M-Pesa at 8.5 million customers](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2025/11/21/trends/inovacao/ceo-talks-queremos-garantir-95-de-cobertura-de-rede-ate-2030-simon-karikari/>). Civil servants' [Adianta-La salary advances are paid into M-Pesa accounts](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7081/m-pesa-maximo-microbanco-and-cedsif-open-formal-credit-access-to-380000-mozambican-civil-servants>). M-Pesa's [Paga FÃ¡cil merchant payment service is in use](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/02/negocios/empresas/facim-2026-absa-e-m-pesa-reforcam-parceria-para-facilitar-pagamentos-digitais-e-gestao-financeira-dos-negocios/>), as reported in September 2026. WHO Mozambique has [paid nearly 246,000 health workers and volunteers by mobile money since 2024](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/11/who-mozambique-channels-14-4m-through-mobile-money-for-health-campaigns/>), over US$14.4 million so far.

**Between providers.** Under an [Absa and M-Pesa agreement of 2026](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/09/02/negocios/empresas/facim-2026-absa-e-m-pesa-reforcam-parceria-para-facilitar-pagamentos-digitais-e-gestao-financeira-dos-negocios/>), merchants move Paga FÃ¡cil receipts straight into Absa bank accounts, normally within minutes. The Bank of Mozambique [credits part of the growth to interoperability through SIMOrede](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>), among e-money institutions, banks and microbanks. In October 2026 its governor [spoke of progress through interoperability between banks and mobile money](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/governor-felisberto-navalha-delivers-opening-address-at-51st-banco-de-mocambique-advisory-board-meeting-in-tete/>).

**National switch.** The Bank of Mozambique's [instant payment system, run by SIMO around the clock](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-banks-unveil-instant-payment-system/>), has connected banks and mobile wallets since 2 March 2026. Called Metix, it [joins banks, microbanks and e-money institutions](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/metix-instant-payment-system-surpasses-11000-daily-transfers/>), and wallet operators must take part. The governor said in October 2026 that it [handled about 12,000 transactions a day](<https://www.bancomoc.mz/en/media/highlights/governor-felisberto-navalha-delivers-opening-address-at-51st-banco-de-mocambique-advisory-board-meeting-in-tete/>), up from about 3,000 at launch.
