# Cote d'Ivoire

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [APAnews reports the fintech PUSH in Cote d'Ivoire has over 700,000 individual accounts and over 20 million transactions worth more than CFAF 600 billion, with an interoperability and merchant-payment agreement signed with Orange Money](<https://fr.apanews.net/cote-divoire/rci-push-veut-digitaliser-3-000-mds-f-de-flux-financiers/>). [Africtelegraph reports that Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money and Wave have converted millions of Ivorians to mobile money transactions, which merchants, metered taxis and ride-hailing platforms have largely adopted](<https://africtelegraph.com/blog/paiement-mobile-a-abidjan-les-gnambros-freinent-la-modernisation/>). [SimoCash, launched in May 2026, carries payments and money transfers over mobile money networks such as Orange Money in Cote d'Ivoire](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>).

**Between providers.** [SimoCash lets funds move directly from one mobile money operator to another, Orange Money being named as one](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>). [Fraternite Matin reports PI-SPI, launched by the BCEAO on 30 September 2025, lets users transfer instantly between bank accounts, mobile money wallets and microfinance accounts, with 80 institutions connected](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>).
