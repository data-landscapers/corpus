# Cote d'Ivoire

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Orange Money, MTN Mobile Money and Wave [have brought millions of Ivorians to mobile money](<https://africtelegraph.com/blog/paiement-mobile-a-abidjan-les-gnambros-freinent-la-modernisation/>), Africtelegraph reports. Merchants, metered taxis and ride-hailing platforms have largely taken it up. The fintech PUSH [has over 700,000 accounts and has carried more than CFAF 600 billion](<https://fr.apanews.net/cote-divoire/rci-push-veut-digitaliser-3-000-mds-f-de-flux-financiers/>), APAnews says. SimoCash, launched in May 2026, [carries payments and transfers over mobile money networks](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>).

**Between providers.** SimoCash [moves funds directly from one mobile money operator to another](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>). PI-SPI, which the BCEAO launched on 30 September 2025, [carries instant transfers between bank accounts, wallets and microfinance accounts](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2641755/economie/uemoa-linteroperabilite-des-paiements-devient-obligatoire-des-le-30-juin-2026>). [From 2 November 2026 transfers between e-money providers, and between banks and wallets, must route through it](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/11/03/failed-transfers-hidden-fees-and-phantom-aliases-inside-the-messy-birth-of-west-africas-unified-payments-network/>). On 6 November Wave said [all its customers in Cote d'Ivoire can send and receive through PI-SPI](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>), free up to 8,000 FCFA a day.

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/tech-stars/avec-simocash-l-ivoirien-wilfried-kouame-interconnecte-les-reseaux-d-argent-mobile>).
