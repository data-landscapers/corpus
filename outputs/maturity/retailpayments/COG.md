# Congo

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [ACI reports Yano, launched 14 September, lets users send and receive mobile payments as a gateway to mobile money operators, which hold the funds](<https://www.aci.cg/fintech-yano-se-positionne-dans-la-structuration-des-transactions-financieres-mobiles/>). [Kerry Payments Brazzaville was authorised by ARPCE as a payment aggregator connecting businesses to mobile money operators, banks and microfinance institutions operating in Congo](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/16/pawapay-subsidiary-kerry-payments-secures-vas-payment-aggregator-licence-in-congo/>). [A school management platform shown to over 200 principals in Brazzaville integrates school fee payment through Airtel Money, per Tech Review Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5111/airtel-money-supports-digitalisation-of-education-sector-in-republic-of-congo>). [GIMAC said that in the first half of 2026 payment institutions and mobile money operators, including those of Congo, dominate GIMACPAY transaction volumes](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

**Between providers.** [Yano's product manager says the platform lets users pay and be paid by mobile payment regardless of telephone operator, via QR code or link](<https://www.aci.cg/fintech-yano-se-positionne-dans-la-structuration-des-transactions-financieres-mobiles/>). [The CEMAC QR Code standard, launched 29 July 2026 across six states including Congo, lets a client with a bank account, mobile money wallet or microfinance account pay by scan whatever their institution](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
