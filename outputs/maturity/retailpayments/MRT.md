# Mauritania

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The AfDB says QR-code payments at the point of sale are now common in Nouakchott](<https://www.afdb.org/en/success-stories/mauritania-finance-track-modern-age-92328>). [Electronic wallets, payment apps and digital banking carry payments and transfers in Mauritania, taxed at 0.1% above 5,000 MRU from January 2026 (Invest-Time)](<https://invest-time.com/2026/01/17/mauritanie-budget-2026-taxe-electro/>). [On 9 November 2026 the operator Mattel launched its licensed e-money subsidiary, Mattel Money, and its Mechili wallet](<https://lequotidien.mr/?p=42746>), Le Quotidien reports. The wallet is meant for payments and transfers; no usage figure was given.

**Between providers.** [In October 2024 GIMTEL launched transfers between bank apps and e-wallets](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/gimtel-annonce-le-debut-des-transactions-interbancaires-des-differentes-applications/>). BreadCash, Bamis Digital, BCI Pay, Bankili, Amanti, Sedad and Qlik had already joined.

**National switch.** [GIMTEL's launched interconnection links national bank apps and e-wallets in one system, with further providers in technical testing](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/gimtel-annonce-le-debut-des-transactions-interbancaires-des-differentes-applications/>).
