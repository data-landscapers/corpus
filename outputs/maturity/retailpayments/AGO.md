# Angola

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The BNA governor said the payment system carried 2.5 billion electronic payments in 2024](<https://scm.gov.ao/web/noticias/pagamentos-electronicos-registam-operacoes-de-74-bilhoes-de-kwanzas-em-2024?utm_source=chatgpt.com>). They were worth Kz 74 trillion, about 35% more than in 2023. [Digital wallets sent Kz 212.7bn of the Kz 557.4bn moved over Kwik in the first half of 2026](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>), Expansão reports from EMIS data. Wallets had been allowed to open e-money accounts about two years earlier. [On 9 July 2026 Minister José de Lima Massano said mobile and digital payments had grown about 56% in a year](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/governo-anuncia-nova-etapa-na-estrategia-nacional-de-inclusao-financeira/>). [EMIS, the interbank services company, runs the MULTICAIXA Express app and KWiK instant transfers](<https://fintechs.ao/emis-usa-inteligencia-artificial-para-reforcar-a-seguranca-dos-pagamentos-digitais-em-angola/>).

**Between providers.** [EMIS describes its instant payment system as account-to-account transfers between banks and non-bank providers](<https://fintechs.ao/africa-ja-tem-36-sistemas-de-pagamentos-instantaneos-o-proximo-desafio-e-faze-los-conversar/>). KWiK carried 4.48 million transfers worth Kz 123.85 billion in August 2026. [In the first half of 2026 wallets sent Kz 209.6bn to bank accounts over Kwik](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>). Banks sent Kz 2.8bn to non-bank providers.

**National switch.** [The Banco Nacional de Angola launched Kwik in June 2023](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>). It carries instant transfers between banks and digital wallets. [In February 2026 KWiK handled 3.14 million transfers worth Kz 74.6 billion](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/paypay-ultrapassa-1-6-milhoes-de-transferencias-mensais-no-kwik/>), EMIS reported. It then held 12.3 million registered accounts.
