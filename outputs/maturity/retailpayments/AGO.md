# Angola

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [EMIS, Angola's interbank services company, manages the MULTICAIXA Express app and KWiK instant transfers, per an EMIS executive director at the 2026-10-12 CyberSecur Summit](<https://fintechs.ao/emis-usa-inteligencia-artificial-para-reforcar-a-seguranca-dos-pagamentos-digitais-em-angola/>). [Minister Jose de Lima Massano said on 9 July 2026 that mobile and digital payments in Angola grew about 56% year on year by the end of the first half of 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/governo-anuncia-nova-etapa-na-estrategia-nacional-de-inclusao-financeira/>). [Expansão reports EMIS data that digital wallets originated Kz 212.7bn of the Kz 557.4bn moved over Kwik in the first half of 2026, and that wallets were authorised to open e-money accounts about two years earlier](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>).

**Between providers.** [EMIS describes the STI as account-to-account interoperability between bank and non-bank payment service providers, and KWiK processed 4.48 million transfers worth Kz 123.85 billion in August 2026](<https://fintechs.ao/africa-ja-tem-36-sistemas-de-pagamentos-instantaneos-o-proximo-desafio-e-faze-los-conversar/>). [Expansão reports EMIS data that wallets sent Kz 209.6bn to bank accounts and banks sent Kz 2.8bn to non-bank payment providers over Kwik in the first half of 2026](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>).

**National switch.** [Expansão reports Kwik, launched by the Banco Nacional de Angola in June 2023, carries instant transfers between banks and digital wallets, with Kz 557.4bn moved in the first half of 2026](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/movimentos-entre-carteiras-digitais-valem-apenas-06-no-kwik-74789.html>). [KWiK, reported by EMIS, handled 3.14 million transfers worth 74.6 billion kwanzas in February 2026 across wallets and banks, with 12.3 million registered accounts](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/paypay-ultrapassa-1-6-milhoes-de-transferencias-mensais-no-kwik/>).
