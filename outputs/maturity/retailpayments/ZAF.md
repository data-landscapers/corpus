# South Africa

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Reserve Bank governor said in October 2026 that PayShap, the real-time low-value payment rail launched in 2023, had passed 1 billion transactions worth over R1 trillion](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>). [ASAPP says it has proposed to the SARB how existing acquiring infrastructure could extend PayShap merchant acceptance and support wider adoption of instant payments](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). [ITWeb reports that Samsung Wallet users in South Africa can now pay by scanning QR codes through EFT Corp's Scan to Pay, which is accepted at more than 600 000 locations](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/samsung-sa-expands-digital-wallet-ecosystem/xA9POvNE6n8qo4J8>). [Capitec Pay is a bank-to-bank online payment service that lets clients pay on websites and apps using their phone number, and PayShap and the Rapid Payments Programme rail are named as competing payment rails (ITWeb, 6 August 2026)](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/compcom-approves-capitecs-walletdoc-acquisition/Olx4z7kaoeyq56km>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>).
