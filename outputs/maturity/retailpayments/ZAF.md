# South Africa

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In October 2026 the Reserve Bank governor said PayShap, the real-time low-value payment rail launched in 2023, had passed 1 billion transactions](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>). [They were worth over R1 trillion](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>). [ASAPP says it has proposed to the SARB how existing acquiring infrastructure could extend PayShap merchant acceptance](<https://marketingspread.co.za/payment-challengers-move-from-consultation-to-action-with-proposals-for-sas-payments-future-2/>). [Samsung Wallet users can now pay by scanning QR codes through EFT Corp's Scan to Pay](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/samsung-sa-expands-digital-wallet-ecosystem/xA9POvNE6n8qo4J8>). [Scan to Pay is accepted at more than 600 000 locations](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/samsung-sa-expands-digital-wallet-ecosystem/xA9POvNE6n8qo4J8>). [Capitec Pay is a bank-to-bank online payment service that lets clients pay on websites and apps using their phone number](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/compcom-approves-capitecs-walletdoc-acquisition/Olx4z7kaoeyq56km>). [PayShap and the Rapid Payments Programme rail compete with it](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/compcom-approves-capitecs-walletdoc-acquisition/Olx4z7kaoeyq56km>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 PayShap, the instant payments system, had passed six million registered users](<https://techcentral.co.za/three-years-in-payshap-pivots-to-merchants/281687/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 PayShap was a shared instant-payment service used by participating banks](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/south-africa-fintech-payment-network/>).
