# DR Congo

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In July 2026 Flash's FlashApp offered over 27 financial services in the DRC, mobile money among them, to more than three million customers](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/14/mastercard-and-flash-partner-to-expand-digital-financial-inclusion-in-drc/>). [Visa's Godfrey Sullivan says M-Pesa wallet top-ups in DR Congo are settled in stablecoins in the background via Visa Pay with Onafriq](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/business-tech/visa-tests-stablecoins-in-drc-for-cross-border-payments-5515040>). [In May 2026 almost 30 million citizens had a mobile money account, though cash still dominates](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mainmoney-palm-biometrics-platform-to-support-drcs-financial-inclusion-drive>). [MainMoney's director general put mobile money account holders at 29 million or more](<https://actualite.cd/2026/04/30/rdc-lancement-de-mainmoney-une-technologie-monetaire-pour-eviter-la-population-du-poids>). [MainMoney, a palm-vein payment terminal, was launched in Kinshasa on 29 April 2026](<https://actualite.cd/2026/04/30/rdc-lancement-de-mainmoney-une-technologie-monetaire-pour-eviter-la-population-du-poids>).

**Between providers.** [In April 2026 Vodacom Congo's CEO said about $88 million had moved into the formal banking sector through wallet-to-bank transfers over the past year](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4723/vodacom-congo-calls-for-integrated-financial-ecosystem-to-enhance-inclusion-in-drc>). [Onafriq supported the Visa Pay launch in the DRC in late 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/financial-inclusion/how-onafriq-is-growing-digital-finance-amid-uncertainty-in-drc>). [Onafriq says it brings interoperability between mobile money and card networks at scale](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/financial-inclusion/how-onafriq-is-growing-digital-finance-amid-uncertainty-in-drc>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 about $88 million moved from wallets to banks over the past year](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4723/vodacom-congo-calls-for-integrated-financial-ecosystem-to-enhance-inclusion-in-drc>).
