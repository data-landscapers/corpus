# South Sudan

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [MTN MoMo, a mobile money service, trained 1,225 South Sudanese entrepreneurs to accept digital payments, sell airtime and provide cash-in services, per Tech Africa News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/mtn-momo-equips-1225-entrepreneurs-digital-finance-skills/>). [MTN MoMo mobile money is in service in South Sudan, letting users send and receive money, pay bills and shop by phone, as MTN launched its 'Mata fekir' campaign with the Bank of South Sudan governor present, per One Citizen Daily](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/08/29/boss-vows-to-safeguard-robust-payment-system/>). [One Citizen Daily calls MTN and Digitel two of South Sudan's leading mobile money operators, and MTN MoMo urges traders and customers to use mobile money to buy goods and services](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/08/15/mobile-money-providers-pledge-nationwide-expansion-to-support-digital-payment/>).

**Between providers.** No source held since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/mtn-momo-equips-1225-entrepreneurs-digital-finance-skills/>).
