# South Sudan

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The article describes rules for operating electronic money systems in South Sudan, with transaction limits by customer tier and agent reporting, implying mobile money services are in operation](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [MTN MoMo, a mobile money service, trained 1,225 South Sudanese entrepreneurs to accept digital payments, sell airtime and provide cash-in services, per Tech Africa News](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/mtn-momo-equips-1225-entrepreneurs-digital-finance-skills/>). [MTN MoMo mobile money is in service in South Sudan, letting users send and receive money, pay bills and shop by phone, as MTN launched its 'Mata fekir' campaign with the Bank of South Sudan governor present, per One Citizen Daily](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/08/29/boss-vows-to-safeguard-robust-payment-system/>).
