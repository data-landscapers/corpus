# South Sudan

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 South Sudan had rules for operating electronic money systems, with transaction limits by customer tier and agent reporting](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>). [In August 2026 MTN MoMo trained 1,225 South Sudanese entrepreneurs to accept digital payments, sell airtime and provide cash-in services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/24/mtn-momo-equips-1225-entrepreneurs-digital-finance-skills/>). [MTN MoMo lets users send and receive money, pay bills and shop by phone](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/08/29/boss-vows-to-safeguard-robust-payment-system/>). [In August 2025 MTN launched its 'Mata fekir' campaign with the Bank of South Sudan governor present](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/08/29/boss-vows-to-safeguard-robust-payment-system/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the Bank of South Sudan said mobile money is a legal form of payment](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/07/19/central-bank-affirms-mobile-money-as-legal-tender/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 electronic money rules set transaction limits by customer tier](<https://radioyei.org/news/technology/boss-sets-strict-usd-caps-on-mobile-money/>).
