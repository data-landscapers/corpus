# Sudan

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Bank apps carry most digital payments in Sudan. [Millions use Bank of Khartoum's Bankak app for transfers and everyday payments](<https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/sudan/2026/10/09/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86>), Al Arabiya reported in October 2026, including transport, fuel, food and government payments. The app suffered outages that month. [A banking expert told Dabanga that Bankak remains the leading transfer platform](<https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bankak-outages-disrupt-sudans-digital-payments-and-markets>), with Fawry and other bank services also running. [In Darfur, customers use it to pay traders](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>), The Sudan Times reports. [Proparco says Cashi's MyCashi wallet has 2.2 million registered users](<https://www.proparco.fr/en/news/proparco-partners-cashi-expand-access-essential-financial-services-sudan>) (November 2026), who use local merchants to deposit, withdraw, pay bills and send money.

**Between providers.** Wallets in Sudan now reach bank accounts. [Sudani Fintech launched Sah on 14 April 2026](<https://www.sudatel.sd/en/news-and-announcement/sudani-fintech-launches-sah-platform/>), a licensed app and USSD service that gives each user a basic bank account number. Users can send and receive money to and from all banks in Sudan. [Zain's Bede wallet added the same bank account number service in October 2026](<https://sudanhorizon.com/bede-sudan-launches-bban-service-to-strengthen-banking-integration-and-expand-digital-transaction/>), Sudan Horizon reports, for transfers across all Sudanese banks.

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>).
