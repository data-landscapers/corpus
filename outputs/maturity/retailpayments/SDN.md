# Sudan

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Sudan Times describes Bankak as one of Sudan's most widely used banking applications, used by customers to pay traders in Darfur](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>). [Banking expert Abdullatif Ali Ibrahim says Bankak, the Bank of Khartoum app, remains Sudan's leading digital transfer platform, used for daily and commercial transactions, with Fawry and other bank services also operating (Dabanga)](<https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/bankak-outages-disrupt-sudans-digital-payments-and-markets>). [Al Arabiya reports that millions of Sudanese use Bank of Khartoum's Bankak app for everyday transfers and payments (transport, fuel, treatment, food, government payments), and that it suffered disruptions in early October 2026](<https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/sudan/2026/10/09/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86>).

**Between providers.** [Sudan Horizon says the BBAN service lets Bede wallet users send and receive funds across all Sudanese banks](<https://sudanhorizon.com/bede-sudan-launches-bban-service-to-strengthen-banking-integration-and-expand-digital-transaction/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>).

***Also on file***. [Baladna Bibasata](<https://alwijha24.com/83548>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service.
