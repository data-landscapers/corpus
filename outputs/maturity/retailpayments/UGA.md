# Uganda

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [MTN Uganda launched Mobile Money toll payments on the Kampala-Entebbe Expressway, where motorists approve a PIN prompt on their phone, Tech Africa News reported](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/mtn-uganda-mobile-money-toll-payments-kampala-entebbe-expressway/>). [Ericsson and MTN Group Fintech say they completed the migration of the MTN MoMo mobile money platform to a cloud-native architecture in Uganda, among four markets](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/21/ericsson-mtn-complete-momo-cloud-migration-across-four-african-markets/>). [Drivers at Entebbe International Airport car parks pay through the *227# USSD mobile money gateway, which currently carries a 2% integration fee, according to the Uganda Civil Aviation Authority](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/smarter-parking-at-entebbe-ucaa-rolls-out-ai-camera-system-digital-payments-5625876>). [Red Pepper reports Bank of Uganda statistics showing 6.5 billion to nine billion mobile money transactions registered annually in Uganda](<https://redpepper.co.ug/mobile-money-fraud-network-woes-threaten-trust-fintech-bosses-sound-alarm/151792/>).
