# Uganda

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 MTN Uganda launched Mobile Money toll payments on the Kampala-Entebbe Expressway](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/mtn-uganda-mobile-money-toll-payments-kampala-entebbe-expressway/>). [Motorists approve a PIN prompt on their phone](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/mtn-uganda-mobile-money-toll-payments-kampala-entebbe-expressway/>). [In August 2026 Ericsson and MTN Group Fintech said they had moved the MTN MoMo platform in Uganda to a cloud-native architecture](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/21/ericsson-mtn-complete-momo-cloud-migration-across-four-african-markets/>). [Drivers at Entebbe International Airport car parks pay through the *227# USSD mobile money gateway](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/smarter-parking-at-entebbe-ucaa-rolls-out-ai-camera-system-digital-payments-5625876>). [It carries a 2% integration fee, the Uganda Civil Aviation Authority says](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/smarter-parking-at-entebbe-ucaa-rolls-out-ai-camera-system-digital-payments-5625876>). [Bank of Uganda statistics show 6.5 billion to nine billion mobile money transactions a year](<https://redpepper.co.ug/mobile-money-fraud-network-woes-threaten-trust-fintech-bosses-sound-alarm/151792/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest fact: [in October 2025 pawaPay processed mobile money payments through direct links with MTN MoMo and Airtel](<https://pawapay.io/blog/pawapay-q3-round-up>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in 2025 MTN MoMo processed over 5 billion transactions](<https://www.mtn.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/2026/08/MTN-Uganda-Sustainability-Report-2025-Digital-1.pdf>).
