# Gambia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Central Bank of The Gambia reports mobile money and fintech cash-in of D28.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/7a57e5cf-9cba-11f1-8725-02e599c15748>). Cash-out was D32.1 billion. [In July 2026 the Nafa cash transfer paid its beneficiaries through QMoney's platform](<https://standard.gm/thousands-of-beneficiaries-receive-nafa-cash-transfer/>), with the programme covering the fees. [Mobile money kept working through the nine-digit numbering migration in September 2026, PURA said](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-opens-new-chapter-in-digital-growth-with-nine-digit-numbering-system>). [Yonna Wallet is live for payments, per The Standard](<https://standard.gm/govt-payments-now-digitalised-live-on-yonna-wallet-app>). [Since June 2025 Wave users can pay Gambia Transport Service Company bus fares in the app](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/wave-gambia-gtsc-launch-digital-payment-partnership>).

**Between providers.** [Gamswitch called BANTABA 2.0 the country's first real-time, fully interoperable payment system in February 2026](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-national-payment-systems-advisory-committee-npsac-to-strengthen-payment-system-governance>). [Since its launch in December 2025 it has moved money instantly between bank accounts and mobile wallets, whatever the provider](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-its-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>).

**National switch.** [The Point calls BANTABA 2.0 a national backbone run by Gamswitch](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>). [One switch connects banks, mobile money operators, microfinance institutions, fintechs and payment providers](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-its-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>). It went live after a pilot, and more institutions are to join.
