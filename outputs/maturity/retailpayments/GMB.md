# Gambia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [PURA's Bah said mobile money services continued to function smoothly throughout the overnight nine-digit numbering migration](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-opens-new-chapter-in-digital-growth-with-nine-digit-numbering-system>). [The Central Bank of The Gambia reports fintech and mobile money cash-in of D28.2 billion and cash-out of D32.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.cbg.gm/downloads-file/7a57e5cf-9cba-11f1-8725-02e599c15748>). [The Yonna Wallet app, run by Yonna Group with over 200 branches and outlets, is live for payments in The Gambia, per The Standard](<https://standard.gm/govt-payments-now-digitalised-live-on-yonna-wallet-app>). [The Point reports Wave Gambia's mobile money platform now lets passengers pay Gambia Transport Service Company bus fares through the Wave app, launched 19 June 2025](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/national-news/wave-gambia-gtsc-launch-digital-payment-partnership>).

**Between providers.** [BANTABA 2.0, the country's first real-time, fully interoperable payment system, has been launched, providing the backbone for instant digital payments](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-national-payment-systems-advisory-committee-npsac-to-strengthen-payment-system-governance>). [BANTABA 2.0 allows instant transfers between bank accounts and mobile wallets, and funds to move between accounts regardless of provider](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-its-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>).

**National switch.** [The Point says BANTABA 2.0 is a national backbone connecting banks, mobile money operators, MFIs, fintechs and PSPs through a single interoperable switch with real-time clearing and settlement, operated by Gamswitch after a pilot](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>). [BANTABA 2.0 connects banks, mobile money operators, microfinance institutions, fintechs and PSPs through a single interoperable switch, live after a pilot, with further institutions to join](<https://www.gamswitch.com/press-release/the-gambia-launches-bantaba-2-0-its-first-real-time-interoperable-payment-system>).
