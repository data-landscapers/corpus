# Tunisia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Central Bank of Tunisia data show 719,000 e-wallets at the end of September 2026](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/paiement-mobile-en-tunisie-71-millions-de-transactions-et-une-croissance-de-20-a-fin-septembre-2026/>). They carried 7.1 million transactions, 64.2% of them payments, Tunisie Numérique reports. [Five payment institutions were active at the end of 2025, with 113,739 payment accounts](<https://africanmanager.com/paiement-mobile-ouverts-dormants/>). [In February 2026 Ooredoo Fintech Tunisie received central bank approval to launch its walletii wallet](<https://www.thd.tn/ooredoo-fintech-tunisie-obtient-lagrement-de-la-bct-et-lance-walletii-by-ooredoo/>). [The BCT says Société Monétique Tunisie runs the mobile payment system](<https://www.bct.gov.tn/bct/siteprod/documents/Note_2026_132_fr.pdf>), and banks, the Post and payment institutions issue wallets. [The Fintech Times says Flouci offers an app combining electronic payments and financial services](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service.
