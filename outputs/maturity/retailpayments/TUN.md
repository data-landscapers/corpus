# Tunisia

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Fintech Times says Flouci offers an app combining electronic payments and financial services, and Konnect and Paymee accept La Poste's e-Dinar products](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>). [The BCT note says Societe Monetique Tunisie manages the mobile payment system in Tunisia, and banks, the Post and payment institutions issue mobile wallets](<https://www.bct.gov.tn/bct/siteprod/documents/Note_2026_132_fr.pdf>). [African Manager says Tunisian payment institutions operate payment accounts at three levels, with balance caps of TND 500, 1,000 and 5,000 under Circular 2018-16, to be raised under Circular 2026-10](<https://en.africanmanager.com/bct-raises-cash-withdrawal-limits-in-bid-to-curb-cash-use/>).

**Between providers.** No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thefintechtimes.com/tunisia-a-fintech-ecosystem-caught-between-cash-and-code/>).

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://en.africanmanager.com/tunisian-banks-urged-to-join-pan-african-papss-system/>).
