# Senegal

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Wave dominates person-to-person mobile money with free deposits and withdrawals and transfers charged about 1%, and Orange Money is also in service, according to economist Seydou Bocoum](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>). [Senegal's government pays family security grants of CFA140,000 per household for 2026 through mobile money operators, so mobile money services are in operation (APS, 2026-10-01)](<https://aps.sn/bourses-de-securite-familiale-le-paiement-des-allocations-officiellement-relance-apres-deux-ans/>). [The op-ed's author says some shops in Dakar accept electronic payments only above minimum amounts, and refuse digital payment below them, so electronic payments are in everyday use there](<https://www.seneplus.com/article/quand-linclusion-financiere-devient-un-facteur-dexclusion-sociale>). [The article describes Orange Money and Wave as the established mobile money services in Senegal's digital payment market](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>).

**Between providers.** [PI-SPI, the BCEAO's instant payment platform, allows instant transfers and payments between banks, e-money institutions and payment institutions, and Wave was authorised on it for Senegal on 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). [Financial Afrik says PI-SPI gives instant interoperable transfers 24 hours a day between bank accounts and mobile wallets, with 19 Senegal institutions authorised](<https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/05/instant-payments-pi-spi-in-the-waemu-updated-list-of-establishments-authorized-by-the-bceao/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
