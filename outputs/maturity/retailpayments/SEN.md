# Senegal

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** Orange Money and Wave [are the established mobile money services](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>). Wave [leads person-to-person transfers, charging about 1%](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>), an economist said in 2026. The government [pays 2026 family security grants of CFA140,000 a household through mobile money operators](<https://aps.sn/bourses-de-securite-familiale-le-paiement-des-allocations-officiellement-relance-apres-deux-ans/>). Some Dakar shops [refuse digital payment below a minimum amount](<https://www.seneplus.com/article/quand-linclusion-financiere-devient-un-facteur-dexclusion-sociale>), an op-ed says.

**Between providers.** PI-SPI, the BCEAO's instant payment platform, [carries transfers and payments between banks, e-money and payment institutions](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). In February 2026 [19 Senegal institutions were authorised on it](<https://www.financialafrik.com/en/2026/03/05/instant-payments-pi-spi-in-the-waemu-updated-list-of-establishments-authorized-by-the-bceao/>). [From 2 November 2026 bank-wallet transfers run through PI-SPI](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/bank-to-wallet-et-wallet-to-bank-la-bceao-chamboule-les-regles-du-jeu/>), replacing the banks' own services. On 6 November Wave said [all its customers in Senegal can send and receive through it](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>), free up to 8,000 FCFA a day.

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
