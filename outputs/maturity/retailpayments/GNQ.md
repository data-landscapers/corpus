# Equatorial Guinea

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The Fintech Times says mobile money, for example Orange Money operating across Central Africa, is the primary entry point to digital finance in Equatorial Guinea](<https://thefintechtimes.com/equatorial-guineas-fintech-ecosystem-in-2026/>). [GIMACPAY, the CEMAC card-mobile-transfer ecosystem in service since 2021, processed over sixteen million mobile transactions in 2025](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [The World Bank diagnostic says digital financial services are nascent, with no licensed e-money operator for mobile money](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/equatorialguinea/publication/equatorial-guinea-digital-economy-country-diagnostic-bridging-the-gaps-to-develop-a-safe-and-inclusive-digital-transform>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries P2P and merchant payments to and from 11 mobile money operators across the six CEMAC states including Equatorial Guinea](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Between providers.** [The CEMAC QR Code standard, launched 29 July 2026 across six states including Equatorial Guinea, lets a client with a bank account, mobile money wallet or microfinance account pay by scan whatever their institution](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY gives all-to-all interoperability between banks, microfinance institutions and mobile money operators in the six CEMAC states including Equatorial Guinea](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**National switch.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY also provides domestic instant payment functionality connecting banks and mobile money wallets in each CEMAC state, including Equatorial Guinea](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
