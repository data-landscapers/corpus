# Sao Tome and Principe

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [GTI Holdings launched the Sao digital financial platform in Sao Tome and Principe, with a mobile wallet, a prepaid card, POS payments and agents, and its commercial director said the platform already supports agents](<https://www.telanon.info/economia/2025/01/05/46928/sao-redefinindo-a-inclusao-financeira-e-o-empoderamento-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [UNDP reports the youth ministry signed a contract on 3 December 2020 with Golfintech for Dobra Digital, a phone money-transfer service, with its initial service expected in February 2021 and the projects still in development (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.undp.org/pt/sao-tome-principe/news/moeda-digital-sera-uma-realidade-em-sao-tome-e-principe>).
