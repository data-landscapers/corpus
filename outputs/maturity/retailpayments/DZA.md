# Algeria

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Algerie Poste's Edahabia cards rose from 6 million in 2020 to 14.1 million in November 2024, with over 73 million card transactions that year, per the post ministry](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/achievements/>). [Its BaridiMob app lets users move money between accounts and top up airtime](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/coordination-qualitative-avec-differents-secteurs/autonomisation-des-personnes-a-besoins-specifiques/>). [Interbank mobile payment, DZ Mob Pay, launched in January 2025](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/02/18/algerie-le-montant-des-paiements-electroniques-sest-eleve-a-939-milliards-de-dinars-en-2025-46/>). [By the end of August 2026 it had 131,705 personal and 18,730 merchant accounts, per GIE Monetique data](<https://ecotimesdz.com/numerique-algerie-efficacite-services-care/>). [In September 2026 the CNESE president said payment by mobile phone had widened](<https://elmawkie.dz/fr/finances-les-avancees-de-lalgerie-en-matiere-de-paiement-electronique-mises-en-avant/>).

**Between providers.** [A national mobile switch has been in service since June 2024](<https://alger16.dz/?p=65795>). [Interbank QR code and person-to-person payments under DZ Mob Pay run on it, with seven pilot banks](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/02/18/algerie-le-montant-des-paiements-electroniques-sest-eleve-a-939-milliards-de-dinars-en-2025-46/>). [The post ministry says Algerie Poste's e-payment methods work with the bank network](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/achievements/>).

**National switch.** [The post ministry says the mobile switch lets clients of the banks and of Algerie Poste pay and transfer money instantly by QR code](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/achievements/>). [GIE Monetique's director says the banks' mobile solutions are integrated into it](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/economie/assia-benchabla-queiroz-directrice-du-gie-de-nombreux-obstacles-entravent-le-paiement-electronique-en-algerie-236510>).
