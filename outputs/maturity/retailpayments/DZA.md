# Algeria

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The CNESE president said Algeria has widened the use of electronic payment and payment by mobile phone in recent years](<https://elmawkie.dz/fr/finances-les-avancees-de-lalgerie-en-matiere-de-paiement-electronique-mises-en-avant/>). [Alger 16 reports DZ Mob Pay mobile payments launched officially in early 2025, with 126,114 individual accounts and 18,294 merchant accounts by mid-2026, in rollout with seven pilot banks](<https://alger16.dz/?p=65795>). [Algérie Éco, citing GIE Monétique, says interbank mobile payment DZ Mob Pay launched officially in January 2025 and had 95,014 personal accounts and 14,283 merchant accounts at end December 2025](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/02/18/algerie-le-montant-des-paiements-electroniques-sest-eleve-a-939-milliards-de-dinars-en-2025-46/>). [We Are Tech says mobile money transfers rose sharply in 2025, supported by applications such as BaridMob and Wimpay](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-s-electronic-payment-market-surpasses-22-million-cards>).

**Between providers.** [Alger 16 reports interbank QR code and P2P payments under DZ Mob Pay run on the interoperability of the national mobile switch put in service in June 2024](<https://alger16.dz/?p=65795>). [Algérie Éco, citing GIE Monétique, says DZ Mob Pay interbank payments rest on the national mobile switch put in service in June 2024, with seven pilot banks](<https://algerie-eco.com/2026/02/18/algerie-le-montant-des-paiements-electroniques-sest-eleve-a-939-milliards-de-dinars-en-2025-46/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://alger16.dz/?p=65795>).
