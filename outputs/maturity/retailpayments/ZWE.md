# Zimbabwe

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 the World Bank said mobile money is used by 63 percent of Zimbabwe's population](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226030010924/pdf/P512930-4ebf2401-e24e-459b-9b56-b00c30f983fc.pdf>). [Mobile and internet payments exceed 80 percent of retail transaction volumes](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226030010924/pdf/P512930-4ebf2401-e24e-459b-9b56-b00c30f983fc.pdf>). [In August 2026 the Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe asked banks, mobile money operators and other digital payment providers to eliminate ghost accounts](<https://itweb.africa/article/zimbabwe-flags-cyber-risks-in-digital-payments/6GxRKMYQ1mVMb3Wj>). [A Topline Research Solutions survey found mobile money leading digital platforms at 52 percent adoption](<https://bullszimbabwe.com/digital-payments-surge-but-adoption-lags-survey/>). [EcoCash, O-Mari, Innbucks and NetOne One Wallet operate as mobile money platforms](<https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle>).

**Between providers.** [In July 2026 O-Mari wallet holders could withdraw cash at CABS ATMs and EcoCash customers at TN CyberTech Bank ATMs](<https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle>). [ZIPIT already allows some cross-platform transfers](<https://www.equityaxis.net/post/19212/2026/7/the-o-mari-effect-zimbabwe-s-mobile-money-war-goes-physical-as-netone-remains-idle>). [ZimSwitch's chief executive described it as the national payment switch, with mobile wallet integration on its e-commerce platform](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/electronic-transactions-top-zig1-trillion-as-zimbabwe-deepens-digital-finance/>). [Interoperable QR payments are being rolled out](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/electronic-transactions-top-zig1-trillion-as-zimbabwe-deepens-digital-finance/>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 ZimSwitch's chief executive called it the national payment switch, integrating mobile wallets on its e-commerce platform](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/electronic-transactions-top-zig1-trillion-as-zimbabwe-deepens-digital-finance/>).
