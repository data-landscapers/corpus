# Togo

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Le Nouveau Reporter reports that Moov Money and Mixx by Yas mobile money are in service in Togo, charging 280 and 300 FCFA to withdraw 10,000 FCFA](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/mobile-money-comment-les-frais-de-retrait-freinent-encore-linclusion-financiere-des-populations-a-faible-revenu/>). [Togo First reports that Togo had nearly 4.96 million mobile money subscribers at end September 2025, and that Mixx (YAS Togo) and Flooz (Moov Africa Togo) operate the market per ARCEP data, with Moov launching insurance payments, card-less withdrawal transfers and gift cards on 7 July 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/telecoms/0807-19499-mobile-money-moov-africa-togo-lance-de-nouveaux-services-pour-renforcer-sa-position>). [Mobile Money is used for transfers, bill payment and services, and CGAR pensions were paid by Mobile Money in July 2026](<https://actu-togo.tg/2026/08/04/togo-paiements-des-allocations-de-retraite-les-beneficiaires-invites-a-utiliser-le-numero-vert-8220/>).

**Between providers.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI interoperable instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, connects banks, e-money institutions and payment institutions, with 80 connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [Mixx users can send and receive money between mobile wallets, banks and microfinance institutions in the eight UEMOA countries through the BCEAO's PI-SPI platform, with an interoperable merchant QR code](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).
