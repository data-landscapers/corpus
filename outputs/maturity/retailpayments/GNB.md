# Guinea-Bissau

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [The BCEAO list of 30 September 2026 names Orange Money Guinea-Bissau among 7 institutions in Guinea-Bissau authorised to open PI-SPI services to the public](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>). [Guinea-Bissau had 5 institutions authorised on BCEAO's PI-SPI instant-payment platform at 31 July 2026, which lets users pay instantly between different financial institutions across the UEMOA](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>). [The Fintech Times says an estimated 5-15 fintech firms operate in Guinea-Bissau in 2026, primarily in payments and mobile money](<https://thefintechtimes.com/guinea-bissaus-fintech-landscape-in-2026/>). [BCEAO reports UEMOA electronic-money accounts reached 248 million in 2024, with 76.9 million active, in a region that includes Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>).

**Between providers.** [BCEAO says interoperable e-money transactions in UEMOA, which includes Guinea-Bissau, go through PI-SPI, with 175 connected participants including banks, e-money issuers and payment institutions](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [The BCEAO list shows banks and Orange Money Guinea-Bissau, a mobile money provider, authorised on PI-SPI in Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>).
