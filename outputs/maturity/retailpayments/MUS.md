# Mauritius

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [MCB reported that QR payments through its Juice app grew 67% in 2025](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553532>), L'Express says. The payments include those made with MAUCAS QR, the central bank's QR code. [Citing a 2021 COMESA report, Bizweek put mobile account holding at 6% of adults](<https://bizweek.mu/financial-inclusion-bank-accounts-vs-digital-finance/news/>), a figure from before the three-year window.

**Between providers.** [AfricaNenda's 2024 case study describes MauCAS as Mauritius's national instant-payment switch for real-time retail payments](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf>). [In September 2026 Platform Africa reported that customers can move funds between their bank accounts through fintech platforms](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/09/17/pulse-of-fintech-2026-why-collaboration-is-the-key-to-unlocking-mauritius-financial-future/>). It said Mauritius had made progress on payments and interoperability.

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact is [AfricaNenda's 2024 account of MauCAS as the national instant-payment switch](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips_2024_MauCas_CaseStudy_en.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [National Fintech Strategy 2026-2030](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/mauritius-unveils-national-fintech-strategy-to-modernize-payments>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says payments between providers, or between wallets and banks, are in service.
