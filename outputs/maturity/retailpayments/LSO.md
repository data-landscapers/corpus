# Lesotho

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In July 2026 Vodacom Lesotho said M-Pesa customers across urban, peri-urban and rural Lesotho use their mobile wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/vodacom-lesotho-launches-m-pesa-investment-solution>). [They can now invest from them via Tsetela](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/vodacom-lesotho-launches-m-pesa-investment-solution>). [Vodacom Lesotho says M-Pesa has been used mainly for airtime, bill payments and person-to-person transfers, through an agent network](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91054>). [VCL Financial Services operates M-Pesa in Lesotho](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/24/vodacom-lesotho-launches-tsetela-investment-service-on-m-pesa/>). [Its customers can invest from their wallets through the Tsetela service launched in July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/24/vodacom-lesotho-launches-tsetela-investment-service-on-m-pesa/>).

**Between providers.** [LeSwitch fast payments, launched on 20 March 2024, give digital interoperability between banks and non-bank entities](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>). [Standard Lesotho Bank's Samuel Koatla says the LeSwitch instant payments stream has enabled instant transfers across mobile networks since 2022](<https://lestimes.com/?p=87043>).

**National switch.** [In May 2025 LeSwitch, formerly the National Payment Switch, connected banks and mobile money wallets](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>). [It now carries local ATM and POS debit card transactions, with POS in pilot from 11 May 2025](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>). [LeSwitch connects POS terminals, ATMs and mobile money in a single central hub, with its fast-payments phase launched on 20 March 2024](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>).
