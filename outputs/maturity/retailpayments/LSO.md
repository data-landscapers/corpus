# Lesotho

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Vodacom Lesotho says M-Pesa customers across urban, peri-urban and rural Lesotho use their mobile wallets, from which they can now invest via Tsetela](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/vodacom-lesotho-launches-m-pesa-investment-solution>). [M-Pesa mobile money in Lesotho has been used primarily for airtime purchases, bill payments and person-to-person transfers, through an agent network, according to Vodacom Lesotho](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91054>). [VCL Financial Services operates M-Pesa in Lesotho, and its customers can invest from their M-Pesa wallets through the Tsetela service launched in July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/24/vodacom-lesotho-launches-tsetela-investment-service-on-m-pesa/>). [Vodacom Lesotho's M-Pesa, run by VCL Financial Services, is in service as a mobile wallet and has launched the Tsetela investment service](<https://www.telemediamagazine.com/vodacom-lesotho-brings-micro-investing-to-m-pesa-as-africas-mobile-wallets-move-into-wealth/>).

**Between providers.** [LeSwitch fast payments, launched 20 March 2024, give digital interoperability between banks and non-bank entities, per Standard Lesotho Bank](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>). [Standard Lesotho Bank's Samuel Koatla says the LeSwitch instant payments stream, implemented in 2022, enables instant fund transfers across mobile networks (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://lestimes.com/?p=87043>).

**National switch.** [LeSwitch, formerly the National Payment Switch, connects banks and mobile money wallets and now carries local ATM and POS debit card transactions, with POS in pilot from 11 May 2025](<https://publiceyenews.com/2025/05/20/leswitch-expands-to-pos-transactions>). [LeSwitch connects POS terminals, ATMs and mobile money into a single central hub, with its fast-payments phase launched 20 March 2024](<https://www.standardlesothobank.co.ls/lesotho/personal/About-us/press-releases/press-release-lesotho-national-payment-switch-leswitch>).
