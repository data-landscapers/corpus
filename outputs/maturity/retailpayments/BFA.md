# Burkina Faso

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [Orange Money Burkina Faso's director general says the service has about 100,000 points of sale in the country, and SONAR Assurance signed a convention on 18 September 2026 to sell policies and take premium payments through it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/au-burkina-faso-l-assureur-sonar-mise-sur-orange-money-pour-numeriser-ses-services>). [Burkina24 reports Orange Burkina Faso's Max It app, relaunched on 18 September 2026, offers Orange Money mobile money to subscribers of all networks, per Orange's Abdramane Diallo](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>). [Wave Burkina offers digital finance used for payment, money transfer and savings by traders, farmers and young entrepreneurs, per its representative at an ANPFI training launch](<https://www.aib.media/guiriko-lanpfi-et-wave-burkina-lancent-une-campagne-de-formation-au-profit-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers/>).

**Between providers.** [Burkina24 reports Max It is interconnected with almost all the country's banks for transfers between bank accounts and Orange Money accounts, per Orange's Abdramane Diallo](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>). [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI interoperable instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, connects banks, e-money institutions and payment institutions, with 80 connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>).
