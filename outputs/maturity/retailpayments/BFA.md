# Burkina Faso

## P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

**Mobile money in service.** [In September 2026 Orange Money Burkina Faso's director general said the service has about 100,000 points of sale](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/au-burkina-faso-l-assureur-sonar-mise-sur-orange-money-pour-numeriser-ses-services>). [On 18 September 2026 SONAR Assurance signed a convention to sell policies and take premiums through it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/au-burkina-faso-l-assureur-sonar-mise-sur-orange-money-pour-numeriser-ses-services>). [Orange Burkina Faso's Max It app, relaunched that day, offers Orange Money to subscribers of all networks](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>). [In July 2026 Wave Burkina said traders, farmers and young entrepreneurs use its service for payment, money transfer and savings](<https://www.aib.media/guiriko-lanpfi-et-wave-burkina-lancent-une-campagne-de-formation-au-profit-des-consommateurs-des-services-financiers/>).

**Between providers.** [In September 2026 Orange said Max It is interconnected with almost all the country's banks for transfers between bank accounts and Orange Money](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>). [The BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform, launched on 30 September 2025, connects banks, e-money institutions and payment institutions](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [By 24 June 2026, 80 were connected](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).

**National switch.** No source found since October 2023 says a national switch connects wallets and bank accounts. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 Max It linked almost all the country's banks with Orange Money accounts](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>).
