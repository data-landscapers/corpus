# Zimbabwe

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Zimbabwe Population Registration System is a central database of citizens' demographic information](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zims-digital-population-registry-sets-benchmark-for-africa/>), the minister told registrars in July 2026. [It stores name, date of birth, sex, nationality, marital status, place of birth and residence, and the national ID number](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/issuance-of-id-cards-going-on-smoothly-rg/>), The Herald reported in 2023. [Biometric Update reports it was upgraded in a reform from about 2023 to mid-2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>). In February 2026 the minister said [the Registrar-General holds records of everyone](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/registrar-general-to-get-voters-roll-custody/>).

**Updated from vital events.** In February 2026 the minister said [the system updates automatically and in real time when a death is registered](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/registrar-general-to-get-voters-roll-custody/>). It also updates when a citizen reaches voting age.

**Source for others.** [The system is integrated with the Department of Immigration's Online Border Management System](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zims-digital-population-registry-sets-benchmark-for-africa/>), which is fully operational, The Herald reports. A link to the police is under way, the minister said. [In 2025 Biometric Update reported the upgraded registry was linked to other services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>).
