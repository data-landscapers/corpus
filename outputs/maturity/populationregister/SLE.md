# Sierra Leone

## Population register

**Register in service.** Sierra Leone holds its residents' details in a national population registry. [In January 2024 Biometric Update reported that the 11-digit NIN on the national ID card is a unique number](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202401/sierra-leone-urges-citizens-to-obtain-biometric-id-cards-for-security-services>), linked to each holder's personal details in that registry. Citizens were being urged to obtain the biometric card.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register.
