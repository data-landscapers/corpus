# Population register: population register

*As at 2026-11-10.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy puts an electronic population register first among the base registers, continuously updated from civil registration. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## Countries

### Algeria: 5 Leading

The civil-status register, holding births and deaths from every commune, was in use in 2025, and 23 ministries and bodies drew on it.

- The Interior Ministry says 23 ministries and public bodies drew on Registre national de l'Etat civil data in 2023, nearly 111 million consultations ([source, 2023](https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/)).

### Benin: 5 Leading

Other systems drew on the population register in 2026.

- Banouto reports the ANIP eServices portal's Explorez votre identite service takes its data from the RNPP ([source, 2026-09-18](https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260918-anip-voici-comment-decouvrir-combien-de-beninois-partagent-votre-nom-prenom-ou-date-de-naissance)).
- ANIP says the RNPP is the only source for identity cards, passports and driving licences, and that phone SIM acquisition and public service platforms authenticate against the NPI ([source, 2026-05](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf)).

### Chad: 5 Leading

The biometric population register was in service in 2026, takes the births a national campaign registers, and the finance ministry consults it.

- The Ministry of Finance and ANATS signed a protocol giving the ministry a secured channel to consult data from the registre national biométrique des populations for identification and verification in administrative, budgetary and financial processes, and the ANATS director-general says all public and private administrations will be able to connect to it ([source, 2026-03-10](https://atpe.td/donnees-securisees-le-ministere-des-finances-dispose-dun-cadre-securise/)).

### Cote d'Ivoire: 5 Leading

The national register of persons was in service in 2026, fed from the digitised civil-status registers and drawn on by the social registry.

- BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026 interconnecting the Registre Social Unique with ONECI's databases, so beneficiaries are identified through the Registre National des Personnes Physiques run by ONECI ([source, 2026-04-02](https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/)).

### DR Congo: 5 Leading

Other systems drew on the population register in 2024.

- ONIP's legal expert said the FGP is the single data source for producing all secure titles and documents ([source, 2024-08-09](https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/)).

### Kenya: 5 Leading

Other systems drew on the population register in 2026.

- The State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services says public and private entities with a signed MOU access the register for identity verification, at a Ksh. 20 fee per hit ([source, 2026-08-30](https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service)).

### Namibia: 5 Leading

The population register was in service in 2026, fed by electronic birth and death notices from 135 health facilities and used to verify grant recipients.

- The Auditor General's report says social grant recipients on the Integrated Social Assistance System were checked against records in the National Population Registration System ([source, 2026-10-15](https://neweralive.na/n44m-paid-to-ghost-grant-beneficiaries/)).
- Namibia's Chief Registrar said all applicants for the Covid-19 Emergency Income Grant were verified against the NPRS, and social protection verifies live status against it ([source, 2026-07-18](https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf)).

### South Africa: 5 Leading

Other systems drew on the population register in 2026.

- In the demonstration a verifier app requested the population register details linked to the minister's ID, which appeared on the official's phone after he approved sharing ([source, 2026-10-01](https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/)).
- Banks, mobile operators and insurers verify customers' identities in real time against the National Population Register through the Online Verification System, at ZAR 10 a check since 1 July 2025 ([source, 2025-07-01](https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/)).

### Tunisia: 5 Leading

Other systems drew on the population register in 2026.

- The playbook says the register is the pivot for sectoral identifiers and that its data are exchanged with state structures through the national interoperability platform ([source, 2026-05](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf)).

### Djibouti: 3 Established

A population register was in service in 2026; no source found reports updating from births and deaths.

- La Nation reports that the DGPF searches the national population database for each ID applicant and integrates each file into the Registre national des personnes physiques platform, with data recorded there before biometric enrolment ([source, 2026-07-23](https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/)).

### Eswatini: 3 Established

A population register was in service in 2025; no source found reports updating from births and deaths.

- Eswatini's Home Affairs ministry says its mopping exercise, registering births and deaths and converting manual birth certificates in Manzini and Hhohho in the second quarter of 2025/26, has improved the National Population Register ([source, 2025-10-27](https://swazidailynews.com/4842/future/mopping-exercise-brings-government-closer-to-the-people/)).

### Lesotho: 3 Established

Home Affairs maintained a National Population Register in 2026; whether births and deaths are entered in it is not known.

### Mali: 3 Established

A population register was in service in 2025; no source found reports updating from births and deaths.

- DNEC says a biometric file of the population has been built from civil status under RAVEC, administered by ANSAEC ([source, 2025-05](https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf)).
- The Malian authorities launched the new Mali Kura Biométrie system managing the RAVEC (administrative census for civil-status purposes) data of the Malian population at a ceremony on 13 February 2024, saying the population data had been recovered and secured ([source, 2024-02-13](https://malikonews.com/mali-mali-kura-biometrie-un-nouveau-systeme-numerique-souverain-des-donnees-ravec/)).

### Morocco: 3 Established

The population register held 22 million people in 2026 and is used to target social aid; whether births or deaths are entered is not known.

- The Times of India reports that Morocco has registered 22 million of its 39 million people in its national population register ([source, 2026-06](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms)).
- The ANSS annual report, as reported by Le Brief, says the Registre national de la population (RNP) is crossed with the RSU to target direct social aid ([source, 2025-12](https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/)).

### Seychelles: 3 Established

A national population database was in service in 2025; no source found says births or deaths are entered in it.

### Sierra Leone: 3 Established

A national population registry held ID card holders' details in 2024; whether births and deaths are entered in it is not known.

- Biometric Update reports that the 11-digit NIN on Sierra Leone's ID card is a unique identifier linked to the card holder's personal details in the national population registry ([source, 2024-01-11](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202401/sierra-leone-urges-citizens-to-obtain-biometric-id-cards-for-security-services)).

### Zimbabwe: 3 Established

The national population registry was upgraded by mid-2025 and linked to other services; whether births or deaths are entered is not known.

- Biometric Update reports that Zimbabwe upgraded its national population registry in a reform begun around 2023 and concluded by mid-2025 ([source, 2025-07](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform)).
- The Herald reports ZPRS is the official platform that stores and manages demographic data of all Zimbabwean citizens, including name, date of birth, sex, nationality, marital status, place of birth and residence, and national ID number, with an upgrade being finalised ([source, 2023-11-12](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/issuance-of-id-cards-going-on-smoothly-rg/)).

### Burkina Faso: 2 Nascent

A population register was under way in 2024; none in service is on record.

- Law 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 provides for a national register of the unique identifier of natural persons, centralising their biographic and biometric data, with the body in charge to be created by decree and enrolment to follow the system's set-up ([source, 2024-10-11](https://assembleenationale.bf/loip/117)).

### Guinea: 2 Nascent

A register built from the 2025 census held 9 million people, but in 2026 it was still being set up; none in service is known.

- ONECI launched civil-status archive digitisation at Kindia on 15 July 2026, which the electoral director general Djenabou Toure said will set up a national register of natural persons, covering the 33 prefectures and 13 Conakry communes ([source, 2026-07-15](https://www.africaguinee.com/fichier-electoral-et-etat-civil-bientot-un-numero-identifiant-unique-pour-chaque-citoyen/)).
- A workshop launched on 18 September 2026 on extending the Registre national des personnes physiques, with a platform for verifying and validating identity to have its first phase done before end-2026, per the minister ([source, 2026-09-18](https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/)).
- PN-RAVEC workshop of 12-17 January 2026 reviewed draft implementing texts on the mechanisms for constituting an interoperable Registre National des Personnes Physiques under Laws L/2023/019/CNT and L/2023/020/CNT ([source, 2026-01](https://ravec.gov.gn/ravec-kindia-atelier-de-relecture-et-validation-des-textes-dapplication-portant-etat-civil-et-identification-des-personnes-physiques/)).

### Madagascar: 2 Nascent

### Niger: 2 Nascent

A population register was under way in 2026; none in service is on record.

- Niger's director general of territorial administration said on 2026-09-03 that the reforms include creating a National Population Register, to become a single interoperable referential, with a unique number for each person ([source, 2026-09-03](https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/)).
