## Population register

**Register in service.** [Decree D/2025/052 of 14 April 2025 sets up the Registre national des personnes physiques from communal and consular resident registers](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>). [A workshop launched on 18 September 2026 on extending the Registre national des personnes physiques, with a platform for verifying and validating identity to have its first phase done before end-2026, per the minister](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>). [PN-RAVEC workshop of 12-17 January 2026 reviewed draft implementing texts on the mechanisms for constituting an interoperable Registre National des Personnes Physiques under Laws L/2023/019/CNT and L/2023/020/CNT](<https://ravec.gov.gn/ravec-kindia-atelier-de-relecture-et-validation-des-textes-dapplication-portant-etat-civil-et-identification-des-personnes-physiques/>). [The MATD minister says ONECI is pursuing the setting up of the Registre national des personnes physiques, with a biometric census at national scale in 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>).
