# Guinea

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Decree D/2025/052 of 14 April 2025 sets up the national register of natural persons](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>) from communal and consular resident registers. The territorial administration ministry said in 2025 that ONECI was [building the register through a national biometric census](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>). On 19 July 2025 the ministry [put the register at 9,010,181 people after removing duplicates from the census](<https://www.guinee114.com/2025/07/19/matd-les-statistiques-provisoires-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-et-du-fichier-electoral-biometrique-presentees-aux-acteurs-socio-politiques/>). A workshop in January 2026 [reviewed draft texts on how the register is to be constituted](<https://ravec.gov.gn/ravec-kindia-atelier-de-relecture-et-validation-des-textes-dapplication-portant-etat-civil-et-identification-des-personnes-physiques/>). On 18 September 2026 the minister [launched work on extending it, with an identity verification platform due in a first phase by the end of 2026](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the register. Decree D/2025/052 [says the civil status register keeps it up to date](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>), but no source says this is done.

**Source for others.** No source found since October 2023 says other systems take records from the register. The minister's September 2026 workshop [planned an identity verification platform on it](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>).
