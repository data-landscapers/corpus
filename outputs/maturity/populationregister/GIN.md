# Guinea

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Decree D/2025/052 of 14 April 2025 sets up the national register of natural persons](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>) from communal and consular resident registers. A [census launched on 15 April 2025 was to build it](<https://guineenews.org/2025/04/22/etat-civil-aucun-document-didentite-navait-de-base-legale-selon-djenabou-toure/>). On 19 July 2025 the ministry [put it at 9,010,181 people after removing duplicates](<https://www.guinee114.com/2025/07/19/matd-les-statistiques-provisoires-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-et-du-fichier-electoral-biometrique-presentees-aux-acteurs-socio-politiques/>). In December 2025 the elections director [said it recorded nearly 10 million people](<https://www.lindependant.org/actualites-etat-civil-guineen-la-refondation-en-marche-lidentification-au-coeur-du-futur-fichier-electoral/>). A workshop in January 2026 [reviewed draft texts on how the register is to be constituted](<https://ravec.gov.gn/ravec-kindia-atelier-de-relecture-et-validation-des-textes-dapplication-portant-etat-civil-et-identification-des-personnes-physiques/>). On 15 July 2026 ONECI [began digitising civil status archives, which the director said will set up the register](<https://www.africaguinee.com/fichier-electoral-et-etat-civil-bientot-un-numero-identifiant-unique-pour-chaque-citoyen/>). In September the minister [launched work on extending it](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the register. Decree D/2025/052 [says the civil status register keeps it up to date](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>), but no source says this is done.

**Source for others.** No source found since October 2023 says other systems take records from the register. The minister's September 2026 workshop [planned an identity verification platform on it](<https://www.guinee114.com/2026/09/18/matd-dge-djenabou-toure-lance-latelier-sur-lextension-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>).
