# Benin

## Population register

**Register in service.** [ANIP reported more than 13.8 million people registered in the RNPP in May 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Banouto reports ANIP's eServices tool returns name and birth-date statistics drawn from the Registre National des Personnes Physiques 2026, covering about 14 million inhabitants](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260918-anip-voici-comment-decouvrir-combien-de-beninois-partagent-votre-nom-prenom-ou-date-de-naissance>). [ANIP is stated to manage the Registre National des Personnes Physiques, a trusted identity referential for people in Benin, whose data a tender dated 2026-08-31 seeks to exploit](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/312907>). [The ANIP director general says a part of the population is not yet enrolled in the Registre national des personnes physiques, so the register is operating and enrolling people](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). [The article describes the RNPP as the database of Beninese and foreigners living in Benin, managed by ANIP](<https://lanationbenin.bj/actualites/interoperabilite-avec-le-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-des-structures-publiques-et-privees-entretenues-sur-les-avantages>).

**Updated from vital events.** [ANIP says a birth declaration enters the person in the RNPP and a death automatically updates the RNPP](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Source for others.** [Banouto reports the ANIP eServices portal's Explorez votre identite service takes its data from the RNPP](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260918-anip-voici-comment-decouvrir-combien-de-beninois-partagent-votre-nom-prenom-ou-date-de-naissance>). [ANIP says the RNPP is the only source for identity cards, passports and driving licences, and that phone SIM acquisition and public service platforms authenticate against the NPI](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [It put the national public services linked to the RNPP at 19 in 2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>).
