# Benin

## Population register

**Register in service.** In May 2026 [ANIP reported more than 13.8 million people in the RNPP](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). In September 2026 [its eServices tool drew statistics from the 2026 register](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260918-anip-voici-comment-decouvrir-combien-de-beninois-partagent-votre-nom-prenom-ou-date-de-naissance>), covering about 14 million inhabitants. [An August 2026 tender describes the register as Benin's trusted identity referential](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/312907>), managed by ANIP. [ANIP's director general says part of the population is not yet enrolled](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). [The register holds Beninese and foreigners living in Benin](<https://lanationbenin.bj/actualites/interoperabilite-avec-le-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques-des-structures-publiques-et-privees-entretenues-sur-les-avantages>).

**Updated from vital events.** ANIP says [a birth declaration enters the person in the register and a death updates it automatically](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). In February 2025 its director general said [more than 400,000 digitised death records had been taken into the register](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/2994/inquietudes-autour-fiabilite-liste-electorale-informatisee-benin-anip-reaffirme-credibilite-grace-systeme-mise-jour-continue/>). Since February 2026 [children born in pilot health facilities are entered in it from birth](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/b%C3%A9nin-journ%C3%A9e-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-2026>), ANIP says.

**Source for others.** [Banouto reports the ANIP eServices portal's Explorez votre identite service takes its data from the RNPP](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20260918-anip-voici-comment-decouvrir-combien-de-beninois-partagent-votre-nom-prenom-ou-date-de-naissance>). [ANIP says the RNPP is the only source for identity cards, passports and driving licences, and that phone SIM acquisition and public service platforms authenticate against the NPI](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [It put the national public services linked to the RNPP at 19 in 2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>).
