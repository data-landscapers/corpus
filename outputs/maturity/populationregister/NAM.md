# Namibia

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The ministries of civil registration and social protection signed an agreement integrating the National Population Registration System with the social protection system to verify existence and live status of persons](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). [The Ministry says its mandate is to manage the National Population Register and that the National Population Registration System (NPRS, e-NPRS) holds ID application records and verifies them (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mhaiss.gov.na/documents/292728/537582/HAI+Annual+Report+2019-2020+web-+23+November+2023-1.pdf/36f0e050-9a59-a5db-7c2a-13373d1502a2?download=true&t=1701160032086>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The Ministry says the e-birth notification system electronically notifies the e-NPRS of a birth and captures the child's birth details, and deaths are notified electronically in line with births (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mhaiss.gov.na/documents/292728/537582/HAI+Annual+Report+2019-2020+web-+23+November+2023-1.pdf/36f0e050-9a59-a5db-7c2a-13373d1502a2?download=true&t=1701160032086>).

**Source for others.** [Namibia's Chief Registrar said all applicants for the Covid-19 Emergency Income Grant were verified against the NPRS, and social protection verifies live status against it](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). [The Ministry says the e-birth notification system verifies the mother's identity against the NPRS (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://mhaiss.gov.na/documents/292728/537582/HAI+Annual+Report+2019-2020+web-+23+November+2023-1.pdf/36f0e050-9a59-a5db-7c2a-13373d1502a2?download=true&t=1701160032086>).
