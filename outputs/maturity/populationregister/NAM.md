# Namibia

## Population register

**Register in service.** The home affairs ministry [runs the National Population Registration System](<https://neweralive.na/n44m-paid-to-ghost-grant-beneficiaries/>), against which the Auditor General checked grant records in October 2026. The civil registration and social protection ministries [signed an agreement linking it to the social protection system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>).

**Updated from vital events.** In July 2024 the deputy minister said [births and deaths at 135 connected health facilities are notified electronically to the register](<https://thebrief.com.na/2024/07/over-94000-namibians-issued-with-identity-documents/>). The ministry described the same e-birth and e-death notices in 2020.

**Source for others.** In October 2026 the Auditor General [checked 488,753 social grant beneficiaries against the population register and found 8,099 with no match](<https://neweralive.na/n44m-paid-to-ghost-grant-beneficiaries/>). The Chief Registrar has said [Covid-19 emergency grant applicants were verified against the register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). Social protection checks whether people are alive against it.
