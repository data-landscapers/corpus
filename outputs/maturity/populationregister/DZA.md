# Algeria

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Interior Ministry says the national civil-status register is in use](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/>), with nearly 111 million consultations in 2023. [It entered service on 15 February 2014](<https://dknews-dz.com/article/3758-collectivites-le-ministere-de-linterieur-presente-le-registre-national-de-letat-civil-automatise-mis-en-service-samedi.html>), with about 59 million digitised birth acts from 1,541 communes. [The operation behind it began in 2010](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>), to build the population database with the national identification number. [On 18 May 2025 the Council of Ministers sent back a draft civil-status law](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/le-president-tebboune-exige-une-reforme-approfondie-de-letat-civil/2025/20/09/>). It aims at a unified civil-status database open to every administration concerned.

**Updated from vital events.** [A 2025 study says the national automated civil-status register links every commune and holds birth, marriage and death certificates](<https://imcra-az.org/uploads/public_files/2025-10/81246.pdf>). The register is the population database, so each birth and death registered is entered in it.

**Source for others.** [The Interior Ministry says 23 ministries and public bodies drew on the register's data in 2023](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/>). They made nearly 111 million consultations.
