# Algeria

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Interior Ministry says the Registre national de l'Etat civil is in use, with access to its data giving nearly 111 million consultations in 2023](<https://www.lnr-dz.com/2024/01/18/merad-senquiert-de-letat-davancement-des-projets-de-modernisation/>). [The Interior Ministry put the national automated civil-status register into service on 15 February 2014, holding about 59 million digitised birth acts collected from the databases of 1,541 communes (2014, before the three-year window)](<https://dknews-dz.com/article/3758-collectivites-le-ministere-de-linterieur-presente-le-registre-national-de-letat-civil-automatise-mis-en-service-samedi.html>). [The Ministry of the Interior says the operation launched in 2010 aims to constitute the population database through the centralised national civil-status register (RNEC) and the NIN (2010, before the three-year window)](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). [On 18 May 2025 the Council of Ministers sent back a draft civil-status law whose aim is a unified national civil-status database open to all the administrations concerned](<https://lavoiedalgerie.dz/le-president-tebboune-exige-une-reforme-approfondie-de-letat-civil/2025/20/09/>).
