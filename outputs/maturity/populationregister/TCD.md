# Chad

## Population register

**Register in service.** [In March 2026 ANATS's director-general said the government has deployed an integrated population and secure-titles system](<https://atpe.td/donnees-securisees-le-ministere-des-finances-dispose-dun-cadre-securise/>). Its base is the national biometric population register, designed to keep each person in it unique.

**Updated from vital events.** In July 2026 ANATS launched a campaign to register a million children's births. [Its director general said the births collected will be entered in the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>). Community teams with smartphones collect them household by household.

**Source for others.** [In March 2026 the Ministry of Finance and ANATS signed a protocol to consult the biometric population register](<https://atpe.td/donnees-securisees-le-ministere-des-finances-dispose-dun-cadre-securise/>). The channel serves identification and checks in budget and financial work. ANATS's director-general says all public and private administrations will be able to connect.
