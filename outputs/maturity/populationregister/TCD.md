# Chad

## Population register

**Register in service.** [ANATS's director-general says the government has deployed an integrated population and secure-titles management system whose base is the registre national biométrique des populations, designed to guarantee each person's uniqueness](<https://atpe.td/donnees-securisees-le-ministere-des-finances-dispose-dun-cadre-securise/>).

**Updated from vital events.** In July 2026 ANATS launched a campaign to register a million children's births. [Its director general said the births collected will be entered in the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>). Community teams with smartphones collect them household by household.

**Source for others.** [The Ministry of Finance and ANATS signed a protocol giving the ministry a secured channel to consult data from the registre national biométrique des populations for identification and verification in administrative, budgetary and financial processes, and the ANATS director-general says all public and private administrations will be able to connect to it](<https://atpe.td/donnees-securisees-le-ministere-des-finances-dispose-dun-cadre-securise/>).
