# Djibouti

## Population register

**Register in service.** [La Nation reports that the DGPF searches the national population database for each ID applicant and integrates each file into the Registre national des personnes physiques platform, with data recorded there before biometric enrolment](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). [The Djibouti digital ministry's page says Law 39/AN/19 of 21 January 2019 provides for a centralised biometric national register of natural persons (RNPP) and a national identification number (NNI) assigned at birth (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).
