# Djibouti

## Population register

**Register in service.** In July 2026 [La Nation reported that the DGPF searches the population database for each ID applicant](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). Each file goes into the RNPP platform before biometric enrolment. [Law 39/AN/19 of 21 January 2019 provides for the register and an identification number given at birth](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>). That law predates the three-year window.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). The July 2026 report does not say births or deaths enter the register.
