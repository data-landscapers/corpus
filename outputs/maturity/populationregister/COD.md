# DR Congo

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Radio Okapi reports that Kinshasa's provincial Interior Minister launched on 4 November 2025 a project to digitise the Kinshasa population register, with census agents collecting household data into a compilation server, supported by UNICEF among partners](<https://www.radiookapi.net/2025/11/04/actualite/politique/debut-du-projet-de-digitalisation-de-registre-de-la-population-de>). [ONIP's legal expert described the FGP as the single reference for identifying individuals, covering Congolese and foreigners resident and Congolese abroad](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). [ONIP, created in 2011, was entrusted with building the general population file (fichier général de la population), and a 2022 decree organised identification and census operations, per BBC News Afrique](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>).

**Updated from vital events.** [ONIP's legal expert said civil-status events (births, deaths, marriages) feed the FGP after being registered at the commune and reported to ONIP](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

**Source for others.** [ONIP's legal expert said the FGP is the single data source for producing all secure titles and documents](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).
