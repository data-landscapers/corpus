# DR Congo

## Population register

**Register in service.** [In August 2024 ONIP's legal expert called the general population file (FGP) the single reference for identifying individuals](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). It covers Congolese, resident foreigners and Congolese abroad. [ONIP was created in 2011 to build the file, and a 2022 decree organised identification and census operations](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). [On 4 November 2025 Kinshasa's provincial Interior Minister launched the digitisation of the city's population register](<https://www.radiookapi.net/2025/11/04/actualite/politique/debut-du-projet-de-digitalisation-de-registre-de-la-population-de>). Census agents collect household data into a central server, with UNICEF among the partners. [In 2024 Brussels funded a USD 43,262 pilot to give the commune of Kintambo a digital register linked to the Ville-Province](<https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?lc=en&df[ds]=DcdDisseminateFinalDMZ&df[id]=DSD_CRS%40DF_CRS&df[ag]=OECD.DCD.FSD&dq=BEL.COD.1000.._T._T.C.Q._T..&pd=2024%2C2024&to[TIME_PERIOD]=false>).

**Updated from vital events.** [ONIP's legal expert said births, deaths and marriages feed the FGP](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). Each is registered at the commune first and then reported to ONIP.

**Source for others.** [ONIP's legal expert said the FGP is the single data source for producing all secure titles and documents](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).
