# Mali

## Population register

**Register in service.** [DNEC says a biometric population file has been built from civil status under RAVEC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). ANSAEC administers it. On 13 February 2024 [the authorities launched Mali Kura Biométrie to manage the RAVEC data](<https://malikonews.com/mali-mali-kura-biometrie-un-nouveau-systeme-numerique-souverain-des-donnees-ravec/>). They said the population data had been recovered and secured. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register. The nearest fact: [in May 2025 DNEC said the population's biometric file was built from civil status records under RAVEC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).
