# Cote d'Ivoire

## Population register

**Register in service.** [ONECI, under the Interior Ministry, is described as managing the RNPP and assigning the national identification number, which is the basis the PARAE feasibility study launched on 5 October 2026 builds on](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). [The RNPP (Registre national des personnes physiques) holds citizens' biometric and civil data and assigns each a national identification number (NNI), with 11 million people holding one according to the ONECI director-general as relayed by the article](<https://digitalmag.ci/identite-numerique-la-cote-divoire-pays-connecte-mais-pas-encore-pleinement-identifie/>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source held since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register. The nearest held fact: [BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026 interconnecting the Registre Social Unique with ONECI's databases, so beneficiaries are identified through the Registre National des Personnes Physiques run by ONECI](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).
