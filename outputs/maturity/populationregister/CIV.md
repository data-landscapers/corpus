# Cote d'Ivoire

## Population register

**Register in service.** [ONECI, under the Interior Ministry, runs the RNPP and assigns the national identification number](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-gouvernement-mise-sur-le-parae-pour-renforcer-la-confiance-dans-les-services-publics-numeriques/>). A feasibility study launched on 5 October 2026 builds on it. [The RNPP holds citizens' biometric and civil data and gives each a national identification number](<https://digitalmag.ci/identite-numerique-la-cote-divoire-pays-connecte-mais-pas-encore-pleinement-identifie/>). ONECI's director general puts holders at 11 million. [On 16 September 2025 he said ONECI's civil-status system and national server are linked to the register](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2025-lidentite-est-le-parapluie-de-tout-individu-dg-oneci/>). [A civil-status request platform reported in October 2026 is part of building out the RNPP](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/cote-divoire-le-logiciel-national-detat-civil-deploye-la-connectivite-reste-un-defi_65266>).

**Updated from vital events.** In October 2026 [ONECI was digitising the civil-status registers of births, marriages and deaths to feed the RNPP](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-trois-outils-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>). About 3.8 million records had been digitised in 14 centres.

**Source for others.** [BCPFS and ONECI signed a convention on 2 April 2026 linking the Registre Social Unique to ONECI's databases](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). Beneficiaries of the social register are now identified through the RNPP.
