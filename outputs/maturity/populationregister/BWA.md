# Botswana

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The National Registration (Amendment) Act 2025, assented on 23 March 2025, provides for a Unified Population Register](<https://www.botswanalaws.com/PDF/StatutesActpdf/2025Actpdf/007.pdf>). The registrar is to keep it for citizens, dual citizens and non-citizen residents. The Act comes into force on a date the minister sets by notice. Earlier that month the minister [told Parliament the amendment would also bring in an electronic national identity system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>). The national identity card is the Omang, and [12,241 Omang cards lay uncollected at offices across the country](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>).

**Updated from vital events.** The minister [did not say how births and deaths would reach the planned register](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>). His statement covered the Act's amendment and the identity cards.

**Source for others.** [The register is to hold citizens, dual citizens and non-citizen residents](<https://www.botswanalaws.com/PDF/StatutesActpdf/2025Actpdf/007.pdf>). The minister [named no other register or service that would draw on it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>).
