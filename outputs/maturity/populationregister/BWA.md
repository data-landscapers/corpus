# Botswana

## Population register

**Register in service.** In March 2025 the Minister of Labour and Home Affairs told Parliament that [the National Registration Act was being amended to introduce a population register](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>). The same amendment brings in an electronic national identity system and the registration of non-citizens. He gave no date for the bill. The national identity card is the Omang, and [12,241 Omang cards lay uncollected at offices across the country](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>).

**Updated from vital events.** The minister [did not say how births and deaths would reach the planned register](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>). His statement covered the Act's amendment and the identity cards.

**Source for others.** The amendment [is meant to introduce the register, the electronic identity system and the registration of non-citizens](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/85066>). The minister named no other register or service that would draw on it.
