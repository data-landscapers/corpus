# South Africa

## Population register

**Register in service.** [In a live demonstration, Minister Schreiber showed a digital identity credential issued through the population register](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). Its record held his photograph. [He told Parliament that 377,060 identity numbers remain blocked on the National Population Register](<https://iol.co.za/news/politics/2026-07-17-home-affairs-struggles-to-unblock-identity-numbers-due-to-unserviceable-addresses/>), which Home Affairs runs. [President Ramaphosa said government is building an Intelligent Population Register with biometric data for everyone in the country](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-unveils-biometric-population-register-tighten-migration-controls>). It is to be the base for a digital ID. [Home Affairs says the existing register records names, births and deaths](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>). [In January 2024 a mainframe fault at SITA cut access to it](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/south-africas-home-affairs-taken-offline-by-mainframe-failure/>), and Home Affairs services depend on it.

**Updated from vital events.** [The existing National Population Register records births and deaths, per the Department of Home Affairs](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>).

**Source for others.** [In the same demonstration a verifier app asked for the register details linked to the minister's ID](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). They appeared on the official's phone once he approved. [Banks, mobile operators and insurers check customers in real time against the register](<https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/>), through the Online Verification System. Each check has cost ZAR 10 since 1 July 2025.
