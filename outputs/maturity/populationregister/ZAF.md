# South Africa

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Minister Schreiber said his digital identity credential was formally issued through the population register, whose record held his photograph, in a live demonstration](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [Minister Schreiber told Parliament that 377,060 identity numbers remain blocked on the National Population Register, which Home Affairs runs](<https://iol.co.za/news/politics/2026-07-17-home-affairs-struggles-to-unblock-identity-numbers-due-to-unserviceable-addresses/>). [President Ramaphosa said government is establishing an Intelligent Population Register containing biometric data for every person in the country, as the foundation for a Digital ID](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sa-unveils-biometric-population-register-tighten-migration-controls>). [The existing National Population Register records basic information including names, births and deaths, per the Department of Home Affairs](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The existing National Population Register records births and deaths, per the Department of Home Affairs](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>).

**Source for others.** [In the demonstration a verifier app requested the population register details linked to the minister's ID, which appeared on the official's phone after he approved sharing](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-digital-id-schreiber-demo/286764/>). [Banks, mobile operators and insurers verify customers' identities in real time against the National Population Register through the Online Verification System, at ZAR 10 a check since 1 July 2025](<https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/>).
