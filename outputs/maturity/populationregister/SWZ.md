# Eswatini

## Population register

**Register in service.** [In October 2025 Eswatini's Home Affairs ministry said its mopping exercise had improved the National Population Register](<https://swazidailynews.com/4842/future/mopping-exercise-brings-government-closer-to-the-people/>). [The exercise converted manual birth certificates in Manzini and Hhohho](<https://swazidailynews.com/4842/future/mopping-exercise-brings-government-closer-to-the-people/>). [In November 2025 the Times of Eswatini reported that an audit of the National Population Registry traced irregular birth certificates and documents to specific typist and registrar user logins](<https://times.co.sz/28643/news/2-home-affairs-employees-fired-over-id-fraud/>).

**Updated from vital events.** [Home Affairs registered births and deaths in Manzini and Hhohho in the second quarter of 2025/26](<https://swazidailynews.com/4842/future/mopping-exercise-brings-government-closer-to-the-people/>). It says these entries improved the National Population Register.
