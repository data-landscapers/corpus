# Kenya

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Directorate of Population Registration Services is mandated to manage, maintain and operate the Maisha Integrated Population register, which the State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services says currently offers identity verification to approved users](<https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service>). [IPRS Director General Judy Kilobi said the government has integrated key agencies and strengthened the national population register](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ngao-trained-on-revised-national-id-registration-system/>). [The EACC article describes the Integrated Population Register System (IPRS) as a system that consolidates citizen registration data held by the State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services](<https://eacc.go.ke/en/default/commission-to-link-ict-systems-with-integrated-population-register-system/>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The EACC article says the IPRS consolidates citizen registration data including births and deaths, national registration bureau records, refugee records and immigration data](<https://eacc.go.ke/en/default/commission-to-link-ict-systems-with-integrated-population-register-system/>).

**Source for others.** [The State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services says public and private entities with a signed MOU access the register for identity verification, at a Ksh. 20 fee per hit](<https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service>).
