# Kenya

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Directorate of Population Registration Services runs the Maisha Integrated Population register](<https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service>). The State Department for Immigration says it offers identity verification to approved users. [The IPRS director general said key agencies are integrated with it](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/ngao-trained-on-revised-national-id-registration-system/>). [The EACC describes the IPRS as consolidating the State Department's citizen registration data](<https://eacc.go.ke/en/default/commission-to-link-ict-systems-with-integrated-population-register-system/>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The EACC article says the IPRS consolidates citizen registration data including births and deaths, national registration bureau records, refugee records and immigration data](<https://eacc.go.ke/en/default/commission-to-link-ict-systems-with-integrated-population-register-system/>). [Identity Week said in October 2026 that births registered through the e-service pass directly to the population registry](<https://identityweek.net/birth-registration-e-service-reduces-admin-from-months-to-days/>). [Over 270,000 births had been registered that way by September 2026](<https://identityweek.net/birth-registration-e-service-reduces-admin-from-months-to-days/>).

**Source for others.** [Public and private bodies with a signed MOU check identities against the register](<https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service>), the State Department for Immigration says. Each check costs Ksh. 20.
