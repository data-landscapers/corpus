# Niger

## Population register

**Register in service.** On 3 September 2026 [the director general of territorial administration said the reforms include a National Population Register](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>). It is to be a single interoperable referential, with a unique number for each person. [At a workshop in Agadez officials said the register is being built around biometric enrolment](<https://anp.ne/ouverture-a-agadez-dun-atelier-de-sensibilisation-sur-les-reformes-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-au-niger/>). [The Interior Ministry says a first enrolment phase begins in Niamey's five districts](<https://www.lesahel.org/tillaberi-reformes-de-letat-civil-et-de-lidentification-une-mission-de-sensibilisation-sillonne-la-region/>). Other regions follow in 2027.
