# Morocco

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Times of India reports that Morocco has registered 22 million of its 39 million people in its national population register](<https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms>). [The Head of Government's department says 22.5 million people were enrolled in the Registre National de la Population as of mid-September 2025, with enrolment continuing](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [The World Bank project description says it supports the national rollout of the National Population Registry (RNP) as a trusted source of identity with digital authentication](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>). [The World Bank says the first project designed and implemented the National Population Registry, established by Law 72-18, and enrolled about half the population](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/04/world-bank-greenlights-70m-boost-for-morocco-s-innovative-social-protection-delivery-systems>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source held since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register.
