# Morocco

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Times of India reports 22 million of 39 million people in the population register](<https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms>). [ANSS says households must be in the register to receive direct social aid](<https://www.anss.gov.ma/fr/conditions-d-eligibilite>). [The Head of Government's department counted 22.5 million people enrolled by mid-September 2025](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [A World Bank project supports the register's national rollout](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>) as a trusted source of identity.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register. The nearest held fact is [its use for 5 million households' social programmes](<https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms>).

**Source for others.** The Times of India reports that [the population register already helps 5 million eligible households reach social programmes](<https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms>). In November 2025 the government [said social support targeting under Law 72.18 rests on two databases](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/12/272839/morocco-expands-direct-social-support-with-measured-data-driven-reform/>). They are the population register and the Unified Social Register. The social support agency [crosses the population register with the social register to target direct aid](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>).
