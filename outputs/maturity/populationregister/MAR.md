# Morocco

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Times of India reports that Morocco has registered 22 million of its 39 million people in its national population register](<https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/times-techies/theyre-taking-dpi-to-the-world/articleshow/131849408.cms>). [ANSS says households must be enrolled in the Registre National de la Population to receive direct social aid (ASD)](<https://www.anss.gov.ma/fr/conditions-d-eligibilite>). [The Head of Government's department says 22.5 million people were enrolled in the Registre National de la Population as of mid-September 2025, with enrolment continuing](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/le-gouvernement-attache-la-mise-en-oeuvre-optimale-du-chantier-de-generalisation-de-la-protection>). [The World Bank project description says it supports the national rollout of the National Population Registry (RNP) as a trusted source of identity with digital authentication](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=44000-P504398>).
