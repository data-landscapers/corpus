# Seychelles

## Population register

**Register in service.** [A March 2025 tender for a new Land Valuation System says it is to link to the existing National Population Database](<https://www.ntb.sc/2-stage-tender/consultancy-2-stage/dict-development-of-the-land-valuation-system-lvs>). The National Tender Board treats the database as a system already running. [The Civil Status Division keeps the database](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>), within the Department of Immigration and Civil Status. It says it works to keep it up to date.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the database. The nearest held fact is that [the division that registers civil status holds it](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).

**Source for others.** No source found says another register takes its records from the database yet. [The Land Valuation System is to link to it](<https://www.ntb.sc/2-stage-tender/consultancy-2-stage/dict-development-of-the-land-valuation-system-lvs>), under a tender of March 2025.
