**Register in service.** [In October 2026 Liberia's Senate sent President Boakai's proposed US$52 million concession with OeSD International GmbH to committee](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/16/liberian-senate-orders-review-of-us52-million-digital-identity-concession-agreement/>). [The concession is for a National Identification Central Digital Civil Registry and Vital Statistics System](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/16/liberian-senate-orders-review-of-us52-million-digital-identity-concession-agreement/>). [It would hold a single national database of identity records](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/16/liberian-senate-orders-review-of-us52-million-digital-identity-concession-agreement/>). [It awaits a ratification vote](<https://liberiapublicradio.com/2026/10/16/liberian-senate-orders-review-of-us52-million-digital-identity-concession-agreement/>).
