# Lesotho

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The Principal Secretary of Home Affairs said in May 2026 that the ministry alone maintains the National Population Register](<https://www.gov.ls/elections/the-iec-has-launched-its-civic-and-voter-education-strategy-and-voter-registration-programme/>). The ministry also issues legal identity documents. She said the electoral commission's plans for the voters' roll depend on the ministry keeping that register updated, accurate and accessible.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the register. [In July 2022 the Health Minister said children registered at birth in hospitals would all be in the National Population Database](<https://www.homeaffairs.gov.ls/lekala-la-tsa-lehae-le-khakola-ngoliso-ea-litlhaho-le-mafu-sepetleleng-sa-st-josephs-roma/>). The civil registry then registered births and deaths at St Joseph's Roma and three other hospitals.

**Source for others.** No source found since October 2023 says another register or service takes records from the population register. [In August 2020 the Finance Ministry's pension payments in Qacha's Nek relied on Home Affairs checking pensioners against its identity and civil registry database](<https://www.homeaffairs.gov.ls/ministry-of-home-affairs-database-eases-service-delivery-for-the-ministry-of-finance-department-of-pensions/>). [The national identity document is to become the only document needed to register as a voter](<https://www.gov.ls/elections/the-iec-has-launched-its-civic-and-voter-education-strategy-and-voter-registration-programme/>).
