# Mauritania

## Population register

**Register in service.** In June 2026 [the interior minister told the National Assembly the register had passed 5.1 million citizens](<https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/>). It held 3.4 million in 2019, and enrolment is now a permanent public service. [A magistrates' club official refers to civil-status facts recorded in the register](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/267622>). [The government procedures portal sets out a standing procedure to enrol a child in the register](<https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The government procedures portal, updated on 21 November 2023, sets out how a child born to Mauritanian parents is enrolled in the register](<https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105>). Enrolment is free at citizen reception centres. In March 2025 [a magistrates' club official referred to civil-status facts recorded in the population register](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/267622>).
