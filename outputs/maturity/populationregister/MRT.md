# Mauritania

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The interior minister told the National Assembly that the Registre national de la population grew from 3.4 million registered citizens in 2019 to over 5.1 million, with enrolment now a permanent public service](<https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/>). [A magistrates' club official refers to civil-status facts recorded in the population register, which the national population register and secure documents agency runs](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/267622>). [The government procedures portal (last updated 2023-11-21) describes a standing procedure to enrol a child born to Mauritanian parents in the population register (national biometric register) run by the ANRPTS, free of charge at citizen reception centres](<https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105>).

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the population register.
