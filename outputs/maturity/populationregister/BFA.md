# Burkina Faso

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Law 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 provides for a national register of the unique identifier](<https://assembleenationale.bf/loip/117>). It is to centralise people's biographic and biometric data. A decree is to create the body in charge, and enrolment follows the system's set-up. It is the only fact held on the subject.
