# Burkina Faso

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Law 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 provides for a national register of the unique identifier](<https://assembleenationale.bf/loip/117>). It centralises people's biographic and biometric data, under a body to be created by decree. [On 11 November 2026 Burkina Faso launched mass enrolment of residents into the register](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150706=>), leFaso.net reports. A pilot had enrolled over 16,000 people. Enrolment takes all ten fingerprints and the irises, and each record is checked for duplicates. A first count of those enrolled is due at the end of December 2026.

**Updated from vital events.** No source found since October 2023 says births or deaths are entered in the register. The nearest held fact: [mass enrolment into it began in November 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150706=>).

**Source for others.** No source found since October 2023 says other systems take records from the register. The nearest held fact: [the law that provides for it](<https://assembleenationale.bf/loip/117>).
