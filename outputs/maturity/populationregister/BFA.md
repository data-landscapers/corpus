# Burkina Faso

## Population register

**Register in service.** [Law 027-2024/ALT of 11 October 2024 provides for a national register of the unique identifier of natural persons, centralising their biographic and biometric data, with the body in charge to be created by decree and enrolment to follow the system's set-up](<https://assembleenationale.bf/loip/117>).
