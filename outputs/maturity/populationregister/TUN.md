# Tunisia

## Population register

**Register in service.** In May 2026 [a playbook described the citizen identifier register](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>), held under the ministry for local affairs. It gives an identifier to each person entered in the civil register at birth, to citizens abroad and to naturalised persons. The citizen portal gives access to it. On 16 July 2025 [the INS and the Interior Ministry held a workshop on a unified population register](<https://tunisia.unfpa.org/fr/news/r%C3%A9vision-de-la-l%C3%A9gislation-et-de-la-r%C3%A9glementation-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-pour-la>). It prepared the legal reform.

**Updated from vital events.** [The playbook shows the register built and updated from the Madania civil-status database by API](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). It holds references to birth and death acts, with the date and place of death.

**Source for others.** [The playbook calls the register the pivot for sectoral identifiers](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). Its data pass to state bodies through the national interoperability platform.
