# Tunisia

## Population register

**Register in service.** [The playbook describes the citizen identifier register, held under the ministry in charge of local affairs, giving an IUC to each person entered in the civil register at birth, to citizens registered abroad and to naturalised persons, and the citizen portal offers access to the national citizen register](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [UNFPA reports a national workshop on 16 July 2025, organised by the INS with the Ministry of the Interior, to prepare legal reform for establishing a unified national population register in Tunisia](<https://tunisia.unfpa.org/fr/news/r%C3%A9vision-de-la-l%C3%A9gislation-et-de-la-r%C3%A9glementation-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-pour-la>).

**Updated from vital events.** [The playbook shows the citizen identifier register initialised and updated from the Madania civil status database by API and holding birth-act and death-act references, the date and place of death and civil status](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Source for others.** [The playbook says the register is the pivot for sectoral identifiers and that its data are exchanged with state structures through the national interoperability platform](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
