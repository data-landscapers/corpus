# Malawi

## Off-site backup for government systems

**A backup site 200 km or more away in service, and for the shared government systems.** [The IDEA progress report for January-June 2026 says resilient telecom links with the disaster recovery site in Blantyre were commissioned for the Government national data centre in Lilongwe, and a redundancy link between the Lilongwe and Blantyre data centres was completed; main site in Lilongwe, backup in Blantyre](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>). These place a backup site 200 km or more from the main one in service for the government's shared systems.

**A recovery plan or test on record.** No source held from the past three years states a recovery plan or a failover test for [the backup site](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>).

**Primary and backup both Tier III.** No source held says both the main site and [the backup site](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>) are Tier III or above.

***Not held***. No source held from the past three years states a recovery plan or test for the government's backup site.
