# Off-site backup capacity: off-site backup for government systems

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The Smart Africa Data Center and Cloud Blueprint asks for 24/7 availability of e-government on a Tier-classified primary and secondary site, with disaster recovery and continuity management. This study asks whether a public body keeps a backup site, whether one serves the government's shared systems, whether its recovery is planned or tested, and whether both sites are built to Tier III; it does not measure capacity, recovery times or how many systems are covered.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says government systems have no off-site backup or disaster-recovery site |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: one public body's backup site in service, 200 km or more away |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 2: a backup site for the government's shared systems |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 3: a recovery plan or test on record for it |
| 5 Leading | Yes to 4: main and backup sites both Tier III or above |

## Countries

### Malawi: 3 Established

A backup site 200 km away serves the government's shared systems by 2026; no recovery plan or test held.

- The IDEA progress report for January-June 2026 says resilient telecom links with the disaster recovery site in Blantyre were commissioned for the Government national data centre in Lilongwe, and a redundancy link between the Lilongwe and Blantyre data centres was completed; main site in Lilongwe, backup in Blantyre. The date of the DR site entering service is not stated; the report's period is used ([source, 2026-06](https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf)).

### Sudan: 2 Nascent

One public body keeps a backup site 200 km away by 2026; none found for the government's shared systems.

- Sudan's General Traffic Police director says the police restored the full vehicle database and set up backup data centres inside the states and outside the country to secure the national vehicle register; main site place not stated, backup places not stated (states inside Sudan and an unnamed foreign country) ([source, 2026-07-20](https://sudantribune.net/article/316412)).

### Sierra Leone: 1 Absent

A 2026 source says government systems have no off-site backup or disaster-recovery site.

- Ministry of Finance officials confirmed on 7 January 2026 that no comprehensive disaster-recovery system is yet in place for the national e-GP platform hosted in the Ministry's data centre; disaster recovery is planned for Phase Two (Ministry framework or cloud). Main site place not stated; no backup site ([source, 2026-01-07](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nppa-inspects-e-gp-data-center-finance/)).
