# Sierra Leone

## Off-site backup for government systems

**A backup site 200 km or more away in service, and for the shared government systems.** [Ministry of Finance officials confirmed on 7 January 2026 that no comprehensive disaster-recovery system is yet in place for the national e-GP platform hosted in the Ministry's data centre; disaster recovery is planned for Phase Two (Ministry framework or cloud); Main site place not stated; no backup site](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nppa-inspects-e-gp-data-center-finance/>). A dated statement that government systems have no off-site backup sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one.

**A recovery plan or test on record.** No source found from the past three years states a recovery plan or a failover test for [the backup site](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nppa-inspects-e-gp-data-center-finance/>).

**Primary and backup both Tier III.** No source found says both the main site and [the backup site](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nppa-inspects-e-gp-data-center-finance/>) are Tier III or above.
