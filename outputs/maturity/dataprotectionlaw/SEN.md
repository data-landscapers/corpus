# Senegal

## General law

**Authority.** The Commission de Protection des Données Personnelles (CDP) is constituted: [it held two plenary sessions and issued decisions in April to June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). The law [set it up as an independent administrative authority](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>), and [its president, Ousmane Thiongane, signed a partnership convention with Benin's authority in Cotonou, reported on 17 April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-benin-et-le-senegal-scellent-une-alliance-strategique-a-cotonou>).

**Standing.** [Law no. 2008-12 on the protection of personal data was enacted on 25 January 2008 and has been in force since 2014](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Senegal-Factsheet.pdf>). On 27 March 2026 [an expert told APS it is the current law](<https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/>), and [the CDP issued receipts, authorisations and a rejection under it in April to June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>).

**Regulations.** A workshop [reported in July 2026 worked to finalise a draft data protection law and its implementing decree](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>).

**Application.** For April to June 2026 the [CDP reported 195 files handled, 153 declaration receipts and 39 authorisations issued, one processing rejected, and eight complaints and one report received and handled](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). Its plenary [served a formal notice on SOLID SA, operator of the Kay Voter platform, for unsolicited prospecting by SMS, reported on 20 April 2024](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The CDP [relaunched the updating of the law at a two-day workshop at Saly, reported in July 2026, with the ministry committed to finalising the draft law and decree](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). In March 2026 [an expert said a bill was reported to be in the circuit and that the National Assembly must vote it](<https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/>).
