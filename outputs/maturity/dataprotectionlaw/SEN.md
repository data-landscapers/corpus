# Senegal

## General law

**Authority.** The Commission de Protection des Données Personnelles (CDP) is constituted. [It held two plenary sessions and issued decisions in April to June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). The law [set it up as an independent administrative authority](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>). [Its president, Ousmane Thiongane, signed a partnership convention with Benin's authority in Cotonou](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-benin-et-le-senegal-scellent-une-alliance-strategique-a-cotonou>). The signing was reported on 17 April 2026.

**Standing.** [Law no. 2008-12 on the protection of personal data was enacted on 25 January 2008](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Senegal-Factsheet.pdf>). It has been in force since 2014. On 27 March 2026 [an expert told APS it is the current law](<https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/>). [The CDP issued receipts, authorisations and a rejection under it in April to June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>).

**Regulations.** A workshop [reported in July 2026 worked to finalise a draft data protection law and its implementing decree](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>).

**Application.** For July to September 2026 the [CDP reported 214 files handled](<https://aps.sn/la-cdp-mise-sur-la-formation-et-la-sensibilisation-pour-promouvoir-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles/>): 139 declarations and 75 authorisation requests. It examined 13 complaints and carried out five inspections. For April to June 2026 it [reported 195 files handled](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/donnees-personnelles-plus-de-190-dossiers-traites-par-la-cdp-entre-avril-et-juin_n_503047.html>). In 2024 it [served a formal notice on SOLID SA over unsolicited SMS](<https://senego.com/la-plateforme-kay-voter-mise-en-demeure-par-la-cdp-pour-des-sms-non-sollicites-sur-le-report-de-lelection_1688747.html>). In October 2026 [it said the state's CCTV for the Youth Olympic Games had its approval](<https://aps.sn/joj-le-dispositif-de-videosurveillance-mis-en-place-par-letat-est-conforme-a-la-loi-cdp/>).

**Last twelve months.** The CDP [relaunched the updating of the law at a two-day workshop at Saly, reported in July 2026](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). The ministry committed to finalising the draft law and decree. In March 2026 [an expert said a bill was reported to be in the circuit](<https://aps.sn/donnees-personnelles-la-legislation-senegalaise-doit-etre-modernisee-et-adaptee-aux-enjeux-de-lia-expert/>). The expert said the National Assembly must vote it.
