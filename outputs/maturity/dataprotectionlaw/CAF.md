# Central African Republic

## General law

**Authority.** [Loi n° 24.001 provides for a personal data protection agency](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>). It gives the ministry in charge of the digital economy twelve months from promulgation to set it up. That ministry holds the agency's missions meanwhile. [Data Protection Africa stated on 28 January 2026 that the authority had not yet been appointed](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>).

**Standing.** [Loi n° 24.001 on the protection of personal data was adopted in mid-January 2024 and promulgated](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>). Its text gives no commencement date and no compliance period. [Data Protection Africa stated on 28 January 2026 that the country has a data protection law in place](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>). [The World Bank recorded in December 2024 that the law was adopted in January 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>).

**Regulations.** [The World Bank reported in December 2024 that activities were planned to support drafting of the law's implementing decrees](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122024184018568/txt/P1746201172f3b05d1a9d118f1eb4b91506.txt>). [The law's text names no composition, seat or financing for the agency](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>).

**Application.** [Until the agency is set up, its missions rest with the ministry in charge of the digital economy](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Loi_24_001_portant_protection_des_donnes_a_caractere__personnel.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 28 January 2026 Data Protection Africa recorded the authority as not yet appointed](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under the law, and no decision, sanction or count of decisions, has been found. The date the law came into force and any appointment to the agency are not held.
