# Gabon

## General law

**Authority.** Article 7 of Loi n°025/2023 [creates the APDPVP as an independent administrative authority](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>). Its commissioners were [appointed on 12 July 2023, and its bureau was elected on 6 September 2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>). Its president, Joël Dominique Ledaga, [gave the authority's 2025 review on 27 January 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/journee-mondiale-de-la-protection-des-donnees-le-gabon-affirme-sa-strategie-face-aux-defis-du-numerique/>).

**Standing.** Gabon's data protection law is Loi n°001/2011, [amended by ordinance in 2020](<https://journal-officiel.ga/4803-00002-pr->). [Loi n°025/2023 of 12 July 2023 amended it again](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>), with no grace period for compliance. The authority [applied it as law in force in April 2024](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>).

**Regulations.** Loi n°025/2023 was [promulgated by Décret n°166/PR](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>).

**Application.** [The authority's 2025 review counts 12 administrative and pecuniary sanctions](<https://www.gabonreview.com/journee-mondiale-de-la-protection-des-donnees-le-gabon-affirme-sa-strategie-face-aux-defis-du-numerique/>), 178 declarations of conformity and 16 opinions on draft texts. On 25 April 2024 its plenary [fined Citibank Gabon S.A. 90,000,000 FCFA](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>). [The bank had diverted employee data from its declared purpose and processed data on ethnic origin](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>). In December 2025 its president [demanded the suspension of data sharing between three state agencies](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>), launched without referral to it.

**Last twelve months.** On 20 January 2026 the authority [supervised the destruction of the personal data of unsuccessful 2022 bidders](<https://www.gabonreview.com/marches-publics-les-donnees-personnelles-des-soumissionnaires-de-2022-officiellement-detruites/>). It [accompanied a data centre project for several months before commissioning](<https://www.gabonreview.com/hebergement-des-donnees-le-gabon-met-fin-a-sa-dependance-aux-serveurs-etrangers-avec-son-premier-datacenter/>), reported in June 2026. In September 2026 its president [counted about 715 declarations of conformity, 50 reasoned opinions and 30 complaints](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).
