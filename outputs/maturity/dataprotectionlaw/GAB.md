# Gabon

## General law

**Authority.** The Autorité pour la Protection des Données Personnelles et de la Vie Privée (APDPVP) is [created by article 7 of Loi n°025/2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>) as an independent administrative authority. Its commissioners were [appointed by a Council of Ministers decision of 12 July 2023 and its bureau was elected on 6 September 2023](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>). Its president [gave an interview as head of the authority in September 2026](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).

**Standing.** Gabon's data protection law is Loi n°001/2011, [amended by ordinance in 2020](<https://journal-officiel.ga/4803-00002-pr->) and again by [Loi n°025/2023 of 12 July 2023, published in the Journal Officiel on 15 July 2023 with no grace period for compliance](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>). The authority [applied it as law in force in April 2024](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>).

**Regulations.** Loi n°025/2023 was [promulgated by Décret n°166/PR](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Gabon-Loi-025-2023-du-12-juillet-2023-portant-modification-de-la-loi-001-2011-du-25-septembre-relative-a-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel.pdf>).

**Application.** On 25 April 2024 the authority's plenary [fined Citibank Gabon S.A. 90,000,000 FCFA](<https://www.apdpvp.ga/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SANCTION-CITIBANK-25-AVRIL-2024.pdf>) for diverting employee data from its declared purpose and processing data on ethnic origin, with a formal notice of two months. The decision answered a complaint of 27 November 2023.

**Last twelve months.** On 20 January 2026 the authority [supervised the destruction of the personal data of unsuccessful 2022 bidders](<https://www.gabonreview.com/marches-publics-les-donnees-personnelles-des-soumissionnaires-de-2022-officiellement-detruites/>) held by the public procurement directorate. It [accompanied a data centre project for several months before commissioning](<https://www.gabonreview.com/hebergement-des-donnees-le-gabon-met-fin-a-sa-dependance-aux-serveurs-etrangers-avec-son-premier-datacenter/>), reported in June 2026. Its president said in September 2026 that it had [logged about 715 declarations of conformity, issued about 50 reasoned opinions and received about 30 complaints from individuals](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/joel-dominique-ledaga-pour-echanger-directement-avec-meta-tiktok-ou-instagram-il-faut-une-reponse>).
