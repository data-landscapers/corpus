# Ghana

## General law

**Authority.** The Minister for Communication [swore in a new Governing Board, chaired by Teki Akuetteh](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/>), reported on 1 August 2025. In October 2026 the Commission was [conducting compliance checks and enforcement under the Act](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-flags-six-companies-for-data-protection-breaches/>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) is in force: a circuit court [treated its sections 17 to 30 and 43 as binding on a data processor](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>) on 18 September 2024, and in February 2026 the Minister for Communication [called on all institutions that process personal data to comply with it](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4137/minister-for-communication-stresses-data-protection-as-foundation-of-ghanas-digital-transformation>).

**Regulations.** [Amendments to the Act are in process and a draft Data Protection Act, 2025 has been published](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), according to a roundup by Digital Policy Alert and Tech Hive Advisory of 11 January 2026.

**Application.** On 18 September 2024 a circuit court in Accra [found Bolt Holdings OU in breach of sections 20, 21, 28 and 30 of the Act](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>) and awarded GHC1,900,000 in compensation and GHC20,000 in costs. On 14 October 2026 the Commission [cited six named companies for operating unregistered or with expired certificates](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-flags-six-companies-for-data-protection-breaches/>) and directed them to report to the CID by 15 October, after [inspecting a clinic and two hospitals on 12 October](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghanaian-clinic-stored-patient-records-on-an-unassessed-system-before-regulator-intervened/>).

**Last twelve months.** In January 2026 the Commission [announced that 2026 would be a year of enforcement](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-to-crack-down-on-breaches-as-2026-marked-for-strict-enforcement/>). On 12 October 2026 it [directed Nyaho Medical Centre to report to the police CID](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/nyaho-medical-centre-referred-to-cid-over-allegedly-expired-data-protection-registration/>) over an expired registration certificate. On 20 October 2026 it [warned a ministry and media over disclosing evacuees' personal data](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/dpc-warns-against-disclosure-of-evacuated-ghanaians-personal-data/>).

***Also on file***. [Electronic Communications Act 2025 amendments](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>); [National Identity Register Regulations, 2012 (L.I. 2111), as amended 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/photocopying-ghana-card-now-attracts-fines-of-up-to-ghc24-000.html>); [National Data Sharing Policy](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/Ghana-AI-Strategy-2025-2035.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation made under the Act has been found, and no source says there is none. No published count of decisions, sanctions or complaints settled in a stated year has been found; the 2026 figures given are registrations and training.
