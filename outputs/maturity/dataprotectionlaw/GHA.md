# Ghana

## General law

**Authority.** The Data Protection Commission is [established under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and enforces it](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/data-protection-commission-acquires-vehicles-to-boost-nationwide-enforcement/>). The Minister for Communication [swore in a new Governing Board, chaired by Teki Akuetteh](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/01/ghana-takes-major-step-towards-secure-digital-future-with-new-data-protection-board/>), reported on 1 August 2025. In August 2026 the Commission [operated only from its head office](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/08/data-protection-commission-acquires-vehicles-to-boost-nationwide-enforcement/>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) is in force: a circuit court [treated its sections 17 to 30 and 43 as binding on a data processor](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>) on 18 September 2024, and in February 2026 the Minister for Communication [called on all institutions that process personal data to comply with it](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4137/minister-for-communication-stresses-data-protection-as-foundation-of-ghanas-digital-transformation>).

**Regulations.** [Amendments to the Act are in process and a draft Data Protection Act, 2025 has been published](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/blog/data-protection-in-africa-roundup>), according to a roundup by Digital Policy Alert and Tech Hive Advisory of 11 January 2026.

**Application.** On 18 September 2024 Circuit Court 2, Adentan, Accra [found Bolt Holdings OU in breach of sections 20, 21, 28 and 30 of the Act](<https://superlawgh.com/judgements/justice-noah-adade-vrs-bolt-ghana-limited/>) over a driver profile registered with the plaintiff's stolen identity, and awarded GHC1,900,000 in compensation and GHC20,000 in costs. The Commission reported that in 2025 it [conducted compliance audits across several sectors and expanded registrations of data controllers](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/deputy-communications-minister-calls-for-stronger-institutional-collaboration-on-data-protection/>).

**Last twelve months.** In January 2026 the Commission [announced that 2026 would be a year of enforcement](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/data-protection-commission-to-crack-down-on-breaches-as-2026-marked-for-strict-enforcement/>). The Minister reported that in January to June 2026 [its regulatory activity rose 51.5 per cent and newly registered data controllers rose 98.4 per cent](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>).
