# Egypt

## General law

**Authority.** The supervisory authority is the Personal Data Protection Center, which [the executive regulations confirm as responsible for implementation, monitoring and enforcement](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>). In November 2025 it was [run by an acting chief executive, Soussan El-Okbawy](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5335073.aspx>), and in early October 2026 it [launched an electronic portal for accreditation, licence and permit applications](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).

**Standing.** Egypt's general law is the Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. 151 of 2020, which the Cairo Review [treated in June 2025 as the country's operating data protection law](<https://www.thecairoreview.com/essays/regulating-privacy-and-digital-identities-in-the-age-of-ai-the-view-from-egypt>). Shura Law Firm said on 13 October 2026 that it [entered into force in October 2020, with a transitional period under the executive regulations expiring on 31 October 2026](<https://shuralawfirm.com/en/egypt-pdpl-compliance-checklist-what-to-do-before-the-october-31-2026-deadline/>).

**Regulations.** The executive regulations were [still pending in February 2025](<https://shehatalaw.com/law-update/legal-alert-egyptian-court-judgment-tightens-enforcement-of-privacy-and-data-protection-laws>). They were [issued by ministerial decree on 1 November 2025 as Executive Regulations No. 816/2025](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>).

**Application.** In January and February 2026 the Personal Data Protection Center [published guidelines on consent, lawful bases, direct marketing, data protection officers, records of processing, licences and permits and privacy notices, with templates for notifying a breach](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>). Its portal [also publishes guidelines and frameworks for controllers and processors](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).

**Last twelve months.** The executive regulations [were issued on 1 November 2025](<https://accesspartnership.com/opinion/egypt-finalises-executive-regulations-to-the-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl>), and the Center's [guidelines followed in January and February 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>). It [launched its licensing and permit portal in early October 2026](<https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/10/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-update>).
