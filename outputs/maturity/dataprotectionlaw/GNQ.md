# Equatorial Guinea

## General law

**Authority.** [Articles 15 and 29 of the law](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>) name the Órgano Rector de Protección de Datos Personales as the supervisory body, with a Secretaría Técnica and a Registro General under it. The law states that the body is to be created by a decree that will fix its structure.

**Standing.** [Ley Núm. 1/2016, de Protección de Datos Personales](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>), was sanctioned and promulgated by the President on 22 July 2016. It sets rules for the processing of personal data in the public and private sectors, and enters into force twenty days after its publication in the Boletín Oficial del Estado.

**Regulations.** The law leaves [the structure of the Órgano Rector to a decree](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>).

**Application.** The law places [the power to sanction with the Minister of Telecommunications and New Technologies](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The law in force is [the one dated 22 July 2016](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Guinee-equatoriale-ley-1-2.016-de-proteccion-de-datos.pdf>).
