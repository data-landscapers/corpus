# Djibouti

## General law

**Authority.** The Digital Code's data-protection regime [creates the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data (CNDP)](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>). The commission is independent, with administrative and financial autonomy. Jones Day reported at the National Assembly's vote that the [competent authorities were still to be operationalised](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>).

**Standing.** The National Assembly [passed Djibouti's first Digital Code on 30 June 2025](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>), with a data-protection regime embedded in it. The Code was to be promulgated by the President and published in the Journal Officiel. TechHive Advisory states that the regime was [enacted in June 2025](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>).

**Regulations.** The data-protection regime [runs to 156 articles and sits within the Digital Code](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>).

**Application.** The Code's embedded data-protection regime [establishes the CNDP](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>), as reported at the Assembly's vote on 30 June 2025.

**Last twelve months.** TechHive Advisory, writing on 5 November 2025, [described the regime as enacted in June 2025](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-djiboutis-data-protection-law>). It described the commission as created by the regime.
