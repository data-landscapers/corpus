# Chad

## General law

**Authority.** The digital law that Chad's government is preparing is to [incorporate international standards on cybersecurity, data protection and internet governance](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>), as Minister Boukar Michel announced on 9 December 2025.

**Standing.** Chad is drafting a Digital Code, which the country calls the Code du Numérique. The minister said on 9 December 2025 that [authorities were finalising the law](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>), and a report published on 16 March 2026 described the code as [under development since December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>).

**Regulations.** The code that would carry the data protection provisions was [under development](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) in the report published on 16 March 2026.

**Application.** The Digital Code is to [incorporate international standards on data protection](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>), according to the report published on 16 March 2026, which described it as under development.

**Last twelve months.** The [announcement of the law](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>) came on 9 December 2025, and the [report that the Digital Code was under development](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>) followed on 16 March 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. A law on the collection, retention, use and destruction of biometric data in judicial proceedings, [adopted by the National Assembly on 10 April 2026](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/>) and [by the Senate on 28 April 2026](<https://atpe.td/donnees-biometriques-les-senateurs-adoptent-un-projet-de-loi/>); and the [law reforming ANSICE, ratified by the Senate on 13 March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>).

***Not held***. Whether a body supervises data protection under a general law, whether any regulations exist and whether any decision has been made are not held. No dated event shows the Digital Code advancing or stalling.
