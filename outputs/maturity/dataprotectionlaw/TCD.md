# Chad

## General law

**Authority.** A law firm's analysis of July 2026 says that under Loi n°007/PR/2015, transfers of personal data abroad need [notification to or prior authorisation from ANSICE](<https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/>). ANSICE is the National Agency for Information Security and Electronic Certification. The analysis adds that the agency's operational effectiveness is not documented.

**Standing.** A [July 2026 analysis by the Cabinet Thomas Dingamgoto](<https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/>) applies Loi n°007/PR/2015 to personal data. It treats the law's articles 29 to 32 as the transfer rules in force. A replacement is being prepared. On 9 December 2025 the minister said [authorities were finalising a digital law](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>) carrying international data protection standards. A report of 16 March 2026 described the Digital Code as [under development since December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>).

**Regulations.** The nearest fact found is the [July 2026 analysis](<https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/>). It reads the rules for sending personal data abroad from articles 29 to 32 of the 2015 law itself.

**Application.** The same analysis says the law makes transfers abroad subject to [notification to ANSICE or its prior authorisation](<https://thomasd-avocats.com/transfert-international-rapport-enquete-tchad-rgpd/>). It says how far the agency acts on this is not documented.

**Last twelve months.** On 27 April 2026 the minister [installed a committee of 34 members](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-la-refonte-du-cadre-juridique-du-numerique-pour-encadrer-lia-et-renforcer-la-souverainete-digitale/>) with 45 days to propose modernised digital texts. One subject is personal data protection, which the minister called inadequately covered by the current rules.

***Also on file***. The National Assembly [adopted a law on the collection, retention, use and destruction of biometric data in judicial proceedings on 10 April 2026](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/04/11/tchad-les-deputes-adoptent-a-une-large-majorite-la-loi-encadrant-les-donnees-biometriques-judiciaires/>). The Senate [adopted it on 28 April 2026](<https://atpe.td/donnees-biometriques-les-senateurs-adoptent-un-projet-de-loi/>). The [law reforming ANSICE was ratified by the Senate on 13 March 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1603-53795-chad-approves-reform-strengthening-national-cybersecurity-agency>).

***Not found***. No source has been found on whether a body is constituted to supervise data protection. Nor has any regulation made under the 2015 law been found, or any decision taken under it.
