# Eritrea

## General law

**Authority.** [No supervisory authority has been established](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>) in Eritrea, as of 1 August 2026. The Horn of Africa Initiative's update of June 2025 [recorded the country as without regulatory oversight for data protection](<https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf>), while Kenya, Somalia and Ethiopia had supervisory authorities.

**Standing.** Eritrea has [no comprehensive data protection law in force](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>), as of 1 August 2026. The Horn of Africa Initiative's update of June 2025 [placed it at a nascent stage, without data protection legislation](<https://hoainitiative.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/25th%20HoAI%20Ministerial%20-%20Background%20Paper%20on%20Digital%20Integration%20Update.pdf>), and a UNECA country profile published in 2022 [said it lacked a comprehensive legal framework for data protection](<https://dtri.uneca.org/v1/uploads/country-profile/eri-country-profile-en.pdf>).

**Regulations.** [No comprehensive data protection law is in force](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>), as of 1 August 2026.

**Application.** [No enforcement decisions are published and none could be found](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>), as of 1 August 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The position [was unchanged at 1 August 2026](<https://research.lawlab.africa/eritrea/>): no law, no regulator and no enforcement.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No bill or draft of a data protection law is on record, and whether one is in preparation is not held. Nothing is held on regulations.
