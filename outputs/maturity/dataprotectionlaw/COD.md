# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## General law

**Authority.** [Article 262 of the Digital Code creates an independent Autorité de protection des données](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>) with legal personality and its own budget. [A ministerial order of 17 August 2024 provisionally transferred its missions to the telecommunications regulator, ARPTIC](<https://droitnumerique.cd/arptic-arptc-investie-des-missions-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-en-rd-congo/>). In March 2026 the Minister of Digital Economy [described the authority's creation as forthcoming](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>).

**Standing.** [Ordonnance-loi n° 23/010 of 13 March 2023, the Digital Code, sets the rules on the protection of personal data in its Book III](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>). It [entered into force in 2023](<https://droitnumerique.cd/arptic-arptc-investie-des-missions-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-en-rd-congo/>).

**Regulations.** The Code provides that [a Prime Minister's decree will fix the authority's organisation and functioning](<https://are.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/04042023-ORDONNANCE-LOI-23-010-DU-13-MARS-PORTANT-CODE-DU-NUMERIQUE_compressed.pdf>). On 6 August 2024 the think tank Law & Technologies [called on the government to draft and sign it](<https://7sur7.cd/2024/08/07/rdc-le-gouvernement-appele-operationnaliser-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees>).

**Application.** On 12 March 2026 the Ministry of Digital Economy [launched a national network of data protection officers in Kinshasa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/numerique-la-rdc-met-en-place-un-reseau-de-responsables-de-la-protection-des-donnees>), to standardise personal data practice and support compliance in the public and private sectors, backed by RUDI International and Droit-Numérique.cd.

**Last twelve months.** The network launched in March 2026 [was reported as part of plans to establish a dedicated Data Protection Authority](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/17/drc-launches-data-protection-officers-network-to-strengthen-digital-governance/>). On 20 April 2026 the co-author of a book on personal data protection [said the Code's Book III replaces the embryonic provisions of the 2020 telecommunications law](<https://actualite.cd/2026/04/21/rdc-un-nouvel-ouvrage-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-porte-sur-les-fonts>).
