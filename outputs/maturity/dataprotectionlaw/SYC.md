# Seychelles

## General law

**Authority.** The Act [names the Information Commission, established under the Access to Information Act, as the competent authority](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>), with powers to investigate, audit, issue enforcement notices and impose administrative fines of up to SCR200,000. The Commission [took on the mandate at the end of December 2023](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/ATIA-Updated-Whitepaper-10.11.2025.pdf>) and in 2025 [had a Chief Information Commissioner, two Information Commissioners and a chief executive in post](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>).

**Standing.** The [Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act 24 of 2023) was assented to on 19 December 2023 and bears the operative date 22 December 2023](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>); it repeals the former Data Protection Act. The Commission [administered it during 2025](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>).

**Regulations.** The Act itself [gave controllers and processors already processing data 18 months from commencement, to 22 June 2025, to conform](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2023-12/Act%2024%20-%202023%20-%20Data%20Protection%20Act%202023.pdf>).

**Application.** The Commission [received four data protection cases in 2024](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Information-Commission-Annual-Report-2024.pdf>), mostly from the non-banking financial sector, and [14 in 2025: six on data transfers, five breaches, two refused deletion requests and one consultation](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>). It handled the 2025 cases by guidance, assessment and corrective advice.

**Last twelve months.** In January 2026 the Commission [advertised a Senior Data Protection Analyst post](<https://www.infocom.sc/data-protection-day-2026-building-trust-in-a-digital-seychelles/>). The National Assembly [lists the Commission's annual report for 2025 as published on 13 April 2026](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/publications/reports/information-commission-seychelles-annual-report-2025>). In that report the [Chief Commissioner states that the Act remains at risk of being largely unenforced because the one budgeted technical post is unfilled](<https://www.infocom.sc/wp-content/uploads/Annual-Report-2025.pdf>).
