# Libya

## General law

**Authority.** SMEX [said on 22 July 2026 that Libya has no functioning data protection authority](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). The same source places the country's data protection law still at the drafting stage, so there is no law for an authority to administer.

**Standing.** A committee formed under National Council for Public Freedoms and Human Rights decision 253 of 2023 was [drafting a national personal data protection law in January 2024](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2024/01/24/the-committee-responsible-for-implementing-the-national-project-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-holds-its-regular-meeting-at-the-headquarters-of-the-general-information-authority/>), according to the General Information Authority. SMEX [reported on 22 July 2026 that the Data Protection and Privacy Law remains in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), with adoption targeted for 2026.

**Regulations.** No regulations have been found. The nearest fact is the committee that was [drafting the national personal data protection law in January 2024](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2024/01/24/the-committee-responsible-for-implementing-the-national-project-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-holds-its-regular-meeting-at-the-headquarters-of-the-general-information-authority/>), which comes before any regulations could be issued.

**Application.** No application of a data protection law has been found. SMEX [reported in July 2026 that the law remains in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), so no decision under it can exist yet.

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 a working group led by the Annir Initiative with Internet Society Libya [published an informal draft Personal Data Protection Law for public consultation](<https://annir.ly/assets/dpp-ly/personal-data-protection-law-(draft).pdf>), marked unofficial and not a government bill. In July 2026 SMEX [reported the official law still in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source says a government bill has been tabled or when one will be. No regulations or decisions under a law are on record.
