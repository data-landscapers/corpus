# Libya

## General law

**Authority.** SMEX [said on 22 July 2026 that Libya has no functioning data protection authority](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). The same source places the country's data protection law still at the drafting stage, so there is no law for an authority to administer.

**Standing.** In January 2024 the General Information Authority reported a committee [drafting a national personal data protection law](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2024/01/24/the-committee-responsible-for-implementing-the-national-project-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-holds-its-regular-meeting-at-the-headquarters-of-the-general-information-authority/>). The committee was formed under a 2023 decision of the National Council for Public Freedoms and Human Rights. SMEX [reported on 22 July 2026 that the law remains in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>), with adoption targeted for 2026.

**Regulations.** None has been found. A committee was still [drafting the law itself in January 2024](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2024/01/24/the-committee-responsible-for-implementing-the-national-project-for-the-protection-of-personal-data-holds-its-regular-meeting-at-the-headquarters-of-the-general-information-authority/>).

**Application.** None has been found. SMEX [reported in July 2026 that the law remains in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>).

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 a working group led by the Annir Initiative with Internet Society Libya [published an informal draft law for consultation](<https://annir.ly/assets/dpp-ly/personal-data-protection-law-(draft).pdf>). It is marked unofficial and is not a government bill. In July 2026 SMEX [reported the official law still in draft](<https://smex.org/libyas-new-ai-strategy-celebrates-participation-yet-was-built-without-it/>). On 26 October 2026 the National Council and a House of Representatives committee [began a joint review of the council's draft](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=368592&lang=ar>). The draft had gone to the House as an initial proposal.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source says a government bill has been tabled or when one will be. No regulations or decisions under a law are on record.
