# Mozambique

## General law

**Authority.** No data protection authority exists. The Data Protection Office was [not yet established in October 2025](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>), and a June 2025 report found [no data regulatory authority](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). The draft law reviewed in September 2025 [gives the role to the existing Regulatory Authority for ICT](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-mozambiques-proposed-personal-data-protection-law>).

**Standing.** Mozambique has a bill before parliament and no law. In April 2026 the ministry said the government had [approved the bill and sent it to the Assembly of the Republic](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_mr_mozambique_miquidade_en.pdf>). On 1 October 2026 the Assembly's Standing Commission [put it on the agenda of the session opening 28 October](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-convenes-extraordinary-and-ordinary-sessions-for-october/>). On 26 October it [set the plenary debate for 6 November 2026](<https://aimnews.org/2026/10/26/parlamento-alarga-calendario-de-trabalhos-para-responder-a-agenda-legislativa/>).

**Regulations.** A June 2025 report says Mozambique [has drafted a Data Protection Bill](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). It has not enacted a comprehensive law.

**Application.** A World Bank and African Development Bank diagnostic of spring 2025 [recommends adopting a data protection law](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099817512092543722/pdf/IDU-7812f059-6c08-413f-af64-c7a8611c88d2.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 the bill had [not been approved by Cabinet or sent to Parliament](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>). On 25 August 2026 the head of INTIC said the authorities were [preparing to submit it](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-calls-to-strengthen-data-protection-digital-space-legislation/>). A round table that month [discussed the proposed law](<https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/68263>).
