# Mozambique

## General law

**Authority.** No data protection authority exists. The Data Protection Office was [not yet established in October 2025](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>), and a June 2025 report found [no data regulatory authority](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). The draft law reviewed in September 2025 [gives the role to the existing Regulatory Authority for ICT](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-mozambiques-proposed-personal-data-protection-law>).

**Standing.** Mozambique has a bill before parliament and no law. The ministry told a UN commission in April 2026 that the government had [approved the proposed Personal Data Protection Law and submitted it to the Assembly of the Republic](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_mr_mozambique_miquidade_en.pdf>), and on 1 October 2026 the Assembly's Standing Commission [put the draft law on the agenda of its session opening 28 October](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-convenes-extraordinary-and-ordinary-sessions-for-october/>).

**Regulations.** A June 2025 inception report says Mozambique [has drafted a Data Protection Bill and has not enacted a comprehensive data protection law](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).

**Application.** A World Bank and African Development Bank diagnostic, with evidence to spring 2025, [recommends that the government adopt a data protection law](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099817512092543722/pdf/IDU-7812f059-6c08-413f-af64-c7a8611c88d2.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 the bill had [not been approved by Cabinet or submitted to Parliament](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099012626175535228>). On 5 December 2025 the president of INTIC said it would [go to Cabinet by June 2026](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-end-impunity-lack-of-state-authority-in-cybercrime-minister/>). A round table reported on 28 August 2026 [discussed the proposed law](<https://integritymagazine.co.mz/arquivos/68263>), and the INTIC president said on 25 August that the authorities were [preparing to submit the draft to parliament](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-calls-to-strengthen-data-protection-digital-space-legislation/>).
