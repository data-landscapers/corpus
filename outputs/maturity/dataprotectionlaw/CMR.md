# Cameroon

## General law

**Authority.** The law [defines the Autorité as an independent public body in charge of data protection](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) and gives it supervision, notification and enforcement functions.

**Standing.** Cameroon's general law is [Loi n° 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>). The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications [told the National Assembly in April 2025 that the country had adopted it](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>). An African Law & Business review of July 2025 describes [an 18-month grace period from enactment](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-cameroons-new-data-protection-law/>) before controllers and processors are bound. The Ministry of Finance's budget directorate states that [the deadline for its entry into force was reached on 23 June 2026 and that the law is now in force](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>).

**Regulations.** The law itself [sets the principles, the obligations of controllers and the rights of data subjects](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>), as the minister described it to the Assembly.

**Application.** The law has been [in force since 23 June 2026](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>), and enforcement under it [is a function of the Autorité](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>).

**Last twelve months.** The law entered into force on 23 June 2026, which [the budget directorate reported on 6 July 2026](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>).
