# Cameroon

## General law

**Authority.** The law [defines the Autorité as an independent public body in charge of data protection](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>) and gives it supervision, notification and enforcement functions. The OBS-CC observatory reported on 12 September 2026 that [the authority had not yet been installed](<https://obs-cc.org/suivi-recommandations-fncc/>).

**Standing.** Cameroon's general law is [Loi n° 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>). The Minister of Posts and Telecommunications [told the National Assembly in April 2025 that it had been adopted](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>). A July 2025 review describes [an 18-month grace period from enactment](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-cameroons-new-data-protection-law/>). The finance ministry's budget directorate states that [the law came into force on 23 June 2026](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>).

**Regulations.** The implementing decree called for by article 53 [had not been taken by 12 September 2026](<https://obs-cc.org/suivi-recommandations-fncc/>), the OBS-CC observatory reported. The law itself [sets principles, controller obligations and data subject rights](<https://leconomie.info/minette-libom-li-likeng-cest-en-construisant-un-numerique-au-service-de-tous-que-nous-poserons-les-fondations-dun-developpement-inclusif/>).

**Application.** The law has been [in force since 23 June 2026](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>). Enforcement under it [is a function of the Autorité](<https://prc.cm/fr/multimedia/documents/10258-loi-n-2024-017-du-23-12-2024-web>).

**Last twelve months.** The law entered into force on 23 June 2026, which [the budget directorate reported on 6 July 2026](<https://www.dgb.cm/la-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-entre-officiellement-en-vigueur/>).
