# Tunisia

## General law

**Authority.** The 2004 law [names the Instance nationale de protection des données à caractère personnel and gives it the powers to receive declarations and to authorise processing and transfers](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). [Government decree no. 2021-230 of 16 April 2021 appointed twelve members to it for three years](<https://legislation-securite.tn/ar/latest-laws/%D8%A3%D9%85%D8%B1-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-230-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2021-%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B1%D8%AE-%D9%81%D9%8A-16-%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%84-2021-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9/>): two members of parliament, two judges, ministry representatives, a human rights committee member and an ICT expert.

**Standing.** [Organic law no. 2004-63 of 27 July 2004 on the protection of personal data](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>) sets the general regime for processing, consent and rights, and was the operative statute in September 2026. A [draft organic law, no. 95/2025, was deposited before the Assembly of the Representatives of the People on 14 July 2025](<https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237>) and would repeal the 2004 law six months after publication.

**Regulations.** The law itself [has the authority receive declarations and authorise processing and transfers](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>).

**Application.** A representative of the authority [was scheduled to be heard by the Assembly's Rights and Freedoms Committee on 25 February 2026](<https://www.arp.tn/reunion/details/7644>), on the proposed new law.

**Last twelve months.** The Rights and Freedoms Committee [scheduled a hearing of the sponsoring deputies on 17 February 2026](<https://www.arp.tn/reunion/details/7625>) and [began examining the articles of the bill's first title that month](<https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/>), a text that [adds rules on artificial intelligence, surveillance cameras and health data](<https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/>). Tech Hive Advisory [lists Tunisia among countries moving to amend an existing law in January and February 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under organic law no. 2004-63 is held, and no dated sanction, decision or count of decisions under it. The three-year term of the members appointed in 2021 has run out and no later appointment has been found.
