# Tunisia

## General law

**Authority.** The 2004 law [names the Instance nationale de protection des données à caractère personnel](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). It receives declarations and authorises processing and transfers. [A decree of 16 April 2021 appointed twelve members to it for three years](<https://legislation-securite.tn/ar/latest-laws/%D8%A3%D9%85%D8%B1-%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%AF-230-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2021-%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B1%D8%AE-%D9%81%D9%8A-16-%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%84-2021-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9/>). In February 2026 a health ministry official [told the Assembly it has ceased its activities since 2024](<https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/protection-des-donnees-audition-strategique-a-larp-sur-la-future-loi-organique/393696/>).

**Standing.** [Organic law 2004-63 of 27 July 2004](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>) sets the rules for processing, consent and rights. It was still the law in force in September 2026. [A draft organic law, 95/2025, went before the Assembly on 14 July 2025](<https://www.arp.tn/loi/project/4237>). It would repeal the 2004 law six months after publication.

**Regulations.** The law itself [has the authority receive declarations and authorise processing and transfers](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2004-63-du-27-juillet-2004-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>).

**Application.** A representative of the authority [was due before the Assembly's Rights and Freedoms Committee on 25 February 2026](<https://www.arp.tn/reunion/details/7644>). The hearing was on the proposed new law.

**Last twelve months.** The Rights and Freedoms Committee [heard the bill's sponsors on 17 February 2026](<https://www.arp.tn/reunion/details/7625>). It [began examining the articles of the bill's first title that month](<https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/>). On 3 June 2026 it [was still examining the bill and sought written opinions from ministries and public bodies](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/parlement-la-concertation-au-coeur-du-debat-sur-la-loi-des-associations/>). The text [adds rules on artificial intelligence, surveillance cameras and health data](<https://en.africanmanager.com/personal-data-protection-tunisian-parliament-moves-to-curb-ai-and-algorithm-risks/>). Tech Hive Advisory [lists Tunisia among countries amending a law in early 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under the 2004 law, and no dated sanction, decision or count of decisions under it. The members appointed in 2021 served three years, and no later appointment has been found.
