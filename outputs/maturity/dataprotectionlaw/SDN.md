# Sudan

## General law

**Authority.** The draft law that would carry data protection is being prepared by [the Justice Ministry together with the digital transformation ministry](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>).

**Standing.** A data and artificial intelligence bill is in draft. On 6 August 2026 the Council of Ministers [discussed it and directed further study](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) before approval by the interim parliament, a joint sitting of the Sovereignty and Ministers councils. On 11 August 2026 the Justice Ministry's under-secretary [named it the 2026 Law on Regulating Data and Artificial Intelligence](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>), meant to provide the legislative framework, data protection included.

**Regulations.** The bill is one of [several draft bills from the digital transformation ministry](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) that the Council of Ministers discussed on 6 August 2026.

**Application.** The law is one of [three draft laws the Justice Ministry said on 11 August 2026 it is preparing](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>).

**Last twelve months.** Both steps fall in August 2026: the Council of Ministers' [direction of further study](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) on 6 August, and the Justice Ministry's [statement that the draft is in preparation](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>) on 11 August.
