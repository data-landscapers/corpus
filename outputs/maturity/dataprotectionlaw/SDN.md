# Sudan

## General law

**Authority.** No independent body oversees data protection, as [an analyst wrote in July 2026](<https://alakhbar-alsudan.com/?p=42822>). The draft law is being prepared by [the Justice Ministry and the digital transformation ministry](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>).

**Standing.** A data and artificial intelligence bill is in draft. On 6 August 2026 the Council of Ministers [discussed it and directed further study](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) before approval by the interim parliament, a joint sitting of the Sovereignty and Ministers councils. On 11 August 2026 the Justice Ministry's under-secretary [named it the 2026 Law on Regulating Data and Artificial Intelligence](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>), meant to provide the legislative framework, data protection included. The cabinet [approved the bill on 1 November 2026](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/11/10/%D8%A5%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A>); its text is unpublished.

**Regulations.** The bill is one of [several draft digital bills](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>). The Council of Ministers discussed them on 6 August 2026.

**Application.** The law is one of [three draft laws the Justice Ministry said on 11 August 2026 it is preparing](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>).

**Last twelve months.** The Council of Ministers [directed further study](<https://sudantribune.net/article/317108>) on 6 August 2026, and the Justice Ministry [said the draft was in preparation](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/08/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B1>) on 11 August. The cabinet [approved the bill on 1 November 2026](<https://www.sudanindependent.com/news/politics/2026/11/10/%D8%A5%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A>).

***Not found***. The bill's text is unpublished. No vote by the interim parliament is on record. No authority acting under a data protection law, no regulations and no decisions have been found.
