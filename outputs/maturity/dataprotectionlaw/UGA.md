# Uganda

## General law

**Authority.** The Personal Data Protection Office is [established under the Data Protection and Privacy Act and headed by a director, Baker Birikujja](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>). It [keeps a register of data collectors, controllers and processors](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap 97, is in force. The office's director said in August 2026 that it [requires data collectors and processors to register and makes failure to register a criminal offence](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>). The office has held that [the Act applies to foreign entities serving persons in Uganda](<https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf>).

**Regulations.** The office's decision of 20 February 2026 was [made under the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021](<https://adlegalug.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/PDPO-Decision-in-Ad-legal-International-Ltd-v-WhatsApp-LLC-Meta-Platforms-Inc.pdf>). Its decisions apply [Regulation 15 on registration, Regulation 30 on cross-border transfers, Regulation 46 on appeal and Regulation 48 on offences](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>).

**Application.** On 18 July 2025 the office [found Google LLC in breach of the Act's registration and cross-border transfer duties and ordered it to register within 30 days](<https://pdpo.go.ug/media/2025/07/Ssekamwa-Frank-3-Ors-vs-Google-LLC-PDPO-Decision-18th-July-2024.pdf>). On 20 February 2026 it partly upheld a complaint and [issued five corrective orders to WhatsApp LLC, with no fine](<https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 3 December 2025 a magistrate [fined the finance manager of Vuka Africa Ltd sh100,000 for failing to register with the office](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/kampala-data-collection-company-prosecuted-ov-NV_224311_032026>). By letter of 17 July 2026 the office [confirmed that WhatsApp LLC had implemented the corrective measures](<https://businesstimesug.com/whatsapp-updates-privacy-policies-after-uganda-data-protection-enforcement/>). In August 2026 its director said [14,697 of an estimated 50,000 entities required to register had done so](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/over-35-000-entities-unregistered-as-regulator-cracks-down-on-data-privacy-violations-5563326>).
