# Cabo Verde

## General law

**Authority.** The Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD) [began work in 2015–2016 and completed a decade in 2025](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>), under its president, Faustino Varela. The National Assembly's own budget [gave it CVE 36,313,115 for 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>), with no revenue of its own.

**Standing.** [Law 133/V/2001 is the general data protection regime](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde>), applying within the country and to the processing of data of people located in it. The CNPD's president [dates the base law to 2001](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>) and says the commission has worked from it since 2015–2016.

**Regulations.** The law [was amended in 2013 and again in 2021](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>): the first amendment led to the CNPD being set up, and the second added the obligation to appoint a data protection officer.

**Application.** The CNPD's president said in February 2025 that [cases involving artificial intelligence and big data have reached the commission](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>), among them two of images manipulated with generative AI and a first image-manipulation case in 2021. Cases that may be criminal are sent to the Ministério Público.

**Last twelve months.** In March 2025 the CNPD [held an international conference on personal data protection for its tenth anniversary](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/03/19/presidente-da-cnpd-defende-dialogo-entre-os-ecossistemas-de-governanca-de-dados-para-equilibrar-regulacao-da-ia/96153>), opened by the Prime Minister.
