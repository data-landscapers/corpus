# Cabo Verde

## General law

**Authority.** The Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD) [began work in 2015–2016 and completed a decade in 2025](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). Its president is Faustino Varela. The National Assembly's own budget [gave it CVE 36,313,115 for 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/4674538/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202025.pdf>), with no revenue of its own.

**Standing.** [Law 133/V/2001 is the general data protection regime](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Cabo-Verde>), covering processing in the country and data on people located there. The CNPD's president [dates the base law to 2001](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). The CNPD says the regime [was amended on 17 March 2021](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>), adding breach notification and impact assessments.

**Regulations.** The law [was amended in 2013, which led to the CNPD being set up](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). [The 2021 amendment added a duty to appoint a data protection officer](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>).

**Application.** The CNPD's 2023 report went to the National Assembly in April 2024. It [counts 297 processes decided out of 326 requests, and seven complaints received](<https://tiver.cv/cnpd-apresenta-relatorio-de-atividades-de-2023/>). From 2015 to 2025 the CNPD [issued thousands of authorisations and hundreds of opinions](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>). Its president said in February 2025 that [cases involving artificial intelligence and big data have reached it](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>). [Cases that may be criminal go to the Ministério Público](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/02/02/a-ia-ou-o-big-data-nao-podem-por-em-causa-os-direitos-das-pessoas/95448>).

**Last twelve months.** In March 2025 the CNPD [held an international conference for its tenth anniversary](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/03/19/presidente-da-cnpd-defende-dialogo-entre-os-ecossistemas-de-governanca-de-dados-para-equilibrar-regulacao-da-ia/96153>), opened by the Prime Minister. In April 2026 it [published an account of its eleven years](<https://rlpd.org/cnpd-cabo-verde-um-percurso-de-onze-anos/>).
