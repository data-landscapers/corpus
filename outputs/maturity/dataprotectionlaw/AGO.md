# Angola

## General law

**Authority.** Lei n.º 22/11 [creates the Agência de Protecção de Dados (APD)](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/>) as a public-law body of seven members. [Its organic statute was approved on 10 October 2016](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/>), and [three board members were appointed on 6 September 2019](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2019/decreto-presidencial-n-o-277-19-de-06-de-setembro/>). [Its board took a sanction decision on 10 August 2026](<https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b>).

**Standing.** Lei n.º 22/11, the Lei da Protecção de Dados Pessoais, [was published on 17 June 2011 and entered into force that day](<https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2011/lei-n-o-22-11-de-17-de-junho/>). On 25 June 2026 [the Assembleia Nacional rejected a UNITA bill to amend it](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/parlamento-rejeita-proposta-sobre-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>).

**Regulations.** [Decreto Presidencial n.º 60/21 of 10 March 2021](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2021/decreto-presidencial-n-o-60-21-de-10-de-marco/>) sets the fees the APD charges for registering files and authorising processing. [Decreto Presidencial n.º 214/16](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2016/decreto-presidencial-n-o-214-16-de-10-de-outubro/>) approves the APD's organic statute.

**Application.** The APD [fined Lizíria, Lda., owner of Hotel Diamante, USD 115,000 in kwanzas on 10 August 2026](<https://sapo.pt/artigo/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-de-angola-multa-hotel-diamante-em-115-mil-dolares-6a857707bf642b2fcc16d93b>). PTI, citing the APD's register, [counted three fines totalling USD 250,000 in 2025 and five totalling USD 600,000 in 2024](<https://pti.ao/apd-ja-aplicou-mais-de-1-milhao-de-dolares-em-multas-este-ano/>). In July 2024 the APD [fined COSAL and ENDE-EP USD 75,000 and USD 225,000 in kwanzas](<https://www.rapdp.org/en/node/215>) over ransomware breaches.

**Last twelve months.** The law is under revision. [An APD administrator said on 17 June 2026 that the public consultation was complete](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/nova-lei-de-proteccao-de-dados-pode-vigorar-no-proximo-ano-legislativo/>), and that the bill would go to the Assembleia Nacional in the next legislative year. On 21 April 2026 [terms of reference were issued for drafting a new organic law for the APD](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Td_Rs_Consultoria_para_Elaboracao_da_Lei_Organica_da_Agencia_de_Proteccao_de_Dados_5a7f75b595.pdf>). In October 2026 the APD [warned of critical PHP flaws](<https://radionova.co.ao/agencia-de-proteccao-de-dados-alerta-para-riscos-em-plataformas-digitais/>).
