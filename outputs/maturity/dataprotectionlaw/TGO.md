# Togo

## General law

**Authority.** Article 55 of the law creates a national authority, the [Instance de protection des données à caractère personnel (IPDCP)](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>). The IPDCP [officially launched its activities on 28 March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>) in Lomé, and its head says it [holds powers of control, injunction and sanction](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>).

**Standing.** [Loi n° 2019-014 of 29 October 2019](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) on the protection of personal data was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated; article 95 gave State and public-service processing two years to comply and all other processing one year. On 15 September 2026 the IPDCP [briefed ministry cabinet directors and secretaries-general](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) on their obligations under it as data controllers.

**Regulations.** Article 58 of the law has the President of the Republic [appoint the IPDCP's president by decree](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) from its steering committee.

**Application.** The IPDCP [gave attestations to 32 data protection correspondents](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises>) on 24 June 2026 after three days of training in Adétikopé. On 15 September 2026 it [convened public administrations in Lomé](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) on prior formalities, declaration of existing processing and designation of correspondents.

**Last twelve months.** The IPDCP and the broadcast regulator HAAC [signed a cooperation memorandum on 10 December 2025](<https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle>) that gives the IPDCP access to HAAC's monitoring room. The IPDCP [opened its first evaluation and certification session for correspondents](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026, with 32 professionals. On 19 October 2026 the IPDCP [placed a team in the media regulator HARC's monitoring service](<https://ipdcp.tg/medias-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-lipdcp-et-la-harc-renforcent-leur-partenariat-2/>). It [set a first national data protection day for 29 October 2026](<https://levisionnaire.tg/jnpdp-2026-lipdcp-mobilise-les-citoyens-pour-une-meilleure-protection-des-donnees-personnelles/>).
