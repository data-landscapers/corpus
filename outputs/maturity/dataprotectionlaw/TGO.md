# Togo

## General law

**Authority.** Article 55 of the law creates a national authority, the [Instance de protection des données à caractère personnel (IPDCP)](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>). The IPDCP [officially launched its activities in Lomé on 28 March 2025](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2903-16040-numerique-l-instance-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-demarre-ses-activites>). Its head says it [holds powers of control, injunction and sanction](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>). In November 2026 its president was [Colonel Bédiani Belei](<https://levisionnaire.tg/togo-lipdcp-met-en-garde-contre-lexposition-publique-des-donnees-personnelles-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

**Standing.** [Loi n° 2019-014 of 29 October 2019](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>) on the protection of personal data was adopted and promulgated. Article 95 gave State and public-service processing two years to comply, and all other processing one year. On 15 September 2026 the IPDCP [briefed ministry cabinet directors and secretaries-general](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) on their duties as controllers under it.

**Regulations.** Article 58 of the law has the President of the Republic [appoint the IPDCP's president by decree](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_29_10_2019-64E%20ANNEE-N%C2%B026%20TER.pdf>).

**Application.** On 24 June 2026 the IPDCP [gave attestations to 32 data protection correspondents](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/tic/2506-19349-protection-des-donnees-l-ipdcp-forme-ses-premiers-correspondants-pour-renforcer-la-conformite-des-entreprises>) after three days of training. On 15 September 2026 it [convened public administrations in Lomé](<https://levisionnaire.tg/protection-des-donnees-lipdcp-appelle-les-administrations-togolaises-a-accelerer-leur-mise-en-conformite/>) on declaring their processing and naming correspondents. On 5 November 2026 it [warned social media users against exposing others' personal data](<https://ipdcp.tg/document/communique-n-009-relatif-a-la-mise-en-garde-contre-la-collecte-et-la-divulgation-de-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

**Last twelve months.** The IPDCP and the broadcast regulator HAAC [signed a cooperation memorandum on 10 December 2025](<https://cybersecuritymag.africa/la-haac-et-ipdcp-scellent-un-accord-de-cooperation-institutionnelle>). The IPDCP [opened its first certification session for correspondents](<https://lenouveaureporter.com/togo-lipdcp-lance-la-certification-des-correspondants-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) in Atakpamé on 30 September 2026. On 19 October 2026 it [placed a team in the media regulator HARC's monitoring service](<https://ipdcp.tg/medias-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-lipdcp-et-la-harc-renforcent-leur-partenariat-2/>).
