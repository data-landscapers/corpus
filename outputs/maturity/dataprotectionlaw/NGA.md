# Nigeria

## General law

**Authority.** The [Nigeria Data Protection Commission supervises the Act](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/donnees-nigeria-letat-reprend-la-ndpc-apres-plus-de-9-millions-de-recettes-generees-en-3-ans/>). National Commissioner Vincent Olatunji heads it, and it keeps a [register of data processors of major importance](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-investigation-ebis-illegal-data-market-shuts-down-but-ndpc-retains-him-as-licensee/>).

**Standing.** President Tinubu [signed the Nigeria Data Protection Act on 12 June 2023](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/donnees-nigeria-letat-reprend-la-ndpc-apres-plus-de-9-millions-de-recettes-generees-en-3-ans/>), and the High Court of the Federal Capital Territory [applied it in a judgment of 29 July 2026](<https://saharareporters.com/2026/08/01/court-slams-stanbicibtc-over-data-privacy-breach-awards-ex-customers-n15million-orders>). A [Nigeria Data Protection (Amendment) Bill, 2026](<https://saharareporters.com/2026/07/19/rulaac-warns-data-protection-bill-could-give-nigerian-govt-sweeping-powers-over-social>) was before the National Assembly in July 2026.

**Regulations.** The minister's office names the [General Application and Implementation Directive 2025](<https://thesun.ng/fg-ngf-to-states-implement-data-protection-act-or-risk-trust-investment/>) as the operating framework under the Act for all tiers of government. The Commission's [Guidance Notice on Registration](<https://punchng.com/data-commission-issues-fresh-registration-directive-following-court-victory/>) designates data controllers and processors of major importance.

**Application.** The Commission [fined Multichoice Nigeria N766,242,500](<https://www.rapdp.org/index.php/en/node/222>) in July 2025. On 29 July 2026 the court [ordered Stanbic IBTC Bank to pay two former customers N15 million and erase their data](<https://saharareporters.com/2026/08/01/court-slams-stanbicibtc-over-data-privacy-breach-awards-ex-customers-n15million-orders>). A Commission order fining Meta $32.8 million was [set aside when the Federal High Court adopted a settlement](<https://www.premiumtimesng.com/news/top-news/913097-court-dismisses-25-million-privacy-suit-against-meta-in-imo.html>), [by consent judgment on 3 November 2025](<https://technext24.com/policy-and-tech/ndpc-data-protection-meta/>). By 11 June 2024 the Commission said [sanctions on seven firms had brought in N400 million](<https://nairametrics.com/2024/06/12/seven-firms-pay-n400-million-to-ndpc-as-sanction-for-data-breach/>). By October 2026 [41,000 organisations had registered, against 630 in the first year](<https://businessday.ng/interview/article/protection-of-personal-privacy-and-rights-is-central-to-ndpcs-mandate-olatunji/>).

**Last twelve months.** The Federal High Court [upheld the Commission's power to register controllers and processors of major importance](<https://leadership.ng/court-strengthens-ndpc-oversight-of-pos-agents-banks-telcos-data-controllers/>), and on 28 July 2026 the Commission [directed every unregistered one to register](<https://punchng.com/data-commission-issues-fresh-registration-directive-following-court-victory/>). It opened investigations into [Temu in February 2026](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/02/ndpc-probes-temu-over-alleged-data-protection-violations/>) and [Remita and Sterling Bank on 1 April 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/04/07/ndpc-investigates-remita-and-sterling-bank-over-alleged-data-breach/>).

[Section 37 of the 1999 Constitution](<https://saharareporters.com/2026/08/01/court-slams-stanbicibtc-over-data-privacy-breach-awards-ex-customers-n15million-orders>), the right to privacy; the [Nigeria Data Protection Regulation](<https://www.dataphyte.com/topic/technology/who-protects-nigerias-digital-citizens/>), issued by NITDA before the 2023 Act.
