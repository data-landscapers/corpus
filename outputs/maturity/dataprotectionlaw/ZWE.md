# Zimbabwe

## General law

**Authority.** [Section 5 of the Act designates POTRAZ, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority, as the Data Protection Authority](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf>). In May 2026 it [had a Director for Data Protection](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/>). [Its powers include audits, investigations, warnings, directions and prosecutions](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/>).

**Standing.** The Cyber and Data Protection Act, No. 5 of 2021, was [gazetted on 11 March 2022](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20%26%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Cap1207%20No%205%20of%202021%20gaz%202022-03-11.pdf>). It [applies to all public and private organisations](<https://www.michalsons.com/blog/zimbabwes-cyber-and-data-protection-act-overview/78795>). In May 2026 it [provided criminal sanctions for accountable officers](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/>), including up to seven years in prison.

**Regulations.** In September 2024 the ICT minister made [licensing regulations, Statutory Instrument 155 of 2024, under section 32 of the Act](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20(Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers)%20Regulations,%202024.pdf>). Organisations processing the data of 50 or more people [must hold a data controller licence](<https://cite.org.zw/potraz-data-licence-fees-slammed-as-costly-burden-on-businesses-and-consumers/>). [Fees run from US$50 to US$2,500 a year](<https://cite.org.zw/potraz-data-licence-fees-slammed-as-costly-burden-on-businesses-and-consumers/>), and the first deadline was 12 March 2025.

**Application.** [POTRAZ handed the first data controller licences to 570 companies in June 2025](<https://technomag.co.zw/570-institutions-receive-data-controllers-licences-to-bolster-digital-trust-and-compliance/>), and [721 were licensed by 15 October 2025](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047300/government-enforces-data-privacy-laws>). By October 2025 it had [received 20 formal complaints and investigated and resolved 15](<https://privacyscorecardreport.com/privacy-scorecard-report-2025/zimbabwe/zimbabwes-data-protection-regime/>). In March 2026 POTRAZ reported [1,123 licence applications so far that year and more than 60 breaches notified](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/financial-sector-bears-brunt-of-data-breaches-potraz-calls-for-stronger-data-management/>). [Its Director for Data Protection said in May 2026 that administrative sanctions were not yet in place](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/potraz-plots-crackdown-on-firms/>).

**Last twelve months.** [Regulatory Notice 2 of 2026, published in July 2026, announced mandatory compliance inspections of data controllers from 1 September 2026](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/07/potraz-starts-data-protection-inspections-on-1-september-here-is-what-it-means-for-you/>). [In September 2026 POTRAZ consulted on guidelines under the Act and on amendments to the regulations covering administrative fines](<https://sirdc.ac.zw/the-inaugural-africa-data-protection-day/>).
