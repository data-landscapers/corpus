# Zambia

## General law

**Authority.** The Data Protection Commissioner, Likando Luywa, [spoke in June 2026 on oversight and the registration of data controllers and processors](<https://lusakastar.com/news/data-protection-act-faces-enforcement-hurdles>). A [tender of 10 August 2026 seeks a firm to build a compliance management system for the Data Protection Commission of Zambia](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461498>).

**Standing.** The law is the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, which [a tender of 10 August 2026 names as the Act being implemented](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461500>). It was [described in July 2026 as part of the legal framework businesses must comply with](<https://africabusinessinsight.com/zambia-begins-enforcing-controversial-cyber-crimes-act-amid-digital-economy-growth/>), and a [World Bank appraisal document published on 5 September 2024 gives it as the standard for the Department of National Registration's personal-data procedures](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090924120535793/txt/BOSIB12c22668f0f51a0d117949b7b8fe34.txt>).

**Regulations.** A [tender of 10 August 2026 seeks consultants to help the Commission develop regulatory instruments for implementing the Act](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461500>).

**Application.** Data controllers and processors [had until 30 April 2025 to register under the Act](<https://bloggersofzambia.org/6th-state-of-digital-rights-report/>). UNDP, writing on 17 September 2024, [listed training staff in more than 145 offices across 10 provinces on the Act as a requirement of the digital ID rollout](<https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration>). Lusaka Star [reported on 7 June 2026 that enforcement and compliance remained a challenge](<https://lusakastar.com/news/data-protection-act-faces-enforcement-hurdles>).

**Last twelve months.** A [World Bank-financed tender opened on 10 August 2026, with expressions of interest due on 21 August, for a three-month assignment to review and develop national data protection guidelines and compliance frameworks](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00461500>). The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner [joined Zambia's delegation to the ID4Africa conference in Abidjan, which ended on 15 May 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/18/zambia-advances-digital-identity-agenda-through-multi-sectoral-engagement/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under the Act is on record, and the date the Act came into force is not stated. No decision, sanction or count of cases under the Act is on record; registration and training are the only application shown.
