# Congo

## General law

**Authority.** The [National Personal Data Protection Commission was inaugurated after its members were appointed under Decree No. 2025-538 of 31 December 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>), as reported on 22 January 2026.

**Standing.** Congo's general law is [Law No. 29-2019 of 10 October 2019 on personal data protection](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>). It was in force in January 2026, when the Commission it provides for was inaugurated.

**Regulations.** [Decree No. 2025-538 of 31 December 2025 completes the institutional framework the law established](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>), by appointing the Commission's members.

**Application.** The [Commission elected its President, Vice President and Rapporteur at its inaugural meeting](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>), reported in January 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The [Commission's members were appointed by decree of 31 December 2025 and the Commission was inaugurated with its leadership elected](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/congo-launches-commission-to-strengthen-personal-data-protection-and-digital-governance/>), reported on 22 January 2026.
