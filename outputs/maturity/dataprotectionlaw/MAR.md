# Morocco

## General law

**Authority.** The CNDP, the commission that supervises Law 09-08, was [installed in 2010](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf>). [The King named six of its members on 24 January 2025](<https://www.cndp.ma/nomination-des-nouveaux-membres-de-la-cndpcommission-nationale-de-controle-de-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). Its president, Omar Seghrouchni, [said on 4 August 2026 that its alerts can go as far as sanctions](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>).

**Standing.** Law 09-08 on personal data is in force. The CNDP [cited ten of its articles to political parties in August 2026](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/elections-legislatives-la-cndp-fixe-les-imperatifs-respecter-dans-le-traitement-des-donnees-caractere>). Its president [spoke that month of a law more than fifteen years in force](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>).

**Regulations.** The CNDP [validated five deliberations on 28 November 2025](<https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/>). [They set model declaration and authorisation forms](<https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/>) for cookies, newsletters, patient follow-up, healthcare video surveillance and workplace access control.

**Application.** Moroccan courts [handled 153 personal data cases in 2024](<https://madar21.com/315419.html>), the Supreme Council of the Judicial Power said in January 2025. Of 109 cases in the criminal courts, [80 were decided](<https://madar21.com/315419.html>). The Nador court of first instance [convicted a person for putting a complainant's photo and name on a student statement](<https://madar21.com/315419.html>) without consent. The CNDP [published a national register of notified processing in January 2023](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf>). On 28 August 2026 it [issued guidance to political parties and voters](<https://fr.walaw.press/country/cirdi/QWSP/articles/legislatives_2026_la_cndp_encadre_l_usage_des_donnees_personnelles_et_de_l_ia_pendant_la_campagne/GPFLSXGWQQFM>). In October 2026 it [agreed a pilot to train 20,000 young people in data privacy](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/343292/cndp-niiat-partner-to-train-20000-young-moroccans-in-data-privacy/>).

**Last twelve months.** A recast of Law 09-08, prepared with the Justice Ministry, [has been sent to the Secrétariat général du gouvernement](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>). The CNDP president said so on 4 August 2026. The CNDP [signed a cooperation memorandum with Portugal's data protection authority on 8 February 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).
