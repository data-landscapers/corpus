# Morocco

## General law

**Authority.** The Commission nationale de contrôle de la protection des données à caractère personnel (CNDP) was [installed in 2010 and acts under Law 09-08](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf>). Its president, Omar Seghrouchni, [said on 4 August 2026](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>) that it is strengthening an alert system that can go as far as sanctions.

**Standing.** Law 09-08 on personal data is in force: the CNDP [cited ten of its articles to political parties in August 2026](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/elections-legislatives-la-cndp-fixe-les-imperatifs-respecter-dans-le-traitement-des-donnees-caractere>), and its president [spoke that month of a law more than fifteen years in force](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>).

**Regulations.** The CNDP [validated five deliberations on 28 November 2025](<https://www.cndp.ma/la-cndp-publie-de-nouvelles-deliberations/>) setting model declaration and authorisation forms for cookies, newsletters, patient follow-up, healthcare video surveillance and workplace access control.

**Application.** The CNDP [published a national register of notified processing operations in January 2023](<https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/CNDP-Communiqu_2_Publication_du_Registre_National_FR_-20230111.pdf>). On 28 August 2026 it [issued guidance to political parties and voters](<https://fr.walaw.press/country/cirdi/QWSP/articles/legislatives_2026_la_cndp_encadre_l_usage_des_donnees_personnelles_et_de_l_ia_pendant_la_campagne/GPFLSXGWQQFM>) for the legislative elections of 23 September 2026. It runs a programme with public and private controllers that it calls [Data-Tika](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>), and in October 2026 [agreed a pilot to train 20,000 young people in data privacy](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/10/343292/cndp-niiat-partner-to-train-20000-young-moroccans-in-data-privacy/>).

**Last twelve months.** A proposed recast of Law 09-08, prepared with the Justice Ministry, [has been sent to the Secrétariat général du gouvernement](<https://fnh.ma/article/actualite-economique/protection-donnes-confiance-moteur-economique-numerique>), the CNDP president said on 4 August 2026. A draft framework law on AI, data governance and digital identity, called Digital X.0, was [under review by the same secretariat in November 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-drafts-landmark-law-to-govern-ai-data-and-digital-identity>). The CNDP [signed a cooperation memorandum with Portugal's data protection authority on 8 February 2026](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/bimonthly-update-on-privacy-in-africa-january-february-2026>).
