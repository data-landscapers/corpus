# Niger

## General law

**Authority.** The law [creates the Haute Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (HAPDP), attached to the Presidency, with ten members named by decree](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>). Its president said in July 2025 that [he took office on 18 September 2024 and that the plenary deliberates on authorisations and sanctions](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). Africa Data Protection reported on 13 June 2026 that [the government had abolished the HAPDP by decree and split its powers between the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of the Interior](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>). [The government's own communiqué confirms the decree of 8 June 2026 and the transfer of the authority's missions to the Justice and Interior ministries](<https://anp.ne/niger-plusieurs-services-rattaches-ou-sous-tutelle-de-la-presidence-de-la-primature-et-du-secretariat-general-du-gouvernement-supprimes/>).

**Standing.** [Law no. 2022-59 of 16 December 2022 on personal data protection](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>) is the general law, held in the HAPDP's consolidated text of April 2026. It [repealed laws 2017-28 and 2019-71](<https://www.lesahel.org/la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp>).

**Regulations.** The law has been [amended by Law 2023-31 of 4 July 2023 and by Ordinances 2024-16 of 26 April 2024 and 2024-29 of 24 June 2024](<https://www.hapdp.ne/uploads/files/Loi-2022-59-consolidee.pdf>).

**Application.** The HAPDP's president said in July 2025 that the authority had [processed 139 compliance files since September 2024, covering declarations, video-surveillance declarations and processing and transfer authorisations, and had run four control missions on mobile operators in 2025](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>).

**Last twelve months.** The [abolition of the HAPDP and the transfer of its powers to two ministries](<https://www.africadataprotection.org/actualites/Niger-Suppression-de-la-Haute-Autorite-de-Protection-des-Donnees-a-caractere-Personnel-et-transfert-de-ses-competences.html>) was reported on 13 June 2026.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under the law has been found, and no decision, sanction or count of complaints settled under it. The date the law came into force and the date of the decree abolishing the HAPDP are not held.
