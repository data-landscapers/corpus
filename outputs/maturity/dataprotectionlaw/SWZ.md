# Eswatini

## General law

**Authority.** The Data Protection Act designates the [Eswatini Communications Commission as the Eswatini Data Protection Authority](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/EDPA%20NOTICE%201%202024%20-%20FINAL%20DECISION%20ON%20THE%20REGISTRATION%20OF%20DATA%20CONTROLLERS%20AND%20DATA%20PROCESSORS.pdf>). In June 2026 the Authority had [powers to investigate breaches, resolve complaints and impose sanctions](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>), and took complaints by website, email and phone.

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2022 (No. 5 of 2022) was [assented to and gazetted on 4 March 2022](<https://www.esccom.org.sz/legislation/DATA%20PROTECTION%20ACT.pdf>). In June 2026 the Commission's acting chief executive said the Act [binds public and private bodies, ministries included, and requires a breach to be notified to the Authority within 72 hours](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>). The [deadline for organisations to comply was 31 March 2026](<https://independentnews.co.sz/40549/science-technology/eswatini-launches-data-protection-campaign/>).

**Regulations.** [Notice No. 1/2024](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/EDPA%20NOTICE%201%202024%20-%20FINAL%20DECISION%20ON%20THE%20REGISTRATION%20OF%20DATA%20CONTROLLERS%20AND%20DATA%20PROCESSORS.pdf>), issued in February 2024, made registration mandatory from 1 March 2024 for controllers and processors handling the data of more than ten people. [General Notice No. 4/2024](<https://edpa.org.sz/assets/documents/notice%204.pdf>) directed every data controller to appoint a data protection officer by 30 May 2024.

**Application.** The Authority lists [enforcement notices under section 41 of the Act](<https://www.edpa.org.sz/enforcement.html>) issued to MTN Eswatini and Liberty Life Swaziland Limited in June 2024 and to the Royal Science Technology Park in March 2025. In June 2026 it [opened a formal investigation into the Ministry of Home Affairs](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45057/news/home-affairs-investigated-for-for-sensitive-data-breach/>) over unblurred national identity documents posted online.

**Last twelve months.** By February 2026 the Authority had [launched its 2026 awareness campaign, "Take Control of Your Data"](<https://independentnews.co.sz/40549/science-technology/eswatini-launches-data-protection-campaign/>). In September 2026 it was [setting up information desks in major towns](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/edpa-takes-data-protection-services-closer-to-emaswati/>), after its own survey found 55.2% of the public finding the Authority difficult to access.
