# Botswana

## General law

**Authority.** The Information and Data Protection Commission was [established in 2022](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/87795>), and in July 2025 its commissioner had employed three contract officers while the deputy commissioner and two director posts were unfilled. Kepaletswe Somolekae was [the newly appointed commissioner in February 2026](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024) [came into operation on 14 January 2025](<https://itlawco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/1736836435941.pdf>). It was [published on 29 October 2024 and repeals the Data Protection Act No. 32 of 2018](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/understanding-botswanas-new-data-protection-act-key-updates-and-changes>). It [applies to all organisations processing personal data, public entities included](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>).

**Regulations.** Statutory Instrument No. 4 of 2025, [made by the Minister for State President on 13 January 2025](<https://itlawco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/1736836435941.pdf>), sets the Act's commencement date.

**Application.** In a public notice dated 17 July 2026 the Commission [issued a Determination and an Enforcement Notice against DPSM](<https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/>) for contraventions of the Act. It ordered the ePayslip portal suspended, and a data protection impact assessment and a revised privacy notice submitted within one month. DPSM [suspended the portal on 22 July 2026](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-e-payslip-fails-data-protection-compliance-test/news>).

**Last twelve months.** In February 2026 the new commissioner [vowed firmer enforcement against non-bank financial institutions](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/new-data-commission-tightens-compliance-oversight/news>). The July 2026 notice [left an administrative fine possible if DPSM did not comply](<https://na.co.bw/data-commission-orders-suspension-of-dpsm-epayslip-portal-over-privacy-law-breaches/>).
