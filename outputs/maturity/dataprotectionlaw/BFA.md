# Burkina Faso

## General law

**Authority.** The [Commission de l'informatique et des libertés (CIL) was created by a 2004 law and has worked since December 2007](<https://www.sidwaya.info/journee-nationale-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-cil-sollicite-laccompagnement-du-premier-ministre/>). The [2021 law makes it the supervisory authority, with nine members appointed by decree](<https://www.fonction-publique.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/22-08-2021-C2-LOI-N__001-2021_AN_portant_protection_des_personnes.pdf>). It [held its sixth plenary session of the year on 26 June 2025](<https://cil.bf/?p=1506>). A [bill adopted on 3 August 2026 merges the CSC and the CIL into a new regulator, ARCOD, whose first college is still to be formed](<https://www.aib.media/burkina-les-deputes-adoptent-la-loi-portant-creation-de-larcod-issue-de-la-fusion-du-csc-et-de-la-cil/>).

**Standing.** [Law n°001-2021/AN was adopted on 30 March 2021, repeals the 2004 law and gives one year from entry into force for all processing to comply](<https://www.fonction-publique.gov.bf/fileadmin/user_upload/storage/22-08-2021-C2-LOI-N__001-2021_AN_portant_protection_des_personnes.pdf>). On [1 July 2025 the CIL president recalled that it makes data protection compulsory](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>). The [legislature adopted a new data protection bill on 3 August 2026](<https://assembleenationale.bf/830>).

**Regulations.** The [2021 law itself sets the declaration duties and the fines](<https://africanlegalfactory.com/2024/01/30/understanding-personal-data-protection-in-burkina-faso/>).

**Application.** By [communiqué of 24 December 2024 the CIL warned that repeatedly posting people's images on social media without consent violates the law](<https://burkina24.com/2025/02/11/droits-numeriques-au-burkina-faso-le-centre-national-de-presse-norbert-zongo-presente-son-rapport-2024/>). On [26 June 2025 it validated its public report for 2024, covering compliance controls and the handling of complaints and authorisation requests](<https://cil.bf/?p=1506>). On [1 July 2025 the Prime Minister launched its awareness sessions for ministries and public institutions](<https://www.sidwaya.info/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-le-premier-ministre-sonne-la-mobilisation-au-sein-de-ladministration/>).

**Last twelve months.** The [bill voted on 3 August 2026 indexes fines to turnover and bans the transfer of sensitive data abroad](<https://www.sidwaya.info/surete-aerienne-et-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-lassemblee-legislative-du-peuple-adopte-les-projets-de-lois/>), and [requires prior consent for commercial prospecting](<https://assembleenationale.bf/830>). The [law organising ARCOD as the single authority for communications regulation and personal data protection was adopted the same day](<https://www.lobspaalga.com/2026/08/03/87181/>).
