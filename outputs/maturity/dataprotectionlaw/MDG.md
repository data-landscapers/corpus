# Madagascar

## General law

**Authority.** Article 28 of the law [institutes the Commission Malagasy de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CMIL)](<https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf>) as an independent authority of nine members named by decree in the Council of Ministers. Seven members [were sworn in at the Cour suprême on 4 August 2025](<https://assemblee-nationale.mg/article/prestation-de-serment-des-membres-de-la-commission-malagasy-de-linformatique-et-des-libertes-cmil/>), and the commission [elected a president and two vice-presidents on 14 August 2025](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>).

**Standing.** Loi n° 2014-038 on the protection of personal data was [adopted by the Assemblée nationale on 16 December 2014](<https://www.assemblee-nationale.mg/media/archives_textes/pl/2014-038/Loi-n2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donn%C3%A9es-%C3%A0-Kre-personnel.pdf>) and [promulgated on 9 January 2015](<https://digital.gov.mg/en/2022/07/05/loi-n-2014-038-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). The commission's procedures manual, approved in October 2025, [takes the law as its legal basis](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf>).

**Regulations.** [Décret n°2023-1541 of 6 December 2023](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf>) sets the commission's attributions, organisation and operation and governs the processing of personal data; Décret n°2025-696 of 18 June 2025 amended it. The commission's report of January 2026 [cites a significant delay in adopting the law's application decrees](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>).

**Application.** The commission reported in January 2026 that [no processing declaration had been registered](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>), that citizens' complaints had gone untreated for ten years and that it had neither premises nor staff. Its manual [sets procedures for declarations, authorisations, audits, complaints and administrative sanctions](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf>), with a decision on a complaint due within 30 working days.

**Last twelve months.** The commission [approved its first procedures manual on 17 October 2025](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/manuel-procedures-cmil-03-dec-2025.pdf>), published on 3 December 2025. It [joined the AFAPDP on 24 November 2025 and obtained a budget line of Ariary 2,411,579,000 in the 2026 Finance Law](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>).

***Also on file***. The African Union's [Malabo Convention, ratified by Madagascar in 2024](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/11XR_I-6z6Y28qnrEjHABu3SOIbjWy6yW/view>); [Loi n°2024-004 of 21 June 2024, authorising that ratification](<https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9255>).

***Not found***. No decision, sanction or count of cases under the law. The date the law came into force is not stated.
