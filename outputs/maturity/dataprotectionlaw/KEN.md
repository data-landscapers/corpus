# Kenya

## General law

**Authority.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was [established under the Data Protection Act, 2019](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>). [Capital FM reported on 10 November 2026 that its first commissioner, Immaculate Kassait, is leaving after six years](<https://capitalfm.africa/kassait-making-data-protection-a-national-conversation-was-my-key-achievement/>). [A successor was nominated that month](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/11/10/kenya-nominates-lydia-sitienei-second-data-commissioner/>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2019 is in force. The ICT Cabinet Secretary told the Senate in May 2026 that [the Act is being enforced](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>), and the office has [issued fines under it since 2022](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>).

**Regulations.** The Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 are [in force under the Act](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-28-odpc-gives-data-handlers-14-days-to-renew-expired-certificates>). They set 24-month registration certificates and make processing on an expired certificate an offence.

**Application.** As at September 2024 the office had [fined at least nine businesses a total of KES 26,275,000](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). An April 2025 analysis [counted 51 determinations by the office in 2024](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/11/13/kenya-data-protection-law-ai-regulation/>). Its bulletin states that by March 2025 it had [issued 215 determinations and imposed penalties on 10 entities](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>). In February 2026 it [ordered St Luke's Orthopaedic Hospital to compensate patients and an events company to pay KES 300,000](<https://www.amnestykenya.org/kenyas-digital-rights-journey-half-year-scorecard/>), according to Amnesty Kenya.

**Last twelve months.** In August 2026 the office [published a draft guidance note on transfers of personal data to offshore AI providers](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/regulator-plans-tighter-data-shields-on-offshore-ai-platforms-5546966>). In November 2026 [the High Court set aside a KSh1.25 million award by the office](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/11/15/odpc-compensation-powers/>). The court found the Act does not expressly let the Commissioner award damages. That month the office [issued an enforcement notice to the Business Registration Service](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/11/17/brs-data-breach-odpc-investigation>).
