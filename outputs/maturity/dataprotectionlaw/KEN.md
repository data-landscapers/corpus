# Kenya

## General law

**Authority.** The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner was [established under the Data Protection Act, 2019](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/30/kenya-data-protection-commissioner-shortlist/>). Its first commissioner has been in office since 2020. In March 2025 it had [offices in Nairobi, Kisumu, Mombasa, Eldoret, Machakos, Nyeri and Nakuru](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2019 is in force. The ICT Cabinet Secretary told the Senate in May 2026 that [the Act is being enforced](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/register-or-face-shutdown-kabogo-to-betting-firms-with-children-s-data-5458126>), and the office has [issued fines under it since 2022](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). In October 2026 a trade report described organisations in Kenya as [facing growing compliance demands under the Act](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/15/dama-kenya-launch-train-10000-data-professionals/>).

**Regulations.** The Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 are [in force under the Act](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-28-odpc-gives-data-handlers-14-days-to-renew-expired-certificates>). They set 24-month registration certificates and make processing on an expired certificate an offence.

**Application.** As at September 2024 the office had [fined at least nine businesses a total of KES 26,275,000](<https://www.cadmuscyber.com/public/insight/kenyan-businesses-fined-over-kes-26-million-for-data-privacy-violations>). Its bulletin states that by March 2025 it had [issued 215 determinations and imposed penalties on 10 entities](<https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ODPC-Data-Bulletin-12th-Edition.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In August 2026 the office [published a draft guidance note on transfers of personal data to offshore AI providers](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/regulator-plans-tighter-data-shields-on-offshore-ai-platforms-5546966>). On 28 August 2026 it [gave holders of expired registration certificates until 11 September 2026 to renew](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-08-28-odpc-gives-data-handlers-14-days-to-renew-expired-certificates>) or face enforcement. In April 2026 it [announced an investigation into privacy concerns over Meta's smart glasses](<https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y7yvgy0w6o>).
