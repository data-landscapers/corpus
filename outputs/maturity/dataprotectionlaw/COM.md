# Comoros

## General law

**Authority.** The law [institutes a supervisory body, which its Article 19 names the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>), with nine members. [As of mid-2024 the authority had not been formally constituted or made operational](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>).

**Standing.** The Assembly of the Union [adopted Loi n°14-029/AU on the protection of personal data, in 68 articles, in plenary session on 26 June 2014](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>). The Union of Comoros [enacted its Protection of Personal Data Law on 29 June 2021](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>), as a comprehensive framework.

**Regulations.** Under Article 24 of the law, [the nine members of the commission are named by decree](<https://anaden.org/uploads/media/63076edf43d1e/loi-portant-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-de-lunion-des-comores.pdf>).

**Application.** The authority that the law gives the task of supervision [was not operational as of mid-2024](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated position is that of mid-2024, when [the authority had not been formally constituted](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-the-comoros-data-protection-law>).
