# Liberia

## General law

**Authority.** The Act [confers data protection functions on the Independent Information Commissioner](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>), the oversight body created by the Freedom of Information Act of 2010.

**Standing.** The Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act of 2024 was [approved on 9 March 2026 and printed on 23 March 2026](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>). It takes effect on publication into handbills, and existing businesses have one year from the effective date to comply. President Boakai's [promulgation of the law was announced on 19 August 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/le-liberia-adopte-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-et-la-protection-des-donnees>).

**Regulations.** The Act requires [implementing rules within 24 months of its taking effect](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>).

**Application.** The Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection [said on 13 September 2026 that it is aware of its responsibilities under the Act](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/mgcsp-admits-authentic-beneficiary-data-leaked-launches-internal-investigation/>), and that it took part in validating the legal framework.

**Last twelve months.** The House of Representatives had [referred the President's bill to committee on 10 June 2025](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-reviews-privacy-data-protection-bill/article_6675211a-e8bc-43c6-8e04-e73cbf26278a.html>). The Act was [approved on 9 March 2026](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/An_Act_For_The_Collection_Processing_-Transmission_-Storage_-Protection_-And_Use_Of_Personal-_Information_In_Liberia_Printed_Version_20260323_063904.pdf>), and its [signing was reported on 20 August 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/le-liberia-adopte-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-et-la-protection-des-donnees>).

***Also on file***. [Article 16 of the Constitution of Liberia](<https://africachinareporting.com/liberia-struggles-to-enforce-privacy-and-data-protection-laws/>); a [policy to implement the ECOWAS Data Protection Act](<https://africachinareporting.com/liberia-struggles-to-enforce-privacy-and-data-protection-laws/>), being drafted in February 2025.

***Not found***. Any regulation issued under the Act, and any decision, sanction or count of decisions under it. Whether the Independent Information Commissioner has begun acting under the Act is not held.
