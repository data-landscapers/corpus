# Rwanda

## General law

**Authority.** The [National Cyber Security Authority acts as the supervisory authority](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) for personal data protection and privacy under the Data Protection Act.

**Standing.** Rwanda's [Data Protection Act was introduced on 15 October 2021](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). It is in force: its [two-year grace period ended on 15 October 2023](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>), the date from which controllers and processors are held to it.

**Regulations.** Under the Act the authority [issues registration certificates to data controllers and processors](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>).

**Application.** By January 2026 the authority [had registered data controllers and processors](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>), [trained over 100 Data Protection Officers in the public sector](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) and [run sector workshops for pharmacists and local government entities](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>), all over the previous four years.

**Last twelve months.** For Data Privacy Week, 26 to 30 January 2026, the authority [adopted the theme "Compliance is Beyond Registration"](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). It moved from the awareness phase of 2022 to 2024 to capacity building, and [now expects controllers to prove that they comply](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>).
