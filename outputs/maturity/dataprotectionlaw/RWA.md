# Rwanda

## General law

**Authority.** The [National Cyber Security Authority is the supervisory authority](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>) under the Data Protection Act. It oversees personal data protection and privacy.

**Standing.** Rwanda's [Data Protection Act was introduced on 15 October 2021](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). It is in force: its [two-year grace period ended on 15 October 2023](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>), the date from which controllers and processors are held to it.

**Regulations.** Under the Act the authority [issues registration certificates to data controllers and processors](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>).

**Application.** By January 2026 the authority [had registered data controllers and processors](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). It had [trained over 100 Data Protection Officers in the public sector](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). It had also [run sector workshops for pharmacists and local government](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). In June 2025 its data protection office [held workshops on sector guides to the law](<https://dpo.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Newsletter_June_2025.pdf>). It also offers controllers a compliance self-assessment form.

**Last twelve months.** Data Privacy Week ran from 26 to 30 January 2026. The authority [chose the theme "Compliance is Beyond Registration"](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>). Awareness work ran from 2022 to 2024, then capacity building. It [now expects controllers to prove that they comply](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32861/opinions/why-2026-is-the-year-of-accountability-for-data-controllers-and-processors>).
