# Guinea-Bissau

## General law

**Authority.** Guinea-Bissau has [no dedicated national data protection authority](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/>). A professor at the Bissau Faculty of Law said so on 22 September 2026.

**Standing.** The country [does not yet have specific data protection legislation](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/>), the same speaker said. The government's February 2025 report to the Universal Periodic Review lists [a data protection bill among several awaiting discussion](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-09/A_HRC_WG.6_49_GNB_1_F.pdf>) in the National People's Assembly. That month a WARDIP coordinator said the country [urgently needs a data protection law](<https://rdngbissau.gw/2025/02/20/guine-bissau-necessita-urgentemente-de-legislacao-para-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais-defende-especialista/>).

**Regulations.** The bill [leaves the organisation of the body it would create to a decree](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/>).

**Application.** Data protection matters are [handled by several institutions, sector by sector](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 8 May 2026 the Council of Ministers [approved the Data Protection Law with amendments](<https://forbesafricalusofona.com/guine-bissau-anuncia-inicio-do-recenseamento-geral-da-populacao-e-habitacao-para-junho/>). It told an interministerial commission to make the changes. On 22 September 2026 the professor said the bill would create a ["Data Protection Agency"](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-inter-la-cour-de-justice-de-la-cedeao-dispose-dun-cadre-pour-statuer-sur-les-droits-numeriques-selon-un-universitaire/>). The professor added that its membership puts its independence at risk.

***Also on file***. The [Malabo Convention](<https://ang.gw/cnt-aprova-com-alteracoes-lei-contra-divulgacao-de-falsas-informacoes-nas-redes-sociais/>); the [Política Nacional de Governança e Proteção de Dados](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/>).

***Not found***. No law is enacted, and no source found says the bill approved in May 2026 has gone to the National People's Assembly. Nothing is held on regulations or on any decision.
