# The Gambia

## General law

**Authority.** The Act [establishes an independent oversight Commission](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>). A notice published on 17 August 2026 has the [Information Commission of The Gambia preparing a communication strategy](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>) to raise awareness of the Access to Information Act, 2021 and the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2025.

**Standing.** The National Assembly [passed the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Bill 2025](<https://babl.ai/gambia-passes-landmark-data-protection-and-privacy-law/>) in its Third Ordinary Session, and President Barrow [assented to the Act on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>). It is [Act No. 11 of 2025](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>). It is the country's first comprehensive data protection law and [has no grace period before it becomes enforceable](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>).

**Regulations.** In February 2026 regulations and guidance under the Act, on research purposes, the income base for fines and transfers, [were still to be issued](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>).

**Application.** Paradigm Initiative [called in January 2026 for the relevant authorities to implement and enforce the law](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-applauds-countries-with-data-protection-laws/>).

**Last twelve months.** Parliament's passage of the bill was [reported on 6 October 2025, when it awaited presidential assent](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-praised-for-passing-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-bill>), and [assent followed on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>).
