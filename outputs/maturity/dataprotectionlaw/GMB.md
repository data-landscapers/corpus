# The Gambia

## General law

**Authority.** The Act [establishes an independent oversight Commission](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-gambias-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025>). In May 2026 the minister said [oversight had gone to the Access to Information Authority's commissioners, who were being trained](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambia-has-ecowass-most-advanced-data-protection-law-minister-jabbi-says/>). A notice of 17 August 2026 has the [Information Commission preparing a communication strategy](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/vacancies/development-communication-strategy-information-commission-gambia>). It covers the Access to Information Act, 2021 and the 2025 data protection Act.

**Standing.** The National Assembly [passed the Personal Data Protection and Privacy Bill 2025](<https://babl.ai/gambia-passes-landmark-data-protection-and-privacy-law/>) in its Third Ordinary Session, and President Barrow [assented to the Act on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>). It is [Act No. 11 of 2025](<https://media2.mofo.com/v3/assets/blt5775cc69c999c255/blt7a10e6071e9d3fad/69d554b553df724b615c685d/personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act-2025.pdf>). It is the country's first comprehensive data protection law and [has no grace period before it becomes enforceable](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>). On 13 May 2026 the minister said [the country already has the law in place](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambia-has-ecowass-most-advanced-data-protection-law-minister-jabbi-says/>).

**Regulations.** In February 2026 [regulations and guidance under the Act were still to be issued](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>). They cover research purposes, the income base for fines, and transfers.

**Application.** Paradigm Initiative [called in January 2026 for the relevant authorities to implement and enforce the law](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-applauds-countries-with-data-protection-laws/>).

**Last twelve months.** Parliament's passage of the bill was [reported on 6 October 2025, awaiting presidential assent](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/gambia-praised-for-passing-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-bill>). [Assent followed on 7 November 2025](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-the-gambian-personal-data-protection-and-privacy-act/>).
