# Mauritania

## General law

**Authority.** The Autorité de Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (APD) was [created by article 64 of the law](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/112890/MRT-112890.pdf>) as an independent body attached to the Prime Minister, and [decree 2022-13 of 18 February 2022](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7/>) governs its composition and operation. It [met on 30 June 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-18/>) under its president, with its members and the Government Commissioner present.

**Standing.** Law 2017-020 on the protection of personal data was [promulgated on 22 July 2017](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/112890/MRT-112890.pdf>). The APD's [2023-2026 strategic plan](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/plan-stragtegique-2023-2026/>) places itself under the law's entry into force, and its [deliberation 002/2026 of 24 March 2026](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>) applies articles 7, 11, 27, 37 and 40.

**Regulations.** [Deliberation 004/2025 of 14 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-004-2025-%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%af%d8%af-%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d9%88%d9%82%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b9%d8%af-%d8%a5%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7/>) requires the APD's prior authorisation for transfers to countries not on its list and lists 105 countries as adequate. [Deliberation 005/2025 of 30 July 2025](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7/>) requires it for public and private controllers using cloud services abroad.

**Application.** On 24 March 2026 the APD [gave a favourable opinion, conditional on its remarks](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>), on the joint decision authorising the system Maaden Mauritanie calls Maadeni. On 19 November 2025 it [examined complaints 004-2025, 006-2025 and 007-2025](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-13/>), one of them with a [bank's reply](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%ac%d8%aa%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d9%87%d8%b1%d9%8a-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d8%af%d9%8a-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%84%d8%b7%d8%a9-%d8%ad%d9%85%d8%a7%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84-13/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 19 November 2025 the APD [approved a control reference framework and empowered its sworn agents to take part in inspection missions](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-13/>). On 29 April 2026 it [approved amendments to deliberations 004/2025 and 005/2025 and discussed complaints 008-2025 and 001-2026](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-16/>). Its [30 June 2026 session](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-18/>) had authorisation requests and ministries' compliance answers on its agenda.
