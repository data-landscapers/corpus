# São Tomé and Príncipe

## General law

**Authority.** The Agência Nacional de Protecção de Dados Pessoais (ANPDP) is [the body that by law authorises and supervises all data processing](<https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>), says Téla Nón. Its president is José Manuel Costa Alegre. Portugal's data protection commission [received an ANPDP delegation on 16 June 2025](<https://www.cnpd.pt/comunicacao-publica/noticias/cnpd-recebeu-a-agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-de-sao-tome-e-principe/>). It names the agency a founding member of the Lusophone Data Protection Network.

**Standing.** [Lei n.º 03/2016 on the protection of personal data was published on 10 May 2016](<https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/STP%20-%20Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2003_2016%20-%20Lei%20de%20Garantia%20e%20Protec%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20dos%20Dados%20Pessoais%20d....pdf>). An International Bar Association chapter of 2024 [names it the main instrument of the framework](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). It applies to controllers based or established in the country.

**Regulations.** The law itself [provides for fines and criminal sanctions](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). It [leaves the agency's organic law, staff and members' statute](<https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/STP%20-%20Lei%20n.%C2%BA%2003_2016%20-%20Lei%20de%20Garantia%20e%20Protec%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20dos%20Dados%20Pessoais%20d....pdf>) to a later law.

**Application.** The IBA chapter says the ANPDP [imposes fines and ancillary measures](<https://www.ibanet.org/document?id=Digital-Regulations-in-the-Metaverse-Era-Sao-Tome-Principe>). In January 2026 its director [said it would publish a list of non-compliant bodies and apply fines](<https://glebatv.st/todas-noticias/28-de-janeiro-sao-tome-e-principe-assinala-o-dia-internacional-da-privacidade-e-protecao-de-dados-pessoais>). In April [he reported more complaints about personal data on social networks](<https://rstp.st/2026/04/24/agencia-nacional-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais-celebra-8o-aniversario/>). That month he met the speaker [to plan oversight of the automatic electoral roll](<https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 1 June 2026 the ANPDP [signed a cooperation memorandum with Macau's data protection office](<https://www.gov.mo/pt/noticias/831008/>). Macau's executive orders [had authorised it by 27 May](<https://macaubusiness.com/macau-to-sign-personal-data-protection-pacts-with-cape-verde-sao-tome-and-principe/>).
