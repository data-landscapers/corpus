# Sierra Leone

## General law

**Authority.** The Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill [would establish a single independent authority responsible for both data protection and access to information](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>), the first such body in the country.

**Standing.** The law is a bill. The Information Minister announced in November 2025 that the [Data Protection and Right to Access Information Bill, 2025 had concluded its final national validation](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>). On 21 April 2026 he announced that [Cabinet had authorised enactment of a new Data Protection and Right to Access Information Act](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>) and approved the repeal of the Right to Access Information Commission Act.

**Regulations.** On 21 April 2026 the Ministry of Information and Civic Education and the Attorney-General's office were [still to finalise the Bill and table it in Parliament](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>).

**Application.** In November 2025 the Bill had [submission to Cabinet and then to Parliament still to come](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>).

**Last twelve months.** The Bill moved from [final national validation in November 2025](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/government-data-protection-information-bill>) to [Cabinet approval on 21 April 2026](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>).

***Also on file***. The [National Data Protection Policy](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>), approved by Cabinet in April 2026.

***Not found***. No law is enacted, and whether the Bill has been tabled in Parliament is not held. No regulations and no decisions have been found, and no source says there are none.
