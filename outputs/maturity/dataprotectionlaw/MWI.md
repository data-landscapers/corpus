# Malawi

## General law

**Authority.** [Section 4 of the Act designates the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) as the Data Protection Authority](<https://cyrilla.org/api/files/1714974153599cf41tfelimj.pdf>). MACRA [issued guidance in that role in October 2025](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>), and in March 2026 [the Authority operated under the regulator, which was working to make it an independent institution](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/>).

**Standing.** The Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 3 of 2024) was [passed on 7 December 2023, assented to on 31 January 2024 and published on 2 February 2024](<https://www.mca.ac.mw/mca-siteup/uploads/Data%20Protection%20Act%202024.pdf>). The Authority's guideline of October 2025 says [the Act applied at once to foreign controllers and to Malawian controllers with over 10,000 data subjects, and to all others from 3 June 2026](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>).

**Regulations.** MACRA [drafted the Data Protection (Data Processors Engagement) Regulations, 2025 under section 52 of the Act, dated 6 October 2025](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/data-protection-data-processors-engagement-regulations/?wpdmdl=8809>). In July 2026 [the draft regulations it put to consultation were still to be finalised](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/macra-consults-stakeholders-on-proposed-data-protection-registration-fees/>).

**Application.** The Authority [published a compliance guideline and checklist on 17 October 2025, with a registration requirement for controllers of significant importance](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guideline-and-checklist-on-compliance/?wpdmdl=8808>), and [breach notification guidelines on 22 October 2025, setting a 72-hour notice to the Authority and to data subjects](<https://www.dpa.mw/download/guidelines-on-personal-data-breach-notifications-and-communications/?wpdmdl=8818>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 [the Public Private Partnership Commission issued terms of reference for a consultancy to operationalise the Authority and draft regulations](<https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ToRs%20OPERATIONALIZATION_OF_THE_DATA_PROTECTION_Act.pdf>). In March 2026 [the World Bank announced renewed technical assistance to MACRA for data protection](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/>). On 13 July 2026 [MACRA consulted stakeholders in Blantyre on draft regulations carrying annual registration fees](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-advances-data-protection-framework/xnklOvz14wkq4Ymz>) of [K50,000 to K7 million by turnover](<https://malawi24.com/2026/07/16/macra-targets-stronger-data-protection-compliance/>).
