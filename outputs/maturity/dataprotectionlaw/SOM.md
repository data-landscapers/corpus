# Somalia

## General law

**Authority.** The [Data Protection Authority was inaugurated on 24 February 2024](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>). The Act [sets it up as an independent agency under a General Manager, with a Board of up to nine members](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Somalia-Data-Protection-Act-2.pdf>).

**Standing.** [Law No. 005 of 2023, the Data Protection Act, was ratified by both chambers of parliament and signed by the President in March 2023](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>). A notice from the Authority, reported on 2 June 2026, [requires registration under Article 32 of every entity that collects, stores, processes or shares personal data in Somalia](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>).

**Regulations.** A review published on 11 June 2024 says [the Authority issued an Implementation Regulation, guidance and an Alternative Dispute Resolution Procedure under Article 41](<https://www.techhiveadvisory.africa/insights/review-of-somalias-data-protection-act-and-implementing-regulations>). The [Council of Ministers approved the Regulatory Framework for the Public Data Protection Law by majority vote](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/08/somalia-advances-digital-governance-with-approval-of-public-data-protection-regulation/>), reported on 8 January 2026.

**Application.** The Authority [published guidelines for implementing the law at the beginning of February 2024](<https://blog.africadataprotection.org/en/2024/02/26/somalia-inauguration-of-the-data-protection-authority>). By 2 June 2026 it [had begun accepting registration applications from controllers and processors, and said it will issue certificates only to those meeting the conditions](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>).

**Last twelve months.** The Regulatory Framework was [approved after the Ministry of Communications and Technology said it had cleared constitutional, legal and technical procedures](<https://itweb.africa/article/somalia-adopts-data-regulatory-framework/>), reported on 6 February 2026. The Authority [opened registration of controllers and processors](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/02/somalia-data-protection-authority-enforces-nationwide-data-compliance-rules/>), reported on 2 June 2026.
