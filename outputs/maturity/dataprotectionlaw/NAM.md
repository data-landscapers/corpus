# Namibia

## General law

**Authority.** The [2020 draft of the Data Protection Bill](<https://dataprotection.africa/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Namibia_DPA-Bill.pdf>) establishes a Data Protection Supervisory Authority of Namibia in section 25, and the [revised draft dated 2022](<https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Data-Protection-Draft-Bill-March-2022....pdf>) provides in its long title for the same authority and its powers, duties and functions.

**Standing.** The Data Protection Bill is a draft. The ICT minister, Emma Theofelus, [said on 13 July 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A>) that Namibia was finalising it.

**Regulations.** The bill the Ministry of Information and Communication Technology [published for comment in 2022](<https://action-namibia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Data-Protection-Draft-Bill-March-2022....pdf>) is a 59-page draft with its gazette notice number left blank.

**Application.** An opposition MP, Rodney Cloete, [said in August 2026](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) that Namibia has no data protection statute, and so no legal definition of a data controller and no rules on cross-border transfers.

**Last twelve months.** In August 2025 the minister said the bill, [about 12 years in preparation](<https://www.namibiansun.com/local-news/data-protection-bill-returns-to-parliament2025-08-18167195>), would be [tabled in Parliament in September 2025](<https://www.namibian.com.na/govt-to-introduce-data-protection-bill-in-september/>). In January 2026 she said it had been through consultation and drafting and was [ready for resubmission to the Cabinet Committee on Legislation](<https://neweralive.na/data-legislations-under-final-polishing/>), and in [July 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-pushes-cyber-security-reforms/mQwkoq6YJWlM3r9A>) that it was being finalised.

***Also on file***. [Article 13 of the Constitution (privacy)](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>).

***Not found***. No source states that the bill has been tabled in Parliament, passed or enacted. Nothing dated since the 2022 draft says the supervisory authority has been set up, and no regulations or decisions have been found.
