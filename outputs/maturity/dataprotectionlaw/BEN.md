# Benin

## General law

**Authority.** The Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles (APDP) is constituted. [Decree 2026-422 of 1 July 2026 appointed its eight members](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2026-422/download>), and [they were sworn in on 6 November 2026](<https://www.24haubenin.info/?Les-nouveaux-membres-de-l-APDP-pretent-serment=>). [Seventeen of its officers were sworn in before the Cotonou court on 14 September 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7230/17-apdp-benin-officers-sworn-in-to-strengthen-commitment-to-personal-data-protection>).

**Standing.** The data protection rules are Book V of the Code du numérique, [Law no. 2017-20, promulgated on 20 April 2018](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>). It [replaced Law no. 2009-09 of 22 May 2009](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>) and was [amended by Law no. 2020-35 of 6 January 2021](<https://fraternite.bj/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-au-benin-ce-que-dit-la-loi-du-06-janvier-2021/>). In September 2026 [La Nation treated the Code as the framework governing personal data processing](<https://www.lanation.bj/societe/societe-votre-patron-peut-il-lire-vos-messages-whatsapp-professionnels>).

**Regulations.** [Decree 2022-107 of 16 February 2022](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2022-107/>) is taken under the Code. It authorises the interior ministry's national video-protection data processing. [Decree 2020-187 of 11 March 2020](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-187/>) is also taken under it. It lets the national police collect traveller identity and fingerprint data at borders.

**Application.** La Nation reported the APDP's annual report for 2025. It gives [907 files examined, 793 validated, 64 inspection missions and 20 complaints treated](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite>). The authority counted [347 decisions from 2016 to 15 December 2020](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>). Among them was a 2020 injunction ordering a controller to honour a complainant's right of deletion.

**Last twelve months.** [The eight members named in July 2026 took office on 6 November 2026](<https://www.24haubenin.info/?Les-nouveaux-membres-de-l-APDP-pretent-serment=>). The [swearing-in of seventeen APDP officers on 14 September 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7230/17-apdp-benin-officers-sworn-in-to-strengthen-commitment-to-personal-data-protection>) was done under Article 468(2) of the Code.
