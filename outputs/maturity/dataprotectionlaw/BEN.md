# Benin

## General law

**Authority.** The Autorité de Protection des Données Personnelles (APDP) is constituted: [seventeen of its officers were sworn in before the Court of First Instance of Cotonou on 14 September 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7230/17-apdp-benin-officers-sworn-in-to-strengthen-commitment-to-personal-data-protection>). It succeeds the CNIL, [whose members were installed in October 2010](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>).

**Standing.** The data protection rules are Book V of the Code du numérique, [Law no. 2017-20, promulgated on 20 April 2018](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>). It [replaced Law no. 2009-09 of 22 May 2009](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>) and was [amended by Law no. 2020-35 of 6 January 2021](<https://fraternite.bj/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-au-benin-ce-que-dit-la-loi-du-06-janvier-2021/>). In September 2026 [La Nation treated the Code as the framework governing personal data processing](<https://www.lanation.bj/societe/societe-votre-patron-peut-il-lire-vos-messages-whatsapp-professionnels>).

**Regulations.** [Decree 2022-107 of 16 February 2022](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2022-107/>), taken under the Code, authorises the interior ministry's national video-protection data processing. [Decree 2020-187 of 11 March 2020](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2020-187/>), also taken under it, authorises the national police to collect traveller identity and fingerprint data at borders.

**Application.** For 2025, [La Nation's account of the APDP's annual report gives 907 files examined, 793 validated, 64 inspection missions and 20 complaints treated](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-en-2025-au-benin-lapdp-dresse-un-bilan-sous-le-signe-de-la-conformite>). The authority counted [347 decisions from 2016 to 15 December 2020](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>), among them a 2020 injunction to a controller to guarantee a complainant's right of deletion.

**Last twelve months.** The [swearing-in of seventeen APDP officers on 14 September 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7230/17-apdp-benin-officers-sworn-in-to-strengthen-commitment-to-personal-data-protection>) was done under Article 468(2) of the Code. The APDP's president [spoke at a regulators' forum in Johannesburg on 1 to 3 September 2026](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/ia-et-flux-transfrontaliers-en-afrique-lappel-de-lapdp-benin-a-une-gouvernance-fiable-des-donnees>).
