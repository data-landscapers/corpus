# Ethiopia

## General law

**Authority.** The proclamation [designates the Ethiopian Communications Authority as the regulator](<https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law>). In November 2025 the Authority, whose mandate was extended from telecom regulation to data protection, [was enforcing the law and expanding its staffing and technical capacity](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>), its Director General Balcha Reba said.

**Standing.** Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, the country's first comprehensive personal data protection law, was [approved on 4 April 2024 and published in the Federal Negarit Gazette on 24 July 2024](<https://www.aln.africa/ethiopia-new-data-protection-law>). It [took effect on publication](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>).

**Regulations.** In November 2025 [four directives were expected to be approved in the coming months](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>), and delays in issuing them had slowed implementation.

**Application.** The Director General said in November 2025 that the Authority had [received about 10 requests to transfer data abroad and approved them as a temporary measure](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>), and that data processors and collectors now need its permission.

**Last twelve months.** At a workshop co-hosted with Huawei in November 2025, Authority officials [previewed a digital portal for registering data controllers and processors](<https://shega.co/news/one-year-on-ethiopia-s-personal-data-protection-law-faces-a-slow-path-to-enforcement>).
