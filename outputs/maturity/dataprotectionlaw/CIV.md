# Côte d'Ivoire

## General law

**Authority.** [Decree no. 2015-79 names ARTCI](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>) as the body that receives declarations, rules on authorisation requests within one month and imposes withdrawals and fines under the law. [ARTCI was handling compliance and authorisation requests in July 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642985/economie/ntic/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-certinum-entre-en-service-pour-accelerer-la-conformite-numerique-en-cote-divoire>).

**Standing.** Law no. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013 on personal data protection is the general law, and [the 2015 decree made under it sets filing fees, prior authorisations and fines of up to FCFA 500 million on repeat breach](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>). On 18 May 2026 [the digital minister cited the 2013 law as the country's existing framework](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>).

**Regulations.** [Decree no. 2015-79 of 4 February 2015](<https://www.artci.ci/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/decret_2015_79.pdf>), signed by the President, sets the filing, authorisation, withdrawal and fine-recovery procedures under the law.

**Application.** The minister listed [compliance control operations carried out in 2022 and national awareness campaigns launched from 2015](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>). In July 2026 [ARTCI was handling about 100 paper compliance and authorisation files each month](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642985/economie/ntic/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-certinum-entre-en-service-pour-accelerer-la-conformite-numerique-en-cote-divoire>).

**Last twelve months.** On 2 July 2026 [ARTCI launched Certinum, an online platform for compliance and processing-authorisation requests](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642985/economie/ntic/protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-certinum-entre-en-service-pour-accelerer-la-conformite-numerique-en-cote-divoire>), with the Treasury and ONECI the first two bodies to use it. The minister said [a national register of data protection correspondents was created in 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>).
