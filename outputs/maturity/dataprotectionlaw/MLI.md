# Mali

## General law

**Authority.** Article 20 of the law [institutes the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel (APDP) as an independent administrative authority of 15 members](<https://primature.ml/apdp-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). It [sat in ordinary session on 3 August 2026 with all commissioners present](<https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/>) and [runs regional offices in Kayes, Sikasso, Ségou and Mopti](<https://techgriot.co/english/africa/2026/09/mali-and-benin-deepen-cooperation-on-data-protection/>).

**Standing.** Law no. 2013-015 on the protection of personal data was [adopted by the National Assembly on 9 May 2013 and promulgated on 21 May 2013](<https://primature.ml/apdp-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/>). On 3 August 2026 the APDP's president [cited the law, as amended, as the basis for the authority's sanctions](<https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/>).

**Regulations.** [Decree no. 2024-0300/PT-RM of 14 May 2024](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2024/mali-jo-2024-11.pdf>), made under the law, sets the fees payable to the APDP for declarations, authorisations and certification, and exempts non-profit public services.

**Application.** For 2025 the APDP's annual report, as summarised by Sahel Tribune, counts [fines totalling 22 million FCFA and 51 complaints received, of which 28 were handled and four referred to the cybercrime prosecutor](<https://saheltribune.com/donnees-personnelles-lalerte-du-regulateur-malien/>). The fines include [5 million FCFA on a Bamako clinic for obstructing a check of its video surveillance](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/apdp-mali_sanction-apdp-amende-de-5-millions-de-f-activity-7358807138284032001-tkD3>) and [5 million FCFA on Société Aminata Konaté SARL for refusing a verification mission](<https://malijet.com/les_faits_divers_au_mali/303061-sanction-pour-non-respect-des-donnees-personnelles--penalite-de-.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The APDP [opened an extraordinary session on 6 October 2025 to review about 2,000 declaration files](<https://fr.apanews.net/community/mali-lapdp-examine-2-000-dossiers-et-un-projet-darrete/>) and [opened a regional office at Ségou on 4 December 2025](<https://lessor.ml/posts/autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-la-region-de-segou-a-son-antenne-6932a107b56bd>). Its session of 3 August 2026 [had over 1,000 files to examine](<https://orbiteinfo.com/mali-lapdp-ouvre-sa-2e-session-2026-avec-1-000-dossiers-au-programme/>).
