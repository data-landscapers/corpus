# Lesotho

## General law

**Authority.** The Data Protection Act has a Data Protection Commission under it. Cenfri, in an article published on 23 March 2026, says [the Commission has remained unappointed](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

**Standing.** Lesotho's general law is the Data Protection Act, which Cenfri dates to 2011. Cenfri says, in the article it published on 23 March 2026, that [the Act has been law for over a decade](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

**Regulations.** The nearest dated position is Cenfri's, published on 23 March 2026: [the Data Protection Act has been law for over a decade](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

**Application.** The nearest dated position concerns the body under the Act: Cenfri says, in its article of 23 March 2026, that [the Data Protection Commission has remained unappointed](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated position is Cenfri's article of 23 March 2026, which says that [the Act has been law for over a decade and that its Commission has remained unappointed](<https://cenfri.org/articles/african-data-governance-reform-in-an-era-of-reform/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No regulation made under the Act and no decision applying it has been found, nor is any event in the twelve months to 30 September 2026. The source that says the Commission is unappointed gives no date for that position beyond its own publication on 23 March 2026.
