# Mauritius

## General law

**Authority.** The [Data Protection Office has been operational since 16 February 2009](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) and enforces the Data Protection Act 2017. [Drudeisha Madhub is the Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>).

**Standing.** Mauritius [adopted its current data protection regime in 2017](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>). The Office [reports on its enforcement of the Data Protection Act 2017 during 2024](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>).

**Regulations.** Controllers and processors register under the [Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 2020](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). Registrations reached 21,219 controllers and 1,157 processors by 31 December 2024. On 29 October 2026 the Minister [said 23,000 of some 60,000 entities required to register had done so](<https://lexpress.mu/node/564216>).

**Application.** For 2024 the Office [reports 179 new complaints and 39 closed](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). [It also counts 105 breaches notified, 112 cross-border transfers authorised and 12 rejected, and four inspections](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). In May 2026 the Commissioner [said her office issues 3,000 enforcement actions a year](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula>). Of these, 99 per cent are resolved without prosecution.

**Last twelve months.** On 17 June 2026 the Minister [made regulations requiring every controller to designate a data protection officer](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf>), from 1 January 2027. In May 2026 the Commissioner said Mauritius [is going for the EU adequacy assessment in 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>). She said [amendments to bring in administrative fines are being introduced](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula>). In September 2026 the Office [told management companies to register as controllers and designate their own officers](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Documents/Advice%20to%20%20MCs%20on%20Registration.pdf>).
