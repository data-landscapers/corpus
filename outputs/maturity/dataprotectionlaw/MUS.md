# Mauritius

## General law

**Authority.** The [Data Protection Office has been operational since 16 February 2009](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>) and enforces the Data Protection Act 2017. [Drudeisha Madhub is the Data Protection Commissioner](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>).

**Standing.** Mauritius [adopted its current data protection regime in 2017](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>), after a first law in 2004 that was partly implemented in 2009. The Office [reports on its enforcement of the Data Protection Act 2017 during 2024](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>).

**Regulations.** Controllers and processors register under the [Data Protection (Fees) Regulations 2020](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). Registrations since 1 August 2020 reached 21,219 controllers and 1,157 processors by 31 December 2024.

**Application.** For January to December 2024 the Office [reports 179 new complaints, 39 complaints closed, 105 personal data breaches notified, 112 cross-border transfers authorised and 12 rejected, and four compliance inspections](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/AnnualReports/265-26-DPO%20AR%202024-SOFT%20COPY.pdf>). The Commissioner [said in May 2026 that her office issues 3,000 enforcement actions a year](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula>), 99 per cent of them resolved without prosecution.

**Last twelve months.** The Minister [made regulations on 17 June 2026 requiring every controller to designate a data protection officer](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/117_The-Data-Protection_Designation-Tasks-and-Position-of-Data-Protection-Officers_Reg.pdf>), to come into operation on 1 January 2027. The Commissioner [said in May 2026 that Mauritius is going for the EU adequacy assessment in 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>), and [amendments to bring in administrative fines are being introduced](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/world-bank-african-dpas-outline-formula>). In July 2026 the ICT Minister [spoke of forthcoming amendments to the Act](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-to-join-america-india-connect-subsea-cable-programme-announces-pm-ramgoolam/>).
