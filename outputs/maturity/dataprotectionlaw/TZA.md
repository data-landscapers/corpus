# Tanzania

## General law

**Authority.** The Act [establishes the Personal Data Protection Commission as a body corporate, with a seven-member Board and a Director General](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>). The Commission [delivered a ruling on a complaint on 19 August 2025](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/instagram-photo-triggers-payment-of-fine/>), and its Director General, Dr Emmanuel Mkilia, [announced on-site inspections in Dodoma on 28 August 2026](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>).

**Standing.** The [Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 (Act No. 11 of 2022, Chapter 44) commenced on 1 May 2023 and applies to Mainland Tanzania and, for union matters, Zanzibar](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/media/media/THE_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_ACT.pdf>).

**Regulations.** The [Personal Data Protection (Collection and Processing) Regulations, Government Notice No. 449C of 2023, apply alongside the Act](<https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/notification-obligations-arising-from-personal-dat>). The Commission's ruling of 19 August 2025 [applied regulation 17, on erasure, and regulation 18, on children's data in commercial advertisements](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/instagram-photo-triggers-payment-of-fine/>).

**Application.** On 19 August 2025 the Commission [ruled in Complaint No. PDPC/CMP/002/2025 against the operator of an Instagram business page, awarding the complainant TZS 20 million in damages, imposing a fine of TZS 5 million and ordering a newborn's photographs deleted within 14 days](<https://fbattorneys.co.tz/instagram-photo-triggers-payment-of-fine/>). Its preliminary assessments, reported on 28 August 2026, [found inaccurate registration data and unlawful transfers of data abroad](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>).

**Last twelve months.** On 8 January 2026 the Minister of Communication and Information Technology [gave unregistered institutions a final three months to register and told the Commission to prepare nationwide compliance audits](<https://www.pdpc.go.tz/core/en/minister-kairuki-issues-final-three-month-ultimatum-on-personal-data-registration2026-02-02-133701/>). On 11 February the Commission [announced inspections from 9 April](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-to-begin-data-protection-inspections-in-april-5356792>), on 12 June it [gave institutions with incomplete registration seven days to finish](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/wananchi-waonywa-kutoa-taarifa-binafsi-kwa-taasisi-zisizosajiliwa-5494426>), and [on-site inspections began on 31 August](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/data-protection-regulator-begins-compliance-crackdown-5574710>).
