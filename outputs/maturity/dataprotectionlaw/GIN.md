# Guinea

## General law

**Authority.** [The 2016 law defines an Autorité de Protection and names no constituted body or appointed members](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). [On 20 June 2024 the CNT's commissions debated a bill to establish the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel, with the designation of members and their mandates contested and plenary adoption pending](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>).

**Standing.** [Loi L/2016/037/AN on cybersecurity and personal data protection was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by President Alpha Condé in 2016, and covers personal data processing by individuals, companies and public institutions](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l2016037an_relative_a_la_cybersecurite_et_protection_des_do.pdf>). [Part Two of the text is a full personal data protection regime](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). [In October 2025 the ministry's secretary-general said Guinea has put in place the law on cybersecurity and protection of personal data](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>).

**Regulations.** [Article 5 of the law sends processing declarations to the competent authority "designated by regulation"](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Application.** [The law gives the Autorité de Protection the functions of receiving declarations, granting authorisations, approving transfers, investigating and issuing guidelines](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 19 November 2025 Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said Guinea is strengthening its regulatory framework with a new data-protection law](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>). [The ministry reported for 2025 that texts on personal data protection are in progress](<https://www.africaguinee.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Realisations_MPTEN_2025_compressed.pdf>).
