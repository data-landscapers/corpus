# Guinea

## General law

**Authority.** [The 2016 law defines an Autorité de Protection and names no constituted body or appointed members](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>). [On 20 June 2024 the CNT's commissions debated a bill to establish the Autorité de Protection des Données à caractère Personnel, with the designation of members and their mandates contested and plenary adoption pending](<https://cnt.gov.gn/apdp/>).

**Standing.** [Loi L/2016/037/AN on cybersecurity and personal data protection was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by President Alpha Condé in 2016, and covers personal data processing by individuals, companies and public institutions](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/loi_l2016037an_relative_a_la_cybersecurite_et_protection_des_do.pdf>). [Part Two of the text is a full personal data protection regime](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Regulations.** [Article 5 of the law sends processing declarations to the competent authority "designated by regulation"](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Application.** [The law gives the Autorité de Protection the functions of receiving declarations, granting authorisations, approving transfers, investigating and issuing guidelines](<https://assembleeguinee.org/loi-ndeg-l-2016-relative-la-cybersecurite-et-la-protection-des-donnees-caractere-personnel-en>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 19 November 2025 Minister Rose Pola Pricemou said Guinea is strengthening its regulatory framework with a new data-protection law](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. The date the 2016 law came into force, any regulation made under it, any decision applying it and the constitution of its authority. The stage and timetable of the new law announced in November 2025.
