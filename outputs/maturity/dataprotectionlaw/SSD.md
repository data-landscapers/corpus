# South Sudan

## General law

**Authority.** The draft bill, when passed, [will establish a Data Protection Commission](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>) to safeguard the personal data of online users, Radio Tamazuj reported on 20 November 2025.

**Standing.** South Sudan has no data protection law: the World Bank's identity diagnostic, published on 1 May 2026, states that [the country has no national data protection or privacy law](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>). A bill is being drafted. Digital security experts [finalised what Radio Tamazuj calls the first Data Protection Policy Bill in October 2025](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>), with the National Communication Authority and civil society, and a post dated 5 May 2026 reports [a two-day workshop of over 30 stakeholders to review and validate the Data Protection Bill, 2025 (Version 1.0)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>).

**Regulations.** Koneta's Executive Director [said in a release dated 17 October 2025 that existing ICT regulations are limited](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-partners-with-koneta-hub-to-drive-dialogue-on-data-protection-law-in-south-sudan>).

**Application.** The World Bank diagnostic states that South Sudan [has no national data protection and privacy regime](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Paradigm Initiative and Koneta Hub [convened lawmakers, civil society organisations and officials in Juba to review the draft bill and seek a parliamentary commitment to table it](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-partners-with-koneta-hub-to-drive-dialogue-on-data-protection-law-in-south-sudan>), in a release dated 17 October 2025. The ICT minister, Ateny Wek Ateny, [said in December 2025 that the government would introduce the bill in 2026](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister>), drafted jointly by the ministries of information and justice. The validation workshop [was reported on 5 May 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>), with members of parliament, civil society, the UNMISS legal unit and the National Communication Authority taking part.

***Also on file***. [Article 22 of the Transitional Constitution of 2011](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>); the [National Civil Registry and Identity Regulations of 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

***Not found***. Any bill tabled in parliament or any law enacted. No regulation and no decision on a controller has been found, and what has happened to the bill since May 2026 is not held.
