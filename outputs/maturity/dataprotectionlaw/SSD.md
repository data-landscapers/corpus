# South Sudan

## General law

**Authority.** The draft bill [would set up a Data Protection Commission](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>) to guard the personal data of online users. Radio Tamazuj reported this on 20 November 2025.

**Standing.** South Sudan has no data protection law. The World Bank's identity diagnostic of 1 May 2026 says [the country has no national data protection or privacy law](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>). A bill is being drafted. In October 2025 digital security experts [finished what Radio Tamazuj calls the first Data Protection Policy Bill](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>). A post of 5 May 2026 reports [a two-day workshop of over 30 people to validate the Data Protection Bill, 2025](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>). On 8 September 2026 the ICT ministry's undersecretary [said the bill was still in preparation](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/south-sudan-data-governance-workshop-juba/>).

**Regulations.** Koneta's Executive Director [said in a release dated 17 October 2025 that existing ICT regulations are limited](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-partners-with-koneta-hub-to-drive-dialogue-on-data-protection-law-in-south-sudan>).

**Application.** The World Bank diagnostic states that South Sudan [has no national data protection and privacy regime](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 Paradigm Initiative and Koneta Hub [met lawmakers and officials in Juba to seek a commitment to table the bill](<https://paradigmhq.org/press-release-paradigm-initiative-partners-with-koneta-hub-to-drive-dialogue-on-data-protection-law-in-south-sudan>). In December 2025 the ICT minister, Ateny Wek Ateny, [said the bill would be introduced in 2026](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-to-get-data-protection-bill-in-2026-says-minister>). The validation workshop [was reported on 5 May 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/digital-rights-frontlines_dataprotection-digitalrights-cybersecurity-activity-7457423027161620480-l2o_>). At a Juba workshop in September 2026 the deputy minister [urged the legislature to speed the bill's passage](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/south-sudan-data-governance-workshop-juba/>).

***Also on file***. [Article 22 of the Transitional Constitution of 2011](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/feature-digital-rights-ignorance-puts-south-sudanese-at-risk>); the [National Civil Registry and Identity Regulations of 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

***Not found***. Any bill tabled in parliament or any law enacted. No regulation and no decision on a controller was found.
