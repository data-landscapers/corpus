# Malawi

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Crime Management System is [the Malawi Police Service's own platform, launched in partnership with UNDP](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/dockets-data-malawi-police-and-undp-malawi-advance-digital-transformation-crime-management>), which said so in July 2026. The earlier case management system for sexual and gender-based violence [was rolled out with support from the Spotlight Initiative, funded by the European Union from 2019 to 2023](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-08/final_spotlight_initiative_malawi_cumulative_report_revised.pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** The system [covers a case from the moment it is reported until its final disposal](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/dockets-data-malawi-police-and-undp-malawi-advance-digital-transformation-crime-management>), by UNDP's account of July 2026. In 2022 [officers entered cases into a crime record system combined with the gender-based violence one](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-08/final_spotlight_initiative_malawi_cumulative_report_revised.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** At a conference in September 2024 the police [said it was working towards digitising its processes, a crime management system among them, and UNDP's assessment found most justice-sector processes still on paper](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/e-justice_national_conference_malawi_report_pub.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 29 July 2026 the police and UNDP [opened a three-day training on the system in Lilongwe, for officers from across the country ahead of its introduction in stations](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/dockets-data-malawi-police-and-undp-malawi-advance-digital-transformation-crime-management>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In 2022 the combined system [was piloted in six districts: Mzimba, Nkhata Bay, Ntchisi, Dowa, Nsanje and Machinga](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-08/final_spotlight_initiative_malawi_cumulative_report_revised.pdf>). In July 2026 the Crime Management System [was being introduced across stations and formations, with the trained officers as its early adopters](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/dockets-data-malawi-police-and-undp-malawi-advance-digital-transformation-crime-management>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Statistics: [RapidPro for police reporting of gender-based violence data, a national data observatory hub with six district hubs at the National Statistical Office, and the gender ministry's web-based information system](<https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2024-08/final_spotlight_initiative_malawi_cumulative_report_revised.pdf>). Identification: [a vendor-provided fingerprint identification system](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/e-justice_national_conference_malawi_report_pub.pdf>). For the public: [a feature of the crime management system through which victims report incidents](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/e-justice_national_conference_malawi_report_pub.pdf>), and [cashless payment of police fees and fines, set in the 2026-27 budget statement to start on 1 April 2026](<https://finance.gov.mw/documents/uploads/2026-03/Doc%201_2026-27%20Budget%20Policy%20Statement_0.pdf>). Single-function: [electronic payment of traffic fines through banks and mobile money, scheduled to start in the week of 4 September 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2>). Surveillance: [a camera network proposed for Blantyre by the communications regulator in September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/28/macra-and-blantyre-city-assembly-explore-ai-powered-smart-city-surveillance/>), and [a public CCTV network being deployed in Lilongwe](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-backs-ai-crime-cameras/G98Yd7LGnmzvX2PD>).

***Not held***. No source names or counts a station using the Crime Management System, and whether the system piloted in six districts in 2022 stayed in use is not stated. How a record is entered in the new system, a current plan naming it and who pays for it are not held, and nothing held describes rural stations.
