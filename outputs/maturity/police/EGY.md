***Also on file***. Public: the interior ministry's [biometric verification and authentication platform](<https://moi.gov.eg/news/getdetails?newsId=ac472335-1f97-4862-b559-87b1c87b554f>), in trial operation on 14 February 2026. Ten [mobile technology centres issuing the criminal status certificate](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5472804.aspx>) had been handed over by 18 January 2026. The cabinet runs a [unified government complaints system](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3569705>), and there is a [Public Prosecution e-portal](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/98882>). Surveillance: the [Cairo CCTV network](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>) and the city's [integrated command and control centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>), with the emergency number 112. Enabling: the communications ministry's [digital training for the law enforcement system](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/files/ict-sector/>), which had reached 13,493 people by the end of 2023. Identification: the [Ministry of Interior's criminal records system](<https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/egypt>). Statistics: the Public Prosecution's [digital reporting and statistics system](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Press_Room/Press_Releases/98882>), named in December 2025.
