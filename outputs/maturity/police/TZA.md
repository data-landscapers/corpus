# Tanzania

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Minister for Home Affairs told Parliament in May 2024 that [the ministry had installed the digital case file management information system itself, in carrying out the Criminal Justice Commission's recommendations](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>). The ministry's 2024/25 budget carried [TZS 40.5 billion for strengthening the ICT system, installing the biometric system and buying police radios](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>).

**Records made digitally.** The case file. The minister said in May 2024 that the ministry [has installed a digital case file management information system, to be connected with the criminal justice institutions](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>).

**How the record is made.** By April 2024 the regional Central Police Stations were [connected over the national broadband backbone and linked to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>).

**Last twelve months.** In May 2024 the minister said [police stations in other regions were still being connected to the system](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The minister said that by April 2024 [the Central Police Stations in 23 regions had been connected](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>).

Single-function: the [Trafficking in Persons Management Information System](<https://www.parliament.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1779711628-HOTUBA%20YA%20BAJETI%20YA%20WIZARA%20YA%20MAMBO%20YA%20NDANI%20YA%20NCHI%20KWA%20MWAKA%202026-2027_compressed.pdf>), the [system for supervising private security companies](<https://dailynews.co.tz/state-allocates-40-5bn-for-installation-of-abis-strengthening-ict/>) and the [traffic police's vehicle tracking system](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1755675635-Crime%20and%20Traffic%20Incidents%20Statistics%20January%20-%20June,%202024.pdf>). Personnel: the [electronic performance appraisal system](<https://www.parliament.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1779711628-HOTUBA%20YA%20BAJETI%20YA%20WIZARA%20YA%20MAMBO%20YA%20NDANI%20YA%20NCHI%20KWA%20MWAKA%202026-2027_compressed.pdf>). ***Not held***. Whether a station enters the case file when the case is opened, how many stations there are in all, and whether the link to the prosecutor carries the case file. No plan or funding source for the system, and nothing dated after May 2024.
