# Namibia

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Safety and Security [launched the E-Policing eco-system, as it names the force's system, in 2010 at a cost of N$22 million](<https://neweralive.na/certificate-conduct-issued-weeks/>), the minister said on 8 July 2016.

**Records made digitally.** In July 2016 the eco-system [comprised 16 databases, case docket management among them](<https://neweralive.na/certificate-conduct-issued-weeks/>). A paper of May 2022 says e-policing in Namibia [records, stores and shares police information electronically](<https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360748631_Leveraging_Technology_to_Enhance_Effective_Electronic_Policing_in_Developing_Countries>).

**How the record is made.** A 2022 University of Namibia study of police stations in the Omusati region found that [officers there record by hand, first in the station crime register and then in paper files kept in cabinets or boxes](<https://digital.unam.edu.na/xmlui/handle/11070.1/18365>), and that [no digital record system was in place](<https://digital.unam.edu.na/xmlui/handle/11070.1/18365>) in those stations.

**Last twelve months.** In May 2022 officers at Okahandja police station [saw storing the occurrence book electronically as duplication of data](<https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360748631_Leveraging_Technology_to_Enhance_Effective_Electronic_Policing_in_Developing_Countries>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In May 2022 implementation was [immature, because not all units of the Namibian Police had been introduced to the system](<https://www.researchgate.net/publication/360748631_Leveraging_Technology_to_Enhance_Effective_Electronic_Policing_in_Developing_Countries>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Enabling: the police force's [digital mobile radios](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Vote-39-Home-Affairs.pdf>), acquired in 2024/25 to replace the analogue system. Surveillance: the planned [Smart City Project](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Vote-39-Home-Affairs.pdf>) of cameras in Windhoek and Swakopmund, as at May 2025, and the [CCTV coverage](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Vote-06-Ministry-of-Safety-and-Security.pdf>) deployed to some facilities by April 2018. Identification: [Forensic Science Services](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Vote-39-Home-Affairs.pdf>), which processed 2,898 cases in 2024/25, the [Namibian Police Criminal Record Bureau](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Vote-39-Home-Affairs.pdf>) and [N-ABIS](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Vote-06-Ministry-of-Safety-and-Security.pdf>), the force's biometric identification system, as the ministry named it in April 2018.

***Not held***. No source says which stations enter reports or cases in the e-policing system, how many do, or whether it is past a pilot; the one study of stations, in the Omusati region in 2022, found none doing so there.
