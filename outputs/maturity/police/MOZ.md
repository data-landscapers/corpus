# Mozambique

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** An information technology plan for the Polícia da República de Moçambique [had not been achieved at all by October 2007, its needs assessment waiting on a bilateral funding agreement with Spain](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>), UNDP's evaluators stated.

**Records made digitally.** The evaluation mission, visiting stations and commands in September 2007, verified [the almost complete absence of digital records of autos, case files or any other procedure across the force](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>).

**How the record is made.** In 2007 [piles of paper served as case file archives, and stations lacked paper and typewriter ribbons](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>), the mission found.

**Last twelve months.** Club of Mozambique reported on 23 August 2024 [a public security management information system for the Ministry of the Interior, described as nationwide in scope](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-to-have-a-public-security-management-information-system-watch/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In September 2007 the use of information technology [was non-existent at district and police station level, and most stations the mission visited had a computer that did not work or outdated typewriters](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identification: the justice ministry's [criminal records registry](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-74-of-legal-entity-registration-applications-now-submitted-electronically-minister-says/>). Single-function: [InfoViolência](<https://www.spotlightinitiative.org/news/innovative-data-tool-strengthens-services-gender-based-violence-survivors-mozambique>), the interior ministry's register of gender-based violence cases. Public: the [Portal do utente](<https://utente.srn.gov.mz>) of the Serviço de Registos e Notariado. Personnel: the [police salary payment system](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>), as at 2007. Enabling: the [computers at model police stations](<https://erc.undp.org/evaluation/documents/download/3514>), as at 2007.

***Not held***. Nothing dated after 2007 is held on how stations record, so the owner, plan and finance of a station records system and the last twelve months are not held. No fact says that stations enter reports or cases in the gender-based violence register or in the public security information system.
