# South Sudan

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNDP [supported the setup in Juba of a unified case management system](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>) for the courts, the justice ministry and the South Sudan National Police Service, its rule of law report on 2021 states.

**Records made digitally.** UNDP reports that [10,836 cases, 2,508 of them brought by female claimants, were recorded in 2021](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>) through the system, which it names the Case Monitoring Information Management System, set up for the judiciary, the Ministry of Justice and Constitutional Affairs and the police service.

**How the record is made.** The police service [uses a paper-based system for handling crime reporting](<https://library.afribary.com/works/development-of-web-based-application-for-crime-report-handling-in-police-force-a-case-of-jubek-state-south-sudan-national-police-services>), with crime files misplaced or lost from police shelves, the abstract of a thesis listed on 7 June 2026 states.

**Last twelve months.** The thesis listed on 7 June 2026 [describes a web application for crime reports that its researcher built and tested for Jubek State](<https://library.afribary.com/works/development-of-web-based-application-for-crime-report-handling-in-police-force-a-case-of-jubek-state-south-sudan-national-police-services>); the application is the researcher's own.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** UNDP's report on 2021 [places the case management system in Juba only](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Statistics: the [crime data analysis centre at Buluk police division](<https://unmiss.unmissions.org/en/news/unmiss-police-hands-over-building-serve-crime-data-analysis-facility-south-sudanese>) in Juba. Identification: the [forensic laboratory equipment for digital forensic investigations](<https://www.eyeradio.org/15-police-officers-trained-in-china-to-operate-new-forensic-lab-equipment-igp/>) and the scheduled [electronic fingerprint technology](<https://www.eyeradio.org/15-police-officers-trained-in-china-to-operate-new-forensic-lab-equipment-igp/>). Public: the [Emergency Call Centres in Juba and Wau](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/news/crime-reporting-speed-dial-responsiveness-wau-police-emergency-call-centre>). Surveillance: the [Smart City project](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-police-to-adopt-advanced-surveillance-under-smart-city-plan/>) and the [security cameras on Juba's major streets](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-police-to-adopt-advanced-surveillance-under-smart-city-plan/>). Personnel: the [headcount and biometric registration of the organised forces](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/06/24/government-launches-biometric-registration-exercise-for-organised-forces/>).

***Not held***. No count of police stations using the Juba system is held, nor how many of its cases the police entered or whether the file passes digitally to the court. Nothing on the system is dated after 2021, and the thesis gives no year of its own.
