# South Sudan

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** [In 2021 UNDP supported a unified case management system in Juba](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>). [It serves the courts, the justice ministry and the police service](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>).

**Records made digitally.** [UNDP says 10,836 cases were recorded in the system in 2021, 2,508 of them from women](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>). [It calls it the Case Monitoring Information Management System](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>). [A crime management system for incidents and reports was handed to the police ICT directorate in Juba on 5 December 2025](<https://cand.vn/canh-sat-viet-nam-thiet-ke-thanh-cong-he-thong-quan-ly-toi-pham-tai-nam-sudan-post790283.html>).

**How the record is made.** [A thesis listed on 7 June 2026 says the police handle crime reports on paper](<https://library.afribary.com/works/development-of-web-based-application-for-crime-report-handling-in-police-force-a-case-of-jubek-state-south-sudan-national-police-services>). [It says crime files go missing from police shelves](<https://library.afribary.com/works/development-of-web-based-application-for-crime-report-handling-in-police-force-a-case-of-jubek-state-south-sudan-national-police-services>).

**Last twelve months.** [On 5 December 2025 a UN mission police officer handed a crime management system he had built to the police ICT directorate in Juba](<https://cand.vn/canh-sat-viet-nam-thiet-ke-thanh-cong-he-thong-quan-ly-toi-pham-tai-nam-sudan-post790283.html>). [Bringing it into official nationwide use was still to come](<https://cand.vn/canh-sat-viet-nam-thiet-ke-thanh-cong-he-thong-quan-ly-toi-pham-tai-nam-sudan-post790283.html>). [A thesis listed on 7 June 2026 describes a crime-report web application its researcher built and tested for Jubek State](<https://library.afribary.com/works/development-of-web-based-application-for-crime-report-handling-in-police-force-a-case-of-jubek-state-south-sudan-national-police-services>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** UNDP's report on 2021 [places the case management system in Juba only](<https://rolhr.undp.org/annualreport/2021/africa/south-sudan.html>).

***Also on file***. Statistics: the [crime data analysis centre at Buluk police division](<https://unmiss.unmissions.org/en/news/unmiss-police-hands-over-building-serve-crime-data-analysis-facility-south-sudanese>) in Juba. Identification: the [forensic laboratory equipment for digital forensic investigations](<https://www.eyeradio.org/15-police-officers-trained-in-china-to-operate-new-forensic-lab-equipment-igp/>) and the scheduled [electronic fingerprint technology](<https://www.eyeradio.org/15-police-officers-trained-in-china-to-operate-new-forensic-lab-equipment-igp/>). Public: the [Emergency Call Centres in Juba and Wau](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/news/crime-reporting-speed-dial-responsiveness-wau-police-emergency-call-centre>). Surveillance: the [Smart City project](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-police-to-adopt-advanced-surveillance-under-smart-city-plan/>) and the [security cameras on Juba's major streets](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-police-to-adopt-advanced-surveillance-under-smart-city-plan/>). Personnel: the [headcount and biometric registration of the organised forces](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/06/24/government-launches-biometric-registration-exercise-for-organised-forces/>).

***Not found***. No count of police stations using either Juba system has been found. Nor is it known how many cases the police entered, or whether the file passes digitally to the court.
