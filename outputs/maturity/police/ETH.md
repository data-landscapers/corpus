# Ethiopia

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Justice says CRIMS, as it names its Criminal Records Information Management System, [is being developed by the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute for the ministry, the Federal Police Commission and the Addis Ababa Police Commission to work in jointly, steered by a coordinating committee](<https://justice.gov.et/en/news/development-of-technology-to-digitalize-the-criminal-justice-system-is-underway-officials-announced/>), in an item published on 20 January 2026.

**Records made digitally.** The system [is under development and not yet in use, and is to record digitally everything from the crime report through investigation, charge, trial and sentence](<https://justice.gov.et/en/news/development-of-technology-to-digitalize-the-criminal-justice-system-is-underway-officials-announced/>), the ministry said in January 2026.

**How the record is made.** Its modules [cover report intake, investigation, juvenile offenders, offence type, biometric data, and prosecutors' and courts' decisions](<https://justice.gov.et/en/news/development-of-technology-to-digitalize-the-criminal-justice-system-is-underway-officials-announced/>), on the ministry's account of January 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In January 2026 the ministry [reported a coordinating-committee forum, with the minister and senior leaders of the three bodies, at which the system was said to be in development](<https://justice.gov.et/en/news/development-of-technology-to-digitalize-the-criminal-justice-system-is-underway-officials-announced/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In August 2023 the ministry [was training federal investigating police and prosecutors, about 100 in all, in order to launch the system widely](<https://justice.gov.et/en/news/training-aimed-at-broadly-launching-the-case-record-information-management-system-crims-is-being-provided/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: the [Smart Police Station](<https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjd9jgnrdpko>), one unmanned pilot station in the Bole district of Addis Ababa launched on 9 February 2026, and the Federal Police's [EFP App](<https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/ethiopias-emerging-ai-in-criminal-justice-regulation-tools-and-insights-ai-atlas>) for reporting crimes. Surveillance: [CCTV surveillance systems](<https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijriss/articles/the-coloniality-of-chinese-surveillance-technologies-in-africa/>) and [communications monitoring infrastructure](<https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijriss/articles/the-coloniality-of-chinese-surveillance-technologies-in-africa/>).

***Not held***. No station, place or way of making the record is given for the system, and no launch is recorded since the 2023 training. No plan or source of finance is stated.
