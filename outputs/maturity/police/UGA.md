# Uganda

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Uganda Police Force's ICT Directorate [developed the Crime Records Management System itself](<https://upf.go.ug/police-develops-crime-records-management-system/>), and the force [resolved in October 2022 to embark on the Electronic Policing Information System, ePIS](<https://jlos.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/THE-JLOS-BULLETIN-06-MAY-2024_FInal.pdf>). A UNODC consultancy notice of 2023 [gives the ePIS programme four interventions: business process reform, automation of case management, enabling infrastructure and digital skilling](<https://untalent.org/jobs/international-consultant-to-review-and-assess-crime-management-business-processes-in-the-uganda-police-force-upf>).

**Records made digitally.** The case file. The force said in December 2017 that its crime records system [processes and tracks the progress of case files](<https://upf.go.ug/police-develops-crime-records-management-system/>). A journal article of June 2024 says ePIS is [to manage all cases registered and investigated by the police from inception to conclusion](<https://iacajournal.org/articles/10.36745/ijca.580>).

**How the record is made.** The UNODC notice of 2023 calls the force's collection, processing and sharing of data [largely manual](<https://untalent.org/jobs/international-consultant-to-review-and-assess-crime-management-business-processes-in-the-uganda-police-force-upf>). The Independent reported in 2021 that [stations had one records officer for case files and detectives kept the files of their own cases](<https://www.independent.co.ug/police-upgrades-digital-crime-record-management-system/>).

**Last twelve months.** In June 2024 ePIS was [in its design phase](<https://iacajournal.org/articles/10.36745/ijca.580>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In December 2017 the crime records system was [being piloted in ten divisions of Kampala Metropolitan](<https://upf.go.ug/police-develops-crime-records-management-system/>). The Independent reported in 2021 that it had been [upgraded and tested in Kampala, with tests in upcountry stations to follow](<https://www.independent.co.ug/police-upgrades-digital-crime-record-management-system/>). The justice sector's bulletin of May 2024 lists [lack of internet connectivity at upcountry police stations](<https://jlos.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/THE-JLOS-BULLETIN-06-MAY-2024_FInal.pdf>) among the obstacles.

***Noted, not assessed***. Surveillance: the [Kampala Safe City CCTV network](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>) and the [National CCTV project](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Police%20Force_27.pdf>). Enabling: [computers for all 186 district police stations, radio communication in 150 of the 186 police districts and divisions, and solar power at 20 facilities, at half year 2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Police%20Force_27.pdf>), and the [National Backbone Infrastructure's last-mile connection of police posts in Kanungu](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/nita/news-and-updates/nita-u-office-president-ministry-ict-and-national-guidance-collaborate-extend>). Single-function: the [Certificate of Good Conduct system](<https://upf.go.ug/police-develops-crime-records-management-system/>) and the [Road Crash Database System](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>). Personnel: the [Human Resource Management System](<https://upf.go.ug/police-develops-crime-records-management-system/>). Public: the [UPF chatbot, announced in September 2026](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609030622.html>), the [999 and 112 emergency and toll-free lines](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/ACR2024-Web.pdf>), the [UPF Mobi app](<https://jlos.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/THE-JLOS-BULLETIN-06-MAY-2024_FInal.pdf>) and [call response centres at 20 sub-county police stations](<https://upf.go.ug/igps-christmas-message-3/>). Identification: the [Integrated Ballistic Identification System](<https://upf.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Official-Annual-Crime-Report-Web-Version.pdf>), the [Criminal Automated Biometric Identification System](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Police%20Force_27.pdf>) and the [Electronic Criminal Records Office](<https://iacajournal.org/articles/10.36745/ijca.580>). Statistics: the [Crime Data Analysis Centre](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Police%20Force_27.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any station outside Kampala Metropolitan enters cases digitally, how many stations the Kampala pilot covered and whether it still runs. No costing, period or funding source for ePIS, and nothing dated in the twelve months to 30 September 2026.
