# Nigeria

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Gombe digital police station of June 2026 was [implemented by Galaxy Backbone with the Nigeria Police Trust Fund](<https://dailytrust.com/gombe-gets-nigerias-first-digital-police-station/>). In September 2025 the Inspector-General said [the police were building the Criminal Database Systems with the support of the Federal Government and partners](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2025/09/25/police-launches-database-to-curb-missing-case-files-others>). Neither source names a budget line.

**Records made digitally.** The case file. In June 2026 Galaxy Backbone said that at Gombe's new digital police station [case files can now be managed digitally on a cloud-based system](<https://dailytrust.com/gombe-gets-nigerias-first-digital-police-station/>). In September 2025 the Inspector-General said the [Criminal Database Systems would capture and track complaints and document every case](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2025/09/25/police-launches-database-to-curb-missing-case-files-others>).

**How the record is made.** In April 2024 Abia State Police Command [trained officers on a computerised crime and incident database](<https://thewhistler.ng/abia-police-command-trains-officers-on-computerised-crime-reporting/>). The database was to run alongside manual record keeping. In June 2021 the Ministry of Police Affairs said [statements and reports at stations were written by hand](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>).

**Last twelve months.** In June 2026 [Gombe State became the first to receive a digital police station](<https://dailytrust.com/gombe-gets-nigerias-first-digital-police-station/>), under a Galaxy Backbone and Police Trust Fund project. Its deputy governor called it a model for modern policing.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** Gombe's is [the first digital police station](<https://dailytrust.com/gombe-gets-nigerias-first-digital-police-station/>), and no other is named. On 25 September 2025 the Inspector-General [opened nationwide training on the new Criminal Database Systems](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2025/09/25/police-launches-database-to-curb-missing-case-files-others>). The trainees are to hold crime data at divisional, zonal and state levels.

***Also on file***. Identification: the [Nigeria Police Crime and Incident Database Centre and the National Criminal Data Fusion Centre](<https://policeaffairs.gov.ng/national-data-fusion-centre-a-game-changer-in-the-crime-management/>), as the ministry named them in December 2024. Also the [National Cybercrime Centre's forensic analysis](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/04/pre-registered-sim-cards-pose-threat-to-national-security-police-assert/>), and a [digital case management solution with a biometric database](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>), in progress in 2019. Public: the [Vigilant App](<https://www.nigeriainfo.fm/news/homepagelagos/police-launch-vigilant-app-for-public-reporting-of-financial-fraud/>), launched on 1 September 2026. Also [Hawk Eye (2017), Rescue Me (2021) and the National Cybercrime Centre's reporting portal](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigerias-police-vigilant-app-raises-questions-duplicate-fraud-reporting/>). The [112 emergency number](<https://www.naltf.gov.ng/shettima-urges-swift-action-as-nigeria-prepares-for-nationwide-rollout-of-112-emergency-line>) was approved and not yet rolled out in September 2026. It has [about 35 Emergency Communications Centres](<https://www.nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/fg-halts-enforcement-of-new-regulations-on-internet-platforms/>). The [Police Public Complaints Committee](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>) was revived in 2021. Surveillance: [Lagos State's CCTV and command and control centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>), and a [CCTV project](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>) being revived in 2021. The [National Command and Control Centre](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>) was announced in December 2019. Enabling: a memorandum of 22 April 2026 to [map and digitalise police stations](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/04/23/fg-ptf-sign-mou-to-map-digitalise-police-stations-nationwide/>). It covers [connected police stations and a national GIS database of stations](<https://thenationonlineng.net/fg-nigeria-police-sign-mou-to-connect-all-police-stations-with-digital-infrastructures-internet-services/>). UNDP gave [computers and solar power to police legal departments in 15 state commands](<https://www.undp.org/nigeria/blog/strengthening-police-accountability-undp-hands-over-ict-and-solar-equipment-nigeria-police-force>). [Korean-funded cybercrime investigation training](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=KR-GOV-010-KR-GOV-051-2026040101698000>) closed in June 2026.

***Not found***. No source found shows a station outside Gombe recording cases digitally. No count of stations using the Criminal Database Systems has been found. No source since 2022 says how stations make their records.
