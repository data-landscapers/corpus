# Nigeria

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Police Affairs' press unit said in June 2021 that [divisional police stations lack basic ICT infrastructure](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>).

**Records made digitally.** The ministry's press unit stated in June 2021 that [statements and reports in police stations are made manually, and that most criminal records of offenders and property across the country are not made electronically](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>). A paper by Jimoh, Wajiga and Garba, published in June 2022, found [criminal records kept by hand in a crime diary or ledger](<https://journals.ui.edu.ng/index.php/uijslictr/article/download/773/681>) at the stations its authors observed.

**How the record is made.** Sources writing to Canada's Immigration and Refugee Board in October 2020 described [statements and police reports written on a standard paper form at the division](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>), with [some records kept electronically at state commands or police headquarters](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>). In June 2021 the ministry said [writing a statement, generating a report for citizens and entering biodata were done manually](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>).

**Last twelve months.** The 2022 paper described [crime reporting and detailing in stations as a manual method](<https://journals.ui.edu.ng/index.php/uijslictr/article/download/773/681>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** A law-firm partner told the Board in October 2020 that [use of computer networks and electronic databases was still very low among police formations](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identification: the [Nigeria Police Crime and Incident Database Centre and the National Criminal Data Fusion Centre](<https://policeaffairs.gov.ng/national-data-fusion-centre-a-game-changer-in-the-crime-management/>), as the Ministry of Police Affairs named them in December 2024; the [National Cybercrime Centre's forensic analysis](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/04/pre-registered-sim-cards-pose-threat-to-national-security-police-assert/>); and a [digital case management solution with a biometric database](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>), being worked on in 2019. Public: the [Vigilant App](<https://www.nigeriainfo.fm/news/homepagelagos/police-launch-vigilant-app-for-public-reporting-of-financial-fraud/>), launched on 1 September 2026; [Hawk Eye (2017), Rescue Me (2021) and the National Cybercrime Centre's reporting portal](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/07/nigerias-police-vigilant-app-raises-questions-duplicate-fraud-reporting/>); the [112 emergency number](<https://www.naltf.gov.ng/shettima-urges-swift-action-as-nigeria-prepares-for-nationwide-rollout-of-112-emergency-line>), approved and not yet rolled out in September 2026, with [about 35 Emergency Communications Centres](<https://www.nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/fg-halts-enforcement-of-new-regulations-on-internet-platforms/>); and the [Police Public Complaints Committee](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>), revived in 2021. Surveillance: [Lagos State's CCTV and integrated command and control centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>); a [CCTV project](<https://fmino.gov.ng/enhancing-effective-policing-with-infrastructural-supportas-wapis-database-centre-debuts/>) being reactivated in 2021; and the [National Command and Control Centre](<https://irb-cisr.gc.ca/en/country-information/rir/Pages/index.aspx?doc=458227>), announced in December 2019. Enabling: the memorandum of 22 April 2026 to [map and digitalise police stations](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/04/23/fg-ptf-sign-mou-to-map-digitalise-police-stations-nationwide/>), with its [connected police stations initiative and national GIS database of stations](<https://thenationonlineng.net/fg-nigeria-police-sign-mou-to-connect-all-police-stations-with-digital-infrastructures-internet-services/>); [computers and solar power for police legal departments in 15 state commands](<https://www.undp.org/nigeria/blog/strengthening-police-accountability-undp-hands-over-ict-and-solar-equipment-nigeria-police-force>), from UNDP; and [Korean-funded training in cybercrime investigation](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=KR-GOV-010-KR-GOV-051-2026040101698000>), closed in June 2026.

***Not held***. Whether stations still record by hand after 2022 is not known.
