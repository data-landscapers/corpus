# Djibouti

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. National police and road-safety officers [wear body cameras](<https://www.lanation.dj/la-bodycam-un-bouclier-de-verite-au-service-des-policiers-et-des-citoyens/>) (August 2026). The National Gendarmerie's services contribute to [case registration in a gender-based violence system being integrated with the health ministry's information system](<https://djibouti.unfpa.org/en/news/strengthening-governance-gender-based-violence-data-integrating-gbvims-health-dhis2-djibouti>) (February 2026). The gendarmerie's intranet, which it calls [IntraGend, was set up in October 2024](<https://www.defense.gouv.fr/operations/actualites/ffdj-partenariat-informatique-gendarmerie-nationale-djiboutienne>) for messaging and file sharing between headquarters and its distant brigades. Its criminal investigations brigade holds [an automated fingerprint file set up in 2004](<https://www.force-publique.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2023-Djibouti-fr.pdf>), whose replacement was due in 2022. The post office [takes orders for the criminal record online](<https://www.laposte.dj/>) (2025).

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether police stations or gendarmerie brigades record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
