# Togo

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** SIPAO, the West African police information system, is [an ECOWAS programme implemented by INTERPOL with European Union funding](<https://togoenlive.tg/sipao-reflexions-a-lome-sur-lappropriation-et-la-perennisation-aux-niveaux-national-et-regional>). After that funding ended, it was handed to ECOWAS and the national authorities on 8 April 2025.

**Records made digitally.** On 14 April 2026 a police divisional commissioner spoke about SIPAO. The commissioner said [the country's commissariats and gendarmerie brigades have SIPAO as a tool](<https://togoenlive.tg/sipao-reflexions-a-lome-sur-lappropriation-et-la-perennisation-aux-niveaux-national-et-regional>).

**How the record is made.** The tool in the stations is [that of the regional programme, SIPAO](<https://togoenlive.tg/sipao-reflexions-a-lome-sur-lappropriation-et-la-perennisation-aux-niveaux-national-et-regional>). The commissioner gave this account on 14 April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The police [stated on 14 April 2026 that every station has the tool](<https://togoenlive.tg/sipao-reflexions-a-lome-sur-lappropriation-et-la-perennisation-aux-niveaux-national-et-regional>). This was a year after the programme's handover of 8 April 2025.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** [All commissariats and gendarmerie brigades in Togo have the SIPAO tool](<https://togoenlive.tg/sipao-reflexions-a-lome-sur-lappropriation-et-la-perennisation-aux-niveaux-national-et-regional>), the police said on 14 April 2026.

***Also on file***. The [Système d'Information Policière d'Afrique de l'Ouest (SIPAO/WAPIS) is managed by the Centre d'Information Policière du Togo](<https://securite.gouv.tg/le-togo-se-dote-dun-centre-dinformation-policiere/>). That national centre in Lomé was inaugurated on 29 April 2022. [SAED, an automated fingerprint system, was installed in Togo between October and December 2024](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/22945/file/25COM00930_WAPIS_Newsletter%2027_2025-FR-LR_Optimized.pdf>). The [*registre de garde à vue*, a paper custody register, was handed to the investigation units of the police and the gendarmerie on 5 November 2019](<https://securite.gouv.tg/ceremonie-de-remise-des-registres-de-garde-a-vue-et-du-premier-lot-de-materiels-du-programme-sipao-wapis/>). The Togo Digital 2025 strategy names the services [*Dépôt de plaintes* and *Demande d'extrait du casier judiciaire*](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/08ec213f7ddd48a1ee4a7130399d4738-0460012022/original/Strat-gie-Togo-Digital-2025.PDF>). [New police commissariats at Mango and Cinkassé, in the Savanes region, were inaugurated by December 2025](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/societe/maillage-securitaire>). They came with vehicles, furniture and computer hardware.

***Not found***. No source found states whether any station enters reports or cases in the tool, which records, or how, and the police give no count of stations. No plan or source of finance has been found.
