# Morocco

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Direction Générale de la Sûreté Nationale (DGSN), the national police, [built GESTARR with its own technical staff](<https://lematin.ma/journal/2019/generalisation-systeme-informatique-gestion-arrondissements-police/320740.html>) and [holds its data on a central DGSN server](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/>). Its [own teams built the case-management system added to GESTARR in 2024](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>).

**Records made digitally.** [Complaints lodged at a police arrondissement are managed in GESTARR](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/>) (October 2019). In 2024 the DGSN [added a system for crime and misdemeanour cases at the arrondissements, each case followed through investigation to submission to the judicial authorities](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>). Its 2025 review says [the system it calls Qadaya handles crime and misdemeanour cases in real time](<https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/>).

**How the record is made.** In October 2019 [the arrondissements' information was stored on a central DGSN server and shared between arrondissements across the Kingdom](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/>), on a platform that [links the arrondissements to each other and to the Sûreté nationale's central services](<https://lematin.ma/journal/2019/generalisation-systeme-informatique-gestion-arrondissements-police/320740.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The DGSN's review of 2025 says [Qadaya's rollout continued during the year and reached 16 of the 22 police commands, with national coverage in view](<https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** GESTARR was [in almost all the Kingdom's arrondissements in October 2019](<https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/>), and [its generalisation was completed in 2023, when the DGSN counted 454 police arrondissements and had the case system on trial in 350 police posts](<https://le12.ma/fr/maroc-la-dgsn-dresse-son-bilan-pour-2023/>). The case system was [in use in 12 commands in 2024, among them the regional sûretés of Ouarzazate, Errachidia, Taza and Al Hoceima](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>), and [Qadaya covered 16 of the 22 commands in 2025](<https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: [the electronic extract of the criminal record](<https://www.village-justice.com/articles/intelligence-artificielle-dans-justice-marocaine-vers-une-nouvelle-mutation,58965.html>), [E-Blagh, for reporting offences committed on the internet](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>), [e-Police and CNIE.MA](<https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/>), [online renewal of the national identity card on the police portal](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/morocco-moves-national-id-renewal-online-as-digital-identity-modernization-advances>) and [the police recruitment portal](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>). Surveillance: [the Amane patrol vehicle and the Madar mobile platform](<https://assabah.ma/938960.html>), [Rabat's camera network and command centres](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>), [the urban video-protection system](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>), [patrol geolocation and number-plate reading on urban cameras](<https://le12.ma/fr/maroc-la-dgsn-dresse-son-bilan-pour-2023/>) and [the call-management application of emergency line 19](<https://maroc-diplomatique.net/bilan-2021-des-services-de-la-dgsn1/>). Identification: [the identity card system's fingerprint identification](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>), and [the national database of suspects' composite portraits and the national forensic laboratory's evidence tracking](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>). Single-function: [the digitisation of traffic-accident reports](<https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/>), [Tifli Moukhtafi, for missing children](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>), and [the road-control document applications and electronic payment of traffic fines](<https://maroc-diplomatique.net/bilan-2021-des-services-de-la-dgsn1/>). Personnel: [E-formation](<https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/>). Enabling: [the police's unified fibre-optic network and the citizen reception spaces' information system](<https://le12.ma/fr/maroc-la-dgsn-dresse-son-bilan-pour-2023/>) and [the DGSN's electronic messaging system](<https://maroc-diplomatique.net/bilan-2021-des-services-de-la-dgsn1/>).

***Not held***. The share of police arrondissements entering their records: the DGSN counts commands, not stations. No plan or finance for the systems is stated, and nothing dated places the Royal Gendarmerie's brigades in or out of a record system.
