# Algeria

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. The national police launched [an online platform for declaring a lost document](<https://algerie-eco.com/2025/11/27/sayoud-lance-la-plateforme-numerique-de-declaration-de-perte-de-documents/>) on 27 November 2025 and [an electronic survey of citizens' sense of security](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7-%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B3>) on 4 August 2026. Under a convention signed on 24 May 2025, police and gendarmerie agents [take payment of traffic fines on electronic terminals](<https://www.algerie360.com/permis-de-conduire-et-amendes-en-algerie-ce-qui-change-pour-les-conducteurs/>), 2,000 of them distributed at first. An order of 10 February 2026 set up, at the justice ministry, [a national automated database of seized narcotics](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2026/F2026015.pdf>), fed by the courts and open to the security services. The justice ministry keeps [the criminal record, a register of traffic offences and a register of alcoholism and narcotics](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2018/F2018034.pdf>) at the registry of each court and at a central service (2018), and the digital strategy for 2025-2030 lists [the withdrawal of criminal records as a digital service](<https://mail.webservices.dz/images/faq/hcn/SNTN-En.pdf>). An interior ministry presentation of June 2019 describes [a security-control application that identifies a person by the fingerprints on the biometric identity card](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether police stations or gendarmerie brigades record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
