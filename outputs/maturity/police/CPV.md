# Cabo Verde

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The state's NOSi [built the criminal-process subsystem of the Sistema de Informação da Justiça with the justice institute IMIJ](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>). It handed the subsystem to the Ministry of Justice on 16 March 2026. The Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Internal Administration [issued the order bringing it into phased operation on 27 December 2024](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View/73167>).

**Records made digitally.** The Ministry of Justice says the subsystem [takes in the Polícia Nacional's stations and the Polícia Judiciária's delegations](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>). Complaints are handled digitally from the police to the courts. By 16 March 2026 it had processed about 25,000 police reports and more than 18,000 cases from end to end.

**How the record is made.** The ministry says the system [joins the stations and the delegations to the courts](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>). Complaints are handled digitally in the one system.

**Last twelve months.** On 16 March 2026 NOSi [formally handed the subsystem over](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal>). The stated target is all courts fully digital within three years.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The joint order of 27 December 2024 [makes use of the electronic criminal process obligatory in the Polícia Nacional](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View/73167>). The Attorney General says it [entered service in the Praia and São Vicente police districts](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73434>). That was on 30 January 2025.

***Also on file***. The [Cidade Segura command centres and video surveillance](<https://www.governo.cv/governo-avanca-com-3a-fase-do-projeto-cidade-segura/>). The Ministry of Internal Administration's [video surveillance system](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/05/13/pj-defende-acesso-ao-sistema-de-videovigilancia-para-reforco-da-investigacao-criminal/>). The [Plataforma Unificada de Segurança, the Sistema Integrado de Estatísticas Criminais and the Laboratório de Polícia Científica](<https://www.governo.cv/documentos/programa-do-governo-02-07-2026/>), commitments of the government's programme of 2 July 2026. The [Criminal Information System](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>), which holds the criminal record. The [Arquivo Nacional de Identificação Civil e Criminal](<https://www.menosfios.com/cabo-verde-e-sao-tome-e-principe-assinam-protocolos-para-informatizar-a-justica/>). An [automated fingerprint identification system](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/18784/file/WAPIS%20Newsletter%20no.%2021%20December%202022%20FR.pdf?inLanguage=fre-FR&version=4>). The [Portal da Justiça](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>), which issues the criminal record certificate online. The Polícia Judiciária's [free line 134](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/27/pj-investiga-esquema-de-extorsao-sexual-online-que-ja-fez-varias-vitimas/>).
