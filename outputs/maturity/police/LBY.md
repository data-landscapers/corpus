# Libya

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Tripoli Security Directorate [launched its digital transformation system itself at its headquarters on 31 December 2025, with the Attorney General, the security director and a representative of the acting Minister of Interior present](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>), the Libyan News Agency reports.

**Records made digitally.** Among the components inaugurated that day is [a system for the evidence-gathering reports of police stations and traffic sections](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>). On 3 May 2025 Tripoli's security director [told the heads of investigation of the police stations and traffic departments, whose officers receive reports and complaints and open the case minutes, to attend to the running of "the digital system"](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331890&lang=ar>).

**How the record is made.** The instruction of May 2025 [names the digital system as a primary source for the database and for documenting legal rights](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=331890&lang=ar>).

**Last twelve months.** The directorate [inaugurated its digital transformation system, including the police-station investigation-reports system, on 31 December 2025](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The agency's report of the launch names the system as one [for police stations and traffic sections of the Tripoli Security Directorate](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Statistics: the Tripoli directorate's [criminal statistics system](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>). Identification: the Public Prosecution's [criminal record cards issued electronically](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=345074&lang=ar>) and the interior ministry's [criminal status certificate issuing system](<https://lana.gov.ly/art.php?id=269371&lang=ar>). Single-function: the directorate's [motor vehicle registration system](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>). Public: the ministry's [online criminal status request form](<https://lana.gov.ly/art.php?id=269371&lang=ar>). Surveillance: the directorate's [AI-assisted traffic and security monitoring system](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347992&lang=ar>).

***Not held***. No station outside the Tripoli Security Directorate is shown using a records system, and no count of stations, plan or source of finance is given. How the record is made is not stated.
