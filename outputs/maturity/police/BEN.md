# Benin

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. The Police républicaine [put an automated fingerprint and palm-print file into service on 30 September 2025](<https://www.dgpr.bj/mise-en-exploitation-du-fichier-automatise-des-empreintes-biometriques-la-credibilite-du-systeme-denquete-policiere-et-judiciaire-au-coeur-des-enjeux/>) at its headquarters, and the judicial police's [forensic service reopened the same day](<https://fraternite.bj/lutte-contre-la-criminalite-au-benin-un-systeme-automatise-didentification-par-empreintes-digitales-inaugure/>), renovated and re-equipped. The data-protection authority gave a favourable opinion on 19 August 2016 to [a computerised national criminal file](<https://archive.apdp.bj/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Rapport-Bilan_2015-2020_Version-finale.pdf>) kept by the Centre de Documentation de la Sécurité Publique; its report for 2015-2020 also lists the centre's 2017 authorisation to process identification data on persons prosecuted, arrested or wanted, and video surveillance declared by police headquarters in 2019 and by the centre in 2020. From 1 October 2026 [loss certificates are issued only through the police's online platform](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/benin-les-certificats-de-perte-desormais-delivres-en-ligne>), and stations no longer issue them. The Centre national d'investigations numériques [takes complaints on digital offences by email](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-le-cnin-ouvre-trois-points-de-plainte-a-porto-novo-abomey-et-parakou/>), as reported in October 2026. Citizens [request the criminal-record extract on the national public services portal](<https://fraternite.bj/le-benin-a-lere-du-numerique-une-revolution-administrative-en-marche/>) (2025). A March 2026 programme describes [the application it calls Justice BJ](<https://pub-f2abea1e4da540aa9a3e39fedb257f21.r2.dev/PROGRAMME-WADAGNI-TALATA-2026.pdf>), for following court cases, as being generalised, and the justice sector's plan for 2026-2033 provides for [a single portal for lodging a complaint online](<https://lematinal.bj/justice-beninoise-de-2026-2033-un-secteur-connecte-et-accessible-pour-tous/>). [SECUROUTE](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/27/cybastion-advances-digital-government-solutions-supports-benins-securoute-infrastructure-upgrade/>), as Benin names it, centralises road enforcement data (January 2026). The state's digital agency [gave 300 police officers its tools for signing and encrypting exchanges](<https://www.dgpr.bj/techniques-de-protection-des-informations-sensibles-de-letat-lagence-des-systemes-dinformation-et-du-numerique-asin-et-direction-des-systemes-dinformation-et-de/>), 150 of them arrondissement commissioners, in training from 26 June to 8 July 2026.

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether stations record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
