# Digitalisation of rural police stations: station records

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The frame's anchor (`lookups/maturity-norms.csv`) is Corpus-defined: the AFRIPOL Statute of 2017 connects national police agencies and says nothing below the national level, and the lookup's search found no AU instrument on local stations.

**No norm measures what the sub-indicator measures.** The ladder borrows nothing; the 90 per cent line is the health study's. The African Commission's Luanda Guidelines (2014), part 4, require arrest and custody registers wherever people are held and name no medium; the custody register is a noted class here. *(Part 4 read 2026-10-10.)*

## The ladder

| Stage | Records | How the record is made | Stations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that stations record on paper only | | |
| 2 Preparing | A system for stations is being prepared or piloted; or one runs at headquarters or in the capital only | Any, or not stated | Pilot stations, or none |
| 3 Establishing | O or C: past its pilot and live in some stations outside the capital | `connected`, `local` or `keyed-elsewhere` | A minority, a count with no total, or not published |
| 4 Operating | O or C | `connected` | More than half, on a share or a count with a denominator |
| 5 Leading | O and C | `connected` | 90 per cent or more, on a figure published within two years that gives the rural share |

## Countries

### South Africa: 4 Operating

Case dockets were captured digitally at 998 of 1,157 police stations in May 2021, 86.3% of all police stations; the rural share is not known.

- The Acting Minister of Police replies that when a complaint is taken at the station's Community Service Centre and the elements of a crime exist, a case docket is opened and captured on the Crime Administration System (CAS)/Investigate Case Docket Management System (ICDMS); the reply is undated and the question's date, 31 December 2025, is used ([source, 2025-12-31](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/)).
- The Acting Minister of Police replies that the docket is captured on CAS/ICDMS at the station by the Community Service Centre and signed off on the system by the Shift Commander to the Detective Commander, and that Crime Registrar officials at station, provincial and national level verify the registered dockets on the same system, the station checking each 24 hours' registrations; a physical docket is kept beside it; the reply is undated and the question's date, 31 December 2025, is used ([source, 2025-12-31](https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/)).
- SAPS told the Portfolio Committee on Police on 4 May 2021 that the Integrated Case Docket Management System (ICDMS) had been implemented in 998 police stations with 159 still to be covered, a total of 1 157 by addition (the same meeting's plan counts 1 156 stations); stated by Lt Gen Mfazi, Deputy National Commissioner: Crime Detection, and repeated by Maj Gen Mavundla, Head: Technology Management, in answer to a question about the position at the end of 2020/21 ([source, 2021-05-04](https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/)).
- SAPS told the Portfolio Committee on Police on 4 May 2021 that cases are registered and processed on ICDMS at the police stations where it is implemented, and on the old system at the rest ([source, 2021-05-04](https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/)).
- SAPS's Head of Technology Management told the Portfolio Committee on Police on 4 May 2021 that ICDMS depends on the station's network link, low network hampering the processing of cases, and that cases registered on the old system at the other stations are merged into the same database as ICDMS ([source, 2021-05-04](https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/)).

### Cape Verde: 3 Established

Complaints ran digitally from national police stations to the courts by March 2026; how many stations use the system is not known.

*Justice linked; Advancing.*

- The Ministry of Justice says the SIJ's digital criminal-process subsystem takes in the Polícia Nacional's stations and the Polícia Judiciária's delegations, so that the handling of complaints is essentially digital from the police to the courts, with about 25,000 autos and more than 18,000 cases processed end to end by 16 March 2026 ([source, 2026-03-16](https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal)).
- The Ministry of Justice says the SIJ solution integrates the Polícia Nacional's stations and the Polícia Judiciária's delegations, complaints being handled digitally from the police through to the courts in the one system; it does not describe how the station enters the complaint ([source, 2026-03-16](https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal)).
- The Ministry of Justice's account of the SIJ criminal-process subsystem in the Polícia Nacional's stations gives no count or share of stations using it ([source, 2026-03-16](https://justica.gov.cv/w/nosi-entrega-sistema-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-justi%C3%A7a-penal)).

### Ghana: 3 Established

Case dockets were held digitally in 55 police stations across seven regions in 2022; the total of stations, and anything since, is not known.

*Justice linked.*

- The Asokwa Municipal Police Commander says the Case Tracking System has eliminated the use of paper as case dockets in the prosecution of criminal cases; the event is undated, so the date is the document's published date ([source, 2022-05-18](https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/)).
- The Asokwa Municipal Police Commander says he can sit in his office and monitor cases in all stations within his jurisdiction through the Case Tracking System, and names unstable internet and electricity as hindering it; no count of stations is given, and the date is the document's published date ([source, 2022-05-18](https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/)).
- The Legal Resources Centre's workshop report says 55 police stations and 3 prisons services are using the Case Tracking System, in seven regions (Greater Accra, Ashanti, Northern, Volta, Bono, Western, Eastern) and over 40 districts, and gives an unreconciled total of 757 user centres; no total of stations is given, and the date is the document's published year, a proxy ([source, 2022](https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/)).
- The Legal Resources Centre's monitoring specialist says 2,475 cases were registered using the Case Tracking System from December 2021; the document does not say whether the figure is national or the Volta Region's, and the date is its published year, a proxy ([source, 2022](https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/)).

### Mauritius: 3 Established

All police stations had computers linked to the headquarters complaint and case system in 2025; how many stations enter records in it is not known.

- The Prime Minister, on information from the Commissioner of Police, told the National Assembly that the Crime Occurrence Tracking System is used for the management and tracking of complaints and declarations from initial reporting to final case disposal, and has been operational since 2011 ([source, 2025-06-17](https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf)).
- The Prime Minister, on information from the Commissioner of Police, described the Crime Occurrence Tracking System as a digital platform connecting all police stations to the main server at Police Headquarters, used on personal computers in the stations ([source, 2025-06-17](https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf)).
- The Prime Minister, on information from the Commissioner of Police, said all police stations, including Moka, Saint Pierre and Quartier Militaire, are equipped with personal computers for the use of the Crime Occurrence Tracking System, which connects all stations to the headquarters server; no count of stations or of stations entering records is given ([source, 2025-06-17](https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf)).

### Morocco: 3 Established

Police stations record complaints and cases in connected national systems, cases in 16 of 22 commands in 2025; the share of stations is not known.

*Advancing.*

- The DGSN's 2025 annual review, as La Vie éco reports it, says the Qadaya computer system networks the police services so that cases of misdemeanours and crimes are handled quickly and in real time ([source, 2025](https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/)).
- The DGSN's 2025 review says the generalisation of Qadaya continued in 2025 to cover 16 of the 22 police commands (commandements de sûreté) nationally, with national generalisation in view; it gives no count of arrondissements or stations ([source, 2025](https://www.lavieeco.com/pouvoirs/securite/la-dgsn-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-rapprocher-ses-services-des-citoyens/)).
- The DGSN's 2024 annual review says its technical teams added to GESTARR, the computerised management system of the police arrondissements, a system for managing crime and misdemeanour cases at those proximity units, with the procedures drawn up digitised, following each case from the GESTARR data through investigation to submission to the judicial authorities ([source, 2024](https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/)).
- The DGSN's 2024 review says the case-management system is in use in 12 commands (the police prefectures of Kénitra, Tangier, Settat, Marrakech and Meknès, the regional sûretés of Ouarzazate, Errachidia, Taza and Al Hoceima, and the provincial sûretés of Salé, Safi and El Jadida), with national generalisation in view; it gives no count of arrondissements and no total ([source, 2024](https://www.lereporter.ma/la-direction-generale-de-la-surete-nationale-presente-son-bilan-annuel-au-titre-de-lannee-2024/)).
- A DGSN principal police officer of the public-security directorate, speaking at the DGSN open days in Tangier (2 to 6 October 2019), says GESTARR computerises the work of the police arrondissements and manages the complaints citizens lodge there, as well as residence certificates and declarations of loss and of theft ([source, 2019-10](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/)).
- The DGSN officer says the information coming from the arrondissements is stored on a central DGSN server so that complaints and documents issued can be followed, with information shared between arrondissements across the Kingdom ([source, 2019-10](https://fr.le360.ma/societe/dgsn-gestarr-le-nouveau-systeme-de-gestion-des-arrondissements-de-police-199731/)).

### Seychelles: 3 Established

Police stations are all connected to a national case system, by a 2025 vendor account; how many enter their own records is not known.

- The vendor Winsoft says the Seychelles police force's custom app manages cases from start to finish, is the repository of every case recorded and is built to be robust enough for use in court; no deployment date is given, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2025-01-06](https://winsoft-international.com/seychelles-police-department-chooses-the-filemaker-platform-to-aid-fight-against-crime/)).
- Winsoft says development began by digitising the intake form for incident reports, the record of a single crime, while listing digitisation of the Occurrence Book itself, the daily record of incidents, as an upcoming enhancement against paper records; no date is given, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2025-01-06](https://winsoft-international.com/seychelles-police-department-chooses-the-filemaker-platform-to-aid-fight-against-crime/)).
- Winsoft says the app runs on desktop computers or iPads as a central resource holding every case, that officers search crimes from other districts, and quotes the detective commander of Mont Fleuri station that all police stations are connected to the whole country; it also says the database is bedding in at headquarters in Victoria. No date is given, so the published date is used ([source, 2025-01-06](https://winsoft-international.com/seychelles-police-department-chooses-the-filemaker-platform-to-aid-fight-against-crime/)).
- Marcus Jean, detective commander of Mont Fleuri station, is quoted by Winsoft saying all police stations are connected; no count of stations and no year is given, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2025-01-06](https://winsoft-international.com/seychelles-police-department-chooses-the-filemaker-platform-to-aid-fight-against-crime/)).

- The article states that the platform is to replace the physical registers in which main courantes are kept at police commissariats and gendarmerie brigades; at late December 2023 those registers were still the record ([source, 2023-12](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/)).
- At late December 2023 no commissariat or gendarmerie brigade was using the computerised main courante: the platform was over 80% built and due to be operational by mid-January 2024; no number of stations is given ([source, 2023-12](https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/)).

### Liberia: 2 Nascent

Six officers in the capital's county began entering crime reports on tablets in a 2021 pilot; whether any station outside it does is not known.

- UNDP reports a police officer saying she now inputs information into a tablet as crime reports come in, with suspects' biodata, photos and GPS locations, before any arrest; no date is given for the practice, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2023-03-09](https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system)).
- UNDP describes CSIS as an online system of two parts, a smartphone or tablet on which the officer enters the crime report and an online portal that aggregates and analyses the information for Liberia National Police decision-makers; reports are also submitted by email. No date is given for the description, so the document's published date is used ([source, 2023-03-09](https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system)).
- UNDP states that when CSIS was rolled out in January 2021 six officers, five men and one woman, were selected to pioneer its use in Montserrado County, the seat of the capital; no count of stations is given ([source, 2021-01](https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system)).
- UNDP's rule of law adviser states that a case management information system similar to the Judiciary's, which tracks cases online, was developed and rolled out at the Liberia National Police in 2021 and was up and running in June 2022; he gives no count of stations using it ([source, 2022-06-21](https://www.undp.org/liberia/blog/innovative-online-case-management-system-ushers-liberias-judiciary-digital-age)).
- UNDP Liberia says the system went live at Liberia National Police headquarters on 25 January 2021 with a five-day training of trainers for 15 personnel, and is to be piloted separately by each institution at first; it names no station or county using it ([source, 2021-01-25](https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/national-police-online-crime-statistics-system-goes-live)).

Officers were trained in July 2026 for a crime management system's introduction, after a 2022 pilot in six districts; stations using it are not known.

*Advancing.*

- UNDP Malawi says the Crime Management System training builds officers' skills in digitally recording, tracking and managing crime information from the moment a case is reported until its final disposal, the system covering the entire case cycle ([source, 2026-07-29](https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/dockets-data-malawi-police-and-undp-malawi-advance-digital-transformation-crime-management)).
