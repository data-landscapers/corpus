# South Africa

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The South African Police Service [runs the case docket system as its own](<https://pss.lgv.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SAPS-REGISTRATION-OF-DOCKETS.pdf>). Its Strategic Plan 2025 to 2030, tabled on 4 April 2025, [lists the enhancement of case docket management, with full functionality in 2027/28](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2030/SAPS_2025_to_2030_SP_(04_April_2025).pdf>). The 2025/26 plan [funds its information systems from Programme 1 of Vote 28](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** A case docket is opened when a complaint is taken at a station and the elements of a crime exist. [It is captured on what the police call the Crime Administration System and the Investigate Case Docket Management System](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>). The Acting Minister gave this reply to a question of 31 December 2025.

**How the record is made.** The docket is [captured at the station's Community Service Centre and signed off on the system by the Shift Commander](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>). It is verified on the system at station, provincial and national level, with a physical docket kept beside it. In May 2021 the docket system [depended on the station's network link](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 April 2025 the Police Minister [announced a private-sector partnership to digitise dockets and upgrade five police stations](<https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/saps-partners-with-private-sector-in-digitizing-case-dockets/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The police told Parliament's police committee on 4 May 2021 that the docket system [was in 998 police stations with 159 still to be covered](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>), 86.3% of all police stations. The 159 [registered cases on the old system, which fed the same database](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>). In 2023/24, [789 of 1,160 stations sent complainants text messages about a docket through the system](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Quarterly crime statistics](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>) and the [Crime Management Information System](<https://pss.lgv.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SAPS-REGISTRATION-OF-DOCKETS.pdf>) are on file, as is the [Integrated Person Management system](<https://static.pmg.org.za/RCW331-2026-04-30.pdf>). Also on file are the [Automated Fingerprint Identification System, the Criminal Record System and the ballistics systems](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>). So are the [Laboratory Information Management System, the Firearm Control Management System and the second-hand goods dealers system](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>). So are the [National Key Points system, the 10111 incident management system and station CCTV](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>). The [National Forensic DNA Database and station desktops, laptops, scanners and network upgrades](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/atc/01ls62wgfybfozcgywlzc3mye5ridnoi4x.pdf>) are on file. So are the [Firearm Permit System, the Property Control and Exhibit Management System and VA-AMIS](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/government/632371->), switched off in 2019. So are the [Central Firearm Registry, the P-Chem system and wide area network and digital radio sites](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>). The [Rate Our Service application and digital radio](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2030/SAPS_2025_to_2030_SP_(04_April_2025).pdf>) are on file. So are the [Reservist System, a drone surveillance capability and an Integrated Command Centre](<https://dcstm.nwpg.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/national_rural_safety_strategy_2019.pdf>). [Body-worn and dashboard cameras with facial recognition](<https://techcentral.co.za/saps-bodycam-tender-facial-recognition/285986/>) and an [incident management solution](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/top-ict-tenders-sita-sees-activity-surge/lwrKx73YyZRqmg1o>) are both out to tender. The [Numerus data centre and the police switching centres](<https://www.sita.co.za/sites/default/files/Annual%20Report%202023%20-%202024.pdf>) are on file. So are [Johannesburg's CCTV networks, the JMPD IIOC command centre and the AURA dispatching system](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>). A [Hikvision camera network in Johannesburg](<https://rsisinternational.org/journals/ijriss/articles/the-coloniality-of-chinese-surveillance-technologies-in-africa/>) is on file. So are the [Gauteng provincial CCTV network and Panic Button App](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/thousands-of-cameras-to-help-catch-ekurhuleni-killer/KBpdg7pm5pXMLEew>) and the [Cybersecurity Hub's incident reporting](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>).

***Not found***. No count of stations registering dockets on the system later than May 2021, and no rural share. Whether the first report of an occurrence is recorded digitally, and whether the docket passes digitally to the prosecutor or the court.
