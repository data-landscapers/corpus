# South Africa

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The South African Police Service [runs the case docket system as its own](<https://pss.lgv.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SAPS-REGISTRATION-OF-DOCKETS.pdf>). Its Strategic Plan 2025 to 2030, tabled on 4 April 2025, [lists the enhancement of case docket management, with full functionality in 2027/28](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2030/SAPS_2025_to_2030_SP_(04_April_2025).pdf>). The 2025/26 plan [funds its information systems from Programme 1 of Vote 28](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** When a complaint is taken at a station and the elements of a crime exist, [a case docket is opened and captured on what the police call the Crime Administration System and the Investigate Case Docket Management System](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>), by the Acting Minister's reply to a question of 31 December 2025.

**How the record is made.** The docket is [captured at the station's Community Service Centre, signed off on the system by the Shift Commander and verified on it at station, provincial and national level](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35281/>), with a physical docket kept beside it. In May 2021 the docket system [depended on the station's network link](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 11 April 2025 the Police Minister [announced a partnership with the private sector to digitise case dockets and upgrade five police stations](<https://www.sabcnews.com/sabcnews/saps-partners-with-private-sector-in-digitizing-case-dockets/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The police told Parliament's police committee on 4 May 2021 that the docket system [was in 998 police stations with 159 still to be covered](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>), 86.3% of all police stations. The 159 [registered cases on the old system, which fed the same database](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/32761/>). In 2023/24, [789 of 1,160 stations sent complainants text messages about a docket through the system](<https://www.saps.gov.za/about/stratframework/strategic_plan/2025_2026/SAPS_2025-26_APP_(04_April_2025).pdf>).
