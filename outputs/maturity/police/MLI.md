# Mali

## Station records

***Also on file***. The [Fichier national automatisé des Empreintes digitales (FNAED) and the Fichier manuel des Empreintes digitales (FMED)](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2025/mali-jo-2025-05.pdf>) are sections in a staffing table. The table is that of the Direction générale de la Police technique et scientifique, dated 7 February 2025. [SIPAO (WAPIS) had its operational activity in Mali suspended until late 2022, and the same programme named Mali to be prepared for an AFIS](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/18784/file/WAPIS%20Newsletter%20no.%2021%20December%202022%20FR.pdf?inLanguage=fre-FR&version=4>). The Code de procédure pénale of 2024 permits or provides for several files. It calls them [*fichiers d'antécédents*, *fichiers d'analyse sérielle*, a *fichier des personnes recherchées* and *logiciels de rapprochement judiciaire*](<https://sgg-mali.ml/codes/codeprocedurepenale.pdf>). It also names [*fichiers de police ou de gendarmerie*, a *fichier national automatisé des empreintes génétiques* and a *casier judiciaire national centralisé et informatisé*](<https://sgg-mali.ml/codes/codeprocedurepenale.pdf>). The special register of its [*registre de garde à vue* may be kept in dematerialised form](<https://sgg-mali.ml/codes/codeprocedurepenale.pdf>). A decree of 3 November 2022 has police commissioners and gendarmerie brigade commanders establish the [Carte nationale d'Identité biométrique sécurisée](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2022/mali-jo-2022-35.pdf>). The criminal record portal [ecasierjudiciaire.gouv.ml was officially tested on 10 February 2026](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/mali-digitalisation-du-casier-judiciaire-et-du-certificat-de-nationalite/>). That followed a [full-scale test of its modules on 23 October 2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>). [E-verif checks the documents the portals issue](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/le-ministere-de-la-justice-a-lere-du-numerique-voici-le-casier-et-la-nationalite-en-ligne-3113977.html>). The [e-Justice platform was not yet launched on 21 July 2026](<http://news.abamako.com/h/305410.html>). An interministerial instruction of 19 July 2024 provides for [digital payment of police fines](<https://afrikinfos-mali.com/2024/07/23/mali-le-gouvernement-devoile-la-liste-des-premieres-obligations-de-paiement-numerique-dans-les-services-publics/>).
