# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** A police statement [must by law be written out and signed by the officer who drafts it, on pain of nullity](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>). The programme officer of the Ligue des droits de la personne dans la région des Grands Lacs said so on 15 March 2023. The officer was speaking to Canada's Immigration and Refugee Board.

**Records made digitally.** A case [is referred to the Congolese National Police by a written and signed complaint](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>). The Executive Secretary of Héritiers de la justice told the board this on 9 March 2023.

**How the record is made.** Police documents in criminal proceedings [are typed, and the record of the parties' hearings is handwritten in most cases](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>). He said so in March 2023 after consulting lawyers at the South Kivu Court of Appeal.

**Last twelve months.** The samples the board received in March 2023 were [blank paper forms of findings, arrest and witness testimony reports](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The [typed and handwritten police documents](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>) were described in March 2023 for the country as a whole.
