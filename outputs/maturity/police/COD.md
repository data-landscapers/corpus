# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** A police statement [must by law be written out and signed by the officer who drafts it, on pain of nullity](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>), the programme officer of the Ligue des droits de la personne dans la région des Grands Lacs told Canada's Immigration and Refugee Board on 15 March 2023.

**Records made digitally.** A case [is referred to the Congolese National Police by a written and signed complaint](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>), the Executive Secretary of Héritiers de la justice told the board on 9 March 2023.

**How the record is made.** Police documents in criminal proceedings [are typed, and the record of the parties' hearings is handwritten in most cases](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>), he said in March 2023 after consulting lawyers at the South Kivu Court of Appeal.

**Last twelve months.** The samples supplied to the board in March 2023 were [blank paper forms of findings, arrest and witness testimony reports](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The [typed and handwritten police documents](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2092757.html>) were described in March 2023 for the country as a whole.
