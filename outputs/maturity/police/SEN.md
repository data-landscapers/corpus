# Senegal

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: [e-Sénégal](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>), the single-window application launched on 24 March 2026 with requests for the criminal record extract, the [Treasury e-payment platform](<https://aps.sn/le-new-deal-technologique-a-pose-les-bases-dune-infrastructure-numerique-robuste-et-souveraine-apres-un-an-dexistence-selon-alioune-sall/>) for taxes, fines and fees, and the Police nationale's [online platform for reporting cybercrime](<https://aps.sn/une-plateforme-numerique-de-signalement-des-infractions-cybercriminelles-officiellement-lancee/>), launched on 12 February 2026. Enabling: the [network infrastructure in prefectures and gendarmerie brigades](<https://www.socialnetlink.org/2026/09/04/senegal-services-isidore-diouf-erige-la-connectivite-en-priorite-absolue-pour-reussir-la-dematerialisation/>) of the north, found in September 2026 to need maintenance, and the [project to interconnect the commissariats](<https://privacyinternational.org/sites/default/files/2020-11/Doc%207.8%20Etud%20faisab_Redacted.pdf.pdf>), in preparation in April 2019. Surveillance: the [Dakar CCTV network and its command and control centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>). Identification: the planned [national criminal record platform](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2025/07/senegal-la-lettre-de-politique-sectorielle-de-la-justice-adoptee/>) of the justice policy letter adopted on 29 July 2025, the [biometric national identity card system](<https://privacyinternational.org/sites/default/files/2020-11/Doc%207.8%20Etud%20faisab_Redacted.pdf.pdf>), with applications taken in commissariats in April 2019, the [ECOWAS biometric identity card enrolment](<https://interieur.gouv.sn/le-ministere/directions/direction-de-l-automatisation-des-fichiers-daf>) begun at six Dakar commissariats on 16 August 2017, the [automated police information database of CREDIPOL](<https://www.interpol.int/en/content/download/18009/file/Wapis%20Newsletter%20no.%2020%20August%202022%29.pdf>), the national data centre inaugurated on 17 May 2022, and the regional [AFIS project](<https://www.interpol.int/en/content/download/18009/file/Wapis%20Newsletter%20no.%2020%20August%202022%29.pdf>) launched on 1 March 2022. Single-function: the [Points de Digitalisation des Amendes](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/economie/digitalisation-des-amendes-forfaitaires-164-014-attestations-traitees-8419-millions-fcfa-collectes-par-les-forces-de-securite/>), 50 installed in 2024 for fixed fines, the police's [terminals for verifying traffic e-passes](<https://sonatel.sn/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Sonatel-Groups-2020-annual-report.pdf>) in 2020 and the [firearm permit file](<https://interieur.gouv.sn/le-ministere/directions/direction-de-l-automatisation-des-fichiers-daf>). Statistics: the [Police nationale's annual report](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1708-140914-senegal-vers-la-mise-en-place-d-une-autorite-nationale-de-cybersecurite>), which counted 3,794 cybercrime offences in 2025, and the [criminal analysis cell ANACRIM](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/22945/file/25COM00930_WAPIS_Newsletter%2027_2025-FR-LR_Optimized.pdf>), in place at CREDIPOL since February 2025.

***Not held***. No dated statement says how stations or gendarmerie brigades record today. The one records tool held was shown at a design workshop on 2 October 2025, its technical form not stated and its use in the units still under discussion; no training, delivery, plan or finance is held for it.
