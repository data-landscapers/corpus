# Liberia

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Liberia National Police [was the first to roll out the case management system under the Ministry of Justice, in 2021](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/blog/innovative-online-case-management-system-ushers-liberias-judiciary-digital-age>). The UN Joint Rule of Law Programme [supported its design, development and roll-out](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/liberia-designs-and-roll-out-online-case-management-information-system-justice-institutions>). The crime statistics system [was developed with UNDP support](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system>).

**Records made digitally.** UNDP reported in March 2023 an officer [entering crime reports into a tablet as they come in](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system>). The entries include suspects' biodata, photos and GPS locations. The case management system [records the parties, the offences, the proceedings and the outcome of a case](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/liberia-designs-and-roll-out-online-case-management-information-system-justice-institutions>). It [was up and running at the police in June 2022](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/blog/innovative-online-case-management-system-ushers-liberias-judiciary-digital-age>).

**How the record is made.** The Crime Statistics Information System, as UNDP names it, is [online and in two parts](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system>). Officers enter reports on a smartphone or tablet, and a portal aggregates them for police decision-makers. Case data is [captured on a web and mobile application that works online and offline](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/liberia-designs-and-roll-out-online-case-management-information-system-justice-institutions>), by UNDP's account of April 2022.

**Last twelve months.** FrontPageAfrica reported in 2026 that [Nyehn Town Police Station, the only police facility serving Todee District, has no computers](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-rural-security-at-risk-as-nyehn-town-police-station-falls-apart/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The crime statistics system [went live at police headquarters on 25 January 2021, with training for 15 personnel](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/national-police-online-crime-statistics-system-goes-live>). [Six officers were chosen to pioneer it in Montserrado County, the seat of the capital](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/policewoman-breaks-digital-barrier-new-crime-statistics-and-information-system>), that month.

***Also on file***. Crime statistics: [43 reporting officers had a refresher on the Crime Statistics Information System in Monrovia in August 2025](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/news/lnp-trains-officers-upgraded-crime-reporting-platform>). UNDP states that [a Crime Statistics Information Management System was rolled out nationwide in 2024](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>). For the public, [an online police clearance platform was launched on 1 May 2026](<https://gnnliberia.com/lnp-lra-digitize-police-clearance-in-major-reform/>). [The police call centre and the 911 number are to be refurbished and reactivated under an agreement with Orange Liberia of 10 March 2026](<https://analystliberiaonline.com/orange-police-seal-security-partnership-deal-sign-mou-targeting-cybercrime-infrastructure-protection/>). [A cybercrime reporting channel is planned](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf>). Single-function: [a National Cybercrime Management System, planned in the same strategy](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Liberia_National-Cyber-Security-Strategy-2025-2029.docx.pdf>). Identification: [a Digital Forensic Lab at the police training academy, not yet operating in March 2026](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). Enabling: [a closed user group plan on the Orange network for calls and SMS among officers](<https://analystliberiaonline.com/orange-police-seal-security-partnership-deal-sign-mou-targeting-cybercrime-infrastructure-protection/>). The police stated on 21 August 2026 that they get [internet service to police facilities from the Liberia Telecommunications Corporation](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-lnp-denies-starlink-use-clarifies-vehicle-procurement-amid-public-questions/>). [VHF and UHF radio frequencies were assigned in 2010](<https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/LTA-ANNUAL-REPORT-for-2010.pdf>).

***Not found***. No fact shows either system in use at a station outside Montserrado County. No count or share of stations entering records has been found. The newest facts on use are of 2021 to 2023. Nothing has been found on who owns, plans or pays for the systems since 2022, or on any change in the last twelve months.
