# The Gambia

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNDP's terms of reference of 22 April 2021 state that [the crime-reporting app will be owned by the Gambia Police Force](<https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=251674>), and UNDP reports that in 2020 it [supported the work technically and financially with UN Peacebuilding Fund money](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2022-09/Finalfinal.pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** The Inspector General of Police, at a media briefing reported on 24 March 2026, [described the force's documentation as paper-based](<https://standard.gm/police-must-transition-from-paper-documentation-to-digitalised-mode-igp>). UNDP reports that in 2020 it [supported a Mobile Data Collection App within the police's digitalised case management system and trained 24 officers to use it](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2022-09/Finalfinal.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The Inspector General said the force [needs to move from paper-based documentation to a digitalised mode](<https://standard.gm/police-must-transition-from-paper-documentation-to-digitalised-mode-igp>), as reported on 24 March 2026.

**Last twelve months.** At the briefing reported on 24 March 2026 the Inspector General put [digitalisation of police workflow and records management as work still to come](<https://standard.gm/police-must-transition-from-paper-documentation-to-digitalised-mode-igp>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** UNDP's terms of reference of 22 April 2021 describe [a mobile app for crime reporting by police officers as in its pilot phase, to be extended to other police stations in phases](<https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=251674>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [A fingerprint identification system launched on 28 April 2026 at the Criminal Records Office](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/gambia/gambia-launches-automated-fingerprint-identification-system-afis-strengthen-criminal-investigations_en>); [a National Computer Forensic Laboratory and a Cybercrime Complaint Center, both planned in the 2022-2026 cybersecurity strategy](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/National-Cybersecurity-Strategy-2022-2026-Final-.pdf>); [the Drivers License System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>); [the Quarterly Crime Statistics Template](<https://procurement-notices.undp.org/view_file.cfm?doc_id=251674>); [an information management system for the national statistical system that covers the police, and GIS mapping of all police stations](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2022-09/Finalfinal.pdf>); [the Drone Surveillance Unit](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Budget-Speech_Dec_2.pdf>); [surveillance equipment from the German government, and CCTV linked to a traffic management centre planned in 2025](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).

***Not held***. What became of the case management system and the crime-reporting app after April 2021, and how many stations took part in the pilot. Who owns, plans or pays for digital station records has not been stated in the last three years.
