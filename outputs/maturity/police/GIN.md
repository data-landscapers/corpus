# Guinea

## Station records

***Also on file***. Surveillance: [Guinea Safe City](<https://www.guinee360.com/17/06/2026/guinee-mamadi-doumbouya-donne-le-feu-vert-a-un-vaste-reseau-de-videosurveillance-urbaine/>), a planned network of 324 video surveillance sites in eight cities, as at June 2026. Identification: the justice ministry's [platform for criminal-record bulletins](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/05/digitalisation-du-casier-judiciaire-la-guinee-enclenche-une-reforme-majeure/>), announced in 2026. Staff of the [Casier Judiciaire Central](<https://judicalex-gn.org/blog/post/modernisation-de-la-justice-trois-jours-de-formation-pour-le-personnel-du-casier-judiciaire-central-a-kindia/>) were trained at Kindia from 27 November 2025. The police technique et scientifique runs an [ABIS](<https://www.innovatrics.com/resources/digitalization-of-police-investigation-in-west-africa/>) in Conakry. The 2023-2027 digital strategy plans a [Système de E-Justice](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). Single-function: [national identity card issuance](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/1146298.html>) at central police stations, as described between 2011 and 2014. [Biometric passport issuance](<https://www.guinee360.com/09/01/2026/passeports-biometriques-fini-le-deplacement-a-conakry-pour-les-pelerins-de-linterieur/>) has sent mobile teams to the interior from 2026. Statistics: the security ministry's [Annuaire statistique de la police et de la protection civile 2018](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/SSN/mspc/MSPC_ANNUAIRE_2018.pdf>).

***Not found***. A decree of 13 May 2019 created the police data centre and its information system, and names no station.
