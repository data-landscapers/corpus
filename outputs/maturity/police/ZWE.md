# Zimbabwe

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Home Affairs minister said on 17 January 2024 that [computerising police operations, under what he called the ZRP Integrated Information System, is part of the ministry's ICT strategy approved by Cabinet in 2022](<https://nehandaradio.com/2024/01/17/police-in-zimbabwe-looking-for-computerization-partner-kazembe/>), and that the police had finished a needs assessment and were looking for a partner. In December 2021 the Information minister said [government would bear all costs of digitising rural stations](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>).

**Records made digitally.** The officer commanding Harare Province said, in remarks reported on 9 December 2025, that [the police would soon move to an electronic system for processing all dockets](<https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/>).

**How the record is made.** The force's Horizon 2025 strategic plan, as reported on 16 December 2021, [decries the manual management of information, which it says makes information hard to retrieve and leads to its loss and destruction](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>).

**Last twelve months.** In December 2025 the Zimbabwe Republic Police [presented the planned electronic docket system as a measure against bribery in the handling of case files](<https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Information minister said after Cabinet on 14 December 2021 that [a pilot study for digitising rural police stations had been completed, with gadgets and connectivity still to be provided](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [An Electronic Traffic Management System](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimbabwe-urges-human-oversight-as-ai-enters-criminal-justice/>); [the ZRP Smart Traffic Management System](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609230312.html>); [the Smart Transport and Traffic Management System planned in 2021](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>); [point-of-sale machines planned in October 2025 for all police roadblocks, with Starlink connectivity](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-to-roll-out-pos-machines-starlink-kits-at-police-roadblocks/>); [drones acquired to monitor traffic and public spaces](<https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/>); [a smart traffic management system launched in Harare under the Safe City Project](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>); [the Presidential Internet Scheme, which by 2025 had connected over 100 government institutions, police stations among them](<https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2026-ZIMBABWE-INFRASTRUCTURE-INVEST-PROGRAMME-FINAL-WEB.pdf>).

***Not held***. Which stations, if any, entered reports or dockets in a system by 30 September 2026, and which record the planned system will hold. How the system is to be paid for has not been stated since 2021.
