# Zimbabwe

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In October 2026 the Home Affairs minister said [the ZRP Integrated Information System is a Ministry of Home Affairs programme](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zrp-pilots-digital-crime-reporting-system-developed-by-harare-institute-of-technology-at-warren-park-police-station/>). Its crime management subsystem was built by the state Harare Institute of Technology. In June 2025 he said [a ZRP Integrated Information Management System Strategy had been developed](<https://newsreelzimbabwe.co.zw/2025/06/14/brick-by-brick-strategy-will-benefit-the-police-minister-kazembe/>). On 17 January 2024 he said [the police were looking for a computerisation partner](<https://nehandaradio.com/2024/01/17/police-in-zimbabwe-looking-for-computerization-partner-kazembe/>). In December 2021 the Information minister said [government would bear all costs of digitising rural stations](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>).

**Records made digitally.** Crime reports. In October 2026 the Herald described the system piloted at Warren Park as [a digital crime reporting system](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zrp-pilots-digital-crime-reporting-system-developed-by-harare-institute-of-technology-at-warren-park-police-station/>). In December 2025 the officer commanding Harare Province said [the police would soon move to an electronic system for all dockets](<https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/>).

**How the record is made.** On 2 September 2024 the Commissioner General said [the Zimbabwe Republic Police is not computerised](<https://www.263chat.com/police-yet-to-embrace-technology-matanga/>). He called it the only force in the region in that state.

**Last twelve months.** In October 2026 the Home Affairs minister told the National Assembly that [crime management is being tested at Warren Park and a couple of other stations](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zrp-pilots-digital-crime-reporting-system-developed-by-harare-institute-of-technology-at-warren-park-police-station/>). Wider deployment is to follow once it is seen to work.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In June 2025 the minister said [a prototype crime management system was being tested at Warren Park Police Station](<https://newsreelzimbabwe.co.zw/2025/06/14/brick-by-brick-strategy-will-benefit-the-police-minister-kazembe/>), in Harare. By October 2026 [the pilot had reached a couple of other stations](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zrp-pilots-digital-crime-reporting-system-developed-by-harare-institute-of-technology-at-warren-park-police-station/>). No count of stations has been found.

***Also on file***. Traffic: [an Electronic Traffic Management System](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zimbabwe-urges-human-oversight-as-ai-enters-criminal-justice/>) and [the ZRP Smart Traffic Management System](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609230312.html>). [A Smart Transport and Traffic Management System was planned in 2021](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pledges-to-provide-internet-services-to-rural-police-officers/>). In October 2025 [point-of-sale machines with Starlink were planned for all police roadblocks](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zim-to-roll-out-pos-machines-starlink-kits-at-police-roadblocks/>). Surveillance: [drones to monitor traffic and public spaces](<https://stalliontimes.com/zimbabwe-police-embrace-electronic-docket-system/>), and [a smart traffic system launched in Harare under the Safe City Project](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>). Enabling: [the Presidential Internet Scheme](<https://zimtreasury.co.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2026-ZIMBABWE-INFRASTRUCTURE-INVEST-PROGRAMME-FINAL-WEB.pdf>), which by 2025 had connected over 100 government institutions, police stations among them.

***Not found***. No source found shows a station outside the pilot recording reports or dockets. No source found since 2021 says how the system is to be paid for.
