# Comoros

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. The criminal record is kept by [a directorate of civil status and criminal records in the justice ministry](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Decret-N26-003PR-du-16-jan-2026.pdf>), as a decree of 16 January 2026 names it. The budget framing letter for 2025 names [a national programme for biometric driving licences](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LETTRE-DE-CADRAGE-BUDGETAIRE-2025.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether stations record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
