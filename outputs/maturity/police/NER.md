# Niger

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The national police's Direction de la Police Technique et Scientifique in Niamey [works the database, and its director is responsible for recording criminal acts in Niger](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>), EUCAP Sahel Niger reported on 23 May 2019. The system, launched in Niger in 2012, is [financed by the European Union under the Emergency Trust Fund for Africa and implemented by INTERPOL with EUCAP Sahel Niger](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>).

**Records made digitally.** The database, which Niger calls the Système d'Information Policière en Afrique de l'Ouest (SIPAO), [holds offences, criminals' identities and criminal proceedings](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>), and the team recording criminal acts had entered more than 8,000 cases by May 2019.

**How the record is made.** Cases are [entered by a team of 8 under the director in Niamey](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>), while operators are being trained to record data directly from their own service, by the same report of May 2019.

**Last twelve months.** EUCAP Sahel Niger [reported on 23 May 2019 that 700 new cases were recorded in the month](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In May 2019 the entry was [made in Niamey, by the team of 8](<https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/news/wapis-sipao-un-fichier-de-police-pour-le-niger-2019-05-23_en>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identification: the [fichier d'identification et de traçage biométriques](<https://www.lesahel.org/reception-de-materiel-didentification-et-de-tracage-biometriques-a-la-dst-pour-mieux-lutter-contre-les-reseaux-criminels-dimmigration-irreguliere-et-de-traite-des-personnes-au-nige/>) of the Direction de la surveillance du territoire, the [Fichier automatisé des empreintes digitales](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_FR-LR.pdf>) and the [fichier central de la Gendarmerie](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_FR-LR.pdf>). Public: the portal [service-public.ne](<https://service-public.ne/fr>).

***Not held***. Nothing since May 2019 says the system reached stations outside Niamey or still runs. No count of stations is held, and what the Niamey team enters from is not stated.
