# Kenya

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** On 13 May 2026 the National Police Service [presented the programme carrying digital Occurrence Books to Parliament as its own investment](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>). On 1 April 2026 the Interior Cabinet Secretary said [the government was itself procuring the digital occurrence book](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/state-turns-to-ai-to-curb-insecurity-5410358>).

**Records made digitally.** The occurrence book. The service's annual report for 2022 says its Digital Occurrence Book was [launched at Kasarani Sub-County, Nairobi, on 21 November 2019](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>). On 30 June 2023 the Interior Cabinet Secretary said [complaints, statements and details of crimes made at stations are recorded in it](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>).

**How the record is made.** In June 2023 a deputy inspector general said [reports entered at the station could be followed by senior commanders](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>).

**Last twelve months.** In May 2026 the Inspector General told MPs that [digital Occurrence Books would come in three to four months](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>). In August 2026 the Interior Cabinet Secretary [said all 1,200 stations would have them by the end of 2026](<https://peopledaily.digital/news/kenya-police-to-install-cctv-cameras-in-1200-stations-roll-out-digital-ob-by-end-of-2026>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Star reported that [76 police stations in and around Nairobi had digital Occurrence Books by 30 June 2023](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>). In March 2026 the Uasin Gishu police commander said the [digital Occurrence Book had been rolled out at five Eldoret stations](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/counties/rift-valley/2026-03-12-eldoret-police-shift-to-digital-ob-system-to-boost-crime-reporting>). More were to follow. In May 2026 the Inspector General told MPs [the service did not yet have digital Occurrence Books](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>).

***Also on file***. Surveillance: the [IC3 command centre at police headquarters](<https://capitalfm.africa/big-brother-watching-movements-police-command-centre/>), with number-plate recognition cameras. Also the [Integrated Public Safety Communication and Surveillance System and the CCTV Installation Project](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/30364/>), and the [Nairobi Safe City system](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>). A [CCTV facial recognition network was proposed for six cities](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/kenya-considers-linking-national-id-database-to-cctv-facial-recognition-network>). The [IC4 system and body-worn cameras](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>) are planned. So are [police communication centres and CCTV in about 1,200 stations](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126592-police-use-ai-tools-assess-police-deployment-cs-murkomen-announces>). Single-function: [instant fines for traffic offences](<http://nationalpolice.go.ke/inspector-general-douglas-kanja-attends-presentation-digitization-police-services>) and the [Electronic Crash System, e-Crash](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>). Personnel: the [Utumishi Project's HR system, duty roster and patrol system](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>). Also the [automation of police administration](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2020/08/kenyas-national-police-service-goes-digital/>), the [National Police Reservists' data](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-12-02-all-chiefs-to-get-tablets-to-report-incidents-digitally-ruto>) and an [AI-powered deployment system](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126592-police-use-ai-tools-assess-police-deployment-cs-murkomen-announces>). Statistics: the [Utumishi Project's crime-trend report generator](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>). Identification: the [DCI National Forensic Laboratory](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>). Public: [police services on eCitizen](<https://www.oagkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AUDITOR-GENERALS-REPORT-ON-NATIONAL-GOVERNMENT-2024-2025.pdf>), and the [police complaints system and police clearance on Gava Mkononi](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>). Enabling: [network connectivity and ICT equipment](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/national/130982/national-police-service-seek-sh20-billion-to-digitize-operations>), sought from development partners in March 2025.

***Not found***. No source found says what became of the Nairobi system after June 2023. The Eldoret rollout of March 2026 and the Inspector General's statement of May 2026 disagree, and nothing found reconciles them. No domestic budget line or dated plan naming the system has been found.
