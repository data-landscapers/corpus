# Kenya

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Police Service [presented the programme that carries digital Occurrence Books to Parliament on 13 May 2026 as its own investment](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>), and the Interior Cabinet Secretary said on 1 April 2026 that [the government was itself procuring the digital occurrence book](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/state-turns-to-ai-to-curb-insecurity-5410358>). On 26 March 2025 the service [asked development partners for Sh20 billion for digitisation, the digital Occurrence Book included](<https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/national/130982/national-police-service-seek-sh20-billion-to-digitize-operations>).

**Records made digitally.** The occurrence book. The service's annual report for 2022 says its Digital Occurrence Book was [launched at Kasarani Sub-County, Nairobi, on 21 November 2019](<http://www.nationalpolice.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-10/ANNUAL%20REPORT%202022%20A4%20SIZE.pdf>), and on 30 June 2023 the Interior Cabinet Secretary said [complaints, statements and details of criminal activity made at stations are recorded in it](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>).

**How the record is made.** On 30 June 2023 the Deputy Inspector General of Administration Police said [reports entered at the station were open to monitoring by ward, county and regional commanders and the Inspector General](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>).

**Last twelve months.** On 13 May 2026 the Inspector General told a parliamentary committee that [digital Occurrence Books would come within three to four months](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>). On 28 August 2026 the Interior Cabinet Secretary said [they would accompany police communication centres set up first in Nairobi, Mombasa, Nyeri, Nakuru, Eldoret and Kisumu](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126592-police-use-ai-tools-assess-police-deployment-cs-murkomen-announces>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Star reports that [76 police stations in and around Nairobi had their Occurrence Books digitised by 30 June 2023](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-07-03-76-police-occurrence-books-digitised-in-modernisation-drive>). On 13 May 2026 the Inspector General told MPs [the service did not yet have digital Occurrence Books](<https://www.capitalfm.co.ke/news/2026/05/police-turn-to-technology-cctv-and-body-cameras-to-secure-2027-polls/>).
