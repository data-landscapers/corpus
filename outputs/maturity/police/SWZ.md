# Eswatini

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Integrated Electronic Case Management System, which Eswatini calls IECMS, [is the Ministry of Justice's programme](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>). A Technical Working Group supports its training sessions, and Synergy International Systems supports implementation. Independent News reported this in August 2025.

**Records made digitally.** Royal Eswatini Police Service officers [were trained on entering new cases into the system](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>) at the ministry's training of trainers in August 2025. They [were walked through all the components of a docket](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>).

**How the record is made.** At the Mbabane and Manzini One Stop Centres [the police record by hand in occurrence books](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>), UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2025. Police, prosecution and social welfare keep separate paper registers.

**Last twelve months.** The [training of police trainers on the system](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>) was reported on 20 August 2025.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** At the Manzini One Stop Centre [the police complete paper forms that go to headquarters every 2 to 3 months](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>). The forms are not kept on site, UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2025.

***Also on file***. Identification: the police's [Digital Forensic Unit](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/natcom-praises-digital-forensic-unit-for-cybercrime-fight/>). Single-function: the [national register for sexual offenders](<https://gov.sz/images/DPMOFFICE/NATIONAL-STRATEGY-TO-END-VIOLENCE-IN-ESWATINI-2023-2027-Final.pdf>) and the paper [Police Service forms and RSP-88 form for cases of sexual and gender-based violence](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>).

***Not found***. No station, region or start date has been found for police entry of cases in the IECMS. The paper recording is described at two One Stop Centres only, not at stations nationally. Nothing has been found on a plan or the finance, or on whether the police case file passes digitally to the court.
