# Eswatini

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Integrated Electronic Case Management System, which Eswatini calls IECMS, [is the Ministry of Justice's programme, with a Technical Working Group supporting its training sessions and Synergy International Systems supporting implementation](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>), Independent News reported in August 2025.

**Records made digitally.** Royal Eswatini Police Service officers [were trained on entering new cases into the system and were walked through all the components of a docket](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>) at the ministry's training of trainers in August 2025.

**How the record is made.** At the Mbabane and Manzini One Stop Centres [the police record by hand in occurrence books, and police, prosecution and social welfare keep separate paper registers](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>), UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2025.

**Last twelve months.** The [training of police trainers on the system](<https://independentnews.co.sz/22407/news/reps-attend-iecms-training/>) was reported on 20 August 2025.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** At the Manzini One Stop Centre [the police complete paper forms that go to headquarters every 2 to 3 months and are not kept on site](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>), UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. Identification: the police's [Digital Forensic Unit](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/natcom-praises-digital-forensic-unit-for-cybercrime-fight/>). Single-function: the [national register for sexual offenders](<https://gov.sz/images/DPMOFFICE/NATIONAL-STRATEGY-TO-END-VIOLENCE-IN-ESWATINI-2023-2027-Final.pdf>) and the paper [Police Service forms and RSP-88 form for cases of sexual and gender-based violence](<https://www.unicef.org/eswatini/media/2241/file/OSC%20Eswatini%20Evaluation%20Report.pdf.pdf>).

***Not held***. No station, region or start date is held for police entry of cases in the IECMS, and the paper recording is described at two One Stop Centres only, not at stations nationally. A plan, the finance and whether the police case file passes digitally to the court are not held.
