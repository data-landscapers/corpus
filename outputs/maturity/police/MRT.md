# Mauritania

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The central office for collecting police information, in the judicial police directorate of the General Directorate of National Security, [supervises the police information system](<https://mourassiloun.com/node/36165>), which the directorate calls WAPIS and which its Director General launched on 19 June 2025.

**Records made digitally.** The system is meant to digitise the work of police commissariats and to [document the reports and complaints made at commissariats and their offices](<https://mourassiloun.com/node/36165>), a report of the launch states.

**How the record is made.** Central commissioners and heads of judicial police sections in the commissariats [register in a database held by the central office](<https://mourassiloun.com/node/36165>), to which police centres are being linked, by the same report of 19 June 2025.

**Last twelve months.** On 19 June 2025 the Director General [put the system to work](<https://mourassiloun.com/node/36165>) for central directors, the regional directors of the Nouakchott wilayas, central commissioners and heads of judicial police sections in the commissariats.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** At the launch the director of the central office [gave the centres linked as 27 of the National Police and 7 of the National Gendarmerie](<https://mourassiloun.com/node/36165>), and the report puts the rollout across Nouakchott's commissariats at 90 per cent complete.
