# Burkina Faso

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The computerised main courante platform is the security ministry's name for its occurrence-book system. [It belongs to the Ministère de l'Administration Territoriale, de la Décentralisation et de la Sécurité](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/>). In late December 2023 that ministry's director of information systems reported on it to a delegation of the digital transition ministry.

**Records made digitally.** In late December 2023 the platform [was not yet operational](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/>). The digital transition ministry put it at more than 80 per cent built.

**How the record is made.** The platform [is to replace the physical registers in which main courantes are kept at police commissariats and gendarmerie brigades](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/>). In late December 2023 those registers were still the record.

**Last twelve months.** The ministry's director of information systems spoke in late December 2023. The director said the platform [would be operational by mid-January 2024, once amendments were taken in](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** In late December 2023 [no commissariat or gendarmerie brigade was using the computerised main courante](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2023/12/30/digital-burkina-faso-main-courante-informatisee-la-fin-du-papier-pour-les-forces-de-lordre/>).

***Also on file***. Public: the [online criminal-record request](<https://www.moussonews.com/digitalisation-des-services-au-burkina-un-soulagement-pour-les-citoyens/>) and [online cybercrime complaints and reporting](<https://www.moussonews.com/digitalisation-des-services-au-burkina-un-soulagement-pour-les-citoyens/>). The justice ministry names its platform for filing and following a complaint [Justice Pénale en ligne](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>). The ministry also runs [e-Permis de Communiquer](<https://burkina24.com/2025/12/15/justice-et-transformation-numerique-le-burkina-faso-passe-a-la-vitesse-superieure-avec-un-triple-lancement/>) for visits to detainees. The cybercrime brigade's reporting platform is [Alerte BCLCC](<https://libreinfo.net/cybercriminalite-au-burkina-2/>). Single-function: [video fining of traffic offences in Ouagadougou](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/03/securite-routiere-a-ouagadougou-naissance-de-la-bricir-pour-en-finir-avec-lincivisme-sur-les-routes/>), in service since 1 August 2026. Enabling: the [office equipment of the Galgouli border police post](<https://burkina24.com/2025/10/30/securite-aux-frontieres-de-nouvelles-infrastructures-pour-le-poste-de-police-frontiere-de-galgouli/>).

***Not found***. Nothing dated on or before 30 September 2026 says the platform went live in any commissariat or brigade. No plan, source of finance, count of stations or event in the last twelve months has been found.
