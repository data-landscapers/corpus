# Botswana

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. The Botswana Police Service runs [cameras at the international airport and nine border posts, monitored from police headquarters](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/91996>), under what it calls the safer city programme, and [traffic enforcement technologies](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/91996>) (June 2026). Statistics Botswana [gave the police tablets from the 2021 census](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>) in the year to 31 March 2024, to move the capture of administrative statistics from manual forms to digital entry.

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether stations record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
