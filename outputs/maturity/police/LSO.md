# Lesotho

## Station records

***Also on file***. Public: [Lehokela](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/blog/lehokela-seven-years-making-one-national-platform>), the police service's mobile application for reporting incidents, launched nationally on 9 April 2026. Enabling: the communications authority's [Wi-Fi project for thirteen police stations](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFP-POLICE-STATIONS-WI-FI-PROJECT-31-JULY-2026_final-.pdf>), at request for proposal in July 2026. In June 2026 [20 desktop computers were handed to the police service](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/undp-lesotho_undplesotho-peacebuilding-digitaltransformation-activity-7471073069931941888-3nAP>). The [ICT Enablement Initiative](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/news/lesotho-advances-digital-and-strategic-policing-launch-lehokela-app-policy-frameworks-and-ict-initiative>) was launched in April 2026. [Servers, tablets, laptops and desktops were procured for Lehokela](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/blog/lehokela-seven-years-making-one-national-platform>). The inspection begun in July 2023 found [office computers working at Mohale's Hoek police station](<https://ombudsman.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Final-Police-Inspections-2023-A.pdf>). Single-function: the paper [prisoner's property register](<https://ombudsman.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Final-Police-Inspections-2023-A.pdf>). Identification: [LMPS_RIMS](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sekake-lenka-a7a8b0190_lesotho-mounted-police-service-record-information-activity-7178391503185076224-uSJu>), a private developer's project begun in 2019.

***Not found***. No source found says that stations record on paper only. The Ombudsman's inspection begun in July 2023 found posts without stationery keeping registers by hand. The Inspector of Police's 2024/25 report describes a makeshift paper incident book at a station. No records system for stations has been found.
