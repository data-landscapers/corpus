# Lesotho

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: [Lehokela](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/blog/lehokela-seven-years-making-one-national-platform>), the police service's mobile application for reporting incidents, launched nationally on 9 April 2026. Enabling: the communications authority's [Wi-Fi Project - Thirteen Police Stations](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/RFP-POLICE-STATIONS-WI-FI-PROJECT-31-JULY-2026_final-.pdf>), at request for proposal in July 2026, the [20 desktop computers handed to the police service](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/undp-lesotho_undplesotho-peacebuilding-digitaltransformation-activity-7471073069931941888-3nAP>) in June 2026, the [ICT Enablement Initiative](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/news/lesotho-advances-digital-and-strategic-policing-launch-lehokela-app-policy-frameworks-and-ict-initiative>) launched in April 2026, the [servers, tablets, laptops and desktops procured for Lehokela](<https://www.undp.org/lesotho/blog/lehokela-seven-years-making-one-national-platform>) and the [office computers at Mohale's Hoek police station](<https://ombudsman.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Final-Police-Inspections-2023-A.pdf>), found working in the inspection begun in July 2023. Single-function: the paper [prisoner's property register](<https://ombudsman.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Final-Police-Inspections-2023-A.pdf>). Identification: [LMPS_RIMS](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sekake-lenka-a7a8b0190_lesotho-mounted-police-service-record-information-activity-7178391503185076224-uSJu>), a private developer's personal project begun in 2019.

***Not held***. The one fact on station records, from the Ombudsman's inspection begun in July 2023, shows named posts without stationery keeping registers and forms by hand; it does not say that stations record on paper only, nor what the other inspected stations do. No records system for stations is named, and nothing is held on its owner, the records made, the stations or the last twelve months.
