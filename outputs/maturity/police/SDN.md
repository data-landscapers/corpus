# Sudan

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The police plan for 2024 to 2030, as reported in June 2026, [lists digital systems development among its 39 projects in 21 programmes](<https://www.sudaninet.net/78278>).

**Records made digitally.** A thesis abstract from Sudan University of Science and Technology, of 2022, [proposes converting the reports taken in Sudanese police stations to an electronic system](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

**How the record is made.** The same abstract [describes the system for reports in police stations as the old paper one](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>). Its subject is the report that opens the legal procedure for medical treatment after an incident, [known as Form 8](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

**Last twelve months.** Police databases were reported in June 2026 as [restored after the war](<https://www.sudaninet.net/78278>), and as having helped settle administrative and financial matters and the payment of entitlements.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The 2022 thesis [reports no electronic report system in use in police stations](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: the police's [electronic report platform](<https://soutalwatan.net/?p=703>), through which citizens had filed 80,902 reports from its creation in June 2023 to January 2025; the [Salem application](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>), renewing driving licences for citizens abroad; [smart applications for passports, identity cards and driving licences](<https://fjajpress.net/en/police-forum-5-digital-transformation-and-identity-protection-take-center-stage-in-the-battle-of-dignity/>), planned in September 2026; and [cashless payment at the public service complexes](<https://fjajpress.net/en/police-forum-5-digital-transformation-and-identity-protection-take-center-stage-in-the-battle-of-dignity/>). Single-function: the [national vehicle database and the Ansab platform for transfers of vehicle ownership](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>), the second under development in July 2026; the [traffic system](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/en/archives/1171>) in which stolen vehicles were blocked from licensing and sale in 2023; and a [unified national smart electronic traffic platform](<https://alnawrs.com/222769>), not yet launched in June 2026. Surveillance: [tracking of intercity buses and 20 cameras in Khartoum State](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>).

***Not held***. How stations have recorded reports since 2022, and since the war began, is not known. Which records any station system would cover, who owns and funds it, and how many stations use one are not held.
