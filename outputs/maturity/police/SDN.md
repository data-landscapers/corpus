# Sudan

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The police plan for 2024 to 2030 was reported in June 2026. It [lists digital systems development among its 39 projects in 21 programmes](<https://www.sudaninet.net/78278>).

**Records made digitally.** A thesis abstract from Sudan University of Science and Technology dates from 2022. It [proposes converting the reports taken in Sudanese police stations to an electronic system](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

**How the record is made.** The same abstract [describes the system for reports in police stations as the old paper one](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>). Its subject is the report that opens the legal procedure for medical treatment after an incident, [known as Form 8](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

**Last twelve months.** Police databases were reported in June 2026 as [restored after the war](<https://www.sudaninet.net/78278>). They were reported to have helped settle administrative and financial matters and the payment of entitlements.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The 2022 thesis [reports no electronic report system in use in police stations](<https://repository.sustech.edu/handle/123456789/27396>).

***Also on file***. Public: the police's [electronic report platform](<https://soutalwatan.net/?p=703>), created in June 2023. Citizens had filed 80,902 reports through it by January 2025. The [Salem application](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>) renews driving licences for citizens abroad. [Smart applications for passports, identity cards and driving licences](<https://fjajpress.net/en/police-forum-5-digital-transformation-and-identity-protection-take-center-stage-in-the-battle-of-dignity/>) were planned in September 2026. There is also [cashless payment at the public service complexes](<https://fjajpress.net/en/police-forum-5-digital-transformation-and-identity-protection-take-center-stage-in-the-battle-of-dignity/>). Single-function: the [national vehicle database and the Ansab platform for transfers of vehicle ownership](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>). Ansab was under development in July 2026. In 2023 stolen vehicles were blocked from licensing and sale in the [traffic system](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/en/archives/1171>). A [unified national smart electronic traffic platform](<https://alnawrs.com/222769>) was not yet launched in June 2026. Surveillance: [tracking of intercity buses and 20 cameras in Khartoum State](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>).

***Not found***. No source found says how stations have recorded reports since 2022, and since the war began,. Which records any station system would cover, who owns and funds it, and how many stations use one are not held.
