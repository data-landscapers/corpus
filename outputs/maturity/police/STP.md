# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Station records

***Also on file***. The Polícia Nacional has a [Centro de Comando e Controlo and a Sistema Zulu, which allows the compilation of crime statistics](<https://saotomeandprincipe.unfpa.org/pt/news/unfpa-colabora-na-informatiza%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-pol%C3%ADcia-nacional>). These were expected to launch in August 2023 in the district of Água Grande. They were [spoken of as being implemented in January 2024](<https://saotomeandprincipe.unfpa.org/pt/news/unfpa-oferece-13-computadores-alguns-parceiros>). At the Polícia Judiciária, [XRY and Passware were in operational use as at June 2026](<https://www.unodc.org/westandcentralafrica/en/westandcentralafrica/stories/2026/so-tom-and-prncipe-builds-a-complete-digital-evidence-chain--from-the-phone-to-the-courtroom.html>). [Computer equipment was handed over at its headquarters on 21 January 2026](<https://saotomeeprincipe.un.org/pt/308817-unodc-refor%C3%A7a-capacidade-da-pol%C3%ADcia-judici%C3%A1ria-com-entrega-de-equipamentos-inform%C3%A1ticos>). The [*registo criminal* is kept by the Centro de Identificação Civil e Criminal, a central service in the city of São Tomé](<https://dgrn.gov.st/CICC.php>). [DireitosAoPovo.st and its reporting modules VidaSemMedo and Denuncia were in development in June 2026](<https://www.unodc.org/westandcentralafrica/en/westandcentralafrica/stories/2026/when-walking-into-a-police-station-is-the-barrier--build-a-door-that-opens-from-the-other-side.html>). The [Sistema de Emissão de Documentos issues criminal record certificates and has run at the embassy in Portugal since April 2025](<https://www.jornaltransparencia.st/host134.htm>).

***Not found***. No source found states whether stations record reports or cases on paper or digitally. Nor has a system or a count of stations been found.
