# Ghana

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Government of Ghana [launched what it calls the Case Tracking System in 2018, with the support of USAID, as a common platform for the Police Service, the Attorney General's office, the Prisons Service, the Legal Aid Commission, the Economic and Organised Crime Office and the Judicial Service](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>).

**Records made digitally.** The Asokwa Municipal Police Commander said in May 2022 that the system [had eliminated the use of paper as case dockets in the prosecution of criminal cases](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>). The Legal Resources Centre reported in 2022 that [2,475 cases had been registered on it from December 2021](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>).

**How the record is made.** The commander said he [could monitor cases in all stations within his jurisdiction from his office through the system, with unstable internet and electricity hindering it](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>). In the Volta Region, [104 laptops, 95 tablets and 93 connectivity units were given out to run it](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>), by the centre's 2022 report.

**Last twelve months.** On 23 May 2022 the project [was being supported by USAID, whose Justice Sector Support Activity also paid for an awareness campaign](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/three-ngos-sensitise-greater-accra-communities-on-adr/>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Legal Resources Centre reported in 2022 that [55 police stations and 3 prisons services were using the system, in seven regions and over 40 districts](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>): Greater Accra, Ashanti, Northern, Volta, Bono, Western and Eastern. In July 2019 the West African Police Information System [had more than 35 units in the capital, shared among police, immigration and prisons, and none in the regions](<https://www.interpol.int/es/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_EN_LR.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The police's [suicide mortality data, compiled into district and national crime statistics](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Evidence%20Generation%20for%20Suicide%20Prevention%20in%20Ghana.%20A%20Situational%20Analysis.pdf>); the [automated fingerprint identification system, the Criminal Records Database System and the Police Gazette](<https://police.gov.gh/en/index.php/criminal-data-service-bureau-cdsb/>) of the Criminal Data Service Bureau; the [National Crime Automated Biometric Identification System](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cameras-to-replace-police-mttd-personnel-in-checking-traffic-violations-from-september-2026-muntaka.html>); the Cybercrime Unit's [digital forensic hardware and software, and the Cyber Security Authority's 24-hour reporting line](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cyber-security-authority-czech-republic-partner-to-train-journalists-in-cybercrime-reporting.html>); [forensic laboratories, a Real-Time Crime Centre and a crime-reporting mobile app](<https://myrepubliconline.com/igp-yohuno-outlines-digital-future-for-ghana-police-with-ai-and-real-time-crime-tools/>); the traffic enforcement system the police call [Traffitech-GH](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/police-to-enforce-automated-traffic-offences-from-october-1.html>); [CCTV surveillance in Accra](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>); [CCTV in crime-prone areas and online surveillance with the regulator and internet providers](<https://www.ndpc.gov.gh/media/Ministry_of_the_Interior__2026-2029_MTDP.pdf>); and the [eGovNet network, the online fingerprint check and Ghana.gov](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>).
