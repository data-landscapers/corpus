# Ghana

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Government of Ghana [launched the Case Tracking System, as it calls it, in 2018 with USAID support](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>). It is a common platform for the Police Service, the Attorney General's office and the Prisons Service. The Legal Aid Commission, the Economic and Organised Crime Office and the Judicial Service share it too.

**Records made digitally.** In May 2022 the Asokwa Municipal Police Commander said the system [had eliminated paper case dockets in criminal prosecutions](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>). The Legal Resources Centre reported in 2022 that [2,475 cases had been registered on it from December 2021](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>).

**How the record is made.** The commander said he [could monitor cases in all stations within his jurisdiction from his office through the system](<https://www.newsghana.com.gh/more-training-needed-on-cts-police-superintendent/>). Unstable internet and electricity hindered it. In the Volta Region, [104 laptops, 95 tablets and 93 connectivity units were given out to run it](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>), by the centre's 2022 report.

**Last twelve months.** On 23 May 2022 [USAID was supporting the project](<https://thechronicle.com.gh/three-ngos-sensitise-greater-accra-communities-on-adr/>). Its Justice Sector Support Activity also paid for an awareness campaign.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Legal Resources Centre reported in 2022 that [55 police stations and 3 prisons services were using the system](<https://lrcghana.org/lets-resource-case-tracking-system-lrc/>). They were in seven regions and over 40 districts: Greater Accra, Ashanti, Northern, Volta, Bono, Western and Eastern. In July 2019 the West African Police Information System [had more than 35 units in the capital](<https://www.interpol.int/es/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_EN_LR.pdf>). They were shared among police, immigration and prisons, and none were in the regions.

***Also on file***. The police compile [suicide mortality data into district and national crime statistics](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Evidence%20Generation%20for%20Suicide%20Prevention%20in%20Ghana.%20A%20Situational%20Analysis.pdf>). The Criminal Data Service Bureau runs the [automated fingerprint identification system, the Criminal Records Database System and the Police Gazette](<https://police.gov.gh/en/index.php/criminal-data-service-bureau-cdsb/>). There is a [National Crime Automated Biometric Identification System](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cameras-to-replace-police-mttd-personnel-in-checking-traffic-violations-from-september-2026-muntaka.html>). The Cybercrime Unit has [digital forensic hardware and software, and the Cyber Security Authority runs a 24-hour reporting line](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/cyber-security-authority-czech-republic-partner-to-train-journalists-in-cybercrime-reporting.html>). The police have [forensic laboratories, a Real-Time Crime Centre and a crime-reporting mobile app](<https://myrepubliconline.com/igp-yohuno-outlines-digital-future-for-ghana-police-with-ai-and-real-time-crime-tools/>). They call their traffic enforcement system [Traffitech-GH](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/police-to-enforce-automated-traffic-offences-from-october-1.html>). There is [CCTV surveillance in Accra](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>). Also on file are [CCTV in crime-prone areas and online surveillance with the regulator and internet providers](<https://www.ndpc.gov.gh/media/Ministry_of_the_Interior__2026-2029_MTDP.pdf>). So are the [eGovNet network, the online fingerprint check and Ghana.gov](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>).

***Not found***. No total of stations to set beside the 55, and nothing on the system since 2022. Whether the first report of an occurrence is recorded digitally, and whether rural stations use the system.
