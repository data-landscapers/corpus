# Côte d'Ivoire

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. A decree adopted on 28 April 2021 created [the police information system the government names SIP CI](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/691443/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-28-avril-2021>), to be shared by all law-enforcement services. The steering committee of the regional fingerprint project recommended on 21 July 2022 that [the country's existing fingerprint system be restored or upgraded as a priority](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/18784/file/WAPIS%20Newsletter%20no.%2021%20December%202022%20FR.pdf?inLanguage=fre-FR&version=4>), and the national police ran [biometric checks against files of suspected terrorists](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/21338/file/24COM004829%20-%20WAPIS_Newsletter_%202024-01_FR_LR_v2_Optimized.pdf>) during the football tournament of January and February 2024. The government's record for 2011-2025 lists [urban video protection in the 12 police prefectures, a platform against cybercrime, a project to buy counter-terrorism software and computer equipment at the Didiévi commissariat](<https://bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19393>), which opened on 25 April 2025. Abidjan has [a city-wide camera network with a command centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/01/08/smart-cities/>) (2026). The justice ministry's e-Justice platform had [all 39 first-instance tribunals connected by 29 June 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642911/societe/e-justice-les-39-tribunaux-de-premiere-instance-desormais-connectes-au-reseau-numerique-ivoirien>) and issues criminal-record extracts online. The post office's application, launched on 16 January 2026, [takes requests for criminal records](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1768657500>). A November 2025 review of digital services lists [a portal for vehicle registration, licences and fines, and recommends an electronic criminal record](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/177385013966.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing held states whether police stations or gendarmerie brigades record reports or cases on paper or digitally, in what system, or in how many stations.
