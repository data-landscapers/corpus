# Sierra Leone

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Sierra Leone Police's Strategic Development Plan 2025-2029 names [what it calls an e-Case Management System, for case tracking and processing](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>). It is key intervention 4.3, with Crime Services the responsible directorate. The plan says [government allocation, limited and late, remains the force's main funding](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** The plan sets the system [against a 2024 baseline](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>). A survey on the status of case management is due in 2025, with its recommendations implemented from 2026 to 2028. An effective system is the 2029 target.

**How the record is made.** The plan lists [a transition from a paper-based to a digital records management system among its priorities](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>), on a 2024 baseline. A report of 27 July 2026 says [stationery would be kept mainly for operational work](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/slp-embraces-digital-transformation-with-distribution-of-computers-to-local-unit-commanders/>). Its examples are statement-taking and physical record-keeping.

**Last twelve months.** The force [handed 39 laptops to Local Unit Commanders for electronic correspondence](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/slp-embraces-digital-transformation-with-distribution-of-computers-to-local-unit-commanders/>), as reported on 27 July 2026.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The 2025-2029 plan describes [internet connectivity as poor in most police facilities](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>). It schedules connection for the six regional headquarters in 2025 and provincial divisional headquarters in 2027 and 2028.

***Also on file***. The [West Africa Police Information System had 21 work stations by October 2024](<https://www.police.gov.sl/latest-news-and-events/igp-sellu-receives-equipment-for-100-wapis-work-stations-in-sierra-leone/>). [A fingerprint programme is attached to it](<https://voiceofsalonenewspaper.com/interpol-wapis-afis-team-engages-sierra-leone-police-eu-delegation-to-strengthen-regional-security-cooperation/>). [A Personnel Management System was under development in July 2025, alongside digital skills training for 1,000 officers](<https://ayvnews.com/dsti-police-partner-to-digitise-human-resource-processes/>). [Local Unit Commanders received 39 laptops](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/slp-embraces-digital-transformation-with-distribution-of-computers-to-local-unit-commanders/>). The 2025-2029 plan lists [CCTV cameras in the Western Area and a digital radio system](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-04/police_strategy_booklet_29th_jan-2025.pdf>). It plans number-plate recognition, a Safe City project, a command and control centre, body-worn cameras and drones. It also plans smart police stations with apps for public reporting and a digital records management system for personnel. Bar-coded badges, station internet and solar power are planned too.

***Not found***. Whether the 2025 survey of case management was done, or any other step taken to prepare the planned system. No count of stations, and no budget for the system.
