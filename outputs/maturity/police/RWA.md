# Rwanda

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [Ministry of Justice manages the Integrated Electronic Case Management System](<https://www.rib.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RIB/Journey/RIB-The_2017-2023_Journey.pdf>). The Rwanda Investigation Bureau's history of its first years, published in October 2024, states this.

**Records made digitally.** The bureau [has incorporated the system to process criminal investigation files](<https://www.rib.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RIB/Journey/RIB-The_2017-2023_Journey.pdf>), by the same history. The bureau wrote that history of its own work, covering the years 2017 to 2023.

**How the record is made.** The handling of cases is [digitalised from investigation to final judgment through the system](<https://www.rib.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RIB/Journey/RIB-The_2017-2023_Journey.pdf>), the bureau's history states.

**Last twelve months.** The bureau's history was published in October 2024. It [covers 2017 to 2023 and gives the system as the place its investigation files are processed](<https://www.rib.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RIB/Journey/RIB-The_2017-2023_Journey.pdf>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The history gives the entry of criminal investigation files as [the bureau's own](<https://www.rib.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RIB/Journey/RIB-The_2017-2023_Journey.pdf>). The system it uses is the one the justice ministry manages.
