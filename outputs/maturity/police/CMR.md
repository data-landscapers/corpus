# Cameroon

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** GIZ's factsheet on its support to the Cameroonian police, status June 2020, [presents the digitised investigations and proceedings system as the Délégation générale à la sûreté nationale's own, and says the DGSN must also overcome unreliable internet connection, electricity and computer equipment](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** In June 2020 the police had [a digitised system for processing and transmitting information on investigations, criminal proceedings, arrest warrants, fines and current sanctions](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The system [processes and transmits information on investigations and criminal proceedings](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>), on the June 2020 factsheet's account.

**Last twelve months.** At June 2020, the date of GIZ's factsheet on its programme with the Cameroonian police, the digitised system for investigations and criminal proceedings was [barely used](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** GIZ [put the system's slight use down to weak institutional and management capacity](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>) at the Délégation générale à la sûreté nationale in its factsheet of June 2020.

***Noted, not assessed***. Identification: the [criminal records kept on paper index cards](<https://ijnrd.org/papers/IJNRD2504292.pdf>) at the courts of first instance and the Ministry of Justice, and the police judiciaire's [digitised fingerprint database](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). Single-function: the DGSN's planned [digital platform for users' complaints](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>) and its [internal mechanism for complaints against police officers](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). Public: [the 1500](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>), as the police name their telephone number for complaints, and the [mobile numbers for following a national identity card](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>).

***Not held***. Which units use the system, whether any is outside the capital, where and how entries are made and how many stations enter are not held. Nothing is held since June 2020.
