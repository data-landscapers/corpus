# Cameroon

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** In June 2020 GIZ [presented the digitised investigations system as the Délégation générale à la sûreté nationale's own](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). It said the DGSN also had to overcome unreliable internet, power and computers.

**Records made digitally.** In June 2020 the police had [a digitised system for investigations, criminal proceedings, arrest warrants, fines and sanctions](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** On GIZ's June 2020 account the system [processes and transmits information on investigations and criminal proceedings](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). It does not say where entries are made.

**Last twelve months.** Nothing is held from the last twelve months. GIZ said in June 2020 that the system was [barely used](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** GIZ [put the system's slight use down to weak institutional and management capacity](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). It gave no count of units using it.

***Also on file***. Identification: [criminal records on paper index cards](<https://ijnrd.org/papers/IJNRD2504292.pdf>) at the courts and the Ministry of Justice. Also the police judiciaire's [digitised fingerprint database](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). Single-function: the DGSN's planned [digital platform for users' complaints](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>). Also its [internal mechanism for complaints against officers](<https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2020_fr_appui_a_la_police_camerounaise.pdf>). Public: [the 1500](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>), the police complaints number. Also the [mobile numbers for following an identity card](<https://www.stopblablacam.com/societe/0803-8394-le-cameroun-veut-mettre-en-place-une-plateforme-numerique-pour-traiter-les-plaintes-des-usagers-contre-la-police>).

***Not found***. Which units use the system, whether any is outside the capital, where and how entries are made and how many stations enter are not held. Nothing is held since June 2020.
