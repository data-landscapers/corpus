# Somalia

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The International Development Law Organization (IDLO) [procures the planned case management system with the Office of the Attorney General](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>). That office is to receive the source code on completion, and the vendor reports to IDLO (April 2024). The money is [from the United States Department of State, under its two-year Somalia Justice Support Programme](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>).

**Records made digitally.** IDLO stated on 19 April 2024 that [the police use paper-based systems](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>). It said the planned system would be the first electronic case management system for Somalia's federal justice institutions.

**How the record is made.** On the same date IDLO wrote about the police and the Custodial Corps. It said they [had never made a serious attempt to develop an electronic case management system](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** IDLO [invited bids on 19 April 2024, due 3 June 2024](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>). The system was to be developed over 15 months.

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The tender's criminal investigation module would let investigators create a complaint when it is received at a police station or call centre. [Its first phase covers only the Criminal Investigation Department in Mogadishu](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>), and nothing was built in April 2024. Puntland is a member state with its own police. There, computer stations for nine regional investigation offices, linked to a server in Garowe, [were still to be installed in 2021](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eucap-som/puntland-state-police-enhance-their-information-management-system-support-eucap-somalia_en>). The European Union's mission gave the equipment.

***Also on file***. Statistics: a [digital crime statistics system](<https://www.unodc.org/documents/commissions/CCPCJ/CCPCJ_Sessions/CCPCJ_31/TD_presentations/UNODC_Somalia_Elena_JULVE_session_1_PM.pdf>), created under UNODC's lead and populated with data reported by police across Somalia (2022). Identification: the Somali Police Force's [Automated Biometric Identification System](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Jan/199793/fbi_seeks_vendors_to_support_somalia_s_biometric_identification_system.aspx>), housed at Aden Adde International Airport in Mogadishu (2025). Public: [online filing of a complaint to a nearby police station, with payment of fees](<https://www.idlo.int/sites/default/files/rfp_n-96-2024-som_electronic_case_management_system.pdf>). This was a planned function of the tendered system in 2024. Enabling: [nine police offices refurbished and given vehicles and IT equipment](<https://www.unodc.org/documents/commissions/CCPCJ/CCPCJ_Sessions/CCPCJ_31/TD_presentations/UNODC_Somalia_Elena_JULVE_session_1_PM.pdf>) with UNODC's support (2022). The national ICT policy of 2019 states a [strategy of connectivity for rural police stations](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says whether the police enter reports or cases in any system built since the 2024 tender. No station is named as doing so. Nothing has been found on a plan naming a station record system, or dated in the last twelve months.
