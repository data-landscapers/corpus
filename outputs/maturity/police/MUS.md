# Mauritius

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Crime Occurrence Tracking System is [the police's own, answered for by the Commissioner of Police through the Prime Minister as Minister of Home Affairs, with the government's Central Informatics Bureau working with the police on its replacement](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf>), as the National Assembly heard on 17 June 2025.

**Records made digitally.** The Prime Minister told the Assembly on 17 June 2025 that the system [manages and tracks complaints and declarations from initial reporting to final case disposal, and has been operational since 2011](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf>). Défi Media reported in March 2023 that [statements made at police stations, once handwritten in Occurrence Books, have been entered in it since 2015](<https://defimedia.info/police-ce-qui-change-entre-1968-et-2023>).

**How the record is made.** The system is [a digital platform connecting all police stations to the main server at Police Headquarters, used on personal computers in the stations](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf>) (June 2025). The [entries are made directly on computers linked to a server](<https://defimedia.info/police-ce-qui-change-entre-1968-et-2023>) (March 2023).

**Last twelve months.** On 17 June 2025 the Prime Minister said the system [had been enhanced but never upgraded since 2011, and that the police had begun to replace it with a new case management system, with a special consultant to be retained](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** The Prime Minister said on 17 June 2025 that [all police stations are equipped with personal computers for the system](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/index.php/download/472/2025/23646/debate-no-16-of-2025-unrevised-tuesday-17-june-2025.pdf>), which [Défi Media's account of his reply also carries](<https://newsmoris.com/mauritius-police-to-modernize-systems-with-rs-43-million-budget/>).

Public: [the police hotlines 999 and 112, Crime Stoppers 148 and the complaint line 800 2345](<https://govmu.org/EN/infoservices/justiceanddefence/Pages/justice.aspx>), [the L'Espwar hotline](<https://police.govmu.org/police/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Annual-Report-2024-2025-C.pdf>), [the Sekirite app](<https://achpr.au.int/sites/default/files/files/2024-08/mauritius-11th-periodic-report2019-2024.pdf>), and [the online application for a Certificate of Character and the Panic Button](<https://police.govmu.org/police/?mdocs-file=6214>). ***Not held***. The number of police stations and the share of them entering records in the system: the one figure is of stations equipped. No plan or finance for the system is stated, and nothing is dated in the twelve months to 30 September 2026.
