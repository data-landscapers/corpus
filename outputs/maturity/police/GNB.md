# Guinea-Bissau

## Station records

**Governance, planning, finance.** A steering committee of several entities oversees the centre for collecting and processing criminal information that Guinea-Bissau calls SIPAO. [INTERPOL accompanies it and the European Union finances it](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=43960>). The Minister of Justice and Human Rights said so on 17 April 2023.

**Records made digitally.** Guinea-Bissau [does not have a system for recording criminal occurrences in police stations](<https://ads.ifba.edu.br/dl2773>). The author of an undergraduate final project at IFBA in Brazil stated this in August 2024.

**How the record is made.** The author gave that absence as [the first reason for building a prototype](<https://ads.ifba.edu.br/dl2773>). The prototype was a web application tested in 2024 by ten users of Guinean nationality.

**Last twelve months.** On 12 September 2025 INTERPOL described the West African Police Information System. It said the system was [strengthening information exchange and coordination among the region's law enforcement agencies](<https://www.interpol.int/News-and-Events/News/2025/President-of-Guinea-Bissau-visits-INTERPOL-headquarters>).

**Stations doing the digital entry.** A new SIPAO service beside the Guarda Nacional command in Bissau was described in August 2024. It was called [a centre where all police forces work and share information](<https://ang.gw/policia-judiciaria-diretor-nacional-diz-que-a-luta-contra-a-criminalidade-organizada-exige-nova-estrategia/>). In 2024 the centre's director named [extension to all law-enforcement institutions as the main challenge still ahead](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/21338/file/24COM004829%20-%20WAPIS_Newsletter_%202024-01_FR_LR_v2_Optimized.pdf>).

***Also on file***. Identification: the [criminal register at Buba, digitised in a test](<https://www.undp.org/guinea-bissau/blog/e-justice-guinea-bissau-learning-what-works-or-not>), and the [fingerprint identification system for which Guinea-Bissau was to be prepared](<https://www.interpol.int/fr/content/download/18784/file/WAPIS%20Newsletter%20no.%2021%20December%202022%20FR.pdf?inLanguage=fre-FR&version=4>), as at 2022. Public: the Polícia Judiciária's [pj.gw online services portal](<http://web.archive.org/web/20250715214735/https://pj.gw/>) and the [justice portal for online criminal record applications](<https://www.undp.org/guinea-bissau/blog/e-justice-guinea-bissau-learning-what-works-or-not>), planned in 2023. Enabling: the [Model Police Station at Sintchan Tombom, Gabú](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-08/undp_annual_report_2024.pdf>), opened in 2024.

***Not found***. No source found says how stations record. The statement that no station system exists is one author's, with no source cited. No station is named as entering its cases at the criminal-information centre in Bissau. Nothing has been found on a plan, on the last twelve months or on stations.
