In 2023 the World Bank named the [digitisation of criminal records a government priority](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>). On 20 July 2026 the state agency ADETIC [handed the national police 74 desktop computers, 59 laptops and 105 surveillance cameras](<https://techgriot.co/english/africa/2026/07/chad-equips-its-national-police-with-new-tech-to-fight-cybercrime/>). The donation is to bring the police public hotline, out of service, back online. In February 2025 the government pledged [the construction, renovation and equipping of police services and commissariats for all provinces](<https://primature.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Projet-de-Declaration-politique-du-Gouvernement-.pdf>). From 1 January 2026 [recruitment and career acts of the national police are initiated and validated in SIGFiP](<https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Loi-n008-AN-SENAT-2025-Portant-loi-de-Finances-2026-LF-2026.pdf>).
