# Gabon

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [digital driving licence](<https://www.union.sonapresse.com/fr/permis-de-conduire-digitalise-le-coup-de-frein>), launched on 24 March 2026 with enrolment suspended in September 2026, the [harmonised register of places of detention](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-justice-ouverture-de-latelier-de-validation-du-registre-harmonise-des-lieux-de-privation-de-liberte/>), at its validation workshop on 14 July 2026, the [standardised custody register](<https://www.apt.ch/news/gabon-moves-strengthen-judicial-safeguards-standardised-custody-registers>), a paper model presented in February 2025, and the [digitalisation of road transport documents](<https://gouvernement.ga/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Communique-final-publiee-du-Conseil-du-29-janvier-2026-1.pdf>), noted as operational by the Council of Ministers on 29 January 2026. Identification: the national police's [Fichier Automatisé de Recherche d'Identités et d'Antécédents Judiciaires](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/804-forces-de-police-nationale-la-digitalisation-en-marche-/>), a project presented on 16 February 2026. Personnel: the [Système Informatisé du Tableau d'Avancement](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/804-forces-de-police-nationale-la-digitalisation-en-marche-/>), presented the same day. Public: the gendarmerie's [Identity Secu](<https://ethiquemediagabon.net/?p=14508>) mobile application, deployed at the PK9 brigade in Libreville on 8 May 2026. Enabling: the state network [RAG](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), connecting the Oyem commissariat by April 2026, and the [computer equipment given to the PK9 brigade](<https://ethiquemediagabon.net/?p=14508>).

***Not held***. The only records system held is a vendor's design presented at a Libreville seminar on 10 January 2026, with no adoption by the police, the gendarmerie or a ministry stated and no station using it. No dated statement says how stations record today, and no plan, finance or count of stations is held.
