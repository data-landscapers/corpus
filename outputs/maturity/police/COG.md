# Congo

## Station records

***Also on file***. Public: the [criminal-record extract in the first wave of state services to go online](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>), planned in July 2026. An [eGov portal](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-44.pdf>) is to be set up under European Investment Bank financing. A [Portail e-Citizen](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>) is planned. The UN country team reported in December 2025 that the [numéro vert d'alerte](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/RCO_CONGO_UPR45_COG_F_Main.pdf>) for violence against children was not working. Single-function: [eTraffrika](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/securite-routiere-des-policiers-et-gendarmes-formes-lutilisation-des-radars-mobiles-167214>), traffic-offence software in a pilot in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire in September 2025. A commissariat keeps a [special register](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>) of lost, stolen or destroyed identity cards under the decree of 21 November 2024. Surveillance: two [mobile radars](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/securite-routiere-des-policiers-et-gendarmes-formes-lutilisation-des-radars-mobiles-167214>) in the same pilot. Identification: the [central criminal records service](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/APC%20Rapport%20enquete%20ATTI_FINAL_1.pdf>), for which a network with the judicial police was planned in 2014. Orders of June 2022 organised the [national civil identification file](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2022/congo-jo-2022-22.pdf>), alphabetical and fingerprint.

***Not found***. The one statement that stations work by hand is a survey of commissariats in Pointe-Noire and Nkayi from January 2014. Nothing has been found on Brazzaville, the rest of the country or the gendarmerie beyond one brigade. Nothing dated since has been found. Nothing has been found on who would own a station system, or from the last twelve months.
