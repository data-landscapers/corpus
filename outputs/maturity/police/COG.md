# Congo

## Station records

***Noted, not assessed***. Public: the [criminal-record extract in the first wave of state services to go online](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>), planned in July 2026, the [eGov portal](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-44.pdf>) to be set up under European Investment Bank financing, the planned [Portail e-Citizen](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>) and the [numéro vert d'alerte](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/RCO_CONGO_UPR45_COG_F_Main.pdf>) for violence against children, which the UN country team reported in December 2025 as not working. Single-function: [eTraffrika](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/securite-routiere-des-policiers-et-gendarmes-formes-lutilisation-des-radars-mobiles-167214>), traffic-offence software in a pilot in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire in September 2025, and the [special register](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>) a commissariat keeps of lost, stolen or destroyed identity cards under the decree of 21 November 2024. Surveillance: two [mobile radars](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/securite-routiere-des-policiers-et-gendarmes-formes-lutilisation-des-radars-mobiles-167214>) in the same pilot. Identification: the [central criminal records service](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/APC%20Rapport%20enquete%20ATTI_FINAL_1.pdf>), for which a network with the judicial police was planned in 2014, and the [national civil identification file](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2022/congo-jo-2022-22.pdf>), alphabetical and fingerprint, organised by orders of June 2022.

***Not held***. The one statement that stations work by hand is a survey of commissariats in Pointe-Noire and Nkayi from January 2014; nothing is held on Brazzaville, the rest of the country or the gendarmerie beyond one brigade, and nothing dated since. Who would own a station system, and anything from the last twelve months, is not held.
