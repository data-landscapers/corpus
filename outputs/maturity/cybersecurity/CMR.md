# Cameroon

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [ANTIC, the national ICT agency, runs Cameroon's national Computer Incident Response Team](<https://www.ictacam.org/understanding-the-hidden-face-of-cybercrime-in-cameroon-a-deep-dive-into-the-numbers/>). [The team received new equipment worth over FCFA 735 million on 20 January 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/21/cameroon-strengthens-cybersecurity-with-advanced-systems-at-national-cirt/>). [In May 2025 its application to join FIRST was under way](<https://www.antic.cm/index.php/en/component/k2/item/443-strengthening-cameroon-s-cyber-resilience-through-global-cooperation-and-capacity-building.html>).

**Responding to incidents.** [In March 2026 ANTIC's team went on site to a ransomware attack that took down half a public body's IT system](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/81162/en.html/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-riposte-nationale-s-organise>). [In 2025 ANTIC blocked 33 fraudulent loan apps and closed 4,110 fake Facebook accounts](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/prosper-pagou-antic-en-2025-nous-avons-enregistre-17-500-plaintes-pour-un-prejudice-de-58-milliards-de-fcfa/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [ANTIC says the law requires companies to report cybercrime to it](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/prosper-pagou-antic-en-2025-nous-avons-enregistre-17-500-plaintes-pour-un-prejudice-de-58-milliards-de-fcfa/>). No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure.

**Published figures.** [ANTIC states it recorded 17,500 complaints in 2025, with losses of 5.8 billion FCFA](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/prosper-pagou-antic-en-2025-nous-avons-enregistre-17-500-plaintes-pour-un-prejudice-de-58-milliards-de-fcfa/>). [It counted 21,280 machines in the country caught in botnets that year](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/prosper-pagou-antic-en-2025-nous-avons-enregistre-17-500-plaintes-pour-un-prejudice-de-58-milliards-de-fcfa/>).
