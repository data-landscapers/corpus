# Cameroon

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Cameroon Tribune reports ANTIC, the national ICT agency, has an incident-handling service and co-hosted a national cyberspace security forum in Yaoundé opened 2026-09-29](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/81162/en.html/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-riposte-nationale-s-organise>). [The ISS report of August 2026 states that ANTIC is the institutional hub for cyber governance in Cameroon and operates a national computer emergency response team](<https://issafrica.org/research/books-and-other-publications/digital-deception-online-job-scams-amid-cybercrime-vulnerability-in-cameroon-and-chad>).

**Responding to incidents.** [ANTIC presented a March 2026 ransomware case at a public body, where its team went on site, after which 50% of the body's IT system was down and email was cut for nearly a month](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/81162/en.html/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-riposte-nationale-s-organise>). [The ISS report states that ANTIC's national CERT issues public alerts](<https://issafrica.org/research/books-and-other-publications/digital-deception-online-job-scams-amid-cybercrime-vulnerability-in-cameroon-and-chad>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/81162/en.html/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-riposte-nationale-s-organise>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/81162/en.html/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-riposte-nationale-s-organise>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [CSIRT/SOC/PKI platform (planned, feasibility study)](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=4499&type_publication=AMI>).

***Not held***. No source held says the country has designated its critical information infrastructure.
