# Mauritania

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Ecofin says Mauritania implements a National Digital Security Strategy for 2022-2026 with six strategic objectives](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>). [The National Cybersecurity Strategy 2022-2025, which includes a planned national CERT/CSIRT, is still being implemented](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>). [Ecofin says a April 2024 decree created the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Electronic Certification to protect national cyberspace](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).
