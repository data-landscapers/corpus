# Mauritania

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Ecofin says Mauritania implements a National Digital Security Strategy for 2022-2026 with six strategic objectives](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>). [The National Cybersecurity Strategy 2022-2025, which includes a planned national CERT/CSIRT, is still being implemented](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>). [Ecofin says a April 2024 decree created the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Electronic Certification to protect national cyberspace](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>), and [a government presentation of November 2024 dates the agency to 23 April 2024 and gives it the task of setting up CERT Mauritanie](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session6/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20(Fr)_MrAmar_Mau.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1301-51879-u-s-mauritania-discuss-cybersecurity-partnerships-and-capacity-building>).
