# Namibia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** NAM-CSIRT is Namibia's national incident response team, housed at the Communications Regulatory Authority (CRAN). [A new executive took over national incident response on 1 October 2026](<https://technews.com.na/2026/10/03/cran-appoints-cornelia-shipindo-to-lead-namibias-cyber-incident-response/>), with CRAN's chief executive as head of the team. [It issued an advisory on data protection in September 2026](<https://economist.com.na/nam-csirt-calls-for-heightened-data-protection-and-governance-amid-rising-cyber-risks/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [In September 2026 NAM-CSIRT confirmed unauthorised activity on the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs network](<https://technews.com.na/2026/09/20/namibias-defence-ministry-hit-by-double-extortion-ransomware-attack/>). It blamed RansomHouse and said it was coordinating investigation and remediation. [After the March 2026 breach at Namibia Airports Company, it worked with the company to contain it](<https://neweralive.na/namibias-cyber-threats-soar/>). [On 22 October 2026 it warned of new PHP flaws](<https://www.namibian.com.na/php-security-flaws-put-namibian-websites-at-risk/>) and advised upgrades.

**Critical infrastructure.** Namibia has listed its critical systems. [In October 2024 the ICT ministry said a register of critical information infrastructure had been developed](<https://www.namibiansun.com/focus-tel-and-tech/cybersecurity-strategy-well-on-track-ministry-says2024-10-29135112>). It covers national critical infrastructure too.

**Published figures.** NAM-CSIRT publishes quarterly counts of what it detects. [Its newsletter for July to September 2026 counted 506,143 vulnerabilities and 163,053 cyber events](<https://neweralive.na/namibian-cybersecurity-risks-remain-elevated-nam-csirt/>), New Era reports. It also warned of SharePoint and VMware vCenter flaws and reviewed two incidents, one at the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs. [The newsletter for April to June 2026 counted 513,921 vulnerabilities and 161,547 events](<https://www.namibian.com.na/hackers-hit-ndf-ransomhouse-threatens-state-secrets/>). [For 2025 it gave 1,701,883 cyber-attack events and 2,231,970 reported vulnerabilities](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>).
