# Namibia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [NAM-CSIRT, the national cyber security incident response team housed at CRAN, is stated to exist and operate: a new Executive took over on 1 October 2026 to lead national incident response, with CRAN's CEO as Head of NAM-CSIRT](<https://technews.com.na/2026/10/03/cran-appoints-cornelia-shipindo-to-lead-namibias-cyber-incident-response/>). [NAM-CSIRT is stated to be housed at the Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia (CRAN) and to have issued an advisory](<https://economist.com.na/nam-csirt-calls-for-heightened-data-protection-and-governance-amid-rising-cyber-risks/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [NAM-CSIRT issued a public advisory urging stronger data protection and responsible handling of data leaked in cyber incidents](<https://economist.com.na/nam-csirt-calls-for-heightened-data-protection-and-governance-amid-rising-cyber-risks/>). [NAM-CSIRT confirmed unauthorised activity on the Ministry of Defence and Veterans Affairs network, attributed it to RansomHouse and said it is coordinating investigation, remediation and post-incident review](<https://technews.com.na/2026/09/20/namibias-defence-ministry-hit-by-double-extortion-ransomware-attack/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.namibian.com.na/hackers-hit-ndf-ransomhouse-threatens-state-secrets/>).

**Published figures.** [NAM-CSIRT's Q2 2026 newsletter reports 513,921 cyber vulnerabilities and 161,547 cyber events detected in April-June 2026, up 39.8% and 56.7% on the previous quarter. (Reported by The Namibian, 2026-09-20.)](<https://www.namibian.com.na/hackers-hit-ndf-ransomhouse-threatens-state-secrets/>). [NAM-CSIRT states 1,701,883 cyber-attack events and 2,231,970 reported vulnerabilities for January to December 2025](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>).
