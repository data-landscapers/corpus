# Tunisia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Agence nationale de la cybersécurité (ANCS) relayed government circular no. 5 and the 2023 cybersecurity decree-law framework; the body operates in September 2026](<https://www.challenges.tn/high-tech/cybersecurite-en-tunisie-deux-circulaires-en-trois-semaines-les-entreprises-privees-sont-prevenues>). [ANCS published a list of N-Cloud entries on 17 September 2026, showing the national agency operating](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/22/573106/cybersecurite-et-entreprises-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-publiques-et-leurs-fournisseurs-prives/>). [Incidents are declared to the Agence nationale de la sécurité informatique (ANSI), so the national body exists and operates (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/07/cyberattaques-tunisie-explosion-ransomwares/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [The national cybersecurity agency issued a public warning on 30 July 2026 about an SMS phishing campaign using fake traffic-fine notices from foreign numbers to harvest banking data](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/151319-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B0%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%B1%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%84-%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%81-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.challenges.tn/high-tech/cybersecurite-en-tunisie-deux-circulaires-en-trois-semaines-les-entreprises-privees-sont-prevenues>).

**Published figures.** [According to ANCS, Tunisia recorded over 57,000 cyberattacks in the first half of 2025](<https://www.challenges.tn/high-tech/cybersecurite-en-tunisie-deux-circulaires-en-trois-semaines-les-entreprises-privees-sont-prevenues>). [Incidents declared to ANSI rose by over 146% in 2022, from 63,000 in 2021 to over 155,000 (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/08/07/cyberattaques-tunisie-explosion-ransomwares/>).
