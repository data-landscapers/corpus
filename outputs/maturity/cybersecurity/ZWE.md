# Zimbabwe

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [MISA Zimbabwe states that a Cybersecurity and Monitoring Centre operates under the Office of the President with no judicial oversight](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>). [NewsDay reports the government unveiled a national cybersecurity strategy at a Harare conference on Wednesday 15 October 2025, the ICT minister saying its development was completed; the period is not stated](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047300/government-enforces-data-privacy-laws>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).
