# Zimbabwe

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [POTRAZ, the telecom regulator, set up a Computer Incident Response Team to handle security incidents in the country](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/zimbabwe-intensifies-fight-against-rising-cybercrimes>). [A Cybersecurity and Monitoring Centre also operates under the Office of the President](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>). [The government unveiled a national cybersecurity strategy on 15 October 2025](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047300/government-enforces-data-privacy-laws>).

**Responding to incidents.** [The POTRAZ team handled incident reports through 2025, a third of them fraud](<https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/>). [Cyberbullying made up 27% of cases and hacking 17%](<https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [The stage rests on the POTRAZ team's 2025 caseload](<https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/>).

**Published figures.** [POTRAZ gave its 2025 caseload as shares by type, not as a count](<https://technomag.co.zw/33-of-zimbabwe-crimes-now-cyber-related/>). No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more.

***Also on file***. [AI-Cybersecurity Fusion Centre](<https://misa.org/blog/implications-of-zimbabwes-national-ai-strategy-on-freedom-of-expression-privacy-and-access-to-information/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the country has designated its critical information infrastructure.
