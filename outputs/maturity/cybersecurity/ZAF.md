# South Africa

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cybersecurity Hub, South Africa's National CSIRT, was set up in 2015 by the then Department of Telecommunications and Postal Services](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>). [Its mandate is the National Cybersecurity Policy Framework, passed by Cabinet in March 2012](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>). [It receives and routes incident reports on weekdays](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>). [On 13 September 2018 a SADC statement said South Africa had an established and operational national CIRT](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>).
