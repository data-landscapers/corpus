# South Africa

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cybersecurity Hub, South Africa's National CSIRT, was established in 2015 by the then Department of Telecommunications and Postal Services, mandated by the National Cybersecurity Policy Framework passed by Cabinet in March 2012, and receives and routes incident reports on weekdays](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>). [The SADC statement of 13 September 2018 says South Africa has an established and operational national CIRT (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.cybersecurityhub.gov.za/incident-management-process>).
