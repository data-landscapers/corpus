# Gabon

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The ANINF (Agence nationale des infrastructures numériques et des fréquences) is stated to provide protection, supervision and resilience of the digital infrastructures in its perimeter, with security-event analysis, forensics, isolation and restoration support, and to have been approached by public bodies for audits and investigations in recent months](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cybermenaces-ce-que-laninf-dit-vraiment-des-900-000-alertes-qui-font-polemique/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cybermenaces-ce-que-laninf-dit-vraiment-des-900-000-alertes-qui-font-polemique/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cybermenaces-ce-que-laninf-dit-vraiment-des-900-000-alertes-qui-font-polemique/>).
