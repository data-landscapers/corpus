# Central African Republic

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Agence Nationale de la Cybersecurite was created by the cybersecurity law of January 2024, and that it appears in neither FY2026 budget volume; the network-security line is FCFA 4,000,000 (proposed, 2026)](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf>).
