# Tanzania

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Minister states Tanzania, through TCRA, established TZ-CERT under the Electronic and Postal Communications Act, 2010, and that it monitors, detects and responds to cybersecurity threats](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>). [The Tanzania Computer Emergency Response Team (TZ-CERT) reports a steady increase in cyber incidents in the country, so it exists and operates](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/experts-back-tanzania-s-cyber-security-push-amid-growing-digital-threats-4998368>). [Tanzania recently launched a National Cybersecurity Strategy (2023-2028)](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/experts-back-tanzania-s-cyber-security-push-amid-growing-digital-threats-4998368>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>).
