# Tanzania

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [TZ-CERT was set up within TCRA, the communications regulator, under the Electronic and Postal Communications Act, 2010](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>). [The minister says it monitors, detects and responds to cyber threats](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/uploads/text-editor/files/MINISTERS%20STATEMENT%20WITSD%202026_1779118337.pdf>). [Tanzania launched a National Cybersecurity Strategy for 2023-2028](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/experts-back-tanzania-s-cyber-security-push-amid-growing-digital-threats-4998368>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. [TZ-CERT reports a steady rise in cyber incidents in the country](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/experts-back-tanzania-s-cyber-security-push-amid-growing-digital-threats-4998368>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Government Notice No. 147 of 19 June 2026 designates 25 categories of computer systems as critical information infrastructure](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>). [Owners must register them with the ministry and submit quarterly risk assessments](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more. [The stage rests on the June 2026 designation order](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>).
