# Togo

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** Togo has a national cybersecurity agency, ANCy, and a response team, CERT.tg. [Law 2018-026 created ANCy in December 2018](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_07_12_2018-63E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B024TER.pdf>). [In August 2025 the head of the identity agency said Cyber Defense Africa, a public-private company, runs CERT.tg](<https://www.toutafrica.com/2025/08/25/e-id-togo-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-le-togo-a-fait-le-choix-strategique-dutiliser-mosip/>). [ANCy presented the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2024-2028 in Lomé in October 2024](<https://ancy.gouv.tg/communique-de-presse-relatif-a-latelier-de-presentation-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-cybersecurite-2024-2028/>). [In September 2026 it was touring the country to raise awareness](<https://togoregard.tg/cybersecurite-lancy-sensibilise-pres-de-200-acteurs-a-kougnohou/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [CERT.tg issued a public alert on a surge of compromises of WhatsApp, Facebook and other online accounts in Togo](<https://savoirnews.net/togo-le-cert-tg-alerte-sur-une-nouvelle-vague-de-piratage-de-comptes-en-ligne/>). [ANCy issued a high-severity alert on an active account-compromise campaign hitting WhatsApp, Facebook and other services, urging two-factor verification](<https://www.republicoftogo.com/toutes-les-rubriques/high-tech/verification-en-deux-etapes>). [More than 333,000 incidents were handled between 2021 and 2024, per ANCy's director-general](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-le-luxembourg-renforce-son-appui-a-la-transformation-numerique-et-a-la-cybersecurite>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says Togo has designated its critical information infrastructure. [The 2018 law makes ANCy the national authority for the security of essential infrastructure](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_07_12_2018-63E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B024TER.pdf>). No list of such infrastructure is on file.

**Published figures.** [ANCy is reported to have recorded and handled 333,000 incidents over 2021-2024, rising from 39,168 in 2021 to 181,088 in 2024](<https://launetogo.tg/cybersecurite-le-togo-erige-la-confiance-numerique-en-priorite-nationale/>). [ANCy director-general Gbota Gwaliba is quoted giving more than 333,000 incidents handled between 2021 and 2024](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-le-luxembourg-renforce-son-appui-a-la-transformation-numerique-et-a-la-cybersecurite>).

***Also on file***. [TogoCyber+](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/togo-le-luxembourg-renforce-son-appui-a-la-transformation-numerique-et-a-la-cybersecurite>).

***Not found***. No source found says the country has designated its critical information infrastructure.
