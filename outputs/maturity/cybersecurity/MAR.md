# Morocco

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Decree 2-11-509 of 21 September 2011 created the DGSSI](<https://north-africa-middle-east-gulf.ec.europa.eu/document/download/4c8fe664-36fd-4721-81eb-5514bd6c0b96_en?filename=C_2026_5364_F1_COMMISSION_IMPLEMENTING_DECISION_FR_V2_P1_4911910.PDF>). [It sits under the National Defence Administration and runs maCERT, its centre for cyber watch and response](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html>). [The DGSSI publishes the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2030, successor to the strategy of 2012](<https://www.dgssi.gov.ma/sites/default/files/publications/pdf/2024-10/Strat%C3%A9gie%20Nationale%20de%20Cybers%C3%A9curit%C3%A9%202030.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** [In June 2026 maCERT warned of the FortiBleed leak, saying several Moroccan institutions were affected](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html>). [Of 879 attacks recorded in 2025, 109 needed direct intervention by the DGSSI's centre](<https://h24info.ma/maroc/dgssi-879-cyberattaques-recensees-en-2025/>). [From January to September 2026 the centre received 419 reports of possible incidents, 123 of which needed direct intervention](<https://www.hespress.com/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a8-%d9%8a%d8%b1%d8%b5%d8%af-%d8%ab%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a8%d8%b1%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%ad%d8%b1%d8%ac%d8%a9-%d9%88%d9%8a%d8%b9%d8%b2%d8%b2-1839739.html>). [On 12 November 2026 the DGSSI issued a critical alert on attacks on ScreenConnect users](<https://lematin.ma/nation/cybersecurite-15-centres-de-surveillance-prevus-la-loi-en-revision/376014>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Decree 2-15-712 of 2016 sets the protection regime for vital-importance infrastructures](<https://www.ausimaroc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Conformite-avec-le-Decret-02-15-712-Apport-de-la-Norme-internationale-ISO-27001.pdf>). It delimits their sectors, has their sensitive systems listed and requires incidents to be declared to the national centre. A white paper by AUSIM, the IT users' association, sets this out. [In 2026 a DGSSI study found these infrastructures' compliance above average and sent each a report](<https://lematin.ma/nation/cybersecurite-15-centres-de-surveillance-prevus-la-loi-en-revision/376014>).

**Published figures.** [For 2026 the minister gave 968 bulletins, 373 of them critical, and 2,154 threats notified to the bodies concerned](<https://www.hespress.com/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a8-%d9%8a%d8%b1%d8%b5%d8%af-%d8%ab%d8%ba%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%b3%d9%8a%d8%a8%d8%b1%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%ad%d8%b1%d8%ac%d8%a9-%d9%88%d9%8a%d8%b9%d8%b2%d8%b2-1839739.html>). [For 2025 the minister gave 879 attacks recorded, 109 direct interventions, 22 alerts and 511 bulletins](<https://h24info.ma/maroc/dgssi-879-cyberattaques-recensees-en-2025/>). [For 2024 to mid-November the DGSSI recorded 644 attacks and issued 575 bulletins](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/11/15/hackers-have-launched-644-cyberattacks-against-morocco-in-2024/>).
