# Morocco

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Ministry of Digital Transition's communique of 25 July 2026 says Morocco, in its statement at the African Telecommunications Union conference, highlighted progress under its national cybersecurity strategy to 2030](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031>). [Morocco's DGSSI, under the National Defense Administration, is stated to host Morocco CyberSecurity Week and to have deployed security operations centres and strengthened oversight and auditing in recent years](<https://www.maroc.ma/en/news/morocco-cybersecurity-week-kicks-rabat>).

**Responding to incidents.** [MaCERT, the DGSSI's cyber watch and response centre, issued a security warning bulletin (ref. 65471806/26) on the FortiBleed leak affecting Fortinet firewalls and SSL VPN gateways, saying several Moroccan institutions were affected and urging MFA and password changes](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.hespress.com/%D8%AD%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8-%D8%B6%D8%AE%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%A8%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA-1765087.html>).
