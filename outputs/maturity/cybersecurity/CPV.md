# Cape Verde

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Ecofin Agency reports Cabo Verde launched a CSIRT within the State's Private Technological Network (RTPE)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>). It is described as a national cyber alert-and-response hub for public institutions and private organisations. [The record states the national SOC and CSIRT were launched on 30 October 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [Cabo Verde launched a CSIRT within the State's private technology network, Ecofin reported in October 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [Cabo Verde launched a CSIRT within the State's private technology network, Ecofin reported in October 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>).
