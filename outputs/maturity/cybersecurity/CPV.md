# Cape Verde

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Ecofin Agency reports Cabo Verde launched a CSIRT within the State's Private Technological Network (RTPE), described as a national cyber alert-and-response hub for public institutions and private organisations](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>). [The record states the national SOC and CSIRT were launched on 30 October 2025](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3110-50013-cape-verde-steps-up-cybersecurity-efforts-with-national-soc-incident-response-team>).
