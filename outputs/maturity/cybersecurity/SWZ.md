# Eswatini

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act of 2022 made the Eswatini Communications Commission (ESCCOM) the national cybersecurity agency](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/esccom-engages-journalists-on-cybersecurity-scams/>). [In October 2025 the Prime Minister said its Cyber Incident Response Team was fully operational](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>). [He also cited the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2022-2027](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [In July 2025 ESCCOM investigated ransomware attacks on public bodies, among them the Eswatini Water Services Corporation](<https://times.co.sz/24234/news/hacking-on-rise-water-services-halts-digital-payments/>). [An alert from its own threat intelligence tools set off the inquiry](<https://times.co.sz/24234/news/hacking-on-rise-water-services-halts-digital-payments/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [A World Bank project paper noted that no critical information infrastructure framework yet existed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062825152022388/pdf/P508948-c554abd8-33f6-48f0-87f8-1d3c340f194c.pdf>). No source found says any system has since been designated.

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more. [The stage rests on ESCCOM's ransomware investigation](<https://times.co.sz/24234/news/hacking-on-rise-water-services-halts-digital-payments/>).
