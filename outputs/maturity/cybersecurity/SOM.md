# Somalia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Prime Minister inaugurated the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team (SOMCIRT) on 7 March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/somalia-strengthens-cyber-defenses-with-launch-of-national-incident-response-center>). It was created under the January 2026 Cybersecurity Law to coordinate prevention, detection and response to incidents nationally. [The National Communications Authority states that the Cybersecurity Law establishes the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team (SOM-CIRT)](<https://nca.gov.so/somalias-parliament-approves-the-cybersecurity-law/>). The House of the People approved the law on 26 January 2026.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record.
