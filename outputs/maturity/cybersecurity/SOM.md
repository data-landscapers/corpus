# Somalia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Prime Minister inaugurated the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team (SOMCIRT) on 7 March 2026, created under the January 2026 Cybersecurity Law to coordinate prevention, detection and response to incidents nationally](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/somalia-strengthens-cyber-defenses-with-launch-of-national-incident-response-center>). [The National Communications Authority states the Cybersecurity Law approved by the House of the People on 26 January 2026 establishes the Somalia Computer Incident Response Team (SOM-CIRT)](<https://nca.gov.so/somalias-parliament-approves-the-cybersecurity-law/>).
