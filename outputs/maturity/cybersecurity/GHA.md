# Ghana

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The op-ed says the Cyber Security Authority, led by Dr Albert Antwi-Boasiako, coordinates national cyber defence under the Cybersecurity Act of 2020](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bold-ambitions-fragile-foundations-is-ghana-ready-for-the-ai-and-data-centre-risks-it-is-inviting/>). [The Cyber Security Authority, headed by Director-General Divine Selase Agbeti, organised a workshop in Accra for National Cyber Security Awareness Month 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-miif-calls-for-stronger-cyber-resilience-to-secure-mining-operations.html>). [The minister states the Cyber Security Authority operates a CERT-GH, which handled incidents in January-June 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [Ghana's Cyber Security Authority recorded 4,938 cybersecurity incidents from January to September 2026, a CSA officer told an awareness-month engagement at Cape Coast](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-records-ghc209m-in-cybercrime-losses-in-nine-months.html>). [The Cyber Security Authority's CERT-GH responded to 3,363 cybersecurity incidents between January and June 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>). [CERT-GH of the Cyber Security Authority responded to 3,363 cybersecurity incidents between January and June 2026, per the Communications Minister](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/cybersecurity-authority-responds-to-3363-incidents-in-six-months-sam-george/>). [On 22 October 2026 the Cyber Security Authority and NITA issued a joint alert on two critical PHP vulnerabilities](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/csa-and-nita-order-government-institutions-to-fix-critical-php-vulnerabilities-within-seven-days/>), directing government institutions to patch within seven days and report back.

**Critical infrastructure.** [The communications minister said Ghana has 13 designated Critical Information Infrastructure sectors and about 200 institutions identified as CII Owners, and that compliance is mandatory](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/08/ghana-strengthens-cyber-resilience-critical-infrastructure/>). [The Minister said vulnerability assessments were completed for 25 Critical Information Infrastructure institutions in education, health, public administration, security and utilities](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/cybersecurity-authority-responds-to-3363-incidents-in-six-months-sam-george/>).

**Published figures.** [The CSA presented counts of 4,938 incidents for January to September 2026 and 4,604 incidents for the whole of 2025, with GH¢209.7 million and GH¢22.5 million in reported losses](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-records-ghc209m-in-cybercrime-losses-in-nine-months.html>). [The minister reported CERT-GH responded to 3,363 incidents for January-June 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>).
