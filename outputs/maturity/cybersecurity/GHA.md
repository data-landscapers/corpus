# Ghana

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cyber Security Authority, headed by Director-General Divine Selase Agbeti, organised a workshop in Accra for National Cyber Security Awareness Month 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-miif-calls-for-stronger-cyber-resilience-to-secure-mining-operations.html>). [Ghana's Cyber Security Authority is stated to exist and operate, having led and coordinated a cybercrime operation in Greater Accra (press release of 8 October 2026)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/09/ghana-rejects-fbi-led-claims-operation-blackout-cybercrime-raids/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [CERT-GH of the Cyber Security Authority responded to 3,363 cybersecurity incidents between January and June 2026, per the Communications Minister](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/cybersecurity-authority-responds-to-3363-incidents-in-six-months-sam-george/>). [The Cyber Security Authority's CERT-GH responded to 3,363 cybersecurity incidents between January and June 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The Minister said vulnerability assessments were completed for 25 Critical Information Infrastructure institutions in education, health, public administration, security and utilities](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/cybersecurity-authority-responds-to-3363-incidents-in-six-months-sam-george/>). [The communications minister said at a CSA workshop on Thursday 8 October 2026 that Ghana had identified 13 critical information infrastructure sectors and about 200 institutions as CII owners](<https://itweb.africa/article/ghana-elevates-cybersecurity-to-essential-services-level/xnklOqz1P1nM4Ymz>).

**Published figures.** [The national CERT, coordinated by the CSA, recorded 3,876 cybersecurity incidents between January and July 2026, about 47 percent online fraud, as cited at the NCSAM 2026 opening](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7574/x>). [The minister reported CERT-GH responded to 3,363 incidents for January-June 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/full-text-sam-georges-speech-at-government-accountability-series-2/>).
