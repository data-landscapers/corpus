# Equatorial Guinea

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Article 21 of Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024 creates the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructures (ANCIC)](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>). It is an autonomous public body under the Presidency of the Government. Its functions are to manage cybersecurity through coordinated CERT/CSIRT teams, issue alerts on cyber incidents and respond to them.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024 created the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructures (ANCIC) to issue alerts and respond to incidents](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024 created the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructures (ANCIC) to issue alerts and respond to incidents](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years. [A December 2024 law created ANCIC to issue alerts on cyber incidents](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).
