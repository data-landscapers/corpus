# Equatorial Guinea

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Article 21 of Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024 creates the National Agency for Cybersecurity and Critical Infrastructures (ANCIC) as an autonomous public body under the Presidency of the Government, with functions to manage cybersecurity through coordinated CERT/CSIRT teams, issue alerts on cyber incidents and respond to them](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/ley_por_la_que_se_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>).
