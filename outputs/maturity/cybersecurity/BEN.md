# Benin

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [ASIN's director general says ASIN was created to set cybersecurity policies and check compliance, and that it collects data through its laboratories and its alert and incident response centre](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/marc-andre-loko-apres-le-lancement-du-rapport-sur-les-vulnerabilites-du-cyberspace-beninois-nous-disposons-aujourdhui-dun-dispositif-solide>). [The digital minister says Benin has set up a national incident response centre](<https://fraternite.bj/5eme-edition-du-cyber-africa-forum-les-assises-de-cotonou-pour-le-renforcement-de-la-cybersecurite/>). [The annexed policy states Benin has adopted its Strategie Nationale de Securite Numerique (SNSN) (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2021-550/download>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/03/benin-releases-first-national-cybersecurity-report-highlighting-rising-threats>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/03/benin-releases-first-national-cybersecurity-report-highlighting-rising-threats>).

**Published figures.** [ASIN presented Benin's first national report on cybersecurity vulnerabilities and incidents, covering 2021 to 2024, which counts 878 vulnerabilities and incidents including malware infections (23), website hacking (12) and botnet attacks (7)](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/03/benin-releases-first-national-cybersecurity-report-highlighting-rising-threats>).
