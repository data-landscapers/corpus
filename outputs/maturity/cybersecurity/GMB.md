# Gambia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [GmCSIRT, the Gambia Computer Security Incident Response Team, describes itself as the national CSIRT](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>). It is a government agency founded in 2020, advising operators of critical information infrastructure. [The ministry's presentation lists a Gambia National Cybersecurity Strategy and policy for 2020-2026 among the country's existing policies and strategies](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [gmCSIRT, the national CSIRT founded in 2020, says it advises operators of critical information infrastructure](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body.
