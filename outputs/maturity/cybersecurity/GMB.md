# Gambia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [GmCSIRT, the Gambia Computer Security Incident Response Team, describes itself as the national CSIRT, a government agency founded in 2020, advising operators of critical information infrastructure](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>). [The ministry's presentation lists a Gambia National Cybersecurity Strategy and policy for 2020-2026 among the country's existing policies and strategies](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gmcsirt_cybersecurity-gmcsirt-pura-activity-7482750733738610688-ab_m>).
