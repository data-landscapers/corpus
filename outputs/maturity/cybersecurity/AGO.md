# Angola

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [President Lourenço's State of the Nation address, as reported by PTI, refers to the approval of the National Cybersecurity Strategy and the structuring of a National Cybersecurity Centre](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>). [Angola's minister said it established the National Cybersecurity Council and the National Cybersecurity Centre](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-strengthens-regional-cybersecurity-and-cybercrime-cooperation>). [Angola's minister said it approved its National Cybersecurity Strategy](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-strengthens-regional-cybersecurity-and-cybercrime-cooperation>). [The Executive approved the National Cybersecurity Strategy in 2025](<https://pti.ao/proposta-de-lei-da-ciberseguranca-entra-na-fase-de-analise-detalhada/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Lei n.º 9/26 requires operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres, cloud and digital services to register with the Centre within 180 days and notify significant incidents to CERT.ao, with deadlines to be set by separate instrument](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).
