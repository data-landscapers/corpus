# Angola

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Lei n.º 9/26 of 28 September 2026, in force on publication, creates a National Cybersecurity System comprising the Conselho Nacional de Cibersegurança, the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança and a national CSIRT network; CERT.ao is the Centre's operational arm (PTI, 2 October 2026)](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>). [The Executive approved the National Cybersecurity Strategy in 2025](<https://pti.ao/proposta-de-lei-da-ciberseguranca-entra-na-fase-de-analise-detalhada/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Lei n.º 9/26 requires operators of critical infrastructure, essential services, electronic communications, data centres, cloud and digital services to register with the Centre within 180 days and notify significant incidents to CERT.ao, with deadlines to be set by separate instrument](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://pti.ao/nova-lei-da-ciberseguranca-impoe-registo-e-notificacao-de-incidentes-a-operadores/>).
