# Chad

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The 2026 budget volume lists ANSICE, the data-protection and cybersecurity agency, with a 400,000,000 FCFA transfer line under the Ministry of Public Security, the third year at that figure](<https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Fascicule-budgAtaire-2026-VF.pdf>). [ANSICE presented its record and six recommendations to the CESCE on 12 August 2026, including operationalising a national CERT and a security operations centre](<https://lendjampost.com/transition-numerique-le-tchad-face-a-lurgence-de-securiser-les-donnees-de-ses-citoyens/>). [Ordinance 001/PR/2026 of 22 January 2026 reformed ANSICE, extending its missions, powers and operational capacity](<https://tchadvision.com/lassemblee-nationale-approuve-la-reforme-de-lansice-un-pas-vers-le-numerique-de-demain/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ecofinances.gouv.td/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Fascicule-budgAtaire-2026-VF.pdf>).
