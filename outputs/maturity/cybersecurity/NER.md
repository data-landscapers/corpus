# Niger

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Council of Ministers adopted on 11 October 2025 the decrees creating the Centre National de Cybersécurité (CNAC) as a public administrative establishment and approving its statutes, to coordinate national cybersecurity strategy including critical systems](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).
