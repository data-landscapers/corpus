# Niger

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [On 11 October 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted the decrees creating the Centre National de Cybersécurité (CNAC)](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>). The decrees make it a public administrative establishment and approve its statutes. It is to coordinate national cybersecurity strategy, including critical systems.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [On 11 October 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted decrees creating the Centre National de Cybersécurité (CNAC)](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [The CNAC, created by decree in October 2025, is to coordinate national cybersecurity, critical systems included](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years. [Niger adopted a national cybersecurity strategy in 2022](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/919-communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-samedi-11-octobre-2025>).
