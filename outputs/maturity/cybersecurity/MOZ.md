# Mozambique

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [INTIC says Law 13/2026 (Cybersecurity Law) formally creates the national-level cyber incident response team and sets its operation at INTIC](<https://intic.gov.mz/seminario-sobre-seguranca-cibernetica-debate-solucoes-para-reforcar-a-proteccao-do-espaco-cibernetico-nacional-2/>). [AIM reports the minister said the National CSIRT Network already has operational teams in government, the Cyber Defence Unit, MoRENet, INCM, telecoms and four provinces](<https://aimnews.org/2026/10/08/governo-reforca-medidas-de-seguranca-cibernetica-em-mocambique/>). [The national cybersecurity policy and strategy were approved by Resolution 69/2021 of 31 December (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Relatorio_NCRA_Maio-2025_assinado.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/intic-na-campanha-contra-ransomware-nao-paguem-resgate-denunciem/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Lei n.º 13/2026 requires critical information infrastructure operators to register with the National Cybersecurity Authority and to notify the National CSIRT of incidents with significant impact (Arts. 17, 59), with fines for failing to report (Arts. 75-76); notification deadlines are left to the Authority](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244>).

**Published figures.** [INTIC's chairman stated the National CSIRT detected 5,654 ransomware infection attempts between January and July 2026](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/intic-na-campanha-contra-ransomware-nao-paguem-resgate-denunciem/>).
