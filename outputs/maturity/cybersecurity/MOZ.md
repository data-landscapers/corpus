# Mozambique

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Law 13/2026, in force from 29 September 2026, makes INTIC the National Cybersecurity Authority](<https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/>). [The law sets up the national CSIRT, nCSIRT.MZ, inside the ICT regulator](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244>). [The 2021-2025 Cybersecurity Policy and Strategy was approved by Resolution 69/2021](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/BR_253_I_SERIE_12.o-SUPLEMENTO_2021_Politica-de-Seguranca-Cibernetica-e-Protecao-e-Estrategia-da-sua-Implementacao.pdf>). [A 2026-2030 strategy was out for consultation in October 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7628/mozambique-consults-sofala-stakeholders-on-national-cybersecurity-strategy-2026-2030>).

**Responding to incidents.** [The Prosecutor-General said the national CSIRT logged 182,031 incidents in 2024](<https://aimnews.org/2025/04/29/mocambique-regista-centenas-de-burlas-de-pagamentos-eletronicos/>). [On 28 September 2025 nCSIRT.mz issued a public alert on fake social media profiles of senior officials](<https://ncsirt.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/COMUNICADO-SOBRE-CONTAS-FALSAS-NAS-REDES-SOCIAIS.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Law 13/2026 requires critical information infrastructure operators to register with the authority](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244>). [They must notify the national CSIRT of significant incidents, with fines for failing to](<https://www.incm.gov.mz/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=5244>). [Reporting became mandatory when the law took effect](<https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/>).

**Published figures.** [INTIC's chairman said the national CSIRT detected 5,654 ransomware infection attempts from January to July 2026](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/intic-na-campanha-contra-ransomware-nao-paguem-resgate-denunciem/>).
