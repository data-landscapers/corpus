# Egypt

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [An NTRA vice president and a deputy minister are reported saying the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023-2027 is being implemented](<https://www.technotime.net/17231>). [The NTRA vice president for cybersecurity is reported saying Egypt established the Supreme Cybersecurity Council and that NTRA monitors cyber threats around the clock; national computer emergency response capabilities are cited](<https://www.technotime.net/17231>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.technotime.net/17231>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.technotime.net/17231>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.technotime.net/17231>).
