# Sierra Leone

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The National Cyber Security Coordination Centre (NC3) is stated to exist](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-plans-digital-systems-sectors/>). With MoCTI it developed draft sector-specific critical information infrastructure protection plans for energy, health, education and defence and security. They were presented for validation on 2026-08-26. [SierraLoaded reports that Sierra Leone's National Cybersecurity Coordination Centre (NC3) acts through the national computer security incident response team, SL-CSIRT](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nc3-warns-of-critical-security-flaws/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [NC3, through SL-CSIRT, issued a critical alert reported on 24 October 2026 on two PHP remote-code-execution flaws](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nc3-warns-of-critical-security-flaws/>). They are CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, with severity 9.2. [NatCA, the telecom regulator, issued a public notice on 30 July 2026 acknowledging ongoing cybersecurity incidents](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-reassures-public-national-cyber-attacks/>). It said it coordinates a response and advised the public on precautions.

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [NC3, through SL-CSIRT, issued a critical alert on two PHP flaws in October 2026](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nc3-warns-of-critical-security-flaws/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years. [SL-CSIRT is Sierra Leone's national incident response team](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nc3-warns-of-critical-security-flaws/>).
