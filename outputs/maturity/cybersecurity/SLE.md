# Sierra Leone

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The National Cyber Security Coordination Centre (NC3) is stated to exist and to have developed, with MoCTI, draft sector-specific critical information infrastructure protection plans for energy, health, education and defence and security, presented for validation on 2026-08-26](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/sierra-leone-plans-digital-systems-sectors/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [NatCA, the telecom regulator, issued a public notice on 30 July 2026 acknowledging ongoing cybersecurity incidents, saying it coordinates a response and advising the public on precautions](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-reassures-public-national-cyber-attacks/>). [NC3 published an advisory on its website in July 2024 warning of a surge in WhatsApp takeover attacks](<https://sierraloaded.sl/feature/inside-sierra-leone-whatsapp-takeover-scam/>). [In October 2026 NC3, through SL-CSIRT, issued a critical alert on two PHP remote-code-execution flaws](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nc3-warns-of-critical-security-flaws/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-reassures-public-national-cyber-attacks/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-reassures-public-national-cyber-attacks/>).
