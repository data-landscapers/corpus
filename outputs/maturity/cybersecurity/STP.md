# Sao Tome and Principe

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [AGER lists the National Cybersecurity Strategy (2024-2029) among São Tomé and Príncipe's existing actions on universal and meaningful connectivity](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>). [AGER states that implementation of the National Cybersecurity Strategy of Sao Tome and Principe (2024-2028) with its annexed action plan is under way, while submitting both to a 30-day public consultation](<https://www.ager.st/index.php/pt/actualidades/19-annonces/106-estrategia-nacional-de-ciberseguranca-de-sao-tome-e-principe>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Dia-3-Sao-Tome-e-Principe-AGER.pdf>).
