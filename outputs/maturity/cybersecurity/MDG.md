# Madagascar

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Madagascar's Computer Incident Response Team (CIRT) is stated to exist and operate, with a Director General, Eric Rakotomaniraka, speaking for it at the inaugural Cybersecurity Symposium in Antananarivo on 22-23 June 2026](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>). [The article names Eric Rakotomaniraka as team head of the CIRT, which is described as taking part in the December 2025 strategy launch workshop](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/vers-la-mise-en-place-dune-strategie-nationale/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>).
