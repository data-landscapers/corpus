# Madagascar

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Madagascar's Computer Incident Response Team (CIRT) is stated to exist and operate, with a Director General, Eric Rakotomaniraka](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>). He spoke for it at the inaugural Cybersecurity Symposium in Antananarivo on 22-23 June 2026. [The article names Eric Rakotomaniraka as team head of the CIRT](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/vers-la-mise-en-place-dune-strategie-nationale/>). The CIRT is described as taking part in the December 2025 strategy launch workshop.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://trendsnafrica.com/madagascar-nears-completion-of-sweeping-cybersecurity-reform-as-authorities-move-to-strengthen-national-cyber-resilience/>).
