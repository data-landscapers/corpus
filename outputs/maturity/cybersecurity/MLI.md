# Mali

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Decree No. 2026-0097/PT-RM of 25 February 2026 adopted the National Cybersecurity Strategy and its action plan for 2026-2030](<https://koulouba.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-10-juin-2026-cm-n2026-23-sgg/>). [On 10 June 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted the texts creating ANSSI](<https://koulouba.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-10-juin-2026-cm-n2026-23-sgg/>), the national information systems security agency. [ANSSI is to ensure national cybersecurity and coordinate the response to cyberattacks](<https://maliactuel.com/cybersecurite-le-mali-se-dote-dune-agence-nationale-pour-proteger-ses-systemes-dinformation/>), Mali Actuel reports.

**Responding to incidents.** No national body is known to have handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. [ANSSI is to coordinate the response to cyberattacks](<https://maliactuel.com/cybersecurite-le-mali-se-dote-dune-agence-nationale-pour-proteger-ses-systemes-dinformation/>), under texts adopted in June 2026.

**Critical infrastructure.** No designation of critical information infrastructure is known. Nor is a duty on the operators of critical systems to report incidents. The nearest fact is the [2026-2030 strategy adopted by decree](<https://koulouba.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-10-juin-2026-cm-n2026-23-sgg/>).

**Published figures.** No count of incidents handled by a national body is known for any period in the last two years. [The texts creating ANSSI were adopted only in June 2026](<https://koulouba.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-10-juin-2026-cm-n2026-23-sgg/>).

***Also on file***. [Pole national de lutte contre la cybercriminalite](<https://www.lessor.ml/posts/pole-de-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-le-ministre-kassogue-salue-loperationnalisation-de-la-chambre-criminelle-6a0efcf332029>).

***Not found***. No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
