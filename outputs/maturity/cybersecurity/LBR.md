# Liberia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The March 2026 National Data Governance Policy lists the National Cybersecurity Strategy among the policy instruments Liberia has adopted](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). In October 2026 [a senator cited the strategy as a step that strengthened Liberia's policy framework](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/senator-cyrus-urges-stronger-cybersecurity-protection-of-citizens-rights/>). Both sources have it running to 2029.

**Responding to incidents.** No national body is known to have handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. The nearest fact is the [strategy listed in the data governance policy](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). The same policy [proposes a national CERT](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No designation of critical information infrastructure is known. Nor is a duty on the operators of critical systems to report incidents. The nearest fact is the [national strategy a senator cited in October 2026](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/senator-cyrus-urges-stronger-cybersecurity-protection-of-citizens-rights/>).

**Published figures.** No count of incidents handled by a national body is known for any period in the last two years. The nearest fact is the [strategy listed in the 2026 data governance policy](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Liberia National CERT (proposed)](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>); [Digital Forensic Lab](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
