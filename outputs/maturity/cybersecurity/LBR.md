# Liberia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The March 2026 National Data Governance Policy lists the National Cybersecurity Strategy (2024-2029) among the policy instruments Liberia has adopted](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Liberia National CERT (proposed)](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>); [Digital Forensic Lab](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
