# Ethiopia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [INSA, with its Director General speaking, is stated on 29 September 2026 to co-chair a newly established Financial Sector Cybersecurity Forum with the National Bank of Ethiopia, for threat sharing and coordinated incident response](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7510/ethiopia-establishes-financial-sector-cybersecurity-forum-to-improve-digital-resilience>). [A National Computer Emergency Response Team is stated to exist as the body receiving incident reports under the newly enacted Proclamation No. 1426/2026, alongside INSA](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-digitalethiopia2030-cybersecurity-activity-7491413571923406848-yYmL>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The proclamation identifies 12 critical infrastructure sectors (ICT, finance, transport, health, energy and others) and requires owners and operators to meet 18 cybersecurity obligations](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Gotera Cloud (G-Cloud)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_digitalethiopia2030-insa-ethiopia-activity-7446822764042293248-2bIo>).

***Not held***. No source held says the national body published a count of incidents for a period ending since September 2024.
