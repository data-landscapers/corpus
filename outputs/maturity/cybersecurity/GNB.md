# Guinea-Bissau

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [ANG reports the Council of Ministers approved the National Cybersecurity Strategy of Guinea-Bissau at its ordinary meeting](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

***Also on file***. [Estratégia Nacional de Cibersegurança (draft decree)](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/>).
