# Guinea-Bissau

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [ANG reports the Council of Ministers approved the National Cybersecurity Strategy of Guinea-Bissau at its ordinary meeting](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [The Council of Ministers approved a National Cybersecurity Strategy in September 2025](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [The Council of Ministers approved a National Cybersecurity Strategy in September 2025](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-diploma-de-conclusao-dos-estudos-do-ensino-secundario/>).
