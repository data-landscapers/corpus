# Malawi

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Malawi CERT is a specialised unit under MACRA and the national focal point for coordinating cybersecurity incidents and threat responses](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>). [MACRA said the Malawi Computer Emergency Response Team (MwCERT) currently operates under the regulator, with work under way to make it an independent institution](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [Malawi CERT, through MACRA, publicly alerted individuals, businesses and ISPs to the actively exploited MikroTik RouterOS vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-67276, CVE-2026-86060, CVE-2026-67277) and advised patching](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>).
