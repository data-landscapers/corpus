# Malawi

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Malawi CERT is a specialised unit under MACRA](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>). It is the national focal point for coordinating cybersecurity incidents and threat responses. [MACRA said the Malawi Computer Emergency Response Team (MwCERT) currently operates under the regulator](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/26/malawi-secures-world-bank-backing-to-strengthen-cybersecurity-and-data-protection-frameworks/>). Work is under way to make it an independent institution.

**Responding to incidents.** [Malawi CERT, through MACRA, publicly alerted individuals, businesses and ISPs to actively exploited MikroTik RouterOS vulnerabilities](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>). They are CVE-2026-67276, CVE-2026-86060 and CVE-2026-67277, and it advised patching.

The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/malawi-warns-of-mikrotik-vulnerabilities/o1Jr5qxPod3qKdWL>).
