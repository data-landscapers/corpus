# Burkina Faso

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** The national information systems security agency, ANSSI, [presented the security of the FasoSign e-signature platform at its launch on 15 September 2026](<https://libreinfo.net/burkina-ladministration-publique-entre-dans-lere-de-la-signature-electronique-avec-fasosign/>). [The Council of Ministers appointed its director-general on 16 April 2026](<https://burkina24.com/2026/04/16/compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-jeudi-16-avril-2026/>). In April 2025 the government [put the agency's security operations centre and response team into service](<https://burkina24.com/2025/04/29/cybercriminalite-les-directeurs-generaux-des-structures-publiques-de-letat-sensibilises-sur-la-problematique/>).

**Responding to incidents.** ANSSI [treated 79 major alerts in the first half of 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cybersecurite-le-niger-sollicite-l-experience-du-burkina-faso-pour-son-soc-national>), its director-general says. It resolved 67% of the incidents reported to it. [In 2024 it sent 19 security alerts](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cybersecurite-le-niger-sollicite-l-experience-du-burkina-faso-pour-son-soc-national>) to the organisations linked to its operations centre. It had sent 50 in 2023.

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country has designated its critical information infrastructure or bound its operators to report incidents. In October 2026 the digital minister said the ministry [plans to strengthen the agency's monitoring of critical infrastructure](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149856>).

**Published figures.** The director-general's figures [cover the first half of 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/cybersecurite-le-niger-sollicite-l-experience-du-burkina-faso-pour-son-soc-national>), more than a year ago. No source found gives the agency's figures for a later period.
