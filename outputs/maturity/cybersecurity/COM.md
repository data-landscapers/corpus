# Comoros

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Under Article 6 of Law 21-012/AU the Comoros national digital development agency (ANADEN), through its cybersecurity directorate, was designated the competent national cybersecurity authority, in charge of oversight of information-system and network security and trust services (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://ancs.gouv.km/about/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ancs.gouv.km/about/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ancs.gouv.km/about/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://ancs.gouv.km/about/>).
