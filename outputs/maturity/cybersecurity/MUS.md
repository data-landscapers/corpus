# Mauritius

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The ICT minister refers to the Computer Emergency Response Team of Mauritius (CERT-MU) as an existing body that is to sign a content takedown agreement with Meta](<https://lexpress.mu/s/avinash-ramtohul-il-faut-apporter-un-peu-de-discipline-dans-les-reseaux-sociaux-563444>). [L'Express reports the Cabinet noted promulgation of the CII Designation Regulations 2026, which empower the National Cybersecurity Committee to designate a system as critical information infrastructure under the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act](<https://lexpress.mu/node/558304>).

**Responding to incidents.** [CERT-MU published a security bulletin on 22 October 2026 rating the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and -109619) critical at 9.2, and the government took some state sites offline as a precaution](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563942>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563942>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563942>).
