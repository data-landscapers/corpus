# Mauritius

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [ITWeb Africa states the National Cybersecurity Committee is the main strategic and advisory body for protecting Mauritius against digital threats and managing national cyber resilience, with legal authority to enforce compliance alongside law enforcement](<https://itweb.africa/article/mauritius-sets-cyber-laws-framework/JN1gP7OAEZYqjL6m>). [The SADC statement of 13 September 2018 says Mauritius has an established and operational national CIRT (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://itweb.africa/article/mauritius-sets-cyber-laws-framework/JN1gP7OAEZYqjL6m>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://itweb.africa/article/mauritius-sets-cyber-laws-framework/JN1gP7OAEZYqjL6m>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://itweb.africa/article/mauritius-sets-cyber-laws-framework/JN1gP7OAEZYqjL6m>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
