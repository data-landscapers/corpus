# Mauritius

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [CERT-MU, under the ICT ministry, runs MAUCORS+, the national platform for reporting cyber incidents](<https://lexpress.mu/node/551421>). [The National Cybersecurity Committee is set up under section 3 of the Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/113_The-Cybersecurity-and-Cybercrime_Critical-Information-Infrastructure-Designation_Reg-2026.pdf>). [The National Audit Office found the committee not yet working at the end of 2025](<https://lexpress.mu/node/555707>).

**Responding to incidents.** [On 22 October 2026 CERT-MU rated the PHP2Shell flaws critical in a security bulletin](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563942>). [On the committee's advice the government took about 70% of its sites offline](<https://defimedia.info/risque-de-cyberattaque-10-15-des-services-gouvernementaux-toujours-hors-ligne>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Regulations gazetted in June 2026 let the National Cybersecurity Committee designate critical information infrastructure](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/113_The-Cybersecurity-and-Cybercrime_Critical-Information-Infrastructure-Designation_Reg-2026.pdf>). No source found says any system has yet been designated.

**Published figures.** [Figures from CERT-MU and ICT Authority data show reported security incidents rising from 5,221 in 2024 to 6,073 in 2025](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-and-meta-strengthen-cooperation-on-online-safety-and-digital-challenges/>). [CERT-MU's MAUCORS+ platform logged 47 phishing cases in 2025](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/faits-divers/escroqueries-et-fraudes-en-ligne-plus-de-900-affaires-traitees-en-3-ans-pour-130-arrestations/700378/>).
