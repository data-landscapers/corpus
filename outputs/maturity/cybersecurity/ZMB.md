# Zambia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cyber Security Act, 2025 (Act No. 3 of 2025) establishes the Zambia Cyber Security Agency in the Office of the President (s.3)](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>). The Agency constitutes the Zambia Cyber Incident Response Team (s.6) to handle incidents and issue alerts. The Act commenced on 12 May 2025.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).

The stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).
