# Zambia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cyber Security Act, 2025 (Act No. 3 of 2025) establishes the Zambia Cyber Security Agency in the Office of the President (s.3) and has it constitute the Zambia Cyber Incident Response Team (s.6) to handle incidents and issue alerts; the Act commenced on 12 May 2025](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/Act%20No.%203%20of%202025%2C%20The%20Cyber%20Security_0.pdf>).
