# Zambia

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The article describes the Zambia Cyber Security Agency (ZCSA) as operating and issuing a public advisory](<https://zedgossip.net/zcsa-warns-zambians-against-rising-disinformation-amid-election-tensions/>). [The Zambia Cyber Security Agency is stated to exist and issue public statements, here a statement in Lusaka on 22 August 2026](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/cyber-security-agency-warns-against-hate-speech-cyberbullying/>). [The SADC statement of 13 September 2018 says Zambia has an established and operational national CIRT (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://zedgossip.net/zcsa-warns-zambians-against-rising-disinformation-amid-election-tensions/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://zedgossip.net/zcsa-warns-zambians-against-rising-disinformation-amid-election-tensions/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://zedgossip.net/zcsa-warns-zambians-against-rising-disinformation-amid-election-tensions/>).
