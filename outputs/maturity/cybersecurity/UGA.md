# Uganda

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [A World Bank implementation mission visited Uganda's National CERT, among other sites, to assess progress under the UDAP-GovNet project](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7186/world-bank-mission-reviews-uganda-digital-acceleration-project-progress>). [The document defines CERT-UG as Uganda's national computer emergency response team, operated under NITA-U, providing incident coordination, vulnerability advisories and threat intelligence, and states it functions as the national CSIRT](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The policy, stated to be mandatory for operators of critical information infrastructure, requires MDAs, local governments and critical infrastructure operators to report information security breaches to the National CSIRT/CERT-UG; this is a national policy requirement, and the document lists no designated CII operators](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).
