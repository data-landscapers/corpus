# Uganda

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [CERT.UG, operated under NITA-U, is Uganda's national computer emergency response team](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>). [It provides incident coordination, vulnerability advisories and threat intelligence](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>). [The 2026 information security policy says the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2022-2026 is in place](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).

**Responding to incidents.** [On 24 July 2025 CERT.UG/CC issued a critical advisory on the SharePoint 'ToolShell' flaw chain](<https://www.cert.ug/spoofing-and-remote-code-execution-chain-vulnerability>). [In July 2024 it advised on the CrowdStrike outage and asked for incidents to be reported to it](<https://www.cert.ug/mass-it-outage-crowdstrike-update>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The 2026 information security policy is mandatory for operators of critical information infrastructure](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>). [It requires them, ministries and local governments to report security breaches to CERT.UG](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more. [The stage rests on the 2026 reporting duty](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-07/Updated-National%20Information%20Security%20Framework%20(NISF).pdf>).
