# Senegal

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Direction générale du chiffre et de la sécurité des systèmes d'information (DCSSI) is stated to co-organise the second cybersecurity providers forum and to set the course and norms for cybersecurity](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>). [The state digital operator's page says its cybersecurity actions follow the national cybersecurity strategy (SNC2022) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Direction générale du Chiffre et de la Sécurité des Systèmes d'Information (DCSSI)](<https://stcc-ssi.sn/protection-des-infrastructures-dinformation-critiques-une-nouvelle-etape-pour-la-souverainete-et-la-resilience-numeriques-du-senegal/>); [CSIRT ADIE/SN](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

***Not held***. No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. The bill creating a national cybersecurity authority and naming critical information infrastructure, adopted on 20 August 2026, is not stated promulgated.
