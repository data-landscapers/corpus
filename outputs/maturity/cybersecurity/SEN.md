# Senegal

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The state digital operator's page says its cybersecurity actions follow the national cybersecurity strategy (SNC2022) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>). [The Direction générale du chiffre et de la sécurité des systèmes d'information (DCSSI) is stated to co-organise the second cybersecurity providers forum and to set the course and norms for cybersecurity](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [CSIRT ADIE/SN](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

***Not held***. No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
