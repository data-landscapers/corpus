# Senegal

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Direction générale du chiffre et de la sécurité des systèmes d'information (DCSSI) is stated to co-organise the second cybersecurity providers forum and to set the course and norms for cybersecurity](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>). [The state digital operator's page says its cybersecurity actions follow the national cybersecurity strategy (SNC2022) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://atlanticactu.com/cyberattaques-recurrentes-le-senegal-se-dote-dune-autorite-nationale-de-cybersecurite/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The adopted law classes systems of national defence, public security, the courts, the prison administration and customs as critical information infrastructure by operation of law, and requires operators to report serious incidents to the Authority within 24 hours](<https://atlanticactu.com/cyberattaques-recurrentes-le-senegal-se-dote-dune-autorite-nationale-de-cybersecurite/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://atlanticactu.com/cyberattaques-recurrentes-le-senegal-se-dote-dune-autorite-nationale-de-cybersecurite/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Direction générale du Chiffre et de la Sécurité des Systèmes d'Information (DCSSI)](<https://stcc-ssi.sn/protection-des-infrastructures-dinformation-critiques-une-nouvelle-etape-pour-la-souverainete-et-la-resilience-numeriques-du-senegal/>); [CSIRT ADIE/SN](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

***Not held***. No source held says the national body published a count of incidents for a period ending since September 2024.
