# Senegal

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Law 2026-20 on digital security, published in the Journal officiel in November 2026, sets up a National Cybersecurity Authority](<https://www.osiris.sn/cybersecurite-publication-de-la-loi-no-2026-20-sur-la-securite-numerique.html>). [The DCSSI sets the course and norms for cybersecurity](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>), and [in October 2026 it co-organised the second cybersecurity providers forum](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>). [In 2022 the state digital operator said its cybersecurity work followed the national strategy, SNC2022](<https://www.adie.sn/expertise/s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. The nearest fact: [the 2026 law provides for security operations centres and closer monitoring of information systems](<https://www.osiris.sn/cybersecurite-publication-de-la-loi-no-2026-20-sur-la-securite-numerique.html>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Law 2026-20 provides for identifying critical infrastructure in energy, health, telecommunications, financial services and administration](<https://www.osiris.sn/cybersecurite-publication-de-la-loi-no-2026-20-sur-la-securite-numerique.html>). [Its operators must notify cyber incidents within 24 hours](<https://www.osiris.sn/cybersecurite-publication-de-la-loi-no-2026-20-sur-la-securite-numerique.html>), name information security officers and carry out security audits.

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years. The nearest fact: [the DCSSI sets the course and norms for cybersecurity](<https://aps.sn/cybersecurite-des-prestataires-locaux-appellent-a-plus-de-confiance/>).
