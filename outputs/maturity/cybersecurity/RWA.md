# Rwanda

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The National Cyber Security Authority is stated to have issued Application Software Security Directives on 16 April 2026, showing the body exists and operates](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>). [ITWeb Africa reports the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) as an operating national body that made statements on the CyberHub centre of excellence opened on 2025-10-02](<https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-unveils-cyber-security-centre-of-excellence/kLgB17ez2x4M59N4>). In October 2026 the Authority [signed partnerships with Airtel Rwanda and MTN Rwanda on cybersecurity capacity](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/rwanda-cyber-authority-boosts-security-with-airtel-mtn>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Cyber security Centre of Excellence (CyberHub)](<https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-unveils-cyber-security-centre-of-excellence/kLgB17ez2x4M59N4>).

***Not held***. No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023.
