# Rwanda

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The National Cyber Security Authority is stated to have issued Application Software Security Directives on 16 April 2026](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>). This shows the body exists and operates. [ITWeb Africa reports the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA) as an operating national body](<https://itweb.africa/article/rwanda-unveils-cyber-security-centre-of-excellence/kLgB17ez2x4M59N4>). It made statements on the CyberHub centre of excellence opened on 2025-10-02. In October 2026 the Authority [signed partnerships with Airtel Rwanda and MTN Rwanda on cybersecurity capacity](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/cybersecurity/rwanda-cyber-authority-boosts-security-with-airtel-mtn>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [The National Cyber Security Authority issued Application Software Security Directives on 16 April 2026](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body. [The National Cyber Security Authority issued Application Software Security Directives on 16 April 2026](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years. [The National Cyber Security Authority issued directives on 16 April 2026](<https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/ncsa-publishes-application-software-security-directives/>).
