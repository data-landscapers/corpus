# South Sudan

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2026, signed 18 February 2026, designates the authority as lead national responder to cyber threats](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>). It requires the authority to establish a National Computer Incident Response Team Coordination Centre (SS-CIRT/CC). [Diplo's 2026 profile says the National Communication Authority has created the South Sudan Computer Incident Response Team](<https://dig.watch/countries/south-sudan>). The team is the national focal point for cyber threats.

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023. No response to an attack is on record. [The Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act, signed on 18 February 2026, requires a national incident response coordination centre](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure. None says operators of critical systems must report incidents to the national body.
