# South Sudan

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2026, signed 18 February 2026, designates the authority as lead national responder to cyber threats and requires it to establish a National Computer Incident Response Team Coordination Centre (SS-CIRT/CC)](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>). [Diplo's 2026 profile says the National Communication Authority has created the South Sudan Computer Incident Response Team as the national focal point for cyber threats](<https://dig.watch/countries/south-sudan>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>).
