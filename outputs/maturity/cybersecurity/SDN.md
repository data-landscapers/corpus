# Sudan

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Sudan's Justice Ministry undersecretary told an Arab experts meeting in Tunis that a cybersecurity agency has been created](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/okyl-ozar-alaadl-ykod-ofd-alsodan-almshark-fy-alagtmaaa-altasaa-lfryk-alkhbraaa-alaarb-almaany-bmoagh-graym-tkny-almaalomat>). [The Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority is stated to implement the SUDAPASS digital identity project launched on 21 May 2026](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>). [The Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority officially launched the National Root Key for the Public Key Infrastructure at a ceremony](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/news>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/okyl-ozar-alaadl-ykod-ofd-alsodan-almshark-fy-alagtmaaa-altasaa-lfryk-alkhbraaa-alaarb-almaany-bmoagh-graym-tkny-almaalomat>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/okyl-ozar-alaadl-ykod-ofd-alsodan-almshark-fy-alagtmaaa-altasaa-lfryk-alkhbraaa-alaarb-almaany-bmoagh-graym-tkny-almaalomat>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/okyl-ozar-alaadl-ykod-ofd-alsodan-almshark-fy-alagtmaaa-altasaa-lfryk-alkhbraaa-alaarb-almaany-bmoagh-graym-tkny-almaalomat>).
