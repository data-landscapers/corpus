# Nigeria

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The op-ed states the Nigeria Computer Emergency Response Team (ngCERT) provides a national incident-response function and publishes security advisories](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/26/would-nigeria-know-when-an-ai-agent-breaks-into-our-system/>). [NgCERT, hosted by the Nigerian Communications Commission, is shown operating by issuing advisory ngCERT-2026-050005 on 2026-09-24](<https://csirt.ncc.gov.ng/index.php/resources/security-advisories/423-ngcert-security-advisory-on-whatsapp-vulnerabilities-leveraging-instagram-reels-to-execute-malicious-url>). [NgCERT is stated to be Nigeria's national CERT operating under the Nigerian Communications Commission, issuing advisories in September 2026](<https://csirt.ncc.gov.ng/index.php/resources/security-advisories/261-escalation-of-ransomware-attacks-in-nigeria>).

**Responding to incidents.** [NCC-CSIRT of the Nigerian Communications Commission issued a public security advisory on DDoS threats driven by botnet malware strains (Andromeda, BruteForceBot, Win.AsyncRAT, Gamut, StealRat) in the communications sector, rating damage and probability high](<https://csirt.ncc.gov.ng/index.php/resources/security-advisories/426-ddos-threats-driven-by-malware-strains-targeting-the-communications-sector>). [NgCERT, published on the NCC CSIRT site, issued advisory ngCERT-2026-050005 on 2026-09-24: a critical-rated advisory on WhatsApp vulnerabilities CVE-2026-23866 and CVE-2026-23863](<https://csirt.ncc.gov.ng/index.php/resources/security-advisories/423-ngcert-security-advisory-on-whatsapp-vulnerabilities-leveraging-instagram-reels-to-execute-malicious-url>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The NCC Executive Vice-Chairman and another speaker said at a 2026-08-11 workshop that telecommunications and fibre-optic infrastructure has been designated Critical National Information Infrastructure](<https://punchng.com/fibre-cuts-hit-5000-as-road-works-intensify/>). [The op-ed states the 2024 Designation and Protection of Critical National Information Infrastructure Order provides a policy foundation for critical information infrastructure](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/26/would-nigeria-know-when-an-ai-agent-breaks-into-our-system/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://punchng.com/fibre-cuts-hit-5000-as-road-works-intensify/>).
