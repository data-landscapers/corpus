# Cote d'Ivoire

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Tech Africa News reports ANSSI present on 15 September 2026 to validate electronic signature enrolments in Marcory](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/18/cote-divoire-expands-electronic-signature-paperless-administration/>). [ANSSI-CI is the national authority for security and defence of information systems, setting security standards and supporting critical infrastructure operators; the national cybersecurity strategy is cited as in force (no period given)](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-lanssi-veut-former-1200-experts-certifies-en-trois-ans/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-ne-pas-signaler-une-cyberattaque-expose-a-des-poursuites-penales-et-administratives/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [The source states that reporting cyber incidents to ANSSI is a legal obligation for companies, with administrative sanctions for non-declaration](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-ne-pas-signaler-une-cyberattaque-expose-a-des-poursuites-penales-et-administratives/>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-ne-pas-signaler-une-cyberattaque-expose-a-des-poursuites-penales-et-administratives/>).
