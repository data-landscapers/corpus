# Cote d'Ivoire

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Decree 2024-958 of 30 October 2024 created ANSSI, the national information systems security agency](<https://artci.ci/en/cybersecurity-handover-of-responsibilities-between-artci-and-anssi/>). [It took over ARTCI's cybersecurity missions on 30 April 2025](<https://artci.ci/en/cybersecurity-handover-of-responsibilities-between-artci-and-anssi/>). [ANSSI runs CI-CERT, the team that handles major incidents nationally or by sector](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/documents/90/LE_RAPPORT_2024_SUR_LETAT_DE_LA_CYBERCRIMINALITE_EN_C%C3%94TE_DIVOIRE.pdf>). [The 2021-2025 national strategy was adopted in December 2021, and its 2026-2030 successor is a draft](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-cybersecurite-lanssi-prepare-une-nouvelle-strategie-nationale-face-a-la-montee-des-menaces-numeriques/>).

**Responding to incidents.** [CI-CERT's register lists four public security alerts from 22 January to 15 February 2024](<https://www.cicert.ci/>). [They include an AnyDesk compromise and a phishing campaign](<https://www.cicert.ci/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Decree No. 2021-916 lists the critical sectors, sub-sectors and services whose operators are covered](<https://ncsi.ega.ee/country/ci_2022/>). [Companies must by law report cyber incidents to ANSSI, with administrative sanctions for failing to](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-ne-pas-signaler-une-cyberattaque-expose-a-des-poursuites-penales-et-administratives/>).

**Published figures.** [ANSSI states it recorded more than 12,100 cybercrime cases in 2024](<https://anssi.gouv.ci/panorama-des-menaces/>). [In October 2026 the digital minister cited ANSSI as the source of the 2025 complaint figures](<https://www.ivoirematin.com/fr/news/Economie/cybercriminalite-en-cote-divoire-plus-de-14-700-plaintes-et-23-milliards-fcfa-de-pertes-en-2025_n_126144.html>).
