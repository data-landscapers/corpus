# Burundi

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Burundi's Council of Ministers adopted a National Cybersecurity Strategy on 14 October 2026, per We are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-une-strategie-nationale-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-de-cybersecurite>). [The Prime Minister's office records the same adoption in its communique of the meeting](<https://primature.gov.bi/communique-de-presse-n-13-2026-de-la-reunion-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-14-octobre-2026-le-numerique-la-securite-alimentaire-lenergie-et-la-transformation-agricole-au-coeur-du-conse/>). [The digital master plan reported in March 2026 noted that Burundi had no computer incident response team](<https://communityvoice.bi/burundi-embarks-on-a-full-digitalization-journey/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-une-strategie-nationale-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-de-cybersecurite>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-une-strategie-nationale-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-de-cybersecurite>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-une-strategie-nationale-pour-renforcer-les-capacites-de-cybersecurite>).
