# DR Congo

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The minister said a national cybersecurity agency (Agence nationale de cybersécurité) has been created by presidential ordinance to strengthen the national response to digital threats](<https://depeche.cd/2026/09/29/rdc-augustin-kibassa-detaille-les-avancees-dun-an-dans-la-transformation-numerique/>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://fr.scribd.com/document/1057825809/J-O-n-special-du-16-juin-2026-ANCY>).

**Critical infrastructure.** [Article 4 of the ordonnance requires public institutions and operators of vital importance to inform ANCY of all attacks and intrusions; Article 3 also tasks ANCY with identifying such operators with ministries and sector regulators](<https://fr.scribd.com/document/1057825809/J-O-n-special-du-16-juin-2026-ANCY>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://fr.scribd.com/document/1057825809/J-O-n-special-du-16-juin-2026-ANCY>).
