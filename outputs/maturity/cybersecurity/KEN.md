# Kenya

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The guide states the National KE-CIRT/CC, housed within the Communications Authority, functions as Kenya's operational national point of contact for cybersecurity incidents; published 2026-09-03, no earlier date given](<https://nc4.go.ke/regulations/rapid-reference-guide/>). [The Standard reports the Cabinet Secretary told the Senate that the National KE-CIRT/CC operates around the clock to detect, prevent and respond to cyber threats](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/national/article/2001559644/govt-steps-up-safety-against-cybersecurity-and-data-privacy>).

**Responding to incidents.** [The National Kenya Computer Incident Response Team Coordination Centre, with State House ICT, restricted access to the defaced president.go.ke site to contain the attack detected 18 July 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-restores-presidential-website-after-cyber-attack/Gb3Bw7WadxLq2k6V>). [The National KE-CIRT/CC states it issued 20,748,489 cyber threat advisories in April-June 2026](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-07/Cyber%20Security%20Report%20Q4%202025-2026_1.pdf>). [KBC, citing the Communications Authority, reports the National KE-CIRT/CC issued advisories in January to March 2026](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/dr-martin-koyabe-appointed-to-lead-national-cybersecurity-agency/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/web-attacks-up-44pc-as-hackers-target-state-isp-systems-5581506>).

**Published figures.** [The Communications Authority of Kenya reported 2.36 billion cyber threat events for April to June 2026 (system attacks 2.25 billion, malware 59 million, brute-force 24.2 million) and 10.6 million advisories issued, a 1.9% rise on the prior quarter](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/web-attacks-up-44pc-as-hackers-target-state-isp-systems-5581506>).
