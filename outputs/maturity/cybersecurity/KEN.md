# Kenya

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The national KE-CIRT/CC is Kenya's point of contact for cyber incidents](<https://nc4.go.ke/regulations/rapid-reference-guide/>), says a guide of September 2026. It sits in the Communications Authority. [The Cabinet Secretary told the Senate it works around the clock against threats](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/national/article/2001559644/govt-steps-up-safety-against-cybersecurity-and-data-privacy>).

**Responding to incidents.** [The National Kenya Computer Incident Response Team Coordination Centre, with State House ICT, restricted access to the defaced president.go.ke site to contain the attack detected 18 July 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-restores-presidential-website-after-cyber-attack/Gb3Bw7WadxLq2k6V>). [The National KE-CIRT/CC states it issued 20,748,489 cyber threat advisories in April-June 2026](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-07/Cyber%20Security%20Report%20Q4%202025-2026_1.pdf>). [KBC, citing the Communications Authority, reports the National KE-CIRT/CC issued advisories in January to March 2026](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/dr-martin-koyabe-appointed-to-lead-national-cybersecurity-agency/>).

**Critical infrastructure.** Kenya [promulgated regulations on critical information infrastructure in early 2024](<https://www.ca.go.ke/ca-lay-2500km-fibre-accross-19-counties>), says the Communications Authority's director general. They cover e-government, telecommunications, banking, transport, energy and health.

**Published figures.** [In April to June 2026 the Communications Authority counted 2.36 billion threat events and 10.6 million advisories issued](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/web-attacks-up-44pc-as-hackers-target-state-isp-systems-5581506>). [KE-CIRT/CC's report for the quarter adds 358 digital investigations](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-07/Cyber%20Security%20Report%20Q4%202025-2026_1.pdf>).
