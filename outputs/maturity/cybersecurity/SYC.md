# Seychelles

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [Seychelles Nation reports that the National Computer Emergency Response Team of Seychelles (CERT-SC) designed and delivered the third National Cyberdrill hosted by the Department of ICT, so the national team exists and operates](<https://nation.sc/articles/32649/cert-sc-takes-the-lead-in-third-seychelles-national-cyberdrill>). [The Seychelles Department of ICT launched the newly established National Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-SC) as the country's frontline for monitoring cyber threats and coordinating national responses](<https://nation.sc/articles/26959/seychelles-activates-cert-sc-to-lead-coordinated-cyber-threat-response>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source found says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nation.sc/articles/32649/cert-sc-takes-the-lead-in-third-seychelles-national-cyberdrill>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source found says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nation.sc/articles/32649/cert-sc-takes-the-lead-in-third-seychelles-national-cyberdrill>).

**Published figures.** No source found says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<https://nation.sc/articles/32649/cert-sc-takes-the-lead-in-third-seychelles-national-cyberdrill>).
