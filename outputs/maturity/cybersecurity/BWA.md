# Botswana

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The BOCRA newsletter states the first National Cyber Drill in late February 2026 was led by the Botswana Computer Security Incident Response Team (BWCSIRT), with FIRST facilitating](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). [The minister said the Act formally strengthened the mandate of the National Computer Security Incident Response team, stated as existing at the 25 February 2026 cyber drill](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90260>).

**Responding to incidents.** No source held says the national body handled an incident or issued a public alert since October 2023, so no response to an attack is on record; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>).

**Critical infrastructure.** No source held says the country designated its critical information infrastructure or required the operators of its critical systems to report incidents to the national body; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>).

**Published figures.** No source held says the national body published a count of the incidents it handled for a quarter or more ending in the last two years; the stage rests on [the source that sets it](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
