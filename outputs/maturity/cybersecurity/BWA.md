# Botswana

## National cyber response

**A strategy or a national body.** [The BOCRA newsletter states the first National Cyber Drill was held in late February 2026](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). The Botswana Computer Security Incident Response Team (BWCSIRT) led it, with FIRST facilitating. [The minister said the Act formally strengthened the mandate of the National Computer Security Incident Response team](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90260>). The team was stated as existing at the 25 February 2026 cyber drill. [BOCRA's 2025 annual report says the national CSIRT sits within BOCRA](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA2025_ANNUAL_REPORT_%28WEB%29_compressed.pdf>). It was set up in 2020 and is funded from the Authority's own resources.

**Responding to incidents.** [On 23 October 2026 the national CSIRT, through BOCRA, issued an urgent public advisory on the PHP2Shell flaws](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/node/62863>). It said exploitation attempts had been seen. It asked organisations to patch and to report compromise to it.
