# Djibouti

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In 2023, [83.6 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's SDG 4.a.1 series. The series gives no figure for the years just before. So no source found says whether it is rising or falling. At 50 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts. The figure counts schools with a connection used for teaching, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** [In 2023, 83.6 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No source found since October 2023 says digital literacy or computing is a compulsory subject in the national secondary curriculum.
