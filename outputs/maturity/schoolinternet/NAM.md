# Namibia

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In 2024, [81.2 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's SDG 4.a.1 series. The same series [gives 79 in 2021, 81.8 in 2022 and 80.3 in 2023](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the figure rose by 2.27 points. At 50 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts schools with a connection used for teaching, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** [The NIED Information and Communication Syllabus (Grade 8 & 9) states the subject is "compulsory, non-promotional (but assessable)" for Grades 4-12, covering digital, media and information literacy, implemented in Grade 8 in 2025 and Grade 9 in 2026](<https://www.nied.edu.na/assets/documents/02Syllabuses/04JuniorSecondary/InformationandCommunication/JSInformationandCommunicationSyllabusDecember2025.pdf>).
