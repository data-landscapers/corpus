# Cameroon

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In 2023, [24.1 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's SDG 4.a.1 series. The same series [gives 22.6 in 2022](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2022 the figure rose by 1.53 points. It is 8.91 points short of 33, the next line. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts. The figure counts schools with a connection used for teaching, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** [In 2023, 24.1 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), fewer than half. No source on digital skills in the curriculum has been found.
