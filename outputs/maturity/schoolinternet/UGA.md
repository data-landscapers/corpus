# Uganda

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In February 2026, [63 per cent of the 3,257 secondary schools assessed had internet access, and 12 per cent had it school-wide](<https://www.unicef.org/uganda/media/21466/file/From%20Unplugged%20to%20ICT-Ready%20-%20A%20Digital%20Readiness%20Assessment%20of%20Secondary%20Schools%20in%20Uganda%20-%20print%20file.pdf.pdf>), by UNICEF's digital readiness assessment with the Ministry of Education and Sports, which covered 78 per cent of the schools registered with the secondary heads' association. No earlier figure has been found, so no source found says whether it is rising or falling. At 50 or more, it is past the study's highest line for the figure. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts schools with a connection, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** No source found since October 2023 says digital literacy or computing is a compulsory subject in the national secondary curriculum.
