# Comoros

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In 2024, [24.5 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's SDG 4.a.1 series. The same series [gives 24.8 in 2021](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). Since 2021 the figure fell by 0.32 points. It is 8.52 points short of 33, the next line. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts schools with a connection used for teaching, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** The curriculum is asked about only once half of secondary schools have internet for teaching, so with [the figure below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>) none was looked for in this first pass.
