# Cote d'Ivoire

## Internet in secondary schools

**Secondary schools with internet for teaching.** In 2024, [35.2 per cent of secondary schools had internet for teaching](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>), by UNESCO's SDG 4.a.1 series. The series gives no figure for the years just before. So no source found says whether it is rising or falling. It is 14.8 points short of 50, the next line. The study's lines are 17, 33 and 50 per cent of secondary schools, the frame's own cuts. The figure counts schools with a connection used for teaching, not how well it serves them or what is taught.

**Digital skills in the curriculum.** The curriculum is asked about only once half of secondary schools have internet for teaching. [The figure is below that line](<https://databrowser.uis.unesco.org/>). No source on digital skills in the curriculum has been found.
