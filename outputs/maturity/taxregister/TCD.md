# Chad

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [PwC states that taxpayers can apply on the E.Tax platform, online or at an office, for a certificate of tax identification number (NIF), among developments of 2023](<https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/chad/corporate/significant-developments>). [A finance ministry circular of 10 January 2024 requires each bidder's NIF to be active in E-Tax before a public contract is awarded](<https://fen.finances.gouv.td/assets/landing/documents/Circulaire002_2024.pdf>), and [the customs directorate says the NIF can be checked online](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>). [In January 2025 FurtherAfrica described E-Tax registration as a pilot open to businesses](<https://furtherafrica.com/2025/01/14/rwanda-leads-africas-digital-public-finance-transformation/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [Chad's customs directorate says every importer, exporter and customs agent needs a valid NIF, required on every declaration, and the SYDONIA World customs system blocks a declaration made without one](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>). The customs e-payment it describes is integrated with the tax directorate's E-Tax platform.

**Tied to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is the national ID number or is checked against the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the NIF required on customs declarations](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Nothing held says the taxpayer number is the national ID number or is checked against it.
