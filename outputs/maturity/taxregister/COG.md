# Congo

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The NIU project coordinator said in October 2025 that the NIU file is the tax directorate's taxpayer file](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>). [Its database holds over 28,000 legal persons as well as individuals](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>). [The new NIU, with fingerprints, iris and a QR code, went into production in August 2020](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/nouveau-niu_100820>). [It is a compulsory taxpayer number issued by the tax directorate](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/nouveau-niu_100820>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [Commercial firms request the NIU through the business creation agency ACPCE](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>), the coordinator said in October 2025. [An order of 12 October 2022 makes it compulsory for customs duty declarations and payments](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>). [Enrolment equipment was placed at the ACPCE counter in 2020](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/nouveau-niu_100820>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest fact found is [the NIU database of October 2025](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/en/node/4803>), which covers individuals as well as firms.
