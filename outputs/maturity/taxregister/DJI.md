# Djibouti

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found since October 2023 says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the tax directorate (DGI) assigned the taxpayer number (NIF)](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [In January 2026 Djibouti's business-creation guide said the Guichet Unique passes the registration file to the DGI automatically](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). [The DGI then assigns the NIF as a step of business creation](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the NIF is assigned from the Guichet Unique's registration file](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>).

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says whether taxpayers are registered on computer.
