# Djibouti

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The guide says the Guichet Unique automatically passes the registration file to the tax directorate (DGI), which assigns the taxpayer number (NIF) as a step of business creation](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The guide says the Guichet Unique automatically passes the registration file to the tax directorate (DGI), which assigns the taxpayer number (NIF) as a step of business creation](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>).
