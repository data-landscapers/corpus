# Seychelles

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In 2025 the Seychelles e-Service Gateway offered an online TIN registration form for companies](<https://eservice.egov.sc/TinRegistration/pages/RegistrationForCompany.aspx>). [The form covers presumptive tax, VAT, employer, business tax and excise registration](<https://eservice.egov.sc/TinRegistration/pages/RegistrationForCompany.aspx>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the TIN form registers a company for VAT, employer and excise taxes](<https://eservice.egov.sc/TinRegistration/pages/RegistrationForCompany.aspx>).

**Tied to ID.** [The 2025 online company TIN form requires the contact person's 11-digit National Identity Number](<https://eservice.egov.sc/TinRegistration/pages/RegistrationForCompany.aspx>). [The form shows 'NIN not valid' when the number fails validation](<https://eservice.egov.sc/TinRegistration/pages/RegistrationForCompany.aspx>).
