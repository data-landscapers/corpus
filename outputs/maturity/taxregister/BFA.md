# Burkina Faso

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The tax directorate's FAQ of October 2023 says taxpayers are registered in the register of taxpayers or the software for the single financial identifier (IFU)](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>). The same FAQ says salaried users can file declarations on the eSintax platform. [The Direction Générale des Impôts published the FAQ on 17 October 2023](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The October 2023 FAQ says customs clearance for foreign trade needs an IFU](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>). It calls the IFU the single number for users of the financial administration.

**Tied to ID.** No source found says the IFU is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact found is that [the IFU is the single number for users of the financial administration](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>), as the 2023 FAQ states.

***Also on file***. [eSintax](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 ties the taxpayer number to the national ID. None restates the 2023 FAQ.
