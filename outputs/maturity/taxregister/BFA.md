# Burkina Faso

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Direction Générale des Impôts FAQ says taxpayers are registered in the registre des contribuables or the software for Identifiants financiers uniques (IFU), and that salaried users can use the eSintax platform to file declarations](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The Direction Générale des Impôts FAQ says customs clearance procedures linked to foreign trade cannot be carried out without an IFU, the single number issued to users of the financial administration](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dgi.bf/faq-immatriculation-des-contribuables/>).
