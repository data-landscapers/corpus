# Cape Verde

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The GOV.CV portal says a person can request a tax identification number (NIF) online](<https://www.gov.cv/nif-pessoa-singular>). The request goes through the portal or its app. The page, dated 2026, says the first number is free. It is issued within two working days. [The Empresa no Dia platform (PEO) includes the NIF in its company-creation package](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). It says the platform works with the tax administration.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The PEO page says a company obtains its NIF in the same package as its commercial registration and social security enrolment](<https://peo.gov.cv/sobre-nos>). The page is dated 2026.

**Tied to ID.** No source found says the taxpayer number is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact found is that [individuals request the NIF online on GOV.CV](<https://www.gov.cv/nif-pessoa-singular>).

***Also on file***. [GOV.CV](<https://www.gov.cv/nif-pessoa-singular>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID.
