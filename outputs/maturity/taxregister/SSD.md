# South Sudan

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The eServices portal says all taxpayers, citizens, foreigners, businesses and NGOs can now apply online for a Taxpayer Identification Number on eTax from the South Sudan Revenue Authority](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>).
