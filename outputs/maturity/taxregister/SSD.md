# South Sudan

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The government eServices portal says all taxpayers can apply online for a Taxpayer Identification Number on eTax](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>). Citizens, foreigners, businesses and NGOs are all named. [The portal still offered the service in November 2026](<https://accounts.eservices.gov.ss/mdas>), through the revenue authority's domestic division. [On 12 June 2026 the authority told taxpayers to update their details in its electronic TIN system](<https://radioyei.org/news/business/economy/revenue-authority-hunts-unregistered-businesses/>). It records arrears in its Domestic Tax Integrated System.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact is [the online TIN application on eTax](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.eservices.gov.ss/>).
