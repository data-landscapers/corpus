# Cameroon

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Finance Ministry communique of 10 April 2026, as reported by LeFisk, says the DGI's ATOM platform has been in service since 1 July 2026 and is mandatory for all new registrations, issuing the NIU taxpayer number online](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [The Directorate General of Taxation says tax registration is done exclusively online by the taxpayer, and the computer system allots a unique taxpayer number (NIU) automatically](<https://www.impots.cm/en/node/148>). [LeFisk reports that ATOM is progressively replacing the Harmony platform, with taxpayers already registered on Harmony required to re-register on ATOM between 1 July and 31 December 2026, after which only ATOM registrations are valid](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>).
