# Cameroon

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Finance Ministry communique of 10 April 2026, as reported by LeFisk, says the DGI's ATOM platform has been in service since 1 July 2026](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [It is mandatory for all new registrations and issues the NIU taxpayer number online](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [The Directorate General of Taxation says tax registration is done exclusively online by the taxpayer](<https://www.impots.cm/en/node/148>). [The computer system allots a unique taxpayer number (NIU) automatically](<https://www.impots.cm/en/node/148>). [LeFisk reports that ATOM is progressively replacing the Harmony platform](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [Taxpayers already registered on Harmony must re-register on ATOM between 1 July and 31 December 2026](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [After that only ATOM registrations are valid](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>). [In April 2026 the finance minister approved a EUR 6m contract for SIGIT](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/1811-17220-cameroon-two-tenders-canceled-under-15-million-german-funded-tax-digitalization-program>), a new tax system that will handle taxpayer registration over 24 months.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest fact found: [ATOM is mandatory for all new registrations](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest fact found: [ATOM issues the NIU taxpayer number online](<https://lefisk.cm/blog/atom-dgi-cameroun-immatriculation-fiscale-guide-complet>).
