# Burundi

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The revenue office OBR said that from 4 August 2026 all new taxpayer registrations are made online on its e-KORI platform](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/06/e-kori-toutes-les-nouvelles-demandes-dimmatriculation-desormais-en-ligne-a-partir-du-4-aout/>). The certificate of registration is sent by email. [The OBR confirms e-KORI entered service that day](<https://obr.bi/index.php/e-kori>). [In July 2026 it told owners to declare land tax online on its UMUTANGAKORI II platform](<https://www.obr.bi/index.php/communiques/2612-communique-declaration-et-paiement-en-ligne-de-l-impot-foncier>). Taxpayers not yet registered ask for their tax number through e-KORI.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact is [that e-KORI has taken all new registrations since August 2026](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/06/e-kori-toutes-les-nouvelles-demandes-dimmatriculation-desormais-en-ligne-a-partir-du-4-aout/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact is [the August 2026 move of registration to e-KORI](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/06/e-kori-toutes-les-nouvelles-demandes-dimmatriculation-desormais-en-ligne-a-partir-du-4-aout/>).
