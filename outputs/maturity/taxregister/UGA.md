# Uganda

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [Akech said existing taxpayers can update their details through an account on the URA online portal or at URA service points, and new taxpayers are registered by URA](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [URA's Ritah Elizabeth Akech said the move to the NIN as the individual tax identifier is being implemented in phases, starting with mapping existing NINs to TINs](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [Mirembe said the government will phase out the TIN for individuals and replace it with the NIN](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/transitioning-from-tin-to-nin-will-create-job-NV_240692_092026>). [Mirembe said the transition from TINs to NINs is under way to create a single identifier and improve the accuracy of taxpayer records](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/369514/ura-explains-use-of-nins-to-replace-tins>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [Akech said non-individual taxpayers will be issued an Entity Registration Number through the Uganda Registration Services Bureau as their tax identifier](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [Mirembe said the URSB registration number will become the tax identifier for companies](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/transitioning-from-tin-to-nin-will-create-job-NV_240692_092026>).

**Tied to ID.** [Akech said taxpayers give fingerprint consent at a URA service point so URA devices can pull basic biodata from NIRA servers and verify identity against NIRA's records](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [Cabinet approved use of the NIN as a tax identifier on 31 August 2026, and Musasizi said the NIN and TIN must be interoperable](<https://mulengeranews.com/minister-musasizi-explains-why-nin-is-to-be-used-as-tin-as-icpau-commits-to-enforce-ai-adoption-in-accountancy-sector/>).
