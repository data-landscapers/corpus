# Uganda

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** The Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) keeps a computerised register of taxpayers. [It held about 5.7 million records at 30 June 2026, the finance ministry says](<https://softpower.ug/nin-tin-transition-will-not-automatically-create-tax-liability-lugoloobi/>). [Taxpayers log in to their account on the URA portal with their tax number](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>). [New taxpayers are registered by URA, at its service points or online](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** Firms are to use their business registration number for tax. [URA said in September 2026 that companies will get an Entity Registration Number from the business registry as their tax identifier](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [Another URA official said the same of the registry's number](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/transitioning-from-tin-to-nin-will-create-job-NV_240692_092026>). Both describe a change still to come.

**Tied to ID.** The national ID number (NIN) is becoming the tax number for individuals. [Cabinet approved the change on 31 August 2026](<https://mulengeranews.com/minister-musasizi-explains-why-nin-is-to-be-used-as-tin-as-icpau-commits-to-enforce-ai-adoption-in-accountancy-sector/>). [URA says the portal now asks taxpayers to add their NIN while records are mapped](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-37/>). [By 30 September 2026, 2.13 million of 5.41 million individual records were linked to a NIN](<https://ugbusiness.com/2026/10/economy/more-than-3m-tax-records-await-nin-match-before-login-switch>). [URA offices check fingerprints against the identity register](<https://nilepost.co.ug/business/374460/linking-nins-to-tax-records-will-tackle-identity-theft-says-ura>). [Scanners went to Kampala and Wakiso first, with other offices from 26 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/uganda-moves-to-make-national-digital-id-number-its-tax-identifier>).
