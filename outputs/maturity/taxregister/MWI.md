# Malawi

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Tax Administration (Electronic System) Rules, 2025, made 4 August 2025 by the Commissioner General, require taxpayers to be registered electronically and the Commissioner General to keep an electronic register of taxpayers](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>). [The revenue authority's Msonkho Online system lets taxpayers register, file and pay online, per Nyasa Times reporting on the revenue authority's September 2019 MoU (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/mra-immigration-join-forces-to-enhance-tax-laws-permits-compliance/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** [The revenue authority's director general says Malawian taxpayers will require a national ID to register and be issued a TPIN (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/mra-immigration-join-forces-to-enhance-tax-laws-permits-compliance/>).
