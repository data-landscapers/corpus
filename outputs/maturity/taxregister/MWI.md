# Malawi

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [On 4 August 2025 the Commissioner General made the Tax Administration (Electronic System) Rules, 2025](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>). [They require taxpayers to be registered electronically](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>). [They also require the Commissioner General to keep an electronic register of taxpayers](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>). [In September 2019 Nyasa Times reported that the revenue authority's Msonkho Online system lets taxpayers register, file and pay online](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/mra-immigration-join-forces-to-enhance-tax-laws-permits-compliance/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the 2025 rules require an electronic register of taxpayers](<https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/0deaa96755badce1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** [In September 2019 the revenue authority's director general spoke on taxpayer registration](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/mra-immigration-join-forces-to-enhance-tax-laws-permits-compliance/>). [He said Malawians would need a national ID to register and get a taxpayer identification number (TPIN)](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/mra-immigration-join-forces-to-enhance-tax-laws-permits-compliance/>).
