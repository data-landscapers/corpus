# South Africa

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In October 2026 SARS said its Client Information System was implemented in 2014](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>). [Taxpayers register for and maintain tax and customs registrations in one consolidated profile](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>). [Additional registrations are made on eFiling or at a branch](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [tax and customs registrations sit in one SARS profile](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [taxpayers can register at a SARS branch](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>).
