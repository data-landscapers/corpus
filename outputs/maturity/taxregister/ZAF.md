# South Africa

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [SARS says its Client Information System, implemented in 2014, lets taxpayers register for and maintain tax and customs registrations in one consolidated profile, with additional registrations done on eFiling or at a branch](<https://www.sars.gov.za/client-segments/client-information-system/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
