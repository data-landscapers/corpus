# Senegal

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The tax directorate, DGID, lets taxpayers register online through its SENTAX portal](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>). Its services validate each request. [A finance ministry order lets foreign suppliers register online from 1 April 2024](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/2024/07/Order-GTC-355-Digital-VAT-DEFINITIVE-VERSION.pdf>). They receive a NINEA tax identification number and a login to Etax, the directorate's online service at eservices.dgid.sn. [LoGRI says the directorate manages taxpayer data for property tax on its SIGUIL system](<https://logri.org/2025/01/optimizing-property-tax-administration-the-importance-of-an-effective-information-sharing-system/>), with a successor, SIGUIL 2, used in property censuses.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says customs use the taxpayer number. None says it is issued when a business registers. The nearest held fact: [foreign suppliers get the NINEA, the tax number, on registering online](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/2024/07/Order-GTC-355-Digital-VAT-DEFINITIVE-VERSION.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [SENTAX registers taxpayers online, and the directorate's services validate each request](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>).

***Also on file***. [Répertoire national des Entreprises et Associations (RNEA)](<https://www.ansd.sn/Indicateur/immatriculation-au-ninea-chiffres-cles-tendances-et-evolutions>); [SENTAX](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>); [NINEA](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/fiches/ninea.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says customs or business registration use the taxpayer number. None ties it to the national ID.
