# Eswatini

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The Eswatini Trade Portal procedure page says companies dealing in imports and exports must register in ASYCUDA, where the TIN registration must be uploaded](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>).
