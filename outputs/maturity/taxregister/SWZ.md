# Eswatini

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found since October 2023 says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 importing and exporting companies had to hold a TIN registration](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [In 2024 the Eswatini Trade Portal said companies dealing in imports and exports must register in ASYCUDA](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>). [They must upload their TIN registration there](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [companies upload the TIN registration to the customs system](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>).

***Also on file***. [ASYCUDA](<https://eswatinitradeportal.com/index.php?id=25&r=searchProcedure%2Fview1>).

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says whether taxpayers are registered on computer.
