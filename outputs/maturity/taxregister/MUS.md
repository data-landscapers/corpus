# Mauritius

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority (MRA) says it issues a Tax Account Number (TAN) at registration in its databases](<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/mauritius-tin.pdf>). The number goes to individuals with a tax obligation and to entities. The authority said so in its 2026 statement to the OECD.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The MRA says it allocates a TAN to entities and business individuals as soon as they receive their Business Registration Number](<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/mauritius-tin.pdf>). The same 2026 statement gives this. A firm therefore holds its TAN from the time it is registered as a business. [The statement covers individuals with a tax obligation and entities alike](<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/mauritius-tin.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found says the TAN is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact found is that [the MRA issues the TAN to individuals with a tax obligation](<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/mauritius-tin.pdf>).
