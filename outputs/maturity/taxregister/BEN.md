# Benin

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The DGI guide says a person can register for tax online on the e-services portal and the IFU number is generated instantly](<https://e-services.impots.bj/_espaces/FACILE/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [The government guide says individuals apply for the 13-digit IFU taxpayer number exclusively on the online platform ifu.impots.bj, with a certificate downloadable on approval (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/doc/158/download>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The guide says sole traders and companies being created are registered for the IFU at the business formalities one-stop shop (GUFE) of APIEx (2020, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/doc/158/download>).

**Tied to ID.** [ANIP says the IFU is issued from the same national civil-status database as the national identity card](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [The DGI guide says the applicant enters their NPI and birth date and personal data is retrieved automatically from ANIP before the IFU is issued](<https://e-services.impots.bj/_espaces/FACILE/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).
