# Benin

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** Taxpayers register online and receive their tax number, the IFU, from a computerised register. [The tax directorate's 2025 guide says the IFU is generated instantly on its e-services portal](<https://e-services.impots.bj/_espaces/FACILE/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [La Marina reported in November 2026 that the online platform has issued an IFU within 24 hours since December 2021](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>). [A 2020 government guide already made the online platform the only way to apply](<https://www.gouv.bj/doc/158/download>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The 2020 government guide says new sole traders and companies get their IFU at the business one-stop shop of APIEx](<https://www.gouv.bj/doc/158/download>). Nothing more recent says the same.

**Tied to ID.** The IFU is issued from the personal number held by the identity agency, ANIP. [The 2025 guide says the applicant enters that number and their data is drawn from ANIP's records](<https://e-services.impots.bj/_espaces/FACILE/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [ANIP said in September 2025 that the IFU comes from the same civil-status database as the identity card](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). Older tax numbers are still being matched. [On 25 October 2023, 439,333 of 1,077,679 IFU were attached to a personal number, La Marina reported](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>).
