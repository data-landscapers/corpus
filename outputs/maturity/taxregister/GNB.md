# Guinea-Bissau

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [DGCI Instruction 25/2025 states that production, validation and issue of the NIF may be done only through the module in the DGCI's Kontaktu system, and bans issue through SIGEF](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>). [The Kontaktu portal reproduces DGCI Instruction 25/2025, which confines production, validation and issue of the NIF to the Kontaktu module and bans SIGEF for it](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>). [The CFE page states that after commercial registration on the CFE platform the DGCI agent validates the data and the NIF is generated electronically and assigned to the company](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The CFE page states that the NIF is issued as part of company registration at the CFE, the data being sent automatically to the DGCI, so applicants need not go to the DGCI](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>).

***Also on file***. [SIGEF](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>); [CFE platform](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
