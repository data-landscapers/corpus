# Guinea-Bissau

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [DGCI Instruction 25/2025, of 3 December 2025, says the NIF may be issued only through a module of the tax directorate's Kontaktu system](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>). It bans issue through SIGEF. [The Kontaktu portal reproduces the instruction](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>). [The company formalities centre (CFE) says a DGCI agent validates the data after commercial registration](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>). The NIF is then generated electronically.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The CFE page, dated 2026, says the NIF is issued as part of company registration](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>). The data go to the DGCI automatically, so applicants need not go there.

**Tied to ID.** No source found says the NIF is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact found is that [the NIF is issued only through Kontaktu](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>).

***Also on file***. [SIGEF](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/instructions>); [CFE platform](<https://cfe.gw/numero-de-identificacao-fiscal/>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID.
