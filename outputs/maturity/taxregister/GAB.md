# Gabon

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Direction Générale des Impôts' e-tax guide says taxpayers can start registration online through the 'Je m'immatricule' button, with a taxpayer number (NIF) held by enrolled taxpayers and DGI agents completing checks at a tax office](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).
