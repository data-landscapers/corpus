# Gabon

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In October 2025 the Direction Générale des Impôts' e-tax guide said taxpayers can start registration online](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [They use the 'Je m'immatricule' button](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [Enrolled taxpayers hold a taxpayer number (NIF)](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>). [DGI agents complete the checks at a tax office](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [enrolled taxpayers hold a NIF](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [DGI agents check registrations at a tax office](<https://e-tax.dgi.ga/_espaces/ECONTRIBUABLEDGIGA/domain/engine/application/guides/guide-utilisateur-adhesion-et-connexion.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Déclaration unique des salaires (DUS)](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
