# Mauritania

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [Since 4 April 2025 a tax registration can be obtained through the APIM 'Mes services' portal](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [Since April 2025 the APIM portal has given three registrations in one procedure at company creation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>). [They are the commerce register, tax registration and social security affiliation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [tax registration is obtained through the APIM portal](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>).

***Also on file***. [Registre biométrique national (ANRPTS)](<https://procedures.gov.mr/fr/procedure/1105>); [Jibaya](<https://numericite.eu/numericite-x-mauritanie-episode-1-immersion-en-pays-maure/>).
