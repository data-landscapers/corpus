# Mauritania

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [Since 4 April 2025 a tax registration can be obtained through the APIM 'Mes services' portal as part of company creation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The portal gives commerce register, tax registration and social security affiliation in one procedure at company creation](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/268316>).
