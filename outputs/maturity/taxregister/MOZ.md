# Mozambique

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In September 2026 CEDSIF, the state's finance systems centre, listed e-Tributação as the tax system for domestic taxes](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/?page_id=81>). [It includes a module for registering the taxpayer number, the NUIT](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/?page_id=81>), with modules for state revenue and collection. [In July 2026 the government was advancing a new integrated tax management system](<https://aimnews.org/2026/07/22/governo-avanca-com-sistema-integrado-para-modernizar-gestao-tributaria/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The tax authority's FAQ says the NUIT must be used for all taxes, including customs duties](<http://www.at.gov.mz/por/Perguntas-Frequentes2/NUIT>). It is required for imports and exports and for licensing an economic activity. [Its English FAQ says the same of imports and business licensing](<http://www.at.gov.mz/eng/Internacional-Trade/FAQ-s/NUIT>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the NUIT is required for all taxes and for customs](<http://www.at.gov.mz/por/Perguntas-Frequentes2/NUIT>).
