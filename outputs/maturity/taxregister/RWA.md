# Rwanda

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority tells property owners to log in to its website with their TIN and password](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/home?tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2415&cHash=ed34adc0d34fabbccdf4972aa88585f1>) to declare property tax, in a 2024 notice. The declaration is made online against the taxpayer's number. [The authority says it issues TINs at all its offices](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>), to individuals, NGOs, joint ventures, estates and public bodies. Its registration page is dated only to 2026.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The same page says firms registering through the RDB portal get a single TIN](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>), since the RDB portal is linked to the authority's systems. The page gives no date beyond the year 2026.

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact is that [the authority issues TINs to individuals at its offices](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).
