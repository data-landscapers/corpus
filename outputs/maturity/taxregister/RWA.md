# Rwanda

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) tells property owners to log in to its website with their TIN and password](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/home?tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2415&cHash=ed34adc0d34fabbccdf4972aa88585f1>). They declare property tax that way, under a 2024 notice. [Its 2026 registration page says the RRA issues TINs at all its offices](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>). They go to individuals, NGOs, joint ventures, estates and public bodies.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The same page says firms registering through the Rwanda Development Board (RDB) portal get a single TIN](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>). That portal is linked to the RRA's systems. [The page names the RDB portal as the route by which a business registers for tax](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found says the TIN is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact found is that [the RRA issues TINs to individuals at its offices](<https://www.rra.gov.rw/en/domestic-tax-services/registration-de-registration/default-title>).
