# Ethiopia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found describes the Ministry of Revenue's taxpayer register itself. The nearest held fact is that [National ID Ethiopia's 2026 deck says over 5 million existing taxpayer numbers (TINs) have been harmonised with Fayda, the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>); it does not say how the register is kept.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found says the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest held fact is that [over 5 million existing TINs are harmonised with Fayda, according to National ID Ethiopia in May 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** [National ID Ethiopia told the ID4Africa meeting in May 2026 that the Ministry of Revenue has harmonised over 5 million existing TINs with Fayda and that Fayda has been made mandatory for new TINs nationally](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>). Every new taxpayer number is therefore issued against the national ID.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found describes the taxpayer register itself or says the TIN is shared with business registration or customs.
