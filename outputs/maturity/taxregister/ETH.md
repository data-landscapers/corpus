# Ethiopia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** The Ministry of Revenue registers taxpayers on computer and captures their fingerprints. [In May 2024 the research centre ICTD said the ministry was already using biometric data to register taxpayer numbers (TINs)](<https://ictd.ac/general/ethiopia-national-digital-id-breakthrough-tax-system-transformation/>). Government bodies use those numbers for several public services, the same source says.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found says the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest held fact is that [over 5 million existing TINs had been matched with Fayda, the national ID, by May 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** New taxpayer numbers are issued against the national ID. [ICTD says each new TIN has been connected to a Fayda number since December 2023](<https://ictd.ac/general/ethiopia-national-digital-id-breakthrough-tax-system-transformation/>). [A 2024 Fayda deck lists a taxpayer number based on the Fayda number as one of its uses](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-1-Ethiopia-final.pdf>). [In May 2026 the ID programme said Fayda is mandatory for new TINs and over 5 million older ones have been matched](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-1-Ethiopia-FINAL.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs.
