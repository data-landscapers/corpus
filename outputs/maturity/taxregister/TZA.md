# Tanzania

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** Taxpayer numbers are issued online. [The identity authority NIDA lists online TIN registration among the services run through its consent-based identity check](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). The Revenue Authority then moved to a new system. [Its trainers said in January 2026 that IDRAS, the new domestic tax system, would start on 9 February 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>). Under it a parent can obtain a TIN for a child.

**Shared with firms or customs.** Nothing found says whether the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest fact is that [the Revenue Authority's new domestic tax system was due to start in February 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Tied to ID.** Taxpayer numbers are issued from the national ID. [In February 2024 NIDA said its national register made the issuing of TINs possible](<https://nida.go.tz/Habari-Maelezo?value=1>). [Its 2026 deck lists the Revenue Authority's taxpayer registration among the services joined to the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [By September 2026, 8.4 million TINs had been registered using national ID numbers, NIDA said](<https://nida.go.tz/uploads/speeches/20260911123900_doc.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [IDRAS](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tax-administration-driver-licence-goes-digital/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
