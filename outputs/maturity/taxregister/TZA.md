# Tanzania

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The identity authority NIDA lists online TIN registration among the services delivered through its consent-based eKYC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). [Tanzania Revenue Authority trainers said on 27 January 2026 that IDRAS, the integrated domestic tax system due to start on 9 February 2026, lets a parent obtain a TIN for a child and requires existing TIN holders to reset their passwords](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** Nothing held says whether the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest fact is that [the Revenue Authority's new domestic tax system, IDRAS, was due to start in February 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Tied to ID.** Nothing held says the TIN is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest fact is that [NIDA names online TIN registration as one of the services its eKYC delivers](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [IDRAS](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tax-administration-driver-licence-goes-digital/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
