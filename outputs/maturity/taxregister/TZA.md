# Tanzania

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The identity authority NIDA lists online TIN registration among the services delivered through its consent-based eKYC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>). [Tanzania Revenue Authority trainers said on 27 January 2026 that IDRAS, the integrated domestic tax system due to start on 9 February 2026, lets a parent obtain a TIN for a child and requires existing TIN holders to reset their passwords](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** Nothing found says whether the TIN is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest fact is that [the Revenue Authority's new domestic tax system, IDRAS, was due to start in February 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Tied to ID.** [NIDA's 2026 deck lists the Tanzania Revenue Authority's taxpayer registration among the services integrated with the national ID, and names the Revenue Authority among the bodies that require the ID for their services](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [NIDA also lists online TIN registration among the services delivered through its consent-based eKYC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [IDRAS](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tax-administration-driver-licence-goes-digital/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
