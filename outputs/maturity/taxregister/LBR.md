# Liberia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found since October 2023 says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [in February 2024 new taxpayers sought a TIN from the Liberia Revenue Authority](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the identification registry's API connects to the Liberia Revenue Authority](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

**Tied to ID.** [In February 2024 the National Identification Registry (NIR) described its API](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>). [The API connects the Liberia Revenue Authority with new taxpayers wanting a TIN](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says whether taxpayers are registered on computer, or that the TIN is shared with business registration or customs.
