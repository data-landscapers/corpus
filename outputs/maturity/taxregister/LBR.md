# Liberia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** No source found says a computerised taxpayer register is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The NIR says its API connects the Liberia Revenue Authority with new taxpayers wanting a TIN](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

**Tied to ID.** [The NIR says its API connects the Liberia Revenue Authority with new taxpayers wanting a TIN](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says whether taxpayers are registered on computer, or that the TIN is shared with business registration or customs.
