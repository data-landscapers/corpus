# Ghana

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** The Ghana Revenue Authority keeps a taxpayer numbering system. The Revenue Administration Act 2016 sets it up. In August 2026 the authority said it [issues TINs to organisations at its offices](<https://gra.gov.gh/tin/>). It also runs an online portal to check a tax number.

**Shared with firms or customs.** The authority's [TIN Centre sits at the Registrar-General's Department as a one-stop shop](<https://gra.gov.gh/tin/>). It issues an organisation's TIN during business registration. Organisations register for a TIN only after registering with the Registrar-General, the authority said in August 2026.

**Tied to ID.** For individuals the national ID number is the tax number. The authority says [the Ghana Card PIN replaced the TIN it used to issue](<https://gra.gov.gh/tin/>) from 1 April 2021. In September 2025 the identity authority's head said [the Ghana Card already serves as the tax number](<https://atlfmnews.com/nia-to-integrate-student-database-with-ghana-card-for-easy-access/>). In May 2026 he added that [a child registered for the card gets a tax number automatically](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/05/nia-registers-over-32000-children-in-volta-oti-regions/>).
