# Namibia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In a notice of 1 April 2026 the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA) told traders they could apply for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) online through its Integrated Tax Administration System, ITAS](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice-asycudaworld-golive-date-extended-e62d0a7fe7.pdf>). The same notice moved the go-live of the ASYCUDAWorld customs upgrade to 5 May 2026.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [NamRA says the TIN it issues is mandatory on every customs declaration in ASYCUDAWorld from 1 April 2026, and declarations without a valid TIN are rejected automatically](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice--mandatory-tin-on-asycudaworld--9-dec-2025-7d1548a64e.pdf>). [Its April notice sent traders to ITAS for the number ahead of the upgrade](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice-asycudaworld-golive-date-extended-e62d0a7fe7.pdf>), so one number serves both tax and customs.

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is the national ID number or is checked against the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the TIN is applied for through ITAS](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/public-notice-asycudaworld-golive-date-extended-e62d0a7fe7.pdf>).
