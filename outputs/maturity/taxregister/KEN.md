# Kenya

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Kenya Revenue Authority's release notes of November and December 2025 describe the iTax Taxpayer Registration Module in service](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>). [Taxpayers apply for a PIN, check a PIN, amend their profile and register obligations online](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>). [They use the iTax portal, the M-Service app or USSD](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [taxpayers register their tax obligations on iTax](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [iTax offers a PIN checker](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Kenya Investment Single Window (KISW)](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
