# Kenya

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Kenya Revenue Authority's November/December 2025 release notes describe the iTax Taxpayer Registration Module in service, with PIN application, PIN checker, profile amendment and obligation registration online, via the portal, M-Service app and USSD](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/ITAX-ENHANCEMENTS-NOV-DEC-2025.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
