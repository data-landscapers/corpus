# DR Congo

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The DGI says NIF registration is free and that applicants should request the NIF online via the e-NIF platform or else at tax offices](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>). [Actualite.cd lists as the first step to a new passport obtaining a NIF by applying on the e-NIF website](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The DGI lists the Guichet Unique de Création d'Entreprise among the places where a NIF can be requested](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>).
