# DR Congo

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [In December 2025 the tax directorate (DGI) said registration for the taxpayer number, the Numéro d'Identification Fiscale (NIF), is free](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>). [Applicants request the NIF online through the e-NIF platform, or else at tax offices](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>). [In June 2025 Actualite.cd listed a NIF as the first step to a new passport](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance>). [Applicants obtain it on the e-NIF website](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The DGI's December 2025 notice lists the Guichet Unique de Création d'Entreprise among the places to request a NIF](<https://dgi.gouv.cd/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/COMMUNIQUE%CC%81-OFFICIEL-N%C2%B001_061.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [in June 2025 Actualite.cd said applicants for a new passport must first obtain a NIF](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2025/06/06/rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir-du-processus-dobtention-du-nouveau-passeport-biometrique-lance>).
