# Somalia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** A computerised tax system is being built. [In November 2026 an opinion piece said ITAS, the revenue authority's new platform, will bring taxpayer registration and a single record of every taxpayer onto one system](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Nov/206761/feisal_mohamed_hashi_the_vision_behind_somalias_revenue_reforms.aspx>). [Its rollout is due to begin at the end of 2026](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Nov/206761/feisal_mohamed_hashi_the_vision_behind_somalias_revenue_reforms.aspx>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found says the taxpayer number is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest fact found: [ITAS is to hold a single record of every taxpayer](<https://www.hiiraan.com/op4/2026/Nov/206761/feisal_mohamed_hashi_the_vision_behind_somalias_revenue_reforms.aspx>).

**Tied to ID.** [In August 2024 the Deputy Minister of Finance announced the coming launch of a taxpayer number linked to the national ID system](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2024/Aug/197694/somalia_introduces_modern_digital_identification_to_enhance_banking_services.aspx>). No source found says it has been launched.
