# Madagascar

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The Direction Generale des Impots page says applicants for a NIF (taxpayer identification number) fill in an online registration form on NIFONLINE, get a reference, then present originals at their tax office](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>). [A finance ministry arrete of 12 October 2017 makes the online tax number (NIF online), issued by the tax directorate, the single identifier across all ministry departments and checked in public procurement (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://textes.lexxika.com/lois-malagasy/arrete-n25508-2017-du-12-octobre-2016-fixant-le-numero-dimmatriculation-fiscale-en-ligne-nif-online-comme-etant-lidentifiant-unique-utilise-par-tous-les-departements-au-sein-du-ministere-d/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>).
