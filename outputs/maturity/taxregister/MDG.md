# Madagascar

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [The tax directorate says NIF applicants fill in an online form on NIFONLINE and get a reference](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>). [They then present originals at their tax office](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>). [In October 2017 a finance ministry arrete made the online tax number the single identifier across the ministry](<https://textes.lexxika.com/lois-malagasy/arrete-n25508-2017-du-12-octobre-2016-fixant-le-numero-dimmatriculation-fiscale-en-ligne-nif-online-comme-etant-lidentifiant-unique-utilise-par-tous-les-departements-au-sein-du-ministere-d/>). [It is checked in public procurement](<https://textes.lexxika.com/lois-malagasy/arrete-n25508-2017-du-12-octobre-2016-fixant-le-numero-dimmatriculation-fiscale-en-ligne-nif-online-comme-etant-lidentifiant-unique-utilise-par-tous-les-departements-au-sein-du-ministere-d/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the NIF online is the single identifier across all finance ministry departments](<https://textes.lexxika.com/lois-malagasy/arrete-n25508-2017-du-12-octobre-2016-fixant-le-numero-dimmatriculation-fiscale-en-ligne-nif-online-comme-etant-lidentifiant-unique-utilise-par-tous-les-departements-au-sein-du-ministere-d/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [applicants present originals at their tax office](<https://hetraonline.impots.mg/accueil/papier_inscri>).
