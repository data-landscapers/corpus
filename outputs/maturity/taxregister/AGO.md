# Angola

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [Angola's SEPE e-services portal lists a free online service, 'Consulta de NIF'](<https://sepe.gov.ao/catalogo/eservicos>). It consults the information held against a person's taxpayer number. [The civil identification systems are synchronised with the tax administration, the AGT](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>). Secretary of State for Justice Osvaldo Amaro said so in September 2025. The number is therefore issued from a register held on computer. [The AGT's taxpayer register held 11,173,618 taxpayers in January 2024](<https://www.expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/apenas-2-em-cada-10-cidadaos-tem-oficialmente-numero-de-identificacao-fiscal-61658.html>). Of these, 10,849,787 were individuals. The figures are AGT data cited by Expansão.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found says the taxpayer number, the NIF, is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest held fact is that [the identity card number also serves as the NIF, according to the Secretary of State for Justice in September 2025](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Tied to ID.** [Osvaldo Amaro said in September 2025 that synchronising the identification systems with the tax administration lets the number of the identity card, the Bilhete de Identidade, correspond to the taxpayer number](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>). One number therefore serves a person as both identity number and tax number. [The SEPE portal's NIF lookup](<https://sepe.gov.ao/catalogo/eservicos>) is the online service held for that number.
