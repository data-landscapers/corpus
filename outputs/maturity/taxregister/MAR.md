# Morocco

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** In May 2026 the tax directorate [launched an online platform, Taxation on Digital Services, for foreign providers of remote services](<https://fr.hespress.com/489385-publicite-meta-applique-la-tva-au-maroc-des-octobre.html>). [Non-resident providers register there, declare their Moroccan turnover and pay VAT](<https://fr.hespress.com/489385-publicite-meta-applique-la-tva-au-maroc-des-octobre.html>). The platform rests on [decree 2.25.862 of 27 November 2025](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>). [It requires those providers to register on the tax administration's electronic platform](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>). [The administration then issues them a tax identification number electronically](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>). [The decree took effect six months after publication](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is issued at business registration or used by customs. The nearest held fact: [tax identification numbers are issued electronically to non-resident providers when they register](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is the national ID number or is checked against it. The nearest held fact: [the decree setting how non-resident suppliers register for VAT](<https://ecoactu.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/decret-pour-la-TVA-sur-les-services-a-distance-au-Maroc-fournis-par-des-prestataires-non-residents-Art-115-Bis-du-CGI.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 shows the register covering taxpayers other than non-resident suppliers. None says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs, or tied to the national ID.
