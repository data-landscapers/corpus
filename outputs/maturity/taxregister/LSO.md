# Lesotho

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [Revenue Services Lesotho's online form registers individual taxpayers (employees, directors, partners) and asks for a TIN (New) in the format 200000000-0](<https://eservices.rsl.org.ls/ereg/public/individualform>). [Revenue Services Lesotho says individual taxpayers can register online through eTax to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number, while companies obtain the TIN from RSL after registering with the relevant ministries](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-06/Guide%20on%20Tax%20Registration.pdf>). [Revenue Service Lesotho said every client can register online through its fully implemented e-Taxation system](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/rsl-implements-e-taxation-system/>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eservices.rsl.org.ls/ereg/public/individualform>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://eservices.rsl.org.ls/ereg/public/individualform>).
