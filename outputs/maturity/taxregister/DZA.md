# Algeria

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [El Watan reports the High Commission for Digitisation said it integrated NIF tax registration attestations for individuals and for legal persons into the Dzair Digital Services portal](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>). [The portal was launched with the DGI at the Tipasa proximity tax centre](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>). [The Direction Générale des Impôts says the NIF taxpayer number is obtained online through the online tax registration and NIF authentication portal](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>). [There applicants file, track and print the registration attestation](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>). [Decentralised NIF registration is also in place at DIW, CDI and CPI offices](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** [The commercial register's company-creation portal lets a founder register once online with every administration concerned](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [The founder receives one common identification number within three days](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [This follows approval of the filing by the commercial register centre, the tax directorate, the two social security funds and the statistics office](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the NIF is obtained online through the tax directorate's registration portal](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>).

***Also on file***. [Dzair Digital Services](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is the national ID number. None found says it is checked against that number.
