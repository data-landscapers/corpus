# Algeria

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [El Watan reports the High Commission for Digitisation said it integrated NIF tax registration attestations for individuals and for legal persons into the Dzair Digital Services portal, launched with the DGI at the Tipasa proximity tax centre](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>). [The Direction Générale des Impôts says the NIF taxpayer number is obtained online through the online tax registration and NIF authentication portal, where applicants file, track and print the registration attestation, and that decentralised NIF registration is also in place at DIW, CDI and CPI offices](<https://www.mfdgi.gov.dz/fr/professionnels/nouveau-contribuable-fr/ouvrir-dossier-fiscal>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>).

**Tied to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Dzair Digital Services](<https://elwatan.dz/dzair-digital-services-impots-integration-de-cinq-nouveaux-services-numeriques/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
