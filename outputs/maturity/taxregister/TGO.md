# Togo

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [OTR says individuals can create a NIF particulier online at nif.otr.tg or at tax offices, the platform also searching existing NIFs and issuing a registration sheet](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/otr-actu/598-magazine-otr-actu-n-18-janvier-juin-2025/file.html>). [The OTR says individual taxpayers can now create or recover their tax identification number (NIF) online at nif.otr.tg, presented 18 December 2023](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/1290-l-otr-innove-la-creation-du-nif-et-le-payement-de-la-tvm-desormais-disponible-en-ligne.html>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** [OTR says article 7 of the tax procedures book requires one unique NIF per taxpayer, and the individual NIF is required for customs clearance of personal goods and for property registration](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/otr-actu/598-magazine-otr-actu-n-18-janvier-juin-2025/file.html>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/otr-actu/598-magazine-otr-actu-n-18-janvier-juin-2025/file.html>).
