# Zimbabwe

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [ZIMRA states taxpayers register online through its TaRMS Self-Service Portal](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>). [A Taxpayer Identification Number is allocated there once an official approves the application](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>). [ZIMRA Public Notice 75 of 2024 says taxpayers are held in TaRMS under a Taxpayer Identity Number (TIN)](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>). [Banks validate the TIN against payments, and tax clearance certificates are issued automatically](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>). [ZIMRA says TaRMS Taxpayer Registration launched on 12 October 2023](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2294-tarms-innovation-heralding-modernity-tax-agent-module-is-live>). [By early 2024, 103,859 taxpayers had registered (36,745 new, 67,114 on-boarded)](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2294-tarms-innovation-heralding-modernity-tax-agent-module-is-live>). [By August 2025 ZIMRA had issued more than 1.2 million Taxpayer Identification Numbers through TaRMS](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2025/08/zimra-commissions-tarms-digital-tax-system/>), with over 184,000 active taxpayers.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [banks validate the TIN against payments](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since November 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [an official approves each TIN application](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>).
