# Zimbabwe

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [ZIMRA states taxpayers register online through its TaRMS Self-Service Portal, where a Taxpayer Identification Number is allocated once an official approves the application](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>).

**Tied to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs.
