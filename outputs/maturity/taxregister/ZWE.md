# Zimbabwe

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** [ZIMRA states taxpayers register online through its TaRMS Self-Service Portal, where a Taxpayer Identification Number is allocated once an official approves the application](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>). [ZIMRA Public Notice 75 of 2024 says taxpayers are held in TaRMS under a Taxpayer Identity Number (TIN) that banks validate against payments, with tax clearance certificates issued automatically](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>). [ZIMRA says TaRMS Taxpayer Registration launched on 12 October 2023 and 103,859 taxpayers had registered by early 2024 (36,745 new, 67,114 on-boarded)](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2294-tarms-innovation-heralding-modernity-tax-agent-module-is-live>).

**Shared with firms or customs.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is shared with business registration or customs. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>).

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the taxpayer number is tied to the national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2346:taxpayer-registration>).
