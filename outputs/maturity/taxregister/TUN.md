# Tunisia

## Tax register

**Computerised register.** The report on the 2027 State budget bill says the tax administration [launched an online platform for the declaration of existence](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/10/27/575585/tunisie-la-digitalisation-fiscale-permet-deconomiser-6-000-journees-de-travail/>). Through it a taxpayer can obtain a tax identification card online. The report, published in October 2026, says the platform is meant to simplify procedures and shorten processing times.

**Shared with firms or customs.** The National Business Register [says the unique identifier of each registered firm is its matricule fiscal](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>), the tax number. Its statement is dated 2026.

**Tied to ID.** No source found since October 2023 ties the tax number to the national ID. None says it is checked against the ID either. The nearest fact found is [the National Business Register's use of the tax number for firms](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>). That fact is dated 2026.
