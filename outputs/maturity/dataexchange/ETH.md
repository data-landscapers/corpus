# Ethiopia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In July 2026 an official said a board set up by the prime minister approved a national data exchange platform for government systems](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>). [DT Global, an EU-funded adviser, submitted the technical design of the EthioConnect platform in 2024](<https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/politics/dt-global-has-supported-ethiopias-digital-government-strategy/20241227125859209382.html>). [It was preparing priority datasets for a pilot, with procurement still to come](<https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/politics/dt-global-has-supported-ethiopias-digital-government-strategy/20241227125859209382.html>). [A World Bank financing agreement of December 2023 funds a whole-of-government data exchange platform with open APIs](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123090519104/pdf/P1790401f9447f0c8185031717ad18a7a00.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).
