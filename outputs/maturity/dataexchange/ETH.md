# Ethiopia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [An official named Anteneh said the prime minister recently established a board that approved the creation of a national data exchange platform to let government systems communicate, which the article's passages do not describe as live](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>). [DT Global, EU-funded adviser, says it has submitted the technical design of the EthioConnect national data exchange platform and is preparing priority datasets for its pilot project, with procurement still to come](<https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/politics/dt-global-has-supported-ethiopias-digital-government-strategy/20241227125859209382.html>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).
