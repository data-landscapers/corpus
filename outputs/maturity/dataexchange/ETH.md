# Ethiopia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [An official named Anteneh said the prime minister recently established a board that approved the creation of a national data exchange platform to let government systems communicate, which the article's passages do not describe as live](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>). [The World Bank (IDA) financing agreement, signed 20-21 December 2023, funds development of a whole-of-government data exchange platform with open APIs to integrate Fayda, payment systems and other digital public infrastructure (Part 4.2)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123090519104/pdf/P1790401f9447f0c8185031717ad18a7a00.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [MESOB](<https://birrmetrics.com/mesob-meets-the-monster-it-was-built-to-kill/>); [Fayda](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123090519104/pdf/P1790401f9447f0c8185031717ad18a7a00.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the platform is in service.
