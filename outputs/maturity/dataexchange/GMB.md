# Gambia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In November 2025 the World Bank recorded no interoperability across the social registry, the education management systems, civil registration and the health insurance system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [Its target is at least three of those systems interoperable by October 2028](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). [One instance was being verified](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>). It is the only fact found on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included. The systems themselves were not examined.

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>).
