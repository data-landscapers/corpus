# Mozambique

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The health ministry's SIS-MA has an interoperability platform with live links to the medicines-logistics system and the Ministry of Justice's civil registration system](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>). [In June 2026 ATDI said INATRO, the civil identification directorate and the Ministry of Justice were joining the national interoperability platform](<https://noticias.karingana.co.mz/2026/06/26/atdi-e-bau-alinham-visao-de-servicos-publicos-digitais-integrados-e-centrados-no-cidadao/>). [In October 2026 ATDI trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies to join their systems to X-Road](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-reforca-integracao-digital-do-estado-atraves-da-tecnologia-x-road/>). [ATDI's government interoperability platform is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>).
