# Mozambique

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The health ministry's SIS-MA has an interoperability platform with live links to the medicines-logistics system and the Ministry of Justice's civil registration system](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>). [In October 2026 ATDI trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies to join their systems to X-Road](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-reforca-integracao-digital-do-estado-atraves-da-tecnologia-x-road/>). [ATDI's government interoperability platform is expected in the fourth quarter of 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). [In June 2025 INAGE's technical group reviewed data mapping between the Bank of Mozambique and the Tax Authority](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/inage-ip-gov-518591357_inage-ip-lidera-implementa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-plataforma-activity-7339268558721343489-Y9nP>).
