# Mozambique

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [MISAU's SIS-MA page describes an interoperability platform, a mediating layer centralising communication between SIS-MA and other systems, with live integrations to the medicines-logistics system of CMAM (Ferramenta Central) and to the Ministry of Justice's SIRCEV for death-certificate data](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>). [ATDI trained 25 technicians from nine public bodies, including civil identification, migration, tax, social action and registries, on the X-Road data-exchange layer to prepare them to integrate their institutions' systems, with World Bank EDGE support](<https://mznews.co.mz/mocambique-reforca-integracao-digital-do-estado-atraves-da-tecnologia-x-road/>). [ATDI is developing an interoperability platform for government that is expected to be available in the fourth quarter of 2026, so citizens give their data only once](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/mozambique-plans-unified-citizen-portal-to-streamline-digital-govt-services>). [On 12 June 2025 INAGE's technical group for the Government Interoperability Platform reviewed data mapping between the Bank of Mozambique and the Tax Authority and the test environment for the systems to be linked](<https://pt.linkedin.com/posts/inage-ip-gov-518591357_inage-ip-lidera-implementa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-plataforma-activity-7339268558721343489-Y9nP>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sisma.misau.gov.mz/interoperability.html>).
