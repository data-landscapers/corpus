# Burundi

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** On 17 November 2026 the PAFEN project [issued a request for proposals for a government interoperability platform on the X-Road model](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00482177>), funded by IDA. The project is the US$50 million Digital Foundations Project, whose [2022 appraisal provided for a national data exchange layer](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf>) and its connection to information systems. [The 2026 tender pairs the platform with a public key infrastructure](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00482177>). The notice says [institutions work largely in silos, with few standard means of exchanging data](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00482177>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found counts the bodies connected to a data exchange platform. [The platform's build is to run 15 months from contract](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00482177>), working with the ICT secretariat SETIC and the participating institutions. [In 2022 the World Bank found ministries and agencies running their own separate servers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found gives a figure for exchanges through a government platform. [The tender closes in January 2027](<https://projects.banquemondiale.org/fr/projects-operations/procurement-detail/OP00482177>).
