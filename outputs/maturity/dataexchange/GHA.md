# Ghana

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [On 21 October 2026 the communications minister said the government is building a national data exchange hub](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-national-data-exchange-hub-to-connect-48-public-registries/>). [It is to connect 48 public registries, among them the identification authority, the statistics service, the births and deaths registry and the police](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-national-data-exchange-hub-to-connect-48-public-registries/>). [An October 2026 analysis says the ministry and NITA are moving to operationalise the hub under the Ghana Digital Acceleration Project](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>). [DIAL says bids for the hub were reissued in 2019 and 2022](<https://dial.global/research/national-data-exchange-ghana-case-study/>).
