# Ghana

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The analyst says the Ministry of Communication, Digital Technology and Innovations and NITA, under the World Bank-backed Ghana Digital Acceleration Project, are moving to operationalise a National Data Exchange Hub, described elsewhere as soon-to-be-established](<https://imaniafrica.org/2026/10/the-missing-plumbing-of-the-state-why-ghanas-next-digital-leap-depends-on-a-whole-of-government-data-exchange/>). [Communications minister Sam George said the ministry is developing a national data exchange hub intended to connect 48 government data registries](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/ghana-targets-5bn-in-annual-economic-gains-from-ai-sam-george/>). [On 21 October 2026 he said the hub is being built as a single backbone for 48 public registries, among them the National Identification Authority, the statistics service, the births and deaths registry and the police](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/sam-george-announces-national-data-exchange-hub-to-connect-48-public-registries/>). [DIAL says the Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation re-issued bids for a National Data Exchange Hub in 2019 and 2022, with status unclear (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://dial.global/research/national-data-exchange-ghana-case-study/>).
