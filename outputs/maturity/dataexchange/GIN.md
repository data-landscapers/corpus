# Guinea

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Minister Bourouno told the prime minister on 20 August 2026 that the national interoperability portal for existing information systems had been set up (finalisation de la mise en place), run by ANDE](<https://www.visionguinee.info/transformation-numerique-le-ministre-francois-bourouno-presente-a-bah-oury-deux-plateformes-pour-moderniser-ladministration-guineenne/>). [On 20 August 2026 the minister presented the state of operationalisation of X-Road, the national interoperability platform piloted by ANDE and meant to enable exchange between administration information systems](<https://www.africaguinee.com/acceleration-de-la-modernisation-de-ladministration-le-ministre-bourouno-presente-deux-outils-strategiques-au-premier-ministre/>). [ONECI and NG Tech Innovation signed an MoU on 24 July 2026 in Conakry to set up a national interoperability and identity-verification platform for the RNPP, under the Diama Djigui project](<https://horoya.net/2026/07/25/guinee-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-sallient-pour-moderniser-lidentification-et-letat-civil/>).
