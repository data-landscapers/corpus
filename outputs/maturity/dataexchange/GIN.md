**Exchange platform built and in service.** [On 20 August 2026 the minister told the prime minister that the national interoperability portal had been set up](<https://www.visionguinee.info/transformation-numerique-le-ministre-francois-bourouno-presente-a-bah-oury-deux-plateformes-pour-moderniser-ladministration-guineenne/>). [On 24 July 2026 ONECI signed an MoU with NG Tech Innovation for an identity-verification and interoperability platform for the RNPP](<https://horoya.net/2026/07/25/guinee-loneci-et-ng-tech-innovation-sallient-pour-moderniser-lidentification-et-letat-civil/>). [On the same day the platform's launch was presented to the prime minister as a first step](<https://nimba224.com/revolution-numerique-la-guinee-veut-mettre-fin-a-la-bureaucratie-et-transformer-radicalement-ses-services-publics/>).
