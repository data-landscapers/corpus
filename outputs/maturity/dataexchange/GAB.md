# Gabon

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In September 2026 Gabon was developing an interoperability platform for government information systems to exchange data securely](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>). [The sixth e-Gabon-SIS and Gabon Digital steering committee reviewed it](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>). [The World Bank's December 2024 report set a 2026 target of five government entities exchanging data on it, against none at baseline](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924052032986/pdf/P175987-0914ff29-7941-4230-8202-16cca6789388.pdf>). [The World Bank loan that finances it had disbursed 1.92 percent of its funds by December 2024](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924052032986/pdf/P175987-0914ff29-7941-4230-8202-16cca6789388.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>).
