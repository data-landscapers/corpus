# Gabon

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Tech Africa News reports Gabon is developing an interoperability platform meant to let government information systems securely exchange data, reviewed at the sixth e-Gabon-SIS and Gabon Digital steering committee](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>). [The World Bank's Digital Gabon ISR (Dec 2024) sets a closing-2026 target of 5 government entities exchanging data on the government interoperability platform, with the actual at baseline 0, and the loan only 1.92% disbursed](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121924052032986/pdf/P175987-0914ff29-7941-4230-8202-16cca6789388.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>).

***Also on file***. [National Digital Identity System](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>); [government messaging system](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/gabon-four-digital-projects-modernise-public-services/>).
