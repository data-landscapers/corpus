# Algeria

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The CNRC says the company-creation portal gives access to an electronic exchange platform between CNRC, the tax directorate (DGI), CNAS, CASNOS and ONS](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [El Chaab's dossier says the national interoperability system lets ministries such as health, justice and interior coordinate without duplicating data, relying on data classification and indexing references](<https://www.echaab.dz/2026/09/20/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A9/>). [The expert lists, among official figures, the launch of a secure data exchange platform and the national digital services portal, giving no description of bodies connected](<https://elmasdareliktissadi.dz/?p=44354>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>).
