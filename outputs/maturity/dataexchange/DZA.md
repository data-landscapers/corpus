# Algeria

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In October 2026 the CNRC said its company-creation portal opens onto an electronic exchange platform](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [It links the CNRC with the tax directorate, CNAS, CASNOS and ONS](<https://sidjilcom-entreprise.cnrc.dz/acceuil>). [El Chaab wrote in September 2026 that the national interoperability system lets ministries such as health, justice and interior share data without duplicating it](<https://www.echaab.dz/2026/09/20/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D9%81%D8%A7%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%B9%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A9/>). [In February 2026 the High Commission for Digitalisation said ministries had been feeding data into that system since 2025](<https://almostathmir.dz/%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7/>).
