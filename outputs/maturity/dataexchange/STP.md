# Sao Tome and Principe

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In September 2025 a World Bank mission saw live demonstrations of prototypes of the DEX interoperability and data-exchange platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>). [Ooru is building it with INIC and DGRN](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>). [Ooru says the national Data Exchange Layer linking ministries' systems comes in the programme's second phase, in 2026](<https://ooru.io/case-studies/sao-tome>). [In April 2025 the World Bank counted no government systems on the national data exchange layer, against five by May 2027](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). [The University of Aveiro was building a link between the civil registry and the electoral roll in 2025](<https://www.telanon.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NOTA-DE-IMPRENSA-1.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>).
