# Sao Tome and Principe

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The World Bank's 8-12 September 2025 mission to the STP Digital project saw live demonstrations of functional prototypes of the interoperability and data-exchange platform (DEX), being designed and implemented by the System Integrator Ooru with INIC and DGRN, with integration of the interoperability layer with the verifiable credentials infrastructure planned for March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>). [Ooru, the implementer, says the national Data Exchange Layer that links the ministries' systems comes in the programme's second phase, in 2026](<https://ooru.io/case-studies/sao-tome>), after the identity system and Citizen Portal of the first.

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>).
