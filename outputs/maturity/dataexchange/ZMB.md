# Zambia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ZamPortal says the Government Service Bus integrates government institutions, departments and agencies](<https://zamportal.gov.zm/director-sibulowa-introduces-the-gsb/>). [In July 2026 the Accountant General's office called it central to public service delivery](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [Eight local authorities joined it from June 2026, so residents can pay rates, levies and permits electronically](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>). [In August 2026 labour ministry staff were trained to handle cases arriving through it](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/08/23/labour-ministry-pushes-digital-services-to-expand-access-across-zambia/>).

**Many systems connected.** [In July 2026 the Accountant General's office said more than 400 government services had joined the Government Service Bus, ZamPortal and ZamPay](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [In 2024 SMART Zambia said 290 services were deployed on the bus in six months](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=2814>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>).
