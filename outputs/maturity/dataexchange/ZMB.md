# Zambia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Ministry of Labour and Social Security staff were trained to handle cases submitted through the Citizen Support Services Portal and the ZELMIS system on the Government Services Bus, per Permanent Secretary Zechariah Luhanga](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/08/23/labour-ministry-pushes-digital-services-to-expand-access-across-zambia/>). [The Office of the Accountant General, speaking through a Ministry of Finance principal accountant, called the Government Service Bus, with ZamPortal and ZamPay, central to public service delivery, and said eight councils were already onboarded](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [Eight local authorities were onboarded onto the Government Services Bus from June 2026, letting residents and businesses pay property rates, business levies and permits electronically](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>).

**Many systems connected.** [The Accountant General's office says more than 400 government services have been onboarded onto the digital ecosystem of the Government Service Bus, ZamPortal and ZamPay, and plans a rollout to all 116 local authorities](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [SMART Zambia's National Coordinator said 290 services were deployed on the Government Service Bus in the past six months](<https://www.szi.gov.zm/?p=2814>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>).
