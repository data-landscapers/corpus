# Senegal

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [A government presenter at the WSIS Forum 2026 session said the X-Road based interoperability platform PINs has had an MVP 1.0 launched with two use cases, social protection and public finance](<https://dig.watch/event/wsis-forum-2026/new-technological-deal-or-or-how-a-global-south-digital-strategy-for-impactful-ict-transformation>). [The platform's own portal describes the Plateforme d'Interoperabilite du Senegal (e-Jokkoo, built on X-Road) as connecting state information systems for secure data exchange between administrations, with API Gateway, Identite Numerique and Data Exchange services listed](<https://pins.sn/>). [MCTN/PAENS called on 24 September 2026 for expressions of interest, closing 7 October 2026, to study infrastructure needs of the future X-Road-based national interoperability platform PINS, financed by a World Bank IDA credit](<https://guindima.sn/news/article/appel-a-manifestation-dinteret-etude-des-besoins-en-infrastructure-de-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite-nationale-pins/>).
