# Senegal

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [At the WSIS Forum 2026 a government presenter said PINs, built on X-Road, has launched a minimum viable product with two use cases](<https://dig.watch/event/wsis-forum-2026/new-technological-deal-or-or-how-a-global-south-digital-strategy-for-impactful-ict-transformation>). [They are social protection and public finance](<https://dig.watch/event/wsis-forum-2026/new-technological-deal-or-or-how-a-global-south-digital-strategy-for-impactful-ict-transformation>). [The platform's portal, e-Jokkoo, lists API gateway, digital identity and data exchange services](<https://pins.sn/>). [On 24 September 2026 the ministry called for a study of the infrastructure the future PINS platform needs](<https://guindima.sn/news/article/appel-a-manifestation-dinteret-etude-des-besoins-en-infrastructure-de-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite-nationale-pins/>). [The project was launched with JICA on 22 April 2025, with large-scale deployment planned for 2025 to 2027](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/le-senegal-lance-une-plateforme-interministerielle-et-interoperable-pour-les-echanges-de-donnees/>).
