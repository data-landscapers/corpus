# Nigeria

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In August 2025 the government was preparing to launch NGDX, a data-sharing platform for agencies and businesses](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-to-launch-national-data-exchange-platform-to-boost-digital-econom>). [It was expected by the end of 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-to-launch-national-data-exchange-platform-to-boost-digital-econom>). [In March 2026 Gofore consultants set up NGDX's first X-Road instance in Abuja](<https://haus.fi/en/topical/gofore-supports-nigeria-in-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi-development/>). [Its first uses were agreed but not live](<https://haus.fi/en/topical/gofore-supports-nigeria-in-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi-development/>). [In May 2026 an EU-funded EUR 23 million agreement tasked ESTDEV with building an X-Road exchange layer](<https://estdev.ee/en/articles/estonia-and-finland-join-forces-implement-eu-digital-governance-project-nigeria>). [The Government Service Portal, in pilot since 2025, is to integrate with the Nigerian Digital Exchange](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-unveils-digital-govt-platform-for-unified-access-to-public-services>).
