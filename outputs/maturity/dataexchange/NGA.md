# Nigeria

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In August 2025 the government was preparing to launch NGDX, a data-sharing platform for agencies and businesses, expected by the end of 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-to-launch-national-data-exchange-platform-to-boost-digital-econom>). [The Government Service Portal, in pilot since 2025, is to integrate with the Nigerian Digital Exchange so agencies can share data](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-unveils-digital-govt-platform-for-unified-access-to-public-services>). [It integrates some government institutions before a national rollout](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-unveils-digital-govt-platform-for-unified-access-to-public-services>). [NITDA's Abuja workshop of 25 August 2025 presented NGDX as a unified exchange system for government institutions, backed by the EU](<https://www.tekedia.com/fg-to-end-multiple-data-submissions-with-nigerian-data-exchange-platform-ngdx/>).
