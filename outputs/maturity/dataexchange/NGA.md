# Nigeria

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The Government Service Portal, soft-launched with KOICA and Galaxy Backbone, is in a pilot that has run since 2025 and integrates some government institutions before nationwide rollout, with a key feature of integration with the Nigerian Digital Exchange so agencies can share data among them](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/nigeria-unveils-digital-govt-platform-for-unified-access-to-public-services>). [The Nigerian government is preparing to launch the NGDX data-sharing platform between government agencies and businesses, expected operational by end 2025, as discussed at an Abuja workshop on 25 August 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/nigeria-to-launch-national-data-exchange-platform-to-boost-digital-econom>).
