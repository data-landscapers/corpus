# Congo

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In July 2026 an adviser to the digital economy minister wrote that administrations' systems often do not communicate with each other](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>). [He said the government wants to build a data exchange platform between administrations](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>). [The ministry says Congo is committing to a national digital architecture](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). [Its X-Road layer is to link the systems of the Interior, Justice, Finance, Health and Higher Education ministries](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). [The architecture also names MOSIP for identity and Mojaloop for payments](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).
