# Congo

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The ministry (MPTEN) says Congo is committing to develop a national digital architecture whose interoperability layer, X-Road, is to link the information systems of the Interior, Justice, Finance, Health and Higher Education ministries, with no stated go-live or connection count](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>). [An adviser to the digital economy minister, in an op-ed, says administrations often work with systems that do not communicate with each other and that the government wants to build a data exchange platform between administrations](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>).
