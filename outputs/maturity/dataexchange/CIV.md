# Cote d'Ivoire

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [On 3 September 2026 the head of SNDI said the national interoperability platform already improves data exchange between several administrations](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644285/economie/transformation-numerique-la-cote-divoire-priorise-les-services-publics-a-fort-impact>). [The digital transition minister launched it, built on Cybernetica's UXP, in Abidjan on 25 September 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-cote-d-ivoire-lance-une-plateforme-d-interoperabilite-pour-fluidifier-l-administration>). [Five public administrations had joined it by July 2026](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>). [The first phase is meant to connect 12 of the 31 bodies judged ready](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>). [SNDI's contract with Cybernetica covers the platform core, five priority services and skills transfer](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/actualite/146>).
