# Cote d'Ivoire

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The head of SNDI said at the 3 September 2026 workshop that the national interoperability platform is a reality and already improves data exchange between several administrations](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2644285/economie/transformation-numerique-la-cote-divoire-priorise-les-services-publics-a-fort-impact>). [The digital transition minister launched the public administration interoperability platform, built on Cybernetica's UXP, in Abidjan on 25 September 2025; the pilot phase covers twelve public bodies](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-cote-d-ivoire-lance-une-plateforme-d-interoperabilite-pour-fluidifier-l-administration>). [Digital Mag reports that five public administrations have joined the UXP interoperability platform, to be operational before 31 July 2026, within a phase 1 meant to interconnect 12 bodies of 31 judged mature; the project began in September 2025](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-cinq-administrations-publiques-integrent-la-plateforme-dinteroperabilite/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected.
