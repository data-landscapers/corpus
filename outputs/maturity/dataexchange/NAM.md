# Namibia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Cybernetica says an ESTDEV-funded project begun in 2024 continues scaling the Nam-X platform, developing two digital services for it, and was expected to end in March 2025](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/namibia/>). [The World Bank blog says Namibia adopted the Estonian open-source X-Road software after 2011, so the National Population Register now links functional public and private sector registries, and it was used during COVID-19 to validate identity data for emergency income support](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/legal-identity-all-what-can-madagascar-learn-namibia>). [The government is deploying NamX, a data-exchange platform for secure inter-ministry sharing, per UNDP Namibia](<https://www.undp.org/namibia/blog/season-3-namibias-digital-identity-journey-vision-reality>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/namibia/>).
