# Benin

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ASIN's legal note describes Xroad-BJ as Benin's national platform for secure data exchange between administrations' information systems](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>). [Decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020 instituted it, with ASIN managing it and the national interoperability committee supervising](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>). [In July 2026 La Nation reported that it lets administrations' systems communicate securely, and set out the conditions for joining](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj>).

**Many systems connected.** [In September 2026 the same post counted 75 members, 193 information systems and 542 active services](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7506299769514774531>). ANATT, ANIP and DGI [were the platform's top providers in July 2026](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>). [In June 2025 ASIN speakers said more than 55 institutions were connected, banks, telecoms and insurers among them](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/benin-shares-its-x-road-inspired-dpi-interoperability-experience-in-peer-learning-webinar>).

**Use reported.** [In September 2026 an ASIN staff member put the requests through XROAD BJ at 205 million, 26.6 million of them in the last 30 days](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7506299769514774531>). [The dashboard reports 57,322,278 requests in the 12 months to 11 July 2026](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>). [The ASIN speakers said over 70 million data exchange transactions had been completed by June 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/benin-shares-its-x-road-inspired-dpi-interoperability-experience-in-peer-learning-webinar>).
