# Benin

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ASIN's legal note describes Xroad-BJ as Benin's national platform for secure data exchange between administrations' information systems](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>). [Decree 2020-209 of 18 March 2020 instituted it, with ASIN managing it and the national interoperability committee supervising](<https://asin.bj/doc/142/download>). [In July 2026 La Nation reported that it lets administrations' systems communicate securely, and set out the conditions for joining](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/interoperabilite-au-benin-les-regles-dacces-a-xroad-bj>).

**Many systems connected.** [In July 2026 the national dashboard counted 73 members, 176 information systems and 512 services](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>). ANATT, ANIP and DGI [were its top providers](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>). [In June 2025 ASIN speakers said more than 55 institutions were connected, banks, telecoms and insurers among them](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/benin-shares-its-x-road-inspired-dpi-interoperability-experience-in-peer-learning-webinar>). [They said more than 1,500 services were integrated](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/benin-shares-its-x-road-inspired-dpi-interoperability-experience-in-peer-learning-webinar>).

**Use reported.** [The dashboard reports 138,174,337 total requests and 57,322,278 requests in the last 12 months, statistics updated 11 July 2026](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>). [The ASIN speakers said over 70 million data exchange transactions have been completed to date](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/benin-shares-its-x-road-inspired-dpi-interoperability-experience-in-peer-learning-webinar>). [The ministry's scorecard reports more than 42,000 data exchanges through XROAD BJ (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.gouv.bj/doc/191/download>).
