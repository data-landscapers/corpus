# Egypt

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Communications and IT Minister Raafat Hindy said the state has connected government bodies through the Government Digital Transformer, allowing real-time, secure data exchange with more than 100 government databases](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>). [The ICT minister told a cabinet meeting that under the government digital switch project 105 government bodies have been linked, allowing secure data exchange between them](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>). [Ahram Online reports that on 2025-07-17 the PM inaugurated an integration system, run through the G2G digital hub, linking the Justice Ministry's Real Estate Registry and Notarization Authority with the Housing Ministry's New Urban Communities Authority across 16 real estate registry offices in new cities](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>). [The Egyptian Tax Authority's head said in July 2026 that it exchanges data in real time with 15 government bodies, aiming for 37 in a first phase](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657>).

**Many systems connected.** [Minister Hindy said 105 government bodies are connected through the Government Digital Transformer, with integration to more than 100 government databases](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>). [The ICT minister said 105 government bodies are linked through the government digital switch project, and 100 national databases have been linked under a data integration system in development](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>).
