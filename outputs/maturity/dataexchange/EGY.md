# Egypt

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In July 2026 the communications minister said government bodies are connected through the Government Digital Transformer](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>). [It allows real-time, secure exchange with more than 100 government databases](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>). [On 17 July 2025 the prime minister opened a link through the G2G hub between the real estate registry and the New Urban Communities Authority](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/549747/Egypt/Society/Egypt-PM-inaugurates-Real-Estate-Registry-and-Nota.aspx>). [The tax authority's head said in July 2026 that it exchanges data in real time with 15 government bodies](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657>).

**Many systems connected.** [The minister said in July 2026 that 105 government bodies are connected through the Government Digital Transformer](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>). [He told a cabinet meeting the same month that 105 bodies are linked](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>). [He said 100 national databases are linked under a data integration system in development](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5813424.aspx>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://almalnews.com/2126687/>).
