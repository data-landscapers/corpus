# Sudan

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In September 2026 the digital ministry and the National Investment Authority put the needs-disclosure service live on the Baladna platform](<https://atheernews.net/222149/>). [It links the industry, transport and agriculture bodies and passes approved items to Customs electronically](<https://atheernews.net/222149/>). [In December 2025 Baladna carried the first fully electronic export contract, between the central bank, the trade ministry, customs, ports and the tax authority](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b0-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%a5%d9%84/>). [The ministry offers the CONSOLEX API gateway, which integrates government systems for secure exchange](<https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan>).
