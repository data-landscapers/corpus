# Sudan

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** The digital ministry [describes Baldna as the national unified platform for data exchange and government transactions](<https://baldna.gov.sd/>). In November 2026 it listed four services between government bodies. [In September 2026 the ministry and the National Investment Authority put a needs-disclosure service live on it](<https://atheernews.net/222149/>). The service links the industry, transport and agriculture bodies and passes approved items to Customs. [In December 2025 Baladna carried the first fully electronic export contract](<https://sudan.gov.sd/%d8%a8%d8%af%d8%a1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%aa%d9%86%d9%81%d9%8a%d8%b0-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%8a-%d9%84%d9%85%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%b1-%d8%a5%d9%84/>). It joined the central bank, the trade ministry, customs, ports and the tax authority. The ministry also [offers the CONSOLEX API gateway for secure exchange between government systems](<https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan>).

**Many systems connected.** The Baldna home page [counted more than 48 government institutions on the platform](<https://baldna.gov.sd/>) on 11 November 2026. It also counted more than 141 digital services.

**Use reported.** The same page [reported more than 265,785 completed transactions](<https://baldna.gov.sd/>) on 11 November 2026. Of these, 148 were between government bodies.
