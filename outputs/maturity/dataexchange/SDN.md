# Sudan

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The Ministry of Digital Transformation and Communications and the National Investment Authority announced the needs-disclosure service live on the Baladna platform, linking the industry, transport and agriculture bodies and passing approved items to the Customs Authority, with data moving between them electronically](<https://atheernews.net/222149/>). [The National Investment Authority's Secretary-General said the electronic single window will operate once all entities related to investment work are linked electronically, with no date given](<https://sudanhorizon.com/the-national-investment-authority-in-the-process-of-electronic-single-window/>). [The Ministry of Digital Transformation and Telecommunications is said to offer the UNIVERSAL SECURE (CONSOLEX) unified API gateway, which integrates government systems and enables secure data exchange among institutions](<https://www.sudanembassyberlin.com/index.php/de/news-tt/706-ministry-of-digital-transformation-launches-phase-two-of-the-3-3-digital-transformation-plan>).
