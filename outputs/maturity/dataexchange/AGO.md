# Angola

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Presidential Despacho 344/26 of 27 August 2026 authorised USD 13 million for the Plataforma de Interoperabilidade da Administracao Publica](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>). [The order describes it as a secure data exchange channel and API layer between state and private systems, and calls a public tender](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>). [IMA started the USD 150 million Public Digital Infrastructure Project with TIS Angola on 31 July 2026](<https://lidermagazine.ao/revolucao-digital-em-marcha-ima-inicia-projecto-que-vai-mudar-a-administracao-publica/>), to strengthen the interoperability of public systems. Valor Económico [reported the same USD 13 million authorisation](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>).
