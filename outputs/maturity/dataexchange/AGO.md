# Angola

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Presidential Despacho 344/26 of 27 August 2026 authorised USD 13 million to acquire and implement the Plataforma de Interoperabilidade da Administracao Publica, a secure data exchange channel and API layer between state and private-sector systems, and ordered a public tender for its implementation](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>). [IMA formally started the USD 150 million, 12-month Public Digital Infrastructure Project with the implementation contract with TIS Angola on 31 July 2026, aiming to strengthen interoperability of public systems, digital identity and e-service security](<https://lidermagazine.ao/revolucao-digital-em-marcha-ima-inicia-projecto-que-vai-mudar-a-administracao-publica/>). Valor Económico [reported the same authorisation of USD 13 million for a secure channel to exchange data between public and private systems](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/pr-autoriza-mais-70-milhoes-de-dolares-para-a-transformacao-digital-do-pais>).

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-autoriza-13-milhoes-de-dolares-para-plataforma-de-interoperabilidade-da-administracao-publica/>).
