# Sierra Leone

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The NCRA Director-General said the Ministry of Finance is piloting a national Data Exchange Layer based on Estonia's X-Road](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [The Ministry of Finance's 20 May 2026 expression-of-interest notice requires the rollout firm to ensure data exchange between MOPTAX and CGMRS and integration with the Government Service Bus, and refers to emerging national interoperability standards including the GSB](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>). [The terms of reference say there is no government service bus with agreed protocols within the PFM ecosystem and the systems operate standalone](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).
