# Sierra Leone

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In August 2026 the NCRA head said the Ministry of Finance is piloting a national Data Exchange Layer based on X-Road](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [A May 2026 tender asks the tax system's supplier to integrate with the Government Service Bus](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/REoI-for-Tax-System_RUSLP.pdf>). [The World Bank's 2025 terms of reference say the finance systems run standalone, with no service bus](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>). [A Japanese grant signed in April 2025 funds a Government Service Bus prototype on a platform such as X-Road](<https://wbgeprocure-rfxnow.worldbank.org/rfxnow/waffle/upload/advertisementAttachment/603116/file.html>).
