# South Africa

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [National Treasury launched the one-year MzansiXchange pilot on 9 October 2025 with 11 partner institutions](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/government-activities/national-treasury-launches-mzansixchange-pilot-9-oct-03>). [By January 2026 testing was under way between SASSA, NSFAS, DPSA and Home Affairs](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202601/operation-vulindlela-q3-report-2026.pdf>). [In October 2026 ITWeb said MzansiXchange was still largely a pilot](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/citizen-services-project-mymzansi-gets-r150m-boost/lLn14MmQNEYMJ6Aa>). [The roadmap sets a data exchange platform for grant eligibility by late 2026, rolled out to 20 more databases by late 2027](<https://www.mymzansi.gov.za/roadmap/initiative-2>). [SARS's single window shares inspection cases with the border agency's agriculture unit, in phased testing](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>).
