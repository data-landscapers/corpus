# South Africa

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Daily Maverick reports a Presidency Digital Services Unit official presenting a data exchange, described as X-Road, as the second DPI component being implemented, hosted by SITA, within a three-phase roll-out over 24 months](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-09-23-estonian-echoes-sas-digital-public-infrastructure-looks-like-it-was-made-in-estonia/>). [The MyMzansi roadmap page refers to a data exchange pilot and sets a milestone to implement a data exchange platform for grant eligibility testing by Q4 2026 and to roll it out to at least 20 more databases by Q4 2027](<https://www.mymzansi.gov.za/roadmap/initiative-2>). [National Treasury's media advisory announces the MzansiXchange pilot launch on 9 October 2025, with 11 named partner institutions](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/government-activities/national-treasury-launches-mzansixchange-pilot-9-oct-03>). [ITWeb reported in October 2026 that MzansiXchange was still largely a pilot, meant to check grant beneficiaries against the population register and SARS](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/citizen-services-project-mymzansi-gets-r150m-boost/lLn14MmQNEYMJ6Aa>). In September 2026 SARS said its National Single Window [lets it share inspection cases with the Border Management Authority's agriculture function, which returns the outcomes through a secure interface](<https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/no-completion-date-for-sas-single-window>), with selected plant importers in phased testing.

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected.
