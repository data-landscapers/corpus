# Niger

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [WURI-Niger's Component 3 (World Bank financing of about USD 81 million, in force 19 August 2021) funds an interoperability and secure data exchange framework between the base ID system and public and private service providers' systems](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>).
