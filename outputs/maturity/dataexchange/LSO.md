# Lesotho

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In February 2026 a review at the ICT ministry heard X-Road presented as a system to connect government ministries](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>). [The ministry and UNICEF held a workshop in Berea on 20 to 23 October 2026 to plan an X-Road pilot](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/the-government-of-lesotho-remains-committed-to-strengthening-secure-data-exchange-across-government-institutions-and-enhancing-service-delivery-for-basotho?lang=en>). [It assessed ministries' readiness to join the National Data Exchange](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/the-government-of-lesotho-remains-committed-to-strengthening-secure-data-exchange-across-government-institutions-and-enhancing-service-delivery-for-basotho?lang=en>). [The ministry and UNICEF also set out to draw up an implementation roadmap for the National Data Exchange](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/the-government-of-lesotho-remains-committed-to-strengthening-secure-data-exchange-across-government-institutions-and-enhancing-service-delivery-for-basotho?lang=en>).
