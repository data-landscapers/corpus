# Lesotho

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [A progress review at MICSTI and the National University of Lesotho received a presentation of X-Road as a system to connect government ministries and enable electronic information sharing, among systems that 'will modernize' services](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>). [The ICT ministry and UNICEF held a workshop in Berea on 20 to 23 October 2026 to plan an X-Road pilot, assess ministries' readiness to join the National Data Exchange and set a roadmap](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/the-government-of-lesotho-remains-committed-to-strengthening-secure-data-exchange-across-government-institutions-and-enhancing-service-delivery-for-basotho?lang=en>).
