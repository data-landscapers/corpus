# Lesotho

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [A progress review at MICSTI and the National University of Lesotho received a presentation of X-Road as a system to connect government ministries and enable electronic information sharing, among systems that 'will modernize' services](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/micsti-and-nul-advanced-lesotho-digital-public-infrastructure/>).
