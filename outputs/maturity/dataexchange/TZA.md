# Tanzania

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Officials cite the Government Enterprise Service Bus (GovESB) as the backbone for secure data exchange across ministries, and the e-Government Authority's Director General urges institutions to ensure systems are connected through it](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>). [The Prime Minister ordered all public institutions to join GovESB, built by e-GA so government systems can read each other and exchange information, by 30 July 2025, saying joining is free](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/majaliwa-aagiza-taasisi-za-umma-kujiunga-na-e-ga-atoa-wiki-tano-5092568>). [On 17 October 2024 the Prime Minister said the government was continuing with the Jamii X-Change system so that government systems read each other, and the ICT minister said his ministry was still coordinating its construction](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/hakikisheni-mnapata-namba-ya-nida-jamii-namba-waziri-mkuu-majaliwa>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>).
