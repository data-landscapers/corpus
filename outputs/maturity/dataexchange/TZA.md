# Tanzania

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The e-Government Authority's 2024 roadmap says public institutions use GovESB to exchange information](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>). [In June 2025 the prime minister ordered every public institution to join it by 30 July 2025](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/majaliwa-aagiza-taasisi-za-umma-kujiunga-na-e-ga-atoa-wiki-tano-5092568>). [In January 2026 officials called GovESB the backbone for secure data exchange across ministries](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>). [In October 2024 the government was still building Jamii X-Change so that its systems could read each other](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/hakikisheni-mnapata-namba-ya-nida-jamii-namba-waziri-mkuu-majaliwa>). [The 2019 e-Government Act requires all government systems to exchange data through GovESB](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>).
