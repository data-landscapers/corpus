# Tanzania

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The e-Government Authority's 2024 roadmap says public institutions use GovESB to exchange information](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>). [In June 2025 the prime minister ordered every public institution to join it by 30 July 2025](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/majaliwa-aagiza-taasisi-za-umma-kujiunga-na-e-ga-atoa-wiki-tano-5092568>). [In January 2026 officials called GovESB the backbone for secure data exchange across ministries](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/tanzania-s-govtech-success>). [NIDA said in 2025 that Jamii X-Change, its X-Road exchange platform, is operational](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-4_TANZANIA-EN-final.pdf>). [The 2019 e-Government Act requires all government systems to exchange data through GovESB](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** [In December 2023 the e-Government Authority said 109 public institutions were connected to GovESB, with 117 systems registered](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>). [They include the identity authority, the judiciary and the police](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the e-Government Authority's count of connected institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/pressreleases/sw-1703229775-MIFUMO%20YA%20TEHAMA%20YA%20TAASISI%20ZA%20UMMA%20KUBADILISHANA%20TAARIFA%20KIDIJITALI%20.pdf>).
