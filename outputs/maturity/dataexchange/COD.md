# DR Congo

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [DR Congo's finance minister said on 3 September 2026 that a data warehouse interconnecting the three revenue agencies is in place, with two of the three having loaded their data and the third due to join within a month](<https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux>). [He said the E-Finance programme, structured with Huawei, mobilises over US$150 million for interconnecting the revenue agencies, for data centres and for management applications](<https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux>).
