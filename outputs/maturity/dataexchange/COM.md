# Comoros

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ANADEN's procurement notice of 12 March 2026 lists an interoperability and data-exchange platform under project COM-1026](<https://www.isdb.org/project-procurement/fr/appels-doffres/2026/gpn/projet-de-modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-comorienne-par-le-bais>). [An Islamic Development Bank loan of 5 million Islamic Dinars finances it, and a consultant is still to prepare its tender documents](<https://www.isdb.org/project-procurement/fr/appels-doffres/2026/gpn/projet-de-modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-comorienne-par-le-bais>). [The EU-funded e-Administration project, launched in October 2025, covers interoperability between ministries for secure data sharing](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/modernisation-de-l%E2%80%99administration-publique-i-lancement-officiel-du-projet-e-administration.html>). [In October 2024 the AfDB approved EUR 9.51 million for a project that also funds such a platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/comoros-to-implement-digital-govt-program-with-10m-afdb-grant>).
