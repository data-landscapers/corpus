## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ANADEN's general procurement notice of 12 March 2026 lists the setting up and operation of an interoperability and data-exchange platform under Component B of project COM-1026, financed entirely by an Islamic Development Bank loan of 5,000,000 Islamic Dinars, with its tender documents still to be prepared by a design and supervision consultant](<https://www.isdb.org/project-procurement/fr/appels-doffres/2026/gpn/projet-de-modernisation-de-ladministration-publique-comorienne-par-le-bais>). [The government launched the EU-funded, Expertise France-implemented e-Administration project, whose stated scope includes interoperability between ministries and institutions for secure data sharing; no platform is described as built or in service](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/modernisation-de-l%E2%80%99administration-publique-i-lancement-officiel-du-projet-e-administration.html>).
