# Togo

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Togo's presentation says the RSIA federated inter-administration exchange network was in a pilot phase launched in Q4 2024, with the pilot to finish in Q2 2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most fifteen stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.
