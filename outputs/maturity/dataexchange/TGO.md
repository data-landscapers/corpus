# Togo

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Togo's 2025 presentation says the RSIA inter-administration exchange network entered a pilot in late 2024, due to end in mid-2025](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>). [The identification agency's May 2026 deck sets out an API and data-sharing platform on RSIA, built on X-Road](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [It is to link the agency with ministries, banks and hospitals, with consent, audit logs and sign-in by national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). [The deck was presented at the ID4Africa meeting in May 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T1-P2-S1-3_TOGO-final.pdf>).
