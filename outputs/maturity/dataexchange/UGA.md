# Uganda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [A NITA-U IT professional said on 2026-10-01 that UGHub, a national data integration platform managed by NITA-U, connects NIRA's identity registry to MDAs and private institutions, which verify identities against it in real time](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [NITA-U stated at the 16 June 2026 launch of its strategic plan that UGHub currently connects 37 percent of MDAs, targeted to reach 73 percent by FY 2029/30](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/nita-u-launches-strategic-plan-fy202526-fy202930-boost-reach-digital-government>). [NITA-U's executive director said at the strategic plan launch that the UGHub data integration platform currently connects 37 percent of MDAs, with a target of 73 percent by FY 2029/30; the document gives no count of entities](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/22/nita-u-launches-five-year-digital-strategy-to-increase-e-government-use-in-uganda/>).

**Many systems connected.** [UGHub is fully operational and serves over 150 entities, with others onboarding](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>). [DIAL says UGHub is used by over 100 government ministries, departments and agencies as well as private entities such as banks, insurers and fintech firms](<https://dial.global/research/integrated-national-data-exchange-uganda-case-study>). [The same NITA-U speaker said UGHub connects NIRA's registry with more than 150 government ministries, departments and agencies, plus banks and telecoms](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). In July 2026 NITA-U reported [168 organisations, 70 public institutions and 98 private entities, exchanging information through the government interoperability platform](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>).
