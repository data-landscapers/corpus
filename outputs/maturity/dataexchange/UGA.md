# Uganda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [On 1 October 2026 a NITA-U specialist said UGHub connects NIRA's identity registry to agencies and private bodies, which verify identities in real time](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-to-register-learners-correct-identity-er-NV_241639_102026>). [NITA-U said in June 2026 that UGHub connects 37 percent of government agencies](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/nita-u-launches-strategic-plan-fy202526-fy202930-boost-reach-digital-government>). [It aims for 73 percent by 2029/30](<https://www.nita.go.ug/news-updates/nita-u-launches-strategic-plan-fy202526-fy202930-boost-reach-digital-government>). [NITA-U's page says UGHub is fully operational, connecting government systems for real-time data sharing](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>).

**Many systems connected.** [In July 2026 NITA-U reported 168 organisations exchanging information through the platform, 70 public and 98 private](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/familiarisation-visit-highlights-nita-us-digital-progress>). [Its UGHub page says the platform serves over 150 entities](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>). [DIAL says over 100 government agencies use it, with banks, insurers and fintech firms](<https://dial.global/research/integrated-national-data-exchange-uganda-case-study>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nita.go.ug/services/e-government-services/integration-service-ughub>).
