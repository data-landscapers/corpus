# Somalia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The Ministry of Finance's PFM Reform Strategy 2026-2030 lists developing a central data exchange platform as a priority measure, with piloting and rollout phased after working-group and data-standards stages, and says lack of interoperability between PFM IT systems limits consolidation of financial information](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>). [GovStack states that an interoperability platform has not yet been developed in Somalia and all agencies have their own systems](<https://govstack.global/showcase/digital-leaders-spotlight-rwanda/>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.
