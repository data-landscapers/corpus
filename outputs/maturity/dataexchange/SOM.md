# Somalia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The finance ministry's PFM Reform Strategy 2026-2030 makes a central data exchange platform a priority](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>). [Piloting and rollout are to follow working groups and data standards](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>). [The strategy says finance systems do not interoperate, which limits consolidated financial information](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>). [GovStack said in 2024 that no interoperability platform had been developed and every agency runs its own systems](<https://govstack.global/showcase/digital-leaders-spotlight-rwanda/>). These are the only facts found on the subject. No news story or official notice read for this study says more about it.

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mof.gov.so/sites/default/files/Publications/Somalia%20Public%20Financial%20Management%20Reform%20Strategy%20-%20March%2031.pdf>).
