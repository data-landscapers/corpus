# Morocco

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The Agence de développement du digital says Jisr carries secure data exchange between public administrations and institutions](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>). [The unified social registry, the Tayssir programme and the Masar school system use it](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>). [In October 2025 the minister described the national interoperability platform as already in place](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/connectivite-5g-et-e-gouvernement-amal-el-fallah-seghrouchni-fait-le-bilan-de-la-transformation-numerique.html>). [It was launched in 2022, built by Minsait](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform>). [We Are Tech Africa reported that the social registry and the Massar school system were among its first users](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform>).

**Many systems connected.** [In April 2025 the Agence de développement du digital said more than 76 bodies were connected to the national interoperability platform](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>). [They exchange data for more than 12 uses, among them the social registry, health insurance and the civil status register](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>). [The agency says Jisr runs 46 APIs giving access to 350 types of beneficiary information](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>).

**Use reported.** [The agency says the platform processed more than 95 million transactions in 2024, touching 18 million citizens](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>). [It says Jisr recorded more than 55 million data exchange operations from February 2023 to 31 January 2024](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>).
