# Morocco

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The Agence de développement du digital says the Jisr platform carries secure data exchange between public administrations and institutions, used in the unified social registry, the Tayssir programme and the Masar school system](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>). [We Are Tech Africa reports that the Agency for Digital Development launched an e-government interoperability platform, built by Minsait, for data and document exchange between public administrations, and that the Single Social Register and the MASSAR school management system and some social programmes are operational on it (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/morocco-launches-e-government-interoperability-platform>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [The ADD says over 55 million data exchange operations were recorded on Jisr from February 2023 to 31 January 2024, through 46 APIs giving access to 350 types of beneficiary information](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plateforme-jisr-55-millions-doperations-dechange-de-donnees-enregistrees-en-une-annee/>).
