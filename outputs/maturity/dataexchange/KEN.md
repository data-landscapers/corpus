# Kenya

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ICT Secretary Mary Kerema told WSO2Con Africa that a new interoperability platform to let government entities share data is still in development, alongside the launched Government Enterprise Architecture (Techweez report)](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/23/kenya-digital-transformation-backend-interoperability/>). [Kenya's ICT PS John Tanui said at WSO2Con Africa in Nairobi on 23 September 2026 that the government is building an Enterprise Platform to orchestrate data and services across government, linking institutions' systems through secure APIs](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/23/state-plans-unified-platform-to-link-government-systems/>).
