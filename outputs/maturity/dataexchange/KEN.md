## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [On 23 September 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary said a new interoperability platform for government entities to share data is still in development](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/23/kenya-digital-transformation-backend-interoperability/>). [The ICT principal secretary said the same day that the government is building an Enterprise Platform](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/23/state-plans-unified-platform-to-link-government-systems/>). [It is to link institutions' systems through secure APIs](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/23/state-plans-unified-platform-to-link-government-systems/>). [From April 2024 to June 2025 a donor-funded pilot built an X-Road test environment with two demo services](<https://cyber.ee/resources/case-studies/cybernetica-piloted-an-interoperability-platform-to-improve-and-optimise-digital-public-services-in-kenya/>).
