# Liberia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The World Bank's GREAT Project report lists a new Service Bus for data exchange among government systems, including the National Identification Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110625073415416/pdf/P177478-9397c8d1-edb6-4a96-a39d-0222e717f2c4.pdf>). [No system used it at 30 September 2025, against a target of ten by December 2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110625073415416/pdf/P177478-9397c8d1-edb6-4a96-a39d-0222e717f2c4.pdf>). [The project's 2026 procurement plan lists a US$50,000 consultancy to design and configure a Government Service Bus prototype](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021326063510836/txt/P177478-d6a96244-44d6-44c4-855d-bbbd85a9394e.txt>). [The plan marks the firm package for that prototype as cancelled](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021326063510836/txt/P177478-d6a96244-44d6-44c4-855d-bbbd85a9394e.txt>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021326063510836/txt/P177478-d6a96244-44d6-44c4-855d-bbbd85a9394e.txt>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021326063510836/txt/P177478-d6a96244-44d6-44c4-855d-bbbd85a9394e.txt>).
