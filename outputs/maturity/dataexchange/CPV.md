# Cape Verde

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Cabo Verde launched the Autentika interoperability platform, linking its civil registration and national identity systems](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). [NOSi's PDEX lets data owners publish their data as APIs](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>). [It carries live integrations for the civil identification system, NhaCard's traffic with the national health system and the Treasury's DUC system](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>). [Public and private bodies already consume its citizen-validation APIs](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>). [Autentika is one of the reforms behind near-universal birth registration, Biometric Update reported in May 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).
