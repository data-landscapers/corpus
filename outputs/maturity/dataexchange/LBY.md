# Libya

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In April 2026 the General Information Authority described the National Data Distributor as a sovereign platform for secure data exchange between entities](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/enhancing-digital-transformation-in-the-business-sector-joint-meeting-to-launch-the-electronic-system-of-the-chambers-union-and-align-it-with-national-frameworks/>). [The Union of Chambers' new electronic system is to connect to it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/enhancing-digital-transformation-in-the-business-sector-joint-meeting-to-launch-the-electronic-system-of-the-chambers-union-and-align-it-with-national-frameworks/>). On 1 June 2026 the communications authority's chairman said [the Distributor links the banking sector, government agencies and service sectors](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358533&lang=en>). [In July 2026 the information authority named LY-NDEX, the national data exchange, among its projects](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/07/09/the-general-information-authority-participates-in-a-national-workshop-to-enhance-information-exchange-in-combating-money-laundering-and-terrorism-financing/>). [In January 2025 a hackathon prototype service bus linked four institutions through 23 web services](<https://cjos.histr.edu.ly/index.php/journal/en/article/view/2167>).

**Many systems connected.** The nearest held fact is [the chairman's statement that the Distributor links banks, government agencies and service sectors](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358533&lang=en>). No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected.

**Use reported.** The chairman said [the National Data Distributor recorded over 300 million data transactions in 2025](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358533&lang=en>).
