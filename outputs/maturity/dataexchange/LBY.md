# Libya

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In April 2026 the General Information Authority described the National Data Distributor as a sovereign platform for secure data exchange between entities](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/enhancing-digital-transformation-in-the-business-sector-joint-meeting-to-launch-the-electronic-system-of-the-chambers-union-and-align-it-with-national-frameworks/>). [The Union of Chambers' new electronic system is to connect to it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/enhancing-digital-transformation-in-the-business-sector-joint-meeting-to-launch-the-electronic-system-of-the-chambers-union-and-align-it-with-national-frameworks/>). [In July 2026 the authority named the Libyan National Data Exchange, LY-NDEX, among its national projects for exchange between government bodies](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/07/09/the-general-information-authority-participates-in-a-national-workshop-to-enhance-information-exchange-in-combating-money-laundering-and-terrorism-financing/>). [In January 2025 a hackathon prototype service bus linked four institutions through 23 web services](<https://cjos.histr.edu.ly/index.php/journal/en/article/view/2167>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected.
