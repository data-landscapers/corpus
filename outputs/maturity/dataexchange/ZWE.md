# Zimbabwe

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In May 2026 Zimbabwe's ICT minister said in an official post that the government is implementing a Government Service Bus and an online portal](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hon-tatenda-mavetera-7821b96b_digitalizezimbabwe-vision2030-zimbabweisopenfordigitalbusiness-activity-7458359047386234880-OgCR>). [They are for sharing data once across all ministries](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hon-tatenda-mavetera-7821b96b_digitalizezimbabwe-vision2030-zimbabweisopenfordigitalbusiness-activity-7458359047386234880-OgCR>). [The 2027 budget statement allocates ZiG5 million to a Zimbabwe Government Data Exchange](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2026/11/ict-ministry-gets-zig1-billion-in-2027-budget/>), for ministries and agencies to share data.
