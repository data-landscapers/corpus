# Madagascar

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [L'Express reports that the Rahaka national exchange system is already operational at five bodies, EDBM, CNaPS, Instat, the Ministry of Justice and the Direction generale des Impots, at the 28 April 2026 launch](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>). [INSTAT reports the official launch on 28 April 2026 of national interoperability, a shared IT system for ministries and public bodies to exchange data, with the first RAHAKA usage convention signed by UGD, several ministries, private actors and INSTAT; it gives no count of connected systems](<https://www.instat.mg/p/lancement-officiel-de-linteroperabilite-nationale>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>).
