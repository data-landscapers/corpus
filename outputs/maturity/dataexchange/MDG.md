# Madagascar

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [L'Express reports that Rahaka was already operational at five bodies at its launch on 28 April 2026](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>). [They are EDBM, CNaPS, Instat, the Ministry of Justice and the tax directorate](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>). [INSTAT says the launch opened a shared system for ministries and public bodies to exchange data](<https://www.instat.mg/p/lancement-officiel-de-linteroperabilite-nationale>). [The UGD, several ministries, private firms and INSTAT signed the first convention on its use](<https://www.instat.mg/p/lancement-officiel-de-linteroperabilite-nationale>). [A World Bank-backed project funds Rahaka, built on X-Road](<https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>).
