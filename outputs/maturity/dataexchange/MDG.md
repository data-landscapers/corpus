# Madagascar

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [L'Express reports that Rahaka was already operational at five bodies at its launch on 28 April 2026](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>). [They are EDBM, CNaPS, Instat, the Ministry of Justice and the tax directorate](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>). [INSTAT says the launch opened a shared system for ministries and public bodies to exchange data](<https://www.instat.mg/p/lancement-officiel-de-linteroperabilite-nationale>). [The national exchange system, run by the digital governance unit, carries services such as Orinasa, Hetra Online and the trade register](<https://2424.mg/modernisation-de-letat-14-institutions-deja-interconnectees-via-le-systeme-dechange-national-et-extension-vers-des-secteurs-vitaux-en-perspective/>). [A World Bank-backed project funds Rahaka, built on X-Road](<https://newsmada.com/2026/04/29/systeme-rahaka-vers-linteroperabilite-nationale-des-donnees/>).

**Many systems connected.** In April 2026 [14 institutions were connected to the national exchange system](<https://2424.mg/modernisation-de-letat-14-institutions-deja-interconnectees-via-le-systeme-dechange-national-et-extension-vers-des-secteurs-vitaux-en-perspective/>), 2424.mg reports. [L'Express counted five bodies operational at the launch](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/04/administration-mise-en-place-dun.html>).

**Use reported.** The administrative reform programme reports [more than 162,000 exchanges recorded through the system](<https://2424.mg/modernisation-de-letat-14-institutions-deja-interconnectees-via-le-systeme-dechange-national-et-extension-vers-des-secteurs-vitaux-en-perspective/>), as at April 2026.

***Also on file***. [CNECI civil registration and identification databases](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/legal-identity-all-what-can-madagascar-learn-namibia>).

***Not found***. Sources differ on the count at launch: five bodies in one report, 14 institutions in another.
