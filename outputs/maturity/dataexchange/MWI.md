# Malawi

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [ITWeb Africa reports that Malawi's Ministry of ICT proposed a Government Data Sharing Regulation for the National Data Exchange Platform (Boma Lathu), which it describes as currently under implementation](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>). [An ICT expert (former ICTAM president) said in January 2026 that the Department of e-Government would be under pressure in 2026 to onboard all major ministries, departments and agencies onto the Enterprise Service Bus for interoperability of government systems, with no count of connected bodies](<https://times.mw/ict-sector-eyes-policy-enforcement/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>).
