# Malawi

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In June 2026 the ICT ministry proposed a data sharing regulation for Boma Lathu, the National Data Exchange Platform](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>). [ITWeb describes the platform as under implementation](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>). [In January 2026 an ICT expert said the e-Government department must onboard all major ministries onto the Enterprise Service Bus in 2026](<https://times.mw/ict-sector-eyes-policy-enforcement/>). [The World Bank says its Digital Foundations Project of 2017 to 2024 built the Bomalathu exchange platform](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2025/06/23/digitalizing-afe-malawi-to-improve-access-to-education-public-services-and-income-opportunities>). [In 2025 the e-Government department had not made the Enterprise Service Bus operational](<https://times.mw/the-year-ict-sector-grappled-with-implementation-woes/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-devises-operational-rules-for-national-data-exchange-platform/xnklOvz1R2bq4Ymz>).
