# Tunisia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Cybernetica states phase 1 introduced UXP to five key ministries and institutions](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>). [Le Quotidien reports the first phase of the national interoperability platform, UXP, was completed in 2025 in five ministries, with first applications deployed in 2024](<https://www.lequotidien.com.tn/fr/nationale/10496-digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-tunisie-debut-du-deploiement-a-grande-echelle-de-l-interoperabilite>). [Cybernetica states a phase 2 contract was signed with the Ministries of Communication Technologies and of Economy and Planification, extending the platform to RNE, TIA, APII, CEPEX, the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank, scheduled to complete in 2027](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>). [Le Quotidien reports a contract signed with Cybernetica for phase two, running to end-2027, to integrate the platform across all ministries](<https://www.lequotidien.com.tn/fr/nationale/10496-digitalisation-des-services-publics-en-tunisie-debut-du-deploiement-a-grande-echelle-de-l-interoperabilite>).

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>).
