# Tunisia

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Cybernetica states a phase 2 contract was signed with the Ministries of Communication Technologies and of Economy and Planification, extending the platform to RNE, TIA, APII, CEPEX, the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank, scheduled to complete in 2027](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>). [The playbook names the Plateforme Nationale d'Interoperabilite, operated by the Centre National Informatique, carrying exchanges of citizen register data with state structures including civil status, schools, universities, vocational training and health, under decree-law 2020-31 and decree 2020-777](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [Cybernetica states phase 1 introduced UXP to five key ministries and institutions](<https://cyber.ee/resources/news/phase-2-tunisia-interoperability/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected.
