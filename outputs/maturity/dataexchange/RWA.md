# Rwanda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In October 2026 RISA said the Shared Government Data Hub and a Data Interoperability Platform are being worked on, the hub moving into implementation](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>). [RISA set out a nine-month build for both in its draft tender of November 2025](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=151133&t=f&token=ea2f00488a3d4ebb4cde95d0c365c403869d9fb7>). [It calls the existing Data Sharing Platform an interim system for data governance, discovery and access](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>). [NISR lists building the data sharing platform among its current foundation work](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found counts ten or more bodies connected to a government exchange platform. [DIAL reports that Irembo works with systems and registries of over 35 government partners through an API](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>).
