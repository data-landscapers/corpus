# Rwanda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [Cenfri, interviewing NISR's Data Governance Project Lead, lists building the data sharing platform itself among the Data Governance Unit's current foundation work, and says institutions should publish quality-assured datasets through it over the next three to five years](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>). [RISA held an early market engagement session on its draft RFP for the Shared Government Data Hub and Data Interoperability Platform, stating a 9-month implementation period, 12 months of post-deployment support, and a final RFP to be published before the end of 2025](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=151133&t=f&token=ea2f00488a3d4ebb4cde95d0c365c403869d9fb7>).

**Many systems connected.** No source held since October 2023 counts ten or more institutions or systems connected. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>).
