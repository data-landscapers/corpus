# Rwanda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [The New Times reports that, according to RISA, the Shared Government Data Hub and a Data Interoperability Platform are being worked on, with the Data Hub moving into implementation](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>). [The New Times reports that, according to RISA, the existing Data Sharing Platform serves as an interim system for data governance, data discovery and access](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>). [Cenfri, interviewing NISR's Data Governance Project Lead, lists building the data sharing platform itself among the Data Governance Unit's current foundation work, and says institutions should publish quality-assured datasets through it over the next three to five years](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>).

**Many systems connected.** [DIAL reports Irembo works with key systems and registries from over 35 partners across the government through an API](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the platform. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>).
