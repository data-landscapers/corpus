# Rwanda

## National data exchange system

**Exchange platform built and in service.** [In March 2025 the social registry shared household data with other institutions through the government enterprise service bus](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Talking-Interoperability-18-Rwanda_vFinal_Updated.pdf>). [The government told the World Bank its Government Enterprise Service Bus is in use](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037889/govtech-dataset>). [In October 2026 RISA said a Shared Government Data Hub and a Data Interoperability Platform are being worked on. The hub is moving into implementation](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/39395/news/health/africa-needs-to-take-charge-of-its-digital-health-systems-says-minister>). [Its draft tender of November 2025 sets out a nine-month build](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=151133&t=f&token=ea2f00488a3d4ebb4cde95d0c365c403869d9fb7>).

**Many systems connected.** No source found counts ten or more bodies connected to the bus. [In March 2025 the social registry exchanged data with seven systems, among them NIDA, CRVS and RRA](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Talking-Interoperability-18-Rwanda_vFinal_Updated.pdf>). [DIAL reports that Irembo, a citizen services portal, works with systems and registries of over 35 government partners through an API](<https://dial.global/modern-governments-run-on-data-exemplars/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for the exchanges through the bus. The nearest fact: [the social registry uses Kafka streaming for real-time updates alongside it](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Talking-Interoperability-18-Rwanda_vFinal_Updated.pdf>).
