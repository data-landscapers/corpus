# Ethiopia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ethiopia's rule covers personal data and banking data. [Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 entered into force on 24 July 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). [It requires data controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). [In October 2025 Birr Metrics reported that it lets data leave the country only under strict conditions](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>). A law firm says [National Bank of Ethiopia Directive No. SBB/94/2025 requires all banks to store and process customer and core banking data in Ethiopia](<https://haymanotbelay.com/banking-on-data-regulatory-expectations-for-data-storage-protection-and-localization-in-ethiopias-financial-sector/>). It covers foreign banks' subsidiaries and branches, and backups too.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** [The Ethiopian Communications Authority's consumer rights directive of August 2021 requires telecom providers to process consumers' personal data only in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). [The authority issued it for the operators it licenses](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). [It also oversees compliance with the data protection proclamation](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). [The National Bank issued its directive for the banks it licenses, and must approve in writing any transfer of core banking data abroad](<https://haymanotbelay.com/banking-on-data-regulatory-expectations-for-data-storage-protection-and-localization-in-ethiopias-financial-sector/>). No order, penalty or exemption under any of these rules has been found.

***Also on file***. [Gotera Cloud (G-Cloud)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_digitalethiopia2030-insa-ethiopia-activity-7446822764042293248-2bIo>).

***Not found***. No organisation is known to have been ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule. No count of those that have moved data to comply is known.
