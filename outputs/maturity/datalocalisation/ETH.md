# Ethiopia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ethiopia's rule covers personal data. [Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 entered into force on 24 July 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). [It requires data controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). [In October 2025 Birr Metrics reported that it lets data leave the country only under strict conditions](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>). [A July 2025 market brief by Xalam Analytics says it requires companies to host Ethiopian citizens' data in the country](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** [The Ethiopian Communications Authority's consumer rights directive of August 2021 requires telecom providers to process consumers' personal data only in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). [The authority issued it for the operators it licenses](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). [It also oversees compliance with the data protection proclamation](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). No order, penalty or exemption under either rule has been found.

***Also on file***. [Gotera Cloud (G-Cloud)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_digitalethiopia2030-insa-ethiopia-activity-7446822764042293248-2bIo>).

***Not found***. No organisation is known to have been ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule. No count of those that have moved data to comply is known.
