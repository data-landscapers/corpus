# Ethiopia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ethiopia's rule covers personal data. Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, adopted by Parliament on 4 April 2024, [entered into force on 24 July 2024 and requires data controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). The record gives no transition period, and does not say on what conditions a copy of the data may also be held abroad.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same record, which [describes the storage duty on controllers and processors](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>) and names no body that checks it. No source held names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No source held names an organisation ordered to bring data into Ethiopia, penalised or exempted under the proclamation, and none counts those that have complied.

***Not held***. No source held names a body that enforces the rule: a regulator, agency or ministry unit that checks compliance or grants exemptions.
