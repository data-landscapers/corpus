# Ethiopia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ethiopia's rule covers personal data. Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, adopted by Parliament on 4 April 2024, [entered into force on 24 July 2024 and requires data controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). The record gives no transition period, and does not say on what conditions a copy of the data may also be held abroad. Birr Metrics reported in October 2025 that [the Proclamation lets data leave the country only under strict conditions](<https://birrmetrics.com/us-based-cisco-eyes-ethiopia-market-warns-cloud-rules-limit-access/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The Ethiopian Communications Authority issued the older rule for the operators it licenses: its consumer rights directive of August 2021 [requires telecom providers to process consumer personal data only on servers or data centres in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>), and the same authority [oversees compliance with the data protection proclamation](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ethiopia>). The record adds that no official sources on enforcement are published. No source found names an organisation ordered to bring data into Ethiopia, penalised or exempted under either rule.
