# Libya

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Libya's rule covers the financial sector. A write-up of the Central Bank of Libya's Circular No. 18, which it dates 1 June 2025, says the circular's data protection regulation [prohibits storing personal, financial, credit or sensitive data outside Libya](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>). It binds banks, branches of foreign banks, fintechs, payment providers, exchange companies and leasing companies, from 1 July 2026.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The central bank, which issued the regulation, [supervises the institutions it covers and sets a fine of 100,000 dinars for each violation](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>). It may revoke a licence for repeated gross violations, and each institution must appoint a Libyan data officer as its liaison with the bank. No fine, revocation or exemption under the regulation has been found.

***Also on file***. [Libyan Spider Tripoli data center](<https://libyanspider.com/libyan-spider-opens-new-data-center-in-tripoli-to-enhance-security-and-availability/>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years naming an organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule, or counting those that have moved data to comply, is held.
