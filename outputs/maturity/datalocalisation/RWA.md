# Rwanda

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Rwanda's rule is partial. A market brief of July 2025 says [there is no blanket localisation requirement in Rwanda, but some data must be stored locally depending on the circumstances](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>), under the 2021 Law on Data Privacy and Protection. It adds that public sector data must be hosted in the government data centre or in facilities that meet standards set by the Rwanda Development Board. The brief gives no date for either requirement.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The same brief says [local storage of some data depends on certification by the National Cyber Security Authority](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>), which makes the authority the body that decides where such data may be held. No certificate granted or refused has been found, and no source names an organisation ordered to bring data into Rwanda or penalised.

***Also on file***. [AWS Outposts](<https://www.montjalinews.org/2026/04/17/amazon-web-services-launches-outposts-in-rwanda/>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years naming an organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule, or counting those that have moved data to comply, is held.
