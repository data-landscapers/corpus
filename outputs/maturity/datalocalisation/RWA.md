# Rwanda

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Rwanda's rule is partial. A market brief of July 2025 says [there is no blanket localisation requirement in Rwanda, but some data must be stored locally depending on the circumstances](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>). It places that under the 2021 Law on Data Privacy and Protection. Public sector data must be hosted in the government data centre or in facilities meeting Rwanda Development Board standards. [Digital Policy Alert says the October 2021 law generally requires personal data to be stored in Rwanda](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-rwanda>), unless the supervisory authority grants a certificate.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The July 2025 brief says [local storage of some data depends on certification by the National Cyber Security Authority](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>). That makes the authority the body that decides where such data may be held. No certificate granted or refused has been found. The one penalty found is older: in May 2017 the Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority [fined MTN Rwanda FRW 7.03 billion for hosting its IT services outside the country](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-rwanda>). No order or penalty from the last three years has been found.

***Also on file***. [AWS Outposts](<https://www.montjalinews.org/2026/04/17/amazon-web-services-launches-outposts-in-rwanda/>).

***Not found***. No source from the last three years names an organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule. None counts those that have moved data to comply.
