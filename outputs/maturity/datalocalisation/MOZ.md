# Mozambique

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Mozambique's rule covers the financial sector. A report of June 2025 on the country's data governance says [Banco de Moçambique Notice 4/GGBM/2003 requires data centres to be located in Mozambique, with replicas abroad allowed only with prior approval](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>), and records no repeal. A second rule is in draft: the cloud services regulation that the regulator INTIC put to public consultation in February 2025 [says classified information must always stay in national territory unless the regulator expressly authorises otherwise](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The notice is [the central bank's own, issued for the institutions it licenses](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>), and its prior approval is needed for a replica abroad. The report names no inspection or penalty. No approval granted or refused under the notice has been found.
