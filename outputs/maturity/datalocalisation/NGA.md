# Nigeria

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Nigeria's rule covers payment data. [The Central Bank of Nigeria issued a circular on 15 June 2026 requiring banks, mobile money operators, switches and all licensed payment operators to store and manage payment transaction data generated in Nigeria within Nigeria by 1 January 2027](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>). At a forum in September 2026 [industry speakers said the six-month deadline was too short and asked for an extension](<https://techtrends.africa/growthx-by-techeconomy-industry-leaders-identify-infrastructure-trust-as-keys-to-data-localisation/>). A wider rule is in draft: the chairman of the National Sovereign Cloud Initiative's working group said [data on national security, financial services and health is to be stored in Nigeria by law](<https://businessamlive.com/data-localisation-opens-new-investment-window-for-nigerias-data-centres/>). [A May 2026 release from the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority says the National Cloud Policy 2025 requires sensitive government and financial data to be hosted in the country](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The circular came from [the central bank's Payments System Supervision Department, for the institutions it licenses](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>). Banks and fintechs [say they have been unable to get clarity from the central bank on hybrid cloud arrangements](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/banks-say-cbn-six-month-data-localisation-deadline-too-short-risks-disrupting-payments/>). The department says [the central bank will begin enforcement and review compliance from 1 January 2027](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/15/as-cbn-begins-data-migration-local-hosting/>), and the bank [says it will monitor compliance and may impose supervisory sanctions, though the circular sets no penalty schedule](<https://thetrumpet.ng/cbn-insists-on-january-1-2027-deadline-for-banks-fintechs-to-store-payment-data-in-nigeria/>). No order, penalty or exemption has been found.
