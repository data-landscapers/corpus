# Nigeria

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Nigeria's rule covers payment data. [On 15 June 2026 the Central Bank of Nigeria ordered payment transaction data generated in Nigeria to be stored and managed there by 1 January 2027](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>). [The circular binds banks, mobile money operators, switches and all licensed payment operators](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>). [In September 2026 industry speakers called the deadline too short and asked for more time](<https://techtrends.africa/growthx-by-techeconomy-industry-leaders-identify-infrastructure-trust-as-keys-to-data-localisation/>). [In October 2026 deputy governor Lamido Yuguda held banks to the 2027 date](<https://businessday.ng/news/article/cbn-warns-banks-on-cybersecurity-risks-as-cloud-dependence-grows/>). [The National Cloud Policy 2025 requires sensitive government and financial data to be hosted in Nigeria, a May 2026 NSIA release says](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>). [A wider rule is in draft, covering data on national security, financial services and health](<https://businessamlive.com/data-localisation-opens-new-investment-window-for-nigerias-data-centres/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The circular came from [the central bank's Payments System Supervision Department, for the institutions it licenses](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>). Banks and fintechs [say the central bank has not told them how hybrid cloud set-ups are treated](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/banks-say-cbn-six-month-data-localisation-deadline-too-short-risks-disrupting-payments/>). The department says [the central bank will begin enforcement and review compliance from 1 January 2027](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/10/15/as-cbn-begins-data-migration-local-hosting/>). The bank [says it will monitor compliance and may impose supervisory sanctions, though the circular sets no penalty schedule](<https://thetrumpet.ng/cbn-insists-on-january-1-2027-deadline-for-banks-fintechs-to-store-payment-data-in-nigeria/>). [In October 2026 a NITDA survey presented with the central bank found 22 of 44 financial institutions yet to begin](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/22-financial-institutions-yet-to-begin-data-localisation-nitda-survey/>). No order, penalty or exemption has been found.
