# Djibouti

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Decree 2020-146/PR/MI, issued after the Council of Ministers of 7 July 2020 and published on 13 July 2020, states in [Article 9](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-146-pr-mi-portant-modalites-dacces-dexploitation-et-de-communication-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>) that the processed data of the National Register of Natural Persons (RNPP) are stored only on national territory. The decree governs access to, exploitation of and communication of the register, and Article 9 applies to that one register. The text gives its publication date and no separate date of entry into force.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [decree](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2020-146-pr-mi-portant-modalites-dacces-dexploitation-et-de-communication-du-registre-national-des-personnes-physiques/>), whose title sets the terms for access to, use of and communication of the register's data, with Article 9 as its storage provision. The register is a relational database run by the Direction Generale de la Population et de la Famille (DGPF), with ANSIE responsible for its development and security. Its users are public authorities, public and private bodies under Djiboutian law, and their subcontractors.
