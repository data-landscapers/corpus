# Angola

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Angola's rule covers credit information bureaux. [Presidential Decree 275/20 of 21 October 2020 requires private credit bureaux to keep their primary data processing centres in national territory](<https://angolex.com/paginas/decreto-presidencial/regulamento-da-actividade-das-centrais-privadas-de-informacao-de-credito.html>). Their replica facilities for data recovery must also be inside the country, at least 30 km from the primary centre. The decree gives no later date for compliance and nothing found says it has been repealed.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The decree [makes the Agência de Protecção de Dados responsible for authorising, supervising, inspecting and fining private credit bureaux](<https://angolex.com/paginas/decreto-presidencial/regulamento-da-actividade-das-centrais-privadas-de-informacao-de-credito.html>). The agency must also approve any use of cloud storage for their data. Breaching the regulation, including adopting cloud storage without that approval, is a contravention that carries a fine. No order, fine or exemption under the rule has been found.
