# Gabon

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Gabon's rule sits in its cybersecurity statute. [Article 27 of Law No. 027/2023 on cybersecurity and cybercrime requires data judged confidential or sensitive to be hosted exclusively on national territory](<https://journal-officiel.ga/20087-027-2023-/>). The law was promulgated on 11 July 2023 and published in the official journal of 15 July 2023. Which data count as confidential or sensitive is set by the law's own article 22.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The law allows an exception: [the competent administrative authority may grant a written derogation](<https://journal-officiel.ga/20087-027-2023-/>). The text held does not say which authority that is. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No derogation granted or refused has been found, and no source names an organisation ordered to bring data into Gabon or penalised under the article.
