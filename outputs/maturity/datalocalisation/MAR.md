# Morocco

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Morocco's rule sits in its cybersecurity law. [Article 11 of Law No. 05-20, published on 6 August 2020, requires sensitive data to be hosted only on national territory](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>). [Article 49 fines the heads of vital entities that host such data abroad 200,000 to 400,000 dirhams](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>). [Article 53 makes the law operative once its implementing texts are published](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>). [A June 2025 report says Moroccan law mandates that sensitive data be stored locally](<https://en.hespress.com/112805-morocco-overtakes-south-africa-as-africas-top-data-center-hub.html>). [Global Finance wrote the same month that the rule had led to data being repatriated](<https://gfmag.com/emerging-frontier-markets/morocco-boom-in-data-centers/>). [A November 2026 opinion piece says the draft 2027 finance bill would store Moroccans' data in a sovereign national cloud](<https://ladsi.ma/article/tribune-souverainete-numerique-maroc-cout>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the law's penalty article, which [sets the fines](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>) and, in the text held, does not name the body that imposes them. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No fine or exemption under article 11 has been found.
