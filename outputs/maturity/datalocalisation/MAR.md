# Morocco

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Morocco's rule sits in its cybersecurity law. [Article 11 of Law No. 05-20, published in the official bulletin of 6 August 2020, requires sensitive data to be hosted exclusively on national territory](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>). Article 49 sets fines of 200,000 to 400,000 dirhams for the heads of vital entities or infrastructures that host such data abroad, and article 53 makes the law operative once its implementing texts are published. A report of June 2025 [says a 2021 law mandates that sensitive data be stored locally](<https://en.hespress.com/112805-morocco-overtakes-south-africa-as-africas-top-data-center-hub.html>), which puts the requirement in effect.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the law's penalty article, which [sets the fines](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>) and, in the text held, does not name the body that imposes them. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No fine or exemption under article 11 has been found.
