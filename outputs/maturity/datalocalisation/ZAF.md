# South Africa

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** South Africa's rule covers the state's security data. A presentation by the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies to Parliament's communications committee on 24 February 2026 says [the National Data and Cloud Policy provides that government data carrying content on national security and the sovereignty of the Republic shall be stored only in digital infrastructure located within South Africa's borders](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/260224_PCCDT_presentation_-_Data_Centres.pdf>). An earlier draft went further: a 2025 book chapter reports that [the draft commented on in 2021 required a copy of data to be kept within South African borders](<https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/digital-sovereignty-in-the-brics-countries/south-african-digital-sovereignty-at-the-crossroad-of-securitization-and-development/08C1119DE09286B84626FA49F6AD0586>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the department's presentation, which [states the storage rule](<http://www.ellipsis.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/260224_PCCDT_presentation_-_Data_Centres.pdf>) and names no body that checks it. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No order or exemption under the policy has been found.
