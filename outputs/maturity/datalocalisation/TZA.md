# Tanzania

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Tanzania's rules cover critical infrastructure and the financial sector. The Cybercrimes (Protection of Critical Information Infrastructure) Order, 2026, of 19 June 2026, [requires owners of critical information infrastructure to host all related data inside the country](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>). Hosting primary or secondary data abroad needs the Minister's approval. The Bank of Tanzania's Cloud Computing Guidelines of August 2025 [bar licensed financial providers from hosting mission-critical systems in a data centre or cloud outside Tanzania](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>). They are issued under the banking and payment systems Acts.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** Under the 2026 Order [the Minister approves offshore hosting, and the ICT ministry keeps the register and audits the infrastructure](<https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/400769/source_file/b94ac284c6b6b841/cybercrimes-protection-of-critical-information-infrastructure-order-2026.pdf>) at least once a year. The bank guidelines [make the Bank of Tanzania the body that approves providers' cloud arrangements](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>). It may impose sanctions, including civil money penalties. No approval, refusal or penalty under either rule is on record.
