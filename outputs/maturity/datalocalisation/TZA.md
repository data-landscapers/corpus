# Tanzania

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Tanzania's rule covers the financial sector. The Bank of Tanzania's Cloud Computing Guidelines for Financial Service Providers, dated August 2025, [bar a licensed financial service provider from hosting a mission-critical system, or one whose data the Bank deems critical, in a primary data centre or cloud whose infrastructure is outside Tanzania](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>). They are issued under the Banking and Financial Institutions Act 2006 and the National Payment Systems Act 2015.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The guidelines [make the Bank of Tanzania the body that evaluates and approves providers' cloud arrangements and contracts](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>), and say it may impose sanctions, including civil money penalties, for contravention. No approval, refusal or penalty under the guidelines has been found, and no source counts the providers that have moved systems into the country.
