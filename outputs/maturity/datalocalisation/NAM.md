# Namibia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** The Bank of Namibia's [Determination on Localization of Core Banking Systems (BID 19)](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2008/4109.pdf>), signed on 5 August 2008 and effective from 11 August 2008, requires all authorised banking institutions to locate and maintain their core banking systems in Namibia. The determination defines core banking systems as the systems where accounting and other banking records are kept. The requirement binds every banking institution the Bank authorises.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The [determination](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2008/4109.pdf>) was issued by the Bank of Namibia under section 71(3) of the Banking Institutions Act 1998 for the banking institutions it authorises. On non-compliance the Bank may pursue any remedial measure under that Act. The text also refers to dispensations already granted to individual institutions, so the Bank has exempted named institutions from the requirement.
