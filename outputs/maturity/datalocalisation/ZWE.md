# Zimbabwe

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Zimbabwe's rule covers highly sensitive systems. In its implementation guidelines for the Cyber and Data Protection Act, published in 2025, the regulator POTRAZ, as the designated Data Protection Authority, [states that it will not permit highly sensitive information such as core banking platforms and critical financial systems to be hosted outside Zimbabwe, and that such systems must be hosted locally](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>). The guidelines carry no date of their own.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The guidelines [name POTRAZ as the body whose prior written approval is required for any exception to local hosting](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>), and say a request must be backed by a risk assessment and a justification. No approval granted or refused has been found, and no source names an organisation ordered to bring a system into Zimbabwe or penalised.
