# Zimbabwe

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Zimbabwe's rule covers highly sensitive systems. The regulator POTRAZ, as the designated Data Protection Authority, published implementation guidelines for the Cyber and Data Protection Act in 2025. [It states that it will not permit highly sensitive information such as core banking platforms and critical financial systems to be hosted outside Zimbabwe](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>). [It says such systems must be hosted locally](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>). The guidelines carry no date of their own.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The guidelines [name POTRAZ as the body whose prior written approval is required for any exception to local hosting](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>). They say a request must be backed by a risk assessment and a justification. No approval granted or refused has been found. No source names an organisation ordered to bring a system into Zimbabwe or penalised.
