# Cameroon

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Cameroon's rule is reported at second hand. Speakers at the state operator Camtel's Cloud Days on 10 December 2025 [recalled that the country's personal data protection law imposes strict requirements on consent, on local storage and on the responsibilities of those who handle data](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>). The report gives neither the law's date nor the wording of the storage requirement, and does not say which data or which organisations it covers. The event presented Camtel's Zamengoé data centre to businesses and government agencies as a place to meet those requirements.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same report, which [names the storage requirement](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>) and no body that enforces it. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No order, penalty or exemption under it has been found.
