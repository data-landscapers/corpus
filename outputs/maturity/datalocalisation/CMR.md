# Cameroon

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Cameroon's rule is reported at second hand. [In June 2025 ST Digital's chief executive said the law obliges firms in sensitive sectors to host their data in the country](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/datacenters-certifies-ecosysteme-delaisse-le-paradoxe-de-la-gouvernance-numerique-au-cameroun/>). [At Camtel's Cloud Days on 10 December 2025, speakers said the personal data protection law requires local storage](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>). [They said it also sets strict rules on consent and on the duties of those who handle data](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>). [The event offered Camtel's Zamengoé data centre to businesses and government agencies as a place to meet those requirements](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** [In June 2025 ST Digital's chief executive said the local hosting duties are neither checked nor applied](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/datacenters-certifies-ecosysteme-delaisse-le-paradoxe-de-la-gouvernance-numerique-au-cameroun/>). [The December 2025 report names the storage duty and no body that enforces it](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>). No order, penalty or exemption under it has been found.
