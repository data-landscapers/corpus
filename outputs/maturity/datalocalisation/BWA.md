# Botswana

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Botswana's rule is a copy requirement. A report of November 2025 says the Data Protection Act, in effect since January 2025, [allows data to be transferred abroad only to approved countries and requires a copy of the data to remain in Botswana](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>). The same report says the Act was at first intended to go further and compel businesses to store data in the country, and that the minister still intends that.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same report, which [describes the copy requirement and the limits on which countries may host data](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>) without saying who checks them. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No order, penalty or exemption under the copy requirement has been found, and no count of organisations that keep a copy in the country.

***Also on file***. [Delta Digital Data Centre](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>); [Botswana adequacy list](<https://dial.global/africas-digital-future-seamless-data-flows/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a body that enforces the rule: a regulator, agency or ministry unit that checks compliance or grants exemptions.
