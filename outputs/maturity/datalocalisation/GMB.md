# Gambia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** The Gambia has a rule in draft. Version 2.0 of the Government Cloud Policy, dated 29 June 2023 and commissioned by the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy, [says government data classed as classified shall be stored and processed within The Gambia](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/b007fc10-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>), on a government private cloud or on the government's own premises. Data classed as sensitive may sit on a government private cloud or on a public cloud hosted in the country. The policy says there is no law on data residence. Its issue date and effective date are blank, pending signature.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The draft [gives the job of ensuring agencies comply with its data classification to the government's principal cloud provider, GICTA, with the Gambia Information Commission](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/b007fc10-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>), and sets up a working group to monitor compliance. None of that has effect until the policy is issued.
