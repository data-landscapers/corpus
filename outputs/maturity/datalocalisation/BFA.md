# Burkina Faso

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Burkina Faso's rule covers the state's own data. On 23 January 2026 the Prime Minister [ordered all public platforms and data held by private hosts or abroad brought back](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>) to two new government datacentres. [The government made the two datacentres the single base for hosting public platforms](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>), Digital Magazine Burkina reports. The order is part of the digital ministry's programme called Zero data hosted abroad. The two modular centres [hold about 3,000 terabytes and were opened the same day](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest fact is the same [order to bring public data home](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>), which names no body to check compliance. The magazine says [the move of data still held abroad or by private hosts is to be gradual](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>). No order, penalty or exemption under the rule has been found.
