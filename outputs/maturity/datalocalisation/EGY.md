# Egypt

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Egypt's rule covers classified data. The Cloud First Policy, adopted by the Supreme Council of Digital Society on 13 March 2024, [entered into force on 22 August 2024 and requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted exclusively within Egypt](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>). The record of the policy does not say where data of lower classifications may be held, and names no date by which existing systems must move.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same record, which [sets out the hosting requirement](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>) and names no body that checks compliance. No source found names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No source found names an organisation ordered to bring data into Egypt, penalised or exempted, and none counts public bodies that have moved classified data to hosting inside the country.

***Also on file***. [National data-centre strategy (in preparation)](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5722522.aspx>).

***Not found***. No source found names a body that enforces the rule: a regulator, agency or ministry unit that checks compliance or grants exemptions.
