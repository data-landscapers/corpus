# Malawi

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** The UN Economic Commission for Africa published its digital trade regulatory profile of Malawi in 2022, with data covering 2014 to 2021. The profile states that [Malawi has local storage requirements only for health-related data](<https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Malawi-%20ENG.pdf>). The requirement is one of local storage, it applies to health-related data and to no other category, and the profile names no instrument for it. The profile gives no date of its own, so 2022 is its publication date.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same [profile](<https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Malawi-%20ENG.pdf>), the UN Economic Commission for Africa's digital trade regulatory profile of Malawi. It describes the requirement as one on health-related data alone, within data covering 2014 to 2021, and says nothing further about how the requirement is overseen or applied. It names no body, penalty or exemption.

***Also on file***. [Sparc Systems data centre, Lilongwe](<https://malawi24.com/2026/05/01/sparc-moves-to-end-malawis-reliance-on-offshore-data/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found names a body that enforces the rule, checks compliance or grants exemptions.
