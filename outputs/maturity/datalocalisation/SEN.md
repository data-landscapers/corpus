# Senegal

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Senegal's rule covers the state's own data and rests on a presidential instruction. The Diamniadio National Datacenter opened in June 2021. An account of September 2025 says President Macky Sall then [told his government to migrate all state data and platforms to it](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>). He also told it to bring home national data hosted abroad. The same account says Senegal requires servers to be housed within its borders. It cites secondary sources and names no legal instrument. In December 2024 the health ministry [was carrying a draft digital health law and decree that cover hosting health data in Senegal](<https://www.enda-sante.org/letat-du-senegal-entre-transformation-numerique-et-renforcement-de-la-legislation/>).

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is that account. It [describes the instruction and the data centre](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>), and no body that checks compliance. No source found names a body that checks compliance or grants exemptions. No source from the last three years names or counts public bodies that moved their data to Diamniadio.
