# Cote d'Ivoire

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Côte d'Ivoire has a rule in preparation and none in force. The Ministry of Digital Transition's national data governance strategy to 2030 [sets as a principle that all data classified as critical information infrastructure must be processed and stored inside Côte d'Ivoire](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>). It adds that the staff handling it should be at least 20 per cent Ivorian, and that a copy of data must be kept in the country for law enforcement. The strategy carries no date of its own and was published in 2024. No law, decree or regulation enacting the principle has been found.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the strategy itself, which [states the storage principle](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>) and names no body to enforce it. No source found names a regulator or agency given that job, and none reports the principle applied to any organisation.
