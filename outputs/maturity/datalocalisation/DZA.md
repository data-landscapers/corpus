# Algeria

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Algeria's rule sits in its personal data law. The United States government's country commercial guide of 2024 [states that data localisation is mandatory in Algeria under Law No. 18-07](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>), with data to be stored in the country. The law came into force on 10 August 2023. The guide does not say whether the requirement reaches all data or only personal data. It gives no exemption procedure.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** In November 2017 the Regulatory Authority of Post and Electronic Communications [required public cloud computing operators to build their infrastructure in Algeria and host and store their data there](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>). It issued the rule for the operators it licenses, and cloud data hosting providers must hold its authorisation. Since December 2024 the Law on Audiovisual Activity has also [required online audiovisual services to be hosted on servers in Algeria](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>). No order, penalty or exemption under either rule has been found.

***Not found***. No source from the last three years names an organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule. None counts those that have moved data to comply.
