# Algeria

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Algeria's rule sits in its personal data law. The United States government's country commercial guide of 2024 [states that data localisation is mandatory in Algeria, with data to be stored in the country, under Law No. 18-07](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>), which came into force on 10 August 2023. The guide does not say whether the requirement reaches all data or only personal data, and it gives no exemption procedure.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The rule for cloud services was issued by a regulator for the operators it licenses: in November 2017 the Regulatory Authority of Post and Electronic Communications [required public cloud computing operators to build their infrastructure in Algeria and host and store their data there](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>), and providers of cloud data hosting must hold its authorisation. A second rule, in the Law on Audiovisual Activity in force since December 2024, [requires online audiovisual services to be hosted on servers in Algeria](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-algeria>). No order, penalty or exemption under either rule has been found.

***Not found***. No source from the last three years names an organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under the rule, or counts those that have moved data to comply.
