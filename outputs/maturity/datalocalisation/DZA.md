# Algeria

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Algeria's rule sits in its personal data law. The United States government's country commercial guide of 2024 [states that data localisation is mandatory in Algeria, with data to be stored in the country, under Law No. 18-07](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>), which came into force on 10 August 2023. The guide does not say whether the requirement reaches all data or only personal data, and it gives no exemption procedure.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the same guide, which [describes the requirement as mandatory](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/algeria-digital-economy>). Its text says the national data protection authority enforces the law in general and does not tie the authority to the storage requirement. No source held names a body that checks compliance with the rule or grants exemptions from it. No order, penalty or exemption under the requirement is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a body that enforces the rule: a regulator, agency or ministry unit that checks compliance or grants exemptions.
