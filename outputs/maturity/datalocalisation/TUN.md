# Tunisia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Tunisia's rule covers credit information. [Article 21 of decree-law 2022-2 of 4 January 2022 bars credit information companies from transferring their databases outside Tunisia](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-n-2022-2-du-4-janvier-2022-portant-organisation-de-lactivite-du-renseignement-de-credit/>), or from setting up sites outside the country to protect the data given to them. The same article bars them from hosting credit information in the cloud. No wider rule on keeping data in the country has been found.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** Under the decree-law [the Banque centrale de Tunisie licenses credit information companies, and its governor may fine one 10,000 to 50,000 dinars](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/decret-loi-n-2022-2-du-4-janvier-2022-portant-organisation-de-lactivite-du-renseignement-de-credit/>), suspend its activity for three months or withdraw its licence, after formal notice. No fine, suspension or withdrawal for keeping data abroad has been found, and no source names a company ordered to bring data into Tunisia.
