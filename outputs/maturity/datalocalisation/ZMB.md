# Zambia

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** A D4D Hub research paper states that [Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 requires sensitive personal data](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Data-Policies-and-Regulations/Examining-Sensitive-Personal-Data-Protection-and-Data-Sovereignty-in-Zambia.pdf>) (health records, biometrics, religious beliefs and political opinions) to be stored within the country. It quotes Section 70: sensitive personal data can only be stored in Zambia, and other personal data goes on servers in the Republic unless a transfer is approved. The Act is dated 2021.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The same [paper](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Data-Policies-and-Regulations/Examining-Sensitive-Personal-Data-Protection-and-Data-Sovereignty-in-Zambia.pdf>) says the Data Protection Commission, established in 2023 under the Act and reporting to the Minister of Technology and Science, enforces it. The paper quotes Section 70 as allowing personal data to leave Zambia only with the approval of both the data subject and the Data Protection Commissioner. The Commission is the body that holds that approval power.
