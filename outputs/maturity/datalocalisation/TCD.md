# Chad

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Chad's rule covers internet users' data held by one operator. A ministerial order of 27 August 2025 [requires Starlink to keep its users' connection logs, IP addresses and traffic data for at least 24 months](<https://ecomatin.net/telecoms-ndjamena-accentue-la-pression-sur-airtel-moov-et-starlink>). [The data must be hosted in Chad and open to the national authorities](<https://ecomatin.net/telecoms-ndjamena-accentue-la-pression-sur-airtel-moov-et-starlink>), EcoMatin reports. Breach can bring [fines, suspension or withdrawal of Starlink's licence](<https://ecomatin.net/telecoms-ndjamena-accentue-la-pression-sur-airtel-moov-et-starlink>). Starlink's service had been allowed in Chad two months earlier.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest fact is the [same order](<https://ecomatin.net/telecoms-ndjamena-accentue-la-pression-sur-airtel-moov-et-starlink>), issued by the telecommunications minister. It requires Starlink to [identify its customers with the regulator, Arcep](<https://ecomatin.net/telecoms-ndjamena-accentue-la-pression-sur-airtel-moov-et-starlink>). It names no body to check where the data are kept. No order, penalty or exemption under it has been found.
