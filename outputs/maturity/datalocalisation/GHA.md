# Ghana

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** [The Bank of Ghana's Cyber and Information Security Directive of 2026 requires sensitive financial information to be stored in Ghana](<https://cioafrica.co/africa-is-arguing-with-itself/>). It keeps core systems and critical data in the country and replaces a 2018 directive. In October 2025 the communications minister, Sam George, [said Ghana has a localisation policy](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). Under it, local data and traffic processed on clouds abroad will have to be hosted in Ghana. He named no instrument and no date by which data must move. In January 2024 NITA's Director General said [a draft National Data Centre and Cloud Computing Regulatory Framework was under review](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/stakeholders-push-for-unified-ict-policies-for-africas-digital-space.html>). It covers licensing, data localisation, security and competition. In May 2026 critics at IMANI Africa said [Section 31 of the NITA Bill, then before Parliament, appears to allow a rule](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-is-considering-passing-a-host-of-new-ict-related-laws-an-analyst-says-they-would-hamper-africa-digital-trade/>) that operational data be kept on government-managed local servers.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** [The Bank of Ghana, which issued the directive, enforces it](<https://cioafrica.co/africa-is-arguing-with-itself/>), CIO Africa reports. The minister [presented the new Accra data centre as the capacity his localisation policy needs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>), and named no body to enforce that policy. No source found reports any organisation ordered, penalised or exempted under either.
