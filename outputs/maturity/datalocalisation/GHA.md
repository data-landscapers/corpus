# Ghana

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ghana has a rule in preparation and none shown to be in force. In October 2025 the communications minister, Sam George, [said Ghana has a localisation policy](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). Under it, local data and traffic processed on clouds abroad will have to be hosted in Ghana. He named no instrument and no date by which data must move. In January 2024 NITA's Director General said [a draft National Data Centre and Cloud Computing Regulatory Framework was under review](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/stakeholders-push-for-unified-ict-policies-for-africas-digital-space.html>). It covers licensing, data localisation, security and competition. In May 2026 critics at IMANI Africa said [Section 31 of the NITA Bill, then before Parliament, appears to allow a rule](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-is-considering-passing-a-host-of-new-ict-related-laws-an-analyst-says-they-would-hamper-africa-digital-trade/>) that operational data be kept on government-managed local servers.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The minister [presented the new Accra data centre as the capacity the policy needs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). He named no body to enforce it. No source found names a regulator given that job, and none reports the policy applied to any organisation.

***Also on file***. [Digital Realty ACR2 data center, Accra](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>); [Huawei-built data centers in Ghana](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a rule that data must be kept in the country is in force.
