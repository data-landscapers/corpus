# Ghana

## Data localisation rule

**Rule in force.** Ghana has a rule in preparation and none shown to be in force. At the opening of a data centre in Accra in October 2025 the communications minister, Sam George, [said Ghana has a localisation policy under which local data and traffic processed on clouds outside the country will have to be hosted in Ghana](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). He spoke of it alongside a data harmonisation act. The report names no instrument, gives no date of approval and sets no date by which data must move, so the policy is counted as one being written.

**Enforcer named and rule applied.** The nearest held fact is the minister's statement, which [presents the new data centre as the capacity the policy needs](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>) and names no body to enforce it. No source held names a regulator given that job, and none reports the policy applied to any organisation.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Digital Realty ACR2 data center, Accra](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>); [Huawei-built data centers in Ghana](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>).

***Not held***. No source held says a rule that data must be kept in the country is in force.
