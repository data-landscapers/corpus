# Kenya

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The Startup Bill, 2022 was published in the Kenya Gazette in December 2022 for the Senate](<https://parliament.go.ke/sites/default/files/2023-02/The%20Startup%20Bill%2C%202022.pdf>). [KeNIA's Kenya Innovation Outlook 2024 says the Startup Bill has been before Parliament since 2021](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>). It is not enacted, and the government missed its April 2024 target. [The State Department for MSMEs Development posts the text of a Start-up Bill, 2024](<https://msme.go.ke/sites/default/files/Resource%20Center/Acts%20and%20Regulations/Start-up%20Bill%202024.pdf>). Kenya Industrial Estates would label start-ups, incubators and accelerators. The bill would also give start-ups tax reliefs and funds.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest fact: [in 2024 the Startup Bill, in Parliament since 2021, was not yet enacted](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest fact: [the government missed its April 2024 target for enacting the Startup Bill](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>).
