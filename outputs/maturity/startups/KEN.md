# Kenya

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [KeNIA's Kenya Innovation Outlook 2024 says the Startup Bill has been in parliamentary debate since 2021 and is not yet approved or enacted, and that the government missed its April 2024 enactment target](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>). [The State Department for MSMEs Development posts the text of a Start-up Bill, 2024, which would make Kenya Industrial Estates the body that labels start-ups, incubators and accelerators and would provide tax reliefs and funds for start-ups; the text is a bill, not an enacted act](<https://msme.go.ke/sites/default/files/Resource%20Center/Acts%20and%20Regulations/Start-up%20Bill%202024.pdf>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://kenia.go.ke/storage/pub-docs/ken_pub_Kenya%20Innovation%20Outlook%20Report%202024.pdf>).
