# Eswatini

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** The commerce ministry's [National MSME Policy 2024-2029](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/eswatini_msme_national_policy_2024-2029.pdf>) provides for a start-up matching fund and a fellowship fund for entrepreneurs. It also commits to review the tax requirements for start-ups. The policy sets presentation to Cabinet, and adoption there, as its last step.

**Labelling.** No startup has been found labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The [2024 MSME policy](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/eswatini_msme_national_policy_2024-2029.pdf>) proposes reviewing and modifying the tax requirements for start-ups.

**Funded.** The [E45 million MSME Revolving Fund](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20COMMERCE%202025.pdf>) has been run by Eswatini Bank since February 2021. It lends to start-ups and to firms hit by COVID-19, and the commerce ministry reported E54.5 million lent by September 2024. The [2024 policy](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-02/eswatini_msme_national_policy_2024-2029.pdf>) proposes a separate start-up matching fund.
