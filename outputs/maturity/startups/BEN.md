# Benin

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Decree No. 2023-095 of 22 March 2023 sets how the start-up label is granted](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2023-095/>) to micro, small and medium firms. In October 2026 La Marina reported that [article 147 of the tax code exempts labelled startups](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/10/14/benin-vers-un-avantage-fiscal-des-startups-decompte-a-partir-de-lobtention-du-label/>) from corporate tax and the payroll levy for two years. The exemption falls to 50% in the third year.

**Labelling.** The first labelling session, [held from 2 to 12 December 2024, drew 95 applications and labelled fifteen firms](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/10/14/benin-vers-un-avantage-fiscal-des-startups-decompte-a-partir-de-lobtention-du-label/>). Labelled start-ups [attended the launch of the FAEN fund](<https://lanation.bj/economie/accord-adpme-biic-un-fonds-pour-propulser-lentrepreneuriat-numerique-au-benin>) on 1 October 2025.

**Funded.** The bank BIIC and the SME agency ADPME [launched FAEN to finance labelled start-ups in their first three years](<https://www.biic-bank.com/fr/actualite/lancement-operationnel-du-fonds-dappui-a-lentrepreneuriat-pour-accompagner-et-financer-des-start-ups-labellisees.html>), along with growth-stage SMEs. [La Nation's account of the October 2025 launch](<https://lanation.bj/economie/accord-adpme-biic-un-fonds-pour-propulser-lentrepreneuriat-numerique-au-benin>) names no investment made.
