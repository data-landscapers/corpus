# Cameroon

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The Nkafu Policy Institute brief says Cameroon has not adopted a Startup Act but that a startup law has been in preparation since July 2021 at the Ministry in charge of Telecommunications, its elements not publicly available](<https://nkafu.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SBEC-Report-english-Oct-2023-final-1.pdf>). [Dayspring Law Firm states Cameroon's General Tax Code gives eligible innovative ICT start-ups a five-year full tax exemption then a further five years of reduced company tax (15%) and a 30% research tax credit](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>).

**Labelling.** [Dayspring Law Firm states start-ups must be approved by state-approved management centres, usually business incubators, before they can benefit from the tax incentives](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nkafu.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SBEC-Report-english-Oct-2023-final-1.pdf>).
