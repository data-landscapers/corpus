# Cameroon

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In March 2025 the Nkafu Policy Institute said a Startup Act is proposed but not yet adopted](<https://nkafu.org/unlocking-the-potential-of-cameroons-startup-ecosystem/>). [Its November 2023 brief said a startup law had been in preparation at the telecommunications ministry since July 2021](<https://nkafu.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SBEC-Report-english-Oct-2023-final-1.pdf>). [Dayspring Law Firm says the General Tax Code exempts eligible innovative ICT start-ups from tax for five years](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>). [Five more years of 15% company tax and a 30% research tax credit follow](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>).

**Labelling.** [Dayspring Law Firm states start-ups must be approved by state-approved management centres before they can benefit from the tax incentives](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>). [These centres are usually business incubators](<https://dayspringlaw.com/tax-incentives-for-innovative-startups-in-the-digital-industry/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest fact: [in March 2025 the Startup Act was still a proposal](<https://nkafu.org/unlocking-the-potential-of-cameroons-startup-ecosystem/>).
