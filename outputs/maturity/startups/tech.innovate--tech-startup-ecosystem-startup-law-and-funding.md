# Tech startup ecosystem: startup law and funding

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The Digital Transformation Strategy and the AU Startup Model Law Framework ask for a national startup law and strategy across seven areas, an innovation fund and angel networks. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says no startup law, strategy or public support for startups exists. |
| 2 Draft | Yes to 1 |
| 3 Adopted | Yes to 2 |

## Countries

### Cape Verde: 5 Leading

A public fund under the startup law or strategy financed startups in 2025.

- Lopes said the government funds around 100 start-ups, two co-founders each receiving six minimum salaries, and a $24 million Morabeza Fund targets youth- and women-led tech start-ups ([source, 2025-05](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy)).

### Rwanda: 5 Leading

A public fund under the startup law or strategy financed startups in 2024.

- The Rwanda Innovation Fund, a venture capital initiative of about $68.6 million backed by the African Development Bank and the Government of Rwanda and managed by Angaza Capital, had backed 12 technology companies by 2024, according to KT Press ([source, 2024](https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/07/why-tech-is-rwandas-fastest-growing-source-of-new-jobs/)).

### Algeria: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2026; a public fund under it is not known.

- El Watan reports 673 startups created, 3949 patents registered and 2339 'projet innovant' labels issued between 2022 and July 2026 ([source, 2026-07](https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/)).
- Authorities reported Algeria had registered 130 start-ups and 1,175 projects labeled as innovative ([source, 2026-04-20](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster)).

### Benin: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2025; a public fund under it is not known.

- BIIC and ADPME launched the FAEN programme to finance start-ups holding the 'start-up' label in seed phase (under 3 years) and growth-stage SMEs ([source, 2025-10-02](https://www.biic-bank.com/fr/actualite/lancement-operationnel-du-fonds-dappui-a-lentrepreneuriat-pour-accompagner-et-financer-des-start-ups-labellisees.html)).

### Cote d'Ivoire: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2025; a public fund under it is not known.

- Diallo says a 'levelisation' process grants certified startups access to public markets ([source, 2025-07-01](https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/)).

### Angola: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2026; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Angola's parliament unanimously passed a start-up law defining startups with a maximum annual revenue cap of $3.5 million and no minimum, according to ITWeb Africa and the Minister of Industry and Commerce ([source, 2026-03-23](https://itweb.africa/article/angola-passes-landmark-start-up-law/lLn14MmQwnVMJ6Aa)).

### Congo: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2025; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- The Council of Ministers of 2 July 2025 adopted decrees under loi n 71-2022 of 16 August 2022 on the startup label: the withdrawal procedure and the labelling commission ([source, 2025-07-02](https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-cmd-2025-07-02.pdf)).

### DR Congo: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2026; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Article 384 of the Congolese Digital Code makes digital start-ups with entrepreneur status eligible for the tax, para-fiscal, customs and exchange advantages of the legislation on entrepreneurship and start-ups, per We are Tech ([source, 2026-08-06](https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up)).

### Djibouti: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2026; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Dr Houssein Ahmed Assoweh lists the Startup Act among the texts Djibouti already has, and the roadmap recommends adapting its mechanisms to AI-using firms ([source, 2026-09-01](https://www.adi.dj/article/138857)).

### Libya: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2025; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Libya's Prime Minister's Decision 23 of 2025, in force from issue, creates the Sunduq Intilaqa fund for seed finance (up to LYD 150,000 per company) and loan guarantees for startups, and defines a start-up in law ([source, 2025-01-23](https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-23-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7/)).

### Mauritania: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2024; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Webmanagercenter states Mauritania has Law No. 2024-002 on technological and innovative startups ([source, 2024](https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/25/573435/mauritanie-fintech-la-mauritanie-peut-elle-devenir-un-laboratoire-numerique-regional/)).
- The digital ministry's page lists Loi n° 2024-002 P.R on technological and innovative startups, dated 2 January 2024 ([source, 2024-01-02](https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/loi-de-startup/)).

### Morocco: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2026; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- Decree No. 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 strengthened the regulatory framework of the Startup Catalytic Fund, set up by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform and managed by Tamwilcom, with $36 million public money over three years into venture capital funds, per The Morocco Post ([source, 2026-08-03](https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/)).
- Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 authorises Tamwilcom to take stakes for the Fonds Catalytique Startups in funds investing in startups, capped at 347 million dirhams over three years, under the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy ([source, 2026-08-03](https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/)).

### Senegal: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2025; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- APS reports the startup law was adopted 'in January last', granting three years of tax exemption and easing access to finance and public procurement, with its implementing decree and the Ecosysteme Startup platform launched on 20 November 2025 ([source, 2025-01](https://aps.sn/ecosysteme-startup-un-nouveau-dispositif-pour-accompagner-linnovation/)).
- Senegal's government adopted the implementing decree for Startup Law No. 2020-01 of 6 January 2020, setting criteria (one-third Senegalese capital) and incentives ([source, 2025-01-29](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegals-startup-law-advances-into-implementation-phase)).

### Tunisia: 3 Established

A startup law or strategy was adopted by 2026; startups labelled under it are not on record.

- The article says Tunisia's entrepreneurial ecosystem is carried by a pioneering Startup Act ([source, 2026-08](https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/)).
- The article describes the Startup Act as the legal framework promoting startups in Tunisia, on which the JICA programme draws ([source, 2026-07-28](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/la-tunisie-et-le-japon-sallient-pour-renforcer-les-ecosystemes-de-startups-en-afrique/)).

### Uganda: 2 Nascent

A startup law or strategy was being prepared in 2026; none adopted is on record.
