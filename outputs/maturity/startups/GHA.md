# Ghana

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In July 2026 the communication minister, Samuel Nartey George, described a forthcoming Innovation and Startup law](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/07/ghana-and-undp-strengthen-partnership-on-digital-transformation-projects/>). He said it will set eligibility criteria for startups. It will also give a legal basis to a planned national fintech fund. [In May 2026 the Labari Journal listed the Ghana Innovation and Startup Bill among 15 ICT bills](<https://labarijournal.com/ghana-is-considering-passing-a-host-of-new-ict-related-laws-an-analyst-says-they-would-hamper-africa-digital-trade/>) to be laid before or being considered by Parliament. It gave no sign that they will pass.
