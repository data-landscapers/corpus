# Guinea

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [On 8 April 2025 a ministry workshop pre-validated the draft Startup Act](<https://gas-gn.org/?p=514>). [The draft gives labelled startups tax relief and sets up a four-year support fund](<https://gas-gn.org/?p=514>). On 29 April 2025 the commerce ministry [held a review of the draft startup law](<https://www.mipme.gov.gn/seance-de-relecture-du-projet-de-loi-sur-les-startups/>). [In July 2026 Guinée Alliance Startup urged the authorities to pass the bill](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/guinee-alliance-startup_l%C3%A9cosyst%C3%A8me-startup-au-c%C5%93ur-de-la-future-activity-7478024722543349763-xQOy>). In August 2026 the head of the payments firm Lengo Pay [said Guinea has no Startup Act](<https://business360.media/startups-guinee-diagnostic-dg-lengo-pay/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. [The startup law was still a bill in July 2026](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/guinee-alliance-startup_l%C3%A9cosyst%C3%A8me-startup-au-c%C5%93ur-de-la-future-activity-7478024722543349763-xQOy>), and no label scheme outside it has been found.

**Funded.** No public fund under a startup law or strategy has been found financing startups. In August 2026 [the head of Lengo Pay said Guinea has no Startup Act](<https://business360.media/startups-guinee-diagnostic-dg-lengo-pay/>). He compared it with Senegal and Tunisia.

***Also on file***. None found.
