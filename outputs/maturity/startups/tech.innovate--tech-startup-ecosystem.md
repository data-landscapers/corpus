# Tech startup ecosystem: startup law and funding

*As at 2026-11-13.*

## The norm

The Digital Transformation Strategy and the AU Startup Model Law Framework ask for a national startup law and strategy across seven areas, an innovation fund and angel networks. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says no startup law, strategy or public support for startups exists. |
| 2 Draft | Yes to 1 |
| 3 Adopted | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Labelling | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Funded | Yes to 4 |

## Countries

### Algeria: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2026; no source found reports a public fund under it.

- El Watan reports 673 startups created, 3949 patents registered and 2339 'projet innovant' labels issued between 2022 and July 2026 ([source, 2026-07](https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/)).
- Authorities reported Algeria had registered 130 start-ups and 1,175 projects labeled as innovative ([source, 2026-04-20](https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster)).

### Benin: 4 Operating

A 2023 decree sets a startup label with tax exemptions and fifteen firms were labelled in 2024; no public fund investment in them is known.

- La Marina BJ reports that Benin's startup label rests on decree 2023-095 and that article 147 of the tax code already gives labelled startups two years of corporate tax and payroll levy exemption, then 50% in year three ([source, 2026-10-14](https://lamarina.bj/2026/10/14/benin-vers-un-avantage-fiscal-des-startups-decompte-a-partir-de-lobtention-du-label/)).
- The digital ministry reports that the first startup-label call, 2 to 12 December 2024, drew 95 applications and labelled 15 firms for three years ([source, 2025-05-12](https://innovation.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/labellisation-des-startups-resultats-du-premier-appel-a-candidatures_)).

### Cote d'Ivoire: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2025; no source found reports a public fund under it.

- On 4 November 2026 the government named 14 start-ups under Ivoire Tech Next 15 and 18 SMEs under Ivoire Tech Scale Up for 24 months of state support, as a step in implementing the Startup Act ([source, 2026-11-04](https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-le-ministre-djibril-ouattara-annonce-24-mois-daccompagnement-de-letat-pour-32-entreprises-du-secteur-du-numerique/)).
- Diallo says a 'levelisation' process grants certified startups access to public markets ([source, 2025-07-01](https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/)).

### Egypt: 4 Operating

Startups were labelled under the startup law or strategy in 2026; no source found reports a public fund under it.

### Morocco: 4 Operating

Over 540 startups held the state's innovative-startup label by 2025; investments by the 2026 public startup fund are not known.

- The ADD says the Label JEI, created in 2019, has benefited more than 540 startups out of 1,303 applications, with an international payment card allowance of up to 1 million dirhams a year ([source, 2025-04](https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/)).
- Decree No. 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 strengthened the regulatory framework of the Startup Catalytic Fund, set up by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform and managed by Tamwilcom, with $36 million public money over three years into venture capital funds, per The Morocco Post ([source, 2026-08-03](https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/)).
- Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 authorises Tamwilcom to take stakes for the Fonds Catalytique Startups in funds investing in startups, capped at 347 million dirhams over three years, under the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy ([source, 2026-08-03](https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/)).
