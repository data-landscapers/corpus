# Rwanda

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** Rwanda announced a Startup Act about five years before June 2025. That month The New Times reported [the act had not been enacted or tabled in Cabinet](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/26957/news/technology/how-far-are-we-from-having-a-startup-act>). The ICT Chamber's chief executive said several drafts were still being validated.

**Labelling.** No source found says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. [The act that would set up such a label was still in drafting in June 2025](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/26957/news/technology/how-far-are-we-from-having-a-startup-act>). No other scheme for registering startups has been found.

**Funded.** The Rwanda Innovation Fund [had backed 12 technology companies by 2024](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/07/why-tech-is-rwandas-fastest-growing-source-of-new-jobs/>), KT Press reported. It is a venture capital fund of about $68.6 million, backed by the African Development Bank and the government. Angaza Capital manages it. No fund set up under a startup law has been found.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No startup act or strategy adopted has been found. The innovation fund is not tied to a startup law.
