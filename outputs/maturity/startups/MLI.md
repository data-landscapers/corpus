# Mali

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [At the closing of the 2nd Semaine du Numerique on 27 June 2024, Minister Alhamdou Ag Ilyene mentioned the imminent adoption of the Startup Act (Loi sur les Startups) and an upcoming monitoring framework for young startups](<https://semainedunumerique.gouv.ml/ceremonie-de-cloture/>). [Mali's Council of Ministers adopted a start-up bill (projet de loi) setting the legal framework for labelling and developing start-ups, under the 2016-2020 digital economy action plan (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/234029-compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-09-octobre-201.html>).
