# Mali

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [On 27 June 2024 Minister Alhamdou Ag Ilyene said the Startup Act's adoption was imminent](<https://semainedunumerique.gouv.ml/ceremonie-de-cloture/>). [He also announced a monitoring framework for young startups](<https://semainedunumerique.gouv.ml/ceremonie-de-cloture/>). [In October 2019 the Council of Ministers adopted a start-up bill](<https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/234029-compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-09-octobre-201.html>). [It set the legal framework for labelling and developing start-ups](<https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/234029-compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-09-octobre-201.html>). [It fell under the 2016-2020 digital economy action plan](<https://malijet.com/conseil_des_ministres_du_mali/234029-compte-rendu-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-09-octobre-201.html>).
