# Morocco

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Decree No. 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 strengthened the regulatory framework of the Startup Catalytic Fund, set up by the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform and managed by Tamwilcom, with $36 million public money over three years into venture capital funds, per The Morocco Post](<https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/>). [Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 authorises Tamwilcom to take stakes for the Fonds Catalytique Startups in funds investing in startups, capped at 347 million dirhams over three years, under the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy](<https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/>).
