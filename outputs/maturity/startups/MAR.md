# Morocco

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Decree 2.26.576 of 3 August 2026 strengthened the rules of the Startup Catalytic Fund](<https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/>). [The digital ministry set it up and Tamwilcom manages it, with $36 million of public money over three years for venture capital funds](<https://themoroccopost.com/morocco-targets-258-million-in-startup-financing-through-new-venture-capital-mechanism/>). [The decree lets Tamwilcom take stakes for the fund in funds investing in startups, up to 347 million dirhams over three years](<https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/>). It sits under [the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy](<https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/>).

**Labelling.** [The digital development agency created its JEI label in 2019](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>). [By April 2025 it had gone to more than 540 startups from 1,303 applications](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>). [Labelled startups get an international payment card worth up to 1 million dirhams a year](<https://www.lavieeco.com/tech/add-silence-on-digitalise/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the 347 million dirham cap on the fund's stakes](<https://librentreprise.ma/2026/09/10/feu-vert-a-tamwilcom-pour-investir-347-mdh-dans-les-startups/>).
