# South Africa

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In July 2026 Business Day said South Africa had no enacted startup act, and that one was proposed](<https://www.businessday.co.za/economy/2026-07-06-south-african-startup-growth-trails-regional-peers/>). No source found says a startup bill is drafted or tabled, or a startup strategy adopted.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest fact found: [in July 2026 no startup act was in force](<https://www.businessday.co.za/economy/2026-07-06-south-african-startup-growth-trails-regional-peers/>).

**Funded.** [In February 2025 the science department said its public Innovation Fund had supported 12 venture capital firms and around 96 technology investments and enterprises](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/science-technology-and-innovation-innovation-fund-21-feb-2025>). It gives risk capital to early-stage startups and tech-enabled small firms.
