# Tunisia

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** Tunisia has a Startup Act: [a programme for diaspora entrepreneurs described the country's startup ecosystem in August 2026 as carried by it](<https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/>), and [a July 2026 report on a programme with Japan's JICA called it the legal framework for promoting startups](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/la-tunisie-et-le-japon-sallient-pour-renforcer-les-ecosystemes-de-startups-en-afrique/>). [In April 2025 the minister said a revised bill, Startup Act 2.0, was being prepared with the Ministry of Economy and Planning, keeping the earlier framework](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/sofiane-hmissi-annonce-le-lancement-du-portail-unique-des-services-administratifs-au-debut-du-mois-de-mai/>).

**Labelling.** Startups are labelled in Tunisia: [a March 2026 column put the number labelled in Tunisia at 1,450](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>).

**Funded.** Nothing found says a public fund set up under the Startup Act has financed startups. The nearest fact is [the count of 1,450 labelled startups that a March 2026 column gives for the country](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups.
