# Tunisia

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** Tunisia has a Startup Act. [A programme for diaspora entrepreneurs said in August 2026 that the act carries the country's startup ecosystem](<https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/>). [A July 2026 report on a programme with Japan's JICA called it the legal framework for startups](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/la-tunisie-et-le-japon-sallient-pour-renforcer-les-ecosystemes-de-startups-en-afrique/>). [In April 2025 the minister said a revised bill, Startup Act 2.0, was being prepared](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/sofiane-hmissi-annonce-le-lancement-du-portail-unique-des-services-administratifs-au-debut-du-mois-de-mai/>). It would keep the earlier framework.

**Labelling.** Startups are labelled in Tunisia. [A March 2026 column put the number labelled at 1,450](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>). [Agence Ecofin reports that about 1,400 firms held the Start-up label in 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0911-143235-tunisie-356-8-millions-pour-la-transformation-numerique-en-2027-face-a-des-defis-persistants>), and that the government aims for 2,000 in 2027.

**Funded.** Nothing found says a public fund set up under the Startup Act has financed startups. The nearest fact is [the count of 1,450 labelled startups that a March 2026 column gives for the country](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups.
