# Tunisia

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** Tunisia has a Startup Act. [A programme for diaspora entrepreneurs said in August 2026 that the act carries the country's startup ecosystem](<https://www.tunisie.fr/the-dot-lance-un-programme-dedie-aux-entrepreneurs-de-la-diaspora-tunisienne/>). [A July 2026 report on a programme with Japan's JICA called it the legal framework for startups](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/07/28/la-tunisie-et-le-japon-sallient-pour-renforcer-les-ecosystemes-de-startups-en-afrique/>). [In April 2025 the minister said a revised bill, Startup Act 2.0, was being prepared](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/sofiane-hmissi-annonce-le-lancement-du-portail-unique-des-services-administratifs-au-debut-du-mois-de-mai/>). It would keep the earlier framework. [In April 2026 he said several ministries were still working on the bill](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/04/17/que-change-le-futur-startup-act-2-0-pour-les-startups-tunisiennes/>).

**Labelling.** Startups are labelled in Tunisia. [A March 2026 column put the number labelled at 1,450](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>). [Agence Ecofin reports that about 1,400 firms held the Start-up label in 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0911-143235-tunisie-356-8-millions-pour-la-transformation-numerique-en-2027-face-a-des-defis-persistants>), and that the government aims for 2,000 in 2027. [El Ghazala Technopark said 1,214 startups held the label by July 2025](<https://www.tunisienumerique.com/1214-startups-labellisees-a-fin-juillet-2025/>), with an eight-year tax exemption. [The June 2026 labelling session granted 7 labels and 9 pre-labels from 40 applications](<https://startup.gov.tn/fr/startup_act/results>).

**Funded.** ANAVA is a fund of funds run by Smart Capital. It sits under the government's Startup Tunisia programme. The World Bank, the CDC and KfW back it. [In September 2025 it committed USD 4 million to the Rasmal Innovation Fund](<https://smartcapital.tn/?p=1848>). Rasmal is a venture fund investing in startups. [In July 2026 the CDC's director-general said ANAVA had financed 10 investment funds](<https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/09/24/tunisia-startup-fund/>). Through them, 70 Tunisian startups were financed.
