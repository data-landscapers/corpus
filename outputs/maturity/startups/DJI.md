# Djibouti

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Dr Houssein Ahmed Assoweh lists the Startup Act among the texts Djibouti already has, and the roadmap recommends adapting its mechanisms to AI-using firms](<https://www.adi.dj/article/138857>). [On 9 November 2025 the Minister Delegate for Digital Economy and Innovation presented the Startup Act Djibouti bill, with a label, tax incentives, a proposed National Fund of Funds and a startup visa, to the National Assembly, which gave a favourable opinion with amendments for plenary](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/12/djibouti-advances-startup-act-to-boost-innovation-and-youth-employment/>).

**Labelling.** No source held since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.adi.dj/article/138857>).

**Funded.** No source held since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.adi.dj/article/138857>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy.
