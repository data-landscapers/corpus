# Djibouti

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [On 9 November 2025 the minister for the digital economy presented the Startup Act bill to the National Assembly](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/12/djibouti-advances-startup-act-to-boost-innovation-and-youth-employment/>). It provides a startup label, tax incentives, a proposed national fund of funds and a startup visa. The Assembly gave a favourable opinion, with amendments for the plenary. [In 2026 UNESCO's country profile listed the Startup Act among Djibouti's legal foundations](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/global-hub/djibouti-0>). [In September 2026 La Nation also listed it among the country's existing foundations](<https://www.lanation.dj/premier-forum-national-sur-lintelligence-artificielle-djibouti-devoile-sa-strategie-nationale-2026-2030/>), citing a report backed by UNESCO.

**Labelling.** [A guide for business founders on entreprendre.dj says an innovative, high-growth firm can apply for the Startup label](<https://entreprendre.dj/portals/g2b/guide>). The label brings stronger tax and social advantages.

**Funded.** [The bill presented in November 2025 proposed a national fund of funds](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/12/djibouti-advances-startup-act-to-boost-innovation-and-youth-employment/>). No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the act has financed startups.
