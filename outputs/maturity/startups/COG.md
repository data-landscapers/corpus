# Congo

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [On 2 July 2025 the Council of Ministers adopted decrees under law no. 71-2022 of 16 August 2022 on the startup label](<https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-cmd-2025-07-02.pdf>). [They cover the label's withdrawal procedure and its labelling commission](<https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-cmd-2025-07-02.pdf>). [Journal du Net says the Senate adopted the law in August 2022](<https://www.journaldunet.com/start-up/1548353-brazzaville-prochaine-frontiere-de-la-tech-africaine-ce-que-les-startups-congolaises-revelent-de-l-afrique-centrale-de-demain/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the July 2025 decrees set up the startup label's labelling commission](<https://sgg.cg/ccm/compte-rendu-cmd-2025-07-02.pdf>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [law no. 71 of 16 August 2022 grants the digital start-up and technological innovation label](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy.
