# Zambia

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In July 2024 Minister Felix Mutati said the government was working on a Start Up Bill to create a Venture Capital Fund for start-ups](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/?p=3864>). [In August 2025 he said the Innovators and Inventors Bill would soon be tabled in Parliament](<https://lusakastar.com/news/government-t-to-table-innovators-and-inventors-bill-in-parliament>). [In October 2025 he said the government had finalised it](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/techbytes-zambian-govt-finalises-innovation-and-inventors-bill-sets-up-venture-capital-fund/>). [The ITU's 2026 digital innovation profile says the Ministry of Technology and Science is finalising a Start-up Bill after consultations](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Innovation/Documents/Publications/DIP%20Zambia/D-INNO-PROFILE.ZAMBIA-2026-PDF-E.pdf>). [A Smart Africa workshop in July 2026 assessed progress on the proposed Startup Bill](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/02/smart-africa-and-zambia-advance-digital-entrepreneurship-policy-through-stakeholder-workshop/>).
