# Mauritania

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The digital ministry's page lists Law No. 2024-002 P.R on technological and innovative startups, dated 2 January 2024](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/fr/loi-de-startup/>). [Webmanagercenter states that Mauritania has this law](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/25/573435/mauritanie-fintech-la-mauritanie-peut-elle-devenir-un-laboratoire-numerique-regional/>). [The Africa Capital Report also says the law is in place](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/mauritanias-fintech-startups-met-investors-did-capital-follow/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the Africa Capital Report says Law No. 2024-002 establishes a labelling regime](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/mauritanias-fintech-startups-met-investors-did-capital-follow/>). [It also sets facilitations for innovative technology companies](<https://theafricacapitalreport.com/mauritanias-fintech-startups-met-investors-did-capital-follow/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [in November 2025 the digital ministry was speeding publication of the texts completing the start-up law](<https://fr.tawassoul.net/mauritanie-ue-reunion-pour-evaluer-le-niveau-de-cooperation-numerique-bilaterale/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy.
