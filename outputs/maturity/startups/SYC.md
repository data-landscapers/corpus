# Seychelles

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [UNCTAD's Seychelles National Entrepreneurship Strategy says there is no stand-alone Entrepreneurship Policy or Act and proposes only to consider introducing a Start-Up Act defining innovative start-ups](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>).
