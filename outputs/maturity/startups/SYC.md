# Seychelles

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [UNCTAD's 2023 National Entrepreneurship Strategy for Seychelles found no stand-alone Entrepreneurship Policy or Act](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>). [It proposes only to consider introducing a Start-Up Act that would define innovative start-ups](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the 2023 strategy proposes to consider a Start-Up Act defining innovative start-ups](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [in 2023 Seychelles had no stand-alone Entrepreneurship Policy or Act](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/tcsdiaeinf2023d2_en.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.
