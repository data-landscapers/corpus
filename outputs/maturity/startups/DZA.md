# Algeria

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [El Watan says ministerial order 1275 lets students turn their end-of-studies project into a startup or a patent](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [We Are Tech reports a government strategy to scale up the entrepreneurial ecosystem](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster>). [President Tebboune's target is 20,000 start-ups in Algeria by 2029](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster>).

**Labelling.** [El Watan reports 2,339 'projet innovant' labels issued between 2022 and July 2026](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [It also counts 673 startups created and 3,949 patents registered](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [Authorities reported 130 registered start-ups and 1,175 projects labelled as innovative](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-unveils-first-ai-and-cybersecurity-start-up-cluster>). [In February 2026 about 2,300 of the more than 7,800 startups on startup.dz were certified as official startups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-waives-ipo-fees-for-startups-until-2028>). [Executive decree 25-311 of 1 December 2025 added a Scale-up label to the Start-up label](<https://legal-doctrine.com/fr/edition/creation-du-label-scale-up-lalgerie-renforce-son-dispositif-de-labellisation-des-start-ups-22429708315cfd1b44431f0a1183819d>). [By March 2026 about 15% of more than 100,000 applications on start-up.dz had won the label](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/startups-plus-de-100-000-demandes-de-labellisation-enregistrees-sur-la-plateforme-start-up-dz/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [COSOB, SGBV and Algérie Clearing waived IPO and listing fees for certified startups until 2028](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/algeria-waives-ipo-fees-for-startups-until-2028>).
