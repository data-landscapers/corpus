# Zimbabwe

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** In September 2024 [Zimbabwe's Cabinet adopted the principles of the Science, Technology, Innovation Start-up and Knowledge-based Economy Promotion Bill](<https://openparly.com/bill-alert-science-technology-innovation-start-up-and-knowledge-based-economy-promotion-bill/>). The bill would give start-ups tax incentives, rebates and preferential procurement. Open Parly ZW reported it as a bill, not an act.

**Labelling.** The nearest held fact is [the start-up bill whose principles Cabinet adopted in 2024](<https://openparly.com/bill-alert-science-technology-innovation-start-up-and-knowledge-based-economy-promotion-bill/>). No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy.

**Funded.** In October 2026 the telecoms regulator's director-general said [its Innovation Drive Programme has given milestone-based funding to more than 100 innovations](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/potraz-says-it-is-now-a-gateway-for-investors-not-a-gatekeeper/>). The Universal Service Fund backs it, to absorb early-stage risk. It is not tied to the start-up bill. No fund set up under a startup law or strategy is reported.

***Also on file***. [National Venture Capital Fund](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/08/zim-govt-calls-on-individuals-to-help-fund-startups-through-national-venture-capital-fund/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a startup law or strategy has been adopted.
