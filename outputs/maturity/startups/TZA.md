# Tanzania

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In 2024 the ICT ministry began drafting Tanzania's first National Startup Policy](<https://tsa.co.tz/storage/documents/w9Fsk1RQDCUKtfNiUXxOGijbCajKBHeuB8SNKwxx.pdf>), the Tanzania Startup Association reports. [In March 2025 the government opened consultations and a baseline study](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-kicks-off-consultations-for-tanzania-s-first-startup-policy-4966048>). [In October 2026 Zanzibar's communication minister said the islands' startups bill was complete](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/zanzibar/habari/smz-yatenga-sh53-bilioni-kuendesha-miradi-ya-tehama-5618282>). He put its first reading in November or December 2026. The bill would create a startup fund of grants and low-cost loans.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the national startup policy still in consultation in 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-kicks-off-consultations-for-tanzania-s-first-startup-policy-4966048>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the national startup policy still in consultation in 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-kicks-off-consultations-for-tanzania-s-first-startup-policy-4966048>).
