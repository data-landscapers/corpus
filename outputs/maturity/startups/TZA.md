# Tanzania

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Zanzibar's communication minister Mudrik Ramadhan Soraga said the draft startups bill is complete and due for first reading in November or December 2026, to create a startup fund of grants and low-cost loans](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/zanzibar/habari/smz-yatenga-sh53-bilioni-kuendesha-miradi-ya-tehama-5618282>). [The government has begun formulating Tanzania's first national startup policy through a consultative process and baseline study, The Citizen reports](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-kicks-off-consultations-for-tanzania-s-first-startup-policy-4966048>). [The Tanzania Startup Association reports that in 2024 Tanzania advanced preparations for its first National Startup Policy, coordinated by the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, with the drafting phase under way and consultations still to begin](<https://tsa.co.tz/storage/documents/w9Fsk1RQDCUKtfNiUXxOGijbCajKBHeuB8SNKwxx.pdf>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/zanzibar/habari/smz-yatenga-sh53-bilioni-kuendesha-miradi-ya-tehama-5618282>).
