# Tanzania

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In 2024 the ICT ministry began drafting Tanzania's first National Startup Policy](<https://tsa.co.tz/storage/documents/w9Fsk1RQDCUKtfNiUXxOGijbCajKBHeuB8SNKwxx.pdf>), the Tanzania Startup Association reports. [In March 2025 the government opened consultations and a baseline study](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/government-kicks-off-consultations-for-tanzania-s-first-startup-policy-4966048>). [In February 2026 the ministry said the draft Start-ups Policy 2026 was finalised and submitted for government approval](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-accelerates-digital-revolution-in-100-days/>). [In October 2026 Tanzania still had no dedicated startup law or policy](<https://www.tanzaniainvest.com/industry/startup-policy-within-six-months>), and the Vice-President directed that one be finalised within six months. [Zanzibar's communication minister said in October 2026 that the islands' startups bill was complete](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/zanzibar/habari/smz-yatenga-sh53-bilioni-kuendesha-miradi-ya-tehama-5618282>), with a first reading due in November or December 2026.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the draft Start-ups Policy 2026 was awaiting government approval in February 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-accelerates-digital-revolution-in-100-days/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [Zanzibar's startups bill would create a startup fund of grants and low-cost loans](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/zanzibar/habari/smz-yatenga-sh53-bilioni-kuendesha-miradi-ya-tehama-5618282>).
