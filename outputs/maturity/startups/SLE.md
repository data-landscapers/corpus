# Sierra Leone

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** In September 2026 the communications ministry and the International Trade Centre [held a validation workshop on a startup ecosystem policy](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/7489/sierra-leone-startup-ecosystem-stakeholders-convene-to-strengthen-support-for-entrepreneurs>). The policy is meant to feed a planned Startup Act. On 12 September 2026 the communications minister [said the Act is expected in the first quarter of 2027](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/fatima-bio-launch-flot-financial-platform/>). [Invest Salone's brief of November 2025 said the Startup Act was in development](<https://investsalone.com/resource-bank/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ISL-Angel-Investment-brief-1.pdf>).

**Labelling.** No startup has been found labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. [Invest Salone's November 2025 brief](<https://investsalone.com/resource-bank/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ISL-Angel-Investment-brief-1.pdf>) says the planned Act would recognise startups as a distinct business class.

**Funded.** No public fund under a startup law or strategy has been found financing startups. In September 2026 the minister [also announced plans for an Innovation Investment Fund](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/fatima-bio-launch-flot-financial-platform/>). [Invest Salone's brief](<https://investsalone.com/resource-bank/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ISL-Angel-Investment-brief-1.pdf>) says the planned Act would give tax incentives for certified angel investments.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No startup law or strategy adopted has been found since October 2023.
