# DR Congo

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The DRC's Startup Act is ordonnance-loi 22/030 of 8 September 2022](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/02/fiscalite-des-startups-congolaises-le-ministere-des-finances-met-fin-aux-mauvaises>). Its article 50 gives startups tax advantages, the Ministry of Finance said in August 2026. [Article 384 of the Digital Code makes digital start-ups eligible for these advantages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>). They cover tax, customs and exchange. [In August 2026 a digital specialist told ACP the act was still being finalised](<https://acp.cd/business/rdc-le-secteur-du-numerique-encore-confronte-a-des-nombreuses-contraintes-un-specialiste/>). [Cabinet adopted four draft decrees to apply its advantages in May 2025](<https://bankable.africa/fr/climat-des-affaires/2905-1244-entrepreneuriat-des-decrets-pour-activer-le-soutien-aux-startups-en-rdc>).

**Labelling.** No source found says a startup has been labelled under the Startup Act. [Decree 26/022 of 5 June 2026 created a national labelling committee](<https://doseco.cd/2026/11/07/loi-sur-les-startup-4-ans-apres-la-promulgation-la-rdc-attend-toujours-un-dispositif-de-labellisation-operationnel/>). [It first met on 4 November 2026, with no secretariat or budget and no startup yet labelled](<https://doseco.cd/2026/11/07/loi-sur-les-startup-4-ans-apres-la-promulgation-la-rdc-attend-toujours-un-dispositif-de-labellisation-operationnel/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest fact: [Article 384 gives such start-ups tax, customs and exchange advantages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No startup has been labelled. The labelling committee was created in June 2026. It first met in November 2026, without a budget or secretariat.
