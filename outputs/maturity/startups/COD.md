# DR Congo

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The DRC's Startup Act is ordonnance-loi 22/030 of 8 September 2022](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/02/fiscalite-des-startups-congolaises-le-ministere-des-finances-met-fin-aux-mauvaises>). Its article 50 gives startups tax advantages, the Ministry of Finance said in August 2026. [Article 384 of the Digital Code makes digital start-ups eligible for these advantages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>). They cover tax, customs and exchange. [In August 2026 a digital specialist told ACP the act was still being finalised](<https://acp.cd/business/rdc-le-secteur-du-numerique-encore-confronte-a-des-nombreuses-contraintes-un-specialiste/>). Four draft decrees to apply its advantages were adopted in May 2025.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest fact: [Article 384 of the Digital Code covers digital start-ups with entrepreneur status](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest fact: [Article 384 gives such start-ups tax, customs and exchange advantages](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>).
