# DR Congo

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Article 384 of the Congolese Digital Code makes digital start-ups with entrepreneur status eligible for the tax, para-fiscal, customs and exchange advantages of the legislation on entrepreneurship and start-ups, per We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>). [ACP quotes a digital specialist saying the DRC Startup Act is still being finalised, with four draft decrees adopted in May 2025 to operationalise tax, customs and exchange advantages of a 2022 ordonnance-loi still in preparation per a 3 August 2026 communique](<https://acp.cd/business/rdc-le-secteur-du-numerique-encore-confronte-a-des-nombreuses-contraintes-un-specialiste/>). [The DRC has a Startup Act, ordonnance-loi n 22/030 of 8 September 2022, whose article 50 gives startups tax advantages (Ministry of Finance communique) (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://actualite.cd/index.php/2026/08/02/fiscalite-des-startups-congolaises-le-ministere-des-finances-met-fin-aux-mauvaises>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/la-rdc-confirme-les-nouvelles-taxes-numeriques-mais-exonere-les-start-up>).
