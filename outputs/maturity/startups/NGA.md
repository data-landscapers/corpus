# Nigeria

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The Nigeria Startup Act, 2022 (Act No. 32), assented to by the President on 19 October 2022 and published in the Official Gazette of 21 October 2022, establishes the National Council for Digital Innovation and Entrepreneurship, a startup label, a Startup Portal and a Startup Investment Seed Fund (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NIGERIA-STARTUP-ACT-2022-Final-Publication.pdf>). [NITDA says the Nigeria Startup Act was enacted on 19 October 2022, with a Startup Label granting eligible startups its benefits (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://nitda.gov.ng/nitda-dg-inaugurates-startup-labelling-committee-for-implementation-of-nigeria-startup-act/8254/>). [NAN reports the Nigerian Startup Act was signed into law on 19 October 2022, with NITDA as its implementing secretariat (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://nannews.ng/nitda-inaugurates-startup-consultative-forum-assures-implementation-of-nsa/>).

**Labelling.** [NAN reports the Startup Consultative Forum's membership includes labelled startups, and that certified startups get access to incentives under the Act](<https://nannews.ng/nitda-inaugurates-startup-consultative-forum-assures-implementation-of-nsa/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nannews.ng/nitda-inaugurates-startup-consultative-forum-assures-implementation-of-nsa/>).

***Also on file***. [Boost Africa](<https://von.gov.ng/eu-eib-highlight-boost-africas-impact-on-entrepreneurs/>); [Startup Investment Seed Fund](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NIGERIA-STARTUP-ACT-2022-Final-Publication.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups.
