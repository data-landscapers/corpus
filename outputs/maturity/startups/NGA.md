# Nigeria

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** In July 2024 [NITDA set up a Startup Labelling Committee to implement the Nigeria Startup Act](<https://nitda.gov.ng/nitda-dg-inaugurates-startup-labelling-committee-for-implementation-of-nigeria-startup-act/8254/>). The Act was signed in October 2022, with NITDA as its secretariat. The label opens the Act's benefits to eligible startups after an assessment. [It sets up a startup label, a Startup Portal and a Startup Investment Seed Fund](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NIGERIA-STARTUP-ACT-2022-Final-Publication.pdf>).

**Labelling.** In April 2025 [NITDA's new Startup Consultative Forum included labelled startups](<https://nannews.ng/nitda-inaugurates-startup-consultative-forum-assures-implementation-of-nsa/>). Certified startups get the incentives the Act offers. The forum also brings in support organisations and angel investors to advise on the Act.

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says the Act's seed fund has invested in startups. The nearest held fact is [the Act itself](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NIGERIA-STARTUP-ACT-2022-Final-Publication.pdf>), which sets the fund up.

***Also on file***. [Boost Africa](<https://von.gov.ng/eu-eib-highlight-boost-africas-impact-on-entrepreneurs/>); [Startup Investment Seed Fund](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NIGERIA-STARTUP-ACT-2022-Final-Publication.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups.
