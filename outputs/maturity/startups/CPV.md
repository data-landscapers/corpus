# Cape Verde

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In May 2025 Secretary of State Pedro Lopes said Cape Verde has a dedicated start-up law with tax benefits](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>). [A 2026 finance ministry deck says Law 70/VIII/2014 gives startups financial incentives and institutional support](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Incentivos+Fiscais+do+Or%C3%A7amento+do+Estado+2026+%C3%A0+Di%C3%A1spora+Cabo-verdiana.pdf/77cb6457-8ea4-6d65-a08b-48f436baf849?t=1772281368148>). [It says the 2026 budget taxes startups at 5% for their first five years, or 2.5% for ICT startups](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/Incentivos+Fiscais+do+Or%C3%A7amento+do+Estado+2026+%C3%A0+Di%C3%A1spora+Cabo-verdiana.pdf/77cb6457-8ea4-6d65-a08b-48f436baf849?t=1772281368148>). [In January 2025 the project unit UGPE began preparing a strategy to attract international startups](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/governo-vai-preparar-programa-para-atrair-startups-internacionais/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [in May 2025 Lopes said the government funds around 100 start-ups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>).

**Funded.** [In May 2025 Lopes said the government funds around 100 start-ups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>). [Two co-founders of each receive six minimum salaries](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>). [A $24 million Morabeza Fund targets tech start-ups led by young people and women](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>). [Fundo Morabeza, a €24 million government fund co-financed by the AfDB, funded 1,774 small firms in 2025](<https://www.fundomorabeza.com/en>). [It had spent €5.42 million by 31 March 2026](<https://www.fundomorabeza.com/en>).
