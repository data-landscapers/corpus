**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [in May 2025 Lopes said the government funds around 100 start-ups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>).

**Funded.** [In May 2025 Lopes said the government funds around 100 start-ups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cape-verde-ramps-up-digital-start-up-ecosystem-to-boost-economy>).
