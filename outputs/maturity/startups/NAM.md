# Namibia

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** On 26 November 2024 Cabinet [approved the National Informal Economy, Start-ups and Entrepreneurship Development Policy (NIESED)](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-cabinet-decisions-issued-at-the-21st-decision-making-meeting-on-26-november-2024/>). It directed the trade ministry to put the policy into effect in full. The same decision [told the ministry to finalise an Act to accompany the policy](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-cabinet-decisions-issued-at-the-21st-decision-making-meeting-on-26-november-2024/>). The [December 2023 draft of the policy](<https://consultancy.com.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/DRAFT-NATIONAL-INFORMAL-ECONOMY_STARTUPS_ENTERPRENEURSHIP-POLICY_DECEMBER-2023.pdf>) proposes a National Risk Capital Facility for early-stage firms. The facility would sit beside the National Venture Capital Fund.

**Labelling.** No startup has been found labelled or registered under the policy. In November 2024 Cabinet [ordered the trade ministry to finalise the Act meant to accompany it](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-cabinet-decisions-issued-at-the-21st-decision-making-meeting-on-26-november-2024/>).

**Funded.** In September 2025 Cabinet [authorised the ICT ministry to engage female founders as potential beneficiaries of a US$20 million venture capital fund](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-expedites-data-centre-smart-city-development/KWEBb7yLVARvmRjO>). The fund is to finance small and medium-sized businesses in the ICT industry.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No startup has been found labelled under the policy. No investment by the venture fund has been found.
