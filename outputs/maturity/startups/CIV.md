# Cote d'Ivoire

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Karen Diallo, Director of Digital Transformation, says Cote d'Ivoire has passed a new startup law to ease startups' access to government contracts](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/>). [We Are Tech Africa says Cote d'Ivoire adopted a Startup Act in November 2023 with a Digital Startup label, fiscal and customs incentives and a national labeling committee](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-d-ivoire-calls-for-178m-fund-to-boost-tech-startups>). [The digital ministry's draft 2025 budget presentation cites it as law 2023-901 of 23 November 2023 promoting digital startups](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/173214119048.pdf>).

**Labelling.** [Diallo says a 'levelisation' process grants certified startups access to public markets](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/>). [The 2023 act set up a Digital Startup label, awarded by a national labelling committee, with fiscal and customs incentives for the firms that hold it](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-d-ivoire-calls-for-178m-fund-to-boost-tech-startups>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techcabal.com/2025/07/01/cote-divoire-cyber-africa-forum/>).
