# Burkina Faso

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Agence Ecofin reports that Burkina Faso's AFP-PME is preparing a startup law](<https://ecomnewsafrique.com/2025/08/04/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-burkinabe-a-engage-un-processus-visant-a-elaborer-une-loi-specifique-dediee-a-la-creation-au-financement-et-a-la-croissance-des-start-up-locales/>). [Its draft texts were examined at a national validation workshop on 17-18 July 2025](<https://ecomnewsafrique.com/2025/08/04/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-burkinabe-a-engage-un-processus-visant-a-elaborer-une-loi-specifique-dediee-a-la-creation-au-financement-et-a-la-croissance-des-start-up-locales/>). [The workshop, in Ouagadougou, was to validate the draft startup promotion bill and its implementing decree](<https://directinfoburkina.net/burkina-faso-un-atelier-national-de-validation-des-projets-de-textes-juridiques-pour-la-promotion-des-startups-en-cours/>). [Drafting workshops had been held from March 2025](<https://directinfoburkina.net/burkina-faso-un-atelier-national-de-validation-des-projets-de-textes-juridiques-pour-la-promotion-des-startups-en-cours/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the draft bill comes with an implementing decree, also put to validation in July 2025](<https://directinfoburkina.net/burkina-faso-un-atelier-national-de-validation-des-projets-de-textes-juridiques-pour-la-promotion-des-startups-en-cours/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 the startup law was still in draft](<https://ecomnewsafrique.com/2025/08/04/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-burkinabe-a-engage-un-processus-visant-a-elaborer-une-loi-specifique-dediee-a-la-creation-au-financement-et-a-la-croissance-des-start-up-locales/>).
