# Senegal

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In January 2025 the government adopted the implementing decree for Startup Law No. 2020-01 of 6 January 2020](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegals-startup-law-advances-into-implementation-phase>). [The decree sets criteria, including one-third Senegalese capital, and incentives](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegals-startup-law-advances-into-implementation-phase>). [APS reports the law grants three years of tax exemption and eases access to finance and public procurement](<https://aps.sn/ecosysteme-startup-un-nouveau-dispositif-pour-accompagner-linnovation/>). [The decree and the Ecosysteme Startup platform were launched on 20 November 2025](<https://aps.sn/ecosysteme-startup-un-nouveau-dispositif-pour-accompagner-linnovation/>). [In February 2025 President Faye committed to updating the Startup Act and putting it into effect under the New Deal Technologique](<https://www.presidence.sn/en/actualites/new-deal-technologique-a-national-ambition-to-make-senegal-a-leader-in-the-digital-economy-in-africa-1/>). [In March 2026 Prime Minister Ousmane Sonko highlighted the Act's implementation and support for tech entrepreneurs](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [in February 2025 President Faye committed to putting the Startup Act's labelling into effect](<https://www.presidence.sn/en/actualites/new-deal-technologique-a-national-ambition-to-make-senegal-a-leader-in-the-digital-economy-in-africa-1/>).
