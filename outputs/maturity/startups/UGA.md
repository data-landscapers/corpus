# Uganda

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [In May 2024 a ninth draft of the National Startup Policy was presented](<https://www.ecosystem.build/inside-the-uganda-startup-policy-journey/>). A private member's Startup Bill was introduced in Parliament. [In June 2026 Startup Uganda said the National Startup Act was under technical review at the trade ministry](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/startupug_startupuganda-nationalstartupact-policyleadership-activity-7476260216469368832-9mER>). It was moving towards Cabinet. [In August 2026 a Smart Africa and Ministry of ICT workshop named advancing the Startup Bill a priority](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/31/smart-africa-uganda-reforms-digital-entrepreneurship/>). [In November 2026 the Daily Monitor reported that the trade ministry was finalising a startup policy for Cabinet](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/startup-policies-are-underway-but-what-about-funding-for-the-young-innovators--5654650>). Uganda had none yet. [In April 2025 Uganda proposed a three-year income tax exemption for startups](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/uganda-bets-on-tax-cuts-to-support-startups>). It came in amendments to the 2025 Income Tax Bill.

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [in November 2026 the startup policy had not yet gone to Cabinet](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/startup-policies-are-underway-but-what-about-funding-for-the-young-innovators--5654650>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the proposed exemption would free startups from income tax for three years](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/uganda-bets-on-tax-cuts-to-support-startups>).
