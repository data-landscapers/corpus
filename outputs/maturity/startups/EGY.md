# Egypt

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Egypt launched its Startup Charter on 7 February 2026](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2823>), a five-year startup strategy. [It sets an official startup definition, a financing initiative targeting USD 1 billion and a policy observatory](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2823>). [The planning minister says it holds more than 80 policies and procedures now being applied](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=6969&lang=en>). [Law No. 6 of 2025 sets a simplified tax system for startups](<https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/egypt-launches-first-national-startup-charter>).

**Labelling.** [Firms apply to the SME agency MSMEDA for a Startup Classification Certificate, which grants the official startup label](<https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/egypt-launches-first-national-startup-charter>). [ITIDA's 2026 report names Flend and Olive Finance as the first certified startups](<https://itida.gov.eg/English/MediaCenter/Announcements/Documents/egypt-startup-ecosystem-report-2026.pdf>). [By May 2026 MSMEDA had classified 61 companies under the charter, an MP said](<https://www.elbalad.news/6972755>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest held fact: [the charter's financing initiative targets USD 1 billion](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2823>).
