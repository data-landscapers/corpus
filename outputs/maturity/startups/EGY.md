# Egypt

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [The Minister of Planning, Economic Development and International Cooperation says the Egypt Startup Charter includes more than 80 policies and procedures currently being implemented, with an action plan built from over 500 recommendations](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=6969&lang=en>). [Egypt launched Egypt's Startup Charter on 7 February 2026, a five-year startup strategy with an official startup definition, a financing initiative targeting USD 1bn and a policy observatory](<https://moic.gov.eg/news/2823>). [Egypt launched the National Startup Charter in February 2026, giving a formal startup definition, a certification pathway and a unified policy framework separate from SME regulation, and commits USD 1 billion in financing](<https://itida.gov.eg/English/MediaCenter/Announcements/Documents/egypt-startup-ecosystem-report-2026.pdf>).

**Labelling.** [Clyde & Co reports companies apply to MSMEDA for a Startup Classification Certificate, which gives an official Startup Label in 5 days (fast track) or 14 days (regular), qualifying them for the Charter's incentives](<https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/02/egypt-launches-first-national-startup-charter>). [Many startups obtained the official "startup label" through standard and fast-track application processes, and names Flend and Olive Finance as Egypt's first nationally certified startups](<https://itida.gov.eg/English/MediaCenter/Announcements/Documents/egypt-startup-ecosystem-report-2026.pdf>).

**Funded.** [Egypt Ventures, the sovereign VC vehicle, has deployed investments across 22 companies to date, and that the MSMEDA Fund of Funds co-invests alongside private VCs with capital deployment ongoing](<https://itida.gov.eg/English/MediaCenter/Announcements/Documents/egypt-startup-ecosystem-report-2026.pdf>).
