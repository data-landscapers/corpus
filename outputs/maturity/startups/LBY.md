# Libya

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Libya's Prime Minister's Decision 23 of 2025, in force from issue, creates the Sunduq Intilaqa fund for seed finance (up to LYD 150,000 per company) and loan guarantees for startups, and defines a start-up in law](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-23-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2025-%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%AF%D8%B9%D9%85-%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
