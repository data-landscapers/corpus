# Angola

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [Angola's parliament unanimously passed a start-up law in March 2026, according to ITWeb Africa and the Minister of Industry and Commerce](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-passes-landmark-start-up-law/lLn14MmQwnVMJ6Aa>). [The law defines a startup by a maximum annual revenue of $3.5 million, with no minimum](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-passes-landmark-start-up-law/lLn14MmQwnVMJ6Aa>). [Novo Jornal reports that Parliament had approved the bill in general on 19 March 2026, by a unanimous vote of 181](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/propostas-de-lei-das-startups-e-do-novo-codigo-do-imposto-sobre-os-rendimentos-das-pessoas-singulares-aprovadas-na-generalidade-no-parlamento-70556.html>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest held fact: [the new law defines what counts as a startup](<https://itweb.africa/article/angola-passes-landmark-start-up-law/lLn14MmQwnVMJ6Aa>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups.
