# Togo

## Startup law and funding

**Law or strategy.** [African Business reports that Togo's Council of Ministers approved startup legislation in 2023](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>). [The legislation has yet to be ratified](<https://african.business/2026/03/innov-africa-deals/togos-quiet-rise-as-a-startup-contender>). [In October 2025 the minister, Cina Lawson, said the Togolese Startup Act was being adopted](<https://togo-today.com/grit-lome-fait-vibrer-la-tech-africaine/>). [In May 2026 the opposition deputy Brigitte Adjamagbo-Johnson wrote that the bill on the innovation ecosystem was about to be adopted](<https://togoactualite.com/projet-de-loi-sur-linnovation-au-togo-une-ambition-necessaire-mais-un-texte-qui-nest-pas-a-la-hauteur-des-enjeux/>).

**Labelling.** No source found since October 2023 says startups are labelled or registered under a startup law or strategy. The nearest fact found: [the bill provides a labelling mechanism and tax advantages for labelled firms](<https://togoactualite.com/projet-de-loi-sur-linnovation-au-togo-une-ambition-necessaire-mais-un-texte-qui-nest-pas-a-la-hauteur-des-enjeux/>).

**Funded.** No source found since October 2023 says a public fund under the startup law or strategy has financed startups. The nearest fact found concerns FSEL, the bill's support fund for labelled firms. [In June 2025 the digital ministry sought a law firm to draft its rules](<https://www.emploitogo.info/un-consultant-03-07-2025/>).
