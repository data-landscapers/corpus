# South Africa

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** [On 31 May 2024 the Department of Communications and Digital Technologies gazetted the National Policy on Data and Cloud](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202406/50741gen2533.pdf>). It covers national and provincial government and organs of state. [It was issued under section 3(1) of the Electronic Communications Act, 2005](<https://www.ensafrica.com/news/detail/8715/south-africas-new-national-cloud-and-data-pol/>). [In October 2024 the department listed the drafting of a Data and Cloud Strategy among its work](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/media-statements-releases/532-south-africa-s-significant-improvement-in-the-united-nations-e-government-index-2024.html>). [In September 2024 National Treasury said it had considered the policy and planned cloud-based licences for its financial system](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/publications/other/MinAnsw/2024/PQ%20792_244_%20Bateman__NW1017E%20_FINAL.pdf>). [In June 2025 the deputy minister cited the 2024 policy in a speech to BRICS communications ministers](<https://www.dcdt.gov.za/deputy-minister-speeches/596-opening-remarks-by-the-deputy-minister-of-communications-and-digital-technologies-hon-mondli-gungubele-at-the-11th-brics-communications-ministers-meeting.html>).

**Named hosting and moves.** [The policy says government data shall be stored in unified, cloud-enabled data centres in designated locations](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202406/50741gen2533.pdf>). It names the State Information Technology Agency as the body that sources cloud services for government. It also makes cloud the first option for new ICT purchases. [A law firm wrote in June 2024 that the policy still needs implementing with the agency and departments](<https://www.ensafrica.com/news/detail/8715/south-africas-new-national-cloud-and-data-pol/>). [In June 2025 National Treasury told Parliament it had begun talks with Oracle on moving its financial system to a cloud platform](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/30920/>). It said the move was in line with the government's cloud computing policy.
