# Ghana

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Ghana has a framework in preparation. On 26 June 2024 Smart Africa and the National Information Technology Agency [held a validation workshop in Accra to gather stakeholders' input for an enhanced regulatory framework for cloud and data centres](<https://moc.gov.gh/2024/06/26/government-working-to-host-all-data-centres-locally/>), the Ministry of Communications reported. The minister said the framework would help make Ghana the preferred place to host African data.

**Named hosting and moves.** After the [workshop](<https://moc.gov.gh/2024/06/26/government-working-to-host-all-data-centres-locally/>) the minister said the government is working to have all its data held in other countries hosted in Ghana. Data produced in the country must be stored there, she said, and the large cloud operators are welcome to build data centres in Ghana; paying to host data abroad, to have it managed and to retrieve it means paying twice. She said local data centres would also create jobs.
