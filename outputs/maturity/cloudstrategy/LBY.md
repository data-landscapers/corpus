# Libya

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Libya has a policy in draft. The General Information Authority [held a consultative meeting with ESCWA, ministries and representatives of the private sector and civil society to review the proposed National Cloud Computing Policy](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/01/01/the-general-information-authority-presents-the-proposed-national-cloud-computing-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>), reported in January 2026. The policy is described as a unified framework for hosting government data and services, and the meeting agreed to incorporate the feedback into its final version. [ESCWA reports a further consultative workshop on the draft on 30 December 2025](<https://www.unescwa.org/news/advancing-national-cloud-computing-policy-libya>), held to gather public institutions' views before adoption.

**Named hosting and moves.** The [proposed policy](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/01/01/the-general-information-authority-presents-the-proposed-national-cloud-computing-policy-in-collaboration-with-escwa/>) aims to set one national framework for hosting government data and services, as part of the authority's work on digital infrastructure and the security of national data. The meeting heard a presentation on fitting cloud technology to the needs of Libyan institutions, and ESCWA, the United Nations commission that attended, acts as the authority's consulting partner on the policy.
