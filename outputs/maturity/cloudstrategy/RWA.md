# Rwanda

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Rwanda's rule is [Ministerial Instructions N° 001/2012, issued by the Minister in the Office of the President in Charge of ICT and in force from their signature on 12 March 2012](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/min/2012/1/eng@2012-03-12>). They include a rule on hosting critical government data and systems, and state no end date and no replacement.

**Named hosting and moves.** [Article 17 of the instructions requires all government IT systems and applications that process, store or provide critical government data to be hosted in the National Data Center](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/min/2012/1/eng@2012-03-12>), website, email and shared applications included. Article 18 requires institutions that keep data centres of their own to arrange disaster recovery from the National Data Center. The instructions name the centre as the one place for critical systems, and both articles have stood since 2012.
