# Kenya

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The [Kenya Cloud Policy, 2025 was signed on 7 April 2025 and took effect on 2 May 2025 on publication in the Kenya Gazette](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/29668-kenya-cloud-policy-2025-entered-into-force-including-authorisation-of-cloud-computing-services>). It [requires government data classified as sensitive, top secret, secret or restricted to be stored within Kenya](<https://africaintheroom.substack.com/p/kenyan-citizens-took-a-25-billion>). Implementation guidelines published by the Ministry of ICT and the Digital Economy [bar public entities from hosting systems and data in their own server rooms without the authorisation of a Cloud Adoption Committee, and give all State organs 24 months to migrate](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>) systems that are not exempted.

**Named hosting and moves.** As reported on 6 August 2026, [applications for accreditation as a government cloud service provider are open, to a Cloud Adoption Committee that is yet to be established](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). One provider is to be chosen from the accredited list to host all State data, and the list of eligible providers is yet to be published. [In August 2026 the Cabinet Secretary said the guidelines require institutions to classify their data and buy only from accredited providers](<https://gaa.go.ke/ministry-urges-public-sector-adopt-ai-cloud-computing>).
