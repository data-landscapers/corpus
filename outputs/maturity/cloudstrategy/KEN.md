# Kenya

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The [Kenya Cloud Policy, 2025 was signed on 7 April 2025 and took effect on 2 May 2025](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/29668-kenya-cloud-policy-2025-entered-into-force-including-authorisation-of-cloud-computing-services>), on publication in the Kenya Gazette. It [requires sensitive, secret and restricted government data to be stored in Kenya](<https://africaintheroom.substack.com/p/kenyan-citizens-took-a-25-billion>). Its implementation guidelines [bar public entities from hosting in their own server rooms without authorisation](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). State organs have 24 months to migrate systems that are not exempted. A [draft National Data Governance Policy, 2026 contemplates a tiered sovereign cloud](<https://cioafrica.co/ai-governance-fragmentation-cause-for-concern/>). Its consultation closed on 5 June 2026.

**Named hosting and moves.** In August 2026 [applications to be an accredited government cloud provider were open](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). The Cloud Adoption Committee that judges them was not yet set up. One accredited provider is to host all State data. [The Cabinet Secretary said in August 2026 that institutions must buy only from accredited providers](<https://gaa.go.ke/ministry-urges-public-sector-adopt-ai-cloud-computing>).
