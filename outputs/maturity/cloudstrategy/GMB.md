# Gambia

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The Gambia has a [Government Cloud Policy, with a strategy and an implementation plan, dated 2023](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy lists it among the country's policies, strategies and legal frameworks, beside the e-government strategy for 2021 to 2024 and the Digital Economy Masterplan for 2024 to 2034. The list also holds an open data strategy for 2024 to 2027 and a data protection and privacy bill.

**Named hosting and moves.** The ministry says the [Government Cloud Strategy for 2023 to 2027 builds on the cloud policy and aims to modernise government service delivery through cloud adoption](<https://publicadministration.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/list-of-files/2025/1.2.1%29%20data%20governance%20-mocde.pdf>). The strategy sets goals, objectives and expected outcomes, and addresses the difficulties of adopting cloud. The same presentation lists underdeveloped data centres and limited cloud infrastructure among the bottlenecks to the country's digital transformation.
