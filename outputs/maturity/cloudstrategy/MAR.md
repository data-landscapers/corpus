# Morocco

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Morocco [has a Cloud First Policy under Digital Morocco 2030, which makes cloud the mandatory default for all public-administration technology investments](<https://northafricapost.com/92626-moroccos-cloud-first-policy-transforms-digital-governance-and-economic-strategy.html>), the ministry's director of cloud infrastructure said in 2025. In a white paper of November 2025 the delegate minister for digital transition said her ministry [carries a cloud roadmap for 2025 to 2030, with six objectives and 21 domains, and is putting a public-sector Cloud First policy in place](<https://ausimaroc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Livre-Blanc-RDV-AUSIM-27-nov.025-Cloud-au-Maroc-Kiosque-digital.pdf>). [In November 2025 the director said the Cloud First Policy is backed by a circular of the head of government](<https://leseco.ma/business/strategie-cloud-2025-2030-le-maroc-trace-la-voie-pour-ses-administrations-publiques-video.html>), and [the minister told parliament in December 2025 that it would become the default progressively](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/seghrouchni-la-feuille-de-route-cloud-2025-2030-renforce-la-souverainete-numerique-du-royaume/>).

**Named hosting and moves.** The same [white paper](<https://ausimaroc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Livre-Blanc-RDV-AUSIM-27-nov.025-Cloud-au-Maroc-Kiosque-digital.pdf>), published by AUSIM, describes the cloud roadmap for 2025 to 2030 as not yet published. It says a national reference architecture is still to be defined, from 2026. The director of cloud infrastructure's account is that [cloud is now the default for every technology investment by the public administration](<https://northafricapost.com/92626-moroccos-cloud-first-policy-transforms-digital-governance-and-economic-strategy.html>).
