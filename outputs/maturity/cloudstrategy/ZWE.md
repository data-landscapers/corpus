# Zimbabwe

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Zimbabwe has a government cloud policy announced but not yet adopted. At the first workshop of government ICT directors in May 2026, the ICT minister, Tatenda Mavetera, [set out a Zimbabwe Government Cloud as a companion policy to a cloud-first and mobile-first direction](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>), to end the practice of ministries buying their own servers and to consolidate the state's infrastructure. The same address outlined a legislative agenda to back the directives. No adopted policy, circular or regulation is held.

**Named hosting and moves.** Nothing held names a government cloud or data centre in service that public bodies are told to use, or a list of approved providers. The nearest held fact is [the minister's announcement of the Zimbabwe Government Cloud](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>), which is described as the intended shared platform for ministries; the report does not say whether it is running or whether any ministry has moved onto it.
