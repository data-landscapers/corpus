# Senegal

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Commissioning the Diamniadio National Datacenter in June 2021, President Macky Sall [instructed the government to migrate all state data and platforms to it and to repatriate national data hosted outside the country](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/senegal-to-migrate-all-government-data-and-applications-to-new-government-data-center/>). State-owned businesses such as the electricity company Senelec are to move their data too, he said. A later account says Senegal [then required all servers to be housed within its borders](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>).

**Named hosting and moves.** The instruction names one place: the [Diamniadio National Datacenter, built with Huawei on a Chinese loan at a cost given as $18.2 million](<https://africachinainitiative.georgetown.edu/research-working-group/blog-posts/huawei-helping-or-hindering-african-countries-secure-their-data-sovereignty-2/>). It is [managed by the state IT agency ADIE, and all government data and applications are to be migrated to it and hosted there](<https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/senegal-to-migrate-all-government-data-and-applications-to-new-government-data-center/>). The 2021 report gave its hosting capacity as 500 square metres and said its first phase would open in about six months.
