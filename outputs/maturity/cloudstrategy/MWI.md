# Malawi

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Malawi has no cloud policy in force. A World Bank status report on the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project [records zero government data governance frameworks, data classification and cloud policies included, enacted and mandated for use as at 30 June 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100325094017738/pdf/P505095-2fb01803-deaa-46f1-8e6f-5bc277a0b2ce.pdf>). The count is one of the measures under the project's first component, affordable broadband and secure data hosting.

**Named hosting and moves.** The same [status report](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100325094017738/pdf/P505095-2fb01803-deaa-46f1-8e6f-5bc277a0b2ce.pdf>) sets a target of three such frameworks enacted and mandated for use by October 2030. It records the national data centre in Lilongwe as complete, paid for under an earlier project, with the first few clients migrated to it. The report rates the project's progress as satisfactory and says 19 percent of its money had been disbursed.
