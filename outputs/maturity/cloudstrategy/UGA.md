# Uganda

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** [NITA-U issued the National Guidelines on Cloud Computing for Government, Ministries, Departments and Agencies in October 2018](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2022-01/The%20Cloud%20Computing%20Guidelines%20for%20GoU.pdf>), requiring all ministries, departments, agencies and local governments to adhere to Cloud First for IT-enabled services. The document gives no end date. It is version CPG1 of the guidelines and applies to the government bodies it lists.

**Named hosting and moves.** The same guidelines [require those bodies to host information assets classed Official, Secret and Top Secret in the Government Cloud, the National Data Center run by NITA-U](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2022-01/The%20Cloud%20Computing%20Guidelines%20for%20GoU.pdf>). They must also use it when procuring new ICT, and migrate existing classified services to it at contract expiry or refresh. NITA-U can approve offshore exceptions. The Government Cloud serves ministries, departments, agencies and local governments. NITA-U's 2023 statistical abstract lists the National Data Centre as serving 95 government entities in FY2022/23.
