# Mauritius

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Procurement Policy Office's Directive No. 53 took effect on 15 December 2020](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%2053.pdf>). [It was signed on 10 December 2020 and governs how public bodies buy ICT solutions](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%2053.pdf>). It sets where government systems are to be hosted. It applies to every public body that buys an ICT solution. It has no end date, and no later source has been found replacing it.

**Named hosting and moves.** [The directive names the Government Online Centre as the hosting for common-use ICT solutions](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%2053.pdf>). [Other data centres or clouds may be used only with the ministry's approval](<https://ppo.govmu.org/Documents/Directives/Directives%202020/Directive%2053.pdf>). The directive gives no count of the bodies hosted there. No later source has been found naming the bodies that have moved onto it, or counting them.
