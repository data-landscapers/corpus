# Nigeria

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** On 17 August 2026 the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy [released the National Digital Cloud Policy](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/18/nigerias-new-cloud-policy-puts-government-at-the-centre-of-its-cloud-market/>), which [supersedes the Nigeria Cloud Computing Policy 2019, the policy that first introduced Cloud First](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/18/nigerias-new-cloud-policy-puts-government-at-the-centre-of-its-cloud-market/>). The state IT agency NITDA [signed the National Cloud Computing Guideline, a technical guideline and an assurance framework earlier that month](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/05/nitda-signs-sovereign-cloud-framework-to-boost-ai-data-centre-investment/>). The new policy [targets US$750 million in private cloud and data-centre investment](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/nigeria-launches-national-cloud-taskforce/O2rQGMAERjoMd1ea>). [A May 2026 release from the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority says the National Cloud Policy 2025 requires sensitive government and financial data to be hosted in the country](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>).

**Named hosting and moves.** [Part II of the policy designates the state-owned Galaxy Backbone the primary digital infrastructure provider for government, aggregating its cloud demand and operating shared government platforms, with only certified providers listed on the National Digital Marketplace](<https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/it-and-internet/1851136/a-review-of-nigerias-national-digital-cloud-policy-new-direction-and-implication-for-businesses>). Galaxy Backbone [will aggregate government cloud demand from registered providers and negotiate framework agreements, with buying through a National Digital Marketplace on which NITDA registers providers](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/18/nigerias-new-cloud-policy-puts-government-at-the-centre-of-its-cloud-market/>). A 24-month roadmap plans the migration of priority ministries in months six to twelve. NITDA's director-general said in August 2026 that [the Cloud First policy remains in force](<https://punchng.com/nigeria-seeks-local-cloud-capacity-to-cut-data-costs/>).
