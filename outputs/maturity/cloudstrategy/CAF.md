# Central African Republic

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The Central African Republic has a hosting policy in preparation. On 19 May 2026 the Public Sector Digital Governance Programme (PGNSP) [held a workshop on preparing a National Data Hosting Strategy](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/martial-freddy-amakpio-bemade-80b83444_gouvernancenum%C3%A9rique-cloudcomputing-souverainet%C3%A9num%C3%A9rique-activity-7462633363468722176-u68g>), as a participant reported it. No adopted text has been found. A World Bank-financed project had set a government-wide cloud strategy as one of its targets, and [its September 2023 progress report recorded that no strategy had been developed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121223050026849/txt/P17462005b395404b0998a03dc83a08ea5d.txt>), only that one was being discussed by the government, against a target date of December 2026.

**Named hosting and moves.** Nothing found names a government cloud or data centre that public bodies must use, or a list of approved providers. The nearest held fact is [the May 2026 workshop on the data hosting strategy](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/martial-freddy-amakpio-bemade-80b83444_gouvernancenum%C3%A9rique-cloudcomputing-souverainet%C3%A9num%C3%A9rique-activity-7462633363468722176-u68g>), run under the PGNSP, which is the programme through which the strategy is being drawn up; the workshop's report does not say where the strategy will tell ministries and agencies to host their systems or data.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a government hosting or cloud policy is adopted and in force.
