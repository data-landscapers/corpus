# Cote d'Ivoire

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The ANSUT document [Politique d'hébergement des données dans le cloud, version 1.0 dated 14 June 2021, was approved on 17 June 2021](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174558607847.pdf>) by ANSUT's Directeur de l'Exploitation. It sets the procedure for ministries and institutions to host applications in the cloud. No end or replacement is stated. The document is version 1.0, dated 14 June 2021 and approved three days later, and ANSUT and the Ministry of Digital Transition are its listed owners.

**Named hosting and moves.** The 2021 document is [a procedure for ministries and institutions hosting applications in the cloud](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174558607847.pdf>), issued by ANSUT, the national digital agency under the Ministry of Digital Transition. It names a Microsoft Azure public cloud, bought through the eGOUV licence contract, and the Datacenter eGOUV, both run by ANSUT for national use across ministries, institutions and public administration. The document is the only source for both.
