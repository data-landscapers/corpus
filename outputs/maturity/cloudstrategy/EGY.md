# Egypt

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The Supreme Council of Digital Society [adopted the Cloud First Policy on 13 March 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>). It requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted only within Egypt. The policy, with that requirement, [entered into force on 22 August 2024](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>). The council had been reconstituted under the Prime Minister. At its first meeting after that, the Cabinet's spokesperson said, it [approved and launched the cloud computing policy in line with the Digital Egypt strategy](<https://cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/75421>).

**Named hosting and moves.** The ICT ministry is [preparing a national data-centre strategy with other ministries and bodies](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/20/رئيس-الوزراء-يتابع-مستجدات-العمل-لعدد-من-مشروعات-تكنولوجيا-المعلومات/7552399>), the minister told a cabinet meeting on 20 September 2026. It is to expand data centres and their capacity inside Egypt. On 8 September he said it [covers storage and compute needs, investment, regulation, standards, skills and digital sovereignty](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/08/egypt-vodafone-elsewedy-cassava-1bn-data-centre/>). In July he called it [a core component of digital sovereignty](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5814629.aspx>). [In May 2024 the ICT minister said a government committee hosted by his ministry was preparing a plan](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/3175247>). The plan is for state bodies to use the policy.
