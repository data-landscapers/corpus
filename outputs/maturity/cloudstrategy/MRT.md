# Mauritania

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** On 30 July 2025 Mauritania's data-protection authority [issued deliberation 005/2025 on the minimum conditions for using cloud services abroad](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7/>). It requires public and private data controllers to obtain the authority's authorisation before processing personal data on foreign cloud services, and to write set clauses into the provider contract: compliance with law 2017-020, transfer abroad, retrieval of the data and its permanent deletion. The authority's meeting of 29 April 2026 [had amendments to some of the deliberation's articles on its agenda](<https://www.apd.mr/fr/reunion-mensuelle-ordinaire-de-lautorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-16/>).

**Named hosting and moves.** The [amended text of the deliberation](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-005-2025-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%91%d9%82-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b4%d8%b1%d9%88%d8%b7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%af%d9%86%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a7/>) was published on 30 April 2026. It governs the use of cloud services located abroad, by prior authorisation from the authority, and covers the return of data when a contract expires. It binds public bodies and private firms alike.
