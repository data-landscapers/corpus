# Botswana

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Botswana has no hosting or cloud policy in force. In February 2025 the Deputy Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Communications and Innovation said [the government was developing digital and cloud computing policies, alongside policies on artificial intelligence and the digital economy](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/84714>), to make the country a destination for technology investment and start-ups. He spoke at the launch of a digital facilities management firm in Gaborone, where he also said [the government was committed to extending ICT infrastructure so that innovators across the country could reach the digital tools they need](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/84714>). He gave no date for the policies' adoption and did not say who was drafting them.

**Named hosting and moves.** The same February 2025 statement [names no government cloud or data centre that public bodies must use](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/84714>), no list of approved providers and no public body moving its systems.

***Also on file***. [Digital Delta Data Centre (DDDC)](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/digital-delta-data-centre-to-improve-public-service-delivery/news>); [Delta Digital Data Centre](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90463>).

***Not found***. No source found says a government hosting or cloud policy is adopted and in force.
