# Burkina Faso

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The government's ["Zéro donnée à l'extérieur" programme, one of twelve digital-transformation programmes to 2030, aims to host platforms and data on national territory](<https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/>); the Prime Minister's half-year evaluation of 25 July 2026 reported it as under way. It was [first set out in a roadmap of 12 digital projects at a government seminar on 25 August 2025](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burkina-faso-outlines-12-digital-reforms-to-drive-transformation-by-2030>). A [Prime Minister's circular tells public bodies to give priority to national hosting](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144800>), and requests to operate a platform are screened for where the data is hosted, the director general of digital transformation said in March 2026.

**Named hosting and moves.** The [two government-cloud data centres the Prime Minister inaugurated on 23 January 2026 are to host all public data](<https://primature.gov.bf/souverainete-numerique-le-premier-ministre-inaugure-deux-datacenters-2/>). The state ICT agency's board [set the repatriation of administrations' data to them as its course for 2026](<https://anptic.gov.bf/actualites/lanptic-tient-sa-1ere-session-ordinaire-du-conseil-dadministration-de-lannee-2026/>). By July 2026 the ministry had [inventoried 120 public platforms for hosting in the country, with repatriation begun and due to finish before the end of 2026](<https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/>).
