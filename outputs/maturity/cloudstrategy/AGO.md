# Angola

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Angola has no hosting policy in force. In March 2026 the Instituto de Modernização Administrativa [issued terms of reference for a five-month consultancy to draft a national policy on the management and hosting of public data, with its implementing regulation](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/TDR_Contratacao_de_Servico_de_Consultoria_Apoio_Estrategico_e_Juridico_a810d7f256.pdf>). The policy is to set hosting rules by category of data, covering on-premises systems, a government or sovereign cloud, hybrid arrangements and the conditions for using public cloud, together with rules on where data must reside.

**Named hosting and moves.** A report of November 2025 has the government saying that [all government services must migrate to the national data centre within 30 days of its inauguration](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1411-50505-angola-nears-completion-of-national-data-center-set-for-2026-launch>). The data centre is being developed alongside a unified government cloud, at a combined cost of about USD 90 million.
