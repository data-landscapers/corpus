# Mozambique

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Mozambique has a framework being drafted. In December 2025 the Minister of Communications and Digital Transformation said the government is [drafting an updated regulatory framework for cloud adoption, to replace current legal restrictions](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/04/mozambique-to-build-local-data-centres-and-new-regulations-for-cloud-adoption/>). He said cloud adoption should be treated as a national priority reaching well beyond the financial industry. A [Cloud Computing Regulation approved by Decree No. 72/2025 of 31 December 2025](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation>) licenses cloud providers.

**Named hosting and moves.** Alongside the framework the minister [said the government plans over five years to establish domestic data centres able to host cloud services securely for banks and other institutions](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/04/mozambique-to-build-local-data-centres-and-new-regulations-for-cloud-adoption/>). He was speaking at a bank's anniversary event in Maputo. The [law firm's account of the December 2025 decree](<https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2026/Approval-of-the-Data-Centre-Regulation>) describes the licensing of providers and no rule on where public bodies host.
