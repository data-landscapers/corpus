# Tunisia

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** A [ministerial order of 13 September 2023 requires public bodies' software, platforms and infrastructure to be hosted in-house or with cloud providers according to four tiers](<https://www.ancs.tn/sites/default/files/2024-04/Arrete2023_cloud_fr.pdf>), any other arrangement needing the cybersecurity agency's approval. [Circulaire n° 16 of 22 July 2024, from the Head of Government to ministers, governors and heads of public bodies, generalises cloud adoption under the "TN Government Cloud First Policy"](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/circulaire-n-16-du-22-juillet-2024-portant-sur-la-generalisation-de-ladoption-par-les-structures-publiques-des-solutions-de-cloud-computing-pour-lhebergement-des-logiciels-de/>). A [circular of 2 September 2026 tightens the hosting requirements](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/22/573106/cybersecurite-et-entreprises-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-publiques-et-leurs-fournisseurs-prives/>).

**Named hosting and moves.** The 2024 circular [sends sensitive internal systems to a private cloud or to national or sectoral government cloud centres, shared government systems to government cloud centres, and public e-services to the body's private cloud, government cloud centres or providers holding the N-Cloud label](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/circulaire-n-16-du-22-juillet-2024-portant-sur-la-generalisation-de-ladoption-par-les-structures-publiques-des-solutions-de-cloud-computing-pour-lhebergement-des-logiciels-de/>). The cybersecurity agency [published its list of labelled providers on 17 September 2026, with six N-Cloud entries](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/22/573106/cybersecurite-et-entreprises-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-publiques-et-leurs-fournisseurs-prives/>). [Two providers received the G-Cloud label in July 2024](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>); [the agency's November 2025 register listed seven N-Cloud providers and no G-Cloud label](<https://www.ancs.tn/sites/default/files/2025-11/liste-label-cloud-ANCS-fr-201125.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [G-Cloud label (government cloud service provider)](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/circulaire-n-16-du-22-juillet-2024-portant-sur-la-generalisation-de-ladoption-par-les-structures-publiques-des-solutions-de-cloud-computing-pour-lhebergement-des-logiciels-de/>); [N-Cloud label (national cloud service provider)](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/circulaire-n-16-du-22-juillet-2024-portant-sur-la-generalisation-de-ladoption-par-les-structures-publiques-des-solutions-de-cloud-computing-pour-lhebergement-des-logiciels-de/>); [National and sectoral government cloud centres](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/circulaire-n-16-du-22-juillet-2024-portant-sur-la-generalisation-de-ladoption-par-les-structures-publiques-des-solutions-de-cloud-computing-pour-lhebergement-des-logiciels-de/>); [CNI private cloud](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/22/573106/cybersecurite-et-entreprises-ce-qui-va-changer-pour-les-entreprises-publiques-et-leurs-fournisseurs-prives/>).

***Not held***. No source from the last three years naming or counting public bodies that have moved onto the named hosting is held.
