# DR Congo

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** The Digital Transformation Project terms of reference of [March 2026 say the government, helped by UNECA and GIZ, is developing a national data hosting strategy](<https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_02_05.pdf>), under way mainly at central level. They seek consultants to define the hosting approach, for example cloud-first or cloud-appropriate, and to propose hosting and cloud decrees and directives. [CIO Mag, 8 November 2023, reports that the Council of Ministers had adopted a draft decree on the management of national datacenters](<https://cio-mag.com/rdc-inauguration-du-premier-datacenter-national/>) a year earlier. The article does not say the decree was later issued.

**Named hosting and moves.** The same [March 2026 terms of reference say public-sector data hosting is fragmented, heterogeneous and insufficiently pooled, with no coherent national framework for classification, governance and pooling](<https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_02_05.pdf>). They add that norms on sovereign hosting remain embryonic or inoperative.
