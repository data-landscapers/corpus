# Ethiopia

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** Ethiopia has a cloud policy in draft. In May 2026 the Information Network Security Administration (INSA) [held an internal forum on a proposed National Cloud Security Governance Framework](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-cloudsecurity-cybersecurity-activity-7466419690207277056-34la>), the national framework it is preparing for the security and governance of cloud services. No adopted text has been found, and no source found gives a date by which the framework is to be approved or the body that will approve it. INSA is the government's information and network security agency, and the framework is its own proposal.

**Named hosting and moves.** Nothing found names a government cloud or data centre that the framework tells public bodies to use, or a list of approved providers. The nearest held fact is [INSA's forum on the proposed framework](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-cloudsecurity-cybersecurity-activity-7466419690207277056-34la>), which concerns how cloud services are secured and governed; the forum's report does not say where ministries and agencies are to host their systems or data, or name any move of public bodies onto a named service.

***Also on file***. [own cloud infrastructure (built under Digital Ethiopia 2025)](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/digital-ethiopia-2030-launch>); [Gotera Cloud (G-Cloud)](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_digitalethiopia2030-insa-ethiopia-activity-7446822764042293248-2bIo>).

***Not found***. No source found says a government hosting or cloud policy is adopted and in force.
