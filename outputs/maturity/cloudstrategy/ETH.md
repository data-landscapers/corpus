# Ethiopia

## Government hosting and cloud policy

**Policy adopted.** [In November 2026 the innovation minister restated existing federal directives for public institutions to move off their own server rooms](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8212/x>). They are to use shared national infrastructure instead. She said shared infrastructure would cut duplicated spending on costly equipment. [In May 2026 the security agency INSA held a forum on a proposed National Cloud Security Governance Framework](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/information-network-security-administration_insa-cloudsecurity-cybersecurity-activity-7466419690207277056-34la>), which covers how cloud services are secured and governed.

**Named hosting and moves.** [The National Cloud Data Center at the ICT Park in Addis Ababa is to host government services](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8212/x>). It was still under construction in November 2026, when the minister ordered the work sped up and called for common standards. No source found names a public body that has moved onto shared national hosting.
