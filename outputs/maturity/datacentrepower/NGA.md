# Nigeria

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Kasi Cloud Datacenters commissioned its Lekki campus in Lagos on 19 May 2026, and the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority called its first building, LOS1, open for business; the campus lists a direct 132kV connection to the Transmission Company of Nigeria and hybrid gas, solar and battery supply with N+1 redundancy](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>). Phased commissioning was to continue toward full commercial operation. No source found shows power set aside for data centres by contract, plant or tariff.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [Rack Centre's spec sheet, published in May 2024, gives a design power usage effectiveness of 1.5 for the cooling at its Lagos facility LGS1](<https://rack-centre.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/rack-centre-spec-sheet-and-QR-code.pdf>). [Kasi gives a PUE target of 1.6 or less for its Lekki campus](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found gives a Nigerian data centre's water use or says its cooling uses no water. The nearest held fact is [Rack Centre's design PUE of 1.5 for LGS1](<https://rack-centre.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/rack-centre-spec-sheet-and-QR-code.pdf>).
