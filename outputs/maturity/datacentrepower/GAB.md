# Gabon

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Officials said a photovoltaic power plant provides nearly 22% of the energy of Gabon's sovereign data centre, inaugurated by the president, which also runs on a 15kV main line with N+1 backup units](<https://itweb.africa/article/gabon-launches-sovereign-data-centre/xnklOvz14JDq4Ymz>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.
