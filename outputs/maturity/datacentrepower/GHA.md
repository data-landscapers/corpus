# Ghana

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Onix Data Centres says its Accra facility, with Module 1 fully operational, takes its power from its own 2,200-panel solar farm (640 kW peak) by day and the grid at night, with 3 x 456 kW generators as backup](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No source found from the past three years states a PUE of 1.6 or less for [Onix Data Centre, Accra](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>) or any other data centre in service.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found from the past three years states the water use of [Onix Data Centre, Accra](<https://onixdatacentres.com/home/infrastructure/>) or any other data centre in service, or cooling that uses none.
