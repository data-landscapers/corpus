# Congo

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** On 27 October 2026 the regulator ARPCE said [nationwide power cuts and scarce diesel were badly disrupting data centres](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/crise-du-carburant-les-reseaux-telecoms-bout-de-souffle-172163>), with network sites and core networks. A plant for data centres is being built, not yet in service. On 22 September 2026 the government said [works on the national data centre stood at 82%](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-infrastructures-le-data-center-operationnelle-dici-fin-2026/>). The 12 MW Kintelé solar farm, with 19,354 panels, [is being built to keep its power stable](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-infrastructures-le-data-center-operationnelle-dici-fin-2026/>).

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No PUE is on record for a data centre in Congo. The nearest fact is the [national data centre, 82% built in September 2026](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-infrastructures-le-data-center-operationnelle-dici-fin-2026/>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No water figure, and no cooling that uses none, is on record for a data centre in Congo. The nearest fact is ARPCE's warning that [power cuts and diesel shortages were disrupting data centres](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/crise-du-carburant-les-reseaux-telecoms-bout-de-souffle-172163>) in October 2026.

***Also on file***. [Congo national data centre, Brazzaville](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-infrastructures-le-data-center-operationnelle-dici-fin-2026/>), not yet in service.

***Not found***. No source found says a data centre in service has a secured supply. That would be two grid feeds, its own substation or line, or its own plant.
