# Kenya

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [The Treasury's addendum says the Konza National Data Centre uses 2N utility power with dual independent feeds from Kenya Power plus 2N+1 backup generators and a 2N UPS](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). [The operator's data sheet describes the Nairobi NB01 facility as having a 2N incoming utility supply topology and 37.5 MW of campus capacity secured from the utility, beyond UPS and generator backup](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/9-ADC-Data-Sheet-NB01.pdf>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [IXAfrica's NBOX1 campus on Mombasa Road, in service and hosting the TCS sovereign cloud, operates at a power usage effectiveness of 1.25, in a description attributed to the company](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** [Africa Data Centres' CEO states the company uses no water in any of its cooling systems and bans adiabatic systems, in the release on expanding its existing Sameer facility in Nairobi](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-to-expand-sameer-facility-in-nairobi-by-15mw-of-it-load/>).
