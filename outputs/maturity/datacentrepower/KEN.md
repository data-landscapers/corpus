# Kenya

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [The Treasury says the Konza National Data Centre has dual independent feeds from Kenya Power](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>), with backup generators and UPS. [Africa Data Centres' NB01 in Nairobi has a 2N utility supply and 37.5 MW secured from the utility](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/9-ADC-Data-Sheet-NB01.pdf>). On 12 February 2026 KenGen's chief executive said [the Olkaria green energy park now lets data centres take power direct from the generator](<https://www.the254report.co.ke/p/kengen-extraordinary-general-meeting-egm>). The regulator EPRA has pre-cleared that direct supply. In May 2026 plans for the [Microsoft-G42 data centre at Olkaria stalled over available power](<https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/8/24/can-kenyas-ai-ambitions-coexist-with-naivashas-water-needs>), Al Jazeera reports.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [IXAfrica's NBOX1 campus on Mombasa Road runs at a PUE of 1.25](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>), the company says. It is in service and hosts the TCS sovereign cloud. [The Treasury gives the Konza National Data Centre a PUE of about 1.45](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>) in April 2026.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** The newest statement is from 2022. Africa Data Centres' chief executive said then that [the company uses no water in any of its cooling systems](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-to-expand-sameer-facility-in-nairobi-by-15mw-of-it-load/>), at its Sameer facility in Nairobi. It also bans adiabatic systems.

***Also on file***. [TCS Sovereign Secure Cloud](<https://cioafrica.co/tcs-sovereign-cloud-goes-live-in-nairobi/>). [IXAfrica second hyperscale facility, Nairobi](<https://techtrends.africa/africas-data-centre-boom/>). [Olkaria EcoCLOUD Data Centre](<https://www.citizen.digital/news/geothermal-powered-data-centre-launched-in-olkaria-n326497>). [Microsoft and G42 Naivasha data centre (planned)](<https://techtrends.africa/africas-green-data-centre-opportunity-solar-powered-server-farms/>).

***Not found***. No source from the past three years says a data centre in service publishes its water use. None says one cools without water.
