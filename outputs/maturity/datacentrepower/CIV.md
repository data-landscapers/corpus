# Cote d'Ivoire

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** Raxio's CIV1 data centre in Abidjan opened in September 2024. Its chief technology officer says [it is fed by two independent 15kV feeders direct from the CIE substation](<https://africanreview.com/ict/powering-the-civ1-data-centre-in-cote-d-ivoire>). Each feeder can carry the whole load. In December 2025 he said [Raxio always uses two such lines, plus diesel generators](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>), in every market. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No PUE is on record for a data centre in Cote d'Ivoire. The nearest fact is CIV1's supply: [two 15kV feeders, each able to carry 100% of the load](<https://africanreview.com/ict/powering-the-civ1-data-centre-in-cote-d-ivoire>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No water figure, and no cooling that uses none, is on record for a data centre in Cote d'Ivoire. The nearest fact is Raxio's design: [dedicated medium-voltage lines and standby diesel at each facility](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>).
