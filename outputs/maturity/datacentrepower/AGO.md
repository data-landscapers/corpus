# Angola

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** Raxio Group runs data centres in Angola, as in five other African countries. In December 2025 its chief technology officer said [Raxio builds medium-voltage lines direct from the substation to each of its facilities](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>). He said it [always uses two power lines, each able to carry the load, plus diesel generators](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>). In none of its markets does it rely on the old local grid. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No PUE is on record for a data centre in Angola. The nearest fact is the supply design. Raxio says [its facilities take dedicated medium-voltage lines from the substation](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>), backed by standby diesel.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No water figure, and no cooling that uses none, is on record for a data centre in Angola. The nearest fact is again Raxio's: [two independent power lines and standby diesel at each facility](<https://african.business/2025/12/technology-information/inside-the-race-to-fire-up-africas-power-hungry-data-centres>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says power is set aside for data centres. None names a signed contract, a plant built for them, or a tariff or zone in force.
