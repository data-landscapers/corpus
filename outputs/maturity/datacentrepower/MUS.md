# Mauritius

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Mordor Intelligence says Mauritius Telecom's live Tier IV data centre has N+2 architecture and two independent utility feeds](<https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/mauritius-data-center-market>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No source found from the past three years states a PUE of 1.6 or less for [Mauritius Telecom Tier IV data centre](<https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/mauritius-data-center-market>) or any other data centre in service.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found from the past three years states the water use of [Mauritius Telecom Tier IV data centre](<https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/mauritius-data-center-market>) or any other data centre in service, or cooling that uses none.
