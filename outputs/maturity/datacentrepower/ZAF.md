# South Africa

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Africa Data Centres says it procures clean electricity under a 20-year power purchase agreement for a 12MW solar farm that wheels energy to its Cape Town and Johannesburg facilities](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). [Daily Maverick reports that African Data Centres signed a 12MW power purchase agreement for wind energy, wheeled to three of its South African data centres](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-02-09-major-sa-data-centre-expansion-plans-emerge-set-to-more-than-double-current>). [CPT1 uses wheeled solar power under a 20-year power purchase agreement signed in March 2023 with Distributed Power Africa, part of the Cassava Technologies group](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-announces-additional-6mw-capacity-now-live-in-cape-town/>). [CPT1 uses wheeled solar power under a 20-year power purchase agreement signed in March 2023 with Distributed Power Africa, a Cassava Technologies company](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/africa-data-centres-adding-6mw-capacity-in-cape-town>). These place a data centre on a secured supply, with power set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [Teraco's CEO reported an average annual power usage effectiveness of 1.43 for 2025 across its South African data centres (eight data centres on five campuses)](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** [Equinix South Africa's MD states its local facilities' closed-loop cooling maintains an average water usage effectiveness of 0.1 L/kWh](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). [Teraco states that its completed CT2 data centre in Cape Town uses a zero-water closed-loop cooling system, with zero water used during the cooling process](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/teraco-completes-ct2-data-centre-expansion/>).
