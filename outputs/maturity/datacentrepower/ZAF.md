# South Africa

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Africa Data Centres buys power under a 20-year agreement for a 12MW solar farm](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). The farm wheels energy to its Cape Town and Johannesburg facilities. [Daily Maverick reports a further 12MW wind power agreement](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-02-09-major-sa-data-centre-expansion-plans-emerge-set-to-more-than-double-current>), wheeled to three of its data centres. [CPT1 in Cape Town takes wheeled solar power under an agreement signed in March 2023](<https://www.africadatacentres.com/africa-data-centres-announces-additional-6mw-capacity-now-live-in-cape-town/>). [The supplier is Distributed Power Africa, a Cassava Technologies company](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/data-centers/africa-data-centres-adding-6mw-capacity-in-cape-town>). These place data centres on a secured supply, with power set aside for them.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [Teraco's CEO reported an average yearly PUE of 1.43 for 2025](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>), across eight data centres on five campuses. Its sustainability report gave [1.47 for 2024](<https://www.teraco.co.za/blog/teraco-releases-fourth-annual-sustainability-report/>). Its new CT2 in Cape Town is [designed for a PUE of 1.30](<https://cbn.co.za/industry-news/data-centers-news/teraco-ct2-expands-cape-town-data-centre-capacity-now/>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** [Equinix South Africa's MD says its closed-loop cooling averages 0.1 litres of water per kWh](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-data-centre-operators-push-back-amid-sustainability-probe/5yONP7EroNQMXWrb>). [Teraco gives its water use as 0.04 litres per kWh in 2024, down from 0.10 in 2023](<https://www.teraco.co.za/blog/water-scarcity-in-africa/>). [Its completed CT2 in Cape Town uses a zero-water closed-loop cooling system](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/teraco-completes-ct2-data-centre-expansion/>).
