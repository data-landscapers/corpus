# Cameroon

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** ST Digital's Douala01 data centre has [two utility feeds, one power transformer and three backup generator sets](<https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-ready-v1-site-assessment-v1-0-rev1-2-st-digital-douala01-pdf>). The operator gives these figures in its 2024 OCP Ready self-assessment. Two utility feeds place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres by contract, plant, tariff or zone.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** The same 2024 self-assessment gives Douala01 [a design PUE and a current yearly PUE both under 1.5](<https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-ready-v1-site-assessment-v1-0-rev1-2-st-digital-douala01-pdf>). It is the operator's own figure.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No water figure, and no cooling that uses none, is on record for a data centre in Cameroon. The nearest fact is Douala01's [annual PUE of under 1.5](<https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-ready-v1-site-assessment-v1-0-rev1-2-st-digital-douala01-pdf>), from the same 2024 document.
