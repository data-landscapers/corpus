# Ethiopia

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Raxio's data sheet says the ET1 data centre in Addis Ababa takes a 33kV utility supply directly fed from the nearest substation, with 2N power redundancy and 72 hours of on-site fuel](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/RAX016-4-Data-Sheet-SITE-ET1-v2.pdf>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [Raxio's data sheet states a PUE of 1.3 for the ET1 data centre, with indirect adiabatic cooling](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/RAX016-4-Data-Sheet-SITE-ET1-v2.pdf>). [Ethio telecom's press statement, on launching its Huawei-built Tier III modular data center at Gola Sefer, Addis Ababa, gives the modular data center a power usage effectiveness of 1.4](<https://www.samenacouncil.org/samena_daily_news?news=84581>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found from the past three years states the water use of [Raxio ET1](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/RAX016-4-Data-Sheet-SITE-ET1-v2.pdf>) or any other data centre in service, or cooling that uses none.

***Also on file***. [Raxio](<https://techtrends.africa/africas-data-centre-boom/>); [Bitdeer Oromia bitcoin-mining site](<https://www.bitdeer.com/news/oromia-ethiopia-update>).

***Not found***. No source found says power is set aside for data centres by a signed contract, a plant built for them or a tariff or zone in force.
