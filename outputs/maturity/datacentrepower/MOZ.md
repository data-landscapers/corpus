# Mozambique

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [Raxio's own specification page says the MZ1 data centre in Matola takes a 15 kV utility supply fed directly from the nearest substation with N+N redundancy, with 2N power distribution from MV transformer to rack PDU and 48 hours of on-site fuel](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** [Raxio's own specification page states a PUE of 1.3 for the MZ1 data centre, with indirect adiabatic cooling](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>).

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found from the past three years states the water use of [Raxio Mozambique (MZ1)](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/mozambique/>) or any other data centre in service, or cooling that uses none.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says power is set aside for data centres by a signed contract, a plant built for them or a tariff or zone in force.
