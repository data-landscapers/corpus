# Benin

## Power and water for data centres

**A secured supply in service, and power reserved for data centres.** [CIO Mag reports Benin's data centres run on dual power feeds from the SBEE, plus standby generators](<https://cio-mag.com/datacenters-et-energie-lequation-de-la-transformation-numerique/>). These place a data centre on a secured supply. No source found says power is set aside for data centres.

**A PUE of 1.6 or less published.** No source found from the past three years states a PUE of 1.6 or less for [Benin data centres (ASIN)](<https://cio-mag.com/datacenters-et-energie-lequation-de-la-transformation-numerique/>) or any other data centre in service.

**Water use published, or cooling that uses no water.** No source found from the past three years states the water use of [Benin data centres (ASIN)](<https://cio-mag.com/datacenters-et-energie-lequation-de-la-transformation-numerique/>) or any other data centre in service, or cooling that uses none.

***Also on file***. [Benin data centres (ASIN), one Tier III, about 2 MW](<https://cio-mag.com/datacenters-et-energie-lequation-de-la-transformation-numerique/>).

***Not found***. No source found says power is set aside for data centres by a signed contract, a plant built for them or a tariff or zone in force.
