# Gabon

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** In July 2016 the digital economy ministry announced that it wants a [national broadband promotion strategy and a costed operational plan](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/internet-le-gabon-veut-se-doter-dune-strategie-nationale-de-promotion-du-haut-debit/>) in the coming months. The ministry had launched a broadband study to prepare them. No strategy had yet been adopted. The strategy is titled Stratégie nationale de promotion du Haut débit.

**Money and results.** Nothing found shows money committed or progress published. The digital economy ministry made that announcement in July 2016, on 4 July 2016. It names two documents: a national broadband promotion strategy, and an operational plan with costs.
