# Uganda

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Uganda's [National Broadband Policy 2018](<https://www.itu.int/hub/publication/d-uga-natbrpo-2024/>) is the broadband plan the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance and the ITU describe as under implementation, in a review of it published on 12 January 2026. The review gives no end date for the policy and names no replacement. In June 2025 the ministry validated [fibre deployment guidelines](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/advancing-ict-infrastructure-standards-through-stakeholder-collaboration>) meant to support the policy's implementation.

**Money and results.** The [review of the 2018 National Broadband Policy's implementation](<https://www.itu.int/hub/publication/d-uga-natbrpo-2024/>), published on 12 January 2026 by the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance and the ITU, sets out a matrix covering each of the policy's objectives and strategies. For each it lists the activities undertaken, the outcomes, the level of implementation reported by stakeholders, the challenges met and the measures proposed to mitigate them.
