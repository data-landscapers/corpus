# South Sudan

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** South Sudan's National Communication Authority, as [reported by Eye Radio](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) on 3 July 2025, cited the launch of the National Broadband Roadmap among the developments behind the country's rise in internet users. The Authority names the plan as the National Broadband Roadmap and presents its launch as a development already behind the country, not one still to come. The same [report](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) places the roadmap alongside the Authority's own account of how internet use in South Sudan grew between 2024 and 2025.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact is the Authority's count of internet users, which [Eye Radio reported](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) rose from 1.3 million in 2024 to 1.9 million in 2025. The Authority tied the launch of the National Broadband Roadmap to that rise, and the report gives it as a result of the wider set of developments, not as a figure measured against a target of the plan.
