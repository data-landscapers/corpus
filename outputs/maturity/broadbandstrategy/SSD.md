# South Sudan

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** On 3 July 2025 [Eye Radio reported](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) on South Sudan's National Communication Authority. The Authority cited the launch of the National Broadband Roadmap among the developments behind the country's rise in internet users. The Authority names the plan as the National Broadband Roadmap. It presents its launch as a development already behind the country, not one still to come. The same [report](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) places the roadmap alongside the Authority's own account of how internet use in South Sudan grew between 2024 and 2025.

**Money and results.** [Eye Radio reported](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>) that the Authority's count of internet users rose from 1.3 million in 2024 to 1.9 million in 2025. The Authority tied the launch of the National Broadband Roadmap to that rise. The report gives the rise as a result of the wider set of developments. It does not give it as a figure measured against a target of the plan.
