# Tunisia

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The communication technologies ministry's fibre optic page names a national strategy for very high speed broadband, the Stratégie Nationale pour le Développement du très haut débit, which [dates from 2013](<https://www.mtc.gov.tn/index.php?id=296>). The page sits under the digital infrastructure axis of Tunisie Digitale 2020. It gives no period for the strategy and names nothing that replaces it.

**Money and results.** Under the strategy the ministry [publishes terms of reference](<https://www.mtc.gov.tn/index.php?id=296>) for fibre to the home. They cover network studies, civil works, cabling the access network and cabling buildings, with common annexes. They are meant to set one technical standard for laying fibre in Tunisia and for the studies and works of telecommunications projects. The page states no budget and no figures against the strategy.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No budget line or universal service fund allocation tied to the plan has been found for the last three years. No financed programme tied to it has been found either.
