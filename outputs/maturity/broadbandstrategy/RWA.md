# Rwanda

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The government [updated its National Broadband Policy and Strategy in 2022](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/13149-rwandas-masterclass-in-digital-penetration-a-use-case-for-african-counterparts/>), Telecom Review Africa reported in October 2025. The update ended the commitment to a single wholesale network. It introduced technology neutrality, so that mobile operators can reuse spectrum bands for several technologies at once, 4G and 5G among them.

**Money and results.** To support the change, [more spectrum was allocated](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/reports-and-coverage/13149-rwandas-masterclass-in-digital-penetration-a-use-case-for-african-counterparts/>) in the 700 MHz, 900 MHz, 1800 MHz and 2100 MHz bands, opening the way for more than one operator to build advanced networks. The same report estimates 4G coverage reaching 100% by the end of 2025, with 3G coverage at 99.71%. It sets neither figure against a target of the policy, and it names no money committed to it.
