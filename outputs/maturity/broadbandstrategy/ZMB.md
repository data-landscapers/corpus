# Zambia

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Technology and Science's [National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2027](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-Digital-Transformation-Strategy.pdf>) sets broadband targets, including national broadband coverage of 80 percent and average broadband speed rising from 2.73 to 5.00 mbps. It also lists development of a national broadband master plan. The policy working group at the [Africa Broadband Maps national event](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Africa-BB-Maps-Project-Zambia-National-Event-Report.pdf>) in Lusaka concluded in December 2025 that Zambia lacks a national broadband policy. It recommended formulating one. The targets sit inside the digital strategy. No stand-alone broadband policy has been found.

**Money and results.** The nearest fact on file is the [Africa Broadband Maps project](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Africa-BB-Maps-Project-Zambia-National-Event-Report.pdf>), a national broadband mapping system built in four yearly phases from 2025 to 2028. The government owns it and the regulator ZICTA is its custodian. The report states no broadband targets and no budget.

***Also on file***. [National ICT Policy](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Africa-BB-Maps-Project-Zambia-National-Event-Report.pdf>); [National Digital Transformation Strategy](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Africa-BB-Maps-Project-Zambia-National-Event-Report.pdf>).

***Not found***. No budget line has been found for the plan in the last three years. No universal service fund allocation or financed programme tied to it has been found either.
