# Lesotho

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Lesotho's Minister of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation [signed the Broadband Infrastructure Sharing Policy on 11 June 2026](<https://lestimes.com/?p=90715>), according to Lesotho Times. No plan period is given. Before that, the ministry held a [second draft of a Broadband and Shared Infrastructure Policy and Implementation Plan](<https://www.gov.ls/download/draft-broadband-and-shared-infrastructure-policy_1-0/>), dated 17 February 2025. The Minister of Finance and Development Planning [told Parliament on 19 February 2025](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2025-02/Budget%20Speech%202025%2026%20Final%2019-02-2025.pdf>) that a broadband policy was among those under development.

**Money and results.** No budget, fund allocation or delivery report has been found. The [second draft of the Broadband and Shared Infrastructure Policy and Implementation Plan](<https://www.gov.ls/download/draft-broadband-and-shared-infrastructure-policy_1-0/>) is dated February 2025. The draft includes a monitoring and evaluation framework. The draft states that formal broadband legislation is lacking.
