# Chad

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Chad's government launched Tchad Connexion 2030, which [TechAfrica News reported on 18 July 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/18/chad-launches-1-5b-digital-strategy-to-connect-citizens-by-2030/>) as a national digital strategy running to 2030, aimed at giving most Chadians quality internet access and extending digital coverage across the country. [Biometric Update, on 16 March 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/chad-uses-national-biometric-registry-to-boost-govt-systems-interoperability>), reports that the government is implementing it, with targets of at least 80 percent national internet coverage and about 80 percent mobile penetration.

**Money and results.** The held facts state planned investment and works, not figures against the targets. [Ecofin, on 10 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1012-51293-chad-finalizes-digital-law-to-align-national-framework-with-global-standards>), gives US$1.5bn of planned digital investment. [TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/18/chad-launches-1-5b-digital-strategy-to-connect-citizens-by-2030/>) lists fibre loops, a 509 km fibre link from N'Djamena to the Niger border and satellite coverage as the components of the launch programme.
