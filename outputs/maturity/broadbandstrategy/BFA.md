# Burkina Faso

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Burkina Faso's national plan is the Schéma directeur d'aménagement numérique (SDAN). It is the framework for digital infrastructure and services run by the Ministère de la Transition digitale, des Postes et des Communications électroniques. [Sidwaya reports the minister saying on 24 October 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/ministere-en-charge-de-la-transition-digitale-lobservatoire-de-lamenagement-numerique-lance/>), at the launch of the OAN platform, that the SDAN was adopted in 2019. [Wearetech gives the same year](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/le-burkina-faso-repense-l-amenagement-numerique-de-son-territoire-a-l-horizon-2030>). Neither source states a period for the 2019 document or names a successor to it.

**Money and results.** Nothing has been found on funding or results. [Wearetech reports that ministry and territorial-development representatives met on 6 to 12 September 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/le-burkina-faso-repense-l-amenagement-numerique-de-son-territoire-a-l-horizon-2030>) to consolidate a draft revised SDAN running to 2030. The draft goes next to the national commission for territorial planning (CNADDT).
