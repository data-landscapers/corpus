# Djibouti

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Djibouti's national broadband plan is the Stratégie Nationale du Haut-Débit (SNHD). An editorial in La Nation published on [17 October 2024](<https://www.lanation.dj/editorial-linclusion-numerique-comme-nouvel-horizon-par-ibrahim-miyir-ali/>) says the strategy was recently unveiled. The same editorial describes the SNHD as [aligned with Vision Djibouti 2035](<https://www.lanation.dj/editorial-linclusion-numerique-comme-nouvel-horizon-par-ibrahim-miyir-ali/>), so the strategy is tied to the country's own long-term vision for 2035 and not left as a stand-alone document. The editorial makes it part of its wider argument for digital inclusion.

**Money and results.** No budget or results are held. The nearest held fact concerns the plan's targets and delivery: the same [La Nation editorial](<https://www.lanation.dj/editorial-linclusion-numerique-comme-nouvel-horizon-par-ibrahim-miyir-ali/>) says the SNHD sets out two scenarios for connecting cities by 2027, one reaching ten cities and the other seven, the connection of those cities being the plan's stated aim for that year.
