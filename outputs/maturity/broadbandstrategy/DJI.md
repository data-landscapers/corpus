# Djibouti

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Djibouti's national broadband plan is the Stratégie Nationale du Haut-Débit (SNHD). The Ministry of Communication [launched it on 16 October 2024](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/djibouti-unveils-plan-to-boost-internet-connectivity-and-affordability>) at a workshop, where Minister Radwan Abdillahi Bahdon presented it. An editorial in La Nation describes the SNHD as [aligned with Vision Djibouti 2035](<https://www.lanation.dj/editorial-linclusion-numerique-comme-nouvel-horizon-par-ibrahim-miyir-ali/>), tying the strategy to the country's long-term vision for 2035. The minister said at the launch that the strategy argues for [lower tariffs and doubled speeds](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/djibouti-unveils-plan-to-boost-internet-connectivity-and-affordability>) at no extra cost.

**Money and results.** The strategy is [part of the Djibouti Digital Foundation project](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/telecom/djibouti-unveils-plan-to-boost-internet-connectivity-and-affordability>), which the World Bank supports. Early results reported at the launch were 250 km of fibre laid in the north and more than 100 administrative buildings connected. The [La Nation editorial](<https://www.lanation.dj/editorial-linclusion-numerique-comme-nouvel-horizon-par-ibrahim-miyir-ali/>) says the SNHD sets two scenarios for 2027, connecting ten cities or seven.
