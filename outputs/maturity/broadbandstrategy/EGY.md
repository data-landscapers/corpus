# Egypt

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, through the NTRA, launched the [eMisr National Broadband Plan on 17 November 2011](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/2367>). It was a two-phase plan. The first phase was a Framework for Broadband Development, and the second a Broadband Action Plan. Egypt's own name for it is eMisr National Broadband Plan. The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the NTRA are its named owners, with national scope.

**Money and results.** Nothing found shows money committed or progress published. The [second phase, the Broadband Action Plan, was due in the second quarter of 2012](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/2367>). The first phase was the Framework for Broadband Development. The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the NTRA launched it on 17 November 2011.
