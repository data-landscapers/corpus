# Egypt

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology, through the NTRA, launched the [eMisr National Broadband Plan on 17 November 2011](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/2367>). It was a two-phase plan: the first phase was a Framework for Broadband Development, and the second a Broadband Action Plan. Egypt's own name for it is eMisr National Broadband Plan, and the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the NTRA are its named owners, with national scope.

**Money and results.** Nothing found shows money committed or progress published. The nearest held fact is that the [second phase, the Broadband Action Plan, was due in the second quarter of 2012](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/2367>), after the Framework for Broadband Development that the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology and the NTRA launched on 17 November 2011 as the first phase.
