# Malawi

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Malawi's National Broadband Strategy ran from 2019 to 2023. It [aimed to raise broadband penetration](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0810-49382-malawi-launches-broadband-mapping-system-to-bridge-coverage-gaps>) from 4.5% to 35% and average speeds from 256 Kbps to 5 Mbps, Ecofin Agency reported in October 2025. The report names no plan that follows it.

**Money and results.** The regulator, MACRA, [launched a national broadband mapping system](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0810-49382-malawi-launches-broadband-mapping-system-to-bridge-coverage-gaps>) on 7 October 2025, with support from the International Telecommunication Union and the European Union, to find and fill coverage gaps between 2025 and 2028. Its own figures put 4G coverage at 75.1% by the third quarter of 2024 and mobile broadband subscriptions at 9.4 million, a penetration rate of 47%. Only 18% of Malawians used the internet in 2023. The government licensed Starlink, which began commercial service in July 2023.
