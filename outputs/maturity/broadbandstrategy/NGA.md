# Nigeria

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** A committee led by Funke Opeke of MainOne, with Bashir Gwandu as co-chair, was [inaugurated in December 2019](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>) to develop a five-year broadband strategy through 2025, which produced the National Broadband Plan 2020-2025. In September 2020 BusinessDay NG referred to the [recently updated Nigerian Broadband Plan](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>), and the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) said a grant was in line with it. The plan [expired at the end of December 2025](<https://guardian.ng/technology/broadband-nigeria-misses-2020-2025-target-mulls-new-2025-2030-plan/>), when the NCC's head said talks with the ministry on a third plan had begun. In April 2026 an NCC executive commissioner said the regulator was [drawing up a successor](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/04/30/telecom-operators-push-for-new-policy-as-nnbp-2020-2025-fails-expectations/>); none is reported adopted.

**Money and results.** Citing NCC data, BusinessDay NG reports [broadband penetration of 51.97 percent in December 2025](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>) against the plan's 70 percent target, and 4G at 52.95 percent of mobile subscriptions against a 70 percent goal set for 2023. The only funding held is foreign: a US Trade and Development Agency grant to ipNX, signed on 17 September 2020, to [expand fibre to more than 200,000 residences](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>) in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. Its amount is not stated.
