# Nigeria

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** A committee led by Funke Opeke of MainOne, with Bashir Gwandu as co-chair, was [inaugurated in December 2019](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>) to develop a five-year broadband strategy through 2025, which produced the National Broadband Plan 2020-2025. In September 2020 BusinessDay NG referred to the [recently updated Nigerian Broadband Plan](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>), and the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) said a grant was in line with it. By September 2026 BusinessDay NG said the plan had [ended](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>), with no successor plan named.

**Money and results.** Citing NCC data, BusinessDay NG reports [broadband penetration of 51.97 percent in December 2025](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>) against the plan's 70 percent target, and 4G at 52.95 percent of mobile subscriptions against a 70 percent goal set for 2023. The only funding held is foreign: a US Trade and Development Agency grant to ipNX, signed on 17 September 2020, to [expand fibre to more than 200,000 residences](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>) in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. Its amount is not stated.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Project BRIDGE](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>); [National Digital Economy Policy and Strategy](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>).

***Not held***. No successor to the National Broadband Plan 2020-2025 is held as adopted or being written. The money behind the plan is not stated.
