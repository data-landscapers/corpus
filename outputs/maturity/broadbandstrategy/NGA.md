# Nigeria

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** A committee led by Funke Opeke of MainOne was [inaugurated in December 2019](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>) to write a five-year broadband strategy. It produced the National Broadband Plan 2020-2025. In September 2020 BusinessDay NG called it the [recently updated Nigerian Broadband Plan](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>). The plan [expired at the end of December 2025](<https://guardian.ng/technology/broadband-nigeria-misses-2020-2025-target-mulls-new-2025-2030-plan/>). The head of the Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) then said talks with the ministry on a third plan had begun. In April 2026 an NCC executive commissioner said the regulator was [drawing up a successor](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/04/30/telecom-operators-push-for-new-policy-as-nnbp-2020-2025-fails-expectations/>). None is reported adopted.

**Money and results.** The plan aimed for 70 percent broadband penetration. [NCC data put it at 51.97 percent in December 2025](<https://businessday.ng/pro/article/nigeria-expanded-broadband-but-missed-its-70-penetration-target/>), says BusinessDay NG. 4G was then 52.95 percent of mobile subscriptions, against a 70 percent goal set for 2023. [ThisDay put penetration at 58.22 percent in August 2026](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/11/05/with-58-22-broadband-penetration-nigeria-far-from-attaining-70-target/>). The only funding held is foreign. A US Trade and Development Agency grant to ipNX, signed on 17 September 2020, is to [take fibre to more than 200,000 homes](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/ipnx-ustda-sign-partnership-deal-to-develop-nigerias-ict-infrastructure/>) in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. Its amount is not stated.
