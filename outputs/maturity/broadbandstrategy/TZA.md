# Tanzania

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Tanzania's Ministry of Communication and Information Technology [reported on 26 June 2026](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma) that the Steering Committee overseeing the National High-Speed Internet Communication Strategy (Mkakati wa Taifa wa Mawasiliano ya Intaneti ya Kasi) held its first meeting on 25 June 2026 in Dodoma. The Ministry's Permanent Secretary chaired the meeting. The same [ministry report](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma) gives no period for the strategy. A regulator's presentation of March 2026 lists the [Tanzania National Broadband Strategy 2021-2026](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Tanzania-UMC-workshop.pdf>) among the country's digital policies, with results areas on infrastructure, connectivity and devices.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact on the plan's delivery is that [first meeting of the steering committee](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma), held in Dodoma on 25 June 2026 under the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology. It concerns the oversight of the strategy and says nothing of its targets, funding or results.
