# Tanzania

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** On 26 June 2026 Tanzania's Ministry of Communication and Information Technology [reported](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma) the first meeting of a Steering Committee. The committee oversees the National High-Speed Internet Communication Strategy (Mkakati wa Taifa wa Mawasiliano ya Intaneti ya Kasi). It met on 25 June 2026 in Dodoma. The Ministry's Permanent Secretary chaired the meeting. The same [ministry report](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma) gives no period for the strategy. A regulator's presentation of March 2026 lists the [Tanzania National Broadband Strategy 2021-2026](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Tanzania-UMC-workshop.pdf>) among the country's digital policies. It has results areas on infrastructure, connectivity and devices.

**Money and results.** The ministry reported the [first meeting of the steering committee](https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/news/kamati-ya-uongozi-ya-usimamizi-wa-mkakati-wa-taifa-wa-mawasiliano-ya-intaneti-ya-kasi-yafanya-kikao-cha-kwanza-dodoma), held in Dodoma on 25 June 2026. It was held under the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology. The report concerns the oversight of the strategy. It says nothing of the strategy's targets, funding or results.
