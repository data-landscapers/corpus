# Sierra Leone

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Sierra Leone has a National Broadband Strategy for 2023 to 2028. The Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation held a [validation workshop on it](<https://gleanersl.com/communications-ministry-poise-to-address-broadband-connectivity-challenges/>) on 28 February 2024. [In January 2025 the minister said the government had passed it](<https://moice.gov.sl/minister-salima-outlines-accomplishments-in-the-tech-sector/>). ITEdgeNews [reported on 31 January 2025](<https://www.itedgenews.africa/sierra-leone-partners-with-starlink-to-expand-university-internet/>) that a government agreement to connect universities by satellite is in line with it.

**Money and results.** Under that agreement two ministries [signed with ELCOME SL](<https://www.itedgenews.africa/sierra-leone-partners-with-starlink-to-expand-university-internet/>) to bring Starlink internet to 12 universities. Vocational schools are to follow in a second phase. The minister said three local companies were by then selling Starlink services. The report gives no cost for the agreement. It names no budget line or fund allocation behind the strategy, and sets no figures against its targets.
