# Central African Republic

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The government's [press release of 4 September 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/la-republique-centrafricaine-lance-un-ambitieux-programme-pour-lavenir-302546890.html) launches a USD 12.8bn investment programme built on its National Development Plan 2024-2028. That plan's period, 2024 to 2028, had not ended at that date. Broadband is one part of that larger plan, not a plan of its own. The release sets a stated national target of 3,000 km of fibre-optic deployment by 2028.

**Money and results.** No spending or construction against that target has been found. The same [release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/la-republique-centrafricaine-lance-un-ambitieux-programme-pour-lavenir-302546890.html) gives the 3,000 km of fibre-optic deployment as a target for 2028, inside the USD 12.8bn investment programme. It reports no result delivered at its date. It is the only statement of the plan's delivery found.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No budget line or universal service fund allocation tied to the plan has been found for the last three years. No financed programme tied to it has been found either.
