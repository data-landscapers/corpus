# Botswana

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Transport and Communications issued the [National Broadband Strategy, in its final version of June 2018](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>). The document states that the Government has developed the strategy, and it is the national plan for broadband in Botswana. It gives the Information Age Council the task of guiding implementation and monitoring progress. The June 2018 publication is the date held for the strategy, and no later plan or revision has been found.

**Money and results.** No budget, financing or delivery report for the strategy has been found. The nearest held fact is its targets. The [strategy sets two coverage targets](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>): 2 Mbps for 50% of citizens by the end of 2014, and 20 Mbps for 90% of the population by 2018. The same document says the Information Age Council is to monitor progress against them, but no progress figure has been found.
