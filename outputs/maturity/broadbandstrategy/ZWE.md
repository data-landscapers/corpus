# Zimbabwe

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Zimbabwe has a [National Broadband Plan](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>), which the ICT minister, Dr Tatenda Mavetera, said the government "came up with", as the Herald reported on 12 October 2023. The minister [launched the plan on 7 March 2024](<https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/>) at Somabula; it covers 2020 to 2030. The Herald names the [Ministry of ICT, Postal and Courier Services](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>) as the plan's owner. The minister added that [Potraz had been in sync with Government](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>) in implementing it, and that the plan covers access for government, business, households and individuals.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact on delivery is the minister's statement of October 2023 that [Potraz was implementing the plan alongside Government](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>). The plan's stated aim, as the same report gives it, is to provide broadband access at four levels: government, business, household and individual.

***Also on file***. [Smart Zimbabwe Master Plan](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>); [National ICT Policy](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>).
