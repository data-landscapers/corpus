# Zimbabwe

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Zimbabwe has a [National Broadband Plan](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>), which the ICT minister, Dr Tatenda Mavetera, said the government "came up with". The Herald reported this on 12 October 2023. The minister [launched the plan on 7 March 2024](<https://gemnation.co.zw/mavetera-launches-ict-policies/>) at Somabula. It covers 2020 to 2030. The Herald names the [Ministry of ICT, Postal and Courier Services](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>) as the plan's owner. The minister added that [Potraz had been in sync with Government](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>) in implementing it. The minister said the plan covers access for government, business, households and individuals.

**Money and results.** In October 2023 the minister said [Potraz was implementing the plan alongside Government](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>). The same report gives the plan's stated aim as broadband access at four levels: government, business, household and individual.

***Also on file***. [Smart Zimbabwe Master Plan](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>); [National ICT Policy](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-develops-new-ict-policy/>).
