# South Africa

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** South Africa's national broadband plan is SA Connect, a policy dating from [2013](<https://www.gov.za/documents/notices/electronic-communications-act-international-mobile-telecommunications-roadmap-2>) that sets "broadband for all". The government gazette page on the IMT Roadmap 2026 names that policy. It states no end date or successor for it. In July 2026 the regulator ICASA named the SA Connect Broadband Policy as the existing national policy. ICASA said its draft [IMT Roadmap 2026](<https://www.icasa.org.za/news/2026/icasa-publishes-draft-imt-roadmap-and-implementation-plans>) supports. A [28 July 2026 report](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/icasa-unveils-roadmap-for-south-africas-6g-future/JBwEr7n3WXOM6Db2>) on the same roadmap says ICASA cites the policy for broadband access.

**Money and results.** As an [August 2026 article](<https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-08-03-sas-radio-spectrum-revolution-answers-mobile-network-operators-prayers/>) puts it, the government's SA Connect target is universal broadband access with an average user experience of 100Mbps by 2030. The article says telecoms licensees' spectrum obligations align with that target. It gives no figure for delivery against it.
