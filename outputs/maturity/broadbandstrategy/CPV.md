# Cabo Verde

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Cabo Verde's government presented the National Strategy for the Implementation of 5G on 11 February 2026, and [Resolution No. 33/2026 of 20 February 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/16/cape-verde-launches-national-5g-strategy-to-accelerate-digital-nation-vision/>) formally approved it, according to that report. The same report gives no plan period and names no successor. The strategy had been [launched in public in September 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/09/11/cabo-verde-launches-national-5g-strategy-to-become-digital-hub-by-2030/>), when the government presented it as a proposal. The government's own announcement of the strategy came a day earlier, on [10 September 2025](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-apresenta-estrategia-nacional-para-o-5g-e-ambiciona-ser-hub-digital-regional-ate-2030/>).

**Money and results.** No budget, financing or delivery report for the strategy has been found. The nearest held fact is its targets. When the government [presented the proposed strategy on 10 September 2025](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-apresenta-estrategia-nacional-para-o-5g-e-ambiciona-ser-hub-digital-regional-ate-2030/>), it set a goal of 90% internet penetration by 2026 and regional digital hub status by 2030.
