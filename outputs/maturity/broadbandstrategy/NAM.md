# Namibia

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Minister of Information and Communication Technology, Dr P. Mushelenga, signed the [National Broadband Policy](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2020/7308.pdf>) on 27 July 2020 and issued it to the Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia as policy guidelines under section 7 of the Communications Act 2009. Government Notice No. 189 in Government Gazette No. 7308 published it on 14 August 2020. The National Digital Strategy, launched in 2025 with the Sixth National Development Plan, [charts digital infrastructure](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/>) for 2025/26 to 2029/30. In January 2026 the minister, Emma Theofelus, said the [review of the National Broadband Policy](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/01/namibia-to-roll-out-faster-internet-speeds-from-october-2026/>) was finished and its new targets would apply from October 2026.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact is the National Digital Strategy's target, [stated in February 2026](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/>), of raising internet use from 53% to 90% by 2030, within a plan period that runs from 2025/26 to 2029/30 and had not ended when it was reported.
