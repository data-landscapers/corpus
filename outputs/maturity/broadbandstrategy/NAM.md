# Namibia

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Minister of Information and Communication Technology, Dr P. Mushelenga, signed the [National Broadband Policy](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2020/7308.pdf>) on 27 July 2020. He issued it to the Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia as policy guidelines under section 7 of the Communications Act 2009. Government Notice No. 189 in Government Gazette No. 7308 published it on 14 August 2020. The National Digital Strategy, launched in 2025 with the Sixth National Development Plan, [charts digital infrastructure](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/>) for 2025/26 to 2029/30. In January 2026 the minister, Emma Theofelus, said the [review of the National Broadband Policy](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/01/namibia-to-roll-out-faster-internet-speeds-from-october-2026/>) was finished. [Cabinet approved a revised national broadband policy and implementation plan at its meetings of 13 and 27 October 2026](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/>). [It directed the ICT ministry to begin implementation](<https://neweralive.na/cabinet-briefs-15/>).

**Money and results.** The nearest fact found is a target in the National Digital Strategy, [reported in February 2026](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/28266-towards-a-5g-enabled-digitalized-namibia-in-2026/>). It aims to raise internet use from 53% to 90% by 2030. The strategy runs from 2025/26 to 2029/30.
