# Burundi

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The International Telecommunication Union reported on the national Africa-BB-Maps event held in October 2025. Its report lists the [Stratégie Large Bande 2025, the broadband strategy 2025](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>) in Burundi's legal and regulatory framework. The same report lists a [Universal Service Fund](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>) beside it. It names the telecommunications regulator ARCT and the ICT secretariat SETIC as the [lead bodies](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>). It names no successor to the strategy.

**Money and results.** Nothing is held on money or results. The nearest fact found is the October 2025 event report itself, which [gives no period dates for the strategy and states no broadband targets](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>). It sets out no delivery figures for the plan, and it attaches no budget to the [Universal Service Fund](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>) beside it.

***Also on file***. [Africa-BB-Maps](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>) is the national broadband mapping system run by ARCT. [DQoS](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Bdi-Rapport_Evenement-National-Africa-BB-Maps_28-30-Octobre-2025.pdf>) is a mobile application for quality-of-service data collection run by ARCT.

***Not found***. No budget line or universal service fund allocation tied to the plan has been found for the last three years. No financed programme tied to it has been found either.
