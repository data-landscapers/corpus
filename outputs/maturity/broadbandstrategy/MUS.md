# Mauritius

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Information and Communication Technology issued the [National Broadband Policy 2012-2020 (NBP2012)](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/National-Broadband-Policy-2012-2020.pdf>) in January 2012, as the national policy for broadband in Mauritius. Its stated period ran from 2012 to 2020, so the plan's own dates have passed. The [document](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/National-Broadband-Policy-2012-2020.pdf>) names no successor policy, and it is the only national broadband plan held for Mauritius from any year, including after 2020.

**Money and results.** No budget line, universal service fund allocation or financed programme tied to the plan is held, and no figures published against its targets have been found either. The nearest held fact is the [policy document](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/National-Broadband-Policy-2012-2020.pdf>) itself, which fixes the period to 2020 and says nothing of a later plan or of delivery after that year.
