# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Universal Service Development Fund (FDSU) [unveiled a National Connectivity Strategy 2026-2035](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20678-drc-unveils-10-year-strategy-to-close-connectivity-gaps-through-shared-infrastructure.html>) in August 2026, with a 2030 target of 3,000 localities and 16 million people and further consultations with stakeholders planned. The FDSU's own site [describes a national universal-connectivity strategy](<https://www.fdsu.cd/strategie-nationale-connectivite>) with a 40-site phase 0 and a phase 1 of about 300 sites in five zones, and [a 14 August 2026 report](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/14/dr-congo-plans-national-strategy-to-expand-mobile-coverage-in-underserved-areas/>) says the fund is developing the national strategy to extend connectivity to underserved areas.

**Money and results.** The FDSU signed subsidy agreements with Airtel, Orange, Vodacom and Africell, covering 10 localities each, 40 localities and about 258,000 people in all, as [the first step of the National Connectivity Strategy 2026-2035](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/telecom-regulation/20678-drc-unveils-10-year-strategy-to-close-connectivity-gaps-through-shared-infrastructure.html>), reported by Developing Telecoms on 13 August 2026, with the first localities to be connected under those agreements.
