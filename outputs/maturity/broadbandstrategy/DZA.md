# Algeria

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Post and Telecommunications calls its plan the ["Tout Fibre" strategy](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A5%D9%81%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%B7-%D8%A8>) and aims to generalise fibre by 2027 at the latest. In a [20 February 2026 message](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/02/20/algeria-reaches-milestone-as-3-million-households-connect-to-fibre-broadband/>) the minister said the government had adopted a comprehensive all-fibre communications strategy. A [ministry communique of 30 September 2026](<https://lasentinelle.dz/index.php/2026/10/01/gigacity-2026-alger-premiere-ville-africaine-du-tres-haut-debit/>) says the strategy aims to bring fibre to the whole territory by 2027, with the copper network phased out by the same date.

**Money and results.** The Ministry's [page of 9 July 2025](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/genaralization-of-access-to-fixed-internet/>) sets a goal of 7 million households on fixed internet by the end of 2027, with fibre to the home as the core technology. It reports 6.6 million fixed internet subscribers in the first half of 2025, up from 3.5 million in early 2020, and says the intermediate objectives were reached.
