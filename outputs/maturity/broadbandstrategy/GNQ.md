# Equatorial Guinea

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** On [11 December 2025](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/propuesta_de_colaboracion_con_el_banco_mundial_para_la_transformacion_digital>) the Ministry of Transport and Telecommunications presented to the World Bank a proposal for the Plan Nacional de Telecomunicaciones para la Transformación Digital. The plan includes a competitive network ensuring 100% coverage before 2030. The press article reporting it, published on 12 December 2025, describes the plan as still to be designed, with World Bank technical assistance. The Ministry of Transport and Telecommunications is the owner named for the plan.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact is the same [December 2025 proposal](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/propuesta_de_colaboracion_con_el_banco_mundial_para_la_transformacion_digital>), which looks to World Bank technical assistance for the design of the plan. It states the 2030 coverage aim as a goal of the proposed plan, with no delivery figure against it. The Ministry of Transport, Telecommunications and Civil Aviation also issued a July-December 2026 half-year action plan, a national plan.
