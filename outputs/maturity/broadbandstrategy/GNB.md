# Guinea-Bissau

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Guinea-Bissau's [Strategic Plan of the Access and Universal Service Fund (FASU) for 2026-2031](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Plano-Estrategico-do-UASF.pdf>) is a consultancy final report for the regulator ARN, dated 24 November 2025. The report states that it is still to be disseminated and validated in a public stakeholder consultation, and so it is a draft rather than a plan in force. [ITU's 2019 table of broadband strategies, policies and plans](<http://broadband.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/ITU_BroadbandPlans_2019.pdf>) answered no on whether a national broadband policy existed and named none.

**Money and results.** Nothing is held on funding, spending or results delivered under the plan. The nearest held fact is what the draft itself sets out, its [coverage targets before 2031](<https://wardip.gw/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Plano-Estrategico-do-UASF.pdf>): 99% of the population for 2G, 98% for 3G and 96% for 4G broadband.

***Also on file***. [National Digital Transformation Strategy (ENTD.GW)](<https://unu.edu/egov/news/unu-egov-welcomes-delegation-guinea-bissau>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national broadband plan is adopted and still running.
