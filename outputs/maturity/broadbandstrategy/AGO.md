# Angola

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Presidential Decree 272/24, published in the [Diário da República on 5 December 2024](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2024/decreto-presidencial-n-o-272-24-de-05-de-dezembro/>), approved the ICT White Paper 2023-2027 after Council of Ministers review on 30 August 2024. The White Paper's stated objective is universal access to the broadband service for the whole population. It names the National Broadband Strategy and the National Broadband Network (RNBL) as its basis. The [White Paper itself](<https://livrobranco.gov.ao/site/pdf/LBTIC.pdf>), published on the same date, says its measures follow from implementing that strategy.

**Money and results.** The [White Paper](<https://livrobranco.gov.ao/site/pdf/LBTIC.pdf>) describes the strategy as covering the modernisation and expansion of the primary electronic communications network, now called the National Broadband Network, as State-funded public-domain infrastructure. The [decree text](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2024/decreto-presidencial-n-o-272-24-de-05-de-dezembro/>) holds no numeric coverage, speed or price target for the 2023-2027 period, and no delivery figures are given there.
