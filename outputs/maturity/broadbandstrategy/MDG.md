# Madagascar

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The government told [ITU's ICT Regulatory Tracker](<https://app.gen5.digital/tracker/metrics>) in 2024 that Madagascar has a national plan that involves broadband. The answer was self-reported in the tracker's 2024 round. The tracker entry names no plan and gives no title, adoption date or period for it. It states no broadband targets. It is the only source on file that records a national plan involving broadband for Madagascar.

**Money and results.** The nearest fact on file is the same [2024 tracker entry](<https://app.gen5.digital/tracker/metrics>), which records that the plan exists and says nothing about its funding. It names no budget, fund or programme. It gives no figures on delivery. Because the entry states no targets, there is nothing for any figure to be measured against.
