# The Gambia

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Information and Communication Infrastructure issued the [National Broadband Policy 2020-2024](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/caa6d521-5bad-11ec-9b31-029254d29bb1>) in June 2020 for a five-year period ending in 2024. The same month it commissioned the [National Broadband Strategy 2020-2024](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/BROADBAND-STRATEGY-2024.pdf>), which covers 2020 to 2024. Neither document names a successor plan, and the strategy's own text refers to a review for the next five-year cycle.

**Money and results.** The strategy sets targets of broadband coverage to more than 90% of the population by 2024, at least 75% of homes with affordable 5 Mbps download and upload speeds by 2022, and 4G coverage of at least 85% of the population by 2024 ([strategy](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/BROADBAND-STRATEGY-2024.pdf>)). The policy sets a 5 Mbps minimum for every user and near-100% of homes with affordable 5 Mbps by 2023 ([policy](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/caa6d521-5bad-11ec-9b31-029254d29bb1>)).
