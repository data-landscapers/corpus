# Morocco

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Morocco's Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform lists a Plan National du Haut Debit 2 (PNHD 2) in its Digital Morocco 2030 institutional brochure of 18 September 2024, described as a programme to cover areas with little or no coverage ([Digital Morocco 2030 brochure](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/PlaquetteInstitutionnel_18092024_Ang.pdf>)). A columnist writing in October 2026 refers to the same effort as the national high-speed plan, in Arabic al-mukhattat al-watani lil-sabib al-ali, and treats it as in force in late 2025 ([Anfaspress, 5 October 2026](<https://anfaspress.com/news/voir/179518-2026-10-05-04-07-04>)). Neither source gives a period for the plan.

**Money and results.** No budget line or financed programme is held. The brochure states that PNHD 2 aims to cover 1,800 localities ([Digital Morocco 2030 brochure](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/PlaquetteInstitutionnel_18092024_Ang.pdf>)). The columnist writes that more than 10,000 rural areas had been covered with 3G and 4G networks within the plan by late 2025 ([Anfaspress](<https://anfaspress.com/news/voir/179518-2026-10-05-04-07-04>)).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No budget line, universal service fund allocation or financed programme tied to the plan in the last three years is held.
