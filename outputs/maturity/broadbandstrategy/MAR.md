# Morocco

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** Morocco's national broadband plan, the Plan National du Haut Débit (PNHD), is in its second phase. The ministry says [PNHD 2 was launched in 2024](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/connectivit%C3%A9>) for the areas 4G does not reach, and the Minister for Digital Transition told parliament in December 2024 that [it is to serve 1,800 rural zones with weak or no coverage by 2026](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>). The ministry's [Digital Morocco 2030 brochure of September 2024](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/sites/default/files/2024-09/PlaquetteInstitutionnel_18092024_Ang.pdf>) lists PNHD 2 among its programmes, and a columnist [treats the plan as running in late 2025](<https://anfaspress.com/news/voir/179518-2026-10-05-04-07-04>).

**Money and results.** The second phase [is carried out with the support of the Universal Telecommunications Service Fund](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>), the minister said. The ministry reported that [PNHD 1 had covered 10,620 of the 10,740 localities targeted, 98.88 per cent, at the end of March 2024](<https://mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/connectivit%C3%A9>), and the minister told parliament in December 2024 that [more than 10,640 rural zones were covered between 2018 and 2024](<https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/>), about 99 per cent of the first phase's target.
