# Benin

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The government's project sheet lists the high and very high speed internet deployment (phase 2) as a government project with status "En cours", and gives an execution period of [2022 to 2026](<https://beninrevele.bj/projet/119/deploiement-internet-haut-tres-haut-debit-ensemble-territoire-national-phase-/>). Its aim is to bring broadband to the whole national territory. On 21 November 2024 the minister of digital affairs said the government plans to connect 18 more communes to fibre by mid-2025, to reach all 77 communes, under the [Programme d'action du gouvernement 2021-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2511-123743-haut-debit-le-benin-veut-generaliser-l-acces-d-ici-2025>).

**Money and results.** [Agence Ecofin](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2511-123743-haut-debit-le-benin-veut-generaliser-l-acces-d-ici-2025>) reports the broadband deployment project of the Programme d'action du gouvernement 2021-2026 as costed at about 207 billion FCFA in November 2024. The [project sheet](<https://beninrevele.bj/projet/119/deploiement-internet-haut-tres-haut-debit-ensemble-territoire-national-phase-/>) names one expected result, a backbone capacity extension at 8 sites from 40 to 100 Gbps. It carries no date.
