# Broadband strategy: national plan

*As at 2026-10-23.*

## The norm

The Broadband Commission's first target is a funded national broadband plan in every country by 2025, which gives questions 1 to 3. The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy sets the 2030 outcome: universal access at 6 Mb/s or more, at no more than one US cent a megabyte. This study asks whether the plan exists, is adopted, is funded and is reported on; it does not measure the outcome.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source says the country has no national broadband plan and none is being written |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: a plan being written, or one whose period has ended with no successor adopted |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: a plan adopted and still running |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: money is committed to the plan |
| 5 Leading | Yes to all four: the plan is funded and progress against its targets is published |

## Countries

### Morocco: 5 Leading

Morocco's national broadband plan reached about 99% of its first-phase target by 2024; a second phase funded by the universal service fund runs to 2026.

- The ministry reports that at end March 2024 PNHD1 had covered 10,620 of the 10,740 localities targeted for 2023 with 4G, 98.88 per cent ([source, 2024-03](https://mmsp.gov.ma/fr/nos-metiers/connectivit%C3%A9)).
- The minister reported that PNHD phase one covered over 10,640 rural zones between 2018 and 2024, about 99 per cent of its target ([source, 2024-12-23](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/)).
- The minister said PNHD phase two is carried out with the support of the Universal Telecommunications Service Fund ([source, 2024-12-23](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/)).
- Minister Amal El Fallah Seghrouchni told parliament that a second phase of the national broadband plan (PNHD) has been launched to serve 1,800 rural zones by 2026 ([source, 2024-12-23](https://www.lavieeco.com/au-royaume/plan-national-du-haut-debit-un-taux-de-realisation-de-99-pour-la-premiere-phase/)).

### Kenya: 4 Operating

Kenya has the Digital Superhighway 100,000km fibre plan, announced in 2022 and funded in 2026/27; no source found reports progress against its targets.

- The Digital Superhighway fibre programme is funded through the Universal Service Fund, with the CA and ICT Authority initially signing a Sh5 billion agreement covering 19 counties, and Kenya Power had received Sh940.6 million by June 2025 for design, supply and installation, per the Daily Nation ([source, 2026-09-02](https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286)).
- The FY2026/27 budget allocates Sh1.3 billion to maintain the national fibre backbone, Sh400 million for digital hubs and Sh382 million for digital superhighway cybersecurity, which Business Daily describes as part of the Digital Superhighway agenda to lay 100,000km of fibre ([source, 2026-06-11](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ict-allocation-drops-to-sh8-6-billion-in-budget-5493262)).
- President Ruto announced the Digital Superhighway's 100,000km national fibre target in November 2022 for universal broadband availability over five years, with a deadline of 2027 that the 2 September 2026 report says is still ahead, and no successor named ([source, 2022-11](https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286)).
