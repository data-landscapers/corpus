# Ghana

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The Ministry of Communications issued the [National Broadband Policy and Implementation Strategy as Final Version 1.0 in October 2012](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Broadband-Policy-and-Implementation-Strategy.pdf>). The strategy is the Ministry's national instrument, with targets of 40% household and 40% national broadband penetration by 2015, and of 90% by 2020.
