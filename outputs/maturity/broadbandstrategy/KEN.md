# Kenya

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** President Ruto announced the [Digital Superhighway's 100,000km national fibre target in November 2022](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>), aiming at universal broadband availability across the country over five years. The deadline is 2027, which the Nation's 2 September 2026 report says is still ahead, and no successor plan is named.

**Money and results.** The programme is [funded through the Universal Service Fund](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>): the Communications Authority and the ICT Authority first signed a Sh5 billion agreement covering 19 counties, and Kenya Power had received Sh940.6 million by June 2025 for design, supply and installation of the fibre network. The [FY2026/27 budget](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ict-allocation-drops-to-sh8-6-billion-in-budget-5493262>) allocates Sh1.3 billion to maintain the national fibre backbone, Sh400 million for digital hubs and Sh382 million for digital superhighway cybersecurity, as part of the same agenda.
