# Kenya

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** In November 2022 [President Ruto announced the Digital Superhighway's 100,000km national fibre target](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [It aims at broadband for the whole country within five years, by 2027](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [In September 2026 the Nation reported that deadline as still ahead](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [The National Broadband Strategy 2025-2030 sets the same 100,000km target, against 40,000km of fibre today, Techweez reported in October 2026](<https://techweez.com/2026/10/27/kenya-fiber-optic-network-expansion/>).

**Money and results.** [The Digital Superhighway is funded through the Universal Service Fund](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [The Communications Authority and the ICT Authority first signed a Sh5 billion agreement covering 19 counties](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [Kenya Power had received Sh940.6 million by June 2025 to build the fibre network](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/ruto-s-100-000km-fibre-optic-network-plan-falters-5580286>). [The FY2026/27 budget gives Sh1.3 billion to maintain the national fibre backbone](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ict-allocation-drops-to-sh8-6-billion-in-budget-5493262>). [It adds Sh400 million for digital hubs and Sh382 million for the superhighway's cybersecurity](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ict-allocation-drops-to-sh8-6-billion-in-budget-5493262>).
