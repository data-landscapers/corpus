# Mauritania

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** The ministry's final study of February 2019 [proposes a Stratégie Nationale Haut Débit / Plan National du Haut Débit for 2019-2025](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/strategie_promotion_hd_rim_volum-1_2.pdf>), with objectives dated 2020 and 2025. It sets coverage of all Moughataas as the short-term aim and the whole country as the medium-term aim. The study states no adoption by the government or a minister. The same study is titled "Stratégie de promotion du Haut Débit et d'accès Universel" by its authors, and the ministry's January 2019 action plan carries the name [Stratégie Nationale de Promotion du Haut Débit](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/piecesjointes/strategie_promotion_hd_rim_volum-2.pdf>).

**Money and results.** The February 2019 study [puts the investment at 623 million US dollars over 25 years](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/strategie_promotion_hd_rim_volum-1_2.pdf>), as an estimate. The January 2019 action plan [lists actions by theme with phasing and a preliminary calendar](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/piecesjointes/strategie_promotion_hd_rim_volum-2.pdf>), with one action to launch in September 2019. It leaves the action sheets to a follow-up committee and states no broadband coverage, speed or price target.
