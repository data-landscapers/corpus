# Mozambique

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** In December 2024 the government launched the Internet for All project, which aims to raise internet access in Mozambique from under 25% of the population to 100% by 2030, [according to the U.S. International Trade Administration](<https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/mozambique-digital-transformation-strategy>). The [Wearetech.africa report of 23 July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/mozambique-un-portail-national-du-haut-debit-pour-etendre-la-couverture-telecoms>) also describes an Internet Para Todos programme, with a period running to 2030, which had not ended when it was published in July 2026. Both sources give 2030 as the end of the plan period.

**Money and results.** No funding or delivery figures have been found. The nearest held fact is the targets: [Wearetech.africa reports](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/telecom/mozambique-un-portail-national-du-haut-debit-pour-etendre-la-couverture-telecoms>) that the Internet Para Todos programme aims for 80% mobile penetration, 95% territorial coverage and 99% network availability by 2030. All three targets fall on a date that had not arrived when it was published in July 2026, so no result against them has been found.
