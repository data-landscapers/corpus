# Mali

## National broadband plan

**Plan adopted.** In a [2015 article](<http://bamada.net/connectivite-et-haut-debit-large-concertation-sur-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-large-bande>) L'Essor reports that the regulator AMRTP held a launch workshop in Bamako for a study on the broadband implementation strategy. The study is to produce a short, medium and long-term broadband development plan, together with the introduction of 4G. The article reports a study under way, not a plan adopted by the government or the minister. The article carries no date, and its published field gives 2015. The study is titled Etude sur la stratégie de mise en œuvre du large bande au Mali.

**Money and results.** The nearest held fact is the same [2015 report](<http://bamada.net/connectivite-et-haut-debit-large-concertation-sur-la-mise-en-oeuvre-du-large-bande>) of the launch workshop. It concerns the study that is to write the plan, so it names no budget line, fund allocation or financed programme tied to a plan, and no figures against targets. The report names the regulator AMRTP as the body that held the workshop, in Bamako, and says nothing of who would fund the study or the plan.
