# Burundi

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [8.6 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Burundi used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 91.4 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 6.8 per cent in 2021, 7.1 per cent in 2022 and 7.2 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 1.8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 16.4 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
