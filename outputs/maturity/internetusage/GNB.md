# Guinea-Bissau

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [29.8 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Guinea-Bissau used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 70.2 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 23.4 per cent in 2021, 27.7 per cent in 2022 and 28.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 6.4 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 20.2 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [Internet penetration rate (DataReportal/Kepios): 723,000 internet users, 32.5% penetration, January 2025; DataReportal/Kepios figure](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/starlink-enters-guinea-bissau>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
