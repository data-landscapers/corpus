# Lesotho

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [51.8 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Lesotho used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 48.2 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 45.1 per cent in 2021, 48.1 per cent in 2022 and 49 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 6.7 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 28.2 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The Lesotho Communications Authority, with the Bureau of Statistics, reports from its nationally representative 2023 household survey that 58.4% of individuals use the internet; no age range is given](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>). [The Lesotho Communications Authority, with the Bureau of Statistics, reports from its 2019 household survey that 42.3% of individuals use the internet; no age range is given](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>). [The Lesotho Communications Authority, with the Bureau of Statistics, reports from its 2016 household survey that 32.5% of individuals use the internet; no age range is given](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Households with internet connection: 5.3% of households with internet access, 2023](<https://lca.org.ls/wp-content/uploads/filr/4005/COMPARATIVE-HOUSEHOLD-RESULTS-2016_2023.pdf>); [3G network coverage: 97% of the population covered, 2018](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/196401591179805910/pdf/Lesotho-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic.pdf>); [4G network coverage: 71.4% of the population covered, 2018](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/196401591179805910/pdf/Lesotho-Digital-Economy-Diagnostic.pdf>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
