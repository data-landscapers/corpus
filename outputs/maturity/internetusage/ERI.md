# Eritrea

## People who use the internet

***Noted, not assessed***. [Internet Society estimate of internet users: about 27% of the population uses the internet (Internet Society estimate, year not stated)](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175022/https://dig.watch/countries/eritrea>); [ITU households with internet access at home: 38.52% of households, 2018-2023](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175022/https://dig.watch/countries/eritrea>); [Social media users (DataReportal): 13.8 thousand, 2024](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260824175022/https://dig.watch/countries/eritrea>).

***Not held***. No figure for 2024 or later is held for the share of the population who use the internet. The latest held is 14.3 per cent, for 2020.
