# Somalia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [27.9 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Somalia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 72.1 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 23.9 per cent in 2021, 27.6 per cent in 2022 and 26.1 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4 points. The last survey figure in the series is [27.6 per cent, for 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The share is past a quarter and 22.1 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
