# Cote d'Ivoire

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [41.4 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Cote d'Ivoire used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 58.6 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 36 per cent in 2021, 38.4 per cent in 2022 and 40.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5.4 points. The share is past a quarter and 8.6 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
