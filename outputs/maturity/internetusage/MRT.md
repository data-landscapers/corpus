# Mauritania

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [45.8 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Mauritania used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 54.2 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 35.6 per cent in 2021, 42.5 per cent in 2022 and 45 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 10.2 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 4.2 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [A seminar speaker (a judge, not a government statement), citing World Bank data, says internet users were 46% of Mauritania's population in 2024; no age range is given](<https://arriyada.net/229898/>). [The ITU Digital Development Dashboard gives an ITU estimate of 44% of the population (individuals, no age range given) using the internet in Mauritania in 2022](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MRT.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Internet Society Pulse internet penetration: 46% of the population used the internet at least once over a 3-month period (2024); source of the figure not named](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/reports/mr/>); [DataReportal Digital 2025: Mauritania: about 1.96 million internet users, 37.4% penetration, early 2025](<https://arriyada.net/229898/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
