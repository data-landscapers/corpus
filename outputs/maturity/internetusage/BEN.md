# Benin

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [34 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Benin used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 66.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 28.2 per cent in 2021, 30.5 per cent in 2022 and 32.4 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5.8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 16 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [Internet penetration rate (Afrobaromètre, as cited): 70.40% in 2021 (40.41% in 2017), derived from 8.7 million mobile and 18,489 fixed subscriptions on 12.5 million inhabitants](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/legislation-beninoise-quel-prix-pour-linformation-mensongere>); [Internet penetration (Internet Society, Benin): 34% in 2024 (19% in 2018); source and basis not stated](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/07/how-local-peering-is-strengthening-africas-internet/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
