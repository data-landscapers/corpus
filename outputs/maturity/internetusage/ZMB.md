# Zambia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [17.1 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Zambia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 82.9 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 14.8 per cent in 2021, 15 per cent in 2022 and 16.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.3 points. The last survey figure in the series is [15 per cent, for 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 7.9 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
