# Chad

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [12.6 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Chad used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 87.4 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 9.7 per cent in 2021, 11.2 per cent in 2022 and 12 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.9 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 12.4 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
