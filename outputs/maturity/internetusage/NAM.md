# Namibia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** [The NDP6 implementation plan gives current internet usage as 53% of the population, targeting 90% by 2030](<https://neweralive.na/namibia-targets-full-digital-transformation/>). That leaves 47.0 per cent offline. The plan sets a target of 90 per cent by 2030, so it expects the share to rise by 37 points over its five years. The International Telecommunication Union's series [put the share at 60.3 per cent in 2021, 63 per cent in 2022, 63.7 per cent in 2023 and 64.9 per cent in 2024](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). The share is past half and 27 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
