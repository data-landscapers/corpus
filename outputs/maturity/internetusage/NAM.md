# Namibia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [64.9 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Namibia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 35.1 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 60.3 per cent in 2021, 63 per cent in 2022 and 63.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.6 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 15.1 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Noted, not assessed***. [internet penetration (APC UPR submission): internet penetration exceeding 64 percent, 2025](<https://cadeproject.org/updates/apc-report-highlights-digital-rights-challenges-in-namibia-ahead-of-upr-review/>).

***Not held***. No figure of 80 per cent or more is held. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later is held.
