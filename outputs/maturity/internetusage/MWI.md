# Malawi

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [19 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Malawi used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 81.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 15.3 per cent in 2021, 16.8 per cent in 2022 and 18 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.7 points. The last survey figure in the series is [18 per cent, for 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The National Statistical Office's 2023 ICT Survey [found 18 percent of individuals used the internet in the previous three months, up from 14.6 percent in 2019](<https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/>). Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 6 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The article cites International Telecommunication Union figures that 18% of Malawi's population actively used the internet in 2023; no age range is given](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0810-49382-malawi-launches-broadband-mapping-system-to-bridge-coverage-gaps>).
