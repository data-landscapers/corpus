# Sierra Leone

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [25.1 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Sierra Leone used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 74.9 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 18.7 per cent in 2021, 21.5 per cent in 2022 and 23.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 6.4 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 24.9 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal internet penetration estimate: 20.8% of total population, 1.85 million internet users, end of 2025 (DataReportal)](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/sierra-leone-invests-400m-in-telecoms-60-usage-gap-remains>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
