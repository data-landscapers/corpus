# Guinea

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [33.3 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Guinea used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 66.7 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 28.6 per cent in 2021, 31.3 per cent in 2022 and 32.3 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.7 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 16.7 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
