# Tanzania

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [31.2 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Tanzania used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 68.8 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 22.9 per cent in 2021, 26.9 per cent in 2022 and 28.4 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 8.3 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 18.8 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [TCRA internet penetration rate](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-digital-rise-what-the-latest-tcra-report-tells-us/>): 89.7% internet penetration in June 2026, reported by TCRA alongside 62.79 million internet subscriptions. [TCRA internet penetration rate](<https://www.tcra.go.tz/tcra-tovuti/2026/mamlaka-website/documents/quarterly-statistics/Communications_Sector_performance_report_for_quarter_ending_June_2026_ad44159230.pdf>): 89.7% for the quarter ending June 2026, from administrative supply-side data, with 62.79 million internet subscriptions. [Internet users figure presented at the Girl Agenda 2026 launch](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/girls-demand-a-stake-in-tanzania-s-digital-economy-by-2050-5588978>): 20.2 million internet users, 29.1% of the population, year not given. The series runs from 11.4 million in 2014 to 21.8 million in 2024. [IGP report internet use figure](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>): internet use is approximately 31% of the population ('today', in a 2026 article), roughly one-third adoption.
