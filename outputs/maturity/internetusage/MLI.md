# Mali

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [36.8 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Mali used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 63.2 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 34 per cent in 2021, 35.2 per cent in 2022 and 36 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 13.2 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The ITU Digital Development Dashboard gives individuals using the Internet in Mali, total, at 33% for 2022 (ITU estimate); no age range is given for the total](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MLI.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Households with Internet access at home: 22% of households (2019)](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MLI.pdf>); [Active mobile-broadband subscriptions: 58 per 100 inhabitants (2022)](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_MLI.pdf>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
