# Uganda

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [8.9 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Uganda used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 91.1 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 10 per cent in 2021, 9.6 per cent in 2022 and 9.3 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share fell by 1.1 points. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 16.1 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
