# Ghana

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [72.2 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Ghana used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 27.8 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 69.9 per cent in 2022 and 70.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2022 the share rose by 2.3 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 7.8 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
