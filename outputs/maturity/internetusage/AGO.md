# Angola

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** [INACOM, Angola's communications regulator, reports 55.5% of individuals using the internet in 2024 (indicator HH8); no age range is given](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>). That leaves 44.5 per cent offline. The International Telecommunication Union's series [put the share at 34.6 per cent in 2021, 35.8 per cent in 2022, 37.9 per cent in 2023 and 40.7 per cent in 2024](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). The share is past half and 24.5 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [INACOM reports 48.8% of individuals using the internet in 2023 (indicator HH8); no age range is given](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>). [INACOM reports 42.1% of individuals using the internet in 2022 (indicator HH8); no age range is given](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2025/11/Day-3-Angola.pdf>).
