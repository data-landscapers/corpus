# South Africa

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [78.4 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of South Africa used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 21.6 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 75 per cent in 2021, 78.3 per cent in 2022 and 78.3 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.4 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 1.6 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal internet penetration figure: 79.6% of total population, roughly 51.7 million internet users (DataReportal, cited by ITWeb; year not stated, article dated 2026-10-05)](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/zarc-reaches-15m-active-za-domains/Gb3BwMWaOG1v2k6V>); [Research ICT Africa After Access 2018 survey, offline share: about 50% of South Africans offline (op-ed by Alison Gillwald, 2020-03-03, citing 2018 survey context; the survey basis and age range are not stated for this figure)](<https://theconversation.com/digital-equality-south-africa-still-has-a-long-way-to-go-131864>).
