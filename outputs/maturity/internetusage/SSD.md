# South Sudan

## People who use the internet

***Also on file***. [NCA internet users and penetration rate](<https://www.eyeradio.org/nca-reports-huge-internet-growth-in-south-sudan/>): 1.9 million internet users in 2025, against 1.3 million in 2024. Internet penetration was 15.7% in 2025 and 12% in 2024, per the National Communication Authority. [GSMA CoNUA household survey, four refugee-hosting locations](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/GSMA_M4H_Digital-Access-and-Connectivity-in-South-Sudan-Barriers-for-Refugees-and-Host-Communities_06_2026.pdf>): 72% of respondents were aware of the internet in 2026. Of internet users, 66% cannot use it as much as they would like. The survey gives no share of people using the internet.

***Not found***. No figure for 2024 or later has been found for the share of the population who use the internet. The latest held is 6.7 per cent, for 2019.
