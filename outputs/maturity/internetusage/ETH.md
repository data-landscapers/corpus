# Ethiopia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [21.9 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Ethiopia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 78.1 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 16.7 per cent in 2021, 18 per cent in 2022 and 20.3 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5.2 points. The last survey figure in the series is [16.7 per cent, for 2021](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 3.1 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
