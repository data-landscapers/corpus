# Botswana

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [57.5 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Botswana used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 42.5 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 54.7 per cent in 2021 and 57.9 per cent in 2022](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 2.8 points. The share is past half and 22.5 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [Statistics Botswana's 2024 Household Access and Individual Use of ICTs Survey (nationally representative, household members aged 5 and above, fieldwork June-August 2024) found internet use reaching 57.5%](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/46/10_EGH2026_DataCollectionBotswana_TebogoTsienyane.pdf>).
