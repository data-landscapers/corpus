# Sao Tome and Principe

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [59.1 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Sao Tome and Principe used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 40.9 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 54.9 per cent in 2021, 58.6 per cent in 2022 and 58.7 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.2 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 20.9 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The UNESCO AI observatory profile says the ITU reported 61% of the population of Sao Tome and Principe as using the internet in 2023; no age range is given](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/saotomeeprincipe>).
