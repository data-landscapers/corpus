# Mozambique

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [20.5 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Mozambique used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 79.5 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 16.4 per cent in 2021, 19 per cent in 2022 and 19.9 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.1 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 4.5 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
