# Equatorial Guinea

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [63.3 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Equatorial Guinea used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 36.7 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 58.7 per cent in 2021, 63.7 per cent in 2022 and 63.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.6 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 16.7 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
