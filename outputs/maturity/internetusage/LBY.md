# Libya

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [82 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Libya used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 18.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 77.4 per cent in 2021, 80.6 per cent in 2022 and 82.1 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.6 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past four in five and 13 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The ITU Digital Development Dashboard gives an ITU estimate of 88% of the population using the internet in Libya for 2022 (individuals using the internet, total; no age range given)](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_LBY.pdf>).
