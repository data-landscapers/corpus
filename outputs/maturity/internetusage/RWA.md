# Rwanda

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [31.7 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Rwanda used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 68.3 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 24.4 per cent in 2021, 28 per cent in 2022 and 28.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 7.3 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 18.3 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [TechCabal reports the ITU-sourced share of Rwanda's population online was 34% in 2024, before rising to 38%; no age range is given](<https://techcabal.com/2025/06/12/rwanda-internet-penetration-rate/>). [The article states that 34.2% of Rwanda's population regularly used the internet in 2023 (8% in 2012), citing the World Bank (and a 'WTO'); no age range is given](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/25/hayo-expands-to-rwanda-to-power-digital-growth-with-voice-messaging-and-esim-solutions/>). [The ITU Digital Development Dashboard gives an ITU estimate of 34% of individuals using the internet in Rwanda in 2022; no age range is given](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_RWA.pdf>).
