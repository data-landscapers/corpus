# Central African Republic

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [13.8 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Central African Republic used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 86.2 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 13.8 per cent in 2022 and 13.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2022 the share has not moved. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 11.2 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
