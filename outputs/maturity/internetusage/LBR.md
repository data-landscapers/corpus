# Liberia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [32.2 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Liberia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 67.8 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 26.4 per cent in 2021, 30.4 per cent in 2022 and 31.2 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5.8 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 17.8 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal Digital 2026: Liberia: 1.73 million internet users, about 30 percent of the population, late 2025 (DataReportal estimate)](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-ten-liberian-civil-society-groups-launch-digital-advocacy-and-rights-alliance/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
