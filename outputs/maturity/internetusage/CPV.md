# Cape Verde

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** [INE's IMC 2025 household survey finds 77.3% of individuals aged 10 and over used the internet in the three months before the survey](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/05/28/internet-chega-a-mais-familias-rurais-do-que-urbanas-em-cabo-verde-em-2025-ine/102930>). That leaves 22.7 per cent offline. The survey is the national statistics institute's own, for 2025, and counts people aged 10 and over. The International Telecommunication Union's series [put the share at 70.3 per cent in 2021, 73.3 per cent in 2022, 74.6 per cent in 2023 and 74.7 per cent in 2024](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). The share is past half and 2.7 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [ARME internet penetration rate: 90 internet accesses per 100 inhabitants (ARME, cited Aug 2025); regulator access/subscription statistic, not a survey of people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found.
