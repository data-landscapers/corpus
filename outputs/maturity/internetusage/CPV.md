# Cape Verde

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [74.7 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Cape Verde used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 25.3 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 70.3 per cent in 2021, 73.3 per cent in 2022 and 74.6 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 4.4 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 5.3 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Noted, not assessed***. [ARME internet penetration rate: 90 internet accesses per 100 inhabitants (ARME, cited Aug 2025); regulator access/subscription statistic, not a survey of people](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/08/08/cape-verde-eyes-gdp-boost-through-stronger-digital-skills/>).

***Not held***. No figure of 80 per cent or more is held. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later is held.
