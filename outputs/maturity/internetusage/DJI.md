# Djibouti

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** [In November 2026 the communications minister said nearly 70 per cent of the population uses the internet](<https://www.adi.dj/article/139096>). In 2024, [65.3 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Djibouti used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 34.7 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 61.4 per cent in 2021, 63.8 per cent in 2022 and 64.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.9 points. The minister's figure is past half and about 10 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal internet users figure: approx. 772,000 internet users, 65% internet penetration, end of 2025 (DataReportal)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/djibouti-seeks-itu-smart-africa-support-for-telecoms-and-ai-development>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
