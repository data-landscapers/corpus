# Djibouti

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [65.3 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Djibouti used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 34.7 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 61.4 per cent in 2021, 63.8 per cent in 2022 and 64.8 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.9 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past half and 14.7 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal internet users figure: approx. 772,000 internet users, 65% internet penetration, end of 2025 (DataReportal)](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/djibouti-seeks-itu-smart-africa-support-for-telecoms-and-ai-development>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
