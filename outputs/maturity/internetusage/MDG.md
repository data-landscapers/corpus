# Madagascar

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [18.7 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Madagascar used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 81.3 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 13.7 per cent in 2021, 17.8 per cent in 2022 and 18.2 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 5 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 6.3 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The World Bank's indicator of individuals using the Internet (IT.NET.USER.ZS, underlying source ITU) gives 18.68% of Madagascar's population for 2024, with 2025 not yet populated; no age range is given](<https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/MDG/indicator/IT.NET.USER.ZS?date=2024:2025&format=json>).

***Also on file***. [DataReportal 2026 internet penetration: Internet penetration 20.4% (6.71 million internet users), end-2025, re-reported from DataReportal 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/0210-59411-madagascar-has-80-4g-coverage-yet-mobile-internet-remains-out-of-reach-for-most>).

***Not found***. No figure of 25 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
