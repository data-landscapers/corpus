# Morocco

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [91.2 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Morocco used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 8.8 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 88.1 per cent in 2021, 89.9 per cent in 2022 and 91 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.1 points. The share is past four in five and 3.8 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold. It counts use from any place and on any device. The regulator ANRT's household survey [gives the same 91.2 per cent for 2024](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-10/Enqu%C3%AAte%20TIC%202024-2025.pdf>), up from 74.4 per cent in 2019. It covered 5,760 households in January and February 2025. It counts people aged five and over who used the internet in the last three months.
