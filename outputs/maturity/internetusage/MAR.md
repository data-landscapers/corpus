# Morocco

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [91.2 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Morocco used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 8.8 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 88.1 per cent in 2021, 89.9 per cent in 2022 and 91 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.1 points. The share is past four in five and 3.8 points short of 95 per cent, which is nearly everyone. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The regulator ANRT's national household ICT survey (5,760 households, fieldwork January-February 2025) gives 91.2% of individuals aged 5 and over using the internet in 2024 (use in the last three months), up from 74.4% in 2019](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2025-10/Enqu%C3%AAte%20TIC%202024-2025.pdf>).
