# Congo

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [47.3 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Congo used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 52.7 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 30 per cent in 2021, 41 per cent in 2022 and 43.2 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 17.3 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. The share is past a quarter and 2.7 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [DataReportal / Kepios internet users figure: 2.46 million internet users, 38.4% internet penetration, January 2025 (DataReportal/Kepios)](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/congo-boosts-internet-access-in-rural-areas>).

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found. No national survey or census figure for 2024 or later has been found.
