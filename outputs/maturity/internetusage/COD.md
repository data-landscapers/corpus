# DR Congo

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [19.7 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of DR Congo used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 80.3 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 16.2 per cent in 2021, 18 per cent in 2022 and 19 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 3.5 points. Every figure since 2021 is an estimate, not a survey result. Fewer than a quarter of people use the internet, and the share is 5.3 points short of a quarter. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The ITU Digital Development Dashboard gives an ITU estimate of 27% of the population using the internet in 2022 (individuals using the internet, total; no age range given)](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Statistics/Documents/DDD/ddd_COD.pdf>).
