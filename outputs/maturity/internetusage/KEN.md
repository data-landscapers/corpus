# Kenya

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [35 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Kenya used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 65.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 27 per cent in 2021, 29.4 per cent in 2022 and 32.1 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 8 points. The share is past a quarter and 15 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The Communications Authority of Kenya and the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics report from the 2023/24 Kenya Housing Survey that 35.0% of individuals nationally use the internet (37.8% of males, 32.2% of females); no age range is given for the national figure, and the age breakdown starts at 3-4 years. The survey year is 2023/24, recorded as 2024](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-08/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20ICT%20Analytical%20Report%20Based%20on%20Kenya%20Housing%20Survey%202023-2024%20Final%20WEB%20Version.pdf>).
