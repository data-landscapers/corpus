# Kenya

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [35 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Kenya used the internet, a survey figure the country reported to the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 65.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 27 per cent in 2021, 29.4 per cent in 2022 and 32.1 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 8 points. The share is past a quarter and 15 points short of 50 per cent, which is half. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold. It counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device. [The Communications Authority of Kenya and the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics report from the 2023/24 Kenya Housing Survey that 35.0% of individuals nationally use the internet](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-08/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20ICT%20Analytical%20Report%20Based%20on%20Kenya%20Housing%20Survey%202023-2024%20Final%20WEB%20Version.pdf>). The figure is 37.8% for males and 32.2% for females. No age range is given for the national figure, and the age breakdown starts at 3-4 years. The survey year is 2023/24, recorded as 2024.

***Also on file***. [Kenya Housing Survey 2023/24 internet usage among youths (18-34 years)](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2025-08/Fact%20Sheet%20on%20ICT%20Analytical%20Report%20Based%20on%20Kenya%20Housing%20Survey%202023-2024%20Final%20WEB%20Version.pdf>): 58.6% national, 2023/24, for youths aged 18-34 only. [Urban and rural internet use cited by KICTANet](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/kictanet-sheconnects-empowerher-tot-tana-river-sep2026/>): 56% of urban residents use the internet, and 25% of rural residents use it daily. KICTANet gives no year or source. [KNBS internet access by sex, in a 2026 write-up](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/why-nyandaruas-women-need-digital-skills-now/>): 37.8% of men and 32.2% of women access the internet nationally. The write-up gives no year for the KNBS figures, and gives internet penetration as 56.5% urban against 25% rural. [Ajira gig-economy survey, internet usage and infrastructure access](<https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5145689>): a phone survey of 1,557 adults in November 2023. It gives 75.3% of men and 62.1% of women, adults only, on a combined usage and access measure with no total.

***Not found***. No figure of 50 per cent or more has been found.
