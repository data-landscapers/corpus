# Tunisia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In 2024, [70 per cent of the population](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>) of Tunisia used the internet, an estimate by the International Telecommunication Union. That leaves 30.0 per cent offline. The same series [put the share at 68.3 per cent in 2021, 69.1 per cent in 2022 and 69.5 per cent in 2023](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Since 2021 the share rose by 1.7 points. The last survey figure in the series is [68.3 per cent, for 2021](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>); every later year is an estimate. The share is past half and 10 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts a person once, whatever the number of subscriptions they hold, and counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.
