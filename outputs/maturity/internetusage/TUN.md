# Tunisia

## People who use the internet

**Share of people who use the internet, with its year.** In November 2026 the Inspector General of Youth and Childhood [put internet use at 77.7 per cent of people aged 10 and over](<https://www.nessma.tv/fr/nationale/actu/ministere-de-la-famille-777-dutilisateurs-dinternet-des-10-ans/597664>). He spoke at a national forum on digital harassment and named no survey or year. That leaves 22.3 per cent offline. The International Telecommunication Union's series [put the share at 69.1 per cent in 2022, 69.5 per cent in 2023 and 70 per cent in 2024](<https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/dataportal>). Those are estimates; the last survey figure in it is 68.3 per cent, for 2021. The share is past half and 2.3 points short of 80 per cent, which is four in five. The figure counts anyone who used the internet in the last three months, from any place and on any device.

***Also on file***. [Recensement général de la population et de l'habitat 2024 (RGPH), indicateur TIC: 76.5% of literate individuals aged 10+ used the internet in the 3 months before the 2024 census (INS)](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/TIC-FR.pdf>); [Connexion Internet des ménages: 40.4% of households have an internet connection, 2024 (INS)](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/TIC-FR.pdf>); [Internet mobile (usagers): 70.4% of internet users use mobile internet as main access, 2026 (INT survey)](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-48-des-internautes-utilisent-lia-et-37-achetent-en-ligne/>).

***Not found***. No figure of 80 per cent or more has been found.
