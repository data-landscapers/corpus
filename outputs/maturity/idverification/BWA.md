# Botswana

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Business Tech Africa reports that the Bankers' Association of Botswana has launched a national eKYC project that lets banks verify customer identities against the national citizen database](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). [The speech reports the Health Information Exchange's Client Registry, which identifies patients uniquely by Omang or passport number, as ready for deployment](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). [The Bankers' Association launched an eKYC system in 2021 that allowed commercial banks to verify identity cards against the national citizen database (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). [In July 2026 Bank Gaborone's self-onboarding platform checked new customers against their Omang and a biometric selfie](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/bank-gaborone-launches-digital-self-onboarding-platform-making-banking-simpler-and-more-accessible/>).

**Used across sectors.** [The diagnostic names the electoral system, social benefits (SOBERS), government payroll and the transport system as linked to the national identity system, which is more than one sector (2015, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/864651486101539760/pdf/Botswana-ID4D-DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>).
