# Botswana

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** The Bankers' Association of Botswana has launched [a national eKYC project](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). It lets banks verify customers against the national citizen database. It builds on a [system the association opened to commercial banks in 2021](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>). In July 2026 [Bank Gaborone began checking new customers against their Omang and a selfie](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/bank-gaborone-launches-digital-self-onboarding-platform-making-banking-simpler-and-more-accessible/>). The check is part of automated KYC.

**Used across sectors.** In March 2026 the health ministry said [its Client Registry was ready for deployment](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>). It identifies patients by Omang or passport number. In 2015 a World Bank diagnostic found [the ID system linked to elections, social benefits, payroll and transport](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/864651486101539760/pdf/Botswana-ID4D-DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf>).

**Use reported.** The [Bankers' Association's national eKYC project](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>) lets banks verify customers against the citizen database. [Bank Gaborone's platform](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/bank-gaborone-launches-digital-self-onboarding-platform-making-banking-simpler-and-more-accessible/>) runs the check for every new customer who signs up online.

***Also on file***. [Botswana Savings Bank eKYC and anti-money-laundering automation system](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/botswana-launches-national-ekyc-project-to-make-bank-verification-easier>); [Mandatory SIM card registration](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/public-notice-mandatory-sim-card-registration-all-users-botswana>); [Tiered merchant and KYC framework (Electronic Payment Services Amendment Regulations 2026)](<https://www.bankofbotswana.bw/sites/default/files/press-release-files/Implementation%20of%20the%20Electronic%20Payment%20Services%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202026.pdf>); [Labour Market Information System (LMIS)](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/govt-to-unveil-long-overdue-digital-labour-market-platform/news>); [National Identity Management System (NIS) / Omang](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/864651486101539760/pdf/Botswana-ID4D-DiagnosticWeb040418.pdf>); [Electronic Identification System (Biometric Identity Card) / Digital ID initiative](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking people against the ID.
