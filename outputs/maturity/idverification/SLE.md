# Sierra Leone

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** On 20 October 2026 the National Communications Authority said mobile subscribers must [link their National Identification Number to their SIM by 1 November 2026](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/65628>). [Citizens' Barray, a civil-society account, states that NatCA has implemented NIN-SIM integration, meant to strengthen identity verification and reduce fraud](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/citizens-barray_freeid-identity-telecommunications-activity-7492303807360679936-U3Br>). [The NCRA Director-General said the NCRA operates an eKYC platform integrated with multiple institutions (unnamed, uncounted) enabling identity verification against the NIN, which covers about 93 per cent of the population](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [Minister Salima Bah told Parliament on 4 August 2026 that the Government remains committed to completing the nationwide SIM registration and National Identification Number linkage exercise](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salima-bah-government-on-mobile-money-fraud/>). [The World Bank blog says mobile operators and banks are now connecting their systems to the national ID](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone>). [In August 2025 NCRA, the regulator and the mobile operators signed an agreement on NIN-SIM integration, after NCRA's database was linked to operators' systems for real-time checks, and an ID card is now required for a SIM](<http://www.thesatellitenews.com/index.php/de/announcements/23-news-articles/1837-to-fast-track-nin-integration-with-telecom-services-ncra-natca-sign-mou-with-mnos-for-nin-sim-linkage>).

**Used across sectors.** [NCRA's Deputy Director of ICT named banking, public education and employment as requiring the NIN](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sierra-leone-reaches-93-percent-national-id-coverage-80-percent-services-digitized>). [NCRA's presentation shows the verification ecosystem linked to health care, social protection, banks, social security, payroll, drivers licence, passport, pensions, FIU/ML, education, immigration, elections, tax/revenue and SIM card registration](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sierra-leone-reaches-93-percent-national-id-coverage-80-percent-services-digitized>).
