# Chad

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** On 8 May 2026 the secure titles agency ANATS and Airtel Tchad [signed a partnership to identify mobile subscribers](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/09/securisation-des-donnees-lanats-et-airtel-tchad-signent-un-partenariat-strategique/>). ANATS is to verify Airtel's customer data, and the two systems are to be linked. Enrolment was due to start within days. In April 2025 the telecoms regulator ARCEP [said every SIM card sold must be linked to a national identification number](<https://www.letchadanthropus-tribune.com/tchad-securite-larcep-sonnera-lalerte-sur-lappartenance-des-cartes-sim/>). It added that some are still sold pre-activated, with no identity check, and warned operators of sanctions.
