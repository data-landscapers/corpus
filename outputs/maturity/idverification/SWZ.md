# Eswatini

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [In July 2024 APC said service providers such as commercial banks can access the civil registry to verify identity](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>). [APC added that the national ID card does not yet meet the criteria for digital identification](<https://www.apc.org/en/news/risk-human-rights-infringement-eswatini-rushes-regulate-ict-sector>). [In February 2025 the Central Bank's Deputy Governor said financial institutions are integrated with the national database centre](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Deputy-Governors-Speech-The-Nexus-between-AML-CFT-and-Financial-Inclusion-February-2025.pdf>). [The centre sits at the Royal Science and Technology Park](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Deputy-Governors-Speech-The-Nexus-between-AML-CFT-and-Financial-Inclusion-February-2025.pdf>). [In March 2025 the Deputy Governor said banks verify customer identification against the national civil registry through it](<https://www.bis.org/review/r250311k.htm>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the Times of Eswatini reported that banks, financial service providers, regulators and ministries met on a national e-KYC registry](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+embarks+on+national+e-KYC+system&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1080>). [The e-KYC system, set to roll out soon, is to digitise identity verification for financial services](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+embarks+on+national+e-KYC+system&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1080>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [in September 2025 the Royal Science and Technology Park was providing the infrastructure](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+embarks+on+national+e-KYC+system&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1080>). [It is for a national identity verification platform](<https://www.times.co.sz/business/readmore.php?bhsadjgfoh=Eswatini+embarks+on+national+e-KYC+system&bvhdgsj=Business+and+Economy&yiphi=1080>).
