# Liberia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Since a May 2024 agreement with the Civil Service Agency, [national ID numbers are used to verify the payroll](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>) and find ghost workers. [In June 2025 the registry's director said financial institutions are fully integrated with its KYC system](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/government-extends-national-id-deadline-to-august-31/>). He said the registry was deploying APIs so authorised institutions can reach verified data. [Telecoms rules require SIM cards to be registered with IDs verified on the registry's platform](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/no-national-id-no-transaction/article_bfbb894b-28db-40fc-9766-31946f59eb40.html>). [The April 2026 ID concession plans checks for banking, health, passports, land, business registration and telecoms](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>).

**Used across sectors.** Two sectors check people against the ID: [the civil service payroll](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>) and [banks' customer checks](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/government-extends-national-id-deadline-to-august-31/>). SIM registration against the registry is set by rule, and the wider platform waits on the concession.

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact is [the banks' integration with the registry's KYC system](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/government-extends-national-id-deadline-to-august-31/>).
