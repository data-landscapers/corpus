# Liberia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Since a May 2024 agreement with the Civil Service Agency, [national ID numbers are used to verify the payroll](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>) and find ghost workers. Telecoms rules [require SIM cards to be registered with IDs verified on the registry's platform](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/no-national-id-no-transaction/article_bfbb894b-28db-40fc-9766-31946f59eb40.html>). An executive order opens the registry to ministries, banks and mobile operators. The April 2026 ID concession [plans checks for banking, health, passports, land, business registration and telecoms](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>). Its annex lists a US$36 fee for each bank-account check. A [steering committee set a rollout deadline of 13 April 2026](<https://idtechwire.com/liberia-appoints-steering-committee-to-oversee-national-biometric-id-rollout-by-april-2026>) for the biometric ID system.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>).
