# Liberia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The National Identification Registry says an MoU with the Civil Service Agency (May 2024) allows national ID numbers to be used for payroll verification, helping identify ghost workers](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>). [A presidential steering committee was appointed on the National Biometric Identification System with a rollout deadline of 13 April 2026, the system being designed to support social welfare, banking and SIM registration](<https://idtechwire.com/liberia-appoints-steering-committee-to-oversee-national-biometric-id-rollout-by-april-2026>). [The Liberian Observer reports the LTA has enacted regulations requiring SIM cards to be registered with government-issued IDs verified on the NIR platform, and that Executive Order 147 opens the NIR database to ministries, commercial banks and mobile network operators](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/no-national-id-no-transaction/article_bfbb894b-28db-40fc-9766-31946f59eb40.html>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/liberia-says-national-id-system-drives-digital-economy-facilitates-service-delivery>).
