# Mali

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Decree 2023-0679 of 16 November 2023 makes identification of every state and local civil servant compulsory](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2023/mali-jo-2023-34.pdf>). Each agent's data is matched against the biometric records of RAVEC, the civil-status census, and unidentified agents leave the payroll. [The integrated HR system built for this takes in the RAVEC biometric data](<https://www.maliweb.net/fonction-publique/fonctions-publiques-de-letat-et-des-collectivites-territoriales-mise-en-oeuvre-de-loperation-didentification-des-agents-3043107.html>). [Since February 2023 the telecoms regulator has had operators identify SIM cards by the NINA number](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/mali-lamrtp-edifie-les-consommateurs-sur-limportance-de-lidentification-des-cartes-sim/>). [In July 2025 it barred SIM sales without an ID carrying the NINA](<https://bamada.net/identification-des-abonnes-telephoniques-lamrtp-renforce-les-mesures-de-fiabilite-autour-du-nina-et-de-lacquisition-des-cartes-sim>). The SIM rules record the number; no check against the register is described.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact is [the civil service identification ordered by decree](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2023/mali-jo-2023-34.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact is [the 2025 rule on SIM sales](<https://bamada.net/identification-des-abonnes-telephoniques-lamrtp-renforce-les-mesures-de-fiabilite-autour-du-nina-et-de-lacquisition-des-cartes-sim>).
