# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [BCPFS and ONECI signed a partnership convention on 2 April 2026 to interconnect the RSU with ONECI databases, identifying and verifying beneficiaries through the Registre National des Personnes Physiques](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). [In February 2025 Orange Bank Africa and ONECI signed a convention for the bank to verify customers' identity for know-your-customer checks through ONECI's authentication APIs](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/02/03/cote-divoire/economie/cote-divoire-signature-dune-convention-entre-oba-et-loneci-pour-renforcer-la-securite-des-transactions_184218.html>).
