# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [BCPFS and ONECI signed a partnership convention on 2 April 2026 to interconnect the RSU with ONECI databases, identifying and verifying beneficiaries through the Registre National des Personnes Physiques](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Used across sectors.** No source held since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>).
