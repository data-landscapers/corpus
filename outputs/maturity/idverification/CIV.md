# Cote d'Ivoire

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [ONECI, the identity office, says it put identity checks for know-your-customer rules in place for banks and financial institutions in 2025](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/02/10/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-le-delai-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-ramene-a-moins-de-deux-semaines-perspectives-de-la-structure-pour-2026_194217.html>). [In February 2025 Orange Bank Africa signed a convention with ONECI](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/02/03/cote-divoire/economie/cote-divoire-signature-dune-convention-entre-oba-et-loneci-pour-renforcer-la-securite-des-transactions_184218.html>) to verify its customers through ONECI's authentication APIs. [On 2 April 2026 the social safety net programme signed a convention with ONECI](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>). It is to verify social registry beneficiaries against the national register of persons.

**Used across sectors.** Banking is the one sector with checks in service. [The social registry link agreed with ONECI in April 2026](<https://filetsociaux-ci.org/registre-social-unique-des-menages-pauvres-et-vulnerables-rsu-signature-dune-convention-entre-le-bureau-de-coordination-du-programme-filets-sociaux-bcpfs-et-loffice-nation/>) is the second in preparation, for social protection.
