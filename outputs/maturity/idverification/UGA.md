# Uganda

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Internal Affairs Minister Ephraim Kamuntu launched the integration of the National Identification and National Tax Identification systems](<https://ntv.co.ug/news/national-news/government-launches-integration-of-national-id-tax-identification-systems>). The launch was at the first Identity Expo and Symposium in Kololo. [URA's Christine Mirembe said government will phase out the TIN for individuals and replace it with the NIN](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/transitioning-from-tin-to-nin-will-create-job-NV_240692_092026>). She said banks and microfinance institutions will be able to use the NIN to verify a trader's tax compliance history. [NITA-U's Director of e-Government Services said it is working with government institutions to integrate systems and support digital authentication against the national ID](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/369498/nira-to-host-national-id-expo-as-millions-remain-outside-register>).

**Used across sectors.** URSB's ICT commissioner said in 2025 that business registration has a [real-time link with NIRA](<https://ursb.go.ug/2025/09/02/ursbs-digital-transformation-enhances-service-access-and-transparency/>) for identity verification. [The NIRA Executive Director named the SIM card (telecoms), financial services and taxation as areas where the NIN is recognised](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/369498/nira-to-host-national-id-expo-as-millions-remain-outside-register>). The expo is to show how identity is already used across sectors. [The MP's statement names banking and telecoms (SIM registration) as sectors requiring QR-code verification of the national ID](<https://www.independent.co.ug/new-id-rollout-hits-qr-code-verification-hurdle/>). The gender ministry said in October 2026 that [43,000 older people could not draw SAGE cash grants](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/379844/43000-elderly-ugandans-locked-out-of-sage-benefits-over-national-id-errors>). The ages on their national ID cards made them ineligible.

**Use reported.** [The Bank of Uganda reports 74 financial institutions with NIRA database access for KYC](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/uganda-financial-institutions-sign-on-for-digital-kyc-through-national-id>). A directive of April 2024 requires ID verification for all financial transactions of UGX 1 million and above. NIRA says [10 of its 107 partner institutions were checking customers by NIN and biometrics through its new system by 28 October 2026](<https://edge.ug/new-national-id-verification-glitches-spark-delays-at-banks-agencies/>).
