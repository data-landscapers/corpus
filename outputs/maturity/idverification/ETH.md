# Ethiopia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Faydaverse counted [150 integrated partners and over 208 million eKYC verifications on Fayda](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>) as of 9 October 2026. Banks [retrieve KYC information and biometric authentication from Fayda](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). FaydaVerse has sent them a contract charging 10-20 Br per retrieval and 20-40 Br per authentication. In August 2026 the National ID Program reported [150 agencies integrated and over 194 million eKYC verifications](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). Its director says [a Fayda ID lets holders open a bank account on their phone](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>). FaydaVerse has [charged banks and others for verification since August 2026](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).

**Used across sectors.** [NIDP says Fayda is required or harmonised in banking, telecoms, tax, land administration and education](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). In November 2026 the Agricultural Transformation Institute [linked its farmer registration to Fayda](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/11/08/ethiopia-targets-four-million-farmers-for-digital-registration-by-2028/>). Nearly one million farmers are enrolled, and those without an ID number are sent to be enrolled first.

**Use reported.** [Faydaverse figures reported by Biometric Update give over 208 million eKYC verifications and 150 integrated partners as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Addis Fortune reports FaydaVerse partners with more than 150 institutions and processes up to two million requests a day](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [NIDP reports 65M authentications at 1M a day and 100 integrations](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [FaydaVerse says the system had processed about 200 million authentications by 15 October 2026 and handles about two million verification requests a day](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).
