# Ethiopia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Biometric Update, citing Faydaverse, reports 150 integrated partners and over 208 million eKYC verifications on Fayda as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Addis Fortune reports banks retrieve KYC information and obtain biometric authentication from Fayda, and FaydaVerse has sent them a contract charging 10-20 Br per retrieval and 20-40 Br per authentication](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [Biometric Update, citing the National ID Program, reports 150 agencies integrated with Fayda and over 194 million eKYC verifications to date as of 24 August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). [Ethiopia's Fayda programme director says a Fayda ID lets holders open a bank account on their phone](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>). [FaydaVerse has charged banks and other organisations for its ID verification service since August 2026](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).

**Used across sectors.** [NIDP says Fayda is required or harmonised in banking, telecoms, tax, land administration and education](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [Faydaverse figures reported by Biometric Update give over 208 million eKYC verifications and 150 integrated partners as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Addis Fortune reports FaydaVerse partners with more than 150 institutions and processes up to two million requests a day](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [NIDP reports 65M authentications at 1M a day and 100 integrations](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [FaydaVerse says the system had processed about 200 million authentications by 15 October 2026 and handles about two million verification requests a day](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/18/faydaverse-begins-charging-banks-for-digital-id-verification/>).
