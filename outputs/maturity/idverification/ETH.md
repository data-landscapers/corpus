# Ethiopia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Biometric Update, citing Faydaverse, reports 150 integrated partners and over 208 million eKYC verifications on Fayda as of 9 October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). [Addis Fortune reports banks retrieve KYC information and obtain biometric authentication from Fayda, and FaydaVerse has sent them a contract charging 10-20 Br per retrieval and 20-40 Br per authentication](<https://addisfortune.news/banks-baulk-at-a-bill-from-public-id-that-turns-commercial>). [Biometric Update, citing the National ID Program, reports 150 agencies integrated with Fayda and over 194 million eKYC verifications to date as of 24 August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). [Ethiopia's Fayda programme director says a Fayda ID lets holders open a bank account on their phone](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>).

**Used across sectors.** No source held since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID.
