# Gambia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The diagnostic says the Drivers License System uses the National ID system for biometric verification of applicants to prevent identity fraud and duplication](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). [The World Bank ID4D diagnostic says the national ID's NIN was populated into the eCRVS to cross-verify parents' IDs when new NINs were issued after the mass enrolment](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). [The diagnostic says user acceptance testing of an integrated eCRVS and electronic ID to produce health insurance cards was completed in April 2025, with testing for driver's licence and ID cards due in May 2025, under World Bank operation P173287](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).
