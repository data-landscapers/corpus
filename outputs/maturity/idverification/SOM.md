# Somalia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Office of the Accountant General announced in a letter of 29 August 2026 that the government payroll and SFMIS will be integrated with NIRA's national identification system to verify public employees' identity, with a pilot from 1 October 2026 and nationwide completion by 31 December 2027](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-to-link-government-payroll-system-with-national-id-database/>). [The HSC service checks students' identity against the eAqoonsi/NIRA ID through GovStack's Identity Verification and Consent block, per GovStack](<https://govstack.global/showcase/somalia-hsc/>). [UNDP Somalia reports that the Hubiye eKYC platform, formalised by Somalia's first eKYC legislation in December 2024, gives real-time identity verification across 25 integrated services including the entire commercial banking sector](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>).

**Used across sectors.** [UNDP Somalia reports Hubiye eKYC verification across 25 integrated services spanning government and private sector platforms, including the whole commercial banking sector](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [NIRA says banks (KYC), telecoms (mandatory SIM registration), the Civil Service Commission (payroll identity verification) and the education ministry use the national ID for verification](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [UNDP Somalia reports that Hubiye eKYC serves 1.2 million citizens with real-time identity verification across 25 integrated services](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [The Central Bank of Somalia says about 20 thousand verifications were made since September 2025, and NIRA says 100+ services are integrated](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-3-Somalia-FINAL.pdf>).
