# Namibia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [OPM's Director of Architecture said government has already saved about N$16 million on social grants through electronic identity verification, including preventing payments to deceased beneficiaries](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>). [Namibia's Chief Registrar reports that the home affairs and social protection ministries integrated the National Population Registration System with the social protection system, which verifies beneficiaries' existence and live status against the population register and eliminated ghost beneficiaries](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>). [Instant Payments Namibia's COO said the instant payment system, at the stage of deploying infrastructure, will need to use the national registration database for onboarding, with identity verification likely done by another entity](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/namibia-weighs-biometrics-options-for-foundation-of-instant-payment-system>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>).
