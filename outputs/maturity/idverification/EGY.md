# Egypt

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Digified, licensed by the financial regulator, [checks clients of lenders, investors and insurers against the national ID database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/digified-licensed-to-offer-digital-kyc-e-signature-services-in-egypt>). In September 2026 the Public Prosecution [opened copies of personal-status documents only to users verified by the Digital Egypt identity](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%88%D8%AB%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7/7541763>). Its [online enquiry service on family cases](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/1/النيابة-العامة-تتيح-خدمة-الاستعلام-الإلكترونى-عن-دعاوى-الأسرة-والأحكام/7533251>) has the same rule. [Digital Egypt accounts are opened with the national ID number and the mobile line registered to it](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/02/your-guide-to-navigating-egypts-online-government-services/>). In August 2026 the communications ministry said [it had built a platform with the civil status sector](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ministry-of-communications-and-information-technology_egypt-digitaltransformation-digitalidentity-activity-7498343390770712576-h8Si>) for telecom operators to verify identity biometrically, in a pilot rolled out in stages.

**Used across sectors.** Checks run in finance and justice. [Digified verifies clients for lending, investment and insurance companies](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/digified-licensed-to-offer-digital-kyc-e-signature-services-in-egypt>). [The Public Prosecution's portal admits only users verified by the Digital Egypt identity](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D9%88%D8%AB%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7/7541763>). The Ministry of Social Solidarity's [foster-family services sign citizens in through the digital identity system](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/29/التضامن-تطلق-أولى-مراحل-منظومة-الخدمات-الرقمية-الخاصة-بالأسر-البديلة/7529204>). In August 2026 [SIM registration began comparing each applicant's selfie with the national ID photograph](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/egypt-expands-biometric-sim-registration-after-identity-misuse-case>).

**Use reported.** [Digified says it handles over 25 million verifications a year](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/digified-licensed-to-offer-digital-kyc-e-signature-services-in-egypt>) for lenders, investors and insurers.
