# Malawi

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [IANS reports that on 29 September 2026 India's High Commissioner met UNDP and UNCDF to review the e-KYC and Digital Identity Project in Malawi, funded through the India-UN Development Partnership Fund and implemented with the Reserve Bank of Malawi; the report states no verification service in use](<https://ianslive.in/india-un-agencies-review-e-kyc-and-digital-identity-project-in-malawi--20260929225238>). [Malawi24 reports that the National Registration (Amendment) Regulations 2026, gazetted 31 July 2026, set NRB charges for e-KYC verification (K350 basic, K450 intermediate, K500 advanced) and K500 once-off for mobile money subscribers, SIM card holders, bank account holders and utility services](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>).

**Used across sectors.** [The 2026 NRB fee regulations name e-KYC verification charges for mobile money subscribers, SIM card holders, bank account holders and utility services, per Malawi24](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>). [The NRB homepage lists National ID linkages with public bodies (Malawi Revenue Authority, Immigration, Electoral Commission, Financial Intelligence Authority, Department of Human Resource, Ministry of Agriculture) and private telecoms and banks](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>).

**Use reported.** [UNDP Malawi reports the platform authenticated more than 1.2 million farmers nationwide under the Farm Input Subsidy Programme](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-earns-global-recognition-digital-innovation-e-government>). [The NRB homepage states 7.2 million Malawians were verified for voter registration for the 2025 election and biometric verification covered 1.5 million FISP beneficiaries](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>). [Offline verification has authenticated 3.5 million citizens](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/undp_malawi_idt4m_quarterly_report_jul-sep25_web.pdf>).
