# Malawi

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** The National Registration Bureau lists [links with Airtel Malawi for SIM registration and mobile money, and with seven banks and Mukuru for KYC](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>). Its [2026 fee regulations](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>) set charges for e-KYC checks. UNDP says [Airtel and TNM tied biometric SIM registration to the Bureau's systems](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>) in 2025. The Nzika digital ID wallet, launched on 16 September 2026, [was still piloted with one bank in November, and some banks refused it](<https://malawi24.com/2026/11/09/malawis-nzika-digital-id-bounced-at-the-bank/>).

**Used across sectors.** The [2026 fee regulations](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>) charge for checks of mobile money subscribers, SIM card holders, bank account holders and utility customers. The Bureau also lists [links with the revenue authority, immigration, the Electoral Commission and the agriculture ministry](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>).

**Use reported.** UNDP reports that [the Bureau's QR verification platform authenticated more than 1.2 million farmers](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-earns-global-recognition-digital-innovation-e-government>) for the Farm Input Subsidy Programme. The Bureau says [7.2 million people were verified for voter registration](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw>) for the 2025 election.
