# Algeria

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Echorouk reports CNEP-Banque, Housing Bank for Trade and Finance and Banque Nationale d'Algerie are verifying customers' identity and updating files, which can require a valid identity card; it does not say checks are made against the national ID register](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/توضيحات-أكبر-لـالبنوك-الجزائرية-حول>). [The Sports Ministry says holders of the national digital identity issued by the HCN can now access its WADHIFATI platform without creating an account, the user being identified through the unified digital identity](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>). [The Interior Minister says public bodies connected to the automated NIN generation system obtain identity information electronically, sparing holders of biometric ID documents from presenting several documents](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/توضيحات-أكبر-لـالبنوك-الجزائرية-حول>).
