# Algeria

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In December 2025 the Interior Minister said [public bodies connected to the national identification number system obtain identity data electronically](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>). Holders of biometric ID documents no longer present several papers. In August 2026 the Sports Ministry's [WADHIFATI platform began identifying users through the national digital identity](<https://www.horizons.dz/2026/08/les-plateformes-du-ministere-des-sports-integrees-dans-lecosysteme-national-des-services-numeriques/>). [The ID card also authenticates users on the secure-document and civil-status platforms](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).

**Used across sectors.** Bank of Algeria's [Instruction 04-2026 requires banks and Algérie Poste to verify customers' identity](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%81-%D8%B2%D8%A8%D9%88%D9%86%D9%83-%D8%A8%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AF%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A7>). It does not name the national ID. In October 2026 the digitalisation commission [presented a kit for reading biometric documents](<https://news.prixalgerie.com/identite-numerique-algerie-gouvernance-donnees/>). It is meant for services that verify identity.

**Use reported.** The [Interior Minister's December 2025 statement](<https://www.algerie360.com/les-administrations-nont-plus-le-droit-dexiger-ces-documents-saayoud-promet-des-sanctions-severes/>) covers every public body connected to the identification number system.

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking people against the ID. The banks' KYC rules do not name the national ID register.
