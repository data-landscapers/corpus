# Zambia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The FISP e-Voucher farm-input subsidy uses eKYC checks to cut ghost beneficiaries](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>). It ran in all 116 districts by January 2026. [Home Affairs charges banks about 80 cents for each successful API check, the Bank of Zambia said in May 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>). [The central bank is also building a central eKYC platform on the Home Affairs digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>), with a fingerprint check. [Developers were trained in 2025 to link the SmartCarePro health record to the ID](<https://idtechwire.com/zambia-advances-digital-id-system-with-mosip-training-for-healthcare-integration>).

**Used across sectors.** Two sectors check people against the ID in use: [the farm-input voucher](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>) and [the banks](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>). [Co-Develop, a funder, says the check also reached drought relief and health records in 2024](<https://www.codevelop.fund/zambia>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact is [the fee Home Affairs charges banks for each check](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/id4africa-panel-outlines-steady-kyc-process-policy-improvement>).
