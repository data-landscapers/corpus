# Zambia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Presidential Delivery Unit says the FISP e-Voucher system, a farm-input subsidy programme, uses electronic Know-Your-Customer verification to cut duplication and ghost beneficiaries, rolled out to all 116 districts with redemption closing on 15 January 2026, though the document does not name the national ID register as the thing checked](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>). [SMART Zambia developers were trained on integrating MOSIP with the SmartCarePro electronic health record system, aimed at identity verification using National Registration Card data, and Zambia is preparing to launch an open-source eKYC system on MOSIP, per ID Tech](<https://idtechwire.com/zambia-advances-digital-id-system-with-mosip-training-for-healthcare-integration>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.pdu.gov.zm/blog/how-fisp-e-voucher-is-delivering-support-where-it-matters-most>).
