# Mauritania

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Tech Rek states the Khadamati app, which groups more than 15 digital public services, relies on Houwiyeti to verify users' national identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [ID Tech Wire reports a TECH5/SmartMS/UNDP pilot for MTNIMA, demonstrated in March 2023, in which a citizen matches an e-passport or ID card by selfie, stores a digital ID on the phone and uses biometric login to the government e-services portal, leading to the Houwiyeti app launched in 2024 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://idtechwire.com/mauritania-launches-digital-id-for-e-services-built-on-tech5-platform>).

**Used across sectors.** [Tech Rek states Houwiyeti is the gateway public and private services use to verify citizens' identity, with private financial apps using the identity verification system at user registration alongside the public Khadamati services](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID.
