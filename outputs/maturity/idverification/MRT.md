# Mauritania

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** The Khadamati app [relies on Houwiyeti to verify users' national identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>), Tech Rek reported in August 2026. Khadamati groups more than 15 digital public services. On 24 March 2026 the data protection authority [gave a conditional favourable opinion on Maadeni](<https://www.apd.mr/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d8%af%d8%a7%d9%88%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85002-2026-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d8%b6%d9%85%d9%86-%d8%b1%d8%a3%d9%8a%d8%a7-%d8%a8%d8%ae%d8%b5%d9%88%d8%b5-%d9%85%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%b1-%d9%85/>), Maaden Mauritanie's information system for gold operators. Its stated purposes include a link to the national population register and the Hawiyati service. A pilot shown in March 2023 [let citizens log in to the government e-services portal with a digital ID held on the phone](<https://idtechwire.com/mauritania-launches-digital-id-for-e-services-built-on-tech5-platform>). It led to the Houwiyeti app, launched in 2024.

**Used across sectors.** Houwiyeti is [the gateway public and private services use to verify citizens' identity](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>), Tech Rek reported in August 2026. Private financial apps use it to check users when they register. Public services reach it through the Khadamati app.

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techrek.info/fr/houwiyeti-de-nouveau-operationnelle-de-la-maintenance-periodique-a-la-question-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>).
