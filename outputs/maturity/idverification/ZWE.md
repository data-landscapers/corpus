# Zimbabwe

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In June 2026 the Reserve Bank governor said [all mobile money providers verify customers against the Civil Registry](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>) at onboarding and in later reviews. Every wallet was rescreened against it by 30 June 2026. [Ghost and duplicate wallets were shut down](<https://businesstimes.co.zw/rbz-shuts-down-ghost-wallets/>) once the registry was linked to subscriber records. The police's [Electronic Traffic Management System is linked to the Civil Registry](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/electronic-traffic-system-being-finalised/>) to identify offenders, but its first phase was still awaiting commissioning in October 2026.

**Used across sectors.** Checks run in finance and elections. [Mobile money providers verify customers against the Civil Registry](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>). In July 2026 the Justice Minister said [the Electoral Commission was already using the Registrar-General's population database](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>) to verify voters and clean up the roll.

**Use reported.** The Reserve Bank says [every wallet was rescreened against the registry](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>) by 30 June 2026.

***Also on file***. [Collateral Registry System](<https://collateralregistry.rbz.co.zw/docs/CRS%20Public%20User%20Manual.pdf>).
