# Rwanda

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [NIDA's Director General said banks, hospitals, telecom companies and government institutions are expected to use the SDID authentication system by the June 2027 deadline, and that the digital ID will simplify Know Your Customer processes for service providers](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>). [A pilot of biometric SIM registration was launched under the new RURA regulations, which require operators to verify customers' fingerprints and face against NIDA data and to link each SIM to the owner's unique identification number](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202409/rwanda-conducts-pilot-to-implement-biometric-sim-registration-verification>). [RURA Regulation No. 18/R/SM-ICT/RURA/2024 of 9 August 2024 sets up SIM-registration rules that include identity verification against the national-ID database](<https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Sectors/ICT/Regulatory_Instruments/ICT_Regulations_and_Guidelines/Regulation_Governing_SIM_Card_Registration_in_Rwanda.pdf>).
