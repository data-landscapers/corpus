# Rwanda

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In May 2026 the ID agency showed [the digital ID integrating with Irembo, the social registry, e-Ubuzima health, the education system and finance eKYC](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>). It described these as a ready integration pathway. The agency's head said [banks, hospitals, telecoms and government bodies are expected to use its authentication by June 2027](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>). A [2024 regulation requires SIM registration to be checked against the national ID database](<https://www.rura.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RURA/Documents/Sectors/ICT/Regulatory_Instruments/ICT_Regulations_and_Guidelines/Regulation_Governing_SIM_Card_Registration_in_Rwanda.pdf>). Operators [piloted biometric SIM registration](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202409/rwanda-conducts-pilot-to-implement-biometric-sim-registration-verification>) under it.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>).
