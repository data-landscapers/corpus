# Lesotho

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** A FinMark Trust blog of June 2026 [lists Lesotho's digital identity cards among domestic eKYC frameworks with financial services use cases](<https://finmark.org.za/knowledge-hub/blog/building-a-cross-border-dpi-for-financial-inclusion-in-sadc>). In September 2025 [Minister Lephema said legal identity had let citizens open bank accounts, access credit and use mobile money](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/>). In August 2025 [Co-Develop reported a FinMark Trust pilot for Lesotho migrants in South Africa](<https://www.codevelop.fund/insights-1/strategic-collaboration-establishes-africas-first-cross-border-digital-identity-framework-enabling-financial-inclusion-for-millions>). The pilot let them use their national digital IDs for remittance services.

**Used across sectors.** In July 2026 [the Independent Electoral Commission told an African Union mission that its voter registration system is linked to the National Identity and Civil Registry](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>). The link runs through Unique Identification Numbers. In January 2024 [the commission had said an earlier link with Home Affairs was abandoned](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/01/30/iec-to-partner-home-affairs-ministry-in-seamless-database-project/>). [Banks and mobile money providers also rely on legal identity](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2025/09/16/education-sector-lags-behind-in-id-integrationlephema/>), the minister said in 2025.

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact is [the electoral commission's count of over 579,520 citizens registered](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>), which counts voters.
