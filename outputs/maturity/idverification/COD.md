# DR Congo

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The digital economy ministry and Equity BCDC, a commercial bank, signed an MoU on 2026-08-13 to modernise the financial ecosystem and promote bank inclusion, including innovations tied to citizen digital identity; the article gives no KYC mechanism](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-signature-dun-protocole-daccord-sur-la-modernisation-de-lecosysteme-financier/>). [Biometric Update reports RDC-Pass, launched in Kinshasa on 13 June 2026, includes biometric SIM card registration and automated identity verification for financial institutions, with rollout in phases starting with technical audits and pilot testing](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>). [Biometric Update reports that DRC Pass lets citizens register SIM cards by linking their biometrics, and that its phased deployment is already underway](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/drc-digital-id-phased-deployment-underway-following-contract-do-over>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-signature-dun-protocole-daccord-sur-la-modernisation-de-lecosysteme-financier/>).
