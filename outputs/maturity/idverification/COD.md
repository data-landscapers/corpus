# DR Congo

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In March 2024 the identity office said [its contract includes a KYC platform for private companies to verify identities](<https://scooprdc.net/2024/03/27/rdc-lonip-presente-le-plan-de-mise-en-oeuvre-du-programme-de-fichier-general-de-la-population-et-de-la-delivrance-de-la-carte-didentite-nationale-aux-congolais/>). Online verification was scheduled from December 2025 to 2028. In June 2026 the government [launched RDC-Pass in Kinshasa](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/drc-launches-rdc-pass-digital-id-platform-under-20-year-ppp-with-trident>). It includes biometric SIM registration and automated identity checks for financial institutions, starting with audits and pilots. In August 2026 the digital economy ministry [signed an MoU with Equity BCDC, a commercial bank](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-signature-dun-protocole-daccord-sur-la-modernisation-de-lecosysteme-financier/>), on bank inclusion tied to digital identity. No source found says a check against the ID is in service.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-signature-dun-protocole-daccord-sur-la-modernisation-de-lecosysteme-financier/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-signature-dun-protocole-daccord-sur-la-modernisation-de-lecosysteme-financier/>).
