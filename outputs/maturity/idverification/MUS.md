# Mauritius

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [L'Express reports that since 29 September 2026 licence holders (about 884,000) can view their digital driving licence on the KOREK! app, with MauPass providing the identification and authentication and police systems the data](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>). [OECD OPSI says government is integrating MauPass across e-services, naming learner's licence application, Public Service Commission posts, MoRendezVous booking, Supreme Court portal, e-payslip, e-HR leave and online motor vehicle licence payment](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>). [OECD OPSI says government e-services such as the MoKloud digital safe use MauPass 2FA, which needs prior KYC registration, to confirm users' identity, with name and ID number validated against the Central Population Database (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>).
