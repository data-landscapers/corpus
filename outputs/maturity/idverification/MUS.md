# Mauritius

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Since 29 September 2026 [about 884,000 licence holders can view their digital driving licence on the KOREK! app](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>). MauPass identifies and authenticates them. The OECD says [government is building MauPass into many e-services](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>), from learner's licences to court and payslip portals. In 2022 [MauPass checked names and ID numbers against the population database](<https://oecd-opsi.org/innovations/maupass/>). In August 2024 the government said [public bodies and businesses would get an app to verify identity through the Mobile ID](<https://defimedia.info/carte-didentite-la-mobile-id-est-une-realite>). The citizen accepts or refuses each check.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lexpress.mu/node/563226>).
