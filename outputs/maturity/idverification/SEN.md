# Senegal

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [IPRES, the pension institution, opened its three-month periodic proof-of-life campaign on 1 July 2026 on the Maa ngi nii platform](<https://aps.sn/lipres-annonce-le-debut-de-la-campagne-periodique-de-collecte-de-preuve-de-vie-ce-mercredi/>). Senegalese pensioners must hold their ECOWAS national identity card to complete the proof of life. [The e-Sénégal single-window app was launched on 24 March 2026 and built by SENUM SA](<https://www.ndarinfo.com/Comment-obtenir-ses-documents-administratifs-en-ligne-avec-la-nouvelle-appli-e-Senegal_a43744.html>). It gives access through authentication on the NIN carried on the biometric identity card. Judicial-record, nationality-certificate and similar procedures were live at launch.
