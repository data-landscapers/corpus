# Angola

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Presidential Despacho 300/26 of 13 August 2026 authorised USD 28 million and opened the public tender for a national digital identity and e-signature platform meant to let citizens and companies electronically verify the identity of their customers, per PTI](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>).
