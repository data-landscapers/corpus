# Angola

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** A presidential order, Despacho 300/26 of 13 August 2026, [authorised US$28 million and opened a public tender](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>) for a national digital identity and e-signature platform. The platform is meant to let citizens and companies verify the identity of their customers electronically. It is to be bought through that tender, so no service checks people against it yet.

**Used across sectors.** The [platform put out to tender in August 2026](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>) is meant for citizens and companies alike. It joins identity checks and electronic signatures in one system, for use by any company that needs to know who its customers are.

**Use reported.** The [August 2026 order](<https://pti.ao/pr-aprova-usd-28-milhoes-para-aquisicao-de-plataforma-de-identidade-digital-e-assinaturas-electronicas/>) funds a platform that is still to be bought. The US$28 million it authorises covers both the digital identity and the electronic signatures.
