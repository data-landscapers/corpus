# Togo

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** ANID's 2026 deck says [the unique identification number is used through verification and authentication](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>) across the ecosystem. [It is used to sign in to the public services portal](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>). A [December 2024 decree requires telecom operators to record each subscriber's ANID identification number](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>) at SIM registration and to see the card, giving existing operators 12 months to comply.

**Used across sectors.** The same deck shows [the identification number in use in microfinance institutions](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>) as well as on the public services portal. The [2024 decree](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>) has telecom operators record the number for nationals and residents alike.

**Use reported.** [ANID's 2026 deck](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>) describes the number in use for verification and authentication. It also shows the e-ID card in use in microfinance institutions.

***Also on file***. [NIU / carte d'identification unique](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>); [Registre social des personnes et des ménages](<https://www.jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_30_09_2024%20-%2069E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B0%2098%20TER.pdf>).

***Not found***. Nothing found shows services in two or more sectors checking people against the ID. Microfinance use of the card is not described as a check.
