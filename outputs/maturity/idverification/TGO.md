# Togo

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Décret n° 2024-071/PR of 26 December 2024 requires telecom operators to record the ANID-issued NIU of every national and resident subscriber at SIM registration and to see the ANID identification card, giving existing operators 12 months from signature to comply; the text states no query of ANID's database](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_23_04_2025%20-70%20E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B044%20BIS.pdf>).
