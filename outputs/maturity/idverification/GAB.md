# Gabon

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Gabonreview reports that on 28 April 2026 the DGI, CNSS and CNAMGS launched work on the Declaration unique des salaires, in which the NIF for employers and the NIP for employees becomes compulsory, with operation targeted for July 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.
