# Gabon

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Digital Gabon financing agreement, amended with effect from 2 September 2026, funds digital authentication of social protection beneficiaries against the RBPP ID system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>). The same amendment funds better ID authentication for the civil service HR system. [In April 2026 the tax authority and the social funds began a single salary declaration](<https://www.gabonreview.com/dus-un-tournant-numerique-pour-la-transparence-salariale-au-gabon/>) in which the employee's personal ID number becomes compulsory. No service in use is shown checking people against the ID.

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. [The 2026 financing agreement](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>) names social protection and the civil service as the two planned uses.

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact is [the planned authentication of social protection beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [The electoral list consultation platform](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>).
