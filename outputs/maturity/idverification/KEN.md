# Kenya

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Kenya's 2026 ID4Africa presentation says IPRS and eCitizen give real-time authentication against the national ID](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>). [Civil Registration Services says parents' ID details are verified during birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [A 2026 health campaign is enrolling children aged 7 to 17 into Maisha Namba](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/kenya-expands-digital-id-program-to-minors-through-health-sector-rollout>), to link each child's health record to the ID. [SIM registration rules of 2025 require biometric and ID verification](<https://usajili.go.ke/refugees-can-now-register-sim-cards-using-refugee-id-new-telecom-regulations-affirm>).

**Used across sectors.** [The 2026 presentation lists six sectors connected to ID verification](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>). They are tax, health insurance, education, bank eKYC, SIM registration and social protection. [A 2024 government presentation names SIM registration and bank account opening](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-2-Kenya-Final.pdf>) as transactions validated through the Maisha Card.

**Use reported.** [The 2026 presentation counts over 22,000 government services online that rely on the digital ID for access](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [SIM registration (KYC)](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/sim-deactivation-now-backed-by-law-ca-says-5622384>). [The Maisha Integrated Population Register](<https://usajili.go.ke/integrated-population-registration-service>). [The Huduma card](<https://humanglemedia.com/nigerias-disharmonised-digital-system-leaving-low-income-farmers-behind/>). [The national digital registry for farmers](<https://humanglemedia.com/nigerias-disharmonised-digital-system-leaving-low-income-farmers-behind/>). [National Population Data Management, formerly IPRS](<https://usajili.go.ke/national-population-data-management-npdm-formerly-iprs>).

***Not found***. No count of individual checks against the ID has been found.
