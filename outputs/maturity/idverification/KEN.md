# Kenya

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Kenya's Civil Registration Services says its CRVS system is integrated with the National ID system, so parents' National ID details are held in birth registration records and parents' details are verified during birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [Tech-ish reports that Starlink Kenya recently enforced ID verification so subscribers meet the national KYC requirements set by the Communications Authority; it does not say what the ID is checked against](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/06/19/starlink-hits-25000-subscribers-in-kenya/>). [Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary said a biometric campaign under the Social Health Authority system is enrolling children aged 7 to 17 into Maisha Namba, to link each child's health record to the digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/kenya-expands-digital-id-program-to-minors-through-health-sector-rollout>).

**Used across sectors.** [The same presentation gives SIM card registration (telecoms) and bank account opening (banking) as transactions the Maisha Card is validated for through KYC](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-2-Kenya-Final.pdf>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps1/PS1-2-Kenya-Final.pdf>).
