# Morocco

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The DGSN and the Ministry of Digital Transition and Administration Reform signed a deal to let all government entities digitise services accessed through the national biometric digital ID, with a legal and organisational blueprint for identity verification through a trusted third-party service](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/morocco-boosts-digital-id-use-for-govt-services-with-new-public-sector-partnerships>). [The DGSN 2024 annual report says more than 30 public and private institutions were authorised to use its Trusted Third Party Identity Verification platform, integrating criminal records, prison administration and foreign trade](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>).

**Used across sectors.** [The DGSN says its verification platform has 17 framework agreements covering Bank Al-Maghrib, banking groups and healthcare providers, and authorised institutions in criminal records, prison administration and foreign trade](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>).

**Use reported.** [The DGSN counts 17 framework agreements (including Bank Al-Maghrib, the Moroccan Banking Association and the Ministry of Health) and over 30 approved public and private institutions for direct use of the platform in 2024](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/12/24/2024-dgsn-recap-cnie-2-0-expands-from-identification-to-comprehensive-digital-service-tool/>). [The DGSN reports 17 framework agreements and more than 30 public and private institutions authorised to use the verification platform in 2024](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>).
