# Morocco

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In 2026 [registrations for social programmes were checked against the population register through its authentication service](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>). In 2024 [more than 30 public and private bodies were authorised to use the police's identity verification platform](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). In 2025 the police and the digital transition ministry [agreed to let all government bodies verify the digital ID through a trusted third party](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/morocco-boosts-digital-id-use-for-govt-services-with-new-public-sector-partnerships>).

**Used across sectors.** The police platform has [17 framework agreements, with the central bank, banking groups and health providers](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>). Criminal records, prison administration and foreign trade use it too. [Six social programmes are integrated with the population register](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>), by a 2026 count.

**Use reported.** A 2026 presentation counts [more than 70 million authentications and 47 million KYC checks](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Collaborations_between_Governments_and_Academic_Institutions.pdf>) against the population register. In 2024 the police counted [17 framework agreements and more than 30 authorised bodies](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/12/24/2024-dgsn-recap-cnie-2-0-expands-from-identification-to-comprehensive-digital-service-tool/>).
