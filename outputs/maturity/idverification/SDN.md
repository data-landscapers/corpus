# Sudan

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [SUNA reports Omdurman National Bank became the first bank in Sudan to activate the link, so customers inside and outside Sudan update their data on its digital channels with SudaPass verifying their identity](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>). [The Central Bank of Sudan issued a circular adopting the national number for opening bank accounts and requiring banks to check the number the customer gives against the Civil Registry's system](<https://cbos.gov.sd/ar/news/%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A>). [SUNA reports the Ministry of Digital Transformation, with the Central Bank of Sudan, is connecting the remaining banks one by one as each is technically ready, each to announce its own go-live](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/bnk-am-drman-alotny-aol-bnk-ytyh-thdyth-byanat-alaamlaaa-rkmya-bastkhdam-sodabas>).
