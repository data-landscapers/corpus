# Guinea

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [On 22 January 2026 the electoral body DGE and ONECI signed a convention on voter identification](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [Under it, the national register of persons (RNPP) is to be the sole official source of voter identification data](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [That data feeds the biometric electoral file](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the RNPP was to serve one sector, elections](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [in January 2026 the RNPP's role in the electoral file was set by a signed convention](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>).
