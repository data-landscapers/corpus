# Cape Verde

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Government of Cabo Verde says the Portal de Justiça, launched on 18 December 2024, is integrated with the state's Autentika platform and supports several user authentication methods for services including certificates and online renewal of the CNI](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>). [The PDEX page says all integrations of the Consular Portal with the civil identification system run through PDEX, giving access to citizens' data; it does not say the portal verifies people](<https://pdex.gov.cv/>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>).

**Use reported.** [The Autentika page states that more than 95,000 users use the platform daily to reach work applications](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/>).
