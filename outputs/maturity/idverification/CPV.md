# Cape Verde

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** The Portal de Justiça, launched on 18 December 2024, [is integrated with the state's Autentika platform](<https://www.governo.cv/primeiro-ministro-preside-acto-de-lancamento-oficial-do-portal-de-justica/>). Citizens use it for certificates and to renew the national ID card online. In May 2026 the justice modernisation institute IMIJ said [SNIAC, the national identification and authentication system, links more than 50 services](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-44>). In March 2026 SNIAC [published an ID card toolkit](<https://sniac.cv/mw/>) for public or private services to authenticate card holders in their own applications.

**Used across sectors.** SNIAC [serves the Justice, Consular and National portals](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-44>), IMIJ said in May 2026. It also connects private services, banks above all.

**Use reported.** IMIJ [counted more than 30 million data requests on SNIAC and over 50 connected services](<https://imij.gov.cv/noticias/cms-noticia-44>) in May 2026. The Autentika page [says more than 95,000 users use the platform daily](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/>) to reach work applications.
