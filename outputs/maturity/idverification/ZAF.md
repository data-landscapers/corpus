# South Africa

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Mobile operators [verify customers against the Home Affairs database](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>), and the minister says banks use the same checks. In September 2026 the minister said [voter registration is verified through an electronic interface with the population register](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/40687/>). The health ministry says [its patient registration system is adding real-time checks against the register](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/>). In September 2025 the Treasury was [testing Home Affairs data to flag public servants without valid IDs](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/national-treasury-deploys-tech-to-terminate-ghost-workers/GxwQDM1DWXR7lPVo>).

**Used across sectors.** Banks, mobile networks and insurers [use the population register check](<https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/>), among them Capitec, TymeBank, Vodacom and MTN. The [Electoral Commission checks voters against it](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/40687/>).

**Use reported.** SASSA [biometrically verified 997,379 grant beneficiaries](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/37674/>) from September 2025 to March 2026. Home Affairs says [the justice cluster alone runs over 180,000 checks a day](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-launch-world-class-new-identity-verification-service-and-cost>) through its service.
