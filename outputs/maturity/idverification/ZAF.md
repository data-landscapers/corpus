# South Africa

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Mobile operators and the Department of Justice propose reforms to SIM registration under RICA that add real-time checks against the Home Affairs database; ACT has not disclosed how the new process will work](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>). [The telecoms industry body ACT says its members verify customer identities against the Department of Home Affairs database under existing arrangements with mobile network operators, and the Home Affairs minister said its verification systems are already used by banks](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>). [The Minister of Health says the Health Patient Registration System's biometric verification is being implemented with Home Affairs for real-time identity verification against National Population Register data](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/31749/>).

**Used across sectors.** [Mobile network operators verify identities against the Home Affairs database and that Home Affairs verification systems are already used by banks, so telecoms and banking both check against it](<https://techcabal.com/2026/07/20/south-africa-wants-every-sim-card-to-become-a-trusted-digital-id/>). [The article names banks (TymeBank, Capitec), mobile networks (Vodacom, MTN) and insurers as users of the NPR verification check](<https://weetracker.com/2026/01/27/south-africa-id-verification-fee-court-case/>).

**Use reported.** [The Minister reported that 997,379 social grant beneficiaries were biometrically verified at SASSA local offices from September 2025 to 11 March 2026, with monthly counts from 59,689 (March, part month) to 186,995 (February)](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/37674/>). [Home Affairs says the justice cluster alone processes over 180,000 transactions per day through the service, and one private user has processed over one million batch records](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/home-affairs-launch-world-class-new-identity-verification-service-and-cost>).
