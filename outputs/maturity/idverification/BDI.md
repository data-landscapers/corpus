# Burundi

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [Connecting Africa reports mandatory SIM and eSIM registration in Burundi began in mid-June 2026, requiring registrants to present legible, up-to-date identification documents at a registration point, with ARCT interconnecting operators and state bodies for real-time monitoring of registration and user identity](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/burundi-sim-registration-process-faces-challenges-scams-persist>). [Agence Ecofin reports ARCT's communique of 3 June 2026 requiring Burundian mobile subscribers to regularise SIM identification by 25 June 2026 or have lines deactivated, with only the carte nationale d'identite accepted for the operation, under a ministerial ordinance of 13 January 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0506-139050-burundi-identification-des-cartes-sim-obligatoire-avant-le-25-juin>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/burundi-sim-registration-process-faces-challenges-scams-persist>).

**Use reported.** [Connecting Africa reports ARCT's director general said 70% of SIM cards had been registered, over three months after the campaign began](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/burundi-sim-registration-process-faces-challenges-scams-persist>).
