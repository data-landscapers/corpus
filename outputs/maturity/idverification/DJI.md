# Djibouti

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In July 2026 the director general of the population and family directorate said [it is building an interoperability system](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). Administrations, banks and social protection bodies would then be able to check the authenticity of a national ID card in real time. He spoke of the system in the future tense, as work still under way.

**Used across sectors.** The [planned system](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>) names three kinds of user: public administrations, banks and social protection bodies. That would span government, finance and social protection once it is in service. None of them checks cards against it yet.

**Use reported.** The [July 2026 interview](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>) describes a system still being built. It gives no count of checks or of the bodies to be connected.
