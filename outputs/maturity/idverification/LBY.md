# Libya

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The General Information Authority and the Central Bank of Libya reviewed linking the Level 3 biometric digital identity to banking systems for eKYC, and technical trials with the Central Bank and a number of banks had already begun as a prelude to launch and integration](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Used across sectors.** No source held since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).

**Use reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>).
