# Seychelles

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Agency for Social Protection launched its ASP Connect platform internally on 15 October 2025](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>). [It is reached through the SeyID portal, which hosts all government entities' systems](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>). [Government e-services that require SeyID need a 60% validation level](<https://seyid.gov.sc>). [That level is reached by checking the holder's NIN card or passport video against the Civil Status System](<https://seyid.gov.sc>). [At its launch on 1 November 2022 SeyID was being integrated with eGovernment services and financial institutions](<https://idtechwire.com/seychelles-makes-digital-id-mandatory-for-work-permit-holders>).

**Used across sectors.** No source found since October 2023 shows services in two or more sectors checking against the ID. On 4 April 2025 the Central Bank [directed banks, payment providers and other reporting entities](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/aml/Directives/CBS_Directive_1_of_2025-_SeyID_.pdf>) to begin integrating SeyID into customer due diligence, with physical ID still accepted. The nearest fact: [in October 2025 ASP Connect was reached through the SeyID portal](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest fact: [the SeyID portal hosts all government entities' systems](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/27935/asp-launches-digital-system>).
