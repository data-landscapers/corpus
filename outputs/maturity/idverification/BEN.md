# Benin

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The World Bank feature says the WURI project supports the government's safety net programmes and the operationalisation of the Single Social Registry, without saying that the registry checks people against the ID](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [The World Bank feature says holders of the 'C'est Moi' ID card have used it to open bank accounts and a Mobile Money account and to take loans, and ANIP's director general says the ID lets people open an account and own a mobile phone; it gives no figure and does not describe the check itself](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>).

**Used across sectors.** [The playbook names phone-number acquisition (telecoms) and public service platforms as conditioned on NPI authentication](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [The World Bank blog names microcredit (finance) and the education system among the programmes using the QR-code authentication app for fID certificates, and lists social protection, health, education, finance and SIM providers as services the fID enables](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/identification-system-in-benin-increasing-access-to-services>). ANIP lists health, education, social protection, telecoms, banks and tax among the [user systems on its interoperability platform](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [ANIP reports 25 million digital eKYC verifications completed in 2025 and 3.8 million authentications by telecom operators](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/benin-reports-75-of-identification-services-accessed-through-digital-platforms>). [The playbook reports 25 million identity verifications (eKYC) in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). A second ANIP presentation of May 2026 counts [27 million identity verification requests in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
