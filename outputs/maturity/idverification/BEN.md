# Benin

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** Since 1 November 2026 [the criminal record certificate on service-public.bj is issued at once](<https://beninwebtv.bj/en/digitization-in-benin-criminal-records-now-issued-instantly-and-without-supporting-documents/>). The system checks the applicant against the Personal Identification Number and the civil registry. [The World Bank says holders of the C'est Moi ID card have used it to open bank and mobile money accounts and to take loans](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). The same feature says the WURI project supports the Single Social Registry. It does not say that the registry checks people against the ID. Since December 2021 the tax authority's online platform [fetches a person's identity data from the ID agency by their number](<https://lamarina.bj/2026/11/06/benin-8-millions-de-dollars-pour-renforcer-linterconnexion-des-fichiers-fiscaux-et-didentification/>) when it issues a tax number.

**Used across sectors.** The ID agency's playbook says [getting a phone number and using public service platforms both require authentication by the identity number](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). The World Bank names [microcredit and the education system among programmes using the ID's QR-code check](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/identification-system-in-benin-increasing-access-to-services>). The agency lists health, education, social protection, telecoms, banks and tax among the [systems on its interoperability platform](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Use reported.** [ANIP reports 25 million digital eKYC verifications completed in 2025 and 3.8 million authentications by telecom operators](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/benin-reports-75-of-identification-services-accessed-through-digital-platforms>). [The playbook reports 25 million identity verifications (eKYC) in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). A second ANIP presentation of May 2026 counts [27 million identity verification requests in 2025](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [By 11 July 2026 the government's data-exchange dashboard showed 35,892,607 requests received by ANIP](<https://statistiques.xroad.bj/>), its second most-used provider.
