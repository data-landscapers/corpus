# Tunisia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [A circular of the Ministry of Agriculture, Water Resources and Fisheries requires the bodies under its tutelage to integrate E-Houwiya, the standard for authenticating users, into their platforms and online services by 15 November 2026, building on a pilot that put authorisations for the public hydraulic domain through E-Houwiya](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>). [The Registre National des Entreprises states that from 1 July 2026 all its procedures are online only and access requires a digital identity, Mobile ID for natural persons and DigiGO for legal persons](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>).

**Used across sectors.** [The playbook lists E-Houwiya integration across telecoms, banking, health insurance, social security, education and public portals](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [Biometric Update says E-Houwiya serves banking KYC and onboarding and works as single sign-on for the government portals E-Bawaba and E-CNAM](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/tunisia-drives-digitization-agenda-forward-while-navigating-risks>). [The author describes E-Houwiya as used in banking (onboarding, wire transfers) and in government portals, including E-CNAM, E-CNSS and Madrassati](<https://idtechwire.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025>).

**Use reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for verifications or counts the services relying on the ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
