# Tunisia

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** E-Houwiya is [open to banks for remote onboarding and KYC](<https://idtechwire.com/e-houwiya-a-milestone-in-tunisias-digital-transformation-roadmap-2025>) under Central Bank rules of February 2025. Since 1 July 2026 [the national business register's procedures have required a digital identity](<https://home.registre-entreprises.tn/le-registre-national-des-entreprises-vers-le-full-digital/>), Mobile ID for people. The [investment authority requires Mobile ID for investment declarations](<https://www.apia.com.tn/ar/actualites/detail/217>) from the same date. A [September 2026 circular gives the agriculture ministry's bodies until 15 November 2026](<https://www.tunisie-tribune.com/2026/09/18/numerisation-le-ministere-de-lagriculture-integre-lidentite-numerique-e-houwiya-dici-novembre-2026/>) to authenticate users through E-Houwiya.

**Used across sectors.** The 2026 ID4Africa playbook lists [Tunisie Telecom, CNAM, CNSS, five banks, La Poste and insurers](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>) among E-Houwiya's integrators. It shows [use in telecoms, banking, health insurance, social security, education and public portals](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).

**Use reported.** The [playbook](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>) names the integrators one by one. Five banks are among them, beside the telecom operator, the health and social security funds and the postal service.
