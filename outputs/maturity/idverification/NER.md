# Niger

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** In July 2026 the WURI Niger project said it is [building a National Population Register](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>) to identify and authenticate anyone seeking public or private services that require identification. Its first enrolment, in Niamey's five arrondissements, was due within weeks. The project also [funds an interoperability framework to offer identity verification and authentication](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>) to public and private service providers.

**Used across sectors.** The project names [banks, telecom operators, money transfer firms, social protection, tax and social security bodies](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>) as the services it will serve. None of them checks people against the register yet.

**Use reported.** The [register](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>) had yet to enrol anyone in July 2026, so there were no checks to count. The [verification framework](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>) is funded under the project's third component.
