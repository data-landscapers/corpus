# Niger

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The WURI Niger project says it is developing a National Population Register meant to identify and authenticate anyone requesting public or private services that require prior identification, with a first enrolment phase in Niamey's five arrondissements to start in the coming weeks](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>). [WURI-Niger's Component 3 funds the commissioning of an interoperability framework to offer identity verification and authentication services to public and private service providers; banks, telecom operators, money transfer firms, social protection, tax and social security bodies are named as direct beneficiaries, with no service stated as in use](<https://www.lesahel.org/presentation-du-projet-didentification-unique-pour-lintegration-regionale-et-linclusion-en-afrique-de-louest-wuri-niger-un-projet-pour-renforcer-l/>).
