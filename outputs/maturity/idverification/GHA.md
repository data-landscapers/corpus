# Ghana

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [In February 2024 the NIA said banks and other financial institutions verify clients against its database in real time](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/191-financial-institutions-now-have-seamless-access-to-National-Identification-Authority-Data-1914728>). [In July 2025 it named its IVSP as the only approved way to verify a Ghana Card](<https://nia.gov.gh/institutions-urged-to-use-nia-ivsp-for-authentic-ghana-card-verification/>). [In August 2026 the Interior Minister said public and private institutions use the Ghana Card to verify identity](<https://today.com.gh/muntaka-nia-clears-ghana-card-backlog-registers-418000-children/>). [Parliament passed rules in 2026 for a biometric SIM verification phase](<https://asaaseradio.com/the-resolve-of-this-ministry-is-unshaken-sam-george-on-biometric-sim-verification/>), due before the end of the year.

**Used across sectors.** [In May 2026 the NIA said its onboarded institutions include banks, government bodies, fintechs and telecoms firms](<https://icnghana.com/nia-has-registered-over-19-4-million-ghanaians-executive-secretary/>). Since January 2024 [the government payroll has checked employees and pensioners against the NIA database](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>) to remove ghost names. [The NIA also names healthcare and taxation](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-card-non-negotiable-for-sim-registration-nia-boss-warns.html>) among the services it supports.

**Use reported.** [The NIA said in May 2026 that its verification system had processed over 200 million requests](<https://icnghana.com/nia-has-registered-over-19-4-million-ghanaians-executive-secretary/>). It counted 281 institutions onboarded and 228 more in the pipeline. [The Interior Minister put 2025's biometric verifications at over 125 million](<https://today.com.gh/muntaka-nia-clears-ghana-card-backlog-registers-418000-children/>), with about 284 institutions on the platform by August 2026.
