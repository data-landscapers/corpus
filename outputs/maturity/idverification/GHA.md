# Ghana

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The NIA Executive Secretary said the Ghana Card e-wallet, for bank and mobile money transfers, will be activated in phases, with consultations including the Bank of Ghana at an advanced stage](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/ghana-card-digital-wallet-to-facilitate-bank-transfers-mobile-money-payments>). [The Communications Minister said on 7 September 2026 that Parliament had passed the LI for SIM registration and a biometric verification phase would be introduced before end-2026 so SIMs are linked to verified identities](<https://asaaseradio.com/the-resolve-of-this-ministry-is-unshaken-sam-george-on-biometric-sim-verification/>). [A new SIM registration framework with Ghana Card verification, app self-registration, facial recognition and liveness detection was welcomed by the NIA Executive Secretary on 20 March 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-card-non-negotiable-for-sim-registration-nia-boss-warns.html>). [In February 2024 the NIA said banks and other financial institutions verify clients against its database in real time for know-your-customer checks](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/191-financial-institutions-now-have-seamless-access-to-National-Identification-Authority-Data-1914728>). [In July 2025 it told banks, companies and government agencies that its IVSP is the only approved way to verify a Ghana Card](<https://nia.gov.gh/institutions-urged-to-use-nia-ivsp-for-authentic-ghana-card-verification/>).

**Used across sectors.** [BBC reported on 2026-07-16 that the Ghana Card is needed for banking services, SIM card registration, and passport and driver's licence applications](<https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cn4n9q538z2o>). [The NIA statement says it supports banking, healthcare and taxation services, as well as telecoms, in verifying identity against the Ghana Card](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-card-non-negotiable-for-sim-registration-nia-boss-warns.html>). Since 10 January 2024 the [government payroll has checked employees and pensioners against the NIA database by Ghana Card PIN](<https://nia.gov.gh/government-launches-integrated-database-for-payroll-validation-and-verification/>) to remove ghost names.

**Use reported.** [In February 2024 NIA Executive Secretary Kenneth Attafuah said 191 financial institutions, including the Bank of Ghana, all 24 universal banks and 145 rural banks, had been onboarded onto the NIA's verification platform](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/NewsArchive/191-financial-institutions-now-have-seamless-access-to-National-Identification-Authority-Data-1914728>).
