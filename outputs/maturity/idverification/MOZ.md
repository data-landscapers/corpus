# Mozambique

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** The identification directorate has built a service for other institutions to check people against the national ID. [On 20 March 2026 the World Bank reported its identity verification API was awaiting feedback from institutions](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>). The work is part of the Bank's digital governance and economy project in Mozambique. [No relying party was using the verification services at that date](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>), against a project target of six.

**Used across sectors.** No sector is shown checking people against the ID. The nearest held fact: [the World Bank's report that no institution used the service in March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>), while the API waited on their feedback.

**Use reported.** [The World Bank counted no relying parties on 20 March 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>), against a target of six.

***Also on file***. [PortalGuard / PIN:You biometric IAM at SIMO (SIMOrede)](<https://idtechwire.com/bio-key-and-runlevel-deploy-biometric-iam-across-mozambiques-national-payments-network/>).

***Not found***. No service in use was found checking people against the national ID by March 2026.
