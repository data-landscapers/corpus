# Nigeria

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [NIMC's Alvan Ikoku said about 63 government agencies had integrated with NIMC, including bodies in examinations, student finance, consumer credit and health insurance, through its API for verification services; the story dates it to the 2026-10-14 briefing](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [President Tinubu has ordered every ministry, department and agency to adopt NINAuth as the default verification layer](<https://techmoonshot.com/2026/10/05/nimcs-ninauth-mandate-and-nigerias-180m-nin-push/>). [The Nigeria Police Force's POSSAP platform leans on NIN as its verification backbone](<https://techmoonshot.com/2026/10/05/nimcs-ninauth-mandate-and-nigerias-180m-nin-push/>). [NIMC says individuals can use NINAuth to verify identity for SIM registration, immigration and passport processing, tax filings and financial transactions, with consent required before data is shared for KYC](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2026/09/23/nimc-unveils-consent-based-nin-verification-to-authenticate-identity>).

**Used across sectors.** [The story names integrated agencies in examinations, student finance, consumer credit and health insurance as part of the roughly 63 connected to NIMC, per NIMC at the 2026-10-14 briefing](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [SIM registration, tax filing and banking now run through the same NINAuth identity rail, alongside police, road safety and immigration services](<https://techmoonshot.com/2026/10/05/nimcs-ninauth-mandate-and-nigerias-180m-nin-push/>).

**Use reported.** [NIMC said about 63 government agencies had integrated with it and that 142 million people held NINs as of September 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/>). [NIMC reports over 1.3 million identity verifications a day through NINAuth](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/digital-id-assessments-highlight-financial-inclusion-gains-across-africa>). [NIMC's director-general says Nigeria processes about 3.3 million identity verification requests daily](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/05/nimc-enrolls-206000-refugees-idps-32000-inmates/>).
