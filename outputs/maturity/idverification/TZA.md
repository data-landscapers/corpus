# Tanzania

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Prime Minister directed NIDA to deepen integration with institutions for employment, social services and economic activities, saying all institutions dealing with important services should join the system so data can be cross-checked](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [Prime Minister Nchemba said on International Identity Day that national identity cards enable citizens to access banking, employment, SIM card registration and other government services](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [TCRA's monitoring manager said biometric SIM registration identifies people registering lines and that national identification details linked to lines used in fraud can be blocked](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/new-push-for-tighter-sim-registration-to-curb-cyber-fraud-5590718>).

**Used across sectors.** [The Prime Minister named banking, employment and SIM card registration as services the national ID enables access to](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [The 147 connected institutions comprise 66 public and 81 private institutions, per the Ministry of Home Affairs](<https://dailynews.co.tz/as-tanzania-goes-digital-who-protects-your-privacy/>). [NIDA lists bank account opening and company and TIN registration (tax) among services using its eKYC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).

**Use reported.** By August 2026, the government spokesman said, [157 institutions, 72 government and 85 private, were linked to NIDA's system](<https://www.jamhurimedia.co.tz/nida-yazindua-kampeni-ya-kipo-chukua-kamilisha-safari-yako/>) and could verify the people they serve. [The Ministry of Home Affairs counted 147 institutions connected to NIDA's system, 66 public and 81 private, 17 added July 2025 to April 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/as-tanzania-goes-digital-who-protects-your-privacy/>). [The report counts 138 public and private institutions connected to the NIDA database](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/tanzanias-financial-inclusion-expands-but-usage-gap-is-becoming-the-next-challenge-2026-07-28-120905>). [NIDA counts 124 institutions integrated with its consent-based eKYC, 55 government and 69 private](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-2-Tanzania-final.pdf>).
