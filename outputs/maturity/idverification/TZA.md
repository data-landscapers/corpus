# Tanzania

## Use of digital ID in other systems

**ID checked by another system.** [The Prime Minister directed NIDA to deepen integration with institutions for employment, social services and economic activities, saying all institutions dealing with important services should join the system so data can be cross-checked](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [Prime Minister Nchemba said on International Identity Day that national identity cards enable citizens to access banking, employment, SIM card registration and other government services](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [TCRA's monitoring manager said biometric SIM registration identifies people registering lines and that national identification details linked to lines used in fraud can be blocked](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/new-push-for-tighter-sim-registration-to-curb-cyber-fraud-5590718>).

**Used across sectors.** [The Prime Minister named banking, employment and SIM card registration as services the national ID enables access to](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [The 147 connected institutions comprise 66 public and 81 private institutions, per the Ministry of Home Affairs](<https://dailynews.co.tz/as-tanzania-goes-digital-who-protects-your-privacy/>). [The report names banks, insurance companies and social security institutions as using NIDA-connected e-KYC processes](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/tanzanias-financial-inclusion-expands-but-usage-gap-is-becoming-the-next-challenge-2026-07-28-120905>).

**Use reported.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs counted 147 institutions connected to NIDA's system, 66 public and 81 private, 17 added July 2025 to April 2026](<https://dailynews.co.tz/as-tanzania-goes-digital-who-protects-your-privacy/>). [The report counts 138 public and private institutions connected to the NIDA database](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/tanzanias-financial-inclusion-expands-but-usage-gap-is-becoming-the-next-challenge-2026-07-28-120905>). [NIDA's director general said on 14 April 2025 that NIDA had connected its system with 128 institutions, 71 private and 57 public](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-kutoa-namba-kwa-watoto-na-wageni-kuzifuta-hizi--5002542>).
