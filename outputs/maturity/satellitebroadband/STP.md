# Sao Tome and Principe

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The US Embassy states that Starlink satellite technology has recently arrived in Sao Tome and Principe](<https://ao.usembassy.gov/united-states-celebrates-250-years-of-independence-and-50-years-of-diplomatic-relations-with-sao-tome-and-principe/>). [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries on 17 July 2026 lists Sao Tome e Principe as Available](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Sao Tome and Principe among the 26 African markets where Starlink was active now, as of 29 June 2026 (article date](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Starlink is listed among the 29 African countries with a licence granted, including Sao Tome and Principe](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [The US Embassy states that Starlink satellite internet services had recently launched in Sao Tome and Principe, as discussed with the Prime Minister during a 14-16 January 2026 visit](<https://ao.usembassy.gov/u-s-charge-daffaires-shannon-cazeau-strengthens-u-s-sao-tome-and-principe-partnership/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
