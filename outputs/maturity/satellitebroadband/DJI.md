# Djibouti

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink's coverage map lists Djibouti with no planned launch date, so Starlink is not on sale in DJI; the article does not say whether any licence or application exists](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/07/28/starlink-28-african-countries-kenya-freeze/>). [Starlink's availability table of 17 July 2026 lists Djibouti as 'No planned launch date', so it is not on sale in DJI](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [The article's table of Starlink markets places Djibouti under 'Date Unspecified', not among the 26 active markets, so it is not on sale in DJI](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 says Starlink's site gives Djibouti 'service date unknown' and Djibouti is not in the list of 29 countries that have licensed Starlink as of May 2026](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/07/28/starlink-28-african-countries-kenya-freeze/>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
