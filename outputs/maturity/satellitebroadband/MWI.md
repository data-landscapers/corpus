# Malawi

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's Starlink status table lists Malawi as Available on 17 July 2026 (Starlink satellite internet on sale)](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Malawi among the Starlink markets 'Active Now' in mid-2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [CIO Africa lists Malawi among markets that approved Starlink quickly (without a local-ownership barrier)](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [The Independent (Uganda) states Starlink is already operational in Malawi, among other countries, extending connectivity to rural areas](<https://www.independent.co.ug/uganda-licenses-starlink-as-africa-satellite-internet-rollout-expands/>). [Le360 lists Malawi among 29 African countries where Starlink (SpaceX) had been granted a licence as of May 2026](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Macra's director general cites the licensing of Starlink as creating competition on the market](<https://mwnation.com/data-cost-down-macra/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
