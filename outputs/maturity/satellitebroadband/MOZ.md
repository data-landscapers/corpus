# Mozambique

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [INCM, the regulator, said in February 2025 that it approved Starlink in under a year under fast-track licensing](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/03-Joaquim%20Zindoga.pdf>). [It described a mixed market in which SATCOM, a VSAT provider since 1999 with more than 500 terminals, competes with Starlink](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/03-Joaquim%20Zindoga.pdf>). [MyBroadband listed Starlink as available in Mozambique in July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [In mid-2026 the residential plan cost MZN 3,000 a month and the standard kit MZN 22,000](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Citing Ookla, ITWeb Africa put Starlink's median download speed in Mozambique at about 75 Mbps in the first quarter of 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/starlink-drives-african-broadband-speeds-higher/GxwQD71DnGyvlPVo>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
