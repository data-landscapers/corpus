# Mozambique

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries lists Mozambique as Available as of 17 July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Mozambique among 26 countries where Starlink is active now (mid-2026), with a Residential plan at MZN 3,000 a month, Residential Lite MZN 1,900, Standard kit MZN 22,000 and Mini kit MZN 12,800](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [The article lists Mozambique among markets that approved Starlink quickly, i.e. Starlink is licensed or authorised there](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [The Independent says Starlink is already operational in Mozambique, among other African countries, as of 19 May 2026](<https://www.independent.co.ug/uganda-licenses-starlink-as-africa-satellite-internet-rollout-expands/>). [Le360 lists Mozambique among 29 African countries that had granted Starlink a licence as of May 2026](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
