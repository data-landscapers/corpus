# DR Congo

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The regulator, ARPTC, licensed Starlink as an internet provider on 2 May 2025](<https://africa.businessinsider.com/local/markets/airtel-and-elon-musks-starlink-launch-africas-first-satellite-to-phone-service-in-drc/c3gcrdq>). [In March 2024 it had called the use of Starlink's network illegal](<https://actualite.cd/2025/05/02/rdc-starlink-obtient-une-licence-officielle-pour-operer-comme-fournisseur-dacces>). [Its satellite service, which needs a dedicated terminal, became available after the licence](<https://africa.businessinsider.com/local/markets/airtel-and-elon-musks-starlink-launch-africas-first-satellite-to-phone-service-in-drc/c3gcrdq>). [In 2026 the regulator's tariff page listed Starlink at USD 60 a month among fixed internet providers](<https://www.arptc-solution.cd/license_list/tarifview/1>). [It also listed Konnect Africa, with no tariff](<https://www.arptc-solution.cd/license_list/tarifview/1>). [Radiocom, an authorised reseller, sells Starlink kits and plans in Kinshasa and beyond](<https://www.talkafrica.co.ke/starlink-users-in-kinshasa-face-4-6-months-of-possible-slowdowns-amid-network-congestion/>). [In July 2026 it said Kinshasa's capacity on the residential plan was used up for four to six months](<https://www.talkafrica.co.ke/starlink-users-in-kinshasa-face-4-6-months-of-possible-slowdowns-amid-network-congestion/>). [Somaf Group opened a shop selling Starlink kits in Kindu in April 2026](<https://actualite.cd/2026/04/03/maniema-somaf-group-ouvre-un-bureau-kindu-et-mise-sur-starlink-pour-resorber-le-deficit>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** No source found since November 2023 gives a regulator's count of satellite broadband subscribers. [Radiocom's July 2026 notice described congestion in Kinshasa](<https://www.talkafrica.co.ke/starlink-users-in-kinshasa-face-4-6-months-of-possible-slowdowns-amid-network-congestion/>) but gave no count.
