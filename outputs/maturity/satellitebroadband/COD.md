# DR Congo

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink's conventional satellite broadband service, needing a dedicated terminal, became available in the DRC after the May 2025 licence](<https://africa.businessinsider.com/local/markets/airtel-and-elon-musks-starlink-launch-africas-first-satellite-to-phone-service-in-drc/c3gcrdq>). [On the instruction of the mayor of Baraka, Starlink kits of some humanitarian NGOs used as their connection were seized during the Baraka-Fizi telecoms cut](<http://congorassure.com/societe/2026/08/03/sud-kivu-francine-kaliba-plaide-pour-le-retablissement-de-la-connexion-baraka>). [MyBroadband's Starlink status table lists the Democratic Republic of Congo as 'Available' for Starlink (SpaceX), dated 17 July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Radiocom Communications & Computers, an authorised Starlink reseller, sells Starlink equipment and Residential and Priority plans to customers in Kinshasa and elsewhere in DRC; its 16 July 2026 advisory says Kinshasa capacity on the Residential plan is exhausted for four to six months](<https://www.talkafrica.co.ke/starlink-users-in-kinshasa-face-4-6-months-of-possible-slowdowns-amid-network-congestion/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://africa.businessinsider.com/local/markets/airtel-and-elon-musks-starlink-launch-africas-first-satellite-to-phone-service-in-drc/c3gcrdq>).
