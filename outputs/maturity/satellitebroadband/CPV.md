# Cape Verde

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [A table of Starlink availability in Africa lists Cabo Verde as 'Available'](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Cabo Verde is listed among 26 African countries where Starlink is 'Active Now'](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [ARME's Q1 2026 statistical report lists Starlink as one of three internet access providers in Cabo Verde, with 0.64% of internet subscriptions (about 3,300 of 511,702 total, derived), up 0.20 percentage points on Q4 2025; the other two are CVTelecom (72%) and Unitel T+ (27.3%)](<https://www.arme.cv/index.php/noticia-geral/1392-arme-publica-relatorio-indicadores-estatisticos-das-comunicacoes-eletronicas-1o-trimestre-de-2026>). [Starlink took 0.2% of the internet market in nine months to June 2025, with CV Telecom at 72.3%](<https://mindelinsite.com/opiniao/cabo-verde-na-vanguarda-da-nova-economia-o-desafio-bizgital-e-o-papel-critico-da-cabo-verde-telecom/>). [Starlink went live in Cape Verde in December 2024, bringing low Earth orbit satellite internet to the archipelago, per Space in Africa](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/05/cape-verde-opens-public-consultation-on-new-satellite-communications-regulatory-framework/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage. [The regulator ARME's market indicators for the second quarter of 2026 give Starlink 0.87 per cent of Cabo Verde's 500,263 internet access subscriptions](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/10/22/assinaturas-de-internet-fixa-aumentam-147-num-ano/105202>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** The regulator, ARME, [put Starlink at 0.87% of Cabo Verde's 500,263 internet access subscriptions at the end of June 2026](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/economia/2026/10/22/assinaturas-de-internet-fixa-aumentam-147-num-ano/105202>), against [72.6% for Cabo Verde Telecom and 26.5% for Unitel](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/10/24/mundo/africa/cabo-verde-starlink-triplica-quota-de-trafego-de-internet-e-ja-representa-18-do-total>). Starlink [carried 18% of national internet traffic in the second quarter of 2026](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/10/24/mundo/africa/cabo-verde-starlink-triplica-quota-de-trafego-de-internet-e-ja-representa-18-do-total>), up from 6% a year earlier.
