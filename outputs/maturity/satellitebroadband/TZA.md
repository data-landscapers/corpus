# Tanzania

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [In November 2024 the regulator, TCRA, confirmed that Starlink Satellite Tanzania Limited had applied for network and application services licences](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tanzania-eyes-starlink-as-spacex-expands-satellite-internet-across-africa>). [In October 2026 Starlink still had no operating licence, and its application was still under review](<https://www.todaysw.com/2026/10/08/tanzania-opens-new-talks-with-elon-musks-spacex-and-starlink-over-investment-in-communications-sector/>). No other satellite broadband provider is named as licensed.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [With no provider licensed](<https://www.todaysw.com/2026/10/08/tanzania-opens-new-talks-with-elon-musks-spacex-and-starlink-over-investment-in-communications-sector/>), there is no count of satellite broadband subscribers.

***Also on file***. [TanSat-1](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29151-the-rise-of-non-terrestrial-networks-integrating-leo-satellites-into-core-telecom-strategy/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a satellite broadband provider licensed in Tanzania.
