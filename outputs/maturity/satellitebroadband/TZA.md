A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://doingbusiness.co.tz/tanzania-eyes-starlink-as-spacex-expands-satellite-internet-across-africa>), there is nothing to count.
