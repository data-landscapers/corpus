# Niger

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Decree 2024-728 of 21 November 2024 granted Starlink Niger a five-year licence to run a public low-orbit satellite internet network](<https://arcep.ne/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Decret-accordant-la-licence-a-STARTLINK-NIGER-SARLU.pdf>). [The communications minister announced the service on sale from 13 March 2025](<https://www.actuniger.com/technologie/20854-starlink-enfin-disponible-officiellement-au-niger-lespoir-dune-connexion-fiable-et-rapide-devient-realite.html>), with home and business kits sold online and through authorised sellers. [NINETEC began selling kits as an authorised distributor in April 2025](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-officiel-des-activites-de-distribution-des-kits-starlink-par-ninetec-garantir-une-connexion-internet-fiable-accessible-partout-et-a-moindre-cout/>). [Airtel Niger began selling Starlink to businesses and public bodies on 16 January 2026](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21684-connectivite-et-innovation-airtel-niger-lance-sa-solution-starlink-by-airtel-pour-plus-dinternet-aux-usagers.html>). [MyBroadband still listed Starlink as available in July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
