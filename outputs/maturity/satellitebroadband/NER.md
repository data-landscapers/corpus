# Niger

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's Starlink status table lists Niger as 'Available' as of 17 July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Niger among the markets where Starlink is active as of mid-2026 (date is the article's published date)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 lists Niger among 29 countries that have granted Starlink a licence as of May 2026; the grant date and licensing body are not given](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Airtel Niger officially launched commercial sale of 'Starlink by Airtel' on 16 January 2026 in Niamey, offering Starlink V4 enterprise and V3 High Performance connectivity to state services, NGOs, projects and businesses; the document says it was already operational with some clients](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/21684-connectivite-et-innovation-airtel-niger-lance-sa-solution-starlink-by-airtel-pour-plus-dinternet-aux-usagers.html>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
