# Nigeria

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MTN has a wholesale agreement with Starlink in Nigeria to distribute satellite broadband through its retail channels, per Africa Telecom Review](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29151-the-rise-of-non-terrestrial-networks-integrating-leo-satellites-into-core-telecom-strategy/>). [Starlink sells satellite internet in Nigeria](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/starlink-faces-the-same-network-pressures-as-traditional-telcos-as-demand-soars/>). [Article published 2026-07-17)](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Starlink is live in Nigeria, which the article says leads Africa with nearly 92,000 subscribers (no source named) and lists a Nigerian price plan in NGN](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Starlink services are active in Nigeria, per the article](<https://itweb.africa/article/amazons-9bn-satellite-deal-set-to-benefit-africa/kLgB17ezwwgM59N4>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [Subscribers: 98,642 at 2026-06, per NCC (Starlink, as one of the licensed ISPs in NCC data released 25 August 2026)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/27/three-companies-now-control-nearly-70-of-nigerias-isp-market/>). [Subscribers: 91,991 at 2025-Q4, per the Nigerian Communications Commission (Starlink subscribers)](<https://techcabal.com/2026/06/26/how-starlinks-growth-is-changing-africa-telcos-playbook/>). [Subscribers: 91,991 at 2025-Q4 (end December 2025), per NCC subscriber statistics, for Starlink Internet Services Nigeria Ltd](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/19/starlink-closes-q4-2025-with-91991-subscribers-as-satellite-internet-reshapes-nigerias-isp-market/>). [Subscribers: 65,564 at 2024-12, per NCC data (Starlink active users, Q4 2024)](<https://techcabal.com/2025/09/15/starlink-halts-new-orders-in-lagos-abuja-over-congestion/>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
