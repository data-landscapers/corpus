# Congo

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reports Congo is among 16 countries Starlink marks 'Starting in 2026' on its map, shown in the colour for pending regulatory authorisation, and that service was still not available in Congo despite a 2025 forecast](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Le360 lists Congo-Brazzaville as 'available from 2026' with no licence granted to Starlink as of May 2026](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [ARPCE seized an unauthorised Starlink installation in Congo-Brazzaville in June 2024](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
