# Botswana

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [ITWeb, citing Ookla, reports Starlink users in Botswana recording median download speeds of about 75 Mbps or more, with Botswana one of three countries above 100 Mbps in Q1 2026, among 23 of the 27 African countries where Starlink is licensed; Starlink is in service in Botswana](<https://itweb.africa/article/starlink-drives-african-broadband-speeds-higher/GxwQD71DnGyvlPVo>). [Paratus Botswana, in a company press release, says it provides Eutelsat low-earth-orbit (OneWeb) satellite services combined with terrestrial links to mining operations in Botswana](<https://itweb.africa/article/paratus-botswana-eutelsat-showcase-resilient-connectivity-and-redefine-how-mining-connects-in-southern-africa/lLn14MmQeANMJ6Aa>). [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries lists Botswana as Available on 17 July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Botswana among the African countries where Starlink is active as of mid-2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). Together these place satellite broadband licensed, on sale and sold on two satellite networks, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
