# Cote d'Ivoire

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink (sold directly to households) and Eutelsat Konnect (sold by Orange and MTN) are both on sale in Cote d'Ivoire as of the 28 July 2026 article](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/07/28/starlink-28-african-countries-kenya-freeze/>). [ARTCI authorised Starlink in Côte d'Ivoire subject to the regulator's ability to interrupt network access](<https://theconversation.com/faut-il-sinquieter-de-la-starlinkisation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-afrique-287464>). [Starlink is live in Côte d'Ivoire alongside existing satellite broadband from Orange (Orange Sat with Eutelsat) and an April 2026 MTN-Eutelsat Konnect capacity agreement, so services on two networks, Starlink and Eutelsat Konnect, are on sale](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/starlink-live-in-c-te-d-ivoire-and-seychelles>). [Starlink launched in Côte d'Ivoire and showed as available, with residential plans at XOF40,244 and residential lite at XOF28,746 a month per the Starlink website](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/starlink-live-in-c-te-d-ivoire-and-seychelles>). [Starlink has launched in Cote d'Ivoire with two uncapped home packages, Residential 100Mbps at 28,746 francs](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). Together these place satellite broadband licensed, on sale and sold on two satellite networks, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
