# Sierra Leone

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [A table of Starlink's African markets lists Sierra Leone as 'Available' (no launch date given](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Sierra Leone is listed among 26 African countries where Starlink is 'Active Now' (no launch date given](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360's May 2026 regulatory-status table lists Sierra Leone among countries where Starlink has been granted a licence and is operational](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Sale to households or businesses is implied, not stated)](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-boss-rural-network-expansion-global/>). [NatCA Director-General Amara Brewah said Starlink satellite broadband has been licensed in Sierra Leone (no licence date given](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-boss-rural-network-expansion-global/>). [Minister of Communication, Technology and Innovation Salima Bah said in a 30 September 2025 address that Sierra Leone has introduced Starlink satellite internet service](<https://moice.gov.sl/sierra-leones-digital-leap-minister-salima-bah-highlights-transformation/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
