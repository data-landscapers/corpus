# Sierra Leone

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Sierra Leone licensed Starlink in June 2023, and the service went live in June 2024](<https://techpoint.africa/news/starlink-available-sierra-leone/>), [at NLe1,020 a month with NLe7,860 for the hardware](<https://sierraloaded.sl/technology/order-pay-starlink-internet-subscription-sierra-leone/>). [NatCA, the regulator, announced the licence in September 2024, when no resellers or retailers were yet authorised](<https://sierraloaded.sl/technology/global-internet-starlink-licensed-sierra-leone/>). [In January 2025 the communications minister said three local companies provide Starlink service](<https://www.itedgenews.africa/sierra-leone-partners-with-starlink-to-expand-university-internet/>). [In March 2026 NatCA's director-general said Starlink had been operating for two years, serving remote clinics, schools and farmers](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/natca-boss-rural-network-expansion-global/>). [MyBroadband still listed it as available in July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
