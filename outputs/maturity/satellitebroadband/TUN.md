# Tunisia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin states that Tunisia is on Starlink's map as 'Starting in 2026', with regulatory authorisation pending and no launch date](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [The service is not yet available there despite a 2025 target](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [MyBroadband's table of Starlink's availability map lists Tunisia as '2026', not 'Available', so Starlink was not on sale in Tunisia](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [CIO Africa lists Tunisia among about 34 countries, with South Africa, Egypt, Iraq, Morocco and Algeria, where Starlink sits in regulatory limbo](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [It is neither approved nor explicitly banned there, as at the article's date](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [THD states that Starlink has not obtained a commercialisation licence in Tunisia, its process reportedly paused after a feasibility study, though it takes 9 dollar pre-order deposits online](<https://www.thd.tn/starlink-ouvre-les-precommandes-en-tunisie-avec-un-acompte-de-9-dollars-lancement-commercial-annonce-en-2026/>). Nothing dated later names one licensed.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
