# Tunisia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin states that Tunisia is on Starlink's map as 'Starting in 2026' with regulatory authorisation pending, no launch date, and that the service is not yet available there despite a 2025 target](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [MyBroadband's table of Starlink's availability map lists Tunisia as '2026', not 'Available', so Starlink was not on sale in Tunisia](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Tunisia is listed among about 34 countries, with South Africa, Egypt, Iraq, Morocco and Algeria, where Starlink sits in regulatory limbo, neither approved nor explicitly banned, per CIO Africa (article date used, no other date given)](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [THD states that Starlink has not obtained a commercialisation licence in Tunisia, its process reportedly paused after a feasibility study, though it takes 9 dollar pre-order deposits online](<https://www.thd.tn/starlink-ouvre-les-precommandes-en-tunisie-avec-un-acompte-de-9-dollars-lancement-commercial-annonce-en-2026/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
