# Ghana

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband lists Ghana as 'Available' for Starlink in its table of African markets](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Article lists Ghana among 26 African countries where Starlink is active now, with a Ghana monthly price and a measured 172 Mbps download](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Starlink capacity limits forced sign-up freezes and waitlists in Nigeria and that similar freezes have hit Ghana](<https://techtrends.africa/starlinks-real-impact-in-rural-africa/>). [Starlink's monthly subscription fees are competitive in Ghana, implying the service is on sale there](<https://techcabal.com/2026/06/26/how-starlinks-growth-is-changing-africa-telcos-playbook/>). [Uganda Independent says Starlink is already operational in Ghana, used to extend rural connectivity](<https://www.independent.co.ug/uganda-licenses-starlink-as-africa-satellite-internet-rollout-expands/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [Subscribers: 15,384 at 2025-06 (Q2 2025), per the NCA, satellite internet subscriptions in Ghana, up from 10,362 at Q4 2024 and 11,982 at Q1 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/35/35_ITU_EGTI2025_EmanuelNeizer_SatelliteBasedInternetConnectivityStatistics_GhanaExperience.pdf>). [Subscribers: 2,666 at 2024-03 (Q1 2024), per the NCA, satellite internet subscriptions in Ghana before Starlink was licensed](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/35/35_ITU_EGTI2025_EmanuelNeizer_SatelliteBasedInternetConnectivityStatistics_GhanaExperience.pdf>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.

***Also on file***. [Starlink](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/09/starlink-partners-connects-30-volta-schools-to-high-speed-internet/>); [Amazon Leo](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2809-59286-ghana-discusses-amazon-leo-partnership-for-broadband-expansion>).
