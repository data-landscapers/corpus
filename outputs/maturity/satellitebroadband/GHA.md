# Ghana

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [In December 2023 the regulator, the NCA, said it had not licensed Starlink and told sellers to stop](<https://nca.org.gh/2023/12/07/starlink-operations-are-illegal-in-ghana/>). [In March 2024 the minister told parliament that OneWeb was already licensed and Starlink was in the process](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/ghana-to-licence-starlink-in-response-to-subsea-cable-cuts/>). [The NCA approved Starlink's application in April 2024](<https://nca.org.gh/2024/04/25/nca-approves-starlinks-satellitte-broadband-application/>), and [in August 2024 said Starlink would start operating by the end of that month](<https://nca.org.gh/2024/08/22/starlink-starts-operations-in-ghana-end-of-august-2024/>). [Its November 2024 guidance says customers can buy the Starlink kit online or from four resellers, with the household plan at GHS 770 a month](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FAQs-Starlink-in-Ghana-1.pdf>). [In 2025 the NCA reported 29 satellite licences, 11 of them for last-mile connectivity](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/35/35_ITU_EGTI2025_EmanuelNeizer_SatelliteBasedInternetConnectivityStatistics_GhanaExperience.pdf>). [In July 2026 MyBroadband listed Starlink as available in Ghana](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [The NCA counted 15,384 satellite internet subscriptions in June 2025](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/35/35_ITU_EGTI2025_EmanuelNeizer_SatelliteBasedInternetConnectivityStatistics_GhanaExperience.pdf>). That was up from 2,666 in March 2024, before Starlink was licensed.

***Also on file***. [Starlink](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/09/starlink-partners-connects-30-volta-schools-to-high-speed-internet/>); [Amazon Leo](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2809-59286-ghana-discusses-amazon-leo-partnership-for-broadband-expansion>).

***Not found***. No source found says OneWeb, or any network other than Starlink, is on sale in Ghana.
