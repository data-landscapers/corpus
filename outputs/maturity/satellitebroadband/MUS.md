# Mauritius

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The regulator, ICTA, ruled on 22 May 2025](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>). A low-orbit network may serve Mauritius only through a licensed Mauritian internet provider. Each terminal needs an earth-station licence. [Emtel launched OneWeb low-orbit internet across the country on 4 June 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0606-129017-maurice-emtel-introduit-l-internet-par-satellite-avec-le-soutien-d-eutelsat>). It was a first for Mauritius. [ICTA approved Emtel's satellite internet tariffs, effective 28 May 2025](<https://www.icta.mu/documents/2025/06/tariffs_emtel_satellite_isp_280525.pdf>). They price OneWeb and Kymeta terminals at USD 1,150 to 54,000 a month. [In August 2026 the head of ICTA said OneWeb is operating through its local partner](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>). [Starlink was welcome if it obtained the licences required](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>). No licence for it has been found.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [Emtel's OneWeb service](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/0606-129017-maurice-emtel-introduit-l-internet-par-satellite-avec-le-soutien-d-eutelsat>).
