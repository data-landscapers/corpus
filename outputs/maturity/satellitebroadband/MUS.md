# Mauritius

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [In August 2025 the ICT minister welcomed ICTA's decision to authorise fixed broadband over non-geostationary satellite networks](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-to-roll-out-new-digital-regulations-in-line-with-national-ict-blueprint/>). The head of ICTA, the communications regulator, [told L'Express in August 2026 that OneWeb has been authorised and is operating through its local partner](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>). He said the regulator's decision of 22 May 2025 lets a low-orbit network serve Mauritius only through a licensed Mauritian internet provider. Each terminal needs an earth-station licence. [Starlink had met the regulator and was welcome if it obtained the licences required](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>); no licence for it has been found. The interview does not say whether households or businesses can buy the OneWeb service.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the OneWeb service](<https://lexpress.mu/s/atishay-sookun-starlink-est-le-bienvenu-a-maurice-sil-respecte-nos-regles-561795>).

***Also on file***. [Eutelsat OneWeb](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/08/28/emtel-is-awarded-icta-contract-to-bring-internet-connectivity-to-st-brandon/>). [LEO satellite link, Raphael Island (Emtel, USF-funded)](<https://lexpress.mu/s/lile-raphael-se-connecte-a-internet-562034>).

***Not found***. No source found says households or businesses can buy satellite broadband from a licensed provider. No regulator's count of subscribers has been found.
