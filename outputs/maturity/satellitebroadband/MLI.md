# Mali

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reported on 31 August 2026 that Starlink is not yet available in Mali, which is listed as 'Starting in 2026' with regulatory authorisation pending; it was already listed for 2025 without launching](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [AMRTP, Mali's regulator, stated on 11 June 2026 that installing, operating, importing and selling Starlink/Wi-Fi Zone devices without prior authorisation is a violation, ordered it to stop immediately, and announced nationwide control, seizure and dismantling operations](<https://libreinfos.com/2026/07/02/telecommunications-letat-declare-la-guerre-aux-exploitants-illegaux-de-wifi-zone-starlink-et-talkie-walkie/>). [Le360 reports that Mali is among the countries where Starlink is announced as available from 2026 but no licence has been granted, and that unofficial Starlink use via roaming is reported in Mali](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
