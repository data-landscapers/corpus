# Guinea

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reported on 31 August 2026 that Guinea is among 16 countries marked 'Starting in 2026' on Starlink's availability map, meaning coverage or regulatory authorisation pending, and that Starlink service is still not available there despite a 2025 target](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [ARPT agents were sent into the field to uninstall and confiscate Starlink equipment used by Wi-Fi resellers in Guinea, as reported on 10 August 2026 (date of the seizures not given)](<https://theconversation.com/faut-il-sinquieter-de-la-starlinkisation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-afrique-287464>). [MyBroadband's 17 July 2026 table of Starlink status lists Guinea with estimated availability '2026', not 'Available', so no Starlink service was on sale in Guinea](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag's mid-2026 table lists Guinea among countries where Starlink is 'scheduled for 2026', not among those active now](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count.
