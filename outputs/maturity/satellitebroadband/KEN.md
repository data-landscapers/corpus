# Kenya

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Vodacom has a wholesale agreement with Starlink in Kenya, letting it distribute satellite broadband through existing retail channels](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/features/29151-the-rise-of-non-terrestrial-networks-integrating-leo-satellites-into-core-telecom-strategy/>). [Amazon Kuiper Kenya Limited has applied for a 15-year International Gateway Operator licence, after an earlier application for a Network Facilities Provider licence; no Amazon licence is stated as granted](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/who-will-control-africas-satellite-internet-layer-starlink-amazon-leo-or-the-carriers/>). [Starlink is on sale in Kenya, with a capped plan priced at $10 a month in 2026 (about 5.5% of monthly GNI per capita)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/20/who-will-control-africas-satellite-internet-layer-starlink-amazon-leo-or-the-carriers/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [Subscribers: nearly 27,700 satellite internet subscriptions at 2026-06-30, per the Communications Authority of Kenya (+54.4% year on year), of which Starlink Internet Services Kenya 27,616](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/19/kenya-fixed-internet-fibre-wireless-satellite-growth/>). [Subscribers: 25,088 satellite internet subscriptions at 2026-03-31, per the Communications Authority of Kenya Q3 FY2025/26 report (+11.4% on the previous quarter, which had 22,282 Starlink users); Starlink Internet Services Kenya held 24,999](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/06/19/starlink-hits-25000-subscribers-in-kenya/>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
