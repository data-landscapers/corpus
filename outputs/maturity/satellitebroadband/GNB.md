# Guinea-Bissau

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries lists Guinea-Bissau as 'Available' as of 17 July 2026 (article date used, no launch date given)](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [ARN-TIC, by Despacho 15/CA/ARN/2026, extended to 31 December 2026 the provisional General Authorisation held by Starlink Guiné-Bissau SARL for satellite internet services, to ensure continuity during the transition to the definitive General Authorisation regime](<https://arn.gw/comunicado-arn-tic/>). [TechnoMag lists Guinea-Bissau among the countries where Starlink is 'Active Now' as of mid-2026 (article date used, no launch date given)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [ARN states that unauthorised entities are illegally selling internet over the Starlink network, that using such services violates the law, and that it will intensify enforcement, including possible confiscation of illegal equipment and sanctions](<https://arn.gw/comunicado-a-imprensa-fornecimento-ilegal-de-servicos-de-acesso-a-internet/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
