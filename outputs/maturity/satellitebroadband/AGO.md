# Angola

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Eutelsat secured a 15-year licence for its OneWeb low-earth-orbit service in Angola in 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/06/30/eutelsat-group-secures-15-year-license-to-operate-oneweb-leo-connectivity-in-angola/>). [In June 2026 it was the only low-earth-orbit operator licensed there](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/mercury-reforca-oferta-de-conectividade-em-angola-com-novo-acordo-com-a-eutelsat/>). [That month Mercury agreed to supply its low-earth-orbit service to businesses and public bodies](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/mercury-reforca-oferta-de-conectividade-em-angola-com-novo-acordo-com-a-eutelsat/>). [In October 2026 Q-KON and SISTEC opened a Luanda point of presence for Twoobii OneWeb](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/10/14/q-kon-sistec-launch-sovereign-satellite-service-for-angola/>). [SISTEC uses it to serve enterprise and financial-sector clients](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/10/14/q-kon-sistec-launch-sovereign-satellite-service-for-angola/>). [In July 2026 the regulator, INACOM, licensed the startup NomaTech to sell internet access over ANGOSAT-2](<https://angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/startup-do-moxico-recebe-licenca-para-fornecer-internet-via-angosat-2/>). [In August 2026 Starlink's map still listed Angola as starting in 2026, pending authorisation](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
