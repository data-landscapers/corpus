# Angola

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Mercury signed a multiyear agreement with Eutelsat to supply low-earth-orbit satellite connectivity in Angola to businesses, public institutions, telecom operators and offshore clients; the document gives no subscriber figures or consumer sale](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/mercury-reforca-oferta-de-conectividade-em-angola-com-novo-acordo-com-a-eutelsat/>). [Agence Ecofin reports Angola is among 16 countries where Starlink's availability map shows 'Starting in 2026', meaning coverage or regulatory authorisation pending](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [INACOM, the regulator, authorised the Luena-based startup NomaTech (Weza Tchisseque Tchamy) to install infrastructure and sell internet access over the ANGOSAT-2 satellite network to individuals, institutions and businesses](<https://angop.ao/noticias/tecnologia/startup-do-moxico-recebe-licenca-para-fornecer-internet-via-angosat-2/>). [Angop reports that Eutelsat is currently the only low-earth-orbit satellite operator licensed in Angola, with a local ground station and point of presence](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/mercury-reforca-oferta-de-conectividade-em-angola-com-novo-acordo-com-a-eutelsat/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing held gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/mercury-reforca-oferta-de-conectividade-em-angola-com-novo-acordo-com-a-eutelsat/>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
