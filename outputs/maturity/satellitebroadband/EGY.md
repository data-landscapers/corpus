# Egypt

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [A Starlink availability table lists Egypt as 'No planned launch date', so Starlink was not on sale in Egypt](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [The article lists Egypt's Starlink status as unknown and says Egypt, with Ethiopia, restricts or blocks the service to protect state-owned telecoms](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [The article places Egypt among countries in regulatory limbo for Starlink, neither approved nor explicitly banned, so no approval is stated](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [Telegraph Egypt reported on 2025-06-18 that Starlink was not yet activated in Egypt, that selling or marketing it requires an official licence not yet held, and that talks with the ministry were under way; smuggled terminals are an offence under Article 77 of the telecom law, with confiscation](<https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/154808/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D9%8A%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%A3%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86%D9%83-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B8%D8%B1>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
