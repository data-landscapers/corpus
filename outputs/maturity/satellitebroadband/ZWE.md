# Zimbabwe

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The regulator, POTRAZ, licensed Starlink on 6 September 2024](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/>), and it went live that day. [Kits are sold through cleared agents, dealers and internet providers](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2024/09/potraz-starlink-licence-disruption/>). [In July 2025 the ICT minister said the President had approved the licensing of low-orbit satellite service himself](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-ministry-donates-250-starlink-kits-to-local-authorities/>). [That approval let the ministry give 250 Starlink kits to local authorities](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-ministry-donates-250-starlink-kits-to-local-authorities/>). [In September 2026 Starlink business packages were on sale in Harare and Bulawayo](<https://zimpricecheck.com/analysis-and-opinion/starlink-effectively-kills-roaming-in-zimbabwe-with-its-new-passport-rule/>), directly or through TelOne and Aura.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [POTRAZ counted 23,410 VSAT subscriptions at the end of 2024](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055624/measured-but-not-counted-how-starlink-broke-potrazs-bandwidth-framework>), the category that holds Starlink. [By the end of 2025 it counted 67,057](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/theindependent/business-digest/article/200055625/over-50-of-zims-fixed-broadband-now-wireless>), up from 30,907 in the first quarter. [Space in Africa reported the same POTRAZ figure](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/05/06/starlink-drives-zimbabwes-vsat-subscriptions-to-a-record-67057/>), up from 50,949 in the third quarter.
