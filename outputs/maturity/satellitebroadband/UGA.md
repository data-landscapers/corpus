# Uganda

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Paratus said Starlink services had recently launched in Uganda, adding satellite connectivity for businesses where fixed infrastructure is limited, per TechTrendsKE (article date used, no launch date given)](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/09/09/paratus-expands-east-africa-connectivity-itw-africa/>). [Roke Telkom and Paratus Uganda launched Starlink for Business in Uganda, with Paratus Uganda the first provider to offer Starlink commercially, on 24-month plans of 500GB to 2TB at 40-220 Mbps down, per Space in Africa](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/09/08/roke-telkom-and-paratus-uganda-launch-starlink-for-business-in-uganda/>). [Roke Telkom with Paratus Uganda launched Starlink enterprise connectivity, reselling Starlink LEO capacity to businesses, per Nile Post](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/368680/roke-telkom-paratus-bring-starlink-to-ugandas-enterprise-market>). [Starlink launched commercial satellite internet in Uganda on 2 September 2026 after regulatory approval; Residential 100 Mbps costs Shs203,704 a month, kit entry cost about Shs2.3m](<https://www.pulse.ug/story/starlink-uganda-launch-prices-mtn-airtel-2026090200285472149>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
