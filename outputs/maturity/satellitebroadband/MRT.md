## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink's Mauritanian licence application, sought for about two years, is still pending, with authorities pressing security and data-control conditions (date is the article's, no other given)](<https://maurinews.info/news-reports/95535/>). [Starlink and Amazon are not yet authorised for direct public service in Mauritania](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/29/mauritania-launches-tender-for-satellite-internet-licenses-to-expand-connectivity/>).
