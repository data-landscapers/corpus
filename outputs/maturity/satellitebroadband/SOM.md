# Somalia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries as of 17 July 2026 lists Somalia as Available](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Somalia among 26 countries where Starlink is active as of mid-2026, billed in USD at 70 a month for Residential, 390 for the Standard kit and 200 for the Mini](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Netlink Somalia states it is an authorised Starlink reseller selling satellite internet across Somalia to organisations, businesses and field teams, with local installation and support](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/netlink-somalia_netlink-somalia-is-an-authorised-starlink-activity-7390508555386372096-brvt>). [AstaanConnect, the connectivity arm of Astaan, became Somalia's first official Starlink reseller, selling kits and subscriptions (from $36.9 a month residential) with local installation and support to households, businesses, schools and offices](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Sept/203052/astaanconnect_becomes_somalia_s_first_official_starlink_reseller.aspx>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
