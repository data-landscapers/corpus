# Ethiopia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [TechMag lists Ethiopia among countries that continue to block or restrict Starlink to protect state-owned telecoms, and places it in the date-unspecified group for Starlink rollout](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Birr Metrics reports that the Ethiopian Communications Authority denied granting Starlink a licence and said it had held no discussions with Starlink about one](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-denies-granting-starlink-license-as-satellite-internet-expansion-faces-regulatory-pushback/>). [The Ethiopian Communications Authority stated in a public notice that Starlink has not been granted a licence in Ethiopia and that no discussions, past, ongoing or planned, have taken place with Starlink about one](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ethiopian-communications-authority_home-eca-activity-7346822754987393024-LHoH>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
