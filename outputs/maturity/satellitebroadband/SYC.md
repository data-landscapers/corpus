# Seychelles

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [State House Seychelles reports that Starlink began operations in Seychelles with a soft launch at State House on 24 July 2026, presided over by the Vice-President alongside the SCRA, for households, businesses and students](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7153/an-election-pledge-fulfilled-as-starlink-begins-operations-in-seychelles>). [Households and businesses can subscribe via Starlink's online platform, buying the hardware kit and activating service after a soft launch at State House; officials said several users already have equipment and are using the service](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/starlink-launches-in-seychelles-offering-new-high-speed-internet-option/>). [SCRA officials confirmed that Starlink has been authorized to operate in Seychelles under the telecommunications regulations](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/22/starlink-launches-in-seychelles-offering-new-high-speed-internet-option/>). [State House reports SCRA representatives said Starlink has been authorised to operate in Seychelles under the national regulatory framework, as outlined at the soft-launch press conference](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7148/starlink-begins-operations-in-seychelles-expanding-internet-choices-for-consumers>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated.
