# Chad

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink launched in Chad at a press conference on 4 July 2025](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/07/05/les-services-des-starlink-desormais-disponibles-au-tchad/>). Its plans ran from 12,000 FCFA for 50 GB to 32,000 FCFA unlimited. [An August 2025 order set its conditions of operation](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/28/tchad-le-gouvernement-fixe-les-conditions-dutilisation-des-services-starlink/>), including user identification. [The minister later told deputies its licence is a multiservice internet provider licence](<https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/>). [In June 2026 he regretted the limits of the packages on offer](<https://adetic.td/le-tchad-renforce-ses-partenariats-strategiques-pour-accelerer-sa-transformation-numerique/>). [In November 2026 Russell Southwood wrote that Starlink sells through resellers such as Ilan Telecoms](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>). Ilan had 384 subscribers, and terminals were delivered only to N'Djamena.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** No source found since November 2023 gives a regulator's count of satellite broadband subscribers. [The 384 subscribers Southwood cites are one reseller's](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>).
