# Chad

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries as of 17 July 2026 lists Chad as Available](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechMag lists Chad among Starlink markets active as of mid-2026 and names it among recent launches; says Starlink is most valuable in sparsely populated areas such as Chad](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [At a 29 June 2026 debrief the telecommunications minister acknowledged Starlink's advances in Chad but regretted the limits of the packages currently offered and urged restoring unlimited offers, so Starlink service is on sale in Chad](<https://adetic.td/le-tchad-renforce-ses-partenariats-strategiques-pour-accelerer-sa-transformation-numerique/>). [Telecommunications Minister Boukar Michel told deputies that Starlink's concession is a multiservice Internet Service Provider licence, granted on the operator's request after a favourable ARCEP technical opinion](<https://www.toumaiwebmedias.com/assemblee-nationale-adoption-de-la-loi-sur-la-regulation-des-communications-electroniques-et-des-activites-postales/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
