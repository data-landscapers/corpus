# Libya

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Eutelsat OneWeb is sold in Libya through a multi-year exclusive distribution agreement with a Libyan local partner, extended to LEO connectivity from early 2024, for oil and gas, telecoms, finance, government and humanitarian customers](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/09/13/starlinks-libya-market-entry-remains-on-hold-as-negotiations-continue/>). [Starlink is not licensed in Libya: talks with the regulator GACI, open since 2024 on services, licensing structure, frequencies and coverage, had reached no agreement and were on hold until further notice as of the article's date, per GACI sources](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/09/13/starlinks-libya-market-entry-remains-on-hold-as-negotiations-continue/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
