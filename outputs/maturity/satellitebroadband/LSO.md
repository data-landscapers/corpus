# Lesotho

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Vodacom Lesotho launched its Enterprise Satellite Connectivity solution, a fully managed satellite broadband service powered by Starlink LEO, at a launch in Maseru in the week of publication](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/08/26/vodacom-launches-satellite-broadband-for-enterprises/>). [Vodacom Lesotho launched a managed enterprise satellite broadband service built on Starlink at a launch event in Maseru (no event date given](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/10/09/vodacom-lesotho-launches-enterprise-satellite-connectivity-solution-powered-by-starlink/>). [MyBroadband's Starlink status table of 17 July 2026 lists Lesotho as 'Available'; no provider or reseller is named for Lesotho](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Lesotho among 26 African countries where Starlink is active now, as of mid-2026 (the document gives no date of launch and names no reseller)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [ITWeb Africa, citing Ookla, counts Lesotho among 27 African countries where Starlink is currently licensed (no licence date given)](<https://itweb.africa/article/starlink-drives-african-broadband-speeds-higher/GxwQD71DnGyvlPVo>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing found gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/08/26/vodacom-launches-satellite-broadband-for-enterprises/>).
