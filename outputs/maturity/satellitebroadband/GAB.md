# Gabon

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reports Starlink's availability map shows Gabon as 'Starting in 2026', meaning coverage or regulatory authorisation pending, and the service is still not available in Gabon](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Gabon's regulator Arcep announced a ban on installing, using and marketing Starlink offers in Gabon, with prison and fines for offenders](<https://theconversation.com/faut-il-sinquieter-de-la-starlinkisation-de-lacces-a-internet-en-afrique-287464>). [Le360 lists Gabon among countries where Starlink is announced for 2026 with no licence granted, and says Gabon banned the sale and unauthorised use of Starlink equipment, citing digital sovereignty and security, with financial penalties](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
