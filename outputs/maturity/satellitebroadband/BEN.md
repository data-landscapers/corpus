# Benin

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's Starlink status table of 17 July 2026 lists Benin as 'Available' for Starlink (SpaceX)](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Benin among the 26 countries where Starlink is 'Active Now' as of mid-2026 (article date 2026-06-29, no other date given)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 lists Benin among 29 African countries that had granted Starlink a licence as of May 2026](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Starlink is listed by ARCEP Benin among the operators with fixed internet subscriptions in Benin, with 2,550 subscriptions at 31 March 2026 (1,013 at Q2 2025, 1,593 at Q3 2025, 2,215 at Q4 2025), and satellite is 1.99% of the fixed internet base](<https://arcep.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Observatoire_Internet_T1_2026_TaB.pdf>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [Subscribers: 2,550 at 2026-03-31, per ARCEP Benin (Starlink, in the fixed internet subscriptions table of the Observatoire de l'Internet)](<https://arcep.bj/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Observatoire_Internet_T1_2026_TaB.pdf>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
