# Gambia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Service is not yet available there](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [MyBroadband's 17 July 2026 table of Starlink status lists Gambia with estimated availability '2026', not 'Available', so no Starlink service on sale in the Gambia](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists the Gambia among 19 countries 'Scheduled for 2026', not among the countries where Starlink is active, so not on sale as of mid-2026 (article dated 29 June 2026)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [As of the article's date (no other date given), Starlink holds no licence in The Gambia: the Minister of Digital Economy refused to approve the VSAT permit paid for in February 2026, saying Starlink needs a general satellite licence](<https://orbitaltoday.com/2026/05/24/starlink-licence-gets-delayed-by-the-gambia/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
