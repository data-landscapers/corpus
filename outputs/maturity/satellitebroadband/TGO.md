# Togo

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin says Starlink service is still not available in Togo, which Starlink had shown as planned for 2025 and now lists for 2026, subject to approval by public authorities](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [TechnoMag lists Togo among 19 countries where Starlink is 'Scheduled for 2026', not among the 26 where it is active](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 lists Togo among countries where Starlink is announced as available from 2026 but no licence has been granted](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Techs.tg says Togo does not yet officially have Starlink service; some individuals and firms use imported equipment without official authorisation, and ISOC-Togo began negotiations with Starlink in March 2024](<https://techs.tg/2025/01/14/starlink-en-afrique-une-revolution-de-lacces-a-internet/>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
