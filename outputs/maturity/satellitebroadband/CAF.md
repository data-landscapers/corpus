# Central African Republic

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [A table dated 17 July 2026 lists Starlink in the Central African Republic as 'Available'; no provider or regulator is named](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [ARCEP's half-year 2026 observatory says Starlink CAR obtained a licence to market and operate satellite fixed wireless internet in CAF (licence date not given; document published 2026-09-16)](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/component/edocman/observatoire-des-marches-de-communications-electroniques-s1-2027/download?Itemid=0>). [ARCEP began an inspection operation in Bangui against unregistered Starlink kit users and Wi-Fi providers](<https://www.radiondekeluka.org/104273-telecom-le-ministere-des-telecommunications-et-larcep-lancent-la-chasse-aux-fournisseurs-illegaux-dinternet>). [The article lists the Central African Republic among 26 countries where Starlink is 'Active Now' as of its publication date](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 lists the Central African Republic among 29 countries where Starlink has been granted a licence](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [The document calls it an agreement, not a licence; pricing not yet disclosed](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2025/12/23/starlink-begins-service-in-the-central-african-republic/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** Nothing held gives a regulator's count of the subscribers to [the service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
