# Madagascar

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The regulator, ARTEC, signed a five-year licence for Starlink Madagascar on 29 April 2024, covering broadband, VSAT and GMPC satellite networks](<https://www.2424.mg/starlink-madagascar-obtient-sa-licence-internet-satellite-moyennant-100-000-euros>). [Starlink has been sold through its website since late June 2024](<https://www.2424.mg/lartec-recommande-aux-usagers-de-passer-uniquement-par-le-site-web-de-starlink-pour-acheter-leur-materiel-et-souscrire-a-leur-abonnement>), and [it announced its service fully operational in July 2024, at MGA 226,000 a month](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/broadband/starlink-beams-into-madagascar>). [In February 2025 the digital development minister put the price at MGA 1,585,000 for equipment and MGA 266,000 a month](<https://2424.mg/internet-stephanie-delmotte-attend-plus-dinvestissements-economiques-et-social-de-la-part-de-starlink/>). [MyBroadband still listed Starlink as available in July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Airtel Madagascar sells enterprise satellite internet over Eutelsat's OneWeb network, with installation and technical support](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/01/airtel-madagascar-and-eutelsat-deepen-push-for-rural-broadband-access/>), as reported in June 2026.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** [In February 2025 the digital development minister put Starlink's subscribers at about 5,900](<https://2424.mg/internet-stephanie-delmotte-attend-plus-dinvestissements-economiques-et-social-de-la-part-de-starlink/>). Nothing found gives a regulator's count of satellite broadband subscribers.
