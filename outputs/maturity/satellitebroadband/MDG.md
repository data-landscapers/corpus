# Madagascar

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's Starlink status table of 17 July 2026 lists Madagascar as 'Available' for Starlink](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Airtel Madagascar, through Airtel Business, delivers enterprise satellite internet over the Eutelsat OneWeb low Earth orbit constellation in Madagascar, with installation, onboarding and technical support](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/01/airtel-madagascar-and-eutelsat-deepen-push-for-rural-broadband-access/>). [TechnoMag lists Madagascar among countries where Starlink is active and names it a recent launch (as of mid-2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Le360 lists Madagascar among the countries where Starlink is operational (article date used)](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Le360 lists Madagascar among 29 African countries where Starlink has been granted a licence (article date used, none given for the grant)](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Starlink user-terminal broadband has been operational in Madagascar since 2023, according to Space in Africa (article of 2026-05-19)](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/05/19/madagascar-set-to-launch-starlink-direct-to-cell-services/>). Together these place satellite broadband licensed, on sale and sold on two satellite networks, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
