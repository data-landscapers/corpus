# Zambia

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink's status table for African countries, dated 17 July 2026, lists Zambia as Available](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Starlink lists Zambia among 26 countries 'Active Now' as of the article; Zambia prices are given in ZMW and measured speeds of 181 Mbps down, 24 Mbps up](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Starlink's demand outstripped allocated capacity in 2025-2026, forcing suspended new residential sign-ups in some markets; the article says similar freezes have hit Zambia, so the service was on sale there; it also says Paratus's EduLink reseller programme extended Starlink-powered connectivity to schools in Zambia](<https://techtrends.africa/starlinks-real-impact-in-rural-africa/>). [Zambia is listed among markets that approved Starlink quickly, without local-ownership requirements or with them set aside](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
