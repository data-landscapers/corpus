# South Sudan

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The National Communication Authority signed landing-rights and provisional service licences for Starlink on 26 June 2024](<https://goldentimessouthsudan.com/index.php/2024/06/27/south-sudan-okays-elon-musks-starlink-services/>), and [in July 2024 it approved four Starlink tariff plans](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/regulation/south-sudan-unveils-approved-starlink-tariffs>). [Le Monde reported the service officially available from 22 August 2024](<https://www.lemonde.fr/afrique/article/2024/10/31/starlink-au-soudan-du-sud-un-petite-avancee-dans-l-un-des-pays-les-moins-connectes-au-monde_6369351_3212.html>), with 1,300 terminals in use and four approved resellers. [MTN South Sudan began selling Starlink kits and subscriptions nationwide in October 2025](<https://amnon.co.uk/2025/10/07/starlink-now-available-nationwide-through-mtn-south-sudan/>). [In January 2026 Sudans Post said satellite internet had widened access in some rural and conflict-affected areas, but cost puts it beyond most traders and households](<https://www.sudanspost.com/can-a-digital-economy-exist-in-south-sudan-without-reliable-dpi/>). [In mid-2026 the residential plan cost USD 50 a month](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>).
