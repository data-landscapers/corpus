# South Sudan

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's table of Starlink status in African countries lists South Sudan as Available, dated 17 July 2026 (no South Sudan launch date or provider given](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists South Sudan among 26 countries where Starlink is active now and gives a USD price of 50 a month Residential, 30 Residential Lite, kits 389 Standard and 200 Mini (as of mid-2026](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [Sudans Post says satellite internet such as Starlink has expanded access in select rural and conflict-affected areas of South Sudan and has offered relief to some businesses, NGOs and media organisations, but its cost puts it beyond most traders and households (as_of is the article date)](<https://www.sudanspost.com/can-a-digital-economy-exist-in-south-sudan-without-reliable-dpi/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
