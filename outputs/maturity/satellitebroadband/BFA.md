# Burkina Faso

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [The service is not yet available there, though it had been shown for 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Minister of Digital Transition said negotiations with Starlink are advancing and the state hopes soon to decide whether to let it offer services to the population, so no authorisation has yet been given](<https://www.queenmafa.net/2026/07/26/chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-120-plateformes-deja-repertoriees/>). [MyBroadband's status table lists Burkina Faso with estimated Starlink availability '2026', not 'Available', so no service was on sale](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Le360 lists Burkina Faso among markets shown by Starlink as available from 2026 with no licence granted; ARCEP recalled in March 2024 that Starlink held no authorisation, and unofficial use via roaming is reported](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Semafor reports Starlink had been blocked from approval in seven countries including Burkina Faso](<https://www.semafor.com/article/07/18/2024/elon-musks-starlink-battles-africa-regulators>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
