# Comoros

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reports that Starlink's official availability map lists Comoros among 16 African countries marked 'Starting in 2026' with no launch date, a status the map legend defines as coverage or regulatory authorisation pending, so Starlink service is not yet available there](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Al-Watwan reports that the regulator Anrtic announced on 12 June 2025 a government ban, by ministerial circular signed 9 June 2025, on installing, testing, deploying or using Starlink satellite internet in Comoros, no authorisation having been granted to Starlink, with field checks to identify existing users (some subscribing from Madagascar or France)](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html>). A dated statement that no satellite broadband provider is licensed, or that its sale or use is banned, sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one licensed. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count. Only a count the regulator publishes, from the past three years, answers this question; counts given by an operator or an analyst do not.
