# Comoros

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Agence Ecofin reports that Starlink's official availability map lists Comoros among 16 African countries marked 'Starting in 2026' with no launch date](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [The map legend defines that status as coverage or regulatory authorisation pending, so Starlink service is not yet available there](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Al-Watwan reports that the regulator Anrtic announced a government ban on 12 June 2025, by ministerial circular signed 9 June 2025](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html>). [The ban covers installing, testing, deploying or using Starlink satellite internet in Comoros, where no authorisation had been granted to Starlink](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html>). [Field checks were to identify existing users, some subscribing from Madagascar or France](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9ploiement-de-starlink-i-les-autorit%C3%A9s-interdisent-son-usage-pour-l%E2%80%99instant.html>). Nothing dated later names one licensed.

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** With [no provider licensed](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3108-141168-internet-par-satellite-starlink-cible-16-nouveaux-marches-africains-d-ici-fin-2026>), there is nothing to count.
