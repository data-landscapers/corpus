# Senegal

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Sonatel Orange launched satellite internet with Eutelsat's Konnect in January 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/05/sonatel-orange-achieves-99-coverage-in-senegal-with-new-satellite-internet-offerings/>), for households and professionals. [Starlink launched commercially in February 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1109-141491-senegal-les-raisons-derriere-la-suspension-de-starlink-sur-le-marche-de-l-internet>), [at about CFA 22,000 to 30,000 a month before equipment](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0204-54373-senegal-warns-against-unauthorized-starlink-based-wifi-networks>). [On 7 September 2026 the Supreme Court provisionally suspended Starlink's licence](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>), on a challenge by Sonatel. [The Point reported that on 3 November 2026 the court left the suspension in place](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/starlink-suspended-in-senegal>), pending a final ruling. [It put the licence fee at FCFA 100 million](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/starlink-suspended-in-senegal>). [A Seneweb opinion writer said the court had rejected the State's request to lift the suspension](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Contribution/licence-starlink-100-millions-de-fcfa-un-montant-reste-confidentiel-par-patrice-sane_n_999003115.html>).

**Subscribers counted by the regulator.** No source found since November 2023 gives a regulator's count of satellite broadband subscribers. [Ookla found Starlink users in Senegal had median downloads above 100 Mbps in early 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/starlink-drives-african-broadband-speeds-higher/GxwQD71DnGyvlPVo>), but gave no count.
