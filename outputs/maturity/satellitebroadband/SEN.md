# Senegal

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Sonatel Orange launched Senegal's first satellite internet offers with Eutelsat/Konnect, unlimited service for households and professionals, immediately available](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/05/sonatel-orange-achieves-99-coverage-in-senegal-with-new-satellite-internet-offerings/>). [The analyst states the Starlink licence is currently suspended by the Supreme Court in summary proceedings](<https://vibractu.com/contribution-starlink-au-senegal-la-carte-qui-revele-le-vrai-defi-de-la-fracture-numerique-par-patrice-sane/>). [The Supreme Court ordered on 7 September 2026 the provisional suspension of Starlink Sénégal SUARL's authorisation for fixed satellite internet access, on a challenge by Sonatel; the ruling does not settle the merits](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1109-141491-senegal-les-raisons-derriere-la-suspension-de-starlink-sur-le-marche-de-l-internet>). [Senegal's Supreme Court ordered the provisional suspension of Starlink Senegal's licence on 7 September 2026, on Sonatel's challenge to the 2025 order (reported 2026-09-09)](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/technologie/internet-par-satellite-la-sonatel-obtient-la-suspension-provisoire-de-la-licence-de-starlink/>). [MyBroadband's Starlink status table of 17 July 2026 lists Senegal as 'Available'](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [Starlink Sénégal SUARL launched its service commercially in February 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1109-141491-senegal-les-raisons-derriere-la-suspension-de-starlink-sur-le-marche-de-l-internet>).
