# Equatorial Guinea

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Vice-president Nguema Obiang Mangue announced on 2026-09-09 that Equatorial Guinea officially launched Starlink satellite internet, operated by SpaceX, with sale and distribution centralised and requests handled by ORTEL; earlier connections made with foreign details are to be deactivated](<https://agpgabon.ga/guinee-equatoriale-internet-satellitaire-starlink-officiellement-lance/>). [A 2026-09-09 government session praised the Transport Ministry's agreement with Starlink to market satellite internet in Equatorial Guinea, placed legal selling authority with ORTEL, ordered individuals' contracts outside official channels cancelled, and noted oil companies are already authorised to use the network offshore](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/09/09/el-gobierno-exige-a-meta-tributar-en-guinea-ecuatorial-y-ordena-centralizar-la-comercializacion-de-starlink-en-ortel/>). [Starlink went live on 29 August 2026, confirmed by Starlink and Elon Musk, with an order page on starlink.com and two residential tiers priced in FCFA; sold directly by Starlink under provisional approval](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/31/starlink-is-live-in-equatorial-guinea/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
