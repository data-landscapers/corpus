# Equatorial Guinea

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Vice-president Nguema Obiang Mangue announced on 2026-09-09 that Equatorial Guinea officially launched Starlink satellite internet, operated by SpaceX](<https://agpgabon.ga/guinee-equatoriale-internet-satellitaire-starlink-officiellement-lance/>). [Sale and distribution are centralised and requests handled by ORTEL, and earlier connections made with foreign details are to be deactivated](<https://agpgabon.ga/guinee-equatoriale-internet-satellitaire-starlink-officiellement-lance/>). [A 2026-09-09 government session praised the Transport Ministry's agreement with Starlink to market satellite internet in Equatorial Guinea](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/09/09/el-gobierno-exige-a-meta-tributar-en-guinea-ecuatorial-y-ordena-centralizar-la-comercializacion-de-starlink-en-ortel/>). [It placed legal selling authority with ORTEL and ordered individuals' contracts outside official channels cancelled](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/09/09/el-gobierno-exige-a-meta-tributar-en-guinea-ecuatorial-y-ordena-centralizar-la-comercializacion-de-starlink-en-ortel/>). [It noted oil companies are already authorised to use the network offshore](<https://ahoraeg.com/politica/2026/09/09/el-gobierno-exige-a-meta-tributar-en-guinea-ecuatorial-y-ordena-centralizar-la-comercializacion-de-starlink-en-ortel/>). [Starlink went live on 29 August 2026, confirmed by Starlink and Elon Musk, with an order page on starlink.com and two residential tiers priced in FCFA](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/31/starlink-is-live-in-equatorial-guinea/>). [It is sold directly by Starlink under provisional approval](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/08/31/starlink-is-live-in-equatorial-guinea/>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
