# South Africa

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [YahClick has been sold in South Africa for years through internet service providers such as Vox Telecom](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/656335-state-backed-south-african-satellite-internet-and-phone-company-launched.html>). [Eutelsat OneWeb operates commercially in South Africa through partners such as Paratus and Q-KON, per TechCentral commentary (article date used)](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-broadband-future-is-being-decided-in-orbit-not-in-pretoria/283150/>). [Starlink's South African entry is delayed because it has not met the 30% historically-disadvantaged-ownership requirement](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/icasa-strives-for-balance-in-rapidly-changing-digital-economy/6GxRKqYQLBYqb3Wj>). [Starlink remains unable to launch commercial services in South Africa because it has not yet secured the electronic communications licences ICASA requires, including the 30% historically-disadvantaged ownership rule](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/starlink-rival-maps-out-sa-strategy-beyond-satellite-broadband/rW1xL75nD9bMRk6m>). [TechCentral reports on 2026-08-23 that Starlink remains unlicensed in South Africa after not meeting the 30% historically-disadvantaged ownership requirement](<https://techcentral.co.za/cell-c-in-talks-with-starlink-and-amazon-leo/285187/>). [Starlink is not licensed in South Africa: SpaceX has declined the 30% historically-disadvantaged-ownership requirement and a grey market persists](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africas-broadband-future-is-being-decided-in-orbit-not-in-pretoria/283150/>). Together these place satellite broadband licensed, on sale and sold on two satellite networks, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
