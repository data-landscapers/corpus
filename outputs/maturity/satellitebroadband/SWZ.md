# Eswatini

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [MyBroadband's Starlink status table lists Eswatini as 'Available' on 17 July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>). [TechnoMag lists Eswatini among 26 countries where Starlink is 'Active Now' as of mid-2026 (article dated 29 June 2026)](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>). [The article lists Eswatini among markets that approved Starlink quickly, without naming a date or the licensing body](<https://cioafrica.co/namibia-shuts-the-door-on-starlink-twice/>). [Le360 lists Eswatini among 29 African countries where Starlink has been granted a licence and is operational](<https://afrique.le360.ma/economie/internet-haut-debit-en-seulement-4-ans-starlink-operationnel-dans-un-pays-africain-sur-deux_UYXWC5RPWNGGFIYLU5VHL6L7ZU/>). [Paratus Eswatini, a Paratus Group and Real Image Internet joint venture launched on 25 July 2025, is an authorised Starlink Business reseller that has completed installations across Eswatini, offering managed Starlink hardware, installation and support to businesses (ITWeb Africa)](<https://itweb.africa/article/recently-launched-paratus-eswatini-aims-to-drive-starlink-business-tech-adoption/j5alr7QAOeQ7pYQk>). These place satellite broadband licensed and on sale, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Services on a second satellite network are not stated. Prices and speeds are given where a source states them; they set no stage.
