# Eswatini

## Satellite broadband licensed and on sale

**Licensed, on sale, and from more than one network.** [Starlink became available across Eswatini in December 2023, at R950 a month for the residential service](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/520219-eighth-african-country-gets-starlink-before-south-africa-with-rand-pricing.html>). [In November 2024 the managing director of Paratus Eswatini said the regulator, ESCCOM, had signed off the Starlink licence](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-group-announces-its-joint-venture-with-real-image-and-launches-paratus-eswatini/>). [It said then that it had Starlink stock and a technical team ready, with dozens of enquiries a day](<https://paratus.africa/blog/paratus-group-announces-its-joint-venture-with-real-image-and-launches-paratus-eswatini/>). [By July 2025 Paratus Eswatini, an authorised Starlink reseller, had completed installations across the country, selling managed hardware, installation and support to businesses](<https://itweb.africa/article/recently-launched-paratus-eswatini-aims-to-drive-starlink-business-tech-adoption/j5alr7QAOeQ7pYQk>). [MyBroadband still listed Starlink as available in July 2026](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/broadband/658586-starlink-launches-in-27th-african-country-before-south-africa.html>).
