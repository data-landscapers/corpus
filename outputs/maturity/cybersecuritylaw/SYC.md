# Seychelles

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Cybercrimes and Other Related Crimes Act 2021](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2021-12/Act%2059%20-%20Cybercrimes%20and%20other%20Related%20Crimes%20Act%202021.pdf>) (Act 59 of 2021) was assented on 15 December 2021 and repeals the Computer Misuse Act (Cap 254). [Act 23 of 2025](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2023%202025%20-%20Cybercrimes%20and%20other%20Related%20Crimes%20%28Amendment%29%20Act%202025.pdf>) amends it. The National Assembly passed that amendment on 15 December 2025 and the President assented to it on 22 December 2025. It widens the offences and amends an Act already in operation, so Seychelles has a working cybercrime statute that was extended at the end of 2025.

**Use of the law.** The 2025 amendment, assented on 22 December 2025, [adds search and interception powers](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2025-12/Act%2023%202025%20-%20Cybercrimes%20and%20other%20Related%20Crimes%20%28Amendment%29%20Act%202025.pdf>) to the Act alongside the wider offences. These are powers for investigating offences under it. The amendment states no commencement date of its own. Both texts are published in the official gazette. They sit on top of the 2021 Act, which replaced the earlier Computer Misuse Act, [Cap 254](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2021-12/Act%2059%20-%20Cybercrimes%20and%20other%20Related%20Crimes%20Act%202021.pdf>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
