# Cabo Verde

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Cabo Verde has Decreto-lei n.º 9/2021, its regime jurídico de cibersegurança, together with Decreto-Regulamentar n.º 1/2021, which creates the national CSIRT. The [2026 State Budget law](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>) cites them as in place. The [2024 annual plan and budget of NOSi](<https://www.nosi.cv/documents/20121/2842828/PAO_2024.pdf>) states that both decrees exist, and gives no further date for them. Both documents name the cybersecurity regime as a text that is already part of the country's rules.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact on use is that the national CSIRT the regulating decree creates is named in official documents. The [2026 State Budget law](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei%20que%20aprova%20o%20OE%202026.pdf>) cites Decreto-lei n.º 9/2021 and its regulating decree as in place, and the [NOSi plan for 2024](<https://www.nosi.cv/documents/20121/2842828/PAO_2024.pdf>) lists them among existing texts. Neither document records any case brought under the law, and neither gives a date for any use of it by the authorities of Cabo Verde.
