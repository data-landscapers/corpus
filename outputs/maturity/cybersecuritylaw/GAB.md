# Gabon

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** [Gabonreview](<https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/>), in an article published on 1 June 2025, presents law no. 027/2023 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime as a national law. It is the law with which ANINF's SecBOM, the Security Bill of Material, must comply. The article treats the law as the standing national framework for cybersecurity in Gabon, and names ANINF as the agency whose tool is held to it. ANINF is described there as reinforcing national cybersecurity through the SecBOM, a tool on which the article reports. [A law ratifying the ordinance on social networks, promulgated by decree 0436/PR of 5 October 2026, punishes using another person's identifying details to deceive users and take their confidential data with five years' imprisonment and a fine of FCFA 5 to 20 million](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-amendes-jusqua-50-millions-et-peines-de-prison-pour-les-abus-commis-sur-les-reseaux-sociaux/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact held is the [same article](<https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/>) of 1 June 2025, which links the law to ANINF's SecBOM: the tool must comply with law no. 027/2023. That is a compliance requirement placed on an agency tool, stated in a news article, and it is the only application of the law described in the source.

***Also on file***. [SecBOM (Security Bill of Material)](<https://www.gabonreview.com/laninf-renforce-la-cybersecurite-nationale-avec-secbom/>), run by ANINF.

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
