# Libya

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Libya's Law No. 5 of 2022 on combating cybercrime is in force. [Cabinet Decision 358 of 15 July 2026 cites it among its legal bases](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d9%85%d8%ac%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-358-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-2026-%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a5%d9%86%d8%b4/>), in setting up a National Authority for Cybersecurity and Digital Sovereignty. A wider cybersecurity law is being prepared. On 15 July 2026 [a House of Representatives committee met National Security Council officials in Benghazi](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932>) to work on the bill. [A draft law on cybercrime and digital evidence](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/cybercrime-law-session/>) went to experts for comment in 2025.

**Use of the law.** No source found shows an arrest, charge or conviction under the 2022 law. The nearest fact is [the cabinet decision that cites it](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d9%85%d8%ac%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-358-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-2026-%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a5%d9%86%d8%b4/>) as a legal basis for the new cybersecurity authority. The [committee meeting of July 2026](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932>) worked on a separate bill.
