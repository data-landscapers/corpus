## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Libya's nearest held fact is a bill. The committee was created by Speaker's decision 115 of 2025. The report describes a bill in preparation, not an enacted law, and the work it records sits at the stage of drafting, with the National Security Council officials taking part in the meeting held in Benghazi in the middle of July 2026.
