# Libya

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Libya has bills, not an enacted law. On 15 July 2026 a [temporary House of Representatives committee, created by Speaker's decision 115 of 2025, met National Security Council officials in Benghazi](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932>) to work on preparing a comprehensive cybersecurity bill. A [near-finalized draft law on cybercrime and digital evidence](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/cybercrime-law-session/>) was presented for expert feedback at the Informatics Governance Forum workshop in 2025, with earlier drafts dating from 2008, 2009 and 2015.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held facts concern bills, not a law in operation. The [15 July 2026 committee meeting in Benghazi](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/524932>) was work on preparing the cybersecurity bill. The [draft on cybercrime and digital evidence](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/cybercrime-law-session/>) was at the stage of expert feedback in 2025, and no enacted text is described in either source.
