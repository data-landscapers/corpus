# Uganda

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Uganda's Constitutional Court [struck down Sections 11, 23, 26, 27, 28 and 29 of the Computer Misuse Act](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/constitutional-court-nullifies-key-computer-m-NV_230010_042026>) and Sections 162 and 163 of the Penal Code Act (criminal libel), leaving the rest of the Act standing. Nilepost says the cyber-offence framework is [anchored in the Computer Misuse Act, 2011 as amended in 2022](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/368071/three-arrested-over-alleged-fake-news-personal-data-offences-on-social-media>), with some offences carrying up to 10 years in prison. The Nilepost account is dated 1 September 2026. A July 2026 [joint UPR submission](<https://cipesa.org/wp-content/files/Uganda_UPR_Review.pdf>) urges repeal or amendment of sections 24 and 25.

**Use of the law.** In September 2026 Kampala Metropolitan Police [arrested and held three men](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/368071/three-arrested-over-alleged-fake-news-personal-data-offences-on-social-media>) over alleged fake news and unlawful obtaining or disclosure of personal data on social media, in an investigation the report places under the Computer Misuse Act. The incidents date to July 2026, and charges were not disclosed.
