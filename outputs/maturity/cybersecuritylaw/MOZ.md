# Mozambique

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** President Chapo [promulgated the Cybersecurity Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cybercrime-and-cybersecurity-laws-national-payments-system-law-signed-into-force/>) in June 2026. It protects networks, information systems and critical infrastructure, sets up a regulatory body and carries fines of up to 160 minimum wages. Lusa reports that the president [enacted the Cyber Security and Cybercrime Acts on 12 June](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-updating-national-cybersecurity-strategy>). [A civil-society letter of 23 April 2026 said both bills were then before the Assembly of the Republic](<https://cibercidadaos.org.mz/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Posicionamento-sobre-as-Propostas-de-Leis-da-Seguranca-Cibernetica-e-Crimes-Ciberneticos.pdf>). [INTIC says Law 13/2026 on cybersecurity and Law 14/2026 on cybercrime entered into force on 29 September 2026](<https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/>). The [Penal Code (Law 24/2019, amended by Law 17/2020)](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=MZ>) also defines cybercrimes. Database intrusion carries up to two years and electronic fraud one to five years. [UNESCO's December 2025 profile lists a draft Cybersecurity Law among the country's instruments](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/mozambique>).

**Use of the law.** On 28 July 2026 the communications minister said the government [has approved the Cybersecurity Law and the Cybercrime Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/>). He said companies running critical infrastructure must implement them. In April 2026 Parliament [unanimously approved the Cyber Security Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-unanimously-approves-cyber-security-law/>). It makes INCM technical coordinator for cyberspace security and creates a Cyber Security Fund.
