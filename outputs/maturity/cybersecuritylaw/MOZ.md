# Mozambique

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** President Chapo [promulgated the Cybersecurity Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-cybercrime-and-cybersecurity-laws-national-payments-system-law-signed-into-force/>) in June 2026, protecting networks, information systems and critical infrastructure, with a regulatory body and penalties up to 160 minimum wages. Lusa reports that the president [enacted the Cyber Security and Cybercrime Acts on 12 June](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-government-updating-national-cybersecurity-strategy>). Africa AI News states that Mozambique [already has a Cyber Security Act and a Cybercrimes Act](<https://www.africaainews.com/p/mozambique-presents-draft-ai-strategy>) as part of its legal foundation. The [Penal Code (Law 24/2019, amended by Law 17/2020)](<https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/guide.pdf?c=MZ>) also provides cybercrimes such as database intrusion, punishable by up to two years, and electronic fraud, punishable by one to five years. [UNESCO's December 2025 profile lists a draft Cybersecurity Law among the country's instruments](<https://www.unesco.org/ethics-ai/en/mozambique>).

**Use of the law.** On 28 July 2026 the communications minister said the government [has approved the Cybersecurity Law and the Cybercrime Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-digital-infrastructure-is-as-strategic-as-roads-and-ports-americo-muchanga/>) and that implementation is mandatory for companies operating critical infrastructure. In April 2026 Parliament [unanimously approved the Cyber Security Law](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-parliament-unanimously-approves-cyber-security-law/>), making INCM technical coordinator for cyberspace security and creating a Cyber Security Fund, a proposal the government had submitted the previous Thursday.
