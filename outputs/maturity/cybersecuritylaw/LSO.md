# Lesotho

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Lesotho's nearest held fact is a pair of bills. Cabinet [split the 2024 bill into a Cybercrime Bill 2026 and a Cybersecurity Bill 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>), and the Department of Information reports a second stakeholder consultation on the Cybersecurity Bill 2026 after a draft circulated in August 2026, with the bill still to proceed through the next legislative stages. The [Lesotho Times](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91666>) reports that a 2021 version was withdrawn from Parliament and the 2024 Computer Crime and Cyber Security Bill was referred to Parliament but not enacted. In August 2026 the Director General of ICT said both bills were [ready and waiting for approval by the National Assembly](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/interim-csirt-lesotho-cybersecurity-launch/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact concerns the draft text. The [Computer Crime and Cyber Security Bill, 2024](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/COMPUTER-CRIME-AND-CYBER-SECURITY-BILL-2024.pdf>) would commence on gazettal and be cited as the Act once enacted. In January 2026 the Principal Secretary at the communications ministry said it was [working on the Cybersecurity Bill](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/ramashamole-urges-lca-to-safeguard-end-users-against-cyber-attacks/>).

***Also on file***. The [interim Computer Security Incident Response Team (CSIRT)](<https://www.gov.ls/development/stakeholders-engage-on-cybersecurity-bill-2026/>), the [National Cyber Security Incident Response Team](<https://nationalassembly.parliament.ls/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/COMPUTER-CRIME-AND-CYBER-SECURITY-BILL-2024.pdf>) and the [National Cybersecurity Advisory Council](<https://cybilportal.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Lesotho-CMM-Report-2022-vFinal_31032023.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a cybercrime or cybersecurity law is in force.
