# Zimbabwe

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** On 24 January 2026 the Director General of POTRAZ stated that [Zimbabwe has no standalone cyber crimes act](<https://news.pindula.co.zw/2026/01/25/there-is-no-separate-cyber-crimes-act-in-zimbabwe-potraz/>). Cybercrime offences are in force instead in Part VIII of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act [Chapter 9:23], as amended in 2021. They cover hacking, data interference, cyber-bullying, child abuse material and others, in sections 163 to 168. On 15 October 2026 a POTRAZ official [described a Cybersecurity Act](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/potraz-moves-to-tighten-cybersecurity-for-critical-infrastructure/>) as holding cybercrime definitions alongside cybersecurity provisions in one piece of legislation.

**Use of the law.** The cybercrime provisions in force are the offences in [Part VIII of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act](<https://news.pindula.co.zw/2026/01/25/there-is-no-separate-cyber-crimes-act-in-zimbabwe-potraz/>), sections 163 to 168. The POTRAZ Director General set them out on 24 January 2026. A POTRAZ official on 15 October 2026 [spoke of the same subject](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/potraz-moves-to-tighten-cybersecurity-for-critical-infrastructure/>) in the context of critical infrastructure.
