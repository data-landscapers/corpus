# Zimbabwe

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** On 24 January 2026 the Director General of POTRAZ stated that [Zimbabwe has no standalone cyber crimes act](<https://news.pindula.co.zw/2026/01/25/there-is-no-separate-cyber-crimes-act-in-zimbabwe-potraz/>). Cybercrime offences are in force instead in Part VIII of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act [Chapter 9:23], as amended in 2021. They cover hacking, data interference, cyber-bullying, child abuse material and others, in sections 163 to 168.

**Use of the law.** The cybercrime provisions in force are the offences in [Part VIII of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act](<https://news.pindula.co.zw/2026/01/25/there-is-no-separate-cyber-crimes-act-in-zimbabwe-potraz/>), sections 163 to 168. The POTRAZ Director General set them out on 24 January 2026 while stating that the country has no separate cyber crimes act. They include hacking, data interference, cyber-bullying and child abuse material. The Act, as amended in 2021, is the law in force on these offences, according to the Director General, who gave the date of 24 January 2026 for his statement.
