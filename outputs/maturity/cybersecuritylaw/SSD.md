# South Sudan

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** South Sudan has a cybercrime law in force. Radio Tamazuj [reports](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-i>) that President Kiir signed the Cybercrime and Computer Misuse Act 2026 on 18 February 2026 and that it came into force on signature. Its [offences and penalties](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-offensives-and-penalties>) sit in Chapter Six, and [Chapter 5](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/explainer-cybercrime-and-computer-misuse-act-2026-chapter-v>) sets critical infrastructure designation, 24-hour incident reporting and administrative penalties. The Information Minister [said](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/minister-ateng-defends-cybercrime-law-amid-concerns>) the law is in effect. The Southern Africa Litigation Centre [says](<https://www.southernafricalitigationcentre.org/oral-statement-under-item-3-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa/>) section 6(3) lets the National Communication Authority order service providers to hand over data without judicial authorisation. Parliament had [passed the bill](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/south-sudan-parliament-passes-controversial-cybercrime-bill>) on 25 November 2025.

**Use of the law.** Eye Radio [reports](<https://www.eyeradio.org/controversial-cybercrime-act-takes-effect-amid-free-expression-concerns/>) that the government announced implementation of the Act in July 2026, amid free expression concerns. Under Presidential Directive No. 03 of 2026 the National Cyber-Security Task Force held its [first operational meeting](<https://radioyei.org/news/law-and-courts/south-sudan-activates-cybercrime-law-to-protect-digital-space-ahead-of-elections/>) on 16 July 2026. In September 2026 more than 20 civil society, security and media participants were [trained](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/09/09/over-20-civil-society-and-security-officials-trained-in-yei/>) in Yei River County on the Act, which the report treats as current law.
