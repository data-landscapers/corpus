# Morocco

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Morocco's [Loi n° 05-20 on cybersecurity](<https://www.anrt.ma/sites/default/files/2022-02/loi_05-20_cybersecurite_fr.pdf>) was promulgated by dahir n° 1-20-69 of 25 July 2020 and published in Bulletin officiel n° 6906 of 6 August 2020. Its article 53 makes it operative on publication of its implementing texts. The same page records that décret n° 2-24-921, applying article 25, was in force by the Bulletin officiel of 20 February 2025, so the law has implementing texts in place alongside the 2020 text.

**Use of the law.** Morocco's national police, the DGSN, [counted 13,643 cybercrime cases](<https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/cybercriminalite-au-maroc-13643-affaires-liees-aux-technologies-modernes-et-au-chantage>) handled in 2025, in the annual report it presented on 17 December 2025. In the same year [415 people were arrested and referred to court](<https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/cybercriminalite-au-maroc-13643-affaires-liees-aux-technologies-modernes-et-au-chantage>) for technology-related crimes and online blackmail, the DGSN says. Both figures are the police's own and cover offences committed through computers and networks. Neither names the 2020 law.

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty naming the law in the last three years is held.
