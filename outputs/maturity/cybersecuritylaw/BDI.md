# Burundi

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Burundi's cybercrime law is [Law No. 1/10 of 16 March 2022 on the prevention and repression of cybercrime](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>). At a workshop in Bujumbura in February 2026, a government delegate cited it as adopted and in application. A speaker at the same workshop said the president signed it in 2022. The law is named in the article as the country's cybercrime law, and the Malabo Convention was the standard against which the workshop measured it.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact on use is the same [February 2026 workshop in Bujumbura](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>), where experts and participants discussed the law's enforcement and its gaps against the Malabo Convention. The government delegate presented the law at that workshop as being in application. The article gives that discussion and the 2022 date of signature, and the law is the Burundian text on cybercrime prevention and repression adopted in March 2022.
