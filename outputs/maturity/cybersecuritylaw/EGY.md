# Egypt

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Egypt's cybercrime law is [Law 175 of 2018 on combating information technology crimes](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/3/%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AA%D8%B2%D9%85-%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B5-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%AA%D9%87/7604583>). An Economic Court ruling of November 2025 [applies the law and its executive regulations](<https://www.egyptke.com/99712>), Prime Ministerial Decree 1699 of 2020. An MP's bill of 22 April 2026 [would tighten its provisions](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>), and [a second MP's bill](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>) would raise its penalties. From 8 to 12 November 2026 [a Public Prosecution course taught the law's provisions](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/11/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D9%84%D9%82-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B5%D8%B5%D9%8B%D8%A7-%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9/7614234>), including its search and seizure warrants.

**Use of the law.** In November 2025 [Egyptian courts convicted a series of TikTok content creators under Article 25 of the law](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>). Sentences ran from six-month suspended terms to two years, with fines of EGP 100,000 each. On 29 November 2025 [the Cairo Economic Court sentenced video blogger Mohamed Abdel Aaty to two years and a fine under the law](<https://ldsf.info/blog/2025/12/01/escalates-war-on-digital11225/>). It sentenced Qamar El-Wekala to six months. In the same month [the Economic Court tried a blogger under article 27 of the law](<https://www.egyptke.com/99712>), on running a site to commit a crime, and acquitted her on that count.
