# Egypt

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Egypt's [Law 175 of 2018 on combating information technology crimes](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>) is treated as the current law in a bill an MP put forward on 22 April 2026, which would tighten its provisions. A [separate bill from an MP](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>), referred to committees by the House speaker, also amends Law 175, which its sponsor calls the current law, with penalties too weak.

**Use of the law.** [In November 2025 Egyptian courts convicted a series of TikTok content creators under Article 25 of Law 175 of 2018, with sentences from six-month suspended terms to two years and fines of EGP 100,000 each](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>). People of Internet calls the law [Egypt's principal cybercrime statute](<https://peopleofinternet.com/articles/egypt-reaches-for-misinformation-laws-to-fill-its-deepfake-g.html>). Two bills reported on 22 April 2026, [one from an MP to tighten its provisions](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>) and [another its sponsor says answers penalties that are too weak](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>), take it as the current text.
