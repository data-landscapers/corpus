# Egypt

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Egypt's [Law 175 of 2018 on combating information technology crimes](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>) is treated as the current law in a bill an MP put forward on 22 April 2026, which would tighten its provisions. A [separate bill from an MP](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>), referred to committees by the House speaker, also amends Law 175, which its sponsor calls the current law, with penalties too weak.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is amendment, not enforcement. [Both bills](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>) take Law 175 of 2018 as the baseline to be changed, one to tighten its provisions and one, [according to its sponsor](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>), because the penalties are too weak. Both were reported on the same day, 22 April 2026, by Egyptian outlets [El Watan News](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8270615>) and [El Balad](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>). [The House speaker referred the second bill to committees in April 2026](<https://www.elbalad.news/6945377>), and the law remains the current text while it is amended.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Egypt National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023-2027](<https://mcit.gov.eg/en/Media_Center/Latest_News/News/97759>).

***Not held***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
