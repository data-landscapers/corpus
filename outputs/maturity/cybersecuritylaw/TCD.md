# Chad

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The sector regulator ARCEP's [register of laws](<https://arcep.td/lois.html>) lists Loi N°009/PR/2015 on Cybersecurity and the Fight against Cybercrime among Chad's laws. The register names the law as Loi N°009/PR/2015 portant sur la Cybersécurité et la Lutte Contre la Cybercriminalité. The year 2015 is the year in the law's number. Chad's regulator therefore carries a national cybersecurity and cybercrime law on its list of the laws that apply in the country. [On 16 February 2026 the National Assembly approved, by 160 votes to 1, the ratification of Ordinance 001/PR/2026 of 22 January 2026, which reforms the agency ANSICE to modernise the legal framework on cybersecurity and cybercrime](<https://tchadvision.com/lassemblee-nationale-approuve-la-reforme-de-lansice-un-pas-vers-le-numerique-de-demain/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact on how the law is applied is its place in the same [ARCEP register of laws](<https://arcep.td/lois.html>), where the regulator lists it among Chad's laws alongside the rest of the legal framework for the sector. The law is presented there as part of the framework the regulator works within, in the 2015 numbering, under the title covering both cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime.

***Also on file***. [Cellule de lutte contre la cybercriminalité](<https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-creation-dne-cellule-de-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-a-lansice/>), [ANSICE](<https://www.alwihdainfo.com/Tchad-promulgation-de-la-reforme-de-l-ANSICE_a148767.html>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
