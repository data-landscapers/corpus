# Djibouti

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Djibouti's National Assembly [passed the Digital Code on 30 June 2025](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>), covering cybersecurity and cybercrime and punishable online offences. The legislative section was still to be promulgated by the President and published in the Journal Officiel. [TechAfrica News reported on 2 July 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/02/a-first-for-east-africa-djibouti-enacts-comprehensive-digital-code/>) that the Code has eight books and nearly 800 articles, and that Book VI deals with Cybersecurity and Cybercrime. [It is Djibouti's first Digital Code and was passed in plenary](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/02/a-first-for-east-africa-djibouti-enacts-comprehensive-digital-code/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is in the text itself: [Jones Day reports, in a note of 1 July 2025,](<https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/07/national-assembly-of-the-republic-of-djibouti-approves-digital-code>) that the Digital Code makes online offences punishable. The Assembly adopted it on 30 June 2025, and the same report says its legislative section awaited promulgation by the President, then publication in the Journal Officiel. That adoption was reported within days: [TechAfrica News](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/07/02/a-first-for-east-africa-djibouti-enacts-comprehensive-digital-code/>) carried it on 2 July 2025 under a headline saying Djibouti enacts the Code.
