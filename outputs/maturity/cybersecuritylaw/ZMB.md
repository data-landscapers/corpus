# Zambia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Zambia has a Cyber Crimes Act and a Cyber Security Act, both of 2025. They [replaced the 2021 Cyber Security and Cybercrime Act](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>). [The Global Network Initiative says the President signed both into law on 8 April 2025](<https://globalnetworkinitiative.org/gni-statement-on-zambias-new-cyber-laws-a-blow-to-freedom-of-expression-and-privacy/>), after the bills were tabled in November 2024. On 5 August 2026 [Access Now and the #KeepItOn coalition noted their enactment](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>). The Southern Africa Litigation Centre says the [2025 Cyber Crimes Act made it an offence](<https://www.southernafricalitigationcentre.org/oral-statement-under-item-3-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa/>) to publish information by computer that damages a person's reputation. In July 2026 the opposition running mate Makebi Zulu [promised to repeal the Act if elected](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/04/well-repeal-cyber-law-makebi/>). [CAJ News Africa reports that both Acts were enacted in 2025](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/08/13/alleged-ai-misuse-on-eve-of-todays-elections-in-zambia/>).

**Use of the law.** [Paradigm Initiative, citing the African Union's preliminary report](<https://paradigmhq.org/paradigm-initiatives-statement-to-the-african-commission-on-human-and-peoples-rights-on-the-human-rights-situation-in-africa-at-the-89th-ordinary-session/>), says ZNBC journalist MacPherson Mukuka was arrested under section 10 of the cybercrime law. He had recorded a private conversation during the August 2026 elections. On 5 August 2026 [Access Now and the #KeepItOn coalition urged the government](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>) not to let the two Acts become tools to suppress expression. At a rally before 4 July 2026, [Makebi Zulu said the Act had gagged Zambians](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/04/well-repeal-cyber-law-makebi/>) since its enactment.
