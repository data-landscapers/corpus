# Zambia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Zambia has a Cyber Crimes Act and a Cyber Security Act, both of 2025, which [replaced the 2021 Cyber Security and Cybercrime Act](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>). On 5 August 2026 [Access Now and the #KeepItOn coalition noted the enactment](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>) of the Cybercrimes Act and the Cybersecurity Act. In July 2026 the opposition running mate Makebi Zulu said that, if elected, his government would [repeal the Cyber Crimes and Cyber Security Act](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/04/well-repeal-cyber-law-makebi/>).

**Use of the law.** Critics have warned about how the Acts may be used. [Access Now and the #KeepItOn coalition urged the government](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/keepiton-zambia-internet-access-upcoming-elections/>) on 5 August 2026 not to let the two Acts become tools to suppress expression. At a rally before 4 July 2026, [Makebi Zulu said the Act had gagged Zambians](<https://diggers.news/local/2026/07/04/well-repeal-cyber-law-makebi/>) since its enactment, and that his government would repeal it if elected, a pledge reported by the Diggers news site in July 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Zambia Cyber Security Agency](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>).

***Not held***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is known.
