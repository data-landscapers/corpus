# Burkina Faso

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Council of Ministers of 30 July 2026 [adopted decrees applying law n°014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024 on the security of information systems](<https://burkina24.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/CONSEIL-DES-MINISTRES-N%C2%B025-DU-30-JUILLET-2026.pdf>). One identifies and categorises critical-infrastructure bodies, and the other sets periodic security audits and auditor accreditation. The [law itself](<https://www.an.bf/storage/libelrapportautres/rXFCav065KRZXiB2PB65xip11Qg8fPpCZ3sjGpsf.pdf>), headed Loi n°014-2024/ALT of 9 July 2024, sets rules to control and protect information systems and govern the actors in them, with licensing and fines.

**Use of the law.** Burkina Faso's cybercrime brigade, the BCLCC, [arrested two people in August 2025](<https://burkina24.com/2025/08/14/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-deux-individus-arretes-pour-usurpation-de-fonction-et-diffamation-en-ligne/>) over a TikTok video. It referred them to the Ouaga II prosecutor for offences committed through ICT, under articles 375-2, 375-3 and 524-4 of the Penal Code. During Opération Cyber-Balayage, from 27 to 31 October 2025, the brigade [arrested 22 people](<https://ouaga24.com/operation-cyber-balayage-2025-22-interpellations-et-plus-de-18-millions-fcfa-de-prejudice/>) for online fraud, identity theft, sextortion and privacy offences. Eleven of them were referred to the judicial authorities.
