# Mauritius

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021 (Act No. 16 of 2021) was passed by the National Assembly on 19 November 2021, assented to on 24 November 2021 and gazetted as Legal Supplement 567; [the retrieved text records it as proclaimed in force on 10 December 2021](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>), and it repeals the Computer Misuse and Cybercrime Act. On 7 October 2026 the Ministry of Technology, Communication and Innovation [discussed proposed amendments to the Act](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/government-engages-meta-to-curb-online-abuse-for-safer-digital-environment/>) with Meta in Ebene.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is how the Act is relied on. The Ministry [treats the Act as the operating legal framework for online content](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/government-engages-meta-to-curb-online-abuse-for-safer-digital-environment/>), the subject of its October 2026 working session with Meta. The National Crime Agency Bill text published by the National Assembly in September 2026 [defines cybercrime by reference to offences under Part III of the Act](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-National-Crime-Agency-Bill-2026.pdf>), the same offences the Act sets out.

***Also on file***. The [National Crime Agency](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-National-Crime-Agency-Bill-2026.pdf>), the [National Cybersecurity Committee](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>) and CERT-MU, the [Computer Emergency Response Team of Mauritius](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
