# Mauritius

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Act 2021](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>) (Act No. 16 of 2021) was passed on 19 November 2021 and assented to on 24 November 2021. The retrieved text records it as proclaimed in force on 10 December 2021, repealing the Computer Misuse and Cybercrime Act. In June 2026 [regulations designating critical information infrastructure](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/113_The-Cybersecurity-and-Cybercrime_Critical-Information-Infrastructure-Designation_Reg-2026.pdf>) came into operation under the Act. On 7 October 2026 the Ministry of Technology, Communication and Innovation [discussed proposed amendments to the Act](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/government-engages-meta-to-curb-online-abuse-for-safer-digital-environment/>) with Meta in Ebene. On 29 October 2026 the ICT minister, Avinash Ramtohul, told the National Assembly that [amendments to the Act had been prepared](<https://lexpress.mu/node/564170>). They cover cyberharassment, doxxing, impersonation, fake profiles and disinformation, with duties for platforms and a new agency.

**Use of the law.** Le Mauricien reports that the police Cybercrime Unit [handled 930 cases from January 2023 to January 2026](<https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/faits-divers/escroqueries-et-fraudes-en-ligne-plus-de-900-affaires-traitees-en-3-ans-pour-130-arrestations/700378/>), leading to 130 arrests. Of those cases, 177 were closed and 660 were under investigation. Another 33 awaited advice from the Director of Public Prosecutions, and 7 were before court. In November 2025 the [Prime Minister gave a count of 837 cases](<https://lexpress.mu/node/551421>) to that date. That count included 120 arrests and no conviction. Neither source names the Act.

***Also on file***. The [National Crime Agency](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-National-Crime-Agency-Bill-2026.pdf>), the [National Cybersecurity Committee](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>) and CERT-MU, the [Computer Emergency Response Team of Mauritius](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/act1621.pdf>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty naming the law in the last three years is held.
