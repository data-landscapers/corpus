# Madagascar

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Madagascar's cybercrime framework [still rests in part on loi n°2014-006, adopted in June 2014](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/madagascar-actualise-son-arsenal-face-a-la-montee-des-risques-numeriques>), which defines offences such as fraudulent access to information systems and data breaches. The Council of Ministers [adopted a bill recasting the law](<https://www.2424.mg/cadre-juridique-le-gouvernement-propose-une-mise-a-jour-de-la-loi-sur-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/>) on 7 July 2026. The government [tabled it as bill n°042/2026 at the National Assembly on 28 August 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/madagascar-actualise-son-arsenal-face-a-la-montee-des-risques-numeriques>). [It sets penalties of two to ten years and fines of 100,000 to 100 million ariary](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/madagascar-actualise-son-arsenal-face-a-la-montee-des-risques-numeriques>). On 4 September deputies [voted to postpone adoption](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/09/tsimbazaza-les-deputes-ajournent-le.html>) of the 96-article text. It was [not among the 17 bills on the agenda](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/09/session-extraordinaire-le-texte-sur-la.html>) of the extraordinary session opening 17 September 2026. By 24 October it was [back on the Assembly's agenda](<https://newsmada.com/2026/10/24/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-les-parlementaires-reexaminent-le-projet-de-loi/>), with committee examination set for 2 November. In early November [some of its provisions drew reservations over free expression, social media content and personal data](<https://www.madagascar-tribune.com/Gouvernement-Mahefa-Andriamampiadana-demissionne-pour-raisons-de-sante-des.html>).

**Use of the law.** The 2026 bill [would create the Upin digital police](<https://www.madagascar-tribune.com/Loi-sur-la-cybercriminalite-l-Etat-renforce-les-controles-alors-que-l-acces-a-l.html>). A digital-law specialist interviewed by Midi Madagasikara says it would [require suspicious-transaction reports on Mobile Money transfers of 10 million ariary or more](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/legislation-numerique-fenitra-ravelomanantsoa-plaide-pour-un-equilibre-entre-securite-numerique-et-libertes-individuelles/>) and tighten SIM identification.

***Also on file***. [Unité de protection et d'investigation numérique (Upin)](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/09/session-extraordinaire-le-texte-sur-la.html>), [CIRT Madagascar](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/madagascar-set-to-publish-new-cybersecurity-law-amid-rising-digital-threats>), [UN Convention against Cybercrime](<https://newsmada.com/2026/09/10/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-madagascar-signe-le-traite-de-lonu/>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
