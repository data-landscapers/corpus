# Comoros

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Comoros has cybercrime offences in its Penal Code. According to the national digital agency ANADEN, the President promulgated by decree on 16 February 2021 the [new Penal Code](<https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique>), loi n°20-38/AU of 29 December 2020, whose articles 449 to 505 set out cybercrime offences, online intellectual property infringement and service-provider liability. The same ANADEN publication of 4 March 2021 lists a specific cybersecurity law as drafted but not yet adopted, to follow the Penal Code.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact on use is the promulgation itself. ANADEN states that the President promulgated the [Penal Code](<https://anaden.org/actualites/anaden-information-les-avancees-sur-le-code-numerique>) by decree on 16 February 2021, with its cybercrime offences in articles 449 to 505. The same source gives the Penal Code as the first step, with a specific cybersecurity law and a digital code to follow. It records no case brought under these articles.
