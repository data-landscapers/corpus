# Ghana

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** [Digital Policy Alert](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-ghana>) states that Ghana's 2020 Cybersecurity Act establishes measures for critical information infrastructure protection, cybercrime prevention and incident response, and that Ghana implemented the Act. It names the Cyber Security Authority as the body created under the Act. In August 2026 the [Cyber Security Authority sanctioned bodies](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/office-of-registrar-of-companies-and-service-provider-fined-ghc240-000-for-breaches-cybersecurity-directive-breaches.html>) under Section 92 of the Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038), which the article treats as the law in force. An [October 2026 op-ed](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bold-ambitions-fragile-foundations-is-ghana-ready-for-the-ai-and-data-centre-risks-it-is-inviting/>) describes the Act as the framework for protecting critical information infrastructure and prosecuting cybercrimes.

**Use of the law.** In an article published on 13 August 2026, the [Cyber Security Authority fined the Office of the Registrar of Companies](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/office-of-registrar-of-companies-and-service-provider-fined-ghc240-000-for-breaches-cybersecurity-directive-breaches.html>) GH¢240,000, being 10,000 penalty units for each of two instances of non-compliance under Section 92(2) of Act 1038. It also fined Purpleline Solutions Limited GH¢120,000 for providing cybersecurity services without a licence. Both were fined as bodies, one a state office and one a private company.
