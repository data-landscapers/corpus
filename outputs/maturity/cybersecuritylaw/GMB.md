# The Gambia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Information and Communications Act, 2009](<https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/10478>) (No. 2 of 2009) entered into force on 29 May 2009. Its Part III of Chapter III, "Computer Misuse and Cyber Crime", creates offences such as unauthorised access to computer data. Parliament introduced a proposed Cybercrime Bill in March 2024, and the [Oxford Institute of Technology and Justice](<https://www.techandjustice.bsg.ox.ac.uk/thegambia-3447303d-c9d6-4476-963c-02898588dc35>) states that, as at June 2026, it has not been enacted. In May 2026 the [Gambia Press Union](<https://gambiaj.com/society/human-rights/gpu-warns-press-freedom-gains-mask-growing-threats-against-journalists-in-the-gambia/>) cited the Cybercrime Bill as pending legislation with broad surveillance powers, heavy fines and prison terms.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is the penalty the [Act's offences](<https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/10478>) carry: unauthorised access to computer data is punishable by a fine of 200,000 dalasis or up to five years' imprisonment, or both. Separately, The Gambia signed the [African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>) on 2 December 2022, a continental treaty covering both subjects.

***Also on file***. [African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention)](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years.
