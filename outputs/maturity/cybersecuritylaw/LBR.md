# Liberia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Liberia's [Cybercrime Act of 2025](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/koung-seeks-release-of-ai-image-suspect/>) was signed into law by President Boakai in 2026, and the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications has [published the approved Act](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/koung-seeks-release-of-ai-image-suspect/>). One report [says the Act requires regulations within six to twelve months](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-senator-dopoh-backs-enforcement-of-cybercrimes-law-advances-recommendations-for-smooth-implementation/>), and [another](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/liberias-new-cybercrime-law-the-new-digital-police-power-and-the-fight-over-free-speech/>) says the country has entered the enforcement phase. The Act is being [operationalized by the Liberia Telecommunications Authority and the Ministry of Justice](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609240628.html>), and ten civil society organisations plan to [engage lawmakers on amendments](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/ten-csos-form-digital-rights-coalition/article_af9fdbc9-c6ab-4b6a-b1f1-3981a3e53c4f.html>). [President Boakai approved the Cybercrime Act on 3 July 2026, after the House passed it in 2023 and the Senate in November 2025](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/dopoh-liberia-cybercrime-law-enforcement-safeguards/>).

**Use of the law.** In September 2026 police [arrested the commentator Jerry Nyantee Wrato](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-cdc-prepares-to-take-cybercrime-law-fight-to-supreme-court-after-wrato-release/>) over AI-generated images of the Vice President, then charging him and forwarding him to court. The Inspector General's letter of 21 September resolved to prosecute him under [sections 10.1, 15.1, 18.1 and 19.1 of the Act](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609240628.html>), covering misuse of devices, cyberstalking, incitement and harmful data messages. A court [ordered him released on habeas corpus on 22 September](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-cdc-prepares-to-take-cybercrime-law-fight-to-supreme-court-after-wrato-release/>). The arrest was presented as the Act's [first major public test](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberias-cybercrime-law-faces-its-first-real-trial-as-vp-koung-case-exposes-liberias-widening-fault-lines/>).
