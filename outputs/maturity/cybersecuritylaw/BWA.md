# Botswana

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Cybersecurity Act, 2025 (Act No. 21 of 2025) was [assented to on 5 November 2025 and gazetted on 11 November 2025](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Cybersecurity_ACT_-_5%20Nov_2025.pdf>). Its commencement is on a date the Minister appoints by Order, and it repeals and replaces the Cybercrime and Computer Related Crimes Act 2018. In March 2026 the communications minister said the Act was [expected to come into force in April](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90463>). Parliament had passed the Cybersecurity Bill, 2025 on [14 August 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88151>) under a certificate of urgency presented by the Minister of Communications and Innovation, after a [public consultation on the draft Bill held on 20 June 2025](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Preamble_to_the_Cybersecurity_Bill_Revised.pdf>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is that the ICT minister said, as [ITWeb reported in May 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-advances-robust-digital-ecosystems/rxP3jMBEb527A2ye>), that the Act was passed in November 2025 and establishes a National Cybersecurity Authority. [Botswana Daily News reported on 25 February 2026](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90260>) that the Act, signed into law the year before, sets up that Authority.

***Also on file***. [National Cybersecurity Authority](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/Cybersecurity_ACT_-_5%20Nov_2025.pdf>); [National Computer Security Incident Response](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90260>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
