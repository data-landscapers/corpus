# Sierra Leone

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone has a cybercrime law in force. The [Cyber Security and Crime Act, 2021](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2021/7/eng@2021-11-25/source.pdf>) was published as a supplement to the Gazette dated 25 November 2021, setting up a response coordination centre, an advisory council and a fund, and defining offences. The [national development plan](<https://moped.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Final_Sierra-Leone-MTNDP-2024-2030-1.pdf>) lists the Cybercrime Act 2021 among its sector milestones, and the [digital development strategy](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>) says the bill drafted in 2018 was signed into law in 2021.

**Use of the law.** The [national data strategy](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Data-Strategy_2023_v1.pdf>), published in January 2023, calls for capacity to implement the Cybersecurity and Cybercrimes Act, 2021. The [digital development strategy](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>) lists operationalising the institutions the law sets up: the Council, NC3, NCSIRCC and the Fund. A [World Bank project document](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/274311635244389705/pdf/Concept-Project-Information-Document-PID-Sierra-Leone-Digital-Transformation-Project-P177077.pdf>) of October 2021 lists the Cybercrime Law (2021) as progress, with a Cybersecurity Emergency Response Center still to be set up.

[ECOWAS directive on confidence-building measures in cyber/ICT](<https://www.ecowas.int/the-19th-meeting-of-ecowas-ministers-in-charge-of-telecommunications-ict-and-digitalization-adopts-the-ecowas-digital-sector-development-strategy-for-the-period-2024-2029/>); [National Cybersecurity Strategy and Policy 2021-2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>); [NC3](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>); [NCSIRCC](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>); [National Computer Security Incidence Response Coordination Center](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2021/7/eng@2021-11-25/source.pdf>); [National Cybersecurity Advisory Council](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2021/7/eng@2021-11-25/source.pdf>); [National Cyber Security Fund](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2021/7/eng@2021-11-25/source.pdf>); [Cybersecurity Emergency Response Center](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/274311635244389705/pdf/Concept-Project-Information-Document-PID-Sierra-Leone-Digital-Transformation-Project-P177077.pdf>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
