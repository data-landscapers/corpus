# Sierra Leone

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone has a cybercrime law in force. The [Cyber Security and Crime Act, 2021](<https://sierralii.gov.sl/akn/sl/act/2021/7/eng@2021-11-25/source.pdf>) was published as a supplement to the Gazette dated 25 November 2021, setting up a response coordination centre, an advisory council and a fund, and defining offences. The [national development plan](<https://moped.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Final_Sierra-Leone-MTNDP-2024-2030-1.pdf>) lists the Cybercrime Act 2021 among its sector milestones, and the [digital development strategy](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>) says the bill drafted in 2018 was signed into law in 2021.

**Use of the law.** A Freetown councillor was [charged at Pademba Road Court in November 2025](<https://sierraloaded.sl/local/councillor-court-alleged-cyber-charges-mp/>) with cyberstalking and cyberbullying under sections 44(1)(b) and 44(2)(b) of the Act. The councillor pleaded not guilty and was granted bail. On 5 August 2025 Joseph Sesay was [arraigned at the same court](<https://www.premiermedia-sl.com/man-arraigned-for-alleged-cyberbullying-in-freetown/>) on two counts of cyberstalking under section 44(1)(a). Hawa Hunt was [arrested on 22 December 2024](<https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2025/01/sierra-leone-fair-trial-hawa-hunt/>) and charged with two counts of sending insulting messages through a computer system, Amnesty International reports.
