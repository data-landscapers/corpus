# Malawi

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Malawi's Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act, Act 33 of 2016 and Chapter 74:02 of the laws, is the country's statute on computer and ICT offences. [The Act was published in the Gazette on 4 November 2016 and commenced on 1 June 2017](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>). It criminalises offences related to computer systems and ICT, and it does so within a single statute that also covers electronic transactions, as its title shows.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is the Act's commencement. The [Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act took effect on 1 June 2017](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>), more than nine years before October 2026, and it remains the Act that criminalises offences related to computer systems and ICT. Its text is published by the Malawi Legal Information Institute, which gives the Gazette date of 4 November 2016 and the Act number, 33 of 2016.

***Also on file***. [Malawi CERT (MCERT)](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>), a unit under the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority.

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
