# Malawi

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Malawi's Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act, Act 33 of 2016, criminalises offences related to computer systems and ICT. [It was gazetted on 4 November 2016 and commenced on 1 June 2017](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>). In October 2026 the Justice Minister [said a new cybercrime bill](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/malawi-pushes-tough-new-laws-to-track-expose-and-jail-online-scammers/>) should go to Parliament in the February to April 2027 session. The ICT Minister [said the 2016 Act would be reviewed](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/minister-chipungu-reveals-hackers-targeted-malawis-land-database/>) alongside the new bills.

**Use of the law.** Nyasa Times reports that Collins Magalasi was [charged in court in September 2026](<https://www.nyasatimes.com/magalasi-charged-with-hacking-malawi-presidents-phone-and-senior-officials-numbers/>) under section 83(1), read with section 89, of the 2016 Act. The charge is aiding the hacking of the President's and senior officials' phones. In August 2024 police [charged a Mzuzu man with cyber spamming](<https://mwnation.com/divisive-war-on-cybercrimes/>) under section 91. They also arrested the politicians Kamlepo Kalua and Bon Kalindo over voice notes said to breach the cyber law.

***Also on file***. [Malawi CERT (MCERT)](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2016/33/eng@2017-12-31>), a unit under the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority.

***Not found***. No source found shows the law in regular use in the last two years.
