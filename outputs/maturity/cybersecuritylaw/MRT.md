# Mauritania

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** [Law no. 2016-007 on cybercrime](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/loi_2016_-_007_relative_la_cybercriminalite.pdf>) was adopted by the National Assembly and the Senate and promulgated by the President of the Republic. It has 52 articles covering offences, search and interception powers, and international cooperation. The year, 2016, is taken from the law number, and the law is cited as Loi n° 2016-007 relative à la cybercriminalité. Its provisions on offences sit beside its provisions on how investigators may search and intercept, and its provisions on how the state cooperates with other countries.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is the content of the same [2016 cybercrime law](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/sites/default/files/loi_2016_-_007_relative_la_cybercriminalite.pdf>): its 52 articles give search and interception powers, alongside the offences it defines and its provisions on international cooperation. Both chambers of the legislature adopted it before the President promulgated it, so the text that gives investigators those powers is a promulgated law and not a draft.
