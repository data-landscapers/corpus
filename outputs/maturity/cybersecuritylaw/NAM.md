# Namibia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** No cybercrime or cybersecurity law in force is known. The proposed law is the Namibia Cybercrime Bill. NAM-CSIRT's 2025 landscape report says that [in the absence of a cybersecurity law in Namibia it looks to the government to expedite the Cybercrime Bill and related regulations](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>), and it recommends finalising and enacting the Cybercrime, Data Protection and CII regulations. NAMPA reports that the Namibia Cybercrime Bill [is not yet law](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22852343>). [The Speaker said in June 2025 that Namibia is finalising a Cybercrime Bill](<https://thebrief.com.na/2025/06/namibia-finalising-ai-data-security-laws/>). [In January 2026 the Cybercrime Bill was still named as a bill, aimed at technology-enabled and AI-related threats](<https://thebrief.com.na/2026/01/parliament-urges-tighter-ai-regulation-amid-growing-digital-risks/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is the consultation on the bill. In February 2026 [the Ministry of Information and Communication Technology consulted stakeholders from Kavango East, Kavango West and Zambezi at Rundu](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22852343>) on the proposed cybersecurity and cybercrime bill. Namibia's computer incident response team, NAM-CSIRT, [published its 2025 cybersecurity landscape report](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>) and, dated by its publication in April 2026, it asks the government to expedite the Cybercrime Bill alongside related regulations.

***Also on file***. [NAM-CSIRT](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>), the Namibia Cyber Security Incident Response Team, owned by CRAN.

***Not found***. No source found says a cybercrime or cybersecurity law is in force.
