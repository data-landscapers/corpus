# Namibia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Namibia's proposed cybercrime law is the Cybercrime Bill. In December 2024 the regulator CRAN [said Namibia lacks dedicated cybercrime legislation](<https://namibiadailynews.info/cran-and-nam-csirt-tackle-ransomware-attack-on-telecom-operator/>). A draft bill [was presented to stakeholders in Windhoek](<https://www.thevillager.com.na/national/2026/cybercrime-bill-in-the-pipeline-for-namibia/>) on 18 February 2026, where the country was said to have no law yet that directly governs cybercrime. The Windhoek Observer, citing the 2026/27 budget estimates, [reports that drafting is complete](<https://observer24.com.na/cybercrime-bill-heads-to-parliament/>). It says the bill was due to go to Parliament in March 2026, after a validation workshop.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is the consultation on the bill. In February 2026 the ICT ministry [consulted stakeholders at Rundu](<https://www.nampa.org/text/22852343>) from Kavango East, Kavango West and Zambezi. The response team NAM-CSIRT, in its [2025 landscape report](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>) published in April 2026, asks the government to expedite the bill. In July 2025 the ICT Minister [said drafting had been delayed](<https://www.namibian.com.na/lack-of-local-cybercrime-experts-delays-cybercrime-bill/>) by a lack of local cyberlaw expertise.

***Also on file***. [NAM-CSIRT](<https://nam-csirt.na/download/namibia-cybersecurity-landscape-report-2025/>), the Namibia Cyber Security Incident Response Team, owned by CRAN.

***Not found***. No source found says a cybercrime or cybersecurity law is in force.
