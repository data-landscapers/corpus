# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo has the Code du numérique, promulgated in 2023. The news agency ACP states that the [Code du numérique](<https://acp.cd/genre/violence-numerique-une-proposition-de-loi-de-protection-des-victimes-annoncee-a-kinshasa/>) makes non-consensual sharing of intimate content, privacy breaches and cyberharassment criminal offences in the country. On 17 August 2026 the Attorney General at the Court of Cassation treated the [Digital Code](<https://acp.cd/anglais/drc-judicial-police-officers-advised-to-prosecute-perpetrators-of-digital-offences/>) as in force, as the news agency ACP reported that same day.

**Use of the law.** On 17 August 2026 the Attorney General at the Court of Cassation told judicial police officers to take up offences under the [Digital Code](<https://acp.cd/anglais/drc-judicial-police-officers-advised-to-prosecute-perpetrators-of-digital-offences/>) on their own initiative. This is an instruction to the officers who bring cases, issued in the Code's fourth year, and it covers the offences the Code creates. ACP also reports that the offences the [Code du numérique](<https://acp.cd/genre/violence-numerique-une-proposition-de-loi-de-protection-des-victimes-annoncee-a-kinshasa/>) names include cyberharassment and privacy breaches.

***Also on file***. [Conseil national de cybersécurité](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/>) and [Agence nationale de cybersécurité](<https://acp.cd/economie/numerique-le-gouvernement-veut-une-chaine-de-responsabilite-visible-en-cybersecurite/>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
