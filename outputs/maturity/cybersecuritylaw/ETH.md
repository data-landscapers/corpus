# Ethiopia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Federal Negarit Gazette, 32nd Year No. 41](<https://www.insa.gov.et/pdf/download/critical-infrastructure-cybersecurity-proclamation-no-14262026-1786083598>) carries Critical Infrastructure Cyber Security Protection Proclamation No. 1426/2026, done at Addis Ababa on 21 July 2026 and signed by the President. Its article 28 provides that it enters into force one year after publication, which puts commencement in July 2027. [INSA's Director General](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>) said on 7 August 2026 that the House of People's Representatives unanimously ratified the proclamation, which sets 18 obligations for owners of 12 critical sectors.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact held is that, according to [INSA's Director General](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>), institutions are to use a transition period to prepare for compliance with the proclamation's 18 obligations. The [gazette text](<https://www.insa.gov.et/pdf/download/critical-infrastructure-cybersecurity-proclamation-no-14262026-1786083598>) places entry into force one year after publication, so the transition runs until July 2027. The proclamation is a critical infrastructure measure for 12 sectors, under which owners face 18 obligations.

***Also on file***. [Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity Fund](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>), run by INSA.

***Not found***. No source found says a cybercrime or cybersecurity law is in force.
