# Ethiopia

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Ethiopia's cybercrime law is [Computer Crime Proclamation No. 958/2016](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c80lyxz1l1ko>)), which the Federal High Court applied in a conviction in November 2024. A [2024 law journal article](<https://doi.org/10.20372/bdujol.v14i2.1853>)) treats it as the current cybercrime law and describes a 2019 draft proclamation as a further reform. The [Federal Negarit Gazette](<https://www.insa.gov.et/pdf/download/critical-infrastructure-cybersecurity-proclamation-no-14262026-1786083598>)) carries Critical Infrastructure Cyber Security Protection Proclamation No. 1426/2026, signed on 21 July 2026. Its article 28 brings it into force one year after publication, in July 2027. [INSA's Director General](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>)) said it sets 18 obligations for owners of 12 critical sectors.

**Use of the law.** In November 2024 the Federal High Court [found Meskerem Abera guilty](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c80lyxz1l1ko>)) on two counts under Proclamation No. 958/2016. The case concerned two YouTube programmes of Ethio Neqat, which she founded, and her lawyer told BBC Amharic that sentencing was set for 11 November 2024. [Birr Metrics reported](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopian-high-court-frees-tiktok-streaming-passenger-and-co-defendants/>)) in December 2025 that Yohanis Daniel faced an added charge under the computer-crime law. He had recorded and live-streamed an August 2024 flight incident on TikTok.

***Also on file***. [Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity Fund](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9359494>), run by INSA.

***Not found***. No count of cases under the law has been published for a period ending in the last two years.
