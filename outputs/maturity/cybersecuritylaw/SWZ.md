# Eswatini

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act, 2022](<https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/COMPUTER-CRIME--CYBERCRIME-ACT.pdf>) (Act No. 6 of 2022) was published in the Government Gazette Extraordinary on 4 March 2022 and, by section 1(2), came into operation on the date of publication. On 1 October 2026 the Director of Public Prosecutions [described the Act](<https://independentnews.co.sz/49053/news/minister-savannah-launches-cybersecurity-awareness-month/>) as the legal framework for offences committed through or against computer systems. In April 2026 the minister [said the Act is already the legal groundwork](<https://swazi24.com/44934/news/maziya-opens-push-for-national-data-policy/>), with its regulations at an advanced stage. The Prime Minister had [spoken of it in October 2025](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>) as passed in 2022 and among the cyber laws now in force.

**Use of the law.** On 27 August 2026 an officer of the communications regulator ESCCOM [said](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/esccom-warns-emaswati-cyberbullying-is-a-crime/>) that cyberbullying is a crime under the Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act of 2022 and that people can be arrested for it. The Director of Public Prosecutions said on 1 October 2026 that her office has [trained prosecutors](<https://independentnews.co.sz/49053/news/minister-savannah-launches-cybersecurity-awareness-month/>) on the Act. These are official statements on how the law applies and on preparing prosecutors to use it.
