# Eswatini

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The [Computer Crime and Cybercrime Act, 2022](<https://www.gov.sz/images/ICT/COMPUTER-CRIME--CYBERCRIME-ACT.pdf>), Act No. 6 of 2022, was published in the Government Gazette on 4 March 2022. By section 1(2) it came into operation that day. In October 2025 the Prime Minister [named it among the cyber laws now in force](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/cybersecurity-key-to-national-security-and-growth-pm/>). In April 2026 the minister [called the Act the legal groundwork](<https://swazi24.com/44934/news/maziya-opens-push-for-national-data-policy/>), with its regulations at an advanced stage. The judiciary says [its judicial officers are trained on the Act](<https://www.judiciary.org.sz/services>), including the handling of electronic evidence. On 1 October 2026 the Director of Public Prosecutions [described the Act](<https://independentnews.co.sz/49053/news/minister-savannah-launches-cybersecurity-awareness-month/>) as the legal framework for offences through or against computer systems.

**Use of the law.** A Mbabane woman was [convicted by the Magistrates Court in August 2025](<https://swazi24.com/20604/news/crime-and-courts/whatsapp-insult-lands-woman-in-jail/>) over a WhatsApp message. She was sentenced to three years or a E3,000 fine. In September 2025 two accused [appeared in Manzini Magistrates Court](<https://swazidailynews.com/2537/national/two-alleged-facata-scammers-arrested-for-defrauding-victims-over-e80-000/>) on 30 fraud charges, some of them under the Act. A woman arrested by the police in July 2025 [faces counts under the Act](<https://www.eswatiniobserver.com/facata-suspect-facing-190-charges-denied-bail/>). The High Court denied her bail.
