# Côte d'Ivoire

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Ordinance No. 2024-950, a cybersecurity text, is in force, and on 16 June 2025 the Senate [unanimously adopted the bill ratifying it](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2427/ivory-coast-senate-unanimously-approves-cybersecurity-and-e-government-bill>). The same bill updates the 2017 ordinance on electronic exchanges. The source describes the measure as a cybersecurity bill and reports no dissent in the vote. [The 2013 law on the fight against cybercrime, no. 2013-451 of 19 June 2013, was amended by law no. 2023-593 of 7 June 2023](<https://www.linfodrome.com/societe/126588-cybersecurite-en-cote-d-ivoire-l-anssi-la-sentinelle-du-cyberespace-ivoirien>).

**Use of the law.** The national information-security agency's report for 2024, presented on 22 May 2025, says its cybercrime platform [received and handled 12,100 files in 2024](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634113/societe/cybercriminalite-en-cote-divoire-12-100-dossiers-traites-pour-un-prejudice-de-7-milliards-de-fcfa>), up from 8,132 the year before. The largest groups were 2,822 files on attacks on human dignity and 2,326 on online fraud, and the files also covered fraudulent access to information systems. The agency put the financial loss at about 7 billion CFA francs, down from more than 9 billion a year earlier. The report as relayed does not name the law the files were handled under.
