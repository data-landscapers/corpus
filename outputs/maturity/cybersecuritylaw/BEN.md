# Benin

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** [Loi n° 2017-20 of 20 April 2018, the Code du numérique](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/loi-2017-20/download>), was adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President. Its Livre VI defines illegal access to and penetration of an information system. The code carries the date 20 April 2018, and its Livre VI treats unauthorised entry into computer systems as an offence, in two forms: illegal access and penetration.

**Use of the law.** Benin's Centre national d'investigations numériques [reported over 6,500 digital-offence complaints in 2025](<https://africaho.bj/article/infractions-en-ligne-au-benin-ce-que-revele-le-bilan-2025-du-cnin/>), against about 4,000 the year before, and about 1,500 arrests of online-fraud suspects. During [INTERPOL's Operation Sentinel](<https://www.interpol.int/en/News-and-Events/News/2025/574-arrests-and-USD-3-million-recovered-in-coordinated-cybercrime-operation-across-Africa>), from 27 October to 27 November 2025, Benin took down 43 malicious domains and shut 4,318 extortion and scam accounts, leading to 106 arrests. Neither source names the law. The complaints figure covers the whole of 2025, and the arrests figure covers suspects in online fraud.
