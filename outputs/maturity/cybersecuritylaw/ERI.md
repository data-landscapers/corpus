# Eritrea

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Eritrea has no separate cybercrime act. The [Council of Europe cybercrime office's legal profile](<https://rm.coe.int/octocom-legal-profile-eritrea/16809efad6>), in its version of 5 May 2020, maps the Penal Code to the Budapest Convention's offence articles as the domestic equivalent. Article 374 covers unauthorized use of a computer, including access, interception, and data and system interference. Article 298 covers violation of privacy. The same profile lists Eritrea as not a signatory to the Budapest Convention.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is the scope of the provisions themselves. The [2020 legal profile](<https://rm.coe.int/octocom-legal-profile-eritrea/16809efad6>) places unauthorized computer use and interference with data and systems under Article 374 of the Penal Code, and violation of privacy under Article 298, as the offences that stand in for a cybercrime act.
