# Guinea

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The president of the Haute Autorité de la Communication [said in July 2025](<https://aminata.com/guinee_loi-sur-la-cybersecurite-le-president-de-haute-autorite-de-la-communication-apporte-des-precisions/>) that the cybersecurity law, and not the press law, will be applied to web media that break the rules. The same statement, reported by Aminata on 26 July 2025, says the penalty is prison for any person. The law is referred to as the loi sur la cybersécurité, and the statement treats it as the one that governs web media conduct today.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact held is the [same statement of July 2025](<https://aminata.com/guinee_loi-sur-la-cybersecurite-le-president-de-haute-autorite-de-la-communication-apporte-des-precisions/>), in which the president of the Haute Autorité de la Communication said the law will be applied to web media that break the rules, with prison for any person. It is a statement of how the law is to be applied to online media, made by the media regulator's head.

***Noted, not assessed***. ANSSI Guinée, the Agence Nationale de la Sécurité des Systèmes d'Information, under the Ministère de la Communication, de l'Économie Numérique et de l'Innovation.

***Not held***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
