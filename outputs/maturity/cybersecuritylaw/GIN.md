# Guinea

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Guinea has had a law on cybersecurity and personal data protection [since 2016](<https://afrique-diplomatique.com/guinee-consultations-sur-la-revision-du-cadre-juridique-de-la-cybersecurite/>)), mainly aimed at cybercrime, according to the director of ANSSI's alert centre. The ministry's secretary-general, Nouhan Traore, [said in October 2025](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>)) that Guinea has put the law in place. On 13 August 2026 ANSSI [held a consultation workshop](<https://afrique-diplomatique.com/guinee-consultations-sur-la-revision-du-cadre-juridique-de-la-cybersecurite/>)) on draft texts on cybersecurity and cybercrime. The drafts were prepared with SOCSLAWYERS to update the 2016 framework.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact held is the [same statement of 26 July 2025](<https://aminata.com/guinee_loi-sur-la-cybersecurite-le-president-de-haute-autorite-de-la-communication-apporte-des-precisions/>). In it the regulator's president said the cybersecurity law will be applied to web media that break the rules, and that the penalty is prison for any person who breaks them, whoever that person is.

***Also on file***. [ANSSI Guinée](<https://anssi.gov.gn/campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-et-de-formation-sur-la-cybersecurite-et-la-lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite/>), the Agence Nationale de la Sécurité des Systèmes d'Information, under the Ministère de la Communication, de l'Économie Numérique et de l'Innovation.

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law has been reported in the last three years.
