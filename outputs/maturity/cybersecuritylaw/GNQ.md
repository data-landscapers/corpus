# Equatorial Guinea

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** On 1 July 2026 the Government [stated](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_recuerda_la_plena_vigencia_de_la_ley_que_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>) that Ley 7/2024 is fully in force. The law dates from 23 December 2024. It regulates social networks, computer crimes and cybercrime. In September 2026 a law firm treated it as [applicable law](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas>), citing its articles 2, 50 and 60. In October 2026 the telecommunications ministry said it had [drafted a preliminary bill on cybersecurity](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/continuan_las_comparecencias_de_los_miembros_del_gobierno_en_la_comision_parlamentaria_que_analiza_los_presupuestos_generales>).

**Use of the law.** On 16 September 2026, lawyers acting for vice-president Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue [lodged four criminal complaints](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas>) under Ley 7/2024 at Juzgado de Instruccion Numero I de Malabo. They name Maria Luzmila Ondo, Mocache Massoko of Diario Rombe, Jose Carlos Ndong and Maria Teresa Siemfum. The complaints rest on the law's articles 2, 50 and 60, and the case is at an initial stage, awaiting the court. The complainant is the vice-president himself. The law's own title covers social networks, computer crimes and cybercrime together.
