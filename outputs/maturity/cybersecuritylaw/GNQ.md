# Equatorial Guinea

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The Government [stated](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_recuerda_la_plena_vigencia_de_la_ley_que_regula_el_uso_de_las_redes_sociales_los_delitos_informaticos_y_la_ciber_delincuencia>) on 1 July 2026 that Ley 7/2024 of 23 December 2024, regulating social networks, computer crimes and cybercrime, is fully in force. In September 2026 a law firm treated it as [applicable law](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas>), grounding complaints on its articles 2, 50 and 60.

**Use of the law.** On 16 September 2026, lawyers acting for vice-president Teodoro Nguema Obiang Mangue [lodged four criminal complaints](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_despacho_que_representa_a_se_nguema_obiang_mangue_explica_las_acciones_contra_diadario_rombe_y_otras_tres_personas>) under Ley 7/2024 at Juzgado de Instruccion Numero I de Malabo. They name Maria Luzmila Ondo, Mocache Massoko of Diario Rombe, Jose Carlos Ndong and Maria Teresa Siemfum. The case is at an initial stage, awaiting the court. The complainant is the vice-president himself, so the use held is a case brought by a senior state official against four named people, one of them from the Diario Rombe outlet. The law's own title covers social networks, computer crimes and cybercrime together.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
