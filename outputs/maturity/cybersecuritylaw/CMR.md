# Cameroon

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** A Nkafu brief published on 4 September 2026 says Cameroon's legal framework is [anchored in a 2010 cybersecurity law and 2012 decrees](<https://nkafu.org/cybersecurity-as-industrial-policy-protecting-cameroons-digital-economy/>), and it calls for the 2010 law to be revised. The law is [law no. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybercrime](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>), which Le Jour treats as the existing law in Cameroon. The [Nkafu brief](<https://nkafu.org/cybersecurity-as-industrial-policy-protecting-cameroons-digital-economy/>) of September 2026 and the [Le Jour report](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>) of June 2026 therefore point to the same 2010 text as the law the country works under, a law dating from December 2010 that the brief wants revised, sixteen years before either piece appeared.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is a workshop reported by [Le Jour on 16 June 2026](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). Held on 8 and 9 June 2026 in Yaounde, the workshop reviewed articles of law no. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybercrime.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
