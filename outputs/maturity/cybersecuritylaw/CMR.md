# Cameroon

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Law no. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybercrime is [treated as the existing law](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>) in a June 2026 Le Jour report on a workshop in Yaounde on 8 and 9 June that reviewed its articles. A [Nkafu brief of 4 September 2026](<https://nkafu.org/cybersecurity-as-industrial-policy-protecting-cameroons-digital-economy/>) says the legal framework is anchored in a 2010 cybersecurity law and 2012 decrees, and calls for the 2010 law to be revised. A [July 2025 African Law & Business article](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/expert-views/key-features-of-cameroons-new-data-protection-law/>) lists the law among the complementary laws that make the data protection Act enforceable, and an [August 2024 CENADI workshop](<https://www.dgb.cm/le-cenadi-sensibilise-les-administrations-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) heard it listed among the local texts that frame data protection.

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is a workshop reported by [Le Jour on 16 June 2026](<https://lejour.cm/cameroun-quand-les-lois-sur-la-cybercriminalite-menacent-les-libertes/>). Held on 8 and 9 June 2026 in Yaounde, the workshop reviewed articles of law no. 2010/012 of 21 December 2010 on cybercrime.
