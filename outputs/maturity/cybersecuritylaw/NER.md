# Niger

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Niger's cybercrime law is law no. 2019-33 of 3 July 2019. It [was amended on 12 June 2024](<https://www.lesahel.org/lutte-contre-la-cybercriminalite-la-police-judiciaire-face-aux-nouvelles-technologies-de-linformation/>) to set penalties for online defamation, insults and harmful data. Human Rights Watch says the amendment [restored prison terms of up to five years](<https://www.hrw.org/fr/news/2025/11/06/des-journalistes-nigeriens-arretes-en-vertu-de-la-loi-sur-la-cybercriminalite>). They apply to disseminating data likely to disturb public order. In April 2026 a panellist [cited the law among the texts in force](<https://www.lesahel.org/la-cybersecurite-au-niger-la-necessite-dun-espace-numerique-securise-pour-une-souverainete-integrale/>) that regulate cybersecurity.

**Use of the law.** The law was used against journalists and a lawyer. [Police in Niamey arrested six journalists on 30 October 2025](<https://www.hrw.org/fr/news/2025/11/06/des-journalistes-nigeriens-arretes-en-vertu-de-la-loi-sur-la-cybercriminalite>), Human Rights Watch reports, and they were charged under the 2019 law over an invitation to a state press conference. The lawyer Lirwana was [charged and detained from 21 September 2026](<https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/08/nigers-junta-tightens-its-grip-on-free-expression>). On 13 October 2026 the Niamey High Court [convicted him under Article 31](<https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/arrest-and-subsequent-conviction-of-human-rights-defender-and-lawyer-lirwana-abdourahmane-for-social-media-post/>) and sentenced him to two years and a 2 million CFA franc fine. In September 2026 the judicial police's cybercrime division [arrested suspects in three fraud and blackmail cases](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/22433-cybercriminalite-au-niger-faux-entrepreneur-billets-multiplies-credits-detournes-la-dpj-demantele-trois-reseaux>).
