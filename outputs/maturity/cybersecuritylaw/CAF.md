# Central African Republic

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** The official laws index of the telecoms regulator ARCEP lists [Loi 24.002 of 21 February 2024 on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime](<https://www.arcep.cf/fr/index.php/reglementation/lois-et-ordonnances>) among the country's laws. Africa Top Success reported that the National Assembly [voted the cybersecurity and anti-cybercrime bill by acclamation on 25 January 2024](<https://www.africatopsuccess.com/un-projet-de-loi-pour-lutter-contre-la-cybercriminalite-en-centrafrique/>), with promulgation by the President expected within days. The bill sets prison terms of 6 to 10 years and fines of 5 to 10 million CFA francs. In March 2023 [terms of reference for a consultant](<https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/inline-files/TDRS%20Assistance%20Jurique%20Renforcement%20du%20cadre%20l%C3%A9gal.pdf>) described a draft law on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime that needed improving, and asked the consultant to draft amendments and implementing texts.

**Use of the law.** An [opinion piece published on 18 August 2026](<https://corbeaunews-centrafrique.org/cybercriminalite-a-saint-petersbourg-evariste-ngamana-defend-un-combat-perdu-davance-contre-les-reseaux-sociaux/>) refers to the government's cybercrime law as wielded by the prosecutor's office and police. It dates from 2026, more than two years after the Assembly vote of January 2024.

***Also on file***. [Agence nationale de la cybersécurité (ANCy)](<https://www.africatopsuccess.com/un-projet-de-loi-pour-lutter-contre-la-cybercriminalite-en-centrafrique/>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
