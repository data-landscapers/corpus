# Sudan

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Sudan has a Cybercrime Law, and courts apply it: [a criminal court in El Obeid convicted a defendant under Articles 19 and 24 in August 2026](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/>). [The Sudanese Journalists Syndicate cites the Cybercrime Act as the basis of a live arrest and calls for its repeal](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>). [The SudaPass terms cite the Cybercrime Law as the basis of criminal penalties of up to twelve years for electronic sabotage or forgery, and refer to a Cyber Security Act of 2025](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>). [On 1 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved a draft Cybersecurity Regulation Bill 2026 from the digital transformation ministry](<https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya>).

**Use of the law.** The law has been used against an anti-war critic and a journalist. [In August 2026 a criminal court in El Obeid sentenced pharmacist Ahmed Suleiman Ahmed Al-Waqi to six years, three of them under Articles 19 and 24 of the Cybercrime Law](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/>), his lawyer said. A phone search had found anti-war messages. [In August 2026 criminal investigation police arrested journalist Gurashi Awad in River Nile State on a complaint by a General Intelligence Service lieutenant colonel](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>), the Sudanese Journalists Syndicate said.

***Also on file***. [SudaPass](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>), the Sudanese digital identity run by the National Electronic Authentication Authority.

***Not found***. No source found shows the law in regular use in the last two years.
