# Sudan

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Sudan has a Cybercrime Law, and courts apply it. [A rights group says it was amended in 2018, 2020 and 2022](<https://skylineforhuman.org/en/news/details/875/sudans-draconian-cybercrime-law-amendments-pose-grave-threat-to-digital-freedoms>), and that the Cabinet approved a further draft amendment on 13 October 2025. [A criminal court in El Obeid convicted a defendant under Articles 19 and 24 in August 2026](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/>). [The Sudanese Journalists Syndicate cites the Cybercrime Act as the basis of a live arrest and calls for its repeal](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>). [On 1 November 2026 the Council of Ministers approved a draft Cybersecurity Regulation Bill 2026 from the digital transformation ministry](<https://suna.sd/posts/mgls-alozraaa-ydyn-alaaatdaaaat-almtkrr-aal-almnshat-alkhdmy-aabr-almsyrat-alty-tntlk-mn-dakhl-dol-athyobya>).

**Use of the law.** [On 21 July 2026 the Red Sea State cybercrime prosecutor issued 17 arrest warrants](<https://rsf.org/en/sudan-army-backed-authorities-are-prosecuting-15-journalists-and-two-media-outlets-under-anti>), RSF reports. They name 15 journalists and two news sites, charged with defamation, invasion of privacy and false information over reports on alleged corruption. [The Sudanese Journalists' Syndicate says complaints under the Cybercrime Act were filed against 47 journalists and media organisations in one instance](<https://www.dabangasudan.org/en/all-news/article/sudanese-journalists-syndicate-documents-673-violations-and-calls-for-end-to-impunity>). After them 18 journalists were declared wanted. [In August 2026 a criminal court in El Obeid sentenced pharmacist Ahmed Suleiman Ahmed Al-Waqi to six years](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/sudanese-army-accused-of-using-courts-to-silence-anti-war-voices/>), his lawyer said. Three of the years were under the Cybercrime Law, after a phone search found anti-war messages. [That month police in River Nile State arrested journalist Gurashi Awad](<https://theweeklyvisionews.net/2026/08/17/journalist-detained-in-sudan-following-cybercrime-complaint-by-intelligence-officer-syndicate-says/>) on an intelligence officer's complaint. [On 20 October 2025 a court in Port Sudan convicted lawyer Montaser Abdullah under the Cybercrimes Act](<https://www.frontlinedefenders.org/en/case/human-rights-defender-montaser-abdullah-may-face-death-penalty>).
