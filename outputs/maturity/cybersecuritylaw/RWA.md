# Rwanda

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Rwanda has a cybersecurity law in force. The East African Policy Observer, drawing on the Carnegie Endowment's Africa Technology Policy Tracker, says [Rwanda's binding legal instruments include cybersecurity law](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>). The commentary was published on 30 July 2026 and reviews Rwanda, Nigeria and Zambia together. It adds that [all three countries reviewed have cybersecurity laws](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>).

**Use of the law.** The nearest held fact is the same statement of July 2026: [Rwanda's binding legal instruments include cybersecurity law, and Rwanda is one of the three countries the commentary says have cybersecurity laws](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>). Published on 30 July 2026 by the East African Policy Observer, the commentary reports on Rwanda, Nigeria and Zambia and draws on the Carnegie Endowment's Africa Technology Policy Tracker and describes the law as one of Rwanda's binding legal instruments, listed beside the two other countries it reviews.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Africa Technology Policy Tracker](<https://eastafricanpolicyobserver.org/commentary/new-africa-tech-policy-insights-on-nigeria-rwanda-and-zambia-ictworks/>), run by the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace with the African Telecommunications Union.

***Not held***. Any arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years.
