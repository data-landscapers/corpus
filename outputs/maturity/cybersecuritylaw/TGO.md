# Togo

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** [Loi n° 2018-026 of 7 December 2018](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_07_12_2018-63E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B024TER.pdf>) on cybersecurity and the fight against cybercrime was promulgated and published in the Journal officiel, setting the cybersecurity framework and defining and punishing cybercrime offences. [Loi n° 2022-009 of 24 June 2022](<https://ancy.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Loi-n%C2%B0-2022-009-portant-modification-de-la-loi-n%C2%B0-2018-026-du-07-decembre-sur-la-cybersecurite-et-la-lutte-.pdf>), adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President, amends articles 2, 3 and 6 of it. The 2018 text was issued as a special number of the Journal officiel and remains the base text that the 2022 law modifies.

**Use of the law.** The visas of [Décret n° 2022-094/PR of 9 September 2022](<https://monidentite.anid.gouv.tg/documents/d/anid_pub/pr-decret-n-2022-094-pr-relatif-aux-modalites-d-inscription-de-rectification-et-d-actualisation-des-donnees-demographiques-et-biometriques-des-personnes-physiques>) cite the 2018 law as modified by the 2022 law, treating it as standing law. The government thus relies on it in later regulation, which is a use of the text as a legal basis. The decree cites both the 2018 law and the 2022 amendment as current, within three months of the amendment.

***Also on file***. [Agence Nationale de la Cybersécurité (ANCy)](<https://ancy.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Loi-n%C2%B0-2022-009-portant-modification-de-la-loi-n%C2%B0-2018-026-du-07-decembre-sur-la-cybersecurite-et-la-lutte-.pdf>), [Agence nationale de la cybersécurité](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_07_12_2018-63E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B024TER.pdf>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
