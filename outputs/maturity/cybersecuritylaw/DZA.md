# Algeria

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** Algeria's [Law 09-04 of 5 August 2009](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>) sets out particular rules on preventing and fighting offences linked to information and communication technologies. [Presidential decree 20-05 of 20 January 2020](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>) lists it among the laws the decree rests on, and so treats it as standing law. [The decree was published on 20 January 2020](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>), the date it carries.

**Use of the law.** The nearest fact is the state's own reliance on the text, not a case brought under it. [Presidential decree 20-05 of 20 January 2020](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>) cites Law 09-04 of 5 August 2009 among the laws it rests on, which shows the executive treating the law as current in 2020. The law's French title is the [Loi n° 09-04 du 5 août 2009 portant règles particulières relatives à la prévention et à la lutte contre les infractions liées aux technologies de l'information et de la communication](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Conseil national de la sécurité des systèmes d'information](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>); [Agence de la sécurité des systèmes d'information](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2020/F2020004.pdf>).

***Not found***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
