# South Africa

## Cybercrime and cybersecurity law

**Law in force.** IOL, in an article dated 16 September 2026 on whether South Africa will ban under-16s from social media, [reports the Cybercrimes Act (2020)](<https://iol.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-16-south-africa-has-no-plan-to-ban-under-16s-from-social-media2/>) as an existing South African law. The report describes the Act as already providing protections, and it gives no other date for that statement.

**Use of the law.** The same IOL report says the [Cybercrimes Act (2020) deals with hacking, online fraud and sharing intimate images without consent](<https://iol.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-16-south-africa-has-no-plan-to-ban-under-16s-from-social-media2/>). It presents these as protections that South Africa already has, and it makes the point in answer to the question of a social media ban for under-16s. The article is dated 16 September 2026, and IOL presents the Act as law already in place on that date, under the name Cybercrimes Act and the year 2020, as its own statement and not a quotation of the text.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No arrest, charge, prosecution, conviction or penalty under the law in the last three years is held.
