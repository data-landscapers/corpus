# Mali

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [ECOWAS SIGTEL's regional inventory lists one Internet Exchange Point in Mali, Mali IX (MLIX) in Bamako, among 14 in the ECOWAS member states](<https://sigtel.ecowas.int/regional-fiber-infrastructure/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://sigtel.ecowas.int/regional-fiber-infrastructure/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [Point d'Echange Internet du Mali (GIE MLIX)](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2017/mali-jo-2017-47.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
