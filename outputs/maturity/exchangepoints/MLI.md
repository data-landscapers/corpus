# Mali

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The SIGTEL regional inventory of the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) lists one Internet Exchange Point in Mali, Mali IX (MLIX) in Bamako, among 14 in the ECOWAS member states](<https://sigtel.ecowas.int/regional-fiber-infrastructure/>). These place an exchange point in service. The inventory gives no member count or traffic. A global content network serving from it is not stated.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://sigtel.ecowas.int/regional-fiber-infrastructure/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [Point d'Echange Internet du Mali (GIE MLIX)](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2017/mali-jo-2017-47.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
