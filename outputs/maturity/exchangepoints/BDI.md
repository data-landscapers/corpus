# Burundi

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Internet Society's IXP Tracker lists Akamai Technologies (AS20940, 1 Gbps, peers at the route server) and Meta (AS63293, 10 Gbps) as members of BDIXP in Bujumbura, from PeeringDB data of August 2026](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). [The Internet Society's IXP Tracker, from PeeringDB data of August 2026, lists BDIXP in Bujumbura with 9 member ASNs](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). It shows 26 Gbps of cumulative port capacity. No members joined or left in the previous 12 months. These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
