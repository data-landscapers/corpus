# Burundi

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Internet Society's IXP Tracker lists Akamai Technologies (AS20940, 1 Gbps, peers at the route server) and Meta (AS63293, 10 Gbps) as members of BDIXP in Bujumbura, from PeeringDB data of August 2026](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). [The Internet Society's IXP Tracker, from PeeringDB data of August 2026, lists BDIXP in Bujumbura with 9 member ASNs, 26 Gbps of cumulative port capacity and no members joining or leaving in the previous 12 months](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/706/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
