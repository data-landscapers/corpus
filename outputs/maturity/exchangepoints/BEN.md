# Benin

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB data last updated January 2026, lists BENIN-IX in Cotonou with 4 member networks (ISOCEL SA, SBIN, PCH AS3856, PCH AS42), 12 Gbps cumulative port capacity and 1 of 4 members peering at the route server](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/395/>). [The Internet Society says the technical community completed deployment of the Cotonou Internet Exchange (COTIX) at the end of 2024, launching with four members, with Internet Society training of local engineers in IXP operations and BGP](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/07/how-local-peering-is-strengthening-africas-internet/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.
