# Eswatini

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The African Union Commission, with Swaziland's Ministry of ICT and the Internet Society, launched the national Internet Exchange Point in Mbabane on 10 April 2014 under the AXIS project, the AU saying it donated equipment and services for it](<https://au.int/en/newsevents/20140407/launching-internet-exchange-point-swaziland>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://au.int/en/newsevents/20140407/launching-internet-exchange-point-swaziland>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
