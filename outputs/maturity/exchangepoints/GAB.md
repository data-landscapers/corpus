# Gabon

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [ISOC Pulse IXP Tracker, from PeeringDB data, lists 1 active IXP in Gabon with 8 combined members as of September 2026; it covers 1 of 1 population centres over 300,000 and 9 active networks](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/GA/>). [Traffic: over 250 Mbps (level, type not given) at GABIX on 2026-05, local traffic after Gabon Telecom and Airtel Gabon reconnected, per GabonReview](<https://www.gabonreview.com/souverainete-numerique-le-gabon-relance-gabix-son-point-dechange-internet-local/>). [GabonReview reports GABIX, the national IXP created in 2014, is relaunched: Gabon Telecom and Airtel Gabon recently reconnected, joining ANINF and CanalBox/GVA, the two that had kept activity since 2019](<https://www.gabonreview.com/souverainete-numerique-le-gabon-relance-gabix-son-point-dechange-internet-local/>). [The PCH IXP directory lists GABIX (Gabon Internet Exchange) in Libreville as an association-run exchange, status Active, established 2014, with IPv4 and IPv6 peering subnets](<https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/1887>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/GA/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
