# Mauritius

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [MIXP's own public route-server looking glass shows the Mauritius Internet Exchange Point operating, with Route Server 1 (IPv6) reporting 428 days of uptime, last reconfigured 2026-06-15, and about 26 member BGP sessions listed (Emtel, Vodacom, CMC Networks, Canal+ Telecom, PCH and others)](<https://portal.mixp.org/index.php/lg/rs1-mixp-ipv6>). [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB data of May 2026, lists the Mauritius Internet Exchange Point (MIXP) in Ebene with 19 members, 95 Gbps cumulative port capacity, and 15 of 19 members peering at the route server](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/516/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB data of May 2026, shows MIXP members registered abroad: 2 in the United States (PCH AS42 and AS3856), 2 in South Africa (CMC Networks, Vodacom) and 1 in Guadeloupe (Canal+ Telecom), against 14 in Mauritius](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/516/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
