# Mauritius

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [MIXP's own public route-server looking glass shows the Mauritius Internet Exchange Point operating](<https://portal.mixp.org/index.php/lg/rs1-mixp-ipv6>). Route Server 1 (IPv6) reports 428 days of uptime and was last reconfigured 2026-06-15. About 26 member BGP sessions are listed (Emtel, Vodacom, CMC Networks, Canal+ Telecom, PCH and others). [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB data of May 2026, lists the Mauritius Internet Exchange Point (MIXP) in Ebene with 19 members](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/516/>). It shows 95 Gbps cumulative port capacity, and 15 of 19 members peering at the route server. These place an exchange point in service. A global content network serving from it is not stated.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB data of May 2026, shows MIXP members registered abroad](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/516/>). There are 2 in the United States (PCH AS42 and AS3856), 2 in South Africa (CMC Networks, Vodacom) and 1 in Guadeloupe (Canal+ Telecom), against 14 in Mauritius. An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
