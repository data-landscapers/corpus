# Cote d'Ivoire

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [ARTCI says CIVIX, the national exchange point, has been in service since October 2013](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/peering-day-2026-la-rci-vise-des-ports-internet-de-100-gbps/>). [In August 2025 ARTCI said 14 operators and providers were connected](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). [In May 2024 ARTCI said the Netflix cache was hosted at the national exchange point](<https://www.fratel.org/documents/2024/05/8.-TR2-ARTCI.pdf>). [In June 2026 ARTCI said Akamai had joined CIVIX, and that Netflix and PCH connect there](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/peering-day-2026-la-rci-vise-des-ports-internet-de-100-gbps/>). [ARTCI gives average daily traffic of 20 Gbps for 2020 to 2024 and 36 Gbps for 2025 to 2026](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/peering-day-2026-la-rci-vise-des-ports-internet-de-100-gbps/>). [It gives 4 Mbps in 2013 and 10 Gbps in 2019](<https://fr.apanews.net/technologies/peering-day-2026-la-rci-vise-des-ports-internet-de-100-gbps/>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In August 2025 ARTCI announced a third point of presence for CIVIX](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). [It is in Raxio's neutral data centre at Grand-Bassam, outside Abidjan](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). The source gives no launch date and no members at that site. Grand-Bassam is a resort beside Abidjan, so the exchange is not yet shown in a second city, and no operators from other countries are named.
