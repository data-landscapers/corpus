# Cote d'Ivoire

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Netflix and Akamai are among the international content players that CIVIX federates alongside operators and ISPs, per the article on PeeringDay 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642599/economie/peeringday-2026appairage-et-interconnexion-lartci-mobilise-lecosysteme-numerique-autour-dun-internet-performant>). [Raxio says its Cote d'Ivoire data centre at Grand Bassam hosts carriers, cloud providers and Internet Exchange Points present in the facility](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/cote-divoire/>). [Traffic: 36 Gbps (average daily) at CIVIX on 2026-06-15, as stated at the ARTCI PeeringDay 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642599/economie/peeringday-2026appairage-et-interconnexion-lartci-mobilise-lecosysteme-numerique-autour-dun-internet-performant>). [Traffic: 20 Gbps (average, per day) at CIVIX on 2025-08-05, as stated by ARTCI at the first Ivorian peering forum](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage. [In May 2024 the regulator ARTCI said the Netflix cache was hosted at the national exchange and that other content providers' caches were being moved there](<https://www.fratel.org/documents/2024/05/8.-TR2-ARTCI.pdf>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [ARTCI announced recently the official launch of a third point of presence for CIVIX with Raxio in the neutral data centre at Grand-Bassam, a city other than Abidjan](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
