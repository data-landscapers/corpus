# Cote d'Ivoire

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [CIVIX federates operators, ISPs and international content players such as Netflix and Akamai, Fratmat reported at PeeringDay 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642599/economie/peeringday-2026appairage-et-interconnexion-lartci-mobilise-lecosysteme-numerique-autour-dun-internet-performant>). [In May 2024 the regulator ARTCI said the Netflix cache was hosted at the national exchange and other caches were moving there](<https://www.fratel.org/documents/2024/05/8.-TR2-ARTCI.pdf>). [ARTCI gave CIVIX's average daily traffic as 20 Gbps in August 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). [It gave 36 Gbps in June 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642599/economie/peeringday-2026appairage-et-interconnexion-lartci-mobilise-lecosysteme-numerique-autour-dun-internet-performant>). [Raxio says exchange points are present in its Grand Bassam data centre](<https://www.raxiogroup.com/data-centres/cote-divoire/>). [In November 2023 MainOne said customers at its Grand Bassam data centre can reach internet exchanges, among them an AMS-IX one hosted there](<https://mainone.africa-newsroom.com/press/mainone-an-equinix-company-expands-data-center-footprint-in-cote-divoire?lang=en>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [ARTCI has launched a third CIVIX point of presence, with Raxio in the neutral data centre at Grand-Bassam, outside Abidjan](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635918/economie/forum-sur-le-peering-en-cote-divoire-lartci-impulse-une-dynamique-nouvelle-pour-linterconnexion-locale>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. Networks from other countries peering there also count. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not.
