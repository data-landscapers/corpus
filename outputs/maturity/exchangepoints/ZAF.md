# South Africa

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [In November 2025 Teraco said 680 networks peer at NAPAfrica](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>). [It named Akamai, Amazon, Cloudflare, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Netflix and TikTok among them](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>). [Teraco gives NAPAfrica's traffic peak as over 4 Tbps in 2023 and 5 Tbps in February 2025](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-internet-exchange-achieves-5tbps-traffic-milestone-driving-africas-digital-transformation/>). [In November 2025 it said NAPAfrica had reached 6 Tbps](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>). [In August 2026 INX-ZA's member export showed Microsoft, Cloudflare, Akamai, Meta and Fastly at CINX in Cape Town](<https://tech.africa/cinx-oadc-cape-town-expansion/>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [Teraco says NAPAfrica runs exchange points in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>). [In November 2025 it said the members came from over 50 countries](<https://www.teraco.co.za/news/napafrica-and-teraco-acx-growing-africas-digital-community-as-napafrica-hits-6tbps/>). [INX-ZA runs JINX in Johannesburg and CINX in Cape Town, with 208 and 122 networks in August 2026](<https://tech.africa/cinx-oadc-cape-town-expansion/>). [WACREN, the West and Central African research network, said in October 2025 that it peers at the ZAOXI exchange point in Cape Town](<https://wacren.net/en/newsletter/africas-research-and-education-networks-achieve-historic-lagos-cape-town-interconnection/>).
