# Ethiopia

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Internet Society Pulse, from self-reported PeeringDB data last updated January 2026, lists ADDIX in Addis Ababa with 3 members (AddIX Technology Solutions, Bank of Abyssinia, WebSprix), 21 Gbps cumulative port capacity, and all 3 peering at the route server](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/1339/>). [Wingu Africa states that ADDIX, Ethiopia's first IXP, launched over a year before 4 September 2025 and is operated by ADDIX Technology Solutions under the Ethiopian Communications Authority, hosted at Wingu's data centre in the ICT Park, keeping local traffic in Ethiopia (as_of is the article date, which gives no launch date)](<https://www.wingu.africa/latest-news/transforming-connectivity-ethiopias-first-internet-exchange-hosted-at-wingu-africa>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.
