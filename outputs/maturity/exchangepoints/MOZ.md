# Mozambique

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [MOZIX, the Mozambique Internet Exchange in Maputo, says it was created in May 2002 and now has 18 peers](<https://mozix.org.mz/>). It is run by CIUEM, the computing centre of Eduardo Mondlane University. [In May 2025 CIUEM's director called MozIX the only internet exchange point in the country](<https://www.ciuem.mz/ciuem-destaca-papel-da-internet-como-espaco-publico-e-inclusivo-no-forum-de-governacao-da-internet-em-mocambique/>). [The same month he put its peak traffic at over 150 Gbps, with 19 peering members](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/>). [In August 2026 the communications minister called MozIX the national traffic exchange point](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/03/trends/inovacao/uem-inaugura-centro-de-dados-para-reforcar-transformacao-digital-e-investigacao-cientifica/>). No global content network has been found peering there.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In May 2025 MOZIX opened a new point of presence at Digital Realty's MPM1 data centre](<https://capacityglobal.com/news/mozambique-internet-exchange-launches-new-pop-at-digital-realtys-maputo-data-centre/>). That data centre is also in Maputo. No exchange point in a second city, and no network from another country peering at MOZIX, has been found.
