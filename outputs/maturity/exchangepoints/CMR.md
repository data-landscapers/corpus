# Cameroon

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB, lists Akamai as a member of CAMIX Douala with a 4 Gbps port](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/704/>). [In August 2026 it listed 10 member networks and 40 Gbps of port capacity, nine of them at the route server](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/704/>). [In July 2025 Camtel staff said the CAMIX exchange brings several players together but is still embryonic](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-camtel-projette-de-creer-9-datacenters-decentralises-pour-les-prochaines-annees/>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [CAMIX lists points of presence in Yaounde, at the CAMPOST data centre, and in Douala, at CAMTEL Bepanda and ST-Digital](<https://www.camix.cm/>). [It says the Douala and Yaounde sites were both working before the third, at ST-DIGITAL, opened](<https://www.camix.cm/actualite/3e-pop-camix>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. Networks from other countries peering there also count. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not.
