# Cameroon

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Akamai Technologies (AS20940, content network) is listed as a member of CAMIX Douala with a 4 Gbps port, peering at the route server, per PeeringDB via Internet Society Pulse](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/704/>). [Internet Society Pulse, from PeeringDB, lists CAMIX Douala in Cameroon with 10 member ASNs and 40 Gbps cumulative port capacity, 9 of 10 peering at the route server (data as of August 2026)](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/ixp/704/>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [CAMIX lists points of presence in Yaounde (CAMPOST data centre) and in Douala (CAMTEL Bepanda and ST-Digital), per its own homepage](<https://www.camix.cm/>). [CAMIX says its interconnection infrastructure already had two operational sites, a Douala PoP at CAMTEL Bepanda and a Yaounde PoP at the CAMPOST data centre, before the third PoP at ST-DIGITAL](<https://www.camix.cm/actualite/3e-pop-camix>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
