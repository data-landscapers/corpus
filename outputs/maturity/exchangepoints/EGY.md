# Egypt

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Global content, CDN and cloud networks are members at EG-IX: Meta (AS32934), Amazon (AS16509), Microsoft (AS8075), CDN77/DataPacket, ACE CDN, Medianova, Zenlayer, G-Core Labs, Kaopu Cloud and Huawei Cloud, per bgp.tools data cited by tech.africa](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). [Traffic: 722.19 Gb/s (peak over the preceding day) at EG-IX on 2026-09-07, with 528.80 Gb/s flowing at the time and average traffic of about 443 Gb/s in each direction, per the exchange's live counter as cited by tech.africa](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [Two Jordanian carriers, Jordan Telecommunications (AS8697) and Al Bahrainia al Urdunia Liltaknia (AS9038), are members at EG-IX in Cairo, alongside global content networks and ICANN's L-root](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
