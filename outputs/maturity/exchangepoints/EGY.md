# Egypt

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [AMS-IX says EG-IX opened in Cairo with Telecom Egypt in April 2022](<https://www.ams-ix.net/ams/news/eg-ix-strengthening-regional-connectivity-from-cairo>). [It says 19 networks were connected by the third quarter of 2026](<https://www.ams-ix.net/ams/news/eg-ix-strengthening-regional-connectivity-from-cairo>). [In September 2026 Tech Africa listed Meta, Amazon and Microsoft among the members](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). [It said the exchange runs at Telecom Egypt's Regional Data Hub at Smart Village, on AMS-IX's platform](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). [AMS-IX gives a peak of 89 Gb/s in 2023 and 338 Gb/s in 2025](<https://www.ams-ix.net/ams/news/eg-ix-strengthening-regional-connectivity-from-cairo>). [It gives 945 Gb/s by the third quarter of 2026](<https://www.ams-ix.net/ams/news/eg-ix-strengthening-regional-connectivity-from-cairo>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In September 2026 Tech Africa said two Jordanian carriers were members at EG-IX in Cairo](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). [They are Jordan Telecommunications and Al Bahrainia al Urdunia Liltaknia](<https://tech.africa/eg-ix-cairo-18-members/>). No source found names a second exchange in another Egyptian city.
