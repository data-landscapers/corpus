# Cape Verde

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Cabo Verde government's own status booklet states that it has introduced the IXP (Internet Exchange Point), with the effect of lowering costs and latency and improving internet quality](<https://www.governo.cv/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/economiadigital.pdf>). [PeeringDB lists CV-IXP in Praia with 5 peers (Banco Caboverdiano de Negocios, CV Telecom, NOSi EPE, UNITEL T and the CV-IXP route servers), 6 connections and 23G total capacity; the document is dated 2024 only to the year](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4037>). [PCH's Internet Exchange Directory lists CV-IXP (Associação Cabo Verde Internet Exchange Point) in Praia as an Active Ethernet exchange established 29 November 2022](<https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/2324>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.governo.cv/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/economiadigital.pdf>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [DE-CIX Lisbon-Sao Paulo corridor (planned)](<https://www.de-cix.net/en/about-de-cix/media/press-releases/de-cix-and-cabo-verde-telecom-plan-new-south-atlantic-interconnection-corridor>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
