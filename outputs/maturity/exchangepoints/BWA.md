# Botswana

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [BOCRA's newsletter says BINX supports local Internet infrastructure through its Google Cache Cluster and a Packet Clearing House node](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). [ITWeb reports BoFiNet's launch of the Digital Delta Internet Exchange Point, where ISPs, mobile operators, content and cloud providers are to exchange local traffic directly](<https://itweb.africa/article/bofinet-launches-digital-delta-ixp/8OKdWMDXL9VMbznQ>). [PeeringDB lists Digital Delta IX in Gaborone, operated from a BoFiNet facility, with 10G capacity and one peer (BoFiNet's own ASN 37678), so no second network is yet shown exchanging traffic; record last updated 2026-04-21](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5002>). [Mmegi reports BoFiNet relocated the Gaborone IXP to a more secure and resilient environment as part of a P100m gateway upgrade](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bofinet-invests-p100m-in-upgrading-botswanas-internet-gateway/news>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/bofinet-launches-digital-delta-ixp/8OKdWMDXL9VMbznQ>); [SADC regional internet exchange](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>).
