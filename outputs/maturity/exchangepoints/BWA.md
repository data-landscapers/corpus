# Botswana

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [In October 2025 the regulator BOCRA said the Botswana Internet Exchange, BINX, hosts a Google Cache Cluster](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). [BOCRA described BINX as still central to local internet infrastructure](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>). [In April 2025 BoFiNet moved the Gaborone exchange point to a more secure site](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/bofinet-invests-p100m-in-upgrading-botswanas-internet-gateway/news>), as part of a P100m gateway upgrade. [BoFiNet's new Digital Delta IX in Gaborone had one peer, BoFiNet itself, in April 2026](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5002>). [BoFiNet launched it for operators, content and cloud providers in October 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/bofinet-launches-digital-delta-ixp/8OKdWMDXL9VMbznQ>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Both exchanges named are in Gaborone: [BINX](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>) and [Digital Delta IX](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5002>). [BINX also hosts a Packet Clearing House node](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA_Newsletter%2C_Issue_003_compressed.pdf>), which serves name servers at most exchanges. [In 2025 Botswana bid to host a SADC regional internet exchange](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>).

***Also on file***. [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/bofinet-launches-digital-delta-ixp/8OKdWMDXL9VMbznQ>); [SADC regional internet exchange](<https://itweb.africa/article/botswana-seeks-to-host-sadc-internet-exchange/j5alr7QALmK7pYQk>).
