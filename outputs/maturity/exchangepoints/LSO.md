# Lesotho

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [In May 2026 PeeringDB listed the Lesotho Internet Exchange Point, LIXP, in Maseru with three peers](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>). [Econet Telecom Lesotho is one of them, and all three peer openly](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>). [LIXP was relaunched after a revamp in February 2026](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>). [The Lesotho Communications Authority, which hosts it, says it serves five ISPs and two content delivery networks](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>). [The exchange was founded in 2011, stopped, and resumed in 2016](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>). [It is run by the Internet Exchange Point Association of Lesotho, and its record was last updated on 13 May 2026](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [LIXP is hosted at the Lesotho Communications Authority in Maseru](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>). [Its PeeringDB record gives 30G of declared capacity across three connections](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>). [After the revamp the authority counted seven networks there in all](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>).
