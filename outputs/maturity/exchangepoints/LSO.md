# Lesotho

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [PeeringDB's record, last updated 2026-05-13, lists the Lesotho Internet Exchange Point (LIXP) in Maseru, run by the Internet Exchange Point Association of Lesotho, with 3 peers, 3 connections, all open-policy, and 30G total declared capacity; Econet Telecom Lesotho is one of the peers](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>). [The Lesotho Internet Exchange Point (LIXP), hosted at the Lesotho Communications Authority in Maseru, was officially relaunched after a revamp reported on 2026-02-02, with the LCA saying it hosts five ISPs and two content delivery networks (unnamed); the exchange was founded in 2011, stopped, and resumed in 2016](<https://www.thereporter.co.ls/2026/02/02/lesotho-revamps-internet-exchange-infrastructure/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/5015>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
