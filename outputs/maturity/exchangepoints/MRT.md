# Mauritania

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Ministry of Digital Transformation officially launched the national internet exchange centre (IXP) at International Mauritanian Telecommunications (IMT) on 2025-05-28](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/>). The document gives no city or member count. The head of the Digital Infrastructure Foundation gave a technical presentation of its operation, per Sahara Medias reporting the ministry. [The WARCIP-Mauritania feasibility study (consultants Marpij, Neo Telecoms, Eric Veve; final version, June 2014) states that Mauritania has no exchange point yet](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport_final-IXP.pdf>). Operators' traffic to each other is transit, not local peering. Dakar in effect acts as the IXP for Mauritanian operators, with Mauritel and Chinguitel traffic exchanged there. The study proposes creating one. These place an exchange point in service. A global content network serving from it is not stated. [In May 2025 the state news agency said the new Nouakchott Data Center provides an exchange point linking local and international operators](<https://ami.mr/en/archives/24220>). [The ministry called the exchange recently launched in August 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276833>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [Mauritania IXP, proposed for the IMT ACE landing station in Nouakchott and later the Nouakchott datacenter building](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport_final-IXP.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
