# Mauritania

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Ministry of Digital Transformation officially launched the national internet exchange centre (IXP) at International Mauritanian Telecommunications (IMT) on 2025-05-28 (document gives no city or member count), with a technical presentation of its operation by the head of the Digital Infrastructure Foundation, per Sahara Medias reporting the ministry](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/>). [The WARCIP-Mauritania feasibility study (consultants Marpij, Neo Telecoms, Eric Veve; final version, June 2014) states that Mauritania has no exchange point yet: operators' traffic to each other is transit, not local peering, and Dakar in effect acts as the IXP for Mauritanian operators, Mauritel and Chinguitel traffic being exchanged there; the study proposes creating one](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport_final-IXP.pdf>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage. [In May 2025 the state news agency said the new Nouakchott Data Center provides an exchange point linking local and international operators](<https://ami.mr/en/archives/24220>); [the ministry called the exchange recently launched in August 2025](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/276833>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://fr.saharamedias.net/mauritanie-lancement-officiel-du-centre-national-dechange-internet/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [Mauritania IXP (proposed, single site at the IMT ACE landing station in Nouakchott, second site later at the Nouakchott datacenter building)](<https://mtnima.gov.mr/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rapport_final-IXP.pdf>).
