# Comoros

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Comores Câbles states on its own undated corporate page that an IXP has been installed at Moroni](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>). It is there to enable local traffic exchange between Comores Telecom and Yas Comores. The page is dated here by its published year 2026, as it gives no date. These place an exchange point in service. A global content network serving from it is not stated.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.comorescables.km/Technologies>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [IXP (planned)](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/comores-cables_succ%C3%A8s-collaboration-connectivit%C3%A9-activity-7370795347146866689-QwDU>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
