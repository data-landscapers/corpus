# Chad

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [In March 2024 the Internet Society said Chad had one exchange point, run by ADETIC, with at least nine networks peering there](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/03/chads-recent-outages-highlight-resiliency-gaps/>). [In August 2024 it said the exchange connected a third of Chad's networks](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/08/another-outage-shows-challenges-facing-chads-internet-infrastructure/>). [It said no content delivery network had caches in Chad, so most traffic left the country through one transit provider](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/08/another-outage-shows-challenges-facing-chads-internet-infrastructure/>). [In May 2026 Digital Business Africa said TCHADIX, run by ADETIC as an economic interest group, had operated for a year](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-ladetic-celebre-un-an-du-point-dechange-internet-tchadix-bilan-et-perspectives/>). [Internet access providers and mobile operators attended its members' general assembly, opened on 20 May 2026](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-ladetic-celebre-un-an-du-point-dechange-internet-tchadix-bilan-et-perspectives/>). [Russell Southwood reported in November 2026 that DJAMIX, the N'Djamena exchange, had nine members](<https://russellsouthwood.substack.com/p/chad-living-in-a-landlocked-dangerous>), down from 11 in 2024. About half are internet providers, and no local traffic figure is published.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In August 2024 the Internet Society counted one active exchange in Chad](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/08/another-outage-shows-challenges-facing-chads-internet-infrastructure/>). Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [TCHADIX](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-ladetic-celebre-un-an-du-point-dechange-internet-tchadix-bilan-et-perspectives/>).
