# Chad

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Digital Business Africa reports TCHADIX, Chad's internet exchange point run by ADETIC as an economic interest group (GIE), has been operational for a year, with internet access providers and mobile operators attending its members' general assembly opened on 20 May 2026](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-ladetic-celebre-un-an-du-point-dechange-internet-tchadix-bilan-et-perspectives/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/tchad-ladetic-celebre-un-an-du-point-dechange-internet-tchadix-bilan-et-perspectives/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
