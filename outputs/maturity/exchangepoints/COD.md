# DR Congo

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [ISPA-DRC built the Kinshasa Internet Exchange (KINIX) in November 2012](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2024/02/the-decade-that-changed-the-democratic-republic-of-congos-internet/>). [In August 2026 Tech Africa said 23 networks took part at KINIX, including all four major mobile operators](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>). [It said Meta and Cloudflare both peer there](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>). [ISPA-DRC showed Google and Facebook caches connected at KINIX in August 2024](<https://www.afpif.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/RDC-Cache-Fill-Strategies.pdf>). [ISPA-DRC gave KINIX a traffic peak of 84 Gbps in August 2024](<https://www.afpif.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/RDC-Cache-Fill-Strategies.pdf>). [In August 2026 KINIX said it exchanged more than 1 terabit per second](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>). [ACIX, run by DE-CIX, has operated in Kinshasa since 2023](<https://itweb.africa/article/distributed-exchange-boosts-drc-connectivity/4r1ly7R9GkLvpmda>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [The Internet Society says ISPA-DRC opened a second exchange in Lubumbashi (LUBIX) in 2019](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2024/02/the-decade-that-changed-the-democratic-republic-of-congos-internet/>). [It says a third, in Goma (GOMIX), has operated since 2021](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2024/02/the-decade-that-changed-the-democratic-republic-of-congos-internet/>). [In August 2026 Tech Africa said networks registered outside the country peer at KINIX, including GVA of Cote d'Ivoire](<https://tech.africa/kinix-raxio-kinshasa-exchange/>).
