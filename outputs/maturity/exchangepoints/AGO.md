# Angola

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Internet Society Pulse IXP Tracker, drawing on PeeringDB self-reported data, lists the Angola Internet Exchange Point (angonix) in Luanda as active with 23 members, at one physical location, as of June 2026; Pulse says 86% of Angola's 63 active networks are members or customers of members of an IXP](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
