# Angola

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [In June 2026 the Internet Society's IXP Tracker listed the Angola Internet Exchange Point (Angonix) in Luanda as active with 23 members](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). [It gave one physical location for the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). [Pulse said 86% of Angola's 63 active networks were members of an exchange, or customers of members](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). [In late July 2025 the analyst Darwin Costa told Portal de TI that Angonix peaked at 59.5 Gbps](<https://pti.ao/trafego-de-internet-em-angola-atinge-maximos-historicos/>). [He said Angola IXP, the second exchange, peaked above 11 Gbps](<https://pti.ao/trafego-de-internet-em-angola-atinge-maximos-historicos/>). [He said Microsoft and Meta were present at these exchanges](<https://pti.ao/trafego-de-internet-em-angola-atinge-maximos-historicos/>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In November 2025 the same analyst said Angola's two active exchanges, Angonix and Angola-IXP, were both in Luanda](<https://pti.ao/internet-em-angola-especialista-defende-descentralizacao-da-internet-em-angola/>). [In June 2026 the IXP Tracker gave Angonix one physical location, in Luanda](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/AO/>). No source found names members from other countries.
