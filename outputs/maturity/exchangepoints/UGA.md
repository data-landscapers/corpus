# Uganda

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [UIXP deployed a Netflix cache in 2024, reachable by connected networks through its route servers, with cache-fill donated by Lyca Mobile; Netflix also connected to UIXP as a new peer](<https://blog.uixp.co.ug/2025/01/endowment-fund-vas-and-other-updates.html>). [Internet Society Pulse record says the UIXP was exempted from the January 2026 election shutdown order as critical infrastructure and recorded a traffic drop from 13 January, so it was operating (document date is the retrieval date)](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/shutdowns/internet-access-suspended-during-elections/>). [UIXP says it began and ended 2024 with 32 connected networks, including six at the Raxio carrier-neutral data centre, with its switching infrastructure operating reliably](<https://blog.uixp.co.ug/2025/01/endowment-fund-vas-and-other-updates.html>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://blog.uixp.co.ug/2025/01/endowment-fund-vas-and-other-updates.html>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
