# South Sudan

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Internet Society Pulse's Internet Resilience Index (PCH and PeeringDB data, July 2025) scores South Sudan 0/100 on IXP coverage of population centres](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/resilience/ss/>). It also scores 0/100 on peering efficiency, the share of networks peering at an IXP, so no operating exchange point is shown. A dated statement that no exchange point operates in the country sets the lowest stage. Nothing dated later names one in service. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them. Only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** With [no exchange point in service](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/resilience/ss/>), none reaches beyond one city. An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
