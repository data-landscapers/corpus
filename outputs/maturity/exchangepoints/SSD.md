# South Sudan

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Internet Society Pulse's Internet Resilience Index (PCH and PeeringDB data, July 2025) scores South Sudan 0/100 on IXP coverage of population centres and 0/100 on peering efficiency, the share of networks peering at an IXP, so no operating exchange point is shown](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/resilience/ss/>). A dated statement that no exchange point operates in the country sets the lowest stage, and nothing dated later names one in service. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** With [no exchange point in service](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/resilience/ss/>), none reaches beyond one city. An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
