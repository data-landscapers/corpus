# Morocco

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Casablanca Internet Exchange Point (CAS-IX) in Casablanca has 3 members listed in PeeringDB and 30 Gbps aggregate capacity, per Internet Society Pulse's IXP Tracker (data as of May 2026)](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/fr/ixp-tracker/ixp/760/>). [FEZIX in Fez has 3 Moroccan members (Corebach Backbone, HOSTOWEB, PIXI MEDIA), 2 of 3 peering at the route server, and 210 Gbps aggregate capacity, per Internet Society Pulse's IXP Tracker (PeeringDB data, January 2026)](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/fr/ixp-tracker/ixp/849/>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/fr/ixp-tracker/ixp/760/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
