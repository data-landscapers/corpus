# Eritrea

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Internet Society's Pulse IXP Tracker states there are no active internet exchange points in Eritrea and none listed in PeeringDB with three or more member ASNs](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/ER/>). Nothing dated later names one in service.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** With [no exchange point in service](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/ixp-tracker/country/ER/>), none reaches beyond one city. An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Nothing further is asked of this indicator.
