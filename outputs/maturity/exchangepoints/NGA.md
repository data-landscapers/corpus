# Nigeria

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [IXPN, founded in 2006, says it serves more than 130 networks from several Lagos data centres](<https://techtvnetwork.ng/post/ixpn-at-20-how-nigerias-internet-exchange-point-has-transformed-local-internet-connectivity>), as of October 2026. [Google, Meta, Microsoft and TikTok are among its members, per IT Pulse Nigeria in February 2026](<https://itpulse.com.ng/how-local-peering-grew-ixpns-traffic-from-4-gbps-in-2015-to-1-7-tbps-in-2025/>). [Its aggregate peak traffic rose from 4 Gbps in 2015 to 1.7 Tbps in 2025](<https://itpulse.com.ng/how-local-peering-grew-ixpns-traffic-from-4-gbps-in-2015-to-1-7-tbps-in-2025/>). [IXPN announced a record peak of 1 Tbps in April 2025](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/04/24/ixpn-hits-1tbps-traffic-milestone-boosting-nigerias-digital-growth/>).

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In October 2026 IXPN reported regional connectivity in Abuja and Enugu, beside its points of presence in Lagos](<https://techtvnetwork.ng/post/ixpn-at-20-how-nigerias-internet-exchange-point-has-transformed-local-internet-connectivity>). [Digital Realty added an IXPN point of presence at its Ibeju-Lekki data centre in Lagos in April 2026](<https://techtvnetwork.ng/post/ixpn-at-20-how-nigerias-internet-exchange-point-has-transformed-local-internet-connectivity>). [Starlink's Lagos gateway, at Equinix's Lekki data centre, peers at IXPN](<https://technomag.co.zw/starlinks-african-expansion-availability-costs-and-performance/>), per TechnoMag in June 2026. [MainOne offers peering from Lagos to exchanges in Amsterdam, Ghana and London](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/stations/africa/nigeria>).
