# Nigeria

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Global content networks Google, Meta, Microsoft and TikTok are among the current participants (members) at IXPN, per IT Pulse Nigeria](<https://itpulse.com.ng/how-local-peering-grew-ixpns-traffic-from-4-gbps-in-2015-to-1-7-tbps-in-2025/>). [IXPN aggregate peak traffic grew from 4 Gbps in 2015 to 1.7 Tbps in 2025, a 42,400% increase over ten years, per IT Pulse Nigeria](<https://itpulse.com.ng/how-local-peering-grew-ixpns-traffic-from-4-gbps-in-2015-to-1-7-tbps-in-2025/>). [IXPN is hosted at the MDXi Lekki data centre in Lagos, with direct peering available through MainOne to Amsterdam, Ghana and London exchanges, per Submarine Networks](<https://www.submarinenetworks.com/en/stations/africa/nigeria>). [Traffic: 1.7 Tbps (aggregate peak) at IXPN in 2025, per IT Pulse Nigeria](<https://itpulse.com.ng/how-local-peering-grew-ixpns-traffic-from-4-gbps-in-2015-to-1-7-tbps-in-2025/>). Together these place an exchange point in service, a global content network serving from it and its traffic shown growing, which answers the first three of the study's four questions. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [IXPN has regional connectivity in Abuja and Enugu as well as multiple points of presence in Lagos, per TechTV Network; Digital Realty announced a new IXPN point of presence at its Ibeju-Lekki data centre in April 2026 (Lagos)](<https://techtvnetwork.ng/post/ixpn-at-20-how-nigerias-internet-exchange-point-has-transformed-local-internet-connectivity>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
