# Liberia

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [LTA Commissioner Angela Cassell Bush said Liberia's IXP, hosted by the Cable Consortium of Liberia (CCL) in Monrovia area, remains functional, though governance problems and limited ISP participation persist and many ISPs are not yet peering there](<https://lta.gov.lr/lta-highlights-need-to-strengthen-liberias-internet-exchange-point-atnational-peering-forum/>). [The African Union Commission and Liberia's Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications officially launched the Liberian Internet Exchange Point (LIXP) in Monrovia on 6 August 2015, with equipment and technical support from the AXIS project](<https://au.int/en/newsevents/20150802/setting-and-launching-internet-exchange-point-liberia>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.
