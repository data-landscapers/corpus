# Sierra Leone

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The operator's own site says a Microsoft Connected Cache node caches Microsoft content locally at the exchange, and Quad9 DNS is hosted on-net behind Michcom AS209049](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>). [PeeringDB's record for Michcom-IX, an open-access carrier-neutral exchange in Freetown run by Michcom Limited, lists 5 peers (Afcom SL, AI Networks, Fiber One SL, Michcom, route servers)](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4892>). It lists 6 connections and 60G total capacity. Points of presence are at Zoodlabs CLS MMR in Freetown and Leonecom MMR in Jui. The record is operator-maintained and was last updated 2026-06-03. These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it. That answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them. Only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [The operator's own site says operators across Sierra Leone, Guinea and Liberia are exchanging traffic at Michcom-IX, with live points of presence in Conakry (Guilab) and Monrovia (CCL)](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city. It also does so where networks from other countries peer there. The anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
