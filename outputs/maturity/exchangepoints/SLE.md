# Sierra Leone

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The operator's own site says a Microsoft Connected Cache node caches Microsoft content locally at the exchange, and Quad9 DNS is hosted on-net behind Michcom AS209049](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>). [PeeringDB's record for Michcom-IX, an open-access carrier-neutral exchange in Freetown run by Michcom Limited, lists 5 peers (Afcom SL, AI Networks, Fiber One SL, Michcom, route servers), 6 connections and 60G total capacity, with points of presence at Zoodlabs CLS MMR in Freetown and Leonecom MMR in Jui; operator-maintained record last updated 2026-06-03](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/4892>). These place an exchange point in service with a global content network serving from it, which answers the first two of the study's four questions. Traffic shown growing over a year is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [The operator's own site says operators across Sierra Leone, Guinea and Liberia are exchanging traffic at Michcom-IX, with live points of presence in Conakry (Guilab) and Monrovia (CCL)](<https://www.michcom-ix.net/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
