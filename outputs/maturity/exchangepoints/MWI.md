# Malawi

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [MISPA says the Malawi Internet Exchange (MIX) in Blantyre began operating on 4 December 2008](<http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html>). [In April 2024 MISPA's chair said the ISP association owns and runs the MIX, with all ISPs connected and peering](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2024/0408/Documents/Paulos%20B%20Nyirenda.pdf>). [He said the MIX provides content caches from Google, Facebook and Akamai](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2024/0408/Documents/Paulos%20B%20Nyirenda.pdf>). [MISPA's table of 14 November 2024 shows 11 networks peering at the MIX](<http://www.mispa.org.mw/mix.html>). No source found gives its traffic at two dates.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** [In September 2023 a MACRA deck said Malawi had one exchange, in Blantyre](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2023/1004/Documents/Daud%20Elvin%20Suleman.pdf>). [In July 2026 the Internet Society said the Lilongwe Internet Exchange (LLIX) had launched with five peers](<https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/07/how-local-peering-is-strengthening-africas-internet/>). [In February 2026 the Internet Society Foundation gave a grant to upgrade the Lilongwe Open Neutral Internet Exchange (LIONEX)](<https://www.isocfoundation.org/2026/02/introducing-the-2025-sustainable-peering-infrastructure-grantee/>).
