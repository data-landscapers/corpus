# Somalia

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [Packet Clearing House's directory lists the Somali Internet Exchange Point (SoIXP) in Mogadishu, run by the National Communications Authority Somalia and established 20 November 2018, with status Active](<https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/2072>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.pch.net/ixp/details/2072>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.

***Also on file***. [SoIXP (MogIX)](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/2410>); [Somcable Submarine Network (2Africa and PEACE landings, Berbera)](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-09-14-kenyas-digital-future-takes-shape-as-data-giants-converge-in-nairobi>).

***Not found***. No source found says a global content network has a cache at the exchange or peers there.
