# Zimbabwe

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Harare Internet Exchange Point (HIX), housed at the TelOne Exchange in Harare and set up with a USD 200,000 African Union grant, is operating, and POTRAZ says all internet access providers are connected to it](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf>). These place an exchange point in service, which answers the first of the study's four questions. A global content network serving from it is not stated. Member counts and traffic are given where a source states them; only traffic shown growing sets a stage.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/Africa/Documents/2020/contributions/RI2_POTRAZ-Zimbabwe-IXP_RDFAFR2020.pdf>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
