# Zambia

## Exchange points keeping traffic local

**In service, with a content network, and growing.** [The Lusaka IXP calls itself Zambia's neutral, non-profit exchange, with more than 14 networks connected](<https://lusakaixp.co.zm/>). [In August 2026 it gave its peak traffic as 2.8 Tbps](<https://lusakaixp.co.zm/>). [In August 2024 PeeringDB listed 12 peers there, with 76 Gbps of capacity](<https://www.peeringdb.com/ix/615>). [In December 2025 AFRINIC said its name servers at the exchange had gone live](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/afrinic_great-news-from-lusaka-our-deployment-at-activity-7403752672203509763-lW7D>). [They cut query response times from 23 ms to 1.5 ms](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/afrinic_great-news-from-lusaka-our-deployment-at-activity-7403752672203509763-lW7D>). [The African IXP Association lists two exchanges in Lusaka: the Zambia Internet Exchange Point (ZIXP), opened in 2006, and the Lusaka IXP, opened in 2022](<https://af-ix.org/ixps-list>). No source found says a global content network serves from either.

**A second city or networks from abroad.** Nothing found names a second city or networks from other countries at [the exchange](<https://lusakaixp.co.zm/>). An exchange reaches beyond one city where a second exchange or point of presence is in service in another city, or where networks from other countries peer there; the anycast DNS operators found at almost every exchange do not count.
