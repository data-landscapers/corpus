# Cabo Verde

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** NOSi, a public enterprise, [supplies the municipal councils with what it calls Local Digital Governance services, under a contract signed with each council](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/nosi-garante-total-flexibilidade-negocial-junto-das-camaras-municipais-para-evitar-disrupcao-dos-servicos-digitais/>), on its communiqué of 6 October 2025. The councils pay it current invoices under those agreements. Its plan for 2019-2022 [budgeted ECV 25,000,000 for the state's private network](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/1141867/nosi-pa-global-estrategico-2019-2022.pdf/c3749473-6a40-8f4f-7b9d-ca2d353b0e8a>).

**What the office does over it.** The social development ministry, on a page published in September 2018, says [registration in the Cadastro Social Único, a national system, is done locally by the municipal councils](<https://mfids.gov.cv/v2/index.php/cadastro-social-unico>). Until 17 December 2025 [the registry's declaration was issued and printed at the councils](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/12/17/csu-passa-a-permitir-consulta-directa-de-dados-e-elimina-declaracoes-em-papel/100473>). Councils on Sal, Boa Vista, Maio and rural São Vicente [use the shared land platform SIGTP](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/20126/0/TDR+TA+LMITS_ingfinal.pdf/03dfd947-4990-a9f8-5927-71919b6352bd>), on Ministry of Finance terms of reference published in March 2022.

**How the office is connected.** NOSi's strategic plan of March 2020 [lists the municipal councils' local area networks among those served by basic access to the state's private network](<https://nosi.cv/documents/20121/1141867/nosi-pa-global-estrategico-2019-2022.pdf/c3749473-6a40-8f4f-7b9d-ca2d353b0e8a>), which it calls the Rede Tecnológica Privativa do Estado.

**Last twelve months.** By 6 October 2025 [NOSi's services to the Mosteiros council were not in regular supply, over unpaid invoices](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/nosi-garante-total-flexibilidade-negocial-junto-das-camaras-municipais-para-evitar-disrupcao-dos-servicos-digitais/>). The association of municipalities said it was indignant at the suspension of services, and NOSi reported a very low payment rate across the councils.

**Local governments connected.** NOSi says [21 of the 22 municipalities, all but Mosteiros, were receiving its services regularly on 6 October 2025](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/nosi-garante-total-flexibilidade-negocial-junto-das-camaras-municipais-para-evitar-disrupcao-dos-servicos-digitais/>).
