# Central African Republic

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The universal service fund (Fonds du service universel) is [supervised and managed by the Comité de développement des communications électroniques](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>), a body set by law, with the regulator ARCEP selecting the operators it pays. It is [fed mainly by a levy of 2% of telecom operators' turnover of the previous year](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>). ARCEP [listed finishing the fund's set-up among its priorities for 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>) when it presented a draft budget of 6.2 billion FCFA on 15 April 2026, Agence Ecofin reports.

**What the office does over it.** The fund's framework provides for [broadband internet access in mairies](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>).

**How the office is connected.** The fund [was not yet in operation on 15 April 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>).

**Last twelve months.** The committee that manages the fund [began its activities in July 2025](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>), and on 15 April 2026 ARCEP said the fund's effective set-up was planned for 2026.

**Local governments connected.** Mairies are in the fund's scope [alongside schools, health centres, libraries and post offices](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2104-137723-centrafrique-la-mise-en-uvre-effective-du-fonds-du-service-universel-prevue-pour-2026>), under law 18.002 of 17 January 2018 and a decree of February 2019.
