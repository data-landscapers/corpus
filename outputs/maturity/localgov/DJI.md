# Djibouti

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The system the commune centres work in belongs to the city: the PROGOUV terms of reference of February 2024 describe [Djibouti city's computerised civil-status system, which the city calls SIGEC](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>), with its data held on a central server in a room of the town hall.

**What the office does over it.** At February 2024 the civil-status centres of Djibouti city's three communes [recorded civil-status events in SIGEC](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>), as did five maternity antennas.

**How the office is connected.** The records the commune centres enter [sit on a central server at the town hall](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>), as the terms of reference of February 2024 describe it.

**Last twelve months.** In February 2024 [three commune centres and five antennas in health facilities](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>) were working in SIGEC.

**Local governments connected.** [Three commune civil-status centres](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>) worked in SIGEC at February 2024, one in each of Balbala, Ras Dika and Boulaos, the three communes of Djibouti city. All three are in the capital.

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: the city of Djibouti's [SIGEC](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>), its computerised civil-status system, in use in the communes' civil-status centres since 2011. Backbone: the [Djibouti Data Center](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>) in Djibouti city and the [fibre-optic networks of the DFN and EARDIP projects](<https://www.lanation.dj/transition-numerique-les-parties-prenantes-mobilisees-autour-des-projets-dfn-et-eardip/>), to be deployed in Djibouti-ville, Balbala and the five interior regions. Central: the [Djibouti Digital Foundations Project's connectivity for ministries, departments and agencies](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021026071059349/pdf/P174461-c8bd0e28-2653-456e-845a-f8242ec8facc.pdf>), whose pilot phase covers 33 of them, 22 in the regions, the first still being connected at February 2026, and the project's [fibre connection at the sous-préfecture of Damerjog](<https://www.lanation.dj/damerjog-entre-dans-lere-du-numerique-grace-au-projet-djibouti-fondement-du-numerique-dfn/>), installed by 21 October 2025.
