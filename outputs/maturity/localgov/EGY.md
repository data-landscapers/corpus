# Egypt

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Egypt's [project to automate local administration and governorate headquarters](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>), as its minister of communications named it on 15 January 2024, is carried out by the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology with the Ministry of Local Development and the governors. It sits within the state's institutional digital transformation project.

**What the office does over it.** The project is to [start at governorate headquarters, move to governorate capitals and districts, and end with local units](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**How the office is connected.** The minister [expected the Minya governorate headquarters to be linked electronically to the governorate's districts and local units during 2024](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**Last twelve months.** The minister [described the project and named its pilot on 15 January 2024](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**Local governments connected.** [Minya was chosen as the pilot](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>) for the first digital governorate headquarters, with the rest of local administration to follow it.
