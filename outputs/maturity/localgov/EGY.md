# Egypt

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Egypt's minister of communications named a [project to automate local administration and governorate headquarters](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>) on 15 January 2024. The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology carries it out with the Ministry of Local Development and the governors. It sits within the state's institutional digital transformation project.

**What the office does over it.** The project is to [start at governorate headquarters, move to governorate capitals and districts, and end with local units](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**How the office is connected.** The minister [expected the Minya governorate headquarters to be linked electronically to the governorate's districts and local units](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>). [The link was expected during 2024](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**Last twelve months.** The minister [described the project and named its pilot on 15 January 2024](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>).

**Local governments connected.** [Minya was chosen as the pilot](<https://www.dostor.org/4607148>) for the first digital governorate headquarters. The rest of local administration is to follow it.
