# Seychelles

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Office of the President's government directory [places the district administration offices under the Ministry of Local Government and Community Affairs](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>), as from 1 March 2025.

**What the office does over it.** Each of the 27 district administration offices [has its own office mailbox on the government's gov.sc domain](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>), from anseauxpinsda@gov.sc to takamakada@gov.sc, as from 1 March 2025.

**How the office is connected.** The directory [gives each district administration office a gov.sc mailbox beside an office telephone number](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>) (1 March 2025).

**Last twelve months.** The directory lists the district administrations and their mailboxes [as from 1 March 2025](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** The directory's table of district administrations [lists 27 districts, Praslin's two and La Digue among them, and gives every one of the 27 an office mailbox on gov.sc](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>) (1 March 2025).

***Noted, not assessed***. Public access: [small computer rooms or makerspaces in district centres](<https://www.opengovpartnership.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Seychelles_Action-Plan_2023-2025_December.pdf>), proposed in the 2024-2025 open government action plan. Backbone: the [Electronic Government Network](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/media/news/2025-06/media-youth-sports-and-culture-committee-conduct-visit-department-information>) of the Department of Information Communication Technology (2025); the [Government Intranet](<https://ict.gov.sc/Sections/PolicySection>) the same department administers (2026); and the [Airtel Seychelles Data Centre Perseverance](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>), operational since 2021, with government among its customers. Central: the Agency for Social Protection's [Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062625164514304/pdf/P168993-cdd171f8-8f75-415e-9083-d46ea92aa53d.pdf>), pending at May 2025, and its [Electronic Social Protection System](<https://asp.gov.sc/launching-of-esps-sensitisation-programme/>), in development in December 2024; and the [Department of Information and Communications Technology](<https://www.statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/files/50_yYk6GvnA.pdf>), under the Office of the Vice-President (2025).

***Not held***. How the district administration offices are connected is not stated, and none is said to be on the government network. No total of districts, no system the offices work in, and nothing on governance or on the last twelve months is held.
