# Malawi

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Minutes of consultations with councils in June and July 2024 say [the Department of E-Government manages the councils' internet on the government network](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>), and that [the Digital Malawi Project provided it, and the government is to take the service into its budget once World Bank funding ends](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>). A Public Private Partnership Commission page published on 28 November 2023 says a connectivity initiative is [to give over 500 public institutions fibre internet for ten years](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/pppc-e-govt-sensitize-mwanza-mdas-on-ppp-connectivity-initiative/>).

**What the office does over it.** The 2024 minutes record [council officers logging in to what they call the Boma network with passwords, and councils still waiting for government email accounts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>). On 31 July 2026 the Minister of Local Government said [councils upload project information to the national Constituency Development Fund dashboard](<https://www.247malawi.com/phiri-unveils-game-changing-cdf-dashboard-to-fast-track-development-end-delays/>).

**How the office is connected.** The minutes record that [Kasungu has 5MB on the Boma network, and that a M'mbelwa district executive member on 11 July 2024 called the internet the project provided to the councils very slow and unreliable](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** GIZ Malawi says the government [launched in mid-June 2026 a six-month pilot in which District Council Offices in Balaka and Dowa take on-demand updates to the national social registry](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/giz-malawi_socialprotection-socialregistry-digitaltransformation-activity-7475071354057256960-qS1N>).

**Local governments connected.** The minutes [name Kasungu as on the network and record on 25 June 2024 that Dowa was yet to be connected, of the 35 councils the project plan counts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>). The International Growth Centre's study, researched to early 2022, [describes office internet at the four city councils](<https://www.theigc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/Fleck%20et%20al%20Final%20report%20August%202023.pdf>).
