# Tanzania

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The e-Government Authority's roadmap of February 2024 [plans to expand GovNet](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>). [It would reach all facilities of local government authorities, ministries, departments and agencies](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>). The Digital Economy Strategic Framework 2024-2034 [targets 184 local government authorities on a structured office network and GovNet by June 2026](<https://www.ictc.go.tz/public/documents/tanzania_digital_economy_strategy.pdf>). African Business reported in August 2026 [the World Bank-financed Digital Tanzania Project supports extending broadband to local government offices](<https://african.business/2026/08/free-article/tanzanias-digital-dividend>).

**What the office does over it.** The deputy minister for regional administration and local government said [district councils process, disburse and collect repayment of the ten per cent special-group loans](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/digital-economys-systems-adoption-attracts-rapid-growth-in-key-sectors-2026-04-21-130330>). [They use the state-run system Tanzania calls WEZESHA](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/digital-economys-systems-adoption-attracts-rapid-growth-in-key-sectors-2026-04-21-130330>). For the year to 30 June 2025, the Controller and Auditor General reports that [local government authorities issued bills through the revenue system TAUSI](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Annual_General_Report_for_Audit_of_PO-RALG__Regional_Administrations_and_Local_Government_Authorities_FY_2024-25.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The e-Government Authority states [local government authorities are on GovNet, the government communication network](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>). [Ministries, departments and agencies are on it too](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 1 May 2026 the Geita Regional Commissioner [directed District Executive Directors and other employers to fund employees' internet from internal revenues](<https://dailynews.co.tz/deds-directed-to-cover-internet-costs-in-public-offices/>). This followed a statement by the trade union congress. It said [many public employees in the region buy internet bundles with their own money](<https://dailynews.co.tz/deds-directed-to-cover-internet-costs-in-public-offices/>).

**Local governments connected.** [174 district councils were authorised to lend through WEZESHA between July 2024 and December 2025](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/digital-economys-systems-adoption-attracts-rapid-growth-in-key-sectors-2026-04-21-130330>). [This followed a pilot in 10](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/business/read/digital-economys-systems-adoption-attracts-rapid-growth-in-key-sectors-2026-04-21-130330>). The authority's roadmap of February 2024 gives [265 ministries, departments, agencies and local government authorities on GovNet, as one figure](<https://www.ega.go.tz/uploads/standarddocuments/sw-1710314957-SIGNED%20e-Government%20Technology%20Roadmap%202024%20(1).pdf>).
