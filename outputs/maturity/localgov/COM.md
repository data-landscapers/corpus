# Comoros

## Office connection

***Noted, not assessed***. The [Comoros Government Public Administration Data Centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>) in Moroni, serving ministries, departments and agencies. The administration's internal messaging, which the digital agency ANADEN was to relaunch as of 3 September 2025, and [the national ".km" domain name system](<https://www.anaden.org/actualites/v2>) it manages. The [EASSy submarine cable, landing at Moroni, and 2Africa](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099450206282323160/pdf/IDU00f2d51f40fd4a04e630b0b60d27b1a4f548b.pdf>), which lists Comoros as a landing country. The état civil system, kept in the communes, whose [computerisation studies under the Amecc project were validated on 25 March 2026](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/d%C3%A9centralisation/projet-amecc-i-vers-la-%C2%ABmodernisation-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9tat-civil%C2%BB.html>). The tax administration's [plateforme fiscale for online declaration and payment](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decret-portant-promulgation-du-loi-des-finances-2026.pdf>), set out in the finance law for 2026. The interior ministry's [production of national identity cards and passports](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/mohamed-ahamed-assoumani,-ministre-de-l%E2%80%99int%C3%A9rieur-i-%C2%ABle-plan-comores-%C3%A9mergent-ne-va-pas-se-faire-avec-les-minist%C3%A8res-seulement%C2%BB.html>), passing from Semlex to Genkey from 1 April 2026. The finance ministry's [network cabling for its 23 sites on the three islands](<https://finances.gouv.km/appel-doffre/projet-dappui-aux-systemes-dinformation-de-la-gouvernance-financiere-pagf-si-2/>), at tender with bids due on 16 February 2026. The Cartographic Unit's [national geo-referenced database](<https://ggim.un.org/meetings/2025/Maputo/documents/I-2.1_Status_Report-Country_Reports.pdf>), which produces maps for the government and local authorities. The [fichier électoral](<https://justice.gouv.km/texte/code-electoral/>), computerised and centralised under the electoral code of 2023. The [American Corner Moroni](<https://km.usembassy.gov/fr/le-nouvel-american-corner-connecte-les-comores-aux-etats-unis-et-au-monde/>), a public centre at the Université des Comores inaugurated on 30 May 2023.
