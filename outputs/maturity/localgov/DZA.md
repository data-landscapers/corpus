# Algeria

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The network linking the communes to the national civil-status database is [the interior ministry's own](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>), on its presentation of June 2019. It was [on the ministry's directives](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/immatriculation-des-vehicules-passage-au-tout-numerique-des-aujourd-hui-250065>) that municipal services moved onto the national vehicle-registration platform on 5 April 2026. The commune of Bouira [bears its own annual internet subscription](<https://ccomptes.dz/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RA.2025-AR.pdf>), the Court of Accounts found in September 2023.

**What the office does over it.** Communes and their annexes [issue birth, marriage and death acts from any commune in the country](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>), from commune databases linked to a national one (June 2019). Since 5 April 2026 municipal services [work in the national vehicle-registration system](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/immatriculation-des-vehicules-passage-au-tout-numerique-des-aujourd-hui-250065>), a centralised platform that replaced local applications. In September 2023 Bouira used [55 applications, 41 of them the ministry's](<https://ccomptes.dz/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RA.2025-AR.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The ministry's architecture slide of June 2019 shows commune, wilaya and ministry levels [linked over its own network](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>), which it labels "Réseau MICL". Bouira [has no intranet and links its floors to the internet by Wi-Fi](<https://ccomptes.dz/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RA.2025-AR.pdf>), the Court of Accounts found in September 2023.

**Last twelve months.** On 5 April 2026 municipal services [began the third phase of generalising the national vehicle-registration system](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/actualite/immatriculation-des-vehicules-passage-au-tout-numerique-des-aujourd-hui-250065>), ending manual and local procedures.

**Local governments connected.** The interior ministry's presentation of June 2019 shows [1,541 commune databases on the civil-status network](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>): 1,541 of 1,541 communes, on its own account. The Court of Accounts says the ministry's human-resources system was [placed at all communes and wilayas](<https://ccomptes.dz/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/RA.2025-AR.pdf>), in an audit covering 2019 to 2024.
