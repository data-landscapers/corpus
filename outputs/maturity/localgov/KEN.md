# Kenya

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy [presents the County Connectivity Project as its own, with two further phases started in 2022](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). The ICT Authority's plan for 2024 to 2027 states [county governments have not prioritised ICT investment](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-09/SP_2024_-_2027_0912.pdf>). [It says they rely on national government initiatives for their connectivity](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-09/SP_2024_-_2027_0912.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The Controller of Budget reviewed the year to 30 June 2026, as the Daily Nation reports it. It has county governments [processing payroll in the national Human Resource Information System, which Kenya calls HRIS-Ke](<https://nation.africa/kenya/counties/revealed-counties-made-sh8-3bn-manual-payments-outside-payroll-systems-5615212>). At least Sh8.3 billion in salaries was still paid by manual payroll outside it. The National Treasury's draft payment policy of August 2026 says the Central Bank [rolled out internet banking for county governments](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Draft-National-Payment-System-Policy-2026.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The ministry states [the counties were connected by secure connections into the national private governmental voice and data network](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). [It calls the network NOFBI; the last county was connected by 2017](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>).

**Last twelve months.** On 25 August 2026 the National Treasury's Cabinet Secretary announced a deadline of 30 September 2026. By then [all 47 county governments must finish integrating the national electronic procurement platform with its financial management system](<https://www.wearetech.africa/actualites/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/kenya-les-47-comtes-sommes-de-passer-aux-marches-publics-numeriques>). In the year to June 2026 counties cited [connectivity challenges among their reasons for manual payroll](<https://nation.africa/kenya/counties/revealed-counties-made-sh8-3bn-manual-payments-outside-payroll-systems-5615212>).

**Local governments connected.** The ministry states [Phase I, in 2012 and 2013, connected 28 counties and Nairobi](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>). [Phase 2, completed in 2017, connected the remaining 18 of the 47 it names](<https://ict.go.ke/last-mile-county-connectivity-project-lmccp>).
