# Liberia

## Office connection

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: [desktop computers, laptops, printers and UPS units handed to the County Development Planning Units of five counties in March 2026, making nine of 15 equipped, under a UNDP programme financed by Sweden](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/undp-delivers-ict-equipment-strengthen-county-development-planning-units>); and the county administrative buildings' equipment, [about 99% of which, computers and printers included, the Ministry of Internal Affairs says has outlived its usefulness](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). Applications: IFMIS, the government's financial management system, [in 86 ministries, agencies and commissions at 30 September 2025, with four County Treasury Offices connected against a target of 15 by December 2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110625073415416/pdf/P177478-9397c8d1-edb6-4a96-a39d-0222e717f2c4.pdf>); and e-County, [a county-level system the national digital strategy for 2025-2029 says Liberia lacks](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). For the public: [community Wi-Fi in public areas of Gbarnga, Ganta, Kakata and Sanniquellie, and a 20-seat digital learning laboratory at J.W. Pearson Public School in Ganta, both from CSquared's programme completed in September 2026](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>); [the Digital Village Program, whose first village was launched in Lofa County in May 2026](<https://micat.gov.lr/media/press-releases/president-boakai-launches-digital-village-program-lofa-county>); and [public Wi-Fi and access points at schools, hospitals and County Service Centers, and Community Digital Access Centers, both proposed by the infrastructure conference of January 2026](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). Backbone: GovNet, the government network, [with 52 ministries, agencies and commissions connected directly to fibre since its 2019 rollout](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>) and [a conference recommendation of January 2026 to extend it to the counties](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>); [the dark fibre connecting ministries, agencies and commissions](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040723102029990/pdf/P1694820269f8a044087b1012089ee857d6.pdf>); [Libtelco's secure government network, on fibre in Monrovia in 2019](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>); [CSquared's fibre network, over 250km in Monrovia and Montserrado and over 350km to the Côte d'Ivoire and Guinea borders](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>); the national fibre backbone, [about 1,000 km short, mainly in the southeast, with nationwide fibre envisaged by 2029](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>); the National Data Center, [hosted at LTC-Mobile and being upgraded under the World Bank's GREAT project in March 2026](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>); [the LTC Mobile National Data Centre in Monrovia, a carrier-neutral colocation facility](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>); [the Amilcar Cabral submarine cable under WARDIP-SOP2, financed by the World Bank with US$50 million and landing in the Buchanan area](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-ESMF_WARDIP-SOP2-Liberia-11Dec2025.docx.pdf>); and [the CLSG network at the Buchanan substation, where linking the cable to it was assessed](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-Stakeholder-Engagement-Plan.docx.pdf>). Central: the County Service Centers, one in each of the 15 counties, where [the Ministry of Internal Affairs' 2024 assessment found 35 of the 135 computers supplied in 2015 working, no printers and no internet connectivity](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>); [the GREAT project's upgrade of their IT infrastructure, financed by the World Bank with US$5 million, for which a consultant assessed all 15 in 2024](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>); [One-Stop-Shop Service Centers planned in Zwedru, Grand Bassa and Lofa in August 2026](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/undp-and-ministry-local-government-strengthen-partnership-advance-decentralization-liberia>); [county treasuries, which UNDP's programme aims to set up in all 15 counties, and a Vehicle and Driver's License Hub in Ganta City](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>); [the Ministry of Internal Affairs' own ICT network, with internet restored to three headquarters buildings in 2024, and its LERN conflict early-warning platform, shut down for lack of funding](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>); the Liberia Revenue Authority's [ASYCUDA, in 11 of 17 Customs Business Offices, and LITAS, in 5 of 18 Tax Business Offices, in August 2026](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/lra-unveils-new-strategic-plan-as-liberia-moves-toward-historic-us1-billion-revenue-mark/>), and its [real property register, piloted in Margibi County from 15 December 2021 and extended to Buchanan in 2022](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-DRM-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>), with [GIS and valuation systems in the two counties](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>); [the national biometric identification system, with enrolment offices being prepared in counties and districts in August 2026](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-picks-austrian-firm-for-new-biometric-id-system/>); [the Birth Registration Information Management System, in all 15 counties and 58 centres in December 2024](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>); the Ministry of Education's [county data hubs for the Annual School Census, at County Education Offices in all 15 counties](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-moe-launches-2025-2026-national-school-census-to-strengthen-education-system/>) and its [digitized Monitoring and Supervision System, piloted in five counties in March 2026](<https://moe.gov.lr/minister-of-education-engages-press-on-digitized-school-monitoring-system/>); [the eLiberia Portal, developed and with no services online in May 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>); [the Case Management Information System of the courts, county attorneys and police](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/liberia-designs-and-roll-out-online-case-management-information-system-justice-institutions>); [the police's Crime Statistics Information Management System](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>); and [the West African Police Information System, whose first equipment was handed over on 5 July 2019](<https://www.interpol.int/es/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_EN_LR.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any county Superintendent office is connected, how, what it does over the connection and how many are. The one target on record, internet access at every Superintendent office, belongs to the national ICT policy for 2019-2024, and no dated statement that the offices lack a connection is held.
