# Liberia

## Office connection

***Also on file***. Equipment: [desktop computers, laptops, printers and UPS units were handed to the County Development Planning Units of five counties in March 2026](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/undp-delivers-ict-equipment-strengthen-county-development-planning-units>). [That made nine of 15 equipped, under a UNDP programme financed by Sweden](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/undp-delivers-ict-equipment-strengthen-county-development-planning-units>). The Ministry of Internal Affairs says [about 99% of the county administrative buildings' equipment, computers and printers included, has outlived its usefulness](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). Applications: IFMIS, the government's financial management system, was [in 86 ministries, agencies and commissions at 30 September 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110625073415416/pdf/P177478-9397c8d1-edb6-4a96-a39d-0222e717f2c4.pdf>). [Four County Treasury Offices were connected, against a target of 15 by December 2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110625073415416/pdf/P177478-9397c8d1-edb6-4a96-a39d-0222e717f2c4.pdf>). The national digital strategy for 2025-2029 says [Liberia lacks a county-level system, e-County](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). For the public: CSquared's programme, completed in September 2026, provided [community Wi-Fi in public areas of Gbarnga, Ganta, Kakata and Sanniquellie](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>). [It also provided a 20-seat digital learning laboratory at J.W. Pearson Public School in Ganta](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>). [The first village of the Digital Village Program was launched in Lofa County in May 2026](<https://micat.gov.lr/media/press-releases/president-boakai-launches-digital-village-program-lofa-county>). The infrastructure conference of January 2026 proposed [public Wi-Fi and access points at schools, hospitals and County Service Centers](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). [It also proposed Community Digital Access Centers](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). Backbone: GovNet, the government network, [has 52 ministries, agencies and commissions connected directly to fibre since its 2019 rollout](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). [A conference recommendation of January 2026 would extend it to the counties](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). The backbone also includes [the dark fibre connecting ministries, agencies and commissions](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040723102029990/pdf/P1694820269f8a044087b1012089ee857d6.pdf>), and [Libtelco's secure government network, on fibre in Monrovia in 2019](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>). [CSquared's fibre network runs over 250km in Monrovia and Montserrado](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>). [It also runs over 350km to the Côte d'Ivoire and Guinea borders](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>). The national fibre backbone is [about 1,000 km short, mainly in the southeast](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). [Nationwide fibre is envisaged by 2029](<https://mpw.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/1Report%20of%20the%20National%20Infrastructure%20Conference%202026.pdf>). The National Data Center is [hosted at LTC-Mobile and was being upgraded under the World Bank's GREAT project in March 2026](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). The backbone also includes [the LTC Mobile National Data Centre in Monrovia, a carrier-neutral colocation facility](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>). [The Amilcar Cabral submarine cable under WARDIP-SOP2 is financed by the World Bank with US$50 million](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-ESMF_WARDIP-SOP2-Liberia-11Dec2025.docx.pdf>). [It lands in the Buchanan area](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-ESMF_WARDIP-SOP2-Liberia-11Dec2025.docx.pdf>). Linking the cable to [the CLSG network at the Buchanan substation was assessed](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/8-Final-Stakeholder-Engagement-Plan.docx.pdf>). Central: there is one County Service Center in each of the 15 counties. [The Ministry of Internal Affairs' 2024 assessment found 35 of the 135 computers supplied to them in 2015 working](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). [It found no printers and no internet connectivity](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). [The GREAT project's upgrade of the centres' IT infrastructure is financed by the World Bank with US$5 million](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). [A consultant assessed all 15 centres for it in 2024](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). [One-Stop-Shop Service Centers were planned in Zwedru, Grand Bassa and Lofa in August 2026](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/press-releases/undp-and-ministry-local-government-strengthen-partnership-advance-decentralization-liberia>). [UNDP's programme aims to set up county treasuries in all 15 counties](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>). [Also on file is a Vehicle and Driver's License Hub in Ganta City](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>). [The Ministry of Internal Affairs' own ICT network had internet restored to three headquarters buildings in 2024](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). [Its LERN conflict early-warning platform was shut down for lack of funding](<https://www.mlg.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/2025%20Ministry%20of%20Internal%20Affairs%20Annual%20Report.pdf>). In August 2026 the Liberia Revenue Authority's [ASYCUDA was in 11 of 17 Customs Business Offices, and LITAS in 5 of 18 Tax Business Offices](<https://smartnewsliberia.com/lra-unveils-new-strategic-plan-as-liberia-moves-toward-historic-us1-billion-revenue-mark/>). Its [real property register was piloted in Margibi County from 15 December 2021 and extended to Buchanan in 2022](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-DRM-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>). There are [GIS and valuation systems in the two counties](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>). [The national biometric identification system had enrolment offices being prepared in counties and districts in August 2026](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-picks-austrian-firm-for-new-biometric-id-system/>). [The Birth Registration Information Management System was in all 15 counties and 58 centres in December 2024](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>). The Ministry of Education has [county data hubs for the Annual School Census, at County Education Offices in all 15 counties](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-moe-launches-2025-2026-national-school-census-to-strengthen-education-system/>). [Its digitized Monitoring and Supervision System was piloted in five counties in March 2026](<https://moe.gov.lr/minister-of-education-engages-press-on-digitized-school-monitoring-system/>). [The eLiberia Portal was developed, with no services online in May 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>). Central systems also include [the Case Management Information System of the courts, county attorneys and police](<https://www.undp.org/liberia/stories/liberia-designs-and-roll-out-online-case-management-information-system-justice-institutions>). They also include [the police's Crime Statistics Information Management System](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-05/undp-liberia-annual-report-2024-v8-high.pdf>). They also include [the West African Police Information System, whose first equipment was handed over on 5 July 2019](<https://www.interpol.int/es/content/download/14327/file/WAPIS_SIPAO_Newsletter-12_EN_LR.pdf>).
