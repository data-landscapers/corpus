# Lesotho

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The e-Government Project that built and connected two community council buildings [is under the Ministry of Communications, Science and Technology](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>), which handed them to the Ministry of Local Government and Chieftainship Affairs on 31 July 2019. The project [is financially supported by the African Development Bank](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>).

**What the office does over it.** The 31 July 2019 handover report states that [the two buildings have the Internet installed](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>). In January 2018 the secretary of Tenesolo Community Council said e-government would [connect the office to internet access and to a data bank in Mohale's Hoek](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/internet-connection-will-improve-service-delivery-comms-minister/>). In September 2025 the civil registration manager for Qacha's Nek said certificate services decentralised to councils [fail without transport to carry applications to and from the district office](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/sekete-champions-civil-registration-access-in-hard-to-reach-qachas-nek/>).

**How the office is connected.** The Minister said on 31 July 2019 that the two buildings handed over under his ministry's project [have been installed with the Internet](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>). In January 2018 the head of the national web e-portal said council connections [will use the Lesotho government data network](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/internet-connection-will-improve-service-delivery-comms-minister/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 17 June 2024 the communications ministry's Principal Secretary said its plan is to roll out its 74 digital services [by also connecting Community Councils](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/micsti-partners-strategise/>).

**Local governments connected.** [Two community councils, Dinizulu in Thaba-Tseka and Kubake in Maseru district](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>), had the Internet installed by 31 July 2019 under the e-Government Pilot Project begun in 2014. In January 2018 the Minister said the plan is [to assist in connecting all the councils](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/internet-connection-will-improve-service-delivery-comms-minister/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: [ten computers and a server for Tenesolo Community Council](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/internet-connection-will-improve-service-delivery-comms-minister/>) and the [land registration capacity upgrade for eight local councils](<https://assets.mcc.gov/content/uploads/compact-lesotho-health-and-horticulture.pdf>). Applications: [civil registration services decentralised to councils](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/sekete-champions-civil-registration-access-in-hard-to-reach-qachas-nek/>), Maseru City Council's [Construction Permit Automation System](<https://www.cbs.co.ls/project/construction-permit-automation-system/>) and the [Decentralizing Access to Child Development Indicators project](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>). Public access: the ministry's ["digital ambassadors"](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/un-resident-coordinator-pays-coutersy-call-on-information-minister/>), the [digital studio at Makoanyane Square and the UNIPOD studio at Lerotholi Polytechnic](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/advancing-the-national-digital-agenda-and-ai-technologies-remains-a-core-commitment-of-lesotho-government/>), and [free Wi-Fi for communities around the two councils](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/chief-thesele-hands-over-community-councils-buildings/>). Backbone: the [Vodacom data centres at Lekokoaneng and Maseru West](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>), the [government's data hosting and business continuity solutions and the e-Government Infrastructure Phase I and Phase II projects](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>), the [Lesotho E-Government Infrastructure Project](<https://www.gov.ls/download/national-digital-policy-2024-2035/>) and the [government network](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/cef30410-108d-4f8a-a725-2842e5baaf84/content>). Central: the [CRVS and national ID system, the government e-payments gateway and the interoperability platform](<https://50in5.net/lesotho-joins-50-in-5/>), the [shared database of the Independent Electoral Commission and Home Affairs](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/01/30/iec-to-partner-home-affairs-ministry-in-seamless-database-project/>), [IFMIS, CBMS, HRMIS and NID](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>), and [NISSA](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/312581624983186451/pdf/Lesotho-Social-Protection-Programs-and-Systems-Review-Policy-Brief.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any community council beyond the two pilot ones is connected, and how many councils there are in all. What system council staff work in over the connection, and any plan period or financing since 2019.
