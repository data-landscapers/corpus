# South Africa

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Mangaung Metropolitan Municipality told the National Council of Provinces in November 2025 that [its network upgrade is its own project](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). [It said the project runs under a council-approved ICT governance framework](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). [It said it has adopted a three-year ICT strategic plan naming broadband as an outcome](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). It said that [it faces limited resources and an inadequate budget for ICT](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The State Information Technology Agency said in July 2026 that [municipalities across the Eastern Cape are operational on its Provincial Disaster Management Application System](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sita-plugs-gaps-in-govts-disaster-response/DZQ587V83EQqzXy2>). The Road Traffic Infringement Agency lists upgrades behind the AARTO rollout. [Among them is the integration of municipalities with the national traffic information system](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/rtia-presses-ahead-with-tech-driven-aarto-rollout/VgZeyqJlWB47djX9>). Mangaung [has moved its email to a cloud platform](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). [It migrated 900 user accounts, beside 600 already there](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** By November 2025 Mangaung [had appointed a service provider to upgrade its network infrastructure across its seven towns](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). [The first phase was under way](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). [It was still appointing an internet service provider for last-mile connectivity](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Eastern Cape disaster system [went live on 21 November 2025, with training for provincial and municipal users](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sita-plugs-gaps-in-govts-disaster-response/DZQ587V83EQqzXy2>). AARTO [entered its second phase in July 2026, expanding to 62 municipalities](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/rtia-presses-ahead-with-tech-driven-aarto-rollout/VgZeyqJlWB47djX9>).

**Local governments connected.** [One metropolitan municipality, Mangaung, reports an upgrade of its own network](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Pages/2025/07-11-2025_NCOP_Provincial_week/Free_State/19-11-2025/MANGAUNG_Status_of_developments_and_progress_on_digital_transformation.pdf>). Interviews with nine managers of rural local municipalities in Limpopo, published in 2025, [found that only communication had been digitally transformed, through social media and Microsoft Teams](<https://sajim.co.za/index.php/sajim/article/view/2077>).
