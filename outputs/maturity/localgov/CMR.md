# Cameroon

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** On 2 June 2026 the government [presented DIGI-CoR, a CFA 92 billion programme to connect and equip local authorities](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments>). Its financing was still being sought. The posts ministry [runs PATNUC](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>), whose connectivity for local authorities is paid from a World Bank loan of US$100 million. In August 2026 Bertoua 1er [had its own budget line for the internet connection](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>).

**What the office does over it.** APC's 2023 brief says pilot networks at Mayo-Oulo and Bibemi [give the two town halls internet access](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/pb_cameroon_french.pdf>). They also give offline access to a platform of local content.

**How the office is connected.** Bertoua 1er [budgets for the internet connection of its digitised civil status service](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>), UNDP Cameroon reported in August 2026. In 2023 the town halls of Mayo-Oulo and Bibemi [each had a Wi-Fi access point](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/pb_cameroon_french.pdf>). It worked on a long-distance Wi-Fi link, APC's brief says.

**Last twelve months.** On 3 October 2025 the posts ministry [sought a consultant for a 120-day needs assessment under component 2 of PATNUC](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>). DIGI-CoR [went to funders in Yaoundé on 2 June 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments>).

**Local governments connected.** In August 2026 UNDP Cameroon [stated an internet connection at two East Region commune offices](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>): Bétaré-Oya, where it is unstable, and Bertoua 1er. In June 2026 Afrik Info reported that [145 of 384 local authorities had been equipped with FEICOM financing](<https://afrikinfo.net/cameroun-financement-du-projet-digi-cor-letat-recherche-92-milliards-fcfa/>). Cameroon has [360 communes and 14 urban communities](<https://journalcentrifuge.com/societe/534-digitalisation-des-communes-et-villes-camerounaises-la-premiere-phase-du-programme-presentee-a-yaounde>).
