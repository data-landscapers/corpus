# Cameroon

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** On 2 June 2026 the government [presented DIGI-CoR, a CFA 92 billion programme over three years to improve connectivity and equip decentralised local authorities](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments>), and [was still seeking its financing from partners](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments>). The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications [is contracting authority for PATNUC](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>), whose connectivity for local authorities [is paid from a World Bank loan of US$100 million](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>). In August 2026 the commune of Bertoua 1er [had a budget line of its own for maintenance and the internet connection](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>).

**What the office does over it.** APC's policy brief of 2023 says the pilot networks at Mayo-Oulo and Bibemi [give the connected sites, the two town halls among them, internet access and offline access to the GOON platform of local content](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/pb_cameroon_french.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** UNDP Cameroon reported in August 2026 that Bertoua 1er [budgets for the internet connection of its digitised civil status service](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>). The town halls of Mayo-Oulo and Bibemi [were each fitted with a Wi-Fi access point on a long-distance Wi-Fi link](<https://www.apc.org/sites/default/files/pb_cameroon_french.pdf>), by APC's brief of 2023.

**Last twelve months.** On 3 October 2025 the posts ministry [sought a consultant for a 120-day needs assessment under component 2 of PATNUC](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>). DIGI-CoR [went to funders in Yaoundé on 2 June 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/cameroon-launches-163-million-push-to-digitize-local-governments>).

**Local governments connected.** UNDP Cameroon's monitoring of six East Region sites in August 2026 [states an internet connection at two commune offices: Bétaré-Oya, where it is available but unstable, and Bertoua 1er](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>). Cameroon has [360 communes and 14 urban communities](<https://journalcentrifuge.com/societe/534-digitalisation-des-communes-et-villes-camerounaises-la-premiere-phase-du-programme-presentee-a-yaounde>).
