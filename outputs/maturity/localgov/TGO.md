# Togo

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The digital ministry's resettlement plan of July 2026 names [the ministry's project unit as promoter of the fibre extension that is to reach town halls](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PANT-Rapport-PAR-Re%CC%81gion-Maritime.pdf>), under [the Projet d'Accélération Numérique du Togo, a 100 million dollar project with a target of 136 town hall buildings](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PANT-Rapport-PAR-Re%CC%81gion-Maritime.pdf>), [implemented with the World Bank](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PANT-Rapport-PAR-Re%CC%81gion-Maritime.pdf>). KfW's records show [a EUR 20 million grant committed in 2024](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202168912>) and [a EUR 15 million grant committed in 2025](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DE-1-202468155>) to connect town halls, hospitals and schools to the fibre network.

**What the office does over it.** Under a pilot in Vo prefecture, Togo First reported in May 2026, [the mairie reaches in the platform Naissance Keva the newborn's data entered at the maternity, to draw up the birth record](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/2105-19030-a-akoumape-le-togo-teste-un-modele-integre-pour-les-2000-premiers-jours-de-lenfant>). In Lacs 1, UNDP reported in May 2021, [the Kadodo application shares data in real time between health centres and the town hall's civil registry](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>).

**How the office is connected.** The government's site said on 7 February 2026 that the town hall digitalisation project it calls PDiMa is [to connect the 136 municipal buildings themselves](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.com/tic/0702-11581-decentralisation-un-projet-de-digitalisation-des-mairies-en-preparation>).

**Last twelve months.** [The Vo pilot has been deployed since February 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/2105-19030-a-akoumape-le-togo-teste-un-modele-integre-pour-les-2000-premiers-jours-de-lenfant>), and [the final resettlement plan for the Maritime region's fibre works was issued in July 2026, with the operator still to be recruited](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PANT-Rapport-PAR-Re%CC%81gion-Maritime.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** [The platform is a pilot in the four communes of Vo prefecture](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/sante/2105-19030-a-akoumape-le-togo-teste-un-modele-integre-pour-les-2000-premiers-jours-de-lenfant>). The July 2026 plan states that [the fibre works to the 136 town hall buildings had not begun](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PANT-Rapport-PAR-Re%CC%81gion-Maritime.pdf>).
