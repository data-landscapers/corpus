# Madagascar

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [Plan National de Décentralisation Emergente 2023-2030, validated by decree on 2 August 2023](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>), makes local governments' secure connection to the State's strategic information systems a product, with the [ministries of decentralisation, digital development and finance responsible](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>). It [names no source of funds for the product's 23,092,000 thousand ariary](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>). The April 2025 handover was [made through PRODUIR, which the World Bank finances](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/remise-des-materiels-informatiques-et-techniques-dans-le-cadre-de-matac-hanorina-phase-2/>).

**What the office does over it.** The plan's connection indicator is [communes with direct access to the Système d'Information Financière Communal (SIFC), a State information system](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>). In December 2025, [336 communes were using the digital civil registration system SIECM](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>). Communes of Greater Antananarivo use the ministry's [online building-permit tool](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/remise-des-materiels-informatiques-et-techniques-dans-le-cadre-de-matac-hanorina-phase-2/>).

**How the office is connected.** The plan measures communes [having a secure connection to the SIFC, and costs annual connection fees for 1,000 communes over three years](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>). On 30 April 2025 the decentralisation ministry [handed internet boxes and a subscription to all 38 communes of the Antananarivo agglomeration](<https://www.produir-madagascar.mg/remise-des-materiels-informatiques-et-techniques-dans-le-cadre-de-matac-hanorina-phase-2/>).

**Last twelve months.** The number of communes using SIECM [rose from 75 to 336 in the six months to December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>).

**Local governments connected.** The plan, published in June 2024, gives a [baseline of 10 per cent of the country's 1,695 communes with direct access to the SIFC over a secure connection, sourced to the finance ministry, with a target of 50 per cent for 2030](<https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/mdat_plan_national_de_decentralisation_emergente_2024.pdf>). The World Bank puts the [336 communes of December 2025 at 40 per cent of the project's target](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/pdf/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.pdf>).
