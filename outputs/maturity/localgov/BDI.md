# Burundi

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The sector strategy for the National Development Plan 2018-2027, issued by the Ministry of Communication, Information Technologies and Media in February 2021, [names the extension of the government's fibre-optic interconnection to the communes under its e-government project](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burundi/Sektoralnaa%20strategia%20v%20oblasti%20IKT%20do%202027%20g.%20prinata%20v%202021.pdf>). In Rumonge commune [the routers and other equipment for the civil registry service's connection are to come from the local NGO FVS-Amade under a one-year project](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>), its programme officer said on 19 July 2024, and [workshop participants said the commune could not keep digital registration going once that support ends](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>).

**What the office does over it.** The connection announced for Rumonge [is for the commune's civil registry service, at the commune seat and in its zones](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>).

**How the office is connected.** FVS-Amade said on 19 July 2024 that [it will give Rumonge's civil registry service computers, tablets, solar panels and routers to ease its connection](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 18 July 2026 the interior ministry's Director General for territorial administration said that [many civil registration centres have no reliable internet connection for digital services and are poorly equipped with computers](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** The strategy of February 2021 states that [about a hundred public institutions are interconnected by a fibre-optic link and that the project needs to be extended to the communes not yet connected](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Burundi/Sektoralnaa%20strategia%20v%20oblasti%20IKT%20do%202027%20g.%20prinata%20v%202021.pdf>). [One commune, Rumonge, was named for a connection on 19 July 2024](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>).
