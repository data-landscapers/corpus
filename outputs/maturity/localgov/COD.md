# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The provider is a provincial government: [the governor of Haut-Uélé handed the kits to the heads of the province's decentralised territorial entities](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>) at Isiro on 17 March 2026.

**What the office does over it.** The kits are for the heads of the entities [to send security alerts to the provincial capital in real time](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>), in the governor's words.

**How the office is connected.** By satellite: the entities were given [Starlink internet kits](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>), in a province where the lack of a telecom network remains a problem.

**Last twelve months.** The handover of 17 March 2026 [followed the recommendations of the 2025 forum on security governance](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>).

**Local governments connected.** [45 chiefdoms and sectors of Haut-Uélé received kits](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>) on 17 March 2026, beside 6 territories and the security services, according to the provincial information note.
