# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** In Haut-Uélé the provider is the provincial government. [The governor handed satellite kits to the heads of the province's decentralised entities](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>) at Isiro on 17 March 2026. Nationally, the Digital Transformation Project unit [runs the GovNet connection tender](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/02/actualite-rdc-connectivite-batiments-gouvernorats-territoires-rdc/>) and reports to the posts and digital economy ministries. The World Bank [finances it under credit 7602-ZR, with AFD co-financing](<https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/06-juin/01/ugptn_260604_01_05.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The governor says the Haut-Uélé kits are for the entities' heads [to send security alerts to the provincial capital in real time](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>). GovNet is to [link the pilot buildings to Kinshasa over a virtual private network](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/02/actualite-rdc-connectivite-batiments-gouvernorats-territoires-rdc/>).

**How the office is connected.** By satellite. The Haut-Uélé entities were given [Starlink internet kits](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>), in a province that lacks a telecom network. The GovNet pilot is to use [low-orbit satellite links](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/02/actualite-rdc-connectivite-batiments-gouvernorats-territoires-rdc/>).

**Last twelve months.** The handover of 17 March 2026 [followed the 2025 forum on security governance](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>). On 4 June 2026 the project unit [issued a tender to connect the buildings of 7 governorates and 44 territories](<https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/06-juin/01/ugptn_260604_01_05.pdf>) in a pilot phase. Bids were due on 16 July 2026.

**Local governments connected.** On 17 March 2026 [45 chiefdoms and sectors of Haut-Uélé received kits](<https://acp.cd/economie/haut-uele-des-kits-internet-satellite-starlink-aux-institutions-pour-renforcer-la-communication>), beside 6 territories and the security services. The national GovNet tender [covers 7 governorates and 44 territories in its pilot phase](<https://www.congoquotidien.com/2026/06/02/actualite-rdc-connectivite-batiments-gouvernorats-territoires-rdc/>).
