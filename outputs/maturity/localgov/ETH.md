# Ethiopia

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Addis Ababa City Administration [runs the one-stop service centres it calls Addis Mesob and their digital system](<https://www.amn.gov.et/en/addis-ababas-digital-one-stop-platform-streamlines-government-services-bringing-over-150-services-under-one-digital-system-official/>), on its director general's report of July 2026. The city's civil registration and residency system [was launched by the city administration for its own agency and built by the state's Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7858122>) in December 2025.

**What the office does over it.** Over the Addis Ababa connectivity, [family registration, vital events registration, resident services and verification are carried out through a digital system built with locally developed applications](<https://www.gcs.gov.et/en/2025/12/22/digital-ethiopia-2025-has-brought-about-a-significant-change-in-improving-access-to-civil-registration-and-citizenship-services/>), a government statement published on 22 December 2025 says. Outside the capital, the World Bank said in October 2025 that [444 of the safety net programme's 492 woredas use its management information system for programme tracking](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>), which is the programme's own system and not the woreda administration's.

**How the office is connected.** In Addis Ababa [connectivity has been created from the central level down to the woreda level](<https://www.gcs.gov.et/en/2025/12/22/digital-ethiopia-2025-has-brought-about-a-significant-change-in-improving-access-to-civil-registration-and-citizenship-services/>) for civil registration and citizenship services, the deputy director general of the Ethiopian Artificial Intelligence Institute said in the statement of 22 December 2025.

**Last twelve months.** Addis Ababa's mayor [launched the Integrated Civil Registration and Residency Service Technology on 5 December 2025 and said implementation would start in 50 of the city's woredas on 8 December 2025](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7858122>).

**Local governments connected.** The central-to-woreda connectivity [is a major pilot, in Addis Ababa only](<https://www.gcs.gov.et/en/2025/12/22/digital-ethiopia-2025-has-brought-about-a-significant-change-in-improving-access-to-civil-registration-and-citizenship-services/>), on the statement of 22 December 2025.
