# Congo

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Internet access for town halls falls under the *Projet d'accélération de la transformation numérique* (Patn), which [the World Bank finances for US$100 million and whose 2026 work plan and budget was adopted at XAF 21.872 billion](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-le-budget-du-patn-pour-lexercice-2026-arrete-a-21872-milliards-de-fcfa/>) on 26 February 2026, according to ACI. The Bank's appraisal of May 2022 [expects shared connectivity to be supplied to decentralised local authorities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The access is meant to let public administrations [interconnect with the state's digital platforms](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-le-budget-du-patn-pour-lexercice-2026-arrete-a-21872-milliards-de-fcfa/>).

**How the office is connected.** The project is to provide [internet access within the public administrations themselves, town halls included](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-le-budget-du-patn-pour-lexercice-2026-arrete-a-21872-milliards-de-fcfa/>).

**Last twelve months.** At its fifth session on 26 February 2026 the Patn steering committee [recommended continuing and intensifying the extension of internet access and approved reallocating funds towards the connectivity component](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-le-budget-du-patn-pour-lexercice-2026-arrete-a-21872-milliards-de-fcfa/>).

**Local governments connected.** Town halls are [named with ministries and hospitals among the administrations to be given access](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-le-budget-du-patn-pour-lexercice-2026-arrete-a-21872-milliards-de-fcfa/>). The May 2022 appraisal planned [fibre for 22 ministries, departments and agencies in Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/804131654274496053/pdf/Congo-Republic-of-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project.pdf>), national and local government service providers alike.
