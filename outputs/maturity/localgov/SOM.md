# Somalia

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National ICT Policy and Strategy 2019-2024 set a goal for local authorities. [All local authorities of the federal government and the member states were to have internet access by its third year](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>). Costs were left to each agency. It made [the ICT Department of the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Technology responsible for connectivity in government](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The World Bank's Digital Economy Diagnostic of 2022 names the web-based financial management systems of the federal government, Puntland and Somaliland. It calls them SFMIS, PLFMIS and SLFMIS. It says each has been [implemented in all ministries, departments and agencies, state-owned enterprises and municipalities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099045403212411797/txt/IDU105f167fa1085214f8b1919f141249b1e8fae.txt>).

**How the office is connected.** The 2019 policy lists local authorities among [rural public services](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>). [Their connectivity is to be built in a co-ordinated way](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The policy that set the target for local authorities [ran from 2019 to 2024](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** The 2022 diagnostic says the finance systems are implemented in ["all" municipalities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099045403212411797/txt/IDU105f167fa1085214f8b1919f141249b1e8fae.txt>) of the federal government, Puntland and Somaliland.
