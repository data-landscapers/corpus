# Zimbabwe

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The government, through the Ministry of ICT, Postal and Courier Services, [donated 250 Starlink satellite kits to the Ministry of Local Government for local authorities, reported on 24 July 2025, with deployment, management and maintenance left to the councils](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2025/07/starlink-kits-for-local-authorities-zimbabwe/>). Manyame Rural District Council's chief executive said in April 2026 that [its US$491,039 digitalisation programme, connectivity included, is funded from the approved council budget](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/manyame-rdc-to-fully-digitise-by-year-end/>).

**What the office does over it.** Masvingo's Minister of State for Provincial Affairs said [the Starlink kits given to schools, clinics and local authorities in the province in the first half of 2026 improved internet connectivity and access to digital services](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/digital-information-centres-drive-inclusion-and-connectivity-in-rural-communities/>).

**How the office is connected.** Local authorities in Masvingo Province [received Starlink kits in the first and second quarters of 2026](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/digital-information-centres-drive-inclusion-and-connectivity-in-rural-communities/>). For the 250 kits handed over nationally in July 2025, [installation and use were still to come](<https://www.techzim.co.zw/2025/07/starlink-kits-for-local-authorities-zimbabwe/>).

**Last twelve months.** Manyame Rural District Council [began in December 2025 a programme of phased investment in software, hardware, connectivity and training, due to be complete by December 2026](<https://www.masvingomirror.com/manyame-rdc-to-fully-digitise-by-year-end/>). In the first half of 2026 [104 Starlink kits were distributed in Masvingo Province](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/digital-information-centres-drive-inclusion-and-connectivity-in-rural-communities/>).

**Local governments connected.** The provincial minister gives Masvingo's [104 kits as one total for schools, clinics and local authorities](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/digital-information-centres-drive-inclusion-and-connectivity-in-rural-communities/>).
