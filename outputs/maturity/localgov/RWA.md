# Rwanda

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Governance and Decentralisation Sector Strategic Plan 2024-2029, dated 14 November 2024, [costs an intervention giving local government entities ICT equipment and internet connectivity](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Policies/Governance___Decentralisation/Governance___Decentralisation_SSP.pdf>), with the Ministry of Local Government among those responsible. The World Bank-funded Digital Acceleration Project, running 2022 to 2026, [plans a government network linking institutions at central, district, sector and cell levels](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/27947/news/technology/inside-rwandas-plan-to-fast-track-digital-transformation-by-2026>), lawmakers were told on 7 July 2025.

**What the office does over it.** The civil registration system the National Institute of Statistics calls NCI-CRVS was [fully operational at district, sector and cell offices for 2025](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>), as it [was in 2024](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-04/Rwanda%20Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024_N.pdf>). Engineers at City and District One Stop Centers [process building permits in Kubaka, launched in April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122425135026850/pdf/P173373-d56cdb0a-3e0b-4268-aeaa-c9aa3e76e58b.pdf>), and sector offices [make all their financial transactions in the national financial management system](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Policies/Governance___Decentralisation/Governance___Decentralisation_SSP.pdf>), on the sector plan of November 2024.

**How the office is connected.** Ministerial instructions of 12 March 2012 [prescribe state-procured bandwidth delivered to central and local government institutions over the national fibre backbone](<https://rwandalii.org/akn/rw/act/min/2012/1/eng@2012-03-12>), each institution paying the access fee. Cells, the sub-offices below the sector, [have no internet connectivity](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minaloc/Publications/Reports/Governance_and_Decentralization/Impact_Assessment_of_Decentralization_in_Rwanda_over_the_Last_25_Years__2000-2025__Full_Report.pdf>), on the Ministry of Local Government's assessment published in September 2025.

**Last twelve months.** Births and deaths in communities [are registered at the cell, where before they were registered at the sector](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launches-new-integrated-civil-registration-system>), the Ministry of Local Government stated in June 2026.

**Local governments connected.** Connectivity [remains a problem especially at sector and cell level in peri-urban and rural areas](<https://includeplatform.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Digital-divides-or-dividends-Assessing-the-inclusiveness-of-basic-services-in-Rwandas-digitalisation-agenda.pdf>), a study published in September 2022 found.
