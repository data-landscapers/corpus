# Equatorial Guinea

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank's digital economy diagnostic of 2024 describes [a central network of the government, partly hosted by CNIAPGE](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>). It puts manual working at the local level down to [a want of funds and adequate infrastructure](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**What the office does over it.** The diagnostic states that [many ministries at the centre operate on manual systems, and that this is more pronounced at the local level](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>). Data pass between ministries [by hand, USB drive or e-mail](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**How the office is connected.** In 2024 the World Bank stated that [the government's central network does not extend to local government branches](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** On 18 December 2025 Gabon's Council of Ministers approved [an agreement to interconnect the fibre-optic networks of Gabon and Equatorial Guinea](<https://fonction-publique.gouv.ga/object.getObject.do?id=2632&mime=file-mime&object=file>), which are the two countries' national networks.

**Local governments connected.** The World Bank's statement of 2024 is about [local government branches as a whole, which the central network does not reach](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Backbone: the [central network of the government, the government servers and the government mobile service the country calls GOTA](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>); [CNIAPGE's private government network and its TIER III data centre](<https://btw.media/all/company-stories/profiles/cniapge-drives-digital-shift-in-equatorial-guineas-public-services/>); the [Sipopo Datacenter in Malabo, and the NOSi National Data Centre in Cabo Verde, which lists Equatorial Guinea among its customers](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>); the [interconnection of the fibre-optic networks of Gabon and Equatorial Guinea](<https://fonction-publique.gouv.ga/object.getObject.do?id=2632&mime=file-mime&object=file>). Application: the [civil registration of births, marriages and deaths, kept on paper at registrars', mayors' and local authority offices](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/equatorial-guinea/>). Central: [CNIAPGE's digital platform for administrative files](<https://btw.media/all/company-stories/profiles/cniapge-drives-digital-shift-in-equatorial-guineas-public-services/>); the [business single window the government calls Ventanilla Unica Empresarial](<https://www.state.gov/reports/2025-investment-climate-statements/equatorial-guinea/>); the [digitised map layers the statistics institute calls Base Cartográfica](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether a local government office holds any connection other than the government's central network, and how many local governments there are to connect. No owner, plan or finance for connecting them, and no event in the twelve months to 30 September 2026.
