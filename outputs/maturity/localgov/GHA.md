# Ghana

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Communications and Digitalisation [tendered the government network it calls eGovNet in September 2021, awarded it to AirtelTigo and Ascend Digital Solutions in September 2022 and signed a 10-year contract in December 2022](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>). On 3 September 2026 the Head of the Local Government Service [presented a programme his office calls LITISIP to give the 261 metropolitan, municipal and district assemblies a secure digital backbone](<https://kumasimail.com/ohlgs-celltel-seal-deal-to-turn-261-assemblies-in-ghana-into-smart-communities/>).

**What the office does over it.** The minister said in February 2024 that the network [gives the connected agencies, the assemblies among them, faster communication and access to government shared services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>). The Ministry of Finance's budget guidelines of 29 August 2025 [carry instructions to the assemblies with a part on GIFMIS, the national financial management system](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/news/2026-2029_Budget_Preparation_Guidelines_v2.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The minister said in February 2024 that the network [connects government agencies, the assemblies included, by fibre backbone, last-mile fibre and microwave links with satellite backup, on a 10-year indefeasible right of use](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>). At a signing reported on 3 September 2026 the Head of the Local Government Service [described the state the Service is moving from as "disconnected offices"](<https://adomonline.com/local-government-service-partners-celltel-networks-to-drive-digital-transformation/>).

**Last twelve months.** The Local Government Service [signed a memorandum of understanding with Celltel Networks, reported on 3 September 2026, with improved connectivity of local government offices among its expected results](<https://adomonline.com/local-government-service-partners-celltel-networks-to-drive-digital-transformation/>).

**Local governments connected.** The minister's table of February 2024 [gives the assemblies 274 of 274 planned sites connected, within 950 of 951 government sites](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>).
