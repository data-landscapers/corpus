# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Office connection

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: [the state financial administration system the country calls SAFE](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>); [a public information management system with a territorial cadastre, a 2025 target of the Príncipe regional government's 2019 plan](<https://principe.gov.st/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plano-de-Desenvolvimento-Sustentavel-da-Regiao-Autonoma-do-Principe-Principe-2030-PDF-1.pdf>). Web: [the yearly online publication of the Príncipe regional budget, an indicator of the same plan](<https://principe.gov.st/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Plano-de-Desenvolvimento-Sustentavel-da-Regiao-Autonoma-do-Principe-Principe-2030-PDF-1.pdf>). Public access: [the telecentres in the district capitals, set up under the G-net project](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>). Backbone: [the government network the country calls Rede do Governo, built with Brazilian cooperation from 2008](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/EstrGovDigital-STP-Jan2020.pdf>); [G-net, a data and voice network linking all ministries](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>); [the government fibre-optic backbone, serving 82 institutions by June 2020](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/EstrGovDigital-STP-Jan2020.pdf>); [the government's Centro de Dados](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=20>); [the Príncipe submarine cable, not in operation at 19 September 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf>); [Cabo Verde's national data centre in Praia, which lists São Tomé among its government customers](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>).
