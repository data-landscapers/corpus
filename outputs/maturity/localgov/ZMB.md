# Zambia

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Electronic Government Plan 2023-2026 [commits to extending the Government Wide Area Network to the 116 district administrative centres and local authorities](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf>). It [names the Electronic Government Division as the network's deployer](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf>) and [says implementation will depend largely on domestic financing through annual budget allocations](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** Smart Zambia's national coordinator said on 24 March 2026 that [data entry into the Constituency Development Fund system was under way across councils](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>). A survey of 2023 [found every local authority it covered undertaking e-procurement on the national e-GP platform, and 70% reporting constraints including unstable internet](<https://doi.org/10.51867/ajernet.7.1.89>).

**How the office is connected.** The budget address of 26 September 2025 [says Government connected local authorities to the Government Wide Area Network](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/2026%20Budget%20Speech.pdf>). SMART Zambia said on 7 July 2026 that [all local authorities in North-Western Province were on it over fibre](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/08/smart-zambia-expands-digital-infrastructure-to-reach-every-corner-of-the-country/>).

**Last twelve months.** Smart Zambia [called the connection of all 116 councils complete on 24 March 2026](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>), and [announced North-Western Province's local authorities connected on 7 July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/08/smart-zambia-expands-digital-infrastructure-to-reach-every-corner-of-the-country/>).

**Local governments connected.** Each government count [puts at least 93 of the 116 councils on the network](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/2026%20Budget%20Speech.pdf>), the finance minister's figure of 26 September 2025. Smart Zambia's chief technical officer [said on 24 March 2026 that all 116 were connected](<https://kalemba.news/local/government-to-track-cdf-transactions-payment-digitally-as-e-council-develops/>), as did its national coordinator [in a briefing published the next day](<https://www.zanis.gov.zm/?p=3243>). An article of 26 August 2026 [reports one council, Sinazongwe, given high-speed internet under an initiative that seeks to connect all 116](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/26/sinazongwe-town-council-goes-digital-as-smart-zambia-deploys-ict-equipment/>).
