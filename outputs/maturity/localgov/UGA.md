# Uganda

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** NITA-U, the state's IT authority, [provided internet to 70.1 per cent of connected local governments](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>) in its 2022 survey. It [runs the national backbone, which the ICT ministry funded](<https://nita.go.ug/projects/national-backbone-infrastructure-project-nbiegi>). The development plan for 2025/26 to 2029/30 [aims to extend high-speed internet to local government offices](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2024.pdf>). In December 2023 Parliament [authorised a Chinese loan for Phase V of the backbone](<https://www.parliament.go.ug/documents/3743/hansards-2023-december>).

**What the office does over it.** In October 2026 the finance ministry said [the IRAS revenue system had been rolled out to all 176 local governments](<https://habaridaily.com/the-grizzly-wall-behind-governments-heavy-burden-in-funding-local-governments/>). In November 2026 the ICT ministry said [all 178 use its new schools register](<https://pmldaily.com/news/world/2026/11/what-dr-aminah-zawedde-leaves-behind-at-the-ict-ministry.html>). A gender ministry study says [all local governments used the Integrated Financial Management System by July 2022](<https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/mdocs/Report%20on%20possible%20deployment%20of%20Government%20Payment%20Mechanisms%20in%20Social%20Protection%20Programmes.pdf>). In 2022 NITA-U found [email the main use of the internet, at 90.9 per cent](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** In 2022 [local governments gave staff internet over institution wireless networks and USB modems](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>). NITA-U counted [603 public sites on the government network in July 2025](<https://nita.go.ug/projects/national-backbone-infrastructure-project-nbiegi>), local government sites among them, with no count of local governments.

**Last twelve months.** On 22 September 2026 the local government ministry [launched its web-based accounting tool in Hoima city's two divisions](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-rolls-out-digital-tool-to-improve-financ-NV_241123_092026>). On 1 December 2025 the President [launched the backbone's extension in Kanungu District](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/nita/news-and-updates/nita-u-office-president-ministry-ict-and-national-guidance-collaborate-extend>).

**Local governments connected.** NITA-U's survey of early 2022 found [60.6 per cent of responding local government administrations had institutional internet](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>). For 2024/25 the Auditor General [found 18 of 30 sampled local governments on unstable internet in IRAS](<https://www.independent.co.ug/system-failures-blamed-for-shs52-billion-local-revenue-shortfall/>).
