# Uganda

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** NITA-U, the state's IT authority, [was the internet provider of 70.1 per cent of connected local governments in its 2022 survey](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>). The Uganda Communications Commission reports that [the fourth National Development Plan, for 2025/26 to 2029/30, sets a target of extending high-speed internet to local government offices](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/UCC-Annual-Communications-Sector-Report-2024.pdf>). On 6 December 2023 Parliament [authorised borrowing of up to RMB 1,050 million from the Export-Import Bank of China for Phase V of the national backbone](<https://www.parliament.go.ug/documents/3743/hansards-2023-december>).

**What the office does over it.** A gender ministry study states that [as of July 2022 all local governments had been migrated to the Integrated Financial Management System](<https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/mdocs/Report%20on%20possible%20deployment%20of%20Government%20Payment%20Mechanisms%20in%20Social%20Protection%20Programmes.pdf>). NITA-U's 2022 survey found that [local governments used the internet mostly for email, 90.9 per cent, and for services to the public, 74 per cent](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** In NITA-U's 2022 survey [local governments gave staff internet over institution wireless networks and institution-owned USB modems](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 22 September 2026 the Ministry of Local Government [launched its web-based accounting tool, which it calls SCART, in Hoima city's two divisions and supplied a laptop and a router for it](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/govt-rolls-out-digital-tool-to-improve-financ-NV_241123_092026>). On 1 December 2025 the President [launched the backbone's extension in Kanungu District, to connect the district headquarters and sub-counties](<https://oldsite.nita.go.ug/nita/news-and-updates/nita-u-office-president-ministry-ict-and-national-guidance-collaborate-extend>).

**Local governments connected.** NITA-U's National IT Survey, of January to April 2022, found that [60.6 per cent of the local government administrations that answered it had institutional internet access; 95 of 128 sampled districts gave full data, with 17 municipalities and 10 town councils](<https://demo.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2024-09/National%20IT%20Survey%20Report%202022%20-%20Final.pdf>).
