# Burkina Faso

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The government network, RESINA, is [built and run by ANPTIC](<https://assises-resina.gov.bf/images/Rapport_annuel_2020_ANPTIC_Version_finale_septembre_2021_compressed-2.pdf>), a state establishment under the ministry of the digital economy, and the State financed its reinforcement and extension with FCFA 346.2 million in 2020. The national civil status strategy for 2023-2027, steered by the territorial administration ministry, [counts the networking of principal civil status centres among its 2025 results](<https://www.wakatsera.com/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-2023-2027-plus-de-81-dexecution-financiere-enregistres-en-2025/>).

**What the office does over it.** ANPTIC says the connection gives interconnected structures, local governments' buildings among them, [access to the government intranet's computing resources, to email and to the web](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>) (January 2024).

**How the office is connected.** ANPTIC lists [the interconnection of local governments' buildings to RESINA](<https://anptic.gov.bf/nos-realisations/>), with internet access at no monthly fee over WiMAX or LTE, microwave or fibre (January 2024). In 2020 its regional antennas [gave the Dédougou town hall a local network and RESINA access](<https://assises-resina.gov.bf/images/Rapport_annuel_2020_ANPTIC_Version_finale_septembre_2021_compressed-2.pdf>) and connected sites of the Ouagadougou town hall by fibre.

**Last twelve months.** The strategy's results for 2025, validated on 25 June 2026, report [78 principal civil status centres networked and the declaration software deployed in 30 principal and 140 secondary centres](<https://www.wakatsera.com/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-2023-2027-plus-de-81-dexecution-financiere-enregistres-en-2025/>); the ministry names insufficient financing and too few qualified staff as brakes.

**Local governments connected.** The territorial administration ministry gives [78 principal civil status centres networked in 2025](<https://www.wakatsera.com/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-2023-2027-plus-de-81-dexecution-financiere-enregistres-en-2025/>). ANPTIC's report for 2020 names [five town halls on RESINA or being put on it](<https://assises-resina.gov.bf/images/Rapport_annuel_2020_ANPTIC_Version_finale_septembre_2021_compressed-2.pdf>): Dédougou, Fada N'Gourma, Tenkodogo, Manga and Ouagadougou.
