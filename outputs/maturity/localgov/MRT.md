# Mauritania

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The PEFA assessment of public financial management of January 2025 names [the local finance directorate and the directorate-general of local authorities as deploying the system the country calls SIGeL in all local authorities](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** From 2022 the regions and communes [began using SIGeL, the integrated local finance management system, for revenue and expenditure, assets, general accounting and reporting, giving the supervising directorates faster and timely access to their financial data](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>), the assessment states.

**How the office is connected.** Until 2022 the local authorities [ran their budgets in the off-line system El Khazin](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>), on the assessment's account.

**Last twelve months.** At the end of 2023 the two directorates [were in the final phase of deploying SIGeL in all local authorities](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>), the assessment of January 2025 records.

**Local governments connected.** SIGeL [was deployed in 90 per cent of local authorities at the end of 2023, against 232 authorities, 13 regions and 219 communes, at the end of 2022](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>), the assessment states, [a share of authorities with the system deployed, regions included](<https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2025-01/PEFA%20MAURITANIE%20RAPPORT%20FINAL%20Version%20d%C3%A9finitive-janvier%202025.pdf>).
