# Sudan

## Office connection

***Also on file***. Upper tier: Khartoum State's [government fibre and internet network](<https://alakhbar-alsudan.com/?p=45974>) and its [standard systems and cloud data store](<https://alakhbar-alsudan.com/?p=45974>). The upper tier also includes the [state information centres and state informatics projects](<https://nic.gov.sd/public/departments>), coordinated by the National Information Centre. It also includes the [states' network](<https://nic.gov.sd/news/details/6>), which a meeting of 22 November 2022 recommended strengthening. It also includes the [Isali electronic collection and payment system](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/taazyza-lltaaaon-almaly-mdyr-aaam-maly-alkdarf-ystkbl-ofd-olay-ksla-lbhth-alyat-althsyl-alalktrony-201>) of Gadarif State. The Gadarif State information centre's [network link between collection points](<https://www.suna.sd/posts/taazyza-lltaaaon-almaly-mdyr-aaam-maly-alkdarf-ystkbl-ofd-olay-ksla-lbhth-alyat-althsyl-alalktrony-201>) was shown to a Kassala State delegation on 3 May 2026. The upper tier also includes Northern State's [Isali electronic payment project](<https://sudanplatform.net/134522>), put at 95 per cent complete in June 2026. It also includes the [websites of the 18 state governments](<https://learning-gate.com/index.php/2576-8484/article/download/3004/1130/4518>), 89 per cent of them reachable from February to April 2024. It also includes the [Khartoum state government portal](<https://learning-gate.com/index.php/2576-8484/article/download/3004/1130/4518>). Application: the [Isali electronic collection and payment system at Abu Hujar locality](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1809351>), in its land offices and collection departments from 7 July 2026. Public access: the [universal access centres](<https://www.unescwa.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/Sudan-11-A.pdf>) funded by the Informatics Support Fund, as described in 2011. Backbone: the National Information Centre's [National Information Network](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2016/Content/AgendaFiles/document/14d93639-cc47-4322-b464-5d97518a6653/NIC_SUDAN.pdf>), its [National Data Center](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2016/Content/AgendaFiles/document/14d93639-cc47-4322-b464-5d97518a6653/NIC_SUDAN.pdf>), launched in 2010, and its [National Internet Exchange Point](<https://www.itu.int/net4/wsis/forum/2016/Content/AgendaFiles/document/14d93639-cc47-4322-b464-5d97518a6653/NIC_SUDAN.pdf>). The backbone also includes the [Sudatel Data Centre Khartoum](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>), operational since 2012. It also includes Sudatel's [East-Central and West-Central backbone routes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099450206282323160/pdf/IDU00f2d51f40fd4a04e630b0b60d27b1a4f548b.pdf>), not operational for cross-border traffic in 2022. Central: the Sudan Police's [public service complexes](<https://sudan4news.com/?p=35463>) and its [traffic licensing centres and national vehicle register](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>). Central systems also include the police's [traffic lights and surveillance cameras in Khartoum State](<https://sudantribune.net/article/316412>). They also include the Ministry of Interior's [centralised electronic civil registry](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>). They also include the [national email](<https://nic.gov.sd/news/details/6>), whose use the meeting of 22 November 2022 recommended making mandatory.

***Not found***. No source puts a locality office on a connection, names what an office does over one or counts the localities connected. The one statement found is a meeting's recommendation of 22 November 2022. It asked that Sudatel connect by fibre the localities that ask, at their own cost. No locality is reported connected.
