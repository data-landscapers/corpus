# Mali

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Council of Ministers of 1 June 2022 [adopted a draft decree approving the contract for the Projet d'Accès au service de télécommunication des collectivités territoriales, awarded to SIMO TELECOM for CFA 9,407,295,000 over twelve months](<http://news.abamako.com/h/270935.html>). Its communiqué states that the project is [entirely financed by the Agence de Gestion du Fonds d'Accès Universel](<http://news.abamako.com/h/270935.html>).

**What the office does over it.** The 2022 project is [to put telecommunication infrastructure in place at the level of the collectivités territoriales and to give the Administration its own secure, autonomous network](<http://news.abamako.com/h/270935.html>).

**How the office is connected.** The ministry's draft Mali Numérique 2020 strategy of November 2014 counts the [connection of certain communes as a result achieved under the government's 2005 national ICT policy, beside the administration's intranet and the equipping and connection of certain schools](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The contract for the collectivités' telecommunication network was [approved on 1 June 2022, to run twelve months](<http://news.abamako.com/h/270935.html>).

**Local governments connected.** The November 2014 strategy lists the [connection of certain communes among the few results obtained under the 2005 policy](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).
