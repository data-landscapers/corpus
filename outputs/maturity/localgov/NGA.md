# Nigeria

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank's BRIDGE project, approved on 6 October 2025, [sets a target of 3,428 local government administration offices on broadband by September 2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>). The Bank [names the Federal Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy as implementing agency](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>); a company in which private investors are to hold the majority, [not yet incorporated on 4 August 2026](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/04/nigerias-project-bridge-targets-90000km-fibre-network-by-2030/>), is to build the fibre. Of the Bank's US$500 million credit, [nothing was disbursed at 24 July 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>).

**What the office does over it.** The Bank's results framework [records the offices as connected to broadband internet](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>) at 17 December 2025. Local government area offices [enter health facilities' monthly paper returns into the national DHIS2](<https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/NHMIS-Annual-Report-2021-Designed.pdf>), on the Federal Ministry of Health's report for 2021.

**How the office is connected.** The Bank [counts the local government administration offices themselves as facilities connected to broadband](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012026134023499/txt/P508383-6acad398-4988-4bcd-ba9e-6952f14355ec.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** The credit was [signed on 30 December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012026134023499/txt/P508383-6acad398-4988-4bcd-ba9e-6952f14355ec.txt>) and the project [declared effective on 27 April 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>). On 22 September 2026 the communications minister [cut the delivery timeline from five years to three, with cable laying to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026](<https://techsoma.africa/nigeria-project-bridge-fibre-network-2028/>).

**Local governments connected.** The World Bank [counts 510 local government administration offices connected to broadband at 17 December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072426133520910/txt/P508383-134fae3d-1255-4dae-b619-aacd0944fa2f.txt>), the same number as in July 2025.
