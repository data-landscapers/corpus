# Sierra Leone

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Finance [reports the roll-out to the councils as its own work](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>) and [procured the councils' internet service itself](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026135010027/pdf/P172492-9bfaf78e-7d6d-4986-8104-9956e09b9d12.pdf>), on a contract signed on 12 August 2025 and [charged to a World Bank grant](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026135010027/pdf/P172492-9bfaf78e-7d6d-4986-8104-9956e09b9d12.pdf>). The National Digital Development Strategy of December 2022 [sets 2027 for extending broadband to all government institutions, local councils among them](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Digital-Development-Strategy-TRE-v22-3-2023.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** PFM Smart, the ministry's name for a web-based, real-time accounting system, [went live in all 21 local councils in the first quarter of 2026](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>). At Freetown City Council in 2025 [transactions were processed live and payment instructions printed from the system](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The roll-out included the [provision of an internet facility for 21 local councils and a local area network at 21](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>), by the ministry's report. The ministry [hired a provider of internet services for the local councils](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026135010027/pdf/P172492-9bfaf78e-7d6d-4986-8104-9956e09b9d12.pdf>), to support the accounting system. In 2023 seven councils outside Freetown [reached the internet through individually provided modems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121923145172799/txt/P1719901b3798c0ff1bba4194f7c3ff8e6e.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** In the first quarter of 2026 [the system went live in all 21 councils, with solar panels and batteries installed at 18](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>). The internet contract is [shown as completed on 18 November 2025, ahead of a planned 26 February 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063026135010027/pdf/P172492-9bfaf78e-7d6d-4986-8104-9956e09b9d12.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** [21 of 21 local councils](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>), by the Ministry of Finance's count for the first quarter of 2026. In 2025 [one council, Freetown City Council, was live](<https://mof.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Public-Financial-Management-Report-2025.pdf>).
