# Niger

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Projet Villages Intelligents is [run by a project management unit under a steering committee chaired by the national agency ANSI](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). It is [financed by the State of Niger and the World Bank at USD 100 million](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). [It closes on 23 October 2026](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). The communications minister said in March 2025 that [a programme to connect all communes awaited the adoption of a national digital policy](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/>).

**What the office does over it.** The Council of Ministers made a statement on 22 April 2026. It said [the computerised national civil status register is operational in 102 communes](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1143-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-22-avril-2026>).

**How the office is connected.** The project [extended broadband to communes as public service provision, alongside health centres and schools](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). Le Sahel reports [the broadband connectivity of the mairies delivered to the town halls by the project](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>).

**Last twelve months.** [The mairies' connectivity was officially received at a ceremony on 6 May 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>). The communications minister and the World Bank's resident representative were present.

**Local governments connected.** The news agency ANP published a report on 15 April 2026. In it the project said [broadband internet had been extended to 266 communes, 339 health centres and 298 schools](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>).

***Also on file***. Application: the [plateforme digitale de gestion des faits d'état civil](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil/>), in service in 100 communes, and the planned [Registre National d'Etat Civil](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>). Applications also include the [computerisation of civil status in the communes](<https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/>), which UNICEF puts at 68% of communes. They also include the [INCI](<https://actuniger.com/societe/20979-etat-civil-le-niger-revoit-sa-strategie-2023-2027-pour-eradiquer-linvisibilite-de-32-des-enfants.html>) solution, in 103 communes at 7 May 2025, and the [birth-record digitisation pilot at Filingué](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/niger-starts-pilot-under-wuri-project-to-issue-birth-certificates-to-21k-under-fives>). They also include Naneye Yarda, the municipal tax platform of the [Ville de Niamey](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>) and, since 11 December 2025, of the [Commune urbaine de Diffa](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>). They also include the Ville de Niamey's [Niamey-Yarda](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>) platform. Public access: the [124 Centres Numériques Multiservices](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>) opened since 2022. Backbone: the [intranet gouvernemental](<https://www.actuniger.com/politique/22383-ministere-de-la-communication-et-des-nti-le-nouveau-ministre-hamani-kargne-officiellement-installe.html>), stated as a ministerial priority on 22 September 2026. The backbone also includes the [National Backbone Optical Cable Network](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>) of more than 2,275 km and the [Point d'Echange Internet](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). It also includes the [Data Center national and 1,031 km of cross-border fibre](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/>), both under construction at March 2025. Central: the [Registre National de la Population](<https://anp.ne/caravane-nationale-de-sensibilisation-du-projet-wuri-letape-de-niamey-officiellement-lancee/>) and the [Registre National des Nationalités and the Intranet du Ministère de la Justice](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>). Central systems also include the national identification agency's planned [mini centre de données](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>) and the [guichets uniques d'état civil](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil/>). They also include the [national digital identity pilot in four communes](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/bioenable-powers-niger-pilot-for-mosip-based-national-digital-id>).

***Not found***. No total of communes is given beside the 266, and nothing says that the commune offices themselves are on the connection or what they do over it. The civil status register is not said to be reached online.
