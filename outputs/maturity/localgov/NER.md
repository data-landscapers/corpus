# Niger

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Projet Villages Intelligents is [run by a project management unit under a steering committee chaired by the national agency ANSI](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>), and is [financed by the State of Niger and the World Bank at USD 100 million, closing on 23 October 2026](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>). The communications minister said in March 2025 that [a programme to connect all communes awaited the adoption of a national digital policy](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/>).

**What the office does over it.** The Council of Ministers stated on 22 April 2026 that [the computerised national civil status register is operational in 102 communes](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1143-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-22-avril-2026>).

**How the office is connected.** The project [extended broadband to communes as public service provision, alongside health centres and schools](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>), and Le Sahel reports [the broadband connectivity of the mairies delivered to the town halls by the project](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>).

**Last twelve months.** [The mairies' connectivity was officially received at a ceremony on 6 May 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>), in the presence of the communications minister and the World Bank's resident representative.

**Local governments connected.** The project told the news agency ANP, in a report published on 15 April 2026, that [broadband internet had been extended to 266 communes, 339 health centres and 298 schools](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: the [plateforme digitale de gestion des faits d'état civil](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil/>), in service in 100 communes; the planned [Registre National d'Etat Civil](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>); the [computerisation of civil status in the communes](<https://anp.ne/tahoua-lancement-des-activites-commemoratives-de-la-journee-africaine-de-letat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-letat/>), which UNICEF puts at 68% of communes; the [INCI](<https://actuniger.com/societe/20979-etat-civil-le-niger-revoit-sa-strategie-2023-2027-pour-eradiquer-linvisibilite-de-32-des-enfants.html>) solution, in 103 communes at 7 May 2025; the [birth-record digitisation pilot at Filingué](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/niger-starts-pilot-under-wuri-project-to-issue-birth-certificates-to-21k-under-fives>); Naneye Yarda, the municipal tax platform of the [Ville de Niamey](<https://www.lesahel.org/lancement-du-projet-naneye-yarda-renforcer-la-modernisation-et-lefficacite-des-services-publics-municipaux/>) and, since 11 December 2025, of the [Commune urbaine de Diffa](<https://www.undp.org/fr/niger/actualites/diffa-numerise-limpot-local-avec-naneye-yarda>); and the Ville de Niamey's [Niamey-Yarda](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-officielle-de-reception-de-la-connectivite-des-mairies-une-etape-structurante-dans-la-transformation-numerique-des-collectivites-territoriales-au-niger/>) platform. Public access: the [124 Centres Numériques Multiservices](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>) opened since 2022. Backbone: the [intranet gouvernemental](<https://www.actuniger.com/politique/22383-ministere-de-la-communication-et-des-nti-le-nouveau-ministre-hamani-kargne-officiellement-installe.html>), stated as a ministerial priority on 22 September 2026; the [National Backbone Optical Cable Network](<https://china.aiddata.org/projects/58683/>) of more than 2,275 km; the [Point d'Echange Internet](<https://anp.ne/niger-grace-aux-infrastructures-adaptees-fournies-par-le-pvi-plus-de-900-localites-accedent-a-une-connectivite-numerique-haut-debit/>); and the [Data Center national and 1,031 km of cross-border fibre](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-accorde-a-la-rtn-par-le-ministre-de-la-communication-des-postes-et-de-leconomie-numerique-m-sidi-mohamed-raliou-nous-sommes-en-train-de-construire-un-data-center/>), both under construction at March 2025. Central: the [Registre National de la Population](<https://anp.ne/caravane-nationale-de-sensibilisation-du-projet-wuri-letape-de-niamey-officiellement-lancee/>); the [Registre National des Nationalités and the Intranet du Ministère de la Justice](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>); the national identification agency's planned [mini centre de données](<https://www.lesahel.org/wuri-niger-restructure-une-opportunite-pour-une-identification-universelle-de-la-population-au-service-de-linclusion-et-du-un-puissant-facteur-de-developpement-inclusif-et-une-dimension/>); the [guichets uniques d'état civil](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil/>); and the [national digital identity pilot in four communes](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/bioenable-powers-niger-pilot-for-mosip-based-national-digital-id>).

***Not held***. No total of communes is given beside the 266, and nothing says that the commune offices themselves are on the connection or what they do over it. The civil status register is not said to be reached online.
