# Chad

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank's appraisal of the Chad Digital Transformation Project, dated 4 September 2024, names [the Ministry of Telecommunications and Digital Economy as implementing agency](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>), puts its financing at [an IDA grant of US$92.2 million equivalent within a US$120 million total](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>), and provides for [last-mile connectivity to selected public institutions, local government offices among them](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>). The 2027 budget orientations, reported on 4 August 2026, [provide for generalising the public finance system SIGFiP to the collectivités territoriales](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/>).

**What the office does over it.** The national agency ANATS said, in a report published on 3 June 2025, that [its civil registration system links municipalities, among other points, which send their data by SMS to the agency for validation](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>).

**How the office is connected.** The finance ministry stated on 19 May 2026 that the equipment handed to communes under the project it calls PATN included [Starlink Business kits with antenna, router and Wi-Fi, monthly internet subscriptions and Wi-Fi access points](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-des-finances-et-du-budget-tchad_remise-d%C3%A9quipements-aux-23-communes-le-activity-7462569775844548611-TmdC>).

**Last twelve months.** The finance ministry reported on 19 May 2026 [the official handover of the kits and subscriptions, with computers, printers and copiers](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-des-finances-et-du-budget-tchad_remise-d%C3%A9quipements-aux-23-communes-le-activity-7462569775844548611-TmdC>), made [with the World Bank's support](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-des-finances-et-du-budget-tchad_remise-d%C3%A9quipements-aux-23-communes-le-activity-7462569775844548611-TmdC>) at [a ceremony chaired by the Secretary of State for Finance and Budget](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-des-finances-et-du-budget-tchad_remise-d%C3%A9quipements-aux-23-communes-le-activity-7462569775844548611-TmdC>).

**Local governments connected.** [The 23 communes that are provincial capitals received the equipment](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/minist%C3%A8re-des-finances-et-du-budget-tchad_remise-d%C3%A9quipements-aux-23-communes-le-activity-7462569775844548611-TmdC>), the finance ministry stated on 19 May 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: the [computers, printers and photocopiers handed to the 23 provincial-capital communes](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/20/le-gouvernement-dote-les-23-communes-chefs-lieux-des-provinces-dequipements-informatiques/>) under PATN, valued at 272,964,000 FCFA excluding tax. Application: the public finance system [SIGFiP](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>), in use for the state budget since 2021; the [civil registry digitisation project of the Commune of N'Djamena](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>), launched with ANATS on 23 September 2026; the birth registration application [TASDJIL](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/08/08/sarh-un-atelier-pour-renforcer-la-collecte-et-la-transmission-des-donnees-detat-civil/>); the [monthly civil-status collection forms](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/08/05/les-maires-des-deux-logone-formes-pour-renforcer-lenregistrement-des-naissances/>) the communes send to the ministry; the [ANATS digital civil registration system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>), with 97 hospital-based registration points; the planned [digital management tools for the collectivités territoriales and digital cadastral plans for the 125 urban communes](<https://tchadconnexion2030.td/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/20250827_Plan-de-developpement_Tchad-Connexion-2030_Rapport.pdf>); the [civil registration system managed by local governments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf>), essentially paper based at February 2023; and the budget and accounting software [SIM_ba and the street addressing of N'Djaména](<https://www.aimf.asso.fr/actions/ndjamena-mobilisation-des-recettes-et-modernisation-de-la-gestion-financiere/>), in the Mairie de N'Djaména's programme of 2013 to 2016. Public access: the planned [digital centres hosted in town halls, post offices and community centres](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>). Backbone: the [Chad National Data Center](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>), under construction in N'Djamena; the [national fibre backbone from Iriba to Doba](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/12/21/telecommunication-la-fibre-optique-inauguree-a-sarh-un-tournant-pour-le-moyen-chari/>), of 1,200 km; the [Chad-Cameroon cross-border optical fibre](<https://lejour.cm/cooperation-digitale-sotel-tchad-renegocie-sa-dette-envers-camtel/>); the [government eGov network, the PMICE project and the planned last-mile broadband for ministries and agencies](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf>); the [government intranet and messaging system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>); the planned [project fibre backbone and rural last-mile subsidies](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>); and the [Central African Backbone](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/296381468210254878/txt/620000PAD0P1160e0only0900BOX361487B.txt>) programme of 2011. Capacity: the [partnership of the Commune d'Abéché with the telecommunications ministry's provincial delegation and ADETIC](<https://www.alwihdainfo.com/tchad-la-commune-dabeche-et-les-telecommunications-unissent-leurs-forces-pour-accelerer-la-digitalisation/>), of 15 September 2026; the [training of local authorities' credit administrators at Koumra](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>) on 4 and 5 May 2026; and the planned [digital skills training for public servants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102502282341480/pdf/P180000048eed6040a13200ddaf8abe650.pdf>). Upper tier: the [office equipment and Starlink kits handed to the 23 provincial councils](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/01/07/le-ministere-des-finances-appuie-les-conseils-provinciaux-en-materiels-bureautiques/>) on 7 January 2026. Central: the tax directorate's [electronic invoicing platform](<https://lendjampost.com/facturation-electronique-la-commune-de-ndjamena-met-ses-fournisseurs-face-a-une-nouvelle-exigence/>), on which the Commune of N'Djamena's suppliers issue invoices; the planned [virement électronique normalisé](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/>); the tax system [E-TAX](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/05/05/mandoul-les-autorites-administratives-et-locales-formees-au-sigfip-et-aux-outils-numeriques-fiscaux/>); the customs system [Sydonia](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/>); ANATS's identity system [SIGPTS](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>); its [registre national biométrique](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>); its [Guichets Uniques d'Enregistrement des naissances](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/11/16/tchad-la-campagne-denregistrement-universel-a-letat-civil-des-naissances-de-la-ville-de-ndjamena-est-officiellement-lancee/>) in hospitals; the education ministry's [SIGE](<https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/stories/supporting-middle-tier-actors-chad-access-and-use-data-decision-making>), run through its provincial delegations and inspectorates; the school reporting pilot [EduTrac](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>); and the [fibre connection of the provincial governorates](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/12/21/telecommunication-la-fibre-optique-inauguree-a-sarh-un-tournant-pour-le-moyen-chari/>), reported on 21 December 2025.
