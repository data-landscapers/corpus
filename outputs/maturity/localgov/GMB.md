# The Gambia

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The rollout of IFMIS, the government's Integrated Financial Management Information System, to the councils is [a reform of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs, with the system run by the Accountant General's Department](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>). In 2024 it was [the first component of the local government pillar of the Public Financial Management Strategy 2021-2025](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>). The ministry's report for the second and third quarters of 2025 says [the government lacks funds for a localised IFMIS](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** [Most councils were actively using IFMIS in 2024](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>), on the ministry's report. In the second and third quarters of 2025 [Kuntaur Area Council was posting live in it and Basse Area Council used it for its accounting](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** By late 2024 [Janjanbureh and Kuntaur area councils had working internet connections reaching most of their offices and Banjul City Council had a 100 Mbps connection](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>), the ministry reported. In the second and third quarters of 2025 [internet speed and connectivity remained problems at Brikama Area Council](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The ministry's report for the second and third quarters of 2025 has [Kuntaur extending internet across its offices and Brikama installing IFMIS for all its users](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** [IFMIS had been implemented in all Local Government Area Councils by the end of 2024](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>), on the ministry's report, which names eight councils. Its report for the second and third quarters of 2025 [describes IFMIS in use or being installed at four of them](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: [tablet computers for Kanifing Municipal Council's property rate collectors](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>), and [new desktops and laptops bought by Kanifing and other councils for IFMIS](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/2024-PFM-Annual-Progress-Report.pdf>). Application: [Kanifing's Plus Code digital addressing system, with 36,000 properties georeferenced between July 2021 and February 2022](<https://logri.org/publication/implementing-a-comprehensive-digital-addressing-system-a-step-by-step-guide-inspired-by-the-experience-of-kanifing-municipality-the-gambia>); [Kanifing's property rates database and compliance mapping system](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/kmc-says-digital-address-codes-will-improve-emergency-response-and-property-tax-collection/>); [the National Digital Addressing Project](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/13/the-gambia-takes-major-step-toward-a-fully-digital-nation-with-launch-of-ndasc/>); [local and national digital address databases and portals, to be developed under the 2023 addressing policy](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/283c9ca7-80d2-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>); [the Digital Tax Collection System in use at Basse Area Council](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>). Planned in the E-Government Strategy 2021-2024: [local area networks, local government websites, Public Information Centers in local government offices, management information systems for local government units, Smart Cities applications, a Public Secure Network and public integrated data centers](<https://ovp.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/E-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY.pdf>). Backbone: [the Gamtel Kairaba Exchange Data Centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>); [the GAMTEL core backbone](<https://op.gov.gm/sites/default/files/2026-03/Final%20SONA%202026.pdf>); [the ECOWAN national backbone, completed in 2013, the national broadband network and the government network](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/caa6d521-5bad-11ec-9b31-029254d29bb1>); [the Government Cloud](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/ea5d1c6e-80d3-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>). Central: [the Single Window Business Registry](<https://easybusiness.gov.gm/>); [the Land Information System, under development](<https://www.voicegambia.com/land-disputes-consume-major-share-of-gambias-judicial-resources-reveals-minister-bah/>); [the identity system the country calls GAM ID](<https://foroyaa.net/gambians-welcome-biometric-id-rollout/>); [the lands ministry's digital workflow system](<https://www.voicegambia.com/2025/11/28/national-assembly-committee-exposes-severe-gaps-in-land-administration/>); [the electronic civil registration system and the National ID system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>); [the Functional Revenue and Payment Platform, the Electronic Records Management System and ASYCUDA World](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SECOND-and-THIRD-QUARTER-PFM-PERFORMANCE-REPORT-1-1.pdf>); [internet connectivity at four regional offices of the Independent Electoral Commission](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2022-09/Finalfinal.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share of councils connected is held after 2024, no rural share, and no plan covering 2026 or domestic budget line for the councils' connections. Whether IFMIS works at Kanifing is not held: its mayor said in August 2026 that it had not for two years, and the Accountant General's Department disputes that.
