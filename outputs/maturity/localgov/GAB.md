# Gabon

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Réseau de l'Administration Gabonaise, the RAG, [is a public network dedicated to the administrations and controlled by the State, activated at Oyem by ANINF, the State's operational arm for public digital infrastructure](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), Gabon Review reported on 1 April 2026.

**What the office does over it.** The connection at Oyem [improves the working conditions of the public services and prepares the progressive integration of new digital uses in the administrations on it](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), on Gabon Review's account of April 2026.

**How the office is connected.** The Mairie Centrale d'Oyem [is among the administrations of the town already on the RAG, a fibre-optic network that ANINF has brought into service there](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), as reported on 1 April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The RAG fibre at Oyem, [installed in 2010 and inactive since, has been brought into service by ANINF](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), Gabon Review reported on 1 April 2026.

**Local governments connected.** One town hall is named on the network, [the Mairie Centrale d'Oyem in Woleu-Ntem, beside the town's police station and courthouse, with activation to continue to other administrations of the locality and then to other public entities](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), as at April 2026.
