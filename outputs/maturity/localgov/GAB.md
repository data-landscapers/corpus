# Gabon

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Réseau de l'Administration Gabonaise, the RAG, [is a public network for the administrations, controlled by the State](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>). Gabon Review reported on 1 April 2026 that ANINF had activated it at Oyem. ANINF is the State's operational arm for public digital infrastructure.

**What the office does over it.** Gabon Review says the Oyem connection [improves the working conditions of the public services](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>). It also prepares new digital uses in the administrations on it.

**How the office is connected.** The Mairie Centrale d'Oyem [is on the RAG](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), a fibre-optic network ANINF has brought into service there. Gabonclic.info reported in June 2025 that [Akanda and Owendo are linked to the RAG by fibre](<https://gabonclic.info/digitalisation-des-services-municipaux-hermann-immongault-echange-avec-les-delegues-speciaux-dakanda-et-dowendo/>).

**Last twelve months.** The RAG fibre at Oyem was [installed in 2010 and inactive until ANINF brought it into service](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>). Gabon Review reported the activation on 1 April 2026.

**Local governments connected.** Three communes are named on the network. The Mairie Centrale d'Oyem [joined it beside the town's police station and courthouse](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>), as at April 2026. Activation is to continue to the town's other administrations. [Akanda and Owendo were named as linked by fibre](<https://gabonclic.info/digitalisation-des-services-municipaux-hermann-immongault-echange-avec-les-delegues-speciaux-dakanda-et-dowendo/>) in June 2025.
