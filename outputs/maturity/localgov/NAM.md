# Namibia

## Office connection

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: the [Digital Job Card tool](<https://unitac.un.org/en/impact-digital-job-card>), co-developed with the town councils of Rehoboth, Helao Nafidi, Opuwo and Rundu. Web: the [CityMender Namibia](<https://www.we.com.na/local-news-we/citymender-namibia-partners-with-windhoek-express-to-build-a-smarter-namibia-nmh015440-4-19157>) reporting app, a private company's. Public access: [public Wi-Fi hotspots at Radio Access Network sites and Rural Information Communication Technology Centres](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>), the ministry's planned [Community Access Points](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/1009181/MICT+Strategic+Plan+2025-2030.pdf/624fcb34-0078-177f-a2f4-c9772ca16a35?t=1770023679519>), the [multi-purpose community centres](<https://ajlais.com/index.php/ajlais/article/download/209/201/339>) planned in 2005 for each of the 13 regions, and [digital kiosks](<https://observer24.com.na/windhoeks-smart-city-vision-draws-mixed-reactions/>) proposed to the Windhoek municipality by an activist. City systems: the [Innovate4Water](<https://sgciafrica.org/namibia-project-uses-household-data-smart-meters-to-rethink-water/>) smart-water pilot at Ongwediva, a [N$245 million smart-city grant from China](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>), and the [smart city legal framework, beginning with the City of Windhoek](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-expedites-data-centre-smart-city-development/KWEBb7yLVARvmRjO>), that Cabinet directed in September 2025. Backbone: the City of Windhoek's [fibre-optic network](<https://observer24.com.na/cow-eyes-digital-makeover-with-rail-fibre-and-smart-city-push/>), the [18,719.5 km national fibre-optic network and the Universal Service Fund's Radio Access Network sites](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>), the public service's [GRNNet and the GRN Intranet/Internet Gateway](<https://ajlais.com/index.php/ajlais/article/download/209/201/339>), the [planned national datacentre, Paratus's Armada datacentre and the Virtual Space Data Centre](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-encouraged-by-angola-on-datacentre-establishment/JN1gPvOA8oPMjL6m>), the [Telecom Namibia Windhoek Datacenter](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>) and Paratus's [Data Center at Brakwater](<https://paratus.africa/docs/Paratus-Annual-Report-2025-online.pdf>). Capacity: the City of Windhoek's [People-Centred Smart City Strategy 2026-2036](<https://observer24.com.na/cow-eyes-digital-makeover-with-rail-fibre-and-smart-city-push/>). Upper tier: the [regional councils' network link to their ministry and the Decentralisation Extension Programme](<https://ajlais.com/index.php/ajlais/article/download/209/201/339>), begun in 2006. Central: the Roads Authority's [Project Control System](<https://neweralive.na/n13-million-project-control-system-launched/>), the [satellite ground-data receiving station](<https://observer24.com.na/mp-raises-concerns-about-data-control-of-smart-city-grant-from-china/>) handed over in February 2026, and the [regional offices of the Ministry of Information and Communication Technology](<https://mict.gov.na/documents/869282/6089169/MICT_2025_2026_Budget_Speech+Full+Version.pdf/9b15c163-e790-96b8-f1af-ebd9573367b5?download=true&t=1746624578199>).

***Not held***. The only evidence of a connection is a 2007 survey in which one unnamed local authority of two installs e-mail for some of its staff. Whether that authority is outside the capital, how many local authorities are connected, and anything since 2007 on governance or on the last twelve months are not held.
