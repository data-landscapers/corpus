# Guinea

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Enabel, the Belgian development agency, [issued the tender of 27 July 2022 for the internet connection of 10 pilot communes](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>), the notice [citing Belgium's bilateral portfolio of EUR 45 million for 2019 to 2023](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).

**What the office does over it.** The delegated civil-registry officer of Ratoma commune said, as reported on 2 September 2026, that his office [goes on registering births in the digital civil-registration system, which runs from a data centre held elsewhere](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). Enabel's final evaluation, from fieldwork in April 2026, says [civil-status officers and data-entry agents at civil registry centres work in the national civil register's central database, which it calls E-RENEC, with paper still in use in parallel](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>). A policy brief of November 2025 says [weak connectivity, high data costs and low digital literacy force rural municipal offices onto manual reporting and paper records](<https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/47281988.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** That tender was for [a service contract for the internet connection of 10 communes, closing on 17 August 2022](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).

**Last twelve months.** The [relocation of the system's data centre from the Maison centrale disrupted the service to commune offices, on top of a stockout of secure paper lasting about two weeks](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>), as reported on 2 September 2026.

**Local governments connected.** The tender of 2022 [names the 10 pilot communes: the 5 of Conakry, Kassa, Coyah, Kindia, Mamou and Tolo](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>). The evaluation [describes E-RENEC as the product of a pilot project and recommends a maturity diagnosis before any national extension](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>).
