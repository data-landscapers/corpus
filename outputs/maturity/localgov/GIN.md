# Guinea

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** Enabel, the Belgian development agency, [issued the tender of 27 July 2022 for the internet connection of 10 pilot communes](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>). The notice [cites Belgium's bilateral portfolio of EUR 45 million for 2019 to 2023](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).

**What the office does over it.** On 2 September 2026 the delegated civil-registry officer of Ratoma commune was reported as saying his office [goes on registering births in the digital civil-registration system](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). [The system runs from a data centre held elsewhere](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). Enabel's final evaluation draws on fieldwork in April 2026. It says [civil-status officers and data-entry agents at civil registry centres work in the national civil register's central database](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>). [It calls the database E-RENEC; paper is still used in parallel](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>). A policy brief of November 2025 says [weak connectivity, high data costs and low digital literacy force rural municipal offices onto manual reporting and paper records](<https://cseaafrica.org/images/posts/47281988.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** That tender was for [a service contract for the internet connection of 10 communes, closing on 17 August 2022](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>).

**Last twelve months.** The [relocation of the system's data centre from the Maison centrale disrupted service to commune offices](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>), reported on 2 September 2026. [This came on top of a stockout of secure paper lasting about two weeks](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Local governments connected.** The tender of 2022 [names the 10 pilot communes: the 5 of Conakry, Kassa, Coyah, Kindia, Mamou and Tolo](<https://www.piaafrica.com/fr/emplois/guinee/appel-doffre-marche-de-service-pour-la-connexion-internet-de-10-communes-et-des-autres-acteurs-du-projet-pilote-de-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>). The evaluation [describes E-RENEC as the product of a pilot project and recommends a maturity diagnosis before any national extension](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>).

***Also on file***. Civil registration: [E-RENEC](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>), the [ten-commune pilot of a national digital civil registry](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/the-modernisation-of-guineas-civil-registry-inspired-by-belgiums-experience/>) and the [digital civil status solutions the minister reports deployed in 28 communes in 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>). Civil registration also includes the [Registre National de l'État Civil and the platform for digitising civil registration](<https://www.foutanet.com/2026/08/11/journee-africaine-de-la-decentralisation-djenabou-toure-reaffirme-lengagement-de-la-guinee/>). It also includes the [Registre National des Personnes Physiques](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118842/GIN-118842.pdf>) and the [communal civil-status register kept at the seats of urban and rural communes](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118843/GIN-118843.pdf>). It also includes the [digital birth extract issued at commune centres](<https://www.guinee360.com/07/08/2026/sans-papiers-securises-il-ne-peut-pas-y-avoir-dextraits-amadou-konia-camara-explique-lorigine-de-la-penurie/>) and [biometric birth certificates issued in Sanoyah commune](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>). It also includes the [digitisation centres for civil registry archives](<https://matd.gov.gn/matd-oneci-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lappropriation-du-nouveau-cadre-juridique-un-defi/>) and the [civil registry management system the digital strategy plans to extend](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). Commune finance: [TOMPRO and SYSCOHADA for communal accounting](<https://www.anafic-gn.org/2026/06/03/matd-lancement-de-la-mission-de-revue-a-mi-parcours-du-pagl2/>) and the [public finance management software for local governments' accountants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062025132018695/pdf/P177095-6d05b45d-3270-481b-808f-f87a7e563f63.pdf>). Commune finance also includes the [integrated financial information system intended for all communes](<https://www.anafic-gn.org/2024/12/19/recrutement-dun-expert-en-communication-et-accompagnement-du-changement-pour-le-projet-de-digitalisation-de-la-gestion-budgetaire-et-comptable-des-collectivites-locales/>) and the [computerisation of municipal financial statements](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/743731468274209922/pdf/ICR29620P0912900disclosed0100240140.pdf>). The procurement platform [TELEMO](<https://armpguinee.org/projet-telemo-les-parties-prenantes-font-le-point-sur-letat-davancement-de-la-plateforme-de-digitalisation-des-marches-publics/>). Equipment: [computer kits for 21 local authorities and solar kits for 7 rural communes, with the commune accounting software they are bought to run](<https://www.anafic-gn.org/2025/10/07/acquisition-des-des-lots-lot-n1-kits-informatiques-en-faveur-de-21-collectivites-locales-14-communes-de-conakry-gouvernorat-et-7-communes-de-linterieur-du-pays-et-lot-n2/>). Equipment also includes [IT equipment given to 15 municipalities under the Third Urban Development Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/743731468274209922/pdf/ICR29620P0912900disclosed0100240140.pdf>). Public access: [20 regional digital hubs under construction](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026>). Backbone: the [national fibre backbone and the national Tier III data centre](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/guinea-reviews-2025-digital-sector-gains-targets-faster-rollout-in-2026>) and the [Conakry metropolitan fibre network](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/guinea-launches-nine-digital-projects-to-modernize-public-administration>). The backbone also includes the [state's integrated network, the Réseau Intégré de l'État](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), and the [state intranet in the digital agency's founding decree](<https://ande.gov.gn/mission-et-attributions/>). It also includes the [data centre of the digital civil-registration system](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). Central: the [one-stop shops for public services planned in the 13 communes of Conakry](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/10/guichet-unique-des-services-publics-le-ministre-bourouno-mobilise-les-maires-de-conakry-pour-rapprocher-ladministration-des-citoyens/>) and the [citizen service centres planned in the digital strategy](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). Central systems also include the administrative census programme [PN-RAVEC](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>) and the [digital platform for managing NGOs](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>).
