# Tunisia

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Communication Technologies' performance plan for 2025 [makes the connection of public bodies' sites to the integrated administrative network an indicator of its digital development programme](<http://www.gbo.tn/sites/default/files/2025-03/PAP%202025%20TICnv.pdf>), [targeting 98 per cent in 2025 and 100 per cent from 2026](<http://www.gbo.tn/sites/default/files/2025-03/PAP%202025%20TICnv.pdf>). It lists [the network for the municipalities among the projects financed through the communications development fund](<http://www.gbo.tn/sites/default/files/2025-03/PAP%202025%20TICnv.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** Since 24 June 2026 municipalities [issue building permits in the national platform Tunisia calls Taamir, from the application to the final signature](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2026/09/14/572745/tunisie-lancement-de-la-plateforme-numerique-taamir/>). The Centre National de l'Informatique states that [municipalities and municipal arrondissements work on the centralised national civil status database it calls Madania](<http://www.cni.tn/index.php/en/services/project-management/item/200-madania-en>).

**How the office is connected.** The Centre National de l'Informatique states that [workstations installed in municipalities are connected to the servers of the state's data centre](<http://cni.tn/index.php/en/services/hosting-en>). The 2025 plan [names RNIA3 as the administrative network for local collectivities and municipalities](<http://www.gbo.tn/sites/default/files/2025-03/PAP%202025%20TICnv.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 24 June 2026 [the Municipality of Tunis issued the first building permit handled entirely online and Taamir's extension to 16 municipalities began](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/06/24/dans-16-municipalites-coup-denvoi-de-loctroi-en-ligne-du-permis-de-construire-via-la-plateforme-taamir/>). On 31 August 2026 the Ministry of the Interior [launched a remote service for translated birth extracts whose requests go directly to the municipality of registration](<https://www.radionationale.tn/article/6a957fdc07af2054405fddb7/%D9%88%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A5%D8%B7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82-%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B6%D9%85%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%8F%D8%B9%D8%AF>).

**Local governments connected.** [Taamir was extended to 16 named municipalities on 24 June 2026](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/الاخبار/وطنية/154018-بلدية-تونس-الشروع-في-اعتماد-المنصة-الرقمية-تعمير-لايداع-ومتابعة-ملفات-رخص-البناء>). The 2025 plan estimates that [71 per cent of the public bodies' sites programmed for the administrative network were connected in 2024, counting ministries, local collectivities, municipalities and sectoral networks together](<http://www.gbo.tn/sites/default/files/2025-03/PAP%202025%20TICnv.pdf>).
