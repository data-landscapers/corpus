# Benin

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Government Action Programme 2021-2026 [schedules the connection of communes to the administration's network for 2023-2026](<https://beninrevele.bj/doc/6/download>), under the Ministry of Digital Affairs and Digitalisation, and costs its local-government digital project at FCFA 21 billion, FCFA 7 billion of it from the national budget. The finance ministry's 2025 report [allocates EUR 6.43 million of the 2021 sovereign Eurobond](<https://odd.finances.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Benin_Rapport-dallocation-et-dimpact-Eurobond-ODD_2025.pdf>) to digitalising the administration.

**What the office does over it.** The national land platform, which Benin calls e-Foncier Bénin, has been [operational since 1 January 2025 with the participation of town halls](<https://lanation.bj/actualites/reforme-du-e-foncier-au-benin-plus-de-491-000-parcelles-enregistrees-dans-12-communes>), and a parcel is sold at the mairie with [every operation done directly in the platform](<https://fraternite.bj/modernisation-de-landf-e-foncier-au-service-de-la-securite-des-investissements/>) (March 2026).

**How the office is connected.** Town halls [use the services of the Réseau National de l'Administration](<https://odd.finances.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Benin_Rapport-dallocation-et-dimpact-Eurobond-ODD_2025.pdf>), the state's own network (2024). The 2021 programme reported [a network dedicated to the public administration interconnecting 187 decentralised sites](<https://beninrevele.bj/doc/6/download>), town halls among the kinds of office it lists.

**Last twelve months.** The state agency ASIN [presented at Bantè's town hall the connecting of the mairie to the network](<https://lemondelocal.bj/bante-le-maire-daniel-fatchehoun-et-lasin-posent-les-jalons-dune-administration-communale-moderne-et-connectee/>), reported on 18 July 2026. On 11 September 2026 [a connection failure in several of Cotonou's arrondissement offices](<https://lepatriotebenin.com/index.php/2026/09/16/paiement-des-droit-taxi-quand-la-connexion-cree-denormes-prejudices-aux-usagers/>) stopped the registering of taxi-duty payments for the day.

**Local governments connected.** The finance ministry counts [12 new town halls using the network's services in 2024](<https://odd.finances.bj/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Benin_Rapport-dallocation-et-dimpact-Eurobond-ODD_2025.pdf>), in a country of 77 communes. KfW reported on 31 May 2023 that [187 authorities were on the network and that connecting all municipalities was the next step](<https://www.kfw-entwicklungsbank.de/About-us/News/News-Details_765120.html>).
