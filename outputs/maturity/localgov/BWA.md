# Botswana

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The premises Wi-Fi for council centres is recorded as [partnerships between BoFiNet and four local authorities](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>), in the company's annual report for financial year 2022/23.

**What the office does over it.** [District officials uploaded beneficiary data to the national social registry every month](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>) as at May 2021, the World Bank says; its review names the registry the Single Social Registry.

**How the office is connected.** By the end of financial year 2022/23 BoFiNet had partnered with four local authorities for the [deployment of premises Wi-Fi in their Rural Administration Centres](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The [four deployment partnerships](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>) stood at the end of financial year 2022/23, in March 2023.

**Local governments connected.** The four are [Serowe and Mahalapye District Councils and Bobirwa and Boteti Sub District Councils](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>), as BoFiNet lists them for 2022/23. The local government ministry ran the social registry pilot in [four districts in 2019 and 2020](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>), Gaborone, Kweneng, Kgatleng and South East, and had [uploaded beneficiary data in six districts by May 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>), the World Bank says.

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: the [Single Social Registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>) of the local government ministry; its [proxy means test tool](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>), piloted in 2016, and its [Ipelegeng MIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>), neither implemented by 2021; [the system at councils](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/83378>), used from June 2024 to pay central government's outstanding suppliers; the [National e-Procurement System](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/Ministry%20of%20FInance%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%2026-02-2026%20-%20Final.pdf>), planned to cover local authorities and expected at the beginning of 2027; and a [prototype location addressing system for Tlokweng](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275/257074>), built by University of Botswana researchers. City systems: Gaborone City Council's [facial recognition CCTV network](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>). Public access: the [Kitsong Centres](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>) and BoFiNet's [SmartBots Village Connectivity](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>). Backbone: the [secure government cloud at the Delta Digital Data Centre](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93395>), on which Kanye District Council was asked on 21 September 2026 to set up its systems; the [Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>) of BoFiNet and the [Sentlhaga Data Center](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>) of Botswana Telecommunications Corporation; BoFiNet's [national open-access backbone](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>), its [Fibre-To-The-Business](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>) and its [Data Centre project](<https://www.bofinet.co.bw/uploads/218/01M004DM8X2QP95FSJHKFK8XAM.pdf>), 96% complete in March 2023; the [Government Data Network](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>) and the [Government Data Centre](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/National-Broadband-Strategy-FINAL%28June2018%29.pdf>); and the [information gateway](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>) that the Digital Services Act, 2025 requires between public bodies. Central: the [Births and Deaths Registration System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>) and the [National Identification System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>); [OMANG](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099645103062256866/pdf/P1721750af4ddb0ce098d30b331412b0ab8.pdf>); [NARMS](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf>) and the [1-Gov platform](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf>), not launched in February 2026; the [ePayslip and ITW8 portals](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/Ministry%20of%20FInance%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%2026-02-2026%20-%20Final.pdf>); [GABS](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/83378>) and [Great Plains Microsoft Dynamics 365](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/83378>), planned as its fall-back; the [centralised digital platform](<http://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/sites/default/files/documents/digital_services_ACT.pdf>) for public body services that the Digital Services Act, 2025 requires; the [SmartSwitch](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/05/28/smartswitch-botswana-pioneers-dignified-digital-aid-for-75000-beneficiaries/>) smart card platform for food grants; [LAPCAS](<https://www.ajol.info/index.php/sajg/article/view/272275/257074>); the Revenue Service's [electronic billing system](<https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=955%3Atax-administration-act-2026>); and the [centralized Electronic Medical Record](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/HEALTH-Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20-%20FINAL%2004-03-2026%20Vol%20I.pdf>).
