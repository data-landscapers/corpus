# Côte d'Ivoire

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Digital Transition and Digitalization [implements phase 1 of PADCI, with the Republic as borrower](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19406>). The project [runs from 2025 to 2030 and carries US$45 million for broadband to targeted public institutions, town halls among them, in Abidjan, Yamoussoukro and twelve regions](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19406>). In September 2025 a separate project for the connectivity of town halls was [funded by the European Union and implemented by CIVIPOL](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** ONECI's civil-status project lead said on 11 August 2026 that [birth data entered by health staff on new digital terminals is transmitted in real time to the civil-status office, notably the mairies or sous-préfectures](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1786857181>).

**How the office is connected.** The digital ministry's plan for PADCI, dated May 2025, [targets public facilities, mairies among them, that have electricity but no internet, and gives the government's ambition as broadband for all mairies](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174792672528.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Côte d'Ivoire and IDA [concluded the loan agreement for PADCI phase 1 on 17 October 2025](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19406>). On 4 February 2026 the Council of Ministers [ratified the loan of 84,356,070,200 FCFA by decree](<https://www.bilan.gouv.ci/actualite/19406>).

**Local governments connected.** The World Bank's appraisal document of 18 September 2025 says the project [aims to connect more than 1,300 schools, hospitals, courts and municipalities to high-speed internet, phase 1 covering selected regions and phase 2 the country](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>). The ministry's plan [sets the connection of mairies for 2025 to 2030](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174792672528.pdf>).
