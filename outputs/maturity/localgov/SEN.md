# Senegal

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The national civil-status agency ANEC, under the ministry for local authorities, [runs the interconnection of the civil-status centres](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf>), with the state company SENUM SA installing the network equipment, as stated in August 2025. The NEKKAL programme that interconnected them was [financed by the European Union and executed by Civipol and Enabel](<https://aps.sn/le-programme-nekkal-a-permis-dimportantes-avancees-dans-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>).

**What the office does over it.** Agents at the civil-status centres, which the communes run, [register and issue acts in the national civil-status register](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf>), hosted at three national sites: 108,233 declarations from 1 January to 3 July 2025, on ANEC's figures.

**How the office is connected.** SENUM SA [put network equipment in on site at 383 centres](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf>), 235 of them in service and connected to the network, in ANEC's presentation of August 2025. The communal land offices were [not yet connected to the internet](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122035095/pdf/P172422-2ba4e9c7-2b8a-4244-889b-6b95d7f1d611.pdf>) in November 2025, on a World Bank mission's account.

**Last twelve months.** NEKKAL's final steering committee [reported 400 civil registration centres interconnected](<https://aps.sn/le-programme-nekkal-a-permis-dimportantes-avancees-dans-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil/>) on 29 June 2026. SENUM [launched the pilot phase of its PRODAP project](<https://aps.sn/la-phase-pilote-du-projet-de-promotion-de-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-publique-lancee-a-mbour/>) in six communes on 24 April 2026.

**Local governments connected.** The application ANEC calls NEKKAL was [deployed in 400 civil-status centres](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-les-maires-invites-a-mettre-fin-a-lutilisation-des-logiciels-clandestins/>), with access to the central database, at 5 November 2025, on its director-general's word. In the 138 communes of the World Bank's land project, [no municipal land bureau was equipped and operational with access to the land information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122125122530674/pdf/P172422-ab8683a5-9e88-4886-8cab-aa59ce4e604d.pdf>) at 21 November 2025.
