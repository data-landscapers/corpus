# South Sudan

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Maban County has two satellite terminals, one for the Office of the Commissioner and one for the Health Department](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>), on an assessment of eleven local governments that the International Organization for Migration (IOM) carried out from September to December 2021.

**What the office does over it.** [The Commissioner's office in Maban has a satellite terminal](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>), IOM found in late 2021.

**How the office is connected.** The Maban office is connected by [a satellite terminal of its own](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>), as of 2021. In [Renk and Rubkona the only terminal is the Health Department's, and the Commissioner's office and every other department have none](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>). Baliet, Fashoda, Leer, Pariang, Pibor, Raja, Wau County and Wau Municipality [have no terminal and report a need for one across all departments](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [IOM's assessment of the eleven locations dates from September to December 2021](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** [One of the eleven local governments IOM assessed in late 2021, ten counties and Wau Municipality, has a satellite terminal at the Commissioner's office, and ten have none](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: [county governments' computers and printers, which five of the eleven locations assessed in 2021 did not have](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>). Application: [the digitalisation of land records planned in Rubkona and Yei River counties from January 2026](<https://southsudan.iom.int/news/eu-iom-launch-new-initiative-help-displacement-affected-communities-rebuild-south-sudan>). Public access: [the Rumbek ICT Hub, opened on 8 September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/09/mtn-south-sudan-launches-rumbek-ict-hub-to-boost-digital-skills-and-innovation/>). Backbone: [the National Data Centre](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/op-ed-how-south-sudan-can-leverage-chinas-ai-path-to-bridge-the-digital-divide>); [the South Sudan National Data Centre in Juba](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>); [the second phase of the fiber optic project, with a design budget approved in June 2025](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/06/30/govt-approves-9-million-for-fiber-optic-design/>); [the planned first fibre optic network](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/04/07/govt-targets-fibre-optics-to-boost-ai-development/>); [the extension of the fiber optic cable network](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2025/04/05/south-sudan-identifies-key-pillars-for-ai-development/>); [the planned priority fiber optic links from Juba and Wau and the last-mile access program](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051223122541994/pdf/P17618114fa9167d1b80e142901b79718bf02b73d3c9.pdf>); [the government virtual private network for ministries, departments and agencies, with nothing connected in June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>); [the fiber link from the Ugandan border to Juba, deployed in January 2020, and the National Broadband Network, proposed and not built](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099450206282323160/pdf/IDU00f2d51f40fd4a04e630b0b60d27b1a4f548b.pdf>). Capacity: [the computer skills of county staff, of whom 52 per cent of those surveyed in 2021 reported basic skills](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>); [the familiarity of state institutions' staff with computer equipment and software](<https://csps.org.ss/new/uploads/pubrp6.pdf>). Upper tier: [the ICT systems of state ministries and institutions, which 36 of 86 interviewed reported having, with DHIS2 and HMIS, EWARS, ODK, payroll software and online calls among the tools named](<https://csps.org.ss/new/uploads/pubrp6.pdf>); [the connectivity of ministries, departments, agencies and states, unfinished in June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082124032516953/txt/P176181189ab9907919b721aee1da24ba45.txt>). Central: [the revenue authority's e-tax system, electronic work permit and e-visa services](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/03/10/south-sudan-tax-digital-first-strategy-to-combat-revenue-leakages/>); [the NCA e-Services Platform](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/02/13/south-sudan-digitizes-regulation-to-strengthen-revenue-collection/>); [the biometric registration of public servants](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/01/16/15m-biometric-drive-south-sudan-lays-foundations-for-digital-public-infrastructure-to-purge-ghost-workers/>); [the privately owned digital platform for tax and revenue collection](<https://www.sudanspost.com/what-is-actually-functioning-as-dpi-in-south-sudan>); [the planned digitalised land registry](<https://www.eyeradio.org/ministry-of-lands-set-to-finalise-key-land-managment-policies/>); [the National Land Registry and Land Records System proposed in the draft land policy](<https://paanluelwel.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/South-Sudan-National-Land-Policy-April-2023.pdf>); [the civil registry system](<https://www.eyeradio.org/eu-envoy-urges-south-sudan-to-make-nationality-registration-affordable-for-citizens/>); [the National Coordination and Operations Center](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-10/action-plan-ssd-oct-2024-5.pdf>); [the police Emergency Call Centre in Juba and Wau](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/news/crime-reporting-speed-dial-responsiveness-wau-police-emergency-call-centre>); [the health ministry's DHIS2](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/2023/Documents/South_Sudan_-_Marketplace_Poster_HIS_Status_and_Priorities.pdf>); [the satellite terminals of the Maban, Renk and Rubkona county health departments](<https://southsudan.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl1046/files/documents/20220412-ecrp-county-capacity-report_5.pdf>).

***Not held***. No count of connected offices across all the country's counties, no use named for the Maban terminal, who pays for it, and nothing on governance or on any event since 2021. The one assessment held omits Maban from its own recommendations section.
