# Eswatini

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Digital Eswatini Project is [implemented by the Ministry of Information, Communications and Technology](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>) and financed by [USD 65.0 million of World Bank loans and credit approved on 15 June 2026](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P508948>). Town councils meet their ICT costs from their own budgets, in [Ezulwini's accounts for the year to March 2024](<https://ezulwini.co.sz/documents/1742453492.pdf>) and [Mbabane's for the year to March 2023](<https://www.mbabane.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MCM-Annual-Report-22-23.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** Ezulwini Town Council [moved to Microsoft Office 365 for business email, installed virtual conferencing equipment and ran its telephone lines over the internet line](<https://ezulwini.co.sz/documents/1742453492.pdf>) in the year to March 2024. In Mbabane the council's IT Section [has automated municipal activities, mail tracking among them](<https://www.mbabane.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MCM-Annual-Report-22-23.pdf>) (year to March 2023).

**How the office is connected.** Ezulwini Town Council [carries its five office telephone lines over the office's internet line](<https://ezulwini.co.sz/documents/1742453492.pdf>) (year to March 2024). The Municipal Council of Mbabane [pays for internet among its utility services](<https://www.mbabane.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MCM-Annual-Report-22-23.pdf>) (year to March 2023).

**Last twelve months.** The House of Assembly [gave a second reading on 21 September 2026 to the three Bills enabling the project's loans](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P508948>). COMESA reports that from January to June 2026 [the project was in preparation, with its infrastructure assessment begun](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** One urban local authority, Ezulwini Town Council, [reports on its own office's internet line](<https://ezulwini.co.sz/documents/1742453492.pdf>) (year to March 2024), and the capital's council [reports paying for internet at its offices](<https://www.mbabane.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MCM-Annual-Report-22-23.pdf>) (year to March 2023). [Thirty-eight Tinkhundla centres are set to benefit from broadband](<https://independentnews.co.sz/45098/business/ict-trains-change-agents-for-e1-2bn-digitization-project/>), with rollout expected in 2027.
