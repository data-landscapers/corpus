# Angola

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** A presidential order reported on 8 June 2026 [created an interministerial commission, coordinated by the Minister of State and head of the Casa Civil, to reinforce the network of Balcões Únicos de Atendimento ao Público (BUAP), as the Government names its public service counters](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/pr-cria-comissao-liderada-pelo-ministro-de-estado-para-actualizar-dados-dos-cidadaos-maiores-com-prova-de-vida-que-passou-a-ser-obrigatoria-nos-periodos-pre-eleitorais-72374.html>), and [charged it with the network's power, computing equipment, communication systems and connectivity](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/pr-cria-comissao-liderada-pelo-ministro-de-estado-para-actualizar-dados-dos-cidadaos-maiores-com-prova-de-vida-que-passou-a-ser-obrigatoria-nos-periodos-pre-eleitorais-72374.html>). The Ministry of Territorial Administration [runs a national programme of V/SAT satellite antennas for commune seats](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-instala-antenas-v-sat-nas-comunas-para-funcionamento-dos-buap/>).

**What the office does over it.** Since 15 June 2026 [adult citizens confirm their electoral data at counters installed in municipal and commune administrations, feeding the national database of adult citizens](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/eleicoes-2027-arranca-hoje-a-prova-de-vida-para-actualizacao-do-registo-eleitoral-72520.html>). In Zaire province [more than 24,000 adults updated their data at the counters between 15 July and 14 August 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-instala-antenas-v-sat-nas-comunas-para-funcionamento-dos-buap/>).

**How the office is connected.** The ministry's antennas [are to guarantee communications and internet at commune seats so that the counters there can work, and ten Zaire commune seats with no UNITEL network were to get them in the days after 14 August 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-instala-antenas-v-sat-nas-comunas-para-funcionamento-dos-buap/>).

**Last twelve months.** [The data update began on 15 June 2026 with 254 counters in a first phase and 634 intended](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/eleicoes-2027-arranca-hoje-a-prova-de-vida-para-actualizacao-do-registo-eleitoral-72520.html>). The Cubango provincial government [approved on 18 December 2025 a project that provides for internet access in all its municipal administrations, to start in January 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cubango-prepara-transicao-da-administracao-analogica-para-digital-a-partir-de-2026/>).

**Local governments connected.** [Twelve counters were working in the 11 municipalities of Zaire province on 14 August 2026](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-instala-antenas-v-sat-nas-comunas-para-funcionamento-dos-buap/>), on the provincial director's word.
