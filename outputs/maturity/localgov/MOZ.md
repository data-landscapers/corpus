# Mozambique

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The European Union's action document of June 2026 describes [GovNet as the government's own network, with INAGE, the national e-government institute, managing e-government platforms and data centres](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>). Its 2026 action plans [to expand GovNet in selected districts, in support of the national Internet for All 2030 strategy](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>), at [a total estimated cost of EUR 7.1 million, of which EUR 5 million is the EU budget contribution](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>).

**What the office does over it.** Local administrations, with schools, health and justice facilities, [are still hardly providing digital public services, so citizens rely on in-person procedures](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>), the document of June 2026 states.

**How the office is connected.** On the same document's account of June 2026, GovNet [has connected about 1,000 public institutions and organs at central level and across 141 districts, and underinvestment in middle- and last-mile infrastructure leaves many local administrations insufficiently connected](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>).

**Last twelve months.** The EU's annual action plan for Mozambique for 2026, dated 17 June 2026, [carries a EUR 7.1 million Team Europe action to expand GovNet in selected districts](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>).

**Local governments connected.** As at June 2026 the EU document gives GovNet's reach as [about 1,000 public institutions across the central level and 141 districts, and says many local administrations remain insufficiently connected](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/2006980/32b32750cfcf0c9bab9747e7b5c49ebc/PRO202606172006968%20-%20Annex%202.PDF>).
