# Guinea-Bissau

## Office connection

***Noted, not assessed***. Application: [the Bissau municipal chamber's integrated management system, which it calls SIGEM-Bissau](<https://ang.gw/camara-municipal-de-bissau-anuncia-para-quarta-feira-lancamento-do-projeto-de-modernizacao-e-transformacao-digital-da-instituicao/>), and [Nha Terrenu, a land-mapping solution proposed for municipalities](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>). Web: [the Portal Municipal de Bissau](<https://ang.gw/transformacao-digital-deve-estar-ao-servico-dos-cidadaos-de-forma-a-contribuir-para-uma-aministracao-eficiente-diz-carlos-nelson-sano/>). Public access: [four Digital Public Squares with connectivity, at Farim, Gabú, Buba and Mansoa](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>), and [the I-Participate space in Bissau](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). Backbone: [the national fibre optic backbone, with 0 km deployed of a target of 830 km as at 23 January 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031826162536200/text/P176932-ebafb321-395f-4844-93a6-05071dd8a132.txt>); [the National Data Center, whose construction was launched](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>); [Cabo Verde's NOSi National Data Centre in Praia, which lists Guinea-Bissau among its users](<https://data-landscapers.com/lab/2026/06/10/africa-data-centres-v2/>). Central: [the civil registration system the country calls SIREC](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>); [the Centro de Serviços Integrados de Transportes at Gabú, inaugurated on 18 March 2026](<https://ang.gw/regioes-governo-inicia-em-gabu-descentralizacao-dos-servicos-integrados-de-transportes/>); [the tax authority's Kontaktu portal and its earlier SIGEF system](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>); [the education ministry's SIGE 2.0](<https://fecongd.org/pdf/2025_Relatorio_de_Atividades_FEC.pdf>).
