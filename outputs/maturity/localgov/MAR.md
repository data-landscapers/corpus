# Morocco

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Interior Ministry's directorate for local governments [is responsible for the programme](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>) under which GIR-CT, Rokhas, the civil registry system and Chikaya were rolled out to participating municipalities. That programme, the Municipal Performance Program, [is financed by an IBRD loan with AFD co-financing and closes on 31 December 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>). The directorate [drew up a digital transformation strategy for local authorities in 2021](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/strategie-de-transformation-digitale-des-collectivites-territoriales>).

**What the office does over it.** More than 100 large municipalities [manage the whole local taxation chain in the Integrated Revenue Management System for Local Authorities, GIR-CT](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725093035764/pdf/P169330-2f57d0eb-443d-4f4f-8ece-fd23a0d3699f.pdf>), the World Bank reported in August 2025. Rokhas, the platform for issuing building permits and commercial licences, [is operational in all participating municipalities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>), on the Interior Ministry's information of January 2026.

**How the office is connected.** The World Bank's identity diagnostic of 2016 reported [no official statistics on computers at the roughly 2,200 commune civil registry offices](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>), and said the municipal councils [equip those offices from their own budgets](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's status report of 30 January 2026 counts [59 of 97 participating municipalities with the civil registry back-office system operational](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** GIR-CT [was deployed in 969 of 1,590 local authorities of all tiers](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>), 60.9 per cent, as of 19 May 2020, on the annex to the Interior Ministry's circular no. 32. Rokhas [has been deployed in over 91 participating municipalities covering 16.9 million people](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>), as of January 2026.
