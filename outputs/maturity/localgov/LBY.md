# Libya

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Local Government's digital transformation project director [said on 2 November 2023 that official e-mail accounts would be created for the heads of administrative units in the municipalities, inside the ministry's electronic correspondence system, to replace paper correspondence](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=292708&lang=ar>). The system [is the ministry's own, run under its digital transformation project](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=292708&lang=ar>).

**What the office does over it.** In the three local authorities studied by Wynn, Bakeer and Forti, published in 2021, [the information systems are isolated in-house or third-party systems on departmental networks, and data is exchanged on paper](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). Sabratha's finance department [uses online services occasionally, to display and update information on the authority's website](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The study [finds internet access at the authorities' offices that is slow, often disconnected or not always available](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). [Sabratha's finance department has fifteen networked computers and an internet connection that is often disconnected, and in Rujban the internet is normally unavailable in community services and unreliable in human resources](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 [the ministry gave the mayors of 21 municipalities access to its electronic systems](<https://alsabaah.ly/%d9%85%d8%ac%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a8%d9%84%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a9/>).

**Local governments connected.** The 2021 study [covers three local authorities, Misurata, Sabratha and Rujban, by questionnaire and interviews with eighteen officials, and concludes for the three that access to the internet was not always available](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). In October 2025 [the mayors of 21 municipalities were given access to the ministry's electronic systems, including the local revenue system](<https://alsabaah.ly/%d9%85%d8%ac%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a8%d9%84%d8%af%d9%8a%d8%a9/>), which the ministry had [launched to six municipalities in May 2022](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/19194>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Equipment: [local area networks inside single departments in Sabratha and Rujban, with no network across an authority](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). Applications: [an electronic archiving programme in the human resources department of Abu Salim municipality, from 11 September 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=365312&lang=ar>); [an integrated database of state property for Jufra municipality, in preparation on 26 June 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=360304&lang=ar>); and, in the 2021 study, [Sabratha's Purchasing System of 2010, its Warehouse Management System of 2008 and its accounts management and payroll system, and Rujban's in-house systems for civilian details and for personnel and payroll](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). Web: [BaladiyaBOT, a digital assistant whose development Tripoli Centre municipality announced on 19 April 2026, funded by the European Union and Germany](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/04/municipality-of-tripoli-centre-announces-the-start-of-the-development-of-baladiyabot>); and [the three authorities' web portals, one-way and carrying basic information](<https://eprints.glos.ac.uk/9355/3/9355-Wynn-%282021%29-E-government-and-digital-transformation.pdf>). For the public: [coding academies set up in four municipalities under Expertise France's Raqam-e project](<https://www.expertisefrance.fr/en/projects/raqameyon-empowering-tomorrows-digital-innovators-libya>). Backbone: [Hatif Libya's fibre-optic backbone and access networks](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=347932&lang=ar>); [the fibre networks overseen by LPTIC, a state monopoly](<https://proceedings.open.tudelft.nl/DGO2025/article/download/1043/1077/1449>); [the Medusa submarine cable, inaugurated at Misrata on 10 May 2025](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/05/medusa-submarine-cable-project-linking-libya-with-europe-inaugurated>); [the National Data Transmission Network defined in the draft law of 2022 on the national information system](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%84/>); and [the General Information Authority's national data exchange network, linking national bodies' systems to its database](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/interoperability/>). Central: [the civil registry and national number system, run centrally by the Civil Status Authority](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/515274>), whose [257 offices had matched electronic data to paper records for 97% of Libyan households by 3 September 2024](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/450311>); [the Ministry of Local Government's database of municipal council mayors, members and deputies, in preparation on 27 March 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=353436&lang=ar>); [LyGIF, an interoperability framework proposed in December 2025](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Libya-GIA-Interoperability-Plan2.pdf>); [DHIS2, rolled out in 40 municipalities in 2023, with HeRAMS at hospitals and the EWARN and RASED disease surveillance systems](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742584-eng.pdf?ua=1>); [electronic disease surveillance reporting, piloted in 10 municipalities in 2024](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292741716-eng.pdf>); [the Health Number System, launched in Tripoli in January 2024](<https://libyaherald.com/2024/01/national-comprehensive-health-care-programme-and-health-number-system-launched/>); [electronic voter identity verification at election centres in four municipalities in August 2024](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>); and [the Social Solidarity Fund's beneficiary databases](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050006292273831/pdf/P1776930ba6a30000909306dca0ae8beec.pdf>).

***Not held***. No total or share of Libya's municipalities with a connected office is held, nor who provides or pays for the offices' internet. The period and the finance of the ministry's strategy are not stated, and the newest fact on the offices' own connection is of 2021.
