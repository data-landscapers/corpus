# Mauritius

## Office connection

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Local Government's report for financial year 2023-2024 says the system its councils work in was [built in house by a team of the District Council of Moka, extended to the other local authorities by Government decision of 16 March 2020, and is run with a Digital Office at the ministry](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>).

**What the office does over it.** The same report says [all employees of the twelve local authority digital offices do their daily tasks in real time in what the ministry calls the Council Project Management system](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>), and that the local authorities [processed 24,393 Building and Land Use Permit applications in the online National Electronic Licensing System in the year to 30 June 2024](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>).

**How the office is connected.** The 2023-2024 report says the ministry's Digital Office [works in real time with the twelve digital offices, whose employees can also work remotely](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Prime Minister told the National Assembly on 29 July 2025 that the back end of the Citizen Support Portal [connects 583 ministries, departments, local authorities, parastatal bodies and some state-owned companies](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Debate-No.-31-of-2025-UNREVISED-Tuesday-29-July-2025.pdf>).

**Local governments connected.** The ministry's 2023-2024 report says its I-Council project, [piloted at Moka from 27 August 2018, took three years to implement in all local authorities, and that 12 of the 12 it counts, five municipal councils and seven district councils, work in the system](<https://localgovernment.govmu.org/Lists/DocumentsLinks/Attachments/24/Annual%20Report%202023-2024.pdf>). Its [permit table for the year to 30 June 2025 lists all 12](<https://mof.govmu.org/Documents/2026/Annual%20Report%20on%20Performance%20for%20Ministries%20and%20Department/5-1%20Local%20Government/Annual%20Report%202024-2025.pdf>).
