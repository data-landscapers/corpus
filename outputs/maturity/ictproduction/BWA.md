# Botswana

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Botswana were 0.1832 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0767 per cent in 2021, 0.1196 per cent in 2022 and 0.1539 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP rose by 0.11 points. The share was lowest in 2021, at 0.0767 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** Dichaba Consumer Electronics, a Botswana-owned firm in Gaborone, [makes and assembles smartphones, laptops, desktop computers and televisions](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-industry/1008-58072-botswana-leverages-investor-aftercare-to-drive-local-manufacturing-growth>). Ecofin reported this after the investment agency's chief visited the plant on 8 August 2026.
