# Botswana

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Botswana were 0.1832 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0767 per cent in 2021, 0.1196 per cent in 2022 and 0.1539 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.11 points. The share is 0.02 points short of 0.2 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** Dichaba Consumer Electronics, a Botswana-owned firm in Gaborone, [makes and assembles smartphones, laptops, desktop computers and televisions](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-industry/1008-58072-botswana-leverages-investor-aftercare-to-drive-local-manufacturing-growth>). Ecofin reported this after the investment agency's chief visited the plant on 8 August 2026.
