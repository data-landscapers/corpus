# Kenya

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Kenya were 0.7088 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.8872 per cent in 2021, 0.9097 per cent in 2022 and 0.7063 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share fell by 0.18 points. At 0.5 per cent or more, the share is past the study's highest line for exports. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [Tech Africa News reports the Universal Service Advisory Council highlighted progress at the JKUAT Device Assembly Plant in the local assembly of digital devices, whose locally assembled devices are expected to support the Digital Hubs rollout](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/07/kenya-moves-to-boost-digital-inclusion-through-locally-assembled-devices/>). [Digital Economy Advisors' Ben Roberts describes EADAK as a Kenyan smartphone assembler and a natural first buyer for African-made chips](<https://www.digitaleconomy.ke/post/the-fifth-layer-what-will-it-take-for-africa-to-make-its-chips>). [Citizen Digital reports M-Kopa said it had produced more than 1.5 million phones at its Nairobi factory by November 2024](<https://citizen.digital/article/kenyans-shunning-locally-made-smartphones-over-quality-concerns-n373130>). [Citizen Digital reports that Kenya's first smartphone assembly plant opened in Machakos in October 2023 (East Africa Device Assembly Kenya Ltd), and that 268,000 locally-assembled phones had been bought by April 2024](<https://citizen.digital/article/kenyans-shunning-locally-made-smartphones-over-quality-concerns-n373130>).
