# Madagascar

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Madagascar were 1.6735 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 1.5068 per cent in 2021, 1.5548 per cent in 2022 and 1.6055 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.17 points. At 0.5 per cent or more, the share is past the study's highest line for exports. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** No source found since October 2023 says phones, computers or other ICT equipment are assembled or made in the country; [exports alone](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>) set the stage.
