# Ghana

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Ghana were 0.0537 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.1479 per cent in 2021, 0.2891 per cent in 2022, 0.2229 per cent in 2023 and 0.157 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP fell by 0.09 points. The share was highest in 2022, at 0.2891 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [ICT service exports were 0.0537 per cent of GDP in 2025](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). No plant making ICT equipment in the country has been found.
