# Ghana

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from Ghana were 0.0537 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.1479 per cent in 2021, 0.2891 per cent in 2022, 0.2229 per cent in 2023 and 0.157 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share fell by 0.09 points. The share is 0.15 points short of 0.2 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** A plant is asked about only once exports reach 0.5 per cent of GDP, so with [exports below that line](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>) none was looked for in this first pass.
