# Senegal

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2023, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Senegal were 0.4181 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.5807 per cent in 2021 and 0.7077 per cent in 2022](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP fell by 0.16 points. The share was highest in 2022, at 0.7077 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [ICT service exports were 0.4181 per cent of GDP in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). No plant making ICT equipment in the country has been found.
