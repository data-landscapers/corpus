# Mali

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Mali were 0.6433 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.8925 per cent in 2021, 0.8482 per cent in 2022 and 0.7316 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP fell by 0.25 points. The share was highest in 2021, at 0.8925 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** No source found since October 2023 says phones, computers or other ICT equipment are assembled or made in the country. [ICT service exports were 0.6433 per cent of GDP in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>).
