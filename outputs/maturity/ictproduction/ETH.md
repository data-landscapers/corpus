# Ethiopia

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Ethiopia were 0.0284 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.1481 per cent in 2021, 0.1227 per cent in 2022 and 0.1156 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share fell by 0.12 points. The share is 0.02 points short of 0.05 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [In February 2026 Industry Five Group said its Addis Ababa plant had started commercial production](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/02/jp-folsgaard-bak-building-africas-tech-factories-from-addis-ababa/>). It assembles laptops, smartphones and tablets, with 50 staff at first. Ethiopian Investment Holdings owns 49% of it. [Birr Metrics reported in June 2026 that a Danish-run facility in Addis Ababa assembles laptops and desktops](<https://birrmetrics.com/ethiopia-plans-local-laptop-tablet-assembly-in-digital-sovereignty-push/>) from imported parts. [In November 2026 an African Development Bank manager said Ethiopia's device making and assembly base is growing](<https://itweb.africa/article/afdb-backs-local-tech-assembly/j5alrvQAXgxvpYQk>).
