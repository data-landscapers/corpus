# Morocco

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Morocco were 1.5377 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 1.382 per cent in 2021, 1.5774 per cent in 2022, 1.6498 per cent in 2023 and 1.6469 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP rose by 0.16 points. The share was highest in 2023, at 1.6498 per cent of GDP. The share was lowest in 2021, at 1.382 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [Digital Economy Advisors' Ben Roberts says STMicroelectronics has run a chip assembly and test plant near Casablanca for decades](<https://www.digitaleconomy.ke/post/the-fifth-layer-what-will-it-take-for-africa-to-make-its-chips>).
