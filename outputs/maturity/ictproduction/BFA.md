# Burkina Faso

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Burkina Faso were 0.3871 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.312 per cent in 2021, 0.2846 per cent in 2022 and 0.4684 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.08 points. The share is 0.11 points short of 0.5 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [Horizon Industries, at Kossodo in Ouagadougou, assembles laptops, phones, tablets and household appliances](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/02/23/burkina-faso-un-accord-historique-pour-la-production-locale-dequipements-informatiques-avec-horizon-industries-la-premiere-unite-de-montage-dequipements-electroniques-au-burkina/>). The state holds 35% of it through FBDES. On 19 February 2025 the Council of Ministers approved a framework agreement of up to four years for public IT purchases from it. [In December 2025 Sciences Check confirmed it is an assembly plant](<https://sciencescheck.org/faux-cette-usine-ne-fabrique-pas-des-ordinateurs-portables-au-burkina-faso/>). Its own site claims 1,000 units a day, with Haier as technical partner.
