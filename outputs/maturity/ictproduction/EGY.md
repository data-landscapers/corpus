# Egypt

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from Egypt were 0.5644 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.3295 per cent in 2021, 0.3472 per cent in 2022, 0.3664 per cent in 2023 and 0.44 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.23 points. At 0.5 per cent or more, the share is past the study's highest line for exports. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology says 10 million mobile phones were made in Egypt in 2025](<https://www.amcham.org.eg/publications/business-studies/egypt-beyond-borders/121>). About 40% of their value was added locally. The plants include those of Samsung, Xiaomi and Oppo, as AmCham Egypt reports.
