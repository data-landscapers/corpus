# Guinea

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Guinea were 0.1474 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0156 per cent in 2021, 0.0337 per cent in 2022 and 0.3019 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.13 points. The share is 0.05 points short of 0.2 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [On 10 November 2024 the Prime Minister opened Guinea's first laptop assembly unit](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/guinee-une-usine-d-assemblage-d-ordinateurs-portables-ouvre-ses-portes-a-mamou>), at the Institut Supérieur de Technologie in Mamou. It was built with China's Green View and a local partner, Guinea Technologie Innovation. [The institute's director general says the line averages 500 computers a day](<https://www.africaguinee.com/dr-hamidou-barry-dg-de-list-de-mamou-on-a-une-chaine-de-production-moyenne-de-500-ordinateurs-par-jour-avec-le-label-europeen/>). Its first order was for 2,500 computers.
