# Cape Verde

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from Cape Verde were 0.3598 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.4368 per cent in 2021, 0.453 per cent in 2022, 0.4297 per cent in 2023 and 0.4477 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share fell by 0.08 points. The share is 0.14 points short of 0.5 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** A plant is asked about only once exports reach 0.5 per cent of GDP, so with [exports below that line](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>) none was looked for in this first pass.
