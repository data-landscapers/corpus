# Tanzania

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Tanzania were 0.0867 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0345 per cent in 2021, 0.0548 per cent in 2022 and 0.0496 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.05 points. The share is 0.11 points short of 0.2 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [TanzTech Electronics in Arusha has assembled smartphones, tablets, laptops and projectors since 2023](<https://dailynews.co.tz/arusha-electronics-firm-to-boost-local-tech-industry/>). Daily News gave its capacity in February 2025 as 40,000 smartphones a month.
