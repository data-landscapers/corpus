# Zimbabwe

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Zimbabwe were 0.036 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0074 per cent in 2021, 0.0248 per cent in 2022 and 0.0431 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.03 points. The share is 0.01 points short of 0.05 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [In October 2025 the President commissioned the PALPO Technologies plant at Bindura University](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/im-impressed-says-president-commissions-buse-industrial-park-technologies-production-plant/>). It assembles laptops, tablets and phones. At full capacity it makes 12,000 laptops and tablets and 3,000 phones. [In June 2026 it was selling smartphones from US$80](<https://technomag.co.zw/bindura-university-manufactures-80-palp-smartphones/>). [Avantis Technology makes laptops and notebooks at a factory in Msasa, Harare](<https://dailynews.co.zw/avantis-unveils-parote-1030i-laptop/>). By 14 May 2026 it had built about 25,000 of the 60,000 it aims for in 2026.
