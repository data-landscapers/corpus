# Comoros

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2023, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Comoros were 0.9279 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.5718 per cent in 2021 and 0.9259 per cent in 2022](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP rose by 0.36 points. The share was lowest in 2021, at 0.5718 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** No source found since October 2023 says phones, computers or other ICT equipment are assembled or made in the country. [ICT service exports were 0.9279 per cent of GDP in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>).
