# Algeria

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [ICT service exports from Algeria were 0.027 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0459 per cent in 2021, 0.0388 per cent in 2022 and 0.0342 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share fell by 0.02 points. The share is 0.02 points short of 0.05 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [On 20 August 2026 STI, a wholly Algerian-owned company, opened a smartphone assembly plant at Es-Senia, Oran](<https://www.expressdz.dz/2026/08/20/oran-inauguration-dune-nouvelle-usine-specialisee-dans-lassemblage-de-smartphones/>). It makes about 2,000 smartphones a day on two lines. [In August 2025 Sacomi began making ZTE phones at its El Achour unit in Algiers](<https://www.tsa-algerie.com/le-geant-chinois-zte-devoile-ses-smartphones-5g-fabriques-en-algerie/>). Its chief executive gave output as 900 a day, with 30% local integration at the start.
