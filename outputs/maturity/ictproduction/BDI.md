# Burundi

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from Burundi were 0.1266 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0951 per cent in 2021, 0.0878 per cent in 2022, 0.1317 per cent in 2023 and 0.1131 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.03 points. The share is 0.07 points short of 0.2 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [TECOO, a Burundian company at Buringa in Bujumbura Rural, assembles small mobile phones](<https://andikamagazine.net/burundis-tecoo-expands-local-tech-manufacturing-aims-for-smartphones-and-batteries/>). Its founder told Andika Magazine in December 2025 that it employs more than 70 workers.
