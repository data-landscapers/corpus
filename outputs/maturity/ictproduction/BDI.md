# Burundi

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Burundi were 0.1266 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0951 per cent in 2021, 0.0878 per cent in 2022, 0.1317 per cent in 2023 and 0.1131 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP rose by 0.03 points. The share was highest in 2023, at 0.1317 per cent of GDP. The share was lowest in 2022, at 0.0878 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [TECOO, a Burundian company at Buringa in Bujumbura Rural, assembles small mobile phones](<https://andikamagazine.net/burundis-tecoo-expands-local-tech-manufacturing-aims-for-smartphones-and-batteries/>). Its founder told Andika Magazine in December 2025 that it employs more than 70 workers.
