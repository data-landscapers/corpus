# Seychelles

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2024, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from Seychelles were 0.4726 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.6577 per cent in 2021, 0.5013 per cent in 2022 and 0.4732 per cent in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share of ICT service exports in GDP fell by 0.19 points. The share was highest in 2021, at 0.6577 per cent of GDP. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [ICT service exports were 0.4726 per cent of GDP in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). No plant making ICT equipment in the country has been found.
