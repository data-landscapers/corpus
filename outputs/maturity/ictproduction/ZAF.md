# South Africa

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from South Africa were 0.3994 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.1969 per cent in 2021, 0.2315 per cent in 2022, 0.3503 per cent in 2023 and 0.3808 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share rose by 0.20 points. The share is 0.10 points short of 0.5 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [MyBroadband reported in May 2025 that Mobicel is the only maker of smartphones in South Africa](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/smartphones/595479-the-only-smartphone-factory-in-south-africa.html>). It imports parts and assembles the phones locally. [Its Johannesburg plant has run since 2020 with an all-female team of 153](<https://www.citizen.co.za/lifestyle/technology/female-mobile-phone-plant-womens-empowerment-sa/>).
