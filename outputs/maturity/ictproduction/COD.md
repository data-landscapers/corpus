# DR Congo

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [ICT service exports from DR Congo were 0.0028 per cent of GDP](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>), by the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0069 per cent in 2021, 0.0028 per cent in 2022, 0.0024 per cent in 2023 and 0.0054 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share has barely moved. The share is 0.05 points short of 0.05 per cent, the next line. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** A plant is asked about only once exports reach 0.5 per cent of GDP, so with [exports below that line](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>) none was looked for in this first pass.
