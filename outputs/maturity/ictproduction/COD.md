# DR Congo

## ICT exports and production

**ICT service exports, as a share of GDP.** In 2025, [exports of information and communication technology (ICT) services from DR Congo were 0.0028 per cent of gross domestic product (GDP)](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). The source is the World Bank's series of computer and telecommunications services sold abroad. The same series [put the share at 0.0069 per cent in 2021, 0.0028 per cent in 2022, 0.0024 per cent in 2023 and 0.0054 per cent in 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). Since 2021 the share has barely moved. The figure counts computer and telecommunications services sold abroad, not equipment made or assembled at home.

**ICT equipment made in the country.** [ICT service exports were 0.0028 per cent of GDP in 2025](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/BX.GSR.CCIS.CD>). No plant making ICT equipment in the country has been found.
