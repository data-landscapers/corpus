# Ghana

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Ghana's minister [took the chair of the African Union's ICT committee on 29 October 2026](<https://au.int/en/speeches/20261029/auc-commissioner-6th-au-stc-communication-and-ict-stc-cict>), [in Libreville](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/8032/ghanas-communications-minister-confirmed-as-chair-of-au-committee-on-c>). He [was vice chair of Smart Africa's ICT ministers until March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Ghana [signed free roaming with Cote d'Ivoire in February 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>) and [with The Gambia in November 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Ghana, Liberia and Sierra Leone [signed their entry into the West African free roaming agreement](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. Ghana [concluded its agreement with Liberia in May 2026](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>). In July 2025 its regulator [hosted the West African regulators' working groups in Accra](<https://nca.org.gh/2025/07/21/watra-working-groups-convene-in-accra-to-strengthen-sub-regional-regulatory-cooperation/>). Ghana [takes part in Smart Africa's data exchange work](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5165/>). The head of its Data Protection Commission [chairs the working group on cross-border data transfers of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-must-protect-its-data-sovereignty-commission.html>), reported in October 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming between Benin and Ghana took effect on 1 July 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>). [Roaming with Togo and Benin was launched in October 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>). [Liberian subscribers have roamed in Ghana without charges since 17 July 2026](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>). Smart Africa says [a cross-border identity pilot with Rwanda and Benin works](<https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/storys/interview-herken-kone-smart-africa-digital-sovereignty>), reported in August 2026. No figures of use, joint decision or review are recorded.
