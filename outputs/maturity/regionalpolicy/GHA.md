# Ghana

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Ghana, Liberia and Sierra Leone [signed their entry into the West African free roaming agreement](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. Ghana [signed a free roaming agreement with Cote d'Ivoire in February 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>), [agreed bilateral roaming with Togo in November 2023](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>) and [finalised a frequency coordination agreement with Burkina Faso in August 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>). It [takes part in Smart Africa's data exchange work](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5165/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming between Benin and Ghana took effect on 1 July 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>), and [an arrangement with Togo and Benin was launched in October 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/connectivity/ghana-burkina-faso-tackle-cross-border-signal-interference>). Smart Africa says [a cross-border digital identity pilot between Rwanda, Ghana and Benin has been launched and shown to work](<https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/storys/interview-herken-kone-smart-africa-digital-sovereignty>), reported in August 2026. Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
