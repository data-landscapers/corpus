# Mali

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mali's regulator [signed a convention with the regulators of Burkina Faso and Niger](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. It created the Council of Sahel Regulators. In July 2026 Mali [was re-elected to the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union](<https://gouvernement.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-cm-n2026-30-sgg/>) for 2027 to 2031. In November 2025 its minister [signed the Sahel States' agreement on cross-border frequency coordination](<https://gouvernement.ml/signature-de-laccord-aes-de-coordination-des-frequences-une-etape-decisive-pour-lintegration-numerique-regionale/>). Mali [reached roaming agreements with Benin and Togo in December 2023](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). It signed one with Burkina Faso and Niger in November 2024. On 5 November 2026 its delegation [helped finalise the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>), with 16 of 18 deliverables final. From 2 to 4 November 2026 Mali's regulator [hosted the Sahel States' second meeting on coordinating military frequencies in border areas](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/aes-frequences-militaires-des-experts-de-la-confederation-en-conclave-3121278.html>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), which covers harmonised rules and free roaming. The council is not reported at work in Mali. No source found reports a roaming cut, a joint body at work or a shared service opened.

***Also on file***. [Free roaming across the AES](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>).

***Not found***. No source found says a thing the country agreed has been put into effect.
