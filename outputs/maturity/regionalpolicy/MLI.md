# Mali

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mali's regulator [signed a convention with the regulators of Burkina Faso and Niger](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. It created the Council of Sahel Regulators. In July 2026 Mali [was re-elected to the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union](<https://gouvernement.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-cm-n2026-30-sgg/>) for 2027 to 2031. In November 2025 its minister [signed the Sahel States' agreement on cross-border frequency coordination](<https://gouvernement.ml/signature-de-laccord-aes-de-coordination-des-frequences-une-etape-decisive-pour-lintegration-numerique-regionale/>). Mali [reached roaming agreements with Benin and Togo in December 2023](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). In November 2024 its regulator [signed a free roaming memorandum with those of Burkina Faso and Niger in Niamey](<https://www.lesahel.org/integration-numerique-larcep-niger-celebre-son-25e-anniversaire-avec-la-signature-dun-memorandum-dentente-sur-le-free-roaming-entre-les-pays-membres-de-l/>). On 11 December 2025 it [signed a roaming memorandum with Senegal's regulator](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1512-51396-senegal-signs-agreements-to-eliminate-roaming-charges-with-four-neighbours>). On 5 November 2026 its delegation [helped finalise the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>), with 16 of 18 deliverables final. From 2 to 4 November 2026 Mali's regulator [hosted the Sahel States' second meeting on coordinating military frequencies in border areas](<https://www.maliweb.net/economie/developpement/aes-frequences-militaires-des-experts-de-la-confederation-en-conclave-3121278.html>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** On 25 September 2026 Niger's regulator [said free roaming among Burkina Faso, Mali and Niger was already working](<https://www.radarsburkina.net/index.php/fr/politique/4017-cooperation-numerique-dans-l-aes-les-regulateurs-passent-de-la-coordination-a-l-action>), before the three regulators signed their convention. [The convention](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>) covers harmonised rules and free roaming, and the council it created is not reported meeting. No figures of roaming use are reported.

***Also on file***. [Free roaming across the AES](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>).
