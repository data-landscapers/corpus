# Mali

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mali's regulator [signed a cooperation convention with the regulators of Burkina Faso and Niger creating the Council of Sahel Regulators](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. Mali [was re-elected to the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union for 2027 to 2031 in July 2026](<https://gouvernement.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-cm-n2026-30-sgg/>), and its minister [took part in the signing at Ouagadougou of an agreement of the Alliance of Sahel States in November 2025](<https://gouvernement.ml/signature-de-laccord-aes-de-coordination-des-frequences-une-etape-decisive-pour-lintegration-numerique-regionale/>). It [reached roaming agreements with Benin and Togo in December 2023, and signed one with Burkina Faso and Niger under the Alliance of Sahel States in November 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), which covers harmonised rules and free roaming; the council is not reported as at work in Mali. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Free roaming across the AES](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>).

***Not held***. No source held says a thing the country agreed has been put into effect.
