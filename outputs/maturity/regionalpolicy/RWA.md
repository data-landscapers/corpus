# Rwanda

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** [The Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence was signed in Kigali on 4 April 2025 at a summit hosted by Rwanda's ministry](<https://www.africanlawbusiness.com/news/optimism-but-uncertainty-over-africas-ai-declaration/>). Rwanda is [a member state of the East African Communications Organisation, whose secretariat is in Kigali](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), and [hosted a Smart Africa data exchange assessment mission with Benin and Ghana](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5165/>), and the African Telecommunications Union reports that it is [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). [Its national identity system was connected through Smart Africa's data exchange, with those of Benin and Ghana, reported in October 2026, about five months after a technical mission in Kigali](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/smart-africa-moves-cross-border-digital-id-interoperability-toward-live-testing>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The regional regulators' organisation [publishes Rwanda's One Network Area cross-border voice figures for 2025: 107.4 million inbound minutes, up 41 per cent, and 74 million outbound](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). Smart Africa says [a cross-border digital identity pilot between Rwanda, Ghana and Benin has been launched](<https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/storys/interview-herken-kone-smart-africa-digital-sovereignty>), and the Northern Corridor states [opened a joint account for a satellite study](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>). Rwanda's ICT minister said in October 2026 that the country [launched an AI council for Africa with Smart Africa](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>) when it hosted the 2025 summit.
