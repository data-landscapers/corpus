# Nigeria

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Nigeria [hosted the seventh conference of plenipotentiaries of the African Telecommunications Union in Abuja in July 2026](<https://www.techwithafrica.com/2026/07/29/meaningful-connectivity-africa-atu-pushes-digital-inclusion/>), which [adopted the Abuja Declaration on Meaningful Connectivity](<https://www.nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/atu-summit-ends-in-abuja-with-landmark-declaration-to-eradicate-continental-digital-divide/>) and [passed the Union's chair from Algeria to Nigeria](<https://atuuat.africa/seventh-atu-conference-of-plenipotentiaries-concludes-in-abuja-with-decisions-for-unions-future/>). It [hosted the second digital trade forum of the continental free trade area in Lagos on 1 and 2 July 2026](<https://mediatalkafrica.com/296653/nigerias-oduwole-afcftas-mene-urge-acceleration-of-digital-services-trade-to-unlock-africas-180bn-economy/>), and [initialled a digital cooperation communique with Chad in September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/chad-nigeria-advance-digital-cooperation-fibre-connectivity-ai/>). Its data protection commissioner says [the commission works at African level through the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://businessday.ng/interview/article/protection-of-personal-privacy-and-rights-is-central-to-ndpcs-mandate-olatunji/>), in October 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [Nigeria's selection as a pilot country for the free trade area's digital trade framework](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/30/fg-inaugurates-adapt-forum-to-cut-trade-costs-boost-digital-commerce-under-afcfta-2/>), reported in July 2026; the pilot is not reported as running. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
