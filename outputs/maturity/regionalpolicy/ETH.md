# Ethiopia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Ethiopia's national identity programme [signed a three-year memorandum of understanding on digital identity with Mozambique's digital transformation agency on 27 April 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/mozambique-taps-ethiopias-digital-id-expertise-in-dpi-partnership>), and [Zambia holds a memorandum with Ethiopia to learn from its identity model](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>). The prime minister [was named African Union champion for artificial intelligence and digital health](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/mozambique-taps-ethiopias-digital-id-expertise-in-dpi-partnership>), reported in April 2026. Ethiopia [sent representatives to the East African regional digital integration workshop in Nairobi in September 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3596-eac,-igad-and-eastern-african-countries-unite-to-accelerate-regional-digital-integration-and-connectivity>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum with Mozambique](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-and-ethiopia-sign-mou-to-boost-digital-identity/>), signed in Addis Ababa for three years; no identity service or exchange is reported as started under it. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
