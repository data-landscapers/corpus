# Zambia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** In July 2026 the African Telecommunications Union [elected Kezias Kazuba Mwale of Zambia as its secretary general](<https://atuuat.africa/seventh-atu-conference-of-plenipotentiaries-concludes-in-abuja-with-decisions-for-unions-future/>). Zambia's minister of technology and science [spoke at the close of the conference](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/atu-conference-adopts-abuja-declaration-to-advance-meaningful-connectivity-across-africa/>), in which Zambia's delegation took part. In November 2026 Zambia [hosted the COMESA workshop that validated a regional digital inclusion strategy](<https://www.comesa.int/regional-digital-inclusion-strategy-and-action-plan-validated/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the election of the secretary general](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/atu-conference-adopts-abuja-declaration-to-advance-meaningful-connectivity-across-africa/>), an office won; no rule or service adopted in Zambia under a regional agreement is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
