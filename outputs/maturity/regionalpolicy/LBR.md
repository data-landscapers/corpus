# Liberia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Liberia, Ghana and Sierra Leone [signed their entry into the West African free roaming agreement at a ceremony in Monrovia](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026, after [a roaming memorandum with The Gambia and Sierra Leone in 2025](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/11208-west-african-nations-sign-roaming-agreement-to-cut-telecom-costs/>). Liberia [had its arrears to the African Telecommunications Union waived in July 2026 and regained full voting rights](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/03/liberia-secures-us361151-atu-debt-waiver-restores-voting-rights/>). The Executive Mansion says [Liberia joined the Smart Africa Alliance as its 43rd member](<https://emansion.gov.lr/index.php/media/press-release/liberia-joins-smart-africa-alliance-43rd-member-advancing-president-boakais>), in October 2026. It is [one of three countries in the second West Africa regional digital integration programme](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access>). Its Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications reports that [Liberia was admitted to the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities in October 2026](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-deepens-cybersecurity-ties-with-morocco-secures-membership-in-african-digital-security-networks/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Liberia and Sierra Leone launched a free roaming arrangement in August 2025](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609040182.html>), letting subscribers travelling between them communicate at local rates; it has since been extended to other West African countries. Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
