# Liberia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Liberia, Ghana and Sierra Leone [signed their entry into the West African free roaming agreement at a ceremony in Monrovia](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. This followed [a roaming memorandum with The Gambia and Sierra Leone in 2025](<https://www.telecomreviewafrica.com/articles/general-news/11208-west-african-nations-sign-roaming-agreement-to-cut-telecom-costs/>). At a West African meeting in Accra on 30 April 2026 Liberia [signed free roaming memoranda with Ghana and Sierra Leone](<https://nca.org.gh/2026/05/05/tripartite-ecowas-roaming-meeting-concludes-with-key-commitments-to-seamless-regional-connectivity/>). Liberia [had its arrears to the African Telecommunications Union waived in July 2026 and regained full voting rights](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/03/liberia-secures-us361151-atu-debt-waiver-restores-voting-rights/>). The Executive Mansion says [Liberia joined the Smart Africa Alliance as its 43rd member](<https://emansion.gov.lr/index.php/media/press-release/liberia-joins-smart-africa-alliance-43rd-member-advancing-president-boakais>) in October 2026. Liberia is [one of three countries in the second West Africa regional digital integration programme](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/new-137m-initiative-to-enable-digital-service-access>). Its ministry reports that [Liberia was admitted to the African Network of Cybersecurity Authorities in October 2026](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-deepens-cybersecurity-ties-with-morocco-secures-membership-in-african-digital-security-networks/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming between Liberia and Sierra Leone took effect in August 2025](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-and-sierra-leone-roll-out-free-roaming-agreement/>), with calls at local rates across the border. [It has since been extended to other West African countries](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609040182.html>). On 17 July 2026 the regulator [launched free roaming with Ghana, Sierra Leone and The Gambia](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>). No figures of its use, decision of a joint body or review of what it delivered are recorded.
