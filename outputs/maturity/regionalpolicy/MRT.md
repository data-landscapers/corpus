# Mauritania

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mauritania's data protection authority and Morocco's data protection commission [signed a cooperation convention in Marrakech in January 2026 to exchange expertise and experience](<https://panorapost.ma/post.php?id=53790>). Mauritania [was among the states at the general assembly of the West African telecommunications regulators' association in Lome in April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the convention with Morocco](<https://panorapost.ma/post.php?id=53790>), which provides for exchanges between the two authorities; none is reported as having taken place. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
