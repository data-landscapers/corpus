# Mauritania

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mauritania's data protection authority and Morocco's data protection commission [signed a cooperation convention in Marrakech in January 2026](<https://panorapost.ma/post.php?id=53790>). It provides for exchanges of expertise and experience. Mauritania [was among the states at the general assembly of the West African telecommunications regulators' association in Lome in April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres>). Algeria's ministry names Mauritania as [one of six countries in the Trans-Saharan fibre backbone initiative](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/lalgerie-abrite-la-5eme-session-du-comite-de-liason-de-la-dorsale-transsaharienne-a-fibre-optique/>). The initiative's liaison committee held its fifth session in Algiers on 7 July 2024.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact is [the convention with Morocco](<https://panorapost.ma/post.php?id=53790>), which provides for exchanges between the two authorities. None is reported as having taken place. No roaming cut, joint body, regional guideline adopted as a national rule or shared service opened is recorded. Nor are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
