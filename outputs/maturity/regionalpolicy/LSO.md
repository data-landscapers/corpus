# Lesotho

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Lesotho [joined the Smart Africa Alliance in 2025](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-joined-a-continental-alliance-to-advance-ai-data-and-digital-skills-paving-the-way-for-the-establishment-of-the-smart-africa-digital-academy/>). In November 2025 it [hosted southern Africa's sixth cyber drill in Maseru](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/ramashamole-officiates-sadc-cyber-drill/>). It [was chosen for Smart Africa's national data blueprint programme](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-is-taking-a-leading-role-in-advancing-digital-transformation-to-enable-artificial-intelligence-applications-accessible-for-citizens/>) and [held a validation workshop in Maseru](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91596>), reported in 2026. In July 2025 AUDA-NEPAD [held a workshop in Maseru on adopting the African Union's data policy framework](<https://cipesa.org/2025/08/lesotho-charts-a-progressive-path-on-data-governance/>). Its minister [chaired the African Union's ICT committee until 29 October 2026](<https://au.int/en/speeches/20261029/auc-commissioner-6th-au-stc-communication-and-ict-stc-cict>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the data blueprint programme](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91596>). A national blueprint was validated in Maseru. It is not reported as adopted. No roaming cut, joint body, regional rule adopted at home or shared service is recorded.
