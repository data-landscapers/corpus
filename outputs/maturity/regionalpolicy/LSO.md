# Lesotho

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Lesotho [joined the Smart Africa Alliance in 2025](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-joined-a-continental-alliance-to-advance-ai-data-and-digital-skills-paving-the-way-for-the-establishment-of-the-smart-africa-digital-academy/>) and [hosted the sixth Southern African Development Community cyber drill and Africa-CERT meeting in Maseru in November 2025](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/ramashamole-officiates-sadc-cyber-drill/>). It [was selected to take part in Smart Africa's national data blueprint programme](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-is-taking-a-leading-role-in-advancing-digital-transformation-to-enable-artificial-intelligence-applications-accessible-for-citizens/>), [holding a validation workshop in Maseru](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91596>), reported in 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the data blueprint programme](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91596>), under which a national blueprint was validated in Maseru; the blueprint is not reported as adopted. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.

***Also on file***. [UN AI Blueprint](<https://www.gov.ls/development/lesotho-first-country-to-implement-un-ai-blueprint/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a thing the country agreed has been put into effect.
