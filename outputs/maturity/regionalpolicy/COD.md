# DR Congo

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo [hosted in Kinshasa on 24 February 2026 the tenth ordinary session of the conference of Central African telecommunications regulators](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>). It [settled over 1.4 million dollars in arrears to the African Telecommunications Union, securing a seat on its administrative council](<https://thepanafricanpress.info/one-year-of-suminwa-ii-jose-mpandas-telecom-milestones-in-the-drc/>), and the Union's conference [validated the move of its headquarters from Nairobi to Kinshasa on 23 July 2026](<https://thepanafricanpress.info/one-year-of-suminwa-ii-jose-mpandas-telecom-milestones-in-the-drc/>). It is [a member of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the regulators' session in Kinshasa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>), which adopted a resolution on free roaming in the region; the resolution is not reported as applied in the country. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.

***Also on file***. [One Network Area roaming framework](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>); [Central Africa free roaming](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>).

***Not found***. No source found says a thing the country agreed has been put into effect.
