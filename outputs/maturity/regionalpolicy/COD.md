# DR Congo

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo [hosted in Kinshasa in February 2026 the tenth ordinary session of ARTAC](<https://art.cm/en/node/246964>), the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly. At its opening [its regulator signed a roaming agreement with Gabon's](<https://art.cm/en/node/246964>). [The agreement scraps roaming charges between the two countries](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2502-53256-gabon-drc-to-scrap-cross-border-roaming-charges-from-june-2026>). The country [settled over 1.4 million dollars in arrears to the African Telecommunications Union, securing a seat on its council](<https://thepanafricanpress.info/one-year-of-suminwa-ii-jose-mpandas-telecom-milestones-in-the-drc/>). The Union's conference [validated the move of its headquarters from Nairobi to Kinshasa on 23 July 2026](<https://thepanafricanpress.info/one-year-of-suminwa-ii-jose-mpandas-telecom-milestones-in-the-drc/>). It is [a member of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [The roaming agreement with Gabon was to take effect on 30 June 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2502-53256-gabon-drc-to-scrap-cross-border-roaming-charges-from-june-2026>). [The Kinshasa session also resolved on free roaming across Central Africa by that date](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>).

***Also on file***. [One Network Area roaming framework](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>); [Central Africa free roaming](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>).
