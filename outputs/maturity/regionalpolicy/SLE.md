# Sierra Leone

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Sierra Leone's regulator [signed a protocol with Guinea in November 2025 to introduce free mobile roaming between the two countries](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>). Sierra Leone [entered the West African free roaming agreement with Liberia and Ghana](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. Its ministry [signed a digital transformation cooperation framework with Nigeria in Freetown on 21 November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>), and [two technical partnerships followed the next day](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>). In February 2026 a ministry director [chaired the ECOWAS workshop in Accra that validated the review of the regional roaming regulation](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-validates-implementation-review-of-its-regulation-on-roaming/>). The regulator says it is [part of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly](<https://news.scienceafrica.co.ke/emerging-technologies-can-africas-digital-infrastructure-keep-up/>), reported in October 2026. In November 2024 [its minister signed a framework agreement with Guinea in Freetown to interconnect the two countries' internet backbones](<https://lepandaguinee.com/2024/11/26/la-guinee-et-la-sierra-leone-signent-un-accord-pour-renforcer-leur-interconnexion-numerique/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Liberia's regulator says [the free roaming agreement with Sierra Leone took effect in August 2025](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-and-sierra-leone-roll-out-free-roaming-agreement/>). Calls cross the border at local rates. In July 2026 Liberia [launched free roaming with Ghana, Sierra Leone and The Gambia](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>), its subscribers roaming in Sierra Leone without charges.
