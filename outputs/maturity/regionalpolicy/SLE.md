# Sierra Leone

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Sierra Leone's regulator [signed a protocol with Guinea in November 2025 to introduce free mobile roaming between the two countries](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>). Sierra Leone [entered the West African free roaming agreement with Liberia and Ghana](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. Its ministry [signed a digital transformation cooperation framework with Nigeria in Freetown on 21 November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>), and [two technical partnerships followed the next day](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the roaming agreement with Liberia and Ghana](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), which is to remove roaming charges among the three; no source filed to Sierra Leone reports it as started. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
