# Sierra Leone

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Sierra Leone's regulator [signed a protocol with Guinea in November 2025 to introduce free mobile roaming between the two countries](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>). Sierra Leone [entered the West African free roaming agreement with Liberia and Ghana](<https://lta.gov.lr/liberia-ghana-sierra-leone-move-toward-borderless-mobile-communication/>), reported in February 2026. Its ministry [signed a digital transformation cooperation framework with Nigeria in Freetown on 21 November 2025](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>), and [two technical partnerships followed the next day](<https://mocti.gov.sl/joint-communique-nigeria-sierra-leone-digital-economy-bilateral-mission/>). The regulator says it is [part of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly](<https://news.scienceafrica.co.ke/emerging-technologies-can-africas-digital-infrastructure-keep-up/>), reported in October 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Liberia and Sierra Leone introduced their roaming service in August 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
