# Malawi

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Malawi's regulator [holds the rotating chair of the Communications Regulators' Association of Southern Africa](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2026/09/16/southern-african-regulators-call-for-more-agile-digital-regulation/>). The regulator [hosted its 46th executive committee meeting at Mangochi on 14 to 16 September 2026](<https://events.crasa.org/event/46th-ordinary-meeting-of-the-crasa-executive-committee-exco/>). It [hosted the association's regulatory collaboration summit in Lilongwe in April 2026](<https://researchictafrica.net/2026/05/04/how-do-we-move-the-crasa-summits-commitment-to-collaborative-regulation-from-intent-to-evidence-led-action/>). In June 2025 Malawi [became a full member of the African Telecommunications Union](<https://leymanck.com/malawi-secures-full-membership-in-african-telecoms-union/>) on ratifying its constitution. It [joined Smart Africa as its 33rd member](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2023/1004/Documents/Daud%20Elvin%20Suleman.pdf>) and by September 2023 [had signed memoranda with Tanzania and Zambia on data corridors](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2023/1004/Documents/Daud%20Elvin%20Suleman.pdf>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** A March 2026 report cites Botswana's regulator: [mobile operators in Malawi introduced roaming price cuts of 10 to 98.6 per cent](<https://theafricanmirror.africa/news/cheaper-cross-border-call-rates-beckon-on-lower-tariffs-push/>). The cuts fall under SADC's One Network Area. In June 2026 Zambia's regulator said [operators in the area, Malawi among them, had cut roaming tariffs by 3 to 93 per cent](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-five-sadc-states-cut-mobile-roaming-charges-in-push-for-regional-connectivity/>). They had also signed new bilateral roaming agreements.
