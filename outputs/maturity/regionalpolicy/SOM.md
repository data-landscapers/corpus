# Somalia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Somalia's National Communications Authority [held a national consultation with licensed operators, reported in September 2025, on the country's roadmap for joining the East African Community's One Network Area](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-conducts-consultation-on-somalias-adoption-of-the-one-network-area-ona/>). It is the one fact held on the country's part in regional digital policy. An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded. [In April 2026 Somalia presented its plan for adopting artificial intelligence at a meeting of the East African Science and Technology Commission](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news/2026/Apr/wararka_maanta14-193458.htm>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** In November 2026 an East African Community official [said Somalia had yet to put the 2014 One Network Area roaming framework into effect](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-11-03-eac-moves-to-slash-cross-border-call-data-charges>). He said it had committed to aligning its rules with it. Its regulator [consulted operators in 2025 on a roadmap for joining](<https://nca.gov.so/national-communications-authority-conducts-consultation-on-somalias-adoption-of-the-one-network-area-ona/>). No roaming cut, joint body, regional rule adopted at home or shared service is recorded. Figures of use and decisions of a joint body are not recorded either.
