# Seychelles

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The government of Seychelles [signed a memorandum of understanding on digital trade with the secretariat of the African Continental Free Trade Area and a company in March 2026](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/29842/seychelles-set-to-become-digital-trade-hub-of-afcfta>), positioning the country as the trade area's digital trade hub. Seychelles [is to host the joint venture set up in August 2026 for the digital trade corridor](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/08/afcfta-quest-ghana-sign-deal-to-establish-digital-trade-corridor/>) and is [covered by the African Union development agency's island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the joint venture agreement of 31 August 2026](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/08/afcfta-quest-ghana-sign-deal-to-establish-digital-trade-corridor/>), signed in Accra to establish the digital trade corridor; the corridor is not reported as operating. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
