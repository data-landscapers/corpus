# Niger

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Niger's regulator [signed a cooperation convention with the regulators of Burkina Faso and Mali creating the Council of Sahel Regulators](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. Niger's data protection authority [chairs the African network of personal data protection authorities until 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>), presiding over its monthly bureau meetings. It [signed a roaming agreement with Burkina Faso and Mali under the Alliance of Sahel States in November 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>); the council it creates is not reported as at work in Niger, and free roaming between the three states is an aim. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
