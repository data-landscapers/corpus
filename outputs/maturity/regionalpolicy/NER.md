# Niger

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Niger's regulator [signed a free roaming memorandum with those of Burkina Faso and Mali in Niamey in November 2024](<https://www.lesahel.org/integration-numerique-larcep-niger-celebre-son-25e-anniversaire-avec-la-signature-dun-memorandum-dentente-sur-le-free-roaming-entre-les-pays-membres-de-l/>). In October 2025 Niger [hosted the Niamey meeting that drafted the Sahel States' border frequency coordination framework](<https://www.arcep.bf/le-burkina-faso-et-ses-voisins-renforcent-la-connectivite-transfrontaliere-avec-des-accords-majeurs-sur-le-roaming-et-les-frequences/>). On 25 September 2026 its regulator [signed the protocol in Ouagadougou creating the Council of Sahel Regulators](<https://www.aib.media/?p=167411>), with free roaming its first priority. Niger's data protection authority [chairs the African network of data protection authorities until 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). On 5 November 2026 its ministry [helped finalise the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** On 25 September 2026 Niger's regulator head [said free roaming among the three Sahel states was already working](<https://www.radarsburkina.net/index.php/fr/politique/4017-cooperation-numerique-dans-l-aes-les-regulateurs-passent-de-la-coordination-a-l-action>). That was before the council was created. In October 2026 Ecofin [reported no recent confirmation that the roaming agreement announced for January 2025 was working](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2610-142755-telecoms-les-obstacles-a-la-generalisation-du-free-roaming-en-afrique-de-l-ouest>).

***Also on file***. [Free roaming across the AES](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>) and the [Niger-Algeria trans-Saharan fibre-optic link](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>). Also the [African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) and the [Global Privacy Assembly](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>).
