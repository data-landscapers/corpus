# Niger

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Niger's regulator [signed a convention with the regulators of Burkina Faso and Mali](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. It created the Council of Sahel Regulators. Niger's data protection authority [chairs the African network of data protection authorities until 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>). It presides over the network's monthly bureau meetings. In November 2024 Niger [signed a roaming agreement with Burkina Faso and Mali](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>) under the Sahel States. On 5 November 2026 its ministry [helped finalise the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>). The council it creates is not reported at work in Niger. Free roaming between the three states was an aim. No source found reports a roaming cut, a joint body at work or a shared service opened.

***Also on file***. [Free roaming across the AES](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>) and the [Niger-Algeria trans-Saharan fibre-optic link](<https://mcnti.gouv.ne/souverainete-et-integration-numerique-lancement-de-lexploitation-de-la-liaison-transsaharienne-en-fibre-optique-niger-algerie-a-assamaka/>). Also the [African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>) and the [Global Privacy Assembly](<https://www.lesahel.org/entretien-avec-le-president-de-la-haute-autorite-de-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel-hapdp/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a thing the country agreed has been put into effect.
