# Guinea-Bissau

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Regulators and policymakers from Guinea-Bissau [took part in a data governance workshop for Portuguese-speaking African countries in December 2025](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance>). Guinea-Bissau is [named in the programme for African developing island economies](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). The African Union's development agency launched it on 24 July 2026. The African Telecommunications Union [reported on 16 October 2026 that the country is among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). The proposals are for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026. ECOWAS [names Guinea-Bissau among the countries carrying out the West Africa regional digital integration programme](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-participation-in-the-digital-transformation-for-africa-western-africa-regional-digital-integration-program-declared-effective/>), declared effective in March 2024. An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact found is [the island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>), launched in July 2026 with digital transformation among its pillars. Nothing done under it in Guinea-Bissau is reported. No roaming cut, joint body set up, regional guideline adopted as a national rule or shared service opened has been found. Nor have figures of use or a decision of a joint body been found.
