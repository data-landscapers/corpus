## Regional cooperation on digital policy

Algeria's minister [discussed its part in Smart Africa's market initiative in July 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6272/smart-africa-and-algeria-hold-discussions-on-ai-adoption-and-digital-integration>). The African Telecommunications Union reports Algeria [among 23 states that endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [The Algerian-Tunisian joint technical commission on communications and ICT cooperation held its fourth session in June 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/09/algeria-and-tunisia-expand-cooperation-in-telecommunications-and-ict-sector/>), a standing body of the two governments, and agreed joint projects.
