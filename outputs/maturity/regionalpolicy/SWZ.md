# Eswatini

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Eswatini [was among the delegations at the Southern African Development Community's regional workshop on data governance in Harare in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>). Smart Africa [issued a request for proposals for Eswatini's national data policy in July 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf>). Its ministers [attended the Community's meetings of ministers responsible for ICT in June 2023](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-ministers-discuss-strategic-policies-and-activities-accelerate-implementation>) and [in September 2019](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement__ICT_Information_Transport_and_Met_meeting__2019.pdf>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [Smart Africa's request for proposals for a national data policy](<https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf>); the policy is not reported as adopted. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
