# Eswatini

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Eswatini's communications regulator [signed a memorandum of understanding with the Lesotho Communications Authority on 3 October 2026](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatini-lesotho-join-forces-to-tackle-cyber-threats/>) covering information exchange, joint research, training and cybersecurity cooperation. Its ICT ministry and Smart Africa [opened a joint workshop on 7 October 2026 to co-develop and pilot gender impact assessment tools](<https://independentnews.co.sz/49218/news/minister-savannah-vows-to-continue-advocating-for-women-in-tech/>), and Smart Africa [issued a request for proposals for Eswatini's national data policy in July 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf>). Eswatini [was among the delegations at the Southern African Development Community's regional workshop on data governance in Harare in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>) and is [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). Eswatini is [one of six states Smart Africa welcomed as recently joined members in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Its ministers [attended the Community's meetings of ministers responsible for ICT in June 2023](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-ministers-discuss-strategic-policies-and-activities-accelerate-implementation>) and [in September 2019](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement__ICT_Information_Transport_and_Met_meeting__2019.pdf>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum between the two regulators](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/eswatini-lesotho-join-forces-to-tackle-cyber-threats/>), which is an agreement signed, not a thing delivered. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
