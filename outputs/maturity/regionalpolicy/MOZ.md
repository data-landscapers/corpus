# Mozambique

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mozambique and Angola [signed a memorandum on communications and information technology in June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>). In December 2025 Mozambique [held a data governance workshop in Maputo for Portuguese-speaking African countries](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance>). Its regulator [hosted a workshop of southern Africa's regulators' association on 6 October 2026](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/incm-acolhe-workshop-sobre-tecnologias-de-satelite/>). Its ICT institute [joined the regional working group on data protection](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection>), reported in October 2026. It is [among 23 states endorsing the African proposals for the 2026 ITU conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). Its customs and those of four neighbours [adopted a plan for customs data exchange on 26 August 2026](<https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/>). [On 12 October 2026 INTIC was elected to the ANCA-CERT committee for southern Africa](<https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/>). INTIC said in May 2026 that [Mozambique is not yet a member of the African network of data protection authorities](<https://intic.gov.mz/intic-acompanha-avancos-da-rede-africana-de-autoridades-de-proteccao-de-dados-e-reforca-compromisso-com-a-futura-adesao-de-mocambique/>). Membership needs a data protection law.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum with Angola](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>). It provides for technical cooperation and exchanges. Nothing is reported as started under it. No roaming cut, joint body, regional rule adopted at home or shared service is recorded.
