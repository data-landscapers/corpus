# Mozambique

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mozambique and Angola [signed a memorandum of understanding on cooperation in communications, information technology, information and meteorology in June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>). Mozambique [took part in, and held in Maputo, a data governance workshop for Portuguese-speaking African countries on 8 to 10 December 2025](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance>). Its regulator [hosted in Maputo on 6 October 2026 a workshop for a committee of the Communications Regulators' Association of Southern Africa](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/incm-acolhe-workshop-sobre-tecnologias-de-satelite/>). Its ICT institute [joined the Southern African Development Community's regional working group on data protection](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection>), reported in October 2026. It is [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). Its customs, with those of Malawi, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe, [adopted a roadmap and action plan for customs-to-customs data exchange in Pretoria on 26 August 2026](<https://southernafricantimes.com/five-southern-african-countries-move-to-link-customs-data-as-trade-corridors-face-pressure-to-cut-delays/>). [On 12 October 2026 INTIC was elected to the ANCA-CERT management committee for Southern Africa, at the eighth ANCA meeting in Rabat](<https://intic.gov.mz/mocambique-eleito-para-o-comite-de-gestao-da-anca-cert-em-representacao-da-africa-austral/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum with Angola](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>), which provides for technical and institutional cooperation and exchanges; nothing is reported as started under it. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
