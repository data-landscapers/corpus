# Cote d'Ivoire

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Its digital minister [was elected vice chair of Smart Africa's council of ICT ministers for two years in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Cote d'Ivoire [hosted in Abidjan in September 2026 the fourth and last African preparatory meeting of the African Telecommunications Union for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://afriksoir.net/telecommunications-la-4%e1%b5%89-et-derniere-reunion-preparatoire-africaine-avant-la-pp-26-de-doha-sest-ouverte-a-abidjan/>), [opened by its digital minister](<https://www.aip.ci/aip-la-cote-divoire-appelle-a-une-position-africaine-unie-sur-les-enjeux-du-numerique-a-lapproche-de-la-pp-26/>), who [launched the Union's fifth innovation challenge there on 17 September](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7306/atu-opens-fifth-africa-innovation-challenge-with-us60000-prize-pool>). It [hosted the ninth conference of the African network of data protection authorities in May 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>), and [works within the West African monetary union, the West African community and Smart Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5699/cote-divoire-reaffirms-commitment-to-accelerate-digital-cooperation-at-europe-east-africa-dpi-session-in-estonia>), and is [one of 23 states the Union reports as endorsing its 49 African common proposals for the conference, as at 16 October 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). It [signed a roaming agreement with Burkina Faso in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Its regulator says [free roaming with Ghana, launched on 14 June 2023, cut charges for Ivorian subscribers in Ghana by as much as 90 per cent](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
