# Cote d'Ivoire

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Cote d'Ivoire [hosted in Abidjan in September 2026 the fourth and last African preparatory meeting of the African Telecommunications Union for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://afriksoir.net/telecommunications-la-4%e1%b5%89-et-derniere-reunion-preparatoire-africaine-avant-la-pp-26-de-doha-sest-ouverte-a-abidjan/>), [opened by its digital minister](<https://www.aip.ci/aip-la-cote-divoire-appelle-a-une-position-africaine-unie-sur-les-enjeux-du-numerique-a-lapproche-de-la-pp-26/>), who [launched the Union's fifth innovation challenge there on 17 September](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7306/atu-opens-fifth-africa-innovation-challenge-with-us60000-prize-pool>). It [hosted the ninth conference of the African network of data protection authorities in May 2026](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2642003/economie/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-djibril-ouattara-presente-les-progres-realises-par-la-cote-divoire>), and [works within the West African monetary union, the West African community and Smart Africa](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5699/cote-divoire-reaffirms-commitment-to-accelerate-digital-cooperation-at-europe-east-africa-dpi-session-in-estonia>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the Abidjan preparatory meeting](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7329/space-regulation-emerges-as-key-issue-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference-2026>), which agreed common African positions and is a meeting hosted, not a thing delivered in the country. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
