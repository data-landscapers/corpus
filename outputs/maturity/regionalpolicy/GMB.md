# Gambia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Gambia and Senegal [signed a memorandum of understanding on telecommunications, information technology and the digital economy in Dakar on 12 June 2026](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/policy/gambia-senegal-move-to-implement-digital-cooperation-agreement/>). Their regulators [signed a memorandum on regulatory cooperation](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6078/senegal-and-the-gambia-enhance-digital-integration-through-regulatory-cooperation-agreement>) and [one to apply the West African roaming regulation in December 2025](<https://pura.gm/pura-and-artp-sign-mou-to-implement-ecowas-roaming-regulation/>). The Gambia [signed a free roaming memorandum with Liberia in 2025](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>) and [a roaming agreement with Ghana in November 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Its regulator [hosted the West African regulators' working groups in Banjul in June 2024](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/pura-hosts-watra-meeting-to-boost-digital-transformation-in-wafrica>). It is [one of six states Smart Africa welcomed as recently joined members in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Its information commission [was admitted as a full member of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://standard.gm/gambias-information-commission-joins-african-data-protection-network/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming between Senegal and The Gambia was introduced in December 2025](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6078/senegal-and-the-gambia-enhance-digital-integration-through-regulatory-cooperation-agreement>): travellers receive calls free and make calls at local rates for up to 30 days. [Liberian subscribers have roamed in The Gambia without charges since 17 July 2026](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-launches-free-roaming-service-with-ghana-sierra-leone-and-the-gambia/>). [The first steering committee under the memorandum with Senegal met in Banjul](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/policy/gambia-senegal-move-to-implement-digital-cooperation-agreement/>), reported in August 2026, to set a roadmap. No figures of use, joint decision or review are recorded.
