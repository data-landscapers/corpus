# Madagascar

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Madagascar [took part in a data policy training session in Antananarivo on 24 September 2025](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/en/event/data-policy-workshop-regional-cooperation/>), part of a workshop on data governance in Africa. In July 2020 it [was supported by the African Union's first online school on internet governance, run with its policy and regulation initiative](<https://www.intgovforum.org/system/files/filedepot/21/madagascar-fgi2020-en.pdf>). A delegation [attended the Southern African Development Community's public service assembly in August 2026](<https://moov.mg/article/120425-fonction-publique-madagascar-defend-une-administration-tournee-vers-les-citoyens-a-la-sadc>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the data policy training of September 2025](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/en/event/data-policy-workshop-regional-cooperation/>), and no rule adopted in Madagascar as a result is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
