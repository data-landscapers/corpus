# Guinea

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Guinea [hosted the seventh Transform Africa Summit of the Smart Africa Alliance in Conakry in November 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/transform-africa-summit-2025-conakry-becomes-the-epicenter-of-digital-cooperation-in-africa>), [with over 7,000 participants and 45 ministers](<https://smartafrica.org/fr/le-transform-africa-summit-2025-confirme-la-dynamique-continentale-pour-une-afrique-digitale-unie-et-souveraine-depuis-la-guinee>). There [Sierra Leone's regulator and Guinea signed a protocol introducing free mobile roaming between the two countries](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>). Guinea [joined Smart Africa in 2016](<https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/>), [chairs its council of African regulators](<https://smartafrica.org/get-to-know-the-republic-of-guinea-home-of-transform-africa-summit-2025/>), and [signed an agreement with the alliance on 4 September 2025](<https://presidence.gov.gn/la-guinee-se-dote-de-son-premier-data-center-national-et-du-domaine-gn-une-avancee-majeure-pour-la-souverainete-numerique/>), and its minister said in October 2025 that [Guinea has joined the Smart Africa initiative on cybersecurity and artificial intelligence](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>). Its audiovisual regulator is [a member of the PRA-UEMOA-Guinee regulators' platform, whose September 2026 communiqué with Niger's regulator names digital regulation as a main challenge](<https://guineenews.org/2026/09/26/pra-uemoa-guinee-la-hac-et-lonc-niger-passent-aux-actes-avec-plusieurs-resolutions/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the roaming protocol with Sierra Leone](<https://natca.gov.sl/2025/11/17/the-republic-of-sierra-leone-signs-free-roaming-agreement-with-republic-of-guinea-to-ease-cross-border-connectivity/>), signed in November 2025; free roaming between the two is not reported as started. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
