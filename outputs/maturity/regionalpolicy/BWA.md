# Botswana

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Botswana's communications minister [signed a cooperation memorandum with Namibia in Kasane on 7 August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6641/namibia-and-botswana-sign-ict-cooperation-agreement>), covering digital transformation and cybersecurity. Botswana [takes part with five neighbours in the southern African One Network Area framework](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/13/botswana-and-five-sadc-countries-slash-roaming-tariffs-under-one-network-area-framework/>), and its regulator [works within the Communications Regulators' Association of Southern Africa](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/BOCRA%202024%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20WEB%20VERSION.pdf>). It [sent a delegation to the African Telecommunications Union's preparatory meeting in May 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5936/botswana-pushes-for-rrb-seat-as-african-states-forge-common-itu-agenda-in-victoria-falls>), and by October 2026 it was [among 23 states endorsing the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Reduced roaming rates between Botswana and Namibia took effect in August 2024](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/13/botswana-and-five-sadc-countries-slash-roaming-tariffs-under-one-network-area-framework/>). In March 2026 [Botswana's operators applied the new One Network Area roaming tariffs](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/13/botswana-and-five-sadc-countries-slash-roaming-tariffs-under-one-network-area-framework/>), with cuts of 10 to 98.6 per cent and free incoming text messages across participating countries. Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
