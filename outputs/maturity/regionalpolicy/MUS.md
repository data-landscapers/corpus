# Mauritius

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mauritius [hosted the Southern African Development Community's capacity building workshop on cyber security and its first regional cyber drill at Ebene on 10 to 13 September 2018](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>). A cabinet record of 14 August 2026 [states that Mauritius has been a member of the Community since 1995](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2014%20August%202026.pdf>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the cyber drill of 2018](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>), a meeting hosted eight years ago, and nothing adopted in Mauritius from it is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
