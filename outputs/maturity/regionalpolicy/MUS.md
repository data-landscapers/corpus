# Mauritius

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mauritius [hosted southern Africa's first regional cyber drill at Ebene in September 2018](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>). Smart Africa [welcomed it as a new member in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). A cabinet record of 14 August 2026 [dates its membership of the regional community to 1995](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2014%20August%202026.pdf>). Its data protection commissioner [is a member of the African network of data protection authorities](<https://dataprotection.govmu.org/Pages/Home%20-%20Pages/Upcoming%20Events/The-Data-Protection-Commissioner.aspx>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The central bank governor says [the Bank of Mauritius settles COMESA's regional payment system](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>), as at July 2026. The system carries cross-border payments among member states. [The 2018 cyber drill](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>) had delegates from fourteen member states. It was a meeting hosted, and nothing adopted from it is reported. No roaming cut or joint body at work is recorded.
