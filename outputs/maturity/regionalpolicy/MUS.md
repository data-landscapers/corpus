# Mauritius

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Mauritius [hosted the Southern African Development Community's capacity building workshop on cyber security and its first regional cyber drill at Ebene on 10 to 13 September 2018](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>), with delegates from fourteen member states. Mauritius is [one of six states Smart Africa welcomed as recently joined members in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). A cabinet record of 14 August 2026 [states that Mauritius has been a member of the Community since 1995](<https://pmo.govmu.org/CabinetDecision/2026/Highlights%20of%20Cabinet%20Meeting%20Friday%2014%20August%202026.pdf>). The Governor of the Bank of Mauritius says [the Bank serves as settlement bank for the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa's Regional Payment and Settlement System](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>), as at July 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The Governor of the Bank of Mauritius says [the Bank acts as settlement bank for the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa's Regional Payment and Settlement System, which facilitates cross-border payments among member states](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>), as at July 2026. The earlier fact, [the cyber drill of 2018](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2021-06/Media_Statement_SADC_Capacity_Building_Workshop_on_Cyber_Security_and_Cyber_Drill.pdf>), was a meeting hosted eight years ago, and nothing adopted in Mauritius from it is reported.
