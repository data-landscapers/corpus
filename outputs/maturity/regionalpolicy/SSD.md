# South Sudan

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** South Sudan's minister of information and ICT [signed a declaration of trust with the Smart Africa alliance on 30 May 2025 to cooperate on the digital economy, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence](<https://mictps.gov.ss/south-sudan-inks-smart-africa-declaration-of-trust-to-commence-cooperation-on-digital-trade/>). South Sudan is [one of the seven member states of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>) and [took part in the Northern Corridor Integration Projects meeting in Kampala in August 2026](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>). Its minister [visited the African Telecommunications Union in August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6713/south-sudan-pledges-stronger-collaboration-with-atu-on-digital-infrastructure-and-capacity-building>), and the Union reports the country [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the declaration with Smart Africa](<https://mictps.gov.ss/south-sudan-inks-smart-africa-declaration-of-trust-to-commence-cooperation-on-digital-trade/>), which sets out areas of cooperation; nothing is reported as started under it. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
