# South Sudan

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** South Sudan's ICT minister [signed a declaration of trust with the Smart Africa alliance on 30 May 2025](<https://mictps.gov.ss/south-sudan-inks-smart-africa-declaration-of-trust-to-commence-cooperation-on-digital-trade/>). It covers the digital economy, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence. South Sudan is [one of the seven member states of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). It [took part in the Northern Corridor Integration Projects meeting in Kampala in August 2026](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>). Its minister [visited the African Telecommunications Union in August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6713/south-sudan-pledges-stronger-collaboration-with-atu-on-digital-infrastructure-and-capacity-building>). The Union counts it [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** In November 2026 an East African Community official [named South Sudan among six of eight partner states that have put the 2014 One Network Area roaming framework into effect](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-11-03-eac-moves-to-slash-cross-border-call-data-charges>). He said it had cut the cost of cross-border calls. In November 2023 the Northern Corridor ICT ministers, South Sudan's among them, [reported the One Network Area for voice, data and SMS in operation](<https://www.mofa.go.ug/article/ministerial-meeting-ict-infrastructure-development-9th-november-2023-nairobi-kenya>). [Traffic among the partner states had risen over 900 times](<https://www.mofa.go.ug/article/ministerial-meeting-ict-infrastructure-development-9th-november-2023-nairobi-kenya>).
