# Central African Republic

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Central African Republic is [listed among the 49 countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence at Kigali on 4 April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), alongside the African Union and Smart Africa. It is [one of six states Smart Africa welcomed as recently joined members in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>), and [a delegation attended the session of the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly in Libreville in October 2026](<https://www.techafrika.net/Afrique-centrale-a-Libreville-les-regulateurs-telecoms-de-l-ARTAC-posent-les-bases-d-un-marche-unique-du-numerique_a2504.html>). [A January 2026 factsheet lists CEMAC regulations and directives on electronic communications, user protection, payment systems and consumer protection as applying to the country](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the Kigali declaration](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), a statement of shared aims on artificial intelligence, and nothing the country has done under it is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
