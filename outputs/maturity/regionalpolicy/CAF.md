# Central African Republic

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Central African Republic [joined the Smart Africa Trust Alliance in May 2024](<https://techafricanews.com/2024/05/31/comoros-and-central-african-republic-join-smart-africa-trust-alliance-for-enhanced-connectivity/>). It is [listed among the 49 signatories of the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence of April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>). Smart Africa [welcomed it as a member in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Cameroon's regulator [names it one of the eight members of ARTAC](<https://art.cm/en/node/246964>), the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly. [A delegation attended ARTAC's session in Libreville in October 2026](<https://www.techafrika.net/Afrique-centrale-a-Libreville-les-regulateurs-telecoms-de-l-ARTAC-posent-les-bases-d-un-marche-unique-du-numerique_a2504.html>). [CEMAC rules on electronic communications and payments apply to it](<https://dataprotection.africa/central-african-republic>), a January 2026 factsheet says.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Gabon's regulator says [Gabon signed a roaming protocol with the Central African Republic in 2021, its opening still planned in February 2025](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>). [The Kigali declaration](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>) states shared aims on artificial intelligence. No act of the country's own under it is reported.
