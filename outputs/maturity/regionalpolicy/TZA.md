# Tanzania

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Tanzania is [one of the seven member states of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), with its regulator submitting data for 2024 and 2025. It [took part in the third steering committee of the Eastern Africa regional digital integration project in Nairobi on 5 and 6 May 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3502-eac-partner-states-reaffirm-commitment-to-accelerating-regional-digital-integration-for-economic-transformation>). [The steering committee was reported by the East African Community's secretariat](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3502-eac-partner-states-reaffirm-commitment-to-accelerating-regional-digital-integration-for-economic-transformation>), which runs the project with the Intergovernmental Authority on Development. In October 2026 it [hosted the fifth East African Communications Organisation postal and logistics meeting in Arusha on 14 October](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/biashara/serikali-yaweka-mkazo-teknolojia-kuboresha-huduma-za-posta-usafirishaji-5632092>), and the East African Community says its [regional e-commerce engagement platform met in Dar es Salaam from 13 to 15 October](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3609-eac-partner-states-advance-measure-to-strengthen-e-commerce-and-digital-trade>). Bank of Tanzania officials chaired East African Community technical meetings on a Rwanda-Tanzania payment link [in Kigali in November 2025](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3435-eac-begins-building-regional-instant-payment-network-with-rwanda-tanzania-pilot>) and [in Zanzibar in July 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3558-eac-intensifies-efforts-towards-a-regional-instant-payment-network-as-rwanda-and-tanzania-deepen-development-of-cross-border-payment-integration-framework>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The regional regulators' organisation [reports Tanzania's One Network Area cross-border voice traffic for 2025 as 148 million inbound minutes, up 36.4 per cent, and 577.7 million outbound minutes, up 59 per cent from 363.3 million](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). The One Network Area is the East African Community's shared roaming arrangement, under which roaming tariffs between member states were cut.
