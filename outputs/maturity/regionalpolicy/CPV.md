# Cape Verde

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** In September 2026 Cabo Verde's data protection authority [sat on the fifth board of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tsitsi-mariwo_dataprotection-togetherwecandomore-activity-7501689491284320257-D6sq>). On 3 September the network signed a declaration proclaiming 21 September as Africa Data Protection Day. In November 2024 Praia [hosted the sixteenth meeting of the West African regulators' focal points on regional roaming](<https://www.ecowas.int/16th-meeting-of-focal-points-of-ecowas-national-regulatory-authorities-on-the-implementation-of-regional-roaming-concludes-in-praia-cabo-verde/>). Cabo Verde is [listed among the countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence in April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>). It is [one of the seven island states of the African Union development agency's island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). The African Telecommunications Union [reports it among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African common proposals for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>), as of 16 October 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** ECOWAS reported in November 2024 that [Cabo Verde's mobile operators offer roaming at the tariffs the regional roaming regulation sets](<https://www.ecowas.int/16th-meeting-of-focal-points-of-ecowas-national-regulatory-authorities-on-the-implementation-of-regional-roaming-concludes-in-praia-cabo-verde/>). That roaming serves travellers in the Community. No figures of its use, decision of a joint body or review of what it delivered are recorded.
