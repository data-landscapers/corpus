# Cape Verde

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Cabo Verde's data protection authority [sits on the fifth board of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities, which signed a declaration on 3 September 2026 proclaiming 21 September as Africa Data Protection Day](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tsitsi-mariwo_dataprotection-togetherwecandomore-activity-7501689491284320257-D6sq>). Cabo Verde is [listed among the countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence in April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>) and is [one of the seven island states of the African Union development agency's island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). The African Telecommunications Union [reports it among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African common proposals for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>), as of 16 October 2026.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the board's declaration of September 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tsitsi-mariwo_dataprotection-togetherwecandomore-activity-7501689491284320257-D6sq>), which proclaims a day of observance and is not a thing delivered in the country. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
