# Sudan

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Sudan is [listed among the 49 countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence at Kigali on 4 April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>). It is [among the 23 states that had endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026, as the African Telecommunications Union reported on 16 October 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). In 2003 it [held a seat on the executive committee of the association of information and communications regulators for eastern and southern Africa](<https://www.itu.int/ITU-D/treg/Events/Seminars/GSR/GSR03/Informal_meeting/ARICEA%20RESENTATION.pdf>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the Kigali declaration](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), a statement of shared aims on artificial intelligence, and nothing Sudan has done under it is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.

[COMESA ICT Policy and Regulatory Programme](<https://www.itu.int/ITU-D/treg/Events/Seminars/GSR/GSR03/Informal_meeting/ARICEA%20RESENTATION.pdf>); [China-Africa Digital Technology Cooperation Center](<https://www.chinanews.com.cn/sh/2026/08-29/10686400.shtml>); [ITU World Telecommunication Development Conference (WTDC-25)](<https://tpra.gov.sd/en/adoption-of-sudans-resolution-at-the-world-telecommunication-development-conference-azerbaijan/>).
