# Sudan

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Sudan is [listed among the 49 countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence at Kigali on 4 April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>). It is [among the 23 states that had endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). The African Telecommunications Union reported this on 16 October 2026. In 2003 it [held a seat on the executive committee of the association of information and communications regulators for eastern and southern Africa](<https://www.itu.int/ITU-D/treg/Events/Seminars/GSR/GSR03/Informal_meeting/ARICEA%20RESENTATION.pdf>). No agreement signed, meeting hosted or chaired, or seat won in the last three years has been found.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact found is [the Kigali declaration](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), a statement of shared aims on artificial intelligence. Nothing Sudan has done under it is reported. No roaming cut, joint body set up, regional guideline adopted as a national rule or shared service opened has been found. Nor have figures of use or a decision of a joint body been found.
