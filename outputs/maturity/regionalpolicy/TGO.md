# Togo

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The head of Togo's regulator [was elected president of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly in April 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/trombinoscope-uk/3107-19753-michel-yaovi-galley-head-of-arcep-togo>), and the regulator [hosted the assembly's 23rd general meeting in Lome](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/telecoms/2104-18772-infrastructures-numeriques-ppp-la-cle-pour-accelerer-le-haut-debit-en-afrique-artao>). The regulators of Togo, Senegal and Benin [signed agreements there for free roaming between the three countries](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>). It [reached a roaming agreement with Mali in December 2023](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [The free roaming arrangement between Senegal, Togo and Benin took effect on 24 April 2026 in Lome](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>), with free incoming calls for up to 30 consecutive days and no surcharge on incoming international traffic. [The three regulators signed the agreements](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>) at the general meeting Togo hosted. [Free roaming with Benin had taken effect on 1 January 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
