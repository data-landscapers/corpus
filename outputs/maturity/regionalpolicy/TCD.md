# Chad

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Chad [hosted the closing of the 2026 Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena on 15 October 2026](<https://dari-infos.com/numerique-cloture-de-ledition-2026-du-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale/>). The Declaration of N'Djamena was adopted there. Chad's and Nigeria's communications ministers [initialled a final communique setting out a digital cooperation framework](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/chad-nigeria-advance-digital-cooperation-fibre-connectivity-ai/>). This followed talks in N'Djamena in September 2026. Chad's regulator [hosted a workshop of the Central African regulators' assembly in N'Djamena on 15 to 18 December 2025](<https://regulationartac.org/ndjamena-2025-lartac-larcep-tchad-et-luit-tracent-la-nouvelle-feuille-de-route-numerique-de-lafrique-centrale/>). Chad [took part in drafting the founding texts of a planned Central African conference of digital development bodies in November 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0212-51035-chad-accelerates-digital-cooperation-to-support-its-transformation-agenda>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Gabon's regulator said in February 2025 that [free roaming with Chad, under a protocol signed in 2021, had been interrupted](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>). Since January 2025 Chad [has widened its work with its Central African neighbours on free roaming](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0212-51035-chad-accelerates-digital-cooperation-to-support-its-transformation-agenda>). No cut is reported in force.
