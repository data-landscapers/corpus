# Chad

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Chad [hosted the closing of the 2026 Central Africa Internet Governance Forum in N'Djamena on 15 October 2026, where the Declaration of N'Djamena was adopted](<https://dari-infos.com/numerique-cloture-de-ledition-2026-du-forum-sur-la-gouvernance-de-linternet-afrique-centrale/>). Chad's and Nigeria's communications ministers [initialled a final communique setting out a digital cooperation framework after talks in N'Djamena in September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/17/chad-nigeria-advance-digital-cooperation-fibre-connectivity-ai/>). Chad's regulator [hosted a workshop of the Central African regulators' assembly in N'Djamena on 15 to 18 December 2025](<https://regulationartac.org/ndjamena-2025-lartac-larcep-tchad-et-luit-tracent-la-nouvelle-feuille-de-route-numerique-de-lafrique-centrale/>). Chad [took part in drafting the founding texts of a planned Central African conference of digital development bodies in November 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0212-51035-chad-accelerates-digital-cooperation-to-support-its-transformation-agenda>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [a report that Chad has widened its work with its Central African neighbours on free roaming since January 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0212-51035-chad-accelerates-digital-cooperation-to-support-its-transformation-agenda>); no roaming cut is reported as in force. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
