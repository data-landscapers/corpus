# Morocco

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Morocco [was elected to the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union for 2027 to 2031 in July 2026](<https://www.mmsp.gov.ma/fr/actualites/le-royaume-du-maroc-%C3%A9lu-membre-au-conseil-administratif-de-lunion-africaine-des-t%C3%A9l%C3%A9communications-pour-la-p%C3%A9riode-2027-2031>). Its Digital Development Agency [chairs Smart Africa's council of African information technology agencies, which met in Rabat on 14 July 2025 with 11 member states](<https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines>). Morocco is [one of three pilot countries for the continental free trade area's cross-border data exchange system, announced in May 2026](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/05/28/building-same-road-twice/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the data exchange pilot](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/05/28/building-same-road-twice/>), announced in May 2026 with Kenya and Nigeria; it is not reported as running. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
