# Namibia

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Namibia's and Botswana's ministers [signed a cooperation memorandum in Kasane in August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6641/namibia-and-botswana-sign-ict-cooperation-agreement>). It covers digital transformation, cybersecurity, connectivity and skills. Namibia's regulator [hosted the first legal and policy committee meeting of the Communications Regulators' Association of Southern Africa on 10 to 12 August 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6684/crasa-legal-and-policy-committee-opens-meeting-on-ai-data-governance-and-regional-ict-regulation>). Its ministry [announced a pan-African artificial intelligence initiative with four other African ministries in July 2026](<https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/all-documents/gsma-welcomes-abuja-declaration-on-meaningful-connectivity-for-africa-and-joins-partners-to-launch-atlas-umoja/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Botswana's regulator says [Botswana and Namibia were the first in SADC to bring in reduced and harmonised roaming rates, in August 2024](<https://www.bocra.org.bw/sites/default/files/BOCRA_ONA_Press_Release.pdf>). Botswana's ministry [said the same of Namibia in August 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/08/06/botswana-and-namibia-to-sign-ict-cooperation-agreement/>). Namibia's central bank's [directive of March 2025 moved low-value transfers between Common Monetary Area states to the regional TCIB system](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/1b/1b0b2668-5468-400b-8285-c2e3fc386a03.pdf>). It [made binding at home the area oversight committee's position of July 2024](<https://www.bon.com.na/CMSTemplates/Bon/Files/bon.com.na/1b/1b0b2668-5468-400b-8285-c2e3fc386a03.pdf>).
