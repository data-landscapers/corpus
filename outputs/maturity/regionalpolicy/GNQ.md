# Equatorial Guinea

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Equatorial Guinea [took part in the second Lusophone Africa data governance workshop in Luanda on 15 to 17 June 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda>), one of six countries with delegations there. It is [listed among the 49 countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence at Kigali on 4 April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), alongside the African Union and Smart Africa. Its Director General of Telecommunications [spoke for the country, which holds the rotating ECCAS presidency, at the validation of the ECCAS Regional Data Governance Framework in Brazzaville on 19 and 20 October 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/eccas-validates-regional-data-governance-framework-to-advance-digital-integration-in-central>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded. At its tenth session in Addis Ababa on 19 to 23 October 2026, the Statistical Commission for Africa [elected Equatorial Guinea to chair it for 2026 to 2028](<https://inege.org/?lang=fr&p=18389>), the national statistics institute reported.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the Kigali declaration](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>), a statement of shared aims on artificial intelligence, and nothing Equatorial Guinea has done under it is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
