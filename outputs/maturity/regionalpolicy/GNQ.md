# Equatorial Guinea

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Equatorial Guinea [took part in the second Lusophone Africa data governance workshop in Luanda on 15 to 17 June 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda>). It was one of six countries with delegations there. Cameroon's regulator [names Equatorial Guinea among the eight member states of ARTAC, the Central African regulators' assembly](<https://art.cm/en/node/246964>), in February 2026. It is [listed among the 49 countries that signed the Africa Declaration on Artificial Intelligence at Kigali on 4 April 2025](<https://c4ir.rw/docs/Africa-Declaration-on-Artificial-Intelligence.pdf>). The African Union and Smart Africa also signed. Its Director General of Telecommunications [spoke for the country at the validation of the ECCAS Regional Data Governance Framework in Brazzaville on 19 and 20 October 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/eccas-validates-regional-data-governance-framework-to-advance-digital-integration-in-central>). The country holds the rotating ECCAS presidency. An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded. The Statistical Commission for Africa [elected Equatorial Guinea to chair it for 2026 to 2028](<https://inege.org/?lang=fr&p=18389>), the national statistics institute reported. The election came at the commission's tenth session in Addis Ababa on 19 to 23 October 2026.
