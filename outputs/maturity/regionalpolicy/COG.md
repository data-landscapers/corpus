# Congo

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Republic of the Congo [was elected to the 25-member administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union on 24 July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/atu-conference-adopts-abuja-declaration-to-advance-meaningful-connectivity-across-africa/>), its minister [chaired Smart Africa's council of ICT ministers until March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>), and in November 2023 it [was elected rapporteur of the bureau of the African Union's technical committee on communication and ICT](<https://www.au.int/en/newsevents/20261026/au-innovation-festival-au-innofest26-theme-vibrant-youth-ecosystems-ai-and>). Its media regulator [signed a memorandum of understanding with its counterpart in the Democratic Republic of the Congo in Kinshasa in August 2026](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/medias-le-csac-et-le-cslc-formalisent-leur-partenariat-institutionnel-171340>), and [a regional workshop on emergency telecommunications opened in Brazzaville on 15 September 2026](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/telecoms-lafrique-centrale-renforce-ses-dispositifs-face-aux-catastrophes-naturelles-171621>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum between the two media regulators](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/medias-le-csac-et-le-cslc-formalisent-leur-partenariat-institutionnel-171340>), which covers technical cooperation and is an agreement, not a thing delivered. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
