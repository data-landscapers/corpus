# Congo

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The Republic of the Congo [was elected to the 25-member council of the African Telecommunications Union on 24 July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/atu-conference-adopts-abuja-declaration-to-advance-meaningful-connectivity-across-africa/>). Its minister [chaired Smart Africa's council of ICT ministers until March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). In November 2023 it [was elected rapporteur of the African Union's technical committee on communication and ICT](<https://www.au.int/en/newsevents/20261026/au-innovation-festival-au-innofest26-theme-vibrant-youth-ecosystems-ai-and>). Its regulator [signed a free roaming memorandum with Gabon in October 2019 and the CEMAC bilateral memoranda in November 2021](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Mireille%20Koumous.pdf>). [Brazzaville hosted the CEMAC council of ministers on 13 June 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1606-139332-cemac-les-operateurs-recalcitrants-au-free-roaming-menaces-de-mise-en-demeure>), which pressed operators slow on free roaming. [A regional workshop on emergency telecommunications opened there on 15 September 2026](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/telecoms-lafrique-centrale-renforce-ses-dispositifs-face-aux-catastrophes-naturelles-171621>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming with Gabon went into service for voice on 15 October 2019](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>), and later took in text messages and data. Congo's regulator [publishes monthly Congo-Gabon roaming call counts from January 2020 to December 2024](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Mireille%20Koumous.pdf>). [December 2024 had 81,563 calls](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/02-Mireille%20Koumous.pdf>). [In April 2024 MTN Congo's link with MTN Cameroon was one of two live CEMAC roaming links](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1606-139332-cemac-les-operateurs-recalcitrants-au-free-roaming-menaces-de-mise-en-demeure>), of 213 planned.
