# Comoros

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Delegates from Comoros [took part in a SADC workshop on data governance in Harare in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>). [On 24 July 2026 the African Union's development agency launched a programme for island economies](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). [Comoros is one of its seven island states](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). It is [one of six states Smart Africa welcomed as recently joined members in March 2026](<https://smartafrica.org/zimbabwe-elected-chair-of-cmict-as-smart-africa-welcomes-new-member-states/>). Comoros [signed the Smart Africa Trust Alliance declaration on 31 May 2024](<https://techafricanews.com/2024/05/31/comoros-and-central-african-republic-join-smart-africa-trust-alliance-for-enhanced-connectivity/>), the fourteenth country to join. The African Telecommunications Union [lists it among its member states](<https://atuuat.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/4-Status-of-Ratification-and-Accession-by-Member-states-_Jan-2026.pdf>), as at January 2026. [In July 2025 the regulator ANRTIC reported cooperation agreements with four other regulators](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/gsr-25/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2025/07/GSR-25_Contribution_Best-practice-Guidelines_ANRTIC-Comoros2_E.pdf>). [They are those of Madagascar, Senegal, Morocco and Mauritius, for technical work and skills exchange](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/gsr-25/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2025/07/GSR-25_Contribution_Best-practice-Guidelines_ANRTIC-Comoros2_E.pdf>). [It also takes part in meetings of the African Union, Smart Africa and SADC](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/gsr-25/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2025/07/GSR-25_Contribution_Best-practice-Guidelines_ANRTIC-Comoros2_E.pdf>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>), launched in July 2026 with digital transformation among its pillars, and nothing done under it in Comoros is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.

***Also on file***. [Global Digital Government Twinning Initiative (Bahrain iGA - ANADEN MoU)](<https://www.bizbahrain.com/bahrain-comoros-sign-mou-to-strengthen-digital-cooperation-share-experience-in-line-with-united-nations-standards/>).
