# Benin

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Benin's National Assembly [ratified the agreement setting up the Smart Africa Alliance in January 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/2601-45163-benin-joins-smart-africa-alliance-to-boost-digital-transformation>). Its regulator [signed a roaming memorandum with Senegal's in December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1512-51396-senegal-signs-agreements-to-eliminate-roaming-charges-with-four-neighbours>). [Further accords were signed in Lome in April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres>), at the West African regulators' assembly. [Its identity system was linked through Smart Africa's data exchange in October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/smart-africa-moves-cross-border-digital-id-interoperability-toward-live-testing>), with those of Ghana and Rwanda. Burkina Faso [signed roaming agreements with Benin and two others on 23 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming with Togo took effect on 1 January 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). [Free roaming with Ghana was launched on 9 October 2024](<https://nca.org.gh/2024/10/09/ghana-kicks-off-ecowas-free-roaming-with-togo-and-benin/>). In March 2025 Togolese consumer associations [tested it in Cotonou and Porto-Novo](<https://www.republiquetogolaise.tg/telecoms/2804-10595-free-roaming-entre-le-togo-et-ses-voisins-des-performances-enregistrees>). Every test call from Togo's two networks went through. Tariffs, billing and mobile data still fell short. Free roaming with Senegal and Togo together [began in Lome on 24 April 2026](<https://arcep.bj/lancement-officiel-du-free-roaming-entre-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo/>).
