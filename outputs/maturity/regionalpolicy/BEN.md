# Benin

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Benin's regulator [signed a protocol with Senegal in Dakar on 12 December 2025 applying the West African roaming regulation](<https://arcep.bj/lancement-officiel-du-free-roaming-entre-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo/>), and [roaming accords were signed in Lome in April 2026](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres>) at [the general assembly of the West African regulators' association, in which it took part](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/telecommunications-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo-lancent-un-roaming-sans-frontieres>). Benin also [signed a memorandum with Smart Africa in September 2022 launching its digital academy](<https://numerique.gouv.bj/publications/actualites/developpement-des-competences-numeriques-le-gouvernement-du-benin-et-l-alliance-smart-africa-lancent-l-initiative-sada-au-benin>). [Its national identity system was connected through Smart Africa's data exchange, with those of Ghana and Rwanda, reported in October 2026, about five months after a technical mission in Kigali](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/smart-africa-moves-cross-border-digital-id-interoperability-toward-live-testing>). Burkina Faso [signed roaming agreements with Benin, Cote d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The regulators of Benin, Togo and Senegal [launched free roaming between the three countries in Lome on 24 April 2026](<https://arcep.bj/lancement-officiel-du-free-roaming-entre-le-benin-le-senegal-et-le-togo/>), with [free incoming calls for up to 30 consecutive days](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>). [Free roaming with Togo had taken effect on 1 January 2024](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
