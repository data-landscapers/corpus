# Senegal

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Senegal and The Gambia [signed a memorandum on telecommunications, information technology and the digital economy in Dakar on 12 June 2026](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/policy/gambia-senegal-move-to-implement-digital-cooperation-agreement/>). Senegal's regulator [signed a memorandum with The Gambia's to apply the West African roaming regulation in December 2025](<https://pura.gm/pura-and-artp-sign-mou-to-implement-ecowas-roaming-regulation/>) and [roaming agreements with the regulators of Togo and Benin](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>). A [regional data governance workshop was held in Senegal in May 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/26/smart-africa-leads-regional-push-for-stronger-data-governance-in-francophone-africa/>). Senegal is [one of ten members of the West African Backbone Network](<https://aps.sn/lintegration-numerique-ouest-africaine-au-menu-dune-rencontre-a-dakar/>) and [hosted its ministers' meeting in Dakar on 8 October 2026](<https://aps.sn/lintegration-numerique-ouest-africaine-au-menu-dune-rencontre-a-dakar/>). It is [one of three pilot countries of Smart Africa's data governance programme](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/23/senegal-validates-national-blueprint-data-valorisation/>). It [signed a roaming agreement with Burkina Faso in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [Free roaming between Senegal, Togo and Benin took effect on 24 April 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5159/senegal-togo-and-benin-launch-free-roaming-initiative>), with free incoming calls for up to 30 days. [The first steering committee under the memorandum with The Gambia met in Banjul in August 2026](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/policy/gambia-senegal-move-to-implement-digital-cooperation-agreement/>) to set a roadmap. The backbone network's [Interministerial Council, its highest political body, was reported installed in October 2026](<https://aps.sn/lintegration-numerique-ouest-africaine-au-menu-dune-rencontre-a-dakar/>). Published figures of its use, a decision of a joint body or a review of what was delivered in the last three years are not recorded.
