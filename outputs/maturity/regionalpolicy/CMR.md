# Cameroon

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** [On 20-21 October 2026 the Central African telecoms regulators fixed the headquarters of their assembly, ARTAC, in Cameroon](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7974/cameroon-to-host-artac-headquarters-as-central-african-regulators-deepen-digital-coo>). [Cameroon's regulator, ART, was given the right to appoint ARTAC's executive secretary](<https://www.art.cm/fr/article/leadership-num%C3%A9rique-le-cameroun-choisi-pour-abriter-le-si%C3%A8ge-de-l%E2%80%99artac>). [In April 2024 Yaounde hosted an ARTAC and Smart Africa seminar on free roaming](<https://regulationartac.org/seminaire-artac-et-smart-africa-sur-le-free-roaming-du-22-au-23-avril-2024-a-yaounde/>). [In February 2026 the regulator's director general led its delegation to ARTAC's tenth ordinary session](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>). [ECCAS experts met in Douala in September 2026 on a regional cross-border data framework](<https://7joursinfo.com/actualites/atelier-regional-dengagement-des-parties-prenantes-pour-lelaboration-dun-cadre-regional-de-la-ceeac-en-matiere-de-gouvernance-transfrontaliere-des-donnees/>). [In September 2025 ECCAS and the UN Economic Commission for Africa validated a regional digital trade policy in Douala](<https://obs-cc.org/gouvernance-donnees-afrique-centrale-apdp/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [In April 2024 ARTAC counted two live free roaming links between Central African operators](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1606-139332-cemac-les-operateurs-recalcitrants-au-free-roaming-menaces-de-mise-en-demeure>), out of 213 planned. [Both involved Cameroon: MTN Cameroon with MTN Congo, and Orange Cameroun with Airtel Gabon](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1606-139332-cemac-les-operateurs-recalcitrants-au-free-roaming-menaces-de-mise-en-demeure>). Gabon's regulator says [free roaming with Cameroon was in service on the Gabon side only](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>).
