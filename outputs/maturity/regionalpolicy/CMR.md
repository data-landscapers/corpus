# Cameroon

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** [On 20-21 October 2026 the Central African telecoms regulators fixed the headquarters of their assembly, ARTAC, in Cameroon](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7974/cameroon-to-host-artac-headquarters-as-central-african-regulators-deepen-digital-coo>). [Cameroon's regulator, ART, was given the right to appoint ARTAC's executive secretary](<https://www.art.cm/fr/article/leadership-num%C3%A9rique-le-cameroun-choisi-pour-abriter-le-si%C3%A8ge-de-l%E2%80%99artac>). [Local press called it a sign of Cameroon's lead in regional digital affairs](<https://fr.journalducameroun.com/artac-le-cameroun-simpose-comme-capitale-numerique-de-lafrique-centrale>). [In February 2026 the regulator's director general led its delegation to ARTAC's tenth ordinary session](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/4133>). [ECCAS experts met in Douala from 31 August to 4 September 2026 on a regional cross-border data framework](<https://7joursinfo.com/actualites/atelier-regional-dengagement-des-parties-prenantes-pour-lelaboration-dun-cadre-regional-de-la-ceeac-en-matiere-de-gouvernance-transfrontaliere-des-donnees/>). [In September 2025 ECCAS and the UN Economic Commission for Africa validated a regional digital trade policy in Douala](<https://obs-cc.org/gouvernance-donnees-afrique-centrale-apdp/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [The October 2026 session settled where ARTAC sits and who names its secretary](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7974/cameroon-to-host-artac-headquarters-as-central-african-regulators-deepen-digital-coo>). No roaming cut, joint body at work, regional rule adopted at home or shared service is reported. Nor are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
