# Angola

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Angola [signed a memorandum of understanding with Mozambique in June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>) covering communications, information technology, information and meteorology. In August 2026 it [hosted in Luanda the third preparatory meeting of the Southern African Development Community for the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference](<https://angop.ao/en/noticias/sociedade/sadc-harmoniza-posicao-para-conferencia-mundial-de-radio/>), [a five-day meeting of the region's member states](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/crasa-secretariat-communications-regulators-association-of-southern-africa_sadc-crasa-wrc27-activity-7491456518320721920-81Dp>). In October 2026 it [hosted the seventh SADC Cyber Drill in Luanda, from 5 to 9 October](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/sadc-strengthens-regional-cybersecurity-and-cybercrime-cooperation>), and it is [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the memorandum with Mozambique](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6025/angola-and-mozambique-sign-space-and-digital-cooperation-agreements-to-strengthen-connectivity>), which provides for technical and institutional cooperation and is an agreement, not a thing delivered. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
