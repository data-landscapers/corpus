# Gabon

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Gabon was [among the 11 member states at Smart Africa's council of African information technology agencies in Rabat on 14 July 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines>), which drafted guidelines on exchanging data across borders. It is [among 23 states that have endorsed the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026, as the African Telecommunications Union reported on 16 October 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). The African Union [announced the sixth session of its technical committee on communication and ICT for Libreville on 26 to 29 October 2026](<https://www.au.int/en/newsevents/20261026/au-innovation-festival-au-innofest26-theme-vibrant-youth-ecosystems-ai-and>), and its regulator [is to host the Central African regulators' extraordinary conference in Libreville in October 2026](<https://regulationartac.org/artac-la-4%e1%b5%89-session-extraordinaire-de-la-conference-des-regulateurs-se-tiendra-les-20-et-21-octobre-2026-a-libreville/>), and its digital ministry [reviewed preparations to take part in the African digital ministers' council](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/31/gabon-reviews-digital-transformation-progress-sets-new-priorities/>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the cross-border data exchange guidelines drafted in Rabat](<https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines>), and Gabon is not reported as having adopted them. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
