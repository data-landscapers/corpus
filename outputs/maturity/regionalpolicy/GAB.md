# Gabon

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The sixth session of the African Union's technical committee on communication and ICT [was held in Libreville in October 2026](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20261027/au-opens-6th-session-specialized-technical-committee-communication-and-ict>). [Gabon was elected third vice-chair of its bureau](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20261027/au-opens-6th-session-specialized-technical-committee-communication-and-ict>). On 26 October the digital economy minister and the AU commissioner [signed a host country agreement on ICT cooperation](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-union-africaine-un-accord-pour-accelerer-la-cooperation-numerique/>). The regulator ARCEP [hosted ARTAC, the Central African regulators' assembly, in Libreville on 20 and 21 October 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/afrique-centrale-lartac-veut-harmoniser-les-regles-du-jeu-numerique/>). [The session set up six permanent technical working groups](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-artac-six-groupes-de-travail-permanents-crees-pour-harmoniser-la-reglementation-en-afrique-centrale/>). On 2 March 2026 the minister [signed a memorandum with Smart Africa to set up its Digital Academy in Gabon](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-gabon-sallie-a-smart-africa-pour-former-les-talents-du-numerique/>). Gabon was [among 11 member states at Smart Africa's council of IT agencies in Rabat in July 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/smart-africa-mobilises-11-member-states-in-the-kingdom-of-morocco-to-co-create-africas-first-cross-border-data-exchange-guidelines>), which drafted guidelines on cross-border data exchange. It is [among 23 states endorsing the African Common Proposals for the 2026 ITU conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** [The Smart Africa Digital Academy was officially launched in Gabon on 2 May 2026](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/05/04/lancement-du-programme-smart-africa-youth-chapter-gabon-sayc-gabon-en-faveur-de-la-jeunesse/>), with an online platform and the Centre gabonais de l'innovation as national hub. [It carries the Smart Africa Youth Chapter Gabon programme](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/05/04/lancement-du-programme-smart-africa-youth-chapter-gabon-sayc-gabon-en-faveur-de-la-jeunesse/>). No figures of its use are reported.
