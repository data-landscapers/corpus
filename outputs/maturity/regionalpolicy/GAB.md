# Gabon

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** The sixth session of the African Union's technical committee on communication and ICT [was held in Libreville in October 2026](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20261027/au-opens-6th-session-specialized-technical-committee-communication-and-ict>), and [Gabon was elected third vice-chair of its bureau](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20261027/au-opens-6th-session-specialized-technical-committee-communication-and-ict>). On 26 October the digital economy minister and the AU commissioner [signed a host country agreement on ICT cooperation](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-union-africaine-un-accord-pour-accelerer-la-cooperation-numerique/>). The regulator ARCEP [hosted ARTAC, the Central African regulators' assembly, in Libreville on 20 and 21 October 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/afrique-centrale-lartac-veut-harmoniser-les-regles-du-jeu-numerique/>). In February 2026 ARCEP [signed a roaming agreement with the Congolese regulator at ARTAC's session in Kinshasa](<https://art.cm/en/node/246964>). [It removes roaming charges between Gabon and the DRC from 30 June 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2502-53256-gabon-drc-to-scrap-cross-border-roaming-charges-from-june-2026>). As lead country of the One Africa Network, ARCEP [joined a Smart Africa workshop in Lagos in June 2026](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/114>). On 2 March 2026 the minister [signed a memorandum with Smart Africa to set up its Digital Academy in Gabon](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-gabon-sallie-a-smart-africa-pour-former-les-talents-du-numerique/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** ARCEP says Gabon [has opened free roaming with Rwanda, Congo, Togo and Cameroon](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>), as of February 2025. In June 2026 it [put Gabon's application of the One Africa Network at 91%](<https://www.arcep.ga/pages/detail_actu/114>). [The Smart Africa Digital Academy was officially launched in Gabon on 2 May 2026](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/05/04/lancement-du-programme-smart-africa-youth-chapter-gabon-sayc-gabon-en-faveur-de-la-jeunesse/>), with the Centre gabonais de l'innovation as national hub. No figures of the use of either are reported.
