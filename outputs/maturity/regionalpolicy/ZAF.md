# South Africa

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** South Africa's budget [provides a membership transfer of 2.073 million rand to the African Telecommunications Union for 2026/27](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/documents/National%20Budget/2026/ene/Vote%2030%20Communications%20and%20Digital%20Technologies.pdf>). Its delegates [took part in the Southern African Development Community's regional workshop on data governance in Harare in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>). It [hosted and chaired the Community's summit in August 2026, with digitised borders a chairship priority](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-seeks-turn-borders-gateways-trade-travel-and-regional-integration>), and [was a launching partner of the African Union development agency's semiconductor advisory group](<https://www.ninajojer.com/insights/auda-nepad-launches-africa-semiconductor-technical-advisory-group-astag-to-drive-continental-technological-sovereignty>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the digitised borders priority of its regional chairship](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/sadc-seeks-turn-borders-gateways-trade-travel-and-regional-integration>), set in August 2026; no border system is reported as started under it. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
