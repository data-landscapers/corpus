# Kenya

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Kenya is [one of the seven member states of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), through its regulator. It is [a partner state with Uganda, Rwanda and South Sudan in the Northern Corridor Integration Projects](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>), and its ministry [announced a pan-African artificial intelligence initiative with four other African ministries in July 2026](<https://www.gsma.com/newsroom/all-documents/gsma-welcomes-abuja-declaration-on-meaningful-connectivity-for-africa-and-joins-partners-to-launch-atlas-umoja/>). It is [one of three pilot countries for the continental free trade area's cross-border data exchange system](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/05/28/building-same-road-twice/>). Its central bank [signed a memorandum with Rwanda's in March 2026 letting payment providers licensed in one state operate in the other](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The regional regulators' organisation [publishes Kenya's One Network Area cross-border voice figures for 2025: 461.5 million inbound minutes, up 19.2 per cent, and 603.5 million outbound](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). The four Northern Corridor states [opened a joint bank account to pay for a satellite feasibility study](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>), reported in August 2026. Kenya [shares customs data and scanned cargo images through the East African Community's single customs territory platform, operational since July 2024](<https://mag.wcoomd.org/magazine/wco-news-106-issue-1-2025/kenya-uganda-exchanging-images-scanned-cargo/>).
