# Uganda

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Uganda [was elected to a four-year seat on the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union in Abuja on 24 July 2026](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/uganda-wins-seat-on-the-african-telecommunications-union-administrative-council/>). It [chairs and hosted in Kampala the Northern Corridor Integration Projects cluster meeting with Kenya, Rwanda and South Sudan in August 2026](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>), and is [a member state of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). A trade ministry official [chaired the East African Community's regional e-commerce engagement platform meeting of 13 to 15 October 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3609-eac-partner-states-advance-measure-to-strengthen-e-commerce-and-digital-trade>), and the union reports Uganda [among 23 states endorsing 49 African common proposals for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The regional regulators' organisation [reports that Uganda's inbound One Network Area cross-border voice traffic fell 28 per cent in 2025, from 1,103.2 million to 794.4 million minutes](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), which it attributes to internet calling services replacing voice. [The same report](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>) covers the One Network Area, the East African Community's shared roaming arrangement, under which roaming tariffs between member states were cut.
