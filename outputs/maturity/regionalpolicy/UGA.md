# Uganda

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Uganda [was elected to a four-year seat on the administrative council of the African Telecommunications Union in Abuja on 24 July 2026](<https://www.ucc.co.ug/uganda-wins-seat-on-the-african-telecommunications-union-administrative-council/>). It [chairs the Northern Corridor Integration Projects cluster, and hosted its meeting in Kampala in August 2026](<https://chimpreports.com/east-african-states-open-bank-account-to-fund-joint-communications-satellite-study/>). The meeting was with Kenya, Rwanda and South Sudan. It is also [a member state of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). A trade ministry official [chaired the East African Community's regional e-commerce engagement platform meeting of 13 to 15 October 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3609-eac-partner-states-advance-measure-to-strengthen-e-commerce-and-digital-trade>). The African Telecommunications Union reports Uganda [among 23 states endorsing 49 African common proposals for the 2026 ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>). [A parliamentary committee said in November 2026 that Uganda has signed and ratified the East African Community's 2013 protocol on ICT networks](<https://horneconomicreview.com/2026/11/02/uganda-mps-push-eala-to-pressure-eac-states-on-ict-protocol-ratification/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The One Network Area is the East African Community's shared roaming area. It cut roaming tariffs between member states. The regional regulators' organisation [reports Uganda's inbound cross-border voice traffic under it fell 28 per cent in 2025](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>). It went from 1,103.2 million to 794.4 million minutes. The report puts this down to internet calling replacing voice. In November 2026 the regional affairs minister told Parliament that the East African Payment System [links the national RTGS payment systems of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en>).
