# Burundi

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Burundi is [one of the seven member states of the East African Communications Organisation](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), and [its Senate voted to ratify the East African Community protocol on regional digital integration](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-embraces-eac-digital-integration-for-ict-growth/>), reported in January 2025. It [took part in the Community's Digital for Development Forum in Arusha in June 2026](<https://finances.gov.bi/index.php/2026/06/26/le-burundi-prend-part-a-la-construction-du-marche-unique-numerique-de-lafrique-de-lest/>) and in [its technical committee on telecommunications in May 2026](<https://www.eac.int/eadrip-news-updates/eardip-press-releases/3537-eac-advances-development-of-a-regional-mobile-roaming-framework-to-enhance-cross-border-connectivity-and-trade>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Burundi [entered the East African Community One Network Area on 1 August 2024](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-embraces-eac-digital-integration-for-ict-growth/>), with regulations from its regulator cutting regional roaming tariffs. The regional regulators' organisation reports that [inbound cross-border voice traffic rose 126 per cent in 2025, from 89.6 million to 202.9 million minutes](<https://admin.eaco.int/assets/e774142a-21fd-48e2-bdfd-3309d7d559d7?download=EACO%20Regional%20Communications%20Sector%20Statistics%20Report%202025.pdf>), and attributes the rise to that accession.
