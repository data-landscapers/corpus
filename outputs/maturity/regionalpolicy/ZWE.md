# Zimbabwe

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Zimbabwe's ICT minister [chairs Smart Africa's council of ICT ministers](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>), reported in August 2026. Zimbabwe's ministry [hosted in Harare the Southern African Development Community's regional workshop on data governance in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>), [a three-day meeting on harmonising data policy](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>). Its data protection authority [holds the second vice presidency of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://technomag.co.zw/potraz-joins-continental-partners-to-commemorate-inaugural-africa-data-protection-day/>), and Zimbabwe [was the 26th state to join Smart Africa](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the Harare workshop on harmonising data policy](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>); no harmonised rule adopted in Zimbabwe is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
