# Zimbabwe

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Zimbabwe's ICT minister [chairs Smart Africa's council of ICT ministers](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>), reported in August 2026. [In January 2026 she was named one of seven ministers on Smart Africa's artificial intelligence council](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ict-ministry-readies-national-ai-strategy-launchas-investment-grows-over-1/>). Zimbabwe's ministry [hosted in Harare the Southern African Development Community's regional workshop on data governance in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/sadc-puts-spotlight-on-ai-data-governance/JN1gP7OAw5wqjL6m>), [a three-day meeting on harmonising data policy](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>). [The Community's ICT ministers met in Bulawayo on 26 June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7857/mozambique-joins-sadc-regional-working-group-on-data-protection>). Its data protection authority [holds the second vice presidency of the Network of African Data Protection Authorities](<https://technomag.co.zw/potraz-joins-continental-partners-to-commemorate-inaugural-africa-data-protection-day/>), and [has signed a memorandum on data protection with South Africa's Information Regulator](<https://privacyscorecardreport.com/privacy-scorecard-report-2025/zimbabwe/zimbabwes-data-protection-regime/>), reported in 2025. Zimbabwe [was the 26th state to join Smart Africa](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/one-voice-for-africas-data-inside-harares-push-to-harmonise-the-rules-of-the-digital-age/>). By October 2026 it was also [among 23 states endorsing the 49 African Common Proposals for the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7771/atu-secures-endorsements-for-49-african-proposals-ahead-of-itu-plenipotentiary-conference>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** Citing Botswana's regulator, a March 2026 report says [Zimbabwe's mobile operators introduced roaming price cuts of 10 to 98.6 per cent](<https://theafricanmirror.africa/news/cheaper-cross-border-call-rates-beckon-on-lower-tariffs-push/>). The cuts come under the Southern African Development Community's One Network Area. Figures of use of the lower tariffs are not recorded.
