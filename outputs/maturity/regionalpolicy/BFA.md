# Burkina Faso

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Burkina Faso's regulator [signed a free roaming memorandum with those of Mali and Niger in Niamey in November 2024](<https://www.lesahel.org/integration-numerique-larcep-niger-celebre-son-25e-anniversaire-avec-la-signature-dun-memorandum-dentente-sur-le-free-roaming-entre-les-pays-membres-de-l/>). It [signed a free roaming memorandum with Ghana's regulator on 21 November 2025](<https://nca.org.gh/2025/11/25/ghana-and-burkina-faso-sign-mou-to-drive-affordable-cross-border-mobile-communication/>). In April 2026 it [hosted the working groups of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly](<https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/>) in Ouagadougou. In September 2026 it [signed a convention with Mali and Niger creating the Council of Sahel Regulators](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>). In October 2026 it [chaired and hosted the WAEMU committee of national digital regulators](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150075=>). On 23 October 2026 it [signed roaming agreements with Benin, Cote d'Ivoire and Senegal](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). On 5 November 2026 it [led the workshop that finalised the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** On 25 September 2026 Niger's regulator head, in Ouagadougou, [said free roaming among the three Sahel states was already working](<https://www.radarsburkina.net/index.php/fr/politique/4017-cooperation-numerique-dans-l-aes-les-regulateurs-passent-de-la-coordination-a-l-action>). She had tested it with a call. [The Council of Sahel Regulators was then still to adopt its statutes and work programme](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>).
