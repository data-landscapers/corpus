# Burkina Faso

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Burkina Faso's regulator [signed a convention with the regulators of Mali and Niger](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. It created the Council of Sahel Regulators. In April 2026 it [hosted the working groups of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly](<https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/>) in Ouagadougou. It [signed a free roaming agreement with Ghana's regulator on 21 November 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/communications-electroniques-le-burkina-le-ghana-le-mali-et-le-niger-harmonisent-leurs-frequences/>). In October 2026 it [chaired and hosted the WAEMU committee of national digital regulators](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article150075=>). On 23 October 2026 it [signed roaming agreements with Benin, Cote d'Ivoire and Senegal](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>). On 5 November 2026 it [led the workshop that finalised the digital part of the Sahel States' Year II roadmap](<https://www.sidwaya.info/2026/11/05/numerique-dans-lespace-aes-la-feuille-de-route-de-lan-ii-adoptee/>), with 64 experts from the three states.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), which creates the Council of Sahel Regulators. Its statutes, rules and first work programme were still to be adopted. Free roaming between the three states was an aim. No source found reports a roaming cut, a joint body at work or a shared service opened.
