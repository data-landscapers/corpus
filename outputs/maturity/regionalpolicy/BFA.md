# Burkina Faso

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Burkina Faso's regulator [signed a cooperation convention with the regulators of Mali and Niger](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), reported in September 2026. It [hosted the working groups of the West Africa Telecommunications Regulators Assembly in Ouagadougou in April 2026](<https://guardian.ng/news/watra-rallies-africa-for-regions-216b-digital-economy/>) and [signed a free roaming agreement with Ghana's regulator on 21 November 2025](<https://www.sidwaya.info/communications-electroniques-le-burkina-le-ghana-le-mali-et-le-niger-harmonisent-leurs-frequences/>). Its minister [attended the African Telecommunications Union conference in Abuja in July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/infrastructures-ia-et-talents-le-burkina-faso-et-le-nigeria-renforcent-leur-cooperation-numerique>). It [signed roaming agreements with Benin, Cote d'Ivoire and Senegal in Ouagadougou on 23 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2610-60121-west-africa-struggles-to-deliver-free-roaming-nearly-nine-years-after-regional-agreement>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the convention of September 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article149528>), which creates the Council of Sahel Regulators; its statutes, rules of procedure and first work programme are still to be adopted, and free roaming between the three states is an aim. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
