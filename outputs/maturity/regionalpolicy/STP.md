# Sao Tome and Principe

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Regulators from Sao Tome and Principe [took part in a data governance workshop in Maputo on 8 to 10 December 2025](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance>) and [in the second Lusophone Africa data governance workshop in Luanda in June 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda>). The country is [named in the programme for African developing island economies launched by the African Union's development agency on 24 July 2026](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). Its regulator [joined the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly as its eighth member in Libreville on 21 October 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/artac-libreville-acte-une-reforme-importante-et-accueille-sao-tome-et-principe/>). An agreement signed, a meeting hosted or chaired, or a seat won in the last three years is not recorded.

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest held fact is [the island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>), launched in July 2026 with digital transformation among its pillars, and nothing done under it in the country is reported. A roaming cut, a joint body set up, a regional guideline adopted as a national rule or a shared service opened is not recorded, and neither are figures of use or a decision of a joint body.
