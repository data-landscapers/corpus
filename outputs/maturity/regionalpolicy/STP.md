# Sao Tome and Principe

## Regional cooperation on digital policy

**Part taken in regional digital policy.** Regulators from Sao Tome and Principe [took part in a data governance workshop in Maputo on 8 to 10 December 2025](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/lusophone-countries-africa-deepen-digital-cooperation-strengthen-data-governance>). They also took part [in the second Lusophone Africa data governance workshop in Luanda in June 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/advancing-data-infrastructure-and-governance-lusophone-africa-regional-workshop-concludes-luanda>). The country is [named in the programme for African developing island economies](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>). The African Union's development agency launched it on 24 July 2026. Its regulator [joined the Central African telecoms regulators' assembly as its eighth member in Libreville on 21 October 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/artac-libreville-acte-une-reforme-importante-et-accueille-sao-tome-et-principe/>). The African Telecommunications Union [lists it among its member states in January 2026](<https://atuuat.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/4-Status-of-Ratification-and-Accession-by-Member-states-_Jan-2026.pdf>). It is a member under the 1977 convention, without signing the 1999 constitution. In March 2025 it was [one of ten countries selected for Smart Africa's project on digital payments and e-commerce policy](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/telecom/2803-46565-smart-africa-pushes-for-a-unified-e-commerce-policy-across-the-continent>).

**What was agreed put into effect and in use.** The nearest fact is [the island economies programme](<https://www.nepad.org/news/auda-nepad-launches-first-continental-programme-dedicated-africas-island-economies>), launched in July 2026 with digital transformation among its pillars. Nothing done under it in the country is reported.
