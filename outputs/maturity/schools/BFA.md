# Burkina Faso

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's statistics directorate [issued the pilot-phase report on FIUE-BF, its name for a national file of pupils with unique identification, in February 2024](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>). On 25 June 2025 two ministers [reviewed the national pupil file, built with WURI project support](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). A World Bank appraisal of April 2025 [finances a student management platform](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>).

**Levels in use.** At 25 June 2025 [the national platform was meant to follow pupils from primary school to university](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). Enrolment had not begun.

**How the record is kept.** LuxDev states that in 2023 [the establishments in the FIUE-BF pilot handled enrolment, the publication of school results and school identity cards online](<https://luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2025-04/Fiche_BKF025.pdf>). The same appraisal states that [key education data are stored mainly as hard copies](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>). KIX-SEEDS is a university-led pilot. In May 2026 it had [teachers entering pupils' marks in an offline Android app, with heads following attendance as it is entered](<https://seeds.bf/post_detail/4d824034-e6d1-4182-acf5-b8088d4a5a42>).

**Last twelve months.** LuxDev's project page [shows the programme that supported unique pupil identifiers as closed on 31 December 2025](<https://luxdev.lu/fr/projets/appui-au-plan-sectoriel-de-leducation-et-de-la-formation-2017-2030>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In February 2024 [FIUE-BF was at its pilot phase, with generalisation still ahead](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>). In April 2025 [the student management platform was still to be designed](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>). At 25 June 2025 [progressive enrolment of pupils was due to start at the next school year](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). KIX-SEEDS [ran in the pilot communes of Manga and Bingo in May 2026](<https://seeds.bf/post_detail/4d824034-e6d1-4182-acf5-b8088d4a5a42>), with wider extension still planned.
