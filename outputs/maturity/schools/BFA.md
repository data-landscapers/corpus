# Burkina Faso

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's statistics directorate [issued the pilot-phase report on FIUE-BF, its name for a national file of pupils with unique identification, in February 2024](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>). On 25 June 2025 the ministers of digital transition and basic education [reviewed the national pupil file their technical teams are building](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>), [with technical and financial support from the WURI project](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>). The World Bank's appraisal of 14 April 2025 [finances a student management platform from IDA and the Global Partnership for Education](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>).

**Levels in use.** At 25 June 2025 [the national platform was meant to follow pupils from primary school to university, and enrolment had not begun](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).

**How the record is kept.** LuxDev states that in 2023 [the establishments in the FIUE-BF pilot handled enrolment, the publication of school results and school identity cards online](<https://luxdev.lu/sites/default/files/upload/media/document/2025-04/Fiche_BKF025.pdf>). The World Bank's appraisal of 14 April 2025 states that [key education data are stored mainly as hard copies](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** LuxDev's project page [shows the programme that supported tracking each pupil by a unique identifier as closed, having run from 4 May 2018 to 31 December 2025](<https://luxdev.lu/fr/projets/appui-au-plan-sectoriel-de-leducation-et-de-la-formation-2017-2030>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In February 2024 [FIUE-BF was at its pilot phase, with generalisation still ahead](<https://sp-psdebs.education-bf.com/uploads/document/U-1756488494.pdf>). At 14 April 2025 [the student management platform, with a smartphone application for schools, was still to be designed](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>), and at 25 June 2025 [progressive enrolment of pupils was due to start at the next school year](<https://www.latribunedufaso.net/?p=21538>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the primary school statistical survey, keyed in [STATEDUC 2](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/52de6f6c-85ed-41fb-94d9-19244bab25a8/Annuaire_Primaire_2023-2024_26_09_2024.pdf>), the [Annuaire statistique primaire 2023-2024](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>) and the [Annuaire statistique 2019-2020 du MENAPLN](<https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/document/download/2f36acfa-a4d9-4ed3-80e9-3f634e1579d1_en>). Single-function: the planned [shared platform for examination and learner-assessment data](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>). Learning: the planned [digital platforms for in-service teacher training](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-04-program-document-system-transformation-grant-burkina-faso.pdf>). Enabling: the [Digital Acceleration Project](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>).

***Not held***. The number of pilot establishments, and whether they are primary or secondary, is not held, and no source reports the pupil file past its pilot. No current plan naming the system is held, nor whether results are entered during the school year.
