# Zimbabwe

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education [presents the system it calls EMIS, and the report drawn from it, as its own](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). It did so in June 2026. The revised EMIS [has GPE funding of US$2.9 million for 2023 to 2026, with UNICEF supporting implementation](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>).

**Levels in use.** The ministry's EMIS report [counts 39,736 learners who dropped out in 2025, 13,032 at primary and 26,704 at secondary level](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). The ministry describes its Early Warning System without limiting it to either level.

**How the record is kept.** The Early Warning System is now integrated into EMIS. The ministry's spokesperson said in June 2026 that it [flags individual learners by tracking persistent absenteeism and falling grades and produces a real-time list of learners absent for extended periods](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). In November 2026 the revised EMIS [was administered at school level](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>).

**Last twelve months.** The spokesperson spoke in June 2026. The statement said that the Early Warning System [is being rolled out with re-entry and community tracing, and that the government will keep strengthening it](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). By November 2026 the revised EMIS [was replacing paper records at schools such as Masvingo Government Primary School](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** School-based response teams [act on the system's flags, and district and school authorities use its list of absent learners](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). The ministry said so in June 2026. In November 2026 [all 1,444 public schools in Masvingo Province used the new EMIS, and 12,324 schools nationwide had been equipped to use it](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>).

***Also on file***. Zimbabwe calls its yearly school census an EMIS. It is the ministry's [Annual School Census on form ED 46](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/zimbabwe_education_management_information_systems_peer_review_report.pdf>). It was a 20-page questionnaire filled in by hand at about 8,953 schools, with a 95% response rate for 2019. [Climate and disaster-risk questions were added in 2025](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/en/articles/zimbabwes-climate-smart-annual-school-census>). Single-function: the ministry's [Information Management System for school registration](<https://jobs.unicef.org/en-us/job/592432/development-of-an-information-management-system-for-school-registration-at-the-ministry-of-primary-and-secondary-education-harare-zimbabve-5-months-nationals-only-home-based>), not yet built in April 2026. Standalone: [CAMFED's cloud-based database](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-11-gpe-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-grant-girls-education-accelerator-zimbabwe.pdf>), with a record for each student it supports. Enabling: the [Universal Service Fund's connectivity subscriptions for schools](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058417/starlink-kits-gather-dust-at-government-rural-hospitals-as-parent-ministry-dithers-on-subscription>), covered for one year. Also [Giga](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-03-zimbabwe-partnership-compact.pdf>), with 6,111 schools targeted for connectivity in 2022. Also the [pilot to solarise 150 schools](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-03-zimbabwe-partnership-compact.pdf>), 36 of them done by 2022.

***Not found***. No source gives a national share of primary schools entering attendance or grades: the 12,324 are schools equipped, not schools entering. How often schools enter attendance is not stated, and no plan naming the system has been found.
