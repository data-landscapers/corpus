# Zimbabwe

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education [presents the system it calls EMIS, and the report drawn from it, as its own](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>), with its spokesperson speaking for it in June 2026.

**Levels in use.** The ministry's EMIS report [counts 39,736 learners who dropped out in 2025, 13,032 at primary and 26,704 at secondary level](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>), and the ministry describes its Early Warning System without limiting it to either level.

**How the record is kept.** The ministry's spokesperson said in June 2026 that the Early Warning System, now integrated into EMIS, [flags individual learners by tracking persistent absenteeism and falling grades and produces a real-time list of learners absent for extended periods](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>).

**Last twelve months.** The spokesperson said in June 2026 that the Early Warning System [is being rolled out with re-entry and community tracing, and that the government will keep strengthening it](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** School-based response teams [act on the system's flags, and district and school authorities use its list of absent learners](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>), the ministry said in June 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. Zimbabwe calls its yearly school census an EMIS: the ministry's [Annual School Census on form ED 46](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/zimbabwe_education_management_information_systems_peer_review_report.pdf>) was a 20-page questionnaire filled in by hand at about 8,953 schools, with a 95% response rate for 2019, and [climate and disaster-risk questions were added in 2025](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/en/articles/zimbabwes-climate-smart-annual-school-census>). Single-function: the ministry's [Information Management System for school registration](<https://jobs.unicef.org/en-us/job/592432/development-of-an-information-management-system-for-school-registration-at-the-ministry-of-primary-and-secondary-education-harare-zimbabve-5-months-nationals-only-home-based>), not yet built in April 2026. Standalone: [CAMFED's cloud-based database](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-11-gpe-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-grant-girls-education-accelerator-zimbabwe.pdf>), with a record for each student it supports. Enabling: the [Universal Service Fund's connectivity subscriptions for schools](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058417/starlink-kits-gather-dust-at-government-rural-hospitals-as-parent-ministry-dithers-on-subscription>), covered for one year; [Giga](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-03-zimbabwe-partnership-compact.pdf>), with 6,111 schools targeted for connectivity in 2022; and the [pilot to solarise 150 schools](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-03-zimbabwe-partnership-compact.pdf>), 36 of them done by 2022.

***Not held***. Who enters the attendance and grades, and where, is not held, nor any count or share of schools in the Early Warning System. No plan or source of finance for it is held.
