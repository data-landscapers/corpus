# Central African Republic

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the yearly school count, which the Ministry of National Education calls its [EMIS](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>) or [SIGE](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/891981593669171779/pdf/R%C3%A9publique-Centrafricaine-Plan-Sectoriel-de-l-Education-2020-2029.pdf>), the [school census](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>), the [statistical yearbook](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053124094568400/txt/BOSIB-48de699a-5a74-4683-8c51-ad967d617219.txt>) and, for higher education, [StatEduc_RCA_SUP](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/891981593669171779/pdf/R%C3%A9publique-Centrafricaine-Plan-Sectoriel-de-l-Education-2020-2029.pdf>). Single-function: the teacher systems [HRMIS](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>), [ESIGAPE](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/891981593669171779/pdf/R%C3%A9publique-Centrafricaine-Plan-Sectoriel-de-l-Education-2020-2029.pdf>), [EduTrac](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/republique-centrafricaine/~technologie>) and the planned [SIGRH](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/891981593669171779/pdf/R%C3%A9publique-Centrafricaine-Plan-Sectoriel-de-l-Education-2020-2029.pdf>), and the [National Learning Assessment System](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061526153018839/pdf/BOSIB-a5df712a-2126-49ab-8538-d2e23c305751.pdf>). Learning: [digital readers and digital libraries](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/republique-centrafricaine/~technologie>) and [radio teaching](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/republique-centrafricaine/~technologie>). Enabling: the [PARSE off-grid solar systems for schools](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/republique-centrafricaine/~technologie>).

***Not held***. No dated statement of how primary schools keep their learners' records is held. No system holding a record per learner is described.
