# Togo

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The primary and secondary education ministry (MEPS) [launched the registration of pupils with a number](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>) in 2023. A minister's [service note of 2 August 2021](<https://icilome.com/2021/08/togo-vers-la-creation-dune-plateforme-unique-de-gestion-de-notes-des-eleves/>) gave the building of a single platform for pupils' marks to the ministry's own centre, CNDP-TICE.

**Levels in use.** The [marks platform ordered in August 2021](<https://icilome.com/2021/08/togo-vers-la-creation-dune-plateforme-unique-de-gestion-de-notes-des-eleves/>) was for all public and private schools, consultable at every level of the system.

**How the record is kept.** The independent panel advising the Global Partnership for Education [found on 8 April 2024](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf>) no clear evidence that Togo has student identification numbers.

**Last twelve months.** The joint sector review's aide-mémoire of June 2025 [reports the 2023 launch](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>) of pupil numbering.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The 2023 work was the [design of the enrolment platform](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>) that generates the pupils' numbers. The panel [said in April 2024](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf>) that an automatic data collection system was still being developed.

***Noted, not assessed***. Togo calls its school census an EMIS: data from the 16,000 nursery, primary and secondary schools is collected on paper forms and entered into [StatEduc](<https://education.dhis2.org/emis-togo-2022/>) at the education inspectorates, as described in 2022, and the [SIGE on DHIS2](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>), which the country also calls the EMIS, receives validated data weeks after collection, as reported in 2026. The [annual statistical census and Statistical Yearbook](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>) are yearly, and a [School report card](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo>) is produced on DHIS2. The June 2025 aide-mémoire names the teachers' [carte unique de compétences](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>), a [carte scolaire prospective numérique](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>) piloted in seven prefectures and a [platform for teachers' continuing training](<https://education.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Aide-me%CC%81moire-RSCE_Togo_2025.pdf>). A World Bank report of January 2025 names a [Human Resources Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>) in test, a [monitoring platform for school inspectorates](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>), a [distance learning platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>) prototype and the [CNEA global dashboard](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099011425201027440/pdf/P172674-dd66e6f3-560a-4916-b99c-93ecb5bb6f4a.pdf>) for learning assessment. Also named are a [school observation tool](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>) on Android devices, a [WhatsApp bot for exam results](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo>) and [SYNECAT](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-togo-itap-report-fr.pdf>), a planned sample assessment of learning.

***Not held***. No school is named as using the pupil-numbering platform and no pilot is reported; the marks platform was ordered in 2021, not built. No level, plan, finance or event in the last twelve months is held.
