# Chad

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Chad calls its school census an EMIS: the [EMIS](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>) of the education ministry's planning directorate, described in 2021, is one paper questionnaire of four to ten pages per school a year, filled by the director, carried up to the centre and keyed there. Under its French name, the [SIGE](<https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/stories/supporting-middle-tier-actors-chad-access-and-use-data-decision-making>), it has collected data from schools since 2009 and is decentralised to 16 provinces, with 12,863 primary schools as of 2025. Its yearbook, the [annuaire statistique scolaire](<https://www.ndjamenaactu.com/2025/10/07/education-la-statistique-scolaire-2024-2025-en-validation/>), was validated for 2024-2025 at a workshop in N'Djamena on 7 October 2025. The higher education ministry trained its statistics staff on the [StatEduc application](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/le-mesrsfp-renforce-les-capacites-de-ses-agents-sur-lapplication-stateduc-pour-ameliorer-la>) in July 2026. The 2021 study also describes the [Rapport de la rentrée scolaire and Rapport de la fin de l'année](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>) that every school director sends to the inspector and district; UNHCR's [refugee education data collection](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>) in all 107 schools in camps and settlements, on paper at the school and in Excel above it; UNHCR's yearly [tablet questionnaire through Kobo and ProGress](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>) for refugee children at enrolment; UNICEF's [EduTrac](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>), a mobile-phone pilot in two provinces; and a [teacher database](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Chad.pdf>) being created by APICED. The 2023 partnership compact proposes a [computerised tool for managing teachers](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-11-pacte-partenariat-tchad.pdf>) and a [distance-training platform for teachers](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-11-pacte-partenariat-tchad.pdf>). A 2024 World Bank project document lists a [distance learning platform](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>) among existing public platforms.

***Not held***. Nothing held states how primary schools keep their pupils' records. The one statement on paper, from 2021, is about the yearly census questionnaire.
