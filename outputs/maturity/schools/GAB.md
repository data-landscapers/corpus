# Gabon

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The learner identification is [run by SOSUP, a state body, and announced by the ministry's Secretary-General as compulsory](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-de-la-seconde-phase-didentification-des-etablissements-publics-et-prives-du-05-au-30-novembre/>), on 4 November 2025. Xgest [belongs to the start-up Groupe Tereza, with the State its client](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-lapplication-xgest-bientot-utilisee-dans-lenseignement-superieur/>), as at 27 September 2024. On 24 September 2024 the ministry [closed pupil enrolment on X-Gest after irregularities and ordered that no personal data pass through it](<https://agpgabon.ga/education-nationale-fermeture-des-inscriptions-sur-lapplication-x-gest/>).

**Levels in use.** The identification for 2025/2026 [covers public and private pre-primary, primary and secondary schools](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-de-la-seconde-phase-didentification-des-etablissements-publics-et-prives-du-05-au-30-novembre/>), the Secretary-General stated on 4 November 2025. The World Bank's 2019 diagnostic put X-Gest [in 92 secondary schools, expanding to the remaining secondary schools and to primary schools](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P17016008155bc0570b002059059dfec929.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** Schools [managed pupils' numbers and marks and published results in Xgest](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-lapplication-xgest-bientot-utilisee-dans-lenseignement-superieur/>), on Gabonreview's account of 27 September 2024, and [enrolments and re-enrolments for 2024-2025 were made on it](<https://agpgabon.ga/education-nationale-fermeture-des-inscriptions-sur-lapplication-x-gest/>). The 2025/2026 identification [is made online once for the school year](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-de-la-seconde-phase-didentification-des-etablissements-publics-et-prives-du-05-au-30-novembre/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 4 November 2025 the ministry [opened a second phase of school identification, announced the online learner identification for 2025/2026 and barred pupils of unidentified schools from it](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-de-la-seconde-phase-didentification-des-etablissements-publics-et-prives-du-05-au-30-novembre/>). Focus Groupe Media reported in March 2026 that SOSUP [had been deploying eFormation, giving every establishment and every pupil a unique identifier](<https://www.focusgroupemedia.com/gabon-eformation-loffensive-numerique-contre-lanarchie-scolaire-avec-des-identifiants-uniques-pour-les-etablissements-et-apprenants/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Gabonreview reported on 27 September 2024 that [Xgest is used by 728 schools](<https://www.gabonreview.com/gabon-lapplication-xgest-bientot-utilisee-dans-lenseignement-superieur/>), a count of schools of all levels together. The ministry [posts the list of schools registered for the identification on SOSUP's site](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-lancement-de-la-seconde-phase-didentification-des-etablissements-publics-et-prives-du-05-au-30-novembre/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: [KEWA](<https://gabonactu.com/blog/2026/06/27/cep-2026-kewa-revolutionne-la-publication-des-resultats-laninf-confirme-son-leadership-dans-la-transformation-numerique-du-gabon/>), which publishes primary certificate results online, the ministry's [examination management database](<https://pointnoirtv.com/2025/03/11/gabon-renforcement-de-la-collecte-des-donnees-scolaires-une-priorite-pour-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale>) and its [school assets database](<https://pointnoirtv.com/2025/03/11/gabon-renforcement-de-la-collecte-des-donnees-scolaires-une-priorite-pour-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale>). Learning: the [distance education system](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/UNESCO_org_UPR42_GAB_F_Main.pdf>). Enabling: the [tablets and interactive screens delivered in 2025-2026](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-education-camelia-ntoutoume-leclercq-fixe-les-priorites-pour-lannee-scolaire-2026-2027/>), the [tablets for seven primary schools of Ogooué-Ivindo](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-education-des-tablettes-et-manuels-scolaires-remis-aux-etablissements-de-logooue-ivindo/>), the [1,000 tablets and 148 connected schools of the national strategy](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-remise-de-1-000-tablettes-au-ministere-de-leducation-nationale/>) and the [connected multimedia rooms and digital classrooms](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P17016008155bc0570b002059059dfec929.pdf>).

***Not held***. The 728 schools come with no total and no level of schooling, so no share of primary schools is held. Whether the school or the family makes the yearly identification entry, how many schools are in the register, and any plan or budget for either system are not stated.
