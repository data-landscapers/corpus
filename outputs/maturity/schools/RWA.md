# Rwanda

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** HISP Rwanda, on a page of 7 May 2026, [names the Ministry of Education as owner of the KARAME attendance app](<https://hisprwanda.org/project/karame-mobile-application/>). The Education Sector Strategic Plan 2024-2029 [lists improving digital attendance tracking and an early warning response system](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/PLANS/Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan/The_Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan__ESSP__2024-2029.pdf>) among its interventions.

**Levels in use.** UNICEF Rwanda, on 12 June 2024, describes the School Data Management System, SDMS, [in use at Nyarubuye Primary School in Rwamagana District](<https://www.unicef.org/rwanda/stories/power-internet-connectivity-enhances-learning>), where the head teacher enters the data.

**How the record is kept.** SDMS [holds a profile for each student and captures national examination results](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>), a World Bank study presented in February 2024 found. In KARAME, teachers and headteachers [record each student's daily attendance at the school, and the data syncs to the national education server](<https://hisprwanda.org/project/karame-mobile-application/>), on HISP Rwanda's account of May 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The ministry [rolled out a new digital attendance tracking system](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/32152/news/technology/new-digital-attendance-system-aims-to-curb-school-dropouts>), The New Times reported on 23 December 2025. HISP Rwanda reported on 11 February 2026 a mission to [strengthen the absence tracking system and move its technical support to the local team](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** KARAME is live and [supports the monitoring of 177,000 reintegrated students](<https://hisprwanda.org/project/karame-mobile-application/>), as HISP Rwanda stated in May 2026. The same body names [two schools using the absence tracking system](<https://hisprwanda.org/hisp-rwanda-partners-with-save-the-children-waliku-and-mineduc-to-strengthen-education-data-systems-and-advance-zero-out-of-school-children-goal/>), and the World Bank study describes SDMS as [in use by schools and teachers](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school count is the school census behind the [Education Statistical Yearbook](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/REPORTS/Education_Statistics/Education_statistical_yearbook/Education_Statistical_Year_Book_2024-2025.pdf>), whose 2023/24 edition [draws on SDMS](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/REPORTS/Education_Statistics/Education_statistical_yearbook/Final_Education_Statistical_Yearbook_23_24.pdf>); the ministry's Partnership Compact of March 2023 calls the system producing its annual statistical reports the [Education Management Information System, EMIS](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/EDUCATION_POLICIES/Rwanda_Partnership_Compact.pdf>). Single-function: the [Teacher Management Information System](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>), the [ICT in Education Management Information System](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>), the [Teaching and Learning Materials Management Information System](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/PLANS/Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan/The_Education_Sector_Strategic_Plan__ESSP__2024-2029.pdf>) and the [Quality Assurance Management Information System](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/EDUCATION_POLICIES/Rwanda_Partnership_Compact.pdf>). Standalone: the [Berulo Foundation attendance monitoring system](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>) and [Brain Ink's grading and assessment tool](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>). Learning: the [REB e-learning platform](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>), [RwandaEQUIP](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/EDUCATION_POLICIES/Rwanda_Partnership_Compact.pdf>), [Itetero and Ingazi-Passport to Earning](<https://www.unicef.org/rwanda/stories/power-internet-connectivity-enhances-learning>), [SOMABOX](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>) and the [Nedu App](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/eight-rwandan-startups-are-reimagining-how-technology-can-improve-learning/>). Enabling: [One Laptop Per Child and the smart classrooms](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/e4ce55dbf9e381faa76ce4bad55d336d-0140022025/related/2Rwanda-TF0C2272-Final-Report-Learning-Resilience-in-Rwanda-Basic-Education-System.pdf>), UNICEF and Airtel's [Reimagine Education](<https://www.unicef.org/rwanda/stories/power-internet-connectivity-enhances-learning>) school connections, and school internet, which the 2023/24 yearbook gives as [61.8 per cent of schools connected](<https://www.mineduc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Mineduc/Publications/REPORTS/Education_Statistics/Education_statistical_yearbook/Final_Education_Statistical_Yearbook_23_24.pdf>).

***Not held***. A share or count of primary schools entering attendance or results, and whether attendance reaches SDMS per learner or as a monthly total. Nothing is held on rural schools or on current finance.
