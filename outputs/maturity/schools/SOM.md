# Somalia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In May 2022 the Puntland education ministry [proposed to expand what it calls the Digital Attendance Application](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>) and took on the collection and analysis of its attendance data, with the roll-out placed under a [US$3.35 million GPE programme running May 2022 to April 2023](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>), UNICEF the grant agent.

**Levels in use.** The application's 2019 pilot in Puntland was in [3 primary schools](<https://www.wvi.org/sites/default/files/2021-03/WVS%20Annual%20Report%20Repaired_compressed.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** The application [collects daily student and teacher attendance on mobile devices](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>), offline where needed, and uploads it to the server of what Puntland calls its EMIS, as described in May 2022. In 2019 the pilot schools [moved from a paper attendance tracker to the application](<https://www.wvi.org/sites/default/files/2021-03/WVS%20Annual%20Report%20Repaired_compressed.pdf>), which records each student's attendance daily with reasons for absence.

**Last twelve months.** In May 2022 [state-wide collection of attendance data was not in place in Puntland](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>) and the state's EMIS was under development.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The application was [on trial in 42 schools in Puntland State in May 2022](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>), after the [2019 pilot in 3 primary schools](<https://www.wvi.org/sites/default/files/2021-03/WVS%20Annual%20Report%20Repaired_compressed.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Somalia calls its school census an EMIS: the federal education ministry's [EMIS](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>), started in 2016, collects data from schools once a year, as described in February 2024, and carries a [national examination results function](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>) for grades 8 and 12. The 2023 joint education sector review lists an [Annual School Census](<https://moe.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Somalia-JRES-Report-2023.pdf>), and Puntland's ministry publishes the [Puntland Education Statistics Yearbook](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-Program-document-Puntland-State-Somalia.pdf>). Single-function systems are the [Teacher Management Information System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100225092557188/pdf/P172434-c7c7f078-1bc2-439f-a6a6-aa0c69ca52b6.pdf>), to be expanded under a project amended in October 2025; Somaliland's [Education Registration and Certification System](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099045403212411797/txt/IDU105f167fa1085214f8b1919f141249b1e8fae.txt>), listed as operational in 2022; and the [National Digital Trust System](<https://wardheernews.com/digital-public-infrastructure-dpi-somalia-risks-repeating-old-mistakes-in-a-digital-form/>), under development in April 2026 for verifying academic credentials. The ministry's distance learning platform is [Fogaan-Dersi](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100225092557188/pdf/P172434-c7c7f078-1bc2-439f-a6a6-aa0c69ca52b6.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing is dated after May 2022, so whether the planned roll-out to about sixty schools took place is not established, and the level of the 42 trial schools is not stated. Nothing held describes a per-pupil record outside Puntland.
