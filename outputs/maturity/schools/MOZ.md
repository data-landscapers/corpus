# Mozambique

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's Plano Tecnológico da Educação of May 2019, with targets for 2020-2025, [names the ministry as responsible for designing the academic information system it calls SEGI, with provincial directorates, district services and schools implementing it](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>). The plan [costs the programme at 39.36 million meticais a year](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>).

**Levels in use.** The 2019 plan's [first-phase target was for 100% of secondary and 10% of primary schools to take part in SEGI](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** The policy brief on the KIX Africa 19 knowledge café of 22 July 2025 states that [the ministry's statistics system, StatEduc, is fed by censuses on physical questionnaires and holds data aggregated by class, subject and gender, not individual student records](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** GPE KIX reported on 21 August 2026 that [the collection of education data at school level is largely paper-based](<https://www.gpekix.org/news/mozambique-sets-priorities-stronger-education-data-system>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The KIX brief of July 2025 [recommends mandating individual student tracking "as piloted in Mozambique"](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the [Levantamento Escolar do 3 de Março](<https://mozdata.ine.gov.mz/index.php/catalog/119>) and the end-of-year [Levantamento do Aproveitamento Escolar](<https://mozdata.ine.gov.mz/index.php/catalog/119>), the two yearly school counts, held in [StatEduc](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf>); Mozambique calls this census system its EMIS, or [SIGE](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/238711617069817754/pdf/Mozambique-Improving-Learning-and-Empowering-Girls-in-Mozambique-Project.pdf>). Single-function: the [teachers' absenteeism annual monitoring system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/238711617069817754/pdf/Mozambique-Improving-Learning-and-Empowering-Girls-in-Mozambique-Project.pdf>), the [Plataforma online para a Gestão escolar](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2022-10-mozambique-jsr-aide-memoire.pdf>), the [automatic marking of examination papers](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>) and the [personnel management system](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>). Learning: the [national repository of digital content](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>) and [MozEduca](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>). Enabling: [computer rooms, equipment and internet for schools](<https://www.ined.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Plano-Tecnolo%CC%81gico-da-Educac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-2019-Vfinal-copy.pdf>) and [community and school connectivity projects](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>).

***Not held***. The pilot of individual student tracking is named with no system, level, number of schools or method of entry, and nothing says it has gone past its pilot. No ownership, plan or financing dated within the last three years is held.
