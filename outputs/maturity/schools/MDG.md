# Madagascar

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The digital enrolment of learners [is carried out by the Ministry of National Education and the Unité de gouvernance digitale, within the PRODIGY project](<https://2424.mg/education-le-suivi-scolaire-passe-a-lere-digitale-avec-le-numero-unique-didentification-pour-chaque-eleve/>), by the report of its launch on 11 May 2026. The World Bank's mission of December 2025 [counted its field activities among the items to draw down the remaining funds of the Bank-financed PRODIGY project before 30 June 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>).

**Levels in use.** The Numéro Unique d'Identification Scolaire, as the ministry names it, [gives each learner a permanent identifier from pre-school to lycée, in public and private establishments](<https://2424.mg/education-le-suivi-scolaire-passe-a-lere-digitale-avec-le-numero-unique-didentification-pour-chaque-eleve/>), the ministry said in May 2026.

**How the record is kept.** Learners [are enrolled digitally, once, to receive the identifier](<https://2424.mg/education-le-suivi-scolaire-passe-a-lere-digitale-avec-le-numero-unique-didentification-pour-chaque-eleve/>), in an operation of May 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In December 2025 the enrolment's [field activities were still to be carried out](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/pdf/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.pdf>), the World Bank's mission recorded. On 11 May 2026 [the scale-up was launched at a public lower-secondary school in Ambatolampy district, with a ministry target of 4.2 million learners from 19,303 establishments in 48 school districts by 12 June 2026](<https://2424.mg/education-le-suivi-scolaire-passe-a-lere-digitale-avec-le-numero-unique-didentification-pour-chaque-eleve/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Between March and April 2026 the pilot in the Atsimondrano school district [enrolled 180,000 learners from 792 public and private establishments of all levels](<https://2424.mg/education-le-suivi-scolaire-passe-a-lere-digitale-avec-le-numero-unique-didentification-pour-chaque-eleve/>), by the report of May 2026: a figure for schools covered by the enrolment.

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the annual school census and statistical yearbooks, which the education ministry calls its [EMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040125074586096/pdf/P506569-03c93c61-c7e5-4acc-b84f-07ba811417dc.pdf>) and its [SIGE](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/files/2025/07/PAPESI_%20Rapport%20Final%20Evaluation_FR.pdf>), the [FPE collection application](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/files/2025/07/PAPESI_%20Rapport%20Final%20Evaluation_FR.pdf>), the [in-house Excel collection tool of the planning directorate](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/files/2025/07/PAPESI_%20Rapport%20Final%20Evaluation_FR.pdf>) and the planned [StatEduc for the Fiches d'enquête rapides](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/files/2025/07/PAPESI_%20Rapport%20Final%20Evaluation_FR.pdf>). Single-function: the [online consultation of examination results](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>), the [HRMIS enrolment of state employees and geolocation of schools](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>), the [teacher attendance tracking system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040125074586096/pdf/P506569-03c93c61-c7e5-4acc-b84f-07ba811417dc.pdf>) and the [TMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040125074586096/pdf/P506569-03c93c61-c7e5-4acc-b84f-07ba811417dc.pdf>). Learning: the [pilot of WhatsApp and Messenger bots for French lessons](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>). Enabling: [Smart Village](<https://2424.mg/inclusion-numerique-le-programme-smart-village-pour-soutenir-linclusion-numerique-et-desenclaver-les-zones-les-plus-reculees/>), [DECIM connectivity and devices](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf>) and [school devices and connectivity](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099040125074586096/pdf/P506569-03c93c61-c7e5-4acc-b84f-07ba811417dc.pdf>).

***Not held***. No total of establishments in the pilot district and no separate count of primary schools is held, and for the scale-up only the target is held, not a result. Who keys the record and any plan are not held.
