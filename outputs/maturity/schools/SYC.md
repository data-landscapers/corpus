# Seychelles

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** A Ministry of Education team [ran the schools' training](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-dogley-3838a8140_educationseychelles-openemis-dataineducation-activity-7435535437164699648-Wdcy>) on what it calls the "MoE Seychelles" OpenEMIS platform, in a post published in March 2026. The attendance guide is [issued by the EMIS helpdesk](<https://moesyi-emis.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/501000295774-taking-students-attendance-web-version->) of the Ministry of Education, Skills, Youth and Information, on a page published in October 2025.

**Levels in use.** The training was for the [leadership teams of primary and secondary schools](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-dogley-3838a8140_educationseychelles-openemis-dataineducation-activity-7435535437164699648-Wdcy>) across Mahé: headteachers, deputies and office managers.

**How the record is kept.** The helpdesk [gives the procedure](<https://moesyi-emis.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/501000295774-taking-students-attendance-web-version->) for recording attendance in OpenEMIS on the web: under the school, by academic period, week, day, class and grade, each student is present by default and is changed to an excused or unexcused absence.

**Last twelve months.** The training [published in March 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-dogley-3838a8140_educationseychelles-openemis-dataineducation-activity-7435535437164699648-Wdcy>) covered student admissions, promotions and transfers, classes and subjects, and staff records.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The [training](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/david-dogley-3838a8140_educationseychelles-openemis-dataineducation-activity-7435535437164699648-Wdcy>) published in March 2026 reached school leadership teams across Mahé.

***Noted, not assessed***. The national framework for artificial intelligence in education, dated 2026, names four systems of the Ministry of Education and Human Resource Development for primary and secondary schools: the [Smart Classroom initiative](<http://statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/cabinet_downloads/140_MBMFFUcf.pdf>), [Britannica Digital Learning](<http://statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/cabinet_downloads/140_MBMFFUcf.pdf>), [Teacher Coach](<http://statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/cabinet_downloads/140_MBMFFUcf.pdf>) and [Student Tutor](<http://statehouse.gov.sc/uploads/cabinet_downloads/140_MBMFFUcf.pdf>). A 2021 article names the ministry's [classmate portable computers](<https://nation.sc/articles/8865/integration-of-instructional-technology-in-our-educational-system-the-debate-is-on>) in primary schools, a [mobile and web application in secondary schools](<https://nation.sc/articles/8865/integration-of-instructional-technology-in-our-educational-system-the-debate-is-on>) and a proposed [multimedia learning portal](<https://nation.sc/articles/8865/integration-of-instructional-technology-in-our-educational-system-the-debate-is-on>).

***Not held***. No report of a school entering attendance or results is held, and nor is which levels use the platform or a count or share of primary schools. No plan or finance for it is held.
