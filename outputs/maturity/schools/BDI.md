# Burundi

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Burundi calls its yearly school census an EMIS, in French the [Système d'information et de gestion de l'éducation (SIGE)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-08-plan-sectoriel-education-burundi-2022-2030.pdf>): in 2023 the census was [collected on paper at the school and entered electronically at central level](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-08-assessment-enabling-factors-burundi.pdf>), and it is published as the [Annuaire statistique de l'éducation](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Tome-2_Annuaire__2024_2025.pdf>), whose 2024-2025 edition is dated December 2025. Two systems do one job each: the education ministry's [human resources module for the SIGE](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>), being built in August 2024, and a [high frequency survey](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099070925113027808/pdf/BOSIB-c53e9e83-1496-430f-bc4c-bcd22d7b016b.pdf>) in which 40 municipal inspectors captured monthly data on tablets from a sample of 200 schools in 2020/21 and 2021/22. On equipment and connections: [computers for 39 écoles centrales](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/310460>), put to tender by UNICEF in August 2026; the [PAFEN-ONATEL rural network coverage contract](<https://setic.gov.bi/pafen-onatel-couverture-reseau-et-connectivite-en-milieu-rural/>) of April 2026, for 92 collines; the [PAFEN-Lumitel high-speed internet project](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/04/lumitel-burundi-launches-10m-high-speed-internet-project-across-the-country/>) of March 2026, which is to connect nearly 300 schools; and the [rural 4G extension](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/03/01/le-burundi-ambitionne-detendre-la-4g-aux-zones-rurales-pour-reduire-la-fracture-numerique/>) of PAFEN and the Fonds de Service Universel, to 178 collines.

***Not held***. No dated statement says whether primary schools keep their pupils' records on paper, and nothing held describes a system with a record for each pupil that a school adds to during the year.
