# South Sudan

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The schools' attendance system, which the country calls SSSAMS, was [developed under and is supported by the Girls' Education South Sudan programme of the Ministry of General Education and Instruction, funded by UK aid](<https://www.sssams.org/ams/index.html>), as its site stated in December 2024. A separate system for refugee learners is [managed by UNHCR](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>), on a working paper of September 2025.

**Levels in use.** The attendance system's tables for academic year 2024 [count primary, secondary and other schools in every state](<https://www.sssams.org/ams/index.html>). The refugee system [reports on early childhood, primary and secondary education, in refugee-hosting schools and camps only](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>).

**How the record is kept.** The attendance system [takes enrolment from the class registers every year; head teachers add and remove pupils by SMS, and class teachers report each absent pupil by SMS to the free number 6464](<https://www.sssams.org/ams/index.html>), as of December 2024. An African Union release of March 2026 states that [school-level collection relies heavily on handwritten classroom registers and school record books](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/pressreleases/46167-pr-PR-AU_IPED_Supports_South_Sudan_to_Strengthen_Education_Data_Systems_through_EMIS_Reform.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 the African Union reported visits to public schools in Juba, where [data moved from paper registers to state-level aggregation and national reporting](<https://au.int/en/pressreleases/20260324/au-iped-supports-south-sudan-strengthen-education-data-systems-through-emis>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The attendance system's panel for academic year 2024 showed [6,650 schools of all levels as having reported attendance at least once, beside 5,915 open schools holding enrolment for the year and 1,321 messages from 3 phones in the last month](<https://www.sssams.org/ams/index.html>). An app holding individual pupil records [was being piloted in South Sudan in September 2024](<https://education.dhis2.org/semis-app-launch/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. South Sudan calls its school census an EMIS: the ministry's [Education Management Information System](<https://mogei.gov.ss/super/assets/documents/resources/Final%202023-2027%20South%20Sudan%20General%20Education%20Sector%20Plan.pdf>) is described through the yearly census in the sector plan for 2023 to 2027, and is [fed once a year by a paper questionnaire or by enumerators with tablets](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>). The [Annual Education Census](<https://mogei.gov.ss/super/assets/documents/resources/2023%20Nat-Education%20Census%20Report_Final.pdf>) captured [7,737 schools in 2023](<https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30192664>). The ministry's [school performance monitoring tool and Child-friendly School Index](<https://mogei.gov.ss/super/assets/documents/resources/Final%202023-2027%20South%20Sudan%20General%20Education%20Sector%20Plan.pdf>) are forms collected by Payam education supervisors. The ministry received [84 computers, 33 motorbikes and one vehicle for monitoring and supervision](<https://www.mogei.gov.ss/news_details?title=42>) on 5 November 2025.

***Not held***. No share of primary schools reporting attendance is held, nor a count of them given with its total. No current plan for the attendance system is held, and nothing dated in the past twelve months says whether it is advancing or falling back.
