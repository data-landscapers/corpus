# Nigeria

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Federal Ministry of Education [launched the Learner Identification Number as its own system](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-first-ever-nationwide-learner-identification-number-to-transform-education-system/>), on its statement of 9 April 2026. Biometric Update, on 5 August 2026, names the ministry as the body that, with NIMC, [linked the learner numbers to the national identity number registry](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/nigeria-integrates-national-student-ids-digital-identity-system>).

**Levels in use.** In the first phase the numbers went to [candidates registered for the 2026 WAEC and NECO examinations](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-launches-first-ever-nationwide-learner-identification-number-to-transform-education-system/>).

**How the record is kept.** Admission into Edo State's public primary and junior secondary schools is [entirely manual, on forms and photocopied certificates](<https://nannews.ng/nigerias-digital-identity-gap-leaves-schoolchildren-behind/>), state officials told the News Agency of Nigeria in a report of 31 July 2026. At Nassarawo Primary School in Adamawa State a teacher [marks about 50 pupils' attendance each day by hand in a paper register](<https://dpi.africa/classroom-attendance-in-nigeria-still-relies-on-paper/>), DPI Africa reported on 12 March 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The learner number [began rolling out in April 2026, with 1.9 million issued](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/nigeria-integrates-national-student-ids-digital-identity-system>). From 14 September 2026 every public and private school in Lagos State [must take each learner's residents' registration number at admission and enter it in its records](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/2026-27-session-lagos-makes-lasrra-number-compulsory-for-school-admission/>), towards a state database of learners.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Oyo State's system to give every child a learner number was [still under development](<https://nannews.ng/nigerias-digital-identity-gap-leaves-schoolchildren-behind/>) on 31 July 2026. Edo State [plans a system tracking each student, and the one it runs takes school-level data](<https://dhis2.org/school-reporting-edo-state-nigeria/>), DHIS2 stated on 30 May 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school count is the [Annual School Census](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/nigeria/>), whose 2025/26 round is to run entirely on what the Federal Ministry of Education calls its [Digital National Education Management Information System, DNEMIS](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-unveils-national-digital-education-platform-to-track-every-school-and-learner-strengthen-planning-transparency-and-accountability-dnemis/>), with a [Public DNEMIS Portal](<https://education.dhis2.org/nigeria-launches-nationwide-education-data-system/>); the census system is also named as the [Nigeria Education Management Information System, NEMIS](<https://ubec.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/HOPE-EDU-ESSA-Final-March-2025.pdf>), and a [DHIS2 EMIS module](<https://dpi.africa/classroom-attendance-in-nigeria-still-relies-on-paper/>) is being rolled out in twelve states. Single-function: [EdoEMIS](<https://dhis2.org/school-reporting-edo-state-nigeria/>), as Edo State names its school-level reporting system, the [UBEC digital quality assurance platform](<https://dpi.africa/classroom-attendance-in-nigeria-still-relies-on-paper/>), [EdoCert](<https://dpi.africa/classroom-attendance-in-nigeria-still-relies-on-paper/>), the [Nigerian Education Repository Data Bank](<https://www.thestar.ng/fg-nysc-plan-digital-link-to-curb-certificate-fraud/>) and the [National Credential Verification Service](<https://www.thestar.ng/fg-nysc-plan-digital-link-to-curb-certificate-fraud/>). Learning: [uLesson](<https://techtrends.africa/edtech-for-out-of-school-youth-reaching-the-unreached/>), [Teesas](<https://techtrends.africa/edtech-for-out-of-school-youth-reaching-the-unreached/>), [Tuteria](<https://techtrends.africa/edtech-for-out-of-school-youth-reaching-the-unreached/>), [AltSchool Africa](<https://techtrends.africa/edtech-for-out-of-school-youth-reaching-the-unreached/>) and the [Nigeria Learning Passport](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/09/fg-scraps-data-charges-for-students/>). Enabling: [GenU9ja](<https://dpi.africa/classroom-attendance-in-nigeria-still-relies-on-paper/>) and the [Initiative for Zero-Rated Access to Educational Platforms and Content](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/10/fg-to-give-students-100mb-data-daily-from-october-1/>).

***Not held***. Which school levels use the learner number, who enters it, and whether any primary school fills the register. The paper statements cover one state's admissions and one school, and no plan or finance for the system is stated.
