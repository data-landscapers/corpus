# Sudan

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school count is taken by survey: a [comprehensive educational statistical survey](<https://sudannabaa.com/en/news-en/79364/79364/>) counting schools, students and teachers, begun in Khartoum, Al-Gezira and Sennar states and extended in phases to 7 May 2026, and a [Rapid School Census Survey](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-09-program-document-system-transformation-grant-sudan.pdf>) planned for all 18 states. The Ministry of Education's [EMIS](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-09-program-document-system-transformation-grant-sudan.pdf>) collects through parallel survey instruments, on a programme document of September 2025, and Sudan's statistics strategy of 2012 names [EMIS as the vehicle for school censuses](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/migration/arabstates/NSDS-Abridged-Version_Final_English_Nov-2012.pdf>); an [Education Statistic Yearbook](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/66fa9b61-3d8f-5b20-aa21-bf2bcc12df0e/content>) is cited in a survey of 2007. Single-function: the ministry's [ICT system for examinations](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/66fa9b61-3d8f-5b20-aa21-bf2bcc12df0e/content>), in the same 2007 survey. Enabling: [Giga's Project Connect](<https://www.omdena.com/blog/giga-unicef>), a map of school connectivity, and [computer labs in 4,363 of 11,752 public schools](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/66fa9b61-3d8f-5b20-aa21-bf2bcc12df0e/content>) as counted in 2007.

***Not held***. Any dated statement of how primary schools keep their learners' records, on paper or otherwise, and any fact on a system holding a record per learner.
