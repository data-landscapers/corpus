# Malawi

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Cohort tracking gives each pupil a Learner Identification Number on entry to school. It is the [Ministry of Education's](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>). It sits inside what the country calls its Education Management Information System. A DHIS2-based EMIS piloted in 2026 [was launched by the Unlocking Data Initiative with HISP-UNIMA at the University of Malawi](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unlocking-data-initiative-udi_transforming-education-data-systems-in-malawi-activity-7488488777280151552-3Td2>).

**Levels in use.** Cohort tracking passes from [schools to zonal and district officers](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** Outside the pilot, on paper. A May 2026 policy brief from the Unlocking Data Initiative says cohort tracking [rests on paper records and several manual registers](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>). They are class attendance and performance, master enrolment, and admission. The DHIS2 pilot [has modules for teacher and learner attendance, enrolment and transfers, with an offline Android app for rural schools](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unlocking-data-initiative-udi_transforming-education-data-systems-in-malawi-activity-7488488777280151552-3Td2>). The Unlocking Data Initiative said so in July 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In May 2026 a DHIS2 cohort tracking prototype [was running in four pilot schools in Machinga and Dedza, with validation due by end-June](<https://unlockingdata.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Unlocking-DATA-NEWSLETTER-May-edition-2.pdf>). In July the platform [was being piloted in Dedza and Machinga](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unlocking-data-initiative-udi_transforming-education-data-systems-in-malawi-activity-7488488777280151552-3Td2>). The May brief says the cohort tracking data has [rarely been analysed since 2012](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>) and is difficult to use for planning. In May 2026 digitising it was a recommendation.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Four pilot schools [entered cohort tracking data offline in May 2026](<https://unlockingdata.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Unlocking-DATA-NEWSLETTER-May-edition-2.pdf>). Elsewhere schools [make the registers themselves](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>), by hand, as of May 2026.

***Also on file***. Malawi calls its school census an EMIS: the Ministry of Education's [Education Management Information System](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) is a web-based system developed with UNICEF support. The only collection described for it in 2024 is the [Annual School Census](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>). It is taken in all schools once a year and published as the Malawi Education Statistics Report. A 2023 assessment says the [EMIS covers primary education only](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-07-malawi-itap-report.pdf>). A [teacher deployment monitoring and reporting system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) was planned for public primary schools in all 34 education districts by October 2025. In Blantyre district, [Teaching at the Right Level](<https://mwnation.com/blantyre-gets-tablets-for-collecting-education-data/>) collected learner performance data on tablets in 48 public primary schools in 2025. The [BEFIT](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) learning tablets reached 500 public primary schools in 2023-2024. Press Trust planned [off-grid electric power](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) for at least 200 hard-to-reach primary schools.

***Not found***. No source names the pilot schools' level or any school using the system past its pilot. No ministry plan or budget for a digital record of each pupil has been found.
