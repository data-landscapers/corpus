# Malawi

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Cohort tracking, which gives each pupil a Learner Identification Number on entry to school, is the [Ministry of Education's](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>), and sits inside what the country calls its Education Management Information System.

**Levels in use.** Cohort tracking passes from [schools to zonal and district officers](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** On paper. A May 2026 policy brief from the Unlocking Data Initiative says cohort tracking [rests on paper records and several manual registers](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>): class attendance and performance, master enrolment, and admission.

**Last twelve months.** Nothing moved. The same brief says the cohort tracking data has [rarely been analysed since 2012](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>) and is difficult to use for planning, and in May 2026 digitising it was a recommendation.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Schools [make the registers themselves](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>), by hand, as of May 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. Malawi calls its school census an EMIS: the Ministry of Education's [Education Management Information System](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) is a web-based system developed with UNICEF support, and the only collection described for it in 2024 is the [Annual School Census](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>), taken in all schools once a year and published as the Malawi Education Statistics Report; a 2023 assessment says the [EMIS covers primary education only](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-07-malawi-itap-report.pdf>). A [teacher deployment monitoring and reporting system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) was planned for public primary schools in all 34 education districts by October 2025. In Blantyre district, [Teaching at the Right Level](<https://mwnation.com/blantyre-gets-tablets-for-collecting-education-data/>) collected learner performance data on tablets in 48 public primary schools in 2025. The [BEFIT](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) learning tablets reached 500 public primary schools in 2023-2024, and Press Trust planned [off-grid electric power](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-malawi-partnership-compact.pdf>) for at least 200 hard-to-reach primary schools.

***Not held***. Nothing held names a school level, a count of schools, or an owner, plan or budget for a digital record of each pupil. No preparation or pilot of one for primary schools is on record.
