# Zambia

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the [annual reports from schools](<https://docs.dhis2.org/en/topics/user-stories/zambia-education-management-system-emis.html>), which schools send yearly on paper and the Ministry of General Education enters into its database, enrolment only. Single-function: what its user story calls an [EMIS in Namwala District](<https://docs.dhis2.org/en/topics/user-stories/zambia-education-management-system-emis.html>), run by Akros with the ministry from 2016, takes one monthly and one termly report entered on tablets; the [KGS digital checklist verification](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/24/government-trains-25-teachers-in-kgs-digital-checklist-verification/>), a tablet application for school guidance teachers in 25 of 28 schools in Lavushimanda District, reported in September 2026; the Ministry of Education's [textbook management system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062526113012291/pdf/P158570-ea7a80cb-bfaf-4dd0-99bf-758801451031.pdf>), in place in June 2026; its [GIS school mapping](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062526113012291/pdf/P158570-ea7a80cb-bfaf-4dd0-99bf-758801451031.pdf>), on which provincial and district offices have been oriented; the [Teacher Development Data Platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062526113012291/pdf/P158570-ea7a80cb-bfaf-4dd0-99bf-758801451031.pdf>), not yet developed in June 2026; and the [Teaching Council of Zambia's teacher database](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062526113012291/pdf/P158570-ea7a80cb-bfaf-4dd0-99bf-758801451031.pdf>), due to be enhanced.

***Not held***. No dated statement of how primary schools keep their learners' records is held. No system holding a record for each learner that a primary school adds to during the year is described.
