# The Gambia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The system is [the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education's, run by its own unit](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>), as GPE KIX described it in October 2025. An October 2022 account says it was [aligned to the Education Policy 2016-2030](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>) and built [with financial support from GPE-KIX and Norad](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>).

**Levels in use.** A learner census run with the Gambia Bureau of Statistics [collected individual-level data on children attending primary schools](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>), DHIS2 reported in October 2022, with work continuing to extend the system to all basic and secondary schools.

**How the record is kept.** Schools [record each learner's socio-economic background, attendance and progression](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>) in the ministry's digital student registry under a unique ID, DHIS2 reported in August 2025, with modules for daily attendance and exam results.

**Last twelve months.** GPE KIX in October 2025 described [attendance tracking and digital school report cards in use at school level](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The registry [began as a pilot in 200 schools in 2020](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>) and the ministry [scaled it to national level in 2021](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/the-gambia/>). By August 2025 it [included nearly 80% of Gambian learners](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>), a share of learners, by DHIS2's account.

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school census, which The Gambia calls its [Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>): the [Annual School Census](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>), its [aggregate forms](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/the-gambia/>) and the [statistical yearbook and its summary report](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260429205056/https://mobse.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/The-Gambia-Education-Statistics-Summary-Report-ESSR-2025.pdf>); the [Teacher Attendance System](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>); and [separate databases for human resources, examinations and infrastructure](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>).

***Not held***. No count or share of primary schools entering data is published, and the sources disagree on whether the school application is still a pilot. Finance within the past three years and any dated event in the last twelve months are not held.
