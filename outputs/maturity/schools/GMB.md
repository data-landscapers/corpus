# The Gambia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** [In October 2025 GPE KIX said the system belongs to the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education and is run by its own unit](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>). [A 2022 account says it was aligned to the Education Policy 2016-2030](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>). [It was built with money from GPE-KIX and Norad](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>).

**Levels in use.** [In October 2022 DHIS2 reported a learner census of children attending primary schools](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>). It was run with the Gambia Bureau of Statistics. Work continued to extend the system to all basic and secondary schools.

**How the record is kept.** [In August 2025 DHIS2 said schools record each learner's background, attendance and progression in the ministry's student registry](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). Each learner has a unique ID. Modules for daily attendance and exam results feed the ministry's data centre.

**Last twelve months.** [In September 2026 the ministry opened its Education Data Center to the public](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>). The portal brings together the school census, attendance reports and other sources. The ministry is building a drop-out warning system on it.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** [The registry began as a pilot in 200 schools in 2020](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). [The ministry scaled it to national level in 2021](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/the-gambia/>). [By August 2025 it held nearly 80% of Gambian learners](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). That is a share of learners, not of schools. [A separate SEMIS app started in two schools in 2022, and its pilots began in 2023](<https://dhis2.org/strengthening-school-management-gambia-eswatini/>).

***Also on file***. The yearly school census is what The Gambia calls its [Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>). On file are the [Annual School Census](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>), its [aggregate forms](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/the-gambia/>) and the [statistical yearbook and its summary report](<http://web.archive.org/web/20260429205056/https://mobse.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/The-Gambia-Education-Statistics-Summary-Report-ESSR-2025.pdf>). Also noted are the [Teacher Attendance System](<https://dhis2.org/gambia-emis-tracker/>) and [separate databases for human resources, examinations and infrastructure](<https://education.dhis2.org/wp-content/uploads/UO-DHIS2-CO_Gambia-Long-screen.pdf>).

***Not found***. No count or share of primary schools entering data has been published. Sources disagree on whether the school application is still a pilot. No source on finance since November 2023 has been found.
