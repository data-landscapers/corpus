# DR Congo

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** A Corus International article of 4 August 2026 by the director of SERNIE, the education ministry's service for individual learner data, states that [ownership and use of the system it calls EDUSER rest with the ministry, operated by SERNIE with technical support from CGA Technologies](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). It says [the pilot was funded and its future is uncertain without continued investment](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**Levels in use.** The April 2026 pilot of EDUSER [was run in 20 primary schools of Kasai province](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**How the record is kept.** SERNIE's director writes that since 1991 [its staff have recorded learners in schools on paper forms carried to Kinshasa for central data entry](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). Under EDUSER, [SERNIE staff on their usual school visits register each learner by app](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>), replacing the paper forms.

**Last twelve months.** In April 2026 SERNIE [completed the first test of EDUSER in schools, a three-week pilot, after training its staff at national, provincial and local levels](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>). The director says it laid the basis for [a national rollout, which is not funded](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** SERNIE teams [registered 8,634 learners in 20 primary schools of Kasai province in the three weeks](<https://corusinternational.org/fr/blog/2026/case-scaling-education-data-drc>), which the director calls only a pilot phase.
