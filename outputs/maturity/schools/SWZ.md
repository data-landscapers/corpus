# Eswatini

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education and Training [designed and scaled the system, with help from UNICEF and civil society](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>), and its departments presented their uses of it in February 2025. Its EMIS Unit [runs regular webinars for users on data entry, validation and extraction](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>), as of November 2025. The ministry is [named as a funding partner beside UNICEF Eswatini, GPE KIX and UNESCO Eswatini](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/eswatini/>), on a page of 2024.

**Levels in use.** The Budget Speech of February 2024 describes a web-based system [designed to capture data on individual learners in Grade 0, primary and high schools by their Personal Identification Numbers](<https://parliament.gov.sz/media/speeches/budget/2024.pdf>). In October 2025 it [covered primary and secondary schools](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>).

**How the record is kept.** As of February 2025, [information on each learner, such as demographics and performance, is captured with tools that collect it at the school level](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/towards-inclusive-education-data-systems-integration-disability-indicators-eswatinis-national>). The ministry [holds learner-level data under unique personal identification numbers, including each learner's orphan status and bursary](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>), as of November 2025.

**Last twelve months.** In November 2025 the team behind the software [called the system fully operational](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The system, launched nationally in March 2024, [covers all 976 primary and secondary schools](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>), a figure for schools covered that a GPE KIX blog gave in October 2025. The app that takes attendance, which Eswatini calls SEMIS, [was still a pilot with its roll-out to come](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>) in October 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. Eswatini calls its school census an EMIS: the ministry's [EMIS for aggregate data](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/eswatini/>) is national, and in it [the head teacher enters enrolment, staffing and infrastructure](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>); [more than 900 school administrators and focal teachers were trained on its updated tools](<https://education.dhis2.org/eswatini-disability-indicators-emis/>). The EMIS Unit runs the yearly [Annual Education Census](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/towards-inclusive-education-data-systems-integration-disability-indicators-eswatinis-national>) of all schools and the [16th Day Survey](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/towards-inclusive-education-data-systems-integration-disability-indicators-eswatinis-national>) of enrolment and attendance. The census questionnaires were [collected from schools on paper](<https://www.gov.sz/images/CabinetMinisters/Comprehensive%20report%20of%20Government%20performance%202024.pdf>), and a [system on MS Access](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>) was replaced from 2020. The [Giga Schools Connectivity Initiative](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/education-ict-ministries-and-unicef-push-for-internet-access-in-all-schools/>) had 965 schools uploaded and 402 of them connected on 31 October 2025. A [project financed by the Government of Taiwan](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20EDUCATION%202025.pdf>) gave 440 computers to 14 high schools and 11 primary schools in 2024/25, and the [Digital School Initiative](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20EDUCATION%202025.pdf>) put 50 teachers through an online course.

***Not held***. No figure is held for primary schools entering attendance or results during the year, and how often a school adds to a learner's record is not stated. No current plan is held, and nothing dated in the past twelve months says whether the system is advancing or falling back.
