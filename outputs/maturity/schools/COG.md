# Congo

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Decree 2023-1751 of 2 November 2023](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-46.pdf>) creates an education management information system, which Congo calls the SIGE, inside the ministry of preschool, primary and secondary education and literacy. The ministry's information systems directorate administers it and the state agency ACSI maintains it. The decree [finances it in public schools from the State budget, gifts and partners](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-46.pdf>), and names no amount.

**Levels in use.** No level uses a school-level system. In May 2023 a digital workspace for primary and secondary schools, the ENT, [existed only as a specification](<https://apprendre.auf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ENT-Congo-Cahier-des-charges-APPRENDRE-MAI-2023-1.pdf>) written for the ministry by a consultant of the AUF's APPRENDRE programme.

**How the record is kept.** The decree [assigns data entry to the schools or their inspectorates](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-46.pdf>): each learner registered under a unique number, with grades, report cards and school record books digitised.

**Last twelve months.** The decree creating the system is dated [2 November 2023](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2023/congo-jo-2023-46.pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** None. The May 2023 specification [proposed a trial from September 2023](<https://apprendre.auf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ENT-Congo-Cahier-des-charges-APPRENDRE-MAI-2023-1.pdf>) in a few schools to be chosen by the ministry, for example two primary schools and one secondary.

***Noted, not assessed***. Congo calls its yearly school count the [SIGE](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-pacte-de-partenariat-republique-congo.pdf>): paper questionnaires collected from schools once a year for the [annuaire statistique](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-pacte-de-partenariat-republique-congo.pdf>), with a [répertoire des établissements scolaires](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-pacte-de-partenariat-republique-congo.pdf>) planned. The same count appears as the [annuaire scolaire](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-06-congo_republic-itap-report.pdf>), as the [EMIS at the ministry](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf>), as the [Annuaire Statistique 2017-2018](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Republique-du-Congo-DE4A-en-Francais.pdf>) and as the awaited [Statistical Yearbook 2025-2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925064520650/pdf/P179410-b8959451-e7dc-4e80-8fd1-c81ec062a66f.pdf>). Planned: an [integrated EMIS for all education ministries](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf>) and a [comprehensive teacher database](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-07-program-document-republic-congo.pdf>). Single-function: [Exatrust](<https://lejournalducongo.com/28/03/2026/modernisation-scolaire-le-congo-digitalise-la-gestion-des-examens-detat/>), for checking state examination results, the [tablet survey locating examination centres](<https://lejournalducongo.com/28/03/2026/modernisation-scolaire-le-congo-digitalise-la-gestion-des-examens-detat/>) and the [biometric registration of teachers](<https://apprendre.auf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ENT-Congo-Cahier-des-charges-APPRENDRE-MAI-2023-1.pdf>). Learning: [Ecole à domicile](<https://apprendre.auf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ENT-Congo-Cahier-des-charges-APPRENDRE-MAI-2023-1.pdf>) and [RESSEN.ORG](<https://apprendre.auf.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/ENT-Congo-Cahier-des-charges-APPRENDRE-MAI-2023-1.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether staff were trained, equipment delivered or any school took part in a trial. How schools keep their learners' records, and anything dated after the decree of 2 November 2023.
