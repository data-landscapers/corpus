# Guinea-Bissau

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: [SIGE](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>), the yearly school count that Guinea-Bissau calls its EMIS, the [Plataforma Estatística SIGE](<https://www.fecongd.org/project/peside/>), [SIGE 2.0](<https://www.fecongd.org/2025/10/20/projeto-peside-quer-estatisticas-de-qualidade-para-a-educacao-na-guine-bissau/>), [STATEDUC](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>), the [anuário estatístico](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-01-pacto-parceria-guine-bissau.pdf>), last published for 2014/2015, the [carta escolar](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-01-pacto-parceria-guine-bissau.pdf>), the [school-mapping database](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/better-data-improve-education-guinea-bissau>) and the [new electronic form for education statistics](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/from-paper-forms-to-data-driven-decisions--how-gems-improved-sch>). Single-function: the [GEMS digital inspection form](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/from-paper-forms-to-data-driven-decisions--how-gems-improved-sch>). Learning: [Neska](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>), a proposal.

***Not held***. No record for each learner that schools add to during the year is held. No dated statement that primary schools keep learner records on paper only is held either.
