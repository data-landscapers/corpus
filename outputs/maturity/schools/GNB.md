# Guinea-Bissau

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In 2017/18 the pilot [was developed by the Gabu regional government and the NGO FEC with ministry units, on a platform built by a consortium of the CPLP training institute and Codevision](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>), and [was financed by the European Union](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>).

**Levels in use.** The pilot [covered the basic and secondary schools of Gabu region active in 2017/18](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>).

**How the record is kept.** In the 2017/18 school year [teams from the ministry and FEC collected learner, teacher and class records school by school in Gabu and keyed them into the platform at a regional hub](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>), with ministry statisticians to monitor each school term.

**Last twelve months.** The latest account held is the 2018 report of [the pilot the country calls Firkidja di Skola](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The 2018 report [describes the system as a pilot for Gabu region, with a hope that it will be extended to the whole territory](<https://www.fecongd.org/2018/07/04/reportagem-fec-reforco-da-gestao-de-dados-educativos-na-regiao-de-gabu/>).
