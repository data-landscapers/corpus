# Senegal

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** PLANETE 3 [is run by the education ministry's informatisation system unit, whose coordinator presented it at a launch chaired by the minister](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>) on 3 October 2025. The minister [placed it within the Stratégie du Numérique pour l'Éducation 2025-2029](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>).

**Levels in use.** The system's coordinator said PLANETE 3 [now covers all cycles from preschool to secondary, preschool and elementary having long been left out of computerisation](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>).

**How the record is kept.** Its teacher module [lets teachers enter absences and produce report cards, while pupils and parents follow marks and absences and school heads supervise](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>). A December 2025 brief by APHRC [lists manual, paper-based data collection and isolated digital files among Senegal's education data challenges](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>).

**Last twelve months.** The ministry [launched the new version on 3 October 2025, with a mobile app for pupils and parents, online school-fee payment and dashboards at school, inspectorate, central and ministry level](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The coordinator said at the October 2025 launch that [PLANETE 3 now covers elementary schools](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/education/plateforme-planete-3-une-innovation-numerique-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-de-lecole/>).

***Also on file***. Senegal calls its school census an EMIS: the education ministry's [Système d'information et de gestion de l'éducation (SIGE)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>) is a yearly collection, described in 2024, that planners in the regional and district inspectorates key into StatEduc. The ministry has developed [EDUSTAT](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>) to replace StatEduc, with school heads to enter the data themselves. What the country calls [SIMEN](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/WHITEpaperENG809.pdf>), run by the ministry's planning directorate, is fed by statistical campaigns at the beginning of each year, as described in 2024. The national statistics strategy for 2019-2023 lists the [Recensement scolaire](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>) and the [Recensement des daaras](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>) as priority activities. [DHIS2 for Education](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/senegal/>) has run in two districts since 2024, as a research project of the ministry with GPE KIX and HISP WCA. Single-function systems described in 2024 are [SENORIENTATION](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), which holds pupils admitted to general secondary, [SYSGAR](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), the dashboard of the ministry's annual work plan, and [FINPRONET](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), for its finance, accounts and procurement. Learning platforms described in 2024 are [SENKAGGU](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), for distance teaching and then in test, and [SENPROF](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), a portal of digital teaching resources. [NELVIA](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Education/education-une-nouvelle-plateforme-pour-moderniser-la-gestion-scolaire_n_504109.html>), a privately initiated school management platform for public and private schools, was launched at Thiès on 12 September 2026.

***Not found***. No school is named as using PLANETE 3, and no count or share of primary schools entering data has been found. How it is financed is not stated.
