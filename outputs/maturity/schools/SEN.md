# Senegal

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Senegal calls its school census an EMIS: the education ministry's [Système d'information et de gestion de l'éducation (SIGE)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>) is a yearly collection, described in 2024, that planners in the regional and district inspectorates key into StatEduc. The ministry has developed [EDUSTAT](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>) to replace StatEduc, with school heads to enter the data themselves. What the country calls [SIMEN](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/WHITEpaperENG809.pdf>), run by the ministry's planning directorate, is fed by statistical campaigns at the beginning of each year, as described in 2024. The national statistics strategy for 2019-2023 lists the [Recensement scolaire](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>) and the [Recensement des daaras](<https://smartdatafinance.org/storage/2021-09-28/Lz84pK3Twh8ZGzX.pdf>) as priority activities. [DHIS2 for Education](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/senegal/>) has run in two districts since 2024, as a research project of the ministry with GPE KIX and HISP WCA. Single-function systems described in 2024 are [SENORIENTATION](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), which holds pupils admitted to general secondary, [SYSGAR](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), the dashboard of the ministry's annual work plan, and [FINPRONET](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), for its finance, accounts and procurement. Learning platforms described in 2024 are [SENKAGGU](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), for distance teaching and then in test, and [SENPROF](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/fr/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-10-document-programme-financements-faveur-senegal.pdf>), a portal of digital teaching resources. [NELVIA](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Education/education-une-nouvelle-plateforme-pour-moderniser-la-gestion-scolaire_n_504109.html>), a privately initiated school management platform for public and private schools, was launched at Thiès on 12 September 2026.

***Not held***. No dated statement says that primary schools keep their pupils' records on paper only, and nothing held describes a system with a record for each pupil, in preparation, on pilot or in use.
