# Mali

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In February 2020 the PAIS project [was a partnership of UNICEF, the World Food Programme, the International Rescue Committee and the Norwegian Refugee Council](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>), UNICEF Mali states, and [was funded by the European Union](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>).

**Levels in use.** UNICEF Mali, in February 2020, describes the scheme [at the Kénèkolo village school, in a passage on children aged seven to twelve missing primary education](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>).

**How the record is kept.** Under PAIS each family in the target areas [holds an electronic card with each child registered on it, which the school principal scans onto a tablet as children attend](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>), to take attendance and make it available to the Ministry of Education, by UNICEF Mali in February 2020.

**Last twelve months.** In February 2020 UNICEF Mali said the Kénèkolo school [would digitise its attendance system that year](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** By February 2020 families in the target areas of Kayes, Koulikoro, Ségou and Sikasso [had been given cards](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/niani-diarra-future-president-mali>), UNICEF Mali states, naming the one school.

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the annual school census, which the education ministry calls its [SIGE](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/revue_par_les_pairs_du_systeme_dinfromation_pour_la_gestion_de_leducation_du_mali.pdf>), an education management information system, the [recensement scolaire](<https://instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/sdsmli20-24_ca.pdf>), the [collecte rapide 2024-2025](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>) and the [education statistical yearbooks](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925133063670/pdf/P164032-3dbd8877-99ba-4d00-8051-52eac68d5d74.pdf>). Single-function: the [portail numérique de gestion de la mobilité des enseignants](<https://malijet.com/education_et_formation_au_mali/311884-education-le-mali-digitalise-la-gestion-de-la-mobilite-des.html>), the [outil de gestion digitalisée for teachers' presence and mobility and pupils' attendance](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>) and the [digital tool for collecting teaching-staff data](<https://bamada.net/mali-education-bientot-des-agents-techniques-formes-a-lusage-dun-outil-numerique-de-collecte-des-donnees>). Learning: the planned [scripted lesson plans on solar-charged tablets](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925133063670/pdf/P164032-3dbd8877-99ba-4d00-8051-52eac68d5d74.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing is held since February 2020, so who runs and pays for the scheme now and any event in the last twelve months are not held. No count or share of schools scanning attendance is held, and no school is named as doing so.
