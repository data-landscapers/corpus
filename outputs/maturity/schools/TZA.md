# Tanzania

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Prime Minister's Office (TAMISEMI) [directed that the School Information System come into official use when schools opened on 13 January 2026](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>), Handeni Town Council's education officer says. The education ministry's Permanent Secretary [announced a planned student-tracking system as the ministry's own in November 2025](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-seeks-to-resolve-student-dropout-crisis-through-digital-tracker-5268662>).

**Levels in use.** Handeni Town Council [trained the heads of its primary and secondary schools and one teacher from each school on the system on 9 January 2026](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>).

**How the record is kept.** A primary head teacher trained in Handeni says [a pupil marked absent three times in a row triggers a message to the parent](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>), and the head of Mshikamano Primary School says [the school kept its data on paper before the system](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>). An August 2025 investigation in Mbeya District Council describes [digital systems designed to track attendance and examination results, which teachers are expected to use daily](<https://www.policyforum-tz.org/blog/2025-09-22/major-knowledge-gap-education-information-systems-unrecognized-challenge-mbeya>).

**Last twelve months.** The Handeni training of 9 January 2026 [came ahead of the official start of use directed for 13 January](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>). In November 2025 the government [was preparing a system to follow each learner from early childhood to higher education](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/tanzania-seeks-to-resolve-student-dropout-crisis-through-digital-tracker-5268662>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In Handeni [every head and one teacher per school was trained on 9 January 2026](<https://maarifaonlinemedia.co.tz/mfumo-wa-sis-kukomeshautoro-shule-za-handeni-mji/>). In Mbeya District Council in August 2025, [one school in every five surveyed had a single teacher with a basic understanding of the systems, none formally trained](<https://www.policyforum-tz.org/blog/2025-09-22/major-knowledge-gap-education-information-systems-unrecognized-challenge-mbeya>), the Rural Youth Collective reports.

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the yearly school count, [which Tanzania calls its EMIS](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1752867053-Education%20and%20Literacy%20Monograph.pdf>), collected as the [Annual School Census](<https://hepo.iesalc.unesco.org/pc/static/countrydocs/cp/2025/tza/tza_HEEdPlanLi.pdf>), held in [BEMIS](<https://hepo.iesalc.unesco.org/pc/static/countrydocs/cp/2025/tza/tza_HEEdPlanLi.pdf>) and coordinated through [ESMIS](<https://hepo.iesalc.unesco.org/pc/static/countrydocs/cp/2025/tza/tza_HEEdPlanLi.pdf>). Single-function: the examinations council's planned [electronic assessment for learning system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-03-tanzani-gpe-grant-program-document-teachers-support-program.pdf>), the [Online Teacher Application System](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-03-tanzani-gpe-grant-program-document-teachers-support-program.pdf>), the [Schools Accreditation System](<https://hepo.iesalc.unesco.org/pc/static/countrydocs/cp/2025/tza/tza_HEEdPlanLi.pdf>) and the [School Quality Assurance System](<https://hepo.iesalc.unesco.org/pc/static/countrydocs/cp/2025/tza/tza_HEEdPlanLi.pdf>). Learning: the Tanzania Institute of Education's [Open Public School platform](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-digital-education-transformation-begins-with-the-teacher/>) and its [teachers' professional development learning system](<https://dailynews.co.tz/tanzanias-digital-education-transformation-begins-with-the-teacher/>).

***Not held***. Nothing shows a primary school entering attendance or results after the start directed for 13 January 2026, and no count of schools trained or using the system is held. No plan or source of finance is held.
