# Uganda

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Guidelines of 8 June 2026 [make the Ministry of Education and Sports primary custodian of all information entered into the system](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). An Auditor General's report, reported on 9 June 2026, finds that [its administration and configuration remain largely with the vendor, SMSOne, under the ICT ministry's supervision](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>).

**Levels in use.** For the 2025 academic year [7,408 pre-primary, 26,597 primary and 4,571 secondary schools responded](<https://www.education.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGANDAS-EDUCATION-STATISTICAL-ABSTRACT_2025.pdf>), the ministry reports.

**How the record is kept.** The system [collects data on each learner, issues a Learner Identification Number and tracks transfers, with 11,787,396 primary learners registered at December 2025](<https://www.education.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGANDAS-EDUCATION-STATISTICAL-ABSTRACT_2025.pdf>). [School heads register the learners](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>).

**Last twelve months.** In January 2026 the ministry [published its first annual statistical abstract since 2017, compiled from the system for the 2025 academic year](<https://www.education.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGANDAS-EDUCATION-STATISTICAL-ABSTRACT_2025.pdf>). It [launched the new guidelines on 8 June 2026, with sanctions for institutions that fail their data obligations](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>), and the contractor [was reported on 9 June 2026 to be upgrading the platform to a second version](<https://www.thekampalareport.com/latest/community/2026060963856/concerns-grow-as-private-firm-retains-control-of-critical-education-data-system.html>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The ministry reports that [26,597 of the country's 45,164 primary schools (58.9%) responded to the data upload for 2025: 12,511 of 12,544 government schools and 14,086 of 32,620 private schools](<https://www.education.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGANDAS-EDUCATION-STATISTICAL-ABSTRACT_2025.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the paper yearly school count [that Uganda ran as its EMIS from 1999 and discontinued in 2017](<https://www.education.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UGANDAS-EDUCATION-STATISTICAL-ABSTRACT_2025.pdf>), the [Annual School Census](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>), the [Baseline Education Census 2025](<https://statistics.ubos.org/bec/index.php>), the [Termly School Report](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>), [DHIS2-DEMIS](<https://education.dhis2.org/routine-data-collection-uganda/>) and the [School Report Card](<https://aphrc.org/publication/enhancing-coherence-and-use-of-education-data-addressing-system-fragmentation-across-uganda-senegal-and-burkina-faso/>). Single-function: [TELA](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>), the examinations board's [results portal](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>) and the [module for registering and licensing schools](<https://www.independent.co.ug/emis-no-more-physical-registration-licensing-of-schools/>).

***Not held***. No source describes schools entering attendance or results: what is held is the registration of learners, transfers and a yearly upload. No plan, source of finance or share for rural schools is held.
