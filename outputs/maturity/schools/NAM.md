# Namibia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** School-Link, an EDUPAC product, came to the Ministry of Education, Arts and Culture in 2013, was [approved as the national system for all schools](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>) and is offered to them free, with a helpdesk in the ministry's ICT division. The 2017-18 attendance pilot cost NAD 291,000, met jointly by ADEA and the ministry.

**Levels in use.** Primary and secondary. The 103 schools that piloted attendance capture in School-Link from May 2018 [include primary, combined and secondary schools](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** The pilot schools [captured each day's teacher and learner attendance](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>), with the reason for absence, and updated School-Link weekly through the trimester ending August 2018, recording 71,387 cases of learner absence. School-Link carries a learner unit record module, which the country calls LURITS. Outside the pilot, the ministry's November 2018 report with ADEA has [paper registers marked daily by teachers](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The attendance pilot [ran from May to the end of July 2018](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The ministry's report with ADEA, published in November 2018, says [807 public schools out of 1,848 schools are connected to School-Link](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>), at all levels together. Daily attendance was entered by the 103 pilot schools.

***Noted, not assessed***. Namibia calls its school census an EMIS: the ministry's [Education Management Information System](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>) division has the education regions collect data twice a year from all state and private schools and publishes the Fifteenth School Day Report and the Annual Education Census, with 2,036 schools counted in January 2024. [School-Write](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>), a commercial school administration program on annual subscription, was in a considerable number of schools in 2018, and most private schools and a few urban public schools used an [electronic biometric clocking system](<https://www.adeanet.org/sites/default/files/project_implementation_report_innovations_for_monitoring_teacher_and_learner_attendances_namibia_pilot_poject.pdf>). National examinations are managed in [OpenEMIS Exams](<https://communitysystemsfoundation.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/CSF%20CIES%202026%20Paper%20-%20Leveraging%20OpenEMIS.pdf>), which [gained verification, case management and online examination features in 2025](<http://www.communitysystemsfoundation.org/blog/openemis-country-highlights-2025>). A September 2026 report names [EduTV](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) for school students, [NOLeaMS](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) for technical education and [school connectivity and hotspots](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>) under the Universal Service and Access Fund and the operators.

***Not held***. Nothing held is dated after 2018, so the system's present ownership, plan, budget and direction are not established. The 807 schools are connected, not shown entering data, and no figure separates primary schools.
