# Mauritius

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNESCO IITE reported after a mission of 11-15 February 2019 that [the system is the Ministry of Education's to establish, coordinated by its Director of E-Education with UNESCO technical support](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>), and that [the needs assessment and design reports were to support including its resource needs in the 2019/2020 budget](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

**Levels in use.** In February 2019 the system was [planned for national, zone and school levels, from pre-primary to tertiary](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

**How the record is kept.** The 2019 mission [set out to design a school integrated management information system that is to track each student's progress, including classroom assessment](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

**Last twelve months.** The education ministry's [indicative procurement plan for financial year 2026-2027, dated 17 August 2026, names an EMIS](<https://education.govmu.org/Documents/2026/PROCUREMENT/Form%20A%20Updated%20(1)(4)%20(2).pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In February 2019 [the system was still to be developed, over twelve months following a three-month technical document](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. Nothing dated since February 2019 says whether the system was built, piloted or used in any school, at which level, or how pupils' records are kept. No ownership, plan or financing dated within the last three years is held.
