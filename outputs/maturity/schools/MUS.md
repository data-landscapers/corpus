# Mauritius

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNESCO IITE reported after a mission of 11-15 February 2019 that [the system is the Ministry of Education's to establish, coordinated by its Director of E-Education with UNESCO technical support](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>). [The needs assessment and design reports were to support including its resource needs in the 2019/2020 budget](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

**Levels in use.** In February 2019 the system was [planned for national, zone and school levels, from pre-primary to tertiary](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>). A project of UNESCO IITE and the Chengdu Group, [completed by August 2025, piloted an EMIS in secondary schools](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/unesco-iite-and-chengdu-group-complete-project-on-inclusive-digital-education-in-mauritius-and-rwanda/>); no primary school is named.

**How the record is kept.** The 2019 mission [set out to design a school integrated management information system that is to track each student's progress, including classroom assessment](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).

**Last twelve months.** The education ministry's [indicative procurement plan for financial year 2026-2027, dated 17 August 2026, names an EMIS](<https://education.govmu.org/Documents/2026/PROCUREMENT/Form%20A%20Updated%20(1)(4)%20(2).pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In February 2019 [the system was still to be developed, over twelve months following a three-month technical document](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/mauritius-emis-support-and-monitoring-of-education-2030-agenda/>).
