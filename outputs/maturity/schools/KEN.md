# Kenya

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The ministry's sector plan for 2023-2027 [names KEMIS, its education management information system, with costed yearly lines for developing it](<https://education.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/NATIONAL%20EDUCATION%20STRATEGIC%20PLAN%202023-2027-compressed_removed_0.pdf>). In September 2026 KEMIS [was funded through a World Bank-backed primary education programme](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>).

**Levels in use.** NEMIS, which KEMIS replaces, [covered all basic education institutions, primary and secondary](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/WHITEpaperENG809.pdf>), in May 2024. KEMIS [is built to run from early childhood education to university](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>). By 10 November 2026 KEMIS [had captured about 5.06 million primary school learners](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/education/ghost-students-puzzle-of-2-million-primary-school-learners-missing-from-ministry-records-5667184>).

**How the record is kept.** A ministry circular [tells schools to enter and correct each learner's record in KEMIS themselves](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>). It is dated 17 August 2026. Schools [entered pathway, subject and senior-school choices against the records of nearly 1.2 million Grade 9 learners](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>) in September 2026. In March 2024 teachers in 10 partner primary schools in Baringo [recorded each child's attendance daily in the Onekana app, linked to NEMIS](<https://www.sotheycan.org/stories/school-attendance-application>).

**Last twelve months.** KEMIS [became the required platform for the 2026 Grade 10 selection](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>), and its [Learner Validation Engine was operational by August 2026](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>). In September 2026 schools [reported failed logins, slow responses and submissions that did not save](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>). In late August 2026 the Principal Secretary said [the latest capitation payment used manually verified data because KEMIS was not yet complete](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The World Bank reported in March 2024 that [about 60 per cent of primary school learners were registered](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041724160510814/pdf/P176867127c2460bf1bdec12180716a1d7f.pdf>). The register is NEMIS.
