# Kenya

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education's National Education Sector Strategic Plan 2023-2027 [names the Kenya Education Management Information System (KEMIS), as the ministry calls it, and carries costed yearly lines for developing it](<https://education.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-07/NATIONAL%20EDUCATION%20STRATEGIC%20PLAN%202023-2027-compressed_removed_0.pdf>). Techweez reported in September 2026 that KEMIS [is funded through the World Bank-backed Kenya Primary Education Equity in Learning Programme](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>).

**Levels in use.** NEMIS, the system KEMIS replaces, [covered all basic education institutions, primary and secondary](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/WHITEpaperENG809.pdf>), as Development Gateway and IREX reported in May 2024. KEMIS [is built to hold data from early childhood education through to university](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>), on Techweez's account of September 2026.

**How the record is kept.** A ministry circular published on 17 August 2026 [tells schools to enter and correct each learner's record in KEMIS themselves: examination or assessment number, names, demographic details and parent or guardian identification](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>). Schools [entered pathway, subject and senior-school choices against the records of nearly 1.2 million Grade 9 learners](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>) in September 2026.

**Last twelve months.** KEMIS [became the required platform for the 2026 Grade 10 selection](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>), and its [Learner Validation Engine was operational by August 2026](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>). In September 2026 schools [reported failed logins, slow responses and submissions that did not save](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>). In late August 2026 the Principal Secretary told the Public Accounts Committee that [the latest capitation payment used manually verified data because KEMIS was not yet complete](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The World Bank reported in March 2024 that [about 60 per cent of primary school learners were registered in NEMIS](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041724160510814/pdf/P176867127c2460bf1bdec12180716a1d7f.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the [capitation verification exercise of September 2025](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/education/98pc-of-public-schools-submit-data-in-efforts-to-curb-capitation-fraud--5184528>), the [education data validation exercise of 2025](<https://educationboard.co.ke/ministry-circular-on-validation-of-schools-data-for-kemis-by-5th-sept/>) and the [Education Statistical Booklets](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/WHITEpaperENG809.pdf>). Single-function: the [Teacher Management Information System](<https://edu-design.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Digital-Literacy-Programme.pdf>), the [Financial Management Information System](<https://edu-design.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Digital-Literacy-Programme.pdf>), the Teachers Service Commission's [TPAD system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041724160510814/pdf/P176867127c2460bf1bdec12180716a1d7f.pdf>) and the [online item portal for competency-based assessment](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041724160510814/pdf/P176867127c2460bf1bdec12180716a1d7f.pdf>). Standalone: [Nyansapo AI](<https://www.primeafrica.co.ke/disability-outreach-in-kwale-exposes-gaps-in-identity-documents-education-and-social-protection/global-news/sport/afcon/9486/>). Learning: the [Digital Literacy Programme](<https://edu-design.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Digital-Literacy-Programme.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source describes schools entering attendance or results for each learner during the year, and no rural figure is held. No share or count of primary schools entering data is held for either system: two figures for 2021, 65 and 90 per cent, disagree and count schools that had submitted data.
