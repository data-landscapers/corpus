# Mauritania

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** Tefassil reported on 28 September 2026 that [the registration and transfer of pupils through the platform the ministry calls Siraj runs to a timetable set by the Ministry of Education and Reform of the Education System](<https://www.tefassil.mr/ar/node/8801>). The state news agency reported on 3 June 2025 that [a ministerial committee chaired by the Prime Minister follows the system](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/272574>).

**Levels in use.** On 28 April 2025 [education inspectors and heads of service of the Brakna regional directorate were trained on the system](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/269802>). Radio Mauritanie reported on 12 June 2026 that [district inspectors and the heads of the beneficiary schools in Trarza are to update data through it](<https://www.radiomauritanie.mr/index.php/ar/node/5976>).

**How the record is kept.** [Registration, transfers and corrections of pupils' status for the 2026-2027 school year go through Siraj, open from 28 September to 20 October 2026](<https://www.tefassil.mr/ar/node/8801>).

**Last twelve months.** [Pupil registration for 2026-2027 opened through Siraj on 28 September 2026, the ministry urging parents to register their children early](<https://www.tefassil.mr/ar/node/8801>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Tefassil's report of 28 September 2026 [presents Siraj as the channel for registering and transferring pupils for 2026-2027](<https://www.tefassil.mr/ar/node/8801>).
