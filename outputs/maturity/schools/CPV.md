# Cabo Verde

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education's school management system, SIGE, was [created by the state agency NOSi in partnership with the ministry](<https://minedu.gov.cv/recursos_educativossigemenu>). The [Plano Estratégico da Educação 2022-2026](<https://minedu.gov.cv/media/plano/2024/07/24/Plano_Estrategico_24072024.pdf>) names it throughout and costs 3 million escudos to extend it to pre-school and private schools. In July 2025 the national director of education named [UNICEF as main funder of a new special-needs module](<https://www.governo.cv/direcao-nacional-da-educacao-realiza-acao-formativa-no-novo-modulo-de-criancas-com-necessidades-educativas-especiais-no-sige/>).

**Levels in use.** Primary and secondary. The ministry states that SIGE was [completed in all 43 secondary schools in July 2018 and in basic education schools in February 2019](<https://minedu.gov.cv/recursos_educativossigemenu>). A ministerial order [obliges every school, public, semi-public and private, to adopt it](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=86791>) from 1 September 2025.

**How the record is kept.** The ministry's guidance for 2023/2024 [requires teachers to record absences daily in SIGE's digital class register and to enter each assessment within 15 days](<https://minedu.gov.cv/media/orientacao/2024/09/04/CADERNO_DE_ORIENTA%C3%87%C3%95ES_GERAIS_ANO_LETIVO_2023-2024_-_vers%C3%A3o_final_2_1.pdf>). Since June 2025 the eSkola app [shows parents each pupil's marks and absences](<https://www.governo.cv/damos-mais-um-passo-significativo-no-caminho-da-modernizacao-e-inclusao-digital-no-setor-da-educacao-dne/>) from SIGE in real time.

**Last twelve months.** The order for the 2025/2026 school year, in force from 1 September 2025, [requires teachers to record pupils' assessment on SIGE's result-entry sheets](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=86791>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The ministry states that SIGE was [implemented in all 395 basic education schools](<https://minedu.gov.cv/recursos_educativossigemenu>) by February 2019, a count of schools where it was implemented. On 25 June 2025 the government said it was [already used in all basic and secondary schools](<https://www.governo.cv/damos-mais-um-passo-significativo-no-caminho-da-modernizacao-e-inclusao-digital-no-setor-da-educacao-dne/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. A [distance learning platform for teacher training courses](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031026114025875/pdf/P175828-f967c7fb-95be-4e0d-aa08-09adcfcf0b76.pdf>); [WebLab](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>); [SIGAE](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>), for scholarship recipients; and [SIGAA-RH](<https://minedu.gov.cv/media/plano/2024/07/24/Plano_Estrategico_24072024.pdf>), the ministry's human resources system.

***Not held***. A published share or count of basic education schools entering attendance or marks, and any share for rural schools. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months, and the donor finance stated is for one module only.
