# Tunisia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry has a digital transformation strategy, which its national centre for technologies in education presented in December 2023. It [makes digital school services one of its fields](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). The ministry [runs the portal it calls Vie scolaire](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/inscription-scolaire-2026-2027-53-des-eleves-enregistres-sur-la-plateforme-vie-scolaire/>) and publishes enrolment figures from it.

**Levels in use.** The centre's December 2023 presentation [names Madrassati for the primary stage and EduservPlus for preparatory and secondary](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** In December 2023 the services listed for schools [included uploading absences, sanctions and marks](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). The same deck gives the rate of uploading absences as 2% for primary and 71% for preparatory and secondary. Children entering the first year of basic education in 2025/26 [were registered once and given a unique identifier](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2025-06-09/Com09062025.pdf>). Parents registered them online, or the regional education delegations did.

**Last twelve months.** In November 2026 the minister said [the first lot of the school information system started in April 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/11/06/sofiene-hemissi-annonce-la-numerisation-de-plusieurs-services-administratifs-avant-fin-2026/>), aimed at more than two million pupils. Vie scolaire [was launched at the end of July 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/inscription-scolaire-2026-2027-53-des-eleves-enregistres-sur-la-plateforme-vie-scolaire/>). By 20 August 2026, 53 per cent of all pupils were enrolled on it for 2026/2027. On 7 September 2026 the secondary teachers' federation [called a boycott of the ministry's new platform until it works](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/09/07/a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-la-nouvelle-plateforme-du-ministere-dysfonctionne-les-enseignants-appellent-au-boycott/1417198/>). It reported failures in enrolment and pupil transfers.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In December 2023 the centre counted [4,322 of 4,662 public primary establishments on Madrassati](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>), or 92.7%. It counted 52 of 721 private ones.

Enabling: the school broadband programme [Modern School on Every Hill](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/tunisia-unveils-digital-platform-to-boost-education-transparency-equity>).
