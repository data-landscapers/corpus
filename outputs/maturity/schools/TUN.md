# Tunisia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's digital transformation strategy, presented by its national centre for technologies in education in December 2023, [has the development of digital school services as one of its fields](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). The ministry [runs the portal it calls Vie scolaire and publishes the enrolment figures from it](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/inscription-scolaire-2026-2027-53-des-eleves-enregistres-sur-la-plateforme-vie-scolaire/>).

**Levels in use.** The centre's December 2023 presentation [names Madrassati for the primary stage and EduservPlus for preparatory and secondary](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** The services listed for schools in December 2023 [include uploading pupils' absences, sanctions and marks, with the rate of uploading absences given as 2% for the primary stage and 71% for preparatory and secondary](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). Children entering the first year of basic education in 2025/26 [were registered once, by their parents online or by the regional education delegations, and each given a unique identifier](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2025-06-09/Com09062025.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Vie scolaire [was launched at the end of July 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/inscription-scolaire-2026-2027-53-des-eleves-enregistres-sur-la-plateforme-vie-scolaire/>), and ministry figures put [53 per cent of all pupils as enrolled on it for 2026/2027 at 20 August 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/08/29/inscription-scolaire-2026-2027-53-des-eleves-enregistres-sur-la-plateforme-vie-scolaire/>). On 7 September 2026 the secondary teachers' federation [called on school staff to boycott the ministry's new platform until it works, reporting failures in enrolment and pupil transfers](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/09/07/a-lapproche-de-la-rentree-la-nouvelle-plateforme-du-ministere-dysfonctionne-les-enseignants-appellent-au-boycott/1417198/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The centre counted [4,322 of 4,662 public primary establishments (92.7%) and 52 of 721 private ones on Madrassati in December 2023](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the drop-out prevention model the ministry calls [M4D](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099031026105517065/pdf/BOSIB-4b996e72-5f8f-4efa-a0b6-e58471608bd1.pdf>) and [remote registration for national examinations](<http://www.echos.education.gov.tn/2023-12-20/Dvpt_Num.pdf>). Enabling: the school broadband programme [Modern School on Every Hill](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/tunisia-unveils-digital-platform-to-boost-education-transparency-equity>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary schools entering attendance or results is held, nor what the 2% rate for primary absences is a share of. The strategy's period, the source of finance and the school levels using the 2026 platform are not held.
