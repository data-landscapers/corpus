# Sierra Leone

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** What the country calls Wi De Ya is [led by the Teaching Service Commission with the Ministry of Basic and Senior Secondary Education](<https://www.wideya.org/en/faqs>) and was built by CGA Technologies, as stated in 2024, with [funding from the Multi Donor Trust Fund](<https://www.wideya.org/en/faqs>) of the World Bank, GPE, the EU, Irish Aid and FCDO. The ministry's Unique Learner ID initiative, reported in April 2026, is [funded by GPE](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>) and [sits within a 2024-2027 programme](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>) aligned with the Education Sector Plan 2022-2026.

**Levels in use.** Wi De Ya was [rolled out to primary schools in academic year 2022-23](<https://cgatechnologies.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-04/WDY%20Final%20Project%20report%202022-23%20vf.pdf>) and was not in use in early childhood centres or secondary schools.

**How the record is kept.** School leaders [enter each learner's profile and daily attendance on the school's tablet](<https://cgatechnologies.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-04/WDY%20Final%20Project%20report%202022-23%20vf.pdf>), submitting attendance at the end of the school day, and the data appears on dashboards for the commission and the ministry, as reported in 2023.

**Last twelve months.** The ministry has [launched an initiative to build a learner-centred data system](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>), reported in April 2026, whose Unique Learner ID system is [to be piloted in four districts, in at least 20 schools in each](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The rollout phase of 2022-23 covered [300 selected primary schools, each given a tablet and training](<https://cgatechnologies.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-04/WDY%20Final%20Project%20report%202022-23%20vf.pdf>); reporting rates for learner attendance varied by chiefdom and were lower in some rural ones. Rollouts to [1,500 and then 4,000 schools](<https://www.wideya.org/en/faqs>) were planned.

***Noted, not assessed***. Sierra Leone calls its school census an EMIS: the ministry's [EMIS](<https://mbsse.gov.sl/emis/>) lists the annual school list and the census questionnaires and reports as its data. The [Annual School Census](<https://mbsse.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Annual-School-Census-Report_2022.pdf>) covered 12,466 schools in 2022, with enumerators keying a paper questionnaire into tablets on a visit, and the [Education Data Hub](<https://educationdatahub.dsti.gov.sl/education>) publishes the 2019 census beside the West African Examinations Council's [national examination results](<https://educationdatahub.dsti.gov.sl/education>) for 2016-2019. Single-function systems are the Teaching Service Commission's [Teacher Management Information System](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MBSSE_National-Digital-Learning-Strategy_2025.pdf>); the [Situation Room](<https://mbsse.gov.sl/emis/>), which tracks indicators for a sample of schools in every district; the [foundational literacy and numeracy assessments, school quality assurance data and examination results](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>) the national data hub is to connect; the [SQAO AI Support Tool](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Report-on-SQAO-AI-tool.pdf>), tested by five quality assurance officers and reported in March 2026, with the [SQA Form](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Report-on-SQAO-AI-tool.pdf>) officers use on school visits and an [SQAO management system](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Report-on-SQAO-AI-tool.pdf>) not yet in place; the [School Catchment Area Plan and Policy App](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825083539527/pdf/P167897-44649b52-078f-42eb-b1a7-7e491f515e1e.pdf>), rolled out in June 2024; an [online platform for school subsidies and approvals](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825083539527/pdf/P167897-44649b52-078f-42eb-b1a7-7e491f515e1e.pdf>), planned at November 2024; and the Accountant General's [teacher payroll](<https://cgatechnologies.org.uk/sites/default/files/2024-04/WDY%20Final%20Project%20report%202022-23%20vf.pdf>). Learning systems named in the digital learning strategy for 2025-2030 are the [Learning Passport](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MBSSE_National-Digital-Learning-Strategy_2025.pdf>), the [One Billion tablets initiative](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MBSSE_National-Digital-Learning-Strategy_2025.pdf>) and [Teacher.AI](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MBSSE_National-Digital-Learning-Strategy_2025.pdf>). [GIGA](<https://mbsseknowledgeplatform.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/MBSSE_National-Digital-Learning-Strategy_2025.pdf>), UNICEF's school connectivity mapping with the ministry, found 205 of 11,200 schools with internet.

***Not held***. How many of the 300 schools enter data, and the total of primary schools to set them against, are not given. Nothing describes Wi De Ya in use after 2023, or says whether the rollouts to 1,500 and 4,000 schools took place.
