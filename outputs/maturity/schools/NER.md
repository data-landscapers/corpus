# Niger

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. The Ministry of National Education's [recensement scolaire 2024-2025](<https://www.stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/publication_sectorielle/annuaire/MEN/Annuaire_MENAFPPLN_2024_2025_vf.pdf>) ran on printed questionnaires that heads of pre-primary, primary and secondary establishments filled in April 2025; the regional directorates keyed them into the platform the country calls StatEduc2 from June 2025, and the census covered 17,904 of 18,282 primary establishments (97.9%). The higher-education ministry runs its own [census of schools and universities](<https://www.stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/publication_sectorielle/annuaire/MESRI/Annuaire_2024_2025.pdf>) once a year, partly on paper and partly in Excel, as it did [for 2023-2024](<https://www.stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/publication_sectorielle/annuaire/MESRI/Annuaire_2023_2024.pdf>). The primary-education ministry's [Annuaire statistique 2015](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/ner218785.pdf>) is an earlier edition of the school census yearbook. A World Bank project recorded on 14 July 2020 that the [annual school census through new digital tools](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/861041595264178002/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Niger-Learning-Improvement-for-Results-in-Education-Project-P168779-Sequence-No-01.pdf>) had not yet been conducted, with a target of 2026; the same project planned a [national online education platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/861041595264178002/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Niger-Learning-Improvement-for-Results-in-Education-Project-P168779-Sequence-No-01.pdf>) for teachers and [digitised inspection and data collection reports](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/861041595264178002/pdf/Disclosable-Version-of-the-ISR-Niger-Learning-Improvement-for-Results-in-Education-Project-P168779-Sequence-No-01.pdf>) for inspectorates in five regions. The [iPayMoney](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9055/how-uncdfs-catalytic-finance-is-driving-digital-finance-solutions-for-smarter-university-enrollment-in-niger>) platform handles online registration and fee payment at public universities, with more than 7,700 students enrolled online at the University of Maradi in 2023. The [Projet Villages Intelligents](<https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/project-detail/P167543>), World Bank financed for 2020-2026, covers rural broadband, digital financial services and digital skills.

***Not held***. No dated statement says whether primary schools keep their pupils' records on paper, and nothing held describes a system with a record for each pupil.
