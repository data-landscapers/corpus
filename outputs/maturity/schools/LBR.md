# Liberia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education [is drafting the EMIS Policy itself](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>). Its policy dialogue of 1 to 5 September 2025 [validated a costed three-year action plan whose priorities include a national unique learner and teacher ID](<https://au.int/en/pressreleases/20250910/liberia-launches-roadmap-strengthened-and-modernized-education-data-systems>). The plan is for USD 4.5 million, [60 per cent from the government and 40 per cent from development partners](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/strengthening-emis-through-peer-learning-how-liberia-leading-its-own-data-transformation>).

**Levels in use.** The African Union's peer review of 1 and 2 September 2025 [found unique learner and teacher IDs absent](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>), and describes a system that reaches about 1.5 million learners in 6,500 schools through a school census.

**How the record is kept.** At a consultation on 28 and 29 September 2026 the Ministry's planning director [described the existing system as paper-based](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>) and called for a digitised one. In September 2026 the Ministry [opened a five-day session to develop and validate a record-keeping booklet](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-ministry-moves-to-standardize-school-records-nationwide?lang=en>) for school records on students, teachers and attendance.

**Last twelve months.** The Ministry [held the two-day consultation of September 2026 to draft, review and validate an EMIS Policy](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>) for a real-time school data system, and said a study tour for its technicians was being arranged.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The Deputy Minister for Administration said at the close of the consultation that [systems at schools feeding student and teacher attendance to a central dashboard are a goal not yet reached](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>), and that the Ministry still relies largely on periodic data collection.

***Noted, not assessed***. The [Annual School Census](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-moe-launches-2025-2026-national-school-census-to-strengthen-education-system/>), which the Ministry [calls its EMIS](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>), with the Ministry's [integrated data dashboard, planned school report cards and School Quality Assessment tool](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099110424061038986/pdf/P18145519a0ea40a196e01818775bca858.pdf>); the Ministry's [Digitized Monitoring and Supervision System](<https://moe.gov.lr/minister-of-education-engages-press-on-digitized-school-monitoring-system/>); [WAEC exam data](<https://www.iped.africa/media/Liberia_Emis_Review_Report_Final.pdf>); Orange's [Digital Schools and the Wologizi Computer Lab](<https://thenewsnewspaperonline.com/orange-digital-center-releases-2025-report-exceeds-targeted-impact-goals-across-liberia/>); [education technology in public and community senior secondary schools](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099127201142611782/pdf/IDU-c1fd2132-1789-4fc6-9006-b2ae51d8770c.pdf>); and Bridge Liberia's [digital teacher guides and the Ministry's Teach by Radio](<https://www.lgtvp.com/resource/blob/233566/cde1eccd983f30aa28c5e52788b47f69/reflections-and-learnings-from-leap-2016-24-data.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing shows staff trained, equipment delivered, an agreement signed or pilot schools chosen for a school-level system or the learner ID. The Ministry's paper statement names no level and no school count, and no budget line for the plan is shown.
