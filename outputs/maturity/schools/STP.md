# São Tomé and Príncipe

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The system the country calls SIGE, the Sistema Integrado de Gestão Escolar, [was launched in July 2016 by the Ministry of Education, Science and Culture, whose minister explained it at the ceremony](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>).

**Levels in use.** The 2016 report says the first phase [puts the school library, statistics, secretariat, desk and parts of finance in one database](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>).

**How the record is kept.** By the 2016 report the system [gives each pupil a single enrolment, which the pupil only confirms each year](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>), and [pupils and parents are to see marks online](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest account held of this system is of [its launch by the ministry in July 2016](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The 2016 report describes [the launch of the system's first phase by the ministry](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2016/07/01/22182/ministerio-da-educacao-lancou-sige/>).

***Also on file***. São Tomé and Príncipe calls its school census an EMIS: the education ministry's [Sistema Integrado de Gestão Escolar (SIGE)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-10-sao-tome-principe-fortalecendo-sistemo-educativo.pdf>), which the country also writes as EMIS/SIGE, is one that schools send data to, described in 2023 beside the ministry's [Boletim Estatístico Anual](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-10-sao-tome-principe-fortalecendo-sistemo-educativo.pdf>). The ministry's planning directorate runs the [Censo Escolar](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/ENDE/ESTRAT%C3%89GIA%20NACIONAL%20DE%20DESENVOLVIMENTO%20DA%20ESTAT%C3%8DSTICA%20DE%20STP%202025-2029.pdf>). The [Sistema de Supervisão Pedagógica](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063025092532022/pdf/P169222-7421c166-6830-4b52-b6f8-f3477f354d51.pdf>) is an online platform on which 22 supervisors enter classroom observation forms for 111 pre-schools, and in May 2026 UNICEF was tendering for [dashboards for national school supervision](<https://www.telanon.info/anuncios/2026/05/07/52802/unicef-contratacao-de-uma-instituicao-de-ensino-superior-universidade-para-o-desenvolvimento-de-dashboards-para-a-supervisao-educativa-nacional/>). The ministry ran the [AALEB national learning assessment](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/676871580236018535/pdf/Sao-Tome-and-Principe-Quality-Education-for-All-Project.pdf>) in grades 2, 4 and 6 in 2016 and 2019, and has a revamped [inspection system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/676871580236018535/pdf/Sao-Tome-and-Principe-Quality-Education-for-All-Project.pdf>). The country names a national digital learning platform, the [Passaporte para a aprendizagem](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-10-sao-tome-principe-fortalecendo-sistemo-educativo.pdf>).
