# Equatorial Guinea

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the [Censo Escolar Nacional](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/educacion/educacion-inicia-en-bata-el-censo-escolar-nacional-2025-2026-para-radiografiar-las-aulas/>), the [Anuario Estadístico de la Educación](<https://prodegeguineaecuatorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Anuario-Estadistico-2020-2021-Version-Final.pdf>) built on it, and the [Sistema de Información y Mapa Escolar (SIME)](<https://prodegeguineaecuatorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/Anuario-2014-2015.pdf>), which processes the census returns and which the ministry's yearbook gives as its EMIS. Standalone: [SICOLO](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2026/07/17/lanzamiento-oficial-de-sicolo-el-primer-portal-digital-del-ecosistema-educativo-en-guinea-ecuatorial/>). Learning: [ProFuturo](<https://profuturo.education/paises/guinea-ecuatorial/>). Enabling: the [computer room at the Centro Integrado de Nsok Nsomo](<https://farolegnoticias.com/el-centro-integrado-de-nsok-nsomo-fortalece-la-educacion-digital-con-una-nueva-sala-de-informatica/>).

***Not held***. No record for each learner that schools add to during the year is held. No dated statement that primary schools keep learner records on paper only is held either.
