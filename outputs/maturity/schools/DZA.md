# Algeria

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of National Education [says it designed and built its digital platform itself](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>), in a presentation dated 2024. In April 2017 the minister [announced the system as the ministry's own information system](<https://algeriepatriotique.com/2017/04/17/benghebrit-annonce-le-lancement-officiel-du-systeme-dinformation-du-secteur-de-leducation/>).

**Levels in use.** [First-year primary enrolment for 2025-26 ran through the ministry's information system](<https://fildalgerie.dz/education-nationale-linscription-en-1ere-annee-primaire-se-fera-via-lespace-parents-du-systeme-dinformation-a-partir-du-4-mai/>), from 4 to 31 May 2025.

**How the record is kept.** The ministry's 2024 presentation says the platform [manages the schooling of 11,709,830 pupils, including marks, averages, teachers' observations and orientation, with accounts for school staff, teachers and parents](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session4/Pr%C3%A9sentation_MrKhelladi_Ministere%20de%20l'Education%20Nationale%20Algerie.pdf>). In May 2025 [first-year primary enrolment was done only through the system's parents' space, and children in preparatory classes already on the platform were enrolled automatically](<https://fildalgerie.dz/education-nationale-linscription-en-1ere-annee-primaire-se-fera-via-lespace-parents-du-systeme-dinformation-a-partir-du-4-mai/>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated step held is the [enrolment of first-year primary pupils through the system in May 2025](<https://fildalgerie.dz/education-nationale-linscription-en-1ere-annee-primaire-se-fera-via-lespace-parents-du-systeme-dinformation-a-partir-du-4-mai/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In April 2017 the minister [announced the system's launch as an experiment in the third term of 2016-17, before nationwide generalisation in 2017-18](<https://algeriepatriotique.com/2017/04/17/benghebrit-annonce-le-lancement-officiel-du-systeme-dinformation-du-secteur-de-leducation/>).
