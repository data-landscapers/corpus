# Comoros

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of National Education [supervises e-Msomo, validates its data and authorises the printing of report cards](<https://alwatwan.net/education/e-msomo-et-bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-explique-sa-strat%C3%A9gie-de-modernisation.html>), its deputy secretary general said on 25 November 2025. The digital agency ANADEN places the platform [within the Comores Numérique 2028 strategy](<https://anaden.org/realisations>). In June 2025 it was reported as [funded by UNICEF](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/comoros-launches-e-learning-and-edtech-platforms-to-modernize-schools>).

**Levels in use.** Primary and secondary. At 2 February 2026 the ministry counted [all public secondary schools and 45% of public primary schools](<https://alwatwan.net/education/bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9ducation-donne-encore-une-semaine-aux-%C3%A9coles.html>) as integrated into e-Msomo.

**How the record is kept.** School heads [enrol pupils and generate report cards, and teachers log in to enter marks](<https://alwatwan.net/education/e-msomo-et-bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-explique-sa-strat%C3%A9gie-de-modernisation.html>) that cannot be changed afterwards; each pupil gets a school identification number (November 2025). In February 2026 schools' administrative teams were [entering pupil lists, teacher lists and marks statements](<https://alwatwan.net/education/bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9ducation-donne-encore-une-semaine-aux-%C3%A9coles.html>).

**Last twelve months.** By 25 November 2025 the ministry gave registration as [100% in Ngazidja and Mwali and 65% in Ndzuwani](<https://alwatwan.net/education/e-msomo-et-bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-explique-sa-strat%C3%A9gie-de-modernisation.html>). On 2 February 2026 it gave late schools one more week and announced that the platform's single report card [will be the only document accepted for university enrolment](<https://alwatwan.net/education/bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9ducation-donne-encore-une-semaine-aux-%C3%A9coles.html>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The ministry's secretary general put the [rate of integration into e-Msomo at 45% of public primary schools](<https://alwatwan.net/education/bulletin-unique-i-le-minist%C3%A8re-de-l%E2%80%99%C3%A9ducation-donne-encore-une-semaine-aux-%C3%A9coles.html>) on 2 February 2026. It is a rate of schools integrated.

***Noted, not assessed***. [e-Shiyo](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/digitalisation-de-l%E2%80%99enseignement-les-comores-passent-%C3%A0-l%E2%80%99%C3%A8re-du-num%C3%A9rique-avec-e-msomo-et-e-shiyo-.html>), an online library of approved textbooks for pre-primary and primary pupils.

***Not held***. The number of schools behind the 45%, the share of primary schools entering marks, and any figure for private primary schools. Whether a national plan names e-Msomo, and any domestic finance for it.
