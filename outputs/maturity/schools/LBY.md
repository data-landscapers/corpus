# Libya

## EMIS

***Also on file***. The Ministry of Education calls one system its EMIS. It was [launched on 28 July 2024 after a pilot in 205 educational institutions](<https://libyaobserver.ly/education/education-ministry-launches-emis-cooperation-unicef>), and collects data from institutions by online survey. The Ministry also has an [electronic platform for private education institutions](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=362098&lang=ar>), where parents check that a private school is accredited.

***Not found***. Nothing found says how primary schools keep their learners' records, on paper or digitally. No system held is shown to hold a record per learner that a school adds to during the year.
