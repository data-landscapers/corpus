# Côte d'Ivoire

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's national strategy for digitalising education, covering 2024 to 2028, [names the project it calls DOBCONNECT among the achievements it builds on and carries a budgeted action matrix](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>). The vendor's 2019 page for the platform named SMART ECOLE says it [was designed for the ministry by its information systems directorate and is run by its schools directorate](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**Levels in use.** SMART ECOLE's page [invites pupils of nursery schools, primary schools, lycées and collèges to identify themselves online for school year 2019-2020](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**How the record is kept.** The strategy says DOBCONNECT [collects information on pupils' school performance so that each can be followed individually](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>), from January 2024 in its pilot establishments. SMART ECOLE, by its 2019 page, [manages schools from online enrolment to the proclamation of school results](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**Last twelve months.** The strategy gave [DOBCONNECT's general rollout as due in May 2024](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** DOBCONNECT [was started in January 2024 in 5 pilot establishments of the Abidjan regional directorates](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>), the strategy says.

***Noted, not assessed***. Single-function: the [digital national school map](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2508-58296-cote-d-ivoire-turns-to-digital-mapping-to-improve-school-planning>) the ministry is preparing, [bursary payment by mobile money](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>) and the ministry's mail platform [MENA-GEC](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>). Learning: the girls' application [NZASSA GIRL](<https://telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/177385013966.pdf>) and the [UVCI digital spaces and Orange Fab training modules](<https://paradigmhq.org/report/digital-inclusion-in-africa-mapping-the-ecosystem-gap/>).

***Not held***. Nothing shows DOBCONNECT beyond its five pilot establishments, their level, or who enters its data, and no count of schools using SMART ECOLE is held. No source of finance is held, and nothing dated in the last twelve months.
