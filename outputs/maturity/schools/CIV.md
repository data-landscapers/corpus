# Côte d'Ivoire

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The education ministry's national strategy for digitalising education, covering 2024 to 2028, [names the project it calls DOBCONNECT among the achievements it builds on and carries a budgeted action matrix](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>). Agence Ecofin reported in July 2025 that the Millennium Challenge Corporation's Skills for Employability and Productivity programme [finances the SIGE](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1307-130038-la-cote-d-ivoire-lance-la-plateforme-sige-pour-mieux-gerer-son-systeme-educatif>). The vendor's 2019 page for the platform named SMART ECOLE says it [was designed for the ministry by its information systems directorate and is run by its schools directorate](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**Levels in use.** SMART ECOLE's page [invites pupils of nursery schools, primary schools, lycées and collèges to identify themselves online for school year 2019-2020](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**How the record is kept.** The strategy says DOBCONNECT [collects information on pupils' school performance so that each can be followed individually](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>), from January 2024 in its pilot establishments. SMART ECOLE, by its 2019 page, [manages schools from online enrolment to the proclamation of school results](<http://smartecole.cloud/accueil.php>).

**Last twelve months.** The strategy gave [DOBCONNECT's general rollout as due in May 2024](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** DOBCONNECT [was started in January 2024 in 5 pilot establishments of the Abidjan regional directorates](<https://www.education.gouv.ci/assets/pdf/Document/Orientation/document_220.pdf>), the strategy says. Agence Ecofin reports the SIGE [was launched on 10 July 2025 in the Bouaké and San Pedro regions ahead of a national extension](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/1307-130038-la-cote-d-ivoire-lance-la-plateforme-sige-pour-mieux-gerer-son-systeme-educatif>).
