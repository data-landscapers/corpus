# Lesotho

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In 2018 the Ministry of Education and Training [was itself implementing OpenEMIS as its new education information system](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>). UNICEF supported the rollout, and the pilot [drew on an existing World Bank project](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), the Lesotho Basic Education Improvement Project. In October 2026 the Ministry [ran LEMIS, its DHIS2 for Education system, with training support from AU-IPED](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>).

**Levels in use.** The 2018 pilot [was set in one hundred low-performance schools](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), according to Development Gateway's diagnostic for UNICEF Lesotho. In October 2026 [all 365 secondary schools reported through LEMIS](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>); no primary school is named.

**How the record is kept.** The pilot [tracked individual learners](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>) in those schools, under OpenEMIS, the platform the Ministry was adopting that year.

**Last twelve months.** A training from 30 September to 4 October 2026 [covered LEMIS's individual teacher records, class management and attendance](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Development Gateway reported in 2018 that [one hundred schools were in the pilot](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), a count of pilot schools and not a share of all primary schools.

***Also on file***. The Ministry calls the yearly school census its EMIS. It has [paper annual statistical forms keyed centrally](<https://untalent.org/jobs/international-consultant-to-support-strengthening-of-the-education-management-information-system-emis-maseru-lesotho>), [OpenEMIS used only to capture the Annual School Census](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/webform/submit_to_the_library/460/Policy%20Brief_KIX%20EMIS%20Peer%20Review_cdv1.pdf>) and the [Education Statistical Bulletin](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-02-lesotho-itap-report.pdf>). Its DHIS2 successor is [LEMIS](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>), into which [district offices are to enter school data](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20Education%20Data%20Management%20ES%20LS%20_May.%202026.pdf>). Also a [teacher management information system](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>) being introduced, the [ChildSteps app and the Teaching Service's planned e-governance platforms](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-lesotho-partnership-compact.pdf>), and the [T-Connect Impact](<https://publiceyenews.com/2026/01/17/digital-dawn-in-the-highlands/>) school connection.

***Not found***. The level of the pilot schools and who entered the records are not stated, and nothing dated since 2018 shows whether the pilot still runs or went further. No source says primary schools use LEMIS or enter each learner's attendance in it. No current plan or domestic budget for a per-learner system has been found.
