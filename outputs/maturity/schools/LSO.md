# Lesotho

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** In 2018 the Ministry of Education and Training [was itself implementing OpenEMIS as its new education information system](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>). UNICEF supported the rollout, and the pilot [drew on an existing World Bank project](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), the Lesotho Basic Education Improvement Project.

**Levels in use.** The 2018 pilot [was set in one hundred low-performance schools](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), according to Development Gateway's diagnostic for UNICEF Lesotho.

**How the record is kept.** The pilot [tracked individual learners](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>) in those schools, under OpenEMIS, the platform the Ministry was adopting that year.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated event is from 2018, when, with UNICEF supporting it, [the rollout was beginning with the pilot](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** Development Gateway reported in 2018 that [one hundred schools were in the pilot](<https://developmentgateway.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UNICEF_Lesotho_Diagnostic.pdf>), a count of pilot schools and not a share of all primary schools.

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school census, which the Ministry calls its EMIS: [paper annual statistical forms keyed centrally](<https://untalent.org/jobs/international-consultant-to-support-strengthening-of-the-education-management-information-system-emis-maseru-lesotho>), [OpenEMIS used only to capture the Annual School Census](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/webform/submit_to_the_library/460/Policy%20Brief_KIX%20EMIS%20Peer%20Review_cdv1.pdf>), the [Education Statistical Bulletin](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-02-lesotho-itap-report.pdf>) and its DHIS2 successor, [LEMIS](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>), into which [district offices are to enter school data](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20Education%20Data%20Management%20ES%20LS%20_May.%202026.pdf>). Also a [teacher management information system](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>) being introduced, the [ChildSteps app and the Teaching Service's planned e-governance platforms](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-lesotho-partnership-compact.pdf>), and the [T-Connect Impact](<https://publiceyenews.com/2026/01/17/digital-dawn-in-the-highlands/>) school connection.

***Not held***. The level of the pilot schools and who entered the records are not stated, and nothing dated since 2018 shows whether the pilot still runs or went further. No current plan or domestic budget for a per-learner system is held.
