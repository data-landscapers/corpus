# Egypt

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education and Technical Education [signed the agreement for the platform on 10 August 2026](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/574335/Egypt/Society/Egypt-to-roll-out-nationwide-studentmonitoring-pla.aspx>). It calls the platform part of its integrated national digital system for managing and monitoring education. Its Education Sector Plan 2023-2027 [gives "strengthen EMIS" a programme and an expenditure line in each subsector, primary included](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>). The plan makes the ministry responsible for building the planned system.

**Levels in use.** The 2023-2027 plan says the EMIS [will be expanded to hold data down to class and student level](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>). Each student is to have a digital profile.

**How the record is kept.** By the announcement of August 2026 the platform [is to give teachers digital tools to record attendance and absences and to follow each student's performance and assessment results](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/574335/Egypt/Society/Egypt-to-roll-out-nationwide-studentmonitoring-pla.aspx>). The ministry is to receive updated data daily.

**Last twelve months.** On 10 August 2026 the ministry [signed an agreement with the Japanese company SPRIX](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/574335/Egypt/Society/Egypt-to-roll-out-nationwide-studentmonitoring-pla.aspx>). It is for a nationwide attendance, performance and assessment platform.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The ministry said the platform [will start in several education departments in 2026/2027 before gradual national expansion](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/574335/Egypt/Society/Egypt-to-roll-out-nationwide-studentmonitoring-pla.aspx>).

***Also on file***. The ministry calls its yearly count of students, teachers, classrooms and schools [its EMIS, the source of the statistical yearbooks](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>). The same 2023-2027 plan names [planned online enrolment application platforms and the Egyptian Knowledge Bank](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>). It also names the learning management system, and school internet and computers. Also noted are the [yearly verified national education dataset and the smart school criteria](<https://web.archive.org/web/20260106131323/https://world-education-blog.org/2025/12/22/monitoring-equity-through-verified-data-how-egypts-2025-education-reforms-are-reframing-progress-and-revealing-what-still-must-be-done/>), and the [Kirio platform](<https://moe.gov.eg/en/what-s-on/news/structur/>). The [Takaful and Karama cash transfer programme's management information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060624125531275/txt/BOSIB169cd52710ff1abfc17842717d1e22.txt>) holds school attendance monitoring information on beneficiary children. Also noted are the [Digital Content Dashboard and the tablet-based assessment platform for grades 10 to 12](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072825125534851/pdf/P157809-2b9a8bd3-ac47-4dd6-a7a4-6468117f2e33.pdf>). The learning platform [QUREO](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/574335/Egypt/Society/Egypt-to-roll-out-nationwide-studentmonitoring-pla.aspx>) is for first-year secondary.

***Not found***. At which levels the platform will run, how many schools will start on it, and whether any school yet enters data. How it is financed is not stated.
