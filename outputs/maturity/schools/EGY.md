# Egypt

## EMIS

***Noted, not assessed***. The ministry calls its yearly count of students, teachers, classrooms and schools [its EMIS, the source of the statistical yearbooks](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>); the same 2023-2027 plan names [planned online enrolment application platforms, the Egyptian Knowledge Bank and learning management system, and school internet and computers](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>). Also noted are the [yearly verified national education dataset and the smart school criteria](<https://web.archive.org/web/20260106131323/https://world-education-blog.org/2025/12/22/monitoring-equity-through-verified-data-how-egypts-2025-education-reforms-are-reframing-progress-and-revealing-what-still-must-be-done/>); the [Kirio platform](<https://moe.gov.eg/en/what-s-on/news/structur/>); the [Takaful and Karama cash transfer programme's management information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060624125531275/txt/BOSIB169cd52710ff1abfc17842717d1e22.txt>), which holds school attendance monitoring information on beneficiary children; and the [Digital Content Dashboard and the tablet-based assessment platform for grades 10 to 12](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099072825125534851/pdf/P157809-2b9a8bd3-ac47-4dd6-a7a4-6468117f2e33.pdf>).

***Not held***. At which levels a system of pupil records is in use, how the record is kept and how many primary schools enter data. How the planned system is financed is not stated, and nothing dated in the past twelve months is held.
