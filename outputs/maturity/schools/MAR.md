# Morocco

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** MASSAR [was developed by the Ministry of National Education and Vocational Training and launched at the end of 2013](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>), the World Bank's identity diagnostic states. On 7 May 2026 the ministry's secretary-general [instructed the regional academies on who records and who validates absence in 'Massar+ Idara'](<https://www.taalimpress.info/2026/05/blog-post.html>).

**Levels in use.** In May 2014 MASSAR's register [covered every child aged 6 to 18 enrolled in any national educational establishment, 6,512,192 pupils](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>), by the same diagnostic. The national evaluation body's atlas of 2019 [follows cohorts of primary pupils into lower secondary from MASSAR's individual records for 2014 to 2018](<https://www.csefrs.ma/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/ATLAS-TERRITORIAL-DE-LABANDON-SCOLAIRE-18-12-web.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** In June 2014 each establishment [registered and vetted its own pupils into a central database, and each pupil kept one number for the whole of their schooling](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>). By the 2019 atlas MASSAR [has a pupil assessment component through which parents consult their children's marks online](<https://www.csefrs.ma/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/ATLAS-TERRITORIAL-DE-LABANDON-SCOLAIRE-18-12-web.pdf>). In the pioneer middle schools, by the ministry's letter of 7 May 2026, [teachers record absence at the start of each lesson and the school administration keys it into 'Massar+ Idara'](<https://www.taalimpress.info/2026/05/blog-post.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The letter of 7 May 2026 [rolls out a new digital component for daily monitoring of pupil absence in the pioneer middle schools](<https://www.taalimpress.info/2026/05/blog-post.html>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** In June 2014 pupils [were vetted at each of the country's 10,000 learning establishments](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>), the World Bank's diagnostic states: a figure for schools of all levels registering pupils.

***Noted, not assessed***. Census: the [recensement scolaire annuel 2025-2026](<https://fr.expresstv.ma/2025/10/le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-lance-le-recensement-scolaire-annuel-pour-lannee-scolaire-2025-2026/>). Single-function: the [Tayssir identity register](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/Morocco_ID4D_Diagnostic_Web404018.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of primary schools entering attendance or results is held, and daily entry is shown for lower secondary schools only. Who keys primary pupils' marks, and the plan and financing, are not held.
