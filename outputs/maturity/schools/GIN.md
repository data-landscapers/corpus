# Guinea

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Pre-University Education [instituted the unique pupil number it calls the matricule unique](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>), within a digitalisation run by its examinations directorate, which also [coordinates training on the registration platform](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2602-136190-la-guinee-lance-l-immatriculation-numerique-des-eleves-du-primaire>). A roadmap [validated on 2 November 2021](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>) provides for a single integrated system whose pupil module covers attendance, class assessments and examination results.

**Levels in use.** The matricule [began in 2022 at collège and lycée and was extended to primary](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>) in a nationwide registration from 5 to 25 March 2026. In Labé prefecture a mission ordered on 30 March 2026 [accompanied the registration of primary pupils](<https://guineematin.com/2026/05/14/labe-un-cadre-de-la-dpe-cite-dans-une-affaire-presumee-de-detournement-de-pres-de-200-millions-gnf/>), each photographed for the matricule.

**How the record is kept.** Each pupil [is given one matricule in the registration](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>), for which public and private school heads were mobilised in March 2026; transfers between schools are then detected automatically.

**Last twelve months.** The ministry [announced the digital registration of primary pupils on 25 February 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2602-136190-la-guinee-lance-l-immatriculation-numerique-des-eleves-du-primaire>), with officers of the regional, prefectoral and communal education offices starting training. Registration [was launched in Matoto on 5 March 2026](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>) and [ran school by school in Labé](<https://guineematin.com/2026/05/14/labe-un-cadre-de-la-dpe-cite-dans-une-affaire-presumee-de-detournement-de-pres-de-200-millions-gnf/>) under the order of 30 March.

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The March 2026 registration [covered the whole national territory, public and private schools alike](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>), by the account of Matoto's communal education director.

***Noted, not assessed***. The yearly school data collection, which Guinea calls its [Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf>), with the [annual statistical collection (RSA)](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>) and the [basic education data collection financed by the World Bank](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>) behind it; a [planned mobile entry of yearly school-level information, cancelled in June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf>); the [examinations management application](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>) and [online registration of examination candidates](<https://www.avenirguinee.org/2026/03/05/lancement-immatriculation-unique-eleves-matoto-guinee/>); [teacher personnel tools](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>); [paper school inspection tools](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf>) and a [planned pilot of unique codes for staff posts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099021025115512289/pdf/BOSIB-e98530da-4262-4114-a3b8-9e4daa06717a.pdf>); [teacher tablets for training](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123075517241/txt/P1674781dab5d60fb1b3b41a1e5a3eb4e1d.txt>); [digital teaching and distance-training initiatives](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>); and [school connection initiatives](<https://mepua.gov.gn/file/2023/10/Rapport-final-activites.pdf>).

***Not held***. No number or share of primary schools registered is given, and no source says who keys the record: those trained are officers of the education offices, not school staff. No plan dated within three years and no source of finance is held.
