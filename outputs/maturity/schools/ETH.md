# Ethiopia

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Education [developed and completed its web-based EMIS and finalised a directive for it, with World Bank support](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>), by June 2025. It was [built under a Global Partnership for Education grant through the World Bank](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>). The ministry's sector plan for 2020/21 to 2024/25 [planned a student database and a digital application for schools to report pupils' daily data](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/Ethiopian%20Education%20Sector%20Development%20Program%206%20(ESDP%20VI).pdf>).

**Levels in use.** At June 2025 the system [covered federal, regional, woreda and school levels](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>), and [deployment to 4,500 primary schools in 156 weredas was planned for July 2025 to June 2026](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>).

**How the record is kept.** The system [gives unique identification numbers to students, teachers and schools](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>), as reported at June 2025. A UNESCO-commissioned case study researched in late 2019 found that [schools keep registers on paper and collect attendance without storing it centrally](<https://www.educationcannotwait.org/sites/default/files/2022-03/Strengthening%20Education%20Management%20Information%20Systems%20%28EMIS%29%20and%20Data%20for%20Increased%20Resilience%20to%20Crisis_%20country%20case%20study_%20Ethopia.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In November 2025 UNICEF [partnered with HISP Ethiopia and the HISP Centre to assist the ministry and regional education bureaus on DHIS2 for Education](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>), working with schools in four regions, where [tools to collect learning outcomes and student attendance were still to be developed](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** UNICEF, the grant agent, reports that by June 2025 the digitised system [had been finalised and piloted, with deployment to primary schools not begun](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>); [the tender for 4,500 primary-school computers was set for August 2025](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2025-10-annual-progress-report-system-transformation-gea-grant-ethiopia.pdf>). The sector plan published in 2021 gave [a baseline of none for a student database and zero institutions networked](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/Ethiopian%20Education%20Sector%20Development%20Program%206%20(ESDP%20VI).pdf>).
