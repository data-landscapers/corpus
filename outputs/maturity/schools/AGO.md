# Angola

## EMIS

***Also on file***. Angola calls its school census an EMIS. The education ministry's [Sistema de Informação de Gestão Escolar (SIGE)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099925009082232091/pdf/BOSIB09680251b0f10bcb7096ad5c063d35.pdf>) is also called the EMIS and the School Management System. It is a questionnaire that schools answer, described in 2022. The ministry's [Censo Escolar Anual](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MjMzMDA%3D>) covered every school in operation in all 18 provinces in 2021/2022, in two collections a year. It is published as the [Anuário Estatístico da Educação](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MjMzMDA%3D>). The yearly national return is also called the [Cadastro](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099925009082232091/pdf/BOSIB09680251b0f10bcb7096ad5c063d35.pdf>). In a [pilot in Huíla province](<https://www.unicef.org/stories/using-data-improve-education-angola>) reported in 2017, 28 technicians visited 1,843 primary and secondary schools and keyed the principal's answers on tablets.

***Not found***. No dated statement says whether primary schools keep their pupils' records on paper. A June 2025 decree lists a pupil data platform among the instruments for recording assessment results. Nothing found says whether it has been built or whether any school uses it.
