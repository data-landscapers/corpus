# Benin

## EMIS

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Minister of Nursery and Primary Education, in a letter of 16 February 2026, [calls EDUCMASTER a management tool for the sub-sector](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-le-ministre-karimou-exige-la-mise-a-jour-complete-des-donnees-des-classes-intermediaires-sur-educmaster/>) and orders its use by his departmental directors, district heads and school heads. The government's progress report of December 2020 [presents the system, which it calls the SIGE, as a reform of its own action programme](<https://beninrevele.bj/doc/29/download>).

**Levels in use.** The December 2020 report says use of the SIGE, which it also calls Educmaster, [is compulsory in all public and private pre-primary, primary, secondary and technical schools](<https://beninrevele.bj/doc/29/download>). La Nation [reported a public primary school head in Cotonou entering her pupils](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-educmaster-la-revolution-numerique-au-service-de-lecole>) on 18 December 2024.

**How the record is kept.** The minister's letter of February 2026 says [schools themselves enter enrolments and update class lists](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-le-ministre-karimou-exige-la-mise-a-jour-complete-des-donnees-des-classes-intermediaires-sur-educmaster/>). In secondary schools, the ministry's information systems director said in December 2024, [collèges and lycées enter enrolments, transfers and absences and load each pupil's end-of-semester averages](<https://lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-educmaster-la-revolution-numerique-au-service-de-lecole>).

**Last twelve months.** On 16 February 2026 the minister [ordered every school to complete its enrolments for all nursery and primary classes by 23 February](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-le-ministre-karimou-exige-la-mise-a-jour-complete-des-donnees-des-classes-intermediaires-sur-educmaster/>), with heads of schools that did not to be barred from examination work in June 2026. UNICEF reported in February 2026 that [an extension with a school evaluation and grades module was under way](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/au-b%C3%A9nin-educmaster-simpose-comme-un-outil-cl%C3%A9-pour-piloter-l%C3%A9ducation>).

**Primary schools doing the digital input.** The letter of February 2026 says [several schools enter only their final-year examination candidates](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-le-ministre-karimou-exige-la-mise-a-jour-complete-des-donnees-des-classes-intermediaires-sur-educmaster/>) and leave the other nursery and primary classes not updated.

***Also on file***. The primary ministry's [emp.educmaster.bj](<https://memp.gouv.bj/communique/9/telecharger>), where primary schools and parents looked up candidates' primary-certificate marks in August 2026, and the government's examination results portal, [eresultats.bj](<https://beninrevele.bj/doc/29/download>), in service in December 2020.

***Not found***. No number or share of primary schools entering data is published, and nothing shows primary schools entering attendance or results during the year. How the system is paid for is not stated, and no current plan naming it has been found.
