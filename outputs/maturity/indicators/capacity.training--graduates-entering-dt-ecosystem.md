# Graduates entering DT ecosystem

*Measure · Capacity · `capacity.training--graduates-entering-dt-ecosystem`*

**Norm**: Agenda 2063 STYIP; Continental TVET Strategy (AU) — ≥ 40 % STEM graduates; TVET enrolment + 60 %; youth unemployment 14 % by 2033

**Band**: quintiles, higher is better; cuts 0.2286 · 0.3278 · 0.5808 (quintiles cut 2026-07-31). employment in information and communication (ISIC Rev. 4 section J), per cent of total employment. **Direction** higher. The STYIP's 40 %-STEM figure is the university row's; using it here would count one fact twice, so this row reads absorption into the sector instead.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Information and communication employment in the bottom Africa quintile; provisionally below 0.3 % of total employment. |
| 2 below | Second quintile; provisionally 0.3–0.6 %. |
| 3 approaching | Middle quintile; provisionally 0.6–1.0 %. |
| 4 meets | Fourth or top quintile, short of the STYIP figure; provisionally 1.0 % or more. |
| 5 exceeds | Top quintile, with youth unemployment at or below 14 % on the labour force survey — the STYIP 2033 figure. |
