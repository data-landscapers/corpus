# Use of digital id in other systems

*System · DPI · `dpi.exchange--use-of-digital-id-in-other-systems`*

**Norm**: AU Interop. Framework; AfCFTA DTP Arts 14, 19 (AU) — Use cases in finance, health, social protection; mutual recognition

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no other system relies on the national ID for enrolment or verification. |
| 2 Nascent | Use of the ID by another system planned, piloted or contracted (a KYC pilot, a SIM-registration link announced). |
| 3 Established | The ID used by one or two systems in service — SIM registration, a bank's KYC, a social register — with no verification service offered to others. |
| 4 Operating | Services, public and private, consuming a verification or authentication service built on the ID — banks' KYC, telecoms, health, social programmes — across sectors on record, with usage published; the public registers keyed on the number are `dpi.id--use-by-other-systems`'s evidence, not this row's. |
| 5 Leading | The Framework's end state: the ID the common credential for public and private services, with selective disclosure and privacy protection in law, and mutual recognition provided for (DTP Art. 19). |
