# Digital literacy (civil service)

*Measure · Capacity · `capacity.literacy--digital-literacy-civil-service`*

**Norm**: Public Service Charter Art. 21; DPF (AU) — Systematic capacity development; data skills in state institutions

**Band**: quintiles, higher is better; cuts 0.1 · 10 · 40 (provisional). civil servants who have completed a government digital-skills programme or certification, per cent of the civil service headcount. **Direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | No civil-service digital-skills programme in operation, on a citation (0 %). |
| 2 below | Less than 10 % of the civil service trained (provisional). |
| 3 approaching | 10–40 % (provisional). |
| 4 meets | 40 % or more (provisional), short of the Charter condition. |
| 5 exceeds | The Charter's Art. 21 end state: a systematic programme in force across the public service with results published, and 40 % or more trained. |
