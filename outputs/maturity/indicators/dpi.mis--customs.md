# Customs

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--customs`*

**Norm**: AfCFTA Protocol on Trade in Goods Annexes 3 and 4 (AU) — Customs automation; single window; risk management; e-payment

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that customs declarations are processed on paper. |
| 2 Nascent | A customs management system procured, piloted at some posts, or being upgraded. |
| 3 Established | An automated customs system in service at the main ports and airports, with paper at other posts and no single window. |
| 4 Operating | Automated processing at all posts, risk management in use, electronic payment of duties, and a single window in service or under phased rollout with agencies connected. |
| 5 Leading | The Annexes' end state: customs automation, a national single window connecting all border agencies, electronic exchange with trading partners, and the trade-facilitation measures in effect. |
