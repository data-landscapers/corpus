# Satellite broadband licensing and availability

*Instrument · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.connect--satellite-broadband-licensing-and-availability`*

**Norm**: ATU-R Rec. 005-0 (2021); African Space Strategy (2016) (continental) — Blanket licensing of user terminals, mutual recognition, streamlined NGSO authorisation

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that satellite broadband is not licensed, or that applications are refused or unanswered. |
| 2 Nascent | A licence application pending or a framework in drafting; a licence granted to one operator with no service launched. |
| 3 Established | Satellite broadband licensed and in service, with user terminals authorised individually or through a single licensee (ATU-R 005-0's *individual* case). |
| 4 Operating | Terminals licensed on a blanket or class basis, more than one operator or reseller licensed, and NGSO authorisation streamlined with published fees (the *blanket licensing* case). |
| 5 Leading | The ATU recommendation's end state: blanket licensing, free circulation of visiting terminals on mutual recognition, spectrum fees published with no refusal or withdrawal on fee grounds on record, and availability reported. |
