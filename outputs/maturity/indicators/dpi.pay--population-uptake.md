# Population uptake

*Measure · DPI · `dpi.pay--population-uptake`*

**Norm**: World Bank Global Findex (global) — Account ownership and digital-payment use (2024: SSA 58 %, global 79 %)

**Band**: target, higher is better; cuts 26 · 53 · 79. adults 15+ with an account at a financial institution or a mobile-money provider, per cent, with adults who made or received a digital payment in the past year as the secondary figure. **Direction** higher.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Account ownership below 26 % of adults. |
| 2 below | 26–53 %. |
| 3 approaching | 53–79 %. |
| 4 meets | 79 % or more — the global rate at the frozen Findex vintage. |
| 5 exceeds | Ownership 79 % or more, and 79 % or more of adults making or receiving a digital payment — use as well as access. |
