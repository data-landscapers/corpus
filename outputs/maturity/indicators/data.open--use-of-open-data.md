# Use of open data

*System · Data · `data.open--use-of-open-data`*

**Norm**: DPF; Africa Data Consensus (2015); Charter Art. 3 (AU) — Open data standards; openness across the data value chain; statistics not made inaccessible

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no public body publishes data for reuse — statistics behind request or fee, no licence, no portal. |
| 2 Nascent | Data published on a portal or site with no open licence, bulk download or interface stated; or a portal planned or piloted. |
| 3 Established | Data published under an open licence by at least one body (the statistics office), with download and no interface or reuse on record. |
| 4 Operating | Open data published across several bodies under a licence, with bulk download or an interface, and reuse on record — applications, research, journalism citing it, or a published usage figure. |
| 5 Leading | The DPF and Consensus end state: open by default across the data value chain, timely and disaggregated, interoperable through standards, and reuse reported — applications, research and reporting built on it on record. |
