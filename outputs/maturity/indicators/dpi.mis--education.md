# Education

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--education`*

**Norm**: AU Digital Education Strategy (2022) (AU) — EMIS 1.0 → 2.0

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no EMIS exists. |
| 2 Nascent | An EMIS procured, piloted or under construction. |
| 3 Established | EMIS 1.0 in service: aggregate school returns and an annual census published. |
| 4 Operating | EMIS 2.0 in service: individual learner, teacher and institution records with unique identifiers, used for planning and payroll. |
| 5 Leading | The DES end state: EMIS 2.0 linked to the national ID and other sectors, analytics in use for policy, and the data model published. |
