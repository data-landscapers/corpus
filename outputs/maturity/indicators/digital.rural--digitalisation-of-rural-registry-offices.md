# Digitalisation of rural registry offices

*System · Digitalisation · `digital.rural--digitalisation-of-rural-registry-offices`*

**Norm**: DTS; APAI-CRVS / CAMCR; Kampala Convention Art. 13 (AU) — 99.9 % legal identity by 2030; register all vital events; IDP documentation without unreasonable conditions

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that registration offices outside the main cities work on paper with no connectivity. |
| 2 Nascent | A digital registration platform piloted in a first group of communes or districts, or equipment rehabilitated at some posts. |
| 3 Established | Digital registration in service in some rural offices or districts, with the rest on paper or transmitting by hand. |
| 4 Operating | Rural offices connected and registering digitally across most districts, records flowing to the central register, and the share of offices covered published. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: every office registering digitally into the civil register and the ID system, with the DTS legal-identity target met on the published figure and IDP and displaced populations served (Kampala Art. 13). |
