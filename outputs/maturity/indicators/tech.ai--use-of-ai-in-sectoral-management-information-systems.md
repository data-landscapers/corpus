# Use of AI in sectoral management information systems

*System · Technology · `tech.ai--use-of-ai-in-sectoral-management-information-systems`*

**Norm**: Continental AI Strategy; DPF §5.3 (AU) — "Accelerate the adoption of AI in the core sectors" (agriculture, education, health, climate)

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no sectoral system (health, agriculture, education, tax, climate) uses AI or advanced analytics. |
| 2 Nascent | A sectoral AI tool in pilot or testing (a diagnostic tool in two provinces, a risk-analysis model in testing). |
| 3 Established | One sectoral AI system in service and producing decisions or outputs on record (a tax risk engine issuing notices, a disease model informing a campaign). |
| 4 Operating | AI in service in several sectors' systems, built or adapted nationally, with data warehouses or pipelines supporting them and results reported. |
| 5 Leading | The AI Strategy's end state: AI adopted in the core sectors, national centres of excellence or datasets supporting it, and outcomes reported. |
