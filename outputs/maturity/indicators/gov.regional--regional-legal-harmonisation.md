# Regional legal harmonisation

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.regional--regional-legal-harmonisation`*

**Norm**: Malabo Art. 28; DTS; AfCFTA DTP Art. 43 (AU) — Art. 28 regional harmonisation of cybercrime law and mutual legal assistance; DTS harmonised regional strategies

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no domestic instrument is harmonised with a REC or AU model or act. |
| 2 Nascent | A REC model law or supplementary act referenced in a draft or bill. |
| 3 Established | A REC instrument domesticated in one area of the cyberlaw set (data protection, cybercrime or e-transactions). |
| 4 Operating | REC instruments domesticated across the cyberlaw set, with mutual legal assistance or cross-border cooperation arrangements on record. |
| 5 Leading | Malabo and the AfCFTA Digital Trade Protocol ratified, the full set harmonised, and harmonisation reported to the REC or AU. |
