# Non-governmental contribution to national policy

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.discourse--non-governmental-contribution-to-national-policy`*

**Norm**: ACDEG; Public Service Charter; DPF (AU) — ACDEG Arts 12, 27, 28, 30 civil society and participation; Charter Art. 2(4); Malabo Art. 24 policy "in collaboration with stakeholders"; ACHPR Principle 17 multi-stakeholder regulation

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that digital instruments are made without consultation, or that civil society is excluded from the process. |
| 2 Nascent | Ad hoc consultation on a digital instrument on record, with no requirement or standing mechanism. |
| 3 Established | Consultation required by law or standing practice for digital policy, with published consultations and responses on record. |
| 4 Operating | Standing multi-stakeholder bodies (a national IGF, an advisory council, a regulator's consultative committee) with a role in policy, and non-governmental input traceable in adopted instruments. |
| 5 Leading | The ACDEG and Declaration end state: a multi-stakeholder model of regulation, an enabling environment for civil society in law, and co-created commitments (an OGP action plan or equivalent) reported against. |
