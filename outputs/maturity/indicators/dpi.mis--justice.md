# Justice

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--justice`*

**Norm**: ACHPR Fair Trial Principles (2003) (AU) — Systems for recording proceedings, storing information, public access

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that court proceedings and records are paper-based with no case-management system. |
| 2 Nascent | A case-management or e-justice system procured, piloted in some courts, or under construction. |
| 3 Established | A case-management system in service in some courts or one tier, with decisions not published online. |
| 4 Operating | Case management in service across the main courts, e-filing available, and decisions published online with public access to case status. |
| 5 Leading | The Principles' end state: systems for recording proceedings, storing information and public access in service across the judiciary, all decisions published, and integration with police, prosecution and prisons on record. |
