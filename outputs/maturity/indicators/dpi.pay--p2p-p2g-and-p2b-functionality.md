# P2P, P2G and P2B functionality

*System · DPI · `dpi.pay--p2p-p2g-and-p2b-functionality`*

**Norm**: DTS (AU) — Interoperability of e-money and DFS; low-cost channels and agents

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no mobile money or retail digital payment service is licensed. |
| 2 Nascent | A mobile money or e-money service licensed or piloted. |
| 3 Established | Retail digital payment services in service with no interoperability between providers, and payments to government or merchants limited. |
| 4 Operating | Provider interoperability in service (a switch or bilateral links), merchant and government payments accepted through the services, agent networks reported. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: national interoperability across e-money and bank accounts, low-cost channels and agents reaching underserved areas, and usage reported. |
