# Tech startup ecosystem

*Instrument · Technology · `tech.innovate--tech-startup-ecosystem`*

**Norm**: DTS; AU Startup Model Law Framework (2024) (AU) — National start-up strategies and laws; innovation fund; angel networks; seven policy areas

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no startup law, strategy or public support instrument exists and no venture or angel financing is on record. |
| 2 Nascent | A startup bill drafted or tabled, a competition or grant scheme opened, or a strategy in consultation. |
| 3 Established | A startup law or strategy enacted or adopted covering some of the Framework's seven areas (definition, governance, institutional support, taxation, funding, regulatory enablement, skills), with implementing measures pending. |
| 4 Operating | The law in force with implementing measures across most of the seven areas — a labelling or registration regime operating, tax or procurement measures applied, a fund or angel network financing startups on record. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: a national startup strategy and law in force across the seven areas, an innovation fund and angel networks operating, patents and outcomes reported. |
