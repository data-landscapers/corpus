# Access to information legislation

*Instrument · Governance · `gov.legislate--access-to-information-legislation`*

**Norm**: ACHPR Declaration 2019; ACHPR Model Law on Access to Information (2013) (AU) — Principle 26 right of access; Principle 29 proactive disclosure; Principle 34 independent oversight mechanism established by law

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no access-to-information law exists, with at most a constitutional right. |
| 2 Nascent | An access-to-information bill drafted, tabled or under consultation. |
| 3 Established | An access-to-information law enacted, with the oversight mechanism, procedures or commencement pending. |
| 4 Operating | The law in force with procedures, proactive-disclosure obligations and an oversight mechanism established by law (Principle 34), with requests or decisions on record. |
| 5 Leading | The Model Law met: proactive disclosure including through digital means, an independent oversight body operating with published decisions, and access that is expeditious and inexpensive. |
