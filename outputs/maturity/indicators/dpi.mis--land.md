# Land

*System · DPI · `dpi.mis--land`*

**Norm**: AU Declaration on Land (2009); Framework and Guidelines on Land Policy (AU) — As land register: F&G §3.6.2 computerised Land Information Systems for land rights delivery

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that land administration (transactions, valuation, planning) runs on paper. |
| 2 Nascent | A land information system procured, piloted in some regions or under construction. |
| 3 Established | A land information system in service for registration in some areas, with valuation, planning and dispute records separate. |
| 4 Operating | A land information system in service nationally linking registration, cadastre and valuation, with transactions processed electronically and linked to the tax authority. |
| 5 Leading | The F&G end state (§3.6.2): a computerised land information system through which land rights are registered and tracked across the administration functions, customary tenure included, with public access — e.g. integrated across FELA's pathways on the LADM standard. |
