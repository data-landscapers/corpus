# Gender equity

*Measure · Inclusion · `include.access--gender-equity`*

**Norm**: Maputo Protocol Arts 12, 18, 19; DTS; African Digital Compact (AU) — Women's education in science and technology; access to and control of information technologies; DTS STEAM skills for women and girls

**Band**: quintiles, lower is better; cuts 40.4116 · 27.0698 · 14.8157 · 2 (quintiles cut 2026-07-31). gender gap in internet use — (men's rate − women's rate) ÷ men's rate, per cent. **Direction** lower.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 far below | Gender gap in internet use in the Africa quintile with the widest gaps; provisionally more than 30 %. |
| 2 below | Second-widest quintile; provisionally more than 20 % and at most 30 %. |
| 3 approaching | Middle quintile; provisionally more than 10 % and at most 20 %. |
| 4 meets | The two narrowest quintiles, short of parity; provisionally more than 2 % and at most 10 %. |
| 5 exceeds | Parity — a gap of 2 % or less — on a survey figure: women's access to and use of information technologies on equal terms (Maputo Art. 18(2)(b)). |
