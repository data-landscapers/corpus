# Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

*System · DPI · `dpi.id--interoperability-of-birth-registration-and-digital-id`*

**Norm**: AU Interop. Framework §2.2; CAMCR 2019 (Lusaka Declaration paras 3, 7) (AU) — Builds on APAI-CRVS; digitisation for interoperability between identity management and CRVS systems (para 3); harmonised law "including interoperability of systems" (para 7)

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that the civil register and the ID system do not exchange records. |
| 2 Nascent | A linkage planned, contracted or piloted; a single agency mandated over both registers with no exchange yet. |
| 3 Established | Exchange in service in one direction or for one event (births feed the ID, or deaths retire credentials), or the two registers held by one agency on separate platforms. |
| 4 Operating | Two-way exchange in service across vital events — births enrol, deaths retire, name and status changes propagate — with reconciliation reported. |
| 5 Leading | The Handbook's end state: civil registration, vital statistics, the population register and the ID system fully interoperable and simultaneous, as recognised by the CRVS programme or a published assessment. |
