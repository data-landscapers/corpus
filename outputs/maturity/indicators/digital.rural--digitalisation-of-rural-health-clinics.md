# Digitalisation of rural health clinics

*System · Digitalisation · `digital.rural--digitalisation-of-rural-health-clinics`*

**Norm**: Africa CDC DTS (2023); PHC Digitalisation Framework (2025–26) (continental) — HealthConnekt: 100,000 facilities connected and 2 m CHWs equipped by 2030; 90 % digitally enabled PHC by 2035

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that rural or primary facilities have no connectivity, equipment or digital reporting. |
| 2 Nascent | Connectivity or devices delivered to some facilities, staff trained on a digital tool, or a rollout beyond pilot sites planned. |
| 3 Established | Rural facilities connected and reporting digitally in some districts, or one function digital (surveillance, laboratory, supply chain) with the rest on paper. |
| 4 Operating | Rural facilities connected and equipped across most districts, patient or reporting records digital, linked to the national HMIS, and the share of facilities covered published. |
| 5 Leading | The Africa CDC end state: the country's facilities and community health workers connected and equipped on the published figure, primary care digitally enabled to the framework's standard, and coverage reported. |
