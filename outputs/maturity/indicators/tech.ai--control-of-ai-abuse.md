# Control of AI abuse

*Instrument · Technology · `tech.ai--control-of-ai-abuse`*

**Norm**: Continental AI Strategy focus area 2; DTP Annex on Emerging Technologies (AU) — Risk-based regulation; independent oversight institutions; regional AI Ethics Board; safety standards

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no authority, rule or mechanism addresses AI harms — misinformation, manipulation, bias, unsafe systems. |
| 2 Nascent | A draft law providing for a competent authority without designating it; a workshop, guideline or ethics principle with no legal effect. |
| 3 Established | A rule in force addressing at least one AI harm (deepfakes, automated decisions, election manipulation) or an authority designated with a mandate, with no enforcement on record. |
| 4 Operating | An authority operating with powers over AI harms, risk-based rules in force, and enforcement, audit or redress decisions on record. |
| 5 Leading | The AI Strategy's end state: risk-based regulation, an independent oversight institution with enforcement and redress, technical safety standards, and participation in regional oversight (the AI Ethics Board or REC arrangements). |
