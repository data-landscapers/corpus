# Censuses and surveys

*Instrument · Data · `data.statistics--censuses-and-surveys`*

**Norm**: SHaSA 2 SO 1.1; Charter Art. 3 (AU) — Coordination around census rounds; no frequency mandate

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no census has been conducted in the current or previous round and no household survey programme runs. |
| 2 Nascent | A census planned, mapped or funded for the current round, or a survey programme announced, with no enumeration. |
| 3 Established | A census enumerated in the current round with preliminary results only, or a survey programme with irregular rounds. |
| 4 Operating | Definitive census results published for the current round with disaggregation and public access (portal, microdata), and a regular survey programme on record. |
| 5 Leading | The SHaSA and Charter end state: censuses in every round and surveys on a published calendar, produced under the Charter's principles and disseminated openly, with the round's requirements met. |
