# Use of AI in government administration

*System · Technology · `tech.ai--use-of-ai-in-government-administration`*

**Norm**: Continental AI Strategy (AU) — "Promoting the adoption of AI in the public sector": use cases, capacity, procurement

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no AI system is in use in public administration, or that public data is unfit for advanced analytics. |
| 2 Nascent | An AI platform for administration authorised, tendered or piloted in one body. |
| 3 Established | One or two AI systems in service in administration (a chatbot for a service, document processing in one ministry) on record. |
| 4 Operating | AI in service across several bodies with a published inventory or use-case register, procurement rules for AI, and impact or performance reported. |
| 5 Leading | The AI Strategy's end state: AI adopted in the public sector under a governance framework, innovation-friendly procurement, capacity in place, and use cases shared and reported. |
