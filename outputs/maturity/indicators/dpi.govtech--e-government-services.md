# e-government services

*System · DPI · `dpi.govtech--e-government-services`*

**Norm**: DTS; Public Service Charter Art. 8 (AU) — "single digital gateway"; G2B, G2G, G2C services; modern technologies in service delivery

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no government service is available online, or that no inventory of online services exists and none is on record. |
| 2 Nascent | A portal launched with information only, or a few transactional services piloted, or one-stop service desks under operationalisation. |
| 3 Established | Transactional services in service — tens of services dematerialised — on several portals with no single gateway or shared authentication. |
| 4 Operating | A single gateway or integrated portal in service carrying the main services with shared authentication through the national ID, usage published, and an inventory of services maintained. |
| 5 Leading | The DTS end state: a single digital gateway integrating all e-government services (G2C, G2B, G2G) on the exchange layer and the national ID, with service levels and usage reported. |
