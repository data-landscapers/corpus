# National cybersecurity readiness

*Instrument · ICT Infrastructure · `infra.cybersec--national-cybersecurity-readiness`*

**Norm**: Malabo ch. III; DTS (AU) — National policy, strategy, laws, institutions; DTS complete set of legislation

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no national cybersecurity policy, strategy, law or institution exists. |
| 2 Nascent | A strategy, council or cyber centre created by decree or announced, none with an operating record; or a law in drafting. |
| 3 Established | A strategy adopted and a law in force, with the institutions (agency, CERT) constituted but no incident-response, enforcement or reporting record. |
| 4 Operating | Strategy, law and institutions operating: a national CERT responding to incidents on record, enforcement or prosecutions, a critical-infrastructure regime, and reporting published. |
| 5 Leading | Malabo chapter III met — policy, strategy, law, institutional mechanism, Malabo ratified — with regional cooperation on record and readiness reported to a recognised assessment. |
