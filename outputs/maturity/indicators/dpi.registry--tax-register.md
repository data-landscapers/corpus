# Tax register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--tax-register`*

**Norm**: TADAT Field Guide (2019) POA1 P1-1 (global) — A–D: unique high-integrity TIN, centralised database, whole-of-taxpayer view

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no taxpayer register exists or that it is paper-based. |
| 2 Nascent | TADAT D: a computerised register with multiple identifiers, decentralised, no linkage, or integrity not assessed. |
| 3 Established | TADAT C: decentralised databases linked by a common identifier. |
| 4 Operating | TADAT B: a centralised database with more than one identifier scheme linked, or a whole-of-taxpayer view not yet complete. |
| 5 Leading | TADAT A: each taxpayer with a unique high-integrity identifier, one centralised database, a whole-of-taxpayer view, and the identifier shared with other registers. |
