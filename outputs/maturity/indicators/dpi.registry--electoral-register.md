# Electoral register

*System · DPI · `dpi.registry--electoral-register`*

**Norm**: ACDEG Art. 17; AU EOM Guidelines (2002) (AU) — Independent electoral bodies; registration without discrimination; public access to registers

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated statement that no electoral register is maintained between elections, or that the register is compiled by hand for each poll. |
| 2 Nascent | A digital or biometric register procured or piloted; a register compiled digitally but not maintained. |
| 3 Established | A digital register in service, with public access to the roll provided (Guidelines §5.2.9) and periodic revision. |
| 4 Operating | The register maintained continuously and drawn from or reconciled with the civil or population register, with published audits and non-discriminatory registration on record (§4.6.10). |
| 5 Leading | The ACDEG end state: an independent electoral body maintaining a register recognised as accurate by observation missions, continuously updated from the base registers. |
