# Lesotho

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Department of National Identity and Civil Registry, in the Ministry of Home Affairs, [is the lead agency for civil registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>), it said in July 2026. Its presentation lists [a costed CRVS Strategic Plan 2026-2030 as still to be developed during 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). The Government's 30 birth registration stations had [support from the Government of Japan and UNICEF](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>), UNICEF Lesotho said in August 2026.

**Events registered digitally.** Birth registration stations in health facilities [are supported by an SMS-based platform for registering births](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>), UNICEF Lesotho said in August 2026. The Ntlafatsa Bana project's digital birth registration system [registers births on tablets](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>), UNICEF reported in June 2025.

**How the record is made.** UNICEF reported in June 2025 that [real-time tablet-based registration, operated by village health workers and civil registration officers, has replaced manual processes](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>). A World Bank note of May 2022 says [all 15 enrolment centres that allow for birth registration are linked to a central server](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content>).

**Last twelve months.** UNICEF Lesotho reported on 5 August 2026 that [30 birth registration stations had been established in health facilities in Maseru, Berea and Leribe](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>). In July 2026 the Department listed [nationwide scale-up of digital birth registration as an action for 2026-2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** UNICEF Lesotho counts [30 birth registration stations in health facilities across three districts](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-lesotho_foreverychild-digitaltransformation-activity-7490765967325597697-36h5>), in August 2026. The World Bank note of May 2022 counts [15 enrolment centres, across Maseru and each of the 10 districts](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: the [national identity management system](<https://www.gov.ls/development/ministry-of-home-affairs-advances-digital-transformation-of-national-identity-system/>) of the Ministry of Home Affairs, a [digital service identification system](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/lesotho-first-introduced-new-digital-service-identification-system/>) in pilot, an [integrated national digital ID ecosystem on MOSIP](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/lesotho-unicef-partner-to-develop-mosip-based-integrated-national-id>) and, for refugees, the [Population Registration and Identity Management Eco System](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/unhcr-donates-equipment-to-home-affairs/>). Exchange: the Department's [integrated system, which issues a unique personal identification number upon birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>), the [integration of birth registration with the social assistance database](<https://lesotho.un.org/en/296588-every-child-counts-linking-nutrition-water-and-identity-better-future>), an [identity verification API](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content>), [civil registry integrations with banks and mobile money providers](<https://pub.sbc4d.com/2022/lesotho_full_final.pdf>), the [transfer of civil registration records to the Bureau of Statistics](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/lesotho-2022-vital-statistics-report.pdf>), a [national all-cause mortality surveillance and CRVS integration system](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/WHO%20LESOTHO%202025%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20.pdf>) in design and a planned [whole-of-government data sharing platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012624174578070/pdf/BOSIB1128fca200361b2de12ad6f80d422e.pdf>). Outreach: about fifteen [mobile registration units](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/9ba0954b-f540-5038-9311-d1bd69f69cf6/content>) and the Department's [outreach at Libobeng, Qacha's Nek](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/sekete-champions-civil-registration-access-in-hard-to-reach-qachas-nek/>).

***Not held***. No total of local registration offices is published, so neither count is a share, and the two counts are of different systems. Where the SMS platform's record is held, and whether deaths are entered at any local office, is not known.
