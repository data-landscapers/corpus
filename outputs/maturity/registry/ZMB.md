# Zambia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Minister of Home Affairs and Internal Security describes [the Integrated National Registration Information System, INRIS, as the ministry's national and civil registration management system, run through the Department of National Registration, Passport and Citizenship](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/INRIS%20IMPLEMENTATION%20-%20Mr%20Mwiimbu.pdf>), in a 2026 statement to the National Assembly. The Eighth National Development Plan 2022-2026 [commits the Government to operationalise it](<https://www.zda.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/8th-NDP-2022-2026.pdf>). The Minister [puts the cost of the exercise at K1,069,366,200](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/INRIS%20IMPLEMENTATION%20-%20Mr%20Mwiimbu.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The Minister states that [enrolment has commenced in the INRIS districts, including registration of births and issuance of birth certificates using the system](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/INRIS%20IMPLEMENTATION%20-%20Mr%20Mwiimbu.pdf>). The ministry said in December 2025 that [about 1.3 million citizens have been enrolled on INRIS and over 500,000 births and deaths registered](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).

**How the record is made.** A preprint of September 2025 states that [the department runs a paper-based system for birth and death registration, and that completed death notification forms are carried weekly from local offices to provincial or national headquarters for entry into INRIS](<https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.09.25335397v1>). The Lavushimanda district officer said in September 2026 that [the district office is implementing digital civil registration through INRIS](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).

**Last twelve months.** The Lavushimanda district office [registered over 3,000 children under five in the four months to September 2026](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Minister's 2026 statement has [INRIS deployed in eleven districts](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/images/publication_docs/INRIS%20IMPLEMENTATION%20-%20Mr%20Mwiimbu.pdf>). A CDC Foundation case study of 2025 says [the expansion of INRIS has brought access to civil registration services to 81 of 116 districts](<https://data4healthlibrary.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/CDCF%20D4H%20Zambia%20Case%20Study%20EMR%202025.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: the [digitisation of legacy civil registration records](<https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration>). Notification: the [electronic Form 13 death notification pilot](<https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.09.09.25335397v1>), the [e-death notification system](<https://data4healthlibrary.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/CDCF%20D4H%20Zambia%20Case%20Study%20EMR%202025.pdf>), the draft [e-MCCD system within SmartCare](<https://data4healthlibrary.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/CDCF%20D4H%20Zambia%20Case%20Study%20EMR%202025.pdf>), the [birth and death notification desks in health facilities](<https://zambia.un.org/en/208722-launch-national-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-policy>) and the [INRIS API Manager](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>). Certificate: the government e-services portal, [ZamPortal](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/a-more-connected-africa--zambia-and-ethiopia-join-forces-on-digi>). Outreach: the [2025 registration campaign for birth certificates and National Registration Cards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/zambia-says-using-multiple-strategies-to-meet-50-percent-birth-registration-goal-by-2026>) and the [refugee enrolment campaigns with mobile registration camps](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090924120535793/txt/BOSIB12c22668f0f51a0d117949b7b8fe34.txt>). Identity: the [National Registration Card](<https://www.crvslaws.org/zambia/birth-registration/id-related-services>), the [biometric National Registration Cards](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>), the [electronic National Registration Cards](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Government-Digital-Services-and-Digital-ID-in-Kenya-and-Zambia-Summary.pdf>), the planned [MOSIP-based digital ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>), the [computerised issuance systems for national registration cards, passports and citizenship documentation](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/govt-embarks-on-digitalisation-of-identity-services/>) and [UNHCR ProGres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090924120535793/txt/BOSIB12c22668f0f51a0d117949b7b8fe34.txt>). Enabling: the [stationary biometric kits](<https://zambia.un.org/en/208722-launch-national-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-policy>) and the planned [IT upgrading at ministry offices in three refugee settlements](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090924120535793/txt/BOSIB12c22668f0f51a0d117949b7b8fe34.txt>). Exchange: [ZamConnect](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349>), the [INRIS-SmartCare integration](<https://data4healthlibrary.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/CDCF%20D4H%20Zambia%20Case%20Study%20EMR%202025.pdf>) and the [link from birth registration records to the national registration card number](<https://www.zambialaws.com/Zambia2019Pdfsi/SI%2034%20of%202019.pdf>).

***Not held***. No count of local offices that enter events in INRIS themselves is held, and the accounts differ on whether entry is made at the office or at provincial or national headquarters. No source of funds is stated for the current exercise, and nothing dated in the last twelve months shows a national advance or retreat.
