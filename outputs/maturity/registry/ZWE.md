# Zimbabwe

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Registrar General's department [presents the computerisation of its provinces, districts and sub-offices as its own decentralisation programme](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/>), in July 2026. UNICEF says Zimbabwe [developed a strategic action plan for civil registration and vital statistics for 2023-2027](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/press-releases/birth-registration-steadily-increases-worldwide-150-million-children-still-invisible>). The Appropriation (2026) Act [appropriated ZiG 1,470,460,000 to the Civil Registration programme](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Appropriation%20Act%202026%2C%20Act%20No.%206%20of%202025.pdf>), and the ten sub-offices announced in August 2026 are [funded by China and delivered by UNICEF](<https://humanitarianpost.co.zw/unicef-boosts-zims-civil-registry-efficiency-in-eight-districts/>).

**Events registered digitally.** UNICEF Zimbabwe stated in July 2025 that [at the computerised sub-office at Mpilo Hospital mothers register newborns and obtain a birth certificate before discharge](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/stories/zimbabwe-expands-civil-registration-services-hard-reach-areas>).

**How the record is made.** UNICEF Zimbabwe stated in July 2025 that [the computerised registry offices and sub-offices allow real-time data entry at the office](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/stories/zimbabwe-expands-civil-registration-services-hard-reach-areas>). UNICEF's chief of child protection said on 7 August 2026 that [some decentralised sub-offices are not computerised](<https://newziana.co.zw/unicef-government-move-to-digitise-birth-registration-services/>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2025 the Minister of Home Affairs and Cultural Heritage [announced plans to computerise sub-offices across the country](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/civil-registry-department-to-computerise-sub-offices/>). On 7 August 2026 UNICEF and the government [announced a China-funded project to computerise, digitalise and solar-power 10 sub-offices in eight districts](<https://newziana.co.zw/unicef-government-move-to-digitise-birth-registration-services/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Deputy Registrar General said on 22 July 2026 that [all 10 provinces and 63 districts have been computerised and more than 20 of 209 sub-offices](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/>). UNICEF Zimbabwe named, in July 2025, [digital registration as operational at Mpilo Hospital, Kariangwe in Binga, Bubi, Manama Mission Hospital, Mahusekwa, Murambinda and Epworth](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/stories/zimbabwe-expands-civil-registration-services-hard-reach-areas>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification: the [BD1 forms issued by traditional leaders](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/stories/zimbabwe-expands-civil-registration-services-hard-reach-areas>) and the [birth and death notifications from health facilities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>). Certificate: the planned [online application for birth certificates and passports](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/local/article/200055327/minister-denies-id-backlog-amid-growing-public-outcry>). Outreach: the [national mobile registration exercise of 2022](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/civil-registry-issues-over-3-million-identity-documents-for-free-ahead-of-july-elections/>), the [mobile registration exercises in Binga district](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200043868/bringing-identity-home-mobile-registration-changes-lives-in-binga>), the [targeted mobile registration of the 2026 programme](<https://humanitarianpost.co.zw/unicef-boosts-zims-civil-registry-efficiency-in-eight-districts/>) and its [outreach services for legal identity documents](<https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202608/21/WS6a87bd6ea3106bc57421cc05.html>). Identity: the [Zimbabwe Population Registry System](<https://infomin.qurious.consulting/uploads/documents/68df7ba0624cd.pdf>), the [National Population Register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>), the [Registrar-General's national population database](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>), the [e-passport system](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/african-registrars-look-to-replicate-zimbabwes-e-passport-success/>) and the planned [national digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/zimbabwe-looks-forward-to-digital-id-rollout-after-population-registry-reform>). Enabling: the [National Data Centre](<https://thisisafrica.me/politics-and-society/the-passport-the-database-and-the-state-who-controls-digital-zimbabwe/>) and the [Mutare Provincial Registry Complex](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/classy-e-passport-office-opens-in-mutare/>). Exchange: the [CRVS-Health interoperability platform](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>), the [Civil Registry integration with mobile money providers](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/rbz-closes-loopholes-on-mobile-money-illicit-financial-flows-fraud/>), the [integration of the Tax and Revenue Management System with the Civil Registry](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4062%3Apublic-notice-69-of-2024-tarms-release-3>), the [Civil Registry integration with the High Performance Computing centre](<https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/>) and the proposed [social registry integration with civil registry systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926024025866/txt/P514401-e5a07636-f85a-4936-8383-b57dbd46cf7c.txt>).

***Not held***. No count of offices registering births digitally is held: the Registrar General's count is of offices computerised, and what a computerised office does is not stated. Where the digital record is held is not known, and no digital entry of deaths is held.
