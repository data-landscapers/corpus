# Senegal

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [Agence nationale de l'état civil (ANEC), an agency attached to the ministry of Urbanisme, Collectivités territoriales et Aménagement des territoires](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-les-maires-invites-a-mettre-fin-a-lutilisation-des-logiciels-clandestins/>), deploys the software under a [Plan national de remédiation begun in May 2026](<https://aps.sn/la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-une-veritable-politique-publique-et-strategique-selon-le-directeur-general-de-lanec/>). The [European Union financed the NEKKAL programme, which held its final steering committee on 29 June 2026](<https://aps.sn/le-programme-nekkal-a-permis-dimportantes-avancees-dans-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-selon-un-responsable/>), and [ANEC's budget for 2026 had not been notified by 17 August 2026](<https://aps.sn/kaolack-lanec-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil/>).

**Events registered digitally.** On ANEC's data the system held [14,839,577 births and 487,259 deaths at 17 July 2026](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/etat-civil-lanec-franchit-le-cap-des-70-de-deploiement-du-lgec/>). ANEC's director general said on 16 September 2026 that [health services can register births in it directly from maternities](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>).

**How the record is made.** ANEC's data describe [437 centres as connected to one system, which it calls the Logiciel de gestion de l'état civil (LGEC), with 5,610 new acts registered in it on 17 July 2026](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/etat-civil-lanec-franchit-le-cap-des-70-de-deploiement-du-lgec/>).

**Last twelve months.** ANEC's director general said in an interview published on 5 August 2026 that the [LGEC had passed its pilot phase](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Dossier/enquete-nes-mais-invisibles-33-un-enfant-sur-cinq-nest-toujours-pas-enregistre-a-letat-civil-au-senegal-les-eclairages-du-dg-de-lanec_n_500404.html>). By 16 September 2026 [deployment was effective in the Dakar, Thiès, Louga, Diourbel, Fatick and Kaolack regions, with Tambacounda and the southern regions to follow in 2026](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** ANEC's director general said on 16 September 2026 that the [modernisation process stood at 77% of the country's 629 civil-status centres](<https://aps.sn/etat-civil-77-des-centres-couverts-par-le-processus-de-modernisation-dg-anec/>). ANEC's data at 17 July 2026 put the [LGEC in 437 of the 629 centres, 70%, from 98% in the Thiès region to 42% in Kédougou](<https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/etat-civil-lanec-franchit-le-cap-des-70-de-deploiement-du-lgec/>).
