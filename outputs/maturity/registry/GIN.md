# Guinea

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The 2023 civil status law [makes the ministry in charge of civil status responsible for buying, installing and maintaining the equipment of civil status centres](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/118843/GIN-118843.pdf>). In September 2026 [an unnamed company in charge of the secure paper was reinstalling the data centre](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). An evaluation for Enabel found in April 2026 that [national technicians had no access to the source code of the register system it names E-RENEC](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>), and that the project closed in February 2026 with [no maintenance or operating budget secured](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>).

**Events registered digitally.** Ratoma commune's civil status officer said on 2 September 2026 that [commune offices establish digital birth certificates on secure paper](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**How the record is made.** The officer said [the system depends on a data centre, whose relocation disturbed it while registrations continued](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). In March 2026 [Conakry's 13 communes received paper registers for the year's registrations](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/03/conakry-le-pn-ravec-dote-les-13-communes-dun-important-lot-dintrants-detat-civil-dont-les-certificats-de-non-remariage/>), and in February 2026 [registrars still used paper in parallel](<https://open.enabel.be/en/evaluation/39/download/162>).

**Last twelve months.** By 2 September 2026 [printing of digital birth certificates had been halted for about two weeks by a stockout of secure paper](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** Ratoma's officer said [the secure paper serves to establish digital birth certificates in all the communes of Guinea](<https://mosaiqueguinee.com/2026/09/etat-civil-la-guinee-confrontee-a-une-penurie-dactes-de-naissance-numeriques/>). Enabel, in a text published in August 2023, described [a digital national civil registry piloted in ten communes](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/en/the-modernisation-of-guineas-civil-registry-inspired-by-belgiums-experience/>).
