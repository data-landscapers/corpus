# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Direcção Geral dos Registos e do Notariado, [an autonomous body within the justice ministry, is responsible for civil registration and developed its system with its own technicians, while the University of Aveiro in Portugal maintains and hosts it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>), the World Bank said in 2023. The same report found [no strategic plan specifically for civil registration and identification](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The chief conservator of the civil registry said in March 2026 that [births are registered by entering the data directly into the directorate's database](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). The World Bank said in 2023 that the system the directorate calls SIGA, [implemented by 2018, registers births, deaths and marriages](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** At the maternities [the directorate's technicians and trained nurse-midwives make the registration directly in the system, on computers and printers there](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>), the chief conservator said in March 2026. In the directorate's centres [registration is done digitally, connected to servers hosted in Portugal](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>), the World Bank said in 2023.

**Last twelve months.** In March 2026 the chief conservator said the directorate [is eliminating paper by registering births directly from the maternities](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The chief conservator said in March 2026 that [registration is done in all the country's maternities](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). The World Bank in 2023 counted [11 centres for all civil registration services and seven birth registration posts in maternities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: [birth records from 1920 to 2010 digitised into the central database, with births of 2010 to 2023 and other life events still to be digitised](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>). Certificate: [a document-issuing system at the embassy in Portugal, from April 2025](<https://www.jornaltransparencia.st/host134.htm>), and [QR code verification of certificates by authorised third parties](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/779811655323104127/pdf/Sao-Tome-and-Principe-Digital-Sao-Tome-and-Principe-Project.pdf>). Outreach: [awareness campaigns on birth registration for mothers who give birth at home](<https://rstp.st/2026/03/10/direcao-geral-dos-registos-e-notariados-com-mais-de-3-mil-registos-de-nascimento-por-ano/>). Enabling: [equipment for the civil registry systems, handed over by the PReSE project in September 2025](<https://rstp.st/2025/09/04/apresentada-plataforma-de-recenseamento-automatico-de-sao-tome-e-principe-que-esta-98-pronta/>), and [a satellite link and portable generator with the identity-card brigade](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/01/22/51507/campanha-de-atribuicao-gratuita-do-bilhete-de-identidade-iniciou-se-no-ilheu-das-rolas-e-decorre-em-porto-alegre/>). Identity: [the identity card system and its central database, and the personal identification number issued at birth](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>); [a free identity card campaign, ended 15 March 2026](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe>); [an identity card campaign for the diaspora in Portugal](<https://magazinesantomensidade.com/bilhetes-de-identidade-ja-disponiveis-para-levantamento-no-ambito-da-campanha-bi-para-todos-os-sao-tomenses/>); [a MOSIP-based digital ID pilot, begun in 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/sao-tome-and-principe-pilots-mosip-based-national-digital-id-system>); [a new digital ID system in design at September 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185019928/pdf/P177158-b0ee36fd-86ba-4e5c-96a3-d291d6d35813.pdf>); [the passport issuance system SEP 2.0](<https://rstp.st/2026/04/15/stp-lanca-novo-sistema-de-emissao-de-passaportes-2-0-para-reforco-da-seguranca-e-eficiencia/>); [passport issuance without attendance for the diaspora](<https://mne.gov.st/noticias/atualidades/governo-disponibiliza-emissao-passaporte-por-nao-presencial-para-cidadaos-na-diaspora>); and [the electronic passport, with a proposed digital identity and authentication platform](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>). Exchange: [Leginon, which gives other institutions controlled access to the register's data and links the civil registration and identification databases](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032824212523838/pdf/P171592-effa7cbb-e47a-4467-bd3c-4bf28f84f93f.pdf>), and [a proposed interoperability platform](<https://inic.gov.st/docs/DRn60_2021_Resolu35-2020CM_Aprova%20Estrategia%20de%20GovernaDigitaleCGD.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share or count of the local offices entering records is published, and nothing says where deaths are entered. No finance fact from the last three years is held, and no current plan for civil registration.
