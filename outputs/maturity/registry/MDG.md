# Madagascar

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** A decree of 2 December 2020 [set up the Centre National d'État Civil et de l'Identité under the technical supervision of the Interior ministry](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/114753/MDG-114753.pdf>) to keep the national digital civil status and identity registers. The digital registration system was rolled out under the PRODIGY project, [financed by an IDA credit of US$133 million and a GFF grant of US$3 million and closing on 30 June 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>).

**Events registered digitally.** By December 2025 [61,892 birth certificates carrying a unique identity number had been issued](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>) through the digital civil registration system, which Madagascar calls SIECM.

**How the record is made.** [Communes themselves use SIECM to register civil-status events and issue birth certificates with a national unique number](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>), the World Bank reported in December 2025; the system's offline module had been in demonstration since November 2025.

**Last twelve months.** Communes using SIECM [rose from 75 to 336 in the six months to December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>). In March 2026 the Unité de Gouvernance Digitale described the birth registration platform as [being deployed progressively at national scale](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** In December 2025 [336 communes were using SIECM, which the World Bank gives as 40% of the project's target](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925094025414/txt/P169413-92234883-0626-410f-a2c4-c2b2e93265f7.txt>); a further 112 communes had been trained and were not yet operational.

Notification: the [digitalisation of notifications from health centres and health workers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/876981602696410522/pdf/Official-Documents-Financing-Agreement-for-Credit-6780-MG.pdf>) and the [declaration of births and deaths by basic health centres and matrones](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/16MxUCzF1IiuoMQA08LGbruTioQPE16n7/view>). Identity: the [Izaho Tokana biometric enrolment](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>), the [identity management system IDMS and the biometric national identity card](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Webinaire-PRODIGY-CNI-Biometrique-Malagasy-11062025.pdf>), [e-Fokontany](<https://ccomptes.mg/uploads/AUDIT-DU-MNDPT1774881345.pdf>) and the [registre national numérique de la population](<https://www.lexpress.mg/2026/07/recensement-lidentite-biometrique.html>). Enabling: [Smart Village](<https://2424.mg/inclusion-numerique-le-programme-smart-village-pour-soutenir-linclusion-numerique-et-desenclaver-les-zones-les-plus-reculees/>), [DECIM](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121625122027284/pdf/P178701-e5cb57fc-5618-472d-b195-56cc26358652.pdf>) and the [IT kits planned for commune civil status offices](<https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z9JmMN-hOeQUeA0tvtD-4ykLeHY_Z_Sb/view>). Exchange: the [government interoperability platform](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/nasikiliza/legal-identity-all-what-can-madagascar-learn-namibia>) and the [interoperability between the Justice Department, the Civil Registry and other public services](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/30/national-biometric-enrolment-begins-paving-way-for-digital-inclusion-in-madagascar/>).

***Not held***. The share of all communes using the system is not held, nor where the record is kept. No digital death registration is reported, and nothing held says what has funded the system since the project's financing closed on 30 June 2026.
