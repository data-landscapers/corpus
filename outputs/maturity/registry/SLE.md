# Sierra Leone

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [National Civil Registration Authority (NCRA), the state agency formed under the 2016 Act, presented the integrated eCRVS and ID management system as its own in May 2024](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf>). Its Director General said in August 2023 that the [system was developed with European Union support through UNOPS](<https://ncra.gov.sl/sierra-leone-through-ncra-joins-other-africa-countries-to-commemorate-the-6th-africa-crvs-day/>).

**Events registered digitally.** NCRA told the ID4Africa meeting of 22 May 2025 that [over 80% of its integrated civil registration and identity services are digitised, birth and death registration among the services listed](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>). UNICEF's representative said in August 2023 that its support covered the [piloting and scale-up of a mobile application for the notification and registration of births](<https://ncra.gov.sl/sierra-leone-through-ncra-joins-other-africa-countries-to-commemorate-the-6th-africa-crvs-day/>).

**How the record is made.** UNICEF reported in March 2023 that at [NCRA's registration centres in hospitals the child's details are entered with the application when the mother registers the birth, then transferred through the central server, which generates the identification number, to the Central Registry System](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>).

**Last twelve months.** NCRA's presentation of 22 May 2025 says the [2016 Act mandates the Authority to deliver the integrated civil registration and identity services](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Calabash reported in September 2024 that an NCRA project had [distributed 48 laptops and internet modems to 48 selected health facilities across the 16 districts](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/45757>). UNICEF reported in March 2023 [more than 200 babies registered a month with the application at Ola During Hospital in Freetown](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>).
