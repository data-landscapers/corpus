# Sierra Leone

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [National Civil Registration Authority (NCRA) is the state agency formed under the 2016 Act](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf>). [In May 2024 it presented the integrated eCRVS and ID management system as its own](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-3-Sierra-Leone-Final.pdf>). In August 2023 its Director General said the [system was developed with European Union support through UNOPS](<https://ncra.gov.sl/sierra-leone-through-ncra-joins-other-africa-countries-to-commemorate-the-6th-africa-crvs-day/>).

**Events registered digitally.** NCRA told the ID4Africa meeting of 22 May 2025 that [over 80% of its integrated civil registration and identity services are digitised](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>). [Birth and death registration are among them](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-4-SIERRALEONE_FINAL.pdf>). In August 2023 UNICEF's representative said its support covered the [piloting and scale-up of a mobile application for the notification and registration of births](<https://ncra.gov.sl/sierra-leone-through-ncra-joins-other-africa-countries-to-commemorate-the-6th-africa-crvs-day/>).

**How the record is made.** UNICEF reported in March 2023 that at [NCRA's registration centres in hospitals the child's details are entered with the application when the mother registers the birth](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>). [They then pass through the central server to the Central Registry System](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>). [That server generates the identification number](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>).

**Last twelve months.** In October 2026 UNICEF said [digital birth registration is now an integral part of Sierra Leone's civil registration system](<https://www.unicef.org/digitalimpact/stories/putting-children-heart-sierra-leones-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Calabash reported on an NCRA project in September 2024. The project had [distributed 48 laptops and internet modems to 48 selected health facilities across the 16 districts](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/45757>). UNICEF showed [NCRA staff registering a birth on a portable digital device in Bombali District](<https://www.unicef.org/digitalimpact/stories/putting-children-heart-sierra-leones-digital-public-infrastructure>) in October 2026. UNICEF reported in March 2023 [more than 200 babies registered a month with the application at Ola During Hospital in Freetown](<https://www.unicef.org/sierraleone/stories/making-birth-registration-more-accessible-every-child-sierra-leone>).
