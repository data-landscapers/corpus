# Democratic Republic of the Congo

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [Ministry of Interior and Security is responsible for civil registration through its Civil Registration Division](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf>), as of March 2019. The one pilot is [executed by four United Nations entities](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>) and [financed by the UN's Joint SDG Fund](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>), UNFPA reported on 18 August 2025. A [national civil-registration strategy and a master plan for digitising the centres were still to be written](<https://www.7sur7.cd/2026/10/01/rdc-lidentification-de-la-population-au-coeur-des-echanges-entre-lonip-lugptn-et-ses>) on 1 October 2026.

**Events registered digitally.** UNFPA stated on 18 August 2025 that the Masina pilot [aims to automate the collection and management of data on births, marriages and deaths](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>).

**How the record is made.** Government terms of reference posted on 3 April 2026 state that [the civil registers are mostly manual and are not integrated into any central file](<https://www.mediacongo.net/dpics/filesmanager/jobaof/2026/04-avril/03/ug_260413_01_05.pdf>). In March 2019 the Civil Registration Division reported that [each act is written on paper with four carbon copies](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A [UNDP tender closing on 25 April 2026 sought a consultant to assess the digitalisation of civil registration and support the design of digital registration tools](<https://allbusiness.africa/tenders/undp-cod-ic-crvs-2604-05-strategic-innovation-2026>). On 23 July 2026 [a sector office in Walikale, North Kivu, had been out of birth registers for several months](<https://actualite.cd/2026/07/25/walikale-labsence-prolongee-de-registres-de-naissance-prive-les-enfants-de-leur-identite>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** One commune, Masina in Kinshasa, [has a pilot at its municipal civil-status office](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>), UNFPA reported on 18 August 2025. In March 2019 the Civil Registration Division [counted 979 local offices, 102 urban and 877 rural, none using a computer or mobile device](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_CongoDRSnapshot_e.pdf>).
