# Rwanda

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Identification Agency, under the Ministry of ICT, [supervises and coordinates the civil registration system nationally](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>), on the statistics institute's account for 2025. The statistics strategy for 2024/25 to 2028/29 [names strengthening the system a top priority](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>). The finance law of 30 June 2026 [appropriates RWF 116,184,997 to civil registration for 2026/27, domestically financed](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/REPORTS/National_Budget/Annual_State_Finance_Laws/2026_to_2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law/2026-2027_Original_Annual_Finance_Law.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The system the country calls NCI-CRVS registered [356,838 births and 39,355 deaths that occurred in 2025](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>), among the nine events registered through it.

**How the record is made.** The statistics institute states that in 2025 the system [captures events in real time at the site where official registration takes place](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>), as a single point of entry. The Agency states that [all civil registers have been available only in electronic format since 17 February 2023](<https://www.nida.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=92643&t=f&token=7ea9d6c4602ce4d2ae43fc6b669787b89edad1c1>).

**Last twelve months.** Birth registration completeness [rose from 90.3% in 2024 to 92.9% in 2025, and death registration completeness from 46.1% to 50.5%](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>). The share of deaths registered within the legal 30 days [fell from 96.3% to 89.9%](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>) over the same year.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The statistics institute states that in 2025 the system is [fully operational at all five categories of registration office](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2026-04/Rwanda%20Civil%20Registration%20Vital%20Statistics%202025_0.pdf>): health facilities, cells, sectors, districts and embassies. The Agency [counted 3,220 civil registration service points](<https://www.nida.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=92643&t=f&token=7ea9d6c4602ce4d2ae43fc6b669787b89edad1c1>) in its procedures of December 2023.
