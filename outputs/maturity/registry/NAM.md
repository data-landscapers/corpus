# Namibia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** [The Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security registers births and deaths on the National Population Registration System](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>), the statistics agency said in April 2024. [The system was developed in the country with its own resources](<https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf>), by a UNICEF and UN case study of September 2023. [Cabinet approved a civil registration plan for 2015/16 to 2020/21 in 2015](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** [The system's death module was deployed in 2012 and its birth module in 2013](<https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf>). The statistics agency counts [85,853 births and 24,388 deaths registered and captured on it in 2021](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** [The local civil registration office captures the application on the system and issues the certificate, and the record is available in real time across the 14 regions](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>), the agency said in April 2024. [Of births registered in 2021, 68.9 per cent were captured within 14 days, and 89.6 per cent of deaths](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [In December 2025 the ministry said it had deployed its staff to 24 state hospitals to issue birth certificates right after delivery](<https://www.namibian.com.na/home-affairs-deploys-staff-to-24-hospitals-for-instant-birth-registration/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** [Some registration offices in remote areas lack computers or internet connectivity, which delays capture](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>), the statistics agency said in April 2024. [Birth and death registration took place at local registration centres and 24 health facilities and hospitals](<https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf>), by the case study of September 2023.

Notification: the [e-birth and e-death notification systems](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>) and a [mobile identity prototype for submitting a birth from a phone](<https://www.nbcnews.na/node/113073>). Certificate: a [portal for verifying birth, marriage and death certificates](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/184451466711154296>) and [verification systems provided for in the 2024 Bill](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Explanatory_Memorandum_Civil_Registration_and_Identification_Bill.pdf>). Outreach: [mobile registration campaigns](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), [identity registration at Khomas schools](<https://neweralive.na/ministry-takes-id-registration-to-khomas-schools/>), [integrated outreach registration for San communities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>), the [mass registration campaign of 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/namibia-to-step-up-civil-registration-efforts-with-9m-budget>) and [planned mobile verification units](<https://www.namibiansun.com/government/namibia-to-launch-groundbreaking-electronic-id-by-20262025-06-10159527>). Identity: the [electronic identity card, which the ministry calls the e-ID](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), the [national identity card and its fingerprint system](<https://unstats.un.org/legal-identity-agenda/documents/Paper/2023/CRVSID-Namibia.pdf>), [remote biometric enrolment on hand-held devices](<https://www.undp.org/namibia/blog/season-3-namibias-digital-identity-journey-vision-reality>), the [African Guest Identity Card](<https://idtechwire.com/namibia-prepares-to-roll-out-national-digital-id-system>), the [passport system](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/CRVS_Namibia_e_WEB.pdf>) and the [card system IMAGO with its track-and-trace barcodes](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/184451466711154296>). Enabling: the [National Public Key Infrastructure](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/namibia-advances-national-digital-trust-framework/lwrKx73Y1pAqmg1o>), [regional and sub-regional civic affairs offices built or renovated](<https://www.parliament.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Vote-39-Home-Affairs.pdf>) and the [government wide area network](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/184451466711154296>). Exchange: the [data-exchange platform NamX](<https://www.undp.org/namibia/blog/season-3-namibias-digital-identity-journey-vision-reality>), the [National Data Exchange Service Bus](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/CRVS_Namibia_e_WEB.pdf>), the [register's integration with social protection](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-namibia.pdf>), [identity verification for social grants](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), a [planned link from the grants system for reporting deaths](<https://www.namibian.com.na/system-says-no-to-hungry-pensioners/>), the [transfer of registration data to the statistics agency](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2018-2021_Namibia-Vital-Statistics-Report_April-2024.pdf>) and the [transfer of death information to the Electoral Commission](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/184451466711154296>).

***Not held***. No count or share of local offices registering digitally is published, and the gap at remote offices has no figure. No civil registration plan later than 2020/21 is named.
