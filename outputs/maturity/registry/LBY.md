# Libya

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Civil Registration Authority, of the Ministry of Interior, [manages the collection of births and deaths data and issues the legal documents](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>), by the health ministry's vital statistics report published in 2022. In January 2025 the Ministry of Interior said the Authority was [continuing the Intilaqa project, under the supervision of the Attorney General's office](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=325035&lang=ar>), matching the electronic data in the civil status system against its paper originals.

**Events registered digitally.** The Authority's electronic system [recorded 231,670 births and 31,051 deaths in 2020](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>), says the Ministry of Health's Health Information Centre, which generated its report for that year from it.

**How the record is made.** The Health Information Centre's report, published in 2022, states that [all of the Authority's offices are fully automated and electronically linked to its centre, working through a single automated system on the same database](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 1 March 2026 the Attorney General's office reported that he had [discussed with the head of the Authority the latest completed phase of the project to create the electronic civil registry database](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351962&lang=ar>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** [All of the Authority's offices are automated and linked to the single database](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>), the Health Information Centre said in 2022; its report counts [12 branches, 133 issuing offices and at least 380 service offices](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: the [Intilaqa project](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/450311>), matching and scanning the paper registers. Identity: the [National Number project](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>), the General Information Authority's [national digital identity system, which it calls LY-DNID](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2025/11/20/the-general-information-authority-launches-the-trial-phases-of-the-second-level-of-the-national-digital-identity-system-ly-dnid/>), its [Digital Identity Management System for Citizens](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/19/general-information-authority-central-bank-of-libya-national-authority-for-combating-money-laundering-with-the-relevant-authorities/>) and its [national database](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-130-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-2018-%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a5%d8%b9%d8%a7%d8%af%d8%a9-%d8%aa%d9%88%d8%b2%d9%8a%d8%b9-%d8%a7%d8%ae%d8%aa%d8%b5/>), the [electronic identity card and electronic passport issuing system](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/515274>), the [passport issuing system](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=340448&lang=ar>) and a [biometric data system the Civil Status Department intended in 2019](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050006292273831/pdf/P1776930ba6a30000909306dca0ae8beec.pdf>). Exchange: the [national ID provided at birth since 2013](<https://seha.ly/media/statistics_reports/vital-statistics-report-for-the-year-2020mD5xx9G.pdf>), the General Information Authority's [data exchange, which it calls LY-NDEX](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/07/09/the-general-information-authority-participates-in-a-national-workshop-to-enhance-information-exchange-in-combating-money-laundering-and-terrorism-financing/>), and its [electronic linkage service to the national database](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/interoperability/>), the [supply of births and deaths data to the health ministry's information centre](<https://seha.ly/media/legislation/2021LpP7vhL.pdf>), the [data flow to the Bureau of Statistics and Census](<https://paris21.org/sites/default/files/2018-04/NSDS-Libya-En.pdf>), [manual data sharing between the Civil Registry and social assistance agencies](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099050006292273831/pdf/P1776930ba6a30000909306dca0ae8beec.pdf>) and the health ministry's [DHIS2](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789292742584-eng.pdf?ua=1>). Enabling: a [national data transmission network](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%85%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%B9-%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D9%84/>) in a draft law of 2022.

***Not held***. Whether a birth or death is entered at the office when it is registered is not stated, and the office figure is undated and gives no rural share. No plan or finance for the system is held.
