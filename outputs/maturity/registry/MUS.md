# Mauritius

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The CSD System is [the Civil Status Division's own, under the Prime Minister's Office and headed by the Registrar of Civil Status](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>). The Division [spent Rs 387,863,137 against government budget estimates of Rs 445,600,000 in 2023/2024](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>), with no donor named. Its [Strategic Directions 2023/2024 name the revamping of the CSD System](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The Division's report for 2023/2024 states that the CSD System, operational since November 2001, [provides for the registration of births, deaths and marriages](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>). Offices [opened on 6 and 7 September 2025 for the registration of births and deaths](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>) during the system's revamp.

**How the record is made.** The Registrar stated in June 2019 that [civil events have been registered online since November 2001 at computerised civil status offices](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf>), a national identity number being generated on each birth registered. The record is [stored in the Division's single Civil Status Database](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>), the Division stated in 2024.

**Last twelve months.** The Civil Status Division announced on 2 September 2025 that [its CSD System was being revamped](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>), with a transition period in which delays might be met.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Registrar listed [35 Civil Status Offices by district, two of them in Rodrigues](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf>), in June 2019, and described them as computerised.
