# Mauritius

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The CSD System is [the Civil Status Division's own, under the Prime Minister's Office and headed by the Registrar of Civil Status](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>). The Division [spent Rs 387,863,137 against government budget estimates of Rs 445,600,000 in 2023/2024](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>), with no donor named. Its [Strategic Directions 2023/2024 name the revamping of the CSD System](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The Division's 2023/2024 report says [the CSD System has run since November 2001](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>). It [provides for the registration of births, deaths and marriages](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>). Offices [opened on 6 and 7 September 2025 to register births and deaths](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>) during a revamp of the system.

**How the record is made.** The Registrar said in June 2019 that [civil events are registered online at computerised civil status offices](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf>). [A national identity number is generated on each birth registered](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf>). The Division stated in 2024 that [the record is stored in its single Civil Status Database](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Division announced on 2 September 2025 that [its CSD System was being revamped](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>). It warned of [delays during the transition](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Division's 2023/2024 report counts [36 Civil Status Offices](<https://csd.govmu.org/DocumentsList/FINALISED%20ANNUAL%20REPORT%20CSD.pdf>) and gives no share entering records. The 2025 notice [opened all offices but three, and Rodrigues](<https://csd.govmu.org/Documents/Communique/Communique%20-%20Revamping%20of%20CSD%20System%20New%20.pdf>), without a count. The Pay Research Bureau's 2016 review says the Division [operates its computerised system in all its Civil Status Offices](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>). The Registrar [listed 35 computerised Civil Status Offices by district](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF5/3-William-Ayelou-Mauritius.pdf>) in June 2019. Both are more than three years old.
