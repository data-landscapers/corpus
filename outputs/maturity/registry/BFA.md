# Burkina Faso

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Direction générale de la modernisation de l'état civil [trains commune agents on the platform it calls VENEEM](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>). The Stratégie nationale de l'état civil 2023-2027 was [adopted by joint order on 25 January 2024](<https://www.sidwaya.info/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-un-taux-dexecution-physique-de-6411-en-2024/>). In 2024, [2.52% of its financing came from the state budget, 33.46% from local authorities and 64.01% from partners](<https://www.sidwaya.info/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-un-taux-dexecution-physique-de-6411-en-2024/>).

**Events registered digitally.** The country's delegate told an Abidjan symposium in December 2024 that [births are declared directly from health centres through a national application](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-lexperience-du-burkina-faso-de-la-centrafrique-et-du-congo/>). Agents of the commune of Pouni were [trained from 31 August to 2 September 2026 to enter births, marriages and deaths](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>).

**How the record is made.** The Pouni training covered [entering events in real time by smartphone, at central offices and secondary centres alike](<https://www.aib.media/sanguie-pouni-des-agents-de-letat-civil-outilles-a-lutilisation-de-la-plateforme-veneem/>). A November 2024 account says the platform [captures events where they occur, on computers and smartphones](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/la-digitalisation-des-declarations-des-naissances-et-autres-actes-detat-civil-une-realite-au-burkina-faso/?lang=fr>). The Code des personnes et de la famille, in its 2025 text, [institutes a national digital civil-status register](<https://www.minute.bf/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Nouveau-Code-de-la-famille.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Agents of the commune of Koloko were [trained on the software and given equipment](<https://www.aib.media/kenedougou-les-agents-de-la-mairie-de-koloko-formes-a-lutilisation-du-logiciel-veneem/>), as reported on 22 June 2026. On 25 June 2026 the director general for the modernisation of civil status said [deployment in communes was continuing](<https://www.wakatsera.com/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-2023-2027-plus-de-81-dexecution-financiere-enregistres-en-2025/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The ministry reported the software [deployed in 30 main and 140 secondary centres in 2025](<https://www.wakatsera.com/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-2023-2027-plus-de-81-dexecution-financiere-enregistres-en-2025/>), and [in 20 main and 96 secondary centres in 2024](<https://www.sidwaya.info/strategie-nationale-de-letat-civil-un-taux-dexecution-physique-de-6411-en-2024/>).
