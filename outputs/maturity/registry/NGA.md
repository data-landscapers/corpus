# Nigeria

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Population Commission [runs the registration and its centres](<https://von.gov.ng/commission-commences-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>), and said in July 2026 that the platform is [operated under a public-private partnership with a technical partner](<https://von.gov.ng/commission-commences-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>). The Registrar General's Department [lists a Digital CRVS Roadmap for 2024 to 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-nigeria.pdf>). A 2026 programme to speed up birth registration in Kano State is [funded by the Government of the Netherlands](<https://www.radionigeriakaduna.gov.ng/2026/05/18/digital-birth-registration-expands-identity-access-for-children/>).

**Events registered digitally.** Births and deaths [have been registered digitally across the country since 1 July 2026](<https://von.gov.ng/commission-commences-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>), on the platform the Commission calls [VitalReg](<https://von.gov.ng/national-population-commission-launches-digital-registration-platform/>).

**How the record is made.** The Registrar General's Department described in July 2026 [an officer in ward and local government offices registering the birth on the platform](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-nigeria.pdf>), with real-time reporting of registration data. A registrar in Lagos said in August 2026 that his team [takes details on a form when the network fails](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/3-5m-missing-births-how-hospitals-can-close-nigerias-registration-gap/>) and calls parents back.

**Last twelve months.** The Commission says the platform [became operational in all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory on 1 July 2026, replacing paper registration](<https://von.gov.ng/national-population-commission-launches-digital-registration-platform/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Registrar General's Department reported [3,372 wards activated and 304 registration workstations deployed](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-nigeria.pdf>) at 18 July 2026. The Commission said that month that it has [4,011 registration centres across the 774 local government areas](<https://von.gov.ng/commission-commences-nationwide-digital-birth-death-registration/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: the [National Identification Number and the National Identity Database](<https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/constitutional-administrative-law/1829270/the-national-identity-management-commission-establishment-act-2026-key-new-provisions>) of the National Identity Management Commission, its [National Identity Management System and Automated Biometric Identification System](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122424123515222/pdf/P167183-7da88040-b609-473f-bc3c-7667ef8f9f33>), and [Smile ID](<https://techcrunch.com/2023/02/15/costanoa-ventures-and-norrsken22-back-smile-identity-in-20m-series-b-round/>), a private company's. Notification: a [health-facility birth notification pipeline](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/3-5m-missing-births-how-hospitals-can-close-nigerias-registration-gap/>) and [community birth notification by health workers and traditional birth attendants](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-nigeria.pdf>). Outreach: [registration of pupils in schools](<https://nannews.ng/nigerias-digital-identity-gap-leaves-schoolchildren-behind/>), [mobile registration units and ad hoc registration exercises](<https://cresthillsmedia.com/why-millions-of-nigerian-children-remain-undocumented-despite-digital-birth-registration-push/>), the programme named [Accelerating Birth Registration and Leave No Child Behind in Nigeria 2026](<https://www.radionigeriakaduna.gov.ng/2026/05/18/digital-birth-registration-expands-identity-access-for-children/>) and the [Renewed Hope Initiative birth registration project](<https://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-unicef-first-lady-partner-on-birth-registration>). Exchange: [a NIN generated for the child at birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-nigeria.pdf>), [integration of birth registration with primary healthcare and immunisation](<https://www.radionigeriakaduna.gov.ng/2026/05/18/digital-birth-registration-expands-identity-access-for-children/>) and [the e-CRVS certificate required to change a date of birth on the NIN database](<https://dpi.africa/nigeria-intensifies-efforts-to-digitalise-birth-death-registrations/>).

***Not held***. The share of local offices entering on the platform: the wards activated have no total, and the count of registration centres does not say how many of them enter on it. No domestic budget line is held.
