# Congo

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The state agency ACSI [designed and built the civil registration system Congo calls SIFEC](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-ladministration-du-territoire-receptionne-les-documents>) for the Interior ministry's territorial administration directorate. On 20 February 2026 the validation workshop's main recommendation was that [the human, material and financial means to deploy it be provided](<http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226>). The World Bank's Congo Digital Acceleration Project [counts an operational civil registration database among its results](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063025115539514/pdf/P175592-6921d01e-6c0d-4e80-ac5a-746d4f697d53.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** None. SIFEC's [Births, Marriages and Deaths modules](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-societe-le-sifec-un-levier-de-securisation-des-donnees-detat-civil/>) were under validation in February 2026, and the World Bank scored a national digital database of civil registration records [not operational at 31 May 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063025115539514/pdf/P175592-6921d01e-6c0d-4e80-ac5a-746d4f697d53.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** On paper: in February 2026 the Interior ministry's security adviser called the system [long manual and fragmented](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-societe-le-sifec-un-levier-de-securisation-des-donnees-detat-civil/>). In 2021 the World Bank found [electronic records stored locally](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/801771619776798100/txt/Concept-Project-Information-Document-PID-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project-P175592.txt>) in civil registration centres of Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire, and rural areas [largely paper-based](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/801771619776798100/txt/Concept-Project-Information-Document-PID-Congo-Digital-Acceleration-Project-P175592.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** On 20 February 2026 a workshop at Kintélé [validated 17 recommendations on SIFEC](<http://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/cloture-travaux-SIFEC_200226>). On 7 August 2026 the Interior minister told the Senate [its rollout was imminent](<https://brazzavilleinsider.com/congo-bets-on-digital-records-to-curb-civil-fraud/>). On 22 September 2026 ACSI [handed the system's operating documents to the territorial administration directorate](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-ladministration-du-territoire-receptionne-les-documents>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** None. After the handover of 22 September 2026 [seven steps remained, user training and deployment among them](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-ladministration-du-territoire-receptionne-les-documents>). In 2020 the World Bank counted [2 of Brazzaville's 10 civil status offices as digitised](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/Republique-du-Congo-DE4A-en-Francais.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: a [project with FamilySearch to digitise civil status registers and national archives](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-administration-le-gouvernement-satisfait-des-avancees-du-projet-de-numerisation-de-letat-civil/>), under a protocol being formalised in September 2024. Outreach: a [pilot at Talangaï town hall issuing birth certificates, identity cards and NIU numbers to vulnerable households](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/talangai-des-documents-didentite-remis-des-menages-vulnerables-168666>) from 15 January 2026, the Ministry of Justice's [audiences foraines in Likouala and Sangha](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/RCO_CONGO_UPR45_COG_F_Main.pdf>), which issued 558 birth certificates in 2022, and [birth registers for 10,000 certificates](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/RCO_CONGO_UPR45_COG_F_Main.pdf>) handed over that year. Enabling: [computer equipment supplied by UNHCR to civil status services in Pointe-Noire](<https://upr-info.org/sites/default/files/country-document/2025-12/RCO_CONGO_UPR45_COG_F_Main.pdf>). Certificate, all planned: the [Portail e-Citizen](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>), the [Services numériques de l'État](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/le-numerique-au-service-dun-congo-plus-performant-plus-souverain-et-plus-proche-de-ses>) and an [eGov portal](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099063025115539514/pdf/P175592-6921d01e-6c0d-4e80-ac5a-746d4f697d53.pdf>) with none of its 10 targeted services online at 31 May 2025. Identity: the [carte nationale d'identité informatisée et sécurisée](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>), the [fichier national de l'identification civile](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2022/congo-jo-2022-22.pdf>), a [second card and passport production centre at Pointe-Noire](<https://www.dac-presse.com/la-production-de-passeports-et-de-cartes-nationales-didentite-sinstallera-a-pointe-noire>), the [NIU biometric card](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>), whose production was relaunched from September 2025, a [card distribution campaign in Pointe-Noire](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913>) from 9 September 2025, and a planned [digital identity on MOSIP](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>) with a [Digital Wallet](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-numerique-mise-en-place-dun-systeme-didentite-numerique-pour-securiser-les-donnees-personnelles>). Exchange, planned: an [exchange layer on X-Road](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/architecture-et-digital-public-infrastructure-dpi>), an [interoperability framework and exchange platform](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2025/congo-jo-2025-44.pdf>) financed by the EU and the EIB, and [interoperability of the social register with the civil registration databases](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099111124112029221/pdf/BOSIB-b55c4f49-75e9-42ca-9a1b-b7a848347be2.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether the electronic records kept in some Brazzaville and Pointe-Noire centres in 2020 and 2021 are still kept. Who pays for SIFEC's deployment, and the period of the civil status digitisation roadmap.
