# Chad

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank states that [Chad created ANATS in 2016](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061124175525537/pdf/BOSIB1811b26290ef1a3561032ec244ac8a.pdf>) as the government institution in charge of operationalising the civil registry. ANATS's director general said in November 2023 that digital birth registration [follows the validated roadmap for universal birth registration by 2030](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/11/16/tchad-la-campagne-denregistrement-universel-a-letat-civil-des-naissances-de-la-ville-de-ndjamena-est-officiellement-lancee/>). Kiosks for birth registration in eleven N'Djamena health centres were [being set up with UNICEF support and EU funding](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090624105035738>) in September 2024.

**Events registered digitally.** ANATS's coordinator of civil registration said in an account published in June 2025 that [more than 120,000 children had been registered through hospital-based registry offices](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>) since 2023.

**How the record is made.** The World Bank stated in 2023 that civil registration officers in six hospitals [enter the declaration in a mobile application ANATS calls TASDJIL](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062923114552178/txt/P17712007468b1040987f0e2e23aeb2064.txt>), after which ANATS issues the birth certificate electronically. In the June 2025 account [all data is sent by SMS to the national agency](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>), which validates it and returns it for issuance. On 23 September 2026 the N'Djamena town hall cited [the still heavy recourse to physical procedures](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/tchad-n-djamena-lance-la-numerisation-de-son-etat-civil>) in its civil-status service.

**Last twelve months.** On 23 September 2026 the commune of N'Djamena [launched a project to digitise its civil registry and signed a memorandum with ANATS](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>); an integrated management platform is planned.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The June 2025 account gives [97 digitised hospital-based civil registration points](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>), which the government plans to raise to 600.
