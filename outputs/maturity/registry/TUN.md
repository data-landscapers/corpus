# Tunisia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** Births and deaths in the civil register [come under the Ministry of Interior](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>), on a 2025 country report. The agreement to rebuild the platform, signed on 19 March 2021, was [financed by the African Development Bank](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2021/03/20/465229/modernisation-de-la-plateforme-de-letat-civil-madania-signature-de-laccord-avec-un-consortium/>) and [falls under the national plan Tunisie Digitale 2020](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2021/03/20/465229/modernisation-de-la-plateforme-de-letat-civil-madania-signature-de-laccord-avec-un-consortium/>).

**Events registered digitally.** The national civil status system, which Tunisia calls Madania, [holds births and deaths as well as marriages and divorces in a centralised database](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>), on a 2025 report. It held [18.53 million birth acts and 4.14 million death acts as of 2018](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/368931596533672339>).

**How the record is made.** The registrar [writes each event by hand in the paper register and then records it directly in the central database](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/SocialProtection_5_Tunisia_e_WEB.pdf>), where it is available nationwide the next morning, on a 2020 account.

**Last twelve months.** On 20 November, 4 December and 11 December 2025 [workshops trained civil status officers and heads of municipal districts from 72 municipalities on the system](<https://tunisia.unfpa.org/fr/news/%C3%A9tat-civil-72-municipalit%C3%A9s-tunisiennes-sunissent-pour-une-meilleure-gestion-des-donn%C3%A9es>), organised by the Ministry of the Interior and the National Institute of Health.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** [All civil registration offices have interconnected computer systems and all municipalities have access to Madania](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/files/SocialProtection_5_Tunisia_e_WEB.pdf>), an IDRC chapter of 2020 states, counting 350 principal offices and 170 subsidiary offices, annexes and mobile units.
