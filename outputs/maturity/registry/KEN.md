# Kenya

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** Civil Registration Services [sits in the Ministry of Interior and National Administration's State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2023-Kenya-Vital-Statistics-Report.pdf>), under an executive order of January 2023. ESTDEV, the implementing partner, said in September 2026 that [it funded the development of the digital birth registration service](<https://www.estdev.ee/en/articles/over-270000-births-registered-two-months-kenyas-e-birth-registry-developed-together>), with project activities funded by Germany and the European Union. A costed plan for 2018 to 2022 [listed the upgrade and rollout of the registration system among its high priorities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_KenyaSnapshot_e.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** [More than 270,000 births were registered in two months](<https://www.estdev.ee/en/articles/over-270000-births-registered-two-months-kenyas-e-birth-registry-developed-together>) through the new digital birth registration solution, ESTDEV said in September 2026. The principal secretary said [the same system may in future be used for deaths](<https://www.estdev.ee/en/articles/over-270000-births-registered-two-months-kenyas-e-birth-registry-developed-together>).

**How the record is made.** Parents [confirm the birth and enter the data while still in the hospital, and it goes directly to the population registry](<https://www.estdev.ee/en/articles/over-270000-births-registered-two-months-kenyas-e-birth-registry-developed-together>), with the certificate issued the same day or within days, on ESTDEV's account of September 2026.

**Last twelve months.** The solution's first two months were [the first time Kenyan parents could register a birth online](<https://www.estdev.ee/en/articles/over-270000-births-registered-two-months-kenyas-e-birth-registry-developed-together>), ESTDEV said in September 2026. The Star reported in March 2026 that the Civil Registration Service [led the validation of the Births and Deaths Registration Bill 2025](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/realtime/2026-03-05-government-moves-to-introduce-digital-civil-registration-system>), which gives a legal framework for digital registration.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** Civil Registration Services reported for 2023 that [it had offices in 153 of 380 sub-counties](<https://www.knbs.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2023-Kenya-Vital-Statistics-Report.pdf>), where registration was manual, on paper registers.
