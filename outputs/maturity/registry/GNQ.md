# Equatorial Guinea

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Justice [put the system it calls SIREC into service in December 2018](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/discurso_de_la_representante_de_unicef_en_el_dia_del_ni%C3%B1o_africano_2021>), with the support of UNICEF and the European Union. The statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 [sets a civil registry system established as an expected result](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). The World Bank said in 2024 that [funding to extend the digital registry to the mainland is lacking](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**Events registered digitally.** The pilot in Malabo [allows the digital registration of new births](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), the World Bank said in 2024. Its platform was built to collect births and deaths. In 2025 the state IT centre CNIAPGE [set up a new system to register newborns from hospitals](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/continuan_las_comparecencias_de_los_miembros_del_gobierno_en_la_comision_parlamentaria_que_analiza_los_presupuestos_generales>), its director told parliament in October 2026.

**How the record is made.** The seven regional offices [collect and store data on paper](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), the World Bank said in 2024. Local judges registering at commune level pass their manually collected data to them.

**Last twelve months.** In 2025 CNIAPGE [set up the hospital newborn system](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/continuan_las_comparecencias_de_los_miembros_del_gobierno_en_la_comision_parlamentaria_que_analiza_los_presupuestos_generales>). It also maintained the civil registry platform, it told parliament in October 2026.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The pilot [was launched in late 2018 exclusively in the capital, Malabo](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), by the Ministry of Justice with UNICEF.
