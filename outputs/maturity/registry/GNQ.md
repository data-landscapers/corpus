# Equatorial Guinea

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Justice [put the system it calls SIREC into service in December 2018](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/discurso_de_la_representante_de_unicef_en_el_dia_del_ni%C3%B1o_africano_2021>), with the support of UNICEF and the European Union. The statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 [sets a civil registry system established as an expected result](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). The World Bank said in 2024 that [funding to extend the digital registry to the mainland is lacking](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**Events registered digitally.** The pilot in Malabo [allows the digital registration of new births](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), on a platform developed to collect births and deaths, the World Bank said in 2024.

**How the record is made.** The seven regional offices [collect and store data by manual, paper-based processes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), and local judges registering at commune level share manually collected data with them, on the same account of 2024.

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank's diagnostic, published in 2024, [reports UNICEF as saying the pilot is operational and functions well](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The pilot [was launched in late 2018 exclusively in the capital, Malabo](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), by the Ministry of Justice with UNICEF.

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: [the digitisation of historic civil registry records in Malabo](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>) and [the conversion of 192,000 birth records of 1980 to 2019 to digital form](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/discurso_de_la_representante_de_unicef_en_el_dia_del_ni%C3%B1o_africano_2021>). Notification: [Pre-registro de Nacimientos](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/audiencia_entre_martina_masugu_oyana_y_melanio_ebendeng_oyana>), being implemented in Bata in March 2025. Certificate: [APP RC](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/audiencia_entre_martina_masugu_oyana_y_melanio_ebendeng_oyana>), for electronic certificates with QR codes. Outreach: [a free civil registration campaign for children aged 0 to 5](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2026/08/10/dia-africano-de-registro-civil-2026-ministerio-de-justicia-abre-una-campana-gratuita-para-registrar-a-menores-de-0-a-5-anos/>), 10 to 14 August 2026. Identity: [CNEDOGE's issuance of the identity document it calls the DIP and of passports](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2025/04/02/la-camara-de-diputados-examina-las-tarifas-y-los-procesos-de-emision-de-documentos-oficiales-en-cnedoge/>), its [provincial offices in Mongomo and Ciudad de la Paz and online appointment system](<https://ahoraeg.com/sociedad/2026/06/12/cnedoge-inaugura-nuevas-oficinas-en-mongomo-y-ciudad-de-la-paz-para-acercar-los-servicios-documentales-a-la-ciudadania/>), its [documentation mission to Annobón](<https://infoannobon.com/el-gobierno-despliega-en-annobon-una-jornada-masiva-de-documentacion-para-acercar-la-administracion/>), its [national DIP tour](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/el_gobierno_quiere_implementar_un_mecanismo_riguroso_de_control_para_la_adquisicion_del_certificado_literal>), its [internal database of document issuance](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099051724134540301/txt/P179339-f4a4c89e-b212-4802-9f00-4c68aefed3c8.txt>), the [CEMAC biometric passport](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/index.php/noticias/comparecencia_de_simeon_oyono_esono_ante_el_pleno_de_la_camara_de_los_diputados_para_presentar_propuestas_en_los_convenios_internacionales>), and what the World Bank's ID4D dataset lists as the [National Identity Document](<https://id4d.worldbank.org/global-dataset>). Enabling: the [e-Gob development platform](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/audiencia_entre_martina_masugu_oyana_y_melanio_ebendeng_oyana>).

***Not held***. Nothing on the pilot is dated after 2024. How the pilot makes the record, and how many offices in Malabo use it, are not held.
