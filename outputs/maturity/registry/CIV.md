# Côte d'Ivoire

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** ONECI, the civil status and identification office, is [the executing arm of the national civil status policy](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634662/politique/etat-civil-vagondo-diomande-veut-batir-un-systeme-securise>). A convention of 2 March 2026 [implements the national civil status and identification strategy](<https://cinews.ci/modernisation-et-de-securisation-loneci-et-la-communaute-saintegidio-sallient-pour-accroitre-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances/>). The 2026 finance law [appropriates FCFA 500 million from domestic revenue](<https://www.dgbf.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Loi-de-Finances-2026.pdf>) for modernising civil status.

**Events registered digitally.** ONECI supplied software to the centres, which AIP names Citiweb. It is [intended for the digital registration of births, marriages, deaths and other acts](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>).

**How the record is made.** ONECI's director general said on 15 July 2024 that [298 civil status centres were connected to the centralised City Web system](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1721159722>) for registering events. The World Bank said on 18 September 2025 that [the registry is still on paper](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>). A [2019 decree keeps the paper register as the legal record](<https://www.oneci.ci/uploads/files/articles/1638800505-decret-n2019-805-du-02-octobre-2019.pdf>), with computer records beside it.

**Last twelve months.** At the end of 2025 ONECI [supplied all town hall and sub-prefecture centres with computers carrying the software, and began training agents](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>). From 27 to 29 July 2026 it [trained 44 agents of the Gôh region](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** In July 2024 ONECI said [City Web was deployed in 302 of 690 centres](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-le-numerique-au-service-de-la-securite-et-la-fiabilite-de-lidentite-des-ivoiriens/>), or 43.8 per cent. In September 2025 it said [more than 84 per cent were linked to a national server](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2025-lidentite-est-le-parapluie-de-tout-individu-dg-oneci/>). On 2 March 2026 its director general said [98 per cent of centres were computerised and connected to the software](<https://cinews.ci/modernisation-et-de-securisation-loneci-et-la-communaute-saintegidio-sallient-pour-accroitre-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances/>). On 11 August 2026 he said [the national system was deployed in 699 centres](<https://www.koaci.com/index.php/article/2026/08/12/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-operation-zero-enfant-invisible-loneci-deploie-283-terminaux-numeriques-dans-le-tonkpi-pour-garantir-lidentite-des-la-naissance_199500.html>).

The decree keeps [paper collection registers with health and community agents](<https://www.oneci.ci/uploads/files/articles/1638800505-decret-n2019-805-du-02-octobre-2019.pdf>). Certificate: [MaPoste](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1768657500>), [e-justice.ci](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634927/politique/tribunal-de-1re-instance-dabidjan-le-ministre-sansan-kambile-lance-la-plateforme-digitale-e-justiceci>), the [Yopougon town hall kiosk](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/09/02/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-yopougon-mise-en-service-de-la-deuxieme-borne-digitale-interactive-detablissement-de-documents-administratifs-3000-extraits-pourront-etre-etablis-par-jour_189848.html>) and [kiosks planned in 50 municipalities](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>). Enabling: [broadband for 835 civil registry offices, targeted for July 2029](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>).
