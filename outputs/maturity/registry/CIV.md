# Côte d'Ivoire

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** ONECI, the civil status and identification office, [runs the national civil-status software with the digital transition ministry](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/cote-divoire-le-logiciel-national-detat-civil-deploye-la-connectivite-reste-un-defi_65266>). The 2026 finance law [appropriates FCFA 500 million from domestic revenue](<https://www.dgbf.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Loi-de-Finances-2026.pdf>) for modernising civil status. In 2025 the European Union [paid for the software's roll-out in Cavally and Guémon](<https://digitalmag.ci/logiciel-etat-civil-oneci-cavally-guemon/>).

**Events registered digitally.** ONECI's software, which AIP names Citiweb, is [for the digital registration of births, deaths and other acts](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>). Since August 2024 [a birth can be registered in it from the maternity ward](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-lenregistrement-des-naissances-desormais-possible-depuis-les-maternites-en-cote-divoire/>).

**How the record is made.** ONECI's director general said in July 2024 that [298 centres were connected to the central City Web system](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1721159722>) for registering events. The World Bank said on 18 September 2025 that [the registry is still on paper](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>). A [2019 decree keeps the paper register as the legal record](<https://www.oneci.ci/uploads/files/articles/1638800505-decret-n2019-805-du-02-octobre-2019.pdf>), with computer records beside it.

**Last twelve months.** At the end of 2025 ONECI [gave every town hall and sub-prefecture centre computers carrying the software](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>). On 23 October 2026 its director general said [it is deployed in all centres, with connectivity still a problem](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/cote-divoire-le-logiciel-national-detat-civil-deploye-la-connectivite-reste-un-defi_65266>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** In August 2024 the interior minister [counted 346 of 690 centres connected to City Web](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-lenregistrement-des-naissances-desormais-possible-depuis-les-maternites-en-cote-divoire/>). In September 2025 ONECI said [more than 84 per cent were linked to a national server](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2025-lidentite-est-le-parapluie-de-tout-individu-dg-oneci/>). In March 2026 its director general said [98 per cent of centres were computerised and connected](<https://cinews.ci/modernisation-et-de-securisation-loneci-et-la-communaute-saintegidio-sallient-pour-accroitre-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances/>).
