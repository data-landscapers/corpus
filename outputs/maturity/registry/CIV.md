# Côte d'Ivoire

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** ONECI, the national civil status and identification office, is [the executing arm of the national civil status policy](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2634662/politique/etat-civil-vagondo-diomande-veut-batir-un-systeme-securise>), the Minister of the Interior and Security said on 16 June 2025. A convention of 2 March 2026 [implements the Stratégie nationale de l'état civil et de l'identification](<https://cinews.ci/modernisation-et-de-securisation-loneci-et-la-communaute-saintegidio-sallient-pour-accroitre-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances/>). The 2026 finance law [appropriates FCFA 500 million from domestic revenue](<https://www.dgbf.ci/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Loi-de-Finances-2026.pdf>) for modernising civil status.

**Events registered digitally.** The software ONECI supplied to the centres, which AIP names Citiweb, is [intended for the digital registration of births, marriages, deaths and other civil status acts](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>).

**How the record is made.** The World Bank said on 18 September 2025 that [the registry is still on paper](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099092225092021892/pdf/BOSIB-af66fe3f-3dfc-4703-ab9c-9f1fb5cf5714.pdf>). The [decree of 2 October 2019 keeps the paper register as the legal record](<https://www.oneci.ci/uploads/files/articles/1638800505-decret-n2019-805-du-02-octobre-2019.pdf>) and permits acts to be established by computer beside it.

**Last twelve months.** At the end of 2025 ONECI [supplied all town hall and sub-prefecture centres with computers carrying the software, and began training agents](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>). From 27 to 29 July 2026 it [trained 44 agents of the Gôh region](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-loneci-renforce-les-capacites-en-logiciel-citiweb-des-agents-de-letat-civil-de-la-region-du-goh/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** ONECI's director general said on 2 March 2026 that [98 per cent of civil status centres were computerised and connected to the software](<https://cinews.ci/modernisation-et-de-securisation-loneci-et-la-communaute-saintegidio-sallient-pour-accroitre-le-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances/>), and on 11 August 2026 that [its Système national de gestion de l'état civil was deployed in 699 centres](<https://www.koaci.com/index.php/article/2026/08/12/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-operation-zero-enfant-invisible-loneci-deploie-283-terminaux-numeriques-dans-le-tonkpi-pour-garantir-lidentite-des-la-naissance_199500.html>).
