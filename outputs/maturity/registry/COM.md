# Comoros

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of the Interior [carries the modernisation project Comoros calls AMECC](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/%C3%A9tat-civil-l%E2%80%99amecc-veut-enterrer-les-registres-sur-papier-.html>), with the French embassy and UNICEF. By 14 August 2026 the country held [a costed modernisation plan and a national mapping of civil registration centres](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/3012-la-modernisation-de-l-etat-civil-un-chantier-strategique>). France and UNICEF [financed the work to that date](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/3012-la-modernisation-de-l-etat-civil-un-chantier-strategique>), and national institutions validated [a residual financing need of EUR 9,009,900](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/3012-la-modernisation-de-l-etat-civil-un-chantier-strategique>).

**Events registered digitally.** None. Births, marriages and deaths [are registered in paper registers](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>), and the digital system, an eCRVS, [is only proposed, by a preliminary study](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>) validated on 25 March 2026.

**How the record is made.** In August 2026 the system was [still largely dependent on old paper registers](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/%C3%A9tat-civil-l%E2%80%99amecc-veut-enterrer-les-registres-sur-papier-.html>). In January 2026 the Minister of the Interior said acts are made on [secured printed forms produced abroad, with recurring shortages](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/mohamed-ahamed-assoumani,-ministre-de-l%E2%80%99int%C3%A9rieur-i-%C2%ABle-plan-comores-%C3%A9mergent-ne-va-pas-se-faire-avec-les-minist%C3%A8res-seulement%C2%BB.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The study preceding digitalisation was [launched in Moroni on 2 October 2025](<https://www.lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/vers-un-%C3%A9tat-civil-num%C3%A9rique-moh%C3%A9li-ouvre-la-voie-%C3%A0-la-modernisation-nationale-.html>) and [validated there on 25 March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-advances-digital-identity-reform-with-nationwide-registry-overhaul>). On 11 March 2026 the Council of Ministers [did not adopt the draft decree for a national civil status agency](<https://beit-salam.km/actualit%C3%A9s/conseil-des-ministres-du-11-mars-2026-des-initiatives-pour-l%E2%80%99agriculture,-la-s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9,-l%E2%80%99innovation-et-l%E2%80%99environnement.html>). A UNICEF-recruited consultant [ran a resource-mobilisation mission from 30 July to 16 August 2026](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/3012-la-modernisation-de-l-etat-civil-un-chantier-strategique>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** None. The preliminary study covered 37 civil status centres and [proposes a pilot in three, at Moroni, Mutsamudu and Fomboni](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>). Of the 37, [fewer than half have stable electricity and one in five has internet access](<http://comoresdroit.centerblog.net/2912-une-revolution-numerique-se-prepare-pour-l-etat-civil>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification, planned: the health ministry's [Système National Automatisé de Collecte et de Saisie des naissances et des décès](<https://extranet.who.int/cpcd/sites/default/files/public_file_repository/com_comoros_strategie-nationale-de-sant-numerique_2022-2026.pdf>), for births and deaths recorded in health facilities or by community health agents. Outreach: [Ruwa Mwana](<https://www.francophonie.org/le-projet-etat-civil-deploye-aux-comores-8175>), a birth registration awareness campaign launched in Moroni on 25 September 2025. Identity: [biometric passport and identity card production](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/confection-des-papiers-biom%C3%A9triques-i-semlex-remet-les-donn%C3%A9es-au-gouvernement.html>), run by Semlex from 2007 to March 2026 and by GenKey since, the [carte nationale d'identité](<https://www.comoresinfos.net/controle-didentite-obligatoire-des-le-12-aout-mineurs-etrangers-accidents-ce-qui-va-changer/>), compulsory at identity checks from 12 August 2025, the [carte d'identité comorienne numérique](<https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n14-137-pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n14-023-au-instituant-la-carte-didentite-comorienne-numerique/>) instituted by law, a [biometric driving licence programme](<https://finances.gouv.km/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/LETTRE-DE-CADRAGE-BUDGETAIRE-2025.pdf>) named in the 2025 budget framing letter, and a planned [Personal Identification Number](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/comoros-advances-digital-identity-reform-with-nationwide-registry-overhaul>).

***Not held***. When the three-centre pilot starts, and the period the costed plan and the 2024 strategic plan cover. Whether any office keeps records other than on paper.
