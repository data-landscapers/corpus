# Cabo Verde

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, names the [Directorate of Registries, Notaries and Identification of the Ministry of Justice](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>) as the authority registering births and deaths. Decreto-Lei 43/2007 [places the civil registry database in that directorate's ownership](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/375>).

**Events registered digitally.** Births and deaths. UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, states that [all vital events are recorded directly in the computer](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>). NOSi's catalogue of 2025 lists a Civilian ID System that [collects and processes civil registry data on births, marriages, deaths and stillbirths](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>).

**How the record is made.** In 2017 events were recorded in [applications located in the civil registry conservatórias](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>), with a certificate issued immediately. The Civil Registry Code of 2014 [requires entries in electronic form, held in a national civil registry database](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/1940>), and allows paper only when the system is unavailable.

**Last twelve months.** NOSi's catalogue of 2025 describes the Civilian ID System as [a stream linking the civil registries to other government systems](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** UNICEF's profile, compiled to November 2017, puts the applications in [the conservatórias of all 22 municipalities](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>), 22 of 22. Registration stations [exist only in the urban centres of those municipalities](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>); mobile teams reached rural communities during the birth registration project.

***Noted, not assessed***. Outreach: the [mobile teams of the birth registration project](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/cabo-verde/>). Identity: [SNIAC](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>), the [Cartão Nacional de Identificação](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>) and its [middleware](<https://sniac.cv/mw/>), the [electronic passport](<https://www.nosi.cv/en/solu%C3%A7%C3%B5es-egov>), the [residence title for foreigners](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/3901074/ANEXO+INFORMATIVO_OE_2024_DEFINITIVO.pdf/bf68c237-a941-fb9c-d290-018e20641a01?version=1.0&t=1696269705355>), the [Chave Móvel Digital](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/en/cabo-verde-mobile-digital-key>), [Autentika](<https://portal-autentika.gov.cv/>), [card and passport issuance through embassies](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=101439>) and the EU-financed [GESTDOC](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/guine-bissau-evento-de-encerramento-do-projeto-gestdoc-e-lancamento-dos-sistemas-passe-e-sirec>) project, closed in January 2025. Enabling: the state network, [NOSinet](<https://mindelinsite.com/opiniao/cabo-verde-na-vanguarda-da-nova-economia-o-desafio-bizgital-e-o-papel-critico-da-cabo-verde-telecom/>), and [computers handed to health delegations by WHO](<https://www.afro.who.int/pt/countries/cabo-verde/news/parceria-oms-e-ministerio-da-saude-impulsiona-modernizacao-do-sistema-de-registo-de-obitos>) for death registration. Exchange: a [unique identification number issued to every child at birth](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>), a [web-service link from the health information system to the civil register](<https://backend-ugpe.gov.cv/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/ToR-TA-FOR-THE-UPGRADE-OF-THE-HEALTH-INFORMATION.pdf>), and the statistics institute's [vital statistics from civil registry records](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2025/10/01/estatisticas-vitais-de-2023-registam-queda-na-natalidade-obitos-e-casamentos/99233>), 6,760 births and 2,925 deaths in 2023.

***Not held***. Whether the entry made at the conservatória is held in the national register in practice. Any share of offices, or rural share, newer than 2017; any fact on ownership, plan or finance from the last three years, and any event in the last twelve months.
