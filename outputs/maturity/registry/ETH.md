# Ethiopia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Immigration and Citizenship Service is the registration organ, with structures from federal to the lowest administrative level, [under Proclamation No. 1370/2025 of 18 March 2025](<https://ethiodata.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Civil-and-Family-Registration-Proclamation-No.-1370_2025.pdf>). The national eCRVS, which Ethiopia calls the Civil & Family Registration System, is [homegrown, driven by the Prime Minister's Office and carries a three-phase rollout plan](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>) (July 2026).

**Events registered digitally.** The national eCRVS [lists birth and death registration and certificates among its services](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>), alongside marriage, divorce and adoption (July 2026).

**How the record is made.** The eCRVS is a decentralised, multi-tenant system on a shared architecture in which city administrations and regions provide all services, with [offline access, rural connectivity, power and tablets listed as gaps](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>) (July 2026). Civil registration is [mainly paper-based and manual](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Taju-Mohammed-Presentation.pdf>), three copies of the form passing from kebele to woreda to the Service (July 2025).

**Last twelve months.** On 5 December 2025 the mayor of Addis Ababa launched the Integrated Civil Registration and Residency Service Technology and [announced full implementation in 50 woredas from 8 December](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_7858122>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The eCRVS rollout [begins with a pilot in Addis Ababa](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>), then Amharic-speaking regions, then the rest of the country (July 2026). A tablet system, ME-CRVS, [has been piloted in 1,300 kebeles and health facilities](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Taju-Mohammed-Presentation.pdf>), in a country the Service counts at 19,202 kebeles, 70% of which provide civil registration (July 2025). In Addis Ababa the city agency's digitisation was [in a pilot phase in 21 of 116 woredas](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/school-registration-adds-up-to-expanding-use-cases-of-ethiopias-digital-id>) in January 2025.

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: [Fayda](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>), the national digital ID; the [kebele ID](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>); [VeriFayda 2](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/ethiopia-banks-harmonize-customer-account-information-with-fayda-digital-id>), EthSwitch's identity check for banks; UNHCR's refugee register [PRIMES](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/7d074d2a-3696-4937-a37f-41911c4756b3/unhcr-prospects-ethiopia-report.pdf>); and [family registration](<https://ethiodata.et/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Civil-and-Family-Registration-Proclamation-No.-1370_2025.pdf>) as the 2025 proclamation provides it. Notification: [birth and death notification by health facilities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitization-ethiopia.pdf>) within the eCRVS, and [health workers' birth notification](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Taju-Mohammed-Presentation.pdf>). Certificate: [Addis MESOB](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mulugeta-eshetu-4696061b4_addismesob-customerservice-publicservice-activity-7492500515633803264-T-2I>), a one-stop service centre in Addis Ababa. Outreach: a [joint field mission on refugee birth registration in Afar](<https://rrs.et/a-joint-field-mission-aimed-at-strengthening-birth-registration-and-child-protection-services-was-successfully-conducted-in-the-afar-region/>); [birth registration at sites for displaced people in Oromia](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/CRC-factsheet-ETHIOPIA-ENG.pdf>); and a [special civil registration drive for Fayda in Addis Ababa](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/school-registration-adds-up-to-expanding-use-cases-of-ethiopias-digital-id>) in October 2024. Exchange: the [integration of civil registration with Fayda](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B_iGkxWXNbg>); the [PRIMES Interoperability Gateway](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/7d074d2a-3696-4937-a37f-41911c4756b3/unhcr-prospects-ethiopia-report.pdf>) between the refugee and national ID systems; and the [Central Statistical Agency's compilation of vital statistics from register copies](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_EthiopiaSnapshot_e.pdf>).

***Not held***. Whether any digital system is past its pilot outside Addis Ababa, which rollout phase the eCRVS had reached by July 2026, and whether the entry is made at the office. No source of finance is stated for the eCRVS, and nothing establishes that registering a birth feeds the Fayda number.
