# Seychelles

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** [The Civil Status Act as in force on 1 November 2022](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/1883/4/eng@2022-11-01>) makes the Chief Officer of Civil Status, appointed by the President and answering to the Minister responsible for civil status, keeper of the registers of births and deaths.

**Events registered digitally.** A contributed article in Seychelles Nation of 10 August 2018 states that [the Civil Status records were computerised in 1989](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>), and that a computerised record of vital events is kept beside the manual register and updated daily from the occurrences the Civil Status registers.

**How the record is made.** [The Act as in force on 1 November 2022](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/1883/4/eng@2022-11-01>) prescribes the registers as numbered pages kept in duplicate, and its section 107A lets the Chief Officer accept statements, forms and signatures through an electronic medium.

**Last twelve months.** [The Civil Status (Amendment) Act 2021](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2021-11/Act%2045%20-%20Civil%20Status%20(Amendment)%20Act%202021.pdf>) was assented to on 4 November 2021, to commence on a date the Minister appoints by Gazette notice.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** [The Act as in force on 1 November 2022](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/1883/4/eng@2022-11-01>) provides a Central Civil Status Office in Victoria and offices at Praslin, La Digue and Anse Royale.

***Noted, not assessed***. [Electronic storage registers under section 18A](<https://seylii.org/akn/sc/act/1883/4/eng@2022-11-01>); [notification of birth and of death by medical practitioners, nurses and midwives](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2021-11/Act%2045%20-%20Civil%20Status%20(Amendment)%20Act%202021.pdf>); [CertExpress](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/media-centre/publications/launch-of-new-digital-solutions-for-public-services>); [SeyID](<https://seyid.gov.sc>), with its [ID-Wallet](<https://www.seyid.gov.sc/IDWallet>), [Minor ID](<https://beta.gov.sc/seyidportal/IDWallet/MinorID>) and [eKYC](<https://seyid.gov.sc/eKYC/AbouteKYC>); the [National Identity Card](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/civil-status/national-identity-card>); the [National Population Database and the National Identity Number](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>); the [digital ID for permit holders](<https://www.nationalassembly.sc/sites/default/files/2025-02/VERBATIM%2013TH%20NOVEMBER%202024.pdf>); the [monthly list of deceased sent to the Electoral Commission](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>); the [communication of civil status information to the Chief Registration Officer](<https://ecs.sc/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Ch.-262-Elections-Act.pdf>); the [verification of eKYC against the Civil Status System](<https://seyid.gov.sc>); and the [submission of vital records to the National Bureau of Statistics](<https://www.nation.sc/article.html?id=259946>).

***Not held***. Where the computerised record is entered, at the offices at Praslin, La Digue and Anse Royale or centrally, and how many local offices enter it. Nothing dated after 1 November 2022 on governance or on change in the last twelve months.
