# Tanzania

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** On the mainland the Registration Insolvency and Trusteeship Agency (RITA), the statutory registrar of births and deaths, [introduced the Civil Registration System in 2017 and runs it](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>). Its [Strategic Plan for 2021/22 to 2025/26 names that system](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>) for all its birth and death registration. For 2018/19 RITA reported [a treasury allocation of USD 510,768 against a requirement of USD 27.5 million](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_TanzaniaMainlandSnapshot_e.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** RITA's platform, which it calls e-RITA, [registers births, deaths, marriages, divorces and adoptions](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Patricia-Tanzania-PPT-RITA-presentation.pdf>), on its 2025 presentation. The system held [18,464,255 births as of September 2024 and 977,761 deaths registered since 2017](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** At the under-five registration centres, which are ward offices and health facilities, [officers record a birth by hand in a register and then enter the same details into the online system by phone](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>), the national audit found in October 2024.

**Last twelve months.** Zanzibar's registration agency reported [a 2025/2026 improvement of its system, with streamlined workflows and a unified mobile app](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>). On the mainland RITA [extended its system to all districts in October 2024](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** RITA's monitoring plan, as the 2024 audit reports it, counts [6,341 of 11,053 under-five registration centres as active](<https://www.nao.go.tz/uploads/Performance_Audit_Report_on_the_Identification__Registration_and_Issuance_of_Birth_and_Death_Certificates.pdf>), 57 per cent of them. In Zanzibar the agency states in 2026 that [registration runs on its own platform in all 11 districts](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-tanzania_0.pdf>).
