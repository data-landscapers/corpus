# Botswana

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Department of Civil and National Registration of the Ministry of Labour and Home Affairs [operates the registration offices and holds the records of the Births and Deaths Registration System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). Its [recurrent provision of P108,505,050 for 2026/27 covers the system's maintenance](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf>) among others. A 2019 health ministry roadmap [scheduled a registration strategic plan for 2021-2026](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Country_documents/Botswana/BHDC_Roadmap_2020.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The system [registers births, stillbirths and deaths, and was the source for the 49,180 births and 14,070 deaths registered in 2024](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** Statistics Botswana in August 2026 calls the system ["partially automated and decentralized", designed to give real-time statistics and integrated with the National Identification System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>), which has assigned a birth registration number at birth since 2003. A September 2015 report says [births and deaths records pass online from local and regional offices to a central repository](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/0f5b85a0-2c35-534b-bf92-0880430d4ffb/content>).

**Last twelve months.** The minister told Parliament on 6 March 2026 that [of about 15 hospital kiosks set up to register births on site, 9 were operating and 6 had closed](<https://www.parliament.gov.bw/documents/DAILY-HANSARD--6th-MARCH-2026---Budget-Meeting--2nd-meeting-of-the-2nd-session-of-the-13th-Parliament_10_05_04_11_03_2026.pdf>), chiefly for lack of staff. On 24 February 2026 he said [online registration of births and deaths was built and would go live with the 1-Gov platform](<https://www.botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/MLHA%20-%20Committee%20of%20Supply%20Speech%20FINAL%20%20-%2024-02-2026.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The department [operates 35 district registration offices and 17 on-site registration points at hospitals and clinics](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>), as Statistics Botswana reported in August 2026. Electronic on-site registration [had opened in 13 hospitals by September 2015](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/0f5b85a0-2c35-534b-bf92-0880430d4ffb/content>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: [the national identity card, Omang](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202608260258.html>); [the National Identification System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>); [the Electronic National Identification System, called Biometric Omang](<https://www.finance.gov.bw/images/Budget-Tables/EXPENDITURE_ESTIMATES_26-27_FINAL_DRAFT.pdf>); [the Digital ID project](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>); [the National Digital Identification System](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2026/02/botswana-doubles-down-on-digital.html>). Exchange: [the link between the registration system and the National Identification System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). Outreach: [the department's quarterly outreach programmes](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). Notification: [the birth and death notification forms CRB-2 and CRD-2](<https://www.healthdatacollaborative.org/fileadmin/uploads/hdc/Documents/Country_documents/Botswana/BHDC_Roadmap_2020.pdf>). Enabling: [the Public Key Infrastructure system](<https://weekendpost.co.bw/p420-million-digital-bombshell/>).

***Not held***. How many of the 35 district offices and 17 on-site points enter records in the system, and whether the entry is made at the office. No plan in force is held, and the kiosk closures are undated.
