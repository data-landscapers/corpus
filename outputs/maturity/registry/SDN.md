# Sudan

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** Landinfo's report of March 2023 places responsibility for civil registration with [the General Directorate for Civil Registration under the Ministry of Interior](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The civil registry centre of South Gezira locality [issued an electronic birth certificate at its Al-Hosh office on 17 May 2026](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>). A child's birth is registered in [the centralised electronic registry introduced in 2011](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>), on Landinfo's report of March 2023.

**How the record is made.** Landinfo's report of March 2023 describes a centralised electronic registry in which [registration is possible at any civil registry office in the country](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>), a birth being registered there on application against a paper notice of birth.

**Last twelve months.** The Al-Hosh office [resumed work in May 2026 after a stoppage caused by insecurity and displacement](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>), and the governor of Gezira State [inaugurated civil registry work in Umm al-Qura locality on 29 April 2026](<https://alnawrs.com/209412>). A lawyer told the BBC in a report of August 2026 that the conflict in Darfur [has shut down civil registry offices](<https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cr7kldz4djno>), and an article of June 2026 states that Khartoum's civil registry [has ceased almost entirely](<https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/06/09/sudans-governance-collapse-struggle-for-civil-authority/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** One office is named as issuing an electronic birth certificate in 2026: [the Al-Hosh office, which serves South Gezira locality and its surrounding rural villages](<https://alnawrs.com/212689>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification: the paper [notice of birth](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>), and the health ministry's [death notification form, birth notification form and notification by community leaders and teachers](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>). Outreach: [mobile registration units](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>), the [CRVS office's registration campaigns](<https://applications.emro.who.int/docs/9789290229681-eng.pdf>) and [birth registration within the Mother and Child Cash Transfer Plus programme](<https://www.unicef.org/media/178991/file/Sudan-Humanitarian-Situation-Report-No.38-(End-of-Year)-31-December-2025.pdf.pdf>). Identity: the digital identity the government calls [SUDAPASS](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>), the [national identity number](<https://landinfo.no/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Report-Sudan-Civil-Registration-ID-documents-and-passports-03032023.pdf>), the [civil fingerprint and national number service](<https://nabdsudan.net/?p=173873>), the [national card](<https://www.alnilin.com/13423167.htm>), the [personal identity card](<https://moi.gov.sd/home/show/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AE%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%B4%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%AE%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A8-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A3%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9>), the [Foreign Number system](<https://www.fananews.com/language/en/refugees-commission-announces-foreign-number-system-to-strengthen-national-security/>) and the [Public Service Complexes](<https://fjajpress.net/en/police-forum-5-digital-transformation-and-identity-protection-take-center-stage-in-the-battle-of-dignity/>). Exchange: links to the civil registry from [SUDAPASS](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>), the [Bayan personnel platform](<https://www.sudanakhbar.com/1822477>), the [customs system ASYCUDA](<https://sudanevents.com/index.php/2025/10/04/customs-authority-completes-digital-integration-of-asycuda-with-civil-registry/>), the [Federal Ministry of Health](<https://sudanplatform.net/128817>), the [Tax Chamber](<https://tax.gov.sd/en/projects-2/>) and [31 banks](<https://nabdsudan.net/?p=173873>), and the [Sudan Family Support Project's data-sharing platform](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022825170010485/pdf/BOSIB-b08fcd99-e483-44a2-b32b-5fe82545b0b8.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing since the war began says how or where the birth record is made, and no total of offices is held beside the one named. Digital registration of deaths is not evidenced, and no governance fact dates from the last three years.
