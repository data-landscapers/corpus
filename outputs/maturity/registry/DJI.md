# Djibouti

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ville de Djibouti states that it [has held the civil-status service since the Ministry of the Interior transferred the competence in 2008](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>), and that [its communes have exercised it since 2011 through SIGEC](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>), as the city names its computerised civil-status system. Terms of reference of February 2024 [place a diagnostic of SIGEC and a budgeted action plan under PROGOUV, a EUR 8 million governance support project for the city](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The city stated on 28 April 2024 that [the registration of births, marriages, divorces and deaths in Djibouti-Ville has rested since 2011 on software for entering, transmitting and using civil-status data](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>). The February 2024 terms of reference state that [maternity antennas enter birth declarations in the system and the central centre draws up death and marriage acts](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** By the city's account of 2024, [civil-status agents have fed its database continuously since 2011 as they make new entries](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>). The terms of reference place [the data on a central server in a room of the Hôtel de Ville](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The city reported on 28 April 2024 [a diagnostic of SIGEC by an international expert, commissioned under PROGOUV](<https://villededjibouti.dj/actualite2.php?id=31>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The February 2024 terms of reference list [nine civil-status sites, all in Djibouti-Ville: the Mairie's central centre, three commune centres and five antennas in maternities](<https://expertise-france.gestmax.fr/_expertise_france/public_files/mdj-tdr-diagnostic-etat-civil-vf.pdf>).

***Not held***. Nothing dated 2025 or 2026 says any office outside Djibouti-Ville enters digitally, and no national count of offices, no plan and no event of the last twelve months is held. A 2025 ESCWA survey, held only in summary, says local offices hold no electronic records of births or deaths, which disagrees with the city's 2024 account.
