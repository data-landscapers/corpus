# Benin

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** ANIP, the national identification agency, [runs the one-stop birth registration desk with its own teams and the Ministry of Health's health workers](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). Benin's playbook of May 2026 states that [ANIP is attached to the Presidency and is in charge of the population register and the national civil-status file](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). ANIP [began the reform with the support of the WURI project and UNICEF](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/guichet-unique-denregistrement-des-naissances-une-reforme-qui-change-le-quotidien-des-familles>).

**Events registered digitally.** From 20 August 2025 [the declaration and registration of a birth are done on site in about ten public health facilities](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3209/declaration-enregistrement-obtention-acte-naissance-nouveau-jour-etat-civil-beninois-anip-lance-phase-pilote-guichet-unique-dans-plusieurs-formations-sanitaires-/>). In six months [17,071 children were declared there and 13,069 registered](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3454/guichet-unique-declaration-naissances-benin-bond-spectaculaire-taux-enregistrement-mois/>), ANIP reported on 24 February 2026.

**How the record is made.** Desk agents at Natitingou and Djougou [register the child in the system at the maternity, working on tablets and centralised databases](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/guichet-unique-denregistrement-des-naissances-une-reforme-qui-change-le-quotidien-des-familles>), La Nation reported on 16 July 2026. [Paper declaration forms remained in use during the pilot](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>).

**Last twelve months.** [The pilot ended in February 2026 and a consolidation phase is running in the same ten facilities, with generalisation three to four months behind the initial calendar](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>), ANIP's director general said on 16 July 2026.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The desk's pilot [was launched on 20 August 2025 at CHU-MEL Cotonou and runs in ten public health facilities](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3209/declaration-enregistrement-obtention-acte-naissance-nouveau-jour-etat-civil-beninois-anip-lance-phase-pilote-guichet-unique-dans-plusieurs-formations-sanitaires-/>). On 24 February 2026 [the desk was running in 10 health facilities, with gradual national scale-up among the prospects](<https://www.gouv.bj/article/3454/guichet-unique-declaration-naissances-benin-bond-spectaculaire-taux-enregistrement-mois/>).
