# Morocco

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of the Interior runs the civil-status management system it calls SGEC, [under its civil-status modernisation programme](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). In April 2025 its directorate general of territorial authorities was [running a project to digitise the registers and build one information system for 1,821 offices](<https://fr.le360.ma/politique/vaste-projet-de-numerisation-des-registres-de-letat-civil_2D3JKX433FBJ5JN747O5G2VFOU/>). [Over 194 million dirhams were allocated to that project](<https://fr.le360.ma/politique/vaste-projet-de-numerisation-des-registres-de-letat-civil_2D3JKX433FBJ5JN747O5G2VFOU/>), in five lots. The World Bank's Municipal Performance Program counts a civil registry back-office system among its results. It is [financed by an IBRD loan with AFD co-financing and closes on 31 December 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The ministry said in October 2024 that SGEC [handles birth and death declarations digitally](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). Over 1,300,000 acts [had been declared or issued from it](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>).

**How the record is made.** In SGEC, by the ministry's October 2024 account, [an entry agent at the civil-status office enters the act](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). [The civil-status officer then checks and validates it](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). In October 2025 Hespress reported [communes short of the paper registers essential for registering births](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank gives the back-office system as [operational in 59 of 97 participating municipalities on 30 June 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>). The figure was [unchanged on 30 January 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The ministry said in October 2024 that SGEC was [generalised to all the Kingdom's civil-status offices](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). In October 2025 its circular called for staff to be trained on the national digital platform. [Access to the platform was to be opened shortly](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).
