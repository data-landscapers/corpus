# Morocco

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of the Interior runs the civil-status management system, which it calls SGEC, [under its civil-status modernisation programme](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>), reported in October 2024. The World Bank's Municipal Performance Program, which counts a civil registry back-office system among its results, [is financed by an IBRD loan with AFD co-financing and closes on 31 December 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The ministry said in October 2024 that SGEC [handles birth and death declarations digitally](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>), and that over 1,300,000 acts had been declared or issued from it.

**How the record is made.** By the ministry's October 2024 account, [an entry agent at the civil-status office enters acts in SGEC and the civil-status officer checks and validates them](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). In October 2025 Hespress reported [communes short of the paper registers essential for registering births](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank gives the civil registry back-office system as [operational in 59 of 97 participating municipalities on 30 June 2024 and unchanged on 30 January 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926112020747/pdf/P168147-76ebb47f-2ef4-42ba-8d67-88b617eb1f27.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The ministry said in October 2024 that SGEC was [generalised to all the Kingdom's civil-status offices](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/registre-national-linterieur-renforce-son-dispositif-digital-de-letat-civil>). In October 2025 its circular called for staff to be trained on the national digital platform, [access to which was to be opened shortly](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Back-capture: the [reprise de l'historique de l'état civil](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/fr/programme-de-modernisation-de-letat-civil>), the [digitisation of paper registers under Law 36-21](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>) and the [DGCT's tender for digitising the civil register](<https://www.atalayar.com/fr/articulo/nouvelles-technologies-innovation/maroc-numerise-son-registre-civil-grace-investissement-historique-194-millions-dollars/20250423140207213825.html>). Notification: the [preliminary declaration of births and deaths through the civil status portal](<https://www.collectivites-territoriales.gov.ma/ar/fy-alwajhtakhbar/ahm-mstjdat-qanwn-alhalt-almdnyt-rqm-3621-alsadr-bshan-ttbyqh-alzhyr-alshryf-rqm>). Certificates: [watiqa.ma](<https://aueks.ma/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Cir-du-Ministre-de-linterieur-N%C2%B0-32-du-19-5-2020-la-mise-en-oeuvre-et-suivi-de-dematerialisation-des-procedures-vers-Zero-papier.pdf>). Identity: the [electronic national identity card](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2024/12/166797/dgsn-issued-record-4-6-million-digital-ids-slashed-crime-rates-in-2024>) and its [online renewal](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/morocco-digitizes-national-id-renewal-process>), [Mon e-ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/idakto-cso-explains-moroccos-move-to-prioritize-digital-id-after-cyberattacks>), the [national digital identity wallet](<https://idtechwire.com/morocco-signs-cooperation-agreements-with-idakto-and-shareid-for-national-digital-wallet>), the [national digital ID platform](<https://www.idemia.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/idemia-national-digital-id-program-kingdom-morocco-case-study-202206.pdf>), the [Registre national de la population](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/12/04/world-bank-greenlights-70m-boost-for-morocco-s-innovative-social-protection-delivery-systems>), the [identifiant digital civil et social](<https://aujourdhui.ma/actualite/operation-extension-de-lidentifiant-digital-civil-et-social>) and [MOSIP](<https://cyberdaily.securelayer7.net/a-global-foundational-id-system-that-will-provide-identity-to-millions-of-people/>). Exchange: the [statistics office's access to the civil-status database](<https://www.hcp.ma/JMS-2025-Allocution-de-Monsieur-le-Haut-Commissaire-au-Plan_a4198.html>).

***Not held***. No count of offices registering digitally against the country's total of offices is held. The modernisation programme's period and budget, and whether an identifier is assigned at birth in practice, are not held.
