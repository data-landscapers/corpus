# Malawi

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Registration Bureau, under the Ministry of Homeland Security, [is the body responsible for civil registration](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>), by its plan of September 2025. That plan, for 2025 to 2030, [costs civil registration at MWK 66.06 billion](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>) and [names limited domestic budget allocations as a threat to the service](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>). [UNDP's basket fund spent USD 2,107,228 on digital identity and civil registration in 2025](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** In 2025 [registration officers at Traditional Authority offices began registering community births and deaths on a mobile application the Bureau calls Nzika](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>). [Kits at district registration offices register births and deaths and print certificates](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>), by the plan of September 2025.

**How the record is made.** [The district kits synchronise with the central system over the government network or mobile links](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>), by the same plan. [Nzika runs on mobile kits with biometric authentication, works offline and is integrated with the national civil registration system](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>), UNDP reported for 2025. [In some remote areas the digital birth system had not fully replaced paper by 2024, and forms were lost or not entered centrally](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** [Nzika registration moved from pilot to 87 service points during 2025](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>), and [national rollout is planned for 2026](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** UNDP counts [87 service points in five districts in 2025](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>), against [277 Traditional Authorities and 138 Sub-Traditional Authorities in 28 districts listed for expansion by 2030](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>). [Certificate printers were in 58 health facilities in 2025](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: the [National Registration and Identification System](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw/faqs/>) and its [online pre-registration](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw/faqs/>), a [digital identity wallet the Bureau calls Nzika](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-nzika-digital-id-wallet-and-removes-expiry-date-national-id>), [portable biometric registration kits](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/>), [e-KYC verification services](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>), a [digitally signed QR code and identity verification kit](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>) and [biometric SIM registration against the national identity system](<https://idtechwire.com/malawi-to-launch-mandatory-biometric-sim-registration-in-july-2025>). Outreach: the [National Birth Registration Campaign](<https://www.nrb.gov.mw/news/nrb-completes-phase-one-of-national-birth-registration-campaign/>) and the [Mass Child Registration campaign of 2022](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>). Notification: [birth notification in health facilities](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-01/malawi_nrb_strategic_plan_2025_web.pdf>). Certificate: the [Bureau's redesigned website, with pre-registration and application tracking](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>). Exchange: the national identity link to the [Malawi Social Registry](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>), its [pilot link to electronic medical records in Karonga](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/undp_idt4m_annual_report_2025_web.pdf>), the [Bureau's database integration with the Malawi Revenue Authority and the Immigration Department](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/malawi-launches-national-registration-bureau-strategic-plan-2025-2030-identity-day-and-civil-registration-day>), [government systems connected to the identification system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/malawi-advances-data-harmonization-interoperability-agenda>) and a [planned integrated system joining identification and civil registration](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226165032945/pdf/P505095-4dd2a6b0-e8a4-47cd-9f0a-203fea4ca680.pdf>).

***Not held***. No share of local offices registering digitally is published, and the health facilities registering births carry no total. The gap in remote areas has no figure.
