# Togo

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** WHO's Togo office reports that a national diagnostic of the birth and death registration system in 2024 [produced a national roadmap for the digitalisation of civil registration and vital statistics](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>) and a multisectoral task force. UNDP Togo states that in 2024 it [supported the development, deployment and operation of the digital birth registration platform](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** UNDP Togo's annual report for 2024 states that the platform, in its pilot phase at the Golfe 3 town hall, [simplified the registration of births and of other civil status acts](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>). On 27 May 2021 UNDP described an application the commune of Lacs 1 calls Kadodo as [recording births, deaths and marriages](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>).

**How the record is made.** WHO's assessment summary for Togo, on data from 2013 to 2018, records the exchange of civil registration data from local to regional and central offices as a [paper-based system](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_tgo_en.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The territorial administration ministry [launched the re-reading of law 2009-010 on the organisation of civil status on 21 August 2026](<https://levisionnaire.tg/etat-civil-au-togo-le-matglac-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-pour-une-reforme-du-cadre-juridique/>), with support from the OIF and UNECA, and [reform proposals are expected before the end of 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** UNDP Togo states that in 2024 the platform ran [in its pilot phase at one town hall, Golfe 3](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>), and that the civil status centres of the 16 communes of the Savanes region [were digitised and trained to use it](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>).
