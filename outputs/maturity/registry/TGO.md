# Togo

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** A national diagnostic of birth and death registration in 2024 [produced a national roadmap for digitalising civil registration and vital statistics](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/RAPPORT%20ANNUEL%20OMS%20TOGO%202024%20FINAL%20%20-%20OK.pdf>). In 2024 UNDP Togo [supported the development, deployment and operation of a digital birth registration platform](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>). [The digital ministry tendered a sixteen-commune digitalisation pilot in July 2026, with German financing](<https://bk.numerique.gouv.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MESPTN-Consultant-international-2.pdf>). The e-Vital Togo pilot is [a one-year project financed by Vital Strategies](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).

**Events registered digitally.** The platform, piloted at the Golfe 3 town hall, [simplified the registration of births and other civil status acts](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>). In May 2021 UNDP described Lacs 1 commune's application, Kadodo, as [recording births, deaths and marriages](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/chaque-nom-compte-la-digitalisation-des-actes-detat-civil-une-experience-du-pnud-dans-les-communes>). Under e-Vital, [health facilities send births and deaths electronically to the civil-status offices](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).

**How the record is made.** On 2013 to 2018 data, WHO recorded the exchange of civil registration data between offices as [paper-based](<https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/documents/ddi/score/country-profiles/who_score_tgo_en.pdf>). Under e-Vital, [the civil-status office verifies, prints and signs the act](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 21 August 2026 the territorial administration ministry [launched the re-reading of the 2009 civil status law](<https://levisionnaire.tg/etat-civil-au-togo-le-matglac-renforce-les-capacites-des-acteurs-pour-une-reforme-du-cadre-juridique/>). [Reform proposals are expected before the end of 2026](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>). In November 2026 [e-Vital Togo began as a pilot in four communes](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** In 2024 the platform ran [as a pilot at one town hall, Golfe 3](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>). That year the civil status centres of the 16 Savanes communes [were digitised and trained to use it](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-07/rapport_annuel_2024_pnud_togo_soft_02.pdf>). The e-Vital pilot covers [four communes: Golfe 3, Agoe-Nyive 1, Kozah 1 and Bassar 1](<https://togoscoop.tg/togo-e-vita-un-projet-pour-moderniser-lenregistrement-des-naissances-et-des-deces/>).
