# Eswatini

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Civil Registration Department of the Ministry of Home Affairs [manages the system, and the register was developed in-house with the government's National Data Centre, which hosts it](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>). A [CRVS Strategic Plan 2022-26 was launched in 2022](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>). The World Bank's appraisal of 8 June 2026 [allots US$5.5 million to digital ID and civil registration](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** Births and deaths [are captured into the National Population Register on registration, with 47,524 birth and 5,968 death registrations in 2022](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The Central Statistical Office's report for 2022 says [the registration officer fills the form by hand and a typist enters it into the register, which the service centres reach over a wide-area network, the registration being complete only once it is in the system](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The World Bank appraised the Digital Eswatini Project on 8 June 2026, [to upgrade the existing ID and civil registration systems](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>). Its document says [paper birth and death records are stored locally without centralised backups](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Central Statistical Office's 2022 report [lists 14 civil registration service centres with births and deaths registered in the register that year](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>). The Ministry of Home Affairs' report for 2024/25 gives [15 service centres and 5 hospital registration points](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>). In answers collected from October 2022 to April 2023, Eswatini told an AfDB and COMESA assessment that [entry of vital events is electronic in all its civil registration offices](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/Digitalization%20of%20Civil%20Registration%20and%20Vital%20Statistics%20in%20Africa.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: [the conversion of manual birth, marriage and death certificates at headquarters and the service centres](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>) and [a planned digitisation of paper records](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>). Notification: [paper notifications of birth and death given to families by health institutions, with no electronic movement to the registrar](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>). Outreach: [the Mopping Up campaign, which took registration into chiefdoms](<https://40years.gov.sz/home-affairs-at-the-heart-of-the-nation/>); [the Nkwe Program of community outreach in 2024/25](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20DPMO%202025.pdf>); [mass registration of vital events in Lubombo and Shiselweni in 2022](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>); [trucks equipped for mobile registration](<https://ecastats.uneca.org/asn/en-us/Sections/News-and-events/NE-Eswatini-became-the-2nd-country-to-officially-launch-phase-two>); and [planned mobile campaigns for hard-to-reach communities](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>). Identity: [national identity card enrolment at service centres from age 16](<https://parliament.gov.sz/publications/parliament_reports/docs/ANNUAL%20REPORT%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>); [identity card production at ten service centres, resumed in February 2026](<https://times.co.sz/31079/news/id-card-services-resume-in-boost-for-citizens/>); [the Home Affairs system for identity card and passport applications, down in a network blackout on 16 September 2026](<https://times.co.sz/43838/news/network-blackout-strikes-home-affairs-eptc/>); [the ministry's record of Chief Codes](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/home-affairs-ministry-urges-emaswati-to-verify-chief-codes-2/>); [the population register of the Identification Order of 1998](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/order-in-council/1998/4/eng@1998-12-01>); [the Government In Your Hand mobile app, launched 21 May 2025](<https://independentnews.co.sz/14361/science-technology/eswatini-launches-new-apps-to-ease-access-to-government-services/>); and [a planned national digital ID system](<https://idea.comesa.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IDEA-Biannual-implementation-Progress-January-to-June-2026-V-J20-FK-006.pdf>). Exchange: [the Personal Identity Number, created at birth registration, and the Ministry of Health's Client Management Information System, networked with the register](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2023-10/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202022%20%281%29.pdf>); [a link between the civil registration system and patient health records](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/Digitalization%20of%20Civil%20Registration%20and%20Vital%20Statistics%20in%20Africa.pdf>); [planned modules connecting digital ID with the civil registry](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061826123027004/pdf/BOSIB-eeab9c88-27f5-404b-bf04-d448d9780618.pdf>); and [Mchumanisi, a proposed national data exchange platform](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/mchumanisi-system-to-give-emaswati-one-stop-access-to-government-services/>).

***Not held***. No total of local offices is held for 2022, so the 14 centres are a count without a denominator, and no rural share or office figure from the last two years is held. The World Bank's June 2026 account of civil records on paper is not reconciled with the 2022 and 2023 accounts of electronic entry.
