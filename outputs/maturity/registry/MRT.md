# Mauritania

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The interior ministry's DGCT stated in November 2022 that [civil status acts are the exclusive competence of ANRPTS, the population register agency](<https://www.dgct.mr/modules/cms/posts/162-Rapport%20MDP_MIDEC.pdf>). The Prime Minister [inaugurated the agency's integrated system for the population register and secure documents on 3 August 2021](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/119459>); the agency's director general [put its cost at 12,157,616 euros](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/119459>).

**Events registered digitally.** Law 2025-011 of 24 February 2025 [requires a birth to be declared within 60 clear days at the Centre d'Accueil des Citoyens](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>) and has civil status acts signed electronically. Law 2011-003 [has births and deaths declared at the centre and recorded in the central database](<https://www.coursupreme.mr/docs/loi0032011.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** Article 18 of Law 2011-003 provides that [declarations of civil status events are recorded immediately in the database of the central system](<https://www.coursupreme.mr/docs/loi0032011.pdf>). Under the law of 24 February 2025, [heads of centres receive declarations only at the centres, where they are registered](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Civil Status Code [was amended on 24 February 2025](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>), treating the recorded data as data of the Registre National Biométrique des Populations.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The ANRPTS director general said on 3 August 2021 that [153 citizen reception centres had been received and equipped out of 168 to be built in communes and administrative centres](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/119459>), to receive civil status declarations, enrol people and hand over secure documents.
