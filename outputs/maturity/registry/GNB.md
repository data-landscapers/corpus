# Guinea-Bissau

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The system the country calls SIREC [was implemented at the Ministry of Justice's Direção-Geral de Identificação Civil, Registo e Notariado](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/guine-bissau-evento-de-encerramento-do-projeto-gestdoc-e-lancamento-dos-sistemas-passe-e-sirec>), launched in January 2025. The digital transformation strategy approved in March 2025 [names the civil registry among the services to consolidate and expand](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>). UNDP reports for 2025 that [the Swiss Confederation, the European Union and Portuguese Cooperation financed it](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** Officials at the Santa Luzia Civil Registry Office in Bissau [record births, marriages and deaths directly on SIREC](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>), UNDP said in April 2026. Health workers at registration posts in hospitals and health centres [register births on a tablet](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>), UNICEF reported in September 2025.

**How the record is made.** Santa Luzia [has replaced paper register books with workstations connected to the national platform](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>), with records entered directly and held centrally, on UNDP's account of April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In the first quarter of 2026 Santa Luzia [became the first civil registry office in the country to operate entirely digitally](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>). In June 2026 a mobile caravan [was to take birth registration on SIREC to 16 communities](<https://ang.gw/pnud-e-uniao-europeia-lancam-terceira-edicao-da-candonga-di-justica-2026/>) in Cacheu, Oio, Bafatá and Gabu.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** UNDP names [one office, Santa Luzia, as entirely digital](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>) in April 2026. UNICEF said in September 2025 that [digital registration is available in hospitals and health centres of Bissau, Tombali and Quinara](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>). The statistics office [counts 13 conservatórias and 80 registry posts](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf>) in its yearbook for 2018 to 2022.

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: [the digitisation of civil registry books](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/_undp_g.b_newsletter_jan-mar_2026_eng_1.pdf>), over 2.4 million records in 11,745 registers by April 2026, and [GESTDOC](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>). Certificate: [requests for birth, marriage and death entries from any location](<https://ang.gw/justica-servicos-de-identificacao-civil-registos-e-notariado-serao-digitalizados-e-informatizados/>), announced in June 2025. Outreach: the mobile operations the country calls [Candonga di Justiça](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>), the [Registo Móvel](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/guine-bissau-evento-de-encerramento-do-projeto-gestdoc-e-lancamento-dos-sistemas-passe-e-sirec>) vehicle, [birth registration in vaccination campaigns](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>) and [birth registration at school enrolment](<https://ang.gw/ministerios-da-justica-e-educacao-assinam-acordo-de-parceria-para-emissao-de-registo-de-nascimento-no-ato-de-matricula/>). Identity: the [biometric national identity card production centres](<https://www.semlex.com/en/2026/02/11/semlex-guinee-bissau-renforce-lacces-a-lidentification-biometrique>), the [ECOWAS biometric identity card](<https://ang.gw/regioes-mulheres-comerciantes-de-zonas-fronteiricas-recebem-bilhete-de-identidadebi-biometrico-da-cedeao-em-sao-domingos/>), the planned [legal framework for digital identity and authentication platform](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>), and the [biometric registration kits and biometric passport system](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/61714f214ed04bcd6e9623ad0e215897-0400012021/related/P177016084979202b08dd501a5690c82506.pdf>). Enabling: [national connectivity](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2908-131059-deploiement-de-la-fibre-optique-la-guinee-bissau-veut-apprendre-du-tchad>), with no national backbone in August 2025. Exchange: the planned [interoperability of SIREC with the identity card, voter register, social protection and judiciary](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-07/undp_guinea-bissau-annual_report_2025.pdf>), the [unique citizen identification number](<https://sirec.web.ua.pt/Home/About>) SIREC is to manage, the planned [digital platform supporting voter registration](<http://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/gbs235634.pdf>), [health workers registering on the registry's behalf](<https://www.unicef.org/guineabissau/pt/historias/nascer-com-identidade-nascimentos-registados-nos-servi%C3%A7os-de-sa%C3%BAde-na-guin%C3%A9-bissau>), the [national statistical report on civil registration](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-08/undp_annual_report_2024.pdf>), and [RapidPro and INNOSTATS](<https://www.stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/IV_RGPH/rgph1/projecoes/Anuario%20Final_de_Estatisticas_Vitais.pdf>) for vital statistics.

***Not held***. The number of offices and posts entering records digitally, against the country's total, is not held. Nor is the platform used at the health-service posts, or whether it is connected to the national register.
