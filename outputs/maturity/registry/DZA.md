# Algeria

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Law 14-08 of 9 August 2014 creates the national automated civil-status register under the Ministry of the Interior and Local Authorities](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2014/F2014049.pdf>). A university article published on 10 October 2025 [places the register within the e-municipality programme of the national e-Algeria strategy, which it dates 2008 to 2028](<https://imcra-az.org/uploads/public_files/2025-10/81246.pdf>). The ministry's presentation of July 2026 [lists the availability of financing among the levers of the digitalisation](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The same presentation states that [since 2018 each newborn has a birth act and a national identification number at the end of its registration, the number generated automatically from the register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). The ministry's deck of 19 June 2019 [shows the register holding three databases, of births, deaths and marriages](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The 2019 deck places [entry, control and update at commune level, with 48 wilaya databases for error correction and a national database at the ministry](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>). Under the 2014 law [each commune still inscribes acts on three paper registers kept in duplicate and transmits a digitised copy of every act to the national register](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2014/F2014049.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In July 2026 the ministry presented the register as [its own, created in 2014, with unified national governance of civil-status data](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The 2019 deck shows [1,541 commune databases where entry, control and update are done](<https://www.id4africa.com/2019_event/presentations/InF3/4-Abderrazak-Henni-MOI-Algeria.pdf>). The 2025 article states that [paper documentation persists alongside the digital services](<https://imcra-az.org/uploads/public_files/2025-10/81246.pdf>).
