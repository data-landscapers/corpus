# Ghana

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Births and Deaths Registry, under the Ministry of Local Government, [develops and administers the births and deaths registration system under Act 1027 of 2020](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Evidence%20Generation%20for%20Suicide%20Prevention%20in%20Ghana.%20A%20Situational%20Analysis.pdf>). A [Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Strategic Plan for 2025 to 2030 was validated at a workshop in July 2025](<https://www.adomonline.com/births-and-deaths-registry-validates-plan-to-digitise-national-vital-statistics/>). The [2025 budget gives births and deaths registration GHS 7,409,170, of which GHS 6,909,170 is internally generated funds and nothing is from donors](<https://www.mofep.gov.gh/sites/default/files/budget-estimates/2025/2025-Budget-by-Sub-Prog_011_MLGRD.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** Births in Ablekuma West, a municipal district of Greater Accra, are [registered on the Registry's digital registration platform, 1,722 of them from March to October 2024](<https://getinthepicture.org/system/files/sites/default/files/C1_Session%204.2_Owusu_Paper_0.pdf>). The Registry's [electronic database is the source of its count of 50,992 deaths registered in 2022](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** In 2022 a district registration officer [entered a death in the register and completed a paper form, which was scanned into an electronic database at the national office](<https://bdr.gov.gh/storage/formidable/7/STATISTICAL-REPORT-ON-DEATH-REGISTRATION-2022.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In a 2026 report the Eastern Regional Registrar said [officers are expected to complete birth registrations digitally in communities and that poor internet connectivity is a major obstacle](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1530759/unicef-bdr-and-kuami-eugene-push-for-improved.html>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The [two registry centres of Ablekuma West, Dansoman and Gbegbeyise, registered 1,045 and 677 births on the digital platform from March to October 2024](<https://getinthepicture.org/system/files/sites/default/files/C1_Session%204.2_Owusu_Paper_0.pdf>). In April 2025 the Acting Registrar said the Registry was [taking steps to digitise all offices](<https://gna.org.gh/2025/04/all-16-regions-to-receive-new-office-complexes-bdr-acting-registrar/>).

Certificates: a [digital system issuing certified copies at regional offices](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-births-and-deaths-registry-receives-security-papers-begins-clearing-backlog.html>), the [e-Services platform](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/FEB-2024-PRESS-BRIEFING_MOCD.pdf>) and a [one-stop portal for e-services](<https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Reimagining-identity-ecosystems-in-Sub-Saharan-Africa-with-mobile.pdf>). Enabling: the [Ghana Rural Telephony and Digital Inclusion Project](<http://www.commodafrica.com/17-11-2020-une-facilite-de-eu155-millions-de-la-china-exim-bank-pour-la-connectivite-rurale-au-ghana>).
