# Niger

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** [The civil-status system belongs to the Interior Ministry's Direction Générale de l'état civil, des Migrations et des Réfugiés](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>), the ministry said on 11 August 2026. [A civil registration strategic plan covers 2023 to 2027](<https://actuniger.com/societe/20979-etat-civil-le-niger-revoit-sa-strategie-2023-2027-pour-eradiquer-linvisibilite-de-32-des-enfants.html>). [The computerisation announced that day sits under the WURI-Niger project, which the World Bank finances](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>).

**Events registered digitally.** [The ministry's database for the five arrondissements of Niamey holds 72,459 births and 6,524 deaths registered in 2023](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/etat_civil/rapport_etat_civil_2023.pdf>), by the statistics office.

**How the record is made.** [Events are declared at the centres and passed to the commune, where agents key the acts on machines connected to a server at the ministry's directorate, and each validated act is saved there](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/etat_civil/rapport_etat_civil_2023.pdf>), the statistics office wrote of 2023. [Acts are printed on secured paper from the computerised system](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1143-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-22-avril-2026>), by the government's communiqué of 22 April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** [An ordinance of 22 April 2026 provides for the national civil-status register](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1143-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-22-avril-2026>), and [its decree followed on 18 May 2026](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1171-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-lundi-18-mai-2026>). [On 11 August 2026 the ministry announced the computerisation of all communes and declaration centres and a new platform as works to come](<https://anp.ne/lancement-a-tahoua-dune-tournee-nationale-de-vulgarisation-des-textes-juridiques-et-des-operations-denregistrement-intermediaire-au-registre-national-de-la-population/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** [The computerised system was in production in 101 of 266 communes and communal arrondissements in December 2024](<https://stat-niger.org/wp-content/uploads/etat_civil/rapport_etat_civil_2023.pdf>), by the statistics office, and [the government counted 102 communes on 22 April 2026](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1143-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-22-avril-2026>). [Only towns in urban areas had electronic civil registration databases in 2023](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/Digitalization%20of%20Civil%20Registration%20and%20Vital%20Statistics%20in%20Africa.pdf>).

***Not held***. Digital registration of deaths is not established outside Niamey. Whether the declaration centres in villages and health facilities are computerised is not stated, and the gap between urban and rural offices has no figure.
