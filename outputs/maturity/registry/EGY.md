# Egypt

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The development plan for fiscal year 2026/27, as Youm7 reported it on 2 August 2026, [credits the Ministry of Health and Population and the bodies concerned with developing the electronic birth and death registration system](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/2/%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-2026-2027-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9/7497444>) and [names it within the digital-transformation axis of the national family development project](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/8/2/%D8%AE%D8%B7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%86%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9-2026-2027-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9/7497444>). [Law 143 of 1994 gives the Civil Status Organization of the Ministry of Interior the registration of births and deaths](<https://mksegypt.org/ar/laws/23813>).

**Events registered digitally.** Egypt's presentation to the 2026 APAI-CRVS registrars-general meeting states that [a central database for births and deaths runs on a secure platform and directly updates the statistics office's population clock](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** A conference paper on the system implemented in 2017 states that [data entry is done at the health office, which prints the birth or death certificate once entry is complete, with the offices linked online to a central database](<http://www.au.int/sites/default/files/newsevents/workingdocuments/37474-wd-crvs-v-the_electronic_birth_and_death_registration_system_in_egypt.pdf>). The 2026 presentation describes [automated health offices feeding the central database](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** An article of 20 January 2026 reports that the health ministry [instructed all health affairs directorates to unify and improve the quality of data entered on the automated births and deaths registration system at health offices](<https://www.newsroom.info/263435>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The 2026 presentation gives [full nationwide automation of 4,571 health offices across the 27 governorates](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). The planning ministry's report of 2 August 2025 says [4,666 health offices are electronically linked](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=6465>).
