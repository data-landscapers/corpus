# The Gambia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The [Ministry of Health holds the mandate for birth registration, under the Registrar General of Births and Deaths](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). The [system was deployed under a World Bank lending operation that ended in August 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). A [National Digital ID and Digital Transformation Strategy for 2023 to 2028 went to a validation workshop in May 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The Registrar General said that by June 2026 [over 1.4 million people were registered in the system he calls the eCRVS and issued a birth certificate](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>). A 2025 World Bank diagnostic has [its birth module in operation and a marriage module developed](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The diagnostic describes [a centralised register, with registration carried out locally by Ministry of Health staff in each district and civil registration offices holding digitalised registers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>). [Before 2022 the processes were manual and paper-based](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A [Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Bill had its first reading in the National Assembly on 5 March 2026](<https://www.voicegambia.com/2026/03/06/civil-registration-bill-2026-receives-first-reading-in-national-assembly/>). Registrations on the system [rose from 1.17 million in February 2023 to over 1.4 million in June 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The diagnostic counts [79 civil registration offices with digitalised registers issuing birth certificates in 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>), beside [258 health facility outlets where Ministry of Health staff carry out registration](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: the [Biometric National Identity Card](<https://standard.gm/new-biometric-id-card-issuance-starts/>), the [National Identity Management System, named GAM ID](<https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/07/margins-delivers-first-gam-id-president-barrow-is-inaugural-recipient/>), the [Semlex biometric identification system](<https://dailyobservergambia.com/gid-tells-court-it-cannot-access-semlex-id-data/>), the [Gambia Biometric Identification System of 2009](<https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657>), the [eID system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-gambia.pdf>), the [proposed National Digital ID System](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/9c7cefe5-80cf-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>) and the [Biometric Passport](<https://gambia.gov.gm/passport-office/>). Archive: the [digitisation of paper birth and death registers in six of the seven health regions](<https://moh.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/FINAL-NATIONAL-HEALTH-POLICY_28-FEB-2024.pdf>). Outreach: the [mass registration campaign of August 2022 to February 2023](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>). Enabling: [satellite internet in health facilities](<https://mofea.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/2026-Budget-Speech_Dec_2.pdf>) and [user devices for registration centres](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/550301641909565798/pdf/Gambia-Essential-Health-Services-Strengthening-Project-Additional-Financing.pdf>). Exchange: the [integration of the eCRVS with the electronic ID system, and its interoperability with the health human resource and health insurance systems](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025103014657/pdf/P502279-e8627dee-4568-45e7-bb51-9a1aea1ff040.pdf>), the [interoperability of the social registry, education systems, CRVS and health insurance system sought under a World Bank project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099112925154035950/pdf/P179233-79b99711-b6ff-4c20-b5cb-2d5ef7838226.pdf>), the [planned integration of the social registry with the civil registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061424110583308/pdf/BOSIB-e80861d4-2c05-4433-890b-f23960cfa011.pdf>), the [proposed data exchange from the civil registration database to the social registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/730471557474095639/txt/Building-The-Gambias-Social-Registry-Final-Draft-Framework-for-the-Design-and-Road-Map-Leading-to-Implementation.txt>), the [planned integration of the National Identity Management System with civil registration](<https://www.voicegambia.com/2026/07/01/barrow-launches-national-identity-management-system-becomes-first-enrollee/>), the [proposed registration at birth under the digital ID programme](<https://policies.gov.gm/d/9c7cefe5-80cf-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1>) and the [linkage of the education and health information systems](<https://gambiana.com/mobse-and-health-ministry-sign-deal-to-link-education-and-health-data-systems/>).

***Not held***. The total number of civil registration offices is not held, so the 79 have no share, and no source states that the record is keyed at the registration point when the birth is registered. No death module is in operation and no domestic budget line is stated.
