# Mali

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** An ordinance of 14 February 2025 [creates the Agence nationale de Sécurisation des Actes d'Etat civil, a national public establishment, to develop and maintain the systems that manage civil status acts](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2025/mali-jo-2025-05.pdf>), with an initial endowment from the State. UNICEF says it [has supported the Government's efforts to digitise birth registration since 2022](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>).

**Events registered digitally.** A platform approved by December 2024 [is to consolidate births, deaths and marriages](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>), which UNICEF's representative describes as the first fully digital platform for civil status events.

**How the record is made.** In July 2026 the head of digitalisation at the National Directorate of Civil Status [described the birth chain as paper](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>): community health workers fill village notebooks, the declaration agent sends a slip to the civil status centre, and the officer there draws up the act.

**Last twelve months.** In July 2026 the directorate [named complete digitalisation of the system as a priority](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** By December 2024 the National Directorate of Civil Status [had identified ten pilot areas for the platform](<https://www.unicef.org/mali/en/stories/celebration-progress-birth-registration-mali>), taking in civil registration centres, health facilities and courts.

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification: the [community declaration model of village declaration agents](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>) and [DISK-Mali](<https://www.populationmedicine.eu/From-policy-to-practice-facilitating-the-notification-of-perinatal-deaths-and-births,227439,0,2.html>). Outreach: the [UNHCR-supported documentation of people at risk of statelessness](<https://reliefweb.int/report/mali/unhcr-applauds-malis-adoption-landmark-law-protect-stateless-people>). Certificates: [digital payment in civil status centres](<https://fassoactu.com/2026/07/31/transparence-financiere-les-collectivites-sommees-daccelerer-la-digitalisation-des-paiements/>) and the [platform for citizens abroad](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/chad-mali-senegal-pursue-birth-registration-reforms-to-close-gaps>). Identity: the [RAVEC census and the NINA](<https://id4africa.com/2016/presentations/1-1-3_MATD_SY_Abdou.pdf>), [Mali Kura Biométrie](<https://malikonews.com/mali-mali-kura-biometrie-un-nouveau-systeme-numerique-souverain-des-donnees-ravec/>), the [Répertoire national d'Identification des Personnes](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2025/mali-jo-2025-05.pdf>), the [carte nationale d'identité biométrique sécurisée](<https://www.maliweb.net/societe/carte-didentite-biometrique-du-mali-la-confection-est-assuree-par-une-societe-malienne-et-non-une-entreprise-allemande-3005191.html>), the [cahier de recensement](<https://gouvernement.ml/communique-du-conseil-des-ministres-cm-n2026-29-sgg/>), the [biometric registration of refugees](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/71934176-0890-4bed-8159-9c9c145773bc/Operational%20Update%20UNHCR_%20Mali_%20Q2-%202026.pdf>) and the [E-AES biometric passport](<https://idtechwire.com/mali-launches-e-aes-biometric-passport-taps-chinas-emptech-in-digital-sovereignty-move>). Exchange: the [NINA written on the birth act](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2006/mali-jo-2006-28.pdf>) and the [transmission of civil status changes to the national repertoire](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2006/mali-jo-2006-33.pdf>).

***Not held***. No office is shown entering an event digitally, and whether the pilots have started is not held. No current plan and no budget line for the system is held.
