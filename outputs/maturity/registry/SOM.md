# Somalia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** [Law No. 46, published on 5 April 2026, charges a Directorate under the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation with creating and running a digital registration system with a central database](<https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2026/sharciga_Dad-dhigista_iyo_Tirakoobka_Xaaladaha_Madaniga.pdf>). A government roadmap, reported on 11 August 2025, [plans to scale the service to every district, subject to funding and infrastructure, and UNICEF, with the government and Germany, supported the foundational work](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>).

**Events registered digitally.** The ministry said on 10 August 2025 that the [platform enables local administrations to register births, deaths, marriages and divorces digitally](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). OpenCRVS's country page, published in January 2026, counts [28,391 completed birth registrations](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).

**How the record is made.** The ministry announced on 10 August 2025 that [local administrations in Jubbaland, Southwest, Hirshabelle, Galmudug and Northeastern State register events on the platform and all records are stored in a centralised national database](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). OpenCRVS's page, published in January 2026, says the system [captures births at the point of occurrence in the districts and linked hospitals](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).

**Last twelve months.** The platform, which the ministry calls the Unified Digital Civil Registration Service, was [launched as a pilot in 19 districts on 10 August 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** OpenCRVS's country page, published in January 2026, states that the [system operates in 30 districts and is linked to 160 hospitals](<https://www.opencrvs.org/about-us/country-stories/somalia>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification: [hospital birth notification](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2090364.html>), the [notification of births and deaths by health institutions and community health workers planned under the 2021 policy](<https://moifar.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/CRVS-Policy-.pdf>) and the [UNICEF-supported birth registration pilot in Burao district](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/185701524689472792/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Somalia.pdf>). Outreach: the [Baidoa national ID registration campaign for displaced persons](<https://undpsom.medium.com/baidoas-fast-tracked-cost-effective-and-scalable-legal-id-model-for-idps-0dbb909900eb>). Identity: the [national identification system of the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA)](<https://en.goobjoog.com/somalia-moves-to-link-national-id-with-public-services/>), its [National Identity Management System](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Jan/199818/somalia_launches_ambitious_civil_registration_overhaul.aspx>), the [Digital National Identification System set out in Regulation 158](<https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/cabinet-decisions/2024/Xeer-nidaamiyaha_Sharciga_Aqoonsiga_Diiwaan-galinta_Dadwaynaha.pdf>), the [eAqoonsi app](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/somalia-pushes-for-stronger-adoption-of-eaqoonsi-digital-id-app>), the [Hubiye eKYC platform](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>), the [National ID Registration Initiative for Internally Displaced Persons](<https://nira.gov.so/news/nira-launches-national-id-registration-initiative-for-internally-displaced-persons-in-somalia>), the [NIRA service centre in Boondheere](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Apr/201207/somalia_opens_national_id_center_in_boondheere_to_expand_access_to_services.aspx>), the [family register under Law No. 46](<https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/legislation/2026/sharciga_Dad-dhigista_iyo_Tirakoobka_Xaaladaha_Madaniga.pdf>) and the [Mogadishu and Puntland ID cards and the passport system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/185701524689472792/ID4D-Country-Diagnostic-Somalia.pdf>). Exchange: the [interfaces from the civil registration platform to health, education, national ID and immigration services](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>) and the [planned linkage of the civil registration system with the national ID system](<https://moifar.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/CRVS-Policy-.pdf>).

***Not held***. No total of districts or offices is held, so no share can be read, and only births are counted as registered. The roadmap carries no period, no domestic budget line is stated, and nothing dated falls inside the last twelve months.
