# Burundi

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The interior ministry [presents the civil registration reform and its pilot digitalisation as the government's, with the World Bank, UNICEF, UNECA and UNDP as supporting partners](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>), in its presentation of 18 July 2026. Its director general for territorial administration [put the platform's cost at about BIF 15 billion](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-unveils-biometric-national-id-system-awaits-law-before-full-rollout/>) on 6 May 2026.

**Events registered digitally.** The project launched at Rumonge on 19 July 2024 [is for the digital registration of births and deaths at the commune's civil status service, at its seat and in its zones](<https://www.iwacu-burundi.org/region-sud-rumonge-enregistrement-numerique-a-letat-civil/>).

**How the record is made.** The ministry's director general told the African registrars general on 18 July 2026 that [the majority of civil-status acts are still recorded in physical registers and that Burundi has no single national database of them](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). The ministry's director general said on 6 May 2026 that [the legal framework still rests on manual registration procedures](<https://burundi-eco.com/la-carte-didentite-biometrique-en-marche-entre-ambitions-et-defis/>).

**Last twelve months.** On 6 May 2026 the ministry [presented a digital platform for civil registration and biometric identity card production, its infrastructure nearly complete and awaiting a decree](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative>). On 11 August 2026 it stated that [the government continues to scale up the platform](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/13/le-burundi-veut-construire-un-etat-plus-proche-des-citoyens-plus-efficace-et-inclusif/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The ministry's presentation of 18 July 2026 [records a pilot digitalisation project in 12 communes](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). On 10 August 2026 [thirteen communes had a biometric registration system](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>), with [civil registration digitalised in pilot communes](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: the statistics institute's [Saisie numérique des faits d'état civil 2025](<https://www.insbu.bi/actualitesEtEvenements>). Notification: the planned [notification through chefs de collines](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/burundi-l-etat-civil-se-numerise-pour-renforcer-l-identite-des-citoyens>) of events at home. Outreach: [late registrations and registration in the Congolese refugee camps](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). Identity: the [national digital identity system](<https://armp.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PPM-finances-budgetaire-2026-2027-final.docx-Revise1.pdf>) to be procured in 2026/27, the [carte nationale d'identité biométrique](<https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>), the [Fichier National de l'Identification and Carte Nationale d'Identité](<https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite>), the [national ID smartcard pilot](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/337501535031584335/pdf/129621-ACS.pdf>) launched in 2013, and UNHCR's [proGres](<https://help.unhcr.org/burundi/asile/>) and [BIMS](<https://help.unhcr.org/burundi/asile/>). Exchange: the [interoperability between civil registration and the health services](<https://abpinfo.bi/2026/08/13/le-burundi-veut-construire-un-etat-plus-proche-des-citoyens-plus-efficace-et-inclusif/>) and the [interoperability module in the platform for the statistics institute and health facilities](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

***Not held***. No source says the system is past its pilot, how the digital record is made in the pilot communes or where it is held. No plan with a current period and no domestic budget line for the platform is on record.
