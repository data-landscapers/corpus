# Mozambique

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Directorate of Registries and Notaries [holds the main database of the system it calls e-RCEV](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>). A ten-year plan, [SIRCEV 2019-2029, targets 80 per cent birth registration of children under five](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>), the National Director said in 2025. Cooperation partners [support 100 new posts in maternities, mainly with computer equipment](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/registo-civil-chega-a-mais-100-maternidades/>), reported in September 2026.

**Events registered digitally.** The registrar's portal, read in August 2026, gives [the registration of births and deaths as the system's initial focus](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/Home/About>). UNICEF's evaluators state that [births and deaths were registered electronically as of October 2018](<https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=16474&fileID=45640>). The National Director listed [registering deaths in the community as a current challenge](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>) in 2025.

**How the record is made.** District registration offices [connect to the central platform and enter registrations directly into the central database](<https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=16474&fileID=45640>), UNICEF's evaluators stated in 2021. At 534 community registration points [births and deaths are taken on forms that the district office then processes](<https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=16474&fileID=45640>).

**Last twelve months.** Jornal Notícias reported on 24 September 2026 that [the justice sector is installing 100 civil registration posts in health units with maternity wards](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/registo-civil-chega-a-mais-100-maternidades/>), beside about 250 birth registration posts already working, about 25 per cent of some 1,000 health units.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The National Director of Registries and Notaries said in 2025 that [all 68 civil registration conservatories are digitised](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Arnaldo-ENglish-Reuniao-de-Lanc%C2%B8amento-Regional-do-Quadro-de-Programa-Estrate%C2%B4gico-para-o-Fortalecimento-do-RCEV-ENGL-1-002-en-GB.pdf>). By 2018 the system [was functional in 75 districts and 183 administrative posts](<https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=16474&fileID=45640>), UNICEF's evaluators state.
