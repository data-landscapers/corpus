# Uganda

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA), under the Minister of Internal Affairs, [holds all of Uganda's civil registration systems](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>), it said on 18 July 2026. Its [Strategic Plan III for 2025/26 to 2029/30 was finalised in 2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/National%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Authority_17.pdf>), and its [2026/27 budget estimate of UGX 155.948 billion is all Government of Uganda money](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/National%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Authority_17.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** NIRA's system, which it calls the Mobile Vital Records System, [registered 498,439 live births and 22,182 deaths in 2024](<https://www.ubos.org/wp-content/uploads/publications/Uganda-Vital-Statistics-Report-2024.pdf>), on the statistics bureau's figures. On 18 July 2026 NIRA said it had [built a births and deaths module on its identity system that issues an identity number once a birth is notified](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** A paper notification form is [entered into the web-based system at district offices and health facilities, and each entry is recorded directly in the central register](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/images/CRVS_Webinar_Uganda_e_WEB.pdf>), on a 2020 brief.

**Last twelve months.** In 2025/26 to 31 December 2025 NIRA [registered 123,526 births and 9,935 deaths](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/National%20Identification%20and%20Registration%20Authority_17.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The system was [used in at least 112 of 135 districts and in 222 of the 5,230 health facilities that handle births](<https://crvssystems.ca/sites/default/files/assets/images/CRVS_Webinar_Uganda_e_WEB.pdf>), an IDRC brief said in 2020.
