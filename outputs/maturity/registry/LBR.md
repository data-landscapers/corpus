# Liberia

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Health [registers and certifies births and deaths through its Bureau of Vital Statistics](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), by its report for 2024, and that year [developed a National Birth and Death Registration Policy](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>). [UNICEF, the World Bank and other partners helped it decentralise birth registration](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), and the Ministry says [budget constraint limits its ability to sustain the birth registration centres](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The Ministry's report for 2024 says [birth and death registration have been integrated into the DHIS2 platform](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>). As of 2023, [93,031 birth registrations had been recorded in DHIS2](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), which the Ministry gives as 67 per cent of all birth registrations and certifications. UNICEF Liberia calls the system the [Birth Registration Information Management System](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>).

**How the record is made.** County birth registration teams [register and certify births online on a web-based platform](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), the Ministry reports for 2024, collecting application forms from health facilities and returning certificates to them. In May 2024 the World Bank found [most services at the 15 County Service Centers, birth registration for ages 0 to 12 among them, remained paper-based](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In 2024 the Ministry [trained over 45 birth registration staff on the platform and procured 35 antivirus licences for county birth registration computers](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Ministry says [birth registration centres have been established in all 15 counties since 2010](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>). UNICEF Liberia in December 2024 counted birth registration services in [all 15 counties and 58 centres](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Identity: the [National Biometric Identification System](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>) of the National Identification Registry, its [Identity Query Portal and e-Verification platform](<https://emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/Executive%20Order%20147%20%20-%20Mandatory%20Enrollment%20of%20all%20Citizens%20and%20the%20use%20of%20NBIC_0.pdf>) and a [UNICEF pilot enrolling children](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202506/liberia-needs-more-funding-to-widen-national-id-coverage>). Exchange: a proposed [NID-CRVS system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>), a [UNICEF-supported pilot integrating birth registration with the national ID system](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/f00c8c982f5308548d0f81dd427e550c-0360012025/original/digital-liberia-event-book.pdf>), a planned [government service bus](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>) and an [interface for data sharing between the identification system and the health ministry's birth and death records](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps2/PS2-4-Liberia-Final.pdf>). Enabling: the [County Service Centers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>). Certificate: the [eLiberia Portal](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099052924164514688/pdf/BOSIB-0aa50b62-6e1c-4715-bcbb-a6d38f4bdc2e.pdf>). Outreach: the Ministry's [birth registration campaigns](<https://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Ministry-of-Health-Annual-Report.pdf>), UNICEF-supported [mobile registration and certification campaigns](<https://www.unicef.org/liberia/press-releases/new-era-inclusion-millions-children-gain-legal-identity>) and a planned [national birth registration census for children under five](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_LiberiaSnapshot_e.pdf>). Notification: the [Community Health Assistant program for the registration of community events](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourcefiles/CRVS_LiberiaSnapshot_e.pdf>) and health facilities' [Birth Notification](<http://moh.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/Birth-and-Death-Registration-Policy_Dec_18_2017.pdf>).

***Not held***. How many of the 58 centres enter births on the platform is not known, and no source gives a total of local offices. Nothing is dated inside the last twelve months, and whether the 2024 policy names the platform is not stated.
