# Central African Republic

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The territorial administration ministry, through its Direction Générale de la Décentralisation et de l'État Civil, [is preparing the deployment of a civil registration application with CIVIPOL as technical operator](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/07/20/centrafrique-le-matddl-lance-la-numerisation-des-documents-detat-civil-pour-securiser-lidentite-nationale/>). The Politique Nationale de l'État Civil was [adopted by the Head of State on 4 April 2025](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/07/20/centrafrique-le-matddl-lance-la-numerisation-des-documents-detat-civil-pour-securiser-lidentite-nationale/>). The supporting MODERO project [is financed by the European Union](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-car.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The software under development [is to cover births, marriages and deaths](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-car.pdf>), the director general said on 18 July 2026. UNICEF's profile of 2017 gives [births and deaths as processed by hand on paper](<https://data.unicef.org/crvs/central-african-republic/>).

**How the record is made.** The director general described [an all-paper system on 18 July 2026, with 70% of civil registration centres without electricity](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-car.pdf>). [Civil registration registers were distributed to communes of the Ouham, and registers for declaring births to the maternity of Bossangoa university hospital](<https://www.undp.org/fr/central-african-republic/actualites/bossangoa-le-president-de-la-rca-decouvre-une-mairie-au-service-des-citoyens-rehabilitee-avec-le-financement-de>), UNDP reported in April 2026.

**Last twelve months.** In July 2026 the ministry [was preparing to deploy the application, which was not yet deployed](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2026/07/20/centrafrique-le-matddl-lance-la-numerisation-des-documents-detat-civil-pour-securiser-lidentite-nationale/>). [An operational map of 45 centres had been validated](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-car.pdf>) by 18 July 2026.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The country's delegate told an Abidjan symposium in December 2024 that [the digitalisation of civil status was at the pilot phase and should extend to 40 localities in 2025](<https://digitalmag.ci/digitalisation-de-letat-civil-lexperience-du-burkina-faso-de-la-centrafrique-et-du-congo/>).
