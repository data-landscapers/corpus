# Angola

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Ministry of Justice and Human Rights [runs civil registration through its directorate DNIRN and introduced RCI, as it names its civil registration system, in 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824094544617/pdf/P179485-40c865ab-d980-432a-acf1-940f22b02d4c.pdf>). [Presidential Decree 267/24 of 28 November 2024 approved a plan to December 2026 for a single database fed daily by civil registration](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2024/decreto-presidencial-n-o-267-24-de-28-de-novembro/>). Nascer com Registo, the government's maternity registration programme, [is financed by the European Union](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>), ANGOP reported on 4 March 2026.

**Events registered digitally.** Births [have been registered in RCI since 2023 at civil registration posts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824094544617/pdf/P179485-40c865ab-d980-432a-acf1-940f22b02d4c.pdf>). Angola told the registrars general on 18 July 2026 that [biographic and biometric data are collected when the birth entry is issued](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** In RCI [the birth is entered at the post, in a system with a centralised database](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/d1c06154-9015-4e69-9112-ccf5b7092b4a/download>). The older SIRC [has no central database](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824094544617/pdf/P179485-40c865ab-d980-432a-acf1-940f22b02d4c.pdf>) and [the majority of civil registration units still rely on paper records](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824094544617/pdf/P179485-40c865ab-d980-432a-acf1-940f22b02d4c.pdf>), as of 2024.

**Last twelve months.** Novo Jornal reported on 5 May 2026 that [identification and civil registration posts in almost the whole country had been without their system for four months](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/minjusdh-diz-que-falhas-do-sistema-nos-postos-de-identificacao-e-registo-civil-que-ja-duram-ha-meses-se-devem-as-quebras-de-telecomunicacoes-71601.html>). [A civil registration post opened on 3 March 2026 in the provincial hospital's maternity at Mbanza Kongo](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** RCI [is in Luanda's registries and in one post each in Huambo, Benguela and Lubango](<https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/d1c06154-9015-4e69-9112-ccf5b7092b4a/download>), as of 2024. The World Bank's diagnostic [counts 39 civil registration units in Luanda province and at least 255 in the country in 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062824094544617/pdf/P179485-40c865ab-d980-432a-acf1-940f22b02d4c.pdf>).
