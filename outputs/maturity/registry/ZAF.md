# South Africa

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The Department of Home Affairs [itself rolls out the online birth registration system to health facilities](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/AnnualReports/DHA-Annual-Report-2024-25.pdf>), on its 2024/25 annual report. Its amended annual performance plan for 2025/26 [targets nine health facilities](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/AnnualReports/Updated-Revised-APP-2025-26_3Nov25.pdf>) and [allocates R21 million to the rollout](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/AnnualReports/Updated-Revised-APP-2025-26_3Nov25.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** The department reports that [its online birth registration system was available at 242 health facilities by the end of 2024/25](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/AnnualReports/DHA-Annual-Report-2024-25.pdf>). Its Annual Performance Plan 2026/27 [schedules the digitalising of death registration for 2028/29](<https://pmg.org.za/files/2/DHA_APP_2026-27_Final_26_Mar_2026.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** The National Department of Health states for 2024/25 that [births are registered and certificates issued at the health facility, by Home Affairs officials posted there, through an online system](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/NDoH-2024-25-Annual-Report-_-19-September-2025.pdf>). Home Affairs stated in September 2025 that [births are captured onto the National Population Register at local offices and health facilities from the paper Notice of Birth form](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/AnnualReports/Updated-Revised-APP-2025-26_3Nov25.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The department [reported progress on online birth registration in the fourth quarter of 2025/26](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/43115/>). The Portfolio Committee on Home Affairs reports that [the 2025/26 target of automated birth registration at 47 health facilities was missed and the rollout deferred to 2026/27](<https://pmg.org.za/tabled-committee-report/6581/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The Annual Performance Plan 2026/27 counts [242 health facilities equipped with online birth registration systems, beside 325 front offices and 220 mobile offices](<https://pmg.org.za/files/2/DHA_APP_2026-27_Final_26_Mar_2026.pdf>). The National Department of Health counts [299 health facilities able to register births at the end of 2024/25, against 1,445 public health facilities with maternity wards](<https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/NDoH-2024-25-Annual-Report-_-19-September-2025.pdf>).
