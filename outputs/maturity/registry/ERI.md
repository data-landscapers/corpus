# Eritrea

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** Eritrea has developed a five-year Strategic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics Plan for 2024-2028, [recorded by the African Committee of Experts on the Rights and Welfare of the Child in observations adopted in April 2026](<https://reporting.acerwc.africa/sites/default/files/2026-07/503.26_Eritrea%20Concluding%20Observations%2022.07.2026.pdf>).

**Events registered digitally.** Births and deaths are both [registered in the sub-zoba's digital database after entry in the kebabi's handwritten register](<https://fln.dk/media/ow3jujfq/erit402.pdf>), according to a Dutch foreign ministry report of February 2017 cited by the Danish National ID Centre, which adds that death registration has no fixed or automatic routine.

**How the record is made.** A birth is first entered in the handwritten family register at the local kebabi office, which issues a transfer letter that the parents take to the sub-zoba office, where [the data is entered into the database and the hard copies are archived](<https://fln.dk/media/ow3jujfq/erit402.pdf>) (2017). A death goes the same way, by a "death letter" from the kebabi to the sub-zoba.

**Last twelve months.** The death certificate issued by the local municipality is computerised and carries a registration number, [the US State Department said in 2025](<https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/Visa-Reciprocity-and-Civil-Documents-by-Country/Eritrea.html>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** Each of the six zobas keeps an electronic population register, first introduced in zoba Maekel in 2003 and updated by the 54 sub-zoba administrations, while each of about 2,000 kebabi administrations keeps a handwritten family register, [the Danish National ID Centre states, citing the European Asylum Support Office in May 2015](<https://fln.dk/media/ow3jujfq/erit402.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. The [civil registry office of the municipality of Asmara](<https://fln.dk/media/ow3jujfq/erit402.pdf>), which issues birth, marriage, divorce and death certificates; the [reference number on residence cards](<https://fln.dk/media/ow3jujfq/erit402.pdf>), which Eritrea calls the ASC number; the [digital population register kept in each zoba](<https://www.metas.ch/dam/sem/en/data/internationales/herkunftslaender/afrika/eri/eri-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf.download.pdf/ERI-identitaets-und-zivilstandsdokumente-e.pdf>); the [national identity card](<https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2011310.html>) of the Department of Immigration and Nationality; and the Washington embassy's [Consular Affairs Processing System](<https://us.embassyeritrea.org/national-id/>) for identity card applications from abroad.

***Not held***. How a birth is entered today, how many offices do the digital entry, who owns and pays for the registers, and whether the 2024-2028 plan names a digital system. The record-making facts date from 2015 and 2017, and nothing is held for the last twelve months.
