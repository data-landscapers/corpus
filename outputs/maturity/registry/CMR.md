# Cameroon

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** BUNEC, the national civil status office, [works under the supervision of the decentralisation ministry](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/4868e327-c188-42f8-8918-0b68a9e2b363/content>). Its [National Strategic Plan for the Modernisation of the Civil Status System 2025-2029 was launched on 11 September 2025](<https://kamerinfosplus.com/2026/08/10/etat-civil-la-cdhc-veut-un-systeme-moderne-digitalise-et-accessible-a-tous/>). Commune equipment comes through UNDP projects: [Legal Identity II in the East Region](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>) and [a Denmark-funded one in ten municipalities](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/first-ever-digital-birth-certificate-north-west-and-south-west-region-cameroon-new-era-civil-registration-and-access-legal-identity>).

**Events registered digitally.** In September 2025 [Maroua 3 council issued birth, marriage and death certificates, births while the parent waits](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/digitalizing-birth-certificates-where-technology-giving-every-child-name-and-future-0>). On 17 December 2025 [Dschang commune issued its first computerised birth certificates](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-la-commune-de-dschang-delivre-ses-premiers-actes-de-naissance-informatises/>), after Nitoukou, Garoua II and Bikok. In the East Region [birth acts were being produced in the system UNDP names SIGEC](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>) by August 2026.

**How the record is made.** In December 2025 [the communes' computerised birth acts were reported integrated into the central SIGEC platform](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-la-commune-de-dschang-delivre-ses-premiers-actes-de-naissance-informatises/>). By August 2026 [each East Region commune had its own server, workstations, network and software](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>). On 8 October 2026 BUNEC's Director General [described paper as dominant](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). [Digital registration runs in selected councils only](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>).

**Last twelve months.** On 7 January 2026 [Limbe I Council issued the first birth certificate from a digitalised system in the North-West and South-West regions](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/first-ever-digital-birth-certificate-north-west-and-south-west-region-cameroon-new-era-civil-registration-and-access-legal-identity>). By August 2026 [two East Region centres, Bertoua 1er and the Bertoua City Council, were in production](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** On 14 September 2026 BUNEC's Director General counted [60 of 432 principal civil status centres were digitised, 13.9 per cent](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>), against [42 in April 2022](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>). The country also has [2,439 secondary centres](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>).

***Also on file***. Archive: BUNEC's [retro-digitalisation of legacy paper archives, planned for all centres](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). Also the [Sous-système d'archivage numérique de l'état civil (SANEC) in the East Region communes](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>), and the [European Union's PROCIVIS scanning and indexing of paper registers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012324023052410/pdf/P1795471b60b614c14d0214b971aed2105a95cebf79b.pdf>). Notification: the health ministry's [birth and death declaration forms entered in DHIS2](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>). Outreach: the [PAREC school birth certificate drive and its special mobile court hearings](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-restarts-birth-registration-drive-using-schools-as-gateway-to-legal-identity>). Also BUNEC's [catch-up registration exercise for Baka people at Djoum](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). The Francophonie runs a [Civil Registration project in the Far North](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/la-francophonie-supports-birth-registration-efforts-across-central-africa-madagascar>). Remote areas get [mobile registration units and birth registration campaigns](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012324023052410/pdf/P1795471b60b614c14d0214b971aed2105a95cebf79b.pdf>). Identity: the [national identity card system of the General Delegation for National Security, in its new generation from February 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/cameroons-new-generation-biometric-id-card-system-goes-live-feb-17>). Also its [idcam.cm pre-enrolment platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/le-cameroun-numerise-la-procedure-d-obtention-de-la-carte-d-identite-nationale>) and its [digital archive of birth certificate copies](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012324023052410/pdf/P1795471b60b614c14d0214b971aed2105a95cebf79b.pdf>). Enabling: the [programme de digitalisation des communes et des régions in preparation](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-georges-elanga-obam-veut-mettre-fin-aux-ilots-numeriques-de-ladministration/>). Also the [Digital Master Plan for decentralised entities](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/cameroon-unveils-plan-to-modernize-local-digital-govt-services>) and the [PATNUC broadband connectivity of public entities](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>). Exchange: BUNEC's [inter-administrative platform linking civil registration and the national identity card, being deployed](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). Also the [interoperability framework for birth declaration between DHIS2 and BUNEC's system](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>). A [unique personal identification number is planned at birth](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012324023052410/pdf/P1795471b60b614c14d0214b971aed2105a95cebf79b.pdf>). The [Civil Registration System has been assessed for integration with the Unified Social Registry](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>).

***Not found***. The share of all local offices is not published. The 60 are counted against principal centres only, and the secondary centres are outside the figure. Only one report says the commune record reaches the central platform, and a newer one has it on the commune's server. No national budget line is stated.
