# Cameroon

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** BUNEC, the national civil status office, [works under the supervision of the decentralisation ministry](<https://biblio-minsante.cm/server/api/core/bitstreams/4868e327-c188-42f8-8918-0b68a9e2b363/content>). Its [National Strategic Plan for the Modernisation of the Civil Status System 2025-2029 was launched on 11 September 2025](<https://kamerinfosplus.com/2026/08/10/etat-civil-la-cdhc-veut-un-systeme-moderne-digitalise-et-accessible-a-tous/>). The equipment in the communes comes through UNDP projects, [Legal Identity II in the East Region](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>) and [a Denmark-funded one in ten municipalities](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/first-ever-digital-birth-certificate-north-west-and-south-west-region-cameroon-new-era-civil-registration-and-access-legal-identity>).

**Events registered digitally.** UNDP reported in September 2025 that [Maroua 3 council issues birth, marriage and death certificates, producing birth certificates on the system while the parent waits](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/digitalizing-birth-certificates-where-technology-giving-every-child-name-and-future-0>). In the East Region [birth acts were being produced in the system UNDP names SIGEC](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>) by August 2026.

**How the record is made.** Each East Region commune [receives its own server, workstations and network with the software installed](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>), UNDP reported in August 2026. In an interview published on 8 October 2026 BUNEC's Director General describes [paper as the dominant process, with digital registration in selected councils only](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>).

**Last twelve months.** On 7 January 2026 [Limbe I Council issued the first birth certificate from a digitalised system in the North-West and South-West regions](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/first-ever-digital-birth-certificate-north-west-and-south-west-region-cameroon-new-era-civil-registration-and-access-legal-identity>). By August 2026 [two East Region centres, Bertoua 1er and the Bertoua City Council, were in production](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** On the figures BUNEC's Director General gave on 14 September 2026, [60 of the country's 432 principal civil status centres were digitised, 13.9 per cent](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>), against [42 in April 2022](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-52-milliards-fcfa-recherches-pour-numeriser-letat-civil>). The country also has [2,439 secondary centres](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: BUNEC's [retro-digitalisation of legacy paper archives, planned for all centres](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>), the [Sous-système d'archivage numérique de l'état civil (SANEC) in the East Region communes](<https://www.undp.org/cameroon/stories/dans-lest-du-cameroun-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-transforme-progressivement-lacces-lidentite-legale-bertoua-cameroun-aout-2026>) and the [European Union's PROCIVIS scanning and indexing of paper registers](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports>). Notification: the health ministry's [birth and death declaration forms entered in DHIS2](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Rapport-sur-les-statistiques-de-letat-civil-au-Cameroun_version-finale-13-03-2024-1.pdf>). Outreach: the [PAREC school birth certificate drive and its special mobile court hearings](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/cameroon-restarts-birth-registration-drive-using-schools-as-gateway-to-legal-identity>), BUNEC's [catch-up registration exercise for Baka people at Djoum](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>), the [Civil Registration project of the Organisation internationale de la Francophonie in the Far North](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/la-francophonie-supports-birth-registration-efforts-across-central-africa-madagascar>) and the [mobile registration units and birth registration campaigns in remote areas](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports>). Identity: the [national identity card system of the General Delegation for National Security, in its new generation from February 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/cameroons-new-generation-biometric-id-card-system-goes-live-feb-17>), its [idcam.cm pre-enrolment platform](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/breves/breves-simple/le-cameroun-numerise-la-procedure-d-obtention-de-la-carte-d-identite-nationale>) and its [digital archive of birth certificate copies](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports>). Enabling: the [programme de digitalisation des communes et des régions in preparation](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-georges-elanga-obam-veut-mettre-fin-aux-ilots-numeriques-de-ladministration/>), the [Digital Master Plan for decentralised entities](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202507/cameroon-unveils-plan-to-modernize-local-digital-govt-services>) and the [PATNUC broadband connectivity of public entities](<https://armp.cm/details?id_publication=5782&type_publication=AMI>). Exchange: BUNEC's [inter-administrative platform linking civil registration and the national identity card, being deployed](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>), the [interoperability framework for birth declaration between DHIS2 and BUNEC's system](<https://www.minsante.cm/site/sites/default/files/PSNSN%202026-2030%20Fr.pdf>), the [unique personal identification number planned at birth](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports>) and the [assessment of the Civil Registration System for integration with the Unified Social Registry](<https://spdci.org/resource/across-21-countries-a-shared-journey-toward-inclusive-and-digital-social-protection/>).

***Not held***. The share of all local offices is not published: the 60 are counted against principal centres only, and the secondary centres are outside the figure. The evidence does not establish that a record made in a commune is held in the national register, and it states no national budget line.
