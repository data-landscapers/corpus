# South Sudan

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** The World Bank's diagnostic of May 2026 [gives the Directorate of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passports and Immigration as operator and owner of the planned civil registration system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>). Its director-general said on 28 July 2026 that [implementation would start with support from partners](<https://www.eyeradio.org/civil-registry-to-introduce-digital-system-for-birth-death-and-marriage-registration/>), and named none.

**Events registered digitally.** The World Bank's May 2026 diagnostic found that [birth registration is yet to be implemented and birth certificates do not exist, with about 90 per cent of births in no official register](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

**How the record is made.** Births [may be declared at civil registration or identification offices within 45 days, which the World Bank said in May 2026 is uncommon in practice](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 24 July 2026 the government [announced plans for a digital system recording births, deaths, marriages and divorces](<https://www.eyeradio.org/government-to-adopt-modern-digital-birth-death-and-marriage-records-system/>). On 31 July the Directorate was reported to be [planning to link civil registration with the national ID system, with births declared from health facilities](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>). At the handover of 10 August 2026 the outgoing director-general cited [a memorandum of understanding, signed in Nairobi, for an integrated civil registration system](<https://www.sudanspost.com/outgoing-immigration-chief-says-he-tackled-passport-id-card-shortages/>).

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The World Bank's May 2026 diagnostic [lists the Directorate's civil registration system as planned only and describes no office registering digitally](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. Notification: [a Notification of Birth letter issued in seven of the country's 73 hospitals, the Ministry of Health's DHIS2 database for birth notifications and a DHIS2 CRVS module](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>); [birth notification at 2 per cent of live births in 2024-25, and death notification in a pilot phase](<https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/South%20Sudan%20Annual%20health%20sector%20performance%20report_%282024_25%29%20%281%29.pdf>); [a birth notification system in several health centres, non-operational since 2018](<https://www.avaloninfosys.com/portfolio/south-sudan-birth-notification-assessment>); [an electronic birth notification register piloted in seven health facilities with UNICEF](<https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=15983&fileID=35630>); and [planned notification of births from health facilities to the Directorate](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>). Outreach: [100 mobile registration kits bought for rural communities](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>) and [mobile civil registration centres planned for areas of return](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-10/action-plan-ssd-oct-2024-5.pdf>). Identity: [the Nationality Certificate, the National Identity Card and a Personal Identification Number not yet activated](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/22/in-the-worlds-youngest-nation-how-do-you-prove-you-exist>); [the national ID and passport system run with a German vendor, the Refugee Identity Card and a biometric registration of public servants](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>); [five production machines for passports and national ID in Juba](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/05/08/government-procures-5-advanced-machine-systems-for-national-id-and-passport-production/>); [passport and nationality card issuance, with online passport applications planned for citizens in Egypt](<https://www.eyeradio.org/over-37000-new-passport-booklets-arrive-stocks-to-last-until-december-officical/>); [school-based national ID registration, launched in Juba on 4 August 2026](<https://www.eyeradio.org/school-based-national-id-registration-launched-for-exam-candidates-in-juba/>); [a planned digital national identification card](<https://www.eyeradio.org/civil-registry-to-introduce-digital-system-for-birth-death-and-marriage-registration/>); [a personal identity card issued to adults from January 2026](<https://www.eyeradio.org/2026-p-8-s-4-candidates-to-get-priority-as-govt-launches-national-id-cards/>); [national identity card issuance at the Yei River County office](<https://www.onecitizendaily.com/index.php/2026/07/13/thousand-begin-collecting-national-identity-cards-in-greater-yei/>); [National Identity Card issuance supported by UNHCR and the European Union](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/over-17400-south-sudanese-receive-national-identity-cards-thanks-to-unhcr-and-the-european-union>); [national ID registration by a mobile team with UNHCR](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/south-sudan-helping-to-secure-a-future-one-national-id-card-at-a-time/>); [a planned registration of foreigners](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/juba-passport-id-card-issuance-resumes>); [alien registration](<https://cms.nra.gov.ss/uploads/NRA_e_Tax_Training_Manual_V_001_Registration_eae52f383f.pdf>); [the immigration database](<https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-introduces-digital-payment-system-for-passports-after-supply-crisis/>); and [IOM's biometric registration at displacement sites](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/f69f5563-7a0a-42ba-9885-fa1839a1b4a5/ZM_CFM_2026_SSD_Q2_FINAL.pdf>). Certificate: [a bank-linked digital payment system for passports and national ID in Juba, from 19 January 2026](<https://www.sudanspost.com/south-sudan-introduces-digital-payment-system-for-passports-after-supply-crisis/>). Exchange: [a planned link between civil registration and the national ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>).

***Not held***. No design, procurement, pilot office or start date for a digital system is described, and whether the birth-registration pilot in Juba is digital is not stated. No dated statement that local offices register on paper is held, and the sources do not agree on whether a costed plan is in force.
