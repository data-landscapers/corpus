# Gabon

## Civil registration

**Governance, planning, finance.** Law 008/2022 of 15 June 2022 makes the Centre National de l'Etat Civil (CNEC) [the public service that builds the digital, biometric and centralised civil register and directs the civil status centres](<https://journal-officiel.ga/18873-008-2022-/>). The World Bank's Digital Gabon Project [keeps US$10.5 million for digitalising civil registration](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>), in a project closing on 30 June 2028 (June 2026).

**Events registered digitally.** No digital registration of births or deaths is in service: the Gabon Digital general coordinator said in September 2026 that [the digital platforms for managing civil registration come second](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>), after the decisions on the rules for attributing identity.

**How the record is made.** Civil registration runs on manual procedures, [which the coordinator said the reform should break with](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>) (September 2026). The World Bank described the civil registry as [entirely paper-based](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/301741627956043619/pdf/Gabon-Digital-Gabon-Project.pdf>) in June 2021.

**Last twelve months.** The project's technical assistance was recruited in April 2026 and [a mapping exercise to prioritise the departments whose offices will register digitally was under way at 31 May 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>). On 28 September 2026 [an inter-ministerial workshop opened at Ntoum](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>) to define the conditions for putting the CNEC into operation.

**Local offices doing the digital entry.** The World Bank [reports no administrative department with a civil registration office digitally registering vital acts at 31 May 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>), against a target of nine departments by June 2028.

***Noted, not assessed***. Archive: the planned [mapping of civil registry archives and feasibility study for digitising them](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>). Certificate: [Akiba](<https://www.gabonreview.com/poste-s-a-akiba-le-numerique-et-la-logistique-au-coeur-de-la-relance/>), Poste S.A.'s application, announced on 9 October 2026. Outreach: the joint programme [« Citoyenneté et Protection sociale »](<https://www.gabonreview.com/ngounie-500-personnes-sans-acte-de-naissance-prises-en-charge/>) in Ngounié province. Identity: the planned [Registre biométrique des personnes physiques and Système national d'identité digitale](<https://www.gabonreview.com/etat-civil-des-experts-reunis-a-ntoum-pour-preparer-la-reforme-numerique/>); the [Numéro d'identification personnel](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>); the [Carte nationale d'identité électronique](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>); and the biometric database [IBOGA](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identite-digitale-le-gabon-franchit-un-cap-vers-une-gouvernance-inclusive-et-securisee/>). Enabling: the state fibre network [Réseau de l'Administration Gabonaise](<https://www.gabonreview.com/le-rag-operationnel-a-oyem-laninf-accelere-la-modernisation-administrative-en/>) at Oyem; the [universal service programme for mobile and internet coverage](<https://www.gabonreview.com/fracture-numerique-au-gabon-plus-de-117-000-habitants-nont-pas-acces-a-internet-en-2024/>); and the planned [kits, connectivity and building renovations for civil registration offices](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062726133542471/pdf/P175987-e83bc197-693c-4c5b-bf60-39949daa03f8.pdf>). Exchange: the planned [interoperability of civil registration with the biometric population register](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/301741627956043619/pdf/Gabon-Digital-Gabon-Project.pdf>), and the [common biometric reference](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identification-legale-et-digitale-le-gabon-enclenche-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-trois-projets-strategiques/>) meant to connect the three identity projects.

***Not held***. No pilot office is named, and the count held is of departments, with no total, not of offices. No domestic budget line is stated, and the CNEC is not yet in operation.
