# Uganda

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [UBOS states the National Population and Housing Census 2024 was enumerated door-to-door from 10 May to 26 May 2024 (reference night 9 May), counting 45,905,417 persons and 10,698,913 households; a post-enumeration survey put net coverage at 95.7%](<https://www.ubos.org/wp-content/uploads/publications/National-Population-and-Housing-Census-2024-Final-Report-Volume-1-Main.pdf>). [UBOS reports finalising and disseminating a thematic report (Disability monograph) from the National Population and Housing Census (NPHC) 2024, showing the census was enumerated in 2024](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>). [UBOS donated 4,050 tablets and six computers previously used in the 2024 National Population and Housing Census, which was therefore enumerated](<https://chimpreports.com/ubos-donates-4050-census-tablets-to-strengthen-national-data-systems/>).

**Results published.** [UBOS presents the final report (Volume I, Main) of the National Population and Housing Census 2024, dated December 2024; preliminary results came out on 27 June 2024 and provisional on 3 October 2024](<https://www.ubos.org/wp-content/uploads/publications/National-Population-and-Housing-Census-2024-Final-Report-Volume-1-Main.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [UBOS released the findings of the 8th Uganda National Household Survey in May 2025, per the article](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/361056/ubos-commissions-80-data-collectors-for-9th-uganda-national-household-survey>). [UBOS reports producing and disseminating UDHS 2022 thematic reports (water quality, refugee health status, family planning, gender-based violence) in FY2025/26](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/Uganda%20Bureau%20of%20Statistics_25.pdf>).
