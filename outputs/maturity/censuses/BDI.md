# Burundi

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Burundi carried out its combined population, housing, agriculture and livestock census (RGPHAE) in 2024, run entirely in digital mode](<https://burundi.unfpa.org/fr/news/coop%C3%A9ration-sud-sud-la-guin%C3%A9e-%C3%A9quatoriale-sinspire-de-lexp%C3%A9rience-burundaise-du-recensement>). [INSBU's results pages state the RGPHAE 2024 census was carried out in 2024, counting 12,332,788 residents by exhaustive enumeration; the results shown are preliminary, published under decree 100/032 of 27 March 2025](<https://www.insbu.bi/resultatRGPHAE2024>). [Decree 100/032 of 27 March 2025 publishes preliminary results of the RGPHAE 2024 census, counting 12,332,788 residents and 2,724,457 ordinary households, pending definitive results](<https://presidence.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/decret-RGPHAE-2024.pdf>).
