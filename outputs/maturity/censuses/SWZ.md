# Eswatini

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The minister said the last population and housing census was conducted in 2017 and was the country's first digital census](<https://times.co.sz/27831/news/ministry-unfpa-launch-population-housing-economic-census/>). [In November 2025 the planning ministry and UNFPA launched the 2027 Population and Housing Census](<https://times.co.sz/27831/news/ministry-unfpa-launch-population-housing-economic-census/>). [The Central Statistical Office is running its first phase, mapping and listing](<https://times.co.sz/27831/news/ministry-unfpa-launch-population-housing-economic-census/>). [An August 2019 health information profile gave 2007 as the last census year](<https://measureevaluation.cpc.unc.edu/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/swaziland.html>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016.
