# Senegal

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [ANSD's national data archive lists the fifth general population and housing census, Senegal 2023 (RGPH-5), as a catalogued dataset with licensed files and 19,910 views](<http://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog>). [The ANEC director general cites RGPH-5 data showing 5.3% of the population and 8.9% of under-5s have no birth certificate, so the fifth census has been taken](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Dossier/enquete-nes-mais-invisibles-33-un-enfant-sur-cinq-nest-toujours-pas-enregistre-a-letat-civil-au-senegal-les-eclairages-du-dg-de-lanec_n_500404.html>). [The ENTICS sample was drawn from the base of the fifth Population and Housing Census (RGPH-5) of 2023](<https://www.rewmi.com/enquete-nationale-sur-les-tic-au-senegal-entics-les-femmes-plus-connectees-que-les-hommes/>). [The DGPSN presentation gives Senegal's population as 18,000,000 individuals from the 2023 census](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).
