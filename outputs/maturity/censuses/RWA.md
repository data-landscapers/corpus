# Rwanda

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [NISR states the Fifth Rwanda Population and Housing Census was enumerated from 16 to 30 August 2022](<http://alpha.statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-02/RPHC5_MainIndicatorsReport_Final.pdf>). [A March 2025 presentation gives its count as 13,246,394 people in 3,312,743 households](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Talking-Interoperability-18-Rwanda_vFinal_Updated.pdf>).

**Results published.** [NISR published the census's Main Indicators Report in February 2023](<http://alpha.statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-02/RPHC5_MainIndicatorsReport_Final.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [NISR announced the release of the seventh Demographic and Health Survey, the 2025 RDHS, on 6 January 2026](<https://statistics.gov.rw/node/805>). [The survey, run with the Ministry of Health and ICF across over 14,000 households, published its final report in 2026](<https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/FR401/FR401.pdf>). [NISR added the 2025 Labour Force Survey microdata to its catalogue on 8 April 2026](<https://microdatabk.statistics.gov.rw/home>). [The same catalogue added the Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (EICV7) for 2023-2024 on 18 July 2025](<https://microdatabk.statistics.gov.rw/home>).
