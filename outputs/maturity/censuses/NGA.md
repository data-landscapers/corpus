# Nigeria

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** Nigeria last counted its population in 2006. [The last national census was held in November 2006](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/03/08/nigerias-first-biometric-census>). The next one is being prepared as the country's first digital census. [By March 2025 the National Population Commission had mapped and demarcated the whole country and trained staff](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2025/03/08/nigerias-first-biometric-census>). A budget of N980 billion had been proposed to the President. [In October 2026 a minister said the commission had mapped more than 65 million properties for the count](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7586/x>). No date for the count is on file.

**Results published.** No census has been counted since 2006, so there are no recent results. The nearest fact is [the mapping of more than 65 million properties for the next census, reported in October 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7586/x>).

**A household survey published.** National surveys are published. [The statistics bureau released microdata of the Labour Force Survey for the first quarter of 2024](<https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/151>). It covered 8,836 households in all 36 states and the capital. [UNICEF's June 2026 brief uses results of the Demographic and Health Survey 2023-2024](<https://data.unicef.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/A-Statistical-Profile-of-Birth-Registration-in-Nigeria_final-June-2026.pdf>).
