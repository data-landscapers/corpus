# Algeria

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The World Bank gives 2022 as the latest census](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [Its SPI lists counts in 1998, 2008 and 2022](<https://github.com/worldbank/SPI>). The statistics office's own account of the 2022 count is not on file. Nor is any press report of it. The World Bank is the only source on file for any census in Algeria.

**Results published.** No release of 2022 results has been found. The latest fact on file is the count: [the World Bank dates it to 2022](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).

**A household survey published.** [The World Bank names the 2018-2019 MICS as the latest health survey](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [It names a 2011 household survey as the latest source on income](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). Both predate the three years to the as-at. Nothing more recent has been found, from the statistics office or the press.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found reports results of the 2022 census. Nor does any report a household survey published since October 2023.
