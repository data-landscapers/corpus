# Zambia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Zambia's 2022 Census of Population and Housing, its sixth national census, was enumerated from 18 August to 21 September 2022; preliminary results were released on 23 December 2022](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2022-Census-of-Population-and-Housing-Summary-Report-Part-2.pdf>). [The Zambia Statistics Agency's national analytical report presents results from the 2022 Census of Population and Housing, which collected education and literacy data (national literacy 62.6 percent among people aged 5 and older); the document gives no exact enumeration date, so the year 2022 is used](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2022-Census-National-Analytical-Report.pdf>). Five facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Final results published.** [The Zambia Statistics Agency's Population Summary Report Volume Two, dated June 2024, gives final population figures of the 2022 census (total 19,693,423)](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2022-Census-of-Population-and-Housing-Summary-Report-Part-2.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [The Zambia Statistics Agency, Ministry of Health and ICF published the final report of the 2024 Zambia Demographic and Health Survey in November 2025; fieldwork ran in 2024](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ZDHS-2024.pdf>).
