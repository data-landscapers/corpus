# Zambia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Zambia's 2022 Census of Population and Housing, its sixth national census, was enumerated from 18 August to 21 September 2022; preliminary results were released on 23 December 2022](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2022-Census-of-Population-and-Housing-Summary-Report-Part-2.pdf>). [The 2025 ICT survey drew its sample from the 2022 Census frame, so a census was counted in 2022](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf>). [The Zambia Statistics Agency says Zambia's 2022 Census of Population and Housing, its sixth and first electronic census, was conducted](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/10/zamstats-hosts-malawi-statistical-office-for-census-knowledge-exchange/>).

**Results published.** [The Zambia Statistics Agency's Population Summary Report Volume Two, dated June 2024, gives final population figures of the 2022 census (total 19,693,423)](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2022-Census-of-Population-and-Housing-Summary-Report-Part-2.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [ZamStats presented highlights of the 2025 National Survey on Access and Usage of ICTs, a household survey of 32,500 individuals targeted in 325 enumeration areas](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Zambia_2025-ICT-Survey.pdf>). [The Zambia Statistics Agency, Ministry of Health and ICF published the final report of the 2024 Zambia Demographic and Health Survey in November 2025; fieldwork ran in 2024](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ZDHS-2024.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [M-SAT E-census software / MoE Statistical Tables](<https://idi.no/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Office-of-the-Auditor-General-Zambia-Simplified-Audit-Report.pdf>).
