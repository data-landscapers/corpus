# Seychelles

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The bulletin states the NBS population estimates are benchmarked to the Population and Housing Census](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1889-mid-2025-estimated-resident-population/download>). The 2010 and 2022 censuses are the anchor points for backcasting 2011-2021 estimates. [The 2022 Population and Housing Census, Seychelles' sixth since independence and first digital census, was carried out in 2022 under the Census Act](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>).

**Results published.** [The NBS published Volume 2, the main census report with results at national, regional and district level, in March 2024](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>). It refers to its compilation as the final report.

**A household survey published.** No source found reports results of a national household survey published since October 2023. [The NBS published Volume 2, the main census report with results at national, regional and district level, in March 2024](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>). It refers to its compilation as the final report. [The Quarterly Labour Force Survey](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>) is also named, with no results dated.
