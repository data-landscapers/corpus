# Seychelles

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The bulletin states the NBS population estimates are benchmarked to the Population and Housing Census and that the 2010 and 2022 censuses are the anchor points for backcasting 2011-2021 estimates](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1889-mid-2025-estimated-resident-population/download>). [The 2022 Population and Housing Census, Seychelles' sixth since independence and first digital census, was carried out in 2022 under the Census Act](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>). Three facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** [The NBS published Volume 2, the main census report with results at national, regional and district level, in March 2024, and refers to its compilation as the final report](<https://www.nbs.gov.sc/downloads/1555-seychelles-population-and-housing-census-2022/download>).
