# Togo

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The document cites results of the fifth general population and housing census (RGPH-5) as showing 141,757 children aged 9-14 out of formal school](<https://togopresse.tg/60e-journee-de-lalphabetisation-le-togo-mise-sur-linitiative-paaff-et-le-numerique-pour-faire-reculer-lillettrisme/>). [The document gives the date of the RGPH-5 census as November 2022](<https://inseed.tg/resultats-definitifs-du-rgph-5-novembre-2022/>). It reports a resident population of 8,095,498 counted by the census.

**Results published.** [INSEED published the definitive results of RGPH-5 (November 2022) by region, prefecture, sex and urban/rural, totalling 8,095,498 residents](<https://inseed.tg/resultats-definitifs-du-rgph-5-novembre-2022/>).

**A household survey published.** [Detailed results of the second harmonised household living conditions survey (EHCVM-2) were presented at a workshop on 13 December 2024](<https://archive.presidence.gouv.tg/2024/12/14/developpement-presentation-des-principaux-resultats-du-rgph-5-et-de-la-ehcvm-2/>). The survey's fieldwork ran in 2021-2022. The workshop was chaired for the head of state, and the results of the RGPH-5 census were presented with the survey's results.
