# Mauritius

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The 2022 Housing and Population Census results were used to review sampling strata from 2023 and to revise the population figures behind the labour force estimates](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1936/LF_Emp_Unemp_1Qtr26_300626.pdf>).
