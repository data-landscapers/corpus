# Mauritius

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The 2022 Housing and Population Census results were used to review sampling strata from 2023](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1936/LF_Emp_Unemp_1Qtr26_300626.pdf>). They were also used to revise the population figures behind the labour force estimates. [Statistics Mauritius says its labour force estimates from 2025 use population figures revised to the results of the 2022 population census](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1925/LF_Emp_Unemp_Yr25_190526.pdf>). The document gives no enumeration day, so 2022 is taken as the year of the census.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. They were also used to revise the population figures behind the labour force estimates.

**A household survey published.** [Statistics Mauritius released its ICT statistics report on 10 July 2026, with 2024 results of the Continuous Multi-Purpose Household Survey](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/Statistics/ESI/2026/EI1939/ICT_Yr25_100726.pdf>). The results show 83.2% of persons aged 12 and above using the internet. They show 85.8% of households with internet access.
