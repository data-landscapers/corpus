# Namibia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The NSA says the 2023 PHC counted all persons present on census night 24 September 2023; enumeration ran 25 September to 6 October 2023 with mop-up to 30 October 2023 (population 3,022,401)](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Media-Statement-2023-Census-Main-Report-30-October-2024.pdf>). [The presentation cites a population of 3,022,401 from the NSA 2023 Population and Housing Census](<https://www.fig.net/resources/proceedings/fig_proceedings/fig2024/ppt/ts02g/TS02G_mazambani_12660_ppt.pdf>). [The NSA Statistician-General cited the 2023 Population and Housing Census as the country's first fully digital census](<https://neweralive.na/stats-key-for-development-bon-nsa/>). [Its institutional maternal mortality figures are consistent with those in the 2023 census report](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Namibia-Synthesis-Report.pdf>).

**Results published.** [The NSA released the 2023 PHC Main Report on 30 October 2024, which it says contains final results; preliminary results had been launched in March 2024](<https://nsa.org.na/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Media-Statement-2023-Census-Main-Report-30-October-2024.pdf>).
