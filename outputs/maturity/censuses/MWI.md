# Malawi

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Malawi held a 2018 Population and Housing Census](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/10/zamstats-hosts-malawi-statistical-office-for-census-knowledge-exchange/>). [Finance minister Joseph Mwanamvekha said Malawi's coming national census and related economic surveys are budgeted at about $55 million](<https://mwnation.com/afdb-backs-malawi-on-census-gdp-rebasing/>). He said the US Census Bureau, UK ONS and UNFPA are expected to co-finance them. [Malawi is preparing its 7th Population and Housing Census, scheduled for September 2028](<https://efficacynews.africa/2026/09/10/zamstats-hosts-malawi-statistical-office-for-census-knowledge-exchange/>). Its statistics office held a benchmarking mission in Zambia on 7-11 September 2026.
