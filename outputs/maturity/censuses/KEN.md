# Kenya

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [On 31 January 2025 the director general of KNBS said groundwork had been laid for the 2029 census](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/108566-knbs-rules-out-new-census-garissa-wajir-mandera-citing-4-year-preparation-time>), Kenya's seventh. He spoke after a court cancelled the results of the 2019 census for parts of Wajir, Mandera and Garissa.

**Results published.** [Pollicy reported in 2025 that Kenya's open data portal gives free access to the 2019 census](<https://pollicy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Policy-brief-13-Mar.pdf>), with health statistics and other government data. The data are in machine-readable formats. Pollicy says Kenya was the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to launch such a portal.

**A household survey published.** [In December 2024 the Communications Authority published a fact sheet on ICT uptake](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-12/ICT-KDHS%20Analytical%20Fact%20Sheet.pdf>) from the 2022 Kenya Demographic and Health Survey. [The Authority and KNBS released the full analytical report](<http://www.ca.go.ke/households-electricity-have-higher-internet-usage-and-ict-device-ownership-survey-shows>), covering 42,022 households in 1,692 clusters.
