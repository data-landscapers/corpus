# Sierra Leone

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Statistics Sierra Leone said the 2026 census budget rose from US$7 million to nearly US$60 million after the nationwide cartographic mapping](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/census-budget-million-stats-andrew-johnny/>). [Stats SL took delivery of 25,000 government-funded tablets for electronic data collection in the 2026 census, handed over 7 October 2026, with Census Night set for 1 December 2026](<https://sierraleonews.com/2026/10/09/stats-sl-receives-25000-tablets-for-2026-census/>). [Stats SL announced on 7 October 2026 deployment of 500 tablets and mapping software, completion of the cartographic mapping begun in October 2025, and enumeration set for 2-16 December 2026; government has invested over US$20 million](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/stats-mapping-deploys-digital-system-census/>). [A mock census household enumeration was closing on 19 October 2026, ahead of the national census scheduled for December 2026](<https://www.statistics.sl/index.php/minister-of-planning-economic-development-statistician-general-and-unfpa-country-representative-undertake-joint-monitoring-visit-to-2026-mock-population-and-housing-census-area.html>). [By 14 October 2026 all 29,000 tablets had arrived and 22,508 enumeration areas were mapped](<https://sierraleoneconcordtimes.com/unfpa-stats-sl-train-147-master-trainers-ahead-of-census/>).

**Results published.** [Statistics Sierra Leone published the final results of the 2021 Mid-Term Population and Housing Census in a report dated September 2022 (total population 7,548,702), after provisional results announced on 31 May 2022 changed by only 7,061 persons](<https://statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/Census/MTPHC_Preliminary_Report/Final_Preliminary_Report_2021_MTPHC.pdf>).
