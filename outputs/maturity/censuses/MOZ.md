# Mozambique

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** INE says [the last census, in 2017, counted just over 26 million people](<https://360mozambique.com/innovation/tech/mozambique-invests-430000-in-digital-census-pilot-ahead-of-2027-count/>). A [pilot census ran in Magude district in August 2026](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/22/desenvolvimento-2/ine-quer-melhorar-sistema-digital-do-censo-de-2027-apos-falhas-nos-testes/>), ahead of the first digital census planned for 2027. In September 2026 INE's national planning meeting [put the preparation of the 2027 census at the centre of its agenda](<https://mznews.co.mz/ine-aposta-na-era-digital-e-prepara-censo-2027-em-reuniao-nacional-de-planificacao/>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [INE's estimate cites the last census in 2017 as having recorded just over 26 million inhabitants](<https://www.diarioeconomico.co.mz/2026/08/02/desenvolvimento-2/governo-testa-piloto-digital-do-censo-populacional-investimento-de-430-mil-dolares/>).

**A household survey published.** In December 2024 AIM reported that [the 2024 DHS put under-five birth registration at 32 per cent](<https://aimnews.org/2024/12/12/mocambique-taxa-de-registo-de-nascimento-regista-cai-nos-ultimos-anos/>). Gavi cites [the 2022-2023 DHS on children with no vaccine dose](<https://www.gavi.org/vaccineswork/how-ai-helping-mozambique-boost-vaccine-decision-making>).

***Also on file***. [Census 2007 population projections 2007-2040](<https://ons.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/INS-Mapeamento-das-bases-de-dados_Marco_2022.pdf>).
