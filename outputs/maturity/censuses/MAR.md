# Morocco

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The 2024 national census (RGPH 2024) was taken, giving a legal population of 36,828,330 at 1 September 2024](<https://www.hcp.ma/Population-legale-du-Royaume-du-Maroc-repartie-par-regions-provinces-et-prefectures-et-communes-selon-les-resultats-du_a3974.html>). [Morocco's High Commission for Planning presented results of the general population and housing census conducted in September 2024](<https://aljarida24.ma/p/actualites/298884/>). [The High Commissioner's World Statistics Day 2025 address cites the 2024 General Population and Housing Census (RGPH 2024) as carried out; it gives no enumeration day or count](<https://www.hcp.ma/JMS-2025-Allocution-de-Monsieur-le-Haut-Commissaire-au-Plan_a4198.html>). [The RGPH 2024 census counted 36.8 million inhabitants in 2024, including 148,152 foreign residents, with a new immigration module](<https://www.hcp.ma/file/246070/>).

**Results published.** [The government adopted decree 2.24.1009 fixing the legal population by region, province and commune from the RGPH 2024 results](<https://www.hcp.ma/Population-legale-du-Royaume-du-Maroc-repartie-par-regions-provinces-et-prefectures-et-communes-selon-les-resultats-du_a3974.html>).

**A household survey published.** [HCP published the first Q1 2026 results of the new national labour force survey (EMO2026), which replaces the Enquete nationale sur l'emploi and has an annual sample of 135,000 households](<https://www.hcp.ma/Situation-du-marche-du-travail-au-Maroc-au-premier-trimestre-de-2026-a-partir-de-la-nouvelle-enquete-sur-la-main-d_a4309.html>).
