# Liberia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [LISGIS released preliminary results of the 2022 census in February 2023](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>). [LISGIS enumerated the 2022 Liberia Population and Housing Census, the fifth and first fully digital one, with census night on 11 November 2022 and a total enumerated population of 5,250,187](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>). [The document cites the LISGIS 2022 census for the under-20 population share](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

**Results published.** [LISGIS published the final results of the 2022 Liberia Population and Housing Census](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>). Preliminary results had been released in February 2023. The document gives June 2023 for its map and is published 2023-06-01.

**A household survey published.** No source found reports results of a national household survey published since October 2023. [LISGIS published the final results of the 2022 Liberia Population and Housing Census](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>). Preliminary results had been released in February 2023. The document gives June 2023 for its map and is published 2023-06-01. Three surveys are also named, with no results dated. They are [the Liberia Household Income and Expenditure Survey](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-52090066>), [the National Labor Force Survey](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>) and [the Liberia Demographic and Health Survey 2019/2020](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/admin_area/uploads/census/CENSUS_PUBLICATION_20260826152056_039d1199.pdf>).
