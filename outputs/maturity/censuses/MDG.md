# Madagascar

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The third general population and housing census (RGPH-3) was held in 2018](<http://mail.mfb.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1673>). [RGPH-3 was carried out in 2018](<https://www.instat.mg/p/presentation-du-document-de-projet-prodoc-du-rgph-4>). [INSTAT presented the RGPH-4 project document to partners on 12 May 2026](<https://www.instat.mg/p/presentation-du-document-de-projet-prodoc-du-rgph-4>). Enumeration is planned for 2028, with digital cartography and data collection. Support comes from the World Bank's STATCAP II project and from UNFPA.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [The third general population and housing census (RGPH-3) was held in 2018](<http://mail.mfb.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1673>).

**A household survey published.** [A UN Madagascar account dated 2026-03-05 describes MICS7 2025 as a reference data source](<https://madagascar.un.org/fr/311176-madagascar-enqu%C3%AAte-par-grappes-%C3%A0-indicateurs-multiples-2025-et-les-objectifs-de>). INSTAT carried out the survey with UNICEF, on 21,000 households in 23 regions with 99% response.
