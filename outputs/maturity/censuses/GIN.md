# Guinea

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [RGPH-4 was conducted 1 to 31 July 2025 across the national territory](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/index.php/actu/404-decret-de-publication-des-resultats-du-rgph-4>). [A PN-RAVEC official cites results of the fourth general population census (RGPH-4) giving a resident population of 17,521,167, of which 3,407,327 in Conakry](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/03/conakry-le-pn-ravec-dote-les-13-communes-dun-important-lot-dintrants-detat-civil-dont-les-certificats-de-non-remariage/>). [The Ministry of Plan, through INS, published preliminary results of RGPH-4 on 25 February 2026 (population 17,521,167), and the Prime Minister called the census completed; enumeration date not given](<https://mpcid.gov.gn/index.php/rgph-4-la-guinee-revele-son-nouveau-visage-et-sancre-dans-la-gouvernance-par-la-donnee/>). [On 30 October 2025 the Minister launched the post-enumeration survey of RGPH-4, which assesses the coverage of the census's main enumeration already carried out](<https://mpcid.gov.gn/index.php/rgph-4-le-ministre-ismael-nabe-lance-officiellement-lenquete-post-censitaire-un-pas-decisif-vers-la-fiabilite-des-donnees-demographiques/>). Four facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** [The Ministry of Plan, through INS, published preliminary results of RGPH-4 on 25 February 2026](<https://mpcid.gov.gn/index.php/rgph-4-la-guinee-revele-son-nouveau-visage-et-sancre-dans-la-gouvernance-par-la-donnee/>), a population of 17,521,167, and a presidential decree of 6 March 2026 gave them effect; final results are not yet held.
