# Somalia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [In April 2025 WorldPop trained Somali statisticians to map pre-census enumeration areas in five districts](<https://www.worldpop.org/blog/building-census-readiness-geospatial-training-boosts-capacity-for-somali-statisticians/>). Full enumeration of those districts is to follow if security permits. [Since August 2024 the Gates Foundation has funded UNFPA to collect pre-census enumeration data for population modelling](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=DAC-1601-INV-070843>). [The World Bank lists 1987 as the year of the latest census](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>), with settled people counted in November 1986 and nomads in February 1987.

**Results published.** No source found reports results of a census counted since October 2016. [The census for which enumeration areas were mapped in 2025 had not been counted](<https://www.worldpop.org/blog/building-census-readiness-geospatial-training-boosts-capacity-for-somali-statisticians/>).

**A household survey published.** [The statistics bureau's Director General lists the Household Budget Survey among its outputs for 2025](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>). [In April 2026 UNDP quoted him naming the Socioeconomic and Demographic Survey as well](<https://undpinsomalia.medium.com/promoting-innovation-and-digital-transformation-in-somalia-db2d48bc9643>). [The World Bank lists the 2011 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey as the latest survey of demography, education or health](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).

***Also on file***. [Business Establishment Census](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>); [Food Security and Vulnerability Survey](<https://www.undp.org/somalia/stories/driving-digital-transformation-somalia-2025-highlights>); [2024 Business Establishment Census](<https://nbs.gov.so/somalia-business-establishment-analysis-booklet/>); [Socio-Demographic Survey 2026](<https://nbs.gov.so/the-socio-demographic-survey-2026/>).

***Not found***. No source found reports a census counted since October 2016.
