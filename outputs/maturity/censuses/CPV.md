# Cape Verde

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The World Bank gives 2021 as the latest census](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>). [Its SPI lists counts in 2000, 2010 and 2021](<https://github.com/worldbank/SPI>). The statistics office's own account of the 2021 count is not on file. Nor is any plan for the next one.

**Results published.** No release of 2021 results has been found. The latest fact on file is the count: [the World Bank dates it to 2021](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).

**A household survey published.** [A July 2026 opinion piece says INE released partial poverty figures in October 2024](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/07/25/dados-do-banco-mundial-desmascaram-os-do-ine-sobre-a-pobreza-em-cabo-verde/>). [It put extreme poverty at 2.28% in 2023, against 4.56% in 2015](<https://www.anacao.cv/noticia/2026/07/25/dados-do-banco-mundial-desmascaram-os-do-ine-sobre-a-pobreza-em-cabo-verde/>). The full results of the income survey begun in December 2022 were still unpublished. [The World Bank names a 2015 income survey as the latest full source](<https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0037712/world-development-indicators>).

***Also on file***. [VI Recenseamento Geral da Agricultura](<https://www.governo.cv/lancado-o-vi-recenseamento-geral-da-agricultura-em-cabo-verde/>).

***Not found***. No source found reports results of the 2021 census.
