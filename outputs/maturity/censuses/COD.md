# DR Congo

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Census-cartography operations for the second census (RGPH-2) were launched on 28 September 2026](<https://mail.7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/10/01/recensement-identification-et-enrolement-en-rdc-ce-quil-faut-savoir>). General enumeration is planned from July 2027, and operations are costed at USD 192 million. [Training of 1,868 census cartographic operators began in Kinshasa on 9 September 2026](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/18/rgph-2-le-gouvernement-implique-les-operateurs-de-telecommunication-pour-renforcer-la>). Fieldwork followed from 15 September, with general enumeration scheduled for July 2027. [A second report gives the same training start, fieldwork from 15 September and general enumeration planned for July 2027](<https://actualite.cd/2026/09/10/rgph-2-lancement-kinshasa-de-la-formation-de-plus-de-1-800-operateurs-cartographes>).
