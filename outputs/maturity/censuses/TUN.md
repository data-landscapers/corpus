# Tunisia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INS Flash TIC indicators are drawn from the 2024 census](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/TIC-FR.pdf>). Internet use was asked for the three months preceding the census. [The RGPH 2024 was carried out, the previous census having been in 2014](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). The document uses its results, such as 66.3% effective social coverage of the employed aged 18+. [INS gives 2024 census housing counts of 4,265,636 dwellings](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/LOGEMENTS-FR.pdf>). It states the dwellings were enumerated at the date of the 2024 census.

**Results published.** [Tunisia's Institut National de la Statistique announced the publication of the official results of the 2024 general population and housing census](<http://www.ins.tn/event_rgph2024>). It did so at a press conference on 17 May 2025.

**A household survey published.** [The economy ministry, the INS and UNICEF presented the results of Tunisia's 2023 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS) on 12 February 2024](<https://www.unicef.org/tunisia/communiqu%C3%A9s-de-presse/publication-des-r%C3%A9sultats-de-lenqu%C3%AAte-nationale-par-grappe-%C3%A0-indicateurs>). The survey covered 11,000 households with a 90% response rate.
