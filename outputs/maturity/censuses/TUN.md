# Tunisia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INS Flash TIC indicators drawn from the 2024 census, with internet use asked for the three months preceding the census; year 2024 used](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/TIC-FR.pdf>). [The RGPH 2024 was carried out, the previous census having been in 2014, and uses its results (66.3% effective social coverage of the employed aged 18+); year 2024 used](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/2025-09/PROD-ProtectionSociale-Bulletin-FR.pdf>). [2024 census housing counts (4,265,636 dwellings) from INS, stating the dwellings were enumerated at the date of the 2024 census; year 2024 used](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files-ftp3/files/publication/pdf/LOGEMENTS-FR.pdf>).

**Results published.** [Tunisia's Institut National de la Statistique announced the publication of the official results of the 2024 general population and housing census at a press conference on 17 May 2025](<http://www.ins.tn/event_rgph2024>).

**A household survey published.** [The economy ministry, the INS and UNICEF presented the results of Tunisia's 2023 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey (MICS), covering 11,000 households with a 90% response rate, on 12 February 2024](<https://www.unicef.org/tunisia/communiqu%C3%A9s-de-presse/publication-des-r%C3%A9sultats-de-lenqu%C3%AAte-nationale-par-grappe-%C3%A0-indicateurs>).
