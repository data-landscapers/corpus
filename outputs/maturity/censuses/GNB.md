# Guinea-Bissau

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The World Bank resident representative spoke at the 23 July 2026 ceremony presenting preliminary results of the fourth population and housing census (RGPH4), so the count had been carried out](<https://ang.gw/ivo-rgph4-banco-mundial-diz-que-guine-bissau-acaba-de-reforcar-a-sua-capacidade-de-tomar-decisoes-com-a-realizacao-do-novo-recenseamento/>). [INE released preliminary results of RGPH4 on 23 July 2026 after data collection closed: 2,191,300 residents (raw base 2,246,300), 304,939 households, 559,303 dwellings and 376,258 buildings](<https://ang.gw/ivo-rgph4-guine-bissau-conta-atualmente-com-2-191-300-habitantes-segundo-dados-preliminares-divulgados-hoje-pelo-ine/>). [UNFPA states the first fully digital (CAPI) census, RGPH-4, was completed and its preliminary results presented on 23 July 2026 (2,191,202 residents)](<https://guinea-bissau.unfpa.org/en/news/guinea-bissaus-future-numbers-preliminary-results-first-ever-fully-digital-census-unveiled>).

**Results published.** [INE released preliminary results of RGPH4 on 23 July 2026](<https://ang.gw/ivo-rgph4-guine-bissau-conta-atualmente-com-2-191-300-habitantes-segundo-dados-preliminares-divulgados-hoje-pelo-ine/>): 2,191,300 residents, 304,939 households and 559,303 dwellings; final results are not yet held.
