# Niger

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** Niger's fifth population and housing census follows those of 1977, 1988, 2001 and 2012. On 8 October 2025 the acting head of the statistics institute INS [said it was fully financed with partners and in active preparation](<https://www.lesahel.org/7e-congres-ordinaire-du-syndicat-national-du-personnel-de-linformation-statistique-synpis-le-recensement-general-de-la-population-le-bilan-du-mandat-ecoule-et-le-renouvellement-du-b/>). He put its budget above 35 billion FCFA. The INS [reported continued pilot enumeration on 7 April 2025](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?p=5392>). A UNFPA posting [set a field pretest for July 2025 and the start of enumeration for December 2025](<https://www.unfpa.org/jobs/consultant-international-en-infrastructure-et-s%C3%A9curit%C3%A9-informatique>). The INS data centre and a backup site were being set up to hold its data.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest fact found is that the fifth census [was in active preparation in October 2025](<https://www.lesahel.org/7e-congres-ordinaire-du-syndicat-national-du-personnel-de-linformation-statistique-synpis-le-recensement-general-de-la-population-le-bilan-du-mandat-ecoule-et-le-renouvellement-du-b/>).

**A household survey published.** The INS data catalogue [lists the harmonised household living conditions survey of 2021/2022](<https://www.stat-niger.org/anado/index.php/catalog>), with entries created in February and December 2024. The INS [posted the final report of the national fertility survey on 30 March 2023](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?p=5392>).

***Also on file***. [ReGEN](<https://www.stat-niger.org/?p=5392>); [Agricultural surveys (50x2030)](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/can-today-s-agricultural-surveys-power-tomorrow-s-agricultural-a>).

***Not found***. No source found reports a census counted since October 2016.
