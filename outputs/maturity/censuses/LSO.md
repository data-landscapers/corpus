# Lesotho

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Government states enumeration of the 2026 Housing and Population Census concluded, with mop-up in Maseru and cattle pounds](<https://www.gov.ls/government/lesotho-completed-2026-housing-and-population-census-enumeration-successfully/>). Results had not yet been published. [Enumeration of the 2026 Population and Housing Census, reference night 11 April 2026, has concluded](<https://publiceyenews.com/2026/09/17/we-felt-like-part-of-the-basotho-census-gives-lgbtiq-community-a-place-in-national-data/>). Mop-up operations continued in Maseru and cattle pounds, and over 8,000 temporary field staff were employed. The Bureau of Statistics says results come after review. [The Bureau of Statistics was engaging and deploying enumerators for the 2026 Population and Housing Census](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91243>). There were payment delays and an internal probe into enumerators outside the approved lists.

**Results published.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists Census 2016 reports for download](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>). They are Vol IIIA Population Dynamics and Vol IIIB Socio-Economic Characteristics.

**A household survey published.** [The Bureau of Statistics Lesotho page lists the Labour Force Survey 2024 Indicators as its latest release](<https://www.bos.gov.ls/publications.htm>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No results of the 2026 census, the one that counts, have been found.
