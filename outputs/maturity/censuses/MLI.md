# Mali

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INSTAT's Q4 2025 bulletin reports validation, on 16-18 December 2025 in Bamako, of the Directory of Localities of Mali produced from RGPH5 data, so the fifth general population census has been counted](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>). [INSTAT's microdata catalogue lists the fifth general population and housing census (RGPH5) of Mali, dated 2022](<https://microdata.instat.ml/index.php/catalog/>). [DNEC cites RGPH5 (2022) as the source of the 89.5% birth registration rate, so a census was counted in 2022](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). [The Sida activity record says the fifth census (RGPH5), planned for 2016-2021, was delayed and concluded in 2022](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=SE-0-SE-6-11793-11793A0103>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [INSTAT's Q4 2025 bulletin reports validation, on 16-18 December 2025 in Bamako, of the Directory of Localities of Mali produced from RGPH5 data, so the fifth general population census has been counted](<https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/pub/khideux51_pub.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [The bulletin states INSTAT published the 2025 annual report of the EMOP household survey in the second quarter of 2026, with consumption spending of 10,276 billion FCFA in 2025 and a poverty and consumption profile nationally and by region](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/353/khideux53_pub.pdf>).
