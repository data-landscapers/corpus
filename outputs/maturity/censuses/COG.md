# Congo

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** ADIAC says [the fifth general population census, RGPH-5, was conducted in December 2023](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/numerique-les-tuyaux-ont-gagne-du-terrain-lusage-cherche-encore-le-sien-171228>). On 27 November 2025 the national statistics institute [opened a workshop to validate indicators and tables drawn from it](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/statistiques-lins-ouvre-latelier-national-de-validation-des-indicateurs-du-rgph-5-168128>). Its data had been collected by then.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of the RGPH-5 published. At the November 2025 workshop [its preliminary results were on display](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/statistiques-lins-ouvre-latelier-national-de-validation-des-indicateurs-du-rgph-5-168128>), while indicators and tables were still being validated.

**A household survey published.** No source found reports results of a national household survey published since October 2023. The nearest fact: [on 27 November 2025 the INS opened a workshop to validate tables from the fifth census, RGPH-5](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/statistiques-lins-ouvre-latelier-national-de-validation-des-indicateurs-du-rgph-5-168128>). Named in what was read, with no results dated: [Enquete harmonisee sur les conditions de vie des menages (EHCVM), second edition](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-statistiques-le-comite-de-pilotage-du-projet-hiswaca-valide-le-ptba-2026-estime-a-pres-de-11-milliards-de-fcfa/>); [Enquete sur l'emploi et le secteur informel du Congo (ESIC)](<https://www.aci.cg/congo-statistiques-le-comite-de-pilotage-du-projet-hiswaca-valide-le-ptba-2026-estime-a-pres-de-11-milliards-de-fcfa/>); [Quarterly labour market survey](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/statistiques-des-donnees-regulieres-pour-mieux-mesurer-le-marche-du-travail-170792>).
