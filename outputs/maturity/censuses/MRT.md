# Mauritania

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The ANSADE regional head in Adrar says the fifth general population and housing census was carried out](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/265803>). For the first time it was done entirely electronically, from mapping and collection to processing and analysis. [The document describes the fifth general population and housing census (RGPH) of 2023 as carried out under government supervision](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/289781>). [La Dépêche reports that the census counted nearly 5 million inhabitants](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=780743>).

**Results published.** [A regional workshop opened in Nouadhibou on 18 February 2026 to disseminate the definitive results of the fifth RGPH 2023](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/289781>). ANSADE's deputy director general attended. [ANSADE held a workshop in Atar on 3 August 2026](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/265803>). It presented the final results of the fifth general population and housing census for Adrar wilaya.
