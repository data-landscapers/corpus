# South Africa

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Statistics South Africa states Census 2022 was enumerated with reference night 2 February 2022 and provinces completing enumeration at different times between February and May 2022, counting 62,027,503 people](<https://census.statssa.gov.za/assets/documents/2022/P03014_Census_2022_Statistical_Release.pdf>). [The Deputy Minister in the Presidency says Census 2022 was conducted during the pandemic and 62 million South Africans were counted](<https://www.presidency.gov.za/keynotes-address-deputy-minister-presidency-nonceba-mhlauli-occasion-celebrating-stats-sa-census>). Four facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** [Statistics South Africa released the first phase of Census 2022 results in October 2023 and has since published thematic reports, withholding some themes such as income and labour](<https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/statistics-south-africa-census-2022-results-october-2023-22-aug-2024>).

**A household survey published.** [Statistics South Africa released the Quarterly Labour Force Survey Q2 2026 (P0211, fieldwork April to June 2026), a household-based sample survey of about 30,000 dwellings, reporting an unemployment rate of 33.6%](<https://www.statssa.gov.za/publications/P0211/P02112ndQuarter2026.pdf>).
