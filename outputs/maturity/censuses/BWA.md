# Botswana

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The brief refers to the 2022 Population and Housing Census as a de facto count of the entire population](<https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/BOTSWANA%20MULTI%20TOPIC%20HOUSEHOLD%20SURVEY%202024%205%20%20%20%20Labour%20Force%20Preliminary%20Stats%20Brief.pdf>). [The annual report gives a 2022 actual count of 2,359,609 people and states the Population and Housing Census was conducted](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>). [Minister Segokgo, reported by BOPA, cites the 2022 census as the source of Botswana's population figure, so a census was counted in 2022](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81395>). [The profile lists a population census within the last 10 years as yes, dated 2011 (2011, before the ten-year window)](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [The brief refers to the 2022 Population and Housing Census as a de facto count of the entire population](<https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/BOTSWANA%20MULTI%20TOPIC%20HOUSEHOLD%20SURVEY%202024%205%20%20%20%20Labour%20Force%20Preliminary%20Stats%20Brief.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [Statistics Botswana presented findings of the nationally representative 2024 Household Access and Individual Use of ICTs Survey (fieldwork June to August 2024), e.g. internet use 57.5%](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/46/10_EGH2026_DataCollectionBotswana_TebogoTsienyane.pdf>). [The profile lists MICS2 (2000) as an available national health survey (2000, before the three-year window)](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>). [The profile lists a DHS (1988) as an available national health survey (1988, before the three-year window)](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>).
