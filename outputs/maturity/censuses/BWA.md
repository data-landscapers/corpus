# Botswana

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The brief refers to the 2022 Population and Housing Census as a de facto count of the entire population](<https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/BOTSWANA%20MULTI%20TOPIC%20HOUSEHOLD%20SURVEY%202024%205%20%20%20%20Labour%20Force%20Preliminary%20Stats%20Brief.pdf>). [The annual report gives a 2022 actual count of 2,359,609 people and states the Population and Housing Census was conducted](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>). [Minister Segokgo, reported by BOPA, cites the 2022 census as the source of Botswana's population figure](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81395>). [An August 2019 profile gave 2011 as the year of the previous census](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [The brief refers to the 2022 Population and Housing Census as a de facto count of the entire population](<https://www.statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/BOTSWANA%20MULTI%20TOPIC%20HOUSEHOLD%20SURVEY%202024%205%20%20%20%20Labour%20Force%20Preliminary%20Stats%20Brief.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [In September 2026 Statistics Botswana presented findings of the nationally representative 2024 Household Access and Individual Use of ICTs Survey](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/46/10_EGH2026_DataCollectionBotswana_TebogoTsienyane.pdf>). [Fieldwork ran from June to August 2024, and internet use stood at 57.5%](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/meetings/wp-content/uploads/IPS/documents/46/10_EGH2026_DataCollectionBotswana_TebogoTsienyane.pdf>). [An August 2019 profile listed MICS2 from 2000 and a DHS from 1988 as national health surveys](<https://www.measureevaluation.org/his-strengthening-resource-center/country-profiles-1/botswana.html>).
