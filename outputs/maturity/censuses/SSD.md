# South Sudan

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Radio Tamazuj quotes the NBS chair saying the Council of Ministers approved the budget for the census, which is still awaiting funding](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/qa-census-should-be-rolled-out-before-elections-dr-ting>). [The plan cites the 2008 Sudan Housing and Population Census as having recorded a disability prevalence of 5.1% in South Sudan (424,000 of 8.28 million), though the census is described as controversial (2008, before the ten-year window)](<https://mgcsw.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/national-diability-plan-of-Action.pdf>). Four facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [Radio Tamazuj quotes the NBS chair saying the Council of Ministers approved the budget for the census, which is still awaiting funding](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/qa-census-should-be-rolled-out-before-elections-dr-ting>).

**A household survey published.** [Eye Radio reported on 21 April 2026 that the National Bureau of Statistics, with UNICEF, released the 2025 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey covering more than 10,000 households nationwide](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-first-ever-survey-exposes-gaps-in-women-and-children-services/>). [The National Bureau of Statistics with UNICEF released the results of the 2025 South Sudan MICS on 21 April 2026, sampling 12,000 households and interviewing over 10,000 across all states](<https://www.unicef.org/southsudan/press-releases/new-national-survey-shows-serious-challenges-children-south-sudan-also-clear-areas>).
