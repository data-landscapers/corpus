# South Sudan

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [With UNFPA support the Bureau of Statistics prepared a census dossier and an operations manual in 2025](<https://amnon.co.uk/2026/01/14/bureau-of-statistics-launches-new-website-as-south-sudans-data-capacity-grows/>). [Radio Tamazuj quotes the NBS chair saying the Council of Ministers approved the budget for the census, which is still awaiting funding](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/qa-census-should-be-rolled-out-before-elections-dr-ting>). [The last census, the 2008 Sudan Housing and Population Census, recorded a disability prevalence of 5.1% in South Sudan](<https://mgcsw.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/national-diability-plan-of-Action.pdf>). [That was 424,000 of 8.28 million people, in a census the plan calls controversial](<https://mgcsw.gov.ss/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/national-diability-plan-of-Action.pdf>).

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. [Radio Tamazuj quotes the NBS chair saying the Council of Ministers approved the budget for the census](<https://www.radiotamazuj.org/en/news/article/qa-census-should-be-rolled-out-before-elections-dr-ting>). The census is still awaiting funding.

**A household survey published.** [Eye Radio reported on 21 April 2026 that the National Bureau of Statistics, with UNICEF, released the 2025 Multiple Indicator Cluster Survey](<https://www.eyeradio.org/s-sudan-first-ever-survey-exposes-gaps-in-women-and-children-services/>). It covered more than 10,000 households nationwide. [The National Bureau of Statistics with UNICEF released the results of the 2025 South Sudan MICS on 21 April 2026](<https://www.unicef.org/southsudan/press-releases/new-national-survey-shows-serious-challenges-children-south-sudan-also-clear-areas>). It sampled 12,000 households and interviewed over 10,000 across all states.
