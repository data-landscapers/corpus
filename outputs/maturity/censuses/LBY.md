# Libya

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The Libya Observer reports the Government of National Unity reviewed progress on the seventh national population census, the first since 2006](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-prepares-national-population-census>). Libya has been divided into census areas, and preparations are said to be on track per the approved schedule. [LibyaReview, citing an Al Jazeera report, says Libya's last nationwide census was completed in 2006](<https://libyareview.com/67171/whats-preventing-libya-from-holding-a-new-population-census/>). It says political division has since prevented the launch of a new one. The Ministry of Planning's statistics director says the same.

**Results published.** No source found reports preliminary or final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest fact: [in August 2024 the government reviewed progress on the seventh national census, the first since 2006](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-prepares-national-population-census>).

**A household survey published.** No source found reports results of a national household survey published since October 2023. The nearest fact: [in August 2024 Libya had been divided into census areas for the seventh census](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-prepares-national-population-census>).

***Not found***. No census counted since October 2016 has been found. Whether preparation continues is not established: it was on track in 2024, and in 2026 a new census had not been launched.
