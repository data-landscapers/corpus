# Central African Republic

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [ICASEES held the restitution of the third phase of data cleaning of the RGPH-4](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/398-icasees-la-troisieme-phase-dapurement-du-rgph-4-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape-vers-la-finalisation-des-resultats-du-recensement>). This is the country's first digital census, and its results are not yet final. [ICASEES states that after enumeration the fourth general population and housing census (RGPH-4) entered its processing phase](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/428-icasees-evaluation-a-mi-parcours-de-la-deuxieme-strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique>). Data cleaning ran from May to September 2026, before tabulation and publication of results. A committee [reviewed the first provisional results on 23 to 25 September 2026](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/425-icasees-retraite-de-travail-consacree-a-lapurement-et-a-la-tabulation-des-donnees-du-rgph-4>). ICASEES then [held a retreat at Boali on 2 to 5 October 2026 to clean and tabulate the data](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/427-icasees-la-mission-dappui-de-la-banque-mondiale-au-projet-hiswaca-sop2>).
