# Tanzania

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The ILFS 2025 sampling frame came from enumeration areas listed in the 2022 Population and Housing Census](<https://microdata.nbs.go.tz/index.php/catalog/65>). [Voter-eligibility estimates rest on the census counted in 2022](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [The deck gives Tanzania's population as 61.7 million from the 2022 census](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [A March 2026 TCRA presentation gives 61,741,120 people in 14,152,803 private households](<https://www.itu.int/itu-d/sites/projectumc/wp-content/uploads/sites/42/2026/03/Tanzania-UMC-workshop.pdf>).

**Results published.** [Tanzania released its 2022 census report within 45 days of data collection](<https://dailynews.co.tz/statistical-master-plan-implementation-on-track/>), the Statistician General said in March 2024.

**A household survey published.** [The NBS microdata catalogue released the Integrated Labour Force Survey 2025 (14,232 households, fieldwork January to December 2025, 99.63 percent response) on 15 July 2026 as edited final data for public use, with a 2026 NBS key-indicators report cited](<https://microdata.nbs.go.tz/index.php/catalog/65>).

***Also on file***.
