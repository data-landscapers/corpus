# Angola

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INE states the RGPH 2024 was enumerated nationwide from 19 September to 20 December 2024 (census moment 19 September), using 79,423 field agents](<https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/ICOLE%20E%20BENGO.pdf>). [INE states it carried out the RGPH nationwide in 2024, reference date 19 September 2024, with collection extended to 20 December 2024](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/NDc0MTE%3D>). [Angop reports the RGPH 2024 enumeration began on 19 September 2024 and ended on 19 November 2024, counting 36,604,681 residents](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/censo-2024-luanda-continua-a-concentrar-maior-numero-da-populacao-angolana/>). [Angop reports INE counted 8,665,510 people in Luanda province in the RGPH 2024](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/populacao-de-luanda-cresce-em-media-3-8-por-ano/>).

**Results published.** [INE Angola states it released the definitive results of Census 2024 (RGPH 2024) on 20 November 2025, ten months after the end of mass data collection and six months after the coverage survey](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Relatorio_Sintese_CENSO_2024_INE_Nov_2025_c5fba11d70.pdf>). [INE states the definitive national results of the RGPH 2024 were published on 20 November 2025, and this Icolo e Bengo provincial report followed](<https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/ICOLE%20E%20BENGO.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** No source found reports results of a national household survey published since October 2023.
