# Angola

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INE states the RGPH 2024 was enumerated nationwide from 19 September to 20 December 2024 (census moment 19 September), using 79,423 field agents](<https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/ICOLE%20E%20BENGO.pdf>). [INE states it carried out the RGPH nationwide in 2024, reference date 19 September 2024, with collection extended to 20 December 2024](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/NDc0MTE%3D>). [Angop reports the RGPH 2024 enumeration began on 19 September 2024 and ended on 19 November 2024, counting 36,604,681 residents](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/censo-2024-luanda-continua-a-concentrar-maior-numero-da-populacao-angolana/>). [Angop reports INE counted 8,665,510 people in Luanda province in the RGPH 2024](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/populacao-de-luanda-cresce-em-media-3-8-por-ano/>).

**Results published.** INE [released the final results of the RGPH 2024 on 20 November 2025](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/Relatorio_Sintese_CENSO_2024_INE_Nov_2025_c5fba11d70.pdf>). That was ten months after the main count ended. [Provincial reports followed](<https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/ICOLE%20E%20BENGO.pdf>), the last for Luanda in February 2026.

**A household survey published.** A 2026 Countdown 2030 report on Angola [measures health coverage against the results of the DHS 2023 household survey](<https://www.countdown2030.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Angola.pdf>). It compares them with health-facility data and breaks them down by wealth, schooling and residence.

***Also on file***. [REMPE 2019-2020](<https://estamosjuntos.co.ao/registadas-83-mil-empresas-em-angola/>); [Inquérito de Cobertura do Censo 2024](<https://censo2024.ine.gov.ao/ICOLE%20E%20BENGO.pdf>).
