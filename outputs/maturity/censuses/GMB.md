# Gambia

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [UNFPA The Gambia reports that the Gambia Bureau of Statistics launched the preliminary report of the 2024 digital population and housing census on 17 September 2024, giving a population of 2.4 million, over 300,000 more than in 2013](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/news/unfpa-joins-gambia-bureau-statistics-and-partners-launch-2024-digital-population-and-housing>).

**Results published.** [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics launched the preliminary report of the 2024 census on 17 September 2024](<https://gambia.unfpa.org/en/news/unfpa-joins-gambia-bureau-statistics-and-partners-launch-2024-digital-population-and-housing>), a population of 2.4 million; final results are not yet held.

**A household survey published.** [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics lists the Gambia Labour Force Survey GLFS 2026 (Q1), alongside the 2019/20 DHS, MICS6 and IHS 2020, with reports and microdata for download](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads>). [The Gambia Bureau of Statistics published a report comparing its labour force survey across three rounds, 2023 Q1, 2025 Q1 and 2026 Q1](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/628-the-gambia-labour-market-progress-and-trends-2>).

***Also on file***. [Annual school census (MoBSE)](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>); [GBoS Microdata Catalog](<https://microdata.gbosdata.org/index.php/home>).
