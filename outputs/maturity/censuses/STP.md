# Sao Tome and Principe

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The 5th population and housing census was enumerated from 15 November to 15 December 2024](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/Recenseamentos/2024/Resultado_VRGPH%202024.pdf>). [It counted 212,017 people, with a legal population of 209,607](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/Recenseamentos/2024/Resultado_VRGPH%202024.pdf>). [Preliminary results were published on 31 December 2024; a July 2025 report still called its figures provisional](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/Recenseamentos/2024/Resultado_VRGPH%202024.pdf>). [The World Bank said the enumeration phase was completed in December 2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). [Final counts were to follow the post-enumeration survey, which ended on 4 April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>).

**Results published.** [The INE released on 25 July 2025 the final data of the fifth general population and housing census (V RGPH), counting 212,017 people on 15 November 2024, 209,607 of them the legal resident population](<https://rstp.st/2025/07/26/sao-tome-e-principe-tem-quase-210-mil-residentes-e-regista-aumento-drastico-da-emigracao-ine/>).

**A household survey published.** [The 2017 household budget survey put the poverty rate at 55.5%](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/03/26/52331/pobreza-extrema-atinge-milhares-de-familias-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>). [The INE president gave further poverty shares from it](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/03/26/52331/pobreza-extrema-atinge-milhares-de-familias-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).
