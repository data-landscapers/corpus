# Sao Tome and Principe

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The 5th national population and housing census (RGPH) was enumerated nationwide from 15 November to 15 December 2024, counting 212,017 people (209,607 legal population); preliminary results were published on 31 December 2024 and this July 2025 report calls its own figures provisional results](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/Recenseamentos/2024/Resultado_VRGPH%202024.pdf>). [The World Bank ISR states the enumeration phase of the housing and population census (Component 3 of the Digital STP project) was completed in December 2024; final population counts were still to come after the post-enumeration survey, which ended on 4 April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). Four facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** [The INE released on 25 July 2025 the final data of the fifth general population and housing census (V RGPH), counting 212,017 people on 15 November 2024, 209,607 of them the legal resident population](<https://rstp.st/2025/07/26/sao-tome-e-principe-tem-quase-210-mil-residentes-e-regista-aumento-drastico-da-emigracao-ine/>).

**A household survey published.** [The 2017 household budget survey (Inquerito ao Orcamento Familiar) put the poverty rate at 55.5%, so its results were published; the INE president gave further poverty shares (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/03/26/52331/pobreza-extrema-atinge-milhares-de-familias-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).
