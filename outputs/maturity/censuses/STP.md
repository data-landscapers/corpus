# Sao Tome and Principe

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The World Bank ISR states the enumeration phase of the housing and population census (Component 3 of the Digital STP project) was completed in December 2024; final population counts were still to come after the post-enumeration survey, which ended on 4 April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>). [The 5th national population and housing census (RGPH) was enumerated nationwide from 15 November to 15 December 2024, counting 212,017 people (209,607 legal population); preliminary results were published on 31 December 2024 and this July 2025 report calls its own figures provisional results](<https://www.ine.st/phocadownload/userupload/Documentos/Recenseamentos/2024/Resultado_VRGPH%202024.pdf>). Three facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Final results published.** No source held reports final results of a census counted since October 2016. The nearest held fact: [The World Bank ISR states the enumeration phase of the housing and population census (Component 3 of the Digital STP project) was completed in December 2024; final population counts were still to come after the post-enumeration survey, which ended on 4 April 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099041125035024996/pdf/P177158-11847402-82b3-4460-9bf4-0fe106ed4291.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [The 2017 household budget survey (Inquerito ao Orcamento Familiar) put the poverty rate at 55.5%, so its results were published; the INE president gave further poverty shares (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.telanon.info/sociedade/2026/03/26/52331/pobreza-extrema-atinge-milhares-de-familias-em-sao-tome-e-principe/>).
