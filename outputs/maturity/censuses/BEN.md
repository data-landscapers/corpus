# Benin

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [INStaD director general Laurent Mahounou announced that main mapping for the fifth census (RGPH5) began on 19 March 2024 in the remaining 74 communes for two months, after a pilot in Cotonou, Cove and Parakou](<https://www.banouto.bj/societe/article/20240323-recensement-general-de-la-population-au-benin-rgph5-demarrage-de-la-cartographie-principale-dans-74-communes>). [INStaD says it trained about fifty trainers from 22 to 25 January 2024 ahead of cartographic mapping for the fifth census (RGPH5), after a mapping pilot in Cotonou, Cove and Parakou in September-October 2023](<https://rgph5.instad.bj/2024/02/01/linstad-organise-la-formation-des-formateurs-rgph5/>). Five facts are held on the country's census and surveys, all given here: no news story or official notice read for this study says more, and the census and survey reports themselves were not read.

**Results published.** [INSAE states the Council of Ministers adopted the definitive RGPH4 results on 21 May 2015, and the definitive results leaflet is dated June 2015 (2015, before the ten-year window)](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/insae-statistiques/enquetes-recensements/RGPH/1.RGPH_4/Indicateurs-et-Projetcions/R%C3%A9sultats%20d%C3%A9finitifs%20RGPH4.pdf>).

**A household survey published.** [INStaD published the main indicators of the national EHCVM (2021-2022, fieldwork 2021 and 2022), representative for the 12 departments and by residence area, across 13 themes and about ten SDG indicators](<https://www.instad.bj/actualites/790-document-des-principaux-indicateurs-ehcvm2>).

***Also on file***. [Recensement National de l'Agriculture (RNA)](<https://d-portal.iatistandard.org/ctrack.html#view=act&aid=XM-DAC-41301-762022>); [ENBT1-2023](<https://www.instad.bj/actualites/804-tableaux-de-synthese-de-la-premiere-edition-de-l-enquete-nationale-budget-temps-enbt1-2023>).

***Not found***. No source found reports a census counted since October 2016.
