# Zimbabwe

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [UNICEF Zimbabwe and ZIMSTAT announced in April 2024 a rollout of provincial and district data profiles derived from analysis of the 2022 Population and Housing Census](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/press-releases/unicef-zimbabwe-and-zimstat-launch-provincial-and-district-data-profiles-nationwide>). [The Census and Statistics Act refers to the census of 2012 as already held, setting the next census to be completed by 1 July 2021 (2012, before the ten-year window)](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2007/1/eng@2020-09-04>). **A household survey published.** [ZIMSTAT and POTRAZ jointly released the 2025 ICT Access by Households and Use by Individuals Survey, a nationwide survey of 9,245 households in all 10 provinces with a 91.3% response rate](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058521/mobile-internet-access-surges-as-zimbabwe-embraces-digital-shift>). [ZIMSTAT published the 2023-24 Zimbabwe Demographic and Health Survey (seventh DHS in Zimbabwe, fieldwork 4 December 2023 to 29 May 2024, 10,725 households interviewed) with a full report available](<https://zimstat.co.zw/zimbabwe-demographic-and-health-survey/>).
