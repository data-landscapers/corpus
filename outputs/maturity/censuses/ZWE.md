# Zimbabwe

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [The Population and Housing Census was taken in April 2022](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusivity-leaving-no-place-no-person-behind/>), The Herald reports. In April 2024 [UNICEF Zimbabwe and ZIMSTAT began rolling out provincial and district data profiles](<https://www.unicef.org/zimbabwe/press-releases/unicef-zimbabwe-and-zimstat-launch-provincial-and-district-data-profiles-nationwide>) drawn from it. [The Census and Statistics Act names the 2012 census](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2007/1/eng@2020-09-04>) as already held and set the next for 2021.

**Results published.** ZIMSTAT has published [a preliminary report of the 2022 census on functional difficulties](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/digital-inclusivity-leaving-no-place-no-person-behind/>). It counts 1.2 million people with difficulty in physical activities and 192,000 with permanent disabilities. No final results of the census have been found.

**A household survey published.** ZIMSTAT and POTRAZ released [the 2025 ICT Access by Households and Use by Individuals Survey](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200058521/mobile-internet-access-surges-as-zimbabwe-embraces-digital-shift>) in July 2026. It covered 9,245 households in all ten provinces. ZIMSTAT also published [the 2023-24 Demographic and Health Survey](<https://zimstat.co.zw/zimbabwe-demographic-and-health-survey/>), with 10,725 households interviewed.

***Also on file***. [2023 Economic Census](<https://zimbabwenow.co.zw/articles/15650/76-of-zim-businesses-unregistered-zimstat>); [ED46 annual school census](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/en/articles/zimbabwes-climate-smart-annual-school-census>).

***Not found***. No final results of the 2022 census have been found.
