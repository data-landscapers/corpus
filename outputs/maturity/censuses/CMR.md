# Cameroon

## Censuses and surveys

**A census taken or in preparation.** [Counting for the 4th general population and housing census, launched on 24 April 2026, closed on 15 September 2026](<https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-le-4e-recensement-general-de-la-population-et-de-lhabitat-sacheve/>). [It was coupled with the agriculture and livestock census](<https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-le-4e-recensement-general-de-la-population-et-de-lhabitat-sacheve/>). [Unofficial coverage was about 75%, against standards of 90-95%](<https://fr.journalducameroun.com/cameroun-le-4e-recensement-general-de-la-population-et-de-lhabitat-sacheve/>). [A Prime Minister's order made public 31 July 2026 set a complementary enumeration period from 1 August to 15 September 2026 for the 4th general population and housing census](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79313/en.html/details_2>). [ The aim was to find households not yet identified or counted](<https://www.cameroon-tribune.cm/article.html/79313/en.html/details_2>). [The government, with FAO and UNFPA, field-tested a hybrid method in October and November 2025 for the fourth population census](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/news/detail/cameroon-tests-an-innovative-census-strategy-in-crisis-zones/en>). [ The census was merged with the agricultural census and scheduled for 2026](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/news/detail/cameroon-tests-an-innovative-census-strategy-in-crisis-zones/en>).
