# Zimbabwe

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union keeps a [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>). It shows no signature, ratification or deposit for Zimbabwe. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. [MISA said in July 2026](<https://misa.org/blog/legislative-and-regulatory-reform-in-zimbabwes-digital-sector-a-rights-based-perspective/>) that Zimbabwe has signed the convention. It has not ratified it, MISA said.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Zimbabwe, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
