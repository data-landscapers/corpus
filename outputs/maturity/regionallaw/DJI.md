# Djibouti

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Djibouti as having signed it on 12 May 2023, with no ratification or deposit. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. A [November 2025 report](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1211-50395-djibouti-advances-bill-to-create-national-cybersecurity-authority>) says Djibouti ratified the convention in November 2023.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Djibouti, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.

***Also on file***. [UN Convention on Cybercrime](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1211-50395-djibouti-advances-bill-to-create-national-cybersecurity-authority>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national law or rule was passed to give effect to a shared text. The African Union's register of February 2026 shows no ratification deposited.
