# Ghana

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Ghana as having signed it on 4 July 2017, ratified it on 13 May 2019 and deposited the instrument on 3 June 2019. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. The Bank of Ghana and the National Bank of Rwanda [signed a memorandum on 25 February 2025](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/PRESS-RELEASE-GHANA-AND-RWANDA-SIGN-A-MEMORANDUM-OF-UNDERSTANDING-MOU.pdf>) to let fintech firms licensed by one operate under the other.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Ghana, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
