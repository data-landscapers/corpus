# Lesotho

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** Lesotho signed the African Union's Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, on 30 November 2023. The Union's [register dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records its ratification on 5 February 2025. The instrument was deposited on 16 February 2025. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The convention has been in force since 8 June 2023.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African text is recorded for Lesotho. Nor is a licence or signature recognised under one, a request between authorities answered, or a ruling made. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists when each state signed, ratified and deposited. It says nothing of national law.
