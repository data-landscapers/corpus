# Togo

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>), records Togo's signature on 2 April 2019. It records ratification on 30 September 2021 and the instrument deposited on 19 October 2021. On 23 July 2024 in Niamey, [Togo signed a protocol with the Confederation of Sahel States](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>) on linking customs IT systems, its revenue office says.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** On 6 March 2025 [the Office Togolais des Recettes issued notice 004/2025](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/blog/avis-communiques/1431-avis-aux-operateurs-economiques-n-004-2025-otr-cg-cddi-del-relatif-a-l-interconnexion-des-systemes-informatiques-douaniers-du-togo-et-du-mali.html>) applying that protocol. It says a pilot link between the customs systems of Togo and Mali, through Burkina Faso, runs on the Lomé-Bamako corridor from 10 March 2025. No national law giving effect to the Malabo Convention has been found.
