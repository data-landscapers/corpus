# Gambia

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The minister of communications and the digital economy told a UN commission in April 2024 that [the Government of The Gambia ratified the African Union's Malabo Convention in March 2024](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162024_wsis_s01_Gambia_OBah_en.pdf>). The [minister signed the convention for the country in Addis Ababa on 2 December 2022](<https://mocde.gov.gm/3189-2/>). The African Union's [register of the convention dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records the signature and no ratification or deposit.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Gambia, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
