# Rwanda

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Rwanda as having signed it on 16 April 2019, ratified it on 14 November 2019 and deposited the instrument on 21 November 2019. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. The National Bank of Rwanda and the Bank of Ghana [signed a memorandum on 25 February 2025](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/PRESS-RELEASE-GHANA-AND-RWANDA-SIGN-A-MEMORANDUM-OF-UNDERSTANDING-MOU.pdf>) to let fintech firms licensed by one operate under the other.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Rwanda, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
