# Rwanda

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The [African Union's register of the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>), records Rwanda's signature on 16 April 2019. It records ratification on 14 November 2019 and the instrument deposited on 21 November 2019. The register counts 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The National Bank of Rwanda and the Bank of Ghana [signed a memorandum on 25 February 2025](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/PRESS-RELEASE-GHANA-AND-RWANDA-SIGN-A-MEMORANDUM-OF-UNDERSTANDING-MOU.pdf>). Its aim is to let fintech firms licensed by one operate under the other. The Bank of Ghana's governor [offered that licence passporting framework as a model for more than 10 other countries](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>). That was in October 2026. The National Bank of Rwanda and the Central Bank of Kenya [signed a memorandum to build licence passporting for payment firms](<https://www.centralbank.go.ke/uploads/press_releases/470135796_Press%20Release%20-%20MOU%20Signing%20Between%20CBK%20and%20NBR%20on%20Mutual%20Recognition%20Framework.pdf>). They signed it on 11 March 2026.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law or decree giving effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Rwanda. No licence recognised across a border, request between authorities or ruling under such a text is recorded either. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) gives the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
