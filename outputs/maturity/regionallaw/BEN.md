# Benin

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Benin as having signed it on 28 January 2015. Benin ratified it on 14 May 2024 and deposited the instrument on 12 November 2024. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The convention has been in force since 8 June 2023.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** [A paper to a National Assembly workshop in January 2024 said Benin's digital code omits parts of an ECOWAS act](<https://julienhounkpe.info/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Contribution0.pdf>). The act is the 2010 ECOWAS Additional Act on personal data protection. The paper named its rules on sensitive data and on transfers abroad. No national law passed to give effect to a shared African text has been found. Nor has a licence recognised across a border, or a ruling under such a text.
