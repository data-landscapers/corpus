# Angola

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Angola as having ratified it on 21 February 2020 and deposited the instrument on 11 May 2020. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. Angola's [Resolution 33/19 of 9 July 2019](<https://angolex.com/paginas/resolucoes/convencao-da-uniao-africana-sobre-ciberseguranca-e-proteccao-de-dados-33a-19a.html>) is the national instrument on the convention.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Angola, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.

***Also on file***. [Carta CPLP de Direitos e Princípios em Ambientes Digitais](<https://secretariadoexecutivo.cplp.org/media/axzbbtqn/2_cartacplp_princi-piosdireitosambientesdigitais.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national law or rule was passed to give effect to a shared text.
