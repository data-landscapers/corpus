# Central African Republic

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The Central African Republic has taken no action on the African Union's Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention. That is what an [ACTPOL brief of September 2026](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) says, citing the Union's register dated 2 February 2026. The country has neither signed nor ratified it. No other shared African text on a digital matter is recorded as signed by the country or binding on it. That covers a treaty, a regional community's act and a model law alike.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African text is recorded for the country. Nor is a licence or signature recognised under one, a request between authorities answered, or a ruling made. The [ACTPOL brief](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) records no step taken under the convention.
