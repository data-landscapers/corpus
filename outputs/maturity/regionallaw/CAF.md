# Central African Republic

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The Central African monetary union has a shared [QR-code payment standard](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). Its ministerial committee validated the standard in April 2026. It was launched on 29 July 2026 for the six member states. The Central African Republic is named among them. The country has taken no action on the African Union's Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection. That is what an [ACTPOL brief of September 2026](<https://www.actpol.org/research/central-africa-laws-before-regulators-cameroon>) says, citing the Union's register of 2 February 2026. The country has neither signed nor ratified the convention.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law passed to give effect to a shared African text is recorded. No licence or signature recognised under one is recorded either. Nor is a request between authorities answered, or a ruling made. The [payment standard](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>) was first tested in Gabon. In July 2026 its extension to the other five states was still under way.

***Not found***. No ratification of a shared African text is recorded. Nor is a national law giving effect to one.
