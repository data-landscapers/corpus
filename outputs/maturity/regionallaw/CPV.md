# Cape Verde

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>), records Cape Verde's ratification on 13 November 2020. It records the instrument deposited on 5 February 2022. The National Assembly [approved accession by Resolution 153/IX/2020, published on 17 March 2020](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/3161>). The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. On [an ECOWAS mission to Praia on 11 June 2024](<https://www.ecowas.int/ecowas-conducts-advocacy-on-the-deployment-of-the-ecowas-national-biometric-identity-card-in-cabo-verde/>), the ECOWAS Commission said Cape Verde had signed up to the regional biometric identity card. Cape Verde's officials said adopting the card would need a legal review and a political decision.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No law, decree or regulation giving effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Cape Verde. No licence or signature recognised across a border is recorded either. Nor is a request between authorities answered, or a ruling under such a text. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) gives the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited. It says nothing of national law.
