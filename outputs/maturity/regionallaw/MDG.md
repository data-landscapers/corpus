# Madagascar

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Madagascar as having ratified it on 17 July 2025 and deposited the instrument on 15 September 2025. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. The [constitutional court ruled on 10 July 2024](<https://www.hcc.gov.mg/?p=9255>) that the convention and the law authorising its ratification conform to the Constitution.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Madagascar, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
