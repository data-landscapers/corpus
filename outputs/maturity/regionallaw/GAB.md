# Gabon

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union keeps a [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>). It shows no signature, ratification or deposit for Gabon. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states. The convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. A [September 2026 report](<https://notreafrik.com/convention-de-malabo-ratifications/>) says Gabon ratified the convention in 2026. Its regulator says Gabon is bound by the [Central African roaming regulation that the community's telecoms ministers adopted on 8 September 2020](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/2025/0217/Documents/01-Rene%cc%81-Pierre%20Favre.pdf>).

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Gabon. Nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited. It says nothing of national law.
