# Cote d'Ivoire

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Cote d'Ivoire as having ratified it on 8 March 2023 and deposited the instrument on 3 April 2023. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. A [2022 fact sheet](<https://dataprotection.africa/cote-divoire/>) lists the country as a signatory to the West African community's Supplementary Act on Personal Data Protection.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Cote d'Ivoire, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
