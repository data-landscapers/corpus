# Cameroon

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Cameroon as having signed it on 12 August 2021, with no ratification or deposit, and a [September 2024 report from the Cameroonian press](<https://www.dgb.cm/le-cenadi-sensibilise-les-administrations-a-la-protection-des-donnees/>) likewise says ratification is awaited. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. Cameroon is also bound by [regulation 02/26 of the Central African monetary union, of 8 April 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>), under which an [interoperable QR-code payment standard was launched for the six member states on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>).

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Cameroon, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.

***Also on file***. [GIMAC Pay](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/17/africa-is-building-a-single-payments-market-but-its-currencies-remain-divided/>).

***Not found***. No source found dates a ratification or accession.
