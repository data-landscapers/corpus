# Equatorial Guinea

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** The African Union's [register of its Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) shows no signature, ratification or deposit for Equatorial Guinea. The register counts 21 signatures and 20 instruments deposited among the Union's 55 member states, and the convention has been in force since 8 June 2023. The [Chamber of Deputies approved ratification on 25 March 2026](<https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/la_camara_de_diputados_aprueba_la_ratificacion_de_dos_convenios_internacionales>), and the [foreign minister signed the convention in Addis Ababa on 28 July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/au-infrastructure-and-energy_equatorial-guinea-signs-the-malabo-convention-activity-7488275982521565184-ETtI>).

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Equatorial Guinea, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The [African Union's register](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) lists the dates each state signed, ratified and deposited, and says nothing of national law.
