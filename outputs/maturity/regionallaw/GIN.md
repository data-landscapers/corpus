# Guinea

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** [Presidential decree D/2017/184/PRG/SGG, signed at Conakry on 26 July 2017](<https://anssi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ratification.jpg>), ratifies the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, known as the Malabo Convention, under authorising law L/2017/023/AN of 16 June 2017. The African Union's [status list of 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>) records Guinea's ratification on 31 July 2018 and its deposit on 16 October 2018. In October 2025 the ministry's secretary-general [said Guinea had ratified the convention](<https://lepunch.net/2025/10/14/intelligence-artificielle-la-guinee-sengage-vers-une-strategie-nationale-via-un-atelier-diagnostic/>).

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law, decree or regulation passed to give effect to a shared African legal text is recorded for Guinea, and nor is a licence or signature recognised, a request between authorities answered or a ruling under one. The nearest held fact is the [African Union's status list](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>), which gives the dates of ratification and deposit and says nothing of national law.
