# Uganda

## Regional digital law taken into national law

**African digital law signed and ratified.** [Uganda's parliamentary foreign affairs committee said in November 2026 that Uganda has ratified the East African Community Protocol on ICT Networks](<https://horneconomicreview.com/2026/11/02/uganda-mps-push-eala-to-pressure-eac-states-on-ict-protocol-ratification/>). [It said only Uganda and Rwanda have signed and ratified it](<https://horneconomicreview.com/2026/11/02/uganda-mps-push-eala-to-pressure-eac-states-on-ict-protocol-ratification/>). [The protocol, adopted in 2013, covers infrastructure sharing, cross-border connectivity and cybersecurity](<https://horneconomicreview.com/2026/11/02/uganda-mps-push-eala-to-pressure-eac-states-on-ict-protocol-ratification/>). The African Union's [register of the Malabo Convention, dated 2 February 2026](<https://au.int/sites/default/files/treaties/29560-sl-AFRICAN_UNION_CONVENTION_ON_CYBER_SECURITY_AND_PERSONAL_DATA_PROTECTION_0.pdf>), shows no signature for Uganda. A police director [said on 12 August 2026](<https://observer.ug/technology/african-cybersecurity-solutions-akin-to-glass-house-with-fancy-curtains-uganda-police/>) that Uganda has not ratified it.

**Taken into national law and applied across a border.** No national law giving effect to the ICT protocol or another shared text has been found. No use of one across a border has been found either. [The committee asked ministers in November 2026 how Uganda aligns its ICT policies with the protocol](<https://horneconomicreview.com/2026/11/02/uganda-mps-push-eala-to-pressure-eac-states-on-ict-protocol-ratification/>). A police director [said in August 2026](<https://observer.ug/technology/african-cybersecurity-solutions-akin-to-glass-house-with-fancy-curtains-uganda-police/>) that Uganda still needs to carry the Malabo Convention into national law.
