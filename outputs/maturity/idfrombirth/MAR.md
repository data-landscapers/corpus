# Morocco

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Hespress says Law 36-21 provides for a national digital platform with a civil and social identifier assigned to every birth, Moroccan or foreign resident](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>). [CRVS Laws states that under Law 36.21 Article 30 a civil and social digital identifier is assigned at birth registration, but only to citizens](<https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/birth-registration/id-related-services>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).
