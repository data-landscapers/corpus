# Morocco

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In October 2025 Hespress said Law 36-21 provides for a national digital platform with a civil and social identifier](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>). [The identifier is assigned to every birth, Moroccan or foreign resident](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>). [In 2024 CRVS Laws said Article 30 of the law assigns the identifier at birth registration, but only to citizens](<https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/birth-registration/id-related-services>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in October 2025 Hespress said Law 36-21 provides for a national digital platform](<https://fr.hespress.com/447050-interieur-les-walis-sommes-daccelerer-la-numerisation-de-letat-civil.html>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 CRVS Laws said the identifier is assigned at birth registration](<https://www.crvslaws.org/morocco/birth-registration/id-related-services>).
