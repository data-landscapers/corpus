# Benin

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [ANIP director Aristide Adjinacou says the single-desk pilot in ten health facilities registered over 13,000 births in six months (over 76%), with the aim that every child receive a personal identification number (NPI) and birth certificate in the first days of life](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>). [Benin piloted from October 2025 a digital civil registration that issues a newborn a Personal Identification Number on registration, supported by UNICEF and WURI](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/benin-reports-75-of-identification-services-accessed-through-digital-platforms>).

**Given nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>).

**All births registered.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/aristide-adjinacou-directeur-general-de-lanip-nous-voulons-faire-de-chaque-maternite-une-veritable-porte-dentree-vers-lidentite-legale>).
