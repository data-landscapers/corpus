# Benin

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [ANIP's May 2026 deck says that for every birth since 1 April 2021 the child's NPI is obtained by declaring the birth to the RNPP, with the birth certificate generated automatically](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Triomphe Mag, citing ANIP's report of 24 February 2026, says that in the ten pilot health facilities running since 20 August 2025 parents can receive the child's NPI by SMS in under 48 hours after the birth is registered](<https://www.triomphemag.com/enregistrement-des-naissances-au-benin-le-guichet-unique-de-lanip-fait-bondir-le-taux-de-2195-a-7656-en-six-mois/>). [Benin piloted from October 2025 a digital civil registration that issues a newborn a Personal Identification Number on registration, supported by UNICEF and WURI](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202602/benin-reports-75-of-identification-services-accessed-through-digital-platforms>).

**Given nationally.** [ANIP's May 2026 deck says that every birth declaration since 1 April 2021 is made to the RNPP and gives the child an NPI and an entry in the national civil status file](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
