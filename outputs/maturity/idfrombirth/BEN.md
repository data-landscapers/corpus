# Benin

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** In Benin a child's personal number, the NPI, comes from declaring the birth. [The identity agency ANIP says every birth declared since 1 April 2021 gives the child an NPI](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). The birth certificate is then generated automatically. [In October 2026 an official said the digital identity is assigned from birth](<https://transcontinents.africa/fr/article/e-gouvernance-le-benin-et-la-mauritanie-a-lecole-estonienne>). [Since August 2025 ten health facilities have registered births at the maternity itself](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/le-b%C3%A9nin-modernise-lenregistrement-des-naissances-avec-un-guichet-unique-digital>). [There, ANIP says, the NPI reaches parents by SMS within 72 hours](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/b%C3%A9nin-journ%C3%A9e-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-2026>). By February 2026 over 13,000 of 17,071 children declared there had been registered.

**Given nationally.** The NPI is given for births across the country. [ANIP's May 2026 deck says every birth declaration since April 2021 goes to the national register](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). Each gives the child an NPI and a civil-status entry.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of all births and deaths registered. The nearest fact is [the pilot's 13,000 registrations by February 2026](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/b%C3%A9nin-journ%C3%A9e-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-2026>).
