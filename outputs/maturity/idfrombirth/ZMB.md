# Zambia

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In October 2023 the INRIS deputy project manager said persons will be allocated a legal identity number at birth registration](<https://dailynationzambia.com/2023/10/govt-digitises-nrcs/>). [The number is to be their citizenship identifier for life](<https://dailynationzambia.com/2023/10/govt-digitises-nrcs/>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in October 2023 INRIS planned a lifelong citizenship identifier](<https://dailynationzambia.com/2023/10/govt-digitises-nrcs/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in October 2023 the number was to be allocated at birth registration](<https://dailynationzambia.com/2023/10/govt-digitises-nrcs/>).
