# Botswana

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In August 2026 Statistics Botswana said the National Identification System gives every citizen child a unique birth registration number at birth](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [The number becomes the Omang number at age 16](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**Given nationally.** [In August 2026 Statistics Botswana said registration runs through 35 district offices and 17 on-site points](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [The number was introduced in 2003](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in August 2026 Statistics Botswana said the number reaches every citizen child at birth](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).
