# Liberia

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The Liberian Investigator reports that the NIR changed its system in June 2023](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-partners-launch-national-id-documents-for-children-under-six/>). [It now assigns unique identification numbers to children under six](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-partners-launch-national-id-documents-for-children-under-six/>). [The programme, called Enrolment at Birth, has a pilot in Montserrado, Grand Bassa and Gbarpolu](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-partners-launch-national-id-documents-for-children-under-six/>). [The NIR launched it in September 2023 to enrol over 500,000 children aged 0-5](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>). [Their records go into a National Childbirth Enrolment System still being developed](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact is [the pilot of Enrolment at Birth in three counties](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-partners-launch-national-id-documents-for-children-under-six/>). It enrols children with the NIR, not at birth registration.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [in June 2023 the NIR began assigning numbers to children under six in an 'Enrolment at Birth' pilot in three counties](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-partners-launch-national-id-documents-for-children-under-six/>).

***Not found***. No source found says children receive a number when their birth is registered. Enrolment by the NIR is separate from birth registration.
