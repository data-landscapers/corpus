# Kenya

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [PS Belio Kipsang said all children will be issued a Unique Personal Identifier (Maisha Number) at birth, kept for life and becoming the national ID card number at 18, through an e-notification system that is built and to be rolled out soon](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>). [Kenya's Civil Registration Services list as next step the adoption of a Unique Personal Identifier, a lifelong identifier assigned at birth](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [The Interior Secretary says the coming system will give every Kenyan registered at birth a Unique Personal Identifier, serving as the birth certificate number and later the national ID identifier at 18](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/kenyas-birth-registration-reforms-set-the-stage-for-automatic-national-id-issuance>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>).

***Also on file***. [Maisha Namba](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/kenyas-birth-registration-reforms-set-the-stage-for-automatic-national-id-issuance>); [IPRS](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a number is given at birth registration in service: a 2024 statement that newborns receive the Maisha number disagrees with the May 2026 statement that the e-notification system is built and yet to roll out.
