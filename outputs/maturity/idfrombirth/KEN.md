# Kenya

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Kenya's Civil Registration Services list as next step the adoption of a Unique Personal Identifier, a lifelong identifier assigned at birth](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [The Interior Secretary says the coming system will give every Kenyan registered at birth a Unique Personal Identifier, serving as the birth certificate number and later the national ID identifier at 18](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/kenyas-birth-registration-reforms-set-the-stage-for-automatic-national-id-issuance>). [Earlier government announcements linked newborn registration to the planned Unique Personal Identifier (UPI), without dates or detail](<https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/10/14/electronic-birth-notifications-kenya/>).

**Given nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Maisha Namba](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/kenyas-birth-registration-reforms-set-the-stage-for-automatic-national-id-issuance>); [IPRS](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/govt-enhances-registration-of-persons-through-new-technology/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says: is a number given at birth in part of the country.
