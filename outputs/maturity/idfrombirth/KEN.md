# Kenya

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [On 6 October 2026 PS Belio Kipsang said birth e-notification would reach SHA-contracted hospitals by December 2026](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>). [That would let children receive a Unique Personal Identifier](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>). [In May 2026 he said every child will be issued the Maisha Number at birth](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>). [It is kept for life and becomes the national ID card number at 18](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>). [Kenya's May 2026 ID4Africa deck shows civil registration and the National Registration Bureau using the identifier](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-1-3-Kenya-FINAL.pdf>), between birth registration and the national ID. [In March 2026 a Births and Deaths Registration Bill was being validated to align registration with the identifier at birth](<https://citizen.digital/article/govt-moves-to-modernise-civil-registration-as-stakeholders-validate-births-and-deaths-registration-bill-n378534>). [Civil Registration Services list a lifelong identifier assigned at birth as a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). In November 2026 he told MPs that [the number had been piloted through electronic birth notification in Nairobi City County and Bomet County](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/how-kenya-s-digital-birth-registration-system-will-work--5663174>). [It is due to be issued through more than 7,500 public hospitals](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/how-kenya-s-digital-birth-registration-system-will-work--5663174>).
