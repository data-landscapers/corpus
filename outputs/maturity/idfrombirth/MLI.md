# Mali

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Mali's DNEC describes a paper community chain of village notebooks feeding birth registration and names a move to a digital model as a perspective, with no identity number given at birth](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Given nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-mali_0.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says: is a number at birth provided for or piloted.
