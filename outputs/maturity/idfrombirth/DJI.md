# Djibouti

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The Ministry of Digital Affairs says the National Identification Number is attributed from birth to any person entered in a civil register](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).
