# Djibouti

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In 2024 the Ministry of Digital Affairs said the National Identification Number is attributed from birth](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>). [It goes to any person entered in a civil register](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the ministry tied the number to entry in a civil register](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the number was attributed from birth](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

***Also on file***. [RNPP / NNI](<https://numerique.gouv.dj/Articles/detailArticleAvancee/15>).

***Not found***. The ministry's 2024 statement says whom the number is for. No source found since October 2023 shows every registered birth across the country receiving it.
