# Mauritius

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The Civil Status Division's database generates a unique identification number for each citizen at birth registration](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>). A 2024 Pay Research Bureau report says so.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the Pay Research Bureau said the number is generated at birth registration](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the Pay Research Bureau tied the number to birth registration](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Mobile digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/theres-no-digital-id-without-data-protection-mauritius-privacy-chief>); [Mauritius National Identity Card (MNIC 3.0)](<https://www.lemauricien.com/le-mauricien/smart-and-mobile-id-in-mauritius-recommendations-for-a-balanced-approach/664957>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. None says how many births and deaths are registered.
