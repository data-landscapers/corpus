# Cameroon

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [MINDDEVEL's deck says law 2024/016 institutes the attribution of a unique personal identification number (NIPU), without saying it is given at birth registration or in service](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).
