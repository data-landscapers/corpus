# Cameroon

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In December 2024 Cameroon's law 2024/016 instituted a unique personal identification number (NIPU), a MINDDEVEL presentation said](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in December 2024 a national law instituted the NIPU](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in December 2024 law 2024/016 provided for attributing the NIPU](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-3-Cameroun_FINAL.pdf>).
