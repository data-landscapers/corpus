# Angola

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Angop reports newborn registration at the Mbanza Kongo maternity leads to issue of the first Bilhete de Identidade, under the Nascer com Registo project, since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [Justice minister Marcy Lopes says every maternity or hospital service has a registration and BI post so newborns can register and get the BI on the spot, and children aged 0 to 12 can do so at these hospitals](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/mais-de-15-milhoes-de-cidadaos-possuem-bi/>). [Angola's Justice Minister says the second phase of the universalisation plan provides for issuing the BI at birth in maternity hospitals with civil registration services](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [The Justice Ministry's Simplifica initiative, presented by Secretary of State Osvaldo Amaro, continues with issuing the identity card (BI) right after birth](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>).
