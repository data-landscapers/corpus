# Angola

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Angop reports that the Mbanza Kongo maternity has issued newborns their first Bilhete de Identidade since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [This is under the Nascer com Registo project](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [Biometric Update said in November 2025 that Angola was beginning to issue ID cards to newborns at maternity wards](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/angola-begins-issuance-of-id-cards-to-newborns>). [It started in Mbanza Kongo, to be followed by the province's other municipalities](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/angola-begins-issuance-of-id-cards-to-newborns>). [The Justice Minister says the plan's second phase provides for the BI at birth in maternity hospitals](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [The Simplifica initiative continues with issuing the BI right after birth](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 newborn registration at the Mbanza Kongo maternity led to issue of a first identity card](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [It was done under the Nascer com Registo project](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [the Nascer com Registo project has run at the Mbanza Kongo maternity since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>).
