# Rwanda

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Rwanda's new Single Digital Identity is to be issued from birth. [A 2026 deck says the June 2023 law provides for a number from birth](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>). Enrolment was piloted in the Southern Province from October 2025, with numbers planned from October 2026. [In March 2026 the identity agency's head said the current system mainly covers people aged 16 and over](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>). [In September 2026 the new card was still described as coming](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/over-7-2-million-rwandans-complete-biometric-enrolment-for-electronic-ids/>). [In June 2024 the same official had said the agency now issues identity from birth](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202406/legal-reforms-adapted-to-tech-changes-data-safeguards-vital-for-expanding-digital-id-reach>). The later sources describe that as still to come.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/05/with-rwandas-new-digital-id-you-will-control-everything-anyone-sees/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/05/with-rwandas-new-digital-id-you-will-control-everything-anyone-sees/>).

***Also on file***. [Single Digital ID (SDID)](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/05/with-rwandas-new-digital-id-you-will-control-everything-anyone-sees/>).

***Not found***. No source found since 2024 says a child has received the new number. A 2024 statement that identity is issued from birth is not borne out by the later sources.
