# Cote d'Ivoire

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Côte d'Ivoire is working to give newborns their national identification number (NNI) in maternity wards. [In June 2025 the head of ONECI, the identity office, said this work was under way](<https://digitalmag.ci/ago-christian-kodia-le-numero-national-didentification-nni-est-lelement-principal-de-la-digitalisation-de-lidentite/>). [In September 2026 he said a campaign would soon create identities on digital terminals on the day of birth](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2026-promesse-dune-identite-legale-et-numerique-a-chaque-citoyen-en-cote-divoire/>). [In March 2025 he had told African registrars that the birth registration stays as a person's ID number](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202503/african-civil-registration-leaders-acsa-point-direction-for-needed-acceleration>). Biometrics are added later for services. No source says how many newborns have received an NNI.

**Given nationally.** No source found says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact is [the campaign announced in September 2026](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2026-promesse-dune-identite-legale-et-numerique-a-chaque-citoyen-en-cote-divoire/>).
