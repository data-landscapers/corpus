# Nigeria

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The NPC's Assistant Director of Civil Registration says a unique NIN is now generated for each child during birth registration and tied to the mother](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>). [The NPC release quotes the First Lady on an initiative by NPC, NIMC and Barnksforte to issue a national identification number to every child at birth, starting in 2024](<http://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/first-lady-launches-new-birth-certificate-to-boost-civil-registration-in-nigeria>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
