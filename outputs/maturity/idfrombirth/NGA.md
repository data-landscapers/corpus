# Nigeria

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The NPC chairman said the NIN has been integrated into the e-CRVS system and newborns can now receive a NIN at birth; Over 15,000 children registered in Jigawa have birth certificates processed for NIN generation, the chairman said](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-chairman-joins-stakeholders-to-celebrate-the-7th-national-day-of-identity-in-abuja>). [The NPC's Assistant Director of Civil Registration says a unique NIN is now generated for each child during birth registration and tied to the mother](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>). [The NPC release quotes the First Lady on an initiative by NPC, NIMC and Barnksforte to issue a national identification number to every child at birth, starting in 2024](<http://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/first-lady-launches-new-birth-certificate-to-boost-civil-registration-in-nigeria>).
