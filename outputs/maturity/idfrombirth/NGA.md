# Nigeria

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Some Nigerian newborns now receive a national identification number (NIN) at birth registration. [In September 2024 a civil registration official said a NIN is generated for each child during registration](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>). It is tied to the mother's record. [In September 2025 the population commission said the NIN is built into its e-CRVS system](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-chairman-joins-stakeholders-to-celebrate-the-7th-national-day-of-identity-in-abuja>). Over 15,000 children registered in Jigawa had certificates processed for a NIN. [Since November 2025 Lagos's e-Birth platform has linked each newborn it registers to a NIN](<https://dpi.africa/from-birth-to-nin-how-lagos-is-building-legal-identity-for-every-child/>). [In May 2025 four agencies agreed a phased rollout in eleven pilot states](<https://independent.ng/npc-unicef-nimc-algon-forge-strategic-alliance-to-scale-up-birth-registration/>). [UNICEF said in June 2026 that the aim is a NIN for every child from birth](<https://data.unicef.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/A-Statistical-Profile-of-Birth-Registration-in-Nigeria_final-June-2026.pdf>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact: [in September 2025 the NPC chairman said newborns can now receive a NIN at birth through the e-CRVS system](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-chairman-joins-stakeholders-to-celebrate-the-7th-national-day-of-identity-in-abuja>).
