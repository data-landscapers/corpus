# Algeria

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Algeria's Ministry of Interior says each newborn has received its own birth certificate and unique national identifier (NIN) once registered, since 2018](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). [It said so in a July 2026 presentation on the digitisation of civil registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Given nationally.** [The ministry's presentation says the NIN is generated automatically from the national automated civil register (RNAEC) for each newborn after registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). [That has been the practice since 2018](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact is the ministry's own statement. [Each newborn registered has had a birth certificate and a NIN of its own since 2018](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>). It gives no registration rate.
