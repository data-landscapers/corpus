# Ghana

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Vice President Bawumia launched the Ghana Card number at birth system on 14 March 2024, generating a number for the baby when a health-facility birth is registered](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth/>). [It captured details at the 338 health facilities where e-tracking was deployed](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). [The programme was suspended after a technical fault](<https://theoverseeronline.com/infant-registration/>). [On 11 August 2026 the NIA, the Births and Deaths Registry and Ghana Health Service tested a renewed application to register children aged 0-5 at selected health facilities](<https://theoverseeronline.com/infant-registration/>). [In May 2026 its head said children born in hospital would get a number automatically, generated with the birth registration number](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/05/nia-registers-over-32000-children-in-volta-oti-regions/>). [In September 2026 the NIA was preparing to begin registration at hospitals, where parents would receive an identity number for newborns](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1528541/ghana-card-to-become-e-wallet-for-electronic-trans.html>). [In October 2026 it said it would begin registering children aged 0-5 the following week](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).
