# Ghana

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The Vice President launched the Ghana Card Number at Birth system, giving newborns a unique identification number at birth, with biometric details captured at health facilities where e-tracking is deployed (338 facilities)](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). [Bedzrah said NIA was extending the system to children from birth and would begin registering children aged 0-5 the following week, after the ongoing registration of ages 6-14](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>). [NIA Executive Secretary Wisdom Kwaku Deku said the NIA is preparing to begin registration at hospitals where parents or guardians would receive an identity number for newborns, later linked to the child's Ghana Card registration at age six](<https://www.modernghana.com/news/1528541/ghana-card-to-become-e-wallet-for-electronic-trans.html>).

**Given nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).

**All births registered.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).
