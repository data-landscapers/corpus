# Chad

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Chad's digital birth register, TASDJIL, puts a national identification number (NNI) on new birth certificates. [In December 2023 it ran desks in more than 50 maternity units](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>). Older certificates carry no NNI. [In July 2025 the Mongo hospital desk was registering 10 to 15 births a day, each with an NNI](<https://ndarason.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-accelere-lenregistrement-des-naissances-a-mongo/>). [In August 2026 a desk at Koukou Angarana hospital also gave children an NNI](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). Covering every health facility is still an aim.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 an ATPE opinion piece said TASDJIL gives children a National Identification Number](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). [It said this happens when births are registered at the Koukou Angarana hospital desk](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 TASDJIL ran at the Koukou Angarana hospital desk](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).
