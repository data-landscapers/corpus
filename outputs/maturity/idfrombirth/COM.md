# Comoros

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The AMECC project says the Comorian civil registration system is still largely dependent on old paper registers, and some people, including children, still hold no birth extract in 2026](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/%C3%A9tat-civil-l%E2%80%99amecc-veut-enterrer-les-registres-sur-papier-.html>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
