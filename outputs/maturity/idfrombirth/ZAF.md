# South Africa

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Home Affairs registers some births at the point of birth. [In November 2025 it told Parliament that a child whose birth is registered through a health facility gets a birth certificate on the spot](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33804/>), under an agreement with the Department of Health. An ID number is allocated at that registration and stays with the child for life. The department described this as a single entry point into the National Population Register, meant to curb identity theft from the start.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every birth registered in the country receives the number. The nearest held fact is [the reply on births registered through health facilities](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33804/>), which describes that route only.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of births or deaths registered. The nearest held fact is [the same reply](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33804/>), which gives no rate.

***Not found***. No source says every registered birth receives the number. None says birth registration reaches all births.
