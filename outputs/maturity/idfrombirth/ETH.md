# Ethiopia

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** In Addis Ababa some children get a Fayda number when their birth is registered. [In March 2025 a Fayda official told BBC Amharic that children collecting a birth certificate at school can take a Fayda number at the same time](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>). Children under five are registered with a parent as witness. No source gives how many children have received one.

**Given nationally.** No source found says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact is [the Addis Ababa arrangement described in March 2025](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>), which covers one city.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of births or deaths registered. The nearest fact is [the registration of under-fives with a parent as witness](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>), reported in March 2025.

***Also on file***. [Fayda](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/08/08/national-id-program-to-expand-digital-wallet-and-credential-verification/>).

***Not found***. No source found says the number is given at birth registration outside Addis Ababa.
