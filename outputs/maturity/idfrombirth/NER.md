# Niger

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [On 15 April 2025 the Ministry of the Interior launched a pilot in four communes](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>). [It registers children under five and issues them a unique identifier number with a new birth certificate](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>). [About 4 million children are targeted over three phases](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in April 2025 the pilot ran in four communes](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in April 2025 the ministry targeted about 4 million children under five for registration](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>).
