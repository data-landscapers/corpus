# Niger

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The Ministry of the Interior says the pilot launched on 15 April 2025 in four communes registers under-5s and issues them a unique identifier number with a new birth certificate, with about 4 million children targeted over three phases](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wuri.ne/etat-civil-wuri-lancement-officiel-des-operations-denregistrement-des-enfants-de-moins-de-5-ans/>).
