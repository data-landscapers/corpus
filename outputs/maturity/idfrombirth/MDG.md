# Madagascar

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** The World Bank's PRODIGY project reports that its [pilot in Antananarivo and Toamasina had registered over 3,000 newborns by October 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123130031407/pdf/P1694131558e640e51a145152595653587b.pdf>). Each was issued a birth certificate copy carrying a unique identification number. In January 2025 [Newsmada reported that the Cneci project aims to deliver birth certificates with a unique biometric identification number](<https://newsmada.com/2025/01/20/etat-civil-et-identite-nationale-lancement-des-activites-du-cneci/>).

**Given nationally.** The number is described only in the two pilot cities. The [PRODIGY report counts newborns in Antananarivo and Toamasina](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123130031407/pdf/P1694131558e640e51a145152595653587b.pdf>), and the [Cneci plan of 2025](<https://newsmada.com/2025/01/20/etat-civil-et-identite-nationale-lancement-des-activites-du-cneci/>) gives no date for every birth to receive it.

**All births registered.** No national count of births registered with the number appears. The nearest figure is the [3,000 newborns of the 2023 pilot](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123130031407/pdf/P1694131558e640e51a145152595653587b.pdf>). Death registration is not reported.

***Also on file***. [IDMS](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>). [Prodigy NUI](<https://2424.mg/education-un-numero-unique-didentification-a-attribuer-pour-chaque-eleve-de-la-cisco-datsimondrano/>).
