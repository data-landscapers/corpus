# Madagascar

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [Newsmada reports the Cneci project aims to deliver birth certificates containing a unique authentic biometric identification number](<https://newsmada.com/2025/01/20/etat-civil-et-identite-nationale-lancement-des-activites-du-cneci/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most fifteen stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsmada.com/2025/01/20/etat-civil-et-identite-nationale-lancement-des-activites-du-cneci/>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://newsmada.com/2025/01/20/etat-civil-et-identite-nationale-lancement-des-activites-du-cneci/>).

***Also on file***. [IDMS](<https://digital.gov.mg/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26-03-02-TDR-AUDIT-IDMS.pdf>); [Prodigy NUI](<https://2424.mg/education-un-numero-unique-didentification-a-attribuer-pour-chaque-eleve-de-la-cisco-datsimondrano/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a number is given at birth registration in any part of the country.
