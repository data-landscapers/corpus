# Burkina Faso

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** Burkina Faso has provided for an identity number from birth. [In October 2024 the digital ministry reported that deputies had approved the unique electronic identification of persons](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=33c705c5a2e4a9768ead889848f29c15&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=808>). The minister said the identifier is a random number. It is to be given to every Burkinabe from birth, at home or abroad. Foreigners living in the country are to get one too. No child is yet recorded as receiving it.

**Given nationally.** No source found says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact is [the October 2024 vote on the unique identifier](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=33c705c5a2e4a9768ead889848f29c15&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=808>), which is meant to cover everyone.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 gives the share of births or deaths registered. The nearest fact is [the plan to number every person from birth](<https://www.mdenp.gov.bf/details?cHash=33c705c5a2e4a9768ead889848f29c15&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=808>), reported in October 2024.
