# Uganda

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [NIRA says the NIN is issued automatically once a birth notification is made and certification is immediate, with no biometrics needed for a child 9 months or under; deploying crvs.nsis.nira.go.ug for birth registration and instant NIN generation is still a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [NIRA says about 636,000 children have been enrolled and it has registration centres in most major hospitals where parents register newborns](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/21-million-ugandans-transferred-to-new-nira-system-as-registration-drive-intensifies-5318862>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).
