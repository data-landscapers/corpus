# Uganda

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [NIRA said in July 2026 that the NIN is issued automatically once a birth notification is made](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [No biometrics are needed for a child of 9 months or under](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [It listed deploying its online system for birth registration and instant NIN generation as a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>). [NIRA says about 636,000 children have been enrolled](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/21-million-ugandans-transferred-to-new-nira-system-as-registration-drive-intensifies-5318862>). [It has registration centres in most major hospitals, where parents register newborns](<https://www.monitor.co.ug/uganda/news/national/21-million-ugandans-transferred-to-new-nira-system-as-registration-drive-intensifies-5318862>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 NIRA said the NIN is issued automatically once a birth notification is made](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 NIRA listed instant NIN generation at birth registration as a next step](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-uganda.pdf>).
