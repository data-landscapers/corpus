# Cape Verde

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In May 2026 Biometric Update said every child born in Cabo Verde receives a Unique Identification Number at birth](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).

**Given nationally.** [The same May 2026 report says the number goes to every child born in the country](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more.
