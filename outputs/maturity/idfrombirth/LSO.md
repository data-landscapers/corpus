# Lesotho

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [In July 2026 Lesotho's NICR said a unique personal identification number is issued upon birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the NICR said the number is issued upon birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 the NICR tied the number to birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).
