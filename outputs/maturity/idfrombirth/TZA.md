# Tanzania

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The NIDA Director said a child-identification plan, registering children with NIDA from birth, is under pilot in three districts](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/why-your-nida-number-is-more-than-just-a-numeral-5598108>). [The Home Affairs Minister said preparations are underway to register children so that every Tanzanian has a National Identification Number](<https://dailynews.co.tz/unclaimed-ids-law-in-offing/>). [The Daily News says the Home Affairs budget continues the Jamii Namba rollout, a system designed to give every citizen a unique identification number from birth, kept for life](<https://dailynews.co.tz/security-digital-identity-and-technology-set-to-spur-tanzanias-growth/>). [Tanzania is stepping up efforts to issue the Jamii Namba, a number assigned from birth through civil registration, to children, with a legal framework being prepared](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/tanzania-pushes-crvs-id-integration-as-birth-registration-becomes-a-dpi-layer>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/why-your-nida-number-is-more-than-just-a-numeral-5598108>).
