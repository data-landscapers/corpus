# Gambia

## Digital id from birth

**Number given at birth.** [The World Bank says the eCRVS issues birth certificates each carrying a unique national identification number, generated in real time at registration](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>). [It did so in a mass registration campaign from August 2022 to February 2023, reported in 2024](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>).

**Given nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says every registered birth across the country receives the number. The nearest held fact is the World Bank's account. [The eCRVS issued birth certificates each carrying a unique national identification number](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>). That covers the 2022-23 campaign, not every registration since.

**All births registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact is the same [World Bank account of the 2022-23 campaign](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>). It gives no share of births registered.
