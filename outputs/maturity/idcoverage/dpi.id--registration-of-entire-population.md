# Registration of entire population: registration of entire population

*As at 2026-11-06.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy sets a target of 99.9% of people in Africa holding a digital legal identity by 2030, from birth. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## Countries

### Benin: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- ANIP, per its director general, said 98% of Benin's population is already enrolled ([source, 2026-10-30](https://lematinal.bj/modernisation-des-services-didentification-au-benin-lanip-accelere-sa-revolution-numerique/)).
- A Benin official says more than 99% of the population has a digital identity ([source, 2026-10-28](https://transcontinents.africa/fr/article/e-gouvernance-le-benin-et-la-mauritanie-a-lecole-estonienne)).

### Mauritania: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- The interior minister told the National Assembly the Registre national de la population holds over 5.1 million people, about 96% of the 5.3 million population (share computed from the population held) ([source, 2026-06-18](https://kassataya.com/ministre-de-linterieur-lenrolement-de-la-population-est-devenu-un-service-public-permanent-qui-garantit-le-droit-de-tous-les-citoyens-a-lenregistrement/)).
- More than 94 percent of the population is enrolled for national ID since the new system of 2020, per the ANRPTS director ([source, 2025-05](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/african-nations-making-digital-id-gains-in-the-face-of-common-challenges)).

### Morocco: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2025.

- The ID4Africa slide gives 31 million citizens holding the electronic national identity card against a total Moroccan population of 36.6 million, about 85% ([source, 2025-05-22](https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S3-MOSIP-Final.pdf)).

### Rwanda: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- NIDA said more than 10 million people have had biometrics captured, about 68% of the 14.6 million population (share computed from the population held), with 27 of 30 districts covered ([source, 2026-09-16](https://umunota.com/en/over-10-million-rwandans-registered-for-digital-id/)).

### Sierra Leone: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- About 93 per cent of the population is registered with a NIN, per the Director-General ([source, 2026-08-05](https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/)).
- The World Bank blog says 6.4 million people hold a national ID number, about 8 in 10 of the 8.8 million target population (6.4/8.8 = 73%) ([source, 2025-12-09](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone)).

### Togo: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- The same figure of more than six million people registered is about 70% of the held population of 8.6 million, above two-thirds (share computed from the held population) ([source, 2026-09-28](https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/)).
- More than 6 million Togolese citizens held a unique identification number (NIU) at end December 2025, per the Permanent Secretariat for reform monitoring; about 70% of the 8.6 million population (computed from the population given) ([source, 2025-12](https://www.togofirst.com/fr/services/2304-18802-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-citoyens-disposent-d-un-niu-a-fin-2025)).

### Uganda: 3 Established

Two-thirds of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- Figures presented at NIRA's expo and cited by Minister Kamuntu put about 36.2 million Ugandans identified, approximately 78.8% of the population, which is over two-thirds ([source, 2026-09](https://nilepost.co.ug/news/374307/identity-is-the-foundation-of-security-development-says-minister-kamuntu-as-nira-expo-opens)).
- About 10.9 million Ugandans remain off the register; against the 51.4 million population given, about 40.5 million are enrolled, 79% (computed, not stated) ([source, 2026-09-09](https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/)).

### Ethiopia: 2 Nascent

### Guinea: 2 Nascent

### Malawi: 2 Nascent

A third of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- More than 13.5 million people are registered in the national ID system, which the ICT minister put at about 99 percent of citizens aged 16 and above; against the 22.2 million population held this is about 61 percent of the whole population (computed) ([source, 2026-09-16](https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/)).
- The ICT Minister said the national ID system has over 13.5 million people registered, about 99 percent of citizens aged 16 and above; against the population of 22.2 million held (2025) that is about 61 percent of all ages ([source, 2026-09](https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/)).

### Nigeria: 2 Nascent

A third of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- NIMC says 142 million people had registered for NINs as of September 2026, about 60% of the 237.5 million population (share computed from the population held) ([source, 2026-09](https://techcabal.com/2026/10/14/nimc-identity-database-locally-hosted/)).
- NIMC's director-general said the national register holds more than 140 million Nigerians and legal residents, about 59% of the 237.5 million population (share computed from the population held) ([source, 2026-09-16](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/16/fg-sets-december-deadline-for-95-nin-coverage/)).

### Tanzania: 2 Nascent

A third of the population or more held a national ID in 2026.

- The Home Affairs Minister said 27,520,835 people had been registered and 23,515,667 had received identification numbers; the registered count is about 39 percent of the 70.5 million population held (2025), calculated here ([source, 2026-09](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/why-your-nida-number-is-more-than-just-a-numeral-5598108)).
- Home Affairs minister Patrobas Katambi reported 27,520,835 people registered with NIDA and 23,515,667 issued a National Identification Number, which is 39% and 33% of the 70.5 million population (shares computed from the population held; figures as corrected in the record's summary, the body text differing) ([source, 2026-09-16](https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344)).

### Liberia: 1 Absent

Fewer than a third of the population held a national ID in 2026.

### Madagascar: 1 Absent

Fewer than a third of the population held a national ID in 2026.

- Interior minister Velonjara Tiaray Rakotonandrasana says nearly 5 million people were enrolled in phase I of Izaho tokana biometric enrolment, about 15% of the 32.7 million population (computed), with reconciliation against the central base still under way ([source, 2026-09-16](https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/)).

### Niger: 1 Absent

Fewer than 3 million people, under a third of the population, held a national ID card in 2026; one earlier report implied 10 million.

- Niger's territorial administration director general said fewer than 3 million people hold a national identity card, against about 13 million Nigeriens obliged to have one ([source, 2026-10-07](https://www.lesahel.org/sensibilisation-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-la-caravane-nationale-fait-halte-a-niamey-pour-cloturer-sa-campagne/)).

### Somalia: 1 Absent

Fewer than a third of the population held a national ID in 2026.

- NIRA has registered more than 1.5 million citizens since 2023, about 7.6% of the 19.7 million population (share computed from the population held), against a target of 15 million by 2029, says the Hiiraan report quoting the interior minister ([source, 2026-09-19](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Sep/206316/somalia_registers_15_million_citizens_under_national_id_system.aspx)).
- NIRA said it had registered more than 1.5 million Somali citizens since 2023, about 7.6% of the 19.7 million population (share computed here), against a target of 15 million citizens and residents by 2029 ([source, 2026-09](https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Oct/206408/puntland_passengers_blocked_from_flights_over_nira_id_requirement.aspx)).

### South Sudan: 1 Absent

Fewer than a third of the population held a national ID in 2026.

- The Sudan Independent, citing a July World Bank report, says nearly 90% of South Sudan's population lacked government-recognised identification of any kind, so about 10% hold one (below the 12.2 million population share of a third) ([source, 2026-07](https://www.sudanindependent.net/news/politics/2026/10/12/NEC-turns-to-chiefs-to-verify-)).
- Anecdotal estimates say only about 10% of South Sudan's roughly 12 million people hold Nationality Certificates (about 1.2 million, share as stated), and the country has yet to issue National ID cards at scale, per Hold the Child ([source, 2025-12-15](https://statelessnessalliance.org/online-marketplace/strengthening-civil-registration-and-ending-statelessness-in-south-sudan)).

### Zambia: 1 Absent

Fewer than a third of the population held a national ID in 2026.
