# South Sudan

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In December 2025 Hold the Child put holders of a Nationality Certificate at about 10% of some 12 million people](<https://statelessnessalliance.org/online-marketplace/strengthening-civil-registration-and-ending-statelessness-in-south-sudan>). [The group, writing on statelessness, said national ID cards had yet to be issued at scale](<https://statelessnessalliance.org/online-marketplace/strengthening-civil-registration-and-ending-statelessness-in-south-sudan>). [A July 2026 World Bank report, cited by the Sudan Independent, said nearly 90% of the population lacked government-recognised identification of any kind](<https://www.sudanindependent.net/news/politics/2026/10/12/NEC-turns-to-chiefs-to-verify->).
