# South Sudan

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [The Sudan Independent, citing a July World Bank report, says nearly 90% of South Sudan's population lacked government-recognised identification of any kind, so about 10% hold one (below the 12.2 million population share of a third)](<https://www.sudanindependent.net/news/politics/2026/10/12/NEC-turns-to-chiefs-to-verify->). [Anecdotal estimates say only about 10% of South Sudan's roughly 12 million people hold Nationality Certificates (about 1.2 million, share as stated), and the country has yet to issue National ID cards at scale, per Hold the Child](<https://statelessnessalliance.org/online-marketplace/strengthening-civil-registration-and-ending-statelessness-in-south-sudan>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sudanindependent.net/news/politics/2026/10/12/NEC-turns-to-chiefs-to-verify->).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.sudanindependent.net/news/politics/2026/10/12/NEC-turns-to-chiefs-to-verify->).
