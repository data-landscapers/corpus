# Zambia

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In September 2024 UNDP said INRIS had enrolled 1.5 million people with biometrics](<https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration>). [A September 2025 World Bank appraisal put biometric registration at 15 percent of adults](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349>). [In July 2026 ITWeb, citing UNDP, still gave about 1.5 million enrolled in a population of around 20 million](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). That is under 8% of the whole population, against 15 percent of adults.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the UNDP put biometric enrolment at roughly 1.5 million people in a population of about 20 million, ITWeb said](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 ITWeb said the UNDP put biometric enrolment at roughly 1.5 million people](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).
