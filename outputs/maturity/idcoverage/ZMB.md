# Zambia

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [ITWeb says the UNDP puts biometric enrolment at roughly 1.5 million people in a population of around 20 million, about 7.5% (6.8% of the 21.9 million held)](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [The World Bank appraisal says INRIS has registered 15 percent of adults using biometrics, a coverage gap of about 85 percent (an adults share, so below a third of the whole population)](<https://documents.worldbank.org/en/publication/documents-reports/documentdetail/099100625152522349>). [UNDP says INRIS has enrolled 1.5 million people with biometrics; against the 21.9 million population held here that is about 6.8% (the source gives no population share and says 9 million adults)](<https://www.undp.org/digital/blog/dpi-safeguards-come-online-zambias-next-phase-digital-integration>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>).
