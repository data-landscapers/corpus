# Niger

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [On 7 October 2026, in Niamey, the director general of territorial administration said fewer than 3 million people held a national identity card](<https://www.lesahel.org/sensibilisation-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-la-caravane-nationale-fait-halte-a-niamey-pour-cloturer-sa-campagne/>). [About 13 million Nigeriens are obliged to have one](<https://www.lesahel.org/sensibilisation-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-la-caravane-nationale-fait-halte-a-niamey-pour-cloturer-sa-campagne/>). [He gave the same figure of about three million holders at Diffa in September 2026](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>). That is about 11% of a population of 27.9 million. [A week before Diffa, at Agadez, he was reported as saying 3 million of the 13 million lacked one](<https://interieur.gouv.ne/agadez-ouverture-de-la-4eme-etape-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-les-reformes-en-matiere-detat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes/>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>).
