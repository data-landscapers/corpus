# Niger

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [The DGATDD says about 3,000,000 of the 13 million Nigeriens old enough for the national ID card do not yet hold one, so about 10 million hold one, roughly 36% of the 27.9 million population (derived by dividing by the stated population)](<https://interieur.gouv.ne/agadez-ouverture-de-la-4eme-etape-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-les-reformes-en-matiere-detat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes/>). [The Secretary General of Diffa region said about three million Nigeriens currently hold a national identity card, out of an estimated 13 million potentially concerned; against the 27.9 million population that is about 10.8% (computed here)](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID.
