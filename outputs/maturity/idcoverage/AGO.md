# Angola

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In 2024 the government's strategic plan for universal identity cards put holders of the Bilhete de Identidade at 14.8 million of 35.1 million Angolans](<https://www.novojornal.co.ao/sociedade/detalhe/governo-estima-que-dos-351-milhoes-de-angolanos-actuais-apenas-148-milhoes-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade-63150.html>). That is about 42%. The plan was published in the official gazette. [In September 2025 the Secretary of State for Justice, Osvaldo Amaro, said 16,297,777 citizens held the card](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>). [He set that against a country of about 35 million](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>), so a little under half the population held one.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest fact: [in September 2025 the Secretary of State for Justice said 16,297,777 citizens held the Bilhete de Identidade, about half the population](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest fact: [in September 2025 about 16.3 million Angolans were on the civil identity record behind the Bilhete de Identidade](<https://angop.ao/noticias/sociedade/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-angolanos-possuem-bilhete-de-identidade/>).
