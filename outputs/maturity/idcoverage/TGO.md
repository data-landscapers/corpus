# Togo

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [More than 6 million Togolese held a unique identification number at the end of 2025, official figures show](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/services/2304-18802-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-citoyens-disposent-d-un-niu-a-fin-2025>). [Each was issued with a biometric ID card, Biometric Update reported](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/togo-issues-6m-unique-numbers-as-mosip-based-digital-id-project-progresses>). [In September 2026 ANID counted more than six million people in the e-ID Togo register](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>). That is about 70% of a population of 8.6 million, above two-thirds but short of everyone.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://levisionnaire.tg/e-id-togo-plus-de-6-millions-de-personnes-enregistrees-la-region-de-la-kara-prochaine-etape/>).
