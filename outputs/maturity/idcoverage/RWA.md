## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In February 2026 the ICT minister told the Senate over 1.8 million people had given biometrics for the new digital ID, against a target of 15 million](<https://www.parliament.gov.rw/news-detail?cHash=349550b59d9f16c1cdbfe62d270d5c3c&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=58639>). [NIDA counted more than 8 million registered by August 2026](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/38236/news/rwanda/enrollment-for-digital-id-surpasses-8-million---nida>). [In September 2026 NIDA said more than 10 million had had biometrics captured, in 27 of 30 districts](<https://umunota.com/en/over-10-million-rwandans-registered-for-digital-id/>). That is about 68% of a population of 14.6 million.
