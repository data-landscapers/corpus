## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In November 2025 NIRA's director general said at least one million citizens were enrolled for the national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/somalia-to-integrate-more-govt-services-with-national-id-as-issuance-reaches-1m>). [About two million had registered by April 2026, against a target of 15 million by 2029](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Apr/204974/somalias_eaqoonsi_app_shows_progress_gaps_in_digital_public_infrastructure_dpi_push.aspx>). [In September 2026 NIRA said it had registered more than 1.5 million citizens since 2023](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Sep/206316/somalia_registers_15_million_citizens_under_national_id_system.aspx>). That is about 8% of a population of 19.7 million.
