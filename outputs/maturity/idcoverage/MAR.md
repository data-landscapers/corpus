# Morocco

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In February 2025 the national police spokesperson said 32 million electronic identity cards had been issued since 2008](<https://www.alhadathlifriqui.com/?p=72708>). Another 2.7 million went to Moroccans abroad. He put the share of citizens identified at 87%. [A May 2025 ID4Africa presentation counted 31 million citizens holding the card, out of 36.6 million people](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S3-MOSIP-Final.pdf>). That is about 85% of the population.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P1-S3-MOSIP-Final.pdf>).
