# Malawi

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In September 2026 the ICT Minister said over 13.5 million people were registered in the national ID system](<https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/>). [He put that at about 99 percent of citizens aged 16 and above](<https://malawi24.com/2026/09/26/nzika-wallet-controversy-who-holds-the-keys-to-malawis-multibillion-digital-identity/>). [A column in the Maravi Post that month gave the same figure of 13.5 million registered people](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawis-digital-id-must-earn-public-trust/>). Against a population of 22.2 million, that is about 61% of people of all ages.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mwnation.com/national-ids-to-last-a-lifetime/>).
