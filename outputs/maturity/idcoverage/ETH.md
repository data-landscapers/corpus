# Ethiopia

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In October 2024 the programme office said close to 10 million people had received Fayda cards](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/42222/>). [In March 2026 the programme's executive director said more than 37 million people were enrolled in Fayda, at one million a week](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/ethiopias-digital-id-joins-sovereign-wealth-fund-as-weekly-enrollments-reach-1m>). [Enrolments reached 49.1 million by 24 August 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/fayda-digital-id-nears-50m-enrollments-as-adoption-reshapes-ethiopias-economy>). [By 9 October 2026 registrations had passed 52 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ethiopia-launches-developer-sandbox-to-support-fayda-digital-id-innovation-integration>). Against a population of 135.5 million, that is about 38%, well short of two-thirds. [The target is 90 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/ethiopias-digital-id-joins-sovereign-wealth-fund-as-weekly-enrollments-reach-1m>).
