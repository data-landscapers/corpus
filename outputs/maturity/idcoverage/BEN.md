# Benin

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [The World Bank says nearly 99% of Benin's population is now biometrically enrolled](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [The World Bank blog, via Biometric Update, says about 99 percent of Beninese citizens hold a legal and digital identity; the WURI project enrolled 2.2 million more people, 4,000 of them abroad; The share is of citizens and no total count is given](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202406/wuri-digital-id-project-improving-access-to-public-services-in-benin>). [The World Bank says more than 7.7 million people have their NPI as of the end of May 2025, about 52% of the 14.8 million population (divided by the population given, not stated in the document)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [The WURI-Benin coordinator reports more than 13.41 million people enrolled, stated as 98% of the 2026 population projection](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>).
