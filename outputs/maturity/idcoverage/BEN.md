# Benin

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In November 2023 the government said more than 90% of the population had been formally identified](<https://www.banouto.bj/politique/article/20231109-nomination-d-un-rwandais-dg-anip-reponse-du-gouvernement-talon-a-l-interpellation-du-parlement>). [In June 2024 the World Bank said about 99 percent of Beninese citizens held a legal and digital identity](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202406/wuri-digital-id-project-improving-access-to-public-services-in-benin>). [In 2025 the WURI-Benin coordinator counted more than 13.41 million people enrolled, 98% of the 2026 population projection](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-2-Benin_FINAL.pdf>). [In May 2026 the World Bank said nearly 99% of the population was biometrically enrolled](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/20/transforming-lives-in-benin-a-unique-identification-system>). [In October 2026 a Benin official put the share with a digital identity above 99%](<https://transcontinents.africa/fr/article/e-gouvernance-le-benin-et-la-mauritanie-a-lecole-estonienne>). [Days later ANIP's director general said 98% of the population was enrolled](<https://lematinal.bj/modernisation-des-services-didentification-au-benin-lanip-accelere-sa-revolution-numerique/>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID.
