[Home Affairs Minister Patrobas Katambi credited NIDA with registering 943,284 Tanzanians, about 1.3% of the 70.5 million population (share calculated here from the population held), with 6,308 printed Zanzibar cards uncollected](<https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/6308-nida-cards-remain-uncollected-in-zanzibar-2026-10-12-135944>). [NIDA reports 27.1 million adults registered (87% adult coverage) and shows 50.8% of the population excluded because children are deferred, which leaves under two-thirds of the whole population registered](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).
