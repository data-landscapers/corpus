# Tanzania

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [An FSDT analysis counted over 22 million Tanzanians holding a National Identification Number in April 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/tanzania-pushes-crvs-id-integration-as-birth-registration-becomes-a-dpi-layer>). [In May 2026 NIDA reported 27.1 million adults registered, 87% of adults, with children not yet enrolled](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [In September 2026 the Home Affairs Minister said 27,520,835 people were registered and 23,515,667 had received a number](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). The registered count is about 39% of a population of 70.5 million.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/why-your-nida-number-is-more-than-just-a-numeral-5598108>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source found since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/why-your-nida-number-is-more-than-just-a-numeral-5598108>).
