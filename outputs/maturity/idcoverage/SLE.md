# Sierra Leone

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In April 2024 the NCRA said over 90 percent of the population was registered for the digital ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202404/sierra-leone-putting-digital-id-at-the-center-of-its-digital-transformation-agenda>). [In December 2025 a World Bank blog counted 6.4 million people holding a national ID number](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone>). [In August 2026 the NCRA's Director-General put registration at about 93 per cent of the population, each person with a number](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [That is about 8.2 million of 8.8 million people](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).
