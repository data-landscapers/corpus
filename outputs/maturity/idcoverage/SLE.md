# Sierra Leone

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [About 93 per cent of the population is registered with a NIN, per the Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [The World Bank blog says 6.4 million people hold a national ID number, about 8 in 10 of the 8.8 million target population (6.4/8.8 = 73%)](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone>). [The NCRA's biometric system has registered about 93 per cent of the population, each with a NIN (about 8.2 million of 8.8 million), per the Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).

**Everyone holds one.** No source held since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).

**Births and deaths registered.** No source held since October 2023 puts birth registration at all births and deaths at 80% or more. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>).
