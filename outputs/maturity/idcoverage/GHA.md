# Ghana

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In March 2024 the NIA's Executive Director said 17.8 million Ghanaians were on the National Identification Register](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). That is about half of a population of 35.1 million. [National Identification Authority data at 30 October 2026 show 20,610,948 people enrolled for the Ghana Card](<https://ghana.dubawa.org/false-91-of-ghanaians-have-not-received-their-ghana-cards/>), as Dubawa reports. The Ghana Statistical Service projects a population of 34,378,768 for 2026, so about 60% are enrolled.
