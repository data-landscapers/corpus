# Liberia

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In February 2024 the registry counted 700,000 people enrolled, 13.46% of 5.2 million](<https://newspublictrust.com/national-identification-registry-deploys-mobile-teams>). [In August 2024 its executive director put enrolment at 723,000 of 5.2 million people, about 14%](<https://liberianinvestigator.com/nir-director-blames-low-registration-on-government-agencies-issuing-unauthorized-identification-numbers/>). [By December 2024 it had enrolled 740,000, about 13% of 5.7 million](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/news/liberia-national-identification-registry-drives-digital-transformation-with-strategic-partnerships-and-enrollment-growth/>). [At a June 2025 briefing the registry gave about 759,000 enrolled, about 14 percent](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>). [Issuance stopped on 23 June 2025, with about 14 percent of the population registered, the Liberian Observer reports](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/national-id-deal-under-scrutiny-over-revenue-data-security/article_e4054c6e-5002-438d-ad8d-761fdecadc47.html>). [In August 2026 Biometric Update put coverage below 15 percent](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/liberia-advances-ppp-backed-digital-id-rollout-with-osd>). [A September 2026 proclamation counted about 720,000 enrolled, near 12.6% of the population](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/liberia-president-boakai-declares-wednesday-september-16-as-national-identification-day?lang=en>). [In October 2026 the registry said fewer than 800,000 people held a national ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id>).
