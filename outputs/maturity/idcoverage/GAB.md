# Gabon

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In June 2026 the Interior Minister told the National Assembly that 335,384 people had enrolled in the electronic identity card campaign since 2024](<https://africacoeurnews.com/2026/06/03/gabon-17720-cartes-didentite-electroniques-toujours-non-retirees/>). That is about 13% of a population of 2.6 million. It is the only such count found. No other news story or official notice read for this study adds to it. The documents the earlier cell cited were not read. The systems themselves were not examined.
