# Madagascar

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [In October 2023 the World Bank put holders of the new ID number at 0.01% of the population, against a 50% target](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123130031407/pdf/P1694131558e640e51a145152595653587b.pdf>). [A January 2026 pilot enrolled nearly 130,000 people](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202604/madagascar-navigates-biometric-enrollment-challenges-with-laxtons-help>). [The World Bank said over 3.25 million adults were enrolled by 10 June 2026](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar>). [Authorities put enrolment above six million by June 2026, against a target of 12 million](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/madagascar-strengthens-trust-architecture-for-national-digital-id>). [In September 2026 the interior minister said nearly 5 million were enrolled in the first phase of Izaho tokana, with reconciliation against the central base still under way](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>). That is about 15% of a population of 32.7 million.

**Everyone holds one.** No source found since October 2023 counts 99.9% of the population or more holding a national ID. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 the interior minister said nearly 5 million people were enrolled in phase I of Izaho tokana](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>).

[They were enrolled in phase I of Izaho tokana](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>).
