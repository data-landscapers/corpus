# Madagascar

## Registration of entire population

**Share holding an ID.** [Interior minister Velonjara Tiaray Rakotonandrasana says nearly 5 million people were enrolled in phase I of Izaho tokana biometric enrolment, about 15% of the 32.7 million population (computed), with reconciliation against the central base still under way](<https://www.2424.mg/identite-numerique-izaho-tokana-2-0-cible-plus-de-10-millions-de-citoyens-pour-sa-deuxieme-phase/>). [More than 3.25 million people are enrolled for the ID under PRODIGY, about 9.9% of the 32.7 million population (divided by the population held, not stated in the document)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/madagascars-last-mile-civil-registration-strategy-closing-digital-id-inclusion-gap>). [The World Bank says PRODIGY had enrolled over 3.25 million adults by 10 June 2026, about 10% of the 32.7 million population (computed from the population given)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar>).
