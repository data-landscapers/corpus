# Djibouti

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The ministry in charge of posts and telecommunications [reported on the policy at the WSIS Forum in July 2025](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>). For the earlier Schéma stratégique intégré, [the 2015 law set up a national commission to follow its implementation](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n80-an-14-7eme-l-portant-approbation-du-schema-strategique-integre-ssi-du-secteur-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-la-communication-tic-en-republique-de-djibouti/>). [The Prime Minister chaired it](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n80-an-14-7eme-l-portant-approbation-du-schema-strategique-integre-ssi-du-secteur-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-la-communication-tic-en-republique-de-djibouti/>).

**Standing.** In July 2025 the minister [drew on the Politique nationale de développement des TIC to report progress on the digital divide](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>). The [Schéma stratégique intégré, approved by a law published in February 2015](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n80-an-14-7eme-l-portant-approbation-du-schema-strategique-integre-ssi-du-secteur-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-la-communication-tic-en-republique-de-djibouti/>), covered the sector before it.

**Text.** The ministry's [news item of July 2025](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>) names the policy and reports the progress made under it.

**Period.** The [Schéma stratégique intégré ran for ten years, from 2014 to 2024](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n80-an-14-7eme-l-portant-approbation-du-schema-strategique-integre-ssi-du-secteur-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-la-communication-tic-en-republique-de-djibouti/>).

**Delivery plan.** The minister [pointed to two large sector projects, Djibouti Digital Foundations and the East Africa regional digital integration project](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>), as the base of a digital economy. The [2015 law approved the Schéma stratégique intégré together with its action plan](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/arabe/texte-juridique/loi-n80-an-14-7eme-l-portant-approbation-du-schema-strategique-integre-ssi-du-secteur-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-la-communication-tic-en-republique-de-djibouti/>).

**Reporting.** At the WSIS Forum the minister [reported broadband access infrastructure covering most of the territory](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>).

**Last twelve months.** The minister's report on the policy [dates from July 2025](<https://communication.gouv.dj/djibouti-porte-sa-vision-dun-numerique-inclusif-au-sommet-mondial-sur-la-societe-de-linformation-2025/>).
