# Ethiopia

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The [Ministry of Innovation and Technology is mandated](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>) under the Communication Services Proclamation of 2019 to develop policy instruments, mobilise resources and guide and monitor the telecom sector. It was formed in October 2018 by [merging the Ministry of Communication and Information Technology with the Ministry of Science and Technology](<http://www.ist-africa.org/home/default.asp?docid=8216&page=doc-by-id>). The policy's 2017 revision had [financial support from the World Bank](<http://www.ist-africa.org/home/default.asp?docid=8216&page=doc-by-id>).

**Standing.** The National ICT Policy and Strategy was [developed in 2016](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>) and [endorsed by the Council of Ministers in 2017](<http://www.ist-africa.org/home/default.asp?docid=8216&page=doc-by-id>).

**Text.** UNCTAD [cites the policy as a 2016 document of the federal government](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy revises one [first drafted and submitted to the Council of Ministers in 2002](<http://www.ist-africa.org/home/default.asp?docid=8216&page=doc-by-id>).

**Delivery plan.** UNCTAD lists the [interventions through which the policy is implemented](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>).

**Reporting.** UNCTAD said in September 2021 that [implementation was fragmented across projects and sectors](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In September 2021 the policy was [still being implemented](<https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/BRI-Project_RP21_en.pdf>).
