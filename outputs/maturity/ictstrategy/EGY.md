# Egypt

## Sector policy

***Noted, not assessed***. [Digital Egypt Strategy](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>); [ICT Strategy: Building the Future of Education in Egypt](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2023-09-education-sector-plan-2023-2027-egypt.pdf>).

***Not held***. The National ICT Strategy 2013-2017 reached the end of its period in 2017, and no sector policy is held as adopted or in preparation after it. The ministry's web page gives the ICT 2030 Strategy a period, to 2030, and nothing else: whether it was adopted, is being drafted or was only announced is not known, and neither are its text, governance, delivery plan or reporting.
