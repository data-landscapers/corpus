# Eswatini

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** [Cabinet adopted](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>) the National Information and Communication Infrastructure (NICI) Policy in August 2006.

**Standing.** The policy is adopted. A stakeholder validation workshop [endorsed the draft ICT policy in January 2006](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>), and Cabinet adopted it that August as what the document calls the Swaziland National Information and Communication Infrastructure (NICI) Policy.

**Text.** The government site gov.sz [hosts the policy as a PDF](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>), in one file with its Implementation Plan 2012-2016. The document's published date is 1 August 2006.

**Period.** The policy is [published with an Implementation Plan covering 2012 to 2016](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The [Implementation Plan 2012-2016](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>) sits in the same document as the policy on the government site.

**Reporting.** The policy document [records its own adoption in 2006 and its launch in 2007](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The Prime Minister [launched the policy on 3 August 2007](<https://www.gov.sz/images/stories/swd%20nici%20plan%20-%20final%203.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Eswatini Digitalisation Strategy 2024-2028](<https://www.separc.co.sz/2024/12/05/eswatini-advances-toward-digital-transformation-key-findings-from-the-digital-readiness-assessment>).

***Not found***. No period of the policy's own has been found: the only one on record, to 2016, is the Implementation Plan's. No adopted delivery plan, no report against the policy, no body or budget in the last three years and nothing dated in the last twelve months is held.
