# Somalia

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of Communications and Technology [published a notice on 11 November 2025](<https://s3.nl.geostorage.net/tottestupload3/1311122902271-YS-54-.pdf>) for a consultant firm to prepare the National ICT Policy and Strategy 2025-2029. The consultancy is [paid from an IDA grant](<https://s3.nl.geostorage.net/tottestupload3/1311122902271-YS-54-.pdf>) under the Regional Digital Integration Project.

**Standing.** The minister told the WSIS+20 forum on 9 July 2025 that the Council of Ministers [approved the National ICT Policy earlier in 2025](<https://moct.gov.so/en/somalia-committed-to-an-inclusive-digital-futureminister-mohamed-soomaali-addresses-wsis20-high-level-event/>). The notice of 11 November 2025 [seeks a firm to renew the 2019-2024 policy as a 2025-2029 one](<https://s3.nl.geostorage.net/tottestupload3/1311122902271-YS-54-.pdf>), over about four months.

**Text.** The ministry [published the full text of the National ICT Policy and Strategy 2019-2024](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>) on its own site.

**Period.** The notice gives the new policy's period as [2025 to 2029](<https://s3.nl.geostorage.net/tottestupload3/1311122902271-YS-54-.pdf>), after one the Cabinet [adopted on 28 November 2019](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/eGovernment-Strategy-2025-2029.pdf>) for 2019 to 2024.

**Delivery plan.** The 2019-2024 policy set [a table of actions and targets, each with a year of delivery](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/National-ICT-Policy-Strategy-2019-2024.pdf>), and left the cost of each line to be determined.

**Reporting.** In August 2025 the minister said the Starlink launch [supports the goals of the 2019-2024 policy](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Aug/202463/starlink_launches_in_somalia_bringing_satellite_internet_to_rural_areas_and_narrowing_the_digital_divide.aspx>).

**Last twelve months.** Expressions of interest for the consultancy [were due on 29 November 2025](<https://s3.nl.geostorage.net/tottestupload3/1311122902271-YS-54-.pdf>).

***Also on file***. The [Digital Transformation Strategy 2025-2030](<https://nca.gov.so/somalia-launches-national-consultation-on-the-digital-transformation-strategy-2025-2030/>), the [National Communications Law of 2017](<https://moct.gov.so/en/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Somalia-Digital-Inclusion-Policy_High-Level-Policy-document.pdf>) and the [Ninth National Development Plan 2020-2024](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099045403212411797/txt/IDU105f167fa1085214f8b1919f141249b1e8fae.txt>).

***Not found***. The text of the 2025-2029 policy, a delivery plan for it and any report against its targets. The minister's statement of July 2025 and the notice of November 2025 differ on whether the policy is approved or still to be written. No domestic budget line is stated.
