# Uganda

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The Ministry of ICT and National Guidance's report for the fourth quarter of 2021/22 shows its E-Services department [running the drafting and Cabinet submission of a replacement National ICT Policy](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>), on an output that also covers the National Cyber Security Strategy.

**Standing.** The Vision for a Digital Uganda 2040, of September 2022, [lists the National ICT Policy 2014 among Uganda's key policies, as being reviewed](<https://ict.go.ug/site/documents/digital-vision-uganda-2040.pdf>). The final draft of its replacement, dated May 2022, [names it among the key policies of the sector's current policy environment](<https://empowerconsult.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/NITA-U_Final-Draft-National-ICT-Policy.pdf>).

**Text.** The final draft of the replacement [is online on the website of the consultant who prepared it](<https://empowerconsult.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/NITA-U_Final-Draft-National-ICT-Policy.pdf>).

**Period.** A case study of August 2024 describes Uganda's policy environment as [starting with the National ICT Policy of 2003](<https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Uganda-Case-Study-Final-1-1.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The 2022 draft [carries no implementation plan and says the ministry will develop a road map](<https://empowerconsult.co.ug/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/NITA-U_Final-Draft-National-ICT-Policy.pdf>) to inform an implementation matrix.

**Reporting.** The ministry's report of June 2022 says [drafting of the replacement was complete and awaiting Cabinet approval](<https://budget.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/Sector%20Spending%20Agency%20Budgets%20and%20Performance/2021-2022_QuarterlyReport_Q4_020_MinistryofICTandNationalGuidance_05082022195509.pdf>). The Ministry of Finance's monitoring report for 2022/23 says [stakeholder engagement was finalised, pending submission to the Cabinet Secretariat](<https://www.finance.go.ug/sites/default/files/reports/Digital%20Transformation%20Programme%20Annual%20Monitoring%20Report%20FY2022-23.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** Terms of reference issued by NITA-U in August 2026 [cite a "Draft National ICT Policy, 2022"](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-08/Terms_of_Reference_for_a_Development_of_a_National_Digital_Skills_Final_0.pdf>) for the ICT sector's share of GDP.
