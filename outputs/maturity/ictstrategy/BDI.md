# Burundi

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The World Bank's 2022 project document [names the ICT ministry as responsible for the sector policy](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf>). The bank's Digital Foundations Project, a USD 50 million grant, [pays for the policy's evaluation and update](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/207441655916156350/pdf/Burundi-Digital-Foundations-Project.pdf>). No domestic budget line for it is known.

**Standing.** In November 2023 the project, PAFEN, [called for consultants to evaluate the national ICT development policy and draft a new one for 2025-2034](<https://pafen.gov.bi/recrutement-dun-bureau-detudes-charge-de-levaluation-et-de-la-mise-a-jour-de-lapolitique-nationale-de-developpement-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-lacommunication-ainsi-que-de-lelabo/>). The policy it would replace was [adopted by the Council of Ministers on 13 July 2011](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/politique_nationale_developpement_tic.pdf>).

**Text.** The text of the 2011 policy [is on the Prime Minister's website](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/politique_nationale_developpement_tic.pdf>). No text of its successor is known.

**Period.** The 2011 policy [ran from 2010 to 2025](<https://primature.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/politique_nationale_developpement_tic.pdf>). The successor [is to cover 2025 to 2034](<https://pafen.gov.bi/recrutement-dun-bureau-detudes-charge-de-levaluation-et-de-la-mise-a-jour-de-lapolitique-nationale-de-developpement-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-lacommunication-ainsi-que-de-lelabo/>).

**Delivery plan.** The consultants were also [to draw up an action plan for the new policy](<https://pafen.gov.bi/recrutement-dun-bureau-detudes-charge-de-levaluation-et-de-la-mise-a-jour-de-lapolitique-nationale-de-developpement-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-lacommunication-ainsi-que-de-lelabo/>). None is known to exist.

**Reporting.** A 2021 meeting of ICT focal points [reviewed progress under the old policy](<https://abpinfo.bi/2021/06/04/toutes-les-parties-prenantes-dans-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-politique-nationale-des-tlc-appelees-a-redoubler-dardeur-dans-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>), without figures against its targets.

**Last twelve months.** The newest dated fact is [the November 2023 call for consultants](<https://pafen.gov.bi/recrutement-dun-bureau-detudes-charge-de-levaluation-et-de-la-mise-a-jour-de-lapolitique-nationale-de-developpement-des-technologies-de-linformation-et-de-lacommunication-ainsi-que-de-lelabo/>).
