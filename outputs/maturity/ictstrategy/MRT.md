# Mauritania

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The ministry's presentation, published in April 2019, [names better regulation of the sector as one of the declaration's four pillars](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).

**Standing.** The Déclaration de politique sectorielle is adopted: the ministry's presentation states that [a new sector policy declaration was adopted in January 2013](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).

**Text.** The ministry's presentation [gives the declaration's four pillars as competition and investment, consumer protection, the sector's spillovers for the economy, and better regulation](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).

**Period.** The presentation [dates the declaration's adoption to January 2013](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The presentation [sets the declaration out as four pillars](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>), the first of them competition and investment.

**Reporting.** The ministry's presentation, published in April 2019, [describes the declaration as new and as adopted in January 2013](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The ministry's account of the declaration [dates from April 2019](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-T/Workshops-and-Seminars/bsg/2019040405/Documents/Presentation%204.pdf>).
