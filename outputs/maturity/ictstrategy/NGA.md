# Nigeria

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The National Telecommunications Policy 2000 [assigns policy formulation to the Ministry of Communications](<http://mail.nesgroup.org/download_policy_drafts/National%20Policy%20on%20Telecommunications%20%282000%29_1661778547.pdf>) and regulation to the Nigerian Communications Commission, and sets up a National Frequency Management Council in the Ministry.

**Standing.** The Minister of Communications' preface of May 2000 [presents the policy as approved by the President](<http://mail.nesgroup.org/download_policy_drafts/National%20Policy%20on%20Telecommunications%20%282000%29_1661778547.pdf>), and the Commission's consultation paper of 9 February 2026 [calls it the subsisting sector policy](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-02/Consultation-Paper-on-the-Review-of-the-National-Telecommunications-Policy-2000---February-9-2026.pdf>), in force for twenty-five years.

**Text.** The Ministry of Communications [published the full text](<http://mail.nesgroup.org/download_policy_drafts/National%20Policy%20on%20Telecommunications%20%282000%29_1661778547.pdf>), in seventeen chapters, in May 2000.

**Period.** The consultation paper [states no end date for the policy](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-02/Consultation-Paper-on-the-Review-of-the-National-Telecommunications-Policy-2000---February-9-2026.pdf>), whose Chapter 16 provides for review from time to time.

**Delivery plan.** The consultation paper [proposes to add an implementation matrix](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-02/Consultation-Paper-on-the-Review-of-the-National-Telecommunications-Policy-2000---February-9-2026.pdf>) with milestones, dependencies and responsible parties, which the 2000 text does not have.

**Reporting.** Chapter 16 of the policy [names annual reports](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-02/Consultation-Paper-on-the-Review-of-the-National-Telecommunications-Policy-2000---February-9-2026.pdf>) among its three implementation instruments, beside implementation machinery and a national forum.

**Last twelve months.** In February 2026 the Commission [published fifteen proposals for rewriting the policy](<https://www.ncc.gov.ng/sites/default/files/2026-02/Consultation-Paper-on-the-Review-of-the-National-Telecommunications-Policy-2000---February-9-2026.pdf>) and invited comments by 20 March 2026, and in May 2026 it [held a review workshop](<https://businessday.ng/technology/article/why-global-hyperscalers-are-avoiding-nigeria/>) with regulators, operators and investors.
