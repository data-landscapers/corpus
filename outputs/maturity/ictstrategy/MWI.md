# Malawi

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The Digital Government Strategy of September 2019 says it will [assist the Ministry of Information in implementing the targets in the National ICT Policy](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Standing.** The National ICT Policy 2013 is [listed in the policy framework of a 2025 management plan for the government data centre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099083025102027538/pdf/P505095-7a647ee6-33d5-44b5-97c1-1c135c16efd2.pdf>). A UNCDF scan of May 2023 [calls it outdated and in need of updating](<https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=2505-malawi-ides-report---designpdf&id=19003>), and the Presidential Delivery Unit [produced a draft National ICT and Digitalization Policy in 2022](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>).

**Text.** An essay of March 2026 [links a copy of the 2013 policy's text in the ITU national strategies repository](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/03/31/africa-data-problem/>).

**Period.** The 2022 draft [is titled 2022-2026](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>).

**Delivery plan.** The Digital Government Strategy of 2019 [lists an action to update the National ICT Policy, owned by the Ministry of Information and the Department of E-Government](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The same strategy says the government [carried out an ICT sector review during 2013-2017](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A GSMA report of August 2026 [lists the National ICT Policy 2013 among the prior policies on which current digital strategies build](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>).
