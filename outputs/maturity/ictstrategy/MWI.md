# Malawi

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The Digital Government Strategy was published in September 2019. It says it will [assist the Ministry of Information in implementing the targets in the National ICT Policy](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Standing.** The National ICT Policy 2013 is [listed in the policy framework of a 2025 management plan for the government data centre](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099083025102027538/pdf/P505095-7a647ee6-33d5-44b5-97c1-1c135c16efd2.pdf>). A UNCDF scan of May 2023 [calls it outdated and in need of updating](<https://www.uncdf.org/Download/AdminFileWithFilename?cultureId=127&filename=2505-malawi-ides-report---designpdf&id=19003>). The Presidential Delivery Unit [produced a draft National ICT and Digitalization Policy in 2022](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>).

**Text.** An essay of March 2026 [links a copy of the 2013 policy's text in the ITU national strategies repository](<https://data-landscapers.com/2026/03/31/africa-data-problem/>).

**Period.** The 2022 draft [is titled 2022-2026](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>).

**Delivery plan.** The Digital Government Strategy of 2019 [lists an action to update the National ICT Policy](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>). [The Ministry of Information and the Department of E-Government own the action](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The same strategy says the government [carried out an ICT sector review during 2013-2017](<https://api.pppc.mw/storage/161/DIGITAL-GOVERNMENT-STRATEGY-BOOK.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A GSMA report of August 2026 [lists the National ICT Policy 2013 among prior policies](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>). [Current digital strategies build on those policies](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>).

***Also on file***. These include the [National Digitalization Policy 2023-2028](<https://www.ogp.gov.mw/index.php/documents/malawi-ogp-national-action-plan-2025-2028/download>), the [National Digital Transformation Strategy 2026](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>) and the [Digital Economy Strategy](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>). They also include the [National Communications Act 2016](<https://www.gsma.com/about-us/regions/africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Driving-digital-transformation-of-the-economy-in-Malawi.pdf>), the [Electronic Transactions and Cyber Security Act 2016](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>) and the [Data Protection Act 2024](<https://ppda.mw/storage/documents/opentenders/ToRs%20OPERATIONALIZATION_OF_THE_DATA_PROTECTION_Act.pdf>). Others are the [ICT Policy for the Malawi Judiciary](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/e-justice_national_conference_malawi_report_pub.pdf>) and the [Judiciary Strategic Plan 2019-2024](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2025-06/e-justice_national_conference_malawi_report_pub.pdf>). They also include [Malawi 2063](<https://www.intgovforum.org/en/filedepot_download/258/21450>), the [Malawi 2063 First 10-Year Implementation Plan 2021-2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099083025102027538/pdf/P505095-7a647ee6-33d5-44b5-97c1-1c135c16efd2.pdf>) and the [Digital Malawi Acceleration Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091824155589106/pdf/P505095-9d6abe69-a9cf-48cb-bdd8-9140a232f79e.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found reports the act and date that adopted the National ICT Policy 2013, and no period is given for it. No delivery plan of the policy and no report against it has been found. Nothing on its governance is dated within three years. No source found says whether the 2022 draft was validated or adopted.
