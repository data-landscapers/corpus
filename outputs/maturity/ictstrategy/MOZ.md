# Mozambique

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** [Resolution 19/2018 gives the National Institute of Electronic Government (INAGE) the task of mobilising the money, materials and staff for the policy](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>). The policy says [its implementation depends on internal and external finance and private investment, and names no budget line](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>). In 2022 [INTIC said its work aimed to implement the policy](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/19-01-Relatorio-de-Actividades-Anual-INTIC-2022-10.02.2023.pdf>).

**Standing.** [The Council of Ministers approved the Política para a Sociedade da Informação on 20 March 2018 by Resolution 17/2018, in force from 21 June 2018](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>). It [revokes the Política de Informática of 2000](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>).

**Text.** [The full text is printed in the Boletim da República, I Série, no. 122, of 21 June 2018](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>).

**Period.** [The policy states no end date and no review year](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The policy says [an Operational Plan sets the actions, those responsible and the calendar for each five years of the Strategic Plan, and states no cost](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_122_I_SERIE_2018.pdf>). [Resolution 52/2019 approved the 2019-2028 Strategic Plan to put the policy into operation](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/BR_199_I_SERIE_2019.pdf>).

**Reporting.** [INTIC's activity report for 2022 says its efforts that year aimed to implement the policy and its Strategic Plan](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/19-01-Relatorio-de-Actividades-Anual-INTIC-2022-10.02.2023.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In April 2026 Mozambique [told a UNCTAD ministerial roundtable](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/ecn162026_s08_mr_mozambique_miquidade_en.pdf>) that the policy, approved by Resolution 17/2018, aims at widespread adoption of ICT; the statement names no change to it.
