# Tanzania

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The ICT Commission, under the Ministry of ICT, [has the role of ensuring the National ICT Policy is implemented](<https://dpi.africa.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Tanzania_case_study.pdf>), a November 2024 case study states. The policy's December 2016 implementation strategy [names the Government budget, development partners and the private sector as its financing sources](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/TZA738.pdf>).

**Standing.** The National ICT Policy 2016 was [signed by the Minister for Works, Transport and Communication in May 2016](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1608389026-National%20ICT%20Policy%20of%202016.pdf>) as the Government's revision of the 2003 policy. A June 2025 report [cites it as the country's ICT policy](<https://aiconference.cipit.org/documents/the-state-of-ai-in-africa-report.pdf>). The ministry [issued a successor text dated June 2023](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1691814917-FINAL%20DRAFT%20VERS%204%2013TH%20JULY%20NICTP%202023.pdf>), which states no adoption date.

**Text.** The full policy is [published as a PDF on the ministry's website](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1608389026-National%20ICT%20Policy%20of%202016.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy's implementation strategy [covers 2016/17 to 2020/21](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/TZA738.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The strategy [estimates that Tsh. 248.2 billion is required over its five years](<https://policyvault.africa/wp-content/uploads/policy/TZA738.pdf>), with a logframe of 22 objectives giving targets, indicators, time frames and responsible institutions.

**Reporting.** The June 2023 successor text says the ministry [will design a monitoring and evaluation system and evaluate after five years](<https://www.mawasiliano.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1691814917-FINAL%20DRAFT%20VERS%204%2013TH%20JULY%20NICTP%202023.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** In August 2026 The Citizen reported the ICT Commission's director general [describing the 2016 policy as the one that followed the 2003 policy](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/experts-urge-tanzania-to-strengthen-digital-trust-5552550>).
