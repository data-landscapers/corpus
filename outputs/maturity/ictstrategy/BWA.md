# Botswana

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The contents of the National ICT Policy (Maitlamo) of 2007 [list a governance structure](<https://publicadministration.un.org/unpsa/Portals/0/UNPSA_Submitted_Docs/2019/f912b59f-5963-4335-9dff-194a1a522c49/Maitlamo%20Policy_26112019_083359_d807e512-ea2e-4d56-8fba-60679904b985.pdf>) of an Information Age Council, an ICT Legal Reform Taskforce, an enhanced Department of Information Technology and an e-Governance Structure.

**Standing.** The National Assembly [approved the policy in August 2007](<https://publicadministration.un.org/unpsa/Portals/0/UNPSA_Submitted_Docs/2019/f912b59f-5963-4335-9dff-194a1a522c49/Maitlamo%20Policy_26112019_083359_d807e512-ea2e-4d56-8fba-60679904b985.pdf>), as Government Paper No 3 of 2007, and a report published in December 2025 [refers to it as Botswana's existing policy](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>).

**Text.** The Ministry of Communications, Science and Technology [published the full text](<https://publicadministration.un.org/unpsa/Portals/0/UNPSA_Submitted_Docs/2019/f912b59f-5963-4335-9dff-194a1a522c49/Maitlamo%20Policy_26112019_083359_d807e512-ea2e-4d56-8fba-60679904b985.pdf>) in August 2007.

**Period.** The World Bank's 2022 diagnostic says the policy [was launched in 2007](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100007222230376/pdf/P1728130fa985906a09c220cb6fb4264212.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The same diagnostic says the policy was launched [to guide the development of the sector](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100007222230376/pdf/P1728130fa985906a09c220cb6fb4264212.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The December 2025 report gives the policy's aim as [faster, cheaper and more transparent government services](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>) in support of socioeconomic development.

**Last twelve months.** The policy of 2007 was still [the one named for Botswana](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>) in the report of December 2025.

***Also on file***. [SmartBots Digital Transformation Strategy (2020)](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found gives the policy an end year, a delivery plan, a report against its targets or a source of finance. Its governance bodies are known only from the 2007 text, and no dated event falls in the last twelve months.
