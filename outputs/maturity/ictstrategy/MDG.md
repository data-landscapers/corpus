# Madagascar

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The MPTDN formulated the Politique nationale des TIC pour le développement in 2004 [with support from UNDP](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Standing.** A JICA study of March 2022 describes the 2004 policy as [the Malagasy government's main ICT policy](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Text.** The JICA study reports the policy as setting [seven strategic objectives](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy's targets run to [2015 for the digital network and to ten years from 2004 for users and engineers](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The targets include [a digital network covering every fivondronana by 2015, 10 million ICT users and 30,000 ICT engineers](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>). The policy [states no cost](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Reporting.** In March 2022 JICA assessed progress against the policy's objectives: [legal and regulatory reform was relatively advanced](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>), while [network extension and the training of ICT engineers were behind for lack of funds](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest account of the policy is [JICA's of March 2022](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>).
