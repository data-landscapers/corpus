# Mali

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The Politique nationale des TIC 2005 [names financing as one of its six priorities](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>), alongside the legal framework, infrastructure, content and applications, media, and cooperation.

**Standing.** The Government [adopted the national information and communication technology policy in 2005](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>). It followed the Déclaration de politique sectorielle des télécommunications, which the Government [adopted in 2000](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Text.** The national digital economy strategy of November 2014, Mali Numérique 2020, [sets out the 2005 policy's six priorities](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy [dates from 2005](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The 2000 telecommunications declaration [set the country's telecommunications objectives and an action programme](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>). The 2005 policy [is built on six priorities](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Reporting.** Mali Numérique 2020 [states in 2014 that the Government adopted the policy](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The policy [was adopted in 2005](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>), and the telecommunications declaration before it [in 2000](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [Mali Numérique 2020 — Stratégie Nationale de Développement de l'Economie Numérique](<https://data.inafran.ru/files/Doc/Mali/Nacionalnaa%20strategia%20razvitia%20cifrovoj%20ekonomiki%202020%20prinata%20v%202015.pdf>).

***Not found***. No text of the 2005 policy has been found, and no source says it is unpublished. No period, delivery plan, results report, mandated body or budget line has been found for it, and nothing is dated within the last twelve months; its adoption is known only from a 2014 document.
