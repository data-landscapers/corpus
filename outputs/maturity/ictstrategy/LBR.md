# Liberia

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The National ICT Policy 2019-2024 [gives the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications overall coordination, implementation, review and oversight](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>), and the national digital strategy published in February 2026 says [its governing board, chief information officer and council of chief information officers were put in place](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). On money, the policy says [the Executive ensures resources are available to the ministries concerned](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>).

**Standing.** The government [adopted the policy in 2019](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>), and [the President signed it that year](<https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/1f7221545bf1b7c89b850dd85cb409b0-0400072021/related/de4a-liberia-final.pdf>).

**Text.** The [full text is on the ministry's website](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>).

**Period.** The policy [set itself five years, 2019 to 2024](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>), and [was extended to 2025](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). The ministry's draft of its successor, the National ICT Policy 2025-2030, dated 7 January 2025, [lists it as expired](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/NATIONAL-ICT-POLICY2025-2030Draft-1-Jan-7-2025.docx.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The policy carries [a summary of measurable targets with timelines of one to five years after adoption](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/ApprovedNationalICTPolicy2019-2024Liberia.pdf>).

**Reporting.** An appendix to the national digital strategy [reviews the policy's initiatives and says its targets have not been met, among them internet access at 50% of clinics and hospitals](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The national digital strategy, published on 3 February 2026, says the successor policy [has been drafted and is in consultation, with approval targeted for the last quarter of 2025](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Liberia-National-Digital-Strategy.pdf>). A press release of 8 September 2026 [lists a draft National ICT Policy](<https://www.einpresswire.com/article/939735610/csquared-completes-commercial-broadband-programme-in-liberia-with-support-from-microsoft>).
