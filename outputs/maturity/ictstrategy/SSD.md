# South Sudan

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The [minister of ICT and postal services](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-optimistic-about-digital-prospects-despite-conflict/8OKdWqDXan4qbznQ>) spoke for the policy at the 30th EACO Assemblies in Juba, reported in June 2025.

**Standing.** The Government of South Sudan published its [National ICT Policy in 2012](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/fr/document/4nwphnnp7r>) as a government policy statement. In June 2025 the minister said the government is [implementing the revised ICT Policy of 2012](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-optimistic-about-digital-prospects-despite-conflict/8OKdWqDXan4qbznQ>).

**Text.** The [policy text](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/fr/document/4nwphnnp7r>) is public on the ICT Policy Africa document page as a government publication.

**Period.** The policy is [dated 2012](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/fr/document/4nwphnnp7r>).

**Delivery plan.** The text sets [sector policy for posts, telecommunications and information technology](<https://ictpolicyafrica.org/fr/document/4nwphnnp7r>).

**Reporting.** The minister's June 2025 statement that the policy is being implemented [gave no figure or measure](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-optimistic-about-digital-prospects-despite-conflict/8OKdWqDXan4qbznQ>) against the policy's targets.

**Last twelve months.** The latest statement on the policy is the minister's, [published on 12 June 2025](<https://itweb.africa/article/south-sudan-optimistic-about-digital-prospects-despite-conflict/8OKdWqDXan4qbznQ>).
