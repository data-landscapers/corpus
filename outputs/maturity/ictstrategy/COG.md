# Congo

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** A [project coordination unit in the Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications in charge of ICT was to monitor implementation of the National ICT Policy](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/123451468204842891/pdf/616460PAD0P1220e0only0900BOX358354B.pdf>), as a project appraisal document of May 2011 set out.

**Standing.** The government [drew up its national ICT development policy in 2008-2009](<https://leonjusteibombo.cg/2023/02/22/congo-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-6-mars-2019/>), the minister told the Council of Ministers on 6 March 2019. An [appraisal document of 4 May 2011 treats the National ICT Policy as existing](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/123451468204842891/pdf/616460PAD0P1220e0only0900BOX358354B.pdf>).

**Text.** The [2011 appraisal document does not describe the policy's scope](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/123451468204842891/pdf/616460PAD0P1220e0only0900BOX358354B.pdf>).

**Period.** The [Council of Ministers communique of 6 March 2019 gives no date of adoption](<https://leonjusteibombo.cg/2023/02/22/congo-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-6-mars-2019/>) for the policy.

**Delivery plan.** In 2011 a [project steering committee was to check project activities against the National ICT Policy](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/123451468204842891/pdf/616460PAD0P1220e0only0900BOX358354B.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The [coordination unit was to monitor the policy's implementation together with the government's ICT strategy](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/123451468204842891/pdf/616460PAD0P1220e0only0900BOX358354B.pdf>), under the 2011 appraisal document.

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated fact is of 6 March 2019, when the [minister said the electronic transactions bill follows the national ICT development policy](<https://leonjusteibombo.cg/2023/02/22/congo-conseil-des-ministres-du-mercredi-6-mars-2019/>).
