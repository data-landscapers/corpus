# Zambia

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The National ICT Policy 2023 [makes the ministry responsible for ICTs answerable for coordinating, implementing, monitoring and evaluating it](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023.pdf>), with ZICTA regulating the sector. It lists the national budget among seven possible sources of finance and states no budget line or amount.

**Standing.** The Ministry of Technology and Science [published the policy on 11 October 2023](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023.pdf>) as the government's revision of the National ICT Policy 2006. In December 2025 the policy working group of an ITU event in Lusaka [recognised it as the overarching framework governing ICTs in Zambia](<https://africabbmaps.itu.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Africa-BB-Maps-Project-Zambia-National-Event-Report.pdf>).

**Text.** The full text is [on the ministry's website](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023.pdf>) as a PDF.

**Period.** The policy [states no end year](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023.pdf>). It is anchored on Vision 2030 and sets no term of its own.

**Delivery plan.** A ZDA sector profile of September 2024 says the 2022 to 2026 ICT Policy Implementation Plan [lists seventeen investment actions](<https://www.zda.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ZDA-ICT-Sector-Profile-2024.pdf>), among them district fibre, a national internet exchange point, regional data centres and rural Wi-Fi, with no costs given.

**Reporting.** The policy [gives monitoring and evaluation of its implementation to the ministry and the Cabinet Office](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-ICT-Policy-2023.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** On 12 November 2025 the Ministry of Community Development and Social Services [launched guidelines it said were aligned with the policy](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/11/13/zambias-community-development-ministry-unveils-three-digital-strategies-to-boost-service-delivery/>), TechAfrica News reports.

[National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2027](<https://www.mots.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/National-Digital-Transformation-Strategy.pdf>); [Eighth National Development Plan (8NDP)](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf>); [Zambia Police Service Strategic Plan 2023-2026](<https://doi.org/10.51867/ajernet.7.1.89>); [Digital Zambia Acceleration Project (DZAP)](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Prepurchase-of-Bulk-Internet-Capacity_Zambia_WB_RVD_Feb.24.pdf>).

***Not found***. No report, review or evaluation against the policy's targets, and no adoption date beyond the text's publication. The implementation plan carries no costs and no budget line is tied to the policy.
