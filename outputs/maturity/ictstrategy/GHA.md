# Ghana

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The National Telecommunications Policy of 2005 [makes the Ministry of Communications responsible for defining and reviewing telecommunications policy](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>) and the National Communications Authority responsible for implementing it. A guide published on 20 September 2024 says the [Ministry of Communications and Digitalization directs communications-sector policy](<https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/ghana-digital-economy>).

**Standing.** The Ministry of Communications [released the National Telecommunications Policy as final on 10 January 2005](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>). A report published on 8 October 2025 [recommends updating it and the ICT for Accelerated Development Policy of 2003](<https://researchictafrica.net/research/digital-africa-post-the-pandemic-ghana-report/>), and calls both outdated.

**Text.** The [full 33-page text](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>) is on the National Communications Authority's website.

**Period.** The policy [states no end date for itself](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>) and provides for the ministry to review and amend it.

**Delivery plan.** The policy [sets dated actions and targets with no costs](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>), among them 25% penetration and universal access by 2010.

**Reporting.** The policy [gives the Ministry of Communications the monitoring of progress](<https://nca.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/National-Telecommunications-Policy-NTP-2005.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** The [report of 8 October 2025](<https://researchictafrica.net/research/digital-africa-post-the-pandemic-ghana-report/>) still names the 2005 policy as Ghana's.
