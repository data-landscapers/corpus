# Guinea

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The national digitalisation strategy, published in June 2024, [puts the drafting of the Lettre de politique sectorielle du numérique under the ministry it calls MPTEN, with the executive, and costs it at USD 50 thousand](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Standing.** On 9 July 2026 the digital economy ministry [opened a national workshop to draft a telecommunications policy, strategy and action plan](<https://agpguinee.com/guinee-tenue-dun-atelier-delaboration-de-la-politique-de-la-strategie-et-du-plan-daction-des-telecommunication-2026-2030/>), with support from the World Bank's WARDIP project. The national digitalisation strategy of 2024 had [announced a digital sector policy letter](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). The government [adopted an ICT policy and strategy in 2010](<https://smartafrica.org/IMG/pdf/srategie_tic_finale_v.6_28_juillet_2016.pdf>).

**Text.** No text of the telecommunications policy is known. The 2024 strategy [lists the sector policy letter as a document still to be drafted](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Period.** The telecommunications policy [is to cover 2026 to 2030](<https://agpguinee.com/guinee-tenue-dun-atelier-delaboration-de-la-politique-de-la-strategie-et-du-plan-daction-des-telecommunication-2026-2030/>). An earlier [national information and communication infrastructure plan covered 2001 to 2004](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Delivery plan.** The 2010 policy [set six aims and proposed one computer for each secondary pupil, higher-education student and teacher, with no cost or dates stated](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Reporting.** UNESCO's profile of 2023 [reports later initiatives launched in line with the 2010 policy](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Last twelve months.** Drafting of the telecommunications policy [began on 9 July 2026](<https://agpguinee.com/guinee-tenue-dun-atelier-delaboration-de-la-politique-de-la-strategie-et-du-plan-daction-des-telecommunication-2026-2030/>). The work is to continue in technical sessions.
