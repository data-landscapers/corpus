# Guinea

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The national digitalisation strategy, published in June 2024, [puts the drafting of the Lettre de politique sectorielle du numérique under the ministry it calls MPTEN, with the executive, and costs it at USD 50 thousand](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Standing.** The letter is [announced as initiative 1.1.8 of that strategy](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>), to be drafted. The government [adopted the Politique et stratégie nationales de développement des TIC in 2010](<https://smartafrica.org/IMG/pdf/srategie_tic_finale_v.6_28_juillet_2016.pdf>).

**Text.** The strategy [lists the letter as a document still to be drafted](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).

**Period.** The strategy [allows six months for the drafting and gives it no date](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>). An earlier [national information and communication infrastructure plan covered 2001 to 2004](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Delivery plan.** The 2010 policy [set six aims and proposed one computer for each secondary pupil, higher-education student and teacher, with no cost or dates stated](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Reporting.** UNESCO's profile of 2023 [reports later initiatives launched in line with the 2010 policy](<https://education-profiles.org/fr/afrique-sub-saharienne/guinee/~technologie>).

**Last twelve months.** The latest dated fact on the letter is [its listing in the strategy published on 11 June 2024](<https://ande.gov.gn/file/2025/01/Strategie-Numerique_Guinee-2027_v1.2.pdf>).
