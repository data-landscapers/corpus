# Namibia

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The National ICT Policy 2026 [sets out clear roles for the institutions responsible](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>) for implementing and monitoring ICT development, The Namibian reported in January 2026.

**Standing.** The Ministry of ICT [launched the policy at Opuwo](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>) in January 2026. It follows the Overarching ICT Policy 2009, which the National Broadband Policy gazetted on 14 August 2020 [says it is built on](<https://www.lac.org.na/laws/2020/7308.pdf>), and the National ICT Policy / Broadband Policy, which the ITU's repository [lists among Namibia's strategies](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Text.** The Namibian [reported the policy's launch](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>) on 29 January 2026.

**Period.** The minister said at the launch that the policy [would guide the ICT sector over the next five years](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>). The ITU's repository gives the earlier National ICT Policy / Broadband Policy [a time frame of 2018 to 2022](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Delivery plan.** The 2026 policy [gives institutions their roles in implementing ICT development](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>).

**Reporting.** For the earlier National ICT Policy / Broadband Policy, the ITU's repository [records training programmes as evidence of implementation](<https://academy.itu.int/research-advocacy-and-convening-platforms/gcbi/repository-national-digital-skills-strategies>).

**Last twelve months.** The ICT minister [launched the policy in January 2026](<https://www.namibian.com.na/ict-ministry-launches-strategic-plan-policy-to-expand-digital-access-and-strengthen-cybersecurity/>).
