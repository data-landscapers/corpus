# Rwanda

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The ICT Sector Strategic Plan 2024-2029 [commits the Ministry of ICT and Innovation and its affiliated agencies](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>) to deliver its programmes. The ministry [convened the sector working group's review of the plan under the finance ministry's guidance](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=112721&t=f&token=a45cd9603c79f2619ff07ff066661e300901aadf>) on 22 November 2024.

**Standing.** The ministry [published the plan, dated 1 January 2025 on its cover, as one it is committed to implement](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>). A sector review of 22 November 2024 [was convened to validate it](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=112721&t=f&token=a45cd9603c79f2619ff07ff066661e300901aadf>).

**Text.** The plan is [a PDF on the ministry's own site](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>).

**Period.** The plan [covers the financial years 2024/25 to 2028/29](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>). The plan before it [covered 2018-2024](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=86845&t=f&token=09f9cf553edf3cbb6e3f576d3105b0c95aa89103>).

**Delivery plan.** The plan's contents [list an implementation plan and a section on cost and financing, with budget allocated across priority areas](<https://www.minict.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/minict_user_upload/Documents/Strategies/ICT__SSP_2024-2029_.pdf>). The November 2024 review [sets out its targets, among them 1.5 million citizens trained in coding and ICT skills and 50,000 digital jobs](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=112721&t=f&token=a45cd9603c79f2619ff07ff066661e300901aadf>).

**Reporting.** The sector review for 2023/24 reports against the 2018-2024 plan, and [put digitised public services at 91% against a target of 100% by 2024](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=112721&t=f&token=a45cd9603c79f2619ff07ff066661e300901aadf>).

**Last twelve months.** After a cabinet briefing on 24 July 2026 the ministry [set 30 June 2027 as the date 3G is switched off nationwide, as part of the plan](<https://www.cnbcafrica.com/2026/rwanda-sets-june-2027-deadline-to-retire-3g-clearing-spectrum-for-4g-and-5g>).
