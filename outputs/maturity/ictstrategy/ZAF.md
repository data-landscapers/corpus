# South Africa

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** In 2019/20 the Department of Telecommunications and Postal Services developed ICT policy and legislation for the sector through [its Programme 3, ICT Policy Research and Capacity Development](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202012/telecommunications-and-postal-services-annual-report-2019-2020.pdf>).

**Standing.** The Department issued the National Integrated ICT Policy White Paper in [Government Gazette 40325 of 3 October 2016](<https://pmg.org.za/files/2601001discussionusaasa.pdf>).

**Text.** The White Paper is published as [a gazette file on gov.za](<https://pmg.org.za/files/2601001discussionusaasa.pdf>) and [on the department's website, dcdt.gov.za](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099653207022613768/pdf/IDU-eb3a6d23-36a4-413f-bc1e-458a577684b7.pdf>).

**Period.** The White Paper's stated main purpose is [to unlock ICTs to eliminate poverty and reduce inequality by 2030](<https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/digital-sovereignty-in-the-brics-countries/south-african-digital-sovereignty-at-the-crossroad-of-securitization-and-development/08C1119DE09286B84626FA49F6AD0586>).

**Delivery plan.** In 2019/20 the Department [pursued its legislative programme in line with the White Paper](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202012/telecommunications-and-postal-services-annual-report-2019-2020.pdf>).

**Reporting.** The Department's 2019/20 annual report records, against the indicator "ICT legislation and policies in line with the White Paper", that [the Digital Development Fund Bill, the Data and Cloud Policy and a framing document for the White Paper's review were developed](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202012/telecommunications-and-postal-services-annual-report-2019-2020.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** A World Bank report of June 2026 lists the White Paper among [the key anchors of South Africa's policy framework](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099653207022613768/pdf/IDU-eb3a6d23-36a4-413f-bc1e-458a577684b7.pdf>), and a discussion document on universal access of October 2026 lists it as [a policy instrument](<https://pmg.org.za/files/2601001discussionusaasa.pdf>).
