# Kenya

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** Research ICT Africa's report of September 2026 says the Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy [is responsible for policy formulation and sector coordination](<https://researchictafrica.net/research/growing-the-digital-economy-in-kenya-its-structure-and-challenges/>), with the Communications Authority as regulator.

**Standing.** The ICT Sector Policy Guidelines 2006 [were reviewed and updated to a National ICT Policy in 2019](<https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-04/Final%20Communications%20Policy%20as%20at%2022nd%20January%202026.pdf>), and the policy [was gazetted in 2020](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>) as Gazette Notice No. 5472.

**Text.** The policy [was published in a special issue of the Kenya Gazette](<https://cms.icta.go.ke/sites/default/files/2022-04/Kenya%20Digital%20Masterplan%202022-2032%20Online%20Version.pdf>), and the September 2026 report [cites it to a page on the ministry's website](<https://researchictafrica.net/research/growing-the-digital-economy-in-kenya-its-structure-and-challenges/>).

**Period.** The draft artificial intelligence policy of July 2026 [lists the National ICT Policy 2019 among the instruments in place](<https://ict.go.ke/sites/default/files/AI%20Policy%20Doc/draft-kenya-ai-and-emerging-technologies-policy-2026.pdf>).

**Delivery plan.** The Communications Authority's consultation of September 2026 says the policy [directs government agencies to promote and license private investment in carrier-neutral data centres](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-09/Public%20Consultation%20on%20Data%20Centres%20September%202026.pdf>).

**Reporting.** A 2022 review of the Digital Literacy Programme says the policy [sets objectives for scaling up ICT in education](<https://edu-design.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Digital-Literacy-Programme.pdf>).

**Last twelve months.** An analysis published in July 2026 [names the ICT policy among Kenya's existing instruments](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/strengthening-legal-and-policy-frameworks-for-ai-accessibility-for-persons-with-disabilities-in-kenya/>).
