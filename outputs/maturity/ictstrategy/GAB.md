# Gabon

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** [A JICA baseline study of public service digitalisation in African countries, published in March 2022](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>), states that Gabon has formulated a national ICT policy, the Politique nationale des TIC.

**Standing.** [The JICA study of March 2022](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>) states that Gabon has formulated a national ICT policy consistent with the PSGE Vision 2025, and gives no date of adoption.

**Text.** [JICA, in March 2022](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>), states that the national ICT policy has been formulated.

**Period.** [The JICA study](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>) states that the policy is consistent with the PSGE Vision 2025.

**Delivery plan.** [JICA, in March 2022](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>), states that Gabon has formulated a national ICT policy.

**Reporting.** [The same JICA study](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>) states that the policy has been formulated, consistent with the PSGE Vision 2025.

**Last twelve months.** [The JICA statement that Gabon has formulated a national ICT policy](<https://openjicareport.jica.go.jp/pdf/12369203.pdf>) dates from March 2022.
