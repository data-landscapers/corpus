# São Tomé and Príncipe

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The ICT Sector Strategy was [prepared in 2012 under the Central African Backbone project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), which the World Bank financed; the programme as a whole carried an IDA grant of SDR 9.8 million.

**Standing.** The strategy was [prepared in 2012 after wide consultation with stakeholders](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>). In June 2015 its [adoption by the Council of Ministers was still pending](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>).

**Text.** The World Bank's completion report on the project, prepared in June 2015, [names the strategy as prepared and not yet adopted](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>).

**Period.** The strategy [dates from 2012](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), the year it was prepared.

**Delivery plan.** The strategy was itself [a product of the Central African Backbone project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), prepared under it in 2012.

**Reporting.** The World Bank's completion report of June 2015 [records the strategy as awaiting adoption by the Council of Ministers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>).

**Last twelve months.** In June 2015 the strategy [was still waiting for the Council of Ministers to adopt it](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/234391468190440376/txt/ICR3141-P117652-Box391488B-OUO-9.txt>), three years after it was prepared.
