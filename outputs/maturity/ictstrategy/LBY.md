# Libya

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The [General Authority for Communications and Informatics (GACI) is the authority competent to regulate and develop the telecommunications and information technology sector](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351103&lang=ar>), and it is [the body that announces work done under the strategy](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi>).

**Standing.** In October 2025 GACI [announced root servers in Tripoli and Benghazi as implementing the National Telecommunications and Informatics Strategy 2023-2027](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi>).

**Text.** The Ministry of Telecommunications and Informatics says the strategy [aims to develop digital infrastructure and the sector's efficiency](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367155&lang=en>).

**Period.** The strategy [covers 2023 to 2027](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi>), and the ministry [gave the same years in October 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=367155&lang=en>).

**Delivery plan.** At a GACI workshop in February 2026, officials [stated the aim of drafting legislation and a guidance manual for data centres and cloud computing](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351103&lang=ar>).

**Reporting.** GACI [presented the root-server launch of October 2025 as the strategy being carried out](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi>).

**Last twelve months.** Root servers [entered service in data centres in Tripoli and Benghazi in October 2025](<https://libyaherald.com/2025/10/general-authority-for-communications-and-informatics-libya-enters-digital-sovereignty-era-with-official-launch-of-root-servers-in-tripoli-and-benghazi>). On 15 February 2026 GACI [held a workshop in Tripoli on regulating data centres and cloud computing, within the national communications strategy](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351103&lang=ar>).
