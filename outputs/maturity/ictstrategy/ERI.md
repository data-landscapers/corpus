# Eritrea

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** [A UNECA paper on Eastern Africa, published in April 2009](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>), states that Eritrea has no National ICT Development Agency.

**Standing.** [The UNECA paper of April 2009](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>) states that Eritrea has no national ICT policy defining the government's orientation and strategies for development, and its table of 13 states enters "No ICT Policy" against the country.

**Text.** [UNECA, in April 2009](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>), states that Eritrea has no national ICT policy.

**Period.** [UNECA, in April 2009](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>), states that Eritrea has no national ICT policy.

**Delivery plan.** [The same UNECA paper](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>) states that no National ICT Development Plan exists for Eritrea.

**Reporting.** [UNECA, in April 2009](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>), states that Eritrea has neither a national ICT policy nor a National ICT Development Plan.

**Last twelve months.** [The UNECA statement that Eritrea has no national ICT policy](<https://archive.uneca.org/sites/default/files/PublicationFiles/03_harmonization_tic-final_en.pdf>) dates from April 2009.
