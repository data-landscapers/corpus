# Angola

## Sector policy

**Governance and finance.** The telecommunications ministry, MINTTICS, [put the proposed Livro Branco das TIC 2023-2027 to public consultation](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/terceira-edicao-do-forum-angola-digital/>) in 2024, and its minister writes that the policy [aligns the sector with the National Development Plan 2023-2027](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/soberania-digital-do-governo-de-angola-uma-necessidade-que-se-impoe/>).

**Standing.** The Livro Branco das TIC 2023-2027 was [approved by Presidential Decree 272/24 of 5 December 2024](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-256-25-de-03-de-dezembro/>), [repealing the Livro Branco das TIC 2019-2022](<https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1585472/white-paper-on-information-and-communication-technologies-2023-2027-strategic-vision-for-icts-in-angola>).

**Text.** The ministry's site carries [Presidential Order 129/19 of 22 July 2019, which approved the Livro Branco das TIC 2019-2022, with a download link](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/documentos/despacho-presidencial-129-19/>).

**Period.** The policy runs from 2023 to 2027, with [five objectives targeted by 2027](<https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1585472/white-paper-on-information-and-communication-technologies-2023-2027-strategic-vision-for-icts-in-angola>).

**Delivery plan.** A later decree describes the policy as [setting policy measures and strategic actions for the ICT sector on six axes](<https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2025/decreto-presidencial-n-o-256-25-de-03-de-dezembro/>).

**Reporting.** In October 2025 the ministry reported that the president's State of the Nation message [credited the Livro Branco das TIC and its instruments with moving the sector from reform to development](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/mensagem-sobre-o-estado-da-nacao-2025/>).

**Last twelve months.** A March 2026 article cites the policy as [setting plans for a space studies centre, expert training and communications, navigation and meteorology infrastructure](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/angola-launches-259-million-earth-observation-satellite-project>).
