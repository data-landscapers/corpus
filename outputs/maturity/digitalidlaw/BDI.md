# Burundi

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Burundi's identity card runs on two ministerial ordinances, of 1978 and 1986, with no law behind them. The government's procedures page for the card [lists those ordinances as its main texts](<https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite>) and names no identity law. The page is held as published in January 2026 and says further texts remain to be adopted before the national identification file is instituted.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** With no law in force, none provides for a digital identity. The same page says [the national identification file still awaits its instituting texts](<https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite>), and the card it describes rests on the two ordinances alone. Burundi therefore answers the first question only: an identity system run on orders, with the texts for its register still to be adopted.

***Also on file***. [Fichier National d'Identification / Carte Nationale d'Identité](<https://isoko.gov.bi/?procedures=fichier-national-de-lidentification-carte-nationale-didentite>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national identity law is in force.
