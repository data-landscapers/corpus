# Tunisia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Tunisia's Organic Law No. 2024-22 of 11 March 2024 [amends and completes the 1993 law on the national identity card](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>); it was published in the official journal on 12 March 2024. Parliament had [adopted it on 6 March 2024](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/tunisia-biometric-id-passport-bills-passed>). A ministerial session on 22 April 2024 [decided to start using the biometric card in the first half of 2025](<https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/tunisia-biometric-id-card-and-passport-usage-to-begin-in-h1-2025-bn9vuol5>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The organic law [provides that the identity card carries an encrypted electronic chip](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>). The chip holds the card number, a fingerprint and a photograph. It also holds an electronic certificate that allows identity to be verified and documents signed electronically. The law leaves the model of the card to a decree and the rules for the certificate to a joint ministerial order. In September 2025 La Presse reported that [the decrees on the card's model, specifications and security standards were still being prepared](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/09/21/tunisie-vers-le-lancement-imminent-de-la-carte-didentite-biometrique>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law has been found from the last three years. Nor has any rule made under it that others must check identity against the system.
