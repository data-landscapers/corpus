# Tunisia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Tunisia's Organic Law No. 2024-22 of 11 March 2024 [amends and completes the 1993 law on the national identity card](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>); it was published in the official journal on 12 March 2024. Parliament had [adopted it on 6 March 2024](<https://www.accessnow.org/press-release/tunisia-biometric-id-passport-bills-passed>). A ministerial session on 22 April 2024 [decided to start using the biometric card in the first half of 2025](<https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/tunisia-biometric-id-card-and-passport-usage-to-begin-in-h1-2025-bn9vuol5>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The organic law [provides that the identity card carries an encrypted electronic chip](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>) holding the card number, a fingerprint and a photograph, and an electronic certificate that allows identity to be verified and documents signed electronically. It leaves the model of the card to a decree and the rules for the certificate to a joint ministerial order. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Biometric national identity card](<https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/north-africa/tunisia-biometric-id-card-and-passport-usage-to-begin-in-h1-2025-bn9vuol5>); [National identity card with electronic chip](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
