# Mauritius

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Mauritius's identity card rests on the National Identity Card Act, No. 60 of 1985, which the identity card unit [lists as the law behind the card system](<https://mnis.govmu.org/>). The Act is an Act of Parliament, and regulations [made under its section 10](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>) came into operation on 19 February 2024, when the new card began to be issued.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The National Identity Card Regulations 2024 were [made by the minister on 16 February 2024](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>). They set the new identity card, its digital seal and an application made with fingerprints, and they revoke the regulations of 2013. Through them the 1985 Act now [provides for a card that is biometric and can be checked electronically](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>). The new card has been issued since 19 February 2024.
