# Chad

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Chad's Ordinance 002/PR/2020 of 14 February 2020 on civil status was [ratified by Law 006/PR/2020 of 29 May 2020](<https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/tchad_etude_de_cas_inclusion_systeme_didentite_fr_avril_2026.pdf>). It sets up the integrated population and secure titles system, SIGPTS, run by the secure titles agency ANATS. The system issues a unique national identification number, the NNI, through the national biometric population register.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Under [article 8 of the ordinance](<https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/tchad_etude_de_cas_inclusion_systeme_didentite_fr_avril_2026.pdf>), the register holds the biographic and biometric data and the NNI of everyone on the territory. Article 2 obliges every resident or visitor to register. Article 10 makes the NNI unique, unintelligible and non-sequential. The number thus reaches citizens, foreign residents and visitors alike. These are the 2020 provisions as the UNHCR describes them, in an April 2026 study. In April 2025 the telecoms regulator ARCEP [said every SIM card sold must be linked to an NNI](<https://www.letchadanthropus-tribune.com/tchad-securite-larcep-sonnera-lalerte-sur-lappartenance-des-cartes-sim/>) under the texts in force.
