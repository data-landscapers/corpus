# Chad

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Chad's Ordinance 002/PR/2020 of 14 February 2020 on civil status, [ratified by Law 006/PR/2020 of 29 May 2020](<https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/tchad_etude_de_cas_inclusion_systeme_didentite_fr_avril_2026.pdf>), sets up the integrated population and secure titles system (SIGPTS), run by the national agency for secure titles, ANATS. The system issues a unique national identification number (NNI) through the national biometric population register.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Under [article 8 of the ordinance the register holds the biographic and biometric data and the NNI](<https://www.refworld.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/tchad_etude_de_cas_inclusion_systeme_didentite_fr_avril_2026.pdf>) of everyone on the territory, and article 2 obliges every resident or visitor to register. Article 10 makes the NNI unique, unintelligible and non-sequential. Because the register covers everyone on the territory, the number reaches citizens, foreign residents and visitors alike. These are the 2020 provisions as the UNHCR describes them in an April 2026 study of refugee inclusion in the system.
