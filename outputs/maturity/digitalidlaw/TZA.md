# Tanzania

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Tanzania's Registration and Identification of Persons Act, first passed in 1986, [is recorded as still in force in the 2023 revised edition of the laws](<https://tanzanialaws.com/r/320-registration-and-identification-of-persons-act>). It provides for the registration of persons and the issue of identity cards. In January 2026 the government said it would [table an Infant Registration Bill in the session starting 27 January 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/tanzania-working-on-legislative-framework-for-infant-id-registration>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The 1986 Act is the law in force, and no source found says it provides for a biometric register or a unique number. The bill announced in January 2026 [would let the identification authority register children from birth](<https://dailynews.co.tz/nida-mulls-child-registration/>) and give each a unique identification number. No report says it has been tabled or passed. The Act is numbered Cap. 36 in the revised laws.

***Also on file***. [NIDA](<https://dailynews.co.tz/nida-mulls-child-registration/>); [eRITA](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/tanzania-working-on-legislative-framework-for-infant-id-registration>).

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force provides for a biometric register, an identity number unique to each person, or an identity that can be checked electronically.
