# Togo

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Togo's law no. 2020-009 of 10 September 2020 on the biometric identification of natural persons [created the national identification agency](<https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/>). Law no. 2022-010 of 24 June 2022, [adopted by the National Assembly and promulgated by the President](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_06_2022-67E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B025%20BIS.pdf>), amended several of its articles.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The amended law [defines a unique identification number issued after verification of demographic and biometric data](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_06_2022-67E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B025%20BIS.pdf>), and a central database against which the number and the data are authenticated electronically. A decree of 9 September 2022 [sets the rules for registering and updating the data and the conditions for using the number](<https://monidentite.anid.gouv.tg/documents/d/anid_pub/pr-decret-n-2022-094-pr-relatif-aux-modalites-d-inscription-de-rectification-et-d-actualisation-des-donnees-demographiques-et-biometriques-des-personnes-physiques>). Those rules all date from before September 2023, and none made under the law since then is on record. The agency the law created issues the number.

[Numéro d'identification unique (NIU)](<https://monidentite.anid.gouv.tg/documents/d/anid_pub/pr-decret-n-2022-094-pr-relatif-aux-modalites-d-inscription-de-rectification-et-d-actualisation-des-donnees-demographiques-et-biometriques-des-personnes-physiques>); [Base de données centrale](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_06_2022-67E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B025%20BIS.pdf>); [Registre social des personnes et des ménages](<https://jo.gouv.tg/sites/default/files/JO/JOS_24_06_2022-67E%20ANNEE%20N%C2%B025%20BIS.pdf>); [Services Publics platform](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>); [Central National Biometric Identification Database](<https://anid.gouv.tg/en/about-us/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
