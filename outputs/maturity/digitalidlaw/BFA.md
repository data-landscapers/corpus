# Burkina Faso

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Burkina Faso's transitional legislative assembly [adopted the bill on unique electronic identification of the natural person](<https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/>) on 11 October 2024. On 19 August 2025 the minister for digital transition [said the law had been adopted the previous year](<https://www.sidwaya.info/identification-electronique-des-personnes-le-programme-wuri-sensibilise-a-bobo-dioulasso/>). Neither source says the law has been promulgated.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The adopted text [underpins a system giving each resident a random unique identifier](<https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/>), issued against biographic data, fingerprints and iris, and linked to the national identity card, the passport, the driving licence, the patient file and the bank account. Until a source says the law is promulgated, that provision is a bill's and not a law's. The identifier is to be issued once to each resident and kept for life, by the text as reported in October 2024.
