# Burkina Faso

## National identity law

**Law in force.** The transitional legislative assembly [adopted the bill on unique electronic identification of the natural person](<https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/>) on 11 October 2024. On 19 August 2025 the minister for digital transition [said the law had been adopted the previous year](<https://www.sidwaya.info/identification-electronique-des-personnes-le-programme-wuri-sensibilise-a-bobo-dioulasso/>). Africa24 TV [reported on 22 November 2026](<https://africa24tv.com/burkina-faso-enrolement-biometrique-de-masse-2-400-agents-deployes-sur-pres-de-12-000-sites/>) that the national enrolment under way since 11 November is framed by the 2024 law.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The law [underpins a system giving each resident a random unique identifier](<https://burkina24.com/2024/10/11/burkina-faso-la-loi-portant-identification-unique-et-electronique-de-la-personne-physique-adoptee/>). The identifier is issued against biographic data, fingerprints and iris. It is linked to the national identity card, the passport, the driving licence, the patient file and the bank account. The identifier, named Yerekun, [has 12 digits and is issued free from age 5](<https://africa24tv.com/burkina-faso-enrolement-biometrique-de-masse-2-400-agents-deployes-sur-pres-de-12-000-sites/>).

***Also on file***.
