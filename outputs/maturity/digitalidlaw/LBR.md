# Liberia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Liberia's National Identification Registry Act was [approved by the President on 1 August 2011 and published on 8 August 2011](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Liberia-NIR-Act-2011.pdf>). It repeals a 1983 decree that had established the earlier identification system and sets up the National Identification Registry as the body in charge. The Act took effect on publication.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [sets up a National Biometric Identification System and a biometric card for each person](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Liberia-NIR-Act-2011.pdf>), enrolled with a fingerprint and a photograph. The Registry the Act creates is the body that runs that system. The Act is therefore a law for a biometric identity from its first day, and no later amendment to it is on record. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [National Biometric Identification System (NBIS)](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Liberia-NIR-Act-2011.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
