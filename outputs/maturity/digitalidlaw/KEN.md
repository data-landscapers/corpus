# Kenya

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Kenya has no stand-alone digital identity law. Its primary identity law is [the Registration of Persons Act, Cap 107](<https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/data-and-policy/article/an-assessment-of-the-legal-and-regulatory-framework-supporting-the-implementation-of-the-national-integrated-identity-management-system-niims-in-kenya/617BC651E2C09DE2CDD175C73A1C90E2>), first passed in 1947, and the government set up its digital identity system by an omnibus bill amending that Act in 2018. The High Court ruled in January 2020 that the government should enact an appropriate and comprehensive regulatory framework before rolling the system out.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** [Section 9A of the amended Act establishes the National Integrated Identity Management System](<https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/data-and-policy/article/an-assessment-of-the-legal-and-regulatory-framework-supporting-the-implementation-of-the-national-integrated-identity-management-system-niims-in-kenya/617BC651E2C09DE2CDD175C73A1C90E2>), to keep a national population register as the single source of personal information on every citizen and resident foreigner, and to assign each person registered a unique national identification number. The system is [anchored on the Huduma Namba regulations of 2020](<https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/data-and-policy/article/an-assessment-of-the-legal-and-regulatory-framework-supporting-the-implementation-of-the-national-integrated-identity-management-system-niims-in-kenya/617BC651E2C09DE2CDD175C73A1C90E2>), which civil society organisations criticised as creating by regulation what should be created by an Act; the system began under an executive order of 2018.

***Not held***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
