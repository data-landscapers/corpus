# Morocco

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Morocco's law no. 72-18, on the targeting of beneficiaries of social support programmes, [was promulgated on 8 August 2020 and published in the Bulletin officiel on 7 January 2021](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf>). It creates the national population register and the national registers agency that runs it. It is a statute and no bill to replace it is on record.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The law's second article [defines authentication as checking declared data online and in real time against the national population register](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf>). An identity entered in the register is therefore one that can be checked electronically, which is what the law provides the register for. The register and the agency are the law's two creations. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Registre national de la population](<https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/mor204689.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
