# DR Congo

## National identity law

**Law in force.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo's population file was created by [the Prime Minister's decree no. 22/07 of 2 March 2022](<https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html>), with no identity law among its legal bases. On 12 June 2026 two deputies [tabled a private member's bill](<https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi>) on the general rules for identifying natural persons, which is to go to committee.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** No law is in force. The 2022 decree [gives the population file a national identification number and biometric data](<https://leganet.cd/Legislation/Droit%20Public/Identification/decret%2022.04.02.03.2022.html>) and puts the national identification office in charge of it, on the strength of a decree alone. The bill tabled in June 2026 [describes the present arrangements as resting on decrees](<https://actualite.cd/2026/06/12/assemblee-nationale-venance-eyanga-et-patrick-namazihana-deposent-une-proposition-de-loi>), which is what it sets out to change. The bill had not been debated by the end of September 2026.
