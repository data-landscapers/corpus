# Ethiopia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Ethiopia's Digital Identification Proclamation, no. 1284/2023, [passed the House of Peoples' Representatives in March 2023](<https://kanatelevision.com/news/what-is-faydaverse-the-ethiopian-national-id-program-that-became-a-company-and-how-it-plans-to/>). It was [signed by the President on 18 April 2023](<https://ethiodata.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Ethiopian-Digital-Identification-Proclamation-No.-1284_2023-1.pdf>) and came into force on publication in the Federal Negarit Gazette. It bars the collection of ethnicity data. Unauthorised disclosure carries up to eight years in prison. It is the law the identity system runs under.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The proclamation [sets up a digital identification system that collects demographic and biometric data in a central database](<https://ethiodata.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Ethiopian-Digital-Identification-Proclamation-No.-1284_2023-1.pdf>). The system it describes is digital from the start: a person is enrolled with biometric data and identified from the central database. The proclamation's second article sets out the terms the rest of the text relies on. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.
