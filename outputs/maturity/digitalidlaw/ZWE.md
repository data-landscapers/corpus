# Zimbabwe

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Zimbabwe's National Registration Act [commenced on 24 September 1976](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1976/36>). It sets up the Registrar-General of National Registration, a register of residents and identity documents, and its consolidated text is up to date to 31 December 2016. A statutory instrument [gazetted on 30 January 2024 was made under its section 11](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The 2024 instrument [amends the registration fees](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-010%20National%20Registration%20%28Fees%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Regulations%2C%202024%20%28No.%2018%29.pdf>), which is not a rule on how identity is held or checked. The Act itself [provides for the registration of residents and a register](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1976/36>) kept by the Registrar-General. No source found says the Act, or an amendment to it, provides for a biometric register or an identity checked electronically. The Act is Chapter 10:17 of the laws of Zimbabwe.
