# Ghana

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Ghana's identity system rests on two Acts of Parliament that the National Identification Authority [lists as its laws](<https://nia.gov.gh/legal-and-regulations/>): the National Identification Authority Act, 2006 and the National Identity Register Act, 2008. The Authority [is the statutory body the 2006 Act set up](<https://nia.gov.gh/about-us/>) to create and maintain the national identity register.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Regulations made under the 2008 Act, [gazetted on 27 March 2026 and in force from 9 June 2026](<https://www.mint.gov.gh/government-strengthens-identification-verification-system/>), require organisations to verify identity through the Authority's biometric verification system, [in real time against its database](<https://www.facebook.com/officialNIAGH/posts/press-releasegovernment-makes-biometric-verification-mandatory-for-ghana-card-ve/1522136326623174/>). An earlier notice under the same laws, [of 15 February 2024](<https://nia.gov.gh/legal-and-regulations/>), specifies the portable card readers that may be used. The 2026 regulations amend the register regulations of 2012.
