# Lesotho

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Lesotho's National Identity Cards Act, 2011 was [published in the Government Gazette on 21 March 2011 and commenced on 1 July 2013](<https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21>). It repealed the National Identity Cards Act, 2004. It is an Act of Parliament and has been the law for the identity register and the card since its commencement.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [establishes a National Identity Register that records fingerprints and an image](<https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21>) for everyone aged sixteen and over. It assigns each entry a unique national identity number and provides for an identity card with a chip that holds the fingerprints. The register, the number and the chip card are all set out in the Act itself. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [National Identity Register](<https://lesotholii.org/akn/ls/act/2011/9/eng@2011-03-21>); [SIM card registration](<https://lca.org.ls/final-call-for-sim-registration/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
