# Botswana

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Botswana's National Registration Act [commenced on 31 December 1986](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/principle-legislation/national-registration>) and was amended up to 2017. It has the Registrar of National Registration keep a register of citizens aged 16 or over and issue identity cards. [The National Registration (Amendment) Act 2025 was assented on 23 March 2025](<https://www.botswanalaws.com/PDF/StatutesActpdf/2025Actpdf/007.pdf>), to start on a date the minister sets by order. In March 2026 the minister [said it now lets passports and driving licences serve as identification](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>) while identity cards are replaced. In July 2026 he [told Parliament what else it changes](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Under the 2025 amendment [the registrar assigns an identity number to each person in a Unified Population Register](<https://www.botswanalaws.com/PDF/StatutesActpdf/2025Actpdf/007.pdf>). The number carries no gender marker, and the identity card is valid for ten years. The register covers citizens, dual citizens and non-citizen residents.

***Also on file***. [National register and district registers of citizens (identity cards)](<https://botswanalaws.com/consolidated-statutes/principle-legislation/national-registration>); [Omang (National Identity Card)](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/92439>); [Electronic Identification System / Digital ID project](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>); [Biometric Omang system](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/90334>).
