# Guinea

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Guinea's Law L/2023/019/CNT of 25 October 2023 on the identification of physical persons [is the law under which a decree of April 2025 is made](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>). On 26 February 2026 the head of the identification office [named it at a workshop](<https://matd.gov.gn/loneci-vulgarise-les-nouvelles-lois-sur-letat-civil-et-de-lidentification/>) where officials said the country now has two new laws, on identification and on civil status. The minister's declaration of 10 August 2026 [lists their adoption and dissemination](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre>) among the year's achievements.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Decree D/2025/052 of 14 April 2025, [in effect from its signature, fixes how people are entered in and kept on the national register of natural persons](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>), in application of the 2023 law. The decree [sets up a register of biographic and biometric data with a personal identification number](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>), and a jurist writes that under the law [fingerprints, face and iris may be taken from the age of ten](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/04/en-avant-pour-le-recensement-biometrique-par-sayon-mara/>). A workshop at Kindia in January 2026 [reviewed and validated further draft decrees and orders](<https://ravec.gov.gn/ravec-kindia-atelier-de-relecture-et-validation-des-textes-dapplication-portant-etat-civil-et-identification-des-personnes-physiques/>) applying the identification and civil status laws.
