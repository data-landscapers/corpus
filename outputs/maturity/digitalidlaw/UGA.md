# Uganda

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Uganda's Registration of Persons Act, 2015 [was assented to on 26 March 2015](<https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2015/4/eng@2015-03-26>). It sets up the National Identification and Registration Authority, the national identification register and national identification cards. A Parliament release of 20 August 2026 [describes the Authority as working under the Act](<https://www.parliament.go.ug/news/4571/new-id-system-faces-questions-over-digital-verification>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [has the Authority assign a unique national identification number to every person registered](<https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2015/4/eng@2015-03-26>) and defines identity data to include fingerprints. Regulations [made under it on 12 August 2024](<https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/si/2024/67/eng@2024-08-12/source>) set the card's validity at eleven years. In June 2026 the Authority said registered foreigners [are given a unique identification number under the Act](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-kicks-off-first-registration-of-foreigne-NV_234932_062026>). The 2024 regulations amend the Registration of Persons Regulations, 2015, and are deemed in force from 20 November 2015.
