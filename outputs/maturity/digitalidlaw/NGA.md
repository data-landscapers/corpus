# Nigeria

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Nigeria's National Identity Management Commission Act 2026 was [signed into law on 26 June 2026](<https://businessandtransport.com/how-nimc-emerges-nigerias-national-trust-authority-under-new-act-2026/>), repealing the 2007 Act. The bill had [passed second reading in the Senate in July 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202407/nigeria-undertakes-reforms-to-strengthen-national-digital-id-legal-framework>). A civil society speaker said on 29 July 2026 that [the gazetted text was not yet available](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/nimc-act-2026-experts-demand-transparency-stronger-data-protection/>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [reaffirms the national identification number as the foundational identity and makes the Commission responsible for verifying electronic identities](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/13/nigerias-identity-law-raises-questions-over-digital-signatures/>). It [makes the Commission the national repository for biometric data](<https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/constitutional-administrative-law/1829270/the-national-identity-management-commission-establishment-act-2026-key-new-provisions>) and, by one account, [makes the number compulsory across fourteen sectors](<https://punchng.com/pros-cons-of-amended-nimc-act/>). The Commission described its work in September 2026 as [in compliance with the Act](<https://nigeriacommunicationsweek.com.ng/2026/09/23/nimc-unveils-consent-based-nin-verification-to-authenticate-identity>). The Act was three months old at the end of September 2026, and no regulation made under it is on record.
