# Mauritania

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Mauritania's identity system rests on its Civil Status Code, law no. 2011-003 of 12 January 2011, which covers the national population register and secure identity documents. Law no. 2025-011 of 24 February 2025 [replaced three of its articles](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>) after adoption by the National Assembly and promulgation by the President.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The code as amended [provides for the national biometric population register](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>) in its new article 31. Its new article 15 provides for applications for secure identity documents, and for digital civil status acts, through information systems the administration adopts. The 2025 amendment is thus the text that writes the biometric register and digital acts into the code. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Registre National Biométrique des Populations](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117696/MRT-117696.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
