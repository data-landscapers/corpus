# Zambia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Zambia's National Registration Act is in force. The National Registration Regulations, 2019 [were made under its section 14](<https://www.zambialaws.com/Zambia2019Pdfsi/SI%2034%20of%202019.pdf>); the Act provides for the national registration card, with a Chief Registrar and registrars. The regulations are Statutory Instrument No. 34 of 2019.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The 2019 regulations [prescribe the registration procedure](<https://www.zambialaws.com/Zambia2019Pdfsi/SI%2034%20of%202019.pdf>): an application, a photograph, the capture of ten rolled and ten plain fingerprints and a birth certificate, and the forms of the card with its number. They are the newest rules under the Act on record, and no source found says the Act provides for a register held digitally or an identity checked electronically. The Act is Cap. 126 of the laws of Zambia, and no amendment to it since 2019 is on record.

***Also on file***. [National Registration Card (NRC)](<https://www.zambialaws.com/Zambia2019Pdfsi/SI%2034%20of%202019.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found says the law in force provides for a biometric register, an identity number unique to each person, or an identity that can be checked electronically.
