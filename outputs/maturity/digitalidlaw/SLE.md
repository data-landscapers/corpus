# Sierra Leone

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone's National Civil Registration Authority [held the final national validation of the draft National Civil Registration and Identification Bill](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/ncra-concludes-final-national-validation-of-draft-civil-registration-and-identification-bill/>) and its regulations on 17 November 2025. The bill has still to go to Cabinet and then to Parliament. It is the draft that would replace the present registration law.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** No source found says a national identity law is in force, so nothing is counted beyond the bill. The validation of November 2025 [covered the draft regulations together with the bill](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/ncra-concludes-final-national-validation-of-draft-civil-registration-and-identification-bill/>), which means the rules are being written with the law and not after it. What the bill provides for the identity itself is not stated in the report. The validation was the last step before the bill leaves the Authority.
