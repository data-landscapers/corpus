# South Sudan

## National identity law

**Law in force.** South Sudan's Civil Registry Act of 2018, with the Nationality Act of 2011, is [the legal foundation of its identification system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>), a World Bank diagnostic of May 2026 says. The Bank says the Act and regulations made under it in 2024 [have established the legal foundation for the country's identity system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/07/22/in-the-worlds-youngest-nation-how-do-you-prove-you-exist>), and calls the framework under-implemented.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Civil Registration and National Identity Regulations, 2024, [were made under sections 33 and 39 of the Act](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>) and regulate the issue of legal documents. The Bank says the Act and the regulations [lay the foundation for an interoperable digital identity system based on a personal identification number](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf>). Both are in force on paper, by the Bank's account.
