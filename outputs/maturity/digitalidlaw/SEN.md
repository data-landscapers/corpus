# Senegal

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Senegal's identity card rests on [a 2016 law instituting the ECOWAS biometric identity card](<https://senegaldirect.com/cartes-didentite-mouhamadou-moukhtar-cisse-explique-la-prorogation-exceptionnelle/>). On 29 April 2026 the Council of Ministers adopted a bill to amend it and extend how long cards are valid, and on 29 September 2026 that bill, numbered 21/2026, [was before the National Assembly](<https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The 2016 law is [a law for a biometric identity card](<https://senegaldirect.com/cartes-didentite-mouhamadou-moukhtar-cisse-explique-la-prorogation-exceptionnelle/>), which is how the reports of the amending bill name it. The bill concerns the card's period of validity; an Interior Ministry official [said on 24 September 2026 that the amending text would extend the 2016 cards](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Societe/cartes-didentite-biometriques-de-2016-vers-une-prorogation-de-la-validite_n_505118.html>). It had not been passed by the end of September. The law in force is the one of 2016, unamended at that date.

***Also on file***. [ECOWAS biometric identity card](<https://www.vipeoples.net/Passeports-et-cartes-d-identite-Vers-la-fin-du-contrat-avec-l-operateur-malaisien-et-le-recours-a-l-expertise-nationale_a184204.html>); [NEKKAL programme](<https://aps.sn/le-programme-nekkal-a-permis-dimportantes-avancees-dans-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-selon-un-responsable/>); [Registre national de l'Etat civil (RNEC)](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf>); [DAF (Identité) biometric identity system](<https://www.child-identity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PRESENTATIONETATSENEGAL-Aliou-Sall.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
