# Congo

## National identity law

**Law in force.** The Republic of the Congo's unique identification number was [instituted by decree no. 2004-469 of 3 November 2004](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>), and no law is named behind it. The national identity card rests on a 2009 decree, which [decree no. 2024-2692 of 13 November 2024 amended](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). Both instruments are decrees.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** No law is in force, so the rules that follow are made under decrees. An order of 12 March 2020 [added the capture of iris and fingerprints](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>) to the identification number, and an order of 12 October 2022 [made the number mandatory](<https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/articles/relance-des-operations-de-production-de-la-carte-niu>) for eight economic transactions and five administrative formalities. The 2024 decree [provides a card holding both thumbprints](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). No bill for an identity law is on record.
