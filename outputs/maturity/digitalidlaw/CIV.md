# Cote d'Ivoire

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Côte d'Ivoire's biometric identity project is [backed by the Law on Digital Identification, no. 2019-566](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>). It is an enacted law of five articles. A 2025 study of ten African identity systems records the law alongside an executive decree and the 2013 data protection law. It treats the three together as the legal basis of the system.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The same study says the 2019 law [establishes the legal basis for the biometric digital identity](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>). It says the law's second article describes the identity document as a secure card serving several applications. The study names it as the one law in the country's framework written for the digital identity itself. The law is short, with five articles in all. No regulation or decree made under it since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP) / Numéro national d'identification (NNI)](<https://digitalmag.ci/identite-numerique-la-cote-divoire-pays-connecte-mais-pas-encore-pleinement-identifie/>); [MaPoste](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1768657500>); [Biometric digital-ID (electronic smart card)](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law has been found from the last three years. Nor has any rule made under it that others must check identity against the system.
