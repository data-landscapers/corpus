# Cote d'Ivoire

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Côte d'Ivoire's biometric identity project is [backed by the Law on Digital Identification, no. 2019-566](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>), an enacted law of five articles. A 2025 study of ten African identity systems records the law with an executive decree and the 2013 data protection law alongside it, and treats the three together as the legal basis of the system.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The same study says the 2019 law [establishes the legal basis for the biometric digital identity](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>) and that its second article describes the identity document as a secure card serving several applications. It is the one law in the country's framework that the study names as written for the digital identity itself, and it is short: five articles in all. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Registre national des personnes physiques (RNPP) / Numéro national d'identification (NNI)](<https://digitalmag.ci/identite-numerique-la-cote-divoire-pays-connecte-mais-pas-encore-pleinement-identifie/>); [MaPoste](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1768657500>); [Biometric digital-ID (electronic smart card)](<https://www.ids.ac.uk/publications/biometric-digital-id-in-africa/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
