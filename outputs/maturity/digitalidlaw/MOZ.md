# Mozambique

## National identity law

**Law in force.** On [16 March 2022 the Assembly of the Republic passed a bill in first reading](<https://www.parlamento.mz/?p=1695>), by consensus. It sets the legal regime for civil identification and the citizen identity card, the Bilhete de Identidade. It was meant to [replace colonial-era legislation](<https://www.parlamento.mz/?p=1695>). In May 2025 the ICT regulator [listed Law 6/2022 on civil identification and the identity card](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS5A-S1-4-Mozambique_FINAL.pdf>) among the laws in force.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The biometric [Bilhete de Identidade dates from Decree 11/2008 of 29 April](<https://www.parlamento.mz/?p=1695>). The Interior Ministry's civil identification directorate runs an [electronic civil identity system, described in March 2023](<https://www.intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/DNIC-SISTEMA-DE-IDENTIFICACAO-CIVIL.pdf>). In May 2026 a [tender was launched for a consultancy on mobile digital identity and signatures](<https://clubofmozambique.com/news/mozambique-launches-tender-for-consultancy-on-mobile-digital-identity-and-signature-system/>). The government [was seeking a partner for the rollout](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mozambique-seeks-partner-for-mobile-digital-id-e-signature-rollout>).
