# Namibia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Namibia's Civil Registration and Identification Act, No. 13 of 2024, is [the statute for civil registration and legal identity](<https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2024/13/eng@2024-12-30>). It sets up a civil register that includes an identity register under a Registrar-General. In July 2026 officials said the electronic identity card [is legally recognised under the Act](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [assigns each person a unique identity number, authorises physical or electronic identity documents and governs how register data is authenticated and verified](<https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2024/13/eng@2024-12-30>). In July 2026 an official of the Office of the Prime Minister said it [allows institutions to accept either the physical card or its digital version](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>), which the ministry said can be verified electronically. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [Electronic identity (E-ID) card and national identity management system](<https://informante.web.na/?p=398234>); [National Civil Register (Birth, Marriage, Death and Identity Registers)](<https://namiblii.org/akn/na/act/2024/13/eng@2024-12-30>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
