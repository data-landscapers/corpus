# Gambia

## National identity law

**Law in force.** [Section 17 of the Nationality and Citizenship Act](<https://jollofnews.gm/barrows-call-for-16-year-olds-to-register-for-national-id-sparks-legal-questions/>) gives Gambian citizens aged 18 or over the right to a national identity card. Jollof News reported this in June 2026. No separate national identity act has been found. The National Identification Authority runs [the National Identity Management System](<https://jollofnews.gm/barrows-call-for-16-year-olds-to-register-for-national-id-sparks-legal-questions/>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** No source found says the law sets up a biometric register. Nor does any say it gives a unique identity number or an identity checked electronically. The nearest held fact is [the June 2026 report](<https://jollofnews.gm/barrows-call-for-16-year-olds-to-register-for-national-id-sparks-legal-questions/>) that the law sets 18 as the age for an identity card. A [National Digital ID and Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2028](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/validation-of-the-gambia's-national-digital-id-and-digital-transformation-strategy>) was validated in 2024. It is a strategy, not a law.

***Also on file***. [National identity cards](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/udp-raises-alarm-over-govt-unlawful-issuance-of-id-cards-abroad>). [National Digital ID and Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2028](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/validation-of-the-gambia's-national-digital-id-and-digital-transformation-strategy>). The [National Identity Management System](<https://jollofnews.gm/barrows-call-for-16-year-olds-to-register-for-national-id-sparks-legal-questions/>), run by the National Identification Authority.

***Not found***. No source found says the law provides for a digital identity. No rule made under it has been found.
