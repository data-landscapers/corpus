# Benin

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Benin's law no. 2017-08 of 19 June 2017 on the identification of natural persons is the law behind the national register and the personal identification number. In June 2025 the National Assembly [adopted law no. 2025-12 amending its article 6](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/assemblee-nationale-larticle-6-de-la-loi-sur-lidentification-des-personnes-physiques-retouche>), extending who the law covers, and the 2017 law stands as amended.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** Article 6 as amended [provides for a national biometric population register and a personal identification number](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/assemblee-nationale-larticle-6-de-la-loi-sur-lidentification-des-personnes-physiques-retouche>) for each person entered in it. The 2025 amendment adds special numbers for foreigners and for naturalised persons of African descent, so the register and the number now reach beyond citizens. The number is the one each person keeps for life in the register. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.
