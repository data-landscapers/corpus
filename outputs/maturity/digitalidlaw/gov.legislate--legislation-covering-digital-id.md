# Legislation covering digital id: national identity law

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Union's Digital Transformation Strategy sets a target of digital legal identity for 99.9% of people by 2030, and its Interoperability Framework for Digital ID (§4.1) asks for a harmonised enabling legal framework. Article 14 of the AfCFTA Digital Trade Protocol commits each party to adopt or maintain digital identity systems. This study asks whether a law exists, is in force, provides for a digital identity and has had rules made under it; it does not measure the law against any of the three.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A dated source says the country has no national identity law and no bill for one |
| 2 Preparing | Yes to 1: a bill, or a system run on a decree alone, and no law in force |
| 3 Establishing | Yes to 1 and 2: a law in force |
| 4 Operating | Yes to 1, 2 and 3: the law in force provides for a digital identity |
| 5 Leading | Yes to all four: rules have been made under that law in the last three years |

## Countries

### Ghana: 5 Leading

Ghana's identity Acts of 2006 and 2008 are in force; 2026 regulations require biometric verification against the national register.

- The Interior Minister says L.I. 2523, signed 2 March 2026, gazetted 27 March 2026 and effective 9 June 2026, requires organisations offering services under Regulation 7 of L.I. 2111 to verify identity through the NIA biometric verification system or approved match-on-card devices ([source, 2026-03-27](https://www.mint.gov.gh/government-strengthens-identification-verification-system/)).
- The NIA says L.I. 2523 was signed and gazetted on 27 March 2026 under Section 73(1) of Act 750 and came into effect on 9 June 2026, requiring institutions that verify identity to use real-time biometric verification against the NIA database via the IVSP ([source, 2026-03-27](https://www.facebook.com/officialNIAGH/posts/press-releasegovernment-makes-biometric-verification-mandatory-for-ghana-card-ve/1522136326623174/)).
- L.I. 2523, made under the National Identity Register Act, 2008 (Act 750), requires identity to be verified biometrically and in real time against the National Identification Authority's database, so the law in force provides for an identity that can be checked electronically ([source, 2026-03-27](https://www.mint.gov.gh/government-strengthens-identification-verification-system/)).
- The Interior Ministry says the National Identity Register Regulations (L.I. 2111) are amended by L.I. 2523, a legislative instrument (regulations) made under the identity register law, in effect from 9 June 2026 ([source, 2026-06-09](https://www.mint.gov.gh/government-strengthens-identification-verification-system/)).

### Guinea: 5 Leading

Guinea's 2023 identification law is in force, and an April 2025 decree applying it set up a biometric register with an identification number.

- Decree D/2025/052/PRG/CNRD/SGG, signed 14 April 2025 and effective on signature, fixes the rules for entering persons in and keeping the national register of natural persons, in application of Law L/2023/019/CNT ([source, 2025-04-14](https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg)).
- Law L/2023/019/CNT of 25 October 2023 on the identification of natural persons is the law under which decree D/2025/052 is made, and the decree sets up a register of biometric and biographic data with a personal identification number ([source, 2023-10-25](https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg)).
- The territorial administration minister's declaration of 10 August 2026 lists among achievements the adoption and dissemination of the laws on the identification of physical persons and on civil status; no number, promulgation date or instrument kind is given, and the date is that of the statement ([source, 2026-08-10](https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre)).

### Mauritius: 5 Leading

Mauritius's 1985 identity card law is in force; 2024 regulations under it set a new card with a digital seal and fingerprints.

- The National Identity Card Regulations 2024 (Government Notice No. 29 of 2024), made by the Minister on 16 February 2024 under section 10 of the National Identity Card Act, set the new identity card, its digital seal and fingerprint-based application, and revoke the National Identity Card Regulations 2013 ([source, 2024-02-16](https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf)).
- The National Identity Card Regulations 2024, made under section 10 of the National Identity Card Act, set an identity card with a digital seal applied for with fingerprints, so the Act in force provides for a biometric, electronically checkable identity ([source, 2024-02-16](https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf)).
- The Mauritius National Identity Card Unit's page lists the National Identity Card Act, No. 60 of 1985 (an Act of Parliament) as the law behind the identity card system, with the new MNIC 3.0 card operational since 19 February 2024; the page is dated 2024-02-19 and gives no other date for the state of the law ([source, 2024-02-19](https://mnis.govmu.org/)).

### Somalia: 5 Leading

Somalia's 2023 identification law is in force; a 2024 regulation applies it and sets rules for banks and phone companies checking identity.

- Implementing Regulation Lr. 158 of 8 June 2024, made under Law No. 009 of 2023, applies the law and sets rules for relying parties (banks, phone companies and public bodies) on certification, consent, and acceptance of other proof of identity while the system is not fully rolled out ([source, 2024-06-08](https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/cabinet-decisions/2024/Xeer-nidaamiyaha_Sharciga_Aqoonsiga_Diiwaan-galinta_Dadwaynaha.pdf)).
- The regulation defines the Digital National Identification System as the system established by the Public Identification and Registration Law and provides for biometric data (10 fingerprints, face or iris), an 11-digit identity number unique to each person for life, and electronic verification of the card by relying parties ([source, 2024-06-08](https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/cabinet-decisions/2024/Xeer-nidaamiyaha_Sharciga_Aqoonsiga_Diiwaan-galinta_Dadwaynaha.pdf)).
- The Prime Minister signed Implementing Regulation (Xeer Ra'iisal Wasaare Lr. 158) on 8 June 2024, after Council of Ministers decision SHGW.19.76.06.24 of 6 June 2024, taking effect on signature and made under the Public Identification and Registration Law, Law No. 009 of 20 March 2023, which shows that law is in force ([source, 2024-06-08](https://www.osagsomalia.com/media/documents/bulletins-contents/cabinet-decisions/2024/Xeer-nidaamiyaha_Sharciga_Aqoonsiga_Diiwaan-galinta_Dadwaynaha.pdf)).

### South Sudan: 5 Leading

South Sudan's 2018 Civil Registry Act and 2024 regulations provide for a personal identification number; the World Bank calls them under-implemented.

- The Civil Registration and National Identity Regulations, 2024, made under sections 33 and 39 of the Civil Registry Act, regulate the issuance of legal documents and the registers, set the national identity cards (NIC, Residence Permit Card, Refugee Identity Card), PIN use and data protection measures; the document gives the year only ([source, 2024](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf)).
- The World Bank diagnostic says the Civil Registry Act (2018) and the 2024 Regulations under it lay the foundation for an interoperable digital ID ecosystem based on a Personal Identification Number tied to biometrically deduplicated IDs, with national identity cards, and a civil registry as national data source; implementation is extremely limited. Published date used, no date of the statement being given ([source, 2026-05](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf)).
- The World Bank diagnostic says South Sudan's Civil Registry Act (2018), with the Nationality Act (2011), is the legal foundation of its identification system, and that the Civil Registration and National Identity Regulations (2024), drafted under the Act's sections 33 and 39, build on it; it calls the framework under-implemented, not absent ([source, 2026-05](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099053126171570627/pdf/P502463-0c06abde-be3d-4f83-b9e1-eca068e7bc1e.pdf)).

### Algeria: 4 Operating

- By its title Loi n° 26-02 of 17 February 2026 sets the rules for electronic identification, and the stories on its draft say it introduces a national digital identity ([source, 2026-02-17](https://www.mpt.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Loi-n%C2%B0-26-02.Services-de-Confiance.Identification-electronique.FR_.pdf)).
- The Ministry of the Interior says executive decree 10-210 of 16 September 2010 instituted the national identification number (NIN) and requires it on official identity, circulation and travel documents ([source, 2010-09-16](https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf)).
- Loi n° 26-02 of 17 February 2026 fixes the general rules on trust services for electronic transactions and on electronic identification; it is a law, published by the ministry of post and telecommunications ([source, 2026-02-17](https://www.mpt.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Loi-n%C2%B0-26-02.Services-de-Confiance.Identification-electronique.FR_.pdf)).

### Angola: 4 Operating

Angola's identity law, amended in 2017 and 2021, gives each citizen a unique number on a chip card. No rules since 2023 are known.

- The amended Lei n.º 4/09 (article 9) gives each identity card a number that acts in law as the Número Único do Cidadão (NUC), an identity number unique to each citizen, and the card carries an encrypted QR code in the photo band ([source, 2021-01-25](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2021/lei-n-o-3-21-de-25-de-janeiro/)).
- The amended article 8 of Lei n.º 4/09 has the identity card carry a data-storage microprocessor holding the visible identifying data and non-visible items such as tax, social security, birth-record and voter numbers, so that the card can interact with public and private institutions ([source, 2017-08-31](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2017/lei-n-o-20-17-de-31-de-agosto/)).
- Lei n.º 3/21, promulgated on 12 January 2021 and in force on publication, amends Lei n.º 4/09 on the legal regime of civil identification and issue of the national citizen identity card (an act of the National Assembly) ([source, 2021-01-25](https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2021/lei-n-o-3-21-de-25-de-janeiro/)).

### Benin: 4 Operating

Benin's 2017 identification law, amended in 2025, sets up a biometric register and a personal number. No rules made under it since 2023 are known.

### Cameroon: 4 Operating

Cameroon's 2024 civil registration law provides a unique personal identification number for every citizen. No rules made under it since 2023 are known.

### Cape Verde: 4 Operating

Cabo Verde's 2013 law and 2014 decree-law set up an electronic identity card; the last rules known under them date from 2017.

### Chad: 4 Operating

### Cote d'Ivoire: 4 Operating

### Djibouti: 4 Operating

Djibouti's 2019 identification law sets up a unique number and biometric register; the last rules known under it date from 2020.

### Ethiopia: 4 Operating

### Lesotho: 4 Operating

Lesotho's 2011 identity cards law, in force since 2013, sets up a biometric register and unique number. No rules since 2023 are known.

### Liberia: 4 Operating

Liberia's 2011 National Identification Registry Act sets up a biometric identification system. No rules made under it since 2023 are known.

- The Act's own text sets up a National Biometric Identification System and a biometric card for each person (fingerprint and photograph enrolled, data encrypted) carrying a unique Social Security Number as the primary government identity number, with enrolment mandatory for citizens ([source, 2011-08-08](https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Liberia-NIR-Act-2011.pdf)).

### Mali: 4 Operating
