# Algeria

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Algeria's Council of Ministers [approved a draft law on trust services and digital identification](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/0511-50150-algeria-updates-digital-services-and-online-identity-law>) on 2 November 2025. It became [law no. 26-02 of 17 February 2026](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Loi-n%C2%B0-26-02.Services-de-Confiance.Identification-electronique.FR_.pdf>), which fixes the general rules on trust services for electronic transactions and on electronic identification and is published by the ministry of post and telecommunications. The Ministry of the Interior [lists law 14-03 of 24 February 2014 on travel titles and documents](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>) among the texts governing issuance of passports and identity cards.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The law [sets the rules for electronic identification](<https://www.mpt.gov.dz/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Loi-n%C2%B0-26-02.Services-de-Confiance.Identification-electronique.FR_.pdf>). Reports on its draft say it [introduces a national digital identification framework](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/algeria-approves-law-to-strengthen-digital-trust-and-e-transactions>) built on the existing biometric identity card and [updates the 2015 electronic signature law](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202511/algeria-approves-draft-legislation-on-digital-id-trust-services>). The identity it provides for is therefore one that is checked electronically, tied to the card citizens already hold. The Ministry of the Interior says [executive decree 10-210 of 16 September 2010 instituted the national identification number](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>) and requires it on official identity, circulation and travel documents.
