# South Africa

## National identity law

**Law in force.** South Africa's Identification Act, 1997 is in force: the Minister of Home Affairs [made the fourteenth amendment of the regulations under it on 23 June 2025](<https://www.dha.gov.za/images/gazettes/gazette-52893-230625-dha.pdf>), in operation from 1 July 2025, and the Department of Transport [states that it makes Home Affairs the sole authority maintaining the National Population Register and issuing identification documents](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/government-amend-regulations-prevent-traffic-documents-being-used-identification>). Under section 15, [anyone aged 16 or over must apply for an identity card](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33815/>), the Minister replied in November 2025. Its replacement, the National Identification and Registration Bill, [is due in Parliament only in 2027 or 2028](<https://techcentral.co.za/south-africa-behind-neighbours-digital-id/286057/>), a September 2026 report says. Home Affairs' first-quarter report to Parliament says it [did not submit the bill for public comment](<https://mybroadband.co.za/news/government/669922-problem-with-plan-for-digital-ids-in-south-africa.html>), as it had to revise it in line with the Digital Identity Policy framework.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The 1997 Act is the law in force, and no source found says it provides for a digital identity. In May 2026 Home Affairs [gazetted draft amended regulations under the Act](<https://www.citizen.co.za/news/south-africa/smart-ids-go-digital-home-affairs-publishes-draft-rules/>) for comment until 6 June 2026, [which would regulate access to identity information](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/south-africa-gazettes-digital-id-draft-regulation-seeks-comments>). They were drafts at the end of September 2026. The Act of 1997 remains the law the population register and the identity document are kept under.
