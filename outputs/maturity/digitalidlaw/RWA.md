# Rwanda

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Rwanda has a law governing registration into its single digital identity system. In March 2026 The New Times reported that the law [sets a June 2027 deadline after which existing national identity cards are no longer valid](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>), and that under it [citizens cannot hold two government-issued identities](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/>). The law's number and date are not given.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The system the law governs is [built on a unique single digital identification number, a derived token and a card with a QR code](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>), with authentication that relies on biometric data. Enrolment under it [takes fingerprints, a facial image and an iris scan](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/>), carried out by the national identification agency. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [e-Indangamuntu (digital national ID)](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/>); [Single Digital ID (SDID)](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34398/news/technology/govt-sets-2027-deadline-for-full-shift-to-digital-id>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
