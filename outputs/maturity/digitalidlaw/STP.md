# Sao Tome and Principe

## National identity law

**Law in force.** São Tomé and Príncipe has a new identity law in draft. A World Bank mission of 8 to 12 September 2025 [flagged as urgent its review, public consultation, finalisation and approval](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>). The mission's note calls it the aggregated identification document law and treats it as drafted and not yet approved.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** No source found says a national identity law is in force. The mission [warned that without the law's approval the collection of data could be constrained](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025185177863/pdf/P177158-d0ebd79d-6646-4e92-b9ae-1183b632c15a.pdf>), which is the reason it gave for urgency. No later report says the law has been consulted on, finalised or approved, and what it provides for the identity itself is not stated. The mission's note is dated September 2025.
