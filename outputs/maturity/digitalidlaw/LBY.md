# Libya

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Libya's Law No. 8 of 2014 on the National Number was [issued by the General National Congress on 24 March 2014](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-8-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2014%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A..pdf>) and took effect on issue. It sets up the national number and a national database, run with the Civil Registry Authority. It is an ordinary law and no amendment to it is on record.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The law [provides for a twelve-digit national number unique to each citizen](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-8-%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9-2014%D9%85-%D8%A8%D8%B4%D8%A3%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A..pdf>) and a national database completed with ten fingerprints, an iris print, a photograph and a signature. The number and the biometric database are both in the law's own articles, the second and the third, and the database is the reference the number is checked against. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.
