# Mali

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Mali's law no. 06-040 of 11 August 2006 [institutes the national identification number and a national identification register](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2006/mali-jo-2006-28.pdf>). It was adopted by the National Assembly on 13 July 2006 and promulgated by the President. A decree of 3 November 2022 instituting the biometric national identity card [cites it as its basis](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2022/mali-jo-2022-35.pdf>).

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The law [gives each person a unique number held in a national register](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2006/mali-jo-2006-28.pdf>), to be shown on the identity card, the passport and civil status records. A decree of 18 October 2006 [fixes the fourteen-digit number and obliges public services to use it](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2006/mali-jo-2006-33.pdf>). The 2022 decree [requires the number for any biometric identity card application](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2022/mali-jo-2022-35.pdf>). Those rules all date from before September 2023, and none made under the law since then is on record.
