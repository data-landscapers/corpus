# Sudan

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Sudan's Civil Registry Act 2011 [was signed by the President, repealed the 2001 Act and came into force on signature](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Sudan-Civil-Registration-Act-2011-EN.pdf>). It sets up the civil registry, run by the Ministry of Interior, which issues the national number and the identity card. It is an act of the National Assembly, and no later amendment is on record.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act [defines the national number as a unique number given to each citizen on registration](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Sudan-Civil-Registration-Act-2011-EN.pdf>), never to be repeated. It provides for civil registry records to be stored electronically on computer and for a central database. The number and the electronic register are both in the Act's own text, which gives only the year 2011. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record.

***Also on file***. [SudaPass (Sudanese Digital Identity)](<https://sudapass.gov.sd/terms?lang=en>); [Civil Registry Information System / National Number](<https://citizenshiprightsafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Sudan-Civil-Registration-Act-2011-EN.pdf>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
