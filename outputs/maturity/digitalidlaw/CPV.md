# Cape Verde

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Cabo Verde's national civil identification and authentication system was [created by Lei n.º 43/VIII/2013 of 17 September 2013](<https://justica.gov.cv/sniac>). The national identification card was then [created by Decreto-Lei n.º 19/2014 of 17 March](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>), which also governs its issue, replacement, use and cancellation. The card was launched on 23 January 2018.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The card is [an electronic one whose chip holds a digital certificate](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>) and applications for identification, authentication and digital signature. Rules were made under the decree-law in 2017: [Portaria n.º 21/2017 of 15 June](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>) regulates the capture of biometric data and [Portaria n.º 35/2017 of 2 September](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>) sets how long the card is valid. Those rules all date from before September 2023, and none made under the law since then is on record.

***Also on file***. [Sistema Nacional de Identificação e Autenticação Civil (SNIAC)](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>); [Cartão Nacional de Identificação (CNI)](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>); [Chave Móvel Digital de Cabo Verde (CMDCV)](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>); [Autentika](<https://sniac.cv/cartao-nacional-de-identificacao/>); [SNIAC CNI middleware and SDK](<https://sniac.cv/mw/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
