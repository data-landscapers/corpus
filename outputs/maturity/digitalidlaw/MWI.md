# Malawi

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Malawi's National Registration Act of 2010 [mandates the registration bureau to run the national registration and identification system](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>), the Principal Secretary says. The Act is in force. On 23 July 2026 the Homeland Security Minister [made amendment regulations under it](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>). The registration bureau runs the national identity card under the Act. In July 2026 a stakeholders' meeting [was held to review the Act](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>) under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** A 2025 presentation on the national identity says [the National Registration Act No. 13 of 2010 was enacted as the legal foundation for the national digital identity system](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-3-Malawi_FINAL.pdf>). The registration bureau was set up under it. The regulations of 23 July 2026 [raised registration fees](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>). The July 2026 meeting [reviewed the registration laws](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>). No bill has been found since.

***Also on file***. [National ID (National Registration Bureau)](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>); [Digital national ID cards (mobile-accessible)](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>).

***Not found***. No regulation applying the Act, and no rule that others must check identity against it, has been found since September 2023. The 2026 regulations change fees only.
