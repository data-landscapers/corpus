# Malawi

## National identity law

**Law in force.** Malawi's National Registration Act of 2010 [mandates the registration bureau to run the national registration and identification system](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>), according to the Principal Secretary. The Act is in force: on 23 July 2026 the Homeland Security Minister [made amendment regulations under it](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>), and the registration bureau runs the national identity card under the Act. In July 2026 a stakeholders' meeting [was held to review the Act](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>) under the Digital Malawi Acceleration Project.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Act is being reviewed and has not been replaced. The meeting reported in July 2026 [brought stakeholders together as the bureau reviews the registration laws](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>), and no bill has followed on record. The regulations of 23 July 2026 [were made under the Act as it stands](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>), and what the Act provides for the identity itself is not stated in any report.

***Noted, not assessed***. [National ID (National Registration Bureau)](<https://malawi24.com/2026/08/17/nrb-hikes-registration-fees/>); [Digital national ID cards (mobile-accessible)](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2026/07/13/malawi-outlines-plans-to-transition-to-digital-ids/>).

***Not held***. No source held says the law in force provides for a biometric register, an identity number unique to each person, or an identity that can be checked electronically.
