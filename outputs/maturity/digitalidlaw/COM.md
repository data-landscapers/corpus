# Comoros

## National identity law

**Law in force.** The Comoros has a law instituting its identity card: law no. 14-023/AU of 24 June 2014, [promulgated by decree no. 14-137/PR](<https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n14-137-pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n14-023-au-instituant-la-carte-didentite-comorienne-numerique/>). The decree is held from the Ministry of Justice's site, which lists it as the instrument that brought the law into force. The law is an act of the Assembly of the Union.

**Digital identity covered and rules made.** The Ministry of Justice [describes the 2014 law as instituting the Comorian digital, biometric identity card](<https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n14-137-pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n14-023-au-instituant-la-carte-didentite-comorienne-numerique/>). That description, in the title under which the ministry publishes the promulgating decree, is what is held on the law's content: it is a law for a biometric card, and the ministry calls the card digital. No regulation or decree made under the law since September 2023 is on record. The card it institutes is the national identity card Comorians hold.

***Also on file***. [carte d'identité comorienne numérique (biométrique)](<https://justice.gouv.km/texte/decret-n14-137-pr-portant-promulgation-de-la-loi-n14-023-au-instituant-la-carte-didentite-comorienne-numerique/>).

***Not found***. No regulation or decree applying the law, and no rule made under it that others must check identity against the system, dated in the last three years, is held.
