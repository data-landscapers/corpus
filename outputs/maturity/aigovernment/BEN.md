# Benin

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The identification agency ANIP presented in February 2025 [Seyna, an AI chatbot in its mobile app](<https://srtb.bj/2025_02_17_anip_nouveaux_solutions_numeriques_faciliter_procedures_populations_prestations_ameliorees/>) that answers frequent questions and helps users make requests. It was [presented at a press conference with the agency's other new digital services](<https://srtb.bj/2025_02_17_anip_nouveaux_solutions_numeriques_faciliter_procedures_populations_prestations_ameliorees/>), among them online identification and civil status certificates.

**Several bodies.** A second body runs its own AI assistant. [GPT BJ, the government's virtual assistant launched in 2023](<https://lamarinabj.com/index.php/2024/06/25/benin-une-expansion-en-vue-pour-lassistant-virtuel-gpt-bj/>), answers citizens' questions on the tax, digital, labour and penal codes; in June 2024 the digital agency ASIN was preparing to expand it with national budget funding.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is ASIN's [legal note on generative AI for public servants](<https://asin.bj/doc/137/download>), which is advisory.

***Also on file***. [Billet juridique (ASIN, advisory)](<https://asin.bj/doc/137/download>).
