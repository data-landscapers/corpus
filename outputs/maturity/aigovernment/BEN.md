# Benin

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In February 2025 the identification agency ANIP presented [Seyna, an AI chatbot in its mobile app](<https://srtb.bj/2025_02_17_anip_nouveaux_solutions_numeriques_faciliter_procedures_populations_prestations_ameliorees/>). It answers frequent questions and helps users make requests. It came [with the agency's other new digital services](<https://srtb.bj/2025_02_17_anip_nouveaux_solutions_numeriques_faciliter_procedures_populations_prestations_ameliorees/>), among them online civil status certificates. ANIP also [uses AI to detect suspicious activity and fake documents](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-3-Benin-final.pdf>), its 2025 presentation says.

**Several bodies.** A second body runs its own AI assistant. [GPT BJ, the government's virtual assistant launched in 2023](<https://lamarinabj.com/index.php/2024/06/25/benin-une-expansion-en-vue-pour-lassistant-virtuel-gpt-bj/>), answers citizens' questions on the tax, digital, labour and penal codes. In June 2024 the digital agency ASIN was preparing to expand it with national budget funding. [In November 2026 ASIN put out a new version of the service-public.bj portal with Yemi, an AI assistant that guides users through their formalities](<https://lanouvelletribune.info/2026/11/benin-le-portail-des-services-publics-fait-peau-neuve-avec-de-nouvelles-fonctionnalites/>). [The portal carries more than 131 public services](<https://matinlibre.com/2026/11/19/benin-budget-2027-1159-milliards-pour-un-benin-numerique-utile-a-tous/>).

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest fact found is ASIN's [legal note on generative AI for public servants](<https://asin.bj/doc/137/download>), which is advisory.
