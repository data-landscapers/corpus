# Malawi

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [UniPod Malawi said in 2026 that government ministries had submitted 18 AI use cases](<https://unipod.ac.mw/blog/applications-now-open-for-the-ai-for-public-good-challenge>) to its AI for Public Good Challenge. A panel shortlisted five and published them as challenges. Graduate teams are to build and test solutions with ministry staff and mentors.

**Several bodies.** No government body has been found with AI in service. The nearest fact is the [five ministry use cases published as challenges](<https://unipod.ac.mw/blog/applications-now-open-for-the-ai-for-public-good-challenge>), which teams had still to build.

**Rules or register.** No rules or register for the government's own use of AI have been found. The nearest fact: [the challenge asks teams to consider privacy, ethics and responsible AI](<https://unipod.ac.mw/blog/applications-now-open-for-the-ai-for-public-good-challenge>).
