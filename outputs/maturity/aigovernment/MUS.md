# Mauritius

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation runs a government chatbot and in December 2025 was [upgrading it into DIVA](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552290>), L'Express reported. The Pay Research Bureau also [refers to the existing Government Chatbot](<https://prb2026.govmu.org/downloads/volume1/digital.pdf>) to be revamped into DIVA. The minister [presented DIVA as a government-led AI platform for public services](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/national-ai-strategy-and-fair-guidelines-unveiled-to-position-mauritius-as-a-high-tech-innovation-driven-economy/>) on 9 April 2026.

**Several bodies.** Cabinet [approved DIVA as the official chatbot for all ministries and departments](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552290>), to roll out in phases starting with 15 of them. Elsewhere AI is at trial: the Bank of Mauritius [has explored AI for document processing and forecasting](<https://defimedia.info/blog-artificial-intelligence-mauritius-progress-regulation-and-road-ahead>), and an AI tutor built with the Ministry of Education [completed pilots in ten schools](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>).

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [Cabinet decision on DIVA](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552290>), which sets its roll-out and no rules.
