# Sierra Leone

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** EdTech Hub reported in July 2026 that the education ministry's delivery team [uses an internal WhatsApp chatbot built on GPT-4 most days](<https://edtechhub.org/case-study/the-graduate-who-brought-ai-to-sierra-leones-ministry-of-education/>). The eight-person team uses it to pull teacher and pupil numbers and exam results for reports. The Ministry of Basic and Senior Secondary Education [had developed EDITH, an AI system for officials' information management, by March 2026](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>). It was moving the system out of pilot.

**Several bodies.** Only the education ministry is found with AI in service. The nearest found fact is [EdTech Hub's account](<https://edtechhub.org/case-study/the-graduate-who-brought-ai-to-sierra-leones-ministry-of-education/>). Both tools serve the education ministries' own staff.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest found fact is [EDITH's development](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/mbsse-develops-revolutionary-ai-platform-to-transform-education-management/>), which states no rules.
