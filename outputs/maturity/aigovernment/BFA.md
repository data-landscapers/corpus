# Burkina Faso

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [On 21 October 2025 the Ministry of Digital Transition and public broadcaster RTB signed an agreement to use AI to make public information accessible](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/>). [A live demonstration showed a Moore-French translation tool](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/>). [In March 2025 Digital Transition Minister Aminata Zerbo/Sabane told parliament her department was finalising three major projects](<https://an.bf/127>). [They are the Djam platform, a chatbot on electronic communications regulation and a chatbot on Burkina Faso's legislative texts](<https://an.bf/127>).
