# Burkina Faso

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In April 2026 Digital Magazine Burkina, citing Lefaso.net, reported that the government uses CITADEL's French-Mooré speech translation to broadcast information in Mooré on Burkina-en-marche.gov.bf](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/04/06/intelligence-artificielle-le-centre-dexcellence-interdisciplinaire-en-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpement-citadel-au-cours-du-loreslm-2026-lia-burkinabe-parle-desorma/>). [On 28 October 2025 the government's secretary general presented LegiChat, a platform that eases access to legislative and regulatory texts, TIC Magazine BF reported](<https://ticmagazine.bf/intelligence-artificielle-burkina-faso-2026/>). [On 21 October 2025 the Ministry of Digital Transition and public broadcaster RTB signed an agreement to use AI to make public information accessible](<https://news.broadcastmediaafrica.com/2025/10/23/burkina-faso-pioneers-ai-integration-in-public-broadcasting-with-rtb-partnership/>). [In March 2025 Digital Transition Minister Aminata Zerbo/Sabane told parliament her department was finalising the Djam platform and two regulatory chatbots](<https://an.bf/127>).
