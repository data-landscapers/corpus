# Tanzania

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The e-Government Authority says its [Msaada AI system is in use on the Tanzania Tourist Board's website and in the e-Mrejesho feedback system](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>) of the President's Office Public Service Management. It was designed at the authority's own research centre, beside two further tools, Muhtasari AI and Tafsiri AI. The authority says it [keeps improving Msaada AI from users' feedback](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Several bodies.** [The e-Government Authority says Msaada AI is used by two institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>), as of July 2026. [The World Bank says NeST, the procurement regulator's e-procurement system, is powered by AI](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/11/from-bureaucracy-to-bytes-digitalization-reshaping-tanzania-s-procurement-future-for-economic-growth>). It raises AI-driven red flags across the procurement cycle. [Maxbit says it won a ten-year, $35 million contract in June 2024 with Zanzibar's e-Government Authority](<https://maxbit.net/2024/06/01/maxbit-has-signed-a-digital-transformation-contract/>), for its AI-integrated Origami platform.

**Rules or register.** In October 2026 the central bank governor said [the Bank of Tanzania has approved policies to guide its own adoption of AI](<https://dailynews.co.tz/bot-instructs-institutions-to-embrace-ai-and-data-analytics-for-operational-efficiency/>). They cover data privacy, accountability and human oversight. They apply to the bank alone. No rules or register for the government's own use of AI are found.
