# Tanzania

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The e-Government Authority says its [Msaada AI system is in use on the Tanzania Tourist Board's website and in the e-Mrejesho feedback system](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>) of the President's Office Public Service Management. It was designed at the authority's own research centre, beside two further tools, Muhtasari AI and Tafsiri AI. The authority says it [keeps improving Msaada AI from users' feedback](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>).

**Several bodies.** One government platform serves several bodies: the e-Government Authority says [Msaada AI is used by two institutions](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>), as of July 2026. No third user is named.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [e-Government Authority's account of its AI tools](<https://www.ega.go.tz/news/e-ga-yaendeleza-ubunifu-wa-mifumo-inayotumia-akili-unde-2>), which states no rules.
