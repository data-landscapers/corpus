# Somalia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In September 2026 HoA DRIVE reported that the Somali Bureau of Standards is building an integrated management information system](<https://hoadrive.info/stories/somalia-standards-services-move-online/>). It includes a Somali-English AI chatbot to guide users through certification. Preparing its rollout is the next phase.

**Several bodies.** No government body has been found with AI in service. The nearest fact is the [Bureau of Standards' system](<https://hoadrive.info/stories/somalia-standards-services-move-online/>), which was still being built in September 2026.

**Rules or register.** No rules or register for the government's own use of AI have been found. The nearest fact is the [Bureau of Standards' system](<https://hoadrive.info/stories/somalia-standards-services-move-online/>), whose report names no rules.

***Also on file***. None found.
