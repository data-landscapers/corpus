# Gambia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In April 2026 Permanent Secretary Cordu L. Jabang said The Gambia had launched an AI-powered fact-checking and verification system](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/gna-gambian-officials-highlight-ai-digital-transformation-in-modern-journalism/>). [The Ministry of Information houses it](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/gna-gambian-officials-highlight-ai-digital-transformation-in-modern-journalism/>). [Citizens send audio, video, text and voice messages, local languages included, through a WhatsApp line](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/gna-gambian-officials-highlight-ai-digital-transformation-in-modern-journalism/>). [They get feedback on whether the messages are true](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/gna-gambian-officials-highlight-ai-digital-transformation-in-modern-journalism/>). [LocaleNLP says it has executive authorization from the Minister of Communications to pilot Wolof and Mandinka speech and translation tools](<https://www.localenlp.com/updates/13>). [The aim is to integrate them into the National e-Government iPortal so citizens can use services by voice](<https://www.localenlp.com/updates/13>).
