# Gambia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Permanent Secretary Cordu L. Jabang of the Ministry of Information said The Gambia had recently launched an AI-powered fact-checking and verification system, housed under the Ministry, that takes citizens' audio, video, text and voice messages, including in local languages, through a WhatsApp line and returns feedback on whether they are true](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/04/gna-gambian-officials-highlight-ai-digital-transformation-in-modern-journalism/>). [LocaleNLP says it has executive authorization from The Gambia's Minister of Communications for a pilot of Wolof and Mandinka speech and translation tools, aimed at integration into the National e-Government iPortal so citizens can use services by voice (vendor's claim only)](<https://www.localenlp.com/updates/13>).
