# Mauritania

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In June 2026 the Ministry of Youth Empowerment, Employment, Sports and Civil Service [launched Mourchid, an AI assistant in Arabic and French](<https://erribat.info/?p=80889>) that answers on jobs, the civil service and youth programmes from ministry databases. In February 2026 the digital transition minister said [AI would check files, set questions and mark papers in recruiting 3,000 civil servants](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/290261>). In March 2026 the ministry and the national security directorate [used AI to check 1,459 applications for the police recruitment exam and to mark it](<https://www.cridem.org/C_Info.php?article=792096>).

**Several bodies.** A second body runs AI in service. In December 2025 the authorities announced that [an AI road-safety system built by the Agence numérique de l'État was operating on the national network](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2612-134553-la-mauritanie-met-en-service-une-solution-d-ia-dediee-a-la-securite-routiere>), run by the national police, with cameras that detect overloaded vehicles and unworn seat belts.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is [Mourchid's launch](<https://erribat.info/?p=80889>), which states no rules.
