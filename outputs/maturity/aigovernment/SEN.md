# Senegal

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** ARCOP says the APPEL public procurement platform, [in its pilot phase since 14 October 2025](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>), includes an AI assistant that guides users on regulatory and technical matters; by February 2026 nearly 400 suppliers had registered. On 5 October 2026 the Pikine-Guédiawaye court [began work on a local AI application for judicial archives](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>), as a pilot court with UNDP and Korean support.

**Several bodies.** Nothing held shows AI in service in two government bodies. The nearest held fact is the [Pikine-Guédiawaye pilot](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>), a second body at the pilot step.

**Rules or register.** Nothing held shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [APPEL pilot](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>), which names none; in its first four months the platform published more than 80 procurement plans and launched 15 procedures.
