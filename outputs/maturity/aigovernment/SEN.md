# Senegal

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Anta, the chatbot on the government's Service Public portal](<https://www.polygon.sn/anta-lia-au-service-du-citoyen/>), built by Sénégal Numérique SA, uses AI intent detection and voice recognition to answer users and guide them through administrative procedures.

**Several bodies.** ARCOP, the procurement regulator, runs a second AI service. Its May 2026 report on the APPEL procurement platform's pilot lists an [AI assistant on the public procurement code and a chatbot](<https://arcop.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Rapport_achevement_APPEL.pdf>) among the support given to the platform's users. APPEL was [in its pilot phase from 14 October 2025](<https://arcop.sn/2026/02/12/premiers-resultats-encourageants-sur-la-plateforme-appel-4-mois-de-mise-en-oeuvre-plus-de-400-fournisseurs-inscrits-80-ppm-publies-et-15-procedures-lancees/>), includes an AI assistant, and on 5 October 2026 the Pikine-Guédiawaye court [began work on a local AI application for judicial archives](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>) as a pilot.

**Rules or register.** Nothing held shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is [Anta's description](<https://www.polygon.sn/anta-lia-au-service-du-citoyen/>), which states no rules.
