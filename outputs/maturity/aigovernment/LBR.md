# Liberia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In July 2026 a Central Bank of Liberia employee was reported to have invented an AI system for macroeconomic forecasting and financial intelligence](<https://fpa.news/undp-reaffirms-commitment-to-liberias-digital-transformation-at-tech-summit/>). [The bank is using it](<https://fpa.news/undp-reaffirms-commitment-to-liberias-digital-transformation-at-tech-summit/>). [The bank's governor said it is at an early stage of AI adoption, moving from experimentation to a more structured approach](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/saamoi-leads-liberia-at-african-central-bank-meetings/article_3c4b46f0-f78a-468d-9db1-ac6e5b38cf6b.html>). In August 2026 the director general of the Civil Service Agency said [its information channels include an AI-powered virtual information assistant](<https://elbcnews.com/2026/08/06/civil-service-agency-launches-monthly-electronic-newsletter/>).

**Several bodies.** Two bodies are found with AI in service: the [central bank](<https://fpa.news/undp-reaffirms-commitment-to-liberias-digital-transformation-at-tech-summit/>) and the [Civil Service Agency](<https://elbcnews.com/2026/08/06/civil-service-agency-launches-monthly-electronic-newsletter/>). In March 2026 a start-up said [its forecasting platform for foreign exchange and liquidity is live inside the central bank](<https://frontpageafricaonline.com/liberia-hellen-s-momohs-surna-technologies-emerged-as-finalist-in-the-harvard-presidents-innovation-challenge/>). In September 2026 the Liberia Revenue Authority said it was [developing an AI-powered contact centre for taxpayers](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/liberiarevenueauthority_transforming-taxpayer-services-lra-advances-activity-7503618260676984832-UCBw>).

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI.
