# Kenya

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In April 2026 the Kenya Revenue Authority launched Shuru, an AI chatbot for filing tax on WhatsApp](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>). Taxpayers use it to see pre-filled details, file returns, raise invoices and get compliance certificates. [On 27 August 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary unveiled Kabogo.AI](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126535-cs-kabogo-unveils-ai-platform-transform-ict-ministry-operations>), an assistant to route citizens to the right agency; it was still being prepared. A GovStack-based AI chatbot for the eCitizen platform was [being developed in May 2026](<https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/news/digital-sovereignty-not-tech-dependency>). The Business Registration Service has [an AI chatbot on business registration](<https://brs.go.ke/development-partners/>), not said to be in service.

**Several bodies.** [On 10 November 2026 the Technopolis Development Authority launched the SecureKE Sovereign Security Operations Centre](<https://www.kbc.co.ke/kenya-launches-sovereign-cybersecurity-centre-to-shield-govt-systems-from-threats/>), with the State Department for ICT. It uses AI-enabled analytics to spot suspicious activity across government networks, servers and cloud systems. Government institutions share it instead of each running their own centre. With [the Revenue Authority's chatbot](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>), AI is in service in two bodies in different sectors.

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its use of AI. The nearest fact is [an AI Accelerator Programme run by the ICT ministry](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>). It invited developers to build AI for public services.

***Also on file***. [KRA AI-powered risk engines](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2329-president-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliancepresident-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliance>).

***Not found***. No source found gives rules for how government bodies use or buy AI. None shows a register of the government's AI systems.
