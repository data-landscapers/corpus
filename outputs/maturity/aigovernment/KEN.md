# Kenya

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [The Kenya Revenue Authority says it has unveiled a WhatsApp tax filing service powered by an AI chatbot, Shuru, through which taxpayers access pre-filled details, file returns, generate invoices and obtain compliance certificates](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>). [Kenya's ICT Cabinet Secretary unveiled Kabogo.AI on 27 August 2026, an AI assistant being developed to route citizens to the right government agency, service or resource, which Kenyans.co.ke reports is still being prepared and will launch once review safeguards are in place](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126535-cs-kabogo-unveils-ai-platform-transform-ict-ministry-operations>). A GovStack-based AI chatbot for the eCitizen platform was [being developed in May 2026](<https://www.giz.de/en/newsroom/news/digital-sovereignty-not-tech-dependency>). The Business Registration Service says GIZ support produced an [AI chatbot on business registration services](<https://brs.go.ke/development-partners/>), without saying it is in service. The ICT ministry, with Konza Technopolis and UNDP, [invited developers to submit AI applications for public services](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>) under an AI Accelerator Programme that closed to applications in September 2026.

**Several bodies.** No source held since October 2023 says AI is in service in two or more government bodies. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2362-kra-surpasses-ksh-2-trillion-revenue-milestone,-records11-4-growth-in-nine-months>).
