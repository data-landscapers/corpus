# Namibia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [A NUST master's thesis evaluates the government's Pocket Guide 2.0 Pocket Bot AI chatbot as a digital self-service tool for civil servants, based on interviews with 20 civil servants who used it](<https://ir.nust.na/items/97b0e777-f3f9-4918-8cfb-26a6cff001d3>). [The Prime Minister said the launched Digital Pocket Guide 2.0 has an AI chatbot, Pocketbot, through which civil servants ask questions and receive instant answers on public service HR policies](<https://nbcnews.na/node/103524>).
