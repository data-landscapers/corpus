# Namibia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In November 2023 the Prime Minister said the launched Digital Pocket Guide 2.0 has an AI chatbot, Pocketbot](<https://nbcnews.na/node/103524>). [Through it civil servants get instant answers on public service HR policies](<https://nbcnews.na/node/103524>). [In January 2025 a NUST master's thesis evaluated Pocket Bot as a self-service tool, interviewing 20 civil servants who used it](<https://ir.nust.na/items/97b0e777-f3f9-4918-8cfb-26a6cff001d3>).

**Several bodies.** No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in two or more government bodies. The nearest fact: [in November 2023 the Prime Minister's office and UNDP launched the Pocket Guide with its Pocket Bot](<https://www.undp.org/namibia/stories/being-public-servant-digital-pocket-guide-20-launched-empower-namibian-government-officials>).

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest fact: [in November 2023 Pocketbot answered civil servants' questions on HR policies](<https://nbcnews.na/node/103524>).
