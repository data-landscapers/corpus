## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [On 15 July 2025 the government announced the launch of Missamou, a chatbot on Facebook Messenger](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-gabon-lance-missamou-une-ia-conversationnelle-pour-moderniser-l-administration>). [It uses natural language processing to answer citizens' questions on official news, public services and administrative procedures](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-gabon-lance-missamou-une-ia-conversationnelle-pour-moderniser-l-administration>). [It answers 24 hours a day from official databases](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/le-gabon-lance-missamou-une-ia-conversationnelle-pour-moderniser-l-administration>).
