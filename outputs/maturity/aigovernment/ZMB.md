# Zambia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In July 2026 the Zambia Revenue Authority's Commissioner-General said it [has an Artificial Intelligence Unit and has introduced an AI chatbot](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/techbytes-zra-turns-to-ai-research-to-strengthen-tax-administration/>), an HS code search engine and a taxpayer nudging system. He said the authority was [also turning to AI research to strengthen tax administration](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/techbytes-zra-turns-to-ai-research-to-strengthen-tax-administration/>).

**Several bodies.** Only the revenue authority is held with AI in service. The pension authority NAPSA [tendered an AI-enabled endpoint detection service, opened on 3 July 2025 and since awarded](<https://eprocure.zppa.org.zm/epps/eawarding/showDetailsOpenedTender.do?resourceId=16986114>), and the statistics office [tested GPT-4 Turbo on coding labour survey answers](<https://www.theigc.org/blogs/promise-practice-ai-lmic-governments>). Neither is reported in service.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [Commissioner-General's account](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/techbytes-zra-turns-to-ai-research-to-strengthen-tax-administration/>), which states no rules.

***Also on file***. [Ask Viamo Anything (AVA)](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/15/leveraging-ai-to-bypass-the-smartphone-barrier-and-advance-digital-inclusion-in-africa/>); ZRA taxpayer nudging system; SMART Zambia AI chatbot.

***Not found***. No source found reports AI in service in a second government body, and rules or a register for the government's own use of AI.
