# Use of AI in government administration: AI in administration

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The Continental AI Strategy asks for AI adopted in the public sector under a governance framework, with innovation-friendly procurement and use cases shared and reported. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1 Absent | A source from the past three years says no AI system is in use in public administration. |
| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |
| 3 In service | Yes to 2 |
| 4 Several bodies | Yes to 3 |
| 5 Rules or register | Yes to 4 |

### Benin: 5 Leading

The government published rules or a register for its own use of AI in 2026.

- ASIN's June 2026 legal note sets measures for public agents' use of generative AI in the administration, including a binding charter, provider contract clauses barring model training on data and transfers under the Cloud Act or FISA, and a data protection impact assessment ([source, 2026-06](https://asin.bj/doc/137/download)).
