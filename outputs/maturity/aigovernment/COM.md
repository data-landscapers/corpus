# Comoros

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In February 2025 Al-watwan reported that AI in the Comoros was limited so far to personal uses](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-les-comores-ne-disposent-toujours-pas-d%E2%80%99une-politique-en-mati%C3%A8re-d%E2%80%99ia.html>). An official of the national digital agency Anaden said the country had no national AI policy yet.

**Several bodies.** No government body has been found with an AI system in service or under test. The nearest fact is [Al-watwan's report of February 2025](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-les-comores-ne-disposent-toujours-pas-d%E2%80%99une-politique-en-mati%C3%A8re-d%E2%80%99ia.html>) that AI use was personal.

**Rules or register.** No rules or register for the government's own use of AI have been found. [In February 2025 an Anaden official said there was no national AI policy](<https://alwatwan.net/societe/intelligence-artificielle-i-les-comores-ne-disposent-toujours-pas-d%E2%80%99une-politique-en-mati%C3%A8re-d%E2%80%99ia.html>), only reflection on one.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found names an AI system in a government body. None names rules or a register for the government's own use of AI.
