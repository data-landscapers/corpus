# Ghana

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [On 26 May 2026 UNESCO handed over an AI-powered multi-hazard chatbot to the National Disaster Management Organization (NADMO)](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ghanas-disaster-agency-turns-ai-reach-people-water-does>). [The agency now owns and runs it](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ghanas-disaster-agency-turns-ai-reach-people-water-does>). [It answers public questions on seven hazards and sends automated responses in several languages during emergencies](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ghanas-disaster-agency-turns-ai-reach-people-water-does>). [In September 2026 an NCA deputy director-general said AI was becoming an integral tool in the NCA's work](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/7222/us-commerce-department-engages-ghanas-nca-on-ai-regulation>).

**Several bodies.** [Ghana Statistical Service's data science unit has built a machine-learning model that codes occupations and industries for its labour force survey](<https://www.data4sdgs.org/blog/why-africas-data-future-depends-nso-leadership>). [It accepts the codes it is confident about and sends the rest to an official, whose corrections retrain it](<https://www.data4sdgs.org/blog/why-africas-data-future-depends-nso-leadership>). [In one quarter of 2025 coders did this by hand for 27,467 workers](<https://www.data4sdgs.org/blog/why-africas-data-future-depends-nso-leadership>). With NADMO's chatbot, that puts AI in service in two bodies.

**Rules or register.** No rules or register for the government's own use of AI have been found. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 the NCA said AI was becoming integral to its processes](<https://www.techreviewafrica.com/news/7222/us-commerce-department-engages-ghanas-nca-on-ai-regulation>).
