# Cape Verde

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In October 2025 the president of the justice modernisation institute [demonstrated its integrated AI initiative](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>): a Portal da Justiça with an AI chat built to help citizens reach legal information and digital services. Nothing found shows it, or any other AI system, in service in a government body. In November 2024 the government said the implementation phase of its [Microsoft programme had begun, with Copilot to be introduced in public administration](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-inicia-fase-de-implementacao-do-acordo-estrategico-com-a-microsoft/>).

**Several bodies.** Nothing found shows AI in service in any government body. The nearest held fact is the [justice portal demonstration](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>) at the Lisbon Forum 2025, where the institute presented the Portal da Justiça and its AI chat for citizens seeking legal information and digital services.
