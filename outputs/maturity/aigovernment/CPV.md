# Cape Verde

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [On 3 November 2026 the labour inspectorate launched its portal and management system](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/nova-plataforma-digital-da-igt-permite-apresentar-queixas-laborais-e-solicitar-servicos-on-line-ministra>). The minister said its services include artificial intelligence features for workers and employers. The International Labour Organization says [the portal has an AI-powered virtual assistant that answers questions on labour law, including in Kriolu](<https://www.ilo.org/resource/article/ilo-supports-digital-transformation-cabo-verde%E2%80%99s-general-labour>). In October 2025 the justice modernisation institute [demonstrated an AI chat on its Portal da Justiça](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>), built to help citizens reach legal information. In November 2024 the government said [Copilot was to be introduced in public administration](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-inicia-fase-de-implementacao-do-acordo-estrategico-com-a-microsoft/>) under its Microsoft programme.

**Several bodies.** [The labour inspectorate's portal](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/nova-plataforma-digital-da-igt-permite-apresentar-queixas-laborais-e-solicitar-servicos-on-line-ministra>) is the one government system found with AI in service. In October 2024 the president of the National Elections Commission said [the commission already uses AI for civic education and content creation](<https://www.balai.cv/noticias/cne-aponta-esforco-colaborativo-conjunto-de-varias-instituicoes-como-estrategia-para-garantir-integridade-do-sistema-eleitoral/>). She named no AI system. The [justice portal's AI chat](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>) was shown at the Lisbon Forum 2025. Nothing found shows it in service.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest found fact is the [justice portal presentation](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>), which spoke of the ethical use of AI.
