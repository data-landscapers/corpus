# Cape Verde

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [On 3 November 2026 the labour inspectorate launched its portal and management system](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/nova-plataforma-digital-da-igt-permite-apresentar-queixas-laborais-e-solicitar-servicos-on-line-ministra>). The minister said its services include artificial intelligence features for workers and employers. In October 2025 the justice modernisation institute [demonstrated an AI chat on its Portal da Justiça](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>), built to help citizens reach legal information. In November 2024 the government said [Copilot was to be introduced in public administration](<https://www.governo.cv/cabo-verde-inicia-fase-de-implementacao-do-acordo-estrategico-com-a-microsoft/>) under its Microsoft programme.

**Several bodies.** [The labour inspectorate's portal](<https://www.inforpress.cv/en/nova-plataforma-digital-da-igt-permite-apresentar-queixas-laborais-e-solicitar-servicos-on-line-ministra>) is the one government system found with AI in service. The [justice portal's AI chat](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>) was shown at the Lisbon Forum 2025. Nothing found shows it in service.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest found fact is the [justice portal presentation](<https://justica.gov.cv/w/lisbon-forum-2025-destaca-inova%C3%A7%C3%A3o-digital-de-cabo-verde-com-o-portal-da-justi%C3%A7a-e-uso-%C3%A9tico-da-intelig%C3%AAncia-artificial>), which spoke of the ethical use of AI.
