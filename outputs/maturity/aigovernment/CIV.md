## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In March 2026 the government launched EMY 101, an AI assistant on WhatsApp, Messenger and toll-free number 101](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-d-ivoire-launches-emy-101-ai-chatbot-for-public-services>). [It gives information on civil status, taxation and ministerial services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-d-ivoire-launches-emy-101-ai-chatbot-for-public-services>). [Land services are covered too](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/20/cote-divoire-launches-emy-101-ai-chatbot-to-transform-public-service-access/>). [In early 2025 the youth ministry presented a digital platform that will use AI to process CVs when recruiting young people](<https://digitalmag.ci/journee-mondiale-des-competences-des-jeunes-51601-jeunes-aux-metiers-du-numerique-et-ia-en-cote-divoire/>).
