# South Africa

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Home Affairs' electronic travel authorisation, live at four airports since its mid-August 2026 launch, uses automated risk-based adjudication, with human review only where the system detects an anomaly, per the minister's spokesperson](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/machine-learning-gets-first-say-on-who-gets-into-sa/dgp45MaBQnyqX9l8>). [Home Affairs' Electronic Travel Authorisation uses machine learning and biometrics to screen applications; the minister says over 203,000 were processed since May and over 5,500 fraudulent ones rejected](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/11/south-africa-machine-learning-border-checks/>). [The Department of Public Service and Administration says Minister Buthelezi launched the Batho Pele AI Chatbot, built with Meta and Juicetel, giving citizens and public servants access to public service policy updates, circulars and legislation](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/thepublicservant/2026/08/01/innovation-meets-impact-as-minister-buthelezi-launches-batho-pele-ai-chatbot/>).
