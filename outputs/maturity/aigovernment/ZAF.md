# South Africa

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** Home Affairs [screens electronic travel authorisation applications with machine learning and biometrics](<https://techcabal.com/2026/08/11/south-africa-machine-learning-border-checks/>); the minister said over 203,000 applications were processed from May and over 5,500 fraudulent ones rejected. Since its [mid-August 2026 launch at four airports](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/machine-learning-gets-first-say-on-who-gets-into-sa/dgp45MaBQnyqX9l8>), the system decides on its own and sends only anomalies to human review. The revenue service SARS [expanded its Lwazi AI assistant across its website and app in October 2025](<https://www.sars.gov.za/latest-news/ask-lwazi-sars-ai-assistant-our-upgraded-artificial-intelligence-assistant/>) to answer queries on tax numbers, statements and refunds.

**Several bodies.** A second body runs AI in service: in August 2026 the Department of Public Service and Administration [launched the Batho Pele AI Chatbot](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/thepublicservant/2026/08/01/innovation-meets-impact-as-minister-buthelezi-launches-batho-pele-ai-chatbot/>), built with Meta and Juicetel, which gives citizens and public servants policy updates, circulars and legislation. SARS also [offers an AI virtual assistant on its website](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/over-5-million-taxpayers-auto-assessed-2025-filing-season-gets-underway>) for taxpayers' queries. In October 2026 TechCabal reported that the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission had [tendered for an AI chatbot to handle about two million customer interactions a month](<https://techcabal.com/2026/10/26/government-ai-adoption-in-africa/>).

**Rules or register.** Nothing held shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [Batho Pele chatbot launch](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/thepublicservant/2026/08/01/innovation-meets-impact-as-minister-buthelezi-launches-batho-pele-ai-chatbot/>), which names no policy for its use.
