# Egypt

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology announced the pilot launch of Karnak Chat, a chatbot built on its Karnak model, open to users by web registration or a Google Play app](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5893918.aspx>). [Minister Raafat Hindi said efforts are under way to develop the capabilities of EG-CERT, under the National Telecom Regulatory Authority, by using AI techniques against cyber threats](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5670246.aspx>). [Egypt's AI council adopted a report saying more than 40 AI applications are in use across the government and banking sectors](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>).

**Several bodies.** [The same report counts more than 40 AI applications across government and banking](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). In October 2026 the head of the Consumer Protection Agency said it [uses AI to analyse complaints, inspection data, prices and online stores](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/22/ee7ef990fe2f40609756579de54d9b28>) to find patterns of violation. The Cabinet Media Center launched [Chat Egypt](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/20/5ec07d8962d14d6b92816e5dc9a4ea52>) in October 2026, an AI platform that checks text, images and video against verified data uploaded by ministries and governorates to detect rumours.

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>).
