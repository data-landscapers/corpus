# Egypt

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology launched a pilot of Karnak Chat](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5893918.aspx>), a chatbot built on its Karnak model. Users register on the web or through a Google Play app. [Minister Raafat Hindi said work is under way to use AI techniques at EG-CERT, under the telecoms regulator, against cyber threats](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5670246.aspx>). [In September 2026 Egypt's AI council adopted a report counting more than 40 AI applications in use](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>) across government and banking.

**Several bodies.** [The AI council's report counts more than 40 AI applications across government and banking](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). In October 2026 the head of the Consumer Protection Agency said it [uses AI to analyse complaints, inspection data, prices and online stores](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/22/ee7ef990fe2f40609756579de54d9b28>). The Cabinet Media Center launched [Chat Egypt](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/20/5ec07d8962d14d6b92816e5dc9a4ea52>) in October 2026. It checks text, images and video against data from ministries and governorates to detect rumours. [In June 2026 the Ministry of Labour said AI tools sort the questions sent to its new WhatsApp inquiry service](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/571908/Egypt/Society/Egypt-moves-citizen-complaints-beyond-hotlines-wit.aspx>).

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest found fact: [the AI council's report](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>).
