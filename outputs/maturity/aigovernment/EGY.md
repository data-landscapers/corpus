# Egypt

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology announced the pilot launch of Karnak Chat, a chatbot built on its Karnak model, open to users by web registration or a Google Play app](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5893918.aspx>). [Minister Raafat Hindi said efforts are under way to develop the capabilities of EG-CERT, under the National Telecom Regulatory Authority, by using AI techniques against cyber threats](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5670246.aspx>). [Egypt's AI council adopted a report saying more than 40 AI applications are in use across the government and banking sectors](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>).
