# Egypt

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology launched a pilot of Karnak Chat](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5893918.aspx>), a chatbot. [Minister Raafat Hindi said work is under way to use AI techniques at EG-CERT, under the telecoms regulator, against cyber threats](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5670246.aspx>). [In September 2026 Egypt's AI council adopted a report counting more than 40 AI applications in use](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>) across government and banking. [In November 2026 the investment minister said his ministry is building its trade regulatory lab with AI](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>).

**Several bodies.** [The AI council's report counts more than 40 AI applications across government and banking](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). In October 2026 the head of the Consumer Protection Agency said it [uses AI to analyse complaints, inspection data, prices and online stores](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/22/ee7ef990fe2f40609756579de54d9b28>). The Cabinet Media Center launched [Chat Egypt](<https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/october/20/5ec07d8962d14d6b92816e5dc9a4ea52>) in October 2026. It checks claims against official data to detect rumours. [In November 2026 the justice and communications ministries launched a remote power-of-attorney service](<https://www.vetogate.com/5767586>). It uses AI in the digital identity app to verify the citizen. [In June 2026 the Ministry of Labour said AI tools sort the questions sent to its new WhatsApp inquiry service](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/1/2/571908/Egypt/Society/Egypt-moves-citizen-complaints-beyond-hotlines-wit.aspx>).

**Rules or register.** [In November 2026 FinTech Gate reported Egypt's guideline for government procurement of AI systems, adopted by the National Council for AI](<https://www.fintechgate.net/258322>). [It sets a minimum technical score of 75 out of 100 for bidders](<https://www.fintechgate.net/258322>). [It binds a contractor only where a tender document includes it](<https://www.fintechgate.net/258322>).
