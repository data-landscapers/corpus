# Angola

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The telecoms regulator INACOM [relaunched its portal in June 2025 with a 24-hour AI chatbot](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/06/12/novo-portal-do-inacom-destaca-inovacao-ao-servico-do-cidadao/>). It answers questions and tracks the status of applications. In January 2026 the state-enterprise institute IGAPE said [its AI platform SEP360 was in its final phase](<https://targeting.ao/inteligencia-artificial/igape-considera-em-fase-final-plataforma-de-ia-para-o-sector-publico/>). [Presidential Order 342/26 authorises US$6 million of World Bank funds for a public-sector AI platform](<https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/>). In October 2026 the IMA [opened an international tender for a new SIMPLIFICA services portal with AI built in](<https://pti.ao/executivo-anuncia-concurso-publico-internacional-para-modernizar-o-portal-simplifica/>).

**Several bodies.** Only INACOM is held with AI in service. In July 2025 the IMA director said [AI in public services was limited to small experimental modules](<https://revistaexecutivo.com/especialistas-debate-sobre-estrategia-da-inteligencia-artificial-no-sector-publico-e-privado/>), with no structured project yet.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [order funding a public-sector AI platform](<https://pti.ao/o-pais-avanca-para-uma-administracao-publica-com-inteligencia-artificial/>), which sets no rules.
