# Morocco

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The digital transition ministry runs Idarati AI, an assistant that guides users through administrative procedures. [The ministry unveiled it on 8 September 2026 as the first phase of a sovereign AI platform](<https://detafour.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86/>). [LesEco lists it among the platform's first working parts](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>). [The ministry also trains AI tools of its own for other administrations](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7504/morocco-mistral-ai-release-first-open-source-ai-tools-under-ai-made-in-morocco-r>), some built with Mistral AI. [In November 2026 the minister said the ministry had begun using AI to analyse about 2,800 administrative procedures](<https://al3omk.com/1208688.html>). [He also named a document-processing suite and a meeting assistant under development](<https://en.hespress.com/148882-morocco-develops-national-ai-roadmap-as-government-seeks-to-regulate-technology-use.html>).

**Several bodies.** A second body runs AI in service. [The tax directorate uses a chatbot to assist taxpayers](<https://fr.le360.ma/economie/lia-encore-embryonnaire-dans-les-services-publics-selon-lexpert-marouane-harmach_G3KDUQCTCNCY5B6TUYNLU2LPVE/>), Le360 reported in September 2025, citing the expert Marouane Harmach. The social security fund CNSS [tendered an AI chatbot for its staff in November 2025](<https://www.lodj.ma/%E2%80%8BLa-CNSS-investit-6-millions-de-dirhams-pour-deployer-un-Chatbot-au-service-de-ses-collaborateurs_a151143.html>). [The finance ministry's 2022-2026 plan includes an eBudget chatbot and AI writing tools](<https://en.hespress.com/90558-moroccan-ministry-of-finance-eyes-ai-to-boost-efficiency.html>), the budget minister said in 2024.

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 Idarati AI was among the first operational parts of the sovereign marketplace](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>).
