# Morocco

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [LesEco says Idarati AI, an agentic assistant on the sovereign marketplace, guides users through administrative procedures and is among the first operational components](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>). [Tech Review Africa reports the Ministry of Digital Transition designed and trained AI solutions internally for its AI marketplace and to meet the needs of administrations and public services, with other models and use cases being developed with Mistral AI](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7504/morocco-mistral-ai-release-first-open-source-ai-tools-under-ai-made-in-morocco-r>). [The ministry unveiled on 2026-09-08 a sovereign AI platform whose first phase includes Idarati AI, a digital assistant guiding users through administrative procedures](<https://detafour.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86/>).

**Several bodies.** A second body runs AI in service: [Le360, citing the expert Marouane Harmach, reports that the tax directorate uses a chatbot to assist taxpayers](<https://fr.le360.ma/economie/lia-encore-embryonnaire-dans-les-services-publics-selon-lexpert-marouane-harmach_G3KDUQCTCNCY5B6TUYNLU2LPVE/>), in September 2025. The social security fund CNSS [tendered an AI chatbot for its staff in November 2025](<https://www.lodj.ma/%E2%80%8BLa-CNSS-investit-6-millions-de-dirhams-pour-deployer-un-Chatbot-au-service-de-ses-collaborateurs_a151143.html>).

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leseco.ma/maroc/maroc-ia-2030-le-royaume-passe-de-lambition-a-laction.html>).
