# Eswatini

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The Eswatini Standards Authority, the national standards body, is building an AI platform for its own work. In November 2026 it and its partner AICE Africa said [ESWASA is developing EswasaOne](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/eswatini-standards-authority-and-aice-africa-launch-ai-safety-and-standards-center-of-excellence?lang=en>), a platform linking standards information, certification and inspection workflows and management oversight. A conversational interface and workflow automation are among its planned features.
