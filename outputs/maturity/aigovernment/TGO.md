# Togo

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In October 2026 Togo's AI laboratory had [extended its collaborations to more than ten public agencies, with over 70 projects](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>) in agriculture, health and public administration, Investdesk reported. These are joint projects between the laboratory and the agencies.

**Several bodies.** No source held says AI is in service in two or more government bodies. The nearest held fact is the AI laboratory's [work with more than ten public agencies](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>), which spreads its projects across several parts of government rather than one ministry.
