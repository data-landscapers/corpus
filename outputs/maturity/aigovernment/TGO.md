# Togo

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [UNDP Togo said in November 2026 that a digital assistant helps staff of the national public procurement control directorate](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>). It searches and analyses procurement information, and users say a task of several days now takes under one. It runs on an AI server that UNDP gave in July 2026. [A second tool is being built for the Ministry of Security](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>) to check applications for recruitment competitions. In October 2026 Togo's AI laboratory [worked with more than ten public agencies on over 70 projects](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>), Investdesk reported.

**Several bodies.** [The procurement control directorate](<https://www.undp.org/fr/togo/actualites/un-supercalculateur-pour-accelerer-linnovation-technologique-lintelligence-artificielle-et-la-prise-de-decision-strategique-au-togo>) is the one government body found with AI in service. The AI laboratory's [projects with more than ten public agencies](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>) are joint projects, not systems stated to be in service.

**Rules or register.** No source found says the government has issued rules for its own use or purchase of AI, or published a register of its AI systems. The nearest found fact is the [AI laboratory's projects with public agencies](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>).
