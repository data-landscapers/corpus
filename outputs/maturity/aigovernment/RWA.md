# Rwanda

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The National Bank of Rwanda [deployed its Intumwa AI agent in 2021](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>) to handle complaints across more than 600 financial institutions, and was expanding it as of November 2025, its developer Proto says. Proto built both agents.

**Several bodies.** Only the central bank is held with AI in service. In October 2025 the Ministry of Local Government [began designing the Mbaza AI agent for citizen complaints](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>), aiming to deploy it in early 2026. Mbaza is [planned with the national ICT agency RISA](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>) to take citizens' complaints.

**Rules or register.** Nothing held shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is [Proto's account of the two agents](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>), which states no rules.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
