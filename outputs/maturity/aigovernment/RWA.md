# Rwanda

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The National Bank of Rwanda [deployed its Intumwa AI agent in 2021](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>) to handle complaints across more than 600 financial institutions, and was expanding it as of November 2025, its developer Proto says. On 4 November 2025 the Ministry of Infrastructure and the Rwanda Housing Authority [released an AI agent on Kubaka.gov.rw](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ishimwe-romalice_yesterday-our-team-at-the-rwanda-ministry-activity-7391804455949676544-5skC>), the national building-permits platform, that reviews architectural designs against the Building Code and zoning rules.

**Several bodies.** Two bodies are held with AI in service: the central bank and the [infrastructure ministry with the housing authority](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ishimwe-romalice_yesterday-our-team-at-the-rwanda-ministry-activity-7391804455949676544-5skC>). In October 2025 the Ministry of Local Government [began designing the Mbaza AI agent for citizen complaints](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>), aiming to deploy it in early 2026.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is [Proto's account of its agents](<https://www.proto.cx/resource/rwandas-government-and-proto-partner-to-deploy-integrated-ai-citizen-support-agents>), which states no rules.
