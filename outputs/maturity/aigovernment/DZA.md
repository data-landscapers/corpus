# Algeria

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [Algeria's Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research launched on 29 April 2025 the Private Digital Adviser, an LLM-based conversational system answering queries on all the ministry's regulatory documents](<https://www.univ-khenchela.dz/en/launching-new-digital-platforms-ministry-of-higher-education-and-scientific-research/>). The National People's Assembly's secretary general said in October 2024 that it [uses AI tools to transcribe and translate its debates](<https://asgp.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wed-16-Oct-PM-Algerie-FR.pdf>).

**Several bodies.** Two bodies are held with AI in service: the Ministry of Higher Education and the National People's Assembly. A parliament counts as a government body here. The [Assembly has also adopted AI-based cybersecurity solutions](<https://asgp.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Wed-16-Oct-PM-Algerie-FR.pdf>), its secretary general said.

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.univ-khenchela.dz/en/launching-new-digital-platforms-ministry-of-higher-education-and-scientific-research/>).
