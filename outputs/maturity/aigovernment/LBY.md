# Libya

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** The Audit Bureau head said it [began developing an institutional AI model, Diwan Bot, at the start of 2026](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358534&lang=en>) to support auditing and oversight. In April 2026 the Minister of Labour and Rehabilitation was told that the ministry's [integrated digital platform, supported by AI, was about 30% complete](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/36835>).

**Several bodies.** Nothing found shows AI in service in any government body. The nearest held fact is the [Labour Ministry platform's progress report](<https://ar.libyaobserver.ly/article/36835>), which describes a system still being built. Neither the Audit Bureau's model nor the Labour Ministry's platform is reported as finished or in use.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is [Diwan Bot's development](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=358534&lang=en>), which states no rules.
