# Nigeria

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** In May 2026 Nigeria [launched GovGuide](<https://techcabal.com/2026/05/22/nigeria-taps-meta-ai-to-help-citizens-access-government-services/>), an AI chatbot on Meta's Llama models that gives government service information by voice and text in English, Hausa, Igbo and Yoruba. It was built with the Ministry of Communications, NCAIR and Publica AI. The Head of the Civil Service [launched Service-Wise GPT as a beta on 21 November 2024](<https://fmino.gov.ng/digitalization-revolution-in-the-federal-civil-service-walson-jack-launches-service-wise-gpt-on-line-academy-and-new-official-e-mail-platform/>), and said in May 2026 that the federal [Service-Wise GPT, which helps civil servants with service rules, had recorded over 50,000 conversations](<https://www.arise.tv/nigeria-deploys-ai-sovereign-digital-infrastructure-to-drive-public-service-reforms/>). The Corporate Affairs Commission, which [piloted an AI registration platform in July 2025](<https://guardian.ng/news/nigeria/national/cac-unveils-ai-powered-portal-for-faster-company-registrations/>), had by February 2026 [put an AI Lawyer and an AI Name Generator](<https://punchng.com/cac-processes-10000-daily-registrations-after-ai-rollout/>) on its website, its Registrar-General said.

**Several bodies.** A second body runs its own AI chatbot. On 24 February 2025 the Lagos State Domestic and Sexual Violence Agency [announced Ask INU](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/nigeria-launches-ai-chatbot-and-self-reporting-portal-for-domestic-violence-survivors>), a WhatsApp chatbot that gives survivors information and referrals at once, launched beside a [self-reporting portal for domestic violence survivors](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/nigeria-launches-ai-chatbot-and-self-reporting-portal-for-domestic-violence-survivors>). The Federal Ministry of Education [runs AskFME](<https://edtechhub.org/case-study/the-ai-adviser-who-supported-a-ministry-to-build-their-own-tool/>), an AI service desk on WhatsApp that resolved 97.3% of queries without a human, EdTech Hub reported in August 2026.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [GovGuide launch](<https://techcabal.com/2026/05/22/nigeria-taps-meta-ai-to-help-citizens-access-government-services/>), which states no rules for its use.
