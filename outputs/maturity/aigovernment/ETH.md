# Ethiopia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** On 12 October 2025 the Ministry of Revenues and the Ethiopian Customs Commission [unveiled eight digital systems, among them an AI-based customs chatbot](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>) and an AI passenger risk-management system.

**Several bodies.** Only the Customs Commission is held with AI in service. The Addis Ababa City Administration announced in December 2025 [an AI-enriched civil registration and resident services system](<https://www.ebc.et/english/Home/NewsDetails?NewsId=3422>), due to begin in 50 woredas.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [customs launch](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>), which states no rules for the systems' use. Both customs systems were [launched together with six other digital tools for revenue and customs administration](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>) on the same day.

***Also on file***. [AI-enriched integrated digital civil registration and resident services system](<https://www.ebc.et/english/Home/NewsDetails?NewsId=3422>); [AI-powered Passenger Risk Management System](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>).
