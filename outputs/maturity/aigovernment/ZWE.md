# Zimbabwe

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In April 2026 the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education launched the Learner's Hub chatbot](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-launches-learners-hub-chatbot-at-zitf/>). It gives learners, teachers and parents academic and psychosocial support. [Consultants UChat Africa built it for the ministry](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-launches-learners-hub-chatbot-at-zitf/>). [On 18 June 2026 the President launched the Citizens Engagement and Scoring Platform](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>), on which residents score their councils. [Its built-in AI model analysed live scores for Harare and produced a recommendation](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>).

**Several bodies.** [The education ministry's chatbot](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/government-launches-learners-hub-chatbot-at-zitf/>) and [the scoring platform, run with the local government ministry](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>), put AI in service in two sectors. [Mutare City Council runs its budget consultations through a WhatsApp chatbot](<https://www.clgf.org.uk/whats-new/news/cscn-peer-learning-clinic-highlights-mutares-digital-transformation-journey-to-strengthen-municipal-finance/>), with more than 20,000 taking part. [The National Budget Automation System, described as AI-driven, was still in pilot in September 2026](<https://sirdc.ac.zw/the-2nd-national-budget-automation-system-piloting-workshop/>).

**Rules or register.** No source found since October 2023 says the government has issued rules or a register for its own use of AI. The nearest held fact: [the scoring platform's launch](<https://gemnation.co.zw/harare-residents-can-now-score-their-council-in-real-time-heres-how/>), which states no rules.
