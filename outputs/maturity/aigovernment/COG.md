# Congo

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** [In November 2026 the minister said the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and the Digital Economy was the first in government to work with AI agents in its daily administration](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/congo-rep-of-the/>). [For now the agents are bought from the market](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/congo-rep-of-the/>). [In September 2024 the DGPME opened an AI-based information platform on business-support schemes to users for testing](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7243556420925759490>).

**Several bodies.** No second body with AI in service has been found. [The minister called the ministry the first in government to work with AI agents](<https://pp.itu.int/2026/en/itu_policy_statements/congo-rep-of-the/>). [The DGPME platform of 2024 was still a test](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7243556420925759490>).

**Rules or register.** No rules or register for the government's own use of AI have been found. The nearest fact: [in September 2024 the DGPME said its chatbot was still to be launched](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7243556420925759490>).
