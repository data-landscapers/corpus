# Tunisia

## AI in administration

**AI in service.** On 5 November 2026 the National Business Registry [launched two AI services](<https://www.assabahnews.tn/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/157289-%D8%B3%D8%AC%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A4%D8%B3%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84-%D8%B9%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AA>). Nawarni is a chatbot that guides founders through company creation. A second bot checks and reserves trade names. Both were built with HiiL and funded by the Netherlands embassy. A March 2026 column counts [18 AI projects among the 138 in the 2025-2026 digital transformation programme](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>).

**Several bodies.** Only the business registry is held with AI in service. In September 2026 the finance ministry listed [a chatbot for the General Directorate of Taxes among AI projects to be announced soon](<https://news-tunisia.tunisienumerique.com/tunisias-finance-ministry-moves-to-integrate-ai-into-taxation-customs-and-accounting/>), so it was not yet live.

**Rules or register.** Nothing found shows rules or a register for the government's own use of AI. The nearest held fact is the [count of AI projects in the 2025-2026 digital programme](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2026/03/13/lia-au-service-de-la-tunisie-les-conditions-dune-adoption-reussie/>), which sets no rules.
