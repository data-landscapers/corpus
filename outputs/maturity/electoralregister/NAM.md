# Namibia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The ECN says biometric kits capture voter data, which are uploaded to a central server and checked for duplicate fingerprints and ID numbers](<https://www.ecn.na/faq/>). [Its digital biometric registration system has been in use since 2014](<https://omutumwa.com/2024/06/ecn-explains-slow-start-of-mobile-voter-registration-system/>). [An enhanced mobile registration system went into use on 3 June 2024](<https://omutumwa.com/2024/06/ecn-explains-slow-start-of-mobile-voter-registration-system/>). [Its fingerprint system removed 15,560 duplicate registrations from about 1.4 million voters in 2024](<https://www.namibian.com.na/over-15-500-duplicate-names-removed-from-voters-roll/>).

**Open for checking.** [The Namibian reports the final 2024 voters' roll was published](<https://www.namibian.com.na/over-15-500-duplicate-names-removed-from-voters-roll/>). [It was given to political parties to inspect](<https://www.namibian.com.na/over-15-500-duplicate-names-removed-from-voters-roll/>). [The chief electoral officer said in August 2024 that a provisional register would follow registration](<https://www.namibian.com.na/well-done-majority-of-namibians-registered-to-vote/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2024 duplicate check compares ID numbers within the roll](<https://www.ecn.na/faq/>).
