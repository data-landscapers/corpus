# Morocco

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voters' roll is under way. None says one is in service. [A Ministry of the Interior order of March 2026 runs a website for the electoral lists](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [Order 503-26 lets registered voters view their own entries on the electoral lists](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [They use the website www.listeselectorales.ma](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [They give their electronic national ID card number](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [They add their surname or birth date](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [The order dates from March 2026](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The lookup on listeselectorales.ma uses the electronic national ID card number](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>).
