# Morocco

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Interior Minister said on 13 January 2026 that the ministry audited the electoral lists in its central information system](<https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/187994>). [Of 382,170 new registrants, 254,740 came through the electoral-lists website](<https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/187994>).

**Open for checking.** [Order 503-26 of March 2026 lets registered voters view their own entries at www.listeselectorales.ma](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [They give their electronic national ID card number with their surname or birth date](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>). [In September 2026 the ministry said voters can also check by text message to 2727 or through the MonBV app](<https://en.hespress.com/145833-morocco-voters-can-check-polling-stations-by-text-website-or-mobile-app.html>). [The check shows their polling station](<https://en.hespress.com/145833-morocco-voters-can-check-polling-stations-by-text-website-or-mobile-app.html>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The lookup on listeselectorales.ma uses the electronic national ID card number](<https://adala.justice.gov.ma/api/uploads/2026/10/13/Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9%20du%20ministre%20de%20l'int%C3%A9rieur%20n%C2%B0%20503-26%20relatif%20au%20site%20%C3%A9lectronique%20r%C3%A9serv%C3%A9%20aux%20listes%20%C3%A9lectorales%20g%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales-1791891611199.pdf>).
