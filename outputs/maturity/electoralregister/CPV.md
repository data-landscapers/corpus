# Cape Verde

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The CNE president said in December 2024 that the electoral database, created in 2007, is outdated](<https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/cne-reivindica-nova-base-de-dados-para-as-eleicoes-de-2026/>). [He asked the Government for a new database for 2026](<https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/cne-reivindica-nova-base-de-dados-para-as-eleicoes-de-2026/>). [A DGAPE page describes a consular portal that feeds diaspora registrations into the electoral database](<https://dgape.cv/rediaspora/>). [The portal is integrated with the electoral management system, SIGE](<https://dgape.cv/rediaspora/>).

**Open for checking.** [The CNE said the registers would be on display for consultation until 23 March 2026](<https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/legislativas-2026-recenseamento-suspenso-a-partir-de-13-de-marco/>). [Claims for omissions or wrong entries were accepted until 28 March](<https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/legislativas-2026-recenseamento-suspenso-a-partir-de-13-de-marco/>). [This came ahead of the legislative elections of 17 May](<https://cne.cv/sala_de_imprensa_/legislativas-2026-recenseamento-suspenso-a-partir-de-13-de-marco/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The DGAPE page says its diaspora system works with the national identification database, SNIAC](<https://dgape.cv/rediaspora/>). [Citizens abroad are registered automatically when they ask for a passport or ID card](<https://dgape.cv/rediaspora/>).
