# Mauritius

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voters' roll is under way. None says one is in service. [The Electoral Commissioner's office ran voter registration from 16 to 30 May 2024](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>). [It used 43 registration centres](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>). [Registration ran for two weeks in May 2024](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>).

**Open for checking.** [Voters could check the register online at electoral.govmu.org](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>). [They could also check by SMS or phone, using their national identity card number](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>). [The 43 registration centres offered the same check](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The online and SMS checks of 2024 ask for the national identity card number](<https://defimedia.info/liste-electorale-plus-que-quelques-jours-pour-verifier-et-sinscrire>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voter register is procured, piloted or in service.
