# Kenya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Standard reports a court order that the IEBC give parties and observers access to the source code and audit logs of its continuous voter registration system to monitor its ongoing operations, with biometric voter identification used in the 2026 by-elections](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Open for checking.** [The Daily Nation reports that the IEBC chairman gazetted 29 September 2025 for resuming continuous voter registration, letting Kenyans register, transfer, correct details or inspect their records at any of the 290 constituency offices](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>).

**Linked to ID.** [A KPMG audit of the 2022 register, quoted in court, found 164,269 registrations with ID numbers invalid against National Registration Bureau records (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>).
