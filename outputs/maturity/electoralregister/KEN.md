# Kenya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Smartmatic supplied the KIEMS kits used in the 2022 election](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/mps-scrap-iebc-guarantee-of-access-to-election-servers-tech-5646998>). [They handled biometric registration, voter identification and result transmission](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/mps-scrap-iebc-guarantee-of-access-to-election-servers-tech-5646998>). [In 2026 a court ordered the IEBC to open its registration system's source code and audit logs](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). [Parties and observers are to have access](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). [The IEBC is targeting 6.3 million new voters before 2027](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/mps-scrap-iebc-guarantee-of-access-to-election-servers-tech-5646998>). [Biometric voter identification was used in the 2026 by-elections](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>).

**Open for checking.** [The IEBC resumed continuous voter registration on 29 September 2025](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>). [Kenyans could register, transfer, correct details or inspect their records](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>). [This was open at all 290 constituency offices](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>).

**Linked to ID.** [A KPMG audit of the 2022 register was quoted in court](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). [It found 164,269 registrations whose ID numbers were invalid against registration bureau records](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>).
