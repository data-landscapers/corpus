# Kenya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** Smartmatic supplied the [KIEMS kits used for biometric voter registration and identification in the 2022 general election](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/mps-scrap-iebc-guarantee-of-access-to-election-servers-tech-5646998>). The electoral commission, IEBC, is targeting 6.3 million new voters before 2027. In September 2026 a court [ordered the IEBC to give parties and observers access to its voter registration system](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>), including its source code and audit logs. Biometric voter identification was used in the 2026 by-elections.

**Open for checking.** The IEBC chairman [set 29 September 2025 for continuous voter registration to resume](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>). Kenyans could then register, transfer, correct their details or inspect their records at any of the 290 constituency offices.

**Linked to ID.** A KPMG audit of the 2022 register, quoted in court, [found 164,269 registrations with ID numbers invalid against National Registration Bureau records](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). The audit covered the roll used in June 2022.
