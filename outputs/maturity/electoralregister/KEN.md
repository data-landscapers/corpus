# Kenya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The IEBC chair said in September 2025 that a new biometric registration module had been built into KIEMS](<https://www.iebc.or.ke/news/?Commission_resumes_Continuous_Voter_Registration_with_colourful_launch_at_Mashuuru_Primary_School_in_Kajiado=>). [It serves the continuous registration, with 24,128 centres gazetted](<https://www.iebc.or.ke/news/?Commission_resumes_Continuous_Voter_Registration_with_colourful_launch_at_Mashuuru_Primary_School_in_Kajiado=>). [In April 2026 a commissioner put the register at 22.1 million voters](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/iebc-to-clean-voter-register-ahead-of-2027-polls/>). [In 2026 a court ordered the IEBC to open its registration system's source code and audit logs to parties and observers](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>).

**Open for checking.** [The IEBC says registered voters can check their details by text message to 70000](<https://citizen.digital/article/how-to-verify-your-iebc-voter-registration-details-online-n366049>). [Its verification portal was back online on 8 July 2025](<https://citizen.digital/article/how-to-verify-your-iebc-voter-registration-details-online-n366049>). [From 29 September 2025 Kenyans could also inspect their records at all 290 constituency offices](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/politics/iebc-turns-to-id-data-to-meet-voter-targets-5623352>).

**Linked to ID.** [A KPMG audit of the 2022 register was quoted in court](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>). [It found 164,269 registrations whose ID numbers were invalid against registration bureau records](<https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/politics/article/2001558186/political-parties-election-observers-to-access-iebc-voter-register>).
