# Gabon

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Biometric Update reports a nationwide biometric voter registration exercise launched 2 January 2025 under the Ministry of Interior and Security, with enrolment kits in the field, adding to the 850,000 names in the electoral database, and a draft electoral code adopting a new biometric voter registration system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/biometric-voter-registration-underway-in-gabon-ahead-of-presidential-vote>). [Gabon launched biometric voter registration early in January 2025, due to end on 31 January, with the government expecting around 300,000 new voters to enroll on the electoral register, according to VOA as reported](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Open for checking.** [The Ministry of the Interior says citizens can check their registration on the electoral roll and their polling centre online at www.consultation.listelectorale.ga by entering their NIP and name](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>).
