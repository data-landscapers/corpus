# Gabon

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Gabon launched biometric voter registration on 2 January 2025, under the interior ministry](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/biometric-voter-registration-underway-in-gabon-ahead-of-presidential-vote>). [Enrolment kits went out to add to the 850,000 names in the electoral database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/biometric-voter-registration-underway-in-gabon-ahead-of-presidential-vote>). [The government expected about 300,000 new voters](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>). [A draft electoral code adopted a new biometric registration system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/biometric-voter-registration-underway-in-gabon-ahead-of-presidential-vote>). [Registration was due to end on 31 January 2025](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>).

**Open for checking.** [The interior ministry says citizens can check their entry and polling centre online](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>). [They enter their NIP and name at consultation.listelectorale.ga](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>). [The check also shows the voter's polling centre](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The online check of September 2025 asks for the citizen's NIP](<https://www.interieur.gouv.ga/9-actualites/601-verifiez-votre-inscription-et-consultez-votre-centre-de-vote-en-quelques-clics-/>).
