The nearest held fact: [Minister Leon Schreiber said in a parliamentary reply that home affairs and the IEC share an electronic interface that verifies identity information submitted at voter registration and voting against the National Population Register](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>).
