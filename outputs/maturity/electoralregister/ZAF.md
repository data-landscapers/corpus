# South Africa

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ITWeb reported 29.2 million South Africans on the roll for the 2026 local government elections](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/elections-national-results-hub-opens/8OKdWMDXOpwMbznQ>). [The IEC runs them on a voters' roll application and voter management devices](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/elections-national-results-hub-opens/8OKdWMDXOpwMbznQ>). [The devices serve the local government elections of 2026](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/elections-national-results-hub-opens/8OKdWMDXOpwMbznQ>).

**Open for checking.** [The IEC's data-free online registration portal lets voters check their registration status](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/iec-sees-strong-online-voter-registration-uptake/xnklOqz1QrGM4Ymz>). [It also confirms their voting station](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/iec-sees-strong-online-voter-registration-uptake/xnklOqz1QrGM4Ymz>). [The chief electoral officer described it in July 2026](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/iec-sees-strong-online-voter-registration-uptake/xnklOqz1QrGM4Ymz>). [Mamabolo said the portal costs voters no mobile data](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/iec-sees-strong-online-voter-registration-uptake/xnklOqz1QrGM4Ymz>).

**Linked to ID.** [The home affairs minister said in September 2026 that his department and the IEC share an electronic interface](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>). [It verifies identity details given at registration and at voting](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>). [The check is made against the National Population Register](<https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2026-09-23-home-affairs-investigating-21-cases-of-identity-theft-and-fraudulent-ids/>). [He gave the same answer in a written reply to Parliament on 16 September 2026](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/40687/>).
