# Burundi

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The CENI president said in 2026 that a voter who loses a card can still vote](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>). [The condition is being registered in the electoral database](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>). [He said the voter keeps the right to vote despite losing the card](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [SOS Medias Burundi reported his statement in 2026](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The CENI president said in 2026 that registration in the electoral database is what counts](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>). [The voter card itself is not the condition for voting](<https://www.sosmediasburundi.org/en/2026/09/03/2027-presidential-election-more-than-one-million-new-voters-ceni-accelerates-preparations/>).
