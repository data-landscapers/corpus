# Angola

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [A 2026 Intelwatch report says Angola's electoral roll is derived from the civil identity database](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [Under a 2025 law, voters are entered automatically from that database](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [Registration in person is no longer needed](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The justice minister said in July 2026 that the citizens database exchanges data with the ID database](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>). [The exchange keeps the electoral register up to date](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

**Linked to ID.** [Intelwatch says the 2025 law replaced registration in person with registration extracted from the identity database](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>). [The justice minister said the two databases are interlinked](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>). [The link also lets voter-card holders without an ID card regularise their status](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

[base de dados de identidade civil](<https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/servicos-de-informacoes-angolanos-acedem-a-dados-biometricos-sem-controlo-judicial-relatorio/>); [Bilhete de Identidade](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/cerca-de-16-milhoes-de-cidadaos-constam-da-base-do-bilhete-de-identidade/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the roll was open to voters to check. Online, text-message and centre checks were looked for.
