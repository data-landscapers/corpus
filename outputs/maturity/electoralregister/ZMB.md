# Zambia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The ECZ deployed 2,354 biometric voter registration kits nationwide](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>). [It deduplicated and cleaned the provisional register of 8,861,918 voters](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>). [The EU mission says it certified a register of 8,786,300 on 4 May 2026](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026/documents/EU%20EOM%20Zambia%202026_Preliminary%20Statement%2015_8_FINAL_website2.pdf>). [Registration combined manual and digital methods with an online pre-registration portal](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/15%20AUGUST%202026%20FINAL%20AU-COMESA%20Preliminary%20Statement%20%20Zambia.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [Voters could inspect the provisional register online or by USSD *214# until 8 March 2026](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>). [It was also on display at all 12,152 polling stations](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>). [The SADC mission saw the register displayed at 73% of polling stations it observed](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/PRELIMINARY%20STATEMENT%20-%20SEOM%20ZAMBIA.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The ECZ's 2026 cleaning of the register was a deduplication of its own records](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>).
