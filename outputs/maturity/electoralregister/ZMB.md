# Zambia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The AU-COMESA mission says the ECZ used a hybrid manual and digital system, with an online pre-registration portal, to register the 8,786,300 voters on the 2026 register](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/15%20AUGUST%202026%20FINAL%20AU-COMESA%20Preliminary%20Statement%20%20Zambia.pdf>). [The EU mission says the ECZ certified and published a voter register of 8,786,300 on 4 May 2026 and that digital kits were used in registration, with technical difficulties reported by some interlocutors](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026/documents/EU%20EOM%20Zambia%202026_Preliminary%20Statement%2015_8_FINAL_website2.pdf>). [The Minister said the ECZ had deployed 2,354 Biometric Voter Registration kits nationwide and had run de-duplication and data cleansing on the provisional register of 8,861,918 voters, which was also served electronically](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/node/13018>).

**Open for checking.** [The EU mission says the ECZ's online pre-registration portal enabled registered voters to electronically inspect the Provisional Register of Voters](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026/documents/EU%20EOM%20Zambia%202026_Preliminary%20Statement%2015_8_FINAL_website2.pdf>). [The SADC mission says the voters' register was displayed at 201 of 277 polling stations observed (73 per cent) on polling day](<https://www.sadc.int/sites/default/files/2026-08/PRELIMINARY%20STATEMENT%20-%20SEOM%20ZAMBIA.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID or civil register.
