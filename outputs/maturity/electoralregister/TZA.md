# Tanzania

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voters' roll is under way. None says one is in service. [INEC's director of elections said in July 2025 that the voter register was updated in stages](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>).

**Open for checking.** [He said the law makes the register public at every stage of its update](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [INEC gave it to 18 political parties on 27 July 2025](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [The director, Ramadhani Kailima, said this on 27 July 2025](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The register given to parties in 2025 was the updated voter register](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [Each stage of the update is open to the public by law](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID register.
