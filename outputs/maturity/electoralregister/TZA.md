# Tanzania

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [INEC said in March 2025 that its biometric registration kits, in use since 2019/2020, were being upgraded to an Android model](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/03/06/inec-highlights-new-bvr-kit-functionality-android-based-more-portable-set-to-commence-voter-register-update-in-dar-between-march-17-and-23/>). [It secured 6,000 kits in 2024 to update the Permanent National Voters' Register for the 2025 elections](<https://dailynews.co.tz/inec-approves-191-csos-to-conduct-voter-education/>). [The register was projected at 34,746,638 voters after the 2024 update](<https://dailynews.co.tz/inec-unveils-upgraded-bvr-kits-for-smooth-register-update/>). [The kits match fingerprints against existing records to catch double registration](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/03/06/inec-highlights-new-bvr-kit-functionality-android-based-more-portable-set-to-commence-voter-register-update-in-dar-between-march-17-and-23/>).

**Open for checking.** [INEC's director of elections said the law makes the register public at every stage of its update](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [INEC gave it to 18 political parties on 27 July 2025](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/04/inec-tanzania-responds-to-concerns-over-voter-register-numbers/>). [The preliminary register was displayed from May 2025 so citizens could verify, correct or object to entries](<https://dailynews.co.tz/inec-announces-2nd-updating-voter-registrar/>).

**Linked to ID.** [INEC said in March 2025 that the upgraded kits integrate with the NIDA database](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/03/06/inec-highlights-new-bvr-kit-functionality-android-based-more-portable-set-to-commence-voter-register-update-in-dar-between-march-17-and-23/>). [Online corrections to the register require a NIDA National Identification Number, which verifies the details being corrected](<https://dailynews.co.tz/inec-approves-191-csos-to-conduct-voter-education/>). [INEC's head of ICT said in March 2025 that registration retrieves a voter's details from NIDA by ID number](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/24/tanzanias-inec-faces-integrity-questions-amid-contradictions-on-voter-data-integration/>). [On 23 August 2025 INEC denied any integration with other systems for electoral purposes](<https://thechanzo.com/2025/08/24/tanzanias-inec-faces-integrity-questions-amid-contradictions-on-voter-data-integration/>).
