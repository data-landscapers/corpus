# Senegal

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [A biometric voter registration drive began on 1 February 2025 and ran to 31 July](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>). [It aimed to enrol nearly 3 million ECOWAS ID card holders missing from the electoral database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>). [They were to join 7.3 million voters already in the database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2025 drive targeted ID card holders absent from the electoral database](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The drive enrolled holders of the ECOWAS biometric ID card](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>).

***Also on file***. [ECOWAS biometric ID card](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/senegal-targets-3m-ecowas-biometric-id-holders-for-voter-registration>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says voters could check the roll. Requiring an ID card to register does not link the roll to the ID register.
