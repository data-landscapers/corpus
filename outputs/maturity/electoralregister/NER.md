# Niger

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [CENI said in January 2024 that its biometric electoral file holds 7,651,870 voters in nine regions](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>). [It records ten fingerprints and a photo for each voter](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>). [Gemalto built the file, which was used for the 2020-2021 elections](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>). [OIF and ECOWAS experts audited it in 2020 and judged it reliable](<https://www.lesahel.org/ceremonie-de-presentation-publique-des-resultats-de-laudit-du-fichier-electoral-biometrique-les-experts-de-loif-et-de-la-cedeao-confirment-la-fiabilite-du-document/>).

**Open for checking.** [CENI said in January 2024 that an extract of each voter's entry can be consulted on its website](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>). [The extract gives the name and first name](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>). [Voters can see their full details at CENI offices](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [Each voter was enrolled in person, with fingerprints and a photo](<https://anp.ne/niger-le-fichier-biometrique-electoral-une-garantie-pour-des-elections-securisees-et-fiables-selon-la-ceni-par-mahamane-sabo-bachir/>).
