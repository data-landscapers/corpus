# Malawi

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Malawi Electoral Commission said in April 2025 that its Election Management System held 7,202,756 valid voter records](<https://mec.org.mw/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/20250408-Speech-for-press-briefing-on-Voter-Transfers-and-Inspection-of-Voters-Register.pdf>). [Fingerprint checks were used for voter transfers](<https://mec.org.mw/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/20250408-Speech-for-press-briefing-on-Voter-Transfers-and-Inspection-of-Voters-Register.pdf>). [It gave all 23 parties the electronic voters' register on 12 May 2025](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-outlines-election-preparations>). [It planned over 15,200 devices to check voters' fingerprints against a preloaded biometric database](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-outlines-election-preparations>). [A national biometric voter registration exercise ended on 11 December 2024](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/malawi-distributes-3m-national-id-cards-printed-in-country>).

**Open for checking.** [MEC held an inspection of the register at registration centres on 30 and 31 May 2026](<https://mec.org.mw/30-june-2026-by-election-and-fresh-election/>). [Voters could also check their entry by dialling *2509# on the TNM and Airtel networks](<https://mec.org.mw/30-june-2026-by-election-and-fresh-election/>). [In 2025, 479,115 voters checked at centres and 605,217 by the same code](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-outlines-election-preparations>). [The 2023 Elections Act limits inspection to verifying entries at centres and regional offices](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>).

**Linked to ID.** [Parliament made the national ID card the only proof of identity for voter registration](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>). [In July 2024 the registration bureau's principal secretary called the national ID the single source of truth for voter registration](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>).
