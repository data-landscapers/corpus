# Malawi

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voters' roll is under way. None says one is in service. [The Malawi Electoral Commission set out in June 2025 how the voters' roll may be inspected](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>). [Inspection is limited to verifying entries](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>).

**Open for checking.** [Sections 20 and 21 of the 2023 Elections Act let voters verify their entries](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>). [This takes place at registration centres and regional offices](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>). [The Commission's chief elections officer said so on 5 June 2025](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>). [The Act allows no other inspection of the roll](<https://africabrief.substack.com/p/mec-rejects-observer-coalitions-electronic>).

**Linked to ID.** [Parliament made the national ID card the only proof of identity for voter registration](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>). [In July 2024 the registration bureau's principal secretary spoke on this](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>). [He called the national ID the single source of truth for voter registration](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>).
