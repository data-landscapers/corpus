# Uganda

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Electoral Commission issued soft copies of the photo-bearing National Voters' Register to presidential candidates](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>). [It covers 21,681,491 voters at 50,739 polling stations](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>). [A December 2025 statutory instrument requires biometric voter verification at every polling station](<https://nadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Electoral-Commission-Adoption-and-Manner-of-Use-of-Biometric-Voter-Verification-System-Regulations-2025.pdf>). [Kits check fingerprints or faces against the register](<https://nadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Electoral-Commission-Adoption-and-Manner-of-Use-of-Biometric-Voter-Verification-System-Regulations-2025.pdf>). [The register is the basis for voting in the 2026 elections](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [The Commission says the National Voters Register was displayed in 2025](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>). [It urges the public to verify their details at ec.or.ug/register](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2025 register is the basis for voting in the 2026 elections](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>).
