# Uganda

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Electoral Commission says it issued soft copies of the photo-bearing National Voters' Register, covering 21,681,491 registered voters at 50,739 polling stations, to presidential candidates, and that the register is the basis for voting in the 2026 elections](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>). [Statutory Instrument 98 of 2025, commenced 24 December 2025, requires the Electoral Commission to use a biometric voter verification system at all polling stations, with kits that verify voters' particulars and fingerprint or face against the voters register](<https://nadvocates.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Electoral-Commission-Adoption-and-Manner-of-Use-of-Biometric-Voter-Verification-System-Regulations-2025.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [The Electoral Commission says the National Voters Register was displayed in 2025 and urges the public to use its official website ec.or.ug/register to verify voter registration particulars and polling station](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID or civil register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://ec.or.ug/sites/default/files/press/Statement%20by%20EC%20Chairperson%20General%20Election%20Updates.31.12.2025.pdf>).
