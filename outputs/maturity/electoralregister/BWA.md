# Botswana

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The 2026/27 budget gives the Independent Electoral Commission BWP 50 million for electronic voter registration systems](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/IEC.pdf>). [The work may span two or three financial years](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/IEC.pdf>). [The IEC said in June 2026 it is modernising registration before the 2029 election](<https://www.mmegi.bw/news/iec-pushes-for-digital-election-reforms/news>). [Under the Electoral Act the IEC compiles a new roll before each general election](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>).

**Open for checking.** [The IEC secretary said in September 2024 that the public may inspect each new roll for 80 days](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>). [Inspection is at the commission's office and at designated places such as District Commissioners' offices](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>). [Anyone may buy hard copies of the roll or parts of it](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>). [The IEC gives out no soft copies and does not publish the roll online](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The IEC said in 2024 it would consider putting the roll online once it complies with the Data Protection Act](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/81588>).
