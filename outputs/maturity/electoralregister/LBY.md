# Libya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [HNEC launched electronic voter-identity checks in municipal elections in August 2024](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [They began in four municipalities: Zliten, Al-Sbiaa, Brak and Al-Abrak](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [Devices record voters' biometric data when they collect their cards](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [HNEC's Decision 253 of December 2025 sets up registration by text message and in person](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2024 pilot checked voters' identity when they collected their cards](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [Decision 253 adds registration by text message from December 2025](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/>).

**Linked to ID.** [Decision 253 defines updating the register as checking HNEC's databases against changes in the Civil Registry Authority's](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/>). [Applicants must hold a national number](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7/>). No source says the check has been carried out.
