# Libya

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [HNEC launched electronic voter-identity checks in municipal elections in August 2024](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [They began in four municipalities: Zliten, Al-Sbiaa, Brak and Al-Abrak](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [Devices record voters' biometric data when they collect their cards](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [Libya Review reported the launch](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2024 pilot checked voters' identity at card collection](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [The checks verify the identity of each voter](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2024 devices captured voters' biometrics at card collection](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>). [HNEC is the High National Elections Commission](<https://libyareview.com/47455/libya-implements-biometric-verification-in-municipal-council-elections>).
