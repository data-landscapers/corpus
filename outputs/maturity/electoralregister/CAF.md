# Central African Republic

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The ANE prepared a biometric registration drive in 2024 with GenKey software and 3,000 enrolment tablets](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/central-african-republic-in-full-biometric-voter-registration-preparations>). [From 24 November to 24 December 2024 it enrolled 567,748 voters in its first area](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/02/07/centrafrique-lautorite-nationale-des-elections-presente-les-resultats-de-revision-du-fichier-electorale-dans-laire-n-1/>). [The data went to the production server of the electoral file](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/02/07/centrafrique-lautorite-nationale-des-elections-presente-les-resultats-de-revision-du-fichier-electorale-dans-laire-n-1/>).

**Open for checking.** [The ANE published the definitive list of 2,398,158 voters on 23 August 2025](<https://french.xinhuanet.com/20250825/216678a5ca894a1d915b2f5528ff0973/c.html>). [During the 2024 drive the president checked that his name was on the list](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2024 drive verified the details of citizens registered in 2020](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years>). [It deployed 1,469 registration agents](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years>).
