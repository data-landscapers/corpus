# Central African Republic

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The ANE enrolled 567,748 voters on tablets in Aire 1 from 24 November to 24 December 2024 and centralised the data on the production server of the electoral file](<https://oubanguimedias.com/2025/02/07/centrafrique-lautorite-nationale-des-elections-presente-les-resultats-de-revision-du-fichier-electorale-dans-laire-n-1/>). [A biometric voter registration exercise supplied by GenKey was ongoing, with 3,000 enrolment tablets and 1,469 registration agents deployed, per the ANE](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years>). [The ANE was preparing a biometric voter registration drive to revise the electoral register, with GenKey software and hardware and at least 3,000 biometric enrolment tablets received](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/central-african-republic-in-full-biometric-voter-registration-preparations>).

**Open for checking.** [The ANE officially published the definitive electoral list of 2,398,158 registered voters on 23 August 2025](<https://french.xinhuanet.com/20250825/216678a5ca894a1d915b2f5528ff0973/c.html>). [The exercise verifies the details of citizens registered in 2020, and the president checked whether his name was on the electoral list](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/central-african-republic-enrols-citizens-for-first-local-elections-in-36-years>).
