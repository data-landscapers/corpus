# Sao Tome and Principe

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The roll for the presidential election of 19 July 2026 was compiled automatically from civil registry data](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [The provisional roll held 146,510 voters](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [The Universidade de Aveiro built the platform under the EU-funded PReSE project](<https://rstp.st/2025/09/02/stp-encerra-o-projeto-de-reforma-do-sistema-eleitoral-financiado-pela-uniao-europeia/>). [It was presented in public on 4 September 2025](<https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/sobre/comunicacao/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-projeto-prese-apoia-a-modernizacao-do-recenseamento-eleitoral>).

**Open for checking.** [The provisional roll was open for consultation from 1 to 8 August 2026](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [Voters could see it at district, regional and diaspora electoral commissions](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [It was also online on the CEN website](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>).

**Linked to ID.** [A new electoral registration law took effect after its publication on 26 March 2026](<https://rstp.st/2026/04/01/eleicoes26-lei-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-entra-em-vigor/>). [It enters citizens in the electoral database at 18 from civil identification data](<https://rstp.st/2026/04/01/eleicoes26-lei-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-entra-em-vigor/>). [The platform links the civil register database with the electoral register database](<https://rstp.st/2025/09/04/apresentada-plataforma-de-recenseamento-automatico-de-sao-tome-e-principe-que-esta-98-pronta/>).
