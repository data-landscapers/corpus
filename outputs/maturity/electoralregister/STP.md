# Sao Tome and Principe

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The electoral roll for the 19 July 2026 presidential election was compiled automatically from civil registry (DGRN) data under the EU-funded PRESE, and the provisional roll holds 146,510 voters, per the CEN president](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [The National Data Protection Agency will supervise personal data during the connection between the civil registry and notary databases and the CEN database for the automatic electoral registration for the 2026 elections, per its president](<https://www.telanon.info/politica/2026/04/15/52530/recenseamento-eleitoral-automatico-vai-ser-fiscalizado-pela-agencia-nacional-de-proteccao-de-dados-pessoais/>). [A platform for automatic and permanent voter registration is being developed under the EU-funded PReSE with the Universidade de Aveiro, and the electoral database is being consolidated, per the project coordinator](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>).

**Open for checking.** [The provisional roll is open for public consultation from 1 to 8 August 2026 at district, regional and diaspora electoral commissions and online on the CEN website, per the CEN president](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The roll for the 19 July 2026 presidential election was compiled automatically from data of the civil registry directorate (DGRN) for the first time](<https://rstp.st/2026/07/31/eleicoes26-cen-lanca-campanha-de-consulta-e-atualizacao-de-dados-com-mais-de-146-mil-eleitores-inscritos/>). [A campaign is under way to consolidate the electoral registration database using interoperability with the civil registry databases, says the PReSE coordinator](<https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/sao-tome-e-principe-pronto-para-um-novo-sistema-de-recenseamento-eleitoral/>).
