# Mozambique

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Commonwealth Observer Group says the CNE has used biometric voter registration technology since 2008 and that 17,169,239 voters were registered for the 2024 elections](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [The Consorcio Eleitoral Mais Integridade observer report says the 2023 voter registration was carried out with mobile digital biometric kits (laptop, ten-finger scanner, camera, signature screen, card printer), used from field capture through data storage and compilation of the final rolls (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/cipeleicoes/CE_Relatorio_Relatorio-da-observacao-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-de-2023-Pt.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [The Commonwealth Observer Group says copies of the voter register are displayed where registration brigades operated so voters can scrutinise it and complain in writing, and that the final register was published on 4-9 July 2024](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>).
