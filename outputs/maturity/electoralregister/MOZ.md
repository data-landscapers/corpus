# Mozambique

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Commonwealth Observer Group says the CNE has used biometric voter registration since 2008](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [It registered 17,169,239 voters for the 2024 elections](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [Observers of the 2023 registration describe mobile biometric kits](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/cipeleicoes/CE_Relatorio_Relatorio-da-observacao-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-de-2023-Pt.pdf>). [The kits were used from field capture to the final rolls](<https://www.cipmoz.org/wp-content/uploads/cipeleicoes/CE_Relatorio_Relatorio-da-observacao-do-recenseamento-eleitoral-de-2023-Pt.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [Copies of the register are displayed where registration brigades worked](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [Voters can scrutinise them and complain in writing](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [The final register was published from 4 to 9 July 2024](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>). [The display let voters check the register before the 2024 elections](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2024 register of 17,169,239 voters was built with biometric registration](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-09/mozambique-cog-report-2024.pdf>).
