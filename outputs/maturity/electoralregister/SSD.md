# South Sudan

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The National Elections Commission chair said on 2 September 2026 that voter registration will be manual](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>). [Biometrics are ruled out](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>). [Records are to be typed into computers later](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>). [The original records are to be kept in state offices](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2026 plan keeps the paper originals in state offices](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>). [The chair spoke on 2 September 2026](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2026 plan is for manual registration without biometrics](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>).
