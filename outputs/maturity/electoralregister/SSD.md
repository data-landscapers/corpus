# South Sudan

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [South Sudan's National Elections Commission chairperson said on 2 September 2026 that voter registration will be manual, ruling out biometrics, with records later transferred to computers and the originals kept in state offices](<https://www.sudanspost.com/nec-rejects-biometrics-cites-low-literacy-in-voter-registration/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
