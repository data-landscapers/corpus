# Tunisia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voters' roll is under way. None says one is in service. [Organic law 2014-16 has the electoral body publish the electoral lists on its website](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2014-16-du-26-mai-2014-relative-aux-elections-et-aux-referendums/>).

**Open for checking.** [From 13 November 2023, voters could check their registration at voting centres and constituencies](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/11/13/isie-debut-derniere-periode-mise-jour/>). [They could use service 195 with their national ID card number, or SMS](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/11/13/isie-debut-derniere-periode-mise-jour/>). [The ISIE said 1.406 million Tunisians used SMS to check their polling place](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/11/13/isie-debut-derniere-periode-mise-jour/>). [From 20 May 2024 the ISIE ran a free SMS service for voters to check their polling centre](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2024/05/06/isie-actualisation-du-registre-electoral-a-partir-du-20-mai-courant/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2023 checks by phone and SMS ask for the national ID card number](<https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2023/11/13/isie-debut-derniere-periode-mise-jour/>).
