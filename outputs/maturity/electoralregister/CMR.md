# Cameroon

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ELECAM said in 2025 that it was deduplicating the biometric voter registry of about 8.2 million voters](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/cameroon-signals-urgency-on-biometric-voter-id-collection-as-presidential-vote-nears>). [It took delivery of 1,000 new biometric enrolment kits in May 2025](<https://lejour.cm/listes-electorales-elecam-acquiert-1000-nouveaux-kits-denrolement/>). [Thousands left out over fingerprint problems were added back to the file](<https://lejour.cm/listes-electorales-elecam-acquiert-1000-nouveaux-kits-denrolement/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [An opposition leader said thousands of names were removed in a December 2024 clean-up](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>). [ELECAM began its 2025 annual biometric registration cycle on 6 January](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2024 clean-up removed names over rejected fingerprints](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/cameroon-gabon-uganda-tackle-biometric-voter-registers-integrity-engagement>).
