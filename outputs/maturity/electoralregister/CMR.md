# Cameroon

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Le Jour reports that after the 2024 registration campaign ELECAM decided to integrate thousands of citizens, previously not recorded because of a fingerprint problem, into the national electoral file](<https://lejour.cm/listes-electorales-elecam-acquiert-1000-nouveaux-kits-denrolement/>). [ELECAM says it is deduplicating the biometric voter registry, which holds around 8.2 million registered voters, and has issued biometric voter cards (Biometric Update)](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202509/cameroon-signals-urgency-on-biometric-voter-id-collection-as-presidential-vote-nears>). [ELECAM presented 1,000 new third-generation biometric enrolment kits on 7 May 2025, with trainer training held 7 to 11 May before deployment to communal branches (Le Jour)](<https://lejour.cm/listes-electorales-elecam-acquiert-1000-nouveaux-kits-denrolement/>).
