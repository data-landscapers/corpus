# Madagascar

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Ceni's 2026 overhaul of the roll collects voter data mainly on paper forms](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608050202.html>). [Tablets are used only in a limited number of centres](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608050202.html>). [In October 2026 Ceni received USD 750,000 of IT equipment from Japan and UNDP to prepare a biometric list](<https://newsmada.com/2026/10/24/liste-electorale-biometrique-la-ceni-et-ses-partenaires-preparent-le-terrain/>). [The overhaul still runs on an alphanumeric system, with over 7.16 million registered against a target of 14 million](<https://newsmada.com/2026/10/24/liste-electorale-biometrique-la-ceni-et-ses-partenaires-preparent-le-terrain/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [A UNDP tender of 2026 covers servers and computers for Ceni's biometric data](<https://newsmada.com/2026/10/24/liste-electorale-biometrique-la-ceni-et-ses-partenaires-preparent-le-terrain/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The government's programme of November 2025 says it will continue digitalising the roll](<http://central.mfb.gov.mg/assets/file/accueil/2025/PMO-PGE_VF.pdf>).
