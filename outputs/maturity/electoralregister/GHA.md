# Ghana

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Electoral Commission certified the 2024 voters register at 18,774,159 valid voters](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ec-releases-summary-2024-certified-voters-register/>). [Of these, 18,640,811 have biometric data](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ec-releases-summary-2024-certified-voters-register/>). [Another 1,870 lack biometric data, and 131,487 are special voters](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ec-releases-summary-2024-certified-voters-register/>). [The Commission released the summary of the certified register in November 2024](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ec-releases-summary-2024-certified-voters-register/>).

**Open for checking.** [The Commission's director of electoral services said in August 2026 that the register is exhibited](<https://ghananewsonline.com.gh/ec-outlines-sweeping-reforms-to-strengthen-ghanas-electoral-system/>). [Voters then have the right to object to names on the roll](<https://ghananewsonline.com.gh/ec-outlines-sweeping-reforms-to-strengthen-ghanas-electoral-system/>). [He called the exhibition a critical stage of the register](<https://ghananewsonline.com.gh/ec-outlines-sweeping-reforms-to-strengthen-ghanas-electoral-system/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The certified 2024 register counts voters with and without biometric data](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/ec-releases-summary-2024-certified-voters-register/>).
