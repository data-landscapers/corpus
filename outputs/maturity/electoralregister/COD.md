# DR Congo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [CENI handed over the electoral file of voters enrolled in 2022, used in the December 2023 general elections, to ONIP on 19 February 2026, and the President said the enrolled voters' biometric data are available](<https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Open for checking.** No source held since October 2023 says voters could check the roll. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier>).

**Linked to ID.** No source held since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID or civil register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says voters could check the roll.
