# DR Congo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [CENI began collecting biometric data when enrolling voters in 2023, BBC News Afrique reports](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>). [In February 2026 CENI handed the electoral file of voters enrolled in 2022 to ONIP](<https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier>). [The President said the enrolled voters' biometric data are available](<https://actualite.cd/2026/02/19/identification-de-la-population-en-rdc-la-ceni-transmet-officiellement-le-fichier>).

**Open for checking.** [In December 2023 CENI said enrolled voters could use its mobile app to look up their polling station](<https://ouragan.cd/2023/12/une-application-mobile-mise-a-jour-pour-consulter-les-electeurs>). [They search with an 11-digit number or the QR code on their voter card](<https://ouragan.cd/2023/12/une-application-mobile-mise-a-jour-pour-consulter-les-electeurs>). [The voter lists were also displayed for consultation](<https://ouragan.cd/2023/12/une-application-mobile-mise-a-jour-pour-consulter-les-electeurs>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The link held runs the other way: ONIP, the identity office, draws on CENI's database](<https://www.bbc.com/afrique/articles/cy633p6ezq1o>).
