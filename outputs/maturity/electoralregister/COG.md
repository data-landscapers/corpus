# Congo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [L'Horizon Africain wrote in September 2025 that the revision of the electoral lists was done by hand](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). [The government had promised biometrics for the 2026 presidential election](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). [Diatou writes that the revision ran from September to October 2025](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [From 1 September to 30 October 2025 voters were called to check their names on the lists](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). [They did so at district and arrondissement town halls](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). [Those not listed could register there](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). [An interior ministry order of 8 August 2025 set the revision](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). A roll kept by hand does not count as open for checking.

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2025 revision ran at town halls without biometrics](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [NIU (numero d'identification unique)](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/administration-lancement-de-la-campagne-de-distribution-de-proximite-des-cni-et-niu-166913>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says a digital roll is planned or in service. None says the roll is checked against the ID register.
