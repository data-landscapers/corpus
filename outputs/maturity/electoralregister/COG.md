# Congo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [L'Horizon Africain's Jean-Clotaire Diatou writes that the September-October 2025 revision of the electoral lists is done manually, without the biometrics the government had promised for the 2026 presidential election](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step. 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Open for checking.** [L'Horizon Africain reports that from 1 September to 30 October 2025 Congolese of voting age are called to check their names on the electoral lists at district and arrondissement town halls and register if absent, under an interior ministry order of 8 August 2025](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID or civil register. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lhorizonafricain.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/302.pdf>).
