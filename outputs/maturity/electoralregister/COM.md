# Comoros

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [A UNDP project page says a biometric electoral file was built for the Comoros](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>). [It enrolled 70% of voting-age citizens](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>). [The voter list for the 2015 elections came from it](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>). [UNDP supported the building of the file](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>).

**Open for checking.** [The Francophonie electoral mission observed the legislative elections of 12 January 2025](<https://www.lexterieur.com/la-une/international/legislatives-aux-comores-la-declaration-preliminaire-de-la-mission-electorale-de-la-francophonie>). [It reports that voter lists were posted outside every polling station it visited](<https://www.lexterieur.com/la-une/international/legislatives-aux-comores-la-declaration-preliminaire-de-la-mission-electorale-de-la-francophonie>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The UNDP page gives the biometric file's coverage in 2015 as 70% of voting-age citizens](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>). [The voter list of 2015 was drawn from the biometric file](<https://www.undp.org/fr/comoros/projets/projet-dappui-la-modernisation-du-fichier-electoral-de-lunion-des-comores>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a digital or biometric voter register is procured, piloted or in service.
