# Egypt

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The National Elections Authority said in September 2025 that it had finished updating the voter database](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [Law 198/2017 tasks the Authority with preparing the voter database and keeping it clean](<https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/NEAlaw2017-198_en.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [Voters can check their electoral status in the Authority's new mobile app or on its website](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [They enter their 14-digit national number](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [The app is on Google Play and the App Store](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [The Authority announced the completed update on 19 September 2025](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>).

**Linked to ID.** [The 2025 update reviewed voter entries and linked each to the national number](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [Its aim was to prevent duplicates and errors](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [The 2017 law bases the voter database on national identification number data](<https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/NEAlaw2017-198_en.pdf>). [The 2017 law also has the Authority keep the database updated and cleaned](<https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/NEAlaw2017-198_en.pdf>).
