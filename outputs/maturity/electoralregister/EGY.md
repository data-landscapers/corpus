# Egypt

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [Egypt's National Elections Authority announced it had officially completed updating the voter database](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Open for checking.** [The National Elections Authority says voters can check their electoral status through its new mobile app (Google Play, App Store) or its website by entering the 14-digit national number](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>).

**Linked to ID.** [The National Elections Authority says the update included reviewing voter entries and linking them to the national number to prevent duplicates or errors](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8137356>). [Law 198/2017 tasks the National Election Authority with preparing the voter database based on national identification number data and keeping it updated and cleaned (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/NEAlaw2017-198_en.pdf>).
