# Eswatini

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The EBC said in October 2026 that over 15,000 people had registered to vote electronically](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/15-000-emaswati-registered-as-ebc-urges-youth-disabled-to-participate/>). [Registration runs by mobile phone and at registration corners](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/15-000-emaswati-registered-as-ebc-urges-youth-disabled-to-participate/>). [In September 2026 it said continuous registration uses computers and biometric systems ahead of the 2028 elections](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/biometric-technology-set-to-strengthen-voter-registration/>). [The biometrics are meant to prevent duplicate registration](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/biometric-technology-set-to-strengthen-voter-registration/>).

**Open for checking.** [The EBC sends a preliminary voters' register to the chiefdoms for validation](<https://www.elections.org.sz/2023/electoral-process/>). [Registered voters there can verify their details](<https://www.elections.org.sz/2023/electoral-process/>). [A copy of the register is kept for a set period](<https://www.elections.org.sz/2023/electoral-process/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [Registration at Mbangweni uses biometric technology, the EBC said in 2026](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/imbali-register-to-vote-ahead-of-elections-at-mbangweni/>).
