# Benin

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ANIP says it is in charge of the electoral file and that the electoral list is available in a few clicks](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Open for checking.** [ANIP says its USSD channel is used massively for consulting and transferring electors](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** [ANIP says a death recorded in the RNPP automatically removes the person from the electoral lists](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
