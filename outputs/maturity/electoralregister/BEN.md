# Benin

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ANIP says it is in charge of the electoral file and that the electoral list is available in a few clicks](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [ANIP says the computerised provisional electoral list (LEIP) is displayed in all voting centres from 13 to 28 September 2025](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>).

**Open for checking.** [ANIP says its USSD channel is used massively for consulting and transferring electors](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [ANIP says voters can check their voting centre on the displayed list, by phone (15110#, option 4) or the Anipbenin.bj app using their NPI, and request corrections or transfers](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>).

**Linked to ID.** [ANIP says a death recorded in the RNPP automatically removes the person from the electoral lists](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [ANIP says the list is extracted directly from the Fichier National de l'État Civil, the database also used for national identity cards](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>).
