# Benin

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ANIP displayed the computerised provisional electoral list in all voting centres in September 2025](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [ANIP said in May 2026 that it is in charge of the electoral file](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Electoral lists are generated automatically from the national register of persons, the RNPP](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** [The provisional list was on display from 13 to 28 September 2025](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [Voters could check their voting centre by phone or in the Anipbenin.bj app, using their NPI](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [They could also ask for corrections or transfers](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [ANIP said in 2026 that its USSD channel is used heavily to consult and transfer voters](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Linked to ID.** [ANIP says the list is extracted directly from the Fichier National de l'État Civil](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [The same database issues national identity cards](<https://beninwebtv.bj/affichage-de-la-liste-electorale-provisoire-lanip-lance-la-phase-de-verification/>). [A death recorded in the RNPP removes the person from the electoral lists automatically](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-2-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).
