# Mali

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The AIGE trained 54 agents on 26 January 2026 on Elector mobile, the central software for the electoral file](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [It covers removing, transferring and validating voters](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [The lists are revised each year from 1 October to 31 December](<https://bamada.net/fichier-electoral-lheure-du-grand-nettoyage>). [The 2026 revision opened on 1 October](<https://bamada.net/fichier-electoral-lheure-du-grand-nettoyage>).

**Open for checking.** [Under the amended electoral law the new list is posted at the commune, embassy or consulate at least seven days before the vote](<https://lessor.ml/posts/loi-electorale-les-articles-52-57-et-70-modifies-677641a527512>). [Errors are corrected after computer processing of rectification tables](<https://lessor.ml/posts/loi-electorale-les-articles-52-57-et-70-modifies-677641a527512>).

**Linked to ID.** [Bamada.net reported in October 2026 that the lists are drawn from the biometric civil-status database](<https://bamada.net/fichier-electoral-lheure-du-grand-nettoyage>). [The national identification number, NINA, is each voter's unique identifier](<https://bamada.net/fichier-electoral-lheure-du-grand-nettoyage>). [The amended law enrols by right citizens holding a NINA or held in that database with photo and fingerprints](<https://lessor.ml/posts/loi-electorale-les-articles-52-57-et-70-modifies-677641a527512>). [A copy of each list goes to the service that manages the database](<https://lessor.ml/posts/loi-electorale-les-articles-52-57-et-70-modifies-677641a527512>).
