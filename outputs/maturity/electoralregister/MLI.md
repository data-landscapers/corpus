# Mali

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The AIGE trained 54 agents on 26 January 2026 on the software Elector mobile, described as the central tool for the fichier electoral, covering the radiation, transfer and validation of voters](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
