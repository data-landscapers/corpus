# Mali

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The AIGE trained 54 agents on 26 January 2026 on its Elector mobile software](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [It describes the software as the central tool for the electoral file](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [It covers removing, transferring and validating voters](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [The AIGE is Mali's independent election management authority](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>). [The software is called Elector mobile](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2026 training covered the electoral file's own procedures](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The Elector software validates voters within the AIGE's own file](<https://malijet.com/elections-presidentielles-et-legisliatives-au-mali/307835-mali--l-aige-forme-ses-agents-sur-le-logiciel--elector-mobile-.html>).

***Also on file***. [www.ralemali.ml](<https://mali24.info/mali-la-revision-annuelle-des-listes-electorales-de-2025-prevue-du-1er-octobre-au-31-decembre/>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says voters could check the roll.
