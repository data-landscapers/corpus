# Cote d'Ivoire

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Independent Electoral Commission says the definitive 2024-2025 roll holds 8,727,431 voters](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [It is a biometric file](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [The Commission says its biometrics stop remaining flaws from affecting the vote](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [The definitive roll was published on 2 June 2025](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>).

**Open for checking.** [The definitive roll could be consulted from 4 June 2025 on the Commission's website](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [Voters could also check by SMS with the codes *919# or #919# on any mobile network](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [Consultation opened two days after the roll was published](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The Commission published the definitive biometric roll in June 2025](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>). [It gave the roll's size as 8,727,431 voters](<https://cei.ci/point-de-presse-relatif-a-larret-de-la-liste-electorale-definitive-2025/>).
