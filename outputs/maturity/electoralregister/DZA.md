# Algeria

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ANIE's president said in December 2023 that digitising registration had removed nearly a million duplicate entries from the electoral file](<https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2023/12/07/charfi-affirme-une-parfaite-maitrise-du-fichier-electoral/>). [The removals ran from 2021 to 2023](<https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2023/12/07/charfi-affirme-une-parfaite-maitrise-du-fichier-electoral/>). [The electorate stood at 23,240,286 when the revision closed on 5 December 2023](<https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2023/12/07/charfi-affirme-une-parfaite-maitrise-du-fichier-electoral/>). [In 2024 he said the file had been digitised in 2019 and is updated at each revision](<https://www.lejourdalgerie.com/charfi-annonce-le-plan-de-developpement-strategique-de-lanie-2022-2024-la-revision-exceptionnelle-des-listes-electorales-entamee/>). [ANIE launched a digital platform for revising the lists in Oran in October 2025, as a pilot for the whole country](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The Oran platform of 2025 is for revising and tracking the lists](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2023 clean-up removed duplicates within the electoral file itself](<https://www.lexpressquotidien.dz/2023/12/07/charfi-affirme-une-parfaite-maitrise-du-fichier-electoral/>).
