# Algeria

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ANIE launched a digital platform for revising and tracking the electoral lists on 27 October 2025](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>). [Oran is the pilot site](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>). [The platform is to be extended nationwide](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>). [The interim president, Karim Khalfan, launched it](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>). [The platform tracks changes to the electoral lists](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The Oran platform of 2025 is for revising and tracking the lists](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>). [Ouest Tribune reported the launch](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [ANIE's interim president launched the pilot in Oran](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/processus-electoral-lanie-enclenche-la-mutation-numerique-a-oran/>).
