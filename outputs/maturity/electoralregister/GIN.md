# Guinea

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Direction générale des élections gave the final figures of the permanent biometric electoral file in August 2025](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/08/guinee-67-millions-delecteurs-inscrits-conakry-et-kankan-concentrent-presque-la-moitie/>). [It held 6,768,458 voters, including those abroad](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/08/guinee-67-millions-delecteurs-inscrits-conakry-et-kankan-concentrent-presque-la-moitie/>). [The minister of territorial administration listed the biometric file among the achievements of 2025](<https://matd.gov.gn/plfi-2026-le-matd-passe-devant-la-commission-des-lois-au-cnt/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [In January 2026 the DGE and the civil registration office signed a convention on the biometric file](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [It aims to make the file more reliable](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The convention makes the national register of persons the single official source of voter identification data](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [A jurist writes that the registration drive launched in April 2025 built that register](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/04/en-avant-pour-le-recensement-biometrique-par-sayon-mara/>). [Its purpose was to extract the electoral file for the referendum and elections](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/04/en-avant-pour-le-recensement-biometrique-par-sayon-mara/>).
