## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The DGE and ONECI signed a convention on 22 January 2026 to strengthen the reliability of the existing biometric electoral file (fichier électoral biométrique), per Mediaguinee](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [The Direction générale des Élections presented the definitive figures of the permanent biometric electoral file, 6,768,458 voters including those abroad](<https://mediaguinee.com/2025/08/guinee-67-millions-delecteurs-inscrits-conakry-et-kankan-concentrent-presque-la-moitie/>).
