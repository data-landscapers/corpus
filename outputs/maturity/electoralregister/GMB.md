# Gambia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The IEC opened a supplementary registration on 8 April 2026 with its own registration software and equipment](<https://gambiaj.com/news/politics/elections/iec-announces-start-of-supplementary-voter-registration-ahead-of-2026-elections/>). [Teams worked at 730 centres, adding voters to the 2021 register](<https://gambiaj.com/news/politics/elections/iec-announces-start-of-supplementary-voter-registration-ahead-of-2026-elections/>). [The IEC said in July 2026 that it holds custody of the voter database, with technical support from the contractor ESI](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/iec-says-canadian-contractor-provides-technical-support-not-control-of-voter-data/>). [Askani Gambia reports 14,334 multiple registrations found in the biometric system](<https://askanigambia.com/gambia-iec-revising-courts-open-voter-register-disputes-as-election-credibility-comes-under-scrutiny/>).

**Open for checking.** [The IEC displayed the provisional voters' list at 730 registration centres from 7 July 2026](<https://standard.gm/revising-courts-to-hear-1600-objections-after-voter-registration/>). [Voters could check their details and lodge corrections, appeals and objections](<https://standard.gm/revising-courts-to-hear-1600-objections-after-voter-registration/>). [It published the full list under the Elections Act 2025](<https://standard.gm/iec-extends-scrutiny-period-of-provisional-voter-list/>). [The objections period was extended to 27 July](<https://standard.gm/iec-extends-scrutiny-period-of-provisional-voter-list/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The multiple registrations of 2026 were found in the IEC's own biometric system](<https://askanigambia.com/gambia-iec-revising-courts-open-voter-register-disputes-as-election-credibility-comes-under-scrutiny/>).
