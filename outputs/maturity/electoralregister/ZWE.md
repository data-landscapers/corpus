# Zimbabwe

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [A 2025 report says the Zimbabwe Electoral Commission keeps biometric voter databases](<https://privacyscorecardreport.com/privacy-scorecard-report-2025/zimbabwe/zimbabwes-data-protection-regime/>). [In August 2026 voter registration and roll management began passing to the Registrar-General](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/>). [The handover includes biometric registration kits and network infrastructure](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/>). [Continuous voter registration carries on](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/>). [The Civil Registry Department and ZEC began the handover together](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/rg-zec-begin-nationwide-handover-of-voter-registration-mandate/>).

**Open for checking.** [In August 2025 the electoral commission told voters in by-election wards to check their registration online or by the *265# code on NetOne](<https://bulawayo24.com/index-id-news-sc-national-byo-255854.html>). [It said it displays the voters' roll two days before polling](<https://bulawayo24.com/index-id-news-sc-national-byo-255854.html>).

**Linked to ID.** [The minister said ZEC already uses the Registrar-General's national population database](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>). [It uses it to clean up the voters' roll](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>). [ZEC uses the database to see who is of voting age](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/registrar-general-to-take-over-voters-roll-under-constitutional-reforms/>).
