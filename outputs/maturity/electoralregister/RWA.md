# Rwanda

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The High Commission in Nigeria said in May 2024 that ID holders aged 18 or over are on the voters' list automatically](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [Each voter's polling place is taken from where the ID was requested](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [The High Commission addressed Rwandans living in Nigeria](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>).

**Open for checking.** [Voters abroad can check where they are registered on the National Electoral Commission's site](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [They can also change their polling station there](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [They sign in with their ID number and mother's name](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [The site is amatora.nec.gov.rw](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>).

**Linked to ID.** [Everyone aged 18 or over with a Rwandan national ID card is registered automatically](<https://www.rwandainnigeria.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=101349&t=f&token=beb4565a7b4f20efef3e58d831d4f6d89bc46150>). [International IDEA reported in 2018 that the register is compiled from the civil registry](<https://www.idea.int/node/126474>). [Organic Law 004/2018 has it updated from civil registry information](<https://www.idea.int/node/126474>).
