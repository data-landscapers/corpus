# Togo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [ZETES and SNEDAI trained data-entry operators on biometric kit software in March 2025](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>). [The kits were for enrolling voters in CENI's 2025 revision of the electoral lists](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>). [Some 5,200 operators were to be deployed](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>). [ATOP reported the training](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2025 revision was to enrol voters with biometric kits](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>). [The revision of the lists was CENI's work for 2025](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2025 training covered enrolment software only](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>). [ZETES and SNEDAI supply the kits' software](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>).
