# Togo

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [An OIF audit mission judged Togo's biometric electoral register reliable in November 2023](<https://togoscoop.tg/23015-2/>). [It counted 4,432,936 voters after the 2023 census](<https://togoscoop.tg/23015-2/>). [A decree of 1 April 2025 opened a revision of the file from 7 to 23 April](<https://togopresse.tg/la-revision-des-listes-electorales-annoncee-du-07-au-23-avril-2025-en-prelude-aux-elections-municipales/>). [CENI issued biometric voter cards in that revision](<https://togopresse.tg/la-revision-des-listes-electorales-annoncee-du-07-au-23-avril-2025-en-prelude-aux-elections-municipales/>). [ZETES and SNEDAI trained data-entry operators on biometric kits for it](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The OIF audit of 2023 covered the register for the regional and legislative polls](<https://togoscoop.tg/23015-2/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [The 2025 training covered enrolment software only](<https://atop.tg/revision-des-listes-electorales-la-premiere-vague-des-operateurs-de-saisie-en-formation/>).
