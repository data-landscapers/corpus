# Seychelles

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** No source found says a digital voters' roll is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [The Electoral Commission of Seychelles says voters can use its new Online Voter Registration system at ecs.sc to check their registration status or register, transfer, correct, change name or object](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/26766/ecs-encourages-voters-to-register-online>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Open for checking.** [The Electoral Commission of Seychelles says voters can use its new Online Voter Registration system at ecs.sc to check their registration status or register, transfer, correct, change name or object](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/26766/ecs-encourages-voters-to-register-online>). [The Electoral Commission of Seychelles FAQ says voters can check their name on the register by SMS of their NIN to 9600, on www.ecs.sc, or at the nearest registration centre](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The Electoral Commission of Seychelles FAQ says it receives a monthly list of deceased from the Department of Immigration and Civil Status and removes those names from the voters' register at certification, and that the NIN is used to check the register](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from or checked against the ID or civil register.
