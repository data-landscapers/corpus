# Seychelles

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Electoral Commission opened an online voter registration system on ecs.sc in July 2025](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/26766/ecs-encourages-voters-to-register-online>). [Voters use it to check their status, transfer, correct details or object](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/26766/ecs-encourages-voters-to-register-online>). [The system also lets voters register, transfer, change names or object](<https://www.nation.sc/articles/26766/ecs-encourages-voters-to-register-online>).

**Open for checking.** [Voters can check their name by sending their NIN by SMS to 9600](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>). [They can also check on www.ecs.sc or at the nearest registration centre](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>). [The online and SMS checks need the voter's NIN](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>).

**Linked to ID.** [The Commission receives a monthly list of the dead from the Department of Immigration and Civil Status](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>). [It removes those names when the register is certified](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>). [The national identity number is used to check the register](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>). [The FAQ says the names come off at certification](<https://ecs.sc/faq/>).
