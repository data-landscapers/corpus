# Guinea-Bissau

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [GTAPE handed the National Elections Commission the updated rolls on 18 August 2026](<https://ang.gw/referendo-30-de-agosto-gtape-entrega-cadernos-eleitorais-a-cne/>). [They listed 966,152 voters, in physical and electronic versions](<https://ang.gw/referendo-30-de-agosto-gtape-entrega-cadernos-eleitorais-a-cne/>). [GTAPE's director-general said in 2025 that the voters are held in its databases](<https://ang.gw/eleicoes-gerais-gtate-anuncia-inscricao-de-966-152-eleitores-para-as-legislativas-e-presidenciais-de-novembro/>). [The 2025 figure was for the elections of 23 November 2025](<https://ang.gw/eleicoes-gerais-gtate-anuncia-inscricao-de-966-152-eleitores-para-as-legislativas-e-presidenciais-de-novembro/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2026 rolls were for the referendum of 30 August](<https://ang.gw/referendo-30-de-agosto-gtape-entrega-cadernos-eleitorais-a-cne/>).

**Linked to ID.** No source since October 2023 says the roll is drawn from the national ID, population or civil register. None says entries are checked against any of them. [GTAPE's databases held 928,187 voters a year before 2025](<https://ang.gw/eleicoes-gerais-gtate-anuncia-inscricao-de-966-152-eleitores-para-as-legislativas-e-presidenciais-de-novembro/>).
