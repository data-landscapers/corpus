# Electoral register: electoral register

*As at 2026-11-03.*

## The norm

The African Charter on Democracy, Elections and Governance and the AU's election observation guidelines ask for an accurate voters' roll, open to public inspection and kept up to date. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| Stage | Answers |

### Togo: 3 Established

The voters' roll was held digitally in 2025; voters checking it is not on record.

- An OIF audit mission judged the biometric electoral register, with 4,432,936 voters counted after the 2023 census, reliable enough for the regional and legislative polls ([source, 2023-11-15](https://togoscoop.tg/23015-2/)).

### Madagascar: 2 Nascent
