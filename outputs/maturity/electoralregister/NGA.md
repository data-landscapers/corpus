# Nigeria

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [INEC first used its Bimodal Voter Accreditation System nationwide in the 2023 general elections](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/nigeria-tests-upgraded-biometric-voter-system-ahead-of-2027-elections>). [It matches voters' fingerprints or faces against the register](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/nigeria-tests-upgraded-biometric-voter-system-ahead-of-2027-elections>). [In October 2026 INEC ran automated biometric checks over the preliminary register](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/inec-blames-registration-officers-for-ai-generated-photos-in-voters-register/>). [About 1.2 million invalid entries were purged, leaving over 103 million voters](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/02/inecs-ai-photo-problem-runs-deeper/>).

**Open for checking.** [INEC put the preliminary register on its website on 26 October 2026](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/inec-blames-registration-officers-for-ai-generated-photos-in-voters-register/>). [The public could view it and report missing details](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/inec-blames-registration-officers-for-ai-generated-photos-in-voters-register/>). [It was also displayed at ward centres from 9 to 15 October](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/02/inecs-ai-photo-problem-runs-deeper/>). [By November 2026 hundreds of thousands of claims and objections had been received, an INEC source said](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/11/inec-in-eye-of-the-storm-electoral-body-battles-to-clean-voters-register-investigation/>).

**Linked to ID.** [INEC's acting chairman said in September 2026 that INEC works with NIMC, the identity agency](<https://thecabal.ng/inec-integrates-nin-to-clean-national-voter-register-ahead-2027-polls/>). [Through its registration portal it has integrated National Identification Numbers](<https://thecabal.ng/inec-integrates-nin-to-clean-national-voter-register-ahead-2027-polls/>). [They are used to audit, verify and clean the register](<https://thecabal.ng/inec-integrates-nin-to-clean-national-voter-register-ahead-2027-polls/>).
