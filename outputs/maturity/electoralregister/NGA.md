# Nigeria

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [On 5 October 2026 INEC chairman Joash Amupitan said the final cleanup of the voters' register with the Automated Biometric Identification System was complete](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/nigeria-voters-register-now-stands-at-103-million-inec/>). He put the national register at more than 103 million voters. [On 30 October 2026 INEC's ICT director, Lawrence Bayode, said the system had been run on the preliminary register](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/inec-blames-registration-officers-for-ai-generated-photos-in-voters-register/>). It found irregular entries. [Continuous registration for the 2027 polls added over 11 million people, and biometric checks purged about 1.2 million invalid entries](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/02/inecs-ai-photo-problem-runs-deeper/>).

**Open for checking.** [INEC uploaded the preliminary register of voters to its website on 26 October 2026](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/inec-blames-registration-officers-for-ai-generated-photos-in-voters-register/>). The public could view it there and complain of missing details. [INEC also displayed the register at ward centres from 9 to 15 October 2026, TechCabal reports](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/02/inecs-ai-photo-problem-runs-deeper/>). [On 5 October 2026 Amupitan said a public display of the preliminary register for claims and objections had been completed](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/nigeria-voters-register-now-stands-at-103-million-inec/>).

**Linked to ID.** [In September 2026 acting INEC chairman Rhoda Gumus said INEC had integrated National Identification Numbers into the voter register](<https://thecabal.ng/inec-integrates-nin-to-clean-national-voter-register-ahead-2027-polls/>), working with NIMC. She said they are used to audit, verify and clean the register.
