# Ethiopia

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [NEBE launched its Mirchaye voter registration system on 5 January 2026](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>). [At selected polling stations, officers register voters on the Board's tablets](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>). [The remaining polling stations keep manual registers](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>). [NEBE says nearly 5.5 million people obtained voter cards digitally, with paper used where networks failed](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/05/31/ballots-beyond-glitches-ethiopia-prepares-to-chart-its-next-five-years/>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [NEBE's launch notice sets out four ways to register for the 2026 general election](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>).

**Linked to ID.** [NEBE's chair spoke in November 2025 of integrating voter registration with the Fayda digital ID to prevent duplication](<https://www.dpgethiopia.org/news/dpg-heads-of-agencies-convene-to-advance-ethiopias-election-preparedness/>). [At the January 2026 launch, Fayda data fed self-registration on the web and by app](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>). [Those channels run only at selected polling stations](<https://nebe.org.et/en/node/1222>).
