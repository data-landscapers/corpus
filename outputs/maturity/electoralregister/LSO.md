# Lesotho

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The Independent Electoral Commission issued an open tender in May 2024 for an Electoral Management Information System to register electors, and awarded the contract to Waymark Infotech, which Laxton contests in the High Court; the IEC says the current register dates from 2001](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>). [UNDP reports the IEC registers voters through Mobile Registration Devices and an outsourced Voter Registration Management System, with 1,388,207 voters on the roll as of September 2023 (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2024-01/web-site_article_on_vr_conference.pdf>).

**Open for checking.** No source found since October 2023 says voters could check the roll.
