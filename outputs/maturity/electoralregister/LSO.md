# Lesotho

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The IEC re-registered voters nationwide from 2024, at its offices and through an online portal](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [It told an AU mission in July 2026 that more than 579,520 citizens had registered on its modernised system](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>). [In September 2026 it hired a private auditor to examine the roll, its servers and its registration systems](<https://lestimes.com/?p=91669>). [In 2024 it said the register then in use dated from 2001](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2024 contract for a new electoral management system went to Waymark Infotech](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>).

**Linked to ID.** [The IEC told the AU mission in July 2026 that its registration system is integrated with the National Identity and Civil Registry](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>). [The link runs through each person's unique identification number](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36187>). [The re-registration accepts the national identity card as the only form of identification](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the roll was open to voters to check. Online, text-message and centre checks were looked for.
