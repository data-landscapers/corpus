# Lesotho

## Electoral register

**Digital roll.** [The IEC said in October 2024 that it had begun re-registering every voter nationwide](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [Voters register at its offices or through an online portal](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [At least 18,570 had registered by mid-October, about 3,000 of them online](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [In 2024 the IEC also tendered for an electoral management information system](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>). [It said the register then in use dated from 2001](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>).

**Open for checking.** No source since October 2023 says the roll was published. None says voters could check their entries online, by text message or at centres. [The 2024 contract for the new system went to Waymark Infotech](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>). [A rival bidder, Laxton, contested the award in the High Court](<https://lestimes.com/?p=85440>).

**Linked to ID.** [The re-registration accepts the national identity card as the only form of identification](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [This applies also to voters first registered on other documents](<https://publiceyenews.com/2024/10/16/800-000-ghost-voters-unearthed/>). [In 2022 observers were told the electronic registration system was linked to the ID card](<https://comsec-web-static.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2025-11/lesotho-cog-report.pdf?VersionId=AI6Cyoaji4.SNee7u4P.jSHT5RJQn.NL>).
