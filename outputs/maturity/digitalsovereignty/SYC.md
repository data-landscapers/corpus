# Seychelles

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Seychelles' Cabinet [approved on 28 October 2026](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026>) the building of a sovereign Government Cloud inside the country. It is to run on two datacentres in separate places. The aim is to rely less on commercial and overseas infrastructure. No law, decree or policy in force is recorded that classifies government data or says where it must be held.

**Hosted nationally.** No government data or core system is recorded as hosted in a government or nationally owned datacentre. The nearest held fact is the plan itself. The Cabinet's [October 2026 decision](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026>) puts the Government Cloud on two datacentres in the country.

**Contract terms.** No government contract with a foreign provider is recorded with terms on where data is held, moving it or leaving. The [Cabinet's decision](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7318/cabinet-business-wednesday-28-october-2026>) names no provider.
