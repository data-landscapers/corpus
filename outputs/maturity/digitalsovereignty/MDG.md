# Madagascar

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Madagascar's digital minister [named a sovereign national data centre for public data](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601200231.html>) among the ministry's priorities in January 2026. Draft Bill 40 of 2026, PL-040/2026, [defines digital sovereignty](<https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/postponement-of-vote-on-cybercrime-and-digital-communications-bills-which-raise-online-civic-space-concerns/>) as state surveillance and control of critical data and infrastructure, Eu SEE reports. In September 2026 the National Assembly [postponed the vote](<https://eusee.hivos.org/alert/postponement-of-vote-on-cybercrime-and-digital-communications-bills-which-raise-online-civic-space-concerns/>) on the bill, together with the vote on a cybercrime bill.
