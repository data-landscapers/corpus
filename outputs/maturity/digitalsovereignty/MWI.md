# Malawi

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Department of e-Government, through the PPPC with World Bank DIGMAP funding, reported the Government Data Centre in Lilongwe 77 percent complete, to consolidate government-wide systems under one roof with Blantyre as backup, due December 2024](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/government-data-centre-project-completion-at-77/>). [The UN Economic Commission for Africa's digital trade regulatory profile of Malawi states that Malawi has local storage requirements only for health-related data, naming no instrument (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Malawi-%20ENG.pdf>).

**Hosted nationally.** [The request for bids says the Government of Malawi's Department of e-Government runs the National Data Centre in Lilongwe, which hosts systems of ministries, departments and agencies, and is being expanded for government institutions' demand over at least five years](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>).
