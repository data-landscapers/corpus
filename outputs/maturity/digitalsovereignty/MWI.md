# Malawi

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Department of e-Government reported](<https://digmap.pppc.mw/government-data-centre-project-completion-at-77/>) the Government Data Centre in Lilongwe 77 percent complete, due December 2024. The work goes through the PPPC with World Bank DIGMAP funding. The centre is to consolidate government-wide systems under one roof, with Blantyre as backup. In 2022 [the UN Economic Commission for Africa's profile of Malawi](<https://www.uneca.org/sites/default/files/ATPC/cp/Digital%20trade%20regulatory%20integration%20-%20Country%20profile%20-%20Malawi-%20ENG.pdf>) stated that Malawi has local storage requirements only for health-related data. It named no instrument.

**Hosted nationally.** [A request for bids says](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>) that the Department of e-Government runs the National Data Centre in Lilongwe. It hosts systems of ministries, departments and agencies. It is being expanded to meet government institutions' demand over at least five years.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://api.pppc.mw/api/download/722>).
