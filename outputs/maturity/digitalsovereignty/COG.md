# Congo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Congo's national data centre in Brazzaville [is meant to keep strategic state data](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>) in the country and cut dependence on foreign hosting, the ministry says. Sika Finance reported in September 2026 that the state's new data centre [will let Congo host, process and secure all its public and civil data](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/congo-le-nouveau-datacenter-national-livre-dici-la-fin-de-l-annee-2026_64496>) on its own territory. In June 2026 the UN Economic Commission for Africa [ran workshops](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>) with the ministry towards a national data governance strategy for digital sovereignty.

**Hosted nationally.** [The ministry's programme page says the Data Center National hosts, stores and backs up critical data and applications of public institutions on Congolese soil](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>).
