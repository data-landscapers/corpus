# Congo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The ministry page says the Data Center National is meant to keep strategic state data in the national territory and reduce dependence on foreign hosting platforms](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>). [The UN Economic Commission for Africa, with GIZ and the EU and the Ministry of Posts, Telecommunications and Digital Economy, ran consultations and workshops on 2-9 June 2026 to support development of a National Data Governance Strategy for digital sovereignty](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>). [Adiac reports the national data centre, 95% built, is intended to let Congo store, host and process all its digital data and applications in-country and guarantee sovereignty](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/construction-du-datacenter-la-societe-en-charge-des-travaux-menace-dabandonner-le-chantier>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Hosted nationally.** [The ministry's programme page says the Data Center National hosts, stores and backs up critical data and applications of public institutions on Congolese soil](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>).
