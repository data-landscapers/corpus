# Congo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Congo's digital economy ministry says the national data centre in Brazzaville [is meant to keep strategic state data](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>) in the country. It is also meant to cut dependence on foreign hosting. In June 2026 the UN Economic Commission for Africa [ran workshops](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>) with the ministry. They worked towards a national data governance strategy for digital sovereignty.

**Hosted nationally.** The ministry says the Data Center National is to [host the critical data of public institutions](<https://numerique.gouv.cg/actualites/data-center-national>) on Congolese soil. In February 2026 the African Development Bank's president [said it would open by May 2026](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-le-datacenter-national-operationnel-dici-mai-2026-selon-le-president-de-la-bad/>). In September 2026 Sika Finance reported it would be [delivered by the end of 2026](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/congo-le-nouveau-datacenter-national-livre-dici-la-fin-de-l-annee-2026_64496>).

**Contract terms.** No government contract with terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit has been found since October 2023. The nearest fact is the [African Development Bank's financing](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/congo-le-datacenter-national-operationnel-dici-mai-2026-selon-le-president-de-la-bad/>) of the data centre, which costs about EUR 66.55 million.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. Sources disagree on whether the national data centre hosted public data by September 2026. The newest says it was not yet delivered. No rule in force on state data and no contract with data terms has been found.
