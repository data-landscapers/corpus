# Senegal

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Seneweb reports](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html>) that in August 2026 the National Assembly unanimously adopted bill n° 25/2026. It enshrines the principle of keeping data sensitive for the State on national territory. In September 2026 the Presidency [confirmed a proposed Cybastion partnership](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/09/cybastion-targets-300-million-investment-to-strengthen-senegals-digital-sovereignty/>) that includes a planned sovereign data centre, with financing not finalised. In October 2026 the manager of the Diamniadio national data centre [said 10 to 20 per cent of public data is still held abroad](<https://senego.com/donnees-publiques-jusqua-20-des-donnees-senegalaises-encore-hebergees-hors-du-pays_2015891.html>). On 4 November 2026 the digital and health ministries [said they are building an infrastructure to host patients' health data](<https://www.seneplus.com/article/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-vers-la-creation-dune-structure-pour-securiser-les>), with no timetable.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud.
