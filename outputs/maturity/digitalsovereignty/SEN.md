# Senegal

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** MFWA reported in 2024 that [a policy in force since 2021 requires state agencies to transfer their data to the Diamniadio national data centre](<https://mfwa.org/impact-stories/senegal-state-agencies-comply-with-policy-on-public-data-management-after-mfwa-fellows-report/>). In August 2026 the National Assembly [unanimously adopted bill n° 25/2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html>). It enshrines keeping data sensitive for the State on national territory. In September 2026 the Presidency [confirmed a proposed Cybastion partnership](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/09/cybastion-targets-300-million-investment-to-strengthen-senegals-digital-sovereignty/>) that includes a planned sovereign data centre.

**Hosted nationally.** State agencies [have moved their data to the Diamniadio centre since November 2023](<https://mfwa.org/impact-stories/senegal-state-agencies-comply-with-policy-on-public-data-management-after-mfwa-fellows-report/>), MFWA reports. The health ministry had reportedly moved over 80% of its data by January 2024. In October 2026 the centre's manager [said 10 to 20 per cent of public data is still held abroad](<https://senego.com/donnees-publiques-jusqua-20-des-donnees-senegalaises-encore-hebergees-hors-du-pays_2015891.html>). On 4 November 2026 two ministries [said they are building an infrastructure to host patients' health data](<https://www.seneplus.com/article/protection-des-donnees-personnelles-vers-la-creation-dune-structure-pour-securiser-les>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact is the [planned Cybastion partnership](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/09/cybastion-targets-300-million-investment-to-strengthen-senegals-digital-sovereignty/>), whose financing was not final in September 2026.
