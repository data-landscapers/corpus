# Senegal

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Senegal's Presidency confirmed a proposed Cybastion partnership](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/09/cybastion-targets-300-million-investment-to-strengthen-senegals-digital-sovereignty/>) that includes a planned sovereign data center. The centre would host and manage critical digital systems within Senegal's borders. The partnership is to mobilise $300 million, and its financing is not finalised. [Seneweb reports](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html>) that the National Assembly unanimously adopted bill n° 25/2026, by 127 votes to none. The bill enshrines the principle of keeping data considered sensitive for the State on national territory. In March 2026 the Prime Minister [said the most sensitive state data will be hosted on national territory, under state control](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>). In October 2026 the manager of the Diamniadio national data centre [said 10 to 20 per cent of public data is still held abroad](<https://senego.com/donnees-publiques-jusqua-20-des-donnees-senegalaises-encore-hebergees-hors-du-pays_2015891.html>). Senegal means to bring it home, by ending hosting contracts early or letting them run out.
