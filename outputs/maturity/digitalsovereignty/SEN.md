# Senegal

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Senegal's Presidency confirmed a proposed Cybastion partnership, with $300 million to be mobilised and financing not finalised, that includes a planned sovereign data center to host and manage critical digital systems within Senegal's borders](<https://panafricanvisions.com/2026/09/cybastion-targets-300-million-investment-to-strengthen-senegals-digital-sovereignty/>). [Seneweb reports the National Assembly unanimously adopted bill n° 25/2026, which enshrines the principle of keeping data considered sensitive for the State on national territory (127 votes in favour, none against)](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Politique/securite-numerique-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-a-lunanimite-le-projet-de-loi-sur-les-infrastructures-critiques_n_501981.html>). In March 2026 the Prime Minister [said the most sensitive state data will be hosted on national territory, under state control](<https://aps.sn/ousmane-sonko-prone-une-coordination-renforcee-pour-reussir-la-transformation-digitale-de-letat/>).
