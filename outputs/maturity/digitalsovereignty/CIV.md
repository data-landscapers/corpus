# Cote d'Ivoire

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** The Ministry of Digital Transition's [national data governance strategy SNGD 2030](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>) sets a principle for data classified as critical information infrastructure. Such data must be processed and stored inside Côte d'Ivoire, by staff at least 20% Ivorian. A copy must be kept in the country for law enforcement. In December 2025 the digital minister [said the national data centre at VITIB Grand-Bassam will host the State's strategic data](<https://www.ivoirematin.com/fr/news/Societe/grand-bassam-ibrahim-kalil-konate-met-la-pression-pour-accelerer-le-chantier-du-data-center-national_n_125095.html>). It was then over 20% complete, at a cost of CFA 42.4 billion.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>).
