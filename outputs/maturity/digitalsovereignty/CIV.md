# Cote d'Ivoire

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** The Ministry of Digital Transition's [national data governance strategy SNGD 2030](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>) sets a principle for data classified as critical information infrastructure. Such data must be processed and stored inside Côte d'Ivoire, by staff at least 20% Ivorian. A copy must be kept in the country for law enforcement. In December 2025 the digital minister [said the national data centre at VITIB Grand-Bassam will host the State's strategic data](<https://www.ivoirematin.com/fr/news/Societe/grand-bassam-ibrahim-kalil-konate-met-la-pression-pour-accelerer-le-chantier-du-data-center-national_n_125095.html>). It was then over 20% complete, at a cost of CFA 42.4 billion. In July 2026 Digital Mag [reported the centre due by the end of 2026](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>).

**Hosted nationally.** In July 2026 Digital Mag [counted five public data centres run by ANSUT that host sensitive state data](<https://digitalmag.ci/stockage-de-donnees-les-10-datacenters-en-cote-divoire-pour-un-debut-de-souverainete-numerique/>). The Presidency has one of them. The health ministry's IT directorate [hosts its patient records only in the State's national data centre](<https://disd.ci/forum-club-sante-2026-la-disd-fixe-le-cap-de-lintelligence-artificielle-en-cote-divoire/>). They cover over 2 million files from more than 541 facilities.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest fact: [the 2024 data governance strategy says critical data must be processed and stored in Côte d'Ivoire](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>).
