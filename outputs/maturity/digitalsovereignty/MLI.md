# Mali

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The government presented Mali Kura Biométrie as a sovereign system keeping the RAVEC identity data under national control, with the president calling it the starting point of Mali's digital sovereignty](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Hosted nationally.** [ITU states the AGETIC, a government agency under the ministry MCENMA, runs a data centre providing hosting (IaaS, SaaS, backup) to the public administration, with a real-time-replicated DR site; the document gives the year only (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>).
