# Mali

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** On 13 February 2024 [the government presented Mali Kura Biométrie](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>) as a sovereign system keeping the RAVEC identity data under national control. At the presentation, the president called it the starting point of Mali's digital sovereignty.

**Hosted nationally.** In 2022 [ITU stated](<https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-d/opb/pref/D-PREF-THEM.29-2022-PDF-F.pdf>) that AGETIC, a government agency under the ministry MCENMA, runs a data centre for the public administration. It provides infrastructure and software as a service, and backup, with a real-time-replicated disaster recovery site.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [AGETIC's data centre, which in 2022 hosted the public administration's services with a real-time-replicated disaster recovery site](<https://bamada.net/donnees-administratives-le-mali-recupere-ses-fichiers-ravec>).
