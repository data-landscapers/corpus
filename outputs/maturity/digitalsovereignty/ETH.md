# Ethiopia

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Digital Policy Alert records that Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024, adopted by Parliament on 4 April 2024, entered into force on 24 July 2024 and requires data controllers and processors to store locally collected personal data on servers in Ethiopia](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>). In December 2025 the innovation ministry [said the Digital Ethiopia 2030 strategy rests on a sovereign national cloud and strict enforcement of data sovereignty laws](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8021907>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>).

**Contract terms.** No source held since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12100-data-localisation-requirements-in-personal-data-protection-proclamation-proclamation-no-13212024>).
