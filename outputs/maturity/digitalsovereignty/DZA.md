# Algeria

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Presidential decree 25-320 of 31 December 2025 sets five levels of data classification, from public to top secret](<https://almostathmir.dz/%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7/>). Data at the top level may not be exchanged, and by February 2026 ministries were applying it. At the first interministerial meeting on the AI strategy, [participants stressed hosting administrative data in national data centres](<https://www.jeune-independant.net/intelligence-artificielle-et-souverainete-numerique-lheure-de-laction-a-sonne/>). [El Watan reports a second data centre is planned in Blida](<https://elwatan.dz/tebboune-inaugure-a-mohammadia-le-premier-centre-national-algerien-des-services-numeriques/>) for the national cloud and the interoperability platform. In late 2025 the government [announced](<https://www.algerie360.com/fin-du-calvaire-administratif-letat-finalise-la-plateforme-tout-en-un-dzair-services/>) and [reviewed](<https://www.algerie62.dz/le-gouvernement-examine-projets-notamment-la-transformation-numerique/>) plans for a first national cloud for ministries' systems.

**Hosted nationally.** [Ech-Chaab cites the High Commission's record from April 2024 to September 2026](<https://www.ech-chaab.com/new/2026/09/09/%D8%AB%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AB-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A/>). It lists a sovereign cloud, the secured government network and national data centres among delivered milestones. The national digital services centre opened on 5 July 2026.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ech-chaab.com/new/2026/09/09/%D8%AB%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AB-%D8%B3%D9%86%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%85-%D9%85%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A/>).
