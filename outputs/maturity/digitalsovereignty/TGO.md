# Togo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** No source found since October 2023 says control of state data is a stated goal or a rule in force. The nearest held fact concerns the national ID. A Togolese delegation said in October 2025 that [its data is all hosted in the country](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/30/senegal-and-togo-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-identity-systems/>).

**Hosted nationally.** At talks with Senegal on digital identity in October 2025, the Togolese delegation said [all Togolese identity data is hosted in national infrastructure](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/30/senegal-and-togo-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-identity-systems/>). The national electronic identification platform is built on MOSIP. No source names the data centre that hosts it.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact is [the October 2025 statement on hosting identity data](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/30/senegal-and-togo-strengthen-cooperation-on-digital-identity-systems/>).
