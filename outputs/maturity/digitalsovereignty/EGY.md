# Egypt

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Digital Policy Alert records](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>) that the Supreme Council of Digital Society adopted Egypt's Cloud First Policy on 13 March 2024. It entered into force on 22 August 2024. It requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted exclusively within Egypt. [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology says](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>) its digital sovereignty strategy includes a nationally developed biometric verification and e-signature system. The system was built locally with national expertise, so the state owns its core digital components. [On 3 November 2026 the chair of the House ICT committee said the government would submit a bill within weeks](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4400426>). It would govern how data is classified and shared between government sectors.

**Hosted nationally.** President El-Sisi [opened the Government Data and Cloud Computing Center](<https://www.sis.gov.eg/Story/192611/President-El-Sisi-Witnesses-Inauguration-of-Government-Data,-Cloud-Computing-Center?lang=en-us>) on 28 April 2024. The State Information Service says it analyses government data. It runs critical and payment applications and backs up the New Capital Data Center.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact is the opening of the [Government Data and Cloud Computing Center](<https://www.sis.gov.eg/Story/192611/President-El-Sisi-Witnesses-Inauguration-of-Government-Data,-Cloud-Computing-Center?lang=en-us>) in April 2024.
