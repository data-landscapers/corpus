# Egypt

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Digital Policy Alert records](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>) that the Supreme Council of Digital Society adopted Egypt's Cloud First Policy on 13 March 2024. It entered into force on 22 August 2024. It requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted exclusively within Egypt. [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology says](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>) its digital sovereignty strategy includes a nationally developed biometric verification and e-signature system. The system was built locally with national expertise, so the state owns its core digital components.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>).
