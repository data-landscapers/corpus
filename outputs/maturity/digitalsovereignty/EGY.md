# Egypt

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Digital Policy Alert records that Egypt's Cloud First Policy, adopted by the Supreme Council of Digital Society on 13 March 2024, entered into force on 22 August 2024 and requires data classified top secret or secret to be hosted exclusively within Egypt](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>). [Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology says the state's strategy for digital transformation and strengthening digital sovereignty includes a nationally developed biometric verification and e-signature system, built locally with national expertise so the state owns its core digital components](<https://www.elmydannews.com/251837>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/change/12346-data-localisation-requirement-in-cloud-first-policy-2024>).
