# Botswana

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Mmegi reports that the Data Protection Act, in effect since January 2025, allows data transfers abroad only to approved countries and requires a copy of the data to remain in Botswana](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>). [Communications and Innovation Minister David Tshere said the whole government system is being migrated to a secure government cloud at the Delta Digital Data Centre in Gaborone, certified in 2025](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/93395>). In April 2026 BoFiNet's chief executive [said government had decided to move its critical services to the Digital Delta Data Centre](<https://businessweekly.co.bw/news/bofinet-pushes-for-local-data-hosting-to-strengthen-security>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mmegi.bw/business/govt-moves-to-in-house-local-data/news>).
