# Libya

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** The Central Bank of Libya's Circular No. 18 [prohibits storing personal, financial, credit or sensitive data outside Libya](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>). It binds banks, foreign bank branches, fintechs, payment providers, exchange and leasing companies from 1 July 2026. Cabinet Decision 358 of 2026 [set up an authority](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d9%85%d8%ac%d9%84%d8%b3-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%88%d8%b2%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%a1-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-358-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-2026-%d9%85-%d8%a8%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a5%d9%86%d8%b4/>) to prepare national digital sovereignty policies and classify national data. It is also to regulate the hosting of national data and the government cloud, and to control data transfers abroad.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>).
