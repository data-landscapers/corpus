# Libya

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Central Bank of Libya issued Circular No. 18 whose data protection regulation states it is prohibited to store personal, financial, credit or sensitive data outside Libya, binding on banks, foreign bank branches, fintechs, payment providers, exchange and leasing companies from 1 July 2026](<https://informatics.ly/en/session/personal-data-protection-regulation-session/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
