# Zimbabwe

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [POTRAZ, as the designated Data Protection Authority, states in its implementation guidelines that it will not permit highly sensitive information such as core banking platforms and critical financial systems to be hosted outside Zimbabwe and that such systems must be hosted locally](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>). In May 2026 the ICT minister [announced a Zimbabwe Government Cloud and proposed a statutory instrument requiring all critical government data to be hosted in Zimbabwe](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>).

**Hosted nationally.** [President Mnangagwa said the Zimbabwe Centre for High Performance Computing (ZCHPC) national HPC data center at the University of Zimbabwe, run by ZCHPC as custodian of national HPC infrastructure, already hosts the Ministry of Health and Child Care's Impilo Electronic Health Records and Telemedicine platform, and the Civil Registry is integrated with it](<https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/>). In January 2025 the ICT minister [said government email and ministry websites run on the government's national cloud and data centre](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-to-boost-online-services-with-new-high-tech-data-centre/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit.
