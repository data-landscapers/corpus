# Zimbabwe

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [POTRAZ, the designated Data Protection Authority, states in its implementation guidelines](<https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20Act%20Implementation%20Guidlines.pdf>) that highly sensitive systems must be hosted locally. It will not permit core banking platforms and critical financial systems to be hosted outside Zimbabwe. In May 2026 the ICT minister [announced a Zimbabwe Government Cloud](<https://spikedmedia.co.zw/minister-mavetera-issues-five-landmark-directives-at-inaugural-ict-directors-workshop/>). The minister proposed a statutory instrument requiring all critical government data to be hosted in Zimbabwe.

**Hosted nationally.** [President Mnangagwa said](<https://developzim.co.zw/news/innovation/president-mnangagwa-commissions-phase-2-of-high-performance-computing-project-at-university-of-zimbabwe/3322/>) the national HPC data center at the University of Zimbabwe already hosts the Ministry of Health and Child Care's Impilo Electronic Health Records and Telemedicine platform. The Zimbabwe Centre for High Performance Computing (ZCHPC) runs it as custodian of national HPC infrastructure. The Civil Registry is integrated with it. In January 2025 the ICT minister [said government email and ministry websites run on the government's national cloud and data centre](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/govt-to-boost-online-services-with-new-high-tech-data-centre/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit.
