# Uganda

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** No source found says control of state data is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [the National Data Centre, run by NITA-U, hosted 305 applications from 100 entities](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>) by the close of the strategic cycle.

**Hosted nationally.** [The National Data Centre, run by NITA-U](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>), hosted a cumulative 305 applications from 100 entities by the close of the strategic cycle. The entities are ministries, departments, agencies and local governments. Only 40% of the 250-entity enrolment target was reached. In June 2024 [a World Bank paper counted 162 applications](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062725151016340/pdf/P171305-a853c0a8-853d-4074-a43a-72d44d653ff3.pdf>) hosted at the existing GovNet data center, against a target of 400.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-03/NITA-U%20Strategic%20Plan%20%28FY202425%E2%80%93FY202930%29.pdf>).
