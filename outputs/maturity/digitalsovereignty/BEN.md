# Benin

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Decree 2026-579 of 2 September 2026 created a National Digital Committee under the President, with protection of strategic data among its priorities and a mandate to give a prior opinion on technology partnerships that could engage national digital sovereignty](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidence-de-la-republique-un-comite-national-du-numerique-cree>). [Benin's digital agency ASIN, in a June 2026 bulletin, proposes a governance framework for generative-AI use in public administration, including sovereign hosting of data on national infrastructure or equivalent, contractual reversibility and full data return, and a ban on transfers to Cloud Act or FISA jurisdictions](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/usages-de-lia-dans-ladministration-publique-un-cadre-de-gouvernance-envisage>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.bj/actualites/presidence-de-la-republique-un-comite-national-du-numerique-cree>).
