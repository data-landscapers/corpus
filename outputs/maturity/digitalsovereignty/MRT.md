# Mauritania

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Al Wiam reports AN-ETAT briefed the World Bank vice president on the WARDIP-funded national government cloud project, including data-security testing and preparations to migrate government systems and data ahead of go-live](<https://alwiam.info/ar/51206>). [Agence Ecofin reports the ministry did not give a migration timetable for administrations' applications and databases to the new national cloud](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/3006-139750-la-mauritanie-se-dote-dune-infrastructure-cloud-pour-heberger-ses-donnees-publiques>). [Ecofin Agency reports the government launched a national cloud to gradually centralise government digital services, with no migration timeline announced](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>). In September 2024 the digital ministry [launched a tender for a government cloud](<https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-D/Regional-Presence/ArabStates/Documents/events/2024/MeaningfulConnectivity/Presentations/Session6/Pr%C3%A9sentation%20(Fr)_MrAmar_Mau.pdf>) under WARDIP.

**Hosted nationally.** [Elilam reports the national government cloud now hosts state services under full sovereignty, as stated at the digital ministry training opening](<https://www.elilam.net/index.php/ar/node/16321>). [Ecofin Agency reports Mauritania launched its national cloud on 29 June 2026 at the National Data Center in Nouakchott to host government data and digital public services domestically](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/3006-56943-mauritania-launches-national-cloud-to-strengthen-digital-sovereignty>). In July 2026 the digital minister [told the National Assembly that all government servers had been moved into a data centre the ministry built](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/263232>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.elilam.net/index.php/ar/node/16321>).
