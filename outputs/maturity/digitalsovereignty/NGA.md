# Nigeria

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Stakeholders at the GrowthX forum said the Central Bank of Nigeria's six-month deadline for full data localisation, January 2027, was too short and asked for an extension, treating the directive as issued; the article gives its own published date](<https://techtrends.africa/growthx-by-techeconomy-industry-leaders-identify-infrastructure-trust-as-keys-to-data-localisation/>). [Business AM reports the Central Bank of Nigeria's directive requires financial institutions and payment system participants (banks, mobile money operators, switching and processing companies, payment service providers) to store and manage payment transaction data generated in Nigeria on servers in the country from 1 January 2027](<https://businessamlive.com/data-localisation-opens-new-investment-window-for-nigerias-data-centres/>). In November 2025 the Head of the Civil Service [announced the adoption of the 1Gov cloud as a single platform for all ministries, departments and agencies](<https://nannews.ng/digitalisation-fg-adopts-1gov-cloud-platform/>), presented as part of a shift to sovereign digitalisation.

**Hosted nationally.** [NIMC says the national identity database is hosted entirely in Nigeria, maintained internally by its own staff, has never been hosted abroad and does not use cloud hosting for its core database (October 2026)](<https://guardian.ng/business-services/nimc-onboards-63-biometric-agents-reaffirms-local-hosting-of-national-database/>). [The Galaxy Backbone data centre in the National Shared Services Centre, Abuja, commissioned on 20 December 2022, serves government as well as corporate customers (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.thecable.ng/a-data-center-to-drive-the-digital-economy/>).

**Contract terms.** No source held since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techtrends.africa/growthx-by-techeconomy-industry-leaders-identify-infrastructure-trust-as-keys-to-data-localisation/>).
