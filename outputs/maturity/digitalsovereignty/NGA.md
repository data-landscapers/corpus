# Nigeria

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** The Central Bank of Nigeria's circular of 15 June 2026 [requires payment transaction data generated in Nigeria](<https://complyan.com/nigerias-cbn-data-localisation-directive-what-banks-and-fintechs-must-do-before-2027/>) to be stored and managed in the country. It binds banks, mobile money operators, switches and other payment operators from 1 January 2027. The Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority says the [National Cloud Policy 2025 mandates in-country hosting](<https://nsia.com.ng/kasi-cloud-datacenters-commissions-west-africas-first-hyperscaleready-ai-capable-data-centre-campus-in-lagos/>) for sensitive government and financial data. In October 2026 NITDA's Director-General said it [had issued the National Sovereign Cloud Regulatory Instrument](<https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/27/ncc-nitda-agf-national-assembly-demand-stronger-cybersecurity-measures-against-ai-driven-threats/>).

**Hosted nationally.** NIMC says the national identity database [is hosted entirely in Nigeria](<https://guardian.ng/business-services/nimc-onboards-63-biometric-agents-reaffirms-local-hosting-of-national-database/>) (October 2026). It says its own staff maintain it and that it has never been hosted abroad. The [Galaxy Backbone data centre in Abuja](<https://www.thecable.ng/a-data-center-to-drive-the-digital-economy/>) was commissioned on 20 December 2022. It serves government and corporate customers.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit.
