# Gabon

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Minister of Digital Economy announced on 9 June 2026](<https://www.gabonreview.com/hebergement-des-donnees-le-gabon-met-fin-a-sa-dependance-aux-serveurs-etrangers-avec-son-premier-datacenter/>) that the first national data centre would open on 30 June 2026. Gabonese firm ST Digital runs it, to keep data from public services and businesses on national territory. [Minister Doumba announced](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>) faster work on a high-security national data centre. The minister wants state and citizen data stored exclusively on national territory. [The Presidency states](<https://presidence.ga/2026/02/19/audience-accordee-a-ika-technology-acceleration-de-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-publique-et-consolidation-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>) that the Head of State called for national control and security of the state's strategic data. National skills are to be built to run critical digital infrastructure.
