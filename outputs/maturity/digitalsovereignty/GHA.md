# Ghana

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Communications Minister Sam George said at the ACR2 opening that Ghana has a localisation policy under which local data and traffic processed on clouds outside the country will have to be hosted in Ghana; the document gives no instrument name or compliance date](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Hosted nationally.** [The bidding document states that NITA's two national data centres, in Accra and Kumasi, operational over the past 10 years, serve as central hubs for the Government of Ghana's IT operations and provide data storage, with about 90% of capacity used; the Accra core network backbones all government network services](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>).
