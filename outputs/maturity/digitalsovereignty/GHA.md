# Ghana

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Communications Minister Sam George said at the ACR2 opening](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>) that Ghana has a localisation policy. Under it, local data and traffic processed on clouds outside the country will have to be hosted in Ghana.

**Hosted nationally.** [A bidding document](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>) states that NITA runs two national data centres, in Accra and Kumasi, operating for the past 10 years. They serve as central hubs for the Government of Ghana's IT operations and provide its data storage. About 90% of their capacity is used. The Accra core network is the backbone of all government network services.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>).

[Digital Realty ACR2 data center, Accra](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/digital-realty-opens-ghanas-largest-data-center-in-accra.html>); [NITA National Data Centres, Accra and Kumasi](<https://moc.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Final-Bidding_Document_for_Data_Center_Upgrade.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a law, decree or policy in force classifies government data or requires it to be held in the country. None says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit.
