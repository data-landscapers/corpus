# Tanzania

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Internet Governance Project says](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>) Tanzania has mandates for data localization and local ownership in place, and recommends revoking them. In August 2025 the Bank of Tanzania issued [Cloud Computing Guidelines for Financial Service Providers](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>). They bar a licensed provider from hosting a mission-critical system in a primary data centre or cloud outside Tanzania. The same applies to a system whose data the Bank deems critical. Neither covers state data. In August 2026 the minister [said government is extending open-source software across public institutions](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-expands-open-source-technology-use/>) so that it owns and controls its digital infrastructure.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 government was extending open-source software to control its digital infrastructure](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-expands-open-source-technology-use/>).
