# Tanzania

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The Internet Governance Project says Tanzania has mandates for data localization and local ownership in place, and recommends revoking the data localization mandates](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>). [The Bank of Tanzania's Cloud Computing Guidelines for Financial Service Providers, 2025 (issued under s.71 Banking and Financial Institutions Act 2006 and s.56(3) National Payment Systems Act 2015) bar a licensed financial service provider from hosting a mission-critical system, or one whose data the Bank deems critical, in a primary data centre or cloud whose hosting infrastructure is outside Tanzania; the document is dated August 2025](<https://www.bot.go.tz/webdocs/Feedback/en/2025082512452979.pdf>). In August 2026 the minister [said government is extending open-source software across public institutions so that it owns and controls its digital infrastructure](<https://dailynews.co.tz/govt-expands-open-source-technology-use/>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.internetgovernance.org/2026/06/09/tanzania-is-getting-online-its-digital-economy-isnt-following/>).
