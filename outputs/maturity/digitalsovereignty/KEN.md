# Kenya

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Implementation guidelines of the 2025 Kenya Cloud Policy](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>) were published by the Ministry of ICT and the Digital Economy. They require top secret State data to be held in a data centre within Kenya. State entities may not host data in their own server rooms without Cloud Adoption Committee authorisation. They have 24 months to migrate. In August 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary [said that under the policy public institutions will classify their data](<https://gaa.go.ke/ministry-urges-public-sector-adopt-ai-cloud-computing>) and buy cloud services only from accredited providers.

**Hosted nationally.** [The Konza National Data Centre](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>), through the Konza Cloud Platform, hosts 145 of Kenya's 451 government institutions. It also hosts national government systems as SaaS. It is planned to host the remaining ministries, departments, agencies and counties. In October 2026 the PPP Directorate [signed a funding agreement](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/ppp-directorate-signs-funding-agreement-for-konza-national-data-centre-cloud-expansion/>) for the [Konza cloud expansion project](<https://peopledaily.digital/business/konza-data-centre-set-for-expansion-through-public-private-partnership>), which it says will strengthen the country's sovereign cloud.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>).
