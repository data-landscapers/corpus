# Kenya

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Implementation guidelines of the 2025 Kenya Cloud Policy, published by the Ministry of ICT and the Digital Economy, require top secret State data to be held in a data centre within Kenya and bar State entities from hosting data in their own server rooms without Cloud Adoption Committee authorisation, with 24 months to migrate](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/corporate/technology/search-for-firm-to-host-sensitive-state-data-on-cloud-begins-5549208>). In August 2026 the ICT Cabinet Secretary [said that under the policy public institutions will classify their data and buy cloud services only from accredited providers](<https://gaa.go.ke/ministry-urges-public-sector-adopt-ai-cloud-computing>).

**Hosted nationally.** [The Konza National Data Centre, through the Konza Cloud Platform, hosts 145 of Kenya's 451 government institutions; it also hosts national government systems as SaaS and is planned to host remaining ministries, departments, agencies and counties](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>). In October 2026 the PPP Directorate [signed a funding agreement](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/ppp-directorate-signs-funding-agreement-for-konza-national-data-centre-cloud-expansion/>) for the [Konza cloud expansion project](<https://peopledaily.digital/business/konza-data-centre-set-for-expansion-through-public-private-partnership>), which it says will strengthen the country's sovereign cloud.

**Contract terms.** No source held since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pppkenya.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Updated-ADDENDUM-III-TRANSACTION-ADVISORY-SERVICES-FOR-THE-KONZA-CLOUD-EXPANSION-AND-SAMRT-CITY-FACILITIES.pdf>).
