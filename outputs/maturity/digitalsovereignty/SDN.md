# Sudan

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Sudan's Minister of Communications and Digital Transformation says the alternative national data centre project is meant to restore sovereign national infrastructure and secure and protect all government data](<https://nabdsudan.net/231651>). [The Sudanese Center for Policy Studies says mobile banking data transits infrastructure Sudan neither owns nor regulates, with no statutory requirement governing data localization or cross-border transfer; it only proposes localisation as a first legislative tranche](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>). [On 12 March 2026 the Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority launched the National Root Key for the country's public key infrastructure](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/news>), saying measures to secure communications between state institutions would follow.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nabdsudan.net/231651>).
