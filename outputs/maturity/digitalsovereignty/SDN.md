# Sudan

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Sudan's Minister of Communications and Digital Transformation says](<https://nabdsudan.net/231651>) the alternative national data centre project is meant to restore sovereign national infrastructure. It is to secure and protect all government data. [The Sudanese Center for Policy Studies says](<https://sudancps.com/en/2026/07/19/sudans-privacy-gap-balancing-digital-transformation-and-data-laws-intro/>) mobile banking data transits infrastructure Sudan neither owns nor regulates. No statute governs data localization or cross-border transfer, and the Center proposes localisation as a first legislative tranche. [On 12 March 2026 the Sudanese Cybersecurity Authority launched the National Root Key](<https://sca.gov.sd/en/news>) for the country's public key infrastructure. It said measures to secure communications between state institutions would follow.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 the minister planned an alternative national data centre for government data](<https://nabdsudan.net/231651>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest fact: [in October 2023 a former central bank official said government bodies worked under an undeclared localisation policy](<https://advox.globalvoices.org/2023/10/21/the-ongoing-conflicts-impact-on-ict-and-digital-transformation-efforts-in-sudan/>).
