# Mozambique

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The UNECA/GIZ/AU inception report says](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>) that Banco de Moçambique Notice 4/GGBM/2003 requires data centres to be located in Mozambique. Replicas abroad are allowed only with prior approval. INTIC's draft [Regulamento dos Servicos de Computacao em Nuvem](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>) went to public consultation on 24 February 2025. Its Art. 11(2) says classified information may be handled only in private clouds. Such information must always stay in national territory unless the regulator expressly authorises otherwise.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).
