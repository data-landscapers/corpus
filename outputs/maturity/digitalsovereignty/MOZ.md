# Mozambique

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The UNECA/GIZ/AU inception report says Banco de Moçambique Notice 4/GGBM/2003 requires data centres to be located in Mozambique, with replicas abroad allowed only with prior approval](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). [INTIC's draft Regulamento dos Servicos de Computacao em Nuvem, in public consultation from 2025-02-24, says in Art. 11(2) that classified information may be handled only in private clouds and must always stay in national territory unless the regulator expressly authorises otherwise](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/INTIC-Regulamento-dos-Provedores-de-Servic%CC%A7os-de-Computac%CC%A7a%CC%83o-em-Nuvem-23-12-2024-versa%CC%83o-consulta-pu%CC%81blica-1.pdf>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>).
