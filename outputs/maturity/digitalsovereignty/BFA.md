# Burkina Faso

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Burkina Faso's digital ministry has made control of infrastructure, platforms and data [a national priority](<https://www.sidwaya.info/la-souverainete-numerique-lautre-front-de-la-revolution/>). This is the 'Zero data abroad' first stage of its twelve digital projects. In January 2026 the Prime Minister [instructed that all state platforms and data](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2026/02/20/burkina-faso-zoom-sur-les-12-chantiers-strategiques-du-ministere-de-la-transition-digitale-focus-sur-le-chantier-zero-donnee-a-lexterieur-souverainete-des-donnees-via-des-datacenters-locaux/>) hosted privately or abroad be moved to the two national government datacentres. In March 2026 the ministry [planned to move and repatriate data](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article145231>) to the new national datacentres during the year. The AI roadmap for 2026 to 2030 [aims to host at least 80% of the data concerned](<https://lefaso.net/?id_article=150234&page=impression>) in the country. It keeps data and models under state control and ownership.

**Hosted nationally.** The government cloud portal is run by ANPTIC. It says the [G-Cloud has 4 datacentres and 8 cloud nodes](<https://www.cloud.gov.bf/>). It hosts 139 websites and 622 virtual machines. It runs government applications such as state messaging and SIGASPE. FasoSign, the national e-signature platform, was launched on 15 September 2026. Its keys, certificates and data [are hosted on national territory](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>) under the control of Burkinabe engineers.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cloud.gov.bf/>).
