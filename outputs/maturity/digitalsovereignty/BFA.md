# Burkina Faso

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [The ministry says it has made control of infrastructure, platforms and data a national priority under the 'Zero data abroad' first stage of its twelve digital transformation projects](<https://www.sidwaya.info/la-souverainete-numerique-lautre-front-de-la-revolution/>). [The ABDI executive director cited a national datacentre project planned for a 2028 horizon](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article148413>). [The ministry's secretary general said the government's twelve digital projects include building national data centres, and that the 'zero data abroad' vision is the basis for sovereign governance of administration, business and citizen data](<https://www.sidwaya.info/grand-salon-de-la-data-en-afrique-un-rendez-vous-de-promotion-de-la-souverainete-numerique/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Hosted nationally.** [The government cloud portal run by ANPTIC (Ministry of Digital Transition) states that the G-Cloud infrastructure, with 4 datacenters and 8 cloud nodes, hosts 139 websites and 622 virtual machines and runs government applications (state messaging, gov.bf directory, Oracle business applications such as SIGASPE and E-conseil des ministres)](<https://www.cloud.gov.bf/>). [The keys, certificates and data of FasoSign, the national e-signature platform launched on 15 September 2026, are hosted on national territory under the control of Burkinabe engineers](<https://cfinance.news/article/burkina-fasomodernisation-de-ladministration-publique-le-premier-ministre-lance-la-plateforme-fasosign-pour-boucler-la-boucle-de-la-dematerialisation/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.cloud.gov.bf/>).
