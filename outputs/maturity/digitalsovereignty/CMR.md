# Cameroon

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Le Jour reports](<https://lejour.cm/stragie-le-cameroun-mise-sur-la-souverainete-numerique/>) that the state, through Camtel, is building the Tier III Zamengoe data centre for a sovereign digital ecosystem. Its aim is to produce and preserve data generated on national territory. Camtel director general Judith Yah Sunday presented it at SAGO 2026. [Speakers at Camtel's Cloud Days of 10 December 2025](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>) recalled that Cameroon's personal data protection law imposes strict requirements on consent, local storage and handlers' responsibilities. That law covers personal data in general, not state data.

**Hosted nationally.** [CAMTEL said at the SAGO conference of 9 June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>) that its Zamengoe Data Centre is a critical national asset. It said the centre ensures the security, continuity and local control of government data.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>).
