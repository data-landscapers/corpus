# Cameroon

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [Le Jour reports that the state, through Camtel, is building the Tier III Zamengoe data centre as a tool for a sovereign digital ecosystem, with the aim of producing and preserving data generated on national territory, as presented by Camtel director general Judith Yah Sunday at SAGO 2026](<https://lejour.cm/stragie-le-cameroun-mise-sur-la-souverainete-numerique/>). [Speakers at Camtel's Cloud Days (10 December 2025) recalled that Cameroon's personal data protection law imposes strict requirements on consent, local storage and handlers' responsibilities](<https://lavoixdesentreprises.info/2025/12/11/digital-sovereignty-camtel-unveils-the-performance-of-its-zamengoe-datacenter-to-businesses-and-government-agencies/>).

**Hosted nationally.** [CAMTEL said at the SAGO conference of 9 June 2026 that its Zamengoe Data Centre is a critical national asset ensuring the security, continuity and local control of government data; the document does not list the specific systems hosted](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5885/camtel-highlights-digital-sovereignty-and-data-protection-at-government-action-fair-in-yaounde>).
