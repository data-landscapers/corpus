# Tunisia

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [G-Cloud labels for government cloud hosting](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>) were set by decree-law 2023-17 on cybersecurity and a ministerial decision of 13 September 2023. They go only to Tunisian hosting providers linked to the administration's network and the national interoperability platform. The first two were awarded in July 2024. The communications ministry [presents the labels as part of the 2025 digital strategy](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>), alongside the N-Cloud label for national providers. Their aim is to guarantee digital sovereignty and build a sovereign cloud offer.

**Hosted nationally.** The Centre National de l'Informatique, under the communications ministry, [hosts sensitive national and sectoral platforms on its own sovereign cloud](<https://www.thd.tn/tunisie-le-centre-national-de-linformatique-celebre-50-ans-au-coeur-de-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat/>), backed by secure data centres. It said so in January 2026, at its fiftieth anniversary.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the label rule given above](<https://www.webmanagercenter.com/2024/07/12/527554/tunisie-3s-et-eo-data-center-recoivent-le-label-g-cloud/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a rule in force classifies government data or requires it to be held in Tunisia. No government contract with data terms has been found.
