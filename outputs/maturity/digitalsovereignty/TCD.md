# Chad

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [ADETIC says the national data centre is to be audited and certified before it starts work](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>). It is to be the secure host for public data. [Chadian authorities told Ecofin the PMICE project is part of a digital sovereignty strategy](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51602-chad-completes-first-phase-of-205mln-digital-connectivity-project>). Its first phase, with a national data centre, was provisionally accepted on 20 December 2025. On 7 October 2026 the minister said [the draft data governance strategy aims at a sovereign national data system](<https://mten.gouv.td/discours-de-haliki-choua-mahamat-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees/>). The state is to know where its strategic data is hosted.
