# Chad

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [ADETIC says the national data centre is to be security-audited and certified by TECHSO-GROUP before official operation, as the secure host for public data and the state's digital services](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>). [Ecofin reports Chadian authorities say the PMICE forms part of a broader digital sovereignty strategy, with state control over fibre-optic networks and data storage capacity as a key lever, and that Phase I, including a National Data Center, was provisionally accepted on 2025-12-20](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51602-chad-completes-first-phase-of-205mln-digital-connectivity-project>).
