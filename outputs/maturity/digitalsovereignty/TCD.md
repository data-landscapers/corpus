# Chad

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [ADETIC says the national data centre is to be audited and certified before it starts work](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>), as the secure host for public data. Its first phase was [provisionally accepted on 20 December 2025](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2212-51602-chad-completes-first-phase-of-205mln-digital-connectivity-project>), within a project the authorities tie to digital sovereignty. On 7 October 2026 the minister said [the draft data governance strategy aims at a sovereign national data system](<https://mten.gouv.td/discours-de-haliki-choua-mahamat-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees/>). In November 2026 the strategy was reported [validated, with rules on local hosting and on classifying sensitive state data](<https://camerpressagency.com/la-strategie-nationale-de-transformation-digitale-en-gestation-le-tchad-amorce-son-grand-virage-numerique-avec-lappui-du-pnud/>). No source says it has been adopted.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>).
