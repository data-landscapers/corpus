# Chad

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [ADETIC says the national data centre is to be security-audited and certified by TECHSO-GROUP before official operation, as the secure host for public data and the state's digital services](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>). [The ministry says the national digital data centre (Centre National des Données Numériques), still being built under the PMICE project with Chinese partners, will give state and private institutions a secure setting to process and store their digital data](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-vers-la-garantie-dune-souverainete-numerique/>).

**Hosted nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://adetic.td/audit-et-certification-du-data-center-national-ladetic-lansice-et-techso-group-en-mission-conjointe/>).
