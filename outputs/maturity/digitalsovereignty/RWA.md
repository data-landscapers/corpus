# Rwanda

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [A 2025 market brief](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>) says Rwanda has no blanket localisation requirement, but some data must be stored locally depending on circumstances. Under the 2021 Law on Data Privacy and Protection, public sector data must be hosted in the government data centre. Facilities meeting Rwanda Development Board standards may host it instead. [Digital Policy Alert says](<https://digitalpolicyalert.org/digest/dpa-digital-digest-rwanda>) the 2017 National Data Revolution Policy requires critical government data to be hosted in a national data centre.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>).
