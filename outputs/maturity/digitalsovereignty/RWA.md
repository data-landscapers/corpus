# Rwanda

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [There is no blanket localisation requirement in Rwanda but some data must be stored locally depending on circumstances, and public sector data must be hosted in the government data center or in facilities meeting Rwanda Development Board standards, under the 2021 Law on Data Privacy and Protection](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Hosted nationally.** No source found since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cms.d4dhub.eu/assets/Initiatives/Data-Governance-in-Africa/Digital-Investment-Facility/2507_Country-Market-Briefs/Data-Center-Market-Brief-Rwanda.pdf>).
