# DR Congo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [A government official quoted in the ministry's own report of 24 July 2025 says the telecommunications law requires all public administration data to be hosted in the government data centre in Kinshasa-Gombe](<https://sgptntic.gouv.cd/transition-numerique-le-ministre-augustin-kibassa-maliba-visite-le-data-center-du-gouvernement/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Hosted nationally.** No source held since October 2023 says government data or a core system is hosted in a national data centre or cloud. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sgptntic.gouv.cd/transition-numerique-le-ministre-augustin-kibassa-maliba-visite-le-data-center-du-gouvernement/>).

**Contract terms.** No source held since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sgptntic.gouv.cd/transition-numerique-le-ministre-augustin-kibassa-maliba-visite-le-data-center-du-gouvernement/>).
