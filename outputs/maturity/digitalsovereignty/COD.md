# DR Congo

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** A government official is quoted in [the ministry's report of 24 July 2025](<https://sgptntic.gouv.cd/transition-numerique-le-ministre-augustin-kibassa-maliba-visite-le-data-center-du-gouvernement/>). The official says the telecommunications law requires all public administration data to be hosted in the government data centre. That centre is in Kinshasa-Gombe. In October 2026 the digital economy minister said the country was preparing to build a [sovereign data centre](<https://augustinkibassa.cd/2026/10/20/cooperation-rdc-russie-s-e-augustin-kibassa-maliba-mobilise-des-partenariats-a-moscou-pour-renforcer-la-souverainete-numerique-de-la-rdc/>). The plan includes a technopole in the Kinshasa extension zone.

**Hosted nationally.** [The Ministry of Social Affairs (MINAS) says](<https://minas.gouv.cd/registre-social-unique-rsu-un-levier-essentiel-pour-la-protection-sociale-en-rdc/>) the Registre Social Unique is a 100% sovereign system. Its data are hosted nationally, and the ministry has full control of infrastructure and source code.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://minas.gouv.cd/registre-social-unique-rsu-un-levier-essentiel-pour-la-protection-sociale-en-rdc/>).
