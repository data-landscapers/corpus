# Angola

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [President Lourenço says the National Cloud and Government Data Center have been structured to strengthen digital services and the security of Public Administration systems](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>). [Angola's national directorate for telecommunications and IT (Matias Borges) announced two new government data centres in Luanda (Camama and a modernised backup at the Centro Nacional de Dados, Rangel) to migrate existing and new government applications to a unified government cloud, a national cloud costing the State USD 89 million](<https://www.menosfios.com/vii-forum-telecom-angola-aposta-na-construcao-de-dois-centros-de-dados/>). In April 2025 the Secretary of State for Telecommunications said the [Government Cloud programme](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/04/15/sessao-tematica-aborda-programa-de-desenvolvimento-e-expansao-das-telecomunicacoes-e-tecnologias-de-informacao/>) puts first the migration of public administration services to a single government data cloud.
