# Angola

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Angola is moving government systems to its own cloud. In October 2026 [President Lourenço said the National Cloud and Government Data Center were built to secure public administration systems](<https://pti.ao/estado-da-nacao-presidente-destaca-progressos-nas-telecomunicacoes-e-transformacao-digital/>). [The national directorate for telecommunications announced two new government data centres in Luanda](<https://www.menosfios.com/vii-forum-telecom-angola-aposta-na-construcao-de-dois-centros-de-dados/>), at Camama and Rangel. They are to move government applications to one cloud, costing the State USD 89 million. In April 2025 the Secretary of State for Telecommunications said the [Government Cloud programme](<https://inacom.gov.ao/2025/04/15/sessao-tematica-aborda-programa-de-desenvolvimento-e-expansao-das-telecomunicacoes-e-tecnologias-de-informacao/>) puts public services first.

**Hosted nationally.** [President Lourenço opened the government's National Data Centre and Cloud at Camama on 28 April 2026](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>). Menos Fios reports that it hosts the data of public and private institutions. [The national PKI root is being installed there](<https://pti.ao/a-confianca-digital-em-angola-como-a-icp-angola-vai-mudar-o-dia-a-dia-dos-cidadaos-e-das-empresas/>), which INFOSI's director-general calls a sovereign base.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest fact is the opening of the [government cloud at Camama](<https://www.menosfios.com/data-centers-em-angola-quais-tem-certificacao-internacional/>) in April 2026.
