# Mauritius

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** In September 2025 the ICT ministry [issued a request for information for a sovereign cloud for the government](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Bidding-Document-RFI-for-Sovereign-Cloud-for-the-Government-of-Mauritius.docx>), to be governed by Mauritian law, under the government's full control and in a local data centre. In April 2026 the [Cabinet discussed a National Sovereign Cloud](<https://ionnews.mu/maurice-veut-lancer-un-national-sovereign-cloud-pour-proteger-ses-donnees/>) under the Digital Transformation Blueprint 2025-2029, to ensure data sovereignty and jurisdictional control.

**Hosted nationally.** [L'Express reports that Mauritius Telecom's Rose-Belle data centre hosts government projects, among them e-Health, Mobile ID and the government portal, as seen on a minister's visit on 29 December 2025](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lexpress.mu/node/552966>).
