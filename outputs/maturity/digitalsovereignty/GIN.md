# Guinea

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Guinea's [AI roadmap](<https://guineematin.com/2025/12/20/intelligence-artificielle-guinee-rapport-national-feuille-de-route/>) proposes a national data hub and a sovereign cloud. Consultant Emmanuel Elolo presented it at a validation workshop on 19 December 2025. He said state data are fragmented. No rule in force on where state data is held was found.

**Hosted nationally.** [Guineenews reports](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>) on the launch of the national Data Center on 4 September 2025. The Tier III centre covers 600 m2 and was built by COREX. The telecommunications minister said Guinea's strategic data now stays in Guinea under state control. It had been hosted abroad at over USD 5 million a year.

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>).
