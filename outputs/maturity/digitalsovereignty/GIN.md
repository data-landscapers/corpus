# Guinea

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** Guinea's [AI roadmap](<https://guineematin.com/2025/12/20/intelligence-artificielle-guinee-rapport-national-feuille-de-route/>) proposes a national data hub and a sovereign cloud. Consultant Emmanuel Elolo presented it at a validation workshop on 19 December 2025. He said state data are fragmented. No rule in force on where state data is held was found.

**Hosted nationally.** Guinea [launched its national Data Center on 4 September 2025](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>). It is a Tier III site of 600 m2 built by COREX. The telecommunications minister said the country's strategic data now stays in Guinea. Hosting it abroad had cost over USD 5 million a year. In April 2025 the civil registration director said [the national ID number is generated and kept in Guinea](<https://guineenews.org/2025/04/22/etat-civil-aucun-document-didentite-navait-de-base-legale-selon-djenabou-toure/>).

**Contract terms.** On 31 July 2025 the Belgian agency Enabel, with the EU, [handed the territorial administration ministry the source code of the civil registration database](<https://planete7.info/modernisation-de-letat-civil-en-guinee-enabel-remet-un-important-lot-de-materiel-informatique-aux-autorites-guineennes/>). The ministry's teams can now run and change the system themselves.

***Not found***. No source found says a law, decree or policy in force covers state data. None says state data must be held in the country.
