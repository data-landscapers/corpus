# Guinea

## Control of state data

**Rule on state data.** [At a 19 December 2025 validation workshop, consultant Emmanuel Elolo said Guinea's AI roadmap proposes a national data hub and a national sovereign cloud because state data are fragmented](<https://guineematin.com/2025/12/20/intelligence-artificielle-guinee-rapport-national-feuille-de-route/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Hosted nationally.** [Guineenews reports that at the 4 September 2025 launch of the national Data Center (Tier III, 600 m2, built and presented by COREX), the telecommunications minister said Guinea's strategic data now stays in Guinea under state control, instead of being hosted abroad at over USD 5 million a year; the document does not name which ministries' systems are moved](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>).

**Contract terms.** No source found since October 2023 says a government contract carries terms on data jurisdiction, portability or exit. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://guineenews.org/2025/09/04/guinee-le-ministre-djiba-diakite-procede-au-double-lancement-du-point-gn-et-le-data-center-national-tier-lll/>).
