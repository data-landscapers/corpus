# Malawi

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** A preprint reports that an [AI tool transcribing photographed HIV testing registers reached 853 of 867 HIV testing facilities (98%) by September 2024](<https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.26343708v1>), and captured over nine million testing encounters to September 2025. Lead farmers have [used an AI crop app since 2023 to get pest advisories, with the data monitored by government experts at the agriculture extension department](<https://mwnation.com/ai-bridges-extension-gap/>). The revenue authority's [digital excise stamp platform uses AI to detect anomalies and flag risks](<https://mwnation.com/digital-tax-stamps-enhance-revenue-compliance-mra/>), as reported in July 2026. The meteorological service [tested an AI weather prediction system in its forecasting workflow](<https://crews-initiative.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ASW_AI-for-EW4All-Pilot-Project-%E2%80%94Malawi.pdf>) from May to October 2025.

**Several sectors.** Health, agriculture and tax each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [HIV register transcription](<https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.26343708v1>), the [crop advisory app](<https://mwnation.com/ai-bridges-extension-gap/>) and the [excise stamp platform](<https://mwnation.com/digital-tax-stamps-enhance-revenue-compliance-mra/>).

**Results reported.** The preprint reports that the [transcription error rate fell from 1.6% to 0.4% over 24 months](<https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.08.26343708v1>), with 99.8% of mandatory data elements complete and about 94.6% of records submitted on time.

***Also on file***. [Smart City Surveillance Initiative](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-backs-ai-crime-cameras/G98Yd7LGnmzvX2PD>).

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
