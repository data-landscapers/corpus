# Malawi

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Nation Online reports lead farmers have used the AI PlantVillage app since 2023 to photograph crops and get written pest advisories, with data monitored by government experts through an AI observatory at the Department of Agriculture Extension Services and the Dedza district office](<https://mwnation.com/ai-bridges-extension-gap/>). [The Malawi Revenue Authority's Kalondola digital excise tax stamp platform is supported by analytics and artificial intelligence to detect anomalies and flag risks, as reported by Nation Online on 22 July 2026](<https://mwnation.com/digital-tax-stamps-enhance-revenue-compliance-mra/>).

**Several sectors.** No source held since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mwnation.com/ai-bridges-extension-gap/>).

**Results reported.** No source held since October 2023 reports measured results of a sectoral AI system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://mwnation.com/ai-bridges-extension-gap/>).
