# Comoros

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** WHO says the Comoros [adopted an AI-assisted open-source epidemic intelligence system and trained health surveillance staff on it with the Ministry of Health on 22-25 April 2025](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/comoros/news/comores-loms-forme-sur-linitiative-dintelligence-epidemique-partir-de-sources-ouvertes-eios>). A 2026 conference poster says [machine-learning models are being developed on routine malaria data to forecast incidence](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/ai-driven-predictive-epidemiology-for-malaria-monitoring-in-the-comoros-using-dhis2-data/72310>). Neither account describes a tool producing outputs in service, and no tax, customs, education or agriculture system with AI has been found. Both efforts sit within the national health information and surveillance work.

**Several sectors.** Every AI tool held is in health, and none is in service. The nearest held fact is the [epidemic intelligence training](<https://www.afro.who.int/fr/countries/comoros/news/comores-loms-forme-sur-linitiative-dintelligence-epidemique-partir-de-sources-ouvertes-eios>).

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [malaria forecasting poster](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/ai-driven-predictive-epidemiology-for-malaria-monitoring-in-the-comoros-using-dhis2-data/72310>) compares models in development.
