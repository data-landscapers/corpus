# Eritrea

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Delft Imaging, the vendor, says the Ministry of Health [had 2 portable X-ray units and 6 units of its TB-reading AI deployed in 2024 to detect TB-related abnormalities automatically](<https://delftimaging.com/impact/eritrea/>), and expanded the roll-out with 8 more in 2026. The ministry's work with the vendor goes back to [a 2020 plan to use the offline AI chest X-ray reader to screen for TB](<https://delft.care/healthcare-radius/>). The units serve the TB programme; no other use is described.

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact is the [vendor's account of the TB units](<https://delftimaging.com/impact/eritrea/>); no tax, customs, education or agriculture system with AI has been found.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [vendor's account](<https://delftimaging.com/impact/eritrea/>) counts units deployed, not people screened or cases found.
