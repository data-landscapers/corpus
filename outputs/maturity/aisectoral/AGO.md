# Angola

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The tax authority AGT has built its own machine-learning tools, [tested from 2024](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>). Its chair said in April 2026 that the [audit of the 2024 tax year used AI to flag deviations and suspect cases](<https://pti.ao/agt-reforca-combate-a-fraude-com-ia-e-mapeamento-de-processos/>), which then triggered prior-hearing notices, and in July 2026 that the tools [cross-checked 2025 corporate income tax filings and flagged irregular filers](<https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/inteligencia-artificial-da-agt-apanha-15-mil-empresas-suspeitas-de-fuga-ao-fisco-73137.html>). In education, the minister said in February 2026 that an [AI pilot in selected primary schools](<https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022026/Educacao/47639/Escolas-prim%C3%A1rias-v%C3%A3o-passar-a-contar-com-Intelig%C3%AAncia-Artificial.htm>) was starting, with teacher training first.

**Several sectors.** Tax is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [primary-school pilot](<https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022026/Educacao/47639/Escolas-prim%C3%A1rias-v%C3%A3o-passar-a-contar-com-Intelig%C3%AAncia-Artificial.htm>), which was only being installed.

**Results reported.** According to AGT's chair in July 2026, the AI found that [15,000 of the 40,000 companies that filed zero-turnover 2025 income tax returns had in fact recorded transactions](<https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/inteligencia-artificial-da-agt-apanha-15-mil-empresas-suspeitas-de-fuga-ao-fisco-73137.html>).
