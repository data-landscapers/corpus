# Angola

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The tax authority AGT has built its own machine-learning tools, [tested from 2024](<https://correiokianda.info/inteligencia-artificial-coloca-incumpridores-sob-analise-reforcada-da-agt/>). Its chair said in April 2026 that the [audit of the 2024 tax year used AI to flag deviations and suspect cases](<https://pti.ao/agt-reforca-combate-a-fraude-com-ia-e-mapeamento-de-processos/>). These flags then triggered prior-hearing notices. In July 2026 the chair said the tools [cross-checked 2025 corporate income tax filings and flagged irregular filers](<https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/inteligencia-artificial-da-agt-apanha-15-mil-empresas-suspeitas-de-fuga-ao-fisco-73137.html>). The ministry MINTTICS said in April 2026 that the space programme office's [Tech Minas and Tech Ecologia platforms are in use](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/minttics-destaca-potencial-do-data-center-e-cloud-nacional-para-reforcar-a-adopcao-da-ia-no-pais/>), supporting mining and detecting offshore oil spills. In education, the minister said in February 2026 that an [AI pilot in selected primary schools](<https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022026/Educacao/47639/Escolas-prim%C3%A1rias-v%C3%A3o-passar-a-contar-com-Intelig%C3%AAncia-Artificial.htm>) was starting, with teacher training first.

**Several sectors.** Tax and customs, both run by AGT, have AI in service. [In June 2026 AGT launched its ASY5 customs system at Luanda port, with AI risk management that checks cargo from the manifest on](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/agt-expande-sistema-automatizado-ao-porto-de-luanda/>). On 30 April 2026 the telecoms ministry MINTTICS said the national space programme office GGPEN [runs AI platforms for mining (Tech Minas), offshore oil (Tech Ecologia) and agriculture (Tech Agro)](<https://minttics.gov.ao/ao/noticias/minttics-destaca-potencial-do-data-center-e-cloud-nacional-para-reforcar-a-adopcao-da-ia-no-pais/>). Tech Agro analyses fields from satellite and drone data. The [primary-school pilot](<https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022026/Educacao/47639/Escolas-prim%C3%A1rias-v%C3%A3o-passar-a-contar-com-Intelig%C3%AAncia-Artificial.htm>) was only being installed.

**Results reported.** AGT's chair gave the results in July 2026. The AI found that [15,000 of the 40,000 companies that filed zero-turnover 2025 income tax returns had in fact recorded transactions](<https://expansao.co.ao/empresas/detalhe/inteligencia-artificial-da-agt-apanha-15-mil-empresas-suspeitas-de-fuga-ao-fisco-73137.html>).
