# Mauritius

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [In August 2026 the ICT minister said the Central Water Authority's AI-enabled water leakage reporting service is available through the government's KOREK super app](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/online-motor-vehicle-licence-system-launched-for-taxi-operators/>), beside power supply information and driving licence services. [Mauritius Telecom says its MyTLearn AI tutor, developed with the Ministry of Education, has completed pilots in ten schools](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>), and it aims to give every secondary school teacher an AI coach by 2027. In June 2026 its chief executive said it had [piloted an AI learning tool on Rodrigues with the education ministry, in Mauritian creole](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-business/operator-news/20434-mauritius-telecom-ai-learning-lead-to-national-ai-registry.html>).

**Several sectors.** No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. [The water authority's leak reporting is the one in service](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/online-motor-vehicle-licence-system-launched-for-taxi-operators/>); the education tools are pilots.

**Results reported.** No source found since October 2023 reports measured results of a sectoral AI system. [The minister gave no figures for the leak reporting service](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/online-motor-vehicle-licence-system-launched-for-taxi-operators/>).
