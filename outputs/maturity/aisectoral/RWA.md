# Rwanda

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** A Global Fund case study says Rwanda's public health system [uses AI to read digital chest X-rays for TB, in mobile screening vans and for triage in case finding](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>), as of January 2026. The agriculture ministry is [testing an AI voice assistant in its farmers' call centre](<https://visionmedia.rw/Rwanda-expands-use-of-AI-to-reach-millions-of-farmers-with-timely-advice.html>), which answered at least 60% of questions correctly in testing. A [pilot extending an AI tutor to up to 2,000 public educators](<https://www.alxafrica.com/news-press/alx-anthropic-and-the-government-of-rwanda-launch-landmark-ai-learning-initiative/>) was announced in November 2025. In October 2026 the ICT minister said [a chatbot that helps medical staff judge whether patients need further care was being rolled out](<https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10911470>), and that the health ministry was testing maternal care tools.

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [agriculture call-centre assistant](<https://visionmedia.rw/Rwanda-expands-use-of-AI-to-reach-millions-of-farmers-with-timely-advice.html>), still in testing in 2026, with [satellite and AI land mapping tested in four districts](<https://www.bmz-digital.global/en/digital-maps-agriculture-rwanda/>).

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system in service have been found. The [case study](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/spalexqj/publication_rwanda-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>) describes the TB tools without figures.
