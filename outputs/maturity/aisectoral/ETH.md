# Ethiopia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The Ethiopian Institute of Artificial Intelligence inaugurated customs systems for the Customs Commission, including a passenger risk management system and a licensing system with a chatbot serving the Commission's services in four languages (TechReviewAfrica)](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2883/ethiopian-institute-of-artificial-intelligence-unveils-new-digital-customs-systems>). [State Minister Zerihun Kebede said the national monitoring and evaluation platform, used by more than 113 public institutions, is designed to support institutions with real-time, AI-powered insights for policymaking](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8870155>).
