# Ethiopia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Ministry of Revenues and the Customs Commission [unveiled an AI-powered passenger risk management system on 12 October 2025](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>), built by the [Ethiopian Institute of Artificial Intelligence](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/2883/ethiopian-institute-of-artificial-intelligence-unveils-new-digital-customs-systems>). CGIAR reports that a [fertilizer advisory system run by the Ministry of Agriculture gave AI-based recommendations to 49,000 farmers in 2025](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/how-ethiopia-built-national-digital-fertilizer-advisory-system>). IDinsight says an [AI assistant built with the Ministry of Health answers health extension workers' clinical queries in five regions](<https://www.idinsight.org/project/building-an-ai-powered-clinical-advisor-to-strengthen-frontline-healthcare-in-ethiopia/>); in April 2026 it was [piloted in 15 woredas, with scale-up planned after evaluation](<https://www.thereporterethiopia.com/50178/>). The Ministry of Education [completed testing of an AI teacher assistant for grades 9-12](<https://birrmetrics.com/ministry-of-education-to-deploy-ai-powered-assistant-for-schools/>) in 2026.

**Several sectors.** Customs, agriculture and health each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [customs risk system](<https://addisfortune.news/revenue-customs-launch-eight-new-tech-systems>), the [fertilizer advisory](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/how-ethiopia-built-national-digital-fertilizer-advisory-system>) and the [health assistant](<https://www.idinsight.org/project/building-an-ai-powered-clinical-advisor-to-strengthen-frontline-healthcare-in-ethiopia/>).

**Results reported.** CGIAR reports that the fertilizer advisory [raised wheat yields by 16-25% and maize yields by 10.5-17.6% among participating farmers in 2025](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/how-ethiopia-built-national-digital-fertilizer-advisory-system>). IDinsight reports [83.3% medical accuracy for the health assistant in real-world use](<https://www.idinsight.org/project/building-an-ai-powered-clinical-advisor-to-strengthen-frontline-healthcare-in-ethiopia/>) by 2025.

***Also on file***. [AI-powered survey dashboard](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8870155>).

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
