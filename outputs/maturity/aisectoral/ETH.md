# Ethiopia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [State Minister Zerihun Kebede said the national monitoring and evaluation platform, used by more than 113 public institutions, is designed to support institutions with real-time, AI-powered insights for policymaking](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8870155>). [The ATI with COSS and Protean eGov announced on 3 February 2026 the launch of Ethiopia OpenAgriNet, an AI-powered agricultural digital public infrastructure with location-specific AI recommendations, meant to reach millions of farmers by 2032, with ATI as first implementing partner](<https://ati.gov.et/the-ati-in-partnership-with-coss-and-protean-egov-launch-ethiopia-openagrinet/>).
