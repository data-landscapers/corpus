# Gambia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [In May 2026 a DIAL case study said the Gambia Revenue Authority uses the Rental Tax Compliance System to choose property owners for audit and visits](<https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-x-DPI-Case-study-The-Gambia.pdf>). [The system gives machine-learning estimates of rental values and compliance risk scores](<https://dial.global/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/AI-x-DPI-Case-study-The-Gambia.pdf>). [On 24 April 2025 the Ministry of Higher Education unveiled the TITA mobile app in Banjul](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-higher-education-ministry-launches-innovative-tita-mobile-app-to-modernize-tertiary-institutions/>). [Its admin panel uses AI to generate academic timetables for whole tertiary institutions](<https://www.kerrfatou.com/gambias-higher-education-ministry-launches-innovative-tita-mobile-app-to-modernize-tertiary-institutions/>).
