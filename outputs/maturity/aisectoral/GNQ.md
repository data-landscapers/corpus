# Equatorial Guinea

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The health ministry officially launched on 6 June 2025 'Guinea Salud', an AI chatbot answering the public on vaccines, medical evacuation, clinic licensing and HIV, in a pilot phase](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/salud/sanidad-lanza-el-asistente-digital-guinea-salud-primer-chatbot-sanitario-con-ia-de-guinea-ecuatorial/>). In July 2025 the transport and AI ministry [funded a five-month pilot in which university students build and run the country's first AI-integrated weather station](<https://africanian.com/livenews/equatorial-guinea-launches-climate-innovation-project-students-to-build-first-ai-powered-weather-station/>), with a national network of such stations as the stated goal.
