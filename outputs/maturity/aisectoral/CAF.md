# Central African Republic

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** MSF reports that, with the health authorities, it [equipped Batangafo district hospital in 2024 with an AI tool that scores chest X-rays](<https://www.msf.ch/nos-actualites/articles/republique-centrafricaine-lia-au-service-du-depistage-tuberculose>) and sends high-score patients for laboratory confirmation. In Bangui, The Union and EPCON [built an AI model predicting TB positivity on 100 m grids with the national TB programme](<https://theunion.org/news/mapping-tb-with-ai-brings-new-hope-for-surveillance-in-low-resource-settings>), which its manager says helped active screening; the partners plan to validate it with the health ministry. Institut Pasteur de Bangui [began a four-year project in August 2024 to build AI-assisted fever diagnostic tools](<https://www.afrique-sur7.fr/diagnostic-de-la-fievre-infantile-lintelligence-artificielle-descend-dans-larene-en-centrafrique>) for health centres and community health workers.

**Several sectors.** Every AI tool held is in health. The nearest held fact is the [Batangafo hospital's X-ray scoring](<https://www.msf.ch/nos-actualites/articles/republique-centrafricaine-lia-au-service-du-depistage-tuberculose>).

**Results reported.** MSF reports that in 2025 the tool [helped detect 90 of the 203 TB cases screened in the Batangafo area](<https://www.msf.ch/nos-actualites/articles/republique-centrafricaine-lia-au-service-du-depistage-tuberculose>).
