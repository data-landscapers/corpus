# Cote d'Ivoire

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** In May 2025 the finance minister said the tax administration [uses AI to segment taxpayers by category and has drones that scan buildings to check declarations](<https://finances.gouv.ci/actualites/65-contenu-dynamique/actualite/1126-lancement-du-rapport-sur-le-developpement-humain-rdh-2025-panel-ministeriel-de-haut-niveau-le-ministre-adama-coulibaly-releve-l-importance-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-budget-national>), presented as tools against tax fraud. Since 5 October 2026, community health workers in Gontougo's five districts have [collected data on a platform whose AI gives treatment recommendations and flags inconsistent data](<https://www.mhealth-africa.org/en/actualites/la-cote-divoire-deploie-mhealth-one-la-version-de-mhealth-integrant-lintelligence-artificielle/>), rolled out under the Ministry of Health. In July 2025 the national computing centre said the AI Hub had [begun a pilot project on agriculture with the Case Agri](<https://cncci.edu.ci/mercredi-30-juillet-2025-seance-de-travail-de-la-task-force-ia-au-centre-national-de-calcul-de-cote-divoire-cncci/>). In 2024 the ministry [selected 52 health facilities to receive AI-driven forecasts for family planning commodities](<https://community.dhis2.org/t/leveraging-dhis2-data-to-create-ai-driven-health-facility-forecasts-in-cote-d-ivoire/58136>).

**Several sectors.** Tax, health and land-cover mapping each have AI in service, in separate accounts. In March 2024 the state technical bureau BNETD [presented a land-cover map with AI recognition of forests on satellite imagery](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/729948/cote-divoire-le-bnetd-presente-la-carte-doccupation-des-sols-2020-avec-lappui-du-programme-cacao-durable-de-lunion-europeenne>), offered as a monitoring tool for the national sustainable cocoa strategy. Customs [is deploying AI fraud-detection tools in its Douane Intelligente project, with Huawei](<https://apps.douanes.ci/info/modernisation-douaniere>).

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system in service are held. The [minister's account of AI in tax](<https://finances.gouv.ci/actualites/65-contenu-dynamique/actualite/1126-lancement-du-rapport-sur-le-developpement-humain-rdh-2025-panel-ministeriel-de-haut-niveau-le-ministre-adama-coulibaly-releve-l-importance-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-budget-national>) gives no figures for fraud detected or revenue recovered.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
