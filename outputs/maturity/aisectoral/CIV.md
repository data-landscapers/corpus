# Cote d'Ivoire

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** In May 2025 the finance minister said the tax administration [uses AI to segment taxpayers by category and has drones that scan buildings to check declarations](<https://finances.gouv.ci/actualites/65-contenu-dynamique/actualite/1126-lancement-du-rapport-sur-le-developpement-humain-rdh-2025-panel-ministeriel-de-haut-niveau-le-ministre-adama-coulibaly-releve-l-importance-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-budget-national>), presented as tools against tax fraud.

**Several sectors.** A second sector has AI in service. In March 2024 the state technical bureau BNETD [presented the 2020 land-cover map, with AI recognition of forests on Sentinel imagery](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/729948/cote-divoire-le-bnetd-presente-la-carte-doccupation-des-sols-2020-avec-lappui-du-programme-cacao-durable-de-lunion-europeenne>), offered through an online platform as a monitoring tool for the national sustainable cocoa strategy. The map was produced with support from the European Union's sustainable cocoa programme.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system are held. The nearest held fact is the [minister's account of AI in tax](<https://finances.gouv.ci/actualites/65-contenu-dynamique/actualite/1126-lancement-du-rapport-sur-le-developpement-humain-rdh-2025-panel-ministeriel-de-haut-niveau-le-ministre-adama-coulibaly-releve-l-importance-de-l-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-budget-national>), which gives no figures for fraud detected or revenue recovered.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
