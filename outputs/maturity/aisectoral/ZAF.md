# South Africa

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [SARS says machine learning and AI models manage the risk of impermissible or fraudulent refunds on returns submitted online](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/over-5-million-taxpayers-auto-assessed-2025-filing-season-gets-underway>). [SARS says its artificial intelligence fraud-risk detection and verification work in tax refunds is operating, with the report crediting it with preventing the outflow of over R417 billion in impermissible refunds since 2020](<https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Docs/StratAnnualPerfplans/Strategic-Plan-End-Term-Report-2020-2025.pdf>). [SARS says it applies artificial intelligence and machine learning to about 150 million third-party data records to auto-assess taxpayers and runs each outcome through fraud detection](<https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/media-statement-commissioner-edward-kieswetters-address-on-filing-season-2024/>).

**Several sectors.** No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. The nearest held fact: [SARS reports that its AI fraud-risk detection and verification work prevented the outflow of over R417 billion in impermissible refunds over the 2020-2025 plan period](<https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Docs/StratAnnualPerfplans/Strategic-Plan-End-Term-Report-2020-2025.pdf>).

**Results reported.** [SARS reports that its AI fraud-risk detection and verification work prevented the outflow of over R417 billion in impermissible refunds over the 2020-2025 plan period](<https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Docs/StratAnnualPerfplans/Strategic-Plan-End-Term-Report-2020-2025.pdf>). [SARS reports over 5 million taxpayers auto-assessed in 14 days, 99.13% accepting the outcome, and R100 billion of impermissible refunds prevented last year](<https://www.sars.gov.za/media-release/media-statement-commissioner-edward-kieswetters-address-on-filing-season-2024/>).
