# Cape Verde

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Cabo Verdean American Medical Society, working with the Ministry of Health, says it has [distributed cloud ECG devices across all islands that make automatic diagnoses with AI](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/23296-missoes-medicas-do-projeto-continuidade-dos-eua-reforcam-saude-em-cabo-verde-com-tecnologia-de-ponta>), and that AI lets nurses diagnose cataracts, glaucoma and retinopathy. The account dates from April 2026 and describes the devices as part of the society's medical missions with the ministry. It is the only account held of AI at work in a public sector's system.

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact is the [society's account of AI-read ECGs and eye screening](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/23296-missoes-medicas-do-projeto-continuidade-dos-eua-reforcam-saude-em-cabo-verde-com-tecnologia-de-ponta>); no tax, customs, education or agriculture system with AI has been found.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [society's account](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/23296-missoes-medicas-do-projeto-continuidade-dos-eua-reforcam-saude-em-cabo-verde-com-tecnologia-de-ponta>) gives no count of diagnoses or patients.

***Also on file***. [Monitoring Tools and Artificial Intelligence for Smart Agriculture](<https://www.undp.org/acceleratorlabs/blog/agricultural-transition-using-internet-things-and-artificial-intelligence>).

***Not found***. No source found reports AI in service in a second sector, and measured results of a sectoral AI system.
