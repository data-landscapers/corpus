## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Comptroller-General Adewale Adeniyi says Nigeria Customs uses AI-enabled scanners to guide image analysts](<https://punchng.com/customs-deploy-ai-to-curb-revenue-leakages-deepen-transparency/>). [In March 2022 customs said it used AI in some processes](<https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/pres_saidu.pdf>). [They included fraud detection in NICIS, an AI HS-code search and AI vehicle valuation](<https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/pres_saidu.pdf>). A report relayed in November 2025 says [AI is being applied in social welfare programmes to map urban poverty](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/11/05/funding-gap-threatens-nigerias-ai-growth-despite-strong-implementation-report/>). [AI traffic management pilots run in Lagos and Abuja](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/02/nigeria-is-adopting-ai-fast-but-foreign-firms-are-winning-report-warns/>), reported in August 2026. [The national AI and robotics centre leads health projects](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/fg-moves-to-regulate-ai-use-in-healthcare-protect-patients-data/amp/>), the health minister of state said in October 2026.
