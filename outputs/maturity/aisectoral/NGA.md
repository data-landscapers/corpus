# Nigeria

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Nigeria Customs Service Comptroller-General Adewale Adeniyi says it has deployed AI-enabled scanners that guide image analysts to predict the nature of imported items](<https://punchng.com/customs-deploy-ai-to-curb-revenue-leakages-deepen-transparency/>). [The Nigeria Customs Service says it uses AI in some processes, including an AI/machine-learning Smart Fraud Detection model in NICIS that helps officers assess declaration risk, an AI HS-code search and an AI vehicle valuation system (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/reser_e/pres_saidu.pdf>). A report relayed in November 2025 says [AI is being applied in social welfare programmes to map urban poverty](<https://nairametrics.com/2025/11/05/funding-gap-threatens-nigerias-ai-growth-despite-strong-implementation-report/>). Pilots run in two other sectors: [AI traffic management in Lagos and Abuja](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/08/02/nigeria-is-adopting-ai-fast-but-foreign-firms-are-winning-report-warns/>), reported in August 2026, and [health projects led by the national AI and robotics centre](<https://tribuneonlineng.com/fg-moves-to-regulate-ai-use-in-healthcare-protect-patients-data/amp/>), described by the health minister of state in October 2026.

**Several sectors.** No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://punchng.com/customs-deploy-ai-to-curb-revenue-leakages-deepen-transparency/>).
