# Benin

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Benin's health ministry awarded a public contract for 20 AI-assisted miLab MAL malaria diagnostic devices for main referral hospitals, after the national malaria programme classified it as a point-of-service diagnostic, per South Korean media reports](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/paludisme-le-benin-integre-l-ia-dans-son-dispositif-national-de-diagnostic>). [GAINAfrica's offline-first clinical decision-support system, built by partner OneTreck, is being tested in Benin, per AIM](<https://aimnews.org/2026/08/25/europa-introduz-inteligencia-artificial-generativa-para-transformar-africa/>). [Benin launched on 30 June 2026 a Japan-funded $2.3m malaria project that will use AI with geospatial mapping to find high-risk mosquito breeding areas for drone larvicide spraying in six pilot municipalities, per the government and We Are Tech Africa](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/benin-combines-ai-drones-and-geospatial-mapping-in-fight-against-malaria>).
