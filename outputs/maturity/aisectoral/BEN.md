# Benin

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Webb Fontaine, the vendor, says Benin's customs system [now bases risk management on AI profiling of importers and goods and detects fraud patterns automatically](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-customs-the-new-digital-weapon-against-customs-fraud-in-benin>), and in February 2025 that the system was [in use at 44 of the 49 customs offices](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/benin-customs-management-milestones>). In health, the ministry [awarded a contract for 20 AI-assisted malaria diagnostic devices for referral hospitals](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/paludisme-le-benin-integre-l-ia-dans-son-dispositif-national-de-diagnostic>) in 2026, and a [Japan-funded malaria project launched on 30 June 2026 will use AI mapping to target larvicide spraying](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/benin-combines-ai-drones-and-geospatial-mapping-in-fight-against-malaria>) in six pilot municipalities. For the tax directorate, an [invoice-label classification model was developed](<https://numericite.eu/datagov-nos-cas-dusage-emblematiques/>) in 2025.

**Several sectors.** Customs is the only sector held with AI in service. The health tools, such as the [malaria diagnostic devices](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/paludisme-le-benin-integre-l-ia-dans-son-dispositif-national-de-diagnostic>) and a [clinical decision-support system under test](<https://aimnews.org/2026/08/25/europa-introduz-inteligencia-artificial-generativa-para-transformar-africa/>), are still being procured or tested.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The nearest held fact is the [vendor's account of customs risk profiling](<https://webbfontaine.com/news/webb-customs-the-new-digital-weapon-against-customs-fraud-in-benin>), which gives no figures.
