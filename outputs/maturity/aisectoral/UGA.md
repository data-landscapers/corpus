# Uganda

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The revenue authority's Commissioner General said in 2025 that [URA used AI to identify tax evasion such as undeclared imports and false VAT claims](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>). Authors from the Uganda Catholic Medical Bureau state that [TB screening in Uganda uses AI-assisted chest X-ray reading at a national threshold](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>). Makerere University says an [AI mosquito-identification app piloted with the National Malaria Control Programme is to be scaled to 22 districts](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2025/09/vectorcam-project-makerere-johns-hopkins-and-moh-to-scale-up-ai-powered-malaria-surveillance-across-uganda/>), and a [crop-disease identification app is being tested](<https://aimnews.org/2026/08/25/europa-introduz-inteligencia-artificial-generativa-para-transformar-africa/>) under a European project.

**Several sectors.** Tax and health each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [revenue authority's evasion analysis](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>) and [TB X-ray screening](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>).

**Results reported.** The Commissioner General said URA [recovered 200 billion shillings in revenue leakages uncovered with AI in the previous year](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>). A review of 6,000 West Nile X-ray records from 2021 to 2024 found the [AI reader identified 181 of 188 confirmed TB cases](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>).

***Also on file***. [ISBAT University AI and Robotics Laboratory](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>).

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
