# Uganda

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The revenue authority's Commissioner General said in 2025 that [URA used AI to find tax evasion](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>). He named undeclared imports and false VAT claims. Authors from the Uganda Catholic Medical Bureau state that [TB screening uses AI to read chest X-rays at a national threshold](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>). In March 2026 The Observer reported that [judicial officers use AI platforms for legal research and case analysis](<https://observer.ug/news/judiciary-goes-paperless-rolls-out-ai-systems/>). They include a ULII feature that summarises judgments. Makerere University says an [AI mosquito-identification app piloted with the malaria programme is to reach 22 districts](<https://news.mak.ac.ug/2025/09/vectorcam-project-makerere-johns-hopkins-and-moh-to-scale-up-ai-powered-malaria-surveillance-across-uganda/>). A European project [is testing a crop-disease identification app](<https://aimnews.org/2026/08/25/europa-introduz-inteligencia-artificial-generativa-para-transformar-africa/>).

**Several sectors.** Tax, health and the courts each have AI in service, in separate accounts. They are the [revenue authority's evasion analysis](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>), [TB X-ray screening](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>) and the [judiciary's research platforms](<https://observer.ug/news/judiciary-goes-paperless-rolls-out-ai-systems/>).

**Results reported.** The Commissioner General said URA [recovered 200 billion shillings of leaked revenue found with AI](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>). That was in the year before he spoke. A review of 6,000 West Nile X-rays from 2021 to 2024 tested the AI reader. It [identified 181 of 188 confirmed TB cases](<https://doi.org/10.37432/jieph-confpro6-00043>).

***Also on file***. [ISBAT University AI and Robotics Laboratory](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ura-cg-launches-university-ai-and-robotics-laboratory/>).

***Not found***. No single source found says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
