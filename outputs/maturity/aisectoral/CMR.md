# Cameroon

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The director general of the social insurance fund CNPS said in February 2026 that the fund [runs a symbolic-AI system for automatic processing of old-age pension files](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-avancees-piliers-et-dangers-de-l-ia-presentes-a-un-atelier-du-minpostel-dedie-aux-administrations-publics/>), and that its [AI tools detect fraud by finding inconsistencies in declarations and contributions](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/6734/en/homeat-govt-artificial-intelligence-workshop-cnps-director-general-showcases-ai-use-in-social-security-services>) and calculate rights in real time. The secondary-education ministry [presented the official integration of AI-generated simulations in secondary schools](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/e-education-cameroun-le-minesec-valide-la-simulation-ia-avantages-et-limites/>) on 17 August 2026. University researchers say a [tax fraud detection model was tested at three tax centres](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2708-16627-cameroon-built-ai-claims-97-2-precision-in-tax-fraud-detection-but-revenue-impact-remains-unclear>), which the tax directorate has not corroborated.

**Several sectors.** Social security and secondary education each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [CNPS tools](<https://theguardianpostcameroon.com/post/6734/en/homeat-govt-artificial-intelligence-workshop-cnps-director-general-showcases-ai-use-in-social-security-services>) and the [school simulations](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/e-education-cameroun-le-minesec-valide-la-simulation-ia-avantages-et-limites/>).

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system in service have been found. The [tax model's reported gains](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/public-management/2708-16627-cameroon-built-ai-claims-97-2-precision-in-tax-fraud-detection-but-revenue-impact-remains-unclear>) come from its authors' test, not from a system in service.

***Also on file***. [AI Doctor](<https://www.cnps.cm/fr/post/visite-medicale-preventive-la-cnps-deploie-le-dispositif-ai-doctor.html>).

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
