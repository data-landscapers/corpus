# Botswana

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** A vendor partner says the national TB programme [deployed AI chest X-ray reading in April 2025 and trained local teams](<https://vertice.co.za/advancing-tb-screening-in-botswana-with-quire-ai-technology/>). The U.S. Embassy says PEPFAR [donated AI software for TB detection to the Ministry of Health](<https://bw.usembassy.gov/usg-donates-tb-ai-software-to-moh/>) in 2025. The health minister said in February 2025 that [trial systems incorporating AI were approved under the ministry's digital health pilot](<https://www.thegazette.news/news/moh-pilots-digital-health-ecosystem/>), and an assistant minister said in August 2025 that [government is piloting AI for health diagnostics in remote areas](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/88106>).

**Several sectors.** Every AI tool held is in health. The nearest held fact is the [TB programme's chest X-ray reading](<https://vertice.co.za/advancing-tb-screening-in-botswana-with-quire-ai-technology/>); no tax, customs, education or agriculture system with AI has been found.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [embassy's account of the donated software](<https://bw.usembassy.gov/usg-donates-tb-ai-software-to-moh/>) expects better TB case identification but gives no figures.
