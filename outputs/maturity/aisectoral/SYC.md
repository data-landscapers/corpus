# Seychelles

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [UNDP says a EUR 400,000 AI-driven customs and logistics upgrade has been implemented through the Port Victoria Management Information System, with real-time analysis of port, logistics and customs data](<https://sdglocalaction.org/how-cities-are-using-artificial-intelligence-to-deliver-services/>). [The Ministry of Education is implementing two World Bank AI pilots: an AI Teacher Coach Programme for Primary 5 and 6 teachers in six schools and an AI Student Tutor Programme for Secondary 3 Mathematics and Geography in four schools (State House)](<https://statehouse.gov.sc/news/7158/president-herminies-vision-for-artificial-intelligence-takes-root-in-seychelles-classrooms>). [The Ministry of Education, with the World Bank, launched the AI Teacher Coach and AI Student Tutor pilots, introduced at a workshop for educators from six named primary schools](<https://nation.sc/articles/30564/seychelles-launches-ai-pilot-projects-to-transform-teaching-and-learning>).
