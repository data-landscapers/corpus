# Liberia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Executive Mansion says [four AI X-ray machines for TB screening were dedicated and distributed to four hospitals in Montserrado and Margibi](<https://www.emansion.gov.lr/media/press-release/first-lady-dedicates-four-ai-x-ray-machines-tb-diagnosis>) in August 2024, with Global Fund support. A Partners In Health clinician says [AI chest X-ray reading supported TB screening at James Jenkins Dossen Hospital in Maryland County from March 2023 to January 2025](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/otuba-paul-29344955_cphia2025-tb-digitalhealth-activity-7387896239935369216-fBQX>), with the national TB programme, and helped find patients other tests missed. The Environmental Protection Agency [sought a consultant in April 2026 to build an AI system forecasting floods and droughts](<https://emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/5a_REOI_INTERNATIONAL%20CONSULTANTS%20TO%20DEVELOP%20AND%20OPERATIONALIZE%20AN%20AUTOMATED%20DECISION%20MANAGEMENT%20SYSTEM.pdf>).

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [EPA's forecasting system](<https://emansion.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/5a_REOI_INTERNATIONAL%20CONSULTANTS%20TO%20DEVELOP%20AND%20OPERATIONALIZE%20AN%20AUTOMATED%20DECISION%20MANAGEMENT%20SYSTEM.pdf>), which was still to be designed.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [clinician's account](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/otuba-paul-29344955_cphia2025-tb-digitalhealth-activity-7387896239935369216-fBQX>) says the tool helped identify patients, without figures.
