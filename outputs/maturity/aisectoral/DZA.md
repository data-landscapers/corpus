## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Algeria's higher education ministry's coordination meeting programmed, for the short term, an AI-based tax management and administration system](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/09/le-ministre-de-lenseignement-superieur-et-de-la-recherche-scientifique-preside-la-premiere-reunion-de-coordination-consacree-a-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelli/>). [El Watan reports that from the 2026 academic year the higher education ministry will deploy an intelligent assistant to personalise student paths and a large language model to assist teachers](<https://elwatan.dz/strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-kamel-baddari-fixe-la-feuille-de-route/>). [The higher education minister said the 2026-2027 academic year will launch an AI-supported personal study path, with an intelligent assistant helping students choose modules, and an AI language model for lecturers is being developed to generate exercises and assess student answers](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/إطلاق-المسار-التعليمي-الشخصي-للطالب-ب>).
