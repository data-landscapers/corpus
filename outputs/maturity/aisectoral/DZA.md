# Algeria

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Ministry of Foreign Trade said in September 2026 that its [AI-boosted data analysis and cross-checking system, built into its digital platform, detected anomalies in import programme files](<https://www.algerie360.com/importations-suspectes-en-algerie-lia-demasque-des-documents-identiques-chez-plus-de-1000-operateurs/>). The Ministry of Finance says the customs information system, [used at all customs offices and posts, relies on AI techniques for risk analysis of imports and exports](<https://www.mf.gov.dz/index.php/ar/documentation-ar/grands-dossiers-ar/2174-2025-02-06-14-39-01>). In April 2024 the higher education minister [launched an AI platform that simulates study-field choices for new baccalaureate holders](<https://www.mesrs.dz/en/2024/04/the-launch-of-a-new-digital-platform-to-assist-new-baccalaureate-holders/>). A [language model on tax legislation for the Directorate General of Taxes](<https://www.mesrs.dz/fr/2026/10/m-kamel-baddari-preside-la-cinquieme-reunion-multisectorielle-consacree-au-suivi-de-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-la-strategie-nationale-de-lintelligence-artificielle-2/>) was given a four-month deadline on 20 October 2026.

**Several sectors.** Trade, customs and higher education each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [trade ministry's cross-checking](<https://www.algerie360.com/importations-suspectes-en-algerie-lia-demasque-des-documents-identiques-chez-plus-de-1000-operateurs/>), the [customs system](<https://www.mf.gov.dz/index.php/ar/documentation-ar/grands-dossiers-ar/2174-2025-02-06-14-39-01>) and the [orientation platform](<https://www.mesrs.dz/en/2024/04/the-launch-of-a-new-digital-platform-to-assist-new-baccalaureate-holders/>).

**Results reported.** The trade ministry's cross-checking [flagged 1,013 economic operators whose import files held identical documents](<https://www.algerie360.com/importations-suspectes-en-algerie-lia-demasque-des-documents-identiques-chez-plus-de-1000-operateurs/>), including 278 identical social security declarations and 56 files sharing one tax number.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
