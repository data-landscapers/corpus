# Ghana

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The revenue authority's deputy commissioner said in May 2025 that it [uses AI and machine learning to find anomalies in tax filings, expose suspicious trade and trigger audits](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/GRA-doubles-down-on-AI-ML-to-combat-tax-leakages-1984925>). The health ministry and Ghana Health Service, with WHO and UNDP, [run a programme on AI-enabled disease surveillance](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ghana/news/who-and-undp-support-ghana-integrate-artificial-intelligence-digital-health-strategy>) that joins health and climate data to predict malaria, cholera and meningitis.

**Several sectors.** Customs is a second sector with AI in service. The Ministry of Finance and the revenue authority said [Publican Trade Solution, an AI tool that helps customs officers value and classify imports, was fully rolled out at ports of entry on 11 March 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>), beside the ICUMS clearance system, after [a November 2025 pilot that flagged anomalies in 18 of 43 test transactions](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/01/ghana-to-plug-us127bn-revenue-leakages-with-new-ai-customs-tool/>).

**Results reported.** The Ministry of Finance said that by 30 March 2026 Publican had [endorsed clearing of 75.3% of customs declarations and flagged 24.7% as below accepted global values](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>).
