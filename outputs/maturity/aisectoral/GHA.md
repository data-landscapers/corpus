# Ghana

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The revenue authority's deputy commissioner said in May 2025 that it [uses AI and machine learning to find anomalies in tax filings, expose suspicious trade and trigger audits](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/GRA-doubles-down-on-AI-ML-to-combat-tax-leakages-1984925>). Ghana Health Service and Jacaranda Health [launched an AI maternal health platform in July 2026 that sorts mothers' SMS questions by urgency and refers emergencies](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/07/ghs-jacaranda-deploy-ai-tool-for-maternal-care/>); Ghana Health Service clinicians have [staffed its helpdesk since March 2026](<https://jacarandahealth.org/building-a-sustainable-future-for-prompts-in-ghana/>). A [WHO-UNDP programme launched in May 2026 aims to deploy AI early warning for climate-sensitive diseases](<https://www.afro.who.int/countries/ghana/news/ghana-launches-artificial-intelligence-driven-health-programme-strengthen-systems-and-safeguard>) in national platforms.

**Several sectors.** Customs is a further sector with AI in service. The Ministry of Finance and the revenue authority said [an AI tool that helps customs officers value and classify imports was fully rolled out at ports of entry on 11 March 2026](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>), after [a November 2025 pilot](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/01/ghana-to-plug-us127bn-revenue-leakages-with-new-ai-customs-tool/>).

**Results reported.** The Ministry of Finance said that by 30 March 2026 the customs tool had [endorsed clearing of 75.3% of declarations and flagged 24.7% as below accepted global values](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/freight-forwarders-importers-raise-concern-over-publican-ai-o-system-flags-only-values-lower-than-minimum-global-average-ministry-of-finance.html>). Jacaranda reports the maternal helpdesk [handles about 6,500 responses a month, 44% resolved by AI directly](<https://jacarandahealth.org/building-a-sustainable-future-for-prompts-in-ghana/>).
