# DR Congo

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The education minister said in August 2025 that [an AI system marked the 2025 State Exam papers](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>). [Results came out three days after the exams](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>). The ministry says AI grades papers at [the national centre in Kinshasa and its Lubumbashi branch](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/press-releases/decentralization-of-state-examination-correction-centers-a-first-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo>). WHO says [its AI-based preparedness platform was in use in the country by October 2026](<https://afro.who.int/countries/democratic-republic-of-congo/news/who-deploys-pdx-preparedness-intelligence-platform-democratic-republic-congo>), giving national teams early warning during the Ebola response. [A health official said in March 2025 that the government had given the national TB programme radiology equipment using AI](<https://acp.cd/science-sante-environnement/rdc-nous-devons-nous-engager-pour-mettre-fin-a-la-tuberculose-dici-2035-directeur-du-programme-national-de-lutte-contre-la-tuberculose/>). PATH and the Ministry of Health [piloted an AI tool that reads paper health registers into DHIS2](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>) in 12 health zones. On 8 November 2026 the health ministry announced [Etoile Bleue, an emergency medical response system that uses AI to handle alerts](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-un-systeme-connecte-pour-mieux-organiser-les-interventions-medicales>). [It is to start in Kinshasa and Matadi](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/rdc-un-systeme-connecte-pour-mieux-organiser-les-interventions-medicales>), with no launch date given. In August 2026 Kinshasa's revenue directorate DGRK [presented its tax platform IRMS-DGRK as integrating AI tools to assist users](<https://lepoint.cd/dgrk-apres-la-vignette-kinshasa-veut-digitaliser-les-impots-et-taxes/>). It is to be extended from the vehicle tax to other city taxes.

**Several sectors.** Education and health each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [exam marking](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>) and [WHO's epidemic intelligence platform](<https://afro.who.int/countries/democratic-republic-of-congo/news/who-deploys-pdx-preparedness-intelligence-platform-democratic-republic-congo>).

**Results reported.** In August 2025 the minister said [AI cut exam processing time by 30-40%](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>). [Scanning rose from 8-10 to 80-100 papers a minute](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>).

***Also on file***. [Voyage Santé](<https://afro.who.int/countries/democratic-republic-of-congo/news/who-deploys-pdx-preparedness-intelligence-platform-democratic-republic-congo>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in two or more sectors. Each source names one.
