# DR Congo

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The education minister told the Council of Ministers on 15 August 2025 that [an AI system automated State Exam marking in 2025, with results published three days after the exams](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>). The ministry says AI is integrated into grading at the [national centre in Kinshasa and at the Lubumbashi branch](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/press-releases/decentralization-of-state-examination-correction-centers-a-first-in-the-democratic-republic-of-congo>). WHO says its [AI-based preparedness platform is now in use in the country, giving national teams early-warning signals during the Ebola response](<https://afro.who.int/countries/democratic-republic-of-congo/news/who-deploys-pdx-preparedness-intelligence-platform-democratic-republic-congo>), as of October 2026. PATH and the Ministry of Health [piloted an AI tool reading paper health registers into DHIS2 in 12 health zones](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>).

**Several sectors.** Education and health each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [exam marking](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>) and [WHO's epidemic intelligence platform](<https://afro.who.int/countries/democratic-republic-of-congo/news/who-deploys-pdx-preparedness-intelligence-platform-democratic-republic-congo>).

**Results reported.** The minister reported that AI exam processing [cut processing time by 30-40% and lifted scanning from 8-10 to 80-100 papers a minute](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>), in August 2025.
