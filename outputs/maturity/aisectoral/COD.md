# DR Congo

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [PATH, with the Ministry of Health, piloted SnapiForm, an AI tool reading paper health registers into DHIS2, in 12 DRC health zones for several months; PATH reports reporting time fell from over two hours to about 15 minutes](<https://www.path.org/our-impact/articles/streamlining-health-data-reporting-with-snapform/>). [Education minister Raissa Malu told the Council of Ministers on 15 August 2025 that an AI system automated exam-paper marking in 2025, with results published three days after the exams](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>). [The education ministry inaugurated a modernised State Exam grading centre in Kinshasa on 10 April 2025 running the AI-using software Snote Manager to process test items](<https://edu-nc.gouv.cd/news/artificial-intelligence-at-the-heart-of-state-exam-correction>).

**Several sectors.** No source held since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. The nearest held fact: [Minister Malu reported AI exam processing cut processing time by 30-40 percent, raised speed tenfold, and lifted scanning from 8-10 to 80-100 papers a minute](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/sustainability/innovation/ai-cuts-exam-marking-time-in-congo-5161318>).
