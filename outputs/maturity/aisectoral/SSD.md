# South Sudan

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** UNDP says the Ministry of Health, with UNDP and Global Fund support, [installed two portable AI-assisted digital X-ray machines for TB case detection at Torit and Rumbek state hospitals in December 2023](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>), with four more to follow. It is the only account held of AI at work in a public sector's system, and it concerns two state hospitals, Torit and Rumbek, serving TB case finding.

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact is [UNDP's account of the X-ray machines](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>); no tax, customs, education or agriculture system with AI is held.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system are held. [UNDP's account](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>) reports the installation, not cases detected.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. AI in service in a second sector, and measured results of a sectoral AI system, are not known.
