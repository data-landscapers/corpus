# South Sudan

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [UNDP says the Ministry of Health installed two AI-assisted X-ray machines for TB case detection](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>). [They went to Torit and Rumbek state hospitals in December 2023](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>). [In September 2026 WHO reported that the Digital Care Companion, a clinical decision tool built on symbolic AI, had logged 3,168 visits](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>). [It runs in 12 facilities across 10 states and administrative areas](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>).

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector found with AI in service. The nearest facts are [UNDP's account of the X-ray machines](<https://www.undp.org/south-sudan/blog/technology-accelerates-tb-detection-south-sudan>) and [WHO's account of the Digital Care Companion](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>). No tax, customs, education or agriculture system using AI has been found.

**Results reported.** [WHO reports that clinicians accepted 64.9% of the Digital Care Companion's diagnosis recommendations](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>). [Of 3,168 logged consultations, 95.0% went through all nine steps](<https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/Knowledge%20Management%20Series%20for%20Health%20-%20DCC%20low%20cost%20technology%20solutions%20for%20expanding%20quality%20services%20into%20hard%20to%20reach%20areas.pdf>).
