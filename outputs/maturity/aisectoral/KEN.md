# Kenya

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Health Cabinet Secretary told the Senate in October 2025 that the Social Health Authority's [claims platform uses AI and machine learning to detect abnormal claim patterns, inflated billing and duplicate submissions in real time](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-10-30-sha-system-stopped-theft-of-millions-in-fake-claims-duale>); a parliamentary committee report in December 2025 said the system [also rejects some legitimate hospital claims as fraudulent](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2025-12-04-ai-system-flaws-push-sha-into-chaos-mps>). President Ruto said in November 2025 that the revenue authority KRA has [AI-powered risk engines integrated into tax compliance](<https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/press-release/2329-president-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliancepresident-ruto-hails-kra%E2%80%99s-ai-led-reforms,-honours-taxpayers-for-upholding-compliance>). The Digital Health Agency [tendered for AI-driven maternal and child health and blood bank systems](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/126464-taifa-care-eyes-ai-powered-system-track-pregnancy-births-and-child-health>) in 2026.

**Several sectors.** President Ruto said in September 2024 that the government [uses AI for education-funding means testing, for distributing fertiliser to farmers and for identifying health coverage beneficiaries](<https://www.tuko.co.ke/business-economy/563190-william-ruto-attempts-woo-tech-giants-invest-kenya-using-ai/>).

**Results reported.** The Health Cabinet Secretary said in February 2026 that the authority's AI claims system had [flagged, rejected and left unpaid Sh12.7 billion of claims](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-02-27-duale-ai-blocked-sh127bn-fake-claims-at-sha>), with 1,188 fraud files sent to the police and 26 suspects charged.
