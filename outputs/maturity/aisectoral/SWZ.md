# Eswatini

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** In August 2025 the health minister launched [an AI diagnostic system for diabetic retinopathy, operating at six public hospitals and health centres](<https://swazi24.com/20702/news/health/six-hospitals-in-eswatini-roll-out-ai-eye-screening-tech/>). They include Mbabane Government, Piggs Peak and Mankayane. The system is part of the Taiwan Technical Mission's non-communicable disease project. [In September 2025 the health minister commissioned mobile TB trucks with AI chest X-ray detection](<https://swazidailynews.com/3484/future/e21mln-mobile-tb-trucks-12-motorbikes-to-enhance-fight-against-tb/>) for the national TB programme. In April 2026 the government said it had [procured four TB screening trucks with AI-assisted diagnostics, operating across the country](<https://40years.gov.sz/a-healthier-kingdom-40-years-of-expanding-care-capacity-and-hope-2/>).

**Several sectors.** Only health is held with AI in service. The Eswatini Revenue Service [sought proposals for a fiscalisation system using machine learning for tax risk assessment and fraud detection](<https://eswatinifinancialtimes.africa/daily-business-transactions-monitored-ai-to-uncover-potential-tax-fraud/>) in May 2025. No tool is reported awarded or in service under that call.

**Results reported.** [The health minister said one AI X-ray truck screened 229 people at the September 2025 trade fair and found eight TB cases](<https://swazidailynews.com/3484/future/e21mln-mobile-tb-trucks-12-motorbikes-to-enhance-fight-against-tb/>). They were referred to health facilities. The [eye screening system's launch](<https://swazi24.com/20702/news/health/six-hospitals-in-eswatini-roll-out-ai-eye-screening-tech/>) gives no count of patients screened.
