# Senegal

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The tax directorate DGID says its [data-mining project uses data analysis and AI to detect tax anomalies and fraud](<https://www.impact.sn/SENEGAL-La-DGID-se-convertit-au-numerique-pour-des-pratiques-fiscales-plus-efficaces_a51008.html>), with a data-science cell, as of January 2025. AI4PEP Senegal says its [AI-powered One Health platform for community zoonotic disease surveillance went live](<https://ai4pep.org/ai4pep-senegal-launches-plateforme-3s-one-health/>) in February 2025. The health ministry told the World Health Assembly in May 2026 that it has [begun integrating AI into its national DHIS2 platform](<https://apps.who.int/gb/statements/WHA79/PDF/Senegal-12.10.pdf>), and an [offline AI assistant for community health workers](<https://aps.sn/un-assistant-ia-pour-agents-communautaires-en-cours-de-deploiement-au-senegal/>) was being rolled out from July 2025. In education, IDinsight is [testing two early AI pilots with the ministry for the bilingual reform](<https://www.idinsight.org/project/ai-driven-support-for-bilingual-education-in-senegal/>).

**Several sectors.** Tax and health surveillance each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [DGID's data mining](<https://www.impact.sn/SENEGAL-La-DGID-se-convertit-au-numerique-pour-des-pratiques-fiscales-plus-efficaces_a51008.html>) and the [One Health platform](<https://ai4pep.org/ai4pep-senegal-launches-plateforme-3s-one-health/>).

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [DGID's account](<https://www.impact.sn/SENEGAL-La-DGID-se-convertit-au-numerique-pour-des-pratiques-fiscales-plus-efficaces_a51008.html>) gives no figures for fraud detected or revenue recovered.
