# Senegal

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The DGID says its Datamining project uses data analysis and AI for tax anomaly and fraud detection, with a data-science cell and the ASREF app tracking intelligence use](<https://www.impact.sn/SENEGAL-La-DGID-se-convertit-au-numerique-pour-des-pratiques-fiscales-plus-efficaces_a51008.html>). [RTS says the TGI de Pikine-Guediawaye launched works on 5 October 2026 to set up a local AI application for judicial archives, with UNDP and Korean support, as a pilot court](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/justice-le-tgi-de-pikine-guediawaye-experimente-une-ia-pour-les-archives-judiciaires>). [In health, the social enterprise Kajou began deploying kSanté, an offline AI assistant for community health workers trained on Ministry of Health documents and launched with the ministry on 11 July 2025, with 2,000 cards being distributed](<https://aps.sn/un-assistant-ia-pour-agents-communautaires-en-cours-de-deploiement-au-senegal/>).

**Several sectors.** No source held since October 2023 says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.impact.sn/SENEGAL-La-DGID-se-convertit-au-numerique-pour-des-pratiques-fiscales-plus-efficaces_a51008.html>).
