# Burkina Faso

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The national TB programme's coordinator said the AI tool CAD4TB was [introduced in 2024 to read images from the programme's mobile digital X-ray units](<https://delftimaging.com/resources/webinars/advancing-tb-screening-in-burkina-faso-with-portable-x-ray-and-cad4tb/>) in field screening campaigns. In weather forecasting, the digital transition minister and the meteorological agency ANAM [signed a research convention on 10 October 2025 to bring AI into forecasting](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/10/14/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-integre-lintelligence-artificielle-au-service-de-la-meteo/>), with ANAM as the pilot body.

**Several sectors.** Health is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [ANAM convention](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/10/14/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-integre-lintelligence-artificielle-au-service-de-la-meteo/>), which covers research, with no tool reported in service.

**Results reported.** The coordinator reported that [from September 2024 to April 2025 the campaigns screened 1,730 people and found 61 TB cases](<https://delftimaging.com/resources/webinars/advancing-tb-screening-in-burkina-faso-with-portable-x-ray-and-cad4tb/>). The AI flagged 50 suspected cases, of which 37 were confirmed and 13 were not TB.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No single source says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
