# Burkina Faso

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** On 10 October 2025 the digital transition minister and the director general of the meteorological agency ANAM [signed a research convention to bring AI into weather forecasting](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/10/14/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-integre-lintelligence-artificielle-au-service-de-la-meteo/>), with ANAM chosen as the pilot body. No tool is reported in service under it. The convention covers research and collaboration between the two signatories, with forecasting as its first field of use.

**Several sectors.** Nothing held shows AI in service in any public sector's system. The nearest held fact is the [ANAM convention](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/10/14/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-integre-lintelligence-artificielle-au-service-de-la-meteo/>), which covers forecasting alone. No tax, customs, health, education or agriculture system is held with an AI tool, built or in service.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system are held. The nearest held fact is the [convention's signing](<https://digitalmagazine.bf/2025/10/14/burkina-faso-le-gouvernement-integre-lintelligence-artificielle-au-service-de-la-meteo/>), which reports none, as the work had only begun.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. An AI tool in service in a public sector's system is not known.
