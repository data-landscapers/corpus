# Zimbabwe

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** In September 2026 police and the Home Affairs ministry said [AI-run cameras at Harare intersections detect traffic violations](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>). The violations are verified and used to fine offenders. In August 2026 the transport minister said the government was [moving toward a nationwide AI traffic system](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200059127/zimbabwe-expands-ai-traffic-cameras-to-tackle-us400m-road-crash-losses>). Infervision, a vendor, says the [health ministry has used its AI chest X-ray reading for TB screening in Gweru since 2024](<https://global.infervision.com/blog/boosting-tb-dectection-in-rural-zimbabwe>). In October 2025 the revenue authority ZIMRA's [2026-2030 strategy adopted AI analytics to find non-compliance](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047040/zimra-turns-to-ai-to-boost-revenue-collection>). In May 2025 UNDP was [piloting AI and drones against quelea birds](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2025/05/12/japan-pilots-ai-projects-in-zimbabwe-agro-health-sectors/>).

**Several sectors.** Police use [AI traffic cameras in Harare](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>). Since 2024 the health ministry has used [AI X-ray reading for TB screening in Gweru](<https://global.infervision.com/blog/boosting-tb-dectection-in-rural-zimbabwe>).

**Results reported.** In September 2026 police said the traffic system had [detected over four million violations](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>). The count stood at 2.3 million in mid-August.

***Also on file***. [AI Fuel Manager](<https://itweb.africa/article/infraco-expands-ai-powered-towers/RgeVDvPRmeoMKJN3>).

***Not found***. No source found says AI is in service in the public systems of two or more sectors.
