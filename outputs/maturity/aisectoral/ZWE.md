# Zimbabwe

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Police and the Home Affairs ministry said in September 2026 that [cameras at Harare intersections, run by an AI traffic management system, detect violations that are verified and used to fine offenders](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>), and the transport minister said the government is [moving toward a nationwide AI traffic system](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200059127/zimbabwe-expands-ai-traffic-cameras-to-tackle-us400m-road-crash-losses>). Infervision, a vendor, says the [Ministry of Health and Child Care has used its AI chest X-ray reading in a TB screening project in Gweru since 2024](<https://global.infervision.com/blog/boosting-tb-dectection-in-rural-zimbabwe>). The revenue authority ZIMRA's [2026-2030 strategy adopts AI analytics to find non-compliance](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047040/zimra-turns-to-ai-to-boost-revenue-collection>), and UNDP is [piloting AI and drones against quelea birds](<https://cajnewsafrica.com/2025/05/12/japan-pilots-ai-projects-in-zimbabwe-agro-health-sectors/>).

**Several sectors.** Traffic policing and health each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [Harare traffic cameras](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>) and the [Gweru TB screening](<https://global.infervision.com/blog/boosting-tb-dectection-in-rural-zimbabwe>).

**Results reported.** Police said the traffic system had [detected over four million violations by the end of September 2026, up from 2.3 million in mid-August](<https://www.zimbabwesituation.com/news/govt-pursues-traffic-violators-as-4m-offences-caught-on-camera/>).
