# Tanzania

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Health, tax and procurement agencies run AI tools. [In March 2025 the Ministry of Health added AI triage to its disease surveillance system](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>). [A Muhimbili University researcher said in August 2026 that the ministry uses AI to read chest X-rays for TB](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/makala/afya/watafiti-waja-na-ai-kubaini-tb-kwa-wenye-vvu-5576206>). [The National Health Insurance Fund says AI in claims processing helped cut settlement time from 120 to 50 days in 2024/25](<https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/digital-transformation-cuts-nhif-claims-payment-period-to-50-days-5661158>). [The revenue authority's AI has flagged duplicated tax stamp codes since November 2023](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>). [Its new domestic tax system answers taxpayers' questions with AI](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>). The World Bank says [NeST, the public procurement system run by PPRA since July 2023, uses AI-driven red flags](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/05/11/from-bureaucracy-to-bytes-digitalization-reshaping-tanzania-s-procurement-future-for-economic-growth>). [A trial of AI eye screening for diabetes was under way in November 2026](<https://newsroom.amref.org/op-ed/2026/11/why-africa-should-build-trust-in-ai-differently/>).

**Several sectors.** Health and tax each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [surveillance triage tool](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>) and [TRA's stamp platform](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>).

**Results reported.** DHIS2 reports the triage tool [cut average time to triage for most alerts from 36 hours to almost instantaneous](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>). TRA says stamp inspections [rose to 1 million scans in 2024 from 250,000 the year before](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>).

***Also on file***. [e-IDSR](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says AI is in service in two or more sectors. Each source names one.
