# Tanzania

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Ministry of Health has [introduced an AI-based triage tool in its DHIS2 event-based surveillance system](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>), as reported in March 2025. The revenue authority TRA says an [AI system on its electronic tax stamp platform, adopted in November 2023, flags stamp codes scanned in different places](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>). A Muhimbili University researcher said in August 2026 that the [Ministry of Health already uses AI to support TB diagnosis from chest X-rays](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/makala/afya/watafiti-waja-na-ai-kubaini-tb-kwa-wenye-vvu-5576206>). TRA said in January 2026 that its [new domestic tax system is connected to AI to answer taxpayers' questions](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/mfumo-wa-idras-kuunganishwa-na-ai-kulinda-taarifa-binafsi-5339964>).

**Several sectors.** Health and tax each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [surveillance triage tool](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>) and [TRA's stamp platform](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>).

**Results reported.** DHIS2 reports the triage tool [cut average time to triage for most alerts from 36 hours to almost instantaneous](<https://dhis2.org/ai-driven-alert-triage-tanzania/>). TRA says stamp inspections [rose to 1 million scans in 2024 from 250,000 the year before](<https://dailynews.co.tz/ai-transforms-tras-measures-to-curb-falsified-electronic-stamps/>).
