# Tunisia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [On 16 May 2026 customs added a machine-learning module to the national selectivity system](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/la-douane-integre-lia-au-sein-de-son-systeme/>). [It targets high-risk customs declarations](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/la-douane-integre-lia-au-sein-de-son-systeme/>). [On 12 October 2026 the customs spokesman, Chokri Jabri, said customs would soon use AI to adapt risk targeting](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [First trials improved targeting quality by 20 to 25 percent, he said](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [AI is also to analyse container-scanner images](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [The head of the National Customs School named the Sanad 2 risk-targeting system among projects under way](<https://news-tunisia.tunisienumerique.com/tunisias-finance-ministry-moves-to-integrate-ai-into-taxation-customs-and-accounting/>). In health, the minister said in February 2025 that the ministry is [developing AI software with InstaDeep for remote analysis of medical images](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/02/21/forum-realites-sur-la-sante-numerique-3500-nouvelles-recrues-et-95-de-dossiers-medicaux-numerises/>), including diabetic retinopathy and mammography. [On 9 October 2026 a customs official said AI in the risk-management and selectivity system would enter use at the end of November](<https://www.radionationale.tn/article/6ac89e5024f658fa460a9f66/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B3-%D8%A8%D9%86-%D8%B2%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%86%D8%B8%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%81-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D8%B7%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D9%81%D8%B9%D9%84-%D9%85%D9%88%D9%81%D9%89-%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%B1-%D9%86%D9%88%D9%81%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%B1>).
