# Tunisia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The Direction Generale des Douanes officially integrated a machine-learning module into the national customs selectivity system to target high-risk customs declarations, per its 16 May 2026 communique](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/la-douane-integre-lia-au-sein-de-son-systeme/>). [Tunisian Customs spokesman Chokri Jabri said on 12 October 2026 that customs is about to integrate AI to adapt risk-targeting criteria automatically and that first trials improved targeting quality by 20 to 25 percent, with AI also to analyse container-scanner images](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [The Director General of the National Customs School said the Sanad 2 system, designed to support risk targeting and certificates of origin in Customs, is among projects currently underway](<https://news-tunisia.tunisienumerique.com/tunisias-finance-ministry-moves-to-integrate-ai-into-taxation-customs-and-accounting/>). In health, the minister said in February 2025 that the ministry is [developing AI software with InstaDeep for remote analysis of medical images](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/02/21/forum-realites-sur-la-sante-numerique-3500-nouvelles-recrues-et-95-de-dossiers-medicaux-numerises/>), including diabetic retinopathy and mammography.
