# Tunisia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [On 16 May 2026 customs added a machine-learning module to the national selectivity system](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/la-douane-integre-lia-au-sein-de-son-systeme/>). [It targets high-risk customs declarations](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/05/16/la-douane-integre-lia-au-sein-de-son-systeme/>). [On 12 October 2026 the customs spokesman, Chokri Jabri, said customs would soon use AI to adapt risk targeting](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [First trials improved targeting quality by 20 to 25 percent, he said](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [AI is also to analyse container-scanner images](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/10/12/douane-lintelligence-artificielle-bientot-utilisee-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-et-controler-les-marchandises/1422415/>). [The head of the National Customs School named the Sanad 2 risk-targeting system among projects under way](<https://news-tunisia.tunisienumerique.com/tunisias-finance-ministry-moves-to-integrate-ai-into-taxation-customs-and-accounting/>). In health, the minister said in February 2025 that the ministry is [developing AI software with InstaDeep for remote analysis of medical images](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/02/21/forum-realites-sur-la-sante-numerique-3500-nouvelles-recrues-et-95-de-dossiers-medicaux-numerises/>), including diabetic retinopathy and mammography.
