# Namibia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The Health Minister said in March 2025 that Namibia has [introduced AI-powered digital chest X-ray technology for TB screening and early diagnosis](<https://english.news.cn/africa/20250329/a143c7051c3f442db05e338cfee1f561/c.html>), and was screened with it at a World TB Day event. In September 2026 [two AI tutoring labs opened at a government secondary school in Luderitz](<https://aitsafrika.com/education/namibias-first-ai-tutoring-labs-launched-in-lderitz-nmh018656-3014-22949-22972>), open to all 436 learners and funded by private firms. The agriculture ministry [signed a N$39.5 million contract for AI and satellite crop monitoring](<https://observer24.com.na/questions-mount-over-n39-5m-us-agriculture-ai-deal/>), which the government [terminated in August 2026](<https://www.namibian.com.na/cabinet-blocks-us-ai-crop-monitoring-tender/>).

**Several sectors.** Health and education each have AI in service, in separate accounts: the [TB screening](<https://english.news.cn/africa/20250329/a143c7051c3f442db05e338cfee1f561/c.html>) and the [school's tutoring labs](<https://aitsafrika.com/education/namibias-first-ai-tutoring-labs-launched-in-lderitz-nmh018656-3014-22949-22972>), one school.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [minister's account of TB screening](<https://english.news.cn/africa/20250329/a143c7051c3f442db05e338cfee1f561/c.html>) gives no count of people screened or cases found.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No single source found says AI is in service in two or more sectors, and no source found reports measured results.
