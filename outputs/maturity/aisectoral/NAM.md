# Namibia

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The agriculture ministry signed a one-year N$39.5 million contract with 6th Grain Corporation to introduce satellite imagery and AI to monitor staple crops, forecast crops and assess drought (Windhoek Observer, citing the ministry and US Embassy)](<https://observer24.com.na/questions-mount-over-n39-5m-us-agriculture-ai-deal/>). [The Namibian reports a N$40m contract awarded in June 2026 to 6th Grain Corporation to deploy an AI and satellite-imagery crop-monitoring system for the agriculture ministry, which the government terminated in August 2026 as not meeting legal and procedural requirements](<https://www.namibian.com.na/cabinet-blocks-us-ai-crop-monitoring-tender/>).
