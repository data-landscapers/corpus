# Morocco

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The medicines agency [launched a licensing platform with AI-based analysis tools on 31 March 2026](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-plateforme-tarkhiss-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-du-secteur-de-la-sante>), starting with cosmetics and hygiene products for about 3,500 operators. A parliamentary working group report says an [AI system in the Draa-Tafilalet education academy predicts pupil performance and flags dropout risk](<https://lematin.ma/nation/maroc-et-intelligence-artificielle-qui-pilote-la-machine/279065>). In customs, [the ADII's 2024 activity report says its BADR intelligence system, which it calls applied AI, handled 1,540 fraud alerts and that predictive algorithms targeted checks](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>), after [machine-learning pilots on declarations](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/08/12/minister-morocco-to-use-ai-to-enhanced-public-services/>).

**Several sectors.** Customs, health regulation and education each have AI in service, in separate accounts: [customs targeting](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>), the [medicines agency's platform](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-plateforme-tarkhiss-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-du-secteur-de-la-sante>) and the [regional dropout-risk system](<https://lematin.ma/nation/maroc-et-intelligence-artificielle-qui-pilote-la-machine/279065>).

**Results reported.** [The ADII says algorithm-based targeting detected 34 million dirhams of evaded duties and taxes in 2024, against 24 million in 2023; 20 million was recovered and 14 million levied as fines](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>). [In July 2026 authorities were working to bring AI into PortNet, the customs single window](<https://en.7news.ma/portnet-processes-more-nearly-200000-customs-declarations-in-june/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No single source says AI is in service in two or more sectors.
