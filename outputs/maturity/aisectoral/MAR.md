# Morocco

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Customs, health regulation and education run AI tools. [Customs says its BADR system, which it calls applied AI, handled 1,540 fraud alerts in 2024](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>). [It followed machine-learning pilots on customs declarations](<https://barlamantoday.com/2024/08/12/minister-morocco-to-use-ai-to-enhanced-public-services/>). [The medicines agency launched a licensing platform with AI analysis tools on 31 March 2026](<https://maroc.ma/fr/actualites/lancement-de-la-plateforme-tarkhiss-pour-accelerer-la-transformation-digitale-du-secteur-de-la-sante>). [An AI system in the Draa-Tafilalet education academy flags pupils at risk of dropping out](<https://lematin.ma/nation/maroc-et-intelligence-artificielle-qui-pilote-la-machine/279065>). [In 2025 the Interior Ministry trained a model to detect false declarations in the social register](<https://telquel.ma/instant-t/2025/11/06/le-ministere-de-linterieur-developpe-un-systeme-intelligent-pour-detecter-les-fraudes-dans-le-registre-social-unifie_1959757/>).

**Several sectors.** [In September 2024 the budget minister told parliament that customs uses machine learning to predict risk in clearance and detect fraud](<https://en.hespress.com/90558-moroccan-ministry-of-finance-eyes-ai-to-boost-efficiency.html>). [In the same answer he said the tax directorate uses AI in chatbots for taxpayers and against tax fraud](<https://en.hespress.com/90558-moroccan-ministry-of-finance-eyes-ai-to-boost-efficiency.html>). Health regulation and a [regional education academy](<https://lematin.ma/nation/maroc-et-intelligence-artificielle-qui-pilote-la-machine/279065>) have AI tools of their own.

**Results reported.** Customs reports what its targeting finds. [Algorithm-based targeting detected 34 million dirhams of evaded duties and taxes in 2024](<https://fr.hespress.com/438610-adii-2024-plus-de-recettes-plus-de-fluidite-plus-de-technologie.html>). That compares with 24 million in 2023. Of the 2024 sum, 20 million was recovered and 14 million levied as fines. [In July 2026 work began to bring AI into PortNet, the customs single window](<https://en.7news.ma/portnet-processes-more-nearly-200000-customs-declarations-in-june/>). [In November 2026 customs and the Korea Customs Service entered the last phase of a project applying AI to revenue management](<https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/11/345265/morocco-korea-advance-customs-cooperation-on-ai-digitalization/>), begun in May.
