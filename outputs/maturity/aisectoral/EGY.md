# Egypt

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Several Egyptian agencies run AI tools. [In November 2025 the customs head said three smart systems run on the Nafeza platform](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8169196>). They classify tariffs, value goods and score each shipment's risk. [In September 2025 a health ministry adviser said some hospitals use AI daily to diagnose tumours](<https://www.masrtimes.com/587268>). [In November 2026 a social solidarity official said the Takaful and Karama cash programme uses AI](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). It targets aid, answers complaints and checks applicants' data. [In November 2026 Youm7 reported that the justice ministry's remote power-of-attorney service checks identity with AI](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/11/%D8%A8%D8%B5%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A2%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%82-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF-%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1/7612009>). It matches a face scan against state biometric records. [In September 2026 the tax authority head said AI now calculates real estate disposition tax](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4359416>). [In November 2026 she said AI analyses its store of about 4 billion e-documents](<https://radarmisr.com/article/%D9%85%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%87-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-4-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AF>). [In November 2026 the irrigation minister said AI services identify where farmland needs water](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>). [They are in use in Ismailia and Suez governorates](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>).

**Several sectors.** Each sector's AI is reported in a separate account. [The AI council's 2026 report lists a smart judicial assistant](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [In November 2024 the remote sensing authority mapped winter crops with AI](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2024/11/05/narss-agriculture-ministry-launch-map-of-winter-crops/>). [In November 2026 a Senate study listed government AI initiatives in health, agriculture, planning and banking](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/23/أحمد-أبوهشيمة-مصر-لا-تبدأ-من-الصفر-فى-الذكاء-الاصطناعى/7629258>). [They include MASA for early breast cancer detection and GEO AI for state property](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/23/أحمد-أبوهشيمة-مصر-لا-تبدأ-من-الصفر-فى-الذكاء-الاصطناعى/7629258>).

**Results reported.** In August 2025 the justice and ICT ministers [launched remote criminal hearings at East Alexandria Court with AI speech-to-text minutes](<https://www.technotime.net/10747>). The ICT minister said the [speech-to-text reaches an accuracy above 96%](<https://www.technotime.net/10747>).
