# Egypt

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [The national AI council's first-half 2026 report lists the launch of a smart judicial assistant among its achievements](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). The cabinet's think tank IDSC [says AI projects are under way in government on health, water management and agriculture](<https://idsc.gov.eg/upload/DocumentLibraryIssues/AttachmentA/9154/Digitalization%20in%20Egypt.pdf>).

**Several sectors.** A second sector has AI in service. In November 2024 the remote sensing authority NARSS and the agriculture ministry [used remote sensing and AI to inventory and classify crops by governorate](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2024/11/05/narss-agriculture-ministry-launch-map-of-winter-crops/>), producing the 2023-24 winter crop map. The irrigation ministry [began a pilot in Kafr El Sheikh in June 2024](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/132896/Egypt-introduces-new-satellite-image-analysis-application-to-enhance-irrigation>) using machine learning to map crop types from satellite images for water distribution.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system are held. The nearest held fact is the [winter crop map's launch](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2024/11/05/narss-agriculture-ministry-launch-map-of-winter-crops/>), which gives no accuracy figure.
