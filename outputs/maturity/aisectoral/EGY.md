# Egypt

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** Several Egyptian agencies run AI tools. [In November 2025 the customs head said three smart systems run on the Nafeza platform](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8169196>). They classify tariffs, value goods and score each shipment's risk. [In September 2025 a health ministry adviser said some hospitals use AI daily to diagnose tumours](<https://www.masrtimes.com/587268>). [In November 2026 a social solidarity official said the Takaful and Karama cash programme uses AI](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). It targets aid, answers complaints and checks applicants' data. [In November 2026 Youm7 reported that the justice ministry's remote power-of-attorney service checks identity with AI](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/11/%D8%A8%D8%B5%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%AC%D9%87-%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%87%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%82%D9%8A%D8%B9-%D8%A2%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%82-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%87%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF-%D8%A5%D8%B5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1/7612009>). It matches a face scan against state biometric records. [The tax authority said in July 2026 that an AI tax app was about to launch](<https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/4306657>). [In November 2026 the irrigation minister said AI services identify where farmland needs water](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>). [They are in use in Ismailia and Suez governorates](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/7/وزراء-الاتصالات-والاستثمار-والمالية-والري-يعرضون-خططًا-حكومية-للتحول-الرقمي/7609762>).

**Several sectors.** Each sector's AI is reported in a separate account. [The AI council's 2026 report lists a smart judicial assistant](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/egypt-ai-council-national-ai-strategy-quantum-computing/>). [In November 2024 the remote sensing authority mapped winter crops with AI](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2024/11/05/narss-agriculture-ministry-launch-map-of-winter-crops/>). No one source covers two sectors.
