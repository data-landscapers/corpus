# Madagascar

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The customs director general told an IMF panel on 27 March 2025 that the [AI-based risk assessment tool analyses millions of clearance records](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/la-douane-malagasy-un-modele-regional-dans-lusage-de-la-donnee-et-de-lia/>), with a data warehouse in operation since late 2024, automated controls cutting clearance times and predictive analysis used against fraud. The tool came from a [project with the Korea Customs Service launched on 4 December 2024](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1550>). On 7 October 2025 customs [launched a generative-AI agent that analyses invoices and detects false or inconsistent ones](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/lagent-svo-deploye-la-douane-franchit-une-nouvelle-etape/>). The judicial authorities [are evaluating AI tools for judicial support](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/madagascar-develops-digital-criminal-records-platform-to-modernize-justice-system>), announced in June 2026.

**Several sectors.** Customs is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [judicial evaluation](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/madagascar-develops-digital-criminal-records-platform-to-modernize-justice-system>), not yet in service.

**Results reported.** No measured results of a sectoral AI system have been found. The [director general's account](<https://www.douanes.gov.mg/la-douane-malagasy-un-modele-regional-dans-lusage-de-la-donnee-et-de-lia/>) describes shorter clearance times without figures.

***Also on file***. [EC-MADA programme civil registration digitisation](<https://www.intelligentcio.com/africa/2026/05/08/ai-powered-digitisation-project-positions-madagascar-as-a-model-for-african-digital-identity-modernisation/>).

***Not found***. No source found reports AI in service in a second sector, and measured results of a sectoral AI system.
