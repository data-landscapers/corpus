# Madagascar

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Midi Madagasikara says Malagasy customs presented results obtained through its Enhanced Risk Assessment (ERA) smart targeting system at an IMF panel](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/douanes-madagascar-un-modele-dans-lutilisation-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [Madagascar's judicial authorities are evaluating AI tools for judicial support functions, announced 2 June 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/public-management/madagascar-develops-digital-criminal-records-platform-to-modernize-justice-system>). [Midi Madagasikara says the customs data and AI centre CEDIA, launched in May 2025, automates anomaly detection and refines risk-targeting tools](<https://midi-madagasikara.mg/douanes-madagascar-un-modele-dans-lutilisation-de-lintelligence-artificielle/>). [Newsmada says Madagascar's Customs has used AI since 2022 to analyse data, strengthen controls and anticipate risks (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://newsmada.com/2025/08/22/gestion-des-donnees-donnees-ia-et-souverainete-madagascar-engage-le-debat/>).
