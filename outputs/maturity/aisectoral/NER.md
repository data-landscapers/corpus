# Niger

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [ADN says Rayuwa, an AI farming platform, is free on the Play Store](<https://adn.ne/formation-rayuwa-ladn-renforce-les-capacites-des-institutions-techniques-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>). [It has a text and voice assistant, crop disease diagnosis from images and local forecasts](<https://adn.ne/formation-rayuwa-ladn-renforce-les-capacites-des-institutions-techniques-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>). [On 27-28 August 2025 staff of three national directorates were trained to manage its content](<https://adn.ne/formation-rayuwa-ladn-renforce-les-capacites-des-institutions-techniques-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>). [They cover meteorology, water resources and plant protection](<https://adn.ne/formation-rayuwa-ladn-renforce-les-capacites-des-institutions-techniques-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>). [ILLIMI Niger, a joint government and UN project, is building a data platform with AI tools to anticipate crises](<https://illimi-niger.com/illimi-niger-les-donnees-au-service-de-la-securite-humaine-et-de-la-paix/>). [It began with its first National Technical Group meeting](<https://illimi-niger.com/illimi-niger-les-donnees-au-service-de-la-securite-humaine-et-de-la-paix/>).
