[ILLIMI Niger, a joint Government of Niger and UN project, is building a digital data platform integrating ethical AI tools for predictive analyses to anticipate crises, launched with its first National Technical Group meeting](<https://illimi-niger.com/illimi-niger-les-donnees-au-service-de-la-securite-humaine-et-de-la-paix/>).
