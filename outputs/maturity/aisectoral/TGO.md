# Togo

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** The economy minister [put a market-intelligence platform into service on 12 March 2026](<https://togopresse.tg/le-gouvernement-lance-la-solution-digital-market-intelligence-au-profit-de-la-mobilisation-des-ressources-publiques/>), using AI to detect suspicious transactions, fraud and smuggling, run from a command centre staffed by tax, customs and commerce services. It [cross-checks tax-marking and inspection data to point inspectors to risk zones](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/togo-letat-deploie-lintelligence-artificielle-pour-traquer-les-fraudes-et-renforcer-ses-recettes_60463>). The Togo Data Lab is [developing a satellite and AI project for farmers with the Ministry of Agriculture](<https://news.berkeley.edu/2025/07/25/togo-and-uc-berkeley-a-data-science-alliance-to-build-a-dynamic-west-african-nation/>), and Togo AI Lab [was recruiting in September 2026 to lead AI tools for agriculture, health, education and the environment](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1009-20017-togo-ai-lab-seeks-technical-lead-for-public-sector-ai-projects>).

**Several sectors.** Revenue is the only sector held with AI in service. The nearest held fact elsewhere is the [agriculture project](<https://news.berkeley.edu/2025/07/25/togo-and-uc-berkeley-a-data-science-alliance-to-build-a-dynamic-west-african-nation/>), not yet in service. Earlier, in December 2020 to April 2021, the government [used machine learning on phone and satellite data to target Novissi cash transfers to 154,238 people](<https://investdesk.africa/2026/10/22/african-governments-become-customers-for-local-ai/>), which Berkeley researchers found 42% more accurate than targeting the poorest districts.

**Results reported.** The platform's [announced precision rate is 89.89%](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/togo-letat-deploie-lintelligence-artificielle-pour-traquer-les-fraudes-et-renforcer-ses-recettes_60463>), as reported at its launch in March 2026.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source says AI is in service in a public sector other than revenue.
