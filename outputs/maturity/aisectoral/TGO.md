# Togo

## AI in sector systems

**AI in service.** [Togo-Presse reports the minister of Economy put the Digital Market Intelligence platform, built by SICPA, into service on 12 March 2026, using AI and data analysis to detect suspicious transactions and fraud and smuggling, run from a command centre staffed by tax, customs and commerce services](<https://togopresse.tg/le-gouvernement-lance-la-solution-digital-market-intelligence-au-profit-de-la-mobilisation-des-ressources-publiques/>). [Togo First says Togo AI Lab, led by the Ministry of Public Service Efficiency and Digital Transformation with CEGA at Berkeley, is recruiting a technical lead to oversee machine-learning, generative AI and satellite-imagery tools for sectors including agriculture, health, education and the environment](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/itc/1009-20017-togo-ai-lab-seeks-technical-lead-for-public-sector-ai-projects>).
