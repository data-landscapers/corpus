# Congo

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In September 2026 Kazakh deputy PM Zhaslan Madiyev said Congo and Kazakhstan are working to build and launch Congo's first Earth-observation satellite](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/technologies-spatiales-le-congo-se-prepare-lancer-son-premier-satellite-dobservation-171586>). [It is meant to help monitor forest resources, agricultural and mining activity and flood-exposed zones](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/technologies-spatiales-le-congo-se-prepare-lancer-son-premier-satellite-dobservation-171586>). [FAO's GIEWS country brief, reference date 23 June 2026, reports from satellite-based imagery on rainfall in most croplands](<https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/COG/pdf/COG.pdf>). [It found precipitation adequate and well distributed since December 2025](<https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/COG/pdf/COG.pdf>). [It lists GIEWS Earth Observation for Crop Monitoring among its tools](<https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/COG/pdf/COG.pdf>).
