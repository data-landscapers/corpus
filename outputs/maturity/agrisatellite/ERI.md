# Eritrea

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [A study dated December 2025, funded by the Ministry of Agriculture through the IFAD-supported IADP, mapped irrigated land in Dighe and Gala Nefhi](<https://repository.up.ac.za/items/24403df4-7cf1-4497-83cc-c278af581f1f/full>). [It used Sentinel-2 imagery for 2015-2024](<https://repository.up.ac.za/items/24403df4-7cf1-4497-83cc-c278af581f1f/full>). [In September 2025 FAO's Desert Locust Information Service trained national locust information officers in Rome, Eritrea's among them](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [The tools taught included Google Earth Engine satellite image analysis](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>).
