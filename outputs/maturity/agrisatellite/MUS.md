## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [A conference paper by University of Mauritius and Government of Mauritius authors trained a CNN on Satellogic high-resolution satellite images to classify agricultural farmland, urban, forest and barren land in the Black River district, at up to 99% reported precision](<https://doi.org/10.1109/icares67579.2025.11371462>). [UNCTAD says the Food and Agricultural Research and Extension Institute (FAREI) has been involved in CropWatch-ICP activities since 2021 and held a CropWatch training workshop on 18-19 September 2025 to build staff capacity in satellite-based crop monitoring and early warning](<https://unctad.org/meeting/national-training-workshop-satellite-based-crop-monitoring-using-cropwatch-system>).
