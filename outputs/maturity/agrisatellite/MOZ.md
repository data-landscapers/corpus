# Mozambique

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In 2025 the Statistics from Space project tested an Earth-observation crop-type mapping workflow in Mozambique](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>). [It generated openly accessible crop type maps and statistics for Gaza, Manica and Zambezia for the 2024/25 season](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>). [The authors' affiliations include Universidade Eduardo Mondlane, IFPRI and the University of Twente](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>). [In June 2024 CGIAR's Statistics from Space project, run with Eduardo Mondlane University, the agriculture ministry and the statistics institute, piloted satellite-based crop sampling at 92 sites in Gaza province](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/statistics-from-space-a-new-approach-to-agricultural-production-data-for-mozambique>). [In August 2026 FAO trained technicians of the National Irrigation Institute on a satellite dashboard of irrigation performance for the Lower Limpopo schemes](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/news-and-events/news/news-detail/mozambique-strengthens-use-of-satellite-data-to-assess-irrigation-performance/en>).
