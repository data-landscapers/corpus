## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In 2025 the Statistics from Space project tested an Earth-observation crop-type mapping workflow in Mozambique](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>). [It generated openly accessible crop type maps and statistics for Gaza, Manica and Zambezia for the 2024/25 season](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>). [The authors' affiliations include Universidade Eduardo Mondlane, IFPRI and the University of Twente](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/items/d99da2a4-6d50-4402-9a4a-83be3fe4260e>).
