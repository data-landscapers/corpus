# Cote d'Ivoire

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [SODEXAM says it signed a partnership convention with JooL International on 10 August 2026](<https://sodexam.com/climat-et-agriculture-la-sodexam-et-jool-international-misent-sur-lintelligence-artificielle/>). The convention puts meteorological data, AI and satellite technologies to work anticipating climate risk and building solutions for more resilient agriculture. [AICCRA said in May 2026 that the agriculture ministry can now draw seasonally updated satellite maps and forecasts of rice area and yield](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/space-soil-cote-divoire-harnesses-riice-secure-its-rice-future>). They come from the adapted RIICE tool. It is the first time the ministry has had such data.
