# Namibia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In September 2025 Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [AgriWatch covers Namibia among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [China handed Namibia its first satellite data receiving ground station, at the Telecom Earth Station outside Windhoek](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-gets-chinese-satellite-ground-station/LPwQ57lbLyAqNgkj>). [The station lets Namibia receive satellite data for fields including agriculture, and the president asked all sector ministries and agencies to maximise its benefits](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-gets-chinese-satellite-ground-station/LPwQ57lbLyAqNgkj>). [NASA Harvest says satellite tools and cropland masks built under a USAID-funded FAO pilot were provided to the agriculture ministry and used in 2023](<https://www.nasaharvest.org/news/nasa-harvest-partners-fao-improve-agricultural-monitoring-across-malawi-namibia-and-kazakhstan>).
