# Namibia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, with AgriWatch covering this country among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [China handed Namibia its first satellite data receiving ground station at the Telecom Earth Station outside Windhoek, which will let Namibia receive satellite data for fields including agriculture, and the president asked all sector ministries and agencies to maximise its benefits](<https://itweb.africa/article/namibia-gets-chinese-satellite-ground-station/LPwQ57lbLyAqNgkj>). [NASA Harvest says satellite tools and cropland masks built under a USAID-funded FAO pilot were provided to the agriculture ministry and used in 2023](<https://www.nasaharvest.org/news/nasa-harvest-partners-fao-improve-agricultural-monitoring-across-malawi-namibia-and-kazakhstan>).
