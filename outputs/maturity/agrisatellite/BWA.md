# Botswana

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [University of Botswana researchers used CHIRPS satellite rainfall and CHIRTS satellite-derived temperature data (25 km) to compute drought indices](<https://doi.org/10.3390/cli14040077>). They tested the indices against national crop-yield data for agricultural drought monitoring in Botswana. [University of Botswana researchers also used CHIRPS satellite rainfall estimates (1981-2024, 25 km) to regionalise Botswana into drought zones](<https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06154-6>). They recommended building Nino 3.4 and SOI indices into national drought forecasting and early-warning systems. [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). It is for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, and covers this country among the six SASSCAL states.
