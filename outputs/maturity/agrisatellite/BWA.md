# Botswana

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [University of Botswana researchers used CHIRPS satellite rainfall and CHIRTS satellite-derived temperature data (25 km) to compute drought indices and test them against national crop-yield data for agricultural drought monitoring in Botswana](<https://doi.org/10.3390/cli14040077>). [University of Botswana researchers used CHIRPS satellite rainfall estimates (1981-2024, 25 km) to regionalise Botswana into drought zones and recommended building Nino 3.4 and SOI indices into national drought forecasting and early-warning systems](<https://doi.org/10.1007/s00704-026-06154-6>). [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, with AgriWatch covering this country among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>).
