# Niger

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The World Bank paper says that from 2021 to 2025 the Government of Niger ran a rapid drought response pilot under the Wadata Talaka safety net that used satellite data (the WRSI index) to trigger emergency payments, and that it was activated in October 2021 and again in 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>). [The World Bank says the Government of Niger used FAO WRSI satellite early-warning data in 2021, for the first time and as a pilot, to identify drought-affected areas and trigger cash transfers to about 15,400 households in four communes (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/climatechange/responding-faster-droughts-satellites-and-adaptive-social-protection-niger>).

**Published regularly.** No source found since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>).

**Reaching farmers.** [The World Bank paper says the WRSI trigger activated emergency payments to 15,400 drought-affected households across 4 communes in October 2021, and that in 2023 this took place in 20 communes (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [IGNN Teledetection et technique spatiales (TTS)](<https://ignn.net/la-teledetection-et-technique-spacialestts.html>); [SARRA crop model](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government satellite-based farming service is in use. The WRSI drought trigger is described as a pilot.
