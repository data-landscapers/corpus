# Niger

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [A World Bank paper says the government ran a rapid drought response pilot from 2021 to 2025 under the Wadata Talaka safety net](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>). [The pilot used the WRSI satellite index to trigger emergency payments, and was activated in October 2021 and again in 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>). [The World Bank says the government first used FAO WRSI satellite early-warning data in 2021, as a pilot, to identify drought-affected areas](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/climatechange/responding-faster-droughts-satellites-and-adaptive-social-protection-niger>). [The data triggered cash transfers to about 15,400 households in four communes](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/climatechange/responding-faster-droughts-satellites-and-adaptive-social-protection-niger>).

**Published regularly.** No source found since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest fact: [a World Bank paper says the WRSI trigger was activated in October 2021 and again in 2023](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>).

**Reaching farmers.** [The World Bank paper says the WRSI trigger activated emergency payments to 15,400 drought-affected households across 4 communes in October 2021](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>). [In 2023 the trigger activated payments in 20 communes](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099943305072619092/pdf/IDU-63c24804-35f9-40e0-b867-a958698a0cd7.pdf>).
