# Uganda

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [KijaniSpace deployed a field monitoring station at Greater Hope Farm, Mayuge District, as part of a crop farming pilot](<https://www.kijanispace.eu/2026/08/03/kijanispace-deploys-smart-field-monitoring-infrastructure-at-greater-hope-farm-in-uganda/>). The station is to be integrated with its Space-IoT Box and Copernicus Earth Observation data for irrigation, crop health and advisory services. [NASA Harvest uses satellite imagery for agriculture](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). It and its Africa director Catherine Nakalembe, of the University of Maryland, map cropland in Uganda, Kenya and Senegal. They classify crop types and estimate yields using machine learning.
