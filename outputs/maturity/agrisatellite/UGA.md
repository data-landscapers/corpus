## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [KijaniSpace deployed a field monitoring station at Greater Hope Farm, Mayuge District, as part of a crop farming pilot to be integrated with its Space-IoT Box and Copernicus Earth Observation data for irrigation, crop health and advisory services](<https://www.kijanispace.eu/2026/08/03/kijanispace-deploys-smart-field-monitoring-infrastructure-at-greater-hope-farm-in-uganda/>). [NASA Harvest, which uses satellite imagery for agriculture, and its Africa director Catherine Nakalembe of the University of Maryland map cropland, classify crop types and estimate yields in Uganda, Kenya and Senegal using machine learning](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>).
