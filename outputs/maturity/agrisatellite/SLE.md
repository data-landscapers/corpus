# Sierra Leone

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Farmerline says it signed a five-year MoU with the Ministry of Communication, Technology and Innovation on 12 June 2024](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [The MoU commits it to use high-resolution satellite imagery for boundary delineation, tree counting, deforestation tracking and monitoring illegal mining, for better decisions in agriculture](<https://farmerline.co/revolutionizing-sierra-leones-agriculture-farmerline-group-and-moctis-groundbreaking-tech-partnership/>). [In May 2025 the agriculture ministry launched a weather-index insurance scheme with satellite-triggered payouts](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salone-launches-first-index-insurance-farmers/>). [It was piloted in three districts for rice and maize with Activa, SLICOM and UNCDF](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/salone-launches-first-index-insurance-farmers/>).
