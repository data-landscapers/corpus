# DR Congo

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [SPACEBEL says it signed contracts on 16 June 2026 with the DRC research ministry to supply and maintain the RDC-SAT Earth observation satellite](<https://www.spacebel.com/en/news/the_first_%E2%80%98made_in_belgium%E2%80%99_commercial_satellite_for_the_democratic_republic_of_the_congo_a_landmark_contract_for_spacebel_000260/14>). [The satellite supports the National Centre for Remote Sensing, and SPACEBEL says it will help manage natural resources including agriculture](<https://www.spacebel.com/en/news/the_first_%E2%80%98made_in_belgium%E2%80%99_commercial_satellite_for_the_democratic_republic_of_the_congo_a_landmark_contract_for_spacebel_000260/14>). [In March 2026 the national remote-sensing centre held the first coordination committee of a JICA project](<https://kt.cd/2026/03/30/projet-teledetection-le-cnt-et-la-jica-mettent-en-place-le-comite-conjoint-de-coordination/>). [The project equips the centre to supply satellite analysis to the agriculture ministry](<https://kt.cd/2026/03/30/projet-teledetection-le-cnt-et-la-jica-mettent-en-place-le-comite-conjoint-de-coordination/>). [AXA Climate says the agriculture ministry underwrites a parametric insurance product for up to 300,000 farmers in three provinces](<https://climate.axa/publications/the-first-climate-insurance-product/>). [It is triggered by satellite rainfall indices and was to launch from 2025](<https://climate.axa/publications/the-first-climate-insurance-product/>). [The agriculture ministry holds the contract for a World Bank-backed crop insurance pilot in Kasai-Central that uses satellite-derived rainfall data to trigger payouts](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2210-60021-dr-congo-compensates-17-180-farmers-under-climate-insurance-scheme>). [It began paying compensation on 14 October 2026](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2210-60021-dr-congo-compensates-17-180-farmers-under-climate-insurance-scheme>).
