## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [SPACEBEL says it signed contracts on 16 June 2026 with the DRC Ministry of Higher Education, Universities, Scientific Research and Innovation to supply and maintain the RDC-SAT Earth observation satellite supporting the National Centre for Remote Sensing, which it says will help manage natural resources including agriculture](<https://www.spacebel.com/en/news/the_first_%E2%80%98made_in_belgium%E2%80%99_commercial_satellite_for_the_democratic_republic_of_the_congo_a_landmark_contract_for_spacebel_000260/14>). [The national remote-sensing centre held the first coordination committee of a JICA project to equip it to supply satellite analysis to the agriculture ministry (March 2026)](<https://kt.cd/2026/03/30/projet-teledetection-le-cnt-et-la-jica-mettent-en-place-le-comite-conjoint-de-coordination/>). [AXA Climate says the agriculture ministry underwrites a parametric insurance product for up to 300,000 farmers in three provinces, triggered by satellite rainfall indices, to launch from 2025](<https://climate.axa/publications/the-first-climate-insurance-product/>).
