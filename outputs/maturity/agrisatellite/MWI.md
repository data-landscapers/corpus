# Malawi

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The CEOS Earth Observation Handbook says NASA Harvest, with FAO, adapted its satellite-based GEOCIF yield forecast model at the request of Malawi's Ministry of Agriculture for the 2024 cropping season, and the National Committee on Crop Estimates recommended incorporating it into the crop estimates survey](<https://eohandbook.com/2026casestudies/malawi.html>). [The Department of Climate Change and Meteorological Services piloted WFP's PRISM tool, which combines satellite imagery with rainfall, soil moisture and vegetation data, for drought monitoring in five southern districts (August 2025)](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>).
