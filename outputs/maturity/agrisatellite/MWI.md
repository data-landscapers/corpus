# Malawi

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The CEOS Earth Observation Handbook says NASA Harvest, with FAO, adapted its satellite-based GEOCIF yield forecast model for the 2024 cropping season](<https://eohandbook.com/2026casestudies/malawi.html>). [It did so at the request of Malawi's Ministry of Agriculture](<https://eohandbook.com/2026casestudies/malawi.html>). [The National Committee on Crop Estimates recommended incorporating it into the crop estimates survey](<https://eohandbook.com/2026casestudies/malawi.html>). [In August 2025 the Department of Climate Change and Meteorological Services piloted WFP's PRISM tool for drought monitoring in five southern districts](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>). [PRISM combines satellite imagery with rainfall, soil moisture and vegetation data](<https://www.maravipost.com/malawi-pilots-prism-drought-monitoring-tool-to-strengthen-climate-resilience/>).
