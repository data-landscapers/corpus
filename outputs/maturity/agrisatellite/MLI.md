# Mali

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [AICCRA reports that, with the Ministry of Agriculture, the Institut d'Economie Rurale and Mali-Meteo, it extended RIICE satellite rice monitoring to major irrigated and rainfed rice zones and prepared institutional frameworks to operate the tool within national agricultural monitoring systems](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/publications/institutionalization-riice-tool-agricultural-investment-planning-and-climate-risk>). [IWMI says the WaPOR-based Mali Drought Monitoring System, co-developed with the National Directorate of Agriculture, was in beta in October 2025, to be run by the directorate once validated](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e772f400-92b7-402b-891d-d6210329e6f2/Drought%20monitoring%20-%20Mali-H053675.pdf>). [A March 2024 workshop with the directorate began co-developing the platform](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/news-and-events/news/news-detail/workshop-to-co-design-an-agricultural-drought-monitoring-platform-in-mali/en>).
