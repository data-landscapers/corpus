# Togo

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The agriculture ministry states that, with FSRP support, ANAMET has been equipped with a satellite-image reception antenna and now accesses satellite data used for the agro-hydro-climatic forecasts it prepares each year for farmers and planners](<https://agriculture.gouv.tg/au-coeur-de-linnovation-climatique-une-nouvelle-meteo-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>). [Digital minister Cina Lawson announced on 1 October 2026 a cooperation under which Kyrgyzstan is to transmit satellite earth-observation data to Togo, with training and technology transfer, to improve agricultural productivity among other uses](<https://togopresse.tg/technologies-spatiales-le-togo-et-le-kirghizistan-lancent-une-cooperation/>). [The Togo Data Lab and the agriculture ministry were developing a satellite and AI project on land use, yields and soil fertility in July 2025](<https://news.berkeley.edu/2025/07/25/togo-and-uc-berkeley-a-data-science-alliance-to-build-a-dynamic-west-african-nation/>).
