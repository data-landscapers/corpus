# Togo

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The Togo AI Lab, run by the digital-transformation ministry with UC Berkeley, uses satellite imagery to track agricultural plots and is building models to forecast annual maize and sorghum production, per the funder FID](<https://fundinnovation.dev/news/immersion-togo-ai-lab-service-politiques-publiques>). [The agriculture ministry states that, with FSRP support, ANAMET has been equipped with a satellite-image reception antenna and now accesses satellite data used for the agro-hydro-climatic forecasts it prepares each year for farmers and planners](<https://agriculture.gouv.tg/au-coeur-de-linnovation-climatique-une-nouvelle-meteo-pour-une-agriculture-plus-resiliente/>).
