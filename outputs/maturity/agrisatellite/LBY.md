# Libya

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO trained Libyan national stakeholders in Tunis on 20-23 April 2026 in remote-sensing workflows for generating land cover maps on the MERWAT geospatial platform, which supports water use and agricultural infrastructure decisions in Libya](<https://www.fao.org/agroinformatics/news/news-detail/tunisia-strengthens-geospatial-platforms-and-ai-tools-for-sustainable-water-and-oasis-management/en>). [Experts of the Grain Production Authority and Misrata's Agronomic Research Center trialled the GMES services MISBAR, GUETCROP and SAQIYA on their own plots in 2025](<https://www.oss-online.org/en/GMES%26%20Libye>). [The Agricultural Research Center was trained in February 2024 on the MISBAR, MISLAND and GUETCROP Earth-observation tools](<https://projet.oss-online.org/GMES-Africa/en/blog/oss-hosting-representatives-libyan-agricultural-research-center-arc-training-workshop-seasonal>). [The Libyan remote-sensing centre used MISLAND to map cultivated-area change and crop growth at Misrata for the Agricultural Research Center (December 2023)](<https://alsabaah.ly/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%85%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/>).
