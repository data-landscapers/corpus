# Eswatini

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The handbook says the Eswatini DroughtMap Hub, run with the National Drought Management Center, Ministry of Agriculture and Meteorological Service, computes a Combined Drought Indicator from CHIRPS and MODIS satellite data and publishes it per inkhundla in a public area](<https://wiki.cloud.akvo.org/books/droughtmap-hub/page/operational-handbook>). [The handbook says the Combined Drought Indicator is computed automatically each month for the previous month, then reviewed, validated and published](<https://wiki.cloud.akvo.org/books/droughtmap-hub/page/operational-handbook>). [An RCMRD report for Eswatini's National Disaster Management Agency (CTCN-funded) says crop monitoring pilots combining drones and Sentinel-2 imagery were run at four sites in April 2021, and a satellite-based maize yield model was applied nationally for 2020 (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.ctc-n.org/system/files/dossier/3b/Framework%20and%20Case%20Study%20Report_UAV_and_Satellite_Remote_Sensing_for_Agricultural_Monitoring_and_Vulnerability_Assessments_in_the_Kingdom_of_Eswatini_final%20%281%29.pdf>).
