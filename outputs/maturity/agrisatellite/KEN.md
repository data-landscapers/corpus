## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [NASA Harvest's Catherine Nakalembe says she set out to map crop types in western Kenya using satellite image data](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). [She uses machine learning to map cropland, classify crop types and estimate yields in Kenya and other African countries](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). [The Kenya Space Agency says Kenya launched the CroME Initiative with the agriculture ministry, Microsoft AI for Good Lab and NASA Harvest](<https://ksa.go.ke/launch_crome.html>). [CroME uses satellite images and AI to provide crop maps, crop growth monitoring, yield predictions and crop damage detection](<https://ksa.go.ke/launch_crome.html>). [In 2024 the ADM-Kenya project, co-developed with the agriculture ministry, delivered four satellite drought and crop-condition products](<https://publications.admkenya.eu/Final_ADMKenya.pdf>). [The ministry plans to add them to its monthly food security bulletin](<https://publications.admkenya.eu/Final_ADMKenya.pdf>).
