# Kenya

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [NASA Harvest's Catherine Nakalembe says she set out to map crop types in western Kenya using satellite image data, and uses machine learning to map cropland, classify crop types and estimate yields in Kenya and other African countries](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). [The Kenya Space Agency says Kenya launched the CroME Initiative with the Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development, Microsoft AI for Good Lab and NASA Harvest, using satellite images and AI to provide crop maps, crop growth monitoring, yield predictions and crop damage detection](<https://ksa.go.ke/launch_crome.html>). [The ADM-Kenya project, co-developed with the agriculture ministry, delivered four satellite drought and crop-condition products, which the ministry plans to add to its monthly food security bulletin (2024)](<https://publications.admkenya.eu/Final_ADMKenya.pdf>).
