# Kenya

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [NASA Harvest's Catherine Nakalembe says she set out to map crop types in western Kenya using satellite image data](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). [She uses machine learning to map cropland, classify crop types and estimate yields in Kenya and other African countries](<https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/>). [The Kenya Space Agency says Kenya launched the CroME Initiative with the agriculture ministry, Microsoft AI for Good Lab and NASA Harvest](<https://ksa.go.ke/launch_crome.html>). [CroME uses satellite images and AI to provide crop maps, crop growth monitoring, yield predictions and crop damage detection](<https://ksa.go.ke/launch_crome.html>). [In 2024 the ADM-Kenya project, co-developed with the agriculture ministry, delivered four satellite drought and crop-condition products](<https://publications.admkenya.eu/Final_ADMKenya.pdf>). [The ministry plans to add them to its monthly food security bulletin](<https://publications.admkenya.eu/Final_ADMKenya.pdf>). [Kenya's National Drought Management Authority operates the Drought Early Warning System, which uses satellite imagery with ground stations to monitor rainfall, temperature and soil moisture](<https://ndma.go.ke/drought-information/>). [The State Department for Livestock Development says its livestock insurance programme uses satellite NDVI forage data to decide payouts in arid counties](<https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Aligning%20KLIP%20to%20UNFCCC%20De-risking%20strategies%20to%20enhance%20adaptation%20finance.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [NDMA says it publishes monthly drought early warning bulletins that draw on satellite imagery](<https://ndma.go.ke/drought-information/>).

**Reaching farmers.** No count of farmers reached by the bulletins has been found. The nearest fact: [the livestock insurance programme has covered 100,000 people in six years](<https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Aligning%20KLIP%20to%20UNFCCC%20De-risking%20strategies%20to%20enhance%20adaptation%20finance.pdf>). [Its payouts total KSh 1.3 billion](<https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Aligning%20KLIP%20to%20UNFCCC%20De-risking%20strategies%20to%20enhance%20adaptation%20finance.pdf>).
