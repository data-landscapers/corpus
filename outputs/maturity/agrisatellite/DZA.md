# Algeria

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The CGIAR tool series says the WaPOR-based Water Use Tool is used by the WaPOR Digital Unit at the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development, hosted by its Department of Statistics and Strategies, and uses 20 m WaPOR satellite data to compute dekadal irrigation water use and map irrigated and rainfed parcels, with a dashboard for the Mitidja West scheme](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [The Algerian Space Agency (ASAL) says it works with the Ministry of Agriculture on pilot satellite mapping of cereals (Sidi Bel Abbes, Sentinel-2 NDVI time series) and of potatoes (Mostaganem), and plans to customise CropWatch for these two pilot zones (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cw070823_p01_Algeria.pdf>). [The agriculture ministry's secretary general said in February 2025 that Algerian Space Agency imagery let the country control the locust invasion, with 23,000 hectares treated preventively](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lutte-antiacridienne-lasal-met-son-imagerie-satellitaire-au-service-de-la-clcpro/>). [The space agency's first national conference on geospatial agriculture, in November 2023, heard of Alsat-1B soil studies and CropWatch crop monitoring in Algeria](<https://asal.dz/?lang=ar&p=10622>).
