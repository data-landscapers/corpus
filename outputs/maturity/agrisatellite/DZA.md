# Algeria

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The CGIAR tool series says the WaPOR Digital Unit at the agriculture ministry uses a WaPOR-based tool](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [It computes ten-day irrigation water use from 20 m satellite data](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [In February 2025 the ministry's secretary general said Algerian Space Agency imagery helped control the locust invasion](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lutte-antiacridienne-lasal-met-son-imagerie-satellitaire-au-service-de-la-clcpro/>). [He said 23,000 hectares were treated preventively](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lutte-antiacridienne-lasal-met-son-imagerie-satellitaire-au-service-de-la-clcpro/>). [In October 2025 FAO honoured ASAL for locating locust breeding sites with Alsat imagery](<https://www.awras.com/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%a7%d9%88-%d8%aa%d9%83%d8%b1%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%a6%d8%b1-%d9%84%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%ac%d9%87%d8%aa%d9%87%d8%a7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%af/>). [ASAL also runs a national platform that serves the plant-protection services](<https://www.awras.com/%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%81%d8%a7%d9%88-%d8%aa%d9%83%d8%b1%d9%85-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b2%d8%a7%d8%a6%d8%b1-%d9%84%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%ac%d9%87%d8%aa%d9%87%d8%a7-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ac%d8%b1%d8%a7%d8%af/>). [In March 2026 the press reported that the locust response relies on an early-warning system supported by ASAL satellite data](<https://www.independentarabia.com/node/644775/%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A6%D8%A9/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%AD%D8%B5%D9%86-%D8%B6%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%A3%D8%B1%D8%B6%D8%A7-%D9%88%D9%81%D8%B6%D8%A7%D8%A1>). [In July 2026 a meeting in Tiaret discussed ASAL's draft report on satellite monitoring of cereal crops](<https://www.aljazairalyoum.dz/%d9%86%d8%ad%d9%88-%d8%a8%d9%8a%d8%a7%d9%86%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%81%d9%84%d8%a7%d8%ad%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%af%d9%82%d9%8a%d9%82%d8%a9-%d8%a8%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a3%d9%82%d9%85%d8%a7%d8%b1-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b5%d9%86/>).
