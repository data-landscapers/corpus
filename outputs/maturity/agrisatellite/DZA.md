# Algeria

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The CGIAR tool series says the WaPOR Digital Unit at the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development uses the WaPOR-based Water Use Tool](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [The unit sits in the ministry's Department of Statistics and Strategies](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [The tool uses 20 m WaPOR satellite data to compute ten-day irrigation water use and map irrigated and rainfed parcels](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [It has a dashboard for the Mitidja West scheme](<https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ecbc9233-d7ab-4c68-ac2b-4ce6928ecb05/content>). [In August 2023 the Algerian Space Agency (ASAL) said it was working with the agriculture ministry on pilot satellite mapping of cereals and potatoes](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cw070823_p01_Algeria.pdf>). [The cereal pilot at Sidi Bel Abbes used Sentinel-2 NDVI time series, and the potato pilot was at Mostaganem](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cw070823_p01_Algeria.pdf>). [ASAL planned to customise CropWatch for both zones](<https://unctad.org/system/files/non-official-document/cw070823_p01_Algeria.pdf>). [In February 2025 the agriculture ministry's secretary general said Algerian Space Agency imagery let the country control the locust invasion](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lutte-antiacridienne-lasal-met-son-imagerie-satellitaire-au-service-de-la-clcpro/>). [He said 23,000 hectares were treated preventively](<https://lesenjeuxeco.dz/lutte-antiacridienne-lasal-met-son-imagerie-satellitaire-au-service-de-la-clcpro/>). [The space agency's first national conference on geospatial agriculture, in November 2023, heard of Alsat-1B soil studies and CropWatch crop monitoring in Algeria](<https://asal.dz/?lang=ar&p=10622>).
