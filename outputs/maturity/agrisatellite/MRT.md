# Mauritania

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The OSS says Mauritania has institutionalised the GMES services MISLAND-Mauritanie (land degradation monitoring) and SAQIYA-Mauritanie (irrigation management app), both hosted on national servers, with ministries including Agriculture sharing their experience of using the services at the CUCT workshop](<http://www.oss-online.org/fr/GMESMauritania>). [A pilot with the National Employment Agency gave rice farmers in Rosso and R'kiz near-real-time Sentinel-2 vegetation and irrigation indicators through the TechghilAgro app and dashboard in 2025, with a second season planned for October](<https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/web/success-stories/-/empowering-agriculture-in-mauritania-with-sentinel-2-the-techghilagro-success-story>).

**Published regularly.** No source found since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<http://www.oss-online.org/fr/GMESMauritania>).

**Reaching farmers.** [The TechghilAgro pilot's first season, in 2025, reached 102 rice farmers in Rosso and R'kiz over 612 hectares](<https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/web/success-stories/-/empowering-agriculture-in-mauritania-with-sentinel-2-the-techghilagro-success-story>).
