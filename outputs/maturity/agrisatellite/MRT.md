# Mauritania

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The OSS says Mauritania has institutionalised two GMES services, both hosted on national servers](<http://www.oss-online.org/fr/GMESMauritania>). They are MISLAND-Mauritanie, for land degradation monitoring, and SAQIYA-Mauritanie, an irrigation management app. Ministries including Agriculture shared their experience of using the services at the CUCT workshop. [In 2025 a pilot with the National Employment Agency gave rice farmers in Rosso and R'kiz near-real-time Sentinel-2 vegetation and irrigation indicators](<https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/web/success-stories/-/empowering-agriculture-in-mauritania-with-sentinel-2-the-techghilagro-success-story>). The indicators came through the TechghilAgro app and dashboard. A second season was planned for October.
