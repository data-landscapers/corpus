# Mauritania

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The OSS says Mauritania has institutionalised two GMES services, both hosted on national servers](<http://www.oss-online.org/fr/GMESMauritania>). They are MISLAND-Mauritanie, for land degradation monitoring, and SAQIYA-Mauritanie, an irrigation management app. Ministries including Agriculture shared their experience of using the services at the CUCT workshop. [In 2025 a pilot with the National Employment Agency gave rice farmers in Rosso and R'kiz near-real-time Sentinel-2 vegetation and irrigation indicators](<https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/web/success-stories/-/empowering-agriculture-in-mauritania-with-sentinel-2-the-techghilagro-success-story>). The indicators came through the TechghilAgro app and dashboard. A second season was planned for October. [The national meteorological office reads satellite data on vegetation in its ten-day rainy-season bulletin and gives advice to farmers and herders](<https://meteomauritanie.mr/documents/11/Bulletin_007.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** [The office issues the bulletin every ten days; No. 007 is dated 31 August 2025](<https://meteomauritanie.mr/documents/11/Bulletin_007.pdf>).

**Reaching farmers.** [The TechghilAgro pilot's first season, in 2025, reached 102 rice farmers in Rosso and R'kiz over 612 hectares](<https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/web/success-stories/-/empowering-agriculture-in-mauritania-with-sentinel-2-the-techghilagro-success-story>).
