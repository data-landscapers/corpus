# Zambia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). It is for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, and covers this country among the six SASSCAL states. [Zambia's National Crop Monitor was launched in Lusaka in August 2024, to track crop conditions during the drought](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/zambia-launches-national-crop-monitor-enhance-agricultural-resilience-amidst-drought-crisis>). It is a GEOGLAM tool built on MODIS, VIIRS, CHIRPS, Landsat and Sentinel-2 data. Provincial teams from all ten provinces joined the launch. [UNDP says Zambia's FISP crop index insurance, run through the agriculture ministry's ZIAMIS platform, uses a weather index built on satellite data](<https://irff.undp.org/blog/zambias-digital-approach-crop-index-insurance-and-mobile-payouts>). [NASA Harvest says an ESA WorldCereal workshop in November 2025 trained participants from national statistics offices and research institutes to make crop maps from 7,600 Zambian field samples](<https://www.nasaharvest.org/news/nasa-harvest-and-esa-worldcereal-collaborate-on-custom-crop-mapping-system>).
