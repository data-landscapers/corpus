# Zambia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, with AgriWatch covering this country among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [Zambia's National Crop Monitor, a GEOGLAM tool built on MODIS, VIIRS, CHIRPS, Landsat and Sentinel-2 data, was launched in Lusaka in August 2024 with provincial teams from all ten provinces, to track crop conditions during the drought](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/zambia-launches-national-crop-monitor-enhance-agricultural-resilience-amidst-drought-crisis>).
