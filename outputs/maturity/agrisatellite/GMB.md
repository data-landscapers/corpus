# Gambia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO's 2024 publication says the Gambian government's IFAD-supported ROOTS project developed a geospatial monitoring platform covering rice production, vegetable gardening, mangroves, forestry, agroforestry and infrastructure, with FAO generating project indicators starting from an updated land cover map under a new National Land Cover Reference System](<https://openknowledge.fao.org/items/f3175f42-56ff-4b85-892b-528dd124b73a>). [FAO says the Gambia GeoLab, a national platform on Google Earth Engine built under the ROOTS project, turns satellite data into indicators on land cover, rice production and forests (2025)](<https://www.fao.org/geospatial/news/news-detail/fao-presents-the-gambia-geolab-at-geo-for-good-summit-in-singapore/en>). [A GCA-funded Earth Engine platform of satellite agricultural indices was built and 12 government departments trained on it from April 2024](<https://www.weatherimpact.com/products/gambia/>).
