# Gambia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO's 2024 publication says the government's IFAD-supported ROOTS project developed a geospatial monitoring platform](<https://openknowledge.fao.org/items/f3175f42-56ff-4b85-892b-528dd124b73a>). [The platform covers rice production, vegetable gardening, mangroves, forestry, agroforestry and infrastructure](<https://openknowledge.fao.org/items/f3175f42-56ff-4b85-892b-528dd124b73a>). [FAO generates project indicators starting from an updated land cover map under a new National Land Cover Reference System](<https://openknowledge.fao.org/items/f3175f42-56ff-4b85-892b-528dd124b73a>). [In 2025 FAO said the Gambia GeoLab, a national Google Earth Engine platform built under ROOTS, turns satellite data into indicators](<https://www.fao.org/geospatial/news/news-detail/fao-presents-the-gambia-geolab-at-geo-for-good-summit-in-singapore/en>). [They cover land cover, rice production and forests](<https://www.fao.org/geospatial/news/news-detail/fao-presents-the-gambia-geolab-at-geo-for-good-summit-in-singapore/en>). [A GCA-funded Earth Engine platform of satellite agricultural indices was built and 12 government departments trained on it from April 2024](<https://www.weatherimpact.com/products/gambia/>).
