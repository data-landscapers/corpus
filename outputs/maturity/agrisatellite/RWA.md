# Rwanda

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [A national program managed by the Rwanda Space Agency will use Planet's near-daily satellite data to monitor agriculture and food security, as announced by Planet and the Government of Rwanda](<https://itweb.africa/article/planet-and-the-government-of-rwanda-launch-first-of-its-kind-national-satellite-data-program-in-africa/nWJadMbNDxlMbjO1>). [The Rwanda Space Agency's GeoHub, supported by an AFD-backed project, uses satellite images to support sectors including agriculture, and geospatial data is already used to build climate projections informing agriculture and food security policy, per the interweave newsletter](<https://interweavegov.substack.com/p/rwandas-digital-transformation-an>).
