# Somalia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In August 2026 FAO SWALIM stated that its remote sensing unit has used medium- and high-resolution satellite images to estimate cultivable areas](<https://faoswalim.org/information-management/information-services/remote-sensing>). [The unit also estimated production by cropping system at regional and district level](<https://faoswalim.org/information-management/information-services/remote-sensing>). [FSNAU published E-MODIS NDVI maps of Somalia month by month from January 2007 to July 2026](<https://fsnau.org/downloads/NDVI-Index-for-Somalia-Jan-2007-Jul-2026.pdf>). [The maps show vegetation deviations from short-term averages](<https://fsnau.org/downloads/NDVI-Index-for-Somalia-Jan-2007-Jul-2026.pdf>).
