# Somalia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In August 2026 FAO SWALIM stated that its remote sensing unit has used medium- and high-resolution satellite images to estimate cultivable areas](<https://faoswalim.org/information-management/information-services/remote-sensing>). [The unit also estimated production by cropping system at regional and district level](<https://faoswalim.org/information-management/information-services/remote-sensing>). [FSNAU published E-MODIS NDVI maps of Somalia month by month from January 2007 to July 2026](<https://fsnau.org/downloads/NDVI-Index-for-Somalia-Jan-2007-Jul-2026.pdf>). [The maps show vegetation deviations from short-term averages](<https://fsnau.org/downloads/NDVI-Index-for-Somalia-Jan-2007-Jul-2026.pdf>). [In September 2026 payouts under the World Bank-financed Index-Based Livestock Takaful programme were triggered by satellite vegetation monitoring](<https://hoadrive.info/stories/drought-takaful-payout-september-2026/>). [FAO says its SWALIM programme uses very-high-resolution satellite image analysis to identify suitable growing conditions for at least 14 crops](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/from-data-to-decision--somalia-s-early-warning-systems-light-the-way-for-resilience-across-africa/en>).
