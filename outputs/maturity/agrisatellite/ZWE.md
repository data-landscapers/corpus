# Zimbabwe

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO says the EOSTAT-Zimbabwe project, launched in 2023 with African Development Bank funding, is rolling out an Earth-observation crop monitoring system](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/fao-s-earth-observation-for-agricultural-statistics-initiative-scoops-ict-innovator-of-the-year-award/en>). [It is implemented with the agriculture ministry, ZIMSTAT, ZINGSA and others, and covers crop type and yield data practices](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/fao-s-earth-observation-for-agricultural-statistics-initiative-scoops-ict-innovator-of-the-year-award/en>). [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [AgriWatch covers Zimbabwe among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [FAO says its EOSTAT system helps Zimbabwe produce crop and yield estimates from satellite data (July 2025)](<https://www.fao.org/africa/news-stories/news-detail/from-soil-to-systems--how-zimbabwe-is-scaling-innovation-for-food-security-and-rural-transformation/en>).
