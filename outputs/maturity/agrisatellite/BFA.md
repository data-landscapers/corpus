# Burkina Faso

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [SNV says its GARBAL call service has run since 2015 with the Ministry of Animal Resources and ANAM](<https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso>). [It combines satellite data with weather station and government data to advise farmers and herders](<https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso>). [In May 2023 the climatology directorate's drought early-warning process used NDVI and VCI vegetation indices among its indicators](<https://gwpo-gwp.org/assets/legacy/globalassets/global/gwp-waf_files/vfdm_mai_2023/bf_processus_sap_secheresse.pdf>). [It set agricultural alert thresholds by climate zone, and the country had one satellite-image reception station](<https://gwpo-gwp.org/assets/legacy/globalassets/global/gwp-waf_files/vfdm_mai_2023/bf_processus_sap_secheresse.pdf>). [ANAM, the national meteorological agency, describes its ten-day agro-meteorological bulletin as a current product](<https://meteoburkina.bf/produits/bulletin-agrometeologique-decadaire/>). [The bulletin carries NDVI and NDVI anomalies alongside rainfall, temperature and humidity anomalies](<https://meteoburkina.bf/produits/bulletin-agrometeologique-decadaire/>). [A 2024 study by Burkinabe and partner researchers tested satellite NDVI and FAPAR variables to estimate pasture fodder yields](<https://revues.cirad.fr/index.php/REMVT/article/view/37009>). [The results were weak outside the Sahel](<https://revues.cirad.fr/index.php/REMVT/article/view/37009>). [The government's PPACI-BF index insurance project, run by the environment ministry, assesses seasons with rain-gauge data from ANAM and with satellite data](<https://faso7.com/2025/11/10/burkina-faso-lassurance-climatique-indicielle-le-nouveau-filet-de-securite-des-agriculteurs/>).

**Published regularly.** [ANAM issues the bulletin every ten days; the newest update listed in October 2026 covers 13 to 19 October](<https://meteoburkina.bf/produits/bulletin-agrometeologique-decadaire/>).

**Reaching farmers.** No source found since October 2023 counts farmers or districts reached by the bulletin. The nearest fact: [the PPACI-BF coordinator said 4,197 people had subscribed to the index insurance by 2025](<https://faso7.com/2025/11/10/burkina-faso-lassurance-climatique-indicielle-le-nouveau-filet-de-securite-des-agriculteurs/>).
