# Burkina Faso

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [SNV says its GARBAL call service, run since 2015 with the Ministry of Animal Resources and ANAM, integrates satellite data with weather station and government data to advise farmers and herders](<https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso>). [Burkina Faso's national drought forecasting and early-warning process (climatology directorate, May 2023 GWP presentation) uses NDVI and VCI vegetation indices among its drought indicators, with agricultural alert thresholds by climate zone, and the country has one satellite-image reception station (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://gwpo-gwp.org/assets/legacy/globalassets/global/gwp-waf_files/vfdm_mai_2023/bf_processus_sap_secheresse.pdf>).

**Published regularly.** No source held since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.snv.org/update/using-satellite-data-to-transform-agri-food-in-burkina-faso>).
