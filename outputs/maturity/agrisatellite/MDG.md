# Madagascar

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [UNDP says its satellite-based geospatial assessment found up to 174,100 hectares of Madagascar cropland flooded by the February 2026 cyclones](<https://www.undp.org/africa/press-releases/cyclones-devastated-madagascars-poorest-communities-new-satellite-analysis-reveals>). [UNDP is working with national and local authorities to prioritise recovery](<https://www.undp.org/africa/press-releases/cyclones-devastated-madagascars-poorest-communities-new-satellite-analysis-reveals>). [It combines satellite data with field verification](<https://www.undp.org/africa/press-releases/cyclones-devastated-madagascars-poorest-communities-new-satellite-analysis-reveals>). [UNICEF Madagascar, with the EU Joint Research Centre and the water ministry, set up a drought monitoring system for the south using satellite rainfall and vegetation data](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/rapports/bulletin-dalerte-s%C3%A9cheresse-du-grand-sud-de-madagascar-2025>). [The document notes that Madagascar has no national drought monitoring system](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/rapports/bulletin-dalerte-s%C3%A9cheresse-du-grand-sud-de-madagascar-2025>).

**Published regularly.** [UNICEF and the national water authority ANDEA, with the water ministry, run the drought monitoring system for the south on satellite vegetation and rainfall data](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/media/13086/file/Bulletin%20Juin%202024%20WASH.pdf.pdf>). [Its monthly bulletin goes to all stakeholders; issue 62, of June 2024, put 30.1% of the area on alarm](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/media/13086/file/Bulletin%20Juin%202024%20WASH.pdf.pdf>). [UNICEF said in April 2025 that the monthly drought alert bulletin is still produced and distributed](<https://www.unicef.org/madagascar/rapports/bulletin-dalerte-s%C3%A9cheresse-du-grand-sud-de-madagascar-2025>).
