# Guinea-Bissau

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO's GIEWS country brief for Guinea-Bissau says satellite-based data showed some dry spells affected crops in localized areas in 2025, and lists GIEWS Earth Observation for Crop Monitoring among the tools used to prepare it](<https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/GNB/pdf/GNB.pdf>). [WFP Guinea-Bissau says it carries out monthly monitoring of rainy-season conditions, using CHIRPS rainfall, MODIS NDVI and land surface temperature processed in Google Earth Engine, to report vegetation state and flood risk for agriculture](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000168803/download/>). [The meteorological institute says the multidisciplinary crop-campaign working group was trained by AGRHYMET in August 2026 on satellite imagery, NDVI and yield simulation to improve its bulletin](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/capacita%C3%A7%C3%A3o-dos-quadros-de-sistemas-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-guin%C3%A9-bissau-sobre-a-utiliza%C3%A7%C3%A3o-de-produtos-de-observa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-terra-pot/>).
