# Guinea-Bissau

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO's GIEWS country brief for Guinea-Bissau says satellite-based data showed some dry spells affected crops in localized areas in 2025](<https://www.fao.org/giews/countrybrief/country/GNB/pdf/GNB.pdf>). It lists GIEWS Earth Observation for Crop Monitoring among the tools used to prepare it. [WFP Guinea-Bissau says it monitors rainy-season conditions every month](<https://docs.wfp.org/api/documents/WFP-0000168803/download/>). It uses CHIRPS rainfall, MODIS NDVI and land surface temperature processed in Google Earth Engine, and reports vegetation state and flood risk for agriculture. [The meteorological institute says AGRHYMET trained the multidisciplinary crop-campaign working group in August 2026](<https://meteoguinebissau.gw/noticias/capacita%C3%A7%C3%A3o-dos-quadros-de-sistemas-de-informa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-guin%C3%A9-bissau-sobre-a-utiliza%C3%A7%C3%A3o-de-produtos-de-observa%C3%A7%C3%A3o-da-terra-pot/>). The training covered satellite imagery, NDVI and yield simulation, to improve the group's bulletin.
