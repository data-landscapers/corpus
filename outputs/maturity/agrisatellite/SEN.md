# Senegal

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Since late 2025 the national met agency ANACIM has run the AgData Hub, which produces district-scale agro-climatic bulletins for each crop from forecasts, satellite observations and seasonal outlooks](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>). [Digital Earth Africa says it signed a five-year framework agreement in February 2026 with Senegal's national statistics agency ANSD to bring satellite data into agricultural statistics, developing methods to calculate cropped area, crop types and seasonal yields and giving crop-mapping support for rice, groundnut, millet and maize](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/de-africa-and-senegals-ansd-partner-to-bring-satellite-data-into-national-statistics/>). [IRRI says it, the Senegalese agricultural research institute ISRA and sarmap piloted the RIICE platform using Sentinel-1A, Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2 data to map rice area, track cropping seasons and simulate yields in Senegal's rice regions](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/leveraging-satellite-technology-in-senegals-rice-regions-for-national-food-and-economic-security>).

**Published regularly.** [The agrometeorological working group coordinated by ANACIM publishes a bulletin at the end of every ten days, now produced in the AgData Hub app](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>).
