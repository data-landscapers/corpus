# Senegal

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Since late 2025 the national met agency ANACIM has run the AgData Hub](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>). It produces district-scale agro-climatic bulletins for each crop from forecasts, satellite observations and seasonal outlooks. [Digital Earth Africa says it signed a five-year framework agreement in February 2026 with Senegal's national statistics agency ANSD](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/de-africa-and-senegals-ansd-partner-to-bring-satellite-data-into-national-statistics/>). The agreement brings satellite data into agricultural statistics. It develops methods to calculate cropped area, crop types and seasonal yields. It also gives crop-mapping support for rice, groundnut, millet and maize. [IRRI says it piloted the RIICE platform with the Senegalese agricultural research institute ISRA and sarmap](<https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/leveraging-satellite-technology-in-senegals-rice-regions-for-national-food-and-economic-security>). The pilot used Sentinel-1A, Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2 data to map rice area, track cropping seasons and simulate yields in Senegal's rice regions.

**Published regularly.** [The agrometeorological working group coordinated by ANACIM publishes a bulletin at the end of every ten days](<https://aiccra.cgiar.org/news/information-action-nationally-integrated-agdata-hub-turning-point-senegals-agriculture>). The bulletin is now produced in the AgData Hub app.
