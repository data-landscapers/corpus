# Egypt

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [ESA used Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery from 2015 and 2025 to show reclaimed irrigated cropland in Egypt's Western Desert, stating such data are useful for mapping land use, crop type and crop health](<https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/06/Earth_from_Space_Desert_cropland>). [FAO's WaPOR Phase 2 project, in partnership with Egypt's Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation and Ministry of Agriculture and Land Reclamation, produced a 10 m Sentinel-2 crop type map of Mallawi, Minya for 2023-2024, published 2026-06-24](<https://data.apps.fao.org/catalog/dataset/59eee02c-ff3f-43d1-a7d1-a85b087fb2bf>). [NARSS and the Ministry of Agriculture presented a remote-sensing crop map of the 2023-24 winter season in November 2024, each governorate inventorying wheat, clover and sugar beet](<https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2024/11/05/narss-agriculture-ministry-launch-map-of-winter-crops/>). [The irrigation ministry began piloting satellite crop-type mapping for water distribution in Kafr El Sheikh in June 2024](<https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/1/132896/Egypt-introduces-new-satellite-image-analysis-application-to-enhance-irrigation>).
