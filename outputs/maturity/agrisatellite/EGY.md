# Egypt

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [ESA used Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery from 2015 and 2025 to show reclaimed irrigated cropland in Egypt's Western Desert, stating such data are useful for mapping land use, crop type and crop health](<https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2026/06/Earth_from_Space_Desert_cropland>). [FAO's WaPOR Phase 2 project, in partnership with Egypt's Ministry of Water Resources and Irrigation and Ministry of Agriculture and Land Reclamation, produced a 10 m Sentinel-2 crop type map of Mallawi, Minya for 2023-2024, published 2026-06-24](<https://data.apps.fao.org/catalog/dataset/59eee02c-ff3f-43d1-a7d1-a85b087fb2bf>).
