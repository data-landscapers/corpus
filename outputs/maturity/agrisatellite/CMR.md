# Cameroon

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO says a national cocoa-mapping project in Cameroon, funded by the EU, uses satellite data to identify cocoa production zones, and a 16-18 July 2025 Ebolowa workshop trained 50 participants including ministry representatives on SEPAL and Google Earth Engine](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/actualites/detail/fao-supports-the-government-of-cameroon-in-using-ai-and-satellite-imagery-to-produce-cocoa-map-under-the-sustainable-cocoa-programme/fr>). [The climate observatory ONACC cites a 2023 pilot combining satellite analysis and drought indices to map flood and drought risk to crops in seven Far North localities](<https://www.onacc.cm/fr/data>). [The government, with FAO and UNFPA, began field-testing an earth-observation method to estimate cultivated area and crop types for the agricultural census in October 2025](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/news/detail/cameroon-tests-an-innovative-census-strategy-in-crisis-zones/en>).
