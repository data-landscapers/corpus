# Cameroon

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO says a national cocoa-mapping project in Cameroon, funded by the EU, uses satellite data to identify cocoa production zones](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/actualites/detail/fao-supports-the-government-of-cameroon-in-using-ai-and-satellite-imagery-to-produce-cocoa-map-under-the-sustainable-cocoa-programme/fr>). A workshop in Ebolowa on 16-18 July 2025 trained 50 participants, including ministry representatives, on SEPAL and Google Earth Engine. [The climate observatory ONACC cites a 2023 pilot combining satellite analysis and drought indices](<https://www.onacc.cm/fr/data>). It mapped flood and drought risk to crops in seven Far North localities. [In October 2025 the government, with FAO and UNFPA, began field-testing an earth-observation method for the agricultural census](<https://www.fao.org/cameroun/news/detail/cameroon-tests-an-innovative-census-strategy-in-crisis-zones/en>). The method estimates cultivated area and crop types.
