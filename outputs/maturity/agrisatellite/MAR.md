# Morocco

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [Morocco's delegation told COPUOS in June 2024 that it is engaged in projects using Earth observation data, including Mohammed VI A and B satellite images, that yield value-added products and services in areas including agriculture](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Published regularly.** No source found since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>).
