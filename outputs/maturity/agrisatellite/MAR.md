# Morocco

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In June 2024 Morocco's delegation told COPUOS that it is engaged in projects using Earth observation data](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). [The data include Mohammed VI A and B satellite images](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). [The projects yield value-added products and services in areas including agriculture](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>).
