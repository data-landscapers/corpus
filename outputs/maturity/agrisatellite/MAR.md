# Morocco

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In June 2024 Morocco's delegation told COPUOS that it is engaged in projects using Earth observation data](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). [The data include Mohammed VI A and B satellite images](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). [The projects yield value-added products and services in areas including agriculture](<https://www.unoosa.org/documents/pdf/copuos/2024/statements/9_Morocco.pdf>). [The Digital Hub for Agriculture, Forests and the Drought Observatory says it now operates a drought geoportal using CHIRPS, MODIS and SMAP satellite sources, crop maps from Sentinel-2 imagery and a farmer e-service with NDVI, SPI and SPEI maps](<https://poledigital.ma/satellite-et-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpementde-geoservices-agricoles-foncier-cultures-eau/>). [A FAO-funded agreement behind them closed on 30 May 2026](<https://poledigital.ma/satellite-et-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpementde-geoservices-agricoles-foncier-cultures-eau/>). [The drought warning and cereal yield forecasting system CGMS-Maroc, run since the early 2010s by the agronomic research institute with the meteorological service, uses satellite imagery to monitor cereal crops](<https://www.agrimaroc.ma/cgms-maroc-systeme-alerte-secheresse-prevision-rendements-cerealiers/>).

**Published regularly.** No source found since October 2023 says a satellite-based farming service publishes on a schedule. The nearest fact: [the Digital Hub's farmer e-service gives NDVI, SPI and SPEI maps](<https://poledigital.ma/satellite-et-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpementde-geoservices-agricoles-foncier-cultures-eau/>).

**Reaching farmers.** No source found since October 2023 counts farmers or districts reached by satellite-based advice or alerts. The nearest fact: [the Digital Hub runs an agrometeorological e-service for farmers](<https://poledigital.ma/satellite-et-intelligence-artificielle-pour-le-developpementde-geoservices-agricoles-foncier-cultures-eau/>).
