# South Africa

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The Agricultural Research Council's ARC-ISCW states the operational Coarse Resolution Imagery Database, funded by the Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, holds NOAA AVHRR, MODIS, VEGETATION and Meteosat imagery used for crop production and grazing capacity estimation](<https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/Pages/Earth-Observation.aspx>). [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, with AgriWatch covering this country among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>).

**Published regularly.** [ARC-ISCW states information from the CRID is disseminated through the monthly Umlindi newsletter and presented at monthly Crop Estimates Committee meetings and to the National Agrometeorological Committee every three months](<https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/Pages/Earth-Observation.aspx>).
