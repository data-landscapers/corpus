# South Africa

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The Agricultural Research Council's ARC-ISCW states that the Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries funds the operational Coarse Resolution Imagery Database](<https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/Pages/Earth-Observation.aspx>). The database holds NOAA AVHRR, MODIS, VEGETATION and Meteosat imagery. The imagery is used for crop production and grazing capacity estimation. [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). It is for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, and covers this country among the six SASSCAL states.

**Published regularly.** [ARC-ISCW states information from the CRID is disseminated through the monthly Umlindi newsletter](<https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/Pages/Earth-Observation.aspx>). It is presented at monthly Crop Estimates Committee meetings, and to the National Agrometeorological Committee every three months.
