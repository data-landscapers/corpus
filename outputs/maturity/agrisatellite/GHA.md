# Ghana

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [GEOGLAM's account by the project leads says the Ghana Space Science and Technology Institute and VITO are building GEOMAIZE maize yield estimation from Sentinel-2 time series](<https://geoglam.org/from-partnership-to-practice-advancing-yield-intelligence-and-food-security-through-geomaize/>). [It says Ghana has a foundation for an operational pipeline](<https://geoglam.org/from-partnership-to-practice-advancing-yield-intelligence-and-food-security-through-geomaize/>). [SERVIR West Africa and the University of Florida report that GMet successfully tested initial operational capability of CRAFT for district-level maize yield hindcasts](<https://servir.icrisat.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DOUBLE_UF_2024.pdf>). [CRAFT links earth-observation crop type maps to production forecasts, and operationalising the service is listed as a next step](<https://servir.icrisat.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DOUBLE_UF_2024.pdf>). [ESA says agriculture ministry officials were trained in March 2024 on Earth-observation crop classifiers built from FSRP field data](<https://gda.esa.int/story/eo-tools-supporting-ghanas-food-system-resilience/>). [Ghana plans to run the CLIMTAG dashboard in its GhAAP platform](<https://gda.esa.int/story/eo-tools-supporting-ghanas-food-system-resilience/>). [The agriculture ministry said in March 2024 it would use satellites to watch each farm enrolled in Planting for Food and Jobs II twice a day](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/pfj-2-0-agric-ministry-to-deploy-satellite-for-effective-monitoring-of-farms/>).
