## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In March 2026 IGAD sought a consultant to monitor and forecast crop conditions across the IGAD region](<https://necjogha.net/igad-seeks-consultant-for-crop-monitoring-and-forecasting-to-strengthen-food-systems-resilience/>). [The work uses satellite-derived NDVI, VCI and soil moisture, and feeds ICPAC's regional agricultural early warning dashboards](<https://necjogha.net/igad-seeks-consultant-for-crop-monitoring-and-forecasting-to-strengthen-food-systems-resilience/>). [The World Bank and IDA finance it under FSRP-AF, and its scope is regional, not specific to Djibouti](<https://necjogha.net/igad-seeks-consultant-for-crop-monitoring-and-forecasting-to-strengthen-food-systems-resilience/>).
