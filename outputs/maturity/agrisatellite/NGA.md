# Nigeria

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The Agriculture Ministry signed an MoU with NASRDA under which, the minister said, NASRDA will provide satellite imagery and real-time monitoring for a national framework on produce traceability and farmland monitoring](<https://agriculture.gov.ng/fg-unveils-white-paper-on-approach-for-eudr-compliance/>). [UNCTAD says Nigeria, with NASRDA as lead partner, has used the CropWatch cloud system since 2021 for crop condition, acreage and yield assessments and pest and drought early warnings](<https://unctad.org/news/harnessing-frontier-technology-food-security-nigeria>). [Punch reports that NASRDA launched Crop Watch in Abuja, a satellite-based agricultural monitoring system using remote sensing and ground data to evaluate crop growth and yield, with UNCTAD and the Chinese Academy of Sciences](<https://punchng.com/fg-unveils-satellite-based-crop-watch-system-to-boost-food-security/>).

**Published regularly.** [UNCTAD says CropWatch work in Nigeria feeds a quarterly bulletin informing government policy, with the January to April 2024 bulletin reporting rainfall 64% below the 15-year norm](<https://unctad.org/news/harnessing-frontier-technology-food-security-nigeria>).
