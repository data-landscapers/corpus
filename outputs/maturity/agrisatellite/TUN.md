# Tunisia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [FAO's Desert Locust Information Service trained national locust information officers including from this country in Rome on tools including Google Earth Engine satellite image analysis](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [FAO's WaPOR page lists Tunisia's agriculture ministry, Institut de l'Olivier, Institut National des Grandes Cultures and the national meteorology institute as project partners, and says the national technical team ran two crop-type data collection campaigns in Jendouba and Kairouan for WaPOR 2 high-resolution (20 m) remote-sensing data](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/country-activities/tunisia/2/fr>).
