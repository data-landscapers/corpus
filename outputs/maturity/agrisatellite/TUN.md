# Tunisia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In September 2025 FAO's Desert Locust Information Service trained national locust information officers in Rome, including from Tunisia](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [The tools taught included Google Earth Engine satellite image analysis](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [FAO's WaPOR page lists the agriculture ministry, Institut de l'Olivier, Institut National des Grandes Cultures and the national meteorology institute as project partners](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/country-activities/tunisia/2/fr>). [It says the national technical team ran two crop-type data collection campaigns in Jendouba and Kairouan for 20 m WaPOR 2 remote-sensing data](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/country-activities/tunisia/2/fr>). [In September 2024 a workshop closed the AssurCereals project, led by the national engineering school with the national remote sensing centre](<https://www.apad-tunisie.org/images/apad/evenement/Programme_Atelier-cloture-PAQ-COLLABORA_final11_09.pdf>). [It computed drought indices for cereal growers from weather and satellite data](<https://www.apad-tunisie.org/images/apad/evenement/Programme_Atelier-cloture-PAQ-COLLABORA_final11_09.pdf>). [In June 2024 the National Institute of Field Crops was developing Sentinel-2 nitrogen uptake models for durum wheat](<https://www.apni.net/2024/06/10/from-field-to-satellite-advancing-nitrogen-use-for-durum-wheat-in-tunisia/>). [FAO reported a Sentinel-2 land cover map of Tunisia covering cultivated land, olive trees and date palms](<https://www.fao.org/geospatial/news/news-detail/Mapping-land-cover-in-Tunisia-In-the-context-of-the-Soil-Land-and-Water-Digital-Information-System-initiative/en>).
