# Tunisia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In September 2025 FAO's Desert Locust Information Service trained national locust information officers in Rome, including from Tunisia](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [The tools taught included Google Earth Engine satellite image analysis](<https://www.fao.org/transboundary-plant-pests-diseases/news/detail/eritrea--tunisia-and-kenya-strengthen-locust-early-warning-capacities-through-fao-led-training/en>). [FAO's WaPOR page lists the agriculture ministry, Institut de l'Olivier, Institut National des Grandes Cultures and the national meteorology institute as project partners](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/country-activities/tunisia/2/fr>). [It says the national technical team ran two crop-type data collection campaigns in Jendouba and Kairouan for 20 m WaPOR 2 remote-sensing data](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/country-activities/tunisia/2/fr>).
