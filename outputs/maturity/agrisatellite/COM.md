## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [As at August 2026 FAO GIEWS runs an Earth Observation portal for Comoros](<https://eo.apps.fao.org/country/index.jsp?code=COM&lang=en>). [It gives an Agricultural Stress Index and drought-intensity indicators from METOP-AVHRR satellite vegetation data](<https://eo.apps.fao.org/country/index.jsp?code=COM&lang=en>). [These identify cropped land at risk of drought](<https://eo.apps.fao.org/country/index.jsp?code=COM&lang=en>).
