# Burundi

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [IGEBU, the national mapping institute, says its cartography serves to identify and manage agricultural zones and that its map work uses remote sensing (télédétection) alongside GPS, GIS and drones](<https://www.igebu.bi/Services/cartographie/>). [Enabel's PAIOSA programme, run with the Belgian and Burundian governments, began satellite monitoring of 4,500 hectares of smallholder farmland with EOSDA Crop Monitoring in November 2021, adding field scouting and targeted advice to the smallholder farmers it covered](<https://eos.com/blog/enabel-helps-african-farmers-by-using-sattech/>).
