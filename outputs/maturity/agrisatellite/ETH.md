# Ethiopia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In February 2025 FAO's EOSTAT project and the Ethiopian Statistics Service ran a pilot wheat-yield study in Arsi Zone](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/eostat/resources/news/news-detail/harnessing-earth-observation-and-process-based-models-for-crop-yield-estimation-in-ethiopia/en>). It combined crop models with Earth-observation data. [The Ministry of Agriculture's data hub lists soil-nutrient maps made by the Agricultural Transformation Institute](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>). They were built from field samples and satellite data in machine-learning models, and last updated in March 2026. [In June 2026 two national research institutes co-hosted training in Addis Ababa on satellite crop-mapping platforms](<https://english.aircas.ac.cn/newsroom/nu/202607/t20260706_1176333.html>). [The Ethiopian Meteorology Institute's satellite bulletin for 21 to 30 September 2026 analysed vegetation greenness from METEOSAT products and TAMSAT rainfall estimates](<https://www.ethiomet.gov.et/products/dekadal-satellite-bulletin/satellite-bulletin-11-20-september-2026/>). [It found sparse to bare vegetation in Afar, Somali and southern Oromia](<https://www.ethiomet.gov.et/products/dekadal-satellite-bulletin/satellite-bulletin-11-20-september-2026/>).

**Published regularly.** [The institute issues the bulletin every dekad; the newest issue covers 21 to 30 September 2026](<https://www.ethiomet.gov.et/products/dekadal-satellite-bulletin/satellite-bulletin-11-20-september-2026/>). [The ministry's data hub dates its soil-nutrient layers October 2024, updated March 2026](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>).

**Reaching farmers.** No count of farmers reached by the bulletin has been found. The nearest fact: [an index insurance scheme run with WFP and Pula reached 122,000 smallholders in Amhara in 2023](<https://ethiopianmonitor.com/2024/05/03/new-crop-insurance-initiative-offers-farmers-lifeline/>).
