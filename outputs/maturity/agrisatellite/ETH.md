# Ethiopia

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [In February 2025 FAO's EOSTAT project and the Ethiopian Statistics Service ran a pilot wheat-yield study in Arsi Zone](<https://www.fao.org/in-action/eostat/resources/news/news-detail/harnessing-earth-observation-and-process-based-models-for-crop-yield-estimation-in-ethiopia/en>). It combined crop models with Earth-observation data. [The Ministry of Agriculture's data hub lists soil-nutrient maps made by the Agricultural Transformation Institute](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>). They were built from field samples and satellite data in machine-learning models, and last updated in March 2026. [In June 2026 two national research institutes co-hosted training in Addis Ababa on satellite crop-mapping platforms](<https://english.aircas.ac.cn/newsroom/nu/202607/t20260706_1176333.html>).

**Published regularly.** [The ministry's data hub dates its soil-nutrient layers October 2024, updated March 2026](<https://data.moa.gov.et/dataset/>). No satellite-based farming service publishing bulletins or maps on a schedule has been found.

**Reaching farmers.** [The June 2026 Addis Ababa workshop trained more than 50 staff from seven countries, Ethiopia among them](<https://english.aircas.ac.cn/newsroom/nu/202607/t20260706_1176333.html>). No count of farmers or districts receiving satellite-based advice or alerts has been found.

***Also on file***. [National Spatial Data Infrastructure](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/09/7d/097d0421-91ff-466f-9239-1f735b2f96be/2018_e_c_budget_year_goe_federal_budget_proclamation_part_two.xlsx>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says a government satellite service for farming is in use.
