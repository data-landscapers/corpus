# Angola

## Satellite data in agriculture

**Satellite service.** [The National Space Program's Earth observation component, built around the ANGEO-1 satellite, is described as focused on environmental monitoring, precision agriculture, natural resource management and disaster prevention](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/angola-launches-259-million-earth-observation-satellite-project>). [Digital Earth Africa and SASSCAL agreed to build the AgriWatch dashboard on DE Africa Earth-observation products for crop-cycle, rangeland and rainfall monitoring, with AgriWatch covering this country among the six SASSCAL states](<https://digitalearthafrica.org/en_za/growing-resilience-together-digital-earth-africa-and-sasscal-collaborate-on-agriwatch/>). [GGPEN, under the telecoms ministry, and Portuguese firm GEOSAT signed a letter of intent to develop geospatial solutions for strategic sectors including agriculture and forestry under the ANGEO-1 programme](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7726>).
