# Guinea-Bissau

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In June 2025 the Justice Minister announced that civil identification and registry services will be digitised](<https://ang.gw/justica-servicos-de-identificacao-civil-registos-e-notariado-serao-digitalizados-e-informatizados/>). [They are to sit under one directorate-general](<https://ang.gw/justica-servicos-de-identificacao-civil-registos-e-notariado-serao-digitalizados-e-informatizados/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [UNDP financed a workshop supporting the Integrated National Identification System](<https://ang.gw/justica-servicos-de-identificacao-civil-registos-e-notariado-serao-digitalizados-e-informatizados/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the announcement also covers notary services](<https://ang.gw/justica-servicos-de-identificacao-civil-registos-e-notariado-serao-digitalizados-e-informatizados/>).
