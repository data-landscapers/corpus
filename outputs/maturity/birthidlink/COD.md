# DR Congo

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In August 2024 an ONIP legal expert said civil-status events are reported to ONIP](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>). [They are entered in the general population file, the FGP, which they feed](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [births, deaths and marriages are all events reported to ONIP](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the reported events are entered in the FGP](<https://acp.cd/genre/enregistrement-a-letat-civil-le-renforcement-de-la-communication-recommande-a-la-rdc-2/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers.
