# Lesotho

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In July 2026 Lesotho's NICR, in the Ministry of Home Affairs, said CRVS, identification and passport data are 'brought together under one roof'](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [It describes them as one integrated system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>). [An ID4Africa deck says the NICR implemented a CRVS-based ID system](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-4-Lesotho_FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the NICR says a unique personal identification number is issued upon birth registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-lesotho.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the ID4Africa deck dates the NICR milestones from 2010 to 2015](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T3-P3-S2-4-Lesotho_FINAL.pdf>).
