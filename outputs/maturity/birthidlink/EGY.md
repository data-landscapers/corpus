# Egypt

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In 2026 Egypt's CRVS presentation said a central births and deaths database operates as an interoperable platform](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). [In 2024 CITC gave technical support to restructure databases, integrating the national number project with the civil registration databases](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the CITC work covers the birth and death databases](<https://www.citc.com.eg/National%20ID%20SYSTEM.html>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the interoperable platform includes identity management](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-egypt_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).
