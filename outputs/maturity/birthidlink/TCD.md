# Chad

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The N'Djamena civil registry digitization MoU with ANATS plans to connect municipal civil-registration systems with the national identification system (SIGPTS), reported by We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [The ANATS director general says household enrolment teams in a six-month, twelve-province birth registration operation will collect data to be integrated into the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>). [ANATS and the OIF discussed interoperability between communal civil-status services and the SIGPTS, and ANATS reaffirmed its commitment to support N'Djamena's digital transition (Tribune Echos)](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-lanats-et-loif-sunissent-pour-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat-civil/>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>).
