# Chad

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [An ATPE opinion piece says TASDJIL registers births at a hospital desk at Koukou Angarana and issues the child an NNI, so births feed the ID number](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). [The N'Djamena civil registry digitization MoU with ANATS plans to connect municipal civil-registration systems with the national identification system (SIGPTS), reported by We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [The ANATS director general says household enrolment teams in a six-month, twelve-province birth registration operation will collect data to be integrated into the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [SIGPTS](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-lanats-et-loif-sunissent-pour-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat-civil/>); [national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers.
