# Chad

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [An ATPE opinion piece says TASDJIL registers births at a hospital desk at Koukou Angarana and issues the child an NNI, an experience it says must now be scaled up](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). [The N'Djamena civil registry digitization MoU with ANATS plans to connect municipal civil-registration systems with the national identification system (SIGPTS), reported by We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>). [The ANATS director general says household enrolment teams in a six-month, twelve-province birth registration operation will collect data to be integrated into the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>). [In December 2023 Tchadinfos reported that new TASDJIL birth certificates carry an NNI, with registration desks in more than 50 maternity units and the data held on the servers of ANATS, which issues ID documents](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

***Also on file***. [SIGPTS](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-lanats-et-loif-sunissent-pour-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat-civil/>); [national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says records pass between the civil register and the ID system beyond TASDJIL's maternity desks.
