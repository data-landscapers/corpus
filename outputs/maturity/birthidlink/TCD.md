# Chad

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Tchadinfos reported in December 2023 that new TASDJIL birth certificates carry a national ID number (NNI)](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>), with desks in more than 50 maternity units. [The data sit on the servers of ANATS, which issues ID documents](<https://tchadinfos.com/2023/12/10/societe-la-digitalisation-des-actes-de-naissance-une-transformation-cruciale-pour-letat-civil-au-tchad/>). [In July 2025 births at Mongo provincial hospital were entered digitally with an NNI and sent to ANATS](<https://ndarason.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-accelere-lenregistrement-des-naissances-a-mongo/>). [An August 2026 opinion piece calls the Koukou Angarana hospital desk an experience to scale up](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>). [N'Djamena's civil registry plans to connect to the national ID system](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/chad-n-djamena-launches-civil-registry-digitization-drive>), under a September 2026 agreement with ANATS. [A twelve-province birth registration drive is to feed the national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.atpe.td/etat-civil-au-tchad-des-reformes-pour-les-enfants-non-enregistres/>).

***Also on file***. [SIGPTS](<https://tribuneechos.com/tchad-lanats-et-loif-sunissent-pour-la-transformation-numerique-de-letat-civil/>); [national biometric register](<https://atpe.td/un-enfant-un-acte-de-naissance-une-operation-pour-enregistrer-un-million-denfants/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says records pass beyond TASDJIL's maternity desks.
