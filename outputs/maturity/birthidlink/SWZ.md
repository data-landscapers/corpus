# Eswatini

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In April 2026 a Vital Strategies and UNHCR study said Eswatini already operates an integrated identity system model](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the model links civil registration and national identity systems](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [eight other Southern African countries operate the same model, the study says](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>).
