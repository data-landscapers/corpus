# Eswatini

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Vital Strategies/UNHCR study says Eswatini, with eight other Southern African countries, already operates an integrated identity system model linking civil registration and national identity systems](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.vitalstrategies.org/wp-content/uploads/UNHCR_Birth-Registration_Citizenship.pdf>).
