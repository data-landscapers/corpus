# Ghana

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Ghana Card Number at Birth system, launched by the Vice President, integrates the Births and Deaths Registry, Ghana Health Service and National Identification Authority systems so a newborn's birth notification goes to the registry, which returns a birth registration number, and the child receives a unique ID number linked to the mother's identity](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). [Bedzrah said the system would facilitate deactivating the identity of deceased persons once their deaths are properly registered and verified](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).
