# Ghana

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Ghana Card Number at Birth system, launched in March 2024, joins the systems of the Births and Deaths Registry, the Ghana Health Service and the National Identification Authority](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>). A newborn's birth notification goes to the registry. The registry returns a birth registration number. The child receives an ID number linked to the mother's identity. In June 2024 the Vice-President said [children aged up to 13 would get a Ghana Card number from 17 June 2024](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/06/all-pupils-will-receive-ghana-card-numbers-dr-bawumia/>) through the same link. [The NIA's Bedzrah said the system would help deactivate the identity of the dead](<https://www.ghanaweb.com/GhanaHomePage/business/NIA-biometric-system-to-tighten-fraud-controls-2055880>) once their deaths are registered and verified. [In May 2026 the NIA said it would begin registering children aged 0-5 with the health service and the registry](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/05/nia-registers-over-32000-children-in-volta-oti-regions/>), issuing the birth registration number and the Ghana Card PIN together.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://gna.org.gh/2024/03/dr-bawumia-launches-ghana-card-number-at-birth-to-enhance-public-services/>).
