# Niger

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Niger's director general of territorial administration announced reforms creating the ANEI agency for registration and identification of natural persons, a National Population Register and a National Civil Status Register, with secure interconnection of the systems](<https://anp.ne/diffa-lancement-de-la-tournee-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-la-modernisation-de-letat-civil-et-lidentification/>). [Officials said the planned National Population Register (RNP) will be interoperable with the civil-registration management system and the biometric ID card, with progressive interconnection of national registers](<https://anp.ne/ouverture-a-agadez-dun-atelier-de-sensibilisation-sur-les-reformes-en-matiere-de-letat-civil-et-didentification-des-personnes-au-niger/>). [Le Sahel says Niger created the ANEI, the agency for registration and identification of persons, to administer the RNP and its associated registers, alongside operationalising a national civil-status register (ordonnance 2026-21)](<https://www.lesahel.org/tillaberi-reformes-de-letat-civil-et-de-lidentification-une-mission-de-sensibilisation-sillonne-la-region/>).
