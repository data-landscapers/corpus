# Seychelles

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Civil Status Division registers births, deaths and marriages, keeps the National Population Database and issues identity cards to all residents](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>), so one body holds both the civil register and the ID. [The division sits in the Department of Immigration and Civil Status, which owns both the population database and the identity card](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the division registers births and deaths in the same office that issues the identity card](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ics.gov.sc/about/department/civil-status-division>).
