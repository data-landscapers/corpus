# Sierra Leone

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The National Civil Registration Authority is the single body described as handling both civil registration and national identification](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>). [NCRA Director General Massaquoi said NCRA has integrated civil registration with identity management, one body holding both functions](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-calls-stronger-investment-in-digital/>). [One body, the NCRA, holds both civil registration and the national identity system, per its Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [The NCRA, under the 2016 Act, is legally mandated to manage civil registration, vital statistics and national identity systems, per its Director-General](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ncra-urges-identity-approach-sierra-leones/>). [The World Bank blog says progress is underway with civil registration integrating with the national ID](<https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/africacan/unlocking-opportunity-how-digital-id-can-transform-lives-in-sierra-leone>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thecalabashnewspaper.com/archives/64446>).
