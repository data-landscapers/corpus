# Nigeria

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The population commission says it generates a NIN for each child at birth registration](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>) and synchronises the data with NIMC. [Its chairman said in September 2025 that the NIN had been built into the e-CRVS system](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-chairman-joins-stakeholders-to-celebrate-the-7th-national-day-of-identity-in-abuja>). [Births registered in Jigawa are processed for a NIN](<http://www.nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/npc-chairman-joins-stakeholders-to-celebrate-the-7th-national-day-of-identity-in-abuja>). [The commission's VitalReg platform, launched in August 2026, connects to NIMC](<https://nigeria24.ng/npc-launches-vitalreg-digital-birth-and-death-registration-platform-across-nigeria/>). Self-service birth registration on it needs the child to hold a NIN already. [Since November 2025 Lagos's e-Birth platform links a newborn to a NIN where a parent has one](<https://dpi.africa/from-birth-to-nin-how-lagos-is-building-legal-identity-for-every-child/>). [In August 2026 a birth record on the VitalReg portal was found under a Lagos man's NIN](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-npc-removes-wrong-wife-strange-son-listed-as-lagos-mans-family/>). [UNICEF said in 2026 that linking health facilities had stalled, with none of 371 targeted facilities live](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/birth-registration-npc-unicef-unite-to-give-every-child-legal-identity/>). [A 2025 compact set up ward-level points issuing birth certificates and NINs to under-fives in eleven pilot states](<https://independent.ng/npc-unicef-nimc-algon-forge-strategic-alliance-to-scale-up-birth-registration/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 birth records on the VitalReg portal were linked to the NIN](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-npc-removes-wrong-wife-strange-son-listed-as-lagos-mans-family/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 UNICEF said none of 371 targeted health facilities had live birth-to-ID links](<https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/08/birth-registration-npc-unicef-unite-to-give-every-child-legal-identity/>).
