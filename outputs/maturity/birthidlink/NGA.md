# Nigeria

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [FIJ reports that a birth record on the NPC's VitalReg digital birth registration portal was found registered under a Lagos man's National Identification Number (NIN), linking birth records to the NIN](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-npc-removes-wrong-wife-strange-son-listed-as-lagos-mans-family/>). [The NPC says it now generates a NIN for each child at birth registration and synchronises birth registration data with NIMC's NIN records](<https://blueprint.ng/birth-registration-npc-introduces-nin-integration-for-newborns-linked-to-mothers/>). [The NPC release says NPC, the Health Ministry, NIMC and Barnksforte Technologies have an initiative to issue a national identification number to every child at birth, starting in 2024](<http://nationalpopulation.gov.ng/news/first-lady-launches-new-birth-certificate-to-boost-civil-registration-in-nigeria>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-npc-removes-wrong-wife-strange-son-listed-as-lagos-mans-family/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fij.ng/article/after-fijs-story-npc-removes-wrong-wife-strange-son-listed-as-lagos-mans-family/>).
