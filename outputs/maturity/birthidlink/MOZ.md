# Mozambique

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In the 2025 Nacala birth campaign, people could get an identity card on the spot](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>). It was issued from the registrar's data. [By March 2026 a QR code on birth certificates was improving data exchange with the ID registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099042226205030950/pdf/P172350-ea7be2a4-0727-4372-a851-4df42faaf183.pdf>), the World Bank reports. A new campaign process is tied to the identification directorate's. [The Civil Registration Code of 2018 set up the e-SIRCEV system](<https://www.crvslaws.org/mozambique/summary>), said to be integrated with the ID system. In May 2026 the digital agency's chairman said [a joint registration process with the identification directorate was still being built](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/atdi-destaca-capacidade-nacional-para-digitalizacao-do-registo-integrado-do-cidadao/>). A single app is to follow.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>).

***Also on file***. [e-SIRCEV](<https://civil.registos.gov.mz/crvs/>). [Bilhete de Identidade (BI)](<https://mznews.co.mz/en/governo-alarga-emissao-gratuita-de-bilhetes-de-identidade-ate-2027/>). [Mobile digital ID and e-signature](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/mozambique-seeks-partner-for-mobile-digital-id-e-signature-rollout>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says records pass between the two registers beyond the Nacala campaign.
