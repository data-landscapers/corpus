# Mozambique

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Jornal Notícias reports that people registered in the Nacala birth campaign can have the Bilhete de Identidade issued on the spot from the electronic data the registrar captured, through interoperability of the two services](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>). [The Civil Registration Code as revised by Law 12/2018 established the e-SIRCEV electronic system, said to be integrated with the national ID and health systems (CRVS Law and Policy Compendium) (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.crvslaws.org/mozambique/summary>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://jornalnoticias.co.mz/destaque/mocambique-reforca-sua-estrategia-de-expansao-do-registo-civil/>).
