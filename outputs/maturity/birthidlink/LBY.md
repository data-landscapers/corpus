# Libya

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** No source found since October 2023 says records pass between the two in service. The nearest held fact: [a team comparing national ID system data with the civil status registry found discrepancies, and 160 prosecution committees were sent to every civil registry office to verify records](<https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system or deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the Attorney General's review of the Civil Registry Authority, begun around 2016](<https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/>).

**Exchange reported.** No figure has been found for records the two systems exchange. The figure held comes from an audit, which is not their exchange: [in December 2025 Attorney General Al-Siddiq Al-Sour said the review had deleted more than 17,000 national ID numbers that do not exist, with nearly 40,000 salary numbers linked to them, and that about 34,000 family records were still under investigation](<https://libyareview.com/61619/libyan-prosecutors-uncover-large-scale-identity-manipulation/>).

***Also on file***. [Civil Registry (Intilaqa project)](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/450311>); [National Number (Rakam Watani) system](<https://alwasat.ly/news/479468>); [electronic civil registry database](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=351962&lang=ar>).

***Not found***. Nothing found says records pass between the civil register and the ID system, or that a link is under way; the one figure held comes from an audit, which is not their exchange.
