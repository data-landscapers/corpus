# Mauritius

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Civil Status Division keeps the civil status database, which generates each citizen's identification number at birth registration](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>). A 2024 Pay Research Bureau report says so. [Regulations of February 2024 end a national identity card's validity at the registration of the holder's death](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>).

**Both ways.** Records pass at birth and at death. [The identification number is generated when a birth is registered](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>). [Before a dead holder's card is perforated, the Head of the Unit must check the civil identity register records the death](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the civil status database is kept by the Civil Status Division](<https://prb.govmu.org/prb/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/csd.pdf>).
