# Mauritius

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The regulations end a card's validity at the registration of the holder's death, and require the Head of the Unit to check the holder is recorded as deceased in the civil identity register, or obtain the death certificate, before the card is perforated](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://paps.mnis.govmu.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/29_The-National-IC-Reg-2024-.pdf>).
