# Tunisia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The playbook shows the citizen identifier register initialised and updated from the national civil status system Madania by API, and holding birth-act and death-act references, the date and place of death and civil status](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [The deck says the 11-digit unique citizen number is assigned at birth or on acquiring Tunisian nationality, and shows the IUC system drawing on the civil status system Madania](<https://id4africa.com/2024/ps3/PS3-3-Tunisia-Final.pdf>).

**Both ways.** [The citizen number is given to every person entered in the civil register at birth, and the register takes the death-act reference and the date and place of death from Madania](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>). [A 2024 law requires administrative services to report a cardholder's death within thirty days, and the card's chip is then deactivated](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-2-Tunisia-FINAL.pdf>).
