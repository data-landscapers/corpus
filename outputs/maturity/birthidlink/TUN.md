# Tunisia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The law requires administrative services to inform the national security directorate of a cardholder's death within thirty days, whereupon the card chip is deactivated, and requires civil-status changes to be notified for the card within 90 days of entry in the civil register](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://legislation-securite.tn/latest-laws/loi-organique-n-2024-22-du-11-mars-2024-modifiant-et-completant-la-loi-n-93-27-du-22-mars-1993-relative-a-la-carte-nationale-didentite/>).
