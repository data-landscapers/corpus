# Togo

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Togo First says civil registration is becoming more closely integrated with the identification system through the rollout of the unique identification number](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited included, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most fifteen stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>).
