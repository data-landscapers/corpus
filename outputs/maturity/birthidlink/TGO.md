# Togo

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In August 2026 Togo First said civil registration is becoming more closely integrated with the identification system](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>). [A 2026 ID4Africa deck lists integrating the unique identification number across public services, including the civil registry, as a planned step](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [Togo First ties the integration to the rollout of the unique identification number](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/justice/2408-19853-togo-reviews-civil-registration-law-to-support-digital-transformation>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the deck places that step in its revenue and adoption model](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-2-4-Togo-FINAL.pdf>).
