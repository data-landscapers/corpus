# Gambia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In the 2025 State of the Nation Address President Barrow said ID cards will be integrated with the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>). [Passport and other databases are to join them in a unified digital ID system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>). [On 22 March 2024 the Ministries of Health and Interior signed an MoU to harmonise and integrate the two](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>). [The National Identity System is run by the Immigration Department, the eCRVS by the Registry of Births and Deaths](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>). [The MoU provides for unified NIN generation and sharing between them](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 President Barrow said ID cards would be integrated with the electronic civil registration system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in March 2024 the health and interior ministries signed an MoU to integrate the national ID system and the eCRVS](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>).
