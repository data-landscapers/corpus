# Gambia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [President Barrow said in the 2025 State of the Nation Address that ID cards will be integrated with the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics system, passport and other databases into a unified digital ID system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>). [The Ministries of Health and Interior signed an MoU on 22 March 2024 to harmonise and integrate the National Identity System run by the Immigration Department and the eCRVS run by the Registry of Births and Deaths, with unified NIN generation and sharing between them](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/health-ministry-signs-mou-with-interior-to-harmonize-nis>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).
