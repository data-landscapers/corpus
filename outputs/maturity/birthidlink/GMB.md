# Gambia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [President Barrow said in the 2025 State of the Nation Address that ID cards will be integrated with the Electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics system, passport and other databases into a unified digital ID system](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>). [The World Bank says the Interior Ministry's national ID system was integrated with the Health Ministry's civil registration through an eCRVS that generates a unique national identification number at registration (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/03/28/afw-transformative-journey-of-mass-birth-registration-and-health-insurance-in-the-gambia>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/govt-to-integrate-id-cards-with-electronic-civil-registration>).
