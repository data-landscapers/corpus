# Comoros

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [On 25 March 2026 a study under the Amecc project was validated, according to We are Tech](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/les-comores-lancent-la-numerisation-de-leur-etat-civil>). [It creates a Personal Identification Number (NIP) for civil registration](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/les-comores-lancent-la-numerisation-de-leur-etat-civil>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the study plans interoperability with other state systems](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/les-comores-lancent-la-numerisation-de-leur-etat-civil>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the interoperability plan dates from a study validated in March 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/les-comores-lancent-la-numerisation-de-leur-etat-civil>).
