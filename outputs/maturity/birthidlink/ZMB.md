# Zambia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Zambia's national registration department runs birth registration, digital identification for adults and death certificates through one system, INRIS, deployed to 26 district offices in March 2022, says the Lavushimanda district officer](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>). [ITWeb says Zambia's Integrated National Registration Information System (INRIS) is intended to digitise civil registration and identity management together](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [Zambia's Smart Zambia Institute is seeking a system integrator to integrate the MOSIP-based digital ID system with the national civil registration architecture](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says it is implementing end-to-end automation of birth and death notifications through the INRIS API Manager, with real-time updates to population records](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).
