# Zambia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In September 2026 the national registration department ran birth registration, digital identification for adults and death certificates through one system, INRIS](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>). [INRIS was deployed to 26 district offices in March 2022](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>). [INRIS is intended to digitise civil registration and identity management together](<https://www.itweb.co.za/article/from-rails-to-routine/dgp45vaB8nQ7X9l8>). [The Smart Zambia Institute is seeking a system integrator to join the MOSIP-based digital ID system to the civil registration architecture](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202603/zambia-looking-for-mosip-system-integrator-to-support-digital-id-project>). [The Ministry of Home Affairs says it is automating birth and death notifications end to end through the INRIS API Manager](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>). [Population records are updated in real time](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 one system, INRIS, ran birth registration, adult ID and death certificates](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/03/govt-implements-digital-civil-registration/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in December 2025 the home affairs ministry was automating birth and death notifications through the INRIS API Manager](<https://znbc.co.zm/?p=10211>).
