# Mali

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In May 2025 the DNEC said a biometric population file had been built from the civil register](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). [NINA numbers are attributed through the CTDEC/ANSAEC](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). [In 2025 the Ministry of Territorial Administration gave ANSAEC the mission of building the civil status database](<https://matd.gouv.ml/agence-nationale-de-securisation-des-actes-detat-civil>). [ANSAEC also assigns each person in it a National Identification Number, with the national statistics service](<https://matd.gouv.ml/agence-nationale-de-securisation-des-actes-detat-civil>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [one body, the CTDEC/ANSAEC, holds the RAVEC database](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the DNEC reported more than 20 million NINA attributed](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [NINA](<https://dpimap.org/mali/>); [RAVEC / NINA biometric file](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers.
