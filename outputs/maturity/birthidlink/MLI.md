# Mali

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [DNEC says a biometric population file has been built from the civil register and more than 20 million NINA have been attributed through the CTDEC/ANSAEC, one body holding the RAVEC database](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-3-MALI_FINAL.pdf>).
