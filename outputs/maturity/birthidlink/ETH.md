# Ethiopia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Addis Ababa's new civil registration system shares data with the national ID programme](<https://www.amn.gov.et/en/mayor-launches-new-digital-civil-registration-and-residency-service-platform/>), as well as with health, immigration and the courts, its mayor said in December 2025. [In March 2025 Fayda registration began in Addis Ababa schools alongside civil registration](<https://www.bbc.com/amharic/articles/c204dl15p96o>), so a child taking a birth certificate can take a Fayda number too. [The ID programme says Fayda is to be issued at every vital event: birth, marriage and death](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>). [Talks on joining the two systems continue](<https://connect.mosip.io/resources_pdf_2026/Inclusive_Identity_at_Scale_Ethiopia.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ebc.et/english/Home/NewsDetails?NewsId=3422>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ebc.et/english/Home/NewsDetails?NewsId=3422>).
