# South Africa

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [SAnews reports Cabinet approved a White Paper turning the National Population Register into an Intelligent Population Register using interoperability and real-time data integration as the foundation for a Digital ID, with mandatory digital birth and death registration](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
