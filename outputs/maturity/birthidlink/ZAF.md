# South Africa

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Home Affairs told Parliament in November 2025 how births at health facilities are handled](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33804/>). They enter the population register through a single entry point. The child is given an ID number at birth and keeps it for life, under an agreement with the Department of Health. [In April 2026 Cabinet approved a White Paper to turn the register into an Intelligent Population Register](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>), the foundation for a digital ID.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says deaths retire ID numbers. [The White Paper makes digital birth and death registration mandatory](<https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/schreiber-welcomes-approval-revised-white-paper-citizenship>), but as a plan. [The December 2025 draft also proposed registering both events digitally](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/minister-leon-schreiber-invites-public-comment-draft-revised-white-paper-citizenship>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact is [the single entry point for births at health facilities](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/33804/>).
