# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Zimbabwe's Civil Registry Department registers births and deaths and issues national identity documents, so one body holds both functions, per the home affairs minister](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [The Civil Registry Department issues birth and death certificates and national identity cards and is custodian of the national citizens' database, so one body holds both](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/civil-registry-department-to-computerise-sub-offices/>). [Zimbabwe's Civil Registry Department says it has introduced automated validation and synchronization with the National Population Register, and a pilot of CRVS-Health interoperability in 3 provinces is planned for Q1-Q2 2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).
