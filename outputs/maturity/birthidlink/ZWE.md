# Zimbabwe

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In September 2026 the home affairs minister said the Civil Registry Department registers births and deaths and issues national identity documents](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [One body holds both functions](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>). [The department issues birth and death certificates and national identity cards, and is custodian of the national citizens' database](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/civil-registry-department-to-computerise-sub-offices/>). [In July 2026 it said it had introduced automated validation and synchronisation with the National Population Register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>). [A pilot of CRVS-Health interoperability in 3 provinces is planned for the first half of 2027](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 the Civil Registry Department registered births and deaths and issued national IDs](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/30-new-vehicles-boost-civil-registry-services/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the Civil Registry Department synchronised records with the National Population Register](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-zimbabwe.pdf>).
