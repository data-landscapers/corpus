# Sudan

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The police spokesman said in August 2024 that the Civil Registry's system holds the national number with birth and death certificates](<https://awalalnahar.net/archives/22743>). [It also holds marriage, divorce and inheritance records](<https://awalalnahar.net/archives/22743>). [Its data had been recovered up to 13 April 2023](<https://awalalnahar.net/archives/22743>). [At the 21 May 2026 launch of SUDAPASS, the new digital ID, officials briefed on its integration with civil registry databases](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>). [The project runs with the Interior Ministry's Civil Registry](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://diplomat.so/articles/1395/Sudan-launches-SUDAPASS-digital-ID-in-Khartoum>).
