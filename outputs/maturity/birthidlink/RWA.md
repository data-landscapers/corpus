# Rwanda

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Rwanda has digitised birth registration and integrated it with the national ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>), Vital Strategies says. [A civil registration system launched in June 2026 links the national CRVS to the population registry and health records](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launches-new-integrated-civil-registration-system>). [The ID agency's director general says its electronic system records births and deaths](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/over-7-2-million-rwandans-complete-biometric-enrolment-for-electronic-ids/>). [The same agency runs electronic ID card enrolment](<https://radiotv10.rw/en/over-7-2-million-rwandans-complete-biometric-enrolment-for-electronic-ids/>). [A May 2026 presentation says the single digital identity is being rolled out from birth](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-1-Rwanda-FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).
