# Rwanda

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Vital Strategies says Rwanda has digitised birth registration and integrated it with the national ID system, giving real-time data sharing](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>). [The new civil registration system launched by MINALOC and the National Identification Agency has interoperable capabilities linking the national CRVS to the National Population Registry and the Health Information Management System](<https://www.minaloc.gov.rw/news-detail/rwanda-launches-new-integrated-civil-registration-system>). [NIDA's director general says the new digital ID system is integrated with Rwanda's civil registration and vital statistics system, without saying which records pass](<https://www.newtimes.co.rw/article/34090/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/birth-registration-emerges-as-foundation-for-digital-public-infrastructure>).
