# South Sudan

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Directorate General of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passport and Immigration handles civil registration and issues national identity cards, so one body holds both functions (Eye Radio report of the handover)](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>). [South Sudan's Directorate of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passport and Immigration says it is planning to digitise civil registration and link it with the national ID system, with Justice, Interior and Health ministries involved, per Biometric Update](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>).
