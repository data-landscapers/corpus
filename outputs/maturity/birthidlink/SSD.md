# South Sudan

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In August 2026 the Directorate General of Civil Registry, Nationality, Passport and Immigration handled civil registration and issued national identity cards](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>). [One body holds both functions](<https://www.eyeradio.org/new-immigration-chief-pledges-digital-reform-as-outgoing-director-leaves-stock-of-passports/>). [In July 2026 the directorate said it plans to digitise civil registration and link it with the national ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>). [The Justice, Interior and Health ministries are involved](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202607/south-sudan-plans-interoperable-birth-registration-digital-id-systems>).
