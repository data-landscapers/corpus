# Angola

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Angola's Justice Minister says the Bilhete de Identidade results from integrating data in the birth record through interoperability between the respective computer systems](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [Angop reports that Mbanza Kongo (Zaire) began civil registration of newborns with consequent attribution of the Bilhete de Identidade at a maternity post, under the nationwide EU-funded 'Nascer com Registo' project, running at the hospital since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [Angola says changes to the civil registry and identity card laws led to operations to merge the two systems, and a multifunctional database under the Ministry of Justice holds the civil registry and identity card management systems](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). [In September 2025 the Secretary of State for Justice said the Plataforma Única do Cidadão, joining civil registration and the ID card, was close to being institutionalised](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Both ways.** [The Justice Minister says the ID card is issued from birth-record data through linked systems](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [A death registered at the conservatória is sent to the database to deregister the citizen, which is why the deceased's Bilhete de Identidade is asked for, said Aristides Marques, national director of Identification, Registries and Notary, in April 2026](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/a-nossa-medida-visa-esclarecer-que-o-cidadao-deve-ter-o-bi-e-nao-e-so-a-pensar-na-questao-da-morte-aristides-marques-director-nacional-de-identificacao-registos-e-notariado-70892.html>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>).
