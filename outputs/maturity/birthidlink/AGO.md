# Angola

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Angola's Justice Minister says the Bilhete de Identidade results from integrating data in the birth record through interoperability between the respective computer systems](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [Angop reports that Mbanza Kongo (Zaire) began civil registration of newborns with consequent attribution of the Bilhete de Identidade at a maternity post, under the nationwide EU-funded 'Nascer com Registo' project, running at the hospital since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [Angola says changes to the civil registry and identity card laws led to operations to merge the two systems, and a multifunctional database under the Ministry of Justice holds the civil registry and identity card management systems](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>).
