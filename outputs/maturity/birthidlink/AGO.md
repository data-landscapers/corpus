# Angola

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In July 2026 Angola's Justice Minister said the Bilhete de Identidade results from integrating birth-record data](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [The two computer systems are interoperable](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [In March 2026 Mbanza Kongo began registering newborns at a maternity post, with a Bilhete de Identidade attributed in consequence](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [It runs under the nationwide EU-funded 'Nascer com Registo' project, at the hospital since October 2025](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/zaire-mbanza-kongo-inicia-registo-de-recem-nascidos-a-partir-da-maternidade/>). [Changes to the civil registry and identity card laws led to operations to merge the two systems, Angola said in July 2026](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). [A multifunctional database under the Ministry of Justice holds both management systems](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-digitisation-angola.pdf>). [In September 2025 the Secretary of State for Justice said the Plataforma Única do Cidadão, joining civil registration and the ID card, was close to being institutionalised](<https://pti.ao/ministerio-da-justica-reitera-aposta-nas-tic-para-modernizar-e-simplificar-servicos/>).

**Both ways.** [The Justice Minister says the ID card is issued from birth-record data through linked systems](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>). [In April 2026 Aristides Marques, national director of Identification, Registries and Notary, said a death registered at the conservatória is sent to the database to deregister the citizen](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/a-nossa-medida-visa-esclarecer-que-o-cidadao-deve-ter-o-bi-e-nao-e-so-a-pensar-na-questao-da-morte-aristides-marques-director-nacional-de-identificacao-registos-e-notariado-70892.html>). [The deceased's Bilhete de Identidade is asked for so that this is reliable](<https://novojornal.co.ao/politica/detalhe/a-nossa-medida-visa-esclarecer-que-o-cidadao-deve-ter-o-bi-e-nao-e-so-a-pensar-na-questao-da-morte-aristides-marques-director-nacional-de-identificacao-registos-e-notariado-70892.html>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the Justice Minister said the identity card draws on birth record data through system interoperability](<https://angop.ao/noticias/politica/impressao-do-bi-abrange-todas-as-provincias-do-pais/>).
