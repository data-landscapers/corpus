# Cameroon

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** In October 2025 [BUNEC partnered with Liber Technologies to build a digital civil registration system by 2029, integrated with the new biometric ID](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202510/cameroon-partners-with-liber-technologies-to-pursue-crvs-reforms>). In October 2026 [BUNEC's director general said it was deploying a platform to link civil registration and the national ID system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). [BUNEC's plan for 2025 to 2029 provides for linking the main civil registration centres with identification services](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/etat-civil-le-cameroun-s-inspire-de-la-cote-d-ivoire-pour-interconnecter-ses-donnees>). [It sets 2029 for the two to work together](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroon-looks-to-cote-divoire-to-advance-digital-civil-registration>).

**Both ways.** The nearest held fact is [the plan to link registration centres with identification services by 2029](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/etat-civil-le-cameroun-s-inspire-de-la-cote-d-ivoire-pour-interconnecter-ses-donnees>). No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system or deaths retire their numbers.

**Exchange reported.** The nearest held fact is [the platform BUNEC was deploying in October 2026](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/cameroons-civil-registration-reform-faces-85m-funding-gap>). No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two.
