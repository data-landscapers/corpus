# Benin

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ANIP, the national identification agency, holds both the population register and the civil status file](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [Since April 2021 every birth is declared to the register, which issues the child's NPI](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>) and enters the birth in the civil status file. [In September 2026 ANIP issued 50,109 free birth certificates to children under 14](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-50-109-enfants-ont-obtenu-gratuitement-leur-acte-de-naissance-en-septembre/>). [From August 2025 a single birth desk in ten public health facilities generated the NPI when a birth was declared](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/le-b%C3%A9nin-modernise-lenregistrement-des-naissances-avec-un-guichet-unique-digital>). [Over 13,000 of 17,071 children declared there were registered](<https://www.unicef.org/benin/recits/b%C3%A9nin-journ%C3%A9e-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-et-des-statistiques-de-l%C3%A9tat-civil-2026>).

**Both ways.** [ANIP says a birth declared to the register is entered automatically in the civil status file](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>). [A declared death sets the person's status to deceased in the ID system and is registered with civil status](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** [ANIP's review of its birth-desk pilot counts 30,160 number updates in the register](<https://www.triomphemag.com/enregistrement-des-naissances-au-benin-le-guichet-unique-de-lanip-fait-bondir-le-taux-de-2195-a-7656-en-six-mois/>). [The pilot ran in ten health facilities from 20 August 2025 to 20 February 2026](<https://www.triomphemag.com/enregistrement-des-naissances-au-benin-le-guichet-unique-de-lanip-fait-bondir-le-taux-de-2195-a-7656-en-six-mois/>). [Parents receive the child's NPI by SMS within 48 hours](<https://www.triomphemag.com/enregistrement-des-naissances-au-benin-le-guichet-unique-de-lanip-fait-bondir-le-taux-de-2195-a-7656-en-six-mois/>).
