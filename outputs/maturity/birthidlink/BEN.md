# Benin

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ANIP, the national identification agency, issued 50,109 free birth certificates to children under 14 in September 2026 and enrolled 814 children in Agonve, one body doing both civil and identity work](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-50-109-enfants-ont-obtenu-gratuitement-leur-acte-de-naissance-en-septembre/>). [La Nation reports that the national ID agency ANIP, with UNICEF, issued free birth certificates from 16 September to 30 September 2026 to registered children under 14, including those enrolled in the RAVIP, using the number on the birth declaration form or RAVIP receipt, so one body handles both](<https://www.lanation.bj/societe/acte-de-naissance-gratuit-derniere-journee-pour-regulariser-lidentite-de-votre-enfant>). [Since April 2021 every birth is declared to the RNPP, which issues the child's NPI and enters the birth in the national civil status file, both run by ANIP](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** [ANIP says a birth declared to the RNPP registers automatically in the civil status file, and a declared death sets the person's status to deceased in the ID system and registers with civil status](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-3-Benin-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** [ANIP's review of its birth-desk pilot in ten health facilities, 20 August 2025 to 20 February 2026, counts 30,160 number updates in the RNPP; parents receive the child's NPI by SMS within 48 hours](<https://www.triomphemag.com/enregistrement-des-naissances-au-benin-le-guichet-unique-de-lanip-fait-bondir-le-taux-de-2195-a-7656-en-six-mois/>).
