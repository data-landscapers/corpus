# Benin

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ANIP, the national identification agency, issued 50,109 free birth certificates to children under 14 in September 2026 and enrolled 814 children in Agonve, one body doing both civil and identity work](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-50-109-enfants-ont-obtenu-gratuitement-leur-acte-de-naissance-en-septembre/>). [La Nation reports that the national ID agency ANIP, with UNICEF, issued free birth certificates from 16 September to 30 September 2026 to registered children under 14, including those enrolled in the RAVIP, using the number on the birth declaration form or RAVIP receipt, so one body handles both](<https://www.lanation.bj/societe/acte-de-naissance-gratuit-derniere-journee-pour-regulariser-lidentite-de-votre-enfant>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-50-109-enfants-ont-obtenu-gratuitement-leur-acte-de-naissance-en-septembre/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://beninwebtv.com/benin-50-109-enfants-ont-obtenu-gratuitement-leur-acte-de-naissance-en-septembre/>).
