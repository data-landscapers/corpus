# Algeria

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In October 2026 the Interior minister said his ministry has a central role in legal identity](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>). [It holds both the civil register (etat civil) and the issue of identity documents](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>). [In May 2026 the ministry said its central biometric document system is connected to the national civil register (RNEC) and to AFIS](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). [The national identification number (NIN) is the key between the databases](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). [Since 2018, the ministry says, the NIN has been generated automatically from the civil register when a birth is registered](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 the NIN was the key between the civil register and ID databases](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 the Interior minister said his ministry runs both the civil register and identity documents](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>).
