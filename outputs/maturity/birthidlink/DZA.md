# Algeria

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Interior minister said his ministry has a central role in citizens' legal identity through both the civil register (etat civil) and the issue of identity documents](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>). [The Ministry of the Interior says the central biometric document issuance system is connected to the national civil register (RNEC) and to AFIS, with the national identification number (NIN) as the key between the databases](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T2-3-S3-4-Algeria-FINAL.pdf>). [Algeria's Ministry of Interior says that since 2018 the NIN is generated automatically from the civil register (RNAEC) when a birth is registered (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-decentralisation-algeria.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://capdz.dz/archives/21340>).
