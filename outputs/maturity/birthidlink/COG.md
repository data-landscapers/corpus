# Congo

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Under a November 2024 decree, civil registration officers drawing up a death certificate for a Congolese over 16 must collect the holder's ID card](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). [They pass the card to the direction de l'identification civile](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). [In July 2025 the Interior Ministry was stated to be the guarantor of both identification and civil status](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the 2024 decree sets the step at the drawing up of a death certificate](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the Interior Ministry carries the digital identity effort with World Bank support](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865>).
