# Congo

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The decree requires civil registration officers drawing up a death certificate for a Congolese over 16 to collect the holder's ID card and pass it to the direction de l'identification civile](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>). [The Interior Ministry is stated to be the guarantor of both identification and civil status, and is carrying the digital identity effort with World Bank support](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/digitalisation-de-ladministration-lidentite-numerique-un-pilier-de-la-citoyennete-165865>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/117585/COG-117585.pdf>).
