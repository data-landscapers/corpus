# Botswana

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Statistics Botswana says the Births and Deaths Registration System is integrated with the National Identification System](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [Since 2003 that system has assigned a unique birth registration number to every citizen child at birth](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [In April 2026 one budget provision of P108,505,050 for the Department of Civil and National Registration covered maintenance of both systems](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/LABOUR%20AND%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>). [One department holds both](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/LABOUR%20AND%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the national ID system assigned each citizen child a unique birth registration number](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 one budget provision of P108.5 million covered both the births and deaths system and the ID system](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/LABOUR%20AND%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>).
