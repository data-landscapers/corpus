# Botswana

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Statistics Botswana says the Births and Deaths Registration System is integrated with the National Identification System, which assigns a unique birth registration number to every citizen child at birth (introduced 2003)](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>). [The Botswana Committee of Supply speech for 2026/2027 says one provision of P108,505,050 for the Department of Civil and National Registration covers maintenance of both the Births and Deaths Registration System and the National Identification System, so one department holds both](<https://botswanaspeaks.gov.bw/media/COMMITTEE%20OF%20SUPPPLY%20SPEECHES/LABOUR%20AND%20HOME%20AFFAIRS.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/publications/Vital%20Statistics%20Report%202024.pdf>).
