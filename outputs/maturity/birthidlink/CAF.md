# Central African Republic

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In November 2025 an article said the country plans a national digital identity system, the SNID](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>). [The SNID is to unify the country's existing biometric and civil status databases](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the SNID is to bring the biometric and civil status databases into one](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [implementation of the SNID is set for 2027 to 2032](<https://ambition-journal.org/fr/economie/systeme-national-d-identite-numerique-moderniser-l-administration>).
