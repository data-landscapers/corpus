# Kenya

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Kenya's Civil Registration Services say interoperability between CRVS and National ID systems is established: parents' national ID details are in birth records and the Birth Entry Number is in National ID registration, with birth records used to verify identity at ID registration](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). [Immigration PS Belio Kipsang said on 6 October 2026 the Interior Ministry was finalising talks with the Ministry of Health to integrate the civil registration system with hospital management systems, and that the UPI system is being integrated with education and civil registration records](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>).
