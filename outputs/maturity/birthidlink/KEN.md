# Kenya

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Kenya's Civil Registration Services say the civil register and the National ID system are linked](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>). Birth records carry the parents' ID details. ID registration carries the Birth Entry Number. Birth records verify identity when people register for an ID. [In May 2024 the Principal Secretary said the National Registration Bureau, civil registration and immigration all connect to the IPRS database](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/digital-identity-id-roll-out-on-course-ps/>). In January 2026 [he said KEMIS would be linked to civil registration and the registration bureau](<https://educationnews.co.ke/government-to-roll-out-kemis-on-january-9-2026-to-track-learners-nationwide/>) so each learner holds a personal identifier from birth. [In May 2026 he told MPs a hospital e-notification system will pass each birth to Civil Registration Services](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/inside-new-reforms-to-curb-birth-certificate-fraud-5463104>), which issue the child a Maisha Number. On 6 October 2026 [he said civil registration would be integrated with hospital systems](<https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/127644-state-expand-digital-birth-registration-sha-hospitals-within-3-months>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-interoperability-kenya.pdf>).
