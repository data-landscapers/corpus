# Gabon

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In June 2025 ANINF and Interior Ministry officials said the Gabon Digital programme plans to interconnect three systems](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identification-legale-et-digitale-le-gabon-enclenche-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-trois-projets-strategiques/>). [They are the biometric persons register, the digital ID system and the national civil registry centre](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identification-legale-et-digitale-le-gabon-enclenche-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-trois-projets-strategiques/>). [The amended Digital Gabon financing agreement took effect on 2 September 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>). [It provides for a civil registry interoperable with, and distinct from, the RBPP ID system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the planned link runs through a common biometric referential](<https://www.gabonreview.com/identification-legale-et-digitale-le-gabon-enclenche-la-mise-en-oeuvre-de-trois-projets-strategiques/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the 2026 agreement provides for a fully digitalised civil registry](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090226132027563/pdf/P175987-8dfb5a41-ff83-4fc0-a597-c588997e88ba.pdf>).
