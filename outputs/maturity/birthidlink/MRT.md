# Mauritania

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Interior Ministry's report for early 2026 lists the biometric databases' interconnection with civil status as done](<https://dgat.interieur.gov.mr/node/397>). [One agency, the ANRPTS, handles the population register, civil status, ID cards and passports](<https://www.elmourageb.com/node/25951>), an August 2026 opinion piece says. [So one body holds both the civil and the ID functions](<https://www.elmourageb.com/node/25951>). [It was set up by decree 150/2010](<https://www.elmourageb.com/node/25951>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. [The ministry's report names the interconnection without saying which records pass, or which way](<https://dgat.interieur.gov.mr/node/397>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. [The ministry's report of early 2026 names the interconnection and gives no figure](<https://dgat.interieur.gov.mr/node/397>).
