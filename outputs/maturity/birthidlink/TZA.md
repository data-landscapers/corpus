# Tanzania

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The NIDA Director General said NIDA plans to connect the national ID with services including the birth certificate, from four services now to nine](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>). [NIDA director general James Kaji said birth certificates and some RITA functions are intended to move onto the national ID, so that many civil services sit with NIDA](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [Tanzania's civil registration agency RITA is to be linked with the National Identification Authority NIDA, and a legal framework for this was announced in January as being prepared](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202608/tanzania-pushes-crvs-id-integration-as-birth-registration-becomes-a-dpi-layer>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>).
