# Tanzania

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In May 2026 NIDA said the Jamii Number is issued once in a lifetime and deactivated at death](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [A CRVS-ID data sharing API is fully operational](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [In September 2026 NIDA director general James Kaji said birth certificates and some RITA functions are to move onto the national ID](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [Many civil services would then sit with NIDA](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [NIDA plans to connect the national ID with nine services, the birth certificate among them, up from four](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>). [NIDA says the NIDA-RITA merger announced in 2023 is to be completed from 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 NIDA listed merging national ID and civil registration among its priorities for 2026-2029](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T1-3-4-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 NIDA said a data-sharing API between civil registration and ID was fully operational](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).
