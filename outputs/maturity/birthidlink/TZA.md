# Tanzania

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [NIDA says the Jamii Number is issued once in a lifetime and deactivated at death, with a CRVS-ID data sharing API fully operational](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>). [NIDA director general James Kaji said birth certificates and some RITA functions are intended to move onto the national ID, so that many civil services sit with NIDA](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/nida-yaondoa-kikwazo-cha-umri-namba-kuunganisha-huduma-za-kiraia-5597344>). [The NIDA Director General said NIDA plans to connect the national ID with services including the birth certificate, from four services now to nine](<https://www.ippmedia.co.tz/nipashe/habari/kitaifa/read/nida-yako-kuwa-jamii-namba-huduma-9-kuunganishwa-2026-09-17-152047>). [NIDA says the NIDA-RITA merger announced in 2023 is to be completed from 2026](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-2-Tanzania-FINAL.pdf>).
