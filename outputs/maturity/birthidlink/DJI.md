# Djibouti

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In July 2026 the DGPF director said one directorate handles both the biometric ID card and civil-status records](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [Birth and marriage certificates are being integrated into a digital database](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [An integrated identity and civil-status system is the long-term aim](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [Birth, marriage and death records are being archived electronically in the national database the DGPF also uses for identity-card enrolment](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>). [Children born abroad must have their birth transcribed before applying for the card](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the DGPF was archiving birth, marriage and death records in its ID enrolment database](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 the DGPF director said an integrated identity and civil-status system was the long-term aim](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>).
