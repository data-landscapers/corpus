# Djibouti

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The DGPF director says one directorate handles both the biometric ID card and civil-status records, with birth and marriage certificates being integrated into a digital database, and an integrated identity and civil-status system as the long-term aim](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>). [The DGPF holds both registers: birth, marriage and death records are being archived electronically in the national database it also uses for identity-card enrolment, and children born abroad must have their birth transcribed before applying for the card](<https://www.lanation.dj/carte-nationale-didentite-biometrique-dans-les-coulisses-de-la-nouvelle-identite-nationale/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lanation.dj/notre-vision-est-de-faire-de-la-direction-generale-de-la-population-et-de-la-famille-une-administration-entierement-modernisee-interconnectee-et-proche-des-citoyens-abdi-abdillahi-obsie/>).
