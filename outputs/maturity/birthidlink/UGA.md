# Uganda

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Nile Post reports that NIRA's mandate covers registration and identification of citizens and resident foreigners as well as civil registration of births, deaths, adoptions, guardianship orders and marriages, so one body holds both](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/374307/identity-is-the-foundation-of-security-development-says-minister-kamuntu-as-nira-expo-opens>). [Under the Registration of Persons Act 2015 as amended in 2024, NIRA holds the National Identification Register and also handles births, deaths and, since 2024, marriage registration](<https://www.independent.co.ug/nira-staffing-crisis-leaves-millions-of-ids-gathering-dust/>). [NIRA runs both birth registration (issuing birth certificates) and the National Identification Numbers, one body handling both, according to its registrar Claire Ollama](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/news/nira-intensifies-birth-registration-drive-lau-NV_234727_062026>). [Civil registration moved from the Uganda Registration Services Bureau to NIRA on 1 October 2024](<https://ursb.go.ug/2024/09/20/civilregistrytransition/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/374307/identity-is-the-foundation-of-security-development-says-minister-kamuntu-as-nira-expo-opens>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://nilepost.co.ug/news/374307/identity-is-the-foundation-of-security-development-says-minister-kamuntu-as-nira-expo-opens>).
