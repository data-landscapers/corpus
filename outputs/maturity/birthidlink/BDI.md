# Burundi

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In August 2026 the territorial administration directorate said civil registration is to be integrated with the national digital ID](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). [The interior ministry presented one platform for civil records and the new biometric ID card on 6 May 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative>). [Burundi's own presentation says the two systems are not yet fully interoperable](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>). [Under the ID for D plan of July 2024, births at health facilities are to feed a unique ID number](<https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/vers-une-identification-sure-et-securisee/>). [It is to start in eleven communes](<https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/vers-une-identification-sure-et-securisee/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in July 2024 the ministry said births under ID for D would feed a unique ID number](<https://mininterinfos.gov.bi/vers-une-identification-sure-et-securisee/>).
