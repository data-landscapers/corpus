# Burundi

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Directorate General for territorial administration says the civil registration, legal identity and health systems are to be interconnected, and civil registration is to be integrated with the national digital identity system](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>). [We Are Tech Africa reports the Ministry of the Interior presented on 6 May 2026 one digital platform for managing civil registration and producing the new biometric national ID card, with civil records being digitised](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/burundi-launches-digital-civil-registry-and-biometric-id-initiative>). [Burundi's presentation says the civil registration system is 'not yet fully interoperable' with the future national digital identification system](<https://apai-crvs.uneca.org/sites/default/files/resourceuploads/day-2-burundi_crvs_transformation_journey.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.rtnb.bi/?mypost%3Fid=596>).
