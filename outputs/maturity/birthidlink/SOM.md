# Somalia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation is testing interfaces that would integrate the digital civil registration platform with national ID, health, education and immigration services](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). [Hiiraan Online reports that the Civil Registration Management and Vital Statistics System (CRMVS), delivered in November with NADRA funding, is a cornerstone of Somalia's National Identification System project, and that NIRA is tasked with managing the system](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Jan/199818/somalia_launches_ambitious_civil_registration_overhaul.aspx>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>).
