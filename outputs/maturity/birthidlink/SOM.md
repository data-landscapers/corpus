# Somalia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In August 2025 the Ministry of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation was testing interfaces to link the digital civil registration platform with national ID](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). [Health, education and immigration services are also to be linked](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1108-48024-somalia-pilots-19-district-digital-civil-registry-lays-groundwork-for-nationwide-legal-identity>). [The ministry says it is working to integrate the digital registration system with other government services, national ID among them](<https://sonna.so/en/article/somalia-marks-africa-civil-registration-and-vital-statistics-day-with-launch-of-unified-digital-registration-service>). [The Civil Registration Management and Vital Statistics System (CRMVS) was delivered in November 2024 with NADRA funding](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Jan/199818/somalia_launches_ambitious_civil_registration_overhaul.aspx>). [It is a cornerstone of the National Identification System project, and NIRA is tasked with managing it](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2025/Jan/199818/somalia_launches_ambitious_civil_registration_overhaul.aspx>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers.
