# Guinea

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ID Tech Wire reports that ONECI, Guinea's National Office for Civil Registration and Identification, handles both biometric birth certificates and the biometric national ID card, with the birth certificate the foundational document for the ID](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>). [The Office national de l'etat civil et de l'identification (ONECI), one body for civil status and identification, carries out biometric census in civil status centres and signed a convention with the DGE on using civil status data](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [The minister lists the deployment of a modern National Civil Register backed by the attribution of a unique personal identification number (NPI) among government priorities](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre>). [Decree D/2025/052 of April 2025 has the national register of persons run by the body in charge of civil status and identification and fed by the national civil status register](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>).
