# Guinea

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ONECI handles both biometric birth certificates and the biometric national ID card](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>), reported in March 2026. The birth certificate is the base document for the ID. In January 2026 [ONECI signed a convention with the electoral body on using civil status data](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). [Decree D/2025/052 has the register of persons fed by the civil status register](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-052-prg-cnrd-sgg>). [Decree D/2025/053 keys both registers to one personal identification number](<https://guineelex.com/decrets-arretes/decret-d-2025-053-prg-cnrd-sgg>). In August 2026 [the minister listed a civil register backed by that number among government priorities](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/08/decentralisation-et-etat-civil-la-guinee-accelere-sa-transformation-avec-un-taux-denregistrement-des-naissances-passe-a-72-ministre>). The RENEC project, launched on 28 October 2026, [will give each citizen in the civil register a unique number in later phases to 2028](<https://guistat.com/guinee-le-matd-lance-via-loneci-le-projet-renec-pour-moderniser-et-securiser-letat-civil/>).

**Both ways.** The nearest held fact is [ONECI's single office for birth certificates and ID cards](<https://idtechwire.com/guinea-launches-biometric-birth-certificates-in-sanoyah-in-civil-registry-modernization-push>). No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system or deaths retire their numbers.

**Exchange reported.** The nearest held fact is [ONECI's convention on civil status data with the electoral body](<https://mediaguinee.com/2026/01/conakry-la-dge-et-loneci-scellent-une-alliance-pour-un-fichier-electoral-fiable/>). No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two.
