# Liberia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [Liberia's National Identification Registry plans to link national ID with civil registration in one NIDCRVS system](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>). [It rests on a signed $54 million agreement with OeSD International that awaits approval by the legislature](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>). [The concession was signed in April 2026](<https://newrepubliclib.com/concerns-mount-over-nir-us52m-biometric-id-concession-jeopardizing-jobs-data-and-revenue-security/>). [On 13 October 2026 the House sent it to a joint committee](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). [Its executive director said the upgrade includes a component linking the two systems in a life-cycle identity architecture](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id>). [He said on 4 September 2026 that implementation waits on ratification](<https://allafrica.com/stories/202609080549.html>).
