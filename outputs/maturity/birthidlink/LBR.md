# Liberia

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The Liberian Observer reports a proposed NIDCRVS concession with OeSD International GmbH, referred to a House joint committee on 2026-10-13, would integrate national identification with civil registration in one digital platform](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/politics/house-begins-review-of-boakai-s-proposed-national-digital-id-overhaul/article_0c964a7e-9e8c-4c1e-9e05-46bfae096890.html>). [Liberia's National Identification Registry executive director says it plans to link national ID with civil registration and vital statistics in one NID-CRVS system under a signed $54 million agreement with OSD that awaits parliamentary approval](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/liberia-to-link-national-id-crvs-in-planned-infrastructure-upgrade>). [Liberia's National Identification Registry executive director Andrew Peters said the ID upgrade includes a component to link the civil registration and national ID systems in a life cycle identity management architecture](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/liberian-mps-begin-scrutinizing-ppp-concession-deal-for-new-national-id>).
