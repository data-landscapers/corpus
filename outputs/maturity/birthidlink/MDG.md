# Madagascar

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [An expert quoted in one report called Madagascar's digital ID linked with civil registration](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/madagascar-completes-2-6m-digital-id-enrollments-in-two-months>). A retroactive civil registration framework lets undocumented people get foundational documents. [A Studia Inc. project digitised over 10 million civil registration records](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/madagascar-strengthens-trust-architecture-for-national-digital-id>), which the report says sped up ID enrolment. [A law adopted on 28 May 2026 allows mass birth registration within the national biometric enrolment](<https://www.banquemondiale.org/fr/news/feature/2026/07/01/no-longer-invisible-unlocking-legal-identity-for-millions-in-madagascar>), the World Bank says. Adults enrolled can then get a birth certificate without going to court. [By October 2023 a World Bank pilot run by CNECI and UGD had registered over 3,000 newborns](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122123130031407/pdf/P1694131558e640e51a145152595653587b.pdf>) in Antananarivo and Toamasina. Their birth certificates carry a unique identification number.

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest fact: [in June 2026 an expert described the digital ID as linked with civil registration](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/madagascar-completes-2-6m-digital-id-enrollments-in-two-months>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 over 10 million civil registration records had been digitised, speeding digital ID enrolment](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202609/madagascar-strengthens-trust-architecture-for-national-digital-id>).
