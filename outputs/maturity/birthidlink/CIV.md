# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ONECI director-general Ago Christian Kodia said a parent's national identity card now supplies the identity information directly on the new birth-registration terminal](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1786857181>). [ONECI, the Office national de l'etat civil et de l'identification, runs both civil status and identification, and its director general said on 16 September 2025 that its digital civil-status server is linked to the Registre national des personnes physiques](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2025-lidentite-est-le-parapluie-de-tout-individu-dg-oneci/>). [AIP reports that the civil-registry digitisation reform is piloted by ONECI, the national office for civil status and identification, with all civil registration centres equipped with Citiweb software at end 2025](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-les-sous-prefets-du-goh-mobilises-pour-accompagner-la-reforme-de-loneci/>).

**Both ways.** No source held since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1786857181>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1786857181>).
