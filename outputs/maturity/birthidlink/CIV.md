# Cote d'Ivoire

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [ONECI runs both civil status and identification, and its civil-status server is linked to the register of persons](<https://digitalmag.ci/id-day-2025-lidentite-est-le-parapluie-de-tout-individu-dg-oneci/>), its director general said on 16 September 2025. [Since October 2024 the national identification number is printed on civil status certificates](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/1974954/politique/cni-double-identite-enregistrement-des-naissances-nni-les-clarifications-du-dga-de-lonceci-pelagie-gonto>). [A parent's identity card now supplies the parent's details on the new birth-registration terminal](<https://www.oneci.ci/actualites/1786857181>). [On 11 August 2026 ONECI began deploying birth tele-declaration terminals nationally](<https://digitalmag.ci/cote-divoire-digitalisation-de-letat-civil-la-tele-declaration-des-naissances-commence-a-man/>). Health workers record the newborn in the register of persons, which passes the record to the civil-status centre.

**Both ways.** Births give people an identification number. [ONECI issued 1,291,891 of the numbers it attributed in 2025 through the civil registry](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/02/10/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-le-delai-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-ramene-a-moins-de-deux-semaines-perspectives-de-la-structure-pour-2026_194217.html>). Deaths are only planned for. [ONECI's 2026 slides set out a path in which later events, death among them, update the same identity](<https://id4africa.com/2026/files/T4-1-S2-4-Cote-d-Ivoire-FINAL.pdf>).

**Exchange reported.** [ONECI reports that 1,291,891 of the 9,323,415 national identification numbers attributed in 2025 were issued via the civil registry](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2026/02/10/cote-divoire/societe/cote-divoire-le-delai-de-delivrance-de-la-carte-nationale-didentite-ramene-a-moins-de-deux-semaines-perspectives-de-la-structure-pour-2026_194217.html>).
