# Malawi

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [UNDP says the Nzika registration application, which connects civil registration with national identity management, has been piloted in 87 locations across five districts, with expansion to 250 locations planned by 2028](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration>). [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo told Parliament that between April 2024 and February 2025 NRB integrated electronic birth and death registration systems with the national ID system](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [The NRB, per its Principal Secretary, is the only department mandated to register births, deaths, marriages and citizens aged 16+ for national IDs, so one body holds both registers](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>). [EGPAF says the CRVS is fully integrated with Malawi's National ID System](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>).

**Both ways.** [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo said both the electronic birth and the electronic death registration systems were integrated with the national ID system between April 2024 and February 2025](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [Registration officials say an ID number is generated when a baby is born, and the system freezes the record when a deceased person's ID is presented](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>).

**Exchange reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>).
