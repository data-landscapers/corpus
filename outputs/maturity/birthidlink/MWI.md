# Malawi

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [UNDP says the Nzika registration application, which connects civil registration with national identity management, has been piloted in 87 locations across five districts, with expansion to 250 locations planned by 2028](<https://www.undp.org/malawi/news/undp-hands-over-152-printers-national-registration-bureau-expand-civil-registration>). [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo told Parliament that between April 2024 and February 2025 NRB integrated electronic birth and death registration systems with the national ID system](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [The NRB, per its Principal Secretary, is the only department mandated to register births, deaths, marriages and citizens aged 16+ for national IDs, so one body holds both registers](<https://mwnation.com/nrb-under-scrutiny/>). [EGPAF says the CRVS is fully integrated with Malawi's National ID System](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>). [Its community death registration exercise, in its seventh phase in April 2024, registers deaths since 2017 to remove the deceased from the National ID database](<https://mbc.mw/nrb-in-phase-7-of-community-death-registration-exercise/>).

**Both ways.** [NRB Secretary Mphatso Sambo said both the electronic birth and the electronic death registration systems were integrated with the national ID system between April 2024 and February 2025](<https://times.mw/national-registration-bureau-targets-8000-children-in-2025/>). [Registration officials say an ID number is generated when a baby is born, and the system freezes the record when a deceased person's ID is presented](<https://pedaids.org/2024/11/06/the-value-of-a-birth-certificate/>).

**Exchange reported.** [The National Registration Bureau's 2025 slides report 3.5 million births registered with unique identifiers and 179,000 deaths recorded with automated flagging in the NRIS, with electronic registration in 142 health facilities](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/PS1-S1-5-Malawi-final.pdf>).
