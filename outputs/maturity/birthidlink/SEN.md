# Senegal

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [The data protection commission (CDP) says it is working with the Direction de l'automatisation des fichiers (DAF) on integrating civil status and identity, with two options (one integrated system or interoperability) reported to the authorities for decision](<https://aps.sn/identite-numerique-la-cdp-mise-sur-linteroperabilite-entre-letat-civil-et-les-systemes-publics/>). [RTS reports that ANEC (civil status agency) and DAF (identity file directorate) signed an interoperability protocol to exchange civil-status and biometric identity data](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/vers-une-identite-numerique-fiable-le-senegal-franchit-un-cap-dans-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>). [ANEC's May 2025 slides show its NEKKAL civil status system exchanging with the identity directorate, health, the statistics agency and the electoral file](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-1-SENEGAL_FINAL.pdf>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/identite-numerique-la-cdp-mise-sur-linteroperabilite-entre-letat-civil-et-les-systemes-publics/>).
