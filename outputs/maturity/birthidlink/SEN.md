# Senegal

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In July 2025 the civil status agency ANEC and the identity directorate DAF signed a protocol to exchange civil status and biometric identity data](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/vers-une-identite-numerique-fiable-le-senegal-franchit-un-cap-dans-la-digitalisation-de-letat-civil>). [ANEC's May 2025 slides show its NEKKAL system exchanging with the identity directorate, health, statistics and the electoral file](<https://www.id4africa.com/2025/files/T2-P1-S2-1-SENEGAL_FINAL.pdf>). [In January 2026 a PINS X-Road pilot connected the civil status databases and the identity card system with the RNU and CMU](<https://pins.sn/actualites/2026-01/plateforme-interministerielle-et-interoperable-dechange-de-donnees-du-senegal>). [In September 2026 the data protection commission said it was working with DAF on joining civil status and identity](<https://aps.sn/identite-numerique-la-cdp-mise-sur-linteroperabilite-entre-letat-civil-et-les-systemes-publics/>). [It put two options to the authorities: one integrated system, or interoperability](<https://aps.sn/identite-numerique-la-cdp-mise-sur-linteroperabilite-entre-letat-civil-et-les-systemes-publics/>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://aps.sn/identite-numerique-la-cdp-mise-sur-linteroperabilite-entre-letat-civil-et-les-systemes-publics/>).
