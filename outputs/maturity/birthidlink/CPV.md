# Cape Verde

## Interoperability of birth registration and digital id

**Civil register linked to ID.** [In May 2026 Biometric Update reported that Cabo Verde linked its civil registration and national identity systems](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). [The link runs through the Autentika interoperability platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>). [At the 2026 ID4Africa assembly, officials cited Cabo Verde as an example of CRVS-ID integration](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/can-africas-new-crvs-decade-close-the-death-registration-gap>).

**Both ways.** No source found since October 2023 says births enrol people in the ID system and deaths retire their numbers. The nearest held fact: [officials said in 2026 that death registration is high and linked to the integrated systems](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/can-africas-new-crvs-decade-close-the-death-registration-gap>).

**Exchange reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for records exchanged between the two. The nearest held fact: [the IMIJ executive administrator presented the Autentika platform](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202605/cabo-verdes-crvs-id-reforms-drive-near-universal-birth-registration>).
