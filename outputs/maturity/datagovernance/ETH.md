# Ethiopia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Ethiopia has a national data governance strategy and a law in draft. [Work on the strategy began under the Office of the Prime Minister, with a technical committee whose draft framework was expanded into a full draft strategy](<https://dial.global/lessons-from-ethiopias-data-strategy-approach/>), and [in March 2026 it was described as forthcoming](<https://dial.global/ethiopias-approach-to-use-cases/>). [On 28 September 2026 the Council of Ministers referred the National Data Governance Draft Proclamation to the House of Peoples' Representatives](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9629721>). No source found says the strategy has been launched or the bill enacted.

**Body named and rules issued.** [A strategy launch and validation workshop were listed among plans for 2026](<https://dial.global/work/strengthening-ethiopias-national-data-governance-ecosystem/>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
