# Libya

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Libya has a national guide for government data governance in use. [The General Information Authority reported on 23 April 2026 that it applies the guide](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>). [The guide is its reference framework for how government bodies manage data, keep it accurate and share it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>). [The Authority pressed the Libyan Zakat Fund to bring its platforms into line with the guide](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>). No source found gives the date the guide was issued, or its text.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The authority that applies the guide is the one responsible for government data in general](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>). No source found names a council, office, committee or unit set up to carry the guide out. No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
