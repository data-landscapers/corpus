# Libya

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Libya has a national guide for government data governance in use. [The General Information Authority reported on 23 April 2026 that it applies the guide as the reference framework for how government bodies manage data, keep it accurate and share it, and pressed the Libyan Zakat Fund to bring its platforms into line with it](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>). No source held gives the date the guide was issued, or its text.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The authority that applies the guide is the one responsible for government data in general](<https://www.gia.gov.ly/en/2026/04/23/the-general-information-authority-strengthens-data-governance-at-the-libyan-zakat-fund-according-to-the-national-guide-for-government-data-governance/>); no source held names a council, office, committee or unit set up to carry the guide out. No source held from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
