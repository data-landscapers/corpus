# Nigeria

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** [President Buhari unveiled and launched the National Data Policy at the State House, Abuja, on 2 February 2023](<https://fmino.gov.ng/at-the-launch-of-two-policies-on-digital-economy-president-buhari-says-diversification-has-been-most-successful-in-the-sector/>). It was one of two policies developed by the Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy, and the Minister said the Federal Executive Council had approved it earlier. [On 19 July 2023 the Permanent Secretary of the Federal Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy said the government had officially launched the National Data Strategy, built on seven pillars](<https://leadership.ng/nitda-unveils-national-data-strategy-to-drive-nigerias-digital-economy/>). [NITDA's draft National Data Strategy to 2030, dated 2022, carries a directive from the Minister of Communications and Digital Economy](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Final-Draft-National-Data-Strategy.pdf>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [On 19 July 2023 the Director-General of the National Information Technology Development Agency inaugurated a Technical Working Group for implementing the National Data Strategy, to recommend how regulatory agencies implement it](<https://leadership.ng/nitda-unveils-national-data-strategy-to-drive-nigerias-digital-economy/>). [The draft strategy's timetable lists a governance structure and a strategy implementation committee still to be established from November and December 2022](<https://nitda.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Final-Draft-National-Data-Strategy.pdf>). No source found names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under the policy.
