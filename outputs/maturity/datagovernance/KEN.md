# Kenya

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Kenya has a national data governance policy in draft. [Drafting began at a workshop in September 2025, with support from the EU and GIZ](<https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/national-data-governance-policy-formulation-kicks-off/>). [The ICT ministry held a validation workshop for the public sector on 5 February 2026, with the draft then to be finalised, approved by cabinet and launched](<https://www.ict.go.ke/government-moves-strengthen-kenyas-data-governance-framework>). [A final draft is dated 25 May 2026](<https://rosemutiso.substack.com/p/kenya-wants-to-sell-your-data-but>), and [the ministry invited public comment on it until 5 June 2026](<https://posts.kictanet.or.ke/have-your-say-kenyas-draft-national-data-governance-policy-comments-due-5-june-2026/>). [On 9 July 2026 the ICT Principal Secretary discussed the draft with United States embassy officials and the Data Protection Commissioner](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/10/kenya-and-u-s-discuss-draft-data-governance-policy-to-strengthen-digital-economy/>). [In August 2026 it was still described as being developed](<https://cipit.strathmore.edu/the-standing-cabinet-committee-on-artificial-intelligence-implications-for-kenyas-evolving-ai-ecosystem/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The draft proposes a National Data Governance and Emerging Technologies Council and a Data Governance Office](<https://nation.africa/kenya/business/state-plans-ecitizen-data-sale-to-boost-revenue--5488496>). Neither is yet set up. It also proposes [a state marketplace for data](<https://itweb.africa/article/kenya-proposes-marketplace-for-state-data/RgeVDMPRldKvKJN3>). The policy is not adopted, so there is nothing yet for a body to carry out. No source found names a body set up to carry it out. None names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
