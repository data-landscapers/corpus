# Somalia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A GIZ African Union post of 13 March 2026 says Somalia validated a National Data Policy Framework under the AU-GIZ Data Governance in Africa Initiative](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7438141176852996097>). The framework covers collection, management, sharing, classification, retention and data sovereignty in Somalia. The post gives validation only, with no date for it, and no source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The 13 March 2026 GIZ African Union post on the National Data Policy Framework, issued under the AU-GIZ Data Governance in Africa Initiative, names no body set up to carry the framework out and no rule issued under it](<https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7438141176852996097>). The post names the AU-GIZ Data Governance in Africa Initiative as the programme under which the framework was validated, and gives no implementing agency, committee or regulation.
