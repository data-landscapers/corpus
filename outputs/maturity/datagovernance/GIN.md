# Guinea

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Guinea has a national data governance strategy being written. [The government launched the drafting on 19 June 2026, led by the National Directorate of Information Technology and the Digital Economy](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2606-56858-guinea-begins-work-on-national-data-governance-strategy>). [The minister opened the stakeholder workshop that day](<https://mceni.gov.gn/la-guinee-engage-lelaboration-de-sa-strategie-nationale-de-gouvernance-des-donnees/>), [after an assessment mission in Conakry from 16 to 19 June that consulted 27 institutions](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/guinea-lays-the-foundations-for-a-trusted-and-inclusive-national-data-governance-framework>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The directorate leading the drafting is the body writing the strategy, not one named to carry it out](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news/2606-56858-guinea-begins-work-on-national-data-governance-strategy>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
