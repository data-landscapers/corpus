# Guinea-Bissau

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Guinea-Bissau has a national data governance and protection policy approved by cabinet. [The Council of Ministers, chaired by the Prime Minister, approved the draft decree on the policy, reported by the state news agency on 10 July 2025](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/>). The report gives no date for the meeting other than the day of the week, and no text of the policy has been found.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The cabinet report names the policy and nothing about how it is to be carried out](<https://ang.gw/governo-aprova-projeto-de-regulamento-sobre-a-interoperabilidade-de-sistemas-e-plataformas-digitais/>). No source found names a council, office, agency, committee, chief data officer or ministry unit set up or named to carry it out. No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
