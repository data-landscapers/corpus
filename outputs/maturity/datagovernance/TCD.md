# Chad

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Chad's national data governance strategy is a validated draft. [The Ministry of Telecommunications, with the UN Economic Commission for Africa and GIZ, held consultations in N'Djamena from 13 May 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/fr/node/2449>). [A workshop opened on 18 May to prepare the drafting](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/transition-numerique-le-tchad/>). [A national workshop of 50 participants reviewed and validated the strategy on 7 October 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/chad-validates-national-data-governance-strategy%2C-marking-a-key-milestone-in-its-digital>). [Opening it, the minister said working-group amendments go to plenary before the strategy is adopted](<https://mten.gouv.td/discours-de-haliki-choua-mahamat-sur-la-gouvernance-des-donnees/>). [It has seven pillars and a roadmap to 2030](<https://camerpressagency.com/transformation-numerique-le-tchad-batit-sa-souverainete-de-la-donnee/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The ministry is developing the strategy with the commission, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-chad-developing-national-data-governance-strategy-advance-au-data-policy-framework>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the government has adopted or launched the strategy.
