# Chad

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Chad has a national data governance strategy being drafted. [The Ministry of Telecommunications, with the UN Economic Commission for Africa and GIZ, held consultations in N'Djamena from 13 May 2026](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/fr/node/2449>), and [a workshop opened on 18 May to prepare the drafting](<https://www.lepaystchad.com/transition-numerique-le-tchad/>). [The first phase closed with a stakeholder workshop on 21 May 2026 on the findings of the country assessment](<https://www.uneca.org/fr/node/11605>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The ministry is developing the strategy with the commission, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-chad-developing-national-data-governance-strategy-advance-au-data-policy-framework>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
