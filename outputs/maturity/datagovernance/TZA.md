# Tanzania

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Tanzania has an electronic data governance strategy in draft. [Its preparation was expected to be complete by January 2026](<https://www.mwananchi.co.tz/mw/habari/kitaifa/tanzania-yajipanga-ukinara-ulinzi-wa-data-uchumi-wa-kidijitali-afrika-5173946>). [A national validation workshop was held in Dodoma on 12 March 2026, where the ministry's director reaffirmed the commitment to take it to formal adoption](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/the-blueprint-for-a-digital-sovereign-nation-tanzania-validates-its-landmark-electronic-data>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The March 2026 workshop validated the draft, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/the-blueprint-for-a-digital-sovereign-nation-tanzania-validates-its-landmark-electronic-data>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
