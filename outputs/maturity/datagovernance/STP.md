# Sao Tome and Principe

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** São Tomé and Príncipe has a data strategy and data governance policy being written. [The national innovation institute published a first version of the design of the 2025 to 2030 data strategy and data governance policy in July 2025, prepared by a consultant, which says no data governance policy yet exists and lists its approval as a future step](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=27>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The design document is the consultant's, and names no body yet given the job of carrying the policy out](<https://inic.gov.st/viewdoc.php?id=27>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
