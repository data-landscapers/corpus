# Senegal

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** [A report of July 2026 says Senegal has adopted its National Data Strategy](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). The report, on a workshop of the data protection commission, gives no adoption date. [The ministry was also writing a national blueprint on creating value from data, with a workshop on 1 and 2 July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-elevates-data-and-ai-as-core-pillars-of-public-governance-reform>). [The blueprint was validated on 21 and 22 September 2026, with its roadmap and monitoring still to be refined](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/23/senegal-validates-national-blueprint-data-valorisation/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The data protection commission cites the strategy as a reason to update the data protection law](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). The source does not say that carrying out the strategy is the commission's job. No source found names a body set up to carry out the strategy. No source found from the last three years names a rule issued under it.
