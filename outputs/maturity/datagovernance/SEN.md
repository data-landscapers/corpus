# Senegal

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Senegal has a National Data Strategy that a source describes as adopted. [A report of July 2026 on the data protection commission's workshop says Senegal has adopted the strategy, and gives no date](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). [The ministry was also developing a national blueprint on creating value from data, with a workshop on 1 and 2 July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-elevates-data-and-ai-as-core-pillars-of-public-governance-reform>), [validated on 21 and 22 September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/23/senegal-validates-national-blueprint-data-valorisation/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The data protection commission cites the strategy as a reason to update the data protection law; the source does not say carrying out the strategy is its job](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). No source found names a council, office, agency, committee, chief data officer or ministry unit set up or named to carry it out. No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
