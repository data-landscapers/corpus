# Senegal

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Senegal's National Data Strategy was adopted by October 2024, when [Le Tech Observateur listed it among the authorities' recent strategies](<https://letechobservateur.sn/mise-a-niveau-du-projet-de-loi-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-une-avancee-majeure-pour-le-cadre-legal-senegalais/>). [A report of July 2026 also says the strategy is adopted](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). [The ministry was also writing a national blueprint on creating value from data, with a workshop on 1 and 2 July 2026](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-elevates-data-and-ai-as-core-pillars-of-public-governance-reform>). [The blueprint was validated on 21 and 22 September 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/23/senegal-validates-national-blueprint-data-valorisation/>), with its roadmap and monitoring still to be refined.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The data protection commission cites the strategy as a reason to update the data protection law](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). Carrying out the strategy is not said to be the commission's job. No body set up to carry out the strategy has been found. Nor has a rule issued under it in the last three years.

***Also on file***. [Stratégie sur l'intelligence artificielle (SNIA)](<https://lequotidien.sn/mbour-gouvernance-des-donnees-et-intelligence-artificielle-vers-un-nouveau-dispositif-normatif/>). [New Deal Technologique](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/senegal-elevates-data-and-ai-as-core-pillars-of-public-governance-reform>).

***Not found***. No body set up to carry out the strategy has been found. The exact date it was adopted is not given.
