# Congo

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Congo has a national data governance strategy being drafted. [The UN Economic Commission for Africa supported consultations in Brazzaville from 2 to 9 June 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/5882/eca-supports-republic-of-the-congo-in-developing-national-data-governance-strategy>), [after a self-assessment found the country had no integrated strategy](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/eca-supporting-republic-congo-shaping-national-data-governance-strategy-digital-sovereignty-and>). [A workshop of 60 participants on 8 and 9 June 2026 followed an assessment that consulted 21 institutions](<https://www.uneca.org/fr/stories/la-r%C3%A9publique-du-congo-veut-%C3%A9tablir-les-fondements-d%E2%80%99un-%C3%A9cosyst%C3%A8me-de-donn%C3%A9es-fiable>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The ministry's chief of staff reaffirmed the government's commitment to put a strategy in place](<https://www.uneca.org/fr/stories/la-r%C3%A9publique-du-congo-veut-%C3%A9tablir-les-fondements-d%E2%80%99un-%C3%A9cosyst%C3%A8me-de-donn%C3%A9es-fiable>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
