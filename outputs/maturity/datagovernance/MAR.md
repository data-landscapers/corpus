# Morocco

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Morocco's Open Government Partnership commitment page, covering August 2018 to August 2020, [lists setting up a national data policy, called the politique nationale de la donnée, with drafting of the public-data strategy and regulation from May to October 2019 shown as 90% in progress by March 2020](<https://www.gouvernement-ouvert.ma/engagements.php?engagement=4&lang=fr>). [An article by Kassimi and Salam, published in December 2024, states that the government has not formalised a specific public data governance policy and that there is no unified national data governance strategy](<https://ijafame.org/index.php/ijafame/article/download/1680/1679/3271>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The same December 2024 article states that Morocco has no national data committee and no regional data committees, and that few administrations have a dedicated data governance unit](<https://ijafame.org/index.php/ijafame/article/download/1680/1679/3271>). No source found names a rule issued under a national data policy.
