# Mozambique

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Mozambique has a national data governance policy and strategy in draft. [A workplan of June 2025 set the drafting for July to December 2025](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TICID/data-governance-framework-mozambique/en_project_inception_report_mozambique_data_governance_framework.pdf>). [A workshop in Maputo on 5 December 2025 validated the proposed policy, strategy and action plan](<https://techafricanews.com/2025/12/05/mozambique-prioritizes-data-governance-as-cornerstone-of-digital-sovereignty/>). [The communications minister said on 22 July 2026 that they were still in preparation and not yet approved](<https://news.isocmedias.co.mz/2026/07/23/governo-prepara-lei-de-protecao-de-dados-pessoais/>), and [the information technology institute said the same in August 2026](<https://itweb.africa/article/mozambique-steps-up-ai-drive/nWJadvbND3dvbjO1>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The draft is written as a Council of Ministers resolution with its number and approval date left blank](<https://intic.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Mozambique-Zero-Draft-2-Data-Governance-Policy-1.pdf>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
