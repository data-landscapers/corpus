# South Africa

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** South Africa has a National Policy on Data and Cloud. [The Department of Communications and Digital Technologies issued the final policy in the Government Gazette of 31 May 2024, setting government policy on the collection, storage, use and sharing of data across national and provincial government, organs of state and the private sector](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202406/50741gen2533.pdf>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The policy text is held in part, and what is held names no council, office or unit to carry the policy out](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202406/50741gen2533.pdf>); it gives the State Information Technology Agency the buying of data infrastructure and cloud services for government, under the agency's existing mandate. No source held from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under the policy. Nothing on the policy later than its gazetting is held.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a body set up or named to carry out the policy: a council, office, agency, committee, chief data officer or ministry unit.
