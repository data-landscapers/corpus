# South Africa

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** South Africa has a National Policy on Data and Cloud. [The Department of Communications and Digital Technologies issued the final policy in the Government Gazette of 31 May 2024, setting government policy on the collection, storage, use and sharing of data across national and provincial government, organs of state and the private sector](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202406/50741gen2533.pdf>). [National Treasury's reply of 10 September 2024 refers to it as the National Policy on Data and Cloud (2024) and says Treasury considered its implications and plans to convert its 2016 IFMS licences to cloud-based](<https://www.treasury.gov.za/publications/other/MinAnsw/2024/PQ%20792_244_%20Bateman__NW1017E%20_FINAL.pdf>).

**Body named and rules issued.** On 2 April 2025 [the Minister for Public Service and Administration signed a directive on data governance in the public service, in effect on signature, with the Public Service Data Governance Framework annexed](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/dpsa2g/documents/km/2025/Determination%20and%20Directive%20on%20the%20Implementation%20of%20Data%20Governance%20in%20the%20Public%20Service.pdf>). It applies to national and provincial departments and [requires each head of department to set up a data governance committee and a data governance office and to appoint a chief data officer](<https://www.dpsa.gov.za/dpsa2g/documents/km/2025/Determination%20and%20Directive%20on%20the%20Implementation%20of%20Data%20Governance%20in%20the%20Public%20Service.pdf>), a post that may be filled within 24 months.
