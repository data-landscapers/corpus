# Liberia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Liberia has a national data governance policy in draft. [The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications, with the African Union Commission, held a consultation in Monrovia on 13 and 14 November 2025 to prepare it](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberia-moves-to-strengthen-data-governance-under-au-led-initiative/>). [The policy text of March 2026 is marked draft and says it will be published upon approval](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The March 2026 text records no approval, and so no body has yet been given the job of carrying it out](<https://mopt.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2026-Liberia-Data-Governance-Policy-REVISED-CIPESA-1.pdf>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
