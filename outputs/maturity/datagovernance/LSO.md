# Lesotho

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Lesotho has a Data Management Policy for the government, signed in 2026. [A second draft was produced with UNDP and Norway's registers agency](<https://www.gov.ls/download/draft-data-management-policy_1-0/>), and [a validation workshop on 13 October 2025 aligned it with the African Union's Data Policy Framework](<https://www.gov.ls/government/follow-up-on-lesotho-data-governance-capacity-building-and-engagement-workshop/>). [The Minister of Information, Communications, Science, Technology and Innovation signed the policy at a ceremony in Maseru on 11 June 2026](<https://lestimes.com/?p=90715>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [A separate draft blueprint on creating value from data went to a validation workshop in Maseru in September 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/the-government-of-lesotho-tabled-a-national-data-value-creation-blueprint-draft-to-position-lesotho-competitively-on-the-international-digital-landscape/>).
