# Lesotho

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Lesotho has a Data Management Policy for the government, signed in 2026. [A second draft was produced with UNDP and Norway's registers agency](<https://www.gov.ls/download/draft-data-management-policy_1-0/>). [Stakeholders reviewed the 2025 draft at a workshop on 28-30 July 2025](<https://cipesa.org/2025/10/validation-workshop-for-the-recommendations-made-to-lesothos-draft-data-management-policy-2025/>). [A validation workshop on 13 October 2025 aligned it with the African Union's Data Policy Framework](<https://www.gov.ls/government/follow-up-on-lesotho-data-governance-capacity-building-and-engagement-workshop/>). [The ICT minister signed the policy in Maseru on 11 June 2026](<https://lestimes.com/?p=90715>). [In November 2026 he told an ITU conference that government had approved it](<https://www.gov.ls/development/lesotho-calls-for-affordable-international-connectivity-for-landlocked-countries-to-ensure-they-are-not-left-behind-in-the-digital-transformation-agenda/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [A separate draft blueprint on creating value from data went to a validation workshop in Maseru in September 2026](<https://www.gov.ls/ict/the-government-of-lesotho-tabled-a-national-data-value-creation-blueprint-draft-to-position-lesotho-competitively-on-the-international-digital-landscape/>). No source found names a body set up to carry out the policy. None from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
