# Cote d'Ivoire

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Côte d'Ivoire has a national data governance strategy to 2030. [It was presented on 13 March 2025, after eight months of preparation, and handed to the Prime Minister for adoption](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>), and [the ministry presented it to business leaders on 14 May 2025](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2633904/economie/strategies-nationales-de-lia-et-de-la-gouvernance-des-donnees-les-deux-outils-presentes-au-secteur-prive>). [The strategy's own text carries the Prime Minister's foreword on its official launch by the government, after a process begun in April 2024 with more than 200 experts](<https://www.telecom.gouv.ci/new/uploads/publications/174196707541.pdf>). The text gives no launch date.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The March 2025 presentation named a national committee for AI and data governance as a first action still to be created](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cote-divoire-unveils-ai-and-data-governance-strategy>). No source found names a council, office, agency or committee set up to carry out the strategy. Nor does any name a chief data officer or ministry unit for it. No source found from the last three years names a rule issued under it.
