# Comoros

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** The Comoros has a national data governance strategy for 2027 to 2031 in draft. [Government, private-sector and civil-society representatives met in Moroni on 24 and 25 August 2026 to review and validate it, with its implementation plan](<https://www.nepad.org/news/lauda-nepad-sassocie-lunion-des-comores-pour-impulser-la-transformation-numerique>). [It was prepared with the African Union, the European Union and GIZ](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/research-ict-africa_digitaltransformation-nationaldatastrategy-activity-7498401504303845376-wyiI>). No source held says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The August 2026 workshop examined the implementation plan, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://www.nepad.org/news/lauda-nepad-sassocie-lunion-des-comores-pour-impulser-la-transformation-numerique>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
