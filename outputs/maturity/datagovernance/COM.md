# Comoros

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** The Comoros has a national data governance strategy for 2027 to 2031 in draft. [Government, private-sector and civil-society representatives met in Moroni on 24 and 25 August 2026 to review and validate it, with its implementation plan](<https://www.nepad.org/news/lauda-nepad-sassocie-lunion-des-comores-pour-impulser-la-transformation-numerique>). [It was prepared with the African Union, the European Union and GIZ](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/research-ict-africa_digitaltransformation-nationaldatastrategy-activity-7498401504303845376-wyiI>). [On 21 October 2026 the telecommunications minister presented the strategy to the Council of Ministers](<https://alwatwan.net/politique/conseil-des-ministres-i-approbation-d%E2%80%99un-projet-d%E2%80%99%C3%A9lectrification-compl%C3%A8te-de-la-r%C3%A9gion-de-mledjele-%C3%A0-mwali.html>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The August 2026 workshop examined the implementation plan, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://www.nepad.org/news/lauda-nepad-sassocie-lunion-des-comores-pour-impulser-la-transformation-numerique>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says the Council of Ministers approved the strategy it was given in October 2026, or that any national data policy is adopted and still running.
