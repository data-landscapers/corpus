# Cape Verde

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Cabo Verde has a data strategy being written. [The National Statistics Council held a conference in Praia on 24 and 25 April 2025 on the country's first national data strategy](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/mf/-/conselho-nacional-de-estat%C3%ADstica-promove-confer%C3%AAncia-para-discutir-a-estrat%C3%A9gia-nacional-de-dados>). [On 28 April 2026 senior officials, the Secretary of State for the Digital Economy, consultants and European Union representatives held a kick-off meeting to develop a Data Strategy for Public Administration](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/cape-verde-launches-national-data-and-ai-strategies-to-modernize-public-administration/>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The April 2026 meeting launched the drafting, and the source names no body to carry the strategy out](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/05/04/cape-verde-launches-national-data-and-ai-strategies-to-modernize-public-administration/>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
