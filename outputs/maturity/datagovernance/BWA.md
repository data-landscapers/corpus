# Botswana

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Botswana has a national data policy in draft. [The Ministry of Communications and Innovation held five days of training and stakeholder engagement from 7 to 11 September 2026 on the national data strategy, with African Union Commission and European Union support](<https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement>). [On 25 November 2026 the assistant minister said the draft National Data Policy was complete](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/94332>). [A workshop in Gaborone was validating it with the National Data Strategy 2027-2032, ahead of formal approval](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/94332>). No source found says the government has approved it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The November 2026 report names no body to carry the policy out](<https://dailynews.gov.bw/news-detail/94332>). The policy is not yet adopted. There is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out. No source found names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
