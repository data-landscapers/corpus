# Botswana

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Botswana has a national data strategy being drafted. [The Ministry of Communications and Innovation held five days of training and stakeholder engagement from 7 to 11 September 2026 to support its development, with African Union Commission and European Union support](<https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement>). The same source mentions an earlier national data policy and does not say it was adopted. No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The September 2026 engagement was for drafting, and the source names no body to carry the strategy out](<https://dig.watch/updates/botswana-national-data-strategy-engagement>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
