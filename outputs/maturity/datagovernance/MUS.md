# Mauritius

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Mauritius has a National Data Strategy for 2025 to 2029. [The Ministry of Information Technology, Communication and Innovation, with the Data Protection Office, officially launched it at Ebene on 28 January 2026, setting the country's long-term aims for governing and using data](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553938>). [The minister spoke on 16 July 2026 of the government carrying out a national data governance strategy](<https://mitci.govmu.org/mitci/mauritius-to-join-america-india-connect-subsea-cable-programme-announces-pm-ramgoolam/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The Data Protection Office took part in the launch; the source does not say that carrying out the strategy is its job](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553938>). No source found names a council, office, agency, committee, chief data officer or ministry unit set up or named to carry it out. No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
