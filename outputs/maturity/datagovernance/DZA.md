# Algeria

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Algeria has a national data governance framework in force. [Presidential Decree 25-320 of 30 December 2025 set it up for public institutions, with a data classification reference, a data cataloguing reference and a national interoperability system](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). [The Prime Minister presided over its official launch on 9 February 2026](<https://www.maghrebinfo.dz/2026/02/09/un-socle-fondamental-pour-ledification-dun-etat-moderne-apte-a-anticiper-les-defis-futurs/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [At the launch the High Commissioner for Digitalisation installed a national commission of all ministries and national bodies to follow the framework's implementation](<https://www.maghrebinfo.dz/2026/02/09/un-socle-fondamental-pour-ledification-dun-etat-moderne-apte-a-anticiper-les-defis-futurs/>). [The decree names the High Commission for Digitalisation to draw up and update the two references and run the interoperability system](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). In February 2026 the High Commission's director of digital governance said [the classification reference sets five levels, from public to top secret](<https://almostathmir.dz/%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7/>). [Every ministry had been written to to start classifying its data, and workshops had explained the classification and indexing references](<https://almostathmir.dz/%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7/>).
