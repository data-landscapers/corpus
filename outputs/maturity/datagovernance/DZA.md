# Algeria

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Algeria has a national data governance framework in force. [Presidential Decree 25-320 of 30 December 2025 set it up for public institutions, with a data classification reference, a data cataloguing reference and a national interoperability system](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). [The Prime Minister presided over its official launch on 9 February 2026](<https://www.maghrebinfo.dz/2026/02/09/un-socle-fondamental-pour-ledification-dun-etat-moderne-apte-a-anticiper-les-defis-futurs/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [At the launch the High Commissioner for Digitalisation installed a national commission, made up of representatives of all ministries and national bodies, to follow the framework's implementation](<https://www.maghrebinfo.dz/2026/02/09/un-socle-fondamental-pour-ledification-dun-etat-moderne-apte-a-anticiper-les-defis-futurs/>). [The decree names the High Commission for Digitalisation to draw up and update the two references and run the interoperability system, and the personal data protection authority to check compliance](<https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/JO-FRANCAIS/2025/F2025087.pdf>). No source found says the classification or cataloguing reference has been issued, and none from the last three years names another law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under the framework.
