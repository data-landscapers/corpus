# DR Congo

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** DR Congo has a national data governance strategy in draft. [The UN Economic Commission for Africa reports that the six-pillar strategy was validated at a ceremony in Kinshasa on 13 November 2025 and handed over to the government, with amendments still being taken in](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/eca-officially-hands-over-the-national-data-governance-strategy-to-the-government-of-the-drc>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The handover of November 2025 is the last step held, and the source names no body set up to carry the strategy out](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/eca-officially-hands-over-the-national-data-governance-strategy-to-the-government-of-the-drc>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
