# Sierra Leone

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Sierra Leone has a National Data Strategy, its first. [The Ministry of Information and Communications published it in January 2023, and it carries no end date](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Data-Strategy_2023_v1.pdf>). [A five-month engagement in 2025 updated it to align with the African Union's Data Policy Framework](<https://dial.global/work/strengthening-national-data-strategy-sierra-leone>). [In July 2025 the ministry held a focus group on a draft National Data Governance Strategy](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-communication-data-strategy/>), with government, civil society, academics and business. [That strategy was validated in Freetown on 7-8 August 2025, still to be finalised and adopted](<https://www.kictanet.or.ke/sierra-leone-leads-bringing-the-au-data-policy-framework-to-life/>). [In August 2026 the ministry described training its technical team as part of carrying the strategy out](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6855/sierra-leone-trains-government-technical-team-on-ai-and-data-protection-by-design>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [In September 2026 the ministry said it is developing a data governance framework](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/communication-strengthens-partnership/>). It is to set how government data is classified, shared and used, and is not yet issued. No source found names a body set up to carry out the strategy. None from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
