# Sierra Leone

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Sierra Leone has a National Data Strategy, its first. [The Ministry of Information and Communications published it in January 2023, with the minister's foreword, and it carries no end date](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Data-Strategy_2023_v1.pdf>). [A five-month engagement in 2025 updated it to align with the African Union's Data Policy Framework](<https://dial.global/work/strengthening-national-data-strategy-sierra-leone>). [In August 2026 the ministry described training its technical team as part of carrying the strategy out](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6855/sierra-leone-trains-government-technical-team-on-ai-and-data-protection-by-design>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The ministry said in September 2026 that it is developing a data governance framework on how government data is classified, shared and used](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/communication-strengthens-partnership/>); it is not yet issued. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee, chief data officer or ministry unit set up or named to carry it out. No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. [National Data Protection Policy](<https://moice.gov.sl/cabinet-approves-first-ever-data-protection-policy-paving-way-for-new-data-law/>); [National Digital Development Council](<https://mocti.gov.sl/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Sierra-Leone-National-Data-Strategy_2023_v1.pdf>).
