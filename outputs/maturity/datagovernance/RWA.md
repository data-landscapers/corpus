# Rwanda

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Rwanda has a National Data Sharing Policy for the government. [Cabinet approved it on 26 May 2025; the ICT ministry developed it with the Rwanda Information Society Authority, for the exchange of data across public institutions](<https://www.risa.gov.rw/news-detail/national-data-sharing-policy-approved>). [It is supported by seven annexes](<https://iapp.org/news/a/rwanda-charts-a-new-course-for-government-data-sharing>) and [covers data sharing among government agencies](<https://cenfri.org/articles/introduction-to-the-rwanda-national-data-sharing-policy-2/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [A Data Governance Unit has been set up at the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda as the operational arm of the policy, with staff in place and projects started, reported in May 2026](<https://cenfri.org/articles/what-will-determine-the-success-of-rwandas-data-governance-push/>). No source found from the last three years names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under the policy; the seven annexes came with the policy itself. No source found says what the unit has issued.
