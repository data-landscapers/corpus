# Zambia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Zambia has a national data policy being drafted. [Research ICT Africa says it supported the drafting of the Zambian National Data Policy under its DataCipation project with GIZ and AUDA-NEPAD, which helps countries put the African Union Data Policy Framework into national policy](<https://researchictafrica.net/project/datacipation-shaping-harmonised-and-inclusive-digital-and-data-policies-in-africa/>). The project page, from September 2026, gives no date for a finished draft. No source found says the government has validated, approved or launched the policy.

**Body named and rules issued.** [Research ICT Africa's project page names no body set up to carry out the Zambian policy and no rule issued under it](<https://researchictafrica.net/project/datacipation-shaping-harmonised-and-inclusive-digital-and-data-policies-in-africa/>). Because the policy is still being drafted, there is nothing yet for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency or ministry unit given the task, or a regulation, standard or guideline issued under the policy.

***Also on file***. [National Electronic Government Plan 2023-2026, which budgets for developing a data governance framework](<http://www.szi.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Final-National_e-Government_Plan_-2023-Final-17.08.2023.pdf>); [ZNSDI geoportal](<https://spaceinafrica.com/2026/06/18/zambia-launches-national-spatial-data-infrastructure-policy-and-geoportal-built-on-open-source-software/>).

***Not found***. Nothing found names a national data policy, or says there is none.
