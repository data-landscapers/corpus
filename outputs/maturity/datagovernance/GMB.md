# Gambia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** The Gambia has a national data policy in draft. [The Ministry of Communications and Digital Economy's draft National Data Policy 2025 is held](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/National-Data-Policy-2025-The-Gambia-draft.docx>). [On 2 and 3 July 2025 stakeholders from government, the private sector, civil society and academia endorsed it at a national validation workshop, and the European Union delegation said the finalised policy would be submitted for government approval](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/gambia/gambia-moves-closer-stronger-data-governance-validating-its-national-data-policy-validated_en>). [A contractor, 2M Corp, says its 2024 work for the Ministry of Public Service, financed by the World Bank, produced a Data Governance Policy](<https://2m-corp.com/projects/government-data-assessment>). No source found says the government has approved or launched either.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The draft is described as a reference instrument still to be approved](<https://mocde.gov.gm/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/National-Data-Policy-2025-The-Gambia-draft.docx>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
