# Eswatini

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Eswatini has a national data governance policy for 2027 to 2031 in draft. [Smart Africa sought a firm to write it in July 2025](<https://smartafrica.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFP-Eswatini-Data-Policy.pdf>). [A first stakeholders' workshop opened in Mbabane on 29 April 2026, with European Union support](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/eswatini/eu-proud-support-eswatini-developing-national-data-policy_en>), where [the ICT minister said a public comment period is planned](<https://swazi24.com/44934/news/maziya-opens-push-for-national-data-policy/>); and [the draft was reviewed and validated in July 2026](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6470/eswatini-validates-national-data-governance-policy>). [The ICT minister told the Senate in August 2026 that it is expected to be completed before the end of 2026](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/data-governance-policy-to-be-completed-by-year-end-minister-savannah/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The draft has nine pillars and was presented at the validation by the ministry's under secretary](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/mchumanisi-system-to-give-emaswati-one-stop-access-to-government-services/>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. [Mchumanisi (proposed National Data Exchange Platform)](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/mchumanisi-system-to-give-emaswati-one-stop-access-to-government-services/>).

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
