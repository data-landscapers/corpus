# Uganda

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Uganda has a national data strategy being written. [The Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of ICT and National Guidance wrote on 17 December 2025 that the government is developing it, and that it will provide a framework once finalised and approved](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/laying-the-foundations-for-a-data-driven-digital-economy>). [A source of March 2026 describes the same work and gives no adoption or launch](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/ministry-engages-stakeholders-on-ugandas-national-emerging-technologies-strategy>). No source held says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The ministry is writing the strategy, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/laying-the-foundations-for-a-data-driven-digital-economy>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
