# Uganda

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** [The Minister of State for ICT and National Guidance launched the Uganda National Data Strategy in Kampala on 20 October 2026, with a costed implementation plan of sh1.6 trillion over five years](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/uganda-launches-national-data-strategy-NV_242720_102026>). [The Uganda Bureau of Statistics rejected the strategy the next day, calling the ICT ministry's launch illegal and redundant](<https://www.independent.co.ug/ubos-rejects-new-national-data-strategy-labels-ict-ministry-launch-illegal-and-redundant/>). [A ministry page of June 2024 says the draft was first written at a workshop in Jinja in February 2024](<https://ict.go.ug/media/news/paving-the-way-for-a-data-driven-future-through-a-national-data-strategy>).

**Body named and rules issued.** [The strategy provides for a National Data Steering Committee chaired by the finance ministry, and a National Data Office within the ICT ministry to coordinate delivery](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/science/uganda-launches-national-data-strategy-NV_242720_102026>). No source found names a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under the strategy, such as a rule on classifying data or on sharing it between public bodies.
