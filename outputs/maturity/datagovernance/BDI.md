# Burundi

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Burundi has a national data governance strategy in draft. [It was validated at a workshop in Bujumbura on 7 November 2025, with more than 60 delegates](<https://www.uneca.org/stories/burundi-validates-national-data-governance-strategy>), [after a consultation that began with a stakeholder workshop in April 2025](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy>). [The UN Economic Commission for Africa co-led the work](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/burundi-adopts-national-data-governance-strategy>), and [the ceremony was chaired by the Ministry of Finance and Digital Economy](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/burundi-la-strategie-nationale-de-gouvernance-des-donnees-devoilee/>). No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [A national data governance committee and an action plan for 2026 to 2028 are listed as next steps, not yet in place](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/burundi-validates-its-first-national-data-governance-strategy>). [The strategy's second pillar requires the country to adopt comprehensive data legislation](<https://communityvoice.bi/1489-2/>), and [the government has committed to develop a legal framework](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/burundi-adopts-national-data-governance-strategy>). No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.
