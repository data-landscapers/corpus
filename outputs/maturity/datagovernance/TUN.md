# Tunisia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Tunisia has a national strategy for the governance, opening and use of public data in draft. [A workshop on 26 March 2026 presented and discussed the final draft, prepared under a government decree of 2021](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>). No source held says the government has approved or launched it. Nothing later than the workshop is held.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The workshop discussed a final draft, and the source names no body to carry the strategy out](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source held names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
