# Tunisia

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Tunisia has a national strategy for the governance, opening and valorisation of public data, and it stood as a final draft in March 2026. [OGP Tunisie reports a workshop on 26 March 2026 to present and discuss the final draft, prepared under government decree 2021-3](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>). [The national open data portal's news lists a workshop the next day presenting the final version of the draft, which it titles "Stratégie nationale de gouvernance, d'ouverture et de valorisation des données"](<https://data.gov.tn/fr/>).

**Body named and rules issued.** The strategy is at the draft stage, so the nearest held facts are the workshops themselves. [The workshop of 26 March 2026 was held with the PAGOF programme](<http://www.ogptunisie.gov.tn/?p=6651>), and [the portal lists a further workshop on the final version](<https://data.gov.tn/fr/>). Neither names a council, office or agency to carry the strategy out.
