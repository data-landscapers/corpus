# Ghana

## National data policy

**Policy adopted.** Ghana has a national data strategy in draft. [On 2 July 2024 the government, the European Union and Smart Africa completed a two-day national validation workshop on the draft and its implementation roadmap](<https://smartafrica.org/ghana-develops-its-national-data-strategy-in-collaboration-with-smart-africa-and-team-europe>). Nothing newer on the strategy is held. No source found says the government has approved or launched it.

**Body named and rules issued.** [The July 2024 workshop reviewed an implementation roadmap, and the source names no body to carry it out](<https://smartafrica.org/ghana-develops-its-national-data-strategy-in-collaboration-with-smart-africa-and-team-europe>). Because the policy is not yet adopted, there is nothing for a body to carry out or for a rule to follow. No source found names a council, office, agency, committee or ministry unit set up to carry it out, or a law, regulation, standard or guideline issued under it.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a national data policy is adopted and still running.
