# Burkina Faso

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Burkina24 reports public-administration payments such as the vehicle tax and levies can be paid directly through Orange's Max It app, per Orange's Abdramane Diallo](<https://burkina24.com/2026/09/21/orange-burkina-faso-lance-la-nouvelle-version-de-son-application-max-it/>). [The CNSS social-security fund launched the eCNSS Burkina app on 28 October 2025, which lets employers pay contributions by bank transfer order or mobile money](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202510290346.html>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says the Prime Minister officially launched FASO ARZEKA, a single platform for payments to central government, local authorities and public establishments that consolidates the Treasury, tax and customs platforms](<https://www.finances.gov.bf/forum/detail-actualites?cHash=28c13a8a998127250e0d7f17528dcd44&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews%5D=2004>).
