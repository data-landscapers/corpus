# Niger

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The tax directorate says its e-sisic portal for electronic declaration and payment of taxes has operated since 13 September 2019](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/communiques-de-presse/666-communique-de-la-direction-generale-des-impots-relatif-a-la-declaration-et-au-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-par-voie-electronique>). [From 1 April 2026 customs duties and taxes on declarations are paid electronically through SONIBANK counters, payment terminals and ATMs](<https://www.lesahel.org/point-de-presse-a-la-direction-generale-des-douanes-les-operations-de-paiement-electronique-en-banque-des-droits-et-taxes-liquides-en-douanes-reprennent-le-1er-avril-2026/>). At first this was only at offices where SONIBANK has counters. Webb Fontaine says [stamp duties on foreign trade are calculated and paid digitally through the GUCE-Niger single window](<https://webbfontaine.com/fr/success-stories-niger-is-transforming-foreign-trade-procedures-with-webb-single-window>), in July 2026. [Since 1 October 2026 the vehicle single window run by SONILOGA takes some of its fees through Airtel Money](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/22417-guichet-unique-automobile-soniloga-ouvre-le-paiement-de-ses-prestations-a-airtel-money>). [On 12 November 2026 it added the AmanaTa app](<https://actuniger.com/societe/digitalisation-des-services-soniloga-sassocie-a-amana-pour-elargir-les-options-de-paiement-numerique-au-guan.html>). [In August 2025 Niamey received 100 electronic payment terminals for taxpayer payments](<https://www.journalduniger.com/100-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-pour-niamey/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are both filed and paid electronically. [The tax directorate's e-sisic portal takes declaration and payment of taxes](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/communiques-de-presse/666-communique-de-la-direction-generale-des-impots-relatif-a-la-declaration-et-au-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-par-voie-electronique>). [Customs duties have been paid electronically through SONIBANK since April 2026](<https://www.lesahel.org/point-de-presse-a-la-direction-generale-des-douanes-les-operations-de-paiement-electronique-en-banque-des-droits-et-taxes-liquides-en-douanes-reprennent-le-1er-avril-2026/>). At first this was only at some offices.
