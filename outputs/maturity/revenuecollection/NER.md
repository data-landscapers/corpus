# Niger

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Niger Direction Générale des Impôts states that its e-sisic portal for electronic declaration and payment of taxes has been operational since 13 September 2019](<https://finances.gouv.ne/index.php/communiques-de-presse/666-communique-de-la-direction-generale-des-impots-relatif-a-la-declaration-et-au-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-par-voie-electronique>). [SONILOGA, the delegated operator of the Guichet Unique Automobile du Niger, launched payment of some of its vehicle import and registration services through Airtel Money on 1 October 2026, which the transport ministry framed as securing public revenue](<https://www.actuniger.com/societe/22417-guichet-unique-automobile-soniloga-ouvre-le-paiement-de-ses-prestations-a-airtel-money>). [The Niamey city administrator received 100 electronic payment terminals from the Programme Village Intelligent to ease taxpayer payments and speed up collection of public funds, per Journal du Niger](<https://www.journalduniger.com/100-terminaux-de-paiement-electronique-pour-niamey/>). Webb Fontaine says [stamp duties on foreign trade are now calculated and paid digitally through the GUCE-Niger single window](<https://webbfontaine.com/fr/success-stories-niger-is-transforming-foreign-trade-procedures-with-webb-single-window>), in July 2026.
