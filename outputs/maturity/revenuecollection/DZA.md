# Algeria

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The DGI's Jibayatic platform lets taxpayers file declarations and pay taxes and duties online by bank transfer or CIB or Edahabia card, mandatory for large enterprises and optional for others, for taxes from electronic declarations only](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>). [The GIE Monetique director says electronic payment in the public sector is almost fully generalised, covering payment of taxes, bills, insurance, fees and administrative documents](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/economie/assia-benchabla-queiroz-directrice-du-gie-de-nombreux-obstacles-entravent-le-paiement-electronique-en-algerie-236510>). [The Direction generale des impots announced the launch of electronic payment of duties and taxes online through the Jibayatic tele-declaration and tele-payment portal](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/economie/banque-et-finances/ms0wxt1l-lancement-du-paiement-electronique-a-distance-des-droits-et-taxes>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
