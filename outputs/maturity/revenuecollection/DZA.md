# Algeria

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** The tax directorate's [Jibayatic platform lets taxpayers file and pay taxes and duties online](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>), by bank transfer or card. It is mandatory for large enterprises and optional for others. The directorate [announced the launch of online payment of duties and taxes through Jibayatic](<https://www.aps.dz/fr/economie/banque-et-finances/ms0wxt1l-lancement-du-paiement-electronique-a-distance-des-droits-et-taxes>). In February 2024 the finance minister said [the Moussahamatic portal had reached 242 tax offices by the end of 2023](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/numerisation-du-secteur-des-finances-les-applications-et-plateformes-ont-permis-de-realiser-des-progres-notables/>). Customs e-payment was still being finalised. The GIE Monétique director says [electronic payment in the public sector is almost fully general](<https://www.elmoudjahid.dz/fr/economie/assia-benchabla-queiroz-directrice-du-gie-de-nombreux-obstacles-entravent-le-paiement-electronique-en-algerie-236510>). Lawyers [must pay court fees electronically from 18 January 2026](<https://fildalgerie.dz/justice-numerique-lechange-electronique-des-documents-et-le-paiement-en-ligne-obligatoires-des-janvier-2026/>), except in courts not yet equipped. In October 2026 Echorouk reported [electronic channels in use to pay taxes, insurance, housing instalments and Hajj fees](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%88%D9%83-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the DGI's Jibayatic platform made online filing and payment mandatory for large enterprises and optional for others](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 Jibayatic took payment by bank transfer or CIB or Edahabia card](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>).
