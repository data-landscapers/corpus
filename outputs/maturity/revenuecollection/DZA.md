# Algeria

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Since 25 July 2026 the tax directorate's Jibayatic portal has been open to all taxpayers for remote payment of taxes](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/direction-generale-des-impots-lancement-du-paiement-electronique-a-distance-des-impots-via-le-portail-jibayatic/>). Payment is by CIB or Edahabia card or by transfer through five public banks. [Its guide says any tax, duty or levy declared online there can be paid online](<https://www.upgrowth.dz/actualites/paiement-impots-en-ligne-algerie-guide-jibayatic-dgi>). [Under the 2026 Finance Law, taxpayers on the real-profit regime in equipped tax offices must file electronically](<https://www.britacom.org/zt/BRPolicies/Algeria/Booklets/202601/P020260126579296296093.pdf>). They may also pay electronically. In February 2024 the finance minister said [the Moussahamatic portal had reached 242 tax offices by the end of 2023](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/numerisation-du-secteur-des-finances-les-applications-et-plateformes-ont-permis-de-realiser-des-progres-notables/>). Lawyers [must pay court fees electronically from 18 January 2026](<https://fildalgerie.dz/justice-numerique-lechange-electronique-des-documents-et-le-paiement-en-ligne-obligatoires-des-janvier-2026/>), except in courts not yet equipped. In October 2026 Echorouk reported [electronic channels in use to pay taxes, insurance, housing instalments and Hajj fees](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%88%D9%83-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%A8%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%BA%D9%8A%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%B1>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact: [taxes declared on Jibayatic can be paid online](<https://www.upgrowth.dz/actualites/paiement-impots-en-ligne-algerie-guide-jibayatic-dgi>). In February 2024 [customs e-payment procedures were still being finalised](<https://ouest-tribune.dz/numerisation-du-secteur-des-finances-les-applications-et-plateformes-ont-permis-de-realiser-des-progres-notables/>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 Jibayatic took payment by bank transfer or CIB or Edahabia card](<https://www.echoroukonline.com/bank-cards-and-transfers-introduced-for-tax-payments>).
