# Nigeria

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [FIRS states all tax and levy collections must be processed through assessments raised on the TaxPro Max platform](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [The platform handles payment processing and receipts](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [The Federal Government will unveil the National Single Window, a unified payment platform bringing revenue collection agencies together, in the first quarter of 2026](<https://thenationonlineng.net/customs-b-odogwu-other-payment-platforms-to-link-with-national-single-window/>). [In 2010 the Central Bank of Nigeria's draft guidelines set out requirements for electronic payment of all taxes, levies, duties and fees at all levels of government](<https://www.cbn.gov.ng/OUT/2010/CIRCULARS/BOD/DRAFT%20GUIDELINES%20OF%20ELECTRONIC%20PAYMENTS%20OF%20ALL%20FORMS%20OF%20TAXES.PDF>). [Business taxes were to be paid electronically on a stated timetable](<https://www.cbn.gov.ng/OUT/2010/CIRCULARS/BOD/DRAFT%20GUIDELINES%20OF%20ELECTRONIC%20PAYMENTS%20OF%20ALL%20FORMS%20OF%20TAXES.PDF>).
