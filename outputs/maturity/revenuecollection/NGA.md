# Nigeria

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [FIRS states all tax and levy collections must be processed through assessments raised on the TaxPro Max platform, which handles payment processing and receipts](<https://theeconomictimes.com.ng/2025/04/30/firs-orders-banks-to-shut-down-unauthorised-tax-collection-accounts-mandates-use-of-taxpro-max/>). [The Federal Government will unveil the National Single Window, a unified payment platform bringing revenue collection agencies together, in the first quarter of 2026](<https://thenationonlineng.net/customs-b-odogwu-other-payment-platforms-to-link-with-national-single-window/>). [The Central Bank of Nigeria's draft guidelines set out requirements for electronic payment of all taxes, levies, duties and fees at all levels of government, with business taxes to be paid electronically on a stated timetable (2010, before the three-year window)](<https://www.cbn.gov.ng/OUT/2010/CIRCULARS/BOD/DRAFT%20GUIDELINES%20OF%20ELECTRONIC%20PAYMENTS%20OF%20ALL%20FORMS%20OF%20TAXES.PDF>).
