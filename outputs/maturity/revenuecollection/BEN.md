# Benin

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [SIRATPay, run by SIRAT SA with the ministries of Living Environment and of Economy and Finance, lets Benin motorists pay road tolls and penalties remotely without cash, per Fraternité](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>). [The Senegalese finance minister said PI-SPI, launched 30 September 2025, facilitates payments of taxes, duties and fees among other operations](<https://lanation.bj/economie/lancement-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-interoperable-la-pi-spi-une-solution-rapide-et-inclusive-au-profit-des-citoyens-uemoa>). [Benin's Directorate General of Taxes unveiled new online services on 23 May 2024 to streamline tax payments for small businesses and property owners in Cotonou, on the e-services.impots.bj portal first launched in 2017](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/benin-launches-new-online-tax-services-for-businesses-and-property-owners>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>).
