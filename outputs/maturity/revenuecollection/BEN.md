# Benin

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [SIRATPay, run by SIRAT SA with the ministries of Living Environment and of Economy and Finance, lets Benin motorists pay road tolls and penalties remotely without cash, per Fraternité](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>). [The Senegalese finance minister said PI-SPI, launched 30 September 2025, facilitates payments of taxes, duties and fees among other operations](<https://lanation.bj/economie/lancement-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-interoperable-la-pi-spi-une-solution-rapide-et-inclusive-au-profit-des-citoyens-uemoa>). [Decree 2026-617 of the President of Benin, signed 18 September 2026 and effective from signature, requires fees for acts issued by the justice chancellery and the courts, tax stamps included, to be paid only electronically, with receipts going to a dedicated Treasury account](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2026-617/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>).
