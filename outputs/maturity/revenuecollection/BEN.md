# Benin

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In March 2026 a release said taxpayers can declare and pay taxes online, by automated bank transfer or mobile money](<https://anssfd.bj/benin-la-digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-double-les-ressources-de-letat-et-accelere-linclusion-financiere/>). Receipts are instant. [SIRATPay lets motorists pay road tolls and penalties remotely without cash](<https://fraternite.bj/siratpay-le-paiement-numerique-simplifie-les-peages-au-benin/>), per Fraternité. [PI-SPI, the regional instant payment system launched on 30 September 2025, takes payments of taxes, duties and fees among other operations](<https://lanation.bj/economie/lancement-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-interoperable-la-pi-spi-une-solution-rapide-et-inclusive-au-profit-des-citoyens-uemoa>). [Decree 2026-617, signed on 18 September 2026, requires fees for justice chancellery and court acts, tax stamps included, to be paid only electronically](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2026-617/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in March 2026 taxpayers could declare and pay taxes online by bank transfer or mobile money](<https://anssfd.bj/benin-la-digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-double-les-ressources-de-letat-et-accelere-linclusion-financiere/>).

**Share reported.** [In March 2026 a release said nearly a third of state revenue was collected through digital channels](<https://anssfd.bj/benin-la-digitalisation-des-paiements-publics-double-les-ressources-de-letat-et-accelere-linclusion-financiere/>). [The tax directorate's 2025 activity report puts electronic payments above 96% for most taxes, mostly from medium and large firms](<https://ictd.ac/general/benins-e-tax-rollout-small-businesses-digital-public-infrastructure/>), ICTD reports.
