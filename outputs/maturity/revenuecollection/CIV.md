# Cote d'Ivoire

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The BCEAO says Treasuries connected to PI-SPI can receive taxes and duties from any account, and Cote d'Ivoire's Treasury has met all PI-SPI requirements](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>). [Clotchor Secongo says his digital solutions, including the Treasury's TresorPay e-payment platform, have improved some municipal revenues in 75 local authorities in Cote d'Ivoire; the source is his own note to the newspaper](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2640916/societe/collectivites-territoriales-clotchor-secongo-developpe-un-systeme-pour-digitaliser-les-services>). [The finance ministry says online filing and online payment of taxes and duties (télé-déclaration, télépaiement) have been in operation in Côte d'Ivoire since 2017 (2017, before the three-year window)](<https://finances.gouv.ci/gouvernance-et-reformes/fiscalite-interieure>). The finance minister says [collections through TresorPay reached FCFA 36 billion from 2.5 million transactions in 2024](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/09/15/cote-divoire/economie/cote-divoire-modernisation-des-finances-de-letat-le-tresor-public-accroit-ses-performances-grace-a-un-systeme-de-digitalisation-efficace-et-transparent_190194.html>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>).

**Share reported.** The finance minister gives [collections through the Treasury's TresorPay platform as FCFA 3 billion in 2021 and FCFA 36 billion in 2024](<https://www.koaci.com/article/2025/09/15/cote-divoire/economie/cote-divoire-modernisation-des-finances-de-letat-le-tresor-public-accroit-ses-performances-grace-a-un-systeme-de-digitalisation-efficace-et-transparent_190194.html>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [PI-SPI](<https://www.bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-des-tresors-publics-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement>); [Facture normalisée électronique (FNE)](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-la-fenacci-cree-un-observatoire-pour-accompagner-la-transition-vers-la-facture-normalisee-electronique/>); [Reçu numérique électronique (RNE)](<https://www.aip.ci/cote-divoire-aip-la-fenacci-cree-un-observatoire-pour-accompagner-la-transition-vers-la-facture-normalisee-electronique/>); [TresorPay](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2640916/societe/collectivites-territoriales-clotchor-secongo-developpe-un-systeme-pour-digitaliser-les-services>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
