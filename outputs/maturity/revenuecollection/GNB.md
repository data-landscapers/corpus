# Guinea-Bissau

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In July 2024 O Democrata GB reported on the tax directorate's Kontaktu platform. [It lets taxpayers declare and pay taxes at any time by bank transfer, Orange Money and Mobile Money](<https://www.odemocratagb.com/?p=49407>). [The IMF said electronic tax payment options rose from none to six after March 2022](<https://www.imf.org/en/publications/high-level-summary-technical-assistance-reports/issues/2024/04/30/guinea-bissau-further-improvements-in-tax-compliance-548436>). [A tax directorate circular of 10 December 2025 makes import VAT payable only through Kontaktu](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>). [An African Development Bank grant to WATAF, launched in August 2026, is to support digital tax tools and e-invoicing in six countries](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/liberia-five-others-benefit-from-us-5-3m-afdb-tax-administration-project/article_09a09868-8c9e-49c2-ad5e-1dcf6a739fe8.html>). Guinea-Bissau is among them.

**Main taxes and duties.** The nearest fact on file is [the Kontaktu circular](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>), which covers import VAT. No source has been found since October 2023 saying the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.

**Share reported.** The nearest fact on file is again [the Kontaktu circular](<https://kontaktu.mef.gw/>). It gives no figure. No source has been found since October 2023 putting a share on revenue paid electronically.
