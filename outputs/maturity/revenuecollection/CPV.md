**Paid electronically.** [The DNRE says tax returns are submitted and the taxes paid electronically through its portal, with the deadline moved from 31 May to 14 June 2024 after an outage was cleared](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/-/funcionamento-do-portal>).
