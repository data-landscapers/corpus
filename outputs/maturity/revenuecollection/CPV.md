# Cape Verde

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In May 2024 the tax directorate (DNRE) said tax returns are submitted and taxes paid through its portal](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/-/funcionamento-do-portal>). [It moved the filing deadline from 31 May to 14 June 2024 after a portal outage was cleared](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/-/funcionamento-do-portal>). [In 2026 the DNRE's online customs clearance page took payment by DUC reference](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/pagamento-online-de-despacho>). [It accepts Vinti4 or Visa cards issued by Cabo Verdean banks, with SISP one-time-password security](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/pagamento-online-de-despacho>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [In May 2024 the DNRE extended to 14 June the deadline for filing tax returns and paying the taxes through its portal](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/-/funcionamento-do-portal>). [In 2026 its customs clearance page took payment online by card against the clearance's DUC reference](<https://www.mf.gov.cv/web/dnre/pagamento-online-de-despacho>).
