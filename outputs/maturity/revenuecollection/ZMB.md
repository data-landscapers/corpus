# Zambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In February 2024 the Zambia Revenue Authority said it takes tax payments through TaxOnApp, TaxOnPhone, the TaxOnline Portal and WhatsApp Payment](<https://www.dailynationzambia.com/2024/02/zra-adopts-advanced-technology-system-in-income-collection/>). [ZamPay and ZamPortal let citizens and businesses pay for more than 400 government services electronically](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>), per the Office of the Accountant General. They include passport applications and road tax. Payment is by bank transfer, card and mobile money. [Eight councils have taken property rates, business levies and permits electronically through the Government Services Bus since June 2026](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>). [Toll fees are paid by mobile money through Lipila at toll gates across the country](<https://times.co.zm/cashless-shift-how-hobbiton-is-driving-fintech-agenda/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [In December 2023 the revenue authority brought in its Integrated Payment System](<https://www.dailynationzambia.com/2024/02/zra-adopts-advanced-technology-system-in-income-collection/>). It added customs payments on mobile platforms to its tax payment platforms.

**Share reported.** [In February 2024 the revenue authority said about 99 percent of its payments were made electronically](<https://www.dailynationzambia.com/2024/02/zra-adopts-advanced-technology-system-in-income-collection/>). [The Office of the Accountant General says the government's digital payment and service platforms have collected more than K8.8 billion (US$488 million)](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [About K21.7 million came through the Government Services Bus in the month after its June 2026 rollout to eight councils](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>).
