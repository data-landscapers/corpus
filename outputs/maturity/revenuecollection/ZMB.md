# Zambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The National Road Fund Agency takes toll fees by mobile money through Lipila at toll gates across the country, with over 40,000 transactions in about two months of rollout, per Hobbiton and the NRFA](<https://times.co.zm/cashless-shift-how-hobbiton-is-driving-fintech-agenda/>). [ZamPay and ZamPortal let citizens and businesses pay for more than 400 government services electronically, including passport applications and road tax, by bank transfer, card and mobile money, per the Office of the Accountant General](<https://itweb.africa/article/digital-services-strengthen-zambia-finances/JBwEr7n3zk3M6Db2>). [Eight local authorities were onboarded onto the Government Services Bus from June 2026, letting residents and businesses pay property rates, business levies and permits electronically, per the Zambian Business Times](<https://zambianbusinesstimes.com/k22-million-collected-from-8-councils-via-electronic-payments/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
