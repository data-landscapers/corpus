# Uganda

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Toll fees at the Busega, Kajjansi and Mpala plazas on the Kampala-Entebbe Expressway can now be paid by mobile money](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/09/25/mtn-uganda-mobile-money-toll-payments-kampala-entebbe-expressway/>). The service was launched with the Ministry of Works and Transport, per Tech Africa News. [MTN Mobile Money and the Uganda Revenue Authority announced an enhanced URA tax and government payment menu on *165*18#, live nationwide](<https://www.mtn.co.ug/newsbusiness/mtn-momo-and-ura-enhance-digital-tax-services-for-ugandans-on-165/>). [Uganda's Ministry of Works and Transport said test runs of mobile money toll payment on the Kampala-Entebbe Expressway were complete](<https://chimpreports.com/mobile-money-toll-payments-coming-to-kampala-entebbe-expressway/>). It said the service would launch soon, with no start date given. In June 2026 the Revenue Authority said [tax can be paid by EFT, RTGS, VISA, bank counter and mobile USSD](<https://ura.go.ug/en/ask-ura-commissioner-general-19/>). It said VAT, rental income tax and local excise duty returns are filed on the web.
