# Ghana

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** The government's CitizenApp, launched on 7 October 2024, lists [filing of tax returns among the services it reaches](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>), and was built by the Office of the President together with the Ghana Revenue Authority and other agencies; the launch did not say tax can be paid in the app. Earlier, the Ghana Revenue Authority said that from May 2021 [domestic taxpayers pay on the Ghana.gov platform](<https://gra.gov.gh/cashless-policy/>) by card, QR code, mobile money and bank transfer.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact is the [CitizenApp's tax-return filing](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact is the [Ghana Revenue Authority's 2021 cashless policy](<https://gra.gov.gh/cashless-policy/>).

***Also on file***. [E-VAT](<https://gra.gov.gh/e-services/e-vat/>); [Paperless Port System](<https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003274322-9>).

***Not found***. Nothing found since October 2023 says taxes or fees are paid electronically, or that the main taxes and customs duties are.
