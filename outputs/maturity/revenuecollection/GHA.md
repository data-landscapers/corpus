# Ghana

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** From 1 November 2026 [Ghana Police say traffic fines notified by their TraffiTech-GH cameras can be paid by mobile money or card](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/tech/ghana-la-police-deploie-des-cameras-connectees-pour-automatiser-le-controle-routier>) within 14 days. The government's CitizenApp, launched on 7 October 2024, [lists the filing of tax returns among its services](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>). The Ghana Revenue Authority helped build it. The launch did not say tax can be paid in the app. From May 2021 the authority said [domestic taxpayers pay on the Ghana.gov platform](<https://gra.gov.gh/cashless-policy/>) by card, QR code, mobile money and bank transfer.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact is the [CitizenApp's tax-return filing](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202410/ghana-launches-citizenapp-to-facilitate-access-to-digital-govt-services>), announced in 2024.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact is the [Ghana Revenue Authority's 2021 cashless policy](<https://gra.gov.gh/cashless-policy/>).

***Also on file***. [E-VAT](<https://gra.gov.gh/e-services/e-vat/>); [Paperless Port System](<https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003274322-9>).
