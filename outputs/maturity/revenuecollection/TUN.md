# Tunisia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The national administrative services portal says citizens can pay fines (Ministry of Finance) online or by USSD](<https://services.gov.tn/finances/>). [Parents paid school registration fees remotely through La Poste Tunisienne e-Dinar wallets and D17, and about 287,300 students (about 76%) completed remote university registration by electronic payment by end-September 2026](<https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/10/09/numerisation-la-poste-tunisienne-valide-plus-de-18-million-dinscriptions-scolaires-et-universitaires-en-ligne/>). The customs spokesman said in November 2025 that [customs has offered electronic payment in several offices among services digitised since 2018](<https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/11/12/la-douane-muscle-ses-controles-et-digitalise-ses-services/1372410/>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
