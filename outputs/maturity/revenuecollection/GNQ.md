# Equatorial Guinea

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Presidential Order 3/2025 sets the abandonment of cash collection of certain public revenues](<https://diariorombe.es/tecnologia/muni-dinero-la-plataforma-financiera-de-pastor-hassan-obiang-mangue-creado-con-dinero-publico-que-aspira-a-entrar-en-las-cuentas-del-estado-y-concentrar-informacion-economica-de-los-ciudadanos/>), Diario Rombe reports. [On 30 September 2026 the e-money firm Muni proposed its wallet as a channel for state taxes and fees](<https://diariorombe.es/tecnologia/muni-dinero-la-plataforma-financiera-de-pastor-hassan-obiang-mangue-creado-con-dinero-publico-que-aspira-a-entrar-en-las-cuentas-del-estado-y-concentrar-informacion-economica-de-los-ciudadanos/>). The transport ministry said it would study the proposal.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. [The 2025 order covers certain public revenues only](<https://diariorombe.es/tecnologia/muni-dinero-la-plataforma-financiera-de-pastor-hassan-obiang-mangue-creado-con-dinero-publico-que-aspira-a-entrar-en-las-cuentas-del-estado-y-concentrar-informacion-economica-de-los-ciudadanos/>).
