# Zimbabwe

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Justice Minister Ziyambi Ziyambi said applicants on the DLAP can pay deed fees electronically under the Gazette's Fourth Schedule or by the manual process in the Second Schedule](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>). [ZIMRA Public Notice 75 of 2024 says taxpayers file returns online through the Self-Service Portal and pay VAT, PAYE, income, withholding and presumptive taxes into the ZIMRA bank account by cash deposit or bank transfer from linked bank accounts, posted in TaRMS](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>). [ZIMRA says the TaRMS Payments Management and Return Management modules were launched on 6 December 2023](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2294-tarms-innovation-heralding-modernity-tax-agent-module-is-live>). [Customs payments stay on the old platform](<https://zimra.co.zw/news/22-taxmans-corner/2267-introducing-the-single-account-concept-in-tax-payments-under-the-new-zimra-tax-revenue-management-system-tarms>).
