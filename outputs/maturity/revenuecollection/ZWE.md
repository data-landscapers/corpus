# Zimbabwe

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In October 2026 ZINARA [introduced a multi-currency e-wallet on which motorists pay tolls, vehicle licences, radio licences and insurance](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/zinara-rolls-out-multi-currency-e-wallet-platform/>). It runs on phones, the web and USSD. [Applicants on the DLAP can pay deed fees electronically](<https://newziana.co.zw/zimbabwe-launches-digital-platform-to-modernize-title-deed-processing/>), the Justice Minister said. [ZIMRA Public Notice 75 of 2024 says taxpayers file returns online through its Self-Service Portal](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/public-notices?download=4100%3Apublic-notice-75-of-2024-submission-of-outstanding-returns-and-payment-of-tax>). They pay VAT, PAYE and other taxes by deposit or bank transfer, posted in TaRMS. [ZIMRA launched the TaRMS payment and return modules on 6 December 2023](<https://www.zimra.co.zw/news/2294-tarms-innovation-heralding-modernity-tax-agent-module-is-live>). [Customs payments stay on the old platform](<https://zimra.co.zw/news/22-taxmans-corner/2267-introducing-the-single-account-concept-in-tax-payments-under-the-new-zimra-tax-revenue-management-system-tarms>).
