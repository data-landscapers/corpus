# Botswana

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Andersen says Botswana's tax rules in force from 1 July 2026 make electronic billing and invoicing mandatory and support tax administration with digital filing, with compliance deadlines still to apply](<https://bw.andersen.com/botswanas-new-tax-rules-take-effect/>). [The Ministry of Finance, as reported on 2025-10-31, seeks P4.13 billion for the Botswana Unified Revenue Service in the NDP 12 programme, with projects including the rollout of electronic VAT invoicing and digital taxation for online services](<https://www.sundaystandard.info/finance-ministry-bets-p8-8bn-on-digital-discipline/>). [The Bank of Botswana said digitalisation of revenue collection, announced in 2023, is nearing completion pending integration with the Botswana Unified Revenue Service and the Accountant General](<https://www.bwtechzone.com/2025/07/national-payments-switch-act-drafted.html>).
