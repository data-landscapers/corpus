# Seychelles

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In March 2024 the Seychelles Revenue Commission (SRC) said taxpayers pay by bank transfer or at SRC offices from January 2024](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [They send the payment slip, return form and proof of payment by email](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [The SRC said it had begun migrating to a new online tax portal](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [Payment advice numbers were to be activated when it went live](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [In May 2024 the WTO trade facilitation database listed an electronic payment module among completed additions to the SRC's ASYCUDA World customs system](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/seychelles/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [in May 2024 the SRC's ASYCUDA World customs system had an electronic payment module](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/seychelles/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>).
