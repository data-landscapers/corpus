# Seychelles

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The SRC says from January 2024 taxpayers are to pay by bank transfer or at SRC offices, sending the payment slip, return form and proof of payment by email](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). [The SRC says it has started migrating to a new online tax platform, with payment advise numbers to be activated when the new tax portal goes live](<https://src.gov.sc/src-introduces-dedicated-return-forms-and-tax-payment-slip-to-replace-the-business-activity-statement/>). The WTO trade facilitation database lists [an electronic payment module among the completed additions to the Revenue Commission's ASYCUDA World customs system](<https://tfadatabase.org/en/members/seychelles/technical-assistance-projects/article-10-4>), in May 2024. 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.
