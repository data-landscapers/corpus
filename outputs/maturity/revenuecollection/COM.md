# Comoros

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 15 April 2026 the Directorate General of Taxes launched a three-day workshop](<https://finances.gouv.km/actualite_post/atelier-dgi-au-retaj-sur-la-validation-du-cadre-juridique-des-teleprocedures-fiscales-2026/>). [AFD's PAGFSI project funded it, to validate the legal framework for fiscal teleprocedures](<https://finances.gouv.km/actualite_post/atelier-dgi-au-retaj-sur-la-validation-du-cadre-juridique-des-teleprocedures-fiscales-2026/>). [It prepared the progressive rollout of tax e-filing (télédéclaration) and e-payment (télépaiement)](<https://dgi.gouv.km/fr/actualite.php?article_id=93>). [The rollout falls under the 2026 finance law](<https://dgi.gouv.km/fr/actualite.php?article_id=93>).
