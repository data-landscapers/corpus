# Angola

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In 2025 the UN's global survey on trade facilitation reported electronic payment of customs duties in Angola as partially implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=AGO>). [In January 2026 ANGOP reported that the KWiK roadmap foresees payments to the State through the Multicaixa network](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/operacoes-no-kwik-registam-crescimento-acima-dos-100/>).
