# Angola

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In July 2026 [the tax authority AGT directed that taxes be paid electronically as a rule, through its taxpayer portal](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/fim-do-papel-agt-exige-via-digital-para-pagamento-de-impostos-e-reclamacoes>). Its offices help only where internet access or digital skills are lacking. RUPE is a 20-digit reference generated in the AGT system. [Payments to the State by RUPE ran to 12.5 million operations in 2025](<https://expansao.co.ao/economia/detalhe/pagamentos-via-rupe-ja-valem-36-das-receitas-correntes-do-estado-70130.html>), Expansão reports. [In 2025 the UN's global survey on trade facilitation reported electronic payment of customs duties in Angola as partially implemented](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=AGO>). [In January 2026 ANGOP reported that the KWiK roadmap foresees payments to the State through the Multicaixa network](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/operacoes-no-kwik-registam-crescimento-acima-dos-100/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact found: [in July 2026 AGT made electronic payment through its portal the general rule for taxes](<https://valoreconomico.co.ao/artigo/fim-do-papel-agt-exige-via-digital-para-pagamento-de-impostos-e-reclamacoes>). [Electronic payment of customs duties was partially implemented in 2025](<https://www.untfsurvey.org/economy?id=AGO>).

**Share reported.** Expansão, from EMIS data, [put payments through RUPE in 2025 at about Kz 6.7 trillion](<https://expansao.co.ao/economia/detalhe/pagamentos-via-rupe-ja-valem-36-das-receitas-correntes-do-estado-70130.html>). That is roughly 36% of Kz 18.9 trillion of current revenue, oil taxes excluded.
