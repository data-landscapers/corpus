# Somalia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Central Bank of Somalia says SOMQR, launched in March 2023, lets customers pay for goods, services, taxes and fees by scanning a code](<https://centralbank.gov.so/nps/>). [In July 2026 the Central Bank said its instant payment system, SIPS, supports payments to government](<https://www.hiiraan.com/news4/2026/Jul/205808/somalia_to_expand_instant_payment_system_linking_banks_and_mobile_money_platform.aspx>). [SONNA reported the same month that SIPS carries payments made to government](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 SIPS carried payments made to government](<https://sonna.so/en/article/Central-Bank-sets-out-plan-to-bring-banks-and-mobile-money-onto-one-payments-network>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [SOMQR has taken payment of taxes and fees by code since March 2023](<https://centralbank.gov.so/nps/>).
