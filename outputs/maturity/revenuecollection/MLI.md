[At the Bamako dermatology hospital, payments for consultations, medical tests and medicines are electronic, which the health minister's 27 July 2026 visit said secures revenue](<https://www.africascoop.net/index.php/2026/07/30/sante-le-mali-accelere-la-digitalisation-de-ses-services-publics/>). [Mali's digital payment system at civil registry centres is in service and mobilised nearly FCFA 200 million for local authorities between July 2024 and May 2026, per the Ministry of Territorial Administration and Decentralisation](<https://fassoactu.com/2026/07/31/transparence-financiere-les-collectivites-sommees-daccelerer-la-digitalisation-des-paiements/>).
