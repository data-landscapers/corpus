# Sudan

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** The finance ministry says [its Isali collection and payment system has been in use since early 2024](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>), with 22 banks under contract. MTN SIM users [can pay through it and track their transactions](<https://sudanhorizon.com/finance-ministry-signs-sms-service-agreement-with-mtn-sudan/>). Kassala State [launched Isali on 11 November 2025](<https://www.alnilin.com/13412714.htm>), starting in Kassala locality. In September 2026 [Rabak locality launched mobile electronic collection](<https://nilepost.net/4582/>), the first in White Nile State. The Sudan Tax Authority says [its e-portal takes returns, while electronic payment channels were still being activated](<https://tax.gov.sd/projects/>) in February 2025. A POS payment pilot ran in 70 offices. Kafuta said [institutions in RSF-held areas were told to stop collecting through Bankak](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>). They were to deposit cash or use Future Bank's application. Al Arabiya reports that [disruption of the Bankak app reaches government electronic collection](<https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/sudan/2026/10/09/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86>), including taxes, customs and public fees.

**Main taxes and duties.** In April 2026 the finance ministry said [Isali collects all public revenue fees approved in the state budget](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>). It is linked to the ASYCUDA customs system.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in September 2026 the finance ministry said MTN users can pay through the state-owned Eesaly collection system](<https://sudanhorizon.com/finance-ministry-signs-sms-service-agreement-with-mtn-sudan/>).
