# Sudan

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Sudan Ministry of Finance statement says MTN SIM users can use the state-owned electronic collection and payment system Eesaly and track the transactions they complete through it](<https://sudanhorizon.com/finance-ministry-signs-sms-service-agreement-with-mtn-sudan/>). [Kafuta said government institutions in RSF-held areas were told to open Future Bank accounts and stop collecting official payments through Bankak, depositing revenue in cash or transferring it through Future Bank's application](<https://thesudantimes.com/sudan/dagalo-orders-unified-cash-digital-payment-prices-in-darfur/>). [Al Arabiya reports that disruption of the Bankak banking app of Bank of Khartoum reaches government transactions and electronic collection, and that payment and collection channels tied to taxes, customs and public fees could be disrupted](<https://www.alarabiya.net/arab-and-world/sudan/2026/10/09/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B9%D9%86%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7-%D9%8A%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%87%D9%85-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%83%D9%8A-%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86>). The finance ministry says [its Isali collection and payment system has been in use since early 2024, with 22 banks under contract](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>).

**Main taxes and duties.** The finance ministry says [Isali collects all public revenue fees approved in the state budget and is linked to the ASYCUDA customs system](<https://spokesperson-sd.com/ar/archives/39743>), in April 2026.

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://sudanhorizon.com/finance-ministry-signs-sms-service-agreement-with-mtn-sudan/>).
