# Eswatini

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 4 March 2024 the Eswatini Revenue Service's Tax Ease portal went live with integrated payments for taxes](<https://eswatinipositivenews.com/ers-launches-tax-portal/>). [In June 2025 Eswatini Positive News reported that the Government-In-Your-Hand app takes payment for government services from mobile money accounts](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>). [On 12 June 2026 the ERS launched its Integrated Payment Solution with FNB Eswatini](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>). It lets customs duties and import VAT be paid through the bank. [In October 2026 the ERS said 2026 annual returns are filed and paid wholly online, through Tax-Ease or the TaxPAL app](<https://swazidailynews.com/11479/national/your-side-hustle-could-cost-you-if-you-dont-file-ers-warns/>). Payment is by EFT, bank deposit, MoMo Pay or speed point.

**Main taxes and duties.** [Income tax returns for 2025/26 must be filed electronically through eFiling](<https://swazidailynews.com/11962/business/ers-targets-multiple-income-earners-ahead-of-deadline/>), and no manual returns are accepted, the ERS said in November 2026. [Customs duties and import VAT have been payable through FNB Eswatini since June 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact found: [the 2026 annual returns are filed and paid 100 per cent online](<https://swazidailynews.com/11479/national/your-side-hustle-could-cost-you-if-you-dont-file-ers-warns/>).
