# Eswatini

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 4 March 2024 the Eswatini Revenue Service's Tax Ease portal went live with integrated payments for taxes](<https://eswatinipositivenews.com/ers-launches-tax-portal/>). [In June 2025 Eswatini Positive News reported that the Government-In-Your-Hand app takes payment for government services from mobile money accounts](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/over-2k-downloads-government-in-your-hand-app/>). [On 12 June 2026 the ERS launched its Integrated Payment Solution with FNB Eswatini](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>). [Customs duties and import VAT can be paid through the bank, and domestic tax payments are the next phase](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [since June 2026 customs duties and import VAT can be paid through FNB Eswatini](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brightwell-nkambule-9997209_today-we-launched-the-ers-integrated-payment-activity-7471306041775849472-7YZM>).
