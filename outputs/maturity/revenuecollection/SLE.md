# Sierra Leone

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 24 April 2025 Bo District Council launched the GovPay Sierra Leone app so property rates can be paid by mobile money](<https://bodc.gov.sl/ova_dep/electronic-tax-payment/>). [In July 2026 the National Revenue Authority was rolling out the NTR Payment Mobile App for non-tax service fees](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-train-surveyors-mobile-payment-app-makeni/>). [Training was done in the southern and eastern regions](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-train-surveyors-mobile-payment-app-makeni/>). [The current phase covered Port Loko, Makeni, Karene, Kono, Kambia and Falaba](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-train-surveyors-mobile-payment-app-makeni/>).
