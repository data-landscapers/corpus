# Sierra Leone

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In August 2025 the National Revenue Authority's Commissioner General said all excise taxpayers file and pay electronically](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>). [Non-tax government fees are paid by mobile money through an NRA app](<https://awokonewspapersl.com/nra-hits-100-digital-tax-filing-compliance-as-sierra-leones-revenue-reforms-accelerate/>), he said. [On 24 April 2025 Bo District Council launched the GovPay Sierra Leone app so property rates can be paid by mobile money](<https://bodc.gov.sl/ova_dep/electronic-tax-payment/>). [In July 2026 the NRA was rolling out its NTR Payment Mobile App for non-tax service fees](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/nra-train-surveyors-mobile-payment-app-makeni/>). Training was done in the southern and eastern regions. The current phase covers Port Loko, Makeni, Karene, Kono, Kambia and Falaba.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
