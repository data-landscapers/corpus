# Liberia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [CBL Directive 002/2025 says that from 1 December 2025 all Government of Liberia revenue collections by mobile money, including taxes, fees and levies, must be routed through NEPS/IIPS and settled into the consolidated Revenue Accounts at the CBL](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>). [The Liberia Revenue Authority says its Integrated Orange Mobile Money Platform, linked to LITAS, was launched for taxes and fees across 18 ministries and agencies, paid by USSD with an SMS receipt](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/no-more-standing-in-lines-to-pay-taxes-lra-orange-liberia-ecobank-launch-new-platform/>). [The African Development Bank's DIGIGOV Liberia project aimed to implement an e-payment solution hosted by the Liberia Revenue Authority to facilitate customs tax collection; the completion-report abstract states no outcomes](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/multinational-government-payments-p2g-g2p-digitization-mano-river-union-liberia-project-completion-report>). The Liberia Revenue Authority says [taxpayers can pay tax through LITAS by mobile money, phone or computer, with seven of nine commercial banks connected](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>), in August 2025.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>).
