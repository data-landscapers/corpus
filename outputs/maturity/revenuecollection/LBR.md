# Liberia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In April 2026 the revenue authority said its LITAS system lets taxpayers register, file returns, generate payment slips and pay in real time](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-hosts-first-digital-symposium-to-showcase-technology-driving-tax-compliance-and-payment-systems/>). [By August 2025 taxpayers could pay through LITAS by mobile money, phone or computer](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/lra-trains-staff-on-new-digital-tax-payment-system/>). Seven of nine commercial banks were connected. [An Orange Money platform linked to LITAS takes taxes and fees for 18 ministries and agencies by USSD](<https://revenue.lra.gov.lr/no-more-standing-in-lines-to-pay-taxes-lra-orange-liberia-ecobank-launch-new-platform/>). [From 1 December 2025 a central bank directive routes all government revenue collected by mobile money, taxes included, through NEPS/IIPS](<https://cbl.org.lr/sites/default/files/documents/NEPS-IIPS%20Directive%20-%20092325.pdf>) [to the central bank](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/business/orange-mtn-mobile-money-users-to-enjoy-instant-cross-network-transfers/article_42a4f4e2-29fe-4ad0-90b4-bceabca5721a.html>). [An African Development Bank project aimed to set up an e-payment solution at the revenue authority for customs collection](<https://www.afdb.org/en/documents/multinational-government-payments-p2g-g2p-digitization-mano-river-union-liberia-project-completion-report>). In November 2026 [the consulate general in New York began taking fees for all consular services online](<https://www.thenewdawnliberia.com/liberias-new-york-consulate-introduces-online-payment-for-services/>).
