# Lesotho

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [A whole-of-government e-payment gateway to be run by the Central Bank of Lesotho is being developed and will soon be integrated with the Lesotho National Single Window, per CAFI's newsletter](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/>). [Minister Moorosi says the government is close to launching an integrated online services platform with an electronic payment gateway so citizens can pay for services digitally instead of cash](<https://lestimes.com/?p=90492>). [Revenue Services Lesotho and Standard Lesotho Bank launched on 11 February 2026 an integrated e-payment system letting taxpayers pay taxes electronically by USSD, internet banking and the bank's mobile app](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [Revenue Services Lesotho says individuals and businesses can pay Corporate Income Tax, PAYE, VAT and customs duties electronically through the new RSL-SLB e-payment system](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>).
