# Lesotho

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** Revenue Services Lesotho's September 2026 guide says [its e-Payments platform takes domestic taxes by EcoCash, M-Pesa or bank channels](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2026-09/Guide%20on%20e-Payments-_3.pdf>). [In July 2024 it added EcoCash wallet payment of taxes and customs duties through its website](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/rsl-adds-new-digital-tax-payment-platform/>). [On 11 February 2026 it launched e-payment with Standard Lesotho Bank by USSD, internet banking and the bank's app](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>). [A whole-of-government payment gateway at the Central Bank of Lesotho is being developed](<https://lesothotribune.co.ls/public-private-dialogue-reforms-stalled-in-nine-areas-matekane-says/>), to be linked to the National Single Window.

**Main taxes and duties.** [Revenue Services Lesotho says Corporate Income Tax, PAYE, VAT and customs duties can be paid through the RSL-SLB e-payment system](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>). [Its September 2026 guide says domestic taxes and customs duties are paid on its e-Payments platform](<https://www.rsl.org.ls/sites/default/files/2026-09/Guide%20on%20e-Payments-_3.pdf>). [A September 2025 African Development Bank report says the tax modernisation project built e-filing for VAT, PAYE and corporate income tax](<https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/projects-and-operations/lesotho_-_lesotho_tax_modernisation_project_-_supplementary_financing_-_ipr_september_2025.pdf>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [on 11 February 2026 the revenue service and Standard Lesotho Bank launched e-payment by USSD, internet banking and mobile app](<https://www.lena.gov.ls/rsl-slb-launch-e-payment-integration>).
