# Senegal

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Wave is used to pay for transport tickets (BRT, TER) and at public health establishments, according to economist Seydou Bocoum](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>). [The DGID says SENTAX is its platform for online filing and payment (télépaiement) of taxes, mandatory for all declarations and payments of Large Enterprise Directorate taxpayers from 1 September 2026](<https://www.dgid.sn/sentax>). [The DGID's communique of 27 August 2026 says the SENTAX platform replaces ETAX for online payment (telepaiement) of taxes by Large Enterprises Directorate taxpayers](<https://www.dgid.sn/storage/uploads/communique-sentax-1790185473.pdf>). [The DGID announces that taxpayers can now pay all taxes, duties, fees and charges through Orange's MAX IT mobile app, under the menu 'FAY SAMA IMPOT'](<https://www.dgid.sn/articles/communique-lancement-du-paiement-des-impots-taxes-droits-et-redevances-sur-max-it-orange>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>).
