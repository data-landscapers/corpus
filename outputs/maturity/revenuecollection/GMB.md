# Gambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Deputy Accountant General says the government's digital payment system already links several commercial banks and other channels](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/accountant-general-seals-pact-with-sutura-to-strengthen-digital-revenue-collection>). [A July 2026 agreement added SUTURA for taxes, fees and other charges](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/accountant-general-seals-pact-with-sutura-to-strengthen-digital-revenue-collection>). [Fees such as rates, business registration, ID cards and passports can be paid through the Yonna Wallet](<https://standard.gm/govt-payments-now-digitalised-live-on-yonna-wallet-app>). [Court filing fees can be paid online through the judiciary's case system](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>). [In May 2024 the revenue authority signed for a new tax system with e-filing and e-payment](<https://standard.gm/gra-signs-agreement-to-improve-efficiency-transparency/>). [In October 2026 the finance ministry and the revenue authority signed a 10-year agreement with InTouch](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/finance/senegalese-fintech-intouch-signs-10-year-partnership-with-gambia-to-strengthen-tax-revenue-collection/>). [Its platform is to monitor transactions and process government payments through several channels](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/finance/senegalese-fintech-intouch-signs-10-year-partnership-with-gambia-to-strengthen-tax-revenue-collection/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** In October 2025 the revenue authority [presented its new tax system to stakeholders ahead of nationwide implementation](<https://standard.gm/gra-introduces-itas-system-to-stakeholders/>). It is to handle registration, filing and payment for income tax, VAT, customs and excise.
