# Gambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Point reports the Gambia's Deputy Accountant General said government has an integrated digital payment system already integrating several commercial banks and other channels, with digital payments making tax payments easier, and an MoU signed 2026-07-17 adds SUTURA for taxes, fees and other charges](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/accountant-general-seals-pact-with-sutura-to-strengthen-digital-revenue-collection>).
