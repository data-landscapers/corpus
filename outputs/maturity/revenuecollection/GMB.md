# Gambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In April 2026 [the Accountant General said citizens and businesses can pay taxes, fees and levies through GovPay](<https://standard.gm/aps-wallet-partners-accountant-generals-department-to-enhance-digital-revenue-collection/>), the government payment gateway that banks and microfinance companies had already joined. [APS Wallet was added that month](<https://foroyaa.net/aps-wallet-powers-the-gambias-digital-future-with-govpay-partnership/>). [A July 2026 agreement added SUTURA for taxes, fees and other charges](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/accountant-general-seals-pact-with-sutura-to-strengthen-digital-revenue-collection>). [Fees such as rates, business registration, ID cards and passports can be paid through the Yonna Wallet](<https://standard.gm/govt-payments-now-digitalised-live-on-yonna-wallet-app>). [Court filing fees can be paid online through the judiciary's case system](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>). [In October 2026 the finance ministry and the revenue authority signed a 10-year agreement with InTouch for a platform to process government payments](<https://gambiaj.com/economy/finance/senegalese-fintech-intouch-signs-10-year-partnership-with-gambia-to-strengthen-tax-revenue-collection/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** In October 2025 the revenue authority [presented its new tax system to stakeholders ahead of nationwide implementation](<https://standard.gm/gra-introduces-itas-system-to-stakeholders/>). It is to handle registration, filing and payment for income tax, VAT, customs and excise. [In June 2026 the system was still being introduced ahead of full rollout](<https://standard.gm/gra-hosts-workshop-on-itas/>).
