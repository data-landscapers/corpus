# Gambia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Point reports the Gambia's Deputy Accountant General said government has an integrated digital payment system already integrating several commercial banks and other channels, with digital payments making tax payments easier, and an MoU signed 2026-07-17 adds SUTURA for taxes, fees and other charges](<https://thepoint.gm/africa/gambia/headlines/accountant-general-seals-pact-with-sutura-to-strengthen-digital-revenue-collection>). [Under a partnership with the Accountant General's Department, government fees such as rates, business registration, ID cards and passports can be paid through Yonna Wallet, which the source says is live](<https://standard.gm/govt-payments-now-digitalised-live-on-yonna-wallet-app>). [The NHRC's 2024 report says the Judiciary's Case Management System includes e-payment for filing processes](<https://foroyaa.net/government-urged-to-expand-automated-transcription-system-in-courts/>). The Gambia Revenue Authority [signed an agreement in May 2024 for a new integrated tax administration system to bring e-registration, e-filing and e-payment](<https://standard.gm/gra-signs-agreement-to-improve-efficiency-transparency/>).
