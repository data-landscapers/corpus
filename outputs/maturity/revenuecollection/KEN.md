# Kenya

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Since a 17 November 2023 notice, stamp duty has been paid through Ardhipay on the Ardhisasa portal](<https://anoadvocates.com/insights/stamp-duty-payments-simplified-by-ardhipay>). [Payment is by M-PESA Paybill 222222 or bank RTGS](<https://anoadvocates.com/insights/stamp-duty-payments-simplified-by-ardhipay>). [In March 2024 The Star reported Nairobi County's e-JijiPay, run by JamboPay, collecting permits, market fees, cess and parking electronically](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/counties/nairobi/2024-03-22-how-fintechs-are-making-millions-from-counties-in-revenue-collection>). [Most counties had automated only a small share of revenue streams](<https://www.the-star.co.ke/counties/nairobi/2024-03-22-how-fintechs-are-making-millions-from-counties-in-revenue-collection>). [In July 2025 KRA gave taxpayers five more days to file annual returns and pay taxes after the iTax system crashed](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>). [On 27 October 2026 the Treasury's Principal Secretary told the National Assembly Finance Committee that the Treasury manages eCitizen, the government's digital payment platform](<https://www.tnx.africa/national/article/2001560974/national-treasury-pushes-new-regulations-plan-to-run-e-citizen>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 annual returns were filed and taxes paid through KRA's iTax system](<https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/kra-in-major-customs-income-tax-systems-upgrade-after-snags-5167392>).
