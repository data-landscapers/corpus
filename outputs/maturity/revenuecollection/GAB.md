# Gabon

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [L'Union says the city of Libreville already has its own operational system for digitalising municipal revenue, developed with the Treasury to secure and modernise collection](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). [CLIKPAY offers tax and duty payments among its services, according to UNCDF](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9193/unlocking-capital-for-african-fintech-beyond-the-big-four-a-story-from-gabon>). [Citizens can pay administrative fees ("frais administratifs") as well as purchases by scanning the GIMACPAY QR code with a Mobile Money application, according to GIMAC](<https://gimac-afr.com/2025/12/09/cemac-madigipaie-revolutionne-leconomie-numerique/>). [On 21 November 2025 the Gabonese government launched a project to digitize payments for e-visa applications (Ecofin Agency)](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/1112-51310-gabon-speeds-up-digital-payments-rollout-with-madigipaie-program>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Digitax Gabon](<http://gouvernement.ga/2025/08/18/etax-direction-generale-des-impots>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
