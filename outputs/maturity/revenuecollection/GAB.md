# Gabon

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [L'Union says the city of Libreville already has its own operational system for digitalising municipal revenue, developed with the Treasury to secure and modernise collection](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>). [CLIKPAY offers tax and duty payments among its services, according to UNCDF](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9193/unlocking-capital-for-african-fintech-beyond-the-big-four-a-story-from-gabon>). [Minister Doumba said the government is working to progressively end cash payments for administrative and judicial acts, through generalised Mobile Money and a single public administration portal](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>). [Citizens can pay administrative fees ("frais administratifs") as well as purchases by scanning the GIMACPAY QR code with a Mobile Money application, according to GIMAC](<https://gimac-afr.com/2025/12/09/cemac-madigipaie-revolutionne-leconomie-numerique/>). [Electronic payment was among the customs measures the economy minister reviewed on 23 January 2025](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-vers-une-modernisation-des-douanes-pour-faciliter-le-commerce-et-le-soutien-aux-pme/>).
