# Gabon

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** From October 2026 [eTax is part of Gabon Connect, and TrésorPay takes some non-tax revenue](<https://gabonmediatime.com/digitalisation-le-gouvernement-annonce-le-deploiement-progressif-de-gabon-connect/>), GabonMediaTime reports. Payment is by mobile money, card or transfer, with a secure receipt. [Libreville already runs its own system for municipal revenue, built with the Treasury](<https://union.sonapresse.com/fr/paiement-numerique-la-mairie-de-libreville-accompagne-le-deploiement-de-madigipaie>), L'Union says. [CLIKPAY offers tax and duty payments, according to UNCDF](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9193/unlocking-capital-for-african-fintech-beyond-the-big-four-a-story-from-gabon>). [Citizens can pay administrative fees by scanning the GIMACPAY QR code](<https://gimac-afr.com/2025/12/09/cemac-madigipaie-revolutionne-leconomie-numerique/>) with a mobile money app. Minister Doumba said [the government aims to end cash payments for administrative and judicial acts](<https://legabonais.com/bilan-des-100-jours-internet-cybersecurite-administration-numerique-marc-alexandre-doumba-deroule-la-strategie-digitale/>). [Electronic payment was among the customs measures reviewed on 23 January 2025](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-vers-une-modernisation-des-douanes-pour-faciliter-le-commerce-et-le-soutien-aux-pme/>).
