# Ethiopia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In January 2024 Addis Fortune reported the Ministry of Revenues extending its e-tax system](<https://addisfortune.news/ministry-embarks-on-e-tax-services>). [Late and advance payments, partial filing and audit findings were to go through the platform](<https://addisfortune.news/ministry-embarks-on-e-tax-services>). [In June 2024 Webb Fontaine said electronic payment had been introduced in the Webb Customs system](<https://webbfontaine.com/success-stories/ethiopia/customs-management-project>). [In May 2025 Directive 1069/2025 required federal public bodies to accept electronic payment of fees, charges, taxes and penalties within 90 days](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>). [They must also link their collection systems to authorised payment gateways](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>). [In July 2026 M-PESA Ethiopia launched a Tax Payment Mini App for the Amhara Bureau of Revenue](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/28/m-pesa-ethiopia-launches-digital-tax-payment-service-for-amhara-region/>). [Taxpayers pay by Bill ID, and other regional revenue authorities are planned to follow](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/28/m-pesa-ethiopia-launches-digital-tax-payment-service-for-amhara-region/>). [In August 2026 Addis Fortune reported a Unified Government Payment Platform piloted in the health sector](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-tests-a-payment-window-for-the-whole-government-starting-with-health>). [About 16 institutions were in the pipeline, with wider rollout targeted for 2029](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-tests-a-payment-window-for-the-whole-government-starting-with-health>). [In October 2026 EthSwitch presented the platform as a single route through the national switch for government bill payments](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/01/ethswitch-unified-government-payment-platform/>). [In November 2026 the government eServices portal said users can pay online for services](<https://www.eservices.gov.et/en>).
