# Ethiopia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In July 2026 Ethio telecom said taxpayers can pay the federal Ministry of Revenues and twelve regional and city revenue authorities through telebirr](<https://techtrends.africa/ethiopia-expands-mobile-tax-payments-nationwide-through-telebirr/>), by app or USSD. [The same month M-PESA Ethiopia launched a tax payment mini app for the Amhara Bureau of Revenue](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/28/m-pesa-ethiopia-launches-digital-tax-payment-service-for-amhara-region/>). [In June 2024 Webb Fontaine said electronic payment had been introduced in the Webb Customs system](<https://webbfontaine.com/success-stories/ethiopia/customs-management-project>). [In January 2024 Addis Fortune reported the Ministry of Revenues extending its e-tax system](<https://addisfortune.news/ministry-embarks-on-e-tax-services>). [Directive 1069/2025 of May 2025 required federal public bodies to accept electronic payment of fees, charges, taxes and penalties within 90 days](<https://www.mofed.gov.et/media/filer_public/7e/a5/7ea5bf66-de9b-447d-b34d-8ca9667ddf50/1069_2017_yamanegesete_maseriyaa_beetoce_faqaade_katasathaacawe_yakefeyaa_agalegelote_sachiwoce_kefeyaa_yamiqabalubatene.pdf>). [In October 2026 EthSwitch presented a Unified Government Payment Platform, a single route through the national switch for government bills](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/01/ethswitch-unified-government-payment-platform/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [in July 2026 taxpayers could pay the federal and twelve regional and city revenue authorities through telebirr](<https://techtrends.africa/ethiopia-expands-mobile-tax-payments-nationwide-through-telebirr/>). [Customs e-payment had been introduced by June 2024](<https://webbfontaine.com/success-stories/ethiopia/customs-management-project>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [in August 2026 about 16 institutions were in the pipeline for the unified payment platform](<https://addisfortune.news/fed-tests-a-payment-window-for-the-whole-government-starting-with-health>).
