# DR Congo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** Kinshasa's provincial revenue directorate [launched irms-dgrk.com on 5 March 2026](<https://deskeco.com/2026/03/06/kinshasa-la-dgrk-lance-la-plateforme-irms-dgrkcom-pour-la-declaration-et-le-paiement-des-impots-en>), where taxpayers file and pay provincial taxes online. Tshopo province [launched Veri-Gov on 21 August 2026](<https://www.opinion-info.cd/politique/2026/08/21/tshopo-le-gouvernement-lance-veri-gov-une-plateforme-pour-payer-les-impots-et>) for paying provincial taxes by mobile phone. Kinshasa also [said the Go Pass airport fee would be paid electronically from July 2026](<https://rdc-monde.com/aeroport-de-ndjili-le-paiement-electronique-du-go-pass-annonce-des-juillet/>). Nationally, the finance minister said in September 2026 that [VAT e-filing at the tax directorate lifted monthly VAT receipts](<https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux>) from about FC 280bn to over FC 320bn. [On 5 November 2026 the Ministry of Justice began moving court-fee collection to digital payment, with UNDP support and EU funds](<https://actualite.cd/2026/11/05/rdc-lue-et-le-pnud-accompagnent-la-digitalisation-de-la-justice-pour-renforcer-la>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact is [VAT e-filing at the tax directorate](<https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux>), reported in 2026.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact is the [rise in monthly VAT receipts after e-filing](<https://7sur7.cd/index.php/2026/09/03/rdc-les-recettes-mensuelles-de-la-tva-passent-de-1237-plus-de-1414-millions-grace-aux>), which does not say how much was paid electronically.
