## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [DGI Director General Younes Idrissi Kaitouni says SMEs file simple corporate tax and VAT declarations online through the EFI electronic form exchange on the DGI website, and microenterprises submit simplified VAT and corporate tax declarations electronically](<https://medias24.com/2026/04/17/dgi-electronic-invoicing-rollout-in-preparation-details-from-younes-idrissi-kaitouni-1661609/>). [CMI says Fatourati is the reference platform for multichannel settlement of bills, taxes and duties in Morocco, used by over 100 administrations and billers](<https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/fatourati-collect-le-cmi-ouvre-un-acces-simple-et-immediat-au-reglement-multicanal>). [The DGI says its Simpl teleservices let taxpayers declare and pay taxes electronically (telepayer)](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/e-services-la-dgi-etoffe-son-systeme-simpl>).
