# Morocco

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [DGI Director General Younes Idrissi Kaitouni says SMEs file simple corporate tax and VAT declarations online through the EFI electronic form exchange on the DGI website](<https://medias24.com/2026/04/17/dgi-electronic-invoicing-rollout-in-preparation-details-from-younes-idrissi-kaitouni-1661609/>). Microenterprises submit simplified VAT and corporate tax declarations electronically. [The Ministry of Justice says lawyers can pay judicial fees electronically through its Mahakim exchange platform](<https://mahakim.ma/>). Professionals pay property-agency registration fees online. [CMI says Fatourati is the reference platform for multichannel settlement of bills, taxes and duties in Morocco, used by over 100 administrations and billers](<https://www.cmi.co.ma/fr/nos-actualites/fatourati-collect-le-cmi-ouvre-un-acces-simple-et-immediat-au-reglement-multicanal>). [The DGI says its Simpl teleservices let taxpayers declare and pay taxes electronically (telepayer)](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/e-services-la-dgi-etoffe-son-systeme-simpl>). [It counted 12,304,943 tele-payments in 2025](<https://fr.le360.ma/economie/impots-914-des-recettes-fiscales-encaissees-par-telepaiement-en-2025_UYH2TYM6ENA5HCQIPRGMWZML44/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact found: [the DGI's 2025 activity report puts tax revenue paid by tele-payment at 91.4%](<https://fr.le360.ma/economie/impots-914-des-recettes-fiscales-encaissees-par-telepaiement-en-2025_UYH2TYM6ENA5HCQIPRGMWZML44/>), without naming customs duties.

**Share reported.** [The DGI's 2025 activity report, as le360 reports it, puts the share of tax revenue paid by tele-payment at 91.4% in 2025, up from 91.2% in 2024](<https://fr.le360.ma/economie/impots-914-des-recettes-fiscales-encaissees-par-telepaiement-en-2025_UYH2TYM6ENA5HCQIPRGMWZML44/>). [The DGI's figure for 2023 was 91.1%](<https://aujourdhui.ma/economie/e-services-la-dgi-etoffe-son-systeme-simpl>).
