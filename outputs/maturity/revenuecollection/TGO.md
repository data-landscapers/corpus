# Togo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Ministry of Finance and Budget officially launched e-TPU on 17 September 2026, a platform for online filing and electronic payment of the Taxe Professionnelle Unique, including via mobile money (Flooz and Mixx by Yas), with an electronic receipt, available 24/7](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/1619-la-plateforme-de-declaration-et-de-paiement-en-ligne-de-la-taxe-professionnelle-unique-e-tpu-officiellement-lancee.html>). [Togo's justice ministry launched on 2 July 2026 online payment of fees for nearly forty services of the Tribunal de Grande Instance de Lomé](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>). [Administrative fees for company registration are paid online via the CFE site, by Flooz, Tmoney, Mastercard or Visa, and cites electronic payment of public services as part of the digital strategy](<https://full-news.tg/promotion-infrastructures-publiques-numeriques-cfetogo-tg-plateforme-cle-entrepreneurs/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [OTR publishes electronic declaration and payment rates by directorate for 2020 to 2025, with telepaiement at 24% for DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR in 2025, up from 13% in 2024](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).
