# Togo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 17 September 2026 the Ministry of Finance and Budget launched e-TPU for online filing and electronic payment of the Taxe Professionnelle Unique](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/1619-la-plateforme-de-declaration-et-de-paiement-en-ligne-de-la-taxe-professionnelle-unique-e-tpu-officiellement-lancee.html>). [It takes mobile money through Flooz and Mixx by Yas and issues an electronic receipt](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/en/1619-la-plateforme-de-declaration-et-de-paiement-en-ligne-de-la-taxe-professionnelle-unique-e-tpu-officiellement-lancee.html>). [Since December 2023 the OTR TVM app and web platform have taken the motor vehicle tax by Flooz or Tmoney](<https://afreepress.tg/otr-la-creation-du-numero-dimmatriculation-fiscale-et-le-paiement-de-la-tvm-desormais-digitalises/>). [Togo's justice ministry launched on 2 July 2026 online payment of fees for nearly forty services of the Tribunal de Grande Instance de Lomé](<https://justice.gouv.tg/les-services-du-tribunal-de-grande-instance-de-lome-passent-en-mode-paiement-numerique/>). [Administrative fees for company registration are paid online via the CFE site](<https://full-news.tg/promotion-infrastructures-publiques-numeriques-cfetogo-tg-plateforme-cle-entrepreneurs/>). Payment is by Flooz, Tmoney, Mastercard or Visa.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [OTR publishes electronic declaration and payment rates by directorate for 2020 to 2025, with telepaiement at 24% for DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR in 2025, up from 13% in 2024](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).

**Share reported.** [OTR publishes electronic declaration and payment rates by directorate for 2020 to 2025, with telepaiement at 24% for DCIG/DOFAN/DOFR in 2025, up from 13% in 2024](<https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/statistiques-performances/teledeclaration-et-telepaiement.html>).
