# Rwanda

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In October 2026 KT Press reported on the Rwanda Governance Scorecard. It said [citizens pay fees through online platforms such as Irembo](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>), the national services portal. Payment runs [through Mobile Money and Airtel Money](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>). The report names fees for public services, not the main taxes.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact is the October 2026 report that [fees for public services are paid online through Irembo](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>). It says nothing of income tax, VAT or customs.

**Share reported.** No source since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact is again KT Press: [fees paid through Irembo by mobile money](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/10/why-rwanda-is-unhappy-with-only-11-of-government-services-fully-digitised/>), with no amount or share given.

***Also on file***. [eKash](<https://african.business/2026/07/innov-africa-deals/rwanda-unifies-digital-payments-with-national-launch-of-ekash>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
