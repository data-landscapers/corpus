# Namibia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [NamRA says domestic taxes are paid by electronic fund transfer to its Bank of Namibia accounts after liabilities are confirmed on the ITAS portal](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/namra-communique-wagon--newsletterjanuary--march-99188d048f.pdf>). [Customs payments can also be made by EFT, alongside cash and card at its offices](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/namra-communique-wagon--newsletterjanuary--march-99188d048f.pdf>). [In October 2019 Finance Minister Schlettwein said the Integrated Tax Administration System became operational in January 2019 with online filing of tax returns](<https://mfpe.gov.na/documents/76368/1282874/Media+Statement+on+Tax+and+Procument+Matters+by+Hon++Schlettwein.pdf/a5c442d2-9138-130a-ec26-06c45d0ccc21?download=true&t=1667295738513>). [The ministry targeted 90% of taxpayers registered as e-filers by June 2020](<https://mfpe.gov.na/documents/76368/1282874/Media+Statement+on+Tax+and+Procument+Matters+by+Hon++Schlettwein.pdf/a5c442d2-9138-130a-ec26-06c45d0ccc21?download=true&t=1667295738513>).
