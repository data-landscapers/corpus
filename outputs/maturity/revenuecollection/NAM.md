# Namibia

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [NamRA says domestic taxes are paid by electronic fund transfer to its Bank of Namibia accounts after liabilities are confirmed on the ITAS portal, and customs payments can also be made by EFT, alongside cash and card at its offices](<https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/namra-communique-wagon--newsletterjanuary--march-99188d048f.pdf>). [Finance Minister Schlettwein says the Integrated Tax Administration System became operational in January 2019 with online filing of tax returns, and the Ministry targets 90% of taxpayers registered as e-filers by June 2020 (2019, before the three-year window)](<https://mfpe.gov.na/documents/76368/1282874/Media+Statement+on+Tax+and+Procument+Matters+by+Hon++Schlettwein.pdf/a5c442d2-9138-130a-ec26-06c45d0ccc21?download=true&t=1667295738513>).
