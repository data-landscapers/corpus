# Central African Republic

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Orange Centrafrique, Webb Fontaine and NGSER launched automated payment of the DPI (prior import declaration) customs fee through Orange Money, with receipts passed in real time to the SYDONIA customs system](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>). [Agence Ecofin reports that the Central African tax administration launched the e-Tax platform on 24 March 2025 for online payment of taxes, with online tax information, declaration and payment, in four modules including registration for the unique taxpayer identification number (NIU)](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [The Ministry of Finance's FY2026 draft budget proposes XAF 3,000,000 to operationalise electronic payment of duties and taxes](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf>).
