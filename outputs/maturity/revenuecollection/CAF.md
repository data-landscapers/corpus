# Central African Republic

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [On 24 March 2025 the tax administration launched e-Tax for online tax information, declaration and payment, per Agence Ecofin](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-numerique/2503-126985-la-centrafrique-lance-officiellement-e-tax-sa-plateforme-de-paiement-d-impots-en-ligne>). [In August 2025 the finance minister said taxes are to be paid online through e-Tax](<https://ecomatin.net/fiscalite-bangui-menace-de-sanctionner-les-grandes-entreprises-qui-boudent-la-plateforme-e-tax>). Over half of the large taxpayers concerned did not yet use it. [Orange Centrafrique, Webb Fontaine and NGSER launched payment of the DPI customs fee through Orange Money](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>). Receipts pass in real time to the SYDONIA customs system. [In January 2025 the finance ministry and bank heads discussed launching digital payment of customs duties once Sydonia World was linked to the banks](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/rca-la-digitalisation-des-paiements-des-taxes-et-impots-simpose/>). [The FY2026 draft budget proposes XAF 3,000,000 to operationalise electronic payment of duties and taxes](<http://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2026-03/Cadre%20des%20D%C3%A9penses%20%C3%A0%20Moyen%20Terme%202026.pdf>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact: [since 22 March 2025 all tax declaration and payment operations are meant to go through e-Tax](<https://ecomatin.net/centrafrique-malgre-la-mauvaise-connectivite-du-pays-les-autorites-optent-pour-le-paiement-des-impots-en-ligne>). The finance minister says so.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in July 2026 receipts for that fee passed in real time to the SYDONIA customs system](<https://investirencentrafrique.com/2026/07/03/orange-centrafrique-et-webb-fontaine-lancent-le-paiement-automatise-de-la-redevance-dpi-via-orange-money/>).
