# Cameroon

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Ministry of Finance's Tresor Pay lets citizens pay non-tax revenues online by mobile money, bank card or Campost counters, and a Prime Minister's order of 28 May 2025 requires departments to use it exclusively within three months](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-mandates-digital-payment-platform-for-non-tax-revenues>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY enables tax payments and e-government services, and the treasury of Cameroon is the only treasury connected to date](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [Licensed payment institution Konoom Cameroun plans to let users pay taxes, levies and customs duties from a phone once Konoom Mobile Money launches in the coming months, per Agence Ecofin](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1709-141667-cameroun-konoom-obtient-son-agrement-et-se-lance-sur-le-marche-du-paiement-mobile>).
