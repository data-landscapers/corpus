# Cameroon

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** The finance ministry's [Tresor Pay takes non-tax revenue online by mobile money, bank card or Campost counters](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/cameroon-mandates-digital-payment-platform-for-non-tax-revenues>). A Prime Minister's order of 28 May 2025 requires departments to use it alone within three months. In May 2026 [taxpayers at a Yaoundé tax centre could pay tax notices through Tresor Pay or by MTN MoMo and Orange Money](<https://www.digitalbusiness.africa/cameroun-la-dgi-simplifie-le-paiement-des-avis-dimposition-via-mtn-momo-orange-money-otp-et-tresor-pay/>), with receipts printed at once. AfricaNenda says [GIMACPAY takes tax payments, with Cameroon's treasury the only one connected](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). The payment institution Konoom [plans to let users pay taxes and customs duties by phone](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1709-141667-cameroun-konoom-obtient-son-agrement-et-se-lance-sur-le-marche-du-paiement-mobile>), Agence Ecofin reports.

**Main taxes and duties.** In June 2026 an editorial said [the tax directorate has for several years taken tax declarations and payments online](<https://actucameroun.com/2026/06/02/recouvrement-des-impots-locaux-le-coup-de-pouce-salutaire-de-ladministration-fiscale/>). These now include the local taxes unified as the Impôt Général Synthétique. In March 2026 Visa's country manager said [customs duties paid with the GUCE x BGFIBank Business Card are now paid fully digitally](<https://haurizonnews.com/vers-une-douane-sans-cash-visa-bgfibank-le-guce-et-trustpayway-misent-sur-la-digitalisation-du-paiement>). They pass through the GUCE single window.
