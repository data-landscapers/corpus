# Malawi

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Malawi Police Service introduced an electronic payment system for traffic fines, scheduled to become operational at Police Headquarters and the six police regions from the week of 4 September 2026, payable through commercial banks](<https://itweb.africa/article/malawi-police-digitises-traffic-fines/JBwEr7n3lrzM6Db2>). [UNCTAD's ASYCUDA Programme says the ASYCUDAWorld 4.3.3 upgrade aims at implementing e-payment for collection of Malawi Bureau of Standards fees](<https://asycuda.org/en/asycudaworld-upgrade-in-malawi/>). [In September 2024 the E-Government Director said the ministry had piloted moving non-tax revenue collection agencies to e-payment, starting with the National Registration Bureau](<https://mbc.mw/govt-advocates-for-improved-digital-service-delivery/>).
