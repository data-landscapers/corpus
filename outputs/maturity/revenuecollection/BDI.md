# Burundi

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** The OBR says [taxes have been declared and paid online through the Umutangakori portal since August 2023](<https://abpinfo.bi/2023/12/01/lancement-officiel-des-activites-de-celebration-de-la-8eme-edition-de-la-journee-dediee-aux-contribuables/>), with receipts issued electronically. [From 6 August 2025 small and micro taxpayers could pay taxes and non-tax revenue by e-money through CASHTEL agents](<https://www.obr.bi/index.php/actualites/2310-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-via-la-plateforme-cashtel>). [On 19 January 2026 the OBR launched e-KORI for online tax filing and payment of taxes and fees](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/burundi-modernizes-tax-and-non-tax-revenue-collection-with-ekori-launch>). [In June 2026 the finance and digital economy minister said E-Kori already allows online filing and payment for some taxpayers](<https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
