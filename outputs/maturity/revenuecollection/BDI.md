# Burundi

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Burundi's finance and digital economy minister said the E-Kori tax collection platform, supported by the World Bank-funded PAFEN project, already allows online payment and online filing for some taxpayers](<https://burundi-eco.com/le-gouvernement-accelere-t-il-la-digitalisation-des-services-publics/>). [The OBR launched the e-KORI platform for online tax filing and the payment of taxes and fees on 19 January 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/01/22/burundi-modernizes-tax-and-non-tax-revenue-collection-with-ekori-launch>). [The OBR launched the implementation phase of E-KORI, a platform for online declaration and payment of taxes and duties, which will let taxpayers pay remotely once fully operational, per the Finance Ministry](<https://www.burunditimes.com/burundi-revenue-authority-rolls-out-e-kori-digital-tax-platform/>).
