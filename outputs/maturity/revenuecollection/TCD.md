# Chad

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Tchadinfos says digitalisation of public revenue limits fraud and speeds the entry of funds into the State's coffers](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [The Director General of Taxes said that through the E-Tax platform, launched in 2022, anyone can declare and pay taxes online, with banks and mobile operators facilitating electronic payments](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [Le N'Djam Post reports that under the 2026 finance law 3,000 electronic payment terminals have been deployed in the financial agencies (régies financières), and that télépaiement is expected to become general for taxes and duties](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/>). [The 2027 budget orientations say tax, customs and Treasury systems are to be integrated with electronic payment solutions](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/>).
