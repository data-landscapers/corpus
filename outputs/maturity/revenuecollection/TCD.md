# Chad

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Tchadinfos says digitalisation of public revenue limits fraud and speeds the entry of funds into the State's coffers](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>). [The Director General of Taxes said that through the E-Tax platform, launched in 2022, anyone can declare and pay taxes online, with banks and mobile operators facilitating electronic payments](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [Le N'Djam Post reports that under the 2026 finance law 3,000 electronic payment terminals have been deployed in the financial agencies (régies financières), and that télépaiement is expected to become general for taxes and duties](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/>). [The 2027 budget orientations say tax, customs and Treasury systems are to be integrated with electronic payment solutions](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2027-le-tchad-mise-sur-le-numerique-pour-verrouiller-les-finances-publiques/>). [The customs e-payment system launched on 21 February 2024 lets all customs duties and taxes be paid by SYDONIA World tele-payment, bank transfer or mobile money](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** The tax directorate says [E-Tax lets taxpayers pay all the taxes and duties they owe online](<https://tchadinfos.com/2024/03/20/vulgarisation-de-e-tax-la-direction-des-impots-echange-avec-les-experts-comptables-et-les-conseillers-fiscaux/>), [and declare them](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>), and customs says [all customs duties and taxes can be paid electronically since February 2024](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://tchadinfos.com/2026/09/04/monnaie-electronique-entre-engouement-frais-eleves-et-espoir-de-concurrence/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Système intégré de gestion des finances publiques (SIGFIP)](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/>); [Moov Money](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-lintegration-du-paiement-mobile-des-services-publics/>); [Airtel Money](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-lintegration-du-paiement-mobile-des-services-publics/>); [Konoom](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-lintegration-du-paiement-mobile-des-services-publics/>); [Cashi Tchad](<https://journaldutchad.com/tchad-le-gouvernement-accelere-lintegration-du-paiement-mobile-des-services-publics/>); [SIGFIP](<https://lendjampost.com/budget-2026-au-tchad-zero-cash-zero-papier-zero-facture-fantome/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically.
