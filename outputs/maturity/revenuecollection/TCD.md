# Chad

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In 2025 the tax directorate said its E-Tax platform, launched in 2022, lets anyone declare and pay taxes online](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [Banks and mobile operators carry the payments](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [Since 21 February 2024 customs duties and taxes can be paid by SYDONIA World, bank transfer or mobile money](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>). [In February 2026 the finance ministry signed a five-year convention with mobile money operators](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/tchad-letat-sallie-aux-operateurs-mobile-money-pour-encaisser-les-impots-et-payer-les-salaires_59931>). [It lets taxpayers pay taxes, customs duties and public fees by mobile money](<https://www.sikafinance.com/marches/tchad-letat-sallie-aux-operateurs-mobile-money-pour-encaisser-les-impots-et-payer-les-salaires_59931>). [Under the 2026 finance law 3,000 electronic payment terminals were deployed in the revenue agencies](<https://lendjampost.com/tchad-la-digitalisation-de-ladministration-face-au-defi-de-lelectricite-et-de-la-connectivite/>), Le N'Djam Post reports.

**Main taxes and duties.** [In 2024 the tax directorate said E-Tax lets taxpayers pay all the taxes and duties they owe online](<https://tchadinfos.com/2024/03/20/vulgarisation-de-e-tax-la-direction-des-impots-echange-avec-les-experts-comptables-et-les-conseillers-fiscaux/>). [Taxpayers also declare them there](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>). [Customs says all customs duties and taxes can be paid electronically since February 2024](<https://douanes.td/e-douane/e-paiement/>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [E-Tax lets anyone declare and pay taxes online](<https://tchadinfos.com/2025/08/29/la-direction-generale-des-impots-lance-une-campagne-nationale-de-sensibilisation-sur-le-civisme-fiscal-et-la-plateforme-e-tax/>).
