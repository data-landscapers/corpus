# Mauritania

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The finance ministry, through the DGI, announced that from 1 November 2026 public establishments must use an electronic platform](<https://fr.tawassoul.net/mauritanie-les-etablissements-publics-passeront-a-la-facturation-et-aux-paiements-electroniques-des-novembre/>). The platform is for invoicing, tax declarations and payment of taxes, duties and withholdings. The DGI was to give training in October. [The Direction Générale des Impôts says its STT teledeclaration and telepayment system lets taxpayers declare and pay taxes online](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). It also lets them add payment means. [The finance minister says the Espace du contribuable platform lets taxpayers make declarations and tax procedures and pay electronically at a distance](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/300004>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact found: [in January 2026 the finance ministry said taxpayers can declare remotely and pay all taxes without exception through bank apps and approved e-wallets](<https://www.rimtoday.net/?q=node%2F49170>). It does not name customs duties.

**Share reported.** [The finance minister said more than 500 taxpayers joined the DGI electronic platform](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/265398>). [It contributed 36.45% of DGI revenue during 2026](<https://www.ami.mr/archives/265398>). [A second report gives the same count and says the platform contributed to collecting 36.45% of the directorate's revenue in 2026](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/300004>).
