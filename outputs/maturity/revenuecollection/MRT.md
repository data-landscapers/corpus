# Mauritania

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The finance ministry, through the DGI, announced that from 1 November 2026 public establishments must use an electronic platform for invoicing, tax declarations and payment of taxes, duties and withholdings, with DGI training in October](<https://fr.tawassoul.net/mauritanie-les-etablissements-publics-passeront-a-la-facturation-et-aux-paiements-electroniques-des-novembre/>). [The Direction Générale des Impôts says its STT teledeclaration and telepayment system lets taxpayers declare and pay taxes online and add payment means](<https://impots.gov.mr/DGI/teleservices.html>). [The finance minister says the Espace du contribuable platform lets taxpayers make declarations and tax procedures and pay electronically at a distance](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/300004>).
