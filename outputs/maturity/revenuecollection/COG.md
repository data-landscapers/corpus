# Congo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Since 3 August 2026 Congolese taxpayers can pay taxes, levies and customs duties online through the Fouta national switch after filing on the E-Tax or Sydonia platforms, with an electronic Treasury receipt issued, according to L'Economie](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). [Arrete no. 1009 of 22 May 2026 makes payment of public revenue exclusively electronic through the FOUTA platform, described as a national switch for tax and customs collection, with a 60-day compliance period](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).
