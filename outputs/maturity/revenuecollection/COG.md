# Congo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Since 3 August 2026 taxpayers can pay taxes, levies and customs duties online through the Fouta national switch, L'Economie reports](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). They file first on the E-Tax or Sydonia platforms and receive an electronic Treasury receipt. [Arrêté no. 1009 of 22 May 2026 makes payment of public revenue exclusively electronic through Fouta](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf>), with 60 days to comply. [In August 2024 the French Treasury said platforms built with the mobile operators took payment of utility bills and land transport taxes](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 Fouta online payment was being rolled out progressively](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). [It was to reach all taxpayers by the end of 2026](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [since 3 August 2026 the Fouta national switch issues an electronic Treasury receipt for taxes paid online](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).
