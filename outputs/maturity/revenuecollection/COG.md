# Congo

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Arrêté no. 1009 of 22 May 2026 makes payment of public revenue exclusively electronic through the Fouta platform](<https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-24-2.pdf>). It allows 60 days to comply. [On 23 June 2026 the finance ministry showed Fouta to the National Assembly's finance committee](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/execution-du-budget-de-letat-les-deputes-simpregnent-du-fonctionnement-de-fouta-170602>). Tax and customs filings are paid through three banks, with receipts sent by email. [In August 2024 the French Treasury said platforms built with the mobile operators took payment of utility bills and land transport taxes](<https://www.tresor.economie.gouv.fr/Pays/CG/le-secteur-du-numerique>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [Since 3 August 2026 taxpayers file on the E-Tax or Sydonia platforms and pay taxes, levies and customs duties online through Fouta](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>), L'Economie reports. [The rollout is to reach all taxpayers by the end of 2026](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>). MTN Congo's page says [taxpayers who file on impots.gouv.cg can pay e-tax and e-douane bills by MTN Mobile Money](<https://www.mtn.cg/momo/particuliers/e-pay-for-e-tax-e-douane/>), free of charge.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [since 3 August 2026 the Fouta national switch issues an electronic Treasury receipt for taxes paid online](<https://leconomie.info/congo-le-paiement-des-impots-et-taxes-bascule-en-ligne/>).
