# Libya

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Libyan prime minister said the government has adopted electronic payment as the official means of collecting government fees and dues](<https://alwasat.ly/news/libya/521750>). [On 4 December 2025 the finance ministry told customs and tax offices to take amounts over 500 dinars only by certified cheque or electronic payment](<https://sada.ly/en/electronic-payment-500lyd/>). [A decree by Prime Minister Aldabaiba made e-payment an approved method for collecting state revenues and public dues](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/03/aldabaiba-government-and-cbl-take-further-actions-to-encourage-increased-e-payments-use-cbl-reduces-new-fx-bureaux-commission>). Cash and cheques remain approved alongside it. [The finance ministry said the Unified Tax System implements Decision No. 448 of 2025 on tax e-payment and e-collection](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=346934&lang=ar>). Its digital platforms are to launch in 2026.
