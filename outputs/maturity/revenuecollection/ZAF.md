# South Africa

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [SARS says Customs and Excise clients declaring goods electronically must use the SARS eFiling service to make payments, and Personal Income Tax and Travellers' payments can be made on the SARS MobiApp](<https://www.sars.gov.za/guide-to-sars-payment-rules/>). [BusinessTech reports SARS says taxpayers pay through eFiling or EFT to public beneficiary IDs covering customs, excise, income tax, PAYE, provisional tax, VAT and dividends tax, and that Customs and Excise clients declaring goods electronically must use eFiling to pay (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://businesstech.co.za/news/finance/718788/sars-updates-tax-payment-rules-changes-for-banks-in-south-africa/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [BusinessTech reports SARS says taxpayers pay through eFiling or EFT to public beneficiary IDs covering customs, excise, income tax, PAYE, provisional tax, VAT and dividends tax, and that Customs and Excise clients declaring goods electronically must use eFiling to pay (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://businesstech.co.za/news/finance/718788/sars-updates-tax-payment-rules-changes-for-banks-in-south-africa/>).
