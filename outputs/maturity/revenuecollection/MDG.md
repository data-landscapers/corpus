# Madagascar

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says it launched SIGRNF on 9 May 2025](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1642>). It lets users pay non-tax government revenues 24/7 via mobile banking, with progressive nationwide rollout. [Madagascar's tax administration steering committee announced that the SAFI integrated tax platform was tested and ready for deployment from the end of September 2025](<https://new.moov.mg/actualite/106644-modernisation-fiscale-la-plateforme-safi-prete-pour-la-mise-en-production>). SAFI covers online tax filing (télédéclaration) and diversified payments. [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says the SAFI integrated tax administration system, started in October 2024, will bring e-Declaration, mandatory tele-declaration and e-Payment to the tax directorate](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1491>). It targets full deployment in all tax centres by January 2026. In September 2025 the finance minister said [the e-Hetra online tax filing and payment system has been extended nationwide](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-rindra-rabarinirinarison-minister-of-economy-and-finance-of-madagascar>). The minister said SAFI takes online tax payments at any hour. [SAFI has been in production since September 2025](<https://cabinet-fenitra.mg/actualites/safi-cap-sur-le-deploiement-effectif-a-madagascar/>).
