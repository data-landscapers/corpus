# Madagascar

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says it launched SIGRNF on 9 May 2025, letting users pay non-tax government revenues 24/7 via mobile banking, with progressive nationwide rollout](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1642>). [Madagascar's tax administration steering committee announced that the SAFI integrated tax platform, covering online tax filing (télédéclaration) and diversified payments, was tested and ready for deployment from the end of September 2025](<https://new.moov.mg/actualite/106644-modernisation-fiscale-la-plateforme-safi-prete-pour-la-mise-en-production>). [The Ministry of Economy and Finance says the SAFI integrated tax administration system, started in October 2024, will bring e-Declaration, mandatory tele-declaration and e-Payment to the tax directorate, targeting full deployment in all tax centres by January 2026](<http://central.mef.gov.mg/page_personnalisee/index/news/1491>). The finance minister says [the e-Hetra online tax filing and payment system has been extended nationwide, and SAFI takes online tax payments at any hour](<https://kaori-media.com/posts/interview-with-hon-rindra-rabarinirinarison-minister-of-economy-and-finance-of-madagascar>), in September 2025; [SAFI has been in production since September 2025](<https://cabinet-fenitra.mg/actualites/safi-cap-sur-le-deploiement-effectif-a-madagascar/>).
