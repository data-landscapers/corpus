# Egypt

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said real-estate transaction tax can be paid electronically with bank cards or direct-debit cards through the Tax Authority's Real Estate Transactions mobile app](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said the Tax Authority's Real Estate Transactions app lets citizens register transactions, upload documents and pay taxes online, with payment by electronic methods, banks and bank cards, and InstaPay planned for a later stage](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-launches-new-tax-services-center-real-estate-transactions-app-as-part-of-tax-relief-drive/>). [The Meeza card has become an essential tool for many government payments and official services such as traffic and the Real Estate Registry, per banking expert Hisham Hamza](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8325979>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>).
