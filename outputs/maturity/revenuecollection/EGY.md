# Egypt

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said real-estate transaction tax can be paid electronically with bank cards or direct-debit cards through the Tax Authority's Real Estate Transactions mobile app](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said the Tax Authority's Real Estate Transactions app lets citizens register transactions, upload documents and pay taxes online, with payment by electronic methods, banks and bank cards, and InstaPay planned for a later stage](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-launches-new-tax-services-center-real-estate-transactions-app-as-part-of-tax-relief-drive/>). [The Meeza card has become an essential tool for many government payments and official services such as traffic and the Real Estate Registry, per banking expert Hisham Hamza](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8325979>). A deputy finance minister told the cabinet in May 2025 that [electronic payment is available within the customs system](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5174016.aspx>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [Minister Maait says electronic customs collections reached EGP 284.3 billion and electronic tax collection transactions about EGP 613.1 billion in July 2023 to May 2024](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>).

**Share reported.** [The Ministry of Finance gives EGP 2.5 trillion of e-payment and collection for July 2023 to May 2024, with electronic tax collection of EGP 613.1 billion and customs collection of EGP 284.3 billion](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>). The cabinet reports the finance minister's figures for [electronic customs receipts of EGP 284.3 billion and electronic tax collection of EGP 613.1 billion in July 2023 to May 2024](<https://www.cabinet.gov.eg/News/Details/76152>).
