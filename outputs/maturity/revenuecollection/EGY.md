# Egypt

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** In August 2026 Finance Minister Ahmed Kouchouk said [real-estate transaction tax can be paid by bank card through the Tax Authority's Real Estate Transactions app](<https://www.fintechgate.net/250244>). [InstaPay is planned for a later stage](<https://sis.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/egypt-launches-new-tax-services-center-real-estate-transactions-app-as-part-of-tax-relief-drive/>). [The Meeza card has become an essential tool for many government payments, such as traffic and the Real Estate Registry, per banking expert Hisham Hamza](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8325979>). A deputy finance minister told the cabinet in May 2025 that [electronic payment is available within the customs system](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5174016.aspx>). [In November 2026 Orange Egypt said guardians can pay family-prosecution fees through Max it and Orange Cash](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/11/14/%D8%A3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%86%DA%86-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B8%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AE%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D9%86-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B0%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D8%B7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AD%D9%84%D8%A9%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%86/7618130>).

**Main taxes and duties.** [Minister Maait said electronic customs collections reached EGP 284.3 billion in July 2023 to May 2024](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>). Electronic tax collection was about EGP 613.1 billion in the same period. [From May 2019 to November 2023 EGP 1.9 trillion of taxes was collected electronically](<https://dailynewsegypt.com/2024/01/04/e-payment-collection-services-exceed-egp-8-1trn-in-egypt-finance-minister/>). In the same period EGP 829.5 billion of customs revenue was paid through the Nafeza platform.

**Share reported.** [The Ministry of Finance gives EGP 2.5 trillion of e-payment and collection for July 2023 to May 2024](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/525882/Business/Economy/Epayment,-collection-services-in-Egypt-rise-to-EGP.aspx>). Electronic tax collection was EGP 613.1 billion and customs collection EGP 284.3 billion. [In January 2024 it put e-payment and collection since May 2019 at EGP 8.1 trillion in 607.3 million transactions](<https://dailynewsegypt.com/2024/01/04/e-payment-collection-services-exceed-egp-8-1trn-in-egypt-finance-minister/>).
