# Mauritius

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority states that VAT charged or collected by foreign digital-service suppliers must be remitted electronically to the MRA, and lists online VAT registration, electronic submission of VAT returns and electronic payment of VAT due as available facilities from 1 January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [The Mauritius Judiciary says registered online users of the e-Judiciary system can top up their account, used for court filing fees, by credit card (module implemented 28 February 2022) and by MauCAS QR code or account transfer (from 23 October 2023)](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically.
