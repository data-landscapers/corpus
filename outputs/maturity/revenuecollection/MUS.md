# Mauritius

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority states that foreign digital-service suppliers must remit VAT electronically to the MRA](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [It lists online VAT registration, electronic VAT returns and electronic VAT payment as available from 1 January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [The Mauritius Judiciary says registered e-Judiciary users can top up their account by credit card from 28 February 2022](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). [The top-up pays court filing fees](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). [Top-ups by MauCAS QR code or account transfer followed from 23 October 2023](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). Mauritius Network Services says [commercial banks are linked to the TradeNet portal for electronic payment of duties and taxes](<https://tradenetmns.mu/WEBVEHICLE/Index>). [It dates a notice on electronic payment instructions to March 2025](<https://tradenetmns.mu/WEBVEHICLE/Index>).

**Main taxes and duties.** The Revenue Authority [ran its 2025 e-filing season for individual income tax returns](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>), [VAT returns from foreign digital suppliers are filed and paid electronically from January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>), and [customs duties and taxes are paid electronically through TradeNet](<https://tradenetmns.mu/WEBVEHICLE/Index>), in March 2025.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact: [from 1 January 2026 the revenue authority lists electronic payment of VAT due among its facilities](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).
