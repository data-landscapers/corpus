# Mauritius

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority states that VAT charged or collected by foreign digital-service suppliers must be remitted electronically to the MRA, and lists online VAT registration, electronic submission of VAT returns and electronic payment of VAT due as available facilities from 1 January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [The Mauritius Judiciary says registered online users of the e-Judiciary system can top up their account, used for court filing fees, by credit card (module implemented 28 February 2022) and by MauCAS QR code or account transfer (from 23 October 2023)](<https://ejudiciary.govmu.org/>). Mauritius Network Services says [commercial banks are linked to the TradeNet portal for electronic payment of duties and taxes](<https://tradenetmns.mu/WEBVEHICLE/Index>), with a March 2025 notice on electronic payment instructions.

**Main taxes and duties.** The Revenue Authority [ran its 2025 e-filing season for individual income tax returns](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>), [VAT returns from foreign digital suppliers are filed and paid electronically from January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>), and [customs duties and taxes are paid electronically through TradeNet](<https://tradenetmns.mu/WEBVEHICLE/Index>), in March 2025.

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically.
