# Mauritius

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [The Mauritius Revenue Authority states that VAT charged or collected by foreign digital-service suppliers must be remitted electronically to the MRA, and lists online VAT registration, electronic submission of VAT returns and electronic payment of VAT due as available facilities from 1 January 2026](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>). [The Mauritius Revenue Authority announces the Individual Income Tax Returns e-Filing Season 2025, in which taxpayers file returns electronically with a User Id (NID, NCID or TAN) and password and pay any tax due by 15 October 2025](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/individual-e-filing-season>).

**Main taxes and duties.** No source held since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

**Share reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/vat-eservices/vat-on-digital-or-electronic-services-from-foreign-suppliers>).

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.
