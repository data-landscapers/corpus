# Sao Tome and Principe

## Revenue collection

**Paid electronically.** [In May 2025 the Minister of State, Economy and Finance said the new Sydonia brought real-time electronic payment of customs revenue](<https://rstp.st/2025/05/27/autoridade-geral-aduaneira-avanca-na-reforma-digital-com-nova-versao-do-sydonia-world/>). It works through direct integration with national banks. [The customs authority says electronic payment of customs declarations through participating banks began on 1 September 2026](<https://www.kezir.st/announcements/750389679240511488>). Electronic channels are mandatory from 1 October 2026. [Since February 2025 the tax directorate has taken tax payments by bank transfer](<https://impostos.b-software.st/index.php/component/k2/item/320-pagamento-via-transferencia-bancaria>). The form and proof of payment are exchanged by email.

**Main taxes and duties.** No source found since October 2023 says the main taxes and customs duties are filed and paid electronically. [Customs declarations must be paid through electronic channels from 1 October 2026](<https://www.kezir.st/announcements/750389679240511488>). [Taxes can be paid by bank transfer](<https://impostos.b-software.st/index.php/component/k2/item/320-pagamento-via-transferencia-bancaria>). Forms are sent by email.

**Share reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for revenue paid electronically. The nearest fact found: [customs declarations are paid through participating banks using a 20-digit reference](<https://www.kezir.st/announcements/750389679240511488>).
