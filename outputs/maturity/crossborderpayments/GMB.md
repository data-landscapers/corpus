# Gambia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Africell says Afrimoney Gambia and Afrimoney Sierra Leone are directly connected, so customers can send money instantly between the two countries, including by USSD](<https://www.africell.com/story/afrimoney-sierra-leone-the-gambia-connected/>). [The IFAD PRIME Africa end-of-project profile (April 2026) says 184,000 people in The Gambia received remittances into APS wallets from European Union corridors (France, Germany, Italy, Norway, Spain, Sweden)](<https://ffremittances.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_PF_TheGambia_APS_completion_WEB.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS lists Gambia as a country with live send-and-receive participants: seven banks (Access, AGIB, Bloom, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank, Vista)](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). [PAPSS lists Gambia as available for transfers in April 2026, through six live commercial banks (AGIB Bank, Bloom Bank, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank, Vista Bank)](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_APRIL2026.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** [The IFAD PRIME Africa end-of-project profile (April 2026) reports US$135 million in remittances received digitally through APS and an average APS remittance cost of 5 per cent across PRIME corridors](<https://ffremittances.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_PF_TheGambia_APS_completion_WEB.pdf>).
