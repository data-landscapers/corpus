# Gambia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Africell says Afrimoney Gambia and Afrimoney Sierra Leone are directly connected](<https://www.africell.com/story/afrimoney-sierra-leone-the-gambia-connected/>). Customers can send money instantly between the two countries, including by USSD. [The IFAD PRIME Africa end-of-project profile (April 2026) says 184,000 people in The Gambia received remittances into APS wallets](<https://ffremittances.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_PF_TheGambia_APS_completion_WEB.pdf>). They came from European Union corridors (France, Germany, Italy, Norway, Spain, Sweden).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS lists Gambia as a country with live send-and-receive participants](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). There are seven banks: Access, AGIB, Bloom, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank and Vista. [In April 2026 PAPSS listed Gambia as available for transfers through six live commercial banks](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_APRIL2026.pdf>). They are AGIB Bank, Bloom Bank, Ecobank, First Bank, Trust Bank and Vista Bank.

**Volumes reported.** [The IFAD PRIME Africa end-of-project profile (April 2026) reports US$135 million in remittances received digitally through APS](<https://ffremittances.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_PF_TheGambia_APS_completion_WEB.pdf>). It gives an average APS remittance cost of 5 per cent across PRIME corridors.
