# South Sudan

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In May 2025 ITWeb Africa reported that MTN MoMo in South Sudan, with Onafriq, offers inbound international remittances](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-onafriq-partner-on-remittances-in-south-sudan/kYbe97XbmYYqAWpG>). [Money reaches MoMo wallets from 15 countries](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-onafriq-partner-on-remittances-in-south-sudan/kYbe97XbmYYqAWpG>). [They include the UAE, Uganda, Kenya, the US and Australia](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-onafriq-partner-on-remittances-in-south-sudan/kYbe97XbmYYqAWpG>).

**Regional system.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [in May 2025 MTN MoMo took remittances from Uganda and Kenya through Onafriq](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-onafriq-partner-on-remittances-in-south-sudan/kYbe97XbmYYqAWpG>).
