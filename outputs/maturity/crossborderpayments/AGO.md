# Angola

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Banco Angolano de Investimentos and Bank of China's Luanda branch signed a memorandum on 8 September 2026 to open a renminbi clearing account, develop RMB settlement and clearing services and support BAI's indirect accession to China's CIPS cross-border interbank payment system, with no live date stated (Mercado)](<https://mercado.co.ao/bai-reforca-pagamentos-em-renminbi-e-cooperacao-com-a-china/>). [ANGOP reported that Banco Angolano de Investimentos and Bank of China signed an MoU on 8 September 2026 for an RMB clearing account and support for BAI's indirect CIPS membership through Bank of China](<https://angop.ao/noticias/economia/bai-e-bank-of-china-assinam-acordo-para-pagamentos-transfronteiricos/>).

**Regional system.** [Angola takes part in the SADC-RTGS, the regional settlement system, with the kwanza as its second settlement currency since 2026-07-27](<https://www.noticiasaominuto.com/mundo/3030615/angola-elimina-intermediarios-em-pagamentos-entre-paises-da-sadc>). [SADC states that on 27 July 2026 the Angolan kwanza was introduced as a settlement currency in the SADC-RTGS system, the second after the rand, which 15 countries use](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system>).

**Volumes reported.** [SADC says 2025 trade and interbank transactions between Angola and the other 14 SADC states were about US$3.77 billion across nine currencies, South Africa taking about US$2.99 billion](<https://www.sadc.int/latest-news/angolan-kwanza-introduced-sadc-rtgs-system>).
