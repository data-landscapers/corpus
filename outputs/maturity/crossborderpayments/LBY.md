# Libya

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Governor Naji Issa said the project to connect Libya to China's CIPS cross-border payment system has begun, with a pilot phase targeted for early 2027](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>). [The Central Bank of Libya and the People's Bank of China formally agreed that Libyan banks will join China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS), with no start date given](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=361748&lang=ar>). [Circular 6 of 2026 of the Central Bank of Libya authorises licensed exchange companies to send direct foreign remittances for companies and small traders via a unified platform, with a USD 100,000 per-transfer and USD 300,000 annual ceiling](<https://regalert.today/document/circular-6-2026-instructions-for-regulating-direct-foreign-remittances-executed-0f4311d3-a55d-43f0-94bd-8dbfa13d1a66>).
