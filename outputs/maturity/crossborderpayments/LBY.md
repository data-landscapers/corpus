# Libya

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In February 2026 the Central Bank of Libya's Circular 6 let licensed exchange companies send direct foreign remittances](<https://regalert.today/document/circular-6-2026-instructions-for-regulating-direct-foreign-remittances-executed-0f4311d3-a55d-43f0-94bd-8dbfa13d1a66>). They go through a unified platform for companies and small traders, up to USD 100,000 a transfer and USD 300,000 a year. [In July 2026 the central bank and the People's Bank of China agreed that Libyan banks will join China's CIPS](<https://lana.gov.ly/post.php?id=361748&lang=ar>). No start date was given. [In September 2026 Governor Naji Issa said the CIPS project had begun](<https://libyaherald.com/2026/09/cbl-governor-issa-launches-several-initiatives-and-reviews-achievements-at-tripoli-arab-savings-and-financial-literacy-conference>), with a pilot targeted for early 2027. [The DPI Map lists the central bank's LYPay as facilitating real-time cross-border transactions](<https://dpimap.org/data/>), as checked in October 2026.
