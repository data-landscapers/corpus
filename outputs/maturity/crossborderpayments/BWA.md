# Botswana

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In March 2026 PayInc's TCIB scheme [went live into Botswana through First Capital Bank](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/02/payincs-tcib-unlocks-real-time-cross-border-payments-between-south-africa-and-botswana/>). The bank can now receive money instantly from South Africa via SendHome and from Zimbabwe via ZB Bank. Its platform [takes payments from across the SADC region in real time](<https://www.sundaystandard.info/first-capital-bank-rolls-out-instant-sadc-payments/>), the Sunday Standard reported. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Orange Money wallets in Botswana](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). On 17 November 2026 Stanbic Bank Botswana [opened direct access to China's CIPS](<https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/11/18/rmb-cross-border-transactions-system-launched-in-botswana>), settling renminbi payments through Standard Bank South Africa.

**Regional system.** Botswana is [a live corridor of TCIB](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/02/payincs-tcib-unlocks-real-time-cross-border-payments-between-south-africa-and-botswana/>), the cross-border scheme PayInc runs for SADC as its regional settlement operator. TechAfrica News reported the link in March 2026. The first link runs through one bank, First Capital Bank Botswana. Five Botswana institutions [took part in SADC-RTGS in December 2025](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-banks-handle-25-of-sadc-payments/>).
