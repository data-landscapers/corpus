# Botswana

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In March 2026 PayInc's TCIB scheme [went live into Botswana through First Capital Bank](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/02/payincs-tcib-unlocks-real-time-cross-border-payments-between-south-africa-and-botswana/>). The bank can now receive money instantly from South Africa via SendHome and from Zimbabwe via ZB Bank. Its platform [takes payments from across the SADC region in real time](<https://www.sundaystandard.info/first-capital-bank-rolls-out-instant-sadc-payments/>), the Sunday Standard reported. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Orange Money wallets in Botswana](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>).

**Regional system.** Botswana is [a live corridor of TCIB](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/03/02/payincs-tcib-unlocks-real-time-cross-border-payments-between-south-africa-and-botswana/>), the cross-border scheme PayInc runs for SADC as its regional settlement operator. TechAfrica News reported the link in March 2026. The first link runs through one bank, First Capital Bank Botswana.
