# Burundi

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** On 1 August 2025 the Commercial Bank of Burundi, BANCOBU, [launched instant international transfers on its eNoti platform](<https://andikamagazine.net/bancobu-launches-instant-international-money-transfers-via-enoti/>). Built with Thunes, it lets holders of a foreign-currency account send and receive money in 48 countries. A launch demonstration sent money to Uganda, France and Singapore. Burundi is also [connecting its banks to COMESA's regional payment systems](<https://www.channelafrica.co.za/channelafrica/news/burundi-integrates-comesa-payment-systems-to-boost-cross-border-trade/>), and has pledged to [join the East African Payment System by December 2026](<https://www.theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/news/east-africa/burundi-to-join-east-african-payment-network-by-december-2026-5562616>).

**Regional system.** The central bank BRB [has acceded to PAPSS](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>), with four Burundian banks authorised to take part. In July 2026 EAC central bank governors decided that [BurundiPay must connect to other member states' systems](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>) for cross-border payments.
