# Burundi

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Burundi has pledged to join the East African Payment System by December 2026 and its integration project components were unfinished when the African Development Bank grant closed in June, the East African Legislative Assembly Committee on Accounts reported](<https://www.burunditimes.com/regional-lawmakers-flag-unfinished-burundi-payment-systems-work-as-grant-closes/>). [The Bank of the Republic of Burundi hosted training for local banks and the Post Office on connecting to COMESA's Regional Payment and Settlement System and Digital Regional Payment Platform, with connection under way and not yet stated in service](<https://www.channelafrica.co.za/channelafrica/news/burundi-integrates-comesa-payment-systems-to-boost-cross-border-trade/>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Regional system.** [The BRB has acceded to PAPSS, with four Burundian banks authorised to take part, says BRB's Nduwimana Libere](<https://www.jimberemag.org/une-revolution-de-leconomie-digitale-au-burundi/>).
