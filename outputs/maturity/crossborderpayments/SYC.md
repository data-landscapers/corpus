# Seychelles

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In July 2026 Mauritius Telecom's CEO announced cross-border payment capabilities between Mauritius and Seychelles](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>). [The partner is the Seychelles fintech Eboo](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>). [He made it at the AllMyT Summit, the first of a series of Indian Ocean regional payment announcements](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>).

**Regional system.** [In July 2025 Governor Caroline Abel said Seychelles remains active on the SADC-RTGS platform](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>). [The Central Bank processes cross-border payments through it, though volumes are limited](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [in July 2025 the Governor said SADC-RTGS volumes for Seychelles are limited](<https://www.cbs.sc/Downloads/publications/Speeches/Opening%20Remarks%20by%20Governor%20Caroline%20Abel%20-SADC%20CCBG%20PSS%20and%20PSOC%20meetings.pdf>).
