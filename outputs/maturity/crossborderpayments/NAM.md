# Namibia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Namclear says TCIB services will allow consumers and businesses to send money between Namibia, South Africa and other Common Monetary Area countries](<https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/>). [Namclear has joined the SADC TCIB network operated with PayInc](<https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/>). It connects Namibian banks and authorised non-bank institutions to near-instant cross-border push payments. [The South African Reserve Bank directive says low-value cross-border electronic funds transfers within the Common Monetary Area are currently executed through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). The area covers Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia and South Africa.

**Regional system.** [The South African Reserve Bank directive says Common Monetary Area low-value transfers, including Namibia's, run through the SADC-RTGS regional system](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). They must move to the TCIB scheme by April 2027.

**Volumes reported.** [The Bank of Namibia says Namibian banks processed R708 billion through SADC-RTGS in 2025](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-banks-handle-25-of-sadc-payments/>), 25% of the system's R2.8 trillion. In December 2025 they processed R76.33 billion, second by value after South Africa.
