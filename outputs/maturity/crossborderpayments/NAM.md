# Namibia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Namclear says TCIB services will allow consumers and businesses to send money between Namibia, South Africa and other Common Monetary Area countries](<https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/>). [Namclear has joined the SADC TCIB network operated with PayInc, connecting Namibian banks and authorised non-bank institutions to near-instant cross-border push payments](<https://technews.com.na/2026/03/31/namibia-plugged-into-sadc-digital-payments-system-through-namclear-payinc-deal/>). [The South African Reserve Bank directive says low-value cross-border electronic funds transfers within the Common Monetary Area (Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, South Africa) are currently executed through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [The South African Reserve Bank directive says Common Monetary Area low-value transfers, including this country's, run through the SADC-RTGS regional system and must move to the TCIB scheme by April 2027](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>).
