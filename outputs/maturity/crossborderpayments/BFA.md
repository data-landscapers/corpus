# Burkina Faso

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The Africa Report says the BCEAO began piloting an interoperable instant payment system in early August 2024](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). The pilot covers all eight WAEMU states, including Burkina Faso, and is intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone. [L'Oeil du Sahel cites BCEAO data for 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>). Intra-UEMOA e-money transfers made up 7.25% of the value of Burkina Faso's mobile money transactions that year.

**Regional system.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO launched its PI-SPI instant payment platform on 30 September 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). It serves the UEMOA, and 80 institutions were connected by 24 June 2026. [As of 24 February 2026, nine institutions in Burkina Faso were authorised to offer services on the WAEMU-wide platform](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144889>).

**Volumes reported.** [L'Oeil du Sahel cites BCEAO data for 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>). It puts intra-UEMOA transfers at 7.25% of the value of Burkina Faso's 20,139,735 million FCFA of e-money transactions that year.
