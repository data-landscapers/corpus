# Burkina Faso

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** No source held says a cross-border digital payment channel is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, serves the UEMOA, with 80 institutions connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Regional system.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, serves the UEMOA, with 80 institutions connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [Nine institutions in Burkina Faso are authorised to offer services on the BCEAO's WAEMU-wide PI-SPI instant payment platform, as of 24 February 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144889>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).
