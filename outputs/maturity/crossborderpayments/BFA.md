# Burkina Faso

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The Africa Report says the BCEAO pilot of an interoperable instant payment system covering all eight WAEMU states, including Burkina Faso, began in early August 2024, intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). [L'Oeil du Sahel, citing BCEAO 2024 data, says intra-UEMOA e-money transfers made up 7.25% of the value of Burkina Faso's mobile money transactions in 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).

**Regional system.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, serves the UEMOA, with 80 institutions connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [Nine institutions in Burkina Faso are authorised to offer services on the BCEAO's WAEMU-wide PI-SPI instant payment platform, as of 24 February 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article144889>).

**Volumes reported.** [L'Oeil du Sahel, citing BCEAO 2024 data, puts intra-UEMOA transfers at 7.25% of the value of Burkina Faso's 20,139,735 million FCFA of e-money transactions in 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).
