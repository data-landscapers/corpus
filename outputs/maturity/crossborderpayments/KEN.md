# Kenya

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In August 2026 Wise said customers abroad [can send to Kenyan shilling accounts and M-PESA wallets](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/08/the-platforms-that-help-kenyans-get-paid-are-changing-the-rules/>), and Zepz said its Sendwave service ran as normal. Since October 2024 [M-Pesa wallets in Kenya and Ethiopia can send to each other](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>). Since February 2026 Pesalink's link to PAPSS [makes Kenyan wallets a destination for African payments](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/17/papss-payments-grew-1000-in-a-year-and-kenya-is-now-on-the-network/>). In August 2025 the stablecoin payments firm HoneyCoin [received no-objection letters from regulators](<https://techcabal.com/2025/08/12/kenyas-honeycoin-raises-4-9-million/>) in Kenya, Nigeria and Tanzania.

**Regional system.** Kenya is [on PAPSS](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/17/papss-payments-grew-1000-in-a-year-and-kenya-is-now-on-the-network/>): the central bank joined in September 2023 and KCB went live in March 2025. In September 2026 [I&M Bank customers gained PAPSS transfers](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7347/papss-goes-live-at-im-bank-kenya-to-expand-cross-border-payments>) in Kenyan shillings across participating markets. In November 2026 Uganda's EAC minister said the East African Payment System [links the RTGS systems of Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en>).
