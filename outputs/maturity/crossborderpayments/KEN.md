# Kenya

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [TechCabal says Pesalink, Kenya's instant payments network, is linking local banks and mobile money providers to PAPSS](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/08/the-platforms-that-help-kenyans-get-paid-are-changing-the-rules/>). [Zepz says its core Sendwave remittance services in Kenya operate as normal, and Wise says customers abroad can send money to Kenyan shilling accounts and wallets such as M-PESA](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/08/the-platforms-that-help-kenyans-get-paid-are-changing-the-rules/>). [In a speech published 13 March 2026, the National Bank of Rwanda says the Central Bank of Kenya and National Bank of Rwanda signed an MoU on a licence passporting framework letting payment service providers licensed at home operate in the other state, on a Letter of No Objection from the sending authority](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20260313-signing-mou-between-central-bank-kenya-and-national-bank-rwanda>).

**Regional system.** [TechReviewAfrica reports PAPSS is now available to customers of I&M Bank Kenya, who can send money across participating African markets in Kenyan shillings through branches](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/7347/papss-goes-live-at-im-bank-kenya-to-expand-cross-border-payments>). [Kenya is on PAPSS: the central bank joined in September 2023, KCB went live in March 2025 and Pesalink plugged into PAPSS in February 2026, per tech-ish](<https://tech-ish.com/2026/09/17/papss-payments-grew-1000-in-a-year-and-kenya-is-now-on-the-network/>).
