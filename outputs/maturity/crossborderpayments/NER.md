# Niger

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS chief executive Mike Ogbalu said on 5 September 2026 that transaction corridors linking Nigeria with Niger, Cameroon and Benin are growing](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/05/nigerian-churches-drive-rising-cross-border-payments-across-africa-papss-says/>). He said trade in Nigerian goods drives them. [ActuNiger reports that Airtel Money Niger launched international transfers on 25 August 2026](<https://actuniger.com/societe/22299-transfert-dargent-airtel-money-niger-lance-son-service-international-pour-des-operations-simples-et-securisees-au-dela-des-frontieres.html>). Inbound corridors come from the US, Canada, Belgium, Germany, China, Turkey, Gabon and Cameroon. Outbound corridors go to Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Senegal and Togo. [The Africa Report says the BCEAO began piloting an interoperable instant payment system in early August 2024](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). The pilot covers all eight WAEMU states, including Niger, and is intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone.

**Regional system.** [Niger is a UEMOA member covered by PI-SPI, the regional instant payment system in service since 30 September 2025](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [L'Oeil du Sahel says the BCEAO lists 13 participants in Niger authorised to open PI-SPI services to the public](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>). The count is as of 30 September 2026.

**Volumes reported.** [L'Oeil du Sahel cites BCEAO data for 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>). It puts intra-UEMOA transfers at about 1.03% of the value of Niger's 480,220 million FCFA of e-money transactions that year.
