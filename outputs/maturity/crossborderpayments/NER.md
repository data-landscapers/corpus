# Niger

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS chief executive Mike Ogbalu said on 5 September 2026 that transaction corridors linking Nigeria with Niger, Cameroon and Benin are growing, driven by trade in Nigerian goods](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/05/nigerian-churches-drive-rising-cross-border-payments-across-africa-papss-says/>). [Airtel Money Niger launched international transfers on 25 August 2026: inbound corridors from the US, Canada, Belgium, Germany, China, Turkey, Gabon and Cameroon, and outbound to Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Senegal and Togo, per ActuNiger](<https://actuniger.com/societe/22299-transfert-dargent-airtel-money-niger-lance-son-service-international-pour-des-operations-simples-et-securisees-au-dela-des-frontieres.html>). [The Africa Report says the BCEAO pilot of an interoperable instant payment system covering all eight WAEMU states, including Niger, began in early August 2024, intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>).

**Regional system.** [Niger is a UEMOA member covered by PI-SPI, the regional instant payment system in service since 30 September 2025](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [L'Oeil du Sahel says the BCEAO lists 13 participants in Niger authorised to open PI-SPI services to the public, as of 30 September 2026](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).

**Volumes reported.** [L'Oeil du Sahel, citing BCEAO 2024 data, puts intra-UEMOA transfers at about 1.03% of the value of Niger's 480,220 million FCFA of e-money transactions in 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).
