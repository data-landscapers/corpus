# Niger

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS chief executive Mike Ogbalu said on 5 September 2026 that transaction corridors linking Nigeria with Niger, Cameroon and Benin are growing, driven by trade in Nigerian goods](<https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/05/nigerian-churches-drive-rising-cross-border-payments-across-africa-papss-says/>). [Airtel Money Niger launched international transfers on 25 August 2026: inbound corridors from the US, Canada, Belgium, Germany, China, Turkey, Gabon and Cameroon, and outbound to Benin, Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Senegal and Togo, per ActuNiger](<https://actuniger.com/societe/22299-transfert-dargent-airtel-money-niger-lance-son-service-international-pour-des-operations-simples-et-securisees-au-dela-des-frontieres.html>).

**Regional system.** [Niger is a UEMOA member covered by PI-SPI, the regional instant payment system in service since 30 September 2025](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [BCEAO says the UMOA payment infrastructure includes STAR-UMOA, SICA-UMOA and SAGETIL-UMOA and the PI-SPI instant-payment platform, regional systems of the Union that includes Niger](<https://www.lesahel.org/digitalisation-et-services-financiers-numeriques-de-la-bceao-renforcer-linclusion-financiere-la-modernisation-des-services-financiers-et-la-resilience-du-systeme-financier-de-l/>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments.
