# Rwanda

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Bank of Kigali, per its own announcement reported by KT Press, became a direct participant in China's CIPS under an agreement signed 2026-09-08, giving direct renminbi clearing and settlement for Rwanda-China payments without several intermediary banks](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/09/bank-of-kigali-joins-chinas-cross-border-payments-system-first-in-this-region/>). [Ghana News Agency reports the Bank of Ghana saying it is piloting cross-border interoperability with CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets in the Ghana-Rwanda corridor](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>). [The Bank of Ghana has started testing interoperability of CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets with Rwanda and Nigeria, per Governor Asiama](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). In October 2026 the governor of the National Bank of Rwanda said the bank had [launched a pilot linking eKash with Tanzania's Instant Payment System](<https://gfmag.com/transaction-banking/rwandas-ekash-instant-payment-system-growth/>).

**Regional system.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.ktpress.rw/2026/09/bank-of-kigali-joins-chinas-cross-border-payments-system-first-in-this-region/>).
