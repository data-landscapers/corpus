# Egypt

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Tech Africa News reports that Orange Egypt and UAE fintech Botim announced on 20 October 2026 a corridor letting users in the UAE send remittances directly into Orange Cash wallets in Egypt](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/10/20/orange-egypt-botim-uae-egypt-remittances/>). [Al Manassa says six Egyptian banks have applied to join PAPSS, with plans to link InstaPay for personal transactions](<https://almanassa.com/en/stories/34165>). [WAYA reports Afreximbank is working to connect PAPSS Card with Egypt's Meeza network for cross-border card payments in local currencies, at an early stage with no availability date, per PAPSS chief executive Mike Ogbalu](<https://waya.media/egypts-meeza-cards-set-for-wider-african-use-through-papss-integration/>).

**Regional system.** [Ahram Online reports PAPSS currently works across 30 African countries, including Egypt](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>). [Egypt is described as one of the largest economies in the PAPSS network, which the Central Bank of Egypt agreed to join in November 2024](<https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/09/23/instapay-to-connect-with-pan-african-payment-system-as-six-egyptian-banks-apply-for-integration/>). [Al Manassa says the Central Bank of Egypt joined PAPSS two years after its 2022 launch](<https://almanassa.com/en/stories/34165>).

**Volumes reported.** [Youm7, citing the NTRA H1 2026 wallet report, says incoming transfers from abroad were 3% of wallet deposits by count and 10% by value](<https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/9/14/%D9%83%D9%8A%D9%81-%D8%BA%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D9%84%D9%83%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%81%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1/7540929>). [Ogbalu says PAPSS transaction volume rose about 1,000 percent in 2026 and value about 120 percent, with transfer costs down by up to 95 percent and average processing time 7 seconds](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/576342.aspx>).
