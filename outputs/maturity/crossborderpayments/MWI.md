# Malawi

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The Bankers Association of Malawi's president said the COMESA digital retail payments platform is being piloted between Malawi and Zambia for local-currency transactions, as reported on 27 March 2026](<https://mbc.mw/regional-digital-payments-to-boost-trade-ease-forex-pressure-rbm/>). [COMESA's Digital Retail Payments Platform is stated to be rolled out, linking Malawi and Zambia for instant local-currency cross-border transactions](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>). [Finhive reports that Airtel Money Africa and pawaPay launched pawaPay's inbound remittance service for licensed international money transfer operators, with real-time deposits to Airtel Money wallets, in this country among seven markets](<https://finhive.africa/news/airtel-money-africa-expands-pawapay-partnership-to-supercharge-imto-remittances-across-seven-markets/>).

**Regional system.** [Malawi takes part in the COMESA Digital Retail Payments Platform, described as the first interoperable cross-border payment system of the 21-member bloc](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>).
