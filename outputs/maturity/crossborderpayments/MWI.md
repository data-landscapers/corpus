# Malawi

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In April 2024 MTN's MoMo app [opened a wallet corridor from South Africa to Malawi](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-increases-remittance-services-to-10-new-countries/lLn147mQr947J6Aa>), ITWeb reported. Since August 2025 licensed transfer operators [can pay into Airtel Money wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>) through pawaPay. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Airtel Money wallets in Malawi](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). COMESA's retail payments platform [links Malawi and Zambia](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>) for instant transfers in local currency.

**Regional system.** Malawi takes part in [COMESA's Digital Retail Payments Platform](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>), described in October 2025 as the bloc's first interoperable cross-border payment system. COMESA, with EU funds, [supports its rollout in eight countries](<https://www.comesa.int/comesa-eu-partnership-shaping-a-digital-future/>), Malawi among them.

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact is [COMESA's account of the platform](<https://mwnation.com/comesa-payment-system-connects-malawi-zambia>), which gives no volumes.
