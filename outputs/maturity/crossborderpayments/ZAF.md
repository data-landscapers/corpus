# South Africa

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Standard Bank's Nthabiseng Sibanda says the bank uses China's CIPS as a direct route for renminbi transactions, reaching 12 African countries](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>). [Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis (TCIB) is being developed to enable immediate low-value cross-border payments in the region](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>). [Standard Bank says China's CIPS has gone live for interbank payments between Africa and China in renminbi, inaugurated at the South African Reserve Bank in November 2025](<https://corporateandinvestment.standardbank.com/cib/global/who-we-are/about-us/news/china-cross-border-interbank-payment-system-goes-live-with-standard-bank-as-first-african-bank-to-offer-service>). [ITWeb reports, citing Standard Bank, that CIPS interbank payments between Africa and China in renminbi have gone live and were inaugurated at the South African Reserve Bank](<https://itweb.africa/article/standard-bank-connects-africa-to-chinas-payment-system/lwrKxq3YVeX7mg1o>).

**Regional system.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [Standard Bank's Nthabiseng Sibanda says the bank has processed more than R21 billion ($1.3 billion) through CIPS since its inception](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>).
