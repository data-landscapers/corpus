# South Africa

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Standard Bank's Nthabiseng Sibanda says the bank uses China's CIPS as a direct route for renminbi transactions, reaching 12 African countries](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>). [Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis (TCIB) is being developed to enable immediate low-value cross-border payments in the region](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>). [The South African Reserve Bank says on 23 June 2026 that its payment system and financial surveillance departments are drafting rules for cross-border payment facilitators, with a draft FinSurv circular issued in December 2025 on cross-border settlement and reporting](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/media-releases/2026/cross-border-regulation>).

**Regional system.** [The SARB speech says lower-value retail regional payments run through SADC-RTGS now, with qualifying payments under R100,000 to move to TCIB by end-April 2027 and a hub-and-spoke pilot across eight countries by October 2027](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/publications/publication-detail-pages/speeches/speeches-by-governors/2026/kganyago-payments-conference>). [Notice 2024 of 2022 records the SARB designating the SADC Real Time Gross Settlement System (SADC-RTGS) as a designated settlement system in place of SIRESS (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.resbank.co.za/en/home/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/regulation>).

**Volumes reported.** [Standard Bank's Nthabiseng Sibanda says the bank has processed more than R21 billion ($1.3 billion) through CIPS since its inception](<https://techcabal.com/2026/09/10/south-africa-payments-system-changes/>).
