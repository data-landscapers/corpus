# Togo

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [A user in Togo can transfer funds in real time to a bank account in Cote d'Ivoire or pay a supplier in Burkina Faso through PI-SPI](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>). [TechCabal says Semoa's Level 3 BCEAO licence lifts its earlier self-imposed limit on cross-border transfers and lets it offer national and cross-border money transfers within UEMOA](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>). [Togo First reports Ecobank launched an instant cross-border transfer service in Togo with Thunes, sending and receiving money from or to more than 130 countries](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/3110-17454-ecobank-launches-instant-international-payment-service-in-togo>).

**Regional system.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform, launched 30 September 2025, serves the UEMOA, with 80 institutions connected by 24 June 2026](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). [Togo takes part in the BCEAO's PI-SPI regional instant payment platform, in service across the eight UEMOA states](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).
