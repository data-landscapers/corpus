# Togo

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Through PI-SPI, a user in Togo can transfer funds in real time to a bank account in Cote d'Ivoire or pay a supplier in Burkina Faso](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>). [TechCabal says Semoa's Level 3 BCEAO licence lifts its earlier self-imposed limit on cross-border transfers](<https://techcabal.com/2026/02/17/francophone-weekly-by-techcabal-012/>). It lets Semoa offer national and cross-border money transfers within UEMOA. [Togo First reports that Ecobank launched an instant cross-border transfer service in Togo with Thunes](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/finance/3110-17454-ecobank-launches-instant-international-payment-service-in-togo>). It sends and receives money from or to more than 130 countries.

**Regional system.** [LeFaso.net reports that the BCEAO launched its PI-SPI instant payment platform on 30 September 2025](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>). It serves the UEMOA, and 80 institutions were connected by 24 June 2026. [Togo takes part in the platform, which is in service across the eight UEMOA states](<https://www.togofirst.com/fr/finance/1404-18708-mixx-togo-lance-les-paiements-et-virements-instantanes-dans-luemoa-via-la-plateforme-pi-spi>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest fact: [by 24 June 2026 80 institutions were connected to the BCEAO's PI-SPI instant payment platform](<https://lefaso.net/spip.php?article147670>).
