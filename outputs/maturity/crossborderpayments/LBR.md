# Liberia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In June 2026 BnB Liberia and Orange Money Liberia launched a cross-border mobile remittance service](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/10/bnb-liberia-and-orange-money-launch-cross-border-mobile-remittance-service-across-eight-african-countries/>). [Orange Money wallet users in Liberia can send money instantly to eight African countries](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/10/bnb-liberia-and-orange-money-launch-cross-border-mobile-remittance-service-across-eight-african-countries/>). [They are Ghana, Sierra Leone, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Mali, Uganda and Rwanda](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/06/10/bnb-liberia-and-orange-money-launch-cross-border-mobile-remittance-service-across-eight-african-countries/>). [In August 2024 BnB and Lonestar Cell MTN MoMo launched outbound mobile money transfers from Liberia](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/financial-services/17109-new-partnership-aims-to-boost-liberia-s-mobile-money-transfer-services.html>). [They reach Ivory Coast, Ghana, Sierra Leone, Guinea, Uganda, Rwanda, Mali and Senegal](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/financial-services/17109-new-partnership-aims-to-boost-liberia-s-mobile-money-transfer-services.html>). [Each transaction is capped at US$250](<https://developingtelecoms.com/telecom-technology/financial-services/17109-new-partnership-aims-to-boost-liberia-s-mobile-money-transfer-services.html>).

**Regional system.** [The Central Bank of Liberia's Executive Governor said PAPSS was officially launched in Liberia on 27 March 2025](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>).

**Volumes reported.** [The Executive Governor said Liberia's cross-border transactions grew 17.7% in 2024, to $494.5 million](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>). [PAPSS transactions reached about L$37.7 million between June and December 2024](<https://www.cbl.org.lr/media/features-articles/eg-saamoi-speaks-joint-launch-fined-papss-monrovia>).
