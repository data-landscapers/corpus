# Congo

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Belmoney and DigiPay Group launched the DigiTransfer app enabling transfers from France and Belgium to mobile money wallets and bank accounts in the Republic of Congo; the service is limited to outbound transfers from those two countries](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/belmoney-digipay-enable-congolese-remittances-from-france-and-belgium>). [Belmoney and DigiPay Group launched the DigiTransfer app, sending money from France and Belgium to mobile wallets and bank accounts in the Republic of Congo within minutes](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/belmoney-digipay-launch-instant-transfer-app-for-congo-and-drc>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries domestic and cross-border payments within the six CEMAC states including the Republic of Congo](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Congo is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Congo takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
