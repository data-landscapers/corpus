# Congo

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** Since August 2025 licensed money transfer operators [can pay into Airtel Money wallets in Congo](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>) through pawaPay, Connecting Africa reported. In June 2025 Airtel Money [opened transfers to 31 more African countries](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1706-129266-airtel-money-elargit-ses-services-au-dela-de-la-cemac-et-connecte-31-pays-d-afrique>), beyond the CEMAC. In May 2026 the DigiTransfer app [began sending money from France and Belgium](<https://www.wearetech.africa/en/fils-uk/news/tech/belmoney-digipay-launch-instant-transfer-app-for-congo-and-drc>) to wallets and bank accounts in Congo. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Airtel Money and MTN wallets in Congo](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>).

**Regional system.** Congo is one of six CEMAC states on [GIMACPAY, the regional payment platform](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>), whose interoperable QR code was launched on 29 July 2026. Airtel says its [transfers to the 31 countries run over GIMAC Pay](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1706-129266-airtel-money-elargit-ses-services-au-dela-de-la-cemac-et-connecte-31-pays-d-afrique>). In July 2026 the central bank BEAC [joined PAPSS](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>), with CEMAC banks to be connected by the end of 2026.

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for Congo's own cross-border digital payments. GIMAC reported that its platform [handled 20.3 million transactions worth FCFA 863 billion in 2025](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>), across the six states together.
