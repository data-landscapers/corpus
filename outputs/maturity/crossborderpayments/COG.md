# Congo

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [TechAfrica News reports on 9 July 2026 that BEAC, serving the Republic of Congo among six CEMAC states, has joined PAPSS, which connects 28 countries and 16 switches, with operationalisation and onboarding of CEMAC institutions planned through end 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Belmoney and DigiPay Group launched the DigiTransfer app enabling transfers from France and Belgium to mobile money wallets and bank accounts in the Republic of Congo; the service is limited to outbound transfers from those two countries](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/fintech/belmoney-digipay-enable-congolese-remittances-from-france-and-belgium>).

**Regional system.** [Congo is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [Congo takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>). [The Airtel Congo communique says the Airtel Money service reaches those 31 countries over the GIMAC Pay network](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1706-129266-airtel-money-elargit-ses-services-au-dela-de-la-cemac-et-connecte-31-pays-d-afrique>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
