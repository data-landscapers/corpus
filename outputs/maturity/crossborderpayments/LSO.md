# Lesotho

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Standard Bank says it has expanded access to China's CIPS to clients in Lesotho among others](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/27/standard-bank-surpasses-cny-8-billion-in-china-cross-border-payment-transactions/>). Clients can settle cross-border payments directly in RMB. [TechCabal reports that Lesotho migrant workers in South Africa send money home through the fintech Mukuru, after tighter South African banking rules](<https://techcabal.com/2026/06/12/south-africa-remittance-rules-migrants/>). [Newsday reported that Sasai Econet Financial Services is adding inbound remittance corridors to EcoCash Lesotho through a partnership with Thunes](<https://newsdayonline.co.ls/ecocash-unlocks-new-global-remittance-avenues-with-thunes-partnership/>). They cover Zimbabwe, Tanzania, Europe, the UK and others, with a Botswana corridor due from October 2025.

**Regional system.** [The South African Reserve Bank directive says Common Monetary Area low-value transfers, including Lesotho's, run through the SADC-RTGS regional system](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). They must move to the TCIB scheme by April 2027.

**Volumes reported.** [In December 2025 Lesotho's five participants processed R30.57 billion through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.namibian.com.na/namibian-banks-handle-25-of-sadc-payments/>). That was third by value among the system's 15 countries. The Namibian took the figure from the South African Reserve Bank's settlement report.
