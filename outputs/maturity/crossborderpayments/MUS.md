# Mauritius

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Blink by Emtel became the first PSP licence holder in Mauritius to let Mauritians scan and pay in India, per Platform Africa](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/09/17/pulse-of-fintech-2026-why-collaboration-is-the-key-to-unlocking-mauritius-financial-future/>). [Mauritius Telecom CEO Veemal Gungadin announced the launch of cross-border payment capabilities between Mauritius and Seychelles, in partnership with Seychelles fintech Eboo, at the AllMyT Summit](<https://platformafrica.com/2026/07/16/from-connectivity-to-intelligence-how-mauritius-telecom-is-building-the-intelligent-layer-of-everyday-mauritius/>). [Bank of Mauritius Governor Thakoor says MauCAS is interlinked with India's UPI so Mauritian and Indian travellers can now use MauCAS QR payments settled in rupees in both countries](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>). [MCB card holders use services such as Orange Money, with a very active corridor to Madagascar (L'Express)](<https://lexpress.mu/node/553532>).

**Regional system.** [Bank of Mauritius Governor Thakoor says the bank is settlement bank for COMESA's REPSS, which facilitates cross-border payments among member states](<https://www.bis.org/speeches/20261013-cross-border-payments-necessity-africas-resilience-and-economic-transformation>).
