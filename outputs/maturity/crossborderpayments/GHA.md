# Ghana

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Ghana News Agency reports the Bank of Ghana saying it is piloting cross-border interoperability with CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets in the Ghana-Rwanda and Ghana-Nigeria corridors, with the aim of moving to live services](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>). [Governor Asiama said PAPSS has already been extended to mobile wallets along the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [The Bank of Ghana has started testing interoperability of CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets with Rwanda and Nigeria, per Governor Asiama](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [The Bank of Ghana's Updated Guidelines for Inward Remittance Services set a framework for DEMIs and Enhanced PSPs partnering with Money Transfer Operators](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Governor Asiama described PAPSS as existing infrastructure in use by Ghana, extended to mobile wallets on the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [Ghana News Agency reports the Bank of Ghana saying it has extended PAPSS to mobile wallets in the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>).
