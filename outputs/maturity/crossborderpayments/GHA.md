# Ghana

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The Bank of Ghana said in October 2026 that PAPSS had reached mobile wallets on the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). It is [piloting payments with Rwanda and Nigeria](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>) across digital currencies, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets. [Stanbic Bank Ghana offered direct yuan settlement through China's CIPS in August 2026](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/stanbic-bank-becomes-first-bank-in-ghana-to-offer-direct-chinese-yuan-settlement-via-cips/>), with central bank approval. [In November 2026 the Bank of Ghana and the Monetary Authority of Singapore finished a first Project DESFT trial](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/news/press-release-bank-of-ghana-announces-successful-completion-of-cross-border-trade-using-digital-credentials/>). It tested digital credentials for trade and cross-border payment. [Updated inward remittance guidelines of October 2026](<https://www.bog.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/REMARKS-BY-GOVERNOR-DR-JOHNSON-PANDIT-ASIAMA-AT-THE-OFFICIAL-LAUNCH-OF-SAOPAY-091026.pdf>) set terms for payment firms working with money transfer operators.

**Regional system.** [Governor Asiama described PAPSS as existing infrastructure in use by Ghana, extended to mobile wallets on the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [Ghana News Agency reports the Bank of Ghana saying it has extended PAPSS to mobile wallets in the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/bank-of-ghana-pilots-digital-cross-border-payments-with-rwanda-nigeria/>).
