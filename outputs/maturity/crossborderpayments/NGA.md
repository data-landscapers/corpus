# Nigeria

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Ghana's central bank governor said PAPSS has been extended to mobile wallets on the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [He said the Bank of Ghana is testing links between CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and wallets with Rwanda and Nigeria](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [Remita's app, launched on 22 September 2026, lets users send money anywhere in Africa over PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>). [The PAPSS chief executive says it carries significant trade payments between Nigeria and Ghana, Rwanda and Kenya](<https://thesun.ng/nigeria-fintechs-critical-to-africas-payment-integration/>). [In January 2026 Paga's PayPal partnership let Nigerian freelancers receive payments from customers abroad](<https://techcabal.com/2026/11/05/paga-engine-expands-direct-debit-access/>).

**Regional system.** [The PAPSS chief executive says Nigeria was the first chair of its Governing Council](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>). [He says Nigeria still leads its rollout](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>). [Remita's consumer app sends instant transfers from Nigerian bank accounts to other African countries through PAPSS](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>).
