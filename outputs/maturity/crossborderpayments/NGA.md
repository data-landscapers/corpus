# Nigeria

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Governor Asiama said PAPSS has already been extended to mobile wallets along the Ghana-Nigeria corridor](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [The Bank of Ghana has started testing interoperability of CBDCs, stablecoins, bank accounts and mobile wallets with Rwanda and Nigeria, per Governor Asiama](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-ghana-rwanda-nigeria-test-new-cross-border-digital-payment-systems.html>). [Remita's app launched 22 September 2026 lets users transfer to anyone in Africa over PAPSS, per its managing director](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>). [Ogbalu says PAPSS carries significant transactions between Nigeria and Ghana, Rwanda and Kenya, with Nigerian traders paying electronically instead of carrying cash](<https://thesun.ng/nigeria-fintechs-critical-to-africas-payment-integration/>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS CEO Mike Ogbalu said Nigeria was the first chair of the PAPSS Governing Council and continues to be a leader in its rollout](<https://nigeriaworks.substack.com/p/papss-notes-from-a-briefing-with>). [Remita's consumer app, launched 22 September 2026 in Lagos, supports instant transfers from Nigerian bank accounts to other African countries through PAPSS, per Remita's managing director](<https://thecondia.com/remita-launches-super-app/>).
