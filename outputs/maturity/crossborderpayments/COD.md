[TechAfrica News says Visa, M-PESA Africa and Onafriq are set to pilot stablecoins for cross-border mobile money transactions, with DR Congo the initial testing ground](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/06/visa-m-pesa-africa-and-onafriq-launch-stablecoin-pilot-in-dr-congo/>).
