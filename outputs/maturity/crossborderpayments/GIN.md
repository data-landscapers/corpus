# Guinea

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In January 2025 TechCrunch reported that Guinea-based Cauridor [runs remittance corridors to Ghana and Nigeria](<https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/cauridor-has-a-fix-for-cross-border-payments-issues-in-francophone-africa/>). It has agents across Guinea, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Sierra Leone and Liberia. Since March 2026 Ecobank's Guinean subsidiary [has sent renminbi payments to China](<https://www.togofirst.com/en/banking/0211-20515-estelle-komlan-managing-director-of-ecobank-togo-with-cips-we-want-to-make-payments-to-china-faster-and-cheaper>) through a correspondent bank. In December 2022 Orange Money [opened outbound transfers to six neighbours](<https://guineematin.com/2022/12/12/guinee-lancement-du-transfert-international-dargent-entre-orange-finances-mobiles-guinee-et-bnb-transfert-corp/>).

**Regional system.** UBA Guinée [joined PAPSS on 9 September 2024](<https://www.guinee360.com/10/09/2024/uba-guinee-adopte-le-papss-une-nouvelle-ere-pour-les-transactions-financieres-en-afrique-2/>). Its clients can make cross-border payments in local currencies across the participating African countries. The system lets them pay and be paid without converting through euros or dollars.

**Volumes reported.** Cauridor told TechCrunch it [processed $500 million in 2024](<https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/29/cauridor-has-a-fix-for-cross-border-payments-issues-in-francophone-africa/>), up from $300 million in 2023, across all its payment rails. It handled 2 million transactions in 2023.

***Also on file***. [PAPSS](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2807-57779-guinea-launches-nimbapay-its-national-instant-payment-system>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for Guinea's own corridors; Cauridor's figure covers its whole network.
