# Sierra Leone

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** Since June 2026 [Afrimoney wallets in Sierra Leone and Gambia are directly connected](<https://www.africell.com/story/afrimoney-sierra-leone-the-gambia-connected/>), Africell says, so customers can send money instantly, including by USSD. In May 2025 Afrimoney and BnB [launched an Overseas Money service](<https://thisdaysl.com/afrimoney-and-bnb-connect-to-promote-cross-border-transactions/>) to more than ten destinations. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Orange Money wallets in Sierra Leone](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). Each of these routes ends in a mobile wallet.

**Regional system.** PAPSS's list of live banks for May 2025 shows [eight of Sierra Leone's commercial banks on the system](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_19052025.pdf>). They can send and receive payments through it with other participating African countries.

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments.
