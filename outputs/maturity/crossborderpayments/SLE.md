# Sierra Leone

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Africell says Afrimoney Gambia and Afrimoney Sierra Leone are directly connected, so customers can send money instantly between the two countries, including by USSD](<https://www.africell.com/story/afrimoney-sierra-leone-the-gambia-connected/>). [Tranglo's 1 July 2025 press release says its payment hub now supports e-wallet payouts in Sierra Leone, with near-instant transfers in local currency at launch](<https://tranglo.com/press/tranglo-grows-footprint-in-africa-with-5-new-countries/>). [Afrimoney and BnB launched an 'Overseas Money' USSD service on 23 May 2025 in Freetown, letting Afrimoney users send money to over ten international destinations and receive BnB remittances into their wallets](<https://thisdaysl.com/afrimoney-and-bnb-connect-to-promote-cross-border-transactions/>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS's live-banks list of May 2025 shows eight commercial banks in Sierra Leone live on PAPSS](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_19052025.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/PAPSS-LIST-OF-LIVE-COMMERCIAL-BANKS_19052025.pdf>).
