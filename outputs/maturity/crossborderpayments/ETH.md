# Ethiopia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Connecting Africa reports Safaricom extended M-Pesa Global to Ethiopia in October 2024](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>). [ M-Pesa wallets in Kenya and Ethiopia can make cross-border transfers, with Ethiopian customers receiving from M-Pesa Kenya](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>). [Ethio telecom says its Visa Direct service lets Visa cardholders in over 190 countries send money to telebirr virtual cards](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/the-first-virtual-visa-card-in-ethiopia-remittance-visa-direct-telebirr-remit/>). [Its telebirr Remit app receives remittances from nine named countries, among them the US, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and South Africa](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/the-first-virtual-visa-card-in-ethiopia-remittance-visa-direct-telebirr-remit/>). [Afreximbank's release says Ethiopia has officially joined the PAPSS network](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>). [ It enables Ethiopian financial institutions to connect and make cross-border payments with institutions across Africa](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>).

**Regional system.** No source found since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest fact: [in October 2024 Safaricom extended M-Pesa Global to Ethiopia, linking wallets in Kenya and Ethiopia](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest fact: [from October 2024 Ethiopian customers could receive transfers from M-Pesa Kenya](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>).
