# Ethiopia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Capital reports Ethiopia's non-bank mobile-money operators and payment-service providers are preparing to integrate with PAPSS, each needing a National Bank of Ethiopia Letter of No Objection first](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>). [FSX Business reports Ethiopia became the 31st PAPSS member country and four Ethiopian banks have begun the regulatory process to join, awaiting National Bank of Ethiopia no-objection letters, with no bank yet operational](<https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss>). [The National Digital Payment Strategy 2026-2030 proposes enabling low-value outbound cross-border transfers via cards, wallets and digital banking for the first time (FMC/ENA/Shega)](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/ethiopia-ndps-2026-2030>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Regional system.** [ITWeb Africa reports Ethiopia has joined PAPSS, enabling financial institutions across the country to connect and take part in instant cross-border payments with counterparts across Africa](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>). [Afreximbank's release says Ethiopia has officially joined the PAPSS network, enabling Ethiopian financial institutions to connect and make cross-border payments with institutions across Africa](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>).
