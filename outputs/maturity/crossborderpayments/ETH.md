# Ethiopia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [FSX Business reports Ethiopia became the 31st PAPSS member country and four Ethiopian banks have begun the regulatory process to join, awaiting National Bank of Ethiopia no-objection letters, with no bank yet operational](<https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss>). [The National Digital Payment Strategy 2026-2030 proposes enabling low-value outbound cross-border transfers via cards, wallets and digital banking for the first time (FMC/ENA/Shega)](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/ethiopia-ndps-2026-2030>). [Capital reports Ethiopia's non-bank mobile-money operators and payment-service providers are preparing to integrate with PAPSS, each needing a National Bank of Ethiopia Letter of No Objection first](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2026/10/04/mobile-money-wallets-set-to-link-with-papss-opening-access-to-african-markets/>).
