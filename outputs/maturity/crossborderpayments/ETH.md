# Ethiopia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [FSX Business reports Ethiopia became the 31st PAPSS member country and four Ethiopian banks have begun the regulatory process to join, awaiting National Bank of Ethiopia no-objection letters, with no bank yet operational](<https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss>). [Afreximbank's release says Ethiopia has officially joined the PAPSS network, enabling Ethiopian financial institutions to connect and make cross-border payments with institutions across Africa](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>). [ITWeb Africa reports Ethiopia has joined PAPSS, enabling financial institutions across the country to connect and take part in instant cross-border payments with counterparts across Africa](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/ethiopia-modernises-cross-border-payments-via-papss/RgeVDMPR3w9vKJN3>).

**Regional system.** No source held since October 2023 says the country takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.fsxbusiness.com/ethiopia-joins-papss>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [National Digital Payment Strategy 2026-2030](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/ethiopia-ndps-2026-2030>); [PAPSS](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>).

***Not held***. No source held says an Ethiopian institution is live on PAPSS, or that any cross-border digital corridor is in service.
