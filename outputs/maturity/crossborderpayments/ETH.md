# Ethiopia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Connecting Africa reports Safaricom extended M-Pesa Global to Ethiopia in October 2024, so M-Pesa wallets in Kenya and Ethiopia can make cross-border transfers, with Ethiopian customers receiving from M-Pesa Kenya](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/safaricom-enables-m-pesa-transfers-between-kenya-ethiopia>). [Ethio telecom says its Visa Direct service lets Visa cardholders in over 190 countries send money to telebirr virtual card numbers, and its telebirr Remit app receives remittances from nine named countries (US, Canada, Israel, Saudi Arabia, UAE, UK, South Africa, Italy, Sweden)](<https://www.ethiotelecom.et/the-first-virtual-visa-card-in-ethiopia-remittance-visa-direct-telebirr-remit/>). [Afreximbank's release says Ethiopia has officially joined the PAPSS network, enabling Ethiopian financial institutions to connect and make cross-border payments with institutions across Africa](<https://www.afreximbank.com/ethiopia-joins-papss-network-as-national-bank-of-ethiopia-and-papss-announce-the-2nd-edition-of-cowry-in-addis-ababa/>).
