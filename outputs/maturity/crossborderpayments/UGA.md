# Uganda

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Payment company pawaPay says it launched remittances as a payment flow in Uganda, fully live with MTN and Airtel](<https://pawapay.io/blog/pawapay-q3-round-up>). [Finhive reports that Airtel Money Africa and pawaPay launched pawaPay's inbound remittance service for licensed international money transfer operators, with real-time deposits to Airtel Money wallets, in this country among seven markets](<https://finhive.africa/news/airtel-money-africa-expands-pawapay-partnership-to-supercharge-imto-remittances-across-seven-markets/>). [Connecting Africa reports that Airtel Money Africa and PawaPay launched inbound remittances into Uganda on 7 August 2025, letting licensed international money transfer operators pay into Airtel Money wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS announces that cross-border payments in local currencies are now available through United Bank for Africa Uganda](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-africasglobalbank-itsallaboutu-activity-7490084196724621313-kD1a>). [Uganda takes part in the East African Payment System, with inflows of UGX 1.3 trillion in FY2024/25, 99% in Kenyan and Tanzanian currency](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>).

**Volumes reported.** [EAPS inflows were UGX 1.3 trillion, up 17%, in FY2024/25, per the Bank of Uganda report](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>). [The Bank of Uganda says 2025 remittance inflows were US$2.5 billion, 73 per cent of them through digital channels and nearly 61 per cent through mobile money, with the United States the largest sending country at US$702 million](<https://bou.or.ug/uploads/Bank_of_Uganda_and_IFAD_launch_Remittance_Dashboard_to_strengthen_transparency_and_financial_inclusion_4843a57817.pdf>).
