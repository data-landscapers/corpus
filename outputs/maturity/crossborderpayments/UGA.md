# Uganda

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Payment company pawaPay says it launched remittances as a payment flow in Uganda, fully live with MTN and Airtel](<https://pawapay.io/blog/pawapay-q3-round-up>). [Finhive reports that Airtel Money Africa and pawaPay launched pawaPay's inbound remittance service in Uganda, one of seven markets](<https://finhive.africa/news/airtel-money-africa-expands-pawapay-partnership-to-supercharge-imto-remittances-across-seven-markets/>). It serves licensed international money transfer operators, with real-time deposits to Airtel Money wallets. [Connecting Africa dates the launch of inbound remittances into Uganda to 7 August 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS announces that cross-border payments in local currencies are now available through United Bank for Africa Uganda](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pan-african-payment-settlement-system_papss-africasglobalbank-itsallaboutu-activity-7490084196724621313-kD1a>). [Uganda takes part in the East African Payment System](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>). Its inflows were UGX 1.3 trillion in FY2024/25, 99% in Kenyan and Tanzanian currency.

**Volumes reported.** [The Bank of Uganda report puts EAPS inflows at UGX 1.3 trillion in FY2024/25, up 17%](<https://ubc.go.ug/2025/10/06/bous-new-payment-system-cuts-delays-and-boosts-e-money-by-28-6>). [The Bank of Uganda says 2025 remittance inflows were US$2.5 billion](<https://bou.or.ug/uploads/Bank_of_Uganda_and_IFAD_launch_Remittance_Dashboard_to_strengthen_transparency_and_financial_inclusion_4843a57817.pdf>). Of these, 73 per cent came through digital channels and nearly 61 per cent through mobile money. The United States was the largest sending country, at US$702 million.
