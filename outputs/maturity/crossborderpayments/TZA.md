# Tanzania

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Connecting Africa reports that Airtel Money Africa and PawaPay launched inbound remittances into Tanzania on 7 August 2025](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>). Licensed international money transfer operators can pay into Airtel Money wallets. [Finhive reports the launch of pawaPay's inbound remittance service in Tanzania, one of seven markets](<https://finhive.africa/news/airtel-money-africa-expands-pawapay-partnership-to-supercharge-imto-remittances-across-seven-markets/>). Deposits to Airtel Money wallets are made in real time. [Techreviewafrica reports that Rwanda and Tanzania are advancing a cross-border Instant Payment System proof of concept](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6973/rwanda-and-tanzania-advance-cybersecurity-framework-for-regional-instant-payments>). A technical mission in Kigali under the EU-EAC DEEP Programme is working toward a joint roadmap.

**Regional system.** On 3 November 2026 [Uganda's Minister for East African Community Affairs told Parliament about the East African Payment System](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en>). [It links the national RTGS systems of Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania and Rwanda](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en>), he said. [Banks use it to transfer funds across borders and settle in regional currencies](<https://www.africa-newsroom.com/press/uganda-east-african-community-affairs-eac-member-states-plot-to-reduce-dollar-dependence?lang=en>).
