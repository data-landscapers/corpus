# Tanzania

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Connecting Africa reports that Airtel Money Africa and PawaPay launched inbound remittances into Tanzania on 7 August 2025, letting licensed international money transfer operators pay into Airtel Money wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>). [Finhive reports that Airtel Money Africa and pawaPay launched pawaPay's inbound remittance service for licensed international money transfer operators, with real-time deposits to Airtel Money wallets, in this country among seven markets](<https://finhive.africa/news/airtel-money-africa-expands-pawapay-partnership-to-supercharge-imto-remittances-across-seven-markets/>). [Rwanda and Tanzania are advancing a cross-border Instant Payment System proof of concept, with a technical mission in Kigali under the EU-EAC DEEP Programme working toward a joint roadmap, per techreviewafrica](<https://techreviewafrica.com/news/6973/rwanda-and-tanzania-advance-cybersecurity-framework-for-regional-instant-payments>).
