# Benin

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The Africa Report says the BCEAO pilot of an interoperable instant payment system covering all eight WAEMU states, including Benin, began in early August 2024, intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). [Benin recorded cross-border e-money transfers within the UEMOA in 2024, per the BCEAO annual report on digital financial services](<https://www.lanation.bj/economie/finance-numerique-et-inclusion-plus-de-248-millions-de-comptes-momo-dans-luemoa>).

**Regional system.** [PI-SPI is a unified regional instant payment platform covering the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [Benin is a UEMOA member state on PI-SPI, the regional instant payment platform operated by BCEAO, with 80 participants connected on 2 April 2026](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>).

**Volumes reported.** [Benin had 8 million intra-UEMOA cross-border transfer operations worth CFA 230 billion in 2024, an average of CFA 5,172, per the BCEAO](<https://www.lanation.bj/economie/finance-numerique-et-inclusion-plus-de-248-millions-de-comptes-momo-dans-luemoa>). [ARCEP Benin's 2024 annual report, as reported by Les 4 Vérités, puts mobile financial services at 2,512 million transactions in 2024, of which 0.5% were international transfers](<https://www.les4verites.bj/2-911-milliards-fcfa-de-depot-effectues-en-2024/>).
