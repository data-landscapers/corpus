# Benin

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [The BCEAO began piloting an instant payment system across all eight WAEMU states, Benin included, in August 2024](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). [The BCEAO's report on digital financial services records cross-border e-money transfers from Benin within the UEMOA in 2024](<https://www.lanation.bj/economie/finance-numerique-et-inclusion-plus-de-248-millions-de-comptes-momo-dans-luemoa>). [From 2 November 2026 MTN Mobile Money Benin customers can send and receive money through PI-SPI across the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.24haubenin.info/?Communique-de-MTN-Mobile-Money-Benin=>).

**Regional system.** [PI-SPI is a unified regional instant payment platform covering the eight UEMOA countries](<https://www.lanation.bj/numerique/plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-banques-et-mobile-money-desormais-interconnectes-a-100-dans-luemoa>). [Benin is a UEMOA member state on PI-SPI, the regional instant payment platform operated by BCEAO, with 80 participants connected on 2 April 2026](<https://bceao.int/fr/communique-presse/connexion-la-plateforme-interoperable-du-systeme-de-paiement-instantane-pi-spi-de>).

**Volumes reported.** [Benin had 8 million intra-UEMOA transfers worth CFA 230 billion in 2024, the BCEAO says](<https://www.lanation.bj/economie/finance-numerique-et-inclusion-plus-de-248-millions-de-comptes-momo-dans-luemoa>). [That is an average of CFA 5,172 a transfer](<https://www.lanation.bj/economie/finance-numerique-et-inclusion-plus-de-248-millions-de-comptes-momo-dans-luemoa>). [ARCEP Benin counted 2,512 million mobile financial service transactions in 2024](<https://www.les4verites.bj/2-911-milliards-fcfa-de-depot-effectues-en-2024/>). [Of these, 0.5% were international transfers, Les 4 Vérités reports](<https://www.les4verites.bj/2-911-milliards-fcfa-de-depot-effectues-en-2024/>).
