# Morocco

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In September 2025 Thunes said members of its Direct Global Network can make real-time cross-border payouts to bank accounts in Morocco](<https://www.thunes.com/news/thunes-launches-real-time-payments-into-morocco/>). [The payouts are made in Moroccan dirhams](<https://www.thunes.com/news/thunes-launches-real-time-payments-into-morocco/>). [In October 2024 Tranglo said it launched real-time bank account transfers into Morocco in dirhams](<https://tranglo.com/press/tranglo-launches-bank-account-transfers-in-morocco/>). [The transfers add to its existing cash pickup corridor into Morocco](<https://tranglo.com/press/tranglo-launches-bank-account-transfers-in-morocco/>). [In June 2026 PAPSS listed Morocco as a participant country 'live soon', not yet in service](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).
