# Djibouti

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [IGAD, whose member states include Djibouti, issued terms of reference on 11 February 2026 for a consultant to develop a harmonised regional payment interoperability regulatory framework under the World Bank-financed Eastern Africa Regional Digital Integration Project](<https://igad.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/ToR-IGAD-Payments-Interoperability-V6-1.pdf>). [Capital Newspaper reports SantimPay (Ethiopia) and Meras Djibouti signed a partnership agreement, witnessed by Djibouti ministers, to provide cross-border digital payments, remittances and merchant solutions between the two countries](<https://capitalethiopia.com/2025/09/21/santimpay-meras-djibouti-join-hands-to-strengthen-financial-services/>). [D-Money (Djibouti Telecom) announced in a post of 29 July 2025 that its wallet now sends money abroad to 15 destinations, including Belgium, Ethiopia, Senegal, Canada and Saudi Arabia, through Herman International Exchange](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/djiboutimoney_dmoney-transfertinternational-djibouti-activity-7355990440086515714-KwfX>).

**Regional system.** [PAPSS lists Djibouti as a country with live send-and-receive participants: East African Bank and Salaam African Bank](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>). [AfricaNenda's SIIPS 2024 says Djibouti has integrated to PAPSS, though it is unclear whether any retail transactions are processed](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/siips-2024-mainreport-EN.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>).
