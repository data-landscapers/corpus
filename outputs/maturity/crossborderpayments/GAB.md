# Gabon

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Gabon is piloting GIMACPAY's interoperable QR-code payment system through the national programme MADIGIPAIE, according to Biometric Update](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/gabon-updates-digital-payment-laws-to-support-regional-interoperability>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>). [CLIKPAY offers sub-regional remittances across the CEMAC zone and holds a COBAC licence to operate as a payment service provider in all CEMAC countries](<https://www.uncdf.org/article/9193/unlocking-capital-for-african-fintech-beyond-the-big-four-a-story-from-gabon>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries domestic and cross-border payments within the six CEMAC states including Gabon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Gabon is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC launched on 29 July 2026 an interoperable QR code letting any bank or microfinance app in the CEMAC read a merchant code in any member country, with regional operations routed and cleared via GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
