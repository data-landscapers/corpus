# Gabon

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** On 15 January 2025 Moov Africa Gabon Telecom [launched international transfers in its Moov Money app](<https://www.gabonreview.com/moov-africa-gabon-telecom-lance-le-transfert-dargent-international-avec-moov-money/>). They reach CEMAC countries and about 20 others, among them Senegal, Kenya and Nigeria. Since August 2025 licensed transfer operators [can pay into Airtel Money wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>) through pawaPay. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Airtel Money wallets in Gabon](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>).

**Regional system.** Gabon is one of six CEMAC states on [GIMACPAY, the regional payment platform](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). On 29 July 2026 the central bank BEAC [launched an interoperable QR code](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>), with regional payments cleared through GIMAC. GIMAC figures for the first half of 2026 [rank Moov Gabon and Airtel Gabon as the network's top two issuers](<https://leconomie.info/article/monetique-moov-gabon-devient-le-premier-emetteur-de-paiements-electroniques-de-la-cemac>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for Gabon's own cross-border digital payments. GIMAC reported that its platform [handled 20.3 million transactions worth FCFA 863 billion in 2025](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>), across the six states together.
