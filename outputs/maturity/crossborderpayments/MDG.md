# Madagascar

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Madagascar Newsroom reports that since mid-January 2024 Orange Money Europe app users in Germany, Belgium, Spain, France, Italy and the Netherlands can send money to MVola accounts in Madagascar via the Thunes aggregator, received instantly in ariary](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/02/thunes-partenaire-remittance-mvola.html>). [MFS Africa went live as the first aggregator with MVola in Madagascar, carrying inbound remittances from Europe, the US and the UK and MNO-to-MNO transfers from the rest of Africa](<https://expression.africa/madagascar-mfs-africa-partners-with-mvola-paving-way-for-financial-inclusion/>).
