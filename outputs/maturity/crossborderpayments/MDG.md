# Madagascar

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In October 2023 MFS Africa [went live with the MVola wallet](<https://expression.africa/madagascar-mfs-africa-partners-with-mvola-paving-way-for-financial-inclusion/>), carrying remittances from Europe, the US and the UK. Since January 2024 Orange Money Europe users in six EU countries [can send to MVola accounts](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/02/thunes-partenaire-remittance-mvola.html>), Madagascar Newsroom reported. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Orange Money and Airtel Money wallets in Madagascar](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). Each of these routes ends in a mobile wallet.

**Regional system.** No source found since October 2023 says Madagascar takes part in PAPSS or a regional payment system. The nearest held fact is [MFS Africa's MVola link](<https://expression.africa/madagascar-mfs-africa-partners-with-mvola-paving-way-for-financial-inclusion/>), which also carries transfers from mobile operators elsewhere in Africa.

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact is the [Madagascar Newsroom report](<https://www.madagascarnewsroom.com/2024/02/thunes-partenaire-remittance-mvola.html>) of the Orange Money Europe corridor, which gives no volumes.
