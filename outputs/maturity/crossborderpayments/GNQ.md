# Equatorial Guinea

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including Equatorial Guinea, and the rest of the continent](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). They are to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026. [Afreximbank's release of 9 July 2026 says BEAC has joined PAPSS](<https://www.afreximbank.com/fr/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). BEAC serves Equatorial Guinea among six CEMAC states. PAPSS links 28 countries and 16 payment switches, and CEMAC institutions are to be onboarded through end 2026. [TechAfrica News reported the same on 9 July 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/>). It says PAPSS connects 28 countries and 16 switches, with operationalisation and onboarding of CEMAC institutions planned through end 2026. [Diario Rombe reported in November 2026 that the Muni Dinero wallet, issued with BANGE, allows transfers within the CEMAC zone](<https://diariorombe.es/tecnologia/muni-dinero-la-plataforma-financiera-de-pastor-hassan-obiang-mangue-creado-con-dinero-publico-que-aspira-a-entrar-en-las-cuentas-del-estado-y-concentrar-informacion-economica-de-los-ciudadanos/>).

**Regional system.** [Equatorial Guinea is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [Equatorial Guinea takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>). [AfricaNenda says Equatorial Guinea is one of the six CEMAC states served by GIMACPAY, a regional interoperability platform in service](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY carried about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across the six CEMAC states](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The figure combines domestic and cross-border payments](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
