# Equatorial Guinea

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including Equatorial Guinea, and the rest of the continent, to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [Afreximbank's release of 9 July 2026 says BEAC, serving Equatorial Guinea among six CEMAC states, has joined PAPSS, which links 28 countries and 16 payment switches, with onboarding of CEMAC institutions through end 2026](<https://www.afreximbank.com/fr/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [TechAfrica News reports on 9 July 2026 that BEAC, serving Equatorial Guinea among six CEMAC states, has joined PAPSS, which connects 28 countries and 16 switches, with operationalisation and onboarding of CEMAC institutions planned through end 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/>).

**Regional system.** [Equatorial Guinea is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [Equatorial Guinea takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>). [AfricaNenda says Equatorial Guinea is one of the six CEMAC states served by GIMACPAY, a regional interoperability platform in service](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
