# Equatorial Guinea

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including Equatorial Guinea, and the rest of the continent, to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [BANGE says its new BANGE MOBILE GE app is designed to integrate with regional payment systems, enabling CEMAC operations via the G-MAT Pay gateway](<https://realequatorialguinea.com/economia/bange-lanza-bange-mobile-ge-la-nueva-app-que-facilita-pagos-y-servicios-financieros-en-guinea-ecuatorial/>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries domestic and cross-border payments within the six CEMAC states including Equatorial Guinea](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Equatorial Guinea is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>). [Equatorial Guinea takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
