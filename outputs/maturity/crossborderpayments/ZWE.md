# Zimbabwe

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [EcoCash launched an outbound international money transfer service from Zimbabwe to bank accounts and mobile wallets in countries including South Africa, Zambia, Kenya (M-Pesa) and China (Alipay), with all South African banks activated, per the company statement reported by NewsDay](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries>). [Econet Wireless Zimbabwe says that from 2 May 2024 people in the UK and South Africa can send money free of charge via Sasai Money Transfer into EcoCash US dollar wallets in Zimbabwe](<https://www.econet.co.zw/zimbabwe-econet-opens-first-free-remittance-corridors-to-zimbabwe/>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Regional system.** [Afreximbank's Peter Olowononi says Zimbabwe processes $5.8 million a month through PAPSS, which also clears Chinese currency via a CIPS link](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>).
