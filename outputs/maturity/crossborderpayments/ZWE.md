# Zimbabwe

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Econet Wireless Zimbabwe said that from 2 May 2024 people in the UK and South Africa can send money free of charge into Zimbabwe](<https://www.econet.co.zw/zimbabwe-econet-opens-first-free-remittance-corridors-to-zimbabwe/>). [The money goes through Sasai Money Transfer into EcoCash US dollar wallets](<https://www.econet.co.zw/zimbabwe-econet-opens-first-free-remittance-corridors-to-zimbabwe/>). [In May 2025 EcoCash launched outbound international money transfers from Zimbabwe, NewsDay reported](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries>). [They go to bank accounts and mobile wallets in countries including South Africa, Zambia, Kenya and China](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries>). [Transfers to Kenya reach M-Pesa and those to China reach Alipay](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries>). [All South African banks were activated](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/southerneye/business/article/200041920/ecocash-launches-international-money-transfers-from-zimbabwe-to-other-countries>).

**Regional system.** [In October 2026 Afreximbank's Peter Olowononi said Zimbabwe processes $5.8 million a month through PAPSS](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>). [PAPSS also clears Chinese currency through a link to CIPS](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>).

**Volumes reported.** [In October 2026 Olowononi cited Zimbabwe statistics of $5.8 million a month through PAPSS](<https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/ceo-africa-roundtable-zimbabwe-processes-5-8-million-a-month-through-papss-as-local-currency-settlement-takes-hold/>).
