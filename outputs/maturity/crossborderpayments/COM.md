## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [La Gazette des Comores reports that on 21 February 2024 Mvola Comores launched transfers from Orange Money Europe and Paysend into Mvola Comores and from Mvola Comores to Orange Senegal, adding to existing transfers with Madagascar, Tanzania and Uganda](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/transfert-d%E2%80%99argent-mvola-comores-lance-de-nouveaux-services-.html>). [The Banque Centrale des Comores signed a PAPSS membership agreement in July 2024 and PAPSS implementation in Comoros commenced on 23 August 2024, with commercial banks to begin integrating, per Afreximbank](<https://www.afreximbank.com/central-bank-of-comoros-commencement-of-activities-for-the-implementation-of-papss-in-comoros>).
