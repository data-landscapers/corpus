# Comoros

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** Mvola Comores runs wallet transfers abroad. [On 21 February 2024 it launched transfers in from Orange Money Europe and Paysend, and out to Orange Senegal](<https://lagazettedescomores.com/soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9/transfert-d%E2%80%99argent-mvola-comores-lance-de-nouveaux-services-.html>), La Gazette des Comores reports. These joined existing transfers with Madagascar, Tanzania and Uganda. [In January 2025 MVola listed transfers out to Madagascar, Tanzania, Uganda, Cote d'Ivoire, Morocco, Guinea and Senegal](<https://www.mvola.km/particuliers/>). It also receives transfers from Ria and Orange Money Europe. [The central bank signed a PAPSS membership agreement in July 2024](<https://www.afreximbank.com/central-bank-of-comoros-commencement-of-activities-for-the-implementation-of-papss-in-comoros>). Afreximbank says implementation began on 23 August 2024, with commercial banks still to integrate.
