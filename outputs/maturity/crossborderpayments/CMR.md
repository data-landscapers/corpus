# Cameroon

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Business Tech Africa reports that fintech REasy and Africa Global Logistics are piloting a service, presented in Douala on 2026-10-01, that lets Cameroonian importers pay Chinese suppliers by converting CFA francs to yuan through Alipay, WeChat Pay and Chinese bank accounts](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/cameroon-s-reasy-and-agl-pilot-digital-payment-and-logistics-service-for-china-imports>). [TechAfrica News reports on 9 July 2026 that BEAC, serving Cameroon among six CEMAC states, has joined PAPSS, which connects 28 countries and 16 switches, with operationalisation and onboarding of CEMAC institutions planned through end 2026](<https://techafricanews.com/2026/07/09/beac-joins-papss-to-strengthen-cross-border-payments-in-central-africa/>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>).

**Regional system.** [Cameroon is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC launched on 29 July 2026 an interoperable QR code letting any bank or microfinance app in the CEMAC read a merchant code in any member country, with regional operations routed and cleared via GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
