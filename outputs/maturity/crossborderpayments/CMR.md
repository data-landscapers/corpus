# Cameroon

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** Since September 2026 Nigerian fintech Grey [sends CFA francs to MTN and Orange mobile money wallets in Cameroon](<https://www.businessincameroon.com/finance/2509-16831-nigerian-fintech-grey-opens-direct-transfers-to-mtn-momo-and-orange-money-in-cameroon>), from dollar, euro or pound balances. Business in Cameroon put the fee at $1.80 a transfer. In October 2025 TechCabal said fintech REasy [links local mobile money to Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay](<https://techcabal.com/2025/10/09/cameroons-reasy-raises-1-8m-seed/>). It serves thousands of importers paying suppliers in China. In October 2026 REasy and Africa Global Logistics [presented a pilot for paying Chinese suppliers in yuan](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/cameroon-s-reasy-and-agl-pilot-digital-payment-and-logistics-service-for-china-imports>).

**Regional system.** Cameroon is one of six CEMAC states on [GIMACPAY, the regional payment platform](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). On 29 July 2026 the central bank BEAC [launched an interoperable QR code](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>) that any bank or microfinance app in the zone can read. Regional payments are routed and cleared through GIMAC. In June 2026 PAPSS listed Cameroon as [reachable only through partners](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>), with no direct participant.

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for Cameroon's own cross-border digital payments. GIMAC reported that its platform [handled 20.3 million transactions worth FCFA 863 billion in 2025](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>), across the six states together.
