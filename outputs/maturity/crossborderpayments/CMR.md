# Cameroon

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Business Tech Africa reports that fintech REasy and Africa Global Logistics are piloting a service, presented in Douala on 2026-10-01, that lets Cameroonian importers pay Chinese suppliers by converting CFA francs to yuan through Alipay, WeChat Pay and Chinese bank accounts](<https://www.businesstechafrica.co.za/article/cameroon-s-reasy-and-agl-pilot-digital-payment-and-logistics-service-for-china-imports>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries domestic and cross-border payments within the six CEMAC states including Cameroon](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Cameroon is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC launched on 29 July 2026 an interoperable QR code letting any bank or microfinance app in the CEMAC read a merchant code in any member country, with regional operations routed and cleared via GIMAC](<https://www.gabonreview.com/cemac-moins-de-cash-plus-de-numerique-la-beac-deploie-le-qr-code-interoperable/>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [Gimacpay](<https://lejour.cm/mobile-money-gimacpay-franchit-la-barre-des-600-milliards-fcfa-de-transactions/>).
