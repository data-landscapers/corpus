# Guinea-Bissau

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** Since 28 May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send money to Orange Money and MTN wallets in Guinea-Bissau](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). Revolut opened the route among 14 new corridors to Africa that day. A transfer needs only the recipient's name and phone number.

**Regional system.** Guinea-Bissau is part of [PI-SPI, the WAEMU's regional instant payment system](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>), in service since 30 September 2025. The BCEAO's list of September 2026 [places it among the eight member states on the rail](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>). Five Guinea-Bissau institutions [were authorised on PI-SPI at 31 July 2026](<https://financesao.com/uemoa-pi-spi-atteint-104-etablissements-autorises/>). PAPSS lists the country as [reachable for inbound payments through one mobile wallet](<https://papss.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PAPSS-PAYMENTS-NETWORK-COVERAGE_JUNE2026-5.pdf>), with no direct participant.
