# Central African Republic

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including the Central African Republic, and the rest of the continent, to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY, live since July 2020, carries domestic and cross-border payments within the six CEMAC states including the Central African Republic](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [The Central African Republic is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
