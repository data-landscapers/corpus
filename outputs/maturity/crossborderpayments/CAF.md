# Central African Republic

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including the Central African Republic, and the rest of the continent, to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [Afreximbank's release of 9 July 2026 says BEAC, serving the Central African Republic among six CEMAC states, has joined PAPSS, which links 28 countries and 16 payment switches, with onboarding of CEMAC institutions through end 2026](<https://www.afreximbank.com/fr/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). [BEAC joined PAPSS, announced by Afreximbank on 9 July 2026, and CEMAC banks are to be connected to it by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>).

**Regional system.** [The Central African Republic is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform whose interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). [The Central African Republic takes part in GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform operated by GIMAC](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY processed about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across all six states, domestic and cross-border combined (2022, before the three-year window)](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
