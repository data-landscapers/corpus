# Central African Republic

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [PAPSS says BEAC joining opens new payment corridors between Central Africa, including the Central African Republic, and the rest of the continent](<https://papss.com/media/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). They are to be operationalised with CEMAC institutions through end-2026. [Afreximbank's release of 9 July 2026 says BEAC has joined PAPSS](<https://www.afreximbank.com/fr/beac-joins-papss-connecting-payments-between-cemac-and-the-rest-of-africa/>). BEAC serves the Central African Republic among six CEMAC states. PAPSS links 28 countries and 16 payment switches, and CEMAC institutions are to be onboarded through end 2026. [CEMAC banks are to be connected to PAPSS by end-2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/1007-140077-les-banques-de-la-cemac-seront-integrees-au-systeme-de-paiement-papss-d-ici-fin-2026>).

**Regional system.** [The Central African Republic is one of six CEMAC states on GIMACPAY, the regional platform](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiement-numerique-le-gimac-officialise-la-norme-qr-code-interoperable-de-la-cemac>). Its interoperable QR Code standard was launched on 29 July 2026. [GIMAC operates GIMACPAY, the CEMAC regional payment platform in which the Central African Republic takes part](<https://leconomie.info/article/paiements-numeriques-gimacpay-enregistre-203-millions-de-transactions-pour-863-milliards-de-fcfa-en-2025>).

**Volumes reported.** [AfricaNenda says GIMACPAY carried about 10 million transactions worth $657 million in 2022 across the six CEMAC states](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>). [The figure combines domestic and cross-border payments](<https://www.africanenda.org/uploads/files/SIIPS2023_CaseStudy_CEMAC.pdf>).
