# Zambia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** On 1 March 2024 BankservAfrica [opened an instant low-value payment corridor between South Africa and Zambia](<https://it-online.co.za/2024/03/01/cross-border-payments-now-live-between-sa-and-zambia/>). In January 2026 MTN MoMo Zambia [let customers send to bank accounts in the EU, UK and Canada](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/momo-fnb-launch-zambia-atm-cash-out/G98YdqLGnQ9MX2PD>). Since August 2025 licensed transfer operators [can pay into Airtel Money wallets](<https://www.connectingafrica.com/mobile-money/airtel-money-pawapay-enable-remittances-for-seven-african-countries>) through pawaPay. Since May 2024 Revolut customers in the UK and most of Europe [can send to Airtel Money and MTN wallets in Zambia](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/100931/revolut-connects-mobile-wallets-to-african-telcos-airtel-orange-money-and-mtn>). In September 2026 Stanbic [began settling Zambia-China trade in renminbi](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-says-rmb-payment-system-can-cut-trade-costs-deepen-china-investment/>).

**Regional system.** Zambia is on [TCIB, the SADC cross-border scheme](<https://it-online.co.za/2024/03/01/cross-border-payments-now-live-between-sa-and-zambia/>) live since 2021, which carries the South Africa corridor. COMESA [launched its retail payments platform in October 2025](<https://www.lusakatimes.com/2025/10/10/zambias-zamtel-to-pilot-new-comesa-system-for-instant-cross-border-money-transfers/>) with a pilot on the Zambia-Malawi corridor.
