## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Stanbic Bank Zambia launched direct renminbi settlement for Zambia-China trade over China's CIPS in Lusaka on 23 September 2026, per the commerce ministry and the bank](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-says-rmb-payment-system-can-cut-trade-costs-deepen-china-investment/>). [MTN MoMo Zambia introduced direct wallet-to-bank transfers in January 2026, the first Zambian mobile money provider to let customers send funds to international bank accounts in the EU, UK and Canada, per ITWeb Africa](<https://www.itweb.africa/article/momo-fnb-launch-zambia-atm-cash-out/G98YdqLGnQ9MX2PD>). [COMESA, with EU funding, is supporting deployment of its Digital Retail Payment Platform across eight pilot countries including Zambia, with an implementation plan still to be drawn up](<https://www.comesa.int/comesa-eu-partnership-shaping-a-digital-future/>).
