# Zambia

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [IT-Online reports that BankservAfrica opened the first instant low-value digital payment corridor between South Africa and Zambia on 1 March 2024, after SendHome and SamPay joined the TCIB scheme](<https://it-online.co.za/2024/03/01/cross-border-payments-now-live-between-sa-and-zambia/>). [Stanbic Bank Zambia launched direct renminbi settlement for Zambia-China trade over China's CIPS in Lusaka on 23 September 2026, per the commerce ministry and the bank](<https://www.zambiamonitor.com/zambia-says-rmb-payment-system-can-cut-trade-costs-deepen-china-investment/>). [The Bankers Association of Malawi's president said the COMESA digital retail payments platform is being piloted between Malawi and Zambia for local-currency transactions, as reported on 27 March 2026](<https://mbc.mw/regional-digital-payments-to-boost-trade-ease-forex-pressure-rbm/>).

**Regional system.** [IT-Online reports that the SADC TCIB scheme, live since 2021 and operated by BankservAfrica, now carries the South Africa-Zambia corridor, with SendHome and SamPay as its newest participants](<https://it-online.co.za/2024/03/01/cross-border-payments-now-live-between-sa-and-zambia/>).
