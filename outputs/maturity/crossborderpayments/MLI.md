# Mali

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [TerraPay's network carries remittances from money-transfer operators in the US, Canada and Europe into Wave mobile wallets in Mali through a single integration, so users receive funds instantly](<https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/105780/terrapay-enables-cross-border-remittances-for-wave-mobile-money-users-in-mali>). [The Africa Report says the BCEAO pilot of an interoperable instant payment system covering all eight WAEMU states, including Mali, began in early August 2024, intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). [Orange Finances Money Mali and TerraPay say Orange Money Mali customers can now receive money transfers from several countries into their wallets through TerraPay's direct connection and global network](<https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/orange-finances-money-mali-et-terrapay-etablissent-un-partenariat-strategique-pour-des-paiements-transfrontaliers-fluides-302028891.html>).

**Regional system.** [Mali is a UEMOA member covered by PI-SPI, the regional instant payment system in service since 30 September 2025](<https://www.forbesafrica.com/current-affairs/2026/10/07/interoperability-not-truly-free-fintech-experts-on-west-africas-instant-payments-rollout>). [The BCEAO list places Mali in the eight-country WAEMU PI-SPI regional instant payment rail](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/inline-files/Liste%20des%20participants_PI-SPI_30_sept_2026_0.pdf>). [Mali has 12 institutions on the BCEAO list authorised to open PI-SPI, the UEMOA regional instant payment platform, to the public as of 2 April 2026](<https://www.bceao.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/Liste-des-participants_PI-SPI_2%20avril_2026.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** [L'Oeil du Sahel, citing BCEAO 2024 data, puts intra-UEMOA transfers at 5.63% of the value of Mali's 18,744,827 million FCFA of e-money transactions in 2024](<https://oeildusahel.com/pi-spi-mobile-money-impact-economique-aes/>).
