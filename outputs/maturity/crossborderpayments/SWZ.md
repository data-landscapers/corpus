# Eswatini

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Low-value transactions within the Common Monetary Area (Eswatini, South Africa, Lesotho, Namibia) are migrating from domestic electronic funds transfer rails to the Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis payment scheme, per ITWeb Africa](<https://itweb.africa/article/egypt-eswatini-deepen-banking-cooperation/KWEBb7yL4wbvmRjO>). [The South African Reserve Bank directive says low-value cross-border electronic funds transfers within the Common Monetary Area (Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, South Africa) are currently executed through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). [The directive says low-value cross-border electronic funds transfers within the Common Monetary Area are currently executed through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Eswatini payment service providers route low-value CMA cross-border transfers through the SADC-RTGS system and must move them to the TCIB scheme by 31 March 2027](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf>). [The South African Reserve Bank directive says Common Monetary Area low-value transfers, including this country's, run through the SADC-RTGS regional system and must move to the TCIB scheme by April 2027](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>).
