# Eswatini

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [ITWeb Africa reports that low-value transactions within the Common Monetary Area are migrating from domestic electronic funds transfer rails to the Transactions Cleared on an Immediate Basis payment scheme](<https://itweb.africa/article/egypt-eswatini-deepen-banking-cooperation/KWEBb7yL4wbvmRjO>). The area covers Eswatini, South Africa, Lesotho and Namibia. [The South African Reserve Bank directive says low-value cross-border electronic funds transfers within the Common Monetary Area are currently executed through SADC-RTGS](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). [The Eswatini directive says the same](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf>).

**Regional system.** [Eswatini payment service providers route low-value CMA cross-border transfers through the SADC-RTGS system](<https://www.centralbank.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Eswatini-CMA-Retail-EFT-Payment-Directive-v1.pdf>). They must move them to the TCIB scheme by 31 March 2027. [The South African Reserve Bank directive says Common Monetary Area low-value transfers, including Eswatini's, run through the SADC-RTGS regional system](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202504/52435gon3106.pdf>). That directive gives April 2027 as the date to move to the TCIB scheme.
