# Senegal

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [Wave is integrated with Sendwave Wallet, which lets diaspora users hold USDC and withdraw it in CFA francs through Wave, according to economist Seydou Bocoum](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>). [The Africa Report says the BCEAO pilot of an interoperable instant payment system covering all eight WAEMU states, including Senegal, began in early August 2024, intended to allow cross-border transactions across the zone](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>).

**Regional system.** [Senegal has 43 of the 175 participants authorised to open PI-SPI services to the public across the eight WAEMU countries, per the list the BCEAO updated on 30 September 2026](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). [PI-SPI, the BCEAO regional instant payment platform covering the UMOA, has been in service since 30 September 2025, with 80 participants connected at 24 June 2026](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>).

**Volumes reported.** No source held since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
