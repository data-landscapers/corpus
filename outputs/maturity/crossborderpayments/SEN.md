# Senegal

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** In August 2024 the BCEAO [began piloting an instant payment system across the eight WAEMU states](<https://www.theafricareport.com/359378/west-africa-five-questions-about-the-regions-new-interoperable-payment-system/>). It was meant to carry cross-border transactions in the zone. Wave [is linked to Sendwave Wallet](<https://www.osiris.sn/le-vrai-gagnant-du-nouveau-reglement-bceao-n-est-pas-wave-mais-orange-money.html>), an economist said in October 2026. Its diaspora users can hold USDC and withdraw it in CFA francs through Wave.

**Regional system.** PI-SPI, the BCEAO's regional instant payment platform, [has run since 30 September 2025](<https://www.seneweb.com/fr/news/Economie/mobile-money-pourquoi-le-duel-orange-money-wave-ne-resume-plus-le-marche_n_505004.html>). At 30 September 2026 [Senegal held 43 of its 175 authorised participants](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>). In November 2026 Wave said [its customers in Senegal can send and receive through it](<https://bifinances.com/actualites/wave-precise-lacces-a-pi-spi-au-senegal-et-en-cote-divoire>).

**Volumes reported.** No source found since October 2023 gives a figure for cross-border digital payments. The nearest held fact is [Senegal's count of 43 authorised PI-SPI participants](<https://www.agenceecofin.com/actualites-finance/0110-142036-wave-rejoint-la-plateforme-de-paiement-instantane-de-la-bceao-un-an-apres-son-lancement>).
