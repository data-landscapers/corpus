# Mozambique

## Cross-border functionality

**Corridor in service.** [In April 2024 ITWeb reported that MTN's MoMo app opened a wallet corridor from South Africa to Mozambique](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-increases-remittance-services-to-10-new-countries/lLn147mQr947J6Aa>). [Migrants in South Africa can send money home to wallets in Mozambique](<https://itweb.africa/article/mtn-momo-increases-remittance-services-to-10-new-countries/lLn147mQr947J6Aa>). [In October 2026 Banco de Moçambique Governor Felisberto Navalha said Mozambique is finalising preparations to join the SADC RTGS](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/mozambique-to-join-sadc-real-time-payments-system-in-q1-2027/>). [The South African Reserve Bank (SARB) hosts the system](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/mozambique-to-join-sadc-real-time-payments-system-in-q1-2027/>). [He expects Mozambique to be integrated in the first quarter of 2027](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/mozambique-to-join-sadc-real-time-payments-system-in-q1-2027/>). [Customers would then settle South African invoices from metical accounts](<https://360mozambique.com/economy/banking/mozambique-to-join-sadc-real-time-payments-system-in-q1-2027/>).

**Regional system.** [SARB's SADC-RTGS statistics for June 2026 list Mozambique as a participating country](<https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20June%202026.pdf>). [Six banks take part: Absa, Access Bank, FNB, Nedbank, Mozabanco and Standard Bank](<https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20June%202026.pdf>).

**Volumes reported.** [SARB's per-country table for June 2026 gives Mozambique 215 settled transactions](<https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20June%202026.pdf>). [Their value was ZAR 261.79 million](<https://www.resbank.co.za/content/dam/sarb/what-we-do/payments-and-settlements/settlement-services/sadc-rtgs/2026-stats/SADC-RTGS%20Settlement%20Statistics%20and%20Indicators%20-%20June%202026.pdf>).
