# Madagascar

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In January 2025 the African Development Bank's energy factsheet [put rural access at 7 per cent](<https://www.afdb.org/en/mission-300-africa-energy-summit/accelerating-africas-energy-transition/madagascar-energy-factsheet>), against 57 per cent in urban areas. The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 26.5 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 10.9 in 2021, 11.1 in 2022 and 14.6 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The factsheet's figure is 20 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
