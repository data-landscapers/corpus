# Cameroon

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In January 2025 the World Bank [said 75 per cent of rural Cameroonians remain without electricity](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/01/16/cameroon-journey-toward-affordable-reliable-and-universal-electricity-access-for-all>), so about 25 per cent have it. Its rural electricity access series [gives 42.7 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 24.8 in 2021, 25 in 2022 and 26 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The January 2025 figure is about 2 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
