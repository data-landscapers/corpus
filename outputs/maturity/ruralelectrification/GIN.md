# Guinea

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In November 2025 figures at the round table on the national rural electrification plan [put rural access at 25.7 per cent](<https://www.guinee360.com/26/11/2025/electrification-rurale-un-plan-a-36-milliards-de-dollars-pour-sortir-la-guinee-du-noir-dici-2040/>). The rural electrification agency's director general cited 23 per cent at the same event. The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 29.4 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 21.3 in 2021. The UN in Guinea [gave 7 per cent of rural households for 2023](<https://guinee.un.org/fr/274963-lacc%C3%A8s-%C3%A0-l%C3%A9nergie-un-levier-pour-acc%C3%A9l%C3%A9rer-la-r%C3%A9alisation-des-objectifs-de-d%C3%A9veloppement>). The round table's figure is 1.3 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
