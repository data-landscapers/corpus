# Senegal

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In February 2025 the rural electrification agency [put the rural electrification rate at 65.67 per cent](<https://www.rts.sn/actualite/detail/a-la-une/acces-a-lelectricite-vers-une-acceleration-de-lelectrification-rurale-au-senegal>), with an estimated access level of 86 per cent. The energy ministry [gives 65.84 per cent for 2023](<https://energie-mines.gouv.sn/electricite/>). The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 67.1 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 43.4 in 2021. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The agency's rate is 14.3 points short of 80, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
