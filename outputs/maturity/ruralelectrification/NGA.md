# Nigeria

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In January 2026 the National Bureau of Statistics was cited as [counting 40.4 per cent of rural households connected to power](<https://www.thecable.ng/can-nigeria-achieve-100-electricity-access-by-2030-as-pledged-in-new-ndc/>). The same count gave 82.2 per cent of urban households. The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 23.5 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 26.3 in 2021, 27 in 2022 and 32.9 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The bureau's figure is 12.6 points short of 53, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
