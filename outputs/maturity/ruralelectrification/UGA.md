# Uganda

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In August 2026 an opinion piece by the Community Climate and Energy Shield Initiative [put rural access at 9.1 per cent](<https://dailyexpress.co.ug/2026/08/03/can-mission-300-close-ugandas-electricity-access-gap/>). It put national grid access at 25.3 per cent. The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 44.8 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 35.9 in 2021, 35.9 in 2022 and 42.4 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The August 2026 figure is 17.9 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
