# Mali

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In March 2025 an official of the rural electrification agency [put rural access at about 25 per cent](<https://apnews.com/article/mali-electricity-energy-crisis-ea3846c19723b3bfbd275155440d1517>). The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 31.4 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 18.3 in 2021, 19.7 in 2022 and 22.7 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. Since 2021 the series rose by 13.1 points. The official's figure is about 2 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
