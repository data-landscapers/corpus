# Mali

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In 2024, [31.4 per cent of rural people had electricity](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), by the World Bank's rural electricity access series. The same series [gives 18.3 in 2021, 19.7 in 2022 and 22.7 in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>). Since 2021 the figure rose by 13.1 points. It is 21.6 points short of 53, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The figure counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it, not how reliable or affordable it is. The series is the one Tracking SDG7 publishes, built from household surveys and modelled between them, so a country's own survey can give a different figure. In March 2025 an official of the rural electrification agency [put rural access at about 25 per cent](<https://apnews.com/article/mali-electricity-energy-crisis-ea3846c19723b3bfbd275155440d1517>). The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
