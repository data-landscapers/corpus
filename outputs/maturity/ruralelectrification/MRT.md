# Mauritania

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In October 2023 the African Development Bank put [Mauritania's rural electrification rate at nearly 6 per cent](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1052984/0a8f772c021b78a8401a4c30cd56b06a/PRO202311151052970.pdf>), with 48 per cent of the population living in rural areas. The World Bank's rural electricity access series last gives a figure for 2012, when [0.8 per cent of rural people had electricity](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), so no source found says whether the rate has risen since 2023. It is about 21 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts rural people with any electricity, not how reliable or affordable it is. The government's stated aim, the bank reports, was to double the rate by 2024/25 and reach universal access by 2030. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
