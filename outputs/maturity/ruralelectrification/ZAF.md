# South Africa

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In 2024, [86.3 per cent of rural people had electricity](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), by the World Bank's rural electricity access series. The same series [gives 93.4 in 2021, 93.4 in 2022 and 94 in 2023](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>). Since 2021 the figure fell by 7.1 points. It is 8.7 points short of 95, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts, and the latest figure for 2023 or later sets the stage, so a fall is a fall. The figure counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it, not how reliable or affordable it is. The series is the one Tracking SDG7 publishes, built from household surveys and modelled between them, so a country's own survey can give a different figure. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
