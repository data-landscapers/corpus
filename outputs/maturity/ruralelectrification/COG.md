# Congo

## Rural access to electricity

**Rural access to electricity.** In September 2025 the energy minister [put rural access at 1 per cent](<https://www.adiac-congo.com/content/electricite-rurale-les-partenaires-techniques-et-financiers-invites-soutenir-le-pezor-166853>), counted from the national utility's low-voltage subscribers. He said it was no more than 10 per cent even counting clandestine connections. The World Bank's rural electricity access series [gives 19.4 per cent of rural people for 2024](<https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/EG.ELC.ACCS.RU.ZS>), and 12.4 in 2021, 13 in 2022 and 10.8 in 2023. The series counts rural people with any electricity, from the grid or off it. The minister's figure is 26 points short of 27, the next line. The study's lines are 27, 53, 80 and 95 per cent of rural people, the frame's own cuts. The 80 per cent line is the Agenda 2063 figure for households, applied here to rural people, and 95 per cent is universal access.
