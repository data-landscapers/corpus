# Uganda

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [New Vision reports EMIS was rolled out in July 2022 to replace the Annual School Census, collects data from schools nationwide, and the Ministry uses its data to determine capitation grants, with registration mandatory for all schools](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). [The national EMIS, adopted in 1993, collects and aggregates enrollment, teacher and infrastructure data, and the Annual School Census was most recently run in 2025](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). 4 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Learner records.** [New Vision reports that under EMIS every learner is assigned a unique Learner Identification Number (LIN), with registration mandatory for all schools nationwide](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>).

**Linked.** [The DHIS2-Ed system was linked to the Ministry of Health's eIDSR platform for school-based surveillance indicators and collates examination data](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).
