# Uganda

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In June 2026 New Vision reported that EMIS was rolled out in July 2022 to replace the Annual School Census](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). [It collects data from schools nationwide, and registration is mandatory for all schools](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). [The Ministry uses its data to determine capitation grants](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). [In April 2026 a HISP Uganda brief said the national EMIS, adopted in 1993, collects and aggregates enrolment, teacher and infrastructure data](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [The brief says the Annual School Census was most recently run in 2025](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).

**Learner records.** [In June 2026 New Vision reported that under EMIS every learner is assigned a unique Learner Identification Number (LIN)](<https://www.newvision.co.ug/category/education/govt-rolls-out-new-emis-rules-makes-complianc-NV_235163_082026>). [In October 2024 Insight Post said the Ministry of Education assigns Learner Identification Numbers through the EMIS](<https://insightpostug.com/concern-as-unique-identifiers-confuse-students-parents-educators/>).

**Linked.** [In April 2026 the HISP Uganda brief said the DHIS2-Ed system was linked to the Ministry of Health's eIDSR platform](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [The link carries school-based surveillance indicators](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>). [DHIS2-Ed also collates examination data](<https://website.hispuganda.org/pdfs/PolicybriefPDHIS2EdTermlySchoolReportinUganda.pdf>).
