# Liberia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry said regular Annual School Census collection has resumed following interruptions after 2021, alongside the Statutory School Record System at school level](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>). [The Liberian Observer reports the Ministry of Education is seeking a real-time education data system with a central dashboard and a digitised EMIS, after a 28-29 September 2026 consultation to draft an EMIS Policy backed by a three-year EMIS Action Plan](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>). [Deputy Minister Thomas Momo Parker says Liberia is moving to EMIS 2.0 and is committed to establishing a digitized EMIS under a costed three-year EMIS Action Plan with a USD 4.5 million budget, 60% government funded](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/strengthening-emis-through-peer-learning-how-liberia-leading-its-own-data-transformation>).

**Learner records.** No source held since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.liberianobserver.com/news/moe-pushes-for-real-time-school-data-system/article_8b8f6a05-6bcd-4adb-9460-d004f8f07b16.html>).
