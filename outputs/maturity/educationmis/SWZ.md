# Eswatini

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [DHIS2 for Education describes a fully operational Eswatini EMIS that served as the source for the country's 2022 UIS reporting on enrolments, repeaters and staffing and produced a 2026 bursary list for orphans and vulnerable children](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>). [DHIS2 for Education says Eswatini's Ministry of Education and Training is modernising its EMIS with DHIS2, a transition begun in 2020 that replaced a centralised MS-Access system](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>). [Eswatini's Ministry of Education and Training, with UNICEF and HISP Uganda, integrated disability indicators into its DHIS2-based national EMIS and trained over 900 school administrators and focal teachers on the updated data-capture tools](<https://education.dhis2.org/eswatini-disability-indicators-emis/>).

**Learner records.** [DHIS2 for Education says the Eswatini DHIS2 implementation supports school-level and learner-level data and introduces unique personal identification numbers (PINs)](<https://education.dhis2.org/data-the-power-is-in-the-use/>). [GPE KIX says Eswatini's PIN-driven EMIS introduced individual-level student and staff tracking across all 976 schools](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>). [The SEMIS App holds individual data on learners and staff using a personal identification number (PIN), at national scale](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/eswatini/>).

**Linked.** [Aggregate and individual EMIS data are connected to national identity](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/eswatini/>).
