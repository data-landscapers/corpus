# Nigeria

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Minister Alausa said the digitised Nigeria Education Management Information System was providing data on enrolment, teachers, classrooms, laboratories, libraries and connectivity](<https://newstimes.com.ng/2026/09/ncc-launches-zero-rated-access-to-educational-platforms/>). [He said over 240,000 schools had been geomapped](<https://newstimes.com.ng/2026/09/ncc-launches-zero-rated-access-to-educational-platforms/>). [Minister Alausa said the government is expanding education digitalisation through the Digital Nigeria Education Management Information System](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/10/fg-to-give-students-100mb-data-daily-from-october-1/>). [He said over 240,000 schools were geo-mapped and the Annual School Census was being digitised](<https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/09/10/fg-to-give-students-100mb-data-daily-from-october-1/>). [Education minister Alausa gave the same account of the system, the 240,000 geo-mapped schools and the digitised census](<https://www.thepointng.com/reactions-as-fg-plans-daily-free-data-for-nigerian-students/>).
