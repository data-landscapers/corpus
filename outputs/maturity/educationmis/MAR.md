# Morocco

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The ministry ran the 2025-2026 annual school census from 20 October to 20 November 2025](<https://fr.expresstv.ma/2025/10/le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-lance-le-recensement-scolaire-annuel-pour-lannee-scolaire-2025-2026/>). A ministry letter named Massar as the main source of its statistics. [An earlier census memorandum, reported in November 2024, told regional officials to complete the census data in Massar](<https://fr.hibapress.com/news-144060.html>). Primary enrolment was part of it.

**Learner records.** [The 2024 memorandum orders every new first-year primary pupil enrolled in Massar](<https://fr.hibapress.com/news-144060.html>). It covers public, private and non-formal schools, and forbids double enrolment. Each pupil's nationality and any disability are recorded. Nothing found names a unique learner number.

**Linked.** Nothing found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or exchange data with another government system. The nearest held fact is [the enrolment of each pupil in Massar](<https://fr.hibapress.com/news-144060.html>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says Massar gives each learner a unique number. No link to the national ID or another government system has been found.
