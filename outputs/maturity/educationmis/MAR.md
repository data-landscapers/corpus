# Morocco

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The ministry ran the 2025-2026 annual school census from 20 October to 20 November 2025](<https://fr.expresstv.ma/2025/10/le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-lance-le-recensement-scolaire-annuel-pour-lannee-scolaire-2025-2026/>). A ministry letter named Massar as the main source of its statistics. [An earlier census memorandum, reported in November 2024, told regional officials to complete the census data in Massar](<https://fr.hibapress.com/news-144060.html>). Primary enrolment was part of it.

**Learner records.** A 2024 ministry circular says Massar [digitises each pupil's school file and gives each pupil a digital identifier](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>), towards a single national learner register. [The 2024 census memorandum orders every new first-year primary pupil enrolled in Massar](<https://fr.hibapress.com/news-144060.html>), in public, private and non-formal schools. It forbids double enrolment.

**Linked.** The same 2024 circular says Massar's enrolment services [rely on digital identity and can download data from the civil status register](<https://ar.hibapress.com/details-517593.html>). The pupil record holds the civil status number.
