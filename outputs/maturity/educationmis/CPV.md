# Cape Verde

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In March 2025 the national education directorate said SIGE was in use in basic and secondary schools](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/14072-implementacao-do-sige-e-passo-fundamental-para-eficiencia-da-gestao-educativa-no-pais-dne>). Pre-school was still to follow. Teachers and school secretariats must enter their data in it. [In July 2025 the directorate began national training on a new special-needs module in SIGE](<https://www.governo.cv/direcao-nacional-da-educacao-realiza-acao-formativa-no-novo-modulo-de-criancas-com-necessidades-educativas-especiais-no-sige/>), built by Devtrust with UNICEF funding. [In April 2026 the GPE approved a US$1 million grant for PRISE 2026-2028](<https://www.governo.cv/parceria-global-para-educacao-aprova-subvencao-nao-reembolsavel-de-um-milhao-de-dolares-ao-governo/>). Its work includes expanding and modernising SIGE.

**Learner records.** Nothing found since October 2023 says SIGE holds a record for each learner with a unique number. [The directorate says teachers and school secretariats enter data in it](<https://www.asemana.cv/index.php/atualidade/14072-implementacao-do-sige-e-passo-fundamental-para-eficiencia-da-gestao-educativa-no-pais-dne>).
