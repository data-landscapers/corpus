# Zimbabwe

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In November 2026 the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education said it compiles school enrolment figures through the revised EMIS](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>). [It sends them to the Ministry of Finance for each year's education budget](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>). [It said 12,324 schools nationwide are equipped to use the system](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/data-systems-transform-zimbabwes-schools>). [The ministry's EMIS Report, cited by NewZimbabwe, counts 39,736 learners who dropped out in 2025](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [They were 13,032 primary and 26,704 secondary](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [Kuyenda Collective says Zimbabwe has adopted an EMIS](<https://www.kuyendacollective.org/article/investing-in-education-management-information-systems-emis/>). [It is used to collect, store and manage education data including enrolment, teacher numbers and educational facilities](<https://www.kuyendacollective.org/article/investing-in-education-management-information-systems-emis/>). [Most rural schools lack access to feed it in real time](<https://www.kuyendacollective.org/article/investing-in-education-management-information-systems-emis/>).
