# Zimbabwe

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education's EMIS Report, cited by NewZimbabwe, shows 39,736 learners dropped out in 2025 (13,032 primary, 26,704 secondary), so the ministry publishes national education statistics through its EMIS](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>). [Kuyenda Collective says Zimbabwe has adopted an EMIS that is used to collect, store and manage education data including enrolment, teacher numbers and educational facilities, though most rural schools lack access to feed it in real time](<https://www.kuyendacollective.org/article/investing-in-education-management-information-systems-emis/>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.newzimbabwe.com/zimbabwe-targets-to-re-enrol-out-of-school-learners-as-40000-dropped-out-in-2025-alone/>).
