# Ghana

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Education completed a review of Education Management Information System indicators at a 17-18 December 2025 workshop, and stakeholders agreed to integrate the revised indicators into the next national school census](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/education-ministry-updates-emis-indicators-to-strengthen-ict-integration-in-schools/>). [The Ministry of Education's PMDV Dashboard API documentation lists an EMIS schools statistics endpoint returning school counts by level and public or private status, with regional and district filters, and an enrolment rates endpoint](<https://pmdv.moedatadashboard.com/api_documentation>).
