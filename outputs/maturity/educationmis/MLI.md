# Mali

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In October 2024 UNESCO said the education ministry's planning unit (CPS) collected 2023-24 national data on school-health indicators integrated into the SIGE](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-la-production-des-statistiques-liees-la-sante-du-systeme-dinformation-et-de-gestion>). [A workshop in Segou analysed them on 21-26 October 2024](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-la-production-des-statistiques-liees-la-sante-du-systeme-dinformation-et-de-gestion>). [In March 2026 UNESCO said the SIGE holds national data, collected regularly by the ministry](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>). [A March 2026 workshop analysed its data for the 2023-2024 and 2024-2025 school years](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>). [In June 2025 a call for tenders said the ministry has committed to setting up and operationalising a SIGE](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>). [EUMC was recruiting a consultant to strengthen it for integrating JIGITUGU](<https://www.malipages.com/appel-offre/acquisition-et-dotation-en-materiels-informatique/>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [in March 2026 UNESCO said the SIGE holds national data for two school years](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest held fact: [in March 2026 UNESCO said school-health indicators are being integrated into the SIGE](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/lunesco-appuie-le-ministere-de-leducation-nationale-pour-la-production-des-statistiques-en-sante>).
