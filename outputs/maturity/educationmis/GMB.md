# Gambia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Gambia has a national EMIS running on DHIS2 built with the education ministry, where data clerks enter the annual census and regional planners use dashboards daily](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>). [DHIS2 says MoBSE's Education Data Centre, on DHIS2-Ed, now gives live dashboards that update as new information flows in, with historical census data migrated into it, replacing paper census forms and yearbooks](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). [GPE KIX says The Gambia's Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education is integrating individual-level learner and teacher data and has SEMIS pilots](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/south-south-collaboration-stronger-education-systems-lessons-learned-cross-country-learning>).

**Learner records.** [DHIS2 says MoBSE's digital student registry, piloted in 2020 in 200 schools, gives each learner a unique ID and has scaled nationwide, with nearly 80% of Gambian learners included](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). [After a 2020 pilot in 200 schools, the ministry scaled the individual learner module to national level in 2021, launching the individual student identification number (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/the-gambia/>).
