# Gambia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education began building its education information system on DHIS2 in 2018](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>). [Data clerks enter the annual school census into it, and regional planners use its dashboards daily](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>). [In August 2025 DHIS2 said the ministry's Education Data Centre had replaced paper census forms with live dashboards](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). [In September 2026 the ministry opened the Education Data Center to the public](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>). It brings the school census, attendance reports and other sources into one portal.

**Learner records.** [In August 2025 DHIS2 said the ministry's digital student registry gives each learner a unique ID](<https://education.dhis2.org/gambia-modernizing-education-data-kix-knowledge-cafe/>). Piloted in 200 schools in 2020, it has since scaled nationwide and holds nearly 80% of Gambian learners. [In September 2026 DHIS2 said learner records and daily attendance reporting had been added to the system since 2018](<https://education.dhis2.org/the-gambia-launches-online-education-data-centre/>).
