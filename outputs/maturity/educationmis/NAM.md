# Namibia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In 2024 the Ministry of Education, Arts and Culture said its EMIS division collects data from all state and private schools twice a year](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>). [It publishes the Fifteenth School Day Report and the Annual Education Census](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>). [The ministry lists census reports for 2019 to 2023](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the EMIS division collected data from all state and private schools](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the ministry published the Annual Education Census](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>).
