# Namibia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Education, Arts and Culture says its EMIS division collects data from all state and private schools twice a year and publishes the Fifteenth School Day Report and the Annual Education Census, listing census reports for 2019 to 2023](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.moe.gov.na/index.php/dep/dep-fa/dir-pad/emis>).
