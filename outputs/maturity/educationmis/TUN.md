# Tunisia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** On 18 November 2026 [Tunisia's education minister told parliament that the school-life system is an integrated government system](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A>). It was built with the communication technologies ministry. [Teachers enter and follow pupils' marks in it](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A>), and it holds pupils' school records and teachers' careers. The minister said the state hosts its data in a sovereign site, and that national exams will follow.

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system gives each learner a unique number. The nearest fact found: in November 2026 [the minister said it holds information on each pupil's school path](<https://www.alchourouk.com/article/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%B9%D8%B7%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%81%D9%88%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D9%82%D8%B1-%D8%B3%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%8A>).
