## Education information system

**Education information system.** [On 19 April 2024 Eritrea's Ministry of Education submitted a programme document to GPE](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-eritrea-gpe-grant-program-document.pdf>). [It seeks US$14.02m, through UNICEF](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-eritrea-gpe-grant-program-document.pdf>). [The funds include strengthened routine data collection, processing, storage and use in the national EMIS](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/node/document/download?file=document%2Ffile%2F2024-04-eritrea-gpe-grant-program-document.pdf>).
