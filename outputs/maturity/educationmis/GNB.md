# Guinea-Bissau

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In October 2025 FEC said practical training began on the SIGE 2.0 education information and management platform](<https://www.fecongd.org/2025/10/20/projeto-peside-quer-estatisticas-de-qualidade-para-a-educacao-na-guine-bissau/>). [It started on 6 October 2025 in Tombali, Quinara, Bafata and Gabu](<https://www.fecongd.org/2025/10/20/projeto-peside-quer-estatisticas-de-qualidade-para-a-educacao-na-guine-bissau/>). [The EU-funded PESIDE project runs it with the Ministry of Education](<https://www.fecongd.org/2025/10/20/projeto-peside-quer-estatisticas-de-qualidade-para-a-educacao-na-guine-bissau/>). [In February 2026 a Global Partnership for Education blog said PESIDE aims to strengthen the Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/better-data-improve-education-guinea-bissau>).
