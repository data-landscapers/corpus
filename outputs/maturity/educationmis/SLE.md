# Sierra Leone

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Basic and Senior Secondary Education has launched a national initiative, funded by GPE, to build a DHIS2-based learner-centred education data hub joining the annual school census, teacher management and examination data, rolling out across all 16 districts](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>). [UNICEF published request for proposal 9200546 for a Unique Learner ID (ULID) system and data warehouse with Sierra Leone as beneficiary country, deadline 28 October 2025](<https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/281417>). [The MBSSE EMIS page lists Annual School Census reports for 2015 to 2022 and 2025, and says EMIS is a centralised database with information about every student, teacher and school](<https://mbsse.gov.sl/emis/>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest fact: [in April 2026 the education ministry began building a learner-centred education data hub on DHIS2](<https://dhis2.org/sierra-leone-national-education-data-hub/>).
