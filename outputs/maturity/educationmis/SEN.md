# Senegal

## Education information system

**Education information system.** In August 2026 the coordinator of SIMEN, the ministry's integrated management system, said the ministry [had built 41 platforms in-house and was deploying them across the country](<https://letechobservateur.sn/seyni-ndiaye-fall-coordonnateur-du-simen-linteroperabilite-est-une-realite-chez-nous/>). All of them feed one data warehouse. Inspectors used it to analyse school results at a workshop in Saly from 13 to 15 August 2026. The workshop planned the national roll-out of five of the platforms for the 2026-2027 school year, among them PLANETE 3 for school management.

**Learner records.** The coordinator said [pupils, teachers and other staff each receive a unique national education identifier](<https://letechobservateur.sn/seyni-ndiaye-fall-coordonnateur-du-simen-linteroperabilite-est-une-realite-chez-nous/>). It decides which platforms each person sees. The ministry described [the same identifier in 2018](<https://www.education.sn/pages/systeme-dinformation-et-de-management-de-leducation-nationale-simen>).

**Linked.** The coordinator said the ministry would [open its system to other state platforms through the government's interoperability platform](<https://letechobservateur.sn/seyni-ndiaye-fall-coordonnateur-du-simen-linteroperabilite-est-une-realite-chez-nous/>). He gave no date and named no exchange in service.
