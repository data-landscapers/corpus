## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In September 2025 La Nation reported a circular of the education minister dated 1 September 2025](<https://www.lanation.dj/une-rentree-scolaire-2025-2026-placee-sous-le-signe-de-la-performance-et-de-la-reforme/>). [It makes consolidating the Système d'Information pour la Gestion de l'Éducation (SIGE) a priority](<https://www.lanation.dj/une-rentree-scolaire-2025-2026-placee-sous-le-signe-de-la-performance-et-de-la-reforme/>). [The work runs through the GOSE and SMIS platforms, to produce reliable, regularly updated data](<https://www.lanation.dj/une-rentree-scolaire-2025-2026-placee-sous-le-signe-de-la-performance-et-de-la-reforme/>).
