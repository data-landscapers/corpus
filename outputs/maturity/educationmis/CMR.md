# Cameroon

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [UNESCO says it, the Government of Cameroon and the World Bank officially launched the Integrated Sectoral Platform for the Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-and-partners-officially-launch-integrated-emis-platform-and-its-methodological-tools>). [PAREC says the ministry of basic education held an official ceremony on 8 May 2025 presenting the integrated sectoral SIGE platform (sige-sectoriel.cm), designed to centralise statistics of all education and training subsectors and hosted by the National Institute of Statistics](<https://www.parec-cameroun.net/presentation-officielle-de-la-plateforme-sectorielle-integree-du-sige-et-des-documents-et-outils-conceptuels-et-methodologie/>). [In June 2024 the National Institute of Statistics published the first education and training sector yearbook, for 2021-2022, and said more detailed data can be found on the online EMIS platform](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/statistique/statistical-yearbook-of-the-education-and-training-sector-in-cameroon-2021-2022/>).
