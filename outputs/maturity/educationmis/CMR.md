# Cameroon

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In June 2024 the National Institute of Statistics published the first education and training sector yearbook, for 2021-2022](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/statistique/statistical-yearbook-of-the-education-and-training-sector-in-cameroon-2021-2022/>). It pointed users to the online EMIS platform for more detailed data. [On 4 June 2024 PAREC handed the institute a server, computers and a SQL Server licence to run the platform](<https://ins-cameroun.cm/en/emis-operationalization-new-it-hardware-for-the-nsi/>). [On 8 May 2025 the basic education ministry presented the integrated sectoral SIGE platform](<https://www.parec-cameroun.net/presentation-officielle-de-la-plateforme-sectorielle-integree-du-sige-et-des-documents-et-outils-conceptuels-et-methodologie/>). It is designed to hold the statistics of every education and training subsector, hosted by the institute. [UNESCO, the government and the World Bank launched it together](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-and-partners-officially-launch-integrated-emis-platform-and-its-methodological-tools>).
