# Congo

## Education information system

**Education information system.** In December 2025 the World Bank's TRESOR project [recorded no integrated EMIS in Congo](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121925064520650/pdf/P179410-b8959451-e7dc-4e80-8fd1-c81ec062a66f.pdf>), with one targeted by July 2030. In May 2026 an African Union workshop in Brazzaville [reviewed the state of the country's EMIS](<http://www.au.int/en/pressreleases/20260519/congo-moves-modernize-education-data-systems-au-iped>). In August 2026 the state IT agency ACSI [presented its integrated education management project, the SIGE](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608110263.html>), to the National Assembly's Ecofin committee. It is one of the applications ACSI is building for the state under the 2025 finance law.

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact is [ACSI's account of the SIGE project](<https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202608110263.html>), which does not describe its records.
