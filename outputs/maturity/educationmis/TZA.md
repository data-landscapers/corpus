# Tanzania

## Education information system

**Education information system.** PO-RALG publishes Basic Education Statistics in Tanzania each year. [The statistics bureau's 2025 abstract draws its school tables from the 2025 edition](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/statistics/documents/en-1790933026-Statistical%20Abstract%202025.pdf>). They count 3,425,822 secondary students in 2025, in government and non-government schools. [The ministry launched PReM, an electronic student register, in 2018](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/news/tanzania-launched-electronic-student-enrollment-system>).

**Learner records.** [PO-RALG's privacy policy says SIS data is shared with education authorities such as NECTA and MOEST, and keys each student by a Personal Registration Number (PREM)](<https://sis.tamisemi.go.tz/privacy-policy>). [Under PReM each student is assigned a unique identification number, with information on students and their guardians on record, for public and private primary schools (2018, before the three-year window)](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/news/tanzania-launched-electronic-student-enrollment-system>).

**Linked.** [The School Information System shares learner data with NECTA, the examinations council](<https://sis.tamisemi.go.tz/privacy-policy>), and with the ministry.
