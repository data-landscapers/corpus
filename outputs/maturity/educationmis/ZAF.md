# South Africa

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Department of Basic Education records learner enrolment through LURITS](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35150/>), with 13,596,869 learners recorded for 2025. [On 2-4 February 2026 the department's EMIS directorate tested a modernised, cloud-based SA-SAMS with NECT](<https://www.education.gov.za/ArchivedDocuments/ArchivedArticles/SA-SAMSModernisation.aspx>). A phased primary-school pilot and a national rollout are to follow.

**Learner records.** [In January 2026 the minister said LURITS holds data for each learner, with ID numbers](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35453/>). Records for about 10.3 million public-school learners in grades 1 to 11 were linked across 2020 to 2025. [A December 2025 reply says learners without ID numbers are held under other unique identifiers](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35150/>).

**Linked.** Nothing found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or exchange data with another government system. The nearest held fact is [the linking of LURITS records across years](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35453/>).
