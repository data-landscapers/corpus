# South Africa

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The DBE's EMIS Directorate, with NECT, held user acceptance testing on 2-4 February 2026 of the modernised SA-SAMS, a cloud-based system to strengthen data management across public schools nationally, ahead of a phased primary-school pilot and national rollout](<https://www.education.gov.za/ArchivedDocuments/ArchivedArticles/SA-SAMSModernisation.aspx>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Learner records.** [The Minister of Basic Education's written reply says the DBE's LURITS holds data at individual learner level with ID numbers, and that LURITS data for 2020 to 2025 covering about 10.3 million public-school learners in Grades 1-11 was linked across years](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35453/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-question/35453/>).
