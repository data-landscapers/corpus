# Ethiopia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The Ministry of Education's EMIS and ICT Executive Office published the Education Statistics Annual Abstract 2022/2023 (October 2023) and says the EMIS, available at decentralized levels, has been collecting and processing education performance data](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>). [The deal record says the US$69m World Bank additional financing includes full integration of refugee learners into the national EMIS and US$750,000 earmarked for EMIS enhancement and disaggregated reporting](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099032326144529868/pdf/BOSIB-3d398de8-e525-4d3e-bf20-dd47590f809c.pdf>). [HISP Ethiopia and the HISP Centre say Ethiopia has adopted DHIS2-Ed as its education management information system, with implementation starting with schools, woreda offices and zones in the SER, SWEPR, Oromia and Benishangul Gumuz regions](<https://education.dhis2.org/using-data-to-power-teaching-at-the-right-level-in-ethiopia/>).

**Learner records.** No source held since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://moe.gov.et/storage/Books/ESAA%202022-23%20Final.pdf>).
