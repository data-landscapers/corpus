# Angola

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The ministry's planning office says the data for its 2022/2023 statistical yearbook were collected through SIGE](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>). It calls the platform in operation but not a finished product. Structured Excel sheets were used to check the data and calculate indicators. [ADEA says the ministry is discussing an EMIS legal framework and a modernised national EMIS](<https://adeanet.org/en/news/angola-embarks-journey-transform-education-data-systems>). [In August 2026 the Angolan firm Mwango Brain took on the technical work of that project with the planning office](<https://revistaoutside.com/2026/08/05/empresa-angolana-assume-implementacao-tecnica-do-sistema-de-dados-da-educacao/>). Its tasks include a national EMIS policy and training for national technicians. [A World Bank project modernises the higher education ministry's own HEMIS](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/26pad_english_test_p179154_mpa_phase_1_gpe_qar_answered_74704772466e18d05d13fd_9552e1cf64.pdf>).

**Learner records.** Nothing found since October 2023 says SIGE holds a record for each learner. [The planning office describes it as a source of yearbook statistics](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>).

**Linked.** Nothing found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or exchange data with another government system. The nearest held fact is [the planned EMIS legal framework](<https://adeanet.org/en/news/angola-embarks-journey-transform-education-data-systems>).
