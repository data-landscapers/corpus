# Angola

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The education ministry's planning office says the data for its 2022/2023 statistical yearbook were collected through SIGE, the education management information system, which is in operation but not a finished product: structured Excel sheets were used to finish checking the data and calculating indicators](<https://www.ine.gov.ao/publicacoes/detalhes/MzgzMzA%3D>). [ADEA says the ministry is discussing an EMIS legal framework and a modernised national EMIS](<https://adeanet.org/en/news/angola-embarks-journey-transform-education-data-systems>), and [a World Bank project modernises the higher education ministry's own HEMIS](<https://c2a.portais.gov.ao/uploads/26pad_english_test_p179154_mpa_phase_1_gpe_qar_answered_74704772466e18d05d13fd_9552e1cf64.pdf>).
