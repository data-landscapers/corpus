# Burkina Faso

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In September 2024 IIEP said it had helped integrate education-in-emergencies data into Burkina Faso's SIGE](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/fr/articles/proteger-leducation-contre-les-attaques-les-donnees-esu-au-burkina-faso>). [This let the education ministry update the annual school census results and report school reopenings to central government](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/fr/articles/proteger-leducation-contre-les-attaques-les-donnees-esu-au-burkina-faso>). [The director general of education studies and statistics, Sylvain Ouédraogo, said the SIGE is being renovated](<https://faso7.com/2024/12/30/burkina-faso-les-journees-des-statistiques-de-leducation-nationale-et-de-lalphabetisation-jsena-pour-reflechir-a-la-production-de-statistiques-adaptees-aux-besoins/>). [He named a geo-referenced census of structures, a national pupil file, online enrolment and school ID cards as the means](<https://faso7.com/2024/12/30/burkina-faso-les-journees-des-statistiques-de-leducation-nationale-et-de-lalphabetisation-jsena-pour-reflechir-a-la-production-de-statistiques-adaptees-aux-besoins/>).
