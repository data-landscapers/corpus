# Burkina Faso

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [IIEP says it supported integrating education-in-emergencies data into Burkina Faso's SIGE, which let the education ministry update the results of the annual school census and keep central government informed of school reopenings (ministry official Benjamin Kohoun, 2024)](<https://www.iiep.unesco.org/fr/articles/proteger-leducation-contre-les-attaques-les-donnees-esu-au-burkina-faso>). [The director general of education studies and statistics, Sylvain Ouédraogo, said the education management information system (SIGE) is being innovated, with a geo-referenced census of structures, a national pupil file, online enrolment and school ID cards named as levers to renovate it](<https://faso7.com/2024/12/30/burkina-faso-les-journees-des-statistiques-de-leducation-nationale-et-de-lalphabetisation-jsena-pour-reflechir-a-la-production-de-statistiques-adaptees-aux-besoins/>).
