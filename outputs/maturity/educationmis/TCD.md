# Chad

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [UNICEF Innocenti says Chad's EMIS has routinely collected data from schools since 2009, analysed centrally with a statistical yearbook produced annually](<https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/stories/supporting-middle-tier-actors-chad-access-and-use-data-decision-making>). [UNESCO says the higher-education ministry (MESRSFP) trained its statistics and digitalisation staff on 20-25 July 2026 on the StatEduc application, as part of modernising Chad's education information management system (SIGE), under the GPE-financed PRET](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/le-mesrsfp-renforce-les-capacites-de-ses-agents-sur-lapplication-stateduc-pour-ameliorer-la>). [UNESCO says it handed over on 15 June 2026 about USD 550,000 of GPE-financed computing equipment to education ministry bodies, aimed at modernising and decentralising Chad's education information management system (SIGE)](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/investir-dans-les-donnees-pour-transformer-leducation-au-tchad-lunesco-remet-des-equipements>).
