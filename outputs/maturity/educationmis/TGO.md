# Togo

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Togo's SIGE adds DHIS2 on top of CSPro-based statistical infrastructure, with StatEduc used for the annual school census](<https://education.dhis2.org/gpe-kix-study-tour/>). [Togo's education ministry processes annual census data through StatEduc, per the implementing researchers and ministry staff](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>). [DHIS2 is integrated to process the data and provide information products to subnational levels](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/togo-empowering-schools-and-districts-data>). [The Ministry of Primary, Secondary, Technical Education is piloting DHIS2 alongside the existing StatEduc application, with Norad, GPE KIX and HISP WCA support](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo>). [StatEduc continues as the data-collection tool; the page gives national scale since 2019](<https://education.dhis2.org/implementation/togo>).
