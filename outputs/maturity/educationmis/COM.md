# Comoros

## Education information system

**Education information system.** Comoros runs a national education platform, e-Msomo, built by the education ministry with the digital agency ANADEN and UNICEF. In December 2025 UNICEF said it [held over 80,000 pupils, 700 schools and 3,000 teachers](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-comores_anaden-scolaires-%C3%A9l%C3%A8ves-activity-7411757191952580608-VR1Z>) in one national database. It produces data for planning and decisions. Schools and records are being added as the platform grows. An earlier AFD project, run from 2019, [listed deploying the SIGE information system](<https://www.afd.fr/fr/projets/projet-de-performance-et-gouvernance-de-leducation-aux-comores-pgec>) among its aims.

**Learner records.** UNICEF says e-Msomo [follows each pupil's school path, attendance and marks](<https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/unicef-comores_anaden-scolaires-%C3%A9l%C3%A8ves-activity-7411757191952580608-VR1Z>), at every level from pre-school to the last year of secondary. Parents can follow their children's progress. Not every school is on the platform yet.
