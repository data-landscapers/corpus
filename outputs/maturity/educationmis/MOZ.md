# Mozambique

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [A GPE KIX-supported assessment on 3-4 August 2026 found Mozambique has an established EMIS with multiple education data subsystems, electronic data platforms and processes for validating and disseminating education statistics, and that education statistics already inform teacher deployment and resource allocation](<https://www.gpekix.org/news/mozambique-sets-priorities-stronger-education-data-system>). [The education ministry's Planning and Cooperation Directorate runs the annual 3 March school census (Levantamento Escolar 2024, public and private schools) and its Statistics Department publishes the resulting education statistics brochure each year](<https://mozdata.ine.gov.mz/index.php/catalog/119>). [The Ministry's Head of Statistics said Mozambique plans a fully digital platform for real-time entry, piloting tools like DHIS2 for offline dashboards](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2025/10/KIX%20Knowledge%20Cafe%20Policy%20Brief_Building%20Responsive%20EMIS%20Policies%20for%20Robust%20Education%20Data%20Systems.docx%20%282%29.pdf>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.gpekix.org/news/mozambique-sets-priorities-stronger-education-data-system>).
