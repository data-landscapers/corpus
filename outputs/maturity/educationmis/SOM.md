# Somalia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The ministry's July 2025 terms of reference describe its online EMIS, rolled out nationwide in 2019](<https://somalijobs.com/tenders/somalia/16723600513019036/terms-of-reference-(tor)-for-revision-and-expansion-of-the-education-management-information-system-(emis)>). [It holds primary (grades 4 to 8), secondary and alternative basic education data on one platform](<https://somalijobs.com/tenders/somalia/16723600513019036/terms-of-reference-(tor)-for-revision-and-expansion-of-the-education-management-information-system-(emis)>). [GPE says MOECHE initiated the EMIS in 2016](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>). [It collects and analyzes data from schools on an annual basis](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>). [All primary schools in the Federal Member States and Banaadir are registered in it](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>). [The Ministry of Education's Joint Education Sector Review report incorporates quantitative data from the Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://moe.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Somalia-JRES-Report-2023.pdf>). [The Springer study says the Somali Ministry of Education has recently taken steps to close education system gaps through EMIS application](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43621-026-02703-1>). [It surveys 210 users on its effectiveness](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43621-026-02703-1>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest fact: [in July 2025 the ministry sought a firm to add individual student-level data to the EMIS](<https://somalijobs.com/tenders/somalia/16723600513019036/terms-of-reference-(tor)-for-revision-and-expansion-of-the-education-management-information-system-(emis)>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest fact: [in February 2024 GPE said the EMIS collects data from schools each year](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>).
