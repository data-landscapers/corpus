# Somalia

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [GPE says the EMIS, initiated in 2016 by MOECHE, collects and analyzes data from schools on an annual basis, and all primary schools in the Federal Member States and Banaadir are registered in it](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>). [The Joint Education Sector Review report of the Ministry of Education says it incorporates quantitative data from the Education Management Information System (EMIS)](<https://moe.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Somalia-JRES-Report-2023.pdf>). [The Springer study says the Somali Ministry of Education has recently taken steps to close education system gaps through EMIS application, and surveys 210 users on its effectiveness](<https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43621-026-02703-1>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.globalpartnership.org/blog/somalia-better-data-management-system-improving-education>).
