# Burundi

## Education information system

**Education information system.** Burundi's education ministry is modernising its information system, the SIGE, with UNESCO. Under a December 2022 agreement it [built a human-resources module for the SIGE](<https://www.jobrapide.org/offres/avis-recrutement/lunesco-recherche-une-consultante-internationale-sige-burundi/>), with hardware, internet and solar backup, UNESCO said in December 2024. A workshop in mid-2024 [planned the module's link to the STATEDUC statistics tool](<https://mesrs.gov.bi/atelier-dappropriation-et-de-revue-de-la-planification-des-activites-du-projet-dappui-a-loperationnalisation-du-module-de-gestion-des-ressources-humaines-du-sige-burundi/>). In May 2026 UNESCO [trained seven ministry staff in database administration](<https://www.unesco.org/fr/articles/renforcement-du-sige-au-burundi-des-cadres-du-menrs-en-formation-yaounde>). In June 2026 an African Union Commission workshop in Bujumbura [set out to strengthen the system](<https://www.continental-infos.com/flash-infos/bujumbura-burundi-from-strategy-to-systems-au-iped-supports-burundis-next-step-in-strengthening-education-data/>), as the country validated its EMIS strategy.
