# Sudan

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [UNESCO states it handed IT equipment to the Red Sea State Ministry of Education on 12 May 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/strengthening-education-red-sea-state-through-digital-transformation>). [The equipment comprised 30 tablets, two desktops, routers and other gear](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/strengthening-education-red-sea-state-through-digital-transformation>). [It supports implementation of the Education Management and Information System Pilot Project (EMIS)](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/strengthening-education-red-sea-state-through-digital-transformation>). [UNESCO states it handed over EMIS and Monitoring and Evaluation hardware to Sudan's Ministry of Education, funded by the Global Partnership for Education](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/sudan-advances-education-reform-ministry-declares-2026-year-tvet>). [The handover was reported on 26 April 2026](<https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/sudan-advances-education-reform-ministry-declares-2026-year-tvet>).
