# Cote d'Ivoire

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Ecofin Agency reports that since 2022 the Education Ministry has deployed an Integrated Education Management Information System (SIGE) with MCC support under a $536 million Compact](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2508-58296-cote-d-ivoire-turns-to-digital-mapping-to-improve-school-planning>). [It was tested in Bouaké and San Pedro, with nationwide extension planned](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2508-58296-cote-d-ivoire-turns-to-digital-mapping-to-improve-school-planning>). [Fraternité Matin reports the education minister launched the SIGE on 10 July 2025, funded by the MCC](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635269/societe/education-digitale-mariatou-kone-lance-un-outil-revolutionnaire-pour-une-ecole-plus-performante>). [It was tested in Bouaké and San Pedro and is to be extended nationwide when financing allows](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635269/societe/education-digitale-mariatou-kone-lance-un-outil-revolutionnaire-pour-une-ecole-plus-performante>).
