# Cote d'Ivoire

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Ecofin Agency reports that since 2022 the Education Ministry has deployed an Integrated Education Management Information System (SIGE) with MCC support under a $536 million Compact, tested in Bouaké and San Pedro with nationwide extension planned](<https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-services/2508-58296-cote-d-ivoire-turns-to-digital-mapping-to-improve-school-planning>). [Fraternité Matin reports the education minister launched the Système d'Information et de Gestion de l'Éducation (SIGE) on 10 July 2025, funded by the MCC, tested in the pilot regions of Bouaké and San Pedro and to be extended to the whole country pending financing](<https://www.fratmat.info/article/2635269/societe/education-digitale-mariatou-kone-lance-un-outil-revolutionnaire-pour-une-ecole-plus-performante>).
