# Madagascar

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Madagascar's authorities launched at a 15-17 July 2024 workshop in Toamasina the digitisation of schools, starting in Atsinanana, covering data collection and a pupil register, with the education ministry planning extension to other regions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/actualites/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/madagascar-met-ses-ecoles-au-numerique>). Work to [modernise the education administration's information system began in 2023](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/du-contenu-a-la-gestion-madagascar-accelere-la-numerisation-de-son-systeme-educatif>), and a financing law for the TALIM programme, adopted by the Senate in 2025, provides for modernising the education management information system. [INSTAT reports the education ministry's planning directorate collected the 2022-2023 annual school census from schools, entered it in statistical software developed by the ministry's DTIC, and published the national yearbook (2023, before the three-year window)](<https://instat.mg/p/men-annuaire-statistique-de-leducation-2022-2023>).
