# Madagascar

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Madagascar's authorities launched at a 15-17 July 2024 workshop in Toamasina the digitisation of schools, starting in Atsinanana, covering data collection and a pupil register, with the education ministry planning extension to other regions](<https://www.wearetech.africa/actualites/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/madagascar-met-ses-ecoles-au-numerique>). [Work to modernise the education administration's information system began in 2023](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/du-contenu-a-la-gestion-madagascar-accelere-la-numerisation-de-son-systeme-educatif>). [A financing law for the TALIM programme, adopted by the Senate in 2025, provides for modernising the education management information system](<https://www.wearetech.africa/fr/fils/actualites/gestion-publique/du-contenu-a-la-gestion-madagascar-accelere-la-numerisation-de-son-systeme-educatif>). [In April 2023 INSTAT said the planning directorate collected the 2022-2023 school census and entered it in ministry software](<https://instat.mg/p/men-annuaire-statistique-de-leducation-2022-2023>). [The directorate published the national yearbook](<https://instat.mg/p/men-annuaire-statistique-de-leducation-2022-2023>).
