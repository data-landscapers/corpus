# Kenya

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Techweez reports Kenya is migrating from NEMIS to the Kenya Education Management Information System (KEMIS), which became the required platform for the 2026 Grade 10 selection, with the PS telling the Public Accounts Committee that KEMIS is not yet complete](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>). [Biometric Update reports Kenya will begin a pilot in July 2025 of the Kenya Education Management Information System (KEMIS) to replace NEMIS, set to fully go live by September, per Principal Secretary Julius Bitok](<https://www.biometricupdate.com/202505/kenya-to-harmonize-education-sector-data-through-maisha-namba-id-integration>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Learner records.** [The Ministry of Education KEMIS Secretariat circular says KEMIS holds learner records marked Valid or Invalid by an operational Learner Validation Engine and generates a Unique Learner Identifier (ULI) for learners in schools](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

**Linked.** No source held since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

***Noted, not assessed***. [KNEC examination and assessment databases](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

***Not held***. No source held since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system.
