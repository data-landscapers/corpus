# Kenya

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In September 2025 the government moved from NEMIS to KEMIS, an integrated system meant to hold student records from early childhood to tertiary, the Daily Nation reports](<https://nation.africa/kenya/news/education/education-data-systems-switch-schools-millions-in-funding-gaps-5633286>). [KEMIS was the required platform for the 2026 Grade 10 selection, though the principal secretary told the Public Accounts Committee it is not yet complete](<https://techweez.com/2026/09/09/the-nemis-to-kemis-dilemma/>).

**Learner records.** [The Ministry of Education KEMIS Secretariat circular says KEMIS holds learner records marked Valid or Invalid by an operational Learner Validation Engine and generates a Unique Learner Identifier (ULI) for learners in schools](<https://arena.co.ke/ministry-circular-guidance-on-learner-validation-in-kemis/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system.
