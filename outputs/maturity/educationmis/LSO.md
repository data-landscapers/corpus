# Lesotho

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [UNESCO-IICBA says Lesotho's district education offices collect, validate and transmit school-level information into the national EMIS, which runs on DHIS2](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20Education%20Data%20Management%20ES%20LS%20_May.%202026.pdf>). [The Ministry of Education and Training is developing LEMIS on DHIS2 covering 4,819 institutions, not yet live for nationwide collection](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>). [A ministry officer writing for GPE KIX says Lesotho's education ministry is making a transition to an EMIS operating system that can process both aggregated and individual data, alongside a draft EMIS policy](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>).
