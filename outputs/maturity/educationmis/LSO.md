# Lesotho

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [In October 2026 an AU-IPED brief said Lesotho had adopted DHIS2 for Education](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>). [It is the platform for the national EMIS, LEMIS](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>). [All 365 secondary schools report through it, the brief said](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>). [A JMERC call of the same month says district education officers collect, validate and transmit school data into the national EMIS](<https://jmerc.org/careers/consultancy-to-consolidate-education-data-management-and-data-use-in-lesotho-and-eswatini-phase>). [In May 2026 the ministry said LEMIS was still being built](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>). [It is meant to cover 4,819 institutions](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>). [It was not yet live for nationwide collection](<https://education.dhis2.org/building-a-smarter-education-data-system-in-lesotho-with-dhis2/>). [In November 2025 a ministry officer said the ministry is moving to an EMIS](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>). [That EMIS can process both aggregate and individual data](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>).

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest fact: [in October 2026 the AU-IPED brief said LEMIS covers individual learner and staff records](<https://www.iped.africa/news-event/from-data-entry-to-data-leadership-lessons-from-lesothos-dhis2-for-education-training>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 UNESCO-IICBA said the national EMIS runs on DHIS2](<https://www.iicba.unesco.org/sites/default/files/medias/fichiers/2026/05/Request%20for%20Proposals_Technical%20Assistance%20for%20strengthening%20Education%20Data%20Management%20ES%20LS%20_May.%202026.pdf>).

***Also on file***. [teacher management information system](<https://www.gpekix.org/blog/enhancing-education-data-systems-lesotho-through-peer-exchange>).

***Not found***. No school census run through the system has been found since October 2023; only secondary schools are shown reporting through it. No record for each learner has been found.
