# Malawi

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [The ministry's Education Statistics Report 2023/24 comes from the Annual School Census, run through the EMIS](<https://cms.nsomalawi.mw/api/download/400/ASC-Report-2024.pdf>). It counts 5,420,159 primary pupils and 538,800 secondary students. [Malawi also collects data through cohort tracking, school mapping and learning assessments](<https://unlockingdata.africa/2026/10/29/how-can-education-systems-build-education-data-systems-that-enable-better-decisions/>). Paper reporting, manual aggregation and parallel systems still slow the data down, the 2026 piece says.

**Learner records.** [The Unlocking Data Initiative policy brief says cohort tracking within the EMIS generates longitudinal learner-level data through Learner Identification Numbers assigned to pupils on entry to school, though the data has rarely been analysed since 2012](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest fact: [in May 2026 a policy brief said pupils get Learner Identification Numbers on entry to school](<https://www.gpekix.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Saddick%20et%20al.%20-%202026%20-%20From%20Data%20Collection%20to%20Data%20Use%20Strengthening%20decision-making%20at%20subnational%20levels%20in%20Malawi.pdf>).
