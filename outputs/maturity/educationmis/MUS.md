# Mauritius

## Education information system

**Education information system.** [A UNESCO IITE project with Chengdu Group, completed in 2025, piloted an education management information system for secondary schools](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/unesco-iite-and-chengdu-group-complete-project-on-inclusive-digital-education-in-mauritius-and-rwanda/>). The pilot is the only education information system on record for the country. Nothing found says the ministry runs its school census through a national system. Nothing found says it publishes its education statistics from one either.

**Learner records.** Nothing found says a national system holds a record for each learner. The nearest held fact is the [2025 secondary-school pilot](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/unesco-iite-and-chengdu-group-complete-project-on-inclusive-digital-education-in-mauritius-and-rwanda/>). It is not described as holding learner records.

**Linked.** Nothing found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or exchange data with another government system. The nearest held fact is the same [2025 pilot](<https://iite.unesco.org/news/unesco-iite-and-chengdu-group-complete-project-on-inclusive-digital-education-in-mauritius-and-rwanda/>).

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the school census runs through a national system. None says education statistics come from one.
