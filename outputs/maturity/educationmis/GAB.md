## Education information system

**Education information system.** [Gabon Media Time reports that the Ministry of National Education has begun integrating school health and well-being indicators (hygiene, nutrition, water access, safety) into its Education Management Information System (SIGE)](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-integration-de-la-sante-scolaire-dans-le-systeme-de-gestion-educative>). [SOSUP launched a national operation to identify schools and assign each learner a unique national identifier (INA), collecting school and pupil files through provincial and zonal academic delegates to centralise school data, per Le Journal des Lycées](<https://lejournaldeslycees.ga/2024/10/19/operation-nationale-didentification-des-etablissements-et-des-apprenants/>).
