# Gabon

## Education information system

**Education information system.** In August 2025 the education ministry [began adding school health indicators to its education management information system, the SIGE](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-integration-de-la-sante-scolaire-dans-le-systeme-de-gestion-educative>). They cover hygiene, nutrition, water access and safety. In October 2024 SOSUP [launched a national operation to identify schools and give each learner a unique national identifier](<https://lejournaldeslycees.ga/2024/10/19/operation-nationale-didentification-des-etablissements-et-des-apprenants/>). Provincial and zonal delegates collect the school and pupil files for it.

**Learner records.** No source found since October 2023 says the system holds a record for each learner. The nearest is the [October 2024 operation to give each learner a national identifier](<https://lejournaldeslycees.ga/2024/10/19/operation-nationale-didentification-des-etablissements-et-des-apprenants/>).

**Linked.** No source found since October 2023 says learner records are linked to the national ID or another government system. The nearest is the [SIGE's August 2025 extension to school health](<https://gabonmediatime.com/gabon-integration-de-la-sante-scolaire-dans-le-systeme-de-gestion-educative>).

***Also on file***. The education ministry's [statistical yearbook for 2020/2021](<https://new.instatgabon.org/uploads/folder_1/RGOE/Annuaire_2020-2021_31_10_2023_.pdf>), published in February 2023. It rests on a school census entered on tablets in 2021.

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the school census is run through the system. The 2021 census is not tied to it.
