# Egypt

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Egypt's Ministry of Electricity [reports 1.3 interruptions per subscriber for FY2025-26](<https://newdemo.elwatannews.com/news/details/8360120>), across the distribution companies in every governorate. That is 0.11 outages a month, down from 1.7 a year in FY2021-22. The ministry's figure counts how often each customer's supply was cut in the year. The survey instead asks firms how many outages they had in a typical month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count firms report at 16.55 a month in 2013, 1.72 in 2016, 0.82 in 2020 and 4.16 in 2025](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). The ministry's figure is newer and is the utility's own, so it is the one used. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, none has fewer outages a month than Egypt.
