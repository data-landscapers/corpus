# Sudan

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [Firms in Sudan reported 3.42 power outages in a typical month in the World Bank's 2014 Enterprise Survey](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). It is older than 2023, so it sets no stage.

***Also on file***. [Load shedding and power interruptions in the national and isolated grids (World Bank Sudan Energy Sector Update, Sept 2024): No outage count given; interruptions sometimes last for months; load shedding from network damage](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099080625100525928/pdf/P171810-aceaafc9-7439-4838-974c-81284effd33a.pdf>); [Summer peak load shedding (World Bank diagnostic, June 2019): No count given; load shedding during summer peak hours](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/486961588608080192/pdf/From-Subsidy-to-Sustainability-Diagnostic-Review-of-Sudan-Electricity-Sector.pdf>).

***Not found***. No figure for 2023 or later has been found for how often the power goes off. The latest held is 3.42 outages a month, for 2014.
