# Senegal

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [The energy regulator CRSE reports Senelec's SAIFI at 9.12 interruptions per customer per year in 2024 (9.12/12 = 0.76 a month), against a norm of 15](<https://www.crse.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-2024-CRSE-VF.pdf>); that is 0.76 outages a month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count at 5.54 in 2014 and 1.88 in 2024](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). An interruption frequency counts how many times in a year the average customer's supply was cut; divided by 12 it gives the outages in a month, the figure this study's lines are drawn on. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; at one or fewer, Senegal is within all four. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 5 have fewer outages a month than Senegal. [The energy regulator CRSE reports Senelec's SAIFI at 8.4 interruptions per customer per year in 2023 (8.4/12 = 0.70 a month)](<https://www.crse.sn/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-2024-CRSE-VF.pdf>).
