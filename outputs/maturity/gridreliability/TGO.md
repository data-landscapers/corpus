# Togo

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [ARSE, Togo's electricity regulator, reports a national SAIFI of 23 for 2024 in its key-figures table, which is 1.92 outages a month when divided by 12](<https://www.arse.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-Activites-ARSE-2024_13022026.pdf>); that is 1.92 outages a month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count at 5.23 in 2016 and 2.74 in 2023](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). An interruption frequency counts how many times in a year the average customer's supply was cut; divided by 12 it gives the outages in a month, the figure this study's lines are drawn on. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; Togo is within two a month and 0.9 above one. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 14 have fewer outages a month than Togo. [ARSE reports a national SAIFI of 26 for 2023 in its key-figures table, which is 2.17 outages a month when divided by 12](<https://www.arse.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-Activites-ARSE-2024_13022026.pdf>). [ARSE reports a national SAIFI of 28 for 2022 in its key-figures table, which is 2.33 outages a month when divided by 12](<https://www.arse.tg/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rapport-Activites-ARSE-2024_13022026.pdf>).
