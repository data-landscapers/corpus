# Gambia

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [NAWEC's electricity chief operating officer said records show two to six blackouts a month, against nearly 100 before 2017 (6 is taken); the document, dated 2026-06-04, gives no other date](<https://standard.gm/nawec-blames-maintenance-challenges-for-worst-power-outages1/>). That is 6 outages a month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count at 20.71 in 2018 and 7.41 in 2023](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). The figure is the utility's own count of blackouts, stated in public. It is used in place of the survey because it is the utility's and is newer. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month. Gambia is within ten a month and 1.0 above five. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 31 have fewer outages a month than Gambia.
