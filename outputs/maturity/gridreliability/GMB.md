# Gambia

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [NAWEC's Chief Operating Officer for Electricity said current records show an average of between two and six blackouts a month (the top of the range, 6, is taken), against nearly 100 a month before 2017; the document is dated 2026-06-04 and gives no other date](<https://standard.gm/nawec-blames-maintenance-challenges-for-worst-power-outages1/>); that is 6 outages a month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count at 20.71 in 2018 and 7.41 in 2023](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). The figure is the utility's own count of blackouts, stated in public, and is used in place of the survey because it is the utility's and is newer. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; Gambia is within ten a month and 1.0 above five. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 30 have fewer outages a month than Gambia.
