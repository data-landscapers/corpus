# Cameroon

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** The regulator ARSEL's 2025 annual report, as quoted by EcoMatin, [puts interruptions at 64.04 per customer in 2025, up from 53.30 in 2024](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-coupures-delectricite-126-heures-par-client-en-2025-selon-larsel-46-heures-selon-socadel>). That is 5.34 outages a month. Minutes of a validation meeting in July 2026 [give 59.42 a year by ARSEL's reckoning, 4.95 a month](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-coupures-delectricite-126-heures-par-client-en-2025-selon-larsel-46-heures-selon-socadel>). The same minutes [give 15.09 a year by the operator Socadel's reckoning, 1.26 a month](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-coupures-delectricite-126-heures-par-client-en-2025-selon-larsel-46-heures-selon-socadel>), and the two sides did not agree. Firms in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey [reported 10.65 outages in a typical month in 2024](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>), and [7.72 in 2016](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). An interruption frequency counts how many times in a year the average customer's supply was cut. Divided by 12, it gives the outages in a month. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month, and Cameroon is 0.34 above five. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 29 have fewer outages a month than Cameroon.

***Also on file***. [ARSEL SAIDI: 147.83 hours per customer in 2025](<https://ecomatin.net/cameroun-coupures-delectricite-126-heures-par-client-en-2025-selon-larsel-46-heures-selon-socadel>).

***Not found***. No agreed figure of 5 or fewer outages a month has been found.
