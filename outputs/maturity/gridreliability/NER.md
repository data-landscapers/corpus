# Niger

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in Niger reported [6.07 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2025. Earlier surveys [put the count at 23.15 in 2017](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). Since 2017 the count fell by 17.1 a month. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers. It says nothing of how long each outage lasted. It counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month. Niger is within ten a month and 1.1 above five. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 33 have fewer outages a month than Niger. [Doing Business 2020 reports a SAIFI of 288 interruptions a customer a year for Niger's distribution utility (data to May 2019), 24 a month, a SAIDI of 165 and a regulator monitoring reliability](<https://archive.doingbusiness.org/content/dam/doingBusiness/country/n/niger/NER.pdf>).
