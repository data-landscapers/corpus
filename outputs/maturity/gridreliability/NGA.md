# Nigeria

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in Nigeria reported [11.12 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2025. Earlier surveys [put the count at 38.47 in 2014](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). Since 2014 the count fell by 27.4 a month. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers; it says nothing of how long each outage lasted, and it counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; at more than ten, Nigeria is above all four, by 1.1. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 41 have fewer outages a month than Nigeria.

***Also on file***. [Center for Global Development and ONE Campaign survey of Nigerian tech firms: average tech firm reports 30+ outages a month (93 firms surveyed, 2019); one customer class, not the whole grid](<https://www.cgdev.org/article/new-survey-finds-frequent-power-outages-major-constraint-nigerian-tech-entrepreneurs>).

***Not found***. No figure of 10 or fewer outages a month has been found. No regulator's or utility's interruption figure for 2023 or later has been found.
