# Guinea

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [Electricité de Guinée (EDG-SA) reports a SAIFI of 23 interruptions for 2025 in its annual report, which is 1.92 a month](<https://edg.com.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-EDG-2025-Vf.pdf>); that is 1.92 outages a month. The World Bank's Enterprise Surveys [put the count at 4.37 in 2016 and 3.24 in 2025](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). An interruption frequency counts how many times in a year the average customer's supply was cut; divided by 12 it gives the outages in a month, the figure this study's lines are drawn on. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; Guinea is within two a month and 0.9 above one. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 14 have fewer outages a month than Guinea. [Electricité de Guinée (EDG-SA) reports a SAIFI of 39.8 interruptions for 2024 in its 2025 annual report, which is 3.32 a month](<https://edg.com.gn/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/RAPPORT-ANNUEL-EDG-2025-Vf.pdf>).
