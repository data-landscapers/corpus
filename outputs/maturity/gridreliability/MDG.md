# Madagascar

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** [Firms in Madagascar reported 6.59 power outages in a typical month in the World Bank's 2022 Enterprise Survey](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). It is older than 2023, so it sets no stage.

***Also on file***. [Rotating load-shedding on the Antananarivo Interconnected Network (RIA)](<https://newsmada.com/2026/08/29/tac-1-ambohimanambola-la-jirama-entreprit-des-travaux-durgence/>). Rotating load-shedding in Antananarivo from late August 2026 after the TAC 1 breaker failed. No outage count. [Toamasina grid load-shedding after Cyclone Gezani](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/08/14/the-missing-ingredient-how-power-is-fueling-madagascar-s-economic-future>). About 4 MW offline causing continuing load-shedding in Toamasina (August 2026). One exporter, Jacarandas, reports an average of five outages a day (one customer, not the grid). [Load-shedding on the Antananarivo Interconnected Network (RIA)](<https://www.jirama.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/FAQ-JIRAMA.pdf>). RIA short of 40 to 80 MW of generation (JIRAMA FAQ, published 2025-07-01). Load-shedding expected to ease with first results by end 2026.

***Not found***. No figure for 2023 or later has been found for how often the power goes off. The latest held is 6.59 outages a month, for 2022.
