# DR Congo

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in DR Congo reported [11.55 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2024. Earlier surveys [put the count at 12.23 in 2013](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). Since 2013 the count fell by 0.7 a month. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers; it says nothing of how long each outage lasted, and it counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; at more than ten, DR Congo is above all four, by 1.6. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 42 have fewer outages a month than DR Congo.
