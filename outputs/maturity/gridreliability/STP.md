# Sao Tome and Principe

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in Sao Tome and Principe reported [6.03 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2025. No earlier survey has been found, so no source found says whether outages are rising or falling. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers; it says nothing of how long each outage lasted, and it counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month; Sao Tome and Principe is within ten a month and 1.0 above five. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 31 have fewer outages a month than Sao Tome and Principe.
