# Zambia

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in Zambia reported [21.42 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2025. Earlier surveys [put the count at 4.62 in 2013 and 12.72 in 2019](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>). Since 2013 the count rose by 16.8 a month. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers. It says nothing of how long each outage lasted. It counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month. At more than ten, Zambia is above all four, by 11.4. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 44 have fewer outages a month than Zambia.

[Load-shedding schedules](<https://www.theglobalfund.org/media/w4gdfjob/publication_zambia-digital-health_casestudy_en.pdf>). 17-hour load-shedding schedules (hours, not outage count). [Loadshedding](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099012025023033676/txt/P505094-630a5ef9-d23a-4221-b7c8-397c94a90b4d.txt>). 20+ hours daily loadshedding during drought from January 2024 (hours, not outage count). [Load-shedding blackouts reported by mobile operators](<https://pulse.internetsociety.org/en/blog/2024/09/how-does-zambia-accelerate-its-internet-resilience/>). Up to 14 hours a day.

***Not found***. No figure of 10 or fewer outages a month has been found. No regulator's or utility's interruption figure for 2023 or later has been found.
