# Comoros

## Power outages a month

**Power outages in a month, with their year.** Firms in Comoros reported [38.84 outages in a typical month](<https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/data>) in the World Bank's Enterprise Survey for 2025. No earlier survey has been found, so no source found says whether outages are rising or falling. The survey asks firms how many power outages they had in a typical month and averages their answers. It says nothing of how long each outage lasted. It counts the outages firms report, not how many people the grid reaches. The study's lines are ten, five, two and one outage a month. At more than ten, Comoros is above all four, by 28.8. Of the 48 countries with a figure for 2023 or later, 47 have fewer outages a month than Comoros.

***Also on file***. [Power cuts recorded in 2024](<https://beninwebtv.com/comores-trois-centrales-solaires-de-20-mw-renforcent-un-reseau-dependant-du-diesel/>). About 3,000 power cuts recorded in 2024, a count with no frequency per customer.

***Not found***. No figure of 10 or fewer outages a month has been found. No regulator's or utility's interruption figure for 2023 or later has been found.
