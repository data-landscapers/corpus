# Comoros

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The PFSS-RRC says the Mayendeleyo transfer programme digitalises identification, registration and follow-up of beneficiary households](<https://pfss-comores.org/articles/le-gouvernement-comorien-%C3%A9largit-la-protection-sociale-%C3%A0-55-000-m%C3%A9nages-vuln%C3%A9rables>). [The World Bank mid-term aide-memoire says all technical operations of the PFSS-RRC cash transfer project, from targeting to payment statements, are already digitalised and its Management Information System (Beneficiary Management Platform, Complaints Platform, Internal Management Platform) is already available](<https://pfss-comores.org/ressources/download/8>). [The World Bank aide-memoire says extension and improvement of the project MIS, with the firm ASTRUM, is 75% done, with a payment module and a tamper-proof digital card still to be improved](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121724164029872/pdf/P179291-8124ca5b-87dd-4490-be73-04c5cb249a2c.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [The World Bank mid-term aide-memoire reports 41,703 beneficiaries receiving payments by mobile money, and says ARIEP beneficiaries hold accounts with Sanduk and are all paid by bank transfer](<https://pfss-comores.org/ressources/download/8>). [The World Bank aide-memoire says the project created a mobile-money account for every social cash transfer beneficiary household and reports 39,904 beneficiaries receiving payments by mobile money (41,203 including flood-affected households)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121724164029872/pdf/P179291-8124ca5b-87dd-4490-be73-04c5cb249a2c.pdf>).
