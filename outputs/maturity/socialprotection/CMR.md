# Cameroon

## Social protection

**System in service.** No source found says a social programme management information system is under way or in service. The nearest held fact: [by April 2025 ASNEIP was paying beneficiaries digitally](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Paid electronically.** [In 2025 a World Bank project paper said ASNEIP is scaling up its digital government-to-person payment system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>). [By April 2025, 9,337 beneficiaries had received digital payments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 ASNEIP's digital payment system was being scaled up](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099082625105536477/pdf/BOSIB-c1db46ff-563d-4cc3-b382-1eb9e5eb6876.pdf>).
