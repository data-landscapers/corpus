# Cape Verde

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Decreto-Lei n.º 3/2025 requires offices outside the central service to register social pension applications within 24 hours](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>). [They register them in the 'aplicativo informático' used to manage the pension](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>). [The decree republishes Lei 38/VIII/2013](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>). [Its article 47 enters approved pensioners in the database of the non-contributory pensions system, which the CNPS runs](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [under the 2025 decree approved pensioners are entered in the pensions database](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the database serves the non-contributory pensions system](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>).

***Also on file***. [Cadastro Social Único (CSU)](<https://www.governo.cv/governo-lanca-mecanismo-de-gestao-das-informacoes-e-reclamacoes-do-cadastro-social-unico/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically.
