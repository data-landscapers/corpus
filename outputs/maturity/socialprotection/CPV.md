# Cape Verde

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Decreto-Lei n.º 3/2025 (republishing Lei 38/VIII/2013, art. 41) requires offices outside the central service to register social pension applications in the 'aplicativo informático' used to manage the pension within 24 hours, and art. 47 enters approved pensioners in the database of the non-contributory pensions system, run by the CNPS](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/DownloadAct?id=73659>).
