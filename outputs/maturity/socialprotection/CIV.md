# Cote d'Ivoire

## Social protection

**System in service.** The World Bank's May 2025 report on the safety-net project [counts beneficiaries from the programme's information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099061325143037563/pdf/P175594-249b8381-1fd4-41f5-b036-e784a28bc057.pdf>). Until November 2024 its household counts came from the cash-transfer programme's own database. Since then they come from the single social registry's system. The project pays its transfers by mobile money.

**Paid electronically.** [In July 2024 an ENSEA study said the productive social safety nets project delivered 94% of its digital payments on time](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732473/plus-de-400-000-menages-beneficient-de-transferts-monetaires-et-de-mesures-daccompagnement-de-letat-de-2017-a-2024-selon-une-etude-de-lensea>). [It called this the first digital payment system of its kind for a national programme in West Africa](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732473/plus-de-400-000-menages-beneficient-de-transferts-monetaires-et-de-mesures-daccompagnement-de-letat-de-2017-a-2024-selon-une-etude-de-lensea>). [Mobile financial services cut transaction fees](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732473/plus-de-400-000-menages-beneficient-de-transferts-monetaires-et-de-mesures-daccompagnement-de-letat-de-2017-a-2024-selon-une-etude-de-lensea>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest fact: [in July 2024 the productive social safety nets project paid by mobile financial services](<https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732473/plus-de-400-000-menages-beneficient-de-transferts-monetaires-et-de-mesures-daccompagnement-de-letat-de-2017-a-2024-selon-une-etude-de-lensea>).
