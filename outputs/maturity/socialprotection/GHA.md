# Ghana

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The terms of reference list LEAP, Labour Intensive Public Works, Productive Inclusion, the Ghana School Feeding Programme and the Capitation Grant among the ministry's existing Management Information Systems, currently integrated peer-to-peer](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>). [The Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection issued terms of reference for a software firm to develop the Ghana Integrated Social Protection Information System (GISPIS), a unified platform integrating its social protection systems with beneficiary tracking and case management](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>).

**Paid electronically.** [The LEAP programme history says LEAP moved to electronic payments in Q3 2013 through AYA Technologies, MTN Mobile Money and GhIPSS, with biometric e-zwich payments rolled out nationwide in 2015 (2013, before the three-year window)](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>).
