# Ghana

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The terms of reference list LEAP, Labour Intensive Public Works, Productive Inclusion, the Ghana School Feeding Programme and the Capitation Grant among the ministry's existing Management Information Systems](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>). These are currently integrated peer-to-peer. [The Ministry of Gender, Children and Social Protection issued terms of reference for a software firm to develop the Ghana Integrated Social Protection Information System (GISPIS)](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>). It is to be a unified platform integrating the ministry's social protection systems, with beneficiary tracking and case management.

**Paid electronically.** [In the third quarter of 2013 LEAP moved to electronic payments through AYA Technologies, MTN Mobile Money and GhIPSS](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>). [In 2015 biometric e-zwich payments were rolled out nationwide](<http://leap.mogcsp.gov.gh/background.php>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest fact: [in August 2026 the ministry's programme systems, among them LEAP and school feeding, were linked only peer-to-peer](<https://www.mogcsp.gov.gh/?mdocs-file=8618>).
