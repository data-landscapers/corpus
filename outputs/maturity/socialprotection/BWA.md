# Botswana

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The IMF says Botswana has no single fully operational electronic registry of beneficiaries or joint information management system across its 30 programmes, so targeting and selection frequently require extensive paperwork](<https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2024/287/article-A002-en.xml>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined. This is a first pass, which read at most twenty stories a country; the nightly deep dive reads further and may move the stage either way.
