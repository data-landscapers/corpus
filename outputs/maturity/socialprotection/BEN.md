# Benin

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In 2025 Sambieni, Gnammou and Bourgeois wrote that Benin's ARCH health insurance (AM-ARCH) uses digital solutions to manage memberships and benefits](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>). [Its beneficiaries hold an insurance card](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>). [The authors say effectiveness depends on digital infrastructure, especially in rural areas](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [in 2025 AM-ARCH managed its benefits with digital solutions](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [those digital solutions serve the ARCH health insurance](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>).
