# Benin

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Sambieni, Gnammou and Bourgeois state that the ARCH health insurance (AM-ARCH) in Benin uses digital solutions dedicated to managing memberships and benefits, with beneficiaries holding an insurance card, though effectiveness depends on digital infrastructure, especially in rural areas](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>). It is the only fact held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://doi.org/10.3917/afco1.279.0039>).
