# Uganda

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The MGLSD feasibility report says numerous Ugandan social protection programmes operate their own management information systems, including those for the Senior Citizens Grant, the Youth Livelihood Programme and SAGE](<https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Feasibility-Report-for-Ugandas-Social-Registry.pdf>). [Development Pathways says Uganda's Ministry of Gender, Labour and Social Development commissioned the digital National Single Registry on 4 February 2021, with WFP supporting its implementation and maintenance since 2020 (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.developmentpathways.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Ugandas-National-Single-Registry_v3-compressed.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [The MGLSD feasibility report says the MISs of various programmes are linked to the National Single Registry, which provides an integrated beneficiary management platform](<https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Feasibility-Report-for-Ugandas-Social-Registry.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** [The MGLSD feasibility report says the MISs of various programmes are linked to the National Single Registry, which provides an integrated beneficiary management platform](<https://socialprotection.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Feasibility-Report-for-Ugandas-Social-Registry.pdf>). [Development Pathways says the National Single Registry consolidates information on beneficiaries across multiple social protection schemes and integrates the WFP Karamoja Social Registry (2021, before the three-year window)](<https://www.developmentpathways.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Ugandas-National-Single-Registry_v3-compressed.pdf>).
