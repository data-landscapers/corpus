# South Sudan

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The World Bank says Shabaka Meisha (SNSOP) beneficiaries were biometrically registered and enrolled into the Management Information Systems before two transfer rounds reached 87,867 households in 15 counties](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/09/18/cash-transfers-are-helping-women-create-businesses-and-regain-hope-in-south-sudan>). [The ministry says Shabaka Meisha cash transfers began on 17 December 2024 after beneficiaries were biometrically registered into the Government-led Management Information System](<https://mafs.gov.ss/cash-transfer/>). [The SSSAMS site says its enrolment and attendance monitoring, holding details of every student, is the basis on which the GESS programme pays cash grants to girls attending school and capitation grants to schools](<https://www.sssams.org/ams/index.html>).
