# Chad

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The World Bank's September 2025 infographic lists a 'nouveau système de gestion des beneficiaires intégrant le MGP' (new beneficiary management system incorporating the complaints mechanism) among Chad's adaptive social protection system strengthening under the PFSAP 2025-2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>).
