[It is part of strengthening Chad's adaptive social protection system under the PFSAP 2025-2030](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099735205212519675/pdf/IDU-f63a01b3-734b-45cc-bdf8-323adb04c51d.pdf>).
