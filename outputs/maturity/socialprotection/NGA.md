# Nigeria

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The World Bank report says shock-responsive cash transfer households from the Social Registers are enrolled into the payment system, with about 5.9 million households having received transfers by July 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt>). [NSIPA lists an integrated and fully digitalized management information system under the government's restructuring of NASSP-SU](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).

**Paid electronically.** [The Imo State governor welcomed the transition from physical cash disbursement to direct bank account payments under the federal HoPE-CT programme, which the minister said pays verified beneficiaries from the National Social Register linked to NIN](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-disburses-n6-6-billion-to-over-278000-imo-beneficiaries-under-empowerment-programme/>). [NSIPA lists digital payments via bank accounts and wallets under the restructuring of NASSP-SU](<https://nsipa.gov.ng/programmes/nassco.html>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-disburses-n6-6-billion-to-over-278000-imo-beneficiaries-under-empowerment-programme/>).
