# Sao Tome and Principe

## Social protection

**System in service.** [openIMIS says the government adopted Ke-Non, built on CORE-MIS and openIMIS, to manage the national social protection system](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>). [Ke-Non also manages the conditional cash transfer programme and is housed at the Institute of Innovation and Knowledge](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>). [A September 2025 World Bank project paper says the government now owns and operates Ké-Non directly, for the first time](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>). [The financing continues investment in it for the Vulnerable Family Program cash transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>).

**Paid electronically.** [The same paper says the financing continues an electronic cash-transfer payment system for beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>). [A partnership with a financial institution built that system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099091725161547918/txt/BOSIB-92986af7-9307-44ac-b3b9-5f75cb4949c8.txt>). [openIMIS says Ke-Non's payment module works with Banco Internacional de Sao Tome e Principe for the conditional cash transfer programme](<https://openimis.org/blog/em-powered-openimis-island-nation-sao-tome-and-principe-effectively-managing-its-social>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes.
