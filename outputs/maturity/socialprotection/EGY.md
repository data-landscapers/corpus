# Egypt

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Egypt's unified government services card system is being rolled out in phases, with phase one in Port Said, phase two in Ismailia and Luxor and phase three planned for the remaining governorates, as reported to the Prime Minister](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5672837.aspx>). [The World Bank says it continues to support Egypt's Takaful and Karama programme by building the management information system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). A later answer places the country above that step.

**Paid electronically.** [World Bank's Stephane Guimbert says Takaful and Karama pays through digital Meeza cards, which cut leakages and enhanced transparency](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx>). [The World Bank says 100 percent of Takaful and Karama beneficiaries are paid through electronic means, via Meeza debit cards, as of December 2023](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx>).
