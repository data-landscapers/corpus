# Egypt

## Social protection

**System in service.** In November 2026 the Ministry of Social Solidarity said [Takaful and Karama runs on an integrated software system](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). The system checks applicants' social and economic data. In 2024 the World Bank said it was [helping build the programme's management information system](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>). A [unified government services card](<https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5672837.aspx>) was being rolled out by governorate in June 2026.

**Paid electronically.** In December 2023 the World Bank said [all Takaful and Karama beneficiaries were paid electronically](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>), on Meeza debit cards. In June 2026 the ministry said [4.7 million families drew their payments](<https://www.businessmen-eg.com/en/5359/social-solidarity-launches-june-takaful-and-karama-disbursement>) at bank ATMs, Nasser Social Bank, Fawry outlets and on Meeza cards. Since May 2026 [pensions can also go monthly to mobile wallets](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8292004>). A social unit links each pension to the wallet on the system.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 Takaful and Karama paid through digital Meeza cards](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx>).
