# Egypt

## Social protection

**System in service.** In November 2026 the Ministry of Social Solidarity said [Takaful and Karama runs on an integrated software system](<https://almessa.gomhuriaonline.com/764443/>). It checks applicants' social and economic data. In July 2026 the ministry said it had [fully redeveloped the system](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8317494>). Field researchers now record data on tablets instead of paper forms. [In 2024 applicants were already recorded on tablets or computers](<https://newdemo.elwatannews.com/news/details/7600755>). Their data went to a central database for eligibility checks. In August 2026 the World Bank said [over 94 percent of the ministry's offices were automated](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099083126121521430/pdf/P179665-709615b1-e962-4bdc-a989-4f143ccfe4be.pdf>). In May 2026 the minister said [the programme's database was linked to more than 20 state bodies](<https://newdemo.elwatannews.com/news/details/8287324>). It reaches about 4.7 million families.

**Paid electronically.** In December 2023 the World Bank said [all Takaful and Karama beneficiaries were paid electronically](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs>), on Meeza debit cards. In June 2026 the ministry said [4.7 million families drew their payments](<https://www.businessmen-eg.com/en/5359/social-solidarity-launches-june-takaful-and-karama-disbursement>) at bank ATMs, Nasser Social Bank, Fawry outlets and on Meeza cards. Since May 2026 [pensions can also go monthly to mobile wallets](<https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8292004>). A social unit links each pension to the wallet on the system.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest fact: [in June 2025 Takaful and Karama paid through digital Meeza cards](<https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx>).
