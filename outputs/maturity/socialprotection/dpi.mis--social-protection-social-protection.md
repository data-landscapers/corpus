# Social protection: social protection

*As at 2026-09-30.*

## The norm

The African Union's Protocol on Social Protection asks for an integrated social protection information system across schemes, with disaggregated data published. This study asks the four questions above; it does not measure a country against the norm.

## The ladder

| 2 Under way | Yes to 1 |

## Countries

### Kenya: 5 Leading

One information system served several social programmes in 2025.

- The document says KSEIP 2 strengthens cash transfer delivery through the CCTP-MIS, and that the CCTP comprises the Older Persons, Orphans and Vulnerable Children, and Severe Disabilities cash transfers ([source, 2025-08](https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf)).

### Burundi: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2026; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- The World Bank states that investments in CORE-MIS and digital payments enable delivery of Merankabandi assistance nationwide ([source, 2026-05-01](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises)).

### Congo: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2025; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- The World Bank implementation status report records 91% of transfers under the Social Protection and Youth Productive Inclusion Project delivered through digital payments, target 80% marked achieved ([source, 2025-11-30](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122225174011681/pdf/P174178-d3f57ca8-c3c8-481f-b065-df5bd1e79798.pdf)).

### Cote d'Ivoire: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2024; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- An ENSEA study presented in July 2024 says the productive social safety nets project delivered 94% of its digital payments on time, calling it the first digital payment system of its kind for a national programme in West Africa, with mobile financial services cutting transaction fees ([source, 2024-07](https://news.abidjan.net/articles/732473/plus-de-400-000-menages-beneficient-de-transferts-monetaires-et-de-mesures-daccompagnement-de-letat-de-2017-a-2024-selon-une-etude-de-lensea)).

### Egypt: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2025; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- World Bank's Stephane Guimbert says Takaful and Karama pays through digital Meeza cards, which cut leakages and enhanced transparency ([source, 2025-06-01](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/547276.aspx)).
- The World Bank says 100 percent of Takaful and Karama beneficiaries are paid through electronic means, via Meeza debit cards, as of December 2023 ([source, 2023-12](https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2024/05/28/promoting-inclusive-human-capital-development-and-building-resilience-in-egypt-through-cash-transfer-programs)).

### Ethiopia: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2025; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- The World Bank restructuring paper says 252 woredas use electronic payments and bank accounts are opened for 1.3 million beneficiary households ([source, 2025-10](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf)).

### Malawi: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2025; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- NLGFC confirmed that 95,520 SCTP and 195,520 CSPWP beneficiaries were enrolled on e-payments in 10 districts in 2024/25 and received payments in e-wallets through payment service providers including TNM, Airtel, FDH, Standard Bank, Kaku Pay and NBS Bank ([source, 2025-03](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt)).
- The World Bank's Malawi Economic Monitor says about 440,000 households across 10 districts are paid SCTP benefits or CS-EPWP wages electronically under the SSRLP ([source, 2024-06](https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf)).

### Mozambique: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2025; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- The World Bank says INAS contracts payment service providers (M-Pesa, Emola, Mkesh) centrally since 2024, about 136,000 digital accounts were opened by May 2025, and Child Grant payments started in March 2025 ([source, 2025-05](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf)).

### Nigeria: 4 Operating

A social programme's system paid beneficiaries electronically in 2026; one system serving several programmes is not known.

- The Imo State governor welcomed the transition from physical cash disbursement to direct bank account payments under the federal HoPE-CT programme, which the minister said pays verified beneficiaries from the National Social Register linked to NIN ([source, 2026-06-04](https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-disburses-n6-6-billion-to-over-278000-imo-beneficiaries-under-empowerment-programme/)).
- The World Bank report says the National Social Safety Net Program-Scale Up payment mechanism is fully digital, with all cash transfers going directly to verified, biometrically backed beneficiary-owned accounts, 100 percent of households as of July 2025 ([source, 2025-07-04](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099090325094525591/txt/P176935-a76b292b-0876-428f-bd15-82f5f0c9fba7.txt)).

### South Sudan: 3 Established

A social programme was run on an information system in 2025; electronic payment from it is not known.

- The World Bank says Shabaka Meisha (SNSOP) beneficiaries were biometrically registered and enrolled into the Management Information Systems before two transfer rounds reached 87,867 households in 15 counties ([source, 2025-09-18](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/09/18/cash-transfers-are-helping-women-create-businesses-and-regain-hope-in-south-sudan)).
- The ministry says Shabaka Meisha cash transfers began on 17 December 2024 after beneficiaries were biometrically registered into the Government-led Management Information System ([source, 2024-12-17](https://mafs.gov.ss/cash-transfer/)).
