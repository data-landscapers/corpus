# Sierra Leone

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Sierraloaded reports that in 2024 the labour ministry and NaCSA completed a digitized registration, verification and enrolment of 13,091 aged beneficiaries](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>). [The enrolment was finished before the cash transfer it served was paid](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>). [In June 2023 the World Bank recorded that NaCSA had set up the SP Registry for Integrated National Targeting, and that 100,695 households received regular transfers](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062023164040814/pdf/P1435880bb78fa01f091990677538ffcfd2.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [Orange Mobile Money then paid each of them a one-time transfer](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>). [It acted as payment service provider across 10 districts](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>). [In June 2023 the World Bank said the Safety Nets Project had built an electronic payments system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062023164040814/pdf/P1435880bb78fa01f091990677538ffcfd2.pdf>). [A provider delivered its transfers to beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062023164040814/pdf/P1435880bb78fa01f091990677538ffcfd2.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. [The 2024 digitized enrolment served a single cash transfer to aged beneficiaries](<https://sierraloaded.sl/news/ministry-labour-cash-transfer-vulnerable-aged/>). [The registry the World Bank described in 2023 served the Safety Nets Project](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062023164040814/pdf/P1435880bb78fa01f091990677538ffcfd2.pdf>).
