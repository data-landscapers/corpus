# Lesotho

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In June 2024 the EU said Lesotho's Ministry of Gender, Youth and Social Development had begun to upgrade NISSA and MISSA](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>). [These are the National Information System for Social Assistance and the Management Information System for Social Assistance](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>). [The upgrade comes as the ministry moves to digital payments](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>). [Tablets and laptops will support the existing NISSA and MISSA systems, used for the Child Grants Programme](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [in June 2024 the ministry was transitioning to digital payments](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [NISSA and MISSA are used for the Child Grants Programme](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>).

***Also on file***. [NISSA](<https://www.eeas.europa.eu/delegations/lesotho/eu-and-unicef-provide-new-resources-government-enhance-child-well-being-and-social-services-lesotho_en>); [M-Pesa and EcoCash social grant payments](<https://www.gov.ls/uncategorized/mgysacs-on-mobile-payment/>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically.
