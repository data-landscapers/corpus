# Somalia

## Social protection

**System in service.** No source since October 2023 names the system that enrols and manages the programme's beneficiaries. [Baxnaano's page says the Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs runs the unconditional cash transfer as Baxnaano's regular window](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [Each household is paid on the mobile number it registered at enrolment](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>).

**Paid electronically.** [Baxnaano's 2025 page says the transfer reaches 50,000 of the poorest households in 21 districts](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [Each household receives US$20 a month, disbursed quarterly](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). [All payments go by mobile money to the household's registered phone number](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>).

**Several programmes.** No source since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. [The mobile money payments the page describes are those of the unconditional cash transfer alone](<https://baxnaano.so/unconditional-cash-transfer/>). It does not say whether other programmes are paid the same way.
