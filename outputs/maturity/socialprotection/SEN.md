# Senegal

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In November 2023 DGPSN said the RNU MIS and the PNBSF information system are in operation](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>). [The two systems are interconnected and hosted by SENUM-SA](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [in 2023 the PNBSF information system was in operation](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [in 2023 the RNU and PNBSF systems were interconnected](<https://spdci.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Talking-Interoperability-13-Senegal-Eng-revised.pdf>).
