# Malawi

## Social protection

**System in service.** [World Bank aide memoire says Mbwezera MIS is being configured to serve the public works programme, with modules under development for project creation, attendance tracking and wage calculation](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [The World Bank's Malawi Economic Monitor says the Unified Beneficiary Registry is partially integrated with the management information systems of the Social Cash Transfer Program (SCTP) and the CS-EPWP public works programme, with data shared through a one-way API](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf>). [IDinsight reports that SCTP beneficiaries are registered with the Transfer Agency, which sets up individual e-wallets and records the details in the Management Information System](<https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [NLGFC confirmed that 95,520 SCTP and 195,520 CSPWP beneficiaries were enrolled on e-payments in 10 districts in 2024/25 and received payments in e-wallets through payment service providers including TNM, Airtel, FDH, Standard Bank, Kaku Pay and NBS Bank](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).
