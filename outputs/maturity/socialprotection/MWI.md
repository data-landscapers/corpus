# Malawi

## Social protection

**System in service.** [IDinsight reported in 2024 that the Transfer Agency registers Social Cash Transfer Programme beneficiaries](<https://www.idinsight.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Report-Malawi-SCTP-E-Payment-Study_26072024-1.pdf>). It sets up an e-wallet for each and records the details in the programme's management information system. [In March 2025 a World Bank mission said the Mbwezera system was being configured for the public works programme](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). [In June 2026 the Ministry of Gender, Children, Disability and Social Welfare launched an upgraded child protection system on DHIS2](<https://www.projecthope.org/news-stories/press-release/malawi-launches-upgraded-national-child-protection-system/>). Project HOPE said it was active in 16 of Malawi's 28 districts.

**Paid electronically.** [In March 2025 the National Local Government Finance Committee confirmed 95,520 cash transfer and 195,520 public works beneficiaries on e-payments in 10 districts](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>). They were paid into e-wallets through providers including TNM, Airtel, FDH, Standard Bank and NBS Bank. [In 2024 the World Bank put the number paid electronically at about 440,000 households](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** [The World Bank's Malawi Economic Monitor of 2024 said the Unified Beneficiary Registry is partly linked to the cash transfer and public works systems](<https://reliefweb.int/attachments/e9804959-688c-45c7-aadf-0e4bf2f49aae/P17952911466830541bbed1eb3d3ade0049.pdf>). Data passes one way, through an interface. No single system serving several programmes has been found.

***Also on file***. [Unified Beneficiary Registry (UBR)](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099062325114042048/txt/P169198-441cfc05-221d-412e-9c5e-a5b191e1d0bb.txt>).

***Not found***. No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes.
