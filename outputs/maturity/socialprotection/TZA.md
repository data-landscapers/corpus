# Tanzania

## Social protection

**System in service.** [TASAF's PSSN III Payment Handbook says payment lists, approvals and receipts are generated and processed in the TASAF Management Information System from households enrolled in the TASAF Registry](<https://www.tasaf.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1776692069-PAYMENT%20PROCESS%20HANDBOOK.pdf>). 2 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Paid electronically.** [TASAF's handbook says PSSN III benefits are paid bimonthly through banks and mobile money operators, with payment instructions from the TASAF MIS routed via the Government electronic Payment Gateway (GePG)](<https://www.tasaf.go.tz/uploads/documents/sw-1776692069-PAYMENT%20PROCESS%20HANDBOOK.pdf>).
