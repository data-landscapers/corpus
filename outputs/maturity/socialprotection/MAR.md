# Morocco

## Social protection

**System in service.** [At the end of December 2025 the Direct Social Aid programme supported 3.9 million households, Le Brief reports from the ANSS annual report](<https://www.lebrief.ma/soutien-social-un-an-apres-impact-de-lanss-100155241/>). Its management information systems were run for the interim by the CNSS and the CNRA. They are being moved as they stand to the ANSS, the national social support agency. Full switchover is aimed for 2026.
