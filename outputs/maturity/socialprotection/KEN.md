# Kenya

## Social protection

**System in service.** [All National Safety Net Programme cash transfers use the Enhanced Single Registry for, among other things, secure management information systems, and that delivery systems run through the Consolidated Cash Transfer Programme MIS (CCTP-MIS)](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). 3 facts are held on the subject: no news story or official notice read for this study says more about it, the documents the earlier cell cited were not read, and the systems themselves were not examined.

**Paid electronically.** [All National Safety Net Programme programmes use the Enhanced Single Registry for electronic transfers to bank accounts and mobile money](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**Several programmes.** [KSEIP 2 strengthens cash transfer delivery through the CCTP-MIS, and that the CCTP comprises the Older Persons, Orphans and Vulnerable Children, and Severe Disabilities cash transfers](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).
