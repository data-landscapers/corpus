# Kenya

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In August 2025 a social protection ministry document said all National Safety Net Programme cash transfers use the Enhanced Single Registry](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [The registry serves, among other things, secure management information systems](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [Delivery systems run through the Consolidated Cash Transfer Programme MIS (CCTP-MIS)](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [The same document says all National Safety Net Programme programmes use the Enhanced Single Registry for electronic transfers](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [Those transfers go to bank accounts and mobile money](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [From the January 2025 disbursement the State Department pays all Inua Jamii beneficiaries through the eCitizen-M-Pesa platform, with Safaricom](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/press%20release/Press%20release%20for%20Inua%20Jamii%20rollout%20-%20JAN.%202025.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** [The August 2025 document says KSEIP 2 strengthens cash transfer delivery through the CCTP-MIS](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>). [The CCTP comprises the Older Persons, Orphans and Vulnerable Children, and Severe Disabilities cash transfers](<https://www.socialprotection.go.ke/sites/default/files/Downloads/ESMP%20Template-August%202025%20(3).pdf>).
