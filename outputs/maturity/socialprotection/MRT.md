# Mauritania

## Social protection

**System in service.** [A World Bank SASPP policy note says Mauritania's Tekavoul national safety-net programme runs on its own information system (the Tekavoul information system), whose limited interoperability with the Social Registry delayed the beneficiary list renewal, while the programme made quarterly cash transfers to nearly 138,000 households in June 2026](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099229507202629093/pdf/IDU-435478dd-a19c-4ed7-ba75-1aa272bee8b2.pdf>). [Taazour's delegate general says it has a digital database of over 1.5 million citizens and an advanced digital platform through which it provides social assistance to beneficiaries](<https://ami.mr/fr/archives/253780>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099229507202629093/pdf/IDU-435478dd-a19c-4ed7-ba75-1aa272bee8b2.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099229507202629093/pdf/IDU-435478dd-a19c-4ed7-ba75-1aa272bee8b2.pdf>).
