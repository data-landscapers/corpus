# Mozambique

## Social protection

**System in service.** The National Social Action Institute (INAS) runs its beneficiaries on e-INAS. [The state IT centre CEDSIF lists e-INAS among the systems it runs](<https://www.cedsif.gov.mz/cedsifportal/?page_id=81>), as the beneficiary management system for basic social assistance. [In May 2026 the World Bank said INAS has an information system and a registry of over 1.2 million households](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). It added that the system needs modernising. [A World Bank financing of US$154.4 million will pay for that work](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). [The Child Grant's target counts beneficiaries fully registered on e-INAS by January 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>). [In April 2025 O Económico reported an IGM evaluation](<https://www.oeconomico.com/avaliacao-expoe-fragilidades-no-subsidio-social-basico-e-recomenda-reforma-urgente-para-garantir-dignidade-dos-idosos-em-mocambique/>): e-INAS is in use for the Programa de Subsídio Social Básico, about 450,000 beneficiaries, but does not yet cover every province well.

**Paid electronically.** [INAS has contracted payment providers centrally since 2024: M-Pesa, Emola and Mkesh](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>). By May 2025 about 136,000 digital accounts had been opened, the World Bank says. Child Grant payments through them started in March 2025.

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The sources name e-INAS for the [Child Grant](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>) and the [Programa de Subsídio Social Básico](<https://www.oeconomico.com/avaliacao-expoe-fragilidades-no-subsidio-social-basico-e-recomenda-reforma-urgente-para-garantir-dignidade-dos-idosos-em-mocambique/>) it belongs to, and no other programme.
