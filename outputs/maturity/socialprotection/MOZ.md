# Mozambique

## Social protection

**System in service.** [The World Bank Additional Financing of US$154.4 million will finance modernization of the core INAS Management Information System for performance, interoperability, data quality and analytics](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). [The World Bank project document says a management information system and a beneficiary registry of over 1.2 million households are in place at INAS, though the MIS needs modernization](<https://www.gtai.de/resource/blob/1999512/d5f145dad744ac85e8c8fd6d42f16a49/PRO202605201999506.pdf>). [The World Bank says the PSSB Child Grant target is set on beneficiaries fully registered on eINAS by January 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>). [The World Bank says the project contributes to a government milestone of full operation of e-INAS, the National Social Protection Institute's system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [The World Bank says INAS contracts payment service providers (M-Pesa, Emola, Mkesh) centrally since 2024, about 136,000 digital accounts were opened by May 2025, and Child Grant payments started in March 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060625133026871/pdf/P173640-21420c54-0ced-4347-8192-6604ebd266d9.pdf>).
