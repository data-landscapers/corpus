# Zimbabwe

## Social protection

**System in service.** The World Bank's February 2026 document says [the social protection information system, now called ZISO, is still being made operational](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099030926024025866/txt/P514401-e5a07636-f85a-4936-8383-b57dbd46cf7c.txt>). It needs household enumeration and hosting first. No source names the system that runs the cash transfer. [In August 2023 the social welfare ministry, with the World Bank and UNICEF, launched the Integrated Social Protection Management Information System, piloted in Cyclone Idai districts](<https://healthtimes.co.zw/2023/08/18/zimbabwe-launches-social-protection-management-system/>). [In 2024 Gemnation reported that the cash transfer pays by EcoCash](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>).

**Paid electronically.** [Gemnation reported in July 2024 that the Harmonised Social Cash Transfer pays beneficiaries by EcoCash rather than in cash](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>). [It cites the Auditor-General's note that the payment method has moved from cash to electronic transfer](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>).

**Several programmes.** No source since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. [The 2023 launch piloted the new system with one programme, the Food Deficit Mitigation Strategy](<https://healthtimes.co.zw/2023/08/18/zimbabwe-launches-social-protection-management-system/>). [Gemnation's 2024 report concerns the Harmonised Social Cash Transfer alone](<https://gemnation.co.zw/cash-transfer-scheme-lacks-transparency/>).

***Also on file***. [Zimbabwe Social Registry (ZISO)](<https://www.zbcnews.co.zw/government-develops-social-registry-to-strengthen-safety-nets/>).

***Not found***. No source since October 2023 shows a programme's beneficiaries managed in a working system.
