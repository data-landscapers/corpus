# Tunisia

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In April 2024 the World Bank said the AMEN programme's management information system has been strengthened](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [This sped up beneficiary applications and eligibility verification](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [The system is being enhanced further, including interoperability with other agencies' databases](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [A case management system is being set up with UNDP under the SPLA project](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>).

**Paid electronically.** [In April 2024 the World Bank said 44.4% of AMEN cash transfers are digitalised](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>). [The Ministry of Social Affairs and the Tunisian Post have a convention for digital payment cards tied to an e-wallet](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/teri/brief/enhancing-the-social-protection-system-to-better-serve-vulnerable-populations>).
