# Burundi

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Merankabandi II coordinator Michel Nyabenda said a modernised management information system (MIS) has been deployed in the Burundian government's data centre, while enrolment of 249,671 households in the Registre social unique continues](<https://lerenouveau.bi/projet-merankabandi-ii-bilan-a-mis-parcours/>). [The World Bank states that investments in an open-source management information system (CORE-MIS) have established the foundations of Burundi's national social protection system, supporting the Merankabandi programme nationwide](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>). [OpenIMIS states that since May 2025 the CORE-MIS solution powered by openIMIS has managed cash transfers and income-generating-activity financing for 1.25 million Merankabandi beneficiaries across five provinces, after over 1 million records were migrated from the legacy system](<https://openimis.org/blog/burundis-growing-social-protection-system-now-runs-openimis>).

**Paid electronically.** [The World Bank states that investments in CORE-MIS and digital payments enable delivery of Merankabandi assistance nationwide](<https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2026/05/01/building-burundi-s-national-social-protection-systems-to-deliver-jobs-and-respond-to-crises>). [OpenIMIS states that Burundi's multiple payment providers are integrated with the Merankabandi system for automated payments and reconciliation](<https://openimis.org/blog/burundis-growing-social-protection-system-now-runs-openimis>).
