# South Africa

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In November 2025 SASSA told Parliament's social development committee that its Social Relief of Distress system handles about 17.8 million applications](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/42085/>). It approves about 8 million a month and checks them against Home Affairs, SARS and other records in real time. [In February 2025 the committee said a review rated the grant's application and payment system at medium threat level](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-committee-welcomes-findings-sassas-srd-grant-payment-system>). [In the Western Cape, a case management app for social workers went live in April 2024](<https://www.westerncape.gov.za/article/innovative-new-swims-app-game-changer-social-workers>). The provincial government said 1,054 practitioners were using it by July 2024.

**Paid electronically.** [SASSA told the committee in November 2025 that it runs banking checks to stop fraudulent grant payments](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/42085/>). It said only fully FICA-compliant bank accounts are now used to pay grants.

**Several programmes.** [In August 2024 the Department of Social Development signed agreements with other departments to build a National Integrated Social Protection Information System](<https://dsdnews.org/ijs-modernising-social-development-for-better-accountability/>). The system is meant to bring data from several sectors together. No source found says it is in service.
