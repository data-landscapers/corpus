# South Africa

## Social protection

**System in service.** [SASSA's acting Chief Information Officer told Parliament's social development committee that the Social Relief of Distress (SRD) system, established in 2020, processes about 17.8 million applications with an average of 8 million approvals a month, validated through real-time interfaces with Home Affairs, SARS, GEPF and DPSA](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/42085/>). [The Portfolio Committee on Social Development says SASSA runs the SRD grant application and payment system, which a review rated at medium threat level](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-committee-welcomes-findings-sassas-srd-grant-payment-system>).

**Paid electronically.** [SASSA told the committee it uses banking checks to prevent fraudulent grant payments and that only fully FICA-compliant bank accounts are now used for grant payments](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/42085/>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [the one given above](<https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/42085/>).
