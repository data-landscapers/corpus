# Rwanda

## Social protection

**System in service.** [Rwanda's 2024 social protection sector plan says the Monitoring and Evaluation Information System (MEIS) serves as a beneficiary registry](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>). [It gives real-time information on who receives what support](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>). [The plan cites it for VUP beneficiary counts for 2023-2024](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>). [A Disability Management Information System (DMIS) is in the final stages of completion](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>). [It will hold details of over 500,000 persons with disability to target the new Disability Grant within VUP](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** No source found since October 2023 says the programme's system pays beneficiaries electronically. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 MEIS recorded who receives what support](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>).

**Several programmes.** No source found since October 2023 says one information system serves several social programmes. The nearest held fact: [in 2024 the DMIS was being completed to target the Disability Grant within VUP](<https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/Sector_Strategic_Plans_Abridged_2024_2029/Social_Protection/Final_and_validated_SPSSP.pdf>).
