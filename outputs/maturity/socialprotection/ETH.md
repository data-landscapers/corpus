# Ethiopia

## Social protection

**System in service.** [In October 2025 a World Bank restructuring paper said 444 of Ethiopia's 492 woredas use the PSNP management information system for programme tracking](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>). [The programme has 7.9 million beneficiaries](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>). [Data on 5.1 million of them had been synched in the federal management information system](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>). [Full operationalisation of the PSNP management information system was under way, with completion expected by the end of December 2025](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>).

**Paid electronically.** [The same restructuring paper says 252 woredas use electronic payments](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>). [Bank accounts had been opened for 1.3 million beneficiary households](<https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099102025134040722/pdf/P172479-f2834346-c6c0-4568-a19e-46544cf006e6.pdf>).
