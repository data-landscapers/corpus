# Guinea

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Guinea's statistics law is [Law L/2014/019/AN of 8 July 2014 on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/Textes/loiStatistique2016.pdf>), which repealed the law of 1995. The statistics institute publishes it in a compendium with the texts that apply it.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules were made under the law in 2015 and its council was appointed. The compendium reproduces [the decree of 5 August 2015 on the attributions, organisation and operation of the national statistics council](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/Textes/loiStatistique2016.pdf>). It also carries [a 2015 decree on managing the financial resources of the national statistical programme, and ministerial orders on the specialised commissions and on the duty of administrations to send data to the statistical system](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/Textes/loiStatistique2016.pdf>). After the decree, [an order of the Prime Minister named the council's members, with the Prime Minister as its president](<https://www.stat-guinee.org/images/Documents/Publications/Textes/loiStatistique2016.pdf>). All of it dates from 2015.
