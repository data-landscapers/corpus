# Egypt

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Egypt's statistics law is [Law No. 35 of 1960 on statistics and censuses](<https://old.capmas.gov.eg/Pages/StaticPages.aspx?page_id=23>), issued at the Presidency on 9 February 1960 and amended by Law No. 28 of 1982. The statistics agency publishes it as the law that regulates statistical work.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the agency's own publication of the law. [The agency presents Law 35 of 1960, as amended in 1982, as the law regulating statistical work](<https://old.capmas.gov.eg/Pages/StaticPages.aspx?page_id=23>). The text is the one source found for Egypt. It gives the law's date of issue and its one amendment, twenty-two years later, and it shows the agency treating a law of 1960 as its governing text. The law is the oldest statistics law in force among the countries assessed. No decree, regulation or order made under it is named in what has been found, and no bill to replace it.

***Also on file***. [National strategy for developing Egypt's statistical system](<https://mped.gov.eg/singlenews?id=5528&lang=en>).

***Not found***. No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law.
