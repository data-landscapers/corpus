# Gabon

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Gabon's statistics law is law 15/2014 instituting and organising the national statistical system, amended by law 16/2022 of 6 September 2022. The state news agency reported in May 2026 that [it is the legislation in force](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-conseil-des-ministres-vers-la-revision-du-cadre-legal-du-systeme-statistique-national/>), and the economy ministry says [the Head of State promulgated it](<https://economie.gouv.ga/direction-generale-de-la-statistique/>). An amendment is in progress: on 22 May 2026 [the planning minister presented to the Council of Ministers a bill amending ten articles of the law](<https://agpgabon.ga/gabon-conseil-des-ministres-vers-la-revision-du-cadre-legal-du-systeme-statistique-national/>) to bring it into line with the Constitution of December 2024.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** A rule was made under the law in 2024. [Decree 0314/PR/MPP of 1 August 2024 approves the statutes of the National Institute of Statistics](<https://journal-officiel.ga/21242-0314-pr-mpp-/>) and [cites the statistics law, as amended in 2022, as the basis it is taken under](<https://journal-officiel.ga/21242-0314-pr-mpp-/>). It was published in the Journal Officiel of 15 August 2024.
