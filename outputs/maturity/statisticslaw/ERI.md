# Eritrea

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Eritrea has a draft statistics law and none in force on record. A review of the statistics office's cooperation with Statistics Norway, published on 8 January 2007, says [the Statistical Proclamation had been submitted to government and its formal issue was still pending](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>). The review describes the proclamation as the equivalent of a statistical act.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the same review. [The 2007 review records the proclamation as submitted and not issued](<https://www.norad.no/contentassets/7f7da719f25e49169fd844b5bb9b6f15/mid-term-review-of-cooperation-between-national-statistics-office-in-eritrea-and-statistics-norway.pdf>), and gives no date for the submission. It is the one source found for Eritrea on the subject and is nineteen years old. No other document held on the country's statistics mentions a statistics law, a proclamation or a draft. The draft therefore stands as last reported: with the government, awaiting issue.

***Also on file***. None found.

***Not found***. No source found says a statistics law is in force. No source found says the proclamation was issued, or withdrawn, after 2007.
