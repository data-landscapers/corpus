# Somalia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Somalia's statistics law is Law No. 24, the National Statistics Law of the Federal Republic of Somalia. The National Bureau of Statistics says [the law's enactment mandated the Bureau's establishment and repealed Statistical Law No. 35 of 1970](<https://nbs.gov.so/about-us/>). The Bureau's page gives no date for the law; the record held dates it 24 February 2020.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the Bureau's own account. [The Bureau presents Law No. 24 as the act that created it in place of the 1970 law](<https://nbs.gov.so/about-us/>). It is the one source found for Somalia that states a fact on the statistics law, and it comes from the body the law set up. No other document held on the country's statistics says anything of the law. No decree, regulation or order made under the law is named in what has been found, and the text of the law is not among the documents held.

***Also on file***. [National Statistical System](<https://nbs.gov.so/about-us/>); [Statistical Law No. 35 of 1970](<https://nbs.gov.so/about-us/>).

***Not found***. No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law. The law's own text is not held.
