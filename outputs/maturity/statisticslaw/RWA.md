# Rwanda

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Rwanda's statistics law is [Law N° 45/2013 of 16 June 2013 on the organisation of statistical activities in Rwanda](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-05/Official_Gazette_no_Special_of_16.06.2013%20%281%29.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the law's own text. [The text held is the law as made on 16 June 2013](<https://statistics.gov.rw/sites/default/files/documents/2025-05/Official_Gazette_no_Special_of_16.06.2013%20%281%29.pdf>), published by the national statistics institute. It is the one source found for Rwanda on the subject. The law is thirteen years old and governs how statistical activities are organised in the country. No other document held on the country's statistics states a fact on the statistics law. No decree, regulation or order made under the statistics law is named in what has been found, and no bill to amend or replace it. The law therefore stands as the institute publishes it.
