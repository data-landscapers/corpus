# Djibouti

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Djibouti's statistics law is Law no. 124 of 20 November 2011 on the organisation of statistical activity and of the national statistical system. It is held through the texts that cite it: [a decree of 21 September 2015](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2015-263-pr-mtra-portant-organisation-et-fonctionnement-de-lobservatoire-de-lemploi-et-des-qualifications/>), [a 2026 decree on the statistics institute's staff](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2026-075-pr-mefi-portant-creation-du-statut-particulier-des-cadres-de-linstad/>) and [the order approving the institute's 2026 budget](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>) each name it among their legal bases.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the 2026 decree. [The decree made in 2026 cites the law of 20 November 2011 among its legal bases](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2026-075-pr-mefi-portant-creation-du-statut-particulier-des-cadres-de-linstad/>), as does [the order approving the statistics institute's budget for 2026](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>). Both show the law standing fifteen years after it was made and used by the government as the ground for acts on the institute. Neither source says the decree or the order was made to apply the law.
