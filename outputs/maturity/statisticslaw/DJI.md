# Djibouti

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** [Loi n° 26/AN/18/8ème L of 27 February 2019 created Djibouti's national statistics institute](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n26-an-18-8eme-l-relatif-a-la-creation-de-linstitut-national-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-insd/>). [It is a public body that coordinates the national statistical system and produces official statistics](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n26-an-18-8eme-l-relatif-a-la-creation-de-linstitut-national-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-insd/>). [A law of 18 February 2021 renamed it INSTAD and attached it to the ministry in charge of statistics](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/loi-n108-an-20-8eme-l-portant-modification-de-la-loi-n26-an-18-8eme-l-relative-a-la-creation-de-linstitut-national-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-insd/>). The older law of 20 November 2011 on the organisation of statistical activity is still cited, in [a 2026 decree on the institute's staff](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2026-075-pr-mefi-portant-creation-du-statut-particulier-des-cadres-de-linstad/>) and [the order approving its 2026 budget](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [A decree of 20 September 2021 set INSTAD's statutes](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-185-pr-mefi-portant-nomination-des-membres-du-conseil-dadministration-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad/>). [Decree 2023-185 named the eleven members of the institute's board for three-year terms from 6 July 2023](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-185-pr-mefi-portant-nomination-des-membres-du-conseil-dadministration-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad/>). [They represent seven ministries, the central bank, the chamber of commerce, the ports authority and the institute](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/decret-n2023-185-pr-mefi-portant-nomination-des-membres-du-conseil-dadministration-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad/>).

***Also on file***. [Stratégie Nationale de Développement de la Statistique (adopted by Loi n°123 of 20 July 2011)](<https://www.journalofficiel.dj/texte-juridique/arrete-n2026-041-pr-mefi-portant-approbation-du-projet-de-budget-previsionnel-de-linstitut-de-la-statistique-de-djibouti-instad-pour-lexercice-2026/>).
