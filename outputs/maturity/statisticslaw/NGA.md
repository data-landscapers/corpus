# Nigeria

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Nigeria's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 2007 (Act No. 9), published in the Official Gazette of 11 June 2007](<https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/resource/NBS_ACT_2007.pdf>), which establishes the National Statistical System and the National Bureau of Statistics. [Section 27 of the Act sets data producers' obligation to release anonymised microdata](<https://microdata.nigerianstat.gov.ng/index.php/catalog/151>), according to the Bureau's microdata catalogue. A bill to repeal it is before parliament: [the House of Representatives passed the Statistics Bill, 2025 at third reading on 10 December 2025](<https://naltf.gov.ng/house-of-representatives-moves-to-modernize-nigerias-statistical-system-with-new-bill/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the hearing of June 2026. [A House committee held a public hearing on the Statistics Bill, 2025 on 8 June 2026](<https://guardian.ng/featured/reps-begin-review-of-statistics-act-link-reform-to-1tn-economy-drive/>), and the report [describes the 2007 Act as the current law, eighteen years old, in place until the bill repeals it](<https://guardian.ng/featured/reps-begin-review-of-statistics-act-link-reform-to-1tn-economy-drive/>). The legislative reform office likewise [refers to the existing Act of 2007 as the one the bill would repeal](<https://naltf.gov.ng/house-of-representatives-moves-to-modernize-nigerias-statistical-system-with-new-bill/>).
