# Tanzania

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Tanzania's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, Chapter 351, originally Act No. 9 of 2015, which commenced on 2 November 2015](<https://www.mof.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1676548401-THE%20STATISTICS%20ACT,%20CAP%20351%20R.E%202019.pdf>). It continues the National Bureau of Statistics and sets up its Governing Board. It is held in [the revised edition of 2023](<https://www.vkadvocates.co.tz/LIBRARY/Cap.%20351%20Statistics%20Act,%20R.E%202023.pdf>) as well as that of 2019. The Bureau's dissemination policy of September 2024 says [the Bureau operates as an autonomous public institution in accordance with the Act](<https://www.nbs.go.tz/uploads/documents/en-1741674437-NBS%20Dissemination%20Policy%202024%20.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is a 2025 survey. [The Bureau's catalogue page for the 2025 Integrated Labour Force Survey says survey information is collected under the Statistics Act](<https://microdata.nbs.go.tz/index.php/catalog/65>). A survey carried out is not counted as the law applied. The commencement notice of 2015 brought the Act into force and is not a rule under it.
