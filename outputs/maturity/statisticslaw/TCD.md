# Chad

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Chad's statistics law is [Loi N°026/PR/2019 on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities, dated June 2019](<https://www.inseed.td/index.php/systeme-statistique-national/textes-reglementaires>), which makes the statistics institute the central body of the national statistical system. The institute's data archive [requires users to comply with articles 4 to 10 of the law of 11 June 2019](<https://anad.inseed.td/index.php/politique-dacc>), and a government adviser [listed the law among reforms strengthening statistics at a regional forum in 2024](<https://www.uneca.org/eca-events/sites/default/files/resources/documents/TCND/ARFSD2024/Presentations/VNRVLR/donnees_pour_ameliorer_la_pertinence_et_l_exactitude_des_revues_nationales_volontaires_adoumtogue_rubain.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules were made under the law in 2019. The institute's page of regulatory texts [lists three decrees dated July 2019 on the attributions, organisation and operation of the National Statistics Council, of the institute and of the National Statistics Development Fund](<https://www.inseed.td/index.php/systeme-statistique-national/textes-reglementaires>). They followed the law by a month. Nothing made or done under the law in the last three years is held.
