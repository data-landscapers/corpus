# Central African Republic

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** The Central African Republic's statistics law is [Law No. 01.008 of 16 July 2001 regulating statistical activities](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2170>), in force from its promulgation. It creates the national statistical system, the national statistics council and the statistics institute. A replacement is being prepared: [a workshop in Bangui on 23 April 2026 validated a draft law on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/332-icasees-atelier-pour-la-validation-du-projet-de-loi-statistique-en-republique-centrafricaine>), with recommendations to be worked in before it goes to the bodies that adopt it.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact concerns the replacement and not rules under the law of 2001. The statistics institute reported that [stakeholders validated the draft law at the April 2026 workshop](<https://www.icasees.org/index.php/actualites/332-icasees-atelier-pour-la-validation-du-projet-de-loi-statistique-en-republique-centrafricaine>), which is to replace the 2001 text once adopted. Until then [the 2001 law](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2170>) is the one in force.
