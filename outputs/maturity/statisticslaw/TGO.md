# Togo

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Togo's statistics law is law no. 2011-014 of 3 June 2011 on the organisation of statistical activity. The statistics institute says [the law sets the principles and institutional frame of the national statistical system and was promulgated on 3 June 2011](<https://inseed.tg/a-propos-de-linseed/>); the statistics strategy says [it creates the National Statistics Council and the institute](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/22_Togo-SNDS-II_Adopte-CNS-le-27aout20_avant-propos-signe-MPDC.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules have been made under the law and its council meets. The institute says [the council was made operational by a decree of 7 November 2012 on its composition and functioning](<https://inseed.tg/conseil-national-de-la-statistique-13e-session-ordinaire-le-togo-adopte-des-orientations-strategiques/>), and the strategy cites [a decree of 24 February 2015 setting the institute's functions and organisation](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/22_Togo-SNDS-II_Adopte-CNS-le-27aout20_avant-propos-signe-MPDC.pdf>) as an implementing text. [The council held its 13th ordinary session on 30 June 2026 and adopted the national statistical activity reports for 2023 and 2024](<https://inseed.tg/conseil-national-de-la-statistique-13e-session-ordinaire-le-togo-adopte-des-orientations-strategiques/>).

***Also on file***. [INSEED](<https://inseed.tg/a-propos-de-linseed/>); [Conseil national de la statistique (CNS)](<https://inseed.tg/a-propos-de-linseed/>); [Stratégie Nationale de Développement de la Statistique (2009-2013)](<https://inseed.tg/a-propos-de-linseed/>).
