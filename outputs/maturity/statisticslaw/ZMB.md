# Zambia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Zambia's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 2018 (Act No. 13 of 2018), assented to on 23 December 2018](<https://www.parliament.gov.zm/sites/default/files/documents/acts/The%20Statistics%20Act%202018.pdf>). A ministerial foreword says [it repealed the Census and Statistics Act of 1955 and the Agricultural Statistics Act of 1964 and reconstituted the Central Statistics Office as the Zambia Statistics Agency](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The Act has been applied in the last three years. [The Agency issued the National Statistical Release Calendar for 2026 on 1 December 2025, which section 27(1) of the Act requires it to prepare](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-NSRC-1.12.2025.pdf>), and the calendar [names the Act as the law under which the Agency acts](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-NSRC-1.12.2025.pdf>). The statistics strategy published in July 2023 says [the Act's operation began with the appointment of the Board of Directors and the Statistician General and the setting up of a coordinating committee of fifteen ministries](<https://www.zamstats.gov.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/SECOND-NATIONAL-STRATEGY-FOR-THE-DEVELOPMENT-OF-STATISTICS-2023-2027.pdf>).
