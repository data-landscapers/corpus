# Cape Verde

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Cabo Verde's statistics law is [Lei n.º 48/IX/2019, published in the Boletim Oficial of 19 February 2019](<https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View/40755>), which sets the principles, norms and structure of the National Statistical System. In June 2026 [the board of the statistics institute said it asserts its technical independence under the law](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/06/01/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-reafirma-independencia-tecnica/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is that statement. [The institute's board, reported on 1 June 2026, invoked the framework law of the National Statistical System as the ground of its technical independence](<https://expressodasilhas.cv/pais/2026/06/01/ine-rejeita-acusacoes-de-manipulacao-de-dados-e-reafirma-independencia-tecnica/>). The statement shows the law relied on by the body it governs seven years after it was published. It is a position taken by the board in public and is not a decree or regulation. The gazette text and that report are the two sources found for Cabo Verde.
