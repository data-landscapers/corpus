# Ghana

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Ghana's statistics law is the [Statistical Service Act, 2019 (Act 1003)](<https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2019/1003>). It was assented to on 24 September 2019 and commenced on 27 September 2019. The Ghana Statistical Service [publishes the Act's operative text as its governing law](<https://statsghana.gov.gh/about/statistical-service-act>). A replacement is being prepared. In July 2026 [the Service held a stakeholder engagement on a proposed Statistics Ghana Bill, 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ghana-statistical-service_ghana-statistics-statsbill26-activity-7487065310030630913-ZlEw>). Ministries, development partners, civil society and academia took part. On 17 November 2026 [the Government Statistician said the draft Statistics Bill, which creates a Statistics Fund, would go to Cabinet and then Parliament](<https://www.myjoyonline.com/make-data-funding-compulsory-in-all-ministries-government-statistician/>). [The 2026 Data Forum urged the Service to speed its submission](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/data-forum-urges-government-to-expedite-statistics-bill-and-national-statistics-fund.html>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest fact found is the engagement on the bill. [The Service announced the consultation on the Statistics Ghana Bill in July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ghana-statistical-service_ghana-statistics-statsbill26-activity-7487065310030630913-ZlEw>), which would replace the 2019 Act seven years after it commenced. In November 2026 the bill had still to go to Cabinet. [The Data Forum asked producers of official statistics to apply the 2019 Act's confidentiality provisions](<https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/data-forum-urges-government-to-expedite-statistics-bill-and-national-statistics-fund.html>).
