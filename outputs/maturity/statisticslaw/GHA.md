# Ghana

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Ghana's statistics law is the [Statistical Service Act, 2019 (Act 1003), assented to on 24 September 2019 and commenced on 27 September 2019](<https://ghalii.org/akn/gh/act/2019/1003>). The Ghana Statistical Service [publishes the Act's operative text as its governing law](<https://statsghana.gov.gh/about/statistical-service-act>). A replacement is being prepared: in July 2026 [the Service held a stakeholder engagement on a proposed Statistics Ghana Bill, 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ghana-statistical-service_ghana-statistics-statsbill26-activity-7487065310030630913-ZlEw>) with ministries, development partners, civil society and academia.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the engagement on the bill. [The Service announced the consultation on the Statistics Ghana Bill in July 2026](<https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ghana-statistical-service_ghana-statistics-statsbill26-activity-7487065310030630913-ZlEw>), which would replace the 2019 Act seven years after it commenced. The bill is at the stage of consultation by the Service, and the 2019 Act is the law in force.

***Also on file***. [National Strategy for the Development of Statistics (NSDS III)](<https://gna.org.gh/2026/03/gss-25-mdas-sign-data-sharing-agreements/>).
