# Botswana

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Botswana's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, No. 15 of 2009](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Act%202009.pdf>), which establishes Statistics Botswana and the national statistical system. Its text gives a commencement date of 1 December 2010. Statistics Botswana's annual report for 2023/24 says [the body is established under the Act and guided by it in carrying out its mandate](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The Act has been applied in the last three years. Statistics Botswana's annual report says that in the year to 31 March 2024 [its Board was constituted in conformity with section 6 of the Statistics Act](<https://statsbots.org.bw/sites/default/files/documents/Statistics%20Botswana%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf>): nine members appointed by the Minister, with the Statistician General sitting ex officio. The Board is the body the Act sets up to govern the office, and the report presents its membership as following the Act's terms.
