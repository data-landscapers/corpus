# Algeria

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Algeria's statistics law is Legislative Decree 94-01 of 15 January 1994 on the statistical system. The statistics office said in a presentation of 1 July 2025 that [the decree remains the legal basis of official statistics and makes the office the central authority, and that the law is under review](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/dataquality/meetings/training-wks-Jul2025/Session2_ALGERIA.pdf>). An executive decree of 1995 [cites it as the law under which the office is the central statistics institution](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2193>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** A rule has been made under the law. [Executive Decree 95-159 of 3 June 1995 restates the statutes of the statistics office](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2193>), and was made under articles 11 and 17 of the legislative decree. It is the one rule held and is thirty years old. Nothing made or done under the law in the last three years is held, beyond the office's statement that the law is being reviewed.
