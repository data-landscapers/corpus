# Algeria

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Algeria's statistics law is Legislative Decree 94-01 of 15 January 1994 on the statistical system. The statistics office said in a presentation of 1 July 2025 that [the decree remains the legal basis of official statistics and makes the office the central authority](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/dataquality/meetings/training-wks-Jul2025/Session2_ALGERIA.pdf>). It said the law is under review. An executive decree of 1995 [cites it as the law under which the office is the central statistics institution](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2193>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** A rule has been made under the law. [Executive Decree 95-159 of 3 June 1995 restates the statutes of the statistics office](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2193>), and was made under articles 11 and 17 of the legislative decree. It is thirty years old. [In March 2025 the Prime Minister said the National Statistics Council had set up working committees, one of them to review legal texts not updated since 1994](<https://elmaouid.dz/%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%B4%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A1%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B7/>).
