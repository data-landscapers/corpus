# Liberia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Liberia's statistics law is the [National Statistics and Geo-Information Act, approved on 22 July 2004](<https://lisgis.gov.lr/document/lisgisact.pdf>). It adds a chapter to the Executive Law and sets up the statistics institute as an autonomous agency. The institute's catalogue entries for [the 2024 agricultural survey](<https://microdata.lisgislr.org/index.php/catalog/43>) and [the 2024 women's empowerment and nutrition survey](<https://microdata.lisgislr.org/index.php/catalog/41/study-description>) say the Act guarantees respondents' confidentiality. The institute's [open data licence of June 2026](<https://liberia.opendataforafrica.org/atrwrfc>) is governed by the Act.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held facts are those two survey entries. [The institute's entry for the 2024 Annual Agricultural Survey says the Act of 2004 guarantees the confidentiality of respondents](<https://microdata.lisgislr.org/index.php/catalog/43>), and [the entry for the nutrition survey of the same year says the same](<https://microdata.lisgislr.org/index.php/catalog/41/study-description>). They show the Act cited to respondents twenty years after it was approved. A survey carried out is not counted as the law applied, and the entries name no regulation.
