# Zimbabwe

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Zimbabwe's statistics law is the [Census and Statistics Act (Chapter 10:29), Act 1 of 2007, which commenced on 1 July 2009](<https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2007/1/eng@2020-09-04>). It establishes the Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency. The agency says [official statistics are carried out on the basis of the Act, which sets up the agency and its Board](<https://zimstat.co.zw/national-statistical-system/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest fact found is the agency's own criticism of the law. In October 2025 [the agency's spokesperson said the Act is regarded as outdated and does not support an effective national statistical system or the sharing of administrative data](<https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200047510/outdated-laws-hinder-delivery-of-statistics-zimstat>). In April 2025 [the SADC Secretariat sought a consultant to review the Act and draft a revised one](<https://sadc.int/procurement-opportunities/request-expression-interest-individual-consultancy-review-current>).
