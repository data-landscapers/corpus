# Mauritius

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Mauritius's statistics law is the [Statistics Act (Act 38 of 2000), which commenced on 2 April 2001](<https://statsmauritius.govmu.org/Documents/About_Us/Legislations/Statistics_Act_2000_All_Amended_Yr11.pdf>) and makes Statistics Mauritius the central statistical authority. It has been amended twice lately: the IMF's data review of September 2025 says [most recommended amendments were made through the Finance Act 2025](<https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2025/english/1musea2025003-source-pdf.pdf>), and [Act No. 14 of 2026, assented to on 12 August 2026, amends it again](<https://mauritiusassembly.govmu.org/mauritiusassembly/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Act1426.pdf>), adding public sector debt to the statistics it covers.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the 2026 budget. [The Budget Annex for 2026-2027 refers to the governance, quality and confidentiality safeguards provided under the Statistics Act and says the Act will be amended](<https://govmu.org/EN/budget/Documents/2026_27_annexBudget.pdf>); the amendment followed in August. An amendment changes the law and is not a rule made under it.
