# Cote d'Ivoire

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Côte d'Ivoire's statistics law is law 2013-537 of 30 July 2013 on the organisation of the National Statistical System, amended and completed by law 2020-950 of 7 December 2020. The Council of Ministers [named both laws on 18 June 2025 as the law its decrees apply](<https://web.sgg.gouv.ci/publications/conseils-des-ministres/details/3009>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules were made under the law in 2025. On 18 June 2025 [the Council of Ministers adopted three decrees applying the statistics law](<https://web.sgg.gouv.ci/publications/conseils-des-ministres/details/3009>). The first sets how the visa is granted for surveys and censuses carried out by bodies of the National Statistical System. The second sets the conditions and prices for statistical products and services. The third sets how administrative files are passed to the system's administrations for statistical use. The communiqué says the decrees are meant to give statistical production a coherent frame and to protect personal information exchanged within the system.
