# DR Congo

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** The Democratic Republic of the Congo made its first statistics law in 2026. A bill had been [drafted in December 2024 by the National Institute of Statistics](<https://drc.unfpa.org/fr/news/rdc-masina-commune-pilote-pour-la-digitalisation-des-faits-de-letat-civil>). On 28 August 2026 [the Council of Ministers adopted a draft ordonnance-loi organising statistical activity](<https://beto.cd/ordonnance-loi-statistique-rdc-recensement-habilitation/>), under powers the National Assembly voted on 24 July 2026, and the state news agency reported on 12 September 2026 that [the President had signed it](<https://acp.cd/nation/le-president-tshisekedi-fixe-les-conditions-dorganisation-de-lactivite-statistique-en-rdc/>). On 11 September [the Council validated a bill to have parliament ratify the ordonnance-loi](<https://www.opinion-info.cd/politique/2026/09/12/rdc-le-gouvernement-adopte-le-projet-de-loi-autorisant-la-ratification-du>), and [on 21 October 2026 the Senate ratified it as ordonnance-loi n° 26/015 of 7 September 2026, a 95-article text that sets the institutional framework and provides sanctions for breaches of statistical secrecy](<https://actualite.cd/2026/10/22/rdc-le-senat-ratifie-lunanimite-lordonnance-loi-portant-organisation-de-lactivite>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the ratification itself: [the Senate ratified the ordonnance-loi on 21 October 2026, unanimously, with 73 of its 109 members voting](<https://actualite.cd/2026/10/22/rdc-le-senat-ratifie-lunanimite-lordonnance-loi-portant-organisation-de-lactivite>). The ordonnance-loi was six weeks old then, and nothing made under it is on record.
