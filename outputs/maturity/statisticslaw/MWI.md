# Malawi

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Malawi's statistics law is the [National Statistics Act (Chapter 27:01), which commenced on 19 July 2013](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2013/6/eng@2014-12-31>). It establishes the National Statistical Office and the National Statistical System.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the Act's own text. [The text held gives the commencement date of 19 July 2013 and the two bodies the Act sets up](<https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/2013/6/eng@2014-12-31>); the capture stops partway through, so no later section has been found. It is the one source found for Malawi that states a fact on the statistics law. No other document held on the country's statistics states a fact on the Statistics Act. No decree, regulation or order made under the Statistics Act is named in what has been found.
