# Lesotho

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Lesotho's statistics law is the [Bureau of Statistics Act, No. 8 of 2001](<http://www.bos.gov.ls/BOS_Act_2001.htm>). The African Union's peer review says [the Act established the Bureau, the national statistical system and the National Statistical Council, and needs modernising](<https://statafric.au.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/ef_peerreview_lesotho_vf.pdf>), and in September 2026 a newspaper reported that [the Bureau is mandated under it to conduct censuses and compile official statistics](<https://publiceyenews.com/2026/09/17/we-felt-like-part-of-the-basotho-census-gives-lgbtiq-community-a-place-in-national-data/>). A replacement is being drafted: the reviewers were told in August 2025 that [the Office of Parliamentary Counsel was drafting a fourth Statistics Bill](<https://statafric.au.int/sites/default/files/2025-12/ef_peerreview_lesotho_vf.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the bill's progress. In June 2026 [the Bureau's director told a media workshop that the Statistics Bill is expected to be enacted before the end of 2026](<https://sundayexpress.co.ls/?p=36134>). The Bureau [lists the 2001 Act first among its current documents](<http://www.bos.gov.ls/about_bos.htm>).
