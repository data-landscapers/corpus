# Mauritania

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Mauritania's statistics law is law no. 2005-017 of 27 January 2005 on public statistics. The national statistics strategy for 2021 to 2030 [lists it as the main text governing public statistical activity, and says it had not been revised](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules were made under the law and its council was appointed. The strategy records that [decree no. 2006-024 of 17 April 2006 sets the organisation and operating rules of the National Statistical Council, created in application of the statistics law](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>), and that [order no. 1012/MEF/08 of 30 March 2008 appointed the council's members](<https://afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/17_SNDDS-2021-2030-plan-dactions-2021-2025.pdf>). Both acts date from the three years after the law was made. No other document held on the country's statistics names anything made or done under the law since 2008.
