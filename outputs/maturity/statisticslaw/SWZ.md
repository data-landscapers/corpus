# Eswatini

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Eswatini's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 1967 (Act 14 of 1967), which commenced on 27 October 1967](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/1967/14/eng@1998-12-01>). The text held is consolidated at 1 December 1998 and is stated to be the latest version.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is that consolidated text. [The legal database gives the 1967 Act as in force and unamended since the 1998 consolidation](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/1967/14/eng@1998-12-01>). It is the one source found for Eswatini that states a fact on the statistics law. The Act predates the country's independence in 1968 and is fifty-nine years old. No other document held on the country's statistics names a regulation made under the Act or a bill to replace it. The Act gives a power to make regulations; no regulation made with that power has been found.

***Also on file***. [Central Statistical Office](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/1967/14/eng@1998-12-01>).

***Not found***. No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law.
