# Eswatini

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Eswatini's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 1967 (Act 14 of 1967), which commenced on 27 October 1967](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/1967/14/eng@1998-12-01>). The text held is consolidated at 1 December 1998 and is stated to be the latest version.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest fact found is the Act's use in the economic census. In October 2026 [the Principal Statistician said the census operates under the Statistics Act, which protects confidential statistical information, including from the tax authorities](<https://eswatinipositivenews.online/economic-census-to-shape-business-growth-and-job-creation-in-eswatini/>). A census carried out is not counted as the law applied. [The 1967 Act gives a power to make regulations](<https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/1967/14/eng@1998-12-01>), and the text held is unamended since its 1998 consolidation.
