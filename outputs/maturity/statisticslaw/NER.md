# Niger

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Niger's statistics law is [Loi n° 2004-011 of 30 March 2004 on the organisation of statistical activity](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=2129>), which creates the National Institute of Statistics and the national statistical system. A replacement is close: in October 2025 [a statisticians' union said a committee of the institute had drafted an ordonnance on the principles of statistical activity and sent it to the government's secretariat](<https://www.lesahel.org/7e-congres-ordinaire-du-syndicat-national-du-personnel-de-linformation-statistique-synpis-le-recensement-general-de-la-population-le-bilan-du-mandat-ecoule-et-le-renouvellement-du-b/>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the cabinet's decision. [The Council of Ministers of 23 January 2026 adopted a draft ordonnance on the fundamental principles of statistical activities, instituting a National Statistical System and a national fund for statistical activity](<https://www.gouv.ne/index.php/les-communiques-du-gouvernement/1028-conclusions-du-conseil-des-ministres-du-vendredi-23-janvier-2026>). The communiqué does not say the ordonnance was signed and published, so the law of 2004 is counted as the one in force.
