# Equatorial Guinea

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Equatorial Guinea's statistics law is Law 3/2020 of 16 October 2020 regulating statistical activity. The national statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 says [statistical activity in the country is governed by that law, which replaced Law 3/2001 of 17 May 2001 and was aligned with the African Charter on Statistics](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the strategy's account. [The strategy says the government amended the 2001 law to meet the new needs of the statistical system, and names the national statistics council, the statistical programmes committee and the statistics institute as the system's parts under the 2020 law](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). No other document held on the country's statistics ties a meeting or a decree to the statistics law.
