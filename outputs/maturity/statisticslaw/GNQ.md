# Equatorial Guinea

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** The national statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 says [Law 3/2020 of 16 October 2020 governs statistical activity in Equatorial Guinea](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). It [replaced Law 3/2001 of 17 May 2001 and was aligned with the African Charter on Statistics](<https://inege.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ENDE-2022-2026-1.pdf>). An [African Union peer review in September 2025 credited the country with a modernised statistics law](<https://statafric.au.int/en/news/press-releases/2025-11-17/peer-review-progress-and-priority-actions-equatorial-guineas-nss>). [It found the national statistics council and the programmes committee working since 2020](<https://statafric.au.int/en/news/press-releases/2025-11-17/peer-review-progress-and-priority-actions-equatorial-guineas-nss>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The same review [called for key decrees to be updated to align with the 2020 law](<https://statafric.au.int/en/news/press-releases/2025-11-17/peer-review-progress-and-priority-actions-equatorial-guineas-nss>). [In May 2022 the national statistics council gave 60 enterprises 72 hours to register for the first enterprise census](<https://aacapital.gq/2022/05/18/consejo-nacional-de-estadisticas-aquellas-empresas-que-no-se-han-empadronado-hasta-ahora-tienen-72-horas-o-seran-sancionadas/>). [Firms that did not would be fined under articles 7.1 and 36.1 of the law](<https://aacapital.gq/2022/05/18/consejo-nacional-de-estadisticas-aquellas-empresas-que-no-se-han-empadronado-hasta-ahora-tienen-72-horas-o-seran-sancionadas/>).
