# Mozambique

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Mozambique's statistics law is [Lei n.º 7/96 of 5 July 1996](<https://ieda.gov.mz/poema/EST-S1/EST-S1-Lei_7_96_de_5_de_Julho.pdf>), by which the Assembly of the Republic created the National Statistical System.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the law's own text. [The text held is an excerpt of the opening chapters of the 1996 law](<https://ieda.gov.mz/poema/EST-S1/EST-S1-Lei_7_96_de_5_de_Julho.pdf>), which set up the system and its bodies. It is the one source found for Mozambique that states a fact on the statistics law. The law is thirty years old. No other document held on the country's statistics states a fact on the statistics law. No decree, regulation or order made under the 1996 law is named in what has been found, and no bill to amend or replace it.
