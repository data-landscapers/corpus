# Guinea-Bissau

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Guinea-Bissau's statistics law is [Lei n.º 6/2007 of 10 September 2007, the framework law of the National Statistical System](<https://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Regulamento%20e%20Estatuto%20Organico/Legisla%C3%A7%C3%A3o_INE.pdf>), made by the National People's Assembly. It sets up the national statistical system with its higher statistics council and the national statistics institute.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** A rule has been made under the law. The statistics institute's compilation of the law [also carries Decreto n.º 4/2023 of 31 March 2023, the regulation of the 2007 statistics law](<https://stat-guinebissau.com/Menu_principal/Pubica%C3%A7%C3%B5es/Regulamento%20e%20Estatuto%20Organico/Legisla%C3%A7%C3%A3o_INE.pdf>). The regulation came sixteen years after the law. It is known by its title in the compilation: the text held breaks off before it, so what it regulates is not held. It is dated six months before the three years that end on 30 September 2026, and nothing later is held.
