# Sao Tome and Principe

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** São Tomé and Príncipe's statistics law is [Lei n.º 5/98, the framework law of the National Statistical System, passed by the National Assembly on 12 August 1998 and promulgated on 31 August 1998](<https://www.ine.st/Recenseamento/Lei%20n5-98-%20Leis%20de%20Base%20do%20Sistema%20Estat%C3%ADstico%20Nacional_PDF.pdf>). Its article 18 brings it into force at once.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the law's own text. [The text held is published by the national statistics institute and gives the dates of passage and promulgation](<https://www.ine.st/Recenseamento/Lei%20n5-98-%20Leis%20de%20Base%20do%20Sistema%20Estat%C3%ADstico%20Nacional_PDF.pdf>). It is the one source found for São Tomé and Príncipe on the subject. The law is twenty-eight years old and sets the bases of the national statistical system, which the institute leads. No decree, regulation or order made under the law is named in what has been found, and no bill to amend or replace it.
