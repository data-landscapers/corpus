# Congo

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Congo's statistics law is law no. 36-2018 on official statistics. [The Journal officiel, issue no. 42 of 18 October 2018, carries its text](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2018/congo-jo-2018-42.pdf>). The national statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 says [statistical activity in Congo is governed by the law, which it dates 5 October 2018](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SNDS_2022-2026_Congo.pdf>). The statistics institute INS Congo's release calendar for 2026 [names law 36-2018 on official statistics as the national legal framework it rests on](<https://www.ins-congo.cg/uploads/publications/ins_6ad8553134ba03.75061568.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** In October 2026 [INS Congo, the statistics institute, issued its advance release calendar for 2026, stating that it answers the requirements of law 36-2018](<https://www.ins-congo.cg/uploads/publications/ins_6ad8553134ba03.75061568.pdf>). The calendar sets the dates on which the year's official statistics are to be released.
