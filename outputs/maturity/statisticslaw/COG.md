# Congo

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Congo's statistics law is law no. 36-2018 on official statistics. [The Journal officiel of 18 October 2018 carries its text](<https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2018/congo-jo-2018-42.pdf>). The national statistics strategy for 2022 to 2026 says [statistical activity in Congo is governed by the law, which it dates 5 October 2018](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SNDS_2022-2026_Congo.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the strategy's account of the law. [The 2022 to 2026 strategy](<https://www.afristat.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/SNDS_2022-2026_Congo.pdf>) names law 36-2018 as the text governing statistical activity and builds its programme on it. The strategy is a plan for the statistical system and is not a rule made under the law. The gazette issue and the strategy are the two sources found for Congo, and both concern the law itself: its publication in October 2018 and its standing as the governing text four years later.
