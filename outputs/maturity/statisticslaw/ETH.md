# Ethiopia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Ethiopia's statistics law is the [Central Statistics Authority Establishment Proclamation No. 442/2005](<https://www.lawethiopia.com/images/federal_proclamation/proclamations_by_number/442.pdf>), published in the Federal Negarit Gazeta on 20 April 2005. Its successor has passed parliament: [the Council of Ministers took up a draft statistics proclamation on 26 May 2026](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8922874>), and [the House of People's Representatives approved the Ethiopian Statistical Service Proclamation unanimously on 30 June 2026](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopian-lawmakers-approve-carbon-market-tax-administration-statistical-service-proclamations/>). [President Taye Atske Selassie said on 21 October 2026 that Ethiopia has reformed its statistical legislation and set up an independent statistical service](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_9786837>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the vote of June 2026. [The House approved the new proclamation at its 29th regular session on 30 June 2026](<https://www.fanamc.com/english/ethiopian-lawmakers-approve-carbon-market-tax-administration-statistical-service-proclamations/>); the draft the Council of Ministers considered [covers the Statistical Service's independence, its oversight and the confidentiality of data](<https://www.ena.et/web/eng/w/eng_8922874>). The report of the vote does not say the proclamation has been published in the gazette, so the 2005 proclamation is the law in force.
