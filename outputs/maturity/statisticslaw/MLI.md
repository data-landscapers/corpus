# Mali

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Mali's statistics law is [Loi n°2016-005 of 24 February 2016 governing public statistics, published in the Official Gazette of 18 March 2016](<https://sgg-mali.ml/JO/2016/mali-jo-2016-12.pdf>). The statistics master plan says [the law gave the national statistical system a legal frame in line with the African Charter on Statistics](<https://instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/sdsmli20-24_ca.pdf>). A methods manual of December 2025 [cites its first article as the ground of statistical quality](<https://www.instat-mali.org/storage/319/manuel-harmonise-methodologie-collecte-statistique_pub.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** Rules were made under the law and its council was appointed. The master plan says [the implementing decrees of the statistics law were taken in 2016](<https://instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/sdsmli20-24_ca.pdf>). It adds that [the order appointing the members of the National Statistics Council, the coordinating body the law sets up, was taken only in 2018, which delayed putting the body in place](<https://instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/ca/sdsmli20-24_ca.pdf>). Nothing made or done under the law in the last three years is held.
