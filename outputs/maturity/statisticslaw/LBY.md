# Libya

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Libya's statistics law is [Law No. 16 of 1963 on statistics and censuses, issued by King Idris I on 19 September 1963](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-16-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1963-%d9%85-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84/>). The Law Society's database of legislation lists it as in force. In August 2024 the Libya Observer reported that a government meeting [addressed a committee's findings on reviewing and updating the 1963 Statistics and Census Law](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-prepares-national-population-census>), to improve the work of the Statistics and Census Authority.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the August 2024 report that the Government of National Unity [took up a committee's findings on reviewing and updating the 1963 law](<https://libyaobserver.ly/news/gnu-prepares-national-population-census>). The same report links the review to the Statistics and Census Authority's operations. The 1963 law itself [remains the text the database lists as in force](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-16-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1963-%d9%85-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84/>).
