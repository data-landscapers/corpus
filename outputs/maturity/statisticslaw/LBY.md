# Libya

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Libya's statistics law is [Law No. 16 of 1963 on statistics and censuses, issued by King Idris I on 19 September 1963](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-16-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1963-%d9%85-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84/>). The Law Society's database of legislation lists it as in force.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is that listing. [The database carries the text of the 1963 law and marks it as in force](<https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/%d9%82%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%86-%d8%b1%d9%82%d9%85-16-%d9%84%d8%b3%d9%86%d8%a9-1963-%d9%85-%d9%81%d9%8a-%d8%b4%d8%a3%d9%86-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%ad%d8%b5%d8%a7%d8%a1%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d9%88%d8%a7%d9%84/>). It is the one source held for Libya on the subject. The law predates every later constitution and government of the country and is sixty-three years old, the second oldest statistics law in force among the countries assessed. The law therefore stands as the database gives it. Its title covers both the regular statistics of the state and the taking of censuses.

***Noted, not assessed***. None held.

***Not held***. No source held names a decree, regulation or order made under the law.
