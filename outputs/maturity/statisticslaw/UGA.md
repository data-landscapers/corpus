# Uganda

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Uganda's statistics law is the [Uganda Bureau of Statistics Act (Chapter 310), which commenced on 11 June 1998](<https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/1998/12/eng@2000-12-31/source>). It establishes the Bureau and the national statistical system.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is a statement of August 2026. [The chairman of the Bureau's Board told the Finance Minister that the Bureau was established under the Act of 1998 and continues to coordinate the National Statistical System under it](<https://www.finance.go.ug/media-center/news-and-updates/finance-minister-calls-stronger-evidence-based-statistics-support>), as the finance ministry reported on 7 August 2026. The statement shows the Act standing twenty-eight years after it commenced and names the Board the Act provides for. It does not date the Board's appointment or a meeting, and it names no regulation. No other document held on the country's statistics names a regulation made under the Act.
