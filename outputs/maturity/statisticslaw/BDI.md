# Burundi

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** [Loi n°1/08 of 20 May 2021 amends Burundi's statistics law of 25 September 2007](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>). [The 2007 law organises the national statistical system](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>). [The 2021 law entered into force on the day of its promulgation](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** No source found names a decree, regulation or order made under the law. The nearest held fact: [in May 2021 a new law amended the 2007 statute](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>).
