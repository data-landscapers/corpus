# Burundi

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Burundi's statistics law dates from 25 September 2007 and was amended in 2021. The held text is [Loi n°1/08 of 20 May 2021](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>), which modifies the 2007 law on the organisation of the national statistical system. It takes effect on the day of its promulgation. The law is the one text held on the subject for Burundi, and it carries the amendment and not the 2007 law it changes.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the 2021 amendment itself. [The law of 20 May 2021](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>) changes the 2007 statute on how the national statistical system is organised, and names the national committee on statistical information and its technical committee as the system's bodies. It was made by parliament and is a change to the law, so it is counted as the law in force.
