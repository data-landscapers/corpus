# Burundi

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** [Loi n°1/08 of 20 May 2021 recast Burundi's statistics law of 25 September 2007](<https://amategeko.gov.bi/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/BOB_No5BIS-2021.pdf>), which organises the national statistical system. [Loi n°1/18 of 24 July 2023 amended it again](<https://www.insbu.bi/documents/lois/Loi-N%C2%B0-1-18-du-24-juillet-2023-portant-organisation-du-SSN-du-Burundi.pdf>). [It sets up the national statistical authority and the national statistical information committee (CNIS)](<https://www.insbu.bi/documents/lois/Loi-N%C2%B0-1-18-du-24-juillet-2023-portant-organisation-du-SSN-du-Burundi.pdf>). [It also makes every nationally representative survey subject to a statistical visa](<https://www.insbu.bi/documents/lois/Loi-N%C2%B0-1-18-du-24-juillet-2023-portant-organisation-du-SSN-du-Burundi.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** [Decrees of 16 November 2022 created the CNIS and the national statistical authority, the statistics institute INSBU, and set their rules](<https://www.insbu.bi/documents/visas/DECISION-N%C2%B0540.95.637.2024-DU-22.07.2024-PORTANT-OCTROI-DU-VISA-STATISTIQUE-AU-PROTOCOLE-DE-LETUDE-DEVALUATION-DIMPACTS-SOCIO-ECONOMIQUES-DU-PHENOMENE-EL-NINO-AU-BURUNDI.pdf>). [On 22 July 2024 the authority's head granted a statistical visa to a study of El Niño's socio-economic impact](<https://www.insbu.bi/documents/visas/DECISION-N%C2%B0540.95.637.2024-DU-22.07.2024-PORTANT-OCTROI-DU-VISA-STATISTIQUE-AU-PROTOCOLE-DE-LETUDE-DEVALUATION-DIMPACTS-SOCIO-ECONOMIQUES-DU-PHENOMENE-EL-NINO-AU-BURUNDI.pdf>). [The CNIS held its 13th session on 6 November 2026, chaired by the Prime Minister](<https://burundi-news.com/articles/fr/renforcement-du-systeme-statistique-national-au-burundi.html>). It discussed [draft decrees on an enterprise classification and an order of statistics professionals](<https://burundi-news.com/articles/fr/renforcement-du-systeme-statistique-national-au-burundi.html>).
