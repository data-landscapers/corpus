# Sierra Leone

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Sierra Leone's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 2002, passed in Parliament on 12 September 2002 and published in the Gazette on 7 November 2002](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/StatisticsSL/Documents/statistics_and_census_acts.pdf>). It establishes Statistics Sierra Leone as a body corporate. In February 2024 the office said it is [established by the Act as the central authority for official statistics, supervised by a Statistics Council](<https://www.statistics.sl/images/2023_Stats_SL_Proactive_Publication_Scheme.pdf>). It relies on the Act for confidentiality: its catalogue entries for the annual agricultural surveys of [2024](<https://microdata.statistics.sl/index.php/catalog/16>) and [2023](<https://microdata.statistics.sl/index.php/catalog/14/study-description>) say respondents' confidentiality is guaranteed by the Act.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held facts are those survey entries. [The entry for the 2024 Annual Agricultural Survey says the Statistics Act of 2002 guarantees the confidentiality of respondents](<https://microdata.statistics.sl/index.php/catalog/16>), and [the 2023 entry says the same](<https://microdata.statistics.sl/index.php/catalog/14/study-description>). They show the Act cited to respondents more than twenty years after it passed. A survey carried out is not counted as the law applied, and the entries name no regulation made under the Act.
