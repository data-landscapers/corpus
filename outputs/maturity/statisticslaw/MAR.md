# Morocco

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Morocco's statistics law in force is [Royal Decree-Law no. 370-67 of 5 August 1968 on statistical studies](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=46>), in force from 15 August 1968. Its replacement has passed parliament: [the Council of Government approved draft law 046.26 on the national statistical system on 2 July 2026](<https://www.linformation.ma/news/politique/le-conseil-de-gouvernement-approuve-un-projet-de-loi-relatif-au-systeme-statistique-national/64631>), and [the Chamber of Councillors adopted it unanimously on 13 July 2026](<https://lematin.ma/nation/le-parlement-adopte-la-reforme-du-hcp-et-des-statistiques-nationales/355927>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the vote. [The bill, presented by the Interior Minister, was adopted by the Chamber of Councillors on Monday 13 July 2026](<https://www.maroc24.com/fr/202293-chambre-des-conseillers-adoption-a-l-unanimite-de-deux-projets-de-loi-relatifs-au-hcp-et-au-systeme-statistique-national/>). It sets a new legal frame for the statistical system in place of the 1968 text. The reports of the vote do not say the law has been promulgated, so the decree-law of 1968 is counted as the law in force at the end of September 2026.
