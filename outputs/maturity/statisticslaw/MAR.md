# Morocco

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Morocco has a new statistics law. [Law 046.26 on official statistics was promulgated by dahir of 28 July 2026 and published in the Bulletin officiel of 10 August 2026](<https://canal212.ma/hcp-statistiques-gouvernance-reforme-maroc/>). It takes effect only at the first meeting of the deliberative organ of the national statistics council it sets up. [The Council of Government approved the bill on 2 July 2026](<https://www.linformation.ma/news/politique/le-conseil-de-gouvernement-approuve-un-projet-de-loi-relatif-au-systeme-statistique-national/64631>), and [the Chamber of Councillors adopted it unanimously on 13 July 2026](<https://lematin.ma/nation/le-parlement-adopte-la-reforme-du-hcp-et-des-statistiques-nationales/355927>). Until it takes effect, the text in force is [Royal Decree-Law no. 370-67 of 5 August 1968 on statistical studies](<https://unstats.un.org/unsd/dnss/docViewer.aspx?docID=46>), in force from 15 August 1968.

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the new law's start. [Law 046.26 enters into force only when the deliberative organ of the future national statistics council first meets](<https://canal212.ma/hcp-statistiques-gouvernance-reforme-maroc/>). The bill was [presented to the Chamber of Councillors by the Interior Minister](<https://www.maroc24.com/fr/202293-chambre-des-conseillers-adoption-a-l-unanimite-de-deux-projets-de-loi-relatifs-au-hcp-et-au-systeme-statistique-national/>), together with a bill on the High Commission for Planning.
