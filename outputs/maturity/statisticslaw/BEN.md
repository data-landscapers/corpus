# Benin

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Benin's statistics law is [Loi n° 2022-07 of 27 June 2022 on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/lois_decrets/LOI%20N%C2%B0%202022-07%20DU%2027%20JUIN%202022%20STATISTIQUE.pdf>), adopted by the National Assembly on 2 June 2022 and promulgated by the President. It replaced an older frame: the statistics strategy of 2020 said statistical activity then rested on [a law of 12 April 2000 creating the National Statistics Council](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/cns/programme-SNDS/SNDS-3%20VF.pdf>), and that [a draft of a new statistics law was being adopted](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/cns/programme-SNDS/SNDS-3%20VF.pdf>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is a decree that rests on the law. [Decree 2026-002 of 21 January 2026](<https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2026-002/download>) cites the law of 27 June 2022 among its legal bases, which shows the law in use by the government in 2026. The decree is held for that citation; what it regulates is not stated in what is held of it.

***Also on file***. [Stratégie Nationale de Développement de la Statistique (SNDS-3) 2020-2023](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/cns/programme-SNDS/SNDS-3%20VF.pdf>); [African Charter on Statistics](<https://instad.bj/images/docs/cns/programme-SNDS/SNDS-3%20VF.pdf>).
