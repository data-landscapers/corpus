# Madagascar

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Madagascar's statistics law is [Loi n° 2018-004 of 18 April 2018 on the organisation and regulation of statistical activities](<https://instat.mg/documents/upload/main/INSTAT_LoiStatistique2018-004_18-04-2018.pdf>), adopted by the Senate on 2 March 2018 and by the National Assembly on 12 March 2018. The statistics institute [names the law as an institutional result achieved, on its page for the statistics development strategy for 2023 to 2030](<https://www.instat.mg/p/strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-snds-deuxieme-generation-2023>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the institute's account of the law. [Its strategy page lists the law of 18 April 2018 among the results already secured](<https://www.instat.mg/p/strategie-nationale-de-developpement-de-la-statistique-snds-deuxieme-generation-2023>) and plans the period to 2030 on that footing. The strategy is a plan and is not a rule made under the law. No other document held on the country's statistics names a decree applying the law.
