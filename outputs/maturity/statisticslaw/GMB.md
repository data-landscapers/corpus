# Gambia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** The Gambia's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 2005](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/167-statistics-act-2005>). The statistics bureau's strategy published in September 2023 says [the Act governs statistical activities, provides for the national statistical system and establishes the bureau as its supervisory authority](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). A revision has been pending for years: the same strategy says [the Act is under revision, and plans a review of the 2019 Statistics Bill and a new statistics law by 2026](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the strategy's plan for the bill. [The strategy of September 2023 commits the bureau to review the Statistics Bill of 2019 and to press for its enactment](<https://www.gbosdata.org/downloads-file/526-national-strategy-for-the-development-of-stati>). The bill was four years old when the strategy was written. No other document held on the country's statistics reports the bill tabled or passed.
