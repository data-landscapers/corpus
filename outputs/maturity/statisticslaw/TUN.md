# Tunisia

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** Tunisia's statistics law is [Law no. 99-32 of 13 April 1999 on the national statistical system](<https://www.ins.tn/sites/default/files/loi%20n%C2%B099-32%20du%2013avril%201999%2C%20relatif%20au%20syst%C3%A8me%20national%20de%20la%20statistique%20link%208_0.pdf>), adopted by the Chamber of Deputies and promulgated by the President of the Republic. A revision is planned: in June 2026 [the acting president of the National Statistics Council said the ministry is preparing a new statistics bill as a full revision of the 1999 law](<https://diwanfm.net/news/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B3-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%87-%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88-%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is that statement. [The council's acting president, speaking on 5 June 2026, described the bill as in preparation at the ministry](<https://diwanfm.net/news/%D9%88%D8%B7%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9/%D8%AA%D9%88%D9%86%D8%B3-%D8%AA%D8%AA%D8%AC%D9%87-%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88-%D8%A5%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86-%D8%A5%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B2-%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%83%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AA>). A law in preparation with no draft published is not counted as a draft, so the standing is that of the 1999 law alone. No other document held on the country's statistics names a decree or order made under the law.
