# South Africa

## Statistics law

**Law in force.** South Africa's statistics law is the [Statistics Act, 1999 (Act 6 of 1999), published in the Government Gazette on 21 April 1999](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a6-99.pdf>). It was amended by the [Statistics Amendment Act, 2024, assented to on 10 December 2024 and gazetted on 20 December 2024](<https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202501/51805-statisticsamendmentact29of2024.pdf>). The Deputy Minister in The Presidency told a parliamentary committee that [the amendment was proclaimed for implementation on 1 October 2025](<https://www.gov.za/news/speeches/deputy-minister-nonceba-mhauli-stats-sa-202526-q3-report-portfolio-committee-planning>).

**Rules made under the law and the law applied.** The nearest held fact is the roll-out of the amendment. A parliamentary committee report of 23 June 2026 [records Statistics South Africa briefing it on the Act and lists rolling out the 2024 amendments as work in hand](<https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/Docs/atc/01ls62wgfubry77aimpfb3gfqgvq2vzzwq.pdf>). The office also [cites sections 17 and 18 of the 1999 Act as the guarantee of respondents' confidentiality](<https://www.statssa.gov.za/?page_id=14209>). An amendment changes the law and is not a rule made under it.
